Spearman correlation in the same cross-sectional study of 320 nursing students. Academic performance was measured via self-reported grades; the correlation with real-time AI interaction was "moderate positive" and statistically significant.

> "Real-time interaction with AI showed a moderate positive correlation with learning experience (r = 0.547, p < 0.001) and the academic performance (r = 0.567, p < 0.001)"
