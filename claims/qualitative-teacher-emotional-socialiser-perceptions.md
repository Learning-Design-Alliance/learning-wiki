### Nguyen 2026

Nguyen, T.-A., Hidajat, T., Zhou, T., Ma, T., Hoang, A., Poed, S., & Edwards, E. J. (2026). Relations between Teachers' and Students' Social-emotional Competence: A Systematic Review and Multilevel Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10218-4

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of qualitative and mixed-methods studies (interviews with teachers and students, questionnaires, document review, observation notes). The review reports teachers perceived themselves as emotional socialisers modelling empathy, persistence, and emotion labelling, believed to enhance students' motivation, peer cooperation, and self-efficacy.

> "Teachers who embed respectful language (Cox et al., 2016), treat learners with warmth and care (Sparks et al., 2015), and provide consistent behavioural structures (Kılıç, 2015; Zinsser et al., 2014) were perceived by teachers and students to cultivate classrooms where students felt seen, secure, and unafraid to try new ideas"
