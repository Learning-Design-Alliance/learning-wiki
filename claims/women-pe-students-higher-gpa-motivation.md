## Evidence

### Kuśnierz Cezary 2020

Kuśnierz Cezary, Rogowska Aleksandra M., Pavlova Iuliia. (2020). Examining Gender Differences, Personality Traits, Academic Performance, and Motivation in Ukrainian and Polish Students of Physical Education: A Cross-Cultural Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17165729

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 424 PE undergraduates (62% men) at one Polish and one Ukrainian university; Student's t-tests on Table 2 data show GPA d = 0.39, Intrinsic Motivation d = 0.36, Amotivation d = 0.39, with "Women demonstrated higher academic performance (GPA)".

> "Women demonstrated higher academic performance (GPA), agreeableness, and conscientiousness and scored lower in emotional stability when compared to men."
