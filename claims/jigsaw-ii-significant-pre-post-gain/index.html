Turkmen 2015¶

Turkmen, H. & Buyukaltay, D. (2015). Which one is better? Jigsaw II versus Jigsaw IV on the subject of the building blocks of matter and atom. Journal of Education in Science, Environment and Health (JESEH), 1(2), 88-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105340

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent-group t-test on the 24-student control group taught with Jigsaw II; pre-test mean 6.20 (SD 2.14), post-test mean 7.95 (SD 1.92), t = -3.095, p = 0.005. The article reports the difference "occurred in favor of the post-test".

"A statistically significance level of 0.05 was found in the dependent group t -test carried out for achievement pre-test and post -test of the control group. This difference occurred in favor of the post-test."
