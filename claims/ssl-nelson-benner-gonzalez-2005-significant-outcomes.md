### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Stepping Stones to Literacy. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.steppingstonestoliteracy.com

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Randomized controlled trial of 36 kindergarten students with behavior problems (18 per condition) in seven Midwestern schools, SSL versus core curriculum alone over five weeks. Appendix A3 reports effect sizes from 0.37 (Letter Naming Fluency) to 1.26 (Initial Sound Fluency), all statistically significant.
