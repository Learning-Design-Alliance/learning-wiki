Evidence¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin 2023¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin. (2023). Interventions and Approaches Targeting Early Self-Regulation or Executive Functioning in Preschools: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09740-6

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's cross-approach reconciliation in the Discussion identifies patterns amongst studies achieving comparatively stronger efficacy. It reports that "Cognitive challenge was a feature evident in all the interventions identified as being most efficacious", with challenge increased incrementally with familiarity. This is the authors' interpretive synthesis, not a tested manipulation.

"Cognitive challenge was a feature evident in all the interventions identified as being most efficacious. Uniformly across the approaches reviewed, these interventions would increase EF and/or SR challenge incrementally as children became more familiar with and capable with the intervention activities."
