## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multivariable regression, classroom randomisation, working in hospital, low pretest knowledge and motivation were associated with better learning outcome; being a man, working in hospital, and low pretest knowledge and certainty were associated with reduced risk of error. ## Evidence

### Simonsen Bjoerg O 2014

Simonsen Bjoerg O, Daehlin Gro K, Johansson Inger, Farup Per G. (2014). Improvement of drug dose calculations by classroom teaching or e-learning: a randomised controlled trial in nurses. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-006025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression with all participant characteristics (n=183, table 4): pretest knowledge β=−0.61 (p<0.001) for learning outcome; classroom randomisation β=0.16 (p=0.02); motivation β=0.17 (p=0.02). Total R2 changes were 0.28 and 0.18 for learning outcome and risk of error.

> "the randomisation to classroom teaching was significantly better in learning outcome, adjusted for other variables. Both low pretest knowledge and certainty score were associated with a reduced risk of error after the course, as were being a man and working in hospital."
