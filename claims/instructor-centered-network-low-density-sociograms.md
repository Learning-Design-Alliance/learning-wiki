# The course network was instructor-centered with low student-to-student density, as shown by sociogram visualization

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The overall course sociogram showed the instructor receiving most interactions (high in-degree, highest degree and betweenness), a pattern recognized as instructor-centered and aligned with the course design. [→ Saqr Mohammed 2018](#saqr-mohammed-2018)
`q2 i?` Network density was 0.14, which the authors read as few student-to-student interactions favoring the instructor; information-giving networks were more collaborative than information-receiving networks. [→ Saqr Mohammed 2018 (2)](#saqr-mohammed-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Saqr Mohammed 2018

Saqr Mohammed, Fors Uno, Tedre Matti. (2018). How the study of online collaborative learning can guide teachers and predict students' performance in a medical course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1126-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Visual SNA of the whole-course network (36 nodes, 1251 interactions) in Gephi. The article describes the instructor as "receiving most interactions (high in-degree)" with the largest node and darkest color, a pattern it labels instructor-centered.

> "The graph in Fig. 2 shows the instructor (represented by the node T1) with most edges pointing to him, which indicates that he is receiving most interactions (high in-degree)."

### Saqr Mohammed 2018 (2)

Saqr Mohammed, Fors Uno, Tedre Matti. (2018). How the study of online collaborative learning can guide teachers and predict students' performance in a medical course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1126-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative network properties of the course network. The article reports "graph density was 0.14", average degree 69.5, clustering 0.213, and interprets low density as few peer-to-peer interactions; side-by-side graphs showed information-giving was more collaborative than information-receiving.

> "Students participated in 34 discussion threads, graph density was 0.14, the average degree was 69.5, and average clustering coefficient was 0.213. The low graph density is an indication of a low number of interactions among students, favoring the course instructor during the course."
