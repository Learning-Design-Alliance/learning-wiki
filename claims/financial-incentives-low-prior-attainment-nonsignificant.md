`q3 i0` For low prior attainment pupils, the financial incentive effect on GCSE Maths was an estimated 0.14 effect sizes but not statistically significant. [→ Sibieta et al. 2014](#sibieta-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Sibieta et al. 2014

Luke Sibieta, Ellen Greaves, Barbara Sianesi. (2014). Increasing Pupil Motivation: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581249

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group analysis of the cluster RCT for 2,551 low prior attainment pupils in the financial-incentive arm; Table 1 reports an effect size of 0.14 (-0.02, 0.29) for GCSE Maths, not statistically significant, and 0.17 for English, also not significant.

> "There was a positive impact of both the event and financial incentives on GCSE Maths for pupils with low levels of prior attainment – equivalent to about one quarter of a GCSE grade in Maths, although this is not statistically significant for the financial incentive treatment."
