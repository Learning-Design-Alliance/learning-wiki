### Ličen Sabina 2025

Ličen Sabina, Prosen Mirko. (2025). Disaster Nursing Competencies in a Time of Global Conflicts and Climate Crises: A Cross‐Sectional Survey Study. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis test (H = 14.001, p = 0.003) with post-hoc Dwass–Steel–Critchlow–Fligner comparisons across four facility types in the cross-sectional survey. The higher-roles contrast for social welfare vs tertiary is reported as W = 4.675, p = 0.005.

> "A post‐hoc analysis using the Dwass–Steel–Critchlow–Fligner test showed that nurses in social welfare facilities rated these competencies significantly higher than nurses in tertiary (W = 4.83, p = 0.004) and secondary healthcare facilities (W = 4.38, p = 0.011)."
