Evidence¶

Frijters 2008¶

Frijters, S., ten Dam, G., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2008). Effects of dialogic learning on value-loaded critical thinking. Learning and Instruction, 18(1), 66-82. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2006.11.001

q3 · i? · causal · r?

A published experimental study in pre-vocational secondary education compared a Dialogic and a Non-Dialogic lesson series for value-loaded critical thinking in Biology. The article reports the dialogic condition showed "a more positive effect on the critical-thinking competences of the students"; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"The results indicated that the dialogic learning condition, compared to the non-dialogic, resulted in a more positive effect on the critical-thinking competences of the students, both in terms of generative fluency of reasoning and quality of value orientation."
