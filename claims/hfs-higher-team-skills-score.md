Grissa Mohamed Habib et al. (2023). Comparison of simulation and video-based training for acute asthma. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04836-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teamwork was rated during the seven-day simulation scenario test using the first 8 items of the Mayo High Performance Teamwork Scale (range 0–16). HFS scored "11.5 ± 2.5 vs 8.4 ± 3.1" (p < 0.001).

> "In addition, the mean HFS group’s team skills score was significantly higher than mean IVC group’s score (11.5 ± 2.5 vs 8.4 ± 3.1; p < 0.001)"
