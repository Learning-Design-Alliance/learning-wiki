## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Frikha Mohamed et al 2024

Frikha Mohamed et al. (2024). Towards improving online learning in physical education: Gender differences and determinants of motivation, psychological needs satisfaction, and academic achievement in Saudi students. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0297822

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression on the same 308 students (Nagelkerke R2 = 0.553; Hosmer–Lemeshow p = 0.432). Exp(β) = 6.364 for prior experience, 1.501 autonomy, 1.339 competence, 1.324 intrinsic motivation; amotivation showed "a significant negative relationship" (Exp(β) = 0.731).

> "the odds of students having a high GPA (3 < GPA ≤ 5) and high prior experience with online learning are 6.364 times higher than those of the group having low GPA (1 < GPA ≤ 3) and low prior experience with online learning"
