# Closing knowledge gaps showed the highest association with performance among all considered variables (partial r = 0.17)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In a psychological network analysis, Knowledge gaps closed correlated with Performance at r = 0.17, the only partial correlation above .1 with Performance, and significantly stronger than all other variables' associations with Performance. [→ Spitzer 2025](#spitzer-2025)

## Evidence

### Spitzer 2025

Spitzer, M. W. H., Moeller, K., Richter, E., Frede, L., & Bardach, L. (2025). A red button does it all: A red button nudges students to close their knowledge gaps in an intelligent tutoring system. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102181. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102181

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Psychological network analysis (EBICglasso, bootstrapped 95% confidence intervals) of ten variables from five categories in the badge-on period. The partial correlation of r = 0.17 was "significantly different from zero" and from all other variables' associations with Performance.

> "Results indicated an association between Knowledge gaps closed and the Performance at a partial correlation of r = 0.17, indicating that closing knowledge gaps was associated with better student performance."
