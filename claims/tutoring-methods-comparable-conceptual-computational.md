Zhoumingju Jiang, Mengjun Jiang. (2024). Beyond Answers: LLM-Powered Tutoring System in Physics Education for Deep Learning and Precise Understanding. ACM, New York, NY, USA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.10934](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.10934)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Post-test between-group analysis in the three-group experiment. "all groups achieved perfect scores" on conceptual questions and computational scores were "consistent across groups"; the article reports no test statistics for these comparisons.

> "For Conceptual Questions, all groups achieved perfect scores. Completion times were similar: Teacher lecture (M = 49.5s), General LLM tutoring(M = 50.25s), and Physics -STAR LLM tutoring (M = 53s). For Computational Questions, scores were consistent across groups, with 75% of students achieving full marks."
