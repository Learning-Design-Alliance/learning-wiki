Effects of In-Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Children’s Academic Performance, Cognition, Health Behaviours and Health Outcomes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials. q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Random-effects meta-analysis of three samples (n = 617 students) from two RCTs for reading achievement. The pooled SMD = −0.07 was not significant (p = 0.43) with low heterogeneity (I2 = 11%), and the result held after sensitivity analysis.

"Meta-analysis for reading (based on three samples from two studies [2,22]; n = 617 students) indicated a trivial to small pooled effect size that was not significant favoring the control group before (SMD = −0.07, 95% CI [−0.25 to 0.11], p = 0.43; I2 = 11%, p = 0.33"
