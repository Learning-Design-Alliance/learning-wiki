Evidence¶

Costantini 2025¶

Costantini, A., Bakker, A. B., & Scharp, Y. S. (2025). Playful Study Design: A Novel Approach to Enhancing Student Well-Being and Academic Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10022-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory factor analysis in the CFA framework on the first half of the Study 1 sample (N = 378 Italian psychology students) tested one- to four-factor solutions. The two-factor solution was retained, with loadings "ranged from 0.40 to 0.82" for designing fun and 0.47 to 0.77 for designing competition.

"the two-factor solution showed no such issues, with all factor loadings exceeding 0.35. Loadings for designing fun ranged from 0.40 to 0.82, and loadings for designing competition ranged from 0.47 to 0.77."

Costantini 2025 (2)¶

Costantini, A., Bakker, A. B., & Scharp, Y. S. (2025). Playful Study Design: A Novel Approach to Enhancing Student Well-Being and Academic Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10022-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Confirmatory factor analysis on the second half of the Study 1 sample (N = 375) tested the two-factor structure after adding three residual covariances. The fitted model showed CFI = 0.94 and "outperformed an alternative single-factor model"; reliabilities were ω = 0.89 (fun) and ω = 0.81 (competition).

"Also, this two-factor model outperformed an alternative single-factor model (χ2 = 717.11; df = 54; CFI = 0.69; TLI = 0.62; RMSEA = 0.18; SRMR = 0.13)."
