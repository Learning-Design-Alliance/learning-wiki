RM-ANOVA on team-level Communication Similarity Scores in the same 29-team cluster-randomized study. The time effect was significant, F(1,27) = 11.25, p = 0.002, partial η² = 0.29; the training group effect was not (F = 0.06, p = 0.81). Means rose from about 80.4–80.8% to 82.4–82.6% (Table 2).
