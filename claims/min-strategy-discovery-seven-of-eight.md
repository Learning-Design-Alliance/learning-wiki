## Evidence

### Siegler 1991

Robert S. Siegler and Kevin Crowley. (1991). The Microgenetic Method: A Direct Means for Studying Cognitive Development. American Psychologist, Vol. 46, No. 6, 606-620. https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.46.6.606

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Microgenetic experiment in which ten selected four- and five-year-olds practiced addition about three sessions per week for 11 weeks, with strategy use assessed on every trial via videotape and immediate retrospective reports. Seven of eight completing children discovered the min strategy; discovery times "varied greatly", from the 8th trial to the 209th of 210 trials.

> "The time that they required varied greatly. The first discovery occurred in the 2nd session of the exper- iment, on the particular child's 8th trial. The last discovery occurred in the 30th session, on the 209th of the 210 trials that that child encountered."
