## Evidence

### Parchment Amelia 2025

Parchment Amelia, Lawrence Wendy, Rahman Em, Townsend Nick, Wainwright Elaine, Wainwright David. (2025). Increasing confidence and competence in supporting behaviour change in physiotherapy practice using Making Every Contact Count Healthy Conversation Skills: a before and after evaluation. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12513-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon tests on usefulness ratings. Pre-to-post training showed a significant increase (Z = −2.069, p = 0.039); at 6-month follow-up (n = 11) the pre-to-follow-up increase was not significant. Descriptively, 0% scored 3 or below at follow-up versus 27.3% at pre-training, and 81.9% scored 7 or above versus 45.5%.

> "an increase in perceived usefulness was also observed pre- training (median (IQR) = 6 (3–10)) to follow-up (median (IQR) = 8 (7–9)), but this change was not significant (Z = −1.488, p = 0.137)."
