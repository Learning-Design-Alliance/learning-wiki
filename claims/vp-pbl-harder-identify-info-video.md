## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported missing key information in video, which confused groups and reduced perceived discussion quality. [→ Luke A Woodham 2015](#luke-a-woodham-2015)

## Evidence

### Luke A Woodham 2015

Luke A Woodham, Rachel H Ellaway, Jonathan Round, Sophie Vaughan, Terry Poulton, Nabil Zary. (2015). Medical Student and Tutor Perceptions of Video Versus Text in an Interactive Online Virtual Patient for Problem-Based Learning: A Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3922

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-text survey responses in the pilot; the theme "Hard to identify key information in video" drew 68 code-quotations, and some participants felt they "learned more and had more information available to discuss with text in front of me".

> "Students found that it was harder to identify relevant information in the video compared to text. Many of the students identified that they missed key bits of information in the video and that this confused the group."
