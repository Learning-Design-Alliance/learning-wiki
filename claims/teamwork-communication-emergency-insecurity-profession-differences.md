## Evidence

### Lehmann Ronny 2019

Lehmann Ronny, Seitz Anke, Meyburg Jochen, Hoppe Bettina, Hoffmann Georg Friedrich, Tönshoff Burkhard, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Pediatric in-hospital emergencies: real life experiences, previous training and the need for training among physicians and nurses. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-019-4051-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Content analysis of free-text answers on what went well and what could improve in past emergencies. Physicians' top improvement item was "Own lack of preparedness (39.5%)"; nurses' top insecurity item was "Situations with lack of support by physicians (27.0%)".

> "Physicians reported more frequently than nurses that their own emergency preparedness caused feelings of insecurity. In contrast, nurses mentioned aspects of teamwork as the most frequent cause of feeling insecure."
