Evidence¶

Building Decision Skills 2006¶

Building Decision Skills. (2006). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.globalethics.org/services/edu/bds.htm

q2 · i? · causal · r?

WWC synthesis of one quasi-experimental study (Leming, 2001) comparing 283 twelfth-graders in an elective BDS-plus-service-learning course with senior English literature classes. The domain average effect size was 0.36 and the improvement index "+14 percentile points, with a range of +3 to +30 percentile points across findings."

"When the WWC aggregated all seven outcomes the average effect size was substantively important (greater than 0.25). So the WWC rated the program as having potentially positive effects in the knowledge, attitudes, and values domain."
