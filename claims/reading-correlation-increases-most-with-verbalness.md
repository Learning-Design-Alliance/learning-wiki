## Evidence

### Arthur I. Gates 1923

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The correlations of achievement in school subjects with intelligence tests and other variables (Continued). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1923-02675-001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational results from Tables III and IV for grades IV-VI and VII-VIII show reading correlations rising from 0.32 to 0.76 (IV-VI) and 0.03 to 0.67 (VII-VIII) across verbalness steps; the author states reading "shows the greatest increase in correlation as the material becomes more verbal."

> "For both groups, the higher the material on the verbal scale, the higher the correlation with spelling. The same is true of reading, which shows the greatest increase in correlation as the material becomes more verbal."
