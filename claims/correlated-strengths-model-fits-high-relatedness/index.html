Evidence¶

Gupta 2026¶

Gupta, M.W., Pan, S.C., & Rickard, T.C. (2026). Semantic relatedness and the efficacy of retrieval practice. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00416-8

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Simulation extension of the dual-memory model adding one free parameter ρ representing study–test memory strength correlation. The article reports "The best fit to the mean data was obtained for ρ = .334" with a good distribution fit shown in Figure 5b; no effect size is printed.

"The best fit to the mean data was obtained for ρ = .334, for other ρ fits, see Supplementary Figure 2. And as shown in Figure 5b, that value of ρ yielded a good fit across the bulk of the distribution for high relatedness items."
