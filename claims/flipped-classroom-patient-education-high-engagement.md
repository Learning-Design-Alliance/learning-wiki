## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a pilot of 26 radiation oncology patients, 77.1% of timed education emails were opened, 72.2% of opened emails led to the education material being accessed, and patients re-read the material 4.6 times on average. [→ Schuller BW 2022](#schuller-bw-2022)

## Evidence

### Schuller BW 2022

Schuller BW, Burch C, Casterton T, Crowther C, Fowler J, Stenmark MH. (2022). Precision patient education using a “flipped classroom” approach. J Appl Clin Med Phys. https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13601

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational engagement tracking via CRM software in a single-cohort pilot of 26 enrolled patients (9 breast, 17 prostate). The article reports "77.1% (54/70)" of emails opened and "72.2% (39/54)" of education information opened; material was re-read 4.6 times on average (Table 1).

> "Overall, 77.1% (54/70) of the emails were opened, and of those, 72.2% (39/54) of the education information was opened. Patients accessed the information more than four times on average"
