Barak Rosenshine's tribute states that Brophy's program coded teacher classroom practices and correlated them with achievement gain measures; the correlational studies spanned elementary and junior high grades and both reading and math achievement. The review reports this as historical account, not new data.

> "Between 1969 and 1983, Jere Brophy and his colleagues conducted more process-outcome studies than any other researcher. Their first studies coded a variety of teacher classroom practices and then correlated these practices with measures of student achievement gain."
