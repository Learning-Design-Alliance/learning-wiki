## Evidence

### Akizuki Kazunori 2019

Akizuki Kazunori, Yamamoto Ryohei, Yamaguchi Kazuto, Yabuki Jun, Ohashi Yukari. (2019). The effects of feedback timing and frequency on the acquisition of cardiopulmonary resuscitation skills of health sciences undergraduate students: A 2 x 2 factorial quasi randomized study. PLoS ONE 14(7): e0220004. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0220004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome of the 2x2 quasi-randomized study: one-way ANOVA on overall score at the 3-month follow-up test across the four feedback groups (68 undergraduates). The article reports "no statistically significant difference" (p = 0.173); no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "We did not find any statistically significant difference for the overall score at the follow-up test among the four groups (p = 0.173)."
