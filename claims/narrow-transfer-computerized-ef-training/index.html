Diamond Adele 2012¶

Diamond Adele. (2012). Activities and Programs That Improve Children's Executive Functions. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721412453722

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis of computer-based training studies; the review reports that working-memory training "does not improve inhibition" and only probably not reasoning, citing Klingberg et al. (2005) as a possible exception.

"In children, training on working memory improves performance on untrained working memory tasks, but it does not improve inhibition (Thorell et al., 2009) and probably does not improve reasoning or problem solving (Bergman Nutley et al., 2011; Thorell et al., 2009; but see Klingberg et al., 2005)."
