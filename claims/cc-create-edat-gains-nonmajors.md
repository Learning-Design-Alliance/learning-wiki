## Evidence

### Kenyon Kristy L. 2016

Kenyon Kristy L., Onorato Morgan E., Gottesman Alan J., Hoque Jamila, Hoskins Sally G. (2016). Testing CREATE at Community Colleges: An Examination of Faculty Perspectives and Diverse Student Gains. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0146

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pre/post EDAT administered in seven 2-yr CREATE courses, scored blind by two independent rubric scorers with concordance Pearson's r > 0.8. Pooled gains had "an ES of 0.29 and 0.34 for the pool and the Bio pool, respectively."

> "We found significant gains on the EDAT, with an ES of 0.29 and 0.34 for the pool and the Bio pool, respectively"

### Kenyon Kristy L. 2016 (2)

Kenyon Kristy L., Onorato Morgan E., Gottesman Alan J., Hoque Jamila, Hoskins Sally G. (2016). Testing CREATE at Community Colleges: An Examination of Faculty Perspectives and Diverse Student Gains. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0146

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Majors/nonmajors comparison within the pooled EDAT data (Table 6 footnote). The article prints "ES = 0.54" for nonmajors and reports nonmajors gained a full point (pre 3.4, post 4.5) while majors did not change significantly (pre 3.6, post 3.4).

> "Significant gains were seen in the pooled group (ES 0.29) as well as in the Bio pool (ES 0.34). Notably, nonmajors but not majors also made significant gains, with ES = 0.54."
