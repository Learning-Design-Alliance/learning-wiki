## Evidence

### Herodotou 2019

Herodotou, C., Rienties, B., Boroowa, A., Zdrahal, Z., & Hlosta, M. (2019). A large-scale implementation of predictive learning analytics in higher education: the teachers' role and perspective. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 1273–1306. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09685-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression on pass rates for 1325 students found OUA weekly usage significant (p = .001) alongside best previous course score. The article reports students are "7.5 times more likely to pass the course" per unit usage increase; Nagelkerke's R2 was .177.

> "Odds ratio value indicated that when OUA usage is raised by one unit, the odds ratio is 7.5 times as large and therefore students are 7.5 times more likely to pass the course"
