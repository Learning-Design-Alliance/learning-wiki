Mike Verostek, Molly Griston, Jes´us Botello, Benjamin Zwickl. (2022). Making expert processes visible: how and why theorists use assumptions and analogies in their research. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 11 theoretical physicists using cognitive task analysis methods; the authors coded 128 instances of "assumptions" across 11 interviews and found "making and evaluating the assumptions in their model is an ongoing process."

> "Our analysis reveals that theorists make and reﬁne their assumptions throughout a project, frequently considering how altering their assumptions might impact their work."
