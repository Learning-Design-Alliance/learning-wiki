## Evidence

### Wong Ion Hong 2026

Wong Ion Hong, Gao Xiaoyu, Tan Zhengyi, Fang Yiping, Wang Qun, Lei Wai In, Van Iat Kio, Tan Meijing, Liu Ning, Wu Jianwei. (2026). The impact of experiential learning on healthcare students’ attitudes towards dementia: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-026-07573-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Supplementary sensitivity meta-analysis adding two studies using alternative validated attitude instruments (AADS and ADQ), with standardized mean differences computed for comparability. Leave-one-out analysis showed pooled WMDs from 8.63 to 9.93 across 13 re-analyses.

> "The pooled effect (SMD = 0.68, 95% CI [0.43–0.93], p < 0.001) remained significant and directionally consistent with the primary analysis based on DAS studies (WMD = 9.46, 95% CI [6.33–12.58], p < 0.001)"

### Wong Ion Hong 2026 (2)

Wong Ion Hong, Gao Xiaoyu, Tan Zhengyi, Fang Yiping, Wang Qun, Lei Wai In, Van Iat Kio, Tan Meijing, Liu Ning, Wu Jianwei. (2026). The impact of experiential learning on healthcare students’ attitudes towards dementia: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-026-07573-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Risk-of-bias assessment using ROBINS-I across seven domains, with inter-rater kappa of 0.779. The review reports most studies lacked statistical control for confounders, and Egger's test (p = 0.013) with funnel-plot asymmetry suggested potential publication bias.

> "According to the ROBINS-I assessment, 10 of the 13 included studies were rated as having a serious overall risk of bias, while the remaining three were rated as having moderate risk (Fig. 2)."
