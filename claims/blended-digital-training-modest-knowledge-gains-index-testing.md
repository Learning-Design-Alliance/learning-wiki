## Evidence

### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. (2026). Digital Training for Lay Health Care Workers’ Knowledge and Skills in HIV Index Case Testing: Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/89942

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial of 306 lay health care workers in 33 Malawi facilities, analyzed with generalized estimating equations. Knowledge improved from 63.3% to 70.4% in the enhanced arm versus 63.4% to 66.2% in the standard arm; the between-arm difference was "4.4% (95% CI 0.7%-8.2%) percentage points higher in the enhanced arm (P=.02)".

> "Knowledge improved from 63.3% pretraining to 70.4% posttraining in the enhanced arm. It was relatively consistent in the standard arm: 63.4% to 66.2%. The between-arm difference was 4.4% (95% CI 0.7%-8.2%) percentage points higher in the enhanced arm (P=.02)."

### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026 (2)

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. (2026). Digital Training for Lay Health Care Workers’ Knowledge and Skills in HIV Index Case Testing: Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/89942

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Same cluster randomized controlled trial, secondary knowledge questionnaire. Correct answers for the Tingathe examination increased from 68.2% to 72.8% in the enhanced arm versus no improvement in the standard arm (68.9% to 69.4%); the between-arm difference was "4.0% (95% CI 1.4%-6.7%; P=.002)".

> "Correct answers for the Tingathe program’s index case testing examination increased from 68.2% pretraining to 72.8% in posttraining in the enhanced arm compared to no improvement in the standard arm (68.9% pretraining and 69.4% posttraining; Table 3). The between-arm difference in scores was 4.0% (95% CI 1.4%-6.7%; P=.002)."
