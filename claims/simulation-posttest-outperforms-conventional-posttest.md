## Evidence

### Kızılırmak Burcu 2025

Kızılırmak Burcu, Gözen Mehmet, Güntekin Neslihan. (2025). Evaluation of dental students’ tooth preparation skills using conventional phantom labs versus advanced simulation technologies: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08020-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Tukey comparisons in the randomized trial (n = 20 per group). Table 3 prints the C-Post vs S-Post contrast with mean difference -0.8698, t = -6.191, ptukey <.001, and Cohen's d = -0.8756, supporting that the "simulation group’s post-test performance was significantly higher".

> "Post hoc analysis results further revealed that the simulation group’s post-test performance was significantly higher than both its own pre-test scores and the conventional group’s post-test scores (p <.001)."
