### Luo Mingwei 2024

Luo Mingwei, Pang Jie, Xie Shiwei, Xu Huamin, Yan Jing. (2024). A study of the correlation between residents’ humanistic care skills and their level of emotional intelligence—A cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06097-4

Correlational analysis in the same 236-resident cross-sectional survey. The discussion reports the courage dimension's negative correlation with emotional intelligence, "a correlation coefficient of -0.248" (r = -0.248), while cognition and patience correlated positively (0.838 and 0.699).

> "Courage was negatively correlated with emotional intelligence, with a correlation coefficient of -0.248 (P < 0.00)."
