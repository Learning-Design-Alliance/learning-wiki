# In a single-group pre-test/post-test study with no comparison group, increases in managers' integrity coincided with increases in their subordinates' work engagement in multilevel analyses

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a single-group pre-test/post-test study with no comparison group, increases in managers' integrity coincided with increases in subordinate work engagement in multilevel linear regression (γ = 0.05, P = .027 adjusted), while the other five improved sub-competencies were not significantly associated. [→ Adachi H 2020](#adachi-h-2020)

## Evidence

### Adachi H 2020

Adachi H, Sekiya Y, Imamura K, Watanabe K, Kawakami N. (2020). The effects of training managers on management competencies to improve their management practices and work engagement of their subordinates: A single group pre‐ and post‐test study. J Occup Health. https://doi.org/10.1002/1348-9585.12085

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multilevel linear regression with workers (level 1) nested within managers (level 2), analyzing the six sub-competencies that improved significantly. Only integrity remained significant after adjusting for manager and worker gender and age (γ = 0.05, P = .027).

> "In the crude conditional model (Model 2), improvement in the managers’ integrity had a significant positive association with the change in work engagement of subordinate workers (γ = 0.05, P = .030). This significant relationship was also confirmed after adjusting for the covariates (γ = 0.05, P = .027, in Model 3)."
