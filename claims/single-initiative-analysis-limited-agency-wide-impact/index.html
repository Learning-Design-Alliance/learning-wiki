Cassie Pickens Jewell 2014¶

Cassie Pickens Jewell, Libby Makowsky, Kristin Hallgren, Jacob Hartog. (2014). Changing Education Agencies from the Inside Out: Year 2 Report on the Strategic Data Project and Education Pioneers. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/changing-education-agencies-from-the-inside-out-year-2-report-on-the-strategic-data-project

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Key finding from the implementation study's interviews with agency staff and fellows. The report attributes the limited agency-wide impact to the initiative-specific nature of the analysis, per informant reports. Descriptive; no effect sizes.

"Although agencies analyze data for single initiatives, this kind of measurement and analysis has not always impacted agency-wide policies and practices, according to the staff and fellows from whom we gathered information."

Cassie Pickens Jewell 2014 (2)¶

Cassie Pickens Jewell, Libby Makowsky, Kristin Hallgren, Jacob Hartog. (2014). Changing Education Agencies from the Inside Out: Year 2 Report on the Strategic Data Project and Education Pioneers. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/changing-education-agencies-from-the-inside-out-year-2-report-on-the-strategic-data-project

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Authors' interpretation from the case-study interviews, hedged with "It appears likely": staff other than fellows may lack skills in question formulation, data selection, and interpretation for policy and practice. No effect size is reported.

"It appears likely that many agency staff (other than fellows) do not know what questions to ask, what data to use, or most importantly, how to interpret findings and draw inferences to improve policy and practice."
