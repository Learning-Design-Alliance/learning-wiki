## Evidence

### Liu 2026

Liu, X., Chen, Y., Guan, W., Jiang, P., Yan, L., Fan, M., & Zhou, Q. (2026). The Mediation of AI Trust on AI Uncertainties and AI Competence Among Nurses: A Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70250

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of 550 nurses found AIU and AIT correlated at r = −0.31 (p < 0.001). The article describes this as "a strong positive correlation between AIU and AIT" despite the negative coefficient, consistent with prior findings (Salimzadeh et al. 2024; Li et al. 2024).

> "The findings showed that there was a strong positive correlation between AIU and AIT (r = −0.31, p < 0.001), AIT and AIC (r = 0.768, p < 0.001), AIU and AIC (r = 0.432, p < 0.001)"
