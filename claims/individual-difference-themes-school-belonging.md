### Allen 2018

Allen, K., Kern, M.L., Vella-Brodrick, D., Hattie, J., & Waters, L. (2018). What Schools Need to Know About Fostering School Belonging: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 30, 1–34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-016-9389-8

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review phase identifying the ten themes, including the individual-difference themes "academic motivation, emotional stability, personal characteristics" and "gender, race and ethnicity". These were then meta-analytically examined across 51 studies (N = 67,378).

> "First, a systematic review identified 10 themes that influence school belonging at the student level during adolescence in educational settings (academic motivation, emotional stability, personal characteristics, parent support, peer support, teacher support, gender, race and ethnicity, extracurricular activities and environmental/school safety)."
