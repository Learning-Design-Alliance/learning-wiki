The brief reports, citing Herring et al. (2022), Virginia evidence that EL-labeled children with low kindergarten literacy "were 20 percent less likely to achieve reading proficiency by grade 3" than peers with similar initial skills. The comparison is as the brief attributes it.

"In Virginia, for example, Hispanic/Latino children labeled as English Learners who began kindergarten with low literacy scores and received interventions were 20 percent less likely to achieve reading proficiency by grade 3 compared with their non-Hispanic/Latino white and socioeconomically advantaged peers with similar initial skills (Herring et al., 2022)."
