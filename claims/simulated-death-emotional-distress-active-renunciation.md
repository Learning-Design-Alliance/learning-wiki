## Evidence

### Hinzmann Dominik 2023

Hinzmann Dominik, Wijnen-Meijer Marjo, Corazza Laura, Becker Veronika, Kagerbauer Simone, Haseneder Rainer, Berberat Pascal O., Jedlicska Nana. (2023). Experiences of medical students and nursing trainees from unexpected death through simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04638-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group interviews with nine medical students and six nursing trainees one week after a death simulation, analysed with Grounded Theory coding techniques. The authors report "varying degrees of emotional distress" and attribute intensity to the novelty of the experience; no effect sizes are printed.

> "We could observe that simulated death caused varying degrees of emotional distress in the participants. The intensity of the consternation was attributed to the novelty of the experience."
