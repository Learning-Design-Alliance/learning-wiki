Comparison study the author reports, citing his own PERC Proceedings (2012) work: one class had 5 min of open play with a sim, another none; the teacher had "significantly more difficulty leading a student -centered, inquiry activity" without open play. No effect size printed.

> "One study compared a class where students had 5 min of open play time with a sim to a class without open play time, and found that the teacher had significantly more difficulty leading a student -centered, inquiry activity when no open play time was allowed."
