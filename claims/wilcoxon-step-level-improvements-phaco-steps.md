# Grades for eight of 16 phacoemulsification surgical steps rose significantly from pre-course to post-course in one group with no comparison group, with the greatest rises in IOL insertion, irrigation/aspiration, and capsulorhexis completion

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Half of the 16 OSSCAR surgical steps showed statistically significant pre- to post-course improvement on two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. [→ Geary Amelia 2021](#geary-amelia-2021)
`q2 i3` The steps with the greatest improvement were lens insertion, rotation and final position of IOL, irrigation and aspiration technique, and capsulorhexis formation and circular completion, with effect sizes above 0.7. [→ Geary Amelia 2021 (2)](#geary-amelia-2021-2)

### Geary Amelia 2021 (2)
