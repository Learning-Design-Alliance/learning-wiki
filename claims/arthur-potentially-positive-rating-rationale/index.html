Arthur received the rating of potentially positive effects: at least one study showed a substantively important positive effect and no studies showed statistically significant or substantively important negative effects, but the criteria for positive effects were not met because there was only one study and it lacked a strong design. → Arthur (WWC Intervention Report) 2006

Arthur (WWC Intervention Report) 2006¶

Arthur (WWC Intervention Report). (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://pbskids.org/arthur/index.html

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The WWC intervention rating scheme requires two or more studies showing statistically significant positive effects, at least one with a strong design, for a positive effects rating. For Arthur, "the study lacked a strong design" despite a substantively important average effect size, so the highest applicable rating was potentially positive effects.

"It did not meet the criteria for positive effects, because it only had one study. The remaining ratings (mixed effects, no discernible effects, potentially negative effects, and negative effects) were not consid-ered because Arthur was assigned the highest applicable rating."
