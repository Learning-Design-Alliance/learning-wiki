### Wuryani 2018

Wuryani, M. T., Roemintoyo, & Yamtinah, S. (2018). Textbooks thematic based character education on thematic learning primary school: An influence. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(2), 75-81. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.2.75

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment using a "Matching Only Pretest-Posttest Control Group Design" with 30 experimental and 31 control fourth-graders; independent-samples t-test after normality (Kolmogorov-Smirnov) and homogeneity (Levene) checks. The article reports the "significance result 0.00 0 < 0.05"; no effect size is printed.
