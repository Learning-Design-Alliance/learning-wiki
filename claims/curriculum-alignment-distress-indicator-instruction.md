### National Charter School Resource Center 2021

National Charter School Resource Center, Evan, A., & Sullivan, H. (2021). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools, Part 2: The Roles and Perspectives of Charter School Leaders and Board Members. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616211

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Instruction-domain interview evidence. The report describes schools lacking "a lack of unified curriculum entirely, where teachers identified their own sources of curriculum," producing classroom-to-classroom inconsistency, plus absent instructional supports and student support systems.

> "Schools without a strategy for curriculum alignment struggled with providing students the tools needed to meet proficiency on state assessments and the foundational knowledge to succeed in subsequent grade levels."
