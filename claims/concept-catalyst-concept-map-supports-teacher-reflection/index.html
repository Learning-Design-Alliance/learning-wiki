Evidence¶

Gennie Mansi 2026¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · design · r2

Reflection findings from the WoZ study: before writing content, most teachers revised the concept map to synthesize thoughts and organize learning goals, and several used it to plan topics for lessons within and across classes depending on student interests.

"P2, P6, P7, P9 and P10 all continually revised the concept map, describing it as a “mind map” (P9) that helped them “outline what (they) want” (P2) students to address by helping them visualize and iterate on how the concepts connected."

Gennie Mansi 2026 (2)¶

Gennie Mansi, Sunni Newton, Roxanne Moore, Meltem Alemdar, Mark Riedl. (2026). Concept Catalyst: Exploring Scrutable Interfaces to Structure K-12 Teacher Interactions with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30590

q2 · i? · design · r2

In the same study, teachers who were initially unsure what to add used the highlighted sample paragraph as a starting point; one teacher added construction cost under materials by recalling how students choose materials in class.

"Several teachers were also unsure of what to add to the concept map (P1, P2, P5-P9), but reading the sample paragraph helped them remember their teaching experiences."
