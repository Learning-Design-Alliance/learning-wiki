# Adding a hardware podcast and tutorial reduced the time students needed for the VR simulation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average simulation time fell from 17:03 min in the 2021 pilot to 12:53 min in the 2024 run after adding a hardware podcast and tutorial. [→ Junga A 2025](#junga-a-2025)

## Evidence

### Junga A 2025

Junga A, Kockwelp P, Valkov D, Schulze H, Bozdere P, Hätscher O, Ahrens H, Marschall B, Risse B, Holling M. (2025). Teach the Unteachable with a Virtual Reality (VR) Brain Death Scenario – 800 Students and 3 Years of Experience. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1427

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Comparison of examination time between the 2021 pilot (VR tutorial only) and the January 2024 run (VR tutorial plus hardware and medical podcast), using an independent t-test to measure differences in examination time. Means were "Mean2021 = 17:03 min" and Mean2024 = 12:53 min.

> "A comparison of the pilot (January 2021; available: VR Tutorial, no hardware podcast) with the last run of the course (January 2024; available: VR tutorial + hardware and medical podcast) showed a considerable reduction in the time required for the simulation from an average of Mean2021 = 17:03 min in 2021 to Mean2024 = 12:53 min in 2024."
