Wei Dai and Cecilia Ka Yuk Chan. (2026). Shaping responsible GenAI use in research through AI literacy-oriented guidelines: insights from postgraduate students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00609-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of seven focus groups with 28 PGR students at the University of Hong Kong. The results section reports that "Nearly all participants (27 out of 28) reported using GenAI across multiple stages of the research process", with GenAI framed as a supportive assistant and human oversight retained.

> "Nearly all participants (27 out of 28) reported using GenAI across multiple stages of the research process. Overall, GenAI was primarily positioned as a supportive and efficiency-enhancing research assistant rather than a substitute for core intellectual work, with human oversight consistently retained."
