### Amy Voss Farris 2016

Amy Voss Farris, Amanda Catherine Dickes & Pratim Sengupta. (2016). Development of Disciplined Interpretation Using Computational Modeling in the Elementary Science Classroom. Proceedings of the 12th International Conference of the Learning Sciences (ICLS 2016). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06963)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Authors' account of the teacher-created culture of sharing and critiquing peer models. Criteria for good models originated in teacher-led, popularly voted class discussions and over time became "progressively more grounded in students' mathematical explanations" of their ViMAP simulations.

> "This led students to use progressively more sophisticated computational abstractions, such as loops and conditionals, in order to make their models predictive (Sengupta et al., 2015)."
