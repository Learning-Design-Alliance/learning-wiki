## Evidence

### Philipp Bitzenbauer 2023

Philipp Bitzenbauer, Sarah Navarrete, Fabian Hennig, Malte S. Ubben, and Joaquin M. Veith. (2023). A cross-age study on secondary school students' views of stars. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020165](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020165)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Cross-age ANOVA on domain 3 (properties of stars) responses; cohort means are 55.7%, 64.2%, and 75.3% scientifically accurate answers, described as "a continuous improvement in scientifically accurate views held by students."

> "The cohorts differ highly statistically significantly [ F (2, 363) = 24.0, p <0.01; η2p = 0 .12] and the same is true for all pairwise cohort comparisons ( p <0.01 each). The corresponding effect sizes are all high with d = 0.44 between Cohort 1 and 2, d = 0.57 between Cohort 2 and 3 as well as d = 1.01 between Cohort 1 and 3."
