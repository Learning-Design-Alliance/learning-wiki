## Evidence

### Quansah Frank 2025

Quansah Frank, Essel Daniel William, Gyapong Nancy Asieduwaa, Yabana Isaac N‐Nandi, Dzakadzie Yayra, Asamoah Patrick, Addison Kingsley. (2025). Enhancing Teacher Training Through Self‐Efficacy and Emotional Intelligence: A Conditional Process Model of Pre‐Service Teachers' Well‐Being and Academic Achievements. Brain and Behavior, 15(5), e70578. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.70578

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped conditional process analysis (10,000 samples) of the 660-participant survey: "the indirect effect of the mediating variable ASE in the association between PWB and CGPA for pre‐service teachers with low, moderate, or high EI was significant and stronger at each EI level."

> "Moreover, the indirect effect of the mediating variable ASE in the association between PWB and CGPA for pre‐service teachers with low, moderate, or high EI was significant and stronger at each EI level."

### Quansah Frank 2025 (2)

Quansah Frank, Essel Daniel William, Gyapong Nancy Asieduwaa, Yabana Isaac N‐Nandi, Dzakadzie Yayra, Asamoah Patrick, Addison Kingsley. (2025). Enhancing Teacher Training Through Self‐Efficacy and Emotional Intelligence: A Conditional Process Model of Pre‐Service Teachers' Well‐Being and Academic Achievements. Brain and Behavior, 15(5), e70578. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.70578

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same analysis; the index of moderated mediation was "not significant at a 95 percent confidence level" (B = 0.03, CI [‐0.01, 0.08]), indicating the mediated effect does not differ significantly across EI levels.

> "On the contrary, the index of moderated mediation (B = 0.03, CI [‐0.01, 0.08]) was not significant at a 95 percent confidence level."
