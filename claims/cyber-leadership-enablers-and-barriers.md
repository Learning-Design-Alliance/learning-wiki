## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Leaders identified supports including being listened to and trusted by peers, connection with teachers, recognition and authority from the school, and staff and parent support; barriers included lack of confidence, lack of time, peer pressure and lack of staff and parent support. [→ Cross et al. 2015](#cross-et-al-2015)
