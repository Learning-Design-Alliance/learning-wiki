`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of English oral reading scores by grade attainment and baseline school enrollment status across the four samples (Fig. 1). The article notes the out-of-school gap may reflect weaker skills at school leaving, deterioration after leaving, or pre-existing selection, and is not interpreted causally.

> "Overall, Fig. 1 demonstrates two important patterns in our data: (1) literacy levels are low overall and increase with grade attainment, particularly in the transition to secondary school; and (2) literacy levels are lower among those who have been out of school longer, even at comparable levels of grade attainment."
