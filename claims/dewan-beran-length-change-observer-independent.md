### D. J. Miller 2009

D. J. Miller. (2009). A constructive approach to the special theory of relativity. https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0902 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0907.0902](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0907.0902)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical analysis of the Dewan-Beran thought experiment (Sec. III A) applied to the charge apparatus: observers disagree on magnitudes and timing of the acceleration, yet agree on the contraction upon re-charging. The article concludes this shows the fractional length change "caused by the forces that maintain the equilibrium conﬁguration are real".

> "Despite their disagreement on the magnitude of the separation of the uncharged spheres and whether that separation increased or decreased as the result of the change of frames, as already mentioned, all observers agree that the spheres move together when the charges are replaced and that they move together by the same factor compared with their separation when uncharged."
