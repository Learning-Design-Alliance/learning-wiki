Section 4.1 uses Arizona CPS data (2009-2013) with ACS data (2006-2010) as a validity check, building lifetime earnings profiles adjusted for labor force participation, health benefits, ability, and productivity growth, discounted to present value at age 18.

> "high school dropouts are predicted to earn  $233,260. By contrast, high school graduates (adjusting for college enrollment rates) are predicted to earn $504,300 (net of ability/motivation). Relative to a dropout across Arizona, this amounts to a gain of $271,040 at age 18."
