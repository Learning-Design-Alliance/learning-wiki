## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Julia Gouvea, Benjamin D. Geller, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Chemical energy in an introductory physics course for the life sciences. [doi:10.1119/1.4870391](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4870391)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative classroom episode at the start of the fall 2012 energy unit: students spontaneously answered that energy is ATP, and one student said "In biology it's the chemical bonds which hold energy." The authors conclude instruction must engage with these incoming ideas.

> "teaching about energy to this student population, in a way that builds from their existing knowledge, must engage with chemical bonds and ATP, which are salient in these students’ incoming understandings of energy."
