Evidence¶

Vibeke Normann Andersen and Kasper M. Hansen 2007¶

Vibeke Normann Andersen and Kasper M. Hansen. (2007). How deliberation makes better citizens: The Danish Deliberative Poll on the euro. European Journal of Political Research, Volume 46, Issue 4, pp. 531-556. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-6765.2007.00699.x

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretive conclusion drawn from the Danish Deliberative Poll findings, including that "self-interest and domination also appeared during the deliberative process". This is the authors' argument rather than a tested result; the article offers it as a theoretical recommendation.

"The article emphasizes the need for further elaboration of the theory of deliberative democracy so that it better reflects these features of 'real-life' politics."
