Cross-sectional survey of 88 final-year dental students at a single Turkish dental faculty, analyzed descriptively. The results section reports that "Most students rated their self-perceived knowledge of vital pulp therapy (VPT) as moderate (60.2%)" and that theoretical courses led as the knowledge source.

> "Most students rated their self-perceived knowledge of vital pulp therapy (VPT) as moderate (60.2%). Theoretical courses were the most frequently reported main source of knowledge regarding VPT protocols (42.0%), followed by clinical practice (36.4%)."
