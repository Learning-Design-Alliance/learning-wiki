### Liu 2018

Liu, J., & Zhang, J. (2018). The Effects of Extensive Reading on English Vocabulary Learning: A Meta-analysis. English Language Teaching, 11(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n6p1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis of reading materials in the group contrasts (Graded Readers: k = 14, N = 975; story books: k = 5; Reading Power: k = 2). In pre-post contrasts only Graded Readers produced a huge effect (d = 3.26).

> "Graded Readers (d = 1.48) produces a very large effect, followed by story books (d = 0.62) and reading power (d = 0.50) both yielding medium effect."
