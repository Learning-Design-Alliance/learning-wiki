### Redish 2012

Redish, E. F. (2012). The role of context and culture in teaching physics: The implication of disciplinary differences. Keynote address, World Conference on Physics Education 2012, Istanbul. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1207.4840](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1207.4840)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

In Spring 2011 a graduate student helped translate and implement Tutorials in a class of 140 undergraduate pre-service teachers at Gakugei University, Tokyo, and "did extended interviews with 28 of these students". One student attributed easy adjustment to elementary-school experience of talking and solving problems together.

> "He found that students took to the Tutorials immediately, without the resistance observed in the US. There is evidence to suggest that many of the students were able to reach back to their elementary school experience and activate an epistemological frame that expected them to interact with each other and use and evaluate their own ideas."
