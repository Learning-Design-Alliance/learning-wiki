## Evidence

### Howe‐Heyman Abigail CNM 2025

Howe‐Heyman Abigail CNM, PhD; Schatz Joseph DNP, PMHNP; Siegel Marissa DeCesaris DNP, PMHNP. (2025). An Interprofessional Approach to Teaching About Postpartum Depression. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70056

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pretest–posttest study of midwifery, WHGRNP, and PMHNP students at one private university; paired t tests on 61 matched responses. The "mean (SD) change from pretest to posttest was 5.39 (6.96)" with P <.001 and Cohen's d = 0.775.

> "The mean (SD) change from pretest to posttest was 5.39 (6.96), which was statistically significant (P <.001; 95% CI, 3.61‐7.18) with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d = 0.775)."
