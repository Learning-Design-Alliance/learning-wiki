## Evidence

### Cristi-Montero 2022

Cristi-Montero, C., Solis-Urra, P., Sanchez-Martinez, J., et al. as printed in full byline. (2022). Which one? A comparative study of traditional and sports uniforms on academic achievement, cognitive performance, playtime, bullying, and discrimination in adolescents: The Cogni-Action Project. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.917970

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported yes/no question administered to the 988-adolescent cross-sectional sample; interviewers explained the question referred to a decrease in physical activity during the school day. The article reports 64.1% answered affirmatively with a significant proportion test. No effect size printed.

> "For the question, “Do you think that wearing a traditional uniform in school affects your physical activity?”, 64.1% of adolescents believe that the traditional uniform affects it (proportion test, p < 0.01)."
