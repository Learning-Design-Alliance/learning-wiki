Reed 2026¶

Reed, J. M., Ferdig, R. E., Dodson, T. M., Gunstad, J., & Hughes, J. W. (2026). AI Literacy and Scenario-based Ethical Judgment About Generative AI Among Undergraduate Students. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09757-w

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Omnibus academic-status effect from the RQ3 binomial model. The sophomore contrast (OR = 0.62, unadjusted p = .045) did not remain significant after Bonferroni adjustment (adjusted p = .135), so the authors interpret individual contrasts as exploratory.

"Academic status had a significant overall association with classification accuracy, Wald χ²(3) = 13.24, p = .004."

Reed 2026 (2)¶

Reed, J. M., Ferdig, R. E., Dodson, T. M., Gunstad, J., & Hughes, J. W. (2026). AI Literacy and Scenario-based Ethical Judgment About Generative AI Among Undergraduate Students. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09757-w

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Null sex effect from the same RQ3 binomial model; males had comparable modeled odds to females (OR = 0.88, 95% CI [0.64, 1.21]). No effect size was printed, so impact is null.

"Sex was not significantly associated with classification accuracy, Wald χ²(1) = 0.65, p = .420."
