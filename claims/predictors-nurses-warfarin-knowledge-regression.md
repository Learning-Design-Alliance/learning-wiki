## Evidence

### Subih Maha 2025

Subih Maha, Rababa Majdi, Aryan Fatima Sabri, Alnaeem Mohammad, AlRahahleh Mohammad H, Al niarat Tahany Fareed, Saleh Zyad T., Alsulami Ghaida Shujayyi, Almagharbeh Wesam T., Elshatarat Rami A. (2025). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge and competence in warfarin-drug and nutrient interactions and patient counseling practices. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07074-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on data from 176 nurses, run in SPSS version 26 with significance set at p < 0.05. The article reports the model as printed: R2 = 0.35, F = 31.62, p = 0.003.

> "The overall regression model was statistically significant, with a R2 value of 0.35, indicating that the model accounted for 35% of the variance in knowledge scores. Additionally, the F-test results were significant (F = 31.62, p = 0.003)"

### Subih Maha 2025 (2)

Subih Maha, Rababa Majdi, Aryan Fatima Sabri, Alnaeem Mohammad, AlRahahleh Mohammad H, Al niarat Tahany Fareed, Saleh Zyad T., Alsulami Ghaida Shujayyi, Almagharbeh Wesam T., Elshatarat Rami A. (2025). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge and competence in warfarin-drug and nutrient interactions and patient counseling practices. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07074-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multiple linear regression. The article prints coefficients for each significant predictor: postgraduate degree β = 0.204, p = 0.032; work experience β = 0.210, p = 0.016; direct care β = 0.181, p = 0.045; health education β = 0.252, p = 0.0252; anticoagulant training β = 0.149, p = 0.039.

> "High self-confidence in implementing warfarin-related care was a particularly strong predictor (β = 0.681, p = 0.015), reflecting that nurses who feel more assured in their abilities tend to possess greater knowledge"
