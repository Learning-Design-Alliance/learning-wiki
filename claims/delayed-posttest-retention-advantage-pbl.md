# At delayed posttest, PBL groups retained better overall and higher-order performance, and HPBL alone outperformed lecturing on lower-order performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Eight weeks after the experiment, COTL means for overall and higher-order performance and instructional efficiency were significantly lower than PPBL and HPBL, and COTL lower-order performance was significantly lower than HPBL (p = .028) but not significantly lower than PPBL. [→ Salari Mohsen 2018](#salari-mohsen-2018)

## Evidence

### Salari Mohsen 2018

Salari Mohsen, Roozbehi Amrollah, Zarifi Abdolvahed, Tarmizi Rohani Ahmad. (2018). Pure PBL, Hybrid PBL and Lecturing: which one is more effective in developing cognitive skills of undergraduate students in pediatric nursing course?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1305-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on delayed posttest administered 8 weeks after the experiment to the same three groups. Table 4 reports overall F(2, 92) = 32.82, higher-order F = 45.39, lower-order F = 7.62, mental effort F = 11.23, efficiency F = 20.94, all p = .001, with effect sizes of .42, .50, .14, .20, and .31 as printed.

> "Post-hoc comparisons indicated that the mean score of lower-order performance of the COTL group was significantly lower than that of the HPBL group. However, the PPBL group did not differ significantly from the HPBL and COTL groups on their lower-order performance."
