### Xu Hongxuan 2026

Xu Hongxuan, Möllerberg Marie-Louise, Dalhammar Karin, Bengtsson Mariette, Sjögren Forss Katarina. (2026). Nurse Managers' Awareness of and Competence in Palliative Care in Municipal Care Settings: A Systematic Mixed Studies Review. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70352

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Quantitative questionnaire study (Eriksson et al. 2015, Sweden) of staff in nursing homes, home care and group residential settings, including 43 managers; appraised as high quality by the review. Managers "lacked palliative care education", making it challenging to guide and support their teams.

> "Half (50%) of managers had attended intermediate palliative care workshops, and a third (33%) lacked education in palliative care."

### Xu Hongxuan 2026 (2)

Xu Hongxuan, Möllerberg Marie-Louise, Dalhammar Karin, Bengtsson Mariette, Sjögren Forss Katarina. (2026). Nurse Managers' Awareness of and Competence in Palliative Care in Municipal Care Settings: A Systematic Mixed Studies Review. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70352

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Quantitative questionnaire study (De Bellis and Parker 1998, Australia) of 147 Directors of Nursing in nursing homes; appraised as low quality by the review. Barriers reported included lack of resources (71%), inadequate education (47%), and professional conflicts (18%).

> "While 69% of DONs had attended palliative care training, only 16% held formal qualifications, and most felt that the current education resources for staff were inadequate."
