## Evidence

### Busmin Gurning & Aguslani Siregar 2017

Busmin Gurning & Aguslani Siregar. (2017). The Effect of Teaching Strategies and Curiosity on Students’ Achievement in Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 10(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n11p191

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on the same 76-student quasi-experiment; curiosity was measured with a twenty-item Likert-scale questionnaire and split into high and low groups. The article reports "The students’ achievement in Reading Comprehension with high curiosity got higher than those low curiosity" with F obs (6.92) > F tab (3.98).
