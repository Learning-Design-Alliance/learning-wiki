# Messages posted in CFDE score higher than messages posted in TDE on all seven contribution-quality dimensions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Messages in CFDE exceeded messages in TDE on knowledge validity (F=10.61), cognitive attempt (F=12.93), understanding the subject (F=5.18), sample-reference showing (F=6.31), being understandable (F=4.63), cooperation (F=6.23) and orientation (F=4.51), all significant. ## Evidence

### Yilmaz 2016

Yilmaz, E. O., & Yurdugul, H. (2016). Design and effects of a concept focused discussion environment in e-learning. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 353-374. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.20

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two field-expert observers rated every message on a 0-4 rubric across seven dimensions in the 90-student experiment; all seven ANOVAs favored CFDE (e.g. knowledge validity 2.00 vs 1.16, p=0.00). No effect sizes are printed.

> "the messages sent in CFDE compared to messages sent in TDE demonstrate knowledge validity, cognitive attempt efforts, understanding of the subject, and sample reference and are understandable, involve cooperation, and reveal orientation skills to a significant extent."
