## Evidence

### Clemens Megan 2025

Clemens Megan, Boparai Josheil, Glynn Robert, White Gerry, Curran Vernon, Prazeres Filipe. (2025). Evaluation study of effect of virtual care education on healthcare providers’ knowledge, confidence, and satisfaction. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.20414

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same single-group pre-post quasi-experimental study measured objective knowledge via multiple-choice questions before and after the module. The abstract reports "objective knowledge scores (n = 20–22; p = 0.046)" improved significantly.

> "Statistically significant improvements were observed in confidence levels (n = 20–21; p < 0.001 to 0.009) and objective knowledge scores (n = 20–22; p = 0.046)."
