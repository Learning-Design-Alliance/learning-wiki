`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 69 independent samples (196,473 participants) using Comprehensive Meta-Analysis software; Pearson r as effect size converted to Fisher's z. The article reports a pooled correlation of r = .269 for overall engagement, a significant effect.
