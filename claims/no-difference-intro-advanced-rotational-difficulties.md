Lorenzo G. Rimoldini and Chandralekha Singh. (2005). Student understanding of rotational and rolling motion concepts. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.1.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud demonstration-based interviews with ten introductory students and six physics juniors enrolled in an intermediate mechanics course. The authors report that "no clear diﬀerence emerged in the perfor-mance of students in the lower and upper level courses" and that the higher mathematical sophistication of the juniors appeared not to help them reason qualitatively.

> "A somewhat surprising and discouraging result of our interviews is th at no clear diﬀerence emerged in the perfor-mance of students in the lower and upper level courses"
