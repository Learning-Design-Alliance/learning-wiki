Evidence¶

Clinton 2016¶

Clinton, V., Morsanyi, K., Alibali, M. W., & Nathan, M. J. (2016). Learning about Probability from Text and Tables: Do Color Coding and Labeling through an Interactive-user Interface Help? Applied Cognitive Psychology, 30(3), 440-453. https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3223

q3 · i? · causal · r2

General linear model of total fixation duration on the second website page with color coding, labeling, pretest and need for cognition as predictors; the labeling effect was significant, p = .01, and color coding's was not.

"Consistent with hypotheses, labeling increased the total sum of fixation durations, b = 26.31, χ2(1, N = 98) = 7.84, t = 2.8, p = .01."
