Evidence¶

Bracco 2019¶

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview finding on fall 2018 placement practices across nine CSU campuses. The report notes Directed Self-Placement is used on many campuses for writing placement but is less common in mathematics.

"Many campuses also reported success using Directed Self‑Placement, particularly for WC, although some expressed concerns that multiple measures may lessen the impact of this approach."

Bracco 2019 (2)¶

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview finding on multiple measures implementation. The report also notes implementing multiple measures required strengthened coordination across academic affairs and student affairs, a collateral benefit of EO 1110.

"they identified some challenges with implementing multiple measures, most consistently pointing to the lack of the timely availability of high school transcripts to determine final student placement"
