`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled, single-blind trial in 282 children aged 3–5 across six private kindergartens in Istanbul, with 94 children per group. The abstract reports that "significant positive changes were observed" in behaviour in both trained groups.

> "Significant positive changes were observed in the oral and dental health-related behaviour of the children in the groups that received model- or video-based education."
