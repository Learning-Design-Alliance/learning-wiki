## Evidence

### Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage 2016

Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage. (2016). Developing an Action Concept Inventory. arXiv:1605.01786. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Statistical analysis of ACI post-test response data from the 2014 and 2015 Physics 2 classes found the KR-20 consistency statistic below the desired range; the article states this indicates the tested concepts have a "high degree of independence" with no single construct tested.

> "That it is below the desired range [23, 24] indicates that the concepts being tested, Table I, have a high degree of independence, and that there is no single idea, or construct, whose understanding is being tested by the ACI [25]."
