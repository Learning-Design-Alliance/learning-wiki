The paper reports, citing Wolf et al. (2008) survey data from the 2006-07 school year, that 34 of 48 states used multiple criteria for reclassification; 12 states used an ELP assessment only, 2 used only district-established criteria, and the rest combined the ELP test with one to five additional criteria including parent/guardian input.
