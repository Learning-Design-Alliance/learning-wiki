## Evidence

### Radkowitsch A 2020

Radkowitsch A, Fischer MR, Schmidmaier R, Fischer F. (2020). Learning to diagnose collaboratively: validating a simulation for medical students. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001344](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001344)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scoring analysis in the validation study: two raters independently coded 20% of the cases, and intraclass correlations based on a two-way random effects model with absolute agreement were computed. The article reports "ICC=1" for final diagnoses and further steps and "ICC=0.94" for differential diagnoses.

> "The interrater agreement for the quality of the final diagnoses and for the further indicated steps was ICC=1. For the differential diagnoses, the interrater agreement was ICC=0.94."
