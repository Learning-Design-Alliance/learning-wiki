### Pang Xuefei 2024

Pang Xuefei, Li Ling, Liu Xu, Wang Yan, Yang Bo. (2024). Application of Emerging Teaching Models in Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2024.05.016

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by teaching method (Figure 3B) in the meta-analysis of 29 RCTs. The article states these subgroups "are also superior to LBL, but the results are not significant"; no effect-size magnitude beyond the SMD point estimates is interpretable as significant.

> "CBL (SMD = 0.52, 95% CI = 0.18-0.86, P = .060) and combined methods (using more than 2 kinds of emerging teaching methods) (SMD = 0.54, 95% CI = 0.04-1.04, P = .077) are also superior to LBL, but the results are not significant"
