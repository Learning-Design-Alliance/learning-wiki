### Heskin L. 2021

Heskin L., Simms C., Traynor O., Galvin R. (2021). Designing a synthetic simulator to teach open surgical skills for limb exploration in trauma: a qualitative study exploring the experiences and perspectives of educators and surgical trainees. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01417-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the Discussion of the qualitative study: participants were very descriptive about expected material quality such as elasticity, turgor and malleability, yet prioritised performance, echoing literature that tissue should react and function like the real thing.

> "Functional fidelity was more important than visual realism but they did think that there needed to be enough visual ques to enable buy-in by more senior trainees."
