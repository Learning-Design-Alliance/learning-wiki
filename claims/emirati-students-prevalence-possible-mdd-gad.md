## Evidence

### Awadalla Suheir 2020

Awadalla Suheir, Davies E. Bethan, Glazebrook Cris. (2020). A longitudinal cohort study to explore the relationship between depression, anxiety and academic performance among Emirati university students. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-020-02854-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baseline screening of the representative cohort of 404 students at one UAE university using PHQ-9 and GAD-7 cut-offs of 10. The article reports "34.2%" screening for possible MDD and "22.3%" for possible GAD, with printed confidence intervals.

> "Over a third of students (34.2%; CIs 29.7–38.9%) screened for possible major depressive disorder (MDD; PHQ-9 ≥ 10) but less than a quarter (22.3%; CIs 18.2–26.3%) screened for possible generalized anxiety disorder (GAD; GAD-7 ≥ 10)."
