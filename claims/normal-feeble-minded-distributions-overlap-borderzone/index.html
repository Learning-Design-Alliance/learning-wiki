Annotation of Kohs (1915/1916, Chicago House of Corr., Psychopathic Dept. Bull. No. 2). The entry continues: "Upper age limit for feeble-minded found to be 11.4, the lower limit for normals found to be 10.6." A related entry (ref. 235) discusses overlapping groups and sets tentative limits for the borderzone.

"An analysis of results obtained from the examination of 335 inmates, ages 17-21. The distributions of the normal and feeble-minded groups overlap."
