## Evidence

### Katsantonis I 2023

Katsantonis I, McLellan R. (2023). Profiling students' motivation and metacognitive self-regulation: Links with school language achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0289367

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent multinomial logistic regression via the BCH method with the exceptional profile as reference; neither SES nor home language significantly predicted membership in the adequate profile (SES OR = .91, p = .130; language OR = .55, p = .282).

> "Coming from a household with higher SES was related to lower likelihood of being in the minimal MMSR profile 1 (OR = .80, 95% CI = .70, .92). Speaking Greek language at home was associated only with lower odds of belonging to the minimal MMSR profile 1 (OR = .22, 95% CI .08, .60)."
