F1000Research 14:1416. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.172023.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive content analysis of tutee interviews identified hesitancy and emotional issues as a subtheme; one tutee reported being "nervous about the stigma of how a smart student was receiving peer tutoring."

> "Several participants disclosed that they were anxious at first due to fears of judgment, the stigma of seeking help, or simply the uncertainty of working with someone new."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Tutee interview data in the hesitancy and emotional issues subtheme; participants reported that the friendliness and supportiveness of peer tutors eased their concerns after the first session.
