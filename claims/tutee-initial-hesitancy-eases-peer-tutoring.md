F1000Research 14:1416. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.172023.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive content analysis of tutee interviews identified hesitancy and emotional issues as a subtheme; one tutee reported being "nervous about the stigma of how a smart student was receiving peer tutoring."

> "Several participants disclosed that they were anxious at first due to fears of judgment, the stigma of seeking help, or simply the uncertainty of working with someone new."

### Dias Jacqueline Maria 2025 (2)

Dias Jacqueline Maria, Al Kaabi Fatema Salim Bati Salim, Al Hooti Sara Tamim, Hammadi Nada Osamah, Karim Rana, AlYateem Nabeel, Reefat Ahmed Fatma, Sara Abraham Mini, Seshan Vidya, Arsyad Sub Muhammad, Dias Ambrose Richard, Mottershead Richard (2025). Exploring peer tutoring experiences in improving nursing students’ academic success and performance: A qualitative study among Emirati undergraduate nursing students. F1000Research 14:1416. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.172023.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Tutee interview data in the hesitancy and emotional issues subtheme; participants reported that the friendliness and supportiveness of peer tutors eased their concerns after the first session.

> "Yes, I was a little nervous at the beginning because I didn’t know what to expect, and I was worried that I might not keep up. However, after the first session, I felt much more comfortable"
