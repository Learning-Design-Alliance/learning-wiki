Level 3 (BEHAVIOR) evaluation reported in the review: face-to-face semistructured interviews with junior nursing students after training and subsequent clinical EOL care exposure. The review attributes the finding to Venkatasalu and colleagues; findings were qualitative only, with no effect size printed.

"The authors found that those in the SBLE group were stronger in emotional and clinical preparedness and in converting knowledge into practice compared to those who completed the seminar training."
