## Evidence

### Greenberg AL 2022

Greenberg AL, Syed SM, Alseidi A, O'Sullivan PS, Chern H. (2022). Robotic training for medical students: feasibility of a pilot simulation curriculum. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01508-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-session questionnaire completed by all 14 participating students, using three Likert-scale questions on value and preparedness plus open-ended items analyzed with qualitative content analysis. Results are reported as percentages; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "All found the training very or extremely valuable, thirteen (93%) felt much or extremely more prepared for their next surgical sub-internship, and all felt much or extremely more prepared to enter surgical training."
