## Evidence

### Gartmeier Martin 2019

Gartmeier Martin, Baumgartner Maria, Burgkart Rainer, Heiniger Susanne, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Why hand hygiene is not sufficient: modeling hygiene competence of clinical staff as a basis for its development and assessment. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc39. [doi:10.3205/zma001247](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001247)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Pilot study using the think aloud technique as cognitive pretesting: two medical students (age 21 and 23, both in the sixth semester) verbalized their thoughts while completing a 20-vignette test version. The article reports "a good understanding of the test instructions and materials"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Generally, this procedure showed a good understanding of the test instructions and materials; the general test principle of identifying hygiene-related problems in the picture vignettes was plausible for the students."
