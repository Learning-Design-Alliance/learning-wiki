### Pavan Inguva 2021

Pavan Inguva, Vijesh J. Bhute, Thomas N.H. Cheng, Pierre J. Walker. (2021). Introducing students to research codes: A short course on solving partial differential equations in Python. Preprint submitted to Education for Chemical Engineers. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group feedback from the remote course. The original design centred on students independently exploring boundary/initial conditions and numerical schemes by modifying exercise scripts; "This approach was not favoured by students." Students suggested formal tasks with a correct solution to compare against.

> "Most students indicated a desire for more active and structured learning components related to the actual code and the running of simulations during the course."
