# The Block 3 novel stimulus reliably decreased accuracy only in the age-matched group, whose decrease exceeded that of the other groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The Block 3 introduction of a new sound-pair mapping to an already-associated visual symbol tended to decrease performance in all groups, but the decrease was reliably different from zero only in the age-matched group and was larger than in the DD and reading-matched groups. ## Evidence

### Pasqualotto 2025

Pasqualotto, A., Cochrane, A., Venuti, P. et al. (2025). Impaired audio-visual associations in dyslexia: evidence beyond linguistic processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00382-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian offset coefficient for the Block 3 stimulus introduction, visible in both waves separately (Wave 1 mean = -0.359; Wave 2 mean = -0.329). Group comparisons showed the age-matched decrease was larger than the DD group's (mean = -0.285) and the reading-matched group's (mean = -0.239).

> "the decrease was reliably different from zero change only in the age-matched group (mean = -0.348, CI95 = [-0.47,-0.222])"
