## Evidence

### Kunter 2008

Kunter, M.; Tsai, Y.-M.; Klusmann, U.; Brunner, M.; Krauss, Stefan; Baumert, J. (2008). Students' and mathematics teachers' perceptions of teacher enthusiasm and instruction. Learning and Instruction 18 (5), S. 468-482. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2008.06.008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling on questionnaire data from teachers and their 9th-grade classes found that "teachers who were more enthusiastic about teaching showed higher quality instructional behaviour" across both informants. No effect size is printed.

> "Structural equation modelling revealed that teachers who were more enthusiastic about teaching showed higher quality instructional behaviour—both self-reported and student-rated."
