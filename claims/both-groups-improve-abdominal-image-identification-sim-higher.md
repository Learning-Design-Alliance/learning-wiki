In the randomized trial's Likert self-assessments, "both groups demonstrated significant improvement in abdominal image identification", with the simulation group improving from 3.0 to 5.0 and the traditional group from 3.0 to 4.0.

> "Both groups demonstrated significant improvement in abdominal image identification (Group A: pre 3.0, post 4.0, p = 0.001; Group B: pre 3.0, post 5.0, p = 0.001), with Group B scoring higher than Group A after training (p = 0.002)"
