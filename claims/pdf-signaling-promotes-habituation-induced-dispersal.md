Quantification of displacement over the first and final 30 s of the habituation assay; significance of displacement increase was one-tailed P < 0.01 with Bonferroni correction (Fig. 5). The pdfr-1 deficit was rescued by simultaneous expression in both tissues to the full extent of the behavior.

> "Within-strain comparisons revealed an increase in distance traveled in the ﬁnal 30 s compared to the initial 30 s for the control animals that was not apparent for either the pdfr-1 mutant or the pdf-1;pdf-2 double mutant"
