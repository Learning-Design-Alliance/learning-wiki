## Evidence

### Chance-Revels 2026

Chance-Revels, R., Phan, Q., Crawford, K., Steiger, L., & Swan, B. A. (2026). Enhancing Community Health Worker Communication: Evaluating Simulation-Based Training in Motivational Interviewing. Journal of Community Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10900-025-01543-w

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

A paired-samples t-test on pre- and post-training confidence surveys of 277 CHW trainees showed a small but statistically significant improvement in confidence using culturally appropriate resources to address misinformation (d = 0.25, 95% CI [0.13, 0.37]). This was the smallest effect among the three domains.

> "Trainees reported increased confidence in using culturally appropriate resources to connect with others and address misinformation, t(276) = 4.13, p <.001, d = 0.25, 95% CI [0.13, 0.37]. This reflects a small but statistically significant positive effect of the MI training and simulation exercises."
