Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L 2026¶

Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L, Gaižiūnienė L and Pučėtaitė R (2026) AI in inclusive education: teachers' visions of future-oriented practices. Front. Educ. 11:1890925. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1890925

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative content analysis of 50 essays found ethical and governance themes relatively absent. The article cautions "This relative absence should not be interpreted as evidence that teachers consider these issues unimportant".

"The ethical and governance-related dimensions of AI were less prominent in teachers' narratives than its immediate pedagogical opportunities and challenges. Issues such as algorithmic bias, transparency, data privacy, accountability, and human oversight were not among the most salient spontaneously expressed concerns."
