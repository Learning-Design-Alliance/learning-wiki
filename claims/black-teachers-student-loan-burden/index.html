Carver-Thomas 2025¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & García, E. (2025). Supporting and sustaining a diverse teacher workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/216.666

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of national teacher survey data on student loan borrowing, repayment, and consequences by race/ethnicity (Table 4, 2020–21). The report finds "Black teachers were more likely than other teachers to have taken out a student loan" (71% vs. 60% for White teachers).

"Black teachers were more likely than other teachers to have taken out a student loan to pay for their undergraduate or graduate education, whether related to teacher preparation or otherwise (71% vs. 60% for White teachers)."
