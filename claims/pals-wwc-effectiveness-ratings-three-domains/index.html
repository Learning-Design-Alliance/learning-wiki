Evidence¶

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies 2012¶

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/364

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The report's effectiveness summary synthesizes three studies (2 RCTs meeting standards without reservations, 1 with reservations) covering 3,130 kindergarten and first-grade students; it concludes PALS "was found to have potentially positive effects on alphabetics, no discernible effects on fluency, and mixed effects on comprehension." Extent of evidence is medium to large for alphabetics, small for fluency and comprehension.
