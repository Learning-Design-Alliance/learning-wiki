# In a real syllogistic reasoning analysis, unrestricted estimation assigns 40.7% to 89.5% of examinees to impermissible attribute profiles depending on the CDM

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Impermissible attribute rates under unrestricted estimation range from 40.7% (DINA) to 89.5% (HDCM) across six CDMs applied to the syllogistic reasoning data. [→ Tu 2019](#tu-2019)

## Evidence

### Tu 2019

Tu, D., Wang, S., Cai, Y., Douglas, J., & Chang, H.-H. (2019). Cognitive Diagnostic Models With Attribute Hierarchies: Model Estimation With a Restricted Q-Matrix Design. Applied Psychological Measurement, 43(4), 255-271. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146621618765721

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Real data analysis of responses to 15 syllogistic reasoning items from 769 undergraduate students in two universities of China, with six CDMs fit by MMLE/EM under a divergent seven-attribute hierarchy with 16 permissible profiles. The printed rates are quoted; no effect size is given.

> "Specifically, the impermissible attribute rates of the unrestricted estimation procedure based on the DINA, the rRUM, the ACDM, the GDINA, the LCDM, and the HDCM are 40.7%, 61.1%, 56.2%, 76.9%, 66.1%, and 89.5%, respectively."
