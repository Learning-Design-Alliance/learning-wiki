## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median self-rated confidence ratings on a 10-point scale rose significantly from pre-test to post-test after the e-module, from 4 to 7 across all participants in one group with no comparison group, and within each training-level subgroup. ## Evidence

### Wegner Lauren E. MS 2023

Wegner Lauren E. MS, Shiffermiller Jason F. MD, MPH, Vokoun Chad W. MD, O'Rourke Michael J. MD, Rohlfsen Cory J. MD. (2023). The Effects of an E-learning Module on Medical Trainees Rotating on a Perioperative Medicine Clinical Service. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11325

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey of confidence in making perioperative care decisions on a 10-point scale (1 lowest, 10 highest), analyzed with Wilcoxon rank sum tests among 75 trainees. Median rose from 4 to 7, "p < .001". No effect size reported.

> "Following the e-module, confidence ratings improved significantly across all participants (7 vs. 4, p < .001) as well as within student (7 vs. 3, p < .001), intern (8 vs. 5, p < .001), and resident (8 vs. 7, p < .001) subgroups."
