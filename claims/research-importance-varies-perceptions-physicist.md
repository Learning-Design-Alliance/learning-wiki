## Evidence

### Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre 2014

Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2014). Upper-Level Physics Students' Perceptions of Physicists. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.1197](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.1197)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phenomenographic analysis of the interview data found perceptions differ on research's role: high-research categories (researchers answering unanswered questions; knowledgeable practitioners) versus low-research categories (certain mindset; commitment to physics). The article reports "differences in the importance of research and its association with being a physicist."

> "We find a clear distinction in the exclusivity students associate with being a physicist and the differences in the importance of research and its association with being a physicist."
