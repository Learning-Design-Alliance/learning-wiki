Qualitative analysis of interviews and focus groups with 11 FYSCP county coordinators (three focus groups and two individual interviews, December 2020–January 2021). Coordinators reported that "Data systems are often insufficient to support individual student case management and collaboration between schools and districts and child welfare agencies."
