Ellinor Waaland, Vibeke Rønneberg, Dieuwer ten Braak. (2026). Effects and Characteristics of Mathematics Interventions for Students with Co-Occurring Difficulties in Mathematics and Reading: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10184-x

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Multilevel correlated and hierarchical effects (CHE) meta-analysis of nine RCTs (1036 students, grades 1-3) comparing mathematics interventions to business-as-usual control, estimated with metafor and robust variance estimation. The model with outliers showed a "large and significant weighted mean treatment effect (g = 0.47"; the model without outliers gave g = 0.52 (95% CI [0.19-0.85]).

> "For the model with outliers (i.e., 60 effect sizes), results showed a large and significant weighted mean treatment effect (g = 0.47, RVE SE = 0.13, 95% CI = [0.14, 0.79], p = 0.012)."
