`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Toplak 2013 and Isquith and colleagues, "consistently low correlations observed between task-based and questionnaire-based EF measures", attributing the difference to methodological differences between maximal-capacity tasks and typical-behaviour questionnaires. No correlation coefficients are printed.

> "These methodological differences likely contribute to the consistently low correlations observed between task-based and questionnaire-based EF measures (Toplak 2013). Indeed, evidence suggests that these approaches may assess partially distinct phenomena (Isquith et al. 2013; Isquith 2014; Toplak 2013)."
