Evidence¶

Brunmair 2019¶

Brunmair, M., & Richter, T. (2019). Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators. Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209

q4 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analytic estimates by material type in the same multilevel synthesis (k = 238). Mathematical tasks showed a small significant effect; expository texts did not differ significantly from zero.

"In nonvisual stimuli, a positive interleaving effect was found only for mathematical tasks, g = 0.34 (p = .005, 95% CI [0.11, 0.57]) and no significant effect for expository texts."

Brunmair 2019 (2)¶

Brunmair, M., & Richter, T. (2019). Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators. Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209

q4 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r?

The same material-type moderator analysis found a blocking advantage for word materials, with the printed estimate "g = -0.39" and a confidence interval excluding zero.

"Furthermore, we found a negative effect (i.e., an advantage of blocking over interleaving) for studies based on words as learning materials, g = -0.39 (p > .001, 95% CI [-0.64, -0.14])."
