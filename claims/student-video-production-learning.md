Antony C. Searle, Craig M. Savage, Paul A. Altin, Francis H. Bennet and Michael R. Hush. (2005). Through Einstein's Eyes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508224](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508224)

In 2004 four PhB students used Backlight to create relativistic videos later packaged into the CD; one student contrasted the project with courses where each project is designed to teach a specific topic, saying the product had "no exact answer". These are untested self-reports.

> "There was no exact answer; our only guide was to teach relativity to a very wide audience."
