The article's assessment of the current environment (§7.1) states there are "very few physics department supported courses on ML" and that early-career researchers are often advised against ML positions as career death sentences. The article itself footnotes that "the evidence presented here is primarily anecdotal" due to a lack of community statistics.

> "As a physics graduate student at a US institute it is essentially impossible to focus one’s thesis research on ML, as most departments do not acknowledge this as ‘physics work’"
