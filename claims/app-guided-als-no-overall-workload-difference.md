## Evidence

### Kamphausen Anne 2026

Kamphausen Anne, Pilz Maximilian, Baumgärtel Matthias, Kubitz Jens Christian, Engelen Christian. (2026). Effect of an app-guided advanced cardiac life support algorithm on teamwork, non-technical skills, and workload in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled feasibility trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-026-01673-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized simulation-based feasibility trial with 40 interprofessional teams (80 participants) comparing ALS performance with and without the 'CPR Leader' app. Overall NASA-TLX RAW score showed "mean difference − 4.9 with 95% CI [-12, 2.6]; p = 0.2" from mixed linear regression with random intercept team.

> "The overall NASA-TLX RAW score did not differ meaningfully between groups (mean 49.2 ± 13.6 in intervention vs. 54.1 ± 14.6 in control group; median 50 in intervention vs. 54 in control group; mean difference − 4.9 with 95% CI [-12, 2.6]; p = 0.2 from mixed linear regression with random intercept team)"
