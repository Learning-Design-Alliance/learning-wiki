Exploring barriers and enablers to simulation-based training in emergency departments: an international qualitative study (BEST-ED Study). BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-073099

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion of the interview study; support and leadership was the most frequently mentioned theme (31 of 32 participants, 96.8% per Table 1). The article states "Leadership was mentioned as a crucial factor in both the barriers and enablers section."
