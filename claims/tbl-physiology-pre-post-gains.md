# Students in the TBL physiology course showed significant pre–post gains on a conceptual physiology test and daily concept quizzes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students scored significantly higher on the end-of-class concept test than on the first-day pretest. [→ Leupen 2020](#leupen-2020)
`q3 i?` Pre–post quizzes given on recorded class days all showed significant gains within class time. [→ Leupen 2020 (2)](#leupen-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Leupen 2020

Leupen, S. M., Kephart, K. L., & Hodges, L. C. (2020). Factors Influencing Quality of Team Discussion: Discourse Analysis in an Undergraduate Team-Based Learning Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 19(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-06-0112

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Repeated-measures t test comparing a 20-question conceptual physiology test given on the first day and embedded in the final exam; the instrument was written by the first author and is not externally validated. Printed means: "75.8 ± 17.3% vs. 37.9 ± 14.4%".

> "Students scored significantly higher on the physiology concept posttest taken on the last day of class than they did on the pretest taken on the first day of class (75.8 ± 17.3% vs. 37.9 ± 14.4%, p < 0.0001)."

### Leupen 2020 (2)

Leupen, S. M., Kephart, K. L., & Hodges, L. C. (2020). Factors Influencing Quality of Team Discussion: Discourse Analysis in an Undergraduate Team-Based Learning Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 19(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-06-0112

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Five-question conceptual quizzes given at the beginning and end of the three recorded class days; all showed significant gains, e.g. September "1.9/5" to "3.8/5" (p < 0.0001), October 2.1/5 to 4.1/5, December 3.0/5 to 4.3/5. No effect sizes printed.

> "In September, students scored an average of 1.9/5 on the neurophysiology pretest and a 3.8/5 on the posttest 1 hour later (Figure 1, p < 0.0001)."
