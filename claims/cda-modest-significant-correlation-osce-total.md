`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis comparing CDA3 scores with OSCE scores completed in the same trimester in a cohort of second-year optometry students. The article reports a "modest but significant relationship" with the printed R2 values for total and Part 2 scores.

> "These demonstrated a modest but significant relationship between CDA3 Total score and OSCE Total score (R2 = 0.106, p < 0.05) and CDA3 Part 2 and OSCE Total score (R2 = 0.073, p < 0.05)."
