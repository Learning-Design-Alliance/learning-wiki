## Evidence

### Otis 1918

Otis, A. S. (1918). An absolute point scale for the group measurements of intelligence. Part I. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 9(5), 239-261. https://books.google.com/books/about/the_journal_of_educational_psychology.html

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A check on scoring methods using the first and second spelling scores of the 14 pupils who did not attempt all words, comparing a chance-corrected deduction method against merely counting correct answers; the printed sums of differences were "34 and in the second 45", leading the author to conclude the employed method was more reliable.

> "The sum of the differences in the first case was 34 and in the second 45, although the scores were less in the second case. This suggests that the method employed was the more reliable."
