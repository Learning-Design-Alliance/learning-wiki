### Sebring 1995

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Prairie School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-prairie-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey results for the eight-item LSC Contribution scale, comparing bottom and top quartile schools. In top quartile schools "Three quarters judge the LSC has made significant contributions (scores of 6-10)" and less than 5 percent claim no contribution.

> "Thirty-five percent believe the LSC has made a limited contribution (scores of 3-5), and 40 percent indicate no contribution (scores of 1-2) to the improvement of the school. About a quarter of the teachers claim the LSC has made a significant contribution (scores of 6-10)"
