`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis (Spearman's rho with Bonferroni correction) of survey responses plotted confidence and comfort against years of experience. The article reports "Experience significantly increased confidence to support all FTP issues but not comfort"; correlations for confidence ranged from r = .39 to r = .61.
