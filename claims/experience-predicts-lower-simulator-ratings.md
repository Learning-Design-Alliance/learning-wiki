`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Linear multivariate regression on the 56 experts' total ratings with professional experience, participants per center, hospital care level, and simulator type as predictors. The article reports the printed estimates and p values; hospital care level and center size did not impact the rating.

> "Linear multivariate regression analysis showed that experts with more professional experience gave more unsatisfactory ratings (estimate = −0.4, p < 0.001) (Fig. 5) and confirmed the significantly lower rating of the Premie HAL S2209 (Gaumard) (estimate = −2.1, p < 0.001) compared to the other simulators."
