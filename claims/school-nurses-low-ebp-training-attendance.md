In-service training attendance data from the 1200-nurse survey (Table 2): EBP and school-community collaboration were the least-attended courses (64 nurses, 5.3% each), while health promotion (82.7%) and infectious disease prevention (81.3%) led. Also, 19.0% had ever participated in EBP training courses (Table 1).

> "Conversely, this study found that only 5.3% of school nurses had attended EBP training in the previous year. Therefore, it is necessary and urgent to design a tailored EBP training course for school nurses."
