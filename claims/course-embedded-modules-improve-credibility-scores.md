## Evidence

### Breakstone 2021

Breakstone, J., Smith, M., Connors, P., Ortega, T., Kerr, D., & Wineburg, S. (2021). Lateral reading: College students learn to critically evaluate internet sources in an online course. Harvard Kennedy School (HKS) Misinformation Review. https://doi.org/10.37016/mr-2020-56

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest–posttest study of 87 undergraduates in two sections of an asynchronous online nutrition course, using parallel assessment forms with counterbalanced order. The repeated-measures ANOVA showed "an average gain of 3.13 points" with F(1, 85) = 136.03, p < .001; no effect size was printed.

> "Average scores improved from 3.95 points out of 13 at pretest to 7.08 at posttest, an average gain of 3.13 points. A repeated-measures ANOVA revealed that the gains from pretest to posttest were statistically significant, Mean diff = 3.13; F(1, 85) = 136.03, p < .001."
