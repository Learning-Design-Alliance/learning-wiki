### Carlos González 2023

Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results section on furthering students' digital competencies: the interview study reports teachers perceived students as intensive social-network users who nonetheless did not manage institutionally provided tools such as LMS, digital libraries, or videoconferencing, nor interaction tools like Padlet or Kahoot.

> "Regardless of students' high level of social network use, teachers also generally perceived that they were less confident and struggled with the use of digital technologies for learning and professional purposes."
