George Ann, Blaauw Duane, Dangor Ziyaad, Lala Sanjay G. (2025). Evaluation of the impact of an online introductory paediatric physical examination skills course on students’ OSCE performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08177-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a voluntary post-OSCE online survey (74.9% response rate, 260 of 347). Respondents "regarded the video demonstrations as more helpful than the textbook (86.5%)" and reported the course stimulated interest in paediatrics (79.6%).

> "Most respondents indicated that the course had stimulated their interest in paediatrics (79.6%), regarded the video demonstrations as more helpful than the textbook (86.5%), and felt that the videos provided more structure than bedside teaching (74.2%). A slightly lower proportion agreed that it made them feel confident about the OSCE (62.3%)."
