### David P. Waters 2019

David P. Waters, Dragos Amarie, Rebecca A. Booth, Christopher Conover and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Investigating students seriousness during selected conceptual inventory surveys. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020118)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Application of the PRT to the PhysPort actual populations flagged 'between 0.6% and 1.3% as non-serious for each of the diﬀerent RBAIs,' while the test identified nearly zero non-serious survey-takers in the simulated random-guesser population, which the authors call unsurprising because the sought patterns are non-random.

> "Applying the PRT to the actual population data iden- tiﬁes between 0.6% and 1.3% as non-serious for each of the diﬀerent RBAIs. The PRT identiﬁed nearly zero non- serious survey-takers in the simulated population, how- ever."
