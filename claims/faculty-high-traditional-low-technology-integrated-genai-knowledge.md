## Evidence

### Sutedjo A 2026

Sutedjo A, Chowdhury M and Liu SP (2026) Assessing faculty self-perceived knowledge in using generative AI to teach 21st-century skills. Front. Educ. 11:1913310. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1913310

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 127 faculty at a large Southern U.S. public research university using an adapted TPACK-21 questionnaire. CK mean was 5.15 (SD = 0.82), described as "a high level of self-perceived knowledge in their expertise as subject matter experts."

> "The results indicated a mean score of 5.15 (SD = 0.82) for content knowledge (CK), suggesting that faculty members’ perceived content knowledge falls between “I have good knowledge about the topic” and “I have strong knowledge about the topic.”"
