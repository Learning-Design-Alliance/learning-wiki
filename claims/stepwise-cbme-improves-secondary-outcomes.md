In the RCT, the stepwise CBME group showed superior puncture site selection (94% vs. 82%) and lower pain scores (2.8 ± 0.6 vs. 3.5 ± 0.8 on VAS). Table 5 further reports significant differences in vascular localization accuracy, procedural fluency, patient comfort, blood loss, and cosmetic outcomes.
