### National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program 2010

National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/329

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The Millenky, Bloom, and Dillon (2010) randomized controlled trial is the only one of 14 reviewed studies meeting WWC evidence standards. A subset of 1,508 youth were randomly selected for the 21-month follow-up survey, and the analysis sample included 1,196 youths.

> "The study included 3,074 youth who were randomly assigned to participate in the National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program or to a control group that was not eligible to receive services."
