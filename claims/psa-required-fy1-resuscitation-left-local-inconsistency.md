### Baker Adam 2026 (2)

Baker Adam, Okorie Michael. (2026). UK Foundation Programme training needs a national minimum standard for advanced life support. Clinical Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinme.2026.100605

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Author argument in this opinion article comparing resuscitation and prescribing. Resuscitation is less frequent than prescribing, but arguably even less forgiving, per the authors.

> "Both require rapid decision making, application of algorithms under pressure and the ability to act safely in situations where error can cause immediate harm. Yet one is protected by a national pass requirement, and the other is left to variable local provision and portfolio evidence."
