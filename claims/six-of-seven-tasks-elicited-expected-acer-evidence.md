Amali Priyanka Jambuge and James T. Laverty. (2021). Assessing Scientific Practices in Physics Paper-based Assessments. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.13028](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.13028)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Think-aloud interview study of 8 introductory calculus-based physics students responding to 7 assessment tasks (53 analyzable instances). The authors report that "majority of the tasks (i.e., 6 out of 7)" elicited evidence for each ACER component; Table III shows 37 of 53 instances elicited evidence.

> "As noted in Table III, majority of the tasks (i.e., 6 out of 7) elicited students’ reasoning that enabled us to capture their evidence pertaining to each component in the ACER framework"
