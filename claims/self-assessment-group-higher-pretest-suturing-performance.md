## Evidence

### Hillemans Vera 2024

Hillemans Vera, Buyne Otmar, de Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne M.B.I., Verhoeven Bas H., Joosten Maja. (2024). Self-assessment, and not continuous training, improves basic open suturing skills. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2374101

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group Kruskal Wallis comparisons at pre-test in the three-group design (n=44, n=16, n=16). For example, the Donati suture pre-test composite score showed "clinical exposure group: 64.2 vs. continuous training group: 64.2 vs. self-assessment group: 75.3, p = 0.004". The intracutaneous composite contrast (59.4 vs 64.4 vs 70.6) reached only p = 0.063.

> "The self-assessment group scored better at the pre-test than the other two groups for all tasks, except for ‘knot tying by hand' (0.004 ≤ p ≤ 0.063)."
