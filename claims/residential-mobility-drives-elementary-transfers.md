# Residential mobility is closely associated with elementary school transfers in CPS, with nearly 80 percent of mid-year within-district transfers accompanied by a residential move

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among elementary students, school moves and residential moves are tightly linked, especially during the school year; the relationship is much weaker at the high school level. [→ Marisa de la Torre 2009](#marisa-de-la-torre-2009)

Descriptive analysis of the residential-school move relationship in Chapter 3 (Figure 8). The report states "nearly 80 percent" of mid-year elementary within-district transferrers also changed residences, and two-thirds did so during the summer; only 35 percent of high school mid-year movers changed residence.

> "In 2006, nearly 80 percent of students who transferred mid-year from one CPS school to another CPS school also changed residences"
