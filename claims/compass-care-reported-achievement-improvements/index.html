Zimiles 2025¶

Zimiles, J., Nakamoto, J., & Boal, A. (2025). Compass Care at Valor Collegiate Academies: Attendance program spotlight. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677597

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Perceptual accounts from spring 2025 site-visit interviews, not school-record data. Students described check-ins as keeping them on track, and staff provided strategies for studying and encouraged resources such as Valor's homework hotline and online tools such as Gauth. No quantitative grade data are reported.

"Students, caregivers, and school staff described noticeable improvements in grades and achievement as a result of participating in Compass Care."
