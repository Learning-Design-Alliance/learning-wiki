Related Claims¶

  • Engage-to-Unlock improved error-classification accuracy over Standard Chatbot but showed no significant advantage in overall judgment accuracy or complete diagnosis — related
  • Time-matched unlocking did not reproduce the engagement state that triggered unlocks: most Time-Matched Unlock participants received access before meeting the discourse criteria — related
  • Engage-to-Unlock redistributed effort across tasks: participants spent more time writing and less time evaluating, without increasing overall task duration — related
