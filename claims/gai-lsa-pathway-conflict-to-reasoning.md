## Evidence

### Zou 2026

Zou, J., Zhai, X., Gao, C. (2026). Beyond Information Retrieval: Generative AI as an Epistemic Arbiter to Enhance Collaborative Problem-Solving. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29788-4_18](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29788-4_18)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lag sequential analysis (GSEQ, adjusted residuals > 1.96) of 1,154 behavioral entries coded from 300 minutes of classroom video. The EG pathway "C1 →S3→C3" suggests GAI-supported collaboration helped transform disagreement into solution-oriented reasoning; the CG showed a more fragmented pattern with its most prominent transition from listening and responding to task planning (Z = 3.02).

> "there was a significant transition from “task planning” (C1) to “conflict management” (S3) (Z = 2.29, p < .05), followed by a stronger transition from “conflict management” (S3) to “solution reasoning” (C3) (Z = 3.90, p < .01)"
