## Evidence

### Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck 2023

Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck. (2023). Effects of positive and negative social feedback on motivation, evaluative learning, and socio-emotional processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00178-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design experiment (SID-Photo and SID-Video studies; young n = 101, older n = 107 as stated in the introduction) using a 2 × 2 × 3 permutation F-test on z-transformed RT. Post-hoc tests showed "a signi ﬁcant difference between the positive vs and negative vs neutral (p = 0.0001) but not positive vs negative condition"; a Bayesian ANOVA favored the age + condition model (BF10 = 2.44 × 1013).

> "The 2 × 2 × 3 mixed design permutation F-test showed signi ﬁcant main effects of age (p = 0.0001, Fig. 3a) and condition ( p = 0.0001; Fig. 3b) but no signi ﬁcant main effect of study and no signi ﬁcant interactions."
