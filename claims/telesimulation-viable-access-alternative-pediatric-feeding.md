`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion of the randomized controlled trial results, building on the equivalent clinical reasoning, confidence and anxiety outcomes between modalities; they note telesimulation requires less practical resourcing and minimal travel.

> "Given that telesimulation provides increased access to learning opportunities with similar outcomes demonstrated to in-person simulation, it is reasonable to suggest that telesimulation has the potential to fill a significant professional development gap in this field."
