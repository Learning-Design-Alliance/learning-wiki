### Ngo Hazel 2025

Ngo Hazel, Sokolovic Nina, Hu Julia, Jenkins Jennifer. (2025). A systematic review identifying effective teaching methods and their combinations for increasing empathy in physicians: pairwise and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07917-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression of intervention format in the pairwise meta-analysis (Table 2: format Q = 8.72, p = .03). The article reports "in-person group formats (d = 0.66, 95% CI: 0.50, 0.82) were more effective than interventions with online/independent formats". The authors link this to peer collaboration benefits for interpersonal skills.

> "Interventions with in-person group formats (d = 0.66, 95% CI: 0.50, 0.82) were more effective than interventions with online/independent formats (d = 0.21, 95% CI: 0.08, 0.34)."
