## Evidence

### Yue Xin 2025

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the Results section relating system-recorded simulation scores (V1) to post-training practical scores (T2) in 34 students; "r = 0.73 (p < 0.001)" with R2 = 0.53.

> "A strong positive correlation was observed between virtual simulation performance (V1) and post-training practical performance (T2), with r = 0.73 (p < 0.001), indicating that V1 explained approximately 53% of the variance in T2."

### Yue Xin 2025 (2)

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Same Pearson correlation analysis; the V1–S2 association was "r = 0.42, p = 0.013" (|r| ≥ .37, coded 3), and T2–S2 was r = 0.38, p = 0.026, suggesting simulation performance partially reflects theoretical knowledge acquisition.

> "Moderate correlations were also identified between V1 and post-training theoretical knowledge scores (S2) (r = 0.42, p = 0.013), while a weak but statistically significant association was observed between T2 and S2 (r = 0.38, p = 0.026)."
