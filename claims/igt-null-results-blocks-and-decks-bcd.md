# No significant group differences on Iowa Gambling Task block trends, overall trend, or Decks B, C, and D choices

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Beyond the Deck A contrast, the groups did not differ significantly on any IGT block trend, the overall general trend, or choices from Decks B, C, and D; no effect sizes were printed for these null results. [→ Medeiros W 2016](#medeiros-w-2016)

## Evidence

### Medeiros W 2016

Medeiros W, Torro-Alves N, Malloy-Diniz LF and Minervino CM (2016) Executive Functions in Children Who Experience Bullying Situations. Front. Psychol. 7:1197. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01197

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis analyses of IGT learning curves across the four groups found "no signiﬁcant diﬀerences between the groups with regard to the general score trends in blocks" and no overall trend difference; Decks B (p = 0.286), C (p = 0.325), and D (p = 0.566) also showed no differences. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no signiﬁcant diﬀerences between the groups with regard to the general score trends in blocks ( χ2= 0.241, p= 0.971 in Block 1; χ2= 0.758, p= 0.859 in Block 2; χ2= 1.435, p= 0.697 in Block 3; χ2= 1.685, p= 0.640 in Block 4, and χ2= 0.096, p= 0.995 in Block 5) and the overall general trend (χ2= 0.668, p= 0.881)."
