Nagaoka 2021¶

Descriptive analysis of November 2020 National Student Clearinghouse data for CPS graduating classes 2016–20 (Figure 1). The report states the "rate of immediate enrollment in four-year college declined by 1 percentage point," with no inferential test reported.

"The rate of immediate enrollment in four-year college declined by 1 percentage point from 41.8 percent in 2019 to 40.8 percent in 2020."
