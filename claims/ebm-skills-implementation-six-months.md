## Evidence

### Berger B 2010

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semi-structured telephone interviews six months after the courses with 129 of 156 course finishers, categorising implementation into five levels from none (Level 0) to almost complete (Level 4). The evaluation is descriptive.

> "Implementation of EBM skills was reported by 84 of the 129 (65%) participants available for follow-up interviews."

### Berger B 2010 (2)

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of follow-up interviews by participant type. Professional counsellors predominantly reported Level 2 implementation, while patient advocates reported Levels 2, 3 and 4 at comparable degrees.

> "Self-help group members reported the most successful use of the course programme: 15 participants out of 54 (28%) felt encouraged to take up advocacy activities and 10 (18%) stated implementation, which we ranked high (Level 4)."
