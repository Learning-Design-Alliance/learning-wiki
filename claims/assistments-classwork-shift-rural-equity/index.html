Evidence¶

Feng 2025¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of teacher self-reported online instructional logs across four log rounds. The article states this discrepancy "was likely driven in part by students' limited internet or device access at home" and frames in-class ASSISTments use as a more equitable practice for rural students.

"most math practice was assigned as classwork (82.4% of logged days) rather than homework (only 36.2% of logged days had homework due)"

Feng 2025 (2)¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Teacher instructional log data corroborated by system use data: teachers made ASSISTments assignments during 24.5 weeks on average, totaling 69.5 assignments. Survey estimates put 42.7% of independent practice in ASSISTments.

"students engaged in independent practice on nearly every reported day (85.7%)"
