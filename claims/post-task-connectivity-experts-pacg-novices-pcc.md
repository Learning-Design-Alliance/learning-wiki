## Evidence

### Cortese 2026

Cortese, F., Hruska, P., McLaughlin, K. J., Coderre, S. P., Protzner, A. B., Krigolson, O. E., Hecker, K. G. (2026). Expertise Related Changes in Resting‐State Functional Connectivity Patterns Following a Clinical Reasoning and Decision‐Making Task. Brain and Behavior 16, no. 1: e71153. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.71153

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group-level mixed-effects univariate GLM of seed-based connectivity (right FPPFC seed, pre- vs post-task rest, n = 20) found "experts showed a larger connectivity increase with the right paracingulate gyrus" than novices; PaCG is described as a key node of the executive attentional network.

> "experts showed a larger connectivity increase with the right paracingulate gyrus (PaCG; MNI152 standard space peak voxel coordinate of 6, 22, 44) compared to the novice group"

### Cortese 2026 (2)

Cortese, F., Hruska, P., McLaughlin, K. J., Coderre, S. P., Protzner, A. B., Krigolson, O. E., Hecker, K. G. (2026). Expertise Related Changes in Resting‐State Functional Connectivity Patterns Following a Clinical Reasoning and Decision‐Making Task. Brain and Behavior 16, no. 1: e71153. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.71153

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same univariate GLM contrast found "novices showed a larger connectivity increase with the right posterior cingulate cortex" relative to experts; the PCC is described as a core region of the default mode network.

> "novices showed a larger connectivity increase with the right posterior cingulate cortex (PCC; MNI152 standard space peak voxel coordinate of 6, −47, 24) relative to the expert group"
