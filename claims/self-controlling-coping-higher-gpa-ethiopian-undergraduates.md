## Evidence

### Kassaw Chalachew 2025

Kassaw Chalachew, Demareva Valeriia. (2025). Psychosocial and cognitive predictors of academic achievement among higher education students in Southern Ethiopia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335968

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of 30 Dilla University undergraduates. Self-controlling coping showed a significant positive correlation with GPA, with "Self-controlling (ρ = 0.43, p < 0.05)"; the ANOVA on the regression model also found Self-controlling significant (F = 7.56, p = .011).

> "Significant positive correlations were found between GPA and three coping strategies (Confrontation (ρ = 0.40, p < 0.05), Self-controlling (ρ = 0.43, p < 0.05), Distancing (ρ = 0.44, p < 0.05))"
