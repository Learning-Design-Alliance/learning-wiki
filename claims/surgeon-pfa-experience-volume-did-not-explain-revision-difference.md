### Rasmussen Louise Eggers MD 2025

Rasmussen Louise Eggers MD, Hoffmann Adam Gorm MSc, Blanche Paul PhD, Espersen Frederik MD, Justesen Tobias Freyberg MD, Rasmussen Lasse Enkebølle MD, Hangaard Stine MD PhD, Christensen Robin PhD, Odgaard Anders MD DMSc. (2025). Surgeon Training and Revision Rates After Patellofemoral Arthroplasty. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.17825

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive surgeon-volume analysis in the 482-knee cohort: trial surgeons (8 surgeons, median 28 procedures, range 11-83) vs nontrial surgeons (34 surgeons, median 4 procedures, range 1-33). Descriptive medians only; no test statistic printed.

> "At the time of operation, the median (IQR) level of experience value of the operating surgeon was 22 (9-39) in the trial surgeon group compared with 5 (2-9) for the nontrial surgeon group."

### Rasmussen Louise Eggers MD 2025 (2)

Rasmussen Louise Eggers MD, Hoffmann Adam Gorm MSc, Blanche Paul PhD, Espersen Frederik MD, Justesen Tobias Freyberg MD, Rasmussen Lasse Enkebølle MD, Hangaard Stine MD PhD, Christensen Robin PhD, Odgaard Anders MD DMSc. (2025). Surgeon Training and Revision Rates After Patellofemoral Arthroplasty. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.17825

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supplementary analysis in which surgeon experience (ordinal procedure number, linear splines with knots at 5 and 10) was added to the model. The authors interpret the unchanged revision pattern as pointing to training; they note limited data were available for this analysis.

> "Yet adjusting for the PFA experience revealed no clear pattern in revision rates, suggesting the presence of an association with training among the surgeons."
