## Evidence

### Al‐Kaylani HM 2022

Al‐Kaylani HM, Reasoner EE, Loeffler BT, Mott SL, Madasu S, Liu A, Langbehn K, Conrad AL, Dickens D, Grafft A, Harshman L, Modi AJ, van der Plas E. (2022). Characterizing academic performance in pediatric acute lymphoblastic leukemia with population‐based achievement tests. Cancer Reports. https://doi.org/10.1002/cnr2.1560

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear mixed effects regression on school-based reading scores from 63 survivors (12 radiation-exposed, 51 not). The article reports radiation was "associated with reading performance" with β = −30.81 (95% CI −52.00:−9.62, p = .01); the discussion equates this with approximately two grades below current grade level.
