## Evidence

### Ong C.K.Y. 2021

Ong C.K.Y., Hutchesson M.J., Patterson A.J., Whatnall M.C. (2021). Is There an Association between Health Risk Behaviours and Academic Achievement among University Students? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18168314

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted linear regression in the cross-sectional survey of 1543 Australian university students. The article reports both associations remained significant after adjustment and under the Bonferroni-corrected threshold; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "exceeding single occasion alcohol consumption risk (β = −0.277, p ≤ 0.001), and smoking (β = −0.393, p = 0.001). Applying Bonferroni correction (i.e., 0.05/number of tests) to account for multiple comparisons would give a corrected p-value of 0.005 for significance."
