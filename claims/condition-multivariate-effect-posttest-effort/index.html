Mahir Akgun and Sacip Toker 2026¶

Mahir Akgun and Sacip Toker. (2026). Do Gains from Generative AI–Enabled Adaptive Pretesting Persist? Evidence from a Retention Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22328 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.22328

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

MANCOVA on data from 89 undergraduates (G1: n = 27; G2: n = 28; G3: n = 34) in an applied statistics course, with baseline as covariate. The article reports "Wilks’Λ=.664" and "partialη2 =.185". Pretest performance did not significantly predict the combined outcomes (p = .918).

"MANCOVA revealed a statistically significant multivariate effect of condition,Wilks’Λ=.664,F(4,168) = 9.56, p < .001, partialη2 =.185, indicating that approximately 18.5% of the vari- ance in the combined outcome space was attributable to condition after adjust- ing for baseline."
