Reliability check from the pilot study's evaluator workshop: six evaluators scored a video encounter before and after a consensus discussion, and reliability was computed from the second-round scores. Face validity was checked via two Delphi rounds with eight experts, retaining dimensions averaging at least 2 points.

> "We obtained the inter-rater reliability of this instrument on the basis of the second evaluation. The inter-rater reliability was 0.7."
