Evidence¶

Matthias Stadler 2024¶

Matthias Stadler, Maria Bannert, Michael Sailer. (2024). Cognitive ease at a cost: LLMs reduce mental effort but compromise depth in student scientific inquiry. Computers in Human Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2024.108386

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

ANCOVA in the randomized experiment (n=91) controlling for prior knowledge. LLM group mean GCL was 3.14 (SD 1.53) versus 4.79 (SD 1.18) for web search, with "33.06 <0.001 0.27", i.e., η² = 0.27, a large effect. The text notes "The strongest difference was found for GCL, the smallestforECL."

"H1.3-GCL 4.79(1.18) 3.14(1.53) 33.06 <0.001 0.27"
