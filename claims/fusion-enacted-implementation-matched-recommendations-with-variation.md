Strand Cary, M. G., Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Smolkowski, K., Fien, H., & Baker, S. K. (2017). A practitioner implementation of a Tier 2 first grade mathematics intervention. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 211–224. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714715

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive survey evidence from 14 educator respondents about the enacted intervention. The article reports that implementation "generally matched the research team's recommendations, but that variation existed", citing ranges of 4-10 group members, 2-5 days per week, and coverage through at least lesson 50 for most groups.

> "Survey responses collected at the end of the year (n = 14) reveal that the district's implementation of Fusion generally matched the research team's recommendations, but that variation existed."
