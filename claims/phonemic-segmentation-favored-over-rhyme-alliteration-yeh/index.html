Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Yeh (2003), an RCT with 44 low-income four- to five-year-old Head Start children in Boston, compared phonemic segmentation training with rhyme/alliteration training. The report states "The findings favored the phonemic segmentation group over the rhyme/alliteration group for all measures."

"The average improvement index for phonological processing is +20 percentile points, with a range of +9 to +33 percentile points across findings."

What Works Clearinghouse 2006 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q2 · i? · review · r3

Yeh (2003) print knowledge finding from a letter-sound matching measure; the early reading/writing improvement index was +26 percentile points for oral reading. Differences were "not statistically significant (as calculated by the WWC)" but substantively important.

"The improvement index for print knowledge is +33 percentile points for the one print knowledge outcome in this study."
