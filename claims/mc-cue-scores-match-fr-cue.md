## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Multiple-choice Assessment for Upper-division Electricity and Magnetism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison in E&M 1 at CU: half of the class (25 students) took the MC version and half the FR version, groups randomly selected but matched on average midterm exam score. The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "Using the more nuanced grading rubric (R2), the av- erage score on the MC version, 58.9 ± 3.6 %, was not signiﬁcantly different from the average on the FR ver- sion, 60.5 ± 2.8 % (Student’s t-test, p = 0.7)."
