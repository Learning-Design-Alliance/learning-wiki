# Students in the image condition gave higher draw judgment magnitude than text-only students in Study 1a

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jaeger 2024

Jaeger, A. J., & Fiorella, L. (2024). Metacognitive effects of instructional visuals: the role of cue use and judgment type. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09370-x

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Study 1a online experiment with 163 undergraduates reading biology texts. Draw judgment magnitude was significantly higher in the image condition: "t(161) = 2.02, p = 0.045, d = 0.32". No significant differences for explain or test judgment magnitudes.

> "there was a significant difference in judgment magnitude for the draw judgments, with participants in the text-with-image condition giving higher judgments, t(161) = 2.02, p = 0.045, d = 0.32."
