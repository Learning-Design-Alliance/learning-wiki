## Evidence

### Deiglmayr 2011

Deiglmayr, A., & Spada, H. (2011). Training for fostering knowledge co-construction from collaborative inference-drawing. Learning and Instruction, 21(3), 441-451. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2010.06.004

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way mixed model ANOVA on testing-phase inference-drawing frequency across the four training conditions of the experiment (36 dyads, 9 per condition) confirmed a "positive effect on the number of inferences collaborators drew during testing" (partial η2 = .36). The planned comparison of trained versus control dyads yielded "t(35) = 2.74, p = .01, Cohen's d = 0.65".

> "kind of training had a positive effect on the number of inferences collaborators drew during testing , F(3,32)= 6.03 , p < .01, partial η2 = .36"
