## Evidence

### Fernando Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro, Natacha Boissicat, Fanny Gimbert, Céline Pobel-Burtin, Marie-Caroline Croset, Marie-Line Bosse, and Cécile Nurra. (2024). Involving the Body to Improve Letter Knowledge and Script: An Experimental Study in French Kindergarten. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09923-9

`q4 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Ordinal regression on letters written under dictation (6-point task) in the RCT; in the parsimonious model combining pencil-and-paper and arm training, the arm-body group was "two times less likely to score high on post-tests" (OR = 0.500, d = -.382).

> "Only the effect of arm-body training was significant (b = -0.462, OR = 0.629, d = -.256, p = .043), indicating that students in this group were 59% less likely to score high on the number of correctly written letters under dictation on post-tests"
