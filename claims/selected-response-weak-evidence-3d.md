## Evidence

### Laverty JT 2016

Laverty JT, Underwood SM, Matz RL, Posey LA, Carmel JH, Caballero MD, Fata-Hartley CL, Ebert-May D, Jardeleza SE, Cooper MM. (2016). Characterizing College Science Assessments: The Three-Dimensional Learning Assessment Protocol. PLoS ONE 11(9): e0162333. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0162333

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Design decision reported during protocol development: separate criteria were created for constructed- and selected-response tasks because large-enrollment courses often necessitate multiple-choice formats, while constructed responses provide the strongest evidence of three-dimensional learning.

> "Selected response tasks that meet these criteria do not provide strong evidence of three-dimensional learning, however, we included these criteria because selected responses are widely used in assessments."
