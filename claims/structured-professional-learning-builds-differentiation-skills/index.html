Marks 2021¶

Marks, A., Woolcott, G., & Markopoulos, C. (2021). Differentiating Instruction: Development of a Practice Framework for and with Secondary Mathematics Classroom Teachers. International Electronic Journal of Mathematics Education, 16(3), em0657. https://doi.org/10.29333/iejme/11198

q2 · i? · design · r1

Narrative review attribution: the article reports that professional learning that is "sporadic and disconnected is known to be ineffective in the long term", citing Boyle et al. (2005), Darling-Hammond et al. (2017) and Koellner & Jacobs (2015). No effect size printed.

"professional learning that is sporadic and disconnected is known to be ineffective in the long term, resulting in little change in teachers' beliefs and practices or improvements in student achievement (Boyle et al., 2005; Darling-Hammond et al., 2017; Koellner & Jacobs, 2015)."

Marks 2021 (2)¶

Marks, A., Woolcott, G., & Markopoulos, C. (2021). Differentiating Instruction: Development of a Practice Framework for and with Secondary Mathematics Classroom Teachers. International Electronic Journal of Mathematics Education, 16(3), em0657. https://doi.org/10.29333/iejme/11198

q2 · i? · design · r1

Narrative review attribution: the article reports the Valiandes and Neophytou (2018) study of a professional learning program focussed on designing and applying differentiated instruction, finding skills "could be learned, established, and developed" with positive student outcomes. No effect size printed.

"The study revealed that, through a well-structured training program, the skills and knowledge required to differentiate instruction could be learned, established, and developed, with consequent positive effects on student outcomes."
