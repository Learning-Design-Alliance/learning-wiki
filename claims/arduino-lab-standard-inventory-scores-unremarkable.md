## Evidence

### Andrew J. Haugen and Nathan T. Moore 2014

Andrew J. Haugen and Nathan T. Moore. (2014). A model for including Arduino microcontroller programming in the introductory physics lab. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.7613](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.7613)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' report on their two pilot implementations (Spring 2011 and Spring 2013 University Physics sections) using the modified Arduino lab curriculum. They note that "none of the standard Physics Education pre/post inventories appropriately mapped to the modified curriculum," and the inventory scores showed no notable difference from standard sections. No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "When measured for these sections, MPEX, FCI, CTSR, etc scores were all unremarkable from standard sections of the class."
