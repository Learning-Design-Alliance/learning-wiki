## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The inner-target (unconstrained) group learned faster at training targets while the outer-target (constrained) group generalized better to the probe target (Target Geometry × Target Type interaction: F = 41.9, p < .001, η² = 0.13). [→ Wei Ding 2026](#wei-ding-2026)

## Evidence

### Wei Ding 2026

Wei Ding, Anjuli Niyogi, Jordan A. Taylor & Jonathan S. Tsay. (2026). Hypothesis Testing Governs Strategic Motor Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00428-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Online visuomotor rotation experiment (N = 280) with delayed feedback to bias strategic learning; two-target inner vs. outer geometry groups. The interaction reverses the group advantage: inner group trained faster (Inner: 43.1°±2.4°; Outer: 28.5°±3.2°; p<.001) yet generalized worse (late: Inner: 10.0°±4.2; Outer: 43.4°±3.6).

> "The pattern of group performances reversed in the generalization probes ( Figure 2a, right ) (interaction between Target Geometry x Target Type: F = 41.9, p < .001, η² = 0.13)"
