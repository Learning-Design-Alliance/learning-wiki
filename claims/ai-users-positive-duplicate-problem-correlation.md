### Gerd Kortemeyer and Wolfgang Bauer 2024

Gerd Kortemeyer and Wolfgang Bauer. (2024). Cheat sites and artificial intelligence usage in online introductory physics courses: what is the extent and what effect does it have on assessments? arXiv preprint. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010145](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010145)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Within-cluster correlational analysis for Cluster 4 (n = 42 per Table II); the significant positive correlation "r = 0.31; p = 0.04" links exam AI use with score improvement on the five duplicated problems. Correlational, not causal.

> "For the users who made use of all resources everywhere (Cluster 4), the use of AI during online exams (OnlAI) is significantly positively related to Diff5 (r = 0.31; p = 0.04); this means that AI-usage during online exams is positively correlated with doing better on the final exam instance of duplicate problems"
