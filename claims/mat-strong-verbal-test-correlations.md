## Evidence

### Kuncel et al. 2004

Nathan R. Kuncel, Sarah A. Hezlett, and Deniz S. Ones. (2004). Academic Performance, Career Potential, Creativity, and Job Performance: Can One Construct Predict Them All? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.148

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Psychometric meta-analysis of MAT correlations with other ability tests (Table 1), corrected for range restriction and measurement unreliability. The corrected correlation effect size is ρ = .88 with GRE–V (k = 15, N = 8,328) and ρ = .88 with other verbal tests (k = 23, N = 3,614), described as "equally strong at .88", with SDρ of .06 and .08.

> "The validity of the MAT for predicting GRE–V and other verbal tests were equally strong at .88 (k ⫽ 15, N ⫽ 8,328 and k ⫽ 23, N ⫽ 3,614, respectively) with relatively low values of SDρ (.06 and .08, respectively), which indicate there is little true variability across studies."
