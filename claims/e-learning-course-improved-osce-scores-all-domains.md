## Evidence

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2021

Kim Kyong-Jee, Lee Yeon Ji, Lee Mi Jin, Kim Young Hyo. (2021). e-Learning for enhancement of medical student performance at the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253860

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison of OSCE scores for all Year 3 students (n = 43) at one South Korean medical school, analyzed with the paired t-test. The intervention was a six-month e-learning course combining reflective papers and clinical video analysis. All domain comparisons reached p < .001; "OSCE scores improved significantly after educational intervention in all assessment domains".

> "OSCE scores improved significantly after educational intervention in all assessment domains in the OSCEs and for total scores (p < 0.001)."
