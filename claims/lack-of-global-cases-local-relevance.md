# International students wanted more global cases and closer connections between case analyses and their local work contexts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` International students pointed out a lack of global cases and wanted case analyses more closely related to practical issues faced by their companies. [→ Liu 2010](#liu-2010)

## Evidence

### Liu 2010

Liu, X., Liu, S., Lee, S.-h., & Magjuka, R. J. (2010). Cultural Differences in Online Learning: International Student Perceptions. Educational Technology & Society, 13(3), 177-188. https://www.jstor.org/stable/jeductechsoci.13.3

`q2 · i?`

Interview finding from the case study. Students noted courses focused on U.S. examples and felt some cases were difficult to apply to their countries; they commended one course balancing local and global cases, which also helped U.S. students understand other cultures.

> "Several of the Chinese students mentioned that they would like to see more close relationships between the case analyses and practical issues faced by their companies."
