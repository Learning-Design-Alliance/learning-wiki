# Lexia Reading has potentially positive effects on comprehension

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` The WWC rates Lexia Reading as having potentially positive effects on comprehension, based on a single outcome with an improvement index of +11 percentile points and an effect size of 0.27 that was not statistically significant. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2009](#what-works-clearinghouse-2009)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2009

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Lexia Reading, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/274

`q2 · i1` · `review · r3`

WWC synthesis of comprehension findings from Macaruso and Walker (2008), whose quasi-experimental design met standards with reservations. The Gates-MacGintie Listening Comprehension subtest showed an effect size of 0.27, not statistically significant but substantively important; the report notes the index reflects "only one outcome."

> "The improvement index for comprehension is +11 per - centile points, reflecting only one outcome."
