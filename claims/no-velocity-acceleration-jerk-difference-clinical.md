## Evidence

### Matsumoto Shiro 2024

Matsumoto Shiro, Kawahira Hiroshi, Fukata Kyohei, Doi Yasunori, Kobayashi Nao, Hosoya Yoshinori, Sata Naohiro. (2024). Laparoscopic distal gastrectomy skill evaluation from video: a new artificial intelligence-based instrument identification system. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-63388-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U-test comparisons of tip kinematics from the 18-video clinical dataset; figure captions print velocity 374 vs 350 pixels/sec (P = 1.000), acceleration 135 vs 156 pixels/sec2 (P = 0.436), jerk 111 vs 136 pixels/sec3 (P = 0.340). No effect sizes are printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no significant differences in velocity, acceleration, or jerk at the HARMONIC tip between the two groups (Fig. 4a,b,c)."
