### Urban Eriksson 2014

Urban Eriksson, Cedric Linder, John Airey, Andreas Redfors. (2014). Introducing the anatomy of disciplinary discernment: an example from astronomy. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 2, No. 3, 167-182. [doi:10.30935/scimath/9409](https://doi.org/10.30935/scimath/9409)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of the survey descriptions across educational levels (56 first-year undergraduates, 22 post first-year undergraduates, 11 graduate students, 39 teaching lecturers). Figure 1 shows percentages of participants providing descriptions, illustrating that "less attention gets paid to non-disciplinary characteristics as the education level increases".

> "Under the reasonable assumption that higher educational levels imply more and deeper disciplinary knowledge, the analysis can be seen to show that the more disciplinary knowledge the participants have, the less non-disciplinary discernment they reported."
