LiCalsi, Citkowicz, Friedman, and Brown (2015) compared Massachusetts schools that received School Redesign Grants to schools in the same district that did not. "The study found positive, statistically significant effects of the School Redesign Grants for student achievement in math and English language arts." The report cautions results should be interpreted with caution because baseline similarity was not detailed.
