Evidence¶

Scott 2014¶

Scott, D. and Jelsma, J. (2014). The effectiveness of peer taught group sessions of physiotherapy students within the clinical setting: A quasi-experimental study. Journal of Peer Learning, 7, 105-117. http://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol7/iss1/9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA with post-hoc Tukey test on scores for four health-condition test sections (N = 36). Severe disability had the highest mean (8.1 of 10) versus normal development (5.9), birth defects (6.0), and cerebral palsy (6.6); no effect size is printed.

"There was a significant difference between the scores obtained for the different health conditions, F(3, 105) = 13.93, p < .001). The differences lay between the severe disability and the other three areas ( p < .001 in each case)."
