Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

WWC re-analysis of the Bacon (2003) randomized controlled trial (1,051 students in six elementary schools, grades 3-4) four months after program end. The WWC found the "average effect size was large enough to be considered substantively important (at least 0.25)" — the printed domain average effect size of 0.25 falls in the small range — though not statistically significant; the behavior improvement index averaged +10 percentile points (range 0 to +17).

"Although, as calculated by the WWC, none of these outcomes—individually or averaged—were found to be statistically significant the average effect size was large enough to be considered substantively important (at least 0.25)."
