`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepped-wedge cluster RCT across 12 Scottish maternity units with ≥900 births/year; 87 204 eligible births analysed by logistic mixed regression adjusted for time trend, unit size and pre-study Apgar rate. The ITT analysis showed "a moderate but non-significant reduction" with OR=0.79 (0.63 to 1.01).

> "Once adjusted for a secular time trend, the 'intention-to-treat' analysis indicated a moderate but non-significant reduction in the rate of term babies with an Apgar scores <75mins following PROMPT training (OR=0.79 95%CI(0.63 to 1.01))."
