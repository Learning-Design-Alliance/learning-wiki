## Evidence

### Institute for Effective Education 2016

Institute for Effective Education. (2016). The Teacher Effectiveness Enhancement Programme: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581110

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process evaluation using interviews, focus groups, lesson observations, and online teacher and student surveys in intervention schools, analysed descriptively and via content analysis. Perceptions were positive even though GCSE attainment showed no effect — a contrast the report states explicitly.

> "Interviews and focus groups indicated that most of the teachers in the TEEP schools found the training useful and were keen to implement TEEP methods and principles in their lessons."
