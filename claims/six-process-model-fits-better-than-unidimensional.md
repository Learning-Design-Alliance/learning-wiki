# The six-process structure of inductive reasoning fits fifth graders' figurative-test data better than a unidimensional model

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Confirmatory factor analysis showed the 6-dimensional model defined by Klauer's theory fitted significantly better than the 1-dimensional model (chi-squared difference test chi2 = 2317.05, p < .01). [→ Pásztor 2022](#pasztor-2022)

## Evidence

### Pásztor 2022

Pásztor, Attila, Andrea Magyar, Anita Pásztor-Kovács, and Attila Rausch. 2022. Online Assessment and Game-Based Development of Inductive Reasoning. Journal of Intelligence 10: 59. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence10030059

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

CFA with theta parameterization and WLSMV estimator on the online figurative test data of 267 fifth graders. The 6-dimensional model showed good fit (CFI = .978, RMSEA = .016) and "it fitted signiﬁcantly better than the 1-dimensional model". No effect size is printed.

> "the Chi-squared difference tests showed that it fitted signiﬁcantly better than the 1-dimensional model (χ2 = 2317.05; p < .01). Thus, the six latent factors of inductive reasoning were empirically distinguished"
