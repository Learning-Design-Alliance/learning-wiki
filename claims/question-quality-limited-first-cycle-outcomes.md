# First-cycle underperformance was attributed to low-quality student-generated questions that failed to represent journal article patterns

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the first cycle, student-generated questions were not detailed and failed to represent the fundamental patterns of the journal articles, contributing to unsatisfactory scores. [→ Arifani 2016](#arifani-2016)

## Evidence

### Arifani 2016

Arifani, Y. (2016). The Implementation of Team-Based Discovery Learning to Improve Students’ Ability in Writing Research Proposal. International Education Studies, 9(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n2p111

`q2 · i1`

Authors' reflection on the first cycle of the action research, where only three of six groups performed better and the average score of 75 fell below the success criterion. The questions "were not detail and failed in representing each fundamental pattern from the articles journal."

> "Another obstacle is the quality of questions produced by the students in each team to reflect their curiosity were not detail and failed in representing each fundamental pattern from the articles journal."
