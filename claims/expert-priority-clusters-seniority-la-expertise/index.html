q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cluster profiles from the discussion: cluster 1 (four senior professors) assigned the highest priority to available resources; cluster 2 (three junior experts) to ethics & data governance; cluster 3 (five experts with decision-making experience) to institutional strategic orientation or stakeholder engagement.

"the cluster analysis pointed out that senior experts in LA (cluster 1) found available resources to have the highest influence on the implementation of LA-based interventions."
