## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Cecilia Astolfi 2014

Charles Baily and Cecilia Astolfi. (2014). Student Reasoning About the Divergence of a Vector Field. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.004](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.004)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In-class concept test (Fig. 2) on the divergence of the field due to a point charge, administered during the fourth lecture after a review homework assignment. The printed response distribution was (A) 18%, (B) 0%, (C) 77%, (D) 5% [N = 82].

> "They had seen the differential form of Gauss' law several times in recent lectures, but only 18% of students answered correctly (the divergence is only non-zero at the location of a field source)."
