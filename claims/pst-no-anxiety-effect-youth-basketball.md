### Miçooğullari and Kirazci 2016

Bülent Okan Miçooğullari, Sadettin Kirazci. (2016). Effects of 6 Weeks Psychological Skill Training on Team Cohesion, Self-confidence & Anxiety: A Case of Youth Basketball Players. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(12): 2761-2768. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.041210

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed design ANOVA on STAI trait anxiety scores in the same two-team quasi-experiment; the article reports the time x group interaction, group effect, and time effect all non-significant (p > .05). No equivalence test was reported.

> "Test of time x group inter action did not found to be significant, F (2.83, 96.24) = .80, p > .05 η2 = .023. Test of group effect did not found to be significant, F (1, 34) = .92, p > .05 η2 = .191."
