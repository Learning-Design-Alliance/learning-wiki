### Ali Malik 2024

Ali Malik, Juliette Woodrow, Chao Wang, and Chris Piech. 2024. TeachNow: Enabling Teachers to Provide Spontaneous, Realtime 1:1 Help in Massive Online Courses. In Proceedings of the 2024 Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education V. 1 (ITiCSE 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3649217.3653629

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Student-perspective data gathered through the post-session thank-you mechanism in the six-week CS1 course deployment. The article reports "Nearly 80% of students who had an TeachNow session sent a thank you" and that 68% of those wrote personalised messages describing feeling motivated, supported, and connected to the community.

> "Nearly 80% of students who had an TeachNow session sent a thank you to their volunteer teacher afterwards, and 68% of those students felt compelled to also write a personalised message to the teacher."
