Caldwell 1919¶

Caldwell, Helen Hubbert. (1919). Adult Tests of the Stanford Revision Applied to College Students. The Journal of Educational Psychology, X(10). https://doi.org/10.1037/h0074484

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Error-pattern analysis of verbatim response records in the 48-student study: high-ranked students "missed only those questions in the tests involving auditory memory," and two digit-span tests fall within one group of six, constituting one-third of that group's score.

"A number of students who ranked high in both estimated intelligence and grades, missed only those questions in the tests involving auditory memory, i, e, 1-5, AI 1, II-3 and II-5, thus falling far below in their test rankings.."
