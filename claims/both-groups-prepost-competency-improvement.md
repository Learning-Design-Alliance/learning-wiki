## Evidence

### Budak Vildan 2025

Budak Vildan, Kılıç Hülya Fırat, Cevheroğlu Seda. (2025). The Impact of a Flipped Learning on Nursing Students' Patient Safety Competencies and Satisfaction With the Education Method: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dependent-sample t-tests within each arm of the RCT showed significant pretest-to-posttest improvement on the PSCSE total and all subscales in both the flipped group (e.g., total 152.38 → 183.56) and the traditional group (160.14 → 180.11).

> "the mean posttest scores obtained by both the intervention and control groups from the PSCSE and its subscales of knowledge, skill, and attitude were statistically significantly higher than their pretest scores (p < 0.001 and p < 0.01, respectively)"
