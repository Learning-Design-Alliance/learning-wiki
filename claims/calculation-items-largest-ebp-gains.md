Argimon-Pallàs Josep M, Flores-Mateo Gemma, Jiménez-Villa Josep, Pujol-Ribera Enriqueta. (2011). Effectiveness of a short-course in improving knowledge and skills on evidence-based practice. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-64
