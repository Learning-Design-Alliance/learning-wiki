## Evidence

### Zhang Yanqi 2015

Zhang Yanqi, Zhou Liang, Liu Xiaoyu, Liu Ling, Wu Yazhou, Zhao Zengwei, Yi Dali, Yi Dong. (2015). The Effectiveness of the Problem-Based Learning Teaching Model for Use in Introductory Chinese Undergraduate Medical Courses: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120884

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of nine studies reporting excellence rates (scores ≥80), using a random effects model after moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 68%, P = 0.001). The result shows "a significantly higher course examination excellence rate than the LBL control group" with RR = 1.66. Average excellence rates were 51.6% versus 31.1%.

> "the analytical results reveal that the experimental group produced a significantly higher course examination excellence rate than the LBL control group [RR: 1.66, 95% CI: (1.33, 2.06)]"
