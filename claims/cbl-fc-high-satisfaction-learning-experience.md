### Mao Haijia 2026

Mao Haijia, Zhao Zhenhua, Yang Jianfeng, Zhao Li. (2026). Effectiveness of a case-based learning combined with flipped classroom approach in undergraduate pulmonary imaging education: a prospective randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09208-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-administered 5-point Likert questionnaire completed by the CBL-FC group only; no control-group comparison, so perceptions are descriptive rather than comparative.

> "Learning Interest (Items 1,3,4) demonstrated perfect consensus with mean = 5.00 ± 0; Positive Learning Experiences (Items 2,5,6,7) showed near-ceiling scores (mean = 4.92 ± 0.27); Overall Satisfaction (Items 8,9,10) likewise achieved unanimous maximum ratings (mean = 5.00 ± 0)."
