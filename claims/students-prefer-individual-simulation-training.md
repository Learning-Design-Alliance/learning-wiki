## Evidence

### Abbott Eduardo F. 2021

Abbott Eduardo F., Laack Torrey A., Licatino Lauren K., Wood-Wentz Christina M., Warner Paul A., Torsher Laurence C., Newman James S., Rieck Katie M. (2021). Comparison of dyad versus individual simulation-based training on stress, anxiety, cognitive load, and performance: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02786-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square test of stated training preference on day 4 in the randomized trial; only 23% of individually trained students preferred dyad training. Overall 56% preferred individual training.

> "Preference for dyad training was higher in those randomized to the dyad session (p = 0.03) with 58% of those in the dyad group preferring a paired learning session, whereas only 23% of those in the individual group reported they would have preferred training as a dyad"
