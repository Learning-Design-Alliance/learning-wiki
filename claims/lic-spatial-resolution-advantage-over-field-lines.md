John Belcher and Carolann Koleci. (2007). Using Animated Textures to Visualize Electromagnetic Fields and Energy Flow. http://web.mit.edu/viz/soft/visualizations/DLIC/DLIC.htm [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0802.4034](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0802.4034)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Analytical/conceptual discussion in Section II comparing representation formats. The authors state the LIC method "avoids both of these problems by the use of a texture pattern to indicate the spatial structure of the field at close to the resolution of the display."

> "The use of field lines has the disadvantage that small scale structure in the field can be missed depending on the choice for the spatial distribution of the field lines."
