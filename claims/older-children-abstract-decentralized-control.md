## Evidence

### Chuey 2021

Chuey, A., McCarthy, A., Lockhart, K., Trouche, E., Sheskin, M. & Keil, F. (2021). No guts, no glory: underestimating the benefits of providing children with mechanistic details. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00108-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects logistic regression on decentralized-control expert-detection items, by age group and condition. Neither age group in the delayed test condition performed significantly better than controls. Decentralized control refers to the absence of a central causal agent controlling each part.

> "In all, 8, 9 year-olds, but not 6, 7 year-olds, in the immediate condition performed signi ﬁcantly better on decentralized control items than those in the control condition: [0.32, 0.58] compared with [0.41, 0.66] for 6, 7 year-olds, and [0.54, 0.78] compared with [0.18, 0.41] for 8, 9 year-olds."
