This brief summarizes the 1996-1997 baseline report (Newmann, Lopez, and Bryk, 1998), which found assignments were mostly simple drill and fill-in-the-blank practice; it also reports that "most students were able to com- plete them and demonstrate more complex intellectual performance."

> "However on a more encouraging note, we found that when teachers did assign more demand- ing classroom tasks, most students were able to com- plete them and demonstrate more complex intellectual performance."
