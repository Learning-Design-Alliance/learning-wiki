## Evidence

### Coppens 2019

Coppens, L. C., Hoogerheide, V., Snippe, E. M., Flunger, B., & van Gog, T. (2019). Effects of problem–example and example–problem pairs on gifted and nongifted primary school students' learning. Instructional Science, 47, 279–297. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09484-3

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pre-experiment NFC questionnaire comparison in the 2×2 design: gifted students "reported a higher NFC" than nongifted students, F(1, 122) = 48.87, p < .001, η²p = .29. Posttest competence and autonomy main effects of giftedness were also significant (p = .011 and p = .018).

> "gifted students reported a higher NFC (M = 4.12, SD = 0.65) than nongifted students (M = 3.35, SD = 0.60), F(1, 122) = 48.87, p <.001"
