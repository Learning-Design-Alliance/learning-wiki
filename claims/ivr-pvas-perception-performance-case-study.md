## Evidence

### Liu 2025

Liu, T.-C., & Lin, Y.-C. (2025). Development and implementation of an IVR-based assessment system for student teachers' professional vision. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10463-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Implementation case study in which 24 student teachers completed the IVR-based PVAS perception subtask across seven classroom events, with responses collected via log file and screen recorder. The article reports that 'Most of the participants (15, 62.5%) correctly noted misbehaving students in all the classroom events.' Six participants made 10 or more errors, the highest count being 25.

> "Most of the participants (15, 62.5%) correctly noted misbehaving students in all the classroom events. However, the assessment processes recorded by the system also revealed that the participants made several errors before correctly pointing out the target students"
