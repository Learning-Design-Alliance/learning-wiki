## Evidence

### Zhang et al. 2014

Yi Zhang, Qiang Fei, Munir Quddus, Carolyn Davis. (2014). An examination of the impact of early intervention on learning outcomes of at-risk students. Research in Higher Education Journal, Volume 26. http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate logistic regression from a matched-sample study of 128 at-risk students in 13 multi-sectional classes at an HBCU College of Business, spring 2013, where "The odds of passing the class increase by 2.73 with advising"; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "Column (1) shows that students with advising in the intervention program have a high probability to pass the class. The odds of passing the class increase by 2.73 with advising in the intervention program."

### Zhang et al. 2014 (2)

Yi Zhang, Qiang Fei, Munir Quddus, Carolyn Davis. (2014). An examination of the impact of early intervention on learning outcomes of at-risk students. Research in Higher Education Journal, Volume 26. http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the combined multivariate logistic regression, the advising coefficient was "only significant at about 10% level", and advising reached 5% significance only after gender was omitted due to multicollinearity; no effect size was printed.

> "Column (4) shows that the coefficient of GPA is significant at 5% level and coefficient of advising is only significant at about 10% level while coefficient of gender becomes insignificant."
