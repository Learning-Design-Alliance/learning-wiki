# Copy & paste was the most used interaction strategy with ChatGPT, and 62% of final answers were extracted directly from ChatGPT

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lars Krupp 2024

Lars Krupp, Steffen Steinert, Maximilian Kiefer-Emmanouilidis, Karina E. Avila, Paul Lukowicz, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan K¨uchemann, Jakob Karolus. (2024). Unreflected Acceptance - Investigating the Negative Consequences of ChatGPT-Assisted Problem Solving in Physics Education. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.3233/faia240195](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia240195)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interaction-log analysis of 272 prompts created by the 27 ChatGPT-condition participants, with prompts coded into four interaction types. The article reports "copy & paste was the most used interaction strategy, being used 84 times"; no effect size is printed. Separately, 62% of answers were assigned the strategy 'extracted from ChatGPT'.

> "Overall, copy & paste was the most used interaction strategy, being used 84 times. Preprocessing, the next most common strategy, was used 37 times, followed by post- processing (36) and transformation (16)."
