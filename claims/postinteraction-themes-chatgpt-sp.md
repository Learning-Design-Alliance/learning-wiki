### Cross Joseph 2025

Cross Joseph, Kayalackakom Tarron, Robinson Raymond E, Vaughans Andrea, Sebastian Roopa, Hood Ricardo, Lewis Courtney, Devaraju Sumanth, Honnavar Prasanna, Naik Sheetal, Joseph Jillwin, Anand Nikhilesh, Mohammed Abdalla, Johnson Asjah, Cohen Eliran, Adeniji Teniola, Nnenna Nnaji Aisling, George Julia Elizabeth. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Post-interaction open-ended survey and interviews with the same 9 students, analyzed thematically with inductive category formation. Students reported they "had learnt to improve the output from ChatGPT by tailoring prompts" and noted alignment limits on sensitive topics.

> "Following interaction with ChatGPT, the thematic analysis of student feedback revealed some changes in perceptions (Table 3). While technology limitations were still noted, students mentioned that they had learnt to improve the output from ChatGPT by tailoring prompts. They also reported alignment issues, such as ChatGPT not providing information on sensitive topics such as patient sexual history."
