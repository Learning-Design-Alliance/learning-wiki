Dias Roger Daglius, Scalabrini-Neto Augusto. (2017). Acute stress in residents playing different roles during emergency simulations: a preliminary study. Int J Med Educ 8:239-243. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5929.60f1

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A methodological statement from the Discussion explaining the study's multi-marker design: heart rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, salivary α-amylase, cortisol, interleukin-1β, plus the State-Trait Anxiety Inventory. This is the authors' justification, not a tested result.

> "Since acute stress measurement presents an intricate complexity and there is no gold standard parameter to measure stress response,33 various markers involving cardiovascular, endocrine, immune and psychological parameters were used."
