## Evidence

### Lichtenfeld 2022

Lichtenfeld, S., Pekrun, R., Marsh, H. W., Nett, U., & Reiss, K. (2022). Achievement Emotions and Elementary School Children’s Academic Performance: Longitudinal Models of Developmental Ordering. Journal of Educational Psychology, in press. https://osf.io/ywt8q/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Reciprocal effects models (latent cross-lagged SEM) of emotions and end-of-year grades in the three-wave sample showed significant positive reciprocal effects of enjoyment and grades beyond autoregressive and covariate effects. Initial Year 2 latent correlation r = .26, p < .001. No standardized effect size for the cross-lagged paths is printed here.

> "In support of Hypothesis 2, enjoyment significantly predicted subsequent grades, and grades significantly predicted subsequent enjoyment, in each of the two time intervals."
