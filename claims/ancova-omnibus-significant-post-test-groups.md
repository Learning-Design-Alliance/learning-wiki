## Evidence

### Aldalalah and Fong 2010

Osamah (Mohammad Ameen) Ahmad Aldalalah & Soon Fook Fong. (2010). Effects of Modality and Redundancy Principles on the Learning and Attitude of a Computer-Based Music Theory Lesson among Jordanian Primary Pupils. International Education Studies, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/ies

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus ANCOVA of post-test scores among the three treatment groups (N = 405), using pre-test scores as covariate. The printed result was "F (2,401) = 472.989" with p = 0.000; Table 8 also reports R Squared = .819.

> "Table 8 indicated the results of ANCOVA test of statistical significance on the differences observed in the mean score of the post-test for the various treatment groups with F (2,401) = 472.989, Mean Square = 2600.673 and p = 0.000."
