# Online survey respondents significantly preferred the question and answer label format over the fun fact label as more interesting/engaging

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The question and answer label format was chosen as more interesting/engaging significantly more often than the fun fact label (59.2% vs 40.8%). [→ Lisa F. Smith 2013](#lisa-f-smith-2013)

## Evidence

### Lisa F. Smith 2013

Lisa F. Smith, Kimberly K. Arcand, Jeffrey K. Smith, Randall K. Smith, Jay Bookbinder, Megan Watzke. (2013). Examining Perceptions Of Astronomy Images Across Mobile Platforms. [doi:10.22323/2.13020201](https://doi.org/10.22323/2.13020201)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online survey with 2183 respondents, each randomly assigned one of 12 images and shown both label types; Pearson chi-square compared the two formats. The question format was chosen by 59.2% versus 40.8% for the fun fact, "at p <.001". No effect size is reported.

> "For the item, Which one do you think is more interesting/engaging?, the question and answer label format (59.2%) was preferred significantly more than the fun fact label (40.8%), at p <.001."
