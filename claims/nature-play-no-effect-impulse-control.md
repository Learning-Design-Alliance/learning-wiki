`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports this null result from Amicone et al. (2018, Study 1), a quasi-experiment in which the Go/No-Go task measured impulse control after green structured play. No effect size is printed; the finding is a non-significant test, not a measured small effect.

> "They found no significant positive effect in middle-class public school children in Rome after playtime in a school garden compared with a built playground."
