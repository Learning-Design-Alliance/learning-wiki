# Language and early writing interventions showed larger effects on researcher-developed than standardized outcome measures, while phonological awareness, print knowledge, and decoding effects were comparable across measure types

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The weighted effect size for language was significantly larger on researcher-developed measures than standardized ones (0.43 versus 0.12). [→ Sarah Herrera 2021](#sarah-herrera-2021)
`q3 i?` Phonological awareness effects were statistically comparable across measure types (0.43 researcher-developed versus 0.28 standardized). [→ Sarah Herrera 2021 (2)](#sarah-herrera-2021-2)
`q3 i?` Early writing improved significantly on researcher-developed measures (0.36) but the standardized effect (0.23) was not statistically significant. [→ Sarah Herrera 2021 (3)](#sarah-herrera-2021-3)

## Evidence

### Sarah Herrera 2021

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Figure 4 comparison across 74 language-teaching interventions (31 researcher-developed, 53 standardized measures). Weighted effect sizes were 0.43 for researcher-developed versus 0.12 for standardized measures, a statistically significant difference the review attributes to researcher-developed measures representing skills more similar to those taught.

> "The weighted effect size was significantly larger for researcher-developed outcome measures than for standardized ones (0.43 versus 0.12; figure 4; see also table B7 in appendix B)"

### Sarah Herrera 2021 (2)

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Comparison across 36 phonological awareness interventions in table B10. The review reports the difference "was not statistically significant," suggesting phonological awareness interventions improve performance regardless of outcome measure type.

> "the weighted effect size was 0.43 for researcher-developed outcome measures and 0.28 for standardized outcome measures; the difference was not statistically significant"

### Sarah Herrera 2021 (3)

Sarah Herrera, Beth M. Phillips, Yi-Chieh Newton, Jennifer L. Dombek, and James A. Hernandez. (2021). Effectiveness of Early Literacy Instruction: Summary of 20 Years of Research. Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast, REL 2021-084. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Comparison across 11 early writing interventions (7 researcher-developed, 4 standardized measures) in figure 9. The researcher-developed weighted effect size was 0.36, equivalent to a 14 percentile point increase; the standardized effect of 0.23 was not statistically significant.

> "interventions that taught early writing significantly improved early writing performance on researcher-developed outcome measures, among the 11 interventions evaluated in high-quality impact studies and included at least one effect size estimate (weighted effect size of 0.36; figure 9; see also table B18"
