## Evidence

### Norris E. 2015

Norris E., Shelton N., Dunsmuir S., Duke-Williams O., Stamatakis E. (2015). Teacher and pupil perspectives on the use of Virtual Field Trips as physically active lessons. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1698-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of all 12 teacher interviews found teachers suggested VFTs as starters, plenaries and brain breaks, and proposed topics from Victorian history to PE technique demonstration; some (n=5, 41.7%) called them "cross-curricular".

> "All teachers provided a variety of creative ways that VFTs could be used in their teaching. These ranged from "starters or plenaries' and as brain breaks to increase children's attention in the morning or after lunch."
