## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For both monopoles and dipoles, increasing speed v preferentially excludes magnetic flux from the region z < 0 ahead of the moving source, while field lines behind are hooked back into the direction of motion. [→ Liu 2006](#liu-2006)

## Evidence

### Liu 2006

Liu, Y., & Belcher, J. W. (2006). Magnetic Flux Diffusion and Expulsion with Thin Conducting Sheets. http://web.mit.edu/viz/soft/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.0182](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.0182)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical solutions visualized with field lines and line integral convolution (Figs. 4 and 5) show the asymmetry; the authors report the pattern from their visualizations rather than from a measured experiment. The finding is stated as "preferentially excluded from the region z < 0".

> "From these visualizations, we find that for both monopoles and dipoles, as the speed v increases, the magnetic flux is preferentially excluded from the region z < 0 as compared to the region behind the moving monopole or dipole."
