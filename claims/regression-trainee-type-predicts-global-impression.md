### Liu B. 2019

Liu B., Hunt L. M., Lonsdale R. J., Narula H. S., Mansaray A. F., Bundu I. and Bolkan H. A. (2019). Comparison of surgical skill acquisition by UK surgical trainees and Sierra Leonean associate clinicians in a task‐sharing programme. BJS Open 3(2): 218–223. https://doi.org/10.1002/bjs5.50122

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression (Table 4) on the blinded video assessments found trainee type independently predicted Global Impression Score (Wald χ2 = 7·63, P = 0·006), and trainee type, age and experience predicted assessors' estimated years of experience; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Regression analysis indicated that being a Sierra Leonean trainee (P = 0·001), older age (P = 0·011) and having more actual surgical experience (P = 0·004) all predicted a higher estimation of length of time in surgical training. Only being a Sierra Leonean trainee independently predicted a higher Global Impression Score (P = 0·006) (Table  4)."
