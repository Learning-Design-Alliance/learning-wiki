## Evidence

### Rod Ellis 1987

Rod Ellis. (1987). Interlanguage Variability in Narrative Discourse: Style Shifting in the Use of the Past Tense. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 9, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100006483

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the study of 17 intermediate L2 learners under three planning conditions, irregular past tense accuracy was largely stable across conditions; the abstract reports that "Little style shifting took place in irregular past".

> "Little style shifting took place in irregular past, whereas style shifting for past copula occurred only between planned speech and unplanned speech."
