## Evidence

### Traxler 2018

Traxler, A., Henderson, R., Stewart, J., Stewart, G., Papak, A., & Lindell, R. (2018). Gender Fairness within the Force Concept Inventory. arXiv. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010103](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010103)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mantel-Haenszel DIF analysis of Sample 1 posttest, stratified by total test score, found "Eight FCI items demonstrated large DIF"; seven additional questions showed small to moderate DIF. Lord's statistic agreed on the high-DIF classification except Item 29, also flagged favoring women.

> "Eight FCI items demonstrated large DIF (9, 12, 14, 15, 21, 22, 23, 27), where 9 and 15 were biased in favor of female students."
