## Evidence

### Cuerva Marcos Javier 2026

Cuerva Marcos Javier, Durá Candela, Aramendia Andrés, Cortes Marta, Lopez Francisco, Bartha José Luis. (2026). Vacuum 5-step technique versus OdonAssist: Which is easier to learn for professionals without experience in assisted vaginal births? A simulation study. International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70836

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Case-level analysis of 123 simulated AVBs (74 vacuum, 49 OdonAssist); the article states that mixed-effects analyses accounting for repeated procedures "showed that procedure duration was not independently associated with technique".

> "vacuum AVBs were completed in a shorter time than OdonAssist AVBs, with a procedure duration of 96 (IQR 64–121) versus 117 (IQR 97–137) seconds"
