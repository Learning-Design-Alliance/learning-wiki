## Evidence

### Iwatani 2023

Iwatani, E., Hardy, A., Means, B., Daal, S., & Wei, X. (2023). Evaluation of World History Project. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/199

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student pre- and post-surveys (approximately 293 students with fall-spring overlap) and two focus groups in the 2022-23 comparison showed the negative trend; no effect size was printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "Student surveys and focus groups showed a somewhat negative trend for students who learned from World History Project . For example, they were more likely in the spring to describe their class as "boring," and less likely to report that they want to know what lies behind the story"
