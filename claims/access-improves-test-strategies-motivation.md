# ACCESS produces significantly greater improvement in test strategies and motivation than delayed treatment among college students with ADHD

## Evidence

### Eddy et al. 2021

Laura D. Eddy, Arthur D. Anastopoulos, Melissa R. Dvorsky, Paul J. Silvia, Jeffrey D. Labban & Joshua M. Langberg (2021). An RCT of a CBT Intervention for Emerging Adults with ADHD Attending College: Functional Outcomes. Journal of Clinical Child & Adolescent Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/15374416.2020.1867989

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Latent growth curve analysis of the LASSI-2 Test Strategies scale in the multi-site RCT found a medium between-condition effect ("d = .58 [.32, .83]") favoring immediate ACCESS over delayed treatment.

> "For testing strategies, the immediate ACCESS (b = 4.89, SE = .57, p < .001) and the DTC (b = 1.61, SE = .46, p = .001) condition both increased over time, but the rate of change was significantly higher in the ACCESS group, Wald (1) = 20.97, p < .001, d = .58 [.32, .83]."

### Eddy et al. 2021 (2)

Laura D. Eddy, Arthur D. Anastopoulos, Melissa R. Dvorsky, Paul J. Silvia, Jeffrey D. Labban & Joshua M. Langberg (2021). An RCT of a CBT Intervention for Emerging Adults with ADHD Attending College: Functional Outcomes. Journal of Clinical Child & Adolescent Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/15374416.2020.1867989

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Latent growth curve analysis of LASSI-2 Motivation in the same RCT showed a small-to-medium between-condition effect ("d = .37 [.12, .62]") favoring immediate ACCESS.

> "the immediate ACCESS (b = 3.59, SE = .62, p < .001) and the DTC (b = 1.23, SE = .53, p = .021) conditions both increased significantly over time, but the rate of change in motivation was significantly higher in the ACCESS group, Wald (1) = 8.39, p = .004, d = .37 [.12, .62]."
