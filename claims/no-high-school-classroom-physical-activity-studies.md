## Evidence

### Fenesi B. 2022

Fenesi B., Graham J.D., Crichton M., Ogrodnik M., Skinner J. (2022). Physical Activity in High School Classrooms: A Promising Avenue for Future Research. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19020688

`q3 · i?` · `review · r1`

The essay reports the Masini et al. (2019) review of 22 active-break studies, in which "all studies occurred in elementary schools", and similarly cites Bedard et al. (2019) finding none of 25 physically active lesson studies in high schools.

> "Masini et al. (2019) showed that of 22 studies examining the impact of classroom-based physical activity breaks (i.e., active breaks spaced in between the delivery of academic content), all studies occurred in elementary schools."
