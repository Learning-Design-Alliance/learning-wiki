# Convergent and divergent tasks do not differ significantly in improving EFL learners' motivation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Convergent and divergent task instruction showed no significant difference in improving EFL learners' motivation as measured by the AMTB posttest. [→ Marashi 2015](#marashi-2015)

## Evidence

### Marashi 2015

Marashi, H., & Tahan-Shizari, P. (2015). Using convergent and divergent tasks to improve writing and language learning motivation. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 3(1), 99-117. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on AMTB posttest scores of the same 60 participants found no significant difference between the convergent and divergent groups (p = 0.12). The article reports that "the two treatments were not significantly different in terms of improving the learners' motivation." Motivation increased in both groups from pretest to posttest.

> "The second null hypothesis was not rejected, however, meaning that the two treatments were not significantly different in terms of improving the learners' motivation."
