## Evidence

### Dai Dongjun 2026

Dai Dongjun, Fan Yuzhi, Zhang Wenbao, Wei Qichun. (2026). The effect of narrative medicine-based education on academic performance and humanistic communication in residency training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53552-x

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 6 randomized controlled studies (147 narrative medicine, 150 LBL residents) pooled with the Hedges' g approach. The pooled effect size SMD = 0.98 (95% CI 0.42 to 1.53) was significant at P = 0.001, with high heterogeneity (I2 = 75.9%).

> "the narrative medicine education was associated with the improvement of procedural skill scores (6 studies with 147 residents in narrative medicine group and 150 in LBL group, standardized mean differences [SMD] = 0.98, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.42 to 1.53, P = 0.001; I2 = 75.9%)"
