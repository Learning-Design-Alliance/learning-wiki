Evidence¶

Spitzer 2025¶

Spitzer, M. W. H., Moeller, K., Richter, E., Frede, L., & Bardach, L. (2025). A red button does it all: A red button nudges students to close their knowledge gaps in an intelligent tutoring system. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102181. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102181

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Exploratory linear regression with end performance (last 10 problem sets) as dependent variable, baseline performance (first 10 problem sets) and gap closing in between as predictors. Table 1 reports Knowledge gaps closed b = 0.11, t = 24.67, p < .001; adjusted R2 = 0.209. No standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

"A significant effect of closing knowledge gaps indicated that closing knowledge gaps after the baseline period and before the end period had a positive effect on students' performance during the end period."
