## Evidence

### Zheng 2025

Zheng, Y. S., Goldwater, M., & Gentner, D. (2025). Structural Alignment and Linguistic Contrast Help Children Learn a Key Principle of Spatial Construction. Cognitive Science, 49(12), e70149. https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.70149

Experiment 2 Day 2 repair task after the delay, logistic regression showed significant alignment (odds ratio = 2.47, p < .001) and language (odds ratio = 1.76, p = .012) effects; label children scored "M Label = 0.78" versus 0.57 for controls.
