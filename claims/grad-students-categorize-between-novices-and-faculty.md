## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Categorization of problems to assess and improve proficiency as teachers and learners. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07677](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07677)

`q2 · i?`

Histogram analysis (Fig. 1) comparing the average performance of 21 graduate students at the end of a TA training course with 7 physics faculty and 180 introductory students categorizing the same 25 problems from their own point of view. The article reports graduate students showed "a higher level of expertise in introductory mechanics" than introductory students, and were more likely to group the questions based on physical principles, while remaining below faculty.
