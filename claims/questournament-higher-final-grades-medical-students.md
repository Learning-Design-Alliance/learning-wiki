## Evidence

### Corell A 2018

Corell A, Regueras LM, Verdú E, Verdú MJ, de Castro JP. (2018). Effects of competitive learning tools on medical students: A case study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0194096

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental control group pretest-posttest study of a Human Immunology course (n = 285; experimental n = 172, control n = 113), analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U-test. The article reports the grade difference was "significantly different between the two groups (p < 0.001)". Table 3 prints means of 8.68 (SD 0.81) experimental vs 8.13 (SD 1.77) control.

> "the students’ final score grades in Human Immunology were significantly different between the two groups (p < 0.001), indicating that the hypothesis H1 was supported as the outcomes of the experimental group students were significantly higher"
