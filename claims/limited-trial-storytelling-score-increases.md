Limited trial of the prototype in two schools, reported as pre-test to post-test mean increases with no control group and no test statistics; the article infers "the teaching model prototype increased the students’ story-telling ability."

> "The result of the limited trial in SDN Mangunsari 06 showed an increase in the test mean score, from 13.62 in the pre-test to 21.67 in the post-test. Similarly, the trial in SDN Tegalrejo 05 also showed an increase in the mean score, from 16.27 in the pre-test to 19.77 in the post-test."
