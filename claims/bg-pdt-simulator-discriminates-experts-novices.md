## Evidence

### Kattan Eduardo MD 2019

Kattan Eduardo MD, MMEd; Vera Magdalena MD; Putz Francisca RN; Corvetto Marcia MD; De la Fuente Rene MD, MScEng; Bravo Sebastian MD. (2019). Design and Evaluation of a Low-Cost Bronchoscopy-Guided Percutaneous Dilatational Tracheostomy Simulator. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000399

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Novice-expert comparison in the simulator with 9 novices and 8 experts, using nonparametric tests. Experts outperformed novices on global and first attempt success, time, and movement economy; the article reports these as statistically significant differences with the printed P values.

> "Experts had higher global success rate (100% vs. 44%, P = 0.03), first attempt success rate (100% vs. 33%, P = 0.01), performed the procedure in less time [273 (262–302) seconds vs. 508 (382–630) seconds, P = 0.006] and with a higher movement economy"
