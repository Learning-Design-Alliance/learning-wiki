Randomized evaluation in Philadelphia elementary and secondary schools with a total N of 28,080; at-risk students were randomly assigned to treatment or control. The summary reports "Effects on absences were statistically significant (ES=+0.19)" — a printed effect size of ES=+0.19.

> "Rogers & Feller (2018) carried out a randomized evaluation of the Absence Reports intervention in elementary and secondary schools in Philadelphia. The total N was 28,080; 53% of students were African American, 19% Hispanic, and 14% White. Students at risk for absenteeism were randomly assigned to treatment or control. Effects on absences were statistically significant (ES=+0.19)."
