Computational text analysis of 69 open-access Scopus journal articles and conference papers on ChatGPT in programming education, using term frequency, bigram/trigram pattern analysis, and LDA configured with four topics. The chapter reports that topic modeling "produced four themes that describe how research conceptualizes the use of ChatGPT in programming education."

"The LDA topic modeling analysis produced four themes that describe how research conceptualizes the use of ChatGPT in programming education (Table 3)."
