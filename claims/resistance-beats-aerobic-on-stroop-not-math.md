# Resistance exercise outperforms aerobic exercise on Stroop Test cognition but not on math performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Stroop Dot, Word, and Color performance was significantly better after resistance exercise than after aerobic exercise (η2 = 0.294, 0.192, 0.245), while math performance did not differ between the two exercise modalities. [→ Harveson Andrew T. 2019](#harveson-andrew-t-2019)

## Evidence

### Harveson Andrew T. 2019

Harveson Andrew T., Hannon James C., Brusseau Timothy A., Podlog Leslie, Papadopoulos Charilaos, Hall Morgan S., Celeste EvaRose. (2019). Acute Exercise and Academic Achievement in Middle School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16193527

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within the randomized crossover experiment, repeated measures ANOVA compared the two exercise modalities on Stroop performance. All three Stroop elements favored resistance over aerobic exercise with large η2 values (0.294, 0.192, 0.245); the article reports "Math test performance was not statistically different between the aerobic and resistance exercise treatments (F1,62 = 0.214, p = 0.645)".

> "Significant differences in mean scores were also found between resistance exercise and aerobic exercise in the Stroop Dot test (F1,61 = 25.82, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.294), Stroop Word test (F1,62 = 14.73, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.192), and Stroop Color test (F1,62 = 20.14, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.245)."
