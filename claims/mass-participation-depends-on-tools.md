# Mass participation formats in online communities depend on the tools used (Danmaku versus discussion board)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In a MOOC on basic maths, forms of mass participation differed by tool: indirect crowd speech in the Danmaku environment versus direct communication and collaboration in discussion board threads. [→ Baker 2024](#baker-2024)

## Evidence

### Baker 2024

Baker, M., Reimann, P. (2024). CSCL: a learning and collaboration science?. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09433-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The editorial attributes this finding, quoting the paper's reported contrast, to Yang (2024)'s study of a mass-collaboration MOOC environment; the review reports it in summary, not from the primary data.

> "It was found that the form of mass participation depended on the tools used — indirect crowd speech in the Danmaku environment versus direct communication and collaboration within threads of the discussion board."
