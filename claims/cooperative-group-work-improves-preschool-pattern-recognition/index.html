Evidence¶

Tarım 2015¶

Kamuran Tarım. (2015). Effects of Cooperative Group Work Activities on Pre-school Children’s Pattern Recognition Skills. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.1.0086

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two experimental cooperative-learning groups (n = 19 and 18) against an individually taught control group (n = 20) in two private nursery schools. Post-test ANOVA was significant, F(2,54) = 6.68, p < .05, with Scheffe tests showing "the experimental groups were more successful than the control groups"; the article reports effect sizes of d = 1.02 and d = .93 for the two experimental groups versus control.

"The paired comparison (Scheffe) conducted to examine the direction of the difference showed that the experimental groups were more successful than the control groups. In other words, the cooperative learning method was found to be more effective in developing the children’s pattern recognition skills."
