El-Senousy 2017¶

El-Senousy, H. & Alquda, J. (2017). The effect of Flipped Classroom Strategy using Blackboard Mash-Up Tools in enhancing achievement and Self–Regulated Learning skills of university students. World Journal on Educational Technology: Current Issues, 9(3), 144-157. https://sproc.org/ojs/index.php/wjet

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Semi-experimental two-group comparison of 30 experimental and 30 control students in the Comp101N course. Post-test achievement means were 8.23 (control) versus 12.08 (experimental); "The t-calculated (7.2) is more than t-critical at 5% level of significance", so the first hypothesis was accepted.
