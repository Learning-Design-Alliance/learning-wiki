## Evidence

### Chu Tsung-Lan 2019

Chu Tsung-Lan, Wang Jeng, Monrouxe Lynn, Sung Yu-Chih, Kuo Chen-li, Ho Lun-Hui, Lin Yueh-E. (2019). The effects of the flipped classroom in teaching evidence based nursing: A quasi-experimental study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210606

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-month follow-up comparison in the same 151-nurse quasi-experiment. The experimental group's knowledge score dropped from 80.92 to 74.46 while the control group's dropped from 75.07 to 74.89, yielding "no significant differences in both groups."

> "However, T2 scores showed no significant differences in both groups (knowledge p = 0.893; self-efficacy p = 0.097) (Table 2)."
