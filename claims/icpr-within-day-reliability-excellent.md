## Evidence

### Almeida Debora 2020

Almeida Debora, Clark Carol, Jones Michael, McConnell Phillip, Williams Jonathan. (2020). Consistency and variability in human performance during simulate infant CPR: a reliability study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-day reliability analysis (ICC model 2-way mixed, absolute agreement) of 27 healthcare students performing three 2-min iCPR trials on an instrumented infant manikin immediately after BLS training. Table 1 reports ICCs of .95 (rate), .96 (depth), .94 (leaning) and .95 (duty cycle); the study reports "excellent consistency in repeated within-day performance".

> "A high degree of reliability was found for repeated iCPR for every variable with excellent ICCs and narrow confidence intervals. SEM values were low, demonstrating excellent consistency in repeated within-day performance."
