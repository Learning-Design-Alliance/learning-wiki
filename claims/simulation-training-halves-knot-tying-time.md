# Two-day simulation-based laparoscopic suturing training halved the average time residents took to tie an intracorporeal knot

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After the two-day course, average knot-tying time fell from 205.25 s at pretest to 102.4 s, and residents who could not tie a knot at pretest reached results similar to those who could. [→ Asencio FA 2018](#asencio-fa-2018)

## Evidence

### Asencio FA 2018

Asencio FA, Ribeiro HASA, Romeo A, Wattiez A, Ribeiro PAGA. (2018). The Effect on Performance Time and Quality of the Knots after Mono or Bimanual Training of Laparoscopic Intracorporeal Knot Tying according to the Gladiator Rule Technique. Rev Bras Ginecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0038-1649494

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison within the 41-resident training course using the Wilcoxon test for pre-post continuous variables; the authors report a significant improvement in tying time after supervised training, with no effect size printed.

> "The average time to perform this exercise in the pre-test was of 205.25 s, and after the end of the course, the time was halved: 102.4 seconds."
