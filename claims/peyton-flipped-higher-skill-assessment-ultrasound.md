## Evidence

### Wang Jiajia 2024

Wang Jiajia, Zhan Yunyun, Sun Biyun, Bi Yu, Li Rubing, Jiang Fan, Peng Mei. (2024). Flipped classroom-based application of Peyton’s four-step approach in standardized training of ultrasound residents for thyroid and cervical lymph node zoning. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.18633

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 66 residents split into control (n = 33) and observation (n = 33) groups; skills assessment required a comprehensive thyroid and lymph node scan within 5 min scored out of 100. Observation group scored "(87.64 ± 3.72)" vs control "(80.48 ± 5.92)"; Table 2 reports t = 5.877, P < 0.001.

> "Assessment results demonstrated higher scores in skill operation and clinical case analysis for the observation group compared to the control group [(87.64 ± 3.72) vs. (80.48 ± 5.92) points, (87.94 ± 4.46) vs. (82.85 ± 4.24) points]."
