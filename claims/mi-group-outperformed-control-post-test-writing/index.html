Evidence¶

Zennure Elgün Gündüz 2016¶

Zennure Elgün Gündüz, İsmail Doğan Ünal. (2016). Effects of Multiple Intelligences Activities on Writing Skill Development in an EFL Context. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(7): 1687-1697. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040722

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test essay scores of the experimental (M = 51.80, SD = 16.00) and control (M = 31.20, SD = 8.69) groups, each N = 25. The article reports the difference "is in favor of the experimental group"; no effect size is printed.

"there was a significant difference between the post -test scores of the control group and those of the experimental group on writing test (t = 5.65, df = 48, p≤ 0.00)"
