Related Claims¶

  • Generating or comparing keywords had no substantial effect on posttest learning performance
  • Comprehension judgments and performance predictions correlate highly, supporting the validity of the study's z-standardized bias measure to some extent — related
  • Generating keywords after a delay increases relative judgment accuracy compared with no keyword generation, replicating the keyword effect with a new sample and new materials — related
  • Comparing self-generated keywords with expert keywords increases bias, specifically underconfidence — related
  • Comprehension-test expectancy tended to be more effective than delayed-keywords listing and rereading, though interventions did not substantially differ overall — related
  • Five of seven situation-model interventions significantly improved relative metacomprehension accuracy, concept mapping only marginally (p = .097), and self-explaining not significantly — related
