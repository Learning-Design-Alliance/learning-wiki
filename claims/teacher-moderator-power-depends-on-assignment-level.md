# Teacher-level moderator power depends on whether teachers are the unit of assignment: MDESDTCHR is .37 in two-level CRTs but .21 in three-level CRTs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In two-level CRTs with teachers as the top level, mean MDESDTCHR is .37 (SD = .10), larger than the MDES; in three-level CRTs, mean MDESDTCHR is .21 (SD = .07), and studies with many teachers per school can detect smaller teacher-level moderator effects than main effects. [→ Jessaca Spybrook 2020](#jessaca-spybrook-2020)

Power analysis of the 10 three-level CRTs for a teacher-level (middle-level) moderator, where between-school variance cancels out and total teachers is the critical sample size. Study 2, with 20 teachers per each of 30 schools, had MDESDTCHR ranging approximately .11 to .15.

> "The mean MDESDTCHR(3LCRT) value is .21 (SD = .07). From Figure 4, we can see that in some cases MDESD TCHR(3LCRT) is smaller than the MDES."
