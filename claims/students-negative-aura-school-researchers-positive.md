Network analysis of the students' forma mentis network (159 Italian final-year high school students) shows school labelled negative with a mostly negative aura; the article reports the researchers' perception of school as positive and devoid of anxiety-eliciting associations.

> "The overall mindset of the 159 interviewed students identiﬁes “school” as a negative concept, surrounded by a mostly negative emotional aura."
