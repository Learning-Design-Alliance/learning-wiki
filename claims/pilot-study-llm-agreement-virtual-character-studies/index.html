Schroeder 2025¶

Schroeder, N. L., Jaldi, C. D., Zhang, S., & Yamoah, K. (2025). AI-Assisted Data Extraction for Systematic Reviews in Education. https://osf.io/nraxf/?view_only=879654690be249cc9237525ed7f86ec2

q1 · i? · design · r2

Small pilot study in Spring 2024 using web-based chat interfaces with eight sources (four journal articles, four conference proceedings) and 24 variables. "Claude was the most accurate LLM, showing 93.06% agreement with the human coder". The authors state prompts were not optimized.

"Compared to the human coder, we found that Claude was the most accurate LLM, showing 93.06% agreement with the human coder. GPT-4 was also quite accurate (91.67% agreement), and ChatPDF was reasonably accurate (75% agreement)."
