# In the online sections, exam scores correlated moderately with both online discussion participation points (r = 0.36) and other learning activities (r = 0.37)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Exam scores showed a moderate association with online discussion participation points (r = 0.36, p = .002) and a similar moderate association with all other non-discussion learning activities (r = 0.37, p = .002). [→ Jorczak and Dupuis 2011](#jorczak-and-dupuis-2011)

## Evidence

### Jorczak and Dupuis 2011

Robert L. Jorczak, Danielle N. Dupuis. (2011). Differences in Classroom Versus Online Exam Performance Due to Asynchronous Discussion. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Differences+in+Classroom+Versus+Online+Exam+Performance+Due+to+Asynchronous+Discussion

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pearson product moment correlations computed within the online sections (n = 69) between exam scores and learning-activity measures. The printed effect sizes are r = 0.36 (p = .002) for discussion participation and r = 0.37 (p = .002) for other activities, both called moderate associations.

> "the correlation between exam scores and online discussion participation points was 0.36 ( p = . 002), while the correlation between exam scores and all other (non -discussion) learning activities was 0.37 ( p = .002)."
