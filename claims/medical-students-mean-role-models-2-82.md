`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey analysis of role model counts among medical students with role models (valid n = 260). The article reports "most had two role models (mean = 2.82, SD = 1.94, mode = 2, median = 2.00)"; a Kruskal–Wallis test across faculties was significant (H(df = 6) = 19.88, p 0.003).
