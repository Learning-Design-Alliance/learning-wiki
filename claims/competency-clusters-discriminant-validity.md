`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Discriminant validity analysis reported with results in Table 6: questions in the competency areas were analysed after principal component analysis of the pilot data, with cross-validation by the leave-one-out method. The authors state the clusters "are indeed reflected in the empirical results".

> "It has already been possible to demonstrate using the discriminant validity analysis of the five competency clusters that these clusters are indeed reflected in the empirical results and that differentiation according to these groups makes sense when scoring and providing feedback to students and medical schools [32] (see Table 6 (Tab. 6))."
