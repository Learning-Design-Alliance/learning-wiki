## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yang Z 2025

Yang Z, Yao Z, Tasmin M, Vashisht P, Jang WS, Ouyang F, Wang B, McManus D, Berlowitz D, Yu H. (2025). Unveiling GPT-4V's hidden challenges behind high accuracy on USMLE questions: Observational Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/65146

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of GPT-4V on 150 AMBOSS image questions across five difficulty levels, with and without expert-written hints, compared against medical students' performance; unpaired chi-square tests at significance level 0.05. The quote reports the difficulty trend and hint plateau.

> "Figure 2 shows a decreasing trend in GPT-4V's performance in the AMBOSS data set when the difficulty of questions increased (P=.04) without a hint. However, with the hint, the performance of GPT-4V plateaued across 5 difficulty levels."
