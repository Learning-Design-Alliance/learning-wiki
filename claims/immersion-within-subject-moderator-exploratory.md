`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Within-subject meta-regression of immersion (F = 13.01, p < .01). The no-technology category showed g = 2.05 but was based on only one effect size; the authors call the result unstable and not readily generalizable.

> "the no technology category showed the largest effect ( g = 2.05, 95% CI [1.07, 3.02]) but was based on only one effect size."
