The effect of AI-enabled virtual patient simulation on training outcomes and insecurities in psychotherapy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09917-x

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative inductive content analysis of participants' open-ended responses in the VP group. Participants highlighted "a truly safe setting" to practice communication skills, while noting avatar limitations: "The patient looks a little bit creepy" (P7).

> "the qualitative findings indicate that the VP system was perceived as an interesting and effective educational tool, while also revealing areas with potential for improvement."
