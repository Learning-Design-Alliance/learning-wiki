The review reports a data-driven clustering study (attributed to Bathelt et al.) grouping children on reading, phonological processing, and executive functioning measures: "within a group of 442 struggling learners, three distinct subgroups were found" — elevated inattention/hyperactivity, learning problems, and aggressive behavior with disturbed peer relations. Groups differed in structural connectivity; aggression and peer problems loaded on prefrontal–striatal integration.
