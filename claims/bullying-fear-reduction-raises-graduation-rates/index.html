Kiguel 2023¶

Kiguel, S. (2023). Moving the needle on bullying and educational outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.proquest.com/dissertations-theses/docview/30488487

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental analysis linking school-level bullying-reduction effects to administrative graduation records for 78,951 first-time ninth-graders in Chicago Public Schools. Students at 85th-percentile schools "graduated high school at rates that were 4 percentage points higher vs. the 15th percentile."

"Students who attended a school at the 85th percentile of bullying reduction graduated high school at rates that were 4 percentage points higher vs. the 15th percentile."
