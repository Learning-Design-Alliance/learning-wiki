## Evidence

### Lee Jea Woog 2022

Lee Jea Woog, Park Sung Je, Kim Soyeon, Chung Un Sun, Han Doug Hyun. (2022). The Association Between Temperament and Characteristics, Smartphone App Use Patterns and Academic Performance of University Students. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2022.37.e143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of TCI scores between elite (n = 37) and general (n = 114) sports majors in the four-week observational study. "elite students scored higher on novelty seeking" on all five dimensions, with the printed z and P values; no effect sizes were reported for these comparisons.

> "Compared to general students, elite students scored higher on novelty seeking (z = 3.35, P = 0.007), reward dependence (z = 3.77, P = 0.006), persistence (z = 3.81, P = 0.006), self-directedness (z = 3.04, P = 0.009), and self-transcendence (z = 3.12, P = 0.008)"
