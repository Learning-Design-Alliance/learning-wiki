Evidence¶

Song Hongfeng 2026¶

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

q3 · i? · associational · r2

R3STEP multinomial logistic regressions on the three-wave sample (N = 379) estimating odds of profile membership per one-SD increase in each Wave 1 resource; learning agility showed the clearest differentiation across profiles.

"Higher learning agility was associated with greater odds of Positive Empowerment relative to Anxious Acceptance (OR = 1.899) and Low-Perception Detached (OR = 4.873)."

Song Hongfeng 2026 (2)¶

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

q3 · i? · associational · r2

R3STEP results (Table 5) show a scope limit: digital literacy failed to separate Anxious Acceptance from Low-Perception Detached while the other resources did, so the resource pattern is not uniform across profiles.

"Learning agility, future work self-salience, and perceived university digital support also favored Anxious Acceptance over Low-Perception Detached, whereas digital literacy did not significantly differentiate these two profiles."

Related Claims¶

  • Latent profile analysis of six AI-attitude indicators yields four profiles: Positive Empowerment, Anxious Acceptance, Low-Perception Detached, and High-Perception High-Vigilance — related
  • The High-Perception High-Vigilance profile combines elevated AI use anxiety and STARA awareness with strong capability perceptions and AI creative self-efficacy — related
  • Profiles also differ in self-perceived employability, and elevated AI-related threat is not necessarily incompatible with career preparation — related
  • AI-attitude profiles differ significantly in career crafting, with High-Perception High-Vigilance and Positive Empowerment highest and Low-Perception Detached lowest — related
  • Demographic predictors of engagement profile membership differed across language groups, with employment the only significant predictor for English-speaking parents — related
  • Episodic prospection develops over an extended period and may support academic behaviors aimed at envisioned future selves — related
  • Community and Questions codes most strongly separated the four groups, especially Group A from the others — related
