Evidence¶

Torre Gibney 2024¶

Torre Gibney, T., Fong, P., Lewis, J., Maduli, D., & Castellano, M. (2024). The landscape of middle school career and technical education in Nebraska. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673552

q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative finding from interviews/focus groups with an Educational Service Unit representative, reported in the rural delivery models subsection. The representative stated that "Teacher availability really drives what's offered". In one rural district a single CTE teacher teaches six different courses across middle and high school.

""Teacher availability really drives what's offered because there is such a limited number of teachers. … There might be agriculture, animal science, or welding that's offered, depending on the qualified teacher available.""
