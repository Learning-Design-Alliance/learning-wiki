## Evidence

### ElHassan NO 2018

ElHassan NO, Bai S, Gibson N, Holland G, Robbins JM, Kaiser JR. (2018). The impact of prematurity and maternal socioeconomic status and education level on achievement-test scores up to 8th grade. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198083

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear mixed model coefficients (Table 2), reported in Results. The article reports male gender literacy coefficient -79.5 (95% CI -94.8, -64.2, P <.001) and mathematics -13.6 (95% CI -22.1, -5.0, P = .002).

> "Male gender was associated with a significant reduction in literacy and mathematics scores, while higher grade, white or other race, Apgar score at 1 minute ≥7, and favorable social factors were associated with increased scores."
