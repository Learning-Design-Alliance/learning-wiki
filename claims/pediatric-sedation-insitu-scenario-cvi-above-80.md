Santos Marcos Maciel Candido Justino dos, Lima Sara Fiterman, Slullitel Alexandre, Giovanazzi Rosemeire Dellacrode, Lopes Francisco Diego Negrão Neto, Lima Marjorie de Araújo Vian Pinheiro, Santos René Scalet dos Neto, Pereira Gerson Alves Júnior. Revista do Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões. https://doi.org/10.1590/0100-6991e-20243709-en

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content-validation stage of a descriptive study: 12 experts (eight women, four men; five nursing, seven medicine) rated the scenario items on a five-point Likert scale via Google Forms in June 2023; answers 4 and 5 counted as agreement and the text reports "a CVI greater than 80.0%" on all items.

> "The experts indicated suggestions that were duly used and the scenario obtained, in all items, a CVI greater than 80.0%, demonstrating its high validity and reliability."
