Related Claims¶

  • Test scores at the floor of a test's score scale contain real information about students' performance, so floor scores do not distort teacher value-added estimates — related
  • The lower a test's score ceiling, the more teacher value-added estimates shrink toward the average teacher, because ceiling scores contain imprecise information about achievement — related
  • Multiple-choice state tests are prone to ceiling and floor effects and do not measure higher-order skills well — related
