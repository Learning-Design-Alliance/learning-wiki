## Evidence

### Hammouda Sana Ben 2025

Hammouda Sana Ben, Maoua Maher, Bouchahma Majed. (2025). The effectiveness of VR-based human anatomy simulation training for undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07402-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-simulation survey of 179 medical students at the Faculty of Medicine of Sousse using Likert-scale responses on perceived realism. The article reports "72% of students rated the VR scenarios as realistic" and separately that 86% found the scenarios accurate; no effect size is printed.

> "Additionally, 72% of students rated the VR scenarios as realistic when compared to the traditional anatomy lab experience, suggesting a strong sense of immersion and authenticity (Fig. 8)."
