Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of student-written pretest responses to question 2b about the probability density for measuring position. The article reports "some students could not distinguish between the eigenstates of energy and position operators", writing energy-eigenstate probability densities as the answer.

> "One common difficulty we found from student-written responses is that some students could not distinguish between the eigenstates of energy and position operators [46,47]. Some students wrote that the probability density of measuring position in the state Ψ(𝑥,0) is “|𝛹!(𝑥)|" or |𝛹"(𝑥)|"”."
