## Evidence

### Bialka Szymon 2021

Bialka Szymon, Copik Maja, Ubych Adam, Marciniak Radosław, Smereka Jacek, Szarpak Lukasz, Misiolek Hanna. (2021). Effect of high-fidelity simulation on alpha-amylase activity and concentrations of secretory immunoglobulin class A, cortisol, and testosterone among medical students. Endocrine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12020-021-02696-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective observational study of 55 senior medical students undergoing a 30-min high-fidelity simulation scenario; measurements at T0, T1, and T2. The article reports "SBP, MBP, and HR were higher at the T1" with p < 0.05, noting the effect size was small and values stayed within normal ranges.

> "Among hemodynamic parameters, SBP, MBP, and HR were higher at the T1 than at the T0 and T2 time points (p < 0.05)."
