# Affective responses favour standard recitation tasks: curiosity and interest were higher for Rec than for Exp and Pro tasks, and reference to reality was higher for Rec and Exp than Pro

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Curiosity was significantly higher for the Rec-tasks than for the Exp- and Pro-tasks. [→ Lahme 2025](#lahme-2025)
`q2 i?` Interest was significantly higher for the Rec-tasks than the Exp-tasks and Pro-tasks. [→ Lahme 2025 (2)](#lahme-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Lahme 2025

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative surveys (Com 1 and Com 2) with Bonferroni-corrected Friedman tests and post-hoc analyses across the three task types; curiosity differed significantly, with Rec-tasks highest (pB = 0.002 vs Exp; pB < 0.001 vs Pro). No effect size printed.

> "Post-hoc analyses, Bonferroni-corrected in each case, show that curiosity is significantly higher for the Rec-
tasks than for the Exp- (p <0.001, pB = 0.002) and Pro-tasks (p < 0.001, pB < 0.001)."

### Lahme 2025 (2)

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same comparative-survey analysis: interest was significantly higher for Rec-tasks than both Exp-tasks and Pro-tasks (pB < 0.001 in both pairwise comparisons). No effect size printed.

> "Interest is perceived as signifi-
cantly higher for the Rec-tasks than the Exp-tasks (p <0.001,
pB < 0.001) and Pro-tasks ( p < 0.001, pB < 0.001)."

### Lahme 2025 (3)

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same comparative-survey analysis of the authenticity subscale reference to reality: both Rec-tasks and Exp-tasks scored significantly higher than Pro-tasks (pB < 0.001 each). No effect size printed.

> "Reference to reality is significantly higher for the Rec-tasks
(p < 0.001, pB < 0.001) and the Exp-tasks ( p < 0.001,
pB < 0.001) than the Pro-tasks."

### Lahme 2025 (4)

Lahme, S. Z., Dorsel, D., Heinke, H., Klein, P., Müller, A., Stampfer, C., and Staacks, S. (2025). Recitation tasks revamped? Students’ perceptions of smartphone-based experimental and programming tasks in introductory mechanics. [doi:10.1103/bwpv-z3xh](https://doi.org/10.1103/bwpv-z3xh)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same comparative-survey analysis: disciplinary authenticity was significantly higher for Rec-tasks than Pro-tasks (pB = 0.004); the Exp–Rec contrast on this subscale was not reported as significant. No effect size printed.

> "Disciplinary authenticity is
significantly higher for the Rec-tasks than for the Pro-tasks
(p = 0.001, pB = 0.004)."
