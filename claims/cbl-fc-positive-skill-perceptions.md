# CBL/FC students report more positive perceptions of clinical skill gains than LBT students in both grades

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Grade 4 CBL/FC students rated improvements in medical history collection, physical examination, record writing, clinical thinking, and support for patients more positively than LBT students (all P < 0.01 or P = 0.01). [→ Hu Bibi 2024](#hu-bibi-2024)
`q3 i?` Grade 5 CBL/FC students likewise reported more positive perceptions of these five skill improvements than LBT students (all P < 0.01). [→ Hu Bibi 2024 (2)](#hu-bibi-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Hu Bibi 2024

Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A five-point Likert questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.84 for positive items), modified from Ramsden's Course Experience Questionnaire and Biggs' Study Process Questionnaire, was completed by all students; Grade 4 CBL/FC ratings exceeded LBT ratings, e.g. "medical history collection (4.25 ± 0.79 vs. 3.25 ± 0.74, P < 0.01)".

> "The students in Grade 4 in the CBL/FC group provided a more positive perception of improving the ability of medical history collection (4.25 ± 0.79 vs. 3.25 ± 0.74, P < 0.01), physical examination (4.29 ± 0.75 vs. 3.29 ± 0.75, P < 0.01), record writing (4.63 ± 0.49 vs. 3.63 ± 0.82, P < 0.01)"

### Hu Bibi 2024 (2)

Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same questionnaire administered to Grade 5 students showed CBL/FC ratings higher than LBT on all five skill items, for example "medical history collection (4.68 ± 0.48 vs. 3.91 ± 0.75, P < 0.01)". Only Likert means and P values are printed, with no effect size.

> "the students in Grade 5 in the CBL/FC group also held a more positive perception of improving the ability of medical history collection (4.68 ± 0.48 vs. 3.91 ± 0.75, P < 0.01), physical examination (4.55 ± 0.60 vs. 3.73 ± 0.70, P < 0.01), record writing (4.59 ± 0.59 vs. 3.36 ± 0.85, P < 0.01)"
