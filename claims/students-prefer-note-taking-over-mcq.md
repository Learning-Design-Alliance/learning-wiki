## Evidence

### Wrzesińska Magdalena A. 2025

Wrzesińska Magdalena A., Rakoczy Jarosław, Binder-Olibrowska Katarzyna W., Kostyła Magdalena, Allott Vincent E.S., Harris Samuel R.L., Walsh Jason L., Handa Ashok, Harris Benjamin H.L. (2025). Comparing structured note-taking and multiple-choice question writing as learning strategies among physiotherapy students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08272-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-exam online survey of 134 of 168 course completers (77.5% response rate) asking students to choose one method; chi-square testing found significantly more students preferred NT (86.6%) than MCQ (13.4%). Qualitative justifications cited NT as effective and student-friendly.

> "Quantitatively, significantly more students preferred NT when being able to choose only one between NT or MCQ to support learning (N = 116, 86.6% vs. N = 18, 13.4%; z = 8.379; p < 0.001)."
