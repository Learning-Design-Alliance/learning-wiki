# Students' self-reported interest in exploring quantum computing increased slightly after playing Qubit Touchdown (favorable shift 10.4%, p<0.05), in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A Friedman test found a significant difference in one group's self-reported interest in exploring quantum computing across the pre-test Q1 and post-tests Q3 and Q7 (χ2=8.9589, df=2, p<0.05). [→ Kristina Armbruster 2026](#kristina-armbruster-2026)
`q2 i?` Pairwise Wilcoxon tests show that one group's self-reported interest increased significantly from Q1 to Q3 and from Q1 to Q7 (both p<0.05), but not significantly from Q3 to Q7 (p>0.05). [→ Kristina Armbruster 2026 (2)](#kristina-armbruster-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Kristina Armbruster 2026

Kristina Armbruster, Gintaras Duda, and Thomas G. Wong. (2026). Improving Student Self-Confidence in Quantum Computing with the Qubit Touchdown Board Game. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1119/5.0258097](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0258097)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Friedman test on Likert responses to interest questions Q1, Q3, and Q7 from the same 107-student survey; the article reports "χ 2 =8.9589,d f =2, p<0.05".

> "We found that there was a significant main effect of measuring student interest in exploring quantum computing at different time points (Q1, Q3, and Q7) by utilizing a Friedman test (χ 2 =8.9589,d f =2, p<0.05)."

### Kristina Armbruster 2026 (2)

Kristina Armbruster, Gintaras Duda, and Thomas G. Wong. (2026). Improving Student Self-Confidence in Quantum Computing with the Qubit Touchdown Board Game. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1119/5.0258097](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0258097)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pairwise paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with continuity correction on interest questions; favorable shifts were 10.4% (Q1→Q3), -0.1% (Q3→Q7), and 10.3% (Q1→Q7) per Table III.

> "For students' interest in quantum computing, there was a statistically significant increase in student interest between Q1 and Q3 (p<0.05) and between Q1 and Q7 (p<0.05), but not between Q3 and Q7 (p>0.05)."
