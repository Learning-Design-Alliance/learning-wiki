## Evidence

### Eman Ganaiem 2025

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired t test comparing isomorphic pre- and post-test items across all conditions (n = 113) showed significant gains in experimental design application scores, d = 0.455, supporting the materials' effectiveness.

> "Experimental design mean scores increased from 0.181 ± 0.256 to 0.309 ± 0.289, t (112) = 4.837, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.455, which corresponds to a medium effect size."

### Eman Ganaiem 2025 (2)

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Paired t test on graphing application scores (n = 120) showed a significant but small pre-to-post gain, d = 0.217, across all three sequence conditions.

> "Graphing application scores increased from 0.273 ± 0.2 to 0.3208 ± 0.2, t (119) = 2.375, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.217, which corresponds to a small effect size."
