# No significant main effect of learning approach on behavioral, emotional, or cognitive engagement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The learning approach (flipped vs. non-flipped) showed no significant main effect on any engagement dimension, though descriptive means approached significance for cognitive and emotional engagement. [→ Yorganci 2025](#yorganci-2025)

## Evidence

### Yorganci 2025

Yorganci, S. (2025). The impact of synchronous online discussions and online flipped learning on student engagement and self-regulation among preliminary undergraduates in a basic math course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10459-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA found no significant main effect of learning approach on any engagement dimension. Descriptive means for flipped learning were higher on cognitive (M = 3.73) and emotional (M = 3.55) engagement, with differences "approaching significance" (p = .06).

> "The analysis did not reveal a significant main effect of learning approach. The online learning approach did not show a significant difference between groups in behavioral, emotional and cognitive engagement dimensions."
