Interview-based difficulty identified during QuILT development. These students incorrectly claimed Bob knows the polarization whether the photon is absorbed or transmitted by his polarizer, struggling with measurement collapse and non-zero absorption probabilities.

> "Related to the difficulties with the photon polarization states discussed above, a common difficulty before working on the QKD QuILT was believing that Bob always knows the polarization of the photon sent by Alice in the B92 protocol."
