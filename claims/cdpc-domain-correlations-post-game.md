Spearman correlation analysis of domain-wise scores after playing the CDPC game, reported for both groups; the article prints the effect sizes rho = 0.50 (Preclinical Motivation–Self-efficacy, p = 0.001) and rho = 0.59 (Clinical Motivation–Beliefs, p = 0.033).

> "after playing game, Motivation and Self-efficacy became significantly correlated in the Preclinical group (rho = 0.50, two-tailed p = 0.001, Fig. 4a) and Motivation and Beliefs became significantly correlated in the Clinical group (rho = 0.59, two-tailed p = 0.033, Fig. 4b)."
