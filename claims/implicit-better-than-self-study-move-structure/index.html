Evidence¶

Khodabandeh 2016¶

Khodabandeh, F. (2016). Comparing the Effects of Four Instructional Treatments on EFL Students’ Achievement in Writing Classified Ads. English Language Teaching, 9(3), 139-. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p139

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Discussion-section interpretation of the implicit group (N=18) results, attributed by the author to alignment with Yeganeh et al. (2013). The average abbreviation errors per post-test paper was 3 for implicit versus 7 for self-study, showing implicit genre instruction enhanced move structure employment.

"Although the control group was not exposed to the explicit instruction, results show that lexical and discourse features were also found in the control group post- classified ads and it proves that the implicit genre-based teaching method was better than the self -study instruction method and it is in line with Yeganeh et al’s study (2013) who showed that implicit genre instruction did enhance students’ employment of move structure."
