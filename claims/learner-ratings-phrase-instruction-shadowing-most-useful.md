## Evidence

### Thomson 2017

Haidee Thomson. (2017). Building Speaking Fluency with Multiword Expressions. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3). https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1272

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Activity feedback surveys on 5-point Likert scales completed by experimental participants after each activity, analyzed in SPSS. "Phrase instruction (M = 4.21, SD = 0.71)" and shadowing ranked highest.

> "participants considered phrase instruction (M = 4.21, SD = 0.71) and shadowing (M = 3.84, SD = 0.92) to be the most useful activities; there was no significant difference between their rating, t(33) = -1.66, p  = .107"

### Thomson 2017 (2)

Haidee Thomson. (2017). Building Speaking Fluency with Multiword Expressions. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3). https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1272

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 30-minute focus group with volunteer experimental participants, thematically coded in ELAN. All three participants "stated that “the sound is too fast,”" regarding the marking-pauses activity with short-turn dialogues.

> "All three participants in the focus group discus- sion stated that “the sound is too fast,” and one participant’s comment sheds further light on this complaint: “to be honest I cannot hear the pauses.”"
