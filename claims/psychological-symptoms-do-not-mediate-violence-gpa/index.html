Evidence¶

Borofsky Larissa A. 2013¶

Borofsky Larissa A., Kellerman Ilana, Baucom Brian, Oliver Pamella H., Margolin Gayla. (2013). Community Violence Exposure and Adolescents' School Engagement and Academic Achievement Over Time. Psychology of Violence. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0034121

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical multiple regression with the GPA subsample (n = 96) testing internalizing, externalizing, and posttraumatic stress symptoms as mediators of time 1 community violence predicting time 2 GPA; no symptom variable reached significance.

"None of the psychological symptom variables was found to mediate the relationship between community violence and GPA as neither externalizing, β = −.15, t(95) = −1.51, p = .14, internalizing, β = .01, t(95) = .10, p = .93, nor posttraumatic stress symptoms, β = .12, t(95) = 1.20, p = .23, was significantly associated with GPA."
