`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Case-level paired t-test within the same 271-learner pre-post study. The UTI case improved from 0.79 to 1.37, with "Cohen's d = 0.18", significant after Holm-Bonferroni correction.

> "The UTI case demonstrated a statistically significant domain-specific improvement, with a mean score increase from 0.79 +/- 2.07 to 1.37 +/- 2.35 (mean change = 0.58 +/- 3.18; t = 3.00, p = 0.003, Cohen's d = 0.18)."
