## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2022

Alexandru Maries, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Gender Inequities Throughout STEM: Compared to Men, Women with Significantly Higher Grades Drop STEM Majors. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.02438](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.02438)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 10 years of institutional GPA data plotted in Figure 1 (overall and STEM GPA by year). The article reports persisters "have a GPA of roughly 0.3 to 0.6 grade points higher than those who dropped" in bio and math/physical science, and roughly 0.6 points in CS and engineering; no inferential test is reported for this contrast.

> "Figure 1 shows that in general, the students who dropped any given major have a lower GPA and STEM GPA than students who earned a degree in that major."
