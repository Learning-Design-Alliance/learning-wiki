de Barba 2025¶

de Barba, P. G., Oliveira, E. A., & English, N. (2025). Development and validation of a learning analytics rubric for self-regulated learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10521-x

q2 · i? · design · r2

In Phase 1 of this design-based research, two researchers independently assessed the feasibility of translating each of 211 SRL scale items into Canvas LMS data indicators. Initial inter-rater agreement was low (Kappa = 0.11), with discrepancies focused on discussion forum analytics items. After consensus review, ten items were identified as suitable.

"Overall, ten items (less than 5%) were deemed feasible to be measured using learning analytics out of the 211 items initially considered."
