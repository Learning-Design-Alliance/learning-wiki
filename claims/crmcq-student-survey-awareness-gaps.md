## Evidence

### Luetsch Karen 2016

Luetsch Karen, Burrows Judith. (2016). Certainty rating in pre-and post-tests of study modules in an online clinical pharmacy course - A pilot study to evaluate teaching and learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0783-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous online survey of 24 of 29 participants using a five-point Likert scale, adapted from prior CBM attitude instruments. Results show 92% agreed it made them think about certainty and aware of what they know and don't know, 83% that it assisted identifying knowledge gaps, and 96% disagreed it was a waste of time.

> "Twenty-four of the 29 participants completed an anonymous online survey containing eleven questions relating to their perceptions of benefit and usefulness of assigning a certainty rating to their answers in MCQs in structuring or advancing their learning."
