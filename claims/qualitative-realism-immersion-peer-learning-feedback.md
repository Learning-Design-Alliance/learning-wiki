## Evidence

### Matthew Tennant 2025

Matthew Tennant, Maggie Meeks, Ben Beaglehole, Chris Frampton, Jane Foley, Emily Ide, Dylan Hill, Carol Dean. (2025). Preparing House Officers for Emergencies in a Psychiatric Setting with Simulation-Based Learning. Academic Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-025-02217-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative arm: written responses to open questions after training were coded and categorised using the content analysis method of Kleinheksel et al. Participants reported "the scenarios were believable and something they might really have to manage during their rotations in psychiatry", valued using real equipment, and described peer learning in a safe, contained environment.

> "Participants reported it was helpful that the scenarios felt realistic. They reported that the scenarios were believable and something they might really have to manage during their rotations in psychiatry."
