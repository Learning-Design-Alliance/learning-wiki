### dos Santos 2014

dos Santos, R. P. (2014). TATI - A Logo-like Interface for Microworlds and Simulations for Physics Teaching in Second Life. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.6334](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.6334)

The article asserts, citing Trowbridge and McDermott (1981) and a 2011 review by Rosenblatt and Heckler, that student difficulty distinguishing velocity and acceleration is well documented. This is the article's motivating premise, reported as background literature rather than tested here.

> "Of particular interest to this work, is their general difficulty in distinguishing between the concepts of velocity and acceleration (Trowbridge and McDermott, 1981)."
