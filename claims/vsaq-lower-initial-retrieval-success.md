# VSAQs are answered incorrectly more often than MCQs on practice tests, indicating lower initial retrieval success

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MCQs were answered correctly on the practice tests 73.9% of the time versus 58.6% for VSAQs, and successful retrieval practice (correct on both practice and final test) occurred in 50.4% of MCQ-practiced questions versus 39.8% of VSAQ-practiced questions. ## Evidence

### van Wijk EV 2024

van Wijk EV, de Jonge M, van Blankenstein FM, Janse RJ, Langers AMJ. (2024). The battle of question formats: a comparative study of retrieval practice using very short answer questions and multiple choice questions. BMC Medical Education, 24:1547. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06538-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of practice and final test answers in the within-subjects study (n = 45) shows "50.4% of questions practiced in the MCQ format vs. 39.8% of questions practiced in VSAQ format" were successfully retrieved. The article also reports practice-test correct rates of 73.9% (MCQ) vs 58.6% (VSAQ).

> "Successful retrieval practice, defined as correctly answered questions in both the practice test and the final retention test, occurred in 50.4% of questions practiced in the MCQ format vs. 39.8% of questions practiced in VSAQ format (Table 2)."
