# The general sociability reference factor uniquely and positively predicts student-rated classroom management, student support, and teacher enthusiasm, while assertiveness and energy level add no unique prediction

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In bifactor-(S-1) MSEM, the general sociability reference factor was positively related to classroom management (β = .167), student support (β = .123), and teacher enthusiasm (β = .099). [→ Burić 2023](#buric-2023)

## Evidence

### Burić 2023

Burić, Irena, Butković, Ana and Kim, Lisa (2023) Teacher personality domains and facets: Their unique associations with student-ratings of teaching quality and teacher enthusiasm. Learning and Instruction. 101827. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101827

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Predictive bifactor-(S-1) MSEM on 1,067 teachers and 18,814 students from 73 Croatian secondary schools; the extraversion model showed the general sociability factor "positively related to student-ratings of classroom management" with the printed standardized coefficients. The article also reports assertiveness and energy level failed to uniquely explain the criteria.

> "the general sociability reference factor was positively related to student-ratings of classroom management (β = .167, p < .001), student support (β = .123, p < .001), and teacher enthusiasm (β = .099, p < .01)."
