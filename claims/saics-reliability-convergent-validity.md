## Evidence

### Thomas K. F. Chiu 2025

Thomas K. F. Chiu, Murat Çoban, Ismaila Temitayo Sanusi, Musa Adekunle Ayanwale. (2025). Validating student AI competency self-efficacy (SAICS) scale and its framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10512-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Internal consistency and variability analysis of the eight SAICS dimensions in the 448-student validation sample (Table 4). The article also reports the correlation matrix "supported the scale's convergent validity, with all correlated dimensions (r > 0.34; p < .05)."

> "All Cronbach's alpha (CR) values are equal to or greater than 0.89; All dimensions were near to normal distributions (skewness and kurtosis between −1 and 1) and had sufficient variability (range and standard deviation); All dimensions had high factor loadings, ranging from 0.76 to 0.96 (> 0.80)."
