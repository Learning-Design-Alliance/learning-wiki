`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Final pilot rounds of QCCS v2.2 collected N=777 usable student responses from 55 courses and 46 institutions. The added 'CANNOT be written as a single-qubit ket' option fixed the v2.1 failure; the item showed "strong" discrimination and a monotonically increasing correct-answer slope (Fig. 4b).

> "While difficulty was low (p= 0.23(2)), performance remained far above the guess floor. The discrimination was strong (ρ∗ ≳0.3), lending credence to our belief that the item was indeed simply measuring a hard set of skills."
