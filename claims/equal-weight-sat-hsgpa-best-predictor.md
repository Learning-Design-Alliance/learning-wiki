## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The optimal linear predictor of upper division GPA is a nearly equal weighting of SAT and high school GPA z-scores, improving correlation modestly over either alone (all-majors Rmax = 0.52 vs RSAT = 0.40, RGPA = 0.46). [→ Stephen D.H. Hsu 2010](#stephen-dh-hsu-2010)
