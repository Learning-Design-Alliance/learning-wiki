# Peer question-asking shows a non-significant trend toward higher knowledge scores in both topics

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Knowledge questionnaire scores were higher under Peer-QnA in the Double-Slit Experiment topic, but not significantly. [→ Süleyman Özdel 2025](#suleyman-ozdel-2025)
`q2 i?` In the History of Video Games topic the difference approached but did not reach significance (p = .056). [→ Süleyman Özdel 2025 (2)](#suleyman-ozdel-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Süleyman Özdel 2025

Süleyman Özdel, Can Sarpkaya, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Examining the Role of LLM-Driven Interactions on Attention and Cognitive Engagement in Virtual Classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple-choice knowledge test after each VR session in the within-subjects experiment; Double-Slit scores were higher under Peer-QnA but "the difference was not statistically significant."

> "For the Double-Slit Experiment, the mean score for the Peer-QnA condition wasM = 7.50, SD= 2.07, while the mean score for the Peer-NoQnA condition was M = 6.82, SD= 1.60"

### Süleyman Özdel 2025 (2)

Süleyman Özdel, Can Sarpkaya, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Examining the Role of LLM-Driven Interactions on Attention and Cognitive Engagement in Virtual Classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Knowledge scores for the History of Video Games topic in the same within-subjects experiment were not significantly different (p = .056); the article reports only a potential trend towards improved performance in the Peer-QnA condition.

> "The mean score for the Peer-QnA condition is M = 7 .36, SD= 1 .21, while the Peer-NoQnA condition has a mean score of M = 5 .88, SD= 1 .96."
