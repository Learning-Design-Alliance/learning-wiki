Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 37 purposively selected teacher candidates. The article reports Positive Effects as dominant (f = 28), with "gaining a different perspective" the most cited code (f = 20), followed by not hesitating to criticize (f = 4).

> "Figure 3 shows that Positive Effects was the dominant theme regarding the impact of post-earthquake conditions on critical thinking skills (f = 28), followed by Neutral Views (f = 10) and Negative Effects (f = 7)."
