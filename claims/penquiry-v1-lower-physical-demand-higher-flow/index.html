q3 · i? · design · r3

Study 1 TAM questionnaire (N=16, within-subjects): V1 rated higher on usefulness (5.98 vs. 6.63, p=0.029) and intention to continue use (5.44 vs. 6.31, p=0.008); intuitiveness was n.s. Average SUS for V1 was 80.78, rated "Excellent".

Related Claims¶

  • Pen input supported deictic referencing: 85% of V1 queries contained at least one visible pen mark, and ease of referencing improved significantly over the baseline — related
  • In Study 1, learners preferred Penquiry V1 for part-scoped, specific-target and short questions, and favored the baseline for general topics and longer interpretive questions — related
  • In the TAM structural model, satisfaction mediated sustained use: usability strongly predicted usefulness (β = 0.93), usefulness predicted satisfaction (β = 0.76), but usefulness did not directly predict use intention (β = 0.02, p = .896) — related
  • Unmediated pen input introduced new pen-specific barriers: lack of visual feedback on referential intent and high interaction cost of handwriting complex queries — related
