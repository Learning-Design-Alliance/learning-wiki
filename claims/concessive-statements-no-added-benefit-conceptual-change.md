## Evidence

### Anja Prinz 2022

Anja Prinz, Julia Kollmer, Lisa Flick, Alexander Renkl, Alexander Eitel. (2022). Refuting student teachers' misconceptions about multimedia learning. Instructional Science, 50, 89–110. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09568-z

`q2 · i1`

Second planned orthogonal contrast of the same experiment, comparing concessive against typical refutation texts on the conceptual-change score. The article reports "no significant difference" with "t(93) = −0.81, p = 0.422, η2 = 0.01"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Contrary to Hypothesis 2b, however, there was no significant difference between the refutation-texts condition and the concessive-refutation-texts condition, t(93) = −0.81, p = 0.422, η2 = 0.01"
