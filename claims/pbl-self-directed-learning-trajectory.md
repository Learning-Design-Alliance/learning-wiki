### Wang Sixue 2026

Wang Sixue, Yuan Yue, Zhou Chunyi, He Huijuan, Wang Yuncui. (2026). Postgraduate nursing students' experiences in a project-based learning model: a qualitative focus group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08649-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from thematic analysis of focus groups and reflective journals with 21 postgraduate nursing students. Sub-themes were driving force, ongoing scrutiny and reflection, and adjusting mindset to foster resilience — "a dynamic trajectory, evolving from initially triggered motivation".

> "This theme revealed that the development of self-directed learning within the PBL model followed a dynamic trajectory, evolving from initially triggered motivation to the sustained reinforcement of learning behaviors."
