Wang 2026¶

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey results on TK curricula (Table 4, N = 1,414 LEAs), with year-over-year trends showing declining use of PreK literacy-specific curriculum, kindergarten curriculum, and district- or teacher-developed units.

"most LEAs (88%) reporting using between one and four curricular approaches. The most common curricular approaches include a social-emotional curriculum (49%), PreK literacy-specific curriculum (42%), whole child or comprehensive PreK curriculum (38%), and PreK math-specific curriculum (33%)."

Wang 2026 (2)¶

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey trend analysis (Table 4). Of 256 LEAs using kindergarten curriculum, 125 operated TK–kindergarten classrooms and 131 did not, suggesting about 1 in 10 LEAs may use kindergarten curriculum with only 4-year-olds.

"Fewer LEAs used kindergarten curriculum in TK classrooms in 2024–25 than prior years, though almost 1 in 5 continue to do so."
