## Evidence

### Fares Fawzi 2026

Fares Fawzi, Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Marta Knezevic, Tanya Nazaretsky, and Tanja Käser. (2026). REFINE: Real-world Exploration of Interactive Feedback and Student Behaviour. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of classroom follow-up questions from interaction logs, identifying 15 recurring themes grouped into five categories (Task, Solution, Feedback, Additional Feedback, Off-topic), with inter-rater agreement Cohen's κ = 0.73. Feedback-focused questions were "most common".

> "reflecting active engagement with the generated feedback and primarily involving requests for clarification, elaboration, or interpretation"

### Fares Fawzi 2026 (2)

Fares Fawzi, Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Marta Knezevic, Tanya Nazaretsky, and Tanja Käser. (2026). REFINE: Real-world Exploration of Interactive Feedback and Student Behaviour. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-interaction perception survey on a 5-point Likert scale completed by the 39 classroom-study students after receiving feedback and asking follow-up questions; means and SDs are descriptive with no test reported.

> "correctness (4.1±0.8), relevance (4.2±0.7), usefulness (3.9±0.6), and actionability (3.9±0.8)."
