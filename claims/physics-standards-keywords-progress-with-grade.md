Jibeom Seo, Jun Cho, Sukyung Han, Meesoon Ha. (2024). Semantic Network Analysis of Achievement Standards in Physics of 2022 Revised Curriculum. arXiv. [doi:10.3938/npsm.74.944](https://doi.org/10.3938/npsm.74.944)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semantic network analysis of 127 physics achievement standards from the 2022 Revised National Curriculum; key words by node strength and random-walk betweenness (Table 1) show the shift, with the authors noting "the importance of the term 'everyday life' diminishes with increasing grade level."

> "In elementary school, basic skills such as 'use' and 'observe' are emphasized, whereas in middle and high school, the focus shifts towards higher-order thinking such as 'explain,' 'understand,' and 'utilize.'"
