CT measurement study comparing simulator airway distances with premature stillborn cadaver references. The article states the distances were "within the range measured in the respective reference cadavers only in the Premature Anne simulator (Laerdal Medical)" and that in the other three simulators the distances were markedly too long.

> "The distances from the nasal inlet and mouth inlet to the glottis were within the range measured in the respective reference cadavers only in the Premature Anne simulator (Laerdal Medical)."
