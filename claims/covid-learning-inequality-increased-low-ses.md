## Evidence

### Bastian A. Betthäuser 2023

Bastian A. Betthäuser, Anders M. Bach-Mortensen & Per Engzell. (2023). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the evidence on learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01506-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Harvest plot synthesis (Fig. 5) of 211 estimates of over-time change in inequality, coded as decrease/no change/increase because differing socio-economic measures precluded pooling. Most estimates fall in the increase category across all subgroupings.

> "A large majority of estimates indicate an increase in educational inequality between children from different socio-economic backgrounds. This holds for both maths and reading, across primary and secondary education, at each stage of the pandemic, and independently of how socio-economic background is measured."
