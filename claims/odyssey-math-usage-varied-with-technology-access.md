## Evidence

### usage-1

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2017, January). Primary Mathematics intervention report: Odyssey® Math. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/669

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Implementation detail from the DiLeo (2007) cluster randomized trial. In two schools students could access the software only during weekly computer lab time, while in the other two schools it was also available via classroom wireless laptops and at home.

> "Teachers were asked to use the software a minimum of 90 minutes per week, but usage levels varied across classrooms, in part because of access to technology."
