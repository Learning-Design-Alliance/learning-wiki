## Evidence

### Xin Liu 2023

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

During the analytical process, measurement models were estimated per country for both TALIS and PISA data. For Mexico, the article reports "none of the dimensions of mathematics instructional quality can be identified" in the teacher data.

> "In the TALIS 2013 data, none of the dimensions of mathematics instructional quality can be identified in Mexico."

### Xin Liu 2023 (2)

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article explains that with Mexican data included, cross-country invariance would "only reach the configural level", so the authors removed the Mexico results to Appendix 2 and focused on the seven remaining countries.

> "This implies that if Mexican data are included in the study, the measurement invariance among the eight countries can only reach the configural level."
