## Evidence

### Nurwijayanti 2019

Nurwijayanti, A., Budiyono, & Fitriana, L. (2019). Combining Google SketchUp and Ispring Suite 8: A breakthrough to develop geometry learning media. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 103-116. https://jme.ejournal.uns.ac.id/index.php/jme

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Expert judgement during the product development stage using a media experts' validation sheet. The first validation averaged 3.75, "which is in the good category"; after revisions the second validation scored 3.93, also good.

> "The media first validation average score was 3.75 , which is in the good category. However, there were suggestions and feedbacks from the validators."

### Nurwijayanti 2019 (2)

Nurwijayanti, A., Budiyono, & Fitriana, L. (2019). Combining Google SketchUp and Ispring Suite 8: A breakthrough to develop geometry learning media. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 103-116. https://jme.ejournal.uns.ac.id/index.php/jme

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Material experts' judgement with three validators. Scores of 3.16, 3.04 and 3.64 led to revisions; "According to the second validation, the score was 3.80 which is in a good category."

> "The average score from the first validator was 3.16 and the second validator was 3.04 both were in fair category, and the third validator was 3.64 in a good category."
