## Evidence

### Courteille Olivier 2018

Courteille Olivier, Fahlstedt Madelen, Ho Johnson, Hedman Leif, Fors Uno, von Holst Hans, Felländer-Tsai Li, Möller Hans. (2018). Learning through a virtual patient vs. recorded lecture: a comparison of knowledge retention in a trauma case. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5aa3.ccf2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exit questionnaires with 15 closed questions on cognitive and affective states (5-point Likert scales) were completed after Test 1; the Results section reports "no large discrepancies between the VP and L groups" and high median values, while the Conclusions state engagement was reported in favour of the VP format.

> "In general, there were no large discrepancies between the VP and L groups. The level of engagement, stimulation, general opinion, and learning expectations was reported with high median values."
