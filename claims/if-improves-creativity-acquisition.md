## Evidence

### Junting Yin 2025

Junting Yin, Zheyu Jin, Yuxuan Zhang, Xuening Li, Yangzhuo Li, Guoping Zhang & Junlong Luo. (2025). Applying, not ignoring: how feedback uptake and neural synchrony drive creativity. npj Science of Learning 10:61. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00351-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (Time × Group) on AUT acquisition scores across four feedback groups (100 dyads analyzed). The interaction was significant with printed effect size η2Ps ≥ 0.115, and "posttest creativity scores in the IF group" exceeded pretest on all three scores (ps < 0.001).

> "Results indicated a signiﬁcant interaction effect on creativity scores (ﬂuency, ﬂexibility, and uniqueness; Fs(3, 96) ≥ 4.142, ps ≤ 0.008, η2 Ps ≥ 0.115)."
