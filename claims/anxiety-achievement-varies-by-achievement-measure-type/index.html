Yasser Teimouri 2019¶

Yasser Teimouri, Julia Goetze, and Luke Plonsky. (2019). SECOND LANGUAGE ANXIETY AND ACHIEVEMENT: A META-ANALYSIS. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 41, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263118000311

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analytic moderator analyses of the 216 correlations found heterogeneity "across different types of language achievement measures, educational levels, target languages, and anxiety types." No subgroup effect sizes are printed in the available text.
