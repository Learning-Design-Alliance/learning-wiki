NWEA 2021¶

NWEA. (2021). Spanish MAP Growth Reading Technical Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/spanish-map-growth-reading-technical-report/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Differential item functioning analysis (Chapter 8) comparing items across language background groups using Mantel-Haenszel procedures. The report summarizes that "Most items are classified as A" and C DIF is highest for the Native English/Bilingual group at 6.66% versus about 1% elsewhere.

"Overall, DIF results show the following three patterns: (1) Most items are classified as A, (2) the highest percentage of C DIF is the Native English/Bilingual group (6.66%), and (3) C DIF is rare for the remaining DIF study groups (~1%)."
