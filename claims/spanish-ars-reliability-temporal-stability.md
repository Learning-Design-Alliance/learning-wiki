## Evidence

### Trigueros Rubén 2020

Trigueros Rubén, Magaz-González Ana M., García-Tascón Marta, Alias Antonio, Aguilar-Parra José M. (2020). Validation and Adaptation of the Academic-Resilience Scale in the Spanish Context. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17113779

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability and discriminant-validity analysis of the Spanish ARS factors. The article reports alphas of "0.88 for perseverance, 0.83 for reflection and adaptation of the search for help, and 0.86" for the third factor, with HTMT ratios of 0.45, 0.51 and 0.34.

> "Analysis of internal consistency through Cronbach's alpha test was satisfactory, 0.88 for perseverance, 0.83 for reflection and adaptation of the search for help, and 0.86 for negative affect and emotional response"

### Trigueros Rubén 2020 (2)

Trigueros Rubén, Magaz-González Ana M., García-Tascón Marta, Alias Antonio, Aguilar-Parra José M. (2020). Validation and Adaptation of the Academic-Resilience Scale in the Spanish Context. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17113779

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Temporal-stability analysis with a separate sample of 431 university students retested over time. The printed ICCs were "0.85 (CI = 0.82–0.90) for perseverance", 0.83 for reflection and adaptation of seeking help, and 0.88 for negative affect and emotional response.

> "Temporal-stability analysis, intraclass correlation coefficients (ICCs), and their confidence intervals (CI) were calculated, giving a score of 0.85 (CI = 0.82–0.90) for perseverance"
