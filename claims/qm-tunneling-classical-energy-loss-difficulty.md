## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Review of Student Difficulties in Upper-Level Quantum Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports research on quantum tunneling difficulties, citing studies with written surveys and interviews. Students transferred classical reasoning, with interview responses such as "the particle collides and loses energy in the barriers" and "it requires energy to go through the barrier."

> "Many students state that a particle “loses energy” when it tunnels through a rectangular potential barrier. This reasoning is incorrect because the particle does not lose energy when tunneling through the barrier"
