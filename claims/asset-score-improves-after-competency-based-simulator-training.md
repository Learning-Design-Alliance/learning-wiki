## Evidence

### Rahm Stefan 2018

Rahm Stefan, Wieser Karl, Bauer David E., Waibel Felix WA, Meyer Dominik C., Gerber Christian, Fucentese Sandro F. (2018). Efficacy of standardized training on a virtual reality simulator to advance knee and shoulder arthroscopic motor skills. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-018-2072-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded consensus ASSET scoring (two experienced surgeons) of four arthroscopy tasks performed by 20 residents before and after the competency-based training program. Scores rose "from 55% (range, 27 to 84) to 75% (range, 48 to 92)", p = 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The mean overall ASSET score improved significantly in all four tasks from pre- to post training; from 55% (range, 27 to 84) to 75% (range, 48 to 92); p = 0.001."
