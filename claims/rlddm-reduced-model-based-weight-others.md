## Evidence

### Yangchu Huang 2026

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

RLDDM parameter comparison between recipients in the 92-participant task; parameters were well recovered (all correlation coefficients > 0.86) and posterior predictive checks reproduced key behavioural patterns. The article reports "significant self-other differences in model-based weights" with printed effect size d = 0.71, and no self-other difference in model-free weights, t(91) = 0.46, p = 0.645, d = 0.06.

> "revealed significant self-other differences in model-based weights, t(91) = 6.06, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.09, 0.17], d = 0.71 (model-based weights for self: M ± SD = 0.37 ± 0.16; model-based weights for others: M ± SD = 0.24 ± 0.20)"
