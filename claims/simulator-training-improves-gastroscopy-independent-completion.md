## Evidence

### Qiao Weiguang 2014

Qiao Weiguang, Bai Yang, Lv Ruxi, Zhang Wendi, Chen Yuqing, Lei Shan, Zhi Fachao. (2014). The Effect of Virtual Endoscopy Simulator Training on Novices: A Systematic Review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0089224

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of RCTs comparing virtual endoscopy simulator training with bedside or other training for novices; gastroscopy subgroup pooled from "1 study, 22 participants". The review reports "RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.13–1.39, P<0.0001" favouring simulator training for independent completion.

> "Procedure completed independently was reported in 87.7% of participants in the simulator training group compared to 70.0% of participants in the no simulator training group (1 study, 22 participants, RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.13–1.39, P<0.0001; see Figure 4)."
