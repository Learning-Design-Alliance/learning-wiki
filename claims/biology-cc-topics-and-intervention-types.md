## Evidence

### Aleknavičiūtė 2023

Aleknavičiūtė, V., Lehtinen, E., & Södervik, I. (2023). Thirty years of conceptual change research in biology – A review and meta-analysis of intervention studies. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100556

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Qualitative synthesis of the 96 included studies' topics: evolution leads (28 papers in Table 4), and "The second most frequently investigated topic was ‘photosynthesis’" (15 papers), followed by respiration, ecological concepts, genetics and circulatory systems.

> "The most common biological topic in conceptual change interventions was ‘evolution’ (Table 4)."

### Aleknavičiūtė 2023 (2)

Aleknavičiūtė, V., Lehtinen, E., & Södervik, I. (2023). Thirty years of conceptual change research in biology – A review and meta-analysis of intervention studies. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100556

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Intervention-type coding across the 96 studies: "The most common intervention type was a text-reading intervention (25 studies)", of which 19 were refutational or conceptual change texts; other types included hands-on activities, simulations and games, concept maps, analogies and writing.

> "The most common intervention type was a text-reading intervention (25 studies). More specifically, most of the texts used were refutational texts (19 studies out of 25)"
