Evidence¶

Putwain 2025¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Test anxiety, emotion regulation, and achievement: Lagged and contemporaneous reciprocal relations. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://repository.essex.ac.uk/40904/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Preliminary model comparison: "there were few lagged relations in the CLPMs", whereas the contemporaneous models showed numerous relations, supporting contemporaneous modeling for fast-unfolding ER processes within a twelve-month measurement interval.

"In contrast, the CCLPMs showed numerous relations between test anxiety and ER, suggesting that estimates of contemporaneous effects are an effective method of capturing ER dynamics, and confirming that contemporaneous effects models are a plausible alternative for modeling processes that occur between the measurement waves in longitudinal panel designs"
