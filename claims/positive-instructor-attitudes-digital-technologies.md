## Evidence

### Lahme 2023

Lahme, S. Z., Klein, P., Lehtinen, A., Müller, A., Pirinen, P., Rončević, L., & Sušac, A. (2023). Physics lab courses under digital transformation: A tri-national survey among university lab instructors about the role of new digital technologies and learning objectives. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020159](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020159)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey finding from the 15-item attitude scale rated on a five-point scale; the mean of the items was treated as a new variable attitude, with no significant differences across countries or lab course types.

> "The participants generally showed a positive atti- tude toward using digital technologies in physics lab cours es, especially due to their potential for experiments and students’ competence acquisition, motivational effec ts, and contemporaneity."

### Lahme 2023 (2)

Lahme, S. Z., Klein, P., Lehtinen, A., Müller, A., Pirinen, P., Rončević, L., & Sušac, A. (2023). Physics lab courses under digital transformation: A tri-national survey among university lab instructors about the role of new digital technologies and learning objectives. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020159](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020159)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Principal component analysis with varimax rotation on the 15 attitude items; KMO = .85 and Bartlett's test significant (p < .001). One item's corrected item-scale correlation was .28 but excluding it would not change Cronbach's Alpha.

> "Based on Kaiser's criteria [ 91] and the scree-plot, we chose a single-factor model explaining 46% of the total variance (the next two factors would only explain 9% and 8% of total variance). The Cronbach's Alpha for the single factor is α = .91."
