## Evidence

### Graham 2015

Graham, L., Graham, A., & West, C. (2015). From Research to Practice: The Effect of Multi-Component Vocabulary Instruction on Increasing Vocabulary and Comprehension Performance in Social Studies. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(1), 147-160. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078850

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the three-administration Checkpoints data: posttest 2 means were 14.35 (experimental) vs 12.86 (control), with control scores falling slightly after intervention while experimental scores continued to improve. The article states this "may indicate that the effect of vocabulary instruction on improved comprehensi on performance may be prolonged past the duration of the intervention."

> "It is interesting that no significant difference for the Checkpoints measure was observed in the first six weeks, but a difference did emerge at the 12 -week follow -up point."
