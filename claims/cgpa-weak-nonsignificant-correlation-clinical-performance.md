Pearson correlation analysis in the 72-student study linked CGPA to rubric totals; the article reports "weak and nonsignificant correlations" for both evaluators and the overall average, with no significant association detected.

> "However, CGPA showed weak and nonsignificant correlations with total scores (evaluator 1: r = 0.136, p = 0.459; evaluator 2: r = 0.308, p = 0.086; overall average: r = 0.254, p = 0.160), suggesting the minimal influence of academic performance on clinical rubric outcomes."
