## Evidence

### Ekmark Sergel S 2026

Ekmark Sergel S, Lundberg M, Magnusson C, Ådén U, Bergman G, Siljehav V. (2026). Academic Achievements in Adolescents With Congenital Heart Disease: A Total Population‐Based Cohort Study. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.126.049927

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple regression within general linear models on total grade sum, imputing missing grades as 0, adjusted for the same covariate set. Table 2 prints adjusted means (e.g., UVH boys 168, 95% CI 157–180, versus no-CHD boys 199) with P<0.001 for trend; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "When excluding MCA and stratifying by sex, total grade sum was affected by both the complexity of CHD and sex, where boys with UVH had an average corresponding to just above pass grades in all subjects, but girls with UVH had somewhat higher total grade sum, as shown in Table 2."
