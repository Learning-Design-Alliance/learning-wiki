# 3D VR video acclimatisation improves overall robotic simulator performance among novices compared with 2D video

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across all tasks, novices who watched a 3D VR video scored higher on overall simulator performance than those who watched the same video in 2D. [→ Shakir T. 2025](#shakir-t-2025)

## Evidence

### Shakir T. 2025

Shakir T., Lingam G., Francis N., Chand M. (2025). Acclimatising with 3D virtual reality video improves simulator performance: initial findings of a randomised controlled feasibility trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12153-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feasibility RCT in which 18 novice robotic surgeons were randomised 1:1 to 3D VR or 2D video for 20 minutes before four introductory da Vinci Xi SimNow tasks. The article reports "the mean overall performance score for the 3D VR video group was 52.75" versus 29.78 for 2D, p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Across all tasks, the mean overall performance score for the 3D VR video group was 52.75, while the 2D video group had a mean score of 29.78 (p < 0.001)"
