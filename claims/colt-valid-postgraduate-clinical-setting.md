## Evidence

### Pacifico JL 2020

Pacifico JL, Donkers J, Jacobs J, van der Vleuten C, Heeneman S. (2020). Understanding teaching and learning conceptions among clinical faculty as a means to improve postgraduate training. International Journal of Medical Education, 11, 175-185. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5f2a.76eb

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Confirmatory factor analysis of survey data from 172 postgraduate clinical teachers at De La Salle University Medical Center using the 18-item COLT. The article reports that "Removing 6 items (2,5,7,8,11, and 14) improved the fit to an acceptable level."

> "Removing 6 items (2,5,7,8,11, and 14) improved the fit to an acceptable level since four criteria of goodness of fit reached a sufficient level (SRMR = 0.058, RMSEA = 0.050; p=0.48, CFI = 0.936 and TLI = 0.918)."
