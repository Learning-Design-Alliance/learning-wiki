Learning and Instruction 102 (2026) 102302. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102302

Kendall's tau correlation across 9 teams in the field-based observational study, relating during-simulation MdRQA %REC to leaders' adapted Ottawa GRS CRM scores. The article reports "τb = 0.535, p = .046", a moderate positive correlation; the authors frame the finding as preliminary.

"We found a statistically significant, moderate, positive correlation between During Sim real data %REC and CRM score (τb = 0.535, p = .046)."
