Pretest comparison in the same quasi-experimental design: the article reports the t-test "t(87) = 1.23, p = .220" and interprets it as equivalent starting understanding. No effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "The two classes, 2004 (no tech) and 2005 (tech) were compared on pretest FCI scores using independent samples t-test, t(87) = 1.23, p = .220, indicating that the two classes started with equivalent understanding of Newtonian Mechanics."
