Sentiment analysis of Task 1 interview responses using VADER compound scores, averaged over Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q7 and pooled by model within priming groups. The article reports that "Scores are always positive under the “Math Lover” condition" while Grok 4.1 Fast and Magistral Small receive negative scores under the Math Hater condition.

"Fig. 2b shows that emotional tone is influenced by priming. Scores are always positive under the “Math Lover” condition. Mistral Small 4 and Mistral Small 3.2 achieve the highest scores, while Granite 4 Tiny achieves the lowest."
