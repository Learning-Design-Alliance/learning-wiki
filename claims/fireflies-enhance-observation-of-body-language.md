## Evidence

### Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020

Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An. (2020). How Peripheral Interactive Systems Can Support Teachers with Differentiated Instruction: Using FireFlies as a Probe. To appear in DIS '20, July 6–10, 2020, Eindhoven, Netherlands. https://doi.org/10.1145/3357236.3395497

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Teacher report from the six-week co-design and field-test study: P7 used continuous feedback during his instruction and contrasted it with lessons without the prototype, saying he could "immediately see when students are no longer listening" when red lamps stood out.

> "The red lights stand out. When I look in their direction I immediately see when students are no longer listening."
