### Muellenbach J 2026

Muellenbach J, Robinson K, Chen H, Bright HS IV, Fitterling L. (2026). Board exam preparation resource trends in academic health sciences libraries serving colleges of osteopathic medicine programs. Journal of the Medical Library Association : JMLA. https://doi.org/10.5195/jmla.2026.2320

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Phase one survey with parallel funding items (Q1a library-funded, Q2a non-library-funded) to reduce misclassification. The discussion states that "about half of the funding for these resources originates outside the library budget", referring to board prep resources broadly, not to the high-cost ones specifically; the survey counts subscriptions by funder rather than dollars. For the listed high-cost resources Table 2 shows mostly non-library funding (for example, TrueLearn COMLEX Level 1: 20 other-department and 3 library subscriptions; OnlineMedEd: 7 and 0; n = 35 library directors).

> "Survey responses revealed that high-cost subscriptions, including TrueLearn (COMLEX Level 1, Level 2, and COMAT Shelf Exams), ScholarRx, and OnlineMedEd, were often funded by non-library units such as the Dean's office, Academic Affairs, or student support departments (Table 2)."
