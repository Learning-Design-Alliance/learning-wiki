## Evidence

### Yasuda Yukinori 2026

Yasuda Yukinori, Takehara Shoichiro, Inoue Soichiro. (2026). Quantitative analysis of gaze and body movement differences by proficiency in direct and video laryngoscope intubation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44432-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Eye-tracking experiment with six experts and eight novices using a video laryngoscope; experts gazed 0.469 ± 0.081 m versus novices 0.402 ± 0.075 m, but "no statistically significant difference" was found. Equivalence was not tested.

> "the result revealed no statistically significant difference between the two groups [t (12) = 1.476, p = 0.166]."
