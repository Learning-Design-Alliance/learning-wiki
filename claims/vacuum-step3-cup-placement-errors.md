### Cuerva Marcos Javier 2026

Cuerva Marcos Javier, Durá Candela, Aramendia Andrés, Cortes Marta, Lopez Francisco, Bartha José Luis. (2026). Vacuum 5-step technique versus OdonAssist: Which is easier to learn for professionals without experience in assisted vaginal births? A simulation study. International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70836

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Error analysis across 24 training sessions: 24 mistakes among 21 vacuum AVBs with errors and 10 mistakes among 10 OdonAssist AVBs with errors. The authors suggest the need to adjust cup displacement distance per case may explain why participants found OdonAssist easier.

> "Of the vacuum AVB errors, 20 (83.3%) occurred during step 3, as the center of the cup was not optimally positioned in relation to the flexion point. For the OdonAssist, the most common error occurred four times (40%) and involved holding the sleeve while applying the OdonAssist."
