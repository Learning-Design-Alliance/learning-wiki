Evidence¶

Xie Guanli 2026¶

Xie Guanli, Liao Jianglong, Tang Xiaoxia, Fu Han, Liu Duo, Deng Li, Wang Tao, Hong Xin. (2026). The impact of generative artificial intelligence on clinical skills and knowledge acquisition in medical undergraduates: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09458-3

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of two studies pooling academic writing scores: one cohort study of 128 clinical medicine students showing MD = 5.80 (95%CI 4.58 to 7.02, P < 0.00001) favouring GenAI.

"GenAI can improve the academic writing scores for both clinical medicine students (P < 0.00001, MD = 5.80, 95%CI: 4.58 to 7.02, one cohort study, n = 128), and nursing students (P < 0.00001, MD = 5.20, 95%CI: 3.34 to 7.06, one quasi-experimental study, n = 203)"

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis pooling academic writing scores for nursing students: one quasi-experimental study of 203 nursing students showing MD = 5.20 (95%CI 3.34 to 7.06, P < 0.00001) favouring GenAI.

"GenAI can improve the academic writing scores for both clinical medicine students (P < 0.00001, MD = 5.80, 95%CI: 4.58 to 7.02, one cohort study, n = 128), and nursing students (P < 0.00001, MD = 5.20, 95%CI: 3.34 to 7.06, one quasi-experimental study, n = 203)"
