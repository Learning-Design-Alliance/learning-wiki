Evidence¶

Amy Ogan 2015¶

Amy Ogan, Erin Walker, Ryan Baker, Ma. Mercedes T. Rodrigo, Jose Carlo Soriano, Maynor Jimenez Castro. (2015). Towards Understanding How to Assess Help-Seeking Behavior Across Cultures. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0034-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Tukey post-hoc comparisons following a one-way ANOVA on pre-test scores across the three sites; the article prints means and SDs and reports "significant country by test time interaction" without an effect size.

"the U.S. site (M=0.634, SD= 0.391) had higher prior knowledge than both other groups ( p<0.001), while the Costa Rica site (M=0.331, SD=0.297) had higher prior knowledge than the Philippines site (M=0.181, SD=0.271)"
