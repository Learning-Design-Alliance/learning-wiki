## Evidence

### Smits-van der Nat 2024

Smits-van der Nat, M., van der Wilt, F., Meeter, M., & van der Veen, C. (2024). The Value of Pretend Play for Social Competence in Early Childhood: A Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09884-z

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression covariate result across kstudies = 34, kES = 188, with mean age of each study sample as the centered age predictor. The article states the estimated correlation "can be expected to be 0.26 higher" for 3-year-olds than for 8-year-olds.

> "However, children’s age was found to have a small negative effect on the relation between pretend play and social competence (g =  − 0.004, 95% CI [− 0.008, − 0.000], p < 0.05)."
