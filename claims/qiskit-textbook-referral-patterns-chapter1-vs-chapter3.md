## Evidence

### Wootton 2021

Wootton, J. R., Harkins, F., Bronn, N. T., Carrera Vazquez, A., Phan, A., & Asfaw, A. T. (2021). Teaching quantum computing with an interactive textbook. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1109/qce52317.2021.00058](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce52317.2021.00058)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational referral analysis shown in Fig. 3, comparing referral types for Chapter 1, Chapter 2 and Section 8.1. The authors report that "around a quarter of referrals to Chapter 3 come from search engines", in contrast to Chapter 1's mostly internal referrals. No effect size is reported.

> "Though most referrals come from readers navigating the textbook it- self, around a quarter of referrals to Chapter 3 come from search engines. Indeed, the single URL that makes the most referrals is google.com."
