## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jokinen Ewa 2020

Jokinen Ewa, Mikkola Tomi S., Härkki Päivi. (2020). Simulator training and residents’ first laparoscopic hysterectomy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-07270-3

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis (Pearson/Spearman) within the randomized trial linking simulator practice performance to live-operation assessments. All printed coefficients were below 0.10 in magnitude, e.g. "correlation coefficient − 0.080, − 0.055, − 0.152, and − 0.079"; operative time r = 0.305 and blood loss r = −0.038 were also reported non-significant.

> "There was no correlation between hysterectomy module composite scores and scores in GRS, LH-OSATS, GRS + LH-OSATS, or VAS (correlation coefficient − 0.080, − 0.055, − 0.152, and − 0.079, respectively)"
