Qualitative thematic analysis conducted over three months by two independent reviewers coding free-text responses from the 114 debriefing tools; "six key themes" emerged. The article notes themes appeared as a strength in one event and a weakness in another.

> "Thematic analysis of the survey results for each PRD tool revealed six key themes: (1) communication, (2) quality of care, (3) team function & dynamics, (4) resource allocation, (5) preparation and response, and (6) support."
