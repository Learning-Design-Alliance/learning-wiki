q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative theme from discussion posts and reflections in the 14-week course. Participants reported using AI when having "a difficult time starting an assignment" (P2) and later to "help refine my writing and provide diversity in the types of verbs I use" (P3).

"Initially used to overcome writer's block, the AI interactions later facilitated advanced brainstorming for refining writing styles and achieving rhetorical objectives."
