## Subclaims

## Evidence

### C. Savage 2009

C. Savage, D. McGrath, T. McIntyre, M. Wegener, M. Williamson. (2009). Teaching Physics Using Virtual Reality. https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.5776 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0910.5776](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0910.5776)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

First iteration of the authors' iterative development-and-evaluation cycle: six third-year ANU physics students in a one-hour structured lab covering free evolution, position measurement, and the quantum Zeno effect, with pre/post confidence testing and open-ended written summaries. Ratings are descriptive Likert responses; no effect size is printed.

> "On Likert scale questions, all six students rated QSim as either “enormously useful” or “quite useful” as a learning tool."
