## Evidence

### van der Schaaf 2016

van der Schaaf, M., Donkers, J., Slof, B., Moonen-van Loon, J., van Tartwijk, J., Driessen, E., Badii, A., Serban, O., Ten Cate, O. (2016). Improving workplace-based assessment and feedback by an E-portfolio enhanced with learning analytics. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9496-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online semi-structured survey in two rounds with eight experts (heads of department or main supervisors of the six participating institutes), assessing digital sketches and mock-ups of the feedback modules; results were analyzed descriptively.

> "Findings indicate that the experts reached an agreement that the supervisors considered the VIZ module more useful than the JIT module feedback."

### van der Schaaf 2016 (2)

van der Schaaf, M., Donkers, J., Slof, B., Moonen-van Loon, J., van Tartwijk, J., Driessen, E., Badii, A., Serban, O., Ten Cate, O. (2016). Improving workplace-based assessment and feedback by an E-portfolio enhanced with learning analytics. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9496-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Second survey round using an interactive clickable mock-up after the JIT module was modified (e.g., messages now inform the learner how to reach the next entrustment level, and gaps feedback was added). Experts indicated the feedback "might help to clarify what good performance is for learners".

> "In the second round, only the learner just-in-time feedback was surveyed in an interactive clickable mock-up E-portfolio-environment. All the experts strongly agreed with the design of the personalized feedback just-in-time messages in the second round of the survey."
