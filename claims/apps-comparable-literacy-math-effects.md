# App effects on literacy (g = 0.35) and math (g = 0.29) are comparable

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### James Kim 2021

James Kim, Joshua Gilbert, Qun Yu, Charles Gale. (2021). Measures Matter: A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Educational Apps on Preschool to Grade 3 Children's Literacy and Math Skills. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584211004183

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Unconditional RVE meta-regression models estimated effects separately by domain; literacy g = 0.35 and math g = 0.29. The article reports "no diﬀerences in the impacts of educational apps that focused on literacy or math."

> "similar Hedges’s g of +0.35 in literacy (95% CI [0.13, 0.57]) +0.29 in math (95% CI [0.16, 0.43])"
