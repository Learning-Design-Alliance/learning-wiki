## Evidence

### Golos Anat 2019

Golos Anat, Tekuzener Esti. (2019). Perceptions, expectations and satisfaction levels of occupational therapy students prior to and after practice placement and comparison of practice placement models. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1762-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Paired t-tests among all 155 students (N = 155 in Table 2) show pre-to-post increases in both skill categories: personal skills Cohen's d = .737 and professional skills d = 1.17, both p < .001. The article states these results "support the contribution of practice placements to students' professional behavior and personal practical skills."

> "a significant increase in scores was found from pre- to post-placement regarding personal and professional skills (t[df] = 7.82[154], p < .001; t[df] = 14.24[154], p < .001, respectively)"
