## Evidence

### Beck Amanda P 2025

Beck Amanda P. (2025). Assessment of first-year medical student perceptions in the development of self-directed learning skills in a single medical school course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06890-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA across eleven facilitator groups for the 89 paired students at course midpoint; midpoint total-score means ranged from 13.38 (Facilitator 8) to 17.73 (Facilitator 4). The article prints the F statistic and p value but no effect size.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in student self-assessment total scores among facilitator groups at the midpoint of the course as determined by the ANOVA (F(10,78) = 2.49, p = 0.012)"

### Beck Amanda P 2025 (2)

Beck Amanda P. (2025). Assessment of first-year medical student perceptions in the development of self-directed learning skills in a single medical school course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06890-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA across the same eleven facilitator groups at course endpoint for the 89 paired students; the article reports no statistically significant difference among groups. No effect size is printed.

> "However, there was no statistically significant difference in total score of self-assessed competency among facilitator groups at the endpoint of the CRE course (F(10,78) = 1.11, p > 0.05)"
