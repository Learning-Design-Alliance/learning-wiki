## Evidence

### Zhang Shuai-Long 2024

Zhang Shuai-Long, Ren Si-Jing, Zhu Dong-Mei, Liu Tian-Yao, Wang Lian, Zhao Jing-Hui, Fan Xiao-Tang, Gong Hong. (2024). Which novel teaching strategy is most recommended in medical education? A systematic review and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06291-4

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Network meta-analysis of 13 studies encompassing 849 students reporting satisfaction scores. Printed effect sizes: SBL versus PBL SMD = 1.62 and versus TBL SMD = 2.77; SBL (2.64, 95% CI 1.28–4.00), FC (1.34) and PBL (1.02) also beat LBL; SUCRA showed "a high probability (96.2) that SBL would most significantly enhance student satisfaction scores".

> "the students’ satisfaction scores were significantly higher in students learning with SBL strategy than in those students learning with PBL (1.62, 95% CI (0.01 to 3.22)) and TBL (2.77, 95% CI (0.43 to 5.11))."
