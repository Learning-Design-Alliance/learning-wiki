Evidence¶

Sinaga 2017¶

Megawati Sinaga. (2017). The Effect of Roundtable and Clustering Teaching Techniques and Students' Personal Traits on Students' Achievement in Descriptive Writing. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.6p.69

q2 · i? · causal · r1

A Tukey (Tuckey-test) post-hoc comparison followed the significant interaction in the 2x2 factorial experiment; this pairwise contrast found the introvert-Roundtable cell mean higher than the introvert-Clustering cell mean. No effect size is printed for the contrast.

"The mean of students with introvert personal traits taught by using Roundtable teaching technique is higher than the mean of students with introvert personal trait taught by using Clustering teaching technique;"

Sinaga 2017 (2)¶

Megawati Sinaga. (2017). The Effect of Roundtable and Clustering Teaching Techniques and Students' Personal Traits on Students' Achievement in Descriptive Writing. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.6p.69

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Another Tukey pairwise contrast from the same post-hoc analysis found the extrovert-Clustering cell mean higher than the extrovert-Roundtable cell mean, completing the crossover pattern implied by the significant interaction. No effect size is printed.

"The mean of students with extrovert personal trait taught by using Clustering teaching technique is higher than the mean of students with extrovert personal trait taught by using Roundtable teaching technique."
