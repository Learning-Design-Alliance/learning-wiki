## Evidence

### Wu Yaqiu 2026

Wu Yaqiu, Cheng Meixiong. (2026). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09526-8

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of four studies (185 intervention, 182 control participants). The review reports "a pooled SMD of 1.80 (95% CI: 0.19–3.41)" with very high heterogeneity (I² = 97.0%, p < 0.0001).

> "the flipped classroom teaching model had a significant advantage in enhancing procedural/technical skills among learners in medical education, with a pooled SMD of 1.80 (95% CI: 0.19–3.41), and the difference was statistically significant"

### Wu Yaqiu 2026 (2)

Wu Yaqiu, Cheng Meixiong. (2026). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09526-8

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Learner-type subgroup analysis for procedural/technical skills (Fig. 3D): a significant between-subgroup difference (p = 0.0131), with larger improvement among residents; subgroups partly based on single studies.

> "a statistically significant difference was observed between residents and undergraduates (p = 0.0131), indicating that the flipped classroom model produced significantly different effects across learner groups, with a more pronounced improvement in procedural/technical skills among residents"

### Wu Yaqiu 2026 (3)

Wu Yaqiu, Cheng Meixiong. (2026). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom in medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09526-8

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Course-type subgroup analysis (Fig. 3C): theoretical-course SMD = 4.11 (95% CI 3.42–4.80) from a single study versus SMD = 1.03 (95% CI 0.14–1.93) pooled across three practice-course studies; between-subgroup p < 0.0001.

> "the single study involving a theoretical course reported a very large effect (SMD = 4.11, 95% CI 3.42–4.80), whereas the pooled effect across three practice-oriented course studies was SMD = 1.03 (95% CI 0.14–1.93)"
