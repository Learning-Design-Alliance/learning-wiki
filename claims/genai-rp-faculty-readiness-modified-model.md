## Evidence

### Stella Y. Kim 2026

Stella Y. Kim, Ji Yae Bong, Daniel Maxwell, Beth Oyarzun. (2026). Development and validation of Generative Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Perception (GenAI-RP) Scale for faculty and students in higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10713-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CFA of the faculty readiness scale (N=132): the initial three-factor model showed inadequate fit (CFI 0.87, RMSEA 0.118); modification indices suggested Item 9 load on both Factors 2 and 3 and correlated errors between Items 6 and 15 and Items 15 and 16. The final Model.c reached CFI 0.92, TLI 0.90, RMSEA 0.094, SRMR 0.061.

> "After incorporating these two model modifications, the final model for instructors also revealed adequate fit. This final model is presented in Fig. 1."
