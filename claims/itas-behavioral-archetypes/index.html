Iizalaarab Elhaimeur and Nikos Chrisochoides 2026¶

Iizalaarab Elhaimeur and Nikos Chrisochoides. (2026). From Prototype to Classroom: An Intelligent Tutoring System for Quantum Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24807 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.24807

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative archetype analysis of pilot deployment data across video playback, code execution, chat, checkpoint, and session events. The article describes the self-directed, tutor-reliant, late engager, and passive consumer archetypes; the passive consumer never signals confusion and was detected only by the analytics agent.

"A controlled demonstration cannot produce a late engager. A single-session test cannot produce a passive consumer detectable only through longitudinal data."

Aggregate observation from the pilot deployment's module-level engagement distribution. The re-engagement when Module 5 was introduced is reported descriptively as a pattern emerging only from sustained deployment; no effect size is printed.

"A mid-semester re-engagement was observed when Module 5 was introduced, suggesting that new content can recover students who had paused the self-paced track."
