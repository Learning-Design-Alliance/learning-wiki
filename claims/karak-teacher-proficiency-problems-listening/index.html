Alrawashdeh 2017¶

Alrawashdeh, A. I. & Al-zayed, N. N. (2017). Difficulties That English Teachers Encounter while Teaching Listening Comprehension and Their Attitudes towards Them. English Language Teaching, 10(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n5p167

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Questionnaire domain on teacher proficiency among 55 Karak English teachers; overall mean 2.81 (medium). Several items fell in the medium range, including lack of motivation and insufficient teaching experience.

"The results in Table 2 above show that the means ranged between (2.1 - 4.18 ). Results indicate that the medium ranges are stated as “watching listening comprehension courses from the internet will not add anything to my experience in teaching”, “Teachers are not motivated in teaching listening comprehension”"
