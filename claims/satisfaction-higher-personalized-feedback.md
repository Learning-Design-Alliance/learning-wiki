## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experimental-group ratings exceeded control-group ratings on all four survey items (clarity 4.5 ± 0.5 vs 3.1 ± 0.9; usefulness 4.6 ± 0.4 vs 3.2 ± 1.0; motivation 4.3 ± 0.6 vs 3.0 ± 1.1; satisfaction 4.7 ± 0.3 vs 3.3 ± 0.8; p < 0.001 for all items). ## Evidence

### Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur 2025

Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur, Ozbey Furkan, Yilmaz Birkan Eyup. (2025). Effect of artificial intelligence-assisted personalized feedback on radiographic diagnostic performance of dental students: a controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07875-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test 10-item Likert questionnaire (KMO = 0.83, Cronbach's alpha = 0.89) compared between groups with the Mann-Whitney U test. "significantly higher ratings" on all four items; only means, SDs and p-values are printed.

> "Students in the experimental group gave significantly higher ratings than those in the control group in terms of clarity (4.5 ± 0.5), usefulness (4.6 ± 0.4), impact on motivation (4.3 ± 0.6), and overall satisfaction (4.7 ± 0.3) regarding the feedback they received (p < 0.001 for all items)."
