Between-group comparison of four simulator-use groups (EyeSi n = 75, HMS n = 27, other n = 7, multiple n = 61). "HMS users reported significantly more training time in total compared to all other simulator users"; HMS users also had more frequent supervision (88.9% vs. 63% for EyeSi, p < 0.01) and higher license support (p = 0.009).
