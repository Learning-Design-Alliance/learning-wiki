## Evidence

### Rafiq R 2025

Rafiq R, Matthews H, Abedalreza D, Yahya F, Jones MA. (2025). Card games are effective tools to enhance foundation year health and safety inductions. FEBS Open Bio 15(12). https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.70140

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A one-group pre-post survey of 91 foundation year students measured self-perceived knowledge on 10-point scales before and after the card game session, using paired t-tests. The article reports "Significant increases in perceived knowledge" across all six areas, e.g. health and safety knowledge rising from 6.4 ± 2.1 to 8.4 ± 1.5.

> "Significant increases in perceived knowledge were identified relating to Health and Safety knowledge (Pre: 6.4 ± 2.1, Post: 8.4 ± 1.5, P < 0.0001, Fig. 6A), personal protective equipment (Pre: 7.6 ± 2.0, Post: 8.7 ± 1.5, P < 0.0001, Fig. 6B)"
