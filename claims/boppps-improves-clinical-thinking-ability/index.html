Random-effects pooling of two studies (197 participants) reporting clinical thinking ability scores; pooled effect size SMD = 0.58 (95% CI 0.29–0.86), with I² = 0 reported in Table 5.

"The BOPPPS group exhibited significantly enhanced clinical thinking ability compared to the control group, demonstrating statistical significance (SMD 0.58, 95% CI 0.29–0.86; P < 0.001; Table 5)."
