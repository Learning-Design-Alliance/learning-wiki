## Evidence

### Freel 2011

Freel, B. L., Clark, M. D., Anderson, M. L., Gilbert, G. L., Musyoka, M. M., & Hauser, P. C. (2011). Deaf Individuals’ Bilingual Abilities: American Sign Language Proficiency, Reading Skills, and Family Characteristics. Psychology, 2(1), 18-23. https://doi.org/10.4236/psych.2011.21003

`q3 · i3`

Correlational analysis in the 55-participant sample relating maternal education level, used as a proxy for family socioeconomic status, to the combined Bilingual Ability score. The study reports a significant positive relationship with printed effect size r = 0.521 (p = 0.000).

> "Additionally, the relationship between maternal education le vel and bilingual abilities was significant, with higher levels of  maternal education related to more proficient bilingual abilities, r (54) = 0.521, p = 0.000."
