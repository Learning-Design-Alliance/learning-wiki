## Evidence

### Borman 2013

Borman, G. D., & Grigg, J. (2013). An Effort to Close Achievement Gaps at Scale through Self-Affirmation. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563077

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel ITT models show a Hispanic main effect of -10.887 (reading) and -8.957 (mathematics), both p < 0.01, with Hispanic x Self-Affirmation interactions of 9.367 (p < 0.05) and 11.663 (p < 0.001). The article reports the "0.18 SD" Hispanic gap was "not apparent in the group that was assigned to complete the self-affirmation exercises."

> "Hispanic students in the comparison group scored on average 10.9 and 9.0 points (0.18 SD) lower on the reading and mathematics test, and this gap was not apparent in the group that was assigned to complete the self-affirmation exercises"
