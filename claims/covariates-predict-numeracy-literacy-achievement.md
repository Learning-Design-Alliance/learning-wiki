## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mother's education, income quintile, and age-9 mathematics and literacy scores positively predicted later mathematics achievement; earlier psychological difficulties negatively predicted it. [→ Murphy 2022](#murphy-2022)

## Evidence

### Murphy 2022

Murphy, D., Leonard, S. J., Taylor, L. K., & Santos, F. H. (2022). Educational achievement and bullying: The mediating role of psychological difficulties. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1487–1501. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12511

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Control-variable coefficients from the final GUI path model for mathematics; gender and liking school were the exceptions that did not significantly predict mathematics scores.

> "mother's education (β = .15, p < .001), income quantile (β=.12, p <.001), liking math at age 9 (β=.05, p <.001), and both literacy (β = .20, p < .001) and math (β = .27, p < .001) scores at age 9 were all positively linked to later math achievement, whereas psychological difficulties at age 9 (β = −.06, p < .001)"
