# Elementary student mobility in CPS decreased between 1995 and 2007, with school-year stability rising from 87.9 to 91.2 percent and school-year in-mobility falling from 11.4 to 7.1 percent

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Elementary students became less mobile over time: school-year stability increased from 87.9 percent (fall 1994 cohort) to 91.2 percent (2006-07), and school-year in-mobility decreased from 11.4 to 7.1 percent. [→ Marisa de la Torre 2009](#marisa-de-la-torre-2009)

## Evidence

### Marisa de la Torre 2009

Marisa de la Torre, Julia Gwynne. (2009). Changing Schools: A Look at Student Mobility Trends in Chicago Public Schools Since 1995. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/changing-schools-look-student-mobility-trends-chicago-public-schools-1995

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Trend analysis of CPS student record data from 1995 to 2007 reported in Chapter 1. The report states that "87.9 percent remained in the same school throughout the school year; by 2006–07 this rate increased to 91.2 percent," and the school-year in-mobility rate dropped from 11.4 to 7.1 percent.

> "of the students enrolled in the system in the fall of 1994, 87.9 percent remained in the same school throughout the school year; by 2006–07 this rate increased to 91.2 percent."
