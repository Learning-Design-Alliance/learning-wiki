Oakes 2020¶

q2 · i? · review · r1

Narrative review of 143 research studies of community schools, reported by the Learning Policy Institute and cited in this report. The review found improvements in "student attendance, academic achievement, and behavior" plus more positive school climates; no effect sizes are printed.

"The Learning Policy Institute's review of 143 research studies of community schools found that community schools can meet the needs of students in high-poverty schools by improving student attendance, academic achievement, and behavior, in addition to providing more positive school climates."
