## Evidence

### Bedyńska S 2018

Bedyńska S, Krejtz I and Sedek G (2018) Chronic Stereotype Threat Is Associated With Mathematical Achievement on Representative Sample of Secondary Schoolgirls: The Role of Gender Identification, Working Memory, and Intellectual Helplessness. Front. Psychol. 9:428. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00428

`q3 · i?`

Descriptive correlation analysis of the representative sample of secondary schoolgirls. Mathematical achievement was negatively correlated with stereotype threat and intellectual helplessness, and positively correlated with working memory functions. The article reports "mathematical achievement was negatively correlated (p < 0.01) with stereotype threat".

> "mathematical achievement was negatively correlated (p < 0.01) with stereotype threat and intellectual helplessness, and positively correlated ( p < 0.01) with all three functional aspects of working memory"
