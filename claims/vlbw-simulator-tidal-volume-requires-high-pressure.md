### Lengua Hinojosa Patricia 2022

Lengua Hinojosa Patricia, Eifinger Frank, Wagner Michael, Herrmann Jochen, Wolf Monika, Ebenebe Chinedu Ulrich, von der Wense Axel, Jung Philipp, Mai Aram, Bohnhorst Bettina, Longardt Ann Carolin, Hillebrand Georg, Schmidtke Susanne, Guthmann Florian, Aderhold Martina, Schwake Ida, Sprinz Maria, Singer Dominique, Deindl Philipp. (2022). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ventilator bench testing at three intensities (Pip 15, 20, 25 cm H2O), each measurement performed three times. The article reports that only the highest intensity generated tidal volumes similar to the printed reference range of 5-7 ml/kg in three simulators, with Premie HAL S2209 at 1.6 ± 0 ml/kg.

> "Only ventilation with the highest intensity using a high peak inspiratory pressure of 25 cm H2O generated tidal volumes similar to target tidal volumes during mechanical ventilation of preterm infants in three of the simulators (Premature Anne: 4.9 ± 0.1 ml/kg, Premature AirwayPaul: 6.2 ± 0.1 ml/kg, Preterm Baby: 7.1 ± 0.1 ml/kg, reference: 5–7 ml/kg)."
