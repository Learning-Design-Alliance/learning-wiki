Christina Clark Tuttle 2013¶

Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/kipp-middle-schools-impacts-on-achievement-and-other-outcomes

q1 · i? · causal · r?

The authors' characterization of the existing evidence base: prior studies "have included only a few KIPP schools or have had methodological limitations", motivating a larger, more rigorous national evaluation. This is the authors' summary judgment, not a tested result.

"Prior research has suggested that KIPP schools have positive impacts on student achievement, but most of the studies have included only a few KIPP schools or have had methodological limitations."
