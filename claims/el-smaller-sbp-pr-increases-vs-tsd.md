### Zhu Minmin 2020

Zhu Minmin, Yu Hongbing, Xie Bo, Li Hongwen, He Qian, Li Huimin, Su Jing, Li Xueqi. (2020). Experiential learning for children's dental anxiety: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-020-01204-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome from the cluster-randomized trial: linear mixed-effects model adjusting for gender, school and baseline value. The article reports "a − 2.6 mmHg adjusted mean difference"; no standardized effect size printed.

> "The mean change of SBP from before to after the PFS was − 2.7 in the EL and 0.7 in the TSD group, with a − 2.6 mmHg adjusted mean difference (95% CI: − 4.4 to − 0.9 mmHg; P = 0.036)"

### Zhu Minmin 2020 (2)

Zhu Minmin, Yu Hongbing, Xie Bo, Li Hongwen, He Qian, Li Huimin, Su Jing, Li Xueqi. (2020). Experiential learning for children's dental anxiety: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-020-01204-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary physiological outcome measured with an upper-arm electronic sphygmomanometer before and after PFS, analyzed by linear mixed-effects model with baseline adjustment. No standardized effect size printed.

> "The mean PR in the EL group increased by 0.2 Beats Per Minute (BPM), while in the TSD group it increased by 2.5 BPM, for an adjusted mean difference of − 1.7 BPM (95% CI: − 3.4 to − 0.1 BPM; P = 0.038)"
