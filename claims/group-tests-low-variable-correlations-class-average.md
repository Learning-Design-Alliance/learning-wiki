## Evidence

### Sunne 1917

Sunne, Dagny. (1917). The Relation of Class Standing to College Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology, VIII(4), 193-211. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071727

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of 99 freshmen tested with a series of standardized group tests scored on a scale of 100; the article reports the correlations "range from 0 to .25" and that the only negative correlation was foreign languages with Marble Statue (-.14).

> "The correlations of these tests with the general class averages range from 0 to .25, being in ascending order, Genus-Species 0, Opposites .08, Cancellation .11, Substitution .16, Marble Statue .20, Word-building and Ink Blots .23, and Part-Whole .25."
