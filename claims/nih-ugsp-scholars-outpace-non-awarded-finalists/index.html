Tim Silva 2005¶

The Mathematica evaluation compared scholarship awardees against a built-in comparison group of finalists selected but not awarded scholarships, finding scholars advancing toward biomedical research careers at "substantially higher rates" than non-awarded finalists. No effect size is printed on the page.
