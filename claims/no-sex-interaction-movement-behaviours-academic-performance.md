`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the data-analysis step preceding the main mixed models, linear regressions tested the interaction of sex with each movement behaviour in relation to academic performance. The article reports that "no significant interaction was found between sex and physical activity, recreational screen time, and sleep duration in relation to academic performance (p > 0.01)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "As no significant interaction was found between sex and physical activity, recreational screen time, and sleep duration in relation to academic performance (p > 0.01), all analyses were performed on the entire sample."
