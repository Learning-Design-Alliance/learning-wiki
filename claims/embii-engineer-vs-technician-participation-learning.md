# In engineering maker-based inquiry in physics, only participation in the legitimate form of an engineer contributed to students' learning of the related physics, while participation solely as a technician did not

## Evidence

### J Guisasola 2024

J Guisasola, J Ametller, D Baccino, M Carli, S Kapon, A C Marti, M Monteiro, O Pantano, T Peer, P Sarriugarte, M Schvartzer, A Suárez and K Zuza. (2024). Connecting research in physics education, curriculum decisions and teaching practices. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.10674](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.10674)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Longitudinal sociolinguistic discourse analysis of an 18-month ethnographic case study of two 11th-grade students in an engineering-maker project, identifying two legitimate forms of participation. The analysis found that "only the participation as an engineer contributed to the students' learning of the related physics".

> "While both forms of participation contributed greatly to the success of the EMBI, only the participation as an engineer contributed to the students' learning of the related physics in terms of the figured world of learning physics in school, as well the figured world of authentic scientific inquiry."

### J Guisasola 2024 (2)

J Guisasola, J Ametller, D Baccino, M Carli, S Kapon, A C Marti, M Monteiro, O Pantano, T Peer, P Sarriugarte, M Schvartzer, A Suárez and K Zuza. (2024). Connecting research in physics education, curriculum decisions and teaching practices. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.10674](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.10674)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic case study data (videotaped sessions, interviews, artefacts) show the low-achieving student developed deep ownership and built the physical model, but "did not understand some of the very basic physics principles" of the design; the authors interpret this as an unequitable opportunity to engage in doing physics.

> "our data also demonstrate that he did not understand some of the very basic physics principles that underpinned the engineering design."
