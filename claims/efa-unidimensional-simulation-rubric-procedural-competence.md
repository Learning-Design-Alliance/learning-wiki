## Evidence

### Ntumi Simon 2025

Ntumi Simon. (2025). Validating high-fidelity simulation for assessing procedural skills in nursing education: a kane framework approach in Ghana. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07724-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis of the adapted simulation rubric reported in Results (Table 1). The article states "All 14 items exhibited substantial factor loadings, ranging from 0.60 to 0.81"; it also reports Cronbach's alpha of 0.89, KMO of 0.88, Bartlett's χ²(91) = 864.21 (p < 0.001), and 61.2% of variance extracted.

> "All 14 items exhibited substantial factor loadings, ranging from 0.60 to 0.81, indicating a strong underlying common factor interpreted as procedural competence."
