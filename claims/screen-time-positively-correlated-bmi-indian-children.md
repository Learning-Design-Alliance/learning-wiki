## Evidence

### Sidiq Mohammad et al 2025

Sidiq Mohammad et al. (2025). Screen time exposure and academic performance, anxiety, and behavioral problems among school children. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19409

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of the cross-sectional survey of 537 parents of children aged 8–14 in five private schools in Greater Noida, India. The printed effect size is "r = 0.522, p < 0.001" for the screen time–BMI correlation. Mean BMI was 18.1 ± 3.2 kg/m2.

> "Screen time was positively correlated with children’s BMI (r = 0.522, p < 0.001), academic performance (r = 0.371, p < 0.001), the PSC version of the pediatric symptoms scale total score (r 0.504, p < 0.001)"
