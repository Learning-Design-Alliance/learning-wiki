## Evidence

### Jindal AA 2024

Jindal AA, Agnew T, Siriwardena D, Nikita E, Nolan W. (2024). An education-based intervention investigating the accuracy of community-based optometrists evaluating limbal anterior chamber depth. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-024-03492-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post vignette assessment of 52 optometrists grading 84 LACD images against an expert panel reference grade, before and after a 1.5-h education package. The article reports mean weighted kappa "increased to 0.47 (Moderate)" post-intervention; no effect size or significance test for the kappa change is printed.

> "Interobserver agreement between CBO and the ophthalmologist's reference grade using a mean weighted kappa for the 7-point LACD grading scale for assessment 1 was 0.42 (Moderate), 95% CI 0.36–0.48 (Slight-moderate). Following the education intervention for assessment 2, the mean increased to 0.47 (Moderate) 95% CI 0.42–0.53"
