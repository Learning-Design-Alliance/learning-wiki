Turnbull, B., & Sweetnam Evans, M. (2017). The effects of L1 and L2 group discussions on L2 reading comprehension. Reading in a Foreign Language, 29(1), 133–154. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Genre mentions in written recalls (Table 5) averaged 30% (control), 40% (L2 discussion), and 50% (L1 discussion); group 3 participants connected features like rhyming with poetry and authenticated the expository text.

> "Participants in group 3 showed the most awareness of textual genre across all four texts, with 50% of the participants mentioning genre; 20% more than in group 1 and 10% more than in group 2."
