Survey question #12 asked the 86 participants to evaluate their overall experience on a five-point Likert scale. The article reports ratings reflect "a generally positive perception of the event, with the majority of participants rating their experience as “Good” or higher."

> "A larger number rated the event as “Good” (36 participants, 41.86%), “Very Good” (26 participants, 30.23%), or “Excellent” (13 participants, 15.12%)."
