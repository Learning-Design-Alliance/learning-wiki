Teacher involvement in developing sustainable education materials for AI integration in green energy education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34422-4

q3 · i2 · associational · r1

Same PLS-SEM survey of 122 in-service teachers; the structural path from ESD Teaching Material development to AI Integration Ability was significant at p < 0.05 with a moderate f² = 0.127, per the hypothesis test and effect-size tables.

"The ESD Teaching Material construct also had a significant effect (β = 0.236, t = 1.936, p < 0.05), indicating that teachers who are actively involved in developing ESD-aligned teaching materials are more prepared to integrate AI in their classrooms."
