## Evidence

### Miller 2015

Miller, S., Gildea, A., Sloan, S., & Thurston, A. (2015). Physically Active Lessons: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581164

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test only cluster-randomised pilot in five primary schools (372 pupils analysed: intervention n = 205, control n = 167), analysed intention-to-treat with a clustered sandwich estimator. Adjusted numeracy effect was p=0.81 and literacy p=0.93, with "effect size estimates close to zero in both cases".

> "The results indicate that in this sample of pupils there were no differences between the intervention and control groups in relation to the primary outcomes (attainment in literacy or numeracy) with effect size estimates close to zero in both cases."
