Expanding conceptual evidence-building with the science of learning: integrating evidence weights, alignment estimates and iterative updates for educational innovations. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10732-w

q2 · i? · design · r2

The authors' interpretation drawn from applying the methodology to the I Can Read and SplashLearn cases, one a partially digital literacy program and one an entirely digital math platform. They report the ToC was continuously improved as new knowledge was gained and the product evolved through insights from implementation and feasibility studies. No effect sizes are reported.

"The case studies illustrate that the development of a Theory of Change is a process of supporting an iterative cycle for incorporating new research as it emerges, allowing the ToC to be continuously improved and updated as new knowledge is gained"
