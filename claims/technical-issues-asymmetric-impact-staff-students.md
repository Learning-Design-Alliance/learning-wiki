## Evidence

### MacKay 2018

MacKay, J. R. D. (2018). Show and ‘Tool’: How lecture recording transforms staff and student perspectives on lectures in higher education. University of Edinburgh. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.05.019

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding comparing staff and student responses in the grounded theory analysis. Student respondents complained that 'some lecturers do not know how to use the recording system (the red button)' and that missing sound made recordings pointless, while a staff participant called scheduling mismatch 'a very, very minor practical thing'.

> "The impact of technical issues are certainly lesser for the lecturer, who will give a similar lecture in another course or next year, but for the students in that class technical issues were seen to deprive them of a highly-valued resource."
