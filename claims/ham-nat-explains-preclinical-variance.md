## Evidence

### Kothe Christian 2013

Kothe Christian, Hissbach Johanna, Hampe Wolfgang. (2013). The Hamburg Selection Procedure for Dental Students – Introduction of the HAM-Nat as subject-specific test for study aptitude. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000889](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000889)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of four dental cohorts (2006–2010) predicting course grades from GPA and HAM-Nat; the article reports that "Mostly more than 5% of the academic study performance variance in the first two semesters was explained by the science test". Sample sizes in the regression models varied between 32 and 55 students.

> "Mostly more than 5% of the academic study performance variance in the first two semesters was explained by the science test (see Table 3 (Tab. 3))."
