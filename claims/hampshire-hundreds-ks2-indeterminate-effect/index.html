Evidence¶

Sandra McNally 2014¶

Sandra McNally, Amy Challen, Gill Wyness, Anne West, Philip Noden. (2014). Hampshire Hundreds Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581163

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Additional (non-protocol) intention-to-treat analysis using national Key Stage 2 standardised scores for all Year 6 pupils in the original 36 schools: reading effect size -0.038 (95% CI [-0.211, 0.135], n=1347) and maths -0.006 (95% CI [-0.177, 0.164], n=1351), both non-significant. The report says conclusions were "unchanged" using this sample.

"Again, our point estimates are close to zero with a l arge standard deviation. We cannot rule out positive or negative effects."
