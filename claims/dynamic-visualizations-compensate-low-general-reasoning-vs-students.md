## Evidence

### Sarah I. Hofer 2025

Sarah I. Hofer, Frank Reinhold. (2025). Scaffolding of learning activities: Aptitude-treatment-interaction effects in math?. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102177. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102177

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLMM analysis of post-test deviation scores from a randomized experiment with 332 6th-graders (140 VS students), testing scaffold-by-learner-characteristic interactions. The interaction term for general reasoning was significant, X2(8,68) = 24.96, p < .01, and "there was a positive effect of dynamic visualization for low levels of general reasoning".

> "While no notable interaction effect was found for APS students (Fig. 6), there was a positive effect of dynamic visualization for low levels of general reasoning and no effect for high levels of general reasoning in the VS subgroup (Fig. 7)."
