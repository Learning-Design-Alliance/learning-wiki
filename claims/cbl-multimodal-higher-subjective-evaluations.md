`q2 i?` On a five-point Likert questionnaire, the CBL multimodal group rated learning interest and initiative, study ability, clinical thinking ability and case analysis ability significantly higher than the traditional group, while course satisfaction did not differ. [→ Shi X 2025](#shi-x-2025)

## Evidence

### Shi X 2025

Shi X, Wang H, Yang F, Zhou J, Wei W. (2025). Application and evaluation of case-based multimodal imaging in the ocular fundus disease teaching of postgraduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06967-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous paper questionnaire (modified course evaluation questionnaire, Kirkpatrick levels 1–2) completed by all 34 students, 100% recovery. Table 3 prints learning interest and initiative "4.54 ± 0.29" vs "4.81 ± 0.16" (t = 3.50, P = 0.001); study ability, clinical thinking and case analysis ability also favoured CBL (P = 0.01, 0.02, 0.01); course satisfaction P = 0.83.
