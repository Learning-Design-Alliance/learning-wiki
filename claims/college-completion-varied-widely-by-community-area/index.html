McKoy 2021¶

McKoy, D., Usher, A., Nagaoka, J., & Khatri, S. (2021). Approaching Chicago student attainment from a community perspective. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/comm

Descriptive analysis of two- and four-year college completion for class-of-2013 immediate college enrollees, organized by community area and by high school. The report notes the by-high-school range was even greater, with 29 of 99 schools below 30 percent and four schools above 80 percent.
