## Evidence

### Ataya J 2026

Ataya J, Ismail A, Kousa A, Shahoud Z, Alhalbi R, Ghanem R, Ranjous Y, Zaitouna M. (2026). Assessing theoretical knowledge related to surgical and clinical skills in Syrian medical students: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051241

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of knowledge level across universities among 500 participants. The printed effect size Cramér's V = 0.217 falls in the small range (0.20–0.36 on the correlation-like scale); Damascus students had the highest proportion of excellent ratings.

> "University was significantly associated with knowledge level (χ2(12) = 59.12, Monte Carlo P < .001; bias-corrected Cramér's V = 0.217, 95% CI 0.165–0.294), corresponding to a small-to-moderate association."
