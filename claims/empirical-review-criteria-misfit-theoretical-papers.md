This is the editorial's argument about journal review practice: standard rubrics requiring evaluation of research design are "not relevant to theoretical scholarship" and may produce low reviewer scores. It is an author interpretation, not a tested result; no effect size is printed.

> "These criteria are not relevant to theoretical scholarship, and might lead reviewers to assume that the journal does not welcome theoretical work. Without other guidance, reviewers may provide low evaluation scores, including comments such as, “this paper does not provide empirical research, and thus cannot be published.”"
