## Evidence

### Ramadanov N 2026

Ramadanov N, Heinz M, Hable R, Becker R, Banke IJ. (2026). Surgeon experience in hip arthroscopy improves operative efficiency and reduces conversion to total hip arthroplasty: A meta‐analysis. Knee Surgery, Sports Traumatology, Arthroscopy. https://doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70431

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of six studies reporting modified Harris Hip Score, comparing late versus early experience phases. The estimate showed "a statistically significant improvement in mHHS for late cases", with no relevant heterogeneity (I² = 0%).

> "The pooled random‐effects estimate showed a statistically significant improvement in mHHS for late cases (MD 2.78; 95% CI 0.16 to 5.41)."

### Ramadanov N 2026 (2)

Ramadanov N, Heinz M, Hable R, Becker R, Banke IJ. (2026). Surgeon experience in hip arthroscopy improves operative efficiency and reduces conversion to total hip arthroplasty: A meta‐analysis. Knee Surgery, Sports Traumatology, Arthroscopy. https://doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70431

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of four studies reporting Non-Arthritic Hip Score; all included studies demonstrated higher postoperative scores in late cases. The estimate favoured "late cases (MD 8.41; 95% CI 1.99 to 14.83)", with moderate heterogeneity (I² = 47%).

> "The pooled random‐effects estimate demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in NAHS favouring late cases (MD 8.41; 95% CI 1.99 to 14.83)."
