## Evidence

### Henry 2025

Henry, L. N., Gross, R. A., & Hinshaw, S. P. (2025). Mathematics Achievement in Women with and without ADHD: Childhood Predictors and Developmental Trajectories into Adulthood. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 58(6). https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194241301044

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

LGCM with childhood global EF (Rey-Osterrieth Complex Figure copy-trial error proportion score) predicting math intercept and slope, covarying ADHD status, SES, and PIQ; model fit χ2(18) = 35.595, p = .03. Because higher scores mean more errors, the negative slope coefficient indicates worse EF predicts less math growth.

> "Global EF was not significantly related to initial math when adjusting for covariates (β = 0.003, p = .965), but it predicted growth in math achievement over time, (β = −0.342, p = .027)."
