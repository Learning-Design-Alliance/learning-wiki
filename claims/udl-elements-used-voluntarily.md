## Evidence

### Marvin Roski 2024

Marvin Roski, Ratan Sebastian, Ralph Ewerth, Anett Hoppe, Andreas Nehring. (2024). Learning analytics and the Universal Design for Learning (UDL): A clustering approach. Computers & Education 214 (2024) 105028. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105028

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' interpretive conclusion from the usage-pattern analysis: because elements such as text and video were used voluntarily and choices were stable, "it can be inferred that the different offerings are appreciated". This is an interpretation (type e), not a measured comparison, and no outcome benefit was tested.

> "The findings of this study show that all UDL-guided elements are used voluntarily among the learners. Since the described learning opportunities (e.g., text and video) are repetitive and learners tend to stick to their choices, it can be inferred that the different offerings are appreciated, and learners stick to their choices."
