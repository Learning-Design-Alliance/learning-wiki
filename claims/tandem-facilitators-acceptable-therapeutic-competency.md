Assessing competence of primary care respiratory healthcare professionals to deliver a psychologically-based intervention for people with COPD: results from the TANDEM study. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 35, 11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00416-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fidelity assessment of 180 audio-recorded TANDEM sessions from 36 participant cases (15% of intervention participants), coded with the validated 10-item CFARs scale. The mean total was "35.5 (SD = 7.0)" and "Fidelity (mean score of ≥ 30) was achieved in 28 (78%) out of 36 cases."
