## Evidence

### Tomlin D 2025

Tomlin D, Bowers P, Zonca B, Graydon K, Phillips J, Rance G. (2025). Speech-in-noise recognition as a predictor of academic achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324998

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Scatterplot analysis (Fig 3) of speech-in-noise scores against NAPLAN literacy measures by year level in the 108-child cohort (55 Grade 3, 53 Grade 5). The article reports significant Grade 3 correlations and "No such correlation was seen in the Grade 5 cohorts"; no correlation coefficients for these year-level analyses are printed.

> "These results demonstrate the significant correlations between NAPLAN literacy measures and speech-in-noise ability in the Grade 3 cohort. No such correlation was seen in the Grade 5 cohorts."
