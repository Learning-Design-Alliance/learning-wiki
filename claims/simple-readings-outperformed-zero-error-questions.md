### Wee 2014

Wee, Loo Kang, & Ning, Hwee Tiang. (2014). Vernier caliper and micrometer computer models using Easy Java Simulation and its pedagogical design features—ideas for augmenting learning with real instruments. Physics Education, 49(5), 493. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the same 70-student pilot's Google Site responses: simple rule-reading questions scored above 96% and 90%, while "With the introduction of zero errors, above 81% (Q 3,4) and 77% (Q 7) managed to get the correct answers".

> "Data from the student responses collected show that the students in this study scored above 96% correctly for Question 1 and 2 (Q 1,2) and 90% (Q 5,6) for simple reading of the rules (table 1) of vernier caliper and micrometer readings respectively."
