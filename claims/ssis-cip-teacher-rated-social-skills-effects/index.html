Evidence¶

DiPerna 2014¶

DiPerna, J. C., Lei, P., Bellinger, J., & Cheng, W. (2014). Efficacy of the Social Skills Improvement System – Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS – CIP) in the Primary Grades. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562704

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Multi-site cluster randomized trial in 39 classrooms (20 SSIS-CIP, 19 control) in two Pennsylvania districts; multilevel models adjusted for pretest and covariates. The article reports printed effect sizes of .22 (self-control) to .38 (social engagement): "SSIS-CIP effect sizes on posttest teacher ratings ranged from .22 (self-control) to .38 (social engagement)".
