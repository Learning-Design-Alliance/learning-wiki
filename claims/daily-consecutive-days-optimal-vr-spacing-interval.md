### Fahl Jan Torge 2023

Fahl Jan Torge, Duvivier Robbert, Reinke Laurens, Pierie Jean-Pierre E. N., Schönrock-Adema Johanna. (2023). Towards best practice in developing motor skills: a systematic review on spacing in VR simulator-based psychomotor training for surgical novices. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04046-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis across the limited sample of included studies comparing massed, daily and weekly schedules. The review concludes that "daily training on consecutive days" yielded superior training effects, while cautioning the sample is limited and heterogeneous.

> "A daily training on consecutive days, as adopted by Güldner et al. and Kang et al., resulted in a psychomotor training effect superior to that of a weekly training schedule [35, 37]."
