# The strategy advantage of linear-spatial materials is driven specifically by count-on, which children in that condition attempt and execute accurately more often at posttest

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A MANCOVA showed a time by condition interaction for count-on attempts, with linear-spatial children attempting count-on more frequently at posttest. [→ Schiffman J 2018](#schiffman-j-2018)
`q3 i3` A second MANCOVA showed a time by condition interaction for accurate execution of count-on favoring the linear-spatial condition. [→ Schiffman J 2018 (2)](#schiffman-j-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Schiffman J 2018

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA on proportion of problems attempting each advanced strategy in the randomized experiment. "For the count-on strategy, the results indicated a time by condition interaction, F(1,26) = 5.48, p = .03, ηp2 = .17"; at posttest linear-spatial children attempted count-on more often (Hedges' g = .95).

> "For the count-on strategy, the results indicated a time by condition interaction, F(1,26) = 5.48, p = .03, ηp2 = .17."

### Schiffman J 2018 (2)

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA on accurate execution of advanced strategies in the same experiment "found only a time by condition interaction for count-on: F(1,26) = 6.24, p = .02, ηp2 = .19"; linear-spatial children accurately executed count-on on more problems at posttest (3.79 vs. 1.07, Hedges' g = .86).

> "a MANCOVA examining changes in children's accuracy of executing the advanced strategies found only a time by condition interaction for count-on: F(1,26) = 6.24, p = .02, ηp2 = .19."
