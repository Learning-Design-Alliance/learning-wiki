## Evidence

### Hawthorne 2015

Hawthorne, K. A., Bol, L., Pribesh, S., & Suh, Y. (2015). Effects of Motivational Prompts on Motivation, Effort, and Performance on a Low-Stakes Standardized Test. Journal of General Education, 64(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064767

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study assigning four intact 100-level general education courses (87 undergraduates) to control, university, personal, or combined prompt conditions before the abbreviated ETS PP. The one-way ANOVA showed a "significant effect on the total mean ETS® PP score"; the personal condition's mean of 437.40 exceeded all others (Table 1).

> "Results from the one-way ANOVA indicated that the motivational prompts, and as a consequence, condition had a significant effect on the total mean ETS® PP score, F(3, 83) = 3.035, p < .05, η2 = .099."
