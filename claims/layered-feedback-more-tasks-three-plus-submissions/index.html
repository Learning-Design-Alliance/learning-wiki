Evidence¶

Jie Cao 2026¶

Jie Cao, Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Christian Schunn, Elizabeth A. McLaughlin, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). Assessing the Impact and Underlying Pathways of Sequenced AI Feedback on Student Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07469 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.07469

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Behavioral log analysis during learning-by-doing in the randomized experiment; the number of tasks (out of 13) with more than two submissions was the persistence indicator, with a significant between-group difference, d = 0.36.

"learners in the layered feedback group submitted three or more responses on significantly more tasks (M= 2.1) than those in the non-layered group (M= 1.5),t(197) = 2.5,p=.012,d= 0.36."
