### Stefania Varano 2024

Stefania Varano, Federico Di Giacomo. (2024). Design of a Multisensory Planetarium. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.05801](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.05801)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A preliminary evaluation session with 2 BVI users aged between 50 and 60 at the Institute for Blinds "Cavazza" in Bologna assessed the usability of the representation. After exploring the tablet and being informed of the mapping, users "correctly associated faster vibrations with closer objects and slower ones with objects further away." No effect size is reported.

> "Once informed about the fact that the objects they were touching represented stars visible in the night sky and the different vibrations meant different distances of such objects from Earth, they correctly associated faster vibrations with closer objects and slower ones with objects further away."
