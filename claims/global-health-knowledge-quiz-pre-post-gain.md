## Evidence

### Gruner D 2015

Gruner D, Pottie K, Archibald D, Allison J, Sabourin V, Belcaid I, McCarthy A, Brindamour M, Augustincic Polec L, Duke P. (2015). Introducing global health into the undergraduate medical school curriculum using an e-learning program: a mixed method pilot study. BMC Medical Education 15:142. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0421-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-tests on the 30-item knowledge quiz, analyzed in SPSS-21, showed the pooled pre-post gain from M = 23.19/30 to M = 24.15/30 was significant, t(58) = −2.36, p < 0.05 (two-tailed). No standardized effect size was printed for this quiz gain.

> "Overall there was an increase in conceptual knowledge in both groups with a change from the pre-test (M = 23.19/30, SD = 2.87) to the post-test (M = 24.15/30, SD = 2.94), t(58) = −2.36, p < 0.05 (two-tailed)."
