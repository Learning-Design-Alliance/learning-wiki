## Evidence

### Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Konstantin Fackeldey, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). FACET: Teacher-Centred LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems—Towards Personalized Educational Worksheets. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/13511610.2026.2727110](https://doi.org/10.1080/13511610.2026.2727110)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory expert evaluation with five K-12 in-service mathematics teachers (4 male, 1 female; M=38 years, SD=6.6; M=6.4 years experience, SD=2.7) comparing personalized FACET worksheets against a baseline non-adaptive worksheet on a 1–6 scale with think-aloud feedback. Clarity (M=4.68) and creativity (M=4.53) were also favorable.

> "The dimension didactical structure (M= 4.95,range= 3−6) and suitability of the task for learners were rated positively in all profiles (M= 5.05,range= 4−6), indicating that the personalization logic effectively aligned task demands with each learner’s cognitive and motivational characteristics."
