Evidence¶

Michael Vaden-Kiernan 2015¶

Michael Vaden-Kiernan, Geoffrey Borman, Sarah Caverly, Nance Bell, Veronica Ruiz de Castilla, and Kate Sullivan. (2015). Preliminary Findings from a Multi-Year Scale-Up Effectiveness Trial of Everyday Mathematics. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562455

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Preliminary year-one ITT analysis of the 48-school cluster-randomized trial; Table 2 prints school-level variance 1.838 (SD 0.883) and district-level variance 1.291 (SD 1.096). The article reports significant treatment-effect heterogeneity across schools and districts.

"The results however do indicate that variation in the treatment effect by school was significant (i.e., greater than two standard deviations) and district variation was greater than one standard deviation."
