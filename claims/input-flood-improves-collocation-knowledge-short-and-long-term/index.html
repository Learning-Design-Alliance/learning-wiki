Evidence¶

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan 2012¶

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan. (2012). The Role of Cognitive Style in the Collocational Knowledge Development of Iranian EFL Learners through Input Flood Treatment. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p105

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with two intact classes of Iranian upper-intermediate EFL learners, one randomly assigned to input flood treatment and one to control. Independent-samples t-tests showed the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group, with the article reporting "p=.000<.05" on both post-tests.

"Furthermore, as Table 2 reveals, the participants in the experimental group significantly outperformed the ones in the control group (p=.000<.05) in both immediate and delayed post-tests."

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan 2012 (2)¶

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan. (2012). The Role of Cognitive Style in the Collocational Knowledge Development of Iranian EFL Learners through Input Flood Treatment. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p105

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Within-group paired comparison in the same quasi-experiment: the experimental group's immediate and delayed post-test means exceeded its pre-test mean, reported as significant at "p=.000 <.05". No effect size is printed.

"The results of the within experiment al group comparison reveal ed that the learners’ both immediate post-test (M= 25.45, SD=4.15) and delayed post-test (M=22.34, SD= 3.35) scores were significantly higher than their pre-test (M= 19.53, SD= 1.82) scores (p=.000 <.05)."
