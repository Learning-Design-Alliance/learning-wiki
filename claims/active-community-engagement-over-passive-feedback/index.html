Qualitative interview study with CAC steering committee members (January–April 2025) on cross-sector coalition building. All members "emphasized the importance of centering and engaging the local community to drive systems-level change", citing intentional steps such as going into communities and listening before contributing.

"They emphasized the importance of commitment and intention when engaging community members and stakeholders, including actively and directly engaging with them rather than passively asking for feedback"
