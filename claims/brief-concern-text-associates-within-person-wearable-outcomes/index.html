q2 · i? · associational · r2

Within-person mixed-effects analysis of 458 university students (3,610 person-waves) tracked with Oura rings across 33 weeks, with bimonthly concern text processed through three NLP pipelines. The study reports that "Weeks dominated by academic concern framing were associated with lower physical activity" and emotional exhaustion language with poorer sleep and lower HRV. No effect sizes are printed for these associations.

"Weeks dominated by academic concern framing were associated with lower physical activity; weeks characterized by emotional exhaustion language were associated with poorer sleep quality and lower heart rate variability."
