# Immersive VR conditions outperform 2D video on knowledge retention, and Game-VR yields the highest overall preparedness

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants scored significantly higher on knowledge problems after Game-VR (p < 0.01) and Passive-VR (p < 0.05) than after 2D video, and Game-VR produced significantly higher overall preparedness than 2D video (p < 0.05). [→ Yue Yang 2025](#yue-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Yue Yang 2025

Yue Yang, Mengyao Guo, Yuxuan Wu, Wally Niu, Emmanuel Corona, Bruce Daniel, Christoph Leuze, Fred Baik. (2025). VR MRI Training for Adolescents: A Comparative Study of Gamified VR, Passive VR, 360◦ Video, and Traditional Educational Video. [doi:10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects study (N=11) with a Preparedness Questionnaire after each session covering knowledge problems, reported preparedness, and sensory preparedness. The article reports "significantly higher overall preparedness than the 2D video (p < 0.05)" for Game-VR.

> "When averaging across KP, RP, and SP scores, the Game-VR condition resulted in significantly higher overall preparedness than the 2D video (p < 0.05), revealing the value of interactivity and game design in procedural education."
