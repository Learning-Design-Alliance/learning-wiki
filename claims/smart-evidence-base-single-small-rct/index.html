q2 · i? · causal · r?

The WWC's rating rationale (Appendices A5.1–A5.3) states the positive-effects criterion requires two or more studies; with only one RCT and extent of evidence rated small for alphabetics, fluency, and comprehension, the highest applicable rating was potentially positive effects.

"It did not meet the criteria for positive effects because only one study met WWC evidence standards."
