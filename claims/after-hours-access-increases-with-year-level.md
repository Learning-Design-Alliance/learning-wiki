## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All year levels accessed Physeek more during business hours, but the proportion of access outside business hours increased with year level (68% business hours in year 1 down to 50% in year 4). [→ Maloney S 2013](#maloney-s-2013)
`q2 i?` The correlation between increasing year level and proportion of use outside business hours was significant (Pearson r = 0.98, P=.02). [→ Maloney S 2013 (2)](#maloney-s-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Maloney S 2013

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Audit of 2010 access times by year level (years 1-4), analyzed as percentages of total accesses with a Chi-square test. Business-hours access fell from "68% of the time" in year 1 to 50% in year 4; the between-year-level difference was significant (P=.01).

> "First-year students accessed Physeek during business hours 68% of the time (1315 separate accesses), second-year students 59% of the time (701 accesses), third-year students 55% of the time (1815 accesses), and fourth-year students 50% of the time (1639 accesses)."

### Maloney S 2013 (2)

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post hoc Pearson correlation between increasing year level (1-4) and proportion of Physeek use outside business hours "returned a significant Pearson correlation coefficient of 0.98 (P=.02)".

> "This returned a significant Pearson correlation coefficient of 0.98 (P=.02)."
