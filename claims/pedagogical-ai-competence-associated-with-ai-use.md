# Pedagogical AI competence is positively associated with teachers' AI use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a cross-sectional SEM of 224 teachers, pedagogical AI competence was significantly associated with AI use (β = 0.45, p < .001). [→ Feng 2026](#feng-2026)
`q2 i?` The SEM model showed excellent fit and explained 62% of the variance in AI use and 46% of the variance in pedagogical AI competence. [→ Feng 2026 (2)](#feng-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Feng 2026

Feng, S., Koch, M. J., & Carolus, A. (2026). AI Mindset – An empirically tested theoretical framework on the psychological factors shaping AI competence and AI use. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100402

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional mediation SEM (robust maximum likelihood, final n = 205) of survey data from Bavarian teachers. The results section reports that "AI use was significantly associated with pedagogical AI competence (β = 0.45, p < .001)", with competence modelled as a latent variable indicated by Intelligent-TPACK and TPACK ethics scores.

> "AI use was significantly associated with pedagogical AI competence (β = 0.45, p < .001)."

### Feng 2026 (2)

Feng, S., Koch, M. J., & Carolus, A. (2026). AI Mindset – An empirically tested theoretical framework on the psychological factors shaping AI competence and AI use. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100402

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Fit and variance-explained results of the same mediation SEM. The results section reports the model showed excellent fit (CFI = 1.00, RMSEA = 0.00, SRMR = 0.02) and that "The model explained 62% of the variance in AI use and 46% of the variance in pedagogical AI competence."

> "The model explained 62% of the variance in AI use and 46% of the variance in pedagogical AI competence."
