## Evidence

### Bennion Layne D. 2018

Bennion Layne D., Durning Steven J., LaRochelle Jeffrey, Yoon Michelle, Schreiber-Gregory Deanna, Reamy Brian V., Torre Dario. (2018). Untying the Gordian knot: remediation problems in medical schools that need remediation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1219-x

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The paper reports, citing Papadakis and associates, that early academic failure carried "only a 7% risk of subsequent disciplinary action by state board" versus 26% for documented unprofessional behavior. It also cites a study finding no significant relationship between a written knowledge-exam score and future patient complaints.

> "a student with an academic failure of a medical course during the 1st or 2nd year of medical school, had only a 7% risk of subsequent disciplinary action by state board. In contrast, those with documentation of unprofessional behavior during medical school, had more than three times that risk (26%) of subsequent disciplinary action by state board."
