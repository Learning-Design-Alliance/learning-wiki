## Evidence

### Thoonen Bart P. A. 2024

Thoonen Bart P. A., Scherpbier-de Haan Nynke D., Fluit Cornelia R. M. G., Stalmeijer Renée E. (2024). How Do Trainees Use EPAs to Regulate Their Learning in the Clinical Environment? A Grounded Theory Study. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1403

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of GP-trainees; the authors report that obtaining statements of entrustment "was often perceived as mandatory and as the most bureaucratic aspect of EPAs". No effect size is printed; this is a constructed qualitative finding.

> "The ‘checking the boxes’ stage typically began towards the end of each placement. In this stage, hindering influences related to learning goals, (self-)study and motivation occurred."
