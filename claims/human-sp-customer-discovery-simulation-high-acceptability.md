## Evidence

### Bucher A 2026

Bucher A, Peng T, Mosca L, Hafer N, Chitre M, Fischer M, LeMoine J. (2026). Feasibility of AI and Human Standardized Patients to Enhance Customer Discovery Communication Skills in Medical Students: Preliminary Evaluation of an Observational Cohort Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/95225

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary post-session survey of the pooled 2023 and 2024 human SP simulations, completed by 27 of 30 participating first-year medical students; 85% (23/27) rated effectiveness good or excellent, "with a median score of 4 (IQR 1) on a 4-point Likert scale". Other items were also rated highly (e.g., 96% agreed the simulation enhanced knowledge).

> "Most students (23/27, 85%) said that the effectiveness of the human SP session in meeting the stated learning objectives was good or excellent, with a median score of 4 (IQR 1) on a 4-point Likert scale."
