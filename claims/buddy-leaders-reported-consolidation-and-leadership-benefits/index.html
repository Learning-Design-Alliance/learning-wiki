Evidence¶

Thalluri 2014¶

Thalluri, Jyothi; O'Flaherty, Jackie A.; and Shepherd, Paul L. (2014). Classmate peer-coaching: "A Study Buddy Support scheme". Journal of Peer Learning, 7, 92-104. http://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol7/iss1/8

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Open-ended evaluation comments from Buddy Leaders, who also reported personal benefits such as a sense of responsibility, belonging, meeting new students, and increased empathy for teachers. One negative comment noted feeling rushed to cover topics.

"Having to explain concepts to others meant that I had to explore the topics more than if I were just learning it by myself, so it really consolidated my own learning."
