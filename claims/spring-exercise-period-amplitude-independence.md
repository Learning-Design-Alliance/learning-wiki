# The spring exercise lets students demonstrate interactively that the half-cycle time of harmonic motion does not depend on amplitude, even under Euler-Cromer integration

## Evidence

### Orban 2017

Orban, C., Porter, C. D, Smith, J. R. H., and Brecht, N. K., Britt, C. A., Teeling-Smith, R. M., & Harper, K. A. (2017). A Game-Centered, Interactive Approach for Using Programming Exercises in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5058449](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5058449)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exercise 6 attaches the sticky blob to a spring; after a perfectly inelastic collision the ship-blob system oscillates and a graphing system plots position and velocity versus time (Fig. 5). The authors state that even with Euler-Cromer integration one can show with good accuracy that period and amplitude are independent.

> "The interactive activity is for the students to show that the “half-cycle time” does not depend on the amplitude of the motion."
