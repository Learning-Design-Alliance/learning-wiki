### Kong Mengjie 2025

Kong Mengjie, Fok Elise Hoi Wan, Yiu Cynthia Kar Yung. (2025). A Scoping Review of Large Language Models in Dental Education: Applications, Challenges, and Prospects. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103854

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Danesh et al, that in a study evaluating ChatGPT-3.5 and ChatGPT-4 on 311 MCQs from an AAP in-service examination, "General accuracy for GPT-4 was significantly higher than GPT-3.5."

> "General accuracy for GPT-4 was significantly higher than GPT-3.5. LLMs showed best performance on pharmacology and therapeutics."
