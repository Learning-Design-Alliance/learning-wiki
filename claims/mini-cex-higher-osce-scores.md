## Evidence

### Durrani Shazia Fakhir 2025

Durrani Shazia Fakhir, Hameed Ammara, Rani Pallavi, Ahmed Shakeel, Baqai Shehla, Ali Rida. (2025). Effect of Mini-CEX as formative learning tool for clinical skills in undergraduate medical students in a private medical university in Karachi, Pakistan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07962-6

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same quasi-experimental study of 143 final-year MBBS students, end-of-year OSCE results were compared between the 76 Mini-CEX intervention students and 67 controls; the intervention groups "outperformed controls in OSCE results" with p = 0.001 and Cohen's d = 0.71.

> "Similarly, the intervention groups outperformed controls in OSCE results, scoring a mean of 75.6 ± 6.9 versus 68.1 ± 7.8 (p = 0.001; Cohen's d = 0.71), as shown in Table 3."
