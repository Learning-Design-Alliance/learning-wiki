### Shafiei Somayeh B. 2018

Shafiei Somayeh B., Hussein Ahmed Aly, Guru Khurshid A. (2018). Dynamic changes of brain functional states during surgical skill acquisition. PLoS ONE 13(10): e0204836. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0204836

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of module allegiance matrices (MAM) from all 524 recordings across six sessions and four frequency bands, shown in Fig 1. The paper reports task-relevant areas are "consistently were assigned to the same community (recruited)" while other areas might only be transiently recruited.

> "These architectures display that channels in motor area (Frontal and Central channels [27]) consistently were assigned to the same community (recruited). The same thing happened to channels in cognitive (Prefrontal and Parietal channels [27]) and visual areas (Occipital channels [27])."
