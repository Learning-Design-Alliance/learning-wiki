### Gill Monique 2024

Gill Monique, Hunt Anne, Duncan Andrea. (2024). Innovation and Competency Development in Occupational Therapy Fieldwork During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Canadian Journal of Occupational Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1177/00084174231190768

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-case analysis across the three identified innovation cases from 28 interviews. The authors report that "regardless of the type of innovation" these three competency domains were developed, while within-domain competencies differed by case.

> "A major finding within this study included that regardless of the type of innovation, occupational therapy learners still developed competencies within the domains of occupational therapy expertise, excellence in practice, and communication and collaboration."
