## Evidence

### Kilgore Jenna 2023

Kilgore Jenna, Collins Amanda C., Miller Julie Anne M., Winer E. Samuel. (2023). Grit, depression, social desirability, and GPA. PLoS One 18(7): e0288270. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288270

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis of the moderated-moderation model with class year and age as covariates, requested by a reviewer, in the same 520-participant sample. The article reports both covariate effects were significant and that "including these variables as covariates did not change the original findings".

> "The effect of class year, b = .28, 95% CI [.191, .376], t = 6.03, p < .001, and age, b = -.10, 95% CI [-.139, -.055], t = -4.50, p < .001, were both significant. However, including these variables as covariates did not change the original findings."
