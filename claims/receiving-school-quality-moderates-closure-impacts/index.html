Narrative review of prior closure research: several studies document negative consequences for displaced students, while others (Carlson & Lavertu, 2016; Bross et al., 2016) report improvements when students gain access to higher-performing schools.

"Multiple studies emphasized that academic outcomes hinge on whether displaced students enroll in better educational environments (Engberg et al., 2012; Han et al., 2017)."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The review attributes this national finding to Han et al. (2017), noting it limits closures' potential as an improvement strategy; positive cases like New York City high-school closures (Kemple, 2015) are described as "more exception than rule."

"National analyses reveal that fewer than half of displaced students actually transfer to higher-performing schools, limiting the potential for closures to serve as an effective improvement strategy (Han et al., 2017)."
