## Evidence

### Chenghui Chen 2025

Chenghui Chen, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2025). Development and validation of a scale for metacognitive regulation of interactive metadiscourse strategies in L2 argumentative writing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09435-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis with Sample One (1417 cases) using principal axis factoring and pro-max rotation on the 22-item scale. "three factors were retained, explaining 63.652% of the total variance", labeled PIM, MIM, and EIM; Cronbach's Alpha for the whole scale was 0.934.

> "At this time, the eigenvalues of three factors are greater than 1, meeting Kaiser's criterion. Table 1 reveals the oblique rotated solution and the results of the principal axis factoring of the 22-item, three-factor scale."

### Chenghui Chen 2025 (2)

Chenghui Chen, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2025). Development and validation of a scale for metacognitive regulation of interactive metadiscourse strategies in L2 argumentative writing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09435-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Confirmatory factor analysis with Sample Two (1078 cases) via AMOS maximum likelihood estimation. The modified three-factor correlated model (Model 2) reached good fit; "the more parsimonious model (Model 4), the one-factor second-order model" was retained as the final version, with all loadings significant at p < .001.

> "some important goodness-of-fit indices in Model 2 reach the cutoff value of good fit (e.g., CFI = 0.955 > 0.95; SRMR = 0.0415 < 0.050; RMSEA = 0.058 < 0.060)"
