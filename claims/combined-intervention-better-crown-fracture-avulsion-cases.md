`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Case-by-case GLMM analyses with binary responses and Tukey HSD multiple comparisons on answers from 94 dentists in the post-intervention questionnaire. IC2 outperformed CC on two named cases with the printed p-values of 0.02; no effect size was reported.

> "Statistically significant differences were found in the following clinical cases: complicated crown fracture in immature tooth (p = 0.02) and the best advice on the phone for an avulsed tooth (p = 0.02) with dentists in the IC2 group answering better compared with the CC group"
