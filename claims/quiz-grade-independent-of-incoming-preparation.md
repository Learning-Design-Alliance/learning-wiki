## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' quiz grades were not significantly correlated with FMCE pre-scores (r = 0.095) or physics diagnostic pre-scores (r = 0.137). [→ Burkholder 2021](#burkholder-2021)
`q2 i?` Quiz grades showed a small, statistically significant correlation with SAT/ACT math scores, which the authors attribute to the importance of trigonometry and algebra skills. [→ Burkholder 2021 (2)](#burkholder-2021-2)
