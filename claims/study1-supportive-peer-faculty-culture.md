Lisabeth Marie Santana and Chandralekha Singh. (2024). How the physics culture shapes the experiences of undergraduate women physics majors: A comparative case study of three physics departments. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.12995](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.12995)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study (Johnson 2020) with 6 undergraduate women physics majors, 4 physics faculty, and a third-year student focus group at a small liberal arts college where 25% of physics majors are women. A second-year student describes peer camaraderie: "We're kind of all in it together". Students described faculty of both genders as accessible, nice, and helpful, and said they did not feel more intimidated by male than female professors.

> "We're kind of all in it together, why wouldn't they help me? They know what I'm going through – they've done it themselves!"
