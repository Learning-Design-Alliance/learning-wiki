# PBL+LBL raises satisfactory teaching effect but not general or poor teaching effect ratings

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Satisfactory teaching effect rates were higher in the PBL+LBL group (RR, 1.32; 95% CI, 1.10–1.59; P = .003). [→ Jia Xue MM 2018](#jia-xue-mm-2018)
`q3 i0` There was not a statistically significant difference in general teaching effect or poor teaching effect between the two groups. [→ Jia Xue MM 2018 (2)](#jia-xue-mm-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Jia Xue MM 2018

Jia Xue MM, Zeng Wen MM, Zhang Qin MM, Tarantino. Giovanni. (2018). Combined administration of problem- and lecture-based learning teaching models in medical education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000011366

`q3 · i?`

Meta-analysis of 9 RCTs reporting satisfactory teaching effect (371 and 370 students in the PBL+LBL and LBL groups, respectively), random effects model due to higher heterogeneity (P = .0001, I2 = 75%).

> "The meta-analysis of satisfactory teaching effect in the PBL+LBL teaching model found higher rates compared with the LBL teaching model (RR, 1.32; 95% CI, 1.10–1.59; P = .003)."

### Jia Xue MM 2018 (2)

Jia Xue MM, Zeng Wen MM, Zhang Qin MM, Tarantino. Giovanni. (2018). Combined administration of problem- and lecture-based learning teaching models in medical education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000011366

`q3 · i0`

Meta-analysis of 6 RCTs reporting general and poor teaching effect (251 and 251 students per group), fixed effects model because of lower heterogeneity (I2 = 38% and 12%). No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was not a statistically significant difference in general teaching effect and poor teaching effect between the 2 groups (RR, 0.77; 95% CI, 0.55–1.08; P = .14; RR, 0.52; 95% CI, 0.23–1.51; P = .11; respectively)."
