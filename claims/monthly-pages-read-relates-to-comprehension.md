## Evidence

### Başar 2017

Başar, M. & Gürbüz, M. (2017). Effect of the SQ4R Technique on the Reading Comprehension of Elementary School 4th Grade Elementary School Students. International Journal of Instruction, 10(2), 131-144. http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_2_9.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal Wallis test across five monthly pages-read categories (50 and less to 201 and more) on post-test scores of all 57 students. The article reports "[X 2(4)=14.711; p<.05]" and that the 201-and-more group had the highest average position score (33.06). No effect size is printed.

> "a significant difference was found between the monthly amount of pages read by students and their post -test scores [X 2(4)=14.711; p<.05]"
