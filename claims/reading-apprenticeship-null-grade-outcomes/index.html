Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2010¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Reading Apprenticeship®. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://www.wested.org/ReadingApprenticeship

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Null findings from the randomized controlled trial's first cohort (686 intervention and 454 control students). Appendix A3 prints effect sizes of 0.09 (comprehension) and 0.05 (vocabulary) for Cohort 1, both not statistically significant, and –0.04 for Cohort 2 vocabulary, also not significant. Equivalence was not tested.

"Kemple et al. (2008) did not find statistically significant effects of Reading Apprenticeship ® on the reading comprehension and vocabulary subtests of the Group Reading Assessment and Diagnostic Evaluation (GRADE) for Cohort 1 students."
