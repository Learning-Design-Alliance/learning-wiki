## Evidence

### Eifler Eike Friederike 2019

Eifler Eike Friederike, Starr Alexandra, Riemann Rainer. (2019). The genetic and environmental effects on school grades in late childhood and adolescence. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225946. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225946

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cohort-differentiated ACtE model comparison on TwinLife school-report grades. The article also reports for GPA "a2C17 = .62 and a2C11 = .47, respectively", with shared environmental effects smaller in C17 (.16 vs .40).

> "The heritability of mathematics was found to be higher in C17 (a2C17 = .58) than in C11 (a2C11 = .34), confirming the anticipated cohort effect for this grade variable."

### Eifler Eike Friederike 2019 (2)

Eifler Eike Friederike, Starr Alexandra, Riemann Rainer. (2019). The genetic and environmental effects on school grades in late childhood and adolescence. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225946. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225946

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same cohort-differentiated SEM for German grades. The article reports non-shared environment larger in C17 (e2C17 = .43 vs e2C11 = .24) and attributes this to a curriculum shift toward ambiguous grading tasks such as creative writing.

> "The cohort effects found for mathematics could, however, not be confirmed by the grade in German. Here, the variance attributed to genetics was smaller for the students in C17 (a2C17 = .34) compared to those in C11 (a2C11 = .48)."
