### Valentina De Renzi 2025

Valentina De Renzi, Matteo G. A. Paris, Maria Bondani. (2025). From Staging to Insight: An Educational Path to Understanding Bell's Inequalities. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00415-5](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00415-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-test of the three-day intensive program at the FIM Department in Modena with 26 12th-grade students; Q1 asked pupils to select statements correctly describing Bell's inequalities. The article reports "the two correct sentences are chosen by the majority of pupils" with the printed percentages; no effect size is given.

> "As far as the (Q1) question is concerned, the two correct sentences are chosen by the majority of pupils (10 (50%) and 20 (83%), respectively), while each of the four wrong answers are chosen by less 4 students."

### Valentina De Renzi 2025 (2)

Valentina De Renzi, Matteo G. A. Paris, Maria Bondani. (2025). From Staging to Insight: An Educational Path to Understanding Bell's Inequalities. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00415-5](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00415-5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same 26-pupil post-test, Q2 on the fundamental phases of CHSH experiments. The article reports descriptive percentages only: "almost 80% of the pupils chose the correct sentences" and "more than 60% of the pupils selected all the correct answers or made only one mistake"; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

> "almost 80% of the pupils chose the correct sentences regarding Charlie, Alice and Bob roles (sentences A, D, F), the random choice of Alice and Bob measurements (H and P), and that the observables measured by A and B are not commutative. Moreover, more than 60% of the pupils selected all the correct answers or made only one mistake"
