Evidence¶

Houssa and Nader-Grosbois 2016¶

Marine Houssa, Nathalie Nader-Grosbois. (2016). Experimental Study of Middle-term Training in Social Cognition in Preschoolers. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i1.972

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized experiment with 48 preschoolers (24 per group) in 15 sessions. The interaction on RES total was significant, "F(1,23) = 8.54, p = .005, η²p = .16"; Question 3 showed F(1,23) = 9.10, p = .004, η²p = .17.

"A repeated measure ANOVA showed significant time by group interactions on the SIP measure in favor of the experimental group. This interaction is significant for the RES total ( F(1,23) = 8.54, p = .005, η²p = .16)"

Houssa and Nader-Grosbois 2016 (2)¶

Marine Houssa, Nathalie Nader-Grosbois. (2016). Experimental Study of Middle-term Training in Social Cognition in Preschoolers. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i1.972

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Same repeated measures ANOVA on justification levels: wrong/no justification interaction "F(1,23) = 16.59, p = .00, η²p = .27" and intersubjective level "F(1,23) = 4.01, p = .051, η²p = .08".

"for two levels of justification ( wrong/no justification: F(1,23) = 16.59, p = .00, η²p = .27 and inter-subjective level: F(1,23) = 4.01, p = .051, η²p = .08)"
