Haimovitz's studies found little if any correlation between parents' and children's mindsets; instead "parents' beliefs about failure and their reactions to children's failures" consistently predicted children's mindsets. No effect sizes are printed in the quoted text.

> "Her research found that it was parents' beliefs about failure and their reactions to children's failures that consistently predicted their children's mindsets (Haimovitz & Dweck, 2016; for a discussion of how overt cues such as adults' praise or reactions to failure might be more visible to children than the adults' actual mindsets, see Haimovitz & Dweck, 2017)."
