# Skill portfolio diversity is positively associated with skill acquisition, occupational mobility, and transitions into lower-AI-exposure occupations

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Workers with more diverse skill portfolios are more likely to acquire new skills, make lateral transitions, and move into occupations with lower AI exposure. [→ Carpanelli 2026](#carpanelli-2026)

## Evidence

### Carpanelli 2026

Carpanelli, M., Duszynski, J. & Stephany, F. (2026). Navigating the skill diversity frontier: How skill complexity explains worker resilience. Working paper, Oxford Internet Institute. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02102 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.02102](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.02102)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fully controlled regression models on the LinkedIn individual-year panel, with diversity measured as Hill diversity across Louvain skill domains and all models controlling for total portfolio size. The study reports diversity is "positively associated with skill acquisition, occupational mobility, and transitions into occupations with lower AI exposure". No effect sizes are printed.

> "Diversity, by contrast, is positively associated with skill acquisition, occupational mobility, and transitions into occupations with lower AI exposure, consistent with its interpretation as adaptive capital."
