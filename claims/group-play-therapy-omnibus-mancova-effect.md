### Bahareh Mousavi & Sahar Safarzadeh 2016

Bahareh Mousavi & Sahar Safarzadeh. (2016). Effectiveness of the Group Play Therapy on the Insecure Attachment and Social Skills of Orphans in Ahvaz City. International Education Studies, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n9p42

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus multivariate covariance analysis on posttest and follow-up scores controlling pretests; Wilks Lambda F = 24.17, p = 0.001, printed Eta 0.944 for the group effect. The "significant difference between the control and treatment groups" was on the combined set of outcomes.
