# Within persons, state performance-approach goals predict pride and state performance-avoidance goals predict anxiety and shame

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Intraindividual multilevel regression showed state performance-approach goals positively predicted state pride, and state performance-avoidance goals positively predicted state anxiety and shame. [→ Goetz 2016](#goetz-2016)

## Evidence

### Goetz 2016

Goetz, T., Sticca, F., Pekrun, R., Murayama, K., & Elliot, A. J. (2016). Intraindividual relations between achievement goals and discrete achievement emotions: An experience sampling approach. Learning and Instruction, 41, 115-125. https://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.10.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same ESM multilevel analysis (1409 assessments, 120 students) from the intraindividual results section, with covariates controlled. The article reports the printed unstandardized coefficients and p-values; no standardized effect size is given.

> "Performance-approach goals were positive predictors of pride (b ¼ 0.18,p < .001), and performance-avoidance goals were positive predictors of anxiety ( b ¼ 0.10, p < .05) and shame ( b ¼ 0.07, p < .05)."
