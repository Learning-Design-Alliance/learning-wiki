## Evidence

### Ebru Aydın 2022

Ebru Aydın, Çağlayan Dinçer. (2022). “I did it wrong, but i know it”: Young children’s metacognitive knowledge expressions during peer interactions in math activities. Thinking Skills and Creativity 45 (2022) 101104. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2022.101104

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the “Measuring rods” episode (Table 15): both children indicated what needed attention in the task and behaviorally expressed the strategies that should be implemented based on the task criteria. The authors attribute this to limited vocabulary.

> "As seen in this example, the limited vocabulary of young children causes them to resort to behavioral expressions to express their metacognitive knowledge."
