Scoping review searching PubMed, Scopus and Google Scholar (2000–2025) with the Levac and Colquhoun framework; 2515 records identified and 22 studies included. The review reports "11 focused on PBL, 2 on CBL, 3 on both CBL and PBL and 6 on TBL".

"Overall, 22 studies were included. Of these, 11 focused on PBL, 2 on CBL, 3 on both CBL and PBL and 6 on TBL."

Related Claims¶

  • Student satisfaction with CBL, PBL and TBL in public health education was high, reported in 21 of 22 studies (95.4%) — related
  • Student preference for CBL, PBL or TBL over traditional teaching was reported in 15 of 22 studies (68.1%), but only two studies used a controlled comparison with lecture — related
  • TBL alone and TBL combined with other methods both outperform LBL, with no significant difference between the two TBL formats — related
  • No included studies applied AI-based modalities specifically to public health education, though external reviews report positive satisfaction with AI-assisted PBL and CBL — related
  • One included study reported a 13.2% per-student cost reduction with CBL versus TL, but cost-efficiency was not consistently reported — related
