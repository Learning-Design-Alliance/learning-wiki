González-Fernández Francisco Tomás; Delgado-García Gabriel; Coll Jesús Siquier; Silva Ana Filipa; Nobari Hadi; Clemente Filipe Manuel. (2023). Relationship between cognitive functioning and physical fitness in regard to age and sex. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-023-04028-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-factor ANOVA (sex × age category) on D2 attention scores in 187 students aged 9–15; the study reports "no statistically significant interaction between sex and age in any case (p > 0.05)". Equivalence was not tested.

> "The two-factor ANOVAs conducted with the different DA scores (TR score, TA score, TR + score, TR- score, O score, C Score and DA Score) showed no statistically significant interaction between sex and age in any case (p > 0.05)."
