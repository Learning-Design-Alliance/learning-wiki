Evidence¶

Chou 2013¶

Chou, C. (2013). A study on the effectiveness of applying "Readers' Theater" as English remedial instruction for underachievers. Taiwan Journal of TESOL, 10(1), 77-103. http://english.moe.gov.tw/ct.asp?xItem=7773&ctNode=516&mp=1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on the validated English Learning Attitudes questionnaire (self-efficacy scale Cronbach's alpha .89) administered to the 25 experimental and 24 control underachievers. The article reports the self-efficacy post-test "t -values ... 4.24" at the .001 significance level.

"the post -test t -values of the experimental and control groups’ English self -efficacy and English learning motivation are 4.24 and 2.96, which reached significance levels of .001 and .01, respectively."
