## Evidence

### Berat Ahi 2017

Berat Ahi. (2017). The Effect of Talking Drawings on Five-Year-Old Turkish Children’s Mental Models of the Water Cycle. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 12(3), 349-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141375

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest comparison in the matched semi-experimental design (n = 22 per group). The article reports no significant difference between groups at pretest, supporting that the groups started at similar mental model levels.

> "The Mann -Whitney U Test results revealed that there is no significant difference between the control group ( Md = 1, n = 22) and the experimental group (Md = 1, n = 22) in terms of the level of the drawings. For both groups, U = 231, z = -0.531, p = 0.554, and the effect size was found to be r = 0.08."
