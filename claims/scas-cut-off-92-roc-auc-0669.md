## Evidence

### KOÇAK Cemal 2025

KOÇAK Cemal, GÜVERCİN Hande, ESEN Ecem, ÇÖL Meltem. (2025). Development study of scientific competence and activity scale in medical faculty research assistants. Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences. https://doi.org/10.55730/1300-0144.6026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ROC curve analysis of scale scores for 299 research assistants against the criterion of participation in at least one publication or project. The article reports the AUC as 0.669 (95% CI: 0.608–0.730) and, at the cut-off of 92, sensitivity 63.2%, specificity 65.3%, PPV 66.2%, NPV 62.3%.

> "The area under the curve (AUC) was 0.669 (95% CI: 0.608–0.730), with a standard error of 0.031."
