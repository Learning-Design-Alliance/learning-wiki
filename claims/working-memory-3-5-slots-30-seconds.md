## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Working memory storage of novel data is temporary (about 30 seconds or less per slot) and limited to roughly 3-5 slots for symbol-described data in adults. [→ Hartman 2021](#hartman-2021)

## Evidence

### Hartman 2021

Hartman, J. R.; Nelson, E. A. (2021). A Paradigm Shift: The Implications of Working Memory Limits for Physics and Chemistry Instruction. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.00454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.00454)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports, citing cognitive research summaries, the two quantified limits on novel data in working memory: "about 30 seconds or less" per slot and "only 3‐5 slots" for symbol-described chunks in adults. No effect size is printed.

> "Each slot can retain a chunk of novel data for about 30 seconds or less during other processing.41,42.  Second, at each problem step, if data are described using symbols (such as letters, words, or numbers), working memory in adults usually has only 3‐5 slots that can hold a novel data chunk."
