## Evidence

### Melissa Roderick 2013

Melissa Roderick, Vanessa Coca, Eliza Moeller, and Thomas Kelley-Kemple. (2013). From High School to the Future: The Challenge of Senior Year in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-challenge-senior-year-chicago-public-schools

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interviews with seniors in three CPS high schools (from a longitudinal study begun in 2005) found students characterized senior year as easier than previous years, with classes where little work was required and students feeling they might as well have skipped the grade.

> "A surprising finding is that regardless of achievement level and regardless of whether students took advanced courses, the overwhelming majority of the seniors in our qualitative study describe senior year as unchallenging."
