## Evidence

### Dai et al. 2018

Guiyu Dai, Yang Liu & Shanmeng Cui. (2018). A Study on the Mobile Learning of English and American Literature Based on Wechat Public Account. English Language Teaching, 11(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n6p47

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A control experiment with 97 junior undergraduates randomly assigned to experiment (n=49) and control (n=48) groups. The experiment group used WeChat public account resources to supplement classroom teaching. The mid-term independent samples t-test showed a 2-tailed significance of 0.002, below 0.05, so "the experiment group gets higher score than the control group".

> "Experiment group’s mean is 83.27, higher than the control group’s mean (73.75), which is shown in Table 3."
