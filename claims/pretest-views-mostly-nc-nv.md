### Ayşe Sert Çıbık 2016

Ayşe Sert Çıbık. (2016). The Effect of Project-Based History and Nature of Science Practices on the Change of Nature of Scientific Knowledge. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(4), 453-472. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094587

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive frequency-percentage analysis of pre-test open-ended SUSSI responses in both groups; distributions were similar, dominated by NC and NV categories, with no Informed Views. No effect size applies.

> "most of the participants’ opinions towards the items in the questionnaire are categorized under NC and NV and they have a similar distribution in these cate gories. On the other side, it is also remarkable that they have quite a few views regarding TV categorization and no ideas in IV categorization."
