Within the 32-participant experimental group, change scores (post-test minus pre-test) were compared across demographic groups using independent-samples t-tests. The article reports "no significant difference in learning outcomes between males (M = 12.400) and females (M = 13.117)"; urban–rural and working–non-working contrasts were also non-significant (p > .05).
