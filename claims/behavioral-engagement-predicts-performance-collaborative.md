## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Behavioral engagement showed a weak positive path to performance (β = .25, p < .01), while emotional and cognitive engagement paths were not significant. [→ Ito Takamichi 2022](#ito-takamichi-2022)

## Evidence

### Ito Takamichi 2022

Ito Takamichi, Umemoto Takatoyo. (2022). Examining the causal relationships between interpersonal motivation, engagement, and academic performance among university students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0274229

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

SEM path model (N = 363); Table 3 reports β = .25, p < .01 for behavioral engagement to performance, with emotional (β = -.01, p = .89) and cognitive (β = -.04, p = .62) engagement non-significant. Performance was graded A–C essays integrating partner dialogue.

> "Finally, for performance, the weak positive path from behavioral engagement was statistically significant."
