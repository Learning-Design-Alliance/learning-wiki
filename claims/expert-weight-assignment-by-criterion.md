## Evidence

### Yu Limin 2026

Yu Limin, Chen Zhe, Qin Zeyu. (2026). A novel Z-number based multi-stage assessment framework for problem-based learning in practical courses. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349114

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article describes a two-level expert weighting structure where group weights are set by course instructional design and individual members within groups receive equal weight. In a five-member team, each peer contributes 0.125 and the instructor 0.5 for criterion C1.

> "For example, consider Criterion C1, which is jointly evaluated by peer students and the course instructor, each contributing 50% to the final score."
