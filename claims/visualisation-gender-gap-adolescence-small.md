`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Single-paper meta-analysis of visualisation measured by the DAT:SR (US and Ireland) and RIF platform visualisation task groups (Austria) across adolescence. The analysis reveals a "small" difference favouring boys; the printed effect size value is not in the available text.

> "Single-paper meta-analyses, for both mental-rotation and visualisation tasks, reveal medium and small gender differences respectively favouring boys, with these gender differences already apparent from the beginning of adolescence."
