Pedagogical rating study: pedagogical experts chose 40 simulation requests across STEM topics and age groups; each successful interactive was rated by two raters teaching the respective field on rubrics for acceptance, learning objectives, and UX/LX. "overall acceptance rate is high at86%".

Topic-level contrast in the same 40-request expert rating study. The article attributes the weakness to biology topics requiring more memorization of details than manipulable equations, yielding simulations "more of textbook sequence and less a real interactive experience."

"It is noticeable that simulations for biology are inferior to other topics, particularly along the aesthetics and UX axes."
