## Subclaims
`q3 i?` IQ showed a stepwise association with school difficulties: children with IQ below −3 SD had odds of 42.1 (95% CI 9.2–192.5) relative to the −1 SD to +1 SD group, with decreasing odds at higher IQ categories. [→ Wiingreen R 2025](#wiingreen-r-2025)

## Evidence

### Wiingreen R 2025

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate logistic regression of school difficulties (92 of 248 classified) by preschool IQ category. The lowest IQ group had an odds ratio of 42.1 (95% CI 9.2–192.5), with lower odds at higher IQ categories, consistent with a stepwise gradient. Only odds ratios and confidence intervals were printed, with no effect size.

> "<–3 SD (n = 22; 9%) versus –1 SD to +1 SD 42.1** (9.2–192.5)"

### Wiingreen R 2025 (2)

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The multivariate logistic regression, adjusted for intelligence quotient, motor performance, sex, multiple births and maternal educational level, retained the stepwise pattern; the −3 SD to <−2 SD group had an odds ratio of 13.8 (95% CI 4.0–47.6) versus the reference group. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "–3 SD to <–2 SD (n = 24; 10%) versus –1 SD to +1 SD 16.0** (5.4–47.1) 13.8** (4.0 – 47.6)"
