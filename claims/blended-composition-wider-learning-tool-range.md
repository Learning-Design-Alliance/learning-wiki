## Evidence

### Stouck 2019

Stouck, J. (2019). Complementary Learning: Piloting a Blended Format for Canadian Composition Courses. The Canadian Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5206/cjsotl-rcacea.2019.1.7992

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire responses from 18 experimental-group volunteers across three blended sections, ranked against control-group rankings (Table 2). The article reports "a wider range of responses and less consensus on the least effective learning tool than in the control group".

> "Clearly, students felt class content and online writing exercises were most effective, and, by a small margin, class discussions least effective. However, there is a wider range of responses and less consensus on the least effective learning tool than in the control group"
