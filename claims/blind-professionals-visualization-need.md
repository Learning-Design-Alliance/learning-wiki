### JooYoung Seo 2024

JooYoung Seo, Sile O'Modhrain, Yilin Xia, Sanchita Kamath, Bongshin Lee, and James M. Coughlan. (2024). Designing Born-Accessible Courses in Data Science and Visualization: Challenges and Opportunities of a Remote Curriculum Taught by Blind Instructors to Blind Students. EUROVIS 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02568)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational conclusion (Section 3.3) drawn from recruiting nine blind professionals through the Blind Academics email forum, spanning banking, education administration, and research labs. Participants were required to have "an immediate need for data visualization skills in their academic or professional careers."

> "Overall, this course demonstrated the presence of an active community of blind professionals with a declared need to visualize their own data for analysis purposes, but also a need to confidently and independently generate visual representations of their data to communicate with their sighted peers."
