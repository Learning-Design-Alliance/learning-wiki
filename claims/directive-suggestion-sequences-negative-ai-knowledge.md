Analyzing Middle School Students' Dialogue and Behaviors during Collaborative AI Chatbot Development Using Ordered Network Analysis. arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.21603. Spearman correlations with group-level AI knowledge scores showed negative associations for directive- and suggestion-dominated sequences, including "directive–suggestion(ρ=−0.467;ρ=−0.418)" and suggestion self-loops (ρ=−0.333).
