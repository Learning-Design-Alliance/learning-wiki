q1 · i? · review · r2

The guide's School District as Independent Gatekeeper section explains that districts operate under an entirely autonomous layer of legal authority, control access to student records under FERPA, and typically require a data-use agreement negotiated separately from IRB review.

"A district superintendent, legal counsel, or internal research committee can completely reject a study even if a prestigious IRB has fully cleared it."
