### Murray Suzanne 2011

Murray Suzanne, Lazure Patrice, Schroter Sara, Leuschner Philipp J, Posel Peter, Kellner Thomas, Jenkins Richard D. (2011). International challenges without borders: a descriptive study of family physicians' educational needs in the field of diabetes. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-27

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Consensus classification of the 13 common challenges by the interdisciplinary research team, drawing on the literature review and the EIMSED-NA, BMJ-GAP and AXDEV-NA studies. The article states the issues "were not restricted to the physicians' knowledge, but also related to their skills, attitudes, behaviours and context."

> "These issues were found across the entire continuum of care and included: pathophysiology of diabetes, diagnostic criteria, treatment targets assessment, drugs' modes of action, decision-making in therapies, treatment guidelines, insulin therapy, adherence, management of complications, lifestyle changes, team integration, bureaucracy and third-party payers. The issues reported were not restricted to the physicians' knowledge, but also related to their skills, attitudes, behaviours and context."
