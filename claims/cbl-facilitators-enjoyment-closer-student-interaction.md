### Delany Caroline 2025

Delany Caroline, Illing Jan, McIlroy Marie, Daly Ronan, Bruen Catherine, Alamuddin Naji, Condron Clare, Crehan Martina, Doherty Sally, Meagher Frances, Offiah Gozie, O'Neill Sarah, Stuart Ellen, Torrens Christopher, Morgan Maria P. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

IPA study of ten purposively selected CBL facilitators interviewed via semi-structured online interviews after Year 1 curriculum implementation; the analysis found "enjoyment and fulfilment from their role as a facilitator" tied to closer student interaction, with the vast majority agreeing they would participate again.

> "Participants consistently reported enjoyment and fulfilment from their role as a facilitator. This was associated with the increased proximity and opportunity to interact authentically with students"
