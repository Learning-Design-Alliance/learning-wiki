## Evidence

### Sabeti Fahimeh 2026

Sabeti Fahimeh, Ranjbar Azadeh, Kashaki Mandana, Khanjari Sedigheh, Banazadeh Marjan. (2026). Comparing between face-to-face and mobile learning for improving nurses’ performance in premature infant positioning: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09855-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with 90 NICU nurses. At one month, total mean IPAT scores were 6.63 (face-to-face) vs 7.88 (m-learning), η² = 0.871. The quote confirms "the face-to-face training group was significantly lower than the m-learning group (p < 0.001)."
