Goldonowicz Jessica M., Runyon Michael S., Bullard Mark J. (2018). Palliative care in the emergency department: an educational investigation and intervention. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-018-0293-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-report results from the paired pre/post survey of 40 EM residents. The authors attribute this comfort pattern to prior training that "focused on facilitating difficult conversations such as death notification, but lacked more comprehensive palliative care skill development."

> "Overall, residents noted being most comfortable with delivering bad news and symptom management and least comfortable with disease prognostication."
