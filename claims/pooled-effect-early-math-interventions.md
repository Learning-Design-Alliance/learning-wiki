## Evidence

### Anna H. Miller 2025

Anna H. Miller, Daniel R. Espinas, Daniel McNeish, & Marcia A. Barnes. (2025). Dosage Response in Intensive Mathematics Interventions for Early Elementary Students With or At-Risk for Mathematics Learning Disability. Educational Psychology Review, 37, 91. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10070-y

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects intercept-only meta-regression (Model 1) across 164 effect sizes from 24 RCTs and QEDs meeting WWC standards. The pooled estimate was β = 0.61 (95% CI [0.29, 0.92]) and "the derived I 2 value was 76%", which the authors say justifies moderator analyses.

> "The overall mean effect of mathematics interventions for kindergarten to third grade students was β = 0.61 (95% CI [0.29, 0.92]). The null model showed significant heterogeneity (Q (163) = 682.85, p < .0001), and the derived I 2 value was 76%"
