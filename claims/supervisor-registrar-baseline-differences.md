## Evidence

### Mason Ron 2020

Mason Ron, Doherty Kathleen, Eccleston Claire, Winbolt Margaret, Long Marita, Robinson Andrew. (2020). Effect of a dementia education intervention on the confidence and attitudes of general practitioners in Australia: a pretest post-test study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033218

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline demographic comparisons in the Results of the pretest post-test study of 332 registrars and 114 supervisors. Supervisors averaged 49.8 years versus 33.03 for registrars; 20% versus 5.6% had prior dementia training. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Supervisors were significantly older than GPRs (t(414)=21.121; p<0.001), and more had undertaken prior dementia education (χ²=20.263; p<0.001), although this proportion was small for both groups."
