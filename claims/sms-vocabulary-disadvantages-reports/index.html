Evidence¶

Haisen Zhang 2011¶

Haisen Zhang, Wei Song, Jack Burston. (2011). REEXAMINING THE EFFECTIVENESS OF VOCABULARY LEARNING VIA MOBILE PHONES. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ944968

q1 · i? · causal · r1

From the qualitative analysis of the 25 written reports and the authors' discussion: Fetion's word limit segments long messages that may arrive out of sequence; phone memory limits force deletions; locating earlier messages is time-consuming; screen-only memorization involves "merely superficial cognitive processing" that fails to transfer words to long-term memory.

"Sometimes, a long message has been cut into multiple messages, which are sent separately but received not in the order of times. They look very messy."
