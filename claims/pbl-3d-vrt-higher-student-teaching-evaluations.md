## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2025

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08364-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of the RCT, using a self-designed anonymous questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.87, maximum score 20) covering knowledge acquisition, clinical reasoning, practical skills, self-directed learning, and teamwork. No effect size is printed.

> "The experimental group rated the teaching methods significantly higher than the control group, with a mean score of 17.84 ± 1.12 versus 10.72 ± 1.59 (mean difference = 7.12, 95% CI: 5.51–8.73, P < 0.05)."
