## Evidence

### Bird 1921

Bird, Grace E. (1921). The Devious Path of Slow Work. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ123456

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bird reports Thorndike's multi-year class experiment in adding among "six-hundred seventy-one students," noting the fastest sixty-five averaged one hundred additions per one hundred seconds while the slowest averaged one-fourth as many.

> "The sixty-five individuals who added the most rapidly made seven errors per thousand additions. The twenty who were slowest made an average of seventeen and one-half errors."
