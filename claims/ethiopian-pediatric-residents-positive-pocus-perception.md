### Robel S 2025

Robel S, Tigist W, Feleke Y, Moges M. (2025). Exploring pediatric residents’ readiness for point-of-care ultrasound in two teaching hospitals in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia: insights into training, confidence, and practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07959-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 97 pediatric residents at TASH and SPHMMC using an online questionnaire with ten perception questions dichotomized at the median. Descriptive results show "70.1% of residents have a Good understanding" of POCUS use and 67% rated it valuable for diagnostic accuracy.

> "A significant majority (70.1%) of residents have a Good understanding of the potential use of POCUS for pediatric patients, while 29.9% rated it poor. A majority (67%) of residents agreed that POCUS was a valuable tool for improving diagnostic accuracy."
