## Evidence

### Ralf Teusner 2017

Ralf Teusner, Kai-Adrian Rollmann, Jan Renz. (2017). Taking Informed Action on Student Activity in MOOCs. L@S 2017. https://doi.org/10.1145/3051457.3053971

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

First interview series with an early-stage Cluster Viewer prototype: eight instructors from five different MOOCs, about 30 minutes each. The authors report that "most instructors had encountered and were able to name groups as “no-shows”" and other groups such as private passers and thoughtful thread starters. No effect size is reported.

> "For this series, we interviewed eight instructors from ﬁve different MOOCs, about 30 minutes each. Regardless of knowing the cited publications concerning the different student groups, most instructors had encountered and were able to name groups as “no-shows”"
