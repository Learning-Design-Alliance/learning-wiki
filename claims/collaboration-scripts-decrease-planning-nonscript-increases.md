## Evidence

### Ludvigsen 2017

Ludvigsen, S., Law, N., Rosé, C. P., & Stahl, G. (2017). Frameworks for mass collaboration, adaptable scripts, complex systems theory, and collaborative writing. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn, 12, 127–131. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-017-9257-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial reports this result from Wang, Kollar, and Stegman's experimental study, in which "The students in this study were assigned to three different conditions: adaptable collaborative scripts, non-adaptable scripts, and non-scripted collaboration". Planning is described as one important aspect of self-regulation. No effect size is printed.

> "The results show that collaboration scripts, both adaptable and non-adaptable, decreased the planning required by the students, while in the non-script condition it increased."
