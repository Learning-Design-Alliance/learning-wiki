`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational log analysis of 199 students in 15 sections of an online Python course at five community colleges. The article reports "an average of 66 submissions (σ = 72)" and 64 feedback checks; separately, 28% of feedback pages were never checked, 56% checked once, 16% more than once.

> "Throughout the course, across five projects, students made an average of 66 submissions (σ = 72) and checked feedback an average of 64 times (σ = 73)."
