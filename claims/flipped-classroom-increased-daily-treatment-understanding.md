## Evidence

### Schuller BW 2022

Schuller BW, Burch C, Casterton T, Crowther C, Fowler J, Stenmark MH. (2022). Precision patient education using a “flipped classroom” approach. J Appl Clin Med Phys. https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13601

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post survey comparison in the same pilot cohort using a one-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank test for matched pairs. Mean self-assessed understanding rose from "Avg, 3.3" to "Avg, 4.8"; the article reports p = 0.0078 and no effect size.

> "increased patient understanding of what to expect during daily radiation treatments (before vs. after intervention [Figure 1b]: Avg, 3.3; 95% CI, 2.4–4.3 vs. Avg, 4.8; 95% CI, 4.4–5.1; p = 0.0078)"
