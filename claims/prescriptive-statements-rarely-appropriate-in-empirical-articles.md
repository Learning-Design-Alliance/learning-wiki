This is the author's account of the 'Prescriptives on Prescriptives' meeting in Austin, TX, whose invitees included named researchers. The group's conclusion is reported as expert opinion; no empirical data or effect sizes are printed.

> "Instead of producing a list of such conditions, the group decided that prescriptive statements are rarely appropriate in empirical research articles. Rather, authors should simply state study conclusions, whether they be causal, correlational, or descriptive conclusions."
