`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 4, a randomized two-condition experiment with 75 vocational education students (57 analyzed) learning probability calculation from video worked examples. The t-test showed "no significant difference between the restudy (M=38 .43 %, SD=32.32) and test ( M=50.00 %, SD=35.51) condition", t(55)=1.282, p=0.205, Cohen's d=0.341; equivalence was not tested.
