### Polónyiová K. 2025

Polónyiová K., Teličák P., Vitovič P., Hnilicová S. (2025). Psychometric analysis of the Slovak version of the satisfaction with simulation experience scale (measurement invariance). Medical Education Online, 30(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2534055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and psychometric analysis of SSE subscales in 463 Slovak-program and 334 English-program medical students (Table 4). The article reports "McDonald’s omega (ω) and Cronbach’s alpha (α) values range from 0.89 to 0.95", with negative skewness indicating mostly positive ratings.

> "For both groups, McDonald’s omega (ω) and Cronbach’s alpha (α) values range from 0.89 to 0.95, indicating excellent reliability for all subscales."
