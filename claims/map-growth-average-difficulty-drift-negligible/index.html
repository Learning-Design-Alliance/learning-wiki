q2 · i? · associational · r2

Item recalibration of items analyzed after excluding those with fewer than 1,000 responses, using the post-June 2021 calibration procedure on Fall 2017–Spring 2019 test events. The report calls these changes "quite small" and notes the difficulty difference distributions are normal and symmetrical around zero.

"The average change in item difficulty estimates was 0.03, -0.03, 0.06, and 0.02 logit in math, reading, language usage, and science, respectively."
