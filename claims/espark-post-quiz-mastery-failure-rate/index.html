Evidence¶

Gadgil 2016¶

Gadgil, S., & Louw, M. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Improving Ed-Tech Products through Feedback. Learning Media Design Center, Carnegie Mellon University. Report to Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5364ec3a-0d6d-4252-8885-4484e657fde7

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of eSpark log data on 2078 completed quests from September 10 to May 6. The report found "students scored a mastery level of 80 percent or more only 14 percent of the times" on three-attempt quests; the rest were promoted after making a video.

"Furthermore, of those quests in which students needed three attempts on the post-test, students scored a mastery level of 80 percent or more only 14 percent of the times (See Figure 4)."
