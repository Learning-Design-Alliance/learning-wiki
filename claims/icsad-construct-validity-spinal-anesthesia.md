## Evidence

### Corvetto Marcia A. 2017

Corvetto Marcia A., Fuentes Carlos, Araneda Andrea, Achurra Pablo, Miranda Pablo, Viviani Paola, Altermatt Fernando R. (2017). Validation of the imperial college surgical assessment device for spinal anesthesia. BMC Anesthesiology. [doi:10.1186/s12871-017-0422-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0422-3)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity study of 30 physicians (10 novices, 10 intermediates, 10 experts) performing spinal anesthesia on a simulated torso. Kruskal-Wallis tests showed "all groups had significant differences between them (p = 0.026, p = 0.045 and p = 0.005 respectively)" for the three ICSAD dexterity measures. No effect size was printed.

> "Regarding total path length, number of movements and procedural time measured with ICSAD, all groups had significant differences between them (p = 0.026, p = 0.045 and p = 0.005 respectively)."
