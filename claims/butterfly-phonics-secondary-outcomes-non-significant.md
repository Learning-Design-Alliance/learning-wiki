# Butterfly Phonics showed positive but non-statistically-significant effects on secondary outcome measures of single word reading (g = 0.38) and non-word reading (g = 0.23)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant effect of the intervention on SWRT scores (effect size 0.38, 95% CI -0.14, 0.90) or PhAB non-word reading scores (effect size 0.23, 95% CI -0.03, 0.49), though both effects were positive. [→ Christine Merrell 2015](#christine-merrell-2015)

## Evidence

### Christine Merrell 2015

Christine Merrell, Adetayo Kasim. (2015). Butterfly Phonics: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat multilevel analysis of the two secondary outcome measures in the same randomised controlled trial (SWRT N=170 intervention, 167 control; PhAB same). The evaluators note it "might have been expected" that a phonics programme would show greater improvement on these measures.

> "There was no significant effect of the intervention on the SWRT scores with an effect size of 0.38 (-0.14, 0.90) or on the PhAB scores with an effect size of 0.23 (-0.03, 0.49). While the effects were positive in favour of the intervention group, they did not reach statistical significance."
