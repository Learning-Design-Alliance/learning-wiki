## Evidence

### Hu Jia 2025

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison on the operation exam (Score Three) one week after the intervention, scored on apparatus selection, operation steps, and doctor-patient communication. The experimental group total of "90.65 ± 4.81" was significantly higher, with Dimension 4 at "54.87 ± 3.16"; no effect size is printed.

> "the total score of group A was 90.65 ± 4.81, which was significantly higher than that of the control group (p = 0.000). Specifically, the score for the operational steps (Dimension 4) was 54.87 ± 3.16, which was considerably higher than the control group (p = 0.000)"

### Hu Jia 2025 (2)

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same operation exam, the two dimensions that did not differ significantly between the randomized groups were apparatus selection (26.79 ± 1.99 vs 26.30 ± 1.30) and doctor-patient communication (8.99 ± 0.78 vs 8.77 ± 0.43).

> "The scores were comparable between the two groups in apparatus selection (Dimension 3) and doctor-patient communication (Dimension 5) (p = 0.105, p = 0.052)"
