Evidence¶

Avci Akbel 2018¶

Avci Akbel, B. (2018). A review on effectiveness of cello etudes created for longas and syrtos. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 75, 1-18. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181464

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Individual-level analysis (Table 4, Kruskal-Wallis tests, N=12 observations per cell) shows TMV-Dif values of roughly 10–15 for experimental persons versus roughly 1–3 for control persons; the article calls the etudes "more effective than traditional educational methods."

"The posttest mean scores of both the control group and the experimental group are higher than the pre-test mean scores. In other words, the persons performing the piece made progress later (in the posttest) compared to the beginning (pre -test), and the evaluation scores increased as a result of the posttest evaluation."
