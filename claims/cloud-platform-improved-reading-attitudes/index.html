Evidence¶

Shih 2013¶

Shih, R.-C., Cho, C.-L., Tsai, C.-C., & Lou, S.-J. (2013). Employing Microsoft Live@Edu Cloud Platform to Assist in Teaching Chinese Reading for Junior High School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018038

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-sample t-tests (test value=3, df=31) on the Reading Attitude Learning Scale showed all four domains — cognitive (t=9.16), affective (t=5.73), behavioral (t=6.37), overall (t=8.31) — significantly above midpoint (p=.000<.05).

"The four domains of the reading attitude scale obtained p=.000<.05, indicating the experimental group students have made a significant progress on cognitive, affective, behavioral, and overall performance."
