## Evidence

### WANG Zhong; CUI Zhen; ZHANG Yi 2022

WANG Zhong; CUI Zhen; ZHANG Yi. (2022). The relationship between the interdisciplinary activation of children's scientific concepts and their mastery of basic knowledges： A pre-study based on reaction times. [doi:10.1101/2022.09.12.506518](https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.09.12.506518)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of each student group against one adult participant (an author and science teacher, assumed full basic knowledge). The high group versus adult total-RT difference was not significant (P = 0.276), the low group versus adult was (P = 0.000). The authors note the high-group effect size (c = 0.33) deviates, likely because only one adult was sampled, and urge caution.

> "the reaction times of the participants in the high score group is significantly closer to that of the adult (there is no significant difference between the high score group and the adult, and the difference between the low is extremely significant)."
