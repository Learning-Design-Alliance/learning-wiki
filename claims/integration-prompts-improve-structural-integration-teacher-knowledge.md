# Integration prompts improve structural integration of knowledge across professional domains in pre-service teachers' mental models

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Prompted conditions produced essays with greater structural similarity to an expert reference model than unprompted conditions. [→ Lehmann 2019](#lehmann-2019)

## Evidence

### Lehmann 2019

Lehmann, T., Pirnay-Dummer, P., & Schmidt-Borcherding, F. (2019). Fostering integrated mental models of different professional knowledge domains: instructional approaches and model-based analyses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09704-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Laboratory experiment with 92 pre-service primary mathematics teachers in a 2x2 factorial design of writing task and prompts. The prompted group showed significantly higher GAMMA scores, "F(1,88) = 4.439, p = .038, ηp2 = .048".

> "Results showed a significant effect of prompts on the GAMMA measure, F(1,88) = 4.439, p = .038, ηp2 = .048. Essays of students from experimental conditions with prompts (M = .658, SE = .028) were more comparable to the reference model in terms of the density of vertices than essays of students from unprompted conditions (M = .574, SE = .028)."
