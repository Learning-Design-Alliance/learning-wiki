## Evidence

### Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green 2021

Aaron Cochrane and C. Shawn Green. (2021). Trajectories of performance change indicate multiple dissociable links between working memory and fluid intelligence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00111-w

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in an experiment with 87 participants who completed matrix reasoning measures and a digitized Corsi spatial span task. Final WM accuracy related to Gf with a printed correlation of r = 0.33 (CI = [0.11, 0.52], BF log3 = 2.99).

> "In contrast, both rate of change on the WM task ( r(85) = −0.30, CI = [−0.49, −0.08], BFlog3 = 2.05) and ﬁnal accuracy ( r(85) = 0.33, CI = [0.11, 0.52], BF log3 = 2.99) showed evidence of relations to Gf"
