## Evidence

### Witti MJ 2026

Witti MJ, Coleman J-C, Richters C, Schönberger J, de Ruijter M-A, Stortz A, Bauer M, Kiriakidou R, Stadler M. (2026). Simulation-Based Radiology Training for Medical Technical Assistant Trainees: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/97489

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-design ANOVA with time (pretest vs posttest) as the within-subject factor and group (radiology vs laboratory) as the between-subject factor. The time × group interaction was not significant, "indicating that learning gains did not differ meaningfully between radiology-specialized and laboratory-specialized trainees."

> "The time × group interaction was not significant (F1,48=2.53; P=.12; η2=0.012), indicating that learning gains did not differ meaningfully between radiology-specialized and laboratory-specialized trainees."
