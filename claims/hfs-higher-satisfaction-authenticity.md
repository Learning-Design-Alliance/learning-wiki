### Huh Yeon-Ju 2026

Huh Yeon-Ju, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Narae, Jeong Seoi, Kong Hyoun-Joong, Myung Sun Jung. (2026). Immersive Virtual Reality Training to Improve Novice Physicians’ Emergency Response Skills: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71455

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Satisfaction was a secondary outcome measured with study-specific 7-point Likert questionnaires in the randomized trial. The quote reports the HFS-over-VR comparison with printed means and P values; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "the HFS group was more satisfied overall than was the VR group (mean 6.23, SD 0.92 vs mean 5.89, SD 1.10; t162=2.29, P=.02), particularly regarding tutor guidance (mean 6.49, SD 0.86 vs mean 6.10, SD 1.02; P=.008) and authenticity of the educational experience (mean 6.24, SD 1.05 vs mean 5.77, SD 1.15; P=.006)"
