### Vembye 2022

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 76 treatment and control group studies using the CHE-RVE model with robust variance estimation; 280 short-term effect sizes, 82% pretest-adjusted. The review reports a "moderate, positive, and statistically signif- icant mean effect" of g = 0.11 with CI [0.035, 0.184].

> "We found a moderate, positive, and statistically signif- icant mean effect of 𝑔̅ = 0.11, 95% CI[0.035, 0.184] of collaborative instruction compared to sin- gle-taught controls, using the correlated-hierarchical effects (CHE-RVE) model."
