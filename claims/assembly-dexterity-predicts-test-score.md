## Evidence

### Beattie Kirsty L. 2022

Beattie Kirsty L., Hill Andrew, Horswill Mark S., Grove Philip M., Stevenson Andrew R. L. (2022). Aptitude and attitude: predictors of performance during and after basic laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08668-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression on total test score (the six 3-Dmed tasks repeated twice) for 34 novices. The article reports the model was significant, F(2, 31) = 12.15, p < 0.001, explaining 40.3% of variance, and that as manual dexterity (assembly) increased, test score significantly decreased. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "the final model to predict total test score included test viewing mode (control variable) and manual dexterity (assembly) only."
