q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, from four student-focused primary studies it synthesises, impacts on "learning perception", activity engagement, content knowledge acquisition, and productive discourse; e.g., Dickler et al.'s visualisation reports enabled targeted teacher support improving inquiry engagement.

"Four studies examined student-related measurements to analyse the impact of LA-supported IBL on learning perception (Bodily et al., 2018), activity engagement (Dickler et al., 2021), content knowledge acquisition (Wiley et al., 2023), and productive discourse (de Araujo et al., 2024)."
