A descriptive taxonomy from the examples section: the setup uses two scintillating detectors with a variable-thickness absorber and a coincidence unit. The author lists example projects such as angular distribution of the cosmic ray flux and properties of Poisson data of random events.

> "Student projects which can be pursued using these setups can be divided into two groups: -projects for studying certain properties of cosmic rays, for studying of experimental methods and apparatus of cosmic ray physics and high energy physics; -projects for studying the properties of random processes (in this case, the flux of particles of cosmic rays is considered as a generator of random processes)."
