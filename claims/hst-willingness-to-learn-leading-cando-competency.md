Cross-source thematic finding from the mixed methods study: Table 1 shows willingness to learn in 13 (72%) of 18 job descriptions and 10 (33%) of 30 interviews. The article links this attitude to HSTs actively seeking to fill knowledge gaps in complex, ill-defined roles.

> "According to our findings, a willingness to learn was the leading competency in the can-do domain, appearing in more than two thirds of job descriptions and a third of interviews."
