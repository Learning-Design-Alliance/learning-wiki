### John A. Milsom 2021

John A. Milsom. (2021). The Brachistochrone: An excellent problem for all levels of physics students. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.07726](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.07726)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of student answers to Question 1 across Groups I-IV; the author categorizes the wrong answers as "numerous misconceptions," including constant-acceleration beliefs, velocity-acceleration confusion, and misplaced centripetal reasoning.

> "Overall, the incorrect answers indicated numerous misconceptions. There were inconsistent answers (answer three), students who think the acceleration is constant, mixing up velocity and acceleration, thinking that centripetal acceleration matters, etc."
