## Evidence

### Bomholt Katrine Bjørnshave 2019

Bomholt Katrine Bjørnshave, Krogh Lise Qvirin, Bomholt Svend Rosendahl, Nebsbjerg Mette Amalie, Thim Troels, Løfgren Bo. (2019). Three-Month Retention of Basic Life Support with an Automated External Defibrillator Using a Two-Stage versus Four-Stage Teaching Technique. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/1394972

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled noninferiority follow-up of 129 laypersons tested in a simulated cardiac arrest scenario three months after instructor-led training; video-assessed 17-action ERC checklist. The study reports "a pass rate of 10.8% versus 10.9%, respectively, p=1" — no significant difference; equivalence beyond the noninferiority framing was not further tested.

> "There was no statistical significant difference in retention of BLS/AED skills three months after training with a pass rate of 10.8% versus 10.9%, respectively, p=1."
