Prospective randomised controlled pilot study in which postgraduate year 2 and 3 residents acted as team leader, with the intervention group reviewing performance before a second attempt using the CALM tool; the review reports a "Positive effect of incorporating video review feedback". Gain scores were the outcome measure.
