## Evidence

### Burm Sarah 2020

Burm Sarah, Sebok-Syer Stefanie S., Van Koughnett Julie Ann, Watling Christopher J. (2020). Are we generating more assessments without added value? Surgical trainees’ perceptions of and receptiveness to cross-specialty assessment. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00594-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of trainee focus groups in the cross-specialty assessment study. Although the checklist enabled assessors to record and confirm completion of each step, trainees said it failed to adequately capture the nuance of the task; one remarked "You can't boil everything down to a checklist".

> "Trainees perceived the use of a checklist tool impeding outside assessors’ ability to provide authentic feedback."
