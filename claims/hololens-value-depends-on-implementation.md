### Lin Joseph 2026

Lin Joseph, Ghazi Sarmad, Kuss Ethan, Shehata Sarah. (2026). Medical students’ experiences of mixed-reality-based anatomy instruction in a non-cadaveric curriculum: a qualitative case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09881-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 12 first-year medical students found restricted manipulation, technical barriers such as lagging and Wi-Fi instability, and teacher-controlled viewing modes shaped perceived value. Students "repeatedly recommended a hybrid instructional model."

> "I think a hybrid format would be ideal. Still have the lab, but use HoloLens as a supplement."
