## Evidence

### Young-Suk Grace Kim 2024

Young-Suk Grace Kim, Dandan Yang, Jinkyung Hwang. (2024). Are Mathematics and Writing Skills Related? Evidence from Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09960-4

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic disaggregation of subskill pairs by grade level (Table 2). In primary grades the low-writing/low-math pair showed the strongest relation (r = 0.59), with the higher order pairs "slightly weaker with similar magnitudes" (r = 0.47; r = 0.48).

> "within primary grades, lower order writing and mathematics skills exhibited the strongest relation (r  = 0.59, SE = 0.04, p < 0.001), whereas the relations between lower order writing and higher order mathematics (r  = 0.47, SE = 0.06, p < 0.001) and between higher order writing and higher order mathematics (r  = 0.48, SE= 0.07, p = 0.002)  were slightly weaker with similar magnitudes."

### Young-Suk Grace Kim 2024 (2)

Young-Suk Grace Kim, Dandan Yang, Jinkyung Hwang. (2024). Are Mathematics and Writing Skills Related? Evidence from Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09960-4

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic subskill-pair analysis for university and beyond (Table 2). The higher order pair's correlation of r = 0.26 was weaker than the lower order pair's r = 0.37, suggesting shared variance between higher order skills diverges in adulthood.

> "In addition, for those in university and beyond, the correlation between lower order writing and lower order mathematics skills was 0.37 (SE = 0.07, p = 0.004) whereas it was 0.26 (SE = 0.09, p = 0.03) between higher order writing and mathematics skills."
