## Evidence

### Diamond Adele 2011

Diamond Adele, Lee Kathleen. (2011). Interventions shown to Aid Executive Function Development in Children 4–12 Years Old. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1204529

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a computerized inhibition-training study in which gains appeared on only 2 of 3 practiced games "with no transfers to un-practiced tasks."

> "Using the same dosage, duration, and frequency as CogMed© studies, improvements in 4 and 6-year-olds were found on only 2 of the 3 inhibition games practiced, with no transfers to un-practiced tasks (13)."

### Diamond Adele 2011 (2)

Diamond Adele, Lee Kathleen. (2011). Interventions shown to Aid Executive Function Development in Children 4–12 Years Old. Science. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1204529

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review describes a double-blind randomized-control trial with multiple training- and transfer-tasks in 4-year-olds; transfer was skill-specific and "Transfers were narrow."

> "Those trained on working memory improved more on working-memory transfer tasks than did controls and those trained in reasoning improved more on reasoning transfer tasks than controls (12). Neither group showed transfer to the un-practiced skill"
