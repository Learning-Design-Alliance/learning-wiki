### Lehikko 2025

Lehikko, A., Nykänen, M., & Ruokamo, H. (2025). Conceptualizing a pedagogical model for immersive virtual reality safety training: pedagogical practices in trainer interviews. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10490-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of 18 semi-structured thematic interviews with safety trainers in two Finnish work organizations, coded inductively and reported as frequencies. The category 'match the pedagogy to training goals' comprised the subcategories 'ensure relevance', 'select delivery method', and 'evaluate outcomes'; trainers "sought to incorporate examples of the learners' everyday work situations in the training process."

> "Fifteen trainers mentioned selecting topics from the learners' direct work contexts. Twelve referred to the use of practical examples, and five talked about providing training that focused on teamwork. The second subcategory was select delivery method"
