Evidence¶

Worth 2015¶

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Primary outcome analysis of a three-arm cluster-randomised trial (17 numeracy, 19 literacy, 19 control schools) using multilevel models with pre-test covariates. The report states the numeracy programme had "a significant positive impact on the primary numeracy outcome" with effect size 0.20, equating to three months' additional progress.

"The numeracy programme had a significant positive impact on the primary numeracy outcome, with an effect size of 0.20 (confidence interval 0.02– 0.37)."
