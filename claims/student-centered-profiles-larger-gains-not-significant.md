## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2026

Meagan Sundstrom, Justin Gambrell, Colin Green, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Eric Brewe. (2026). Relative benefits of different active learning methods to conceptual physics learning. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1038/s41567-026-03307-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-026-03307-2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary analysis assigning each course to the profile of at least two of its three observations, with pooled Hedges' g by profile (n = 1,595 students). The article calls this "preliminary evidence" that instructional practices may be related to conceptual learning.

> "The pooled effect size is larger for courses assigned to the Worksheets (Hedges’g = 0.94 [0.60, 1.28]) and Other Groupwork (Hedges’g= 0.95 [0.45, 1.45]) profiles than for courses assigned to the Lecture (Hedges’g= 0.68 [0.33, 1.02]) and Clickers (Hedges’g= 0.60 [0.34, 0.87]) profiles."
