## Evidence

### Janaka 2025

Janaka, N., Zhao, S., Ram, A., Sun, R., Tan, S. T. J. W., Li, D., & Hsu, D. (2025). Progressive Sentences: Combining the Benefits of Word and Sentence Learning. MobileHCI '25 Adjunct. [doi:10.1145/3737821.3749564](https://doi.org/10.1145/3737821.3749564)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pilot 4 (N=4) ecological validation using Norwegian (Bokmål) as L2 with a pre-test. Sitting yielded significantly higher immediate Free-Recall than Walking (η²p = 0.91); other differences were not significant. Trends consistent with Study 1.

> "Sitting resulted in significantly higher immediate Free-Recall than Walking (𝐹1,3 = 32.0,𝑝 = 0.01,𝜂2𝑝 = 0.91); other differences were not significant. The trends observed were consistent with those in Study 1."
