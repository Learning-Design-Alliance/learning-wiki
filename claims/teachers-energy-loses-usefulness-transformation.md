## Evidence

### Daane 2014

Daane, Vokos, and Scherr. (2014). Goals for teacher learning about energy degradation and usefulness. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 10, 020111. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.10.020111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode analysis of a secondary-teacher discussion of a block sliding across the floor: Dennis describes heating of molecules as a loss of "usefulness," supported by peers Tom, Joe, and Doug invoking entropy and the second law. No quantitative data reported; qualitative interpretive analysis.

> "Dennis clarifies that "the energy stays the same, yet we're losing the usefulness." In this statement, he distinguishes between the total amount of energy (constant) and the value of energy (decreasing)."
