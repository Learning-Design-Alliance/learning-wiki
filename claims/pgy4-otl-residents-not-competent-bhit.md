Lelli Danny A., Rourke Ryan, Tse Darren. (2024). Competence of Senior Otolaryngology Residents with the Bedside Head Impulse Test—Has There Been Improvement After 5 Years of Competency By Design? Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1177/19160216241288817

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional performance assessment of 30 consenting PGY4 residents at a national exam review course, rated in real time by two expert examiners using a modified Ottawa Clinic Assessment Tool. The quote reports the proportion reaching the "average score of 4 or greater" competence cutoff.

> "Only 2 (6.7%) and 6 (20%) of residents (DT and DL rating, respectively) were able to perform the bHIT independently with an average score of 4 or greater."
