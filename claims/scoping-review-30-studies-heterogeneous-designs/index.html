PRISMA-ScR flow of the scoping review's study selection: 737 records identified across PubMed, Scopus, LILACS and IEEE Xplore; after de-duplication "670 unique records were retained for title and abstract screening" and 61 full texts were assessed, yielding 30 included studies in the qualitative synthesis.

"Following removal of duplicates using the Rayyan platform, 670 unique records were retained for title and abstract screening. Subsequently, 61 articles were evaluated as full text, of which 31 were excluded for not meeting the previously defined eligibility criteria. Finally, 30 studies were included in the qualitative synthesis."
