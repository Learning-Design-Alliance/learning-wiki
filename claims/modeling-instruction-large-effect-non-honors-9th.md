### Michael J. O'Brien and John R. Thompson 2008

Michael J. O'Brien and John R. Thompson. (2008). Effectiveness of Ninth-Grade Physics in Maine: Conceptual Understanding. The Physics Teacher. http://scitation.aip.org/tpt/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0901.4692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0901.4692)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison within the Maine study (Table II): non-honors 9th graders taught with Modeling (N=32) scored 8.9 post-test with 18% normalized gain (p = 0.000), versus 6.3 and 3% (p = 0.072) for traditionally taught peers (N=80). The authors write it "appeared to have a large effect."

> "The use of Modeling Instruction14 appeared to have a large effect on the non-honors-level, ninth-grade students’ performance on the post-test and their normalized gains."
