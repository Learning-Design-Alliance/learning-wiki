Sanjoy Mahajan. (2005). Observations on teaching first-year physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Tutor's observational study of ten Cambridge first-year physics students given the FCI before lectures; scores were "25/30 (83%)" low, average "27.5/30 (92%)". Yet on free-response tutorial problems the author found students guessing rather than reasoning.

> "I gave the FCI to my ten students as their ﬁrst assignment befo re lectures began. The lowest score was 25/30 (83%) and the average was 27.5/30 (92%); one studen t had a perfect score of 30/30."
