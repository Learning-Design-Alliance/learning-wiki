## Evidence

### Mahoney B 2021

Mahoney B, Luebbert E. (2021). Updates on Simulation in Obstetrical Anesthesiology Through the COVID-19 Pandemic. Anesthesiology Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anclin.2021.08.001

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A randomized trial by Sorenson and colleagues compared simulation-based obstetric anesthesia training for emergency cesarean section and postpartum hemorrhage scenarios conducted in situ versus off-site. Outcomes were "similar individual and team outcomes" across measures; no effect sizes are printed.

> "They found similar individual and team outcomes in patient safety attitudes, stress, motivation, perceptions of the simulations, and team performance while those receiving in situ simulation training did find a greater degree of fidelity than those receiving remote training."
