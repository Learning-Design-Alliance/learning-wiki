`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same 10-interview qualitative study, the engagement theme captured how preconceptions shaped engagement; one debriefer reported that when VR-based simulation was introduced, learners "showed up and they were curious". No effect size reported.

> "Debriefers with positive preconceptions about IVR and gaming, in contrast, were motivated by its ability to engage learners who felt exposed or intimidated in mannequin‐based simulation."
