## Evidence

### Kim Seung Jae 2019

Kim Seung Jae, Kwon Oh Deog, Kim Kyae Hyung, Lee Ji Eun, Lee Seung-Hee, Shin Jwa-Seop, Park Sang Min. (2019). Investigating the effects of interprofessional communication education for medical students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Attitude question in the paired pre/post questionnaires (Table 4) asked how important the class is for future clinical work. The pre-training mean was already 4.17±0.97 on the 5-point Likert scale, leaving little room for change; the non-significant test printed no effect size.

> "Although the mean score slightly increased by 0.1 points after practice, it was not statistically significant (p=0.159)."
