# The Boltzmann Factor tutorial significantly improves students' appropriate use of the Boltzmann factor on exam questions at both schools

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Matched pre/post exam data show statistically significant improvement in Boltzmann factor use after the tutorial at School 1 (p = 0.019 part A, p = 0.012 part B) and at School 2 (p < 0.001). [→ Trevor I. Smith 2015](#trevor-i-smith-2015)
`q2 i?` After tutorial instruction, all 13 matched School 1 students used appropriate Boltzmann factor reasoning on both PRQ parts, and about 95% used it correctly on the PRQ Analog at each school. [→ Trevor I. Smith 2015 (2)](#trevor-i-smith-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2015

Trevor I. Smith, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Student Understanding of the Boltzmann Factor. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Matched pre-/post-tutorial exam data (19 sets at School 1, 29 at School 2) coded into four categories and compared with Fisher's exact tests. "statistically signiﬁcantly better than the results on the PRQ pretest" at School 1; School 2 exam results also significantly better (p < 0.001 for both parts).

> "a Fisher's exact test compar- ing the data at School 1 showed that the results from the exams are statistically signiﬁcantly better than the results on the PRQ pretest on both parts A ( p = 0.019) and B (p = 0.012)"

### Trevor I. Smith 2015 (2)

Trevor I. Smith, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Student Understanding of the Boltzmann Factor. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-tutorial exam results (Fig. 5) for matched students; all 13 School 1 students used appropriate reasoning on both PRQ parts, all but one (~83%) on the PRQ Analog, and all but two School 2 students (~95%) on the PRQ Analog. "all 13 stu- dents at School 1 used appropriate Boltzmann factor rea- soning."

> "The most striking feature of Fig. 5 is that all 13 stu- dents at School 1 used appropriate Boltzmann factor rea- soning on both parts of the PRQ"
