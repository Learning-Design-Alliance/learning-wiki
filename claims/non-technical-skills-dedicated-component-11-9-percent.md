### Schlosser‐Hupf 2025

Schlosser‐Hupf, Sophie, Staudacher, Jonas, Wagner, Verena, Unger, Mira, Sousa, Paula, Donning, Inga Marie, Müller‐Schilling, Martina, Heinrich, Henriette, and the GENIUS Study Group. (2025). United European Gastroenterology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1002/ueg2.70127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of non‐technical skill coverage across the 42 national curricula. The study reports that "Non‐technical skills were explicitly addressed in only 11.9% (5/42) of the curricula", with communication skills in 64.3% (27/42), professionalism in 23.8% (10/42), and leadership in 26.2% (11/42); patient advocacy, shared decision‐making, resilience and interprofessional collaboration were scarcely mentioned.

> "Non‐technical skills were explicitly addressed in only 11.9% (5/42) of the curricula. However, when individual domains were assessed separately, several non‐technical competencies appeared more frequently. Communication skills were included in 64.3% (27/42) of the training programmes"
