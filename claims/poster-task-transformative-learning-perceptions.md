## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ross Andrew 2019

Ross Andrew, Dlungwane Thembelihle, Van Wyk Jacqueline. (2019). Using poster presentation to assess large classes: a case study of a first-year undergraduate module at a South African university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1863-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Student questionnaire item (n = 230 returned): 211 of 229 respondents (92%) agreed the poster let them demonstrate how they made a difference in the community. On the corresponding staff item, 10 of 10 (100%) agreed students could demonstrate this. The authors interpret this as a transformative learning outcome.
