Tan, C. Y. (2024). Socioeconomic Status and Student Learning: Insights from an Umbrella Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09929-3

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The review identifies measurement issues raised across included reviews: students' limited knowledge of parental SES data, reflective versus formative measurement approaches, poverty threshold determination, measurement invariance across sub-populations, and missing data "when only one parent is present in families" (Willms & Tramonte, 2019).

> "Additionally, compared to older students, young children are less able to indicate the education and profession of their parents accurately"
