## Evidence

### Li Xiangyu 2024

Li Xiangyu, Zhang Longsheng, Sun Wenjun, Lei Min, Li Ying, Zhang Jiacheng, Huang Xing. (2024). Comparison of the effects of different teaching methods on the effectiveness of teaching neurology in China: a bayesian network meta-analysis and systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06397-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The discussion's summary of the Bayesian NMA of 31 RCTs involving 2,124 students and 7 teaching methods; CBL ranked first on both outcomes (SUCRA 98.4% theoretical, 89.2% practical). This is the authors' synthesis conclusion, not a standardized effect size.

> "CBL is the optimal strategy when we use theoretical or practical test scores to measure the effect of neurology education."

### Li Xiangyu 2024 (2)

Li Xiangyu, Zhang Longsheng, Sun Wenjun, Lei Min, Li Ying, Zhang Jiacheng, Huang Xing. (2024). Comparison of the effects of different teaching methods on the effectiveness of teaching neurology in China: a bayesian network meta-analysis and systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06397-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias assessment of the NMA: Begg's tests and comparison-adjusted funnel plots showed symmetric scatter for theoretical exam scores (P = 0.060) and practical skill scores (P = 0.348), indicating no small-sample effect or publication bias.

> "The results of Begg' tests suggested no publication bias in the included studies."
