### Rachel E. Scherr 2007

Rachel E. Scherr. (2007). Gesture analysis for physics education researchers. Accepted to Physical Review Special Topics – Physics Education Research, May 2007. https://journals.aps.org/prper/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0712.0581](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0712.0581)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Church and Goldin-Meadow, a study classifying children as conserving or nonconserving and gestures as concordant or nonconcordant; nonconserving nonconcordant children learned the most in a later teaching task. The review offers no effect size.

> "In a subsequent teaching task, nonconserving nonconcordant children learned the most. The suggested explanation is that gesture-speech mismatches indicate when speakers are “of two minds,”"
