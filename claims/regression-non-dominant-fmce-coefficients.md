# In a multiple linear regression of LA-supported courses, non-dominant status predicts effect sizes 0.262 higher than dominant peers, and the FMCE predicts effect sizes 0.2 lower than the FCI

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The non-dominant status coefficient (0.262, p=0.010) was statistically significant, indicating higher projected effect sizes for non-dominant students in LA-supported courses. [→ Van Dusen 2016](#van-dusen-2016)
`q2 i?` The FMCE coefficient (-0.200, p=0.032) was statistically significant, while the CSEM coefficient and the dominance-by-instrument interaction coefficients were not significant. [→ Van Dusen 2016 (2)](#van-dusen-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2016

Van Dusen, B., White, J.-S. S., & Roualdes, E. A. (2016). The impact of learning assistants on inequities in physics student outcomes. 2016 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.085

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on LA-supported courses only (courses without LAs excluded because only 20% of data lacked LAs), with intercept set to dominant students on the FCI. Table III prints Non-dom. coefficient 0.262, S.E. 0.102, t=2.579, p=0.010. The 0.262 is an unstandardized regression coefficient, not an effect size.

> "The non-dominant coefficient indicates that in courses with LAs non-dominant students are projected  to have mean effect sizes that are 0.262 higher than their dominant peers."

### Van Dusen 2016 (2)

Van Dusen, B., White, J.-S. S., & Roualdes, E. A. (2016). The impact of learning assistants on inequities in physics student outcomes. 2016 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.085

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same regression model (Table III) tested concept-inventory and interaction effects. FMCE coefficient -0.200 (S.E. 0.093, t=-2.151, p=0.032) was significant; CSEM (-0.074, p=0.387) and both interaction terms (Non-dom.*FMCE p=0.438; Non-dom.*CSEM p=0.193) were not. Coefficients are unstandardized; no effect size printed.

> "The FMCE coefficient indicates that in courses with LAs the mean  student effect sizes on the FMCE is projected to be 0.2 lower than on the FCI."
