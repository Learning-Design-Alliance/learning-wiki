# Educator perceptions of TPE become more positive with each year of implementation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Year-by-year survey comparisons from 2013 to 2015 show increasing agreement among both principals and teachers across TPE items. [→ Slotnik 2015](#slotnik-2015)

## Evidence

### Slotnik 2015

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2015). Change in Practice in Maryland: Student Learning Objectives and Teacher and Principal Evaluation. Mid-Atlantic Comprehensive Center (WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED568386

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Statewide survey analysis of 2013-2015 responses (2015: 17,344 teachers, 1,160 principals), with Mann-Whitney U tests of group and year differences. The report states there has been “a year by year increase in the percent of teachers who agree with every survey item.”

> "There has been a year by year increase in the percent of teachers who agree with every survey item in the sections on “Systemic Context,” the “Quality of TPE Frameworks and Processes,” and “Student Learning Objectives.”"
