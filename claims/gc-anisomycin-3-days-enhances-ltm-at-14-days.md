### Levitan 2016

Levitan, D., Gal-Ben-Ari, S., Heise, C., Rosenberg, T., Elkobi, A., Inberg, S., Sala, C. & Rosenblum, K. (2016). The differential role of cortical protein synthesis in taste memory formation and persistence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.1

Delayed-test experiment: anisomycin (n = 14) or vehicle (n = 16) was infused 3 days after weak CTA and tested 14 days later; the anisomycin group showed "signi ﬁcantly higher aversion towards saccharin" (Mann–Whitney Z = − 2.534; P = 0.01). No standardized effect size is printed.
