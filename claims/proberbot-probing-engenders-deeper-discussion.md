## Evidence

### Yvonne Rogers 2025

Yvonne Rogers. (2025). Why it is Worth Making An Effort when Learning With GenAI. International Journal of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, Volume 06, Special Issue. https://doi.org/10.56734/ijahss.v6nSa1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Early study of the VoiceViz Proberbot, which appeared at the interface when teams appeared stuck during a task analysing and explaining complex health data shown as visualisations, asking open-ended questions. Findings are reported qualitatively; no effect size or participant count is printed for this result.

> "The findings from our study showed how this Proberbot engendered more collaborative interactions, leading to team members having deeper discussions, involving new trains of thought while enabling them to generate more hypotheses."
