## Evidence

### Roszipal Benjamin 2025

Roszipal Benjamin, Szelesi Gabriella, Ernst Martin, Hoffelner Alexander, Wagner Michael. (2025). Competency development in early nursing training: a cross-sectional OSCE study of self-assessment versus examiner ratings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08279-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Planned paired-samples t-tests in the same OSCE cohort found no significant difference in either technical/analytical domain. Effect size estimates were close to zero with narrow confidence intervals. The article cautions these analyses had limited observed power (≈0.14–0.39), so nulls warrant cautious interpretation.

> "in the professional domain, ratings did not differ significantly between self and examiner assessments, t(102)=–1.69, p =.094, 95% CI [–0.043, 0.003]. Similarly, no significant difference emerged for methodological skills, t(102) = 0.86, p =.394, 95% CI [–0.018, 0.045]."
