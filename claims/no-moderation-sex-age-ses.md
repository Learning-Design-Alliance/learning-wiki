## Evidence

### Valiente Carlos 2011

Valiente Carlos, Eisenberg Nancy, Haugen Rg, Spinrad Tracy L., Hofer Claire, Liew Jeffrey, Kupfer Anne. (2011). Children's Effortful Control and Academic Achievement: Mediation Through Social Functioning. Early Education and Development, 22(3), 411-433. https://doi.org/10.1080/10409289.2010.505259

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation tests in the longitudinal SEM sample: Box's M for SES was significant, F(66, 20,290)=1.64, p<.001, but follow-up multiple-group models comparing chi-square by freeing and releasing paths found "no evidence that the parameter estimates varied for children low versus high in SES."

> "However, the nonsignificant Box's Ms indicated that sex and age did not moderate the pattern of findings, Fs(91, 18,967 and 91, 13,975)=1.19 and .830, respectively, ns."
