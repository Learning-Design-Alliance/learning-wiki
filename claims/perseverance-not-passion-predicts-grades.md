## Evidence

### Huéscar Hernández E. 2020

Huéscar Hernández E., Moreno-Murcia J. A., Cid L., Monteiro D., Rodrigues F. (2020). Passion or Perseverance? The Effect of Perceived Autonomy Support and Grit on Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17062143

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SEM path results in the Portuguese college sample; Table 2 reports Perseverance → Academic Performance β = 0.20 (CI 95%: 0.05–0.26) and Passion → Academic Performance β = 0.10 with CI 95%: −0.05 to 0.30, the latter including zero.

> "Grit-perseverance, but not grit-passion, was significantly and positively related to academic performance. Looking at the indirect effects, all paths were positive and significant (Table 2)."
