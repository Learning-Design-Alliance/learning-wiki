Evidence¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh 2016¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh, Balan Rathakrishnan, Sabariah Sharif, Rosy Talin, Oliver Valentine Eboy. (2016). The Effects of Geography Information System (GIS) Based Teaching on Underachieving Students' Mastery Goal and Achievement. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117621

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pairwise comparison within the experiment group (n=44) at alpha .05; the article reports the difference was significant, F (1, 43) = 35.58, p = .004, representing a large sized effect with partial eta squared = .453.

"The experiment group’s post-test scores ( M = 20.39, SD = 2.86) were higher than the pre-test scores ( M = 17.07, SD = 2.13)."

Soon Singh Bikar Singh 2016 (2)¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh, Balan Rathakrishnan, Sabariah Sharif, Rosy Talin, Oliver Valentine Eboy. (2016). The Effects of Geography Information System (GIS) Based Teaching on Underachieving Students' Mastery Goal and Achievement. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117621

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pairwise comparison within the experiment group on achievement; the article reports the difference was significant, F (1, 43) = 194.03, p = .001, representing a large sized effect with partial eta squared = .819.

"The experiment group post-test scores (M = 65.75, SD = 9.34) were higher than the pre-test scores ( M = 41.74, SD = 9.15)."
