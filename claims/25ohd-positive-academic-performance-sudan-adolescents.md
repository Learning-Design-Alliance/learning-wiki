## Evidence

### Hassan Ahmed A. 2023

Hassan Ahmed A., Elbashir Mustafa I., Al-Nafeesah Abdullah, AlEed Ashwaq, Adam Ishag. (2023). Association between Serum 25-Hydroxyvitamin D Concentrations and Academic Performance among Adolescent Schoolchildren: A Cross-Sectional Study. Nutrients 15(21):4552. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15214552

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 241 schoolchildren in northern Sudan; multiple linear regression of academic score in % on sociodemographic and nutritional covariates found a positive association for serum 25(OH)D level ("coefficient = 0.20, p = 0.010"). No standardized effect size printed.

> "In the multiple linear regression, while there was a positive association between age (coefficient = 3.23, p < 0.001), being female (coefficient = 7.62, p = 0.002), employment (coefficient = 6.27, p = 0.049), serum 25(OH)D level (coefficient = 0.20, p = 0.010), and academic performance"
