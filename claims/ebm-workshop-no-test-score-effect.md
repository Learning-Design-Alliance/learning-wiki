## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Feldstein David A 2010

Feldstein David A, Maenner Matthew J, Srisurichan Rachaya, Roach Mary A, Vogelman Bennett S. (2010). Evidence-based medicine training during residency: a randomized controlled trial of efficacy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-59

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 48 internal medicine residents; ANCOVA adjusted for baseline PGY-1 scores found the workshop group "0.51 points higher" at PGY-2 (p = 0.510) and 0.07 points higher at PGY-3 (p = 0.944); neither significant.

> "The adjusted mean score for the workshop group was 0.51 points higher (95% CI: -1.04, 2.05) than for the control group in the PGY-2 year (p = 0.510), and 0.07 points higher (95% CI: -1.80, 1.93) in the PGY-3 year (p = 0.944)."
