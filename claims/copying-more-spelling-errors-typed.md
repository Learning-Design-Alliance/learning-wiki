## Evidence

### Broc Lucie 2025

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Replication copying study ANOVA on POMAS-coded errors per word; the quote reports "more spelling errors per word in the keyboard-and-computer condition" with η² = 0.05. Study 1 showed the same direction (F(1, 57) = 33.9, η² = 0.07).

> "The medium used had a main effect [F(1, 166) = 47.2, p < .001, η² = 0.05]: students produced more spelling errors per word in the keyboard-and-computer condition (M = 0.11, SD = 0.09) than in the pen-and-paper condition (M = 0.06, SD = 0.06)."
