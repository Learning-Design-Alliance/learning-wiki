## Evidence

### Yu-Shan Cheng 2024

Yu-Shan Cheng, Cheng-Pei Lin, Lu-Yen Anny Chen, Wei-Ren Hwang, Yi-Chun Lin, Yu-Chi Chen. (2024). Short-Term Effects of an eHealth Care Experiential Learning Program Among Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/53509

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of the Patient Health Engagement Scale in the same RCT. The engagement effect appeared only at the immediate posttest; the 3-month follow-up contrast was not significant, so equivalence was not tested. The article notes the scale's 5-point format "making it difficult to observe significant changes."

> "Specifically, the improvement in the intervention group was significantly higher than in the control group (β=.28, SE 0.13; P=.03) at the initial posttest (T1). However, at T2 (3 months after the intervention), there was no significant difference between the 2 groups in the extent of the change (β=.25, SE 0.14; P=.06)."
