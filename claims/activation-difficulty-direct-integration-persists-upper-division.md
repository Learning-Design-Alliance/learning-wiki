## Evidence

### Wilcox 2019

Wilcox, B. R., & Corsiglia, G. (2019). A cross-context look at upper-division student difficulties with integration. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exam-solution coding across two courses at CU found 80% of gravitation students (N=62 of 77) and 70% of electrostatics students (N=113 of 160) correctly activated direct integration. The article reports "This difficulty appears in 20-30% of solutions in both contexts where such simpler/more familiar methods exists."

> "Overall, roughly three-quarters of students (80%, N = 62 of 77 in gravitation; 70%, N = 113 of 160 in electrostatics) correctly activated direct integration via Eqns. 1-2 as the correct approach to the exam questions"
