`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preliminary evaluation in which eleven cardiothoracic surgeons (one professor, two fellows, five senior and three junior residents) performed simple suturing and dissection on four models and rated suitability on a five-point scale. The article states Tango 27 achieved "the highest average score of 3.92"; no effect size printed.

> "The Tango 27 composite ranked the highest in the pooled score of palpation, resistance, extensibility, and gap categories, achieving the highest average score of 3.92 (Supplementary Table 1). Thus, Tango 27 was selected as the final composite hardness."
