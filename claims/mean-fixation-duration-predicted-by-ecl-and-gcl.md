## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In hierarchical regressions, ECL uniquely predicted mean fixation duration in Model 1 (β = -.547), and GCL (β = .099) and O-span score (β = -.143) added unique prediction in Model 2. [→ Tianlong Zu 2017](#tianlong-zu-2017)

## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2017

Tianlong Zu, John Hutson, Lester C. Loschky, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2017). Use of Eye-Tracking Technology to Investigate Cognitive Load Theory. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.113

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression (N = 46) on mean fixation duration recorded with an EyeLink 1000+ during learning, with ICL, ECL, GCL as categorical IVs and O-span added in Model 2. Table 2 reports ECL β = -.547 (p < .001), GCL β = .099 (p = .031), O-span β = -.143 (p = .002), R² = .30 for Model 1.

> "We found that ECL can uniquely predict MFD in the first model . It is worth  noting that GCL also can uniquely predict MFD in addition to ECL and O-span in the second model ."
