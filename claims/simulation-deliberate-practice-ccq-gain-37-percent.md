## Evidence

### Kiernan Llynne C. DNP 2018

Kiernan Llynne C. DNP, MSN, RN-BC. (2018). Evaluating competence and confidence using simulation technology. Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.NURSE.0000545022.36908.f3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest–posttest project with 40 baccalaureate sophomore nursing students (39 posttest) using deliberate practice in a simulation lab; the article reports the posttest "increased by 37.26% compared with the pretest measurement" with no effect size or test statistic printed.

> "The primary outcome was to improve the nursing students' self-report of perceived clinical competence and confidence. That goal was met during the 8-week implementation phase. The posttest measurement increased by 37.26% compared with the pretest measurement."
