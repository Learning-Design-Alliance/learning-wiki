## Evidence

### Neevel Andrew J. MD 2026

Neevel Andrew J. MD, Ramazani Fatemeh MD, Vance Kaitlin NP, Leginza Marie NP, Casper Keith A. MD, Thorne Marc C. MD, MPH, Kupfer Robbi A. MD. (2026). Postsurgical Otolaryngology Emergencies: A Simulation to Improve Multidisciplinary Patient Care During Rare, Critical Situations. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11612

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-test comparison of pre- and postsimulation technical self-efficacy (tracheostomy suctioning, tracheostomy removal, false passage management, alcohol withdrawal management, carotid blowout management) in the same 62-participant curriculum. The overall score rose 0.5 points, and "except for tracheostomy suctioning" all skills improved significantly.

> "increased from 3.8 (95% CI: 3.7-3.9) to 4.3 (95% CI: 4.2-4.3) following the session (P < .001). Confidence with each skill demonstrated statistically significant improvement when independently compared in subanalysis, except for tracheostomy suctioning"
