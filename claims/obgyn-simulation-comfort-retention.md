## Evidence

### Ogunyemi D 2020

Ogunyemi D, Haltigin C, Vallie S, Ferrari TM, Hayashi S. (2020). Evolution of an obstetrics and gynecology interprofessional simulation-based education session for medical and nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000022562

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

2015 MS2 cohort attitude surveys administered pre, post, and 8 months after the simulation; comfort scores were "still significantly increased at 8 months compared to baseline".

> "The comfort level scores with obstetrical procedures compared to baseline significantly increased post-simulation and were still significantly increased at 8 months compared to baseline. The 8-month score was however significantly lower than the immediate post simulation score."
