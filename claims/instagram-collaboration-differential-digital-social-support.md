## Evidence

### Eric Richter 2022

Eric Richter, Jeffrey P. Carpenter, André Meyer, Dirk Richter. (2022). Instagram as a platform for teacher collaboration and digital social support. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104624

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM model (Research Question 3) with the three collaboration forms predicting three support types among 249 teachers. The three forms predicted 31.4% of variance in emotional support, 32.1% in informational support, and 22.3% in instrumental support.

> "Moreover, sharing information was positively related to both emotional support and informational support. This implies that teachers who spend more time sharing information also perceive more emotional and informational support."
