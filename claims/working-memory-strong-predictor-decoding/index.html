Research and the Promise of Personalized Learning 2015¶

Research and the Promise of Personalized Learning. (2015). Digital Promise Global, Making Learning Personal for All series. https://global.digitalpromise.org

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The white paper cites research, without stating a specific study or effect size, that working memory, which allows a person to temporarily hold and manipulate information, is "a strong predictor of decoding skills."

"Working memory is the type of memory that allows a person to temporarily hold and manipulate information for use in simple and complex processes, and research shows that it is a strong predictor of decoding skills."
