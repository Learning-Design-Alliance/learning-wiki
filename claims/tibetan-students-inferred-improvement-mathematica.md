## Subclaims
`q2 i1` The 2019 Tibetan students' TISE quiz scores did not differ significantly from the 2018 Han control group (p=0.723>0.05, effect size 0.1), which the authors interpret as indicating Tibetan students improved after learning QM with Mathematica. [→ Guangtian Zhu 2024](#guangtian-zhu-2024)

## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu 2024

Guangtian Zhu, Jing Hu, Chun Du. (2024). Assisting Tibetan Students in Learning Quantum Mechanics via Mathematica. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of 30 Tibetan 2019 students (mean 0.83) with the 24-student Han 2018 control (mean 0.92) on the six-question TISE quiz. The article reports "no significant difference (p=0.723>0.05)" and "effect size is only 0.1"; no 2018 Tibetan quiz scores exist for direct comparison.

> "the average TISE quiz score of the Tibetan students in 2019 is only slightly lower than their Han peers in 2018 and there is no significant difference (p=0.723>0.05) between these two groups. The effect size is only 0.1"
