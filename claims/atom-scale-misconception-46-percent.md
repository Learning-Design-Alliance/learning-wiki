### M. Tuveri 2025

M. Tuveri, E. Murgia, and V. Fanti (2025). Analogical models to introduce high school students to modern physics: an inquiry-based activity on Rutherford's gold foil experiment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae0386](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae0386)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-activity Mentimeter multiple-choice questions probing prior knowledge of the 15-student cohort. The article reports "only 46% selected the correct order of magnitude" while "all students correctly identified electrons, protons, and neutrons"; the authors attribute the scale error to limited familiarity with scientific notation.

> "In response to “How big is an atom?” , only 46% selected the correct order of magnitude (10⁻¹⁰ m), with others choosing significantly larger values such as 10⁻⁶ m or 10⁻⁴ m"
