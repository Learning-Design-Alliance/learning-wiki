Evidence¶

Ketonen 2026¶

Ketonen, E. E., Kruskopf, M., Lonka, K., & Mattlin, M. (2026). Dynamics of university students' epistemic emotions during game-based learning in an international relations course. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09771-2

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of the 212 session-level emotion ratings; the ICC for curiosity was the largest (0.367), MEAN 5.41 on a 1–7 scale. The article states curiosity may be harder to trigger by instructional means, reflecting a more stable individual approach.

"ICCs, representing the proportion of between-person variance in the total variance, showed that 18–37% of the variance in epistemic emotions was due to individual variability"
