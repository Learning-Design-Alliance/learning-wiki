## Evidence

### Selen Galiç 2026

Selen Galiç, Mathias Tejera, Sebastián Parodi, Markus Hohenwarter, Zsolt Lavicza. (2026). Preservice mathematics teachers' noticing in AI-based simulations: transitions among attending, interpreting, and shaping. ZDM – Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01805-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Descriptive transition-frequency analysis of 304 coded noticing statements from 17 preservice teachers across three weekly AI-simulation tasks. The article reports within-dimension transitions were prominent overall, but "the dominant within-dimension transition varied across weeks" as quoted.

> "In Week 1, transitions within Attend- ing (A→A) occurred most frequently; in Week 2, transitions within Interpreting (I→I) were most frequent; and in Week 3, transitions within Shaping (S→S) were most frequent."
