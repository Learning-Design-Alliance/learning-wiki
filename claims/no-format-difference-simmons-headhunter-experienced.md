## Evidence

### Stolze Franka 2026

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion summary of the Mann-Whitney-U comparisons across catheter types in the 20-participant simulation study; format differences were not significant for the Simmons 2 and Headhunter catheters, while experience level drove performance. Equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant differences were observed between VG and PG with respect to the Simmons 2 and Headhunter catheters. Instead, experience level played a more important role, with experts significantly outperforming novices in speed, accuracy, and success rate across all catheters."

### Stolze Franka 2026 (2)

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experienced-participant subgroup analyses (n = 10) found no significant VG-PG differences (p > 0.050) in objective or subjective outcomes; the authors explicitly state these nulls are inconclusive, not evidence of equivalence.

> "However, there were no significant differences between the VG and the PG when only the results of experienced participants were analyzed (p > 0.050). Given the exploratory sample size and the absence of an a priori power calculation, these null findings are inconclusive and should not be interpreted as evidence of equivalence between the instructional formats."
