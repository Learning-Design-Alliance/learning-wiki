National online survey of CORU-registered dietitians in Ireland, analysed with descriptive and inferential statistics after data cleaning left 114 valid responses. The abstract reports that "only 14% of respondents reported using SBE" against 78% exposure and 85% interest.

> "The study included 114 dietitians, of whom 15 participated in the qualitative component. Although 78% had been exposed to simulation activities and 85% expressed interest in further training, only 14% of respondents reported using SBE."
