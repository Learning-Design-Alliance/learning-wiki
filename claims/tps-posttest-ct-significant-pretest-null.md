# TPS and Non-TPS groups did not differ significantly on pretest CT scores but did differ significantly on posttest overall CT scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No significant pretest difference between groups (t = 0.802, df = 89, p = 0.425; Mann-Whitney Z = .593, p = 0.553), ruling out an initial ability advantage. [→ Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013](#mahmoud-kaddoura-2013)
`q2 i?` Posttest overall CT scores differed significantly between groups (t = 3.437, df = 89, p = 0.001; Mann-Whitney Z = 3.149, p = 0.002). [→ Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013 (2)](#mahmoud-kaddoura-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013

Mahmoud Kaddoura. (2013). Think Pair Share: A teaching Learning Strategy to Enhance Students' Critical Thinking. Educational Research Quarterly, Vol. 36.4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061947

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest comparison of the TPS and Non-TPS sections on the HESI CT test found no significant difference by t test (t=0.802, p = 0.425) or Mann-Whitney test (Z = .593, p = 0.553); equivalence was not formally tested beyond these non-significant results.

> "This was confirmed by both an independent sample t -test (t=0.802, df = 89, p = 0.425) and non -parametric Mann -Whitney test (Z = .593, p = 0.553)."

### Mahmoud Kaddoura 2013 (2)

Mahmoud Kaddoura. (2013). Think Pair Share: A teaching Learning Strategy to Enhance Students' Critical Thinking. Educational Research Quarterly, Vol. 36.4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061947

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest overall CT scores differed significantly between the experimental (TPS) and control (Non-TPS) groups on both the t test (t = 3.437, p = 0.001) and the Mann-Whitney test (Z = 3.149, p = 0.002).

> "On the other hand, differences in the CT posttest scores, as implied by earlier analysis, were significant. This fact was confirmed by both an independent sample t-test (t = 3.437, df = 89, p = 0.001) and non-parametric Mann-Whitney test (Z = 3.149, p = 0.002)."
