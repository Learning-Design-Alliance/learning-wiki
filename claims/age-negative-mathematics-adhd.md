`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM of 504 children with ADHD with age as control variable. The article reports the age effect was significant for mathematics in all four models; no effect size is printed.

> "In all four serial mediation models, the effect of age was significant. Older children showed poorer mathematics performance compared to younger children."
