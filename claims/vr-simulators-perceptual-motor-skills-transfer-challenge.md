The review reports, citing Dunston et al., that VR training platforms with equipment-control mock-ups and 3D-modeled scenarios develop "essential perceptual -motor skills", while true transfer to real-world tasks is hard to assess. No effect size is printed.

> "Their findings i ndicate that while VR simulators effectively develop essential perceptual -motor skills required for operating complex machinery, assessing the true transfer of these skills to real-world tasks remains a significant challenge."
