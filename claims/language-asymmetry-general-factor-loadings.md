## Evidence

### Goodrich J. Marc 2017

Goodrich J. Marc, Lonigan Christopher J. (2017). Language-Independent and Language-Specific Aspects of Early Literacy: An Evaluation of the Common Underlying Proficiency Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bifactor loading patterns for 858 preschoolers; "Spanish items had stronger loadings on the General Phonological Awareness factors than did English items". The authors attribute this to greater L1 exposure, and note English print knowledge showed the opposite pattern, attributed to English being the language of instruction.

> "With the exception of elision items that were multiple choice, Spanish items had stronger loadings on the General Phonological Awareness factors than did English items, indicating that L1 phonological awareness is a better indicator of the common underlying proficiency than is L2 phonological awareness."
