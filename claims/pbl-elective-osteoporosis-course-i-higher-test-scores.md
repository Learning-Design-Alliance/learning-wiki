`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Non-randomized comparison of 25 fourth-year elective participants with 25 fifth-year controls, analyzed with a linear regression model with robust standard errors. The regression showed Study Group I "scored on average 6.7 points higher than Control Group I" on a test scaled from –16 to +21.

> "Study Group I scored on average 6.7 points higher than Control Group I, a difference was significant (p < 0.001, R2 = 0.64, 95% CI: 5.2–8.1)."
