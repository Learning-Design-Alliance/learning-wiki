What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: SpellRead™. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Synthesis of two randomized controlled trials (Torgesen et al., 2006; Rashotte, MacPhee, & Torgesen, 2001) meeting WWC evidence standards, covering 208 students. The WWC computed an improvement index, finding "+18 percentile points across the two studies" for alphabetics, rated positive effects. No standardized effect size is printed for the domain average.

"The average improvement index for alphabetics is +18 percentile points across the two studies, with a range of +2 to +44 percentile points across findings."
