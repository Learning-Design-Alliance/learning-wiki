## Evidence

### Robert A. Mason 2021

Robert A. Mason, Reinhard A. Schumacher and Marcel Adam Just. (2021). The neuroscience of advanced scientific concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00107-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Converging-evidence analysis in which six physics faculty who had not been scanned rated the 45 concepts on 7-point scales along each hypothesized dimension, and these ratings were correlated with fMRI factor scores. The measurable magnitude correlation was "0.74 for all 45 concepts and 0.97 for the 10 concepts at the extremes" (both p < 0.01); other dimensions showed r = 0.45, 0.47, and 0.35 overall with higher extreme-end values.

> "The correlation (Pearson's r) between the concepts' measurable magnitude ratings and their factors scores was 0.74 for all 45 concepts and 0.97 for the 10 concepts at the extremes of the dimension"
