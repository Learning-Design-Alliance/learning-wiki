## Subclaims
`q3 i?` During the tracheal tube insertion phase, expert-novice gaze distance did not differ significantly with a direct laryngoscope (t (12) = 0.541, p = 0.599) or a video laryngoscope (t (12) = 1.468, p = 0.168). [→ Yasuda Yukinori 2026](#yasuda-yukinori-2026)

## Evidence

### Yasuda Yukinori 2026

Yasuda Yukinori, Takehara Shoichiro, Inoue Soichiro. (2026). Quantitative analysis of gaze and body movement differences by proficiency in direct and video laryngoscope intubation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44432-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Eye-tracking analysis of the tracheal tube insertion phase with six experts and eight novices using a direct laryngoscope; experts gazed 0.404 ± 0.180 m versus novices 0.357 ± 0.123 m with no significant difference. The video laryngoscope comparison was likewise non-significant.

> "The result revealed no statistically significant difference between the two groups [t (12) = 0.541, p = 0.599]."
