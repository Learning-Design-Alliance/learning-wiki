## Evidence

### Susan Sporte 2010

Susan Sporte, Marisa de la Torre. (2010). Chicago High School Redesign Initiative: Schools, Students, and Outcomes. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago, Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/chicago-high-school-redesign-initiative-schools-students-and-outcomes

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-level analysis of the 2004-05 cohort (15 CHSRI schools) comparing each school's graduation rate to predicted values, with schools more than about two standard deviations from predicted classified as better or worse than expected. Five schools were significantly higher than predicted; two redesigned schools were significantly lower.

> "the overall positive difference in graduation rates across CHSRI schools is not limited to a few schools; by the third cohort, all but two schools had graduation rates that were at least as high as schools serving similar students"
