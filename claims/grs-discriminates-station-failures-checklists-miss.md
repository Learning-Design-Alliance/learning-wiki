### Ibrahim Olayinka Rasheed 2026

Ibrahim Olayinka Rasheed, McCall Natalie, Bekele Abebe, Zelelew Biniam Ewnte, Ojomo Oluwaseun, Belachew Anteneh Gadisa, Amare Equlinet Misganaw, Gashaw Zelalem Mengistu, Ayana Birhanu Abera, Ndayikeje Ariane Nina. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of station-level pass/fail outcomes for 36 students against a modified-Angoff pass mark of 64.4%. The study reports that "no student failed any station using the 64.4% pass mark" on checklists, whereas GRS flagged failures in four stations.

> "Based on students’ performance using checklists, no student failed any station using the 64.4% pass mark set from standard settings. Based on students’ performance using GRS, stations 5 and 6 had one student fail each, while Stations 7 and 13 had two students fail each of them."
