## Evidence

### Nishiyama Masahiro 2023

Nishiyama Masahiro, Kyono Yuki, Yamaguchi Hiroshi, Kawamura Aoi, Oikawa Shizuka, Tokumoto Shoichi, Tomioka Kazumi, Nozu Kandai, Nagase Hiroaki. (2023). Association of early bedtime at 3 years of age with higher academic performance and better non-cognitive skills in elementary school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-48280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same cohort, national language and math scores were compared across sleep-duration groups (≥11 h, n = 850; 10 h, n = 1913; 9 h, n = 1391; 6–8 h, n = 221). Means were near-identical (e.g., national language 68.8 ± 19.6 for ≥11 h), and the ANOVA was "no significant differences in either national language or math scores".

> "there were no significant differences in either national language or math scores according to sleep duration (one-way ANOVA, national language, P = 0.961; math, P = 0.735)."
