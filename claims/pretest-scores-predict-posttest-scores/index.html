Secondary finding from the multiple regression analyses for both regular and irregular past tense: pretest scores on the GJT or EIT were included as covariates and were generally significant predictors, which the article notes is "in line with previous studies."

"In general, pretest scores were significantly related to posttest scores irrespective of the CF type or L2 outcome measure, in line with previous studies"
