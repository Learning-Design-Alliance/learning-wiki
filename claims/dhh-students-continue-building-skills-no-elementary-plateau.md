## Evidence

### Stephanie Cawthon 2022

Stephanie Cawthon, Johny Daniel, North Cooc, Ana Vielma. (2022). Longitudinal models of reading and mathematics achievement in deaf and hard of hearing students. The Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education. https://doi.org/10.1093/deafed/enac033

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Longitudinal analysis of NWEA MAP Growth data from grades 2 to 8 across seven reading and mathematics domains over 5 years, comparing DHH and hearing students' achievement trajectories. The study reports that "both DHH and hearing students continue to build skills through this period" and that DHH students "do not necessarily plateau in the elementary grades."

> "Results indicate that both DHH and hearing students continue to build skills through this period, and that DHH students, contrary to many previously held assumptions, do not necessarily plateau in the elementary grades."
