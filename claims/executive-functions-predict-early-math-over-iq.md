A correlational study reviewed by the article showed that "measures of working memory and the flexible shifting of attention were shown to independently predict early mathematics achievement" controlling for reading ability and IQ. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "An early demonstration of this relation was seen in a correlational study in which measures of working memory and the flexible shifting of attention were shown to independently predict early mathematics achievement over and above measures of reading ability and IQ (Bull & Scerif 2001)."
