## Evidence

### Shi X 2025

Shi X, Wang H, Yang F, Zhou J, Wei W. (2025). Application and evaluation of case-based multimodal imaging in the ocular fundus disease teaching of postgraduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06967-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 34 postgraduate residents assigned by training period: traditional group (n=16) vs CBL multimodal group (n=18). Table 2 reports multimodal imaging analysis means of "87.19 ± 4.53" vs "90.50 ± 3.61", t = 2.37, P = 0.02; no effect size printed.
