`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study of Sabrina (ACT 25, weighted GPA 3.77), who worked nearly full time, put off other applications while awaiting a scholarship decision, was accepted with a full scholarship without applying, then the school rescinded it after graduation, ostensibly due to a D in a journalism elective.

> "Sabrina had applied to no other colleges and never seriously pursued other financial aid. She was out of options."
