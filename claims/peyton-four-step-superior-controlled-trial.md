### Bugaj T. J. 2016

Bugaj T. J., Nikendei C. (2016). Practical Clinical Training in Skills Labs: Theory and Practice. GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(4) Doc63. http://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2016-33/zma001062.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing controlled trials by Krautter and colleagues, that Peyton's Four-Step Approach (demonstration, deconstruction, comprehension, performance) outperformed simple instructions in technical skills training. No effect size is printed in the review.

> "Compared to the provision of simple instructions, Peyton's approach has been shown to be more conducive, especially, in regard to observed professionalism and concomitant doctor-patient communication and also leads to the more rapid implementation of new activities [29], [30]."
