## Evidence

### Ellington Laura E 2020

Ellington Laura E, Becerra Rosario, Mallma Gabriela, Tantaleán da Fieno José, Nair Deepthi, Onchiri Frankline, Nielsen Katie R. (2020). Knowledge acquisition and retention after a high flow training programme in Peru: a quasi-experimental single group pre–post design. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-035125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon rank-sum comparisons excluding paediatric residents showed no significant difference between post-baseline and pre-refresher scores for indications/contraindications (short-term p=0.180, long-term p=0.097) and for potential adverse events long-term (p=0.427), while protocol-specific details and adverse events declined significantly short-term (p<0.001, p=0.001).

> "In the short- and long-terms, participants retained minimum competency only in the high flow indications/contraindications subtopic. In the long-term, participants also retained competency in adverse events"
