Group-division results in Table 1 (2013/2014): test group 42 students, experimental group 63 students. In the experimental group 28 students (45%) "don't have academic debt" versus 14 (33%) in the test group; 14 (22%) versus 15 (36%) constantly had debt.

"Consequently the experimental group showed the higher percent of students not having academic debt in comparison with a test group; less percent of students having academic debt after the goal -oriented program implementation has been proved as well."
