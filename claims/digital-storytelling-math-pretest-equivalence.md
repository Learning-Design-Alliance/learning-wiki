### İlknur Özpinar 2017

İlknur Özpinar, Semirhan Gö kç e, Arzu Aydoğan Yenmez. (2017). Effects of Digital Storytelling in Mathematics Instruction on Academic Achievement and Examination of Teacher-Student Opinions on the Process. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 10. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i10.2595

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest analysis of the 14-question Achievement Test in the quasi-experimental study found no significant group difference; control mean was 8.68 and experimental mean 8.47, indicating equivalent starting achievement.

> "No significant difference was found between the groups by the achievement pretest scores [t (56)=.327, p>.05]. According to Table 3, the mean of the control group students obtained in the achievement pretest is X =8.68 and the mean of the experimental group students obtained in the same test is X =8.47."
