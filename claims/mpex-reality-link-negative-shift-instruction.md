### Abhilash Nair and Vashti Sawtelle 2019

Abhilash Nair and Vashti Sawtelle. (2019). Operationalizing relevance in physics education: using a systems view to expand our conception of making physics relevant. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020121)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The article reports, citing Redish and colleagues' multi-institution MPEX study of 1,528 students across six institutions, that students "entered with strong favorable responses in the Reality Link cluster" and that "every group showed a deterioration on this measure as a result of instruction." No effect size is printed.

> "In Redish and colleagues' large study using the MPEX, spanning six institutions (N=1,528 students), they report that students entered with strong favorable responses in the Reality Link cluster. By the end, however, "every group showed a deterioration on this measure as a result of instruction, and some of the shifts were substantial"
