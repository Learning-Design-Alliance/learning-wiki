# Stress responses during simulation (salivary cortisol, STAI, cognitive appraisal) do not differ between in situ and off-site settings, though simulation itself raised cortisol and STAI in both

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Baseline-to-peak changes in cortisol, STAI and CA were similar for ISS versus OSS in both simulation scenarios. [→ Sørensen JL 2015](#srensen-jl-2015)
`q3 i?` Post hoc, cortisol and STAI rose significantly and CA fell significantly from baseline to peak in both groups (all p<0.0001). [→ Sørensen JL 2015 (2)](#srensen-jl-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Sørensen JL 2015

Sørensen JL, van der Vleuten C, Rosthøj S, Østergaard D, LeBlanc V, Johansen M, Ekelund K, Starkopf L, Lindschou J, Gluud C, Weikop P, Ottesen B. (2015). Simulation-based multiprofessional obstetric anaesthesia training conducted in situ versus off-site leads to similar individual and team outcomes: a randomised educational trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008344

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trial measuring objective stress via salivary cortisol and subjective stress via STAI and cognitive appraisal during both scenarios; group contrasts showed p values of 0.07 to 0.93, with the largest difference (cortisol, second simulation) at p=0.07.

> "The mean change in baseline to peak was similar for ISS versus OSS for both the first (caesarean section) and the second (postpartum haemorrhage) simulation (table 5)."

### Sørensen JL 2015 (2)

Sørensen JL, van der Vleuten C, Rosthøj S, Østergaard D, LeBlanc V, Johansen M, Ekelund K, Starkopf L, Lindschou J, Gluud C, Weikop P, Ottesen B. (2015). Simulation-based multiprofessional obstetric anaesthesia training conducted in situ versus off-site leads to similar individual and team outcomes: a randomised educational trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008344

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc analysis showing simulation-based medical education triggered objective and subjective stress in both settings; CA "decreased significantly from baseline to peak" (p<0.0001) where an increase was expected.

> "The salivary cortisol and STAI levels increased significantly from baseline to peak in the ISS and OSS groups following the first (caesarean section) and the second (postpartum haemorrhage) simulation (overall tests for no difference between pre and post: all p<0.0001)."
