### Sterpu Irene 2024

Sterpu Irene, Herling Lotta, Nordquist Jonas, Rotgans Jerome, Acharya Ganesh. (2024). Team-based learning (TBL) in clinical disciplines for undergraduate medical students—a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04975-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Alimoglu et al.'s observational study using the STROBE tool, in which mean in-class engagement scores were significantly higher for TBL groups; overall, three studies reported higher engagement versus traditional teaching and two reported high engagement without a comparator. No effect size is printed.

> "The mean in-class engagement scores were significantly higher for TBL groups, both for learners and instructors."
