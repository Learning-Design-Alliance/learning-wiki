## Evidence

### Zhang Y 2026

Zhang Y, Sun J, Marshall K, Mitchell JP, Laprade J, Nederveen JP, Helli PB, Rebalka IA. (2026). A preliminary comparison of bellringer performance across three visual modalities for the assessment of anatomy knowledge. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70145

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mental Rotations Test administered after the mock bellringer exam and correlated with exam performance. The article characterizes the MRT–BR and MRT–VR relationships as "Significant, though negligible (p < 0.05, r 2 < 0.1), positive linear relationships" and finds no MRT–SSQ relationship.

> "Significant, though negligible (p < 0.05, r 2 < 0.1), positive linear relationships were observed between MRT and BR scores (Figure 3A; R 2 = 0.0661, t = 3.03, p = 0.002) as well as between MRT and VR BR scores (Figure 3B; R 2 = 0.0394, t = 2.38, p = 0.019). There was no relationship between MRT and SSQ scores"
