### Ibrahim Olayinka Rasheed 2026

Ibrahim Olayinka Rasheed, McCall Natalie, Bekele Abebe, Zelelew Biniam Ewnte, Ojomo Oluwaseun, Belachew Anteneh Gadisa, Amare Equlinet Misganaw, Gashaw Zelalem Mengistu, Ayana Birhanu Abera, Ndayikeje Ariane Nina. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional analysis of scores from 36 students in a 15-station exit OSCE at the University of Global Health Equity, Rwanda, comparing paired checklist and GRS percentages with paired t-tests. The study reports "which was lower than the mean score using GRS" with p < 0.001.

> "The mean score using the checklist was 84.3 (5.3) %, which was lower than the mean score using GRS, 94.3 (6.9) %, p < 0.001."
