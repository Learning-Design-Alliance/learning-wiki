### Zheng Koulong 2024

Zheng Koulong, Shen Zhiyu, Chen Zanhao, Che Chang, Zhu Huixia. (2024). Application of AI-empowered scenario-based simulation teaching mode in cardiovascular disease education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05977-z

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews with three boys and three girls sampled from six groups across three performance levels, each about 20 min, transcribed within 24 h. Students reported that "many theoretical knowledge are easier to understand" and teachers observed improved analysis during Mini-CEX.

> "In summarizing the interview findings, three primary themes emerge for analysis: (1) A new learning (teaching) experience; (2) Enhancement of clinical critical thinking ability; (3) Suggestions for improvement."
