## Evidence

### Vaz DV 2021

Vaz DV, Ferreira EMR, Palma GB, Atun-Einy O, Kafri M, Ferreira FR. (2021). Testing a new active learning approach to advance motor learning knowledge and self-efficacy in physical therapy undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education 21:62. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02486-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis relating post-course PTP-ML self-efficacy scores to grades on the 42-question final exam for the intervention students, yielding printed effect size rho = 0.49 (p = 0.01) for part A and rho = 0.44 for part B. The article notes the values agree closely with a meta-analytic overall correlation of 0.49 between self-efficacy and grade point average.

> "After the course, the final self-efficacy scores for parts A and B were significantly correlated with final exam grades (rho = 0.49, p = 0.01 and rho = 0.44, p = 0.02, respectively; Fig. 3)."
