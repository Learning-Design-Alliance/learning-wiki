Implementing team-based sequential simulation for medical students in clinical clerkship: preliminary findings and learning efficiency evaluation. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses from 97 students (response rate 89.8%), with codes grouped into themes by investigator consensus. The most common theme was that "mCLEIMS reflected real-world healthcare settings (51.5%, 50/97)".

> "The most common theme was that mCLEIMS reflected real-world healthcare settings (51.5%, 50/97), followed by the role of simulation patients (32.0%, 31/97), interprofessional collaboration (20.6%, 20/97), readiness for future clinical practice (15.5%, 15/97), immediate bidirectional feedback (12.4%, 12/97)"
