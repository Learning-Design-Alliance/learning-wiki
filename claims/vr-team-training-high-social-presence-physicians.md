## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a small pilot test with two surgeons and one medical student, cooperative social presence questionnaire scores were high across team identification (M 4.13), social action (M 4.08), motivation (M 4.06), and team value (M 4.44) on a five-point scale. ## Evidence

### Chheang Vuthea 2020

Chheang Vuthea, Fischer Virve, Buggenhagen Holger, Huber Tobias, Huettl Florentine, Kneist Werner, Preim Bernhard, Saalfeld Patrick, Hansen Christian. (2020). Toward interprofessional team training for surgeons and anesthesiologists using virtual reality. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-020-02276-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot questionnaire administered during development testing with two surgeons and one medical student, using a cooperative social presence questionnaire with five-point Likert items. The article reports "social action (M 4.08, SD 0.83)" and high scores in all four categories; no effect size or inferential test is printed.

> "Second, social action (M 4.08, SD 0.83) involves communication and contribution between interprofessional team members. All participants agreed that they had a mutual understanding."
