`q2 i?` An uncontrolled before-and-after study found significant improvement in all teamwork domain ratings (leadership P = 0.003; situation monitoring P = 0.009; mutual support P = 0.004; communication P = 0.001; overall P < 0.001) and significant decreased time to CT scanner (26.4-22.1 min, P = 0.005), endotracheal intubation (10.1-6.6 min, P = 0.049), and operating room (130.1-94.5 min, P = 0.021). `q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Uncontrolled before-and-after study of 114 trauma team members (28 surgery residents, 6 faculty surgeons, 80 ED nurses) comparing clinical outcome and efficiency of care pre and post team training. Found "significant decreased time... for: arrival to CT scanner (26.4-22.1 min, P = 0.005), endotracheal intubation (10.1-6.6 min, P = 0.049), and operating room (130.1-94.5 min, P = 0.021)" plus improved teamwork ratings.

> "A significant decreased time was observed after the training for: arrival to CT scanner (26.4-22.1 min, P = 0.005), endotracheal intubation (10.1-6.6 min, P = 0.049), and operating room (130.1-94.5 min, P = 0.021)"
