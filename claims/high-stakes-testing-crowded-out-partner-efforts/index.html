Karin Sconzert 2004¶

Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/working-school-improvement-reflections-chicago-annenberg-external-partners

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Context analysis of Chicago school reform policy within the report's section on partners' working conditions. The article qualifies the pattern as occurring "in some schools" rather than systemwide.

"in some schools, high-stakes testing pushed teachers and principals to focus on the quickest means of administrative compliance—test preparation—and abandon, or push aside at least for a while, partners’ efforts to achieve more ambitious, long-term instructional improvement."
