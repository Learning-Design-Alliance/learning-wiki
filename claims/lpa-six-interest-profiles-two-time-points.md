## Evidence

### Akkerman 2020

Akkerman, D. M., Vulperhorst, J. P., & Akkerman, S. F. (2020). A developmental extension to the multidimensional structure of interests. Journal of Educational Psychology, 112(1), 183-203. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000361

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional latent profile analysis of 7,226 interest reports from 204 Dutch high school students, collected via smartphone experience sampling at two time points. The article reports that "the data best supported 2 nearly identical sets of 6 profiles at both time points"; no effect size is printed.

> "Using cross-sectional latent profile analyses, we found that the data best supported 2 nearly identical sets of 6 profiles at both time points."
