### Dave Neale & Deborah Pino-Pasternak 2016

Dave Neale & Deborah Pino-Pasternak. (2016). A Review of Reminiscing in Early Childhood Settings and Links to Sustained Shared Thinking. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-016-9376-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this to Siraj-Blatchford and Manni (2008), an observational analysis of 400 hours of staff–child dialog in preschools. The review adds that other studies (Gjems 2010; McInnes et al. 2013) also found open questions important.

> "Siraj-Blatchford and Manni ( 2008) analyzed 400 h of naturally occurring dialog between staff and children in preschool settings and found that open-ended questions —questions with no single right answer —encouraged children to think and respond in an individual way"
