## Evidence

### Jill S. Cannon 2006

Jill S. Cannon, Alison Jacknowitz, Gary Painter. (2006). Is Full Better than Half? Examining the Longitudinal Effects of Full-day Kindergarten Attendance. Journal of Policy Analysis and Management, Vol. 25, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.1002/pam.20174

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Probit models on teacher-reported ECLS-K behavior scales (external problems n = 6,108; internal n = 5,949), reporting marginal effects. The article reports a 2.3 percentage-point increase in frequent external behavior problems; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "attending a full-day program does not affect the likelihood of exhibiting frequent internal behavior problems and significantly predicts more frequent external behavioral problems (2.3 percentage points)."
