Evidence¶

Rebetez 2010¶

Rebetez, C., Betrancourt, M., Sangin, M., & Dillenbourg, P. (2010). Learning from animation enabled by collaboration. Instructional Science, 38(5), 471–485. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-009-9117-6

q2 · i? · causal · r?

This is the article's introductory characterisation of the prior animation literature, not a new empirical result of the present experiment; the review is narrative and no effect sizes are reported for this assertion. In CSCL settings too, "the empirical studies have not confirmed the benefits that one could intuitively expect from the use of animation".

"As animation directly depicts spatial changes over time, it is legitimate to believe that animated graphics will improve comprehension over static graphics. However, the research failed to find clear evidence in favour of animation."
