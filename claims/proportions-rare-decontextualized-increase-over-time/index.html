Evidence¶

Rowe Meredith L 2012¶

Rowe Meredith L. (2012). A longitudinal investigation of the role of quantity and quality of child-directed speech in vocabulary development. Child development. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3440540/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Linear contrast analysis of input proportions across 50 dyads. "a significant linear increase in the proportion of word types that were rare (t(49) = 5.36, p < 0.001)" and decontextualized utterances (t(49) = 5.63, p < 0.001).

"Linear contrasts (Furr, 2008; Rosenthal, Rosnow & Rubin, 2000) revealed a significant linear increase in the proportion of word types that were rare (t(49) = 5.36, p < 0.001) and the proportion of utterances that were decontextualized (t(49) = 5.63, p < 0.001) from 18 to 42 months."
