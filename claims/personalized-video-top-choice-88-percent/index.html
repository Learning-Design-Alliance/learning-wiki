Tomlinson 2026¶

Tomlinson, B., Black, R.W., Patterson, D.J., van der Hoek, A., Ferguson, J. & Bietz, M.J. (2026). Students prefer personalized, AI-generated educational videos over non-personalized, human-recorded videos. Scientific Reports 16:21804. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-52798-9

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of first-choice frequencies from the end-of-term ranking survey across the Spring 2025 (255) and Fall 2025 (238) cohorts. The article reports "88.4% of students ranked either human-recorded or AI-generated personalized videos as their top choice", with the same ordering within both human-recorded and AI-generated categories.

"88.4% of students ranked either human-recorded or AI-generated personalized videos as their top choice. This proportion was highly above chance (two-sided binomial test against 0.5, p< 0.001) and was consistent across both cohorts."
