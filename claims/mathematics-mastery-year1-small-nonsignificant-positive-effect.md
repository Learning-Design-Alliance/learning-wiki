## Evidence

### Vignoles et al. 2015

Anna Vignoles, John Jerrim, Richard Cowan. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Primary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. http://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/library/maths-mastery-primary

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clustered RCT with school-level randomisation across two cohorts; OLS regression of standardised Number Knowledge post-test on treatment with pre-test covariate, standard errors clustered at school level. The report estimates the effect as equivalent to approximately two months' additional progress, but it is not statistically significant.

> "Children who received the Mathematics Mastery intervention scored, on average, +0.10 standard deviations higher on the post-test. This, however, only reached statistical significance at the 10% level (t = 1.82; p = 0.07), with the 95% confidence interval ranging from -0.01 to +0.21."
