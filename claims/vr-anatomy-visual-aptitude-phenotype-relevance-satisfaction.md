## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alkhammash L 2025

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tukey HSD post-hoc comparisons following a MANOVA on visual aptitude phenotypes (Rotator, Visualizer, Balanced) in a study of 91 high-school learners. The article reports "Rotators reported a statistically lower degree of Satisfaction compared to the Visualizer phenotype" and a Relevance difference between Visualizers and Rotators; no effect sizes printed.

> "Rotators reported a statistically lower degree of Satisfaction compared to the Visualizer phenotype (p < 0.05). We also found a statistical difference in Relevance reported by the Visualizer phenotype compared to the Rotator phenotype (p < 0.05)"

### Alkhammash L 2025 (2)

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Tukey HSD post-hoc analysis of IPQ Presence scores by phenotype in the same cohort. The article reports "participants in the Visualizers phenotype were statistically more likely to report feeling present in the VR environment than the Balanced phenotype"; no effect size printed.

> "participants in the Visualizers phenotype were statistically more likely to report feeling present in the VR environment than the Balanced phenotype (p < 0.05)"
