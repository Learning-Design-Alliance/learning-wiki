# Serial order STM at Age 4 predicts vocabulary knowledge at Age 5 over and above initial vocabulary, item STM, age and nonverbal reasoning

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Serial order reconstruction performance at Age 4 accounted for significant variance in vocabulary at Age 5 after controlling for age, nonverbal reasoning and initial vocabulary (12% of variance), and a further 7% of independent variance once variance shared with nonword repetition was removed. ## Evidence

### Leclercq 2010

Leclercq, A.-L., & Majerus, S. (2010). Serial Order Short-Term Memory Predicts Vocabulary Development: Evidence from a Longitudinal Study. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018540

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression in the longitudinal study of 60 children tested at Age 4 and Age 5, predicting Age 5 vocabulary from Age 4 STM measures. The article reports "nonword repetition accounted for 8 % of variance and serial order reconstruction accounted for 12 % of variance".

> "We observed that performances on both the serial order reconstruction task and on the nonword delayed repetition task at Age 4 accounted for significant parts of variance in vocabulary scores after controlling for age, nonverbal reasoning and initial vocabulary knowledge (nonword repetition accounted for 8 % of variance and serial order reconstruction accounted for 12 % of variance)."

### Leclercq 2010 (2)

Leclercq, A.-L., & Majerus, S. (2010). Serial Order Short-Term Memory Predicts Vocabulary Development: Evidence from a Longitudinal Study. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018540

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same longitudinal regression set: when entered after the nonword task, the serial order task explained "a further 7 % of independent variance in vocabulary scores", whereas the nonword task added none when entered after serial order STM.

> "Furthermore, scores at the serial order reconstruction task accounted for a further 7 % of independent variance in vocabulary scores once the variance shared with nonword repetition abilities was removed, while the reverse was not true (Table 6)."
