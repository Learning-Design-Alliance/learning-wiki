Evidence¶

Vembye 2022¶

Vembye, M. H., Weiss, F., & Bhat, B. H. (2022). The Effects of Co-Teaching and Related Collaborative Models of Instruction on Student Achievement: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. https://osf.io/ur2bs

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analyses testing factors from the co-teaching literature, including provision of common planning time and training in co-teaching methods, against studies without them. The review reports these factors "did not explain variations in effect sizes"; no subgroup effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.

"factors that are highlighted in the co-teaching literature as preconditions for the effectiveness of collaborative instruction did not explain variations in effect sizes"
