Curry 2012¶

Descriptive results from the quasi-experimental pre/posttest comparison reported in Table 2: experimental group pretest M=56.25, posttest M=86.25; comparison group pretest M=59.64, posttest M=86.36. These are descriptive means; no effect size was printed for the gains themselves.
