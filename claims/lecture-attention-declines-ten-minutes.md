## Subclaims

### Tolks D 2016

Tolks D, Schäfer C, Raupach T, Kruse L, Sarikas A, Gerhardt-Szép S, Kllauer G, Lemos M, Fischer MR, Eichner B, Sostmann K, Hege I. (2016). An Introduction to the Inverted/Flipped Classroom Model in Education and Advanced Training in Medicine and in the Healthcare Professions. GMS Journal for Medical Education. Narrative review, citing Hartley and Cameron, on deficits of traditional higher education lectures. The review states attention "decreases after only ten minutes" and is almost fully lost after 15 to 20 minutes, with approximately 20% of content remembered; no effect size is printed.

> "It is, for example, well known that students’ attention decreases after only ten minutes and after an average of 15 to 20 minutes is almost fully lost. Directly following a lecture, students only remember approximately 20% of the transmitted content"
