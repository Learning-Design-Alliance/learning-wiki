# Self-explanation quality correlated with prior knowledge and post-test knowledge but not with learning gains

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Self-explanation quality correlated significantly with prior knowledge (r = .24) and post-test knowledge (r = .37), but not with gain scores (r = .16, p = .188). [→ Bichler 2022](#bichler-2022)

## Evidence

### Bichler 2022

Bichler, S., Stadler, M., Bühner, M., Greiff, S., & Fischer, F. (2022). Learning to solve ill-defined statistics problems: does self-explanation quality mediate the worked example effect? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09579-4

`q3 · i3`

Correlational analysis of the new self-explanation data (N = 74). Self-explanation quality and concept application strongly correlated (r = .72, p < .001); the article continues with self-explanation quality as the mediator of interest. None of the correlations with covariates and moderators except prior knowledge were significant.

> "Self-explanation quality correlated significantly with prior knowledge r = .24, p = .042 and post-test knowledge r = .37, p = .001, but not with gain scores r = .16, p = .188."
