## Evidence

### Wolfram 2023

Wolfram, T. & Morris, D. (2023). Conventional twin studies overestimate the environmental differences between families relevant to educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 8, 24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00173-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary re-analysis of a subsample of the Branigan et al. (2013) meta-analysis, applying Falconer's formulas and Martin's assortative-mating correction, then replicating the fixed-effects meta-analysis. The authors report "mean A estimates were 17 points higher and mean C estimates were 16 points lower".

> "After adjusting for assortative mating, mean A estimates were 17 points higher and mean C estimates were 16 points lower."
