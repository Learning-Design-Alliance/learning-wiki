## Evidence

### Jessica Van Brummelen and Phoebe Lin 2021

Jessica Van Brummelen and Phoebe Lin. (2021). Engaging Teachers to Co-Design Integrated AI Curriculum for K-12 Classrooms. arXiv:2009.11100v1. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1145/3411764.3445377](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445377)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding from co-design workshop transcripts. P8 and P10 grounded the Social Studies curriculum in law and government discourse via an article around the Crown Act; other anchors included "hard questions" about real-world applications of AI, student-driven learning, and gamification. No effect size is printed.

> "Introducing context to the project gives students an “anchor” (P8) or hook to prompt further inquiry."
