## Evidence

### Ozcelik 2013

Ozcelik, E., Ercil Cagiltay, N., & Sahin Ozcelik, N. (2013). The effect of uncertainty on learning in game-like environments. Computers & Education, 67, 12–20. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2013.02.009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized experiment, independent samples t-tests found "the group effect was not signiﬁcant on total reading time of explanations" and response time, and "no signiﬁcant difference in motivation between the groups"; accuracy likewise showed no difference (t(138) = 1.33, p = 0.19).

> "It was found that the group effect was not signiﬁcant on total reading time of explanations, t(138) ¼ 0.40, p ¼ 0.69 and the total response time to questions, t(138) ¼ 1.35, p ¼ 0.18. In addition, there was no signiﬁcant difference in motivation between the groups, t(138) ¼/C0 0.60, p ¼ 0.55."
