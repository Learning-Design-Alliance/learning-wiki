### Chen Yao 2026

Chen Yao, Peng Wentao, Dai Yanmei, Lin Xin, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing patient interview and clinical examination training: the impact of the Simroid dental training robot. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09520-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized controlled study of 63 fifth-year dentistry interns (29 control, 34 experimental). At the first Mini-CEX assessment, the experimental group scored "7.49 points higher than the control group" with "a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.73)".

> "The experimental group (76.94 ± 4.84) scored 7.49 points higher than the control group (69.45 ± 3.61), with a 95% CI of 5.36–9.63 and a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.73)"
