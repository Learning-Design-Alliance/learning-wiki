## Evidence

### Zhong Jinjie 2022

Zhong Jinjie, Li Zhongjie, Hu Xinyang, Wang Linlin, Chen Yingying. (2022). Effectiveness comparison between blended learning of histology practical in flipped physical classrooms and flipped virtual classrooms for MBBS students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03740-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison of final exam scores across the three cohorts (Table 1: FPCR-BL median 85.00 (73.00–95.00) vs FVCR-BL 69.00 and PCR-TL 71.00; H = 69.68, p = 0.00). The authors interpret this as showing the "face-to-face element of the physical classroom" aided deeper understanding.

> "In the final exam with short answer questions, the scores in FPCR-BL were higher than those in the other two groups, and there was no significant difference in the final exam between the PCR-TL and FVCR-BL groups."
