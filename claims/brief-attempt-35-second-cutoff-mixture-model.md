## Evidence

### Chen 2020

Chen, Zhongzhou; Xu, Mengyu; Garrido, Geoffrey; Guthrie, Matthew W. (2020). Exploring the relation between students' online learning behavior and course performance by including contextual information in data analysis. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010138](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010138)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mixture-model fitting (package mixsmsn) of first AC attempt durations aggregated across all 10 modules. The article reports "the cutoff between Brief and Normal attempts is initially set at 35 seconds for all modules", with adjustments to 17 and 24 seconds on m2 and m6 after study.

> "Based on the results from model fitting, the cutoff between Brief and Normal attempts is initially set at 35 seconds for all modules."
