# Mandatory summer curricula are not teacher proof: individualizing instruction and quality of teacher-student interactions were associated with larger gains

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Teacher surveys and classroom observations across the evaluation indicated that mandatory curricula alone did not guarantee effectiveness; individualization and out-of-class work with students were associated with greater learning gains.

> "Students whose teachers spent more time individual izing the curriculum and working with stu dents outside of class had greater learning gains than students in classrooms where teachers spent less time adapting the cur riculum and providing individualized at tention ."

### Melissa Roderick 2003 (2)

Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/ending-social-promotion-results-summer-bridge

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In-depth classroom observations in 12 schools showed a relatively small proportion of teachers taught in ways that engaged students, provided substantive feedback, and addressed individual learning needs; their students had larger learning gains.

> "Our look at in struction showed that the quality of interactions between teachers and students was a distinguishing factor between the most effective Summer Bridge classrooms and the average classroom."
