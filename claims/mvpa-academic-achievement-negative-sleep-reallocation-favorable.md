# Higher MVPA relative to remaining movement behaviors at age 5.5 years is associated with lower academic achievement, and reallocating MVPA time to sleep predicts higher academic achievement

## Subclaims

[doi:10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Compositional regression in the GUSTO cohort (n = 251 for academic achievement at age 5.5-year exposure; Model 2 adjusted) printed a mean difference of −0.367 (−0.726, −0.009) z-score; the article reports this as a statistically significant negative association. No standardized effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "a higher amount of MVPA time relative to the remaining movement behaviors at age 5.5 years was associated with a lower mean academic achievement at age 9 years [Mean difference (95% confidence interval): −0.367 (−0.726, −0.009) z-score]"

### Padmapriya 2024 (2)

Padmapriya, Natarajan; Bernard, Jonathan Y.; Tan, Sarah Yi Xuan; Chu, Anne H. Y.; Goh, Claire Marie Jie Lin; Tan, Shuen Lin; Shek, Lynette P.; Chong, Yap Seng; Tan, Kok Hian; Chan, Shiao-Yng; Yap, Fabian; Godfrey, Keith M.; Lee, Yung Seng; Meaney, Michael J.; Eriksson, Johan G.; Tan, Chuen Seng; Law, Evelyn C.; Müller-Riemenschneider, Falk. (2024). The prospective associations of 24-hour movement behaviors and domain-specific activities with executive function and academic achievement among school-aged children in Singapore. *Frontiers in Public Health*. [doi:10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Compositional isotemporal substitution applied to the fitted adjusted models showed reallocation of 5–60 min/day from MVPA to sleep at age 5.5 years was associated with a significant increase; the printed 30-min estimate was 0.214 (0.023, 0.404) z-score. Similar reallocations at age 8 years were not significant. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "reallocating 30 min from MVPA to sleep at age 5.5 resulted in a 0.214 (0.023, 0.404) increase in academic achievement z-score"
