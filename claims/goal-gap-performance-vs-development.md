# A goal gap misaligns the measurable-performance aims promoted by current technologies with education's broader developmental aims

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The goal gap is the misalignment between the aims implicitly promoted by current technologies — measurable outcomes, assessment efficiency, standardised achievement — and the broader developmental goals education is meant to serve, such as socialisation and individual agency. ### Laura Desiree Di Paolo 2026

Laura Desiree Di Paolo, Andy Clark & Thomas Wachter. (2026). Educating minds with generative AI. Communications Psychology 4:128. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44271-026-00522-8

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the Goal Gap section: the gap predates AI; technologies introduced without interrogating educational aims reproduce and amplify inherited logics of efficiency, standardisation and content coverage. The authors argue GenAI narrows educational discourse toward measurable outputs, obscuring the distinction between learning as a developmental process and performance as a measurable output, marginalising socialisation, individual agency, metacognitive engagement, epistemic responsibility and tolerance of uncertainty.
