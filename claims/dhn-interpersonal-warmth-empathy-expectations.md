## Evidence

### Freisberg L 2026

Freisberg L, Meier-Diedrich E, Heinze M, Schönbeck J, Schubert D, Speck J, Strudwick G, Torous J, Schwarz J. (2026). Expected Competencies and Personal Attributes of Digital Health Navigators to Support Digital Mental Health Care: Focus Group and Interview Study With Patients and Health Care Professionals. JMIR Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.2196/83073

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 17 semistructured interviews and 4 focus groups with 35 patients and HCPs in Brandenburg, Germany. In the soft skills domain, "11/35, 31.43%" of participants emphasized warmth and empathy beyond technical support; patience, openness, and composure were also described as essential.

> "Many participants (11/35, 31.43%) emphasized that the DHN-patient relationship should not be limited to technical support but should be marked by interpersonal warmth and empathy."
