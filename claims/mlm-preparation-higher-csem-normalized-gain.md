## Evidence

### Moore 2018

James Christopher Moore. (2018). Efficacy of Multimedia Learning Modules as Preparation for Lecture-Based Tutorials in Electromagnetism. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 23. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8010023

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with non-equivalent groups (non-MLM n = 44, MLM n = 33) in a calculus-based physics course for natural science majors. The MLM group's average normalized gain was (35 ± 3)% versus (18 ± 2)% for the non-MLM group, a "significantly greater CSEM average learning gain" at p < 1%.
