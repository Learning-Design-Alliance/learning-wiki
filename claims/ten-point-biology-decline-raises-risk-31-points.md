`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Marginal-effect estimation derived from the fitted Logistic Regression model in the same 346-student predictive study. The article presents these as percentage-point changes in predicted risk, not standardized effect sizes.

> "effect size estimation from the Logistic Regression model revealed that a 10-point decrease in Biology midterm scores increased the probability of being at risk by ~ 31% points on average. Comparable decreases in Introduction to Health Sciences and English raised risk by ~ 25 and ~ 18% points, respectively."
