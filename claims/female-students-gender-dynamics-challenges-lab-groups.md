`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended survey responses in a small mixed-methods study. Student comments included "Sometimes as a woman, male partners just assume that they know more and do not want to mess up steps." The authors note these experiences were reported by a minority of students.

> "qualitative feedback revealed that some female students experienced challenges related to gender dynamics, such as perceived assumptions about competence, being overlooked during discussions, and hesitation to voice opinions in male-dominated groups"
