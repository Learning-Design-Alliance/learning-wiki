### Yu Jingjing 2025

Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang, Yu Jian. (2025). Chinese dental students’ knowledge, attitudes, and practice regarding traumatic dental injuries in immature permanent teeth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07584-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of clinical practice among 502 Chinese dental students (Table 5). Most participants never treated TDI cases independently; residents more often treated cases occasionally or often than undergraduates, with grade differences significant by Chi-square test.

> "The majority of participants (70.1%) never treated TDI cases independently, and 2nd - (63.5%) and 3rd -year (49.5%) residents had significantly higher percentages than other grades (p < 0.05)."
