Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2020¶

Megan Kuhfeld, Karyn Lewis, Patrick Meyer, Beth Tarasawa. (2020). Comparability analysis of remote and in-person MAP Growth testing in fall 2020. NWEA Research Technical Brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/comparability-analysis-of-remote-and-in-person-map-growth-testing-in-fall-2020/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The brief reports "high levels of marginal reliability" across all grades as a headline finding of the comparability analysis of over 535,000 K-8 students' test scores.

"We found high levels of marginal reliability and test engagement across all grades, as well as consistent trends in test scores for remote and in-person tests for students in grades 3-8."
