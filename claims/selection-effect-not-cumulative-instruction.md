## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Improvement or selection? A longitudinal analysis of students' views about experimental physics in their lab courses. arXiv:1706.03681. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of matched pre/post E-CLASS responses across three required lab courses over eight semesters at CU Boulder. The article concludes the pseudo-longitudinal increase "is strongly, if not entirely, driven by a selection eﬀect," with persisting students starting more expert-like and their views staying roughly constant.

> "These trends suggest that the increase in students' scores in the higher level lab courses observed in the pseudo-longitudinal data (see Table III) is strongly, if not entirely, driven by a selection eﬀect."
