### Watanabe K 2013

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Positive-predictive-value analysis of endoscopic judgments of 77 patients (462 physician-case results) whose infection status was set by histology, serology, and urea breath test. The study reports the yield "was highest for H. pylori-uninfected (88.9%)" and lowest for eradicated cases.

> "The yield was highest for H. pylori-uninfected (88.9%), followed by H. pylori-infected (62.1%), and H. pylori-eradicated (55.8%) for all six endoscopists."
