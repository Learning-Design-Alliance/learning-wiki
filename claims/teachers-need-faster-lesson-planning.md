## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers expressed that lesson planning and didactic-material creation are time-consuming and desired support to prepare classes more quickly and practically. [→ Ferreira 2024](#ferreira-2024)

## Evidence

### Ferreira 2024

Ferreira, A., Lima, D. A., Oliveira, W., Bittencourt, I. I. B., Dermeval, D., Reimers, F., Isotani, S. (2024). Exploring Brazilian Teachers’ Perceptions and a priori Needs to Design Smart Classrooms. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00410-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of responses from 410 Brazilian teachers; Lesson Planning code. A participant wrote, "Preparing slides faster saves time in material preparation and provides more time to develop dynamic classroom activities". No effect size is reported.

> "lesson planning and class- room management is a complex activity that requires time and dedication from the participants, mainly because many of the didactic resources that assist in the teaching- learning process need to be created from scratch or updated regularly."
