Secondary chi-square analysis in the same study of 120 physicians; all four sex comparisons were non-significant (χ2 values 2.1, 2.1, 1.7, 0.4, all P > 0.05). The quoted sentence reports the post-course outcome; "no dependence relationship" was found for sex.

> "The categorical variable ‘Effective CPR on the mannequin with feedback after 8 h BLSD training course’ shows no dependence relationship with the categorical variable ‘Sex,’ χ2 (1, N = 120) = 0.4, P > 0.05."
