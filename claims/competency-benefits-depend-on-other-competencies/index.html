q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report describes a modeling approach using a flexible functional form to model complex interactions among competencies, so the effect of a competency is estimated as a function of students' other competencies. No interaction estimates are printed in the available text.

"using a flexible functional form to model and analyze complex interactions among competencies. Using this approach, we can estimate the effect of a competency as a function of students’ other competencies."
