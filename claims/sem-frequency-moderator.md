# SEM intervention frequency of two lessons per week is optimal; no significant effect above two lessons per week

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i1`–`i3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` SEM at 2 lessons/week shows a large positive effect (ES = 1.820). [→ Yao Gege 2025](#yao-gege-2025)
`q3 i1` SEM at ≤1 lesson/week shows a small positive effect (ES = 0.484). [→ Yao Gege 2025 (2)](#yao-gege-2025-2)
`q3 i?` SEM at >2 lessons/week shows no statistically significant effect (P = 0.979). [→ Yao Gege 2025 (3)](#yao-gege-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Yao Gege 2025

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analysis by intervention frequency. Two lessons per week showed "Effect size = 1.820" (95% CI 0.486–3.154, P = 0.008), the largest frequency effect.

> "SEM with an intervention frequency of 2 lessons/week showed a significant large positive effect (Effect size = 1.820; P = 0.008)"

### Yao Gege 2025 (2)

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis for ≤1 lesson/week found a significant small effect (ES = 0.484, 95% CI 0.021–0.847, P = 0.009).

> "SEM with an intervention frequency of ≤1 lesson/week demonstrated a significant small positive effect on students' physical education learning (Effect size = 0.484; P = 0.009)"

### Yao Gege 2025 (3)

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis for >2 lessons/week found no statistically significant effect (P = 0.979, ES = 0.003); equivalence was not tested.

> "SEM did not achieve statistical significance for students with >2 lessons/week intervention (P = 0.979)"
