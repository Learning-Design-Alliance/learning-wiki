## Evidence

### Pedigo Ryan MD 2020

Pedigo Ryan MD, Tolles Juliana MD, MHS, Watcha Daena MD, MS, Kaji Amy H. MD, PhD, MPH, Lewis Roger J. MD, PhD, Stark Elena MD, PhD, Jordan Jaime MD. (2020). Teaching Endotracheal Intubation Using a Cadaver Versus a Manikin-based Model: a Randomized Controlled Trial. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2019.10.44522

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Association between post-training self-rated confidence and time to ETI was analyzed with a Cox proportional hazards model in the randomized trial. "The students who rated their confidence after the teaching intervention as a 5" showed decreased time to ETI (HR = 4.2; 95% CI, 1.03–17.2).

> "The students who rated their confidence after the teaching intervention as a 5 ("extremely confident") also demonstrated a decreased time to successful ETI (HR = 4.2; 95% CI, 1.03–17.2)."
