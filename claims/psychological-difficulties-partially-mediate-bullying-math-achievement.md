# Psychological difficulties at age 13 partially mediate the effect of bullying at age 9 on later mathematics achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Bullying at age 9 indirectly lowered mathematics achievement at age 15 via total psychological difficulties at age 13, and a direct effect of bullying on mathematics remained, indicating partial mediation. [→ Murphy 2022](#murphy-2022)

## Evidence

### Murphy 2022

Murphy, D., Leonard, S. J., Taylor, L. K., & Santos, F. H. (2022). Educational achievement and bullying: The mediating role of psychological difficulties. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1487–1501. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12511

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal path mediation model on the Growing Up in Ireland child cohort (N = 8214 analysed for bullying) with bias-corrected bootstrapped indirect effects. The significant indirect effect for mathematics was "β = −.005 [95% CI: −0.007, −0.002]" and a direct effect remained, i.e. "partial mediation".

> "Indicating partial mediation for mathematics achievement, there remained a direct effect of bullying at age 9 (β = −.02, p < .05); those who reported being bullied scored lower on their mathematics junior cert than those who did not report being bullied."
