### Latia P. White 2026 (2)

Latia P. White, Ed. D. (2026). Beyond Participation: The Capacities That Shape Engagement in Collaborative Innovation. Digital Promise, Center for Learner Pathway Innovations. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/e933ec0e-f153-4046-842b-61b696b00ba2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Contrast finding from the same reflection analysis: when staff assumed too much responsibility for facilitation, data activities, or project management, partners had fewer opportunities to practice skills and take up shared ownership.

> "Where staﬀ carried more of the process, the work could continue, but shared ownership and co-leadership were less evident."
