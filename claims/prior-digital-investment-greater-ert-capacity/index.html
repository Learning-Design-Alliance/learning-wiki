Evidence¶

Abaci 2021¶

Abaci, S., Robertson, J., Linklater, H., & McNeill, F. (2021). Supporting school teachers' rapid engagement with online education. Education Tech Research Dev, 69, 29-34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09839-5

q2 · i? · design · r1

The authors' observational reflection (SF2 section) on Scottish primary and secondary schools during the pandemic: prior investment in technology and professional development for digital learning gave schools "a greater capacity to respond quickly". No effect size or test is reported.

"those schools and education authorities who had previously invested in technology and professional development for digital learning, had a greater capacity to respond quickly towards online communication and teaching"
