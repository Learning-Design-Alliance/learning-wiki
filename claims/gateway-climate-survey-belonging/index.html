Cook-Harvey 2020¶

Cook-Harvey, C. M., Flook, L., Efland, E., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2020). Teaching for powerful learning: Lessons from Gateway Public Schools. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. http://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/social-and-emotional-learning-case-study-gateway

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Report of a 2018 local school climate survey at Gateway Middle School, cited alongside high school figures (92% connected, 91% safe, 80% teachers care). The report describes students as feeling "part of the school" at high rates.

"Middle school students who took the local climate survey in 2018 responded that 92% of students feel there is a teacher who cares about them, 88% said they feel part of the school, and 89% feel safe at the school."
