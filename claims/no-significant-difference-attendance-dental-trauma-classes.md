## Evidence

### Pawlaczyk-Kamieńska T 2025

Pawlaczyk-Kamieńska T, Jastrzębski B, Kamieńska P, Kamieńska W, Marushko O, Marushko O, Kruszyńska D. (2025). The evaluation of declarative and objective knowledge of first aid for dental traumatic injuries among medical students: development and validation of a competency assessment tool. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08153-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of TDI-KNOW scores between self-reported attendees (n = 31) and non-attendees (n = 259) in the cross-sectional survey; the difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.09). Attendance was "self-reported and not independently verified".

> "Students who did not attend classes on dental traumatology (n = 259) had a knowledge level of 63.14%, whereas those who attended (n = 31) exhibited a knowledge level of 66.51%. Attendance data were self-reported and not independently verified, therefore these results should be interpreted with caution."
