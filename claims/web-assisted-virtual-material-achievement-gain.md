## Evidence

### Taş 2015

Erol Taş. (2015). The Effects of Activity and Gain Based Virtual Material on Student’s Success, Permanency and Attitudes towards Science Lesson. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(5). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i5.817

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 70 6th graders (35 per group) in Samsun; experimental group used the web-assisted material while the control group was taught the unit in the usual way. Post-test means were 71.8857 (EG) versus 57.6000 (CG), with "t (69) =4,537, p<0.05" favoring the experimental group.

> "A statistically significant difference was seen between experimental and control groups in the post -test results (t (69) =4,537, p<0.05). This result showed a significant difference in favor of experimental group students in terms of achievement."
