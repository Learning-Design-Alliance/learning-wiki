## Evidence

### Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi 2012

Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi. (2012). The Impact of Task-based Approach on Vocabulary Learning in ESP Courses. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p118

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test on the 25 control-group students taught technical vocabulary by the traditional translate-and-answer method over thirteen weeks. Means moved only from 17.88 to 18.04 and the article reports the difference was not significant; no effect size is printed.

> "the t-observed (-.267) was less than t-critical (2.064), at 0.05 level of significance, therefore it can be concluded that the difference between the mean of the pre-test and that of the post-test in control group was not significant."
