### Jungck John R. 2010

Jungck John R., Gaff Holly, Weisstein Anton E. (2010). Mathematical Manipulative Models: In Defense of “Beanbag Biology”. CBE-Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-03-0040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Classroom activity description from the authors' workshop practice: students run the FOXRAB predator–prey board game for six to 10 generations, graph coupled oscillations, phase portraits, and possible chaos, then implement finite difference equations on a hand-held calculator. This is a design description, not a measured comparison.

> "we contrast the stochastic behavior of the physical model, where each group gets different results due to random mixing and distribution of beans, with the deterministic behavior of the mathematical model"
