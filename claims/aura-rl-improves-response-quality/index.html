Jinwen Tang 2026¶

Jinwen Tang. (2026). NEW AI-DRIVEN TOOLS FOR ENHANCING CAMPUS WELL-BEING: A PREVENTION AND INTERVENTION APPROACH. Doctoral dissertation, University of Missouri-Columbia. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10804 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.10804

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Controlled evaluation with n = 20 conversations per condition comparing AURA's within-session RL adaptation against non-adaptive baselines. The abstract reports "a+0.12mean gain in composite response quality" with p = 0.044 and d = 0.66, plus behavioral shifts in prompt types.

"AURA achieved a+0.12mean gain in composite response quality with a statistically significant improvement over non-adaptive baselines (p= 0.044, d= 0.66), driven by a63%reduction in specification prompts and a10×increase in validation behavior."
