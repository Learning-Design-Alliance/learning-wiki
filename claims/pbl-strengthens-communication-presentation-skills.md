### Gupta Nidhi 2026

Gupta Nidhi, Sunil Sruthi, Ali Kamran, Fink Trine, Du Xiangyun. (2026). Learner agency in a problem-based learning curriculum: A qualitative study on perspectives of undergraduate dental students. PLOS One. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13020853/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group thematic analysis of dental students' PBL experiences. Participants reported that regular presentations and collaborative discussions enhanced interpersonal skills, and that PBL sessions "put us in social settings and improve our communication skills."

> "Participants unanimously highlighted communication as one of the most significant strengths they gained from PBL."
