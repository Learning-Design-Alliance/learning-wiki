## Evidence

### Nicholas Judd 2022

Nicholas Judd, Bruno Sauce and Torkel Klingberg. (2022). Schooling substantially improves intelligence, but neither lessens nor widens the impacts of socioeconomics and genetics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00148-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main-effect estimates from the mixed-effects regression discontinuity models in 6567 children. CogPGS effects were similar for fIQ (β = 0.09) and WM (β = 0.09); SES was slightly higher for WM (β = 0.22) than fIQ (β = 0.18).

> "CogPGS and SES both showed independent signiﬁcant effects (all p's< 0.001) for cIQ, fIQ, and WM. The effect sizes of cogPGS ( β = 0.16) and SES ( β = 0.29) were the largest for cIQ."
