## Evidence

### İlter İlhan 2025

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent-samples t tests in the sample of 363 sixth-graders; gender differences were non-significant for mathematics performance but significant for self-efficacy and buoyancy. Only test statistics are printed, no effect sizes.

> "Results revealed no significant difference in MP by gender (t = 0.776, p > 0.05). However, significant gender differences emerged for ASE (t = 2.501, p < 0.001) and AB (t = 3.069, p < 0.001)."

### İlter İlhan 2025 (2)

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA across age groups in the same sample found no significant differences on any main variable; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "One-way ANOVA across age groups showed no significant differences in the main study variables (F = 1.73, p > 0.05)."
