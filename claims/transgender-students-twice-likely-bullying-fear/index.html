Hanson 2019¶

Hanson, T., Zhang, G., Cerna, R., Stern, A., & Austin, G. (2019). Understanding the experiences of LGBTQ students in California. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601909

Descriptive CHKS tabulations of school safety measures by gender identity and school level; the report identifies physical harassment and bullying as the domain with the biggest disparities between LGBTQ subgroups.
