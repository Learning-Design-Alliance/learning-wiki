# Five ASSIST subscales predict GPA, with 'achieving' the strongest positive and 'fear of failure' the strongest negative predictor, and the full model explains 9.6% of GPA variance

## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T 2017

Bonsaksen T, Brown T, Lim HB, Fong K. (2017). Approaches to studying predict academic performance in undergraduate occupational therapy students: a cross-cultural study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0914-3

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear regression adding the 13 ASSIST subscales in three blocks (deep, strategic, surface) predicting GPA in n = 712 students. Five subscales reached significance; the deep block added 1.2% variance (p = 0.08), strategic 1.8% (p = 0.03), and surface 4.0% (p < 0.001).

> "The strongest associations with GPA were shown for 'achieving' (std. β = 0.22, in the strategic category) and 'fear of failure' (std. β = −0.17, in the surface category). The full regression model explained 9.6% of the total variance in GPA among the students, and 7.0% of the GPA variance was explained by the ASSIST subscales."
