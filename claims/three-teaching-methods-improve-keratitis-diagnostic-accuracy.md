# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, medical students' mean overall diagnostic accuracy of infectious keratitis rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in all three teaching groups (real cases, AI-generated images, and real medical images)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within each teaching group, mean overall and per-type diagnostic accuracy improved significantly after the teaching intervention (all P < 0.001, paired t-test), with mean overall improvements of 25.43% (G1), 28.43% (G2), and 27.67% (G3). [→ Xie Wenjia 2025](#xie-wenjia-2025)

## Evidence

### Xie Wenjia 2025

Xie Wenjia, Yuan Zhouhang, Si Yuxuan, Huang Zhengxing, Li Yingming, Wu Fei, Yao Yu-Feng. (2025). Enhancing medical students’ diagnostic accuracy of infectious keratitis with AI-generated images. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07592-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental clerkship study of 97 fourth-year medical students in three teaching groups (G1 real cases n=30, G2 AI-generated images n=37, G3 real medical images n=30), tested before and after with the same 30-question test. The article reports "all P < 0.001, paired t-test" for within-group gains. Mean improvements were 25.43%, 28.43%, and 27.67% respectively.

> "All three teaching methods significantly improved the mean overall diagnostic accuracy, as well as the mean accuracies for each type of keratitis (all P < 0.001, paired t-test)."
