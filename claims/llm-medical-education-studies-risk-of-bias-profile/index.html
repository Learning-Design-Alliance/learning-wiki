Descriptive design-specific appraisal of the 14 included studies using RoB 2-, ROBINS-I-, or JBI-informed domains mapped into six display domains. No effect size applies; no overall score was calculated and the mapping was "not intended as a mathematically merged or separately validated single risk-of-bias tool".

"Across the mapped domains, limitations were most visible in allocation concealment, participant/teacher blinding, and other design-sensitive sources of bias, whereas incomplete outcome data were generally handled better."
