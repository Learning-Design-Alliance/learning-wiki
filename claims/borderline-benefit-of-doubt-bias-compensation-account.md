This is the authors' interpretive account in the Discussion, offered with no direct measure of assessor self-awareness; they link it speculatively to Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion training provided to SPs.

> "For borderline candidates, where there is uncertainty about the quality of the performance, non-white candidates may be given the benefit of the doubt when compared with their white counterparts to compensate for any potential bias."
