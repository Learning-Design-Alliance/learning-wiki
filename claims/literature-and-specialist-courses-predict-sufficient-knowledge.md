## Evidence

### Fortunka Kamila et al 2024

Fortunka Kamila et al. (2024). Knowledge and Training Needs in Nosocomial Infection among Hospital Staff in the City of Kielce, Poland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9243232

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second logistic regression model on knowledge sources in the same 635-nurse survey (Hosmer–Lemeshow p=0.556, AUC = 0.572). Both literature use and course participation independently predicted the standardized knowledge index.

> "the chance of having sufficient knowledge in the field of nosocomial infections is 1.6 times higher among nurses who use specialist medical literature (OR = 1,612; 95% CI: 1,068–2,432; p=0.023) and take part in specialist courses (OR = 1,613; 95% CI: 1,007–2,581; p=0.047)"
