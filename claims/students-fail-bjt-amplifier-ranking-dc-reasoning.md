## Evidence

### Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer 2018

Kevin L. Van De Bogart and MacKenzie R. Stetzer. (2018). Investigating student understanding of bipolar junction transistor circuits. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.00404)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Written free-response task administered in four upper-division electronics courses (total 410 responses; course Ns 57, 42, 169, 142). "varied widely across different courses, ranging from 7% to 42%" gave the correct ranking; fewer than a third in any course gave correct reasoning.

> "the distribution of students giving a correct ranking of the peak-to-peak output voltages for all three circuits (Vout,B > Vout,C > Vout,A) varied widely across different courses, ranging from 7% to 42% of the students in a given course."
