## Evidence

### Florence Martin 2021

Florence Martin, Ting Sun, Murat Turk, and Albert D. Ritzhaupt. (2021). A Meta-Analysis on the Effects of Synchronous Online Learning on Cognitive and Affective Educational Outcomes. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, Volume 22, Number 3. https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias assessment of the four meta-analytic models using funnel plots, classic fail-safe N, and Orwin's fail-safe N at a 0.01 trivial level. The authors note grey literature (dissertations) was included but the problem remained.

> "All these criteria show evidence that our study was subject to the problem of  publication bias, and thus, additional studies could substantially change the results of our models."
