## Evidence

### Abdullah Aydin 2017

Abdullah Aydin, Filiz Biyikli. (2017). The Effect of Jigsaw Technique on the Students' Laboratory Material Recognition and Usage Skills in General Physics Laboratory-I Course. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(7). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050701

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test comparison in the same quasi-experiment: recognition means 30.58 (jigsaw) vs 29.03 (control), t=-0.98, p=.332; usage means 20.38 vs 16.77, t=-1.24, p=.223. The article reports "not a meaningful difference between the scores of two groups in the material recognition pre-test."

> "as (t= -0.98; p=.332) p>.05, there is not a meaningful difference between the scores of two groups in the material recognition pre-test."
