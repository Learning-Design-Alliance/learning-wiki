John T. E. Richardson 2017¶

John T. E. Richardson. (2017). Student Learning in Higher Education: a Commentary. Educational Psychology Review, 29, 353–362. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9410-x

q1 · i? · review · r2

The commentary classifies the five special-issue contributions by review methodology, contrasting narrative review, which leaves the reviewer free to construct his or her own story, with systematic review based on structured mining of the literature. This is the author's interpretive classification, not a tested result.

"The articles by Fryer and Zusho adopted the traditional techniques of narrative review, which leave the reviewer free to construct his or her own story about the chosen topic. In contrast, the articles by Asikainen and Gijbels, by Dinsmore, and by V ermunt and Donche adopted the techniques of systematic review"
