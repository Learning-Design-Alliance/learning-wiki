`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Evaluation study conducted in 2001 comparing an experimental group of 26 participants using KERMIT to a control group of 31 using a version with no feedback on solutions. The article reports "a statistically significant difference in the learning gains" with "the effect size d of 0.63" after an average of 66 min. Students liked the system and rated its feedback highly.

> "There was a statistically significant difference in the learning gains (post-test score – pre-test score) of the two groups, with the effect size d of 0.63, after students spent an average of only 66 min with the system."
