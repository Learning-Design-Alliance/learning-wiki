The review reports Pratama's study of scholarly abstracts, in which "97.22% aggregate accuracy" coexisted with group-level over-detection rates of 25% versus 11% and mean AI scores of 44.61% and 30.68%. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "In one scenario GPTZero achieved 97.22% aggregate accuracy, yet in the AI-assisted readability condition its over-detection rate was 25% for non-native-authored abstracts compared with 11% for native-authored abstracts, with mean AI scores of 44.61% and 30.68% respectively."
