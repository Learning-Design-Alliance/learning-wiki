# Students’ knowledge about genAI, measured both by self-report and performance test, positively predicts their critical use of genAI

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Self-reported knowledge about genAI correlates positively with critical use of genAI (r = 0.55). [→ Markus H. Hefter 2026](#markus-h-hefter-2026)
`q3 i2` Objective (performance-based) knowledge about genAI correlates positively with critical use of genAI (r = 0.31). [→ Markus H. Hefter 2026](#markus-h-hefter-2026)

## Evidence

### Markus H. Hefter 2026

Markus H. Hefter, Benjamin Paaßen, Kirsten Berthold. (2026). GenAI Knowledge, Epistemic Orientation, and Intellectual Values Predict Undergraduate Students’ Critical GenAI Use. AI Educ. 2026, 2, 27. https://doi.org/10.3390/aieduc2030027

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational study of N = 67 undergraduate psychology students using a self-report knowledge scale (18 SNAIL items, α = 0.90) and a 12-item performance knowledge test. The study reports a “significant positive correlation between subjective knowledge about genAI and critical use of genAI, r = 0.55” and r = 0.31 for objective knowledge.

> "Correlation analyses revealed a significant positive correlation between subjective knowledge about genAI and critical use of genAI, r = 0.55, p < 0.001. Furthermore, we found a significant positive correlation between objective knowledge about genAI and critical use of genAI, r = 0.31, p = 0.011."
