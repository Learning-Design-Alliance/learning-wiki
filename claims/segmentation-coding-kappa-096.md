## Evidence

### Gombert 2024

Gombert, S., Fink, A., Giorgashvili, T., Jivet, I., Di Mitri, D., Yau, J., Frey, A., & Drachsler, H. (2024). From the Automated Assessment of Student Essay Content to Highly Informative Feedback: a Case Study. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00387-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Manual annotation study of the segmentation training data: three coders coded the same 100 pilot essays in INCEpTION and pairwise Cohen's Kappa was averaged. The article reports the global average was "determined to be 0.96", after which remaining essays were distributed between the coders.

> "Then we calculated the global average which was determined to be 0.96, indicating an 'almost perfect' agreement between all three coders."
