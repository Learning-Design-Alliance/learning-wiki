# Experimental-group students held more positive attitudes toward flexible hypertext course design than control-group students toward direct design (t = 4.723)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The t-test for equality of means on student attitudes was 4.723 (df 144), exceeding the critical value of 1.962, so Hypothesis 2 was rejected at the 0.05 alpha level. [→ Chokri Barhoumi; Pier Giuseppe Rossi 2013](#chokri-barhoumi-pier-giuseppe-rossi-2013)

## Evidence

### Chokri Barhoumi; Pier Giuseppe Rossi 2013

Chokri Barhoumi; Pier Giuseppe Rossi. (2013). The Effectiveness of Instruction-Oriented Hypertext Systems Compared to Direct Instruction in e-learning Environments. Contemporary Educational Technology, 4(4), 281-308. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105506

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-level Likert attitude survey (8 items, N=146) administered face-to-face to both groups, analyzed with t-test in SPSS. The article reports t = 4.723 against a critical value of 1.962, with control mean 18.44 and experimental mean 15.99 (lower scores indicate more agreement). No effect size is printed.

> "The obtained value of the t-test calculated for the equality of means in attitudes of students of the experimental group and the control group is 4.723 (see Table 10), and this is higher than the critical value of the t-test, which 1.962."
