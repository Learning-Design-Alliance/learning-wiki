## Evidence

### Reviriego E 2014

Reviriego E, Cidoncha MA, Asua J, Gagnon MP, Mateos M, Gárate L, de Lorenzo E, González RM. (2014). Online training course on critical appraisal for nurses: adaptation and assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A prospective pre-post study of the Spanish InfoCritique course administered a 20-question ad hoc knowledge questionnaire before and after the course to nurses in the Basque Health Service. The mean rose "from 70.5 out of 100" to 86.6, a statistically significant improvement (p < 0.001).

> "The mean score on the knowledge questionnaire increased from 70.5 out of 100 (SD 11.96) pre-course to 86.6 (SD of 10.00), and this improvement was statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
