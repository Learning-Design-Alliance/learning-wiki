Evidence¶

Bastian A. Betthäuser 2023¶

Bastian A. Betthäuser, Anders M. Bach-Mortensen & Per Engzell. (2023). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the evidence on learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01506-4

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r3

Bivariate comparison of deficit magnitude by subject using common-effects models with study-clustered standard errors, shown in violin plots (Fig. 6a; medians reading −0.09, maths −0.18). The printed mean difference "δ = −0.07" is statistically significant and robust to dropping individual countries.

"the estimated learning deficits during the COVID-19 pandemic are larger for maths than for reading (mean difference δ = −0.07, t(41) = −4.02, two-tailed P = 0.000, 95% CI −0.11 to −0.04). This difference is statistically significant and robust to dropping estimates from individual countries"
