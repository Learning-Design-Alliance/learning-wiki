# Clique-average mathematics achievement negatively predicts the development of adolescents' academic self-concept (a BFLPE at the clique level)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher clique-average mathematics achievement is associated with a decrease in individual students' ASC over one school year, controlling for individual achievement, gender, and school type. [→ Tanja Auer 2025](#tanja-auer-2025)

## Evidence

### Tanja Auer 2025

Tanja Auer, Marion Reindl, Burkhard Gniewosz. (2025). Is the clique a pond? The big-fish-little-pond effect and the relative meaning of clique and classroom. Learning and Instruction 95 (2025) 101997. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101997

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-wave longitudinal study of 743 German sixth- and eighth-grade students in 93 cliques within 40 classrooms, analyzed with a doubly latent three-level Bayesian SEM (final model 4). The article reports the clique-level BFLPE coefficient: "b =  0.53, posterior SD = 0.16, 95% CI [ 0.852,  0.204], R2 = 0.42".

> "we observed that clique academic achievement was negatively associated (BFLPE) with students' ASCdevelopment: b =  0.53, posterior SD = 0.16, 95% CI [ 0.852,  0.204], R2 = 0.42"
