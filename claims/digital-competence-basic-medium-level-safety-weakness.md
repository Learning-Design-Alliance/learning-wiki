## Evidence

### Zhao Yu 2021

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of research outcomes among studies assessing perception and level of digital competence. The review reports "five of these articles reported that participants' digital competence was maintained at a basic level", four reported intermediate levels, and students were less aware of the safety area.

> "five of these articles reported that participants' digital competence was maintained at a basic level and they could feel limiting capacities when operating technological tools in some areas."

### Zhao Yu 2021 (2)

Zhao Yu, Pinto Llorente Ana María, Sánchez Gómez María Cruz. (2021). Digital competence in higher education research: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 168, 104212. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104212

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of level studies (e.g., Cabezas González et al., 2017; Gutiérrez Porlán & Serrano Sánchez, 2016): students rated competence well in general and basic aspects, with declines in information literacy, digital content creation, digital identity management and digital research skills areas.

> "As for students, they rated their competence quite well in the general and most basic aspects, but it decreased when the level of complexity increased"
