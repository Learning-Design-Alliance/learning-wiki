## Evidence

### Tomlinson 2026

Tomlinson, B., Black, R.W., Patterson, D.J., van der Hoek, A., Ferguson, J. & Bietz, M.J. (2026). Students prefer personalized, AI-generated educational videos over non-personalized, human-recorded videos. Scientific Reports 16:21804. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-52798-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mean rank aggregation of complete four-item rankings from the end-of-term survey, reported separately by cohort and combined. The human-recorded personalized condition was hypothetical (never produced); the article notes this ordering "was stable across course offerings".

> "In the Spring cohort, human-recorded personalized videos had a mean rank of 1.44, followed by AI-generated personalized videos (2.31), human-recorded non-personalized videos (2.69), and AI-generated non-personalized videos (3.56). The Fall cohort showed a nearly identical pattern (1.57, 2.21, 2.69, and 3.52, respectively)."
