## Evidence

### Benjamin Pollard 2020

Benjamin Pollard, Alexandra Werth, Robert Hobbs, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Impact of a course transformation on students' reasoning about measurement uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020160](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020160)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Code-level DMSS analysis (Fig. 7); P2 (means must match) and P3 (means close enough) both lack reasoning around distributions, and their decrease in the transformed course is described by the article as "an indication that the transformation was effective."

> "A decrease in their prevalence after the transformation, given the absence of such a decrease before the transformation, is an indica- tion that the transformation was eﬀective."
