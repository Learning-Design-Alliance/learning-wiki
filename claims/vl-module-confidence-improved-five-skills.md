## Evidence

### Shilling V 2025

Shilling V, Starkings R, Fallowfield L. (2025). Feasibility and acceptability of a virtual learning module for navigating angry conversations in clinical encounters. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06726-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of built-in pre/post module confidence ratings (1–5) from 19 respondents (data available for 19 due to a transfer issue). "Scores on all measures improved, though some key learning elements were already high at baseline, limiting the opportunity for advancement." No effect sizes or significance tests were reported.

> "Figure 1a-1e shows pre and post self-assessment of confidence (rated 1–5) in relation to the five key learning points."
