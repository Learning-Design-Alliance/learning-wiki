## Evidence

### Diaz 2021

Diaz, B., & Easton, J.Q. (2021). CPS Gradebook Technical Report. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/CPS-gradebook-technical-report

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Distributional analysis of 32,234 combined fall and spring final course grade scores (Figure 2). The report states "the most common point score for a given grade was the minimum for that grade" and lists possible explanations such as rounding up or extra credit opportunities.

> "Many students earned the fewest possible points that would earn a higher grade. In fact, the most common point score for a given grade was the minimum for that grade."
