## Evidence

### Chen 2025

Chen, Y., Li, J., Chui, H., & King, R. B. (2025). Peer cooperation and competition are both positively linked with mastery‐approach goals: An achievement goal perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 941–961. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12784

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Step 4 of the doubly latent ML-SEM added the PISA competitiveness measure as a proxy for performance-approach goals. In this model, peer cooperation and competition both still predicted mastery-approach goals, while "only school‐level peer competition was significantly associated with the proxy" at β = .28, p < .001.

> "only school‐level peer competition was significantly associated with the proxy for performance‐approach goals (β = .28, p < .001, large effect)."
