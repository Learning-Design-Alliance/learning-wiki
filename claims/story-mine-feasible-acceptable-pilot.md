Lases Mitch N, Bruins Jojanneke, van Balkom Ingrid DC, Castelein Stynke. (2026). The STORY MINE Digital Recovery Narrative Intervention for Personal Recovery in Mental Health Care: Pilot Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/83948

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Acceptability assessment (n=13) within the pilot feasibility RCT of STORY MINE among adult outpatients at a Dutch psychiatric institute, using study-specific postintervention items. The module was rated user-friendly with a mean of 7.77 (SD 2.52), and participants reported "contributed at least somewhat to their recovery" and connectedness with narrators.

> "Quantitatively, the user-friendliness of the STORY MINE module was rated positively (mean 7.77, SD 2.52; with n=8/13 rating ≥8)."
