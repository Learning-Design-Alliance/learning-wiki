## Evidence

### Søren Marker 2019

Søren Marker, Marlene Mohr, Doris Østergaard. (2019). Simulation-based training of junior doctors in handling critically ill patients facilitates the transition to clinical practice: an interview study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1447-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study of 20 first-year doctors 6 months after SBT; NTS was a main theme with subthemes for communication, teamwork, leadership and situational awareness. The article reports "The use of NTS, in clinical practice was found to be helpful". No effect size is printed.

> "The use of NTS, in clinical practice was found to be helpful in meeting the medical expertise challenges. The examples of increased focus on situational awareness were reported alongside the use of the ABCDE and ACCEPT algorithms."
