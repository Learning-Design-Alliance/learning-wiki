## Evidence

### Ghazali Daniel Aiham 2019

Ghazali Daniel Aiham, Breque Cyril, Sosner Philippe, Lesbordes Mathieu, Chavagnat Jean-Jacques, Ragot Stéphanie, Oriot Denis. (2019). Stress response in the daily lives of simulation repeaters. A randomized controlled trial assessing stress evolution over one year of repetitive immersive simulations. PLoS ONE 14(7): e0220111. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0220111

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA of relative variation of HR and LF/HF ratio at times T1–T7 during the 72 simulations in the 12 randomized teams. The article reports "F = 63.1, p<0.0001 for HR, and F = 26.9, p<0.0001 for LF/HF ratio"; Scheffe post-hoc found significant differences between T2 and T3, and T3 and T5.

> "HR and LF/HF ratio increased during all the simulation sessions and they all decreased during and after debriefing sessions (F = 63.1, p<0.0001 for HR, and F = 26.9, p<0.0001 for LF/HF ratio)."
