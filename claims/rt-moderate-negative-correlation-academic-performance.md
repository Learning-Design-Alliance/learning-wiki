## Evidence

### Prabu Kumar Archana 2020

Prabu Kumar Archana, Omprakash Abirami, Kuppusamy Maheshkumar, K.N. Maruthy, B.W.C. Sathiyasekaran, P.V. Vijayaraghavan, Ramaswamy Padmavathi. (2020). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02416-7

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of 618 undergraduate records in a cross-sectional study at a South Indian private medical university found "a moderate negative correlation with academic performance" for both auditory and visual reaction time, each at p < 0.001.

> "VRT and ART had a moderate negative correlation with academic performance (for ART, r = − 0.42, p < 0.001; for VRT; r = − 0.40, p < 0.001)"
