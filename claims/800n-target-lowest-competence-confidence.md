Chiropractic students’ characteristics influencing confidence and competence in modulating spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a secondary analysis of a cross-sectional study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the FSTT® measurements of 149 students' force–time characteristics, plotted by competence; the article states "very few participants were able to achieve the 800 N peak thrust force". No effect sizes printed.

> "Although very few participants were able to achieve the 800 N peak thrust force, they still performed the thrust in less than 150 ms; in fact, often much faster across all the target forces (Fig. 3)."

Descriptive violin-plot results of self-reported confidence (100 mm VAS) stratified by competence; the article reports confidence "became lower and more variable as the target peak forces increased to 800 N". No effect sizes printed.
