## Evidence

### Goodrich J. Marc 2017

Goodrich J. Marc, Lonigan Christopher J. (2017). Language-Independent and Language-Specific Aspects of Early Literacy: An Evaluation of the Common Underlying Proficiency Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analyses of factor scores from best-fitting models (Table 8); "The General Phonological Awareness and Print Knowledge factors were significantly and positively related to each other". The authors interpret this as consistent with a general language-learning capacity.

> "The General Phonological Awareness and Print Knowledge factors were significantly and positively related to each other and to most other factors, with the exception of the specific English Print Knowledge factor."

### Goodrich J. Marc 2017 (2)

Goodrich J. Marc, Lonigan Christopher J. (2017). Language-Independent and Language-Specific Aspects of Early Literacy: An Evaluation of the Common Underlying Proficiency Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of Table 8 correlations; "Specific English and Spanish early literacy abilities were generally related to each other within but not across languages". An unexpected exception was English-specific print knowledge, not consistently related to other factors.

> "Specific English and Spanish early literacy abilities were generally related to each other within but not across languages (e.g., specific Spanish phonological awareness was related to Spanish expressive vocabulary but not English expressive vocabulary)."
