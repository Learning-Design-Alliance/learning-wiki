## Evidence

### Wyse 2018

Wyse, S. A., & Soneral, P. A. G. (2018). “Is This Class Hard?” Defining and Analyzing Academic Rigor from a Learner’s Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0278

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Forced-choice survey administered to 90 additional 100-level and 20 additional 300-level students in Spring and Fall 2014; chi-square tests showed hard (p = 0.036) and easy (p = 0.025) attribute patterns differed between levels. 100-level students attributed difficulty to lack of prior knowledge; 300-level students to high workload.

> "Patterns for hard (χ2, df = 2, p = 0.036) and easy (χ2, df = 3, p = 0.025) attributes of courses differ between 100- and 300-level students (Figure 4)."
