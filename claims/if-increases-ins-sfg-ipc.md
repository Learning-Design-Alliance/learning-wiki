## Evidence

### Junting Yin 2025

Junting Yin, Zheyu Jin, Yuxuan Zhang, Xuening Li, Yangzhuo Li, Guoping Zhang & Junlong Luo. (2025). Applying, not ignoring: how feedback uptake and neural synchrony drive creativity. npj Science of Learning 10:61. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00351-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVAs on fNIRS hyperscanning INS in task-relevant frequency bands across four groups, with printed effect size η2P = 0.218. IF INS (0.07 ± 0.08) exceeded NF (−0.06 ± 0.10), IC, and OF; a permutation test confirmed the effect was specific to real dyads.

> "the results revealed signiﬁcant variation in INS at CH6 –CH14 (FOI 1) among the four groups ( F(3, 95) = 8.840, pcorr = 0.047; η2 P = 0.218)."
