Narrative synthesis of included studies. The review reports that "PBL [13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18] and flipped classrooms [2, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23] frequently demonstrated better post‐test performance and knowledge retention" while gamification's clinical improvements were inconsistent.
