Evidence¶

Foorman Barbara R. 2015¶

Foorman Barbara R., Koon Sharon, Petscher Yaacov, Mitchell Alison, Truckenmiller Adrea. (2015). Examining General and Specific Factors in the Dimensionality of Oral Language and Reading in 4th–10th Grades. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000026

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Exploratory t tests on estimated factor scores compared students with and without LEP designation in fifth and seventh grades after a linear step-up correction; the article reports "Large effect sizes using Hedge's g" favoring non-LEP students, e.g., oral language g = 1.28 and reading comprehension g = 1.10 in fifth grade.

"Large effect sizes using Hedge's g were observed favoring the nonLEP students in both grades on all factors: a) for oral language in fifth grade, t(318) = 9.14, p < .001 (g = 1.28)"
