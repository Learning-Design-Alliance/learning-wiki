Evidence¶

van de Werfhorst 2024¶

van de Werfhorst, H. G., Zwier, D., Geven, S., Bol, T. & Haelermans, C. (2024). Inequality in pandemic effects on school track placement and the role of social and academic embeddedness. npj Science of Learning 9:71. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00283-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Counterfactual analysis by SES group (parental education combined with household income quintile) in the Dutch register data. The disadvantaged group had no effect on ninth-grade track level, while the other three SES groups showed effects around +0.015 levels; there was no discernable effect on track enrollment for students with a college-educated parent.

"The negative pandemic effect on theﬁrst two schooling outcomes is the largest for students from socioecono mically disadvantaged backgrounds (-0.111 and -0.083 levels, respectively)."
