DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of PubMed, CINAHL, ScienceDirect and Web of Science records (2015–2019) screened down from 619 to 18 controlled trials. The review reports that "Most demonstrated promising results in improving outcomes for children with ASD" across the two platform categories.

> "Eighteen unique international studies were identified that utilize robot interventions (11 studies) and serious computer game interventions (seven studies). Most demonstrated promising results in improving outcomes for children with ASD."
