Helping English Learners Rise to the Challenge of Complex Texts 2014¶

Helping English Learners Rise to the Challenge of Complex Texts. (2014). WestEd R&D Alert, Vol. 14, No. 3. http://ell.stanford.edu/teaching_resources/ela

q1 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative feedback from the year-long field test in six Council of the Great City Schools districts. Teachers reported surprise at students' engagement: "I was surprised at the level of questioning and the conversations that students engaged in." No systematic measures are reported.

"Feedback from participating schools indicates that the unit changed teachers' beliefs about what ELL students were capable of achieving."
