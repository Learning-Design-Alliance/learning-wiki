# Robust RDD methods protect against model misspecification but require much larger sample sizes to detect small effects

`q2 i?` State-of-the-art statistical methods for regression discontinuity designs can protect against inaccuracies from incorrect regression models, but detecting small effects with these methods requires much larger sample sizes. [→ John Deke 2017](#john-deke-2017)
