The role of digital teaching methods in supporting practical skills training in the academic training of health professions – a scoping review. q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's narrative synthesis across its 292 included studies. It reports that "most digital teaching methods demonstrated positive effects" on practical skills acquisition while explicitly flagging substantial variation in magnitude and consistency across disciplines and designs.

"Across the included studies, most digital teaching methods demonstrated positive effects on the acquisition of practical skills, particularly simulation-based, VR-supported or otherwise interactive formats. The magnitude and consistency of these effects varied substantially across disciplines and study designs."
