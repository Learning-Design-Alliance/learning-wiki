## Evidence

### Li Chunxiao 2019

Li Chunxiao, Kee Ying Hwa, Kong Leng Chee, Zou Liye, Ng Ka Lok, Li Hong. (2019). Autonomy-Supportive Teaching and Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction among School Students: The Role of Mindfulness. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16142599

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey regression analyses; mindfulness measured with the Chinese MAAS (α = 0.90). The printed coefficients are "β = 0.19, p < 0.001" for need satisfaction and "β = −0.45, p < 0.001" for need frustration.

> "Autonomy-supportive teaching (β = 0.55, p < 0.001) and mindfulness (β = 0.19, p < 0.001) were significant predictors of need satisfaction in step 2."
