Amry 2018¶

Aicha Amry. (2018). The Effect of Twitter Activities in a Blended Learning Classroom Guided by Activity Theory on Students' Achievement and Attitudes. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176184

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Questionnaire study (three-point Likert scale) of attitudes toward the learning process, distributed to both groups (N = 36 each per Table 6). Table 6 prints a control mean of 22.06 and an experimental mean of 25.47; Table 7 prints t = 2.576, df = 70. The article reports the difference in favor of the experimental group.

"The results of the t-test in Table 7 show that the t-test calculated for the equality of means is 2.54, which is greater than the t-test value of the table (2.00)."
