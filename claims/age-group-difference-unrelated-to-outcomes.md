Secondary analysis in the quasi-experimental study found the FC group was older (21.96 ± 2.84) than the control group (19.96 ± 1.36), Kruskal-Wallis p = 0.001, yet "age, as an independent variable, had no significant relationship with the learning rate".

> "Further analysis indicated that age, as an independent variable, had no significant relationship with the learning rate (p = 0.33) or course content retention (p = 0.573)."
