Evidence¶

Stoddard 2026¶

Stoddard, S. A., Moore, C. R., Polick, C. S., & Moss, J. M. (2026). Cumulative Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) and School Outcomes During Adolescence: A Scoping Review. The Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.70209

q3 · i? · review · r2

Scoping review synthesis of 24 US quantitative studies (21 secondary data analyses; 13 cross-sectional, 11 longitudinal). The review reports cumulative ACEs were associated with "reduced likelihood of high school completion (n = 8)" studies. No pooled effect size is printed.

"Cumulative ACEs were associated with reduced likelihood of high school completion (n = 8), poor academic performance (n = 7), and school disengagement (n = 8)."
