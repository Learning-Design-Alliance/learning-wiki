# Athena participants' GR is much higher than for physics studies at university level in Geneva (GR = 0.32) but still below the maturité-level ratio (GR = 1.4)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The gender ratio of Athena participants is still below the maturité-level overall ratio (GR = 1.4) yet much higher than for physics studies at university level (for example in Geneva: GR = 0.32). [→ Andreas Müller 2020](#andreas-muller-2020)
