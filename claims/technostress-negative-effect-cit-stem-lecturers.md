## Evidence

### Su Wenhao 2025

Su Wenhao, Fadzil Hidayah Mohd, Rauf Rose Amnah Abd. (2025). Moderated mediation of technostress, TPACK, and growth mindset on competency in interdisciplinary teaching among STEM lecturers. Scientific Reports 15, 37633. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-21476-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 405 STEM lecturers at seven public universities in Yunnan, China, analyzed with PLS-SEM (bootstrapping, 5,000 resamples). The structural model showed "Technostress negatively and significantly influenced CIT (β=−0.373, p < 0.001)"; f² = 0.247 and the model explained 58.0% of CIT variance.

> "Hypothesis evaluation revealed that Technostress negatively and significantly influenced CIT (β=−0.373, p < 0.001), thus supporting H1."
