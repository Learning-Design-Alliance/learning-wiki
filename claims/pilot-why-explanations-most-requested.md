## Evidence

### Cristina Conati 2019

Cristina Conati, Oswald Barral, Vanessa Putnam, Lea Rieger. (2019). Toward Personalized XAI: A Case Study in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. Preprint submitted to the JAI – Special Issue on Explainable Artificial Intelligence. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot study with nine university students using an instrumented hint dialogue offering why, how, other, or no explanation options. The article says the results "confirm that participants are generally interested in explanations, although to different extents", consistent with prior work on why-then-how preferences.

> "During their interaction with the ACSP, the participants accessed the “explain hint” functionality for 51% of the hints delivered. Of these responses, 21 asked for a why explanation (48%), 14 for a how explanation (32%), and 6 for no explanation (14%)."
