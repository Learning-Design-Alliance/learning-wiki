## Evidence

### Silventoinen Karri 2023

Silventoinen Karri, Luukkonen Juha, Myrskylä Mikko, Martikainen Pekka. (2023). Birth size, school performance and family social position: a study of 650,000 children. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-023-02757-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fixed-effects linear regression within 55,063 families with at least two brothers and 51,915 families with at least two sisters, plus 2352 male and 2271 female complete same-sex twin pairs. "the effect sizes were weaker than in the individual-level analyses"; e.g., BL linear effect β = 0.016 (boys) and 0.031 (girls).

> "However, when we adjusted the results for gestational risk factors, we found that BW and BL were associated with school performance in a similar direction as in the individual-level analyses (Model 2). However, the effect sizes were weaker than in the individual-level analyses in boys and girls."
