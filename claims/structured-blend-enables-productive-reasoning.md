## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Benjamin D. Geller, Julia Gouvea, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Ontological metaphors for negative energy in an interdisciplinary context. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study of Peter's group on the kinesin task: the group incorporated negative energy after overhearing a TA and negotiated bar charts so that energy was conserved. The article contrasts this with Phillip's group, "which struggles to reach this coherence."

> "Peter’s approach is more successful, suggesting that combining the substance and location ontologies is insufficient; the blended ontology needs to have a structure within which the two metaphors can complement each other."
