## Evidence

### Linstead E 2017

Linstead E, Dixon DR, Hong E, Burns CO, French R, Novack MN, Granpeesheh D. (2017). An evaluation of the effects of intensity and duration on outcomes across treatment domains for children with autism spectrum disorder. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/tp.2017.207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

From the Discussion of the retrospective regression analysis of 1468 children: the motor domain coefficients were 0.78 (intensity) and 2.01 (duration), and the adaptive domain 0.63 and 1.88, the lowest of the eight domains. The authors interpret this as a slower learning rate, consistent with prior reports of weak adaptive-behavior treatment response.

> "While both intensity and duration had a significant effect on treatment response in the motor and adaptive domains, these domains had the lowest effect sizes (that is, learning occurred at a slower rate)."
