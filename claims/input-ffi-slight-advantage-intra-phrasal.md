## Evidence

### Schenck 2024

Schenck, A. (2024). Learning How to Use Input-Based and Output-Based Form-Focused Instruction: A Meta-Analytic Comparison of Persian EFL Learners. Journal of Second Language Acquisition and Teaching, 30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1457419

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of Persian-L1 EFL studies; Table 2 reports intra-phrasal input mean 3.7975 (n=5) vs output mean 3.7405 (n=6). The article reports input-based FFI was only slightly more effective, "(a difference in effect size of .06)".

> "Input-based FFI was only slightly more effective when used with intra-phrasal grammatical features (a difference in effect size of .06)."
