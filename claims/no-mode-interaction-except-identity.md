## Evidence

### Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L 2021

Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L. (2021). Serving Children and Adolescents in Need during the COVID-19 Pandemic: Evaluation of Service-Learning Subjects with and without Face-to-Face Interaction. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18042114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA results on 216 matched cases. The article reports "no interaction effects between change and service mode (FTF vs. nFTF) in all variables except clear and positive identity." Table 4 shows the interaction F(1214) = 5.19, p < 0.05 for clear and positive identity, but no effect size is printed for this interaction.

> "However, there were no interaction effects between change and service mode (FTF vs. nFTF) in all variables except clear and positive identity. Students taking nFTF subject showed greater positive change in clear and positive identity than students taking FTF subject."
