Evidence¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin 2025¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin. (2025). Exploring the role of synchrony in asynchronous, synchronous, and quasi-synchronous online learner engagement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10504-y

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Combined SNA and qualitative content analysis of debate discourse across the three modalities; the article also reports "the last 30 of the total 54 interactions were entirely student–student with no declaratives" in the second quasi-synchronous grouping.

"While the latter two modalities had a more even distribution of declarative statements throughout the debate, the quasi-synchronous debate had declarative statements that were skewed towards the beginning despite having a ratio of declarative/non-declarative statements that was between asynchronous (with the lowest percentage of declaratives) and synchronous (with the highest)."
