## Evidence

### Cele 2026

Cele, W.D., Mbeje, P.N. & Mhlongo, E.M., 2026, 'Factors influencing the competence of student midwives in symphysis-fundal height measurement: A quantitative study', Curationis 49(1), a2855. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v49i1.2855

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 184 student midwives and community service nurses using a self-administered questionnaire; simple descriptive univariate analysis reported frequencies and percentages with no statistical tests. In this study "50.5% (n = 93) agreeing and 23.9% (n = 44) strongly agreeing that communication was effective".

> "Regular communication and constructive feedback from clinical lecturers were perceived positively, with 50.5% (n = 93) agreeing and 23.9% (n = 44) strongly agreeing that communication was effective."
