## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2020

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2020). Student behavior and test security in online conceptual assessment. arXiv:1912.05698v1 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of start-to-submit duration versus score for 200 respondents with time data. The article reports small, statistically insignificant Spearman correlations for both raw total time and time minus hidden-browser time, consistent with paper-based RBAs.

> "For both total time and time minus loss of focus, the correlation with score on the assessment was small ( r = 0.01 and r = 0 .1 respectively) and statistically insignificant."
