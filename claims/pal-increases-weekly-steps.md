## Evidence

### Bacon Paul 2021

Bacon Paul, Lord Rachel N. (2021). The impact of physically active learning during the school day on children's physical activity levels, time on task and learning behaviours and academic outcomes. Health Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyab020

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Accelerometer (Moki) step counts recorded for 7 days per week across a counterbalanced 2-week within-class comparison of PAL and NAL weeks; paired samples t-test showed the PAL week was "significantly (P = 0.003) more active" than the NAL week. Means are printed as above; no effect size reported.

> "Mean cumulative weekly steps indicated that in PAL week, children were significantly (P = 0.003) more active (196 542 ± 18 466 steps per week) than in the NAL week (152 395 ± 23 512 steps per week"
