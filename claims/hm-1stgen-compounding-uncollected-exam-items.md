### Cassandra Paul 2026

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, and Mary Kate Chessey. (2026). Missing Data on Physics Exams: Demographic Patterns, Course-Level Predictions, and Implications for Equity. [doi:10.1103/b4q5-dkn5](https://doi.org/10.1103/b4q5-dkn5)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling of CourseI class databases fitting the average fraction of blank answers for ten demographically-defined groups, with standard errors including student and class variation. The analysis found that "exam items are twice as likely to be uncollected for 1stGen students be- longing to HM groups compared with non-1stGen stu- dents belonging to non-HM groups." A similar result holds in CourseII.

> "For example, exam items are twice as likely to be uncollected for 1stGen students be- longing to HM groups compared with non-1stGen stu- dents belonging to non-HM groups."
