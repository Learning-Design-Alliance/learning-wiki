## Evidence

### Xu Binbin 2026

Xu Binbin, Wang Yan, Mo Yun, Zhang Rong, Chen Hongyan, Zhou Xuezhi, Hu Li. (2026). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge on peripherally inserted central catheter care in Guizhou, China: A web-based cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55786-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Separate multiple linear regressions for secondary/primary (n = 1957) and tertiary (n = 1571) hospitals. The quote reports the secondary/primary coefficients; the tertiary model additionally showed significant associations for workplace guidelines and self-reported training need, which were non-significant in secondary/primary hospitals.

> "In secondary/primary hospitals, significant predictors of higher knowledge scores included being a supervision nurse/co-chief nurse/chief nurse (B = 4.147, 95% CI: 2.567 to 5.727, p < 0.001), holding an undergraduate degree or higher (B = 2.204, 95% CI: 1.002 to 3.406, p < 0.001), having experience in conducting PICC care (B = 4.716, 95% CI: 3.405 to 6.026, p < 0.001), and having experience in attending PICC care training (B = 1.731, 95% CI: 0.457 to 3.005, p = 0.008)"
