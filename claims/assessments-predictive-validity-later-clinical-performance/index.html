Evidence¶

McLachlan JC 2025¶

McLachlan JC, Auta A, Ajiboye A. (2025). Assessment and evaluation of prescribing competences: A systematic review and recommendations. British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.16033

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's narrative synthesis of predictive validity studies (peer ratings, prescribing indicators, supervisor ratings, disciplinary outcomes) concludes that "written tests of declarative knowledge, practical tests such as OSCEs, and Situational Judgement Tests all have predictive validity for later clinical practice."

"The unequivocal conclusion that can be drawn is that written tests of declarative knowledge, practical tests such as OSCEs, and Situational Judgement Tests all have predictive validity for later clinical practice."
