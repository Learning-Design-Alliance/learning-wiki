## Evidence

### Isaacs 2025

Isaacs, J., Kuhfeld, M., Postell, S., & Hashim, A. (2025). Post-Pandemic School Closure Trends: Schools Serving Black Students Disproportionately Closed. NWEA White Paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/post-pandemic-school-closure-trends-schools-serving-black-students-disproportionately-closed/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of NCES Common Core of Data school-level records for brick-and-mortar traditional public schools, 2016-17 to 2024-25. The paper reports "between 671 to 1,174 schools closed per year, displacing between 100,000 to 250,000 students each year."

> "The school closure rate was highest in 2017-18 (1.3% of schools) and dropped steadily to a low point in 2022-23 (.7% of schools). The rate increased slightly to .9% of schools during the 2023-24 and 2024-25 school years."
