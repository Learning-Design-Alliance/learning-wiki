## Evidence

### Jin Hye Kyung 2024

Jin Hye Kyung, Lee Ha Eun, Kim EunYoung. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4 in national licensing examinations for medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05944-8

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias assessment of the meta-analysis using funnel plots, Egger's regression test, trim-and-fill, and Rosenthal's fail-safe N (calculated as 310). Egger's test was significant while trim-and-fill omitted no studies; the authors conclude bias does not appear likely.

> "Egger's regression test resulted in a significant p-value (p = 0.028), suggesting the presence of publication bias in the meta-analysis. Conversely, the trim-and-fill analysis revealed that no studies were omitted or required trimming"
