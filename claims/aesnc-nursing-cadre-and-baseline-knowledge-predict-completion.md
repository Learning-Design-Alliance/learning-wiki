`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the observational study; table 3 reports nursing officer univariate OR 3.4 (1.4 to 8.1) and adjusted OR 5.6 (1.8 to 18.1) versus medical officers. Facility and previous newborn training (ENC or HBB) were not associated with completion.

> "On multivariate modelling, nursing officers, clinical officers and 'other personnel were significantly associated with better initial learning completion or persistent activity when compared with medical officers adjusting for facility, clinical experience and previous newborn training (table 3)."
