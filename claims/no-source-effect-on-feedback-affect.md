### Jacobsen 2025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression models of emotional responses to feedback in the full-sample perception analyses. The article suggests the scenario-based administration may have attenuated affective responses, possibly contributing to the absence of source effects.

> "Neither feedback source, recognition accuracy, nor their interaction was significantly associated with positive affect (all ps ≥ .083) or negative affect (all ps ≥ .058). Coded feedback quality was associated with both (positive affect: b = 1.29, p = .009; negative affect: b = −1.09, p = .007)."
