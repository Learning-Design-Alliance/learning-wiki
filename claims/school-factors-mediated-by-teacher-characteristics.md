# School-related factors influence teachers' digital data use only indirectly, mediated by teacher will-skill-tool characteristics, in a model explaining 50% of variance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` School-related factors (ICT importance, leader support, goal clarity, formal and informal collaboration) had a tiny direct impact on digital data use but significant indirect effects through teacher beliefs, skill, and tool availability. [→ Michos 2025](#michos-2025)
`q3 i?` The mediation model explained 50% of the variance in digital data use, with school factors influencing teacher skill and tool availability most strongly. [→ Michos 2025](#michos-2025)

## Evidence

### Michos 2025

Michos, K., Schmitz, M.-L., & Petko, D. (2025). Learning Analytics in Schools: Is Digital Data Use Influenced by Teacher-Level or School-Level Factors? Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(3), 87–101. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8567

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent one-level SEM with cluster robust standard errors (lavaan) on the same survey; fit indices reported as TLI = 0.951, CFI = 0.960, RMSEA = 0.048, SRMR = 0.043. "all the indirect effects were significant, demonstrating that school-related factors indirectly affect digital data use by teachers" (total standardized indirect effect .31).

> "The R 2 was 0.5, indicating that 50% of the variance in digital data use could be attributed to school -related factors mediated by teacher characteristics (will-skill-tool)"
