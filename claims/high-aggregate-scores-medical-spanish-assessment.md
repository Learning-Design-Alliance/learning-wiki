### Mai AM 2025

Mai AM, Molina RL, Aguayo-Mazzucato C, Diaz AA, Canenguez K, Cheung HC, Graupera NR, Martel B, Bonilla S. (2025). A Standardized Clinical Case-Based Assessment for Evaluating Medical Students' Oral Spanish Communication Skills. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11518

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course assessment in Fall 2021 with 28 students (13 Intermediate, 15 Advanced) evaluated by facilitators using a 20-item binary checklist during standardized patient encounters. The article reports "high aggregate performance scores, at or above 80%" and a between-group difference (p = .0001); no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, students in both the Intermediate and Advanced levels achieved high aggregate performance scores, at or above 80%. Mean scores differed between the two groups (p = .0001)."
