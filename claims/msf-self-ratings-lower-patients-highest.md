## Evidence

### Carenzo L 2021

Carenzo L, Cena T, Carfagna F, Rondi V, Ingrassia PL, Cecconi M, Violato C, Della Corte F, Vaschetto R. (2021). Assessing anaesthesiology and intensive care specialty physicians: An Italian language multisource feedback system. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250404

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-source comparison of mean item scores from the same 64-resident MSF administration. Self-assessment "means ranging from 3.1" (leadership item) "to 4.30" (respecting patients' rights and privacy), below colleague and coworker ratings on every item.

> "The self-assessment scores the participants provided were lower on all items when compared to the same questions answered by medical colleagues and non-medical co-workers, with means ranging from 3.1 for the item on being able to step up as a leader when deemed necessary to 4.30 for respecting the rights and privacy of patients."
