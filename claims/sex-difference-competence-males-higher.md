## Evidence

### Liu Fengqiong 2025

Liu Fengqiong, Huang Shuqian, Chen Fa, Yang Suhua, Wu Lixin, Lin Yulan, He Baochang, Hu Zhijian. (2025). Development of a core competence model for improving medical college students’ ability in respond to public health emergencies. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1467832

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of score distributions by sex in the 441-student survey (168 male, 273 female); Table 3 shows significant differences in every domain (p = 0.003 to 0.015). Only p-values and means are printed, no effect size.

> "Male participants exhibited better performance than females in all the four competence domains, which were with an average total score of 94.58 and 90.66 (p = 0.002) respectively."

### Liu Fengqiong 2025 (2)

Liu Fengqiong, Huang Shuqian, Chen Fa, Yang Suhua, Wu Lixin, Lin Yulan, He Baochang, Hu Zhijian. (2025). Development of a core competence model for improving medical college students’ ability in respond to public health emergencies. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1467832

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the survey data identified sex and admission year as independent factors; the standardized coefficient for sex on total competence is −0.141 with p = 0.003, as printed in Table 5.

> "The β of association between sex and overall performance is −0.141 (p = 0.003)"
