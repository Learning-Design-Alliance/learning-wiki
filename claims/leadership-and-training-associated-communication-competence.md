### Yu Jiaying 2026

Yu Jiaying, Zhu Chengyu, Ren Yuwen, Fan Mingna & Yang Lili (2026). Alexithymia and clinical communication competence in nursing interns in China with the mediating role of self-efficacy. Scientific Reports 16, 10615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44952-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate analysis (t-tests, one-way ANOVA, rank-sum tests) of the 208-intern survey reported with Table 1; only a P threshold is printed, so no effect size is available for this finding.

> "The univariate analysis revealed that only student leadership experience and the number of clinical communication skills training sessions showed statistically significant associations with clinical communication competence subscales (P < 0.05). In contrast, other demographic factors, including "First Internship" (P > 0.05 for all subscales), were not significant."
