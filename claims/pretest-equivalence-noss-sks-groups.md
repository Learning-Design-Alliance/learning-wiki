# Experimental and control groups did not differ significantly on pre-test NOS or SK scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-test NOSS (t = .446) and SKS (t = .931) differences between groups were not statistically significant (p > 0.05). [→ Mustafa Metin 2022](#mustafa-metin-2022)

## Evidence

### Mustafa Metin 2022

Mustafa Metin. (2022). Comparing Effects of Two Different Explicit–Reflective Instructions on Pre-School Prospective Teachers' View about Nature of Science and Scientific Knowledge. Journal of Science Learning, 5(1), 165-175. https://doi.org/10.17509/jsl.v5i1.33190

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on pre-test scores in the quasi-experimental study (n = 66 total); NOSS showed no significant difference (t = .446; p > 0.05), and the article likewise reports the SKS pre-test difference (t = .931; p > 0.05) as not significant.

> "While pre-test mean of experimental group is higher than the control group’s (x̄Experimental = 87.09; x̄Control = 86.53;) in NOSS, there is not statistically a significant difference between pre-test means of experimental and control groups (t = .446; p > 0.05)."
