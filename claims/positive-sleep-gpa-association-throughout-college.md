## Evidence

### Zhao Yao 2026

Zhao Yao, Zhou Haoyu. (2026). Functional data analysis of college students’ sleep patterns and their relationships with academic performance and social networks: A four-year longitudinal study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0351120

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Concurrent functional linear regression linking semester GPA to daily sleep functions in the 76-student sample shows the coefficient positive at all time points and significant at 81.6%; the average association is "27.4 additional minutes of sleep per night (95% CI: 12.3 to 42.5 minutes)". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The coefficient remained positive throughout the entire study period (100% of time points), with 81.6% of time points showing statistically significant positive associations (95% CI excluding zero). On average, each one-point increase in GPA was associated with 27.4 additional minutes of sleep per night (95% CI: 12.3 to 42.5 minutes)."
