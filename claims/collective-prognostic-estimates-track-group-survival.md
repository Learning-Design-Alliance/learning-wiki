## Evidence

### Kupf Irma 2024

Kupf Irma, Thanner Gabriele, Gerken Michael, Crispin Alexander, Braess Jan. (2024). How accurate is clinical prognostication by oncologists during routine practice in a general hospital and can it be improved by a specific prognosis training programme: a prospective interventional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-081661

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group-level calibration analysis of the 800 phase 3 probabilistic estimates, tested with a χ2 test for linear trend across estimated-likelihood groups; the article reports p<0.0001 with no effect size, and calls this the wisdom of the clinical crowd.

> "a predicted higher likelihood of survival (eg, 'group 90%' vs 'group 60%') also correlated with a higher probability of real survival in these groups in a similar magnitude (p<0.0001)"
