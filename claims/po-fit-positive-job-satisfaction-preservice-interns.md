## Subclaims
`q2 i?` P-O fit significantly positively predicts teachers' job satisfaction (β = 0.259 with mediators; β = 0.631 without). [→ Xing Lu 2026](#xing-lu-2026)

## Evidence

### Xing Lu 2026

Xing Lu, Wu Xueqi. (2026). Influence of person-organization fit on job satisfaction among pre-service preschool teachers of China: Mediation of teaching self-efficacy and perceived teacher competence. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0351149

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 493 valid survey responses from pre-service preschool teachers; the direct path from P-O fit to job satisfaction remained significant, "supporting H1". Without mediators the standardized path was β = 0.631.

> "Finally, P-O Fit maintained a significant direct effect on Teachers' Job Satisfaction (β = 0.259, p < .001) even after accounting for the mediators, supporting H1."
