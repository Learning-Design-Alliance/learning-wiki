## Evidence

### Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Final-block consistency (proportion of responses accounted for by the best-fitting strategy) compared across tasks in Experiment 1, excluding random-strategy users (N = 6); between-task difference was negligible (d = 0.13) while the cross-task correlation was moderate (r = 0.43); replicated in Experiment 2 (r = 0.47, t(83) = 0.84, p = 0.40).

> "there were no signi ﬁcant differences in strategy consistency across RB and II tasks ( t(79) = 1.15, p = 0.25, d = 0.13, 95% CI [ −1.43, 5.38]) and consistency was moderately correlated across the in RB and II tasks ( r(78) = 0.43, 95% CI [0.23, 0.59], p < 0.001)"
