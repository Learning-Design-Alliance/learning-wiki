# Performance trajectories diverge over time: the simultaneous condition improves after instruction while sequential conditions hold or decline

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant condition × time point interaction shows the timing conditions followed different trajectories across the three sessions. [→ Congdon Eliza L. 2017](#congdon-eliza-l-2017)
`q3 i?` Within the simultaneous condition, performance improved significantly from immediate test to next-day follow-up; in the sequential conditions performance did not improve and by some measures worsened. [→ Congdon Eliza L. 2017 (2)](#congdon-eliza-l-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Congdon Eliza L. 2017

Congdon Eliza L., Novack Miriam A., Brooks Neon, Hemani-Lopez Naureen, O'Keefe Lucy, Goldin-Meadow Susan. (2017). Better together: Simultaneous presentation of speech and gesture in math instruction supports generalization and retention. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.005

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus mixed-effects model collapsed across problem type (Figure 3) testing condition, time point, and their interaction; only the interaction was significant, indicating diverging trajectories rather than an overall condition difference.

> "An initial model with condition, time point, and their interaction as fixed effects, and participant as a random effect, revealed a significant interaction between condition and time point (χ2 (4) = 19.99, p < .001). Neither the main effect of time point, nor the main effect of condition was significant on its own (time point: χ2 (2) = 4.68, p = .10; condition: χ2 (2) = 2.71, p =.26)."

### Congdon Eliza L. 2017 (2)

Congdon Eliza L., Novack Miriam A., Brooks Neon, Hemani-Lopez Naureen, O'Keefe Lucy, Goldin-Meadow Susan. (2017). Better together: Simultaneous presentation of speech and gesture in math instruction supports generalization and retention. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.005

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up logistic regressions per condition: the simultaneous group improved over time, whereas the speech-then-gesture group worsened significantly between immediate test and four-week follow-up (β = −0.67, p = .032) and the speech-then-speech group worsened at both delays.

> "Within the S G condition, children's performance improved over time: Children in this condition performed significantly better at next day follow-up than at immediate test (β = 0.82, z = 2.46, p = .01), and marginally better at four week follow-up than at immediate test (β = 0.64, z = 1.92 p = .055)."
