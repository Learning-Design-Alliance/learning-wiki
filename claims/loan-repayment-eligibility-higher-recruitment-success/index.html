Steven Glazerman 2005¶

Steven Glazerman, Neil Seftor. (2005). The NIH Intramural Research Loan Repayment Program: Career Outcomes of Participants and Nonparticipants. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-nih-intramural-research-loan-repayment-program-career-outcomes-of-participants-and-nonparticipants

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Evaluation report using new survey data comparing recruitment outcomes of program participants and nonparticipants; the report states that "eligibility for loan repayment is associated with higher recruitment success." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"The findings suggest that eligibility for loan repayment is associated with higher recruitment success and that participation in loan repayment programs is associated with longer stays at the NIH and in research careers more generally."
