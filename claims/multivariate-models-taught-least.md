Katy Klauenberg, Peter Harris, Philipp Möhrke and Francesca Pennecchi. (2025). The three most common needs for training on measurement uncertainty. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.2478/msr-2025-0029](https://doi.org/10.2478/msr-2025-0029)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Course survey and questionnaire data (Tables 1 and 2) for calibration laboratories show the multivariate extensions are "taught even less (<10 %)"; the article adds that because calibrations often imply correlation, awareness of joint modelling may need raising.

> "The multivariate extension of LPU and Monte Carlo is taught even less (<10 %), and the interest is medium and low, respectively – despite the need to account for correlations when measurands depend on common input quantities."
