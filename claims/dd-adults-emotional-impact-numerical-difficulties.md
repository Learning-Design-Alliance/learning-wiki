## Evidence

### Vigna Giulia 2022

Vigna Giulia, Ghidoni Enrico, Burgio Francesca, Danesin Laura, Angelini Damiano, Benavides-Varela Silvia, Semenza Carlo. (2022). Dyscalculia in Early Adulthood: Implications for Numerical Activities of Daily Living. Brain Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci12030373

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative description of psychological and clinical histories obtained through personal interviews with the DD patients. This is the authors' clinical annotation, not a tested comparison; patients reported these manifestations intensified negative academic outcomes and sometimes grounded need for psychotherapeutic treatment.

> "Clinical reports documented negative emotions of tension, worry, frustration, anger, and psychosomatic symptoms such as stomachache and insomnia, particularly before exams."
