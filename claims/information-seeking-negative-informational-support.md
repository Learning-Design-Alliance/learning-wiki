## Evidence

### Eric Richter 2022

Eric Richter, Jeffrey P. Carpenter, André Meyer, Dirk Richter. (2022). Instagram as a platform for teacher collaboration and digital social support. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104624

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of collaboration forms predicting digital social support in the 249-teacher cross-sectional sample. The article reports the negative informational-support link as counterintuitive and explains it via questionable information quality on social media and Instagram's limited hyperlink affordances.

> "The results reveal a significantly negative relationship between seeking information and informational support, but a significantly positive relationship between seeking information and instrumental support."
