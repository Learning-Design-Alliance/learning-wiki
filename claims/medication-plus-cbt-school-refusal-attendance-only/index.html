Evidence¶

Maynard 2015¶

Maynard, B. R., Brendel, K. E., Bulanda, J. J., Thompson, A. M., & Pigott, T. D. (2015). Psychosocial Interventions for School Refusal Behavior with Primary and Secondary School Students: A Campbell Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562325

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Separate meta-analysis of two RCTs (Bernstein et al., 2000; Wu et al., 2013) comparing CBT plus Fluoxetine or imipramine against CBT plus placebo. The review reports attendance effects "moderate, positive and statistically significant (g = 0.61 (95% CI [0.01, 1.21], p< .05)" and nonsignificant anxiety effects.

"effects on anxiety being small and not significant (g= -0.05, 95% CI [-0.40, 0.31], p> .05) and effects on attendance being moderate, positive and statistically significant (g = 0.61 (95% CI [0.01, 1.21], p< .05)."
