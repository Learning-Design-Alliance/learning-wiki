## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rina Oktaviyanthi 2015

Rina Oktaviyanthi, Yani Supriani. (2015). Experimental Design: Utilizing Microsoft Mathematics in Teaching and Learning Calculus. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.25. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078520

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire responses on mathematics confidence from both groups at the end of the lecture, using an instrument adapted from Fogarty et al. (2001); results are reported descriptively by category with no effect size.

> "According to the Figure6 mostly students in the control group had “very good” attitudes related to mathematics confident and generally students in the experimental group had “excellent” attitudes."

### Rina Oktaviyanthi 2015 (2)

Rina Oktaviyanthi, Yani Supriani. (2015). Experimental Design: Utilizing Microsoft Mathematics in Teaching and Learning Calculus. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.25. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078520

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Computer confidence questionnaire responses from both groups, again reported descriptively by category from Figure 7 with no statistical test or effect size printed.

> "Associating with computer confident, from the Figure7 can be noticed that commonly students in the control group had “very good” attitudes and in the main students of the experimental group had “excellent” attitudes."
