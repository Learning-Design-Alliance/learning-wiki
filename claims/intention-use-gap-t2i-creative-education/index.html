Evidence¶

Liu Y 2026¶

Liu Y, Meng M and Zhang Y (2026) The competence paradox: negotiating ease, risk, and creative identity in text-to-image generative AI use among art and design students. Front. Psychol. 17:1858187. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1858187

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Direct-effects result of the latent-variable SEM on the 400-student survey; the BI→UB path printed β = 0.202, SE = 0.104, p = 0.051, and H5 was reported as partially supported.

"BI showed only a weak and borderline association with UB, providing initial evidence that intention did not translate smoothly into self-reported use behavior in this context."

Liu Y 2026 (2)¶

Liu Y, Meng M and Zhang Y (2026) The competence paradox: negotiating ease, risk, and creative identity in text-to-image generative AI use among art and design students. Front. Psychol. 17:1858187. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1858187

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Coefficients of determination from the structural model (UB R² p = 0.01; PR R² p = 0.295). The article concludes the framework was "more eﬀective in explaining students' cognitive readiness" than enacted use frequency.

"The model explained 36.0% of the variance in behavioral intention (BI), 9.8% of the variance in self-reported use behavior (UB), and 3.7% of the variance in perceived risk (PR)"
