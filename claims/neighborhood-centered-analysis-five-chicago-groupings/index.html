A Neighborhood-Centered Approach to Understanding Pre-K Access in Chicago 2024¶

A Neighborhood-Centered Approach to Understanding Pre-K Access in Chicago. (2024). Start Early. https://www.startearly.org/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The brief reports a data-driven analysis of publicly available census data for Chicago that considered many neighborhood characteristics simultaneously, producing what the authors call "a parsimonious set of five neighborhoods groupings in Chicago". No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

"This “neighborhood-centered” (as opposed to a “variable-centered”) method resulted in a parsimonious set of five neighborhoods groupings in Chicago that focused researchers’ attention on the characteristics of neighborhood residents rather than the geographic locale of individual neighborhoods."
