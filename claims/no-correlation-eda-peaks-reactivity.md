International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 14:467–490. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09311-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null result from the same correlational analysis of 12 students' EDA peaks and coded monitoring reactions, in which the study found "no significant correlation between EDA peaks and reactivity"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Thus, monitoring activity was correlated with the number of EDA peaks, whereas there was no significant correlation between EDA peaks and reactivity."
