# Students' reactivity to monitoring events showed no significant correlation with EDA peaks.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no significant correlation between EDA peaks and reactivity (reactions to monitoring utterances). [→ Jonna Malmberg 2019](#jonna-malmberg-2019)

## Evidence

### Jonna Malmberg 2019

Jonna Malmberg, Eetu Haataja, Tapio Seppänen, Sanna Järvelä. (2019). Are we together or not? The temporal interplay of monitoring, physiological arousal and physiological synchrony during a collaborative exam. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 14:467–490. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09311-4

`q2 · i?`

Null result from the same correlational analysis of 12 students' EDA peaks and coded monitoring reactions, in which the study found "no significant correlation between EDA peaks and reactivity"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Thus, monitoring activity was correlated with the number of EDA peaks, whereas there was no significant correlation between EDA peaks and reactivity."
