Evidence¶

Hanna Onyi Yusuf 2013¶

Hanna Onyi Yusuf. (2013). Impact Of Using Joint Productive Activity Approach On Second Language Learners' Performance In Reading Comprehension At The Basic Education Level In Nigeria. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 4(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.4n.1p.51

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Control group of 20 students at GJSS Makera received normal reading comprehension lessons for six weeks. T-tests on the two tests gave t = 2.350 and t = 2.360 (df = 38), not significant at 0.05; means moved from 48.50/45.00 to 52.50/49.00. No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

"From table 4, the observed t-values were found not to be significant at 0.05. Hence one can say that the traditional method has no significant impact on performance of second language learners in reading comprehension."
