## Evidence

### Pilakouta 2011

Pilakouta, M. (2011). Statistical uncertainty in educational experiment on the attenuation of gamma radiation. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4056)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview observations of more than 40 students in the gamma-attenuation laboratory. The author reports that "They connect the uncertainty (“measurement error”) with the counter and not with the random nature of the emitted radiation", treating digital readings as exact values.

> "The majority of students do not seem to realize the source of the uncertainty in a counting experiment such as the radiation measurement. They connect the uncertainty (“measurement error”) with the counter and not with the random nature of the emitted radiation."
