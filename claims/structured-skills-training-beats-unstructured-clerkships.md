## Evidence

### Vogel Daniela 2016

Vogel Daniela, Harendza Sigrid. (2016). Basic practical skills teaching and learning in undergraduate medical education – a review on methodological evidence. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001063](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001063)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of the review's controlled studies. The authors state this as the one unrestricted recommendation, citing studies of physical examination training versus clerkship and injection and suturing training versus no specific program.

> "One recommendation that can be given unrestrictedly is, that providing a skills training of any sort seems to lead to better skills learning in undergraduate medical education than just participating in usually unstructured clerkships or bedside teaching"
