## Evidence

### Bracco Fabrizio 2017

Bracco Fabrizio, Masini Michele, De Tonetti Gabriele, Brogioni Francesca, Amidani Arianna, Monichino Sara, Maltoni Alessandra, Dato Andrea, Grattarola Claudia, Cordone Massimo, Torre Giancarlo, Launo Claudio, Chiorri Carlo, Celleno Danilo. (2017). Adaptation of non-technical skills behavioural markers for delivery room simulation. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1274-z

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Rating study with 86 healthcare workers (18 anaesthetists, 12 gynaecologists, 39 nurses/midwives, 17 assistants) in five 8-h simulation courses. Total-sample usefulness was 4.34 ± 0.65, with one-sample t-tests against 4; the article reports d = 0.52 for the total sample and d = 0.53 for midwives/nurses, d = 0.83 for assistants.

> "Taking into account the rating of usefulness in the total sample, one-sample t-tests showed that the ratings of midwives/nurses and healthcare assistants were statistically higher than 4, while anaesthetist and gynaecologist ratings were not statistically different to 4"
