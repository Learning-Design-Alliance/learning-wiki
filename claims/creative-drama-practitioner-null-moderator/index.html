Evidence¶

Özgür Ulubey 2018¶

Özgür Ulubey. (2018). The Effect of Creative Drama as a Method on Skills: A Meta-analysis Study. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1172791

q3 · i?

Moderator analysis comparing studies implemented by researchers (f=41) versus teachers (f=12) in the 63-study meta-analysis. The article reports a non-significant Qbetween value; equivalence was not tested, only no significant difference detected.

"It was also found that the effect size does not vary significan tly depending on the practitioner moderator variable (QB= .130; p> .05). The effect sizes found in the studies conducte d by both teachers (ES= 1.273) and researchers (ES=1.154) are large according to the Cohen’s (1988) classifica tion."
