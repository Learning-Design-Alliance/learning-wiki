# No existing online SRL questionnaire covers the breadth of motivational beliefs and learning strategies the SRL-O covers

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A review of existing online SRL measures found most focused on either motivational beliefs or strategies, usually not both, and none comprehensively covered motivational, emotional, and learning strategies for online and blended contexts. [→ Broadbent 2023](#broadbent-2023)

## Evidence

### Broadbent 2023

Broadbent, J., Panadero, E., Lodge, J. M., & Fuller-Tyszkiewicz, M. (2023). The self-regulation for learning online (SRL-O) questionnaire. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 135–163. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09319-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The authors' narrative review of existing online SRL questionnaires (Table 1 compares eleven measures including the MSLQ, OSLQ, OAHS, and SOL-Q) concluded that a key gap remains: none incorporate a comprehensive range of motivational, emotional, and learning strategies for online and blended contexts.

> "Despite the availability of a range of SRL self-report measures for online/blended contexts, a key gap remains: none incorporate a comprehensive range of motivational, emotional, and learning strategies specifically designed for online and blended learning contexts."
