A descriptive catalogue of publications across 11 top-tier LDT journals from 2011 to June 2025 identified "242 published reviews" and found the count "increased steadily over time," with the earliest publication in 2015; the authors attribute the trend to growing volume of primary studies.

"In total, we identified 242 published reviews (Table 1), including systematic reviews as well as a small number of meta-analyses, scoping reviews, narrative reviews, and umbrella reviews. The number of these studies has increased steadily over time (Fig. 1), and the earliest identified publication was in 2015."
