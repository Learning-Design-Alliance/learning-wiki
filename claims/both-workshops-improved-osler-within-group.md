Within-group pre-post comparisons in the RCT used the Wilcoxon signed-rank test; the article reports both groups "exhibited significant improvements in OSLER scores following the intervention (p < 0.001 for both, Wilcoxon test)". No effect size is printed.

> "The GD group improved from 4.38 ± 1.04 to 8.43 ± 1.85 (95% CI: 7.82–9.05; p < 0.001), while the VFB group showed a larger increase from 5.10 ± 2.09 to 17.44 ± 1.10 (95% CI: 17.08–17.79; p < 0.001)."
