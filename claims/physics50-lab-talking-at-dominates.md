## Evidence

### Eric Matthew Hickok 2016

Eric Matthew Hickok. (2016). Teaching practices of graduate teaching assistants. San José State University Master's Project. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational result from nine Physics 50 lab observations (Fig. 4.1), with 12.3% of time lecturing at whole class, 7.7% with individuals, and 11.6% with small groups. The author notes observing only the first hour may make TAs appear more interactive than over full sessions.

> "Over half my total observation time in Physics 50 was spent with TAs Not Interacting with their students. I should note here that the RIOT considers Observing an interaction. This means 55.2% of the time I spent observing the Physics 50 labs, the attention of the TAs was somewhere other than their students."
