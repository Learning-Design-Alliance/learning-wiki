# High heterogeneity was present among effect sizes for both academic achievement (I2≈85%) and learning retention (I2≈61%)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Q values exceeded critical chi-square values for both outcomes, with I2 of 85% for academic achievement and 61% for learning retention, indicating substantial between-study heterogeneity. [→ Tutal 2023](#tutal-2023)

## Evidence

### Tutal 2023

Tutal, Ö (2023). Does Context-Based Learning Increase Academic Achievement and Learning Retention?: A Review based on Meta-Analysis. Journal of Practical Studies in Education, 4(5), 1-16. https://doi.org/10.46809/jpse.v4i5.71

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Heterogeneity analysis of the included effect sizes (Table 1): academic achievement Q=362.626, df=53, p=0.000, I2=85.384; learning retention Q=28.632, df=11, p=0.003, I2=61.581. The high I2 values "indicated substantial levels of heterogeneity."

> "Furthermore, high I2 values of 85% and 61% indicated substantial levels of heterogeneity (Deeks et al., 2008)."
