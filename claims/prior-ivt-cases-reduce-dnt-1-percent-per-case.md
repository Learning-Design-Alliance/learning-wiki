## Evidence

### Ajmi Soffien 2024

Ajmi Soffien, Kurz Martin, Lindner Thomas Werner, Dalen Ingvild, Ersdal Hege Langli. (2024). Does clinical experience influence the effects of team simulation training in stroke thrombolysis? A prospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-086413

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted mixed effects linear regression on 148 patients treated before the quality improvement project (15 registrars), with logDNT and random intercepts per registrar. The adjusted estimate was "ΔDNT −1.1%; 95% CI, −2.2% to −0.0%; p=0.048"; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "After adjustment for patient age, hypertension, statin use and diabetes, the effect estimate was slightly lower (ΔDNT −1.1%; 95% CI, −2.2% to −0.0%; p=0.048)."
