Evidence¶

Roderick 2021¶

Roderick, M., & Kelley-Kemple, T., with Johnson, D. W., & Ryan, S. (2021). The Preventable Failure: Improvements in High School Graduation Rates when High Schools Focus on the Ninth-Grade Year. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-preventable-failure

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Consortium analysis of CPS cohort data reports that in 2005, 40 percent of first-time ninth-graders were off-track and "only 22 percent of these students graduated four years later," versus 81 percent of on-track students. The article reports the 3.5-times ratio but no standardized effect size.

"In 2005, 40 percent of CPS first-time ninth-graders were off-track at the end of ninth grade and only 22 percent of these students graduated four years later. In a dramatic contrast, 81 percent of students who were on-track at the end of ninth grade graduated within four years."
