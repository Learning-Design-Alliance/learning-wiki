## Evidence

### Noroozi 2012

Noroozi, O., Busstra, M. C., Mulder, M., Biemans, H. J. A., Tobi, H., Geelen, A., van't Veer, P., Chizari, M. (2012). Online discussion compensates for suboptimal timing of supportive information presentation in a digitally supported learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev (2012) 60:193-221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9217-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Concluding claim of the sequential experiment combining both phases: the compensatory intervention (online discussion with graphical knowledge maps) neutralized the limitation of the information-before timing condition, consistent with the article's stated design rationale that a complementary intervention can compensate for a first intervention's limitations.

> "Online discussion in CSCL appeared to compensate for suboptimal timing of presentation of supportive information before the learning task clusters in IDLM."
