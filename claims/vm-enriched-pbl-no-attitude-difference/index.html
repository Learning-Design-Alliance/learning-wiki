Çakıroğlu 2014¶

Çakıroğlu, Ü. (2014). Enriching project-based learning environments with virtual manipulatives: A comparative study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 55, 201-222. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2014.55.12

q2 · i? · causal · r2

In the quasi-experimental comparison, the Mathematics Attitude Questionnaire (38 items, five-point Likert) was administered before and after the six-week project period. The independent t-test on post-attitude scores found no significant difference between groups; Table 5 reports t(58)=1.70, p=.093.

"Also, after the intervention, no significant difference occurred among the EG and the CG [ t(58) = 1.70, p <.05]."
