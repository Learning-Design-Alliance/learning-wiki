# Burnout dimensions (exhaustion and disengagement) negatively predict future teacher self-efficacy at adjacent time points

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Disengagement at one time point negatively predicts TSE at the next time point. [→ Kim 2020](#kim-2020)

## Evidence

### Kim 2020

Kim, Lisa E. and Burić, Irena. (2020). Teacher self-efficacy and burnout: Determining the directions of prediction through an autoregressive cross-lagged panel model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000424

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged SEM paths in the final covariate-adjusted TSE–Disengagement model. The article reports "disengagement at Time 1 negatively predicted TSE at Time 2" with β = -.120 and β = -.180 for the second interval, both statistically significant.

> "disengagement at Time 1 negatively predicted TSE at Time 2 (β = -.120, p < .001) and disengagement at Time 2 negatively predicted TSE at Time 3 (β = -.180, p < .001)"
