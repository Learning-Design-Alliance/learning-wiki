# Task complexity strongly affects germane cognitive load in Round 1, with effects dissipating by post-test

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ines Zeitlhofer 2024

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach, Judith Schweppe. (2024). Complexity affects performance, cognitive load, and awareness. Learning and Instruction 94 (2024) 102001. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102001

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Univariate analyses in the N = 98 experiment, with GCL measured by the Naïve Rating Questionnaire, showed "a strong effect of task complexity on GCL in Round 1" with printed effect size η2 = 0.19, and a small effect in Round 2 with η2 = 0.05.

> "Addressing the impact of task complexity on GCL univariate analysis revealed a strong effect of task complexity on GCL in Round 1 (F (1,84) = 19.14, p < .001, η2 = 0.19) and a small positive effect in Round 2 (F (1,84) = 4.12, p < .05, η2 = 0.05)."

### Ines Zeitlhofer 2024 (2)

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach, Judith Schweppe. (2024). Complexity affects performance, cognitive load, and awareness. Learning and Instruction 94 (2024) 102001. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102001

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

At the delayed post-test the GCL difference between the consistently high and gradually increasing groups was not significant, with printed η2 = 0.00.

> "However, in the post-test, the difference between groups was not significant (F (1,84) = 0.11, p > .95, η2 = 0.00)."
