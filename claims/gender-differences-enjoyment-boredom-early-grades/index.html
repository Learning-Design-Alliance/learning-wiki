Evidence¶

Rawlings 2023¶

Rawlings, A. M., Niemivirta, M., Korhonen, J., Lindskog, M., Tuominen, H., & Mononen, R. (2023). Achievement emotions and arithmetic fluency – Development and parallel processes during the early school years. Preprint Version 3, Accepted for publication, 27 March 2023. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101776

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Gender added as a covariate in the final growth model of the 232-student Norwegian sample. The article reports the printed coefficients for initial enjoyment and boredom; no effect size is printed, and gender was unrelated to changes in emotions over time.

"Compared with boys, girls reported higher initial levels of mathematics enjoyment (β = -.35, p = .027), whereas boys reported higher initial mathematics boredom (β = .50, p = .001)."
