### Roze des Ordons Amanda 2017

Roze des Ordons Amanda, Kassam Aliya, Simon Jessica (2017). Goals of care conversation teaching in residency – a cross-sectional survey of postgraduate program directors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0839-2

Thematic analysis of free-text survey responses, coded inductively by one investigator, identified four themes beginning with "general perceptions of goals of care conversations, need for goals of care conversation teaching", plus ideas and assessment.

### Roze des Ordons Amanda 2017 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Program director comments and the discussion report that "Many program directors identified formal goals of care conversation teaching as a gap in their curricula", while others perceived such teaching as already well-integrated.

> "Many program directors identified formal goals of care conversation teaching as a gap in their curricula; others perceived that such teaching is already well-integrated into their respective programs."
