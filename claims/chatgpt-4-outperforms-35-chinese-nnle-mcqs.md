## Evidence

### Wu Zelin 2024

Wu Zelin, Gan Wenyi, Xue Zhaowen, Ni Zhengxin, Zheng Xiaofei, Zhang Yiyi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on Nursing Licensure Examinations in the United States and China: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/52746

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of 235 NNLE theoretical and 233 NNLE practical Chinese MCQs (2021-2022 exams, image questions excluded) input to ChatGPT 4.0 and ChatGPT 3.5. The article reports correct rates of "71.9% (169/235) and 69.1% (161/233)" for ChatGPT 4.0, with P<.001 for both comparisons.

> "The correct rates of ChatGPT 4.0 answering NNLE theoretical MCQs and NNLE practical MCQs were 71.9% (169/235) and 69.1% (161/233), respectively, compared with 53.2% (125/235) and 50.2% (117/233) for ChatGPT 3.5"
