`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' stated limitation in the conclusion, citing reference [7]; no data on retention are reported in this article. The authors add that evaluations showed the two half days were "felt to be too dense for many students", and they aim to return to weekly teaching over 8 weeks.

> "A limitation of the adapted course concept might probably be the fact that long term retention is better when skills are learned and repeatedly trained over a longer time frame [7]."
