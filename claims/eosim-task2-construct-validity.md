## Evidence

### Leijte Erik 2019

Leijte Erik, Arts Elke, Witteman Bart, Jakimowicz Jack, De Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne. (2019). Construct, content and face validity of the eoSim laparoscopic simulator on advanced suturing tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06652-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct-validity analysis (69 participants performed Task 2: 39 novices, 21 target, 9 experts) using one-way ANOVA. The results section reports significant differences in "time (p < 0.001), distance (p = 0.003), instruments off-screen (p < 0.001) and working area (p < 0.001)"; post hoc tests showed expert-versus-novice and expert-versus-target differences for off-screen and working area.
