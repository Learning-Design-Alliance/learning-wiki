Finch, J. E., Obradović, J., & Yousafzai, A. (2016). Home environment quality mediates the effects of an early intervention on children's social-emotional development in rural Pakistan. SREE Spring 2016 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567225

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Path analysis of the PEDS four-year follow-up (N = 1302). Home environment quality at 48 months, measured with the HOME Inventory, showed the stated concurrent associations with both SDQ-derived outcomes, controlling for all covariates. Home environment at 24 months also predicted social skills (β = 0.101, p =.007).
