Sophia Hannou; Cristina Nicorici; Wanda Bosshard; Pierre Voirol; Farshid Sadeghipour; Nancy Perrottet; Chantal Csajka. (2025). Opioid Room of Horrors: a simulation approach to strengthen drug administration safety. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2025-003728

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-sample tests of proportions comparing 62 nurse/assistant pairs with 24 physician pairs on error, hazard and double-check items (p<0.05 threshold); table 3 prints p=0.048 for expired drug, p=0.019 for allergy, p=0.0001 for syringe use.

> "Performance varied by professional role: nurses and healthcare assistants were more diligent in verifying expiry dates and selecting the correct syringe, while physicians were more likely to identify documented allergies. The double-check performance did not differ significantly between professional profiles."
