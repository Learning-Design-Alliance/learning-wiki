# Self-explanation-based and inquiry learning environments elicit significantly more total learning-process instances than hypermedia and observational learning environments

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Learners in the self-explanation-based and inquiry learning environments had significantly more identified instances of engagement in learning processes than learners in the hypermedia and observational learning environments (all ps < .001). [→ Tessa H. S. Eysink & Ton de Jong 2012](#tessa-h-s-eysink-ton-de-jong-2012)

## Evidence

### Tessa H. S. Eysink & Ton de Jong 2012

Tessa H. S. Eysink & Ton de Jong (2012). Does Instructional Approach Matter? How Elaboration Plays a Crucial Role in Multimedia Learning. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2011.611776

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Experiment with 40 Dutch Grade 10 students randomly assigned to four conditions who thought aloud while working through one multimedia environment on probability theory. The ANOVA on total coded learning-process instances showed "F(3, 36) = 43.47, p < .001, partial η2 = .78".

> "A univariate analysis of variance showed a signiﬁcant difference between the average number of total identiﬁed instances of engagement in learning processes in each instructional approach, F(3, 36) = 43.47, p < .001, partial η2 = .78."
