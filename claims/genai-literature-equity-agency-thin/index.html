Systematic review of 67 publications on AI and GenAI literacy (2023–2025), each coded against five a priori concept-analysis dimensions (knowledge, skill, ethics, equity, agency). Table 1 reports knowledge at 65 studies (97%), skill at 64 (96%), ethics at 66 (99%), equity at 49 (73%), agency at 40 (60%). "Coverage focuses on knowledge and skills, while equity and agency receive substantive treatment in a minority of studies."

"Coverage focuses on knowledge and skills, while equity and agency receive substantive treatment in a minority of studies, the systematic thinness that RAIL-Ed is designed to address."
