## Evidence

### Brant E. Hinrichs 2005

Brant E. Hinrichs. (2005). Using the System Schema Representational Tool to Promote Student Understanding of Newton's Third Law. [doi:10.1063/1.2084715](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2084715)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched pre/post comparison of two composite cohorts from the same instructor (Workshop Physics N=31 in 1999-2000; modeling/system schema N=28 in 2002-03) on FCI third-law items; post-test means were 2.8 vs 3.7 and the t-test gave "p=.0003 at the 95% confidence level".

> "It indicated that we may safely reject the null-hypothesis that the difference in the means is due to random chance (p=.0003 at the 95% confidence level). That is, the difference is statistically significant."
