## Evidence

### Jérôme Brender 2026

Jérôme Brender, Laila El-Hamamsy, Kim Uittenhove, Aitor Perez, Patrick Jermann, Francesco Mondada, and Engin Bumbacher. (2026). Reflective Dialogue or Prompt Refinement? Effects of Tutor Scaffolding on Students' Independent LLM Use for Programming. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_37](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_37)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster analysis of 1120 project-phase prompts from 52 students using the unconstrained chatbot identified three prompting patterns; cluster membership differed significantly by prior tutor condition (χ² = 8.14, p = 0.017). The authors state these differences "emerged only in the unconstrained setting."

> "the distribution of students across clusters differed significantly by condition (χ 2 = 8.14,p= 0.017). While the number ofImplementerswas similar across conditions (n SG = 14,n P R = 11), students from the SG condition were more likely to enactUnderstanding-drivenprompting patterns (nSG = 13,n P R= 4), whereas students from the PR condition were more likely to enactDebuggerpatterns (n P R= 8,n SG = 2)."
