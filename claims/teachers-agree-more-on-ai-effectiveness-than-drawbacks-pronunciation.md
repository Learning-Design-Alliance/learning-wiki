### Georgios P. Georgiou 2026

Georgios P. Georgiou. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Pronunciation Teaching: Use and Beliefs of Foreign Language Teachers. University of Nicosia. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/02607476.2026.2676279](https://doi.org/10.1080/02607476.2026.2676279)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal regression and Tukey-corrected posthoc comparisons of agreement ratings (1–7) across nine belief statements from the survey of 117 Cypriot EFL teachers. The posthoc tests showed "a higher degree of agreement for the former two clusters" (effectiveness and openness) versus drawbacks.

> "Subsequent posthoc tests with the Tukey correction revealed significant differences notably between the majority of the beliefs regarding perceived effectiveness (i.e., Beliefs 1–3), openness to incorporate AI (i.e., Beliefs 7–9), and beliefs regarding perceived drawbacks (i.e., Beliefs 4–6), with a higher degree of agreement for the former two clusters."
