## Evidence

### Zhao Xin 2019

Zhao Xin, Yu Xiaofeng, Zhang Xiaomei. (2019). The Role of Peer Support Education Model in Management of Glucose and Lipid Levels in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Chinese Adults. Journal of Diabetes Research. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/5634030

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis of a randomized two-group study of 120 T2DM outpatients, comparing baseline with 3 months. The article reports FBG and HbA1c were "significantly lower than those in the baseline after 3 months of intervention" with F = 21.33 and F = 39.27, both p < 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "The FBG and HbA1c levels in the two groups were significantly lower than those in the baseline after 3 months of intervention (F = 21.33, p < 0.05; F = 39.27, p < 0.05)."
