## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interns in patient and family roles described physical discomfort, emotional stress and anxiety, disorientation to the ED process, and examples of poor communication during the simulation. [→ Nelson SW 2018](#nelson-sw-2018)

## Evidence

### Nelson SW 2018

Nelson SW, Germann CA, MacVane CZ, Bloch RB, Fallon TS, Strout TD. (2018). Intern as Patient: A Patient Experience Simulation to Cultivate Empathy in Emergency Medicine Residents. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.11.35198

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus-group analysis of eight of 10 EM interns four months after the simulation, coded with a grounded theory approach and constant comparative method. Participants described "the physical discomfort and the emotional stress of the ED process" and feeling unfamiliar and confused. No effect sizes reported.

> "The intern participants discussed the physical discomfort and the emotional stress of the ED process. They described how the process felt unfamiliar and confusing and they speculated about how patients and families with little medical literacy would feel."
