### Li Chenrui 2026

Li Chenrui, Hu Fan, Qiao Yongxia, Zhang Meihui, Gao Quyige, Wang Fengyi, Du Naiya, Xia Yunyi, Pan Meizhu, Cai Yong, Wang Ying. (2026). The role of AI-mediated learning contexts in the personality–self-directed learning relationship among Chinese medical undergraduates: a cross-sectional study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression in the cross-sectional survey (N = 1,208) with four blocks: covariates (R² = 0.566), AI dimensions (ΔR² = 0.064), personality (ΔR² = 0.129), and interactions. The interaction block was "a statistically significant yet substantively small increment"; the full model explained 76.5% of variance.

> "Finally, the eight interaction terms in Block 4 added a statistically significant yet substantively small increment (ΔR2 = 0.005, p = 0.003, Cohen's f2 = 0.02). The fully specified model accounted for 76.5% of the variance in SDL (adjusted R2 = 0.758)."
