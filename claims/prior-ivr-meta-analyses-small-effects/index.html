Evidence¶

Jongbloed 2024¶

Jongbloed, J., Chaker, R., & Lavoué, E. (2024). Immersive procedural training in virtual reality: A systematic literature review. Computers & Education, 221, 105124. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105124

q2 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports these second-hand meta-analytic results, citing Wu et al. (2020) and Coban et al. (2022): "a small positive ES of g = 0.24" and "g = 0.38" respectively, and interprets its own larger effect as confirming procedural specificity.

"Wu et al. (2020) found a small positive ES of g = 0.24 and Coban et al. (2022) found a small positive ES of g = 0.38. Our larger ES of g = 0.58 confirms the hypothesis posed in several previous reviews that procedural skills respond particularly well to this type of training"
