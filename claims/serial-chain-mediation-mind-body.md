### Zhang Bao Gen 2026

Zhang Bao Gen, Qian Xiao Fang. (2026). Extracurricular physical activity and academic performance among Chinese adolescents with high body mass index: a longitudinal mediation analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47332-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chain mediation test in the 221-participant sample found the serial indirect effect significant ("β = 0.0117, 95% CI [0.0003, 0.0293]"). The authors note the chain effect is likely driven by the M1→M2 association rather than a direct activity effect on mental health.

> "The specific indirect effect for the serial pathway from physical activity through mental health to cognitive ability and then to academic performance (X → M1 → M2 → Y) was also statistically significant (β = 0.0117, 95% CI [0.0003, 0.0293])."
