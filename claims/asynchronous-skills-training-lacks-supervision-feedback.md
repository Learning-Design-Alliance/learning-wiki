## Evidence

### Ebrahim Sumayyah 2024

Ebrahim Sumayyah, Kinoo Suman Mewa, Naidoo Maheshwar, Van Wyk Jacqueline Marina. (2024). The use of household items to support online surgical knot-tying skills training: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05549-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group thematic analysis identified supervision and feedback gaps as the main challenge. Some students "expressed difficulty in translating the two-dimensional (2-D) effect of the video to a 3-D application of the skill" and wanted multiple video angles.

> "The main challenge related to the lack of direct supervision and provision of formal feedback to individual students on their attempts."
