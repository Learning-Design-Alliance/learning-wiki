Global anonymous online survey (March–May 2023, recruited via SurveySwap) of 406 adults from 60 countries, primarily Europe and North America. Participants rated 10 erroneous statements true/false; the mean score was 3.74 (SD = 1.99; 95% CI [3.55, 3.94]), and "only 5.7% (N = 23) of our participants cor- rectly rated all these statements as false".
