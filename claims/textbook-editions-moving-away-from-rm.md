An analysis of textbook editions (figure 5) categorized by College Board levels A-D found "only two have been found that moved from a posi- tion of not employing the concept of relativistic mass to utilizing it": Serway's Physics for Scientists and Engineers (3rd and 4th editions, containing contradicting statements about mass) and University Physics, which reversed its position twice.
