## Evidence

### Leidig 2018

Leidig, T., Grünke, M., Urton, K., Knaak, T., & Hisgen, S. (2018). The Effects of the RAP Strategy Used in a Peer-tutoring Setting to Foster Reading Comprehension in High-Risk Fourth Graders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 231-253. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194573

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests comparing reading comprehension at t1 and t2 for both groups during the initial RAP-only phase. Table 3 reports group 1: t=1.01, p=.34, d=.26; group 2: t=.54, p=.60, d=.18 — no statistically significant change.

> "As shown in Table 3, paired t-tests indicated no noteworthy enhancements between phases 1 and 2 (RAP strategy instruction without a motivational system) in the groups."
