# The DBN accommodation measure scores real conversation pairs higher than lexically matched constructed pairs, validating it as a measure of speech style accommodation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an ANOVA with conversation type nested within conversations, Real pairs scored significantly higher on accommodation than Constructed pairs, F(1, 76) = 1.88, p < .05. [→ Gweon 2013](#gweon-2013)

## Evidence

### Gweon 2013

Gweon, G., Jain, M., McDonough, J., Raj, B., & Rosé, C. P. (2013). Measuring prevalence of other-oriented transactive contributions using an automated measure of speech style accommodation. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2013). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-013-9172-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Validation experiment on the Ottoman Empire corpus: accommodation scores were computed for 38 Real pairs and matched Constructed pairs whose turns preserved lexical content but could not reflect accommodation. The ANOVA comparison was significant, "F(1, 76) = 1.88, p < .05".

> "The difference was significant F(1, 76) = 1.88, p < .05, with the average score for Constructed pairs being .52 with a standard deviation of .27, and for Real pairs .62 with a standard deviation of .31."
