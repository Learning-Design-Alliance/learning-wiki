## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among students with M+F > 125, Class 1 (1/3 lecture) students rated group problem solving as more helpful than Class 2 (1/2 lecture) students did (T-test p = 0.04408), while lecture satisfaction did not differ (p = 0.5397). [→ Heeseung Zoe 2014](#heeseung-zoe-2014)

## Evidence

### Heeseung Zoe 2014

Heeseung Zoe, Kyujin Kwak, Yoonjoo Cho, Yun-Young Seo, Jin-suk Choi, Jin-Hyouk Im, Soogyun Beum and Hai-Woong Lee. (2014). The role of face-to-face lecturing in flipped classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1404.3566](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1404.3566)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Questionnaire taken right before the final exam in Semester 2013, comparing satisfaction of the M+F > 125 groups (n = 31 Class 1, n = 54 Class 2). GPS satisfaction differed significantly between classes; lecture satisfaction did not (p = 0.5397).

> "However, for GPS, the p-value of the T-test is 0.04408 which implies that the two groups have diﬀerent preferences to the group activity."
