## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Veronica and Jan showed unsynchronized behaviors and contrasting, mutually resistant epistemological stances, with no group-level transitions, supporting an individual unit of analysis. [→ Conlin 2010](#conlin-2010)

## Evidence

### Conlin 2010

Conlin, L., Gupta, A., & Hammer, D. (2010). Where to find the mind: Identifying the scale of cognitive dynamics. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0491)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case analysis of a two-student disagreement about light through an aperture. Veronica sat up, gestured, and appealed to intuitive mechanism; Jan hunched over the worksheet and sought a 'physics-oriented' formal answer. The authors add that 'this stability persists on much larger timescales than one tutorial period,' citing Lising & Elby's semester-long analysis of Jan.

> "This lack of synchronicity and coordination among the behaviors suggests a clustering at the individual level in this case, not at the group level."
