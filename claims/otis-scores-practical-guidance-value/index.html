Evidence¶

Geyer 1921¶

Geyer, D. L. (1921). The Reliability of Rankings by Group Intelligence Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeduapsy

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The author's report of practical experience using Otis scores for course guidance in the junior high school of the Chicago Normal School. The classification proved workable with adjustments in cases of extreme divergence from scholarship records.

"For guiding the pupil in the choice between two studies—a foreign language and manual work—the Otis scores have proved by a two-year trial in the junior high school named above to be of real practical value."
