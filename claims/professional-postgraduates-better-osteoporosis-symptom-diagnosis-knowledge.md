## Evidence

### Ding Hong 2025

Ding Hong, Li He-Xi, Ai You-Wei, Zhang Qing-Yi, Tang Hai-Tao, Zeng Wen. (2025). Assessment of understanding, attitudes, and clinical practices regarding osteoporosis among postgraduate students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07785-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 97 postgraduates at West China School of Clinical Medicine, Sichuan University (June–August 2023), analyzed with Pearson's chi-square/Fisher's exact tests. Table 2 reports "46(83.6%)" vs 24(57.1%) for symptoms (p = 0.004), 34(61.8%) vs 14(33.3%) for diagnostic criteria (p = 0.005), and 44(80.0%) vs 22(52.4%) for dangers (p = 0.004).

> "Professional postgraduate students acquired a higher level of knowledge related to common symptoms, diagnostic criteria and the risks of osteoporosis than academic postgraduate students, which may be related to the involvement of professional postgraduate students in more clinical work."
