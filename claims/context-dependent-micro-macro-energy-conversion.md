## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2011

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Edward F. Redish, and Jessica Watkins. (2011). Students' Views of Macroscopic and Microscopic Energy in Physics and Biology. PERC 2011. [doi:10.1063/1.3680024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680024)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview data: despite earlier citing "different units" for kinetic and chemical energy, the student described a spark plug breaking gasoline bonds to turn the piston and a cannon converting "chemical energy... into kinetic energy." The authors note these examples arose in non-biological contexts. No effect size is reported.

> "he explains some phenomena in terms of microscopic energy converted to macroscopic energy.  It is notable that these examples come up in a non-biological, "physics" context"
