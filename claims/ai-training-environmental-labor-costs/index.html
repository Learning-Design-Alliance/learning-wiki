Evidence¶

Judi Fusco 2023¶

Judi Fusco, Pati Ruiz, Jeremy Roschelle. (2023). Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Teaching and Learning. Digital Promise presentation. https://tech.ed.gov/ai/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The presentation cites, without detail, a University of Massachusetts study on training energy use, alongside OECD and Dhar references on the AI footprint; the figures are reported second-hand with no independent analysis.

"a study at the University of Massachusetts to determine how much energy is used to train certain popular large AI models showed that training can produce: about 626,000 pounds of carbon dioxide, nearly 5 times the lifetime emissions of the average car"

Judi Fusco 2023 (2)¶

Judi Fusco, Pati Ruiz, Jeremy Roschelle. (2023). Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Teaching and Learning. Digital Promise presentation. https://tech.ed.gov/ai/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Under a Labor heading, the slides list journalistic headlines on exploited labor behind artificial intelligence, including the OpenAI Kenyan workers report; the presentation attributes these to news reports and conducts no analysis itself.

"Exclusive: OpenAI Used Kenyan Workers on Less Than $2 Per Hour to Make ChatGPT Less Toxic - January, 2023"
