Evidence¶

Madelon van den Boer 2022¶

Madelon van den Boer, Loes Bazen, Elise de Bree. (2022). The Same yet Different: Oral and Silent Reading in Children and Adolescents with Dyslexia. Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-022-09856-w

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' conclusion across Study 1 (children) and Study 2 (adolescents): the dyslexia deficit in silent reading matched or exceeded the oral deficit; for adolescents the silent deficit was "somewhat larger than in oral reading fluency".

"individuals with dyslexia show an impairment in silent reading that is as large as or larger than their impairment in oral reading"
