# Medical students' task completion times on all four trained laparoscopic tasks decreased significantly from pre-test to post-test after a hands-on laparoscopic training workshop, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Evidence

### Chen Pin-Chun 2024

Chen Pin-Chun, Yang Po-Wen, Kao Yi-Kai, Chen Chia-Hung, Tsai Chih-Jong, Chen Yi-Chieh, Song Ling-Chiao, Tsai Kai Lung, Wu Richard C., Chen Chih-I. (2024). Laparoscopic training workshop to assess medical students' skill acquisition and interest in surgical careers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05708-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-group experimental study of 44 medical students in Taiwan; paired t-tests compared pre- and post-workshop times. The article reports "The average decrease of 97 s was significant (p = 0.0015)" for Precision Bead Placement.

> "The average completion time decreased from 283.6 s (SD ± 38.6) during the pre-test to 186.6 s (SD ± 12.2) during the post-test. The average decrease of 97 s was significant (p = 0.0015)."

### Chen Pin-Chun 2024 (2)

Chen Pin-Chun, Yang Po-Wen, Kao Yi-Kai, Chen Chia-Hung, Tsai Chih-Jong, Chen Yi-Chieh, Song Ling-Chiao, Tsai Kai Lung, Wu Richard C., Chen Chih-I. (2024). Laparoscopic training workshop to assess medical students' skill acquisition and interest in surgical careers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05708-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same workshop study, Intracorporeal Suturing task: paired t-test showed the largest time reduction, "The average decrease of 137.8 s was significant (p < 0.0001)". Beads Transfer (88.5 s) and Precision Balloon Cutting (95 s) also decreased significantly.

> "The completion time decreased from an average of 516.4 s (SD ± 56.7) during the pre-test to 378.6 s (SD ± 28.6) during the post-test. The average decrease of 137.8 s was significant (p < 0.0001)."
