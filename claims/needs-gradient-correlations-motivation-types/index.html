Evidence¶

Bureau 2022¶

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analytic correlations (corrected for reliability, robust variance estimator) across the 144-sample database. Autonomy "related negatively with amotivation (ρ = −.34, k = 29), was unrelated to external regulation (ρ = −.04, k = 46)" and rose to ρ = .57 with intrinsic motivation; competence and relatedness showed the same ordering.

"autonomy related negatively with amotivation (ρ = −.34, k = 29), was unrelated to external regulation (ρ = −.04, k = 46), and correlated positively with introjected (ρ = .23, k = 42), identified (ρ = .48, k = 42), and intrinsic motivation types (ρ = .57, k = 52)"
