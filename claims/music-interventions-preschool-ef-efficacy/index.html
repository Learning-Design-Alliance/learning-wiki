Evidence¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin 2023¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin. (2023). Interventions and Approaches Targeting Early Self-Regulation or Executive Functioning in Preschools: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09740-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesis of six music intervention studies (average sample 77.17; average duration 14.20 h; all small-group, external-facilitator led; five included rhythmical movement). The review reports all studies had a significant outcome and "12 (52.2%)" of 23 effects were significant; four interventions met high-efficacy criteria.

"All studies reported at least one outcome that achieved statistical significance. Across the studies 23 effects were evaluated, of which 12 (52.2%) were significant."
