## Evidence

### Betti 2022

Betti, A., Biderbost, P., & García Domonte, A. (2022). Can active learning techniques simultaneously develop students’ hard and soft skills? Evidence from an international relations class. PLoS ONE 17(4): e0265408. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265408

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlations in the control group before the experiment (Table 6); the article reports significance but no effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect). After the experiment (Table 10) self-efficacy again correlated with final grades.

> "It shows that self-efficacy was the only soft skill that significantly correlated with three measurements of the hard skills, that is the average grade of the Degree in IR, the average grade of the Degree in BA, and the initial survey."
