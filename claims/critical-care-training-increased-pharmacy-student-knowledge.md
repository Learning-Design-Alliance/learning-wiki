## Evidence

### Moustafa HAM 2024

Moustafa HAM, Hamid AE, Hassoub G, Kassem AB. (2024). Assessing the impact of critical care training on pharmacy students in Egypt: a pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post interventional study of 72 pharmacy students in Egypt; Wilcoxon signed ranks test comparing pre and post knowledge. Table 2 reports overall knowledge 0.55 ± 0.08 pre vs 0.98 ± 0.02 post, p < 0.001, described as "significant increase from baseline to post-course".

> "their overall knowledge score significantly increased from baseline to post-course, with the median (IQR) rising from 0.53 (0.50–0.61) to 0.98 (0.97–1.0), with a p-value of less than 0.05"
