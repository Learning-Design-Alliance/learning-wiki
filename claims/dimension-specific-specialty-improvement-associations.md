## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After FDR correction, plastic surgery (β = -3.345) and cardiothoracic surgery (β = -1.996) showed less improvement in patient encounter scoring, plastic surgery greater improvement in specialty-specific clinical reasoning (β = 3.915), and neurosurgery greater improvement in communication skills (β = 4.400). ## Evidence

### Zhang Lina 2026

Zhang Lina, Yan Mengling, Qiu Yiwen, Cai Haolei, Yu Yunxian. (2026). Effectiveness of the “Level-Points” online training model and influencing factors in enhancing scoring accuracy among surgical examiners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10033-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Separate multivariable linear regressions per dimension, visualized in forest plots (Fig. 1A–E), on the 670-examiner cohort. "remained statistically significant" for these four specialty-by-dimension associations; unstandardised β coefficients only, no standardised effect size.

> "only plastic surgery in the patient encounter dimension (β = -3.345, se = 1.065, P = 0.002, adjusted P = 0.012) (Fig. 1A), cardiothoracic surgery in the patient encounter dimension (β = -1.996, se = 0.834, P = 0.017, adjusted P = 0.047) (Fig. 1A), plastic surgery in the specialty-specific clinical reasoning dimension (β = 3.915, se = 0.896, P < 0.001, adjusted P < 0.001) (Fig. 1B), and neurosurgery in the communication skills dimension (β = 4.400, se = 0.933, P < 0.001, adjusted P < 0.001) (Fig. 1E) remained statistically significant."
