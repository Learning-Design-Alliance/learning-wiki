# Pilot CoC schools did not improve faster than non-pilot CoC schools or non-CoC schools on any measure

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The six pilot schools, which received more money and support, did not differ in change on any measure from the other 41 CoC schools or from non-CoC schools between 2009 and 2011. [→ Rachel Levenstein 2011](#rachel-levenstein-2011)

## Evidence

### Rachel Levenstein 2011

Rachel Levenstein, Sue Sporte, Elaine Allensworth. (2011). Findings from an Investigation into the Culture of Calm Initiative. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/findings-investigation-culture-calm-initiative

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HLM analysis restricted to CoC schools compared the six pilot schools with the other 41 CoC schools, and separately with non-CoC schools; no significant differences in change were found for any measure. The authors attribute this partly to the small sample of six pilot schools.

> "Our suspicions were confirmed:  pilot schools did not differ in the change in any measure between 2009 and 2011 from non-pilot CoC schools.  Appendix C contains each of the trend lines."
