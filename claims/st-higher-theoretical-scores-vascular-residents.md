## Evidence

### Yang Lin 2020

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort of 95 residents (47 ST, 48 CT) at one Chinese centre; theoretical test (0–10 scale) at 1 month after training. Mean scores were "8.74±1.09 vs 8.13±1.31", a statistically significant difference (p=0.014). No effect size was printed.

> "the mean scores of the residents were higher in the ST group than in the CT group (8.74±1.09 vs 8.13±1.31, p=0.014)"
