## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2003

Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, and Edward F. Redish. (2003). Understanding And Affecting Student Reasoning About Sound Waves. International Journal of Science Education. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.1080/09500690305024](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500690305024)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Matched diagnostic data (Table 2, N=137) show longitudinal oscillation rising from 9% before instruction and 26% post-lecture to 45% post-tutorial, with pushed-away responses falling from 50% to 11%. The article reports "a much larger number of students are able to describe the correct motion"; no effect size is printed.

> "After the Tutorial, a much larger number of students are able to describe the correct motion of the oscillating dust particle."
