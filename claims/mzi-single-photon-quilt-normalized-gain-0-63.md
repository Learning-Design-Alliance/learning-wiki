`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Preliminary in-class evaluation: 18 upper-level undergraduates in a first-semester quantum mechanics course completed a pretest, worked through the QuILT in class (remainder as homework), then took a posttest with the same questions. The article states "The average normalized gain from pretest to posttest was 0.63 [9]."

> "it was administered to 18 upper-level undergraduate students in a first-semester quantum mechanics course. Students were first given the pretest. They then worked through the QuILT in class and were asked to complete whatever they could not finish in class as homework. Then, they were given a posttest"
