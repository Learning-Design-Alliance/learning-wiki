## Evidence

### A. Power 2026

A. Power, C. Lee, C. Corish, and A. Shaw. (2026). Registered Dietitians' Knowledge, Attitudes and Use of Simulation‐Based Education in Ireland: A Mixed‐Methods Study. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70326

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data from 15 dietitians analysed thematically; a participant stated they would "never like to see it used to replace the real‐life experience of placement". The authors align this with educators' views in Australia and the United States.

> "I think it's useful but I don't think you can use it completely…I would never like to see it used to replace the real‐life experience of placement."
