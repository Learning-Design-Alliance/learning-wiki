## Evidence

### Hoyt Garrik 2024

Hoyt Garrik, Adegboyega Samuel, Constantouris Gus, Basu Paramita. (2024). Study of the impact of introducing a multimedia learning tool in podiatric medical courses. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.12018

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-report survey of 53 of 86 treatment-group students, collected before final grades were released. Likert responses showed "students consistently rated Osmosis higher than the textbook in Likert scale responses" across retention, concept learning, and test-score improvement; satisfaction averaged 4.39 vs 3.45 out of 5.

> "Most students (92.2% ± 0.042%) either strongly agreed or somewhat agreed that Osmosis helped them retain information, while a smaller percentage (60.8% ± 0.086%) reported the same for the textbook."
