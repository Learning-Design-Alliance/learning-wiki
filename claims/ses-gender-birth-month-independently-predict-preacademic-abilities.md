## Evidence

### Rémeau 2026

Rémeau, M., Schmitt, P., & Borst, G. (2026). Executive functions as mediators of early educational disparities by SES, gender and birth month. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00422-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression on the nationally representative sample of 2,618 preschoolers (mean age 42.61 months) assessed in January 2022. Main effects were significant: higher SES ("β =.43, p < .001"), girls over boys, and earlier birth month better. The article states these main effects account for 37% of pre-academic variance.

> "A higher SES index was associated with higher pre -academic abilities scores ( β =.43, p < .001). Girls compared to boys displayed better pre-academic abilities scores (β =.17, p < .001). Finally, the earlier the child was born in the year the better the pre -academic scores (β = -.39, p < .001)."
