`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validation involved a panel of seven lecturers with a minimum of 15 years of experience in teaching clinical skills through simulation, who rated each of the 18 items on clarity, importance and relevance using a 1–4 Likert scale. The article reports that 'the overall Aiken V values obtained were greater than 0.8 for each of the three attributes of the questionnaire.'
