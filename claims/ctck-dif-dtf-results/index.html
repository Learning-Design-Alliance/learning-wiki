Monteyne S. 2024¶

Monteyne S., Struyve C., Gesquière N., Neutens T., Wyffels F., van Braak J. & Aesaert K. (2024). Teachers’ computational thinking content knowledge: Development of a measurement instrument. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105181

q2 · i? · design · r2

Mantel-Haenszel DIF analyses on the calibration data (352 pre-service teachers) for gender, home language and teaching subject. The article reports none of the items display DIF for gender, item 16 favors those not speaking the test language at home, and “item 2 advantages pre-service teachers with a STEM background, whereas item 3 favors the other group”.

"More specifically, item 2 advantages pre-524 service teachers with a STEM background, whereas item 3 favors the other group."
