### Awadalla Suheir 2020

Awadalla Suheir, Davies E. Bethan, Glazebrook Cris. (2020). A longitudinal cohort study to explore the relationship between depression, anxiety and academic performance among Emirati university students. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-020-02854-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Baseline non-parametric correlations in the UAE cohort between PHQ-9 scores and socioeconomic factors, reporting the printed correlation effect size "rs = −.276" with family affluence. Anxiety showed a similar pattern (rs = −.271 with affluence).

> "Students with higher PHQ-9 scores came from less affluent families (rs = −.276, n = 404, p < 0.001) and had less maternal education (rs = −.118, n = 403, p < 0.05)."
