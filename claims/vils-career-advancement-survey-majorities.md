Chelimo, S., Cortez, C., Jackson, J., & Weaver, D. (2024, May). Unintended Impact: How the Verizon Innovative Learning Schools Program Affected Its Educators' Job Satisfaction and Career Trajectories. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A Digital Promise survey of teachers in Jefferson County and Miami-Dade County shows "51 percent of teachers from these districts agree" the program enhanced their career advancement opportunities, and 59% agreed it provided opportunities for other teachers. No effect size is printed.

> "The survey Digital Promise administered to this sample shows that 51 percent of teachers from these districts agree that their career advancement opportunities have been enhanced by the program. ### survey-leadership

Chelimo, S., Cortez, C., Jackson, J., & Weaver, D. (2024, May). Unintended Impact: How the Verizon Innovative Learning Schools Program Affected Its Educators' Job Satisfaction and Career Trajectories. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same teacher survey (n = 181 respondents across the two districts) found 59% say the program provides leadership opportunities for them and 67% say it does for colleagues; a separate Miami-Dade coach survey found 57% agreement on advancement opportunities. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The majority of 181 Miami-Dade County and Jefferson County, Kentucky, teachers (59%) who responded to a survey administered by Digital Promise say that Verizon Innovative Learning Schools provides leadership opportunities for them. When asked if it provides opportunities for their colleagues, that figure grows to 67 percent."
