### Henmon 1917

Henmon, V. A. C. (1917). The Measurement of Ability in Latin. Part II. Sentence Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Henmon+Measurement+of+Ability+in+Latin+Sentence+Tests

Administration of Latin Sentence Test A in seventeen schools in June 1916 and 1917 to 262 first-year, 244 second-year, 94 third-year and 74 fourth-year pupils; sentences were scored right or wrong. The article reports "the second by 52 per cent, and the third by 50 per cent" correct.
