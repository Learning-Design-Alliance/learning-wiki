## Evidence

### Urkin B 2025

Urkin B, Parnas J, Raballo A, Koren D. (2025). Clinical Activity and Psychopathological Knowledge Are Related to Real-World Performance of Leading International Psychiatrists in Diagnosing Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders. Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1159/000549313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis of 30 psychiatrists' interview data using Fisher's exact tests (one-tailed, alpha 0.05). The article reports "a significant positive association between perceived usefulness of SD and diagnostic accuracy (p = 0.03)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The results also revealed a significant positive association between perceived usefulness of SD and diagnostic accuracy (p = 0.03)."

### Urkin B 2025 (2)

Urkin B, Parnas J, Raballo A, Koren D. (2025). Clinical Activity and Psychopathological Knowledge Are Related to Real-World Performance of Leading International Psychiatrists in Diagnosing Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders. Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1159/000549313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory stepwise logistic regression analyses combining multiple predictors. The article states "familiarity with SD was the only variable that consistently entered as a significant predictor," while calling the analyses preliminary and limited by sample size.

> "Across all models, however, familiarity with SD was the only variable that consistently entered as a significant predictor."
