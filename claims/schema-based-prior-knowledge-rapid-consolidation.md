## Evidence

### Ruiter D. J. 2010

Ruiter D. J., van Kesteren M. T. R., Fernandez G. (2010). How to achieve synergy between medical education and cognitive neuroscience? An exercise on prior knowledge in understanding. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9244-5

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Tse et al. (2007), rat studies of a complex event arena in which 'prior knowledge on the correct location in a so called complex event arena plays a causal role in system consolidation'. Consolidation using a schema occurred within 48 h, contrasting with the earlier view that intercortical connections need long periods to strengthen.

> "Based on elegant studies in rats (Tse et al. 2007) found that prior knowledge on the correct location in a so called complex event arena plays a causal role in system consolidation. They also showed that system consolidation using a schema based on prior knowledge occurs rapidly, i.e. within 48 h in this study."
