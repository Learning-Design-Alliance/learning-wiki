### Kannan 2025

Kannan, S., & Gowri, S. (2025). Angoff methods in standard setting in health professional education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education, 25, 1727. [doi:10.1186/s12909-025-08300-6](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08300-6)

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The meta-analysis pooled inter-rater reliability coefficients (Fisher's z-transformed) across Angoff variants. The Modified Angoff with reality check achieved "excellent reliability (r = 0.917)" while the Angoff (Yes/No) yielded "the weakest and most variable results (r = 0.536)".

> "The Modified Angoff with reality check achieved excellent reliability (r = 0.917) while the Angoff (Yes/No) yielded the weakest and most variable results (r = 0.536), a finding confirmed by bootstrap analyses."
