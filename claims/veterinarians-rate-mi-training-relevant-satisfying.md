## Evidence

### Svensson C 2020

Svensson C, Wickström H, Emanuelson U, Bard AM, Reyher KK, Forsberg L. (2020). Training in motivational interviewing improves cattle veterinarians' communication skills for herd health management. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1136/vr.105646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-workshop questionnaires (212 responses across six workshops from 32–38 participating veterinarians) with 6-point Likert ratings and open-field qualitative coding. The article reports a mean relevance rating of 4.9 (4.0–6.0) and an overall satisfaction mean of 5.1 (5.0–6.0); valued elements included small groups, the mix of theory and practice, and feedback on recordings.

> "Overall, participating dairy cattle veterinarians perceived their new skills and knowledge in MI to be relevant in their work (figure 2); the mean (25–75th percentiles) rating for relevance from all workshops was 4.9 (4.0–6.0)."
