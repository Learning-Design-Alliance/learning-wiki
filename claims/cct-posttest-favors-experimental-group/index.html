Evidence¶

Dağdelen and Kosterelioglu 2015¶

Orhan Dağdelen, İlker Kösterelioğlu. (2015). Effect of Conceptual Change Texts for Overcoming Misconceptions in “People and Management” Unit. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(1), 99-112. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078838

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group study with 23 experimental and 23 control 4th graders; independent samples t-test on PMAT post-test means Mexp= 14.35 vs Mcontrol= 11.65. The article reports "the calculated size effect of meaningful difference in favour of experimental group is high level (η2= 24.5)". That value cannot be an eta squared, which is at most 1; read as a percentage it still does not match the printed statistics (the experimental group's t(22) = 5.04 implies η² ≈ .54, against a reported 36.5), so no effect size is coded from it (source-validity check, 2026-11-02).

"Post -test scores show significant differences between experimental and control group students ( t(44)= 3.787; p< 0.05)."
