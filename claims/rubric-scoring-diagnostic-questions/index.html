Evidence¶

Hardy 2023¶

Hardy, A., Iwatani, E., Means, B., & Seylar, J. (2023). Rubrics for examining historical thinking skills in high school world history activities and student work. Digital Promise. (Original work published 2021). https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/111

q1 · i? · design · r?

The introduction states the intended analytic uses of the rubrics as three diagnostic questions about opportunities, lesson modification, and demonstrated student skills. This is the authors' stated purpose, not an evaluated outcome.

"By scoring the activities and examples of student work, it's possible to answer questions like: Does this high school world history learning activity provide opportunities for students to use historical thinking skills?"
