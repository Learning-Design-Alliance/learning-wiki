## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lia 2024

Lia, Z. R.-Y., Barry, C., & Cukurova, M. (2024). Adult learners' recall and recognition performance and affective feedback when learning from an AI-generated synthetic video. Technical Report. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A four-condition ANCOVA on recognition post-test scores controlling for pre-test, part of a 400-participant Prolific experiment (380 valid responses after removing invalid and outlier cases). The article reports the condition effect was "insignificant (F(3,374) = 1.18, p = .32, η2 = .01)".

> "Based on the ANCOVA results, as indicated in Table 2, although the recognition pre-test significantly explained the variation of the post-test (F(1,374) = 16.43, p < .001), the mean difference in the post-test performance across the four conditions was insignificant (F(3,374) = 1.18, p = .32, η2 = .01)."
