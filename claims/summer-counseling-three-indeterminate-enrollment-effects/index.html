Evidence¶

Summer Counseling 2018¶

Summer Counseling. (2018). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Transition to College. U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/693

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

This New Mexico RCT assigned 1,602 college-admitted graduates to counselor outreach (based at the University of New Mexico or Albuquerque Public Schools) or comparison; the WWC table shows an enrollment effect size of -0.01 and p > .10.

"Castleman et al. (2015a) reported, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between the intervention group and the comparison group on fall college enrollment. The WWC characterizes this study finding as an indeterminate effect."

Summer Counseling 2018 (2)¶

Summer Counseling. (2018). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Transition to College. U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/693

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

This cluster randomized trial in Tennessee and Missouri paired schools by prior-year college enrollment rate; six intervention and six comparison schools participated. The WWC table shows an effect size of 0.09 and p < .10, not statistically significant.

"Castleman et al. (2015b) reported, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between the summer counseling group and the comparison group on fall college enrollment. The WWC characterizes this study finding as an indeterminate effect."

Summer Counseling 2018 (3)¶

Summer Counseling. (2018). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Transition to College. U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/693

q3 · i? · review · r3

This RCT across five sites compared a text-messaging campaign (2,524 intervention, 2,535 comparison) and peer mentoring (934 intervention, 1,272 comparison) against typical school supports; the appendix tables show effect sizes of 0.06 for both arms, p > .10, and improvement indices of +2 and +3.

"Castleman and Page (2015) reported, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between the intervention groups and the comparison groups on fall college enrollment."
