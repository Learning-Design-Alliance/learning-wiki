# Peer evaluators could not assess training impact because trainee rotation gave them no recollection of working with trainees both before and after simulation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Peer evaluation focus groups highlighted that peers had limited clinical exposure to trainees, remembering a trainee before or after simulation but not both, so they could not evaluate teamwork changes. [→ Brown J 2025](#brown-j-2025)

## Evidence

### Brown J 2025

Brown J, De-Oliveira S, Mitchell C, Cesar RC, Ding L, Fix M, Stemen D, Yacharn K, Wong SF, Dhillon A. (2025). Barriers to and Facilitators of Implementing Team-Based Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation Simulation Study: Exploratory Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/57424

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from the 2 peer evaluation focus groups held 2 months post-training with a group of 4 peers, part of the amended qualitative design. The article attributes the failure to the nature of fellows' ICU rotation.

> "peers did not have any recollection of working with the trainees prior to and following the simulation training to provide an appropriate evaluation of their skills in teamwork during ECMO therapy decision-making"
