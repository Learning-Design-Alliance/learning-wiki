## Evidence

### Zhou Jie 2026

Zhou Jie, Zhang Guowen, Song Peige, Liaw Sok Ying, Luk Tzu Tsun. (2026). Effects of High‐Fidelity Simulation Training on Learning Outcomes and Satisfaction for Practising Registered Nurses: A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. Journal of Clinical Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jocn.70206

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of three RCTs and one quasi-experiment on professional skills in 165 practising registered nurses; half used self-developed instruments. The review reports "a moderate pooled effect on professional skills" with "no notable heterogeneity". One study's skills measure was observer-rated.

> "The meta-analysis with a random‐effects model showed a moderate pooled effect on professional skills (SMD = 0.72, 95% CI, [0.41, 1.04], p < 0.01), with no notable heterogeneity (I2 = 0%, p = 0.57)"
