`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test control group study at Arna W. Bontemps Public School, Chicago: 30 Direct Instruction students and 42 regular-program students, 1996 ITBS reading (Level 12 Form A), analyzed with a t test at the .05 level. The study found "no significant difference on the reading scores of sixth grade students."
