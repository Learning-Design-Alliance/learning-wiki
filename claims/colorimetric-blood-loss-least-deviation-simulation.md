## Evidence

### Mahoney B 2021

Mahoney B, Luebbert E. (2021). Updates on Simulation in Obstetrical Anesthesiology Through the COVID-19 Pandemic. Anesthesiology Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anclin.2021.08.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

Piekarski and colleagues compared a mobile colorimetric application with visual and gravimetric methods used by 53 anesthesiologists in a simulated PPH scenario. The review reports "the least deviation in estimates" for the colorimetric method; no effect size is printed.

> "They found the least deviation in estimates from the actual volume of blood loss among the colorimetric estimation followed by gravimetric and visual methods, whereas overestimation of blood loss occurred most in the visual estimation followed by the gravimetric and colorimetric methods."
