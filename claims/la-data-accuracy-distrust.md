## Evidence

### Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023

Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 24 interviews. Although all teaching staff viewed LA as useful, they raised accuracy concerns (e.g., surveys completed with random answers, Blackboard attendance misrecording) and precision concerns about generalising predictive models across cohorts.

> "Six codes were developed to capture concerns about data accuracy, including outdated data, different data channels, lack of data governance, power relationship, response bias and technical issues."
