## Evidence

### Bor Gregorcic 2024

Bor Gregorcic, Giulia Polverini and Andreja Sarlah. (2024). ChatGPT as a tool for honing teachers' Socratic dialogue skills. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad3d21](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad3d21)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot study in April 2023 with 17 pre-service physics and mathematics teachers from one Swedish and one Slovenian university using ChatGPT-3.5. Participants reported 27 dialogues; "16 were considered successful by the participants" and in 3 of the unsuccessful cases they did not insist on numerical correctness.

> "Out of 21 dialogues with the wrong initial answer, 16 were considered successful by the participants, while in the remaining 5 dialogues the participants gave up before ChatGPT gave an answer they would deem satisfactory."
