## Evidence

### Guo Eddie 2024

Guo Eddie, Ramchandani Rashi, Park Ye-Jean, Gupta Mehul. (2024). OSCEai: personalized interactive learning for undergraduate medical education. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79220

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison survey item in which students rated OSCEai against lecture-based methods they had experienced; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests were used against a midpoint of 3 after Shapiro-Wilk normality checks. The article reports "an average rating of 4.14 (95% CI: 3.94 to 4.33) out of 5 (P < 0.0001".

> "The OSCEai platform was rated favorably against lecture-based teaching methods of learning (e.g., flipped classroom lectures, patient presentations) with an average rating of 4.14 (95% CI: 3.94 to 4.33) out of 5 (P < 0.0001, Fig. 3D)."

### Guo Eddie 2024 (2)

Guo Eddie, Ramchandani Rashi, Park Ye-Jean, Gupta Mehul. (2024). OSCEai: personalized interactive learning for undergraduate medical education. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79220

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second comparison item from the same survey: students rated OSCEai's post-scenario feedback against feedback received in lecture-based learning outlets, analysed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test against a midpoint of 3. The article reports "an average rating of 4.03 (95% CI: 3.74 to 4.32) out of 5 (P < 0.0001".

> "Furthermore, the feedback that OSCEai provided was favoured over common outlets of learning (Fig. 3E), with an average rating of 4.03 (95% CI: 3.74 to 4.32) out of 5 (P < 0.0001)."
