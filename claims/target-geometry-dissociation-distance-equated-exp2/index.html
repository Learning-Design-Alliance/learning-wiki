Evidence¶

Wei Ding 2026¶

Wei Ding, Anjuli Niyogi, Jordan A. Taylor & Jonathan S. Tsay. (2026). Hypothesis Testing Governs Strategic Motor Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00428-4

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Experiment 2 (N = 280) equated the spatial distance between training and probe targets (110° for both groups) using eight training targets, ruling out a distance confound. Inner group learned faster early (Inner: 33.8°±3.1; Outer: 27.4°±4.0; p = .012).

"Crucially, this pattern reversed at the probe target (Figure 3a, right; interaction between Target Geometry x Target Type: F=6.3, p=0.012, η²=0.02): the inner -target group generalized more inconsistently than the outer-target group."
