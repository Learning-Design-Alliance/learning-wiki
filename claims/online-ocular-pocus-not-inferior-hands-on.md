## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized non-inferiority trial, the online group's first-scan total score (lsmean 21.7) was not inferior to the hands-on group's (lsmean 21.3), with the 95% CI lower bound above the pre-set margin of minus 2. [→ Kang Soo-Yeon 2022](#kang-soo-yeon-2022)

## Evidence

### Kang Soo-Yeon 2022

Kang Soo-Yeon, Yoo Jonghoon, Park Sookyung, Jo Ik-Joon, Kim Seonwoo, Cho Hyun, Lee Guntak, Park Jong-Eun, Kim Taerim, Lee Se-Uk, Hwang Sung-Yeon, Cha Won-Chul, Shin Tae-Gun, Yoon Hee. (2022). Online Learning versus Hands-On Learning of Basic Ocular Ultrasound Skills: A Randomized Controlled Non-Inferiority Trial. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina58070960

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled non-inferiority trial in an academic emergency department; 32 residents and interns randomized 1:1 to online (20 min materials and videos) or hands-on (30 min individual session with a standardized patient). Blinded faculty scored the first scan; the "lower bound of the 95% CI was greater than the non-inferiority margin of minus 2". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The difference in the group lsmean of total scores was 0.35 (0.42), and the lower bound of the 95% CI was greater than the non-inferiority margin of minus 2 (95% CI: −0.48–1.17)."
