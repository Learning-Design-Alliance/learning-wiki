## Evidence

### Zhang Meng-lu 2025

Zhang Meng-lu, Bai Wen, Yan Zhi-peng, Ge Hui-min, Zhang Qiu-yang, Ding Xi-yan, Liu Yan-qing, Li Xiang, Jiang Qin, Li Ke-ran, Shang Wei-hong. (2025). Virtual simulation system in cataract education for medical undergraduates: a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08497-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective quasi-experimental study of 71 undergraduates: experimental group (n=35, 2019 class) trained on the VR platform, control group (n=36, 2018 class) received traditional offline training. Table 2 reports traditional total score 82.22 ± 4.34 vs 76.83 ± 5.14 (t = −4.77, P < 0.0001) and VR total 84.06 ± 4.85 vs 71.06 ± 6.84 (t = −9.21, P < 0.0001), confirming the group "demonstrated significantly higher total scores than the control group".

> "Overall, the experimental group demonstrated significantly higher total scores than the control group across both traditional and virtual simulation assessments."
