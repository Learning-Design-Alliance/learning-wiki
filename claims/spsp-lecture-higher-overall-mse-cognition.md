## Evidence

### Singano Violeth E. 2024

Singano Violeth E., Millanzi Walter C., Moshi Fabiola. (2024). Effect of standardized patient simulation-based pedagogics embedded with lecture in enhancing mental status evaluation cognition among nursing students in Tanzania: A longitudinal quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05562-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal controlled quasi-experimental study of 311 diploma nursing students in Tanga and Dodoma, Tanzania, with 109 intervention and 202 control participants. The reported post-test comparison shows the intervention group's "posttest cognition was (M ± SD = 22.15 ± 4.42)" versus 16.52 ± 6.30 for controls, significant at p < 0.0001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "posttest cognition was (M ± SD = 22.15 ± 4.42) for intervention and (M ± SD = 16.52 ± 6.30; p = < 0.0001) for control group."
