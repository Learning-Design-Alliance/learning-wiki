ARC Core evidence summary 2022¶

ARC Core evidence summary. (2022). https://eric.ed.gov

q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative interview data from teachers and school leaders in the Philadelphia-based study (Gray et al., 2022) sharing impressions and professional feedback; findings are self-reported perceptions with no effect sizes printed.

"Teachers reported positive effects of the program on student engagement, learning, and behavior, and that the program was seen to be cost effective. Teachers reported that full implementation (including those involving student learning centers and individual meetings) required additional classroom support"
