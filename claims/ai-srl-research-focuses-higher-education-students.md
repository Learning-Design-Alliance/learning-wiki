## Evidence

### Banihashem 2025

Banihashem, S. K., Bond, M., Bergdahl, N., Khosravi, H., & Noroozi, O. (2025). A systematic mapping review at the intersection of artificial intelligence and self-regulated learning. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 22(50). https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00548-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Stakeholder analysis of the review's 84 included studies (2018–2024) found that "an overwhelming majority of AI-supported SRL initiatives have included stu- dents as participants (98%, N = 82)". Only four studies featured educators, and 74% of studies (N = 62) explored higher education; the corpus's only primary-school-exclusive study was a 60-hour professional development program for 31 in-service primary educators in Hong Kong.

> "As expected, an overwhelming majority of AI-supported SRL initiatives have included stu- dents as participants (98%, N = 82)"
