## Evidence

### Brown 2013

Brown, J. A., Greenfield, D. B., Bell, E., Juárez, C. L., Myers, T., & Nayfeld, I. (2013). Early Childhood Hands-On Science Efficacy Study. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563045

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel growth models on Direct Assessment of Science scores from approximately ten randomly selected, age- and gender-stratified children per classroom, assessed in fall, winter, and spring. The ECHOS growth advantage was a trend at p = .069, not statistically significant.

> "The results revealed that all children showed significant growth in science skills (Slope γ = 5.76, p < .001); a trend indicated faster rates of growth for children in ECHOS classrooms (ECHOSγ = 0.49, p = .069)"
