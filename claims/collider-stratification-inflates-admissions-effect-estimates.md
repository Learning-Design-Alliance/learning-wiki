## Evidence

### M. B. Weissman 2021

M. B. Weissman. (2021). Invalid Methods of Causal Inference in Physics Education Research. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2110.04266](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2110.04266)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Comparison, drawn from Table V of Verostek et al. 2021, between a recent less-stratified analysis and a previous one introducing extra collider stratification via three rank tiers. The author notes the new numbers use only ~80% of the old sample and a probit rather than logit link.

> "The new less-stratified graduation predictive  effect sizes for UGPA, GRE-P, and GRE-Q were increased from those found in the  more-stratified model by factors of 1.7, 1.2, and 1.6, respectively. This substantial change illustrates the importance of collider bias."
