Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The generalization gradient emerges later in the crossmodal group than in the unimodal group — related
  • Participants in both unimodal and crossmodal groups successfully acquire conditioned fear, with higher US expectancy, SCR, and mPFC HbO responses to CS+ than CS− — related
  • The unimodal group shows higher overall US expectancy ratings and SCR than the crossmodal group — related
  • mPFC responds to conceptually related generalization stimuli more than to the non-threat CS−, though without a gradient or group differences
  • US expectancy ratings show a conceptual generalization gradient: stimuli more similar to the CS+ elicit higher ratings (GS1 > GS2 > GS3)
  • Most participants' fear generalization aligns with perceptual rather than physical stimulus distances
