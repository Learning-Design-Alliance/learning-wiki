## Evidence

### Hepburn CY 2026

Hepburn CY. (2026). Evidence to support integrating feedback best practice for computer‐based assessment in pharmacology education. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1002/bcp.70302

`q2 · i1` · `review · r2`

The review reports Wisniewski's meta-analysis of 435 studies representing 994 effect sizes across kindergarten to university settings, finding small or medium effects which "might caution porting of feedback strategies aimed at these outcomes".

> "the effect size of motivational feedback interventions, related to intrinsic motivation and self‐efficacy (d = 0.33; 95% CI [0.23–0.42]) and behavioural feedback interventions, related to behaviour in the educational setting (d = 0.48; 95% CI [−0.09 ‐ ‐1.06])"
