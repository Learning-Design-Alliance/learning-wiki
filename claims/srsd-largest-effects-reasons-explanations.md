## Subclaims
`q3 i2` MANCOVA showed significant main effects favoring the intervention group on total reasons (d = 0.70 posttest; 0.61 maintenance) and total explanations (d = 0.79 posttest; 0.64 maintenance). [→ Mason et al. 2017](#mason-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Mason et al. 2017

Linda H. Mason, Anne Mong Cramer, Justin D. Garwood, Cheryl Varghese, Jill Hamm, and Allen Murray. (2017). Efficacy of Self-Regulated Strategy Development Instruction for Developing Writers With and Without Disabilities in Rural Schools: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Rural Special Education Quarterly, 36(4), 168–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/8756870517723617

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA with children nested within schools, controlling for pretest, gender, grade, and ability, found for total reasons that "the effect size of 0.70 favored the intervention group" at posttest and 0.61 at maintenance; explanations showed d = 0.79 and 0.64.

> "At posttest, F(1, 540) = 74.30, p < .001, the effect size of 0.70 favored the intervention group. At maintenance, F(1, 480) = 42.02, p < .001, the effect size of 0.61 favored the intervention group."

### Mason et al. 2017 (2)

Linda H. Mason, Anne Mong Cramer, Justin D. Garwood, Cheryl Varghese, Jill Hamm, and Allen Murray. (2017). Efficacy of Self-Regulated Strategy Development Instruction for Developing Writers With and Without Disabilities in Rural Schools: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Rural Special Education Quarterly, 36(4), 168–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/8756870517723617

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA results for topic sentence inclusion showed "the effect size of 0.21 favored the intervention group" at posttest and 0.26 at maintenance; the article attributes the small gain to 78.22% of treatment and 84.59% of control students already writing effective topic sentences at pretest.

> "At posttest, F(1, 540) = 6.36, p = .012, the effect size of 0.21 favored the intervention group. At maintenance, F(1, 480) = 8.86, p = .003, the effect size of 0.26 favored the intervention group."
