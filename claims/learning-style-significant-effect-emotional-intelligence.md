## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Analysis of variance showed a significant difference in students' emotional intelligence based on learning style (significance value 0.000, smaller than α 0.05). [→ Leasa et al. 2017](#leasa-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Leasa et al. 2017

Marleny Leasa, Aloysius D. Corebima, Ibrohim, Hadi Suwono. (2017). Emotional intelligence among auditory, reading, and kinesthetic learning styles of elementary school students in Ambon-Indonesia. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017131889

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental 3x3 factorial study of 210 fifth-grade students in Ambon-Indonesia; ANOVA via SPSS 17.00 showed the learning style main effect was significant, with "significance value of the learning styles (0.000)". No effect size for the main effect is printed; R Squared = .193 for the corrected model.

> "Table 2 shows that significance value of the learning styles (0.000) is smaller than α (0.05). It means that there is a difference in students’ emotional intelligence based on the auditory, read, and kinesthetic learning styles."
