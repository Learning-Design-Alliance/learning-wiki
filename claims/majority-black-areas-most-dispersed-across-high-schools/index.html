Evidence¶

McKoy 2021¶

McKoy, D., Usher, A., Nagaoka, J., & Khatri, S. (2021). Approaching Chicago student attainment from a community perspective. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/comm

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis in Chapter 1 using a school dispersion ratio measuring the number of schools attended for every 100 CPS ninth-graders in a community area, shown in Figure 4. Dispersion ratios ranged from about five to over 30 schools per 100 ninth-graders across community areas.

"Figure 4 shows that 21 of the 25 community areas with the highest dispersion ratios were majority-Black, while only one of the 25 community areas with the lowest dispersion ratios was majority-Black."
