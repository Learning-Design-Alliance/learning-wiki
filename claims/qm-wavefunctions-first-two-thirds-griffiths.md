`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item on textbook usage among 76 instructors; 71 reported following a textbook. The article reports Griffiths most used, followed by McIntyre and Townsend, and states "roughly two-thirds of respondents" use wavefunctions-first texts, consistent with Buzzell et al.'s syllabus analysis.

> "The prevalence of Grifﬁths suggests that a wavefunctions-ﬁrst approach to QM instruction is still the most common approach (roughly two-thirds of respondents)."
