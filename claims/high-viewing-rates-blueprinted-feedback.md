## Evidence

### Burr Steven A 2013

Burr Steven A, Brodier Elizabeth, Wilkinson Simon. (2013). Delivery and use of individualised feedback in large class medical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-63

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Behaviour-based online survey of medical students in years 1 and 2 (samples of 180-215 on first release, 21-22 subsequently) and interviewer-administered attitude survey of three year groups (samples of 20-22). Responders rated feedback value at "4.19 on a 5 point Likert scale" and 88-96% viewed the reports.

> "The responders across the three year groups averaged the value of feedback as 4.19 on a 5 point Likert scale (1 = not useful, 5 = very useful). The importance placed on receiving feedback following summative assessment was reflected by 88-96% of the cohort viewing the specific modular blueprinted feedback reports as they are released."
