Quasi-experimental comparison of final exam scores and course grade distributions between the participant group and the comparison group (Figures 11 and 12). The article reports the 6–8% exam advantage and the greater share of A/B grades descriptively, with no significance test printed.

> "The participant group had average final exam grades that were 6-8% higher than those of the comparison group. A greater percentage of students in the participant group also achieved grades of “A” and “B” for the course overall than the students in the comparison group."
