### Atlanoğlu Ş 2026

Atlanoğlu Ş, Gedik MA. (2026). Large language models in radiology exams: A cross-sectional comparative analysis of performance in Turkish and English. Medicine 2026;105:26(e49517). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049517

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparative analysis of four paid-subscription LLMs on 100 multiple-choice radiology questions, benchmarked against 18 radiology residents (years 1–3). Gemini 2.5 Pro scored 90/100; resident accuracy was "75.8% for 3rd-year residents, 66% for 2nd-year residents, and 58.7% for 1st-year residents". Overall group differences were significant (Pearson chi-square, P < .001).

> "Among the 100 radiology questions analyzed, Gemini 2.5 Pro achieved the highest success rate among the LLMs, with 90 correct responses. When radiology residents were grouped by year of residence, the correct response rates were 75.8% for 3rd-year residents, 66% for 2nd-year residents, and 58.7% for 1st-year residents."
