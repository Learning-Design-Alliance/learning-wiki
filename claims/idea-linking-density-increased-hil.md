## Evidence

### Sydney Enock Msonde 2017

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

`q3 · i2` · `design · r3`

Social network analysis with NetMiner 4 of post linking in randomly selected forums 1, 3 and 5, coding the extent to which students built on the ideas of others. The article reports a medium main effect for time (partial η² = 0.070) and a medium between-groups effect (partial η² = 0.083).

> "A repeated-measures ANOVA revealed a main effect for linking of student ideas, F (2, 136) = 5.093, p = 0.007, partial g2 = 0.070 (medium), and significant differences between groups for between-subject effects, F (1, 68) = 6.178, p = 0.015, partial g2 = 0.083 (medium)."

### Sydney Enock Msonde 2017 (2)

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

`q3 · i2` · `design · r3`

One-way ANOVA on forum 5 linking densities found a significant between-group difference (partial η² = 0.067, medium); the article states idea linking "developed somewhat more highly over time in the HIL design". Forums 3 and 1 showed no significant differences (p = 0.054 and p = 0.928).

> "a one-way ANOVA showed that the densities for idea linking in forum 5 were significantly different between groups, F (1, 69) = 4.867, p = 0.031, partial g2 = 0.067 (medium). There were improvements in idea linking between groups, such that student idea linking developed somewhat more highly over time in the HIL design."
