### Kumins Norman H. 2021

Kumins Norman H., Qin Vivian L., Driscoll Erin C., Morrow Katherine L., Kashyap Vikram S., Ning Anne Y., Tucker Nicholas J., King Alexander H., Quereshy Humzah A., Dash Siddhartha, Grobaty Lauren, Zhou George. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post 10-question multiple-choice exam in the 221-student elective cohort; the article reports "significantly improved from a pre-elective average of 54% to a post-elective average of 87%". Only two students (0.9%) scored below the 70% passing threshold post-course.

> "Students’ scores on the 10-question multiple-choice exam testing knowledge of surgical instruments, materials and techniques significantly improved from a pre-elective average of 54% to a post-elective average of 87% (P < 0.01)."
