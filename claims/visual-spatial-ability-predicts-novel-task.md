## Evidence

### Beattie Kirsty L. 2022

Beattie Kirsty L., Hill Andrew, Horswill Mark S., Grove Philip M., Stevenson Andrew R. L. (2022). Aptitude and attitude: predictors of performance during and after basic laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08668-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression on the novel NIS task (passing a curved suture through six flexible loops) for 34 novices. The article reports that as visual-spatial ability increased, NIS score significantly decreased (improved), and as perceived demands increased, NIS score increased (worsened). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The final model was statistically significant, F(3, 30) = 30.68, p < 0.001, and explained 73.0% of the variance in total NIS score, RAdj2=0.730 (large effect)."
