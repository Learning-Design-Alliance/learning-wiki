### Efraimidou et al. 2016

Vasiliki Efraimidou, Maria Sidiropoulou, Paraskevi Giagazoglou, Miltiadis Proios, Vasileios Tsimaras, Anastasios Orologas. (2016). THE EFFECTS OF A MUSIC AND MOVEMENT PROGRAM ON GAIT, BALANCE AND PSYCHOLOGICAL PARAMETRES OF ADULTS WITH CEREBRAL PALSY. International Journal of Special Education, 31(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1111110

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

POMS and RSE questionnaires administered pre and post in the small randomized study; the quote reports significant post hoc differences for intention, aggressiveness, sprightliness and total score, but not for depression, fatigue or confusion. No standardized effect sizes are printed for these t-tests.

> "The post hoc paired t -test revealed statistically significant differences in intervention group between the two measurements regarding intention (score) (t=2.93, df=4, p=.043), aggressiveness (score) (t=2.78, df=4, p=.050), sprightfulness (score) (t= -7.48, df=4, p=.002), total score (t=2.86, df=4, p=.046), but not in depression (score) (t=2.41, df=4, p=.074), fatigue (score) (t=1.33, df=4, p=.255) and confusion (score) (t=2.45, df=4, p=.070)."
