## Subclaims
`q1 i?` No assumptions about score distribution normality or error independence are made, and normalizing each item score by its maximum extends the method from dichotomous to fractional-credit exams. [→ Bakman 2012](#bakman-2012)

## Evidence

### Bakman 2012

Bakman, Y. (2012). Identifying Unfair Items in Educational Measurement. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1205.3380](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1205.3380)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical/definitional statement in the generalization section. The article explains that dividing each score by the item's maximum makes all items equally weighted, after which 'the equation of the ideal line and all the conclusions remain as before'; weights can be regained when computing final scores.

> "It should be noted that no assumptions were made about the normality of the score distributions as it is usually accepted, neither about their error independence, so that the proposed method has the highest domain of applicability."
