# Controlled motivation and mathematics achievement show no reciprocal cross-lagged relations in RI-CLPM models

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within-person changes in controlled motivation did not relate to later changes in achievement, nor achievement changes to later controlled motivation, in either direction. [→ Santana‐Monagas 2025](#santanamonagas-2025)

## Evidence

### Santana‐Monagas 2025

Santana‐Monagas, E., Núñez‐Regueiro, F., & Núñez, J. L. (2025). Does motivation lead to academic success, or conversely? Reciprocal relations between autonomous and controlled motivation, and mathematics achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 513–529. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12736

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null result from the controlled-motivation RI-CLPM of 1042 secondary mathematics students across three trimesters, with and without covariates (Table 4 cross-lagged paths non-significant, some at p < .10). The article reports "no reciprocal effects among controlled motivation and achievement in the RI‐CLPM"; no effect size is printed and equivalence was not tested.

> "Regarding cross‐lagged effects, results showed no reciprocal effects among controlled motivation and achievement in the RI‐CLPM."
