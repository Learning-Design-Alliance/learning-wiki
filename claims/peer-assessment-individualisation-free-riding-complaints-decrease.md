## Evidence

### Homa Babai Shishavan and Mahdi Jalili 2020

Homa Babai Shishavan and Mahdi Jalili. (2020). Responding to student feedback: Individualising teamwork scores based on peer assessment. [doi:10.1016/j.ijedro.2020.100019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijedro.2020.100019)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after comparison of the CES section "What aspects of this course are in most need of improvement?" across the 2018 cohort (151 students) and 2019 cohort (129 students). Pre-implementation, "26% of them explicitly mentioned the marking process" as needing improvement; post-implementation the figure was 7% (Table 3).

> "Furthermore, the implementation of the peer assessment process decreased complains about free riding from 26% to 7%."
