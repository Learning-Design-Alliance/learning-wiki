### Ross AJ 2015

Ross AJ, Reedy GB, Roots A, Jaye P, Birns J. (2015). Evaluating multisite multiprofessional simulation training for a hyperacute stroke service using the Behaviour Change Wheel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0423-1
