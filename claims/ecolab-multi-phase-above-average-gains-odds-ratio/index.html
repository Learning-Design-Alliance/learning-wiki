Rosemary Luckin & Benedict du Boulay 2016¶

Rosemary Luckin & Benedict du Boulay. (2016). Reflections on the Ecolab and the Zone of Proximal Development. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0072-x

q2 · i3 · design · r2

An odds-ratio analysis across the original Ecolab and Ecolab II evaluations, with odds ratios of "11.4 times more likely" (Ecolab II) and "12.4 times more likely" (original Ecolab), illustrating the consistency of results between the two studies. The article concludes that extending learners to interact with more complex concepts is consistent with learning.

"Children who both interacted with different phases of simulation complexity and completed an above average number of actions were 11.4 times more likely to be amongst the learners achieving above average learning gains with Ecolab II, and 12.4 times more likely with the original Ecolab."
