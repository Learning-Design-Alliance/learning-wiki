`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subjective visual-analog ratings (0–100) collected after delayed testing in the same experiment; motivation-to-use and likelihood-of-continued-use ratings showed no differences between tablet and text (all pMMs > 0.277).

> "Real-world training (x¯ = 80.50, sd = 15.58) was perceived as more effective compared to either text- (x¯ = 63.19, sd = 23.48) or tablet- (x¯ = 69.33, sd = 19.93) based training (pMM = 0.010 and pMM = 0.032 respectively)."
