Ximenes Barrat 2025¶

Ximenes Barrat, V., Diercks, C., Berliner, B., Derby, K., Chen Gaddini, M., & Pivnick, L. (2025). Revisiting California's invisible achievement gap: Trends in education outcomes of students in foster care in the context of the local control funding formula. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resource/revisiting-californias-invisible-achievement-gap/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CDE CALPADS data and ESSA Assistance Status Data Files for 2018–19 through 2022–23. Rates fell so that "fewer than one in four students in foster care attended a low-performing school" by 2021–22, yet the group's rate remained the highest of any group studied.

"Compared to students in other high-need groups, students in foster care were also consistently more likely to attend a low-performing school; that is, one eligible for Comprehensive Support and Improvement or for Additional Targeted Support"
