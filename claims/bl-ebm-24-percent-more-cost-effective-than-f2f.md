## Evidence

### Maloney S 2015

Maloney S, Nicklen P, Rivers G, Foo J, Ooi YY, Reeves S, Walsh K, Ilic D. (2015). A Cost-Effectiveness Analysis of Blended Versus Face-to-Face Delivery of Evidence-Based Medicine to Medical Students. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.4346

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cost-effectiveness analysis nested in a randomized trial at Monash University, comparing ongoing operating costs of blended versus face-to-face EBM teaching against Berlin Questionnaire competency (quality-adjusted students educated). The analysis found that "BL was 24% more cost-effective to operate than F2F", excluding transition costs.

> "Independent ICER calculations of F2F and BL compared to baseline found that BL was 24% more cost-effective to operate than F2F."
