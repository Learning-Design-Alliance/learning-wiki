Evidence¶

Stuart Luppescu 1995¶

Stuart Luppescu, Anthony S. Bryk, Paul Deabster, John Q. Easton, and Yeow Meng Thum. (1995). School Reform, Retention Policy, and Student Achievement Gains. University of Chicago draft report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-reform-retention-policy-and-student-achievement-gains

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of gains by propensity category across grades 1988-1992 in the Chicago data. The authors state this is "consistent with our investigation of achievement where we found a definite negative correlation between achievement status and gain in the subsequent year." No effect size is printed.

"First of all, in almost all cases, students in the top category in propensity for retention showed higher gains than those not in the top category."
