Evidence¶

Psyridou 2024¶

Psyridou, M., Koponen, T., Tolvanen, A., Aunola, K., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Torppa, M. (2024). Early prediction of math difficulties with the use of a neural networks model. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(2), 212-232. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000835

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Descriptive correlation analysis (Pearson and Spearman) between Grade 6 math and kindergarten measures in the First Steps sample; the full passage also reports arithmetic (r = .45), spatial relations (r = .27), and Strategy 1 (rho = .32).

"The strongest correlations between the math variable and the kindergarten-age measures were for letter knowledge (fall) (r = .30), counting (fall) (r = .41)"
