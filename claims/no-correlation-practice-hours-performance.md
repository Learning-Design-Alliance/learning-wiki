Development of performance and learning rate evaluation models in robot-assisted surgery using electroencephalography and eye-tracking. npj Science of Learning 9:3. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00216-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis across the 26 subjects, whose self-reported RAS experience hours ranged from 0 to more than 1000. Only p-values are printed, so the finding is a non-detection, not a measured null effect; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results revealed no significant correlation between RAS practice hours and performance in the Tubes task (Pearson correlation; p-value = 0.20), Suture Sponge task (Pearson correlation; p-value = 0.07), and Dots and Needles task (Pearson correlation; p-value = 0.85)."
