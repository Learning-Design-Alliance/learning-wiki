## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretive argument in the Discussion, linking their questionnaire and retrospective findings to prior work on education and feedback; it is an interpretation, not a tested intervention in this study.

> "These results suggest that the knowledge of LPV strategy is directly related to its implementation, and can explain the pattern of increased LPV use in general anaesthesia. Therefore, this study provide further supportive evidence for the effect of education and feedback that decreased the average intraoperative tidal volume and improved the rate of LPV use [21]."
