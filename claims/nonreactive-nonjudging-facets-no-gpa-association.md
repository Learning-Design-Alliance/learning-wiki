## Evidence

### Mathkor Darin Mansor 2025

Mathkor Darin Mansor. (2025). Mindfulness and academic performance among nursing students in Saudi Arabia: a cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports 15, 27381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-11911-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of the 276-student cross-sectional sample found no significant association for these two facets; the abstract reports "no association with GPA in both adjusted and unadjusted models (p = 0.134 and 0.728, respectively)". No effect size was printed for this finding.

> "However, the “non-reactive” and “non-judging” facets showed no association with GPA in both adjusted and unadjusted models (p = 0.134 and 0.728, respectively)."
