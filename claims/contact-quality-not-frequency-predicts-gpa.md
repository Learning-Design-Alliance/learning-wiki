## Evidence

### Kahalon 2023

Kahalon, R., Shnabel, N., Sharvit, K., Halabi, S., & Wright, S. C. (2023). High-Quality Contact With Fellow Majority Group Students Is Associated With Better Academic Performance of Minority Group Students. Personality & Social Psychology Bulletin, 49(12), 1723–1736. https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672221115943

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal regression (N = 90 Arab first-year psychology students) predicting end-of-year GPA from contact quality and frequency at Time 1, controlling for demographics, pre-university achievements, pre-university contact, and intergroup perceptions. The article reports "the quality (but not the frequency) of contact with Jewish students at university statistically predicted higher GPA, R2 = .03".

> "As expected, the quality (but not the frequency) of contact with Jewish students at university statistically predicted higher GPA, R2 = .03, 90% CI[>.01, .16], above and beyond the other predictors, including pre-university frequency of contact, which also predicted better academic performance, R2 = .04, 90% CI[.01, .17]."
