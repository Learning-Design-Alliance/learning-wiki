# Memory recall declines over the 12-hour retention interval between encoding and retrieval

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Paired-associate recall was poorer at session 2 than session 1, a large within-subject decline (d = 1.36). [→ Pontifex 2016](#pontifex-2016)

## Evidence

### Pontifex 2016

Pontifex, M. B., Gwizdala, K. L., Parks, A. C., Pfeiffer, K. A. & Fenn, K. M. (2016). The Association between Physical Activity During the Day and Long-Term Memory Stability. Scientific Reports 6, 38148. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep38148

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Within-sample paired t-test across the 92 participants comparing cued recall at the two sessions. Recall "decrease[d] over time", from 49.4 ± 6.8 to 47.0 ± 7.8 words, t(91) = 7.2, p < 0.001, d = 1.36.

> "Memory recall was observed to decrease over time, with poorer performance observed at session 2 (47.0 ± 7.8 words) relative to session 1 (49.4 ± 6.8 words; t (91) = 7.2, p < 0.001, d = 1.36)"
