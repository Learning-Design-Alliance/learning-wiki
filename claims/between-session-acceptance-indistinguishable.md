Aung Pyae. (2026). AI-Automation Tooling in Computer Engineering Education: Mixed-Methods TAM/UTAUT Evidence for a General Acceptance Attitude. [doi:10.1109/icteched69022.2026.11682906](https://doi.org/10.1109/icteched69022.2026.11682906)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-session comparison of construct scores across the three workshops (n = 31 / 45 / 27). The article reports all tests non-significant with construct medians within 0.50 scale points, and reads this as supporting protocol fidelity and ruling out session-level confounds.

> "Kruskal–Wallis tests across the three workshops (n = 31 / 45 / 27) were non-significant for every construct (PE H = 0.26, p = 0.88; EE H = 1.83, p = 0.40; BI H = 0.21, p = 0.90; SE H = 1.60, p = 0.45; HM H = 0.36, p = 0.84; OQ H = 0.03, p = 0.99) and for the 12-item grand mean (H = 0.008, p = 0.996)."
