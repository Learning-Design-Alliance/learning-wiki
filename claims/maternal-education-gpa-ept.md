### Wiingreen R 2025

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Register-based analysis of GPA by maternal educational level (ISCED low, medium, high) in the nationwide cohort. The stepwise gradient persisted within strata of preschool IQ, supporting maternal education as an independent confounder examined by the authors.

> "Maternal educational level was associated with GPA in a stepwise relationship, with decreasing GPA by decreasing educational level (Table S6). This association persisted when stratified by groups of IQ levels (Figure S2)."
