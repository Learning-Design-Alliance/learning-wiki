## Evidence

### Zdeslav Hrepic 2011

Zdeslav Hrepic. (2011). Students' Concepts in Understanding of Sound. Originally published 1998. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1105.3995](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1105.3995)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results on question C3 (N=79) of the written survey, in which a wooden barrier was very obvious. The author reports "60% of respondents explicitly state the barrier slows down the sound"; characteristic alternative answers were practically the same at all levels.

> "60% of respondents explicitly state the barrier slows down the sound and therefore the sound will be heard first by listener B. 19% correctly stated the sound would propagate faster through the wood than through the air."
