## Evidence

### Stauffer Tobias 2026

Stauffer Tobias, Gutiez Blanca, Babst Reto, Lohmeyer Quentin, Meboldt Mirko. (2026). Augmented reality enhanced physical simulation for femoral nailing training. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-026-03646-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in the same 23-participant cohort showed alignment time "decreased significantly" across runs. At retention the average increased to 80.1 s but remained significantly lower than the first run (p = 0.031).

> "The average completion time decreased significantly from 139.6 s (median 121.0 s) in the first run to 59.4 s (median 37.5 s) in the third run (p < 0.001)."
