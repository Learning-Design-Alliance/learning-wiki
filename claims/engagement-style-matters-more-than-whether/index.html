Evidence¶

Mahbub Ul Alam 2026¶

Mahbub Ul Alam. (2026). PAUSE: A Privacy-Preserving Self-Reflection Tool for AI-Associated Cognitive Offloading. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13155 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.13155

q2 · i? · design · r1

The review's synthesis of the counter-design literature (Gerlich 2025b; Drosos et al. 2025; Wan and Kalman 2026) states these practices "can mitigate or reverse offloading effects".

"Structured prompting, deliberate verification, alternation between AI-assisted and AI-free work, and exposure to diverse outputs can mitigate or reverse offloading effects."

Mahbub Ul Alam 2026 (2)¶

Mahbub Ul Alam. (2026). PAUSE: A Privacy-Preserving Self-Reflection Tool for AI-Associated Cognitive Offloading. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13155

q2 · i? · design · r1

The review attributes to Makransky et al. (2025) that a chatbot prompting students to "connect ideas and explain their reasoning" beat traditional instruction on assessment performance.

"Makransky et al. (2025) showed that a generative-AI tutoring chatbot which prompted students to connect ideas and explain their reasoning produced better assessment performance than traditional instruction."
