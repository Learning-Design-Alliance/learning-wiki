### Mascarenhas Sharon 2021

Mascarenhas Sharon, Al-Halabi Manal, Otaki Farah, Nasaif Mohamed, Davis Dave. (2021). Simulation-based education for selected communication skills: exploring the perception of post-graduate dental students. Korean J Med Educ 33(1):11-25. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2021.183

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group data from the process theme (simulation activity category); students described stress from observation and judgement, e.g., "knowing that everyone is watching and evaluating you is stressful". Qualitative perceptions only.

> ""having professors there evaluating you was stressful…""

### Mascarenhas Sharon 2021 (2)

Mascarenhas Sharon, Al-Halabi Manal, Otaki Farah, Nasaif Mohamed, Davis Dave. (2021). Simulation-based education for selected communication skills: exploring the perception of post-graduate dental students. Korean J Med Educ 33(1):11-25. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2021.183

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' report of a subset of participants' perceptions in the Discussion, aligned by the authors with another study showing SBE-induced stress declining with repetition. Not tested in this study.

> "some participating students reported that going through SBE can decrease their stress levels on the long run when they are faced with similar situations, in their clinical practice."
