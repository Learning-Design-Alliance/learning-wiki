## Evidence

### Kjellenberg Karin 2025

Kjellenberg Karin, Helgadóttir Björg, Ekblom Örjan, Nyberg Gisela. (2025). Fitness and Screen Time at Age 13 Relates to Academic Performance at Age 16. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70024

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Finding from the adjusted multilevel models and margins predictions in the longitudinal cohort; Table 1 shows 77.5% of the A–D group versus 51.8% of the E/F group had parents with long education (p < 0.001).

> "our findings revealed that adolescents with parents who had long education had higher grades, compared to those with parents who had short education. Additionally, we observed that the probability of progressing from a low grade (E or F) at age 13 to an A–D at age 16 was greater among adolescents whose parents had long education."
