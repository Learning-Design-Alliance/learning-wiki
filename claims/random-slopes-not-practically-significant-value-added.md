### Leckie 2023

Leckie, G., Parker, R., Goldstein, H., & Tilling, K. (2023). Mixed-Effects Location Scale Models for Joint Modeling School Value-Added Effects on the Mean and Variance of Student Achievement. [doi:10.3102/10769986231210808](https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986231210808)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of random-intercept (Model 1) and random-slope (Model 4) value-added models fitted to the London sample; Model 4 is preferred on DIC (ΔDIC = 281). The article reports the residual variance "reduces by just 0.70%" and, for Model 5, that the slope-interaction variance component accounts for "less than 1% of the variance in nearly all schools."

> "The plots appear very similar suggesting that while the random slopes are statistically significant they are not practically significant. Indeed, moving from Model 1 to Model 4 the residual variance reduces by just 0.70%."
