### Woolf K 2016

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative subtheme on well-being: trainees valued family support but long hours, inflexible training and lack of autonomy over location made it hard to obtain, with burnout and anxiety described in accounts.

> "BME UKGs and IMGs in our sample were more likely to mention mental health problems caused by work stresses including problematic relationships with colleagues that lowered confidence, burnout, social isolation and lack of pastoral support. These problems impeded learning and performance at work."
