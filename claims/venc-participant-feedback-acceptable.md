## Evidence

### Umoren RA 2025

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Six-month follow-up survey on a 1-to-5 Likert scale completed by participants in the Lagos cohort; the article also reports nausea or headache in VR mode for 17 of 62 (27%) and slow navigation on older devices for 28 of 62 (45%). No effect size printed.

> "participants agreed or strongly agreed that the app was a convenient educational resource (50 of 61 [82%]), provided valuable feedback (51 of 61 [84%]), and was valuable for clinical practice (51 of 60 [85%]). Most participants would use the app again (46 of 58 [79%]) and would recommend it to their colleagues (47 of 55 [85%]). Positive ratings for ease of navigation were somewhat lower (36 of 60 [60%])"
