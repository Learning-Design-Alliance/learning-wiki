### Teresa M. Ober 2026

Teresa M. Ober, Karyssa A. Courey, Michael Flor. (2026). Integrating Topic Modeling and LLM Prompt Engineering into a Human-driven Approach to Analyze Interview Transcripts. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 18, No 1. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/1008

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of focus group interviews with 13 U.S. teachers on skill conceptualization. Communication was described as inherently multimodal, encompassing speaking, listening, writing, and presenting; teachers expressed concerns about vague or "squishy" frameworks. Digital literacy was framed as a fluid skill shaped by rapidly evolving digital tools.

> "Teachers emphasized that both communication and digital literacy require clear definitions and must be framed in ways that are pedagogically and contextually relevant."
