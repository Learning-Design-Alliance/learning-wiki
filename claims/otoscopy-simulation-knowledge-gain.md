`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Trial by Fieux et al. (2021): 196 of 200 participating students completed the knowledge score after low-fidelity otoscopy simulation versus anatomical training. The review reports "Scores were 32.0% higher in the simulation group than in the control group."

> "Scores were 32.0% higher in the simulation group than in the control group (mean scores: 12.0/20 vs. 9.1/20; p < 0.0001)."
