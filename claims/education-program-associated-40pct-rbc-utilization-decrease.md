## Evidence

### Rahav Koren Roni 2017

Rahav Koren Roni, Suriu Celia, Yakir Orly, Akria Luiza, Barhoum Masad, Braester Andrei. (2017). Physicians' lack of knowledge - a possible reason for red blood cell transfusion overuse? Israel Journal of Health Policy Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13584-017-0173-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Uncontrolled before-and-after practice measure at Galilee Medical Center, where annual RBC consumption had been 6500 units. The program included lectures, clinical cases discussed in group meetings, biennial conventions, and a 24/7 blood coordinator. The authors say this measure reinforces their hypothesis.

> "In the year following the questionnaire, RBC utilization decreased to 4000 units hospital wide (approximately 40%) with a total cost savings of approximately 900,000 ILS."
