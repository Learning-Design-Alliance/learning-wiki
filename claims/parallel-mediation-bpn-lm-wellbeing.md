`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap test (5,000 resamples) in the same SEM of 518 students. Both parallel indirect effects were significant; the printed β values (0.108 and 0.045) are small standardized coefficients with confidence intervals excluding zero.

> "Parallel mediation: basic psychological needs (β = 0.108, 95% CI [0.050, 0.184], P < 0.001, supporting H7) and learning motivation (β = 0.045, 95% CI [0.005, 0.106], P = 0.032, supporting H8) exerted indirect effects"
