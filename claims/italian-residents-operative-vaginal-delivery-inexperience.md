Results of the national resident survey: "Fifty-two doctors in training had never performed an operative vaginal delivery" and 86% do not feel confident performing one. The authors attribute this partly to the infrequent occurrence of operative delivery and possible medico-legal consequences.

> "Fifty-two doctors in training had never performed an operative vaginal delivery, whereas only one performed more than 30 procedures. Moreover, 86% of them do not feel confident to perform an operative vaginal delivery."
