## Evidence

### Fieux Maxime 2021

Fieux Maxime, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Bartier Sophie, Tringali Stéphane. (2021). Otoskills training during covid-19 pandemic: a before-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02706-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The linear mixed-effects model included an interaction between assessment time and experience level to investigate variation in learning times with experience; the interaction was not statistically significant (p = 0.139), and the GRS interaction was likewise non-significant (p = 0.520).

> "This improvement was not significantly different between the novice group, 0.44 [95 % CI, 0.28–0.59], and the intermediate group, 0.60 [95 % CI, 0.43–0.78] (p-value for interaction = 0.139)."

### Fieux Maxime 2021 (2)

Fieux Maxime, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Bartier Sophie, Tringali Stéphane. (2021). Otoskills training during covid-19 pandemic: a before-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02706-8

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation (e) of the subgroup pattern: they link the smaller intermediate gains to deficiencies in self-assessment and a possible ceiling effect of the adopted scale, and propose offering exercises adapted to practice level.

> "Subgroup analyses showed that although intermediate residents improved significantly after training, their performance increased less than that of the novices, suggesting that skill levels plateau after an initial rapid improvement."
