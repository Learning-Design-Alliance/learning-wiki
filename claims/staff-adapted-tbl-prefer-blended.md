## Evidence

### Fouda Shaimaa M. 2025

Fouda Shaimaa M., Madi Marwa, Ellakany Passent, Aly Nourhan M., Bahgat Hala A., Elembaby Abeer E., Virtanen Jorma I., AlHumaid Jehan. (2025). The perception of dental students and educators about e-learning during COVID − 19 pandemic. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-06152-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 74 teaching staff (75.6% response rate) with a 17-item attitude questionnaire on a three-point Likert scale. "Most teaching staff easily adapted to the shift from traditional to TBL (74.3%)" and 73% preferred blended learning post-pandemic.

> "Most teaching staff easily adapted to the shift from traditional to TBL (74.3%), believe that institutional technical support is adequate to adopt TBL (71.7%), and prefer blended learning after the pandemic (73%). However, only 14.9% used TBL before the pandemic, 17.6% think that TBL has a positive influence on academic performance, and 19% prefer TBL in all teaching activities"
