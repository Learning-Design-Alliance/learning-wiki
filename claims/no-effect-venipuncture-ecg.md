## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tsikas Stefanos A. 2024

Tsikas Stefanos A., Afshar Kambiz, Fischer Volkhard. (2024). Does voluntary practice improve the outcome of an OSCE in undergraduate medical studies? A Propensity Score Matching approach. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0312387

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Rosenbaum-Bounds sensitivity analysis of PSM treatment effects: for venipuncture (ATT -2.77%) and ECG (ATT -1.92%) the matched differences were not significant, and the article concludes the observed differences are "explained by unobserved factors".

> "Thirdly, for the medical skills venipuncture and ECG, treatment effects become significant only when large confounding is modeled. This suggests that the observed differences between FP and No FP are explained by unobserved factors."
