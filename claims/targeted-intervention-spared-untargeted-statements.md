## Evidence

### Saterbak Ann 2018

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-squared comparisons for Statements 9–13, unlinked to either learning outcome, across PvI, SvI, and RQ3; "13 of 15 comparisons" did not show significant improvement. No effect size printed.

> "Since the majority (13 of 15 comparisons) did not show significant improvement, we can conclude that an intervention that targets one aspect of student learning does not positively or negatively affect student performance in other areas of the assignment."

### Saterbak Ann 2018 (2)

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

PvI chi-squared results for Statements 12–13; "the peer evaluations got worse after the intervention for these two statements". No similar decline appeared for RQ2 or RQ3. No effect size printed.

> "That said, two of 15 comparisons, namely Statements 12 and 13 for RQ1 (PvI), did return significant results, but without convergence. This means that as compared to the instructor, the peer evaluations got worse after the intervention for these two statements."
