## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pairwise comparisons revealed no significant differences between the slopes of retrieval and calculation learning curves, but intercepts differed significantly, with retrieval faster than calculation for both operators. [→ Delaney 1998](#delaney-1998)

## Evidence

### Delaney 1998

Delaney, P.F., Reder, L.M., Staszewski, J.J., & Ritter, F. (1998). The strategy-specific nature of improvement: The power law applies by strategy within task. Psychological Science, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9280.00001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of individual-strategy power-function parameters for the feeling-of-knowing data. Two-tailed pairwise comparisons found no significant slope differences (e.g., t(28) = 1.63 for multiplication), while intercepts differed significantly: "retrieval being faster than calculation for both multiplication, t(27) = 18.67, p < .001."

> "The intercepts did differ, with retrieval being faster than calculation for both multiplication, t(27) = 18.67, p < .001, and sharp, t(17) = 9.13, p < .001."
