`q3 i1` Unmarried residents scored higher than married residents on MHLS total (d = 0.28) and belief subscale (d = 0.30), and residents without children scored higher than those with children (d = 0.24). [→ Aydin Zeynep 2025](#aydin-zeynep-2025)

## Evidence

### Aydin Zeynep 2025

Aydin Zeynep, Borlu Arda, Durmuş Hasan, Çeti̇nkaya Fevziye. (2025). Mental health literacy among resident physicians in Türkiye: a neglected competency in medical training?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08243-y

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Univariate t-tests in the cross-sectional survey (n = 305). Table 6 additionally reports belief-subscale differences by marital status (d = 0.30, p = 0.009) and by having children (d = 0.24, p = 0.044).

> "Unmarried residents demonstrated higher total scores compared to married residents (MD = 0.80, 95% CI: 0.16–1.44, p = 0.015; d ≈ 0.28)."
