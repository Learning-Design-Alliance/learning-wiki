Evidence¶

Ercan 2017¶

Ercan, O., Ural, E., & Köse, S. (2017). The Effect of Web Assisted Learning with Emotional Intelligence Content on Students' Information about Energy Saving, Attitudes Towards Environment and Emotional Intelligence. Science Education International, 28(1), 78-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143753

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Dependent samples t-tests on the 60 experimental-group students' pre- and post-test scores. All three differences were statistically significant in favor of the post test; only test statistics are printed, no effect sizes.

"The results display that there is a statistically significant difference between the pre -test and post -test AAT scores [t(59)= -11,787; p=0.000 < 0.01], EI scores [t(59)=3.282; p=0.002 < 0.01] and EAS scores [t(59)=2.939; p=0.005 < 0.01] in fa vor of the post test."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • At pre-test the control group scored significantly higher on the achievement test, while EI and environmental attitudes were comparable across groups — related
  • Web-assisted instruction with emotional intelligence content produced higher post-test academic achievement, environmental attitudes and emotional intelligence than traditional lecture in 8th-grade energy-saving instruction — related
  • Within the experimental group, attitudes rose significantly from pretest to posttest and monitoring test, and the gain held between posttest and monitoring test — related
  • The experimental group's pretest-to-post-test attitude gain was significantly larger than the control group's, but the behavior dimension showed no significant difference — related
  • Within the experimental group, POESPBEE produced significant gains in attitudes and behaviors that were maintained at follow-up, with large within-group effect sizes — related
