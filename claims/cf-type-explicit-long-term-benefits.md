### Jang 2020

Jang, Sung-Soo. (2020). The efficacy of different types of metalinguistic information in L2 written corrective feedback. English Teaching, 75(4), 33-56. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.75.4.202012.33

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Delayed posttest results of the error correction test in the four-session experiment, taken two weeks after the final CF treatment. The study reports all experimental groups "outperformed the CO group on the delayed posttest with large effect sizes", including the MC group (d = 1.115).

> "All the experimental groups outperformed the CO group on the delayed posttest with large effect sizes ( d = 1.424 for DR; d = 1.115 for MC; d = 2.038 for MS; d = 1.217 for MN)."
