## Evidence

### Zhang Juan 2024

Zhang Juan, Chen Hong, Wang Xie, Huang Xiaofeng, Xie Daojun. (2024). Application of flipped classroom teaching method based on ADDIE concept in clinical teaching for neurology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05343-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective controlled experiment with 90 neurology residents randomised by number table to control (n=45) or observation (n=45) groups for a one-month rotation. Table 2 reports theoretical scores of 41.94 ± 3.11 vs 45.62 ± 2.83 and skills scores of 41.82 ± 3.54 vs 46.76 ± 2.68, t-values 3.56 and 3.97, "statistically significant (P < 0.001)".

> "the theoretical and clinical skills assessment scores of the observation group were significantly higher than those of the control group, and the results were statistically significant (P < 0.001)"
