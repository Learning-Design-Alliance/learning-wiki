## Evidence

### Schleepen TMJ 2016

Schleepen TMJ, Van Mier HI, De Smedt B (2016) The Contribution of Numerical Magnitude Comparison and Phonological Processing to Individual Differences in Fourth Graders' Multiplication Fact Ability. PLoS ONE 11(6): e0158335. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hierarchical regression and correlational analyses (N = 63): the NWR task entered in step 3 explained 7% (β = -.28), but its unique contribution was modest and the authors acknowledge the FDR correction over 36 correlations was fairly conservative.

> "Importantly, it should be mentioned that only a small amount of variance (i.e. 0.7%) was explained by performance in the NWR-task. Also, the correlation between the NWR-task and multiplication facts was not significant anymore after the FDR-correction."

### Schleepen TMJ 2016 (2)

Schleepen TMJ, Van Mier HI, De Smedt B (2016) The Contribution of Numerical Magnitude Comparison and Phonological Processing to Individual Differences in Fourth Graders' Multiplication Fact Ability. PLoS ONE 11(6): e0158335. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analyses (N = 63) found no significant FAT-multiplication correlation; separate subtest correlations were also nonsignificant (phoneme deletion: r = -.18, p = .15; phoneme exchange: r = -.14, p = .28, uncorrected).

> "No significant correlation was found between phonological awareness, as measured by accuracy on the FAT, and performance of multiplication facts."
