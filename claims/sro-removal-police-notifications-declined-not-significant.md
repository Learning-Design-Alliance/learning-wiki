## Evidence

### Arneson 2024

Arneson, A., Hinze-Pifer, R., Franklin, K., & Johnson, D.W. (2024). Removing police officers from Chicago schools: Trends and outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/removing-police-officers-from-Chicago-schools

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

School-level difference-in-difference analysis of police notifications in CPS high schools. Figure 10 shows an estimated difference of -7.4 notifications for full removal; the interpretive summary states notifications were "cut approximately in half, on average" in full-removal schools.

> "High schools that fully removed SROs had a steeper drop in the number of police notifications in 2022–23 than did schools that retained both SROs. This difference-in-difference was not statistically significant, but it was substantively large."
