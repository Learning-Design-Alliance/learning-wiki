## Evidence

### Wilkes S. 2026

Wilkes S., Maggio L. A., Martin P. C., Melton J., Zheng B. (2026). Self-Directed Learning in Health Professions Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2128

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression across five SDL modality categories found no significant moderation. A parallel analysis of teacher role likewise found "no significant moderation effect was detected (F(2, 71) = 0.56, p = 0.574)", with facilitator role showing the largest descriptive effect (d = 0.54).

> "SDL modality analysis revealed no significant differences between delivery methods (F(4, 69) = 0.16, p = 0.759)."
