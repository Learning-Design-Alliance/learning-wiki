Evidence¶

Xiaomeng Xu 2026¶

Xiaomeng Xu, Dan Zhang & Yu Zhang. (2026). Student teacher inter brain coupling forecast cross semester academic fluctuation in real world classrooms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00421-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Subsample dynamic-model analysis of the junior students within the longitudinal hyperscanning study. Broadband and high-beta ΔTI significantly predicted Chinese score increases, and broadband and high-beta baseline TI also positively forecast improvement (e.g., high-beta TI posterior mean = 0.797).

"In the junior student subgroup, changes in both broadband and high -beta TI values significantly predicted improvements in Chinese scores (Δbroadband TI: posterior mean = 0.520, 95% HPDI [0.004, 1.063]; Δhigh-beta TI: posterior mean = 0.643, 95% HPDI [0.163, 1.114])."

Xiaomeng Xu 2026 (2)¶

Xiaomeng Xu, Dan Zhang & Yu Zhang. (2026). Student teacher inter brain coupling forecast cross semester academic fluctuation in real world classrooms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00421-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The same dynamic models estimated for the senior student subsample found no credible effects in either subject, since 95% HPDIs included zero. The authors treat this grade-level heterogeneity as a potential boundary condition related to developmental and instructional shifts.

"By contrast, no credible effects were observed in the senior student subgroup for either subject, as the 95% HPDIs included zero."
