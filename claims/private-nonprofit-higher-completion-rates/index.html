Ed Trust analysis of IPEDS 2014 Cohort Completion Rate data, displayed by race/ethnicity and sector in Figure 1. The report states this pattern "remains even when the data is disaggregated by race and ethnicity," citing it as a reason access to these institutions matters.

"In 2020, the six-year completion rate for students who began seeking a bachelor's degree in 2014 was 68% at private nonprofit institutions, versus 63% at public institutions and 64% across all four-year degree-granting institutions."
