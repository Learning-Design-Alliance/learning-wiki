## Evidence

### Otu Akaninyene 2016

Otu Akaninyene, Ebenso Bassey, Okuzu Okey, Osifo-Dawodu Egbe. (2016). Using a mHealth tutorial application to change knowledge and attitude of frontline health workers to Ebola virus disease in Nigeria: a before-and-after study. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-016-0100-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Attitude component of the before-and-after KAP survey among the same 203 health workers; seven Likert-style questions aggregated into Agree, Not Sure or Disagree and compared pre- and post-tutorial (Table 5).

> "Additionally, there was reinforcement of positive attitudes of avoiding the following: contact with Ebola patients, eating bush meat and risky burial practices as indicated by increases between pre- and post-intervention scores from 83 to 92%, 57 to 64% and 67 to 79%, respectively."

### Otu Akaninyene 2016 (2)

Otu Akaninyene, Ebenso Bassey, Okuzu Okey, Osifo-Dawodu Egbe. (2016). Using a mHealth tutorial application to change knowledge and attitude of frontline health workers to Ebola virus disease in Nigeria: a before-and-after study. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-016-0100-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported fear item in the attitude survey (Table 5): those answering Fearful dropped from 180 (89%) to 105 (52%), with undecided responses rising to 47%.

> "Interestingly, health workers who feared EVD reduced significantly from 89 to 52%."
