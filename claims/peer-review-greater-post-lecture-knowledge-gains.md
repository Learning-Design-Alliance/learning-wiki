## Evidence

### Abbas Uzair 2026

Abbas Uzair, Fatima Mehreen, Kamran Durre Sameen, Rasheed Binish, Naz Ramlah, Arshad Fatima. (2026). Impact of peer assessment on lecture quality and student learning outcomes in undergraduate medical education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09042-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-intervention between-group comparison of post-lecture knowledge scores using identical structured pre/post tests. The results report "a significantly higher number" in the intervention group (83 vs 76, p = 0.003; Mann-Whitney U test per Fig. 4C).

> "We also compared knowledge between control and intervention groups after the intervention. The intervention group scored a significantly higher number (76 vs 83; p = 0.003; Fig. 4C)"

### Abbas Uzair 2026 (2)

Abbas Uzair, Fatima Mehreen, Kamran Durre Sameen, Rasheed Binish, Naz Ramlah, Arshad Fatima. (2026). Impact of peer assessment on lecture quality and student learning outcomes in undergraduate medical education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09042-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre-to-post knowledge comparisons after the intervention using paired sample t-tests: control 69 to 73 (p = 0.032), intervention 71 to 83 (p < 0.001), described as "significantly higher gain of knowledge" in the intervention group.

> "The control group scored 69 vs 73 pre and post intervention period respectively (p = 0.032; Fig. 4A). However, significantly higher gain of knowledge was reported in intervention group (71 vs 83 pre and post intervention respectively; p < 0.001; Fig. 4B)"
