Evidence¶

Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu 2017¶

Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu. (2017). The Impact of Consecutive Interpreting Training on the L2 Listening Competence Enhancement. English Language Teaching, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n1p72

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same 50-student pre-test-post-test experiment, paired-sample t-tests on the CATTI-style summary test showed significant gains for both groups (CG t=3.56, p=0.002; EG t=10.45, p<0.001), which the article reads as improvement in selective listening skill.

"A paired-sample t-test showed that both groups scored significantly higher in the post-test than in the pre-test in summary writing (for CG , t=3.56, p.=0.002 < 0.01; for EG , t=10.45, p.< 0.001)."

Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu 2017 (2)¶

Tongtong Zhang & Zhiwei Wu. (2017). The Impact of Consecutive Interpreting Training on the L2 Listening Competence Enhancement. English Language Teaching, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n1p72

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-sample t-tests on summary writing showed comparable pre-test levels (t=-1.18, p=0.24) but a significant post-test difference (t=-3.29, p=0.002), with EG achieving "much better improvement than CG". No effect size is printed.

"An independent sample t-test showed that the two groups were not statistically diff erent from each in the pre-test (t=- 1.18, p.=0.24>0.05); but significant difference was found in the post-test (t=-3.29, p.=0.002<0.01)."
