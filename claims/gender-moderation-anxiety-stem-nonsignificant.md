## Evidence

### Daker 2021

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Sokolowski, H.M., Green, A.E., & Lyons, I.M. (2021). First-year students' math anxiety predicts STEM avoidance and underperformance throughout university, independently of math ability. npj Science of Learning 6:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00095-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation models testing gender: the article reports the interactions "failed to reach traditional significance levels" for both outcomes, while noting both were near threshold with effect sizes ~.28. Women reported significantly greater math anxiety (t(181) = −5.35, d = −.823).

> "the Gender × Math Anxiety term failed to reach traditional significance levels ( α = .05) for either % STEM Courses ( β = .295, t(173) = 1.79, p = .075, Cohen’s d = .272) or STEM Grades ( β = .267, t(173) = 1.97, p = .051, Cohen’s d = .299)"
