## Evidence

### Benjamin R. Brown 2016

Benjamin R. Brown, Andrew Mason, and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving performance in quantum mechanics with explicit incentives to correct mistakes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010121)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A secondary analysis on four non-repeated final exam problems found no statistically significant difference between groups, suggesting the improvement was specific to the self-diagnosed problems rather than a general self-monitoring gain.

> "we compared the performance of students in the incentivized and comparison groups on four other problems on the same ﬁnal exam for which incentivized group students did not diagnose their mistakes. We ﬁnd that while the incentivized group scored higher than the comparison group, the results are not statistically signiﬁcant."
