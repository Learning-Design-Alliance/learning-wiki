## Evidence

### Linda Argote; Sara L. Beckman; Dennis Epple 1990

Linda Argote; Sara L. Beckman; Dennis Epple. (1990). The Persistence and Transfer of Learning in Industrial Settings. Management Science, Vol. 36, No. 2, pp. 140-154. http://links.jstor.org/sici?sici=0025-1909%28199002%2936%3A2%3C140%3ATPATOL%3E2.0.CO%3B2-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Maximum likelihood estimation of a production function with a depreciating knowledge stock, using monthly data from 13 Liberty Ship shipyards. The estimate of h = 0.75 implies "only 3.2% (0.7512) would remain one year later"; a 93% confidence interval of roughly (0.65, 0.85) is reported.

> "Indeed, these results indicate a rapid rate of depreciation. A value of h = 0.75 implies that, from a stock of knowledge available at the beginning of a year, only 3.2% (0.7512) would remain one year later."
