## Evidence

### Bowman K 2023

Bowman K, Cadman T, Goncalves Soares A, Robinson O, Hughes A, Heron J, Segal AB, Huerta MC, Howe LD. (2023). Mediators of the association between childhood body mass index and educational attainment: Analysis of a UK prospective cohort study. Pediatric Obesity. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpo.13014

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Individual-mediator g-computation models in the imputed ALSPAC sample (females n = 3544, males n = 3405), with bullying as intermediate confounder. The female indirect effect was "NIE β = −1.53" with 44.3% mediated; the male estimate was −0.70 with a CI spanning the null.

> "There was evidence of mediation by externalizing behaviours in females (NIE β = −1.53 95% CI −2.94, −0.12, proportion mediated 44.3%). The direction of the indirect effects was the same in males, however, the confidence interval was wide and included the null value (NIE β = −0.70, 95% CI −2.35, 0.95)."
