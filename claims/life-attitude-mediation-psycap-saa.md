`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 samples, PROCESS Model 6) of the 1,019-student survey. The life-attitude pathway was significant: "life attitude mediated 37.87% of the effect (effect value = 0.114, Bootstrap 95% CI: [0.040, 0.176])". Total mediating effect was 0.121 (40.20%).
