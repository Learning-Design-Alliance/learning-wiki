## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2024

Taewon Kim, Hakjoo Kim, Benjamin A. Philip & David L. Wright. (2024). M1 recruitment during interleaved practice is important for encoding, not just consolidation, of skill memory. npj Science of Learning 9, 77. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00290-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 2 (Group: IP-Sham, IP-ctDCS) × 9 (Block) repeated-measures ANOVA on training performance found a significant interaction, with "higher TT for the IP-ctDCS participants" on all training blocks except Block 9.

> "We found a signi ﬁcant interaction, F (8, 272) = 2.26, p < 0.05, ηp 2 = 0.06), resulting from higher TT for the IP-ctDCS participants com- pared to their IP-sham counterparts for all training blocks except for Block 9"

### Taewon Kim 2024 (2)

Taewon Kim, Hakjoo Kim, Benjamin A. Philip & David L. Wright. (2024). M1 recruitment during interleaved practice is important for encoding, not just consolidation, of skill memory. npj Science of Learning 9, 77. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00290-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

On the retention test blocks (Post 5 min, 6 h, 24 h), a significant main effect of Group showed the encoding deficit from ctDCS during training "remained throughout retention", accounting for poorer novel skill memory for the IP-ctDCS group.

> "The impact the relatively poorer encoding as a result of ctDCS during IP training remained throughout retention, re ﬂected in a signi ﬁcant main effect of Group, F(1, 68) = 7.58, p <0 . 0 1 ,ηp 2 = 0.1, for the test blocks (p < 0.001 for all test blocks: Post 5min,P o s t6h,P o s t24h)"
