# Redundancy principle: animation plus narration beats animation, narration, and on-screen text for transfer

## Evidence

### Richard E. Mayer 2008

Richard E. Mayer. (2008). Applying the Science of Learning: Evidence-Based Principles for the Design of Multimedia Instruction. American Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066x.63.8.760

`q3 · i2` · `review · r2`

Controlled experiments adding concurrent on-screen text to narrated animations on lightning and environmental science; the article reports the quoted 5-of-5 result and that "The median effect size was 0.72, which is a medium effect."

> "in 5 out of 5 experiments, involving computer-based lessons on lightning and environmental science, people who received animation and narration performed better on a transfer test than people who received animation, narration, and on-screen text"
