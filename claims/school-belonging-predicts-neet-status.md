## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher school belonging at age 15 is associated with lower odds of being NEET at ages 16-20 (OR = 0.84 per SD). [→ Philip Parker 2021](#philip-parker-2021)

## Evidence

### Philip Parker 2021

Philip Parker, Kelly-Ann Allen, Rhiannon Parker, Jiesi Guo, Herbert W Marsh, Geetanjali Basarkod, and Theresa Dicke. (2021). School Belonging Predicts whether an Emerging Adult will be Not in Education, Employment, or Training (NEET) after School. https://www.pwc.com.au/government/government-matters/youth-unemployment.html

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel logistic regression of NEET status (waves 2-5) on age-15 school belonging in two LSAY cohorts; Table 3 reports School Belonging (SD Units) odds ratio 0.84 [0.81, 0.87], described as a "significant and strong protective influence".

> "Notwithstanding a number of strong predictors of NEET status, our results show that school belonging at age 15 had a significant and strong protective influence. Participants who felt like they belonged at school at age 15 were less likely to be NEET from ages 16 to 20."
