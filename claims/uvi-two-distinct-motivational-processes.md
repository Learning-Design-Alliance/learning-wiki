## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the combined moderated mediation model, engagement mediated the UVI main effect (z = 2.38, p = .02) and the UVI x FG x URM effect (z = 2.56, p = .01) on course grade, but personal focus did not (ps > .13). [→ Hecht 2019](#hecht-2019)

## Evidence

### Hecht 2019

Hecht, C. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2019). Promoting Persistence in the Biological and Medical Sciences: An Expectancy-Value Approach to Intervention. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000356

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The authors re-ran the original Harackiewicz et al. (2016) moderated mediation model adding personal focus as a mediator. Engagement mediated UVI effects on course grade while personal focus did not, and the reverse held for persistence, supporting two distinct processes.

> "the direct effect on persistence was mediated by personal focus (and not by engagement), the direct effect on course grades for FG-URM students (and for all students, on average) was mediated by engagement with course content (and not by personal focus)."
