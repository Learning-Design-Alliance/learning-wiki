# After critical reading instruction, the experimental group preferred reviewing over summarizing an ironical text while the control group did not

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Before the program, preference for summarizing versus reviewing did not differ significantly between the experimental and control groups (summarization χ2(2)=.181, p>.05; review χ2(2)=.153, p>.05). [→ Karabay 2015](#karabay-2015)
`q2 i?` After the program, the difference between the two groups in preference for review was statistically significant (χ2(1)=4.353, p<.05). [→ Karabay 2015 (2)](#karabay-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Karabay 2015

Aysegul Karabay. (2015). The guiding effects of a critical reading program on the use of external reading strategies when confronting an ironical text. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2419

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square analysis of strategy preferences in the pre-test with 60 pre-service teachers in one experimental and one control group of 30 each. The article reports no statistically significant pre-program difference between the "experimental group and control group".

> "Before the program, a Chi-square analysis revealed that the difference between the experimental group and control group was not statistically significant (for summarization [ χ2 (2) = .181, p>.05]; for review [χ2 (2) = .153, p>.05])."

### Karabay 2015 (2)

Aysegul Karabay. (2015). The guiding effects of a critical reading program on the use of external reading strategies when confronting an ironical text. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2419

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square analysis of post-test review preference in the same two-group quasi-experimental design. The article reports a statistically significant between-group difference "for review" with χ2 (1) = 4.353, p< .05; no effect size is printed.

> "However, after the program, a Chi-square analysis revealed that the difference between the two groups was statistically significant ( for review [ χ2 (1) = 4.353, p< .05])."
