Khalid Rabia, Van Zwieten Anita, Kim Siah, Didsbury Madeleine, Francis Anna, Mctaggart Steven, Walker Amanda, Mackie Fiona E., Prestidge Chanel, Teixeira-Pinto Armando, Barton Belinda, Lorenzo Jennifer, Lah Suncica, Howard Kirsten, Nassar Natasha, Au Eric, Tong Allison, Blazek Katrina, Craig Jonathan C., Wong Germaine. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Likelihood ratio tests of SES × CKD stage, age, and sex interactions in the KCAD cohort, with Bonferroni-corrected α of 0.006. The authors interpret this as socioeconomic disadvantage being equally detrimental across kidney disease severity, while noting the small dialysis subsample limited power.

> "There were no significant interactions between the six different SES measures and the variables of CKD stage, age group, or sex after accounting for multiple testing and comparisons."
