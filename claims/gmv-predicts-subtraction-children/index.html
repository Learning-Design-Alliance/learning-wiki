Zhang 2024¶

Zhang, D., Xie, Y., Wang, L. & Zhou, K. (2024). Structural and transcriptional signatures of arithmetic abilities in children. npj Science of Learning 9, 58. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00270-6

Cross-sectional predictive-modeling analysis of structural MRI and CMAT behavioral data from 130 children (grades 3-8). The subtraction model's observed-predicted correlation was significant, "sub- traction: r = 0.3506", and confirmed by two permutation testing approaches.
