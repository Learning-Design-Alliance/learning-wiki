## Evidence

### Sohail Fadieleh A 2025

Sohail Fadieleh A, Iqbal Marium, Malik Marib Ghulam Rasool, Saeed Sana. (2025). Perspectives of faculty, students and patients on clinical skills teaching in undergraduate medical education in Pakistan: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-103139

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme 1 findings from semi-structured interviews and focus groups at one Pakistani private medical college; students and house officers described mannequin practice reaching a point where, in their words, "performing on a mannequin becomes redundant" and real patients confer added benefit.

> "It is not that performing on a mannequin is bad—it has its benefits—but after a certain point, performing on a mannequin becomes redundant. Performing on a patient then has benefits."
