Evidence¶

Unal 2018¶

Unal, M., & Saglam, M. (2018). Examination of the effect of the GEMS program on problem solving and science process skills of 6 years old children. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(3), 567-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.3.567

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative explanatory-design component: semi-structured interviews with parents of the 11 experimental-group children, analyzed inductively with participants coded P1-P11; one parent reported that "my daughter’s interest in the mathematics and numbers increased with the GEMS education".

"After the application of GEMS education program throughout the year, the parents expressed that the children’s interests in science and mathematics increased in general, children started to ask more questions, started to do more activities with numbers and they were more successful in understan ding and solving mathematical problems."
