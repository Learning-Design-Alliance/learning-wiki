### Orellana Minerva A. 2025

Orellana Minerva A., Beetler Danielle J., Silvano Carmen J., Wuertz Ryan, Weisbrod Jennifer L., Roberts Lewis R., Windebank Anthony J., Enders Felicity T., Walther-Antonio Marina R. (2025). Integrative review in PhD admissions: A case study of efficiently minimizing bias while maximizing the student narrative. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323426

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-after analysis comparing integrative (n = 102) to algorithmic (n = 54) cohorts on the interviewed phase. The study reports students were "more likely to receive interviews with the integrative process" but explicitly attributes the possible cause to increased interview slot numbers introduced simultaneously, not to the review method itself.

> "We observed that students were more likely to receive interviews with the integrative process. However, this could be due to the fact that the number of interview slots increased at the same time that the integrative review process was implemented."
