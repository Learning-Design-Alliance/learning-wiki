## Evidence

### Noguchi 2026

Noguchi, M. (2026). Exploring Applications of Transfer-State Large Language Models: Cognitive Profiling and Socratic AI Tutoring. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.27454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.27454)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Applied Socratic tutoring experiment: the researcher acted as learner across 11 model conditions on the topic of comparative advantage, rated blind by three LLM coders using medians. Transfer conditions averaged "11.4 on average for the five transfer conditions" versus 7.2 for non-transfer; the article reports Cohen's d = 1.27 and per-indicator ratios of 1.73 (CMP-T), 1.62 (USM-T), and 1.42 (CSF-T).

> "The total tutoring-context score was 11.4 on average for the five transfer conditions (range: 5–15) and 7.2 on average for the corresponding five non-transfer conditions (range: 4–10), yielding a difference of 4.2 points."
