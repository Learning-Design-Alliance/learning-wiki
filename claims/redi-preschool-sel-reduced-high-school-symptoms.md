# The REDI preschool SEL enrichment reduced high school behavior problems (d = 0.27) and emotional symptoms (d = 0.41) 14 years later in an RCT of Head Start classrooms.

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Children randomized to REDI-enriched Head Start classrooms had fewer high school behavior problems than controls (d = 0.27, p = 0.03). [→ Bierman Karen L. 2025](#bierman-karen-l-2025)
`q3 i2` Children randomized to REDI had fewer high school emotional symptoms than controls (d = 0.41, p < 0.001). [→ Bierman Karen L. 2025 (2)](#bierman-karen-l-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Bierman Karen L. 2025

Bierman Karen L., Heinrichs Brenda S., Welsh Janet A., Jones Damon E., Crowley D. Max. (2025). How a Preschool Intervention Affected High School Outcomes: Longitudinal Pathways in a Randomized‐Controlled Trial. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.14235

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Two-level HLM (children nested in classrooms) in a cluster-randomized Head Start trial with 356 children followed 14 years; standardized estimates are "equivalent to effect sizes (Cohen's d)", giving d = 0.27 for behavior problems.

> "Hierarchical linear models revealed that children who received the REDI preschool program during Head Start had significantly fewer high school behavior problems (estimate = −0.23, SE = 0.10, p = 0.03)"

### Bierman Karen L. 2025 (2)

Bierman Karen L., Heinrichs Brenda S., Welsh Janet A., Jones Damon E., Crowley D. Max. (2025). How a Preschool Intervention Affected High School Outcomes: Longitudinal Pathways in a Randomized‐Controlled Trial. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.14235

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Same two-level HLM on the cluster-randomized trial; the standardized estimate "equivalent to effect sizes (Cohen's d)" was d = 0.41 for emotional symptoms (estimate = −0.71, p < 0.001).

> "Hierarchical linear models revealed that children who received the REDI preschool program during Head Start had significantly fewer high school behavior problems (estimate = −0.23, SE = 0.10, p = 0.03) and emotional symptoms (estimate = −0.71, SE = 0.21, p < 0.001) than children in the control group"
