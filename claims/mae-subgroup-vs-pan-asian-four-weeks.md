# Mean absolute error between the 19 subgroup means and the pan-Asian aggregate was 3.9 percentage points at pretest and 4.0 at posttest, roughly 4-5 weeks of learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The MAE between subgroup predicted means and the pan-Asian strata was 3.9 points (95% CI [3.2, 4.6]) at pretest and 4.0 points (95% CI [3.3, 4.7]) at posttest, equivalent to about 4-5 weeks of learning in a 16-week course. [→ Vy Le 2025](#vy-le-2025)

## Evidence

### Vy Le 2025

Vy Le, Grace Angell, Jayson Nissen, Ben Van Dusen. (2025). Challenging Data Aggregation Practices: A MAIHDA Study of Asian Student Outcomes in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.19049](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.19049)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Posterior MAE computation across 100,000 posterior samples from the Bayesian MAIHDA model, averaging absolute subgroup-versus-aggregate differences over the 19 subgroups. The article reports the MAE "corresponds to approximately 4–5 weeks of learning" relative to the pan-Asian 14.4-point gain.

> "The mean absolute error between the 19 subgroup predicted means and the pan-Asian strata was 3.9 percentage points (95% CI [3.2, 4.6]) at pretest and 4.0 percentage points (95% CI [3.3, 4.7]) at posttest."
