## Evidence

### Andrew Ferstl 2010

Andrew Ferstl. (2010). Teaching and Learning Science with Learning Assistants. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.3789](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.3789)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post STEBI administered to the eight LAs during 2009-2010. The article reports positive change on 4 of 23 questions, citing the teacher-effort item as an example. Changes are descriptive; no test statistic is printed.

> "Second, the STEBI was given to the LAs.   There was a positive change on 4 of the 23 questions.  One of those was "When a student does better than usual in science, it is often because the teacher exerted a little extra effort".  More of the LAs agreed or strongly agreed with the statement at the end of the semester."
