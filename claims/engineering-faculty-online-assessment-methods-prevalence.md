## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 63% of surveyed faculty used fully online exams (e.g., BB quizzes) and 70% used open-book/open-note exams during Spring 2020 online instruction. [→ Shadnaz Asgari 2021](#shadnaz-asgari-2021)
`q2 i?` Other methods in decreasing prevalence were project/term paper (50%), semi-online synchronous exam (40%), oral presentation/demo (33%), and semi-online asynchronous exam (28%). [→ Shadnaz Asgari 2021](#shadnaz-asgari-2021)

## Evidence

### Shadnaz Asgari 2021

Shadnaz Asgari, Jelena Trajkovic, Mehran Rahmani, Wenlu Zhang, Roger C. Lo, and Antonella Sciortino. (2021). An Observational Study of Engineering Online Education During the COVID-19 Pandemic. IEEE Access. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0250041](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250041)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Faculty survey (110 respondents, Qualtrics, June 20-July 10) asked which assessment methods were used in Spring 2020; Table 1 reports "the fully online exams such as the BB quizzes were used by 63% of the faculty", with open-book/open-note at 70% and respondents allowed multiple choices.

> "We observe that the fully online exams such as the BB quizzes were used by 63% of the faculty."
