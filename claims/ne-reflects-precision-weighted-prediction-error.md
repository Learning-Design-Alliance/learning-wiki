### Marc Deosdad-Díez & Josep Marco-Pallarés 2025 (2)

Marc Deosdad-Díez & Josep Marco-Pallarés. (2025). Note-by-note predictability modulates rhythm learning and its neural components. npj Science of Learning 10:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00353-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Authors' interpretation of the synchronization Ne model, which they report was "enhanced in more complex notes when the asynchronies were high"; they propose low-IC notes yield precise prediction errors used to update internal models, with asynchrony trends at first (β = 0.24) versus last repetition (β = −0.43; t = 4.08, p < 0.001).

> "enhanced in more complex notes when the asynchronies were high. Therefore, Ne was not related solely to erroneous responses, as previously suggested in performance-monitoring"
