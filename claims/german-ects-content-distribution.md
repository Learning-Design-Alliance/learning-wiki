Descriptive account of German university-phase curriculum structure under the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System, where one point represents 25 to 30 hours of study workload. Content courses span mechanics, thermodynamics, electricity, optics, atom and quantum physics, with depth varying by school track.

> "A typical study program for physics teachers includes 90 ECTS-points for subject matter studies and 30 ECTS-points for studies in physics education (40 credit points accounts for general educational studies, 120 points for the second subject) (DPG, 2014)."
