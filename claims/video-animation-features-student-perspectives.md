## Evidence

### Unal Cakiroglu 2017

Unal Cakiroglu, Huseyin Yilmaz. (2017). Using Videos and 3D Animations for Conceptual Learning in Basic Computer Units. Contemporary Educational Technology, 8(4), 390-405. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1158165

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic content analysis of clinical interviews with 6 students (3 from each group) selected for prominent post-test change. One student stated the visuals helped by "concretizing the abstract concepts"; another reported homework with videos made computer units "more visible  in my mind".

> "Students’ perspectives reflected that the videos and 3D animations facilitated the conceptual understanding via concretization, pausing, slowing down, replaying, and enlarging features."
