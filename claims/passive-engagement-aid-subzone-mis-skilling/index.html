Theoretical argument in Section 3.3.2.3 describing an underappreciated mis-skilling form involving engagement quality rather than subzone selection, where wrong clinical structures form through the interaction rather than despite it.

"AI-generated content is processed and potentially internalized, but the incomplete knowledge base means plausible-sounding errors pass undetected, and the cognitive interaction that makes Aid-subzone engagement developmentally beneficial fails to instantiate."

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument posing a practical supervision challenge: because passive and active engagement look identical, the proposed solution has experts audit how the learner engaged with the mode, not which mode was selected, aligning with Vygotsky's ZPD.

"Within a correctly identified Aid task, a learner passively engaging with AI is indistinguishable from the one exercising actively."
