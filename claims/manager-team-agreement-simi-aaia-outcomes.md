## Evidence

### Cioffi 2025

Cioffi, G., Balducci, C., Toderi, S. (2025). Digital Stress-Preventive Management Competencies: Definition, Identification and Tool Development for Research and Practice. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22020267

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Polynomial regression with response surface analysis on 218 remote workers and their 50 managers. Manager–team (in)congruence on SIMI "significantly explained 77% and 53% of the variance for superior support and role, respectively"; the curvature a2 = −1.21, t = −3.64, p < 0.01 indicates the dome shape.

> "the slope along the line of congruence (LOC) (a1 = 3.30, t = 4.36, p < 0.001) was positive and significant, suggesting that when managers and employees agree on high levels of SIMI, employees report greater clarity in their roles"

### Cioffi 2025 (2)

Cioffi, G., Balducci, C., Toderi, S. (2025). Digital Stress-Preventive Management Competencies: Definition, Identification and Tool Development for Research and Practice. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22020267

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fourth polynomial regression with response surface analysis in the dyad sample; manager–team (in)congruence on AAIA significantly explained 24% and 33% of variance for demands and control. The curvature along the LOC (a2 = −0.52, t = −1.61, p = 0.11) was not significant, indicating a linear trend.

> "the LOC slope (a1 = 1.73, t = 2.12, p < 0.05) was positive and significant, indicating that when managers and employees agree on high levels of AAIA, employees perceive greater autonomy"

### Cioffi 2025 (3)

Cioffi, G., Balducci, C., Toderi, S. (2025). Digital Stress-Preventive Management Competencies: Definition, Identification and Tool Development for Research and Practice. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22020267

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Third polynomial regression predicting demands from manager–team AAIA ratings in the dyad sample; the LOIC slope was also insignificant (a3 = 0.57, t = 0.71, p = 0.47). No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "no significance was found regarding the slope and the curvature along the line of congruence (a1 = −0.06, t = −0.06, p = 0.95; a2 = 0.21, t = 0.56, p = 0.57)"
