Narrative synthesis of the 15 included studies, grouped by Kirkpatrick's four levels of educational outcomes. The review reports that studies "showed a modification of skills and attitudes or behavioural or willingness of learners to apply new knowledge & skills (Kirkpatrick Levels 2 and 3)" and none reached Level 4.

"They all showed a modification of skills and attitudes or behavioural or willingness of learners to apply new knowledge & skills (Kirkpatrick Levels 2 and 3) [6]. None of the studies showed an improvement in learning and performance as a direct result of WBA (Kirkpatrick Level 4)."
