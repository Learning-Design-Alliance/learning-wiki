Pardos 2010¶

Pardos, Z.A., Heffernan, N.T. (2010). Modeling Individualization in a Bayesian Networks Implementation of Knowledge Tracing. UMAP 2010, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol 6075. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-13470-8_24

The authors report a predictive evaluation on real-world tutoring data using their new individualization technique, finding "a reliable improvement in prediction of real world data by individualizing the initial knowledge parameter." No effect size is printed.

"With this new individualization technique we are able to show a reliable improvement in prediction of real world data by individualizing the initial knowledge parameter."
