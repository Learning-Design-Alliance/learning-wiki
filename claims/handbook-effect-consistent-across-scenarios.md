### Hall Charlotte 2020

Hall Charlotte, Robertson Dean, Rolfe Margaret, Pascoe Sharene, Passey Megan E., Pit Sabrina Winona. (2020). Do cognitive aids reduce error rates in resuscitation team performance? Trial of emergency medicine protocols in simulation training (TEMPIST) in Australia. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-019-0441-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus interaction test from the binomial generalised mixed model in the same RCT (84 simulations, 21 groups). The article states "the interaction (handbook use by scenario) was not significant" and that the diagonal covariance structure fit best (AIC = 189.327).

> "The results of the binomial generalised mixed model analysis showed statistically significant main effects of handbook use (F1, 64 = 42.8, p < 0.001) and scenario (F3, 35 = 5.27, p = 0.004), but the interaction (handbook use by scenario) was not significant (F3, 35 = 1.65, p = 0.197)."
