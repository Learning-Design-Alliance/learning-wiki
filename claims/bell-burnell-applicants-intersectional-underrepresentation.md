An analysis of all applicants to the Bell Burnell Graduate Scholarship in 2022 and 2023 (Figure 1) found "80% of applicants were women, 56% had disabilities, 56% came from financially disadvantaged backgrounds, 26% were LGBTQ+ and 46% came from ethnic minority groups". The authors argue the intersections between categories highlight intersectional challenges.
