`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis in a cross-sectional survey of 153 mothers of children with dyslexia. The article reports that "a higher quality of support by the clinic was related to a higher level of teaching self‐efficacy"; the corresponding relation with parental self-efficacy was weaker.
