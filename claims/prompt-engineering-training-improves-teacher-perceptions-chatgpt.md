# Secondary education science teachers' self-rated perception of ChatGPT as an educational assistant was higher after three weeks of prompt engineering training than before, in one uncontrolled pre-post pilot study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After three weeks of prompt engineering training, teachers' median questionnaire scores on attitudes toward ChatGPT as an educational assistant were statistically significantly higher than before training (Wilcoxon signed-rank Z = -3.296, p < .001). [→ Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2025](#dimitrios-gousopoulos-2025)
`q2 i?` Bootstrapped 95% confidence interval for the pre-post mean difference in teachers' self-rated questionnaire scores (0.682 to 0.909) excludes zero, with scores higher after training than before training. [→ Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2025 (2)](#dimitrios-gousopoulos-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2025

Dimitrios Gousopoulos. (2025). Exploring Teachers' Perceptions of ChatGPT Through Prompt Engineering. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.08634](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.08634)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot pre/post study of 14 secondary science teachers (convenience sampling, half private and half public schools) completing a 14-item five-point Likert questionnaire before and after training; Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed "statistically significant differences (Z = -3.296, p < .001)" with a higher post-training median. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The results indicated that there were statistically significant differences (Z = -3.296, p < .001). It was also observed that, following the implementation of the training, the me dian of the participants’ mean scores on the questionnaire assessing teachers’ attitudes toward the use of ChatGPT as an educational assistant was higher than the corresponding median prior to the training."

### Dimitrios Gousopoulos 2025 (2)

Dimitrios Gousopoulos. (2025). Exploring Teachers' Perceptions of ChatGPT Through Prompt Engineering. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.08634](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.08634)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bootstrapping analysis of the same pilot data using 10,000 resamples with replacement and the percentile method; the 95% confidence interval for the mean difference "ranged from 0.682 to 0.909", excluding zero. The interval bounds are raw mean-difference units, not a standardized effect size.

> "The results of this analysis showed that the confidence interval for the mean difference between the pre- and post-intervention scores ranged from 0.682 to 0.909. The fact that this interval does not include zero indicates a statistically significant chang e following the intervention."
