## Evidence

### David W. Putwain 2026

David W. Putwain, Andrea Mallaburn, Hanna Gaspard. (2026). Student perceptions of teacher messages about the value of science in the classroom. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102365. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Reciprocal paths in the student-level (L1) portion of the ML-SEMs, testing whether T2 achievement and aspirations predicted T3 motivational beliefs with autoregressive controls, in the four-wave sample of 1591 English secondary students. The article reports large effect sizes in prose without printing coefficients in the quoted text.

> "Students with greater achievement and aspirations relative to their classmates (i. e., the L1 portion of the model) reported higher expectancy and utility value with large effect sizes, beyond prior levels. Student-level achievement and aspirations were unrelated to effort cost."
