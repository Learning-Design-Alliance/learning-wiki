Cochran 1999¶

Cochran, L. M., & Johnson, J. K. (1999). In Memoriam: Robert E. deKieffer. Educational Technology Research and Development, 47(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ602345

The memorial notes that deKieffer's "early research in 1948 was repeated in 1958 and again in 1968," producing a three-decade longitudinal perspective on audio-visual materials in teacher training.

"Early research in 1948 was repeated in 1958 and again in 1968, providing a 30-year view of the use of audio-visual materials in teacher training."
