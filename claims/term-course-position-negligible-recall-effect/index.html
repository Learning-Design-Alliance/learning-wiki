Evidence¶

Dell 1911¶

Dell, John A. (1911). Some Observations on the Learning of Sensible Material. Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 401-406. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0074801

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Term-long examination study: Form III was examined on a term's chemistry work and Forms I and II on nature study, using short questions in the order facts were taught; actual frequencies were tabulated (Tables IV and V) and grouped between minima (Tables VI and VII). The author reports "only the slightest indication (in one case none)" of a position effect.

"In both cases there is only the slightest indication (in one case none) that special parts of the series are remembered better than others in virtue of their position."
