## Evidence

### Mroueh Lara 2025

Mroueh Lara, Bruand Pierre‐Emile, Poudiougou Oumar, Kleinebreil Line, Fall Ndao Amy, Moussaoui Driss, Preux Pierre‐Marie, Bagayoko Cheick Oumar, Boumediene Farid. (2025). Evaluating the Effects of Mental Health e‐Learning on the Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Primary Healthcare Professionals in Mali. A Pilot Study. Early Intervention in Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/eip.70001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired before/after comparison of 1–10 self-assessment scores collected before and after each module; e.g. schizophrenia satisfaction rose from a median 45.0 to 90.0 (Wilcoxon p < 0.001). The article states these scores rose "in a greater way than the knowledge and practices scores".

> "Similarly, scores regarding satisfaction with knowledge and ability to diagnose and manage diseases increased significantly, and in a greater way than the knowledge and practices scores."

### Mroueh Lara 2025 (2)

Mroueh Lara, Bruand Pierre‐Emile, Poudiougou Oumar, Kleinebreil Line, Fall Ndao Amy, Moussaoui Driss, Preux Pierre‐Marie, Bagayoko Cheick Oumar, Boumediene Farid. (2025). Evaluating the Effects of Mental Health e‐Learning on the Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Primary Healthcare Professionals in Mali. A Pilot Study. Early Intervention in Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/eip.70001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Figure 3 (absolute and relative changes of overall scores) and its describing text report relative gains for the completers: "satisfaction with knowledge: +73.7%; ability to diagnose and manage: +60.1%" versus knowledge +16.4% and practices +10.7%.

> "the relative changes between before/after e‐learning were greater in terms of satisfaction and confidence of participants (satisfaction with knowledge: +73.7%; ability to diagnose and manage: +60.1%) than their acquired skills scores (knowledge: +16.4%; practices: +10.7%)"
