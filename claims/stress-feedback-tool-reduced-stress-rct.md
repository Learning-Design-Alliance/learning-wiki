`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review reports a randomized controlled trial by Lemaire et al. assessing a biofeedback stress management tool that alerted physicians when they surpassed threshold stress levels. "the mean stress score declined significantly for the intervention group, but not for the control group". The trial reached Kirkpatrick level 3.

> "During the trial, the mean stress score declined significantly for the intervention group, but not for the control group, demonstrating that stress levels declined significantly when using this stress management method."
