## Evidence

### Bruen Catherine 2025

Bruen Catherine, Illing Jan, Daly Ronan, Meagher Frances, Delany Caroline, Offiah Gozie, Doherty Sally, Stuart Ellen, Crehan Martina, Kelly Helen. (2025). Medical student experiences of Case-Based Learning (CBL) at a multicultural medical school. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06585-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Structured Group Feedback Sessions with 36 first-year medical students from 12 nationalities, analysed with the framework analysis method; the authors report that CBL "can be used to facilitate active learning" and that students "engaged with it and enjoyed it". No effect sizes are printed.

> "We found CBL can be used to facilitate active learning with all students at a multicultural medical university."
