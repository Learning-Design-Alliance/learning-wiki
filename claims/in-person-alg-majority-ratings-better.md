Malta Kiana, Glickman Cynthia, Hunter Krystal, McBride Amanda. (2025). Comparing the impact of online and in-person active learning in preclinical medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06846-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive percentages from the questionnaire of Classes 2023 and 2024 students who experienced one year each of virtual and in-person ALG; these are descriptive response distributions, not tested differences. Camaraderie reached "90.1%" and education "85.1%".

> "In terms of education and interpersonal skills, a majority of the respondents indicated that in person ALG was better for their education (85.1%), participation (66.9%), camaraderie (90.1%), teamwork (79.3%), confidence (52.9%), and relationships with faculty (86.8%)."
