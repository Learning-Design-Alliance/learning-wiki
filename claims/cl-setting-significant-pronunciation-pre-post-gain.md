### Hsuan-Yu Chen (Jack) & Jaya S. Goswami 2011

Hsuan-Yu Chen (Jack) & Jaya S. Goswami. (2011). Structuring Cooperative Learning in Teaching English Pronunciation. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p26

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the 19-student CL group's pre-test (M = 4.90, SD = 3.58) and post-test (M = 5.85, SD = 3.87) pronunciation scores of seven target consonants difficult for Spanish speakers. The researchers concluded instruction in a cooperative learning setting had a statistically significant effect.

> "The results showed that there was a statistically significant diff erence in performance between pre-test and post-test scores in the CL group, with t(19) = -15.985, p = .0000."
