Cross-sectional online survey of 79 physiotherapy clinical educators across eight Australian health services; descriptive statistics summarised FTP incidence and types. The article reports "Sixty-one percent of the 79 respondents reported supervising one or more students with FTP issues" and lists the four concern types with percentages.
