`q3 i3` Pearson correlations between average human student accuracy and teacher representational alignment differ across conditions, e.g., r = 0.91 (p = 0.013) for simple-features quadrants and r = 0.59 (p = 0.221) for simple-features columns. [→ Ilia Sucholutsky 2024](#ilia-sucholutsky-2024)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations (Table 1) of average human student accuracy and machine-teacher representational alignment across quadrant, column, and pooled conditions for both stimuli sets. The printed Pearson correlation for simple-features quadrants is r = 0.91 (p = 0.013) and for salient-dinos columns r = 0.86 (p = 0.027); several correlations are non-significant (e.g., p = 0.221, p = 0.286), so the alignment-accuracy link is condition-dependent.
