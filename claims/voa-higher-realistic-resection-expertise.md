## Evidence

### Fazlollahi AM et al 2022

Fazlollahi AM et al. (2022). Effect of Artificial Intelligence Tutoring vs Expert Instruction on Learning Simulated Surgical Skills Among Medical Students: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.49008

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

One-way ANOVA of ICEMS Expertise Scores on 70 realistic resections (one per randomized student) evaluating learning and transfer. The VOA group's mean differences of 0.53 vs instructor and 0.49 vs control were significant at P < .001. No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The VOA group achieved significantly higher Expertise Scores in the realistic subpial resection than instructor (mean difference, 0.53 [95% CI, 0.40 to 0.67] points) and control (mean difference. 0.49 [95% CI, 0.34 to 0.61] points; P < .001) groups"
