Evidence¶

Filippou 2018¶

Filippou, F., Rokka, S., Pitsi, A., Gargalianos, D., Bebetsos, E., & Filippou, D. A. (2018). Interdisciplinary Greek Traditional Dance Course: Impact on Student Satisfaction and Anxiety. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 363-374. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11325a

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVAs run separately by gender on each factor; female experimental students improved significantly on satisfaction, cognitive anxiety, somatic anxiety and self-confidence, while female control students showed no statistically important difference on any factor.

"the female students of the experimental group presented an important differentiation after every single measurement (F(1,124)=809.84, p<.001, initial measurement M=4.22 & SD=.74, final M= 1.94 & SD= .42) reducing somatic anxiety."
