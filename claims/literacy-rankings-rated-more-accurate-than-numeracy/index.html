Evidence¶

Sun 2026¶

Sun, C., Major, L., Daltry, R., Obradovic, L., & Friedberg, A. (2026). Enhancing literacy and numeracy through teacher dashboards and digital personalised learning: an A/B/C test in Kenyan early years education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10703-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Chi-square analysis of the association between rating type and learning domain across both dashboard partitions. Teachers expressed significantly more confidence in the accuracy of literacy rankings than numeracy rankings.

"teachers rated learners’ literacy ranking as “yes” significantly more than they selected “yes” for learners’ numeracy ranking (χ2 (1, N = 15,609) = 24.96, p <.001). There was also a significant difference for the “unsure” rating, with proportionally more teachers selecting this for learners’ numeracy ranking than literacy ranking (χ 2 (1, N = 3,002) = 114.36, p <.001)."
