### Kavas Mustafa Volkan; Demirören Meral; Koşan Ayşen Melek Aytuğ; Karahan Süleyman Tuna; Yalim Neyyire Yasemin 2015

Kavas Mustafa Volkan; Demirören Meral; Koşan Ayşen Melek Aytuğ; Karahan Süleyman Tuna; Yalim Neyyire Yasemin. (2015). Turkish students' perceptions of professionalism at the beginning and at the end of medical education: a cross-sectional qualitative study. Medical Education Online. [doi:10.3402/meo.v20.26614](https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.26614)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group thematic analysis comparing Grade-1 and Grade-6 accounts found G6 students offered negative associations 'related to clinical overwork (e.g., sleeplessness, weariness) and violence towards physicians' while G1 views were stronger and more diverse. The authors frame this as erosion of professional perception during education.

> "G1 students offered stronger and more diverse opinions than their G6 counterparts. Conversely, G6 students offered negative associations related to clinical overwork (e.g., sleeplessness, weariness) and violence towards physicians"
