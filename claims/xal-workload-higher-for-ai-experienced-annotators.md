## Evidence

### Bhavya Ghai 2021

Bhavya Ghai, Q. Vera Liao, Yunfeng Zhang, Rachel K. E. Bellamy, and Klaus Mueller. (2021). Explainable Active Learning (XAL): Toward AI Explanations as Interfaces for Machine Teachers. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1145/3432934](https://doi.org/10.1145/3432934)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Survey-based analysis of perceived cognitive workload (two NASA-TLX items, Cronbach's alpha 0.86) in the crowdsourced experiment. Mixed-effects regression found the XAL × AI experience interaction (β = 1.30, SE = 0.59, p = 0.04), plotted in Figure 8; the article suggests AI-experienced participants examined explanations more carefully.

> "the XAL condition presented higher cognitive workload compared to baseline AL, but only for those with AI experience."
