Descriptive item analysis of the 9-item LNT scale and 6-item technology scale among 207 students (Tables 3–4). The technology section reports "Only 57.8% of students were satisfied, while 15% encountered technical problems." Scale means: lighting 12.78 (2.13), noise 10.19 (2.73), temperature 11.79 (2.41), technology 23.88 (3.47).
