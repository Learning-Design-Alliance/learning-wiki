Evidence¶

Meloni et al. 2017¶

Carla Meloni, Rachele Fanari, Andrea Bertucci and Sara Berretti. (2017). IMPACT OF EARLY NUMERACY TRAINING ON KINDERGARTENERS FROM MIDDLE-INCOME FAMILIES. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Wilcoxon paired test on the cooperative learning group (n=15) of the 43-child three-condition study, comparing BIN 4-6 z-scores before and after 12 weekly training meetings. The article reports a significant difference with printed effect size |r| = .88 ("V = 0, p = .002, |r| = .88").

"A Wilcoxon test for paired sample was used to compare the CL group performance in early numeracy competence (dependent variable: BIN 4-6 z-score) in the pre- and post-training evaluation. The test results showed a significant difference (V = 0, p = .002, |r| = .88)."

Meloni et al. 2017 (2)¶

Carla Meloni, Rachele Fanari, Andrea Bertucci and Sara Berretti. (2017). IMPACT OF EARLY NUMERACY TRAINING ON KINDERGARTENERS FROM MIDDLE-INCOME FAMILIES. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Wilcoxon paired test on the individual learning group (n=14) of the same study, comparing BIN 4-6 z-scores pre and post training. The article reports a significant difference with printed effect size |r| = .88 ("V = 0, p = .003, |r| = .88").

"A second Wilcoxon test for paired sample was used to compare pre- and post-training early numeracy competence (BIN 4-6 z-score) in the IL group; the results showed a significant difference (V = 0, p = .003, |r| = .88)."

Meloni et al. 2017 (3)¶

Carla Meloni, Rachele Fanari, Andrea Bertucci and Sara Berretti. (2017). IMPACT OF EARLY NUMERACY TRAINING ON KINDERGARTENERS FROM MIDDLE-INCOME FAMILIES. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Wilcoxon paired test on the control group (n=14), who did drawing and block-play activities instead of math training, comparing BIN 4-6 z-scores pre and post. The article reports a significant difference with printed effect size |r| = .83 ("V = 0, p = .006, |r| = .83").

"The third Wilcoxon test for paired sample was used to compare pre-and post-training early numeracy competence performance (BIN 4-6 z-score) of the control group. The results showed a significant difference between pre-test and post-test also in the control group (V = 0, p = .006, |r| = .83)."
