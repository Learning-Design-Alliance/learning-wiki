## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ASAG systems grade content-focused responses to objective, close-ended questions, whereas AEG systems grade style-focused responses to subjective, open-ended questions. [→ Steven Burrows 2015](#steven-burrows-2015)

## Evidence

### Steven Burrows 2015

Steven Burrows, Iryna Gurevych, Benno Stein. (2015). The Eras and Trends of Automatic Short Answer Grading. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0026-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis in the Question Type section, supported by cited ETS systems c-rater and e-rater as examples of ASAG and AEG respectively. The review reports that ASAG focuses on content while AEG focuses on style, with short answers ranging from one phrase to one paragraph. No effect size is printed.

> "Specifically, ASAG systems require answers to objective or close-ended questions. In contrast, AEG systems require answers to subjective or open-ended questions (Leacock and Chodorow 2003; Siddiqi and Harrison 2008b; Wood et al. 2006)."
