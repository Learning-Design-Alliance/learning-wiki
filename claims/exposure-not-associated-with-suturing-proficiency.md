`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same 57-participant retrospective cohort. The article reports cumulative exposure variables were "not significantly associated with final performance"; no effect sizes beyond the printed correlations are given, so impact is coded i? > "cumulative exposure variables were not significantly associated with final performance, including total simulation time (r = 0.125, p = 0.354 for suturing time; r = -0.201, p = 0.134 for Suture Score)"
