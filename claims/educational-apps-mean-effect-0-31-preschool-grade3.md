# Educational apps produce a positive mean effect of about +0.31 standard deviations on preschool to Grade 3 children's literacy and math achievement

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 36 experimental and quasi-experimental studies, educational apps improved overall achievement by a mean weighted Hedges's g of +0.31. [→ James Kim 2021](#james-kim-2021)

## Evidence

### James Kim 2021

James Kim, Joshua Gilbert, Qun Yu, Charles Gale. (2021). Measures Matter: A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Educational Apps on Preschool to Grade 3 Children's Literacy and Math Skills. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584211004183

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random effects meta-analysis with RVE of 36 studies and 285 effect sizes. The RVE yielded "a positive Hedges's g of +0.31 (95% conﬁdence interval [CI] [0.20, 0.42])" with literacy g = 0.35 and math g = 0.29.

> "results from rigorous experimental and quasi-experimental studies yielded a mean weighted effect size of +0.31 standard deviations on overall achievement and comparable effects in both math and literacy"
