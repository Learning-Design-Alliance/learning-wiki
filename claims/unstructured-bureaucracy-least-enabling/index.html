Authors' synthesis of the frequency findings (section 4.4); an interpretive statement grounded in the descriptive survey percentages reported for staff and students.

Discussion-section interpretation (section 5) about the Unstructured Bureaucratic type: no critical mass, no sense of ownership or agency, and staff hampered by institutional processes and lack of support.

"There is little evidence of either top down or bottom up change with regard the use of ICTs for teaching and learning."
