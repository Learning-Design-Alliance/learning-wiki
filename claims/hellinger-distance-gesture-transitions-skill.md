## Evidence

### Vedula S. Swaroop 2016

Vedula S. Swaroop, Malpani Anand O., Tao Lingling, Chen George, Gao Yixin, Poddar Piyush, Ahmidi Narges, Paxton Christopher, Vidal Rene, Khudanpur Sanjeev, Hager Gregory D., Chen Chi Chiung Grace. (2016). Analysis of the Structure of Surgical Activity for a Suturing and Knot-Tying Task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149174

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Transition probability matrix analysis of 135 dVSS trials by 18 surgeons, with Hellinger distances and 95% bootstrap intervals (1000 bootstrap samples) comparing expert and novice transition distributions. The article reports Hellinger distances for transitions among gestures within ST, TLK, and OLK were statistically significant.

> "The Hellinger distance comparing expert and novice trials (experience-based skill definition) was 0.12 (95% BI = 0.00 to 0.38) for transitions emanating from ST, 0.29 (95% BI = 0.07 to 0.51) for transitions emanating from TLK, 0.43 (95% BI = 0.00 to 1.77) for transitions emanating from OLK, 0.19 (95% BI = 0.03 to 0.34) for transitions emanating from GPR, and 0.19 (95% BI = 0.00 to 0.46) for transitions emanating from IMS."
