# More pronounced hearing loss moderates the training benefit: participants with PTA 1 SD above the mean improve significantly more after immersive than auditory training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant two-way interaction between training modality and hearing loss, with post-hoc tests showing significantly greater training-induced SNR change for immersive training in participants with higher PTA, but no significant modality difference in participants with better hearing. ## Evidence

### Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025

Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud. (2025). Immersive auditory-cognitive training improves speech-in-noise perception in older adults with varying hearing and working memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00306-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc analyses on the moderation model of SNR change; the modality-by-PTA interaction was significant (Δχ²(1) = 8.168, p = 0.004) and the quoted post-hoc at p < 0.001. For participants with better hearing the modality difference was not significant (p = 0.094).

> "Post-hoc analyses showed that in participants with higher PTA (i.e., PTA 1 SD above the mean), the training-induced change was signi ﬁcantly greater for immersive training compared to auditory training (PTA 1 SD above average: ChangeAU – ChangeIM: Δβ = −0.729, SE = 0.163, t = −4.481, p < 0.001)"
