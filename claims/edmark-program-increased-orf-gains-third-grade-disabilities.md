## Evidence

### Meeks 2014

Meeks, B. T., Martinez, J., & Pienta, R. S. (2014). Effect of Edmark Program on Reading Fluency in Third-Grade Students with Disabilities. International Journal of Instruction, 7(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085264

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Six-week quasi-experiment with N = 6 third-graders with disabilities (Edmark group N = 4, control N = 2), STAR Estimated ORF pre/post, unpaired one-tailed t-tests. Mean gain/loss was 4.25 for the Edmark group versus -9.5 for the control group; "The intervention had a huge effect ( d = 3.28)."

> "Estimated ORF scores for students i n the Edmark Reading Group using the ERP increased significantly more ( p = 0.02) than scores for students in the Reading Instruction Group instructed using regular classroom reading instruction."
