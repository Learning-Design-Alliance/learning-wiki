Evidence¶

Irwanto 2018¶

Irwanto, Saputro, A. D., Rohaeti, E., & Prodjosantoso, A. K. (2018). Promoting Critical Thinking and Problem Solving Skills of Preservice Elementary Teachers through Process-Oriented Guided-Inquiry Learning (POGIL). International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 777-794. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191708

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Same quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study (N=48, two intact classes); Mann-Whitney U-test on posttest problem solving scores found "a significant difference on posttest score between experimental and control groups (U=76.000; p=.000)". Descriptive means rose from 8.79 to 14.17 (POGIL) versus 8.46 to 12.33 (control).

"Overall, there was a significant difference on posttest score between experimental and control groups (U=76.000; p=.000). Even that significant difference was reflected at all sub-skills (p<.05)"
