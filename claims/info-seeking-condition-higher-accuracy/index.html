Evidence¶

Lorenz Weise 2022¶

Lorenz Weise, Saskia D. Forster, Siegfried Gauggel. (2022). Reverse-correlation reveals internal error-corrections during information-seeking. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:321–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09286-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired t-tests on test-trial accuracy across the three conditions for 27 participants. Means were 0.71 (explicit), 0.74 (wagering), 0.84 (info-seeking); explicit vs info-seeking was significant, t(26) = 6.89, p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

"Mean primary task performance was 0.71 (95% CI = 0.69 – 0.73) f or the explicit reports condition, 0.74 (95% CI = 0.72 – 0.76) f or the wagering condition, and 0.84 (95% CI = 0.8 – 0.88) for the info-seeking condition. The difference between performance in the explicit reports- and info-seeking condition was significant (t(26) = 6.89, p < 0.001)"
