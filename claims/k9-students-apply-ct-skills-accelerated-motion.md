### Mateo Dutra 2025

Mateo Dutra, Álvaro Suárez and Arturo C. Martí. (2025). Code in Motion: Integrating Computational Thinking with Kinematics Exploration. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.03850](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.03850)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' conclusion from the classroom implementation of the didactic sequence with three K9 groups over two 2-hour sessions, in which students programmed free-fall functions, ran 30 experimental fall-time trials, and interpreted provided code. The article reports students "successfully generated position-time and velocity-time graphs for free fall scenarios."

> "Specifically, they successfully generated position-time and velocity-time graphs for free fall scenarios and calculated the falling time of an object using its initial position and velocity."
