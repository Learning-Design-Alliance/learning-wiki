## Evidence

### Larwin et al. 2008

Karen H. Larwin, David A. Larwin, Jennifer Gorman. (2008). Mnemonic aids during tests: Worthless frivolity or effective tool in statistics education? Journal of Instructional Pedagogies. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mnemonic+aids+during+tests+statistics+education

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post hoc comparison from the same factorial ANOVA of exam scores. The article labels the difference "marginal" with p = 0.057; no effect size is printed, so none is reported and equivalence was not tested.

> "while the students in the No aids condition outperformed students in the Instructor-prepared aids condition on the course exams, the post hoc differences revealed only a marginal difference, M difference = 4.12, p = 0.057."
