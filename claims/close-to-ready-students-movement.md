# Students close to college ready in science on the PLAN reached college ready on the ACT at 46% and 52% in the treatment groups versus one-third in the baseline group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The percentage of close-to-college-ready students reaching college ready improved from a third in the baseline to roughly half in the treatment groups. [→ Dye 2013](#dye-2013)

## Evidence

### Dye 2013

Dye, J., Cheatham, T., Rowell, G. H., Barlow, A. T., & Carlton, R. (2013). The Impact of Modeling Instruction within the Inverted Curriculum on Student Achievement in Science. Electronic Journal of Science Education, 17(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1188400

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Categorical movement analysis (Table 9) for students within two points of the PLAN science benchmark; the article also reports regression from college ready to not college ready fell from 22% in the baseline to 11% and 12% in the treatment groups. Descriptive percentages only.

> "one -third of the baseline group who tested ―close to college ready‖ in science on the PLAN were college ready when they completed the ACT. For the treatment groups the percentages were 46% and 52%, respectively"
