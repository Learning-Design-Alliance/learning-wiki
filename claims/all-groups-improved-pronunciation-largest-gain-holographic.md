## Evidence

### Rebeca Cerezo 2024

Rebeca Cerezo, Vicente Calderón, Cristóbal Romero. (2024). A holographic mobile-based application for practicing pronunciation of basic English vocabulary for Spanish speaking children. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired-sample t-tests in the three-group pre-post experiment. "The scores in the CG (t=23.87, p<0.05), EG1 (t=21.10, p<0.05) and EG2 (t=29.73, p<0.05)" were significant; the article reports increased performance of +30.5%, +34.8% and +39.5% respectively (Table 5).

> "The scores in the CG (t=23.87, p<0.05), EG1 (t=21.10, p<0.05) and EG2 (t=29.73, p<0.05) were statistically significantly different before and after the experiment."
