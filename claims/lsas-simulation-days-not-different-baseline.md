Monthly questionnaire administration on simulation days collected 107 responses from 80 students; mean LSAS of "51.95 ± 28.49" was compared with the pre-simulation mean, showing no significant difference (p = 0.30). Equivalence was not tested.

> "On the days when simulations took place, 80 different students provided a total of 107 responses. Then, the mean LSAS score was 51.95 ± 28.49, which did not differ significantly from the pre-simulation LSAS mean (p = 0.30)."
