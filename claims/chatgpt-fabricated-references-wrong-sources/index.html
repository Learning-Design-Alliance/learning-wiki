Mehedi Hasan Anik 2023¶

Mehedi Hasan Anik, Shahriar Nafees Chowdhury Raaz, Nushat Khan. (2023). Embracing AI Assistants: Unraveling Young Researchers' Journey with ChatGPT in Science Education Thesis Writing. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3481002/v1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case-study interview finding from a ChatGPT user (Y-02) about the reference section. The participant reported that "auto-generated references and doi were found fake" most times, leading to use of alternative tools. Qualitative self-report; no effect size.

"most times, auto-generated references and doi were found fake….frustration with referencing ChatGPT made me use Google Scholar, Zotero, and Sci-Hub"
