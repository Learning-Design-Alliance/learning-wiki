# Relationship effects account for about 40% of the variance in students' evaluations of teaching trailers and live lectures

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Some professors elicited unusually favourable evaluations from some students, beyond rater bias and professor effects, in both trailers and live lectures. [→ Gross Jennifer 2015](#gross-jennifer-2015)

## Evidence

### Gross Jennifer 2015

Gross Jennifer, Lakey Brian, Lucas Jessica L, LaCross Ryan, R Plotkowski Andrea, Winegard Bo. (2015). Forecasting the student–professor matches that result in unusually effective teaching. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12049

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Variance-component analysis (VARCOMP, students × professors nested within sections × items) of 145 students rating 10 professors' trailers and live lectures. Relationship effects explained about 40% of variance; Table 1 prints .392 (trailer) and .404 (class).

> "As predicted, there were large relationship effects for teaching evaluations for both trailers and live lectures, with each accounting for about 40% of the variance (Table 1)."
