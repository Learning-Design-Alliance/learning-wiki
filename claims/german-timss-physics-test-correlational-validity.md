## Evidence

### Markus Sebastian Feser and Dietmar Höttecke 2023

Markus Sebastian Feser and Dietmar Höttecke. (2023). Development of a test in German language to assess middle school students’ physics proficiency. Preprint, The Physics Educator. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1142/s2661339523200020](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339523200020)

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis between Rasch-estimated person parameters and background survey data (interest scale αCronbach = 0.87; self-concept scale αCronbach = 0.84) from the same N = 177 sample. Printed effect sizes: rPearson = 0.45 and rPearson = 0.34, which the authors call "a moderate and positive correlation".

> "We found a moderate and positive correlation between the participants’ estimated person parameters and their interest in science ( rPearson = 0.45; p < 0.01), as well as their self-concept in science (rPearson = 0.34; p < 0.01)."

### Markus Sebastian Feser and Dietmar Höttecke 2023 (2)

Markus Sebastian Feser and Dietmar Höttecke. (2023). Development of a test in German language to assess middle school students’ physics proficiency. Preprint, The Physics Educator. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1142/s2661339523200020](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339523200020)

`q3 · i1` · `design · r2`

Same correlational validity analysis of the N = 177 sample. Printed effect sizes: ρSpearman = -0.24 for grades and ρSpearman = 0.22 for cultural and economic capital (number of books at home). The negative grade correlation reflects the German scale from 1 = very good to 6 = insufficient, per the authors' footnote.

> "The rank correlation between estimated person parameters and latest school grades in physics was negative, as expectedb, but low (ρSpearman = -0.24; p < 0.01). For the participants’ cultural and economic capital, the rank correlation was also low but positive ( ρSpearman = 0.22; p < 0.01)."
