### Luke Nearhood and Patti Hamerski 2025

Luke Nearhood and Patti Hamerski. (2025). Student experiences in a computational physics lab through the lens of Physics Computational Literacy. 2025 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.Nearhood

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the interview study, Derek, asked about classroom norms and unwritten rules, described commenting and formatting code as "etiquette" that was not specified in the prior course but was addressed in the next. The authors take this as evidence that students recognize coding as an inherently social activity through commenting, formatting, and documentation.

> "But the, like etiquette of like, code chunks, of putting in com- ments that wasn’t super speciﬁed in 365, but it got brought up in [3]66."
