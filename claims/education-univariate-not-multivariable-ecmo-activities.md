Univariate linear regression models (Table 3, n = 83) examined demographic and professional characteristics; education showed significant associations with calibration/monitoring (B = −46.08, p = 0.038) and medication/blood administration (B = −74.14, p = 0.003). Per the Results section, "Other variables that were significant in the univariate analyses did not remain statistically significant in the multivariable models."

> "Several independent variables, including education, nursing autonomy, nursing team support and perceived ECMO knowledge, showed significant associations with one or more activity domains in the univariate analyses"
