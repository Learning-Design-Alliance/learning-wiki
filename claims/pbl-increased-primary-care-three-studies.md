## Evidence

### Tsigarides Jordan 2017

Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomised controlled trial at Harvard Medical School where 100 students were randomly assigned to a 'new pathway' (PBL) or 'traditional' (non-PBL) curriculum, followed up 12–13 years post-graduation. The review reports "22% more PBL graduates were working in either primary care or psychiatry" (P ≤ 0.05).

> "It was found that 22% more PBL graduates were working in either primary care or psychiatry than Non-PBL graduates. This was found to be statistically significant (P ≤ 0.05)."

### Tsigarides Jordan 2017 (2)

Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Retrospective cohort study comparing 2469 students from two Australian medical schools (University of Newcastle and University of Sydney) with different curricula, matched for graduation year, age and gender. The review reports significantly more PBL graduates worked in primary care or psychiatry (P = 0.0001).

> "It was found that significantly more graduates from the PBL curricula (Sydney) worked in primary care or psychiatry at follow-up (P = 0.0001)."

### Tsigarides Jordan 2017 (3)

Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Prospective cohort study at the University of New Mexico comparing PBL and non-PBL tracts with a population of 38 subjects. The review reports "significantly more PBL graduates chose a primary care residency" (P = 0.025).

> "The study found that significantly more PBL graduates chose a primary care residency than Non-PBL graduates (P = 0.025)."
