## Evidence

### Couper K 2018

Couper K, Velho RM, Quinn T, Devrell A, Lall R, Orriss B, Yeung J, Perkins GD. (2018). Training approaches for the deployment of a mechanical chest compression device: a randomised controlled manikin study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled parallel-group manikin trial: 20 teams of three clinicians randomised 1:1 to pit-crew or standard device training, tested in a video-recorded simulated cardiac arrest immediately after training. "we found no difference in the flow fraction preceding the first mechanical chest compression between study groups", mean difference −0.01, P=0.572.

> "In relation to the primary outcome, we found no difference in the flow fraction preceding the first mechanical chest compression between study groups (0.76 (95% CI 0.73 to 0.79) vs 0.77 (95% CI 0.73 to 0.82), mean difference −0.01 (95% CI −0.06 to 0.03), P=0.572)"
