# Study-related self-efficacy (PsyCap) and eagerness to study do not change over the year and do not differ between SDL and IBL conditions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No significant time effect or group effect was found for PsyCap scales or eagerness to study. [→ van Lankveld W 2019](#van-lankveld-w-2019)

## Evidence

### van Lankveld W 2019

van Lankveld W, Maas M, van Wijchen J, Visser V, Staal JB. (2019). Self-regulated learning in physical therapy education: a non-randomized experimental study comparing self-directed and instruction-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1484-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate test-retest ANOVA on PsyCap subscales and eagerness to study in the same quasi-experimental study. The article reports the time effect "was not-significant (Multivariate F value = 1.15; df = 5, p < .34)" and the group effect was not significant, so no change over time and no difference between groups.

> "In the repeated measures ANOVA the main within subjects effect of time was not-significant (Multivariate F value = 1.15; df = 5, p < .34). The group effect was not significant in the multivariate analysis (Multivariate F = 0.8, df = 5, p = .07)."
