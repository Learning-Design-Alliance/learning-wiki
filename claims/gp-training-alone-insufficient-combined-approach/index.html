Evidence¶

Tatlow-Golden M 2016¶

Tatlow-Golden M, Prihodova L, Gavin B, Cullen W, McNicholas F. (2016). What do general practitioners know about ADHD? Attitudes and knowledge among first-contact gatekeepers: systematic narrative review. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-016-0516-x

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review attributes this conclusion to Sikorski and colleagues' 2012 systematic review of randomised controlled trials of GP training in the care of depression, in which very few studies were found. The review's design is not described as if read directly; no effect size is printed.

"the existing evidence led to the conclusion that GP training alone does not improve care, but that training effects were more promising when combined with implementation of guidelines, and that GP training needs to be linked with more collaborative care models."
