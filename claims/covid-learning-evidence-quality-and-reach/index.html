Evidence¶

Bastian A. Betthäuser 2023¶

Bastian A. Betthäuser, Anders M. Bach-Mortensen & Per Engzell. (2023). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the evidence on learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01506-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Risk-of-bias appraisal of all identified studies using an adapted ROBINS-I tool, with double assessment checked independently. Common bias sources were confounding, sample selection and missing data; the 19 critical-risk studies were excluded from all meta-analyses.

"The overall risk of bias was considered 'low' for 15% of studies, 'moderate' for 30% of studies, 'serious' for 25% of studies and 'critical' for 30% of studies."

Bastian A. Betthäuser 2023 (2)¶

Bastian A. Betthäuser, Anders M. Bach-Mortensen & Per Engzell. (2023). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the evidence on learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01506-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Publication-bias assessment of the 291 estimates via a z-curve (Fig. 2b), a P-curve and a funnel plot; the authors report no spike above the z = 1.96 significance threshold and no left-skewed P-value distribution, concluding "Publication bias thus does not appear to be a major concern".

"If there was publication bias, we would expect a spike just above the threshold, and a slump just below it. There is no indication of this."
