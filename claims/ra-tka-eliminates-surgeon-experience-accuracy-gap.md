## Evidence

### Song Xunzhou 2026

Song Xunzhou, Wang Mingyou, Wang Hongping, Tang Zhuodong, Lan Yuping. (2026). Efficacy study of robot-assisted total knee arthroplasty in patients with different levels of medical experience. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03236-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective single-center comparison of 100 patients in four groups (junior/senior surgeons × RA-TKA/CTKA, 25 each). The primary endpoint analysis reports "their accuracy (92%) reached a comparable level to that of senior doctors' robot-assisted surgery (B-RA-TKA, 96%)" (RD -4%, P = 0.642).

> "Using robot-assisted surgery (A-RA-TKA) by junior doctors significantly reduced the incidence of outliers (RD + 40%, 95% CI: +18% to + 62%; P < 0.001), and their accuracy (92%) reached a comparable level to that of senior doctors' robot-assisted surgery (B-RA-TKA, 96%)"

### Song Xunzhou 2026 (2)

Song Xunzhou, Wang Mingyou, Wang Hongping, Tang Zhuodong, Lan Yuping. (2026). Efficacy study of robot-assisted total knee arthroplasty in patients with different levels of medical experience. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03236-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Surgical-efficiency analysis of the same 100-patient cohort shows the conventional-technique experience gap the robot gap is contrasted with; the HKA outlier comparison A-CTKA vs B-CTKA was RD -20% (P = 0.076), not significant.

> "in the CTKA group, the operation time and incision length of junior doctors were longer than those of senior doctors, with statistically significant differences (P < 0.05)"
