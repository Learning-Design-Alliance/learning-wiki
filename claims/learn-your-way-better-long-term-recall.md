## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Learn Your Way participants (Mdn = 5; M = 4.7, SD = 1.2) scored significantly higher than Textbook participants (Mdn = 4; M = 4, SD = 1.4) on the 6-point long-term recall assessment (Z = 1.89, p = .03, r = .25). [→ Courtney Heldreth 2026](#courtney-heldreth-2026)

## Evidence

### Courtney Heldreth 2026

Courtney Heldreth, Diana Akrong, Laura M. Vardoulakis, Nicole E. Miller, Yael Haramaty, Lidan Hackmon, Lior Belinsky, Abraham Ortiz Tapia, Lucy Tootill, and Scott Siebert. (2026). An Experimental Evaluation of an AI-Powered Interactive Learning Platform. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3389/frai.2026.1783117](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1783117)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A follow-up assessment emailed 3 days post-session (completed by 58 of 60 participants within a 3-7 day window) reassessed the targeted outcomes. The directional Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney test showed Learn Your Way students "scored significantly higher on the long-term recall assessment" (Z = 1.89, p = .03, r = .25).

> "Students in the Learn Your Way condition (M dn = 5; M = 4 .7, SD = 1 .2) scored significantly higher on the long-term recall assessment than students in the Textbook condition (M dn = 4 ; M = 4, SD = 1.4)"
