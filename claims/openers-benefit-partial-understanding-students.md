## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Having an Opener had a significant effect for students starting at KI level 3 (M = .71 vs .18 without Opener, p = .05); level 2 students made modest gains with or without an Opener. [→ Zertuche 2012](#zertuche-2012)

## Evidence

### Zertuche 2012

Zertuche, A., Gerard, L., & Linn, M. C. (2012). How do Openers Contribute to Student Learning? International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 79-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070484

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of pre-to-post Opener gains by pre-Opener KI score (Table 6). Level 3 students with an Opener developed into basic understanding (level 4); those without continued to show only partial understanding (M = .18, SD = .60).

> "Having an Opener had a significant effect on students who began with at least a partial understanding, or level 3 on the knowledge integration scoring rubric (M = .71, SD = .69), t(26) = -2.07, p = .05."
