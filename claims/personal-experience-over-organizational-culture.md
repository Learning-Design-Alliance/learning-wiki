Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L, Gaižiūnienė L and Pučėtaitė R (2026) AI in inclusive education: teachers' visions of future-oriented practices. Front. Educ. 11:1890925. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1890925

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Friedman test on complete cases (N = 43) comparing personal experience (median 9), societal factors (median 9), and organizational culture (median 8). Post-hoc Wilcoxon tests with Holm correction showed "personal experience was rated significantly higher than organizational culture".

> "A Friedman test indicated statistically significant differences among the three dimensions, χ2(2) = 30.29, p < .001 (N = 43) (Figure 2). Post-hoc Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm correction showed that personal experience was rated significantly higher than organizational culture (p < .001) and societal factors (p = .009), while societal factors were rated significantly higher than organizational culture (p = .014)."
