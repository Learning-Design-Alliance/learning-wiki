### Aymee Alvarez-Rivero 2023 (2)

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero, Candice Odgers and Daniel Ansari. (2023). Elementary school teachers' perspectives about learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning 8:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00191-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Country-stratified survey analysis: the SES–attendance association was significant in the US (τc = −0.19, p < 0.001) but not in Canada (τc = −0.03, p < 0.517); the article prints no standardised effect size such as d or r for this association.

> "For teachers in the US, lower levels of attendance were reported more frequently when students came from low-income households ðτc ¼/C0 0:19; p< 0:001Þ. For Canadian teachers, this association was not present ðτc ¼/C0 0:03; p< 0:517Þ"
