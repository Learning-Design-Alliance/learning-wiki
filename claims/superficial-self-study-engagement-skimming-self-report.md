## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Some students reported skimming or looking over tutorials, an engagement mode the authors judge unlikely to support learning. [→ DeVore 2019](#devore-2019)
`q1 i?` Self-reported tutorial times were inconsistent with one-on-one timings, e.g., some students reported up to 1.5 hours on the shortest tutorial that took interviewed students 15-30 minutes. [→ DeVore 2019 (2)](#devore-2019-2)

## Evidence

### DeVore 2019

DeVore, S., Marshman, E., & Singh, C. (2019). The challenge of engaging all students via self-paced interactive e-learning tutorials for introductory physics. [doi:10.3389/fict.2018.00003](https://doi.org/10.3389/fict.2018.00003)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative examination of student comments gathered with paired-problem responses in the self-study group. "skimmed" or "looked over" engagement is interpreted by the authors as unlikely to help learning.

> "Some students explicitly commented that they "skimmed" or "looked over" the tutorials but that type of engagement with the e-learning tool may not help them learn."

### DeVore 2019 (2)

DeVore, S., Marshman, E., & Singh, C. (2019). The challenge of engaging all students via self-paced interactive e-learning tutorials for introductory physics. [doi:10.3389/fict.2018.00003](https://doi.org/10.3389/fict.2018.00003)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' comparison of self-reported time on the angular momentum tutorial against one-on-one interview timings, cited as a reason for distrusting self-report accuracy. Both directions of inconsistency are reported (some far shorter, some far longer).

> "the conservati on of angular momentum e-learning tutorial took most interviewed students 15-30 minutes but some students in the self-study group reported spending up to 1.5 hours on this tutorial."
