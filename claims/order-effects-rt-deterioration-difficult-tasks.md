### Test Boredom While Working on Difficult Versus Easy Tasks: Online Supplementary Materials 2022

Test Boredom While Working on Difficult Versus Easy Tasks: Online Supplementary Materials. (2022). https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Test+Boredom+While+Working+on+Difficult+Versus+Easy+Tasks%22

`q2 · i?`

Supplemental Material S8 reports additional linear models testing whether the order of the difficult and easy conditions influenced results. For working-memory performance, order was not significant in either condition, with β = -0.17 (p = .319) in the difficult part and β = -0.24 (p = .163) in the easy part.

> "Order had no significant effect on working-memory performance in either the difficult (β = -0.17; p = .319) or the easy part (β = -0.24; p = .163)."

### Test Boredom While Working on Difficult Versus Easy Tasks: Online Supplementary Materials 2022 (2)

Test Boredom While Working on Difficult Versus Easy Tasks: Online Supplementary Materials. (2022). https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Test+Boredom+While+Working+on+Difficult+Versus+Easy+Tasks%22

`q2 · i?`

The same additional linear models in Supplemental Material S8 show that for RT deterioration, order was significant in the difficult part (β = 0.40; p = .020) but not in the easy part (β = -0.19; p = .277), "indicating that the sequence of conditions affected the magnitude of RT deterioration when tasks were difficult."

> "For RT deterioration, order was significant in the difficult part (β = 0.40; p = .020) but was not significant in the easy part (β = -0.19; p = .277), indicating that the sequence of conditions affected the magnitude of RT deterioration when tasks were difficult."
