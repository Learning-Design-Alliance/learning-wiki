## Evidence

### Morris 2018

Morris, P. A., Connors, M., Friedman-Krauss, A., McCoy, D. C., Weiland, C., Feller, A., Page, L., Bloom, H., & Yoshikawa, H. (2018). New Findings on Impact Variation From the Head Start Impact Study: Informing the Scale-Up of Early Childhood Programs. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858418769287

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multisite impact-variation analysis (Bloom & Weiland, 2015; n = 3,465–3,529 depending on outcome) of ITT and LATE effects on PPVT, WJ-Letter-Word, WJ-Oral Comprehension, and WJ-Applied Problems. Variation held "even for outcomes for which average treatment effects were not statistically significant." The printed values are cross-site standard deviations of impacts, not an effect size for the variation itself.

> "The amount of significant variation in the cognitive outcomes across centers was substantial (0.12–0.25 SD for assignment, 0.15–0.26 SD for enrollment; see Table 3)."
