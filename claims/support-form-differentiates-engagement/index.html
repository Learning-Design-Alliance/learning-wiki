Task-stratified support-form contrasts from Results Section 2.4 (Fig. 4b). The article interprets this crossover as two task-specific routes: "testing an instructed procedure in coding and adapting a modelled exemplar in writing".

"Instructing was positively associated with constructive engagement in coding but negatively associated in writing, whereas modelling was approximately null in coding but strongly positive in writing."
