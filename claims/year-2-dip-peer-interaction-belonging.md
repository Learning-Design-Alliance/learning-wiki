## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2023

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Progression in self-efficacy, interest, identity, sense of belonging, perceived recognition and effectiveness of peer interaction of physics majors and comparison with non-majors and Ph.D. students. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive trend in sense of belonging (1-5 scale) from Fig. 1e, showing a decline "from the maximum of 4.09 in year 1 pre to the minimum of 3.46 in year 2 post" with recovery to 3.96 in year 3 pre. Peer interaction similarly ranges from a minimum of 2.73 in year 2 post to a maximum of 3.05 in year 1 post.

> "there is a quick decline from the maximum of 4.09 in year 1 pre to the minimum of 3.46 in year 2 post. However, this returns to 3.96 in year 3 pre and remains relatively consistent from that point forward."
