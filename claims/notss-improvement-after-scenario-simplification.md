### Wu Kitty 2019

Wu Kitty, Kim Stephanie, Rajasingham Shihan J., Bruni Ida, Fung Kevin, Roth Kathryn E. (2019). Simulation of Urgent Airway Management in a Postthyroidectomy Hematoma. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10802

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Longitudinal observational analysis of NOTSS ratings across boot camp cohorts, reported in the Results section and Figure: ratings from four independent video raters (2012–2014) and later from 10 onsite faculty plus three video raters (2016–2017), with significance via Student's t test (**p < .01, ***p < .001, ****p < .0001).
