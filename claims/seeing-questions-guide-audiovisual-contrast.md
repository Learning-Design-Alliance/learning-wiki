## Evidence

### Elin Johansson 2017

Elin Johansson, Oskar Lindwall, Hans Rystedt. (2017). Experiences, appearances, and interprofessional training: The instructional use of video in post-simulation debriefings. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-017-9252-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from the analysis of the 40 recorded debriefings: facilitator questions (e.g. 'Do you see something that works well here?') project responses based on what is visible in the clip rather than on first-person experience, contrasting appearances with "their experiences from the situation".

> "The design and sequential environment of facilitators ' questions and comments made it relevant for the students to discuss how the displayed situations appeared audiovisually and how these appearances contrasted with their experiences from the situation."
