## Evidence

### Wang Hai 2025

Wang Hai, Liang Jun-jie, Zhang Zheng-liang, Pei Hong-hong, Pan Long-fei. (2025). Theory–practice discrepancy in cardiopulmonary resuscitation performance: a multi-centre evaluation of emergency healthcare providers in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08433-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional quality-control assessment of 586 emergency physicians and nurses in 34 Shaanxi hospitals compared questionnaire knowledge with instructor-scored manikin performance, finding "only 42.66%" practical success versus 97.95% theoretical (P < 0.01).

> "the practical success rate for achieving adequate chest compression depth was only 42.66%—a drastic reduction from the corresponding theoretical correct rate of 97.95% (P < 0.01)"
