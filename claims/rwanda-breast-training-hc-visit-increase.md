## Evidence

### Pace LE 2019

Pace LE, Dusengimana JMV, Shulman LN, Schleimer LE, Shyirambere C, Rusangwa C, Muvugabigwi G, Park PH, Huang C, Bigirimana JB, Hategekimana V, Rugema V, Umwizerwa A, Keating NL, Mpunga T. (2019). Cluster Randomized Trial to Facilitate Breast Cancer Early Diagnosis in a Rural District of Rwanda. Journal of Global Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1200/JGO.19.00209

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Registry-based comparison across 12 intervention and 6 control health centers, April 2015 to April 2017, found "1,486 unique patients visited intervention HCs for breast concerns" (537.1/100,000 person-years) versus 315 patients (104.0/100,000 person-years) at control HCs (P < .001).

> "1,486 unique patients visited intervention HCs for breast concerns (537.1 patients/100,000 person-years) v 315 patients (104.0 patients/100,000 person-years) who visited control HCs (P < .001; Table 1)."

### Pace LE 2019 (2)

Pace LE, Dusengimana JMV, Shulman LN, Schleimer LE, Shyirambere C, Rusangwa C, Muvugabigwi G, Park PH, Huang C, Bigirimana JB, Hategekimana V, Rugema V, Umwizerwa A, Keating NL, Mpunga T. (2019). Cluster Randomized Trial to Facilitate Breast Cancer Early Diagnosis in a Rural District of Rwanda. Journal of Global Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1200/JGO.19.00209

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-differences model with the health center as unit of analysis, comparing average monthly visits 6 months before versus 12 months after training, yielded "4.7 visits/month for phase 1 HCs (P = .001)" and 7.9 for phase 2 HCs (P = .007).

> "This led to a difference-in-differences result of 4.7 visits/month for phase 1 HCs (P = .001) and 7.9 for phase 2 HCs (P = .007)."
