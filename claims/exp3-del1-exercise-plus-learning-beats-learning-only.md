## Evidence

### Loprinzi Paul D. 2019

Loprinzi Paul D., Harris Faith, McRaney Kyle, Chism Morgan, Deming Raymond, Jones Timothy, Zou Liye, Tan Miaoqing. (2019). Effects of Acute Exercise and Learning Strategy Implementation on Memory Function. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55090568

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc comparison within Experiment 3 found E + L exceeded L at delayed recall 1 (Mdiff = 3.6, t = 2.06, p = 0.04), despite the omnibus interaction being non-significant. No effect size is printed.

> "For the between-subject effects, there was no learning by exercise interaction, F(2, 152) = 1.92, p = 0.17, ƞ2 = 0.005, but notably, for delayed 1 memory, E + L was greater than L (Mdiff = 3.6, SE = 1.7, t = 2.06, p = 0.04)."
