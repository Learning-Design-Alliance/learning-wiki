### Brener A 2020

Brener A, Lewnard I, Mackinnon J, Jones C, Lohr N, Konda S, McIntosh J, Kulinski J. (2020). Missed opportunities to prevent cardiovascular disease in women with prior preeclampsia. BMC Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12905-020-01074-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Engagement measure from the intervention arm of the chart-review study: slides were emailed with a read-receipt link, which confirmed receipt but, as the authors note, could not confirm review of the slides. The article reports the 69% open rate descriptively with no test statistic.

> "When examining the education intervention, it is notable that only 69% of providers who received the presentation by email opened and potentially reviewed the information."
