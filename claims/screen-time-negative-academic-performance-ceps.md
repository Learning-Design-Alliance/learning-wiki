## Evidence

### Feng Xiaosu 2025

Feng Xiaosu, Ren Shuanquan, Shi Peng. (2025). The relationship and mechanism of screen time and academic performance among adolescents: an empirical study based on CEPS. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1533327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression analysis of 17,150 CEPS students with Chinese, mathematics, English and overall midterm scores as dependent variables found "a significant negative correlation (β = −0.113 to −0.022, p < 0.01)"; the largest coefficient was for mathematics (β = −0.048), then English (−0.043), then Chinese (−0.022).

> "there was a significant negative correlation (β = −0.113 to −0.022, p < 0.01) between screen time and adolescents’ academic performance (Models 1 to 4), and research hypothesis H1 was supported."
