## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret the early-term peak in voluntary basic session attendance as reflecting self-directed learning stages of orientation, selection, and implementation rather than assessment proximity. [→ Buss B 2012](#buss-b-2012)

## Evidence

### Buss B 2012

Buss B, Krautter M, Möltner A, Weyrich P, Werner A, Jünger J, Nikendei C. (2012). Can the 'Assessment Drives Learning' effect be detected in clinical skills training? - Implications for curriculum design and resource planning. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000840](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000840)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation (type e) in the Discussion, not a tested result: they propose the early peak reflects self-directed learning, with early weeks as orientation, mid-term as selection, and end of term as implementation and evaluation.

> "the peak at the beginning of the term for voluntary basic skills-lab training attendances might not be so easy to explain through this construct. This finding might mainly be based on characteristic processes of self-directed learning"
