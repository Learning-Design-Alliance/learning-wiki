Chappell, S., Arnold, P., Nunnery, J., & Grant, M. (2015). An Examination of an Online Tutoring Program's Impact on Low-Achieving Middle School Students' Mathematics Achievement. Online Learning, 19(5). https://jolt.merlot.org/2015/vol11no2.htm

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative analysis of 84 student postsession commentaries across both schools using NVivo with inductive coding to identify emic perceptions. The article reports "positive comments outnumbered negative comments by a ratio of three to one," with approximately 40% spontaneously indicating the session was helpful and about a quarter identifying specific learning outcomes.
