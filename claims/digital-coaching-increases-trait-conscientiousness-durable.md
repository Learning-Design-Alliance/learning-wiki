# Digital coaching significantly increased trait conscientiousness, with effects stable one year after the intervention ended

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Melanie Turner 2025

Melanie Turner, Flaviu A. Hodis. (2025). Strengthening Conscientiousness by Means of Interventions: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10016-4

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

RCT with wait-list control of 1,523 Swiss German-speaking adults, 10-week online digital coaching guided by the General (Common) Change Factors framework. The review reports self-report d = 0.58 and observer-report d = 0.22.

> "Coaching significantly increased the conscientiousness of participants who desired to improve their conscientiousness. This effect was found for both self-reports (d = 0.58) and observer-reports of consci- entiousness (d = 0.22)"

### Melanie Turner 2025 (2)

Melanie Turner, Flaviu A. Hodis. (2025). Strengthening Conscientiousness by Means of Interventions: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10016-4

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

One-year follow-up RCT of 157 participants from the original coaching trial. The review reports "significant increases in conscientiousness that remained stable one year after the intervention ended"; no effect size is printed for the follow-up.

> "Coaching produced significant increases in conscientiousness that remained stable one year after the intervention ended"
