### Gyeong-Geon Lee 2024

Gyeong-Geon Lee, and Xiaoming Zhai. (2024). Using ChatGPT for Science Learning: A Study on Pre-service Teachers' Lesson Planning. [doi:10.1109/tlt.2024.3401457](https://doi.org/10.1109/tlt.2024.3401457)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 29 lesson plans from pre-service elementary teachers enrolled in a Science Education 1 course at a Korean teachers' university. The article reports "biology (9/29)" as the most targeted domain, with multiple domains allowed for cross-cutting content.

> "the pre-service teachers targeted five knowledge domains with varying percentages: biology (9/29), chemistry and earth science (7/29 each), and physics and environmental science (4/29  each)."
