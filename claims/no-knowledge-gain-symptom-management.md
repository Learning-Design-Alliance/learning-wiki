`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test results in the same n = 62 pre-post subgroup. The article reports no statistically significant differences for physical symptoms management or mental health knowledge; equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "No statistically significant differences were found in other symptoms management (pre: 2.35 ± 1.04, post: 2.42 ± 1.00, p = 0.726) or mental health (pre: 3.82 ± 0.38, post: 3.79 ± 0.41, p = 0.594)"
