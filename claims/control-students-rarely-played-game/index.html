Evidence¶

Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Neisler, J. & Haldar, L. (2020). Evaluation of an online tutoring program in elementary mathematics [Project Report]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/94

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Implementation data tracked by Cignition in the randomized trial: treatment students logged in a "mean of 27.4 times" while controls averaged 0.86 logins, with most never logging in. Treatment students completed a minimum of 12 and maximum of 23 tutoring sessions (mean 18.2).

"Students in the Treatment condition logged into the game a mean of 27.4 times, as expected. Students in the Control condition logged into the game fewer times (mean 0.86 times; with 73.6% of students never logging in even though it was available to them)."
