## Evidence

### Jenkins Anna 2021

Jenkins Anna, Weeks Molly S., Hard Bridgette Martin. (2021). General and specific stress mindsets: Links with college student health and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256351

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Same MANOVA in the 498-participant cross-sectional study; the duration main effect was significant but small, with "partial η2 = .039", and the authors note it contrasted with expectations.

> "participants rated stress arising from acute stressors (M = 1.89, SD = 0.59) as more debilitating than stress from chronic stressors (M = 2.04, SD = 0.74), Wilks' λ = .961, F(1, 481) = 19.34, p < .001, partial η2 = .039."
