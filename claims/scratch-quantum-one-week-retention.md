## Evidence

### Damian Rovara 2026

Damian Rovara, Robert Wille. (2026). Teaching Quantum Design Automation with Block-Based Programming. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.18206](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.18206)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-week retention re-assessment of the competence questions taken by a voluntary subsample (N=20 of 24). "participants achieved equal or better results in all questions, except forQ2-3"; pass rates changed between -4.2% (compilation) and +3.3% (verification). Because the follow-up was optional, the authors note self-selection bias is possible.

> "Notably, participants achieved equal or better results in all questions, except forQ2-3, where the mean score fell by a total 4.2%. As none of these differences are significant"
