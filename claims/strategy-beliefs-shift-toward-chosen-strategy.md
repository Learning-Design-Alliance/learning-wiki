# Strategy efficacy beliefs shift toward the strategy a student chooses: choosers of blocked practice raised blocked beliefs and lowered interleaved beliefs, and vice versa

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` For participants who chose blocked practice, a significant time × strategy belief interaction (ηp2 = 0.43) reflected rising blocked-efficacy beliefs and falling interleaved-efficacy beliefs. [→ Onan 2022](#onan-2022)
`q3 i3` For participants who chose interleaved practice, a significant time × strategy belief interaction (ηp2 = 0.54) reflected falling blocked-efficacy beliefs and rising interleaved-efficacy beliefs. [→ Onan 2022 (2)](#onan-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Onan 2022

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Within-subjects ANOVA on efficacy beliefs for the 90 participants who chose blocked practice. The interaction was significant, F(1, 89) = 66.71, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.43; pairwise comparisons showed blocked beliefs rose (M 4.42 to 4.99) and interleaved beliefs fell (M 3.59 to 2.80).

> "we found a sig- nificant time × strategy belief interaction, F(1, 89) = 66.71, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.43."

### Onan 2022 (2)

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Within-subjects ANOVA on efficacy beliefs for the 60 participants who chose interleaved practice. The interaction was significant, F(1,59) = 68.174, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.54; blocked beliefs fell (M 4.50 to 3.58) and interleaved beliefs rose (M 3.75 to 4.70).

> "there was a significant time × strategy belief interaction, F(1,59) = 68.174, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.54."
