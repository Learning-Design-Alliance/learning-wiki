## Evidence

### Giallo 2010

Giallo, R., Treyvaud, K., Matthews, J., & Kienhuis, M. (2010). Making the Transition to Primary School: An Evaluation of a Transition Program for Parents. Australian Journal of Educational & Developmental Psychology, 10, 1-17. https://www.newcastle.edu.au/journal/ajedp/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A satisfaction survey developed for this study was completed by 590 parents who received the program. Mean ratings across items ranged from 5.94 to 6.20 on a 7-point scale, indicating high satisfaction with program quality, content, and delivery.

> "On a 7-point scale, results reveal that parents rated the quality and content of the session as excellent, and indicated that there were definitely adequate opportunities for participation."
