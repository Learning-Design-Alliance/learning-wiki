# Barriers and facilitators to SDL in CPD occur at five levels: individual, program, technological, environmental, and workplace/organizational

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Conventional content analysis of 17 Canadian articles generated five categories of SDL barriers and facilitators, with individual barriers coded in most articles (82.3%) and program facilitators in most (70.6%). [→ Dahn J 2018](#dahn-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Dahn J 2018

Dahn J, Presseau J, ElChamaa R, Naumann DN, Mascaro C, Luconi F, Smith KM, Kitto S. (2018). Barriers and Facilitators to Self-Directed Learning in Continuing Professional Development for Physicians in Canada: A Scoping Review. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000002237

`q3 · i?`

Scoping review of 17 Canadian articles on SDL in CPD for physicians; inductive conventional content analysis generated the five level-based categories. The review reports "Individual barriers (n = 14; 82.3%) and program facilitators (n = 12; 70.6%) were coded in most articles."

> "Five categories of barriers and facilitators were generated through a conventional content analysis,40 focusing on the levels at which the barriers/facilitators are perceived to occur: individual, program, technological, environmental, and workplace/organizational."
