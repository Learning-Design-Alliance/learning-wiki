## Evidence

### Gholipour Kamal 2018

Gholipour Kamal, Tabrizi Jafar Sadegh, Farahbakhsh Mostafa, Iezadi Shabnam, Ghiasi Akbar, Jahanbin Hasan. (2018). Evaluation of the district health management fellowship training programme: a case study in Iran. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-020603

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest and post-test examinations were conducted in each of the fellowship courses, normalised to 0–100. The printed post-test results show the highest scores in "managing the district (77 out of 100)" and planning and evaluation (69).

> "According to pretest and post-test results, after training, district health managers obtained the highest scores in managing the district (77 out of 100), planning and evaluation (69), chronic disease management (69), human resources and creativity (68), and epidemiology (67)."

### Gholipour Kamal 2018 (2)

Gholipour Kamal, Tabrizi Jafar Sadegh, Farahbakhsh Mostafa, Iezadi Shabnam, Ghiasi Akbar, Jahanbin Hasan. (2018). Evaluation of the district health management fellowship training programme: a case study in Iran. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-020603

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pretest–post-test difference analysis across the fellowship courses, reported in the Results and figure 1. The courses with the most positive differences were managing the district (51%), research in the health system (42%) and human resources and creativity (37%).

> "Finally, the courses on managing the district (51%), research in the health system (42%), and human resources and creativity (37%) had the most positive differences between pretest and post-test scores (figure 1)."
