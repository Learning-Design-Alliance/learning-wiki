### Rouse 1980

Rouse, W. B., Rouse, S. H., Hunt, R. M., Johnson, W. B., and Pelligrino, S. J. (1980). Human Decision-Making in Computer-Aided Fault Diagnosis. Technical Report 434, Coordinated Science Laboratory, University of Illinois. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=IR008809

`q2 · i?`

Experiment Six: 39 trainees in the last semester of a two-year FAA certificate program, six 90-minute sessions over six weeks, training on context-free Tasks One and Two with transfer trials on the context-specific FAULT task. The article reports reduced cost to solution for two of three powerplants, mainly because "expensive benchtests were avoided"; no effect size is printed.
