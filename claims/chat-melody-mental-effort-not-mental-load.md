### Lingxi Jin 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mental load dimension (five items, Cronbach's alpha .88) of the same post-task questionnaire. The difference "was not statistically significant (𝑡 = 1.89,𝑝 >.05)", so the overall cognitive-load effect rests on the mental-effort dimension.

> "While the experimental group experienced slightly lower mental load (𝑀 = 2.81,𝑆𝐷 =.84) than the control group (𝑀 = 3.32,𝑆𝐷 =.44), the difference was not statistically significant (𝑡 = 1.89,𝑝 >.05), indicating that both groups perceived the task complexity similarly."
