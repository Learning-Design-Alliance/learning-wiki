`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review's synthesis of empathy definitions across included studies (RQ2, Table 2). The review reports that "16 explicitly defined empathy, typically encompassing cognitive, affective, and behavioural components", with 13 using cited definitions, three using original definitions, and three not reporting a definition.

> "Among the 19 studies, 16 explicitly defined empathy, typically encompassing cognitive, affective, and behavioural components. Of these, 13 studies (68.4%) used cited definitions from previous literature [31,32,47,63–71,73], while only three (15.8%) used original definitions [25,62,72], and three (15.8%) did not report a definition [26,56,74] (Table 2)."
