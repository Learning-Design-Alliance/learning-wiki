`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

SciMAT-based bibliometric performance and thematic analysis of 695 PIL articles indexed in Scopus and Web of Science across three periods between 1941 and 2024; the analysis found that "PIL, which was initially conceptualized as a top-down, directive leadership, evolved into a more indirect and integrated perspective of leadership."

> "The results showed that PIL, which was initially conceptualized as a top-down, directive leadership, evolved into a more indirect and integrated perspective of leadership, albeit maintaining the central role of the principal as the leader of leaders."
