# Test fatigue does not affect item difficulty among students who devote effort, but students who quit midway show order effects

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a reverse-ordered administration study, students who devoted effort scored equally well on a question block whether it appeared early or late, while students quitting midway often showed statistically significant (p<0.05) score differences by block position. [→ Follette 2015](#follette-2015)

## Evidence

Fall 2013 reverse-ordered administration to half of 91 pre- and 90 post-semester students (N=43, 42 reverse-ordered), analyzed with an ANOVA on two 11-question blocks. "students who devoted effort scored equally well on a given block whether it appeared in the first or second half"; no effect size printed.
