Evidence¶

Harsh Kumar 2026¶

Harsh Kumar, Zi Kang (Jace) Mu, Jonathan Vincentius, Ashton Anderson. (2026). Human Thinking under Plural LLM Assistance: Mathematical Problem Solving and Open-Ended Writing. Working Paper, July 2026, Toronto, Canada. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02677 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.02677

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Pre-registered controlled Prolific experiment (315 participants passing preregistered checks) with four between-subjects support conditions; test accuracy rose across conditions, with means "(∼42%), Peers Only (∼48%), Tutor Only (∼59%), and Tutor + Peers (∼65%)".

"Points show mean test accuracy (proportion correct in test round) across participants in each condition; vertical error bars indicate±1 SEM. (∼42%), Peers Only (∼48%), Tutor Only (∼59%), and Tutor + Peers (∼65%)."
