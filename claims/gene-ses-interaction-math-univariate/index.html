Evidence¶

Tucker-Drob 2011¶

Tucker-Drob, Elliot M., & Harden, K. Paige. (2011). Learning Motivation Mediates Gene-by-Socioeconomic Status Interaction on Mathematics Achievement in Early Childhood. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2011.11.015

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Univariate behavioral genetic model of math scores in the ECLS-B twin sample; the a′ parameter was significant (Step 2 estimate .124 [.051, .197]). Figure 2 plots the variance components as functions of SES, showing the pattern quoted above.

"At very low levels of SES, genes account for negligible variance in math scores, whereas at very high levels of SES the amount of variance in math scores accounted for by genes exceeds .30 (45% of the SES-independent variation)."
