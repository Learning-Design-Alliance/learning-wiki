## Evidence

### Caballero 2011

Caballero, M. D., Greco, E. F., Murray, E. R., Schatz, M. F., Bujak, K. R., Marr, M. J., Catrambone, R., & Kohlmyer, M. A. (2011). Comparing large lecture mechanics curricula using the Force Concept Inventory: A five thousand student study. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/1.3703517](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.3703517)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors verified that both curricula used similar pedagogical methods and that offering identical incentives to both groups in fall 2009 produced similar performance differences, ruling out pedagogy and incentive effects.

> "all sections (both traditional and M&I) were largely in- distinguishable with respect to interactive engagement: all section s used similar methods (“clicker” questions) with similar intensity (3-6 “clicker” questions p er lecture period)."
