## Evidence

### López-Gil 2020

López-Gil, J.F., Oriol-Granado, X., Izquierdo, M., Ramírez-Vélez, R., Fernández-Vergara, O., Olloquequi, J., & García-Hermoso, A. (2020). Healthy Lifestyle Behaviors and Their Association with Self-Regulation in Chilean Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17165676

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation analysis in a cross-sectional study of 1561 children aged 8–12 from eight low-SES public schools in Santiago, adjusted for age, sex, BMI z-score and sleep quality. Physical activity showed "PA (r = 0.265; p < 0.001)" with self-regulation.

> "PA (r = 0.265; p < 0.001) and MD (r = 0.091; p = 0.030) positively correlated with SR, and ST was negatively related to this outcome (r = −0.125; p = 0.003)."
