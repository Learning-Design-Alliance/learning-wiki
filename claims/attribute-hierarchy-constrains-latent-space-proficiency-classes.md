`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Theoretical statement from the article's abstract about how attribute hierarchies restrict the latent attribute space in cognitive diagnosis models. The article states that "several proficiency classes... are no longer defined" under a hierarchy, since infeasible attribute combinations cannot occur. No empirical data or effect size is reported.

> "A hierarchical organization of attributes constrains the latent attribute space such that several proficiency classes, as they exist if attributes are not hierarchically organized, are no longer defined because the corresponding attribute combinations cannot occur with the given attribute hierarchy."
