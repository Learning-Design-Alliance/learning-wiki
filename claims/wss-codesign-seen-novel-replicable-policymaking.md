### Johnson 2026

Johnson, D.W. (2026). Co-designing new approaches to school safety: District and community perspectives on Chicago's Whole School Safety Framework. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/co-designing-new-approaches-to-school-safety

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative field scan based on 12 interviews with district officials and CBO leaders on the WSS Steering Committee, conducted November 2023 to February 2024, with two rounds of thematic coding and member checking. Interviewees described the process as "trailblazing," "unprecedented," and centered on those most impacted. The article notes the approach aligns with a long-standing tradition of community-engaged policymaking, which "may complicate claims about the novelty of the approach."

> "District officials described the development of the WSS Framework as "trailblazing," "unprecedented," and illustrative of the district's emerging commitment to "centering the voices of those most impacted by [district] decisions.""
