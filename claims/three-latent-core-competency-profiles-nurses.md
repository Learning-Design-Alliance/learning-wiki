Heterogeneity in clinical nurses’ core competency and its associations with self-efficacy and self-directed learning ability: a cross-sectional latent profile analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2026-123528

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 605 registered clinical nurses recruited by convenience sampling from two general hospitals in Yanji, China; latent profile analysis on seven CIRN dimension scores selected a three-profile solution with entropy 0.97 and smallest class proportion 14.1%. The article reports "low-level core competency (266/605, 43.97%)" and the other proportions as printed.

> "Three profiles were identified: low-level core competency (266/605, 43.97%), moderate-level core competency (254/605, 41.98%) and high-level core competency (85/605, 14.05%)."
