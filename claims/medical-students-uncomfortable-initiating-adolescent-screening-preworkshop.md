### Cahill Helen 2015

Cahill Helen, Coffey Julia, Sanci Lena. (2015). ‘I wouldn’t get that feedback from anywhere else’: learning partnerships and the use of high school students as simulated patients to enhance medical students’ communication skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0315-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Two pre-workshop focus groups with 13 medical students explored concerns about initiating conversations using the HEADSS screening tool; one student said "I don’t feel comfortable at this stage asking… or broaching those conversations". Thematic qualitative analysis, no effect sizes.

> "Prior to workshops, medical students reported that they did not feel comfortable because of their own lack of practical experience in talking with adolescents, and because proactive screening necessitated asking about sensitive topics."
