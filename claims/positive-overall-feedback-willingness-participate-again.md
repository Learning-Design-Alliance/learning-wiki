## Evidence

### M Tuveri 2024

M Tuveri, A Steri and D Fadda. (2024). Using storytelling to foster the teaching and learning of gravitational waves physics at high-school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad4b87](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad4b87)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive feedback from the 76-answer questionnaire: 66 students (86.8%) would participate again; overall, 30 (39.5%) rated the activity as good and 26 (34.2%) as very good. The authors read this as students' appreciation of the methodology and its possible implementation at school.

> "Finally, when asked if they would participate again in similar activities, 66 students (86.8%) answered “yes”, 3 (3.9%) “no”, and 7 (9.2%) “I do not know”."
