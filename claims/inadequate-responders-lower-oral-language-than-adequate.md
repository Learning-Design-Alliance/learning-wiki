## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Inadequate responders (both comprehension-only and comprehension-plus-word-reading groups) scored significantly lower than adequate responders on verbal knowledge and listening comprehension after intervention. [→ Cho Eunsoo 2015](#cho-eunsoo-2015)
`q3 i3` Inadequate responders scored lower than typical readers on all six cognitive attributes measured, with corrected p-values below .001. [→ Cho Eunsoo 2015](#cho-eunsoo-2015)

## Evidence

### Cho Eunsoo 2015

Cho Eunsoo, Roberts Garrett J., Capin Philip, Roberts Greg, Miciak Jeremy, Vaughn Sharon. (2015). Cognitive Attributes, Attention, and Self-Efficacy of Adequate and Inadequate Responders in a Fourth Grade Reading Intervention. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4793275/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Planned pairwise contrasts from a MANOVA profile analysis of pretest cognitive measures in a randomized fourth-grade reading intervention study. Inadequate responders differed from adequate responders on "verbal knowledge, t (123) = 5.15, corrected p <.001, and listening comprehension, t (123) = 4.27, corrected p = .001". Hedges' g values from Table 3 show verbal knowledge g = 1.02 and listening comprehension g = .85 for the AR vs. IR contrast.

> "Inadequate (IR-C and IR-CW) and adequate responders differed on verbal knowledge, t (123) = 5.15, corrected p <.001, and listening comprehension, t (123) = 4.27, corrected p = .001."
