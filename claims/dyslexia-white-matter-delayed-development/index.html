Evidence¶

Chyl 2021¶

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing a longitudinal study, that "Already prior to the reading onset, children with dyslexia showed lower FA of the bilateral long AF segments than typical readers", with left AF differences canceled out after reading onset, showing white matter deficits are dynamic.

"Already prior to the reading onset, children with dyslexia showed lower FA of the bilateral long AF segments than typical readers, but after the reading onset differences in the left AF were canceled out"

Chyl 2021 (2)¶

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

q2 · i? · review · r3

Interpreting findings that both dyslexic and control children showed positive reading–FA correlations in left SLF, the review states "This result suggests a delay of white matter development in dyslexic children rather than an altered developmental pathway."

"This result suggests a delay of white matter development in dyslexic children rather than an altered developmental pathway."
