## Evidence

### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Joohyun Lee, Sejun Oh. (2026). Personalized Auto-Grading and Feedback System for Constructive Geometry Tasks Using Large Language Models on an Online Math Platform. Preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1109/access.2026.3657726](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3657726)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Learning-curve modeling of field-study logs: for each student–question pair, the first correct attempt was located and cumulative success proportions computed for attempts 1–4, excluding questions with fewer than 50 students. The curve rises from "approximately 0.60" to "≈ 0.73", reaching ≈ 0.80 by attempt four.

> "The curve rises sharply from the first attempt (approximately 0.60 ) to the second ( ≈ 0.73 ), indicating that a large share of initially incorrect responses are corrected after a single retry."
