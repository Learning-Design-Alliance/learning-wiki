`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative content analysis of 5,823 open-text stressor statements from the three groups, coded into a category system with substantial-to-almost-perfect interrater agreement. The detailed results section was truncated in the supplied text; this summary sentence is from the abstract.

> "In the first half of the lecture period, students primarily indicated the weekly exercise sheets, the physics lab course, and math courses as stressors; later on, preparation for exams and the exams themselves emerged as the most important stressors."
