## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2015

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2015). Emphasizing Expert Practice with Spaced Recall. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.082](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.082)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of two students recalling three physics problem solutions across four interviews over three months. The article reports that "consistent success at recalling the problems did not occur until after at least one unsuccessful spaced recall session." No effect size is printed.

> "Although both students were able to flawlessly recall the solutions to all three problems immediately after watching the solution videos, consistent success at recalling the problems did not occur until after at least one unsuccessful spaced recall session."
