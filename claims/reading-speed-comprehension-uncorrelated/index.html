Sackstein S 2015¶

Sackstein S, Spark L & Jenkins A. (2015). Are e-books effective tools for learning? Reading speed and comprehension: iPad vs. paper. South African Journal of Education, 35(4), Art. #1202. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n4a1202

Pearson's Correlation Analysis on the full sample of 68 students found no significant relationship between reading speed and comprehension (r = .024). This contradicts the hypothesised negative correlation (H3).

"The correlation between the vari - ables was found to be statistically non-significant (r = .024) (where r indicates the correlation, i.e. the relationship (Pietersen & Maree, 2007))."
