# Complete and faded double-content examples did not differentially affect student teachers' professional vision

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant differences emerged between the complete-example group and the faded-example group on any measure of professional vision. [→ Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025](#jasmin-lilian-bauersfeld-2025)

## Evidence

### Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the quasi-experimental pre-post study, Helmert contrasts comparing the complete double-content example group (IG1, n = 200) with the faded group (IG2, n = 136) on residualized change scores showed no significant differences on any dependent variable.

> "However, the second set of Helmert contrasts showed no significant differences between IG1 and IG2."
