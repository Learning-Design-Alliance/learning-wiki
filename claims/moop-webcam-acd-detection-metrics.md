`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Experiment within a reviewed study (Li et al., described by the review) in a computer science exam scenario using Kinect cameras, an S2 eye-tracker, and EEG monitoring; the webcam-only ACD "achieved a recall of 78.6%, precision of 84.6%, and accuracy of 83.3%."

> "The experiments show that the webcam-only version of ACD achieved a recall of 78.6%, precision of 84.6%, and accuracy of 83.3%. On the other hand, the multi-modal ACD, which includes more sophisticated sensors, displayed improvements in recall but at the expense of precision and overall accuracy."
