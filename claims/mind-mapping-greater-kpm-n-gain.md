# Improvement (N-gain) in mathematical reasoning ability is greater for students learning with mind mapping strategy than for students receiving conventional learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The increase in mathematical reasoning skills students acquire learning with SMM is better than the improvement of students who received conventional learning (PK). [→ Ayal 2016](#ayal-2016)

## Evidence

### Ayal 2016

Ayal, C. S., Kusuma, Y. S., Sabandar, J., & Dahlan, J. A. (2016). The Enhancement of Mathematical Reasoning Ability of Junior High School Students by Applying Mind Mapping Strategy. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(25). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1115860

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

N-gain comparison of two groups of 65 students each (SMM and PK) using a one-tailed test. The article reports "sig. (1-taile d) = 0.000", with mean N-gain 0.49 (SMM) versus 0.35 (PK), "an increase in the medium category" by Hake (1999) criteria.

> "Based on Table 7 it can be said that, sig. (1-taile d) = 0.000 less than α = 0.05, which means that H 0 is rejected. It can be concluded that the increase in mathematical reasoning skills students acquire lear ning with SMM better than the improvement of mathema tical reasoning abilities of students who received conventio nal learning (PK)."
