## Evidence

### Hundertmark Jan 2019

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate analyses entering personality variables into the RM-ANOVAs, yielding correlations adjusted for measurement point, day, tutor type and experience (Table 8). The article reports these as correlational findings, not causal effects.

> "Extraversion was correlated with high positive and low negative affect, which is not surprising, given the interactive and interpersonal nature of teaching demands. Furthermore, we found extraversion to be the only personality trait correlated with a physiological stress index, the RMSSD"

### Hundertmark Jan 2019 (2)

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same adjusted correlational analysis of 60 tutors' questionnaire data (BFI-K, RS 13, AAS, SAPS-R) against stress measures. The article reports null or tendency-level correlations for most traits; no effect sizes beyond the printed Pearson correlations are given.

> "Resilience was a protective factor against subjective stress and negative affect. Other personality traits, namely conscientiousness, openness, attachment style, and perfectionism, did not or only in tendency show correlations with stress measures"
