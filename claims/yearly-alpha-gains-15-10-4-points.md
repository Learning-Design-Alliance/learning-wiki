# Median Alpha scores increase each year of high school, by about 15, 10 and 4 points across the successive year intervals

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average median Alpha scores rise about 15 points from freshman to sophomore year, 10 points from sophomore to junior, and 4 points from junior to senior year, with a total freshman-to-senior increase of about 30 points. [→ Cobb 1922](#cobb-1922)

## Evidence

### Cobb 1922

Cobb, Margaret V. (1922). The Limits Set to Educational Achievement by Limited Intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 8. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=The+Limits+Set+to+Educational+Achievement+by+Limited+Intelligence

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Section III reports yearly increases in Alpha medians across surveyed schools (Tables X and XI). The article attributes the gains jointly to mental growth, temporary effects of instruction, and educational selection, and cannot apportion them exactly: "the exact amount which is due to this cause is at present impossible" to determine.

> "The average figures from Table X are, from freshman to sophomore year, 15 points; from sophomore to junior year, 10 points; and from junior to senior year, 4 points."
