## Evidence

### Maciej Jakubowski 2025

Maciej Jakubowski, Tomasz Gajderowicz & Harry Anthony Patrinos. (2025). COVID-19, school closures, and student learning outcomes. New global evidence from PISA. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00297-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regression analysis of all comparable PISA rounds for 72 countries modeling the 2022 departure from country-specific linear trends, with country fixed effects and student and country-average controls. The article reports "the decline in achievement is equal to 14.2% of a SD", roughly 7 months of learning at ~20 points per year.

> "The overall decline in mathematics associated with pandemic-era school closures is 12 points. In 2022, the average within-country SD of mathematics is 86.2. Thus, the decline in achievement is equal to 14.2% of a SD."
