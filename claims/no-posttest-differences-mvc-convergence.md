# No significant condition differences remain at posttest: both approaches converge on MVC strategy and reasoning after both phases

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On Task 2, MANCOVA showed no significant differences between conditions on the four MVC-strategy components. [→ Saba 2025](#saba-2025)

## Evidence

### Saba 2025

Saba, J., Kapur, M., & Roll, I. (2025). Learning about multivariable causality with interactive simulations: exploration before instruction may hurt immediate gains but benefits transfer. Instructional Science, 53, 1603–1632. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09726-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA on Task 2 posttest log files with virus-spread pretest as covariate found "no significant differences between conditions" on Time, Quantity, Quality and Efficiency (ηp2 = 0.042).

> "As shown in Table 5, there were no significant differences between conditions: F(1,69) = 0.718, p = 0.582, ηp2 = 0.042 for the MVC strategy, and no significant effect of the covariate F(1,69) = 0.600, p = 0.664, ηp2 = 0.036."

### Saba 2025 (2)

Saba, J., Kapur, M., & Roll, I. (2025). Learning about multivariable causality with interactive simulations: exploration before instruction may hurt immediate gains but benefits transfer. Instructional Science, 53, 1603–1632. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09726-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on virus-spread pretest versus Task 2 reasoning scores showed a significant main pre-post effect (ηp2 = 0.268); posttest means were Exploration-first M = 1.2 (SD = 1.44) versus Instruction-first M = 1.56 (SD = 1.43), with "no significant difference between conditions".

> "students’ MVC reasoning improved from pretest to posttest, F(1,69)= 25.316, p<0.001, ηp2 = 0.268 with a large effect size. There was no significant difference between conditions on the posttest"
