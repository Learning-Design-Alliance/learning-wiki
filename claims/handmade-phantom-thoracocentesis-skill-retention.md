## Evidence

### Huang Sih-Shiang 2024

Huang Sih-Shiang, Lin Chih-Hsien, Lin Shao-Yung, Huang Chien-Tai, Lien Wan-Ching. (2024). How to enhance the novices' learning in ultrasound-guided procedures utilizing handmade phantoms? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06458-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-center study; 36 novices (18 PGY-1, 18 UGY) trained on handmade phantoms and re-tested at 3 months on a commercial high-fidelity phantom. Global rating improved from "3.5 (3–4)" to "5 (4–5)", p = 0.0005; puncture time also improved (p < 0.0001 per Table 1).

> "novices demonstrated improved skill retention and performance at the 3-month assessment compared to the immediate assessment [5 (4–5) vs. 3.5 (3–4), p = 0.0005] in thoracocentesis"
