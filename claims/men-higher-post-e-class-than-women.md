### Danny Doucette 2022

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Students' attitudes toward experimental physics in a conceptual inquiry-based introductory physics lab. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2021-0121](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2021-0121)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same linear model of matched E-CLASS responses, gender was a statistically significant predictor: "Men scored 1 .46±1.04 points higher on the post E-CLASS than women", independent of pre score, high school GPA, and lab factors.

> "Men scored 1 .46±1.04 points higher on the post E-CLASS than women, independent of their pre E-CLASS, high school GPA, a nd other factors related to the nature of their lab."
