`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Thorndike's illustrative argument about a 12-year-old boy's score on a combination of named tests: it will prophesy book-learning rather accurately, but "less accurately" for concrete realities and still less for responding to people. This is the author's stated claim, not a reported study.

> "It will prophesy less accurately how well he will respond in thinking about a machine that he tends, crops that he grows, merchandise that he buys and sells and other concrete realities that he encounters in the laboratory, field, shop and office."
