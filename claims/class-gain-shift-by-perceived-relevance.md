## Subclaims
`q3 i2` There is statistical significance and a moderate effect size for overall CLASS normalized gains: students perceiving Relevance experienced a slight expert-like shift while students perceiving Irrelevance expressed a slight novice-like shift. [→ Andrew J. Mason 2020](#andrew-j-mason-2020)

## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason 2020

Andrew J. Mason. (2020). Learning goals and perceived irrelevance to major within life science majors in introductory physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.mason](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.mason)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

CLASS normalized gains, Relevant (n = 168) vs Irrelevant (n = 50): overall +0.04 vs -0.06, effect size 0.37, p = 0.02. The novice-like shift for Irrelevance appeared across all item clusters but not always with statistical significance; the strongest cluster differences were Personal Interest, Real-World Connection, and Conceptual Understanding (d ~ 0.33 each).

> "There is also a statistical significance  in normalized gains, as well as  a moderate effect size  difference, for the overall CLA SS. Students who perceived “Relevance” to the course experienced a slight expert -like shift, while students who perceived “Irrelevance” expressed a slight novice -like shift."
