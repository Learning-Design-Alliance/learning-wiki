Review-level synthesis of seven perception studies; the review reports positive acceptance among teachers, students, parents and staff, with benefits such as increased concentration, motivation and autonomy.

> "Our results also suggest that learning spaces are positively perceived and well accepted by the entire educational community, and that teachers teaching in ALCs are more prone to use student-centered and collaborative pedagogies than in traditional classrooms."
