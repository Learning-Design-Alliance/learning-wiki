## Evidence

### Asadi 2026

Asadi, I. (2026). Evaluating social–emotional skills among Arabic‐speaking students: A multi‐informant study of students, parents and teachers. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70044

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Two-way ANOVAs with repeated measures on the 33-item SEL composite from 241 students, 170 parents and 236 teachers. The group effect was significant ("F(2, 168) = 28.5, p < .001, η 2 = .148"), a large effect; Bonferroni comparisons showed "parents > students > teachers" (parents M = 4.03, students M = 3.86, teachers M = 3.68). The abstract says this held "regardless of grade level", but the group × grade interaction was significant (F(2, 168) = 3.23, p < .01, η² = .056): in paired t-tests students did not differ from parents or teachers in third grade, differed only from teachers in fifth grade, and all three groups differed from each other only in seventh and ninth grades.

> "These analyses revealed a significant main effect of group, F(2, 168) = 28.5, p < .001, η 2 = .148, grade level, F(3, 168) = 3.2, p < .01, η 2 = .056."
