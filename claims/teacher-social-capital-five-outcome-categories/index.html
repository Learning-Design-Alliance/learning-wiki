Evidence¶

Demir 2021¶

Demir, Ema Kristina. (2021). The Role of Social Capital for Teacher Professional Learning and Student Achievement: A Systematic Literature Review. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2021.100391

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of 66 empirical items (2004-2019), appraised with the Weight of Evidence framework, reports this five-category outcome association. The article states the outcomes "have in turn been associated with the implicit outcome of promoting educational equity."

"The review finds that social capital among teachers has been associated with five categories of outcomes: 1) teacher professional development, 2) the implementation of change 3) the introduction of new and beginning teachers, 4) teacher retention and job satisfaction, and 5) improved student achievement."
