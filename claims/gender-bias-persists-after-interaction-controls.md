## Evidence

### Sundstrom 2024

Sundstrom, M., Simpfendoerfer, L. N., Tan, A., Heim, A. B., & Holmes, N. G. (2024). Who and what gets recognized in peer recognition. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM analysis included lab group homophily, lab section homophily, discussion section homophily, and lecture-course enrollment variables as controls; the article reports that "these results related to gender bias hold even after controlling for the gender composition of lab groups" and other interaction patterns.

> "We particularly note that these results related to gender bias hold even after controlling for the gender composition of lab groups (lab group homophily variable), which were intentionally made to avoid isolated women, and other patterns of student interactions"
