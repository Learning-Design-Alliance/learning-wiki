## Evidence

### Liu Rong 2025

Liu Rong, Li Lei, Zhou Ying, Xiong Lu. (2025). The application of CBL and mind mapping combined with Mini-CEX teaching mode in the cultivation of clinical competence of ultrasound residents. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327739

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 42 ultrasound residents randomly assigned by random number table to experimental (n = 21) or control (n = 21) groups over a 4-week training period; Table 1 reports graduation theory t = 5.93 and practical t = 6.21, both P < 0.05, with entrance scores not significantly different (t = 0.35, 0.37).
