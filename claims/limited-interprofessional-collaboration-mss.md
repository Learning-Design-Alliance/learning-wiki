## Evidence

### Netzer R. 2025

Netzer R., Levi N., Ganchrow K., Naan Y., Elboim-Gabyzon M. (2025). Clinical Competency in Managing Malnutrition–Sarcopenia Syndrome Among Physical Therapists: A Cross-Sectional Survey. Nutrients 17(2), 281. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17020281

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey items on collaboration behavior in the 337-PT sample: a consultation checklist for sarcopenia and a four-point Likert cooperation scale for malnutrition. The article reports "an average of 2.13 ± 1.0 out of 4" for malnutrition collaboration and "65% of PTs indicated little-to-no cooperation". No effect size printed.

> "Many participants (59.4%) reported collaborating with healthcare professionals in the management of sarcopenia, primarily with clinical dietitians (44.2%), medical doctors (36.7%), and other PTs (28.9%). In contrast, the interprofessional collaboration score for malnutrition was moderate, with an average of 2.13 ± 1.0 out of 4. Notably, 65% of PTs indicated little-to-no cooperation with other healthcare professionals in malnutrition care."
