## Evidence

### Mokhtari 2015

Mokhtari, K., Neel, J. L., Kaiser, F., & Le, H.-H. (2015). Assessing the Promise of a Supplemental Reading Intervention for At-Risk First Grade Students in a Public School Setting. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 7(3), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068056

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post hoc comparisons showed typically developing students outperformed at-risk non-tutored students (p = .001) "but not the at-risk tutored students (p = .218) although they had a higher adjusted mean score". The authors note an achievement gap remains when comparing tutored students to typically developing peers.

> "The post hoc tests further showed that the typically developing students outperformed the at-risk non-tutored students (p = .001) but not the at-risk tutored students (p = .218) although they had a higher adjusted mean score"
