## Evidence

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

General linear mixed model on pre-post changes in PSQ20 total stress score in the RCT (OLP N = 79, control N = 75). The article reports the group × time interaction "− 4.84 ± 2.44, t(153) = − 1.98, p = 0.049, d = − 0.32", a small effect.

> "Participants in the OLP group showed a significantly smaller increase in overall stress scores (Perceived Stress Questionnaire, PSQ20) from baseline to pre-exam in comparison to control group participants as indicated by a group × time interaction (OLP compared to control, − 4.84 ± 2.44, t(153) = − 1.98, p = 0.049, d = − 0.32)"
