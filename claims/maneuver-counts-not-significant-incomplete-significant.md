## Evidence

### Vedula S. Swaroop 2016

Vedula S. Swaroop, Malpani Anand O., Tao Lingling, Chen George, Gao Yixin, Poddar Piyush, Ahmidi Narges, Paxton Christopher, Vidal Rene, Khudanpur Sanjeev, Hager Gregory D., Chen Chi Chiung Grace. (2016). Analysis of the Structure of Surgical Activity for a Suturing and Knot-Tying Task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149174

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of maneuver counts across 135 dVSS trials by 18 surgeons, using bootstrap interval estimates by skill level. The article reports no statistically significant expert-novice difference in total maneuvers under the experience-based definition; equivalence was not tested.

> "Trials by expert surgeons (experience-based skill definition) were performed using fewer maneuvers over the entire task than trials by novice surgeons; the difference between the groups was not statistically significant (Fig 2, Table B in S1 File)."

### Vedula S. Swaroop 2016 (2)

Vedula S. Swaroop, Malpani Anand O., Tao Lingling, Chen George, Gao Yixin, Poddar Piyush, Ahmidi Narges, Paxton Christopher, Vidal Rene, Khudanpur Sanjeev, Hager Gregory D., Chen Chi Chiung Grace. (2016). Analysis of the Structure of Surgical Activity for a Suturing and Knot-Tying Task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149174

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same descriptive dataset of dVSS surgeon's-knot trials; the article reports the expert-novice difference in incomplete maneuvers as statistically significant under the experience-based definition, while differences across GRS categories were not statistically significant.

> "Trials by expert surgeons (experience-based skill definition) were also performed with fewer incomplete maneuvers than trials by novice surgeons; the difference between the groups was statistically significant."
