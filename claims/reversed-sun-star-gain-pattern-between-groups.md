### Bekaert 2025

Bekaert, H., Van Dooren, W., Van Winckel, H., Poessei, M., Thiering, I., Nicolini, M., Artioli, E., Avgerinou, D., Balla, E., & De Cock, M. (2025). Descriptive comparison of gains in the two-treatment pretest-posttest study (Table 5): group 1 gained +12% Sun / +17% star; group 2 gained +16% Sun / +11% star. The article reports the contrast as "the learning gain for the Sun questions is larger than the star questions" in group 2, reversed in group 1.

> "In the second group, the learning gain for the Sun questions is larger than the star questions, while in the first group the strongest learning gain is achieved for the star questions."
