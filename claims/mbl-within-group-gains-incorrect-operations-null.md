## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MBL group ability scores after teaching were higher than before teaching for diagnosis (83.29 ± 18.30% vs. 71.94 ± 19.38%), comprehensive analysis (76.02 ± 18.58% vs. 66.74 ± 14.92%), and prescribing medical orders (85.17 ± 10.17% vs. 76.60 ± 18.58%), all p < 0.05.
