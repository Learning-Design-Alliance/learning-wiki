Evidence¶

Carver-Thomas 2018¶

Carver-Thomas, D. (2018). Diversifying the teaching profession: How to recruit and retain teachers of color. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/diversifying-teaching-profession

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that even after exam modifications, fail-rate disparities persisted without predictive validity for effectiveness. It adds that a PACT study found no pass-rate disparities and that edTPA score gaps were smaller than on traditional exams.

"Studies have continued to find higher fail rates for prospective teachers of color but have not found that the exams accurately and consistently predict their effectiveness as teachers."
