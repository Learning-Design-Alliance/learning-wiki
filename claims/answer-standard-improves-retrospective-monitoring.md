`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of studies by Baars et al. (2014b) and Oudman et al. (submitted), with similar findings for concept definitions, reporting that a comparison standard "improve[d] the accuracy of students' retrospective monitoring judgments". No effect size is printed.

> "providing students with a standard (i.e., the correct answer/solution procedure) against which to compare their own performance while making monitoring judgments was found to improve the accuracy of students' retrospective monitoring judgments"
