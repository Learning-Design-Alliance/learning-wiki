## Evidence

### Fernández‐Sánchez 2022

Fernández‐Sánchez, A.; Redondo‐Tébar, A.; Sánchez‐López, M.; Visier‐Alfonso, M. E.; Muñoz‐Rodríguez, J. R.; Martínez‐Vizcaíno, V. (2022). Sex differences on the relation among gross motor competence, cognition, and academic achievement in children. Scandinavian Journal of Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/sjop.12827

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional multi-group structural equation model of baseline data from the MOVI-daFIT! cluster-randomized trial, 451 children (234 girls), with GMC (MABC-2), EFs (NIH Toolbox) and grades as latent variables. The sex-split mediation paths are reported: "EFs totally mediated the association between GMC and AA (c′; β = 0.14, p = 0.012) in boys" while for girls the indirect path was not significant.

> "In the analysis by sex, it was found that EFs totally mediated the association between GMC and AA (c′; β = 0.14, p = 0.012) in boys (Fig. 2), but in girls (Fig. 3), EFs did not mediate this association (c′; β = 0.10, p = 0.326)."
