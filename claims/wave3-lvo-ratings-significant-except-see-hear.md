`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-sample t-tests (test value 3 = Neutral) on LVO-only ratings from all 40 students in Wave 3 (Section 1 n = 24; Section 2 n = 16). Table 4 shows, e.g., no travel required beneficial M = 4.95, t = 55.88, p = .000; ability to see everything M = 2.90, t = -.75, p = .457.
