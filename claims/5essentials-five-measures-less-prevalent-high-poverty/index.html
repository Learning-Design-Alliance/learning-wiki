Title as printed 2021¶

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Quantitative analysis of 2011–18 CPS data compared the likelihood of strong 5Essentials results across poverty levels, finding most measures similarly likely to be strong while "Strength in five of these measures was less prevalent in high-poverty schools." No effect size is printed.

"Strength in five of these measures was less prevalent in high-poverty schools at both the elementary and high school level. These included one student measure—Safety—and four teacher measures—Parent Involvement, Teacher-Parent Trust, School Commitment, and Student Discussion."
