`q2 i?` D studies show that two raters are enough to achieve a G coefficient above 0.85, and even one rater gives Coef-G above 0.75. [→ Rivière E 2025](#riviere-e-2025)

## Evidence

### Rivière E 2025

Rivière E, Der Sahakian G, Tremblay M-L, Chiniara G. (2025). Development and validation of the QASSH scale: a tool for assessing the quality of simulation scenarios in healthcare education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2486971

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

D-study optimization analyses (Table 4) varying the number of raters from 1 to 4 for each QASSH variant; e.g. immersive simulation Coef-G rises from 0.81 (1 rater) to 0.89 (2 raters). The article states "With two raters, Coef-G increased significantly to values above 0.85".

> "This is further demonstrated by the D studies, which show high generalizability coefficients even when the scenarios were evaluated by a single rater (Coef-G > 0.75). With two raters, Coef-G increased significantly to values above 0.85."
