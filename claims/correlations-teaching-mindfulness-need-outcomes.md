## Evidence

### Li Chunxiao 2019

Li Chunxiao, Kee Ying Hwa, Kong Leng Chee, Zou Liye, Ng Ka Lok, Li Hong. (2019). Autonomy-Supportive Teaching and Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction among School Students: The Role of Mindfulness. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16142599

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive correlations among the 390-student survey variables (Table 1). The printed correlations include "r = 0.60, p < 0.001" and "r = −0.38, p < 0.001"; mindfulness–need frustration was r = −0.53 and mindfulness–need satisfaction r = 0.35.

> "Autonomy-supportive teaching was significantly related to mindfulness (r = 0.28, p < 0.001), need satisfaction (r = 0.60, p < 0.001), and need frustration (r = −0.38, p < 0.001)."
