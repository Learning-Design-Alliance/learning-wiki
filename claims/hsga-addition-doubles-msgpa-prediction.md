### Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin 2012

Farrokhi-Khajeh-Pasha Yasin, Nedjat Saharnaz, Mohammadi Aeen, Rad Elaheh Malakan, Majdzadeh Reza, Monajemi Farshid, Jamali Ehsan, Yazdani Shahryar. (2012). The validity of Iran’s national university entrance examination (Konkoor) for predicting medical students’ academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-60

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression models C4, C5, C7 and C8 on the national cohort (msGPAs for 1822 of 2126 matriculants); hsGPA alone predicted msGPA with r = 0.445, and model C8 (Konkoor total plus hsGPA) reached R2 = 0.225.

> "Table 3 shows that the percentage of variance explained in msGPAs nearly doubled when hsGPAs were added to Konkoor scores as predictors (C5, C8), compared with when Konkoor scores were used alone (C4, C7)."
