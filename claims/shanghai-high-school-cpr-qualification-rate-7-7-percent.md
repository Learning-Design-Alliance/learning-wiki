### Tang Hong-mei 2020

Tang Hong-mei, Wu Xiao, Jin Yin, Jin Yi-qing, Wang Zi-jun, Luo Jin-yan, Hu Yan-qi, Jin Ting, Shang Moses, Chang Qing, Wang Fei. (2020). Shorter training intervals increase high school students’ awareness of cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a questionnaire study. Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060519897692

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 430 students from three Shanghai high schools, scored against the 2015 CPR guideline update with ≥60% correct required to qualify. The study reports the "overall qualification rate of 7.7%" and item pass rates from 74.2% (emergency response) down to 9.5% (defibrillation).

> "Data from the 430 returned questionnaires indicated an overall qualification rate of 7.7%. The pass rate for identification and activation of the emergency response system was 74.2% (319/430), that for chest compression was 19.5% (84/430)"
