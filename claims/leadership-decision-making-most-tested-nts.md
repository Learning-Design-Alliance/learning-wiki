## Evidence

### Gamborg ML 2024

Gamborg ML, Salling LB, Rölfing JD, Jensen RD. (2024). Training technical or non-technical skills: an arbitrary distinction? A scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06419-6

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Descriptive synthesis of which NTS and TS constructs the 40 positive-correlation studies examined. The review reports leadership (n=14) and decision making (n=14) as most common, with situational awareness (n=12), teamwork (n=12) and communication (n=11) also common, and "surgical performance (n = 27)" as the most frequent TS, in simulation or the OR.

> "In general, the most common NTS tested for in the studies that found a positive correlation between NTS and TS, were leadership (n = 14) and decision making (n = 14)."
