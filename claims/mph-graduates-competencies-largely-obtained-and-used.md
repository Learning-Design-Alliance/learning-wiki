### Zweigenthal V 2026

Zweigenthal V, Christofides N, Dlungwane T, Matlala SF, Mokgatle MM, Opare A, Patrick SM, Schaay N, Shung-King M, Tshitangano T, Rispel L. (2026). Self-perceived competencies of Master of Public Health graduates and recommendations for programs in South Africa. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0360789

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional online survey of 221 MPH alumni from eight South African universities graduating 2012–2016, administered via REDCap. The article reports that over 80% "needed the competencies in all 11 domains at work" and most were confident using them.

> "Over 80% of this cohort of 221 graduates, mostly in their mid-30’s, largely from South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa, needed the competencies in all 11 domains at work, which were felt to be largely obtained from the MPH, and most were confident using them."
