Trend analysis of ITBS grade-equivalent scores by age cohort from 1990 to 2001, reported in the Elementary School Test Score Trends section and Figure 2, tracking nine- through fourteen-year-olds against national norms.

"Taken as a whole, trends show improvement across the decade. In both reading and math, scores are considerably higher for all age groups than they were at the beginning of the 1990s. There are indica- tions that trends have flattened in the past few years, however."
