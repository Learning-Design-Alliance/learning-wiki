## Evidence

### Huang Pin-Hsiang 2023

Huang Pin-Hsiang, O'Sullivan Anthony John, Shulruf Boaz. (2023). Development and validation of the student ratings in clinical teaching scale in Australia: a methodological study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2023.20.26

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CFA with structural equation modelling (AMOS 24.0) on the same 352-respondent validation sample. The article reports "The RMSEA was 0.069, and the SRMR was 0.060" and also chi-square χ2=345.71 (df=129, P<0.01), CFI=0.930, and TLI=0.917, with composite reliability above 0.7 for all four factors.

> "The RMSEA was 0.069, and the SRMR was 0.060; both were within the acceptable range."
