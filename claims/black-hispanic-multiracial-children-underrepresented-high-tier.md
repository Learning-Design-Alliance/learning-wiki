# Black, Hispanic/Latino/a, and Multiracial preschool children were underrepresented in higher-quality (Tier 4 and 5) programs

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children who are Black, Hispanic/Latino/a, or Multiracial were more likely to attend Tier 3 programs and less likely to attend Tier 4 and 5 programs than Asian/Pacific Islander, White, or Native American children, with Black children least likely to be in Tier 5 programs. Sussman, J., Melnick, H., Newton, E., Kriener-Althen, K., Draney, K., Mangione, P., & Gochyyev, P. (2022). Preschool quality and child development: How are learning gains related to program ratings? Learning Policy Institute.

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of tier enrollment by race/ethnicity (Figure 5), with proportions compared via logistic regression controlling for multilingual learner status, IEP status, and gender. Overall, 14% of children were in Tier 3, 63% in Tier 4, and 23% in Tier 5.
