## Evidence

### Rim Mekonnen Hadgu 2016

Rim Mekonnen Hadgu, Sophia Hoang-Vy Huynh, & Chaya Gopalan. (2016). The Use of Pre-recorded Lectures on Student Performance in Physiology. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v5n1p105

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison within one Introductory Physiology course of 60 consenting students: exam questions from two replaced live lectures were compared with questions from two pre-recorded lectures. The article reports "any significant difference" was not found for either the overall or the memory-question comparison; equivalence was not tested.

> "We did not find any significant difference in student performance between the LL and PRL groups as well as between LLMQ and PRL+MQ groups."
