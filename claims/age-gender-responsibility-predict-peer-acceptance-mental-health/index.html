Evidence¶

Swords 2011¶

Swords, L, Heary, C & Hennessy, E 2011, 'Factors associated with acceptance of peers with mental health problems in childhood and adolescence', Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry and Allied Disciplines, vol. 52, no. 9, pp. 933-941. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.2010.02351.x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Regression models tested on survey responses from 595 participants across five age groups responding to vignettes of hypothetical peers with depression and ADHD. The article reports that these three variables were "the three most important predictors of acceptance in all models"; no effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.

"The models indicated that age and gender of the participant, and the perceived responsibility of the target character for his/her condition, were the three most important predictors of acceptance in all models."
