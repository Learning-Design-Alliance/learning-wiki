## Evidence

### Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı 2017

Gökhan Özdemir and M. Can Çiftçibaşı. (2017). Effect of pop music on students’ attitudes to music lessons. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3321

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pretest analysis across both groups before application: highest means were items 5 and 6 (e.g. 3.67, 3.74) and lowest were items 4, 14 and 15 (e.g. 1.97, 1.96, 1.86, 1.91). The article reports students "stated that the music teachers do not use instruments in lessons and do not benefit from technological facilities."

> "When the attitude points of all items are examined, students generally can be said to have moderately positive attitude toward music lessons."
