Evidence¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin 2025¶

Duncan Culbreth · Florence Martin. (2025). Exploring the role of synchrony in asynchronous, synchronous, and quasi-synchronous online learner engagement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10504-y

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Findings from the three-week debate study of 19 students; the article states these changes "fundamentally altered behavioral data" and that Discord was the only modality to receive positive comments about flexibility.

"As the synchronous modality was less logistically flexible, some students swapped or missed sessions resulting in one session with a lopsided team structure of one affirmative versus five negative."
