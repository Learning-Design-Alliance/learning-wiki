## Evidence

### O'Neill Lotte Dyhrberg 2014

O'Neill Lotte Dyhrberg, Norberg Karen, Thomsen Maria, Jensen Rune Dall, Brøndt Signe Gjedde, Charles Peder, Mortensen Lene Stouby, Christensen Mette Krogh. (2014). Residents in difficulty—just slower learners? a case–control study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0276-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate conditional logistic regression in the matched case–control study; Table 3 prints OR 0.90 (0.76–1.07), p = 0.246 for average grades. Table 2 shows near-identical mean grades (7.17 vs 7.34, p = 0.320).

> "Neither admission age, gender, medical school grades, nor the total number of academic dispensation applications in medical school were significant univariate predictors of being a resident in difficulty (Table 3)."
