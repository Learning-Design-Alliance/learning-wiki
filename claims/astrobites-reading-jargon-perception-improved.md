[doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010124](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010124)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pre/post Likert survey analysis of 52 undergraduates enrolled in astronomy-related courses (majors and students intending related subjects or careers) across seven courses at six institutions during 2022-2023; paired t-tests with Bayes factors. Table II reports Jargon p-value < 0.001, CI95% [0.10 0.39], BF10 15.1 (strong), power 0.70203. The article states there is "strong evidence in change in perceived ability with jargon".

> "There is strong evidence in change in perceived ability with jargon and moderate evidence for change in perceived ability with conceptual understanding/intuition."
