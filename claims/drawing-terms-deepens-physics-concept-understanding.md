## Evidence

### N. Poljak 2018

N. Poljak, A. Bosilj, S. Brzaj, J. Dragovic, T. Dubcek, F. Erhardt, M. Jercic. (2018). Physionary - a scientific version of Pictionary. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aadde4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aadde4)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' informal observation from three editions of a summer school game, not a controlled study. They state the game "turned out not to be only entertaining, but also highly educational", based on noticed student behaviour during play. No measures or tests are reported.

> "We believe that the drawing of physical terms helps students develop a deeper understanding of physical concepts behind them, as well as apply them in everyday situations."
