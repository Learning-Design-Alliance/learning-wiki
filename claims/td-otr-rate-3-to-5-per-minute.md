# Faster presentation rates of TD-OTRs produced favorable outcomes, with desired outcomes occurring at approximately 3.00-5.00 TD-OTRs per minute

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All four studies exploring increased TD-OTR rates found positive effects on academic and behavioral variables; descriptive data suggest desired outcomes at roughly 3.00-5.00 TD-OTRs per minute. [→ MacSuga-Gage 2015](#macsuga-gage-2015)
`q2 i?` In Sutherland et al. (2003), teacher OTR rates rose from a baseline mean of 1.68 to 3.5 per minute under the increased-OTR condition. [→ MacSuga-Gage 2015 (2)](#macsuga-gage-2015-2)

## Evidence

### MacSuga-Gage 2015

MacSuga-Gage, A. S., & Simonsen, B. (2015). Examining the Effects of Teacher-Directed Opportunities to Respond on Student Outcomes: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Education and Treatment of Children, 38(2), 211-240. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of four rate studies (Carnine, 1976; Sterling et al., 1997; Sutherland et al., 2003; West & Sloane, 1986); the review also reports descriptively that "desired student outcomes occurred with the presentation of approximately 3.00-5.00 TD-OTRs per minute."

> "Overall, studies that focused on increasing the rate of TD-OTR presentation generally showed that faster presentation led to favorable outcomes for students with and without disabilities"

### MacSuga-Gage 2015 (2)

MacSuga-Gage, A. S., & Simonsen, B. (2015). Examining the Effects of Teacher-Directed Opportunities to Respond on Student Outcomes: A Systematic Review of the Literature. Education and Treatment of Children, 38(2), 211-240. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070193

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Sutherland, Alder, and Gunter (2003), ABAB design with nine elementary students with EBD, with teacher performance feedback and self-graphing toward a 3.00 per minute goal; teachers exceeded it, per the printed means.

> "teachers mean rate of OTR per minute during baseline was 1.68 and increased to a mean rate of 3.5 during the increased OTR condition."
