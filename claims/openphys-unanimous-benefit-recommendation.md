### Ashadul Halder and Shibaji Banerjee 2026

Ashadul Halder and Shibaji Banerjee. (2026). Bridging the Gap Between Virtual and Physical Laboratories: A Web-Based Interactive Platform for Undergraduate Physics Practicals. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae29d5](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae29d5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary Likert-scale feedback survey of undergraduate physics students at St. Xavier's College, Kolkata who used the platform during COVID-19 remote learning. All respondents rated it beneficial; the article reports percentages only, no effect size.

> "Student feedback revealed that 100% of the respondents rated the platform as beneficial (rating 4 or 5 on a 5-point scale) to improve conceptual understanding and increase confidence in conducting physical experiments."

### Ashadul Halder and Shibaji Banerjee 2026 (2)

Ashadul Halder and Shibaji Banerjee. (2026). Bridging the Gap Between Virtual and Physical Laboratories: A Web-Based Interactive Platform for Undergraduate Physics Practicals. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae29d5](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae29d5)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Yes/No/Maybe recommendation item from the same structured feedback survey. All students selected Yes; open-ended responses suggested expanding the platform to more experiments. No effect size printed.

> "Results: 100.00% of students would recommend the platform as a regular pre-lab practice tool."
