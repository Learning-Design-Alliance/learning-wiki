## Evidence

### Kim Sunmi 2022

Kim Sunmi, Jeong Seok Hee, Kim Hee Sun, Jeong Young Ju. (2022). Academic Success of Online Learning in Undergraduate Nursing Education Programs in the COVID-19 Pandemic Era. Journal of Professional Nursing, 38, 6-16. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.profnurs.2021.10.005

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate t-tests/ANOVA on the 200-student survey (Table 2); obstacles differed by age, grades, computer-use ability, and internet-search ability (all p < 0.05). The article reports "the perceived level of obstacles to online learning was lower in the fourth grade than in second and third grades."

> "Post-hoc analysis (Scheffé test) found that the perceived level of obstacles to online learning was lower in the fourth grade than in second and third grades."

### Kim Sunmi 2022 (2)

Kim Sunmi, Jeong Seok Hee, Kim Hee Sun, Jeong Young Ju. (2022). Academic Success of Online Learning in Undergraduate Nursing Education Programs in the COVID-19 Pandemic Era. Journal of Professional Nursing, 38, 6-16. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.profnurs.2021.10.005

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate analyses in the same survey (Table 2); satisfaction differed by computer-use ability, internet-search ability, administrative district, and university type (all p < 0.05). The article reports satisfaction "was higher in participants attending an administrative districts of nursing schools in other cities and provinces than in metropolitan cities."

> "Post-hoc analysis (Scheffé test) showed that the level of satisfaction with online class was higher in participants attending an administrative districts of nursing schools in other cities and provinces than in metropolitan cities."
