Qin Yongjie, Wang Yungui, Floden Robert E. (2016). The Effect of Problem-Based Learning on Improvement of the Medical Educational Environment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Medical Principles and Practice. https://doi.org/10.1159/000449036

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Sensitivity analysis addressing the moderate heterogeneity: the pooled estimate was recalculated after excluding the heaviest-weight study, the largest-sample study (WMD 9.62), and the smallest-sample study (WMD 9.14), each remaining significant for PBL. The authors conclude the reanalyses "did not yield results different from those in the the primary analysis".

> "After excluding the study in which the weight was largest [26], the pooled effect size was in favor of the PBL group (WMD 10.01, 95% CI 6.44–13.58, p < 0.00001)"
