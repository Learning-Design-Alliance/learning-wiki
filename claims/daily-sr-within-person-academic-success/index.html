The review reports, citing Blume and colleagues 2022, a within-person association between daily SR and perceived daily academic success in 4th and 5th grade students. The review attributes the finding; no effect size is printed.

"For example, Blume and colleagues 2022 found that the daily SR of 4th and 5th grade students was positively associated with their perceived daily academic success."
