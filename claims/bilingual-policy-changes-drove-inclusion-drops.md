# Changes in CPS bilingual education testing policy drove drops in test-score inclusion, especially the fourth-grade inclusion drop in 1999 and the third-grade drop in 1997

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When CPS moved bilingual students' entry into the accountability system from after three years to after four years in the program, the fourth-grade inclusion rate dropped; an earlier-testing allowance caused the 1997 third-grade drop. [→ John Q. Easton 2000](#john-q-easton-2000)

## Evidence

### John Q. Easton 2000

John Q. Easton, Todd Rosenkranz, Anthony S. Bryk, Brian A. Jacob, Stuart Luppescu, Melissa Roderick. (2000). Annual CPS Test Trend Review, 1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Annual+CPS+Test+Trend+Review+1999

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Documentary analysis of CPS testing policy changes 1997-1999 and their effect on inclusion rates (Part 1). The brief states "the inclusion rate for fourth grade dropped" after the 1999 change, and that allowing earlier testing of bilingual students "is responsible for a drop in inclusion among third graders in 1997." No effect size is printed.

> "In 1999, fifth grade became the point where test scores for large numbers of bilingual education students were included (after four years). As a result, the inclusion rate for fourth grade dropped."
