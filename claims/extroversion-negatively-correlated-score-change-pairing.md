## Evidence

### Faherty Aileen 2020

Faherty Aileen, Counihan Tim, Kropmans Thomas, Finn Yvonne. (2020). Inter-rater reliability in clinical assessments: do examiner pairings influence candidate ratings? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02009-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis (Table 5) relating NEO-FFI personality scores of 12 examiners to total change in their marks from solo to paired scoring; only extroversion contributed significantly, with rho = −0.808 and p = 0.001.

> "There was a statistically significant negative correlation (− 0.808) between extroversion and change in examiners score - the higher an examiners’ score for extroversion the lower the degree of change in his or her score when paired up with a co-examiner (p = 0.001)"
