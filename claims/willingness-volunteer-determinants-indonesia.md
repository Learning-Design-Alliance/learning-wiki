`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic analysis (Table 2) on the 4870-participant survey identified independent determinants of willingness to volunteer; the analysis found those from lower-middle families were "24.2% less likely to be willing to volunteer during the pandemic (OR 0.76 [95% CI: 0.59–0.98], p = 0.034)". Prior health and non-health volunteering also independently increased willingness (OR 1.67 and 1.63, both p < 0.001).

> "Male students and students living in Central Indonesia (i.e. Kalimantan and Sulawesi) were 1.14 (95% CI: 1.01–1.23, p = 0.038) and 2.26 (95% CI: 1.46–3.48, p < 0.001) times more likely to be willing to volunteer, while those from lower-middle families were 24.2% less likely to be willing to volunteer during the pandemic (OR 0.76 [95% CI: 0.59–0.98], p = 0.034)"
