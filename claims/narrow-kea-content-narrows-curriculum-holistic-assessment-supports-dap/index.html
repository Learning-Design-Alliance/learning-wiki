Yun 2021¶

Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. (2021). High-quality early childhood assessment: Learning from states' use of kindergarten entry assessments. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/high-quality-kea

q1 · i? · review · r2

A literature-synthesis assertion in the report's introduction, citing prior studies rather than reporting new empirical data. The report states studies 'have linked a focus on a narrow set of academic skills to less time devoted to other areas, such as science, social studies, music, and art,' and that teaching to the test can displace active, play-based learning.

"Yet the reverse is also true: Holistic, observational assessment can drive developmentally appropriate teaching and learning, as we will highlight in district examples later in this report."
