Controlled prospective study on ultrasound simulation training in fetal echocardiography: FESIM II. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-023-07133-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group RAI comparison in the FESIM II study; Table 2 reports physicians at 91.8 ± 13.2% versus students at 95.9 ± 1.1% after 6 weeks and experts at 95.5 ± 7.7%. Only p-values are printed, so no effect size is available.

> "The physician group obtained 92% of the standard planes correctly, the difference to the student group was not significant (p < 0.576). All other tests turned out insignificant as well, indicating that the RAIs in all groups are comparable."
