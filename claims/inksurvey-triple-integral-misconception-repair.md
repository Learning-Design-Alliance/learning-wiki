Comparison of two large Calculus III lecture sections described in the GG vignette: one used InkSurvey to reveal and repair the misconception, the other covered it in lecture without InkSurvey. The quote reports the InkSurvey section's delayed summative result; the comparison section had "12 out of 103 students" make the mistake on the same exam questions.

> "When 87 students were examined in a summative assessment two weeks after the lesson, only one student still displayed this mistake on any of the three exam questions that addressed the concept."
