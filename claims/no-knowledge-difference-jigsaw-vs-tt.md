## Evidence

### Stokstad JM 2026

Stokstad JM, Nedrehagen ES, Hufthammer KO. (2026). The Jigsaw teaching method compared to traditional teaching on anatomy and physiology knowledge in higher education – a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26:252. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08608-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomised controlled trial (n = 47), the longitudinal regression model adjusting for baseline differences estimated between-group differences on the 25-question MCT; the narrow CI "thereby excluding any large differences in the effect of the two teaching methods".

> "No statistically significant differences in knowledge were observed between the groups (post-intervention difference: −0.5 points, 95% CI: −2.3 to 1.2, p = 0.54; three-month difference: −0.6 points, 95% CI: −2.4 to 1.2, p = 0.52)."
