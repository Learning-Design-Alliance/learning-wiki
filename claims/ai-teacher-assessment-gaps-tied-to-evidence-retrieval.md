`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of an experience assessing Experimental Physics laboratory reports with ChatGPT models against instructor rubric assessments. The article reports that "differences between AI-generated and teacher assessments may be related not only to model capabilities but also to difficulties in retrieving and interpreting" report elements. No effect size is printed.

> "The findings indicate that differences between AI-generated and teacher assessments may be related not only to model capabilities but also to difficulties in retrieving and interpreting equations, graphs, tables, units, and other visual elements."
