## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants in the L1 discussion group were the only ones to speculate about possible meanings via rhetorical questions, make frequent autobiographical connections, and question the reliability of the expository text's information. [→ Turnbull 2017](#turnbull-2017)

## Evidence

### Turnbull 2017

Turnbull, B., & Sweetnam Evans, M. (2017). The effects of L1 and L2 group discussions on L2 reading comprehension. Reading in a Foreign Language, 29(1), 133–154. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of written recalls from the three-group study found higher-order processing only in the L1 discussion group, including metacognitive comments and autobiographical and intertextual connections absent from groups 1 and 2.

> "It was only in group 3 that participants speculated in their recalls about possible meanings in the form of rhetorical questions about the texts, indicating a type of comprehension monitoring."
