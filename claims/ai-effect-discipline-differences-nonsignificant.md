`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Discipline moderator analysis across the 35 studies. Science g = 0.676, mathematics g = 0.650, technology/engineering g = 0.501; the article reports the between-group test as "not statistically significant (Q = 4.85, df = 2, p = 0.088)" and urges caution in interpretation.

> "In terms of subject area, the highest effects were observed in science (g = 0.676) and mathematics (g = 0.650). In technology and engineering courses, the effect size was slightly lower (g = 0.501)."
