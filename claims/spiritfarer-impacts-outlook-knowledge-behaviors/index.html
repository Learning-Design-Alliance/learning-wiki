Evidence¶

Noah Glaser 2024¶

Noah Glaser, Lucas Jensen, Tina Riedy, Maggie Center, Jim Shifflett, Joseph Griffin. (2024). Unlocking the Everdoor: analyzing the serious game Spiritfarer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10357-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative grounded theory study using open-ended LED surveys from Spiritfarer players recruited via purposive and snowball sampling; the article reports that "A total of 56 people (n = 56) responded to our call and completed the LED survey." Findings are the authors' thematic interpretation, not a measured effect size.

"Our findings indicate that the serious game Spiritfarer has the potential to impact the outlook, knowledge, and behaviors of gamers; especially in relation to grief, empathy, and managing loss."
