## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The effective score of the general group (177.25) exceeded the interactive group (136.00), while the categorical score of the interactive group (70.25) was higher than the general group (49.25). [→ Li-Ying Liu 2015](#li-ying-liu-2015)

## Evidence

### Li-Ying Liu 2015

Li-Ying Liu, Meng-Tzu Cheng. (2015). Interactive Projector as an Interactive Teaching Tool in the Classroom: Evaluating Teaching Efficiency and Interactivity. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057376

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Coder-rated scores from the video-based interactivity analysis: teaching efficiency rated 0-4 per action multiplied by occurrences (effective score), and category-weighted points (categorical score), averaged across two researchers. Whole-class interactivity was rated 1-10 for reliability verification.

> "The results reveal that effective score of general group (177.25) was better than interactive group (136.00); contrarily, categorical score of interactive group (70.25) was higher than general group (49.25)."
