## Evidence

### Schmitz Sophia M. 2021

Schmitz Sophia M., Schipper Sandra, Lemos Martin, Alizai Patrick H., Kokott Elda, Brozat Jonathan F., Neumann Ulf P., Ulmer Tom F. (2021). Development of a tailor‐made surgical online learning platform, ensuring surgical education in times of the COVID19 pandemic. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01203-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled comparison of 44 completing students (23 control, 21 experimental) stratified by year of study across seven surgical sessions. The experimental group's "significantly higher percentage of correct choices" (0.67 ± 0.02 vs. 0.60 ± 0.02, p = 0.0001) favored video preparation; no effect size is printed.

> "Students in the video group had a significantly higher percentage of correct choices (Experimental group: 0.67 ± 0.02 vs. control: 0.60 ± 0.02; p = 0.0001, see Fig. 2)"
