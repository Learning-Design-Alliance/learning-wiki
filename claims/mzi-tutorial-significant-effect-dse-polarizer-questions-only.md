# Graduate students who worked on the MZI tutorial significantly outperformed those who did not on DSE polarizer questions (p = 0.011, effect size 0.831), with no effect on other DSE questions

## Subclaims

`q3 i?` A repeated measures ANOVA showed a statistically significant effect of engaging with the MZI tutorial on graduate students' performance on the DSE polarizer questions (p = 0.011), and none of the other interactions were significant. [→ Alexandru Maries 2020](#alexandru-maries-2020)

## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2020

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Can students apply the concept of "which-path" information learned in the context of Mach Zehnder Interferometer to the double-slit experiment?. [doi:10.1119/10.0001357](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0001357)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA comparing graduate students who worked on the MZI tutorial (MZI→DSE cohort) with those who did not (DSE only cohort) on DSE polarizer versus other DSE questions; the article reports p = 0.011 for the tutorial effect on polarizer questions.

> "A repeated measures ANOVA was carried out on these data which shows a statistically significant effect of engaging with  the MZI tutorial on their  performance on the polarizer questions on the DS E pretest (p = 0.011)"

### Alexandru Maries 2020 (2)

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Can students apply the concept of "which-path" information learned in the context of Mach Zehnder Interferometer to the double-slit experiment?. [doi:10.1119/10.0001357](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0001357)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same repeated measures ANOVA analysis of the two graduate cohorts' DSE pretest performance; the printed effect size for the polarizer-question comparison "was 0.831, thus suggesting a large effect" (effect size = 0.831).

> "the effect size [68] for comparing the performance on the DSE polarizer questions of graduate students who worked on the MZI tutorial with the performance of those who did not was 0.831, thus suggesting a large effect"

### Alexandru Maries 2017

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Effectiveness of interactive tutorials in promoting “which-path” information reasoning in advanced quantum mechanics. Physical Review Physics Education Research 13, 020115. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.13.020115

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way repeated measures ANOVA comparing two graduate cohorts (MZI→DSE, N=45; DSE→MZI, N=14) on DSE pretest performance. The MZI→DSE cohort averaged 65% vs 38% on polarizer questions (Cohen's d=0.83) but both cohorts averaged 42% on lamp questions (p=0.955, d=0.016).

> "A repeated measures ANOV A carried out on these data showed a statistically significant interaction between working on the MZI QuILT and performance on the polarizer questions ( Fð1; 51Þ¼ 7.034, p ¼ 0.011). The other interaction (working on MZI QuILT and lamp questions) was not significant"
