## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Knowledge items effective up to T3: "1 in 5" lifetime prevalence (91.9% vs 79.3%, p = 0.020) and "half... by their mid-teens" (73.3% vs 41.5%, p < 0.001), plus total correct (p = 0.009). [→ Kageyama Masako 2023](#kageyama-masako-2023)

## Evidence

### Kageyama Masako 2023

Kageyama Masako, Matsushita Atsunori, Kobayashi Ayuna, Sakamoto Taku, Endo Yasuhiro, Sakae Setsuko, Koide Keiko, Saita Ryotaro, Kosaka Hiyuka, Iga Satoko, Yokoyama Keiko. (2023). Video-based e-learning program for schoolteachers to support children of parents with mental illness: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-023-15426-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ten knowledge items in the cluster RCT compared by chi-square/Fisher's exact test and mixed-effect logistic models. Only correct-response percentages and p-values printed, no effect size.

> "those effective up to T3 were “mental illness affects 1 in 5 people in their lifetime” (no. 1) (91.9% and 79.3% for the intervention and control groups respectively (p = 0.020)), “half of those affected by mental illness develop the illness by their mid-teens” (no. 6) (73.3% and 41.5%, respectively (p < 0.001)), and the total number of correct answers (p = 0.009)"
