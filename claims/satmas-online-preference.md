## Evidence

### Kumar PR 2024

Kumar PR, Large J, Konda N, Hashmi Y, Adebayo O, Sivaraman M, Lee JJ. (2024). Student advanced trauma management and skills (SATMAS): a validation study. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-024-02456-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post-course feedback from students who completed all seven sessions, analysed with the Wilcoxon matched pairs signed rank test. The article also reports ease of access (n = 64) and completing in own time (n = 30) as common motivating factors. No effect size printed.

> "After the teaching programme, students reported more preference to teaching via flexible recorded online sessions than face to face (AD 0.78, p < 0.001)."
