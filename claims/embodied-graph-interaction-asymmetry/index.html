Richard Cox & Paul Brna 2015¶

Richard Cox & Paul Brna. (2015). Twenty Years on: Reflections on "Supporting the Use of External Representations in Problem Solving". Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:193–204. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0054-z

The review reports two studies of Garcia Garcia's (2015) touchscreen graphing system for younger students, assessed with a battery of graph interpretation assessments. The review attributes the finding to Garcia Garcia; no effect size is printed.

"The results of two studies (using a battery of graph interpretation assessments) showed that graph interactions (with racetrack be- havior visualizations) produced greater improvements in students’graph interpretation skills than the converse (racing cars and observing automatic graph construction)."
