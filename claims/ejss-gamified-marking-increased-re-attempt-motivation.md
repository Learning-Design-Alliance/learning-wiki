## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors report that retaining recent re-attempts with a gamified marking scheme increased students' motivation to revisit and improve task performance, and that free navigation among questions enhanced the learning experience. [→ Wee 2024](#wee-2024)

## Evidence

### Wee 2024

Wee, L. K., Tan, D., Garcia Clemente, F. J., & Esquembre, F. (2024). Easy JavaScript Simulation (EJSS) Data Analytics for Singapore. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.09676](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.09676)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' observational report (Section 3.3) on the gamified marking scheme where lower marks (e.g. 1 mark orange, 0 marks red) motivate re-attempts that award higher marks (2 marks green). The evidence is the authors' own informal observations; no measures, sample or statistics are printed.

> "We established a system for retaining recent re-attempts and introduced a gamified marking scheme (figure 11). Our observations indicated that this mechanism increased students' motivation to revisit and enhance their performance in these tasks."
