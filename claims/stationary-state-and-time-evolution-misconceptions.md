## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

QMS item analysis of Question 15, in which the infinite square well is expanded suddenly at t = 0 so the old ground state is not a stationary state of the new system. 26% thought the wavefunction would evolve into the new ground state.

> "Only 42% of the students correctly noted that the probability density function evolves in time for all t > 0. The most common misconception was that the old ground state would eventually evolve into a new stationary state."

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2011 (2)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2011). Surveying students' understanding of quantum mechanics in one spatial dimension. [doi:10.1063/1.3515229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515229)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Think-aloud interview finding accompanying the Question 6 item analysis, in which about 70% of students incorrectly thought the superposition state is an energy eigenstate satisfying the time independent Schrödinger equation; only 25% selected the correct answer.

> "Further interviews indicate that many undergraduate and graduate students hold the misconception that the time independent Schrödinger equation is satisfied for any possible wavefunction."
