Yi Tan Yong 2025¶

Yi Tan Yong, Shreyans Panwar, Vallabhajosyula Ranganath. (2025). Learning by making – student-made models and creative projects for medical education: systematic review with qualitative synthesis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06716-8

q2 · i? · review · r3

Qualitative theme from the review's content analysis of included studies. It reports a high task burden, with one study reporting "students spent on average 9.2 h on creating the models."

"Some students reported that creative projects posed a high task burden on top of an already intensive medical curriculum [11, 24, 31], with one study reporting that students spent on average 9.2 h on creating the models [31]."

Yi Tan Yong 2025 (2)¶

Yi Tan Yong, Shreyans Panwar, Vallabhajosyula Ranganath. (2025). Learning by making – student-made models and creative projects for medical education: systematic review with qualitative synthesis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06716-8

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review theme on student disengagement: students in four included studies "perceiving that the interventions were equivalent to conventional teaching," rejecting projects owing to task burden and perceived absence of educational value.

"Some studies also reported students perceiving that the interventions were equivalent to conventional teaching [11, 27, 31, 34], and thus the use of such creative projects for learning would be a choice of preference or even rejected owing to task burden."
