## Subclaims
`q2 i1` LLM-generated argument scores on Quiz 09 were statistically significantly higher for students who answered the MC question correctly (2.77) than incorrectly (2.53), but the impact was small (Cohen's d = 0.22). [→ Winter Allen 2025](#winter-allen-2025)

## Evidence

### Winter Allen 2025

Winter Allen, Anand Shanker, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Students' Perceptions to a Large Language Model's Generated Feedback and Scores of Argumentation Essays. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.allen](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.allen)

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Table I reports Quiz 09 results, where students (N=565) solved a low difficulty problem with an MC average of about 76%. The authors state a very small p-value and large Cohen's d indicate the average scores are statistically significant "but the impact is small" (d = 0.22).
