## Evidence

### Petersen 2016

Petersen, I. T., Hoyniak, C. P., McQuillan, M. E., Bates, J. E., & Staples, A. D. (2016). Measuring the development of inhibitory control: The challenge of heterotypic continuity. Developmental Review, 40, 25-71. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dr.2016.02.001

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Anecdotal observational analysis of the authors' own longitudinal testing on a Bird/Alligator variant of Bear/Dragon; correlations at 36 months (r[133] = −.22) and 42 months (r[127] = .12) differed significantly (Fisher r-to-z, p < .001). The printed r = −.67 indicates a large inverse association at 30 months.

> "children at 30 months of age who inhibited responses on the no-go trials also tended to inhibit on the go trials (i.e., performance on go and no-go trials was inversely correlated: r[137] = −.67)."
