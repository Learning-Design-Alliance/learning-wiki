## Evidence

### Victoria Borish and H. J. Lewandowski 2025

Victoria Borish and H. J. Lewandowski. (2025). Affordances and Challenges of Incorporating a Remote, Cloud-accessible Quantum Experiment into Undergraduate Courses. arXiv:2502.00613. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of survey responses from 29 instructors plus an interview with one instructor who had used Oqtant, presented in Table II. Access is described as a challenge instructors "have very little control over"; instructors also cited cognitive overload from learning new apparatus and concerns that remote work displaces hands-on experience.

> "One of the big challenges that instructors have very little control over is access to Oqtant."

### Victoria Borish and H. J. Lewandowski 2025 (2)

Victoria Borish and H. J. Lewandowski. (2025). Affordances and Challenges of Incorporating a Remote, Cloud-accessible Quantum Experiment into Undergraduate Courses. arXiv:2502.00613. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010133)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview with the single course-implementing instructor at a Hispanic-serving PUI after the course ended. The instructor attributed the difficulty to the topic not being the center of their research and to uncertainty about what was important to cover, making rubric development a challenge.

> "[Grading/evaluating the activities] was the hardest thing. By far."
