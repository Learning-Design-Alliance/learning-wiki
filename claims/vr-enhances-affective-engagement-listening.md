## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, Y., & Hu, G. (2026). Investigating L2 listening comprehension and experience in immersive and interactive virtual reality: An experimental study. Computers & Education, 248, 105593. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105593

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression on RCT data (N = 84) found VR mode predicted affective state flow. The article states VR "significantly enhanced affective engagement during the listening task" with β = .45.

> "In Model 3, listening mode significantly predicted the affective component of state flow (B = 3.33, β = .45, p < .001), uniquely explaining 23% of its variance (r2 partial = .23), indicating that VR significantly enhanced affective engagement during the listening task."
