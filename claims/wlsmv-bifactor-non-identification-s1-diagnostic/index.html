Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

q3 · i? · associational · r1

WLSMV sensitivity analysis on the polychoric correlation matrix (N = 401). The article reports latent correlations up to .95 (AI–effectiveness = .95; effectiveness–engagement = .95) and that the S-1 model omitting effectiveness converged with good fit [χ2(55) = 193.4; CFI = .992].

"The bifactor model could not be identified under WLSMV (its information matrix was non-invertible); this is consistent with specific factors carrying too little variance to be reliably estimated"
