## Evidence

### Starch and & Elliott 1911

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1911). Reliability of the Grading of High-School Work in English. The School Review. http://www.journals.uchicago.edu/t-and-c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Marks from 86 Wisconsin students (median 92.4 on paper A, 84.5 on paper B, assumed passing grade 70) compared with teachers' medians of 87.2 and 78.8. The article states "the students graded more leniently than the teachers", though the range of variation was approximately the same.

> "The students thus graded the first paper 5.2 points, and the second paper 5.7 points, higher than the teachers."
