`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multinomial logistic regression (Table 6) in the 1371-student national survey. The article reports "a preference for asynchronous teaching (adjusted OR = 1.55, 95% CI = 1.08–2.24)" and similar adjusted OR for interaction avoidance; both intervals barely exclude 1.

> "the odds of improving the accumulative GPA was associated with a preference for asynchronous teaching (adjusted OR = 1.55, 95% CI = 1.08–2.24) and avoidance of engagement in online interaction (adjusted OR = 1.55, 95% CI = 1.06–2.17)"
