### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2011

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Edward F. Redish, and Jessica Watkins. (2011). Students' Views of Macroscopic and Microscopic Energy in Physics and Biology. PERC 2011. [doi:10.1063/1.3680024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680024)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the two-interview case study: Dennis integrated energy across scales and disciplines only when context facilitated it, and "otherwise expresses little use for this integration." This is an interpretive conclusion, not a tested comparison. No effect size is reported.
