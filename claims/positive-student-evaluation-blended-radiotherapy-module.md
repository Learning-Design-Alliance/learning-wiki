## Evidence

### Knöchelmann AC 2025

Knöchelmann AC, Becker J-N, Stiller G, Steinmann D, Behrends M. (2025). Digital redesign of the radiotherapy course for medical students with a blended learning approach. Strahlentherapie Und Onkologie. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00066-024-02348-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive evaluation survey of the new module: 46 students participated, 43 evaluations analyzed; six-point items with scales 1–3 clustered as positive. For example, structure was rated positively by 90.7% and side effects content by 95.3%; "The median score for all the questions was 2."

> "All aspects related to the design of the digital offer, the opportunity to acquire specialist skills, the seminar, and the internship were rated positively by most students. The median score for all the questions was 2."

### Knöchelmann AC 2025 (2)

Knöchelmann AC, Becker J-N, Stiller G, Steinmann D, Behrends M. (2025). Digital redesign of the radiotherapy course for medical students with a blended learning approach. Strahlentherapie Und Onkologie. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00066-024-02348-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same anonymous voluntary evaluation (43 analyzed): 37 respondents (86%) rated effort-to-knowledge ratio balanced, median 2 (SD 1.1); overall module grade averaged 1.8 (SD 0.7). Only descriptive statistics are reported; no inferential tests.

> "The ratio of learning effort to increase in knowledge and skills through the digital learning offer was rated as balanced by 37 of the respondents (86%), with a median of 2 (standard deviation [SD] 1.1). The overall assessment score of the module by the students was 1.8 on average (SD 0.7)."
