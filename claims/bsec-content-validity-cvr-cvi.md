## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Expert content validity judgments using Lawshe's CVR (threshold ≥.62) and CVI (threshold ≥.80) retained 14 of 44 items, yielding scale-level values of .89 and .92. [→ Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021](#cardenas-gutierrez-2021)

## Evidence

### Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021

Cárdenas-Gutiérrez, A. R., Bernal-Guerrero, A., & Montoro-Fernández, E. (2021). Construction and validation of the Basic Scale of Entrepreneurial Competencies for Secondary Education level. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249903

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validity phase in which panels of ten specialists, two per area (business education, firm organizational and legal management, marketing, economics, instrument assessment), rated items on 3-point and 4-point Likert scales; the scale's overall "CVR of the scale was.89 and the score of the CVI was.92".

> "In the CVR, and basing ourselves on the criterion of Lawshe, 23 items which obtained scores lower than.62 were eliminated. In the CVI analysis 7 items were excluded as they did not reach values ≥.80. A total of 14 items were thus left."
