Secondary qualitative finding from the gateway math study of faculty and student experiences. The report states that "limited resources and training for faculty often lead to procedural instruction" despite valuing real-world examples.

"Students and faculty recognize the importance of real-world examples in gateway math, but limited resources and training for faculty often lead to procedural instruction instead of instruction focused on practical applications."
