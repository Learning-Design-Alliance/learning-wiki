# Design principle effects are moderated by pacing and element interactivity, but not by learner prior knowledge

## Evidence

### Noetel 2022

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Harris, N. R., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2022). Multimedia design for learning: An overview of reviews with meta-meta-analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211052329

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderation analysis across 22 pooled effects found pacing significantly moderated design-principle effects (R2 = 0.34, p = 0.02), with larger effects in system-paced media such as lectures (g = 0.41) than learner-paced media such as websites (g = 0.27).

> "The pacing of the materials was a significant moderator of effects (NPooledEffects = 22, R22 = 0.34, p = 0.02). Design principles were more important when the multimedia is system-paced, like in a lecture (g = 0.41, 95% CI [0.33, 0.49]), than when learner-paced, like on a website (g = 0.27, 95% CI [0.19, 0.35])"

### Noetel 2022 (2)

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Harris, N. R., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2022). Multimedia design for learning: An overview of reviews with meta-meta-analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211052329

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderation analysis across 4 pooled effects found element interactivity (material complexity) significantly moderated effects (R2 = 1.00, p < 0.001): effects were larger in complex media (g = 0.70) than simple media (g = 0.20).

> "The element interactivity moderated the effects of design principles (NPooledEffects = 4, R22 = 1.00, p < 0.001). Multimedia design principles were more important in complex media (g = 0.70, 95% CI [0.59, 0.81]) than more simple media (g = 0.20, 95% CI [0.02, 0.39])"

### Noetel 2022 (3)

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Harris, N. R., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2022). Multimedia design for learning: An overview of reviews with meta-meta-analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211052329

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Across 30 pooled effects, learner prior knowledge did not significantly moderate design-principle effects (R2 = 0.14, p = 0.14); no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested. Education level, presentation format, and subject domain also did not consistently moderate results.

> "The prior knowledge of the learner did not moderate effects (NPooledEffects = 30, R22 = 0.14, p = 0.14)"
