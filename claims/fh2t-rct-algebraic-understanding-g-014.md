### From Here to There (FH2T): Program Description and Evaluation Evidence 2023

From Here to There (FH2T): Program Description and Evaluation Evidence. (2023). https://www.evidenceforlearning.org.au/the-toolkit/from-here-to-there/

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r?`

Randomized controlled trial of 1,119 Grade 7 students in 10 middle schools in a large suburban district; students randomly assigned within classrooms to FH2T (n = 753) or an active comparison with online algebra problem sets and delayed feedback (n = 366). Outcome was a 10-item multiple-choice assessment; the article reports "Hedges' g = 0.14" favoring FH2T, a small effect.

> "After adjusting for students' pretest scores and background characteristics, students who used FH2T scored significantly higher than students in the control group (Hedges' g = 0.14), earning a Strong evidence rating."
