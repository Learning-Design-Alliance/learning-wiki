# Interviews indicate individualized AI-generated identity-based relevance was perceived as overly abstract, while collective AI dialogue connected math to current concerns and cross-domain applications

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the individual condition, 7 of 10 interviewed students described AI-generated connections between math and their future job or interests as overly abstract and possibly not useful. [→ Guo 2025](#guo-2025)
`q2 i?` In the collective condition, 8 of 10 interviewees mentioned AI connecting math to their current concerns, and 7 of 10 mentioned broadened perceptions of math's utility across daily life and fields. [→ Guo 2025 (2)](#guo-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Guo 2025

Guo, Z., Fryer, L. K., & Shum, A. (2025). Utilizing generative AI to promote high school students' personal relevance to math and interest in the math class: An intervention. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/srxga_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/srxga_v1)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Inductive thematic analysis of stimulated recall interviews with 20 purposively sampled intervention students (10 per condition), coded in NVivo 14 with κ = 0.82 agreement. The article reports students found identity-based connections "overly abstract and may not be useful".

> "some students (7/10) described the AI-generated connections between math and their future job/individual interest as overly abstract and may not be useful (Theme 1: Lack of Utility of the Relevance)"

### Guo 2025 (2)

Guo, Z., Fryer, L. K., & Shum, A. (2025). Utilizing generative AI to promote high school students' personal relevance to math and interest in the math class: An intervention. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/srxga_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/srxga_v1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Thematic analysis of the same 20 follow-up interviews; the article also reports that "some participants (7/10) mentioned that the generative AI's response broadened their perceptions about the utility of the math in daily life and in different jobs (Theme 3: Cognitive Broadening)".

> "some participants (8/10) mentioned the generative AI's ability to connect math to their current concerns (Theme 2: Immediate Practical Utility)"
