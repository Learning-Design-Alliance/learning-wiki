## Evidence

### Austen Thomas 2019

Austen Thomas, Otter Simon. (2019). The podiatric surgery theatre environment in the UK; is it conducive to learning? A quantitative study using the surgical theatre educational environment measure (STEEM). Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-018-0312-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive per-item analysis in the podiatric surgery STEEM survey (N = 16); the article reports the lowest rated statements and their medians of 2, which the authors attribute to the low Learning Opportunities subscale score of 70.81%.

> "The lowest rated statements were no. 11 "My trainer expects my surgical skills to be as good as his/hers". No. 20 "The number of emergency procedures is sufficient for me to gain the right operative experience", no.21 "The variety of emergency cases gives me the appropriate exposure" and no. 22 "My trainer is in too much of a rush during emergency cases to let me operate". The medians were 2, and IQR were 1–3, 2–2.5, 2–3 and 2–3 respectively."
