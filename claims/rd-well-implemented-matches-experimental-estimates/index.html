q2 · i? · causal · r2

Synthetic within-study comparison generating RD datasets from two experimental evaluations of educational interventions (Educational Technology Study and Teach for America Study) and comparing RD impact estimates with the experimental estimates of the same interventions. The article reports estimates that are "not significantly different from one another" with similar average magnitude; no effect size is printed.

"RD and experimental estimators produce impact estimates that are not significantly different from one another and have a similar magnitude, on average."
