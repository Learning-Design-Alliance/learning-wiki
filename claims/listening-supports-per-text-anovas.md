## Evidence

### Rouhi 2014

Rouhi, A., Nabavi, S.-M., & Mohebbi, H. (2014). The effects of previewing questions, repetition of input, and topic preparation on listening comprehension of Iranian EFL learners. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 2(2), 73-85. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary per-text analyses in the same four-group experiment: each of the three PET-based listening texts was analyzed separately, with significant group differences on all three. No effect sizes printed.

> "Three one -way ANOVAs run on the data obtained from Text One, Text Two, and Text Three revealed that there were statistically significant differences among the participating groups in the three texts given to the participants ( F = 8.84, p < .01, F = 4.29, p < .01,F = 15.03, p< .01, respectively)."
