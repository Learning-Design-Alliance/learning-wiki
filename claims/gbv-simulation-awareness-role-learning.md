## Evidence

### Jiménez-Rodríguez D. 2020

Jiménez-Rodríguez D., Belmonte García M.T., Santillán García A., Plaza del Pino F.J., Ponce-Valencia A., Arrogante O. (2020). Nurse Training in Gender-Based Violence Using Simulated Nursing Video Consultations during the COVID-19 Pandemic: A Qualitative Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17228654

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 48 semi-structured telephone interviews with third-year nursing students after simulated video consultations; the learning-acquisition category included awareness of the problem, with students noting "it is more common than what we think, and we have to be prepared and know how to act" (S2).

> "The students recognized that working with GBV in the clinical simulation had served them to become aware of the importance and complexity of this social problem."
