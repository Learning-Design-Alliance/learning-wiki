### Mostafavi Marzieh 2025

Mostafavi Marzieh, Shokrpour Nasrin, Afraz Shahram. (2025). Beliefs, practices, and awareness of written corrective feedback among EFL teachers: a mixed-methods study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03861-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests over the 1500-essay corpus (N of Valid Cases 1500 per Tables 1-2) tested student gender against feedback strategy. Both tests were non-significant; the article prints no effect size, so equivalence was not tested, only a failure to detect a difference.

> "the relationship between student gender and the use of direct/indirect feedback was not significant (Continuity Correction p =.411). Similarly, Table 2 shows no significant relationship between student gender and comprehensive/selective feedback (Continuity Correction p =.605)"
