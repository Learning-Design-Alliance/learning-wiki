`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Narrative analysis of critical event 3 (2007–2008) in the IMPDET program. The authors report the program responded by decreasing online courses, increasing individually completed course work, and integrating the application process into the host university's general admission. No effect sizes are reported.

> "The two events caused a genuine threat to the continuation of the program, since both the financial and human resources available for running the studies decreased considerably."
