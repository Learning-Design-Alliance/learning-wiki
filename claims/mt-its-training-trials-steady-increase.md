### Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski 2023

Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski. (2023). Moving-Target Intelligent Tutoring System for Marksmanship Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00308-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Trial-level training data (128 trials per Standard participant, 64 per MT-ITS participant, cued trials excluded) were fit per participant and target type to a linear function using SPSS' CURVEFIT. Table 6 slope values show diverse individual trends but predominantly positive significant slopes for MT-ITS.

> "Nevertheless, most of the training curves in the MT-ITS condition show significant increasing function while most of the curves in the Standard condition show flat curves."
