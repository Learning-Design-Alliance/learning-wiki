`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Wyse and Soneral (2018), a survey of biology students at a small liberal arts college; introductory students emphasized "workload as a major factor" while upper-level students emphasized "high cognitive demand". The review does not describe the study's design beyond the survey.

> "When surveyed, introductory biology students at a small liberal arts collage described workload as a major factor for a course being “rigorous”, whereas students taking upper level classes at the same institution characterized rigor as high cognitive demand (Wyse & Soneral, 2018)."
