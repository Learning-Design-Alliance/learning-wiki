# Self-rated total research self-efficacy scores rose significantly from pretest to posttest after an FC-TBL-based literature seminar course, in one single-group quasi-experimental study of graduate nursing students

`q2 i?` Total research self-efficacy scores increased significantly from pretest to posttest (p < 0.001) in the same single-group quasi-experiment. [→ Qi Hong 2026](#qi-hong-2026)

## Evidence

### Qi Hong 2026

Qi Hong, Ban Jingjing, Wang Nana, Guo Huimin, Yang Yang, Tang Shan. (2026). Development and evaluation of a literature seminar course based on flipped classroom and team-based learning in the graduate nursing curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08823-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests in the same 124-student single-group study showed total self-efficacy rose from 2.63 ± 0.33 to 3.48 ± 0.54 (t = -34.605, p < 0.001). The article reports no effect size for this change.

> "The total score and all five dimensions of research self-efficacy were significantly higher after the course than before the intervention (p < 0.001), indicating that the teaching intervention effectively enhanced students' research confidence and motivation."
