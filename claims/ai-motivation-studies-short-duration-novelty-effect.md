### Jiajing Li 2026

Jiajing Li, Ronnel B. King, Ching Sing Chai, Vivian WY Lee & Ruyi Li. (2026). AI and student motivation: A scoping review. Journal of Research on Technology in Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2672515

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's charting of study durations across the 156 included studies, ranging from 80 minutes to 11 months. It reports 61.5% lasted under 10 weeks and flags possible novelty effects.

> "Notably, as most studies lasted less than 10 weeks ( n = 96; 61.5%), their findings may have been clouded by a novelty effect."

### Jiajing Li 2026 (2)

Jiajing Li, Ronnel B. King, Ching Sing Chai, Vivian WY Lee & Ruyi Li. (2026). AI and student motivation: A scoping review. Journal of Research on Technology in Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2672515

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's analysis of statistical approaches used in the included studies. It reports that no study employed longitudinal techniques to reveal long-term effects of AI on motivation.

> "statistical techniques more suitable for longitudinal work, such as cross-lagged structural equation models, latent growth modeling, and latent transition analysis, were not employed in any of the studies to reveal the long-term longitudinal effects of AI on motivation."
