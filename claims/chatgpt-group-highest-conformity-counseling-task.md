Descriptive group-task scoring of written responses from 13 courses of second-year medical students (359 total), each group using one assigned source. The article reports "the ChatGPT group achieved the highest score (107 points)" and the paper group 60 points; no inferential tests were performed.

> "With regard to conformity with the sample solution, the ChatGPT group achieved the highest score (107 points), followed by the video group (100 points), the Google group (97 points), and the paper group (60 points)."
