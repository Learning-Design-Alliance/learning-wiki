### Mahaffie 2022

Mahaffie, S., Malone, M., Usher, A., Mukherjee, D., & Nagaoka, J. (2022). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2021. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort analysis of all CPS high school students, including charter and Options schools, tracking four-year graduation of the 2017–18 ninth-grade cohort (N=26,116). The report notes 2020–21 was the year of mostly remote instruction and that the rate increased from 69.7% in 2011 to 81.8% in 2021.

> "The high school graduation rate of CPS students fell slightly in 2021 for the first time in more than a decade, from 83.3% in 2020 to 81.8% in 2021."
