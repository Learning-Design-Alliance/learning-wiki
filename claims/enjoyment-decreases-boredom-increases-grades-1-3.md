## Evidence

### Rawlings 2023

Rawlings, A. M., Niemivirta, M., Korhonen, J., Lindskog, M., Tuominen, H., & Mononen, R. (2023). Achievement emotions and arithmetic fluency – Development and parallel processes during the early school years. Preprint Version 3, Accepted for publication, 27 March 2023. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101776

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate latent growth curve models fitted to four waves of AEQ-ES emotion data from 232 Norwegian students in grades 1–3. The article reports "a significant overall decrease (M = -.15, p < .01) of mathematics enjoyment" and an increase in boredom; no effect size is printed.

> "The results of the univariate LGCM (see, Table 2) revealed a significant overall decrease (M = -.15, p < .01) of mathematics enjoyment and increase (M = .07, p = .01) in mathematics boredom"
