## Evidence

### Anjali Singh 2024

Anjali Singh, Christopher Brooks, Xu Wang, Warren Li, Juho Kim, Deepti Wilson. (2024). Bridging Learnersourcing and AI: Exploring the Dynamics of Student-AI Collaborative Feedback Generation. arXiv. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1145/3636555.3636853](https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636853)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A generalized linear mixed effects model with binomial family distribution and logit link was used to analyze binary outcome variables. The article reports that "students wrote significantly more fully accurate hints in reflection-1 compared to reflection-2" for both conditions, confirmed by post-hoc pairwise comparisons with Tukey's adjustment.

> "The effect of reflection number on the log-odds of Full Accuracy was statistically significant ( ˆ𝛽 = -1.79, SE = 0.54, p < 0.01). Post-hoc pairwise comparisons with Tukey's adjustment confirmed that students wrote significantly more fully accurate hints in reflection-1 compared to reflection-2"
