## Evidence

### Doorn Neelke N. 2011

Doorn Neelke N., Kroesen J. Otto. (2011). Using and Developing Role Plays in Teaching Aimed at Preparing for Social Responsibility. Science and Engineering Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11948-011-9335-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative, exploratory evaluation of the newly developed pig transport role play, enacted a few times since 2010, based on oral and written student evaluations and tutor evaluations. Observers' value listings surprised students; when confronted with "the value-ladenness of their own argumentation, they were surprised by it".

> "If the observers report by listing the range of implicit appeals to values they noted down during the role game, student often respond with exclamations like “I did not expect the list to be that long.”"
