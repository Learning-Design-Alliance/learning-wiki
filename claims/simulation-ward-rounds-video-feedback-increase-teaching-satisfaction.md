## Evidence

### Zhan Leilei 2026

Zhan Leilei, Liang Ruiyin, Li Yimin, Huang Nuanqin. (2026). Research on the application of scenario-based simulation ward rounds combined with video feedback method in standardized residency training of gastroenterology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09434-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A self-constructed 25-item teaching satisfaction questionnaire (5-point Likert scale) was administered on-site at the conclusion of teaching. The intervention group "scored higher in terms of organization and management, teaching effect, and teaching content"; the total score was 96.70 ± 4.64 vs. 90.88 ± 3.58 (P < 0.001).

> "the intervention group scored higher in terms of organization and management, teaching effect, and teaching content, and all differences reached a statistically significant level (P < 0.05)"
