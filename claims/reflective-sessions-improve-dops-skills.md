## Evidence

### Hassani Nia Nasser 2025

Hassani Nia Nasser, Karami Sahar, Shahraki Farimah, Kohan Mahmoud. (2025). Structured reflective sessions enhance knowledge and clinical skills in operating room technology students: a Solomon four-group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08431-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the Solomon four-group study, DOPS checklists assessed five core procedural skills per semester; the intervention group's skill-specific effect sizes ranged "Cohen's d = 1.48–1.85". Table 1 reports DOPS total 88.0 vs 53.0, t=20.6, p<0.001, d=1.72.

> "Students receiving reflective sessions demonstrated dramatic improvement in all core procedural skills (Cohen's d = 1.48–1.85), regardless of pre-test."
