## Evidence

### Faulkner-Bond 2026

Faulkner-Bond, M. (2026). Rethinking English Learner reclassification in California: What research says about the state's criteria. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resource/finding-the-sweet-spot-for-english-learner-reclassification/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A WestEd and California Department of Education study compared students' overall performance level on the ELPAC against their performance on the SBAC English language arts test in grades 3 through 8 and 11, drawing on data from more than 3 million students. The study found "scoring at PL 4 on the ELPAC remains the most robust indicator." No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Across multiple statistical methods and all grade levels, scoring at PL 4 on the ELPAC remains the most robust indicator that language is no longer the primary driver of academic achievement differences."
