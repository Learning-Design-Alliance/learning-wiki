q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental analysis within a larger multi-site evaluation, matching 177 tutored students with 314 comparison students in Stamford Public Schools grades 6-10. The article reports "significantly greater gains in mathematics achievement (effect size +0.10)"; no standardised effect-size category beyond the printed +0.10 is given.

"Students who received tutoring demonstrated significantly greater gains in mathematics achievement (effect size +0.10), qualifying Go Tutor for a Promising evidence rating."
