# The portfolio-principles class produced more high-level explanations and deeper explanations than the other two Knowledge Forum classes

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Eddy Y.C. Lee 2006

Eddy Y.C. Lee, Carol K.K. Chan, & Jan van Aalst. (2006). Students assessing their own collaborative knowledge building. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-006-8997-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Student responses coded on a 7-point depth-of-explanation scale (inter-rater reliability .83) were compared across the three Knowledge Forum classes. High-level explanations differed with η² = .17, a large effect by the η² threshold; weighted depth of explanation also differed, F (2, 107) = 6.2, p < .01, η² = .10, favoring the portfolio-principles class.

> "Multivariate analyses and Univariate analyses showed significant differences across classes, F (2, 107) = 10.66, p < .001, )2 = .17; paired-comparisons showed that the Knowledge Forum class with portfolio and principles had significantly higher mean scores than the other two classes."
