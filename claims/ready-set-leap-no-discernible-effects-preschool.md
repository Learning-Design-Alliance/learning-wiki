# Ready, Set, Leap!® shows no discernible effects on oral language, print knowledge, phonological processing, early reading/writing, or math in preschoolers

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Across two randomized controlled trials, the WWC rated Ready, Set, Leap!® as having no discernible effects in all five assessed outcome domains. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2008](#what-works-clearinghouse-2008)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Ready, Set, Leap!®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/430

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two randomized controlled trials (RMC Research Corporation, 2003; PCER Consortium, 2008) covering "540 preschool children enrolled in 38 preschools in New Jersey." All domain ratings were "No discernible effects."

> "Based on these two studies, the WWC considers the extent of evidence for Ready, Set, Leap!® to be medium to large for oral language, print knowledge, and phonological processing, and small for early reading/writing and math."
