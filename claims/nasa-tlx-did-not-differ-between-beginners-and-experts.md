## Evidence

### Hinzmann Dominik 2022

Hinzmann Dominik, Singer Maximilian, Schmelter Valerie, Kreiser Kornelia, Gehling Kim, Ströber Lea, Kirschke Jan S, Schulz Christian M and Schneider Frederick. (2022). Differences in beginner and expert neurointerventionalists” heart rate variability during simulated neuroangiographies. Interventional Neuroradiology. https://doi.org/10.1177/15910199221128439

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

NASA-TLX administered after each simulated case; medians [IQR] for mental demand were 11.5 [6.75–16] in beginners vs 9 [5.5–13.5] in experts (p = 0.198, BH-p = 0.338). The study did not detect a difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "Perceived workload in terms of mental, physical and temporal demand values gathered from the NASA-tlx was not different between the groups."
