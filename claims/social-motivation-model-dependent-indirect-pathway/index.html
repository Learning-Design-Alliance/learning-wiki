Hou Jie 2026¶

Hou Jie, Huang Yanhong, Wang Qing, Yao Xiaoxue, Zhang Peizheng, Zhao Fengqing. (2026). AI Dependence and Self-Reported Creativity Among Chinese College Students: The Roles of Motivation for AI Use and Perceived AI Literacy. Behavioral Sciences, 16(9), 1712. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091712

q2 · i? · associational · r2

PROCESS Model 8 moderated mediation in the 5764-participant survey; the social-motivation pathway "was positive and significant at low, mean, and high levels" of perceived AI literacy, with significant pairwise contrasts.

"In the moderated mediation model, however, the conditional indirect association through social motivation was positive and significant at low, mean, and high levels of perceived AI literacy, although its magnitude decreased as perceived AI literacy increased."

Related Claims¶

  • AI dependence is negatively associated with self-reported creativity among Chinese college students, with a residual direct effect after motivational mediators are controlled — related
  • Perceived AI literacy moderates the first-stage paths from AI dependence to escape, social, and entertainment motivation, but not to instrumental motivation — related
  • Entertainment motivation carries a positive indirect association between AI dependence and self-reported creativity, weakening as perceived AI literacy increases — related
  • Escape motivation carries a significant negative indirect association between AI dependence and self-reported creativity — related
  • Instrumental motivation shows a small negative indirect association in the moderated model, contrary to the hypothesized positive pathway, with sign reversal across model specifications — related
  • Perceived AI literacy does not significantly moderate the direct association between AI dependence and self-reported creativity — related
