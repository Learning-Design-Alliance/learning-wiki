## Evidence

### Baliga Kaushik 2022

Baliga Kaushik, Coggins Andrew, Warburton Sandra, Mathias Divya, Yamada Nicole K., Fuerch Janene H., Halamek Louis P. (2022). Pilot study of the DART tool - an objective healthcare simulation debriefing assessment instrument. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03697-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the pilot rating data across Videos A, B and the transcript. They attribute the higher IS variance to a lumper/splitter phenomenon in which raters count long multi-sentence contributions as one or several statements.

> "we observed a lower mean variance for IQ in comparison to IS or TR (Tables 1, 2 and 3). The lower variance in identification of questions (IQ) indicates that debriefing raters are readily able to recognize questions compared to statements"
