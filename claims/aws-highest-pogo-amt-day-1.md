# With the AMT manikin, the AirwayScope gave sufficient vocal-cord visualization from day 1, and both video-laryngoscopes produced significantly higher average POGO scores than the Macintosh laryngoscope

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the AMT manikin, POGO scores were highest in the AWS group (about 78%-79% all days), and 3-day averages were significantly higher in the McG and AWS groups than in the Mac group (P < .0001). [→ Murakami Y 2021](#murakami-y-2021)

## Evidence

### Murakami Y 2021

Murakami Y, Ueki R, Niki M, Hirose M, Shimode N. (2021). Three-day tracheal intubation manikin training for novice doctors using Macintosh laryngoscope, McGRATH MAC videolaryngoscope and Pentax AirwayScope. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023886

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second manikin (AMT) arm of the same 15-participant 3-day training study. Mean POGO was 25%-35% (Mac), 55%-69% (McG, rising over days), and 78%-79% (AWS); averages "were significantly higher in the McG and AWS groups as compared to those in the Mac group" (both P < .0001).

> "POGO scores were highest in the AWS group, with high average scores of the 3 days, which indicates sufficient visualization of the vocal cord from day 1."
