## Evidence

### Xieling Chen 2025

Xieling Chen, Xinyue Li, Di Zou, Haoran Xie, & Fu Lee Wang. (2025). Metacognition research in education: topic modeling and bibliometrics. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10451-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Social network analysis via Gephi visualizing regional collaborations with a minimum frequency of 5 (Fig. 7) and institutional collaborations at frequencies 4–7 (Figs. 8–9). The USA and Canada were the most tightly-knit collaborators, jointly participating in 34 articles; University of Amsterdam and Leiden University jointly engaged in 7 articles.

> "From the results, it is evidenced that counites/regions located in the same continent tend to show close collaborations, for example, (1) Taiwan and China, (2) the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, and Switzerland, and (3) the UK and the Netherlands."
