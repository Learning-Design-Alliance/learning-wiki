# Strategic instruction in four learning strategies produced no significantly different reading comprehension gains than typical adult education instruction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled trial in adult basic education, pre- to post-test gains on two standardized reading comprehension assessments did not differ significantly between experimental (learning strategies) and control classes for any of the four strategies. [→ Hock Michael F. 2011](#hock-michael-f-2011)

## Evidence

### Hock Michael F. 2011

Hock Michael F., Mellard Daryl F. (2011). Efficacy of learning strategies instruction in adult basic education. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 4(2), 134-153. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2011.555291

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with random block assignment across 39 experimental and 38 control classes in Midwestern adult education programs; 100 experimental and 105 control completers analyzed. T-tests on WRMT-R passage comprehension and GORT-4 comprehension gains showed "no significant differences" for all four strategies (p values .101-.965).

> "T-tests showed no significant differences between experimental and control classes pre- to post-test gains by learning strategy for both reading comprehension assessments (Table 3)."
