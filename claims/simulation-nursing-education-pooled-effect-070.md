## Evidence

### Kim Junghee 2016

Kim Junghee, Park Jin-Hwa, Shin Sujin. (2016). Effectiveness of simulation-based nursing education depending on fidelity: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0672-7

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 40 experimental or quasi-experimental studies of simulation-based nursing education, analyzed with a random effects model in Comprehensive Meta-Analysis version 2 after significant heterogeneity (Q = 253.22, P < .001). The pooled effect size was ES = 0.70, "with 95 % confidence intervals of 0.58–0.83"; the funnel plot appeared symmetrical, suggesting minimal publication bias.

> "The overall effect size for the random effects model was 0.70, with 95 % confidence intervals of 0.58–0.83 (Table 3) (Fig. 2)."
