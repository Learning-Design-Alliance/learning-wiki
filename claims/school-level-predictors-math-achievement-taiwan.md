`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Level-2 analysis (Model 5), a means-as-outcomes two-level model with school predictors entered for the intercept only. All three school variables were "signiﬁcant predictors between school differences in math achievement" with the printed coefficients.

> "The results of the Model 5 show that along with the student-level predictors, stueco (γ = 19.41,SE = 6.54,p = 0.004), schlo (γ = 23.41,SE = 9.31,p = 0.013), and schrc (γ = 0.2,SE = 0.08,p = 0.016) are signiﬁcant predictors between school differences in math achievement (Table 9)."
