## Evidence

### Donati 2021

Donati, G., Dumontheil, I., Pain, O., Asbury, K., & Meaburn, E. L. (2021). Evidence for specificity of polygenic contributions to attainment in English, maths and science during adolescence. Scientific Reports, 11, 3851. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-82877-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Genome-wide association analysis of science SAT scores (ages 11 and 14) in the ALSPAC cohort using 6,319,684 SNPs. The article reports "one genome-wide significant SNP association for attainment in science (rs9529641, p = 4.86 × 10–8)"; no significant SNP was found for English or maths.

> "Across the three GWAS we identified one genome-wide significant SNP association for attainment in science (rs9529641, p = 4.86 × 10–8)"
