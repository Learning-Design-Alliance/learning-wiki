Process-evaluation findings from teacher interviews and focus groups on human and technical challenges. Teachers reported limited parental involvement in one school, lack of planning time, and the need for pupil independence for the approach to work; schools responded with homework clubs and downloadable activities.

> "This lack of invo lvement resulted in a small percentage of students (estimated at 10% to 15%) not doing the tasks at home. This proportion is less than the 25% of disengaged pupils reported by Loch and Borland (2014)."
