This is the editor's forward-looking prediction (type e), offered with no evidence or argument beyond the throughput advantage already described. It anticipates routine intelligence testing and subsequent "radical adjustment of school work to meet the needs of pupils."

> "We may confidently expect, therefore, that the National Research Council tests will make intelligence testing a routine measure in the schools, and this will be the entering wedge for other studies of pupils' abilities and attainments, and for a radical adjustment of school work to meet the needs of pupils."
