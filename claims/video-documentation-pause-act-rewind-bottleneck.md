Study 2 structured debriefs with seven first-time builders. The article reports the six-of-seven count and wiring difficulties ranging from pin identification to fitting fingers in a tiny space; no effect size.

> "Whilst six of seven participants identified video guidance as the most helpful feature, they concurrently cited wiring as the primary difficulty."

### Farnaz Baksh 2026 (2)

Farnaz Baksh, Matevž B. Zorec, Feiazie Baksh, and Karl Kruusamäe. (2026). Co-Creating Buildable and Open Social Robot Study Companions with University Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15239

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Study 2 nine-item documentation clarity Likert results, with Orientation and Navigation scoring lowest per Fig. 5b. The article names the structural flaw in the video-only format; descriptive cell means only.

> "These subscales capture “workflow interruption” scenarios where a user pauses the video to act and must then re-establish their position, which points to a structural flaw in the video-only format: “a frustrating pause-act-rewind” cycle."

### Farnaz Baksh 2026 (3)

Farnaz Baksh, Matevž B. Zorec, Feiazie Baksh, and Karl Kruusamäe. (2026). Co-Creating Buildable and Open Social Robot Study Companions with University Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15239

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 1 documentation clarity Likert comparison across versions with four developers. The article explicitly declines to interpret the delta as evidence about v4.1 documentation quality, treating it as a manipulation check.

> "However, v4.1 scored lower on documentation clarity than v4 (M= 3.58vs M= 3.97; Fig. 5b). As the v4.1 guide was an intentionally incomplete alpha, we do not interpret the cross-version delta as evidence about v4.1 documentation quality"
