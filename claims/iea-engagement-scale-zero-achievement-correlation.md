## Evidence

### Kimmo Eriksson 2019

Kimmo Eriksson, Ola Helenius, Andreas Ryve. (2019). Using TIMSS items to evaluate the effectiveness of different instructional practices. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9473-1

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article reports, citing IEA (2011), that the Instruction to Engage Students in Learning Scale formed from six teacher-questionnaire items has alpha values around 0.6 but practically zero correlation to student achievement; a similar null result is attributed to Blömeke et al. (2016).

> "However, the same table from IEA (2011) reports that the scale has practically zero correlation to student achievement (Exhibit 6 in IEA 2011)."
