## Evidence

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subject experiment with 107 adult non-literates randomly divided into an experimental group (54, EPM-based content) and control group (53, TLC), learning via CAL applications one hour a day for four weeks. Independent t-test showed the EG "significantly better than the mean score of the CG".

> "The results showed that the mean score of the EG was significantly better than the mean score of the CG (t (54, 2) = 3.94, p < 0.001). The mean recognition score of the EG (μ = 20.50, SD = 8.489) was greater than the mean score of the CG (μ = 14.99, SD = 5.754)."

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018 (2)

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analyses of recognition scores by age (15–40 vs 41–60) and gender via independent t-tests; the EG outperformed the CG in each subgroup, with printed means for each comparison in Table 4.

> "The results showed that the EG were significantly better than the CG with (t (46, 2) = 2.27, p = 0.028) and (t (61, 2) = 3.19, p = 0.002)."
