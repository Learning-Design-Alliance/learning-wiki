## Evidence

### Mao Chengqiong 2026

Mao Chengqiong, Liu Yang, Zhang Chao. (2026). Open science practices and feedback-based pedagogy in nanomedicine education: a moderated mediation model of glioma treatment competency. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09948-4

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Moderation analysis (Model 2) in the RCT found the FDB × OSP interaction significant with "β = 0.15, p = 0.003", partial η² = 0.024, supporting a synergistic mechanism between the two instructional strategies.

> "The interaction coefficient between feedback-based pedagogy and open science practice was positive and significant (β = 0.15, p = 0.003), indicating that the combination of the two instructional strategies generated an additional value-added effect beyond their independent contributions"

### Mao Chengqiong 2026 (2)

Mao Chengqiong, Liu Yang, Zhang Chao. (2026). Open science practices and feedback-based pedagogy in nanomedicine education: a moderated mediation model of glioma treatment competency. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09948-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Simple-slope analysis in the moderation model showed "each unit increase in feedback intensity enhances innovation capability by 0.48 standard deviations" at high OSP, more than twice the low-OSP effect (β = 0.22). Johnson-Neyman analysis identified significance when OSP exceeds 3.42 (62.4% of sample).

> "at high OSP (+ 1 SD), each unit increase in feedback intensity enhances innovation capability by 0.48 standard deviations (p<0.001)—more than twice the effect at low OSP (β = 0.22, p<0.001)"
