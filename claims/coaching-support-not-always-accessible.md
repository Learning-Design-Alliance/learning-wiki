## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the 3-year interview study, a small number of students did not refer to coaches as potential support because, as this participant explains, they did not need help beyond their PI and close collaborators.

> "I just don't think I really needed to like seek any help outside of my PI or any of the other people I collaborated with closely."

### Williams 2017 (2)

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The article reports that disappointment with lack of engagement and little perceived benefit was largely held by students in coaching groups that met "fewer than three times per year," with scheduling across time zones a stated logistical difficulty.

> "These perspectives were largely held among those students in coaching groups that met less frequently (fewer than three times per year)."
