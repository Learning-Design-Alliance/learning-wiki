### Angela Johnson 2020

Angela Johnson. (2020). Within-year achievement gains for English Learners. NWEA Research working paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/within-year-achievement-gains-for-english-learners/

Large-scale observational analysis of NWEA assessment data covering 186,139 ever-ELs and never-EL peers in 1,520 schools. The study reports that "All three EL groups have lower mean math and reading achievement than the average of all students in kindergarten." No effect sizes are printed in the available text.
