## Evidence

### Juan Pablo Carbajal 2011

Juan Pablo Carbajal, Dorit Assaf, Emanuel Benker. (2011). Promoting scientific thinking with robots. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1108.4440](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1108.4440)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observational report from a modeling-mechatronics-systems class of mainly 3rd year bachelor students at the Baden-Wuerttemberg Cooperative State University Loerrach. They report that "No group actually found the correct solution (Braitenberg's vehicle 3)" and that one group's feedback controller "seemed aligned with Braitenberg's ideas". No effect sizes or tests are reported.

> "No group actually found the correct solution (Braitenberg's vehicle 3) or anything equivalent. However, one group proposed a feedback controller that, despite its complexity, seemed aligned with Braitenberg's ideas."
