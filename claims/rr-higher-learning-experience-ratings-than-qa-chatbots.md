## Evidence

### Robin Schmucker 2024

Robin Schmucker, Meng Xia, Amos Azaria, and Tom Mitchell. (2024). Ruffle&Riley: Insights from Designing and Evaluating a Large Language Model-Based Conversational Tutoring System. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_6)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Evaluation 1 learning-experience ratings on a 7-point Likert survey were tested with one-way ANOVA followed by Bonferroni post-hoc analysis. The study reports R&R "rated as significantly more helpful in aiding participants in understanding, remembering the lesson" and more enjoyable than TQA and LQA; no effect sizes were printed.

> "However, among the three chatbot conditions, R&R was rated as significantly more helpful in aiding participants in understanding, remembering the lesson, and providing the support needed to learn. Further, R&R participants expressed more enjoyment than TQA and LQA participants."

### Robin Schmucker 2024 (2)

Robin Schmucker, Meng Xia, Amos Azaria, and Tom Mitchell. (2024). Ruffle&Riley: Insights from Designing and Evaluating a Large Language Model-Based Conversational Tutoring System. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_6)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same Evaluation 1 survey analysis (ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc, p < 0.05 threshold): participants rated R&R's conversation as significantly more coherent than LQA's. No effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "In addition, participants found R&R provided a significantly more coherent conversation than LQA."
