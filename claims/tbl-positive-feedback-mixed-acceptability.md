Jabeen Kauser, Rehman Javeria, Fatima Syeda Sadia, Ahmed Imran, Ghias Kulsoom. (2026). Does medical students’ perception of team-based learning have an impact on achieving planned learning outcomes?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09185-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey feedback on eight TBL parameters from 64 students on a 5-point Likert scale (Table 2). Agreement rates included 75% for critical/analytical thinking, 71.8% for participation, 73.4% for accountability, and 78.1% for RATs consolidating concepts; only 51.6% agreed TBL should be included in other modules.

> "a higher proportion of students agreed that TBL enhanced critical and analytical thinking, engagement and participation, accountability for learning, and in-depth conceptual understanding of basic sciences"
