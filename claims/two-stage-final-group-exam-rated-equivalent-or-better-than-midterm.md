### Callaghan 2026

Callaghan, K., Milbourne, T., Klales, A., Kestin, G., Arguelles, C., McCarty, L., & Deslauriers, L. (2026). Two-Stage Final Exams: An Assessment Strategy for Enhanced Collaborative Learning and Reduced Student Stress. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2026.2620442](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2026.2620442)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey comparison of student responses after two or three midterms versus after the final group exam in two courses using the "2 hr Individual + 1 hr Group" format, shown in Figure 2. Students "rated their experience with this format for the final group exam as equivalent to or better than a midterm group exam." Survey response rates were 90% (160/178) at Institution 1 and 94% (216/229) at Institution 2.

> "Students at both institutions rated their experience with this format for the final group exam as equivalent to or better than a midterm group exam."
