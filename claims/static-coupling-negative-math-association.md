## Evidence

### Xiaomeng Xu 2026

Xiaomeng Xu, Dan Zhang & Yu Zhang. (2026). Student teacher inter brain coupling forecast cross semester academic fluctuation in real world classrooms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00421-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Static Bayesian LME model relating same-wave TI values to concurrent normalized math scores in the longitudinal hyperscanning dataset. Theta-band TI showed a negative association (posterior mean -0.141, 95% HPDI excluding zero); senior students additionally showed negative delta, theta and low-beta associations.

> "Specifically, in the full sample, theta-band TI was negatively associated with math scores  (theta TI: posterior mean = -0.141, 95%, HPDI [ -0.265, -0.018])."
