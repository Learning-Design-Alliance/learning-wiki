### Pardej S. K. 2026

Pardej S. K., Russo O., Green T. (2026). Effects of Neighborhood Level Health and Environment Quality on Academic and Executive Abilities in Youth with Noonan Syndrome Spectrum Disorder. Journal of the International Neuropsychological Society. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1355617726101969

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression models for Wechsler WMI (F(3, 107)=10.61, R2=.207), PSI (F(3, 120)=13.85, R2=.238), and BRIEF Global Executive Composite (F(3, 211)=34.55, R2=.319) were significant; "having NSSD was associated with lower executive function scores."

> "For each regression, diagnosis was a significant predictor with a large effect size (p<.001, partial η2=.18–.32); having NSSD was associated with lower executive function scores."
