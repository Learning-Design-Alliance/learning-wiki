Evidence¶

Baeten 2010¶

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review of 25 articles on student-centred learning environments and approaches to learning. The review reports that pre/post studies "confirmed the hypothesis that these teaching methods deepened students' learning approaches" in some cases, while other studies showed significant increases in surface approaches or decreases in deep approaches.

"Several studies using pre- and post-test measures of approaches to learning, before and after experiencing student-centred teaching methods, confirmed the hypothesis that these teaching methods deepened students' learning approaches"
