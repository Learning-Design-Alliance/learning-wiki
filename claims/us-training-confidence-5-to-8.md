## Evidence

### Lin Xu-Nian 2026

Lin Xu-Nian, Lu Cheng-Wei, Lin Tzu-Yu, Chou San-Fang, Chang Ya-Ying. (2026). Rapid skill acquisition through task-specific ultrasound training for peripheral cannulation in medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09935-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of pre- and post-training questionnaire ratings from 30 participants on a 1–10 ordinal scale. The median confidence level increased from 5 to 8, with effect size r = 0.81 and P < 0.001.

> "Please rate your confidence level in performing US-guided peripheral venous access using the handheld US system 5 (2) 8 (2) 2.5 (2.0-3.5) 4.43 0.81 < 0.001*"
