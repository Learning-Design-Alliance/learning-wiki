Alpay Suha, Darafarin Yasemen, Dagdelen Burcu, Al-Shammari Sana Mahroos Mkhailef, Buyukbayram Isil Kaya. (2025). Comparison of ChatGPT-4o and expert evaluation in endodontic education: a cross-sectional pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08358-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability analysis of the pilot study's 32 postoperative radiographs scored independently by one 10-year-experienced endodontist and ChatGPT-4o on a five-criterion 5-point rubric. The "ICC values ranged from 0.36 to 0.45, indicating poor to moderate agreement", with the highest ICC for procedural errors and the lowest for overall taper.

> "ICC values ranged from 0.36 to 0.45, indicating poor to moderate agreement (Fig. 2). The highest ICC was observed for procedural errors (ICC = 0.45)"
