Descriptive analysis of turnover by field of main teaching assignment (Table 2) using NTPS/TFS data. The report identifies "ESL or bilingual education (19.0%), foreign languages (18.3%), CTE (17.5%), and special education (16.4%)" as the highest-turnover fields.

> "Turnover also varies across teaching fields, with the highest turnover rates observed among teachers teaching ESL or bilingual education (19.0%), foreign languages (18.3%), CTE (17.5%), and special education (16.4%)."
