### The Education Trust–Midwest 2021

The Education Trust–Midwest. (2021). Engine of Inequality: Michigan's Education System. https://midwest.edtrust.org/advocacy/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's analysis of EdBuild data on state funding weights finds "Michigan’s weight of 11.5% is among the lowest in the country" for low-income students. A separate School Aid Fund boilerplate provision cuts low-income funding first in shortfalls, so only about 9% more has actually been spent in recent budget years.
