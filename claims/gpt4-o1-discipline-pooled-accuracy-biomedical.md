Discipline‐level pooled analysis including disciplines testing OpenAI LLMs in ≥5 studies, comparing GPT‐4 through o1 (GPT‐4_o1) with GPT‐3/3.5. Reports "a pooled accuracy of 87% for the anatomical sciences, compared with 83% for GPT‐3/3.5"; no effect size printed. Figure 3 shows pooled accuracy by discipline and version.

> "GPT‐4_o1 versions achieved a pooled accuracy of 87% for the anatomical sciences, compared with 83% for GPT‐3/3.5 (Figure 2). GPT‐4_o1 demonstrated a pooled accuracy of ≥83% for “USMLE Step 1/Equivalent” and four other biomedical disciplines (Figure 3)."
