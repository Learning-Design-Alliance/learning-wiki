## Evidence

### Faria et al 2017

Faria et al. (2017). Early Warning Intervention and Monitoring System (EWIMS). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=early+warning+intervention+and+monitoring+system

`q4 · i1` · `causal · r?`

A randomized evaluation by Faria et al. (2017) assigned 73 high schools in three Midwestern states to implement EWIMS or continue usual practices, testing 35,888 students in grades 9 and 10 after one year. The percentage of students who were chronically absent was significantly lower in EWIMS schools, with "effect size =  +0.23".

> "The percentage of students who were chronically absent was significantly lower in EWIMS schools (effect size =  +0.23), qualifying EWIMS for the “Strong” evidence category."
