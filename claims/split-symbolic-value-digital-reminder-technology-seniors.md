## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some seniors perceived using the digital calendar as a sign of modernity and societal participation, while others perceived it as signalling aging, dependence, and memory deficits. [→ Baric 2019](#baric-2019)

## Evidence

### Baric 2019

Baric, V., Andreassen, M., Öhman, A., & Hemmingsson, H. (2019). Using an interactive digital calendar with mobile phone reminders by senior people - a focus group study. BMC Geriatrics, 19, 116. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-019-1128-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group content analysis yielded the 'Symbolic value' theme with two contrasting categories, 'A sign of modernity' and 'A sign of aging, dependence, and memory deficits', based on participants' discussions after six weeks of use.

> "Some participants felt that using an interactive digital calendar was a sign of modernity allowing them to take part in the society at large, but others felt that their inability to use the technology was due to their age, dependence, and loss of function."
