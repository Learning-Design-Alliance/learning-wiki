## Evidence

### Ben Clarke 2014

Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Mari Strand Cary, Derek Kosty, Scott Baker, Hank Fien, and Keith Smolkowski. (2014). Preliminary Evaluation of a Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First-Grade Students: Using a Theory of Change to Guide Formative Evaluation Activities. School Psychology Review, 43(2), 160-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567775

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports, from its own recent efficacy trial of a kindergarten mathematics program using the low-inference COSTI-M observation instrument in approximately 400 observations across 129 kindergarten classrooms, that "students in classrooms with higher rates of practice opportunities made substantively important gains". No effect size is printed here.

> "A key finding from the study is that students in classrooms with higher rates of practice opportunities made substantively important gains in critical mathematics outcomes."

### Clarke 2014

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Strand Cary, M., Kosty, D., Baker, S., Fien, H., & Smolkowski, K. (2014). Preliminary Evaluation of a Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First-Grade Students. School Psychology Review, 43(2), 160-177. https://www.nasponline.org/journals/spr

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article attributes this finding to its research team's kindergarten efficacy trial of approximately 400 observations in 129 kindergarten classrooms using the low-inference COSTI-M instrument capturing demonstrations, practice opportunities, and academic feedback. No effect size is printed.

> "A key finding from the study is that students in classrooms with higher rates of practice opportunities made substantively important gains in critical mathematics outcomes."
