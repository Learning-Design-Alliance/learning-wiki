### Vilaplana-Pérez 2021

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Register-based logistic regression within a subcohort of 1 425 340 individuals graduating compulsory school 1998–2013, using National School Register grades. The article reports "adjusted OR (aOR) ranging from 0.19 to 0.44" across subjects, with the core subjects among those impaired.
