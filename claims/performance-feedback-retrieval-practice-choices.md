## Evidence

### Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023

Anique B. H. de Bruin, Felicitas Biwer, Luotong Hui, Erdem Onan, Louise David, Wisnu Wiradhany. (2023). Worth the Effort: the Start and Stick to Desirable Difficulties (S2D2) Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09766-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports this task-level intervention study by Hui and colleagues (2021), in which learners studied human anatomy pictures and names and chose restudy or retrieval practice after an initial study round; those experiencing the benefits of retrieval practice more often chose it after feedback.

> "Individual feedback led to more long-term choices for retrieval practice compared with a general instruction that explained the benefits of retrieval practice."
