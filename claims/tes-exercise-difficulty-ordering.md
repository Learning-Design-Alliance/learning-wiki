### Popa Călin 2023

Popa Călin, Schlanger Diana, Prunoiu Virgiliu Mihail, Puia Ion Cosmin, Zaharie Florin. (2023). A novel step-by-step training program for transanal endoscopic surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04296-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of completion times across the synthetic exercises in the 40-participant validation study, based on time of completion for each exercise. The ordering and p-values are as printed; no effect size is reported.

> "The ‘Teddy bears’ and the ‘Circles’ were the least difficult to perform, followed by the ‘Matches’ and ‘Glove finger’; the ‘Beans’ were the most difficult exercise to perform, with the longest completion times (p < 0.01)."

### Popa Călin 2023 (2)

Popa Călin, Schlanger Diana, Prunoiu Virgiliu Mihail, Puia Ion Cosmin, Zaharie Florin. (2023). A novel step-by-step training program for transanal endoscopic surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04296-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Within-exercise comparison across the 5 cm and 7.5 cm tubular systems in the same validation cohort. The article reports no statistically significant difference; equivalence was not formally tested beyond these p-values, and no effect size is printed.

> "The same exercise performed on different diameter tubular systems (‘Teddy bears’ and ‘Beans’) did not have a statistically significant difference between them (p = 0.12 and p = 0.58), therefore they can be considered of similar difficulty."
