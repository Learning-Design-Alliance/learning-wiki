## Subclaims
`q2 i2` The mean number of attempts correlated negatively with the final post-test score (ρ = −0.40), and growth in attempts correlated negatively with the MCQ score (ρ = −0.48). [→ Sergei Volodin 2025](#sergei-volodin-2025)

## Evidence

### Sergei Volodin 2025

Sergei Volodin, Hala Khodr, Pierre Dillenbourg, Wafa Johal. (2025). Going Down the Abstraction Stream with Augmented Reality and Tangible Robots: The Case of Vector Instruction. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.14562](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.14562)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Correlational analysis of game logs across 79 level-solve data points from 14 teams. The article reports "negatively correlated with the final post-test score (ρ = −0.40, p= 10−4)" and separately a growth-in-attempts vs MCQ correlation of ρ = −0.48, p <10−4.

> "The mean number of attempts is negatively correlated with the final post-test score (ρ = −0.40, p= 10−4). In other words, we see that less attempts result in higher scores."

### Sergei Volodin 2025 (2)

Sergei Volodin, Hala Khodr, Pierre Dillenbourg, Wafa Johal. (2025). Going Down the Abstraction Stream with Augmented Reality and Tangible Robots: The Case of Vector Instruction. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.14562](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.14562)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors rule out prior knowledge as the explanation for the attempts–score relationship, reporting no correlation between pre-test scores and number of attempts. This is a stated interpretation of the correlational data.

> "Since there is no correlation between pre-test scores and the number of attempts, this is not because of the initial difference in the teams’ levels."
