# Learners' attention distribution index in VR lectures changed over weeks, with a significant increase from week 2 to week 3, but did not differ between attentive and non-attentive support groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` ADI changed significantly over the three weeks, localized to a week 2 vs week 3 difference (d = 0.728), with no significant main effect of Group or Week × Group interaction. [→ Shi Liu 2026](#shi-liu-2026)

## Evidence

### Shi Liu 2026

Shi Liu, Martin Feick, Linus Bierhoff, and Alexander Maedche. 2026. AttentiveLearn: Personalized Post-Lecture Support for Gaze-Aware Immersive Learning. In Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790667

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on section-level ADI from eye-tracking in three weekly VR lectures (36 participants initially, 28 completing). The week effect was significant (F(2,52)=5.015, p=.010) with a printed effect size of d = 0.728 for the week 2 vs week 3 post-hoc difference; no Group effect or interaction.

> "Post-hoc pairwise comparisons (Holm-adjusted) localized the effect to a difference between week 2 and week 3 (𝑀𝐷= 0.074,𝑆𝐸= 0.023;𝑡( 26)= 3.189,𝑝 holm =. 011;𝑑= 0.728)"
