Rima Hazra 2026¶

Rima Hazra, Bikram Ghuku, Ilona Marchenko, Yaroslava Tokarieva, Sayan Layek, Somnath Banerjee, Julia Stoyanovich, Mykola Pechenizkiy. (2026). SAFETUTORS: Benchmarking Pedagogical Safety in AI Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17373

q2 · i? · design · r2

In the multi-turn amplification analysis, Informational harm is the only dimension that decreases, falling "from 60.52% in single-turn to 11.90% in multi-turn". The article attributes this to additional conversational context helping models produce more factually grounded responses; no effect size is printed.

"The sole exception is Informational harm, which drops from 60.52% in single-turn to 11.90% in multi-turn, a decrease of 48.62%."
