# Right FFA activation level does not correlate significantly with diagnostic performance in radiologists in training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pearson correlations between right FFA beta values and diagnostic performance were non-significant for both short-presentation (r = -.05) and long-presentation (r = 0.24) trials. [→ Kok Ellen M. 2021](#kok-ellen-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Kok Ellen M. 2021

Kok Ellen M., Sorger Bettina, van Geel Koos, Gegenfurtner Andreas, van Merriënboer Jeroen J. G., Robben Simon G. F., de Bruin Anique B. H. (2021). Holistic processing only? The role of the right fusiform face area in radiological expertise. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256849

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis within the radiologists-in-training group (n = 17) contrasting hypothesis 3. The article reports "r = -.05, p = .86" for short trials and "r = 0.24, p = .35" for long trials, both non-significant.

> "There was no significant correlation between the beta values for short-presentation trials and the diagnostic performance for short-presentation trials (r = -.05, p = .86), and between the beta values for long-presentation trials and the average diagnostic performance for long-presentation trials (r = 0.24, p = .35)."
