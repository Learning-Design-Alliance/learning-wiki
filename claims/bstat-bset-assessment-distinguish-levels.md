`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Prospective cohort study (Davoudi et al., n = 22) in which two independent evaluators scored participants during simulation bronchoscopy; the quote reports correlations of "0.86 for the BSTAT and 0.85 for the BSET" and the intermediate-level limitation. No effect size for the group contrast is printed.

> "A high correlation was found between the two instruments (0.86 for the BSTAT and 0.85 for the BSET), with clear differences between beginners and experts. However, there were no significant differences among intermediate-level trainees."
