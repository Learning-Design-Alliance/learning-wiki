# A 12-item short form of the survey retains acceptable-to-good reliability (alpha = 0.85; test-retest = 0.75)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The reduced 12-item survey achieved coefficient alpha of 0.85 and test-retest reliability of 0.75. [→ Kao 2023](#kao-2023)
`q2 i?` All but two items on the final 12-item survey had item-total correlations greater than 0.40, and none detracted from internal consistency. [→ Kao 2023 (2)](#kao-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Kao 2023

Kao, Y., Murphy, D., Hubbard Cheuoua, A., Kannan, P., Tsan, J., Jennings, K. E., Smith, H., Emanuel, S., & Miller, E. R. (2023). Survey development for assessing student computing career intentions: Technical report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638631

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability computation for the 12-item short form selected from the field-test data. The article reports alpha of 0.85, which it calls unexpectedly high given the item count; no effect size is printed.

> "Coefficient alpha for the reduced survey is 0.85, and remains in the good-to-adequate range as well."

### Kao 2023 (2)

Kao, Y., Murphy, D., Hubbard Cheuoua, A., Kannan, P., Tsan, J., Jennings, K. E., Smith, H., Emanuel, S., & Miller, E. R. (2023). Survey development for assessing student computing career intentions: Technical report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638631

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The same short-form evaluation reports test-retest reliability of 0.75 and item discrimination statistics for the 12 selected items, computed from the field-test and retest samples.

> "The test–retest reliability for the 12-item survey is 0.75. In addition, all but two items on the final survey had item–total correlations greater than 0.40, and therefore good discrimination properties, and none of these 12 items were found to detract from internal consistency"
