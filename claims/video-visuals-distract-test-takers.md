### Başal 2015

Başal, A., Gülözer, K., & Demir, İ. (2015). Use of Video and Audio Texts in EFL Listening Test. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i6.1001

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion section of the posttest-only control group study with 57 participants. This is an explanatory account, not a directly tested result; the authors propose "a loading effect while processing information" as a possible mechanism.

> "Test takers may have been distracted by the images, and not all students may have understood the content, even though their language proficiency was similar. Internal cognitive factors may also have played a role in test-taker performance via a loading effect while processing information."
