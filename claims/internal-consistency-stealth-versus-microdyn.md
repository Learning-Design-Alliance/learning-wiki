## Evidence

### Shute 2016

Shute, V. J., Wang, L., Greiff, S., Zhao, W., & Moore, G. (2016). Measuring problem solving skills via stealth assessment in an engaging video game. Computers in Human Behavior, 63, 106-117. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2016.05.047

`q3 · i?`

Internal consistency analysis of the validation study measures. The article reports alpha = 0.76 for the stealth assessment facets and lower alphas for MicroDYN, attributing the low MicroDYN reliabilities to middle school students' difficulty with the tasks and noting acceptable reliabilities in older samples.

> "For the problem solving stealth assessment, Cronbach 's a ¼ 0.76, which indicates good internal reliability among the four problem solving facets. However, Cronbach 's a ¼ 0.67 for rule acquisition, and Cronbach's a ¼ 0.43 for rule application relative to MicroDYN, were less than ideal."
