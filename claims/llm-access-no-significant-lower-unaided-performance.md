## Evidence

### Sebastian Maier 2026

Sebastian Maier, Kai Schwabe, Manuel Schneider, and Stefan Feuerriegel. (2026). Designing Against Deskilling: Metacognitive Feedback Reduces Cognitive Offloading to LLM Assistants. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20143 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.20143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.20143)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Confirmatory test of H1 comparing the AI-only condition (M = 0.62, SD = 0.34) with the no-AI control (M = 0.65, SD = 0.32) on the six unaided test items, using item-level mixed-effects logistic regression. The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "AI-only participants did not have significantly lower odds of answering a final test item correctly than control participants (𝑏=− 0.22,𝑆𝐸= 0.32,𝑂𝑅= 0.80, 95% CI[0.42, 1.51],𝑧=− 0.69,𝑝=. 244)"
