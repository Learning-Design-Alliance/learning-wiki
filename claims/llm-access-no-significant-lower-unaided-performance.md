# Access to an LLM assistant during practice did not significantly lower subsequent unaided test performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` AI-only participants did not have significantly lower odds of answering a final test item correctly than control participants (OR = 0.80, p = .244). [→ Sebastian Maier 2026](#sebastian-maier-2026)

## Evidence

### Sebastian Maier 2026

Sebastian Maier, Kai Schwabe, Manuel Schneider, and Stefan Feuerriegel. (2026). Designing Against Deskilling: Metacognitive Feedback Reduces Cognitive Offloading to LLM Assistants. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20143

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Confirmatory test of H1 comparing the AI-only condition (M = 0.62, SD = 0.34) with the no-AI control (M = 0.65, SD = 0.32) on the six unaided test items, using item-level mixed-effects logistic regression. The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.
