## Evidence

### Worth 2015

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Pre-specified secondary analysis in the three-arm trial testing cross-domain transfer between the literacy and numeracy interventions. The report states the programmes had "no significant transfer effects"; no effect size is printed.

> "The literacy intervention did not have a significant impact on numeracy scores , and vice versa , showing that the programmes had no significant transfer effects."

### Worth 2015 (2)

Worth, J., Sizmur, J., Ager, R. & Styles, B. (2015). Improving Numeracy and Literacy. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN20621819

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Secondary outcome analysis using Key Stage 1 points derived from teacher-assessed levels. The report notes a risk of bias because the underlying levels were assessed by teachers who participated in the intervention; no effect size is printed.

> "Neither the numeracy nor the literacy interventions had a significant impact on Key Stage 1 mathematics, or reading and writing attainment, respectively."
