Grissa Mohamed Habib et al. (2023). Comparison of simulation and video-based training for acute asthma. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04836-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline check in the randomized study: 20-item MCQ before training. The article reports "p = 0.07" for the pre-test comparison; equivalence was not formally tested. Table 1 prints pre-test means of 8.8 (± 2.5) IVC vs 9.8 (± 1.8) HFS with p = 0.065.

> "There was not a statistically significant difference between HFS group and IVC group mean scores at pre-test MCQ (p = 0.07)."
