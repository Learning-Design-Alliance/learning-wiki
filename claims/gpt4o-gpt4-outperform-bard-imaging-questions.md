## Evidence

### Chen Yikai 2024

Chen Yikai, Huang Xiujie, Yang Fangjie, Lin Haiming, Lin Haoyu, Zheng Zhuoqun, Liang Qifeng, Zhang Jinhai, Li Xinxin. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT and Bard on the medical licensing examinations varies across different cultures: a comparison study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06309-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sub-analysis of imaging-based USMLE questions (Table 3) in which GPT-4o, GPT-4 and Google Bard answered image-containing items, scored against standard answers. Overall accuracies printed are 44/49 (89.8%) for GPT-4o, 42/48 (87.5%) for GPT-4 and 12/24 (50%) for Bard.

> "However, there was no significant difference found between GPT-4o and GPT-4, while both them outperformed Google Bard by 39.8% (p < 0.001) and 37.5% (p < 0.001)."
