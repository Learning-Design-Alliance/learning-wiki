Qualitative strand findings on critical AI literacy from teacher reports in the professional development study. One participant wrote, "Students were surprised when they discovered AI can make mistakes." Several teachers used imperfect AI responses as prompts for evidence evaluation and discussion of bias and misinformation.

"Students were surprised when they discovered AI can make mistakes. It became an opportunity to teach critical thinking rather than passive acceptance."
