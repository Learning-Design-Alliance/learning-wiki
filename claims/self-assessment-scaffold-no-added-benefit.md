# Adding diagram self-assessment to diagram completion does not increase diagnostic cue utilization or metacomprehension accuracy beyond diagramming alone

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to hypotheses, no significant differences were found between the two diagramming conditions in cue utilization or in relative or absolute metacomprehension accuracy for causal relations. [→ Pijeira-Díaz 2023](#pijeira-diaz-2023)

## Evidence

### Pijeira-Díaz 2023

Pijeira-Díaz, H. J., van de Pol, J., Channa, F., & de Bruin, A. (2023). Scaffolding self-regulated learning from causal-relations texts: Diagramming and self-assessment to improve metacomprehension accuracy? Metacognition and Learning, 18, 631–658. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09343-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects experiment comparing diagram completion plus self-assessment (n = 143) with diagram completion only (n = 145); pairwise Bonferroni comparisons found "no significant differences between the two diagramming conditions" for relative metacomprehension accuracy for relations.

> "However, contrary to our expectations, there were no significant differences between the two diagramming conditions (p = 1.000)."
