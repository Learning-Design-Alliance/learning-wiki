Evidence¶

Shayla Sharmin 2025¶

Shayla Sharmin, Mohammad Al-Ratrout, Mohammad Fahim Abrar, and Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2025). How YouTube Frames ChatGPT Use in Education: An Epistemic Network Analysis with Supporting Multimodal Metadata. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08698

q2 · i? · associational · r2

PRISMA-selected corpus of 52 educational YouTube videos (557 coded chunks) analyzed with ENA using nine binary framing and strategy codes; group sizes were G1 n=25, G2 n=10, G3 n=17. The study "identified three structurally distinct discourse groups" with distinct epistemic orientations.

"We identified three structurally distinct discourse groups: (𝐺1) videos that positioned ChatGPT as a conceptual scaffold for thinking, (𝐺2) videos oriented toward retrieval practice and skill-building, and (𝐺3) videos that framed ChatGPT as a tool for output generation."

Related Claims¶

  • Learning-oriented video groups differ significantly from the productivity-oriented group on the primary ENA axis, with large effect sizes — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • G2 is distinguished from G1 by a strong Pedagogical–Active_Recall co-occurrence that code frequencies alone cannot reveal — related
  • Titles and thumbnails mirror transcript framing: learning-oriented language and imagery dominate G1/G2 while productivity and urgency language dominate G3 — related
  • Productivity-oriented ChatGPT videos achieved platform reach comparable to skill-oriented videos despite weaker learning-oriented framing — related
  • Viewers of learning-oriented content describe ChatGPT as a tutor or thinking partner, while viewers of output-oriented content raise over-reliance and cognitive-offloading concerns — related
  • Adaptive spaced retrieval practice produced higher end-of-semester posttest performance than learner-directed AI study, but fixed spaced retrieval did not significantly outperform learner-directed study — related
