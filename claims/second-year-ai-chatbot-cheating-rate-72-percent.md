`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of 4,354 students across six schools (two public, two private, two charter) in the 2023-24 academic year, using a 12-item cheating scale adapted from McCabe (2001) with binary recoding of responses. The article reports an overall rate of "72.06%" and notes prior-year rates of 59.9% and 69.5% were broadly comparable. No inferential test of change was applied.

> "Across the six schools surveyed in the 2023–24 academic year, 72.06% of students reported engaging in at least one academically dishonest behavior in the prior month."
