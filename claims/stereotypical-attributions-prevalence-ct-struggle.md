Lucas Vasconcelos. (2025). “We are too dumb for this”: a critical discourse analysis of stereotypical attributions to struggle with computational thinking. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10532-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative single case study coding of 75 min of video-recorded discourse and two 20-min interviews with two preservice teachers; the article reports "a higher incidence of stereotypical attributions (n = 21) compared to non-stereotypical attributions (n = 6)". No effect size is printed.

> "A total of 27 episodes were coded as either stereotypical or non-stereotypical attributions across all data sources. There was a higher incidence of stereotypical attributions (n = 21) compared to non-stereotypical attributions (n = 6), and all were negative in nature."
