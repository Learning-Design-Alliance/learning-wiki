## Evidence

### Akayuure et al. 2016

Peter Akayuure, S. K. Asiedu-Addo, Victor Alebna. (2016). Investigating the Effect of Origami Instruction on Preservice Teachers’ Spatial Ability and Geometric Knowledge for Teaching. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology. https://doi.org/10.18404/ijemst.78424

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest comparison in the quasi-experiment (experimental n = 52, control n = 42): the MANOVA on pretest scores and univariate ANOVAs on spatial orientation, spatial visualization, and GKT all showed no preexisting group differences, supporting attribution of posttest differences to treatment.

> "Furthermore, the univariate ANOVA results on pretest scores showed no preexisting differences between the two groups in terms of the subscales of spatial orientation, spatial visualization and geometric knowledge."
