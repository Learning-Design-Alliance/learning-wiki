Bergh 2026¶

Bergh, C., Tag, B., Vassar, A., & Renzella, J. (2026). Your Programming Students'Cognition with ChatGPT: Higher Performance, Lower Retention, and Reduced Ownership. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21194

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of task durations derived from Tobii interval markers (n=28 in the AI-Assisted descriptive analysis after one recording failure). On Task 3 the No AI group worked nearly twice as long (M=11.4min vs M=5.9min). The article states these observations are descriptive only, with no confirmatory statistical comparisons.

"Across the full assessment, the No AI participants spent𝑀= 24.1min, while AI-Assisted participants took𝑀= 16.4min, sustaining their higher reported effort over a longer period"
