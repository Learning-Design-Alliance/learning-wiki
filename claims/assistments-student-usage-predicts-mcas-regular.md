## Evidence

### Koedinger 2010

Koedinger, K. R., McLaughlin, E. A., & Heffernan, N. T. (2010). A quasi-experimental evaluation of an on-line formative assessment and tutoring system. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 43(4), 489-510. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.43.4.d

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Usage analysis of the quasi-experiment: high usage was 60 or more completed items (about 2 hours of content); high users averaged 109.6 items and 222.5 minutes versus 22.5 items and 60.2 minutes for low users. The association is correlational.

> "Results of a 3-level ANCOVA (high vs. low vs. no usage) show a main effect for regular student usage,F(2, 744) = 15.05,p = .000, with high usage students (adj M = 61.65, SE = .75) outperforming both the low usage students (adj M = 58.06, SE = .52) and non-usage students (adj M = 54.99, SE = .99)"
