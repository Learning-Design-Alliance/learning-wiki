### Alicia M. Montecinos 2013

Alicia M. Montecinos. (2013). Free fall misconceptions: results of a graph based pre test of sophomore civil engineering students. [doi:10.20308/ejpe.27388](https://doi.org/10.20308/ejpe.27388)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Open-ended graph-guided pre-test of 14 sophomore civil engineering students during a laboratory session. For the acceleration model, "100% of the class presented an acceptable lineal model", while many position and velocity models were incorrect, which the author reads as evidence of memorization without understanding.

> "In regards of the acceleration model, 100% of the class presented an acceptable lineal model."
