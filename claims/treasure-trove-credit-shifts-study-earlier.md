## Evidence

### Chen 2025

Chen, Z.; Singh, C. (2025). Opportunities and Challenges in Harnessing Digital Technology for Effective Teaching and Learning. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.3390/higheredu4010006](https://doi.org/10.3390/higheredu4010006)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

An observational comparison of study-session start dates for a sequence of 10 online homework assignments over 14 days, before (2018) and after (2019) adding treasure trove extra-credit quizzes. Before implementation, most sessions started 'either 2–4 days before or on the same day as the assignment due date'; after adding treasure troves, 'there was a clear shift of study sessions toward earlier dates' concentrated on the treasure trove due dates. Further analysis found high- and low-performing students equally likely to use the opportunity.

> "After adding the treasure troves in 2019, there was a clear shift of study sessions toward earlier dates and concentrated on days −10, −7, and −2, which were the treasure trove due dates."
