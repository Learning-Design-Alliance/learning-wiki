# Undergraduates rate stress from uncontrollable stressors as significantly more debilitating than stress from controllable stressors, with a large effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Stress arising from uncontrollable stressors is rated as more debilitating than stress arising from controllable stressors (partial η2 = .469). [→ Jenkins Anna 2021](#jenkins-anna-2021)

## Evidence

### Jenkins Anna 2021

Jenkins Anna, Weeks Molly S., Hard Bridgette Martin. (2021). General and specific stress mindsets: Links with college student health and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256351

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA with controllability and duration as within-subjects factors in the cross-sectional study of 498 undergraduates, controlling gender and sample. The article reports the controllability main effect with "partial η2 = .469", a large effect.

> "participants rated stress arising from uncontrollable stressors (M = 1.52, SD = 0.67) as significantly more debilitating than stress arising from controllable stressors (M = 2.40, SD = 0.67), Wilks' λ = .531, F(1, 481) = 424.96, p < .001, partial η2 = .469."
