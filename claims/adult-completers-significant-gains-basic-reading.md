### Sabatini John P. 2011

Sabatini John P., Shore Jane, Holtzman Steven, Scarborough Hollis S. (2011). Relative Effectiveness of Reading Intervention Programs for Adults with Low Literacy. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3269067/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures MANOVA of the 148 intervention completers with time of test as within-subject measure and intervention condition as between-subject factor. The article reports "Performance on the test battery improved over time, F(6, 140) = 24.53, p < .001, partial eta squared = 0.51" with significant gains on all six subtests.

> "Performance on the test battery improved over time, F(6, 140) = 24.53, p < .001, partial eta squared = 0.51. For the total sample, significant gains were observed for each of the six subtests."
