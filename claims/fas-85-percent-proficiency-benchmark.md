## Evidence

### Lee Gun Tak 2022

Lee Gun Tak, Park Jong Eun, Woo Sook-young, Shin Tae Gun, Jeong Daun, Kim Taerim, Lee Se Uk, Yoon Hee, Hwang Sung Yeon. (2022). Defining the learning curve for endotracheal intubation in the emergency department. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19337-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article attributes this benchmark to Park et al.'s systematic review and meta-analysis of 42,081 ED intubations; this study cites it second-hand as the rationale for its "FAS target of ≥ 85% for EM trainees". The reviewed meta-analysis was not independently examined here.

> "This data showed that the FAS rate in the ED was 84.1% (95% CI 80.1–87.4]). Based on the findings of previous studies, as well as the importance of the FAS for patient safety, it may be reasonable to establish a FAS target of ≥ 85% for EM trainees"
