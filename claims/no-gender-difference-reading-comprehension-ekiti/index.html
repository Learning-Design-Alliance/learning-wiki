Evidence¶

Akinwumi 2017¶

Akinwumi, J. O. (2017). Effects of Gender and School Location on the Ekiti State Secondary Schools Students' Achievement in Reading Comprehension in English Language. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133080

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A 2 x 3 ANCOVA of 270 SSSII students' reading comprehension scores by gender and treatment, with pre-test as covariate. The sex-by-group interaction was non-significant ("F = 1.284, p > 0.05"), as was the gender main effect (F = 0.227, p > 0.05).

"The result in table 1 showed that there was no significant difference in the achievement of male and female between the experimental and control groups ( F = 1.284, p > 0.05). The null hypothesis is not rejected."
