# Smartphone addiction has a negative, statistically significant direct and total effect on academic achievement in secondary school students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The direct effect of smartphone addiction on academic achievement is negative and statistically significant (B = -0.118). [→ Koğar 2025](#kogar-2025)
`q2 i?` The total effect including mediators is also negative and statistically significant (B = -0.274), indicating partial mediation. [→ Koğar 2025](#kogar-2025)

## Evidence

### Koğar 2025

Koğar, H., Sayın, A., Şekercioğlu, G., Yılmaz Koğar, E., and Kafes, H. (2025). The Mediating Role of Academic Resilience and Cognitive Test Anxiety in the Association Between Smartphone Addiction and Academic Achievement. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.70800

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 828 Turkish seventh- and eighth-grade students analyzed with the PROCESS macro chain mediation model (model 6) using 10,000 bootstrapping. The article reports a statistically significant direct effect of "B = ‐0.118" and total effect of "B = ‐0.274"; no standardized effect size is printed for these effects.

> "The direct effect of smartphone addiction on academic achievement is statistically significant (B = ‐0.118, 95% CI [‐0.219, ‐0.017], p < 0.05). The total effect obtained when the effect of mediating variables is included is also statistically significant (B = ‐0.274, 95% CI [‐0.370, ‐0.177], p < 0.001)."
