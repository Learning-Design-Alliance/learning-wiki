### Ghasempour Saeed 2023

Relationship between academic success, distance education learning environments, and its related factors among medical sciences students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04856-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate t-tests and one-way ANOVAs in the 208-student survey (Table 1); e.g., gender P = 0.909, marital status P = 0.383, GPA P = 0.150, residence P = 0.370, income P = 0.592. No effect sizes are printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "Based on the results, the mean score of students’ academic success according to gender, marital status, GPA, academic year, place of residence, and income adequacy did not have a statistically significant difference."
