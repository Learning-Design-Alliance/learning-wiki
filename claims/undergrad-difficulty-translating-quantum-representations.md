## Evidence

### Marshman 2024

Marshman, E., Maries, A., & Singh, C. (2024). Using multiple representations to improve student understanding of quantum states. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020152)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pretest administered to 85 upper-level undergraduates after traditional lecture-based instruction at a large public research university. On Q1 "only 43% of the undergraduate students provided a correct answer"; Table III further reports 10% correct on Q2, 11% on Q3, and 13% on Q6 and Q7.
