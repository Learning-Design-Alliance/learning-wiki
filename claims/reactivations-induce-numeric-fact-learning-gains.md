### Gilad Schrift 2022

Gilad Schrift, Dror Dotan and Nitzan Censor. (2022). Brief memory reactivations induce learning in the numeric domain. npj Science of Learning 7:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00136-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a repeated-measures ANOVA with Session and Group factors on online number-fact retrieval data, followed by paired-samples t-tests. The Reactivations group showed a "mean test-retest score change= 8.48 ± 2.94 S.E." with t(27) = 2.89, P = 0.008; no standardized effect size is printed.
