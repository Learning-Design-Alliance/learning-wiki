Evidence¶

Rapanta 2013¶

Rapanta, C., Maina, M., Lotz, N., & Bacchelli, A. (2013). Team design communication patterns in e-learning design and development. Educational Technology Research and Development, 61(4), 581-605. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-013-9306-5

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Frequency analysis of the coded verbal protocol dataset from 15 video-recorded design meetings at two European distance universities. The article reports transformation at "50.7 %" of team design activity, with evaluation and presentation nearly equal.

"the frequencies of “evaluation” and “presentation” design sequences are almost the same (24.6 and 24.7% correspondingly) for the whole dataset, whereas the activity of “transformation” occupies half (50.7 %) of the total team design activity."
