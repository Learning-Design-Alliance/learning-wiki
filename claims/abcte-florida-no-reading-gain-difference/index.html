Evidence¶

Christina Clark Tuttle 2009¶

Christina Clark Tuttle, Tara Anderson, Steven Glazerman. (2009). ABCTE Teachers in Florida and Their Effect on Student Performance. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/abcte-teachers-in-florida-and-their-effect-on-student-performance

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of ABCTE and non-ABCTE teachers in Florida using propensity score matching, covering 30 teachers over two years. The authors "found no differences in gains in reading between students of ABCTE and non-ABCTE teachers." No effect size is printed.

"Using data on 30 teachers over two years, the authors found no differences in gains in reading between students of ABCTE and non-ABCTE teachers."
