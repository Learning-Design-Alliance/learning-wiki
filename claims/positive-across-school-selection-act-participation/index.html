Evidence¶

Melissa Clark 2007¶

Melissa Clark, Jesse Rothstein, Diane Whitmore Schanzenbach. (2007). Selection Bias in College Admissions Test Scores. Princeton, NJ: Princeton University, Education Research Section. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/selection-bias-in-college-admissions-test-scores

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental estimates from an Illinois policy change making the ACT a graduation requirement. The authors report "substantial positive selection into test participation both across and within schools," with higher participation at higher-achieving schools. No effect size is printed.

"Estimates based on the Illinois policy change indicate substantial positive selection into test participation both across and within schools—at higher-achieving schools, participation rates are higher; within schools, higher-achieving students are more likely to take the test."
