## Evidence

### Farvardin et al. 2014

Mohammad Taghi Farvardin, Akbar Afghari & Mansour Koosha. (2014). The Effect of Dual N-Back Task Training on Phonological Memory Expansion in Adult EFL Learners at the Beginner Level. English Language Teaching, 7(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n9p137

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests within the control group (n = 60) from Table 4: NWRP pre 15.15 to post 15.50 and NWRC pre 9.93 to post 10.30, neither reaching significance. Equivalence was not tested; the article reports only non-significant tests.

> "there is no significant difference between the mean scores of the control groups before and after the treatment (NWRP test: t = -3.08, p > .05; NWRC test: t = -2.30, p > .05)"
