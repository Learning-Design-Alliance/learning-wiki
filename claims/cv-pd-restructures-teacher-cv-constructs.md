# A two-to-three-week computer vision professional development program shifted the majority of middle school teachers' computer vision constructs across clusters (72%, n = 13), indicating restructuring of their perceptions of computer vision

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After computer vision PD, 72% (n = 13) of teachers' computer vision constructs shifted clusters, indicating that participants restructured how they distinguished computer vision constructs. [→ Kurz 2026](#kurz-2026)

## Evidence

### Kurz 2026

Kurz, T. L., Jayasuriya, S., Swisher, K., Mativo, J., Pidaparti, R., & Robinson, D. T. (2026). Computer vision versus human vision: analyzing middle school teachers' construct restructuring following computer vision professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10594-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post repertory grid study of 12 in-service middle school teachers using hierarchical cluster analysis (Ward's Method) of construct ratings. The results section reports that computer vision constructs shifted clusters far more often than human vision constructs, with "72% (n = 13) of the constructs shifted clusters" for computer vision versus 6% (n = 1) for human vision.

> "Regarding human vision, only 6% (n = 1) of the constructs shifted clusters; regarding computer vision, 72% (n = 13) of the constructs shifted clusters."
