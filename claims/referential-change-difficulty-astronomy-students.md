The article's introduction states, citing prior literature on formal-thinking demands, that "the notion of referential is one of the main causes why students have difficulties interpreting motions in astronomy". No empirical data of its own are reported for this point.

> "The notion of referential is one of the main causes why students have difficulties interpreting motions in astronomy. This is not completely surprising, since the change of referential involves abstract reasoning, which is usually not completely developed in all population, especially young students"
