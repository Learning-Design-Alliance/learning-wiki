`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of Smarter Balanced items finds accurate estimates for information recall and narrative items but less accurate ones for higher-order tasks; even at high input ability SMART generates incomplete responses that only state passage facts, receiving partial credit and inflating difficulty. No effect sizes are printed.
