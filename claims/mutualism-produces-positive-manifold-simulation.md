## Evidence

### Van Der Maas et al. 2006

Han L. J. van der Maas, Conor V. Dolan, Raoul P. P. P. Grasman, Jelte M. Wicherts, Hilde M. Huizenga, and Maartje E. J. Raijmakers. (2006). A Dynamical Model of General Intelligence: The Positive Manifold of Intelligence by Mutualism. Psychological Review. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.113.4.842

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation of the g scenario (Scenario 2), in which resources Ki were sampled from a correlated multivariate distribution (r ⫽ .5) with M ⫽ 0. The article reports that "the one-factor model fits the data well" and all variables load highly on the factor. No effect size is printed.

> "The correlations between the observed variables (x) are all positive (⬇.5) and the eigenvalues suggest a dominant factor; the zero-factor model for the observed variables (and for K) is rejected, whereas the one-factor model fits the data well."
