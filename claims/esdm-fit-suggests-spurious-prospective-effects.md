# Good fit of the ESDM indicates the PALMA data could have been generated without any prospective effects between achievement and self-concept

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The extended skill development model, which assumes no direct prospective effects between occasion-specific scores, fit the simulated PALMA-like data well, suggesting Marsh et al.'s presented effects may have been spurious. [→ Kimmo Sorjonen 2025](#kimmo-sorjonen-2025)

## Evidence

### Kimmo Sorjonen 2025

Kimmo Sorjonen, Bo Melin, Gustav Nilsonne. (2025). Inconclusive Evidence for a Prospective Effect of Academic Self-Concept on Achievement: A Simulated Reanalysis and Comment on Marsh et al. (2024). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10008-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM fit of the ESDM on simulated PALMA-like data. The article reports "a good fit" (χ2 = 597, DF = 45, CFI = 0.977, RMSEA = 0.059), indicating the data "could have been generated by a model without any prospective effects" and that the presented effects may have been spurious.

> "The ESDM (Fig. 1C) had a good fit (χ2 = 597, DF = 45, CFI = 0.977, TLI = 0.977, RMSEA = 0.059). This indicates that the data analyzed by Marsh et al. (2024) could have been generated by a model without any prospective effects between achievement and self-concept"
