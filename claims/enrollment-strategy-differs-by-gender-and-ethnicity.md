## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Shahab Boumi 2020

Shahab Boumi, Adan Vela. (2020). Quantifying the relationship between student enrollment patterns and student performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square test of enrollment strategy distribution by gender among UCF students. Female students are more likely to adopt a full-time enrollment strategy, while male students are more likely to apply a combination of enrollment strategies (the 'other' group).

> "The p-value of the Chi-square test (p-value=2.2e−16) indicates that the enrollment strategy distribution for female students diﬀers from male students."

### Shahab Boumi 2020 (2)

Shahab Boumi, Adan Vela. (2020). Quantifying the relationship between student enrollment patterns and student performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square post hoc tests comparing enrollment strategy distributions across race/ethnicity groups at UCF. White students used FES at a higher rate than Black and Hispanic students, who used FES at similar rates.

> "The p-values of all the tests between possible pairs of race/ethnicity groups are close to 0, implying that students with diﬀerent races/ethnicity have diﬀerent distributions over the enrollment strategies."
