## Evidence

### Widyahening Indah S. 2019

Widyahening Indah S., Findyartini Ardi, Ranakusuma Respati W., Dewiasty Esthika, Harimurti Kuntjoro. (2019). Evaluation of the role of near-peer teaching in critical appraisal skills learning: a randomized crossover trial. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c39.b55b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Attitudes were measured with part of the Johnston Knowledge Attitudes and Behaviors questionnaire (6-point Likert, Cronbach's α 0.76) pre and post module. Table 1 shows the single differing item had t = -2.732, p = 0.007, with the staff-tutored group scoring lower.

> "After the module, there were no statistically significant differences in the mean overall attitude score or for the majority of attitude items other than the statement "If evidence-based medicine is valid, then anyone can see patients and do what doctors do.""
