## Evidence

### Lu Zihua 2024

Lu Zihua, Zhu Jiangtao, Chen Cheng. (2024). Performance of flipped classroom teaching method during hemodialysis training of nurses. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2024-0276en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clinical skills assessment in the same randomized trial (n = 23 per group), conducted per the Standard Operating Procedures for Blood Purification (2021 Edition); each item scored 1 to 10. No effect size printed.

> "In FC group, the scores in critical items like aseptic technique, arteriovenous fistula puncture and management, as well as the management of temporary or semi-permanent catheters, were significantly superior to those in LBL group, with significant differences."

### Lu Zihua 2024 (2)

Lu Zihua, Zhu Jiangtao, Chen Cheng. (2024). Performance of flipped classroom teaching method during hemodialysis training of nurses. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2024-0276en

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same skills assessment for the remaining items: FC advantages on "pre-hemodialysis preparation and post-hemodialysis maintenance of the hemodialysis machine" were not statistically significant. No effect size printed.

> "Conversely, FC group showed higher scores in pre-hemodialysis preparation and post-hemodialysis maintenance of the hemodialysis machine, but with no significant differences (Figure 2)."
