## Evidence

### Ackerman 2017

Ackerman, D. J., & Friedman-Krauss, A. H. (2017). Preschoolers’ Executive Function: Importance, Contributors, Research Needs and Assessment Options. Policy Information Report and ETS Research Report Series No. RR-17-22. https://doi.org/10.1002/ets2.12148

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The report reviews a study of 50 kindergarteners comparing native Spanish/English bilingual students with English-only and immersion peers. After controlling for age, verbal ability, and family SES, "the bilingual students performed significantly better on tests of EF." The report also cites multiple smaller studies showing bilingual advantages on inhibitory control and working memory tasks.

> "After controlling forchildren’sage,verbalability,andfamilysocioeconomicstatus,researchersfoundthatthebilingualstudentsperformed significantly better on tests of EF as compared to the monolingual and immersion kindergartners (Carlson & Meltzoff, 2008)."
