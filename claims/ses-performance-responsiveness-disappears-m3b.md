## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In model M3a the responsiveness to performance is negatively moderated by SES (γ32 = −0.07, p < 0.05), but in model M3b this moderation is zero once the responsiveness to cognitive ability is allowed to vary by SES. [→ Mirko Ruks 2023](#mirko-ruks-2023)

## Evidence

### Mirko Ruks 2023

Mirko Ruks. (2023). SES differences in the responsiveness of educational expectations in a twin design. PLoS ONE 18(8): e0290454. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0290454

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of the two moderated ACE-beta models (M3a and M3b) on N = 1029 twin pairs. The article reports the SES moderation of responsiveness to performance "detected in M3a are completely accounted for by SES differences in the responsiveness to cognitive ability"; hypotheses H2 and H3 are rejected.

> "So, the SES differences in the responsiveness to prior performance detected in M3a are completely accounted for by SES differences in the responsiveness to cognitive ability."
