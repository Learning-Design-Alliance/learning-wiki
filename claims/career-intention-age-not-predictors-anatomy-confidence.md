### Farey John E. 2018

Farey John E., Bui David T., Townsend David, Sureshkumar Premala, Carr Sandra, Roberts Chris. (2018). Predictors of confidence in anatomy knowledge for work as a junior doctor: a national survey of Australian medical students. BMC Medical Education 18:174. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null findings from the 1101-student survey's regression models: age carried unadjusted OR 0.99 (0.97–1.02, p = 0.57), and career intention was significant univariately (p = 0.09) but not in the multivariable model (p = 0.69). No statistically significant difference detected; equivalence was not tested.
