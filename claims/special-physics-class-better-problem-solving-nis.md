### G.S. Djordjevic 2011

G.S. Djordjevic, D. Pavlovic-Babic, J. Stankovic. (2011). Evaluation of High School Programme for Gifted Pupils in Physics and Sciences in Serbia - Experience in Regional Cooperation - SEENET-MTP Network. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1110.5072](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1110.5072)
