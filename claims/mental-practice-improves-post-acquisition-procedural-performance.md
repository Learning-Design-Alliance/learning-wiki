`q3 i1` Pooled analysis of five randomised controlled trials shows a small-to-moderate, statistically significant effect of mental practice on procedural performance on post-acquisition tests (SMD 0.43, 95% CI 0.01 to 0.85). [→ Sattelmayer Martin 2016](#sattelmayer-martin-2016)

## Evidence

### Sattelmayer Martin 2016

Sattelmayer Martin, Elsig Simone, Hilfiker Roger, Baer Gillian. (2016). A systematic review and meta-analysis of selected motor learning principles in physiotherapy and medical education. BMC Medical Education, 16, 15. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0538-z

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of five RCTs of mental practice in medical education, 241 participants analysed on performance tests. The review reports a pooled effect size of SMD: 0.43 (95 % CI 0.01 to 0.85), described as "small to moderate", statistically significant (p: 0.046), with moderate heterogeneity (I2: 59 %).

> "The pooled effect size was small to moderate (SMD: 0.43, 95 % CI 0.01 to 0.85) in favour of mental practice on a post-acquisition test. Furthermore, the result was statistically significant (p: 0.046). Heterogeneity was moderate (I2: 59 %)."
