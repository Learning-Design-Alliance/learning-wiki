Questionnaire results (Table 3) among 176 nurses: "48.3% expressed uncertainty" about correct dressing use and 43.2% felt secure; a Kruskal–Wallis test found no significant variation by health area or experience (p = 0.179).

> "When asked about confidence in correct dressing use, 43.2% reported feeling secure, while 48.3% expressed uncertainty. Confidence in dressing use did not vary significantly according to health area or professional experience (Kruskal–Wallis p = 0.179)."
