## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Having hope for the future is associated with more frequent reading (OR = 1.45, 95% CI 1.19 to 1.75 after PS matching per Table 4 and abstract). [→ Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022](#kashiwabara-tomoka-2022)

## Evidence

### Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022

Kashiwabara Tomoka, Fujiwara Takeo, Doi Satomi, Yamaoka Yui. (2022). Association between Hope for the Future and Academic Performance in Adolescents: Results from the K-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191911890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conditional logistic regression on PS-matched K-CHILD pairs for dichotomized reading time; Table 4 prints the matched reading OR = 1.45 (95% CI 1.19 to 1.75, p < 0.001), stable across crude, adjusted, and matched models.

> "This result did not change before and after PS matching. There was no association between having hope for the future and time spent watching TV or DVDs."
