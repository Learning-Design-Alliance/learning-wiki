## Evidence

### Liu Yu 2017

Liu Yu, Gao Qing, Du Song, Chen ZiChen, Fu JianZhong, Chen Bing, Liu ZhenJie, He Yong. (2017). Fabrication of cerebral aneurysm simulator with a desktop 3D printer. Scientific Reports 7, 44301. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep44301

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fabrication result in the Results section: a PVA sacrificial vascular model coated with low- and high-hardness silicone, driven by a cam-based blood flow driver, produced bulges where 'Blister-like dilations with maximum diameters ranging from 5 to 10 mm were successfully produced'.

> "The pulsation pressure of blood flow controls the size of the dilation. Rotational speed of the cam controls the pulsatile frequency of the dilation. Blister-like dilations with maximum diameters ranging from 5 to 10 mm were successfully produced (see the supplementary video)."
