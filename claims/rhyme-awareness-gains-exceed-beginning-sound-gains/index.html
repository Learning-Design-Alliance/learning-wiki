Harper 2011¶

Harper, L. J. (2011). Nursery rhyme knowledge and phonological awareness in preschool children. The Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1), 65-78. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097164

q1 · i? · causal · r1

The author's interpretation in the Discussion of the experimental group's PALS subtask results, stating "the greatest gains were made in rhyme awareness, and the least gains in beginning sound awareness"; no subtask statistics are printed.

"Although the experimental group scored significantly higher on the pre-K PALS following the phonological awareness training, the greatest gains were made in rhyme awareness, and the least gains in beginning sound awareness."
