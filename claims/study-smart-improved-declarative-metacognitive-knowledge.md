## Evidence

### Biwer Felicitas 2022

Biwer Felicitas, de Bruin Anique, Persky Adam. (2022). Study smart – impact of a learning strategy training on students’ study behavior and academic performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9397154/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post and long-term repeated-measures analysis of effectiveness ratings in the Study Smart cohort (final sample 110 students). The repeated-measures MANOVA showed a main effect of time, "F (18, 888) = 12.69; p < 0.001; ηp2= 0.72". In the posttest, the passive strategies were "rated as not effective and thus more accurate with regard to scientific evidence".

> "There was a significant main effect of time, F (18, 888) = 12.69; p < 0.001; ηp2= 0.72. The strategies rereading, summarizing, highlighting, visualization and self-explanation were rated as significantly less effective in the post-test and long-term compared to pretest."
