## Evidence

### Thielmann Anika 2024

Thielmann Anika, Schmitz Marie-Therese, Welchowski Thomas, Weltermann Birgitta. (2024). Effectiveness of the online-eLearning program KeepCoool at improving the vaccine cold chain in general practices. PLoS ONE 19(4): e0301847. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301847

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of the intervention arm (24 refrigerators in 22 participating practices) using one-minute continuous temperature logging of the 1st vs 6th week; refrigerators served as their own control. The article reports "critically low temperatures (≤0°C) 62.5% less at follow-up" and 16.7% newly reaching the target range.

> "The primary outcome (continuous temperatures within target range of 2 to 8°C at follow-up) was reached by 4 of 24 (+16.7%) refrigerators. Improvement was noted also for secondary outcomes: critically low temperatures (≤0°C) 62.5% less at follow-up"
