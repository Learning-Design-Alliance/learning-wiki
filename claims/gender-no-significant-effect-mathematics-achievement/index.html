Same ANCOVA of posttest achievement scores in the quasi-experiment: female mean 20.66, male mean 19.73. The article reports "gender has no significant effect on students’ achievement in mathematics", so hypothesis 2 was rejected; no effect size is printed.

"Table 2 shows that that gender has no significant effect on students’ achievement in mathematics (F (1, 190) = .58; p < .05). Hence hypothesis 2 is, therefore, rejected."
