## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Formal exposure to concussion-related educational material was 10.8 hours for spiral students versus 3.95 cumulative hours for block students. [→ Fraser S 2019](#fraser-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Fraser S 2019

Fraser S, Wright AD, van Donkelaar P, Smirl JD. (2019). Cross-sectional comparison of spiral versus block integrated curriculums in preparing medical students to diagnose and manage concussions. BMC Medical Education 19(17). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1439-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exposure section of the results: spiral students received 10.8 h (case-based learning 5.5 h, small group 2 h, lecture 3.3 h) versus 3.95 h for block students; the article notes block students' clinical exposure was not included in these hours.

> "Spiral students were exposed to concussion education through lectures, case-based learning, and group discussion seminars adding up to 10.8 h of related or specific concussion education. This greatly exceeded block curriculum exposure, which provided 3.95 h (3h seminar and didactic lecture)."
