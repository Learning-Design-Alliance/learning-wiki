`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 1A,B depict adjusted fall and spring mean GPAs relative to workload by professional pharmacy year in the 384-student cohort; the text reports that statistically there were no significant differences in GPA across the three workload classes within each classification, so this is a descriptive pattern with no printed effect size.

> "For both P1 and P3 students, the distribution was an inverted U shape, and for P2 students, it was flat for 0 and 1–15 h of work and there was a decline in the 16–40 h a week category."
