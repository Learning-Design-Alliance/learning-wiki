# Feedback generated by GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 was significantly more readable than instructor-generated feedback on open-ended writing tasks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 generated feedback rated significantly more readable than instructor feedback, with GPT-4 rated highest (mean 3.91 vs 3.21). [→ Dai 2024](#dai-2024)

## Evidence

### Dai 2024

Wei Dai, Yi-Shan Tsai, Jionghao Lin, Ahmad Aldino, Hua Jin, Tongguang Li, Dragan Gašević, Guanliang Chen. (2024). Assessing the Proficiency of Large Language Models in Automatic Feedback Generation: An Evaluation Study. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 7, 100299. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100299

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Human-rater readability evaluation (three raters, five-point scale) of 103 pieces each of instructor, GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 feedback. Means printed in Table 4: instructor 3.21, GPT-3.5 3.76, GPT-4 3.91; "feedback generated by both GPT-based models was significantly more readable than instructor-generated feedback".

> "We can see that feedback generated by both GPT-based models was significantly more readable than instructor-generated feedback."
