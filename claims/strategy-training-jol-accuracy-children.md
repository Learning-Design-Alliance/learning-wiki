## Subclaims

## Evidence

### von der Linden et al. 2015

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on Goodman-Kruskal gamma correlations between JOLs and recall for children (n = 40). Trained children showed higher JOL accuracy (.32 vs. .03), η2 = .24.

> "In children, the main effect of experimental condition was significant (F(1,38) = 12.25; p < .01; η2 = .24) with children in strategy-instruction group having higher gamma correlations (.32) than those in the control group (.03)"

### von der Linden et al. 2015 (2)

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on JOL gamma correlations for older adults (n = 40). The direction matched children (.29 vs. .14) but only approached significance (p = .077, η2 = .08); no reliable effect.

> "In older adults, the results pointed into the same direction (experimental group: 29; control group: .14): the main effect of experimental condition was just short of being significant (F(1,38) = 3.29; p = .077; η2 = .08)"
