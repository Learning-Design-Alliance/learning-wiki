Phillips, F. M. (1916). Comparison of the work done in the successive minutes of a ten minute practice period in the fundamentals of arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tabulation of average deviations and coefficients of variability across all grades and fundamentals from the ten-minute tests. The article reports coefficients "from 8 per cent, to 17 per cent, lower in the first minute" and average deviations 0–2% lower.
