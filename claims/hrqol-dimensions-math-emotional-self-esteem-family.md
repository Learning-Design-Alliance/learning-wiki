# Emotional, self-esteem, and family HRQoL dimensions are all associated with high mathematics performance in both sexes; for language, only family-related HRQoL is significant in boys

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For mathematics, emotional HRQoL (boys OR 1.12, girls OR 1.14), self-esteem (boys OR 1.12, girls OR 1.15), and family-related HRQoL (boys OR 1.15, girls OR 1.11) are each significantly associated with high achievement for both sexes. [→ Lin Qinyun 2025](#lin-qinyun-2025)
`q3 i?` For language, girls show significant self-esteem (OR 1.09) and family (OR 1.09) associations, while boys show only family-related HRQoL (OR 1.09); friends-related HRQoL is not significant for either subject or sex. [→ Lin Qinyun 2025 (2)](#lin-qinyun-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Lin Qinyun 2025

Lin Qinyun, Hunsberger Monica, Klingberg Sofia, de Henauw Stefaan, Hebestreit Antje, Lauria Fabio, Mazur Artur, Moreno Luis, Noémi Kurdiné Molnár Eszter, Pigeot Iris, Tornaritis Michael, Veidebaum Toomas, Lissner Lauren. (2025). Physical, mental and behavioral health indicators in relation to academic performance in European boys and girls: the I.Family study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-23578-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four separate models per sex-subject combination, each with a single HRQoL dimension plus the other seven indicators and covariates; the quote reports printed ORs and p-values for emotional and self-esteem dimensions in mathematics (family dimension ORs 1.15 boys, 1.11 girls also printed).

> "higher odds of high achievement in mathematics are significantly associated with better emotional HRQoL (boys: 1.12 [1.03, 1.22], p = 0.007; girls: 1.14 [1.06, 1.22], p < 0.001), higher self-esteem (boys: 1.12 [1.05, 1.19], p < 0.001; girls: 1.15 [1.08, 1.22], p < 0.001)"

### Lin Qinyun 2025 (2)

Lin Qinyun, Hunsberger Monica, Klingberg Sofia, de Henauw Stefaan, Hebestreit Antje, Lauria Fabio, Mazur Artur, Moreno Luis, Noémi Kurdiné Molnár Eszter, Pigeot Iris, Tornaritis Michael, Veidebaum Toomas, Lissner Lauren. (2025). Physical, mental and behavioral health indicators in relation to academic performance in European boys and girls: the I.Family study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-23578-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same dimension-specific modeling for language achievement in boys; only the family dimension reaches the 5% significance level, with emotional and self-esteem dimensions marginal (p = 0.067 and 0.084).

> "For boys, however, only family-related HRQoL (1.09 [1.01, 1.18]; p = 0.03) is significant at 5% level, while emotional HRQoL (1.09 [0.995, 1.19], p = 0.067) and self-esteem (1.06 [0.992, 1.14], p = 0.084) show a trend approaching significance"
