## Evidence

### Shangchao Min 2026

Shangchao Min, Shaonan Wang, Xinyu Gao, Hui Wang, Zhiling Jin, and 2 more. (2026). Standardized Assessment of LLM English Proficiency. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8820245/v1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item quality analysis of the 624-item bank using classical item indices. The article reports "the mean item difficulty was 0.486 and the mean discrimination index was 0.324", which it interprets against Ebel and Frisbie's criteria as appropriate difficulty and satisfactory discriminative power.

> "For the whole item bank (CSEBench), the mean item difficulty was 0.486 and the mean discrimination index was 0.324."

### Shangchao Min 2026 (2)

Shangchao Min, Shaonan Wang, Xinyu Gao, Hui Wang, Zhiling Jin, and 2 more. (2026). Standardized Assessment of LLM English Proficiency. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8820245/v1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the two large-scale diagnostic forms. The article reports Cronbach's α of "0.74 for the college section and 0.82 for the junior high section", both exceeding the commonly accepted 0.70 threshold for educational assessments.

> "Test reliability, as measured by Cronbach's α, was 0.74 for the college section and 0.82 for the junior high section."
