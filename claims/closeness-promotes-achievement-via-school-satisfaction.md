## Evidence

### Kiuru 2020

Kiuru, N., Wang, M.-T., Salmela-Aro, K., Kannas, L., Ahonen, T., & Hirvonen, R. (2020). Associations between Adolescents' Interpersonal Relationships, School Well-being, and Academic Achievement during Educational Transitions. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-019-01184-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Combined cross-lagged SEM with academic achievement (N = 848). Table 3 prints indirect effects closeness-to-parents→satisfaction→achievement = 0.012* (SE 0.006) and closeness-to-friends→satisfaction→achievement = 0.022* (SE 0.011), confirmed by bootstrapping.

> "the adolescents' high closeness to their parents and friends in the fall of the sixth grade indirectly promoted the subsequent academic achievement in the spring of the seventh grade through increased school satisfaction in the fall of the seventh grade"
