# Kindergarten children's non-symbolic addition performance correlates with end-of-year mathematics achievement, independent of literacy (Experiment 1)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gilmore Camilla K. 2010

Gilmore Camilla K., McCarthy Shannon E., Spelke Elizabeth S. (2010). Non-symbolic arithmetic abilities and achievement in the first year of formal schooling in mathematics. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.02.002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 1 tested 41 kindergarten children from a low- to middle-class community on a dot-array approximate addition task in March, then used the school's end-of-year mathematics and literacy tests. The correlation with mathematics "remained after correction for differences in literacy performance (r = .530, df = 37, p = .001)".

> "children’s performance of non-symbolic addition only correlated significantly with their performance on the mathematics test (r = .587, df = 38, p < .001; Figure 2b), not with their performance on the literacy test (r = .297, df = 38, p = .063"
