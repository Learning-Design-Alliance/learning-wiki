## Evidence

### Russo and Hopkins 2017

James Russo, Sarah Hopkins. (2017). How does lesson structure shape teacher perceptions of teaching with challenging tasks? Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(1), 30-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1152737

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview-based finding: Rachel attributed persistence to students' uncertainty about how to proceed, while Polly emphasised a psychological aspect — that completing the challenge first made the rest of the lesson feel manageable, giving students an incentive to persist.

> "There was also a perception amongst teachers that the Task -First Approach helped to build student persistence."
