### Esposito 2026

Esposito, N., Tricarico, A., Porzio, L., Aghazadeh Ardebili, A., & Stella, M. (2026). Math Education Digital Shadows for Investigating Learning with GenAI: Mathematics Performance, Anxiety, and Confidence in LLMs. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27618

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Task 3 behavioural forma mentis network analysis of semantic associations and valence ratings for 50 cue words. The article reports negative associations anchoring mathematics in methodological terms such as "algorithm", "computation", and "equation", and states the divergence is a persistent pattern across 14 different LLMs.

> "The LLM-generated network in Fig. 4a reproduces the human bias/cognitive dissonance observed by Stella and colleagues38: like human students, also digital shadows cognitively associate positive perceptions of science with negative perceptions of its building blocks, such as mathematics."
