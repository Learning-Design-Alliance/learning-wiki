## Evidence

### Witti MJ 2026

Witti MJ, Coleman J-C, Richters C, Schönberger J, de Ruijter M-A, Stortz A, Bauer M, Kiriakidou R, Stadler M. (2026). Simulation-Based Radiology Training for Medical Technical Assistant Trainees: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/97489

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 50 German MTA trainees completing a digital radiology simulation. A mixed-design ANOVA on the Spearman footrule distance composite score found a significant main effect of time, "indicating effective learning across both groups."

> "Trainees showed significant improvement in procedural sequencing from pretest to posttest (F1,48=22.05; P<.001; η2=0.103), indicating effective learning across both groups."
