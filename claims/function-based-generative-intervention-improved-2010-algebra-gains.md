## Evidence

### Laxman 2012

Kumar Laxman. (2012). Conceptualizing Alternative Ways of Curricular Teaching Through Technology. li-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101699

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-post study in two Singapore government schools; experimental classes (8) taught function-based networked generative activities, control classes (7) traditional algebra. ANOVA on gain scores showed "a significant treatment effect of the intervention on 2010 test score gains" with printed effect size partial Eta Square = 0.101.

> "The ANOVA test showed a significant treatment effect of the intervention on 2010 test score gains (F(1, 527) = 59.2, p< 0.001, partial Eta Square = 0.101."
