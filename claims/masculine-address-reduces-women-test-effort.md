## Evidence

### Tamar Kricheli-Katz and Tali Regev 2021

Tamar Kricheli-Katz and Tali Regev. (2021). The effect of language on performance: do gendered languages fail women in maths? npj Science of Learning 6:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00087-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time-on-test analysis in the main experiment, restricted to participants who answered all questions, shows women spent significantly less time than men when both were addressed in the masculine (p = 0.019); under feminine address the difference was not significant (p = 0.296). Regression results reported separately in supplementary tables.

> "In fact, when comparing women and men who were both addressed in the masculine, we ﬁnd that women spent signiﬁcantly less time on their maths test relative to men ( t-test, p = 0.019)."
