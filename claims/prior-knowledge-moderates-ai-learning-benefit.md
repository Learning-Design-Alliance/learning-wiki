`q2 i?` When participants learned a topic outside their best field, accuracy in the Active section lowered by 0.109 (significant at 0.05 level); age and manual-writing score positively and significantly affected the correctness boost (0.008 and 0.154). [→ QiRui Ju 2023](#qirui-ju-2023)

Regression on the correctness boost in the Active section (Table 4) of the pilot experiment. The article also reports that "Both age and the writing score in the manual section have a positive significant impact" (0.008 and 0.154), and that coding skill was not significant.

> "When participants were trying to learn a topic that was not their best field, their accuracy lowered by 0.109, which is significant at 0.05 level."
