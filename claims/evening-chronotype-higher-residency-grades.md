## Evidence

### Yang Yi 2025

Yang Yi, Zhu Yanxia, Ni Jianqiang, Yan Jiang, Yin Jun, Yang Le, Li Hao. (2025). Impact of circadian rhythm preference on performance in China's standardized residency training: The mediating role of loneliness. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331551

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational study of 198 residents at one Chinese hospital; generalized linear model with binomial logit link predicting high vs. low grades, FDR-adjusted. The article reports "evening type (OR: 15.431, 95% CI: 3.431–69.395, p < 0.001)" and morning type OR 0.057.

> "Our analysis revealed that the evening type (OR: 15.431, 95% CI: 3.431–69.395, p < 0.001) and morning type (OR: 0.057, 95% CI: 0.022–0.149, p < 0.001) were significantly associated with standardized training grades after adjusting for age and gender"
