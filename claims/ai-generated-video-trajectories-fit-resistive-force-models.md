### Laura B. Alvarado-Cruz 2026

Laura B. Alvarado-Cruz, Josep Ll. Suñer, Pedro Yuste, Juan C. Castro-Palacio, Juan A. Monsoriu, and Francisco M. Muñoz-Pérez. (2026). From Prompts to Physical Laws: A Generative AI Workflow for Engineering Physics Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19400

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical fitting results reported in the Results and discussion section for three AI-generated scenarios (sliding block, sphere in glycerin, skydiver), each fitted to its analytical model via Excel Solver. The article reports that "the coefficients of determination exceedR 2 >0.99" across all three regimes.

> "In all cases, the recovered parameters fall within ranges reported in the literature, and the coefficients of determination exceedR 2 >0.99, indicating a strong agreement between the synthetic trajectories and the analytical models."
