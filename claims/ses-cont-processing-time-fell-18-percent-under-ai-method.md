`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational before-and-after comparison of official SEI-GDF statistics for SES/CONT, consolidated in the unit's Executive Management Report, comparing 2024 (first year under the method) with 2023. The report shows an "18.2% in the aggregate" reduction; output per case remained stable (2.78 vs 2.77).

> "The unit’s central indicator, average processing time, fell from 17 days, 22 hours, and 11 minutes (17.92 days) in 2023 to 14 days, 15 hours, and 44 minutes (14.66 days) in 2024. The reduction was 3.26 days per case, or 18.2% in the aggregate."
