Evidence¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2024). Self-regulated learning strategies in continuing education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100629

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

Pooled three-level random-effects meta-analyses across the included CE studies for learner-related factors. The review reports that "No significant relationships between SRL strategies and the learner-related variables age, educational level, and job tenure were detected," while prior knowledge and CE experience correlations were "significant but weak."

"No significant relationships between SRL strategies and the learner-related variables age, educational level, and job tenure were detected. The average correlations for prior knowledge (r= .05) and CE experience (r= .08) were significant but weak."
