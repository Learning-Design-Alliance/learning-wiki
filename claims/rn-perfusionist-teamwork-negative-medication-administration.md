Melnikov Semyon, Abed Elhai Reem, Hijazi Nashaat, Zaid Nagah, Furmanov Alex. (2026). Associations Between Perceived Knowledge, Interprofessional Collaboration, Autonomy and the Frequency of Nursing Activities in Paediatric Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation: A Cross‐Sectional Study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the multivariable models of the 83-nurse cross-sectional study, RN–perfusionist teamwork was "negatively associated with medication and blood administration through the ECMO circuit". The authors interpret this as possibly reflecting task specialisation whereby perfusionists assume primary responsibility for circuit-related procedures.

> "RN–perfusionist teamwork was negatively associated with medication and blood administration through the ECMO circuit (B = −0.29, 95% CI [−0.50, −0.08], p = 0.008)"
