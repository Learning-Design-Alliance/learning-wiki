### Ertesvåg 2026

Ertesvåg, S. K., Vaaland, G. S., Tvedt, M. S., Virtanen, T. E., Idsoe, T., Panayiotou, M., Lerang, M. S., Sølvik, R. M., & Lerkkanen, M.-K. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized trial in which 98 upper secondary teachers from 12 Norwegian schools were randomly assigned to INTERACT coaching or practice-as-usual, with 1,341 students reporting outcomes. The article reports "small effects, with statistically significant improvements in the three TSI domains"; no effect size value is printed in the available text.

> "Multilevel intent-to-treat (ITT) analysis indicated that the intervention had small effects, with statistically significant improvements in the three TSI domains: emotional support, classroom organization, and instructional support."
