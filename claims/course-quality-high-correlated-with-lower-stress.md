## Evidence

### Hundertmark Jan 2019

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Tutors' self-ratings of course quality on a VAS-type scale, compared between experience groups with an F-test. The article reports no significant difference between senior and novice tutors.

> "The tutors evaluated their courses’ quality generally as high, M = 7.76 (SD = 1.36). Overall course quality ratings did not differ between senior and novice tutors, F(1, 56) = 1.42, p = .24"

### Hundertmark Jan 2019 (2)

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of course-quality ratings against stress measures in the same sample. The article reports significant correlations with subjective measures (rVAS = −.20, rPA = .34, rNA = −.29) and none with physiological measures.

> "however, they were significantly correlated with subjective stress measures, rVAS = −.20, rPA = .34, rNA = −.29, but not with physiological stress measures, rCortisol = −.09, rRMSSD = .02"
