## Evidence

### Katalin Kopasz 2011

Katalin Kopasz, Péter Makra and Zoltán Gingl. (2011). Edaq530: a transparent, open-end and open-source measurement solution in natural science education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/32/2/020](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/32/2/020)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observational account of an optional secondary-school physics class run parallel to the teacher training, in which students carried out experiments with Edaq530. The article reports that "students can learn the use of the device with ease" and were motivated to be creative; one student idea was applying the photogate to examine the picket fence ruler's transmission. No systematic measures are reported.

> "Our experiences have shown that students can learn the use of the device with ease, and they are motivated to be creative in their experiments, coming up with ideas we had not considered before."
