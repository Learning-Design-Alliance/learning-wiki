## Evidence

### Rajan Kiran 2022

Rajan Kiran, Pandit Anand S. (2022). Comparing computer-assisted learning activities for learning clinical neuroscience: a randomized control trial. BMC Medical Education 22:522. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03578-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey item on aspects aiding learning (Table 2): eModule group percentages were interactivity 14 (88%) and clinical case studies 13 (81%); graphics (75% vs 81%, p = 0.7), content (69% vs 56%, p = 0.5) and structure (56% vs 56%, p = 1) showed "no significant difference" between groups.

> "In the eModule group, most participants found the clinical case studies and interactivity aided their learning and a smaller majority found the graphics, content and structure useful."
