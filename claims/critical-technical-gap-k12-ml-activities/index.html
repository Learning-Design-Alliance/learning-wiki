Evidence¶

Pierre Haritz 2026¶

Pierre Haritz, Hendrik Krone, and Thomas Liebig. (2026). Addressing Trust in AI Systems through Education: A Didactic Perspective. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02453 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.02453

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Systematic review of 133 K-12 ML activities analyzing the learning contexts in which ML is introduced. The review reports the co-occurrence of technical and societal perspectives at "roughly 3% of cases" and, separately, only four activities combining both perspectives; no inferential statistics are printed.

"Across a corpus of 133 activities, a technical and a societal perspective on the same algorithm co-occur in roughly 3% of cases."
