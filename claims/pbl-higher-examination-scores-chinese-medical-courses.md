`q3 i2` Across pooled controlled studies, the PBL experimental group produced significantly higher examination scores than the LBL control group (SMD = 0.82, 95% CI 0.63–1.01). [→ Zhang Yanqi 2015](#zhang-yanqi-2015)

## Evidence

### Zhang Yanqi 2015

Zhang Yanqi, Zhou Liang, Liu Xiaoyu, Liu Ling, Wu Yazhou, Zhao Zengwei, Yi Dali, Yi Dong. (2015). The Effectiveness of the Problem-Based Learning Teaching Model for Use in Introductory Chinese Undergraduate Medical Courses: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120884

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 23 studies reporting examination scores, using a random effects model after high heterogeneity (I2 = 86%, P<0.001). The result shows "significantly higher examination scores than the LBL control group" with SMD = 0.82, a medium-to-large standardized effect.

> "The analytical results reveal that the experimental group produced significantly higher examination scores than the LBL control group [SMD: 0.82, 95% CI: (0.63, 1.01)]"
