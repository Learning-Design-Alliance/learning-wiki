### Cristian Candia 2022

Cristian Candia, Alejandra Maldonado-Trapp, Karla Lobos, Fernando Peña, and Carola Bruna. (2022). Disadvantaged students increase their academic performance through collective intelligence exposure in emergency remote learning due to COVID 19. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.05621](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.05621)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Natural language processing analysis (Word2Vec cosine similarity between posts and department content) of forum texts; the article reports low high-school GPA first-year students are exposed to high content-intensive texts, with no effect size printed.

> "We ﬁnd that ﬁrst-year students with low high-school GPA are exposed, on average, to high content-intensive texts."

### Cristian Candia 2022 (2)

Cristian Candia, Alejandra Maldonado-Trapp, Karla Lobos, Fernando Peña, and Carola Bruna. (2022). Disadvantaged students increase their academic performance through collective intelligence exposure in emergency remote learning due to COVID 19. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.05621](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.05621)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical models replicated with an added interaction between node degree and average content exposure (Tables S7–S9, Fig. 4); the article reports the strongest node-degree–GPA relation in the high content-exposure panel, with no effect size printed.

> "We ﬁnd that ﬁrst-year students who are exposed to high content-intensity posts (high panel) show the strongest relation between the node degree and ﬁnal university GPA"
