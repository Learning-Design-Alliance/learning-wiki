# Tutors' learning scores rose from pre-test to post-test in the Jeremiah:Alexis order but fell slightly in the Alexis:Jeremiah order, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-test/post-test study, tutors' learning scores rose from pre-test to post-test in the Jeremiah:Alexis order (M = 0.12, p = .001) and fell slightly in the Alexis:Jeremiah order (M = −0.01, p = .846). [→ Danielle R. Thomas 2025](#danielle-r-thomas-2025)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc contrasts on marginal means from the mixed-effects ANOVA of 81 tutors, examining pretest-to-posttest differences across the two scenario orders. The article reports significant gains in the Jeremiah:Alexis order (M = 0.12, SE = 0.04, t(79) = 3.07, p = .001) and non-significant gains in the Alexis:Jeremiah order (M = −0.01, p = .846); no effect size is printed.
