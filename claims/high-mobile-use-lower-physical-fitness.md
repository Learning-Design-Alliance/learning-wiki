## Evidence

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA of Eurofit battery marks (0–10, rated by age and gender) in 501 students across CERM groups. Four fitness tests showed significant group main effects favoring LMP and MMP over HMP. No effect size printed for this omnibus comparison.

> "significant differences were described for the abdominals test (p = 0.001), medicine-ball throw (p = 0.001), broad jump (p = 0.011), and trunk-flexion test (p = 0.005) with higher values in the LMP and MMP than the HMP group"
