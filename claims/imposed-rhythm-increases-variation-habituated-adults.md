## Evidence

### West 1918

West, P. V. (1918). The Relation of Rhythm to the Handwriting Movement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22The+Relation+of+Rhythm+to+the+Handwriting+Movement%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

First kymographic experiment, adult groups, comparing spontaneous versus imposed rhythm in finger and arm movement. The article attributes the effect to adults having acquired rhythmic movement as a comparatively fixed norm of performance, and adds that better writers showed greater adaptation.

> "For adults, who have already acquired a comparatively high degree of rhythmic movement, probably as a more or less fixed norm of performance, the amount of variation is greater in both finger and arm movement when the rhythmic beat is followed than when the action is natural."
