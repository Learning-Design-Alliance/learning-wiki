## Evidence

### Talamini and Takashima 2008

Lucia M. Talamini, Ingrid L.C. Nieuwenhuis, Atsuko Takashima, and Ole Jensen. (2008). Sleep directly following learning benefits consolidation of spatial associative memory. Learning & Memory 15:233–237. https://doi.org/10.1101/lm.771608

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null decay-inference analysis comparing the second half of the sleep–wake interval with the 12-h wake condition; the article reports the difference "was not significant", so equivalence was not established.

> "(Fig. 3B) was not significant (t = 1.57, df = 35, P = 0.13). This indicates that sleep does not substantially reduce forgetting during the subsequent day, that is, we were not able to establish that sleep protects the memory trace against interference."
