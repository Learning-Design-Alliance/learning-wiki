### Zabludoff 2026

Zabludoff, A., Chuang, C., Johnson, P. T., Liu, Y., Martinez, B. B., Shah, N., Steffes, L., & Weible, G. G. (2026). Can LLMs Produce Original Astronomy Research in a Semester? A Graduate Class Experiment. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.25984](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.25984)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Course-level case report of a Fall 2025 graduate Galaxies class in which seven first-year PhD students used LLMs for individual research projects, typically 5–10 hours each. The authors report that "all students completed a draft paper on an unsolved problem related to galaxies" and that "About half thought that the models saved them time." No effect sizes are reported.

> "While all students completed a draft paper on an unsolved problem related to galaxies by semester's end, their impressions of the models' value varied. About half thought that the models saved them time."
