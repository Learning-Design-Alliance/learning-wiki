## Evidence

### Rezaei Behrooz 2020

Rezaei Behrooz, Falahati Juliana, Beheshtizadeh Raziyeh. (2020). Stress, stressors and related factors in clinical learning of midwifery students in Iran: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1970-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null results from t-tests and ANOVAs across the 70-student survey; printed statistics include e.g. F = 2.822, P = 0.067 for interest and t = 1.469, p = 0.147 for instructor employment type. No effect sizes are printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "PSS’ score was not significantly different based on characteristics of the students (i.e. age, semester, interest in the field of study, GPA, place of residence, marital status, and the number of clinical units completed), the instructors (type of employment, education level, educational work experience and clinical work experience) and the clinical environment (type of hospital and type of ward)"
