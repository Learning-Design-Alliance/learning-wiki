`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal self-assessment of 151 newly graduated registered nurses using the Nurse Competence Scale at four time points over 5 months. The study reports an initial dip then rise in overall perceived competence, with the quote noting scores "from 64.41 out of 100 in the first day of work to 61.82 after 15 days".

> "variations emerged in the NCS scores from 64.41 out of 100 in the first day of work to 61.82 after 15 days, reaching 69.25 and 73.21 at 3 and 5 months"
