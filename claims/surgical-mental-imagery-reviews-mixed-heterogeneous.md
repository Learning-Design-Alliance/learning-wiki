## Evidence

### Souiki Tarik 2021

Souiki Tarik, Benzagmout Mohammed, Alami Badreeddine, Ibn Majdoub Karim, Toughrai Imane, Mazaz Khalid, Boujraf Saïd. (2021). Impact of mental imagery on enhancing surgical skills learning in novice's surgeons: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02987-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Narrative review section of the article reporting prior literature: the review attributes these findings to a 2015 systematic review and meta-analysis (Rao et al.) and a 2017 systematic review of 14 studies with a ratio of 11 out of 14 positive. The article notes the meta-analysis should be interpreted with high caution because populations and assessment tools were extremely heterogeneous; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Five of them objectified a beneficial effect of MI on the learning of surgical technical skills while four studies were negative. A meta-analysis based on this population showed the difficulty to demonstrate a superiority of MI."
