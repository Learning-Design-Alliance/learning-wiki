## Evidence

### Mori Takashi 2022

Mori Takashi, Ikeda Koji, Takeshita Nobuyoshi, Teramura Koichi, Ito Masaaki. (2022). Validation of a novel virtual reality simulation system with the focus on training for surgical dissection during laparoscopic sigmoid colectomy. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01441-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of 21 novices (fewer than 100 laparoscopic surgeries) and 18 experts (more than 100) on the same VR dissection task, rated with GOALS by three blinded raters using Wilcoxon rank sum tests. No statistically significant differences appeared in depth perception, bimanual dexterity, tissue handling, or total score.

> "The experts were significantly superior to the novices in one item of GOALS, efficiency ([4 (4–5) vs. 4 (3–4)], with a 95% CI, p = 0.042) (Table 3)."
