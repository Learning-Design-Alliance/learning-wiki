## Evidence

### Rojas 2016

Natalia Rojas. (2016). The Association of Peer Behavioral Regulation with School Readiness Skills in Preschool. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567502

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Two-level hierarchical linear models on data from an RCT of ParentCorps in New York City public preschools (10 schools, 99 classrooms over four cohorts), predicting spring Speed DIAL-3 outcomes from fall classroom-level engagement. The article reports "d = 0.71, p = 0.008" for motor skills and d = 0.56 for concept skills.

> "The results of RQ1 (Table 1; Block 1) indicated that engagement was a significant predictor of children's residualized spring school readiness skills (d = 0.48, p = 0.029). Peers' engagement significantly predicted children's spring motor skills (d = 0.71, p = 0.008) and spring concept skills (d = 0.56, p = 0.032)."
