Second matched study using propensity score matching to create a well-matched control group for the 1,060 6th-8th grade students in classes of a second cohort of e2L teachers. Students "outperformed students in comparison schools" with the printed effect size +0.13.

"Comparing differences on end-of-the-year STAAR math outcomes showed students of teachers who received e2L coaching outperformed students in comparison schools with no e2L coaching (effect size = +0.13)."
