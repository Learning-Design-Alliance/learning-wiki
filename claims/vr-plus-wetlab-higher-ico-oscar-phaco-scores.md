## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trainees randomized to Eyesi virtual reality plus wet laboratory training scored higher on ICO OSCAR phaco assessment of their first 3 supervised surgeries than wet-lab-only trainees (49.5 vs 39.0, P = .02) and control trainees (P < .001). [→ Ng DS 2023](#ng-ds-2023)

## Evidence

### Ng DS 2023

Ng DS, Yip BHK, Young AL, Yip WWK, Lam NM, Li KK, Ko ST, Chan WH, Aryasit O, Sikder S, Ferris JD, Pang CP, Tham CC. (2023). Cost-effectiveness of virtual reality and wet laboratory cataract surgery simulation. Medicine 2023;102:40(e35067). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035067

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial with 8 trainees per randomized arm and 6 controls; two masked graders scored 3 video-recorded surgeries per trainee with a generalizability coefficient of 0.83. Group A mean was "49.5 ± SD 9.8" versus Group B 39.0 ± SD 15.8 (P = .02); both randomized groups outperformed controls (32.5 ± SD 12.1).

> "Results from the randomized trial indicated that trainees in Group A (Eyesi + Wet lab) had superior mean total ICO OSCAR phaco scores compared with Group B (Wet lab) (49.5 ± SD 9.8 vs 39.0 ± SD 15.8, P = .02)."
