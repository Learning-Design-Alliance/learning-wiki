Tae Lee Lee 2023¶

Tae Lee Lee, Hanall Lee and Nyeonju Kang. (2023). A meta-analysis showing improved cognitive performance in healthy young adults with transcranial alternating current stimulation. npj Science of Learning 8:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00152-9

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Descriptive synthesis across the 56 qualified studies: 23 studies reported side effects (itching in 10, tingling in 11, phosphenes in 8) and 11 confirmed none; "~46.2% of participants ... may experience potential side effects of tACS protocols". 22 studies did not mention side effects.

"In the 34 studies, ~46.2% of participants (i.e., number of participants from studies that reported the presence of side effects / total number of participants from studies that reported presence or absence of side effects × 100) may experience potential side effects of tACS protocols"
