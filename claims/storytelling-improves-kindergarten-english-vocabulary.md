## Evidence

### Abasi and Soori 2014

Maasumeh Abasi, Afshin Soori. (2014). Is Storytelling Effective in Improving the English Vocabulary Learning among Iranian Children in Kindergartens? International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 2(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.2n.3p.7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-test post-test quasi-experiment with 20 five-year-old children (9 boys, 11 girls) in a private kindergarten in Kerman, Iran, taught the storybook Three Bears. The posttest mean was 23.40 versus a pretest mean of 19.30; the article reports "a significant increase" with no effect size printed.

> "Moreover, in comparison to the pre-test, the mean of the group in the post-test showed a significant increase. This indicates that storytelling is effective in improving the children’s vocabulary learning."
