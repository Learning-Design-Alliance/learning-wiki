## Evidence

### Zeeni Carine 2025

Zeeni Carine, Ibrahim Halah, Zgheib Nathalie Khoueiry, Saliba Elie, Moukarzel Marc, Obeid Reine, Abi Younes Nadine, Siddik Midhat, Honein Abou Haidar Gladys, Khoury Fatima Msheik-El. (2025). Exploring the impact of an interventional approach to resident teaching in anesthesiology clerkships. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2500555

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey of medical students after an anesthesiology residents' teaching workshop; 77/106 students responded pre and 55/58 post. Independent-samples t-test showed a significant increase in overall quality-of-teaching means, "(2.98 ± 0.53 vs. 3.40 ± 0.46, p < 0.001)".

> "There was a significant difference between the overall means for the quality of teaching as perceived by medical students before and after the educational intervention program (2.98 ± 0.53 vs. 3.40 ± 0.46, p < 0.001)"
