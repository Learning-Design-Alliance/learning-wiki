Ozbey Furkan, Yasa Yasin. (2026). Impact of self-confidence and personality on dental students’ radiographic interpretation skills. BMC Medical Education, 26, 207. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08481-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive per-case results from 83 students interpreting 11 panoramic radiographs of histopathologically confirmed lesions. The article reports accuracy "for case 11 (stafne bone cavity; 91.7%)" versus 20.5% for cases 6 and 10; confidence was also highest for case 11 (81.9%) and lowest for case 6 (19.3%).

> "The highest diagnostic accuracy was observed for case 11 (stafne bone cavity; 91.7%), while the lowest accuracy rates were found in case 6 (adenomatoid odontogenic tumor) and case 10 (odontogenic keratocyst), each with 20.5% correct responses."
