## Evidence

### Gulley 2012

Gulley, B. (2012). Feedback on developmental writing students' first drafts. Journal of Developmental Education, 36(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035676

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Second mixed design ANCOVA using Editor software error counts as the dependent variable, with COMPASS scores as covariate; the within-subjects test for Editor scores and treatment condition was "not statistically significant, F = 1.014, p < .368".

> "Furthermore, when scores for the three feedback groups derived from the Editor program, designed to look at grammar and style, were compared, they were also not significant at the .05 level, indicating no interaction among groups."
