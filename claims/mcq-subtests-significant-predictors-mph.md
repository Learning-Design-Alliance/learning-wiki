## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Each of the five subtests (Epidemiology, Organization in Health Care, Scientific Method, Health and Human Nutrition, Social Science) significantly predicted first-year GPA, with standardized betas ranging from 0.069 to 0.114. [→ Panczyk M 2017](#panczyk-m-2017)

## Evidence

### Panczyk M 2017

Panczyk M, Juszczyk G, Zarzeka A, Samoliński Ł, Belowska J, Cieślak I, Gotlib J. (2017). Evidence-based selection process to the Master of Public Health program at Medical University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1007-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on 605 students' data. All five subtests were significant predictors of first-year GPA; "The Health and Human Nutrition subtest was found to be the strongest predictor of success (βstand. = 0.114)" and Social Science the lowest (βstand. = 0.069). Each additional entrance exam point gave a higher first-year GPA by 0.013 to 0.026 depending on subtest.

> "The Health and Human Nutrition subtest was found to be the strongest predictor of success (βstand. = 0.114), whereas for the Social science subtest this predictive capacity was the lowest (βstand. = 0.069)."
