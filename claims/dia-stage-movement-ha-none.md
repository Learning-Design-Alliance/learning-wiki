## Evidence

### Antoniou 2011

Antoniou, P., Kyriakides, L., & Creemers, B. (2011). Investigating the Effectiveness of a Dynamic Integrated Approach to Teacher Professional Development. ceps Journal, 1(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130737

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group-randomised intervention study of 130 Cypriot teachers: observational data at baseline and end of year were Rasch-analysed to classify teachers into stages. The article reports that "none of the teachers of the group employing the HA managed to move from one stage to another", while 21 of 65 DIA teachers moved up one stage.

> "By comparing the classification of teachers into different stages at the beginning and at the end of the intervention, it was found that none of the teachers of the group employing the HA managed to move from one stage to another."
