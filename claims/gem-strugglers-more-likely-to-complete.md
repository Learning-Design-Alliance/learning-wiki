## Evidence

### Garrud Paul 2012

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cohort comparison of GEM (n = 362 admitted, 15 did not complete) against previously reported Nottingham undergraduate figures. The article reports the chi-square test and that "a higher proportion of GEM strugglers completing their course"; overall attrition was 15/362 (4.1%) versus 6.1% in the UG programme.

> "There was a significant difference in the proportions that completed or did not complete the course between the GEM and UG courses (Chi square = 5.31, df 1, p = 0.021): a higher proportion of GEM strugglers completing their course."
