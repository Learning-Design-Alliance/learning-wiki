Evidence¶

Muhammad Irfan Arif and Munawar S. Mirza 2017¶

Muhammad Irfan Arif and Munawar S. Mirza. (2017). Effectiveness of an Intervention Program in Fostering Academic Resilience of Students at Risk of Failure at Secondary School Level. Bulletin of Education and Research, 39(1), 251-264. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1210192

q3 · i? · causal · r2

By-factor t-tests on gain scores of the same 64-student pretest-posttest control group experiment. Each factor (creativity, self-esteem, self-efficacy, internal locus of control, problem solving, autonomy, sense of humor, stress coping, sense of purpose, teacher-student relationship) showed "significant difference" with p = .001 and df = 62; experimental gains ranged from 6.62 to 8.07 versus control gains of 0.58 to 1.92.

"Table 6 indicates significant difference between the mean gain scores of the control and experimental groups’ students on each of the ten factors of resilience."
