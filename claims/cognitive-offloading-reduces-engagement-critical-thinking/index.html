The article reports, citing Gerlich (2025), a significant negative correlation between frequent AI tool use and critical thinking abilities, with "cognitive offloading as a mediating factor". No correlation coefficient is printed.

Shan Li 2026 (2)¶

Shan Li, Juan Zheng. (2026). Generativism: Toward a Learning Theory for the Age of Generative Artificial Intelligence. (No DOI or URL: the ORCID link recorded at ingest was malformed, and no record was found on Crossref or DataCite, 2026-10-15.)

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The article reports, citing Stadler et al. (2024), a comparison of university students using an LLM versus conventional web search on a scientific inquiry task: the AI condition showed "significantly lower mental effort" and "reasoning of significantly lower quality". No effect size is printed.

"Stadler et al. (2024) compared university students who used a LLM with students who used conventional web search during a scientific inquiry task. Students in the AI condition reported significantly lower mental effort, yet they produced reasoning of significantly lower quality."
