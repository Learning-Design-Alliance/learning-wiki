`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison of perception scores across three usage frequency groups (high, moderate, low) with post hoc Tukey HSD tests. The article reports "students with high usage (many times/week) expressed significantly more positive perceptions than those with low usage (Less than once weekly) (p < 0.05)" for queries/prompts, with no low-medium or medium-high differences.
