Henderson 2020¶

Henderson, N., Kumaran, V., Min, W., Mott, B., Wu, Z., Boulden, D., Lord, T., Reichsman, F., Dorsey, C., Wiebe, E., & Lester, J. (2020). Enhancing Student Competency Models for Game-Based Learning with a Hybrid Stealth Assessment Framework. Proceedings of The 13th International Conference on Educational Data Mining (EDM 2020), 92-103. https://educationaldatamining.org/EDM2020/proceedings/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Held-out test set evaluation of multiple regression model types across 15 genetics concepts in Geniventure gameplay data from 316 students. The single-task Random Forest achieved the highest mean R² but was "only the optimal model for 7 out of the 15 total concepts."

"The best performing model in terms of average R2 value across all concepts was the single-task Random Forest; however, it was only the optimal model for 7 out of the 15 total concepts."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • GRU is the lowest-performing model across both single-task and multi-task competency modeling, likely due to its two gating units versus LSTMs' three
  • Overlapping concepts (sharing post-test questions with other concepts) yield higher student scores and higher model R², and multi-task modeling across all concepts is detrimental for these concepts
  • Concepts associated with only a single post-test question yield markedly low predictive performance across all modeling approaches
  • Sequential deep learning models outperform static models for concepts mapped to multiple post-test questions — related
  • A hybrid stealth assessment framework selecting the optimal model per concept outperforms any single homogeneous modeling technique for predicting student competencies — a broader claim this one bears on
