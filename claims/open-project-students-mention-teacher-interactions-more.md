## Evidence

### F. Bouquet 2015

F. Bouquet, J. Bobroff, M. Fuchs-Gallezot, and L. Maurines. (2015). Project-based physics labs using low-cost open-source hardware. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06659)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Appreciation question in the same open-ended survey (17 open-project, 21 focused-lab answers). The authors attribute this to the mentoring dynamic, since every setup is as new to the teachers as to the students.

> "The interactions with the teachers were also often mentioned: in 41% of their answers, compared to 19% for the focused lab students, whereas the teachers spent as much time with both type of students."
