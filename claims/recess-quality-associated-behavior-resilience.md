### Howie Erin K. 2023

Howie Erin K., Perryman Kristi L., Moretta Joseph, Cameron Laura. (2023). Educational outcomes of recess in elementary school children: A mixed-methods systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294340

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cross-sectional study (Massey, 2021) of 352 3rd and 5th graders with a mean of 29.5 minutes per recess, measuring classroom behavior with the BASC-3, found recess quality associated with adaptive behavior and recess time associated with lower externalizing problems and bullying. No effect size printed.

> "Recess quality was associated with adaptive classroom behavior, executive functioning problems, resilience, and emotional self-control. Recess time was associated with lower levels of externalizing problems and bullying."
