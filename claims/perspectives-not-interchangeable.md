# Perspectives on teacher behavior are not interchangeable: combining observer and student perspectives on agency approximates the teacher perspective for teacher emotions, while students' own perspective is irreplaceable for student emotions

## Evidence

### Donker 2021

Donker, M. H., van Vemde, L., Hessen, D. J., van Gog, T., & Mainhard, T. (2021). Observational, student, and teacher perspectives on interpersonal teacher behavior: Shared and unique associations with teacher and student emotions. Learning and Instruction, vol. 73, 101414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101414

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the shared/unique variance results (Table 2, Fig. 3): observer and student perspectives on agency combined were "alternatives for teacher report" for teacher emotions, while for student emotions the students' own perspective was irreplaceable. The authors conclude different perspectives are not interchangeable.

> "For teacher emotions, including the combination of observer and student perspectives on teacher agency explained almost the same amount of variance compared to only the teacher perspective, and these could thus be seen as alternatives for teacher report. For student emotions, students’ own perspective was irreplaceable, and the observer and teacher perspective could only add little to this perspective."
