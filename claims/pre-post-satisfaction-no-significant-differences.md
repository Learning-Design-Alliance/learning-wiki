Subgroup satisfaction analysis within the 2023 cohort (pre-class N = 19, post-class N = 20). The only Wilcoxon exception was Q7 (p = 0.048), where the Pre-class group reported a higher satisfaction mean (4.90 vs. 4.58), a delta of +0.32.

> "Within the 2023 P-I-T-D-S flipped classroom cohort, a subgroup analysis comparing the Pre-class group and the Post-class group showed no statistically significant differences in satisfaction scores based on chi-square tests across any of the 19 survey items (p > 0.05 for all items)."
