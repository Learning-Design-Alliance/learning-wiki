## Evidence

### Su Xiaoqun 2026

Su Xiaoqun, Hu Bing, Liu Chengcheng, Ren Hongmiao. (2026). Application of integrated whole-course clinical learning combined with case-based learning in undergraduate otolaryngology clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09871-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled educational intervention with 79 undergraduates in preexisting clerkship groups (IC n=39, LC n=40), group-level allocation. Mann–Whitney U test on the 4-point proficiency scale found "no significant difference in performance"; equivalence was not tested.

> "A comparative analysis of specialty physical examination proficiency between the IC group and the LC group revealed no significant difference in performance (median [IQR]: 3.04 [2.52–3.39] vs. 2.85 [2.38–3.17]; Mann–Whitney U = 98.50; adjusted p = 0.27)."
