# Single-course implementations of problem-driven learning show larger motivation effects than curriculum-wide implementations

## Evidence

### Wijnia 2024

Wijnia, L., Noordzij, G., Arends, L. R. A., Rikers, R. M. J. P., & Loyens, S. M. M. (2024). The Effects of Problem-Based, Project-Based, and Case-Based Learning on Students' Motivation: a Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09864-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

CHE-model moderator analysis of implementation level. The authors report "a moderate, positive effect size for single-course implementations and interventions compared to a trivial, positive effect for curriculum-wide implementations"; the three-level model test gave F = 3.33, p = 0.069, with curriculum d = 0.190 versus course d = 0.535 in Table 3.

> "we found a moderate, positive effect size for single-course implementations and interventions compared to a trivial, positive effect for curriculum-wide implementations."
