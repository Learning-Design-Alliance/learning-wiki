## Evidence

### Aly 2013

Aly, I. (2013). Performance in an online introductory course in a hybrid classroom setting. Canadian Journal of Higher Education, 43(2), 85-99. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1013589

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on twelve weekly assignment scores showed the hybrid mean of 8.73 exceeded the online mean of 8.06, with the article reporting the difference was "significantly higher than that in the online section at F-Value 5.995".

> "the average score on weekly assignments in the hybrid sections (8.73) was significantly higher than that in the online section (8.06) at F-Value 5.995"
