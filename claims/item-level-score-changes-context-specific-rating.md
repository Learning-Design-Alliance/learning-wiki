## Evidence

### Essers Geurt 2013

Essers Geurt, Kramer Anneke, Andriesse Boukje, van Weel Chris, van der Vleuten Cees, van Dulmen Sandra. (2013). Context factors in general practitioner - patient encounters and their impact on assessing communication skills - an exploratory study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-14-65

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level paired comparisons across the 40 consultations (Table 2) show "a significant increase was found in ten out of the thirteen mean item scores"; item 7 decreased significantly (p < .05) and items 2 and 6 were not significant. Effect sizes ranged from 0.15 to 0.87 across items.

> "As a result of applying the context-specific rating protocol, a significant increase was found in ten out of the thirteen mean item scores whereas one item (item 7) showed a decrease (Table 2). In the items 2 (Follow-up consultation) and 6 (Management) the difference in scores was not significant."
