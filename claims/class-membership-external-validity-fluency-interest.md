## Evidence

### Hart 2015

Hart, Sara A.; Logan, Jessica A.R.; Thompson, Lee; Kovas, Yulia; McLoughlin, Gráinne; & Petrill, Stephen A. (2015). A latent profile analysis of math achievement, numerosity, and math anxiety in twins. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000045

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc validity ANOVAs using class membership as the grouping variable found significant differences on the Woodcock-Johnson III math Fluency subtest and a three-item PISA-derived Interest in Math scale; the authors state the results offered some support for the validity of the classes.

> "Results from the two ANOVAs indicated that there was a statistically significant relation between class membership and math Fluency levels (F(4, 493) = 37.55, p < .0001), as well as between class membership and Interest in Math (F(4, 376) = 5.46, p = .0003)."
