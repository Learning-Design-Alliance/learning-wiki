## Evidence

### Jenna Van Sickle 2016

Jenna Van Sickle. (2016). Discrepancies between Student Perception and Achievement of Learning Outcomes in a Flipped Classroom. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 16, No. 2, April 2016, pp. 29-38. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v16i2.19216

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of course-evaluation item responses (1-5 scale) between flipped (N=42) and traditional (N=34) college algebra students taught by the same instructor; the flipped mean was 3.79 versus 4.29 for the item "The instructor encouraged my interest in the course," significant at "p = .008". No effect size is printed.

> "In a one-tailed t-test, this difference was statistically significant, with p = .008."
