## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Observation of clicker class sessions identified three distinct modes of clicker use differing in whether peer discussion was paired with clicker questions. [→ Lewin JD 2016](#lewin-jd-2016)

## Evidence

### Lewin JD 2016

Lewin JD, Vinson EL, Stetzer MR, Smith MK. (2016). A Campus-Wide Investigation of Clicker Implementation: The Status of Peer Discussion in STEM Classes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-10-0224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Classroom-observation classification of the 80 clicker class sessions. Peer Discussion sessions had at least one clicker episode with peer discussion; Individual Thinking sessions had none; Alternative Collaboration sessions had group work but not during clicker questions.

> "Taken together, these results indicate there are three different predominant modes of clicker use: Peer Discussion, Individual Thinking, and Alternative Collaboration."
