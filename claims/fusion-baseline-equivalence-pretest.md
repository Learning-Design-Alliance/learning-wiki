Mari G. Strand Cary, Ben Clarke, Christian T. Doabler, Keith Smolkowski, Hank Fien, and Scott K. Baker. (2017). A Practitioner Implementation of a Tier 2 First-Grade Mathematics Intervention. Learning Disability Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714715

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison of the 253-student sample's pretest means by condition (Table 1 shows Fusion students scored lower descriptively). The authors suggest greater-than-expected within-condition heterogeneity and note this makes the test of Fusion's effects more rigorous.

> "This was indeed the case (Table 1), but there were no statistically significant differences at pretest by condition for ProFusion (p = .0778), EN-CBM (p = .0789), or easyCBM (p = .5739)."
