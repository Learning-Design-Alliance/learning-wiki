## Evidence

### van Cappellen van Walsum AM 2022

van Cappellen van Walsum AM, Henssen DJHA. (2022). E-Learning Three-Dimensional Anatomy of the Brainstem: Impact of Different Microscopy Techniques and Spatial Ability. Anatomical Sciences Education 15(2):317-329. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2056

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Dependent t tests in the total group of 38 medical students after completing the PLI or LFB e-learning module: mean pre-intervention score was 49.87 ± 11.77% and mean post-intervention score was "77.89 ± 14.50%", a significant improvement (P < 0.001; Cohen's d = -2.13).

> "The mean post-intervention test score showed to be 77.89 ± 14.50%, indicating a significant improvement after learning brainstem anatomy through either e-learning module (P < 0.001; Cohen's d = -2.13)."

### van Cappellen van Walsum AM 2022 (2)

van Cappellen van Walsum AM, Henssen DJHA. (2022). E-Learning Three-Dimensional Anatomy of the Brainstem: Impact of Different Microscopy Techniques and Spatial Ability. Anatomical Sciences Education 15(2):317-329. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2056

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Dependent t tests on the one-week follow-up test in the same group: mean long-term score was "66.45 ± 16.23%", significantly above pre-intervention (Cohen's d = -1.18) and significantly below post-intervention (Cohen's d = 0.74).

> "The long-term test score showed to be 66.45 ± 16.23%, which indicated a significant improvement compared to the pre-intervention test score (P < 0.001; Cohen's d = -1.18) and a significant diminishment compared to the post-intervention test (P < 0.001; Cohen's d = 0.74)."
