## Subclaims
`q3 i3` After training, accuracy significantly improved and response time decreased compared to pre-test in both 75° and 150° tasks, with large effects (partial η2 = 0.842–0.856 for accuracy). [→ Linlin Dong 2025](#linlin-dong-2025)

## Evidence

### Linlin Dong 2025

Linlin Dong, Yufeng Ke, Xiaodong Zhu, Shuang Liu & Dong Ming. (2025). Long-term cognitive and neurophysiological effects of mental rotation training. npj Science of Learning 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00309-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pooled one-way repeated-measures bootstrap ANOVAs across the four test sessions in both tasks. The article reports "a signiﬁcant main effect of Time for all measures," e.g., 75° ACC F(3, 99) = 175.80, P < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.842, and post hoc tests showed accuracy improved at all post-tests versus pre-test regardless of task difficulty.

> "Bootstrap-based one-way repeated measures (RM) ANOVAs showed a signiﬁcant main effect of Time for all measures (75° ACC: F(3, 99) = 175.80, P < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.842; 75° RT: F(3, 99) = 196.04, P < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.856"
