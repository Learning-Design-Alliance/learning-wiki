### Puolakanaho 2018

Reducing Stress and Enhancing Academic Buoyancy among Adolescents Using a Brief Web-based Program Based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: A Randomized Controlled Trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mplus moderation analyses using the per-protocol sample (n = 205; poor academic skills n = 100, normally developing n = 105), conducted with separate and combined intervention groups contrasted with control. The article reports "all p-values > 0.05"; no effect sizes were printed.

> "The results showed that the students with poor academic skills did not differ from those with normally developed academic skills and who experienced changes in overall stress, school stress, and academic buoyancy (all p-values > 0.05)."
