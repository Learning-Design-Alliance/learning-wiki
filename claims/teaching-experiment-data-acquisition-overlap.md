The data acquisition section of the teaching experiment reports participation counts across five data sources: SDE interaction, 27 STACK questions over five weeks, manually graded exam questions, MPEX, and TCS. "73 students remain in the intersection" used for the unified analysis.

> "Out of these students, 145 started SDE, 147 completed the MPEX survey, 107 completed the TCS pre-questionnaire, and 93 completed the TCS post-questionnaire. We analyse the data from all of these activities in a unified manner, and 73 students remain in the intersection suitable for this purpose."
