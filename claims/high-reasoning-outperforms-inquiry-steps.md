## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with high prior reasoning skills significantly outperformed low reasoning students on generating scientific questions, identifying variables, and making hypotheses at posttest. [→ Wu 2016](#wu-2016)

## Evidence

### Wu 2016

Wu, H.-L., Weng, H.-L., & She, H.-C. (2016). Effects of scaffolds and scientific reasoning ability on web-based scientific inquiry. International Journal of Contemporary Educational Research, 3(1), 12-24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1216018

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way MANOVA with pretest reasoning as factor. Mean differences favoring high reasoning: generating questions 0.58, identifying variables 0.85, making hypotheses 0.76 at posttest; similar pattern at retention.

> "The one -way multivariate of analysis showed that students with high prior reasoning skills significantly outperformed those with low reasoning skills in identifying variables and making hypotheses in the posttest."
