## Evidence

### Akhter N 2026

Akhter N, Zhou X, Elhabashy S, Huq KATE, Okamoto M, Latif MA, Babaita AO, Moriyama M. (2026). Effect of evidence-based education program with mentoring system for prevention of ventilator-associated pneumonia on nurses' competency to improve quality care for patients in the intensive care unit: a quasi-experimental study in a tertiary hospital in Bangladesh. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1674223

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis among the four competency scores at three time points in the 52-nurse pre-post study. During implementation, "knowledge showed moderate, statistically significant correlations with both practice (r = 0.335, p < 0.05) and motivation/work engagement (r = 0.320, p < 0.05)"; skill–motivation/work engagement was r = 0.275 (p < 0.05).
