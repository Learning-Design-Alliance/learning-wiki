## Evidence

### Huh Yeon-Ju 2026

Huh Yeon-Ju, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Narae, Jeong Seoi, Kong Hyoun-Joong, Myung Sun Jung. (2026). Immersive Virtual Reality Training to Improve Novice Physicians’ Emergency Response Skills: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/71455

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial of 164 analyzed interns, the pre- to posttraining confidence change was the primary outcome. The reported increase, "increased significantly from mean 3.78 (SD 2.12) before training to mean 6.20 (SD 2.02) after training," was analyzed with paired t tests and generalized estimating equations.

> "the mean (SD) confidence score for managing oxygen desaturation increased significantly from mean 3.78 (SD 2.12) before training to mean 6.20 (SD 2.02) after training (t163=−14.04; P<.001)"
