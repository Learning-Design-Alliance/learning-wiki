# Gender was not a determining factor in FACT efficacy, although only males showed a significant pre-to-post gain

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Two-factor ANOVA found no statistically significant gender difference in pre-test (p=.165) or post-test (p=.088) inquiry scores, and no gender × overall-assessment interaction (post-test p=.920). [→ Mária Ganajová 2021](#maria-ganajova-2021)
`q3 i?` Within the experimental group, males improved significantly from pre-test to post-test (p=.005) while females did not (p=.253). [→ Mária Ganajová 2021 (2)](#maria-ganajova-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Mária Ganajová 2021

Mária Ganajová, Ivana Sotáková, Stanislav Lukáč, Zuzana Ješková, Veronika Jurková, Renáta Orosová. (2021). Formative Assessment as a Tool to Enhance the Development of Inquiry Skills in Science Education. Journal of Baltic Science Education, Vol. 20, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.33225/jbse/21.20.204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-factor ANOVA on experimental-group post-test scores (n=73) with gender and overall assessment factors; neither main effect nor the interaction reached significance.

> "Similarly, the post-test did not show statistically significant difference between males and females (p=.088; p>α) or groups of students with different overall assessment at the end of the term (p=.694; p>α). Again, the interaction (gender * overall assessment at the end of the term) was not confirmed: p=.920; p>α."

### Mária Ganajová 2021 (2)

Mária Ganajová, Ivana Sotáková, Stanislav Lukáč, Zuzana Ješková, Veronika Jurková, Renáta Orosová. (2021). Formative Assessment as a Tool to Enhance the Development of Inquiry Skills in Science Education. Journal of Baltic Science Education, Vol. 20, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.33225/jbse/21.20.204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests within the experimental group by gender: males' mean paired difference .084 (p=.005), females' .027 (p=.253); hypothesis H3 was confirmed on this test.

> "In the experimental group, a statistically significant difference in the level of inquiry skills between the pre-test and post-test was identified in males (p=.005; p<α). In females, the development of inquiry skills between the post-test and pre-test was not statistically significant (p=.253; p>α)."
