# Cross-term Foundational Skills scores were moderately correlated, with correlations ranging from 0.48 to 0.82 depending on domain and grade

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Pearson correlations among fall, winter, and spring scores ranged between 0.48 and 0.68 for Phonological Awareness, 0.55 and 0.82 for Phonics & Word Recognition, and 0.54 and 0.63 for Language Comprehension, suggesting moderate correlations across terms. [→ Wei He 2022](#wei-he-2022)

## Evidence

### Wei He 2022

Wei He, NWEA Psychometric Solutions. (2022). Achievement and Growth Norms for English MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/achievement-and-growth-norms-for-english-map-reading-fluency-foundational-skills/

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis (Table 3.2) using listwise deletion, computed only on students with scores in all three terms. Printed correlations include r = 0.48 to 0.68 for Phonological Awareness and r = 0.55 to 0.82 for Phonics & Word Recognition, described as moderately correlated.

> "These coefficients range between 0.48 and 0.68 for Phonological Awareness, 0.55 and 0.82 for Phonics & Word Recognition, and 0.54 and 0.63 for Language Comprehension. These results suggest that, in general, the scores in different terms were moderately correlated."
