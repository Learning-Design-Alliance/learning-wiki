# A mirroring dashboard shifts teacher attention toward epistemic over social aspects of learning, while a guiding dashboard divides interventions more equally

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the mirroring condition teachers gave relatively more attention to epistemic than social aspects; in the guiding dashboard condition interventions were more equally divided. [→ Peter Reimann 2021](#peter-reimann-2021)

## Evidence

### Peter Reimann 2021

Peter Reimann. (2021). Socio-technical configurations for productive talk. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:301–306. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09354-6

`q3 · i?`

Within the same six-teacher within-subjects quasi-experiment, ENA analysis of teacher-action codes revealed this tension between epistemic and social attention; "the epistemic artefacts were not simple yes/no answers" but solutions to programming problems requiring considerable appraisal effort. No effect size is printed.

> "Particularly in the mirroring condition, this led to relatively more attention given by the teachers to the epistemic than the social aspects of learning. In the guiding dashboard condition, teachers’ interventions were more equally divided."
