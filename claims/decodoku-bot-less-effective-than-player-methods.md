Numerical evaluation of the tutorial bot, whose pair-ranking rules incorporate the main elements of methods provided by players. The article reports the bot underperformed the player methods and suggests better ranking parameters from player data. No quantitative comparison is printed.

> "The bot is found to not be as effective as the player methods on which it was based. Improvement could be provided through analysis of player data, and using it to determine better parameters for the ranking system."
