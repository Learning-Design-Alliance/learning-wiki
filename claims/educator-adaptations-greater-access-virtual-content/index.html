Li 2022¶

Li, L., Hayakawa, M., Freese, J., Daniels, B., Weiser, G., Luttgen, K., Lor, M. C., Schneider, M., Huang, C.-W., & Jensen, E. (2022). Pivoting an in-person transmedia science program to a virtual program during a pandemic: Lessons learned. q2 · i? · design · r2

Study 2 educator interviews and telemetry data: educators tried different strategies (live demonstration versus video demonstration, one playlist per week versus biweekly) based on student capacities; older siblings supported younger students, and home-material scavenger hunts made activities hands-on.

"These findings about the successes of implementing Hero Elementary in a virtual environment suggest that educators’ adaptations of the resources, including accommodations and modifications, provided students with greater access to the virtual learning content."
