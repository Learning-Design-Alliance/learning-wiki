## Evidence

### Mosalanejad 2010

Mosalanejad, L., Alipor, A., & Zandi, B. (2010). A Blended Education Program Based on Critical Thinking and Its Effect on Personality Type and Attribution Style of the Students. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 11(2). http://tojde.anadolu.edu.tr/tojde39/articles/article_12.htm

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA on 41 students' final scores and NEO FFI personality factors found significant interactions for openness (F=10.17), extraversion (F=5.90), agreeableness (F=17.31), and conscientiousness (F=7.98), but not neuroticism.

> "Evaluating the interaction between type of teaching, student's final scores and personality type based on MANOVA TEST revealed that, there was a significant relationship between all personality factors(openness, extraversion, agreeableness and conscientiousness), except neuroticism criterion."
