`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

CFA of the student readiness scale (N=460) using robust maximum likelihood estimation confirmed the three-factor solution; Table 4 reports CFI of 0.98, TLI of 0.98, RMSEA of 0.032, and SRMR of 0.035 for Model.a. The article reports that 'all standardized factor loadings exceeded 0.40'.
