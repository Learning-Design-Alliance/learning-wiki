## Evidence

### Mutluhan Yilmaz 2026

Mutluhan Yilmaz, Andrea Gauthier, Mutlu Cukurova. (2026). Supporting online learners' regulation skills with the help of learning analytics and generative artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10595-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U test on the composite score, computed as the mean of standardised SRL proxies, in the quasi-experiment; the low-SRL GenAI condition exceeded the tutor condition, "(U = 133.00, p = .028, r = .340)". The authors note it should be interpreted as a single construct.

> "Participants in the low-SRLs condition who received GenAI feedback showed significantly higher post-intervention composite SRLs mean rank scores compared to those in the Tutor condition (U = 133.00, p = .028, r = .340)."
