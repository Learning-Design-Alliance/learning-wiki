## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Model 1, private-school students and girls were more likely to have high achievement; both became non-significant when health behaviors were added in Model 2. [→ Hayek J. 2020](#hayek-j-2020)

## Evidence

### Hayek J. 2020

Hayek J., Schneider F., Tueni M., de Vries H. (2020). Is Academic Achievement Related to Mediterranean Diet, Substance Use and Social-Cognitive Factors: Findings from Lebanese Adolescents. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051535

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multivariate logistic regression: Model 1 (private school aOR 2.39, girls aOR 1.96) then Model 2 where both lost significance. Bivariate analyses showed private school p < 0.001 and female p = 0.026.

> "The first model including all socio-demographic factors revealed that adolescents from private schools and girls were more likely to have high academic achievements. In the second model, adding health behaviors to Model 1, type of school and gender were no longer significant."
