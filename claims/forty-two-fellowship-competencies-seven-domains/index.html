Kim Junhewk 2026¶

Kim Junhewk, Kim So Yeon, Jung Hoi-In, Kang Jeong-Hyun. (2026). A competency-based curriculum for geriatric dentistry in undergraduate and postgraduate education : a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09625-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this Delphi-based competency set from Hogue (2016), a study of 54 experts in geriatric dentistry; the review itself notes the set "did not establish clear continuity with undergraduate curricula."

"A total of 42 competencies were identified and categorized into 7 domains: (1) Medical management; (2) Cognitive and behavior disorders; (3) Healthcare planning and promotion; (4) General skills; (5) Communication; (6) Assessment; and (7) Treatment planning."
