### Fawns 2026

Fawns, T., Bearman, M., Corbin, T., Henderson, M., McLean, J., Matthews, K. E., Oberg, G., Liang, Y., & Walton, J. (2026). Illuminating complex student realities of artificial intelligence through an entangled pedagogy framework. Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-026-01730-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group analysis of one participant (Howard, arts/psychology) within a study of 20 focus groups with 79 students across four Australian universities. His account shows the article's point that "practical decisions emerged from the intersection of multiple, sometimes contradictory value systems."

> "I feel good emotions largely but there is a bit of guilt and fear because I am often using in a way that I don't think is breaching my true academic integrity but it technically does and I haven't reported my use."
