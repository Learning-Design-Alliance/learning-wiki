The study calculated Cohen's d for individual STATIC factors in the intervention group. The article reports "a small effect was found for Factor 2 (d= .33) and Factor 4 (d=.26)" while cautioning that "due to the limited number of participants (n=8), this effect should be interpreted with caution."

"Utilizing Cohen's (1988) effect sizes, a small effect was found for Factor 2 (d= .33) and Factor 4 (d=.26) in the intervention group however, due to the limited number of participants (n=8), this effect should be interpreted with caution."
