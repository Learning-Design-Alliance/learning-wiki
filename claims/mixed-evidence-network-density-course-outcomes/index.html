Evidence¶

Michael Brown 2026¶

Michael Brown, Rachel Smith, Jennifer Tipton, Gabrielle Leitner. (2026). The Threads of Campus Life: A Review of the Literature on the Use of Social Network Analysis in Research Articles on Undergraduate Students. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543261438444

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of face-to-face classroom studies finds "mixed evidence that, in classrooms using interactive pedagogies, aspects of social network structure are related to course outcomes"; density-performance tests were inconsistent across the reviewed articles.

"There is mixed evidence that, in classrooms using interactive pedagogies, aspects of social network structure are related to course outcomes."

Michael Brown 2026 (2)¶

Michael Brown, Rachel Smith, Jennifer Tipton, Gabrielle Leitner. (2026). The Threads of Campus Life: A Review of the Literature on the Use of Social Network Analysis in Research Articles on Undergraduate Students. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543261438444

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports reviewed authors' own interpretation that "density is not the correct measure for all network-oriented conceptions of engagement", citing Alcock et al. and others questioning which interactions matter for learning.

"It is possible that density is not the correct measure for all network-oriented conceptions of engagement, and a number of the authors in studies we reviewed raised the question of whether we are observing the right interactions that matter for learning"
