## Evidence

### Morimoto Y 2025

Morimoto Y, Shikino K, Nomura Y, Ito S. (2025). Trends in the Japanese National Medical Licensing Examination: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Data-driven BERTopic analysis of all 24 NMLE sessions (2001-2024), with topic trends traced by linear regression of topic frequencies. The article reports 10,129 of 11,540 (88%) items classified into 25 topics; no effect size is printed.

> "Topic modeling effectively classified 10,129 of the 11,540 (88%) items into 25 topics."

### Morimoto Y 2025 (2)

Morimoto Y, Shikino K, Nomura Y, Ito S. (2025). Trends in the Japanese National Medical Licensing Examination: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same BERTopic time-series analysis; the article names the increasing non-organ-specific topics and reports the authors attributed rises to factors such as professionalism emphasis in the MCC, the aging population, and patient-safety awareness after the 2014 Medical Accident Investigation System.

> "Ten topics exhibited an increasing trend, among which topics not limited to specific organs were identified, including “comprehensive clinical items,” “accountability in medical practice and patients’ rights,” “care, daily living support, and community health care,” “intensive care,” and “infection control and safety management in basic clinical procedures.”"
