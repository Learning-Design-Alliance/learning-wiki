## Evidence

### Thacker 2025

Thacker, B., Wang, J., Zhang, Y., Tran, Q. B., Vemula, D. S., & Kushimo, T. (2025). From Research to Resources: Assessing Student Understanding and Skills in Quantum Computing. NSF grant DUE 2235464. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of semi-structured interviews with four student volunteers from each cohort, conducted the semester after each course. F23 students F23_2_c and F23_3_d gave correct qualitative descriptions invoking the no-cloning theorem, while most F21 students' descriptions were "vague, missing information or contained incorrect pieces of information."

> "Overall, the F23 students demonstrated a stronger qualitative understanding of quantum teleportation than the F21 students. This could be due to the mini-tutorial on quantum teleportation which required them to describe the technique qualitatively"
