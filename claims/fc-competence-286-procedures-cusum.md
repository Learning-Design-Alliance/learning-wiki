## Evidence

### MacKenzie Kenneth R. 2016

MacKenzie Kenneth R., Aning Jonathan. (2016). Defining competency in flexible cystoscopy: a novel approach using cumulative Sum analysis. BMC Urology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12894-016-0143-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective single-trainee case series of 419 supervised flexible cystoscopies analysed with CUSUM (acceptable failure rate 0.05, unacceptable 0.15). The full-procedure CUSUM curve shows "Acceptable performance is first achieved when the control line is crossed following the 118th procedure", with competence declared at the 286th procedure.

> "Acceptable performance is first achieved when the control line is crossed following the 118th procedure. However, acceptable performance is not maintained with multiple unacceptable control lines crossed. The maximal point was reached following 257th procedure with a sustained period of acceptable performance following this"
