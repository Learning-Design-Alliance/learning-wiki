## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Reith Dorothee, Walter Tobias, Engeser Peter, Wensing Michel, Flum Elisabeth, Szecsenyi Joachim, Krug Katja. (2020). How to ensure basic competencies in end of life care – a mixed methods study with post-graduate trainees in primary care in Germany. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-020-00540-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Evaluation items in the T2 mail questionnaire (153 respondents of 219 attendees). Separately, 22.9% (n = 35/153) mentioned gaining competencies in end-of-life care by the intervention, and 74.5% reported gaining further palliative care experience after attending.

> "107 out of 153 post-graduate trainees (69.9%) evaluated the participation of the two-day seminar program as helpful or very helpful regarding their practical work."
