One-sample t-tests in the RQ4 results (user subgroup): users found the chatbot "easy to use r ( p < .001, n = 37)", responses clear and relevant, information accurate (p < .001, n = 38), personalised (p = .026), and reported occasional errors (p = .003).

"Users thought that the chatbots were easy to use r ( p < .001, n = 37), that the chatbot responses were clear and understandable ( p < .001, n = 37), that chatbot responses were relevant to their questions or needs ( p < .001, n = 38)."

q3 · i? · qualitative · r3

Reflexive thematic analysis of non-user open-ended comments (Fig. 5): "Usability was the biggest theme (23% of responses) with many students disliking the intrusive pop-up design"; example comments described the pop-up as annoying and intrusive. Among improvement suggestions, pop-up and user-experience issues accounted for 22% of responses.
