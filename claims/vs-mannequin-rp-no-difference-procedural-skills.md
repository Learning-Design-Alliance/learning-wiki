`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 10 RCTs assessing procedural skills. Pooled SMD=–0.12 (95% CI –0.47 to 0.23) was not significant; TSA showed the RIS had been reached, implying a conclusive statistically insignificant result. Leave-one-out analysis was consistent.
