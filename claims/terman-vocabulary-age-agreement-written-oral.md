## Evidence

### Weeks 1919

Weeks, A. L. (1919). Terman Vocabulary as a Group Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Classification agreement analysis of the same 57 secondary-school girls under two norm sets. Under Terman norms 33 of 57 cases were in the same age group across formats; under Hollingworth norms 41 cases, or seventy-two per cent, matched. Disagreements were mostly one age group.

> "According to the Terman norms, thirty-three cases, or fifty-eight per cent, are in the same age group in both tests; twenty-one, or thirty-seven per cent, are one age group lower or higher in the written test than in the oral test."
