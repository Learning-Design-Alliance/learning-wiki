Evidence¶

John Hattie 2025¶

John Hattie, Timothy O'Leary. (2025). Learning Styles, Preferences, or Strategies? An Explanation for the Resurgence of Styles Across Many Meta-analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10002-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Mayer and colleagues (2019), a series of studies on multimedia learning: synced audio-visual narration beat single-channel presentation, and "the worst outcomes occurred when the auditory and visual information were out of sync". No effect sizes are printed.

"For example, in a series of studies, Mayer and colleagues (2019) found that narrated PowerPoints in which auditory and visual information were synced were more meaningful for immediate and future learning outcomes than when just auditory or visual information was given."
