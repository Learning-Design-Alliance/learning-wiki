## Evidence

### David P. Smith 2018

David P. Smith, Laurie E. McNeil, David T. Guynn, Alice D. Churukian, Duane L. Deardorff, Colin S. Wallace. (2018). Transforming the content, pedagogy and structure of an introductory physics course for life sciences majors. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/1.5058685](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5058685)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Before-and-after comparison of average normalized (Hake) gains on the FCI across semesters from Fall 2008 to Spring 2017, spanning traditional, interactive-lecture, and Lecture/Studio pedagogy categories. The article reports that "the biological focus has not led to a decrease in understanding of fundamental physics concepts" and that Spring 2016 produced the highest gains recorded to date.

> "Analysis of the FCI data show that students in the new course sequence perform at least at the same level as an interactive engagement class teaching traditional content, i.e. that the biological focus has not led to a decrease in understanding of fundamental physics concepts."
