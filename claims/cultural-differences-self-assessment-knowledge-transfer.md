`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group discussion data (n = 25) analysed thematically; a provider observed receivers "might say that they have the intermediate level, but they might be expert actually". The effect of hierarchical culture on transfer was described as not obvious in this study. No effect sizes.

> "Providers felt that receivers were humble and under-rated their actual level of knowledge and skills in eLearning development during the initial self-assessment. Providers were aware of the cultural difference in self-assessment among the receivers, which might influence the reporting of their baseline skills and knowledge."
