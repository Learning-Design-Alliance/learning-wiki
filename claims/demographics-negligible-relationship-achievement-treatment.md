### Thoron 2011

Thoron, A. C., & Myers, B. E. (2011). Effects of Inquiry–based Agriscience Instruction on Student Achievement. Journal of Agricultural Education, 52(4). https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2011.04175

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pearson Product Moment correlations among posttest scores, demographics, and treatment for the 305 participants (Table 4); the article characterizes the demographic relationships as "negligible relationships with posttests and type of treatment", with one low relationship between posttest two and year in school.
