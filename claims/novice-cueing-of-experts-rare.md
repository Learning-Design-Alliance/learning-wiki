Christine C. A. van Nooijen · Bjorn B. de Koning · Wichor M. Bramer · Anna Isahakyan · Maryam Asoodar · Ellen Kok · Jeroen J. G. van Merrienboer · Fred Paas. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, from Deken and colleagues (2012), that novice-driven information seeking was scarce (8% of observations), covering problem understanding (36.2%), organisational information (32.4%), past solutions (17%) and future meetings (13.7%). The review attributes the scarcity to novices lacking contextual knowledge to know which questions to ask.

> "the more the design is defined, the less time the novices spent on explicitly querying the expert’s knowledge."
