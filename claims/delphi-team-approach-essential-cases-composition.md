## Evidence

### Lee Jin Young 2025

Lee Jin Young, Min Yul Ha, Yim Jun, Park Kwi Hwa, Yune So Jung. (2025). Core shared competencies between physicians and nurses in primary care: a Delphi study in Korea. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.359

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Round 1 open-ended responses from all 30 panel members (12 physicians, 18 nurses) on the importance and roles of interprofessional team approaches in primary care. Physicians emphasized attending physician-centered collaboration; nurses emphasized integrated, continuous care. No effect size is printed.

> "All experts emphasized that team approaches are essential in various cases including patients with multiple chronic diseases, rehabilitation, end-of-life elderly care, and mental health management."

### Lee Jin Young 2025 (2)

Lee Jin Young, Min Yul Ha, Yim Jun, Park Kwi Hwa, Yune So Jung. (2025). Core shared competencies between physicians and nurses in primary care: a Delphi study in Korea. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.359

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Round 1 consensus on team composition, with profession-specific roles (e.g., physicians for treatment planning, nurses for monitoring and coordination, social workers for resource linkage) summarized in the article's Table 1. No effect size is printed.

> "Essential team members included physicians, nurses, and social workers, with nutritionists, physical therapists, pharmacists, and psychological counselors potentially included depending on circumstances."
