Evidence¶

Darling-Hammond 2026¶

Darling-Hammond, L., Fitz, J., Giani, M. V., Gordon, M. F., & Wechsler, M. (2026). The principal effect: How investing in school leaders is key to solving education's challenges. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/644.197

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review cites research reviews covering more than 400 studies on school climate outcomes, finding climate "improves student attendance, behavioral outcomes, graduation rates, and learning loss" and reduces poverty's negative achievement effects.

"Reviews of research incorporating more than 400 studies have found that a positive school climate improves student attendance, behavioral outcomes, graduation rates, and learning loss overall and reduces the negative effects of poverty on achievement, boosting grades, test scores, and student engagement."

Darling-Hammond 2026 (2)¶

Darling-Hammond, L., Fitz, J., Giani, M. V., Gordon, M. F., & Wechsler, M. (2026). The principal effect: How investing in school leaders is key to solving education's challenges. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/644.197

q2 · i? · review · r?

A cited pathway study reported in the review found "the strongest linkage to be through improvements in school climate," holding even for schools that started with strong climates. Recent research also shows stronger principal impacts on attendance in urban low-income schools.

"In a study examining the potential pathways between principal instructional leadership and student achievement growth, researchers found the strongest linkage to be through improvements in school climate, even for schools that started out with strong climates."
