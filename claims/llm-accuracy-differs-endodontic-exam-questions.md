## Evidence

### Adıgüzel Mehmet A 2026

Adıgüzel Mehmet A. (2026). Accuracy, Self-Reported Confidence, and Overconfidence of Large Language Models in Endodontics: An Evaluation Using National Specialty Examination Questions. Medical Science Monitor. https://doi.org/10.12659/MSM.954052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional in silico analysis of 123 text-based endodontic questions from the Turkish Dental Specialty Examination (2017–2026), answered by three LLMs with a standardized prompt. Reports overall accuracy rates and a significant Cochran's Q test.

> "Overall accuracy was 89.4% for ChatGPT, 76.4% for Claude, and 90.2% for Gemini. Accuracy differed significantly among the models (Cochran's Q, χ2(2) = 21.00, P < 0.001), with both ChatGPT and Gemini performing significantly better than Claude, whereas their accuracies were comparable"
