Moira McCullough 2016¶

Moira McCullough, Stephen Lipscomb, Hanley Chiang, Brian Gill. (2016). Do Principals' Professional Practice Ratings Reflect Their Contributions to Student Achievement? Evidence from Pennsylvania's Framework for Leadership. Working Paper 46. Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-principals-professional-practice-ratings-reflect-their-contributions-to-student-achievement

q1 · i? · associational · r?

Authors' framing statement about the research gap: although states and districts have begun evaluating principals, "there is little evidence on the validity of principal evaluation measures." This is the authors' interpretation, not a tested result.

"This is the first study to find evidence that ratings of principals' professional practice are correlated with credible measures of principals' contributions to student achievement."
