### Yamaguchi Tomoko 2022

Yamaguchi Tomoko, Nakamura Ryoichi, Kuboki Akihito, Otori Nobuyoshi. (2022). Clinical study of skill assessment based on time sequential measurement changes. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-10502-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validation analysis comparing expert and resident SPMs against a standard expert model built from nine experts' data; the Mann–Whitney U test showed "the cost of surgeries performed by experts was found to be significantly lower than that of the residents". No effect size is printed.

> "After performing the Mann–Whitney U test on the average cost between the experts and the residents (Fig. 5), the cost of surgeries performed by experts was found to be significantly lower than that of the residents."
