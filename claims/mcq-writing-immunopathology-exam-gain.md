## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students randomized to write MCQ on Immunopathology obtained significantly better Immunopathology exam results (median 5.13 vs 3.86 over 10; P = 0.03) than students randomized to the electrolyte/acid-base topic. [→ Herrero Jose Ignacio 2019](#herrero-jose-ignacio-2019)

## Evidence

### Herrero Jose Ignacio 2019

Herrero Jose Ignacio, Lucena Felipe, Quiroga Jorge. (2019). Randomized study showing the benefit of medical study writing multiple choice questions on their learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1469-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison within the second-year General Pathology cohort: 37 students wrote Immunopathology MCQ and 38 wrote electrolyte/acid-base MCQ, with topic assigned by university ID parity. The results section reports "5.13 versus 3.86 over 10; P = 0.03"; no effect size is printed.

> "The performance in Immunopathology of the students who designed MCQ of Immunopathology was significantly better (median qualification: 5.13 versus 3.86 over 10; P = 0.03)."
