# Negative cross-domain relations between math and L1 motivation were far more prevalent among girls than boys

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Among girls, initial math self-concept was negatively related to L1 self-concept development (r = –.25) and L1 interest development (r = –.28), with further negative cross-domain correlations between intercepts and slopes in both directions. [→ Anna Widlund 2023](#anna-widlund-2023)
`q2 i2` Among boys, the only significant cross-domain relation was a negative correlation between Grade 7 math interest and L1 interest development (r = –0.29). [→ Anna Widlund 2023 (2)](#anna-widlund-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Anna Widlund 2023

Anna Widlund, Markku Niemivirta, Heta Tuominen, Johan Korhonen. (2023). Growth Trajectories of Self-Concept and Interest in Mathematics and Language – Individual Differences and Cross-Domain Relations. Preprint version 1. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5563-5044

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group growth model correlations among girls: "their seventh-grade mathematics self-concept was negatively correlated with the development of both L1 self-concept (r = –.25, p = 0.002) and interest (r = –.28, p = 0.005)"; the printed effect size r = –.25 is small-to-moderate, with further negative relations r = –.24, –.29, –.25.

> "Concerning girls, cross-domain relations revealed that their seventh-grade mathematics self-concept was negatively correlated with the development of both L1 self-concept (r = –.25, p = 0.002) and interest (r = –.28, p = 0.005)."

### Anna Widlund 2023 (2)

Anna Widlund, Markku Niemivirta, Heta Tuominen, Johan Korhonen. (2023). Growth Trajectories of Self-Concept and Interest in Mathematics and Language – Individual Differences and Cross-Domain Relations. Preprint version 1. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5563-5044

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group growth model correlations among boys: "only one significant cross-domain relation was detected among boys", with the printed effect size r = –0.29 (p = .001), between Grade 7 math interest and L1 interest slope.

> "only one significant cross-domain relation was detected among boys: their seventh-grade mathematics interest (i.e., intercept) was negatively related to their L1 interest development (i.e., linear slope, r = –0.29, p = .001)"
