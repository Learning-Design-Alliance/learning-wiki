### Gu Peng 2026

Gu Peng, Xu Zhendong, Zhang Zheng, Wen Yonghao, Jia Yanqing, Du Ying, Wen Tianlin, Man Chao, Jia Zhiwei, Wu Yaohong, Li Wei. (2026). Comparative effectiveness of orthopedic teaching methods in China: a Bayesian network meta-analysis of randomized trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08582-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Network meta-analysis of learning interest from 16 studies, covering 7 teaching designs and 21 comparisons; the printed mean differences versus LBL are reported, with MDT-CBL and PBL-CBL rated low-quality evidence while 3D-PBL was rated high quality.

> "Compared to LBL, MDT-CBL (MD 4.02, 95% CI 2.49 to 5.56) had the greatest effect on increasing learning interest, followed by PBL-CBL (MD 2.39, 95% CI 1.54 to 3.24) and 3D-PBL (MD 1.88, 95% CI 1.53 to 2.24)."
