Allensworth 2005¶

Allensworth, E. M., & Easton, J. Q. (2005). The On-Track Indicator as a Predictor of High School Graduation. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/track-indicator-predictor-high-school-graduation

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Observational analysis of the fall 2000 CPS cohort by credits earned, reported with Figure 4. Only 40 percent of students with exactly five credits—the promotion minimum—graduated in four years, and the report notes students actually need more than five credits to graduate on time.

"More than 70 percent of students who earned six credits in their freshman year graduated, and fully 85 percent of freshmen who earned seven or more credits graduated in four years."
