### SETH A. MAJOR 2023

SETH A. MAJOR. (2023). Footprinting in a Course on Energy. [doi:10.1119/5.0136958](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0136958)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Team carbon accounting (Appendix C, Fig. 1) covering food, heat, electrical use, and transport during the semester, finding "about 4 tons of CO 2e per full-time community member per semester"; campus-building infrastructure and composting methane were excluded.

> "we studied the Hamilton Adirondack Program's carbon footprint and estimated it to be about 4 tons of CO 2e per full-time community member per semester, see Fig. 1."

### SETH A. MAJOR 2023 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Figure 1 breakdown described in the text of Appendix C: "transportation dominates the program's footprint", attributed to internship structure, food sourcing, and recreation. Descriptive accounting estimate, no inferential test reported.

> "It is easy to see that transportation dominates the program's footprint. The transportation (personal "cars" and program "suv's") results from the structure of the internships, food sourcing, and recreational activities."
