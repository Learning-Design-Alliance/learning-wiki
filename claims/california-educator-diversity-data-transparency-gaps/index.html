The Education Trust 2022¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: California. EDTRUST.ORG. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The policy scan's rating for strategy 1 (making educator diversity data visible and actionable) describes California's actions against printed criteria, earning a green on this criterion while noting the two-year lag and the dashboard omission.

"California posts school-level data on the racial demographics of the educator workforce on the state website. However, the state is two years behind on making public the data and does not include it as a metric in the state's accountability system (California School Dashboard)."

The Education Trust 2022 (2)¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: California. EDTRUST.ORG. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The policy scan reports that California earned red ratings for sharing the racial makeup of candidates entering and completing in-state preparation programs and for publicly available persistence data disaggregated by race/ethnicity; the state's Cradle-to-Career Data System does not plan to include teacher pipeline data.

"California does not post information about the retention rates of educators of color on the state website."
