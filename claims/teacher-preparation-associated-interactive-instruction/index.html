Julia B. Smith 2001¶

Julia B. Smith, Valerie E. Lee, and Fred M. Newmann. (2001). Instruction and Achievement in Chicago Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/instruction-and-achievement-chicago-elementary-schools

HLM analysis of teacher survey data controlling for teacher demographics and classroom/school characteristics. Professional development quality showed the strongest association with interactive instruction, a printed effect size of ".21 effect-size units" (small, below d = 0.2 threshold is not applicable; .21 falls in the small range).

"teachers’ reports of the quality of their professional development were most strongly associated with their use of interactive instruction (.21 effect-size units)."
