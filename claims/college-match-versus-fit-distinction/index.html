Evidence¶

Melissa Roderick 2008¶

Melissa Roderick, Jenny Nagaoka, Vanessa Coca, Eliza Moeller, with Karen Roddie, Jamiliyah Gilliam, and Desmond Patton. (2008). From High School to the Future: Potholes on the Road to College (Case Studies). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-potholes-road-college

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative case study of Sakaarah (IB program, weighted GPA 3.5, ACT in the mid-20s) who conducted extensive research using a college guidebook, called colleges, and sought a strong premedical program — the report frames her as showing "match" is one, but not the only, component of college fit.

"Sakaarah thought carefully about finding a col- lege that was a good fit for her, and she considered any college a realistic option, given her qualifications."
