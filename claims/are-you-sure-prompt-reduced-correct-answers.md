# Asking "Are you sure?" reduced correct answers for both models; ChatGPT-3.5 Turbo's answer changes approached but did not reach significance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the follow-up question, ChatGPT-3.5 Turbo's correct answers fell from 40 (54%) to 28 (38%), a change that approached but did not reach significance (χ²1=3.361, P=.067). [→ Filipe Prazeres 2025](#filipe-prazeres-2025)

## Evidence

### Filipe Prazeres 2025

Filipe Prazeres. (2025). ChatGPT's Performance on Portuguese Medical Examination Questions: Comparative Analysis of ChatGPT-3.5 Turbo and ChatGPT-4o Mini. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65108

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consistency test on the 74 part-1 PNA questions answered by ChatGPT-3.5 Turbo, analyzed with the McNemar test with continuity correction. The model corrected 12 originally incorrect answers but changed 24 originally correct ones to incorrect; the printed test is χ²1=3.361, P=.067.

> "After the follow-up question, “Are you sure?,” the number of correct answers decreased to 28 (38%), while the number of incorrect answers increased to 46 (62%)."

### Filipe Prazeres 2025 (2)

Filipe Prazeres. (2025). ChatGPT's Performance on Portuguese Medical Examination Questions: Comparative Analysis of ChatGPT-3.5 Turbo and ChatGPT-4o Mini. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65108

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consistency test on the 74 part-2 PNA questions answered by ChatGPT-4o mini, analyzed with the McNemar test with continuity correction. The model fixed 12 previously wrong answers but changed 18 previously correct ones; the printed test is χ²1=0.833, P=.361.

> "After being asked, “Are you sure?,” the correct answers dropped to 42 (57%), while incorrect answers rose to 32 (43%)."
