G. Stahl & S. Ludvigsen & N. Law & U. Cress 2014¶

G. Stahl & S. Ludvigsen & N. Law & U. Cress. (2014). CSCL artifacts. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 9, 237–245. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-014-9200-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The editorial reports findings from Jeong, Hmelo-Silver and Yu's analysis of CSCL empirical articles from seven journals (2005–2009), which categorized hundreds of papers along research design, setting, data and analysis dimensions combining automated and manual examination.

"First, the majority of data sources for the selected articles were questionnaires— sources of self-reports rather than knowledge objects or discourse interactions."
