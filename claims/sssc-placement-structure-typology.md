Categorization of director interview responses on center placement across 29 states. Researchers coded responses into three mutually exclusive categories; the report prints the full distribution, including that 18 of the 23 single-agency states house centers within a larger state agency.

> "Of the 29 states represented, 23 states have one center that is housed in one agency or organization, such as a state department or institution of higher education; 3 states have one center that is split between at least two departments or agencies; and 3 states have two centers that operate independently from one another."
