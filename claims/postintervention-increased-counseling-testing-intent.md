## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 100% of post-intervention respondents reported intent for more antenatal cCMV counseling, with counseling intention moving from the never-to-rarely range to often-to-always from pre-test to post-test in the single-group pre-post study (p < .001). [→ Pesch Megan H. 2020](#pesch-megan-h-2020)

## Evidence

### Pesch Megan H. 2020

Pesch Megan H., Anderson Carter, Mowers Erika. (2020). Improving Obstetric Provider Congenital Cytomegalovirus Knowledge and Practices. Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8875494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on paired ordinal counseling-intention responses from the 24 dual survey completers; no respondent stated they would never counsel after the session.

> "Postintervention, counseling on prevention intention increased to the “often to always” range (from “never to rarely” at baseline) (p < .001)."

### Pesch Megan H. 2020 (2)

Pesch Megan H., Anderson Carter, Mowers Erika. (2020). Improving Obstetric Provider Congenital Cytomegalovirus Knowledge and Practices. Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8875494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-intervention intent items from the repeated survey of the 30 post-intervention respondents; testing intention was measured on a 3-point scale and counseling intent was unanimous.

> "Following the educational session, 100% of respondents reported intent to increase antenatal cCMV counseling, with the mean frequency of testing intention increased to 2.4, on a 3-point scale (1 = never, 2 = high-risk patients, 3 = all patients), (range 2-3, p < .001)"
