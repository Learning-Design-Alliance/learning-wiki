John Q. Easton 2000¶

Forced-choice survey item in which districts rated use of test scores on a five-point scale from "not at all" to "a great extent" for evaluating district programs, school improvement, principals, teachers, and students. The report notes "in actual ratings of usage they report more use of assessment results for evaluating school improvement and district programs than students."

"About 77 per- cent of districts chose “to a great extent” or the next high- est category to describe using test score results to evaluate school improvement."
