## Evidence

### Toale Conor 2024

Toale Conor, Morris Marie, Roche Adam, Voborsky Miroslav, Traynor Oscar, Kavanagh Dara. (2024). Development and validation of a simulation-based assessment of operative competence for higher specialist trainees in general surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11024-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot assessment of 13 trainees (6 junior ST1–ST4, 7 senior ST5–ST8) across eight 15-min stations, assessed by consultant surgeons. The group comparison showed "a significant difference in mean station score", with no effect size printed, so the magnitude is untested beyond the reported means.

> "A significant difference in mean station score was observed between ‘Junior’ and ‘Senior’ trainees (44.82 vs 58.18, p = 0.004) (Fig. 1)."
