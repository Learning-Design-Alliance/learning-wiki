Report analysis of Texas retention data by pathway (Figure 1), comparing noncertified entrants with alternatively certified teachers over the 2019–2024 span. The report states "Fewer than 40% of noncertified entrants were still in teaching" after 5 years; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Fewer than 40% of noncertified entrants were still in teaching at the end of the same period, a figure 18 percentage points lower than the retention rate for alternative certification programs."
