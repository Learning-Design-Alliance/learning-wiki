## Evidence

### Stephen D.H. Hsu 2010

Stephen D.H. Hsu, James Schombert. (2010). Data Mining the University: College GPA Predictions from SAT Scores. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1004.2731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1004.2731)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sub-sample analysis of 740 graduating seniors with SAT scores and recent GRE scores (composite SAT 800-1550); the article reports "R values of roughly 0.75 for both Math and Reading," lower than the .86 of Angoff (1990), attributed to the 1995 SAT re-centering.

> "The SAT and GRE scores are highly correlated (with R values of roughly 0.75 for both Math and Reading) and a correlation slope near unity."
