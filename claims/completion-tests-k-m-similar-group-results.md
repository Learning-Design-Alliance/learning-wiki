## Evidence

### Uhl 1916

Uhl, W. L. (1916). Mentality tests for college freshmen. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Score-frequency analysis of the two completion tests given to the 100 freshmen less than five minutes apart; the number reaching the maximum per-sentence score was practically the same, and "The medians for the two tests indicate that M is the easier test even disregarding the difference in time."

> "A comparison of the results indicates, however, that much the same degree of success was attained in one test as in the other."
