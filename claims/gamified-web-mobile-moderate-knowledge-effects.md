## Evidence

### Chen W.-L. 2026

Chen W.-L., Winitchayothin S., Cheng C.-H., Tonapa S.I., Chou F.-H., & Zimmerman P.-A. (2026). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effect of gamified clinical skill learning on learning outcomes in undergraduate nursing education. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000772

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of six RCTs (n=295) of gamified web/mobile platforms in the knowledge meta-analysis. The review reports a moderate pooled effect, SMD = 0.55, quoting "a moderate pooled effect (SMD=0.55, 95% CI [0.23, 0.87])" with substantial heterogeneity (I2=70%).

> "These interventions (six studies, n=295) exhibited a moderate pooled effect (SMD=0.55, 95% CI [0.23, 0.87]) with substantial heterogeneity (I 2=70%). They incorporated web-based or mobile platforms with embedded gamification elements, including quizzes, badges, and leaderboards."
