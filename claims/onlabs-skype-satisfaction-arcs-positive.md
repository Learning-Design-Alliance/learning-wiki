## Evidence

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-report questionnaire (step 12) completed by the With-OnLabs students on 5-point Likert items covering satisfaction, interest, confidence, understanding and cognitive load, based mostly on the ARCS model. The authors report that "all the With-OnLabs students, except one,  believe that a Skype session could be part of their learning procedure".

> "As it is presented in Fig.5(a)  all the With-OnLabs students, except one,  believe that a Skype session could be part of their learning procedure."

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017 (2)

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second questionnaire item from the same With-OnLabs group (Fig. 5b). The authors report "the majority of the With-OnLabs denotes that  a Skype session should precede every exercise in the physical lab". Overall they conclude the digital learning material provides an efficient, interesting and innovative learning situation.

> "Furthermore, Fig.5(b) presents that the majority of the With-OnLabs denotes that  a Skype session should precede every exercise in the physical lab."
