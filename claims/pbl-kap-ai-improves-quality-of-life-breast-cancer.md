## Evidence

### Li Heyu 2025

Li Heyu, Lu Yang, Tian Yang, Zhou Li, Wang Danna. (2025). Mechanism of an integrated PBL-KAP-AI spiral health education model for breast cancer patients. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1687956

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial with implementation limitations comparing 244 intervention and 244 control breast cancer patients pre- and post-intervention (3 months). The between-group post-intervention contrast was significant, "(t = 11.47, p < 0.001)"; no between-group effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "After the intervention, the intervention group's score significantly increased to 80.58 ± 7.28, while the control group's score increased to 71.37 ± 10.21. The difference between the two groups was statistically significant (t = 11.47, p < 0.001)."
