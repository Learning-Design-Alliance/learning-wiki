Nachiket Kotalwar, Conrad Borchers, Shubham Gandhi, Partha Sarathi Purkayastha, David Yaron, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2026). Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive counts of the tutor's intervention decisions recorded in the Visual-Proactive condition; the tutor decided to intervene 187 times and withheld 20 messages because the learner sent a chat message while the analysis was running.

> "Across the 40 Visual-Proactive participants, the tutor completed 5,047 paper analyses, a median of 127 per participant, and after 96% of them it chose to wait."

### Nachiket Kotalwar 2026 (2)

Nachiket Kotalwar, Conrad Borchers, Shubham Gandhi, Partha Sarathi Purkayastha, David Yaron, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2026). Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Counts of the two intervention types under the repair-first policy; because nearly every proactive message was a repair, proactive messages carried correctness feedback, error identification, and directive hints far more often than requested replies did.

> "Nearly every delivered message was a repair of a visible error (157). Transition support for a stalled approach, the second intervention type in Section 3.4, was delivered only 10 times."
