## Evidence

### Cheng Sipei 2026

Cheng Sipei, Zeng Helin, Kang Yanming, Wu Lan, Chen Shouming. (2026). Effectiveness of a novel pork belly simulation model versus traditional training for ultrasound-guided TAP block in anesthesiology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09546-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this randomized controlled trial of 30 anesthesiology residents, the simulation group (Group B) achieved a "93.3%, 14/15" independent success rate versus "26.7%, 4/15" for traditional training (Group A), assessed by blinded instructors using a standardized scoring scale.

> "Most notably, the success rate was substantially higher in Group B (93.3%, 14/15) compared to Group A (26.7%, 4/15) (p < 0.001)."
