## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Multiple-choice Assessment for Upper-division Electricity and Magnetism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary behavioral measure from the same matched-groups E&M 1 comparison, using recorded stop times for the 25 students per version. The difference was not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "the stop time of each student was recorded and, on average, students spent a comparable amount of time on the MC version, 35 ± 2 min, as on the FR version, 37 ± 2 min. The difference in time was not significant (p = 0.4)."
