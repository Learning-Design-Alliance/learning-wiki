## Evidence

### Wei Linna 2025

Wei Linna, Yu Xinyang, Wang Yiming, Shan Nan. (2025). Application of BOPPPS and Flipped Classroom Joint Teaching Model into Clinical Practice Ability of Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Standardized Training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07246-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison (35 per group) of consultation scores after 12 weeks of teaching. The experimental group scored "significantly higher than the control group" with t = 2.844, P = 0.0059; Table 1 prints Cohen's d = 0.690 for this comparison (d = 1.486 for clinical thinking assessment, t = 6.127, P < 0.0001).

> "The consultation score of the experimental group (36.51 ± 5.13 points) was significantly higher than the control group (33.43 ± 3.86 points) (t = 2.844, P = 0.0059)."

### Wei Linna 2025 (2)

Wei Linna, Yu Xinyang, Wang Yiming, Shan Nan. (2025). Application of BOPPPS and Flipped Classroom Joint Teaching Model into Clinical Practice Ability of Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Standardized Training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07246-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Clinical practice skill assessment after the 12-week teaching period, reported in the Results with the Fig. 4 comparison. The experimental group mean of 90.69 ± 3.841 was "significantly higher than that of the control group" at 79.77 ± 4.426, P < 0.0001; Table 1 prints Cohen's d = 2.673 for this comparison.

> "The clinical practice skill assessment scores of the experimental group (90.69 ± 3.841) were significantly higher than that of the control group (79.77 ± 4.426) (P < 0.0001; Fig. 4)."
