### Devine 2025

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate latent change score model linking child-rated well-being and caregiver-rated mental health in the longitudinal sample. The article reports "gains in well-being were associated with declines in mental health problems, std. est. = −.51." A caregiver-informant model showed a weaker coupling (std. est. = −.19, p < .01).

> "There were moderate–strong associations between changes in well-being and changes in mental health. Gains in well-being were associated with declines in mental health problems, std. est. = −.51, z = −3.797, p < .0001."
