## Evidence

### Deiglmayr 2011

Deiglmayr, A., & Spada, H. (2011). Training for fostering knowledge co-construction from collaborative inference-drawing. Learning and Instruction, 21(3), 441-451. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2010.06.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus interaction from the testing-phase two-way mixed model ANOVA of the 36-dyad experiment: "the interaction between kind of training and inference type was not significant" (F(6, 64) = .79, ns). The authors interpret that training "generally enhanced the frequency with which students drew inferences". Hypothesis 2c was not supported.
