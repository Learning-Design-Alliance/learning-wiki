Stone 1913¶

Stone, C. W. (1913). Problems in the Scientific Study of the Teaching of Arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology, IV(1). https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the introductory statement of a 1913 agenda-setting paper; no data are offered. The author reasons that further subject-matter study may safely omit still more obsolete content, and illustrates with fractions whose common denominator cannot be determined by inspection, quoting that "there will be no need of a scientific study to determine the degree to which it pays to explain the processes of reducing to a common denominator."

"It is important that these studies be pushed forward, but it is even more important that the question of uhat to teach be scientifically investigated."
