Roschelle J. (2021). Intelligence Augmentation for Collaborative Learning. In: Sottilare R.A., Schwarz J. (eds) Adaptive Instructional Systems. Design and Evaluation. HCII 2021. Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol 12792. Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-77857-6_17

The presentation contrasts EdTech AI messaging, which it says "over-emphasizes individual, cognitive" approaches such as isolated skills and 1:1 personalization, with curricular frameworks emphasizing discourse, project-based group work, and student identity and culture. No data are printed.
