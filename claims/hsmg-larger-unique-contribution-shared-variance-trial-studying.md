## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2016

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2016). Predicting Performance in Higher Education Using Proximal Predictors. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression with trial-studying score and HSMG as predictors of first-year mean grade in the Dutch-secondary-education subsample. The shared explained variance equaled .12; for credits the model was also significant (F(2,200) = 14.02, p < .01, R2 = .12) with similar unique contributions.

> "For FYMG the model was significant (F(2,198) = 44.45, p < .01 and R2 = .31), and the unique contribution for HSMG was sr2 = .15 and for the trial-studying test it was sr2 = .04."
