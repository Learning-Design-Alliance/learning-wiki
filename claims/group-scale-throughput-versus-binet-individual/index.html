Evidence¶

Bell 1919¶

Bell, J. C. (1919). Editorial: The National Research Council. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Volume X, No. 9. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The editorial contrasts the Binet scale, whose per-pupil individual examination "restrict[s] its employment to special cases," with a group scale that may examine "fifty or a hundred children" per hour. This is the editor's comparative argument, not a measured trial.

"Schoolmen generally have come to see the advantages of the Binet measurements of intelligence, but the necessity of examining each pupil individually has put such a time cost on the use of the scale as to restrict its employment to special cases. With such a group scale fifty or a hundred children may be examined in an hour, and the only further labor is found in scoring and tabulating the results."
