Xie Guanli 2026¶

Xie Guanli, Liao Jianglong, Tang Xiaoxia, Fu Han, Liu Duo, Deng Li, Wang Tao, Hong Xin. (2026). The impact of generative artificial intelligence on clinical skills and knowledge acquisition in medical undergraduates: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09458-3

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup meta-analysis of three studies (one quasi-experimental, two RCTs; n = 205 nursing students) pooling knowledge exam scores, showing a large pooled effect (SMD = 1.11) with I² = 76%.

"nursing students demonstrated significant improvement with GenAI compared with the control intervention (P = 0.0002, SMD = 1.11, 95%CI: 0.54 to 1.69, three studies, one quasi-experimental research and two RCTs, n = 205, I2 = 76%, random-effects model)"
