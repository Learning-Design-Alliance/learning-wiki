# Using iRULER in the writing revision task produced larger unaided skill transfer (post-task minus pre-task gains) than Text-LLM feedback

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants in iRULER improved the most on unaided revision (Mtext = 9.73, Mrubric = 9.66) compared to Text-LLM (Mtext = 4.17, Mrubric = 4.19), with the advantage over Rubric-LLM significant only on the rubric-based score. [→ Jingwen Bai 2026](#jingwen-bai-2026)

## Evidence

### Jingwen Bai 2026

Jingwen Bai, Wei Soon Cheong, Philippe Muller, and Brian Y Lim. 2026. iRULER: Intelligible Rubric-Based User-Defined LLM Evaluation for Revision. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790539

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Skill transfer measured as change in ΔScore (Post−Pre) in the writing revision experiment's unassisted pre- and post-tasks (N=48); significant main effect of Feedback Type (p < .0001). The Rubric-LLM difference (Mrubric = 6.33, p = .0005) was significant only for the rubric-based score.
