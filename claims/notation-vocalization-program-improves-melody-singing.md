## Evidence

### ŞAKTANLI 2011

ŞAKTANLI, S. C., & ÖZDEMİR, G. (2011). THE COMPARISON OF THE EFFECT OF BLOCK FLUTE ACCOMPANIED SONG TEACHING WITH MULTI-SOUND NOTATION AND VOCALIZATION PROGRAM ACCOMPANIED SONG TEACHING ON THE SUCCESS OF STUDENTS’ SONG LEARNING BEHAVIOR. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ926566

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing 51 experimental and 51 pilot students on melody behavior; experimental mean 2.59 (S=0.49) vs pilot mean 1.48 (S=0.46). The article reports "a significant difference in favor of the experimental group".

> "Table 1 shows that in the analysis of behavior of “singing the piece of song in accordance with its melody”, there is a significant difference in favor of the experimental group in comparison to the pilot group [t= 11.66; p< 0.05]."
