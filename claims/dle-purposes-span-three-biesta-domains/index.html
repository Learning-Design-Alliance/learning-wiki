Evidence¶

Fagerlund 2025¶

Fagerlund, J., Palsa, L., & Mertala, P. (2025). Exploration of domains of educational purpose in K-12 data literacy education research. Educational Research Review, 46, Article 100663. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100663

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's summary of findings section characterizes how socialization purposes appeared across the corpus, reporting "aims of assimilating students into the data-rich society, such as the data workforce". Subjectification is separately described as fostering personal ambitions and data agency.

"Socialization primarily included instrumental objectives (Ghodoosi et al., 2023) and aims of assimilating students into the data-rich society, such as the data workforce (see also Inverarity et al., 2022), as well as acclimatize them to leverage the possibilities of data"
