`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Serial mediation path model for GPA including high school mental health as a mediator, reported in Results §3.4 with bootstrapped confidence intervals. Two additional paths ran through grade school adjustment without behavior problems.

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "The final model considered indirect paths from preschool intervention to on‐time graduation and revealed six significant mediation paths (Figure 5, Table S9)."
