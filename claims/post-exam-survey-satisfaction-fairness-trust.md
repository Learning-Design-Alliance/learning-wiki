## Evidence

### Tan Chee-Kiat 2021

Tan Chee-Kiat, Chua Wei-Ling, Vu Charles Kien-Fong, Chang Jason Pik-Eu. (2021). High-stakes examinations during the COVID-19 pandemic: to proceed or not to proceed, that is the question. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2020-139241

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymised web-based survey of all 11 candidates and 8 examiners after the examination, using 5-point Likert-scale questions on pre-examination perceptions and sentiments about the actual examination; results are shown in figures 1 and 2. The article reports satisfaction, fairness and trust but prints no effect sizes.

> "The results showed that both candidates and examiners were satisfied with the conduct of the examinations (figures 1 and 2). Importantly, the candidates found that the examimation to be fair and the examiners trusted the reliability of the examination outcome."
