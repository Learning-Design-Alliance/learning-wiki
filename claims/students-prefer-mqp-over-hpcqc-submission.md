### Minh Chung 2025

Minh Chung, Yaknan Gambo, Burak Mete, Xiao-Ting Michelle To, Florian Krötz, Korbinian Staudacher, Martin Letras, Xiaolong Deng, Mounika Vavilala, Amir Raoofy, Jorge Echavarria, Luigi Iapichino, Laura Schulz, Josef Weidendorfer, Martin Schulz. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course survey of the first Q-BEAST iteration (feedback from 14 of 15 enrolled students). The authors attribute the preference to MQP's simpler access procedure from laptops, noting HPCQC 'is ideal for large-scale use cases' while almost all course jobs were not large scale.

> "Based on student feedback, 71.4% of them prefer to use the MQP for submitting quantum jobs with mqp-qiskit-provider3, while 28.6% prefer to use HPCQC."
