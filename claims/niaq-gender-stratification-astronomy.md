### Christine Lindstrøm 2016

Christine Lindstrøm, Vinesh Rajpaul, Morten Brendehaug, and Megan C. Engel. (2016). Perspectives on astronomy: probing Norwegian pre-service teachers and middle school students. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.044](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.044)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of ranking-task responses from the middle school NIAQ administration (535 year 8 and 387 year 10 students; 452 male and 452 female among those specifying gender). The article reports performance "significantly stratified along gender lines" with the 15–20% gap.

> "the students’ performance was significantly stratified along gender lines, with female students scoring on average 15–20% lower (depending on which metric was used to grade the ranking tasks) than the male students"
