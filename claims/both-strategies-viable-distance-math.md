## Evidence

### DePriter 2012

DePriter, T. (2012). An Investigation of Teaching Strategy in the Distance Learning Mathematics Classroom. Journal of Educators Online, 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1020037

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The article interprets its non-significant ANCOVA finding through Conger's (2005) view that a not statistically significant finding indicates equivalence, concluding both strategies produce equivalent achievement. Posttest means were 18/25 (72%) for both groups.

> "the results indicate that both teaching strategies are equivalent in terms of the resulting student achievement levels. This suggests that objectivist-based methods of instruction and constructivist-based methods both lead to equivalent levels of student achievement in mathematics"
