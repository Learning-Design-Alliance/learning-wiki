## Evidence

### Lee et al. 2010

Cheu-jey Lee, Glenda Moss, & Elaine Coughlin. (2010). Engaging Pre-Service Teachers in an Exploration of the Politics of Language. Scholar-Practitioner Quarterly, 5(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ974353

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Question 4 responses at Midwest University compared across two class cohorts: 13 of 20 fall 2009 participants versus 2 of 10 spring 2010 participants reported marginalized voices. The authors treat this as an observational pattern, not a tested effect.

> "Specifically, in the fall 2009, there were 20 participants, and as many as 13 said that their voices were marginalized. In contrast, in the spring 2010, we had only 10 participants, and only two commented that their voices were marginalized."
