## Evidence

### Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah 2025

Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah. (2025). Leading the AI transformation in schools: it starts with a digital mindset. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10439-w

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r?`

Structural equation model on the 179-leader survey; the mindset model explained 67% of variance in transformational and 43% in digital instructional leadership. Perspective taking: β = 0.671 (p < 0.001) with transformational, β = −0.068 (n.s.) with digital instructional; technology orientation: β = 0.272 (p < 0.05) with digital instructional only.

> "School leaders who see themselves as proactive are more likely to lead their schools both transformatively (β = 0.338, SE = 0.159, p < 0.05) and digital instructionally (β = 0.424, SE = 0.147, p < 0.05)."
