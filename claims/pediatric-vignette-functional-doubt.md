`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pediatric scenario in the same cross-sectional vignette survey (n = 238), presenting the identical adult case as a 7-year-old. The article reports diagnostic testing rates remained elevated, following a pattern similar to the adult scenario.

> "The identical clinical presentation was subsequently reframed as occurring in a 7‐year‐old child, with no alarm features. Under these conditions, 45.8% of students rated the pediatric presentation as unlikely to represent a functional disorder."
