## Evidence

### N. Finkelstein 2005

N. Finkelstein. (2005). Learning physics in context: a study of student learning about electricity and magnetism. Originally published 2004. [doi:10.1080/09500690500069491](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500690500069491)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post analysis in a ten-week design-experiment course for advanced physics majors and graduate students, using a 35-item diagnostic drawn from the CSEM and ECCA. The article reports "The average of individual student gains is 51%" with p<0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean pre- and post-test scores are, respectively, 54% (σ= 25%) and 74% (σ = 24%).  The average of individual student gains is 51% (σ=30%; N=13; p<0.001)."
