A within-course pre/post survey on the T5 camera color correction tutorial, conducted after the classroom lecture on color correction. The authors report "Fewer than 10 students provided a clear answer before taking the tutorial" and "almost all students provided the correct answer after going through the tutorial." No effect size is printed.
