Bachelor 2019¶

Bachelor, J. W., & Barros García, M. J. (2019). A comparison of heritage learners and L2 learners of Spanish: A study on compliment sequences in the classroom. Journal of Foreign Language Education and Technology, 4(1). http://jflet.com/jflet/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired t test comparing pre and post MCDCT scores of the heritage experimental group. The article reports no significant difference; no effect size accompanies this paired test.

"no significant difference was found in the heritage experimental group between the pre test scores (M=80.56, SD=12.59) and the post test scores (M=82.78, SD=16.02); t(17)=0.54, p=0.59."
