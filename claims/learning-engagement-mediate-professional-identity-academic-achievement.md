## Evidence

### Zou Liqin 2024

Zou Liqin, Xie Zhiquan, Tan Meichun, Ou Qiyun, Liao Mingsheng. (2024). The effect of professional identity on nursing academic achievement: the chain mediating effect of general self-efficacy and learning engagement. BMC Medical Education, 24, Article 1014. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05995-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression (Table 2) with learning engagement entered; the article reports "PI significantly and positively predicts LE (β = 0.305, p < 0.01), while LE significantly and positively predicts AA(β = 0.189, p < 0.01)".

> "Upon including LE in the regression equation, PI significantly and positively predicts LE (β = 0.305, p < 0.01), while LE significantly and positively predicts AA(β = 0.189, p < 0.01). Hypothesis 3 is supported."

### Zou Liqin 2024 (2)

Zou Liqin, Xie Zhiquan, Tan Meichun, Ou Qiyun, Liao Mingsheng. (2024). The effect of professional identity on nursing academic achievement: the chain mediating effect of general self-efficacy and learning engagement. BMC Medical Education, 24, Article 1014. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05995-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Deviation-corrected percentile bootstrap test (repeated sampling 5,000 times) of the PI→LE→AA path; the confidence interval [0.082, 0.137] excludes 0, and "the mediating effect value is 0.108, indicating that LE is the mediating variable between PI and AA".

> "For PI→LE→AA, the confidence interval is [0.082, 0.137], excluding 0, and the mediating effect value is 0.108, indicating that LE is the mediating variable between PI and AA."
