## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Student interactions in which pairs negotiate whether and when to move from one EMF phase to the next are not captured by the EMF, and a complementary metacognitive framework is needed to understand them. [→ Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2015](#dimitri-r-dounas-frazer-2015)

## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2015

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Kevin L. Van De Bogart, MacKenzie R. Stetzer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). The role of modeling in troubleshooting: an example from electronics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.021](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.021)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of transcript Lines 18–33, in which two students cyclically sought to understand one another's thinking about whether the Stage 2 output was negligibly small or its inputs were mixed up. The negotiation culminated in a decision not to follow up on the mixed-up-inputs proposal; the authors state a complementary framework is needed.

> "such interactions are not well-captured by the EMF because they do not constitute a modeling phase, but instead represent students’ negotiations about whether and when to move from one phase to the next."
