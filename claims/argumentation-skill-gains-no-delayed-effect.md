Larrain, A., Freire, P., Grau, V., López, P., Salvat, I., Silva, M., & Gastellu, V. (2018). The effect of peer-group argumentative dialogue on delayed gains in scientific content knowledge. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 162, 67–87. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20263

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second multiple regression testing argumentation-skill gains as a moderator of the relationship between peer-group argumentation and content knowledge. "Pre- to post-gains in argumentation skills do not predict delayed learning gains" under the stated controls; the model explained 69.4% of variance. No effect size is printed for the null predictor.

> "The second model (shown in Table 4.8) explained 69.4% of the variance (F(4/30) = 20.32, p < .01) and showed that pre- to post-gains in argumentation skills do not predict delayed learning gains when pre-measures of content knowledge, proportional individual argumentation and time of group work are controlled."
