### Dankbaar Mary E.W. 2017

Dankbaar Mary E.W., Roozeboom Maartje Bakhuys, Oprins Esther A.P.B., Rutten Frans, van Merrienboer Jeroen J.G., van Saase Jan L.C.M., Schuit Stephanie C.E. (2017). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttraining assessment, part of the regular certified emergency care course, rated residents in a 1-case scenario test with a standardized simulation patient (1 rater per candidate). Scores were "similar for both groups"; no effect size was printed.

> "At the end of the 2-week training, scores on the competency and global performance scales were similar for both groups (Table 3)."
