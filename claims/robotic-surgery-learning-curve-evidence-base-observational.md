# The evidence base on surgeon learning curves in robot-assisted surgery consists almost entirely of small, single-arm observational studies of patient data

Systematic review of 49 eligible patient-data studies (from 68 unique studies identified via MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane and hand searches, PRISMA-compliant). The review reports all were observational, with 82 per cent retrospective and "35 of 49, 71 per cent" single-arm; most included fewer than five robotic surgeons.

> "All were observational in design. Data were analysed retrospectively in 40 of 49 studies (82 per cent), and the remaining nine studies (18 per cent) were prospective in design. The majority (35 of 49, 71 per cent) were single‐arm studies"
