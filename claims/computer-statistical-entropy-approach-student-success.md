### Thomas A. Moore and Daniel V. Schroeder 1997

Thomas A. Moore and Daniel V. Schroeder. (1997). A different approach to introducing statistical mechanics. Am. J. Phys. 65 (1), 26–36. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.18490

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' report of implementation in calculus-based introductory courses at five colleges and an upper-division thermodynamics course at Weber State University; this is their stated classroom experience, not a controlled study. They write that students are "quite able to understand the fundamental concepts".

> "We have found that students at either level are quite able to understand the fundamental concepts of statistical mechanics that we are trying to teach."
