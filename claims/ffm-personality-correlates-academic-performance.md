## Evidence

### Poropat 2009

Poropat, A. E. (2009). A Meta-Analysis of the Five-Factor Model of Personality and Academic Performance. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014996

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis (Hunter & Schmidt method) of FFM-academic performance correlations, with aggregated sample sizes ranging from 58,522 (Agreeableness) to 70,926 (Conscientiousness). Correlations were corrected for scale reliability and converted to Cohen's d; the author reports "d = .46" for Conscientiousness, a medium-sized effect.

> "Thus, the average relationship of academic performance with Conscientiousness (d = .46) can be seen as a medium-sized effect, the relationships with Agreeableness (d = .14) and Openness (d = .24) as small effects, and the relationships with Emotional Stability and Extraversion as relatively minor."
