## Evidence

### Jianhao Shen 2024

Jianhao Shen, Ye Yuan, Srbuhi Mirzoyan, Ming Zhang, Chenguang Wang. (2024). MEASURING VISION-LANGUAGE STEM SKILLS OF NEURAL MODELS. Published as a conference paper at ICLR 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.17205](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.17205)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Grade-level analysis plotting model exam scores per grade (Figure 6). The authors attribute the flat trend to neural models being trained on data including all grade-level questions simultaneously while humans gradually learn from lower to higher grade-level questions; the average elementary-grade score is 40.8 versus the human reference of 90.

> "Surprisingly, there is no obvious performance drop as the increase in grade levels. This implies the learning curve for neural models may be different from that of humans."
