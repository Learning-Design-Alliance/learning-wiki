# Fourth-year medical students taught with combined CBL and clinical pathway scored significantly higher on post-internship theoretical and skill tests than students taught traditionally

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized comparison of 104 experimental vs 102 control students, the experimental group performed significantly better on post-internship theoretical and skill tests (P < 0.001). [→ Wang Xiaoxia 2024](#wang-xiaoxia-2024)

## Evidence

### Wang Xiaoxia 2024

Wang Xiaoxia, Che Xuan, Tang Xuedong, Xu Zhengfen. (2024). Application of combined teaching method of case-based-learning and clinical pathway in practical gynecological teaching. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.17813

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group comparison during a 4-week gynecological internship in Jiaxing Maternity and Child Health Care Hospital: experimental group (n = 104) received combined CBL and clinical pathway teaching, control group (n = 102) traditional teaching. Postinternship skill and theoretical means of "94.33 ± 3.53 and 88.39 ± 6.45" exceeded control's 88.91 ± 5.39 and 81.38 ± 7.60 (P < 0.001 per Table 2).

> "In the experimental group, the mean skill test score and the mean theoretical test score were 94.33 ± 3.53 and 88.39 ± 6.45 in the postinternship test and 79.58 ± 4.24 and 70.77 ± 9.54 in the preinternship test, respectively."
