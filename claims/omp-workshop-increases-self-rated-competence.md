# After an OMP workshop, tutors' self-rated tutoring competence and frequency of giving feedback on what was not done well increased significantly

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Tutors' self-rated competence in tutoring increased significantly after the OMP workshop (post mean 4.2 vs pre 3.9, p = 0.05). [→ Rothlind E 2025](#rothlind-e-2025)
`q2 i?` Tutors' self-rated frequency of providing feedback on what was not done well increased significantly (post mean 4.2 vs pre 3.8, p = 0.03). [→ Rothlind E 2025](#rothlind-e-2025)

## Evidence

### Rothlind E 2025

Rothlind E, Salminen H, Rolfhamre K, Karlgren K, Toth-Pal E. (2025). Adapting the one minute preceptor model to a Swedish context – clinical tutors’ experiences of its usage in primary health care. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2025.2508370

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Pre/post survey comparison using Wilcoxon rank sum test in Stata 15.1 with significance level 0.05; 87 tutors completed the pre-survey and 32 the post-survey. Table 5 reports "I feel competent as a tutor" 3.9 to 4.2 (p = 0.05) and "I provide students with feedback on what was not done well" 3.8 to 4.2 (p = 0.03).

> "The results showed a significant increase in the tutors’ self-rated competence in tutoring and in offering feedback on ‘what was not done so well’."
