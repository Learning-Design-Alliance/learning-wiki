Evidence¶

Pamela Portelli 2018¶

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Cross-sectional Spearman correlation analysis at pre-intervention in the cluster RCT (N = 118). The article reports "a statistically significant relationship between Scale 2 AE scores and alcohol consumption" with R = 0.47, p < 0.001, supporting the first hypothesis.

"Results of Spearman’s rho correlation coefficient (R) revealed a statistically significant relationship between Scale 2 AE scores and alcohol consumption at pre-intervention (R = 0.47, N = 118, p < 0.001)."

Related Claims¶

  • Within the intervention group alcohol consumption fell significantly at 1 and 2 months but not at 4 months, while the control group significantly increased consumption at 4 months — related
  • The AEC intervention significantly lowered positive alcohol expectancy scores (Scales 2, 3 and 7) relative to control immediately after and at 4 months post-intervention, but not arousal expectancies (Scale 6) — related
  • The brief Alcohol Expectancy Challenge produced no significant between-group reductions in alcohol consumption at 1, 2 or 4 months post-intervention — related
  • The article argues that challenging alcohol expectancies alone may be insufficient, and that drinking refusal self-efficacy is a more salient cognitive construct to target — related
