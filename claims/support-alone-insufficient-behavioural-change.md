### N.G. Holmes 2015

N.G. Holmes, Carl E. Wieman, D.A. Bonn. (2015). Teaching critical thinking. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1505329112

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of maximum reflective-comment-level distributions in week 2 of the first-year cohort comparison. The article characterizes the week-2 difference as moderate and draws the authors' inference that change required repeated practice over time. No effect size printed.

> "In ﬁgure 4, we see only a moderate diﬀerence between the experimental and control groups in week 2, even though the experimental group received signiﬁcant behavioural support in week 2. This suggests that the support alone is insuﬃcient to create signiﬁcant behavioural change."
