Greenberg Daphne, Wise Justin, Morris Robin, Fredrick Laura, Nanda Alice O., Pae Hye-K. (2011). A randomized control study of instructional approaches for struggling adult readers. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3237050/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics from Table 2 for the 198 participants in the final analyses. The authors state these findings add to knowledge on profiles of struggling adult readers, whose difficulties are apparent in all processes studied, especially receptive vocabulary, fluency, and phonological awareness.

> "Mean standard and scale scores that were available for this age group, were at least 1 standard deviation below the mean on every measure, and scores were 2 standard deviations below the mean for measures assessing receptive vocabulary, phonological awareness, and oral reading fluency of words, nonwords, and connected text."
