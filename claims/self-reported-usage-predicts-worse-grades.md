## Evidence

### Tim Schulz van Endert 2021

Tim Schulz van Endert. (2021). Addictive use of digital devices in young children: Associations with delay discounting, self-control and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253058

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression (Table 4) predicting grade average from gender, age, years of ownership, pocket money, self-reported usage and self-control in 75 children; model R2 = 0.31, F = 4.89, p < 0.001. "self-reported usage (β = 0.26, p = 0.040) were the only significant predictors of grade average".

> "As displayed in Table 4, self-control (β = -0.30, p = 0.009) and self-reported usage (β = 0.26, p = 0.040) were the only significant predictors of grade average. R2 of the overall model was 0.31 with an F-statistic of 4.89 and p < 0.001."
