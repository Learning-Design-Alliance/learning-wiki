## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Expertise in Introductory Physics Using Categorization Task. Originally published 1974. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.08781](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.08781)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Histogram comparison (Figure 3) of the percentage of questions placed in good categories by 21 graduate students, 7 physics faculty and the calculus-based introductory class of 180 students. The article reports a "large overlap between the two groups" and a wide distribution of performance among introductory and graduate students.

> "Although the categorization of problems by the calc-based group is not on par with the categorization by physics graduate students, there is a large overlap between the two groups [47]."
