F. Bouquet, J. Bobroff, M. Fuchs-Gallezot, and L. Maurines. (2016). Project-based physics labs using low-cost open-source hardware. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06659)

### F. Bouquet 2016 (2)

F. Bouquet, J. Bobroff, M. Fuchs-Gallezot, and L. Maurines. (2016). Project-based physics labs using low-cost open-source hardware. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06659)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor feedback; some students considered obtaining data enough to complete a study. Mentoring difficulty was quoted as knowing how much to help without doing the project in students' stead.

> "As downsides, it was noted that the students often focus on the experiment itself to the detriment of the physics at play and the analysis of the data"
