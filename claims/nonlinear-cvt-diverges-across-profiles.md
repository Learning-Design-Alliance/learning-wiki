## Evidence

### Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu 2026

Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu. (2026). Testing the Relative Universalism of Psychological Theories: A Person-Centered Analysis of Control-Value Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10155-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Robustness analyses with nonlinear specifications (control–value interactions and a cubic control term). The article reports results "largely consistent with the linear models" with only modest predictive gains, but more pronounced profile differences; in the low-control profile the cubic coefficient becomes positive, indicating a different functional form.

> "In the full-sample and grouping analyses, the cubic Academic Control term typically shows negative coefficients for negative emotions, suggesting attenuation of the linear effect at both low and high levels of control. This pattern contrasts with the over- and underchallenge hypothesis for boredom"
