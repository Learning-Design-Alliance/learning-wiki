### Luotong Hui 2021

Luotong Hui, Anique B. H. de Bruin, Jeroen Donkers, & Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2021). Does Individual Performance Feedback Increase the Use of Retrieval Practice? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x

Experiment 2 paired-samples t-tests against the day-1 baseline: both the general-feedback group and the retrieval-benefit subgroup of the individual-feedback group "chose retrieval practice more often on day 8", with d = .29 and d = .34 respectively.

### Luotong Hui 2021 (2)

Luotong Hui, Anique B. H. de Bruin, Jeroen Donkers, & Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2021). Does Individual Performance Feedback Increase the Use of Retrieval Practice? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 day-15 comparisons against baseline: only the retrieval-benefit group still chose retrieval practice more often (d = .44), while the general-feedback group's increase was no longer significant (p = .08). No direct day-15 difference between the groups (t(88) = .68, p = .50).

> "In the long term, however, only the retrieval-benefit group chose retrieval practice more often on day 15 compared to the baseline ( t(30) = 2.49, p =. 0 2 , d = .44), which was not the case for the general-feedback group (t(58) = 1.80, p = .08)."
