## Evidence

### Zerbini Giulia 2025

Zerbini Giulia, Schneider Pia, Reicherts Miriam, Roob Nina, Jung-Can Kathrin, Kunz Miriam, Reicherts Philipp. (2025). A novel multi-measure approach to study medical students’ communication performance and predictors of their communication quality - a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07018-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the cross-sectional simulated-consultation study (N = 43), turn-taking was annotated from video as the number of speaker/listener role changes; an average of 59.8 turns occurred. "no significant difference was found" between semesters; equivalence was not tested.

> "When comparing turn-taking between semesters, no significant difference was found (t [41] = 0.94, p =.351, d = 0.29) between 1st and 5th /7th semester"

### Zerbini Giulia 2025 (2)

Zerbini Giulia, Schneider Pia, Reicherts Miriam, Roob Nina, Jung-Can Kathrin, Kunz Miriam, Reicherts Philipp. (2025). A novel multi-measure approach to study medical students’ communication performance and predictors of their communication quality - a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07018-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Verbal dominance, the ratio of students' to SP's speaking time, averaged 2.3 (students spoke 5:05 min vs. SP 2:03 min). "verbal dominance was not significantly different between 1st and 5th /7th semester"; equivalence was not tested.

> "verbal dominance was not significantly different between 1st and 5th /7th semester (t [41] = 0.86, p =.397, d = 0.26)"
