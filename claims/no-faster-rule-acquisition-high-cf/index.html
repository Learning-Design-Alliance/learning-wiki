Evidence¶

Feng X 2020¶

Feng X, Perceval GJ, Feng W and Feng C (2020) High Cognitive Flexibility Learners Perform Better in Probabilistic Rule Learning. Front. Psychol. 11:415. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00415

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive learning-curve analysis of rule learners in the coin-searching task. Both groups reached the learning point in block 2 (high CF 244, low CF 257 trials); the article reports no significance test of this difference.

"The average learning point of all rule learners was 251 trials, which is the 71 st trial in block 2 (this block contains the speciﬁc feedback about coin position). Both the high and low ﬂexibility groups reached their learning point in the second block (high CF group – 244, low CF group – 257)."
