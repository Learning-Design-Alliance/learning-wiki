## Evidence

### Hoyniak 2015

Hoyniak, C. P., Petersen, I. T., McQuillan, M. E., Staples, A. D., & Bates, J. E. (2015). Less Efficient Neural Processing Related to Irregular Sleep and Less Sustained Attention in Toddlers. Developmental Neuropsychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/87565641.2015.1016162

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of 20 cases (15 unique toddlers, ages 2–3) from an EEG lab visit; sustained attention coded from free-play video using the Ruff and Capozzoli system. The study found "a significant negative association between target P3 latencies and the proportion of time focused" with a printed effect size of r[16]=−.51 (p=.032).

> "There was a significant negative association between target P3 latencies and the proportion of time focused during the sustained attention task (r[16]=−.51, p=.032), such that children with longer P3 latencies demonstrated less sustained attention."
