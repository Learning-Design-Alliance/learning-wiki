# PBL students produce more integrated concept maps than LBL students in occupational therapy coursework

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The distribution of the three concept-map categories differed significantly between PBL and LBL groups, with PBL students predominantly producing integrated maps. [→ Hung Chia-Hui 2015](#hung-chia-hui-2015)

## Evidence

### Hung Chia-Hui 2015

Hung Chia-Hui, Lin Chen-Yung. (2015). Using concept mapping to evaluate knowledge structure in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0496-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square homogeneity analysis of map morphology from a quasi-experimental study of 52 third-year occupational therapy undergraduates randomly assigned to PBL or LBL groups. The chi-square test of the three categories was significant (χ2(2) = 6.84, p = 0.033).

> "Based on the analysis, 92.3 % of the first and 84.6 % of the second drawings of the PBL group were integrated maps, and 70.4 % of the first and 66.7 % of the second drawings of the LBL group were sorted into this category."
