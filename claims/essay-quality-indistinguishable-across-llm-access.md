## Evidence

### Christenson 2026

Christenson, J., de Langis, K., Hayati, S. A., & Kang, D. (2026). Effects of Varying LLM Access on Essay Writing Behavior. [doi:10.18653/v1/2026.bea-1.48](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.bea-1.48)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Rubric-based evaluation of essays written by 24 randomly assigned college students across three LLM access conditions, graded independently by two university writing consultants. The study "do[es] not find any significant differences between groups" on total rubric score.

> "The statistical power of our study only allows us to detect very large effects of group on grading, and we do not find any significant differences between groups."
