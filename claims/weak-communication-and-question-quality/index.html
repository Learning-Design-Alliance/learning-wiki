Evidence¶

Daniel C. Humphrey 2009¶

Daniel C. Humphrey, Patrick M. Shields. (2009). High School Reform in Chicago Public Schools: An Overview. SRI International / Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=High+School+Reform+in+Chicago+Public+Schools+An+Overview

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observation ratings on communication dimensions (Exhibit 8) from the 78-classroom exploratory study. The authors attribute these deficiencies to the large numbers of beginning teachers (27% overall) and the early timing of observations; no effect size was printed.

"the majority of teachers in IDS and Renaissance 2010 schools failed to reach the proficient level for such key teaching skills as communicating the importance of the lesson they were teaching, setting high expectations for learning, and providing clear explanation of content."

Daniel C. Humphrey 2009 (2)¶

Daniel C. Humphrey, Patrick M. Shields. (2009). High School Reform in Chicago Public Schools: An Overview. SRI International / Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=High+School+Reform+in+Chicago+Public+Schools+An+Overview

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observation ratings on instructional demand dimensions (Exhibit 9) from the same 78-classroom study. The report states these elements "generally suggested a low level of instructional rigor"; no effect size was printed.

"Insofar as the quality of the questions they asked, a majority of teachers—even AMPS teachers—ranked at the basic or unsatisfactory level."
