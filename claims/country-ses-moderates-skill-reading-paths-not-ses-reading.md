# Country SES moderates the linguistic-skill-to-reading paths, with larger phonological-awareness and vocabulary effects in developed countries, but does not moderate the direct SES-reading paths

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2`–`r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Phonological awareness had more impact on reading accuracy in developed countries than developing countries (β = 0.15, p < 0.001). [→ Li 2021](#li-2021)
`q3 i?` Vocabulary knowledge influenced reading comprehension more in developed than developing countries (β = 0.11, p = 0.001), and country SES did not moderate SES-reading or SES-language paths. [→ Li 2021 (2)](#li-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Li 2021

Li, J., Peng, P., Ding, N., Ma, X., & Zhao, J. (2021). How does family socioeconomic status influence children's reading ability? Evidence from meta-analytic structural equation modeling. https://www.frontiersin.org

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

OSMASEM on the reading accuracy model (omnibus χ2(5) = 40.92, p < 0.001), with country SES coded developed (1) versus developing (0); the country moderation of the PA-accuracy path explained 19.38% of between-study heterogeneity.

> "phonological awareness had more impact on reading accuracy in developed countries compared to developing countries (β = 0.15, p < 0.001). About 19.38% variance of the between-study heterogeneity was explained on the effects of PA on reading accuracy"

### Li 2021 (2)

Li, J., Peng, P., Ding, N., Ma, X., & Zhao, J. (2021). How does family socioeconomic status influence children's reading ability? Evidence from meta-analytic structural equation modeling. https://www.frontiersin.org

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

In the OSMASEM country-SES analyses, the VK-to-comprehension path was also moderated (β = 0.11, p = 0.001, 19.05% of heterogeneity), but no SES-related path was moderated; the fluency model's omnibus test was not significant (χ2(5) = 1.45, p = 0.919).

> "Country SES did not significantly moderate the relations between SES and language and reading abilities and any of the remaining paths in reading accuracy and reading comprehension model"
