`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 420 clinical medical students from 13 Sudanese universities using a structured self-administered online questionnaire. The knowledge section comprised multiple-choice and true/false items scored 0–19; the study reports "73.8% of participants demonstrated a moderate level of knowledge" and a mean score of 11.2 (2.4).

> "Overall, the mean knowledge score (SD) was 11.2 (2.4) with a range of 3–19. 73.8% of participants demonstrated a moderate level of knowledge, 22.1% had a poor level, and only 4% exhibited a good level of knowledge"
