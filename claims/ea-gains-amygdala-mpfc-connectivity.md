# Improvements in empathic accuracy after Crystals gameplay were associated with strengthened amygdala–MPFC resting-state connectivity relative to Bastion

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The relationship between empathic accuracy improvement and VMPFC–right amygdala connectivity change was significantly greater for Crystals than Bastion participants, with no significant relationship within Bastion. [→ Kral 2018](#kral-2018)
`q3 i?` The same pattern held for DMPFC–right amygdala connectivity: improved empathic accuracy was associated with increased connectivity in Crystals compared to Bastion participants. [→ Kral 2018 (2)](#kral-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Kral 2018

Kral, T. R. A., Stodola, D. E., Birn, R. M., Mumford, J. A., Solis, E., Flook, L., Patsenko, E. G., Anderson, C. G., Steinkuehler, C. & Davidson, R. J. (2018). Neural correlates of video game empathy training in adolescents: a randomized trial. npj Science of Learning 3:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0029-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ROI analysis of resting-state connectivity change in the randomized trial; within Crystals, improved empathic accuracy was associated with increased right amygdala–VMPFC connectivity (t(29) = 2.95, p = 0.01, b = 0.90), while "there was no statistically significant relationship in the Bastion group" (t(31) = 0.10, p = 0.60).

> "The relationship between changes in empathic accuracy and VMPFC – right amygdala rs-fMRI was significantly greater for Crystals versus Bastion ( t(60) = 2.23, p = 0.03, b = 0.79, CI = [0.08, 1.50]"

### Kral 2018 (2)

Kral, T. R. A., Stodola, D. E., Birn, R. M., Mumford, J. A., Solis, E., Flook, L., Patsenko, E. G., Anderson, C. G., Steinkuehler, C. & Davidson, R. J. (2018). Neural correlates of video game empathy training in adolescents: a randomized trial. npj Science of Learning 3:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0029-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parallel ROI analysis for DMPFC–right amygdala connectivity showed the same pattern: a significantly positive relationship within Crystals (t(29) = 2.48, p = 0.02, b = 0.79) and no significant relationship within Bastion (t(31) = −0.70, p = 0.95). No group difference in connectivity change itself was found for either seed.

> "improved empathic accuracy from pre-intervention to post-intervention was associated with inreased rs-fMRI connectivity in Crystals participants compared to Bastion ( t(59) = 2.42, p = 0.02, b = 0.96, CI [0.17, 1.70]"
