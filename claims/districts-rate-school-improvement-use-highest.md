# Districts report using test scores more to evaluate school improvement and district programs than to evaluate students, and rarely to evaluate principals or teachers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On a forced-choice usage rating, about 77 percent of districts chose the two highest categories for using test scores to evaluate school improvement, higher than for students, programs, principals, or teachers. [→ John Q. Easton 2000](#john-q-easton-2000)
`q2 i?` Few districts use test scores extensively to evaluate principals or teachers; in both cases the most frequent response is "not at all." [→ John Q. Easton 2000](#john-q-easton-2000)

### John Q. Easton 2000

The report notes "in actual ratings of usage they report more use of assessment results for evaluating school improvement and district programs than students."
