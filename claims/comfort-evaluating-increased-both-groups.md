## Evidence

### Kerr Brendan 2013

Kerr Brendan, Hawkins Trisha Lee-Ann, Herman Robert, Barnes Sue, Kaufmann Stephanie, Fraser Kristin, Ma Irene W. Y. (2013). Feasibility of scenario-based simulation training versus traditional workshops in continuing medical education: a randomized controlled trial. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.21312

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre/post comparisons in the pilot RCT (traditional N=10, simulation N=17) showed comfort in evaluating increased significantly in both groups, with printed effect sizes "d=1.1" and "d=1.8". Comfort in participating in care of medically complicated pregnant patients also increased in both groups.

> "Self-reported comfort in evaluating a pregnant patient with shortness of breath also significantly increased in both groups (d=1.1; p= 0.02 for the traditional group and d=1.8; p<0.001 for the simulation group; Table 2)."
