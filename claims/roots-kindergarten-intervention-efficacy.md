## Evidence

### Clarke 2017

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Kosty, D., Kurtz Nelson, E., Smolkowski, K., Fien, H., & Turtura, J. (2017). Testing the efficacy of a kindergarten mathematics intervention by small group size. AERA Open, 3(2), 1–16. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858417706899

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Partially nested Time × Condition mixed-model analysis of the RCT comparing ROOTS students nested within small groups with unclustered controls. The article reports gains of "18.3 for the ASPENS (g = 0.52)", "2.0 for the TEMA standard score (g = 0.25)", and "4.7 for the RAENS (g = 0.76)".

> "Students in the ROOTS condition made greater gains than control students on the ASPENS (t = 6.41, df = 272, p < .0001), TEMA standard scores (t = 3.76, df = 263, p = .0002), and RAENS (t = 9.36, df = 315, p < .0001)."
