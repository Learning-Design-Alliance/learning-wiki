# Suddenness and Certainty ratings decrease as RAPM item difficulty increases, with Certainty showing a strong effect

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Suddenness reliably decreased as a function of RAPM item difficulty with a moderate effect size. [→ Adam Chuderski 2021](#adam-chuderski-2021)
`q3 i3` Certainty reliably decreased as a function of RAPM item difficulty with a strong effect size. [→ Adam Chuderski 2021 (2)](#adam-chuderski-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Adam Chuderski 2021

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

BANOVA on 1017 correct-solution ratings across 20 RAPM items with participant as random factor, in a study of 100 adults. The article reports Suddenness "reliably decreased as a function of the item difficulty" with ηp2 = .07, BF10 > 999.

> "Suddenness reliably decreased as a function of the item difficulty, BF 10 > 999, and yielded a moderate effect size of ηp2 = .07."

### Adam Chuderski 2021 (2)

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same BANOVA on correct-solution ratings across the 20 RAPM items. Certainty showed a strong difficulty effect, ηp2 = .24, BF10 > 999, and Pleasure yielded a virtually identical effect.

> "A reliable effect was observed also for Certainty, BF 10 > 999, and it was strong, ηp2 = .24 (Pleasure yielded a virtually identical effect)."
