q2 · i? · review · r2

Review discussion of implementation challenges, attributed to the reviewed literature. The review reports common challenges were limited availability of expertise and a lack of willingness to contribute, and underscores the importance of timing initiatives, selecting participants, and ensuring facilitators have sufficient experience and training.

"In particular, IPE could exacerbate negative relationships between disciplines if the training was not well planned and implemented."
