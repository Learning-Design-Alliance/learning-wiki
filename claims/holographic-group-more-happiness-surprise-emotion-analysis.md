## Evidence

### Rebeca Cerezo 2024

Rebeca Cerezo, Vicente Calderón, Cristóbal Romero. (2024). A holographic mobile-based application for practicing pronunciation of basic English vocabulary for Spanish speaking children. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of 140 photos (48 CG, 46 EG1, 46 EG2) with the Microsoft Emotion API during week 4. MANOVA showed group differences (Wilks' Lambda λ = .375; F(16, 260)=10.300; p<.001; η2p=.388); happiness differed with η2p=.409, neutral η2p=.462, sadness η2p=.088, surprise η2p=.292.

> "According to Games-Howell there were statistically significant differences between every group in happiness, neutral, and surprise (p<.001), and between CG and EG2 in sadness; mean values were EG2>EG1>CG for happiness and surprise, and CG >EG1>EG2 for neutral and sadness."
