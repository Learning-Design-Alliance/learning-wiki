## Evidence

### Östlund Ann-Sofi 2015

Östlund Ann-Sofi, Kristofferzon Marja-Leena, Häggström Elisabeth, Wadensten Barbro. (2015). Primary care nurses’ performance in motivational interviewing: a quantitative descriptive study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-015-0304-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of MITI 3.1.1 summary scores across the twelve nurses' 32 recorded sessions. The study reports that "percent complex reflection" was the score most often at or above threshold and "percent open question" and "MI spirit rating" the least often; no effect size is reported.

> "The summary score “percent complex reflection” was the one on which most nurses achieved beginning proficiency/competency, followed by “empathy,” and the summary scores on which the fewest achieved beginning proficiency/competency were “percent open question” and “MI spirit rating”."
