## Evidence

### Willower 1992

Willower, Donald J. (1992). Dewey's Theory of Inquiry and Reflective Administration. Paper presented at the annual convention of the University Council for Educational Administration, Minneapolis. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED355669

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper's stated limitations of reflective methods, in its institutionalization section. It adds that deciding which problems deserve extended reflective analysis is itself a problem, that numerous decisions are not really controversial or complex, and that a few visible failures can have serious negative consequences if realistic expectations have not been established beforehand. This is the authors' interpretive position, not a tested result.

> "Obviously, there are inherent dangers in overanalysis and in seeing reflective methods as endsinstead of means, what Dewey called the vices of reflection"
