## Evidence

### Ataya J 2026

Ataya J, Ismail A, Kousa A, Shahoud Z, Alhalbi R, Ghanem R, Ranjous Y, Zaitouna M. (2026). Assessing theoretical knowledge related to surgical and clinical skills in Syrian medical students: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051241

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-level analysis of 20 MCQs among 500 participants. Only "9.0% answered the nail-pitting item correctly"; radiology items showed 33.8% (MRI hemorrhage) and 29.0% (CT umbilical hernia) correct rates.

> "For clinical knowledge items, the first step in patient examination had the highest correct-response rate (98.4%). In contrast, only 9.0% answered the nail-pitting item correctly, 13.0% answered the jugular venous pulse item corr ectly, and 16.8% correctly identified the early sign of disseminated intravascular coagulation."
