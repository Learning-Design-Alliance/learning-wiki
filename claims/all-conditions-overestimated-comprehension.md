# Students in all conditions significantly overestimated their current understanding after the learning activity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` One-sample t-tests against accurate judgment (bias = 0) showed significant overestimation of comprehension in every condition, with no condition differences in monitoring accuracy. [→ Jacob Leonie 2021](#jacob-leonie-2021)

## Evidence

### Jacob Leonie 2021

Jacob Leonie, Lachner Andreas, Scheiter Katharina. (2021). Does increasing social presence enhance the effectiveness of writing explanations? PLoS ONE 16(4): e0250406. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250406

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

One-sample t-tests on monitoring bias in the four-condition experiment; the article reports d values from .50 to .85. An ANCOVA on post-activity judgments found no condition effect, F(3, 132) = 2.41, p = .070, ηp2=.03, with BF01 = 51.68.

> "Results revealed that students in all conditions significantly overestimated their current understanding (retrieval condition: t(33) = 3.43, p = .002, d = .58; written condition: t(33) = 3.74, p < .001, d = .64; chat condition: t(33) = 5.09, p < .001, d = .85; oral condition: t(33) = 2.81, p = .008, d = .50)."
