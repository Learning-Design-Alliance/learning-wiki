## Evidence

### Reinhard Pekrun 2022

Reinhard Pekrun, Herbert W. Marsh, Felix Suessenbach, Anne C. Frenzel, Thomas Goetz. (2022). School grades and students' emotions: Longitudinal models of within-person reciprocal effects. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101626

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RI-CLPM cross-lagged paths from the PALMA five-wave study (N = 3,425). The article reports "significant negative effects" of grades on anger and hopelessness and of these four negative emotions on grades, within persons.

> "Grades had significant negative effects on subsequent anger and hopelessness; anger, anxiety, boredom, and hopelessness had significant negative effects on subsequent grades."

### Reinhard Pekrun 2022 (2)

Reinhard Pekrun, Herbert W. Marsh, Felix Suessenbach, Anne C. Frenzel, Thomas Goetz. (2022). School grades and students' emotions: Longitudinal models of within-person reciprocal effects. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101626

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same RI-CLPM models: several paths did not reach significance. The article notes "the direction of these effects was negative as well" despite non-significance; equivalence was not tested.

> "The effects of grades on anxiety, shame, and boredom, and the effects of shame on grades were not significant."
