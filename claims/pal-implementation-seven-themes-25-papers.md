A five-stage thematic synthesis (PRISMA-guided searches of seven databases, Garside quality appraisal) of qualitative PAL studies in primary school settings. The synthesis identified "seven themes and 31 sub-themes from 25 eligible papers", four of them teacher-level factors.

> "We identified seven themes and 31 sub-themes from 25 eligible papers. Four themes summarised teacher-level factors: PAL benefits, teachers’ beliefs about own capabilities, PAL teacher training, PAL delivery."
