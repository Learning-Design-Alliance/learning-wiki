Evidence¶

Singaravelu 2014¶

Singaravelu, G. (2014). Impact of Gadget Based Learning of Grammar in English at Standard II. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 2, April-June. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068432

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Equivalent-group experiment in two Mannargudi schools (40 experimental and 40 control students each). Table 2 reports experimental pre-test means of 9.58 and 13.65 rising to post-test means of 19.65 and 19.86, which the author reads as the gadget being "more effective than conventional method".

"Gadget Based Learning is more effective than conventional method in learning English Grammar at standard II in both types of schools through mean score of pre test of Experimental group in two schools respectively 9.58,13.65"
