## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a quasi-experiment comparing a fully flipped format with a semi-traditional format integrating active learning techniques, the flipped classroom per se neither improved nor worsened hard or soft skill outcomes. [→ Betti 2022](#betti-2022)

## Evidence

### Betti 2022

Betti, A., Biderbost, P., & García Domonte, A. (2022). Can active learning techniques simultaneously develop students’ hard and soft skills? Evidence from an international relations class. PLoS ONE 17(4): e0265408. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265408

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 63 third-year Dual Degree IR and BA students split alphabetically into a fully flipped experimental group (31) and a semi-traditional control group (32). The article reports this headline null finding with no effect size, so no magnitude is stated.

> "Our main finding is that the FC per se neither improves nor worsens students’ performance in terms of hard and soft skills."
