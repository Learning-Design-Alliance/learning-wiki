Evidence¶

Mary H. Sawyer 1991¶

Mary H. Sawyer. (1991). A Review of Research in Revising Instructional Text. Journal of Reading Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1080/10862969109547744

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Zimmer (1985), that a full package of textbook-style structural aids produced no improvement, and Zimmer hypothesized the aids might interfere with competent readers' natural strategies.

"Zimmer (1985) found no improvement in readers' performance on immediate and delayed multiple-choice and cued recall tests. He hypothesized that these aids might actually interfere with competent readers' natural strategies"

Mary H. Sawyer 1991 (2)¶

Mary H. Sawyer. (1991). A Review of Research in Revising Instructional Text. Journal of Reading Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1080/10862969109547744

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Anderson and Biddle's (1975) review of adjunct-question research, that post-passage questions improved only verbatim-repetition posttest items, leaving open the possibility that the effect is trivially specific.

"questions inserted after a section of text consistently improved performance only on those posttest items which repeated the questions at a verbatim level and not on those posttest items which paraphrased the adjunct questions"
