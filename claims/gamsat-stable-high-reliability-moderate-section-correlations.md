## Evidence

### Mercer Annette 2015

Mercer Annette, Crotty Brendan, Alldridge Louise, Le Luc, Vele Veronica. (2015). GAMSAT: A 10-year retrospective overview, with detailed analysis of candidates’ performance in 2014. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0316-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Analysis of 2014 GAMSAT data reported in the Results reliability section. Cronbach's alpha was "0.84 in Section 1 and 0.88 in Section 3", and Pearson correlations between sections ranged from 0.24 to 0.51, values the authors state have been consistent since 2005.

> "For the 2014 data, the reliability index calculated by Cronbach’s alpha was 0.84 in Section 1 and 0.88 in Section 3. The Pearson correlation between Sections 1 and 2 was 0.42; between Sections 1 and 3 was 0.51; and between Sections 2 and 3 was 0.24."
