Wang Qiancheng, Wang Kuan, Wang Zhifei, Zhu Guanyu. (2026). Development and validation of a Novel 3D-printed mold-based silicone model for laparoscopic duodenal stump reinforcement suture simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09095-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert evaluation by ten laparoscopic surgeons using a 15-item questionnaire with 5-point Likert scales. Excellent inter-rater reliability was confirmed (Kappa = 0.87, 95% CI: 0.82–0.92, p < .001).

> "the model received the maximum score (5.0) in the categories of overall impression, anatomical size, realism, workspace simulation, tearing tendency, and suture penetration resistance. The texture of the model, however, received a lower rating (3.70 ± 0.82)"
