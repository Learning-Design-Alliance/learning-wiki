# GenAI literacy predicted immediate independent writing performance after AI removal for Visual Data Integration, Critical Thinking, and overall Composite Score

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In ordinal logistic regression, standardised GenAI literacy significantly predicted AI-removal Visual Data Integration (OR=1.14), Critical Thinking (OR=1.15), and Composite Score (OR=1.11), but not Insightfulness, Organisation and Coherence, or Linguistic Quality. [→ Yueqiao Jin 2025](#yueqiao-jin-2025)

## Evidence

### Yueqiao Jin 2025

Yueqiao Jin, Kaixun Yang, Roberto Martinez-Maldonado, Dragan Gašević and Lixiang Yan. (2025). The Agency Gap in AI-Supported Writing: How Reactive and Proactive Agent Designs Shape Multimodal Reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04398 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2507.04398](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2507.04398)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression on 79 participants' AI-removal task scores, controlling for mini-VLAT domain knowledge, agent condition, and AI-support score. Printed effect sizes are small odds ratios (OR = 1.14, OR = 1.15, OR = 1.11 per one-SD literacy increase), which the article calls "modest", raising the odds of a higher score by 11–15%.
