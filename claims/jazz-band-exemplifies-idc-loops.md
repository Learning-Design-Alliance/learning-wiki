`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' interpretive argument from their own experience as students and parents, illustrated by the persistence of high-school jazz bands despite the disappearance of swing culture. They suggest participatory opportunities for harmonious self-expression are powerfully engaging regardless of career relevance.

> "Rather, playing jazz in a group with fellow students is an intense, accessible form of the very three loops highlighted in the IDC framework — the loops of interest, creativity, and habit (and quite literally, harmony)."
