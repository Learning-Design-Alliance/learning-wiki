### Janjigian Michael 2021

Janjigian Michael, Dembitzer Anne, Srisarajivakul-Klein Caroline, Hardower Khemraj, Cooke Deborah, Zabar Sondra, Sauthoff Harald. (2021). Design and evaluation of the I-SCAN faculty POCUS program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02453-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational cohort of hospitalists; hands-on skill assessed on human models by proctors using a checklist adapted from the CHEST Certificate of Completion program, scored as percentage of items rated well done. The article reports medians of 75% post-course and 74% at one year, no significant change.
