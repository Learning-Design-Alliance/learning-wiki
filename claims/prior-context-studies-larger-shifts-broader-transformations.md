`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Narrative review of prior context-sensitivity studies, attributing the reported shifts to those studies (Bao et al. 53%; Metzler 7%; Dancy and Beichner −23% to +25%; Palmer 69%; Steinberg and Sabella −9% to 36%). Reported as the review attributes it; study designs not independently verified.
