Omrani 2012¶

Omrani, S., Fardanesh, H., Hemmati, N., & Hemmati, N. (2012). Exploring an appropriate instructional design model for continuing medical education. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 13(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ997826

Quasi-experiment with 60 general physicians and assistants randomly assigned to experimental (n=30) and control (n=30) groups; Table 2 reports posttest means with t = 7.98, p = 0.001, showing the e-learning group "17.53±0.94" versus control 15.2±1.29.
