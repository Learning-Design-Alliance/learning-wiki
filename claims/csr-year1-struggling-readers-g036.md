`q3 i1` For students identified as struggling at pretest, the model-derived Gates-MacGinitie posttest score was 87.66 for CSR participants, about 3.14 points above comparison students (g = 0.36, p = .066). [→ Vaughn 2013](#vaughn-2013)

## Evidence

### Vaughn 2013

Vaughn, S., Roberts, G., & Reutebuch, C. (2013). Collaborative Strategic Reading: Replications with Consideration of the Role of Fidelity. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563062

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the Year 1 multilevel models using TOWRE as the struggling-reader selection criterion. The quote reports the 3.14-point difference, p = .066, and "an effect size g = 0.36", which the authors call of considerable practical significance.

> "The model-derived posttest score on the Gates-MacGinitie was 87.66 for CSR participants, about 3.14 standard score points greater than initially struggling students in the comparison. Though not statistically significant (p = .066), the difference represents an effect size g = 0.36"
