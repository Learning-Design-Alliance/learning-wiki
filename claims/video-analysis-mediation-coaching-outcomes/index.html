The paper reports Lofthouse et al.'s (2010) study of coaching in thirteen schools in four regions of England, where "challenging teachers supported the emergence of dissonance between planning and enacting instruction" and favored reflection. No effect sizes printed.

"This study also found that positive outcomes were mediated by the use of video to analyze teacher-students interactions with coached teachers ."
