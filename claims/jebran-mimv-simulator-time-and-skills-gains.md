## Evidence

### EL-Andari R 2023

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre-post training study reported in the review: 10 residents and 10 students learned minimally invasive mitral valve techniques over 6 sessions in 4 weeks on a simulator comprising an aluminum conduit simulating the incision; evaluations included skills scores and time to completion, all reported improvements significant at p < 0.05.

> "Students' mean time to completion improved from an initial 54 (SD 5) minutes to a final 35 (SD 6) minutes (p < 0.05), and residents' mean time improved from an initial 47 (SD 6) minutes to a final 31 (SD 6) minutes (p < 0.05)."
