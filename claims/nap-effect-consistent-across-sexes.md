## Evidence

### Vidal 2025

Vidal, V., Pretel, M. R., Capurro, L., Tassone, L. M., Moyano, M. D., Malacari, R. G., Brusco, L. I., Ballarini, F. M. & Forcato, C. (2025). Short naps improve subsequent learning in a high school setting. npj Science of Learning 10:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00307-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory two-way ANOVA with sex and group as between-subjects factors (N = 76). No group × sex interaction (F(1,72) = 0.33, p = 0.569) and no sex main effect (F(1,72) = 2.80, p = 0.099); "a signiﬁcant main effect of group was observed" with no effect size printed.

> "a signiﬁcant main effect of group was observed (F(1,72) = 10.62,p = 0.002), with the Nap group outperforming the Control group in encoding performance"
