### Chaimo Kesinee 2026

Chaimo Kesinee, Thanasilp Sureeporn. (2026). Psychometric properties of instruments assessing the ethics of teaching: a COSMIN-based systematic review. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2026.23.12

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of measurement properties across the 6 included instruments. The review reports that despite acceptable psychometric support, "none were specifically designed for health professions education," and most focused on classroom pedagogy rather than clinical instruction.

> "Although the instruments demonstrated acceptable psychometric support within their respective validation contexts, none were specifically designed for health professions education."
