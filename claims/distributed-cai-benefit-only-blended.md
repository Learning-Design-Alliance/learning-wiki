# Distributed CAI instruction beats face-to-face teaching, but only when CAI is part of blended learning

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Studies using distributed instruction (more than one ECG training opportunity) showed CAI better than face-to-face teaching (SMD=0.65, 95% CI 0.31 to 1.00). [→ Viljoen Charle André 2019](#viljoen-charle-andré-2019)
`q3 i?` The distributed-instruction benefit was present only in blended learning studies (SMD=0.84) and not in non-blended studies (SMD=0.31, CI crossing zero). [→ Viljoen Charle André 2019 (2)](#viljoen-charle-andré-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2019

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i2`

Random-effects subanalysis of five distributed-instruction studies (n=538). Pooled "SMD=0.65 (95% CI 0.31 to 1.00)" favoured CAI, with I2=70% heterogeneity.

> "In another subanalysis, studies using a distributed approach to ECG instruction (ie, more than one ECG training opportunity) showed that CAI was better than face-to-face teaching (SMD=0.65 (95% CI 0.31 to 1.00); five studies, n=538; I2=70%)"

### Viljoen Charle André 2019 (2)

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i?`

Sub-subanalysis splitting distributed-instruction studies by blended versus non-blended CAI. The blended subset pooled SMD=0.84 while the non-blended subset pooled "SMD=0.31 (95% CI −0.21 to 0.84)", whose interval crosses zero.

> "the benefit of distributed instruction was only present in studies where CAI was part of a blended learning approach (SMD=0.84 (95% CI 0.54 to 1.14); three studies, n=422; I2=50%; vs SMD=0.31 (95% CI −0.21 to 0.84); two studies, n=116; I2=%)"
