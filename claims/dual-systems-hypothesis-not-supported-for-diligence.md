## Evidence

### Fuhrmann Delia 2019

Fuhrmann Delia, Schweizer Susanne, Leung Jovita, Griffin Cait, Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2019). The neurocognitive correlates of academic diligence in adolescent girls. Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2018.1504762

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' overall evaluation of their hypothesis-driven test in 40 girls aged 14–15, combining behavioral, structural MRI, fMRI and connectivity measures against the dual-systems prediction that diligence relates to frontal control and striatal reward systems.

> "Our results were mostly inconsistent with the dual-systems hypothesis. While there was a link between diligence and inferior frontal activation and gyrification, there was no association between diligence and striatal structure and function, or diligence and fronto-striatal connectivity."
