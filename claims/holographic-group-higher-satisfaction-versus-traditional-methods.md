## Evidence

### Rebeca Cerezo 2024

Rebeca Cerezo, Vicente Calderón, Cristóbal Romero. (2024). A holographic mobile-based application for practicing pronunciation of basic English vocabulary for Spanish speaking children. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Ad hoc six-item satisfaction survey (3-point scale, Cronbach's alpha 0.88) administered to the two experimental groups. "there were significant differences in A1 (t=-2.42, p<0.05) and A3 (t=-2.11, p<0.05)"; Dimension A means were EG2 M=1.92 versus EG1 M=1.69.

> "We found that there were no significant differences between the EG1 and EG2 scores in questions B1 (t=-1.41, p<0.05), B2 (t=-1.41, p<0.05), B3 (t=-0.99, p<0.05) and A2 (t=-1.70, p<0.05), but there were significant differences in A1 (t=-2.42, p<0.05) and A3 (t=-2.11, p<0.05)."
