## Evidence

### ARC Core evidence summary 2022

ARC Core evidence summary. (2022). https://eric.ed.gov

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Multi-site randomized controlled study in 71 kindergarten classrooms across 21 schools in Philadelphia; treatment classrooms implemented ARC Core for 120 minutes per day for the full school year in place of "regular literacy instruction". Letter Naming Fluency effect size was ES = +0.28.

> "After one year, students in ARC Core classrooms performed significantly higher on the AIMSWeb Letter Naming Fluency and the WMRT Passage Comprehension compared to the control group (ES = +0.28 and +0.16 respectively)"
