## Evidence

### Wei Yaru 2025

Wei Yaru, Peng Zhengjun. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom model based on Bloom’s Taxonomy of Educational Objectives in endodontics education for undergraduate dental students. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.18843

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of the two groups of 16 students on five-point Likert items; Table 2 shows understanding of objectives 3.6 ± 0.6 vs 3.9 ± 0.5, preview-method satisfaction 3.7 ± 0.6 vs 4.0 ± 0.6, curriculum satisfaction 3.6 ± 0.5 vs 4.0 ± 0.6. No effect size printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "Students’ self-reported understanding of teaching objectives improved by 8.3% (t = 1.762, p = 0.083), and satisfaction with the preview method and curriculum increased by 8.1% (t = 1.804, p = 0.081) and 11.1% (t = 1.861, p = 0.072), respectively. There was no statistically significant difference between the two groups."
