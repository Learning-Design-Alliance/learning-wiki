## Evidence

### Wang Enna 2026

Wang Enna, Zhang Junjie. (2026). Disentangling the dynamic directional associations between positive and negative risk-taking behaviors and academic development in adolescents: a four-wave longitudinal analysis. BMC Psychology, 14, 1273. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05248-0

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel analysis of four-wave longitudinal data from 672 Chinese adolescents, controlling for demographic covariates and autoregressive paths. The article reports that "positive risk-taking behaviors at T3 significantly positively predicted academic achievement at T4 (β = 0.18, p < 0.001)".

> "positive risk-taking behaviors at T1 significantly positively predicted academic achievement at T2 (β = 0.11, p = 0.002), positive risk-taking behaviors at T2 positively predicted academic achievement at T3 at a marginally significant level (β = 0.07, p = 0.070), and positive risk-taking behaviors at T3 significantly positively predicted academic achievement at T4 (β = 0.18, p < 0.001)"
