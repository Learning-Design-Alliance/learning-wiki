## Evidence

### Wei 2026

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group-level GLM contrast of retrieval-phase BOLD activity in the randomized two-group fMRI study (short n=28, long n=29), cluster-level FWE-corrected at p < .01. The "short video group exhibited significantly reduced activation" in all three left-hemisphere regions; no effect size is printed.

> "Independent-samples t-tests indicated that, compared to the long video group, the short video group exhibited significantly reduced activation in the left claustrum (MNI coordinates: x = -27, y = 25, z = 3), left caudate (x = -13, y = 20, z = 3), and left middle temporal gyrus (MTG, x = -58, y = -28, z = -7)"
