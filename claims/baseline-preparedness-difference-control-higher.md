## Evidence

### Gaupp R 2019

Gaupp R, Dinius J, Drazic I, Körner M. (2019). Long-term effects of an e-learning course on patient safety: A controlled longitudinal study with medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210947

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison in the controlled quasi-experimental study before the intervention. All outcome measures were comparable except received patient safety education, with the control group higher (4.74 vs 4.32); the printed effect size is "d = 0.46".

> "In the pretest data, there were no statistically significant differences between the two groups for all outcome measures, except for the GAPSQ scale received patient safety education (t(88) = 2.085, p<0.001, d = 0.46), where the control group felt slightly better prepared for safe patient actions than their peers in the intervention group (4.74; SD = .88 [CG] vs. 4.32; SD = .96 [IG])."
