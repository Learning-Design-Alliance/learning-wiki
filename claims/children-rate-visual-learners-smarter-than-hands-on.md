## Evidence

### Xin Sun 2023

Xin Sun, Owen Norton and Shaylene E. Nancekivell. (2023). Beware the myth: learning styles affect parents', children's, and teachers' thinking about children's academic potential. npj Science of Learning 8:46. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00190-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a study of 73 children aged 6–12 who heard descriptions of two students and rated them on 4-point smart/sporty scales. Children rated visual learners as "signiﬁcantly smarter than hands-on learners", d = 0.58; sportiness ratings did not differ (d = 0.18, p = 0.540).

> "children viewed visual learners ( M(SD)Visual = 2.88 (0.99)) as signiﬁcantly smarter than hands-on learners ( M(SD)Hands-on= 2.26 (1.09), Fig. 1 left panel), t = 3.48, padjusted= 0.001, d = 0.58"
