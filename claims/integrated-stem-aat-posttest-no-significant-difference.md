### Sarican 2018

Sarican, G., Akgunduz, D. (2018). The impact of integrated STEM education on academic achievement, reflective thinking skills towards problem solving and permanence in learning in science education. Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences, 13(1), 94-113. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1196043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test–post-test control-group study of 44 6th graders (22 per group); independent t-test on AAT post-test gave t = −1.377, p = 0.176, with means 12.50 (CG) and 14.23 (EG). The article states "Integrated STEM education increases the academic achievement averages more than the constructivist approach, but this does not make a meaningful difference."
