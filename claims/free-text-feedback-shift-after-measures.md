## Evidence

### Fünger S. M. 2016

Fünger S. M., Lesevic H., Rosner S., Ott I., Berberat P., Nikendei C., Sonne C. (2016). Improved self- and external assessment of the clinical abilities of medical students through structured improvement measures in an internal medicine bedside course. GMS Journal for Medical Education 33(4), Doc59. [doi:10.3205/zma001058](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001058)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative categorization of anonymous free-text answers to "In general, what would you change?" and "What did you think was particularly good?", compared before (T0) and after (T1) the measures, with the three most common answers per period reported in Table 3.

> "Thus, the students stated that the groups were too large and that they wished more support and feedback from the doctors for the physical examination. However, this was stated by many fewer students after the improvement measures had been introduced. Moreover, many more students praised lecturers' motivation and commitment and the good case discussion after the introduction of E-learning and scripts."
