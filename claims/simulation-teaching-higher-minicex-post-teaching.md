# After teaching, medical interns taught with practical training scenario simulation score significantly higher on all Mini-CEX clinical-skill dimensions than interns taught with bedside teaching

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled study of 40 neurology interns, all Mini-CEX scores after teaching were significantly higher in the simulation group than the bedside-teaching control group (P-value all < 0.0001), while pre-teaching scores showed no significant difference between groups. [→ Fang Xiang 2025](#fang-xiang-2025)

## Evidence

### Fang Xiang 2025

Fang Xiang, Jin Shan, Sun Zhengzhe. (2025). Application of practical training scenario simulation teaching method oriented to strengthen clinical skills in the clinical internship stage of medical students. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2500557

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective controlled trial: 40 five-year clinical medicine interns randomized by computer-generated random number table into control (bedside teaching) and observation (simulation) groups of 20 each. The article reports baseline Mini-CEX scores with "no significant difference" between the two groups (P-values 0.501–0.735).

> "Prior to receiving instruction, there was no significant difference in the comparison of the Mini-CEX scores related to clinical skills between the two groups of students"

### Fang Xiang 2025 (2)

Fang Xiang, Jin Shan, Sun Zhengzhe. (2025). Application of practical training scenario simulation teaching method oriented to strengthen clinical skills in the clinical internship stage of medical students. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2500557

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled comparison (n = 20 per group) with blinded Mini-CEX raters. After the 4-week placement, "the scores in the observation group had significantly higher scores than the control group", with post-teaching t-values in Table 1 ranging from 9.12 (overall performance) to 41.21 (doctor-patient communication), all P < 0.0001.

> "After receiving the teaching, all the scores of Mini-CEX increased in both groups, and the scores in the observation group had significantly higher scores than the control group (P-value are all  <  0.0001)(Table 1)."
