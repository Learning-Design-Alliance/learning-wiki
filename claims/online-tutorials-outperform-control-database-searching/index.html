Evidence¶

Mery 2014¶

Mery, Y., DeFrain, E., Kline, E., & Sult, L. (2014). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Tools for Online Database Instruction. Communications in Information Literacy, 8(1). https://journals.lib.pdx.edu/index.php/informationliteracy

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized post-test-only control group experiment with 90 undergraduates inexperienced with library instruction, assigned to control, screencast, or GotS groups, each completing an identical 16-item post-test. The ANOVA showed "a significant difference on post-test scores among the three groups" with η2 = .187.

"When analyzed using a one -way ANOVA, results indicated a significant difference on post-test scores among the three groups ( F(2, 87) = 10.009, p < .001, η2 = .187)."
