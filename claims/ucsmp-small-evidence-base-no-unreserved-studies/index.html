What Works Clearinghouse 2016¶

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Systematic review against WWC group design standards identified 12 eligible studies; none were randomized controlled trials meeting standards without reservations, three met standards with reservations, and nine did not meet standards. Extent of evidence is "Small" for the qualifying domains.

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for UCSMP Algebra on the mathematics achievement of secondary students to be small for two outcome domains—general mathematics achievement and algebra."
