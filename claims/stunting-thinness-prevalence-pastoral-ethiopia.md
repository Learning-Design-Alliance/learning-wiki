Descriptive anthropometric results from the cross-sectional survey of 395 grade-4 children, with HAZ and BAZ generated using WHO Anthro Plus software against WHO reference values; the article reports "26.6%" stunted and "28.9%" thin. These are descriptive proportions with no effect size.

> "The prevalence of stunting and thinness of school-age children was 26.6% (95% CI: 21.8%, 31.4%) and 28.9% (95% CI: 24.3%, 33.2%), respectively, in Sewena and Dawe Katchen districts."
