## Evidence

### Abry 2017

Abry, T., Rimm-Kaufman, S.E. & Curby, T.W. (2017). Are All Program Elements Created Equal? Relations Between Specific Social and Emotional Learning Components and Teacher–Student Classroom Interaction Quality. Prevention Science, 18, 193–203. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-016-0743-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural path models conducted in the context of a randomized controlled trial of the Responsive Classroom approach, with 143 treatment teachers of third, fourth, and fifth grade classrooms, controlling for pre-intervention interaction quality and other covariates. The article reports that "teachers' use of Morning Meeting and Academic Choice related to higher levels of emotionally supportive interactions." No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Structural path models indicated that teachers' use of Morning Meeting and Academic Choice related to higher levels of emotionally supportive interactions; Academic Choice also related to higher levels of instructional interactions."
