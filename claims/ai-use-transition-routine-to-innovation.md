Thematic analysis of 18 weeks of recorded meetings identified five prevalent themes of AI integration activities. The analysis found "a transition from using AI for basic, routine tasks to leveraging it as a tool for innovation".

> "The findings also illustrate a transition from using AI for basic, routine tasks to leveraging it as a tool for innovation. Teachers reported using AI to design creative learning experiences, improve student engagement, and inform reflective practice."
