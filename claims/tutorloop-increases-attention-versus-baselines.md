## Evidence

### Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang 2026

Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang. (2026). TutorLoop: Regulating Student Learning Behaviors via Sensor-in-the-Loop Generative Feedback. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.09400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.09400)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Between-subjects user study with 187 Prolific participants in four groups (Control N=48, TutorUp N=46, DRL Only N=46, Full N=47), analyzed with linear mixed-effects models; attention was webcam-derived per second. The Full group's attention (M=0.739) exceeded all baselines, with "significantly lower attention thanFull" in Control.

> "The Controlgroup exhibited significantly lower attention thanFull(𝑝=. 013), whereas differences betweenControland eitherTutorUporDRL Onlywere not significant. Moreover, theFullgroup significantly outperformed bothTutorUp( 𝑝=. 044) andDRL Only( 𝑝=. 002)"
