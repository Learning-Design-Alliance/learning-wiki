## Evidence

### Leon I. 2023

Leon I., Tautolo E.-S., Plank L.D., Rush E. (2023). Pacific Islands Families Study: Household Food Security during Pregnancy and Secondary School Educational Achievement. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15194131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared comparison of highest qualification by PIF-definition food security status in 649 cohort members: University Entrance 24.8% food secure vs 17.4% food insecure, p = 0.041, described in text as "food-secure youths (25%) achieved University Entrance than those categorised as food insecure (17%)".

> "A higher proportion of food-secure youths (25%) achieved University Entrance than those categorised as food insecure (17%) (Table 3)."

### Leon I. 2023 (2)

Leon I., Tautolo E.-S., Plank L.D., Rush E. (2023). Pacific Islands Families Study: Household Food Security during Pregnancy and Secondary School Educational Achievement. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15194131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 3 chi-squared comparison of highest qualification by PIF food security status: NCEA level 1 or 2 was the highest qualification for 157 food-secure (24.2%) versus 69 food-insecure (36.3%) youths, p = 0.002. No qualification recorded did not differ (p = 0.787).
