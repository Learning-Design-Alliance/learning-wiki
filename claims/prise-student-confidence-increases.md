### Martin O. Archer and Jennifer DeWitt 2021

Martin O. Archer and Jennifer DeWitt. (2021). “Thanks for helping me ﬁnd my enthusiasm for physics!” The lasting impacts ‘research in schools’ projects can have on students, teachers, and schools. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.04431](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.04431)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective pre/post questionnaire study of 127 students (2016–2019 conferences) rating confidence on a 6-point Likert scale; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed "statistically signiﬁcant increases for all the topics and practices", with median change 0.92 ± 0.04 points and no real variation by project or school.
