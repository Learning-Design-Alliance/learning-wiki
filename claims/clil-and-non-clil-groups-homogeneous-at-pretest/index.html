Evidence¶

Karimi 2019¶

Karimi, P., Lotfi, A. R., & Biria, R. (2019). Enhancing Pilot’s Aviation English Learning, Attitude and Motivation through the Application of Content and Language Integrated Learning. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 751-766. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201326

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test comparing pretest means of CLIL (M = 64.85, SD = 9.315) and non-CLIL (M = 63.85, SD = 9.366) groups (n = 40). The article reports "r = .055 representing a weak effect size".

"The results of the independent t -test (t (29) = .339, 95 % CI [ -4.97, 6.97], p = .737, r = .055 representing a weak effect size) (Table 6) indicated that the groups were homogenous in terms of their language and content knowledge as measured through the pretest."
