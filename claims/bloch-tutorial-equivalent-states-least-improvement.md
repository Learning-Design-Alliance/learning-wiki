`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post comparison (N = 28) on question 1, which asked about pairs of states differing in overall versus relative phase; on the pre-test the overall correctness rate was close to 50% because most students made no distinction between the two pairs.

> "Students performed better on the post-test for this concept, but there is still room for improvement, especially when compared to the other concepts (see Table 1)."
