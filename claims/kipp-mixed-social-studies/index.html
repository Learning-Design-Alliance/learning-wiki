Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP) 2018¶

Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP). (2018). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Charter Schools. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/688

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Two quasi-experimental studies with 10,363 students. The Tuttle et al. (2015, High School) social studies effect was "not large enough to be considered substantively important (that is, an effect size of at least 0.25)" and was characterized as indeterminate.

"the estimated impact of the intervention on outcomes in the social studies achievement domain was positive and statistically significant for one study, and neither statistically significant nor large enough to be substantively important for one study."
