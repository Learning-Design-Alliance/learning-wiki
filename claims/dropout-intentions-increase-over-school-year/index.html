Grazia 2024¶

Grazia, V., Molinari, L., Mameli, C. (2024). Contrasting school dropout: The protective role of perceived teacher justice. LEARNING AND INSTRUCTION, 89, 1-9. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101826

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Latent growth modeling of the three-wave data from 547 9th graders found "intentions to dropout increased over time" across the school year. No effect size for the growth trend is printed in the supplied text.

"A latent growth model indicated that intentions to dropout increased over time and perceptions of teacher justice had a negative association with the increase both at T2 and T3"
