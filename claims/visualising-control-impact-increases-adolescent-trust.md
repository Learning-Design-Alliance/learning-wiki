# Visualising the impact of exercised control significantly increases adolescents' one-dimensional and multidimensional trust in an e-learning platform

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomised controlled experiment, both one-dimensional trust and multidimensional trust were significantly higher for adolescents who saw a visualisation of their control's impact than for those who did not. [→ Jeroen Ooge 2023](#jeroen-ooge-2023)

## Evidence

### Jeroen Ooge 2023

Jeroen Ooge, Leen Dereu, and Katrien Verbert. 2023. Steering Recommendations and Visualising Its Impact: Effects on Adolescents' Trust in E-Learning Platforms. In 28th International Conference on Intelligent User Interfaces (IUI '23). https://doi.org/10.1145/3581641.3584046

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled experiment with 76 adolescents (22 none, 25 control, 24 control+impact) comparing groups with one-way ANOVA followed by one-sided t-tests. "Both one-dimensional trust and multidimensional trust increased significantly(𝑝 < 0.05)" for control+impact versus the other groups; no standardised effect size was printed.

> "Both one-dimensional trust and multidimensional trust increased significantly(𝑝 < 0.05). The latter relates to an increase in two of its components: trusting beliefs and transparency."
