Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Perspectives of Women and Men Students and Faculty on Conceptual and Quantitative Problem-Solving in Physics from Introductory to Graduate Levels. [doi:10.3390/educsci15121602](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15121602)

Open-ended survey responses from introductory students across all courses; responses were read independently by both researchers and representative quotes selected. Students also overwhelmingly recommended consistent practice for quantitative skills.

> "Students described conceptual problem-solving skills in physics as the ability to understand and apply the underlying principles of physical phenomena without relying solely on numbers or equations."
