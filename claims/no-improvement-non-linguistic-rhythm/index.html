Evidence¶

Gutiérrez-Palma 2026¶

Gutiérrez-Palma, N., Calet, N., Robles-Bello, M.A., & Valencia-Naranjo, N. (2026). The role of rhythm in learning to read in children with and without reading difficulties: an intervention study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102273

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Non-parametric longitudinal analysis of both samples on the On Track Rhythm Test; Table 1 shows a significant G x Time effect for R2 (ATS = 3.19, p < .05, Cliff's delta = .27) in children without difficulties, driven by the phonological-awareness-only group worsening.

"As for the results on the ability to detect the non-linguistic rhythm, there was no improvement in any group. There was only interaction in the case of the 667 ms rhythm, so the only group that worsened was the group where rhythm was not trained."
