### Fargol Seifollahi 2026

Fargol Seifollahi, Christian D. Schunn, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Questioning Your Brilliance in Physics: Differential Shifts in Fixed Mindsets by Grade and Gender. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey study of N = 508 students in a two-semester calculus-based introductory physics sequence, using validated 'my fixed beliefs' items (Cronbach's alpha 0.91) administered at the start of each course. The article reports "small yet statistically-significant declines towards fixed beliefs" for both genders; no standardized effect size for the decline is printed.

> "Also, the means for both women and men showed small yet statistically-significant declines towards fixed beliefs going from Physics 1 to Physics 2."
