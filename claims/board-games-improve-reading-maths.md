## Evidence

### Nuria Vita-Barrull 2024

Nuria Vita-Barrull, Verónica Estrada-Plana, Jaume March-Llanes, Pablo Sotoca-Orgaz, Núria Guzmán, Rosa Ayesa, Jorge Moya-Higueras. (2024). Do you play in class? Board games to promote cognitive and educational development in primary school: A cluster randomized controlled trial. Learning and Instruction, 93. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101946

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of the second-to-sixth-grade subsample of the cluster-randomised trial, assessed with the PREDISCAL screening. Play-group Reading Fluency rose from 17.48 to 23.29 versus 17.51 to 21.99 for controls; the play group also gained more in Maths Fluency and Calculus.

> "Time and time-group effects were found in the three academic tasks: Reading Fluency (time: F = 701.37; p = 0.00; time-group: F = 11.66; p = 0.00), Maths Fluency (time: F = 318.15; p = 0.00; time-group: F = 5.68; p = 0.02), and Calculus (time: F = 283.58; p = 0.00; time-group: F = 4.57; p = 0.03). In all three tasks, the experimental group experienced the most significant improvement."
