Pierce, C. (2026). Ready for Rigor: Expanding Access to Rigorous Coursework in Michigan. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682555

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of Civil Rights Data Collection AP enrollment (2021-2022) comparing Michigan schools where self-selection was allowed versus not allowed; the printed shares were 8.5% versus 5.7% Black student AP enrollment. The report calls this "one way to dismantle gatekeeping policies."
