## Evidence

### Wu Lingyi 2026

Wu Lingyi, Chellappa Vigneshkumar, Xie Qi, Mao Xuan, Luximon Yan, Yu Riji. (2026). Immersive virtual reality for acupuncture knowledge and practical skills: a comparative educational study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1922688

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Practical skills were assessed individually on a silicone training arm using an 18-item checklist scored 0–2 per item (maximum 36), rated by two blinded licensed assessors with ICC above 0.75. The VR group's advantage was significant with a "large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.90, 95% CI [0.36, 1.43])".

> "The experimental group attained a mean score of 32.52 (SD = 2.28), compared with 28.62 (SD = 2.77) in the control group. This difference was also statistically significant (t(58) = 5.947, p < 0.001) and was associated with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.90, 95% CI [0.36, 1.43])."
