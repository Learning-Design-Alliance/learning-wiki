## Evidence

### Francisco José Jiménez-Hornero 2026

Francisco José Jiménez-Hornero, Eduardo Gutiérrez de Ravé. (2026). Conceptual and procedural knowledge retention in data visualization: a longitudinal study of doctoral learning and instructional design implications. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09808-0

`q3 · i?`

LMM analysis of technical-execution rubric scores for the same 40-student cohort across three time points. Predicted means fell from 3.64 to 2.76; the β = −0.44 coefficient "reflected a substantially steeper rate of decline than that observed for conceptual understanding".

> "For the technical-execution rubric, the fixed effect of time was even stronger, F(2, 78) = 47.3, p < 0.001. Predicted means dropped from T0 ( M = 3.64, 95% CI [3.50, 3.78]) to T1 ( M = 3.12, 95% CI [2.98, 3.26]) and T2 (M = 2.76, 95% CI [2.61, 2.91]). The corresponding time coefficient (β = − 0.44, SE = 0.05) reflected a substantially steeper rate of decline than that observed for conceptual understanding."
