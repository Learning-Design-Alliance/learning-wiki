`q2 i?` Introductory astronomy students had FCI pre- and post-test scores very similar to introductory physics students (physics 47% to 58%; astronomy 46% to 62%). [→ Melanie Good 2018](#melanie-good-2018)

## Evidence

### Melanie Good 2018

Melanie Good, Andrew Mason, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Comparing introductory physics and astronomy students’ attitudes and approaches to problem solving. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aae35b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aae35b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-/post-test Force Concept Inventory comparison for typical cohorts of the introductory physics and astronomy students in the study. The printed averages show comparable conceptual performance on force concepts: "47% on the FCI pre-test" for physics versus 46% for astronomy, with post-tests of 58% and 62%.

> "introductory physics students scored an average of 47% on the FCI pre-test, and an average of 58% on the FCI post-test. Introductory astronomy students scored an average of 46% on the FCI pre-test, and an average of 62% on the FCI post-test."
