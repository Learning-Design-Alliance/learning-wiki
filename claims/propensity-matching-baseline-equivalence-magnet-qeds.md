# Propensity score matching produced comparison groups equivalent at baseline (within +/- 0.25 standard deviations) on all pretest achievement measures across the three QEDs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Propensity score matching yielded comparison groups equivalent at baseline on all pretest achievement measures, with effect sizes below +/- 0.08 at Gehring and below +/- 0.04 at Lied and O'Callaghan, none statistically significant. [→ Nakamoto 2022](#nakamoto-2022)

## Evidence

### Nakamoto 2022

Nakamoto, J., Grant, A., Snipes, J., Allender, S., & Figueroa, T. (2022). Clark County School District Magnet Schools Assistance Program (MSAP): Spring 2022 interim evaluation summary report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621271

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline equivalence testing for the three school-level QEDs, using Hedges' g on pretest MAP, SBAC, and CRT measures. The report states groups were "equivalent at baseline" within the WWC 0.25 threshold, with no significant pretest differences; demographic differences were under 3 percentage points.

> "The propensity score matching approach resulted in comparison groups that were equivalent at baseline (i.e., less than +/- 0.25 standard deviations; U.S. Department of Education, 2020) on all of the pretest achievement measures."
