# CNV and P3 scalp topographies show a frontal shift with increasing age during adolescence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Normalized CNV amplitude at Fz preceding standards and random targets was larger in 18–24 year-olds than in younger groups, indicating an age-related increase in frontal contribution. [→ Thillay A 2015](#thillay-a-2015)
`q3 i3` Normalized P3 amplitude at Fz to standards was larger in 18–24 year-olds than in younger groups, indicating a frontal shift in the P3 response with age. [→ Thillay A 2015 (2)](#thillay-a-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Thillay A 2015

Thillay A, Roux S, Gissot V, Carteau-Martin I, Knight RT, Bonnet-Brilhault F and Bidet-Caulet A (2015) Sustained attention and prediction: distinct brain maturation trajectories during adolescence. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 9:519. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2015.00519

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

rmANOVAs on normalized CNV amplitudes at Fz, Cz, Pz in the 36-participant cross-sectional sample showed a significant electrode × age group interaction, driven by the frontal electrode. The frontal effect at Fz for standards was "F(2,33) = 5.86, p = 0.007, ηp = 0.262". No difference was found between the two younger groups.

> "Post hoc analyses revealed that CNV normalized amplitude at Fz preceding standards and randTs were larger in 18–24 year-olds compared to 14–17 year-olds ( p = 0.002, or p = 0.023; respectively) and compared to 12–14 year-olds ( p = 0.003, or p = 0.047; respectively)."

### Thillay A 2015 (2)

Thillay A, Roux S, Gissot V, Carteau-Martin I, Knight RT, Bonnet-Brilhault F and Bidet-Caulet A (2015) Sustained attention and prediction: distinct brain maturation trajectories during adolescence. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 9:519. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2015.00519

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

rmANOVAs on normalized P3 amplitudes (350–600 ms) showed a significant electrode × age group interaction; the frontal effect at Fz to standards was "F(2,33) = 5.59, p = 0.008, ηp = 0.253". P3 amplitude at Pz to random targets was smaller in 18–24 year-olds than 14–17 year-olds, consistent with a parietal-to-frontal shift.

> "Post hoc analyses revealed that P3 normalized amplitude at Fz to standards were larger in 18–24 year-olds compared to 14–17 year-olds ( p = 0.015) and compared to 12–14 year-olds (p = 0.016)."
