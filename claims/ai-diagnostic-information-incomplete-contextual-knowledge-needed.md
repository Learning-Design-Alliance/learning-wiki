Authors' conceptual analysis in the potentials-and-challenges section, attributed to cited literature rather than new data. It states that motivational states, learning histories, and classroom dynamics are largely absent from AI-based assessment, making teacher contextual knowledge indispensable.

> "current implementations typically focus on performance-related data, such as task correctness and completion time. As a result, essential contextual information about students' motivational states, learning histories, or classroom dynamics is largely absent from AI-based assessment."
