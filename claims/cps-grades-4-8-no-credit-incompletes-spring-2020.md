# Students in grades 4-8 were much more likely to receive no-credit grades during remote learning, with 41 percent receiving at least one Incomplete in spring 2020

## Evidence

### Gwynne 2022

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., & Liang, Y. (2022). Student engagement in learning during COVID-19: Students' course grades in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/office-of-equity/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of no-credit grades (Fs or Incompletes) among 223,096 students in grades 4-8 in district-run CPS schools. The report finds "a decline of 30 percentage points in students who completed and earned credits for all of their classes (from 89 percent to 59 percent)" in spring 2020.

> "Forty-one percent of students in grades 4-8 received at least one Incomplete grade that term, which meant there was a decline of 30 percentage points in students who completed and earned credits for all of their classes (from 89 percent to 59 percent)."
