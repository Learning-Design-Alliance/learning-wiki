Fargol Seifollahi, Christian D. Schunn, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Questioning Your Brilliance in Physics: Differential Shifts in Fixed Mindsets by Grade and Gender. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression models for the Hesitant outcome (Table 2) in the N = 508 sample: interaction terms Men with B (OR = 0.75, p = 0.601) and Men with C (OR = 0.74, p = 0.618) were not significant, while grade main effects were. No standardized effect size is printed for the null interactions.

> "Interestingly, there were no significant gender interactions with grade for ending in the Hesitant category. Instead, that status appeared to primarily reflect the initial mindset and grades."
