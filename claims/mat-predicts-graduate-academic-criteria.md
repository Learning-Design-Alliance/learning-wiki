## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Meta-analytic corrected true-score correlations between MAT scores and graduate academic criteria are ρ = .39 for GGPA (k = 70, N = 11,368), ρ = .41 for first-year GPA, and ρ = .37 for faculty ratings, with faculty-rating validity fully generalizable (SDρ = 0). ## Evidence

### Kuncel et al. 2004

Nathan R. Kuncel, Sarah A. Hezlett, and Deniz S. Ones. (2004). Academic Performance, Career Potential, Creativity, and Job Performance: Can One Construct Predict Them All? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.148

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of MAT validity for academic criteria (Table 2), corrected for range restriction and unreliability. Printed correlation effect sizes: ρ = .39 for GGPA (k = 70, N = 11,368), ρ = .41 for first-year GPA (k = 34, N = 2,999), and ρ = .37 for faculty ratings (k = 25, N = 1,909, SDρ = 0).

> "Validity for GGPA was moderately large (k ⫽ 70, N ⫽ 11,368) with a true-score correlation (ρ) of .39 and a small standard deviation of true validity of .09. The ρ for 1st-year GPA was .41, which was slightly larger than ρ for GGPA (k ⫽ 34, N ⫽ 2,999) with, also, a larger SDρ of .15."
