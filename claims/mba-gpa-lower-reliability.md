## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two-year MBA GPA reaches only .84 reliability, and the authors attribute this to grade compression (43% As, mean 3.64) and extensive group projects lowering intergrade correlations. [→ Bacon 2006](#bacon-2006)

## Evidence

### Bacon 2006

Donald R. Bacon and Beth Bean. (2006). GPA in Research Studies: An Invaluable but Neglected Opportunity. Journal of Marketing Education, Vol. 28 No. 1, April 2006 35-42. https://doi.org/10.1177/0273475305284638

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability analysis of grades from 194 MBA graduates (Table 2). The authors' interpretation: average correlation among course grades drops from .30 in Year 1 to .18 in Year 2, and group project grades reflect teammates' abilities as well as one's own.

> "As shown, the MBA GPA is less reliable than the undergraduate GPA, rising to .84 after 2 years, while the undergraduate GPA achieved that reliability after 1 year."
