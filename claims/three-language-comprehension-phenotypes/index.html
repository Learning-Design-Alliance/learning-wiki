Vyshedskiy 2024¶

Vyshedskiy, A., Venkatesh, R., Khokhlovich, E. & Satik, D. (2024). Three mechanisms of language comprehension are revealed through cluster analysis of individuals with language deficits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00284-0

UHCA of all 17,848 participants (Fig. 2 heatmap, Table 3). All 48 neurotypical controls fell in the syntactic phenotype; Table 3 reports modifier phenotype at 25.0% and command phenotype at 30.3% of the total.

"The cluster of participants termed “Syntactic Language Phenotype” shows the predominant blue color that indicated good skills across all three (syntactic, modiﬁer, and command) language com- prehension mechanisms (44.7% of participants, Table 3)."
