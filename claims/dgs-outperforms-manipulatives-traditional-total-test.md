## Evidence

### Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Dynamic Geometry Software and Physical Manipulatives on Candidate Teachers’ Transformational Geometry Success. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.2610

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

3×1 ANCOVA with pre-test as covariate on 117 freshman teacher candidates' total TGAT scores; Bonferroni comparisons showed Computer > Manipulatives (mean difference = 3.50), Computer > Traditional (11.12), Manipulatives > Traditional (7.62), all p < .001; η2 = .60.

> "There was a significant mean difference in the gain scores of students in the Computer Group, Manipulatives Group, and Traditional Group with respect to the overall test [ F (2, 111) = 81.73, p < .001, η2 = .60]"
