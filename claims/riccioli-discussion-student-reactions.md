## Evidence

### Christopher M. Graney 2011

Christopher M. Graney. (2011). Teaching Galileo? Get to Know Riccioli! What a Forgotten Italian Astronomer Can Teach Students About How Science Works. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1107.3483](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1107.3483)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The author's informal report of his own physics (calculus-based) and introductory astronomy courses: "I am pleased with the results", and Riccioli "gets them to open up to science". This is anecdotal teaching experience, not a controlled evaluation; no measures are printed.

> "The reactions of astronomy students, typically non-science majors, are more dramatic -- especially those students with a skeptical or even combative attitude toward science and with weird ideas acquired from the broader culture."
