## Evidence

### Finstad Anne Strand 2023

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion of focus group findings from both interview rounds. Participants described physical and psychological changes, e.g. "simulation can initiate so many physical and psychological processes in the body, like actually being there".

> "Nevertheless, the participants reported fidelity as a crucial prerequisite for simulation-based problem-solving and learning outcome, regarding significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice."

### Finstad Anne Strand 2023 (2)

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant quotes on the simulated patient: the midwife's body mass index was not comparable to the scenario patient's, which some participants found disturbing for realism.

> "It was a bit disturbing with a simulated patient with a pillow on her stomach – you are not in real life anymore (No. 2.3) …forgot that it was an obese patient (No. 7.3)."
