## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Resilience positively predicted self-efficacy (β = 0.25), which positively predicted professional well-being (β = 0.05), with a significant indirect effect. [→ Yang Bin 2025](#yang-bin-2025)

## Evidence

### Yang Bin 2025

Yang Bin, Song Shoujun, Zhang Lili, Xu Chunze. (2025). The relationship between resilience and professional well-being among early childhood teachers: the chain mediating role of teacher pedagogical competence and self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03227-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the same 1,377-teacher survey: the article reports resilience "significantly and positively predicted teacher self-efficacy in Model 2 (β = 0.25, t = 9.80, p < 0.001)"; self-efficacy then predicted well-being (β = 0.05, t = 2.68, p < 0.01) in Model 4.

> "teacher resilience significantly and positively predicted teacher self-efficacy in Model 2 (β = 0.25, t = 9.80, p < 0.001)"

### Yang Bin 2025 (2)

Yang Bin, Song Shoujun, Zhang Lili, Xu Chunze. (2025). The relationship between resilience and professional well-being among early childhood teachers: the chain mediating role of teacher pedagogical competence and self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03227-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bias-corrected percentile bootstrap test (5,000 samples, 95% CI) confirmed indirect effect 1 (resilience → self-efficacy → well-being) was significant; the article reports "none of the three mediated effect paths had 95% confidence intervals that contained a 0".

> "Teacher resilience-teacher self-efficacy-teacher professional well-being (indirect effect 1), teacher resilience-teacher pedagogical competence-teacher professional well-being (indirect effect 2), and teacher resilience-teacher self-efficacy-teacher pedagogical competence-teacher professional well-being (indirect effect 3), none of the three mediated effect paths had 95% confidence intervals that contained a 0"
