## Evidence

### Fei Qin 2025

Fei Qin, Zhanxin Hao, Jifan Yu, Zhiyuan Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). AI instructional agent improves student's perceived learner control and learning outcome: Empirical evidence from a randomized controlled trial. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 140 undergraduates were randomly assigned to human teacher, MOOC, or AI teacher conditions with identical content; 15 responses were excluded, leaving 125 for analysis (41 human, 43 MOOC, 41 AI). One-way ANOVA with Tukey HSD showed the AI group's PLC exceeded both comparison groups; mean differences are reported (0.732 against the human teacher, 0.416 against the MOOC) but no standardized effect size, so impact is coded i? (not a null effect).

> "Significant group differences were observed in the perceived learner control, F(2, 122) = 12.155, p < .001. Post-hoc comparisons revealed that students in the AI instructional group reported significantly higher levels of PLC compared to those in the human teacher group (M difference = 0.732, p < .001) and the MOOC group (M difference = 0.416, p <
.05)."
