## Evidence

### Gadbail AR 2026

Gadbail AR, Gondivkar SM, Yuwanati M, Sonone A, Dhamande M, Panchbhai A, Sarode SC. (2026). Early clinical exposure in undergraduate dental education: a systematic review of effectiveness, experiences, and outcomes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08692-z

`q2 · i3` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Ali et al., a non-randomized quasi-experimental comparison (n = 150) where "theory mean 82% vs. 76%, p = 0.002, d = 1.55" favoring early preclinical exposure over late exposure.

> "Group A showed significantly higher academic performance (theory mean 82% vs. 76%, p = 0.002, d = 1.55), OSCE communication (85% vs. 78%, p = 0.004, d = 2.15), empathy (83% vs. 75%, p = 0.005, d = 2.28)."
