Evidence¶

Su 2013¶

Su, C.-H., & Cheng, C.-H. (2013). 3D game-based learning system for improving learning achievement in software engineering curriculum. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1015378

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental comparison of 33 game-based and 30 traditional-instruction students in the system analysis unit. The post-test t-test gave t=3.025, df=62, Sig.=0.004, comparison A>B; the article reports "Mean=80.24 and SD=9.327" versus Mean=72.14, SD=12.010.

"Table 3 shows the average scores of students in Group A who use the game-based learning are Mean=80.24 and SD=9.327, which are higher than who use the traditional face to face learning (Mean=72.14, SD=12.010)."

Su 2013 (2)¶

Su, C.-H., & Cheng, C.-H. (2013). 3D game-based learning system for improving learning achievement in software engineering curriculum. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1015378

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Within-group pre-post comparison in the same quasi-experiment: the game-based group rose from "pre-test (Mean=71.36)" to post-test Mean=80.24 significantly, while the traditional group's pre-post difference was not significant.

"comparison with the scores of pre-test and post-test shows that, in the Group B who use the traditional face-to-face learning does not have a significant difference on the scores of pre-test and post-test, but in the Group A who use the game-based learning have significant differences in the scores of pre-test (Mean=71.36) and post-test (Mean=80.24)."
