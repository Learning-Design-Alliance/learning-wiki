Roye-Green Karen J., Vickers Ivan, Priestley Sharon, Walker Jerome, Willis Rohan. (2025). Knowledge, practice and attitudes of healthcare students to sepsis management in Jamaica. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07122-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Attitudes section (Q15–17) of the questionnaire administered to 292 students. "A vast majority of medical (98.6%) and nursing students (97.6%) agree that more training on sepsis is needed"; awareness of SSC guidelines was low (8.6% medical, 3.7% nursing).

> "A vast majority of medical (98.6%) and nursing students (97.6%) agree that more training on sepsis is needed. Both groups of students also agreed that sepsis bundles should be implemented at their respective hospitals (91.9% and 100% respectively)."
