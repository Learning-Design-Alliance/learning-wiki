Lixiang Yan, Yueqiao Jin, Xibin Han, Dragan Gasevic. (2026). Socially fluent AI decouples conversational signals from source identity in online interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23426 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23426](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.23426)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation checks among 685 AI targets confirmed the stance manipulation: supportive agents rated more supportive (M = 5.77 vs 4.47) and less conflictual (M = 2.51 vs 4.25) than contrarian agents. Within AI targets, trust varied by stance (supportive M = 5.57, n = 344; contrarian M = 4.87, n = 341).

> "In manipulation checks among AI targets (𝑛 = 685), supportive agents were rated as substantially more supportive than contrarian agents (supportive: 𝑀 = 5.77, 𝑆𝐷 = 1.24; contrarian: 𝑀 = 4.47, 𝑆𝐷 = 1.91) and as markedly less conflictual (supportive: 𝑀 = 2.51, 𝑆𝐷 = 1.73; contrarian: 𝑀 = 4.25, 𝑆𝐷 = 1.85)"
