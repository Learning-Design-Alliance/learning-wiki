Henderson L, Patwardhan N, Dagash H. (2025). Trainee as trainer in paediatric laparoscopic appendicectomy. Annals of The Royal College of Surgeons of England. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2024.0032

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective review of a prospective audit of all appendicectomies in children aged <16 years over two years (1 April 2015–31 March 2017) at one tertiary paediatric surgical centre, analysing operator and assistant level to identify TT cases. The review found "53 (20.7%) of 256 appendicectomies were trainee as trainer (TT) cases".

> "A total of 53 (20.7%) of 256 appendicectomies were trainee as trainer (TT) cases; 22 cases (41.5%) were performed by a CT supervised by a Registrar, and 31 (58.4%) by a JR supervised by a SR."
