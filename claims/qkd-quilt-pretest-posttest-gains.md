## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students' average score improved from 52.7% on the pretest to 90.8% on the posttest after engaging with the QKD QuILT, a statistically significant difference with Cohen's d = 0.92. [→ DeVore 2016](#devore-2016)

## Evidence

### DeVore 2016

DeVore, S. and Singh, C. (2016). An Interactive Learning Tutorial on Quantum Key Distribution. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01301](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01301)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r3`

In-class paired pretest/posttest analysis of 74 upper-level undergraduate quantum mechanics students across three course years. The table caption reports "an average score of 52.7% (pretest) and 90.8% (posttest)"; the table's average row shows t = 10.2 and Cohen's d = 0.92 with p < 0.001.

> "The pretest and posttest results and corresponding t-tests and effect sizes using Cohen’s d are based on 74 students’ paired responses with an average score of 52.7% (pretest) and 90.8% (posttest), respectively"
