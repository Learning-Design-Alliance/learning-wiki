### Mandalapu 2022

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of chronotype cluster membership across demographic groups in the Fall 2019 cohort; the article reports "a meaningful relationship between student ethnicity and chronotypes" and a significant relation for enrollment type (full-time or part-time), with p-values printed in Table 4.

> "From Table 4, we can observe a meaningful relationship between student ethnicity and chronotypes. Another signiﬁcant relation is found between student enrollment type (full-time or part-time) and their chronotypes."
