### Zalewska Katarzyna 2022

Zalewska Katarzyna, Zarzycka Danuta. (2022). Best Educational Techniques in High-Fidelity Simulation According to Nursing Students—Adaptation and Validation of the Educational Practices Questionnaire (EPQ). International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192214688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive preliminary results from the 361-student Polish sample; Table 6 prints the total EPQ mean of 4.3 for both PO and IO sections, with subscale means from 4.2 (Diverse Learning Methods) to 4.5 (Collaboration).

> "The Active Learning subscale earned a score of M = 4.3, SD ± 0.9, in the opinion of the students."

### Zalewska Katarzyna 2022 (2)

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192214688

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the EPQ-PO Collaboration subscale, "the highest rating was given by the respondents to question 12" (M = 4.6, SD ± 0.6); question 11 on collaboration among scenario participants was also highly rated (M = 4.5, SD ± 0.8).

> "the highest rating was given by the respondents to question 12 (M = 4.6, SD ± 0.6), which concerned cooperation among the team caring for the patient in a specific clinical situation"
