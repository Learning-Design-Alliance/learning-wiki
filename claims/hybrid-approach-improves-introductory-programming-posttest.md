## Evidence

### Çakiroğlu 2013

Çakiroğlu, Ü. (2013). Using a hybrid approach to facilitate learning introductory programming. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1), 161-177. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008878

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two naturally occurring groups of 38 second-year students each over one semester; independent-samples t-test on total ProKT scores favored the experimental group. The article reports "t(74): 3.825, p: 0.000<0.05"; no effect size is printed.

> "A statistically significant difference between the mean posttest (ProKT) scores of the EG (M: 58.90; SD: 15.26) and CG (M: 46.63; SD: 14.28) groups was found (t(74): 3.825, p: 0.000<0.05)."
