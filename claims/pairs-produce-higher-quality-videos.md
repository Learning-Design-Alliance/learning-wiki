## Evidence

### Nicolle Zellner 2018

Nicolle Zellner. (2018). Video Killed the Writing Assignment. Journal of Astronomy and Earth Sciences Education, 5. [doi:10.19030/jaese.v5i2.10222](https://doi.org/10.19030/jaese.v5i2.10222)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor observation over 18 semesters of the video project, reported in the student-feedback section: pairs "produce much higher quality videos than students who work by themselves, with a few exceptions." This is anecdotal instructor judgment, not a controlled comparison; no effect size is printed.

> "pairs of students are able to “divide and conquer” them and in the end, produce much higher quality videos than students who work by themselves, with a few exceptions."
