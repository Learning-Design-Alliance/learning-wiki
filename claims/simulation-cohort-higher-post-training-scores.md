## Evidence

### Alghzawi A 2026

Alghzawi A, Alhalaseh L, Khalifa S, Qudah R, Saleh A-A, Okour M, Arabas E, Samhouri S. (2026). Enhancing compassionate communication: comparing lecture and simulation training in breaking bad news training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09034-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample comparisons between cohorts (Table 3): perceived knowledge 78.79 vs 68.81 (/100, p < 0.001), competence 16.78 vs 15.47 (/20, p = 0.001), comfort 3.82 vs 3.17 (/5, p < 0.001). Academic year was fully confounded with modality.

> "When outcomes were stratified by academic year, sixth-year students reported higher post-training scores across the measured domains than fifth-year students (Table 3). However, baseline differences between cohorts were observed and should be considered when interpreting these comparisons."
