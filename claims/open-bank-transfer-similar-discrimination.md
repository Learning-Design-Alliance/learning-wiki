# Open-bank and transfer problems showed high item discrimination with no significant differences between them on either exam

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Study 1, the discrimination of the open-bank problem Q4 (1.65) was nearly identical to that of transfer problem Q8 (1.80). [→ Zhongzhou Chen 2023](#zhongzhou-chen-2023)
`q2 i?` In Study 2, discrimination parameters of the three target items were more similar to other items, with no significant differences between target and other questions. [→ Zhongzhou Chen 2023 (2)](#zhongzhou-chen-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Zhongzhou Chen 2023

Zhongzhou Chen, Emily Frederick, Colleen Cui, Munaimah Khan, Christopher Klatt, Mercedith Huang, Shiyang Su. (2023). Reforming Physics Exams Using Openly Accessible Large Isomorphic Problem Banks created with the assistance of Generative AI: an Explorative Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 IRT analysis fitting a 2-PL model to mid-term exam 1 data. Item difficulties ranged from 0.63 to -1.74, and "The discrimination of Q4 (1.65) is nearly identical to the discrimination of Q8 (1.80)." No effect size was printed.

> "the difference is rather small compared to the variation of difficulties between those two and all other items on the exam, which range from 0.63 to -1.74. The discrimination of Q4 (1.65) is nearly identical to the discrimination of Q8 (1.80)."

### Zhongzhou Chen 2023 (2)

Zhongzhou Chen, Emily Frederick, Colleen Cui, Munaimah Khan, Christopher Klatt, Mercedith Huang, Shiyang Su. (2023). Reforming Physics Exams Using Openly Accessible Large Isomorphic Problem Banks created with the assistance of Generative AI: an Explorative Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.14498)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Study 2 IRT analysis of mid-term exam 2 (2-PL best fit). Discrimination of Q4, Q6 and Q8 was more similar to other items; only Q1 (0.47) was significantly lower in discrimination than any target item. No effect size printed.

> "The discrimination parameters of the three target items, however, are more similar to those of the other items, with Q1 (0.47) being the only item with significantly lower discrimination"
