## Evidence

### Qian Shen 2026

Qian Shen, Fanghua Cao, Min Yao, Shlok Gilda, Bonnie J. Dorr, Walter L. Leite. (2026). Children's English Reading Story Generation via Supervised Fine-Tuning of Compact LLMs with Controllable Difficulty and Safety. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13709 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.13709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.13709)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of toxicity outliers in the generated stories identified body-shaming and disgust-related language and unfriendly characters as contributing factors; such stories numbered five or fewer per 1,290-story dataset.

> "We identified several potential factors contributing to the higher toxicity scores, such as the inclusion of language like "you're too fat," "this smells too stinky," and "the pig can gag," or the appearance of characters portrayed as unfriendly or potentially harmful, such as rats."
