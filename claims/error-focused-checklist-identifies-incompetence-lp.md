## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In ROC analyses of 18 video-recorded simulator LP performances, the error-focused checklist identified incompetence with high accuracy (AUC 0.85) whereas the conventional checklist was poor (AUC 0.11). [→ Ma Irene W Y 2015](#ma-irene-w-y-2015)

## Evidence

### Ma Irene W Y 2015

Ma Irene W Y, Pugh Debra, Mema Briseida, Brindle Mary E, Cooke Lara, Stromer Julie N. (2015). Use of an error-focused checklist to identify incompetence in lumbar puncture performances. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.12809

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ROC analysis of 18 video-recorded simulator LP performances rated with both tools; competence/incompetence reference standard was the global rating scale summary item. The error-focused checklist achieved "AUC 0.85, 95% CI 0.67–1.00" versus 0.11 for the conventional checklist.

> "In the identification of incompetence, the accuracy of the conventional checklist was poor (AUC 0.11, 95% CI 0.00–0.28), whereas that of the error-focused checklist was high (AUC 0.85, 95% CI 0.67–1.00)."
