## Evidence

### Er Nas et al. 2016

Sibel Er Nas, Tülay Şenel Çoruhlu and Arzu Kirman Bilgin. (2016). The effect of fire context on the conceptual understanding of students: “expansion-contraction”. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2958

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Concept and drawing test results across both groups; the article concludes the conducted teaching was effective in eliminating this category for both groups.

> "Students explained the concept of expansion-contraction by relating it with phase change in the pre test implementations in both the experiment and control groups; no answer in this category has been found when the post test implementations of both concept and drawing test are examined."
