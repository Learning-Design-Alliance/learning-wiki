## Evidence

### Chang Chia-Chen 2026

Chang Chia-Chen, Wu Wen-Chi, Chen Li-Chu, Tsai Ping-Hsiu, Tung Chen-Yin. (2026). Effects of online collaborative learning and simulation-based learning on pedagogical knowledge and teaching efficacy in digital education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-48620-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired-samples t tests in a quasi-experimental pre-post study of Taiwanese teachers (OCL n = 399; SBL n = 275). Knowledge scores rose in both groups, e.g. OCL "increased from 0.67 [standard deviation (SD) = 0.20)] to 0.85" with p < 0.001. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Paired-samples t tests revealed significant improvements in pedagogical knowledge following the interventions. The average scores in the OCL and SBL groups increased from 0.67 [standard deviation (SD) = 0.20)] to 0.85 (SD = 0.17; p < 0.001) and from 0.68 (SD = 0.17) to 0.73 (SD = 0.16; p < 0.001), respectively."
