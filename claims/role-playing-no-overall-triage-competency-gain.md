## Evidence

### Seo Yon Hee 2024

Seo Yon Hee, Lim Sun-Og. (2024). Korean Triage and Acuity Scale education using role-playing and its effects on triage competency: A quasi-experimental design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311892

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Triage competency was measured with Moon and Park's 30-item, five-sub-area tool (Cronbach's α .95 in this study) in the 78-student quasi-experiment. The article reports "no significant difference between the two groups regarding triage competency".

> "There was no significant difference between the two groups regarding triage competency when the pre- and post-test values were compared (t = 1.316, p = .192)."

### Seo Yon Hee 2024 (2)

Seo Yon Hee, Lim Sun-Og. (2024). Korean Triage and Acuity Scale education using role-playing and its effects on triage competency: A quasi-experimental design. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311892

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sub-factor analysis of triage competency (Table 4) in the same study: two of five sub-areas favoured role-playing. The article states the experimental group "demonstrated significant improvement in expert assessment" and communication relative to lectures; other sub-factors were not significant.

> "the experimental group demonstrated significant improvement in expert assessment (t = 2.163, p = .034) and communication (t = 2.056, p = .043) compared to the control group"
