## Evidence

### Wang Song 2019

Wang Song, Zhao Yajun, Li Jingguang, Wang Xu, Luo Kui, Gong Qiyong. (2019). Brain structure links trait conscientiousness to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-48704-1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain VBM regression in 148 high school graduates (60 females, 88 males), controlling gender, age and total GMV, with nonstationary cluster correction (cluster p < 0.05, voxel p < 0.0025). The printed correlations were left SPL r = 0.30, right SPL r = 0.31, and right MFG r = −0.31 (all p < 0.001).

> "Scatter plots depicting the correlation between trait conscientiousness and left SPL volume (r = 0.30, p < 0.001) and right SPL volume (r = 0.31, p < 0.001). Gender, age and total GMV were regressed out in the analyses."
