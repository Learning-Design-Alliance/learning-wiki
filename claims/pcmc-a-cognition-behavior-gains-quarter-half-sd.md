## Evidence

### Pakulak 2013

Pakulak, E., Stevens, C., Bell, T. A., Fanning, J., Klein, S., Isbell, E., & Neville, H. (2013). Family-based training program improves brain function, cognition, and behavior in lower socioeconomic status preschoolers. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563293

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Findings section of the randomized trial (141 children randomly assigned to PCMC-A, HS-alone, or ABC). The authors report that all three child outcome domains "favored children in the treatment program relative to both control groups" and that "Effect sizes ranged from one-quarter to one-half of a standard deviation."

> "As illustrated in Figs. 1 and 2, electrophysiological measures of children’s brain functions supporting selective attention, standardized measures of cognition, and parent-reported child behaviors all favored children in the treatment program relative to both control groups."
