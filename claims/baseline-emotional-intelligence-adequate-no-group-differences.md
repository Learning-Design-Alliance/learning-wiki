Pre-intervention paired-samples t-tests on TMMS-24 dimensions (Table 1) for the full sample (n = 240); printed p-values were 0.193 (attention), 0.215 (clarity), and 0.641 (repair). The article states emotional intelligence was "categorised as adequate".

> "As seen in Table 1, the sample in this study demonstrated an average emotional intelligence that was categorised as adequate, and so the students were able to participate in the project. It can also be observed that there are no differences between the two participating groups."
