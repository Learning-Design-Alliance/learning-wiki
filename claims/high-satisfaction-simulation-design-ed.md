Descriptive analysis of the NLN Simulation Design Scale answered by all 91 participants after the intervention. Table 3 gives factor means of 4.47 (objectives and information), 4.60 (support), 4.54 (problem solving), 4.66 (feedback/reflection) and 4.45 (realism); the text's "from 4.34 to 4.79" is the range of item means; the overall scale mean was 4.55 with internal consistency alpha 0.898. No inferential test or effect size is reported for satisfaction.

> "the mean scores of the participants' agreement levels in relation to the objectives and information, support, problem solving, feedback/reflection and realism ranged from 4.34 to 4.79 (from a possible score of 1 to 5), with the highest score being feedback/reflection (4.66) followed by support (4.60)"
