Evidence¶

Geary 2012¶

Geary, D. C., Hoard, M. K., & Bailey, D. H. (2012). Fact Retrieval Deficits in Low Achieving Children and Children with Mathematical Learning Disability. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 45(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219410392046

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel models of the percentage of retrieval errors that were counting string intrusions, second to fourth grade, found significant group, slope, and interaction effects (F(1,225) = 19.34, F(3,219) = 19.63, F(3,219) = 4.77). The study reports the MLD group's "large decrease in intrusion errors across grades" and none for LA-Severe.

"the children with MLD showed a large decrease in intrusion errors across grades (|t|s > 2.60, ps<.01), but the LA-Severe Fact Retrieval children did not (ts < 1)."
