### Sagasser MH 2016

Sagasser MH, Kramer AWM, Fluit CRMG, van Weel C, van der Vleuten CPM. (2016). Self-entrustment: how trainees' self-regulated learning supports participation in the workplace. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9723-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational finding across seven practices: trainees differed in what patients they looked up information for, consulted supervisors for, or discussed; differences in competence and confidence, e.g. prior experience with breathing problems, shaped what they perceived as difficult and their choices.
