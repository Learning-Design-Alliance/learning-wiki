### Follette 2026

Qualitative thematic coding of free-response learning-goal questions by three SURGE coders; a theme required two or more coders' flags. Quote: "these two themes are tied at Rank 5" among formal goals.

> "Two themes, namely “Data, Computational, and Programming Skills” and “Astronomy and Astrophysics Knowledge and Skills” were mentioned in more than 60% of informal learning goal responses, placing them in the 1 and 2 ranks, respectively. Yet, among formal learning goals, these two themes are tied at Rank 5."
