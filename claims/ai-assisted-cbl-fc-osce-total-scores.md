# AI-assisted CBL+FC residents achieve higher OSCE total scores than lecture controls, driven by history collection and physical examination stations

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The AI-assisted CBL+FC group achieved a significantly higher OSCE total score than the control group (47.12 vs. 44.96, P = 0.0003). [→ Wang 2026](#wang-2026)
`q3 i?` OSCE advantages were significant for medical history collection (P = 0.027) and physical examination (P = 0.0006) but not for report analysis (P = 0.264). [→ Wang 2026 (2)](#wang-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, Q., Hu, C., Li, Y., Zhang, T., & Zhang, S. (2026). AI-assisted case-based learning and flipped classroom to improve clinical decision-making: a randomized controlled trial in reproductive medicine. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2670047

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OSCE conducted within one week post-course with three stations (medical history collection, physical examination, report analysis), each scored out of 20 points for a maximum of 60. The AI-assisted group scored 47.12 ± 1.62 versus 44.96 ± 2.26 for controls (P = 0.0003); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Total	44.96 ± 2.26	47.12 ± 1.62	0.0003***"

### Wang 2026 (2)

Wang, Q., Hu, C., Li, Y., Zhang, T., & Zhang, S. (2026). AI-assisted case-based learning and flipped classroom to improve clinical decision-making: a randomized controlled trial in reproductive medicine. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2670047

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Station-level results show significant advantages for the AI-assisted group on medical history collection (P = 0.027) and physical examination (P = 0.0006), while report analysis did not differ significantly (P = 0.264).

> "Medical History Collection	14.92 ± 1.44	15.64 ± 0.64	0.027*	Physical Examination	14.68 ± 1.25	15.76 ± 0.78	0.0006***	Report Analysis	15.36 ± 1.25	15.72 ± 0.98	0.264"
