The Discussion section, citing Martinchuk (2019b), describes the pull-out model and its devaluation problem. Support escalates from the class teacher to a special education teacher, and students with significant difficulties may be offered special full-time education.

"The most common form of support for a child with special educational needs at school is “the pull -out model ” (Martinchuk, 2019 b). This form involves visiting a special education teacher’s room during certain lessons"
