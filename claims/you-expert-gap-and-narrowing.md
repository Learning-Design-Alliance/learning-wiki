## Evidence

### Teichmann 2022

Teichmann, E., Lewandowski, H. J., and Alemani, M. (2022). Investigating students' views of experimental physics in German laboratory classes. arXiv:2201.12145. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

E-/GE-CLASS comparison study: pre-instruction E-CLASS EXPERT score was 0.858±0.002 with d=-0.20; after instruction the difference is "statistically signiﬁcant with a smaller size eﬀect than for the pre-instruction (Cohen's d=-0.10)". Students' expert-perspective pre-agreement (0.789) exceeds their personal-view agreement (0.640).

> "the gap between E-/GE-CLASS results becomes smaller for the EXPERT-questions after instruction (0.857 ±0.002 for the E-CLASS). The diﬀerence is statistically signiﬁcant with a smaller size eﬀect than for the pre-instruction (Cohen’s d=-0.10)."
