## Evidence

### Lock Nicole E. 2024

Lock Nicole E., DeBoer Mark D., Scharf Rebecca J., Miller Sarah E. (2024). Academic performance in moderately and late preterm children in the United States: are they catching up? Journal of Perinatology 44(6). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-024-01938-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression of teacher ratings in the weighted ECLS-K:2011 cohort shows the reading odds ratio "dropped from 1.19 (CI 0.89, 1.60) to 0.92 (CI 0.71, 1.19)"; both intervals include 1.0, so neither grade's odds reached significance.

> "In reading, these children appeared to improve as they progressed in grade level; their odds of performing below grade level dropped from 1.19 (CI 0.89, 1.60) to 0.92 (CI 0.71, 1.19) from kindergarten to fifth grade, respectively."

### Lock Nicole E. 2024 (2)

Lock Nicole E., DeBoer Mark D., Scharf Rebecca J., Miller Sarah E. (2024). Academic performance in moderately and late preterm children in the United States: are they catching up? Journal of Perinatology 44(6). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-024-01938-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same analysis of teacher ratings, mathematics odds of below-grade performance rose "from 1.09 (CI 0.84, 1.42) to 1.23 (CI 0.87, 1.73)" between kindergarten and fifth grade; both confidence intervals cross 1.0 and no significance test is printed.

> "In mathematics, 35-36 6/7 weeks’ children were at higher odds of being below grade level performance as grade level increased, from 1.09 (CI 0.84, 1.42) to 1.23 (CI 0.87, 1.73)."
