# Differences between performance ranks shrank in the trained cohort: the exam × cohort × rank interaction, with bottom-rank students improving substantially

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the Study Smart cohort the top and middle groups no longer differed significantly on the final exam (p = 0.078) and the bottom–middle gap was reduced but still significant (p = 0.049), whereas in the control cohort all ranks still differed significantly (ps < 0.001). ## Evidence

### Biwer Felicitas 2022

Biwer Felicitas, de Bruin Anique, Persky Adam. (2022). Study smart – impact of a learning strategy training on students’ study behavior and academic performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9397154/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simple-effects tests of the three-way interaction within the mixed ANOVA of exam scores. Bottom-rank students in the trained cohort improved 19 percentage points from midterm 1 to midterm 2 and 10 points to the final, versus 8 points and −1.8 points in the control cohort.

> "the interaction between exam, cohort, and rank was significant, F (4, 556) = 5.67, p < 0.001, ηp2= 0.039. Tests of simple effects showed that in the study smart cohort 2020, students in the top and middle group did not differ significantly in their final exam test scores, p = 0.078"
