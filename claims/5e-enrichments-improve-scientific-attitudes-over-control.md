# Both the animation-enriched and cooperative-learning-enriched 5E models improved seventh-graders' scientific attitudes relative to the 5E model alone, with no difference between the two enriched groups

## Evidence

### Daşdemir 2016

İkramettin Daşdemir. (2016). The Effect of the 5E Instructional Model Enriched With Cooperative Learning and Animations on Seventh-Grade Students' Academic Achievement and Scientific Attitudes. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 9(1), 21-38. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1126710

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Games-Howell post hoc comparisons on SAS post-test scores. Both enriched groups significantly exceeded the control group on scientific attitudes; the animation-versus-cooperative contrast was not significant (p= 0.893), which the author attributes to both being active teaching methods.

> "there was a statistically significant difference between the animation group and the control group and in favour of t he former ( p= 0. 006; p<0.05). Also there was a statistically significant difference between the cooperative group and control group, in favour  of the cooperative group ( p=0.023; p<0.05)."
