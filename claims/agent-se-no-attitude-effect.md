### Betty Tärning 2019

Betty Tärning, Annika Silvervarg, Agneta Gulz, Magnus Haake. (2019). Instructing a Teachable Agent with Low or High Self-Efficacy – Does Similarity Attract? International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0167-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Mann-Whitney U-tests on three attitude questionnaire items (instructing, chatting, wanting to continue) comparing 71 low-SE and 71 high-SE agent students. Only Question 8 (continue instructing) was marginally significant (p = .066); the summarized score showed no difference (p = 0.76).
