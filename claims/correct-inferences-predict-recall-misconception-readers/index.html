Evidence¶

Kendeou 2007¶

Kendeou, P., & van den Broek, P. (2007). The effects of prior knowledge and text structure on comprehension processes during reading of scientific texts. Memory & Cognition, 35(7), 1567-1577. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03193491

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Hierarchical multiple regression in Experiment 1 relating the seven think-aloud process categories to proportion of sentences recalled, per group. Correct inferences predicted recall for the misconception group only; bivariate correlations showed correct inferences related to conceptual change processes (r = .45) and recall (r = .42).

"The regression model for the think-aloud responses (second step), however, accounted for a significant 18% of the variance in text recall [F(7,68) 2.2, p .04]. Specifically, the number of correct inferences that occurred during the think-aloud phase strongly predicted the proportion of sentences recalled"
