Self-reported practice survey of the same 320 PHC workers using a 10-item practice score (75% cutoff for adequate). The study reports "The overall adequate practice score was 54.7% (175/320)" with regular follow-up scheduling at 48.1%, guideline adherence at 51.6%, and referral of complicated cases at 46.9%.

> "The overall adequate practice score was 54.7% (175/320). While routine blood pressure screening was reported by 230 (71.9%) and lifestyle counselling by 206 (64.4%), core chronic disease management practices were deficient."
