### Early Literacy and Learning Model (ELLM/Plus) 2008

Early Literacy and Learning Model (ELLM/Plus). (2008). Evidence rating summary, Preschool Curriculum Evaluation Research (PCER) studies. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/early-literacy-and-learning-model-plus-ellm-plus/

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

First PCER randomized study: 48 Florida preschool classes of varied types randomly assigned to ELLM or control. All four TERA-3 scale outcomes were positive and significant, with an "average effect size =+0.26".

> "The first study took place in 48 Florida preschool classes (subsidized, faith-based, Head Start, and schools) randomly assigned to ELLM or control. On the Test of Early Reading Abilities (TERA-3), positive outcomes were found on four scales (average effect size =+0.26). All outcomes were statistically significant."
