## Evidence

### Nomura Roseli Mieko Yamamoto 2023

Nomura Roseli Mieko Yamamoto, Reis Fabiana Medeiros D., Gonçalves Amanda Menezes, de Proença Carolina Matos. (2023). Obstetric simulation for undergraduate medical education: how to improve students’ self-confidence and expectation according to gender. Revista da Associação Médica Brasileira. https://doi.org/10.1590/1806-9282.20221625

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney comparisons of subscale sums by gender in the before-and-after questionnaire study (Table 3). The article reports the printed medians and p-values for the three significant gender contrasts; other subscales showed no significant difference.

> "female students had a significantly higher sum of scores than the male students in the initial subscale for “expectation” (median, 24 vs. 22, p<0.001) and “interest” (median, 23 vs. 21, p=0.032), and a higher sum of scores in the subscale for “expectation” (median, 23 vs. 21, p=0.010) in the final questionnaire"
