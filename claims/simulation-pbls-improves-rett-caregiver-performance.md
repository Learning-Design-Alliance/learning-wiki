## Evidence

### Kocyigit S 2026

Kocyigit S, Akgun-Dogan O, Hatirnaz Ng O, Alanay Y, Guven F. (2026). Pilot study: the impact of simulation-based pediatric basic life support training on the performance of caregivers of children with Rett syndrome. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09158-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre-post pilot study of ten caregivers using a 16-step ERC-aligned checklist. The article reports the total score change, stating it indicates "a substantial improvement in participants' PBLS skills".

> "The median total score increased markedly from a pre-training mean of 25.5 (SD: ± 10.1) to a post-training mean of 127 (SD: ± 22.1), indicating a substantial improvement in participants' PBLS skills."

### Kocyigit S 2026 (2)

Kocyigit S, Akgun-Dogan O, Hatirnaz Ng O, Alanay Y, Guven F. (2026). Pilot study: the impact of simulation-based pediatric basic life support training on the performance of caregivers of children with Rett syndrome. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09158-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test comparing pre- and post-training checklist scores in the same ten-caregiver pilot. The article reports a statistically significant overall effect with "ES > 0.4" but no exact effect size value.

> "Overall, simulation-based PBLS training had a statistically significant effect on participant performance (p < 0.05; ES > 0.4)."
