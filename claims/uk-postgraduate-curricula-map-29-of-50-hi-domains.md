Jidkov Lydia, Alexander Matthew, Bark Pippa, Williams John G, Kay Jonathan, Taylor Paul, Hemingway Harry, Banerjee Amitava. (2019). Health informatics competencies in postgraduate medical education and training in the UK: a mixed methods study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025460

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Curricular content analysis of national training curricula for 71 UK postgraduate medical specialties obtained from the GMC, searched for HI keywords and mapped to the 50-domain IMIA-based framework. The article reports "Out of the 50 domains, 29 were present across 71 curricula documents."
