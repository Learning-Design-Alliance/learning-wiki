Evidence¶

Zeynep Koyunlu Ünlü & İbilge Dökme 2011¶

Zeynep Koyunlu Ünlü & İbilge Dökme. (2011). The effect of combining analogy-based simulation and laboratory activities on Turkish elementary school students' understanding of simple electric circuits. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(4). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojet

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-test for control group I (n=21): mean EPT scores rose from 7.24 to 12.95 after the laboratory-method unit.

"It was found that there had been a significant increase in the academic achievements of the students after having the unit through laboratory method t(20)=6.69, p<.05."

Zeynep Koyunlu Ünlü & İbilge Dökme 2011 (2)¶

Zeynep Koyunlu Ünlü & İbilge Dökme. (2011). The effect of combining analogy-based simulation and laboratory activities on Turkish elementary school students' understanding of simple electric circuits. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(4). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojet

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-test for control group II (n=18): mean EPT scores rose from 6.06 to 11.94 after the analogy-based simulation unit.

"It was found that there had been a significant increase in the academic achievements of the students after having the unit through analogy-based simulation method t(17)=8.97, p<.05."

Zeynep Koyunlu Ünlü & İbilge Dökme 2011 (3)¶

Zeynep Koyunlu Ünlü & İbilge Dökme. (2011). The effect of combining analogy-based simulation and laboratory activities on Turkish elementary school students' understanding of simple electric circuits. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(4). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojet

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-test for the experimental group (n=27): mean EPT scores rose from 6.52 to 15.3 after the combined-method unit.

"it was found that there had been a significant increase in the academic achievements of the students after having the unit through the combination of laboratory method and analogy-based simulation method t(26)=20.34, p<.05"
