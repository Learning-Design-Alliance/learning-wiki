## Evidence

### Kutbay 2020

Kutbay, E. & Akpınar, Y. (2020). Investigating modality, redundancy and signaling principles with abstract and concrete representation. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology (IJEMST), 8(2), 131-145. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1255527

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test in the quasi-experiment comparing spoken-text-only versus written-plus-spoken text with abstract animation. No significant difference: "t (184) = .35, p = .720"; equivalence was not tested.

> "there was not a significant difference between the mean achievement scores for the Sp-A group and of the W+Sp-A group; t (184) = .35, p = .720"

### Kutbay 2020 (2)

Kutbay, E. & Akpınar, Y. (2020). Investigating modality, redundancy and signaling principles with abstract and concrete representation. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology (IJEMST), 8(2), 131-145. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1255527

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing spoken-text-only versus written-plus-spoken text with concrete animation. No significant difference was found; equivalence was not tested.

> "between the mean achievement scores of the Sp-C group and of the W+Sp-C group; t (168) = .89, p = .372"
