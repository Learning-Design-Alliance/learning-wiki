## Evidence

### Tsai 2017

Tsai, Min-hsiu. (2017). Reusing Comprehensive Charts of Tense Forms to Teach EFL Students in a University of Science and Technology. World Journal of Education, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v7n3p115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-study comparison of translation test results: the previous study's experimental group (n = 36, private university) scored zero on all three sentences, while the present study's experimental group (n = 120, national university, A3 level) scored 47-50 correct per item. The comparison is descriptive, not a controlled test.

> "when the experimental groups in both studies are compared, the participants in the present study outperform their counterparts in the previous study on all three translation sentences of specific tense forms. This means learners' language competence play a crucial role in acquiring the tense forms."
