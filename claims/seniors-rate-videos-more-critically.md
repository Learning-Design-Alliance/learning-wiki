# Senior surgeons rate online surgical videos as lower quality and lower educational utility than fellows and trainees do

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Bayesian ordinal regression models of ratings of 25 robotic ventral rectopexy videos, fellows and trainees had a significantly higher probability of perceiving videos as high-quality and high-utility, while senior surgeons tended to rate video quality lower and had a higher probability of rating videos as having low utility. [→ Marotta A 2025](#marotta-a-2025)

## Evidence

### Marotta A 2025

Marotta A, Schena CA, Ascanelli S, Azzolina D, Calabrese P, Iovino DP, Sani V, Gavriilidis P, Laterza V, Marchegiani F, Pellino G, Celentano V, de'Angelis N. (2025). Robotic ventral rectopexy videos on youtube: reliability of quality and educational value assessment among raters with different degrees of surgical experience. Int J Colorectal Dis 40:152. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-04936-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian ordinal regression (cumulative logit) of overall video quality ratings from the 25-video YouTube corpus, with rater group, views, days online, and likes as fixed effects and random effects for evaluator and video. A second model for utility for trainees found "Senior surgeons had a higher probability of rating videos as having low utility".

> "Both fellows and trainees demonstrated a significantly higher probability of perceiving videos as high-quality (Table 5, Panel A)."
