Evidence¶

Bogale 2018¶

Bogale, Y. N. (2018). Conceptualizing Reading to Learn: Strategy Instruction and EFL Students' Reading Comprehension. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 10(2), 93–117. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207221

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Item-level paired analysis of the researcher-developed pre/post tests. The article reports progress on main-idea and inference items but notes students "could not do so even after the instruc tion" on the word-meaning item, citing Wong (2010) on similar difficulty.

"It was found that both groups of students made significant progress on each item, especially on questions number 1, 3, 5 e xcept question number 2. Question number 1 asks students to determine the main idea o f the text, whereas questions number 3 and 5 ask the students to draw inferences and question 2 asks students to guess meaning of words in the reading text."
