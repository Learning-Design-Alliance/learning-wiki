The article's literature review, citing Winne (2020), argues that self-report methods face limitations including memory limitations, social desirability bias, lack of metacognitive awareness, failure to account for context-specific nature of self-regulation, and scalability challenges in large courses.

> "Winne (2020) argues that self-report scales may not adequately reflect the actual regulatory processes that students engage in during learning tasks; a crucial aspect of cognitive information processing models."
