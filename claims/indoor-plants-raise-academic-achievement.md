# Exposure to indoor plants significantly raises academic achievement compared with no plants

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students in classrooms with plants had significantly higher academic achievement (course grades and examination scores) than students without plants (pooled SMD = 0.534, p = 0.004). [→ Han 2022](#han-2022)

## Evidence

### Han 2022

Han, K.-T., Ruan, L.-W., Liao, L.-S., & Tchounwou, P. B. (2022). Effects of Indoor Plants on Human Functions: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analyses. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(12), 7454. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19127454

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Fixed-effect meta-analysis of two studies (119 participants; 58 control, 61 experimental) measuring course grades and examination scores, with low heterogeneity (I2 = 0%). One study was appraised low quality, the other moderate.

> "The pooled effect size (SMD) was 0.534, and the 95% confidence interval ranged from 0.167 to 0.901. The results indicated that the group with plants had significantly higher academic achievement (p = 0.004) than the group without plants"
