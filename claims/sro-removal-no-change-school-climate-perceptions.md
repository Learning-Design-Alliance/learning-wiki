# SRO removal was not significantly related to changes in student or teacher perceptions of physical safety or in Student-Teacher Trust

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The districtwide decrease in student perceptions of physical safety held across all SRO presence categories, with no significant difference-in-difference. [→ Arneson 2024](#arneson-2024)
`q2 i?` No significant difference-in-difference was detected for teacher perceptions of physical safety or for Student-Teacher Trust at student or school levels. [→ Arneson 2024 (2)](#arneson-2024-2)

## Evidence

Arneson, A., Hinze-Pifer, R., Franklin, K., & Johnson, D.W. (2024). Removing police officers from Chicago schools: Trends and outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/removing-police-officers-from-Chicago-schools

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-difference analysis of 5Essentials Student Physical Safety scores (Rasch-calibrated logit scale) across SRO categories; the districtwide downward trend did not differ significantly by removal status.

> "From 2018–19 to 2022–23, there was a districtwide decrease in student perception of physical safety across high schools (see Figure 3) and this trend held in the different SRO presence categories of schools and for their students"

### Arneson 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null results for the 5Essentials Student-Teacher Trust measure and the supplementary Teacher Physical Safety measure, at both school and student levels. The brief notes climate outcomes are distal and may need multiple years of implementation to change.

> "We did not detect significant differences in the Student-Teacher Trust trends with partial or full removal of SROs at the student or school levels"
