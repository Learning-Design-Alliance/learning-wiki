### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview problem 1, question C compared draining times of two containers, one with a vertical pipe of length L. "Approximately two-thirds of the students responded that the draining times were the same", reasoning from equal water levels and output velocities while ignoring the different pressure at the pipe inlet.

> "Approximately two-thirds of the students responded that the draining times were the same (E1.4). The most frequent argument between the students of group C1.1 (constant velocity inside the pipe) was that the amount of water above the mouth of the base of each container was the same."
