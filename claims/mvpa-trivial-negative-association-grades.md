# Minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity show a negative but trivial prospective association with adolescent English and Math grades

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Baseline average daily MVPA was significantly but trivially negatively associated with follow-up English grades (r = -.047) and Math grades (r = -.026). [→ Papasideris M 2021](#papasideris-m-2021)
`q3 i?` In covariate-adjusted mixed models the MVPA association remained significant, negative and trivial (English f = -.022; Math f = -.022). [→ Papasideris M 2021 (2)](#papasideris-m-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Papasideris M 2021

Papasideris M, Leatherdale ST, Battista K, Hall PA. (2021). An examination of the prospective association between physical activity and academic achievement in youth at the population level. PLoS ONE 16(6): e0253142. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253142

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective analysis of 9,898 linked COMPASS participants from year 2 (2013–2014) to year 4 (2015–2016). Zero-order correlations showed "negative in direction and trivial in absolute magnitude" associations with self-reported grades.

> "Zero-order Pearson correlation coefficients linking baseline average daily MVPA with subsequent academic achievement were statistically significant, but negative in direction and trivial in absolute magnitude (English: r = -.047, p < .000; Math: r = -.026, p = .009)."

### Papasideris M 2021 (2)

Papasideris M, Leatherdale ST, Battista K, Hall PA. (2021). An examination of the prospective association between physical activity and academic achievement in youth at the population level. PLoS ONE 16(6): e0253142. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253142

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate-adjusted linear mixed models with random intercepts controlling for baseline grade, demographics, sleep, homework and grade importance, accounting for school clustering, yielded "English: f = -.022; Math: f = -.022".

> "The Cohen’s f effect size for this relationship was as follows: English: f = -.022; Math: f = -.022 (Table 4)."
