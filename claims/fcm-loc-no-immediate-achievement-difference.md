## Evidence

### Aslan Seda Aktı 2025

Aslan Seda Aktı, Turgut Yigit Emrah, Ünlü Gökhan, Allison Jordan. (2025). Effectiveness of flipped classroom model in distance education: An experimental study in Turkish language course. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335721

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Independent groups t-test on immediate post-test scores of the 14-week quasi-experiment found no significant difference; the article reports the experimental group mean (M = 59.70, SD = 20.56) was higher than the control group (M = 54.04, SD = 21.05) with an effect size as measured by Cohen's d of 0.27.

> "When Table 3 is examined, according to the independent groups t-test results of the groups' learning achievement post-test scores, there is no significant difference between the groups (t(57)=1.035, p > .05)."
