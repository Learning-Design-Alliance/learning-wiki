Rohollahzadeh Ebadi, M., Mohd Saad, M. R., & Abedalaziz, N. (2013). Explicit Form Focus Instruction: The Effects on Implicit and Explicit Knowledge of ESL Learners. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086281

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive group means from Table 2 of the ANCOVA study (experimental n=47, control n=44) for implicit knowledge. The article reports these unadjusted means and notes "virtually no difference between the experimental group and control group remains after differences in pretest scores are controlled".

> "students in the experimental group ( M = 47.02, SD = 5.74) scored significantly higher than students in the control group (M = 31.88, SD = 5.10)"
