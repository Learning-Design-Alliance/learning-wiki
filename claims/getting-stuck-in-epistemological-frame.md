## Evidence

### Bing 2011

Bing, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2011). Epistemic Complexity and the Journeyman-Expert Transition. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.8.010105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.8.010105)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Recap of three previously reported observational examples: novices using recursive plug-and-chug on an estimation problem, quantum students stuck in Calculation for fifteen minutes on a wrong integral, and two students unable to reconcile Invoking Authority and Physical Mapping warrants over a path-independence integral.

> "In our previous work 2,3,1 we have given examples of students "getting stuck" in an epistemological framing that is inappropriate for solving the problem they are working on. The result is that for many minutes they fail to bring to bear knowledge that they both know very well and that would have shown them their current approach was in error."
