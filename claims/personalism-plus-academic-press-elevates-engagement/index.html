Evidence¶

Sebring 1996¶

Sebring, P. B., Bryk, A. S., Roderick, M., Camburn, E., Luppescu, S., Thum, Y. M., Smith, B., & Kahne, J. (1996). Charting Reform in Chicago: The Students Speak. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-chicago-students-speak

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Schools were divided into nine categories by low, moderate, or high average ratings on personalism and academic press, and students' reports of their own academic effort were compared across categories. The combination of the two "elevates academic engagement substantially."

"Our analyses indicate that per- sonalism, by itself, appears to have a small influence on students' engage- ment in learning, and the same is true of academic press. But the combina- tion of the two elevates academic engagement substantially."
