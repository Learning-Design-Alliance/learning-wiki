## Evidence

### Yamamoto R 2026

Yamamoto R, Nojiri T, Yoshida D, Oyamada Y, Fukutoku A, Kon K. (2026). Accuracy of dynamic computer-assisted implant surgery among novice operators using three different patient registration methods: an in-vitro study. International Journal of Implant Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40729-026-00666-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Implant-level analysis (n = 25 per group) of two-dimensional entry point deviation; means were 0.95 mm (XC), 0.68 mm (XM), 0.97 mm (ND). The omnibus difference "did not remain statistically significant after Bonferroni correction"; the XC–XM pairwise difference (p = 0.0045) also fell to p = 0.0136 after correction.

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test revealed a significant difference among groups (H = 9.20, p = 0.010); however, this difference did not remain statistically significant after Bonferroni correction (adjusted p = 0.040; α = 0.0125)."
