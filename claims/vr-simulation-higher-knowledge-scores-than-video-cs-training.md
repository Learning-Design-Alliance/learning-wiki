### Kim Hyeon Ji MD 2024

Kim Hyeon Ji MD, Lee Hee Kyeong BS, Jang Ji Yeon MD, Lee Kyong-No MD, Suh Dong Hoon PhD, Kong Hyoun-Joong PhD, Lee Seung-Hee PhD, Park Jee Yoon PhD. (2024). Immersive virtual reality simulation training for cesarean section: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000843

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`
