# Small edits to cGPT instructions and knowledge bases had unpredictable effects on GPT responses during iterative co-design

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` During iterative cGPT development, students found that edits in wording, uploaded documents, and sentence additions or deletions could either clearly affect GPT responses or have no apparent effect. [→ Lo S 2026](#lo-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Lo S 2026

Lo S, Lea T, Mavromoustakos A, Joseph B, Yii M, Exintaris B, Karunaratne N, Pearson DL, Ritchie T, Yuriev E and Pyun J (2026) Co-creating custom GPTs: an autoethnographic study of undergraduate students as partners in generative AI innovation. Front. Educ. 11:1818781. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1818781

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis of summer-scholarship students' SPAR autoethnographic logs written during iterative cGPT construction. Students reported that "Edits in wording, uploaded documents, addition and deletion of sentences and paragraphs" produced inconsistent effects on model responses, requiring sustained critical evaluation of outputs.

> "Edits in wording, uploaded documents, addition and deletion of sentences and paragraphs could either clearly affect how the GPT responded, or have no apparent effect"
