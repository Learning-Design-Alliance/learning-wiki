## Evidence

### Yavuzarslan 2019

Yavuzarslan, M., Olgun, H., & Yazici, S. (2019). A Pilot Study on the Comparison Between Blended and F2F Learning Methods in a SQL Course. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED602380

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experimental comparison of 61 undergraduates: blended group (n=22) using a Google Course Builder SQL course plus 20 minutes of F2F lecturing per lesson, control group (n=39) taught by verbal lecturing. Mann-Whitney U test favored the control group; the article reports "r=.36". The text prints "p>.05" while Table 6 prints p=.005.

> "It can be seen from Table 6 that a statistically significant difference was found between the mean scores of control (Md=62.5, n=39) and experimental groups (Md=30.0, n=22) in favor of the control group (U=242.0, z=-2.813, p>.05, r=.36)."
