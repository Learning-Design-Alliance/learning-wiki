## Evidence

### Abdelkarim 2016

Abdelkarim, R., & Abuiyada, R. (2016). The Effect of Peer Teaching on Mathematics Academic Achievement of the Undergraduate Students in Oman. International Education Studies, 9(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n5p124

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest control-experimental group experiment with 32 female Dhofar University students randomly assigned, all scoring 8-12 of 20 on the midterm. The peer-teaching group mean was "(6.6875)" versus "(4.5000)" for control on the 10-point MACT; the t-test was significant at p=0.012. No effect size is printed.

> "Table 4 shows that “there is a significant difference in the means of the experimental group (M=6.69, SD=1.96) and the control group (M=4.50, SD=2.63) of the post- test; t(30)=2.667, p=0.012”."
