## Evidence

### Russo and Hopkins 2017

James Russo, Sarah Hopkins. (2017). How does lesson structure shape teacher perceptions of teaching with challenging tasks? Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(1), 30-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1152737

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview analysis (Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis) of three Year 1/2 teachers who observed two units taught with challenging tasks; the article reports "a consensus amongst the three teachers" that the task-first structure fostered creative thinking, with Sally valuing that students were not stifled and Rachel emphasising students finding their own way to solve problems.

> "There seemed to be a consensus amongst the three teachers that beginning a lesson with the challenging task appears to foster more creative forms of mathematical thinking."
