## Evidence

### Zhaoquan Fu 2025

Zhaoquan Fu, Yaping Wang, Jiayu Pei. (2025). Effects of reflective diaries guided by Kolbs experiential learning theory on learning engagement and academic emotions in nursing undergraduates a quasi-experimental study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-25940-2

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same paired-samples t-test analysis of the 65-student single-group study using the College Student Academic Emotion Questionnaire, both positive-emotion dimensions rose significantly from pretest to post-test, with printed effect sizes d = 1.07 and d = 0.80, both at or above the large-effect threshold.

> "the scores of positive activity orientation and positive outcome orientation were significantly increased from pretest to post-test, from 48.35 to 49.90 (t = − 8.64, p < 0.001, d = 1.07) and from 25.29 to 26.15 (t = − 6.42, p < 0.001, d = 0.80), respectively. This indicated that participants’ positive academic emotions were enhanced"
