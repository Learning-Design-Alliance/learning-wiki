`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional baseline (n = 1277 with valid sex data) collected February–April 2019 in four quintile three public schools in Port Elizabeth. The article reports "9.1% of the children were categorized as stunted (95% CI: 7.6–10.8)" and anemia at 19.2%.

> "In total, 7.2% of the study population were infected with STH (95% CI: 5.7–8.5) and 9.1% of the children were categorized as stunted (95% CI: 7.6–10.8). Higher proportions of children were reported to be anemic (19.2%, 95% CI: 16.7–21.7)."
