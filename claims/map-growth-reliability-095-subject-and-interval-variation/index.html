MAP Growth Technical Report for 2024–2025 2026¶

MAP Growth Technical Report for 2024–2025. (2026). HMH Education Company. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-technical-report/

q2 · i? · design · r2

The report's reliability chapter summary reports marginal (IRT internal consistency) and test-retest reliability for operational 2024–2025 test events, stating coefficients "average about 0.95." Reliability coefficients are printed but no standardized effect size is reported.

"Across subjects and grades, reliability coefficients are generally high and average about 0.95, with slightly lower values in Science and early grades. Test-retest reliability is stronger for shorter intervals (winter–spring) than longer intervals (fall–spring)."
