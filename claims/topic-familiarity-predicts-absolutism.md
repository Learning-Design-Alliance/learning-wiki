### Merk 2018

Merk, S., Rosman, T., Muis, K. R., Kelava, A., & Bohl, T. (2018). Topic specific epistemic beliefs: Extending the Theory of Integrated Domains in Personal Epistemology. Learning and Instruction, 56, 84–97. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.04.008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multilevel path model M1 fitted to the Study 1 survey data (11 topics nested in persons) tested topic familiarity as a level-1 predictor of topic-specific absolutism; the article reports "small, positive, but signiﬁcant, eﬀects" at both levels, with no standardized coefficient printed for these paths.

> "M1 showed small, positive, but signiﬁcant, eﬀects of topic familiarity on topic-speciﬁc absolutism at both levels, which can be interpreted as a positivistic rather than a naive view on topics associated with high topic familiarity"
