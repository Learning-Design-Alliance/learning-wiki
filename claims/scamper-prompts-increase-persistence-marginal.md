## Evidence

### Farrokhnia 2024

Farrokhnia, M., Noroozi, O., Baggen, Y., Biemans, H., & Weinberger, A. (2024). Improving hybrid brainstorming outcomes with computer-supported scaffolds: Prompts and cognitive group awareness. Computers & Education 227 (2025) 105229. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105229

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on first-individual-phase persistence in the same 94-student randomized experiment: overall condition effect significant (F(2,89) = 3.46, p = .04, ηp² = 0.07); Bonferroni pairwise comparisons against control reached only the 10 percent level (p = .08).

> "Results also showed a significant difference at the 10 percent level (p = .08) regarding the persistence of ideas generated in experimental 1 (Adjusted M = 2.19, SE = 0.17) and 2 (Adjusted M = 2.24, SE = 0.17) conditions compared to those in control condition (Adjusted M = 1.67, SE = 0.17)"
