# Learning gains from ChatGPT-generated hints were not statistically significantly different from human tutor-authored hints, though ChatGPT gains were numerically higher in all subjects

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` ChatGPT hint gains and human tutor hint gains were not statistically significantly separable (p = 0.416), despite ChatGPT hints producing 46.30% higher learning gains overall. [→ Pardos ZA 2024](#pardos-za-2024)
`q3 i?` Human tutor-authored hints were not statistically significantly different from the control (p = 0.087). [→ Pardos ZA 2024](#pardos-za-2024)

## Evidence

### Pardos ZA 2024

Pardos ZA, Bhandari S (2024) ChatGPT-generated help produces learning gains equivalent to human tutor-authored help on mathematics skills. PLoS ONE 19(5): e0304013. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0304013

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise post-hoc comparisons in the same randomized 3x4 experiment (N = 274). The article reports the ChatGPT vs human-tutor contrast as "not statistically significantly separable (p = 0.416)" and human-tutor vs control as p = 0.087; no effect sizes are printed.

> "When comparing the magnitude of learning gain from the human tutor hints and ChatGPT hints, it can be observed that ChatGPT hints produced 46.30% higher learning gains, overall, as compared to human-authored hints."
