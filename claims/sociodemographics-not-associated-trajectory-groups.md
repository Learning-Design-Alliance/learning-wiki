### Yunusova Asal 2026

Yunusova Asal, Huang Caitlin, Price Stephen, Joshi Kunal, Dutcher Janine M., Brown Kirk Warren, Villalba Daniella K., Tumminia Michael J., Creswell Kasey G., Cohen Sheldon, Creswell J. David. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square and Fisher's exact tests on N = 271 first-year students across the four trajectory groups; a Cramer's V for gender indicated a weak association (V = 0.13). Equivalence was not tested; the article reports no standardized effect sizes for these nulls.

> "There was no significant relationship between gender (X2 (3) = 4.93, p = 0.18), international student status (p = 0.25), first-generation student status (p = 0.77), or ethnicity (p = 0.46) and trajectory group membership (see Table 5)."
