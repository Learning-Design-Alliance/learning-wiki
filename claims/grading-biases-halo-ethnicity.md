`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Bonefeld and Dickhäuser (2018), that "despite identical performances, preservice teachers systematically assigned lower grades to students with an immigration background"; rule-based judgments showed no differences. No effect size is printed.

> "They found that, despite identical performances, preservice teachers systematically assigned lower grades to students with an immigration background, whereas no differences were found between students with and without an immigration background in rule-based judgments"
