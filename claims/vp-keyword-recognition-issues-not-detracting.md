## Evidence

### Thompson Jessica; White Simon; Chapman Stephen 2020

Thompson Jessica; White Simon; Chapman Stephen. (2020). Virtual patients as a tool for training pre-registration pharmacists and increasing their preparedness to practice: A qualitative study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238226

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme 'Technical issues' reported by VP-group trainees. The keyword database "not picking up all spellings, words or phrases" was the main problem; one trainee stated it did not impact learning as the feedback still showed the intended point, and some trainees used it to practise rephrasing questions.

> "The main problem associated with the VP technology was the recognition of user inputs when using free-text due to the keyword database not picking up all spellings, words or phrases"
