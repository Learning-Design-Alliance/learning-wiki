Feng 2026¶

Feng, S., Koch, M. J., & Carolus, A. (2026). AI Mindset – An empirically tested theoretical framework on the psychological factors shaping AI competence and AI use. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100402

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Discussion of the SEM results for the teacher sample. The discussion states effort-related appraisals showed an indirect association with AI use through competence; the results section prints the competence path (β = 0.28, p = .006), the indirect effect (β = 0.12, p = .021), and a non-significant direct path (β = -0.08, p = .319).

"Effort-related appraisals showed an indirect association with AI use through competence, indicating that perceptions of the ease, usability, and effort required to use AI tools may be related to AI use primarily by supporting the competence required to implement AI in educational practice."

Discussion of the SEM results for the same sample. The discussion reports hedonic motivation showed a direct positive association with AI use; the results section prints the direct path as β = 0.19, p = .029, with a non-significant indirect effect via competence.

"In contrast, affective reward appraisals, reflected by hedonic motivation, showed a direct positive association with AI use, suggesting that experiential enjoyment may be related to AI engagement indepen - dent of perceived competence."
