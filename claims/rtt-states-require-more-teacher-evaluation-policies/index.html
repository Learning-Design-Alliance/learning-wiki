An evaluation brief on states' teacher evaluation policies compared policy requirements across states. It reports states required "less than half of the eight teacher evaluation policies" aligned with RTT criteria, with RTT states requiring more than non-RTT states. No effect size is printed.

"An evaluation brief on states' teacher evaluation policies showed that on average, states reported requiring less than half of the eight teacher evaluation policies aligned with criteria in the RTT application, although RTT states required more policies than did non-RTT states."
