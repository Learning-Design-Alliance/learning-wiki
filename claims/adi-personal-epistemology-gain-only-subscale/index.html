Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin 2024¶

Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin (2024). Investigating the role of an inquiry-based science lab on students' scientific literacy. Instructional Science (2024) 52:743–760. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09672-w

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-tests on SLA-MB subscales in Results (Table 9 prints d = 0.79; post means 37.3 vs 31.3). The article attributes the increase to argumentation sessions of ADI.

"there was a significant difference in the mean post test scores of experimental and control group students in personal epistemology of science subscales (t(65) = 3.05, p < .05)"

Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin 2024 (2)¶

Gülüzar Eymur · Pınar Seda Çetin (2024). Investigating the role of an inquiry-based science lab on students' scientific literacy. Instructional Science (2024) 52:743–760. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09672-w

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on overall SLA-MB scores in the Results section. No statistically significant difference between the two groups; equivalence was not tested.

"The results of independent sample t-test analyses show that there was not a significant mean difference between the SLA-MB scores of students in the experimental group and those in the comparison group after the treatment (t(65) = 1.35, p > .05)"

Related Claims¶

  • Eighth graders experiencing ADI instruction scored significantly higher on the post-test of demonstrated scientific literacy (SLA-D) than students experiencing structured inquiry
  • Interviewed ADI students reported engaging in scientific practices such as identifying variables, finding patterns in data, and developing methods, while no comparison-group students did
  • ADI students' item-level gains concentrated on items about determining variables for a research question and on scientific inquiry questions — related
