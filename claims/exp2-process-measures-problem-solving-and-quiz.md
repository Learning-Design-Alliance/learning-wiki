# In Experiment 2, I-PS students outperformed PS-I students on problem-solving, idea generation, and embedded video quizzes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` I-PS students significantly outperformed PS-I students during the problem-solving phase and generated more ideas. [→ Cheng-Wen He 2025](#cheng-wen-he-2025)
`q3 i?` I-PS students scored significantly higher than PS-I students on the embedded quizzes during the instruction phase. [→ Cheng-Wen He 2025 (2)](#cheng-wen-he-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Cheng-Wen He 2025

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quade ANCOVA of coded problem-solving performance in Experiment 2, controlling for prior knowledge. The I-PS group also generated significantly more ideas (F(1,136) = 20.56, p < .001), so "outperformed the PS-I students" on both process measures.

> "the I-PS students ( M=6 .64,SD = 4.07 ) outperformed the PS-I students ( M=2 .66,SD = 2.34 ) in the problem-solving phase, F(1,136) = 60.51 , p < .001"

### Cheng-Wen He 2025 (2)

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quade ANCOVA of embedded video-quiz scores in Experiment 2, controlling for prior knowledge. Unlike Experiment 1, the authors suggest "the initial problem-solving phase may have interfered with students' learning from the instructional video".

> "the I-PS group ( M=5 .77,SD = 0.50 ) scored significantly higher on the quizzes than the PS-I group ( M=5 .40,SD = 0.83 ), F(1,136) = 11.41 , p = .001"
