## Evidence

### DiPietro Joan 2019

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized trial at the University of Amsterdam with 121 children with ASD (ages 8–12, IQ above 80) assigned to adaptive working-memory training (N = 40), adaptive cognitive flexibility training (N = 37), or nonadaptive mock training (N = 38). "Statistically significant differences across groups were not achieved."

> "Following intervention, all children in all groups, including the mock control group, exhibited improvements in working memory, cognitive flexibility, attention, and parent ratings. No improvement was observed in inhibition. Statistically significant differences across groups were not achieved. The attrition rate was high: 26%."
