Orfield 2022¶

Orfield, M., & Stancil, W. (2022). Magnet schools and metropolitan civil rights planning: A strategy to revitalize and stabilize distressed communities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/197.312

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report's introduction synthesizes prior integration research, citing benefits including "higher achievement in math, science, language, and reading" and increased high school and college graduation likelihood. The report attributes this to the cited literature rather than testing it.

"By contrast, a substantial body of research has found that racially integrated learning environments have positive impacts on academic achievement for students of all races."
