Follow-up questionnaire results (N = 121) on teaching-practice and affective outcomes: e.g., 75.2% rated 'My knowledge and practices are updated' as very important, and 70–80% bands included belonging, personal and professional appreciation, proximity to students and partnerships with colleagues. The authors interpret these as broader FD effects possibly favouring professional identity formation and communities of practice.
