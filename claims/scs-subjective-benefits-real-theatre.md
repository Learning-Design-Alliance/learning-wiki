Cragg J, Mushtaq F, Lal N, Garnham A, Hallissey M, Graham T, Shiralkar U. (2021). Surgical cognitive simulation improves real-world surgical performance: randomized study. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrab003

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subjective evaluation collected only from the intervention group at 4 months, reported as weighted mean scores (0–10) in Table 3, with "Improved sense of preparedness" scoring 7.9 and quality of focus 7.6; 88 per cent answered yes to a role for cognitive simulation in surgical training. No objective test supports these self-reports.
