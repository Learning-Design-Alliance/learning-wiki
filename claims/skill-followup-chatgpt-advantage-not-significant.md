## Evidence

### Ji Zhe 2024

Ji Zhe, Jiang Yuliang, Sun Haitao, Qiu Bin, Chen Yi, Li Mao, Fan Jinghong, Wang Junjie. (2024). Enhancing puncture skills training with generative AI and digital technologies: a parallel cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06217-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up analysis of the same blinded checklist skill ratings in the 90-doctor parallel cohort. Table 2 prints follow-up skill means of 4.0 ± 0.18 (G3), 4.0 ± 0.32 (G2), 3.8 ± 0.43 (G1), with pairwise p values of 0.026 (G1 vs G2), 0.071 (G1 vs G3), and 0.695 (G2 vs G3).

> "At 3-month follow-up, both Group 2 and Group 3 were superior to Group 1, with statistically significant differences between Group 1 and Group 2 (p < 0.05). However, there were no statistically significant differences between Group 1 and Group 3, or between Group 2 and Group 3 (p > 0.05)"
