# Laban advantage holds for dance Sections I–III but not Section IV in individual learning

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Elyağutu and Hazar 2017

Dilek Cantekin Elyağutu and Muhsin Hazar. (2017). Comparison of movement notation (Laban) and traditional methodological learning success in teaching folk dances. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.3118

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing individual learning scores of the two groups (n=14 each) per dance section. Means: Laban 4.57, 4.53, 4.54, 4.37 vs Traditional 4.20, 4.20, 4.34, 4.25 for Sections I–IV.

> "Section I (t (26)=6.457, p<0.05), Section II (t (26)=3.997, p<0.05) and Section III (t(26)=3.475, p<0.05) results were significant differences in favor of Laban group in all three sections. No significant difference was seen between Laban and Traditional method for the learning of Section IV (t (26)=1.645, p>0.05)."
