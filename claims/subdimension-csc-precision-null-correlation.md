Pearson correlation analysis of sub-dimensions of the CTSS and PPCPS among the same 304 teacher candidates. The article reports the CSC–PA correlation as non-significant (r = 0.050, p > 0.01) and the strongest correlation as Learning Competency with Importance, Relevance and Clarity (r = 0.659, p < 0.01).
