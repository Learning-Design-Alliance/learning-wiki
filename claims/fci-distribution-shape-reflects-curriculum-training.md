# Consistent national curriculum training produces a narrow, high-peaked FCI score distribution in China while the flexible US system produces a broad distribution from the chance level upward

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The FCI score distributions differ in shape: US students show a rather flat distribution starting from the chance level, while Chinese students show a narrow distribution peaking near 90%. [→ Lei Bao 2008](#lei-bao-2008)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the FCI score distributions plotted in Figure 1 for combined Chinese data versus US data: the Chinese five-year nationally required curriculum yields a narrow distribution peaking near 90%.

> "This type of consistent training produces a narrow distribution that peaks at near the 90% score."
