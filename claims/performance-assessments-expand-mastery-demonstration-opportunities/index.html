Jenna Howard Terrell 2023¶

Jenna Howard Terrell, Sarah Barzee. (2023). Graduation Requirements and Measures: A Review of Performance Assessment Implementation in Select States for the New York State Education Department. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633310

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case study of seven states based on interviews and focus groups with state education agency staff or consortium leadership. The report states performance-based assessments "are used to provide more opportunities for students to demonstrate mastery of skills and content."

"Despite the case study states taking different approaches to implementing performance-based assessments, a common theme shared by the states was that performance-based assessments are used to provide more opportunities for students to demonstrate mastery of skills and content."
