The editorial reports the Tissenbaum, Berland and Lyons museum study's finding that BSP and BSA were identified as "critical mechanisms for productive divergent collaboration" among visitors at a loosely-coupled digital tabletop exhibit.

"The authors identified opportunities for boundary spanning perceptions (BSP)—observing what goes on in others'spaces, and boundary spanning actions (BSA)— interacting in others' spaces, both as critical mechanisms for productive divergent collaboration."
