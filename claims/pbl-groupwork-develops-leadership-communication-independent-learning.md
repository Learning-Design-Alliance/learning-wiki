## Evidence

### Dawson 2024

Dawson, H., Davis, G., Ross, K., Miller, M. V., & Tomlinson, A. (2024). Using staged teaching and assessment approaches to facilitate inter-university collaboration and problem-based learning. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1334729

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Deductive thematic analysis of student feedback justifying peer assessment marks and module evaluation data, covering six years of data from 2012 to 2020. Students widely identified development of "leadership, communication and independent learning skills"; no effect sizes were reported.

> "Improvement of self and the development of skills like leadership, communication and independent learning skills are widely identified by students in their evaluation feedback, there is a recognition that participation in the process can be of benefit even though it may not be comfortable."
