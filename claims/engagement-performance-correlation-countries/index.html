The article reports, as prior work rather than its own analysis, that previous research found a "meaningful correlation between the mean engagement and mean performance of countries." This background motivates the study's examination of school-level scores.

"This has led to questions about the validity of aggregated scores from large-scale international assessments such as PISA and TIMSS, as previous research has found a meaningful correlation between the mean engagement and mean performance of countries."
