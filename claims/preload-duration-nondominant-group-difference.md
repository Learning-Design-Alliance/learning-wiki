## Evidence

### Causby Ryan S 2016

Causby Ryan S, McDonnell Michelle N, Reed Lloyd, Hillier Susan L. (2016). A randomised controlled trial of sensory awareness training and additional motor practice for learning scalpel skills in podiatry students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0817-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT's mixed model analysis, Preload Duration (non-dominant hand, Log10 transformed) was the only significant group x time outcome (F2,41 = 3.280, p = 0.038). The control group improved (decreased mean time) while both intervention groups deteriorated slightly.

> "The main reason for this significant outcome was due to a larger improvement (decrease in mean time) displayed by the control group, relative to a smaller deterioration (increase in mean time) for both the sensory awareness and motor groups’ performance."
