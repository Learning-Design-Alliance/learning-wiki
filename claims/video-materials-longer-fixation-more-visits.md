## Evidence

### Li Yawen 2026

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA (N=55) shows significant modality effects on eye-tracking indices in all three modules, e.g. "F = 15.810, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.407" for total fixation duration; video-based materials resulted in higher total fixation duration and number of visits.

> "total fixation duration (F = 11.127, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.326; F = 5.090, P = 0.010, ηp2 = 0.181; F = 15.810, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.407), and number of visits (F = 14.116, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.380; F = 7.384, P = 0.002, ηp2 = 0.243; F = 10.316, P < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.310). Text materials led to higher NASA-TLX scores, while video-based materials resulted in higher total fixation duration and number of visits"
