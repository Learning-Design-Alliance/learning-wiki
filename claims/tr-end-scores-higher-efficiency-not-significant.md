In the same RCT, the Results section reports end-of-study TR scores significantly higher for Group B (P < 0.001), with logarithmic fits yA = 64.775ln(x) + 272.2 and yB = 75.272ln(x) + 364.48.

> "At the end of the study, the scores of Group B were significantly higher than those of Group A (P < 0.001). The scoring curve for each group was simulated using a logarithmic function as follows: yA = 64.775ln(x) + 272.2 (R2 = 0.962); yB = 75.272ln(x) + 364.48 (R2 = 0.945)."
