### Kosmas 2025

Kosmas, P., Nisiforou, E. A., Kounnapi, E., Sophocleous, S., & Theophanous, G. (2025). Integrating artificial intelligence in literacy lessons for elementary classrooms: a co-design approach. Education Tech Research Dev (2025) 73:2589–2615. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10492-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus-group analysis of the 25 participating teachers. The article reports that "most teachers (76%, 19 out of a total of 25)" named accommodating all students' needs as the biggest challenge; 64% (16) also cited preparation, logistics, and classroom characteristics, and 40% (10) said workshop discussion time was insufficient.

> "most teachers (76%, 19 out of a total of 25) discussed that the biggest challenge in this kind of workshop is to find a way to co-create a lesson that accommodates all students’ needs and interests"
