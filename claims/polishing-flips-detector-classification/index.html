Victor Angelier 2026¶

Victor Angelier. (2026). The pitfalls of AI detection in academic writing: bias, false positives, and the need for inclusive assessment. AI and Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-026-01376-w

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Wang et al.'s experiment on JAAD International research letters, where an intervention designed to preserve "original meaning and intent" flipped classifications from human to AI-generated. The authors reported residual meaning changes in two to three sentences per letter among ten highly flagged letters.

"Before polishing, 97–100% of the human-authored originals were classified as human; after polishing, 75–85% of the versions were classified as AI-generated, including 15–25% at high confidence."
