### Roche Adam F. 2023

Roche Adam F., Condron Claire M., Eppich Walter J., O'Connor Paul E. (2023). A Mixed Methods Study Identifying the Competencies of Health Care Simulation Technicians. Simulation in Healthcare 18(5). https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000682

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semistructured interviews with 30 simulationists in three groups (HSTs, educators, managers/directors), analyzed deductively using the job-description KSA framework. Table 1 shows general clinical knowledge in 22 (73%) of interviews versus 2 (11%) of job descriptions, and instructional skills in 16 (53%) of interviews versus 0 (0%) of job descriptions.

> "Thirty interviews were conducted with simulationists based in the Republic of Ireland (n = 11), US (n = 10), UK (n = 6), Denmark (n = 1), Turkey (n = 1), and Mexico (n = 1)."
