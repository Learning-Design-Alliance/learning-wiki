## Evidence

### Ahangama 2026

Ahangama. (2026). Designing assessments in the generative AI era: A tailored assessment framework for ICT tertiary education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00582-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from the thematic analysis of institutional and scholarly documents, reported under the Values and Dispositions theme. The review of sources found detection tools misclassify non-native English speakers' writing and that early reliance on tools such as Turnitin proved problematic due to false positives, bias, and inconsistent accuracy.

> "Non-native English speakers are particularly disadvantaged when AI-detection tools misclassify their writing (S#28), and some students respond by running their work through multiple paraphrasing tools to avoid false positives (S#19)."
