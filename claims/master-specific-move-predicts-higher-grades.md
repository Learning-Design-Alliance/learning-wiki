## Evidence

### de Boer 2024

de Boer, Van Rijnsoever, de Bresser. (2024). Dear admission committee…: Which moves in application essays predict student master grades? PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0304394

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression (model 2 and 4, Table 4) on 820 students who completed the master, with move probabilities as predictors and controls for essay length, bachelor grade, sex, age, international background and program. The article reports a "significant positive effect" with coefficients 0.453** and 0.415*.

> "First, the move ‘master specific’ has a significant positive effect on the average grade and first year grade."
