## Evidence

### Lintschinger Josef Michael et al 2026

Lintschinger Josef Michael et al. (2026). A serious video game for teaching basic point-of-care echocardiography skills: a non-inferiority trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08995-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Randomized controlled non-inferiority trial with 97 analyzed medical students randomized after a two-hour workshop; primary outcome was change in total modified OSAUS score. The "statistically significant non-inferiority as hypothesized (p < 0.001)" was shown; the difference in means was 0.67 with a lower one-sided 95% confidence limit of -0.56 excluding the -2 margin.

> "The mean difference in OSAUS scores (Table 2) from baseline to final assessment was − 1.24 ± 3.72 for the control and − 0.57 ± 3.51 for the intervention group, demonstrating the statistically significant non-inferiority as hypothesized (p < 0.001)."
