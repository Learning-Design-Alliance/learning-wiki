Results of the regression model comparison (Table 3) on the same 922-student dataset. Ensemble (Soft Voting) led all tested regression algorithms; its R² of 0.3832 is described by the authors as explaining approximately 38% of the variance in the target variable.

> "Ensemble (Soft Voting) showed the least MAE and RMSE values and the highest R2 value among all tested regression algorithms, indicating that Ensemble (Soft Voting) on average had an absolute difference between its predictions and the actual values of 0.3383 units"
