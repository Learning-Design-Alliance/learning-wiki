## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Math and science educators consistently rank the same five skills highest for science literacy and educated citizenship, and all five are assessed in the QuaRCS. [→ Follette 2015](#follette-2015)

## Evidence

### Follette 2015

Follette, Katherine B.; McCarthy, Donald W.; Dokter, Erin; Buxner, Sanlyn; and Prather, Edward (2015). The Quantitative Reasoning for College Science (QuaRCS) Assessment, 1: Development and Validation. Numeracy: Vol. 8: Iss. 2, Article 2. https://doi.org/10.5038/1936-4660.8.2.2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Online survey of 48 University-level Astronomy (N=19) and Mathematics/Numeracy (N=29) educators, pilot-tested in paper form with N=34 and N=42 at two workshops. Respondents ranked "the most highly and consistently ranked by both math and science educators" skills, and those five are assessed in the QuaRCS.

> "There are five skills in the list that stand out as being the most highly and consistently ranked by both math and science educators for science literacy and for educated citizenship."
