## Subclaims
`q2 i3` BMI was negatively correlated with science GPA (r = -0.573, p<0.001) and mathematics GPA (r = -0.387, p≤0.05) in 36 young handball players. [→ Hermassi Souhail 2021](#hermassi-souhail-2021)

## Evidence

### Hermassi Souhail 2021

Hermassi Souhail, Chelly Mohamed Souhaiel, Michalsik Lars Bojsen, Sanal Nilihan E. M., D. Hayes Lawrence, Cadenas-Sanchez Cristina. (2021). Relationship between fatness, physical fitness, and academic performance in normal weight and overweight schoolchild handball players in Qatar State. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246476

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlation analysis of 36 schoolchild handball players (age 9±1 years) using Pearson partial correlations with age as covariate; Table 2 prints BMI–science r = -0.573*** and BMI–mathematics r = -0.387*. The authors note "BMI was negatively correlated with science GPA and mathematics GPA".

> "BMI was negatively correlated with science GPA and mathematics GPA (Fig 1, panel A). Fig 1B depicts that %BF was significantly negatively correlated with science GPA and mathematics GPA."
