### Chahal Baldev 2023

Chahal Baldev, Aydin Abdullatif, Ahmed Kamran. (2023). Virtual reality vs. physical models in surgical skills training. An update of the evidence. Current Opinion in Urology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MOU.0000000000001145

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A urological emergency simulation curriculum for military surgeons used low-cost, lightweight physical simulators for dorsal slit, scrotal exploration, orchiectomy and suprapubic catheterisation; "statistically significant improvements in participants’ performances" were reported. No effect size is printed.

> "The use of these simulators alongside a didactic teaching session resulted in statistically significant improvements in participants’ performances."
