## Evidence

### S. Bansal 2025

S. Bansal, M. Yadav, J. Zhou, R. A. Ebert‐Allen, R. M. Klute, W. F. Bond, S. Bhat. (2025). Medical Students' Perception of Automated Note Feedback After Simulated Encounters. The Clinical Teacher 22(6): e70273. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70273

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey data from 37 of 44 MS3 students completing post-activity evaluations after two standardized patient encounters. The retrospective pre-post self-efficacy measure showed a "increasing from 70% (SD = 0.16) to 78.5% (SD = 0.16)" with t = −3.81, p < 0.001.

> "Improvement in average retrospective pre‐post self‐efficacy in note writing with the automated feedback, increasing from 70% (SD = 0.16) to 78.5% (SD = 0.16) (t = −3.81, p < 0.001)."
