Evidence¶

Dumontheil Iroise 2016¶

Dumontheil Iroise, Wolf Laura K., Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2016). Audience effects on the neural correlates of relational reasoning in adolescence. Neuropsychologia. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2016.05.001

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Behavioural ANOVA results from the fMRI study: unlike the neural audience effect, the accuracy cost of the peer audience did not differ between the 19 adolescents and 14 adults; equivalence was not tested. This contrasts with the authors' earlier behavioural study where only adolescents were affected.

"The Audience x Age group interaction (p>0.7) and the three-way interaction between Task, Audience and Age group (p>0.6) were not significant."
