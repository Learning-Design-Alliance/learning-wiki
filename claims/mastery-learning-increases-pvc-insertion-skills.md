## Evidence

### Friederichs Hendrik 2016

Friederichs Hendrik, Brouwer Britta, Marschall Bernhard, Weissenstein Anne. (2016). Mastery learning improves students skills in inserting intravenous access: a pre-post-study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001055](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001055)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post skills assessment of 109 third-year students (after excluding 16 who passed the pre-test and 8 with incomplete records) in a simulation center. The article reports the improvement as "highly significant (P<0.001; effect size Cohens' d 2.79)".

> "Following the educational intervention, 106/109 students (97.2%) passed the test with a mean score (standard deviation) of 20.50 (0.56), whereas the mean score at baseline was 15.56 (2.44)."
