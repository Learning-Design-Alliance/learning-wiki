### Bortolotti Alessandro 2025

Bortolotti Alessandro, Ricci Fabrizio, Cieri Carmelita, Cocco Federica, Martini Chiara, Panunzi Marcello, Rossi Davide, Sorella Anna, Saraullo Silvio, Scordo Davide, Renda Giulia, Gallina Sabina, Palumbo Riccardo. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

After each interpretation, participants completed the NASA Task Load Index. Experts rated their performance higher (8.00 ± 0.0 vs. 7.00 ± 1.21; p = 0.009) and reported lower negative workload (4.5 ± 1.45 vs. 6 ± 0.55; p = 0.041) than residents; heatmap analyses showed workload rose with difficulty for all groups.

> "Experts rated their performance higher than residents across all ECG complexities (mean score 8.00 ± 0.0 vs. 7.00 ± 1.21; p = 0.009). Additionally, experts reported lower perceived negative workload (mean score 4.5 ± 1.45 vs. 6 ± 0.55 for residents; p = 0.041)."
