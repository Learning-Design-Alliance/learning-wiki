# The ANCOVA and CHANGE models can be transformed into one another, so empirical model fit is no criterion for choosing between them

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Köhler 2021

Köhler, C., Hartig, J., & Naumann, A. (2021). Detecting Instruction Effects—Deciding Between Covariance Analytical and Change-Score Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09590-6

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Fit comparison of the two DESI multilevel latent variable models. The article reports identical fit (χ2 = 715.5; df = 226; RMSEA = 0.014; CFI = 0.963; TLI = 0.960) and concludes "the empirical model fit is no criterion for model selection".

> "Both models have the same number of degrees of freedom and an identical global model fit (χ2 = 715.5; df = 226; RMSEA = 0.014; CFI = 0.963; TLI = 0.960). This illustrates that both models can be transformed into another, and the empirical model fit is no criterion for model selection."
