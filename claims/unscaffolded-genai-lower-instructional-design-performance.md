## Subclaims
`q3 i3` After controlling for pretest scores, the control group's adjusted posttest instructional design scores were significantly higher than the experimental group's, with a large effect size. [→ Zhang Jun 2026](#zhang-jun-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang Jun 2026

Zhang Jun, Peng Yuting, Deng Xinyue, Zeng Qin, Wang Kai. (2026). The Impact of Unscaffolded GenAI Use on Pre-Service Teachers’ AI Readiness, Self-Regulated Learning, Critical Thinking, and Instructional Design Performance: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071114

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA on posttest instructional design performance with pretest as covariate, comparing the GenAI-permitted experimental group (adjusted mean 81.87) with the control group (adjusted mean 86.54) in a quasi-experiment with 52 pre-service chemistry teachers. The article reports "F = 8.348, p = 0.006, η2 = 0.146", interpreted as large.

> "The ANCOVA results indicated a significant between-group difference in posttest instructional design performance after controlling for pretest scores, F = 8.348, p = 0.006, η2 = 0.146."
