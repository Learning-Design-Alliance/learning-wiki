Okuyama Ayako, Martowirono Kartinie, Bijnen Bart. (2011). Assessing the patient safety competencies of healthcare professionals: a systematic review. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3203526/

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of relationship-to-other-variables evidence reports that safety competency results "show a high (r=0.65–0.80)" correlation with technical skills, while correlations with final written examination scores were low (r=0.13) or modest (r=0.25). Performance scores increased with training level for 11 of 13 tools examined.
