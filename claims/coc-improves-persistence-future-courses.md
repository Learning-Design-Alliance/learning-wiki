## Evidence

### Hawkins Whitney 2021

Hawkins Whitney, W.; Goddard, K.; Favero, C. (2021). A Cocurricular Program That Encourages Specific Study Skills and Habits Improves Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Undergraduates in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-06-0117

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up of students after Bio101 tracking persistence into Bio102 and Bio201 by CoC participation and predicted preparedness; low-preparedness participants enrolled at much higher rates.

> "Considering students with low preparedness, 87% and 47% of full CoC participants enrolled in Bio102 and Bio201, respectively, compared with 68% and 10% of nonparticipants."

### Hawkins Whitney 2021 (2)

Hawkins Whitney, W.; Goddard, K.; Favero, C. (2021). A Cocurricular Program That Encourages Specific Study Skills and Habits Improves Academic Performance and Retention of First-Year Undergraduates in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-06-0117

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up grade analysis in Bio201 comparing average grades of low- and medium-preparedness CoC participants with nonparticipants; participants earned higher averages.

> "In Bio201, for students with low and medium preparedness, the average grade was a 2.19 and 3.02, respectively, compared with 1.50 and 2.76 for nonparticipants."
