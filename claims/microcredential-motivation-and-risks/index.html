Landscape review, citing Hartnett (2021), describes micro-credentials as an external reward mechanism signaling progress; the report notes extrinsic and intrinsic motivation may affect perceived value. Review synthesis, quality capped at 2.

"Micro-credentials serve not only as recognition of learning and achievement but also as a motivational aid that encourages participation and as an assessment that tracks progress"

q2 · i? · design · r2

The review attributes risk findings to Pitt et al. (2018), noting 80% of participants discussed credibility of metadata and issuing organizations, and separately to Davis & Singh (2015) on student, teacher, and mentor perspectives. Not the present study's own data.

"This study captured the perceived risks of micro-credentials in establishing credibility, possible triviality, and the time, effort, and financial commitment and costs for implementation"
