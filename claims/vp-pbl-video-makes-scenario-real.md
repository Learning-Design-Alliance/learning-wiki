## Evidence

### Luke A Woodham 2015

Luke A Woodham, Rachel H Ellaway, Jonathan Round, Sophie Vaughan, Terry Poulton, Nabil Zary. (2015). Medical Student and Tutor Perceptions of Video Versus Text in an Interactive Online Virtual Patient for Problem-Based Learning: A Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3922

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of student comments; "Video made the scenarios more real" was the largest theme with 70 code-quotations. Students cited visual cues like reactions, facial expressions, and environment details such as IV drips and blood.

> "Many students commented on ways in which they felt the use of video made the scenario more real. The comments reflected a broad range of reasons, but with a common thread that the use of video "brought it to life.""
