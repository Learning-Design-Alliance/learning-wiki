`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected score-band analysis (low <75, medium 75–89, high ≥90) using Fisher's exact test in the cohort comparison. The VSO group had far more high-range comprehensive, operation, and biosafety scores, while "the proportion of high-scoring students decreased from 82.95 to 63.95%" for sterility awareness.

> "Fisher's exact test demonstrated that the proportion of high-range practical comprehensive scores in the test and control groups were 62.79% and 22.73%, respectively, showing a significant difference between groups (P < 0.001)."
