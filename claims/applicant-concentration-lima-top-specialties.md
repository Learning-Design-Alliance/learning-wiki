`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive characteristics of the 28,872 applicants reported in Table 1 of the repeated cross-sectional study. The authors state Lima concentrates more than half of residency spots, which may explain why over half of medical doctors are focused on the capital.

> "Most participants apply for spots in Lima, Peru’s capital (n = 18222, 71.6%), and through the “free modality” (n = 26860, 93.0%)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Specialty application counts reported in the Score per specialty results section of the repeated cross-sectional study of 28,872 applicants. The discussion notes low applicant numbers in Internal Medicine and Family Medicine and a shortage of roughly 1 family doctor per 30,000 inhabitants.

> "The specialties with the most applicants were general surgery (n = 2933, 10.16%), followed by Pediatrics (n = 2406, 8.33%) and Obstetric and Gynecology (n = 2008, 6.95%)."
