## Evidence

### Claussen AH 2018

Claussen AH, Bitsko RH, Holbrook JR, Bloomfield J, Giordano K. (2018). Impact of Tourette Syndrome on School Measures in a Nationally Representative Sample. Journal of Developmental & Behavioral Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1097/DBP.0000000000000550

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted comparison in the pooled National Survey of Children's Health sample (300 children currently with TS vs 129,053 never TS). MEB prevalence was 75% (TS) vs 13% (never TS) and learning/language conditions 57% vs 11%; ADHD and learning disability were the most common co-occurring conditions.

> "Children who currently had TS were more than 4 times as likely to have any of the specified co-occurring conditions compared with children who never had TS (Table 1). Of the children currently with TS, 80% had at least 1 co-occurring condition, compared with 18% of children who never had TS."
