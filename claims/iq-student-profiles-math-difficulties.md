## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wiingreen R 2025

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of raw proportions within four student profiles grouped by IQ (N = 180 who completed all exams). Low grades in both domains decreased from 27% (95% CI 12–41) in the IQ <−1 SD group to 0% in the >+1 SD group, and average/high grades rose from 37% to 84%. Only p-values were printed, with no effect size.

> "The distribution of Profile A and Profile D were the only profiles statistically significantly different within the three groups of IQs (p = 0.003 and p < 0.001, respectively)."
