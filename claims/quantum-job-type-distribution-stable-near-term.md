## Evidence

### Ciaran Hughes 2022

Ciaran Hughes, Doug Finke, Dan-Adrian German, Celia Merzbacher, Patrick M. Vora, H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Assessing the Needs of the Quantum Industry. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1109/te.2022.3153841](https://doi.org/10.1109/te.2022.3153841)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Closed-response survey of 57 quantum-industry companies (of 131 asked) reported in Fig. 1, showing hiring expectations for job roles over two time horizons. The authors note the data "do not say what will be the most common jobs available" because position counts were not asked.

> "An important observation from this graph is that the number of companies who will be hiring in any one job role over the next two years and the next three to ﬁve years is nearly the same. Thus, the distribution in the types of jobs is not predicted to change in the near term."
