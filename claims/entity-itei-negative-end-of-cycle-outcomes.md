# Entity ITEI negatively predicts end-of-secondary-school positive emotions and Portuguese grades, while incremental ITEI unexpectedly shows a positive link to negative emotions

## Evidence

### Ana Costa 2023

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis over three waves (N = 222) showed entity ITEIs in 10th grade negatively affected 12th-grade positive emotions towards school and Portuguese grades; entity was the only ITEI dimension predicting objective achievement indicators.

> "The students’ entity ITEIs (T1) exhibited a generally negative impact on their outcomes in the last year of secondary school, including both positive emotions towards school and achievement in Portuguese (T3)."

### Ana Costa 2023 (2)

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same path model, the authors observed that "incremental ITEIs (T1) exhibited an unexpected positive effect" on 12th-grade negative emotions towards school, a link the authors had not hypothesised.

> "incremental ITEIs (T1) exhibited an unexpected positive effect on students’ negative emotions towards school in the last year of secondary school (T3)."
