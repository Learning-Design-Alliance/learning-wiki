## Evidence

### Tolsgaard MG 2015

Tolsgaard MG, Madsen ME, Ringsted C, Oxlund BS, Oldenburg A, Sorensen JL, Ottesen B, Tabor A. (2015). The effect of dyad versus individual simulation‐based ultrasound training on skills transfer. Medical Education 49: 286–295. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.12624

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-squared comparison of pass rates on the next-day clinical transfer test in the randomised trial, using the criterion-referenced pass/fail level of 50.0%. The article reports "dyads 71.4%, singles 30.0%; χ(1)2 = 4.03, p < 0.05". No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "However, a significantly higher number of dyads than single‐practice students achieved scores above the pre‐specified pass/fail level in the transfer test (dyads 71.4%, singles 30.0%; χ(1)2 = 4.03, p < 0.05)."
