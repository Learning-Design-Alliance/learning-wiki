## Evidence

### Jayatilleke Achini Chinthika 2015

Jayatilleke Achini Chinthika, Yoshikawa Kayoko, Yasuoka Junko, Poudel Krishna C, Fernando Nilani, Jayatilleke Achala Upendra, Jimba Masamine. (2015). Training Sri Lankan public health midwives on intimate partner violence: a pre- and post-intervention study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-1674-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of 408 PHMs' knowledge scores. Table 3 shows domain medians rose: acts of IPV 0.83 to 1.00, health effects 0.50 to 1.00, laws 0.50 to 0.67, each p < 0.001; e.g., correct answers on urinary tract infections rose from 21.8% to 67.6%.

> "after the intervention, there were significant increases (p < 0.001) in the median total scores of PHMs' IPV knowledge (0.62 vs. 0.88)"
