## Evidence

### Jörg 2007

Jörg, T., Davis, B. & Nickmans, G. (2007). Towards a new, complexity science of learning and education. Educational Research Review, 2(2), 145–156. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2007.09.002

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Illustrative argument in Section 4.2: when experienced teachers are asked where a learner's understanding of multiplication comes from, their collective response spans biological predispositions, physical experiences, early formal elaborations, middle-grade applications, and increasingly abstract senior-grade interpretations.

> "In other words, for teachers, the “simple” concept of multiplication is recognized as transphe-nomenal, involving genetic structure, bodily activity, social interaction, and cultural tools. In brief, multiplication is a complex concept."
