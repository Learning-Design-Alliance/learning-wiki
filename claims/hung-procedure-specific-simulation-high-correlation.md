## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In a cross-sectional study of 28 surgeons using a modified dV-Trainer, total GEARS scores for VR renorrhaphy correlated highly with GEARS scores for robotic partial nephrectomy on a live porcine model (r = 0.8, p < 0.0001), as did all GEARS subdomains (r = 0.7–0.9). `q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study (Table 4) of 28 expert and intermediate surgeons assessed on a modified dV-Trainer renorrhaphy task followed by robotic partial nephrectomy on a live porcine model. The article reports a "high correlation" with r = 0.8 (p < 0.0001) for total GEARS and r = 0.7–0.9 for subdomains.

> "Hung and co-workers36 reported a high correlation between the total GEARS scores for the robotic VR simulated task and the total GEARS score of a robotic-assisted partial nephrectomy in a live porcine model (r = 0.8, p < 0.0001), as well as between all GEARS subdomains (r = 0.7–0.9, P < 0.001)"
