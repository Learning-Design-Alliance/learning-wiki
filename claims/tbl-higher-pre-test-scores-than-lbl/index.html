Evidence¶

Xie Zhi-Bo 2025¶

Xie Zhi-Bo, Cheng Xin-Yu, Li Xiao-Yan, Zhang Yi-Fan. (2025). Team based learning pedagogy enhances the education quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07175-x

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 13 studies reporting pre-test scores after team-based preparation. The authors interpret this as indicating students using TBL "could gain more knowledge and had better performance after long-term team-based preparation".

"students with TBL method demonstrated significantly higher pre-test scores compared to those using LBL after team-based preparing lessons (SMD = 0.51, 95%CI 0.11 to 0.92, I2 = 89.3%, random-effects model)"
