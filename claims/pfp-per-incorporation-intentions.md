## Evidence

### Edward Price and Noah Finkelstein 2006

Edward Price and Noah Finkelstein. (2006). Preparing graduate students to be educators. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609003](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609003)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Surveys of UCSD PFP participants from the 2001-2002, 2002-2003, and 2003-2004 cohorts (Table III, 33/38 respondents considering education a substantive career part): 18% agreed education is valued by the research community, 94% planned to incorporate PER results in teaching, 88% viewed PER as legitimate research. Fewer than 40% considered entering education research.

> "While few respondents feel education is valued by the physics research community, most plan on incorporating the results of PER in their own teaching."
