## Evidence

### von Lengerke Thomas 2011

von Lengerke Thomas, Kursch Angelika, Lange Karin, APG-Lehrteam MHH. (2011). The communication skills course for second year medical students at Hannover Medical School: An evaluation study based on students' self-assessments. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 28(4), Doc54. [doi:10.3205/zma000766](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000766)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional T0 survey of 267 course participants using a within-subject ANOVA across learning-needs items (Huynh-Feldt corrected), in which "The highest demand can be found for the “to show how”-items". The article notes the competency self-assessments 'show an inverse pattern compared to the learning needs'.

> "At the same time there were significant differences (F(5.247) 23.4, p<.0001). The highest demand can be found for the “to show how”-items regarding history taking and disclosing diagnoses (both M=4.4 [median=5, not shown here])"
