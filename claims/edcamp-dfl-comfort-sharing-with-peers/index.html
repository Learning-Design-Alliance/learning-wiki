q2 · i? · design · r2

Pre/post survey comparison reported in the Series Impact section. The report prints the percentages directly: "44 percent of respondents reported they agreed or strongly agreed" pre-survey versus 94 percent post-survey for individual variability, and 57 versus 96 percent for motivation. No effect sizes are printed.

"44 percent of respondents reported they agreed or strongly agreed that they were comfortable leveraging their knowledge of individual variability to share with peer educators in the pre-survey, compared to 94 percent in the post-survey. Similarly, in the motivation pre-survey, 57 percent of respondents agreed or strongly agreed with the statement, compared to 96 percent of respondents in the post-survey."
