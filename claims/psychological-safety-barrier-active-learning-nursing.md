# Fear of embarrassment and lack of psychological safety reduce nursing students' participation and retention in active learning activities

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students described feeling tense, withdrawn, or unable to speak when criticized publicly, and avoided asking questions to protect peers. [→ Najdi B. 2026](#najdi-b-2026)

## Evidence

### Najdi B. 2026

Najdi B., Al-Jawabreh A. (2026). Exploring the impact of active learning strategies on learning outcomes and educational experiences in undergraduate nursing education: a qualitative descriptive study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09512-0

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Student focus group data (Theme 3, Subtheme 3.1.3). Participants reported fear of embarrassment as a common barrier, with one student describing confusion, embarrassment, and feeling not good enough that affected retention.

> "When the instructor criticizes me during the presentation, I feel tense the entire time and unable to speak."
