### Jones Lee 2019

Jones Lee, Taylor Deanna J, Sii Freda, Masood Imran, Crabb David P, Shah Peter. (2019). The Only Eye Study (OnES): a qualitative study of surgeon experiences of only eye surgery and recommendations for patient safety. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of audio-recorded, semistructured interviews with 10 ophthalmic surgeons (purposively sampled, all glaucoma surgeons), coded in NVivo with inter-rater kappa of 0.46. The article reports that "Five key themes emerged" spanning consent, risk reduction, training, mentorship and emotional impact.

> "Five key themes emerged relating to surgeons' experiences and perceptions of only eye surgery: (1) differences in approach to consent, (2) strategies for risk reduction, (3) unmet training needs, (4) value of surgical mentor and (5) emotional impact of unsuccessful outcomes."
