Yin David, Cabana François, Tousignant-Laflamme Yannick, Bédard Sonia, Tousignant Michel. (2019). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up recommendations were recorded on the standardized form in the same 86-patient inter-rater study. The article reports "κ = 0.81; 95% CI: 0.69–0.94" and 90.7% raw agreement, with surgeon follow-up needed for 43.0% of patients.

> "Agreement on which physician was most appropriate to assume patient follow-up was almost perfect (κ = 0.81; 95% CI: 0.69–0.94) with a raw percent agreement of 90.7%. Follow-up with an OS was deemed necessary in 43.0% of patients."
