## Evidence

### Brogaard L 2022

Brogaard L, Glerup Lauridsen K, Løfgren B, et al. (2022). The effects of obstetric emergency team training on patient outcome: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14263

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of seven studies reporting brachial plexus injury before and after simulation-based obstetric team training. The cohort pooled estimate showed "low heterogeneity (I 2 = 0%)"; certainty was downgraded for risk of bias and imprecision but upgraded for large magnitude of effect.

> "In one RCT 16 an OR of 1.3 (95% CI 0.39–4.33) was found, whereas a combined OR of 0.47 (95% CI 0.33–0.68) was found in six observational cohort studies 20 , 22 , 23 , 24 , 25 , 26 with low heterogeneity (I 2 = 0%)."
