Discourse analysis of an episode on defining a standing wave's speed, where disagreement over phase versus group velocity builds tension until Chad notices the question asks whether a speed “can” be defined; the group agrees it cannot, relieving tension.

> "Chad has found an escape hatch in the wording of the tutorial, specifically in the use of the word “can” that allowed the group to relieve the tension they were experiencing from their discussion."
