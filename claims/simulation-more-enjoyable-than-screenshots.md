## Evidence

### Kohnle 2014

Kohnle, A., Baily, C., Campbell, A., Korolkova, N., and Paetkau, M. J. (2014). Enhancing student learning of two-level quantum systems with interactive simulations. [doi:10.1119/1.4913786](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4913786)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative classroom study of one entanglement activity, with one group using printed screenshots and the other the interactive simulation in a computer classroom, followed by an enjoyment survey. The article reports "a marked difference in the distributions, with students overall finding it more enjoyable to work with the simulation".

> "One can see a marked difference in the distributions, with students overall finding it more enjoyable to work with the simulation."
