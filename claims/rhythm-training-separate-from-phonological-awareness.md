### Gutiérrez-Palma 2026

Gutiérrez-Palma, N., Calet, N., Robles-Bello, M.A., & Valencia-Naranjo, N. (2026). The role of rhythm in learning to read in children with and without reading difficulties: an intervention study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102273

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' conclusion drawn from the intervention results: the combined group was the only one not to improve in pseudoword reading among struggling readers, while the separately trained groups did.

> "For children with reading difficulties, rhythm training may be useful for improving the reading of novel words, but it should be done separately from phonological awareness."
