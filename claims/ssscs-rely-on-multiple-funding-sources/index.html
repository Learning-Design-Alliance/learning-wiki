McKenna 2023¶

McKenna, J. M., Russo, S., Sutherland, H., Muñoz, E., Boal, A., & Petrosino, A. (2023). Research and evaluation on school safety: The structure, services, and products of state school safety centers. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628907

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Director interview findings on SSSC funding across 29 states. More than half of centers receive some level of state-appropriated funding and federal grants; the report prints that "the majority (65.5%) mentioned having two or three means of funding the centers’ work."

"Although some states (34.5%) only mentioned one source of funding for their center, the majority (65.5%) mentioned having two or three means of funding the centers’ work."
