## Evidence

### Alsamani and Daif-Allah 2016

Abdul-Aziz Saleh Alsamani & Ayman Sabry Daif-Allah. (2016). Introducing Project-based Instruction in the Saudi ESP Classroom: A Study in Qassim University. English Language Teaching, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n1p51

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post experimental-control group design with two intact classes of 43 students each. Post-test means were 29.58 (experimental) vs 12.79 (control), t(84)=15.89, significant at 0.01; no effect size is printed for this contrast, so equivalence was not tested.

> "The table above shows that there are statistically signi ficant differences between the mean scores of the two research groups in post-testing in favor of the experimental group and accordingly proves the validity of the second hypothesis."

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test on the experimental group's pre-post vocabulary scores (means 9.12 and 29.58, N=43). Calculated t exceeded the tabulated value at 42 degrees of freedom, significant at 0.01; no effect size is printed for this contrast.
