## Evidence

### Lufeng Li 2025

Lufeng Li, Das Ghansham, Gopang Aamir Ali, Khuhro Abdul Salam, Memon Shehriyar. (2025). Enhancing innovation in medical students through imaginative driven models. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30293-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fuzzy Delphi evaluation phase with 50 actual respondents; the questionnaire's Cronbach's alpha was 0.982, and expert agreement percentages for the five usability elements ranged from 88.0% to 96.0%, all above the 75% threshold.

> "The third phase of evaluation evidence through defuzzification values ranged from 33.6 to 42.0, confirming the moderating level of experts’ agreement on the usability of the model."

### Lufeng Li 2025 (2)

Lufeng Li, Das Ghansham, Gopang Aamir Ali, Khuhro Abdul Salam, Memon Shehriyar. (2025). Enhancing innovation in medical students through imaginative driven models. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30293-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Table 7 defuzzification results show items BA3 (retention of element implementation over time) at 31.53 and BA4 (few user mistakes) at 31.15, below the 33.6 moderate-agreement floor but above the minimum of 24.

> "In contrast, the (A) values of items BA3 and BA4 are 31.53 and 31.15, respectively, slightly agree because the defuzzification value obtained is 24 to 33.6."
