`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A paired samples t-test was conducted on 11 schools that received Khanya labs between 2004 and mid-2005, comparing 2003 (pre-intervention) and 2007 (post-intervention) results. The test found "no statistically significant change" in the MSS. Eta squared was calculated as 0.084, a very small effect size.

> "There is no statistically significant change in the MSS from before Khanya (mean = 0.955; std deviation = 0.382) to after Khanya (mean = 0.827; std deviation = 0.486), t(10) = 0.958, p = 0.361"
