## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adding qualification year to the regression model explained an additional 22% of variance in knowledge test performance among investigated doctors (B = 0.57, p = 0.001). [→ Mehdizadeh Leila 2015](#mehdizadeh-leila-2015)
`q3 i?` Qualification year remained significantly associated with OSCE performance (B = 2.4, p = 0.02) and communication skills performance (0.34 marks per year) among investigated doctors. [→ Mehdizadeh Leila 2015 (2)](#mehdizadeh-leila-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2015

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Dacre Jane. (2015). Are the General Medical Council’s Tests of Competence fair to long standing doctors? A retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0362-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on 95 investigated doctors (Tables 4 and 6), with volunteer doctors who qualified after 2003 removed for matching. Qualification year added 22% variance to the knowledge test model and increased OSCE variance from 13 to 17%.

> "There was also a significant relationship between qualification year and knowledge test performance; for every one unit increase in qualification year, doctors performed on average 0.6 of a mark better on the knowledge test."

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2015 (2)

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Dacre Jane. (2015). Are the General Medical Council’s Tests of Competence fair to long standing doctors? A retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0362-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression on communication skills station for investigated doctors: each one-unit increase in qualification year raised performance by 0.34 of a mark; OSCE rose 2.4 marks per year (Table 6, PMQ year B = 2.4, p = 0.02).

> "Among the investigated group of doctors only a small proportion of variance in communication skills performance was accounted for by adding year of qualification into the regression model (7%)."
