### Jaya Shivangani Kashyap 2026

Jaya Shivangani Kashyap, Christian D. Schunn, and Chandralekha Singh. (2026). The Gendered Cost of Lower Grades: Women's Physics Perceived Recognition and Identity Suffer Disproportionately If They Earn Less Than A Grade. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.11568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.11568)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation analysis controlling for pre measures found significant grade × gender interactions on post-perceived recognition (Passing C × Gender β = -0.15, p = 0.01; B × Gender β = -0.21, p<0.001), while all identity-path interactions were non-significant; the authors report the grade effect "was twice as large for women".

> "the moderation happened at the first step in the mediation pathway: women were less likely to feel recognized as a physics person when they received a Passing C or a B."
