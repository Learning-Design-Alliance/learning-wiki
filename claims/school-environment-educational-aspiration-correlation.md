## Evidence

### Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi 2025

Podiya Jayalaxmi Kaniyagundi, Navaneetham Janardhana, Bhola Poornima. (2025). Influences of school climate on emotional health and academic achievement of school-going adolescents in India: a systematic review. BMC Public Health 25, 54. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-21268-0

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional survey (Bashir & Kaur 2017, N = 400, Kashmir, grade 9) using the Level of Educational Aspiration test and School Environment Inventory; the review reports "a positive correlation (r = .346) among all students" and a t value of 5.77 at the 0.01 level for rural and urban students.

> "Study result finds a strong association between the school climate and the educational aspirations of students, which motivate them to participate in school activities such as seminars workshops, and debates. This has positively influenced both rural and urban background students (t value of 5.77, 0.01 level of significance), with a positive correlation (r = .346) among all students"
