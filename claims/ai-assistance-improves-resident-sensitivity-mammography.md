### Goh SSN 2025

Goh SSN, Du H, Tan LY, Seah EZY, Lau WK, Ng AHZ, Lim SWD, Ong HY, Lau S, Tan YL, Khaw Ms, Yap CW, Hui KYD, Tan WC, Abdul Haziz SRB, Khoo VMH, Ge S, Pool FJ, Choo YS, Wang Y, Jagmohan P, Gopinathan PP, Hartman M, Feng M. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/66931

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-reader multi-case reader study: 17 radiologists read 500 mammograms (250 malignant) without and then with AI assistance after a 1-month washout. The results report that reading with AI assistance "improved the sensitivity" of junior (4.7%) and senior (8.7%) residents, both P<.001.

> "Reading with AI assistance improved the sensitivity of junior resident radiologists from 56.9% to 61.6% (difference 4.7%, 95% CI difference [1.9, 7.1% ], P<.001) and senior resident radiologists from 55.4% to 64.1% (difference 8.7 %, 95% CI difference (4.5%, 13%) P<.001)"
