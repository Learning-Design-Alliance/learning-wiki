Somya Swarnkar, Rittick Roy, Tejinder Kaur, and David Blair. (2023). On the effectiveness of the early introduction of modern physics in school curriculum: the case of the structure of atom versus wave-particle duality. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.16525](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.16525)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of focus-group interviews and questionnaire responses from 22 students; the authors count "14 out of 22 students" giving classical-model responses and interpret this as supporting the constructivist hypothesis about early-years learning.

> "But despite the recurring nature of the concept, the fact that 14 out of 22 students re- sponded with either the classical Rutherford’s model or Bohr’s model supports the constructivist hypothesis in the early years of a student’s career."
