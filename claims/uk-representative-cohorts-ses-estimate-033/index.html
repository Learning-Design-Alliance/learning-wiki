Evidence¶

Sophie von Stumm 2022¶

Sophie von Stumm, Sophie Nicole Cave and Paul Wakeling. (2022). Persistent association between family socioeconomic status and primary school performance in Britain over 95 years. npj Science of Learning (2022) 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00120-3

q2 · i2 · associational · r3

Supplementary analysis of the 5 cohorts designed to be representative of the wider UK population at inception (overall N = 50,306); the small number of cohorts left the adjusted model with no degrees of freedom. The printed unadjusted correlation effect size was 0.33 [0.22–0.43].

"An unadjusted model produced an estimate of 0.33 [0.22 –0.43], while a model that included all cohort-specific confounders was nonidentified (i.e., no degrees of freedom, because of the small number of UK population-representative cohorts)."
