Related Claims¶

  • Calmness against unexploitable opponents rises when they are played in the context of exploitable, but not exploiting, opponents — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Loss-restlessness is not observed even in structured environments, though restlessness is generally higher against exploiting opponents — related
  • Against unexploitable opponents, neither win-calmness nor loss-restlessness appears; calmness is instead lower after wins and restlessness lower after losses — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Win-calmness appears only against exploitable opponents; exploiting opponents instead reproduce the reversed pattern — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Only against exploitable opponents does individual win rate correlate with win-calmness (positively) and loss-restlessness (negatively) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
