## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There were no significant differences in ratings for the six evaluation questions between health care professionals and students and the general public. [→ Sarvestan Javad 2025](#sarvestan-javad-2025)

## Evidence

### Sarvestan Javad 2025

Sarvestan Javad, Thananithichot Stithorn, Miyosawa Yukihide, Hirabayashi Koichi, Yamada Kodai, Kobayashi Fumiya, Ogihara Nanami, Takeda Noa, Okamura Eri, Matsumura Shogo. (2025). Development of a Serious Game to Simulate Neonatal Intensive Care Unit Experiences: Collaborative Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/73009

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group comparison of survey ratings using unpaired t tests for continuous variables and Fisher exact tests for categorical variables, comparing 74 health care professionals and students with 86 general public respondents. No significant rating differences were found; equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no differences in the ratings for the 6 questions between the 2 groups (Figure 3)."

### Sarvestan Javad 2025 (2)

Sarvestan Javad, Thananithichot Stithorn, Miyosawa Yukihide, Hirabayashi Koichi, Yamada Kodai, Kobayashi Fumiya, Ogihara Nanami, Takeda Noa, Okamura Eri, Matsumura Shogo. (2025). Development of a Serious Game to Simulate Neonatal Intensive Care Unit Experiences: Collaborative Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/73009

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same between-group comparison, categorical playtime variable tested with Fisher exact test; Table 2 prints P=.03 for playtime. The two named groups differ on playtime but not on age or gender.

> "While there were no differences in age or gender, playtime was significantly shorter among health care professionals and students than among the general public (Table 2)."
