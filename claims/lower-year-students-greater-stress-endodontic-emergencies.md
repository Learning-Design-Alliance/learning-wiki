## Evidence

### Chuy Virginie 2025

Chuy Virginie, Matyjasik Pierre, Devillard Raphaël, Kérourédan Olivia. (2025). Stress and Confidence of Undergraduate Dental Students With Different Levels of Clinical Experience in Managing Endodontic Emergencies: A Cross‐Sectional Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13072

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable ordinal logistic regression on 227 students (Table 2) examined gender, year, and site simultaneously; fourth vs. sixth year OR = 2.01 [1.04; 3.91] and fifth vs. sixth OR = 2.36 [1.23; 4.57], p = 0.026 for year of study. Students "were approximately 2–2.4 times more likely to be in a higher stress category than sixth‐year students."

> "Women were 4.4 times more likely to be in a higher stress category than men, and fourth‐ and fifth‐year students were approximately 2–2.4 times more likely to be in a higher stress category than sixth‐year students."
