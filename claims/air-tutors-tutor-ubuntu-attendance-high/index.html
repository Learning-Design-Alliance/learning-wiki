Fong 2025¶

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of tutor attendance data collected from Air Tutors for Ubuntu professional learning sessions, using full attendance (all three sessions) as the threshold. In District A, 117 of 143 tutors (82 percent) met the full attendance threshold; the report separately states 94 percent (168 of 178) of District B tutors did so.

"Thus, 82 percent (117 out of 143) of the tutors in District A met the full attendance threshold."
