## Evidence

### Rezaei Behrooz 2020

Rezaei Behrooz, Falahati Juliana, Beheshtizadeh Raziyeh. (2020). Stress, stressors and related factors in clinical learning of midwifery students in Iran: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1970-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey analysis comparing mean PQSS domain scores (Likert 1–4) across 70 midwifery students; the omnibus test across domains was significant (p = 0.001, f = 11.92). No effect size is printed.

> "The dimensions of “unpleasant feelings” (2.46 ± 0.88) and “humiliating experiences” (2.11 ± 0.74) gained the highest scores. There was a significant difference between the scores of domains of the PQSS (p = 0.001 f = 11.92)."
