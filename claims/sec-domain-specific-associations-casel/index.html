Evidence¶

Nguyen 2026¶

Nguyen, T.-A., Hidajat, T., Zhou, T., Ma, T., Hoang, A., Poed, S., & Edwards, E. J. (2026). Relations between Teachers' and Students' Social-emotional Competence: A Systematic Review and Multilevel Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10218-4

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level mixed-effects models (M2, M3) examined teacher SEC domains and student SEC domains separately with omnibus F tests and follow-up pairwise tests. The article reports the domain pattern quoted, but notes substantial heterogeneity and uneven numbers of effect sizes across domains.

"teachers' responsi-ble decision-making showed the largest observed relationship with overall students' SEC, while students' relationship skills showed the strongest observed association with overall teachers' SEC."
