## Evidence

### Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace 2005

Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace. (2005). Probing physics students' conceptual knowledge structures through term association. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Within-subject comparison of PPTE binnings across sessions C, D, and J for two work-energy prompt problems: for C1/D6/J3, 1 of 16 positive at C, 7 of 16 at D, 6 of 16 at J; for C4/D2/J5, 3 of 16 at C, 6 of 16 at D, 6 of 16 at J. The article calls the evidence "suggestive evidence that the PPTE task is sensitive to course-induced learning."

> "When such exposure occurred, positive responses increased noticeably over one week, and remained higher six weeks later."
