## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The atlas's overlay mode allows students to compare thematic layers (e.g., tectonics and minerals, hydrography and freezing rivers, physical and soil/vegetation maps) to identify relationships, which the authors state is difficult with conventional paper maps. Electronic School Atlas as an Innovative Means of Development of Geographic Education in Kazakhstan Schools. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(18), 12668-12679. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1124623

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' analysis of the atlas's overlay function, illustrated in Figures 3-6 with tectonic/mineral, hydrography/freezing-rivers, physical/soil, and physical/vegetation map overlays of Kazakhstan. The claim reflects the authors' interpretation rather than a tested comparison.

> "Such tasks are difficult to perform with conventional cards, because they are based on the superposition of several card transactions mentally. Electronic training atlas allows us to solve this problem quickly and helps the student to carry out a dual analysis that develops the skills of scientific work."
