### Rogat 2026

Rogat, T. K., Traynor, A., Hmelo-Silver, C. E., & Cheng, B. H. (2026). Situating collaborative disciplinary activity: creating a space for high-quality group engagement. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09772-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Time series regression of engagement ratings across 2.5-minute video intervals (Research Question 2). Table 4 prints CE(t-1)→MEt = 0.19 and ME(t-1)→CEt = 0.13, coefficients interpreted as partial correlations on the within-group standardized metric; no standardized effect size beyond these coefficients is printed.

> "In Model 2, there also appeared to be a temporal pattern in which CEt−1 was followed by increased MEt in the next time segment, and the reciprocal relation was also observed."
