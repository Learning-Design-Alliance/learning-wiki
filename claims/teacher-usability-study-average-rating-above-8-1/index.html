Teacher usability study in which 12 U.S. STEM teachers (6 male, 6 female) each created three simulation requests, for 36 requests total. Ratings were on a usability scale where "an average rating of above 8.1, corresponding to good usability" was achieved.

"Overall reception was quite positive with teachers rating all simulations as 7 or above in terms of usability with an average rating of above 8.1, corresponding to good usability"
