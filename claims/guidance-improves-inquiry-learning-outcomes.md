# Guidance during inquiry learning improves post-inquiry learning outcomes (d = 0.50)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

`q2 i2` The review reports, citing D'Angelo et al. (2014), that supplementing a simulation with learner guidance had a modest effect on learning outcomes (d = 0.49) and inquiry skills (d = 0.41), independent of guidance type. [→ Lazonder 2016](#lazonder-2016)

## Evidence

### Lazonder 2016

Lazonder, A. W., & Harmsen, R. (2016). Meta-analysis of inquiry-based learning: Effects of guidance. Review of Educational Research, 86(3), 681-718. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315627366

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of studies assessing learning outcomes, measured through "posttests, criterion tasks, interviews, or questionnaires" administered immediately or shortly after learning; effect sizes were based on gain scores where possible. The summary effect was d = 0.50.

> "Results showed facilitative overall effects of guidance on learning activities (d = 0.66, 95% CI [0.44, 0.88]), performance success (d = 0.71, 95% CI [0.52, 0.90]), and learning outcomes (d = 0.50, 95% CI [0.37, 0.62])."
