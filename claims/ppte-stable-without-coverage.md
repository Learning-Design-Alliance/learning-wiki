# Without intervening course coverage, PPTE responses to the same problem are stable across consecutive weeks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2005 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person · **overdue for a recheck since 2008**: tool and model accuracy changes quickly

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the absence of course coverage, the likelihood of a solution-relevant PPTE response was approximately the same across two consecutive weeks; at most one subject of sixteen changed categories in each control comparison. [→ Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace 2005](#ian-d-beatty-and-william-j-gerace-2005)

## Evidence

### Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace 2005

Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace. (2005). Probing physics students' conceptual knowledge structures through term association. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Control comparison of PPTE prompts I2/J4 and I5/J1, separated by one week with no relevant course coverage: for I2/J4, thirteen subjects positive both times, two negative both times, one changed; for I5/J1, twelve positive both times, three negative both times, one changed (a marginal case).

> "the likelihood that students will respond with a PPTE term relevant to the prompt problem's solution is approximately the same when the task is given in two consecutive weeks: no coverage, no change."
