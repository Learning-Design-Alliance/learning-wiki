Macdonald Kirstin, Milne Nikki, Orr Robin, Pope Rodney. (2018). Relationships between Motor Proficiency and Academic Performance in Mathematics and Reading in School-Aged Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15081603

`q3 · i1` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of six observational studies of total fine motor scores and reading. One study reported "significant very weak to weak positive correlations (r = 0.18–0.23)" between manual dexterity subtest scores and phonemic awareness and word reading; three ECLS-K studies found fine motor skills a very strong and consistent predictor of later reading achievement.

> "Overall, summary coding suggests there was a strong level of evidence to support a significant very weak-to-weak positive association between total fine motor scores and reading performance (see Table 2)."
