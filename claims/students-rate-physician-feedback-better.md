### Krautter Markus 2017

Krautter Markus, Diefenbacher Katja, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Koehl-Hackert Nadja, Herzog Wolfgang, Nikendei Christoph. (2017). Physical examination skills training: Faculty staff vs. patient instructor feedback—A controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0180308

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student questionnaire ratings (12 items, Likert 1–6) in the same RCT; overall means 1.30 (physicians) vs 1.66 (PIs), p = .034. No significant difference on five items as printed.

> "Concerning overall ratings by students, physician feedback was significantly better rated than PI feedback (p = .034). There was no statistically significant difference concerning five items (see Table 3)."
