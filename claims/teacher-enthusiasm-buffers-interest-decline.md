# Teacher-reported enthusiasm in Grade 5 buffers the decline in class-level mathematics interest but is unrelated to change in perceived teacher support

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Teacher-reported enthusiasm positively predicts the change in class-level mathematics interest (β = .41, p = .04). [→ Lazarides 2018](#lazarides-2018)
`q3 i?` Teacher-reported enthusiasm does not significantly predict change in class-level perceived teacher support (β = .25, p = .19). [→ Lazarides 2018 (2)](#lazarides-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Lazarides 2018

Lazarides, R., Gaspard, H., & Dicke, A.-L. (2018). Dynamics of classroom motivation: Teacher enthusiasm and the development of math interest and teacher support. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.012

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel latent change Model 2 (47 classrooms) with Grade 5 teacher-reported enthusiasm predicting change; the printed standardized coefficient is β = .41, SE = .20, p = .04, described as "significantly and positively related".

> "teacher-reported enthusiasm was significantly and positively related to the average change in students' mathematics interest at the classroom level (β = .41, SE = .20, p = .04)."

### Lazarides 2018 (2)

Lazarides, R., Gaspard, H., & Dicke, A.-L. (2018). Dynamics of classroom motivation: Teacher enthusiasm and the development of math interest and teacher support. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.012

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same Model 2 analysis: the path from teacher-reported enthusiasm to change in class-level perceived teacher support was non-significant (β = .25, SE = .19, p = .19); no equivalence test was reported.

> "Contrary to Hypothesis 2b, teacher-reported enthusiasm was not significantly related to the average change in student-perceived teacher support (β = .25, SE = .19, p = .19)."
