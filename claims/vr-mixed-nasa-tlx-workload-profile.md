### Hing Lie 2023

Hing Lie, Kachina Studer, Zhen Zhao, Ben Thomson, Dishita G Turakhia, John Liu. (2023). Training for Open-Ended Drilling through a Virtual Reality Simulation. [doi:10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

NASA Task Load Index administered after the physical drilling task to both groups, analyzed with t-tests and Mann-Whitney-U tests. The discussion hypothesizes the increased demand may partly stem from the cognitive load of transferring VR-learned skills to the real world, and from greater exposure to task complexity during open-ended practice.

> "While not statistically significant, the VR group reported less time pressure for the drilling task. However, they reported exerting more mental acuity, physical activity, an d effort."
