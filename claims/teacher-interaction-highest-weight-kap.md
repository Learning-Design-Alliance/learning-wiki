Velásquez-Rojas F, Fajardo JE, Zacharias D, Laguna MF. (2022). Effects of the COVID-19 pandemic in higher education: a particular case from the perspective of complex systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0275352

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

SLP ANN fitting of KA model coefficients (Table 5, Fig. 5) across both contexts and all groups. The article reports "greater prominence in the term associated with the interaction with teachers"; MLR confirmed this with βT = 4.6893 (p < 2e-16), the largest coefficient in Table 6.

> "The values obtained indicate a generality in the weights of each factor that participates in the knowledge acquisition process, with greater prominence in the term associated with the interaction with teachers (βT X) for all groups."
