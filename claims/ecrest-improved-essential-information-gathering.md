## Evidence

### Plackett R 2020

Plackett R, Kassianos AP, Kambouri M, et al. (2020). Online patient simulation training to improve clinical reasoning: a feasibility randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02168-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of the observed clinical reasoning measure in the feasibility RCT (n = 148 complete cases): intervention 61.6% (SD 17.6) vs control 53.3% (SD 15.8), "The intervention group identified significantly more essential information than the control group (62% vs 53%)." Odds ratio 1.40 (95% CI 1.12–1.75), p = 0.003.
