Kerr Brendan, Hawkins Trisha Lee-Ann, Herman Robert, Barnes Sue, Kaufmann Stephanie, Fraser Kristin, Ma Irene W. Y. (2013). Feasibility of scenario-based simulation training versus traditional workshops in continuing medical education: a randomized controlled trial. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the pilot RCT: three 1-h workshops ran successfully within the 2011 Rocky Mountain/ACP meeting, and the study "indicates that simulation-based education can be feasible". Satisfaction data supported receptivity, though differences were not significant.

> "Our study indicates that simulation-based education can be feasible, and may result in similar learning outcomes."
