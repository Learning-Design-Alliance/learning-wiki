## Evidence

### Beattie Kirsty L. 2022

Beattie Kirsty L., Hill Andrew, Horswill Mark S., Grove Philip M., Stevenson Andrew R. L. (2022). Aptitude and attitude: predictors of performance during and after basic laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-021-08668-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression on follow-up performance (FLS Peg Transfer and Precision Cutting, 2D viewing) for the 23 participants who completed the in-lab follow-up. As manual dexterity (assembly) improved, follow-up score significantly decreased (improved). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The final model was statistically significant, F(1, 21) = 16.50, p = 0.001, with manual dexterity (assembly) uniquely explaining 41.3% of the variance in total follow-up score, RAdj2=0.413 (medium-to-large effect)."
