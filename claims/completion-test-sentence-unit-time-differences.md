Timed laboratory administration of the completion test to 25 students or faculty at the State University of Iowa yielded per-unit time percentages (unit 1: 12.8%; unit 11: 3.4%), showing "the average of the times of the twenty-five subjects" differed markedly across units. Mean variations were large, the authors noting the need for more observers.
