# Conditionalized on initial retrieval success, testing beats restudy at 24 hours, but a second test adds no reliable benefit

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Phase 3 accuracy was better for pairs that followed a correctly recalled test than for pairs that followed restudy (d = 1.42). [→ Liu 2016](#liu-2016)
`q3 i3` At Phase 4, accuracy showed a large effect of learning condition (ηp2=0.60), with items recalled correctly at least once outperforming restudied-only items (d = 1.47 and d = 1.75). [→ Liu 2016 (2)](#liu-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Liu 2016

Liu, X. L., & Reder, L. M. (2016). fMRI exploration of pedagogical benefits of repeated testing: when more is not always better. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.476

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Conditional analysis including only trials with a prior correct test, same within-subject behavioral experiment (n = 20). "Recall accuracy in Phase 3 was significantly better for trials that followed a previous correctly recalled test (STT-T condition) than for trials that followed a restudy" (d = 1.42).

> "Recall accuracy in Phase 3 was significantly better for trials that followed a previous correctly recalled test (STT-T condition) than for trials that followed a restudy (SST-T condition), t 19 = 5.48, P < 0.001, d = 1.42."

### Liu 2016 (2)

Liu, X. L., & Reder, L. M. (2016). fMRI exploration of pedagogical benefits of repeated testing: when more is not always better. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.476

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Phase 4 accuracy conditionalized on prior correct recall in the same experiment. The article reports "a significant effect of type of learning condition, F 2,38 = 27.88, P < 0.001, ηp2=0.60", with items recalled correctly at least once outperforming restudied-only items (d = 1.47; d = 1.75).

> "Recall accuracy in Phase 4 (final assessment on Day 2) also showed a significant effect of type of learning condition, F 2,38 = 27.88, P < 0.001, ηp2=0.60, such that performance was better for items that had been recalled correctly at least once (STT-T or SST-T condition) compared with items that had only been restudied (SSS-T condition), t 19 = 5.77, P < 0.001, d = 1.47; t 19 = 6.07, P < 0.001, d = 1.75."

### Liu 2016 (3)

Liu, X. L., & Reder, L. M. (2016). fMRI exploration of pedagogical benefits of repeated testing: when more is not always better. Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.476

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pairwise comparison at Phase 4 between the two tested conditions in the same conditionalized analysis. "The difference between subsequent accuracy at Phase 4 when prior learning opportunities involved two tests (STT-T condition) compared with when prior learning involved one restudy and one test (SST-T condition) was not reliable, P = 0.07."

> "The difference between subsequent accuracy at Phase 4 when prior learning opportunities involved two tests (STT-T condition) compared with when prior learning involved one restudy and one test (SST-T condition) was not reliable, P = 0.07."
