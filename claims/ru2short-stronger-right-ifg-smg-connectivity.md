### Li 2018

Li, M., Malins, J. G., DeMille, M. M. C., Lovett, M. W., Truong, D. T., Epstein, K., Lacadie, C., Mehta, C., Bosson-Heenan, J., Gruen, J. R., Frijters, J. C. and the GRaD Study Consortium. (2018). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of READ1 genotype on connectivity strength for four network nodes in the 58-child fMRI subset, with sex and/or SES as covariates from backward selection. Table 5 reports the right SMG effect (B = .077, SE = .037, p = .042).

> "That is, functional connectivity between the right insula/IFG and the right SMG was stronger in subjects with RU2Short alleles than it was in subjects without RU2Short alleles."
