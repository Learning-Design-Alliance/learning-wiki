## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Response patterns about what makes the active-learning course easy differ between 100- and 300-level students, driven by more 300-level students citing logical content and clear alignment. [→ Wyse 2018](#wyse-2018)

## Evidence

### Wyse 2018

Wyse, S. A., & Soneral, P. A. G. (2018). “Is This Class Hard?” Defining and Analyzing Academic Rigor from a Learner’s Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0278

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Survey of students in the active-learning course (100-level n = 41, 300-level n = 11 for ease responses); chi-square comparison of ease-attribution patterns differed (χ2 = 25.28, df = 5, p = 0.000127). Students overwhelmingly cited high faculty support; very few said the courses lacked cognitive challenge.

> "Patterns did differ between 100- and 300-level students (χ2 = 25.28, df = 5, p = 0.000127) due to a few more 300-level students identifying logical content and clear alignment as course attributes that contribute to a course being easy."
