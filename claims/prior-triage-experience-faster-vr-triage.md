### Heldring Sara 2025

Heldring Sara, Jirwe Maria, Wihlborg Jonas, Lindström Veronica. (2025). Acceptability and applicability of using virtual reality for training mass casualty incidents- a mixed method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07319-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory non-parametric analysis of computer-recorded triage times from all 95 participants. The test compared "those with prior experience in performing triage and those with non-prior triage experience" on minutes to triage ten victims, showing a statistical difference (p = 0.005).

> "However, a Mann–Whitney U test showed a statistical difference (p = 0.005) between those with prior experience in performing triage and those with non-prior triage experience on how many minutes it took to triage ten victims (Fig. 6)."
