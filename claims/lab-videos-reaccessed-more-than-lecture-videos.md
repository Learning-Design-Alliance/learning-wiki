## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2016

Shih-Yin Lin, John M. Aiken, Daniel T. Seaton, Scott S. Douglas, Edwin F. Greco, Brian D. Thoms, Michael F. Schatz. (2016). Exploring University Students’ Engagement with Online Video Lectures in a Blended Introductory Mechanics Course. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the fraction of students with multiple accessing sessions (FMSj, Table III) across the 78 videos. The article states "usually less than half of the accessing students would access these videos repeatedly" for lecture videos, versus most for lab videos.

> "While for lecture-oriented videos, usually less than half of the accessing students would access these videos repeatedly , most students accessed the same lab video (especially the lab-specific ones) multiple times"

### Shih-Yin Lin 2016 (2)

Shih-Yin Lin, John M. Aiken, Daniel T. Seaton, Scott S. Douglas, Edwin F. Greco, Brian D. Thoms, Michael F. Schatz. (2016). Exploring University Students’ Engagement with Online Video Lectures in a Blended Introductory Mechanics Course. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03348)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table III reports that the percentage of videos with FMSj ≥ 0.5 was "11%" for lecture-oriented videos versus "86%" for laboratory videos, and 100% for lab-specific videos; median FMSj was 0.41 versus 0.75.

> "Percentage of videos with FMSj ≥ 0.5 11%  86% (100% for lab-specific videos)"
