Evidence¶

Lynch Julia 2022¶

Lynch Julia, O'Donoghue Gráinne, Peiris Casey L. (2022). Classroom Movement Breaks and Physically Active Learning Are Feasible, Reduce Sedentary Behaviour and Fatigue, and May Increase Focus in University Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19137775

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Randomised controlled trial (Niedermeier et al., n = 51) of a 10 min run at 13–15 Borg RPE after 45 min of lecture, measuring visual attention with a modified trail making test and perceived attention. Visual attention p = 0.003; perceived attention and arousal p < 0.01. No effect size printed.

"Compared to a sedentary control group, students who went for a 10 min run halfway through a two-hour lecture had significantly higher objectively measured visual attention scores (p = 0.003) and higher perceived attention and arousal (p < 0.01)"
