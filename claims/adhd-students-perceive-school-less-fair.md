## Evidence

### Martinez 2026

Martinez, A. C., Green, C. D., Peugh, J. L., & Becker, S. P. (2026). Perceived School Fairness and Academic Functioning in Early Adolescents: Differential Associations for Adolescents with or Without ADHD? Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01419-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate comparison in a sample of 341 early adolescents (ages 10–12), roughly half with ADHD diagnosed via K-SADS; fairness measured with the 5-item SCS fairness subscale (α = 0.78). The article states "the effect size was small" but prints no standardized effect size, only p = .046.

> "compared to adolescents without ADHD, adolescents with ADHD reported lower perceived school fairness (p =.046) and having a more negative attitude to school (p <.001)"
