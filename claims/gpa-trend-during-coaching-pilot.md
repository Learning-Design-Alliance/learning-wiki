GPA data drawn from university records for the seven participants in the pilot. The mean rose from 3.05 to 3.22; only Zach directly linked coaching to his GPA, which climbed from 2.0 to 3.01. No statistical test was applied.

> "Participants' average semester GPA at the beginning of the study was 3.05 and increased to 3.22 by the end of the study. While most students did not feel that coaching had a direct impact on their grades, this GPA trend suggests otherwise."
