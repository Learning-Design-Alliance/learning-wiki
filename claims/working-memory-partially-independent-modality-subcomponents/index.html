q1 · i? · review · r?

Theoretical assumption stated in the article's abstract as part of cognitive load theory's cognitive architecture. The article offers this as the theory's premise, describing "partially independent subcomponents" for auditory/verbal and visual information; no empirical test is reported in the available text.

"The theory assumes a limited capacity working memory that includes partially independent subcomponents to deal with auditory/verbal material and visual/2- or 3-dimensional information"
