## Evidence

### Martinez 2026

Martinez, A. C., Green, C. D., Peugh, J. L., & Becker, S. P. (2026). Perceived School Fairness and Academic Functioning in Early Adolescents: Differential Associations for Adolescents with or Without ADHD? Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01419-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression models using teacher-rated BASC-3 study skills (R² = 0.28) and learning problems (R² = 0.27) subscales; teacher ratings were available for 256 of 341 participants. The learning-problems model also showed ADHD status estimate = 0.36, p < .001.

> "an ADHD diagnosis significantly predicted lower teacher-reported study skills (estimate = −0.37, p <.001) as a main effect not moderated by fairness perceptions"
