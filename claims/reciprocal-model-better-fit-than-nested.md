## Evidence

### Beaumont 2022

Beaumont, J., Putwain, D. W., Gallard, D., Malone, E., Marsh, H. W., & Pekrun, R. (2022, in press). Students' emotion regulation and school-related wellbeing: Longitudinal models juxtaposing between- and within-person perspectives. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://osf.io/W5CPE

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Model comparison of the reciprocal CLPM and RI-CLPM against three nested models (baseline, unidirectional each way) in the three-wave data; the reciprocal models also had the lowest AIC values. Fit statistics for the comparison appear in the article's Table 3, not printed in the provided text.

> "The reciprocal relations models showed significantly better fit than the other models using the Satorra–Bentler scaled χ2 difference test (TRd; Bryant & Satorra, 2012)."
