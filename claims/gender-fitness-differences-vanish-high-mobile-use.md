## Evidence

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA (gender × mobile-use group) on Eurofit test marks found significant interactions on all six fitness tests reported in the study of 501 students.

> "Significant interactions between use of mobile phone groups and gender were found for the abdominals test, medicine-ball throw, broad jump, 50 m sprint, trunk flexion test and agility test."

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021 (2)

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons in the high mobile-phone-use group found no significant gender differences on any fitness test; the study reports no effect size for these comparisons, so absence of differences is not tested for equivalence.

> "Finally, we did not find differences between the genders in HMP for any test (p > 0.129)."
