## Evidence

### Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc 2018

Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc. (2018). Effects of Eight Week Plyometric Study on the Balance Performance of Hearing Impaired Athletes. International Education Studies, Vol. 11, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n6p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired Samples T-test on the control group's Biodex static balance parameters, reported in Findings Table 5. Overall Stability-R moved from 2.27±0.80 to 2.30±0.80 (t=-0.549, p=0.594). No effect sizes are printed.

> "When the static balance parameters of the control group were analyzed, medial lateral R value was found to be significant between pretest and post-test (p <0.05). Over all stability R, overall stability L, anterior posterior L, anterior posterior R and medial lateral L values were not significant (p> 0.05)."
