## Evidence

### Dundar Sari Makbule Buse 2026

Dundar Sari Makbule Buse, Sezer Berkant. (2026). Comparative performance evaluation of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini Advanced in the Turkish Dentistry Specialization Exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08621-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of the two chatbots on fundamental medical sciences questions (anatomy, histology-embryology, physiology, biochemistry, microbiology, pathology, pharmacology, medical biology and genetics) from the DUS dataset. Gemini Advanced exceeded 90% in all subfields; ChatGPT-4o answered all Biochemistry questions correctly.

> "ChatGPT-4o demonstrated an accuracy rate of 92.6% (95% CI: 90–94.6), and Gemini Advanced achieved 93.4% (95% CI: 90.9–95.2). The difference between the models was not statistically significant (p = 0.625)."
