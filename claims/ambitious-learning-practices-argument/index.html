q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

The editorial presents the squib's argument as a theoretical position: practices the authors "refer to as 'ambitious learning practices'" are argued to empower students and potentially disrupt social inequality. This is an argument rather than a tested result, and the editorial attributes it entirely to the squib's authors.

"Their overall aim is to argue in favor of what they refer to as “ambitious learning practices”, which they argue are practices that “can empower students, support shared meaning-making, and potentially disrupt social inequality”."
