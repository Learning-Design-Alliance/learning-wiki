# ChatGPT-guided PBL shifted study time toward pre-class preparation without increasing total learning time

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group spent more pre-class time (2.21 ± 0.46 vs 1.44 ± 0.48) but less post-class review time (1.25 ± 0.35 vs 1.90 ± 0.42), with no significant difference in total learning time (3.46 ± 0.54 vs 3.35 ± 0.60). [→ Tong Xiaoya 2025](#tong-xiaoya-2025)

## Evidence

### Tong Xiaoya 2025

Tong Xiaoya, Hu Ying, Long Yanjun, Zhang Qian, Yang Yao, Yuan Jing, Zha Yan. (2025). The application of problem-based learning (PBL) guided by ChatGPT in clinical education in the Department of Nephrology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07427-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of self-reported study times before and after class between the two groups; pre-class time was higher and post-class review lower in the experimental group (both P < 0.001), while total time showed "no significant difference" (P = 0.76). No effect size printed.

> "although the experimental group spent more time before class (2.21 ± 0.46 vs 1.44 ± 0.48; P < 0.001), the time spent on review after class was reduced relative to the control group (1.25 ± 0.35 vs 1.90 ± 0.42; P < 0.001), and there was no significant difference in the total learning time between the two groups of students (3.46 ± 0.54 vs 3.35 ± 0.60; P = 0.76)"
