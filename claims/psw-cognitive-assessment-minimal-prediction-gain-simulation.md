## Evidence

### Miciak Jeremy 2016

Miciak Jeremy, Williams Jacob L., Taylor W. Pat, Cirino Paul T., Fletcher Jack M., Vaughn Sharon. (2016). Do Processing Patterns of Strengths and Weaknesses Predict Differential Treatment Response? Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(6), 898-909. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000096

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simulation of two correlated datasets of 1,000 observations comparing misclassification at mean and 25th-percentile cut points with and without the PSW dimension; the best case improved classification for roughly 5-10 of 1,000 students.

> "Data simulations indicated that the most optimistic scenario would result in only a small improvement in predictive accuracy (approximately 5–10 students for every 1,000 students assessed). Most comparisons resulted in no statistically significant or educationally meaningful improvement in prediction."
