### Olsen RG 2024

Olsen RG, Svendsen MBS, Tolsgaard MG, Konge L, Røder A, Bjerrum F. (2024). Automated performance metrics and surgical gestures: two methods for assessment of technical skills in robotic surgery. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02051-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Consequences analysis comparing pass/fail levels from contrasting-groups' methods for ten novices and six experienced surgeons performing simulated RARP part-procedures on the RobotiX Mentor. The article reports the methods "could fully distinguish between novices and experienced surgeons"; no effect size is printed.

> "We compared the pass/fail scores of APM and surgical gestures and found that both methods could fully distinguish between novices and experienced surgeons (consequences) (Fig. 5)."
