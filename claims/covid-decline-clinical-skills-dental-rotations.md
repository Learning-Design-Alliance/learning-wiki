## Evidence

### Shukla A 2025

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-sample t-test of pre/post-COVID clinical skills scores in Results §3.2. Faculty and students had identical pre-COVID means; post-COVID faculty scores remained higher than student scores. The comparison is "statistically significant decline (p < 0.05)".

> "faculty mean scores for Clinical Skills were 4.45 from 2018 to 2020 and decreased to 4.16 from 2020 to 2022 indicating a statistically significant decline (p < 0.05). Similarly, student mean scores dropped significantly from 4.46 to 3.94 (p < 0.05), as shown in Table 2."

### Shukla A 2025 (2)

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA cohort comparison in Results §3.2. Declines were attributed by the authors to insufficient early preparation compounding over time, echoing Radović et al.'s findings on lower self-confidence among 2020 final-year dental students.

> "cohort‐wise results indicated that both student and faculty scores decreased over time, starting at 4.43 for the Class of 2019 to 3.97 for the Class of 2022. Faculty scores similarly decreased from 4.47 in 2019 to 3.95 in 2022. These changes in scores are statistically significant, with p‐values < 0.05."
