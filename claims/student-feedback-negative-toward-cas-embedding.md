## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Very few students gave positive feedback about wxMaxima in Single-Variable and Multivariable Calculus courses, and the majority of comments voiced negativity and resistance. [→ Karjanto 2021](#karjanto-2021)

## Evidence

### Karjanto 2021

Karjanto, N.; Husain, H.S. (2021). Not Another Computer Algebra System: Highlighting wxMaxima in Calculus. Mathematics 2021, 9, 1317. https://doi.org/10.3390/math9121317

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' report of informal student feedback from their own course implementations, in the Conclusions section; no systematic survey data or statistics are printed.

> "From our experience of embedding wxMaxima into Calculus courses, both Single-Variable and Multivariable, very few students gave positive feedback regarding the software. The majority of students' comments voice an atmosphere of negativity and resistance, they tend to push away wxMaxima."
