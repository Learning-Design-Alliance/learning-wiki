### Douglas H. Clements 2013

Douglas H. Clements, Julie Sarama, Carolyn Layzer, Fatih Unlu, Christopher B. Wolfe, Mary Elaine Spitler. (2013). Longitudinal evaluation of a scale-up model for teaching mathematics with trajectories and technologies: Persistence of effects three years after the treatment. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563385

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Legacy data from the treatment years of the cluster-randomized trial, reported in the Conclusions section. The printed effect sizes are ES = .47 (FT vs control), ES = .26 (FT vs NFT), and ES = .17 (NFT vs control, no longer significant). The article characterizes this pattern as the Follow-Through treatment having "value added."

> "By the end of first grade, the TRIAD-NFT group was no longer significantly higher than the control group (ES = .17). The TRIAD Follow-Through group outperformed the control group (ES = .47) and the TRIAD-NFT group (ES = .26)."
