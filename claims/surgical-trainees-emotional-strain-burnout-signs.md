Five-item Likert-scale assessment of emotional involvement, empathy, and stress in the COSTRUIRE survey. The article reports "a dual emotional pattern" of high engagement alongside emotional strain, with the listed percentages endorsing high scores (4 or 5) on each item.

> "Results revealed a dual emotional pattern: high emotional engagement, 71.4% reported feeling involved in their work and 67.7% found empathizing with patients easy, alongside notable signs of emotional strain, with 61.2% experiencing end-of-day exhaustion and irritability, 55.3% feeling emotionally consumed, and 42.2% concerned about potential desensitization over time."
