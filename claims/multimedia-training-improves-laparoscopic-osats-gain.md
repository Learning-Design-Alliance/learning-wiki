## Evidence

### Pape-Koehler Carolina 2013

Pape-Koehler Carolina, Immenroth Marc, Sauerland Stefan, Lefering Rolf, Lindlohr Cornelia, Toaspern Jens, Heiss Markus. (2013). Multimedia-based training on Internet platforms improves surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-012-2672-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial, 18 participants randomized to multimedia-based training after a pretest laparoscopic cholecystectomy in a Pelvi-Trainer; blinded raters scored videos with a modified OSATS. The article reports "The ΔOSATS results were highest in the multimedia-based training group" with the printed mean, SD, and p value; no effect size is printed.

> "The ΔOSATS results were highest in the multimedia-based training group (4.7 ± 3.3; p < 0.001)."
