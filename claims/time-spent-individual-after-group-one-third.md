## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2002

Chandralekha Singh. (2002). Effectiveness of group interaction on conceptual standardized test performance. [doi:10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational time trend across the two successive testings in the IG and GI treatments. The author interprets that GI students were reasonably sure of their thoughts after peer discussion, while IG students were willing to spend time discussing because they found peer collaboration useful.

> "One the other hand, students working individually after the group work in the GI treatment on an average took roughly one third of the time spend on group work."
