## Evidence

### Bruce Thompson 2006

Bruce Thompson. (2006). Evaluating Three Programs Using a School Effectiveness Model: Direct Instruction, Target Teach, and Class Size Reduction. Third Education Group Review, Vol. 2, No. 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ874476

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-after analysis of poverty-adjusted ratings for 21 Milwaukee schools implementing Direct Instruction school-wide before 2000 (Table 3). The 10 fully-supported schools reached .14 annual growth; the remaining 11 showed -0.01, no improvement.

> "On average their scores increased by .06 per year.  As shown by the p-value, this change is significant at the 90% level but not at the 95% level. For the ten schools with fully-supported programs, annual growth in ratings increased to .14."
