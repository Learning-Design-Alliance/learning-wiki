## Evidence

### Malanchini 2020

Malanchini, M., Rimfeld, K., Shakeshaft, N. G., McMillan, A., Schofield, K. L., Rodic, M., Rossi, V., Kovas, Y., Dale, P. S., Tucker-Drob, E. M. & Plomin, R. (2020). Evidence for a unitary structure of spatial cognition beyond general intelligence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0067-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Psychometric validation of the six-test VR spatial orientation battery in the TEDS twin sample (N = 2660, aged 19–22); tests showed normal distributions with skewness and kurtosis within ±2. Reliability was "average r = 0.74, ranging from 0.60 to 0.89", supporting the battery's ability to capture individual differences.

> "Beyond showing good test –retest reliability (average r = 0.74, ranging from 0.60 to 0.89; see Method for information on the reliability of each tests), the six tests — scanning, perspective taking, navigation based on landmarks, navigation following directions, route memorizing and map reading — showed normal distributions"
