## Evidence

### Iordanou 2010

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Three-way repeated-measures ANOVA on words per utterance across the three conditions. The printed effect size is "partial g2 = .24" (partial η² = .24): "a three-way Topicx Timex Condition interaction, F(2, 53)= 8.50, p= .001". On the social topic SOC rose from 6.71 to 12.66 words while control declined from 9.05 to 6.57.

> "A three-way analysis revealed a three-way Topicx Timex Condition interaction, F(2, 53)= 8.50, p= .001, partial g2 = .24."

### Iordanou 2010 (2)

Kalypso Iordanou. (2010). Developing Argument Skills Across Scientific and Social Domains. Journal of Cognition and Development. https://doi.org/10.1080/15248372.2010.485335

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Three-way repeated-measures ANOVA on number of coded utterances. The printed effect size is "partial g2 = .15" (partial η² = .15): "a significant three-way interaction, F(2, 53) = 4.66, p = .014". Control participants decreased from 9.80 to 4.90 on the social topic while both experimental conditions increased.

> "A three-way analysis of mean number of coded utterances revealed a significant three-way interaction, F(2, 53) = 4.66, p = .014, partial g2 = .15."
