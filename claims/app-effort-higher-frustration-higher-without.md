## Evidence

### Kamphausen Anne 2026

Kamphausen Anne, Pilz Maximilian, Baumgärtel Matthias, Kubitz Jens Christian, Engelen Christian. (2026). Effect of an app-guided advanced cardiac life support algorithm on teamwork, non-technical skills, and workload in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled feasibility trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-026-01673-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized simulation-based feasibility trial with 40 interprofessional teams. The effort subscale showed "mean difference 11 with 95% CI [1.9, 21]; p = 0.02", suggesting added exertion to manage digital guidance alongside resuscitation tasks.

> "Effort was higher with the app (62.4 ± 21.1 vs. 51.0 ± 24.3; median 70 vs. 50; mean difference 11 with 95% CI [1.9, 21]; p = 0.02), suggesting added exertion to manage the digital guidance alongside resuscitation tasks."

### Kamphausen Anne 2026 (2)

Effect of an app-guided advanced cardiac life support algorithm on teamwork, non-technical skills, and workload in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled feasibility trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized simulation-based feasibility trial. The frustration subscale showed "mean difference − 14.0 with 95% CI [-25, 3.0]; p = 0.013", potentially reflecting uncertainty or lack of structured prompts when relying on memory/experience alone.

> "Frustration was higher without the app (32.6 ± 25.9 vs. 46.8 ± 24.0; median 50 vs. 23; mean difference − 14.0 with 95% CI [-25, 3.0]; p = 0.013), potentially reflecting uncertainty or lack of structured prompts when relying on memory/experience alone."
