Clayton C. Kohl. (1911). Needed Research in Method. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Needed+Research+in+Method%22+Kohl

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Illustrative analysis of the Committee of Eight's report on The Study of History in the Elementary Schools. The author quotes the committee's aims and then poses a series of unanswered questions, concluding that "the whole course is permeated with adult conceptions and points of view." No data are collected; this is the author's critical illustration.

> "Can a sixth grade boy or girl acquire any impressions, vague or otherwise, of the march of progress or of world movements ? If so, what kind of impressions? Does chronological sequence have any meaning to a child of twelve?"
