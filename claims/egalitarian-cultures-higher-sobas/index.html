Evidence¶

M.M. Chiu 2016¶

M.M. Chiu, B.W.Y. Chow, C. McBride, S.T. Mol. (2016). Students’ sense of belonging at school in 41 countries: cross-cultural variability. Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022022115617031

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel analyses of survey and test data from 193,073 15-year-old students in 41 countries compared SOBAS across cultures differing in egalitarianism, finding "students in more egalitarian cultures often had higher SOBAS". No effect size is printed in the available text.

"First, students in more egalitarian cultures often had higher SOBAS than those in more hierarchical cultures."
