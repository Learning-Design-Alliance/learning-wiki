`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the knowledge section of the cross-sectional questionnaire (N = 420). The study reports "Just 24.3% of respondents recognized that hand washing should last at least two minutes" and low correct-response rates on PPE sequencing (33.6% donning, 36.9% doffing) and mask purpose (33.8%).

> "Just 24.3% of respondents recognized that hand washing should last at least two minutes, and only 33.8% knew that surgical masks primarily protect surgeons from patient infections."
