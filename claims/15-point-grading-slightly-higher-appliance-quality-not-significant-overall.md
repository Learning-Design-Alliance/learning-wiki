## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bialas MJ 2025

Bialas MJ, Schmid JQ, Middelberg C, Stamm T, Blanck‐Lubarsch M. (2025). 15‐Point versus pass/fail grading in orthodontic education: A randomized controlled trial. J Dent Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13815

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group RCT with 138 evaluable predoctoral dental students randomly assigned via sealed lots to 15-point grading (n = 68) or pass/fail (n = 70); appliances were rated by five calibrated dentists. The article reports the overall comparison as "slightly higher in the test group" with p = 0.1310 in Table 4.

> "The quality of the orthodontic appliances was slightly higher in the test group compared with the control group in all three courses."
