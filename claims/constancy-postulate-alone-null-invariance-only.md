## Subclaims
`q1 i?` From the constancy postulate alone, the diagonals of the (x, ct)-plane can at most be shown to map into each other with arbitrary eigenvalues; full length invariance requires the relativity principle. [→ Alfred Ziegler 2007](#alfred-ziegler-2007)

## Evidence

### Alfred Ziegler 2007

Alfred Ziegler. (2007). The role of the two postulates of special relativity. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.0988 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.0988](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.0988)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in Section II showing how far the constancy postulate carries the derivation: the article states "This is as far as one can get with the postulate of constancy alone", leaving eigenvalues α and β arbitrary until the relativity principle is invoked.

> "Adopting as a further physical assumption that forward-propagating light signals remain forward- propagating in the other frame of reference (and backward-propagating signals backward-propagating) the diagonals turn into eigenvectors albeit with arbitrary eigenvalues"
