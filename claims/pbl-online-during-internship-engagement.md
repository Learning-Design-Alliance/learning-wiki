# During the cardiac surgery internship, PBL-plus-Tencent-Conference teaching produced higher participation, teacher-student interaction and acceptance/understanding scores than traditional teaching, with no difference in concentration

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with PBL combined with Tencent Conference online teaching scored significantly higher on degree of participation (p = 0.047), teacher-student interaction (p = 0.041) and acceptance and understanding (p = 0.049) during the internship than students taught traditionally, while concentration did not differ (p = 0.157). ## Evidence

### Chen Xiuwen 2024

Chen Xiuwen, Xiao Yao, You Yunhui, Zhu Jianxi, Liu Shiqing, Chen Huiqiong, Zhu Hong. (2024). The effectiveness of the combined PBL and Tencent Conference online teaching mode in the clinical internship teaching of cardiac surgery. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0315455

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of 68 students (PBL-online N = 30, traditional N = 38) during internship, reported in Table 3 with t-tests: Q5 participation p = 0.047, Q6 interaction p = 0.041, Q7 understanding p = 0.049. The article reports "no significant difference in student's class concentration levels" between the two teaching modes.

> "there was a significant difference between the scores of the two groups in terms of the degree of participation (experience, hands-on opportunities, etc.), the degree of teacher-student interaction (questions, communication, etc.), and the degree of acceptance of understanding (key points, difficulties, and other learning concerns) of classroom learning during the internship (p < 0.05)."
