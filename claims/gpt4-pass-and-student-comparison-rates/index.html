Mingxin Liu 2024¶

Mingxin Liu, Tsuyoshi Okuhara, XinYi Chang, Ritsuko Shirabe, Yuriko Nishiie, Hiroko Okada, Takahiro Kiuchi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT Across Different Versions in Medical Licensing Examinations Worldwide: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/60807

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative synthesis of the 45 included studies: for GPT-3.5, 6 passed and 4 performed satisfactorily (38.5%, 10/26); for GPT-4, "17 reported that GPT-4 passed the medical licensing examination, and 9 reported satisfactory performance" (89.7%). Student comparisons: GPT-4 surpassed students in 13/17 (76.5%), GPT-3.5 in 4/14 (28.6%).

"Among the 29 studies testing GPT-4, 17 reported that GPT-4 passed the medical licensing examination, and 9 reported satisfactory performance, making up 89.7% (26/29) of the total."
