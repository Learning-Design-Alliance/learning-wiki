## Evidence

### Sternecker K 2026

Sternecker K, Schmitz C, Berndt M. (2026). Students’ Learning Outcomes in a Virtual Microscopic Online and Face-to-Face Anatomy Course: Cross-Over Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/82012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory post hoc regression on low-performing online participants (cutoff <24 points, median of maximum 30) yielded R²=0.15 (P<.001). "The significant predictors were self-organization" with β=.30 and commuting time with β=−.25; preference for intensive reviewing was also significant (β=−.20, P=.005).

> "The significant predictors were self-organization (higher the value, better the performance; B=0.75, SE 0.18; β=.30; t174=4.19; P<.001), commuting time (longer the commute time, worse the performance; B=−0.03, SE 0.01; β=−.25; t174=−3.48; P<.001)"
