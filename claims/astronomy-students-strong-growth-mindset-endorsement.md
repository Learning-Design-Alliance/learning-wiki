### Moire K. M. Prescott 2024

Moire K. M. Prescott, Laura Madson, Sandra M. Way, Kelly N. Sanderson. (2024). Prevalence of a growth mindset among introductory astronomy students. arXiv:2407.06147 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Presurvey and postsurvey results across five semesters of an introductory astronomy course show students agreed strongly with growth-mindset statements, with "high positive mindset beliefs and low negative mindset beliefs." Scores are category means on a 1-5 Likert scale with standard errors; no effect size is printed.

> "They agreed strongly with statements indicating a growth mindset, i.e., high positive mindset beliefs and low negative mindset beliefs."
