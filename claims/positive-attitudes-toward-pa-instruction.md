`q2 i?` Questionnaire responses averaged 3.39 on a 4-point scale, with the strongest agreement (M=3.55) that PA was better than their prior school learning. [→ Ibrahim 2018](#ibrahim-2018)

## Evidence

### Ibrahim 2018

Mahmoud Ibrahim. (2018). Explicit Versus Implicit Modes of EFL Reading Literacy Instruction: Using Phonological Awareness With Adult Arab Learners. English Language Teaching, 11(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n9p144

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-item Likert questionnaire administered in structured interviews in Arabic to 62 participants (both groups, after control students received remedial PA); total weighted mean 3.39 (SD 0.59), overall response "Strongly Agree"; Cronbach's Alpha 0.87.

> "Participants explicitly showed that they had learnt literacy using PA better that the way they had learnt in schools (M= 3.55, SD=0.67)."
