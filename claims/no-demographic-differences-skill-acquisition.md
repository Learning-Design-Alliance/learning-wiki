## Evidence

### Gheith and Aljaberi 2017

Eman Gheith & Nahil M. Aljaberi. (2017). The Effectiveness of an Interactive Training Program in Developing a Set of Non-Cognitive Skills in Students at University of Petra. International Education Studies, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n6p60

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

t-tests comparing 12 male and 45 female students in the experimental group on the overall scale and six dimensions; the article reports no significant differences and prints no effect size.

> "The t-values do not point toward any statistically significant differences on the mean values of male and female students, whether on the overall scale or on each of the six dimensions."

### Gheith and Aljaberi 2017 (2)

Eman Gheith & Nahil M. Aljaberi. (2017). The Effectiveness of an Interactive Training Program in Developing a Set of Non-Cognitive Skills in Students at University of Petra. International Education Studies, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n6p60

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA across freshmen (n=27), sophomores (19), juniors (5), and seniors (6) in the experimental group; no significant differences and no effect size printed. Sophomores had the highest overall mean (86.2).

> "The f-values do not point toward any statistically significant differences in relation to the academic year variable."

### Gheith and Aljaberi 2017 (3)

Eman Gheith & Nahil M. Aljaberi. (2017). The Effectiveness of an Interactive Training Program in Developing a Set of Non-Cognitive Skills in Students at University of Petra. International Education Studies, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n6p60

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA across majors (English, Journalism, Education, Arabic, Others) in the experimental group; Arabic Language students had the highest overall mean (88.3) but differences were not significant and no effect size is printed.

> "the f-values do not point toward any statistically significant differences in relation to the university major."
