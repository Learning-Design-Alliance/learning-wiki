## Evidence

### Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah 2025

Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah. (2025). Leading the AI transformation in schools: it starts with a digital mindset. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10439-w

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (2,000 replications, bias-corrected 95% CIs) re-estimating the SEM; openness (β = 0.052) and technology orientation (β = 0.118) had CIs containing zero, i.e. no detectable total indirect effect.

> "The analyses demonstrate a significant total indirect effect of school leaders' proactivity (β = 0.252, CI [0.036, 0.400], p < 0.05) and perspective taking (β = 0.194, CI [0.041, 0.508], p < 0.05) on AI integration in schools."

### Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah 2025 (2)

Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah. (2025). Leading the AI transformation in schools: it starts with a digital mindset. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10439-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Decomposition of total effects in the bootstrapped mediation model; technology orientation (5.9 points) and openness (3.9 points) contributions were not significant (p > 0.10).

> "The analysis also revealed that 31.7% of the impact that school leaders have on AI integration in schools can be attributed to their digital mindset; 11.6 percentage points are attributable to the perspective taking facet (p < 0.05), 10.3 percentage points to the proactivity facet (p < 0.05)"
