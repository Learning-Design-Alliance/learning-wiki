q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

RQ3 interview findings from ten CS2-completing participants. The article reports that when personalization landed, participants described "a qualitative shift in how memorable or intuitive a concept became," citing P6's durable rethinking of linked lists and P8's changed stance toward analogies.

"When personalization "landed," participants described a qualitative shift in how memorable or intuitive a concept became, consistent with Hallal et al.'s [26] finding that AI-generated analogies increase perceived engagement."
