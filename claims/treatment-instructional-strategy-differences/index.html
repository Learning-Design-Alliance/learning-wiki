Lara-Alecio 2024¶

Lara-Alecio, R., Irby, B. J., Tong, F., Sutton-Jones, K. L., Guerrero, C. L., Tang, S., & Pang, H. (2024). Evaluating Pedagogical Practices in Science Classrooms: A Randomized Controlled Trial Study on Teacher Virtual Professional Development with Virtual Mentoring and Coaching. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 16(4), 479-494. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2024.347

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pairwise category comparisons within the instructional strategies domain from the same POP-coded RCT observations. The article reports percentages with p < 0.05 but no standardized effect size; visual scaffolding was "the most frequently used technique (21.9% in control, 27.1% in treatment, p < 0.05)".

"It was observed that control teachers employed manipulatives and realia strategies more often than treatment teachers (13.2% in control, 8.4% in treatment, p < 0.05), while treatment teachers employed cooperative/collaborative strategies (14.1% in treatment, 11.3% in control, p < 0.05)"
