`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cost accounting of the LLM extraction phase in the evaluation study. The article reports "total computing fees were $50" across the three models, and separately estimates human research assistants would have cost about $26,832 assuming roughly 3 hours per study at $20 per hour; automated extraction took less than 6 minutes per LLM.

> "The total computing fees were $50 to extract the three variables from 156 PDFs using three LLMs (GPT 4.1 = $ 8, Gemini 2.5 Pro 2 = $2, o3 = $40)."
