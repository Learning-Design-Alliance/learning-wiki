## Evidence

### Denison Madaliene 2025

Denison Madaliene, Cai Jinman, Roberson Hannah H., Lockhart Ellen, Adkins Farrell. (2025). Trainee Awareness of US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) Colorectal Cancer Screening Guidelines. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02638-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional 15-item survey of residents and fellows in screening-recommending specialties at one academic medical center, administered via REDCap in February 2023 with 116 respondents (34.5% response rate). "70% of respondents correctly identified age 45," while 30% deviated. No effect size was reported.

> "When asked about the appropriate age to initiate CRC screening in average-risk patients, 70% of respondents correctly identified age 45, in alignment with current guidelines; the remaining 30% of respondents provided responses that deviated from these recommendations"
