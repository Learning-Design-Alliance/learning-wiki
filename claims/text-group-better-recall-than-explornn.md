# Learners using a text-based environment showed significantly better recall of RNN terminology than learners using exploRNN

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alex Bäuerle 2022

Alex Bäuerle, Patrick Albus, Raphael Stork, Tina Seufert, and Timo Ropinski. (2022). exploRNN: Teaching Recurrent Neural Networks through Visual Exploration. [doi:10.1007/s00371-022-02593-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00371-022-02593-0)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects study with 37 participants randomly assigned to exploRNN or text; ANOVA on post-test recall found a significant difference "in favor of the text group" (F(1,34)=3.91, p=.028, η²p=.103). Recall was measured with four open questions rated by a domain expert.

> "Against our hypotheses, we found a significant difference regarding recall ( F (1, 34) = 3 .91, p = .028, η2p = .103) in favor of the text group but not for comprehension (F <1, n.s.) or transfer (F <1, n.s.)."
