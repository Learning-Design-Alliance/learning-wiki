## Evidence

### Goodrich J. Marc 2017

Goodrich J. Marc, Lonigan Christopher J. (2017). Language-Independent and Language-Specific Aspects of Early Literacy: An Evaluation of the Common Underlying Proficiency Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Omega statistics from the bifactor print knowledge model in the sample of 858 preschoolers; "approximately 79% of variance in total print knowledge scores was due to the General factor". The two-factor correlation was r = .61, p < .001.

> "Omega statistics indicated that approximately 79% of variance in total print knowledge scores was due to the General factor, approximately 17% was due to the Spanish factor, and approximately 3% was due to the English factor."
