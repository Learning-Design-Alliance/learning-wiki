# Neighbourhood, district, and municipality variation contributes little to achievement (under 2% of variance collectively) and does not interact with student EA-PGI

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Residential area main effects on achievement are small: ~1% variance for municipalities, ~1% for neighbourhoods, <1% for districts. [→ Rosa Cheesman 2022](#rosa-cheesman-2022)
`q3 i?` The best-fitting model included random slopes for schools but not for residential areas, indicating EA-PGI effects do not depend on neighbourhoods, districts, or municipalities. [→ Rosa Cheesman 2022 (2)](#rosa-cheesman-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Rosa Cheesman 2022

Rosa Cheesman, Nicolai T. Borgen, Torkild H. Lyngstad, Espen M. Eilertsen, Ziada Ayorech, Fartein A. Torvik, Ole A. Andreassen, Henrik D. Zachrisson and Eivind Ystrom. (2022). A population-wide gene-environment interaction study on how genes, schools, and residential areas shape achievement. npj Science of Learning 7:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00145-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Intraclass correlations from the multilevel models fitted to the 23,471-student sample show "1% for municipalities, 1% for neighbourhoods, and <1% for districts" variance explained in achievement by residential levels.

> "The variance explained in achievement by residential areas was 1% for municipalities, 1% for neighbourhoods, and <1% for districts (see Supplementary Table 4a for intraclass correlations)."

### Rosa Cheesman 2022 (2)

Rosa Cheesman, Nicolai T. Borgen, Torkild H. Lyngstad, Espen M. Eilertsen, Ziada Ayorech, Fartein A. Torvik, Ole A. Andreassen, Henrik D. Zachrisson and Eivind Ystrom. (2022). A population-wide gene-environment interaction study on how genes, schools, and residential areas shape achievement. npj Science of Learning 7:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00145-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

AIC-based model comparison among 11 nested multilevel models showed random EA-PGI slopes improved fit for schools only; residential areas retained intercepts only, so EA-PGI-by-residential-area interactions were not supported.

> "The best-fitting model – Model 3a in Table 1 – included random slopes and intercepts for schools, but only random intercepts (not slopes) for residential areas."
