Correlational analysis in the full sample (N = 288) found SR–OB correlations "consistently weak, ranging from 𝑟= 0.07to 𝑟= 0.24"; the strongest was Concept_SR with Conceptual Understanding_OB (r = 0.24) and the weakest Evaluate_SR with Practical and Ethical Use_OB (r = 0.07).

> "Correlations between the three OB factors—Conceptual Understanding_OB,Capability Evaluation_OB, andPractical and Ethical Use_OB—and the four SR factors were consistently weak, ranging from 𝑟= 0.07to 𝑟= 0.24"
