# Explaining did not outperform retrieval on comprehension, failing to replicate prior explaining effects

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to preregistered hypotheses, students in the explaining conditions did not outperform students in the retrieval condition, and oral explaining did not outperform written explaining. [→ Jacob Leonie 2021](#jacob-leonie-2021)

## Evidence

### Jacob Leonie 2021

Jacob Leonie, Lachner Andreas, Scheiter Katharina. (2021). Does increasing social presence enhance the effectiveness of writing explanations? PLoS ONE 16(4): e0250406. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250406

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The authors' discussion of the four-condition randomized experiment (N = 137) states the explaining and modality hypotheses were not supported; only test statistics and Bayes factors are reported, with no effect size for the condition contrast.

> "Additionally, we did not find significant differences between the explaining and the retrieval conditions. Thus, we did not replicate prior findings on the effectiveness of explaining [1,9,22,23,83]."
