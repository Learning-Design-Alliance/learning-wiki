The brief reports, citing Jackson and Bruegmann, the peer-experience spillover above and adds that "the quality of a teacher’s peers in the previous two school years affected his or her current students’ achievement." No effect size printed.

"one study of 3rd to 5th grade teachers in North Carolina found that those whose peer teachers had more experience improved more quickly, with novice teachers seeing the biggest benefits."
