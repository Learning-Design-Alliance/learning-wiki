## Evidence

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the academic-performance analysis subsample (n = 3517, 75.0% of the cross-sectional sample), regression found "no association found between hearing acuity and CITO test scores"; an additional analysis of 87 children placed below their CITO-recommended level also found no significant interaction.

> "There was no association found between hearing acuity and CITO test scores. Additionally, when conducting an extra analysis to explore whether higher hearing thresholds had an impact on students achieving an educational level lower than recommended based on their CITO test scores (n = 87), no significant interaction was observed."
