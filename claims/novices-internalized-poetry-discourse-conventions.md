## Evidence

### Peskin 1998

Peskin, J. (1998). Constructing meaning when reading poetry: An expert-novice study. Cognition and Instruction, 16(3), 235-263. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1603_1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Protocol analysis of the same think-aloud expert-novice study, coded against Culler's three conventions. The General Discussion reports: "As Culler had predicted, they clearly anticipated that the poet was intending to express a significant attitude or make a point."

> "As Culler had predicted, they clearly anticipated that  the poet was intending to express a significant attitude or make a point; that this point would function as a central unifying element to which all the propositions would relate; and that metaphor is important in poetic expression."
