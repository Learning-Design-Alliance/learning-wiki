### Tseng 2023

Tseng, C.-H., Lin, H.-C. K., Huang, A. C.-W., & Lin, J.-R. (2023). Personalized Programming Education: Using Machine Learning to Boost Learning Performance Based on Students' Personality Traits. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1080/2331186x.2023.2245637](https://doi.org/10.1080/2331186x.2023.2245637)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A randomized experiment in a 22-student elective Game Development course, with 10 experiment and 6 control students after withdrawals; learning materials were matched to personality traits for the experiment group and randomly delivered for controls. The article reports students given trait-matched materials "outperformedotherstudentsintheirlearningresultssignificantly".
