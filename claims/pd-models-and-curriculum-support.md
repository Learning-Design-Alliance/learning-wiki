### Darling-Hammond 2017 (2)

Darling-Hammond, L., Hyler, M. E., Gardner, M. (2017). Effective Teacher Professional Development. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-prof-dev. [doi:10.54300/122.311](https://doi.org/10.54300/122.311)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Doppelt et al. (2009) study of 8th-grade science teachers using a new electronics curriculum, with content-based collaborative inquiry sessions. The report concludes students were better off if teachers did not attempt new curricular materials without effective PD supporting them.

> "Students whose teachers used the new curriculum and participated in PD had statistically greater achievement than those students whose teachers used the new curriculum with no PD. Even more significant, achievement for students of those teachers who continued to use the older standard curriculum was greater than that of those students whose teachers used the new curriculum with no PD."
