## Evidence

### Shihui Feng & Nancy Law 2021

Shihui Feng & Nancy Law. (2021). Mapping Artificial Intelligence in Education Research: A Network-based Keyword Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 31, 277–303. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00244-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Macro-level network analysis of the KCN built from 1830 AIED articles (2010–2019). The strength distribution shows a long tail consistent with a power law (fit starting at xmin = 12, scaling exponent 2.406), and "a small portion of keywords in the field have a large number of associated keywords and higher co-occurrence frequency." Hubs include 'intelligent tutoring system' with maximum strength 747.

> "The distribution of node strengths indicates hierarchy in the knowledge structure of the field, as a large portion of keywords have few associated keywords and low co-occurrence frequency, while a small portion of keywords in the field have a large number of associated keywords"
