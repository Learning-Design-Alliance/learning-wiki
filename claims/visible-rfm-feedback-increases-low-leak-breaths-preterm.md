### alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane 2026

alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane. (2026). Impact of real-time respiratory function monitoring on neonatal mask ventilation training: a multicentre simulation-based crossover study. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2026-004894

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover simulation study, hidden-first group, preterm manikin; Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the proportion of breaths with leak≤30% between hidden and visible conditions. The article reports the proportion "increased significantly under visible conditions (p=0.006; r=0.45)"; median leak change (6.79% vs 0.00%) was not significant (p=0.053; r=−0.35).

> "In contrast, the proportion of breaths with leak≤30% increased significantly under visible conditions (p=0.006; r=0.45), indicating improved mask seal."
