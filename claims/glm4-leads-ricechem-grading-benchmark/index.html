Benchmark evaluation (Table 2) following Sonkar et al.'s RiceChem ALAG long-answer setting, measuring Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1 for six LLMs on the same dataset. GLM-4-9B-chat achieved the leading "Accuracy = 0.6492, F1 = 0.6054".

"Overall, GLM-4-9B-chat leads across metrics (Accuracy = 0.6492, F1 = 0.6054), indicating a strong balance between precision and recall; Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 ranks second (Accuracy = 0.6167, F1 = 0.5681) with similarly robust aggregate performance."
