## Evidence

### Molero Jurado María del Mar 2021

Molero Jurado María del Mar, Pérez-Fuentes María del Carmen, Martos Martínez África, Barragán Martín Ana Belén, Simón Márquez María del Mar, Gázquez Linares José Jesús. (2021). Emotional intelligence as a mediator in the relationship between academic performance and burnout in high school students. PLoS ONE 16(6): e0253552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253552

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson bivariate correlation matrix (Table 3) relating the three MBI-SS burnout dimensions to the five EQ-i-M20 factors in the full sample. The matrix shows exhaustion negatively related to "stress management and mood" (r = -0.242, r = -0.195) and self-efficacy positively related to all EI components (r = 0.121 to 0.330).

> "exhaustion was negatively related to the intrapersonal components, stress management and mood. The correlation was positive with the interpersonal factor, although intensity was weak. Cynicism correlated negatively with the intrapersonal factors, stress management, adaptability and mood. For self-efficacy, the correlations were positive with all the emotional intelligence components."
