## Evidence

### Chien 2025

Chien, P.Y.‐H., Hosseinpour, S., Peters, O.A. & Peters, C.I. (2025). Outcomes of root canal treatment performed by undergraduate students: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. International Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14246

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Weighted pooled success rates from a random‐effects meta‐analysis (DerSimonian and Laird) of the three studies eligible for meta‐analysis, covering 771 roots. The review reports "teeth with vital pulps (87.8%)" versus necrotic pulps at 65.6%; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Success rates were notably greater for teeth with vital pulps (87.8%) compared with necrotic pulps (65.6%)."
