Evidence¶

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang 2026¶

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang. (2026). Digital learning innovation: engineering students’ learning motivation for AI scaffolding. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10576-w

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Thematic analysis of interview data from 12 participants (interview #1.1) and 11 participants (interview #2.1) revealed that prior coding and AI experience moderated how students engaged with AI-featured scaffolding activities.

"Additionally, participants with prior experience in coding and using generative AI reported that the activity taught them how to read scripts and compare their own code with AI-generated ones. This experience differed from those who had limited coding, Python, and AI experiences."
