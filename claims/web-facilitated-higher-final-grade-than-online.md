## Evidence

### Xin et al. 2015

Huaibo Xin, Monica Kempland, Faustina H. Blankson. (2015). ADAPTABILITY AND REPLICABILITY OF WEB-FACILITATED, HYBRID, AND ONLINE LEARNING IN AN UNDERGRADUATE EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY COURSE. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology – January 2015, volume 14 issue 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057333

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experimental three-section comparison; one-way ANOVA for final course grade yielded F(2,100) = 7.32, p < .05, and for final letter grade F(2,100) = 3.93, p < .05. Tukey HSD confirmed the web-facilitated vs. online contrast.

> "The means of the final course grade and final letter grade of the web-facilitated section were also significantly higher than the means of the online section (mean difference = .06, p < .05, mean difference = .42, p < .05)."
