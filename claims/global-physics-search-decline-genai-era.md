`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Google Trends observational analysis of weekly Relative Search Volume for the Kinetic energy topic, comparing the 2023 reference academic year with the 2025 integration year via paired-sample t-tests. The article reports "a significant global decline of 28.42%" with d = −1.91 and r = 0.92.

> "The results for the topicKinetic energy, summarized in Table I, indicate a significant global decline of 28.42% in search volume, accompanied by a large effect size (d=−1.91) and a high Pearson correlation (r= 0.92)."
