## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Prespecified logistic regression robustness checks indicated intervention students were less likely to receive an OSS (OR = 0.57, p = .09; one-tailed p = .045, d = 0.31), with the prior-OSS interaction also in the same direction (B = -0.98, p = .08). [→ Huang 2023](#huang-2023)

## Evidence

### Huang 2023

Huang, F., Gregory, A., & Ward-Seidel, A. R. (2023). The Impact of Restorative Practices on the Use of Out-of-School Suspensions: Results from a Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial. [doi:10.1007/s11121-023-01507-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-023-01507-3)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Prespecified robustness analysis using logistic regression with CR2 cluster-robust standard errors in the same CRCT (n = 5,878). The main effect was marginal (two-tailed p = .09; one-tailed p = .045), with the article printing "d = 0.31", a small effect size; subgroup interactions were all non-significant (ps > .20) and the intervention effect did not vary by school type (χ2 = 0.76, p = .86).

> "Robustness checks (see Online Appendix C) indicated that students in the intervention schools were less likely to receive an OSS (OR = 0.57, p = .09; one-tailed p = .045, d = 0.31) while controlling for all other variables in the model."
