Xu Wei, Liu Yan, Pan Peng, Guo Yan, Wu Ren-pei, Yao Yin-zhen. (2015). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group results for group A (n=9): e.g., pancreas visualization rose from 2.9±1.1 to 7.3±0.7 (p=0.000); FNA duration fell from 150.0±29.1 to 90.0±17.3 sec (p=0.000). Group B showed significant improvement for duration, accuracy, and score but not operational errors.

> "Diagnostic procedures: Differences between pretest and post-test were significant for visualization of all target sites. Interventional procedures: For pancreatic FNA, a significant improvement was seen in terms of duration, accuracy, operational errors, and score."
