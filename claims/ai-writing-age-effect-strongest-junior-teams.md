### Haoyang Wang 2026

Haoyang Wang, Mingze Zhang, Yi Bu, Star Xing Zhao, Meijun Liu. (2026). Smaller, Younger, and More Impactful: How AI-Assisted Writing Transforms Research Teams. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27404

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantile regression at the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles of team age on post-November-2022 publications, reported in Table S2. The article reports the strongest effect among younger teams, with a Nature 25th-percentile coefficient of -6.99, described as "AI adoption is most concentrated among already junior teams".

> "In the PLoS dataset, the effect is strongest among younger teams (25th percentile: -2.68, 𝑝 < 0.01), suggesting that AI adoption is most concentrated among already junior teams."
