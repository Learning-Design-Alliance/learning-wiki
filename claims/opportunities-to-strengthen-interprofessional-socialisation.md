## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Educators proposed three opportunity subthemes: enhanced institutional support, opportunities for socialisation such as job shadowing and paid training time, and more collaboration. [→ Dolan 2026](#dolan-2026)

## Evidence

### Dolan 2026

Dolan, S., L. Nowell, N. Moules, A. Kaba, and M. Brockway. 2026. “Investigating Healthcare Educators' Interprofessional Socialisation Following an Interprofessional Simulation Facilitator Training Programme: A Mixed Methods Study.” Journal of Advanced Nursing 82, no. 6: 6726–6749. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70267

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretive description analysis of the same 17 interviews. Educators suggested more formal networking processes for new educators regardless of discipline, more training programmes with paid time or training requirements, and job shadowing with educators of different professions.

> "more action I think is needed, more training programs, more exposure to things like sim, more opportunities that are supported by [organisations] with things like you know paid time, training time, or even training requirements to really actually make these things happen, interprofessional socialisation."
