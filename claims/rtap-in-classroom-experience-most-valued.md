## Evidence

### Nathan Kriha 2025

Nathan Kriha, Diarese George, Jonathan Feinstein, and Anna Skubel. (2025). Building Futures: Strengthening the Educator Workforce Through Teacher Apprenticeships. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED678833

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding from the 32-apprentice qualitative study: unlike traditional programs ending in a single unpaid student-teaching semester, apprentices are embedded in classrooms with paid jobs for the full program, and "participants generally believed their apprenticeship was preparing them to become effective educators."

> "participants generally believed their apprenticeship was preparing them to become effective educators with many identifying the in-classroom experience, that hands-on time spent training under a mentor while working with students, to be the highest value and most central component of their program."
