Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pre-post comparison across the total RCT sample (N = 154). The article reports "an increase in all secondary (i.e., stress, negative mood, somatization, anxiety, depression) and explorative outcomes" from baseline to pre-exam; no effect sizes printed for this overall pattern.

> "Overall, our total study sample showed an increase in all secondary (i.e., stress, negative mood, somatization, anxiety, depression) and explorative outcomes (i.e., sleep quality impairment, caffeine consumption) from baseline to pre-exam assessment."
