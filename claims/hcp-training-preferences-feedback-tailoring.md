### Stuij SM 2018

Stuij SM, Labrie NHM, van Dulmen S, Kersten MJ, Christoph N, Hulsman RL, Smets E. (2018). Developing a digital communication training tool on information-provision in oncology: uncovering learning needs and training preferences. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1308-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Training-preferences theme from the focus groups and interviews: participants "desired a digital training" allowing feedback on videotaped skills from peers, communication experts, and/or patients, progress monitoring, and individually tailored training.

> "Moreover, participants desired a digital training to allow for feedback on their own (videotaped) information-giving skills from peers, communication experts, and/or patients; to monitor their progress and to tailored the training to individual learning needs."
