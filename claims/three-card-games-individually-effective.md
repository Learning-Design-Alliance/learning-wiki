## Evidence

### Rafiq R 2025

Rafiq R, Matthews H, Abedalreza D, Yahya F, Jones MA. (2025). Card games are effective tools to enhance foundation year health and safety inductions. FEBS Open Bio 15(12). https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.70140

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Game-specific survey items (n = 79) for Health and Safety Hazard Hunter showed "the majority of students reported positive experiences relating to enjoyability (87.3 %) and knowledge acquisition (91.1 %)"; PPE Lab Gear Up and Lab Equipment Identifier showed similarly positive response patterns (Figs 4 and 5).

> "It was found that the majority of students reported positive experiences relating to enjoyability (87.3 %) and knowledge acquisition (91.1 %)"

### Rafiq R 2025 (2)

Rafiq R, Matthews H, Abedalreza D, Yahya F, Jones MA. (2025). Card games are effective tools to enhance foundation year health and safety inductions. FEBS Open Bio 15(12). https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.70140

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-game comparisons of student feedback for the three games found "no significant difference in pedagogical impact for all games evaluated in this study"; equivalence beyond non-significance was not tested.

> "Comparisons between each of the games revealed no significant difference in pedagogical impact for all games evaluated in this study."
