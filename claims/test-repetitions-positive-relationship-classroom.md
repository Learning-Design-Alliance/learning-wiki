## Evidence

### Yang 2020

Yang, C., Luo, L., Vadillo, M. A., Yu, R., & Shanks, D. R. (2021). Testing (Quizzing) Boosts Classroom Learning: A Systematic and Meta-Analytic Review. Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000309

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of repeated testing on the same material; the review reports "a positive relationship between the number of test repetitions and the classroom testing effect", contradicting Adesope et al.'s (2017) finding of a larger benefit for a single test.

> "There is a positive relationship between the number of test repetitions and the classroom testing effect, indicating that the more occasions on which class content is quizzed, the larger the learning gains."
