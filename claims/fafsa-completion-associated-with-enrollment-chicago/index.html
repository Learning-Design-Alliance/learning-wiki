Jenny Nagaoka 2008¶

Jenny Nagaoka, Melissa Roderick, and Vanessa Coca. (2008). Barriers to College Attainment: Lessons from Chicago. The Consortium on Chicago School Research at The University of Chicago / Center for American Progress. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/barriers-college-attainment-lessons-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Part of the CCSR analysis of the path Chicago graduates took toward four-year enrollment. The association held after controlling for student characteristics and support from parents, teachers, and counselors; predicted probabilities were 84 percent versus 56 percent.

"Among students who had been accepted to a four-year college, some 84 percent of students who completed a FAFSA by the end of the school year attended a four-year college in the fall, compared to only 55 percent of students who did not file a FAFSA."
