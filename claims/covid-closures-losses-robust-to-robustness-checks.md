# The learning-loss estimates are robust to restricting the sample to constant schools, adding school fixed effects, and coarsened exact matching

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` With school fixed effects, significant negative effects remain, with 0.17 standard deviations for mathematics and 0.34 standard deviations for Dutch; matched-sample estimates are similarly significant. [→ Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020](#joana-elisa-maldonado-and-kristof-de-witte-2020)

## Evidence

### Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Robustness re-estimation of the main mathematics and Dutch regressions with school fixed effects added (Table 10), plus estimations on schools participating every year and on coarsened-exact-matched samples; the fixed-effects effect sizes are 0.17 SD (mathematics) and 0.34 SD (d = 0.34, Dutch), all significant.

> "Again, the results prove to be robust with signiﬁcant negative effects in both subjects. Effect sizes are similar to the main results, with 0.17 standard deviations for mathematics, and 0.34 standard deviations for Dutch."
