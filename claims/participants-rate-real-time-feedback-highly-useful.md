### Smart JR 2015

Smart JR, Kranz K, Carmona F, Lindner TW, Newton A. (2015). Does real-time objective feedback and competition improve performance and quality in manikin CPR training – a prospective observational study from several European EMS. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0160-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stage 2 questionnaire of 200 participants across 10 EMS sites using 10-point Likert scores; site co-ordinators unanimously reported that feedback motivated teams to practice. "the question relating to the first study aim (usefulness of objective feedback for CPR) was positive overall".

> "the question relating to the first study aim (usefulness of objective feedback for CPR) was positive overall (mean score of 8.5, sd = 1.8) and the manikins were thought relatively easy to use (mean score of 8.2, sd = 1.7)."
