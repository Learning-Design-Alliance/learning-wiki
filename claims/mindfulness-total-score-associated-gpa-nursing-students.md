## Evidence

### Mathkor Darin Mansor 2025

Mathkor Darin Mansor. (2025). Mindfulness and academic performance among nursing students in Saudi Arabia: a cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports 15, 27381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-11911-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on survey responses of 276 nursing students (FFMQ plus GPA from the examination department). Model 1 shows the association with "the overall mindfulness score (β = 0.505, CI = 0.402, 0.608, p < 0.001)"; Model 2 adjusted for demographics remained significant (β = 0.524).

> "In the unadjusted model (Model 1), academic grade scores are significantly associated with the overall mindfulness score (β = 0.505, CI = 0.402, 0.608, p < 0.001)"
