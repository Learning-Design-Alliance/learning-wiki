# GPT-4o's unsupported medical claims co-occur with incorrect or vague conclusions (ϕ = 0.386), indicating compounded errors in complex reasoning

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Phi coefficient analysis found significant co-occurrence between unsupported medical claims and vague conclusions (ϕ = 0.386, p = 0.0002) and between unsupported claims and persistence with a wrong diagnosis (ϕ = 0.315, p = 0.0003). [→ Altermatt Fernando R. 2025](#altermatt-fernando-r-2025)

## Evidence

### Altermatt Fernando R. 2025

Altermatt Fernando R., Neyem Andres, Sumonte Nicolás I., Villagrán Ignacio, Mendoza Marcelo, Lacassie Hector J., Delfino Alejandro E. (2025). Evaluating GPT-4o in high-stakes medical assessments: performance and error analysis on a Chilean anesthesiology exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08084-9

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Phi coefficient co-occurrence analysis over the 120 annotated incorrect responses, with multiple-comparison adjustment. The printed effect sizes are ϕ = 0.386 (95% CI: 0.207–0.565) for claims–conclusions and ϕ = 0.315 for claims–wrong diagnosis; when unsupported claims occurred, "there was a 10.2% probability of concurrent vague conclusions".

> "Unsupported medical claims and incorrect or vague conclusions co-occurred in five instances (ϕ = 0.386, 95% CI: 0.207–0.565, p = 0.0002). Unsupported medical claims also co-occurred with sticking to the wrong diagnosis in three cases (ϕ = 0.315, 95% CI: 0.136–0.494, p = 0.0003)."
