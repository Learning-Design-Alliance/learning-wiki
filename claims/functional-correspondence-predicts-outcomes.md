`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Chernikova et al.'s meta-analyses of simulation-based learning in higher education, that "functional correspondence, together with the salience of relevant information" explains more variance in outcomes than physical resemblance. No effect size is printed in the editorial.

> "Meta-analyses confirm this emphasis: functional correspondence, together with the salience of relevant information, explains more variance in learning outcomes than either physical resemblance or general appeals to authenticity (Chernikova et al., 2020, 2024)."
