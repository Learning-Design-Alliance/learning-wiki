### Little 2021

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc multivariate latent-growth-curve models relating reading and math skills within K-2 and grades 3-5 in the same sample. The study reports mostly non-significant slope-slope correlations across domains; no effect size was printed.

> "The majority of the multivariate analyses revealed independent associations between the developmental trajectories of reading and math skills, however, suggesting domain-specific factors may have a greater influence on development than domain-general influences"
