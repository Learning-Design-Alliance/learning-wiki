### Temehy Basema 2025

Temehy Basema, Soundy Andrew, Rosewilliam Sheeba. (2025). Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reflexive thematic analysis of follow-up interviews with 16 participants one month post-training. "The assessment section, outcome measures, and goal-setting were reported to be the most useful elements of the stroke training programme", with new content including G-AP, self-management and GRASP.

> "The assessment section, outcome measures, and goal-setting were reported to be the most useful elements of the stroke training programme. Participants highlighted that the programme offered them a recap of information such as the assessment procedures for stroke."

### Temehy Basema 2025 (2)

Temehy Basema, Soundy Andrew, Rosewilliam Sheeba. (2025). Evaluating an online stroke training programme for Saudi Arabian physiotherapists for improving their knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care: a pilot mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06837-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Theme 2 of the reflexive thematic analysis of 16 interviews. Participants stated "The programme was short and lacked practical aspects" and some preferred in-person workshops over online training for interactive learning and practical application.

> "The participants noted the following areas for improvement in the STP: lack of practical content, short duration, superficial explanations of specific topics, and online delivery."
