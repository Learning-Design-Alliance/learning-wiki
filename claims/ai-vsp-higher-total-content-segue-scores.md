# AI-VSP training yields higher post-intervention SEGUE total and Content scores than traditional SP training

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Post-intervention, the AI-VSP group scored statistically higher than the traditional SP group on SEGUE total score (Cohen's d = −0.51) and Content score (Cohen's d = −0.49), and the difference remained significant after ANCOVA adjustment (both P = 0.01). [→ Sun Ning 2026](#sun-ning-2026)

## Evidence

### Sun Ning 2026

Sun Ning, Zhou Xintong, Yang Zhongqian, Zhou Yu, Ma Ruihang, Wang Zhong. (2026). Application of AI-based virtual standardized patients in physician-patient communication training: a study based on the SEGUE framework. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1768518

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison in the RCT (n = 82 analyzed). The article reports moderate effect sizes "total score: Cohen's d = −0.51; Content score: Cohen's d = −0.49" and ANCOVA-adjusted significance (both P = 0.01).

> "the AI-VSP group demonstrated statistically higher total scores (18.53 ± 2.51) and Content scores (12.55 ± 1.91) compared with the traditional SP group (17.33 ± 2.13 and 11.74 ± 1.38, respectively; both P < .05)"
