## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, P., Ganushchak, L., Welie, C., & van Steensel, R. (2026). Learning proximal cyclic sighing buffers anxiety across learning moments and boredom across days. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00446-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian multilevel phase-contrast analysis of EMA anxiety ratings from the 20-day RCT (6,281 observations, n = 84). Controls showed "anxiety increased from Baseline during the Intervention phase (β = 0.43, 95% HPD [0.25, 0.61])"; the breathwork group's intervention contrast was β = 0.10 and post contrast β = 0.02.

> "In the control group, anxiety increased from Baseline during the Intervention phase (β = 0.43, 95% HPD [0.25, 0.61]) and remained elevated in the Post phase (β = 0.30, 95% HPD [0.08, 0.50])."
