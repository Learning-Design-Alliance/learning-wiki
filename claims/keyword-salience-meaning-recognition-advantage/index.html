Evidence¶

Montero Perez 2014¶

Montero Perez, M., Peters, E., Clarebout, G., & Desmet, P. (2014). Effects of Captioning on Video Comprehension and Incidental Vocabulary Learning. Language Learning & Technology 18(1), 118–141. http://llt.msu.edu/issues/february2014/monteroperezetal.pdf

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Bonferroni post hoc comparisons on the meaning recognition test (17 target words, four Dutch translation options) within the vocabulary MANCOVA; the between-subjects effect for meaning recognition was significant, ηp² = .11. The article states "only KC and FCHK significantly outperformed NC."

"For meaning recognition, only KC and FCHK significantly outperformed NC. No significant differences between KC, FCHK, and FC were found."
