## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Greater endorsement of progressive beliefs predicted higher use of authoritative parenting (β = 0.19), and greater traditional beliefs predicted lower authoritative parenting (β = −0.21). [→ Ren 2024](#ren-2024)

## Evidence

### Ren 2024

Ren, L., Chen, M., Hu, B. Y., & Sun, J. (2024). Parenting beliefs and practices as precursors to academic outcomes in Chinese children. Family Process, 63(4), 2000–2015. https://doi.org/10.1111/famp.13006

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

SEM direct paths from T1 beliefs (preschool) to T2 authoritative parenting (Grade 1) in the 206-family longitudinal sample, controlling child gender and age; standardized coefficients with 95% CIs as printed in Table 2.

> "parents' greater endorsement of progressive beliefs predicted higher use of authoritative parenting (β = 0.19, p = 0.003, 95% CI = [0.07, 0.31]), whereas parents' greater endorsement of traditional beliefs predicted lower use of authoritative parenting (β = −0.21, p = 0.002, 95% CI = [−0.34, −0.07])"
