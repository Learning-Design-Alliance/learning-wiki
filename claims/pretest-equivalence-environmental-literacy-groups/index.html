Evidence¶

Derman et al. 2016¶

Aysegul Derman, Elvan Sahin, Esme Hacieminoglu. (2016). Does Outdoor Education Make any Difference in Environmental Literacy of Pre-service Classroom Teachers? INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF ENVIRONMENTAL & SCIENCE EDUCATION, 11(15), 8491-8506. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-tests on pre-test Environmental Literacy Scale scores (Table 4; e.g., attitudes p=.492, uses p=.120, concern p=.165, knowledge p=.690), establishing baseline equivalence of the randomly selected groups. No effect sizes are printed.

"Results showed that there was no statistically significant difference between pre -test scores of control and experimental groups with respect to environmental knowledge, environmental attitudes, environmental uses, and environmental concern."
