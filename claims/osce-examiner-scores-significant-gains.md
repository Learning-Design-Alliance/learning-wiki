## Evidence

### Liu Mei-Yuan 2025

Liu Mei-Yuan, Liao Li-Ling, Huang Yu-Ting, Lee Yi-Chen, Lai I-Ju. (2025). Effectiveness of a scenario-based simulation course on improving the clinical communication skills of dietetic students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06684-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests of examiner-rated OSCE performance for the experimental group (n = 58), tested one week before and after the 12-week course across two ten-minute stations. Overall nutrition assessment and nutrition education scores were significantly higher at post-test.

> "Regarding students' overall performance, post-test scores showed were significantly higher in nutrition assessment (t = -9.87, p < 0.001) and nutrition education (t = -13.942, p < 0.001) than the pre-test scores."
