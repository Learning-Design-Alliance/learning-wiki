Sandelowsky H, Natalishvili N, Krakau I, Modin S, Ställberg B, Nager A. (2018). COPD management by Swedish general practitioners – baseline results of the PRIMAIR study. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2018.1426148

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "A total of 21% of the GPs chose the incorrect answer, inhaled corticosteroids (ICS)."
