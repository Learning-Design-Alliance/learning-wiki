Narrative review assertion about large language models as candidate cognitive models. The review explains that LLM knowledge is distributed across extremely large parameter spaces rather than localized in separable modules, so human-like output may match outcomes through different means than human cognition.

> "This raises the process-equivalence concern (Firestone, 2020): behavioral similarity does not guarantee mechanistic similarity"
