## Evidence

### Gregor Stiglic 2023

Gregor Stiglic, Leon Kopitar, Zhe He, Primoz Kocbek, Lucija Gosak, Prithwish Chakraborty, Pablo Meyer, Jiang Bian. (2023). Improving Primary Healthcare Workflow Using Extreme Summarization of Scientific Literature Based on Generative AI. DSHealth at SIGKDD '23, August 6 – 10, 2023, Long Beach, CA. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.15715](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.15715)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square comparison of accuracy proportions for EU students across the three summary-format groups. Table 1 reports UC1 accuracy of 0.828 (G1) vs 0.55 (G2) and 0.596 (G3), "G1>G2, G1>G3".

> "Comparing accuracy (percentages) with χ2-test showed that for EU students, there were statistically significant differences in both UC1 (χ2=48.349, p<0.001) as well as UC2 (χ2=23.14, p<0.001), where a post hoc pairwise comparisons showed that for both UC1 and UC2 the accuracy is higher in G1 compared to either G2 or G3."
