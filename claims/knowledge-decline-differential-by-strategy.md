## Evidence

### Haleem Abdul 2016

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

At 12-month follow-up after RR-OHE, dentist-led and peer-led groups showed significant knowledge decline (p < 0.05) while teacher-led group knowledge and behavior/hygiene scores across all groups remained stable.

> "Although the OHK scores of the DL and PL groups decreased significantly at 12-month evaluation of RR-OHE (p < 0.05), the said score of the TL group; and OHB and OHS scores of all three groups remained statistically unchanged during this period."
