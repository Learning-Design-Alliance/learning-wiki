Gider Neslihan Yağmur, Tosun Betül, Dirgar Ezgi (2025). Evaluation of the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices of Intensive Care Unit Nurses Regarding Eye Care: A Descriptive Study. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive finding from the 207-nurse survey: 112 of 207 nurses (54.1%) reported not recording or monitoring eye problems. A second comparison found nurses who recorded problems "had slightly higher mean scores across all sub‐dimensions" but differences were not statistically significant (p > 0.05); no effect size is printed.

> "A crucial finding is that 54.1% of ICU nurses did not record or monitor eye problems, despite the risk of ocular complications in critically ill patients."
