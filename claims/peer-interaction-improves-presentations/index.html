Evidence¶

Dixon and Wu 2014¶

L. Quentin Dixon & Shuang Wu. (2014). Understanding Language Learning: Review of the Application of the Interaction Model in Foreign Language Contexts. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 11, 23-39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137251

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Sundrarajun and Kiely (2010), a study of Thai learners in which group discussion before oral presentations led to "better presentations" with more cohesive texts, richer information and improved lexical features. No effect size is printed.

"when the Thai learners were grouped for discussion followed by oral presentations, the group members indeed collaborated with each other and gave better presentations in terms of manifesting more cohesive texts, richer information, and improved lexical features."
