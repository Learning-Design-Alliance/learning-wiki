# MITCA did not improve information-seeking and help management skills relative to traditional homework

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The hypothesised post-test advantage of the MITCA group in information seeking and help management skills was not confirmed; at pre-test the control group was actually higher (d = 0.14). [→ Tania Vieites 2024](#tania-vieites-2024)

## Evidence

### Tania Vieites 2024

Tania Vieites, Eleftheria Gonida, Fátima Díaz-Freire, Susana Rodríguez, Antonio Valle. (2024). Effects of a homework implementation method (MITCA) on self-regulation of learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09376-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests on pre- and post-test data from the MITCA intervention with 533 students. The article reports the hypothesis "was not confirmed" and a pre-test difference "in favour of the control group" (t = 2.104, p < 0.05, d = 0.14); no effect size is printed for the post-test null.

> "The hypothesised difference in information seeking and help management skills between the two groups was not confirmed. As already referred above, an initial significant difference in favour of the control group had been found in information seeking and help management skills, (t = 2.104, p < 0.05, d = 0.14)."
