### Naumenko and Naumenko 2016

Evgeny A. Naumenko, Olga N. Naumenko. (2016). Pedagogical Experience on Formation of Tolerant and Multicultural Consciousness of Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education. https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2016.17.335

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control groups of students of other specialties (30, 31, and 29 students across 2010–2013) received no course «Religions of the region under study». Average tolerance moved from 73.5 to 73.8, which the article describes as "within a statistical margin of error"; no test statistic is printed.

> "The measures of changing tolerance and multicultural components in consciousness of the control group students changed within a statistical margin of error – 0, 3 %."
