### Villavicencio 2025

Villavicencio, P., Tsay, J. S. & de la Malla, C. (2025). Target configuration determines how and what we learn during sensorimotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning 10:89. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00379-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 solution-based linear mixed model comparing observed reaches at the novel 9 cm target against gain, translation and location predictions: "a signiﬁcant interaction between group and solution", F(3,131) = 9.55, p = 0.0000095, η2 = 0.18.

> "Strikingly, the two groups represented the perturbation differently, as indicated by a signiﬁcant interaction between group and solution (solution-based model: F(3,131) = 9.55,p = 0.0000095, η2 = 0.18)."

### Villavicencio 2025 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "The Extent group seemed to represent the perturbation veridically as a gain: participants ' reaches closely matched the gain solution (Extent group, within-subjects, observed vs gain solution: t(120) = 1.11,p = 0.95, Dz = 0.34)"
