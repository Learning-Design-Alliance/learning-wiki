### Kayaoglu 2013

Kayaoglu, M. N. & Caylak, N. (2013). What is needed for correct pronunciation: a model or a concern? Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 53, 269-290. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1060386

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with six volunteer students from the experimental group explored attitudes toward pronunciation. Students reported that teachers focused on grammar and vocabulary, gave no feedback on pronunciation, and that exams did not assess it, which they cited as reasons for not maintaining target sounds.

> "Why would I bother pronunciation, teacher does not assess and give grades to my pronunciation? All the exams are based on grammar etc not on pronunciation."
