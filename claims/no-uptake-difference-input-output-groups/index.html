Evidence¶

Mousavi 2018¶

Mousavi, K., Alavinia, P., & Gholami, J. (2018). Input Providing vs. Output-Prompting Negotiation Strategies in Learning Grammar among Young EFL Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 497-512. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11234a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Chi-square analysis of transcribed uptake moves from the audio-recorded treatment sessions compared the input-providing group (68 of 110 errors followed by uptake) with the output-prompting group (83 of 133). The article reports no significant difference; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"When the two experimental groups were compared, despite the observable discrepancy between the uptake rates, Chi -square analysis did not provide any significant difference between them (χ2=0.1; df =1; p value=0.75, p> 0.05)."
