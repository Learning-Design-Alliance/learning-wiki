## Evidence

### Hazzam 2024

Hazzam, J., Wilkins, S., Southall, C., & Ibrahim, B. (2024). The influence of LinkedIn group community on postgraduate student experience, satisfaction and grades. Computers & Education, 216, 105052. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105052

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of LinkedIn dashboard analytics across 56 posts (41 informative, 15 interactive). Interactive content achieved 24.3% engagement rate per post versus 18.6% for informative content. "a Chi-square difference test (χ2 = 72.833; df = 2, p < 0.05) indicates that the difference in engagement between the two learning activities is significant."
