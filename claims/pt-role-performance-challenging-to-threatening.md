## Evidence

### Maas MJM 2015

Maas MJM, van Dulmen SA, Sagasser MH, Heerkens YF, van der Vleuten CPM, Nijhuis-van der Sanden MWG, van der Wees PJ. (2015). Critical features of peer assessment of clinical performance to enhance adherence to a low back pain guideline for physical therapists: a mixed methods design. BMC Medical Education 15:203. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0484-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative template analysis of questionnaire comments and six semi-structured interviews (one per peer group, 45 to 90 minutes, transcribed verbatim). Participants reported the PT role as "threatening" (IT-P15) but valued it; no effect size is printed.

> "Although some participants initially felt reluctant to move out of their “comfort zone”, they considered exposure of their routine practice as a necessity for quality improvement. They pointed out that the four PA sessions allowed them to cope with anxiety triggers by training in the PT role."
