## Evidence

### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020

Keenan Tiarnán D., Agrón Elvira, Mares Julie A., Clemons Traci E., van Asten Freekje, Swaroop Anand, Chew Emily Y.; AREDS and AREDS2 Research Groups. (2020). Adherence to a Mediterranean diet and cognitive function in the Age-Related Eye Disease Studies 1 & 2. Alzheimer's & Dementia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8328271/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal analysis in AREDS2 using repeated TICS-M and composite assessments at baseline, 2, 4, and 10 years, testing aMED tertile by study year interaction. The article reports "the rate of change over time in cognitive function scores was not significantly different according to aMED tertiles".

> "In AREDS2, the rate of change over time in cognitive function scores was not significantly different according to aMED tertiles: the P-interaction values between aMED tertile and study year were 0.22 (TICS-M) and 0.91 (composite)."
