## Evidence

### Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro 2014

Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro, Wood Guilherme, Knops André, Krinzinger Helga, Lonnemann Jan, Starling-Alves Isabella, Willmes Klaus, Haase Vitor Geraldi. (2014). In How Many Ways is the Approximate Number System Associated with Exact Calculation? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111155

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlations among the three ANS measures and exact calculation in the combined sample of 202 children, controlling for age, intelligence and spelling. The article reports all intercorrelations were significant, indicating a shared construct, and all three measures also correlated with exact calculation.

> "As expected, all of the three measures of ANS acuity showed significant positive correlations among themselves, even after controlling for the effects of age, spelling and intelligence, which indicates that they share a common construct."
