Descriptive results from the first part of the survey on familiarity with ChatGPT among the physics students surveyed. The article reports that use of ChatGPT "in the context of physics questions ... was denied by 74% of the students".

> "The majority of the students had already heard of ChatGPT (84%) before the survey was conducted, but only about half of them (48%) had used the chatbot. Only a minority reported using the chatbot frequently (8%)."
