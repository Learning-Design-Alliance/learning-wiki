The review's theoretical argument for LRI holds that the sequencing of explicit instruction and guided independent learning is pivotal: explicit instruction first, then guided independence as fluency develops. This is an argued position grounded in CLT research, not a directly tested comparison in this article.

> "But critically, it is the sequencing of these two that is pivotal to optimize learning: students benefit from predominantly explicit instruction in the early stages of task and content mastery and then are further enriched by immersing in a guided independent phase of learning as the automaticity and fluency of knowledge and skill develop."
