This is the article's report of Baker's (2019) LAK keynote challenge to the learning analytics community — an authorially reported claim, not a new study; no effect size or data are printed.

> "One unsolved problem is creating generalizable "detectors" of students' affective states, such as boredom, while they use a DLP. Knowing when a student is bored with educational content in a DLP could allow for targeted interventions to improve their engagement and effort, and although affective detectors have been built for specific platforms, no generalizable approach exists to date."
