## Evidence

### Shi 2015

Jing Shi. (2015). An Analysis of the Application of Wikipedia Corpus on the Lexical Learning in the Second Language Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 8(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n8p171

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the quasi-experiment comparing corpus-based and conventional lexical teaching, the author reports that for Level I learners "students in the control group did better than students in the experimental group, but the differences does not meet the standard of significance." No effect size is printed.

> "First, learners in Level I of two groups did worse in the posttest than the pretest, and students in the control group did better than students in the experimental group, but the differences does not meet the standard of significance."
