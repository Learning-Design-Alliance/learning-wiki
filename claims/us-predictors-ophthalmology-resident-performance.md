`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review discussion attributing this to cited US studies (refs 26–28); the review reports, citing those works, that USMLE scores, honours, clerkship grades and AOA membership are predicted to relate to better performance. The review did not reanalyse the data.

> "There exists evidence to suggest that applicants who have USMLE Step 1 and 2 [26–28], medical school honours [28], high clerkship grades [28] AOA membership [28] are predicted to perform better during and after ophthalmology training."
