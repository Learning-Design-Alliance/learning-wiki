# AI humanizer websites recontextualize detection evasion by deleting misconduct terms and substituting euphemisms like 'humanize' and 'improve flow'

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Humanizer sites systematically exclude terms like cheating, plagiarism, and misconduct and replace them with euphemistic alternatives, foregrounding writing improvement while backgrounding detection evasion. [→ Roe 2026](#roe-2026)

## Evidence

### Roe 2026

Roe, J., Perkins, M., Bannister, P., Furze, L., & Wood, J. (2026). Dramaturgies of Deception: AI Humanizers and the Performance of Legitimacy in Higher Education Assessment. A preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02649 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.02649](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.02649)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Multimodal critical discourse analysis applying Van Leeuwen's recontextualization transformations (substitution, deletion, rearrangement, addition) across humanizer websites; the authors observe "all four at work across humanizer websites."

> "deletion operates through the systematic exclusion of terms like 'cheating,' 'plagiarism,' 'misconduct,' and so on, despite the fact that these words accurately describe the primary use case for many users."
