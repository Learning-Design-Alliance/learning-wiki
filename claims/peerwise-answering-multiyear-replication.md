## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across four cohorts (2015-2018; n = 1695), mean answer significantly predicted Stage 4 results controlling for Stage 3, while mean reputation did not. [→ Guilding C 2021](#guilding-c-2021)

## Evidence

### Guilding C 2021

Guilding C, Pye RE, Butler S, Atkinson M, Field E. (2021). Answering questions in a co-created formative exam question bank improves summative exam performance, while students perceive benefits from answering, authoring, and peer discussion: A mixed methods analysis of PeerWise. Pharmacol Res Perspect. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.833

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-year hierarchical regression across four cohorts (2015-2018; n = 1695) with Stage 3 entered first, then mean reputation and mean answer. The article reports "a significant positive relationship between answering PeerWise questions and exam results". No effect size printed.

> "the regression analysis indicated that while mean answer was a significant predictor of Stage 4 results, controlling for Stage 3 (t = 7.475, p < .001), mean reputation was not (t = −0.846, p = .397)"
