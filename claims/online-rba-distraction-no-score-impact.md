## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2021

Ben Van Dusen, Mollee Shultz, Jayson M. Nissen, Bethany R. Wilcox, N.G. Holmes, Manher Jariwala, Eleanor W. Close, and Steven Pollock. (2021). Online Administration of Research-Based Assessments. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/10.0002888](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0002888)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A study using browser focus data (how often and how long the assessment tab is hidden) found "between half and two-thirds of students lost focus on the assessment at least once", with most events under 1 minute. Neither number nor duration of events correlated with scores; no effect size is printed.

> "browser focus data indicated that between half and two-thirds of students lost focus on the assessment at least once, though the majority of these events (two-thirds) was less than 1 min in duration"
