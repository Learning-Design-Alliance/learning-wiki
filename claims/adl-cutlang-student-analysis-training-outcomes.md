Report from the Vietnam School of Physics (VSOP-26, 2020) for undergraduate or junior graduate students with no coding or analysis experience; after three hours of hands-on Jupyter/binder exercises, students wrote simple analyses for Z boson reconstruction and "a simple supersymmetry search".
