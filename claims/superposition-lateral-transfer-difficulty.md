## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Transfer of Learning in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interviews with QM students found that even those who can explain superposition in classical contexts "have difﬁculty in discerning its relevance to" particle waves; many treat stationary states as the only possible wave functions.

> "In the interviews, we found that even those QM students who can explain the superposition principle in classical contexts have difﬁculty in discerning its relevance to “particle" waves."

### Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Transfer of Learning in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

On a written infinite-square-well question about whether all allowed wave functions satisfy HΨ = EΨ, "roughly one third said" yes, some claiming this holds by definition.

> "When we explicitly posed a question asking whether they agree with a student who claims that all allowed wave functions satisfy HΨ = EΨ, roughly one third said “yes"."
