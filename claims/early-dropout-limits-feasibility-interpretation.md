### Janssen J 2025

Janssen J, Lackenbauer W, Gasselich S, Lickel ME, Schabel L, Beikircher R, Keip C, Wieser M, Mazuquin B, Yeowell G. (2025). Improving physiotherapists’ ability to recognise the presence of a serious pathology with a digital educational training: a mixed methods feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08101-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Limitations section of the feasibility study: of 116 individuals who began registration, 75 dropped out after the first page. The authors state the "findings relating to feasibility may reflect the experiences of a subset of participants who are more motivated or self-selecting."

> "Specifically, 65% of prospective participants dropped out after completing the first page (study details and confirmation). This substantial early dropout rate suggests that the modules may be less feasible or engaging for a broader population than indicated by the responses of those who completed the whole digital educational training programme."
