# Adopted educational philosophy is significantly positively related to teaching style

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all philosophy–style pairings, correlations are statistically significant, indicating a positive relationship between adopted philosophy and teaching style. [→ Emel Saritaş 2016](#emel-saritas-2016)

## Evidence

### Emel Saritaş 2016

Emel Saritaş. (2016). Relationship between philosophical preferences of classroom teachers and their teaching styles. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2787

`q2 · i?`

Relational survey model; correlation analysis across all five philosophies and five teaching styles (N=301) found every Pearson coefficient significant at the 0.01 level (2-tailed), ranging from .101 to .579. The author summarizes "a medium and low level of relationship" overall.

> "Examining Table 4, there is a significant relationship between the educational philosophies and teaching styles of teachers."
