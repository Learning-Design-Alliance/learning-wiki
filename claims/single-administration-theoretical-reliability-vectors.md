# Theoretically defined test reliability computable from a single administration via parallel-half vector lengths and angle exceeds split-half reliability but falls below Cronbach alpha

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Using an iterative parallel-half split and the lengths of the two subtest score vectors and the angle between them, error variance and theoretically defined reliability (rtt = ST²/SX²) can be estimated from a single administration. [→ Chakrabartty 2021](#chakrabartty-2021)

## Evidence

### Chakrabartty 2021

Chakrabartty, S. N. (2021). Assessment of item and test parameters: Cosine similarity approach. International Journal of Psychology and Educational Studies, 8(3), 28-38. https://dx.doi.org/10.52380/ijpes.2021.8.3.190

`q2 · i?`

Empirical verification on the 50-item test split into parallel halves by the iterative process (equal means 10.25; SDs 66.70 vs 67.11). With ‖Xg‖ = 315.6169, ‖Xh‖ = 315.6058 and Cosθgh = 0.975479, theoretical reliability was 0.551577, SE² = 5.362239, true score variance = 6.595749.

> "Theoretical reliability of the test 𝑟𝑡𝑡 = 1 - ‖𝑋𝑔‖2+ ‖𝑋ℎ‖2− 2 ‖𝑋𝑔‖‖𝑋ℎ‖𝑐𝑜𝑠𝜃𝑔ℎ 𝑛.𝑆𝑋 2 = 0.551577"

### Chakrabartty 2021 (2)

Chakrabartty, S. N. (2021). Assessment of item and test parameters: Cosine similarity approach. International Journal of Psychology and Educational Studies, 8(3), 28-38. https://dx.doi.org/10.52380/ijpes.2021.8.3.190

`q2 · i?`

Comparison of reliability estimates on the same data: split-half reliability rgh was 0.380813, theoretical reliability 0.551577, and Cronbach alpha 0.78. The article reports the theoretical value fell between the two.

> "Theoretical reliability of the test was higher than the Split-half reliability but lower than Cronbach 𝛼(0.78)"
