## Evidence

### Acar Eyup & Akinci Yasin 2026

Acar Eyup & Akinci Yasin. (2026). Tactical and assessment competency gaps in pre-service coaches: evidence from a game-based implementation. Scientific Reports 16, 23736. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53681-3

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing 16 CE and 24 PET students scored by expert observers on the Educational Game Playing Skill Observation Scale. The article reports "Game Implementation (t(38) = 6.37, p < .001, d = 0.67)" and overall competency d = 0.70. An independent samples t-test was used to compare the departments.

> "The statistically significant differences, particularly in the Game Implementation (t(38) = 6.37, p < .001, d = 0.67) and Overall Competency (t(38) = 6.94, p < .001, d = 0.70) sub-dimensions, indicate a medium to large practical significance, supporting H1 and H2."
