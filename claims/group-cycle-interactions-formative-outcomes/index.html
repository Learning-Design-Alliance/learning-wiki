Yao X-C and Fan L 2026¶

Yao X-C and Fan L (2026) From automated scoring to learning diagnosis: a mechanism study of AI-supported formative assessment in English writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1905455. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905455

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-effects models with Group, Cycle, and Group × Cycle fixed effects and a student-level random intercept on 288 cycle-level observations. Four "Group × Cycle interactions" were significant, indicating growing group separation over cycles.

"Mixed-eﬀects models yielded Group × Cycle interactions for diagnostic accuracy,F(2,188.4) = 3.57, p = 0.030; feedback comprehension, F(2,188.3) = 3.18, p = 0.044; revision quality, F(2,188.6) = 4.02, p = 0.019; and self-assessment accuracy, F(2,187.9) = 10.31, p < 0.001."

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The same mixed-effects analysis found no significant Group × Cycle interaction for feedback actionability; equivalence was not tested. The group main effect for actionability remained significant.

"The actionability interaction was not signiﬁcant, F(2,188.1) = 1.63, p = 0.199."
