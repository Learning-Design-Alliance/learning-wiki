## Evidence

### Bahar Modir and Eleanor C. Sayre 2016

Bahar Modir and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2016). “Pulling out” as a procedural resource when solving partial differential equations. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.07493](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.07493)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Across episodes, Alex drew a path on the whiteboard and gestured the trajectory of pulling out; the instructor did likewise with another student. The authors read gesture and the "comes out" metaphor as evidence of a procedural resource with a kinesthetic basis.

> "We do not argue that the activation of pulling out resource always goes along with gestural elements indicating the path of pulling out resource. However, we observed that both Alex (and, in a separate interaction with another student, the instructor) used gestural elements to show how this resource works."
