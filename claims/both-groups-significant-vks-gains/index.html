Evidence¶

Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban 2011¶

Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban. (2011). The Impact of Watching Subtitled Animated Cartoons on Incidental Vocabulary Learning of ELT Students. Teaching English with Technology, 12(4), 3-15. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1144953

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-sample t-tests on each group's pre-to-post VKS gains (Group A t=11.391, df=20, mean difference .34127; Group B t=10.985, df=20, mean difference .11111); "the progress made in each group was significant at a .01 level". Post-test means rose from 2.47 to 2.81 (Group A) and 2.42 to 2.53 (Group B).

"As Table 3 illus trates, the progress made in each group was significant at a .01 level."
