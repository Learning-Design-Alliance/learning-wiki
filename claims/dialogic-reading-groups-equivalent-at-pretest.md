Diana Brannon, Linda Dauksas. (2014). The Effectiveness of Dialogic Reading in Increasing English Language Learning Preschool Children's Expressive Language. International Research in Early Childhood Education, 5(1). https://www.education.monash.edu.au/irecejournal/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test ACIRI videotaped interactions (Tables 1 and 2) for the 21 dialogic and 19 traditional Family Time parents showed no significant group differences; equivalence was not otherwise tested. An initial survey also found no significant group differences on parental education, home language, books in the home, library visits, or parent reading.

> "There were no significant differences between the group of parents who were going to receive the dialogic reading training and the traditional Family Time group regarding interactions at the beginning  of the program"
