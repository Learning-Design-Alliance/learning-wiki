## Evidence

### Cappetta 2013

Cappetta, R.W., and Zollman, A. (2013). Agents of Change in Promoting Reflective Abstraction: A Quasi-Experimental Study on Limits in College Calculus. REDIMAT – Journal of Research in Mathematics Education, 2(3), 343-357. https://doi.org/10.4471/redimat.2013.35

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-sided t-test on the mathematical knowledge measure for the twelve-student three-tiered comparison subgroup scored on the extended response rubric; the difference was not significant at p < .05 despite a medium effect size.

> "No significant difference was identified for the mathematical knowledge measure, t (10) = 1.10, p = .15.   Cohen's d = .635 so there was a medium effect."
