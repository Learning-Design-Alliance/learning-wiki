### A.R. Piña 2025

A.R. Piña, Shams El-Adawy, Mike Verostek, H. J. Lewandowski, Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2025). Investigating Opportunities for Growth and Increased Diversity in Quantum Information Science and Engineering Education in the U.S. based on an Analysis of the Current Educational Landscape. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.00104](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.00104)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Catalog analysis of 1,456 US institutions found engineering departments contributed 1,058 quantum courses, "approximately 12% of the total", behind physics (~60%) and chemistry/biochemistry (~24%). ECE alone offers the third-largest collection of quantum courses even excluding other engineering fields.

> "Engineering departments contributed 1,058 courses, accounting for approximately 12% of the total, making it the third-largest discipline represented in this dataset."
