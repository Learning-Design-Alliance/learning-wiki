## Evidence

### Kunnel John Roshin 2020

Kunnel John Roshin, Xavier Boby, Waldmeier Anja, Meyer Andrea Hans, Gaab Jens. (2020). The governmental ranking of class and the academic performance of Indian adolescents. PLoS ONE 15(11): e0241483. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0241483

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Separate multiple regression for school KL2, which alone had sizeable representation of all three governmental classes, controlling for the same five covariates. The article reports "KL2: β = 5.12, SE = 1.23, t = 4.17, p<0.001"; performance was highest in GC and lowest in SC-ST (p<0.001).

> "we found a positive association in this school (KL2: β = 5.12, SE = 1.23, t = 4.17, p<0.001) where the students showed increasing academic performance with increasing class levels."

### Kunnel John Roshin 2020 (2)

Kunnel John Roshin, Xavier Boby, Waldmeier Anja, Meyer Andrea Hans, Gaab Jens. (2020). The governmental ranking of class and the academic performance of Indian adolescents. PLoS ONE 15(11): e0241483. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0241483

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-specific regression models (Table 6) for KL1, KL3, MP1, MP2 and MP3, each controlling for income, gender, age, self-esteem and life satisfaction. None showed a significant class–performance association; several schools had minimal or no SC-ST representation.

> "For the other five schools, a significant pattern of association could not be observed (Table 6)."
