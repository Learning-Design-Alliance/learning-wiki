`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test on the 22-item attitude-toward-physics scale for the 32-student experimental group: post mean 77.000 versus pre 62.875. The article reports "a significant difference between the mean scores of the tribal and post for experimental group"; no effect size is printed.
