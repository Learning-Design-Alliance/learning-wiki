## Evidence

### Tejinder Kaur 2017

Tejinder Kaur, David Blair, Ron Burman, Warren Stannard, David Treagust, Grady Venville, Marjan Zadnik, Warwick Mathews and Dana Perks. (2017). Evaluation of 14 to 15-Year-Old Students' Understanding and Attitude towards Learning Einsteinian Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.02063](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.02063)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Delayed retention test of 9 questions administered to the 2013 class after one year (2014) and the 2014 class after three years (2017); the sample reduced to about half for the retention tests. Non-Euclidean geometry retention was 76% after one year and 71% after three years.

> "After one year of the programme, 76% of students were able to explain how space is related to gravity (RQ 4) and 67% of students retained this concept after three years of the programme."
