Darling-Hammond 2018¶

Darling-Hammond, L., & Cook-Harvey, C. M. (2018). Educating the whole child: Improving school climate to support student success. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/educating-whole-child

q3 · i? · review · r2

The report's synthesis of two research reviews covering more than 400 studies concludes that "a positive school climate improves academic achievement overall and reduces the negative effects of poverty on achievement." The elements contributing most are teacher-student relationships, including warmth, acceptance, and teacher support.

"Two recent reviews of research, incorporating more than 400 studies, have found that a positive school climate improves academic achievement overall and reduces the negative effects of poverty on achievement, boosting grades, test scores, and student engagement."
