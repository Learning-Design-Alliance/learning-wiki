The review reports, citing Terwiesch (2023), a Wharton professor's account that exam creation typically took 20 hours plus 10 TA hours, while ChatGPT produced the exam in 10 hours and cut TA time to 5 hours. A single self-reported case, not a controlled comparison.

> "However, ChatGPT was able to create the exams within 10 hours and reduced TAs time to 5 hours. This shows 100% productivity increase in the “exam writing operation” (Terwiesch, 2023, p. 23)."
