## Evidence

### Luo Wenjing 2026

Luo Wenjing, Yang Runyu, Feng Liwei, and Liang Yuanyuan. 2026. "Effect of the Jeffries Simulation Framework on the Core Competence of New Graduate Paediatric Surgery Nurses: A Quasi‐Experimental Study." Nursing Open 13 (9): e70779. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70779

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same single-group pretest/posttest study (n = 60); preceptors rated decision-making with the 40-item CDMNS at baseline and post-training. The article reports a "mean increase of 7.23 ± 4.17 points", t = −12.515, Cohen's d = 1.62, p < 0.001 (Table 3). This is a within-group gain: the authors state that, without a control group, the change cannot be attributed solely to the intervention, since routine ward experience over the 8 weeks and the Hawthorne effect may have contributed.

> "Similarly, the total score of clinical decision‐making ability showed a marked improvement, rising from 83.92 ± 2.43 to 91.15 ± 3.24, representing a mean increase of 7.23 ± 4.17 points."
