## Evidence

### Winarno 2018

Winarno, S., Muthu, K. S., & Ling, L. S. (2018). Students’ feedback of mdpbl approach and the learning impact towards computer networks teaching and learning. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(1), 37-43. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.1.37

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of the mDPBL group (n=136). Contents items q1, q3, q4 and q5 showed "higher mean scores (mean ≥ 4.0)"; students agreed the contents were suitable for Computer Networks.

> "Nearly all students have a positive response to contents of multimedia learning for Computer Networks, with aggregate mean value = 4.01 (fig.3)."

### Winarno 2018 (2)

Winarno, S., Muthu, K. S., & Ling, L. S. (2018). Students’ feedback of mdpbl approach and the learning impact towards computer networks teaching and learning. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(1), 37-43. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.1.37

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire survey of the mDPBL group (n=136). All navigation items showed mean scores of 4.0 or higher; Figure 3 reports navigation as the highest dimension at mean value = 4.08.

> "Nearly all students have a positive response to navigation of multimedia learning for Computer Networks, with aggregate mean value = 4.01 (fig.3)."
