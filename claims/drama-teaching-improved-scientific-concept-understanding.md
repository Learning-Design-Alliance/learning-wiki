## Evidence

### Osama H. Abed 2016

Osama H. Abed. (2016). Drama-Based Science Teaching and Its Effect on Students' Understanding of Scientific Concepts and Their Attitudes towards Science Learning. International Education Studies, 9(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n10p163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test/post-test quasi-experiment with 87 seventh-grade male students (46 experimental, 41 control); ANCOVA on SCT post-test covarying pre-test. Experimental adjusted mean M=13.66 exceeded control M=10.11, with Group F=41.29, significant at α=0.05.
