## Evidence

### Vignal 2022

Vignal, M., & Wilcox, B. R. (2022). Investigating Unprompted and Prompted Diagrams Generated by Physics Majors During Problem Solving. arXiv. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of the Blocks problem-task pair (19 students) found every student drew an unprompted diagram, but 8 included only given information; only non-given forces on the bottom block, which "5 of 6" correct-answer students drew, tracked correctness.

> "The presence of (and detail in) unprompted diagrams is not strongly correlated with the student selecting the correct answer, as can be seen in Table IV and discussed more in Sec. IV, with the exception that 5 of 6 students who drew the (non-given) forces that were acting on the block in question (the bottom block) got the correct answer."
