Lynch Julia 2022¶

Lynch Julia, O'Donoghue Gráinne, Peiris Casey L. (2022). Classroom Movement Breaks and Physically Active Learning Are Feasible, Reduce Sedentary Behaviour and Fatigue, and May Increase Focus in University Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19137775

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative synthesis of feasibility outcomes across seven PAL and four CMB studies assessed against Bowen's Framework focus areas (demand, acceptability, practicality, integration). Satisfaction ranged from 66% to 73% in three CMB studies; 93% approved continuation in one study.

"In three studies most participants (66% to 73%) reported they were satisfied with the CMB interventions [54,55,57]. Two CMB studies measured desire to continue CMBs with 93% of participants in one study approving the continuation of an active movement break"
