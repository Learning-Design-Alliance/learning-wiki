## Evidence

### Hoang April 2025

Hoang April, Hepburn Stevie-Jae, Morawska Alina, Sanders Matthew R. (2025). The effect of self-reflection on the outcomes of online clinical skills training: a comparative study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10425-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Observational ANCOVA of recorded role-play intake interviews scored by trained raters on a competency checklist (ICC = 0.89). The experimental group's "total Interview Competency score" was significantly higher with "Cohen’s d = 0.46".

> "The total Interview Competency score of the experimental group post-training was significantly higher than that of the Control group, after controlling for pre-training scores (F(1,86) = 4.12, p <.05), with a medium effect size (Cohen’s d = 0.46)."
