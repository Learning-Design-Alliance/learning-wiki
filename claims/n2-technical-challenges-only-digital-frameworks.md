## Evidence

### Henry Cook 2023

Henry Cook, Tiffani Apps, Karley Beckman, Sue Bennett. (2023). Digital competence for emergency remote teaching in higher education: understanding the present and anticipating the future. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10194-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase 2 document analysis of ten Australian university frameworks found technical-challenge accommodation only in the three digital capability frameworks. The authors note the teaching capability frameworks did not explicitly include this competency, suggesting technical aspects are viewed as distinct from teaching.

> "Accommodation of technical challenges was evident in three frameworks (DC1, DC2, DC3). All three were digital capability frameworks and included practices such as finding solutions or workarounds (DC2) and managing technical issues (DC1, DC3)."
