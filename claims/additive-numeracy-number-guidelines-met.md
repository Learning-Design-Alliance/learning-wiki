## Evidence

### Watson Amanda 2022

Watson Amanda, Dumuid Dorothea, Maher Carol, Olds Tim. (2022). Associations between meeting 24-hour movement guidelines and academic achievement in Australian primary school-aged children. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2020.12.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

ANCOVA by number of guidelines met (0-3) in the self-report model showed a significant numeracy difference (F(3,1262) = 5.46, p = 0.01) with an additive pattern of 23, 34, and 40 points; literacy showed no significant differences (F(3,1262) = 2.32, p = 0.07 self-report; F(3,919) = 0.48, p = 0.70 accelerometry). No standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "A post hoc analysis showed that compared with those who met no guidelines, those who met at least 1 guideline had numeracy scores 23 points higher (t(378) = -2.46, p = 0.01). This increased to 34 point t(708) = -4.16, p < 0.001) and 40 points (t(334) = -4.66, p < 0.001) higher for meeting 2 or 3 guidelines, respectively."
