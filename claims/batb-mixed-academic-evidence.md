`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review of a pre-post study in Dallas Independent School District grades 5-12; "Although unadjusted models showed improvements in reading scores following program participation, adjusted models found no significant changes in reading or math scores over time."

> "Another study assessed a districtwide BATB program and found that participants were more than twice as likely to attend school as non-participants [34]."
