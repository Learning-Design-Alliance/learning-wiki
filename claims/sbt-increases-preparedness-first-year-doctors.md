## Evidence

### Søren Marker 2019

Søren Marker, Marlene Mohr, Doris Østergaard. (2019). Simulation-based training of junior doctors in handling critically ill patients facilitates the transition to clinical practice: an interview study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1447-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 20 first-year doctors interviewed 6 months after a 4-day mandatory SBT course; systematic text condensation identified preparedness as a main theme. The article reports "increased preparedness for the handling of critically ill patients following attendance of the course". No effect size is printed.

> "The doctors gave several examples of increased preparedness for the handling of critically ill patients following attendance of the course. Dealing with critically ill patients were seen as the most challenging and anxiety-provoking elements"
