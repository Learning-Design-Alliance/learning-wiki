### Frampton 2025

Frampton, S. E., Vesely, S. E., & Jackson, K. (2025). Teaching undergraduates to solve equivalence problems by using cover, copy, and compare: A translational study. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 58(4), 805–820. https://doi.org/10.1002/jaba.70026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A nonconcurrent multiple-baseline design with seven undergraduate students evaluated the effects of a CCC training package on graphic organizer construction fidelity. All participants' fidelity scores "improved immediately after the initiation of CCC training" and no errors occurred in Phase 1.

> "All participants' graphic organizer fidelity scores improved immediately after the initiation of CCC training. Participants emitted no errors when constructing the graphic organizer during Phase 1 of CCC with A‐B relations."
