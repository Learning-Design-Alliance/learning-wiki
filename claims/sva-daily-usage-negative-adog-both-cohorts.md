## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Among 12-13-year-olds, SVA daily usage correlates negatively with ADOG (r=-0.45, p<0.001). [→ Ziyan Xu 2022](#ziyan-xu-2022)

## Evidence

### Ziyan Xu 2022

Ziyan Xu, Xinqiao Gao, Jun Wei, Huiqin Liu, & Yu Zhang. (2022). Adolescent user behaviors on short video application, cognitive functioning and academic performance. https://www.statista.com/statistics/1095186/tiktok-us-users-age/

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of survey and cognitive-test data from 454 younger and 368 older adolescents in one Beijing middle school. The article reports "a significantly negative relationship with SVA daily usage" for both cohorts, with r=-0.45 younger and r=-0.30 elder.

> "ADOG of both age cohorts has a significantly negative relationship with SVA daily usage (r=-0.45, p<0.001 for the younger cohort and r=-0.30, p<0.001 for the elder cohort)"
