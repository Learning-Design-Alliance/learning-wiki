# Treatment teachers improved on two of three mathematics-task embedded assessment questions, but not on the incorrect-student-method question

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Treatment teachers significantly improved on Questions 1 and 2 (effect size = 0.30 and effect size = 0.32) with no significant change on Question 3. [→ Seago 2013](#seago-2013)

## Evidence

### Seago 2013

Seago, N. (2013). Supporting Teachers' and Students' Knowledge of Geometric Similarity. Proceedings of the 35th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584497

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Tests on a similar-rectangles task administered twice within the Foundation Module to 82 treatment teachers, with False Discovery Rate adjustment; effect size = 0.30 and effect size = 0.32 on Questions 1 and 2. "There was not a significant change on Question 3 scores" for treatment teachers, and comparison teachers showed no significant changes.

> "For the treatment group, on average, there was a significant improvement in the scores on Questions 1 and 2 from pre- to post-test (p values < .05). The effect sizes for treatment group gains in scores on these questions were 0.30 and 0.32, respectively."
