## Evidence

### Pantziaras I 2015

Pantziaras I, Fors U, Ekblad S. (2015). Training With Virtual Patients in Transcultural Psychiatry: Do the Learners Actually Learn? Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3497

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up knowledge test analysis of the 26 residents (81%) who completed the online test several weeks after the virtual patient session. The follow-up mean of 8.38 was "higher than the pre-interaction score by 0.69 points", which was not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "The mean score on the follow-up knowledge test was 8.38, higher than the pre-interaction score by 0.69 points, which was not statistically significant."

### Pantziaras I 2015 (2)

Pantziaras I, Fors U, Ekblad S. (2015). Training With Virtual Patients in Transcultural Psychiatry: Do the Learners Actually Learn? Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3497

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of post-interaction and follow-up scores in the same follow-up cohort: the follow-up score was "0.35 points lower than the post-interaction score", not statistically significant, indicating a non-significant decline from the immediate gain.

> "The follow-up score was 0.35 points lower than the post-interaction score, which was not statistically significant."
