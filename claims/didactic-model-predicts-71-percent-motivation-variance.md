## Evidence

### Saguin Emeric 2024

Saguin Emeric, Salomé Julia, Favodon Benoît, Lahutte Bertrand, Gignoux-Froment Frédérique. (2024). Validation of a didactic model evaluating the usability, usefulness and acceptability of psychological first aid teaching through simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06491-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression with forward selection on questionnaire data from 59 trainers; criteria were added incrementally to maximize explained variance, reaching data saturation at the fourteenth criterion. The article reports "an R2 coefficient of determination of 0.71 (i.e. 71%, F = 4.91, p < 0.001)"; R2 is variance explained, not a standardised effect size.

> "The final model showed an R2 coefficient of determination of 0.71 (i.e. 71%, F = 4.91, p < 0.001)."
