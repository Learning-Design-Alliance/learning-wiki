Correlation and multiple regression analyses (Tables 3 and 5) in the 210-student cross-sectional study. Regression showed relevance to clinical practice (B = 0.45, 95% CI [0.27, 0.63], p = 0.001) and understanding of pharmacology's importance (B = 0.50, 95% CI [0.32, 0.68], p = 0.001) among significant perception predictors; "There was also a moderate positive correlation between academic achievement and perception of pharmacology (r = 0.56, p < 0.01)."
