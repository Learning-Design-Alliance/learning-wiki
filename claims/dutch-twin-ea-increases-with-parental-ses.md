### Eveline L. de Zeeuw 2019

Eveline L. de Zeeuw, Kees-Jan Kan, Catharina E. M. van Beijsterveldt, Hamdi Mbarek, Jouke-Jan Hottenga, Gareth E. Davies, Michael C. Neale, Conor V. Dolan and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2019). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive analysis of national EA test scores for 12,889 Dutch twins from 6929 families, stratified into four parental SES groups; the article reports that "the higher the SES, the higher, on average, the mean EA" (Fig. 2, Table 1).

> "As expected, the higher the SES, the higher, on average, the mean EA."
