Evidence¶

Robinson 2026¶

Robinson, Carly D., David Gormley, Ana Trindade Ribeiro, and Susanna Loeb. (2026). Access is Not Enough: Human Support Improves Engagement with AI Tutoring. EdWorkingPaper No. 26-1451, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai26-1451

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of control-group platform logs from two RCTs (District A: 90 control students; District B: 145 control students). The article reports "only 60.7% and 53.3% of students ever used the AI tutor" and average weekly usage of 2.18 and 5.23 minutes; students used the platform during only four to five weeks on average.

"Despite having access to the AI tutor and scheduled time to use it, only 60.7% and 53.3% of students ever used the AI tutor in District A and District B, respectively. This translated to average weekly usage of 2.18 minutes in District A and 5.23 minutes in District B."
