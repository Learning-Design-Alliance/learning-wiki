Design-decision result from the Results section: after reviewing personas and site goals, the stakeholder team reached a consensus prioritization, believing the most impact comes from "faculty who are already motivated to improve student learning" rather than skeptical or already-successful faculty.

> "So we prioritized in order from most to least important, Raphael the motivated novice, Tim the seeker, Diane the pragmatic satisficer, Marge the proto-researcher and Paula the skeptic."
