# A single wave of pretest measures reduces selection bias in every examined case and eliminates it in half of them

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across six instances drawn from three datasets with two outcomes each, a single wave of pretest always reduced bias and eliminated bias in three of the six instances. [→ Kelly Hallberg 2018](#kelly-hallberg-2018)

## Evidence

### Kelly Hallberg 2018

Kelly Hallberg, Thomas D. Cook, Peter M. Steiner, M. H. Clark. (2018). Pretest Measures of the Study Outcome and the Elimination of Selection Bias: Evidence from Three Within Study Comparisons. Prevention Science, vol. 19, issue 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/pretest-measures-of-the-study-outcome-and-the-elimination-of-selection-bias-evidence-from-three

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The study's synthesis across two within-study comparisons and one especially strong quasi-experiment, each with an educational intervention seeking to improve achievement, reports that "A single wave of pretest always reduces bias across the six instances examined, and it eliminates bias in three of them." No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "Across the three datasets with two outcomes each, there are three cases where this correlation is low and three where it is high. A single wave of pretest always reduces bias across the six instances examined, and it eliminates bias in three of them."
