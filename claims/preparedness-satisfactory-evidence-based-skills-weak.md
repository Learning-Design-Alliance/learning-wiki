### Hattar Susan 2021

Hattar Susan, AlHadidi Abeer, Sawair Faleh A., Alraheam Islam Abd, El-Ma'aita Ahmad, Wahab Fouad Kadim. (2021). Impact of COVID-19 pandemic on dental education: online experience and practice expectations among dental students at the University of Jordan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02584-0

Validated 17-item preparedness scale (scored 0-2 per item, total 0-34) administered only to graduating 5th-year students (N = 193 invited). "The mean total self-perceived preparedness score (TPS)" was 24.4 (SD ±4.7), unaffected by gender (p > 0.05).

> "The mean total self-perceived preparedness score (TPS) for the 131 participants 5th year students was 24.4 (SD ±4.7) with a range of 5 to 34."
