## Evidence

### Dabaliz Al-Awwab 2017

Dabaliz Al-Awwab, Kaadan Samy, Dabbagh M. Marwan, Barakat Abdulaziz, Shareef Mohammad Abrar, Al-Tannir Mohamad, Obeidat Akef, Mohamed Ayman. (2017). Predictive validity of pre-admission assessments on medical student performance. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5a10.04e1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same preclinical multiple linear regression of five pre-admission variables on cGPA found GAT a significant negative predictor. The authors note this "goes hand in hand with internationally published work indicating that measures of aptitude, in general, tend not to correlate with students' university academic performance or may even negatively correlate with it."

> "However, GAT was found to be a significant negative predictor of cGPA in the same group (p=0.048, B=-0.05, t=-2.606)"
