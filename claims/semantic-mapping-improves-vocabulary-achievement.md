# Teaching vocabulary through semantic mapping significantly improves EFL learners' overall vocabulary achievement compared with usual vocabulary instruction

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group significantly outperformed the control group on the vocabulary achievement posttest (Mann-Whitney U=254, p=0.004). [→ Nilforoushan 2012](#nilforoushan-2012)

## Evidence

### Nilforoushan 2012

Somayeh Nilforoushan. (2012). The Effect of Teaching Vocabulary through Semantic Mapping on EFL Learners' Awareness of the Affective Dimensions of Deep Vocabulary Knowledge. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p164

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized two-group experiment with 30 experimental and 30 control participants; a nonparametric Mann-Whitney test replaced the planned t-test because the experimental group's scores were skewed. The experimental group's mean rank of 37.03 exceeded the control group's 23.97, "U=254, N1=30, N2=30, р=0.004<0.05".

> "the results of the Mann-Whitney test indicated that at the 0.05 level of significance, there was a significant difference between the mean rank of the control group (23.97) and that of experimental group (37.03) on the vocabulary posttest (U=254, N1=30, N2=30, р=0.004<0.05)"
