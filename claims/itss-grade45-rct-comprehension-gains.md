# In the grades 4-5 cluster RCT, ITSS produced statistically significant positive effects on all six comprehension outcomes (average effect size 0.24)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r3` · `q3` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Fourth- and fifth-grade students using ITSS outperformed comparison students on the Gray Silent Reading Test (effect size 0.15, improvement index +6, p < .01) and on five researcher-designed structure-strategy measures. [→ gsrt-45-1](#gsrt-45-1)
`q3 i2` The largest effect in the grades 4-5 study was on the Short Comparison Text: Main Idea Test, with an effect size of 0.51 and improvement index +20. [→ mainidea-45-1](#mainidea-45-1)

## Evidence

### gsrt-45-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, April). Web-based Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/703

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Cluster randomized controlled trial in 45 rural and suburban elementary schools; 130 intervention and 129 comparison classrooms. On the standardized GSRT with 4,856 students, the intervention group mean was 32.12 versus 30.42, effect size 0.15, improvement index +6, p < .01.

> "Gray Silent Reading Test (GSRT) Fourth- and fifth-grade students 4,856 32.12 (11.52) 30.42 (11.37) 1.70 0.15 +6 <.01"

### mainidea-45-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, April). Web-based Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/703

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

In the same grades 4-5 cluster RCT, the researcher-designed main idea summary measure showed intervention mean 3.46 versus comparison 2.65 with 4,035 students, effect size 0.51, improvement index +20, p < .01 — the largest printed effect in Table 3.

> "Short Comparison Text: Main Idea Test Fourth- and fifth-grade students 4,035 3.46 (1.58) 2.65 (1.58) 0.81 0.51 +20 <.01"
