Quasi-comparison across six semesters of qualitative and quantitative analysis at Universidad de las Américas Puebla, comparing post-redesign summative quizzes with performance in the same courses four years earlier; mean improvements of 0.6 and 0.5 points out of 10 are reported with no test statistic.

> "These summative assessments were compared with performance of students in the same courses four years before implementing these revisions, and showed mean improvements of 0 .6 (undergraduate course) and 0 .5 points (graduate course) out of 10 possible [4]."
