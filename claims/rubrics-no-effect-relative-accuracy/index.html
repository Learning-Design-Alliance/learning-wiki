Evidence¶

Rebecca Krebs 2022¶

Rebecca Krebs, Björn Rothstein, Julian Roelle. (2022). Rubrics enhance accuracy and reduce cognitive load in self-assessment. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09302-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Gamma correlations between self-assessments and performance across the four criteria; 23 participants with tied scores were excluded. "we did not find a statistically significant difference between the groups"; equivalence was not tested.

"In terms of relative accuracy, by contrast, we did not find a statistically significant difference between the groups, t(67) = -0.85, p = 0.198 (one-sided), d = 0.21."
