Evidence¶

Delgado 2026¶

Delgado, W., & Sartain, L. (2026). Classroom composition affects teacher performance ratings. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 48(2). https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/classroom-composition-affects-teacher-performance-ratings

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Decomposition analysis within the same Chicago Public Schools teacher-fixed comparison design. The study reports that "Prior test scores, GPA, and attendance were the main drivers" of rating effects, with demographics inconsistent for observations.

"Prior test scores, GPA, and attendance were the main drivers, while most demographic characteristics (race, income, special education) did not consistently affect evaluator ratings. Student surveys were somewhat more sensitive to demographics."
