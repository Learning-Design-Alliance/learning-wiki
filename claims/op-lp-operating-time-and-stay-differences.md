## Evidence

### Oomen M. W. N. 2010

Oomen M. W. N., Hoekstra L. T., Bakx R., Heij H. A. (2010). Learning curves for pediatric laparoscopy: how many operations are enough? The Amsterdam experience with laparoscopic pyloromyotomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-0880-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Perioperative comparison in the retrospective 229-patient series. Median operating time was "significantly different between the OP (33 min) and the LP (40 min) groups (p = 0.000)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The median operating times were significantly different between the OP (33 min) and the LP (40 min) groups (p = 0.000)."

### Oomen M. W. N. 2010 (2)

Oomen M. W. N., Hoekstra L. T., Bakx R., Heij H. A. (2010). Learning curves for pediatric laparoscopy: how many operations are enough? The Amsterdam experience with laparoscopic pyloromyotomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-0880-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Postoperative outcome from the same retrospective cohort. Median postoperative stay was "3 days for the OP patients and 2 days for the LP patients (p = 0.002)"; median total stay was 5 vs 4 days (p = 0.048). No effect size is printed.

> "The median hospital stay after surgery was 3 days for the OP patients and 2 days for the LP patients (p = 0.002)."
