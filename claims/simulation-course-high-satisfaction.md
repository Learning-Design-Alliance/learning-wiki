### Liu Mei-Yuan 2025

Liu Mei-Yuan, Liao Li-Ling, Huang Yu-Ting, Lee Yi-Chen, Lai I-Ju. (2025). Effectiveness of a scenario-based simulation course on improving the clinical communication skills of dietetic students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06684-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test satisfaction survey of the experimental group (n = 58), an 11-item five-point Likert scale adapted from Huang et al. Item means ranged from 4.36 to 4.67; the highest-rated item concerned improving communication skills "through repeated practices" (4.67 ± 0.51).

> "Average satisfaction with the 11 items exceeded 4.4 out of five points, indicating that students' overall satisfaction with the course was very high (Table 2)."
