## Evidence

### Laura B. Lang 2014

Laura B. Lang, Robert R. Schoen, Mark LaVenia, and Maureen Oberlin. (2014). Mathematics Formative Assessment System – Common Core State Standards: A Randomized Field Trial in Kindergarten and First Grade. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562773

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Year-long cluster-randomized field trial of 301 K-1 teachers in 31 Florida schools, schools randomized within district and FRPL blocks; multilevel model with pretest control found the MFAS coefficient of 0.26 (p < .05), which the article interprets as an effect size of 0.26.

> "Thus, controlling for pretest, MFAS teachers scored on average a quarter of a standard deviation higher than control teachers on the MKT."
