## Evidence

### Maloney Ciaran M. C. 2026

Maloney Ciaran M. C., Atkin Andrew J., Beaumont Lee C., Dainty Jack R., Warburton Victoria E. (2026). Adolescents’ time-use and academic attainment: A longitudinal, compositional analysis in the Millennium Cohort Study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346302

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Weekday compositional isotemporal substitution analysis of 20-minute reallocations in the Millennium Cohort Study (n=1644), adjusted for sex, ethnicity, free school meals, and maternal education, found significant associations involving sleep. The article reports that "increased sleep duration associated with a lower Attainment 8 score"; for example, adding 20 minutes of sleep from physical activity gave β = −.81 (95%CI −.87 to −.76).

> "Models that simulated the addition or removal of time from sleep were significantly associated with Attainment 8, with increased sleep duration associated with a lower Attainment 8 score."
