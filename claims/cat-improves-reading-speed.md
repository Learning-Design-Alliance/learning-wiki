Belhan Çelik, Sümeyye; Bumin, Gonca. (2025). Improving Refugee Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial on the Impact of Cognitive Activity Training. BMC Psychology, 13, 1186. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03586-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on the Reading Speed Test in the RCT. The article reports that "post-intervention scores significantly improved in the study group (p <.001), with no significant changes observed in the control group"; pre-intervention group means did not differ significantly.

> "Mixed Design ANOVA findings revealed that, the RST results indicated statistically significant interaction effects (F(1.32) = 12.264, p <.001) (Table 4)."
