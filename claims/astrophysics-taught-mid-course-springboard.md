The authors' recommendation section argues that astrophysics placed last in a scheme of work risks running out of time, and that it can instead act as a "springboard" for other topics such as light. This is an author recommendation, not a tested result.

> "Thus we advocate teaching astrophysics in the middle of a physics course, after some groundwork in classical physics such as forces has been taught."
