# First-grade math achievement is significantly higher under Math Expressions and Saxon Math than under Investigations in Number, Data, and Space and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a multischool randomized study of first graders, schools assigned to Math Expressions and Saxon Math achieved significantly higher math achievement than schools assigned to Investigations in Number, Data, and Space and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics. [→ Agodini 2009](#agodini-2009)

## Evidence

### Agodini 2009

Agodini, R., Harris, B., Atkins-Burnett, S., Heaviside, S., Novak, T., & Murphy, R. (2009). Achievement Effects of Four Early Elementary School Math Curricula: Findings from First Graders in 39 Schools. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/achievement-effects-of-four-early-elementary-school-math-curricula-findings-from-first-graders-in-39-schools

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Findings from the first cohort of a large-scale federal experimental study of four early math programs, based on 39 schools that began participation in the 2006-2007 school year. The report states "achievement was significantly higher in schools assigned to Math Expressions and Saxon Math" than in the two comparison curricula; no effect size is printed on this page.

> "Researchers found that achievement was significantly higher in schools assigned to Math Expressions and Saxon Math than in schools assigned to Investigations in Number, Data, and Space and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics."
