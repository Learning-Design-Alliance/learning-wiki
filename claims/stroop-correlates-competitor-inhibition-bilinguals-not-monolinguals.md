## Evidence

### Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica 2011

Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica. (2011). Bilingualism influences inhibitory control in auditory comprehension. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3582323/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis in the same 60-participant study relating Stroop RT differences to negative-priming competitor inhibition. "Bilinguals showed a negative correlation between Stroop inhibition and competitor priming (r = −.5, p = .004)". Monolinguals showed no significant correlation (r = −.1, p = .591).

> "Bilinguals showed a negative correlation between Stroop inhibition and competitor priming (r = −.5, p = .004; see Fig. 5B), suggesting that more efficient Stroop inhibition was associated with less inhibition of the linguistic competitor"
