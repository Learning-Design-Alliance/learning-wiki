# No-loss gambling and gains-only designs avoid loss-aversion complications in PDW

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Payoff designs containing no losses, such as no-loss gambling (6% of four-cell matrices) and gains-only designs (21%), circumvent the problem that loss aversion makes real payoff values unknown. [→ Lorenz Weise 2024](#lorenz-weise-2024)

## Evidence

### Lorenz Weise 2024

Lorenz Weise. (2024). Payoff designs in post-decision wagering: a systematic review. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09411-z

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review describes the no-loss gambling design proposed by Schurger & Sher (2008) and used by Dienes and Seth (2010) and Mealor and Dienes (2012), in which "The no-loss gambling design solves this problem by simply not including losses". It separately reports 16 of 78 matrices (21%) implement gains-only designs per Goodie and Young (2007).

> "The no-loss gambling design solves this problem by simply not including losses. This design, with the low wager's payout determined by a random event, constitutes 5 out of 78 reviewed matrices (6%)."
