Evidence¶

Funa 2021¶

Funa, A. A., & Prudente, M. S. (2021). Effectiveness of problem-based learning on secondary students’ achievement in science: A meta-analysis. International Journal of Instruction, 14(4), 69-84. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2021.1445a

q3 · i?

Moderator analysis of the 11 pooled studies by scientific discipline. The article reports large effect sizes for Biology (1.262), Chemistry (1.022), and Physics (.768), a medium effect for Environmental Science (.605), a small effect for Science and Technology (.304), and an overall discipline ES of .855 with between-group p = .554.

"The heterogeneity between scientific fields (p > .05) showed no significant effects signifying that PBL as applied to different scientific disciplines may share common effect sizes."
