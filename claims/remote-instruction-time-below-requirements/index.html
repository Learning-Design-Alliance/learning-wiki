Evidence¶

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero 2023¶

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero, Candice Odgers and Daniel Ansari. (2023). Elementary school teachers' perspectives about learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning 8:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00191-w

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey-based estimate from the discussion section: teachers reported an average of about 18.87 h of remote class time per week in the Methods, which the authors compare with the roughly 30 h typical requirement in Canada and the US. Descriptive comparison only; no statistical test is printed.

"The average number of hours of remote instruction reported by our participants fell below the 20 h, which represents less than two thirds of these typical requirements. Consistently, most participants reported not being able to deliver all the content they typically taught during a regular school year."
