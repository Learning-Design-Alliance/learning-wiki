# On the OULAD page-click dataset, 6 of 7 low performers fall in the massed-learning cluster, whose grade is significantly lower than the distributed cluster (paired t-test at 5%)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For 100 Open University learners with day-grained page-click time-series, a robust 3-way partition separates massed from distributed learners, with 6/7 low performers in the massed cluster and a significantly lower grade (paired t-test at 5%) than the distributed cluster. [→ Peach 2019](#peach-2019)

## Evidence

### Peach 2019

Peach, R. L., Yaliraki, S. N., Lefevre, D. and Barahona, M. (2019). Data-driven unsupervised clustering of online learner behaviour. npj Science of Learning 4:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0054-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Test of the pipeline on the OULAD dataset (100 learners, day-coarse-grained page clicks rather than task completion). "6/7 of the low-performance learners (<60%) were located in the cluster associated with massed learning"; the partition was robust across sparsification levels.

> "We found that 6/7 of the low-performance learners (<60%) were located in the cluster associated with massed learning, while one low-performance learner was located in the minor outlier cluster and none were in the distributed learning group."
