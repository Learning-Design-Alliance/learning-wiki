### Liu Sha 2026 (2)

Liu Sha, Zhang Jiale, Shang Yuqi, Jiang Yufei, Zhang Xueyan, Hu Zhiyong, Chen Caiyun, Mi Wei. (2026). Practice and application of blended teaching based on RG-UClass in a preventive medicine course. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1885005

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The deep learning motivation dimension (13.12 ± 3.01 vs. 13.21 ± 3.33) showed no significant difference, indicating the deep learning advantage stems from strategies rather than motivation.

> "Specifically, the intervention group showed significantly higher scores only in the deep learning strategies dimension (t = 3.511, p = 0.006), whereas deep learning motivation exhibited no significant between-group difference."
