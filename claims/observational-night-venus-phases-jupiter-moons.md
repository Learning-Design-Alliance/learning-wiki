## Evidence

### Vinicius de Abreu OLIVEIRA 2020

Vinicius de Abreu OLIVEIRA. (2020). Astronomy Like the First Contact with Sciences. International Educational Scientific Research Journal [IESRJ]. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.04084](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.04084)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single observational night with a 150 mm telescope, during which the group observed Venus and Jupiter and pointed the telescope at Orion and its nebula. The authors' "informal and oral questionnaire" found Venus's phases and Jupiter's moons the most interesting sights, the latter showing Earth is not at the Universe's centre.

> "Using an informal and oral questionnaire we verified what was the most interesting thing during that night. The first one was the phases of the Planet Venus, because no one knew that Venus has phases like the Moon"
