## Evidence

### F. Escalante 2021

F. Escalante. (2021). Repeat a physics course more than once: A diagnosis of the most frequent misconceptions in newtonian mechanics of first and second-year engineering students. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.02251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.02251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive statistics per course (Table 3) report means and standard deviations by attempt: Phys. 1 mean 8.1 in all attempt groups; Phys. 2 means 10.4, 9.7, 8.9; Phys. 3 means 11.4, 10.8, 12.9. No inferential test is reported.

> "Each course, independently, shows a similar mean score, which means that there is no big diﬀerence in overall student's performance in the FCI, according to the attempts."
