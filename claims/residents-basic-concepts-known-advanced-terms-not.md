### Al-Zahrani Sami H. MD 2015

Al-Zahrani Sami H. MD, Al-Khail Bahaa A. Aba MD, DPH. (2015). Resident physician's knowledge and attitudes toward biostatistics and research methods concepts. Saudi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.15537/smj.2015.10.11842

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional self-administered questionnaire survey of 162 residents (90% response rate) at a single Saudi training hospital, using a 20-question knowledge test adopted from Windish et al. The survey found most residents "well-informed in basic concepts" while "more than 67%" lacked knowledge of sophisticated terms.

> "Most residents were well-informed in basic concepts, such as, “P” values, study power, and case control studies; more than half had confidence in interpreting the results of scientific papers. Conversely, more than 67% of the residents were not knowledgeable on more sophisticated terms in biostatistics."
