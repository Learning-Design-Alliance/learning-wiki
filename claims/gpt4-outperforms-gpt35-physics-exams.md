## Evidence

### Will Yeadon 2023

Will Yeadon, Douglas P. Halliday. (2023). Exploring Durham University Physics Exams with Large Language Models. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.15609](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.15609)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 on 42 Durham University physics exams (593 questions, 2504 marks), marked by the original examiners. The article reports "an average score of 49.4% compared to 38.6% for GPT-3.5"; no effect size is printed.

> "GPT-4 consistently outperformed GPT-3.5 across all exam types, with an average score of 49.4% compared to 38.6% for GPT-3.5."
