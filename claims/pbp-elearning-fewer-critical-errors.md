## Evidence

### McCarthy Nora 2023

McCarthy Nora, Neville Karen, Pope Andrew, Barry Lee, Livingstone Vicki. (2023). Effectiveness of a proficiency-based progression e-learning approach to training in communication in the context of clinically deteriorating patients: a multi-arm randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open 13(8):e072488. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072488

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis tests at each timepoint with Mann-Whitney U pairwise comparisons, because critical errors were not normally distributed. PBP vs S p=0.001 and PBP vs HSE p<0.001; S vs HSE p=0.626.

> "Post-intervention, the median number of 'critical errors' in the low-fidelity simulation environment decreased in each group and there was a statistically significant difference between the three groups (p<0.001). The median number of 'critical errors' was lowest in the (PBP) group (median (IQR) 1 (0 to 2))"
