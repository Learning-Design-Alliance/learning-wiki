`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of teacher-rated student outcomes in the randomized trial found no main effects but this moderated effect, plotted in Figure 1; only p-values were printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "There was a significant interaction of intervention, grade level, and free/reduced lunch status (b = -0.42, p = .036), such that a beneficial effect of intervention for the R-TSC Emotion Regulation outcome was observed for kindergarteners receiving free/reduced lunch (p = .036) but not for those who do not receive free/reduced lunch (p = .96; see Figure 1)."
