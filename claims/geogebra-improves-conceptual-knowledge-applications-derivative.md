## Evidence

### Ocal 2017

Mehmet Fatih Ocal. (2017). The Effect of Geogebra on Students’ Conceptual and Procedural Knowledge: The Case of Applications of Derivative. Higher Education Studies, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v7n2p67

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

True experimental pre-test post-test control group study with 31 experimental and 24 control second-grade pre-service mathematics students; independent samples t-test on post-test conceptual knowledge questions of the Applications of Derivative Test showed a "statistically significant difference" favoring Geogebra, with a 1.99-point mean difference.

> "there was a statistically significant difference in the mean scores for students’ conceptual knowledge scores from post-tests for the Geogebra group (M=7.26, SD=2.503) and the control group (M=5.33, SD=2.590); t(53)=2.786, p=.007"
