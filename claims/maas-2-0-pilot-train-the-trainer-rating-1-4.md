## Evidence

### Stamer Tjorven 2023

Stamer Tjorven, Essers Geurt, Steinhäuser Jost, Flägel Kristina. (2023). From summative MAAS Global to formative MAAS 2.0 – a workshop report. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2023-40/zma001591.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot evaluation within a train-the-trainer course for physicians with a license for post-graduate training (section 3.4). The 1.4 rating is a descriptive evaluation figure; no inferential statistics or sample size are printed.

> "The participants were immediately able to use the tool. As part of the training course, the same videos were shown and the learners came to the same results compared with previous training courses. In the accompanying evaluation, input using MAAS 2.0 was ranked of 1.4 on a scale from 1 (best) to 6 (worst)."
