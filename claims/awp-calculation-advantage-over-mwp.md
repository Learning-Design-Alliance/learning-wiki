## Evidence

### Fuchs et al. 2016

Lynn S. Fuchs, Robin F. Schumacher, Jessica Long, Jessica Namkung, Amelia S. Malone, Amber Wang, Carol L. Hamlett, Nancy C. Jordan, Robert S. Siegler, and Paul Changas. (2016). Effects of Intervention to Improve At-Risk Fourth Graders' Understanding, Calculations, and Word Problems with Fractions. Elementary School Journal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566704

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Comparison between the two intervention conditions on the Fraction Addition/Subtraction measure in the randomized trial. The article notes "the A-WP condition performed significantly stronger than the M-WP condition, as hypothesized (ES = 0.23)," consistent with A-WPs requiring adding/subtracting fractions.

> "On number line and NAEP, the two WP intervention conditions performed comparably. On calculations, however, the A-WP condition performed significantly stronger than the M-WP condition, as hypothesized (ES = 0.23)."
