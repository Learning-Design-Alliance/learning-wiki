## Evidence

### Shaw Tarren J. 2019

Shaw Tarren J., Yang Suann, Nash Troy R., Pigg Rachel M., Grim Jeffrey M. (2019). Knowing is half the battle: Assessments of both student perception and performance are necessary to successfully evaluate curricular transformation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210030

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-squared tests compared across years the frequencies of SALG survey response ranks for how much help specific curriculum components provided. The article reports students "increasingly report the instructional methods as responsible for large gains in their learning (p<0.0001)" (Fig 3).

> "Student responses shift over time, reducing the frequency of low rankings (No help, Little) relative to high rankings (Much, Great). Over four years, students increasingly report the instructional methods as responsible for large gains in their learning (p<0.0001)."
