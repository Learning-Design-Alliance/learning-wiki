## Evidence

### Yang Hao 2026

Yang Hao, Jing Xiaosha, Guan Yujia, Yang Min, Bai Ting, Xing Lingling, Mu Dan, Wang Xihong, Liu Hongqian. (2026). A BOPPPS‐PBL Nursing Innovation Course for Newly Recruited Nurses: A Single‐Group Quantitative Pilot Evaluation of Creativity, Self‐Efficacy and 1‐Year Project Outputs. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70734

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest–posttest pilot with all 60 newly recruited nurses analysed using paired-samples t-tests. The total creativity score rose with "mean difference 3.13, 95% CI 1.69–4.58" and Cohen's dz = 0.56, a medium effect. The uncontrolled design limits causal attribution.

> "The mean total creativity score increased from 20.50 ± 4.15 before the course to 23.63 ± 3.32 after the course (mean difference 3.13, 95% CI 1.69–4.58; t = 4.345, p < 0.001; Cohen’s dz = 0.56)."
