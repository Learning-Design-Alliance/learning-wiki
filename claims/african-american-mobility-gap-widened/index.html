Descriptive analysis of mobility indicators by race/ethnicity in Chapter 2 (Figures 6A-6B). The report states that by 2005 the summer in-mobility rate for African American elementary students had "increased to 18.9 percent" versus around 11 percent for white and Asian students, and the gap widened after 2000-01.

"By 2005, the in-mobility rate for African American students increased to 18.9 percent; it was around 11 percent for white and Asian students, and 12.7 percent for Latino students."
