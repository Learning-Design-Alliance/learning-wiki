### Taylor et al. 2018

Jonte C. Taylor, Ching-mei Tseng, Brian Hand, Angelique Murillo, William Therrien. (2018). Using Argument-based Science Inquiry to Improve Science Achievement for Students with Disabilities in Inclusive Classrooms. Journal of Science Education for Students with Disabilities, Vol. 21, No. 1. http://scholarworks.rit.edu/jsesd/vol21/iss1/2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test of pre/post ITBS science standard scores for 208 treatment students with IEPs. Mean improvement from 117.23 to 122.75, "t(208) = 7.7590, p < 0.001". No effect size is printed in this passage.

> "Treatment group students had a mean score improve- ment from 117.23 to 122.75. T-test results for the treatment group was significantly different at 0.05 alpha level [t(208) = 7.7590, p < 0.001]"
