## Evidence

### Adeel Sabir 2019

Adeel Sabir, Nisar A. Abbasi, Md Nurul Islam. (2019). An electronic data management and analysis application for ABET accreditation. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1901.05845](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1901.05845)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of three terms of assessment data for SO B in EE-340 (Figures 22-23), comparing direct and indirect scores after the laboratory corrective actions of subsection 4.1. The correlation is the authors' inference, supported by an absence of negative feedback about the laboratory sessions; no effect size is printed.

> "The chart shown in Figure 23 indicates a gradual by steady improvement in the direct scores while the indirect scores exhibit a mixed behavior. The quantitative improvement in the direct assessment scores can, to an extent, be directly correlated to the qualitative measures taken to improve the laboratory part of EE-340 over this period."
