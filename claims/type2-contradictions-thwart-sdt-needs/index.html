CHAT contradiction analysis mapping interview-identified barriers onto activity system elements. Rule-Object contradictions surfaced where mandatory interdisciplinary collaboration conflicted with authentic creation needs; Subject-Community contradictions centered on absence of sustained support after workshops.

"Tool-Subject contradictions emerged where platform complexity exceeded real-world capabilities despite workshop competence, manifesting as cognitive overload when simultaneously managing technology, pedagogy, and content."
