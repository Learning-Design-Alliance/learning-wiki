## Evidence

### Sengupta 2016

Sengupta, P., & Clark, D. (2016). Playing Modeling Games in the Science Classroom: The Case for Disciplinary Integration. Educational Technology, 56(3), 16–22. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.05094](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.05094)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

A study with SURGE NextG (Sengupta, Clark, Krinks, Killingsworth, & Brady, 2014) assigned class sections to grapher vs. no grapher conditions; the article reports a statistically significant pre-post gain advantage for the grapher condition, with no effect size printed.

> "Each class section was assigned to one of two graphing conditions (grapher vs. no grapher).  Students in the grapher sections showed significantly greater pre-post test gains on key Newtonian relationships pertaining to Newton’s second law of motion."
