## Evidence

### Berger B 2010

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-after assessment of 22 consecutive undergraduate students completing a comparable EBM course, powered at 90% expecting a five-point training effect. The article reports the pre-post change as "indicating the validity of the instrument".

> "The mean change gathered by the 22 students was from 4.8 (SD 1.2) before to 13.5 (SD 3.7) scores after the course (p < 0.0001) indicating the validity of the instrument."

### Berger B 2010 (2)

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the competence test scoring, with two independent researchers rating test results. Content validity was checked by an external EBM expert not involved in item construction.

> "Test results were rated by two independent researchers showing high interrater reliability (kappa 0.97)."
