## Evidence

### Singaravelu 2010

G. Singaravelu. (2010). Hybrid Learning in Enhancing Communicative Skill in English. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, Vol. 7 No. 1, April - June. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1098376

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Parallel-group experiment with 40 control and 40 experimental B.Ed in-service teachers. Table 1 reports post-test means of 10.62 (control, SD 3.23) and 16.56 (experimental, SD 3.21), with the article stating the "null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels". No effect size is printed.

> "The calculated ‘t’ value is (7.65) is greater than table value (1.96). Hence null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels. Hence there is significant difference between the post-test of control group and post-test of experimental group"
