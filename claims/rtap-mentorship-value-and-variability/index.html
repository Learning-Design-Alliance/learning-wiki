Nathan Kriha 2025¶

Nathan Kriha, Diarese George, Jonathan Feinstein, and Anna Skubel. (2025). Building Futures: Strengthening the Educator Workforce Through Teacher Apprenticeships. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED678833

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding on mentorship: mentors offered real-world examples, consistent feedback, and gradual release of responsibilities, yet "numerous apprentices expressed frustration at how little time they spent alongside their mentor, instead serving in long-term substitute roles or even as teachers of record."
