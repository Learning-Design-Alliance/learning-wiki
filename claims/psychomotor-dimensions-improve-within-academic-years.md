## Evidence

### Borrego-Balsalobre F.J. 2021

Borrego-Balsalobre F.J., Martínez-Moreno A., Morales-Baños V., Díaz-Suárez A. (2021). Influence of the Psychomotor Profile in the Improvement of Learning in Early Childhood Education. Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health 18(23):12655. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182312655

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-factor repeated-measures ANOVA (GLM) on the longitudinal sample of 82 pupils assessed twice per year; the article reports significant within-subjects time effects for seven dimensions at age 4, with "The rest of the dimensions do not indicate statistically significant differences" and no gender-time effects.

> "In the 4-year-olds, statistically significant differences are found in L, P, B, LC, AC, HC, and BSA in terms of time. The rest of the dimensions do not indicate statistically significant differences, as well as when compared to gender."
