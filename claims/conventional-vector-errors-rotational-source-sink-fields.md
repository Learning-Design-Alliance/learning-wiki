# Errors interpreting conventional unit vector representations concentrate in rotational fields and fields with both sources and sinks, while sink-only field geometries are interpreted well

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Items loading on the conventional-representation factor are predominantly rotational-field items. [→ Christoph Hoyer 2024](#christoph-hoyer-2024)

## Evidence

### Christoph Hoyer 2024

Christoph Hoyer, Raimund Girwidz. (2024). Vector representations and unit vector representations of fields — problems of understanding and possible teaching strategies. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis (minimum residual solution, oblimin rotation) of the 31 pretest items; the factor interpreted as conventional unit vector interpretation is carried by "rotational fields" items.

> "The items that load particularly high on this factor all show rotational fields (CV1, CV5, CV7, and CV8; field configurations VI, IX, VII, and X, see section II.3.h)."

### Christoph Hoyer 2024 (2)

Christoph Hoyer, Raimund Girwidz. (2024). Vector representations and unit vector representations of fields — problems of understanding and possible teaching strategies. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same factor analysis: items showing "field configurations only containing sinks" load on the centered unit vector factor rather than the difficult conventional-representation factor, indicating these geometries were interpreted well.

> "However, items CV2, CV4, and CV6 (field configurations I, II, and IV), which show field configurations only containing sinks, have almost no load on factor 6 but instead load on the factor regarding the interpretation of centered unit vectors (factor 7)."
