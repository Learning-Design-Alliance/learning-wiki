## Evidence

### Ulrich Trautwein 2026

Ulrich Trautwein, Janina Eberhart, Charlotte Dignath, Cavan V. Bonner, Brent W. Roberts. (2026). Self-Regulation Competence as a Superpower: An Integrated Framework for K-12. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10190-z

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

The review reports meta-analytic syntheses of SRL intervention studies, attributing the pooled effect sizes to Donker et al. (2014), with domain-specific mean Hedges's g values "of 1.25 for writing, 0.72 for science, 0.66 for mathematics, and 0.26 for reading".

> "Donker et al. (2014) found mean Hedges's g values of 1.25 for writing, 0.72 for science, 0.66 for mathematics, and 0.26 for reading, whereas earlier syntheses by Dignath et al. ( 2008) corroborate these benefits."
