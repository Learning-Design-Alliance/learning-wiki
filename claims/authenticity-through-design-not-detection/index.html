q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

This is the paper's central conceptual proposition, stated in the introduction and pursued throughout. It is a theoretical argument derived from an integrative review, analysis of sector guidance, and iterative practitioner critique, not an empirical test; the article offers no effect data for it.

"in an AI-mediated world, authenticity is achieved through design rather than detection, operationalised via process transparency, critical/ethical AI use, and assessable judgement."
