`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's overall conclusion from synthesizing 13 studies (8 RCTs, 5 nonrandomized) comparing PBL-type interventions with traditional teaching in oral radiology among undergraduate dental students. The conclusion states the "null hypothesis was rejected".

> "Through this systematic review and meta-analysis, the null hypothesis was rejected, thus indicating that there is a difference in PBL and traditional learning approach in improving acquisition of radiographic interpretation skills among dental students."
