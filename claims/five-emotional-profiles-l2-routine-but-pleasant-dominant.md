# Adult L2 learners' momentary emotions cluster into five profiles, dominated by a low-arousal 'Routine but Pleasant' state (57.8% of episodes), challenging anxiety-centred paradigms

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Clustering momentary emotion reports from 92 adult L2 learners yielded five profiles: Stressful and Tedious (6.3%), Enjoyable and Relaxed (10.2%), Intense but Engaging (18.7%), Routine but Pleasant (57.8%), and Passive and Unengaging (7.0%). [→ Peng Wang 2026](#peng-wang-2026)

## Evidence

### Peng Wang 2026

Peng Wang, Lesya Ganushchak, Camille Welie, Roel van Steensel. (2026). Emotions Are Not Random: Machine Learning Reveals Predictable Patterns in a 21-Day Second Language Learning Trajectory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10141-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Gaussian mixture clustering of pooled momentary emotion ratings from the 21-day EMA study; the five-cluster solution was selected on the lowest BIC. The article reports profile shares including "Routine but Pleasant (57.8%)" as the dominant cluster.

> "Clustering the momentary emotion reports yielded five distinct profiles summarizing co-occurring anxiety–enjoyment–boredom during learning episodes (Fig. 3b–c): Stressful and Tedious (6.3%), Enjoyable and Relaxed (10.2%), Intense but Engaging (18.7%), Routine but Pleasant (57.8%), and Passive and Unengaging (7.0%)."
