As a secondary analysis, the authors constructed a generalized linear model (Annex 3) exploring whether participant gender and age influence ownership perceptions and engagement intention; no dependence was found.

> "These results revealed how neither perception of ownership or intention to engage in open educational collaboration depend on gender or age of the respondent."
