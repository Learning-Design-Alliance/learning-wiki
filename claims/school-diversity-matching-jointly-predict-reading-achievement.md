# School racial/ethnic diversity and same-racial/ethnic peer proportion jointly predict higher reading achievement in kindergarten, with matching benefits concentrated in more diverse schools

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a single model, school diversity (ß = .05) and proportion same-racial/ethnic peers (ß = .05) were each significantly and positively associated with kindergarten reading achievement, though neither predicted reading when entered independently. [→ Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011](#benner-aprile-d-crosnoe-robert-2011)
`q2 i?` The diversity × matching interaction for reading was significant: having a larger proportion of same-racial/ethnic peers was associated with better reading achievement only for children in higher-diversity schools. [→ Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011 (2)](#benner-aprile-d-crosnoe-robert-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011

Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert. (2011). The Racial/Ethnic Composition of Elementary Schools and Young Children's Academic and Socioemotional Functioning. American Educational Research Journal. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4554342/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Stepwise linear regression analysis of ECLS-K kindergarten data (n = 21,260) with child, family, teacher, and school controls. In the joint model, "both were significantly associated with reaching test scores (see Table 2), suggesting the possibility of suppression effects."

> "In models in which school diversity and racial/ethnic matching were entered independently, neither significantly predicted reading achievement (ß = .03 and .01 for diversity and matching, respectively). When these school composition measures were examined together in a single model, however, both were significantly associated with reaching test scores (see Table 2), suggesting the possibility of suppression effects."

### Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert 2011 (2)

Benner Aprile D.; Crosnoe Robert. (2011). The Racial/Ethnic Composition of Elementary Schools and Young Children's Academic and Socioemotional Functioning. American Educational Research Journal. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4554342/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Interaction model with diversity × matching term added to the ECLS-K regression. "having a larger proportion of same-racial/ethnic peers was associated with better reading achievement test scores for children in higher diversity schools," as plotted in Figure 1a.

> "Inclusion of the interaction term revealed a significant diversity × matching coefficient (see Table 3). As seen in Figure 1a, the proportion of same-racial/ethnic peers in the school made little difference in children's reading achievement for those in schools with low levels of racial/ethnic diversity, but having a larger proportion of same-racial/ethnic peers was associated with better reading achievement test scores for children in higher diversity schools."
