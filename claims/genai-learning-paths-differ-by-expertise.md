# How workers learned to use GenAI differed by prior expertise: the technical team built on prior ML knowledge and documentation, while the content team learned through online sources, trial and error, and peers

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Concept participants built GenAI expertise largely on prior machine learning knowledge plus documentation, research papers, and blogs. [→ Aditya Johri 2025](#aditya-johri-2025)
`q2 i?` Content participants with low technical expertise learned GenAI through online sources, trying applications, and peer groups. [→ Aditya Johri 2025 (2)](#aditya-johri-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Aditya Johri 2025

Aditya Johri, Johannes Schleiss, Nupoor Ranade. (2025). Lessons for GenAI Literacy from a Field Study of Human-GenAI Augmentation in the Workplace. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1109/educon62633.2025.11016386](https://doi.org/10.1109/educon62633.2025.11016386)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview findings on the Concept project's learning practices; participants "relied to a large extent on their prior knowledge" from machine learning and data mining work, plus documentation and papers. Qualitative, no effect size.

> "To develop expertise on the use of GenAI, the participants relied to a large extent on their prior knowledge. They were already using machine learning and data mining on other projects and had knowledge of different algorithms and mod -els, and experience at using them."

### Aditya Johri 2025 (2)

Aditya Johri, Johannes Schleiss, Nupoor Ranade. (2025). Lessons for GenAI Literacy from a Field Study of Human-GenAI Augmentation in the Workplace. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1109/educon62633.2025.11016386](https://doi.org/10.1109/educon62633.2025.11016386)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview findings on the Content project's learning practices; participants "largely learned about GenAI through online sources and through trying out different applications" and from peers. Qualitative, no effect size.

> "They largely learned about GenAI through online sources and through trying out different applications. They also learned about it from peer groups and other friends in the industry who were using it."
