# A DL professional development course improved teachers' DL teaching practices and DL self-efficacy but had no effect on their self-innovativeness

## Evidence

### Vidergor Hava E 2023

Vidergor Hava E. (2023). The effect of teachers' self- innovativeness on accountability, distance learning self-efficacy, and teaching practices. Computers & Education, 199, 104777. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104777

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

MANCOVA comparing teachers with and without a DL course (Table 6, 200 teachers): DL teaching practices F = 12.94, η² = 0.06; teaching strategies sub-dimension F = 10.93, η² = 0.05; DL self-efficacy F = 7.59, η² = 0.04.

> "The main significant difference was detected in DL Teaching Practices in all its sub-dimensions, showing that teachers who had taken a DL course felt significantly more capable of using teaching strategies, managing their classes, and engaging their students in learning."

### Vidergor Hava E 2023 (2)

Vidergor Hava E. (2023). The effect of teachers' self- innovativeness on accountability, distance learning self-efficacy, and teaching practices. Computers & Education, 199, 104777. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104777

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the same MANCOVA (Table 6): the self-innovativeness difference by DL course participation was not statistically significant (F = 1.36, η² = 0.01); equivalence was not tested. In the path model, DL Course effects on self-efficacy (.154, p = .011) and practices (.113, p = .015) were low.

> "Teachers' Self-Innovativeness, although higher among teachers who had taken the DL course, was not significantly different from those who had not participated in a DL course."
