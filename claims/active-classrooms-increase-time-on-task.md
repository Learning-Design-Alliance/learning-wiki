## Evidence

### Bedard Chloe 2019

Bedard Chloe, St John Laura, Bremer Emily, Graham Jeffrey D., Cairney John. (2019). A systematic review and meta-analysis on the effects of physically active classrooms on educational and enjoyment outcomes in school age children. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0218633

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of seven studies (n = 541) measuring attention as time on task via structured direct observation. Table 2 reports SMD = 0.37 (95% CI 0.06 to 0.68); the primary-school subgroup (6 studies, n = 480) was SMD = 0.41 (0.03 to 0.79).

> "Data from seven studies were available to estimate the effect of active classrooms on time on task compared to traditional classrooms (See Table 2 and Fig 4). Pooled estimates show increases in time spent on task following active classrooms with a confidence interval that excludes no effect."
