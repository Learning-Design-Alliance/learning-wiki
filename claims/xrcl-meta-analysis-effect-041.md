The review reports, citing Zheng et al. (2018), a meta-analysis of ten articles comparing a collaborative learning prototype against a non-immersive-based approach on student performance. The review attributes the reported "mean effect size advantage of 0.41" to that meta-analysis and notes the authors "did not provide an overview of the included articles or information about sample sizes or the quality of the methods".

> "Although the mean effect size advantage of 0.41 for XRCL (labelled VRCL in their article) was encouraging, the authors did not provide an overview of the included articles or information about sample sizes or the quality of the methods."
