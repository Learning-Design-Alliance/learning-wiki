## Evidence

### Bouras Marwan 2026

Bouras Marwan, Senage Thomas, Meule Emma, David Charles-Henri, Guimbretière Guillaume, Cadiet Julien, Bazin Olivier, Rozec Bertrand, Lejus-Bourdeau Corinne, Vourc’h Mickael. (2026). Full-Scale high-fidelity simulation compared to procedural simulation workshop for veno-arterial ECMO cannulation during refractory cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09618-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 30 anesthesiology and critical care residents, randomized 1:1 to a 3-hour procedural simulation workshop or a full-day high-fidelity simulation session. The Mann-Whitney U test on the primary outcome found no significant difference in median cannulation time (908 s vs 836 s, "p = 0.71").

> "The procedural simulation workshop group showed a median cannulation time of 908 s (IQR 817–1015), compared to 836 s (IQR 801–1029) in the high-fidelity simulation group. This difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.71)"
