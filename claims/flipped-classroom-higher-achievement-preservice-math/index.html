İlknur Özpinar 2016¶

İlknur Özpinar, Arzu Aydoğan Yenmez, Semirhan Gökçe. (2016). Application of Flipped Classroom Model in the Instructional Technologies and Material Development Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 4, No. 12. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i12.1934

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study with 25 students per group, groups formed by prior achievement scores. The flipped group's posttest mean was 64.00 versus 57.40 for the control group, with "[t (48)=2.42, p<.05]"; no effect size is printed.

"It is understood from Table 9 that students' achievement posttest scores differed by the method applied [[t (48)=2.42, p<.05]. The achievement posttest score averages of the students in the control and experimental groups are 57.40 and 64.00 respectively."
