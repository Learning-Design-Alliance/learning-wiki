> "In our study, the frequency of False Positives (categories 1, 2 and 3) is no more than 1% of the total number of responses. It is posited that this surprising statistic is the fruit of the very structure of the enriched three-tier test, which includes justification as an essential part of the evaluation process, discouraging the students from answering at random."
