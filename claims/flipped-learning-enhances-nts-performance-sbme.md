## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021

Moll-Khosrawi Parisa, Zöllner Christian, Cencin Nadine, Schulte-Uentrop Leonie. (2021). Flipped learning enhances non-technical skill performance in simulation-based education: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02766-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled simulation trial at the University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf; intervention group n = 35 rated by instructors with AS-NTS before and after flipped learning. The article reports significant pre-post gains, e.g. "dimension one: t (44) = 5.63, p < .001", with follow-up partial η² of 0.42, 0.31 and 0.36 for dimensions one to three.

> "The AS-NTS scores of the intervention group enhanced significantly after the intervention on all dimensions (dimension one: t (44) = 5.63, p < .001; dimension two: t (44) = 4.47, p < .001; dimension three: t (44) = 4.94, p < .001)."
