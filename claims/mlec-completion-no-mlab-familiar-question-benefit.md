# Completing the microbiology lecture course did not significantly improve MLab exam performance on familiar questions for students who had versus had not taken it

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across three separate MLec prerequisite sections, students who completed MLec showed no statistically significant difference in performance on very familiar or familiar MLab exam questions compared with students who had not. [→ Sato Brian K. 2017](#sato-brian-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Sato Brian K. 2017

Sato Brian K., Lee Amanda K., Alam Usman, Dang Jennifer V., Dacanay Samantha J., Morgado Pedro, Pirino Giorgia, Brunner Jo Ellen, Castillo Leanne A., Chan Valerie W., Sandholtz Judith H. (2017). What’s in a Prerequisite? A Mixed-Methods Approach to Identifying the Impact of a Prerequisite Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0260

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative t-test analysis of MLab exam data from Fall 2014 (n = 94) and Winter 2015 (n = 76), with questions normalized and classified VF, F, or NF. The article reports "no statistical difference in performance on VF or F questions" between MLec completers and non-completers; no effect size is printed.

> "In the context of three separate MLec prerequisite sections, all of the conditions tested showed no statistical difference in performance on VF or F questions for students who had completed MLec versus those who had not"
