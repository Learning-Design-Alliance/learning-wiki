### Selen Galiç 2026

Selen Galiç, Mathias Tejera, Sebastián Parodi, Markus Hohenwarter, Zsolt Lavicza. (2026). Preservice mathematics teachers' noticing in AI-based simulations: transitions among attending, interpreting, and shaping. ZDM – Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01805-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding and transition tabulation of 304 noticing statements from 17 preservice teachers across three weeks of AI-based simulation interactions. The article reports this distributional finding and, in discussion, that noticing "did not operate as a linear sequence, but rather as a dynamically coordinated and iterative process".

> "The dis- tribution of transitions shows that PTs’ noticing was organ- ised through both within-dimension continuity and between- dimension connections."
