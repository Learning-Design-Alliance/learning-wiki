Evidence¶

Koch 2025¶

Koch, M., & Peters Hinton, V. (2025). Voices of Undergraduate Students Taking Introductory Statistics Courses [Project Report]. Digital Promise.

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study in which 50 students gave a guided tour of Lumen One and answered open-ended questions. The report states "A majority of students (72% - 88%) navigated to these features unprompted" and that Readiness and Self Checks were reported as most helpful.

"A majority of students (72% - 88%) navigated to these features unprompted when interviewers asked them to show how they used Lumen One, and students reported these two components as most helpful for their learning."
