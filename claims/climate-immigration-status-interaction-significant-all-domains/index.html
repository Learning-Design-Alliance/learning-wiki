Evidence¶

Anatolia Batruch 2026¶

Anatolia Batruch, Nicolas Sommet, Eva G.T. Green. (2026). Teachers' immigration attitudes and students' performance. Learning and Instruction 102 (2026) 102303. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102303

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Country fixed-effects regression with school-clustered robust standard errors on PISA 2018 plausible values (46,740 students, 1,544 schools, 7 OECD countries). The article reports the omnibus interaction was "significant for all dependent variables"; Table 3 prints F(2, 845) = 11.83 (science), 6.55 (math), 18.37 (reading).

"As seen in Table 3 (line 1), the omnibus tests of the inclusionary school diversity climate × immigration status were significant for all dependent variables."
