## Evidence

### Garrud Paul 2012

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective study of four GEM entry cohorts (2003–6); course progress files of all students with unsatisfactory progress or premature departure were hand-searched into a customised database. The article reports "53 (14.6%) were identified for this study" and no significant difference versus the UG course (p = 0.30).

> "A total of 362 students entered the GEM programme over the 4 years 2003–6. In all, 53 (14.6%) were identified for this study."
