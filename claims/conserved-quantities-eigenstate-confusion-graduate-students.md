### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Investigating Student Difficulties with Time dependence of Expectation Values in Quantum Mechanics. 2013 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Multiple-choice question 2 on conserved quantities administered to the 39 graduate students; 46% answered correctly, while 18% incorrectly treated lack of explicit time dependence as implying conservation and 42% incorrectly treated momentum-eigenstate occupancy as implying conservation.

> "Table 1 shows that 46% of students selected the correct answer (choice A) for question 2. Table 1 also shows that 18% and 42% of the graduate students incorrectly claimed that in question 2 statements (2) and (3), respectively, are correct."
