### Odden 2019

Odden, Lockwood, and Caballero. (2019). Physics Computational Literacy: An Exploratory Case Study Using Computational Essays. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020152)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Exploratory case study of the 2018 computational essay implementation in a 3rd-semester electricity and magnetism course: "17 students participated in this initial implementation of computational essays", producing 11 essays analyzed via thematic analysis of interviews and essays. Student Jeffry described debugging: "The problem arises when the error is not in the math or in the reasoning but in the numerical implementation".

> "In total, 17 students participated in this initial implementation of computational essays, working singly or in pairs to produce a total of 11 completed essays."
