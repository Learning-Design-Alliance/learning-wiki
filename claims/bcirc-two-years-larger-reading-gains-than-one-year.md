# Two years of BCIRC exposure is associated with larger reading gains than one year (effect size 0.87 significant versus 0.33 non-significant)

### Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition 2007

Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED405428

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup findings from the Calderón, Hertz-Lazarowitz, and Slavin (1998) quasi-experiment: students with two years of BCIRC scored 36.83 versus 23.83 in comparison, with a printed effect size of 0.87 ("0.87 Statistically significant +31"). The WWC notes a clustering correction did not change this significance.
