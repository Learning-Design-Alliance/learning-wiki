Pre-post comparison using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in the same 67-patient cohort. The article reports these improvements were "observed significantly in both the experienced and naïve urologist groups"; only descriptive medians with IQR are printed, no effect sizes.

> "IPSS total decreased by a median of 9.0 (IQR 5.0–14.0), which was a 50.0% reduction. IPSS QOL decreased by a median of 2.0 (IQR 1.0–2.5), which was a 40.0% reduction."
