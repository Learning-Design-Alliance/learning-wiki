Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2008¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: JOBSTART. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial by Cave et al. (1993) in 13 sites in nine states; 2,312 youth randomly assigned and 1,941 responding to the 48-month follow-up. Appendix A3 reports a 13.4 percentage-point mean difference, effect size 0.36 via the Cox Index, statistically significant, improvement index "+14".

"Four years after random assignment, 42% of JOBSTART youth had earned a high school diploma or GED certificate, compared with 29% of control group youth."
