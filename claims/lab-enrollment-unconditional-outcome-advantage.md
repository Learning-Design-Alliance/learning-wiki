## Evidence

### DeFeo 2020

DeFeo, D. J., Bibler, A., & Gerken, S. (2020). The Effect of a Paired Lab on Course Completion and Grades in Nonmajors Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 19(3). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-03-0041

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive comparison of institutional records for 3736 Lab and 7057 No Lab students in first-attempt BIOL 102 (2008–2018). The text reports the "about 8.5%" DNF rate for Lab students versus 13.0% for No Lab students; Table 1 shows mean grades of 2.668 versus 2.344 (0–4 scale).

> "Figure 1 illustrates that the proportion of Lab students who did not finish the course is about 8.5%, which is 4.5 percentage points lower than the proportion of No Lab students, 13.0%."
