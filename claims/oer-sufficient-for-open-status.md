### Chiappe 2017

Chiappe, A. and Lee, L.L. (2017). Open Teaching: a New Way on E-learning?. The Electronic Journal of e-Learning, 15(5), pp.369-383. www.ejel.org. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1157965

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Meta-synthesis found OER in 57.5% of evidence items. The review reports that "incorporating OER into an educational practice appears to be a sufficient reason to confer it the status of 'open'", a widely accepted but questionable assertion since the open element is content, not teaching practices.

> "Thus, the research review has shown that incorporating OER into an educational practice appears to be a sufficient reason to confer it the status of "open"."
