Evidence¶

Badali 2023¶

Badali, S., Rawson, K.A. & Dunlosky, J. (2023). How do Students Regulate Their Use of Multiple Choice Practice Tests?. Educational Psychology Review 35, 43. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09761-1

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experiments with undergraduate participants practicing German-English word pairs under self-regulated learning conditions with multiple-choice (SRL-MC) and cued-recall (SRL-CR) options. The article reports that participants "correctly answered each item about one time during practice, similar to students’ use of cued-recall questions." No effect size is printed for this behavioral pattern.

"Participants chose to complete multiple-choice questions until they correctly answered each item about one time during practice, similar to students’ use of cued-recall questions."
