`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariable regression analysis in the LitCog study (n = 882) showing the health literacy coefficient for task performance dropped from "β = −28.9" to "β = −8.5" after entering fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities. No standardized effect size is printed for the reduction itself.

> "In multivariable analyses, the association between health literacy and task performance was substantially reduced once fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities were entered into models (without cognitive abilities: β = −28.9, 95 % Confidence Interval (CI) -31.4 to −26.4, p; with cognitive abilities: β = −8.5, 95 % CI −10.9 to −6.0)."
