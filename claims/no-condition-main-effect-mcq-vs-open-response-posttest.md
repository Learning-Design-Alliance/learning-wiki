# Learning-by-doing condition (MCQ only, open-response only, or both) shows no significant main effect on tutors' posttest performance in advocacy lessons

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A posttest-only RCT found no statistically significant main effect of learning-by-doing condition on posttest scores, F(2, 717) = 0.27, p = .765. [→ Danielle R. Thomas 2025](#danielle-r-thomas-2025)

## Evidence

### Danielle R. Thomas 2025

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Sanjit Kakarla, Jionghao Lin, Shambhavi Bhushan, Boyuan Guo, Erin Gatz, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2025. Does Multiple Choice Have a Future in the Age of Generative AI? A Posttest-only RCT. In LAK25: The 15th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2025), March 03–07, 2025, Dublin, Ireland. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706468.3706530

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on 790 lesson completions by 234 randomly assigned tutors across three learning-by-doing conditions in six advocacy lessons; the article reports "no statistically significant main effect of condition" with F(2, 717) = 0.27, p = .765. No effect size is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "in an analysis of variance, we did not find a statistically significant main effect of condition, F(2, 717) = 0.27, p = .765."
