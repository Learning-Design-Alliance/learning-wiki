Evidence¶

Soboleva et al. 2018¶

Elena V. Soboleva, Nikita L. Karavaev, Nadezhda V. Shalaginova, Marina S. Perevozchikova. (2018). Improvement of the Robotics Cross-Cutting Course for Training of Specialists in Professions of the Future. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.4.845

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Control stage of the pedagogical experiment: differences in competence formation between control and experimental groups were evaluated with the Pearson chi-square criterion at significance level α = 0.05 with 3 degrees of freedom; the null hypothesis was rejected and the alternative accepted, confirming "the qualitative difference in the level of formation of cross-professional competence". No effect size is printed.

"Thus, the experimental assessment confirms the qualitative difference in the level of formation of cross-professional competence in the control and experimental groups on the example of training specialists of the profession of the future “Medical robot designer”."

Soboleva et al. 2018 (2)¶

Elena V. Soboleva, Nikita L. Karavaev, Nadezhda V. Shalaginova, Marina S. Perevozchikova. (2018). Improvement of the Robotics Cross-Cutting Course for Training of Specialists in Professions of the Future. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.4.845

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Forming-stage control work (Table 5): of 100 experimental-group students, 32 received excellent and 40 good ratings, versus 18 excellent and 28 good among 104 control-group students; the control group had more satisfactory (40) and unsatisfactory (18) ratings. Ratings were assigned by criteria for systems thinking, project management and cross-industry communication tasks. Descriptive counts only; no effect size is printed.
