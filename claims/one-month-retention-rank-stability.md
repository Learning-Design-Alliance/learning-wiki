## Evidence

### Pyle 1917

Pyle, W. H. (1917). Retention as Related to Repetition. The Journal of Educational Psychology.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The author retested subjects F and C from experiment III one month after learning using the even-numbered tests, finding practically the same retention; a related class-level check found at least three-out-of-four rank stability a month after material was given. The author takes this as evidence that learning is the important thing, absent reviewing.

> "We have also tested the retention of the members of large classes a month after matter was given, and found the chances to be at least three out of four that students would maintain the same rank in long retention that they had in immediate reproduction."
