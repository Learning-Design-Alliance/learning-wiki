## Evidence

### Starkey et al. 2013

Prentice Starkey, Alice Klein, & Lydia DeFlorio. (2013). Changing the Developmental Trajectory in Early Math Through a Two-Year Preschool Math Intervention. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom observation study using the Early Mathematics Classroom Observation (EMCO); ANOVAs of mean minutes of math (MOM) support showed no baseline differences among the three conditions before the treatment difference emerged.

> "Treatment teachers, relative to controls, provided significantly more MOM through focal, scaffolded, small-group activities during both the pre-pre-K and pre-K years (ps < .01)."
