Evidence¶

Ma 2023¶

Ma, C., Song, M., & Zhao, X. (2023). The impact of executive functions on English academic performance among Chinese primary school students: A network analysis. https://www.nwnu.edu.cn

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the observed negative bridge between working memory span and listening, which "contradicting prior research that suggested a positive association"; the Cognitive Filter Model and Viterbori et al.'s overload finding are cited as possible mechanisms.

"This discrepancy may be attributed to common effect relationships within the network (Hitchcock & Pearl, 2001), where both English writing and listening are influenced by working memory span (Epskamp & Fried, 2018)."
