Evidence¶

Mariale M. Hardiman 2017¶

Mariale M. Hardiman, Ranjini Mahinda JohnBull, Deborah Carran. (2017). The Effects of Arts-Integrated Instruction on Students' Memory for Science Content: Results from a Randomized Control Trial Study. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the American Educational Research Association. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED576190

q3 · i? · causal · r2

2 × 2 factorial ANOVA (instructional method × unit pair) on percent of retained content in the randomized control trial. Both the unit-pair main effect and the method-by-unit-pair interaction were non-significant as printed; no effect size was reported.

"For unit pairs, a non-statistically significant difference was observed on percent of content retained; (MAS-LS = 59.61%, MCH-ES = 73.78%), F(1, 506) = 1.801, p = .180. No interaction effect was observed between instructional method and unit pairs, F(1, 506) = 0.275, p = 0.600."
