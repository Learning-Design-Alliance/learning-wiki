SEM model comparison for the delayed post-test administered 6 to 8 weeks after the immediate test in the same 235-student correlational study; the robust scaled chi-squared difference test supported the profiles model. No standardized effect size is printed.

"we found that latent profiles significantly predicted the delayed knowledge test outcome, ∆χ 2(5) = 37.79, p < .001. The inclusion of the latent profiles explained approximately 16% of the variance in the delayed knowledge test, ∆R2 = 0.16."
