`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of correlation testing across included studies. The review reports "149 studies that did not test for correlation between NTS and TS despite including both skill sets in their analysis"; the majority of these were NTS-based interventions interpreted only via TS improvement, without statistical analysis of the association.

> "Of the 192 included manuscripts, we identified 149 studies that did not test for correlation between NTS and TS despite including both skill sets in their analysis."
