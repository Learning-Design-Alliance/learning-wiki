## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SEM results for the anxiety group confirmed the TAM applies: PEOU→PU (β = 0.652), PEOU→ATT (β = 0.240), PU→ATT (β = 0.698), and ATT→CI (β = 0.886) were all significant and positive. [→ Hu Xuan 2022](#hu-xuan-2022)

## Evidence

### Hu Xuan 2022

Hu Xuan, Zhang Jiaqi, He Shuang, Zhu Ruilin, Shen Shan, Liu Bingsheng. (2022). E-learning intention of students with anxiety: Evidence from the first wave of COVID-19 pandemic in China. Journal of Affective Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2022.04.121

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-step SEM (AMOS 26) on the anxiety group (N = 368); model fit indices printed as chi-square 3.609, NNFI 0.975, CFI 0.982, RMSEA 0.071. The article states "all the hypotheses in the TAM model for the anxiety group are supported" with the path coefficients listed above; no standardized effect size beyond path coefficients is printed.

> "the results (Fig. 1b) confirm that the continuance intention in e-learning for the anxiety group could be explained using the TAM model as well. Similar to the results for the non-anxiety group, all the hypotheses in the TAM model for the anxiety group are supported."
