## Evidence

### Brevik A 2026

Brevik A, Jerpseth H and Lafontan SR (2026). Large language models as grading assistants in public health education: a method-comparison study of essay-style exam assessment. Front. Educ. 11:1904452. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1904452

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Method-comparison study grading 32 anonymized essay submissions from a master's public health course. The weighted Cohen's kappa analysis found "ChatGPT showed the strongest agreement with human examiners (weighted kappa 0.718, 95% CI 0.578–0.859)"; exact match was 50.0% and near match (±1) cumulative 90.6% (Table 2).

> "In the single-file upload setting, ChatGPT showed the strongest agreement with human examiners (weighted kappa 0.718, 95% CI 0.578–0.859), followed by Kimi (weighted kappa 0.571, 95% CI 0.373–0.768)."
