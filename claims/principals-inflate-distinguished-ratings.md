# Principals were far more likely than trained external observers to rate the same instruction Distinguished rather than Proficient

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across all components, principals were significantly more likely than observers to call Proficient-level practice Distinguished. [→ Sartain 2011](#sartain-2011)
`q2 i?` 52 percent of simultaneously observed teachers received at least one Distinguished rating from principals versus 24 percent from observers. [→ Sartain 2011 (2)](#sartain-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Sartain 2011

Sartain, L., Stoelinga, S. R., & Brown, E. R., with Luppescu, S., Kapadia Matsko, K., Miller, F. K., Durwood, C. E., Jiang, J. Y., & Glazer, D. (2011). Rethinking Teacher Evaluation in Chicago: Lessons Learned from Classroom Observations, Principal-Teacher Conferences, and District Implementation. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/rethinking-teacher-evaluation-chicago-lessons-learned-classroom-observations-principal

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Statistical comparison of 501 matched principal-observer ratings of the same lesson found principals "significantly more likely to call practice Distinguished when observers rated the same instruction as Proficient." Figure 3 shows an odds ratio of 6.18, significant at the .01 level; the article prints no standardized effect size for this contrast.

> "Across all components, principals were also significantly more likely to call practice Distinguished when observers rated the same instruction as Proficient"

### Sartain 2011 (2)

Sartain, L., Stoelinga, S. R., & Brown, E. R., with Luppescu, S., Kapadia Matsko, K., Miller, F. K., Durwood, C. E., Jiang, J. Y., & Glazer, D. (2011). Rethinking Teacher Evaluation in Chicago: Lessons Learned from Classroom Observations, Principal-Teacher Conferences, and District Implementation. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/rethinking-teacher-evaluation-chicago-lessons-learned-classroom-observations-principal

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive ratings data from the matched-observation reliability sample of 257 teachers: "52 percent received at least one Distinguished rating from principals, but only 24 percent received a Distinguished rating from observers." Overall, principals gave 17 percent Distinguished ratings versus 3 percent for observers.

> "Of the 257 teachers observed simultaneously by the principal and observer, 52 percent received at least one Distinguished rating from principals, but only 24 percent received a Distinguished rating from observers."
