## Evidence

### Tan Jing 2021

Tan Jing, Mao Jie, Jiang Yizhang, Gao Ming. (2021). The Influence of Academic Emotions on Learning Effects: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18189678

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects main-effect test of the review's nine intervention studies (Table 3: k = 81, N = 5708, p < 0.00001, d = 0.55, 95% CI [0.40, 0.69]) for learning performance. The review reports the "average effect size of learners' academic emotions was 0.55".

> "In terms of learning performance, the average effect size of learners' academic emotions was 0.55. Both the upper and lower limits of 95%CI were greater than 0, indicating that the effect size was not caused by accidental factors."
