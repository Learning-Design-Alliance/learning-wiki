## Evidence

### Kudish Philip 2016

Kudish Philip, Shores Robin, McClung Alex, Smulyan Lisa, Vallen Elizabeth A., Siwicki Kathleen K. (2016). Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on longitudinal grade data spanning 10 years, reported in Results. Across all semesters, Biology 1 averages were 2.8 (non-URM) vs 2.2 (URM) and Biology 2 were 3.1 vs 2.6; no significant overall grade change after implementation was found.

> "In Biology 2, however, a significant interaction between URM status and pre- versus postprogram years (linear mixed model applied to arcsine-transformed grades, p < 0.05) suggests that the historical grade gap between URM and non-URM students narrowed in this course."
