## Evidence

### Dearborn 1918

Dearborn, W. F., and Brewer, J. M. (1918). Methods and Results of a Class Experiment in Learning. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. IX, No. 2. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/9/2/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control-group comparison in the results section: a practiced class and two control classes took the same tests twelve working days apart. The digit-letter substitution gain of the practiced group was "12%", two percent below the unpracticed control group's gain.

> "As a matter of fact the actual per cent, of improvement recorded in this test is 12%, which is actually two per cent, less than the gain made by the unpracticed or control group."
