# Under certain conditions the Bayesian adaptive design yields superior inference with less than one-third the sample size of a standard design

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In simulation, there is more than a 90 percent chance that inference from the Bayesian adaptive design is superior to inference from a standard design, using less than one-third the sample size, under described conditions. [→ Mariel McKenzie Finucane 2015](#mariel-mckenzie-finucane-2015)

Simulation study comparing the Bayesian adaptive design against a standard design, using a randomized experiment of students in an online course as the motivating example. The paper reports the probability advantage and sample-size reduction under stated conditions; no effect size is printed.
