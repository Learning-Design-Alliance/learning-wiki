### Fazlollahi AM 2023

Cohort study (secondary analysis of an RCT) of 46 medical students trained on the NeuroVR simulator with the Virtual Operative Assistant tutor versus 23 controls, comparing 180 metrics between baseline and fifth attempt. The article reports "additional performance change in 32 metrics over the entire procedure and 20 metrics during tumor removal" unique to the AI group.
