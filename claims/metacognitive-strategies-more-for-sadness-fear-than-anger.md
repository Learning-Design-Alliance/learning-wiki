## Evidence

### Davis 2010

Davis, E. L., Levine, L. J., Lench, H. C., & Quas, J. A. (2010). Metacognitive Emotion Regulation: Children’s Awareness that Changing Thoughts and Goals Can Alleviate Negative Emotions. Emotion, 10(4), 498–510. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018428

`q3 · i2` · `qualitative · r3`

Mixed ANOVA on strategy frequencies from 92 children interviewed about autobiographical sad, angry, and scary events. The omnibus emotion × strategy interaction was significant, F(12, 1080) = 4.75, p < .001, η2p = .05. Post hoc tests showed metacognitive strategies more often for sadness than anger, t(91) = 3.25, p < .01, and fear than anger, t(91) = 3.68, p < .001.

> "The results showed significant main effects of emotion regulation strategy, F(6, 540) = 17.26, p < .001, η2p = .16, and emotion, F(2, 180) = 4.85, p < .01, η2p = .05, and an emotion by strategy interaction, F(12, 1080) = 4.75, p < .001, η2p = .05."
