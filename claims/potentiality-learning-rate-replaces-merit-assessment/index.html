q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Definitional/proposal statement from the Beyond Meritocracy section: potentiality is operationalised as "a measure of an individual’s learning rate" via dynamic formative assessments of personal trajectories. The article stresses it does not measure absolute potential; no empirical test is reported.

"it proposes to operationalise the concept of potentiality as a measure of an individual’s learning rate, which is assessed through dynamic, formative assessments that reﬂect personal learning trajectories."

Related Claims¶

  • Because cognitive, social and emotional processing show no clear demarcation in the brain and dynamically interact with the environment, the ISEE Assessment recommends an integrative CASE (cognitive, academic, social, emotional) approach across learning, teaching and assessment — related
  • The ISEE Assessment finds that meritocracy as the great equalizer perpetuates educational, social and economic exclusion and proposes shifting from static merit-based assessment to potentiality — a broader claim this one bears on
