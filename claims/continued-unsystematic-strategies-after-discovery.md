The article reports, citing Kuhn and Phelps (1982), a microgenetic study of 10- and 11-year-olds' experimentation strategies over 11 weekly sessions. Schauble (1990) reported similar within- and between-subject variability over 8 weekly sessions, with causal beliefs fading rather than being conclusively rejected.

> "They found that even after children dis- covered a systematic experimentation strategy, they con- tinued to use a variety of unsystematic strategies as well. This was true of literally every subject in their study."
