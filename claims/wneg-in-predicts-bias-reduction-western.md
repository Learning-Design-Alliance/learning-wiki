## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In the Loss frame, the Wneg_in parameter predicted post-vs-pre intergroup impression change (Spearman rho = −0.37, p = 0.013), specifically the change in ingroup impression. [→ Yuqing Zhou 2025](#yuqing-zhou-2025)

## Evidence

### Yuqing Zhou 2025

Yuqing Zhou, Björn Lindström, Alexander Soutschek, Pyungwon Kang, Shihui Han, Philippe N. Tobler & Grit Hein. (2025). Learning reduces ingroup bias more with perceived losses than gains across cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00362-x

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise regression on Western Sample (Study 1) learning parameters. The Wneg_in parameter specifically predicted ingroup impression change (rho = −0.36, p = 0.011) but not outgroup impression change (rho = −0.16, p = 0.27); robust to outlier removal (t(46)= −2.51, p = 0.016).

> "In the Loss frame, the stepwise regression revealed only a signiﬁcant effect of the weight given to the negative ingroup prediction errors, i.e., the Wneg_in parameter ( β = −0.13, t(52)= −2.0, p = 0.05, Fig. 4a, Spearman correlation: rho = −0.37, p = 0.013)"

### Yuqing Zhou 2025 (2)

Yuqing Zhou, Björn Lindström, Alexander Soutschek, Pyungwon Kang, Shihui Han, Philippe N. Tobler & Grit Hein. (2025). Learning reduces ingroup bias more with perceived losses than gains across cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00362-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Moderation analysis in the Western sample with frame as moderator. The overall three-way moderation was marginal (β = 0.20, t(108)= 1.91, p = 0.059); the ingroup-specific moderation was significant, the outgroup one was not.

> "framing signiﬁcantly moderated the relationship betweenWneg_in and changes in ingroup impressions (i.e., the decrease in ingroup impression β = −0.23, t(107)= −2.32, p = 0.022, Fig. 4b), but not the relationship between Wneg_in and the change in outgroup impression (β =0 . 0 8 ,t(107)= 0.85, p = 0.40, Fig. 4c)"
