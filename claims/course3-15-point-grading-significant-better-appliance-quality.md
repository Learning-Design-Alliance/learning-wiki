# In course 3 (Fränkel functional regulator type 3), the 15-point grading group produced significantly better appliances than the pass/fail group (p = 0.0222)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In course 3, appliance quality was statistically significantly and clinically meaningfully higher in the test group (mean 10.93 ± 1.15) than the control group (mean 10.14 ± 1.03), p = 0.0222. [→ Bialas MJ 2025](#bialas-mj-2025)

## Evidence

### Bialas MJ 2025

Bialas MJ, Schmid JQ, Middelberg C, Stamm T, Blanck‐Lubarsch M. (2025). 15‐Point versus pass/fail grading in orthodontic education: A randomized controlled trial. J Dent Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13815

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results of the RCT's third course (Fränkel's functional regulator type 3, n = 40, 20 per group) rated by calibrated dentists on a 15-point scale; Mann–Whitney U comparison showed "a statistically significant and clinically meaningful difference" favoring the test group.

> "Only in course 3, there was a statistically significant and clinically meaningful difference between the control and test groups (p = 0.0222) with a mean grading value of 10.14 ± 1.03 for the control group and a mean value of 10.93 ± 1.15 for the test group."
