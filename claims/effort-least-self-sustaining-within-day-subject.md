## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Joint multilevel structural equation model (Model 3) of 3,490 time-points from 231 students tested autoregressive paths for both lag types. Effort exertion showed no self-sustaining effect "within days or within school-subjects" and the two effects were not significantly different.

> "Effort exertion at time T was not sustained within days or within school-subjects (with D1 and S1 ρ = .07; p = .043, and ρ = .08; p = .071). The effects were not significantly different (∆B = -.02; p = .723)."
