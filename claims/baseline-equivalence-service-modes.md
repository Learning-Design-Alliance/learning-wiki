## Evidence

### Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L 2021

Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L. (2021). Serving Children and Adolescents in Need during the COVID-19 Pandemic: Evaluation of Service-Learning Subjects with and without Face-to-Face Interaction. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18042114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

MANOVA on 10 positive youth development attributes with service mode as fixed factor, using the 216 matched OOE cases. The article reports "no significant differences in service mode for positive youth development attributes." Similar null results were found for service leadership qualities and life satisfaction.

> "Overall, the results showed no significant differences in service mode for positive youth development attributes (F(10,269) = 1.28, ηp2 = 0.05; p > 0.05)."
