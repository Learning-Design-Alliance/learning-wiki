Evidence¶

Akpinar 2014¶

Akpinar, Y. (2014). Different modes of digital learning object use in school settings: Do we design for individual or collaborative learning? International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, 10(3), 87-95. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1747

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' observational interpretation from researcher notes and usability questionnaires, not a tested result; the article explicitly says "it is not a statistically significant difference" and offers the feedback mechanism as a possible explanation. The authors suggest delayed feedback might have fostered more reflective negotiation.

"the collaboratively studied students seem to benefit less from the LOs possibly due to the fact that the immediate feedback either in the form of textual explanation or as the visual consequence of the simulations provided by the LOs moderated the effect of social interaction between the pairs"
