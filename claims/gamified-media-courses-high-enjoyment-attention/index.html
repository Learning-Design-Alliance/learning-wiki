q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive survey data from the study's 37-question online instrument administered in week seven and week twelve of two gamified courses. "91% reported that they found the course enjoyable or very enjoyable" at midpoint, with 88% at term end; no inferential test is reported.

"At the midpoint, 91% reported that they found the course enjoyable or very enjoyable while 88% reported the same findings at the end of the term."
