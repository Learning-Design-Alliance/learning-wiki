## Subclaims
`q3 i?` SDTS total recorded training time was significantly shorter than the 180-minute traditional session, averaging 77.34 ± 22.00 min for incision/suturing and 84.94 ± 20.64 min for suture removal/dressing change. [→ Liu Boqiang 2026](#liu-boqiang-2026)

## Evidence

### Liu Boqiang 2026

Liu Boqiang, Gong Bin, Zhang Xiaochen, Lu Chen, Zheng Bin, Yang Jin. (2026). Self-directed teaching system driven by video analysis feedback improves surgical skill training for clinical interns: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09104-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Training durations were logged through the SDTS backend for the experimental group. The article reports "the training duration in the SDTS group was significantly shorter (P < 0.001)" compared with the 180-minute traditional session.

> "Thus, the total recorded instructional time in the SDTS group was significantly shorter, reflecting greater flexibility in training time utilization under the self-paced SDTS model."
