## Evidence

### Sparn Moritz B. 2025

Sparn Moritz B., Chatziisaak Dimitrios, Warschkow Rene, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2025). Does mental imagery make a difference in virtual reality simulation training? Results of a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy 39(10). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12012-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial at the 2023 Davos Course: 69 MI-group and 41 control participants performed a porcine cholecystectomy in a box-trainer, assessed with a modified OSATS score by blinded raters. Table 3 reports MI-group estimates of "0.44 (0.03–0.85)" for Respect for tissue (p = 0.037) and "0.41 (0.03–0.80)" for Use of the assistant (p = 0.035) from mixed effects regression.

> "After correction for previous experience between MI group and control group, participants in the MI group performed statistically significantly better in the two subscales “Tissue Respect” and “Use of the Assistant”."
