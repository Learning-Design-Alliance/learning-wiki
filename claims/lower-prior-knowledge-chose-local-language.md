### Phenyo Phemelo Moletsane 2026

Phenyo Phemelo Moletsane, Michael W. Asher, Christine Kwon, Paulo F. Carvalho, and Amy Ogan. (2026). Inclusive Mobile Learning: How Technology-Enabled Language Choice Supports Multilingual Students. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791139

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression on 1,326 learners who attempted at least eight steps, using Week 1 average test scores as a prior-knowledge proxy. Average Week 1 scores were 44% for Leb Lango versus 51% for English and Hybrid choosers; "the odds of selecting English instead of Leb Lango increased by 49%" per SD.

> "for each standard deviation increase in Week 1 score, the odds of selecting English instead of Leb Lango increased by 49% (𝑏= 0.40, 𝑂𝑅= 1.49, 𝑝<. 0001)"
