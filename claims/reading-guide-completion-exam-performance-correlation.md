## Evidence

### Lieu 2017

Lieu, R., Wong, A., Asefirad, A., & Shaffer, J. F. (2017). Improving Exam Performance in Introductory Biology through the Use of Preclass Reading Guides. CBE—Life Sciences Education. [doi:10.1187/cbe.16-11-0320](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-11-0320)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational regression analysis of 790-student Fall 2015 introductory biology course, four multiple linear regression models (n = 426–602 per exam) controlling for gender, ethnicity, SAT math and reading scores, preclass assignment scores, and section. Models show "a low of 1.35 ± 0.26 (exam 3)" per completed guide, all significant at p < 0.05.

> "The model estimates for the impact of completing a single reading guide before class on exam score varied from a low of 1.35 ± 0.26 (exam 3) to a high of 1.71 ± 0.26 (exam 2). The estimates for completing reading guides before class were statistically significant (p < 0.05) for each of the four exams"
