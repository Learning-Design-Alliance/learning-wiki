## Evidence

### Denervaud Solange 2019

Denervaud Solange, Knebel Jean-François, Hagmann Patric, Gentaz Edouard. (2019). Beyond executive functions, creativity skills benefit academic outcomes: Insights from Montessori education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225319

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Kindergarten well-being measured with the Feeling about School faces-scale questionnaire; ANCOVA controlling for age gave d = 0.88 favoring Montessori, with Yuen bootstrapped test p = 0.008.

> "Well-being at school (%)	87.2(12.0)	75.8 (13.9)	3.69, p = 0.008	11.13, p = 0.002	0.88"

### Denervaud Solange 2019 (2)

Denervaud Solange, Knebel Jean-François, Hagmann Patric, Gentaz Edouard. (2019). Beyond executive functions, creativity skills benefit academic outcomes: Insights from Montessori education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225319

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Elementary well-being measured with the Buss and Plomin sociability questionnaire; neither the Yuen test (p = 0.77) nor ANCOVA (p = 0.442) showed a group difference; d = 0.12 is descriptive.

> "Well-being at school (%)	65.7(20.7)	63.4 (19.7)	0.42, p = 0.77	0.60, p = 0.442	0.12"
