Secondary outcome of the randomized cross-over trial. The quote reports the NASA-TLX comparison "significantly lower using the 3D versus 4K system" for all levels of experience.

> "The task load felt by the participants and evaluated by the NASA-TLX questionnaire was significantly lower using the 3D versus 4K system (3D vs 4K: overall 48.6 ± 1.5 vs 61.9 ± 1.3 P < 0.001, for MS 50.3 ± 2.3 vs 66.6 ± 2.1, P < 0.001, for NBC 57.2 ± 2.6 vs 66.4 ± 2.3, P < 0.001 and for BC 38.32 ± 2.6 vs 52.7 ± 2.3, P < 0.001)."
