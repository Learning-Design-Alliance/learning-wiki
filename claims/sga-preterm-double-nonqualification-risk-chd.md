### Ekmark Sergel S 2026

Ekmark Sergel S, Lundberg M, Magnusson C, Ådén U, Bergman G, Siljehav V. (2026). Academic Achievements in Adolescents With Congenital Heart Disease: A Total Population‐Based Cohort Study. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.126.049927

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Stratified Poisson regression in the register cohort reported in Results: "SGA and prematurity, individually, nearly doubled the risk of being nonqualified for USS" among children with CHD without major noncardiac anomalies. GA and SGA were analyzed separately to avoid overadjusting for causal pathways.

> "SGA and prematurity, individually, nearly doubled the risk of being nonqualified for USS in all children with CHD without noncardiac MCA, with RR 2.34 (95% CI, 2.04–2.69) and 1.81 (95% CI, 1.64–1.99), respectively."
