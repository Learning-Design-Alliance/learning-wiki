Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

Narrative review reports, citing Ryan et al. (n=432), that Step 1 scores predicted "between 23% and 44% of the variance in shelf exam scores for all clerkships". Multiple large multi-institutional studies corroborate.

> "Step 1 scores also significantly predicted between 23% and 44% of the variance in shelf exam scores for all clerkships [12]."
