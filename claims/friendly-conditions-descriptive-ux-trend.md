### Francesco Vona 2025

Francesco Vona, Michael Stern, Navid Ashrafi, Julia Schorlemmer, Jessica Stemann, and Jan-Niklas Voigt-Antons. (2025). Sales Skills Training in Virtual Reality: An evaluation utilizing CAVE and Virtual Avatars. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of UEQ-S in the 20-participant within-subjects study: pragmatic quality means were "highest in Condition 2 (FUxUE)" (M=4.65, SD=1.22), with Condition 4 (UUxUE) lowest (M=4.43, SD=1.52); hedonic quality followed the same pattern (4.43 down to 4.14). These are descriptive means only; the RM-ANOVAs were non-significant.

> "For Pragmatic Quality, mean scores were highest in Condition 2 (FUxUE) (M= 4.65,SD= 1.22), followed closely by Condition 1 (FUxFE) (M= 4.60,SD= 1.42) and Condition 3 (UUxFE) (M= 4.60,SD= 1.35)."
