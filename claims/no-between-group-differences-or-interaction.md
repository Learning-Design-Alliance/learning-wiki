## Evidence

### Gongora-Ortega Javier 2012

Gongora-Ortega Javier, Segovia-Bernal Yolanda, Valdivia-Martinez J de Jesus, Galaviz-deAnda J Martin, Prado-Aguilar Carlos A. (2012). Educational interventions to improve the effectiveness in clinical competence of general practitioners: problem-based versus critical reading-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-53

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

3 x 2 ANOVA over the 28 general practitioners; Table 4 prints interaction F-Ratios of 0.22, 0.37 and 0.31 (p = 0.8007, 0.6954, 0.7349) and within-times F-Ratios of 14.29, 55.72 and 21.83. The article reports "no significant differences were observed between groups".

> "In all three dimensions there were significant differences within the groups in the pre- versus post-intervention scores, yet no significant differences were observed between groups. No interaction between pre- and post-intervention differences was detected in any of the groups for any dimension"
