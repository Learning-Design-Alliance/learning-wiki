Method/preliminary analysis in which four nested LCFA models (configural, metric, scalar, strict) were estimated and compared with chi-square difference tests and fit-index thresholds. The article reports the comparison "indicated strict measurement invariance," though chi-square difference tests were significant due to the large sample.

> "Comparison of LCFA models with correlated residuals across the time points indicated strict measurement invariance"
