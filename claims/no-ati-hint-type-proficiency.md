## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No interactions were found between pretest proficiency and performance or hint metrics for the training or posttest, rejecting the hypothesized ATI effect. [→ Christa Cody 2021](#christa-cody-2021)

## Evidence

### Christa Cody 2021

Christa Cody, Mehak Maniktala, Nicholas Lytle, Min Chi, Tiffany Barnes. (2021). The Impact of Looking Further Ahead: A Comparison of Two Data-driven Unsolicited Hint Types on Performance in an Intelligent Data-driven Logic Tutor. https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.05741

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The study hypothesized an aptitude-treatment interaction with higher-proficiency students benefiting from Waypoints and lower-proficiency students from Next-Step hints, but found no significant interactions for either training or posttest. "This means that H2 was rejected; Waypoint hints did not improve performance for higher proﬁciency students."

> "No interactions were found between pretest proﬁciency and performance or hint metrics for the posttest."
