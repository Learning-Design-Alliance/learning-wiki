Thematic analysis of applicant narratives (n=201) using topic modeling with human-in-the-loop coding; theme frequencies are the proportion of applicants mentioning a theme at least once. The dominant theme is "ethical and responsible use of AI" (68%), with AI for social good at 65% and non-STEM access at 61% (Table 1).

> "Analysis of application narratives (n= 201; Table 1) reveals strong convergence in motivations despite wide disciplinary diversity. The dominant theme is ethical and responsible use of AI, with many applicants expressing concerns about bias, misuse, and social consequences."
