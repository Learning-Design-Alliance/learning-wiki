## Evidence

### Thiyagarajan D 2025

Thiyagarajan D, Wheatley C, Salva CR, Lerner V, Saab SS. (2025). Development and validation of a pudendal nerve block simulation‐based educational intervention. Int J Gynecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post knowledge assessment (maximum score 13) within the same multi-institution intervention study, analyzed with paired t-tests; Table 4 reports residents' mean rising from 4.74 to 9.03 and practicing clinicians' from 5.71 to 9.70, with "Assessment P value <0.001".

> "Competence in identifying appropriate patient candidates, risks and benefits, and complications and their management also increased with the simulation intervention based on the assessment for both residents and practicing clinicians (Table 4)."
