Uge 2019¶

Uge, S., Neolaka, A., & Yasin, M. (2019). Development of Social Studies Learning Model Based on Local Wisdom in Improving Students' Knowledge and Social Attitude. International Journal of Instruction, 12(3), 375-388. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12323a

Expert validation stage of the R&D study: three material experts rated 59 statement items using the Lawshe Content Validity Ratio, with every item scoring CVR = 1.00, "which means that e ach item was declared good". Linguist, graphic design, locality and instrument-expert panels likewise returned CVR > 0 on all items.
