## Evidence

### Tedeschi MN 2023

Tedeschi MN, Hose TM, Mehlman EK, Franklin S, Wong TE. (2023). Improving Models for Student Retention and Graduation using Markov Chains. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap uncertainty analysis (1,000 resamples, 2.5-97.5% range) of RIT institutional data comparing Markov and traditional SYGR estimates. Median SYGR estimates are "consistent across the two methods to within 1 percentage point", e.g. 71% for Fall 2013 by both methods.

> "For the Fall 2013-2015 cohorts, using the Markov method results in 95% CI widths that are tightened by between 33% and 44% relative to the CI widths following the traditional calculation"

### Tedeschi MN 2023 (2)

Tedeschi MN, Hose TM, Mehlman EK, Franklin S, Wong TE. (2023). Improving Models for Student Retention and Graduation using Markov Chains. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The all-combined case (Fall 2013 to Summer 2021) shows a 34% CI-width reduction; the article attributes the smaller gain to relatively less information from partial-data cohorts (Fall 2016 to present) compared with full-cohort information.

> "When using all available data, the reduction in 95% CI width is somewhat lower (34% relative to the traditional calculation) but still appreciable"
