## Evidence

### Nabeela C 2025

Nabeela C, Shykhon M, Milward M, Yonel Z. (2025). The use of 360-videos to bridge the gap between pre-clinical and clinical dental teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06946-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group theme on practical experience: students wanted opportunities to practise clinical techniques with feedback, suggested mock patients, and noted simulation removes "the pressure of patient expectations." Some felt videos were less interactive than live demonstrations.

> "Students favoured practical experience over traditional lecture-based tutorials which were deemed “less engaging.”"
