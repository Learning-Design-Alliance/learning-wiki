## Evidence

### Ben-Sasson A 2019

Ben-Sasson A, Lior Y, Krispel J, Rucham M, Liel-Cohen N, Fuchs L, Kobal SL. (2019). Peer-teaching cardiac ultrasound among medical students: A real option. PLoS ONE 14(3): e0212794. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0212794

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective interventional study: 66 first-clinical-year students randomized to TA-led (group A, n=44) or qualified-trainer-led (group B, n=22) hands-on practice after identical lectures, then a blinded 6-minute practical exam. The article reports a "statistically significantly higher total examination score (54±17 vs. 39±21; p = 0.001)". No effect size is printed.

> "Group A also attained a statistically significantly higher total examination score (54±17 vs. 39±21; p = 0.001)."
