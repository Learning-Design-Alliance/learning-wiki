# Most pilot teachers found the Write to Succeed materials helpful, and teachers and coaches benefited from seeing practices modeled

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At least three quarters of teachers rated the binder practice descriptions, templates, and curriculum-integration guidance helpful or very helpful (74-79%, N = 18). [→ Patrick Rich 2025](#patrick-rich-2025)
`q2 i?` Teachers and coaches reported that seeing the practices modeled by coaches during professional learning was highly helpful. [→ Patrick Rich 2025 (2)](#patrick-rich-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Patrick Rich 2025

Patrick Rich, Audrey Altieri, Rachel Garrett. (2025). A pilot study of Write to Succeed: An intervention to enhance writing instruction in New Mexico. Regional Educational Laboratory Southwest (REL 2025-013). https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/pilot-study-write-succeed-intervention-enhancing-writing-instruction-new-mexico

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Teacher survey (N = 18) shows 79% rated the binder practice descriptions, 79% the templates, and 74% the curriculum-integration guidance as helpful or very helpful.

> "At least three quarters of teachers found the professional learning materials helpful or very helpful."

### Patrick Rich 2025 (2)

Patrick Rich, Audrey Altieri, Rachel Garrett. (2025). A pilot study of Write to Succeed: An intervention to enhance writing instruction in New Mexico. Regional Educational Laboratory Southwest (REL 2025-013). https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/pilot-study-write-succeed-intervention-enhancing-writing-instruction-new-mexico

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Winter and spring interviews with purposively selected teachers and coaches produced coded themes that modeling of practices during coach institutes and group professional learning was very helpful; quotes are illustrative, not quantified.

> "Teachers and coaches benefited from seeing the practices modeled."
