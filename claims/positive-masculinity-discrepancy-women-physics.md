## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests of self–reflected masculinity discrepancy scores in a survey of 1845 introductory physics students (Table III). Overall effect size for women was r-type 0.24; algebra-based women showed 26% positive versus 9% negative discrepancies (p < 0.001).

> "In calculus-based courses, 27% of women reported higher self-appraised than reflected masculinity, compared to 11% reporting the reverse. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test indicated a significant positive masculinity discrepancy (p < 0.001)."
