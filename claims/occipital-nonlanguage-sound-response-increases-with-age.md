# Occipital responses to nonlanguage sounds in blind children increase between ages 4 and 17

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In blind children, occipital responses to nonlanguage sounds increase with age across 4–17 years. [→ Bedny 2015](#bedny-2015)

## Evidence

### Bedny 2015

Bedny, M., H. Richardson, and R. Saxe. (2015). "Visual" Cortex Responds to Spoken Language in Blind Children. Journal of Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1523/jneurosci.0634-15.2015

`q3 · i?`

In the developmental study of blind children aged 4–17 listening to stories, unfamiliar foreign speech, and music, responses to nonlanguage sounds increased between the ages of 4 and 17. No effect size is printed for this developmental trend.
