## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MCQ scores rose significantly from pre to post in both ISS and OSS groups, with gains of 13.1% and 12.7%. [→ Sørensen JL 2015](#srensen-jl-2015)

## Evidence

### Sørensen JL 2015

Sørensen JL, van der Vleuten C, Rosthøj S, Østergaard D, LeBlanc V, Johansen M, Ekelund K, Starkopf L, Lindschou J, Gluud C, Weikop P, Ottesen B. (2015). Simulation-based multiprofessional obstetric anaesthesia training conducted in situ versus off-site leads to similar individual and team outcomes: a randomised educational trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008344

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc analysis of the randomised trial's MCQ primary outcome; the gains in "the ISS group" and "the OSS group" were each statistically significant at p<0.0001. No effect size was printed.

> "The average increase in percentage of correct answers in the MCQ test following training was 13.1% (95% CI 11.0% to 15.3%) in the ISS group and 12.7% (95% CI 10.3% to 15.2%) in the OSS group (overall tests of no difference between pre and post MCQ: both p<0.0001)."
