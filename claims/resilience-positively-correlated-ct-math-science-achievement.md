## Evidence

### Beyazhancer Rumeysa 2026

Beyazhancer Rumeysa, Kahraman Nurcan, Demir Barış. (2026). Resilience and academic performance in mathematics and science among middle school students: The mediating role of computational thinking. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0360966

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational study of 401 Turkish middle school students using Pearson correlation analyses on psychological resilience, CT, mathematics achievement, and science achievement. The study reports "psychological resilience was positively correlated with CT (r = .177, p < .01)" and that "CT was moderately and positively associated with both mathematics (r = .406, p < .01) and science achievement (r = .372, p < .01)".

> "Specifically, psychological resilience was positively correlated with CT (r = .177, p < .01), mathematics achievement (r = .130, p < .01), and science achievement (r = .143, p < .01). Moreover, CT was moderately and positively associated with both mathematics (r = .406, p < .01) and science achievement (r = .372, p < .01)."
