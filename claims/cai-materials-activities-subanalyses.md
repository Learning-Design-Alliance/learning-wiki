## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2019

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects subanalysis of three studies (n=280) using case scenarios with CAI. Pooled "SMD=0.90 (95% CI 0.59 to 1.21)" favoured CAI, with low I2=24% heterogeneity.

> "Subanalyses showed that CAI was better than face-to-face teaching when ECGs were accompanied by case scenarios (SMD=0.90 (95% CI 0.59 to 1.21); three studies, n=280; I2=24%)"

### Viljoen Charle André 2019 (2)

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subanalysis based on a single study (n=191) using images to explain impulse conduction alongside the 12-lead ECG. Pooled "SMD=1.09 (95% CI 0.79 to 1.40)" favoured CAI; single-study basis limits generalisation.

> "and if images were used to explain impulse conduction (SMD=1.09 (95% CI 0.79 to 1.40); one study, n=191)"

### Viljoen Charle André 2019 (3)

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects subanalysis of four studies (n=357) with self-administered assessments and automated feedback. Pooled "SMD=0.64 (95% CI 0.14 to 1.13)" favoured CAI; the effect was larger in blended contexts (SMD=0.95, two studies, n=241).

> "Studies in which CAI included self-administered assessments with automated feedback showed better ECG knowledge acquisition than face-to-face teaching (SMD=0.64 (95% CI 0.14 to 1.13); four studies, n=357; I2=77%)"

### Viljoen Charle André 2019 (4)

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q2 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subanalysis based on one study (n=181) providing online chat rooms. Pooled "SMD=0.68 (95% CI 0.38 to 0.98)" favoured CAI; single-study basis limits generalisation.

> "CAI was better than face-to-face teaching when students had access to online chat rooms to discuss the study material (SMD=0.68 (95% CI 0.38 to 0.98); one study, n=181)"
