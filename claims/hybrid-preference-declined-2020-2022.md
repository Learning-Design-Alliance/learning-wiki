## Evidence

### Wang Xiaoran 2024

Wang Xiaoran, Liu Jiangheng, Jia Shuwei, Hou Chunmei, Jiao Runsheng, Yan Yan, Ma Tengchuang, Zhang Ying, Liu Yanyan, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng, Zhu Hui, Liu Xiao-Yu. (2024). Hybrid teaching after COVID-19: advantages, challenges and optimization strategies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05745-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of teaching-method preferences between 2020 (n = 128) and 2022 (n = 200) cohorts using chi-square tests. The hybrid preference "decreased largely from 2020 to 2022, albeit without reaching statistical significance"; the abstract reports 52.0% preferring offline in 2022 versus 39.1% in 2020.

> "the preference of choosing online teaching remained relatively unchanged, while the rate of choosing hybrid teaching method decreased largely from 2020 to 2022, albeit without reaching statistical significance (53.9% in 2020 vs. 42.0% in 2022, P = 0.07)"
