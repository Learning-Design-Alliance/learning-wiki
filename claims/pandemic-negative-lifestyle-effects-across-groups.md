### Goulet‐Pelletier Jean‐Christophe 2026

Goulet‐Pelletier Jean‐Christophe, Collet Ophélie A., Loose Tianna, Haeck Catherine, Larose Simon, Charland Patrick, Côté Sylvana M. (2026). School Readiness, Learning Challenges, and Lifestyle Changes Among Children During the COVID‐19 Pandemic. The Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.70204

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parent reports showed children with and without vulnerability "all manifested increased screen time, less physical activity, less school motivation, less relationship with friends, and lower mental health." About 15% of parents noted adverse effects on sleep, nutrition, and sibling relationships, while around 75% reported no impact on those domains; positive effects were more likely for friend and sibling relationships.

> "Overall, according to parents, children with and without a vulnerability all manifested increased screen time, less physical activity, less school motivation, less relationship with friends, and lower mental health."
