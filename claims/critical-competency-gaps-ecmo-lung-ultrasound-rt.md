## Evidence

### Skariah Tisha Ann 2025

Skariah Tisha Ann, Rao Udaya Kumar, Karthika Manjush. (2025). Competency gaps in respiratory therapy: evidence from a global survey. Korean Journal of Medical Education, 37(4), 437-451. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.355

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 181 RT leaders (Cronbach's alpha 0.821) using gap analysis comparing mandatory versus deficient competencies. The article reports "critical gaps (over 300%) in ECMO and lung ultrasound"; Table 2 prints ECMO gap 404.35% and lung ultrasound 306.06%. No effect size is printed.

> "Findings reveal critical gaps (over 300%) in ECMO and lung ultrasound, signaling an urgent need for structured training (Table 3). High-gap areas (100%–299%) include polysomnography and arterial line insertion, while medium gaps (50%–99%) affect common competencies such as bronchoscopy assistance and pulmonary function testing."
