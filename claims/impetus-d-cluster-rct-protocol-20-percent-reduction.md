`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Study protocol description of the multi-centre, facility-level cluster RCT, in which 24 care homes in three Australian cities will be randomised, 12 per arm, to IMPETUS-D or usual training. The article states the "primary outcome is to reduce transfers to hospital and deaths in hospital by 20%". No outcome data are reported yet.
