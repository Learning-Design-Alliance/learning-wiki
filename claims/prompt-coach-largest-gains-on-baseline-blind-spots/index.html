Evidence¶

Rohit Mehra 2026¶

Rohit Mehra, Kapil Singi, Vikrant Kaulgud, Vibhu Saujanya Sharma, Swapnajeet Gon Choudhury, Swati Sharma, Adam P. Burden, Majd Sakr. (2026). Prompt Coach: An Empirical Evaluation of an Agentic Tutor for Learning Prompt Engineering in Software Development. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06074

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dimension-wise results of the 15-developer pre/post study (Table 2): Inclusion of Constraints +31.63% (50.51 to 66.49), Error Handling +30.66%, Context Awareness +23.61%, each statistically significant per the article. The authors call these "developers' cognitive blind spots".

"the largest gains were observed in Inclusion of Constraints, Error Handling, and Context Awareness, which were also the lowest-scoring di- mensions at baseline, suggesting that PC is particularly effective at addressing developers' cognitive blind spots."
