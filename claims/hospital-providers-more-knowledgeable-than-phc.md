> "The knowledge score about diagnosis, management, and prevention of pre-eclampsia was higher among midwives in hospitals than in PHCs (median (IQR): 8 (6 to 10) vs 6 (4 to 8), p < 0.01), and higher among physicians in hospitals than in PHCs (median (IQR): 10 (8 to 13) vs 8 (7 to 11), p = 0.04)"
