## Evidence

### Yli-Piipari 2016

Yli-Piipari, S., Layne, T., McCollins, T., & Knox, T. (2016). The Impact of Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Middle School Students’ Health-Related Fitness: An Xbox One Kinetic Delivered 4-Week Randomized Controlled Trial. JTRM in Kinesiology, 12:1-18. http://www.health.gov/paguidelines/pdf/paguide.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of covariance in a 4-week cluster-randomized trial of 94 adolescents (experiment n = 52; control n = 42), with posttest scores as dependent variables, baseline scores as covariates, and intervention condition as the independent variable. The article reports "no significant intervention effect on cardiovascular endurance" and the other three FitnessGram outcomes, with no effect sizes printed for these tests.

> "there were no significant intervention effect on cardiovascular endurance (F[2,82] = 2.58, p = .112), upper body strength and endurance (F[2,84] = 3.55, p = .063), abdominal strength and endurance (F[2,82] = .01, p < .973)"
