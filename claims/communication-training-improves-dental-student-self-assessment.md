### Zöll Lisa 2025

Zöll Lisa, Deschner James, Hollinderbäumer Anke, Nowak Thomas, Zajontz Tamara, Behling Lina, Fischbeck Sabine, Schmidt Uwe, Pfeiffer-Grötz Thekla J. (2025). The effects of communication training on communication competence – a 360° evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07676-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 127 dental students in 7th and 10th semesters, with an intervention group of 64 receiving an online theory module and a practical unit with drama patients. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed all self-assessment parameters "improved significantly between survey time points T0 and T1" with p-values from p < 0.001 to p = 0.009.

> "Our results show that all parameters in the intervention group (communication training) improved significantly between survey time points T0 and T1 (S1.1: p < 0.001; S1.2: p = 0.005; S1.3: p < 0.001; S1.4: p < 0.001; S1.5: p = 0.004; S1.6: p = 0.002; S1.7: p < 0.001; S1.8: p < 0.001; S1.10: p = 0.009)"
