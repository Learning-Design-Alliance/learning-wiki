## Evidence

### Jiang and Xiao 2014

Lin Jiang & Hailing Xiao. (2014). The Efficacy of Written Corrective Feedback and Language Analytic Ability on Chinese Learners’ Explicit and Implicit Knowledge of English Articles. English Language Teaching, 7(10). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p22

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scheffe posthoc tests on time-pressured picture-cued writing test scores for 92 learners. In Posttest 1, both DOC and DMC groups performed much better than the control group, and the DMC group also outperformed the DOC group; in Posttest 2, no significant difference between the two treatment groups.

> "As for the picture-cued writing test, the DMC group outperformed the DOC group in Posttest 1, suggesting that the DMC was superior to the DOC on short-term improvement of implicit knowledge."
