## Evidence

### Resnick PJ 2010

Resnick PJ, Janney AW, Buis LR, Richardson CR. (2010). Adding an Online Community to an Internet-Mediated Walking Program. Part 2: Strategies for Encouraging Community Participation. J Med Internet Res 12(4):e72. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1339

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' retrospective interpretation of their single case study. Recruitment used targeted letters from a clinical data warehouse rather than fliers, and only 5 initial forums were shown, with dead zones observed in low-traffic forums such as Men's Locker Room (7 threads).

> "Concentrated study recruitment increased the effective membership size. Having few conversation spaces rather than many specialized ones, staff seeding of the forums before members were invited to visit, and staff posting of new topics when there were conversation lulls, all helped to make the forums appear active."
