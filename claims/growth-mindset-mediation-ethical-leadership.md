Unlocking academic achievement of nursing master’s students: ethical leadership, teacher-student relationship and growth mindset – a cross-sectional survey study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

SEM mediation analysis in the same 262-participant survey. The bootstrap indirect effect via growth mindset was 0.080 with 95% CI [0.002, 0.156], reported as "20.89%" of the total effect; the CI excluded 0, so mediation was considered established.

> "Ethical leadership positively predicted growth mindset (β = 0.134, t = 2.054, P = 0.040), and growth mindset positively predicted academic achievement (β = 0.601, t = 5.542, P < 0.001)."
