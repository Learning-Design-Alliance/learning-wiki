Evidence¶

Yimwilai 2015¶

Supaporn Yimwilai. (2015). An Integrated Approach to Teaching Literature in an EFL Classroom. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p14

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with 78 Thai English majors at two universities; experimental groups taught eight weeks with integrated-approach lesson plans, controls with the conventional method. Two-way ANOVA showed the group effect was significant: "F = 19.87 p < .001" with η2 = .172; the school effect was not significant (p = .135).

"the achievement scores of the students in the experimental groups were significantly higher than those of the control groups, at the level .05 (F = 19.87 p < .001). The effect size was .172."
