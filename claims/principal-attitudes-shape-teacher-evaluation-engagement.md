### Shyjka 2022

Shyjka, A. (2022). Reaching for improvement: Teacher evaluation and its role in instructional improvement. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/talent/

Interview study across 7 CPS schools found that "Principals' attitudes toward and framing of REACH teacher evaluation mattered for teacher engagement in the evaluation process." Teachers in schools led by principals who disliked REACH expressed an "if they don't care why should I?" perspective.
