## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression analysis of CRDC data combined with Project Implicit county-level bias estimates. This pattern contrasts with the enrollment findings and runs in the opposite direction. The authors recommend caution before interpreting this as a selection effect.

> "county-level implicit racial bias was associated with smaller Black–White disparities in AP exam taking (Ps < 0.009; Fig. 1, Middle) and passing (Ps < 0.002; Fig. 1, Right) across all years."
