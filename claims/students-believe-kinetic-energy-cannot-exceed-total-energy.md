## Evidence

### Stephanik 2011

Stephanik, B. M., and Shaffer, P. S. (2011). Examining student ability to interpret and use potential energy diagrams for classical systems. [doi:10.1063/1.3680071](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680071)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

During individual interviews on question 3, in which a particle is released from rest where potential energy is positive and later moves through a region of negative potential energy, one student limited the maximum kinetic energy to the total energy value; the authors report this and many other responses revealed "a strong belief that the numeric value for the kinetic energy cannot exceed the numeric value for the total energy."
