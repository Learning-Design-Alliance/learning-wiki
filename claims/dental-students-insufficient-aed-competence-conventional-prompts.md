### Tayebi-Hillali Hoda 2026

Tayebi-Hillali Hoda, Aballe Marta, Santana Denisse, Varela-Aneiros Iván, Diniz-Freitas Márcio, Diz-Dios Pedro. (2026). Competence in the use of a semi-automated external defibrillator among undergraduate dental students: evaluation and proposal for improvement. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10025-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simulation-based assessment of 42 final-year dental students using conventional AED voice prompts in a confirmed cardiorespiratory arrest scenario. The article reports that "fewer than 10% (range 0-9.5%) of learners correctly completed the required actions" in 8 of 10 items, with the highest compliance rates in connecting electrodes to the AED monitor (81%) and delivering the shock (59.5%).

> "In 8 of the 10 items assessed, fewer than 10% (range 0-9.5%) of learners correctly completed the required actions (Table 4). None of the students checked the battery status of the device, and only 4.8% turned it on correctly."
