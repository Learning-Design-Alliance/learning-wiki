Evidence¶

Chanjuan Fu 2025¶

Chanjuan Fu, Xiaoxin Hao, Xinyue Shi, Hanxin Qian, Shanyun He & Fengji Geng. (2025). Effect of cognitive control strategies on young children’s attention and emotion during coding learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00360-z

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Video-coded three-second sampling of emotion across three lessons in the 46-child quasi-experiment showed the strategy group spent more time in neutral emotion (mean 0.939 vs 0.891), "d = 0.794".

"the experimental and control groups showed a signiﬁcant difference in the proportion of time showing neutral emotions (t(32.126) =−3.142, p = 0.004, d = 0.794"

Chanjuan Fu 2025 (2)¶

Chanjuan Fu, Xiaoxin Hao, Xinyue Shi, Hanxin Qian, Shanyun He & Fengji Geng. (2025). Effect of cognitive control strategies on young children’s attention and emotion during coding learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00360-z

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

The same video-coded analysis found children in the control group stayed longer in positive emotional state than the strategy group, "d = 0.752"; the difference persisted when restricted to time in the attentional state (d = 0.490).

"a signiﬁcant difference between the experimental and control groups in the proportion o f time showing positive emotion (t(32.815) = 2.995,p = 0.005, d = 0.752)"
