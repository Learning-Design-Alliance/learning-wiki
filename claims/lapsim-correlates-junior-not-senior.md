### Tee Yu-San 2024

Optimizing modern surgical simulation through instructor feedback – insights from a retrospective observational study in a tertiary hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06090-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of first-performance scores from a retrospective study of 32 residents at Linkou Chang Gung Memorial Hospital. The article reports "GOALS was weakly correlated with LapSim score 1 (adjusted R2 = 0.149, p = 0.017)"; only correlation coefficients and p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "Overall, GOALS was weakly correlated with LapSim score 1 (adjusted R2 = 0.149, p = 0.017)."

### Tee Yu-San 2024 (2)

Tee Yu-San, Cheng Chi-Tung, Li Pei-Hua, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Liao Chien-Hung, Fu Chih-Yuan. (2024). Optimizing modern surgical simulation through instructor feedback – insights from a retrospective observational study in a tertiary hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06090-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup correlational analysis comparing junior (n = 17) and senior (n = 15) residents' first-performance LapSim and GOALS scores; the table prints Senior GOALS adjusted R2 = 0.024, p = 0.266. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "the junior group had a higher correlation between simulator-based and instructor-based scores (Junior GOALS adjusted R2 = 0.285, p = 0.016). This result was not observed in the senior group."
