### Ferguson M. A. 2014

Ferguson M. A., Henshaw H., Clark D. P. A., Moore D. R. (2014). Benefits of Phoneme Discrimination Training in a Randomized Controlled Trial of 50- to 74-Year-Olds With Mild Hearing Loss. Ear and Hearing. https://doi.org/10.1097/AUD.0000000000000020

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Compliance analysis in the RCT of home-delivered training (15 min/day, 6 days/week over 4 weeks, 360 min total). The article reports "80% (35 of 44) of the sample completed the requested duration of training" with no dropouts, and mean training times of 384.9, 374.3, and 379.7 min for never, beginner, and competent computer users.

> "Compliance with training was high across all participants and there were no dropouts; 80% (35 of 44) of the sample completed the requested duration of training, with 75% (33 of 44) exceeding the required training"
