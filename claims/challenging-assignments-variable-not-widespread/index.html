q3 · i? · theoretical · r2

Bryk, Nagaoka, and Newmann analyzed classroom assignments from 74 to 116 teachers of third, sixth, and eighth grades over three years. In 1999, moderate or extensive challenge levels ranged widely, e.g., 64 percent of third-grade writing assignments versus 9 percent of eighth-grade math assignments.

"The data indicate that while intellectually challenging instruction can be found in under-resourced, low-income schools, its occurrence is highly variable and by no means widespread."
