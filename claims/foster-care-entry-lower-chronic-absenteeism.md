## Evidence

### Sattler 2025

Sattler, Kierra; Font, Sarah; Panlilio, Carlomagno. (2025). Foster care entry and later academic achievement among infants involved with child protective services. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted logistic regression models in the main results (Table 3, n = 8,795) estimating absenteeism as a dichotomous below-90%-attendance outcome. The article reports "lower odds of chronic absenteeism" across all three entry-timing groups; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "children who were placed in foster care in early infancy (OR = .52, p < .001), toddlerhood (OR = .70, p < .001), and preschool (OR = .63, p < .001) all had lower odds of chronic absenteeism compared to children who were never in foster care before age 5."
