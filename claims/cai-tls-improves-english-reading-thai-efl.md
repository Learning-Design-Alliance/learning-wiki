## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a one-group time series (two pretests, then three posttests over 10 weeks of CAI based on TLS), 32 ninth grade Thai EFL students' reading scores rose significantly (p = .001); with no comparison group, the rise cannot be attributed to CAI-TLS itself. [→ Jinajai and Rattanavich 2015](#jinajai-and-rattanavich-2015)

## Evidence

### Jinajai and Rattanavich 2015

Nattapong Jinajai & Saowalak Rattanavich. (2015). The Effects of Computer-Assisted Instruction Based on Top-Level Structure Method in English Reading and Writing Abilities of Thai EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 8(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n11p231

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on reading scores of 32 ninth grade students in a one-group time series design (two pretests, then three posttests; no control or comparison group) found scores rose significantly after CAI-TLS instruction, with "the experimental group registered higher development in reading at a .001 level". The two pretests did not differ significantly (p = 1.000), which does not show that scores were flat before instruction, and without a comparison group the study cannot show that CAI-TLS, rather than other teaching or testing over the 10 weeks, caused the rise.

> "the experimental group registered higher development in reading at a .001 level ( = .512, F-statistic = 6.667, p-value = .001)"
