# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, preclinical dental students' diagnostic accuracy for a tooth defect case was measured before and after SCT, with selection of apical periodontitis falling from 24.49% to 8.16% (p = 0.002)

## Evidence

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled before-and-after study of 96 undergraduate dental students; chi-square comparison of Case 1 diagnosis answers before and after SCT. The incorrect "B: Apical periodontitis" choice fell from "24.49% of students" to 8.16% (p = 0.002), and "C: Discolored tooth" rose from 52.04% to 100% (p < 0.001).

> "Before SCT, 24.49% of students considered B was one of the diagnoses, while the percentage decreased to 8.16% after SCT."

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025 (2)

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same before-and-after comparison, the percentage of students choosing the correct diagnosis combination ACE "increased from 14.29% to 28.57% after SCT", reported descriptively with no test statistic for this combination.

> "The percentages of students who chose ACE increased from 14.29% to 28.57% after SCT (Fig. 4)."
