### Sophia Hannou; Cristina Nicorici; Wanda Bosshard; Pierre Voirol; Farshid Sadeghipour; Nancy Perrottet; Chantal Csajka 2025

Sophia Hannou; Cristina Nicorici; Wanda Bosshard; Pierre Voirol; Farshid Sadeghipour; Nancy Perrottet; Chantal Csajka. (2025). Opioid Room of Horrors: a simulation approach to strengthen drug administration safety. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2025-003728

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational evaluation of 86 paired simulation sessions with 172 geriatric healthcare professionals, scored by two assessors using a standardised form. Detection rates are descriptive proportions; participants "correctly identified or avoided a median of 6 out of 8 items".

> "Detection rates varied depending on the type of error, with documented allergies and incorrect drug (strength or galenic form), each identified by only 55% of participants, expired drugs by 63%, and wristband/EMR name mismatches by 60%."
