`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Proposition 3 of the article's formal model: a theoretical derivation stating that the moderation should appear on the a-path, with no direct effect predicted once the ceding of the promoter function is controlled. No data are reported.

> "Structural location, not volume of use, governs the outcome: two users of equal intensity diverge sharply according to whether the system is answered as one voice among others or installed as the vantage that surveys them all."

### Du P 2026 (2)

Du P, Zhou Y, Zhou Z, Wang H and Cao Q (2026). The synthetic position: generative artificial intelligence and the suspension of self-authorship in emerging adulthood. Front. Psychol. 17:1922087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1922087

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Proposition 4 derives the population association as a weighted average of the associations in promoter-location and ordinary-voice episodes, with three stated testable assumptions: reliable episode-level location coding, linear aggregation, and independence of p from the conditional associations.

> "A null average effect in any single population therefore neither confirms nor disconfirms the mechanism."
