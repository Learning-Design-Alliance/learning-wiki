Evidence¶

Wendy M. Reinke & Keith C. Herman & Nianbo Dong 2018¶

Wendy M. Reinke & Keith C. Herman & Nianbo Dong. (2018). The Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management Program: Outcomes from a Group Randomized Trial. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-018-0932-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multi-level moderation analysis in the group RCT using treatment-by-pretest interaction terms in three-level HLM models. The article reports a significant social competence moderation (b = −0.09, p = 0.04).

"Overall social competence was moderated by pre-test levels of social competence ( b = − 0.09, p = 0.04), indicating that students with lower social competence at pre-test who were in intervention classrooms demonstrated greater increases in social competence over time in comparison to similar peers in control classrooms."
