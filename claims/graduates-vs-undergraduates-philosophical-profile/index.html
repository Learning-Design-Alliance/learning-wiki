Starkey 1972¶

Starkey, John D.; Barr, Rita L. (1972). The Philosophical Nature of Teachers--Graduate and Undergraduate. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED072018

"Here the graduates are shown to have a greater belief in Realism, in Idealism, and in Phenomenology, and a lesser belief in the philosophies of Pragmatism and Existentialism."
