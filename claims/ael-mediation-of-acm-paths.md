## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adoption of e-learning tools significantly mediated the ACM–MW path (β = 0.118) and the ACM–SUSL path (β = 0.123) with bootstrap confidence intervals excluding zero. [→ Li Jiuxiang 2024](#li-jiuxiang-2024)

## Evidence

### Li Jiuxiang 2024

Li Jiuxiang, Wang Rufeng. (2024). RETRACTED ARTICLE: Determining the role of innovative teaching practices, sustainable learning, and the adoption of e-learning tools in leveraging academic motivation for students' mental well-being. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01639-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mediation hypothesis test (H8) using 2000 bc-bootstraps in Mplus on the 308-respondent survey; the specific indirect effect was significant ("β = 0.118") and the 95% CI (0.068–0.169) did not pass through zero.

> "Results demonstrated that AEL significantly linked the ACM-MW path, β = 0.118, with SE = 0.031, t-value = 3.875 and p-value was 0.000."

### Li Jiuxiang 2024 (2)

Li Jiuxiang, Wang Rufeng. (2024). RETRACTED ARTICLE: Determining the role of innovative teaching practices, sustainable learning, and the adoption of e-learning tools in leveraging academic motivation for students' mental well-being. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01639-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Mediation hypothesis test (H9) with 2000 bc-bootstraps on the 308-respondent survey. The ACM–SUSL indirect effect via e-learning tool adoption was significant ("β = 0.123"); the printed upper CI reads "0.071—176" as in the article.

> "The analysis revealed that AEL positively and significantly mediated the effect of ACM on SUSL, β = 0.123, with SE = 0.032, t-value = 3.847, p-value = 0.00 and 95% of CI = 0.071—176."
