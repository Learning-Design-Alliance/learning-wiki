## Evidence

### Gove 2017

Gove, A., Brunette, T., Bulat, J., Carrol, B., Henny, C., Macon, W., Nderu, E., & Sitabkhan, Y. (2017). Assessing the impact of early learning programs in Africa. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 158, 25–41. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article's cross-country synthesis (Table 1) summarizes program evaluations, including cluster randomized controlled trials in Kenya, Liberia, and Uganda. It reports "significant and important learning gains of between 0.2 and 2.57 SD in effect size" and notes gains must be considered against extremely low baseline performance.

> "In several countries with completed impact evaluations, there are significant and important learning gains of between 0.2 and 2.57 SD in effect size; in one case the percentage of students reaching grade‐level reading proficiency increased from 12% to 47%."
