## Evidence

### Shikino Kiyoshi 2021

Shikino Kiyoshi, Rosu Claudia A., Yokokawa Daiki, Suzuki Shingo, Hirota Yusuke, Nishiya Katsumi, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2021). Flexible e-learning video approach to improve fundus examination skills for medical students: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02857-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 104 fifth-year medical students were assigned to a flexible e-learning video group (n = 51) or F2F lecture group (n = 53). Post-post comparison of simulator-based funduscopic diagnostic accuracy showed "intervention group 63.4% vs. control group 34.6%, p < 0.001"; the within-group pre-to-post gain (36.6% to 63.4%) was significant at p < 0.001 (Table 1).

> "Post-post comparisons across the two groups revealed a significant difference (intervention group 63.4% vs. control group 34.6%, p < 0.001)."
