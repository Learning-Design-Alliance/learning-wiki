### Naghshi Maryam 2024

Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 33 informants; the assessors' challenges sub-theme lists lack of expert assessors, "the difference in the assessment method and ability level of the assessors", and a tendency to give balanced scores. A participant (M. 17) illustrated differing impressions of the same behavior between assessors.

> "The challenges in this field are the lack of expert assessors, the difference in the assessment method and ability level of the assessors, and the tendency of the assessor to give a balanced score."
