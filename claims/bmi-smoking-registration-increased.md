Pre-post analysis of preventive-measures indicators 6 and 7 in the Sicilian GP cohort; the article states these percentages are "in line with value recorded in HSD (76.4 and 80.2)". Table 4 reports p<0.001 at 24 months for both.

> "Also the proportion of COPD patients with a registration of BMI (indicator 6) and smoking habits (indicator 7) gradually increased from 75.8% (baseline) to 81.7% (24 months) and from 76.5% (baseline) to 81.0% (24 months), respectively."
