# FTE thinking-time logarithms are approximately normally distributed with a leading spike and slight right skew

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Histograms of the natural logarithms of FTE thinking times are approximately Gaussian, with a slight right skew and a pronounced spike on the leading edge across nearly all subjects. [→ Beatty 2002](#beatty-2002)

## Evidence

### Beatty 2002

Beatty, I. D., Gerace, W. J., & Dufresne, R. J. (2002). Measuring and Modeling Physics Students' Conceptual Knowledge Structures Through Term Association Times. UMass Physics Education Research Group. [doi:10.1119/1.1482067](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1482067)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phenomenological analysis of FTE data: the logarithms of thinking times "appears approximately normal (Gaussian), indicating that the distribution of thinking times themselves is approximately log-normal". Examination of histograms for all 33 subjects in both studies showed most display a pronounced leading-edge spike.

> "Deviating from normality, the histogram has a slight skew to the right and a significant spike superimposed on the leading edge."
