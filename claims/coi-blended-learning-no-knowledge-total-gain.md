# CoI-based blended learning did not significantly improve overall SPD management knowledge scores compared with face-to-face teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` The total SPD theoretical test score did not differ significantly between groups (Cohen d = 0.10, p = .432). [→ Liu Weichu 2021](#liu-weichu-2021)

## Evidence

### Liu Weichu 2021

Liu Weichu, Wang Jun, Zhang Han, Yu Changfeng, Liu Shuai, Zhang Cen, Yu Jingya, Liu Qiao, Yang Bing. (2021). Determining the effects of blended learning using the community of inquiry on nursing students' learning gains in sudden patient deterioration module. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.914

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t test on the 43-item SPD knowledge test in the two-group quasi-experimental trial. The article reports a non-significant difference with the printed Cohen d = 0.10; equivalence was not tested.

> "the total score of SPD theoretical test grade was a little bit higher in the CoI‐based blended learning group (mean = 63.53, SD = 7.30) compared with the control group (mean = 62.75, SD = 7.63); however, the difference was no significant (t = 0.79, p = .432, Cohen d = 0.10)."
