Yin S 2026¶

Yin S, Wu R, Liu X and Li R (2026) GenAI dependence and graduate students' research creativity: the roles of critical thinking and human–AI collaboration quality. Front. Psychol. 17:1896963. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1896963

Bootstrap mediation results (5,000 resamples) for the functional dependence model among 1,157 graduate students. The article reports the indirect association was "primarily concentrated in the pathway through critical thinking (Ind1 = 0.3449)."

"the indirect association involving functional dependence was primarily concentrated in the pathway through critical thinking (Ind1 = 0.3449), accounting for approximately 85.0% of its total indirect eﬀect."
