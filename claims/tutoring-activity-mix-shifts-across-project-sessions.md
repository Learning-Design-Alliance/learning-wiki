Descriptive clustering analysis (Fig. 3) of n=24,587 five-minute chunks from n=195 completed 10-session projects, hierarchically clustered using Anthropic's Clio framework and the Kura library. The article reports the distribution of activities shifts dynamically as projects progress.

> "Rapport and relationship building represents up to 25% of the first session and up to 10% of the last session and remains an important part of the mentoring process throughout the ten sessions (e.g. providing encouragement and emotional support)."
