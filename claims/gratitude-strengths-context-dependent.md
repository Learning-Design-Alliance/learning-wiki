### Stewart 2026

Stewart, T. M., McCluskey, G., Johnson, H., Fry, D., McAra, L., & Booth, J. N. (2026). Co-developing the Child and Adolescent Mentally Healthy Classroom (CAMH Class) intervention for use in routine teaching in secondary schools. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359563

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative finding from co-development workshops with teachers (n = 10) and young people (n = 7). Feasibility and acceptability of mindfulness, gratitude, strengths and muscle relaxation activities were seen as context-dependent on environment, timing and specific classes or subjects.

> "Young people rejected public disclosures of reflecting on strengths or expressing gratitude in the classroom. Teachers mirrored the need for contextual boundaries, citing perceived workload concerns regarding routine integration."
