The preface reports a qualitative hybrid thematic analysis by González, Ponce and Fernández of teachers' experiences of online teaching during COVID-19 in Chile, despite pandemic and political disruption. The quoted lessons learned are the study's findings.

> "teachers employed an array of digital tools for maintaining content delivery and promoting interaction, deepened their understanding of course design and assessment, and developed an emphatic disposition to understand students' situations in the context of Chilean Higher Education."
