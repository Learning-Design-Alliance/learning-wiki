# In the Tubes task, larger average pupil diameter of the nondominant eye and higher rate of saccade are associated with lower RAS simulator performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A one-standard-deviation increase in the nondominant eye's average pupil diameter (subject-wise standardized) was associated with an 8.13-point decrease in Tubes performance score (p = 0.01), and a one-standard-deviation increase in rate of saccade was associated with a 5.87-point decrease (p = 0.04).
