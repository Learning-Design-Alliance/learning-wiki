Evidence¶

Zhao Qianhui and Li Shuhui 2026¶

Zhao Qianhui and Li Shuhui. (2026). Exploring Middle School Students' Interactions and Perceptions of Using Generative AI in STEAM Learning Environments. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091626

q2 · i? · design · r2

Frequency analysis of 310 student–GenAI interaction log entries from six groups across two 120-minute STEAM modules, coded with four sub-dimensions (Cohen's κ 0.943–1.000). The study found "the majority of interaction content was related to STEAM knowledge/information, accounting for 51.1% of the total".

"the majority of interaction content was related to STEAM knowledge/information, accounting for 51.1% of the total. This was followed by problem-solving process (24.9%) and inquiry plan (15.7%)."
