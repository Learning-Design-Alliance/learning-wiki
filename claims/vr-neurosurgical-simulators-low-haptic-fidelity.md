`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's discussion of its six included VR studies: face-validity ratings favoured "the appearance of the VR models to real surgery", but "participants of all six VR studies reported low haptic fidelity"; no effect size printed.

> "Our study revealed favourable ratings for face validity comparing the appearance of the VR models to real surgery revealing the benefits of the model for anatomic understanding. However, participants of all six VR studies reported low haptic fidelity (Table 2)."

### Chawla Shreya 2022 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Sub-group analysis of Alaraj et al.'s ITACS immersive VR aneurysm-clipping simulator (17 residents): "only 12% of participants agreed that it was realistic" regarding haptic sensation, versus favourable overall usefulness ratings.

> "Although the VR model was rated favourably for its use, it was limited by haptic realism as only 12% of participants agreed that it was realistic."
