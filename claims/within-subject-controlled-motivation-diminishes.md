# Within school subjects, controlled motivation shows self-diminishing effects on competence beliefs and autonomous motivation

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Higher controlled motivation in the preceding subject-specific situation predicted diminished competence belief in the subsequent lesson (ES = -0.21). [→ Malmberg and & Martin 2019](#malmberg-and-martin-2019)
`q3 i1` Higher subject-specific prior controlled motivation diminished subsequent autonomous motivation (ES = -0.23 in joint model). [→ Malmberg and & Martin 2019 (2)](#malmberg-and-martin-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Within-school-subject lagged MSEM (Model 2, Fig 2 right). Prior controlled motivation negatively predicted subsequent competence belief.

> "Higher controlled motivation in the preceding situation predicted a diminished competence belief the subsequent lesson (B = -.08, β = -.10; p = .010; ES = -0.21)."

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019 (2)

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Joint MSEM (Model 3) confirmed the negative cross-lags of subject-specific prior controlled motivation on both competence beliefs and autonomous motivation.

> "A higher level of subject-specific prior controlled motivation diminished subsequent competence beliefs (B = -.11, β = -.14, p = .018; ES = -0.29), and autonomous motivation (B = -.11, β = -.13, p = .038; ES = -0.23)."
