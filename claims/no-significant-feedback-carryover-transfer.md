## Evidence

### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Joohyun Lee, Sejun Oh. (2026). Personalized Auto-Grading and Feedback System for Constructive Geometry Tasks Using Large Language Models on an Online Math Platform. Preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1109/access.2026.3657726](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3657726)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Carry-over analysis in the field study comparing students with and without prior feedback-driven conversions on Q10, Q11, and Q18; all p-values exceeded 0.05 (e.g., Q10 p = 0.547, Q11 p = 0.643, Q18 p = 0.624). Equivalence was not tested.

> "None of these comparisons reached statistical significance (all p > 0.05). These results suggest that while feedback-driven conversions were common and often associated with high performance, their direct measurable impact on subsequent complex questions could not be demonstrated in this dataset."
