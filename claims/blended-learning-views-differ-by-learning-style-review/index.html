Suprabha. K 2015¶

Suprabha. K, & Subramonian, G. (2015). Blended learning approach for enhancing students learning experiences in a knowledge society. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 11(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1098578

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review reports, citing Akkoyunlu and Meryem (2008), a study of student perceptions in a blended learning environment based on different learning styles, finding that "students' views on blended learning process, such as easy use of the web environment differs according to their learning styles". No effect size is printed.

"Akkoyunlu, B., and Meryem, Y. S. (2008), have studied student's perceptions in a blended learning environment based on different learning styles. Results revealed that students' views on blended learning process, such as easy use of the web environment differs according to their learning styles."
