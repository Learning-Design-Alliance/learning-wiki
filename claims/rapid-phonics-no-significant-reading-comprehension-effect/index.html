Evidence¶

King 2015¶

King, B., & Kasim, A. (2015). Rapid Phonics Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581293

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Individually randomised two-arm trial with 201 pupils (100 intervention, 101 control) from 22 primary schools; multilevel modelling accounted for school effects. The NGRT effect size was -0.05 (-0.34, 0.25) with 178 pupils analysed, described as "limited benefit in enhancing reading skills".

"Using intention to treat, the multilevel model showed no evidence of significant intervention effects on the primary or secondary outcomes."
