## Evidence

### Jun-ichiro Yasuda 2025

Jun-ichiro Yasuda, Michael M. Hull, Naohiro Mae, Kentaro Kojima. (2025). Chained computerized adaptive testing for the Force Concept Inventory. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/x2rq-z8lm](https://doi.org/10.1103/x2rq-z8lm)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical simulation comparing Chain-CAT (with collateral information, sigma-i = 0.3) against the pre-post method across all assumed progression models. At L = 5 administered nine times, the overall average RMSE of 0.277 is comparable to the pre-post value of 0.275, while the total test length drops from 60 to 45 items.

> "However, the overall averaged RMSE of Chain-CAT is comparable to that of the pre-post method even when the test length is L = 5 (compare the value of 0.275 in Table I to that of 0.277 in Table II)."
