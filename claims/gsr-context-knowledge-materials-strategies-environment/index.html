S. Vonschallen 2026¶

S. Vonschallen, D. Oberle, T. Schmiedel, and F. Eyssel. (2026). Knowledge-Based Design Requirements for Generative Social Robots in Higher Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12873 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2602.12873

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Results-section interview finding on context-knowledge: participants also considered course-related information such as timetables important for assessing the urgency of learning motivation, and required knowledge of educational strategies and required software tools in applied courses.

"Most participants expressed a desire for the robot to have access to official learning materials provided by the lecturer, such as slides, exercises, podcasts, and literature"

S. Vonschallen 2026 (2)¶

S. Vonschallen, D. Oberle, T. Schmiedel, and F. Eyssel. (2026). Knowledge-Based Design Requirements for Generative Social Robots in Higher Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12873

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Results-section interview finding: some participants found physical-environment awareness useful for avoiding distractions and adapting volume or lighting, while others "expressed privacy concerns", leading the authors to conclude perception should be optional.

"Accordingly, the robot's ability to perceive its physical environment should be optional."
