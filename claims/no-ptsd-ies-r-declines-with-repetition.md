## Evidence

### Ghazali Daniel Aiham 2019

Ghazali Daniel Aiham, Breque Cyril, Sosner Philippe, Lesbordes Mathieu, Chavagnat Jean-Jacques, Ragot Stéphanie, Oriot Denis. (2019). Stress response in the daily lives of simulation repeaters. A randomized controlled trial assessing stress evolution over one year of repetitive immersive simulations. PLoS ONE 14(7): e0220111. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0220111

`q3 · i?`

IES-R at day 7 and PCLS at 1 month after each session (response rates 90% and 89%). The article reports "In the experimental group, a significant decrease in IES-R was observed (F = 4.92, p<0.0001)" with Scheffe differences from the 5th session, and "The PCLS score remained unchanged (F = 1.06, p = 0.39)"; control-group IES-R F = 2.86, p = 0.07 and PCLS F = 0.86, p = 0.42.

> "No participant developed PTSD. IES-R score was 11.7±1.9 with a maximum of 34 and PCLS score was 19.0±0.8 with a maximum of 29."
