A. Bessa. (2025). The nox-minima dome: a tangible alternative to flat star maps for astronomy education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.21195](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.21195)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative workshop reports from UEFS (Bahia) and UFRN extension project Cometa Nordestino, including a one-hour session at a high school and 90-minute public assembly workshops. The article reports that "all participants expressed pride in their completed dome and interest in the information it contained". No systematic assessment was reported.

> "Reports from these workshops, carried out with teachers in the Professional Master's Program in Astronomy at UEFS, highlight the rich opportunities for conceptual discussion provided by the dome's concrete representation of the sky."
