# Students with positive prior ChatGPT experience showed confirmation bias, maintaining or improving quality ratings despite recognizing deficiencies

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students whose perception of answer quality stayed the same or improved (+1) acknowledged deficiencies such as superficial, incomplete, and imprecise answers, yet were impressed by ChatGPT’s examples and general capabilities, a tendency the authors explain as confirmation bias and which was mostly observed among students with prior ChatGPT experience. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of students who increased (+1) or maintained their quality ratings from pre- to post-questionnaire. The article states "This tendency was mostly observed among students who had prior experience with ChatGPT", e.g., S34 calling superficial wording clear.

> "This admiration hindered their critical evaluation of the answer quality, a phenomenon explained by confirmation bias [17]."
