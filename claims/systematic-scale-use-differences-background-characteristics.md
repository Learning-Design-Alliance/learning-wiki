## Evidence

### Coenen Johan 2021

Coenen Johan, Golsteyn Bart H. H., Stolp Tom, Tempelaar Dirk. (2021). Personality traits and academic performance: Correcting self-assessed traits with vignettes. PLoS ONE 16(3): e0248629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordered probit regressions of personality measures on background characteristics (Tables 2–4) among first-year Dutch economics students. The article concludes that "people differ in their scale use systematically", e.g. by nationality, making uncorrected predictors potentially unwarranted.

> "Column 2 shows that Belgian students report lower scores of Emotional Stability than Dutch students in the baseline sample (given other characteristics). However, column 3 reveals that this relationship is not robust when using the corrected measure for Emotional Stability."
