### Du P 2026

Du P, Zhou Y, Zhou Z, Wang H and Cao Q (2026). The synthetic position: generative artificial intelligence and the suspension of self-authorship in emerging adulthood. Front. Psychol. 17:1922087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1922087

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

A conceptual derivation of phenomenological markers from the model, made behavioral so that suspension is identified by what happens when the system is withdrawn, not by self-labels. The second marker is deferral of commitment despite exhausted deliberation; the third is the unavailability of the overview without the system.

> "The first is a discrepancy between fluency and ownership: the person can produce an articulate survey of their own options, values, and prospective selves, often composed with the system, while reporting little sense of authorship over it."
