Second-hand report in the article's conclusion of the effect sizes from Hattie's Visible Learning synthesis of over 800 meta-analyses. The article offers these figures as evidence that "quality teaching and teacher -student relationships" bring about meaningful learning; the synthesis itself was not read here.
