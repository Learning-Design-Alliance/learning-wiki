## Evidence

### Tari 2008

Somayeh Kazemi Tari. (2008). The Effect of Two Types of Feedback on Iranian EFL Learners' Tense-marker Production. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.2n.1p.108

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental two-group classroom study of 30 beginner female EFL learners receiving feedback on tense markers for 10 sessions. The article reports "the posttest means are significantly higher than the pretest means" for both groups, with t=6.92 (Group A) and t=4.30 (Group B); no effect size is printed.

> "From Figure 1, it is quite evident that the posttest means are significantly higher than the pretest means: t=6.92, p<0.05 for Group A, and t= 4.30, p<0.05 for Group B."
