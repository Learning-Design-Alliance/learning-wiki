`q2 i?` Latent class analysis identified five profiles: partial misconception, low conception, mixed conception, natural number-biased, and highly developed conception, with the High class largest at all three time points. [→ Kainulainen et al. 2017](#kainulainen-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Kainulainen et al. 2017

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Latent class analysis of 251 students' rational number test sum scores (correct and NNB responses for fractions and decimals) at three time points identified five classes. The article reports "the largest by number of participants was the High class" with the printed percentages above.

> "Through all time points, the largest by number of participants was the High class (34.7% of participants on T1; 41.8% on T2; 49.4% on T3), and the NnB class was the second largest (29.1% of participants on T1; 23.5% on T2; 29.5% on T3)."
