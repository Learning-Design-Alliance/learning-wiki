`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review using WWC 5.0 standards reports Reading Recovery, a one-on-one grade 1 tutoring intervention delivered in "30- minute, one- on-one sessions over 12–20 weeks", met strong ESSA evidence for literacy achievement; the EGM shows an improvement index of +26 for literacy achievement and +27 for academic dispositions (moderate).

> "Reading Recovery had strong evidence of positive effects for general literacy achievement and moderate evidence of positive effects for academic dispositions, which included an outcome from one study that measured students’ attitudes toward reading and literacy."
