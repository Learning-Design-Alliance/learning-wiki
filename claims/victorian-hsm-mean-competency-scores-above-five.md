## Evidence

### Liang Zhanming 2018

Liang Zhanming, Blackstock Felicity C., Howard Peter F, Briggs David S, Leggat Sandra G, Wollersheim Dennis, Edvardsson David, Rahman Aziz. (2018). An evidence-based approach to understanding the competency development needs of the health service management workforce in Australia. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3760-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional descriptive study of 93 middle-level managers (25 hospital, 68 community health services) assessed via the MCAP 360° tool; combined competency means were 5.35/5.39 (SA) and 5.81/5.69 (CCA). The article reports "Both the SA and CCA recorded mean scores were > 5" with no significant sector differences.

> "Both the SA and CCA recorded mean scores were > 5 for both hospital and CHS managers. There were no statistically significant differences between the SA competency means and CCA competency means between sectors."
