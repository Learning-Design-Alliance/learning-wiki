## Evidence

### Fei Yuda 2026

Fei Yuda, Zhang Yaning, Cai Jie, Xu Mei, Huang Jing, Du Bin. (2026). Effects of the BOPPPS model combined with case-based learning on knowledge acquisition and learning engagement in undergraduate nursing students: a multi-cohort quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09428-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Gain-score t-test on the same multi-cohort quasi-experiment; within-group improvements were 19.52 points for LBL and 34.93 points for BOPPPS-CBL, with "mean difference = 15.41" and Cohen's d = 1.77.

> "Gain score analysis confirmed significantly greater improvement in the intervention group (mean difference = 15.41, 95% CI: 10.78–20.03, p < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 1.77)."
