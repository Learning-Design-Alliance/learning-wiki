## Evidence

### Berat Ahi 2017

Berat Ahi. (2017). The Effect of Talking Drawings on Five-Year-Old Turkish Children’s Mental Models of the Water Cycle. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 12(3), 349-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141375

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Matched pretest/post-test semi-experimental study with 22 children per group in Kastamonu, Turkey; drawings classified into five levels. The article reports a significant post-test difference "in favor of the experimental group" with r = 0.8, described as large according to Cohen (1988).

> "The Mann -Whitney U Test results revealed that there is a significant difference between the control group (Md = 1.18, n = 22) and experimental group (Md = 3.54, n = 22) in terms of the levels of their post -test drawings, in favor of the experimental group, where U = 28, z = -5.531, p = 0.000, and the effect size was found to be r = 0.8."
