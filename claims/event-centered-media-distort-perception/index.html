Hanvey 1975¶

Hanvey, Robert G. (1975). An Attainable Global Perspective. Center for War/Peace Studies. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED116993

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

This is the essay's analytical argument, illustrated with examples such as deterrence policy, corporate investment, and protein consumption habits, about how event-centered news shapes public perception. The author states "the general perceptionof important phenomena is limited and distorted" by the media's threshold of excitability. No empirical test is reported; the support is the argument and its examples.

"The result of this pattern is that the general perceptionof important phenomena is limited and distorted; the public sees only those manifestations that arenovel enough to rise above the media's threshold of excitability."
