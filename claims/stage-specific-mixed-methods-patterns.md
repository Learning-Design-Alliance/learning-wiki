`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Joint display analysis and side-by-side comparison integrating quantitative trajectories with qualitative themes from 18 interviews, following established mixed-methods integration frameworks. The article reports "a complementarity pattern in Year 1" and "a convergence pattern in Years 2–3" among other stage-specific patterns.

> "Integrated mixed-methods analysis revealed stage-specific characteristics across academic years: a complementarity pattern in Year 1 (foundational awareness building), a convergence pattern in Years 2–3 (systematic competency construction), an expansion pattern during the Year 3 transition (role adaptation challenges), and a convergence pattern in Year 4 (comprehensive transformation and professional confidence enhancement)."
