Evidence¶

Dolmans 1994¶

Dolmans, D., Schmidt, H. & Gijselaers, W. (1994). The relationship between student-generated learning issues and self-study in problem-based learning. Instructional Science: An International Journal of Learning and Cognition, 22(4), 251–267. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00891779

q1 · i? · associational · r1

The article's framing statement, made in the abstract, that the core PBL assumption linking group-generated learning issues to self-study "has never been tested"; this study provides the test.

"A major assumption of problem-based learning (PBL) is that learning issues, generated by students while discussing a problem, are used as guides for self-directed learning activities. This assumption, though basic to PBL, has never been tested."
