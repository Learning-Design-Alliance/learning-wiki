## Evidence

### Kamimura Go 2025

Kamimura Go, Maeda Koki, Aoki Masaya, Imamura Satomi, Morizono Shoichiro, Tokunaga Takuya, Umehara Tadashi, Harada-Takeda Aya, Nagata Toshiyuki, Ueda Kazuhiro. (2025). Importance of future motivation for skill acquisition among medical students undergoing endoscopic forceps training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07048-3

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of time-trial records from 98 fourth-grade medical students during two-week dry laboratory training; male students' times are reported here, showing "improved from 73.6 ± 67.2 s in the first half to 45.2 ± 45.4 s in the second half".

> "The time (mean ± 2SD) of the male medical students improved from 73.6 ± 67.2 s in the first half to 45.2 ± 45.4 s in the second half (p < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 0.96, 95% CI [17.14, 37.42])."

### Kamimura Go 2025 (2)

Kamimura Go, Maeda Koki, Aoki Masaya, Imamura Satomi, Morizono Shoichiro, Tokunaga Takuya, Umehara Tadashi, Harada-Takeda Aya, Nagata Toshiyuki, Ueda Kazuhiro. (2025). Importance of future motivation for skill acquisition among medical students undergoing endoscopic forceps training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07048-3

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Same two-week dry-laboratory time-trial dataset, female students: times "improved from 67.0 ± 40.6 s in the first half to 48.4 ± 26.6 s in the second half" with Cohen's d = 1.18.

> "Similarly, the time of the female medical students improved from 67.0 ± 40.6 s in the first half to 48.4 ± 26.6 s in the second half (p < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 1.18, 95% CI [11.59, 27.85])."
