## Evidence

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA with Aligned Rank Transformation on unassisted test composite deviation from a within-subjects crossover study with 24 novices. Main effect of condition (F 1,22 = 23.40, p < .001, η² = .52) and condition*group interaction (F 1,22 = 34.81, p < .001, η² = .61). Post-hoc comparisons showed participants who had just trained in AR performed significantly better in their first test (p = .032).

> "Analysis of composite deviation revealed a main effect ofCONDITION(F 1,22 = 23.40, p < .001, η2 =.52) and aCONDITION*GROUPinteraction (F 1,22 = 34.81, p < .001, η2 =.61)."

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026 (2)

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Skill-level analyses in the unassisted test session identified Work Angle and Travel Speed as the primary drivers. Work angle main effect (F 1,22 = 9.80, p = .005, η² = .31) and interaction (F 1,22 = 12.11, p = .002, η² = .36); speed deviation showed even stronger effects for condition (F 1,22 = 17.33, p < .001, η² = .44) and interaction (F 1,22 = 26.64, p < .001, η² = .55).
