Exploratory comparison of normalized change for the five Podium Players who took both surveys in the remote physics laboratory study. Males averaged ⟨c⟩ = 0.348 versus 0.063 for females; the authors write this "seems to contradict the hypothesis" about vicarious experiences benefiting females. No effect size is printed.

> "For male podium players, the average change⟨c⟩ was 0.348, and for females was 0.063. This result seems to contradict the hypothesis that doing well compared to others (as measured by Kahoot! performance) corresponds to greater increases in self-eﬃcacy perceptions for females"
