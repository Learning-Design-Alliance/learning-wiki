Bauer, E., Greiff, S., Graesser, A. C., Scheiter, K., & Sailer, M. (2025). Looking Beyond the Hype: Understanding the Effects of AI on Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10020-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Ma et al. (2014) and Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper (2014), that ITS augmentation effects include AI-driven instructional support enhancing learning beyond unsupported learning or lower-quality non-AI support. Magnitudes are described as "medium to large" and "small to medium"; no numeric effect sizes are printed.

> "meta-analyses found medium to large learning benefits for ITS, which provide feedback and targeted hints, compared to self- reliant learning without support, and small to medium gains when comparing ITS to non- adaptive or less adaptive computer-assisted learning (Ma et al., 2014; Steenbergen- Hu & Cooper, 2014)."
