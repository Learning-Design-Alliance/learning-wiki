Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I 2014¶

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Cluster randomized effectiveness study (RAND Corporation, Pane et al., 2014) with two cohorts in 73 high schools across 7 states and over 13,000 students. The summary reports "students in schools using Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I scored higher on the Algebra Proficiency Exam (effect size = +0.07)" and a second-cohort effect size of +0.21.

"One cluster randomized study evaluated Carnegie Learning's blended solution with two separate cohorts of students in 73 high schools across 7 states with over 13,000 students."
