The article reports the group means and p-values quoted; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "In the total score for the theoretical TestT3, paramedics (82.9 ± 9.2) achieved a comparable result to physicians (81.0 ± 8.7; p = 0.55) without a significant difference. Paramedics achieved a significantly higher result than the group of students (76.7 ± 8.5; p < 0.001)."
