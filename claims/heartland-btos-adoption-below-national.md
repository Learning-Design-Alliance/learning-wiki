Analysis of the U.S. Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey AI Supplement, which measures whether employers have actually adopted AI in their operations. The brief reports Heartland adoption averaging "15.3%" against a national rate of roughly 17–20% of businesses across late 2025 and early-to-mid 2026, concentrated in office-oriented functions.

> "Across the 19 Heartland states, BTOS puts current adoption at an average of 15.3%, ranging from 9.0% in North Dakota to 19.7% in Minnesota. Six of the 19 states meet or exceed the national rate, so the regional lag is real but uneven rather than universal."
