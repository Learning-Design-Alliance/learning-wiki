Jason Paul Siko 2011¶

Jason Paul Siko. (2011). Homemade PowerPoint Games. http://it.coe.uga.edu/wwild/pptgames

q1 · i? · design · r?

The article reports, citing Barbour, Kromrei et al. (2009), an analysis of questions from the Barbour, Kinsella, and Rieber (2011) study: "94%" were Knowledge level and "Further, none of the questions were above the “Application” level."

"In their analysis of over 1,900 questions, the authors found the overwhelming majority of questions (i.e., 94%) were “Knowledge” level questions, which is the simplest form of question based on Bloom’s (1956) taxonomy."
