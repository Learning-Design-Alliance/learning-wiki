### Digital Promise 2020

Digital Promise. (2020). Every Learner Everywhere and Lighthouse Institutions: First-Year Experiences. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/abf8db78-dc7a-4f4f-b06a-b00d1bda63e8

Online survey of instructors at nine of 10 piloting institutions; 39 courseware-using respondents (45 percent response rate). Most used blended approaches with 36 to 65 percent of learning through courseware; "Two-thirds (66 percent)" looked at whole-class dashboards weekly or more.

> "A large majority of the instructors who used adaptive courseware in fall 2019 Every Learner Everywhere pilots (89 percent) believed using the product had improved student learning."
