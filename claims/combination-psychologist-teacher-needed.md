Author's argument in the closing section of the article about who will carry out the needed research. The article counters Münsterberg's objection by maintaining that what is needed is not sympathy for the pupil's personality but "sympathy engendered by insight and directed to a pupil in a particular critical situation."

> "The expert educational psychologist alone cannot solve them. Neither can the ex]>ert empirical teacher. It will be that rare combination of two, just that combination which Miinsterberg, in his Psychology and the Teacher, thinks impossible"
