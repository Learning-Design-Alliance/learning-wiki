### Dale Esther Dasari 2025

Dale Esther Dasari, Abulela Mohammed A. A., Jia Hao, Violato Claudio. (2025). Are medical school preclinical tests biased for sex and race? A differential item functioning analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06540-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-data fit evaluation preceding the DIF analyses, reported in Table 2. Histology (CFI 0.99, RMSEA 0.007) and physiology (CFI 0.96, RMSEA 0.011) fit well; for anatomy, "the RMSEA and SRMSR were very good indicating model-data fit" despite lower CFI/TLI.

> "Based on Table 2, Histology and Physiology had excellent model-data fit, since the M2 statistic was not significant, the CFI and TLI indices were above 0.90, and the RMSEA and SRMSR were below 0.08."
