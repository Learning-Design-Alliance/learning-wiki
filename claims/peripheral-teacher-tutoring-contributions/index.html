de Laat 2007¶

de Laat, M., Lally, V., Lipponen, L. & Simons, R.-J. (2007). Online teaching in networked learning communities: A multi-method approach to studying the role of the teacher. Instructional Science, vol. 35, no. 3, pp. 257-286. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-006-9007-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Content analysis of coded learning and tutoring activities (Cohen's Kappa 0.86 on a 10% sample) across the three phase samples, showing all participants including the teacher covered both learning and tutoring statements (Tables 5 and 6).

"The teacher’s style is to act as a learner, which might suggest he is trying to participate in this community on an equal basis. The teacher’s involvement reduces somewhat in the middle and ending phase (as was supported by the SNA ﬁndings) and is concentrated on tutoring."
