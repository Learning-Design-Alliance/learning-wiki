## Evidence

### Chartrand G 2021

Chartrand G, Soucisse M, Dubé P, Trépanier J-S, Drolet P, Sideris L. (2021). Self-directed learning by video as a means to improve technical skills in surgery residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02524-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group change across the two filmed procedures in the RCT (n = 22, 11 per group), analyzed with repeated-measures two-way ANOVA. The authors report "mean difference between video 1 and 2 with 95% CI" for each group; no effect size was printed.

> "but both groups significantly improved their performances during the interval (mean difference between video 1 and 2 with 95% CI in control: 3.5 [1.1; 5.9] and SDL-V: 5.8 [3.4; 8.2])"
