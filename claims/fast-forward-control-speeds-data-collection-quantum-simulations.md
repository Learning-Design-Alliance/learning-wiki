## Evidence

### Antje Kohnle 2013

Antje Kohnle, Charles Baily, Christopher Hooley and Bruce Torrance. (2013). Optimization of Simulations and Activities for a New Introductory Quantum Mechanics Curriculum. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/35/1/015001](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/35/1/015001)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-interview survey analysis of student suggestions at Boulder. The quote is the printed count of improvement comments: "six comments (out of 40 su ggestions for improvement)" complaining data collection was too slow.

> "From the survey questions on suggestions for improvement, there were six comments (out of 40 su ggestions for improvement) from Boulder students that it took too long to collect proper statistics, and that th ey would like some way to speed up data collection."

### Antje Kohnle 2013 (2)

Antje Kohnle, Charles Baily, Christopher Hooley and Bruce Torrance. (2013). Optimization of Simulations and Activities for a New Introductory Quantum Mechanics Curriculum. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/35/1/015001](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/35/1/015001)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Follow-up survey of St Andrews students after the fast-forward button was incorporated. The article reports that none of "59 in total" improvement comments concerned data-collection speed, suggesting the control resolved the issue.

> "None of the St Andrews students’ comments (59 in total) on suggestions for improvement for the hidden  variable simulation pertained to the speed of data collection, suggesting that the additional control re solved this issue."
