The review reports the result of its own targeted literature searching across PubMed and CINAHL with citation searching. It notes that pre-pandemic consensus competencies for nursing telehealth exist (van Houwelingen and colleagues) and that advanced-practice frameworks such as the Four Ps of Telehealth were developed, but a "literature search failed to identify any post-pandemic scholarship" for the pre-licensure level.

> "However, a literature search failed to identify any post-pandemic scholarship aimed at establishing competencies for pre-licensure telehealth nursing education, or any attempt at defining competencies in relation to the new era of digital health."
