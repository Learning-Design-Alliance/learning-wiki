# Both the Traditional and Reversed algorithm groups significantly outperform a single-tone Control group in soundscape recognition accuracy, without differing from each other

## Evidence

### Kucinkas 2025

Kucinkas, A., Retsa, C., Meijer, P. B. L., Wallace, M. T., Gori, M. & Murray, M. M. (2025). Learning visual to auditory sensory substitution reveals flexibility in image to sound mapping. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00385-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

3×2 mixed-model repeated measures ANOVA in the evaluation phase (randomized assignment, 60 sighted adults); "Participants in both the Traditional and Reversed groups outperformed those in the Control group", with printed effect size η²ₚ = 0.495 (partial eta-squared = 0.495), a large effect.

> "A mixed-model repeated measures ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of Group on accuracy (F(2, 57) = 27.95, p < 0.001, η²ₚ = 0.495). Participants in both the Traditional and Reversed groups outperformed those in the Control group."

### Kucinkas 2025 (2)

Kucinkas, A., Retsa, C., Meijer, P. B. L., Wallace, M. T., Gori, M. & Murray, M. M. (2025). Learning visual to auditory sensory substitution reveals flexibility in image to sound mapping. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00385-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc unpaired t-tests from the same evaluation-phase ANOVA; the Traditional group showed "significantly higher accuracy than the Control group" (M₍diff₎= 33.63%) and Reversed vs Control M₍diff₎= 24.79%; no effect sizes printed, so impact is null.

> "Post-hoc comparisons (2-sided unpaired t-tests) using Bonferroni correction indicated that the Traditional group showed significantly higher accuracy than the Control group (p < 0.001, M₍diff₎= 33.63%), as did the Reversed group compared to the Control group (p < 0.001, M₍diff₎= 24.79%)."
