Evidence¶

Liu 2010¶

Liu, X., Liu, S., Lee, S.-h., & Magjuka, R. J. (2010). Cultural Differences in Online Learning: International Student Perceptions. Educational Technology & Society, 13(3), 177-188. https://www.jstor.org/stable/jeductechsoci.13.3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative case study interviews with seventeen international students from three global companies in an online MBA program. Students described cultural patterns such as U.S. students being "more independent" while Chinese students were "more polite", yet reported no negative impact on collaboration.

"The students noted different culture patterns in online collaboration and communication but indicated that these differences did not affect their learning in a negative way."
