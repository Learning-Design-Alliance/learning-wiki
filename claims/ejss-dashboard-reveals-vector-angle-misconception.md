## Evidence

### Wee 2024

Wee, L. K., Tan, D., Garcia Clemente, F. J., & Esquembre, F. (2024). Easy JavaScript Simulation (EJSS) Data Analytics for Singapore. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.09676](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.09676)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' illustrative example (Section 2.1) of the vectors simulation: a student's |A| answer was correct but the ϕ answer was incorrect, and the pattern across Q1 and Q3 "suggests a consistent misconception about the angle ϕ". This is a demonstration case, not a controlled study; no sample size or effect size is printed.

> "Figure 2 shows the teacher dashboard suggests that the annotated student is likely mistaking the angle ϕ with its complementary angle (90° – ϕ). By looking at similar randomly generated questions such as Q1 and Q3, the teacher can help the student recognise the pattern of errors."
