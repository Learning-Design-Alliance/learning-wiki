# Group differences on the main listening test were statistically significant overall (F = 17.13), with topic preparation and repetition outperforming control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A one-way ANOVA on the main listening test showed a statistically significant difference among the four groups, F = 17.13, p < .01. [→ Rouhi 2014](#rouhi-2014)
`q3 i?` Post-hoc (Student-Newman-Keuls) analysis located the differences: topic preparation and repetition of input outperformed the control group, while the previewing group did not differ significantly from control. [→ Rouhi 2014](#rouhi-2014)

## Evidence

### Rouhi 2014

Rouhi, A., Nabavi, S.-M., & Mohebbi, H. (2014). The effects of previewing questions, repetition of input, and topic preparation on listening comprehension of Iranian EFL learners. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 2(2), 73-85. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on main-test scores of the four groups (25, 26, 25, 26 participants) in the classroom experiment; the omnibus effect was significant at F = 17.13, p < .01. No effect size printed.

> "the results obtained from the ANOVA indicated that the difference among the four participating groups  was statistically significant ( F = 17.13, p< .01).Furthermore, each text's results were also examined."

### Rouhi 2014 (2)

Rouhi, A., Nabavi, S.-M., & Mohebbi, H. (2014). The effects of previewing questions, repetition of input, and topic preparation on listening comprehension of Iranian EFL learners. Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 2(2), 73-85. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student-Newman-Keuls post-hoc analysis of the same four-group experiment located the pairwise differences; topic preparation and repetition did not differ significantly. No effect size printed.

> "Further data analysis revealed that the topic preparation group outperformed the other participating groups. However, the difference between topic preparation and repetition of input was not statistically significant."
