Chandralekha Singh, Laura Moin and Christian Schunn. (2016). Helping science and engineering majors to reflect upon teaching and learning issues. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02950](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02950)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Course evaluation data from content-based pre-/post-tests administered during recitation to introductory physics students taught by the undergraduate pairs. The article reports typical scores of "40% and 90% respectively with a Hake normalized gain of 0 .8"; no sample size or significance test is printed.

> "The typical pre-/post-t est scores were 40% and 90% re- spectively with a Hake normalized gain of 0 .8 [30]. We note that the pre-test refers to the test given after traditional classroom instruction but before the tuto rials."

### C. Singh 2016

C. Singh, L. Moin, and C. Schunn. (2016). Increasing interest and awareness about teaching in science undergraduates. arXiv:1603.02949 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02949](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02949)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content-based pre-/post-tests given to introductory physics students during recitation, before and after student-designed tutorials. The article reports "typical pre-/post-test scores were 40% and 90% respectively with a Hake normalized gain of 0 .8"; the pre-test was given after traditional instruction but before the tutorials.

> "The content-based pre-/post-tests accompanying the tutorials were given to the introductory physics students during the recitation. The typical pre-/post-test scores were 40% and 90% respectively with a Hake normalized gain of 0 .8 [14]."
