## Evidence

### Xie 2024

Xie, C., Zhang, S., Qiao, X., & Hao, N. (2024). Transcranial direct current stimulation targeting the bilateral IFG alters cognitive processes during creative ideation. npj Science of Learning, 9, 75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00285-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Breakpoint mixed-effect models of CRA reaction time in the tDCS experiment; the interaction of RSD with group and the square of RSD with group were both significant (RSD×[(L+R−)—SHAM]: t = 2.508, p = 0.012; RSD²×[(L+R−)—SHAM]: t = −2.448, p = 0.015), and the quote names the U-shaped versus "monotonically decreasing" patterns.

> "Breakpoint regression for the L−R+ and Sham groups revealed a U-shaped relationship between RSD and CRA reaction time in the Sham group, whereas this relationship was monotonically decreasing in the L−R+ group"
