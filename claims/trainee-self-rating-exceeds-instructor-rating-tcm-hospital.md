## Evidence

### Mao Jing 2026

Mao Jing, Sun Ruimin, Yang Yanming, Zhong Yuan, Bai Yanhui, Qin Yangyang, Liu Shu, Qin Yuanmei. (2026). Evaluating Standardised Training Effectiveness for Trainee Nurses and Clinical Instructors in a Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital: A Mixed‐Methods Study in Henan, China. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70621

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing 89 trainee nurses' self-ratings with 80 clinical instructors' ratings of the same trainees on a 30-item competency scale. The article reports "a statistically significant difference between the two groups of scores (t = 3.309, p = 0.001; Cohen's d = 0.51)".

> "The nurse students' self‐evaluation score of competency was 4.52 ± 0.31 (mastery level), whereas the teacher's evaluation score was 4.28 ± 0.45 (mastery level). The independent samples t‐test results showed a statistically significant difference between the two groups of scores (t = 3.309, p = 0.001; Cohen's d = 0.51), indicating a moderate effect size"
