## Evidence

### Faure Theresa 2024

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

PANAS positive-affect scale analyzed by mixed model across the nine measuring points of the randomized study; the article reports the significance of the decline descriptively without an effect size.

> "Positive affect decreased significantly from t0 to t8 in all study groups. However, the decrease was stronger in the CG than in the intervention groups."

### Faure Theresa 2024 (2)

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The group-by-time interaction was significant (F(16, 1117) = 3.15) and post hoc comparisons located the IVFF advantage at t3, t4, t7 and t8; only ps were printed. For negative affect the trend was reciprocal.

> "More importantly, however, the difference between study groups differed between time points (F(16, 1117) = 3.15, p < 0.01): Positive affect decreased significantly from t0 to t8 in all study groups. However, the decrease was stronger in the CG than in the intervention groups. Scale scores for positive affect were significantly higher in IVFF than in the CG at t3 and t4 as well as t7 and t8 (ps <= 0.02)."
