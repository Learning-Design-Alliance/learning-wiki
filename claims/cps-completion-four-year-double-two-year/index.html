Malone 2021¶

Malone, M., Mahaffie, S., Hernandez, G., Usher, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2021). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2020. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/hs

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of six-year college completion for the CPS class of 2013 by immediate enrollment destination (Figures 3 and 4); the four-year-enrollee rate "rose slightly in the most recent year... from 54 percent to 56 percent," and nearly 80 percent of two-year-enrollee completions were associate degrees or certificates.

"CPS graduates who immediately enrolled in a two-year college were half as likely as immediate four-year enrollees to complete a college degree or certificate: 28 percent of immediate two-year enrollees in the class of 2013 completed college by spring 2019, compared to 56 percent of immediate four-year enrollees."
