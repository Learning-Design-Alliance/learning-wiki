### Loibl 2024

Loibl, K., & Leuders, T. (2024). Can failure be made productive also in Bayesian reasoning? A conceptual replication study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09670-y

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

In the same 2×2 experiment (N = 75), the ANOVA on conceptual knowledge found no significant interaction between order of phases and type of instruction (F(1,71) = 0.84, p = .36, ηp² < 0.012), failing to support the hypothesis that PS-Its would outperform all other conditions; descriptively PS-Its scored highest (2.63 points, SD = 1.07).
