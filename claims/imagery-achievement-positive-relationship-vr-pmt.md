Satoshi Fukumori, Kayoko Miura, Ayako Takamori, and Sadao Otsuka. (2025). Preliminary Study on Virtual Reality Framework for Effective Prospective Memory Training: Integration of Visual Imagery and Daily-life Simulations. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.3389/frvir.2025.1555111](https://doi.org/10.3389/frvir.2025.1555111)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author interpretation of the correlation results in the ten-participant validation: the positive relationship between imagery task achievement and overall task performance is read as evidence that imagery strategies matter for the VR tasks.

> "The implementation of visual imagery strategies within the VR environment appears to enhance task performance, as indicated by the positive relationship between imagery task achievement and overall task performance."
