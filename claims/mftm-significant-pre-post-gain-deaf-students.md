### Thai and Yasin 2016

Liong Kon Thai & Mohd. Hanafi Mohd Yasin. (2016). Magic Finger Teaching Method in Learning Multiplication Facts among Deaf Students. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n3p40

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-post analysis of the experimental group (n = 35) using t-test; the article reports a significant gain from pre-test mean 29.86 to post-test mean 40.00, with "t = 4.793, df = 68, p < 0.05".
