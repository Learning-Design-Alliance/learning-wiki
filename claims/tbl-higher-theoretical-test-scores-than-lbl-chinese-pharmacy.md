## Evidence

### Lang Bingchen 2019

Lang Bingchen, Zhang Lingli, Lin Yunzhu, Han Lu, Zhang Chuan, Liu Yantao. (2019). Team-based learning pedagogy enhances the quality of Chinese pharmacy education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1724-6

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 12 randomized controlled studies involving 1271 participants (631 receiving TBL) across Chinese pharmacy curricula. Because of heterogeneity (I2 = 98%), a random-effects model was used; the pooled estimate favored TBL with "SMD = 2.55, 95% CI [1.56, 3.55], p < 0.00001".

> "Compared with the ones receiving traditional LBL pedagogy, students receiving TBL pedagogy were tend to get higher marks in objective theoretical tests (SMD = 2.55, 95% CI [1.56, 3.55], p < 0.00001)."
