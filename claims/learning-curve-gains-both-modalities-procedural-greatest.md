## Evidence

### Raison Nicholas 2021

Raison Nicholas, Harrison Patrick, Abe Takashige, Aydin Abdullatif, Ahmed Kamran, Dasgupta Prokar. (2021). Procedural virtual reality simulation training for robotic surgery: a randomised controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-08197-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning curve analysis of simulator metric data plotted for the first five attempts of comparable tasks, with Z scores used to allow comparison between exercises. Improvement was seen with both techniques, most markedly in clutch usage, instrument collisions and instrument movements; no effect size is printed.

> "Greatest improvements were seen in the procedural (BND and UVA) tasks especially for total time, number of movements for right instrument and path length for right instrument"
