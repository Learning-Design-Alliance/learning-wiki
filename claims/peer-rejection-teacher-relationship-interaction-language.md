## Evidence

### Femke van der Wilt 2025

Femke van der Wilt, Jantine Spilt, Sharisse van Driel, Hilde Colpin. (2025). The moderating role of the teacher-child relationship in the association between peer rejection and language competence in early childhood education. Learning and Instruction 100 (2025) 102217. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel (linear mixed model) analyses with random intercepts in N = 328 children nested in 17 classes; Model 10 added the interaction term. The article reports "the interaction effect of peer rejection and teacher-child closeness/conflict was significant"; Table 2 prints p = .043 (closeness) and p < .001 (conflict), with no effect size.
