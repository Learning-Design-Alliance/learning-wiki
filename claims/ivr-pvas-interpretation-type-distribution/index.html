Evidence¶

Liu 2025¶

Liu, T.-C., & Lin, Y.-C. (2025). Development and implementation of an IVR-based assessment system for student teachers' professional vision. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10463-4

q2 · i? · design · r3

Analysis of interpretation-subtask responses in the 24-participant implementation case study, classified by whether participants consulted the virtual tablet and which option they chose. The article reports 'the likely interpretation was made only 80 times (51.61%)'; irrational interpretation occurred 38 times (24.52%), interpretation without reference 18 times (11.61%), attaching importance to surface behaviours 15 times (9.68%), and looking only at surface behaviours 4 times (2.58%).

"Of the 155 interpretations, the likely interpretation was made only 80 times (51.61%); in these cases, the participant selected the appropriate option after referring to the target student's background."
