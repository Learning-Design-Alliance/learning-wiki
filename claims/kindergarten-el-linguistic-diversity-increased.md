### Novicoff 2024

Novicoff, S., Reardon, S. F., & Johnson, R. C. (2024). California's English learners and their long-term learning outcomes. Learning Policy Institute.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of home-language composition of kindergarten ELs across cohorts. The report also finds that "the prevalence of kindergarten ELs speaking mandarin in the home more than doubled over the time span of our data and is now at 2.9% of kindergarten ELs," and Arabic similarly more than doubled.

> "more than three quarters of all students speak Spanish upon entering California schools as kindergarten ELs. This percentage has decreased over time, with Spanish speakers accounting for 83.8% of kindergarten ELs in 2006 and 77.3% in 2018 (a decrease of 6.5 percentage points, or 8%)."
