Evidence¶

Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim 2026¶

Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim. (2026). Exploring teachers' instrumental orchestration and roles in high school mathematics classes using AI-based digital tools. The Mathematical Education 65(2), 309-335. https://doi.org/10.63311/mathedu.26.6525

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

In the discussion of the case analysis of six lessons, the authors report that among the existing types, explain-the-screen — in which the teacher explained mathematical concepts and problem solving via the electronic board or shared screen — was observed 24 times across the 46 episodes, the most frequent.

"한편 기존 유형 중에서는 ‘화면-설명하기’가 전체 46개 에피소드 중 24회로 가장 많이 관찰되었다."

Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim 2026 (2)¶

Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim. (2026). Exploring teachers' instrumental orchestration and roles in high school mathematics classes using AI-based digital tools. The Mathematical Education 65(2), 309-335. https://doi.org/10.63311/mathedu.26.6525

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

In the discussion, the authors state that in AI-based lessons the teacher immediately reflected student misconceptions and solution status grasped through individual AI feedback and dashboards, adjusting the content, depth, and pace of explanation in real time.

"즉 AI 기반 수업에서의 '화면-설명하기'는 기존 수업과 같이 사전에 계획된 내용을 일방적으로 전달하는 행위가 아니라, AI가 산출한 학습 데이터에 반응하여 실시간으로 재구성되는 역동적 교수 행위로 작동하였다."
