Evidence¶

Al-Ghazo 2016¶

ABEER AL-GHAZO. (2016). The Effect of Explicit Instruction of Meta Cognitive Learning Strategies on Promoting Jordanian Language Learners' reading competence. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.10. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099460

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of two classes of Level one English Course students at Ajloun National University, 30 per group, analyzed with an independent samples T-Test. The article reports "the experimental group outperformed the control group in the reading post-test"; Table 1 shows post-test means of 54.66 (experimental) and 41.45 (control). No effect size is printed.

"Independent samples T-Test was used to analyze the possible variances in reading comprehension between the two groups participated in the reading comprehension tests."
