## Subclaims
`q2 i2` First-grade English Learners in the morphological-awareness vocab+ condition gained more target word vocabulary than the PAD-only control (d = .64), and the semantic-relations condition outperformed it as well (d = .54), but the ANCOVA was not statistically significant. [→ Filippini 2012](#filippini-2012)

## Evidence

### Filippini 2012

Filippini, A. L., Gerber, M. M., & Leafstedt, J. M. (2012). A Vocabulary-Added Reading Intervention for English Learners At-Risk of Reading Difficulties. International Journal of Special Education, 27(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001055

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on posttest target vocabulary (VOC) with pretest VOC as covariate across the three conditions in this exploratory classroom study of 71 first graders. The article also reports "the average student in the MA group performed at the 74 th percentile of the PAD group ( d = .64)" and d = .54 for SR; within-group gains were d = .56 (MA), d = .28 (SR), d = .04 (PAD).

> "Mean differences between groups at posttest were large but not statistically reliable, F(2, 67) = 2.263, ( p = .112)."
