## Evidence

### Orsi Talles Dias 2020

Orsi Talles Dias, Ana Lucia Ribeiro Valadares, Paula Miranda Esteves Orsi, Isabella Miranda Esteves Orsi, Alexandre Sampaio Moura. (2020). Simulation-based Training for Pelvic and Breast Physical Examination: Effect on the Anxiety and Self-confidence of Medical Students. RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0040-1718433

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal pre-post study of 80 fourth-year medical students completing two SBT sessions during an Ob/Gyn clerkship, using a 12-item Likert questionnaire adapted from the F-PEXS. The paired t-test showed scores "decreased significantly from 2.04 ± 0.80 at baseline to 1.46 ± 0.50 following SBT".

> "The anxiety scores in relation to the overall gynecological examination decreased significantly from 2.04 ± 0.80 at baseline to 1.46 ± 0.50 following SBT ( p  < 0.001)."
