## Evidence

### Alkattan Rana 2026

Alkattan Rana, Alreshaid Lulwah, Alfallaj Hayam, Pillai Arun Gopinathan, Alqahtani Hussam M., Suhaym Omar, Almohefer Sami, Madfa Ahmed A. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this randomized study, AI assistance was available only during training and not during the post-test, so the design evaluated the educational carryover effect of AI-assisted training on independent interpretation.

> "students who received AI-assisted training demonstrated diagnostic performance comparable to that of students trained using conventional methods, with no statistically significant differences in diagnostic accuracy observed between the two groups"
