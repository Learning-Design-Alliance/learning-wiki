## Evidence

### Singh Yogen 2025

Singh Yogen, Ramanathan Rangasamy, Ebrahimi Mahmood, Noori Shahab. (2025). A narrative review of Neonatal Echocardiography Simulator Training (NEST): role of simulators in acquiring neonatal echocardiography skills effectively. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-04321-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a pre-post study of 43 neonatal care givers (16 neonatologists, 26 neonatology fellows, 1 nurse practitioner) who had didactic sessions followed by hands-on simulator training and were tested before and after on obtaining 26 standard views. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "the median (interquartile) score for the quality of acquired images increased from 36 (22, 43) to 55 (48, 58), p < 0.0001, and the number of views with acceptable or good image quality (scores of 2 or 3) increased from 11 (6, 16) to 20 (17, 21), p < 0.0001"
