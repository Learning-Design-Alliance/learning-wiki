## Evidence

### Bjork 2016

Bjork, R. A., & Yue, C. L. (2016). Commentary: Is disfluency desirable? Metacognition Learning, 11, 133–137. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9156-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Rummer, Schweppe, and Schwede presented lists of aliens and their characteristics, with four lists in a consistent format and a fifth list in the opposite format so that either the fluent or disfluent list was distinctive (as summarized in this commentary). No effects of disfluency or distinctiveness emerged.

> "Across three experiments (one on the computer and two on paper), they found no effect of disfluency and, surprisingly, no effect of distinctiveness."
