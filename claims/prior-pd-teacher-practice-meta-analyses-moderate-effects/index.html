Evidence¶

Lynch 2025¶

Lynch, K., Gonzalez, K., Hill, H., & Merritt, R. (2025). A Meta-Analysis of the Experimental Evidence Linking Mathematics and Science Professional Development Interventions to Teacher Knowledge, Classroom Instruction, and Student Achievement. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584251335302

q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports, citing Kraft et al. (2018), that coaching programs across content areas produced a pooled mean effect size of "0.49 SD on teacher practice outcomes".

"Combining experimental and rigorous quasi-experimental studies, Kraft et al. found a pooled mean impact of 0.49 SD on teacher practice outcomes."

Lynch, K., Gonzalez, K., Hill, H., & Merritt, R. (2025). A Meta-Analysis of the Experimental Evidence Linking Mathematics and Science Professional Development Interventions to Teacher Knowledge, Classroom Instruction, and Student Achievement. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584251335302

q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

The review reports, citing Garrett et al. (2019), that in-service teacher PD across content areas yielded a printed mean effect size of "0.42 SD on observation scores".

"Pooling across these interventions, the authors found a mean effect size of 0.42 SD on observation scores."

Lynch 2025 (3)¶

Lynch, K., Gonzalez, K., Hill, H., & Merritt, R. (2025). A Meta-Analysis of the Experimental Evidence Linking Mathematics and Science Professional Development Interventions to Teacher Knowledge, Classroom Instruction, and Student Achievement. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584251335302

q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

The review reports, citing Kowalski et al. (2020), a pooled mean effect size of "0.49 SD on knowledge outcomes" from science interventions for preservice and/or in-service teachers.

"The authors found a pooled mean effect size of 0.49 SD on knowledge outcomes and noted that effect sizes from instructional practice and attitude outcomes were substantially smaller than those identified for knowledge outcomes."
