## Evidence

### Feng 2025

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments companion report: Implementation analysis of the ASSISTments intervention. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677784

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Rural subsample quartile analysis of the quasi-experimental study (1,423 rural students total). The rural overall sample did not significantly outperform matched virtual students; the effect was "driven primarily by students in the 2nd achievement quartile."

> "The 421 students in rural schools who scored between the 25th and 50th percentiles at baseline significantly outperformed the comparison group by 1.53 points (p = 0.050; effect size = 0.21)."
