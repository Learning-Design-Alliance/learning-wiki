## Evidence

### Mielke I 2025

Mielke I, Hampe W, Ehrhardt M, Knorr M. (2025). Spoilt for Choice in Undergraduate Medical Admission: Selection of Confident and Considerate Students Using Professional Prequalification and Situational Judgement Test. Perspectives on Medical Education 14(1). https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1571

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Reported in a footnote as a secondary analysis with a printed effect size of "d = .37" for females outperforming males; the article attributes the comparison of d = .11 to a meta-analysis and offers SJT content related to conscientiousness or agreeableness as a possible explanation.

> "The performance difference between females and males on the HAM-SJT corresponds to an effect size of d = .37. A meta-analysis reported a slightly smaller effect of d = .11 for females outperforming males, but with stronger effect sizes for SJTs related to conscientiousness or agreeableness, which may explain the higher effect in the present study [53]."
