## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 14/26 respondents rated the workshop difficult (score 4 or 5), while all 26 respondents rated it interesting (score 4 or 5). [→ Makito Miyazaki 2022](#makito-miyazaki-2022)

## Evidence

### Makito Miyazaki 2022

Makito Miyazaki, Yosuke Yamazaki, and Yamato Hasegawa. (2022). Analysis of Brownian Motion by Elementary School Students. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/5.0059810](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0059810)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-workshop five-grade questionnaire (n' = 26). FIG. 10 reports mean difficulty 3.27 (SD 1.09) and mean interest 4.69 (SD 0.64). The authors state "all the respondents replied that the workshop was very interesting" or interesting; no effect size is printed.

> "more than 50% of the respondents (14/26) replied that the workshop was very diﬃcult (score 5; 2/26) or diﬃcult (score 4; 12/26) to understand, but all the respondents replied that the workshop was very interesting (score 5; 18/26) or interesting (score 4; 8/26)"
