# School-based interventions produce a small but statistically significant reduction in school exclusion

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 67 primary evaluations, school-based interventions were associated with a small but statistically significant reduction in school exclusion (SMD = 0.104). [→ meta-main](#meta-main)

## Evidence

### meta-main

Valdebenito, S., H. Gaffney, M. J. Arosemena‐Burbano, S. Hitchcock, D. Jolliffe, and A. Sutherland. 2025. “School‐Based Interventions for Reducing Disciplinary School Exclusion. An Updated Systematic Review.” Campbell Systematic Reviews 21: 1–96. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.70063

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multilevel random-effects meta-analysis with robust variance estimation of 32 studies comprising 2765 effect sizes from 67 primary evaluations (1980–2022), representing approximately 394,242 students; pooled SMD = 0.104, 95% CI 0.04 to 0.17, p < 0.001, a "small but statistically significant reduction in school exclusion".

> "Findings indicated that school‐based interventions were associated with a small but statistically significant reduction in school exclusion (standardised mean difference [SMD] = 0.104; 95% CI: 0.04 to 0.17; p < 0.001)."
