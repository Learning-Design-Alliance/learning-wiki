## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2019

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i?`

Random-effects subanalysis of six studies of medical students (n=738). The pooled "SMD=0.41 (95% CI −0.03 to 0.84)" marginally crossed zero, described by the authors as a tendency rather than a significant difference.

> "there was a tendency to favour CAI over face-to-face teaching among both medical students (SMD=0.41 (95% CI −0.03 to 0.84); six studies, n=738; I2=87%)"
