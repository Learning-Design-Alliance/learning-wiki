## Evidence

### Tindle 2022

Tindle, R., Castillo, P., Doring, N., Grant, L., & Willis, R. (2022). Developing and validating a university needs instrument to measure the psychosocial needs of university students. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1550–1570. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12515

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Secondary bivariate Pearson correlation analysis of survey timing (academic week) against UNI factors in the 511-student sample. "practical needs were significantly and negatively associated with the academic week (r = −.17, p < .001)"; the other factors tested showed no significant correlation.

> "Bivariate Pearson correlations showed no correlation between academic week and students psychosocial needs for Academic needs (r = −.06, p = .237), financial needs (r = −.05, p = .324), family needs (r = −.03, p = .288), nor emotional needs (r = −.05, p = .326). However, practical needs were significantly and negatively associated with the academic week (r = −.17, p < .001)."
