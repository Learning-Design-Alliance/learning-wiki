Karl Henrik Fredly, Tor Ole B. Odden, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2026). Physics computational literacy: Programming, modeling, and collaboration at the journeyman level. Physical Review Physics Education Research 22, 010103. https://doi.org/10.1103/6fny-yzyb

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Results I subsection on self-directed learning and peer interaction, from the interview thematic analysis. Students named Stack Overflow, YouTube tutorials, GitHub example code, and search engines; one student said "Courses really fail at teaching you how to write good code". AI tools like ChatGPT and peer critique were also cited as helpful.
