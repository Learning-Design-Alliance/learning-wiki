## Evidence

### Michael F. J. Fox 2020

Michael F. J. Fox, Benjamin M. Zwickl, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preparing for the quantum revolution - what is the role of higher education?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020131)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The interview study found that while most institutions considering NQI-related adaptation are weighing a quantum information science master's degree, "only 14% of the companies we interviewed mentioned the introduction of such degrees". This is a sample-based discrepancy report, not a tested comparison.

> "only 14% of the companies we interviewed mentioned the introduction of such degrees as a way for higher education to increase the workforce pipeline"

### Michael F. J. Fox 2020 (2)

Michael F. J. Fox, Benjamin M. Zwickl, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preparing for the quantum revolution - what is the role of higher education?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020131)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

For retraining existing employees, "Companies report a desire for on-demand short courses of no more than a couple of weeks... (52%), tailored to speciﬁc technologies", with hands-on experience with new laboratory technology the most valuable content (33%).

> "Companies report a desire for on-demand short courses of no more than a couple of weeks, or a few hours per week over a longer period of time (52%), tailored to speciﬁc technologies"
