## Evidence

### Cai Z 2025

Cai Z, Liu Y. (2025). A pilot study of digital technology combined with case-based learning in clinical implantology training for specialists. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08176-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre/post comparison in the pilot study of six attending doctors trained with digital navigation plus CBL, scored on a ten-question standardized test. The article reports the significant within-group gain with t = 5.84, P = 0.002; no effect size is printed.

> "The average test score before the course in the experimental group was 6.33 ± 1.03, and the average post-course score was 9.50 ± 0.84. The difference was statistically significant (t = 5.84, P = 0.002), indicating a measurable improvement in theoretical knowledge"
