## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2002

Chandralekha Singh. (2002). Effectiveness of group interaction on conceptual standardized test performance. [doi:10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of average group scores (Table 5a) and second individual test scores (Table 5b) by pair type in the IGI treatment. The article interprets the C-student gap as evidence that pairing with a higher achiever helped group performance but not retention.

> "A comparison of Tables 5a and 5b shows that the average individual score of C stu- dents in the (A C) pairing after two weeks (61%) is lower compared to their group score (72%). Also, a comparison of Tables 4a and 5a shows that A students did not beneﬁt from interactions with C students and the average initial individual score for A students and the group score for the (A C) pairing are the same."
