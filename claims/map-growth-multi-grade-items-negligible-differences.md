# For items aligned to multiple target grades, difficulty estimates from different on-grade calibration samples differ negligibly, at most 0.1 logit on average

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the 43 reading and 67 science items aligned to multiple target grades, lower- and upper-grade on-grade difficulty estimates differ negligibly from each other and from original estimates. [→ Wei He 2021](#wei-he-2021)

## Evidence

### Wei He 2021

Wei He, NWEA Psychometric Solutions. (2021). An Investigation of Item Parameter Invariance Using Focused Calibration Samples for MAP Growth. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/an-investigation-of-item-parameter-invariance-using-focused-calibration-samples-for-map-growth/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 recalibrated 43 reading items (Grades 9–10) and 67 science items (multiple grade spans) with each on-grade sample (Tables 3.4–3.5). The report says "the differences are considered negligible, with 0.1 logit at most," and correlations between the different estimates were high (0.96–1.00).

> "while the average lower and upper on-grade item difficulty parameters are slightly different from their original item difficulty parameters for both subjects, the differences are considered negligible, with 0.1 logit at most."
