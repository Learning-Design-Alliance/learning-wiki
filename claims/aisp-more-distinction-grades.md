# Students who learned with an AISP received significantly more distinction grades than controls (6 vs 1)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There were significantly more distinction grades in the intervention group than in the control group (6 vs 1, p=0.037), with fewer borderline grades in the AISP group. [→ Baraza W. 2026](#baraza-w-2026)

## Evidence

### Baraza W. 2026

Baraza W., Staples G., Wells C., Barbier L., Tonga I., Singh P., Puttick M., Webster C. (2026). Artificially Intelligent Simulated Patients in Undergraduate Surgical Teaching: A Single Blinded Randomized Controlled Study. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70486

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade distribution (distinction, pass, borderline, fail) from the same randomized controlled study's vCSA, compared by chi-square test. The article reports "significantly more distinctions in the intervention group than in the control group (6 vs. 1, respectively, p = 0.037)"; no effect size was printed.

> "In addition, there were significantly more distinctions in the intervention group than in the control group (6 vs. 1, respectively, p = 0.037, chi-square test). The pass and borderline grades were roughly similar across both groups although there were fewer borderline grades in those who had used the AISP for learning."
