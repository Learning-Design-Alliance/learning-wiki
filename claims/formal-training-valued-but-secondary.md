## Evidence

### Quon S 2022

Quon S, Riddell J, Bench KF, Roepke C, Burner E. (2022). Training Leaders in Trauma Resuscitation: Teacher and Learner Perspectives on Ideal Methods. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2021.5.51428

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview study, participants valued simulation for practicing in a lower-stress setting and wanted didactics combined with simulation or hands-on training; "Didactic training alone was not considered effective because interviewees also wanted more active, hands-on learning."

> "Overall, formal training was perceived as less effective than informal techniques but was still valued."
