### Naumann 2023

Naumann, S., Bayer, M., Kirst, S., van der Meer, E. & Dziobek, I. (2023). A randomized controlled trial on the digital socio-emotional competence training Zirkus Empathico for preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00169-8

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ITT ANCOVA on SDQ parent-rated prosocial behavior change scores in the pre-registered RCT (N = 74). Parent ratings "showed larger increases in prosocial behavior" (d = 0.51, p = 0.008); the EMK child assessment for prosocial behavior did not display group differences (d = 0.02, p = 0.57).
