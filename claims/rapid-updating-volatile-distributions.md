## Evidence

### Luthra 2025

Luthra, S., Luor, A., Tierney, A. T., Dick, F., & Holt, L. L. (2025). Statistical learning dynamically shapes auditory perception. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exp 3c (detection) showed a significant Freq × Block interaction (p = 2.495e-5, η² = 0.099) with flips across the first three blocks, but no significant frequency difference in the final block. In Exp 4c (duration decisions), RTs were slowest for the less probable frequency in all blocks (Freq × Block: p = 5.253e-7, η² = 0.040).
