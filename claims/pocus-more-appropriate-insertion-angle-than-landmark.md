`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Angle-of-insertion assessment in a crossover randomized cadaver study with 30 veterinary students. The results section reports that "More POCUS‐guided placements (26/30 [86.7%]) were made at the appropriate angle of insertion than landmark‐guided (16/30 [53.3%])."

> "More POCUS‐guided placements (26/30 [86.7%]) were made at the appropriate angle of insertion than landmark‐guided (16/30 [53.3%]). There were more landmark‐guided attempts inserted outside of 30–60 degrees, with most of the incorrectly angled LPN attempts made at 90 degrees."
