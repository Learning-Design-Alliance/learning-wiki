# The KIPP lottery-based randomized controlled trial yielded treatment and control groups similar on observable and unobservable characteristics

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Random admission lotteries ensured that students offered KIPP middle school admission were similar on average to students not offered admission, on both observable characteristics (such as prior test scores) and unobservable ones (such as motivation and parental support). Design statement about the study's randomized controlled trial, in which admission offers were determined by 5th- or 6th-grade admissions lotteries.
