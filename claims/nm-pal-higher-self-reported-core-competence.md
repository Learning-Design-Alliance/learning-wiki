## Evidence

### Lin Peixin 2026

Lin Peixin, Yin Xiaolin, Zheng Xuankun, He Sijia, Cai Yuexin, Deng Xiaozheng, Chen Tongtong, Liang Weiquan, Liang Suqiong, Liang Yuanke, Lu Yiwen, Lin Haoyu. (2026). Exploring core competence in first-year medical students through narrative medicine and peer-assisted learning: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10160-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 128 first-year students (NM-PAL n=64 vs lecture-based n=64) over 10 months at a single institution, using a 19-item Delphi-developed KSA scale. The experimental group scored higher on total self-reported core competence, with a large printed effect size ("Cohen's d = 1.28").

> "the experimental group reported a significantly higher mean score of 77.42 ± 7.45 compared to the control group's 68.00 ± 7.31 (mean difference = 9.42, 95% CI: 6.84–12.00, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.28)"
