# Students who read interest-aligned passages retain motivation better on subsequent non-personalized tests than students given randomized passages

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the IMMS 7-point scale, Group B's motivation dropped less from Test 1 to Test 2 (4.68±0.66 to 4.61±1.04) than Group A's (4.82±0.38 to 4.44±0.85), per Mann-Whitney U test. [→ Jieun Han 2025](#jieun-han-2025)

## Evidence

### Jieun Han 2025

Jieun Han, Daniel Lee, Haneul Yoo, Jinsung Yoon, Junyeong Park, Suin Kim, So-Yeon Ahn, Alice Oh. (2025). One-Topic-Doesn't-Fit-All: Transcreating Reading Comprehension Test for Personalized Learning. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.09135](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.09135)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

IMMS administered after Test 1 and Test 2 in the 20-student two-group EFL experiment. "Group B shows better motivation retention" than Group A, whose motivation fell more when interest-aligned content was removed in Test 2. Only means and SDs are printed, no effect size.

> "However, the Mann-Whitney U test confirms that Group B shows better motivation retention ( 4.68±0.66 →4.61 ±1.04) than Group A ( 4.82±0.38 →4.44 ±0.85) on a 7 Likert scale."
