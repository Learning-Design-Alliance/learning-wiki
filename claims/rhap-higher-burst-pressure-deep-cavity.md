## Evidence

### Sykes EA 2021

Sykes EA, Lemke M, Potter D, Li T, Mir ZM, Sheahan G, Wu V, Zevin B. (2021). Evaluating bowel enterotomy closures in simulated deep body cavities using the reversing half-hitch alternating post and square knots: a randomized controlled trial. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.016719

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Burst pressure (hydrostatic pressure causing suture-line leakage) was assessed blinded to allocation for 20 randomized novices. Square-knot repairs showed "significantly lower" burst pressure than RHAP (35 [SD 14] v. 105 [SD 39] mm Hg, p = 0.05); no effect size was printed.

> "In simulated deep body cavities, the burst pressure for enterotomy repairs using the square knot was significantly lower than that of the RHAP knot (35 [SD 14] v. 105 [SD 39] mm Hg, p = 0.05)"
