## Evidence

### Secchi Agostina 2025

Secchi Agostina, Whiskey Eromona, Nair Sajitha, Shergill Sukhi, Thomas Trudy. (2025). Competence and confidence of health care professionals in using clozapine: a qualitative systematic review and thematic synthesis. Acta Neuropsychiatrica. https://doi.org/10.1017/neu.2025.10024

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Structured-interview study reported within the review: prescribing rates were higher where psychiatrists were "more exposed to clozapine (p = 0.0048)". The review treats this as evidence that experience had a positive effect.

> "The structured interview used by Nielsen et al. (2010), revealed that prescribing rates were higher in areas where clozapine was used more, where psychiatrists were more exposed to clozapine (p = 0.0048), indicating that experience had a positive effect."

### Secchi Agostina 2025 (2)

Secchi Agostina, Whiskey Eromona, Nair Sajitha, Shergill Sukhi, Thomas Trudy. (2025). Competence and confidence of health care professionals in using clozapine: a qualitative systematic review and thematic synthesis. Acta Neuropsychiatrica. https://doi.org/10.1017/neu.2025.10024

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Comparative survey evidence reported within the review (Takeuchi et al., 2016): psychiatrists with equal years in mental health settings but differing clozapine exposure differed in views on efficacy and safety, showing general experience alone is insufficient.

> "This shows that on its own, mental health experience does not increase awareness and knowledge of clozapine management."
