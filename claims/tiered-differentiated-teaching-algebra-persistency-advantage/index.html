Evidence¶

Bal 2016¶

Bal, A. P. (2016). The effect of the differentiated teaching approach in the algebraic learning field on students’ academic achievements. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 185-204. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.11

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same quasi-experiment: both groups retook the 21-item Algebra Success Test four weeks after the last test. Corrected persistency means were 17.36 (experiment) versus 14.60 (control); the grouping effect was "F (1,54)=6.227 p<.05".

"Table 6 reveals a significant difference in the covariance analysis results in terms of the corrected average persistency points under the scope of the main effect of grouping (F (1,54)=6.227 p<.05)."
