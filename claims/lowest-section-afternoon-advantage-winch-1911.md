## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among boys scoring under 55 marks in the four preliminary tests, the afternoon group showed more improvement than the morning group, contradicting the earlier suggestion that stronger children fare better in the afternoon. [→ W. H. Winch 1911](#w-h-winch-1911)

## Evidence

### W. H. Winch 1911

W. H. Winch. (1911). Mental Fatigue in Day School Children as Measured by Immediate Memory. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Section-by-section comparison (Table II) of matched groups by preliminary-test level; in the under-55 section the afternoon workers improved more. The author notes these boys are among the weakest mnemonically; no effect size is printed.

> "but in the lowest sections, consisting of boys who obtain less than 55 marks in the four preliminary tests, the group of afternoon workers show more improvement than those who work early in the mornng. This is contrary to the suggestion, previously made, that, perhaps, it is the more strongly endowed who do better work in the afternoon"
