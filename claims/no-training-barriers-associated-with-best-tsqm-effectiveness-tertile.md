## Evidence

### Mallick R. 2020

Mallick R., Henderson T., Lahue B. J., Kafal A., Bassett P., Scalchunes C. (2020). Subcutaneous immunoglobulin in primary immunodeficiency – impact of training and infusion characteristics on patient-reported outcomes. BMC Immunology. [doi:10.1186/s12865-020-00371-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12865-020-00371-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on the 366-respondent IDF survey cohort tested training characteristics against TSQM effectiveness tertile membership. "absence of training barriers was associated with higher odds of being in the best tertile" (p = 0.01).

> "For effectiveness: absence of training barriers was associated with higher odds of being in the best tertile (p = 0.01). In the corresponding continuous scale (linear) model, requiring fewer training sessions was significantly associated with a higher effectiveness score (p = 0.01)."

### Mallick R. 2020 (2)
