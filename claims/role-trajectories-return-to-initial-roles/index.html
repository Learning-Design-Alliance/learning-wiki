Evidence¶

Ramy Elmoazen 2025¶

Ramy Elmoazen, Mohammed Saqr, Laura Hirsto, Matti Tedre. (2025). Capturing temporal pathways of collaborative roles: A multilayered analytical approach using community of inquiry. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09431-6

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Sequence and process-mining analysis of 13 weekly role sequences built with TramineR and longest-common-subsequence clustering over the four courses. The finding that students "returned to their initial roles" is the authors' summary of the index plots and Markov transition models.

"Students in the active-constructive trajectory took on leadership roles, while the students in the social interactive trajectory were mostly social mediators, and the free rider trajectory showed a predominant peripheral explorer role. The students in all trajectories returned to their initial roles"
