Pilato Jazelle, Cortes Robert, Peterson Emily Grossnickle, Uttal David, Kolvoord Robert, Green Adam. (2025). Neurocognitive correlates of spatial thinking mediate the relation between socioeconomic status and STEM achievement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-29345-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis on the income-reporting subsample (N=104) using the paper folding task. The article reports "adjusted R2 = 0.06" and "Β = 0.99, t(104) = 2.46, p = .01" for family income.

> "The model including family income, gender, and age also explained a significant proportion of variance in spatial ability, adjusted R2 = 0.06, F(3, 93) = 5.92, p = .03. As family income increased, spatial ability scores also increased, Β = 0.99, t(104) = 2.46, p = .01"
