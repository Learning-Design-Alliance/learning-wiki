# Within the experimental group, attitudes rose significantly from pretest to posttest and monitoring test, and the gain held between posttest and monitoring test

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` One-way ANOVA for repeated measures showed a significant difference among the experimental group's pretest, posttest and monitoring test scores, with Bonferroni tests locating the difference between pretest and the later tests. [→ Yildirim 2018](#yildirim-2018)
`q2 i?` The 0.04-point difference between posttest and monitoring test averages was not significant, indicating the gain was maintained. [→ Yildirim 2018](#yildirim-2018)

## Evidence

### Yildirim 2018

Halil Ibrahim Yildirim. (2018). The Impact of Out-of-School Learning Environments on 6th Grade Secondary School Students Attitude Towards Science Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(12). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i12.3624

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the 30-student experimental group's three attitude measurements; F(2-58)=243.85, p<.05, with Bonferroni tests favoring posttest and monitoring test over pretest; the article reports "The calculated effect size is η2=0.97".

> "a significant difference emerged between the pre, post and monitoring test scores on attitude towards the science course of the students in the experimental group (F(2-58)= 243.85; p<.05). According to t he Bonferroni analysis results in Table 9, this significant difference emerged between the pretest and posttest, in favor of the posttest, and between the monitoring test and pretest, in favor of the monitoring test."
