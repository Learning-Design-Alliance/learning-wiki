Mario Carretero & Floor van Alphen. (2014). Do Master Narratives Change Among High School Students? A Characterization of How National History Is Represented. Cognition and Instruction, 32(3), 290-312. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2014.919298

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Comparative analysis of semistructured interview narratives from 18 8th graders and 20 11th graders at an urban middle-class public high school in Buenos Aires. The article reports the homogeneous subject occurred 'significantly more in the accounts constructed by 8th graders (Fisher's p < .05).'

> "A heterogeneous historical subject was encountered in 50% of the accounts of 11th graders, compared to 11% in the 8th grade."
