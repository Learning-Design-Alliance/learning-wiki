### Bouchefra Said 2023

Bouchefra Said, El Ghouddany Safouane, Ouali Karim, Bour Abdellatif. (2023). Is good dietary diversity a predictor of academic success? Acta Bio Medica : Atenei Parmensis. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10210566/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the cross-sectional study; absenteeism coefficients were −3.508 (GPAf) and −4.214 (GPAm), p < 0.01. The article reports students missing more than 10 sessions per year perform worse than regular attenders.

> "Absenteeism is significant and negatively associated with academic performance (P-value < 0.01). Consequently, students who are missing more than 10 school sessions per year are 3 times less likely to perform well in French and 4 times less likely to perform well in math than students who attend school regularly"
