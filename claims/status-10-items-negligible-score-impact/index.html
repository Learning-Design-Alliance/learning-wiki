Evidence¶

Wang 2020¶

Wang, S., & Li, S. (2020). Calibration of Spanish MAP Growth math tests. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/calibration-of-spanish-map-growth-math-tests/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison of person RIT means and SDs by grade (Tables 3.4 and 3.5) and ethnicity (Tables 3.6 and 3.7) for the selected monolingual and bilingual samples, scoring with versus without status 10 items. Mean differences are about one RIT decimal point and correlations between Spanish RIT and Spanish NRIT are 1.00; no effect size is printed.

"The difference between the monolingual results based on items with and without item calibration status 10 is very small for both grade and ethnicity, which indicates that the impact of treating the newly calibrated status 10 items as status XX items is very small and can be neglectable."
