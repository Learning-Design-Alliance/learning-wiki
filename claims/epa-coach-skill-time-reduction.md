## Evidence

### Perotti Luis 2025

Perotti Luis, Stamm Oskar, Ferizaj Drin, Dietrich Michael, Buchem Ilona, Müller-Werdan Ursula. (2025). Evaluation of an e-learning platform promoting electronic personal health record competence: a pilot trial in older adults. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22242-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Skills test with three interactive ePHR mockup tasks (searching a document, adding a document, assigning access rights) administered at both visits; Wilcoxon pre-post comparison. The article reports the medians and that "A large effect size was also present here (OA: r = 0.82, YOA: r = 0.67)".

> "The median processing time for solving the three practical tasks was reduced from 746.50 s (IQR = 256.00, M = 797.00, SD = 248.70) to 539.00 s (IQR = 186.00, M = 538.36, SD = 139.44) for the OA and from 487.00 s (IQR = 110.00, M = 544.79, SD = 189.66) to 351.00 s (IQR = 175.00, M = 368.29, SD = 115.72) for the YOA (Table 4). A large effect size was also present here (OA: r = 0.82, YOA: r = 0.67)."
