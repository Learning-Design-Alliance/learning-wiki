In the same ChatGPT-4 case study, a follow-up question comparing descent and ascent slopes triggered a repeat of the earlier error. The authors note "it was not enough for the chatbot to admit and correct this mistake once, as it re-emerged after further probing questions."

> "Here, we can once again see the same mistake of claiming that the slope of the graph is positive after the bounce. Thus, it was not enough for the chatbot to admit and correct this mistake once, as it re-emerged after further probing questions."
