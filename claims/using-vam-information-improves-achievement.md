### Koedel 2015

Koedel, C., Mihaly, K., & Rockoff, J. E. (2015). Value-Added Modeling: A Review. Preprint, forthcoming at Economics of Education Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Value-Added+Modeling+A+Review+Koedel

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of policy-evaluation studies (Chetty, Friedman and Rockoff 2014b; Boyd et al. 2011; Goldhaber and Theobald 2013; Winters and Cowen 2013; Rothstein 2015) assessing the merits of acting on value-added estimates despite imprecision. The review reports the studies "consistently show" achievement improvements from using the information.

> "These studies consistently show that using information about teacher value-added improves student achievement relative to the alternative of not using value-added information."
