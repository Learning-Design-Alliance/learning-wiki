## Evidence

### Diekhoff Torsten 2020

Diekhoff Torsten, Kainberger Franz, Oleaga Laura, Dewey Marc, Zimmermann Elke. (2020). Effectiveness of the clinical decision support tool ESR eGUIDE for teaching medical students the appropriate selection of imaging tests: randomized cross-over evaluation. European Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-020-06942-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group comparison for group B (13 participants), who used the tool first. The article reports no decline "after having answered set 1 first with ESR eGUIDE" (p = 0.66). No effect size printed; authors note this comparison may be underpowered.

> "The number of correct answers in group B did not decline when set 2 was answered without ESR eGUIDE (12.4 ± 2.6) after having answered set 1 first with ESR eGUIDE (13.0 ± 2.7, p = 0.66, Fig. 4b)."
