## Evidence

### Wakeford Richard 2015

Wakeford Richard, Denney MeiLing, Ludka-Stempien Katarzyna, Dacre Jane, McManus I C. (2015). Cross-comparison of MRCGP & MRCP(UK) in a database linkage study of 2,284 candidates taking both examinations: assessment of validity and differential performance by ethnicity. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0281-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of the linked examination database, in which 854 (37.4%) candidates were white and 1,430 (62.6%) BME. The article reports the performance differences as statistically significant at p < .001 for every component; no effect sizes are printed for this overall contrast.

> "BME candidates in the present sample performing less well at MRCP(UK) Part 1, Part 2 and PACES, and at AKT, its three subtests, and CSA (detailed results will not be presented but all are p < .001)."
