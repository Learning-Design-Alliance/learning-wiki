### Amin Mahsa 2026

Amin Mahsa, Abedi Farshad, Norouzi Azam, Karimi Gholamreza. (2026). Enhancing rational prescribing skills in pharmacy students: a blended learning model of flipped classroom and think-pair-share for cardiovascular pharmacology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09166-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' stated limitation of the single-group, post-intervention pilot design (n = 62). They frame the study's contribution as "establishing learner buy-in and model feasibility, not as definitive proof of efficacy."

> "The absence of a control group means that while we have established strong feasibility and positive learner perceptions, we cannot definitively attribute these perceptions solely to our intervention, as history or maturation effects could be contributory factors"
