## Evidence

### Mileder L 2018

Mileder L. (2018). 'Titrating' simulation training intensity. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.12949

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The commentary reports a meta-analysis of acute stress effects on core executive functions, citing Shields et al., finding that "acute stress impaired working memory, cognitive flexibility and cognitive inhibition, whereas stress enhanced response inhibition". No effect sizes are printed.

> "A meta‐analysis of acute stress effects on core executive functions found that acute stress impaired working memory, cognitive flexibility and cognitive inhibition, whereas stress enhanced response inhibition."

### Mileder L 2018 (2)

Mileder L. (2018). 'Titrating' simulation training intensity. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.12949

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Among the meta-analysis sub-findings reported by the commentary was that "a high working memory load moderated the effects of stress on working memory". No effect sizes are printed.

> "a high working memory load moderated the effects of stress on working memory, and that cognitive flexibility was impaired with an increased severity of stress."
