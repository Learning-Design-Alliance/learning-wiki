`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Discussion (RQ3) of the review compares its own positive-outcome rate with seven prior SLRs, concluding "our results are within the same range" and that isolating specific apps does not make a significant change.

> "The comparison of our results based on m-learning experiences developed with speciﬁc developed apps is quite similar to SLRs on m-learning experiences presented in related work. Namely, [3] reports 86% of positive results, [19] a 75%, [2] points out that the 87% of the studies present, at least, one learning outcome as statistically signiﬁcant, and [25] report a 70% of positive results in PK-12 education, [26] report a 70% of positive results in higher education, [15] a 86%, and [4] a 75.24%."
