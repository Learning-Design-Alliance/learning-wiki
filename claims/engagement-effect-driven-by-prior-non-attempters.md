Secondary analysis of the randomized experiment's practice-problem engagement data, showing continuation rates of 96% (control) and 99% (experimental) and never-attempter proportions of 19% versus 9%; no effect size printed.

> "The observed difference was largely driven by students who had not previously engaged with practice problems. Most students who attempted practice problems in the pre-intervention phase continued to do so during the intervention phase (control = 96%, experimental = 99%)."
