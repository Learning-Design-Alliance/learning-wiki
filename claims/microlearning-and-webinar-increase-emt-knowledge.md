### Baranipour Jafar 2026

Baranipour Jafar, Hosseini Seyed Mohammad Reza, Sharifzadeh Gholam Reza, Poodineh Moghadam Mahdieh. (2026). Comparison of the effects of microlearning and webinars on knowledge and self-efficacy of emergency medical technicians (EMTs) in the diagnosis and treatment of cardiac arrhythmias. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-025-01428-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the three-group quasi-experiment of 75 EMTs. The article reports "a significant increase in the average knowledge score" across pre-test, post-test, and one-month follow-up in both intervention groups; no effect size is printed.

> "The results of the repeated measures analysis of variance indicated that in the microlearning (F = 160.9, P < 0.001) and webinar (F = 36.8, P < 0.001) groups, a significant increase in the average knowledge score was observed during the three study periods."
