The review identifies five means of assessing RI teaching effectiveness and reports their frequency: "Unvalidated surveys, validated surveys / questionnaire-based, interviews, portfolio / assignment-based, and observational analysis were mentioned as part of an assessment approach for the effectiveness of RI teaching in 31, 12, 5, 7 and 3 sources respectively".
