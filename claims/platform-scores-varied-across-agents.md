### Zhang Hui MM 2026

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Nonparametric omnibus test across 8 agents using 167 dialogues. η2=0.321 indicates a large between-agent effect. "Basic science agents (A2, A3) received the highest scores, while clinical disease agents (A4, A5, A7) received the lowest."

> "Chaoxing platform–generated scores from 167 student-agent dialogues differed significantly across the 8 agents (Kruskal-Wallis H7=57.981; P<.001; η2=0.321; Figure 1), with 9 significant pairwise differences identified by post hoc Dunn tests (Bonferroni-corrected P<.05)."
