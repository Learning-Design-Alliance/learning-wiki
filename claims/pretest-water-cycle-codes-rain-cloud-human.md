## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In pretest drawings, rain appeared in 95.4% of drawings, cloud in 81.8%, and human in 54.5%, while evaporation (6.8%) and condensation (2.1%) were rare. [→ Berat Ahi 2017](#berat-ahi-2017)

## Evidence

### Berat Ahi 2017

Berat Ahi. (2017). The Effect of Talking Drawings on Five-Year-Old Turkish Children’s Mental Models of the Water Cycle. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 12(3), 349-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1141375

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Frequency analysis of 11 codes in the pretest drawings of all 44 children. The article reports very few children indicated evaporation (f = 3, 6.8%) or condensation (f = 1, 0.02%), and the two groups used codes at similar frequencies.

> "The most striking among these codes is rain (f = 42, 95.4%). Almost all of the students in the experimental and control groups (95.4%) included rain in their drawings."
