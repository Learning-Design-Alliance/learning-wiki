`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The trial's T1/T2 self-evaluation showed the simulator-trained group reporting a higher subjective competency gain for pathology recognition than the human-trained group, while for "almost all subcategories" the usual pattern applied; the Δ values are self-reported score changes, not standardised effect sizes.

> "This trend applied to almost all subcategories except for “pathology recognition”. Here, the study group reported a significantly higher increase in competency than the control group (study: Δ 3.7 ± 1.2 vs. control: Δ 2.8 ± 1.8; p < 0.01)."
