Review synthesis of differential effects across the eleven included studies, reporting the age contrast with printed effect sizes d = −0.37 SD for elementary versus d = −0.10 SD for secondary students (Tomasik et al., 2020). The review attributes this to younger children's reliance on cognitive scaffolding and less developed self-regulated learning.
