## Evidence

### Baxmann Martin 2024

Baxmann Martin, Baráth Zoltán, Kárpáti Krisztina. (2024). Efficacy of typodont and simulation training in orthodontic education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06425-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this to Lin-na et al. (2023), a quasi-experimental study of 40 students using scenario simulation combined with case teaching for doctor-patient communication skills. The review reports "significantly higher scores on communication assessment scales" versus lecture-based training.

> "Studies showed a marked improvement in communication skills among students who participated in these simulations, with significantly higher scores on communication assessment scales compared to those who received traditional lecture-based training"
