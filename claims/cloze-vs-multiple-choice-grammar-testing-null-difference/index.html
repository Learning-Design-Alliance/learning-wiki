Evidence¶

Minoo Alemi & Apama Miraghaee 2011¶

Minoo Alemi & Apama Miraghaee. (2011). A Comparative Study of Testing Grammar Knowledge of Iranian Students between Cloze and Multiple-Choice Tests. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1, No. 3, July - September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071285

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent-groups study: 75 Iranian pre-university EFL students in two intact groups received ten weekly 25-item grammar tests (cloze vs multiple-choice), then a 60-item post-test. The t-test found "no significant difference between the mean scores" of the two groups; equivalence was not tested.

"Since the t-observed does not equate or exceed the t-critical value for rejection of the null hypothesis at both 0.01 and 0.05 levels of significance for 73 degrees of freedom, the hypothesis is not rejected."
