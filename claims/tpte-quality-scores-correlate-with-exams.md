## Evidence

### Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace 2005

Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace. (2005). Probing physics students' conceptual knowledge structures through term association. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Scoring study in which five physics experts rated 80 terms on a 0-2 scale; 16 subjects' mean "force" list scores (range 8.0 to 14.5, mean 11.8, SD 2.0) were plotted against summed raw exam scores, yielding "r = 0.608", a large correlation by the r code table.

> "The Pearson's r-value coefficient of correlation was 0.608, where 0.13 is the threshold for statistical significance with 16 data points."
