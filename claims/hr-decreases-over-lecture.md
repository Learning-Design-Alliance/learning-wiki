## Evidence

### Darnell 2019

Darnell, D. K., & Krieg, P. A. (2019). Student engagement, assessed using heart rate, shows no reset following active learning sessions in lectures. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225709. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225709

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 42 medical school lecture classes (310 student traces at one-second intervals) using wrist-worn HR monitors; regression over combined classes shows the decline. The article reports "a decrease of 2.9bpm (P<0.001)" with no effect size.

> "Calculation of the regression for the combined classes shows a statistically significant decrease in HR from an average of 71.8 at the start, to 68.9 by the end the lecture, a decrease of 2.9bpm (P<0.001)."
