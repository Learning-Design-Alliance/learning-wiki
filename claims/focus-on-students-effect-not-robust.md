# The focus-on-students effect was not robust: significant in the ANOVA but not in non-parametric tests

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the pre-post study, the ANOVA for focus on students was significant, F(2,406) = 5.60, p = .004, η² = .025, but the non-parametric Kruskal-Wallis test conducted due to unequal variances showed no significant differences, χ²(5) = 7.66, p = .176.

> "However, Kruskal-Wallis tests, which were conducted because of violations of homogeneity, did not show significant differences between the groups for the variable focus on students, χ² (5) = 7.66, p =.176"
