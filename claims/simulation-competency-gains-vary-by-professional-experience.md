## Evidence

### Monod Cécile 2013

Monod Cécile, Voekt Cora A., Gisin Martina, Gisin Stefan, Hoesli Irene M. (2013). Optimization of competency in obstetrical emergencies: a role for simulation training. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-013-3111-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of Likert responses plotted against professional experience (Figs. 2–5); 156 participants reported experience levels (40 with 0–2 years, 30 with 2–5, 34 with 5–10, 52 with >10 years). The article reports "only improvement in the group with >10 years professional experience reached statistical significance" for algorithms.

> "For the third question on clinical algorithms, although the overall analysis showed statistically significant improvement in self-perceived competency, only improvement in the group with >10 years professional experience reached statistical significance (Fig. 4). For the question regarding team communication, there was a trend towards improvement after 3 months in the overall analysis; in the group of participants with 5–10 years of professional experience, the improvement was statistically significant (Fig. 5)"
