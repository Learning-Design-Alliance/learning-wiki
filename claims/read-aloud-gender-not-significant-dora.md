### Marchessault and Larwin 2013

Jennifer Kohart Marchessault, Karen H. Larwin. (2013). Structured Read-Aloud In Middle School: The Potential Impact On Reading Achievement. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, Volume 6, Number 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1073207

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Gender was included as a factor in the multivariate analysis of the quasi-experimental DORA gain data (n = 70 control, n = 90 treatment). The article reports gender "was found not to be significant"; no effect size was printed, and equivalence was not tested.

> "Gender was assessed in the multivariate analysis and was found not to be significant."
