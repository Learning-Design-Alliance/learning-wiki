## Evidence

### Gerdes 2016

Gerdes, A., Heeren, B., Jeuring, J., & van Binsbergen, L. T. (2016). Ask-Elle: an Adaptable Programming Tutor for Haskell Giving Automated Feedback. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0080-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Assessment experiment on 94 student solutions to the fromBin exercise collected from a first-year functional programming course at Utrecht University (2008), previously graded by hand. "From these 72 programs, our assessment tool recognises 62 programs (86%)" using four model solutions. The article also reports no false positives: all incorrect and imperfect programs were rejected.

> "From these 72 programs, our assessment tool recognises 62 programs (86%). Another, and perhaps better, way of looking at these numbers is that 62 student solutions are accepted based on just four model solutions."
