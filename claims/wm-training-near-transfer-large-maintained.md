## Subclaims
`q4 i3` Effects on tasks similar or identical to those trained are large (treated controls g = 0.80 at posttest), maintained at follow-up, and significantly smaller in treated- than untreated-control studies. [→ Melby-Lervåg 2016](#melby-lervag-2016)

## Evidence

### Melby-Lervåg 2016

Melby-Lervåg, M., Redick, T. S., & Hulme, C. (2016). Working Memory Training Does Not Improve Performance on Measures of Intelligence or Other Measures of “Far Transfer”: Evidence From a Meta-Analytic Review. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616635612

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of near-transfer (criterion) measures. Table 1 reports treated-control g = 0.80 [0.62, 0.97] across 22 comparisons and untreated-control g = 1.88; treated-control effects were significantly smaller, Q(1) = 13.70, p = .0001, and effects persisted at follow-up (g = 0.99).

> "There were large effects on tasks that are similar or identical to those that are trained (see Fig. 2)."
