## Evidence

### Yan Li 2026

Yan Li, Niemi Ripsa, Gutvilig Mai, Elovainio Marko, Flykt Marjo, Hakulinen Christian. (2026). Mental disorders in childhood or adolescence and subsequent comprehensive school achievement. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 67(11), 1796-1806. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70181

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comorbidity analysis categorizing exposure by number of diagnostic categories (0, 1, 2, ≥3) in the nationwide cohort. The printed standardized differences were −0.38, −0.50 and −0.62; students "showed progressively lower GPA" with more categories, similar in both genders.

> "students with diagnoses in multiple diagnostic categories showed progressively lower GPA compared with those without any diagnosis (−0.38 [95% CI, −0.39 to −0.37] for one diagnostic category, −0.50 [95% CI, −0.51 to −0.49] for two, and −0.62 [95% CI, −0.64 to −0.60] for three or more)"
