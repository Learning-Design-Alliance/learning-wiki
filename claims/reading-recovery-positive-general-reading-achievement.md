Reading Recovery®. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. Updated July 2013. http://www.readingrecovery.org

### Reading Recovery® 2013 (2)

Reading Recovery®. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. Updated July 2013. http://www.readingrecovery.org

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The effectiveness summary states that "three studies with strong designs reported statistically significant positive effects" in the general reading achievement domain, on measures including the Observation Survey subtests, Gates-MacGinitie, and Woodcock Reading Mastery Test–Revised. No effect size is printed for this domain-level finding.

> "Thus, for the general reading achievement domain, three studies with strong designs reported statistically significant positive effects. This results in a rating of positive effects, with a small extent of evidence."
