## Evidence

### Westerlaken M 2019

Westerlaken M, Christiaans-Dingelhoff I, Filius RM, de Vries B, de Bruijne M, van Dam M. (2019). Blended learning for postgraduates; an interactive experience. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1717-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of two blended modules with voluntary anonymous questionnaires completed by 16 (online) and 15 (face-to-face) of 21 postgraduates. The article reports a 7.6 median rating and that participants regarded the modules "an effective preparation for face-to-face meetings".

> "The participants rated the online modules overall 7.6 median on a scale from 1 to 10, with range 7–9 for the first online module and range 6–9 for the second online module."
