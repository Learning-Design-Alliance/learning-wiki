Evidence¶

Starkey et al. 2013¶

Prentice Starkey, Alice Klein, & Lydia DeFlorio. (2013). Changing the Developmental Trajectory in Early Math Through a Two-Year Preschool Math Intervention. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563068

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cluster-randomized comparison in 63 Head Start classrooms; 21 classrooms per condition. Treatment 3-year-olds (I-2) outgained non-treatment 3-year-olds (I-1 and C) on the CMA ("ES=1.11") and TEMA-3 ("ES=.89"). Effect sizes printed but not standardized in a form coded here.

"It was found that greater gains in math knowledge were experienced by 3-year-old treatment children (I-2 condition) in the pre-pre-K year than by 3-year-olds who did not receive this intervention (I-1and C conditions). This was found both on the CMA (ES=1.11) and the TEMA-3 (ES=.89)"
