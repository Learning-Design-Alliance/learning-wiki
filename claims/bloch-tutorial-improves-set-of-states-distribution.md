# After engaging with the Bloch sphere tutorial, students showed exceptional improvement in identifying the set of all states consistent with a given probability distribution, reaching 86% on the post-test.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' performance on identifying the set of all states yielding a given probability distribution improved from weak pre-test performance to 86% on the post-test after the tutorial. [→ Peter Hu 2023](#peter-hu-2023)

## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, Roger S. K. Mong, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Student Understanding of the Bloch Sphere. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad2393

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post classroom study in an interdisciplinary undergraduate course (N = 28): question 8 on the pre-test showed no consistent underlying patterns, but the tutorial section using the QuVis Bloch sphere visualization produced what the authors call an "exceptional normalized gain and effect size" with a post-test performance of 86%.

> "However, it appears that the tutorial was helpful in helping them learn this concept, as evidenced by the exceptional normalized gain and effect size yielding a final performance of 86% on the post-test (see Table 1)."
