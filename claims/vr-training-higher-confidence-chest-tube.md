Self-reported confidence rated pre- and post-training via the learning management system in the pilot RCT (n = 30). The article reports the between-group difference at P = 0.002; no effect size is printed.

> "Using a 5-point Likert scale, the participants who trained with VR reported significantly higher confidence in performing chest tube insertion post-training (VR group: 4 [4-5], control group: 3 [3-4] out of 5; P = 0.002)."
