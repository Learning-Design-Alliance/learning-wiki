## Evidence

### Coduri Mara 2023

Coduri Mara, Calandrino Andrea, Addiego Mobilio Giulia, Casadio Maura, Ricci Serena. (2023). RiNeo MR: A mixed reality simulator for newborn life support training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294914

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study of the 3D simulation with 11 controls and 5 paediatric residents; SUS scores were converted to a 1-100 scale where scores greater than 68 indicate excellent or good ratings. No effect size printed.

> "In addition, SUS results indicate good to excellent usability scores for both groups, with only one subject rating the usability of RiNeo MR as poor (Fig 8B)"

### Coduri Mara 2023 (2)

Coduri Mara, Calandrino Andrea, Addiego Mobilio Giulia, Casadio Maura, Ricci Serena. (2023). RiNeo MR: A mixed reality simulator for newborn life support training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294914

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann Whitney comparison of 3D UEQ attractiveness between controls and paediatric residents; the article reports the trend as p = 0.067 with no effect size. The authors attribute the difference to residents understanding the tool's educational potential.

> "we have found a trend toward significance (p = 0.067, Fig 7B) when we compared controls and paediatric residents’ attractiveness scores, with resident rating the attractiveness higher than controls"
