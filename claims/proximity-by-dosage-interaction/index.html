Evidence¶

Anna H. Miller 2025¶

Anna H. Miller, Daniel R. Espinas, Daniel McNeish, & Marcia A. Barnes. (2025). Dosage Response in Intensive Mathematics Interventions for Early Elementary Students With or At-Risk for Mathematics Learning Disability. Educational Psychology Review, 37, 91. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10070-y

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis (Model 3) probing the significant proximity × dosage interaction (β = 0.05, 95% CI [0.01, 0.10]) across 164 effect sizes. Simple slopes were insignificant for distal (0.02, p = 0.73) and proximal (0.07, p = 0.25) outcomes; the crossover thresholds were 5.43 h and 22.6 h.

"The difference between proximal and distal outcomes was significant at dosages below 5.43 h such that distal outcomes outperform proximal outcomes at dosages at or less than 5.43 h of intervention. In contrast, proximal outcomes had higher effect sizes than distal outcomes at or above 22.6 h of instruction"
