q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of student computer use at home and at school by school racial composition and poverty level (Figures 9 and 10). By 2003, elementary students at predominantly African-American schools used computers at school more than students at integrated schools, and high schools no longer magnified the divide by race to the same degree.

"Increases in computer use at school were much more modest than the increases in computer use at home. However, the largest increases were in schools that predominantly served African-American or Latino students."
