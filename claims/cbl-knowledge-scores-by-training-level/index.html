Dong Haitao 2022¶

Dong Haitao, Guo Chunlan, Zhou Lian, Zhao Jizhi, Wu Xiaomin, Zhang Xinyuan, Zhang Xin. (2022). Effectiveness of case-based learning in Chinese dental education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-048497

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis in Table 3 of the meta-analysis by student level. Junior students showed no significant knowledge-score benefit (SMD 0.82, 95% CI −0.17 to 1.82, p=0.11), while senior (SMD 1.53) and 5th-year (SMD 1.05) subgroups were significant. The quote itself prints no effect size.

"a subgroup analysis of the level of training showed that the CBL teaching model also significantly improved the knowledge scores of senior and 5th-year students (table 3)."
