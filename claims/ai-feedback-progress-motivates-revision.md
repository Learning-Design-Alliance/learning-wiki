### Tzirides 2025

Tzirides, Anastasia Olga (Olnancy), Galla, Michele, Cope, Bill, & Kalantzis, Mary. (2025). Thinking Through AI: Advancing Cognitive and Collaborative Research for AI in Education. https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/s8hqe_v1

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observational findings from the case study's thematic analysis. One student, described as thorough, 'only had time to review the first two rubric criteria in the allotted class time'; another 'added five more sentences' and was enthusiastic about a second feedback round.

> "Students were not only motivated by the rating system but also by seeing concrete improvements in their writing. Student Three carefully read the AI Helper’s revision suggestions and asked for more explanation on several points."
