## Evidence

### Bechtiger 2022

Bechtiger, Laura; Steinhoff, Annekatrin; Dollar, Jessica M.; Halliday, Simone E.; Keane, Susan P.; Calkins, Susan D.; Shanahan, Lilly. (2022). Pathways from Maternal Depressive Symptoms to Children's Academic Performance in Adolescence: A 13-Year Prospective-Longitudinal Study. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13685

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path model in a 13-year prospective longitudinal community study with multi-informant data; bootstrapped indirect effects tested via the product-of-coefficients method. The article reports the total indirect effects were "significant" with the printed confidence intervals for GPA and standardized achievement.

> "The total indirect effects of maternal depressive symptoms on both GPA, −.024, 95%CI = (−.043, −.008), and standardized achievement, −.025, 95%CI = (−.042, −.011), were significant."
