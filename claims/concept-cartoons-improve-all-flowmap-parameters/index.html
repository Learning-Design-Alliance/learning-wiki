Evidence¶

Ceylan 2018¶

Ceylan, Ö., & Yiğit, E. A. (2018). Analyzing the Effect of Concept Cartoon Usage on Students’ Cognitive Structures Developments and Science Achievements through Flow Maps. Science Education International, 29(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1205414

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on flow map size parameter for the 27 experimental students taught with concept cartoons. Positive ranks favored the post-test, indicating increased knowledge level about the unit.

"the experimental group students had a statistically significant difference (z = 4.347, ρ < 0.05) between the pre-test scores of the size parameter after 8 weeks of study"
