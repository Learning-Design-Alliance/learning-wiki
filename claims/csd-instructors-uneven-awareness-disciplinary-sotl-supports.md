## Evidence

### Friberg 2023

Friberg, Jennifer; Hoepner, Jerry K.; Sauerwein, Allison M.; and Mandulak, Kerry (2023) "The Integration of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning into the Discipline of Communication Sciences and Disorders," Teaching and Learning in Communication Sciences & Disorders: Vol. 7: Iss. 1, Article 10. https://doi.org/10.30707/TLCSD7.1.1675490380.873306

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 120 CSD course instructors (18 audiology, 102 speech-language pathology) recruited via program directors of accredited programs, replicating a prior effort. Respondents reported awareness of disciplinary supports; "the least awareness of where financial supports for SoTL scholars might exist."

> "Just over half of survey respondents indicated that they were aware of published works or professional development for scholarly teaching and/or SoTL provided by disciplinary groups such as ASHA or CAPCSD."
