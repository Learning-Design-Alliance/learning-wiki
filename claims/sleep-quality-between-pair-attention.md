Grasby KL, Larsen SA, Gregory AM, Blunden S, Lewis KS, Madrid-Valero JJ, Coventry WL, Byrne B, Olson RK. (2026). Associations between sleep habits, performance in reading and mathematics, and inattention and hyperactivity. PLoS One 21(5): e0347892. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347892

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

BW analyses of inattention and hyperactivity found better pair-average sleep quality predicted better attention and less hyperactivity in Grades 3, 5, and 9; the effect was "did not pass the multiple testing threshold" in Grade 7.

> "The between-pair effect for sleep quality, such that better quality sleep (averaged for a twin pair) predicted better attention and less hyperactivity in every grade, except Grade 7 where it did not pass the multiple testing threshold."
