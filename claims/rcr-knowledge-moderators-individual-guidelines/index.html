Evidence¶

Johannes Katsarov 2021¶

Johannes Katsarov, Roberto Andorno, André Krom, Mariëtte van den Hoven. (2021). Effective Strategies for Research Integrity Training—a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:935–955. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09630-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression of knowledge-related learning from the included RCR studies; the model explained all between-groups variance (F = 10.76, p = 0.000). Table 4 reports coefficients of 0.55* for pure individual instruction, 0.26** for applied guidelines, -0.53* for theoretical emphasis, and -0.31** for mandatory courses; these are regression coefficients, not standardized effect sizes.

"Significant positive effects were found for courses that emphasized individual learning, experiential learning (practice orientation), and an application of ethical guidelines. Significant negative effects were found for courses that were mandatory, avoided concrete cases (theoretical), or did not teach ethical guidelines."
