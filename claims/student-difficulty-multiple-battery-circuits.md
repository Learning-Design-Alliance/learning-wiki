Narrative review statement in the Introduction, citing five prior studies of student misconceptions about electric circuits. The article adds that many students found it difficult to analyze circuits with multiple batteries in single or multiple loops.

> "Many different studies [1–5] have shown that many of the pre-college and the undergraduate physics students lack a qualitative understanding of the simple electric circuits and were unable answer some of the fundamental questions."
