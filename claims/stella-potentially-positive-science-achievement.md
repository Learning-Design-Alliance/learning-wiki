# STeLLA® has potentially positive effects on science achievement, with a statistically significant effect size of 0.68 and an improvement index of +25 percentile points

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students whose teachers participated in STeLLA® scored significantly higher on a researcher-developed science content knowledge test than students whose teachers received content-only professional development (effect size 0.68, improvement index +25). [→ wwc-main-finding](#wwc-main-finding)

## Evidence

### wwc-main-finding

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, May). Science Teachers Learning through Lesson Analysis (STeLLA®). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/716

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

WWC synthesis of one cluster randomized controlled trial (Taylor et al., 2017) meeting standards without reservations. The researcher-developed test showed intervention mean 56.28 (9.88) vs comparison 50.17 (7.71), mean difference 6.11, "Effect size 0.68", improvement index +25, p-value .00.
