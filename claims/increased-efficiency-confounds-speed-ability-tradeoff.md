Steven Wise. (2015). Response time as an indicator of test taker speed: assumptions meet reality. Measurement: Interdisciplinary Research and Perspectives, 13(3–4), 186–188. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/response-time-as-an-indicator-of-test-taker-speed-assumptions-meet-reality

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument: because exhibited speed can change "without a commensurate change in exhibited ability", the usefulness of response time for estimating ability is undermined. This is the author's reasoning, not a tested result; no effect size is printed.

> "It confounds attempts to exploit a speed-ability trade-off, which is central to many of the models that Goldhammer discussed. If exhibited speed can change without a commensurate change in exhibited ability, the usefulness of response time in estimating a test taker's ability is undermined."
