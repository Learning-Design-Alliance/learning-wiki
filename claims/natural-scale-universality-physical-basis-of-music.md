Tsuyoshi HONDOU, Masayuki YOSHIZAWA, and Shozo SUTO. (2007). Science-based comparative culture: A new theme of experiment for freshmen in Tohoku University. https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3527 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.3527](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.3527)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Theoretical/design statement of the theme's first learning aspect: the universality of natural laws. The authors expect students to find that musical scale is quantized corresponding to vibration modes and that natural laws are universal, independent of regions and times. No measured outcome is reported.

> "The ratios of frequency Sol/Do, Mi/Do are 3/2 and 5/4, respectively, which are rational numbers. Students will find th e physical basis of music that is common over ethnic groups, because of the universality of natural laws."
