## Evidence

### Saguin Emeric 2024

Saguin Emeric, Salomé Julia, Favodon Benoît, Lahutte Bertrand, Gignoux-Froment Frédérique. (2024). Validation of a didactic model evaluating the usability, usefulness and acceptability of psychological first aid teaching through simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06491-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression analysis of the 59-trainer survey data. The article reports the three largest individual R2 contributions: C4 at "R2 = 0.241", A1 at "R2 = 0.207", and A2 at "R2 = 0.195"; these are variance-explained values, not standardised effect sizes.

> "Analysis of the model revealed that three criteria individually predicted a significant proportion of trainer motivation: (1) criterion C4, assessing how the tool contributes to the trainer’s personal development (R2 = 0.241); (2) criterion A1, assessing how easily trainers can understand the tool (R2 = 0.207); and criterion A2, assessing user-friendliness (R2 = 0.195)."
