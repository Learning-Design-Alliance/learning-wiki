Giveh 2018¶

Fatemeh Giveh. (2018). Self-directed Learning via Contemplative Teaching to Promote Reading Comprehension Ability. English Language Teaching, 11(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n12p58

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis for RQ4 based on interviews with five named MA TEFL students. Problems included shyness, fear of volunteering, individualistic attitudes, and inability to challenge critical thinking despite teacher efforts to make the class collaborative.

"Talking to Fereshteh, Fariba, Maryam, Mojgan, and Sepideh, the MA students of TEFL, who were the passive participants in the contemplativ e class, the researcher could d ecode the following problems they faced throughout the course: 1). Deep thinking in half an hour is too hard. 2). The classroom was mostly teacher-centered not learner-centered."
