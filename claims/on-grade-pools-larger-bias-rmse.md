# Bias and RMSE of ability estimates are larger for on-grade item pools than for ±1-grade and all-grade pools across content areas and simulee types

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In mathematics simulations, biases for on-grade simulees were consistently larger with the on-grade pool (ranging from -0.4 to 1) and average RMSE dropped from 2.53 (on-grade) to 2.39 (±1-grade) and 2.36 (all-grade). [→ Li 2019](#li-2019)

## Evidence

### Li 2019

Li, S., & Meyer, J. P. (2019). Simulation study for evaluating MAP® Growth™ grade-level item pools. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/simulation-study-for-evaluating-map-growth-item-pools-with-grade-level-constraints/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bias and RMSE analyses (Appendix B, Table B.1) comparing final MLE ability estimates against true RIT in the mathematics simulations. For off-grade simulees, average RMSE decreased to 2.5 for the ±1-grade pool and 2.4 for the all-grade pool versus 2.88 for the on-grade pool.

> "biases for on-grade simulees are consistently larger when using an on-grade item pool (ranging from -0.4 to 1). The RMSEs for the on-grade pool are also relatively large with an average value of 2.53."
