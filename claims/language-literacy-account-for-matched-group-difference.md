Bailey, D. H., Nguyen, T., Jenkins, J. M., Domina, T., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2015). Fadeout in an Early Mathematics Intervention: Same Old Schools or Underlying Skills? SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562173

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression models regressing follow-up REMA scores on matched group with sequential covariate blocks, adjusting for age and weighting by likelihood of treatment assignment. The article reports adjusting for language and literacy "reduced the group difference by 95%", eliminating significance.

> "Adjusting for language and literacy reduced the group difference by 95%, at which point it was no longer statistically significant (Model 2)."
