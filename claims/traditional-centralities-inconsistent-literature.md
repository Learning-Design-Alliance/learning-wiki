## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reviews of previous studies found variable or mixed correlations between traditional centrality measures and performance, with betweenness and closeness centralities showing the most inconsistent results. [→ Saqr 2021](#saqr-2021)

## Evidence

### Saqr 2021

Saqr, M., & López-Pernas, S. (2021). Modelling diffusion in computer-supported collaborative learning: a large scale learning analytics study. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09356-4

The article's literature review (summarized in its Fig. 3) of SNA studies of centrality measures as achievement indicators concludes that "the most investigated centrality measures (betweenness and closeness centralities) have shown the most inconsistent results", and that most studies used single courses with limited participants.
