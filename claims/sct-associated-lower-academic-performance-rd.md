## Evidence

### Hossain Bushra 2022

Hossain Bushra, Bent Stephen, Parenteau China, Widjaja Felicia, Davis Matthew, Hendren Robert L. (2022). The Associations Between Sluggish Cognitive Tempo, Internalizing Symptoms, and Academic Performance in Children With Reading Disorder: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547221085493

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted cross-sectional regression at baseline among children with RD, with academic performance measured by standardized teacher-rated progress scores. Teacher-reported SCT was significantly negatively associated with academic performance (p = .01 in adjusted analysis).

> "teacher-reported SCT was significantly negatively associated with academic performance at baseline, which was also measured by teachers (p < .001 for unadjusted analysis and p = .01 after controlling for age, sex, time at school, and interaction with ADHD-IN)"

### Hossain Bushra 2022 (2)

Hossain Bushra, Bent Stephen, Parenteau China, Widjaja Felicia, Davis Matthew, Hendren Robert L. (2022). The Associations Between Sluggish Cognitive Tempo, Internalizing Symptoms, and Academic Performance in Children With Reading Disorder: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547221085493

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted longitudinal mixed-effects model of within-individual change in teacher-rated academic performance (z-score standardized across three schools). A one-point SCT increase corresponded to a 0.03 SD decrease in academic performance score (p = .008).

> "After adjusting for age, sex, time at school, number of teacher-raters, and interaction with ADHD-IN, a one-point increase in SCT score corresponded to a 0.03 SD decrease in academic performance score"
