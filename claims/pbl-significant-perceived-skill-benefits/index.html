Evidence¶

Manaf et al. 2011¶

Nor Aziah Abdul Manaf, Zuaini Ishak & Wan Nordin Wan Hussin. (2011). Application of Problem Based Learning (PBL) in a Course on Financial Accounting Principles. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction: Vol. 8 (2011): 21-47. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137300

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Survey of 267 students who experienced both PBL and non-PBL methods, questionnaire adapted from Newman (2002), 5-point Likert scale (Table 9). PBL mean 3.92 vs non-PBL 3.54, t=3.622, p=.000. Only test statistics are printed; no effect size.
