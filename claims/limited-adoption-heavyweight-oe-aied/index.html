Mizoguchi 2015¶

The authors' own interpretive assessment (type e) in the section on limitations and recent trends. They attribute low adoption to OMNIBUS-SMARTIES complexity and to the Hozo editor while Protégé became the standard, and count 394 Google Scholar citations of the original paper as of August 25, 2015.

"In spite of the authors' efforts in demonstrating how to proceed in the suggested directions for the use of ontology engineering in AIED, research on ontology building has not reached our expectation."
