`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A small study of forty eight undergraduate learners used GazeTutor with attention-aware components enabled or disabled. The article reports that "learning gains for deep reasoning questions were significantly higher for the experimental group compared to the control group, but only for high aptitude learners".
