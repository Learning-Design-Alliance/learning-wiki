# Technology-enhanced adaptive teaching improves delayed but not immediate learning outcomes compared with business-as-usual teaching

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students in technology-enhanced adaptive teaching units significantly outperformed control students on the four-week delayed posttest (β = 0.27, small effect), while the immediate posttest difference was not significant. [→ Sibley 2025](#sibley-2025)

## Evidence

### Sibley 2025

Sibley, L., Fabian, A., Plicht, C., Pagano, L., Ehrhardt, N., Wellert, L., Bohl, T., & Lachner, A. (2025). Adaptive teaching with technology enhances lasting learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102141

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-post-delayed-test study with parallel classes as controls (N = 656, k = 12 units), cluster-robust fixed-effect models. The delayed benefit was "significant in the four-week delayed test (β = 0.27, p = .003, small effect)"; the immediate difference was not significant.

> "this effect, however, was not significant (β = 0.16, p = .069). Interestingly, the effect was significant in the four-week delayed test (β = 0.27, p = .003, small effect)."
