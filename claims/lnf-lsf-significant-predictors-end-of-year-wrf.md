## Evidence

### Alqahtani SS 2025

Alqahtani SS. (2025). Predictive validity of early and mid-year literacy assessments for end-of-year word reading fluency. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327242

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of longitudinal data from 412 Arabic-speaking kindergarten and grade 1 students assessed at three intervals. The kindergarten fall model showed "LNF1 significantly predicted end-of-year WRF (β = 0.33, p = .002)" with LSF1 also significant; mid-year LNF2 was the largest predictor (β = 0.492).

> "For kindergarten, the regression model showed that LNF1 significantly predicted end-of-year WRF (β = 0.33, p = .002), and LSF1 also contributed significantly (β = 0.38, p = .001)."
