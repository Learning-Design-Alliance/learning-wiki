## Evidence

### Herschbach L 2026

Herschbach L, Festl-Wietek T, Sonanini A, Holderried F, Albrecht T, Herrmann B, Herrmann-Werner A. (2026). AI-Simulated Patients for Training Shared Decision-Making: Feasibility Study in Medical Education. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100467

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validation analysis of 61 chats from the preregistered feasibility study, comparing AI (GPT-5) OPTION-12 ratings with one blinded human expert rater. The article reports "strong agreement between the AI and the human rater for the total score (ICC=0.86" and a correlation of r=0.88, with exact agreement in only 9.84% (6/61) of cases.

> "There was strong agreement between the AI and the human rater for the total score (ICC=0.86, df=2,1; P<.001), although the AI systematically assigned slightly higher scores (mean 25.21, SD 7.34) than the human rater (mean 23.21, SD 7.51)."
