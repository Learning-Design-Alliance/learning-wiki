## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Five groups were tutored by medical staff throughout and none of the students were assigned only to a near-peer tutor, a design necessity the authors flag as potentially biasing the final test results upward. [→ Widyahening Indah S. 2019](#widyahening-indah-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Widyahening Indah S. 2019

Widyahening Indah S., Findyartini Ardi, Ranakusuma Respati W., Dewiasty Esthika, Harimurti Kuntjoro. (2019). Evaluation of the role of near-peer teaching in critical appraisal skills learning: a randomized crossover trial. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c39.b55b

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Design limitation stated in the Discussion: because unequal tutor numbers left five groups with staff throughout, the crossover meant every student experienced staff tutoring, which the authors state "may have led to over-estimation of results".

> "As a result, none of the students in this study were assigned only to a near-peer tutor, and this may have led to over-estimation of results, especially in the final test. Future studies should, therefore, extend the utilization of near-peer tutors to all tutorial sessions."
