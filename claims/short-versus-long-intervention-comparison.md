# One-day and 10-week interventions yield comparable core-concept results, but the one-day program scores significantly less on derived concepts

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-test core-concept scores above 95% for Year 8-10 in the one-day program were comparable to the 10-week program (73% and 91% in 2013 and 2014), but derived-concept scores were significantly lower (31-47%). [→ Rahul K. Choudhary 2018](#rahul-k-choudhary-2018)

## Evidence

### Rahul K. Choudhary 2018

Rahul K. Choudhary, Alexander Foppoli, Tejinder Kaur, David G. Blair, Marjan Zadnik, Richard Meagher. (2018). Can a short intervention focused on gravitational waves and quantum physics improve students' understanding and attitude?. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aae26a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aae26a)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison (Table 4) of the one-day intervention with a prior 20-lesson, 10-week Year 9 program by Kaur et al. using similar pre/post questionnaires. The article reports one-day core scores "comparable to the 10-week program" but significantly less for derived concepts; no effect size is printed.

> "In the one day program the average post -test score for core concepts was more than 95% for Year 8-10 students and 75% for Year 7 students. This is comparable to the 10-week program. However, the students score significantly less  when we consider the derived concepts."
