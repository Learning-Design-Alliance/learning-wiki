# iSTART training enhanced deep comprehension on a transfer task, shown by higher bridging inference scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` On the transfer test, iSTART-trained students scored significantly higher on bridging inference questions than controls, qualified by a treatment by question type interaction. [→ McCarthy 2017](#mccarthy-2017)

## Evidence

### McCarthy 2017

McCarthy, K. S., Jacovina, M. E., Snow, E. L., Guerrero, T. A., & McNamara, D. S. (2017). iSTART therefore I understand: But metacognitive supports did not enhance comprehension gains. Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education, Wuhan, China: Springer. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED577122

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A 2 x 2 ANCOVA on transfer test scores controlling for pretest comprehension. The article reports the interaction "F(1, 231) = 6.65, p < .01, η2p = .03", with iSTART-trained students higher on inference questions.

> "This was qualified by a significant treatment by question type interaction, F(1, 231) = 6.65, p < .01, η2p = .03. As shown in Table 2, there was no effect of iSTART training on text-based comprehension question performance, but those who received iSTART training had a significantly higher average score on the inference comprehension questions than those who received no iSTART training."
