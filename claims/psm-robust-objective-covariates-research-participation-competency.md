## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wang Xinyu 2026

Wang Xinyu, Li Xinquan, Zhang Jiahui, Bao Sihan, Bian MaoWang, Sun Jiabin, Cai Weiqin, Xiao Yang, An Hongqing. (2026). Cultivating competent public health professionals: the role of research participation and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09145-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness PSM model restricted to objective pre-treatment covariates (gender, age, grade, residence, school type, baseline academic performance, family economic conditions) in the same 906-student study. The article reports the robustness ATT as "0.0382, which is almost identical to the ATT value of the original model (0.0391)".

> "Under the main analysis method (radius matching), the ATT value of the robustness model is 0.0382, which is almost identical to the ATT value of the original model (0.0391), both being significant at the 1% level (p < 0.001)"
