## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Vezzali 2016

Vezzali, L., Goclowska, M., Crisp, R. J., & Stathi, S. (2016). On the Relationship between Cultural Diversity and Creativity in Education: The Moderating Role of Communal versus Divisional Mindset. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effects analysis in the divisional mindset condition (story featured two opposing groups, Sun and Moon, with different skin colors and magic powers); the article reports "the effect of diversity was nonsignificant, F < 1". No effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "in line with Hypothesis 2b, however, when the mindset was based on divisions, the effect of diversity was nonsignificant, F < 1 (see Table 1)."
