Prospective power analysis carried forward from the bias-adjusted RoBMA-PSMA posterior distribution under the stated planning scenario; the article stresses this is a scenario-specific benchmark rather than a fixed rule.

Bicakci 2026 (2)¶

Bicakci, M., Heller, F., Stoeger, H., & Ziegler, A. (2026). From RAMSing to Rigor: Improving Moderator Analysis in STEM Education Meta-Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10172-1

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Descriptive audit of the same 1,786-effect-size corpus against the k = 14 power benchmark; the article links underpower to unstable estimates and selective reporting patterns.

"approximately 60.2% of the 1,786 moderator effect sizes fell below k = 14, indicating that limited power is common in STEM meta- analytic research."
