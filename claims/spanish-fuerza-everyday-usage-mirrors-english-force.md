The translated post-survey on the word force was administered to freshman engineering students at Universidad Autonoma de Yucatan in Mexico. The article reports the Spanish-speaking students “are very likely to use the word “fuerza” (force) as a synonym for “poder” (power)”, mirroring the Kansas State verb-dominant usage.

> "We found that Spanish-speaking stude nts in Mexico are very likely to use the word “fuerza” (force) as a synonym for “poder” (power) in the sense of “ability to act or produce an effect,” i.e. they use the word “force” as a verb (“forzar”), which is similar to the way English-speaking students at Kansas State responded to the same survey."
