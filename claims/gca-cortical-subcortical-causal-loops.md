Yuan Liu, Shuaifei Huang, Weiguo Xu, Zhuang Wang & Dong Ming. (2024). An fMRI study on the generalization of motor learning after brain actuated supernumerary robot training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00294-y

Granger Causality Analysis on ROIs from the resting-state FC results (SM_cortex integrating S1M1_R and SMA_L, thalamus, cerebellum, Frontal_Mid_L). "signiﬁcant bi- directional increase of causal effect between the SM_cortex with the THA and frontal lobe" was found; SM_cortex to Cere also increased while Cere to SM_cortex decreased (p = 0.024).
