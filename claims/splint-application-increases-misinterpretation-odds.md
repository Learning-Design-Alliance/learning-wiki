`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (Table 4) adjusting for sex, age group, radiographic adequacy, and splint use, from the Results of the retrospective study. Pediatric orthopedists showed significant splint effects across all fracture types; first-year residents showed no significant associations for any factor.

> "In pediatric radiologists, splint application was significantly associated with increased odds of misinterpretation for total fractures (OR = 5.26; 95% CI: 1.55–17.80; p = 0.008) and non-Monteggia fractures (OR = 6.38; 95% CI: 1.01–40.51; p = 0.049), but not for Monteggia fractures."
