## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gouzi F 2019

Gouzi F, Hédon C, Blervaque L, Passerieux E, Kuster N, Pujol T, Mercier J, Hayot M. (2019). Interactive whiteboard use in clinical reasoning sessions to teach diagnostic test ordering and interpretation to undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1834-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feedback study comparing IWB/CRL third-year students (mean 3.54 ± 1.03) with traditional-course students across years 3-6 on a 5-point Likert scale. IWB students scored significantly higher than third-year traditional students (2.77 ± 0.87), similar to sixth-year students (3.47 ± 0.88), p < 0.001.

> "Now I look for positive and negative nonclinical signs directly on the raw data and not on the report 3.54 ± 1.03** 2.77 ± 0.87 2.48 ± 0.85 2.70 ± 0.92 3.47 ± 0.88** < 0.001"
