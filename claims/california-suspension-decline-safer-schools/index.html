The report's state-case analysis of California's policy changes reports that "suspensions declined by 33.6%" and expulsions dropped 40.4% between 2011 and 2016, with declines holding for all racial and socioeconomic groups and school levels.

"Between 2011 and 2016, suspensions declined by 33.6%, driven by a 77% decline in suspensions for “willful defiance,” and expulsions dropped by 40.4%."
