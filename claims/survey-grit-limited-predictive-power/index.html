Evidence¶

Chen 2024¶

Chen, D. L., Ertac, S., Evgeniou, T., Miao, X., Nadaf, A., & Yilmaz, E. (2024). Grit and academic resilience during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00265-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

OLS regressions in Table 3 (N = 1723, 1508, 1710, 1490) show survey grit coefficients of 0.5, 0.8, 0.5, and 0.5 for pre/post math and science, with only pre-Covid science approaching significance (p < 0.10).

"Table3 shows that survey grit is only associated with one of these four measures of student performance—0.8 points in science prior to Covid (p < 0.10)."

Chen 2024 (2)¶

Chen, D. L., Ertac, S., Evgeniou, T., Miao, X., Nadaf, A., & Yilmaz, E. (2024). Grit and academic resilience during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00265-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Table 4 regressions of performance change on survey grit show coefficients of 0.03 (math) and −0.2 (science), neither significant. The null may reflect social desirability or incentive issues the authors acknowledge for self-reports.

"Table 4 shows, in regression form, that survey grit is essentially not correlated or slightly negatively correlated with resilience during Covid-19"
