## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher performance in Session 2 was significantly associated with an overall positive change in teaching self‐efficacy, career intentions and perceived PV fit during the simulations. [→ Wang 2025](#wang-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, H., Thompson‐Lee, S., & Klassen, R. M. (2025). Performance on classroom simulations enhances preservice teachers' motivation in teaching: A latent change perspective. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S239–S255. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent change SEM path from Session 2 performance score to latent change in each outcome, controlling for baseline, in a sample of 1411 preservice teachers. All three paths were significant, with Session 2 performance "significantly associated with an overall positive change" in all three motivational outcomes.

> "in Session 2, higher performance in the simulations was significantly associated with an overall positive change (increase) in participants' teaching self‐efficacy (β = .079, p = .014), career intentions (β = .065, p = .039), and perceived PV fit (β = .101, p = .002) during the classroom simulations."
