`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Speed-accuracy LMM analysis (Table S2) in the same study, reported in the discussion as exploratory. The authors state the absence of a trade-off "(Table S2)" and note high accuracy indicated a potential ceiling effect, limiting sensitivity to anxiety-performance relations.

> "In absence of a speed-accuracy trade-off (Table S2), this indicates that math-anxious individuals may respond more slowly without sacrificing accuracy in tasks that are in principle solvable and not too difficult (relatively high accuracy leading to ceiling effects)"
