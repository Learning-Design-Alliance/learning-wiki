## Evidence

### Bashirian S 2020

Bashirian S, Barati M, Karami M, Hamzeh B, Ezati E. (2020). Effectiveness of E-Learning Program in Preventing WP Smoking in Adolescent Females in West of Iran by Applying Prototype-Willingness Model: A Randomized Controlled Trial. J Res Health Sci. 20(4): e00497. https://doi.org/10.34172/jrhs.2020.31

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of prototype-willingness model construct mean scores at baseline and 3 months post-intervention in the RCT (Table 2). The article reports "a significant decrease in the mean scores" for all five constructs; only P values are printed, no effect sizes.

> "there was a significant decrease in the mean scores of positive attitude toward WP smoking (P=0.010), subjective norms about WP smoking (P=0.010), prototype about WP smokers (P=0.002), intention (P=0.002) and behavioral willingness (P=0.017) in the experimental group compared to the control group after the educational intervention"
