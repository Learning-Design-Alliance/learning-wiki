# Educational trips produced higher adjusted environmental knowledge scores than service learning, which in turn exceeded control

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The educational trip group's adjusted posttest mean (M = 13.97) exceeded service learning (M = 12.42) and control (M = 11.01). [→ Ajitoni 2015](#ajitoni-2015)
`q3 i?` Scheffé post hoc tests showed significant pairwise differences (p<0.05) between service learning and educational trips, and between each experimental group and control. [→ Ajitoni 2015 (2)](#ajitoni-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Ajitoni 2015

Ajitoni, S. O., & Gbadamosi, T. V. (2015). Community-Based Instructional Strategies, School Location, and Primary School Pupils' Environmental Knowledge. JISTE Vol. 19, No. 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177058

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple classification analysis of adjusted environmental knowledge means by treatment group in the quasi-experiment. The article concludes "the educational trip was the most effective of the three strategies"; no effect size is printed for this ordering.

> "pupils in the educational trip instructional group had higher adjusted posttest environmental knowledge score ( M = 13.97; adj dev. = 1.56) than their counterparts in service learning ( M=12.42; adj. dev. = 0.01) and control ( M=11.01, adj. dev. = -1.39) respectively."

### Ajitoni 2015 (2)

Ajitoni, S. O., & Gbadamosi, T. V. (2015). Community-Based Instructional Strategies, School Location, and Primary School Pupils' Environmental Knowledge. JISTE Vol. 19, No. 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177058

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scheffé post hoc analysis tracing the source of the significant treatment effect, with printed means and SDs for each group. Only p<0.05 significance is reported; no effect size is printed for the pairwise contrasts.

> "there were significant differences (p<0.05) between pairs of groups service learning ( M=12.42; SD=4.25) and educational trips (M = 13.97; SD=2.71) and control (M =11.01; SD=6.32) as well as educational trips ( M = 13.97; SD=3.97) and control ( M =11.01; SD=7.82)."
