# The retention gap persisted at 48 hours with no differential forgetting between groups (39% vs 52%)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` AI-Assisted students scored lower on the 48-hour delayed retention quiz (M=0.39 vs M=0.52, Hedges' g = 0.77), and a mixed ANOVA found no group×time interaction, indicating no difference in the rate of forgetting. [→ Bergh 2026](#bergh-2026)

## Evidence

### Bergh 2026

Bergh, C., Tag, B., Vassar, A., & Renzella, J. (2026). Your Programming Students'Cognition with ChatGPT: Higher Performance, Lower Retention, and Reduced Ownership. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21194

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Delayed cued-recall quiz administered 48 hours after the session to all participants, using the same instrument and blind three-marker scoring. The No AI group again recalled more, with a printed medium-to-large effect size Hedges' g = 0.77, surviving Holm correction.

> "The same pattern was observed 48 hours later (𝑀= 0.52,𝑆𝐷= 0.16vs. 𝑀= 0.39,𝑆𝐷= 0.19for No AI and AI-Assisted respectively), also significant (𝑡(52.9)=2.91,𝑝=.005, 𝑔=0.77,𝑝 Holm =.011)"
