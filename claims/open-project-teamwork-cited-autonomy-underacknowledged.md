## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Team work was quoted by 53% of open-project students versus 24% of focused-lab students, and work organization by 24% versus 0%. [→ F. Bouquet 2015](#f-bouquet-2015)
`q2 i?` Autonomy was mentioned as personal impact by only 18% of open-project students versus 57% of focused-lab students, yet 59% of open-project students said they enjoyed being autonomous. [→ F. Bouquet 2015 (2)](#f-bouquet-2015-2)

### F. Bouquet 2015 (2)
