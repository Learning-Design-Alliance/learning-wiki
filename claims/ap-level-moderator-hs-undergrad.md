Van Iddekinge, C. H., Arnold, J. D., Krivacek, S. J., Frieder, R. E., & Roth, P. L. (2024). Making the Grade? A Meta-Analysis of Academic Performance as a Predictor of Work Performance and Turnover. Journal of Applied Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/apl0001212

`q2 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis in Table 3 of the meta-analysis comparing AP level categories. High school measures (k = 24) showed a mean corrected ρ of .15 versus .21 for undergraduate measures (k = 100), consistent with the hypothesis that validity increases with AP level.

> "High school 24 5,702 .12 .15 .16 [.08, .21] .14 24.0 [−.04, .33] Undergraduate 100 17,051 .17 .21 .24 [.18, .25] .13 35.1 [.05, .38]"
