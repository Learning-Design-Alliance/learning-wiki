`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reading-time analysis in the MRMC study: paired t-tests comparing time per mammogram before and after AI assistance show savings of 18.0 s for non-malignant versus 11.1 s for malignant cases (P<.001); Table 4 shows junior residents saved the most (19.7 s).

> "with the highest time savings observed in non-malignant cases (18.0 [SD 34.3] s (95% CI [17.0, 19.0]) vs malignant cases (11.1 [SD 34.2] s (95% CI [10.1, 12.1] per mammogram read after AI assistance, P<.001)"
