# Students rate formative OSCE feedback as highly useful regardless of tutor profile, but report generalists involve them more actively

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On 348 post-session questionnaires students rated feedback as useful (mean 4.73 on a 1-5 Likert scale) with no statistically significant generalist-versus-specialist difference in perceived usefulness or skill improvement. [→ Junod Perron Noelle 2016](#junod-perron-noelle-2016)
`q3 i?` Students reported that generalist tutors involved them more actively than specialists at all stages of the feedback. [→ Junod Perron Noelle 2016 (2)](#junod-perron-noelle-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Junod Perron Noelle 2016

Junod Perron Noelle, Louis-Simonet Martine, Cerutti Bernard, Pfarrwaller Eva, Sommer Johanna, Nendaz Mathieu. (2016). The quality of feedback during formative OSCEs depends on the tutors' profile. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0815-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Student questionnaire study (348 questionnaires: 79 video-based, 269 direct observation) with a 15-item Likert instrument; adjusted p-values modeled OSCE type as fixed effect and supervisor as random effect. Perceived usefulness mean was 4.73 (SD 0.57) overall.

> "There were no statistically significant differences between generalists' and specialists' feedback in the way students perceived its usefulness and impact on skill improvement."

### Junod Perron Noelle 2016 (2)

Junod Perron Noelle, Louis-Simonet Martine, Cerutti Bernard, Pfarrwaller Eva, Sommer Johanna, Nendaz Mathieu. (2016). The quality of feedback during formative OSCEs depends on the tutors' profile. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0815-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same questionnaire study. Items on active involvement favored generalists after adjustment, e.g., tutor asked my learning needs (adjusted p = 0.013), stimulated participation in problem solving (adjusted p = 0.001), checked my understanding (adjusted p = 0.001).

> "However, they reported that generalists involved them more actively than specialists at all stages of the feedback."
