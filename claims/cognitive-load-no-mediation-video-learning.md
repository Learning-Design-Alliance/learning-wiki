## Evidence

### Beege 2025

Beege, M., & Ploetzner, R. (2025). Learning from interactive video: the influence of self-explanations, navigation, and cognitive load. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09693-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory mediation path analysis with the two features as predictors and the three load facets as mediators found "no significant indirect effects"; e.g., intrinsic load coefficients ranged β = [-0.05 to −0.01], p = [0.277–0.646]. The authors call the null unsurprising given the absent direct effects.

> "Overall, mediation analyses revealed no significant indirect effects of self-explanation prompts and navigation features on learning performance. Intrinsic load did not act as medi- ator (β = [-0.05 ˗ − 0.01], SE = [0.02 ˗ 0.05], z = [-1.09 ˗ -0.50], p = [0.277 ˗ 0.646])"
