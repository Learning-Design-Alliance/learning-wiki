### Olde Bekkink M 2011

Olde Bekkink M, Donders R, van Muijen GNP, Ruiter DJ. (2011). Challenging medical students with an interim assessment: a positive effect on formal examination score in a randomized controlled study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9291-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, contrasting their no-feedback design with Larsen et al.'s feedback-supported resident study: "even without such feedback, retention of information, as measured by the formal examination score, occurs." The authors also propose students were engaged in retrieval practice as a manifestation of the testing effect.

> "Our findings suggest that even without such feedback, retention of information, as measured by the formal examination score, occurs."
