## Evidence

### Locatis Craig 2011

Locatis Craig, Berner Eta S, Hammack Glenn, Smith Steve, Maisiak Richard, Ackerman Michael. (2011). Communication and proximity effects on outcomes attributable to sense of presence in distance bioinformatics education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-10

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA of instruction rating scale items (Cronbach's alpha = .89) from 81 medical students in four conditions. Webcast students rated the instructor higher on "encouraging further learning" and "motivating students to follow up on their own"; only F and p values are printed, no effect size.

> "Students in the webcast condition rated the instructor significantly higher than those in the videoconferencing condition on encouraging further learning (F = 8.25, p < .005) and motivating students to follow up on their own (F = 16.16, p < .001)."
