Re-analysis of Rau et al.'s (2017) study comparing sense-making-first versus fluency-building-first sequences for representational competencies with fractions. The article reports the study supported the sense-making-first hypothesis "but not the fluency-first hypothesis" on error rates and post-instruction knowledge. The authors' CID analysis attributes the difference to specificity of the preparation mechanism.

> "Indeed, the study supports the sense-making-first hypothesis, but not the fluency-first hypothesis, regarding the error rates in the activities as well as the knowledge measured after the combined instructional activities."
