## Evidence

### Sakamoto Kayo 2026

Sakamoto Kayo, Mitani Sohei, Nishio Naoki, Kitani Takashi, Sato Eriko, Tanaka Keiko, Ugumori Toru, Wakisaka Hiroyuki, Hato Naohito. (2026). Development of an electrosurgery-compatible simulation task for quantitatively assessing oral cancer resection skills: initial validity evidence. BMC Medical Education, 26, 408. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08743-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Nine-directional margin analysis in the performance study (5 experts, 12 novices): absolute margin error was significantly shorter for experts, the deep margin differed (1.20 ± 1.42 vs 4.72 ± 3.16 mm; P < 0.0001), and "the novices performed resection with longer deep margins (13.83 ± 4.22 mm, P = 0.0014)". The authors report these tests as exploratory; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The overall mean margin distances achieved by the experts and novices were not significantly different (P = 0.57). However, the CV of the experts was significantly lower than that of the novices (0.08 vs. 0.16; P = 0.0023)."
