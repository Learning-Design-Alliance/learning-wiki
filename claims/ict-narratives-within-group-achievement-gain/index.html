Evidence¶

Fatma Taşkin Ekici 2015¶

Fatma Taşkin Ekici, Sultan Pekmezci. (2015). Using ICT-Supported Narratives in Teaching Science and their Effects on Middle School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077655

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-test within the experimental group (n=23): means rose from 6.70 to 10.26; Table 3 prints t=7.80, p=0.000, Cohen's d=-1.26. The article attributes the gain to story-assisted courses.

"there is a significant difference observed between the pre-test and post-test scores of the students in terms of achievement (t=7,80;p<.05)"

Fatma Taşkin Ekici 2015 (2)¶

Fatma Taşkin Ekici, Sultan Pekmezci. (2015). Using ICT-Supported Narratives in Teaching Science and their Effects on Middle School Students. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077655

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test within the control group (n=21) receiving regular curriculum: means rose from 7.48 to 8.33 but the article reports t=0,984; p>.05 (Table 3 prints p=0.337), an increase the authors call typical course achievement.

"there is no significant achievement difference statistically between the pre-test and post-test scores of the control group students, who had regular Ministry of Education curriculum without stories"
