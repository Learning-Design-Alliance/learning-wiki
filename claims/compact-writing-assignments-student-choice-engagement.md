Student feedback survey on the Compact Writing Assignments implemented in Pilot District 3 (Chart 4). The report also states that "67% of students felt that having various ways to express their ideas improved their writing, also with 27% strongly agreeing." Percentages are descriptive agreement rates; no inferential tests reported.
