## Evidence

### Tenison 2015

Tenison, C., & Anderson, J. R. (2015). Modeling the distinct phases of skill acquisition. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on novel-problem latencies comparing assessment vs. no-assessment blocks across three combined time blocks. The article reports "no significant effect of assessment" and no significant interaction; equivalence was not tested.

> "The analysis revealed a marginal main effect of time block, F (1,19)=3.8, MSE=2.0, p=0.06, but no significant effect of assessment, F (1,19)=1.3, MSE=1.3, p=0.27), nor a significant interaction between the variables, F (1,59)=1.1, MSE=0.6, p=0.29."
