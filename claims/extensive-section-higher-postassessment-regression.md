# Extensive-section students score significantly higher on a content postassessment when preassessment score and demographics are controlled

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Using multiple linear regression controlling for preassessment score, high school biology, other science courses, and year in school, Extensive-section students scored about 2.5 points higher on the postassessment (coefficient 2.51, p = 0.045). [→ Connell 2016](#connell-2016)

## Evidence

### Connell 2016

Connell, G. L., Donovan, D. A., & Chambers, T. G. (2016). Increasing the Use of Student-Centered Pedagogies from Moderate to High Improves Student Learning and Attitudes about Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0062

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple linear regression of postassessment scores (n = 158 Extensive, 142 Moderate completing pre- and postassessments) with section and demographic covariates; the section coefficient of 2.51 was significant at "p = 0.045" (Table 4). Preassessment score and number of other science courses also significantly affected postassessment score.

> "The regression coefficient of 2.51 for section on postassessment score indicates that students in the Extensive section scored ∼2.5 points higher than students in the Moderate section, all other factors being held equal."
