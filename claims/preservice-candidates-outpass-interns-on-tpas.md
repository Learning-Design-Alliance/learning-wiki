# Preservice candidates pass teaching performance assessments at higher rates than intern candidates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Preservice candidates were more likely than intern candidates to pass a TPA on their first attempt (77% vs. 67%) and across all attempts (92% vs. 88%). [→ Patrick 2024](#patrick-2024)

## Evidence

### Patrick 2024

Patrick, S. K. (2024). How preparation predicts teaching performance assessment results in California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/544.849

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of CTC TPA records for 14,415 preservice and 3,696 intern candidates taking the CalTPA or edTPA between September 2021 and August 2023. The report states "Both differences are statistically significant based on t-tests." No effect size is printed.

> "77% of the preservice preparation candidates who took CalTPA or edTPA during the 2021–22 and 2022–23 academic years passed on the first try, and 92% of these candidates passed across all attempts. In comparison, 67% of intern candidates passed on the first try and 88% passed across all attempts."
