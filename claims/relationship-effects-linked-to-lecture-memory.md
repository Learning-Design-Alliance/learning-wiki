# Relationship effects on teaching evaluations and positive affect are linked to relational quiz performance (memory for lectures)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` When a student rated a professor's lecture as unusually effective, or the professor elicited unusually high positive affect, the student scored especially well on the corresponding quiz. [→ Gross Jennifer 2015](#gross-jennifer-2015)

## Evidence

### Gross Jennifer 2015

Gross Jennifer, Lakey Brian, Lucas Jessica L, LaCross Ryan, R Plotkowski Andrea, Winegard Bo. (2015). Forecasting the student–professor matches that result in unusually effective teaching. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12049

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of relationship scores (N = 483) linking live-lecture evaluations and positive affect to 12-item multiple-choice quiz scores; Table 2 prints r = .16* for evaluations–quiz. Quiz reliability for relationship effects was only .34.

> "Perhaps most important, relationship effects on teaching evaluations and positive affect for live lectures were linked to relational quiz scores."
