## Evidence

### Morin CE 2019

Morin CE, Hostetter JM, Jeudy J, Kim WG, McCabe JA, Merrow AC, Ropp AM, Shet NS, Sidhu AS, Kim JS. (2019). Spaced radiology: encouraging durable memory using spaced testing in pediatric radiology. Pediatric Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-019-04415-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Unpublished pilot study by the authors: eighteen residents reviewed an 84-card deck of adult chest radiographs twice, separated by a 2-week didactic block. The article reports "a small increase" of 10% with no test statistic or effect size.

> "The average score across residents rose by 10% between the flashcard review sessions, a small increase, which is not surprising."

### Morin CE 2019 (2)

Morin CE, Hostetter JM, Jeudy J, Kim WG, McCabe JA, Merrow AC, Ropp AM, Shet NS, Sidhu AS, Kim JS. (2019). Spaced radiology: encouraging durable memory using spaced testing in pediatric radiology. Pediatric Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-019-04415-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey administered at both pilot time points in the same eighteen-resident pilot; the article reports that "100% of the responding residents" endorsed the method and all recommended deck review before starting independent call. Descriptive percentages only.

> "100% of the responding residents believed the flashcard method would improve their ability to recognize and diagnose the included chest radiograph abnormalities"
