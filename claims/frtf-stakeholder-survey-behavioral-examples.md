# Stakeholder consultation with 60 WIL students, employers, and educators generated 1,806 behavioral examples distilled into 59

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Surveys of WIL students (n = 18), employers (n = 16), and educators (n = 26) produced 1,806 behavioral example responses, averaging 41.1 examples per talent (SD = 3.22). [→ Pretti 2021](#pretti-2021)
`q2 i?` A steering committee reviewed the responses and identified 59 behavioral examples tied to the 12 talents. [→ Pretti 2021 (2)](#pretti-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Pretti 2021

Pretti, T. J., Etmanski, B., & Drewery, D. W. (2021). Development and validation of a future ready talent framework. International Journal of Work-Integrated Learning, Special Issue, 22(3), 369-383. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1313416

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Stakeholder consultation survey in the extension-and-validation study. Participants were given FRTF talent definitions and "asked to share examples of such talents in the workplace." Employers were a convenience sample who had hired a WIL student in the previous year, across varied industries and positions.

> "A total of 1,806 responses were provided. The average number of examples for each talent was 41.1 (SD = 3.22)."

### Pretti 2021 (2)

Pretti, T. J., Etmanski, B., & Drewery, D. W. (2021). Development and validation of a future ready talent framework. International Journal of Work-Integrated Learning, Special Issue, 22(3), 369-383. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1313416

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The selected steering committee, comprising experts in WIL and talent development in higher education, reviewed, deduplicated, and synthesized the survey responses; the resulting 59 behavioral examples appear in Table 4 and were carried into the sorting validation.

> "After removing duplicates and much discussion, 59 behavioral examples were identified (Table 4)."
