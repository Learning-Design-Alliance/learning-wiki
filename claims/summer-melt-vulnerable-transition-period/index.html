Related Claims¶

  • The summer outreach study uses a pretest-posttest randomized design with 40 percent of each graduating class assigned to treatment, analyzed via intent-to-treat models of fall enrollment — related
  • Approximately 10 percent of CPS graduates accepted into a four-year college do not enroll the following fall — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Becoming too focused on a single college option left a highly qualified student with no fallback when that option fell through, and she did not enroll in college — related
  • Even students with stellar qualifications and strong plans can fail to enroll after career uncertainty, incomplete financial aid paperwork, and no extended adult conversation about college combine to make choice feel too risky — related
  • Students with strong qualifications took themselves out of four-year college planning, choosing early two-year enrollment as a low-risk solution when searches felt overwhelming and costly — related
  • Among accepted students, FAFSA completion is associated with roughly 50 percent higher four-year college enrollment — related
