### Shiels 2026

Shiels, L., Tomlin, D., Payne, J.M. et al. (2026). Benefits of classroom remote microphone technology for inattentive children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00427-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Phase one 4-week randomized controlled crossover trial (n=35), assessed in a simulated noisy classroom at 0 dB SNR. Table 1 reports AZ-Bio sentences improving from 58.73 (15.42) without RMT to 85.78 (9.70) with RMT, a Cohen's d of 2.11 (p < 0.001). The text reports that "Both speech recognition and listening comprehension scores improved with device use (P < .001)."
