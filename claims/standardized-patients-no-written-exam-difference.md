## Evidence

### Herbstreit Frank 2017

Herbstreit Frank, Merse Stefanie, Schnell Rainer, Noack Marcel, Dirkmann Daniel, Besuch Anna, Peters Jürgen. (2017). Impact of standardized patients on the training of medical students to manage emergencies. Medicine 96(5):e5933. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000005933

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Written 30-question multiple-choice test of factual knowledge in the same randomized trial. The comparison was not significant ("P = 0.341, Cohen d: −0.123"); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The traditional seminar cohort's average was 27.0 points ± 4.4 and the students taught on standardized patients scored an average of 27.4 ± 2.4 points (t = −0.955 [Student t test; 2-tailed, equal variances], d.f. = 240, difference = −0.427; P = 0.341, Cohen d: −0.123"
