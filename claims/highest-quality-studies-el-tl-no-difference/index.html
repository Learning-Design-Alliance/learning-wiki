Evidence¶

Alexander J Savage 2022¶

Alexander J Savage, Patrick W McNamara, Thomas W Moncrieff, Gerard M O'Reilly. (2022). E‐learning in emergency medicine: A systematic review. Emergency Medicine Australasia. https://doi.org/10.1111/1742-6723.13936

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Review's quality-stratified descriptive analysis: studies were deemed highest quality if they were an RCT with "a moderate size cohort of 30 or more participants", a pre-test and no unclear or high risk of bias. All five such studies showed no significant difference.

"Five studies fit within this subgroup, all of which demonstrated no statistically significant differences between EL and TL. 16 , 17 , 18 , 19 , 20 This supports the suggestion that the two learning resources may be comparable but the heterogeneity within this subgroup precludes any strong conclusions from being drawn."
