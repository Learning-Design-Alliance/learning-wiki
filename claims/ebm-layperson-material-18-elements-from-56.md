## Evidence

### Okabayashi 2022

Okabayashi, S., Kitazawa, K., Kawamura, T., & Nakayama, T. (2022). E-Learning Material of Evidence-Based Medicine for Laypersons. HLRP: Health Literacy Research and Practice, 6(4), e290-e299. https://doi.org/10.3928/24748307-20221113-01

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Development-phase analysis reported in the abstract and Results: two researchers independently rated elements on understandability, usefulness for critical appraisal, and usefulness for self-care decisions, then "reviewed by four expert panelists" before finalizing 18 elements.

> "A total of 217 descriptions related to EBM were extracted from 12 materials and were integrated into 56 major elements. Each element was rated from the viewpoint of usefulness for laypersons and reviewed by four expert panelists, and finally 18 elements were confirmed"
