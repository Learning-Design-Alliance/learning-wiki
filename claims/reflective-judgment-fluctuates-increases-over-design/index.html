Muruvvet Demiral-Uzan 2024¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case analysis of Britney's design process over one semester. Early judgments were made without deep consideration, but as she developed instruction she considered consequences and employed reflection; the article reports her "reflective moments increased as she progressed with her design." Tables 5-12 document similar initially-unreflective-then-reflective patterns across cases.
