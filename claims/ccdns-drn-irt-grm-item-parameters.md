## Evidence

### Lai Jinjia 2025

Lai Jinjia, Chen Hongyi, Huang Yongqi, Ma Chaoqun, Zeng Ping, Chen Hanxi, Tan Yibing. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Core Competencies in Disaster Nursing Scale for Disaster Rescue Nurses in Mainland China. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9957270

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

IRT analysis using the multidimensional item response theory package in R 4.5.0 on the 401-nurse survey data, fitting a graded response model; the authors report average information of items "distributed between 0.961 and 3.424, all surpassing the benchmark value of 0.756 (25/33)."

> "The results revealed that the discrimination indices of all items ranged from 2.037 to 4.970, with all values exceeding 0.6, indicating that all items demonstrate good discrimination. The difficulty parameters of all items ranged from −2.837 to 1.396, showing a unidirectional increasing trend aligned with ascending difficulty levels (b1–b4) without any reversed thresholds, thereby confirming compliance with established requirements."
