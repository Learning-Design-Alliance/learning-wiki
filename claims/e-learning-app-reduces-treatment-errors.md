## Evidence

### Färber Christoph Maria 2022

Färber Christoph Maria, Lemos Martin, Said Yekta-Michael Sareh. (2022). Effect of an endodontic e-learning application on students’ performance during their first root canal treatment on real patients: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03463-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Error-point analysis (maximum 20 error points) of the same 138-student cohort's first real-patient treatments, analyzed with Kruskal–Wallis and Bonferroni post-hoc tests. The article reports errors "significantly decreased from 3.2 ± 2.9 in the control group to 1.2 ± 1.3 in test group 1 (P < 0.001)".

> "The number of treatment errors significantly decreased from 3.2 ± 2.9 in the control group to 1.2 ± 1.3 in test group 1 (P < 0.001). The number of treatment errors did not significantly differ between test group 1 and test group 2 (0.8 ± 0.9); however, it significantly differed between test group 2 and the control group (P < 0.001; Fig. 4)."
