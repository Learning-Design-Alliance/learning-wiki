Evidence¶

Frey 2015¶

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Feil, E. G., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., & Forness, S. (2015). First Step to Success: Applications to Preschoolers at Risk of Developing Autism Spectrum Disorders. Education and Training in Autism and Developmental Disabilities, 50(4), 397-407. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581234

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Process and fidelity data collected during the trial for the ASD-risk subsample: "School implementation quality (fidelity) was excellent for coaches (.94"; range .88 to 1.00) and good for teachers (.82; range .41 to 1.00). Children received 96% of program days and families 89% of home sessions; parent compliance (.59) and implementation quality (.62) were only moderately satisfactory.

"In terms of process measures, coaches and teachers adhered to at least 95% of observed core program components."
