## Evidence

### Sainz‐Rozas 2025

Sainz‐Rozas, P. B., Balaguer‐López, E., García‐Molina, P., and Martínez, P. G. (2025). Evaluation of Paediatric Nursing Students' Medication Calculation Competence: An Observational Study Using a Gamified Contest. Nursing Open 12(9): e70295. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70295

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Discussion of the Mann–Whitney U comparison between Groups 1 and 2; the Results report the 13 discriminating variables as six (46.15%) mathematics, five (38.46%) knowledge and two (15.4%) concentration questions, with difficult questions dropping from 20% to 7.7% of the discriminating set.

> "Among the 13 primary variables that made the difference between both groups, a difference was observed in the complexity of the questions: the questions with the greatest influence were not the difficult ones but rather the moderate ones."
