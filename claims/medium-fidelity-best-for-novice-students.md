## Evidence

### Mocellin D 2026

Mocellin D, Maglio S, Nanyaro M-W, Belardi P, Borellini M, Shayo EH, Ferrari A, Mfaume B, Ntinginya N, Tognarelli S, Menciassi A. (2026). Neonatal Ventilation Training Using a Low-Cost, High-Fidelity Simulator in a Low-Resource Setting: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.33261

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Linear mixed-effects model of ventilation rate across 6 hands-on trials among 14 students randomized to the medium-fidelity passive configuration, reported in the Results section. The article reports "a significant overall improvement trend only in the medium-fidelity passive group" with printed trial means and 95% CIs.

> "Regarding breathing frequency, the mixed-effects model identified a significant overall improvement trend only in the medium-fidelity passive group, reaching the target ventilation rate in trials 3 through 6 compared with trials 1 and 2 (mean BPM per trial: trial 1, 37.0 breaths/min [95% CI, 35.2-38.7 breaths/min]; trial 2, 38.0 breaths/min [95% CI, 36.1-39.5 breaths/min]; trial 3, 40.0 breaths/min [95% CI, 38.1-41.5 breaths/min]; trial 4, 40.8 [95% CI, 39.0-42.5 breaths/min]; trial 5, 41.2 [95% CI, 39.5-43.0 breaths/min]; trial 6, 40.9 breaths/min [95% CI, 39.1-42.6 breaths/min])"

### Mocellin D 2026 (2)

Mocellin D, Maglio S, Nanyaro M-W, Belardi P, Borellini M, Shayo EH, Ferrari A, Mfaume B, Ntinginya N, Tognarelli S, Menciassi A. (2026). Neonatal Ventilation Training Using a Low-Cost, High-Fidelity Simulator in a Low-Resource Setting: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.33261

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Linear mixed-effects model of mean airway pressure across 6 trials among students randomized to the high-fidelity passive configuration, reported in the Results section. The discussion attributes this to overventilation, "applying both pressures and ventilation rates above the target."

> "Across the 6 trials, the overall interaction model for mean airway pressure showed that the high-fidelity passive group showed a significant decline in performance, failing to reach the target pressure in all trials after trial 1"
