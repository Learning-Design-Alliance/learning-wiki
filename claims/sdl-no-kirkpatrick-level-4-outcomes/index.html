Evidence¶

Wilkes S. 2026¶

Wilkes S., Maggio L. A., Martin P. C., Melton J., Zheng B. (2026). Self-Directed Learning in Health Professions Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2128

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic characterization of outcome levels across all 125 included studies using the Kirkpatrick framework. The authors suggest the complete absence of Level 4 studies "may reflect the challenges in measuring patient or system-level outcomes in health professions education."

"In 98 studies, learning outcomes were categorized as Kirkpatrick level 2 outcomes, those that involve the measurement of knowledge or skills (typically through tests or formal assessments), while in 27 studies they were categorized as level 3, those that involve behavioral outcomes such as clinical performance. We did not identify any studies at Level 4"
