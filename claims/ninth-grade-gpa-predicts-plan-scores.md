## Evidence

### Easton 2017

Easton, J.Q., Johnson, E., & Sartain, L. (2017). The predictive power of ninth-grade GPA. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/predictive-power-ninth-grade-gpa

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regression analysis of 2011-13 cohorts taking fall tenth-grade PLAN, using the same controls and fixed effects as the outcome models plus eighth-grade ISAT scores. The article reports "a statistically significant association" with these adjusted PLAN-point differences, unadjusted means ranging from 21.3 (A) to 11.8 (F).

> "Given their eighth-grade test scores, A students scored 1.20 points higher on the PLAN than average students. B students scored 0.25 points more than average. In contrast, C, D, and F students scored lower than average, by 0.46, 1.14, and 1.85 points, respectively."
