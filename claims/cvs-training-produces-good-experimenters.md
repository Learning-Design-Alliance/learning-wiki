# Children trained with explicit instruction and probes became good experimenters at transfer more often than untrained children (44% vs. 22% and 13%).

## Evidence

### Chen 1999

Zhe Chen and David Klahr. (1999). All Other Things Being Equal: Acquisition and Transfer of the Control of Variables Strategy. Child Development, 70(5), 1098-1120. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1132052

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Individual-level transfer analysis in the hands-on study, after excluding ceiling performers (10%, 10%, and 11% across conditions), found "44% (12/27) of the children in the Training-Probe condition, 22% (6/27) in the No Training-Probe condition, and 13% (3/24) in the No Training-No Probe condition were good experimenters."

> "Among the remaining 78 children, 44% (12/27) of the children in the Training-Probe condition, 22% (6/27) in the No Training-Probe condition, and 13% (3/24) in the No Training-No Probe condition were good experimenters."
