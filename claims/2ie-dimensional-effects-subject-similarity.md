`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Studies 2 and 3 tested the 2I/E model with similar subject pairs (math–science, German–English) alongside dissimilar pairs. Study 3 integrated math, German, and English into one model to statistically compare dimensional comparison effects between dissimilar and similar subjects. The article reports effects "were stronger between dissimilar subjects."

> "Dimensional comparison effects were stronger between dissimilar subjects, like math and German, than between similar subjects, like German and English (Study 2 and Study 3)."
