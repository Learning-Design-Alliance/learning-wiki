Evidence¶

Brinkman 2018¶

Brinkman, D. J., Tichelaar, J., Graaf, S., Otten, R. H. J., Richir, M. C., and van Agtmael, M. A. (2018). Do final‐year medical students have sufficient prescribing competencies? A systematic literature review. British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.13491

q2 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review of 25 articles describing prescribing competencies of final-year medical students, searched in PubMed, EMBASE and ERIC up to January 2017. The review reports "no clear consensus among CPT teachers" on required graduate prescribing competencies.

"Although there seems to be some agreement, we found no clear consensus among CPT teachers on which prescribing competencies medical students should have when they graduate."
