### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Micah K. Campbell, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2021). Impact of virtual REU experiences on students' psychosocial gains during the COVID-19 pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Career-decision outcomes from the final post-program interview in the longitudinal remote REU study; e.g. Ivan became more interested in nuclear physics and astronomy, and Freida felt more informed about research options.

> "During the last interview, a majority of mentees (N=8) said the REU experience helped them understand the nature of research work and aided them in figuring out their interests."

### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021 (2)

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Micah K. Campbell, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2021). Impact of virtual REU experiences on students' psychosocial gains during the COVID-19 pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reported outcome of the remote REU interviews: the article states these gains are consistent with multiple in-person studies finding gains in decisions around attending graduate school, but this study's data are self-reported interviews.

> "Most mentees (N=9) gained clearer ideas about their future career goals. Some of them reported that participating in this research program helped them to gain insight into applying to graduate school."
