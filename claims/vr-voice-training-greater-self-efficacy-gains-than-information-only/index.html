Evidence¶

Angélique Remacle 2023¶

Angélique Remacle, Stéphane Bouchard, Dominique Morsomme. (2023). Can teaching simulations in a virtual classroom help trainee teachers to develop oral communication skills and self-efficacy? A randomized controlled trial. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104808

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA in a two-arm randomized controlled trial of 41 trainee teachers (experimental n=21, control n=20) found a significant Time by Group interaction for self-efficacy (F = 7.45, p = .009, η²p = .160). Post hoc comparisons showed the experimental group "was significantly higher for the experimental group than the control group" after the intervention.

"Post-intervention, self-efficacy was significantly higher for the experimental group than the control group (T = –3.972, p = < .001), showing a better evolution for participants who received the direct intervention with VR immersions."
