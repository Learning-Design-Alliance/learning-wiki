## Evidence

### Rouis Sana 2025

Rouis Sana, Chouchene Imed, Gaddas Meriem, Naija Walid, Ben Saad Helmi, Lassoued Latifa. (2025). Residents' contribution to teaching at a Tunisian faculty of medicine. The Libyan Journal of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/19932820.2025.2597563

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Multivariate logistic regression on the survey data (n = 105) testing age, postgraduate year, prior formal teaching, enjoyment, skills, and future intention. The article reports "each additional year of residency was associated with a 3.72-fold increase in the odds of engaging in teaching activities"; all other predictors were non-significant.

> "each additional year of residency was associated with a 3.72-fold increase in the odds of engaging in teaching activities (OR = 3.72, 95% CI: 1.93−7.17, p < 0.001)"
