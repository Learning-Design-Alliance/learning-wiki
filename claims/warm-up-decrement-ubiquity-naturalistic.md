## Evidence

### Aakriti Kumar 2022

Aakriti Kumar, Aaron S. Benjamin, Andrew Heathcote and Mark Steyvers. (2022). Comparing models of learning and relearning in large-scale cognitive training data sets. npj Science of Learning 7:24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00142-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Predictive model comparison on held-out Lumosity data (Table 1): for Lost in Migration, M2 RMSE 3.56 vs M1 3.61 on all data; for Ebb and Flow, 4.54 vs 4.56. Neither simple model was competitive overall.

> "Note first that both the single-parameter learning model ( M1) and the traditional two-timescale model ( M2) are not competitive in accounting for learning in this data set. Yet M2 provides a substantial, across-the-board improvement over M1. This outcome reveals the ubiquity and substantial nature of warm-up decrements in naturalistic learning."
