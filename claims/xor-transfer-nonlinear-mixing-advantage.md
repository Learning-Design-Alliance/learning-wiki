## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Approximately nine months after training, the degree of non-linear mixing on the XOR task was significantly larger for the global-feedback group (NM β = 0.2332; NM×Experiment β = −0.1035, p = 0.0003), while XOR accuracy itself did not differ significantly between groups (p = 0.132).
