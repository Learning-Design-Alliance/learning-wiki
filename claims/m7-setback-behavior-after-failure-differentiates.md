## Evidence

### Chen 2020

Chen, Zhongzhou; Xu, Mengyu; Garrido, Geoffrey; Guthrie, Matthew W. (2020). Exploring the relation between students' online learning behavior and course performance by including contextual information in data analysis. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010138](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010138)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Level III parallel coordinate analysis showing all three cohorts' major paths between m8-m10 "originated from the same ASF-N state on m7". The article reports the top and middle cohorts continued learning while most of the bottom cohort gave up and guessed.

> "This observation suggests that failing on m7 is not a characteristic difference between high and low performing students, but their different choices after experiencing the setback on m7 is: while the top and most of the middle cohort continued with learning (with the middle cohort being less successful), most of the bottom cohort gave up and resorted to guessing on the following modules."
