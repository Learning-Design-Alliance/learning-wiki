## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2025

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Barton, H. D., Rowell, S. F., Chalker, D. L., & Fink, A. (2025). Students Don't Learn the Way They Think They Do in a Large, Active-Learning Genetics Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-10-0251

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Ordinal logistic mixed models on postsurvey performance-goal helpfulness ratings (sample size 454). "The odds of rating the worked example activities as more helpful was 3.34 times the odds of rating prediction activities as more helpful" (95% CI [2.52–4.42]); prediction exceeded tell-then-practice (OR = 2.97, CI [2.27–3.88]).

> "Model comparison to a null model showed that performance goal helpfulness ratings were significantly different among activity types (χ2 (2) = 251.53, p < 0.001, ΔAIC = −247.5). The odds of rating the worked example activities as more helpful was 3.34 times the odds of rating prediction activities as more helpful"
