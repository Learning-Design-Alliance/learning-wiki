## Evidence

### Zure Mert 2025

Zure Mert, Topaloğlu Mahir, İş Enes Efe, Sezikli Selim, Sarıkaya Deniz, Dıraçoğlu Demirhan. (2025). Advancing skills and confidence in ultrasound-guided injection techniques: the impact of a cadaveric workshop. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07270-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Between-group comparison of 109 workshop participants stratified by academic level, using the Mann-Whitney U Test. Table 4 prints per-comparison P-values (e.g., hip improvement P = 0.01; ankle improvement P = 0.0814; post-course hip P = 0.1044); no effect sizes are reported.

> "The comparison of pre-course and post-course confidence scores, as well as improvements in confidence, between specialists and residents revealed that residents had significantly lower confidence both before and after the course but showed significantly greater improvement overall, except in ankle improvement and post-hip confidence (Table 4)."
