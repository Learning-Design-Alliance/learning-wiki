# Post hoc analysis: trait self-control relates to grades positively only for trainees above very low fluid reasoning levels

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The simple slope of trait self-control on mGPA was significantly positive among individuals scoring higher than −1.5 SD below average on fluid reasoning; no such regions emerged for mathematics or physics. [→ Schmidt FTC 2020](#schmidt-ftc-2020)

## Evidence

### Schmidt FTC 2020

Schmidt FTC, Lindner C, Etzel JM and Retelsdorf J (2020) Self-Control Outdoes Fluid Reasoning in Explaining Vocational and Academic Performance—But Does It? Front. Psychol. 11:757. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00757

Post hoc Johnson–Neyman probing of the non-significant interaction (Figures 1–3); the analysis found the TSC–mGPA association "significantly positive among individuals scoring higher than −1.5 SD below average on the fluid reasoning measure", with no such regions for the tests.
