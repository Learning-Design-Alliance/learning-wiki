## Evidence

### Bassah Nahyeni 2016

Bassah Nahyeni, Cox Karen, Seymour Jane. (2016). A qualitative evaluation of the impact of a palliative care course on preregistration nursing students’ practice in Cameroon. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0106-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic findings from focus groups and individual critical incident interviews. Students self-evaluated their post-course practices as comparatively better, and cited specific interventions such as never saying "there is nothing we can do" as learned from the course. Support from some qualified nurses, related to activities of daily living, and collaborative family caregivers were also reported as facilitators.

> "Students reported that the competencies acquired during the palliative care course were significant to their palliative care practice. Some students explained how the course helped them to provide palliative care by reflecting on the deficiencies of their pre-course practices with patients with life-threatening/chronic conditions"
