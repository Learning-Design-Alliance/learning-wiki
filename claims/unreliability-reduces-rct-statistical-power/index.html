May 2009¶

May, Henry, Irma Perez-Johnson, Joshua Haimson, Samina Sattar, and Phil Gleason (2009). Using State Tests in Education Experiments: A Discussion of the Issues (NCEE 2009-013). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/using-state-tests-in-education-experiments-a-discussion-of-the-issues

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Analytical discussion in the reliability section of the paper, drawing on classical true-score theory and prior power research, illustrating how unreliability inflates the minimum detectable effect. The paper states the adjusted MDE becomes 0.22 when reliability is 0.80, showing "power of 71 percent" in that case.

"an experiment with 80 percent power to detect a 0.20 standardized mean difference in true scores is reduced to having power of 71 percent if the actual reliability of the outcome measure is 0.80"
