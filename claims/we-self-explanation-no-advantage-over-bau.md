## Evidence

### Miller‐Cotto 2026

Miller‐Cotto, D., & Medrano, J. (2026). Does working memory moderate the effect of fading on math performance? British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 108–126. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12781

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pooled linear regression (Model 2) on the randomized homework study with BAU as reference; the WE condition coefficient was β = .01, p = .96. The authors connect this to prior meta-analytic work suggesting self-explanation prompts may reduce worked-example benefits, possibly via a redundancy effect.

> "However, there were no significant differences between the BAU condition and the worked example or fading with self‐explanation conditions (ps > .05)."
