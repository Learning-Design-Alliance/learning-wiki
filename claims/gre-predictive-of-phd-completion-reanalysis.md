### Weissman 2023

Weissman, M. B. (2023). Comment on "Rubric-based holistic review represents a change from traditional graduate admissions approaches in physics". Department of Physics, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.028001](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.028001)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The comment reports, citing its references 11 and 12, that reanalyses of two physics PhD studies found these methodological errors. It also notes a single-program thesis with N=54 and an uncited study with N=32 lacked power, while an uncited N=138 study found correlations of 0.55 to 0.70 between GRE and quantitative-methods performance.

> "subsequent papers have shown that their analyses relied on myriad statistical errors, including a biased imputation method for missing data, collider stratification bias, improper treatment of range restriction, improper treatment of collinearity, improper use of null hypothesis significance testing on small subsets, and other errors."
