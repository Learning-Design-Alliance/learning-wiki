Editorial characterization of Harney, Hogan, Broome, Hall and Ryan's experimental manipulation of task-level and process-level prompts in an article in this issue. The editorial reports the manipulation alters group discourse practice, with the effect on individual argument style stated only as potential; no statistics are printed here.
