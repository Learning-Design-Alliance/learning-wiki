## Evidence

### Hammer A 2020

Hammer A, Gustafson LW, Christensen PN, et al. (2020). Implementation of p16/Ki67 dual stain cytology in a Danish routine screening laboratory: Importance of adequate training and experience. Cancer Med. https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.3399

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Concordance analysis of 50 randomly selected dual stain slides (45 from women aged 65-69 in primary HPV screening, 5 from women ≥45 referred to colposcopy) reviewed by two QUATE-certified cytotechnicians and an expert blinded to their results. The article reports "an overall agreement of 84.0%" and eight discordant slides (16.0%).

> "Concordance in dual stain results between cytotechnicians and the expert was good (κ = 0.68, 95% CI: 0.60‐0.76), with an overall agreement of 84.0% (95% CI: 70.9%‐92.8%) (Table 2)."
