Woolley, Helen Thompson. (1915). A new scale of mental and physical measurements for adolescents, and some of its uses. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(9), 521-550. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075644

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison of public and parochial school children within the fourteen- and fifteen-year working samples. The authors attribute the lower-end predominance to parochial schools being less strict about weeding out defectives, and the upper-end predominance to earlier dropout among parochial pupils.

> "the original tables of results showed that in every test except rote memory the public school children were somewhat superior. The tables of percentile averages add one interesting fact, which is that a large proportion of children at both extremes come from paro- chial schools."
