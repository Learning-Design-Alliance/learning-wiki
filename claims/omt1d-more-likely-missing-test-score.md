`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression on missing a test score among public-school attendees in the nationwide cohort. The article reports "aOR 1.30 [95% CI 1.16 to 1.47, p < 0.001]" for O-mT1D versus O-BP, and separately that O-mT1D versus O-fT1D differed at p = 0.003; no standardized effect size (d, r, η²) was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "More O-mT1D had at some point missed a test score than O-BP (aOR 1.30 [95% CI 1.16 to 1.47, p < 0.001]), but no difference was observed for O-fT1D versus O-BP (aOR 1.02 [95% CI 0.92 to 1.13, p = 0.80])."
