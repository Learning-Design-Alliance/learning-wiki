q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Single longitudinal simulation study (Ladyshewsky et al. 2000) comparing 12 undergrad physical therapy students with 4 practising physical therapists on a single-station OSCE. Printed effect sizes were 1.9 and 1.3, with "p = 0.009 and p = 0.03"; practising therapists "performed significantly better than physical therapy students".

"The effect size was 1.9 and 1.3 for the History and Clinical Examination Part and the Clinical Reasoning Part respectively. The p value was statistically significant for History & Clinical Examination and Clinical Reasoning (p = 0.009 and p = 0.03) respectively indicating that practicing physical therapists performed significantly better than physical therapy students."
