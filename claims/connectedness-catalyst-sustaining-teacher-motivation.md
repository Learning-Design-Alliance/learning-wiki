`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 37 interviews with Iranian medical sciences university teachers. The discussion identifies interactions and benevolent relationships with stakeholders as key drivers, consistent with the SDT need for relatedness; the conclusion calls connectedness a critical catalyst.

> "Connectedness emerged as a vital catalyst for enabling professional development, fostering autonomy in inquiry, and serving as a foundational platform for cultivating enjoyment in teaching."
