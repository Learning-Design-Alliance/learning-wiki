## Evidence

### Xu Liyuan 2025

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Diagnosis and differentiation assessment from the randomized controlled trial after 4 weeks of training. The VSP group showed "a significantly greater improvement in initial diagnosis scores" (Cohen d=0.55, P=.045).

> "the medical students in the experimental group showed a significantly greater improvement in initial diagnosis scores (mean 8.74, SD 1.67 vs mean 7.66, SD 2.08; F1, 59=4.21; Cohen d=0.55; 95% CI 7.70-8.70; P=.045) compared to the control group."

### Xu Liyuan 2025 (2)

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same trial's diagnosis and differentiation assessment: "no significant difference between the 2 groups in the improvement of differential diagnosis ability" (Cohen d=0.03, P=.91). Equivalence was not tested.

> "However, there was no significant difference between the 2 groups in the improvement of differential diagnosis ability (mean 2.37, SD 1.24 vs mean 2.33, SD 1.09; F1, 59=0.01; Cohen d=0.03; 95% CI 2.05-2.65; P=.91)."
