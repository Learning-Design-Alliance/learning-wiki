### Rogausch A 2015

Rogausch A, Beyeler C, Montagne S, Jucker-Kupper P, Berendonk C, Huwendiek S, Gemperli A, Himmel W. (2015). The influence of students' prior clinical skills and context characteristics on mini-CEX scores in clerkships – a multilevel analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0490-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariance parameter estimates from the multilevel models of mini-CEX ratings, reported in the Results section. The article states the trainers' random effect was "nearly as large as the covariance parameter estimate of the residual (0.47)". These are variance components, not effect sizes.

> "Trainers were the most influential random effect in the multilevel model, with a covariance parameter estimate of 0.37, compared to 0.05 of the random effect of students and even lower estimates of the two other random effects (i.e. specialties and clinics)."
