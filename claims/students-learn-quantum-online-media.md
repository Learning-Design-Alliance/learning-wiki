### Jessica L. Rosenberg 2024

Jessica L. Rosenberg, Nancy Holincheck, Michele Colandene. (2024). Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics Undergraduates’ Knowledge and Interest in Quantum Careers: Barriers and Opportunities to Building a Diverse Quantum Workforce. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010138](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010138)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Findings section on where students learned about quantum, drawing on survey and focus group data; most students surveyed had not learned about quantum careers in either formal or informal settings, and those who had mostly learned online.

> "For the minority who have some knowledge of quantum and the associated careers, most learned about the subject online, with a few learning about it in high school or college courses."

### Jessica L. Rosenberg 2024 (2)

Jessica L. Rosenberg, Nancy Holincheck, Michele Colandene. (2024). `q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group participant Jabar described a high school teacher presenting quantum as ungraded extra material; the article reports this left a lasting impact on the student's thinking.

> "I appreciated that my teacher in high school that actually took the time out to show something related to anything quantum… it wasn't even part of the curriculum or anything, it was purely just extra"
