`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

LIME feature-importance analysis of baseline predictors for 132 students. "Reduced (< 39.18 weeks) and increased gestational duration (>39.18 weeks) were positive contributors to all responder and non-responder groups respectively." Gestation and object recall were consistently top-ranked contributors across all subgroups.

> "this study found that longer gestation (> 39.18 weeks) predicted literacy non -responsiveness while comparatively shorter gestation of 35 to 39 weeks predicted literacy responsiveness."
