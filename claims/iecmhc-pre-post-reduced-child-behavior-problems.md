## Evidence

### Trigg 2026

Trigg, A. B., Keyes, A. W., Gray, S., Hatch, V., Brunet, K. T., & Heller, S. S. (2026). The Effects of Early Childhood Mental Health Consultation on Early Childhood Teachers' Perceptions of Children's Challenging Behaviors, Expulsion Risk, and the Moderating Role of Teaching Stress. Infant Mental Health Journal, 47, e70079. https://doi.org/10.1002/imhj.70079

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Program evaluation using multilevel linear modeling (three levels: child nested in individual, classroom, center; N=1241 children) with time coded pre=0, post=1 and 20 multiple imputations. The article reports "all outcomes were significantly different from pre‐ to post‐consultation" in the predicted directions; Table 4 shows SDQ-IS child problems fell from 7.31 to 6.32 (Est .99, p=.000).

> "Regarding main effects of time, covarying classroom size and age of classroom, all outcomes were significantly different from pre‐ to post‐consultation (see Table 4), with behaviors and protective factors changing in the directions predicted in hypotheses."
