Conceptual comparison in the Results section between 360-degree video, limited to rotational viewing from a fixed camera point, and native VR environments such as VolumetricOR, which let spectators 'physically move through the scene' by measuring the user's exact location.

> "While 360-degree video only allows users to look left or right and up and down from a fixed point, native virtual reality environments such as VolumetricOR, allow spectators to physically move through the scene due to the ability to measure the user's exact location within the scene."
