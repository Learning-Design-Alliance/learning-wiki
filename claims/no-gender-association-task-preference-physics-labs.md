## Evidence

### Bilas Paul 2024

Bilas Paul, Shantanu Chakraborty, and Ganga Sharma. (2024). Breaking Barriers: Investigating Gender Dynamics in Introductory Physics Lab Classes. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.15049](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.15049)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey-based chi-square analysis of self-identified gender against preferred lab activities (equipment handling, data collection, note-taking/calculations/visualization, report writing) at each institution. No effect size is printed; the article reports only test statistics.

> "The analysis revealed no significant gender associations with task preferences across all three institutions: SUNY Farmingdale State College (χ2 = 1.583, p= 0.2083), Fairmont State University (χ2 = 1.143, p= 0.2850), and Valdosta State University (χ2 = 1.655, p= 0.1983)"
