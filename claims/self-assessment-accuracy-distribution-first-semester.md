Knof Harald, Berndt Markus, Shiozawa Thomas. (2024). Prevalence of Dunning-Kruger effect in first semester medical students: a correlational study of self-assessment and actual academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11515314/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the self-assessment accuracy distribution in the same N = 426 sample of first-semester medical students surveyed before exam results were announced. The study reports "18.5% assessed themselves accurately, 35.5% overestimated, and 46.0% underestimated their performance". Mean deviation was -0.26 points (SD 2.59), indicating slight underestimation on average. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
