## Evidence

### Luthra 2025

Luthra, S., Luor, A., Tierney, A. T., Dick, F., & Holt, L. L. (2025). Statistical learning dynamically shapes auditory perception. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online tone-in-noise detection experiments (Exp 1b-f) sampled five frequencies with unimodal (75%) or bimodal (40.6% at edges) distributions; a significant Freq × Exp interaction (η² = 0.169) showed "better detection of high-probability frequencies at the distribution mode." Detection of a 100%-probable 1000 Hz tone (~77.9%) did not differ from modal detection in unimodal distributions (~75.3%).
