## Evidence

### Balideh Donya 2026

Balideh Donya, Farokhzadian Jamileh, Miri Sakineh, Shahraki Sedigheh Khodabandeh, Motamed‐Jahromi Mohadeseh. (2026). Effects of an Aesthetics‐Based Educational Intervention on Compassion Competence and Aesthetic Care Quality Among Psychiatric Nurses: A Quasi‐Experimental Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70854

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group independent t-test at post-intervention in the same 90-nurse quasi-experimental study. The intervention group reached "60.73 ± 16.17" versus 40.71 ± 4.54 in controls (t = 7.99, p < 0.001); Table 4 reports a between-group Cohen's d of 1.68, while the control group's within-group change was not significant (p = 0.12).

> "The overall compassion competence score also increased significantly in the intervention group, reaching 60.73 ± 16.17 compared with 40.71 ± 4.54 in the control group (t = 7.99, p < 0.001)."
