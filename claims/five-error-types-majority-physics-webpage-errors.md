### Scanlon 2019

Scanlon, E., Taylor, Z. W., & Chini, J. J. (2019). Physics Webpages Create Barriers to Participation for People with Disabilities: Five Steps to Increase Digital Accessibility. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1907.02906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1907.02906)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tenon audit results across the 44,241 errors on 139 webpages, summarized in Table IV by WCAG category. The abstract states these five types accounted for 95.5% of all errors; few errors appeared in the understandable category.

> "The data in Table IV suggest that five Level-A and Level-AA errors were responsible for the majority of web accessibility errors in this study: non-text content (1.1.1), information and relationships (1.3.1), resize text (1.4.4), link purpose in context (2.4.4), and name, role, and value (4.1.2)."
