### Mei Grace Behrendt 2024

Mei Grace Behrendt, Carrie Clark, McKenna Elliott & Joseph Dauer. (2024). Relation of life sciences students' metacognitive monitoring to neural activity during biology error detection. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00231-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral results from the fMRI model evaluation task in 50 undergraduates; students averaged 65% accuracy and were confident ~75% of the time. The article reports d = 1.09 for the accurate-vs-inaccurate confidence contrast, described as a "significant difference".
