## Evidence

### Murata 2023

Murata, H., Asakawa, S., Kawamura, T., Yamauchi, H., Takahashi, O., & Henker, R. (2023). Efficacy of modified team‐based learning in a flipped classroom for an acute‐care nursing course: A mixed‐methods study. Nursing Open, 10, 4786–4796. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1730

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed model analysis of Team Approach Assessment Scale scores from 73 students in 19 groups at four time-points, with group and time-point as fixed effects and participant as random effect. The article reports a significant time effect: "the scores increased significantly across time‐points (F[3,94.199] = 8.848, p < 0.001)". Pre-test mean was 47.625 (SE 1.697); post-test 3 mean 55.340 (SE 1.398).

> "However, the scores increased significantly across time‐points (F[3,94.199] = 8.848, p < 0.001). This increase was particularly conspicuous at post‐test 2 (after the second case study) and post‐test 3"
