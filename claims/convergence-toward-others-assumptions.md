# Learners converge toward an unknown other's assumptions after seeing the other's answers and confidence, reducing the distance between their own and the partner's answers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` After viewing partner information, the absolute distance between own and partner answers decreased significantly (d = 0.78). [→ Schnaubert 2021](#schnaubert-2021)

## Evidence

### Schnaubert 2021

Schnaubert, L., Krukowski, S., & Bodemer, D. (2021). Assumptions and confidence of others: the impact of socio-cognitive information on metacognitive self-regulation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09269-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject experiment (N = 60) in which participants re-judged statements after seeing a computer-generated partner's answers. Distance between own and partner answers fell from 3.02 to 2.62 pre to post adjustment; the paired t-test showed "statistically significant (t(59) = 6.15, p < 0.001, d = 0.78)".

> "There was a high correlation between the distances pre and post adjustment ( r = 0.569, p < 0.001) and a paired t-test revealed that the difference between the values was statistically significant (t(59) = 6.15, p < 0.001, d = 0.78)."
