## Evidence

### Stahl Aimee E. 2019

Stahl Aimee E., Feigenson Lisa. (2019). Violations of Core Knowledge Shape Early Learning. Topics in Cognitive Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/tops.12389

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Unpublished work (Stahl & Feigenson, in preparation a) in which 11-month-olds saw a striped ball pass through a wall and then chose between a new same-category ball and a car. The authors describe these results as preliminary.

> "Infants who saw the surprising outcome showed no significant preference for the new ball over the car, and their preferences did not differ from those of infants who saw the ball behave in an expected way"
