The primer asserts, citing Järvelä and Hadwin (2013), that "All types of regulation can be taught" and that students can internalize social regulation of learning with time, effort, and effective feedback. The primer offers no data of its own for this assertion.

"All types of regulation can be taught. With time, effort, and effective feedback, students can internalize social regulation of learning and deploy it when needed."
