## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Linear hypothesis testing of additivity on EF outcomes in the meta-analysis. The printed under-additivity effect size gAdd = 0.22 (p < 0.01) indicates RDMD children were "less impaired in inhibition, shifting, and updating than expected in the case of additivity".

> "in inhibition, shifting, and updating, profiles of children with comorbid RD and MD were clearly under-additive (gAdd = 0.22, p < 0.01), implying that children with RDMD were less impaired in inhibition, shifting, and updating than expected in the case of additivity"

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023 (2)

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Overall linear hypothesis tests across the three outcome domains. The additivity result for executive functions was marginal (p = 0.06), motivating the outcome-specific analyses showing under-additivity.

> "Overall results (Table 7) provided evidence for an additive effect for the cognitive profile of RDMD in math, reading, and executive function skills, respectively. However, this result seemed to be less clear-cut for EF (p = 0.06) than for math or reading."
