### Karoui Haykel 2025

Karoui Haykel, Rwandarwacu Victor P., Niyonzima Jonathan, Makuza Antoinette, Nkuranga John B., D'Acremont Valérie, Kulinkina Alexandra V. (2025). Identifying clinical skill gaps of healthcare workers using a digital clinical decision support algorithm during outpatient pediatric consultations in primary health centers in Rwanda. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0318284

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective observational study of 188 consultations in 10 health centers; skip rates per sign were documented by the observing study clinician. The study found "respiratory rate, heart rate, and blood oxygen saturation were the most frequently skipped". The most common stated reason for skipping was that the sign was not deemed necessary based on the child's symptoms (e.g., 67% for heart rate, 60% for respiratory rate). No effect size is printed.

> "respiratory rate, heart rate, and blood oxygen saturation were the most frequently skipped (43%, 37%, and 33%, respectively)."
