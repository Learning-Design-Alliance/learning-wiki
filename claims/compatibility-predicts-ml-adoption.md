SEM analysis of the pooled dataset of 588 students; the COMP paths to BI and UB were both significant as quoted. Country-level results showed COMP→BI significant in Morocco and Libya but not Tunisia.

> "COMP influenced both BI and UB, with path coeffi- cients of 0.286 (SD = 0.046) and 0.300 (SD = 0.046), respectively, indicating statistical sig- nificance (p < 0.001)."
