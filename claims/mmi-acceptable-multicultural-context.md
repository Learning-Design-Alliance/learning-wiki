### Allan Pau 2016

Allan Pau, Chen Yu Sui, Lee Verna Kar Mun, Sow Chew Fei, Alwis Ranjit De. (2016). What does the multiple mini interview have to offer over the panel interview? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29874

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous 5-point Likert acceptability questionnaire completed after the MMI (n=173 respondents per Table 3), with t-tests comparing mean item scores by sex and race. Almost all strongly agreed (59.1%) or agreed (30.1%) that interviewers were polite and respectful.

> "The mean scores for each of the acceptability items were compared between sex and race categories, but no statistically significant difference in scores were detected"
