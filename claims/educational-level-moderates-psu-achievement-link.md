## Evidence

### Paterna 2024

Paterna, Adrian; Alcaraz-Ibáñez, Manuel; Aguilar-Parra, Jose M.; Salavera, Carlos; Demetrovics, Zsolt; Griffiths, Mark D. (2024). Problematic smartphone use and academic achievement: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Behavioral Addictions. https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2024.00014

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Univariate mixed-effects meta-regression across the 33 effect sizes testing categorical moderators. Table 2 prints the elementary/middle school intercept as r = −0.214 versus r = −0.092 for tertiary (college), the reference category; the article calls the former relationship medium-sized in the Discussion.

> "Findings from the univariate meta-regression analysis for categorical variables (see Table 2) indicated that educational level was the only significant moderator of the relationship under consideration (omnibus-test [3, 29] = 3.327; p = 0.033; R2 = 21.80), this being stronger among samples from elementary/middle school students."
