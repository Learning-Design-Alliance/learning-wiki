Evidence¶

Aynur Ismayilli Karakoc 2019¶

Aynur Ismayilli Karakoc. (2019). Reading and Listening Comprehension Subskills: The Match between Theory, Coursebooks, and Language Proficiency Tests. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.10n.4p.166

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Document-analysis study compiling subskills from applied linguistics literature, nine international EFL/ESL proficiency test manuals, and tasks from five widely used coursebook series. The article reports that "The findings suggested ten common reading and listening subskills" with seven reading-exclusive and four listening-exclusive subskills.

"The findings suggested ten common reading and listening subskills. The subskills exclusive to reading were seven, and exclusive to listening were four."
