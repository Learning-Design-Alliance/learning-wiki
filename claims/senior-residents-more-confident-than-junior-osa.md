## Evidence

### Ansari Saad 2020

Ansari Saad, Hu Amanda. (2020). Knowledge and confidence in managing obstructive sleep apnea patients in Canadian otolaryngology - head and neck surgery residents: a cross sectional survey. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-020-00417-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon Rank Sum / unpaired t-test comparisons between junior (PGY1–3, n=36) and senior (PGY4–5, n=30) respondents in the resident survey; the article reports the three pairwise confidence contrasts with p-values. No effect size printed.

> "The proportion of Seniors who answered either “agree” or “strongly agree” was significantly higher than Juniors in identifying OSA (96.7% vs 69.4%; p < 0.005), managing OSA (60.0% vs. 33.3%; p = 0.03) and managing CPAP (26.7% vs. 5.6%; p = 0.01) confidence questions."
