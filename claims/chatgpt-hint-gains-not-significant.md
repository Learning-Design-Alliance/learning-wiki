# Within-condition pre-to-post differences were statistically significant in the manual-hint conditions, but not in the ChatGPT conditions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-to-post scores rose in all four conditions, but pre-to-post differences were statistically significant only within the control (manual hints) conditions (p = 0.0219 Elementary, p = 0.0213 Intermediate) and not within the ChatGPT conditions (p = 0.1427 Elementary, p = 0.7912 Intermediate). [→ Zachary A. Pardos 2023](#zachary-a-pardos-2023)

## Evidence

### Zachary A. Pardos 2023

Zachary A. Pardos, Shreya Bhandari. (2023). Learning gain differences between ChatGPT and human tutor generated algebra hints. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06871](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06871)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-condition pre-to-post comparisons in the same 77-participant randomized experiment, tested with Mann Whitney U after Shapiro-Wilk rejected normality. The article reports positive gains in all conditions with the printed p-values; no effect size is given.

> "all experiments showed positive learning gains; however, they were statistically significant differences only between pre and post-test scores of the control condition (p = 0.0219 for Elementary Algebra and p = 0.0213 for Intermediate Algebra), but not the experiment condition (p = 0.1427 for Elementary Algebra and p = 0.7912 for Intermediate Algebra)."
