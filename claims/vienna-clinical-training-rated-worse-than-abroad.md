## Evidence

### Seitz T. 2017

Seitz T., Turk B. R., Löffler-Stastka H. (2017). Can we still stop the migration of physicians from Austria? An evaluation of clinical internships by students of the Medical University of Vienna. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-016-1095-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 304 students (10.1% response rate) rating clinical training by location; Table 3 shows Vienna rated C− (3.1) versus Europe excluding Austria B− (2.3). The worst subcategories "in Austria (including Vienna) were 'Integration into the clinical team' and 'Supervision by medical professionals.'"

> "Excluding these, the best-rated subcategories for all locations (Table 3) were “Improvement of clinical knowledge,” and the worst in Austria (including Vienna) were “Integration into the clinical team” and “Supervision by medical professionals.”"
