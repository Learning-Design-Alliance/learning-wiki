### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, Michael C. Wittmann, Eleanor C. Sayre, Brian W. Frank. (2009). Students’ Responses To Different Representations Of A Vector Addition Question. 2009 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266745

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' observational note from prior interview studies, offered in the discussion as a possible explanation for the absent grid effect: the tendency to keep using the same method "may be nullifying any effects of the grid in our data."

> "In interviews involving a series of similar vector addition questions [6], we have noted that students often choose one solution method and then use that method on all different representations of the vector addition questions during the interview."
