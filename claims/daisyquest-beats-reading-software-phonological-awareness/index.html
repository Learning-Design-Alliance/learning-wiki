DaisyQuest 2006¶

DaisyQuest. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: DaisyQuest. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized comparison of 49 at-risk first graders assigned to DaisyQuest, Hint and Hunt, or math software. WWC analysis found "two of ﬁve positive effects for phonological aware- ness" statistically significant; the Phoneme Elision Test effect was substantively important though not significant.

"The WWC analysis found that two of ﬁve positive effects for phonological aware- ness (Undersea Challenge and Production Test of Segmenting) were statistically signiﬁcant."
