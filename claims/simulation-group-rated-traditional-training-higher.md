## Evidence

### O’Sullivan O 2014

O’Sullivan O, Iohom G, O’Donnell BD, Shorten GD. (2014). The effect of simulation-based training on initial performance of ultrasound-guided axillary brachial plexus blockade in a clinical setting – a pilot study. BMC Anesthesiology 14:110. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2253-14-110

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standard written feedback forms on the traditional training content and delivery, compared via Mann–Whitney U-tests (Table 5); significant items included quality of slides and potential to learn (p = 0.005) and cadaveric delivery of information (p = 0.040), with "the magnitude of the differences tended to be low".

> "Trainees in the SG rated elements of traditional training higher than CG participants. However, the magnitude of the differences tended to be low."
