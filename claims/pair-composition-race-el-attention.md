## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pairs where both students are non-Black receive 5.9 p.p. less attention, and pairs where both are non-EL receive 5.7 p.p. less attention, compared to students in mixed pairings. [→ Qingyang Zhang 2025](#qingyang-zhang-2025)

## Evidence

### Qingyang Zhang 2025

Qingyang Zhang, Rose E. Wang, Ana T. Ribeiro, Dora Demszky, Susanna Loeb. (2025). Educator Attention: How computational tools can systematically identify the distribution of a key resource for students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 2's regression on pair composition finds "pairs of non-Black students receive 5.9 p.p. less attention" and non-EL pairs 5.7 p.p. less, both p <0.001, compared to mixed pairings. The reduction is primarily driven by a decrease in content-focused attention.

> "pairs of non-Black students receive 5.9 p.p. less attention [ β = −0.059(−0.082, −0.037), p <0.001], and pairs of non-EL students receive 5.7 p.p. less attention [ β = −0.057(−0.081, −0.034), p <0.001]"
