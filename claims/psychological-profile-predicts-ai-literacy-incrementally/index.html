Chow Tak Sang 2026¶

Chow Tak Sang, To Ken, Lam Bess Yin Hung, Lau Kung Wong. (2026). A Multidimensional Analysis of AI Literacy Determinants: External Resources, Digital Skills, and Psychological Profile Among University Students. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081448

Final step of the hierarchical regression added personal innovativeness, technology anxiety, and growth mindset in technology. The article reports "The R-square value of model 4 is 0.39 in contrast with 0.32 in model 3" and states all three psychological variables significantly predicted AI literacy; multicollinearity checks showed no concern.
