## Evidence

### Güdük Ömer Faruk 2025

Güdük Ömer Faruk, Berberoğlu İpek Nur, Akturan Selçuk. (2025). A single-group pre–post OSCE evaluation of a simulation-based clinical readiness module in pediatric dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08215-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre–post study of 30 third-year dental students (28 post-test) completing a five-station OSCE before and after the module; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed "Five outcomes showed statistically significant differences between pre- and post-test scores (p < 0.001)" for Medical History Taking, Child–Parent Communication, Team Dynamics, Fissure Sealant Application, and Fluoride Application. Table 2 separately reports effect sizes r from −0.750 to −0.875 for these outcomes.

> "Median scores increased across all six assessed outcomes. Five outcomes showed statistically significant differences between pre- and post-test scores (p < 0.001), while Oral Hygiene Education showed a smaller but still significant difference (p = 0.041)."
