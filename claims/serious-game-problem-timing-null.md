## Evidence

### Graafland Maurits 2017

Graafland Maurits, Bemelman Willem A., Schijven Marlies P. (2017). Game-based training improves the surgeon's situational awareness in the operation room: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5456-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome measure (time) in the randomized trial, calculated independently from the primary measures by a blinded assessor. Recognition time medians were 66 s versus 80 s, p = 0.24; solving time 75 vs. 85 s, p = 0.14; no effect size printed.

> "The game-enhanced curriculum group recognized problems in a median of 66 s (IQR 52–85 s) vs. 80 s (68–86 s) in the regular curriculum group, p = 0.24."
