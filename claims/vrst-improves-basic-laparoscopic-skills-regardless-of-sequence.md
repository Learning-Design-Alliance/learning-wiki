### Sparn Moritz B. 2024

Virtual reality simulation training in laparoscopic surgery – does it really matter, what simulator to use? Results of a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 24, 589. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05574-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 18 participants at the 38th annual Davos Course 2021, randomized into three groups rotating across three simulators. The article reports "Median path length was initially 830 cm and 463 cm on the exit examination (p < 0.001)"; only test statistics are printed, no effect size.
