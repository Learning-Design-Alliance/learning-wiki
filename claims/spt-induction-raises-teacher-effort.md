# The social perspective taking induction increased treatment teachers' effort to understand their focal students (Cohen's d = 0.51)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Treatment teachers put more effort into taking their focal student's perspective than control teachers (Cohen's d = 0.51, 95% CI 0.09-0.92). [→ Gehlbach 2021](#gehlbach-2021)

## Evidence

### Gehlbach 2021

Gehlbach, H., Mascio, B., & McIntyre, J. (2021). Social perspective taking: A professional development induction to improve teacher-student relationships and student learning. Journal of Educational Psychology (in press). https://osf.io/suhvj/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered field experiment regression on estimated factor scores with wild-bootstrap standard errors, from the results robustness table. The preregistered estimate for teacher SPT effort was Cohen's d = 0.51, "0.51 (0.09, 0.92)", and the effect remained similar across OLS, unit-weighted, FIML, and multilevel reanalyses.
