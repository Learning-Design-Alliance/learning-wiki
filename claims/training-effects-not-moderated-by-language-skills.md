Repeated measures ANOVA for the flexibility test (N = 115) found the three-way interaction non-significant; the parallel word problem analysis (Table 5) likewise reported Measurement x treatment x language with p = 0.830. No effect size is printed for these null interactions.

> "The other interaction effects, including the three-way interaction of language group, measurement time, and treatment group, were not significant, indicating that the treatment effect on students' flexibility development was not statistically different for students with low vs. high language skills (RQ2a)."
