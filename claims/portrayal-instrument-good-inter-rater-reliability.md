## Evidence

### Pritchard Shane A. 2020

Pritchard Shane A., Keating Jennifer L., Nestel Debra, Blackstock Felicity C. (2020). Physiotherapy students can be educated to portray realistic patient roles in simulation: a pragmatic observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02382-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Inter-rater reliability analysis using intraclass correlation coefficients on blinded ratings of the 23 pre/post video pairs. For total scores, ICC was "0.82 (95%CI [0.62, 0.92])" pre-intervention and "0.66 (95%CI [0.34, 0.84]" post-intervention.

> "For the complete data set (n = 23), ICC agreement ranged from 0.62 to 0.82 for the total and two sub-group scores for assessor pair 1, indicating a good level of reliability in the use of the instrument."
