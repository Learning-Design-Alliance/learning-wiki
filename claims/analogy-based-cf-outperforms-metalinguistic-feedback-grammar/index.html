Evidence¶

Masjedi 2019¶

Masjedi, H., & Tabatabaei, S. P. (2019). Analogy-based CF or Metalinguistic Feedback: Which One Is Better in EFL Classrooms? International Journal of Instruction, 12(3), 513-528. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12331a

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post hoc multiple comparisons following a significant ANOVA on posttest grammar scores of 45 young EFL learners in three groups; the printed mean difference between analogy-based and metalinguistic groups was "1.200*" with Sig. .016, significant at the 0.05 level.

"Moreover the mean difference between the metalinguistic and analogy -based was significant (P = 0.016≤.05). It revealed that analogy -based group outperformed the other groups."
