Pencak Martin, Stranak Zbynek, Dite Jakub, Vranova Jana, Studeny Pavel, Veith Miroslav. (2025). 25-gauge vitrectomy with gas tamponade for uncomplicated rhegmatogenous retinal detachment: experienced vs. inexperienced surgeons. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19795

Retrospective comparative consecutive case series at a single Prague centre; Table 3 reports the primary endpoint, single surgery retinal reattachment rate, as "100 (94.3%)" in the ESG versus "103 (93.6%)" in the ISG with P = 0.828. Final success rate was 100% in both groups.
