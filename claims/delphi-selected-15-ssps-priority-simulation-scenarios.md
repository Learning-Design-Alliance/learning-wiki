### Thiebaud Pierre-Clément 2024

Thiebaud Pierre-Clément, Philippon Anne-Laure, Plaisance Patrick, Chauvin Anthony, Houze-Cerfon Charles-Henri, Truchot Jennifer, and the CNUMU-SIMU Group. (2024). Designing simulation-based curriculum content for emergency medicine residents in France: a Delphi method. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05901-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The Delphi study rated 103 initial situations as starting points (SSPs) plus 6 added by expert comments; Table 4 lists the 15 retained SSPs, with severe trauma rated highest at 3.87 (0.62) in round 1. SSPs were defined as "generic situations which cover the common circumstances, symptoms, complaints and findings."

> "SSPs are generic situations which cover the common circumstances, symptoms, complaints and findings that the physician should be able to manage on day one of residency [21]. SSPs are an important element of simulation scenarios’ design."
