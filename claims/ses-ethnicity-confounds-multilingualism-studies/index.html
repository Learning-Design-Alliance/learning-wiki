Evidence¶

Perry 2026¶

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Correlation matrix (Table 1) of multilingual status, SES, ethnicity, cognitive skills and attainment in 1,673 SCAMP adolescents, confirming "the possibility of confounds in studies of multilingualism". Supplementary analyses showed ethnicity × language status interactions on SES and age 14 cognition.

"It confirms the possibility of confounds in studies of multilingualism: both SES and ethnicity were correlated with multilingualism (as well as with each other), and both SES and ethnicity showed associations with educational attainment and with the cognitive factor."

Perry 2026 (2)¶

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Methods analysis of SES factor scores by language group in 1,673 pupils: monolinguals came from more advantaged families (M = .23, SD = .70) while "both multilingual groups came from less advantaged families than average for this subsample" (SM M = -.25; ML M = -.23).

"Language groups significantly differed on average SES factor scores, F(1, 1671) = 163.40, p <.001."
