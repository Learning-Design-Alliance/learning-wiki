## Evidence

### Singaravelu 2011

Singaravelu, G. (2011). Effectiveness of e-TLM in Learning Vocabulary in English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071157

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Parallel-group experimental study of 30 standard VI pupils using a researcher-made 50-item vocabulary achievement test (split-half reliability 0.82). The t test comparing experimental pretest and posttest means (52.43 vs 86.78) yielded t = 22.87, so "null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels".

> "In the Table 2,the calculated 't' value is (22.87) which is greater than table value (2.00). Hence null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels."
