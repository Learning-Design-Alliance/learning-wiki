`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review describes the Linn et al. (2006) program-based study with more than 8000 students comparing two cohorts across six science courses, where the WISE-based inquiry curriculum cohort "showed higher science achievement overall" with significance in four of six courses.

> "Students in the inquiry-based curriculum showed higher science achievement overall than students in the typical curriculum; this difference was statistically significant in four of the six courses."

The review describes the Schuster et al. (2018) randomized controlled study of 8-day physics lessons on dynamics and light, delivered over 5 years to more than 100 participants per year, in which "No significant differences between conditions were found."

> "No significant differences between conditions were found, and the authors stated in their abstract, "Findings suggest that teachers need not be bound to one mode throughout and can flexibly decide on the pedagogical approach for each concept and situation"
