# Past parental activity (APAF3) shows non-monotonic associations: medium support groups were least bored and had the best grades

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` APAF3 was significantly correlated with boredom (p = 0.003), academic performance (p = 0.003) and alcohol consumption (p = 0.002), with medium-support adolescents least bored and highest-achieving. [→ Moral-García J.E. 2020](#moral-garcia-je-2020)

## Evidence

### Moral-García J.E. 2020

Moral-García J.E., Urchaga-Litago J.D., Ramos-Morcillo A.J., Maneiro R. (2020). Relationship of Parental Support on Healthy Habits, School Motivations and Academic Performance in Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17030882

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of APAF3 (past parental activity) support levels in the 1100-adolescent cross-sectional study. The pattern is non-monotonic: "being those who were less bored the adolescents receiving a medium support (M = 2.00)", and the highest support group had the worst printed grades (M = 6.80).

> "The IPAF3 factor, corresponding to the past parental activity, was significantly correlated to the variables of boredom (p = 0.003), detecting that the adolescents who were most bored were those who feel a lower support (M = 2.14) and a higher support (M = 2.18), being those who were less bored the adolescents receiving a medium support (M = 2.00); of academic performance (p = 0.003), having the best grades those with medium support (M = 7.12) and having the worst grades those with a higher support (M = 6.80); of alcohol consumption (p = 0.002)"
