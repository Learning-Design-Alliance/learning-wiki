`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing self-reflection rubric scores between intervention and comparison groups. The intervention group's mean was 4.9 (SD = .29) versus 4.3 (SD = .98) for the comparison group on this item. The article reports "a significant difference on one item" with F = 4.29, p < .05.
