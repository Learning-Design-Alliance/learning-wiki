## Evidence

### Hecht 2023

Hecht, C. A., Murphy, M. C., Dweck, C. S., Bryan, C. J., Trzesniewski, K. H., Medrano, F. N., Giani, M., Mhatre, P., & Yeager, D. S. (2023). Shifting the mindset culture to address global educational disparities. npj Science of Learning 8:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00181-y

`q4 · i?` · `review · r2`

A multi-arm randomized controlled trial assigned 897 dual-enrollment high-school teachers (32,172 students) to the values-aligned intervention, an unrelated-topic control, or four non-values-aligned arms. The intervention "increased pass rates by four percentage points overall" and more in lower-SES classrooms.

> "the single-session intervention increased pass rates by four percentage points overall, and by 6 to 11 percentage points in lower-SES classrooms (depending on the model)."
