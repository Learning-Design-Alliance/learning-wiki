## Evidence

### Jingwen Bai 2026

Jingwen Bai, Wei Soon Cheong, Philippe Muller, and Brian Y Lim. 2026. iRULER: Intelligible Rubric-Based User-Defined LLM Evaluation for Revision. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790539

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Per-trial 7-point Likert ratings in the writing revision experiment (N=48); a significant main effect of Feedback Type on perceived helpfulness (p < .0001) was observed.

> "iRULER feedback (M = 2.3) was rated as significantly more helpful than both Rubric-LLM (M = 1.7,p = .0016) and Text-LLM feedback (M=1.5,p<.0001)."

### Jingwen Bai 2026 (2)

Jingwen Bai, Wei Soon Cheong, Philippe Muller, and Brian Y Lim. 2026. iRULER: Intelligible Rubric-Based User-Defined LLM Evaluation for Revision. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790539

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Perceived correctness ratings in the same experiment; the main effect was significant (p < .0001), but the iRULER–Rubric-LLM contrast was not. Sense of control showed no significant main effect despite iRULER's highest rating.

> "iRULER feedback (M = 2.2) was rated as significantly more correct than Text-LLM feedback (M = 1.5,p < .0001) but not Rubric-LLM feedback (M=1.7,p=n.s.)."
