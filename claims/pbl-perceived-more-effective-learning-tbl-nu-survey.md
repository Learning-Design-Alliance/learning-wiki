## Evidence

### Almagribi Ahmad Zaker 2024

Almagribi Ahmad Zaker, Al-Qahtani Saad Misfer, Assiri Abdullah M., Mehdar Khlood Mohammed. (2024). Perceptions of medical students at Najran University on the effectiveness of problem-based learning and team-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06148-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kirkpatrick Level 2 (learning) responses from the same cross-sectional survey of 106 preclinical students. The printed percentages cover three outcome measures: "50.0% of participants viewed PBL as more effective for knowledge acquisition, while 11.3% preferred TBL", 62.3% for teamwork versus 13.2%, and 55.7% higher marks in PBL versus 7.5%. No effect sizes or significance tests are printed.

> "The results revealed that 50.0% of participants viewed PBL as more effective for knowledge acquisition, while 11.3% preferred TBL. The majority (62.3%) identified PBL as the best educational approach for teamwork, while 13.2% favored TBL."
