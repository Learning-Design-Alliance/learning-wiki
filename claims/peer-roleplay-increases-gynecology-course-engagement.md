### Lv Pingping 2026

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative cohort study of course participation in gynecology teaching: the experimental 2020 cohort (n=81) versus the 2019 control cohort (n=72), analyzed with chi-square tests. The article reports "a 22.5% increase in active participation rate (64.2% vs. 41.7%, χ²=8.28, p < 0.01, Cramer's V = 0.23)", a concentration-rate contrast with χ²=9.44, and a post-hoc power (1-β) of 80.2% for n = 153.
