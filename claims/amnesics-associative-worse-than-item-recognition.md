### Yonelinas 2002 (2)

Andrew P. Yonelinas. (2002). The Nature of Recollection and Familiarity: A Review of 30 Years of Research. Journal of Memory and Language. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.2002.2864

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that ROC modeling of amnesic recognition data indicates "only one process (i.e., familiarity) is needed", and that ERP studies find the recollection correlate absent while the familiarity correlate often remains.

> "ROC studies in amnesics have indi- cated that only one process (i.e., familiarity) is needed to account for their recognition performance"
