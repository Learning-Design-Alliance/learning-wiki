# Most students in a redesigned project-based physics lab reported satisfaction with their team's project achievements

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bei Cai 2019

Bei Cai, Lindsay Mainhood, and Robert G. Knobel. (2019). Using reflections to explore student learning during the project component of an advanced laboratory course. arXiv. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.cai](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.cai)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic coding (open, axial, selective) of 55 student reflection essays from a Fall 2017 redesigned lab course; the study reports that 30 students "explicitly wrote that they found their project interesting" or were happy with team achievements. No effect size is printed.

> "Out of the 55 responses we received and analyzed, 30 students explicitly wrote that they found their project interesting, regarded their project as a success, and/or were happy with what their team achieved."
