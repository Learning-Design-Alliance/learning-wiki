`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility evaluation of the ACP-SCT pilot with twelve hemodialysis nurses recruited by convenience sampling at a Taiwanese medical center. The article reports "100% participant recruitment rate" and full consent and retention across the three-week workshop.

> "The results were as follows: (a) 100% participant recruitment rate, (b) 100% of interested participants were willing to provide consent, and (c) there was a 100% retention rate of participants who attended all sessions."
