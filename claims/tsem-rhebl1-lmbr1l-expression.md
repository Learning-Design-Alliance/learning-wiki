### Shiying Wang 2026

Shiying Wang, Joan Bosson-Heenan, Andrew Grotzinger, Jan C. Frijters & Jeffrey R. Gruen. (2026). Shared genetic architecture of reading and attention disorders using genomic structural equation modeling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00433-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

T-SEM analysis using FUSION weights from 13 GTEx v8 brain tissues across 51,223 tissue-specific gene expression estimates. The article reports associations at "a Bonferroni-corrected threshold of p < 9.76 × 10⁻⁷" involving RHEBL1 and LMBR1L, both within the GWAS locus. No effect size is printed.

> "T-SEM identified five significant associations (Table 3) at a Bonferroni-corrected threshold of p < 9.76 × 10⁻⁷. These associations involved two unique genes: RHEBL1 and LMBR1L (MIM: 610007). Both genes are within the locus 12q13.12 identified in the GWAS predicated on Cg (Figure 3B), consistent with the GWAS results."
