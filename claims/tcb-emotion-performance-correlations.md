## Evidence

### Shao Y 2025

Shao Y, Zhu H, Kong W, Li W, Zhang C. (2025). How teacher caring behavior affects high school students’ English academic performance: the mediating role of academic emotions. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000042751

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and correlation analysis of 702 high school students. All correlations were significant at P < .01: TCB with EAP (r = 0.221), enjoyment (r = 0.204), anxiety (r = −0.188), and boredom (r = −0.113).

> "TCB was significantly and positively correlated with EAP (R = 0.221, P < .01), EJ (R = 0.204, P < .01), and negatively correlated with AX (r = −0.188, P < .01) and BR (r = −0.113, P < .01)"
