# The cue-diagnosticity intervention steered cue utilization: diagnostic cue use increased and non-diagnostic cue use decreased

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-reported use of correct boxes (B = 0.25, p = 0.003, R2 = 0.057) and correct relations (B = 0.19, p = 0.004, R2 = 0.040) increased significantly after the intervention. [→ Janneke van de Pol 2024](#janneke-van-de-pol-2024)
`q3 i?` Self-reported use of Dutch grade (B = -1.00, p < 0.001, R2 = 0.412) and interest in the text topic (B = -1.16, p < 0.001, R2 = 0.398) decreased significantly after the intervention. [→ Janneke van de Pol 2024 (2)](#janneke-van-de-pol-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Janneke van de Pol 2024

Janneke van de Pol, Eleanor Rowan, Eva Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2024). Effects of availability of diagnostic and non-diagnostic cues on the accuracy of teachers' judgments of students' text comprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09383-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel comparison of self-reported cue utilization before and after the intervention in the same experiment; "Teachers reported using students' correct boxes significantly more" and correct relations more, with the printed R2 values labelled small effects by the article.

> "Teachers reported using students' correct boxes significantly more after the intervention than before (B = 0.25, SE = 0.08, p = 0.003; R2 = 0.057 [small effect]). Teachers also reported using correct relations significantly more after the intervention than before (B = 0.19, SE = 0.07, p = 0.004; R2 = 0.040 [small effect])"

### Janneke van de Pol 2024 (2)

Janneke van de Pol, Eleanor Rowan, Eva Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2024). Effects of availability of diagnostic and non-diagnostic cues on the accuracy of teachers' judgments of students' text comprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09383-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same multilevel comparison; "Teachers reported using students' Dutch grade as a cue significantly less" and interest less, with the printed R2 values labelled medium effects by the article. Cue utilization was self-reported on a 0-3 scale.

> "Teachers reported using students' Dutch grade as a cue significantly less after the intervention than before (B = -1.00, SE = 0.14, p < 0.001; R2 = 0.412 [medium effect]). Teachers also reported using students' interest in the text topic significantly less after the intervention than before (B = -1.16, SE = 0.17, p < 0.001; R2 = 0.398 [medium effect])"
