Understanding factors related to Chilean students' digital skills: A mixed methods analysis. Computers & Education, 88, 387-398. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.07.016

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

An exploratory qualitative case study compared characteristics of high- and low-performing students by analyzing their activities, perceptions and motivations when using technology. The study "revealed that students who scored highly on the test were generally able to focus and concentrate" and "adopted cognitive and organizational strategies." No effect sizes apply to this qualitative finding.

> "The qualitative study also revealed that students who scored highly on the test were generally able to focus and concentrate on their school assignments when using the Internet. These students also adopted cognitive and organizational strategies in order to be more effective."
