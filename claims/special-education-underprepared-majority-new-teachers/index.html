Evidence¶

Carver-Thomas 2017¶

Carver-Thomas, D. & Darling-Hammond, L. (2017). Addressing California's growing teacher shortage: 2017 update. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/ca-teacher-shortage-2017-update

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CTC authorization data (Figures 5 and 6) shows substandard special education authorizations grew from 2,111 in 2011–12 to 4,004 in 2015–16 while preliminary credentials fell from 3,162 to 2,259. The report states preliminary credentials "actually fallen by 29%."

"In 2014–15, 60% of new special education teachers held substandard authorizations, and by 2015–16, nearly two thirds (64%) held substandard authorizations. In no other major teaching field do interns, permits, and waivers make up a majority of new teachers."
