Practical skills assessment of the same 54 participants using standardized scoring criteria for specimen preparation. The article reports the printed means for both smear techniques and identifies "blood film staining (41.96%)" as the lowest malaria-smear substep; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean scores for malaria blood smears and modified Kato–Katz smears were 6.39 ± 2.35 and 9.13 ± 1.54 points, respectively (Table 1). The radar chart indicated that the highest scoring rate for the malaria blood smear was observed in smear rules (97.39%), while the lowest was in blood film staining (41.96%)"
