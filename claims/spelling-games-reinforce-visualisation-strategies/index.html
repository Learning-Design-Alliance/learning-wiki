Evidence¶

Nahari 2016¶

Nahari, A. A., & Alfadda, H. A. (2016). From Memorising to Visualising: The Effect of Using Visualisation Strategies to Improve Students' Spelling Skills. English Language Teaching, 9(6), 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The researcher's reflective observation during the intervention noted a week-four dip in experimental-group performance. Adding spelling games in week five coincided with the group's strongest results, though no controlled comparison isolates the games' effect.

"I realised that in the fourth week, the students' results were not as good as in the third week, so I integrated visual strategies with spelling games, in order to motivate the students to use the strategies and make the lesson more fun"
