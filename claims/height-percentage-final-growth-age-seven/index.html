Bird T. Baldwin 1922¶

Bird T. Baldwin. (1922). The Relation Between Mental and Physical Growth. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 1. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/1/193

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the Iowa growth-curve data on height. The author reports that "a boy of the average type at 7 years of age has gained 70.3 per cent of his final growth at 17 years of age, and a girl 74.2 per cent."

"knowing that a boy of the average type at 7 years of age has gained 70.3 per cent of his final growth at 17 years of age, and a girl 74.2 per cent of her final growth, we can predict what the ultimate stature of a similiar child should be."
