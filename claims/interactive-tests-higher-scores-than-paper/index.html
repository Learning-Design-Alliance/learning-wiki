Evidence¶

Magdin and Turcáni 2015¶

Martin MAGDIN, Milan TURČÁNI. (2015). A Few Observations and Remarks on Time Effectiveness of Interactive Electronic Testing. Informatics in Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.06

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment B analysed points awarded across 9 tests (8 interactive, 1 multiple-answer) for the same two groups of 14 students. Repeated-measures ANOVA with Huynh-Feldt correction gave F = 25.8022, p = 0.000027 for test form; the authors conclude the results are "explicitly in favour of the electronic form".

"Based on the analysis of the dispersion and adjusted (Table 8) levels of variance, we reject the zero hypothesis, which claims that the difference in the point valuation of the tests between the experimental and control groups is statistically insignificant, i.e. there is a relation between the two groups."
