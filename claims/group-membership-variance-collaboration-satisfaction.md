Scenario A null-model LMM variance decomposition. The intraclass correlation of 0.37 indicates substantial clustering of collaboration satisfaction by group, while pre-simulation variables explained only small proportions of task satisfaction (10%) and post-stress (11%).

> "Analysis based on Model 1.0 revealed that group differences accounted for a substantial portion of the variance in collaboration performance satisfaction, as indicated by the intraclass correlation coefﬁcient (ICC = 0.37; τgr p = 0.64)."
