Evidence¶

Yu Fanyi 2025¶

Yu Fanyi, Nyanamba Juliet M., Anderson Brittany, DeerInWater Kathy, Estrada Mica, Hosoda K. Kanoho, Maldonado Natalia, Smith Tiffany, Flores Lilibeth, & Hernandez Paul R. (2025). Navigating Science Pathways: Belonging Mediates the Impact of Goal Congruity on Persistence (but Not Life Satisfaction) Among Indigenous Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000967

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Regression-based mediation model with bias-corrected bootstrapping (5000 repetitions) in the longitudinal study of Indigenous STEM participants (analytic N = 127), controlling for baseline belonging, intentions, cohort, and education level. The article reports a "significant negative indirect effect on STEM career intentions through belonging"; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Consistent with our expectations, communal and agentic goal incongruity exhibited a significant negative indirect effect on STEM career intentions through belonging (see Figure 3; complete details are shown in Supplemental Table 7)"
