### Dejan Vinković 2010

Dejan Vinković, Dunja Potočnik. (2010). Educational asymmetries in the making: Science Fair Competitions as Proxies of the Economic Development. Sociologija i prostor, 48 (2010) 186 (1): 51–75. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.2443](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.2443)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interpretation of physics competition participation changes across the decade, presented as the authors' assumption, not a tested result. They cite Međimurje's rise and Šibenik-Knin's drop from best in 2001-2003 to a low-SFPI group.

> "We assume that these trends are the result of a group of mentors who appear or disappear over the period of one decade. When these mentors stop their activities the whole system of science fair support in a county collapses."
