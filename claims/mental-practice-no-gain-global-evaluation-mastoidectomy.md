Secondary outcome of the same randomized trial of 12 residents, scored by two blinded otologists on the Global Evaluation of Mastoidectomy checklist covering equipment use, surgical flow and overall performance. The article reports "no statistical difference between the two groups on the Total Global Evaluation score (p = 0.657)"; no effect size is printed.
