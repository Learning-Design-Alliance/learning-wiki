Random-effects meta-analysis of Fisher-z-transformed bivariate correlations between academic resilience/buoyancy scores and external validity criteria in the systematic review. The pooled association was r = .55 with a wide prediction interval [-0.19, 0.89].

> "Construct validity evidence showed a moderate pooled association with theoretically relevant external criteria, r = .55, 95% CI [0.46, 0.63], 95% PI [-0.19, 0.89], with substantial variability across outcome domains."
