Uemura Kazuki, Yamada Minoru, Kuzuya Masafumi, Okamoto Hiroshi. (2021). Effects of Active Learning Education on Arterial Stiffness of Older Adults with Low Health Literacy: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Atherosclerosis and Thrombosis. https://doi.org/10.5551/jat.58354

RCT feasibility reporting: seven participants (11.7%) dropped out before post-intervention assessment, and three adverse events were reported. The article states "The median attendance rate in the weekly class of the active learning program was 91.7%" and that none of the adverse events "were attributable to participation in the intervention program".
