## Evidence

### Pammer-Schindler 2021

Pammer-Schindler, V. & Rosé, C. (2021). Data-Related Ethics Issues in Technologies for Informal Professional Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00259-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Field studies of the KnowSelf activity logger in a German medium-sized company; the anonymized export "hashed filenames, manual labels, and notes, and left only timestamps and application names intact". No effect sizes.

> "Even with this anonymization, a majority of study participants in Cases 1 and 2 in the end decided against handing out this data to researchers."

### Pammer-Schindler 2021 (2)

Pammer-Schindler, V. & Rosé, C. (2021). Data-Related Ethics Issues in Technologies for Informal Professional Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00259-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case 4 field study in four business-2-business call-center teams using the MoodMap App with named authors; logged mood data showed a balanced distribution of positive and negative entries. No effect sizes.

> "Explicit questions about how comfortable users were with sharing the self-tracked data elicited an overall positive response. Nonetheless, even here, three out of 29 study participants reported not being comfortable with data sharing."
