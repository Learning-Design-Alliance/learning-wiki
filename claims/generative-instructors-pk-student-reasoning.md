# Generative instructors drew on pedagogical knowledge to teach lessons focused on students generating reasoning, unlike active instructors

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Instructors exhibiting generative instruction planned and implemented lessons prioritizing time for students to articulate reasoning, demonstrating knowledge of creating opportunities for generative work (a PK component); active instructors instead emphasized practice for exams or valued lecture as central. [→ Andrews Tessa C. 2019](#andrews-tessa-c-2019)

## Evidence

### Andrews Tessa C. 2019

Andrews Tessa C., Auerbach Anna Jo J., Grant Emily F. (2019). Exploring the Relationship between Teacher Knowledge and Active-Learning Implementation in Large College Biology Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education 18(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-01-0010

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of pre- and postinstruction interviews with the 13 instructors, contrasting generative and active groups. Generative instructors also provided explicit verbal instructions and prompted for reasoning in small-group facilitation; no effect size is reported for this qualitative theme.

> "Generative instructors explained that students learn by generating explanations supported with reasoning. Therefore, they planned and implemented lessons that prioritized time for students to articulate their reasoning about biological concepts."
