`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 26 qualitative interviews with learners and staff from the 2013–15 fellowship cohorts, analysed with NVivo using Braun and Clarke's five-step process informed by CFIR. The article reports that "Human resources, variety of rotations, peer support and mentoring fostered implementation of addiction training".

> "Human resources, variety of rotations, peer support and mentoring fostered implementation of addiction training. Money, time and space limitations hindered implementation."
