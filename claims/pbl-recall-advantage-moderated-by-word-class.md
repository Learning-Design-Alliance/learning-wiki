# The PBL advantage over the conventional method held for modules with more adjectives but not for the noun-heavy modules 3 and 5 on recall

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Post-test differences between groups were significant in modules 1, 2 and 4 but not in modules 3 and 5, which the authors attribute to those modules containing more nouns than adjectives. [→ Shafaei 2015](#shafaei-2015)

## Evidence

### Shafaei 2015

Shafaei, A., & Abdul Rahim, H. (2015). Does project-based learning enhance Iranian EFL learners' vocabulary recall and retention? Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research, 3(2), 83-99. https://www.urmia.ac.ir/ijltr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-tests per module (SPSS 13.0) compared post-test scores; the article reports significance at the 99% level in modules 1, 2 and 4 and non-significance in modules 3 and 5, offering the noun/adjective composition as a possible explanation.

> "The t-test reveals that experimental group had a significantly higher post-test score in modules 1, 2 and 4 at a confidence level of 99 % while the difference in post -test scores is not significant in modules 3 and 5.  This is perhaps because the stimulus items in modules 3 and 5 comprise more nouns than adjectives."
