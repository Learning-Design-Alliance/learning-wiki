## Evidence

### Wijaya Tommy Tanu 2023

Wijaya Tommy Tanu, Yu Boran, Xu Fei, Yuan Zhiqiang, Mailizar Mailizar. (2023). Analysis of Factors Affecting Academic Performance of Mathematics Education Doctoral Students: A Structural Equation Modeling Approach. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054518

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

PLS-SEM structural model on questionnaire data from 147 Indonesian mathematics education doctoral students; engagement was among the significant direct predictors, and the article reports "student engagement, teacher support, and well-being also significantly affected academic performance".

> "student engagement, teacher support, and well-being also significantly affected academic performance."

### Wijaya Tommy Tanu 2023 (2)

Wijaya Tommy Tanu, Yu Boran, Xu Fei, Yuan Zhiqiang, Mailizar Mailizar. (2023). Analysis of Factors Affecting Academic Performance of Mathematics Education Doctoral Students: A Structural Equation Modeling Approach. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054518

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hypothesis testing in the same PLS-SEM analysis: of the 14 hypotheses, 9 were supported, and engagement's path to stress was not significant while its path to well-being was. No effect size printed for this null path, so magnitude is not reported.

> "This proved that fear of delay and parental support significantly affected stress levels. Meanwhile, student engagement did not significantly reduce these levels."
