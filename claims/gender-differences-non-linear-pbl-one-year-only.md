## Evidence

### Francis WR 2026

Francis WR, Davies NA, Seeley A. (2026). Using non-linear slide decks to administer individualized problem-based learning assessments within pharmacology education. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1002/bcp.70248

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post-test on grades by gender across five academic years, with zero scores for non-submissions omitted. The gender difference appeared in only one of five years.

> "female student grades for non-linear PBL assessments were significantly higher in the 2022/2023 academic year (n = 14, mean 80.2 ± 8.1%, range 63-92%) than males (n = 14, mean 68.6 ± 11.2%, range 50-87%, P = 0.006"
