## Evidence

### Knobovitch Ryan M. BSc 2025

Knobovitch Ryan M. BSc, Tokuno Junko MD, MSc, PhD, Botelho Fabio MD, PhD, Fried Howard B. BA, Carver Tamara E. PhD, Fried Gerald M. MD. (2025). Virtual Reality Training Improves Procedural Skills in Mannequin-Based Simulation in Medical Students: A Pilot Randomized Controlled Trial. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506251334693

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this assessor-blinded pilot RCT, 30 medical students randomized 1:1 to online-only or online-plus-VR training performed chest tube insertion on a standardized mannequin; expert surgeon assessors blinded to allocation rated skill with an 11-item modified OSATS. The article reports "significantly higher in the VR group than controls" with P = 0.03.

> "The median overall OSATS score for the mannequin-based simulation was significantly higher in the VR group than controls (VR: 46 [42-50] controls: 39 [33-45] out of 55; P = 0.03)."
