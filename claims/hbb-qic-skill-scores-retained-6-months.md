## Evidence

### KC Ashish 2017

KC Ashish, Wrammert Johan, Nelin Viktoria, Clark Robert B., Ewald Uwe, Peterson Stefan, Målqvist Mats. (2017). Evaluation of Helping Babies Breathe Quality Improvement Cycle (HBB-QIC) on retention of neonatal resuscitation skills six months after training in Nepal. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-017-0853-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same 137-worker time-series evaluation, mean scores on checklists and two simulated OSCEs (e.g., bag-and-mask 2.3 ± 0.5 to 6.7 ± 0.6 out of 7) rose after training and "was retained 6 months after the training" with no significant decline in Table 4 follow-up tests.

> "There was a significant change (<0.001) from baseline in the mean skills scores for preparation for resuscitation before every birth, bag-and-mask skills, OSCE A, and OSCE B after the training, which was retained 6 months after the training (Table 4)."
