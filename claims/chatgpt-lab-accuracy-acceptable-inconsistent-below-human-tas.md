## Evidence

### M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer 2026

M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer. (2026). ChatGPT as a Virtual Laboratory Teaching Assistant in Undergraduate Biology. Research in Science Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11165-025-10271-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive SME accuracy ratings (7-point Likert) of ChatGPT responses to eight student-generated enzyme-laboratory questions, from the RQ1 results. SMEs judged accuracy acceptable while noting "no questions showed stronger responses (6 or 7 on the Likert scale)".

> "All ChatGPT responses for the eight questions scored at or above the neutral point of the Likert scale, but no questions showed stronger responses (6 or 7 on the Likert scale)."

### M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer 2026 (2)

M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer. (2026). ChatGPT as a Virtual Laboratory Teaching Assistant in Undergraduate Biology. Research in Science Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11165-025-10271-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-factor repeated measures ANOVA over SME accuracy ratings of ChatGPT responses in the RQ1 analysis. The article reports significant variation across questions (F = 4.88, p = 0.000) and concludes "AI did not respond with consistent content accuracy".

> "there are statistically significant differences in the SME-evaluated content accuracy across the eight questions ( F = 4.88, p = 0.000); AI did not respond with consistent content accuracy, according to the SMEs."

### M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer 2026 (3)

M. Said Doğru & Emily K. Faulconer. (2026). ChatGPT as a Virtual Laboratory Teaching Assistant in Undergraduate Biology. Research in Science Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11165-025-10271-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded repeated measures ANOVA comparison of SME accuracy ratings of AI- versus human-authored responses (RQ3), with Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc tests. On the questions differing significantly, "SMEs considered human TAs more accurate than ChatGPT".

> "SME content accuracy ratings significantly differed between ChatGPT and human TA responses for 5 of the eight questions used in this study. For each of these questions, SMEs considered human TAs more accurate than ChatGPT."
