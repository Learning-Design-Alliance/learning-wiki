## Evidence

### Kevin Wang 2023

Kevin Wang, Seth Akins, Abdallah Mohammed, Ramon Lawrence. (2023). Student Mastery or AI Deception? Analyzing ChatGPT's Assessment Proficiency and Evaluating Detection Strategies. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1109/csci62032.2023.00268](https://doi.org/10.1109/csci62032.2023.00268)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' synthesis of which evaluated tasks resisted ChatGPT: the UML/ER labs scored 0%, the debugging-screenshot task could not be done, and the conclusion identifies open-endedness, images or student-specific outputs, and unspecified output as the resistance features. This is an interpretive summary rather than a controlled comparison.

> "Assessments with some resistance to ChatGPT were more open-ended, involved images or student-specific outputs, or did not specify expected output in detail."
