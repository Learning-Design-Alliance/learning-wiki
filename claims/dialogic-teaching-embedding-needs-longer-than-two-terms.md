## Evidence

### Jay et al. 2017

Tim Jay, Ben Willis, Peter Thomas, Roberta Taylor, Nick Moore, Cathy Burnett, Guy Merchant, Anna Stevens. (2017). Dialogic Teaching: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey-based process evaluation finding. The report adds that teachers felt the impact evaluation was unlikely to show a positive effect for this reason, and that "it is possible that the effect sizes observed in this evaluation are underestimates of the potential impact".

> "The majority of participating teachers felt that it would take longer than two terms  to fully embed a Dialogic Teaching approach in their classrooms. It could therefore be valuable to test the impact of the intervention over a longer period."
