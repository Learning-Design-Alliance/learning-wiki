## Evidence

### Axboe Mette K. 2016

Axboe Mette K., Christensen Kaj S., Kofoed Poul-Erik, Ammentorp Jette. (2016). Development and validation of a self-efficacy questionnaire (SE-12) measuring the clinical communication skills of health care professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0798-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire validation study of 292 clinicians (195 in test-retest) at three Danish hospitals. Exploratory factor analysis, Mokken scale analysis, and test-retest showed "a Cronbach's α of 0.95" and ICC 0.71, just above the 0.70 cut-off.

> "A Cronbach's α of 0.95 and a Loevinger's H coefficient of 0.71 provided evidence of statistical reliability and scalability. The test-retest reliability had a value of 0.71 when evaluated using intra-class correlation."
