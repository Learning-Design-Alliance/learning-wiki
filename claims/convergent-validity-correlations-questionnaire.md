## Evidence

### Luo Taizhen 2025

Luo Taizhen, Gong Yang, Dai Sidi, Xia Hua-an, Jiang Zhiyan, Gao Lingling. (2025). Questionnaire development: midwives' knowledge and attitude on liberal birthing position in 2nd stage. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07466-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Convergent validity test (Step 8) on the formal survey data for the knowledge section; the article reports item-dimension correlations above 0.3 and dimension-total correlations above 0.7, exceeding inter-dimension correlations.

> "The score of each dimension was strongly correlated with the total score (the correlation coefficient was greater than 0.7) and was greater than the correlation coefficient between the score of each dimension, indicating that the clustering validity of the questionnaire was good"
