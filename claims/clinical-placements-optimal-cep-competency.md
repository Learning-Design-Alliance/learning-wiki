## Evidence

### Crozier Anthony 2025

Crozier Anthony, Miller Gemma, Graves Lee, Dawson Ellen A, Osin Connor, Sadler Ian, Naylor Louise H, Green Daniel J, Askew Christopher David, Jones Helen. (2025). How are clinical exercise physiology postgraduate courses taught and assessed in the UK? A multimethod qualitative exploration. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-099240

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of combined interview (n=16 students) and focus group (n=8 academics, n=5 CEPs) data in this qualitative multimethod study; the abstract-level synthesis reports placements as "the most important source of knowledge and skill attainment". No effect size is applicable.

> "The current use of simulated tasks for the delivery of taught content was identified as lacking effectiveness, with clinical placements identified as being the most important source of knowledge and skill attainment due to the real-world exposure to patients and practitioners."
