### Noah Glaser 2024

Noah Glaser, Lucas Jensen, Tina Riedy, Maggie Center, Jim Shifflett, Joseph Griffin. (2024). Unlocking the Everdoor: analyzing the serious game Spiritfarer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10357-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from the LED analysis categorized into three educational themes: perceptions on death, understanding palliative care, and people grieve differently. Participants gave responses such as gaining "a helpful framework for thinking about death" and insights about comforting people during their last days.

> "Participants reported that Spiritfarer facilitated introspection and reflection on their beliefs and experiences related to death and loss."
