Evidence¶

Tran 2014¶

Tran, V. D. (2014). The Effects of Cooperative Learning on the Academic Achievement and Knowledge Retention. International Journal of Higher Education, 3(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v3n2p131

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest comparison of two intact classes of 55 first-year primary education students taught by the same lecturer for eight weeks. The t-test showed "a significant difference (t (108) = 9.60, p = .000)" with a printed mean difference of 10.36 and ES = 0.46.

"the findings obtained from t-test analysis on the psychology posttest scores showed a significant difference (t (108) = 9.60, p = .000) between the experimental group (M = 77.36, SD = 4.52) and the control group (M = 67.00, SD = 6.60)"
