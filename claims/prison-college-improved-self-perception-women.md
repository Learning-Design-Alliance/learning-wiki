# Former students reported positive changes in self-perception and self-confidence after participating in prison higher education

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Former students described shifts from feeling something was wrong with them to feeling capable and qualified, and described empowerment and perseverance gained through coursework. [→ Jillian Baranger 2018](#jillian-baranger-2018)

## Evidence

### Jillian Baranger 2018

Jillian Baranger, Danielle Rousseau, Mary Ellen Mastrorilli, James Matesanz. (2018). Doing Time Wisely: The Social and Personal Benefits of Higher Education in Prison. The Prison Journal. https://doi.org/10.1177/0032885518776380

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Student interview in the pilot qualitative study. Sarah reflected on how her self-perception changed before and after participating in higher education in prison, saying she now feels "capable and I am qualified".

> "“I always thought, well something must be wrong with me, you know? …today I know that … I am capable and I am qualified of doing anything that I put my mind to.”"
