### Song A. An 2015

Song A. An, and Daniel A. Tillman. (2015). Music activities as a meaningful context for teaching elementary students mathematics: a quasi-experiment time series design with random assigned control group. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, 3(1), 45-60. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1107839

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics across the 15 MSA tests (Figure 2) showed "reduction of standard deviations" on all three process ability levels; for example, overall SD fell from 0.46 at pretest to 0.24 at posttest (Table 4). This is a descriptive pattern, not a significance test of the gap change.

> "This finding indicates that not only did most students improve their average mathematics process ability levels throughout the intervention of music-mathematics integrated curriculum and instruction, but also the gap between the high achieving and low achieving students was reduced."
