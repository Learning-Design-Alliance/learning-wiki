## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Perceived difficulty of endocrinology correlated negatively with MED4 exam score (ρ = −0.52, p < 0.0001, p adjusted < 0.01), with weaker negative correlations for heart, renal, and digestive physiology. [→ Heltne 2024](#heltne-2024)

## Evidence

### Heltne 2024

Heltne, Sofie Fagervoll; Hovdenakk, Sigrid; Kvernenes, Monika; Tenstad, Olav. (2024). Study preferences and exam outcomes in medical education: insights from renal physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05964-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rho analysis in the survey study (n = 72) of perceived difficulty against MED4 exam score. The strongest correlation survived Bonferroni correction: endocrinology "ρ = − 0.52, p < 0.0001, p adjusted < 0.01"; the others did not survive correction.

> "perceptions of difficulty in endocrinology (ρ = − 0.52, p < 0.0001, p adjusted < 0.01), heart physiology (ρ = − 0.35, p = 0.002, p adjusted > 0.05), renal physiology (ρ = − 0.30, p = 0.01, p adjusted > 0.05), and digestive system (ρ = − 0.25, p = 0.04, p adjusted > 0.05)."
