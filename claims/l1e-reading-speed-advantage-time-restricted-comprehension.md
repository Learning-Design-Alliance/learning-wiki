### Babayiğit 2026

Babayiğit, S., & Trenkic, D. (2026). Vocabulary knowledge is key to understanding and addressing disparities in higher education. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 127–151. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70000

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA of reading speed and Nelson–Denny time-restricted comprehension across the three groups. The article reports the L1E group "read faster than the EAL and EFL groups" and scored highest on the time-restricted test.

> "Pairwise comparisons showed that the L1E group read faster than the EAL and EFL groups and achieved the highest reading comprehension scores on the time‑restricted test, with medium to large effect sizes."

### Babayiğit 2026 (3)

Babayiğit, S., & Trenkic, D. (2026). Vocabulary knowledge is key to understanding and addressing disparities in higher education. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 127–151. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70000

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up analysis recalculated Nelson–Denny scores as the proportion of correct answers out of total questions attempted to remove the reading-speed bias; the adjustment "eliminated the EAL and EFL disadvantage".
