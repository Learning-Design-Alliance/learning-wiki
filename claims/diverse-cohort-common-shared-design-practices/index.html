Connolly 2021¶

Connolly, C., & Hall, T. (2021). Designing for emergency remote blended and online education: a response to Bennett et al. (2017). Education Tech Research Dev, 69, 281–284. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09892-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports that Bennett et al.'s study, which engaged "a diverse cross-section of university teachers, drawn from a range of different academic disciplines", teaching face-to-face, blended and online, nonetheless "reports common, shared practices across the varied cohort". This is a second-hand report of the reviewed study.

"Interestingly, despite the disciplinary diversity of respondents, and the nuances in their respective approaches, the paper reports common, shared practices across the varied cohort."
