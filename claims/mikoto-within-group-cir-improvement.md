`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified secondary analysis of pre- to post-training change in the cluster-randomized trial, using mixed-effects models with trainee random intercept. The mikoto group's within-group CIR gain was statistically significant; the control group's was not. The group-by-period interaction was not significant (p = 0.394).

> "Model‐based within‐group contrasts showed that CIR increased from 60.0% to 80.0% in the mikoto group (p = 0.003), whereas the increase from 68.9% to 77.8% in the control group did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.119)."
