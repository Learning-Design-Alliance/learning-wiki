## Evidence

### Munzer Brendan W. MD 2020

Munzer Brendan W. MD, Bassin Benjamin S. MD, Peterson William J. MD, Tucker Ryan V. MD, Doan Jessica MD, Harvey Carrie MD, Sefa Nana MD, Hsu Cindy H. MD, PhD. (2020). In-situ Simulation Use for Rapid Implementation and Process Improvement of COVID-19 Airway Management. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.7.48159

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Third domain of the retrospective pre/post survey of 81 physician participants, covering comfort performing responsibilities such as intubating and giving medications without violating PPE precautions. The article reports "a difference of 1.30 points (1.06–1.54, p < 0.001)".

> "Their comfort level increased from 3.08 (2.80–3.35) before to 4.38 (4.23–4.52) after the simulation, a difference of 1.30 points (1.06–1.54, p < 0.001)"
