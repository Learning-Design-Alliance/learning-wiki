## Evidence

### Mike Verostek 2024

Mike Verostek, Casey W. Miller, Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2024). Modeling when and how physics PhD students search for a research group: the role of interests and prior research experiences in timely group integration. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of interview participants sorted by highest interest level expressed before graduate school and by when they committed to a group. Of all 18 early joiners, only 3 (17%) had broad interests, versus 12 of 22 (54%) later joiners.

> "Figure 4a) shows that 12 of 15 students who were categorized as having broad interests found their research groups during the second semester or later, compared to 3 of 15 who joined in their first semester or before."
