## Evidence

### Hill K 2023

Hill K, Jaensch A, Childs J, McDonald S. (2023). Evaluation of point of care ultrasound (POCUS) training on arteriovenous access assessment and cannula placement for haemodialysis. The Journal of Vascular Access. https://doi.org/10.1177/11297298231194100

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective pre-test post-test evaluation with 15 nurses at three regional haemodialysis units completing a validated POCUS competency survey in matched pre/post pairs; paired t-test analysis showed a significant shift for all questions except question 1. The article reports significance without an effect size.

> "This shift towards an increase in confidence was statistically significant (p ⩽ 0.05) for all questions other than question 1 'I am confident in my ability to physically assess vascular access' (p = 0.06)."
