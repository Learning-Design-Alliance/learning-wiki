Evidence¶

Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu 2026¶

Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu. (2026). Testing the Relative Universalism of Psychological Theories: A Person-Centered Analysis of Control-Value Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10155-2

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Latent profile analysis (two-to-ten class models, five-class solution retained on statistical and substantive criteria) of four activity emotions in 14,356 classified students. The article reports the high-boredom profile at "a small proportion of the sample (4.4%)" and a gender gradient across ASC profiles.

"The high-boredom profile represents a small proportion of the sample (4.4%) and is notably overrepresented by male students. Across the four ASC profiles, a clear trend emerges: as academic control decreases, the proportion of female students increases"

  • CVT's control–anxiety relation breaks down across latent emotional profiles, with unstandardized coefficients ranging from −0.292 to +0.024 including non-significant positive effects — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The high-boredom (HghLBO) profile deviates from the linear control–emotion trend, showing elevated boredom and reduced enjoyment relative to its academic-control level — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
