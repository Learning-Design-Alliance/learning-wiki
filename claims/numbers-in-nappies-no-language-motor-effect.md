# The Numbers in Nappies programme showed no statistically significant effect on the Language or Motor scales of the BSID (III)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within the intervention group, pre-to-post changes on the Language Scale (Z = -1.68, p = 0.09) and Motor Scale (Z = -0.76, p = 0.45) were not statistically significant. [→ van Eeden 2017](#van-eeden-2017)
`q2 i?` Between groups after the intervention, no significant effect was found on the Language Scale (U = 622, Z = 1.78, p = 0.07) or the Motor Scale (U = 595, Z = 1.14, p = 0.16). [→ van Eeden 2017 (2)](#van-eeden-2017-2)

## Evidence

### van Eeden 2017

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests within the intervention group (N = 29) on the Language and Motor composite scales before and after the programme; numerical increases were noted but neither reached significance. No effect sizes are printed.

> "However, the intervention programme did not have a statistically significant effect in the case of the Language Scale (Z = -1.68, p = 0.09) or the Motor Scale (Z = -0.76, p = 0.45)."

### van Eeden 2017 (2)

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons of intervention (N = 29) versus control (N = 34) groups after the intervention on the Language and Motor scales; neither difference was statistically significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant effect of the intervention programme was found in the case of the Language Scale (U = 622, Z = 1.78, p = 0.07) or the Motor Scale (U = 595, Z = 1.14, p = 0.16)."
