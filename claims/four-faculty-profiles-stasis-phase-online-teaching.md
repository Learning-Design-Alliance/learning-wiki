## Evidence

### Trevisan 2023

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Second snapshot survey of 54 faculty in late 2021, analyzed independently with two-step cluster analysis. Profiles were Struggling (22.2%), Self-reliant (14.8%), Complying (35.2%) and Thriving (27.8%); gender and recent training attendance differed among clusters (eta2 = .17 and .18).

> "A 2-step cluster analysis of the respondents at S2 revealed four response patterns across the three main factors of the questionnaire, i.e., Enthusiasm, Resolutions, and Support."

### Trevisan 2023 (2)

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ICAP-scale technology-use results at S2 (alpha = .85, 7 items): the article reports the Thriving cluster "diversifying their OT practices to engage students in various ways", while the Complying cluster preferred passive or interactive ICT uses over active and constructive ones. All four ICAP rows in Table 3 are marked significantly different among clusters.

> "The Thriving and the Struggling clusters were opposites in using the most and the least varied ICT affordances respectively."
