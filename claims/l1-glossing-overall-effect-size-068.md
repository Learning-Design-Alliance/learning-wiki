# L1 glossing produces a medium-to-large overall improvement in L2 reading comprehension (g = .68) across experimental and quasi-experimental studies

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across 28 study reports, learners with L1 glosses comprehended L2 texts almost three-fourths of a standard deviation above those without glosses. [→ Taylor 2014](#taylor-2014)

## Evidence

### Taylor 2014

Taylor, A. M. (2014). Glossing Frequency and L2 Reading Comprehension: The Influence of CALL Glossing. CALICO Journal, 31(3). https://doi.org/10.11139/cj.31.3.374-389

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 20 studies with 28 study reports of experiments or quasi-experiments testing L1 glosses versus no glosses on L2 reading comprehension, using Comprehensive Meta-Analysis software. The article reports the printed effect size g = .68 for the overall effect of L1 glossing, with significant heterogeneity (Q = 126.422, p < .001).

> "The overall effect size, Hedges, g, of L1 aides on L2 reading comprehension was .68 with considerable heterogeneity among the studies (Q = 126.422 p < .001)."
