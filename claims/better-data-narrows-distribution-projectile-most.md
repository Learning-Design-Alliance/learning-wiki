Better-data multiple-choice responses from the same survey, shown in Fig. 3 and Table III. The authors identify more narrow as an expert-like response across all experiments, because experts with the best equipment perform measurements with fewer experimental limitations than students with basic equipment.

> "We see that the Projectile Motion scenario receives a much larger proportion of more narrow responses than the other experiments."
