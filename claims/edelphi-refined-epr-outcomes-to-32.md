# A two-round eDelphi with eight external experts refined the EPR domains and learning outcomes to a final set of 32 outcomes

## Evidence

### Pontefract S. K. 2019

Pontefract S. K., Wilson K. (2019). Using electronic patient records: defining learning outcomes for undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1466-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-round eDelphi review: "Experts working in medical education or as healthcare professionals working with, or researching EPRs, were invited to participate in a two-round eDelphi to independently review the domains of competence and associated learning outcomes to gain consensus." The final list was emailed to eight experts; all eight completed Round 2.

> "In Round 1 the eDelphi process, participants made recommendations to remove two learning outcomes, add four, amend 19 and move five (See Fig. 1)."

### Pontefract S. K. 2019 (2)

Pontefract S. K., Wilson K. (2019). Using electronic patient records: defining learning outcomes for undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1466-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Outcome of the iterative refinement: Round 1 suggestions included changing Domain 1 to ‘Digital Health: working as a practitioner in the digital NHS’ because digital literacy referred to terminology rather than use of the technology; in Round 2 one participant suggested that the reporting of adverse events should be included in ‘Monitoring and audit’ (Domain 6).

> "Following two rounds of review (Fig. 1), the six domains of competence and 32 learning outcomes were agreed (see Table 3)."
