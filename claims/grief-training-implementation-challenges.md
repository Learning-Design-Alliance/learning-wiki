# Implementing grief training faces multi-level challenges including small class sizes, faculty turnover and low motivation because some physicians do not consider grief support part of their job

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Logistical, institutional and motivational challenges impeded grief training: class sizes small enough for quality training, sustainability after staff turnover without a faculty champion, competing curriculum needs, and physicians not considering grief support part of their job description.
