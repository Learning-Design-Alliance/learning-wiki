# After baseline adjustment, the BOPPPS group scored significantly higher than controls on all three pre-anesthesia care skill subscales

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Adjusted for pre-test scores, the intervention group outperformed controls in equipment preparation (partial η² = 0.40). [→ Khalafi Ali 2026](#khalafi-ali-2026)
`q2 i3` Adjusted for pre-test scores, the intervention group outperformed controls in patient preparation (partial η² = 0.43). [→ Khalafi Ali 2026](#khalafi-ali-2026)

## Evidence

### Khalafi Ali 2026

Khalafi Ali, Parhizkar Mahdieh, Albooghobeish Masoumeh, Sarvi-sarmeydani Nooshin. (2026). Effect of the BOPPPS model on pre-anesthesia care education for nurse anesthesia students in Iran: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08696-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA with post-test scores as dependent variables and pre-test scores as covariates in the two-group quasi-experiment. Adjusted group differences remained significant for all three skill subscales, with partial η² = 0.40, 0.43, and 0.46 respectively, all p < 0.001.

> "Similarly, for the skill subscales, significant group differences remained after adjustment: equipment preparation (F(1, 57) = 38.15, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.40), patient preparation (F(1, 57) = 42.63, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.43), and drug preparation (F(1, 57) = 47.92, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.46)."
