Ji Pengyu 2026¶

Ji Pengyu, Li Qianqian, Li Na, Zhang Lin. (2026). The efficacy of bilingual immunology teaching on undergraduates in China: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08725-7

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of five non-randomized studies (345 participants) measuring competence in English application — reading and writing English literature, professional English expression, and presenting English-language scientific materials. Substantial heterogeneity (I² = 97%) warranted a random effects model.

"The pooled results indicate a statistically significant improvement in students’ English application ability following the implementation of bilingual instruction (p = 0.003), with a RD of 0.36 (95% CI: 0.13 to 0.59)"
