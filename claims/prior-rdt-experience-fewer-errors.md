## Evidence

### Boyce Matthew R. 2018

Boyce Matthew R., Menya Diana, Turner Elizabeth L., Laktabai Jeremiah, Prudhomme-O'Meara Wendy. (2018). Evaluation of malaria rapid diagnostic test (RDT) use by community health workers: a longitudinal study in western Kenya. Malaria Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12936-018-2358-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable Poisson regression of total error counts at 12-month follow-up for 103 CHWs. The article reports prior RDT experience MER = 0.50 (95% CI 0.31–0.80), male gender MER = 0.70 (95% CI 0.52–0.96), and age 50+ MER = 1.69 (95% CI 1.23–2.31) as significant covariates.

> "The MER for prior RDT experience was 0.50 (95% CI 0.31–0.80), which corresponds to the number of total errors being 50% (95% CI 20–69) lower for those with RDT training prior this training program compared to those without prior training."
