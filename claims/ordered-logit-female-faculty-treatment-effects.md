## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In ordered logit models for female faculty, the treatment coefficient was significant for the instructor rating (0.82) and course rating (0.92) but not for teaching effectiveness (0.42). [→ Peterson David A. M. 2019](#peterson-david-a-m-2019)

## Evidence

### Peterson David A. M. 2019

Peterson David A. M., Biederman Lori A., Andersen David, Ditonto Tessa M., Roe Kevin. (2019). Mitigating gender bias in student evaluations of teaching. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216241

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Ordered logit models (Table 1, N = 159) accounting for the ordinal five-point SET items. Treatment coefficients were 0.82 (SE 0.31) for instructor and 0.92 (SE 0.31) for course, marked significant at p<0.05; effectiveness was 0.42 (SE 0.30), not significant.

> "For female faculty, the treatment has a significant positive effect on the evaluations of the overall rating of the instructor and the overall rating of the course. Unlike the results of the t-tests, there is a positive, but not significant effect of the intervention on the rating of the instructor's teaching effectiveness."
