## Evidence

### Ross E. O'Hara 2019

Ross E. O'Hara, Betsy Sparrow. (2019). A Summer Nudge Campaign to Motivate Community College STEM Students to Reenroll. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419875715

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive engagement analysis of the 1,367 treatment-group students reported in Table 3: 96% received nudges, students received approximately 12 nudges each and answered 25% of questions; 19% responded at least once. Comparisons between summer-enrolled and non-enrolled students are reported with the printed chi-square statistics.

> "these students were less likely to unsubscribe (13% vs. 17%), χ2(1) = 3.88, p  < .05, and more likely to respond at least once (28% vs. 14%), χ2(1) = 49.84, p < .001."
