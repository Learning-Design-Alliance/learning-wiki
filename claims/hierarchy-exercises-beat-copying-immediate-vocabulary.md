## Evidence

### Lin 2013

Lin, Ching-Ying & Hsu, Wei Shu. (2013). Effects of Hierarchy Vocabulary Exercises on English Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 6(9).

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 56 Grade 5 students in Taiwan (27 experimental, 29 control), groups confirmed equivalent on prior English grades and pretest scores. Independent t-test on the immediate vocabulary post-test found the hierarchy group higher, "a significant difference between both groups (t (54) = 2.380, p =.021".

> "For the scores on the immediate vocabulary post-test, the results showed that the mean of the experimental group (M = 88.81, SD = 17.94) was higher than that of the control group (M = 76.21, SD = 21.40). Besides, there was a significant difference between both groups (t (54) = 2.380, p =.021"
