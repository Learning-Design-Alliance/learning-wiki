### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). Helping Babies Breathe, Second Edition: A Model for Strengthening Educational Programs to Increase Global Newborn Survival. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-18-00147

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field trial at AIIMS New Delhi in June 2016 with 24 participants (6 with prior first-edition training, 18 novices); the 'Commit to making a difference' page solicited answers identifying gaps in care.

> "Of the 24 participants, 22 felt well-prepared to be a facilitator after the course."

### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018 (2)

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). Helping Babies Breathe, Second Edition: A Model for Strengthening Educational Programs to Increase Global Newborn Survival. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-18-00147

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of audio-recorded interviews from 3 focus group discussions by independent reviewers; participants valued personalized facilitator interaction and genuine feedback.

> "Participants liked the course emphasis on hands-on learning, rather than lectures, and felt that the small group size allowed participants more time to practice skills and observe and learn from each other's mistakes."
