## Evidence

### Puolakanaho 2018

Puolakanaho, A., Lappalainen, R., Lappalainen, P., Muotka, J. S., Hirvonen, R., Eklund, K. M., Ahonen, T. P. S., & Kiuru, N. (2018). Reducing Stress and Enhancing Academic Buoyancy among Adolescents Using a Brief Web-based Program Based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-018-0973-8

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Per-protocol analysis contrasting the combined intervention group (n = 123, those meeting the adherence criterion) with the control group (n = 82), gender as covariate. The Wald test showed "a statistically significant decrease in overall stress (p = 0.037)"; the printed between-group effect size was d = 0.22.

> "The Wald test (one tailed) showed a statistically significant decrease in overall stress (p = 0.037) and an increase in academic buoyancy (p = 0.013) among all participants"
