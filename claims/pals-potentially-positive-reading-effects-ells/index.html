Evidence¶

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies 2010¶

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies. (2010). WWC Intervention Report. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://kc.vanderbilt.edu/pals

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

WWC re-analysis of the Saenz, Fuchs, and Fuchs (2005) randomized controlled trial, in which 99 English language learners from 12 classrooms in grades 3-6 in one Texas school district were randomly assigned by classroom. The combined effect was "positive and large enough to be considered substantively important (ES = 0.31)".

"There were no statistically significant effects on the three reading achievement measures once clustering corrections were made. However, the WWC found that the combined effect for reading achievement across all measures was positive and large enough to be considered substantively important (ES = 0.31)."
