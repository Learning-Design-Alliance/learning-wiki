Levin 2020¶

Survey findings section of the NAESP-LPI study: descriptive percentages for three authentic learning activities. The report adds that 23% had a mentor or coach and 56% regularly participated in PLCs (Figure 3).

"fewer than one third of all principals (32%) were able to spend time sharing leadership practices with their peers three or more times in the past 2 years, a constructive “applied” activity in reinforcing learning."
