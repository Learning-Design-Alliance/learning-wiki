# Perceived value of creativity informs judgments and expectancies primarily indirectly through task-specific variables, especially task interest

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The perceived value of creativity informed both metacognitive judgments and expectancy before Task 2 primarily through indirect effects, unlike creative self-efficacy. [→ Kamila Urban 2025](#kamila-urban-2025)

## Evidence

### Kamila Urban 2025

Kamila Urban, Marek Urban. (2025). Metacognition and motivation in creativity: examining the roles of self-efficacy and values as cues for metacognitive judgments. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09421-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the two-wave SEM of the 360-student sample, "the indirect effects were stronger than those of creative self-efficacy" for value of creativity, with mediation classified as partial (74% and 77%) for judgment and expectancy pathways.

> "For the perceived value of creativity, the indirect effects were stronger than those of creative self-efficacy, with β = 0.30, p <.001 for expectancy before Task 2, and β = 0.23, p <.001 and β = 0.27, p <.001 for metacognitive judgments."
