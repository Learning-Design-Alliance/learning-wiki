`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot reliability evaluation in which three blinded 10-year experienced ACLS-certified emergency physicians independently scored ten AI-generated scenario transcripts against an AHA-based checklist. The article reports "a very high level of agreement among the raters (Kendall W > 0.754, p < 0.05)"; no effect size is printed.

> "As shown in Table 4, there is a very high level of agreement among the raters (Kendall W > 0.754, p < 0.05). This finding suggests that the raters’ rankings are statistically consistent in this pilot evaluation."
