Evidence¶

Jiayi Wang 2025¶

Jiayi Wang, Ruiwei Xiao, Xinying Hou, John Stamper. (2025). Enabling Multi-Agent Systems as Learning Designers: Applying Learning Sciences to AI Instructional Design. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16659

q3 · i3 · design · r2

Human expert evaluation in which 20 US secondary math and science teachers rated all three systems on QM criteria using repeated-measures ANOVA. The article reports "a statistically significant effect of system on criterion 5.2" with η²p = .20, described as a large effect size.

"ANOVA revealed a statistically significant effect of system on criterion 5.2, F (2, 38) = 4.75,p = .014,η2 p = .20, indicating a large effect size."
