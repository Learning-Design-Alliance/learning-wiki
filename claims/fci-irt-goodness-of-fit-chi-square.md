## Evidence

### Jing Wang and Lei Bao 2010

Jing Wang and Lei Bao. (2010). Analyzing Force Concept Inventory with Item Response Theory. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.5473](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.5473)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Goodness-of-fit analysis of the three-parameter item response model fitted to FCI pre-test data (N=2802), with per-item Q statistics listed in Table I. The article reports all "Q values are less than 1" and concludes none of the 30 questions is significantly misfitted.

> "Here all the Q values are less than 1, falling within the 68% quantile of the χ2(1) distribution, which is the commonly accepted critical value in a one-degree chi-square test."
