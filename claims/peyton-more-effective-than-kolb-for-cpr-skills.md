## Evidence

### Abbasi M 2025

Abbasi M, Taghadosi M, Atoof F, Hosseiniara R. (2025). A quasi-experimental comparison of Kolb and Peyton educational approaches on CPR knowledge and performance among nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06996-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparisons in the three-group quasi-experimental study of 108 nurses. The abstract additionally reports p = 0.001 for cardiac massage and p = 0.01 for breathing and defibrillator skills favouring Peyton over Kolb.

> "Additionally, between-group comparisons indicated significant differences in these performance domains one and six weeks post-intervention, with the Peyton method demonstrating the greatest impact, particularly in cardiac massage skills."
