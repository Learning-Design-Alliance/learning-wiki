## Evidence

### Perry 2026

Perry, R.C., Filippi, R., Toledano, M.B. et al. (2026). Multilingualism educational attainment and cognitive development in UK adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00411-z

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM of age 11 SATs in 1,673 SCAMP pupils. The article reports "a small negative effect of being a multilingual learner"; the effect reduced by more than a third with SES and ethnicity controls (β = -.15, p < .001).

> "a small negative effect of being a multilingual learner (ML) versus monolingual (ϐ = -.26, p < .001) on educational attainment at age 11."
