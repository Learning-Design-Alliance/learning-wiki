### David Roundy 2014

David Roundy, Allison Dorko, Tevian Dray, Corinne A. Manogue, and Eric Weber. (2014). Experts' understanding of partial derivatives using the Partial Derivative Machine. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Across all three disciplinary groups in the expert interviews, participants checked whether their measurement increments were small enough to approximate the derivative. The authors report "a strong recognition of the limit layer of Zandieh" across all groups.

> "Each group immediately proceeded to explore whether their changes were suﬃ- ciently small. This reﬂects a strong recognition of the limit layer of Zandieh, 12 and a recognition that a mea- surement of the derivative must account for this."
