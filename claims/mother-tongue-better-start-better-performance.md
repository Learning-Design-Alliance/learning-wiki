### Behrmann 2018

Behrmann, T. (2018). Evaluating the effects of mother tongue on math and science instruction. Monument, CO, USA: ISTES Organization. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED603513

`q2 · i?`

The monograph's literature review attributes this assertion to Daniel (2003), quoting it verbatim to motivate the study's hypothesis that Kreyol-medium instruction will improve Haitian students' achievement. No effect size is printed; the review reports the claim second-hand.

> ""Years of research have shown that children who begin their education in their mother tongue make a better start, and continue to perform better, than those for whom school starts with a new language" (Daniel, 2003, p. 1)."
