Krišťák 2014¶

Krišťák, Ľ., Němec, M., Danihelová, Z. (2014). Interactive Methods of Teaching Physics at Technical Universities. Informatics in Education, 13(1), 51–71. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2014.03

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' conclusion drawn from the overall and partial didactic-test evaluations across 2007–2012; it is stated as a trend observed across pretest ability levels, with no subgroup statistics printed in the quoted sentence.

"A positive trend when using interactive methods was shown independently from input students’ knowledge, i.e. also with students whose results of pretest were very weak as well as with students who achieved in the pretest (relatively) good results."
