## Evidence

### Julian Rudisch 2024

Julian Rudisch, Luis K. H. Holzhauer, Karmen Kravanja, Fred H. Hamker & Claudia Voelcker-Rehage. (2024). A systematic review of observational practice for adaptation of reaching movements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00271-5

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis of the three studies comparing direct effects of observational and physical practice (Larssen et al.; Lei et al.; Ong et al.). Reports "The overall effect size was 4.38 in favour of physical practice" with study-level values 5.41–9.81 and one null at 0.98.

> "However, in comparison to physical practice, two out of three studies found larger adaptation for physical practice with effect sizes between 5.41 and 9.81. One study found no difference with an effect size of 0.98. The overall effect size was 4.38 in favour of physical practice."
