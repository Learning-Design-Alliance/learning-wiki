## Evidence

### Johnkutty Meenu 2025

Johnkutty Meenu, Kuperstein Harry, Koroma Fatima, Chen Jessi, Mattson James, Ahmad Sahar. (2025). A novel simulation paradigm for medical ICU cardiopulmonary arrest training: the in situ mirror simulation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2528355

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey study of 24 PGY-3 residents leading mirrored in situ simulations in the MICU; confidence rated on a 5-point Likert scale and analyzed with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test at alpha = 0.05, showing "a significant increase in confidence for interpreting an end-tidal CO2 value (p = .007)" among other gains.

> "Learners reported increased confidence in technical skills, including a significant increase in confidence for interpreting an end-tidal CO2 value (p = .007), ruling out reversible causes of CPA (p = .020) and performing post-resuscitative care (p = .003)."
