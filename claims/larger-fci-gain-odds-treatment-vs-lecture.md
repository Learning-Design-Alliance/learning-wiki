## Subclaims
`q2 i3` For students starting in the lowest FCI pretest group, the odds of gaining at least 7 additional correct FCI questions were 4.2 times larger in section I than in the standard-lecture section IV. [→ D. J. Webb 2012](#d-j-webb-2012)

## Evidence

### D. J. Webb 2012

D. J. Webb. (2012). Improving student's problem-solving ability as well as conceptual understanding without sacrificing the physics content of a class. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression on FCI pre/post data comparing the ideas-first section with the standard-lecture section, with GPA and gender as the only significant control variables. The odds ratio was "4 .2 ± 1.7 ( p < 0.001)" for gaining at least 7 additional correct questions.

> "gaining at least an additional 7 correct questions on the FCI postest are 4 .2 ± 1.7 ( p < 0.001) times larger for a student who was in section I than for one who was in section IV."
