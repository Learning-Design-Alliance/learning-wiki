Evidence¶

Daumiller 2021¶

Daumiller, M., Janke, S., Hein, J., Rinas, R., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2021). Do teachers' achievement goals and self-efficacy beliefs matter for students' learning experiences? Evidence from two studies on perceived teaching quality and emotional experiences. Learning and Instruction. Advanced online publication. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2021.101458

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Study 1 latent multilevel analyses (166 teachers, 2,106 students) testing six goal types as teacher-level predictors of four student outcomes; the article reports no expected effects for any achievement goal. No effect sizes are printed for these null findings.

"Contrary to our expectations, our hypotheses for the achievement goals (H1a –f) were not supp orted by the data. We did not find the expected effects for any of the teachers' achievement goals."

Daumiller 2021 (2)¶

Daumiller, M., Janke, S., Hein, J., Rinas, R., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2021). Do teachers' achievement goals and self-efficacy beliefs matter for students' learning experiences? Evidence from two studies on perceived teaching quality and emotional experiences. Learning and Instruction. Advanced online publication. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2021.101458

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Same Study 1 models; the article reports one unexpected significant negative association between teachers' relational goals and students' overall course rating, with no effect size printed.

"Surprisingly, for re-lational goals, we even found an association that con-tradicted our hypotheses: relational goals were nega-tively associated with overall course rating."
