The guide's introduction explains that dropout prevention studies often examine multifaceted interventions whose components cannot be isolated. By contrast, Recommendations 2 (advocates) and 3 (engagement curricula) have studies providing direct tests, supporting their Moderate and Strong evidence ratings.

"However, Recommendations 2 and 3 are each supported by multiple studies that provide a direct test of the recommended practices, so there is stronger evidence of the effectiveness of those practices."
