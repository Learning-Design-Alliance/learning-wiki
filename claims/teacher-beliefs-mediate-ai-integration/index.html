  • Interview data link cluster differences in AI-TPACK integration to distinct profiles of AI-TK, technical self-efficacy, pedagogical beliefs, and support-dependence — related
  • SDT need satisfaction predicted post-intervention willingness beyond baseline capacity, while post-intervention TPACK showed only a weak, non-significant association with willingness — related
  • Among AI-TPACK dimensions, all except AI technological knowledge and integrative AI-TPACK are significantly and positively associated with college teachers' AI teaching innovation behavior — related
  • AI-TPACK positively predicts science teaching self-efficacy among Chinese pre-service science teachers — related
  • Professional identity and AI literacy partially mediate AI-TPACK associations with innovation behavior, while AI-TK and AI-TPACK show indirect-only mediation and TSE mediation is only partially supported — related
  • Additive TPACK–belief integrations produce construct overlap, causal ambiguity, and weak intervention guidance — related
  • AI-TPACK dimensions are significantly and positively associated with college teachers' professional identity and AI literacy — related
  • AI knowledge and attitudes toward AI did not significantly predict teachers' AI integration ability — related
  • Teachers' practical use of AI in science and green energy learning significantly predicts their AI integration ability (β = 0.391, p < 0.01, f² = 0.180) — related
  • AI applications in STEM education foster positive affective perception, including attitude, interest, and motivation — related
  • In the fitted model, teachers' knowledge and willingness to change predicted teaching practice, whereas professional beliefs did not significantly influence teaching practice — reports the opposite
  • AI literacy positively predicts AI-TPACK among Chinese pre-service science teachers — related
  • Content knowledge is not significantly associated with self-perceived knowledge in any technology-integrated GenAI domain — related
  • AI-TK contributes to AI-TPACK only indirectly through AI-TPK and AI-TCK, which positively predict AI-TPACK — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Technological knowledge of GenAI is strongly associated with self-perceived knowledge in technology-integrated domains — related
  • Teaching beliefs are negatively associated with AI-TCK and AI-TPK and show no significant effect on AI-TK, suggesting excessive teaching beliefs may impede AI-TPACK advancement — related
  • Expert perceptions reveal a developmental asymmetry in which content knowledge outweighs integrated pedagogical capacity in AI education — related
  • The four-predictor model explains approximately 55.4% of variance in teachers' AI integration ability — related
  • Engagement in data practices is associated with both cognitive and non-cognitive learning outcomes — related
  • Chinese MTES show medium-level AI-TPACK overall, with AI-TK the lowest-scoring component and teaching beliefs the highest, indicating a basic, preliminary stage — related
  • A developmental teaching approach, positive technology attitudes and expectations, and perceived principal support related to a high extent of technology integration — related
  • Affective characteristics — attitudes and beliefs, emotional responses, and sensitivity to technology gaps — should be taken into account when preparing preservice teachers for digital technology use — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Self-efficacy and attitudes towards technology integration predicted positive experiences in online distance teaching, jointly explaining 52% of variance. — related
