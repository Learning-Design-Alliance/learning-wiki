## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2020

Suzanne White Brahmia, Alexis Olsho, Trevor I. Smith, and Andrew Boudreaux. (2020). A framework for the natures of negativity in introductory physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010120)

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Same multiple-choice administration comparing positive and negative versions of the acceleration item ME1. The article reports an odds-ratio effect size of 0.74, "a statistically small effect size," with the negative version slightly more difficult; the authors suspect this relates to students associating negative acceleration with decreasing speed.

> "The effect size for item ME1 is 0.74, which is a statistically small effect size indicating that students ﬁnd the negative version of this ques- tion slightly more difﬁcult than the positive version"
