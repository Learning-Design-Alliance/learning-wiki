## Evidence

### Yakmaci-Guzel 2013

Yakmaci-Guzel, B., & Adadan, E. (2013). Use of multiple representations in developing preservice chemistry teachers' understanding of the structure of matter. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 8(1), 109-130. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008597

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-ranks comparisons in the same one-group study: pre to delayed posttest total was significant (z=2.79, p=0.005), but "no statistically significant difference was found between post and delayed posttest scores" (part a z=-0.71; part b z=-0.58; part c z=-0.80; total z=-1.20, all p>0.05).

> "no statistically significant difference was found between post and delayed posttest scores, which implies a long-lasting positive impact of the specific instruction"
