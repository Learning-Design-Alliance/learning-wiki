Summer Learning and Beyond: Opportunities for Creating Equity 2021¶

Summer Learning and Beyond: Opportunities for Creating Equity. (2021). Spencer Foundation & Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/summer-learning-creating-equity

q2 · i? · review · r1

Design Principle 1 section, a narrative research synthesis, states that "children learn more effectively when they feel secure" and that high anxiety, fear, or trauma impedes learning. No individual studies or effect sizes are printed.

"Children learn more effectively when they feel secure and when they have positive feelings about the people they are with and the content they are encountering. The brain shuts down, impeding learning, when there is a high level of anxiety, fear, or trauma."
