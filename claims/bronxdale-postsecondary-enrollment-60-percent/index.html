Descriptive comparison from NYC DOE data reported in the Postsecondary Enrollment section: Bronxdale's 60% enrollment rate versus borough 51%, comparison group 52%, and citywide 59%. The report notes the citywide average includes fewer economically disadvantaged students. No statistical test is reported.

"Bronxdale students' enrollment in college or other postsecondary programs within 6 months of graduation (60%) is noticeably better than that of their borough peers (51%) and their comparison group (52%), and slightly higher than the citywide average (59%)"
