## Evidence

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

General linear model on exam scores in the RCT (OLP N = 79, control N = 75) with the BMQ Benefits subscale as covariate. The article reports the significant interaction "∆OLP-control group = 1.57 ± 0.71%, t(153) = 2.21, p = 0.029, d = 0.18", a negligible effect size.

> "inclusion of the general beliefs about medicines questionnaire (BMQ) 'Benefits' subscale as a covariate revealed a significant interaction between group and treatment expectation (group × BMQ 'Benefits' score; ∆OLP-control group = 1.57 ± 0.71%, t(153) = 2.21, p = 0.029, d = 0.18)"
