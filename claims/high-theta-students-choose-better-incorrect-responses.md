Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interpretation of the 2PL-NRM trace-line plots (Fig. 3) for questions 14, 18, and 19, where better-than-common incorrect responses show narrow probability distributions peaking around theta = b and worst responses peak at low theta. This pattern is consistent with the article's ranking assumption.

> "Questions 14, 18, and 19 all indicate that students with above-average understanding ( θ >0) are more likely to choose both the correct response and the highest-ranked incorrect response than students with θ <0."
