David Spence Hill 1917¶

David Spence Hill. (1917). Standardized Illustrative Sentences for the Springfield Spelling List. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org

q2 · i? · design · r2

A documentary comparison of word lists: the author identifies substitutions such as organization for petrified and appreciation for appreciate in survey studies, and notes nine Springfield words absent from the final published thousand-word list, concluding such "rigid comparisons" are questionable.

"Rigid comparisons of the results of the Springfield, Butte, Salt Lake City, Des Moines results in. spelling with the results obtained from lists drawn from Ayres' published chart (3) of the "1000 words most commonly used in writing" are obviously questionable."
