Akengi̇n and Aydemir 2012¶

Hamza AKENGİN, Gökhan AYDEMİR. (2012). Effects of Using Case-Study Method in Social Studies on Students' Attitudes Towards Environment. International Electronic Journal of Environmental Education, Vol.2, Issue 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057493

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest–posttest control group quasi-experiment with 30 experimental and 30 control 6th-grade students; the post-test comparison favored the case-study group, "statistically significant ( t(58) =7.743, p<.01)". No effect size printed.

"the arithmetic mean of the post“attitude scores of the experimental group is 108,90, while it is 78,63 for the control group. These data show that the average of the post“attitude scores of the experimental group is higher than the scores of control group. This difference between two group s is statistically significant ( t(58) =7.743, p<.01)."
