## Evidence

### Bathgate M. E. 2019

Bathgate M. E., Aragón O. R., Cavanagh A. J., Frederick J., Graham M. J. (2019). Supports: A Key Factor in Faculty Implementation of Evidence-Based Teaching. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0272

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis reran models 2 and 3 with personal teaching preference and student receptivity items removed from supports/barriers. Adjusted supports retained the largest relationship (β = 0.35***); adjusted R2 fell to 13% versus 27% in model 3.

> "Model 5 shows growth mindset and confidence still have a significant relationship with reported EBT when supports and barriers are included in the model, although their effect is somewhat attenuated."
