Evidence¶

Zhang J and Yin Y 2026¶

Zhang J and Yin Y (2026). A study on AI-enabled course development, AI proficiency, and first-year students' academic and psychological adjustment. Front. Psychol. 17:1909803. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1909803

q2 · i? · associational · r2

A moderated mediation model (PROCESS Model 14) in the 551-student survey showed the proficiency–adjustment interaction with AI usage pressure was negative and significant (B = −0.11, p = 0.004). Conditional indirect effects fell from 0.161 (low pressure) to 0.088 (high pressure); the article reports "The moderated mediation index was −0.051 (95% CI [−0.095, −0.017]), which does not include zero."

"The moderated mediation index was −0.051 (95% CI [−0.095, −0.017]), which does not include zero, supporting H6: the indirect association between course development satisfaction and self-reported psychological adjustment through AI proficiency was significantly attenuated by AI usage pressure"
