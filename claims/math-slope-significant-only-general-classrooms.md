## Evidence

### Szumski Grzegorz 2022

Szumski Grzegorz, Smogorzewska Joanna, Grygiel Paweł. (2022). Academic achievement of students without special educational needs and disabilities in inclusive education–Does the type of inclusion matter? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent growth curve model without covariants for mathematics in the Polish longitudinal sample. The article reports the general-classroom slope "Est = 0.19, SE = 0.04, p = .0001" while "Results of students from both forms of inclusive setting did not change significantly over time".

> "Students from general education classrooms significantly improved their results with time (Est = 0.19, SE = 0.04, p = .0001). Results of students from both forms of inclusive setting did not change significantly over time"
