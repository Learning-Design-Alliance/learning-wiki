Steinbach, S. (2022). Looking beyond vision: Supports for students who are blind or visually impaired in mathematics. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/looking-beyond-vision-supports-for-students-who-are-blind-or-visually-impaired-in-mathematics/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports preliminary results from Ferrell et al. (2017) on auditory image descriptions in assessments for students with print disabilities: braille readers with both "voiced auditory descriptions and tactile graphics" increased their likelihood of correct answers, while tactile-only access decreased it. No effect size printed.

> "braille readers who had access to both the voiced auditory descriptions and tactile graphics increased their likelihood of answering items correctly; braille users who only had access to tactile images actually had a decreased likelihood of finding the right solution."
