## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2015

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Ruth Harmsen. (2015). Animated pedagogical agents effects on enhancing student motivation and learning in a science inquiry learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9378-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA in the same 61-student experiment; the omnibus three-way interaction on self-efficacy was significant and localized to the during-training interval. Figures 3 and 4 show boys' self-efficacy decreased in the experimental conditions but rose in control, while "For girls, the pattern was the opposite."

> "In addition, for self-efﬁcacy there was a statistically signiﬁcant interaction between time, condition, and gender, F(6,108) = 2.20, p = 0.048. Detailed analyses revealed that this interaction took place during training (SE during-1 to SE during-2), F(2,55) = 4.45, p = 0.016."
