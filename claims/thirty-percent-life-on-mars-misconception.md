Descriptive result from the six-item true-false survey of approximately 1,000 adults at the Franklin Institute. Participants discussing the item raised "evidence for": "bacteria", "single-celled organisms", "fossils", and "water"; some were adamant life had been found.

> "Thirty percent (± 1%) of participants responded that scientists have found life on Mars. This particular question seemed to cause the most confusion and sparked the many discussions and arguments."
