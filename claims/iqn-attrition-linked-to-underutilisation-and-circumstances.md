Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2026). Optimising the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Insights From Recruiting Managers' Perspectives. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70496

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of attrition responses (n = 26 codes) found personal/family/visa/location factors coded 16 times (61%), lack of recognition 6 times (23%) and support/utilisation 6 times (23%).

### Kurup Chanchal 2026 (2)

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2026). Optimising the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Insights From Recruiting Managers' Perspectives. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70496

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Managers described proactive support networks, mentorship and institutional plans as mitigating attrition; one suggested every hospital develop an individual plan to assess, train and retain IQNs.

> "Managers emphasised that retention could be improved by creating supportive environments and systematic utilisation of IQNs' skills."
