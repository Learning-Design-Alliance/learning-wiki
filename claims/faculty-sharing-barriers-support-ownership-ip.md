Figure 2 summarizes survey responses on reasons why faculty members are unwilling to share their e-learning resources, with time pressures cited by 11 respondents (12.2%). The discussion states institutional support "was also the most frequently selected reason for faculty reluctance to share e-learning resources" and that the ownership and intellectual property concerns "calls for institutional policies to support medical faculties to resolve such issues."

> "Thirteen respondents (14.4%) cited lack of institutional support for creating resources, and 11 (12.2%) responded time pressures restricted the development of such resources.
