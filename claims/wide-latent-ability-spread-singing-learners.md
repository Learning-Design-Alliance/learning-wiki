`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IRT parameter estimation (Figure 5) at the sequence level: one ability (theta) was fitted per learner–score practice sequence, 2,432 sequences from 1,074 learners, so a learner who practised several scores contributes several estimates and the spread is not one estimate per distinct learner. Median standard error 0.157 from the log-likelihood curvature. Pitch difficulties had a much narrower spread (mean −0.74, SD 0.38).
