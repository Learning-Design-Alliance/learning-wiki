## Evidence

### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). Helping Babies Breathe, Second Edition: A Model for Strengthening Educational Programs to Increase Global Newborn Survival. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-18-00147

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field trial in August 2016 at Kabala District Health Management Facility with 24 participants without prior HBB exposure, trained in 2 stages; 4 first-stage participants became master trainers who trained the remaining 12.

> "Observations of AAP master trainers showed that these new master trainers from Sierra Leone reverted back to lecturing rather than facilitating discussion about the newly included topics of quality improvement"

### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018 (2)

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). Helping Babies Breathe, Second Edition: A Model for Strengthening Educational Programs to Increase Global Newborn Survival. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-18-00147

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

From written evaluations and focus group discussions following the two-stage training; participants valued hands-on practice, small-group discussion, and facilitator interaction.

> "New master trainers felt comfortable using the materials to impart the knowledge and skills to their learners, but they felt that giving feedback was a skill they needed to improve"
