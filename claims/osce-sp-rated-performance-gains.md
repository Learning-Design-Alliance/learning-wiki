## Evidence

### Liu Mei-Yuan 2025

Liu Mei-Yuan, Liao Li-Ling, Huang Yu-Ting, Lee Yi-Chen, Lai I-Ju. (2025). Effectiveness of a scenario-based simulation course on improving the clinical communication skills of dietetic students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06684-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Standardized-patient feedback forms (four questions each, Likert 1–5) completed for the experimental group's OSCE (n = 58). SP ratings for "both nutrition assessment and nutrition education" were significantly higher at post-test, p < 0.001 for all questions.

> "According to the SPs' assessment of students' OSCE performance, the post-test scores for both nutrition assessment and nutrition education, each consisting of four questions, were significantly higher than the pre-test scores (p < 0.001) (Fig. 3)."
