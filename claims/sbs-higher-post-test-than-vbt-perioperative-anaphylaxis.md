## Evidence

### Yang Xiuli 2026

Yang Xiuli, Jiang Hui, Wang Huan, Wang Lixia, Chen Lijian. (2026). Effect of scenario-based simulation on students’ learning outcomes and satisfaction in perioperative anaphylaxis: a pilot randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2025-003713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomised controlled trial comparing SBS (n=42) and VBT (n=42) among fourth-year anaesthesia undergraduates; the post-test was a 20-question multiple-choice test. The SBS group scored higher, with "80.0 (73.7–85.0) vs 70.0 (65.0-75.0), respectively, p<0.001".

> "After the training session, the post-test score of the SBS group was higher than that of the VBT group (80.0 (73.7–85.0) vs 70.0 (65.0-75.0), respectively, p<0.001)."
