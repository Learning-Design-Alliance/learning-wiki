Scarff Catherine E, Bearman Margaret, Chiavaroli Neville, Trumble Stephen. (2023). Assessor discomfort and failure to fail in clinical performance assessments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04688-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the 20-item PADS in the dermatology assessor survey (overall mean PADS 2.45); Table 1 lists item means from 1.21 to 3.71. Descriptive means only; no inferential effect size printed.

> "The item with the lowest level of discomfort (mean = 1.21) was 'conducting a feedback session with a trainee who is performing well' (n = 44, 83% reported 'no discomfort'). The item with the highest level of discomfort reported (mean = 3.71) was warning a trainee that the assessor would support their removal from the training program unless performance improved"
