## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired-sample comparisons of conflict handling styles before and after training found no statistically significant difference between experimental and control groups on the Thomas-Kilmann MODE subscales. [→ Abel Gitimu Waithaka 2015](#abel-gitimu-waithaka-2015)

## Evidence

### Abel Gitimu Waithaka 2015

Abel Gitimu Waithaka, Shante' Moore-Austin, Priscilla N. Gitimu. (2015). Influence of conflict resolution training on conflict handling styles of college students. Research in Higher Education Journal, 28. http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test control-group study at a Mid-Western public university; 133 experimental and 135 control participants post-tested. Paired samples t-tests on MODE subscales showed, e.g., "No significant difference from precollaborating to postcollaborating was found ( t(130) = 1.029, p>.05)."

> "The results showed there is no statistically significant difference in conflict handling styles   between experimental and control groups as reflected by the pre-test and post-test for  conflict resolution after training on conflict handling."
