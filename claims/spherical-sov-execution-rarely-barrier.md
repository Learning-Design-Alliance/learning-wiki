## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Upper-division student difficulties with Separation of Variables. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020131)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observational analysis of spherical SoV exam solutions: "only a small fraction of students (8%, N=43 of 509) had diﬃculties only with elements of the Execution component". The article also reports most solutions used term matching (89%, N=405 of 455) rather than Fourier's trick (12%, N=56 of 455).

> "Ultimately, only a small fraction of students (8%, N=43 of 509) had diﬃculties only with elements of the Execution component (i.e., no mistakes in Activation or Construction)."
