Introducing virtual microscopy at the University of Ghana during the COVID‐19 pandemic—Identifying and overcoming obstacles in a low‐resource learning environment. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70276

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Likert-scale survey of 322 VM users (Table 4); e.g., 90.7% agreed VM saves time and 94.4% agreed VM helps solidify understanding. Descriptive percentages, no inferential test.

### Koney Nii Koney‐Kwaku 2026 (2)

Koney Nii Koney‐Kwaku, Omenako Smart Nana, Adjei David Nana, Arko‐Boham Benjamin, Hortsch Michael. (2026). Introducing virtual microscopy at the University of Ghana during the COVID‐19 pandemic—Identifying and overcoming obstacles in a low‐resource learning environment. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70276

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Table 4 item: 37.3% disagreed, 8.7% strongly disagreed, and 24.2% were unsure that VM can completely replace traditional microscopy. Descriptive result, no test.

> "However, when asked whether VM could completely replace traditional microscopy for histology instruction, more than 70% of VM student users were either unsure or disagreed."
