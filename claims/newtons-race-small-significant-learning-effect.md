## Evidence

### Anne van der Linden 2022

Anne van der Linden, Ralph F. G. Meulenbroeks and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2022). Learning Newtonian mechanics with an intrinsically integrated educational game. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627)

`q3 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pre/posttest comparison of five matched Force-Concept-Inventory-based questions among 223 museum-recruited participants aged 10-15 after 15 minutes of gameplay. Pretest M = 1.66, posttest M = 1.87; the article prints the effect size d = 0.201 (printed as d = .201) for this finding and reports that a significant difference was found.

> "A significant difference was found; t(222) = 3.000, p = .003; d = .201."
