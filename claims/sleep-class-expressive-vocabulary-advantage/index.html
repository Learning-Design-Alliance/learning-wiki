q2 · i? · associational · r2

Linear regression of school readiness on class membership with covariates (Table 3, column 2), in a secondary analysis of 351 low-income pre-kindergarteners. The article reports "higher WJ-III Picture Vocabulary scores, indicating stronger expressive vocabulary (β=2.75, p<0.1)" for the earlier, longer, consistent class.

"children classified in the earlier, longer, consistent sleep health class had higher WJ-III Picture Vocabulary scores, indicating stronger expressive vocabulary (β=2.75, p<0.1) than children classified in the later, shorter, inconsistent sleep health class."
