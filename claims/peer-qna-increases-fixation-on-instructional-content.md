## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Normalized total fixation duration on the mainboard and teacher was significantly higher in Peer-QnA than Peer-NoQnA for the Double-Slit Experiment topic. [→ Süleyman Özdel 2025](#suleyman-ozdel-2025)

## Evidence

### Süleyman Özdel 2025

Süleyman Özdel, Can Sarpkaya, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Examining the Role of LLM-Driven Interactions on Attention and Cognitive Engagement in Virtual Classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Eye-tracking analysis in the within-subjects VR experiment (Double-Slit topic); normalized fixation duration on the mainboard and teacher, the designated key areas of interest, was "significantly higher in the Peer-QnA condition" (t-test p = .02).

> "The normalized total fixation duration is significantly higher in the Peer-QnA condition ( M = 0 .72, SD= 0 .11) compared to the Peer-NoQnA condition ( M = 0.60, SD= 0.08)."
