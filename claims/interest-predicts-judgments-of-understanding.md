`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis in the 120-participant study: "ratings of interest were found to be the only significant predictor of the composite predictions". The article reports R2 = .13 but no standardized coefficients, so no effect size is coded.

> "three predictors (ratings of interest, ratings of helpfulness, and actual learning outcome composites) were entered into a simultaneous regression, R2 = .13, F (3, 116) = 5.86, p < .001.  As shown in Table 2, ratings of interest were found to be the only significant predictor of the composite predictions."
