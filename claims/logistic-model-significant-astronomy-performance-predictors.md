### Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly 2023

Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly. (2023). Predicting Community College Astronomy Performance through Logistic Regression. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on transcript data from 1909 community college astronomy students (2015-2019), predicting pass (C or better) vs. DFW. The model showed "correctly classifying 71.2% of the cases, with a Nagelkerke R2 of 0.104"; the Hosmer-Lemeshow test indicated good fit (c2=12.117, p=0.146).

> "The Pearson chi-square value (c2=144.229, p<0.001) indicated the logistic model was significant, correctly classifying 71.2% of the cases, with a Nagelkerke R2 of 0.104, a medium effect size."
