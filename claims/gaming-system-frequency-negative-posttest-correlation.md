# The frequency of gaming the system is the only off-task behavior significantly negatively correlated with post-test score in cognitive tutor classrooms (r = -0.38).

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Frequency of gaming the system was significantly correlated with post-test score, r = -0.38, and remained significant controlling for pre-test and general academic achievement, partial correlation = -0.34. [→ Baker et al. 2004](#baker-et-al-2004)
`q3 i?` Other off-task behaviors were not significantly correlated with post-test: talking off-task r = -0.19 (p = 0.12), inactivity r = -0.08, off-task solitary behavior r = -0.08. [→ Baker et al. 2004 (2)](#baker-et-al-2004-2)

## Evidence

### Baker et al. 2004

Ryan Shaun Baker, Albert T. Corbett, Kenneth R. Koedinger, Angela Z. Wagner. (2004). Off-Task Behavior in the Cognitive Tutor Classroom: When Students "Game the System". CHI 2004, Vienna, Austria. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190176v1

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Observational study of 70 students across 5 middle-school classrooms using a cognitive tutor, with 563 20-second behavior observations. The correlation of gaming frequency with post-test was r = -0.38, "the only off-task behavior which was significantly correlated with the post-test".

> "The frequency of gaming the system was the only off-task behavior which was significantly correlated with the post-test, F(1,68)=11.82, p<0.01, r= -0.38."

### Baker et al. 2004 (2)

Ryan Shaun Baker, Albert T. Corbett, Kenneth R. Koedinger, Angela Z. Wagner. (2004). Off-Task Behavior in the Cognitive Tutor Classroom: When Students "Game the System". CHI 2004, Vienna, Austria. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190176v1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same observational study. Inactivity and off-task solitary behavior showed no significant correlation with post-test scores (r = -0.08 each); talking off-task was at best marginal (r = -0.19, p = 0.12). No effect sizes beyond the printed correlations were reported.

> "the frequencies of inactivity (F(1,68)=0.44, p=0.51, r=-0.08) and off-task solitary behavior (F(1,68)=0.42, p=0.52,r=-0.08) were not significantly correlated to post-test scores."
