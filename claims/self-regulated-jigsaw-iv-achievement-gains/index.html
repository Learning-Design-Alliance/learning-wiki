Evidence¶

Esin Özdemir 2016¶

Esin Özdemir, Ali Arslan. (2016). The Effect of Self-regulated Jigsaw IV on University Students' Academic Achievements and Attitudes towards English Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i5.1453

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group pre-test/post-test study with 40 university EFL prep students (n=20 per group); independent samples t-test on gain scores gave t = 6.64, p = 0.019, with experimental gain mean 26.00 versus control 14.15. The finding that "the experimental group students' achievement gain scores are significantly higher" supports the claim.

"According to Table 2, from the point of achievement gain scores; the experimental group students' achievement gain scores are significantly higher than those of control group students'."
