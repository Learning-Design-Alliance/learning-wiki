## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2016

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2016). Predicting Performance in Higher Education Using Proximal Predictors. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153663

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 851 applicants to a Dutch undergraduate psychology program, 652 of whom enrolled, in a high-stakes admission procedure. Correlational results in Table 2 show "The trial-studying test was the best predictor for all performance measures" with r = .49 for FYMG and r = -.32 for dropout.

> "The trial-studying test was the best predictor for all performance measures and showed a large effect size for FYMG (r = .49) and moderate effect sizes for obtained credits and dropout (r = .39 and r = -.32)."
