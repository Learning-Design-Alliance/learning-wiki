Evidence¶

Nicole Shechtman 2019¶

Nicole Shechtman, Jeremy Roschelle, Mingyu Feng, Corinne Singleton. (2019). An Efficacy Study of a Digital Core Curriculum for Grade 5 Mathematics. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419850482

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' discussion interpretation of the null main effect, citing treatment-condition variation in coverage of learning objectives and frequent teacher complaints that students were not experiencing all tested material. The article notes Burris, Heubert, and Levin addressed a similar pace tension in Accelerated Math and suggests RM-CC5's pacing for struggling students may need further adjustment.

"If too much time is spent on mastering topics early in the curricular sequence, the lack of coverage of later topics may interfere with raising achievement on accountability measures such as the WVGSA; assessments like these are designed to gauge progress on curricular expectations across all the topics in a school year."
