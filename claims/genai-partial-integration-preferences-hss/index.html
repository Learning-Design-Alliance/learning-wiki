Training preferences (Figure 8c) indicate "a clear demand for practice-oriented guidance rather than theoretical instruction"; on governance, 68.09% favored clear usage guidelines and 22.51% supported a complete ban.

"in-depth practical courses were most strongly preferred (71.04%), followed by basic introductory courses (61.86%) and online formats (47.76%), while lecture -based approaches were viewed less favorably."
