## Evidence

### J. C. B 1917

J. C. B. (1917). Recent Publications in History. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Horn's investigation examined twenty-seven books and thirty-eight topics of current political, social and economic interest (lists built by Iowa department heads) for dates, periods, phases and persons. The review notes "only twenty-four per cent, fell within that period" for elementary textbooks.

> "More than eighty-five per cent, of the references fell within the period 1861 to 1916, while in the case of the elementary text-books examined only twenty-four per cent, fell within that period."

### J. C. B 1917 (2)

J. C. B. (1917). Recent Publications in History. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the same examination of modern-problems books, eighteen per cent of references dealt with political history, four per cent military, and seventy-eight per cent social and economic, while "the texts showed forty-two per cent, political, forty per cent, military, and eighteen per cent, social and economic."

> "the texts showed forty-two per cent, political, forty per cent, military, and eighteen per cent, social and economic."
