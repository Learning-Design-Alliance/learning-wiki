`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of one dual-moderator focus group of six CHRS students held in April 2022. The article reports "four participants shared that they had been experiencing various communication problems"; no effect size applies.

> "On the second sub-theme of communication patterns after the pandemic, four participants shared that they had been experiencing various communication problems, including initiating speech, nervousness, and lack of confidence in resuming back to regular classes."
