## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant effects of vision or haptics in the first showroom were found on duration of stay, posters read, situational interest, or knowledge acquisition concerning the second showroom, either immediately or at follow-up. [→ Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021](#magdalena-novak-stephan-schwan-2021)

## Evidence

### Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021

Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan. (2021). Does Touching Real Objects Affect Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09551-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the 20-item knowledge test about showroom 2 (M = 14.33, SD = 3.23 of 20) controlling for prior knowledge, interest, and posters read showed "no significant main effects for vision" and haptics; duration of stay, posters read, situational interest, and follow-up knowledge likewise showed no significant effects.

> "an ANCOV A showed no significant main effects for vision, F(1,156) = 0.01, p =. 9 1 2 ,ηp2 < 0.01, and haptics,F(1,156) = 0.45,p = 0.502, ηp2 < 0.01, and no significant interaction between those factors"
