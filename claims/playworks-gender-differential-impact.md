Martha Bleeker, Nicholas Beyler, Susanne James-Burdumy, Jane Fortson. (2015). The Impact of Playworks on Boys' and Girls' Physical Activity During Recess. Journal of School Health, vol. 85, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impact-of-playworks-on-boys-and-girls-physical-activity-during-recess

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Summary finding of the 29-school randomized evaluation, in which impacts were estimated separately for girls and boys using regression models on accelerometer and recess-observation data. The article reports the gender-differential pattern as its overall conclusion, with no printed effect sizes.
