## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The predictability effect on CNV amplitude preceding targets and on target-P3 latency each correlated with the predictability benefit in reaction time. [→ Thillay A 2015](#thillay-a-2015)

## Evidence

### Thillay A 2015

Thillay A, Roux S, Gissot V, Carteau-Martin I, Knight RT, Bonnet-Brilhault F and Bidet-Caulet A (2015) Sustained attention and prediction: distinct brain maturation trajectories during adolescence. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 9:519. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2015.00519

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analyses in the 36-participant sample related ERP predictability effects to the behavioral RT benefit. The study also reports the P3 latency predictability effect correlated with the RT benefit ("r = 0.519, b = 0.632, p = 0.001"), supporting CNV and P3 responses as markers of prediction abilities.

> "Predictability effect on the amplitude of the CNV preceding targets (difference in CNV amplitude between randTs and predTs) at Cz was correlated with the predictability benefit in reaction time (difference in reaction time between randTs and predTs; r = 0.547, b = 0.009, p = 0.001)."
