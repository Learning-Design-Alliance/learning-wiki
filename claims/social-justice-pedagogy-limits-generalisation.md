## Evidence

### Gerdin Göran 2020

Gerdin Göran, Larsson Lena, Schenker Katarina, Linnér Susanne, Mordal Moen Kjersti, Westlie Knut, Smith Wayne, Philpot Rod. (2020). Social Justice Pedagogies in School Health and Physical Education—Building Relationships, Teaching for Social Cohesion and Addressing Social Inequities. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17186904

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' own stated limitation in the Discussion. They note teachers were chosen as examples likely to be inclusive and reflective, so "we cannot make any generalization or claims about the practices of all HPE teachers". The study also did not include students' experiences.

> "One limitation of this study is the small sample size coupled with the fact that we selected the participant teachers through purposive rather than, for instance, random sampling [42]."
