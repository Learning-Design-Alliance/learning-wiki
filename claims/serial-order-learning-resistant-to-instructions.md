## Evidence

### Teodóra Vékony 2022

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on standardized serial-order-based learning scores (random high-probability minus pattern high-probability trials) in the Different Instruction Phase; the printed effect size was ηp2 = 0.003, negligible, with no Epoch effect and a non-significant Epoch × Group interaction indicating similar learning trajectories.

> "The two groups showed similar level of serial-order-based learning in the Different Instruction Phase [M Accuracy Group = 0.03 ± 0.01 SE, M Speed Group = 0.02 ± 0.01 SE, main effect of Group: F(1, 46) = 0.12, p = 0.74, ηp2 = 0.003, BFexclusion = 7.38]."

### Teodóra Vékony 2022 (2)

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U test on standardized serial-order-based learning scores in the Same Instruction Phase; the printed effect size rRB = 0.03 is negligible and the group difference was not significant.

> "Similar to the results of the Different Instruction Phase, no significant difference was found between the two groups in the Same Instruction Phase, U = 295, p = 0.86, rRB = 0.03, BF01 = 3.49"
