`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Year-based Chi-square comparisons of the two chatbots across ten DUS exam years, illustrated in Figure 1. The authors interpret the stable performance as suggesting model accuracy does not decline over time, possibly related to exam difficulty fluctuations and ongoing model updates.

> "Nevertheless, no statistically significant difference was found in year-based performance comparisons (2012: p = 0.898; 2013: p = 0.716; 2014: p = 0.613; 2015: p = 0.082; 2016: p = 0.855; 2017: p = 0.440; 2018: p = 0.753; 2019: p = 0.204; 2020: p = 0.723; 2021: p = 0.722)."
