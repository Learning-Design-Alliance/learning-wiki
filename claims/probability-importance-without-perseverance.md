## Evidence

### Pale 2016

Joseph W. Pale. (2016). Teacher and Student Based Instructions on Probability Achievement Outcomes and Attitudes of Secondary School Students in Bungoma North, Kenya. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1112852

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Written open-response items on importance of probability, career desire and perseverance, collected from both groups. The article reports 90 (45%) student-based vs 82 (41%) teacher-based students agreed probability is important, and a third wanted a job using probability.

> "These results indicate that even when students over whelmingly view probability as important, only abou t half of them were willing to work along time in order to un derstand a new idea in probability or to get a solu tion to a probability problem."
