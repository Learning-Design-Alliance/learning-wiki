## Evidence

### Star 2013

Star, J. R., Rittle-Johnson, B., Durkin, K., Newton, K., Pollack, C., Lynch, K., & Gogolen, C. (2013). The Impact of a Comparison Curriculum in Algebra I: A Randomized Experiment. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564086

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel models with quality (mean score on treatment video coding of important lesson features, out of 6) as Level 2 predictor. Average quality was "4.55 important features in their lessons (SD = 0.94)."

> "Quality did not significantly predict procedural or flexibility knowledge (β = 4.88, t = 1.81, p = 0.078 and β = 3.34, t = 1.77, p = 0.085, respectively), but it marginally predicted conceptual knowledge (β = 3.51, t = 1.97, p = 0.057)."
