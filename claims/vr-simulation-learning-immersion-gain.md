## Evidence

### Kim Kyeng-Jin 2025

Kim Kyeng-Jin, Choi Moon-Ji, Kim MinJi. (2025). Effects of virtual reality-based disaster simulation education on nursing students. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0329563

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Measured with a 10-item, 5-point learning immersion scale (Cronbach's alpha .71 in this study) in the same quasi-experiment (n=67). The post-pre difference was "0.88 ± 0.42 in the experimental group and 0.17 ± 0.43 in the control group", t = 6.88, p < .001. No effect size printed.

> "Learning immersion increased from 3.51 ± 0.33 to 4.35 ± 0.29 in the experimental group and from 3.63 ± 0.26 to 3.85 ± 0.35 in the control group. The post-pre difference was 0.88 ± 0.42 in the experimental group and 0.17 ± 0.43 in the control group, with a statistically significant difference between two groups (t = 6.88, p < .001)."
