Evidence¶

Sibieta 2016¶

Sibieta, L., Kotecha, M., & Skipp, A. (2016). Nuffield Early Language Intervention: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects/language-for-learning/

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Randomised controlled trial with child-level randomisation within 34 schools' nurseries; 350 pupils in the final common sample. The summary impact table reports an effect size of "0.27 (0.07; 0.46)" for the 30-week group versus untreated control, estimated as "4 months" of additional progress; the confidence interval excludes zero.

"30-week vs. untreated control 0.27 (0.07; 0.46) 4 months"

Related Claims¶

  • The 20-week version of NELI improved language skills by an estimated two months of progress, though the evidence is less secure than for the 30-week version — related
  • Follow-up testing six months after the programmes ended suggested the language impact may have increased over time, but this could not be established with confidence — related
  • Staff in participating schools reported improvements in children's spoken language, listening, narrative skills, vocabulary and confidence, attributing them to the small-group format and engaging content — related
  • Neither the 30-week nor the 20-week version of NELI showed reliable short-term impact on children's word-level literacy skills — related
