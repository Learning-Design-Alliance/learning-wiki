Post-hoc pairwise analysis of the BDI test objective (Q1, Q12, Q18) found only the PGY1-3 vs Attending comparison reached significance (p < 0.001); PGY1-3 answered 53% correct versus attendings' 79%.

> "PGY1-3 and PGY4-5's correctly identified BDI 53% and 67% of the time. PGY6-8/Fellows answered 70% of BDI questions correctly, while attendings identified BDI 79% of the time"
