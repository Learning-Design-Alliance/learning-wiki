## Evidence

### Wandong Xu 2024

Wandong Xu, Xinhua Zhu. (2024). Examining metacognitive strategy use in L1 and L2 task-situated writing: effects, transferability, and cross-language facilitation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09387-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of questionnaire and writing-score data from 502 Chinese EFL undergraduates found direct positive paths from metacognitive strategies to writing performance in both languages, with "β = 0.32, p < 0.001 in the L1 context; β = 0.41, p < 0.001 in the L2 context".

> "SEM results indicated that L1 and L2 writing performance were directly and positively influenced by corresponding metacognitive strategies ( β = 0.32, p < 0.001 in the L1 context; β = 0.41, p < 0.001 in the L2 context)."
