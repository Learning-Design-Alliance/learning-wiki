## Evidence

### Boonyapison 2025

Boonyapison, K., Sittironnarit, G., & Rattanaumpawan, P. (2025). Association between the big five personalities and academic performance among grade 12 students at international high school in Thailand. Scientific Reports. [doi:10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-01038-7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate comparison of daily activities between the AA- and AN-groups in the questionnaire study found "significantly more likely to spend more than two hours on self-studying (23.0% vs. 11.1%; p-value = 0.03)". Social media use (p = 0.31), sleep, meals and journey time were comparable.

> "The respondents in the AA-group were significantly more likely to spend more than two hours on self-studying (23.0% vs. 11.1%; p-value = 0.03) and significantly less likely to spend more than two hours on physical activities (9.9% vs. 16.2%; p-value = 0.004)."
