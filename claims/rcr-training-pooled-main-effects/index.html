Johannes Katsarov 2021¶

Johannes Katsarov, Roberto Andorno, André Krom, Mariëtte van den Hoven. (2021). Effective Strategies for Research Integrity Training—a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:935–955. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09630-9

Random-effects main-effects analysis (Table 2) of 75 effect sizes from 30 studies with N = 2,601; the pooled mean Hedges' g of 0.52 is a printed effect size of medium magnitude. Outcome-specific rows print knowledge Mg = 0.64, judgment Mg = 0.41, behavior Mg = 0.69, sensitivity Mg = 0.42.
