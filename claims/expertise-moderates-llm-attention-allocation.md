### Elahe Oveisi and Hemanth Manjunatha 2026

Elahe Oveisi and Hemanth Manjunatha. (2026). LLM-Mediated Human–AI Interaction in Search and Rescue: Impact of Expertise on Attentional Allocation. Accepted for publication at IEEE SMC 2026. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.19514](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.19514)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Run-level eye-tracking mixed-effects analysis of expertise interactions showed "experts maintained greater engagement with the environment than novices" (LLM × expertise: β= 0.11, q= 0.004), with stronger LLM-related fixation (β= 0.82) and saccade (β= 0.96) interaction effects.

> "experts maintained greater engagement with the environment than novices (LLM × expertise: β= 0.11 , SE = 0.035, q= 0.004 ) and exhibited stronger LLM-related changes in visual exploration, as reflected in fixation ( β= 0.82 ) and saccade ( β= 0.96 ) interaction effects."
