## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Achievement status (high-achieving vs. typical-achieving GPA) significantly moderated the convergent thinking–GPA relationship (β = 0.99, t = 2.36, p = .02), with a stronger positive association for high achievers. [→ Abdulla Alabbasi Ahmed M. 2026](#abdulla-alabbasi-ahmed-m-2026)

## Evidence

### Abdulla Alabbasi Ahmed M. 2026

Abdulla Alabbasi Ahmed M., Alansari Ahmed Mohamed, Deifalla Abdelhalim, Runco Mark A., Sequeira Reginald P., Alotaibi Ghazi A., AlSaleh Aseel. (2026). Convergent thinking, divergent thinking, and openness to experience as predictors of academic success among high-achieving and typical-achieving medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08850-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS macro moderation analysis in SPSS with 274 MD students; 83 high achievers (GPA ≥ 90%) and 191 typical achievers. The printed interaction was β = 0.99, t = 2.36, p = .02, with the association "stronger for the high-achieving GPA-based sample".

> "achievement status significantly moderated the relationship between convergent thinking and GPA (β = 0.99, t = 2.36, p = .02). Specifically, the positive association between convergent thinking and GPA was stronger for the high-achieving GPA-based sample"
