## Evidence

### Lukics Krisztina Sára 2022

Lukics Krisztina Sára, Lukács Ágnes. (2022). Modality, presentation, domain and training effects in statistical learning. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-24951-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc comparison following the significant Modality*Domain interaction in the serial-presentation ANOVA of the 360-participant experiment. "performance in the linguistic domain was significantly higher than in the nonlinguistic domain" in the auditory modality (r = 0.54); the visual-modality contrast was not significant (r = 0.10).

> "Post hoc analyses showed that in the auditory modality, performance in the linguistic domain was significantly higher than in the nonlinguistic domain, t(118) = -6.95, p < 0.001, r = 0.54, BF10 = 3.761e+7."
