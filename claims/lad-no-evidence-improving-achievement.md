### Rogers Kaliisa 2023

Rogers Kaliisa, Kamila Misiejuk, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Mohammad Khalil, Mohammed Saqr. (2023). Have Learning Analytics Dashboards Lived Up to the Hype? A Systematic Review of Impact on Students' Achievement, Motivation, Participation and Attitude. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://laka.ub.uni-siegen.de/index.php/laka/article/view/23

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 38 studies synthesizing effect sizes for LAD impact on achievement. The review reports "negligible (i.e., marginal), 14 studies (41.2%), or small effect size, 12 studies (35.3%)" on performance outcomes. No meta-analysis was possible due to incongruent setups.

> "Most of the studies in our review focusing on the impact of LADs on students’ performance had either negligible (i.e., marginal), 14 studies (41.2%), or small effect size, 12 studies (35.3%), totalling 26 studies (76.5%)"
