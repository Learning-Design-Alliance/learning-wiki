## Evidence

### Sundstrom 2024

Sundstrom, M., Simpfendoerfer, L. N., Tan, A., Heim, A. B., & Holmes, N. G. (2024). Who and what gets recognized in peer recognition. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exponential random graph model (ERGM) analysis of four recognition networks from two offerings each of a distinct lab and lecture course at Cornell University; the man→woman coefficient was negative relative to the man→man base term, indicating that "men disproportionately under-nominate women as strong in the course material".

> "Additionally, in both offerings of both courses men disproportionately under-nominate women as strong in the course material as compared to men nominating men (pink dots on both panels of Fig. 5)."
