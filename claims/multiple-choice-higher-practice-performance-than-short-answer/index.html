Maass 2016¶

Maass, J.K. and Pavlik Jr., P.I. (2016). Modeling the Influence of Format and Depth during Effortful Retrieval Practice. Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Educational Data Mining. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592715

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Between-subjects ANOVA on average practice performance in the 2x2 MTurk experiment (n = 178): F(3,174) = 28.49, p<.001. Post hoc comparisons showed only Applied MC and Factual SA did not differ (p = .19); all other pairs differed significantly (all p's < .05).

"A between-subjects Analysis of Variance ( ANOVA) indicated significant differences between the four conditions, F (3,174) = 28.49, p<.001."
