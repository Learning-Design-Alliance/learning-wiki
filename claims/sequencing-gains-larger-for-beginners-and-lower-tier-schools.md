### Angel Tsai-Hsuan Chung 2026

Angel Tsai-Hsuan Chung, Botong Zhang, Ling-Chieh Kung, Hamsa Bastani, & Osbert Bastani. (2026). Effective Personalized AI Tutors via LLM-Guided Reinforcement Learning. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.6423358](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6423358)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Subgroup analysis within the field RCT using baseline-survey self-reports of prior Python skill. The printed effect size for beginners is 0.215 SD (p=0.012), a small standardized effect; the article reports "effects are negligible for students with prior skill" (0.008 SD, p=0.941).

> "among these students, personalization increases performance by 0.215 SD ( p= 0.012 ), whereas effects are negligible for students with prior skill (0.008 SD, p= 0.941 )."

### Angel Tsai-Hsuan Chung 2026 (2)

Angel Tsai-Hsuan Chung, Botong Zhang, Ling-Chieh Kung, Hamsa Bastani, & Osbert Bastani. (2026). Effective Personalized AI Tutors via LLM-Guided Reinforcement Learning. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.6423358](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6423358)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

School-tier subgroup analysis within the same RCT, classifying schools by Comprehensive Assessment Program entrance scores. The printed effect size is 0.173 SD (p=0.045) in lower-tier schools, a small standardized effect; the higher-tier estimate (0.039 SD, p=0.752) is statistically insignificant.

> "As shown in Fig. 3, estimated effects are positive for both school types, with larger gains in lower-tier schools (0.173 SD, p= 0.045 ) and a smaller, statistically insignificant estimate in higher-tier schools (0.039 SD, p= 0.752 )."
