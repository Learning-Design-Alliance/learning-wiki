## Evidence

### Wrzesińska Magdalena A. 2025

Wrzesińska Magdalena A., Rakoczy Jarosław, Binder-Olibrowska Katarzyna W., Kostyła Magdalena, Allott Vincent E.S., Harris Samuel R.L., Walsh Jason L., Handa Ashok, Harris Benjamin H.L. (2025). Comparing structured note-taking and multiple-choice question writing as learning strategies among physiotherapy students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08272-7

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Survey-based comparison between crossover arms: Arm A students who completed the MCQ task first preferred MCQ more often (19%) than Arm B students (7%); the association was significant with a small effect size (phi = 0.19). The authors interpret this via the exposure effect of familiarity.

> "However, those who prepared MCQ as their first post-classes task (Arm A) were more likely to appreciate this method than students from Arm B (19% vs. 7%; χ² = 4.28; p = 0.039; phi = 0.19)."
