### Groener JB 2015

Groener JB, Bugaj TJ, Scarpone R, Koechel A, Stiepak J, Branchereau S, Krautter M, Herzog W, Nikendei C. (2015). Video-based on-ward supervision for final year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0430-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Grounded-theory interview analysis of student interviews (theme 'Video-based feedback', 101 quotations) in the pilot study. The article also reports supervisor feedback helped students assess technical skills (median 5, IQR 5–6) more than communicative skills (median 5, IQR 4–5) in the post-assessment.

> "However, most students felt it was only beneficial as an addition to the previous oral feedback from the supervisor. Sequential feedback was regarded as the most beneficial, that is first receiving feedback from the supervisor and then watching the video:"
