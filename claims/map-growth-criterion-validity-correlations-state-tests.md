# MAP Growth state-test correlations exceed 0.8 for most Grade 3-8 concurrent indices, with mathematics correlations higher than reading

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For Grades 3-8, almost all concurrent validity correlations between MAP Growth and state summative scores were above 0.8, and mathematics tended to show higher correlations than reading at all grades. [→ He 2021](#he-2021)

## Evidence

### He 2021

He, W., & Meyer, P. (2021). MAP Growth universal screening benchmarks: Establishing MAP Growth as an effective universal screener. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics (Tables 2.3 and 2.4) report correlations between linked state scores and MAP Growth for each term; the study treats correlations above 0.8 as strong criterion validity evidence and notes "mathematics tended to see higher correlation coefficients" than reading.

> "For Grades 3–8, almost all concurrent validity indices were above 0.8 for both subjects, and a large proportion of predictive validity indices were either above 0.8 or at least at the high end of 0.7."
