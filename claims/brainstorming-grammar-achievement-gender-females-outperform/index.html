Evidence¶

Nibal Abdelkarim Mousa Malkawi & Mona Smadi 2018¶

Nibal Abdelkarim Mousa Malkawi & Mona Smadi. (2018). The Effectiveness of Using Brainstorming Strategy in the Development of Academic Achievement of Sixth Grade Students in English Grammar at Public Schools in Jordan. International Education Studies, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n3p92

q2 · i? · causal · r1

T-test comparing experimental-group post-test scores by gender: males (n = 70) mean 16.140, SD 4.193; females (n = 50) mean 20.670, SD 4.583; significance "(0.002)", favoring females. The article reports a mean difference of (4.53).

"statistically significant differences between males and females students of the experimental group in the post achievement test in English grammar for the sixth grade in favor of females, where the statistically significance reached (0.002)"
