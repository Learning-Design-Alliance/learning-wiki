`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive qualitative phase using four focus groups with 16 certified preceptors across medical, surgical, critical care and paediatric settings. Thematic analysis identified "Nine potentialities" including deepening assessment competencies, handling emergencies, reliving complex situations, and developing teamwork skills.

> "Nine potentialities have been identified regarding the role of simulation in supporting NGRNs during their transition to independent practice"
