## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2008

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2008). Reflection and Self-Monitoring in Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3266713](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266713)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview and exam-work analysis of the four interviewed students on problem 1. Student 3 solved the problem correctly on the midterm but could not solve it on the final (Figure 1), stating "I never quite mastered the whole bra and ket notation"; student 11, by contrast, had no trouble.

> "The difficulties were specifically around the fact that Dirac notation had not been mastered, and the regression from the midterm attempt to the final exam attempt by two of the students suggests they may have memorized procedures related to Dirac notation in order to “get by”."
