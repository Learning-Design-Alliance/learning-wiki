The executive summary describes Chapter III, where a descriptive approach and an event history approach with various adjustments are applied and compared for EL students in different grade clusters with different initial ELP levels, using Education Agency 1 longitudinal data from 2003–04.

> "As students' initial ELP level influences the expected time frame for their attaining the English-proficient criterion, these data are used to illustrate the ways in which more refined time-to-English-proficiency criteria could be derived."
