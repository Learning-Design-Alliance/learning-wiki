`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey (25-item questionnaire via Survio.de, March–June 2023) of German dentists; of 625 returned questionnaires, 517 were evaluated, and the article reports that "99.2% of respondents treated patients with MIH". No effect size is printed for these descriptive proportions.
