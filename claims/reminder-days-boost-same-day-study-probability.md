## Evidence

### Lea Nobbe 2024

Lea Nobbe, Jasmin Breitwieser, Daniel Biedermann & Garvin Brod. (2024). Smartphone-based study reminders can be a double-edged sword. npj Science of Learning 9:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00253-7

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Within-person encouragement design in a preregistered intervention with 85 students aged 10–12; logistic mixed-effects model on daily log-file data from a vocabulary learning app compared reminder and no-reminder days. The study found "a signi ﬁcant positive effect of Reminder" with OR = 1.77, 95% CI [1.32, 2.37].

> "we found a signi ﬁcant positive effect of Reminder on students ’ likelihood to study vocabulary on a given day (b = 0.57, 95% CI [0.28, 0.826], χ2(1) = 14.39, p < 0.001). On average, stu- dents in the Reminder Group were more likely to study on days they received a reminder ( OR = 1.77, 95% CI [1.32, 2.37])."
