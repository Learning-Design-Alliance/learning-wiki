# Reaction times on the XOR task reveal a strategy difference: the feature-specific feedback group was slower on XOR relative to conjunction than the global-feedback group

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` RTs differed between conjunction and XOR tasks (Task η2 = 0.4732) with a significant Experiment×Task interaction (η2 = 0.0305, p < 10−6), and the RT bias toward XOR was significantly larger in the feature-specific group (p < 10−5). [→ Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik 2024](#behnam-karami-caspar-m-schwiedrzik-2024)

## Evidence

### Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik 2024

Mixed repeated-measures ANOVA on 36 RT values per subject (12 re-invited subjects total) across color-orientation difficulty combinations for conjunction and XOR tasks; the "Experiment × Task" interaction gave η2 = 0.0305 (p < 10−6).
