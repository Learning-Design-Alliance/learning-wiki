Evidence¶

Kuo 2013¶

Kuo, F.-O., Yu, P.-T., & Hsiao, W.-H. (2013). Develop and evaluate the effects of multimodal presentation system on elementary ESL students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018039

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on a 6-item, 7-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.82) administered after the post-test to both groups. All six items individually showed significant differences favoring the experimental group.

"The mean of overall satisfaction was 35.90 (SD = 4.91) for the experimental group, higher than the 25.91 (SD = 3.91) for the control group. There is a significant difference between these two groups, t(127.120) = 13.049, p < .001."
