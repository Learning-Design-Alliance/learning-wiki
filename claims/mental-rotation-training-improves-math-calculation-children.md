## Evidence

### Cheng 2014

Cheng, Y.-L., & Mix, K. S. (2014). Spatial Training Improves Children's Mathematics Ability. Journal of Cognition and Development, 15(1), 2-11. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED580905

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 58 children (spatial training n = 31, control n = 27); MANCOVA on posttests with pretest covariates showed the math test difference favoring the spatial training group, "p = .005, η2= .14".

> "Univariate tests indicated that this difference was evident on both the Mental Rotation test (F (1, 55) = 16.23, p < .001, η2= .23), and the Math test (F (1, 55) = 8. 73, p = .005, η2= .14)."
