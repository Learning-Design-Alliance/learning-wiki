## Evidence

### Gall S 2018

Gall S, Adams L, Joubert N, Ludyga S, Müller I, Nqweniso S, Pühse U, du Randt R, Seelig H, Smith D, Steinmann P, Utzinger J, Walter C, Gerber M. (2018). Effect of a 20-week physical activity intervention on selective attention and academic performance in children living in disadvantaged neighborhoods: A cluster randomized control trial. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0206908. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed linear regression model (Model 3) on 663 children from eight schools in a cluster randomized trial, controlling for baseline EoYR and confounders with random intercepts for school classes. The intervention significantly predicted EoYR (B = 0.34, p = 0.032), with effects "primarily based on EoYR remaining stable in the IG, whereas scores decreased in the CG."

> "the physical activity intervention significantly predicted the EoYR at post-intervention (B = 0.34, 95% CI: 0.03 to 0.65, p = 0.032). However, as can be seen on a descriptive level (Table 2), this intervention effect was primarily based on EoYR remaining stable in the IG, whereas scores decreased in the CG"
