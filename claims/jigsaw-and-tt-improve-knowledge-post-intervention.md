## Evidence

### Stokstad JM 2026

Stokstad JM, Nedrehagen ES, Hufthammer KO. (2026). The Jigsaw teaching method compared to traditional teaching on anatomy and physiology knowledge in higher education – a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26:252. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08608-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial with 47 undergraduate social nursing students (23 TT, 24 JS) assessed on a 25-question multiple-choice test; a longitudinal generalised least squares model estimated mean improvements, described as "a large and similar overall improvement in scores" in both groups.

> "The mean improvement was 5.2 points (CI: 3.9–6.5, p < 0.001) for the TT group and 4.6 points (CI: 3.4–5.9, p < 0.001) for the JS group (p = 0.54 for TT vs. JS)."
