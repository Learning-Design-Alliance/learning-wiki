`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 105 neonatal trainees across 24 UEMS countries found variation in programme length, curriculum type, and assessment requirements. The article concludes that "There currently is no “unified” pathway for training, curricula and formal assessments across member countries of the UEMS."
