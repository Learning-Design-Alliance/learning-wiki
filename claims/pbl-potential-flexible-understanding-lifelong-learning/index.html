Hmelo-Silver 2004¶

The article's overall conclusion from examining the empirical evidence supporting PBL is that "the evidence suggests that PBL is an instructional approach that offers the potential" to develop flexible understanding and lifelong learning skills. The claim is stated as potential, not as a demonstrated effect.
