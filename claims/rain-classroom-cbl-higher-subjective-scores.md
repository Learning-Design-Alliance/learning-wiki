Yu B, Zou H, Li M. (2026). Integrating rain classroom with case-based learning in teaching dental hard tissue non-carious diseases: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09602-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study using historical controls: 21 traditional-lecture students (2019–2020) versus 29 integrated Rain Classroom/CBL students (2022–2023), analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test. The reported difference was statistically significant (P = 0.012).

> "students from the control group in the 2019–2020 academic year’s first semester achieved a median scoring rate of 90.00. Meanwhile, in the first semester of the 2022–2023 academic year, when evaluated on the subjective questions, students from the experimental group achieved a median scoring rate of 100.00"
