Mühling T, Schreiner V, Appel M, Leutritz T, König S. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of anonymous open-ended responses from VRS participants, coded by two independent researchers via consensus; "there were nearly twice as many positive (n=58) as negative comments (n=30)", led by realism and fairness themes.

> "Of note, there were nearly twice as many positive (n=58) as negative comments (n=30). Students predominantly praised the realism and fairness of the examination, with approximately 35% (20/58) and 30% (17/58), respectively, of the positive comments focused on these aspects"
