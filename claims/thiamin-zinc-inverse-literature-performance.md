## Evidence

### Tia Achil 2025

Tia Achil, Konan Amoin G., Hauser Jonas, Ndri Kouassi Y., Ciclet Olivier, Esso Lasme E., Nindjin Charlemagne. (2025). A Cross-Sectional Study of the Relationship Between Dietary Micronutrient Intake, Cognition and Academic Performance Among School-Aged Children in Taabo, Côte d’Ivoire. Nutrients 17(22):3602. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17223602

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted logistic regression from the same cross-sectional study, contrasting higher versus lower intake tertiles on literature scores; the article reports "inverse associations between both micronutrients and literature". No standardized effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The adjusted analysis revealed inverse associations between both micronutrients and literature. Higher intakes of thiamin (AOR = 0.30; 95% CI: 0.10–0.60; p = 0.002) and zinc (AOR = 0.40; 95% CI: 0.20–0.70; p = 0.005) were associated with lower odds of achieving better literature performance"
