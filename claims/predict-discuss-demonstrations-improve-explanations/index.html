Evidence¶

Crouch 2003¶

Crouch, C. H., Watkins, J., Fagen, A. P., & Mazur, E. (2003). Peer Instruction: Engaging Students One-on-One, All At Once. Research-Based Reform of University Physics. https://scholar.google.com

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Controlled experiment by Crouch, Fagen, Callan and Mazur in which discussion sections saw demonstrations in observe, predict, or discuss modes, with some sections as control, and were tested at semester's end on predicting and explaining outcomes. The predict and discuss groups "were able to give correct explanations at significantly higher rates"; no effect size is printed.

"In sharp contrast, the predict and discuss groups were able to give correct explanations at significantly higher rates than the observe group or the group who did not see the demonstration at all."
