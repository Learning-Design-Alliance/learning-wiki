## Evidence

### Wang Chih Jung 2019

Wang Chih Jung, Lin Su Yueh, Tsai Sheng Han, Shan Yan Shen. (2019). Implications of long-term low-fidelity in situ simulation in acute care and association with a reduction in unexpected cardiac arrests: A retrospective research study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0213789

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective analysis of 84 twice-monthly ISS sessions in an acute care ward, split into high- and low-performance groups by Ottawa GRS score; Table 1 reports all five GRS categories differing at p < 0.0001 by design, while "leaders’ seniority, members’ seniority, leaders’ simulation experience, and members’ simulation experience" showed no significant differences (e.g., leader seniority p = 0.2921).

> "We determined that there were no significant differences between the two groups in terms of leaders’ seniority, members’ seniority, leaders’ simulation experience, and members’ simulation experience (Table 1)."
