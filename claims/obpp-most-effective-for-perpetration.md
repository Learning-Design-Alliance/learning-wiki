## Evidence

### Hannah Gaffney 2019

Hannah Gaffney, David P. Farrington, & Maria M. Ttofi. (2019). Examining the Effectiveness of School-Bullying Intervention Programs Globally: a Meta-analysis. International Journal of Bullying Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42380-019-0007-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Program-specific meta-analysis (Table 4) of 12 OBPP evaluations; the Norway–USA difference in perpetration effect magnitude "was not statistically significant." Other significantly effective programs had "markedly lower" effect sizes.

> "In relation to school-bullying perpetration outcomes, overall the OBPP was the most effective intervention program. In addition, evaluations of the OBPP in Norway and in the USA were the most effective individually, in comparison with other included anti-bullying programs."

### Hannah Gaffney 2019 (2)

Hannah Gaffney, David P. Farrington, & Maria M. Ttofi. (2019). Examining the Effectiveness of School-Bullying Intervention Programs Globally: a Meta-analysis. International Journal of Bullying Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42380-019-0007-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Program-specific victimization analysis (Table 4): OBPP Norway (n = 5) OR = 1.57 versus OBPP USA (n = 6) OR = 1.17; the authors attribute the difference possibly to evaluation methodology or "cultural and societal differences."

> "Effect sizes for the OBPP varied significantly between evaluations conducted in Norway and evaluations conducted in the USA."
