### Griesmer Katherine B. MD 2025

Combining Immersive Simulation with a Collaborative Procedural Training on Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity and Fascia Iliaca Compartment Block: A Pilot Study. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-simulation learner experience survey (Table 3) in the pilot: all seven items had medians of 10.0 with LQ 9.0 and UQ 10.0, including "This workshop would be useful for future ED residents and clinicians." The authors note the combination of cases was well received in survey comments although not statistically significant.

> "Overall, perceptions of the simulation experience were also positive with learners indicating support of future sessions for other EM residents and clinicians."
