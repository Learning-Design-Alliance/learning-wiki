Carole Torgerson, Hannah Ainsworth, Hannah Buckley, Gillen Hampden-Thompson, Catherine Hewitt, Deborah Humphry, Laura Jefferson, Natasha Mitchell, David Torgerson. (2016). Affordable Online Maths Tuition: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581116

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process evaluation drawing on case studies of seven schools (nine focus groups with 55 pupils, 16 staff interviews) and two surveys of 32 intervention schools (response rates 69% and 59%). Teachers' perceptions were self-reported and untested against outcome data; the report recommends future research measure these outcomes.

> "Teachers were largely positive about the online tuition, and reported that it appeared to improve pupils’ comprehension, verbal fluency, and confidence in maths."
