### Xu Binbin 2026

Xu Binbin, Wang Yan, Mo Yun, Zhang Rong, Chen Hongyan, Zhou Xuezhi, Hu Li. (2026). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge on peripherally inserted central catheter care in Guizhou, China: A web-based cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55786-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional web-based survey of 3528 nurses from 18 hospitals in Guizhou, China, using a 19-item PICC care knowledge questionnaire converted to a hundred-point scale. The article reports the mean score "48.67 ± 12.94" and pass rate "17.2%", with tertiary nurses scoring higher than secondary/primary nurses.

> "The overall mean score for PICC care knowledge was 48.67 ± 12.94. Nurses in tertiary hospitals scored higher (50.19 ± 13.41) than those in secondary/primary hospitals (47.44 ± 12.42). The overall pass rate (score ≥ 60) was 17.2%, with a higher pass rate in tertiary hospitals (21.6%) than in secondary/primary hospitals (13.6%)"
