## Evidence

### Deravi Noosha D. 2026

Deravi Noosha D., Behrens Nathan, Warnock Brielle, Hunt Maya L., Stefanidis Dimitrios, Holmstrom Amy, Maxey Katie Stanton, Tarras Samantha, Ritter E. Matthew. (2026). Training benchmarks for the Fundamentals of Robotic Surgery virtual reality tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12957-5

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

One-sample t tests comparing 16 novices' first-attempt times to expert means across six tasks; all p < 0.001 with Cohen's d ranging from 1.2 (Knot Tying) to 2.4 (Fourth Arm Cutting). The article reports "a large effect size (Cohen's d > 0.80) for all tasks."

> "Difference in time between novices’ first attempts and the expert-derived mean was statistically discernible (p < 0.05) with a large effect size (Cohen’s d > 0.80) for all tasks (Table 1)."
