Bibliometric trend analysis of annual publication counts (Figure 2) over the 2015–2025 Scopus sample. The article reports moderate growth from 2015 to 2018, a rapid upward trajectory since 2019, and that "The fitted curve suggests that research in this field is expected to maintain continued growth."

"Particularly after 2022, driven by the widespread implementation of LLMs, annual output showed explosive growth, maintaining strong momentum in 2024."
