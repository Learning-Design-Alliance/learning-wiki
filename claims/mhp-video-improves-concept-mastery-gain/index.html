Evidence¶

Wilujeng 2020¶

Wilujeng, I., Suryadarma, I. G. P., Ertika, & Dwandaru, W. S. B. (2020). Local Potential Integrated Science Video to Improve SPS and Concept Mastery. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 197-214. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13413a

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pre/post multiple-choice test of 20 items assessing memorizing, understanding, and applying; experimental class average rose from 49.64 to 70.71. The article states "the g scores of the experimental and control groups are 0.58 and 0.27" in the text.

"According to the g analysis, the g scores of the experimental and control groups are 0.58 and 0.27, which are categorized as medium and low, respectively. This reveals that the improvement of students’ concept mastery in the experimental group is significantly higher than the control group."
