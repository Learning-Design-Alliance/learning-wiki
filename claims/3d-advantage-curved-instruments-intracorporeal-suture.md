# With complex curved instruments, 3D visualization gives novices an advantage over 2D in Intracorporeal Suture performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Novices using curved instruments under 3D visualization achieved significantly better Intracorporeal Suture performance scores than the 2D group at T5 (147.3 ± 191.5 vs. 294.4 ± 153.3, p = 0.017). [→ Hidding Johanna 2024](#hidding-johanna-2024)

## Evidence

### Hidding Johanna 2024

Hidding Johanna, Bucher Julian, Heiliger Christian, Andrade Dorian, Trupka Lukas, Halmen Matthias, Werner Jens, Karcz Konrad, Frank Alexander. (2024). Laparoscopy training of novices with complex curved instruments using 2D- and 3D-visualization. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03297-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of 2D vs. 3D visualization with curved instruments in the randomized trial: "there was a significant advantage in the 3D group in performance" at T5, in an exercise the authors describe as requiring high dexterity and spatial orientation.

> "In the Intracorporeal Suture exercise, there was a significant advantage in the 3D group in performance (T5: 147.3 ± 191.5 vs. 294.4 ± 153.3, p = 0.017)."
