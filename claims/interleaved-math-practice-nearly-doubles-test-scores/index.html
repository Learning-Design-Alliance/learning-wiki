Evidence¶

Rohrer et al. 2013¶

Doug Rohrer, Robert F. Dedrick, Kaleena Burgess. (2013). An Efficacy Study of Interleaved Mathematics Practice. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563060

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Classroom-based controlled experiment at a large public middle school in Tampa, Florida, with three teachers and 140 seventh grade students in a counterbalanced crossover design. The Results section reports the final test contrast: "interleaved assignments were nearly twice as effective as blocked assignments, 72% vs. 38%" with d = 1.05.

"Final test scores revealed that interleaved assignments were nearly twice as effective as blocked assignments, 72% vs. 38%, t (139) = 10.49, p < .0001 (Cohen's d = 1.05)."
