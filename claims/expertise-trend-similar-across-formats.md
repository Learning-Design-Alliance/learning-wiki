Comparison of rubric-graded free-response performance in course A with dichotomous multiple-choice performance in course B, shown in Fig. 3. The article reports the performance trends are similar across the two courses regardless of format.

> "we find that in both courses, students who displayed a higher level of expertise on the final exam (e.g., students in groups 4 and 5) on average typically scored higher on Questions 1 and 2 in both formats than those who did not perform as well on the final exam overall."
