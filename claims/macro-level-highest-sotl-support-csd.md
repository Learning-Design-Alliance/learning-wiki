## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Survey results indicate the highest level of SoTL support in CSD comes from institutions (teaching and learning centers, research offices, libraries), while individual CSD programs provide the fewest supports. [→ Friberg 2023](#friberg-2023)

## Evidence

### Friberg 2023

Friberg, Jennifer; Hoepner, Jerry K.; Sauerwein, Allison M.; and Mandulak, Kerry (2023) "The Integration of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning into the Discipline of Communication Sciences and Disorders," Teaching and Learning in Communication Sciences & Disorders: Vol. 7: Iss. 1, Article 10. https://doi.org/10.30707/TLCSD7.1.1675490380.873306

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Stakeholder survey (n = 120) asked respondents about supports within their own programs and institutions across funding, grants, resources, professional development, mentorship, and awards. Institutions were "the most frequent source for grants, awards/recognitions, mentorship, and travel funding."

> "Across mega, macro, and meso levels of impact, survey results suggest that the highest level of support for SoTL in CSD is currently derived at the institutional level (macro)"
