`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The commentary reports, attributing this to Rohrer (2012), a real-world school mathematics project on spacing and interleaving, such as when and how to use the Pythagorean Theorem. The review attributes the finding to that study; its design is not described here. No effect size is printed.

> "He found strong support for the benefits of spacing, a result that is especially important given that most workbooks, as well as classroom exercises, involved blocked, not interleaved, practice."

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports that Rohrer and Hartwig (2020) summarize evidence that interleaving benefits exceed those of the spacing it creates. This is a second-hand summary attributed to the reviewed authors; no statistics are printed in the commentary.

> "They also, importantly, summarize evidence that the benefits of interleaving can go beyond the benefits of the spacing that interleaving creates."
