### Woloshyn 1992

Woloshyn, Vera E.; Paivio, Allan; Pressley, Mike. (1992). Using Elaborative Interrogation To Help Students Overcome Their Inaccurate Science Beliefs. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED356949

`q3 · i2`

Experiment 2 follow-up: students completed 75-day and 180-day recognition tests in addition to immediate and 14-day tests. The authors report "performance scores maintained up to 6 months following study" and characterize the gains as large relative to reading-control standard deviations.

> "Elaborative-interrogation learning gains were durable, with performance scores maintained up to 6 months following study.In general, these learning gains were large (i.e., relative to the reading-control standard deviations; Hedges & Olkin, 1985)."
