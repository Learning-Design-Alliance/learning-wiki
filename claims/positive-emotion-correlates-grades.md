## Evidence

### Jaebong Yoo & Jihie Kim 2014

Jaebong Yoo & Jihie Kim. (2014). Can Online Discussion Participation Predict Group Project Performance? Investigating the Roles of Linguistic Features and Participation Patterns. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0010-8

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis (N = 173 groups, Table 7) reporting positive emotion at r = .16; the regression retained it with Beta = .13 (p < .05). The authors interpret that "Information providers may present positive attitudes and praise others more often than others."

> "Among LIWC variables, only positive emotion was positively correlated with the project grade. Information providers may present positive attitudes and praise others more often than others."
