### Lucas Vasconcelos 2025

Lucas Vasconcelos. (2025). “We are too dumb for this”: a critical discourse analysis of stereotypical attributions to struggle with computational thinking. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10532-8

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' critical interpretation in the discussion, not a tested result: they argue the attributions echo the stereotypical computer scientist (exceptional intelligence, innate traits) and create a sense of disconnection and exclusion. No effect size is printed.

> "These attributions represent the type of discourse that excludes minoritized populations from the computer science and STEM pipelines. Interventions are needed in preservice teacher education to debunk stereotypical attributions about innate traits determining success in computer science"
