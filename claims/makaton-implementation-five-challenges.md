## Evidence

### Mc Geer 2026

Mc Geer, C., Milton, C., Vorster, C., & Graham, M.A. (2026). South African speech-language therapists’ perceived competencies and use of Makaton. South African Journal of Communication Disorders, 73(1), a1154. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajcd.v73i1.1154

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended survey responses describing difficulties implementing Makaton. Example responses included that "Makaton is not widely known by the general population", that signs are hard for children with fine motor difficulties, and caregiver myths "that it will inhibit speech".

> "Five common themes emerged from their responses: (1) lack of awareness and training, (2) financial and resource constraints, (3) consistency and carryover, (4) motor and cognitive challenges and (5) cultural and regional limitations."
