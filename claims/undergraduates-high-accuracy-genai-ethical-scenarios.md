### Reed 2026

Reed, J. M., Ferdig, R. E., Dodson, T. M., Gunstad, J., & Hughes, J. W. (2026). AI Literacy and Scenario-based Ethical Judgment About Generative AI Among Undergraduate Students. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09757-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 531 undergraduates recruited through two psychology courses. Descriptive results for RQ1 show a mean correct classification of 88.54% and "a pronounced ceiling effect, with more than half of participants (57.6%, n = 306) correctly identifying all six scenarios".

> "The mean percentage of ethical scenarios answered correctly was 88.54%, indicating generally high levels of ethical judgment. Score distributions revealed a pronounced ceiling effect, with more than half of participants (57.6%, n = 306) correctly identifying all six scenarios (100%)."
