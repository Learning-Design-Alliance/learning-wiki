## Evidence

### Pettinger Katherine J 2019

Pettinger Katherine J, Kelly Brian, Sheldon Trevor A, Mon-Williams Mark, Wright John, Hill Liam J B. (2019). Starting school: educational development as a function of age of entry and prematurity. Archives of Disease in Childhood, 105(2), 160-165. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2019-317124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted logistic regression comparing 48 Early Academic Starts cases against three comparator groups from the Born in Bradford cohort. Only 19% of EAS children met GLD versus 52% of preterm non-summer births, 43% of term summer births, and 38% of preterm summer births not entering early. The article reports "less likely to achieve a GLD compared with (1) other children born preterm (fully adjusted OR 5.51 (2.85–14.25))".

> "Children starting school a school year earlier than anticipated during pregnancy were less likely to achieve a GLD compared with (1) other children born preterm (fully adjusted OR 5.51 (2.85–14.25)); (2) term summer births (fully adjusted OR 3.02 (1.49–6.79)); and (3) preterm summer births who remained within their anticipated school year (fully adjusted OR 3.64 (1.27–11.48))."
