Evidence¶

Raffington 2024¶

Raffington, L. (2024). Utilizing epigenetics to study the shared nature of development and biological aging across the lifespan. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00239-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The perspective summarizes the author's own analyses applying adult-derived MPSs in the Texas Twins Project and Future Families cohort, finding "means, distributions, and associations with chronological age that were in line with MPS applications in adults". No effect size is printed.

"we computed MPSs– originally developed in adults—in overn = 3000 children and adolescents from two sociodemographically diverse US cohorts"
