## Evidence

### Yu Huan 2026

Yu Huan, Li Jing, Jia Yan, Zhang Jiayang, Wang Zhitao, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing online teaching of “subgingival scaling and root planing” through periodontal endoscopy assistance: a study on its application in a teaching-practicing module. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-07706-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Timed 30-minute root planing exercise on a standardized maxillary Dentoform typodont, scored by three calibrated teachers (ICC 0.90) on five criteria out of 50 points. PE in-person scored highest; only a P value is printed, no effect size.

> "In the operative skill assessment, students in the PE in-person group scored significantly higher than those in the other two groups (39.57 ± 1.91 vs. 38.35 ± 2.09 vs. 38.30 ± 2.22, P = 0.017)"

### Yu Huan 2026 (2)

Yu Huan, Li Jing, Jia Yan, Zhang Jiayang, Wang Zhitao, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing online teaching of “subgingival scaling and root planing” through periodontal endoscopy assistance: a study on its application in a teaching-practicing module. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-07706-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subscore analysis of the same skill assessment: PE in-person mean calculus removal 7.53 ± 1.14 versus 6.71 ± 1.00 (PE online) and 6.82 ± 1.24 (TT), P = 0.002; the four other criteria showed P > 0.05. No effect size printed.

> "This difference was primarily driven by their superior performance in calculus removal (7.53 ± 1.14 vs. 6.71 ± 1.00 vs. 6.82 ± 1.24, P = 0.002), whereas no significant differences were observed among the three groups in the remaining criteria (P > 0.05)"
