bias-critique¶

Mills, K., Coenraad, M., Ruiz, P., Burke, Q., & Weisgrau J. (2021, December). Computational thinking for an inclusive world: A resource for educators to learn and lead. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/138

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

In the inclusive pedagogies section the report proposes, as an illustrative example rather than a tested result, that "students might discuss the implications of gender biases in natural language processing systems such as Gmail Smart Compose" or higher error rates for marginalized groups in facial and speech recognition, and explore real-world data sets to identify patterns of inequity. It notes these discussions do not need to wait until high school.
