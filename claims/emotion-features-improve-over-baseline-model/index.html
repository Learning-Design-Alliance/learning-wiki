Michelle Taub 2019¶

The Impact of Contextualized Emotions on Self-Regulated Learning and Scientific Reasoning during Learning with a Game-Based Learning Environment. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00191-1

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Model comparison in the CRYSTAL ISLAND regression analysis. The nested F-test, "F(6, 53) = 3.54, p <0 . 0 1", showed the emotion-feature model improved on the baseline; the article reports cross-validation R2 of 0.285 for the full model versus 0.191 for the reduced model, a 9.4% reduction in held-out mean squared error.

"A nested F-test using the full model specified in Table 6 and the reduced model using the strong baseline predictor of total worksheet submits indicates a significant improvement in sum of squared error for the number of parameters introduced F(6, 53) = 3.54, p <0 . 0 1 ."
