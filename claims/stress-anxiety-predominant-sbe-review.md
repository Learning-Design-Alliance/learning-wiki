## Evidence

### Madsgaard 2022

Madsgaard, A., Smith‐Strøm, H., Hunskår, I., & Røykenes, K. (2022). A rollercoaster of emotions: An integrative review of emotions and its impact on health professional students' learning in simulation‐based education. Nursing Open, 9, 108–121. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1100

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Integrative review synthesis (Whittemore & Knafl method) of 16 studies on emotions and learning in SBE. The review's analysis concluded that "stress and anxiety are the predominant emotions reported in the literature" affecting outcomes "mainly in an obstructive way".

> "One of the most important findings of our analyses was that stress and anxiety are the predominant emotions reported in the literature and that these affect academic outcomes mainly in an obstructive way."
