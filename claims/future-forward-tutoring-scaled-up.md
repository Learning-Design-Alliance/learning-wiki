## Evidence

### Jones 2021

Jones, C. J., & Li, D. (2021). Testing the Impact and Scalability of the Future Forward Literacy Program. University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. https://uwm.edu/sreed

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Implementation analysis of EA program data through March 2020. The average FF student received 2.6 tutoring sessions per week, and 78.6% of schools (11 of 14) met the implementation benchmark of 80% of students receiving two or more sessions per week.

> "Of the 281 FF participants, 252 (89.7%) received the full tutoring program (at least two sessions a week) (Table 5; Figure 1)."

### Curtis J. Jones and DongMei Li 2021

Implementation analysis of the 2019-20 scale-up found the average FF student received 2.6 tutoring sessions per week; "252 (89.7%) received the full tutoring program" and 11 of 14 sites met the site-level fidelity benchmark.

> "Of the 281 FF participants, 252 (89.7%) received the full tutoring program (at least two sessions a week) (Table 5; Figure 1).
