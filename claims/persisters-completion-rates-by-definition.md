# Students meeting the continuous four-term persistence definition completed college at higher rates than students meeting the third-semester definition, for both bachelor's and community college enrollees

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among bachelor's degree enrollees enrolled in the third semester, four-year completion was 44% and six-year completion 68%, 5 and 7 points lower than continuously enrolled four-semester students (49% and 75%). [→ Mahaffie 2024](#mahaffie-2024)
`q2 i?` For the 2015 class, 46% of third-semester community college enrollees and 59% of continuous four-semester enrollees completed a credential within six years; among non-persisters, completion was 7% and 9% respectively. [→ Mahaffie 2024 (2)](#mahaffie-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Mahaffie 2024

Mahaffie, S., Nagaoka, J., & Usher, A. (2024). Measuring Patterns of Immediate and Continued College Enrollment among CPS Graduates. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the To&Through Project. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/measuring-patterns-of-immediate-and-continued-college-enrollment-among-CPSs-graduates

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison (Figure 7) of completion rates for CPS bachelor's degree enrollees under the two persistence definitions. Third-semester enrollees completed at 44% (four-year) and 68% (six-year), which the article reports as 5 and 7 percentage points lower than four-semester continuous enrollees (49% and 75%); these are descriptive rates, with no effect size printed.

> "Among CPS graduates who were enrolled in bachelor’s degree granting institutions in the third semester, the most recent four-year completion rate was 44% and the most recent six-year completion rate was 68%"

### Mahaffie 2024 (2)

Mahaffie, S., Nagaoka, J., & Usher, A. (2024). Measuring Patterns of Immediate and Continued College Enrollment among CPS Graduates. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research and the To&Through Project. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/measuring-patterns-of-immediate-and-continued-college-enrollment-among-CPSs-graduates

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis (Figures 10 and 11) of six-year credential completion for the CPS graduating class of 2015 among community college enrollees, comparing third-semester point-in-time persisters with continuous four-semester persisters and with non-persisters under each definition; a 13 percentage point difference is reported.

> "46% of the third semester enrollees (and 7% of those who did not meet this definition of persistence) and 59% of the students who were enrolled continuously through semester four (and 9% of the students who did not meet this definition of persistence) completed a credential within six years"
