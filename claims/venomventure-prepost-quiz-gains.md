`q2 i3` Tree-annotation quiz scores rose significantly from pre to post with a large effect (r = 0.579), with medians increasing from 2 to 4 on the 5-point quiz. [→ Thanukos Anastasia 2026](#thanukos-anastasia-2026)

## Evidence

### Thanukos Anastasia 2026

Thanukos Anastasia, MacDonald Teresa, Quimby Claire, White Lisa D. (2026). VENOMventure, an Immersive Escape-Style Game, Teaches Families the Foundations of Phylogenetics. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0172

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey analysis of 446 family participants at two museums and two libraries; five tree-annotation items scored 0–1 each (maximum 5). The Wilcoxon signed-rank test found "a significant difference of large effect size" with medians rising from 2 to 4 (r = 0.579).

> "found a significant difference of large effect size between pre- (Mdn = 2, IQR = 1–4) and post-test scores (Mdn = 4, IQR = 2–5) on the tree-annotation items in the direction consistent with learning (N = 446, Z = −12.230, p<0.001, r = 0.579)"
