## Evidence

### Belhan Çelik 2025

Belhan Çelik, Sümeyye; Bumin, Gonca. (2025). Improving Refugee Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial on the Impact of Cognitive Activity Training. BMC Psychology, 13, 1186. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03586-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on PedsQL in the RCT. The article states the study group's post-training means differed significantly from pre-intervention (p <.001) while "the control group showed no statistically significant changes".

> "interaction effects were statistically significant in the PedsQL Total and its sub-dimensions Physical Health Summary Score (PHSS) and Psychosocial Health (PsHSS = EFS + SFS + SsFS) scores (respectively; F(1.32) = 42.022, p <.001; F(1.32) = 42.022, p =.001; F(1.32) = 20.646, p <.001; F(1.32) = 21.292, p <.001) (Table 4)."

### Belhan Çelik 2025 (2)

Belhan Çelik, Sümeyye; Bumin, Gonca. (2025). Improving Refugee Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial on the Impact of Cognitive Activity Training. BMC Psychology, 13, 1186. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03586-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on PedsQL emotional, social, and school functioning scores in the RCT. Adjusted post-test means were higher in the study group (65.75, 66.35, 67.51) than controls (57.94, 56.00, 49.99), showing "improvements were observed in the study group's emotional, social, and school functioning".

> "Significant differences were found in the adjusted post-intervention mean scores of the study group compared to the control group (F (1.31) [, ] = 12.586, p <.001; F (1.31) [, ] = 44.343, p <.001; F (1.31) [, ] = 20.342, p <.001)."
