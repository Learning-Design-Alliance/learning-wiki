Tatiana V. Sibgatullina and Olga G. Selivanova. (2016). Organizing Educational Activity of a Pupil on the Basis of Learning Technologies. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(13), 6056-6067. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Control-stage qualimetric assessment of the pedagogical experiment with experimental (167 people) and control (185 people) groups. The final complex assessment was "22.83 from 36 possible, which is 63.4%" in the experimental group versus 18.16 (50.4%) in the control group; no significance test is printed.

"It is ∑ K (n, j) M (n, j) = 22.83 from 36 possible, which is 63.4%. At the same time the final assessment in control group is ∑ K (n, j) M (n, j) = 18.16, that is 50. 4%."
