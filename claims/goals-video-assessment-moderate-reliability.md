## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2025

Thomaschewski M, Vonthein R, Keck T, Laubert T, Benecke C. (2025). Laparoscopic simulation training improves operating room performance of surgical residents: a multicenter randomized trial (NOVICE). International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000002304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Rater-agreement analysis of three experienced MIS surgeons rating randomized, blinded videos of CHE I and CHE II. The article reports "ICC(A,3) was 0.84" for consensus assessments, described in the limitations as moderate reliability.

> "The ICC(A,1) for the total GOALS score was 0.64 (95%CI: 0.47–0.78), when single assessments were considered, and ICC(A,3) was 0.84 (95%CI: 0.73–0.91) considering average assessments as in our trial."
