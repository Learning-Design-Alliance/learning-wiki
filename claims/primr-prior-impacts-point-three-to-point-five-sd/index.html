Related Claims¶

  • Longitudinal PRIMR data suggest pupils transferred literacy skills from English to Kiswahili, a direction opposite to that generally suggested by the Western literature. — related
  • The PRIMR literacy intervention had a positive impact on Kenyan pupils' literacy outcomes, including letter sound identification fluency, oral reading fluency, reading comprehension, and the percentage of pupils reading at the Ministry of Education benchmark, though not on all combinations of language, grade, and school type. — related
  • Frequency of TAC tutors' classroom visits was positively associated with learning outcomes, though the relationship is noncausal — related
  • PRIMR's first-year effects on reading comprehension were positive but inconsistent across language, grade, and school type — related
  • One year of PRIMR produced statistically significant gains in oral reading fluency in grade 1 formal and nonformal schools and grade 2 nonformal schools, in both English and Kiswahili, but not in grade 2 formal schools — related
