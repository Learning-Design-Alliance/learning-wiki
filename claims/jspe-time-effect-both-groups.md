### Serrada-Tejeda S 2026

Serrada-Tejeda S, Obeso-Benítez P, Hernández-Hernández L, Martínez-Cuervo F, Pérez-de-Heredia-Torres M, Sánchez-Herrera-Baeza P, Yusta-Ceacero A, Martínez-Piédrola RM. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA main effect of time on JSPE-HPS total, Perspective Taking, and Compassionate Care; e.g., total F(1, 80) = 8.45; p = .005 (0.096). The authors attribute this to exposure to the theoretical educational sessions.

> "In all three cases a significant effect of time was found, indicating that scores increased throughout the study regardless of group."
