## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Interest is defined broadly as all media of correspondence between mind and object, implying the emotional accompaniment of every attentive state regardless of emotional tone quality or intensity. [→ Waples 1920](#waples-1920)

## Evidence

### Waples 1920

Waples, D. (1920). An Approach to the Synthetic Study of Interest in Education: Part I. The Journal of Educational Psychology, XI(6). https://doi.org/10.1037/h0076045

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the article's own definitional statement in the Scope and Definition of Terms section, a theoretical stipulation rather than an empirical finding; the article adds that "interest implies the emotional accompaniment of every attentive state without regard to the quality or intensity of the emotional tone."

> "the term interest is interpreted in its literal sense to include all media of correspondence between the mind and the object, real or imaginary, of its contemplation."
