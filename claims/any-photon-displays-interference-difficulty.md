### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2014

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Difficulty documented across pretest questions 1, 2, and 3 of the in-class evaluation: students assumed "any photon reaching the detectors displays interference regardless of the polarizers in the setup," even when they correctly computed the percentage of photons passing the polarizers.

> "The difficulty related to incorrectly assuming that any photon reaching the detectors displays interference regardless of the polarizers in the setup was displayed in questions 1, 2, and 3."
