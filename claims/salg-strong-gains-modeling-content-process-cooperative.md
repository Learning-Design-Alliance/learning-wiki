## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Former students reported strong perceived gains in modeling content (mean 4.10/5), model-building process skills (mean 4.25/5), approaching vague problems (mean 4.12/5), and cooperative skills (means 4.13/5 and 4.18/5). [→ Hoskinson Anne-Marie 2010](#hoskinson-anne-marie-2010)

## Evidence

### Hoskinson Anne-Marie 2010

Hoskinson Anne-Marie. (2010). How to Build a Course in Mathematical–Biological Modeling: Content and Processes for Knowledge and Skill. CBE-Life Sciences Education 9(3):333–341. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-03-0041

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Retrospective SALG survey of all 175 course completers across four semesters; 89 students (51%) responded. Respondents rated gains on 43 questions on a 1–5 Likert scale, reporting "strong gains in learning modeling content" and similar gains in process and cooperative skills.

> "Students reported strong gains in learning modeling content (mean of response to each question = 4.10/5, N = 89). They reported similar gains in modeling process skills, including applying the model-building steps (mean = 4.25/5)"
