## Evidence

### Janicki et al. 2011

Thomas N. Janicki, Judith Gebauer, Ulku Yaylacicegi. (2011). Impact of Pre-Grading / Resubmission of Projects on Test Grades in an Introductory Computer Literacy Course. Information Systems Education Journal 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1145436

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational tally from one section of 87 students across three pre-graded assignments (Table 1): 76 of 87 submitted the Access assignment early, 66 of 87 the Excel IF's assignment, and 61 of 87 the Excel Solver assignment. The decline was interpreted as workload from other courses pushing submissions closer to due dates.

> "It was a very favorable observation that 85% of students submitted the first project early; and even at the end of the semester 70% of students submitted for pre-grading. The instructor expected the pre-grading rate to be lower."
