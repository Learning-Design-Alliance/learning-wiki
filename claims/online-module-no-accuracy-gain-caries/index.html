Evidence¶

Tsao and Park 2016¶

Ariel Tsao, Sang E. Park. (2016). The Role of Online Learning in Radiographic Diagnosis in Dental Education. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i3.1387

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational pilot study of the Class of 2016 (n=31): one-tailed t-tests compared percentage of overall correct responses between test and control groups, completers vs non-completers, and completers vs control; printed p-values were 0.45, 0.47, and 0.43, and 0.49 for incipient caries. The article reports "no statistically significant difference in accuracy"; equivalence was not tested.

"There is no statistically significant difference in accuracy between any of the aforementioned combinations."
