### Adelantado-Renau Mireia 2022

Adelantado-Renau Mireia, Monzonís-Carda Irene, Moliner-Urdiales Diego, Beltran-Valls Maria Reyes. (2022). The bidirectional longitudinal association between health-related quality of life and academic performance in adolescents: DADOS study. Quality of Life Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11136-022-03291-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel analysis of 257 adolescents from the DADOS study over 24 months, adjusted for sex, pubertal stage, waist circumference, socioeconomic status, and parents' education. The article reports "all the academic grades and the overall score of academic abilities at baseline were positively associated with HRQoL at follow-up".

> "Nevertheless, all the academic grades and the overall score of academic abilities at baseline were positively associated with HRQoL at follow-up (all p < 0.05)."
