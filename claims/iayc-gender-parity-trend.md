### Melanie Archipley 2021

Melanie Archipley, Hannah S. Dalgleish, Eva-Maria Ahrer, Daniel Mortimer. (2021). Reaching Diverse Groups in Long-Term Astronomy Public Engagement Efforts. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2111.08783](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2111.08783)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of self-reported gender records for IAYC camps since 1984 (data for every year except 1995), plotted in Figure 1 with linear best-fit lines. The authors infer "the gap was ﬁnally closed for participants around 2012" and note the trend towards increasing females has continued; 59% female and 4.7% non-binary at the most recent camp per the abstract.

> "We infer that the gender gap trended towards parity for most of the IA YC’s history, and the gap was ﬁnally closed for participants around 2012. Somewhat surprisingly, the gender representation has not stopped trending towards increasing females and decreasing males in the past decade."
