Linda Argote; Sara L. Beckman; Dennis Epple. (1990). The Persistence and Transfer of Learning in Industrial Settings. Management Science, Vol. 36, No. 2, pp. 140-154. http://links.jstor.org/sici?sici=0025-1909%28199002%2936%3A2%3C140%3ATPATOL%3E2.0.CO%3B2-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Annual-data robustness analysis contrasting lagged output with lagged cumulative output in a model with labor hours, shipways, time and lagged cumulative output. Recent experience was a significant predictor while cumulative experience was not; a single-firm advanced jet dataset also showed knowledge depreciation.

> "The coefficient of lagged output was positive and significant, t = 2.09, df= 40, p < 0.05, while the coefficient of lagged cumulative output was negative and insignificant."
