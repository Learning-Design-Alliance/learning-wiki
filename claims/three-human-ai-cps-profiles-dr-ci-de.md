# Cluster analysis of student-AI dialogue identifies three distinct collaboration profiles: Delegated Reasoning, Concerted Interpretation, and Delegated Elaboration

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Agglomerative hierarchical clustering on coded dialogue proportions yielded three student-AI interaction profiles, labeled Delegated Reasoning (DR), Concerted Interpretation (CI), and Delegated Elaboration (DE). [→ Zhanxin Hao 2025](#zhanxin-hao-2025)

### Zhanxin Hao 2025

Zhanxin Hao, Xiaobo Liu, Jiaxin Fan, Yun Long, Jifan Yu, Wenli Chen, Yu Zhang. (2025). Unpacking Interaction Profiles and Strategies in Human-AI Collaborative Problem Solving: A Cognitive Distribution and Regulation Perspective. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21288

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cluster analysis of proportion vectors of SEDA-adapted dialogue codes from 173 students' CPS sessions with AI agents. The article reports that "the optimal Silhouette index (0.22) was achieved when the number of clusters was set to 3," producing groups of 78, 55, and 40 students, labeled DR, CI, and DE based on their code profiles.
