## Evidence

### Sivaram and Ramar 2014

R.T. Sivaram, R. Ramar. (2014). Effect of CAI on Achievement of LD Students in English. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 1, January-March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068387

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test comparison of the two matched LD groups (25 each); Table 3 reports means of 42.4 (experimental) versus 23.2 (control) with a t-value of 9.23, significant at the 0.01 level. Groups were matched at baseline (t = 0.62).

> "the rate of progress shown by the experimental group LD students taught through computer-assisted instruction is 109.9 percent, while the rate of progress made by the control group LD students is 12.62 per cent."
