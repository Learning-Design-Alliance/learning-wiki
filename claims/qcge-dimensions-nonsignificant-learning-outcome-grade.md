# No statistically significant association between QCGE dimension ratings and the collaborative product grade

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Linear regression predicting the expert-rated group product grade from the four QCGE dimensions found no dimension statistically significant; equivalence was not tested. [→ Paneth 2024](#paneth-2024)

## Evidence

### Paneth 2024

Paneth, L., Jeitziner, L. T., Rack, O., Opwis, K., & Zahn, C. (2024). Zooming in: The role of nonverbal behavior in sensing the quality of collaborative group engagement. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09422-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression model with 38 group-mean QCGE dimension scores predicting the expert-rated product grade (weighted scoring 1–6 scale, ICC = 0.825). The article states "confidence intervals for all betas spanning values that include zero"; no effect size is printed, so impact is null.

> "the effects of BE (β = 3.01), SE (β = 0.31), CE (β = 0.42), and CC (β = –0.52) on the learning outcome (i.e., grade) are statistically nonsignificant, with p-values above the conventional significance threshold"
