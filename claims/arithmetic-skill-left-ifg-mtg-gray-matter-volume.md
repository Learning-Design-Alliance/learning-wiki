## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado. (2023). Arithmetic skills are associated with left fronto-temporal gray matter volume in 536 children and adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00201-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Voxel-based morphometry analysis of six merged structural MRI datasets of children aged 7.5 to 15, with ROI-based linear mixed-effect models. The article reports "a relation between GMV and arithmetic score was only observed in the left IFG" (and left MTG, Table 3/Table 4), with arithmetic coefficients of 0.004 in both ROIs.

> "Across all mixed-model analyses, a relation between GMV and arithmetic score was only observed in the left IFG"
