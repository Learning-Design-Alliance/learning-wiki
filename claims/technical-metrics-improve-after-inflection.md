## Evidence

### Huang Shih-Feng 2026

Huang Shih-Feng, Tan Yung-Lin, Hsu Chao-Wen, Tseng Hsin-Ping, Miskovic Danilo, Wu Chih-Chien. (2026). Technical proficiency assessment of robotic intracorporeal single-stapling colorectal anastomosis using video-based RA-CUSUM. International Journal of Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-05078-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Phase comparison of 36 cases split at case 17 (early n = 17, late n = 19), tested with the Mann–Whitney U test. The interval "was significantly reduced in the late phase"; only p-values and descriptive medians are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "the pure RiSSA interval was significantly reduced in the late phase (38.0 min [25.0–52.1]) compared to the early phase (49.7 min [36.6–79.8], p = 0.002)"

### Huang Shih-Feng 2026 (2)

Huang Shih-Feng, Tan Yung-Lin, Hsu Chao-Wen, Tseng Hsin-Ping, Miskovic Danilo, Wu Chih-Chien. (2026). Technical proficiency assessment of robotic intracorporeal single-stapling colorectal anastomosis using video-based RA-CUSUM. International Journal of Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-05078-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same early-versus-late phase comparison in the same cohort, console time analyzed with Student's t test as a normally distributed variable. Console time "was reduced from 201.3 ± 35.1 to 153.5 ± 31.4 min"; no effect size is printed.

> "console time was reduced from 201.3 ± 35.1 to 153.5 ± 31.4 min (p < 0.001) in the late phase"
