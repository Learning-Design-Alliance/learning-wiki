## Evidence

### Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Exploratory univariable three-level mixed-effects moderator models; guidance was one of three implementation variables meeting the conventional unadjusted 0.05 threshold, alongside feedback timing (p = .025) and learner control (p = .030).

> "Guided interventions produced a larger subgroup estimate ( g = 0.760, 95% CI [0.598, 0.921]) than unguided interventions (g = 0.479, 95% CI [0.275, 0.683]), F(1, 31) = 4.84, p =.035."

### Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026 (2)

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multiplicity analysis across the eight moderator tests in the same meta-analysis; cluster-robust CR2 inference additionally showed feedback timing no longer below threshold (p = .057) while guidance (p = .028) and learner control (p = .031) remained below 0.05 before multiplicity adjustment.

> "Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the eight omnibus moderator tests produced an adjusted p =.095 for guidance, feedback timing, and learner control; no moderator met the false-discovery-rate threshold of 0.05."

### Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026 (3)

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Descriptive three-way cross-tabulation of guidance, feedback timing, and learner control (Table 3); the authors state the overlap means the patterns are hypothesis-generating rather than evidence of independent moderator effects.

> "Fourteen of the 20 guided studies also provided immediate feedback."
