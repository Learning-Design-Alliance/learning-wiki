## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Physics Instructors and Teaching Assistants Using the Force Concept Inventory. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-test data from about 900 algebra-based students across four FCI contexts (truck colliding with car; car pushing truck speeding up; car pushing truck at constant speed; student pushing student). The alternate conception was much more prevalent in some contexts than others.

> "in some contexts, introductory students hold alternate conceptions related to Newton's third law more strongly (questions 4, 15 and 28 for which at least 32% of introductory students hold an alternate conception) than in other contexts (question 16, in which only 19% of introductory students hold an alternate conception)"
