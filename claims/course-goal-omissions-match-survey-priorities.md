# Skills that no exemplary CP course lists as goals are largely the ones experts say can be learned during research, with keeping a TODO list the least consistent case

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The skills absent from all four surveyed courses' goals (D1, D3, D4, D9, D12; E2-4; F7-9, F11; G1, G3) are mostly consistent with expert views that they need not be learned in advance. [→ Amy Lisa Graves 2020](#amy-lisa-graves-2020)
`q2 i?` Keeping a TODO list (D4) is the least consistent omission: under 30% of respondents wanted prior experience, about 50% said it would be learned on the job, and 20% said it was not needed. [→ Amy Lisa Graves 2020 (2)](#amy-lisa-graves-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Amy Lisa Graves 2020

Amy Lisa Graves, Adam Light. (2020). Hitting the ground running: Computational physics education to prepare students for computational physics research. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1109/mcse.2019.2963670](https://doi.org/10.1109/mcse.2019.2963670)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-comparison of Table I (course goals at Wagner, Colorado, Bryn Mawr, Swarthmore) with the survey results in Figures 1 and 2. The authors conclude the shared omissions "are	quite	consistent	with	Figure	1", i.e. learnable during research.

> "Also	consider	the	handful	of	skills	which	none	of	the	four	courses	had	as	an	instructional	goal:	D1,	D3,	D4,	D9,	D12;	E2-4;	F7-9,	F11;	G1	and	G3.	These	omissions	are	quite	consistent	with	Figure	1."

### Amy Lisa Graves 2020 (2)

Amy Lisa Graves, Adam Light. (2020). Hitting the ground running: Computational physics education to prepare students for computational physics research. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1109/mcse.2019.2963670](https://doi.org/10.1109/mcse.2019.2963670)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey response breakdown for skill D4 reported in Section 5. The article prints that "less	than	30%	of	respondents	wanted	prior	experience" while around half expected on-the-job learning and a fifth saw no need.

> "The	least	consistent	was	[D4]	keeping	a	TODO	list;	even	so,	less	than	30%	of	respondents	wanted	prior	experience,	around	50%	said	it	would	be	learned	on	the	job,	and	20%	it	was	not	needed	at	all."
