## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fernández-Soriano Francisco Luis 2019

Fernández-Soriano Francisco Luis, López Belén, Martínez-España Raquel, Muñoz Andrés, Cantabella Magdalena. (2019). Use of Computing Devices as Sensors to Measure Their Impact on Primary and Secondary Students' Performance. Sensors (Basel, Switzerland). https://doi.org/10.3390/s19143226

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis test with Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons of passed subjects by operating system; adjusted p-values indicate "there are differences in the number of passed subjects" for the Windows/MacOS/Android, Android/iOS/MacOS/ChromeOS and iOS/ChromeOS comparisons.

> "The adjusted p-values of this table indicate that there are differences in the number of passed subjects when comparing the use of Windows, MacOS and Android; when comparing Android with iOS, MacOS and ChromeOS; and when comparing iOS and ChromeOS."
