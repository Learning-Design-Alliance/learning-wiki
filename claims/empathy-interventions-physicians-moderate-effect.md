## Evidence

### Ngo Hazel 2025

Ngo Hazel, Sokolovic Nina, Hu Julia, Jenkins Jennifer. (2025). A systematic review identifying effective teaching methods and their combinations for increasing empathy in physicians: pairwise and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07917-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects pairwise meta-analysis of 109 independent samples from 111 included RCTs, totalling 11,111 participants, comparing behavioural empathy interventions to control groups. The article reports "an overall moderate effect size of d = 0.50 (95% CI = 0.40, 0.60)". Publication bias was evident (Egger's test, p <.001) and heterogeneity was high (I2 = 79.19, p <.001).

> "This analysis revealed an overall moderate effect size of d = 0.50 (95% CI = 0.40, 0.60) [46], where higher scores denoted higher empathy levels."
