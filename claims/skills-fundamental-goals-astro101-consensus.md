`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Consensus finding from the AstroEdUNC meeting's Skills theme, synthesising deliberations of 100 participating astronomers, education researchers, and practitioners. The report states the "general consensus of the meeting" that skills are the fundamental learning goals and content is the vehicle. No empirical measurement is reported; this is a documented community position.

> "The general consensus of the meeting was that skills represent the fundamental learning goals for introductory astronomy courses, and that the content is a fun and inspiring vehicle through which to teach the skills that will be important in the workforce these students are entering."
