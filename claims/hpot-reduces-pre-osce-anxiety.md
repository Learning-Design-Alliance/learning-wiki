## Evidence

### Massalou Damien 2025

Massalou Damien, Doyen Jérôme, Almairac Fabien, Rohrlich Pierre-Simon, Fournier Jean-Paul, Vandersteen Clair, Sadaghianloo Nirvana. (2025). Can relaxation exercises improve students’ OSCE grades: a prospective study. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77739

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective single-centre randomized study in Nice medical school; 96 HPOT students compared on VAS before and after the 30-minute relaxation session. The article reports a statistically significant decrease with "a median VAS of 6 [IQR 1;10] before relaxation versus 4"; no effect size printed.

> "Pre-exam anxiety decreased significantly for students in the HPOT procedure group, with a median VAS of 6 [IQR 1;10] before relaxation versus 4 [IQR 1;10] after relaxation (p = 0.001, Mann-Whitney U test)."
