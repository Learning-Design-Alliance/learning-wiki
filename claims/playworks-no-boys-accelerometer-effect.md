### Martha Bleeker 2015

Martha Bleeker, Nicholas Beyler, Susanne James-Burdumy, Jane Fortson. (2015). The Impact of Playworks on Boys' and Girls' Physical Activity During Recess. Journal of School Health, vol. 85, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impact-of-playworks-on-boys-and-girls-physical-activity-during-recess

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null finding from the same randomized evaluation of 29 schools using accelerometer measurement, with impacts estimated separately for girls and boys. No statistically significant difference was detected for boys; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.
