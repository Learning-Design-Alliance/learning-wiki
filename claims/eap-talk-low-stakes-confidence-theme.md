## Evidence

### Zou 2026

Zou, B., Yuan, Y., Wang, C., & Du, Y. (2026). Can AI Reduce Acculturative Stress? Exploring the Role of AI-Mediated Speaking Practice in Chinese International Students' Perceived Language Insufficiency, Social Isolation, and Academic Pressure. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 20 experimental-group participants (Cohen's κ = 0.81). This is the first theme, improved communicative confidence through "low-stakes speaking practice"; participants quoted repeating sentences until comfortable.

> "Many participants explained that EAP Talk offered a private and relatively non-judgemental environment in which they could practise speaking without fear of embarrassment."

### Zou 2026 (2)

Zou, B., Yuan, Y., Wang, C., & Du, Y. (2026). Can AI Reduce Acculturative Stress? Exploring the Role of AI-Mediated Speaking Practice in Chinese International Students' Perceived Language Insufficiency, Social Isolation, and Academic Pressure. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same interview study; a participant noted feedback was useful for pronunciation and fluency but "could not really tell me whether my academic idea was good or not" (P17).

> "Some participants were critical of the automated feedback, noting that it was sometimes too general or insufficiently sensitive to academic content."
