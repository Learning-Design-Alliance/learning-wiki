## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression analysis of CRDC data combined with Project Implicit county-level explicit bias estimates. The association was driven by lower enrollment rates for Black students, not higher rates for White students. In 2017-18, a 2 SD move in explicit bias was associated with a "1.8 percentage point increase" in the disparity.

> "County-level explicit racial bias was also associated with greater Black–White disparities in AP course enrollment across all 3 y (Ps < 0.001; Fig. 1, Left), again driven by greater explicit racial bias associated with lower enrollment rates for Black students, but not White students (Fig. 2, Bottom)."
