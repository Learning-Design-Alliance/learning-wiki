## Evidence

### Karen D. Wang 2024

Karen D. Wang, Zhangyang Wu, L'Nard Tufts II, Carl Wieman, Shima Salehi, Nick Haber. (2024). Scaffold or Crutch? Examining College Students' Use and Views of Generative AI Tools for STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.02653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.02653)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 40 STEM students; 34 students (85%) reported using genAI in STEM courses. The article reports "Using genAI tools to find explanations was the most common use, reported by 30 students (75%)" and, separately, that 20 students (50%) said their main reason was that it saved them time.

> "Using genAI tools to find explanations was the most common use, reported by 30 students (75%). This was followed by exploring related topics (n = 25, 62.5%), summarizing papers/readings (n = 24, 60%), supporting in-class activities (n = 23, 57.5%), and helping with problem-set questions (n = 23, 57.5%)."
