Evidence¶

Khatib and Meihami 2015¶

Mohammad Khatib, Hussein Meihami. (2015). Languaging and Writing Skill: The Effect of Collaborative Writing on EFL Students' Writing Performance. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.203

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Component-level independent-sample t tests in the same 12-session pretest/posttest quasi-experiment. Table 4 prints significance values of .003 (content), .001 (organization), .001 (grammar), .008 (vocabulary), and .001 (mechanics), all favoring the experimental group; no effect sizes are printed.

"The indexes in the Table (4) indicate that the performance of the two groups with regard to different writing components including content, organization, grammar, vocabulary, and mechanics are significantly different."
