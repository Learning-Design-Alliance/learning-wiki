### Momin N. Siddiqui 2025

Momin N. Siddiqui, Vryan Feliciano, Roy Pea, and Hari Subramonyam. (2025). AI in the Writing Process: How Purposeful AI Support Fosters Student Writing. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_14](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_14)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Welch's t-tests on two AI-perception survey items administered to both AI conditions in the RCT found "marginally higher ratings" for voice preservation but "no significant difference" on the replacement item; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Script&Shift (µ = 6.62, σ = 0.86) showed marginally higher ratings compared to the Chat condition (µ = 6.10, σ = 1.08; t(53.38) = 2.01, p < .05). However, for question 2, there was no significant difference between Script&Shift (µ = 5.97, σ = 1.52) and Chat conditions (µ = 6.07, σ = 1.18; t(55.88) = −0.29, p > .05)."
