### Bjerrum Anne Sofie 2016

Bjerrum Anne Sofie, Eika Berit, Charles Peder, Hilberg Ole. (2016). Distributed practice. The more the merrier? A randomised bronchoscopy simulation study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.30517

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retention analysis in the randomised 20-resident bronchoscopy simulator study, assessed 4 weeks after the last post-test with the same test case. The article reports the deterioration "was not statistically significant for any of the variables"; no effect size is printed.

> "Deterioration in performance from the final post-test (post-test 3) to retention test was seen for all variables, as shown in Fig. 2; however, the deterioration was not statistically significant for any of the variables."
