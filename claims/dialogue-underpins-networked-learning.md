The article reports, citing Ravenscroft (2011), that "thinking in networks will usually mean thinking through collaborative dialogue," and that future learning landscapes will still consist of people socially interacting with other people. The article uses this as the anchor for extending the dialogic account into the fuller architectonic tradition.
