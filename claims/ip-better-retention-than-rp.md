## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2024

Taewon Kim, Hakjoo Kim, Benjamin A. Philip & David L. Wright. (2024). M1 recruitment during interleaved practice is important for encoding, not just consolidation, of skill memory. npj Science of Learning 9, 77. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00290-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized three-group experiment, the 2 (Group: IP-Sham, RP-Sham) × 3 (Time Point) repeated-measures ANOVA on retention tests found a significant interaction, with IP-Sham showing lower TT at 6 h and 24 h; Fig. 2a reports "greater long-term performance due to ofﬂine improvement" for IP whereas RP showed forgetting.

> "a IP led to greater long-term performance due to ofﬂine improvement in the immediate (5 min), medium-term (6 h), and long- term (24 h) compared to the ﬁnal block of practice, whereas RP shows signi ﬁcant forgetting at all post-practice recall time points compared to the end of practice."
