Post-intervention five-item Likert survey after each of 68 simulated attempts, analyzed with Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel tests for trend and ANOVA with Tukey/Bonferroni pairwise comparisons. "less effective utilization" and "reduced ease" differences favored side-of-bed placement; CMH tests were significant for questions 1, 2, 3, and 5.
