Kang Eunhee, Kim Ryan Jin Young, Park Young-Seok, Park Shin-Young, Lee Jihyun. (2024). Developing institution-specific admission competency criteria for prospective health sciences students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06495-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of the same 301 responses using maximum likelihood estimation verified the EFA-derived structure. Fit indices printed were "χ2/df = 1.369; Bollen-Stein p < 0.05; CFI = 0.956, NFI = 0.858, IFI = 0.995, TLI = 0.944, RMSEA = 0.035", with significant estimates on every path.

> "The CFA results revealed that the ten-factor model of the competency criteria provided an acceptable level of fit to the data, suggesting that the model was appropriate for assessing the competency criteria"
