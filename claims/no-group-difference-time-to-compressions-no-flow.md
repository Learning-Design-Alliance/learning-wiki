## Evidence

### Creutzfeldt Johan 2012

Creutzfeldt Johan, Hedman Leif, Felländer-Tsai Li. (2012). Effects of pre-training using serious game technology on CPR performance – an exploratory quasi-experimental transfer study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-79

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Video-analysis measures in the three-group simulator study. First-scenario no-flow times were 7.9 (±2.0), 9.4 (±2.5) and 7.8 (±1.4) seconds; equivalence was not tested. Time to start of compressions also did not differ between groups.

> "The no-flow time (time after start of CPR, where no chest compressions were administered) did not differ significantly between groups (during first scenario: 18 months group 7.9 (±2.0) seconds, 6 months group 9.4 (±2.5) seconds and control group 7.8 (±1.4) respectively"
