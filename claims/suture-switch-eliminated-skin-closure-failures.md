Observation from the LC-CUSUM learning curve analysis: Expert 1's failures were concentrated in the first eight trials with 5-0 monocryl, attributed to "the high memory nature of the suture in addition to its smoothness"; Novice 1's failures likewise stopped after switching to V-Loc from Trial 6 onwards. This is a descriptive within-participant observation, not a controlled comparison.

> "After converting from 5‐‐0 monocryl to 4‐‐0 V‐Loc (from Trial 8 onwards), no failures occurred (Figure 4a)."
