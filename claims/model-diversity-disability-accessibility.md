### Markus Pössel 2018

Markus Pössel. (2018). Relatively complicated? Using models to teach general relativity at different levels. Invited talk, DPG Spring Meeting 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1812.11589](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1812.11589)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's argument for model diversity: alternatives such as a visual model or a suitable narrative let learners with different perceptual access understand the same concept, complemented by specific efforts to develop models suitable for people with disabilities. This is the author's argument, not a tested result.

> "Various types of disabilities mean that models that rely on visual or aural perception will not be accessible for a subset of the intended audience."
