## Evidence

### Chen Ding-Ping 2024

Chen Ding-Ping, Hour Ai-Ling, Tsao Kuo-Chien, Huang Chung-Guei, Lin Wei-Tzu, Hsu Fang-Ping. (2024). Examining the factors influencing academic performance of medical technology students in e-learning: A questionnaire survey. PLoS One 19(12): e0311528. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311528

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis within the middle-grade group (n = 11) in the Results section; individual items MSLQ_6 (r = 0.798), MSLQ_9 (r = 0.694), MSLQ_11 (r = 0.641), MSLQ_20 (r = 0.630), MSLQ_26 (r = 0.636), and ALSI_10 (r = 0.648) were also correlated. The article notes this indicates "confidence in one's ability to succeed plays a crucial role".
