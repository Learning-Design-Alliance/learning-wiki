Evidence¶

Alison Wellington 2016¶

Alison Wellington, Hanley Chiang, Kristin Hallgren, Cecilia Speroni, Mariesa Herrmann, Paul Burkander. (2016). Implementation and Impacts of Pay-for-Performance: The 2010 Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) Grantees After Three Years (Study Snapshot). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The snapshot's key findings report impacts from the third year of a national, multiyear evaluation of 2010 TIF grants, based on in-depth analysis in ten evaluation districts. It states pay-for-performance had "small, positive impacts on students’ math and reading achievement"; no effect sizes are printed in the snapshot.

"In 10 evaluation districts that had implemented Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) programs for three years, pay-for-performance had small, positive impacts on students’ math and reading achievement."
