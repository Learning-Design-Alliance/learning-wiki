Li 2023¶

Educational Psychology Review, 35(4), 119. doi:10.1007/s10648-023-09834-1 (read from the PsyArXiv preprint, doi:10.31234/osf.io/7yv6r)

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Two-stage MASEM (TSSEM) of the latent reading model (k = 458 independent samples) fit the data well (RMSEA = .014, CFI = .989). The study reports a "significant direct effect of SES on reading" plus parallel significant indirect effects through both mediators, i.e., partial rather than full mediation. The pooled studies are correlational, so the paths describe statistical mediation and cannot show that SES, a marker of family circumstances, causes the reading differences.

"The direct effect of SES on reading was significant, β = 0.12, 95 % LBCI [0.09, 0.16], and the model revealed significant indirect effects of SES on reading through PA (β = 0.09, 95 % LBCI [0.08, 0.11]) and VK (β = 0.09, 95 % LBCI [0.08, 0.10])"
