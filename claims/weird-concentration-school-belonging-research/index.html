The commentary's audit of systematic reviews and meta-analyses in the topical collection, mapped against three cultural taxonomies (Schwartz values, Inglehart-Welzel map, UN regions), found "the vast majority of empirical work is based in the United States" and other WEIRD countries; Table 2 lists ten non-WEIRD studies (Türkiye, Philippines, South Africa, China, Mexico, Ethiopia).

"Across K–12 STEM classrooms, postsecondary settings, and studies of school racial climate and belonging, the vast majority of empirical work is based in the United States and a small set of other WEIRD countries, with only a handful of studies from non-WEIRD contexts"
