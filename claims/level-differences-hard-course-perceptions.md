## Evidence

### Wyse 2018

Wyse, S. A., & Soneral, P. A. G. (2018). “Is This Class Hard?” Defining and Analyzing Academic Rigor from a Learner’s Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0278

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Open-ended survey coding of hard-course attributions from 100-level (n = 155) and 300-level (n = 39) students; chi-square comparison of response distributions found the patterns differed (χ2 = 39.016, df = 6, p = 0.00000071). 100-level students cited "low faculty support, as well as a fast pace, and facts that have unclear relevance"; 300-level students cited high cognitive demand, workload, and poor alignment.

> "Overall, the factors identified by 100- and 300-level students about what constitutes a difficult course differed (χ2 = 39.016, df = 6, p = 0.00000071; Figure 2)."
