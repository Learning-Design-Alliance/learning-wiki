## Evidence

### Rodriguez Buritica 2025

Rodriguez Buritica, J., Berboth, S., Hoferichter, F., & Raufelder, D. (2025). Neural and behavioural correlates of adolescents changing academic self‐concept. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 234–248. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12667

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal behavioural mixed-effects model of 39 adolescents' ASC, where trait-positivity scores were the proportion of chosen positive over negative adjectives per reference person. The authors report the three-way interaction "for the teacher‐condition (β = 0.69, t = 3.42, p < .001)" and its T2-only follow-up. Here, better achievement and more positive teacher evaluations predicted higher ASC (Figure 2).

> "Interestingly, we found a time x grade x trait‐positivity interaction for the teacher‐condition (β = 0.69, t = 3.42, p < .001). Separate regressions per time revealed a grade x teacher‐trait‐positivity interaction only for T2 (β = 0.4, t = 3.6, p < .001), but not for T1 (p = .9)."
