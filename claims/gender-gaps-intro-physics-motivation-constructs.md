## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Wilcoxon rank-sum tests show women have significantly lower average scores on all four motivational constructs (p < 0.001), with printed effect sizes in the small range (-0.26 physics identity, -0.23 perceived recognition, -0.23 self-efficacy, -0.29 interest). [→ Li 2023](#li-2023)

### Li 2023

Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2023). Statistically equivalent models with different causal structures: An example from physics identity. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2303.13786](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.13786)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics and Wilcoxon rank-sum tests on end-of-semester surveys from 427 women and 776 men in introductory calculus-based physics 1. The article reports "women have significantly lower average scores in all four motivational constructs", with printed effect sizes of -0.26 (physics identity), -0.23 (perceived recognition), -0.23 (self-efficacy), and -0.29 (interest), all p < 0.001.
