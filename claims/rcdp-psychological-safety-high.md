## Evidence

### Manetti Previero Bruno 2026

Manetti Previero Bruno, Spataru Ana, Pinto Ruxandra, Piquette Dominique. (2026). Rapid cycle deliberate practice: a complement to clinical exposure in critical care education. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.1093/atsscholar/aapaf003

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-session survey study using Edmondson's psychological safety questionnaire after weekly RCDP sessions (weeks 1–3). The article reports 71 surveys completed, near-universal perceived safety, and no effect of facilitator number (P = 1.00).

> "A total of 71 anonymous psychological safety surveys were completed after the weekly RCPD simulations over the study period. Participants almost universally perceived the RCPD sessions as being psychologically safe (Figure 3B)."
