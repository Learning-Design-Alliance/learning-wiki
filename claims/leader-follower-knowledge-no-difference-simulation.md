## Evidence

### Vattanavanit Veerapong 2020

Vattanavanit Veerapong, Khwannimit Bodin, Nilmoje Thanapon. (2020). Comparison of knowledge and confidence between medical students as leaders and followers in simulated resuscitation. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5e01.f00c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison within a 3-hour simulation course of 139 fifth-year medical students (32 leaders, 107 followers) using independent-samples t-tests; the study reports "were not different (t(137)= -0.81, p=0.42)". Equivalence was not tested.

> "The post-test scores between the leaders (M=6.72, SD=1.51) and followers (M=6.93, SD=1.26) were not different (t(137)= -0.81, p=0.42)."
