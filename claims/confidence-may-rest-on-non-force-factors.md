Author interpretation in the discussion section, not a tested result. The article offers this as a hypothesis for further exploration, stating confidence "might be built based on factors other than" force–time modulation ability.

> "Even though our study focused on the SMT technique that students are most confident in, we did not find an association between confidence and all SMT skills. Therefore, confidence might be built based on factors other than the ability to modulate force–time characteristics, such as body anthropometrics"
