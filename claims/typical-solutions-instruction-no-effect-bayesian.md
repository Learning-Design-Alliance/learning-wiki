## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Loibl 2024

Loibl, K., & Leuders, T. (2024). Can failure be made productive also in Bayesian reasoning? A conceptual replication study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09670-y

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Same 2×2 experiment (N = 75): the ANOVA on conceptual knowledge found no significant effect of the factor type of instruction, i.e., instruction with vs. without typical student solutions (F(1,71) = 0.35, p = .56, ηp² = 0.005). Descriptively, conditions with typical solutions averaged 2.5 points vs. 1.3 without, but the difference was not significant.
