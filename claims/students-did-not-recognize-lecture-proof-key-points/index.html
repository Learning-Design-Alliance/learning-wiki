Evidence¶

Karavi 2022¶

Karavi, Mali, & Avraamidou. (2022). Commognition as an approach to studying proof teaching in university mathematics lectures. EURASIA Journal of Mathematics, Science and Technology Education. https://doi.org/10.29333/ejmste/12173

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Lew et al. (2016), an analysis of one lecture's proof classified by de Villiers' purposes of proof, with student learning examined via listening, encoding, recording, and reviewing skills. The review attributes the finding that "students did not recognize the identified points on the proof".

"It appeared that students did not recognize the identified points on the proof. One possible explanation was that students paid attention only to what was written on the blackboard."
