## Evidence

### Astroth C 2026

Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reports conflicting findings: Bostwick & Alexander (n=516) found 3–4 week psychiatry clerkships scored lower than 6 weeks (p=0.03; 0.01), while Monrad et al. (n=515) found no significant difference after 25% reduction.

> "when the University of Michigan shortened all clerkships by 25%, it did not result in significant changes on any of the NBME shelf exams [40]."
