Jessica Van Brummelen and Phoebe Lin. (2021). Engaging Teachers to Co-Design Integrated AI Curriculum for K-12 Classrooms. arXiv:2009.11100v1. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1145/3411764.3445377](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445377)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of a two-day co-design workshop with fifteen K-12 teachers; audio recordings and questionnaires were thematically coded by two researchers using open coding. The analysis identified four shared value categories, including "Evaluation, Engagement, Logistics, and Collaboration". No effect size is printed.

> "We identified four categories of values and considerations that our teachers had while creating the curriculum drafts: Evaluation, Engagement, Logistics, and Collaboration."
