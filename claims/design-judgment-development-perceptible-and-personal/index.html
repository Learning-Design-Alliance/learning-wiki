Finding from the qualitative multi-case study of eight ID students across four institutions, drawn from thematic analysis of interviews and design documents. The article reports that "the development of their design judgment is both perceptible and personal." Case tables show divergent individual patterns, e.g., Husain's and Garry's differing trajectories.
