> "For the past year, Kettle Moraine School District in Wisconsin has been implementing micro-credentials to provide educators with access to personalized learning opportunities and recognition. To sweeten the deal, the district provides educators with an increase in their base pay for every micro-credential they earn. The district has seen greater collaboration and collegiality within schools."
