## Evidence

### Amy C. Crosson & Margaret G. McKeown 2016

Amy C. Crosson & Margaret G. McKeown. (2016). Middle School Learners' Use of Latin Roots to Infer the Meaning of Unfamiliar Words. Cognition and Instruction, 34(2), 148-171. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1145121

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative coding of 262 seventh-grade dynamic assessment episodes (52 Honors RAVE, 106 RAVE, 104 control) classified problem-solving strategies; the direct route was, in the authors' words, "the most common for all students," with intervention groups using it more frequently than controls.

> "Although this strategy was the most common for all students, both RAVE and Honors RAVE students employed this strategy more frequently than students in the control group (Table 4)."
