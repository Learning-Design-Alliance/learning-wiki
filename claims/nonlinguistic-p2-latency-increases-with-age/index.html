Karns Christina M. 2015¶

Karns Christina M., Isbell Elif, Giuliano Ryan J., Neville Helen J. (2015). Auditory attention in childhood and adolescence: An event-related potential study of spatial selective attention to one of two simultaneous stories. Developmental cognitive neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.03.001

q3 · i? · associational · r1

In the same cross-sectional dichotic-listening ERP study, the Age-group ANOVA on nonlinguistic P2 latency showed the latency "grew longer with age", F(3.71) = 9.6, p < .0001, in the opposite direction to the age-related shortening of P1 latency.

"the latency of the P2 grew longer with age from 193 ms in 10-year-olds, to 200 ms in 13-year-olds, 202 ms in 16-year-olds, and 209 ms in adults (Figure 3; F(3.71) = 9.6, p < .0001)."
