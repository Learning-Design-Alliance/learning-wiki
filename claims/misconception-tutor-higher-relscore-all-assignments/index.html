Related Claims¶

  • MisconceptionTutor's advantage in feedback success (SuccScore) is mixed: substantial on earlier assignments, diminishing or reversing on later ones — related
  • Engagement-based metrics are more robust predictors of students' perceived feedback helpfulness than pedagogical dimensions — related
  • Pedagogically desired feedback is engaged with more (higher RelScore), but pedagogical quality shows little relation to whether actions are applied correctly (SuccScore) — related
  • MisconceptionTutor achieves higher pedagogical quality (DAMR) than BaselineTutor on nearly all dimensions, with small-to-moderate effect sizes — related
  • For revealing_answer, pedagogically undesired feedback achieves higher SuccScore in both semesters, suggesting success via copying revealed answers — related
