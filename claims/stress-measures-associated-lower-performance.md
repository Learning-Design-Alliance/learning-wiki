## Evidence

### Stegers-Jager KM 2020

Stegers-Jager KM, Savas M, van der Waal J, van Rossum EFC, Woltman AM. (2020). Gender-specific effects of raising Year-1 standards on medical students' academic performance and stress levels. Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable logistic regressions in the subsample with both PSS and HCC data (n = 135 and n = 133); the article reports both stress measures "significantly associated with Year-1 performance" with the printed ORs; these are unstandardised odds ratios, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "In separate models including cohort and gender, both PSS (Wald χ2 (1) = 33.35, P < .001; OR 0.91, 95% CI 0.89-0.94) and HCC (Wald χ2 (1) = 4.17, P < .05; OR 0.39, 95% CI 0.16-0.96) were significantly associated with Year-1 performance"
