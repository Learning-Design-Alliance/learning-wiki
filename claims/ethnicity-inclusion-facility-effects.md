## Evidence

### Mohan Jenanan 2025

Mohan Jenanan, Pishia Michaela, Alim Duaa, McGown Patrick J., Amin Anjali, Pinder Richard J., Halse Omid, Brown Celia, Sam Amir H. (2025). The impact of ethnicity information in single best answer questions on student performance, item response time and psychometric properties. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10489-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level analysis of 195 question pairs from the randomised parallel-forms exam; paired t-test on facility. The article reports a "mean difference of −0.5 pp ... not statistically significant (p = 0.17)".

> "Questions with ethnicity had a mean difference of −0.5 pp (standard deviation = 4.86) compared to questions without ethnicity, which was not statistically significant (p = 0.17)."

### Mohan Jenanan 2025 (2)

Mohan Jenanan, Pishia Michaela, Alim Duaa, McGown Patrick J., Amin Anjali, Pinder Richard J., Halse Omid, Brown Celia, Sam Amir H. (2025). The impact of ethnicity information in single best answer questions on student performance, item response time and psychometric properties. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10489-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank test on absolute facility differences across 195 paired items, used because ethnicity could raise or lower difficulty. The article reports a "median absolute difference in facility was 3.1 pp".

> "the median absolute difference in facility was 3.1 pp, a statistically significant finding (W = 19110, p < 0.01)."
