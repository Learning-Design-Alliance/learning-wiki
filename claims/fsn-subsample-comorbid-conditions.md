## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Subsample analyses (n = 42 ADHD elementary; n = 34 ASD, n = 45 ADHD, n = 38 comorbid anxiety preschool) produced effect sizes of similar magnitude to full samples, many in the large range (e.g., 1.16 on teacher-reported SSBD-ABI). [→ Andy J. Frey 2023](#andy-j-frey-2023)

## Evidence

### Andy J. Frey 2023

Andy J. Frey, Jason W. Small, Hill M. Walker, Brandon Mitchell, John R. Seeley, Edward G. Feil, Jon Lee, and Steven R. Forness. (2023). First Step Next: A Best-Evidence Synthesis of Replication Randomized Controlled Trials From 2009 to 2021. Remedial and Special Education, 44(1), 60-72. https://doi.org/10.1177/07419325211068145

`q3 · i3` · `review · r2`

Preschool ADHD subsample analysis (Feil et al., 2016; n = 45 of 126) from the Feil et al. (2014) RCT. The review reports a large ES of 1.16 on teacher-reported SSBD-ABI and SSiS-SS and a medium ES of 0.65 on parent-reported SSiS-SS, of similar magnitude to full-sample effects.

> "ESs across prosocial and PB domains ranged from 0.65 (medium; improvement index = +24.2) on the parent-reported SSiS-SS to 1.16 (large; improvement index = +37.7) on both the teacher-reported SSBD-ABI and the teacher-reported SSiS-SS."
