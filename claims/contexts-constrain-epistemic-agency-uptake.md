### Odden 2022

Odden, T. O. B., Silvia, D., & Malthe-Sørenssen, A. (2022). Using Computational Essays to Redistribute Epistemic Agency in Undergraduate Science. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1002/tea.21821](https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21821)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative analysis of two implementations found key differences: UiO provided more example essays, seed programs, and pair work options; MSU required novel topics, used template notebooks, and provided more oversight through proposals and project updates. The authors conclude that 'educational contexts, scaffolding, expectations, and student backgrounds can constrain and influence the ways in which students choose to take up epistemic agency.'
