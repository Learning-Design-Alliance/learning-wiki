## Evidence

### Burnette 2012

Burnette, J. L., O'Boyle, E., VanEpps, E. M., Pollack, J. M., & Finkel, E. J. (2012). Mindsets Matter: A Meta-Analytic Review of Implicit Theories and Self-Regulation. Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0029531

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 113 samples (total unique N = 28,217; 68% academic domains; ages 5-42) demonstrated that "implicit theories predict distinct self-regulatory processes, which, in turn, predict goal achievement." No printed effect size for the achievement link appears in the supplied text.

> "meta-analytic results (total unique N = 28,217; k = 113) across diverse achievement domains (68% academic) and populations (age range = 5-42; 10 different nationalities; 58% from United States; 44% female) demonstrated that implicit theories predict distinct self-regulatory processes, which, in turn, predict goal achievement."
