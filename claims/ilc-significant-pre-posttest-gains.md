## Evidence

### Azer Samy A 2013

Azer Samy A, Hasanato Rana, Al-Nassar Sami, Somily Ali, AlSaadi Muslim M. (2013). Introducing integrated laboratory classes in a PBL curriculum: impact on student's learning and satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-71

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pretest-posttest analysis in two of the four ILCs (liver function tests, n = 202; adrenal function, n = 206 completed both tests). The article reports "Paired t-test showed that the difference was significant (P < 0.001) in both tests." No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Students got higher scores in posttests compared to pre-test scores in two classes (12.68 ± 2.03 vs 6.58 ± 3.39 and 13.02 ± 2.03 vs 7.43 ± 2.68, respectively). Paired t-test showed that the difference was significant (P < 0.001) in both tests."
