Editorial interpretation of the special-issue contributions, identifying gaps rather than reporting a tested result. Most authors also pointed out the need for a more advanced understanding of the methods used, including guidelines for setting parameters and significance levels.

> "These papers analysed self-regulated learning at a micro level and a number of authors (Bannert et al. 2014; Kinnebrew et al. 2014 and Molenaar & Chiu ( 2014) signposted the need for more micro level theory to further articulate our understanding of the temporal characteristics of self and socially regulated learning."
