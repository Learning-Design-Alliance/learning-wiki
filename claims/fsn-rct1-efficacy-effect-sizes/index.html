Evidence¶

Andy J. Frey 2023¶

Andy J. Frey, Jason W. Small, Hill M. Walker, Brandon Mitchell, John R. Seeley, Edward G. Feil, Jon Lee, and Steven R. Forness. (2023). First Step Next: A Best-Evidence Synthesis of Replication Randomized Controlled Trials From 2009 to 2021. Remedial and Special Education, 44(1), 60-72. https://doi.org/10.1177/07419325211068145

q3 · i2 · review · r2

Classroom-level randomized efficacy trial with 200 first- through third-grade students from 34 elementary schools in Albuquerque Public Schools. The review reports effect sizes from "0.44 (moderately small) to 0.87 (large)" and states all intervention-control differences were significant.

"Cohen' s d ESs ranged from 0.44 (moderately small) to 0.87 (large) and improvement indices ranged from +17.0 to +30.8 across prosocial, PB, and AET domains."
