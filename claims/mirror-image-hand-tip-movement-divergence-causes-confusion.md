`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Schematic analysis (Fig. 4) of right-hand forceps movement from the left-deep-distal to the right-superficial-proximal spot under mirror image: the tip moves straight while the hand first moves right then makes "a large movement to the left side", which the authors consider a cause of confusion.

> "The tip of the forceps moves in a straight line, but the forceps’ hand arcs greatly. The hand needs to be moved toward the right at first, followed by a large movement to the left side."
