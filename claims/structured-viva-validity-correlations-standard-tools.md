## Evidence

### Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan 2023

Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan, Nabag Wisal Omer Mohamed. (2023). Structured viva validity, reliability, and acceptability as an assessment tool in health professions education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04524-6

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review of validity studies: three studies estimated validity by correlation with criterion variables, showing "a highly significant correlation coefficient (r = 0.52, p < 0.01)" with MCQs, and correlations of 0.48 to 0.51 with MCQs and OSCE. The traditional viva correlation was not significant.

> "A positive highly significant correlation (r = 0.442, p = 0.001) was seen between marks scored in structured viva and structured theory exam while it was not significant (r = 0.202, p = 0.151) for the marks of traditional viva and structured theory exam"
