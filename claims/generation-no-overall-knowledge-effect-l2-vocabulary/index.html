Evidence¶

John Duplice 2025¶

John Duplice. (2025). Generation and L2 Vocabulary Learning: A Classroom Action Study on the Efficacy of the Generation Desirable Difficulty in Learning L2 Vocabulary. Vocabulary Learning and Instruction, 14(1). https://doi.org/10.29140/vli.v14n1.102482

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Classroom action study: generalized linear mixed model (binomial) of Knowledge scores for 23 Japanese university ELLs, generation vs baseline conditions across pre-test and two post-tests. The article reports the overall treatment effect "found not to be significant."

"The overall effects of treatment (β = –0.19, z = – 0.44, p = .661) were found not to be significant."
