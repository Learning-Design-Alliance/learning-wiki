### Runcong Zhao 2025

Runcong Zhao, Artem Bobrov, Jiazheng Li, Cesare Aloisi, Yulan He. (2025). LearnLens: LLM-Enabled Personalised, Curriculum-Grounded Feedback with Educators in the Loop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04295 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2507.04295](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2507.04295)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study with 30 participants, all with prior STEM teaching experience at high school or college level, who interacted with the system and completed a structured questionnaire. The article reports "the mean rating never falls below 4.1/5", with relevance to instructional goals at 4.4/5 and scientific accuracy at 4.3/5.

> "Across the nine Likert-scale items (Q1, Q3, Q5–Q10) the mean rating never falls below 4.1/5, signalling a uniformly positive reception."
