## Evidence

### Espelage 2016

Espelage, D. L., Rose, C. A., & Polanin, J. R. (2016). Social-Emotional Learning Program to Promote Prosocial and Academic Skills Among Middle School Students With Disabilities. Remedial and Special Education, 37(6), 323–332. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932515627475

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wave 2 ANCOVA of the seven-item Empathic Concern scale. The discussion reports that although some gains were noted after 2 years, "at the end of the study, there were no notable differences between students in the two conditions."

> "The empathy outcome actually decreased for intervention students, but the decrease was not statistically significant."
