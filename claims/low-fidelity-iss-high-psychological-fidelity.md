Wang Chih Jung, Lin Su Yueh, Tsai Sheng Han, Shan Yan Shen. (2019). Implications of long-term low-fidelity in situ simulation in acute care and association with a reduction in unexpected cardiac arrests: A retrospective research study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0213789
