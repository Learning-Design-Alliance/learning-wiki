# Publication bias was detected only for self-efficacy (Egger's regression intercept=−6.54, p=0.018), with no effect-size change under Trim and Fill

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Egger's regression showed significant publication bias for self-efficacy (intercept=−6.54, p=0.018), but Trim and Fill found no change in the effect size (d=0.05, 95% CI −0.45 to 0.55); no significant publication bias was detected for the other outcomes. [→ La Cerra Carmen 2019](#la-cerra-carmen-2019)

## Evidence

### La Cerra Carmen 2019

Effects of high-fidelity simulation based on life-threatening clinical condition scenarios on learning outcomes of undergraduate and postgraduate nursing students: a systematic review and meta-analysis. `q3 · i?`

Publication-bias assessment across the meta-analysed outcomes using Egger's regression, Trim and Fill, and fail-safe number (which was 0). Bias was significant only for self-efficacy.

> "For self-efficacy the Egger's regression showed a significant risk of publication bias (intercept=−6.54, p=0.018), even if no change in the effect size was found by the Trim and Fill method between the observed and estimated values (d=0.05, 95% CI [−0.45 to 0.55])"
