# GenAI's immediate answers reduce engineering students' teacher-student interactions for generalizable tasks, while teachers remain preferred for complex problem-solving

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Tiina Leino Lindell 2026

Tiina Leino Lindell, Christian Stöhr. (2026). The AI disruption in engineering education: an analysis of changing student norms through cultural historical activity theory. Journal of Computing in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-025-09488-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' conclusion to the teacher-role theme of the interview study. A student explained: "Often, it takes longer to ask the teacher, and ChatGPT gives immediate answers", valuing the feeling of having figured it out alone; another skipped early-morning TA exercises, asking ChatGPT instead.

> "To conclude, the participating students' use of GenAI reduces teacher-student interactions when performing generalizable tasks."
