Two test-administration comparisons in Normal College groups: 54 freshmen and 64 sophomores given the Thurstone and Brown University tests. The article prints correlations of 0.74 (freshmen) and 0.613 (sophomores) and wrong-class percentages of 26 and 32.8.

> "The Thurstone and the Brown University tests when similarly given to 54 students of college freshmen grade showed a median change in rank of 6.7 places, as compared with a maximum possible change of 27 and a random change of 18."
