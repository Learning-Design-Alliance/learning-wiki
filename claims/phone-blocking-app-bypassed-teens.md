Emre Sezgin, Simon Lin. (2019). Technology-Based Interventions, Assessments, and Solutions for Safe Driving Training for Adolescents: Rapid Review. JMIR mHealth and uHealth. https://doi.org/10.2196/11942

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the Creaser et al three-group study of a phone blocking app (app only, app plus parent reports, and observed control with 274 teens and 272 parents per Table 2); "teens were able to find a way around blocking" despite low observed phone use.

> "Even though the study data revealed low use of the phone during driving, self-reported data showed that teens were able to find a way around blocking, and use of a mandatory setting would not have been helpful in the long run."
