## Evidence

### Lammerding-Koeppel Maria 2017

Lammerding-Koeppel Maria, Giesler Marianne, Gornostayeva Maryna, Narciss Elisabeth, Wosnik Annette, Zipfel Stephan, Griewatz Jan, Fritze Olaf. (2017). Monitoring and analysis of the change process in curriculum mapping compared to the National Competency-based Learning Objective Catalogue for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) at four medical faculties. Part II: Key factors for motivating the faculty during the process. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001083](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001083)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the condensation measure applied in adapted variants at all four locations; the article reports student pre-mapping "has proved itself" and produced a blueprint. No effect size is printed.

> ""Rough preliminary mapping" by students has proved itself: they highlighted which NKLM contents turned up in which course. That yielded a mapping plan or blueprint"

### Lammerding-Koeppel Maria 2017 (2)

Lammerding-Koeppel Maria, Giesler Marianne, Gornostayeva Maryna, Narciss Elisabeth, Wosnik Annette, Zipfel Stephan, Griewatz Jan, Fritze Olaf. (2017). Monitoring and analysis of the change process in curriculum mapping compared to the National Competency-based Learning Objective Catalogue for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) at four medical faculties. Part II: Key factors for motivating the faculty during the process. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001083](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001083)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same analysis; the article reports portioning with time limits led to a temporary reduction in data quantity and effort, easier planning and early intermediate results. No effect size is printed.

> "Portioning the NKLM by dividing the catalogue into manageable chapters and mapping with time limits (e.g.: Chapters 5 to 11 and Chapter 14, Paragraphs a to c during the first mapping round). That approach led to a temporary reduction in the quantity of data and required effort"
