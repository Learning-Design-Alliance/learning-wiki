# GPT-4 achieves a pooled MCQ accuracy of 81% on medical licensing examinations, significantly surpassing GPT-3.5's 58%

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 29 studies, GPT-4's integrated accuracy on medical licensing examination MCQs was 81% (95% CI 78-84), versus 58% (95% CI 53-63) for GPT-3.5 across 25 studies. [→ Mingxin Liu 2024](#mingxin-liu-2024)

## Evidence

### Mingxin Liu 2024

Mingxin Liu, Tsuyoshi Okuhara, XinYi Chang, Ritsuko Shirabe, Yuriko Nishiie, Hiroko Okada, Takahiro Kiuchi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT Across Different Versions in Medical Licensing Examinations Worldwide: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/60807

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 25 studies reporting GPT-3.5 accuracy and 29 reporting GPT-4 accuracy, using random-effects models owing to significant heterogeneity (GPT-3.5: I²=95% and GPT-4 I²=93%). The review reports "The integrated accuracy for GPT-3.5 was 58%" and 81% for GPT-4.

> "The integrated accuracy for GPT-3.5 was 58% (95% CI 53-63; P<.01), and the integrated accuracy for GPT-4 was 81% (95% CI 78-84; P<.01"
