WestEd's review of other states' reclassification policies, summarized in Figures 1a and 1b for school year 2023-24 (excluding South Dakota due to unavailable information). The report concludes California's policy "is out of step with other states' practices."

"As of the 2023–24 school year, California was the only state that used four criteria for exiting EL classification (see Figure 1a) and one of only a small number of states (seven in total) that use any data sources other than their statewide ELP assessments to inform exit decisions"
