## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Intervention classes using a 10-question argumentative script showed a significantly lower mean number of argumentations with five or fewer nodes (a depth measure) than control classes, with a large effect size. [→ Hsu 2015](#hsu-2015)

## Evidence

### Hsu 2015

Hsu, P.-S., Van Dyke, M., & Chen, Y. (2015). Examining the effect of teacher guidance on collaborative argumentation in middle level classrooms. RMLE Online, 38(9). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1074885

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with six seventh-grade science classes randomly assigned to intervention or control; an independent-samples t test on the depth measure found "a significant difference between the means in the depth feature" with t (4) = -2.817, p < .05, d = 2.3.

> "This study indicated a significant difference between the means in the depth feature (i.e., argumentation with 5 or fewer nodes) of the two groups (t (4) = -2.817, p < .05), with a large effect size of d = 2.3."

### Hsu 2015 (2)

Hsu, P.-S., Van Dyke, M., & Chen, Y. (2015). Examining the effect of teacher guidance on collaborative argumentation in middle level classrooms. RMLE Online, 38(9). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1074885

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experiment: the intervention mean on argumentations with five or fewer nodes was "significantly lower (m = 7, sd = 1.73)" than the control mean (m = 14.33, sd = 4.16), indicating fewer shallow, underdeveloped argumentations under script-based guidance.

> "The mean of the intervention group was significantly lower (m = 7, sd = 1.73) than the mean of the control group (m = 14.33, sd = 4.16)."
