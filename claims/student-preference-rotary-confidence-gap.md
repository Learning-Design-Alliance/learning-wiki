## Evidence

### El-Ma’aita Ahmad M. 2024

El-Ma’aita Ahmad M., Mahasneh Sari A., Hamandi Maryam A., Al-Rabab’ah Mohammad A. (2024). Molar root canal treatment performed by undergraduate dental students; an observational study of procedural errors and student perception. BMC Medical Education 24. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05397-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online questionnaire (piloted on 10 students, tested for face and content validity, with test-retest reliability checked on the first 10 respondents) administered to the 109 consenting students. It recorded technique preference and "self-perceived confidence to perform molar RCT without supervision".

> "Most students (93.1%) reported that they preferred RI over manual, while 6.9% of them had no preference. More than one quarter of the students (26.4%) reported that they did not feel confident to perform molar RCT without supervision."
