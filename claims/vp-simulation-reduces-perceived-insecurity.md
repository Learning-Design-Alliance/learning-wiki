## Evidence

### Cecil Julia 2026

Cecil Julia, Kokje Eesha, Kleine Anne-Kathrin, Schaffernak Insa, Vogel Lea, Angerer Selina, Lermer Eva, Gaube Susanne. (2026). The effect of AI-enabled virtual patient simulation on training outcomes and insecurities in psychotherapy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09917-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM analyses in the randomized N = 87 study showed a significant main effect of time for overall insecurity and all subdomains except building a therapist-patient relationship; VP-group contrasts were significant, e.g. "overall insecurity (estimate = −0.68".

> "Within-group contrasts showed significant pre-post reductions in the VP group for overall insecurity (estimate = −0.68, SE = 0.19, t(83) = −3.66, p < .001), insecurity in dealing with complex symptoms (estimate = −1.03, SE = 0.25, t(83) = −4.07, p < .001), insecurity about practical skills (estimate = −1.06, SE = 0.23, t(83) = −4.70, p < .001)"
