`q3 i3` Across 4 RCTs, PBL teaching significantly increased case analysis scores by a mean of 1.54 compared with LBL (95% CI, 0.86–2.23; P < .00001). [→ Ma Yimei MM 2019](#ma-yimei-mm-2019)

## Evidence

### Ma Yimei MM 2019

Ma Yimei MM, Lu Xiaoxi MD, Hayashi Shogo. (2019). The effectiveness of problem-based learning in pediatric medical education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000014052

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 4 studies reporting case analysis scores (154 PBL and 142 LBL students), random-effects model due to heterogeneity (P = .0002, I2 = 85%); the pooled standardized mean difference (SMD = 1.54, a large effect) favored PBL, reported as "a mean of 1.54".

> "The meta-analysis of the case analysis scores found that the PBL teaching model significantly increased case analysis scores by a mean of 1.54 compared with the LBL teaching model (95% CI, 0.86–2.23; P < .00001)."
