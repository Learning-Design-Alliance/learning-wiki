Enrollment and completion figures reported in the Evaluation section for the first course iteration: 35 enrolled, 19 took the final assignment, 3 auditors, and a "total drop-out rate is then about 35%". No effect size is printed.

> "among the 35 students who enrolled in the class, the final assignment was taken by 19 students. To this number, we have to add 3 students who joined the class as auditors and did not take the exam. The total drop-out rate is then about 35%, which is in line with the average of TU Wien for elective classes."
