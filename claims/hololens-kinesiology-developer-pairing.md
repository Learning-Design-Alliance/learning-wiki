Janelle Resch, Ireneusz (Eric) Ocelewski, Judy Ehrentraut, and Mike Barnett-Cowan. (2019). Gamified Automation in Immersive Media for Education and Research. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1901.00729](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1901.00729)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Second case study in Section 2: an instructor enabled each student in his class to rapidly develop a mixed-reality demonstration of how the central nervous system integrates multisensory information, using Microsoft HoloLens and developer-student pairing. No outcome measures are reported.

> "Using the Microsoft HoloLens and pairing undergraduate students in kinesiology with little to no background in computer programming with experienced UE4 developers, students were able to focus on their research concepts of potential MR neuroscience experiments while developers focused on rapid development while teaching students how to code."
