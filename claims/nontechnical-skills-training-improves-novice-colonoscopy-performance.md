The article reports, citing the randomized trial by Walsh et al, that novice trainees given an additional hour of didactic nontechnical skills training "demonstrated significant improvements in overall performance during their first colonoscopy procedures." No effect size is printed in this article.

> "in the randomized trial by Walsh et al,7 novice trainees who received an additional hour of didactic nontechnical skills training (covering communication, leadership, situational awareness, team work, professionalism, and decision making) demonstrated significant improvements in overall performance during their first colonoscopy procedures."
