# SES does not moderate genetic, shared, or nonshared environmental variance in four-year-olds' learning motivation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A univariate twin model of motivation found no statistically significant SES moderation of genetic, shared environmental, or nonshared environmental variance in motivation. [→ Tucker-Drob 2011](#tucker-drob-2011)

## Evidence

### Tucker-Drob 2011

Tucker-Drob, Elliot M., & Harden, K. Paige. (2011). Learning Motivation Mediates Gene-by-Socioeconomic Status Interaction on Mathematics Achievement in Early Childhood. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2011.11.015

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate behavioral genetic model of parent-rated learning motivation in the ECLS-B twin sample. In the final reduced model, genes accounted for 49% and the nonshared environment 51% of remaining motivation variance; equivalence was not tested, only nonsignificance reported.

> "The full Step 1 model indicated no statistically significant evidence for SES moderation of the magnitude of genetic, shared environmental, or non-shared environmental variance in motivation."
