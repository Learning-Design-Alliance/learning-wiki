Profile description from standardized indicator means (Table 4, Figure 1) for the High-Perception High-Vigilance profile (n = 23, 6.1%). The authors call this "suggestive configurational evidence of opportunity-threat coactivation."

"This profile showed markedly above-average scores on all six indicators. Perceived humanlikeness, adaptability, and quality were especially high, indicating strong perceptions of AI capability, while AI creative self-efficacy was also substantially above average."
