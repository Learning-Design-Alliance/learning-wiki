Andrade Italo Rigoberto Cavalcante; de Queiroz Johny Carlos; Farias Maria Sinara; Maia Nicole Paulo da Silva; da Silva Lúcia de Fátima. (2025). Clinical simulation in the teaching of cardiovascular physical examination with nursing students: a systematic review. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2023-0429

q3 · i? · review · r3

Randomized clinical trial with 84 nursing students (42 per group) reported in the review's Chart 4 and results section; the simulation group's first post-test confidence was significantly higher than the control group's, with the printed means and t-test statistic quoted here. No standardized effect size is printed.

"An independent t-test revealed a statistically significant difference (t(67) = -42.95, p < 0.001) in terms of confidence in applying physical examination skills in the first post-test, following the intervention, between the experimental group (mean = 24.00, Standard Deviation (SD) = 1.89) and the control group (mean = 8.02, SD = 1.43)."
