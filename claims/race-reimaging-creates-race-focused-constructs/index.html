Jessica T. DeCuir-Gunby · Paul A. Schutz. (2024). The Evolution of Race-Focused and Race-Reimaged Approaches in Educational Psychology: Future Directions for the Field. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09873-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

This is the authors' theoretical argument about the relationship between the two approaches, illustrated with the example of emotion regulation being reimaged into racialized emotion regulation after a consistent pattern of findings. No empirical test is reported.

"As a construct becomes race-reimaged, as its components and conceptualizations change, and as more evidence is collected, it will no longer be the original construct. It will instead develop into a race-focused construct."
