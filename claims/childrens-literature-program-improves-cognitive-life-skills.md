# A children-literature-based EFL program significantly improved fifth-grade pupils' cognitive life skills versus control

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Fifth-grade EFL pupils taught through a children-literature program scored significantly higher than control pupils on a cognitive life skills situation test (η² = .73). [→ Abdelhalim 2015](#abdelhalim-2015)

## Evidence

### Abdelhalim 2015

Safaa M. Abdelhalim. (2015). Children Literature Based Program for Developing EFL Primary Pupils’ Life Skills and Language Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p178

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 90 fifth-grade EFL pupils in three schools; ANOVA on the situation test post-test showed a large effect size (η² = .73) favoring experimental groups over control. The article reports "the proposed program had a large effect on experimental groups students’ life skills".

> "Table 1 reveals that there is a statistically significant difference between the two Experimental groups, and the control group students’ mean scores on the post-administration of the three life skills measures and the language learning strategies questionnaire at the level of (0.01), in favor of the experimental groups"
