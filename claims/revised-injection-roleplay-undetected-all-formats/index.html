Related Claims¶

  • Basic prompt injection attempts failed or were detected: instruction manipulation alone failed in all 9 iterations, role-playing without concealment was detected in output, and metadata-hidden injections failed in all 9 iterations — related
  • Copilot's detection of one advanced injection did not persist: after reporting an embedded instruction once, the same file's injection succeeded in six subsequent iterations without warning — related
  • Indirect prompt injections combining instruction manipulation, role-playing, and obfuscation altered AI-assigned grades with attack success rates of 94–100% while remaining undetected — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
