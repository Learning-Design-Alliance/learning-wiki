## Evidence

### Amrita Ganguly 2026

Amrita Ganguly, Aditya Johri, Nora McDonald, Areej Ali, Umama Dewan, and Aayushi Hingle Collier. (2026). A Comparative Analysis of Institutional and Course Generative AI Policies within Higher Education: Implications for Instruction in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12296

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Course syllabus coding of 98 CS syllabi found anthropomorphism exclusively at the course level; Table 3 shows an example syllabus advising students to imagine GenAI systems as a helpful but fallible classmate. The discussion links this to faculty and student mental models of teaching.

> "39% (N=38) syllabi were coded as Anthropomorphism of GenAI meaning they attributed living characteristics to GenAI, either as a GenAI Assistant (36%, N=35) or otherwise providing Characterization/ Personification of GenAI (5%, N=5)."
