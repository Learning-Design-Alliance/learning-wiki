# Goal affordances, goal incongruity, and belonging show no association with global life satisfaction among Indigenous STEM students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Neither communal nor agentic affordances, incongruities, nor belonging correlated with life satisfaction, contrary to hypotheses. [→ Yu Fanyi 2025](#yu-fanyi-2025)

## Evidence

### Yu Fanyi 2025

Yu Fanyi, Nyanamba Juliet M., Anderson Brittany, DeerInWater Kathy, Estrada Mica, Hosoda K. Kanoho, Maldonado Natalia, Smith Tiffany, Flores Lilibeth, & Hernandez Paul R. (2025). Navigating Science Pathways: Belonging Mediates the Impact of Goal Congruity on Persistence (but Not Life Satisfaction) Among Indigenous Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000967

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlational analyses (Table 3) in the longitudinal study of Indigenous STEM participants tested associations between goal measures at T2 and life satisfaction at T3. The article reports no correlation; equivalence was not tested, and no effect size is printed.

> "Contrary to our expectations, life satisfaction was not correlated with goal affordances, goal incongruity, or belonging."
