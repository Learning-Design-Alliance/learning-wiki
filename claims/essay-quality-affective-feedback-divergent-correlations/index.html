Evidence¶

Banihashem 2024¶

Banihashem, S. K., Taghizadeh Kerman, N., Noroozi, O., Moon, J., & Drachsler, H. (2024). Feedback sources in essay writing: peer-generated or AI-generated feedback? International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-024-00455-4

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Spearman's correlation analysis (Table 2) of essay quality against the affective component of feedback. Overall correlations printed are r = 0.28* for ChatGPT feedback and r = -0.23* for peer feedback (p < 0.05), small-to-moderate correlations in opposite directions.

"However, a positive correlation was observed between the quality of the essay and the affective feature of feedback generated by ChatGPT, while a negative relationship was observed between the quality of the essay and the affective feature of feedback generated by peers."
