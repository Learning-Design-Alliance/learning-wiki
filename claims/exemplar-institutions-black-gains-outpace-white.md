## Evidence

### Nichols 2016

Nichols, A. H., Eberle-Sudré, K., & Welch, M. (2016). Rising Tide II: Do Black Students Benefit as Grad Rates Increase? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566672

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive IPEDS trend analysis (Figure 5) of Ohio State graduation rates 2003–2013; the report states black rates rose "up 31.1 percentage points (compared with 20.5 points for white students)." A feature section reports a 25.6-point gain on three-year averages.

> "However, rates for black students have increased faster than those of white students, up 31.1 percentage points (compared with 20.5 points for white students). As a result, the graduation rate gap has decreased by nearly half — from 22.5 points to 1 1.9 points (Figure 5)."
