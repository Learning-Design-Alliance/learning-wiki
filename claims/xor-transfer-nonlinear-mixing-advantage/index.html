Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik. (2024). Visual perceptual learning of feature conjunctions leverages non-linear mixed selectivity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00226-w

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

GLME analysis of XOR-task accuracy (β = 0.2332 for NM) in six subjects per group re-tested roughly nine months after Experiments 1 and 2; "NM × Experiment" reached p = 0.0003, favoring the global-feedback group, while the Experiment main effect was not significant (p = 0.2033).

"the degree of non-linear mixing (NM, as assessed using a GLME model akin to the one used to compare Experiments 1 and 3, see Methods) was signi ﬁcantly larger for the group trained with global feedback than the group trained with feature-speci ﬁc feedback (Experiment 1 as a reference; NM: β = 0.2332,p <1 0−23,E x p e r i - ment: β = −0.24,p = 0.2033; NM × Experiment: β = -0.1035, p = 0.0003)"

Rank-sum test of XOR-task transfer indices in the re-invited subjects showed "both groups performed almost equally well on the XOR task in terms of accuracy" (p = 0.132, d = −0.8753); no group difference in accuracy was detected.
