Evidence¶

Polso 2025¶

Polso, K.-M., Tuominen, H., Ihantola, P., & Niemivirta, M. (2025). Within-year stability and changes in adolescents' cross-domain motivational profiles: Predictions on academic achievement and educational choices. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 960-979. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000955

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Latent profile analysis of ten expectancy-value-cost constructs in a nationally representative Finnish sample (N = 1,590 ninth-graders) at two time points. The study reports "five motivational profiles in both semesters," with the Adjusted profile at "33% at T1/33% at T2" and differentiated Math- and Language-motivated profiles also emerging.

"We identified five motivational profiles in both semesters: Adjusted (overall moderate motivation; 33% at T1/33% at T2), Highly motivated (overall high motivation; 23%/26%), and Unattached (overall low motivation; 20%/14%) were characterized by similar motivation for the two subjects"
