## Evidence

### Quan Zhou 2026

Quan Zhou, Liao Wangjingyi, Allegrini Andrea G., Rimfeld Kaili, Wertz Jasmin, Morris Tim T., Raffington Laurel, Plomin Robert, Malanchini Margherita. (2026). Non-cognitive skills mediate education-related polygenic score associations with academic achievement across development. Nature Communications. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72838-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional mediation models in TEDS tested emotional and behavioural functioning (measured via the SDQ) as a mediator of polygenic score associations with academic achievement. Effects were "small in magnitude, ranging from β = 0.01" to β = 0.03, and "developmentally stable" across ages 7 to 16.

> "Indirect (mediated) effects for emotional and behavioural functioning were developmentally stable, similar across different raters, and small in magnitude, ranging from β = 0.01, p = 0.132 to β = 0.03, p < 0.001."
