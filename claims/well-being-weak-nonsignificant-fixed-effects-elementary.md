## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both satisfaction with school and affect toward school show a significant but weak unadjusted relationship (0.13–0.15 SD) with outcomes, which becomes non-significant in models with school fixed effects. [→ Akhmedjanova Diana 2026](#akhmedjanova-diana-2026)

## Evidence

### Akhmedjanova Diana 2026

Akhmedjanova Diana, Kanonire Tatjana, Zakharov Andrey. (2026). Moderation analysis of subjective well-being, self-efficacy, and academic performance of 4th grade children in Russia. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0341318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

OLS analysis of the whole sample (n = 1962). Unadjusted models showed a "statistically significant (p < 0.01) but weak relationship (0.13–0.15 SD)"; controlling for self-efficacy reduced the well-being coefficients toward zero.

> "Finally, in all the models with school fixed effect (Tables 2 and 3, Column 8), the relationship between both types of well-being and both types of students’ outcomes is not statistically significant."
