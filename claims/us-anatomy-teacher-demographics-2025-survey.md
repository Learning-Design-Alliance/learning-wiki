### Hall TR 2025

Hall TR, Mosley CF, Vakil JB, Lambert CK, Savelli DM, Balta JY. (2025). Who is teaching anatomy? An examination of the demographic characteristics, academic backgrounds, and professional responsibilities of US anatomy teachers. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional electronic survey of US anatomy teachers identified via AAA, HAPS, and AACA membership registries; 654 of 1764 invited responded (38%). The survey found "45% of respondents identified as female and 67% identified as white" and 68% held academic doctorates.

> "Results from 654 responses indicated that 45% of respondents identified as female and 67% identified as white. Most (68%) respondents had an academic doctoral degree."
