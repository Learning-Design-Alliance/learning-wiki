## Subclaims

## Evidence

### He 2022

He, W. (2022). MAP Growth item parameter drift study. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-item-parameter-drift-study/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive examination of histograms (Figure 3.5) plotting original item difficulty distributions for stable and unstable items across the four subjects. Histograms of difficulty differences (Figure 3.4) also showed items within 0.3 logit falling into the stable category, consistent with the 0.3 Logit Difference rule.

> "These plots indicate that there was no clear pattern as to which items tended to be flagged, as flagged items in each subject spread out over their entire underlying scale in a similar manner as the unflagged items."
