Authors' concluding interpretation of the latent cross-lagged results: "when adjusting for unreliability (via latent modeling)" the cross-lagged effects run in both directions between buoyancy and adaptability. This is the authors' stated conclusion (type e) drawing on the β = .07 paths reported in the supplementary SEM analysis.

> "We thus conclude that when adjusting for unreliability (via latent modeling), the cross-lagged effects are such that higher buoyancy at an earlier time point is associated with adaptability gains later, and higher initial adaptability is associated with buoyancy gains over time."
