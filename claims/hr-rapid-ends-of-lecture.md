## Evidence

### Darnell 2019

Darnell, D. K., & Krieg, P. A. (2019). Student engagement, assessed using heart rate, shows no reset following active learning sessions in lectures. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225709. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225709

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression analysis of the first and last three minutes of all 42 classes compared with the intervening period (Fig 2). Slopes printed as Start -37.5, End +29.0, Middle -3.75 bpm/h; no standardised effect size reported.

> "Indeed, the rate of HR change is approximately 10-fold greater at the two ends of the lecture period compared to the middle."
