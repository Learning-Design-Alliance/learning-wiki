## Evidence

### Corcoran 2003

Corcoran, Tom. (2003). The Merck Institute for Science Education: A Successful Intermediary for Education Reform. CPRE Research Report Series RR-052. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED480404 [doi:10.1037/e383112004-001](https://doi.org/10.1037/e383112004-001)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Following the non-participant study, the Partnership added general-topic offerings, moved workshops to immediately after the school year, recruited via informal networks, and offered graduate credit in one district; participation rose accordingly.

> "These efforts led to the recruitment during the following summer of 34% of those who had previously not participated, and eventually two-thirds of those who had not participated in the first three years (CPRE, 2000)."
