## Evidence

### Smart JR 2015

Smart JR, Kranz K, Carmona F, Lindner TW, Newton A. (2015). Does real-time objective feedback and competition improve performance and quality in manikin CPR training – a prospective observational study from several European EMS. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0160-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-stage observational study (n = 200 infant assessments per stage); Table 4 shows overall score rose from 55.1 to 86.5 with all metrics significant (p = 0.00). "The overall mean depth of compressions increased by 10.8 mm in Stage 2". The article states the improvement was greater for the infant assessment.

> "The overall mean depth of compressions increased by 10.8 mm in Stage 2 achieving an overall mean depth of 42.7 mm (the ERC (2010) Paediatric/Infant Guidelines state a depth of 1/3 AP which on this manikin is 38 mm)."
