The review's participant analysis over all 165 included articles reports the printed counts and percentages for each staff category, noting that more than half of research across all regions focused on paramedics.

> "published research on simulation in the context of prehospital care (n = 165) was, in the review, conducted with the following professions as participants: paramedics (n = 111, 46%), EMTs (n = 59, 24%), MDs (n = 34, 14%), nurses (n = 31, 13%) and fire-fighters (n = 7, 3%)."
