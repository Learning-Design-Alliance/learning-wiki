`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary composite outcome (arterial puncture, failure, non-functioning perfusion) compared by Fisher's exact test in the same randomized simulation trial; only the composite reached significance, with no printed effect size.

> "The composite outcome of complications was statistically lower in the mental imagery versus control group, occurring in 0 (0%) versus 4 (12.9%), respectively (p < 0.05)."

### Boulet N 2026 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome compared with the Wilcoxon rank-sum test in the same trial; medians reported as 1 [1–1] versus 1 [1–3] with p = 0.03 and no printed effect size.

> "The median number of punctures was significantly lower in the mental imagery compared to the control group (1 [1–1] versus 1 [1–3], respectively; p = 0.03)"

### Boulet N 2026 (3)

Boulet N, Nairv H, Mezzarobba M, Saadi L, Morin D, Roger C. (2026). Mental Imagery to Enhance Procedural Skills in Peripheral Venous Catheterization: A Randomized Simulation Study in Medical Students. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2069

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Component-level analyses of the composite complication outcome in the same trial; individual components did not reach significance and no effect sizes are printed, so the composite finding rests on the combined outcome.

> "Non-functioning perfusion occurred in 0 (0%) and 3 (9.7%) cases in the mental imagery versus control group, respectively (p = 0.11). Failure occurred in 0 (0%) and 1 (3.2%) case in the mental imagery versus control group, respectively (p = 0.48). No arterial puncture occurred in either group."
