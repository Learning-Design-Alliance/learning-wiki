Evidence¶

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad 2010¶

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad. (2010). Effectiveness of Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest comparison for the high-achiever subgroup (n = 6 per group, matched on pretest where p = .831). The t test difference was significant at p = .003, rejecting the high-achiever null hypothesis.

"The means, standard deviations and standard errors of the means of the high achievers of the experimental group was found 80.83 and of the control group was 67.17."

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad 2010 (2)¶

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad. (2010). Effectiveness of Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest comparison for average achievers (n = 8 per group; pretest difference p = .951). t = 3.38, df = 14, p = .004; the average-achiever null hypothesis was rejected.

"The mean of the experimental group was found 68.00 and of the control group was 56.00. The standard errors of the means were 2.77 and 2.19 respectively."

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad 2010 (3)¶

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad. (2010). Effectiveness of Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest comparison for low achievers (n = 10 per group; pretest difference p = .966). t = 4.06, df = 18, p = .001, described as highly significant; the low-achiever null hypothesis was rejected.

"The mean of the experimental group was found 57.50 and of the control group was 47.70. The standard errors of the means were 1.63 and 1.77 respectively."
