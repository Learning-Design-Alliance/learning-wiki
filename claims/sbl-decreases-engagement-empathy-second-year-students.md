### Ochandorena-Acha Mirari 2026

Ochandorena-Acha Mirari, Jolis-Prat Ramon, Escribà-Salvans Anna, Oliveira Vinicius Rosa, Rierola-Fochs Sandra, Parés-Martínez Carles, Terradas-Monllor Marc. (2026). The changes in perceived self-efficacy, academic engagement, and empathy following simulation-based learning among undergraduate physiotherapy students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09389-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stratified analysis of second-year students (n = 161 pre, n = 158 post) using Mann–Whitney U tests. The article reports significant declines in dedication, absorption, total engagement, and perspective taking, all with small effect sizes.

> "In contrast to the expected improvements, the changes in UWES-17 dedication, absorption, total score and JSE perspective taking reflected a decline in scores, suggesting a negative difference of the simulation experience on second-year students' self-efficacy, engagement, and empathy."
