### Hosseinzadeh Adli Ahmad 2018 (2)

Hosseinzadeh Adli Ahmad, Karami Chiman, Baghban Rahimi Sanaz, Mirarab Azam, Tabarraei Alijan. (2018). What family doctors know about congenital CMV: a regional survey in Iran. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-018-0470-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item on neonatal signs and symptoms of cCMV; between-group differences were statistically significant (p value = 0.007), with related maternal-child specialists outperforming the other two groups.

> "growth restriction, microcephaly, and seizures were known as a neonatal symptom of cCMV by 94% of the related medical specialists, while only 53% of the unrelated medical specialists and 59% of the general practitioners knew the correct answer"
