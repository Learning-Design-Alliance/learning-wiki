## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2016

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2016). Predicting Performance in Higher Education Using Proximal Predictors. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analyses predicting mean grades for statistics and theoretical courses from the three selection test scores, with squared semi-partial correlations inspected. The authors note this "only partly confirmed our expectations" regarding the math test.

> "The trial-studying scores and the math scores predicted the mean statistics grade equally well with moderate effect sizes (r = .34, for both tests), and showed equal unique contributions to the model (sr2 = .07 for both tests)."

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2016 (2)

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2016). Predicting Performance in Higher Education Using Proximal Predictors. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multiple regression analyses for theoretical courses (R2 = .27, F = 78.31 per Table 3). The unique contribution was largest for trial-studying scores (sr2 = .16), and the expected unique English contribution did not emerge.

> "The trial-studying score showed a large positive relationship with the mean theoretical grade (r = .51) and the math score and the English score showed small to moderate positive relationships (r = .25 and r = .27)."
