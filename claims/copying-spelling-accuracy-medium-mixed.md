## Evidence

### Broc Lucie 2025

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Replication copying study (N = 167): ANOVA on words correctly spelled divided by words copied gave F(1, 166) = 51,7, p < .001, η² = 0.13, favoring pen-and-paper over keyboard-and-computer.

> "The spelling accuracy was better in the pen-and-paper condition (M = 0.95, SD = 0.05) than in the keyboard-and-computer condition (M = 0.89, SD = 0.09)."

### Broc Lucie 2025 (2)

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot copying study (N = 58, Grades 4–5) found no significant medium effect on spelling accuracy, F(1, 57) = 0.15, p = .70; spelling was correct in 98% of cases regardless of medium.

> "The results showed no significant main effect of the medium used on this measure [F(1, 57) = 0.15, p = .70, η² = 0.001]. Furthermore, the spelling of the copied words was correct in 98% of the cases, regardless of the medium used."
