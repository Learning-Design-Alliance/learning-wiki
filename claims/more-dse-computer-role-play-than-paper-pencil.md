## Evidence

### Fallon 2018

Fallon, T. L., Aylett, R., Minnis, H. and Rajendran, G. (2018). Investigating social vulnerability in children using computer mediated role-play. Computers and Education, 125, pp. 458-464. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.06.027

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures 3 × 2 factorial ANOVA with 54 typically developing children (one removed, so analyzed n implied by df) found a significant main effect of presentation with printed effect size Cohen's f = 0.86, favoring higher DSE scores in the computer role-play task.

> "There was a significant main effect of task presentation (computer mediated role-play, paper pencil) [F (1, 47) = 34.66 p < 0.001 Cohen's f = 0.86]"
