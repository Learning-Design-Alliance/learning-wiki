Murphy et al. 2017¶

Richard Murphy, Felix Weinhardt, Gill Wyness and Heather Rolfe. (2017). Lesson Study: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581145

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Pre-specified subgroup analyses in the cluster RCT, using interaction terms of treatment assignment with pupil characteristics from National Pupil Database data. The report found no impact "on pupils with ethnic minority status, FSM eligibility, low KS1 attainment , or English as an additional language".
