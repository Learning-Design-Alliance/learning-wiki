Mazón Tapia María Gabriela and Borges Marcos Carvalho. (2026). Evaluating Medical Biochemistry Teaching and Learning Through Simulation: A Mixed-Methods, Quasi-Experimental Study in Medical Students in Ecuador. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-11-0258

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental between-group comparison of 404 first-semester medical students using a five-point Likert-scale post-session questionnaire found "86.34% of students in the intervention group reported satisfaction, compared with 29.65% in the control group". No standardized effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "A similar pattern was observed for overall satisfaction with the sessions, where 86.34% of students in the intervention group reported satisfaction, compared with 29.65% in the control group."
