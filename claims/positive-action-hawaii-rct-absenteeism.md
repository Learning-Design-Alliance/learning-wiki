Four-year longitudinal randomized evaluation comparing 10 Hawaii program schools to 10 similar control schools. The study reports an effect size of ES=+0.19 for absenteeism, described as "significantly lower absenteeism (ES=+0.19)", plus positive math and reading outcomes versus controls.

> "Snyder et al. (2010), a four-year longitudinal randomized evaluation, compared 10 schools in Hawaii using the program to 10 similar schools that did not receive the program. Positive Action schools had significantly lower absenteeism (ES=+0.19), as well as positive outcomes compared to controls in math and reading."
