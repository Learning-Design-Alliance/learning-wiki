## Evidence

### Eimer Christine 2020

Eimer Christine, Duschek Max, Jung Andreas Emanuel, Zick Günther, Caliebe Amke, Lindner Matthias, Weiler Norbert, Elke Gunnar. (2020). Video-based, student tutor- versus faculty staff-led ultrasound course for medical students – a prospective randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02431-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized single-blinded study of 96 fourth-year medical students randomized to ViST or faculty-led courses; 77 analyzable OSCE sheets. The Results report "no significant difference in the mean total point score" between groups. No effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "There was no significant difference in the mean total point score between the video-based, student tutor and the faculty staff group (video: mean = 31.3, faculty: mean = 32.7, P = 0.31)."
