Xinyue Wang 2024¶

Xinyue Wang, Kelong Lu, Yingyao He, Xinuo Qiao, Zhenni Gao, Yu Zhang & Ning Hao. (2024). Dynamic brain networks in spontaneous gestural communication. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00274-2

Xinyue Wang 2024 (2)¶

Xinyue Wang, Kelong Lu, Yingyao He, Xinuo Qiao, Zhenni Gao, Yu Zhang & Ning Hao. (2024). Dynamic brain networks in spontaneous gestural communication. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00274-2

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Correlational analysis of dFC state occurrence with behaviour in the 27-dyad study; also correlated with the number of fluid gestures (r = 0.40, P = 0.037) under ONE-user, supporting Hypothesis (III) about fluid gestures and individual cognitive fluency.

"the occurrence rate of State 1 was positively correlated with RPP ﬂuency under the ‘BOTH’(r = 0.38, P = 0.048) and ‘ONE-user’condition (r = 0.38, P = 0.049)"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • State 1 of dynamic inter-brain connectivity is a superior communication state: higher global efficiency, clustering and ROI strength, lower path length, with ISFC increments in mirroring and mentalizing regions — related
  • Behaviour locking shows interactive gestures accompany the superior inter-brain state (State 1), while the absence of gestures accompanies the inferior state under ONE — related
  • When only one participant could gesture, that participant used fewer interactive and more fluid gestures than the per-person rate under BOTH — related
  • Number of interactive gestures correlates positively with team originality and fluency, and number of fluid gestures with individual fluency — related
  • Averaging brain connectivity over the whole task period failed to detect intra-brain differences between gesture conditions, motivating dynamic analysis — related
  • Dyads in which both participants could gesture produced higher team originality and more combined ideas than dyads in which one or neither could gesture — related
