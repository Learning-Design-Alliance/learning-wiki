Evidence¶

Florence Lespiau 2019¶

Florence Lespiau, André Tricot. (2019). Using Primary Knowledge: an Efficient Way To Motivate Students and Promote the Learning of Formal Reasoning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09482-4

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the third experiment, university students (n=227) completed a priming phase with primary or secondary knowledge problems, then a training phase of secondary knowledge only, then a final test. The article reports that "presenting primary knowledge first and then secondary knowledge was more efficient both in terms of performance and motivation"; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.
