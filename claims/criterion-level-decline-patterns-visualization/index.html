q3 · i? · associational · r1

Criterion-level LMMs for the technical-execution rubric with Bonferroni and FDR corrections (α_adj = 0.01); all criteria remained significant under both adjustments.

"The steepest declines were observed for Axis labeling and units ( β = − 0.49, SE = 0.07) and Annotations or key labels (β = − 0.47, SE = 0.07), tasks requiring fine procedural fluency and attention to software conventions."

Francisco José Jiménez-Hornero, Eduardo Gutiérrez de Ravé. (2026). Conceptual and procedural knowledge retention in data visualization: a longitudinal study of doctoral learning and instructional design implications. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09808-0

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Criterion-level LMMs for the conceptual-understanding rubric; Completeness (β = −0.22) was the most stable conceptual aspect, and random-intercept variances ranged from 0.17 to 0.22.

"The steepest declines were observed in Explanation of scales and units (β = − 0.30, SE = 0.06) and Use of appropriate terminology (β = − 0.28, SE = 0.06), reflecting the gradual erosion of precision in quantitative reasoning and disciplinary language."
