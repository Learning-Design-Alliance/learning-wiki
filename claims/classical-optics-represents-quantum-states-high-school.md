## Evidence

### Joel A. Walsh 2021

Joel A. Walsh, Mic Fenech, Derrick L. Tucker, Catherine Riegle-Crumb, and Brian R. La Cour. (2021). Piloting a Full-year, Optics-based High School Course on Quantum Computing. Physics Education. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac3dc2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' summary of lessons from a two-year pilot of a full-year high school quantum computing course at a Texas public high school, mixing real and virtual optical experiments. The paper reports that classical optics "provides a clear and accessible avenue for representing quantum states and gate operators" and supports transfer to STEM skills; no quantitative effect sizes are printed.

> "we find that the use of classical optics provides a clear and accessible avenue for representing quantum states and gate operators and facilitates both learning and the transfer of knowledge to other Science, Technology, and Engineering (STEM) skills."
