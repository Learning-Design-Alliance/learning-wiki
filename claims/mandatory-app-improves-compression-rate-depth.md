# Mandatory app use significantly increased the percentage of chest compressions with correct rate and correct depth versus no app

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` With mandatory app use, the percentage of compressions at the correct rate (100–120/min) was significantly higher than in the control group (β = 18.61; p = .004). [→ Metelmann Camilla 2021](#metelmann-camilla-2021)
`q3 i?` With mandatory app use, the percentage of compressions at the correct depth (5–6 cm) was significantly higher than in the control group (β = 22.91; p < .001). [→ Metelmann Camilla 2021 (2)](#metelmann-camilla-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Metelmann Camilla 2021

Metelmann Camilla, Metelmann Bibiana, Schuffert Louisa, Hahnenkamp Klaus, Vollmer Marcus, Brinkrolf Peter. (2021). Smartphone apps to support laypersons in bystander CPR are of ambivalent benefit: a controlled trial using medical simulation. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00893-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 3 of the controlled simulation trial reports adjusted group effects on compression quality: control vs. mandatory group β = 18.61 [6.10; 31.11]; p = .004 for correct compression rate, with mean 65.4% (±32.7) in the mandatory group versus 43.8% (±36.8) in controls. The β is an unstandardised adjusted effect, so no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Correct compression rate [100–120/min− 1] (%)				β=-4.59 [-16.91; 7.73]; p = .47	β = 18.61 [6.10; 31.11]; p = .004"

### Metelmann Camilla 2021 (2)

Metelmann Camilla, Metelmann Bibiana, Schuffert Louisa, Hahnenkamp Klaus, Vollmer Marcus, Brinkrolf Peter. (2021). Smartphone apps to support laypersons in bystander CPR are of ambivalent benefit: a controlled trial using medical simulation. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00893-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 3 of the same trial reports adjusted effects on correct compression depth: control vs. mandatory group β = 22.91 [10.97; 34.86]; p < .001, with mean 47.6% (±37.3) in the mandatory group versus 24.4% (±30.4) in controls. The facultative-group effect on depth was not significant (p = .06).
