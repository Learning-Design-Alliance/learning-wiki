Evidence¶

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn P. Rosé 2022¶

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn P. Rosé. (2022). Novel CSCL design processes prompted by exploration of role-playing and emotions. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn 17:457–461. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09383-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

Behavioral trace data and social network analysis over eight role-playing game iterations, 26 fully online groups (n = 128) and 12 blended groups (n = 57), compared test and control groups, found MEP scripts "have promise for promoting desirable learning behaviors".

"Their findings indicate that simple and specifically targeted scripts, in the form of MEPs, have promise for promoting desirable learning behaviors in a fully online multiplayer classroom."
