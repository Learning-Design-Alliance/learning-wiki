Supplemental Material S5 (Study 2) explains that the number of manipulation trials was not statistically controlled because its meaning is unclear and, per the authors, "our data show that there was no substantial variation in the number of manipulation trials across participants," with the printed means and standard deviations for both conditions.

> "In the overchallenge condition, the mean number of manipulation trials participants worked on was M = 74.17, with a relatively small standard deviation of SD = 6.29. Similarly, in the underchallenge condition, the mean was M = 174.51"
