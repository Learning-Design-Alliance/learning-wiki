## Evidence

### Noll C 2026

Noll C, Mikuteit M, Golon S, Louzek C, Afshar K, Just I, Steffens S. (2026). Feasibility and implementation of a final year Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) in a medical model curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10350-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive feasibility study of the first mandatory PJ-OSCE administration at Hannover Medical School. The examination was delivered across 15 circuits with a mean score of "603.08 (SD 67.57)/800 points (75.2%)" and a "pass rate was 97.1%".

> "In June 2024, 278 students completed the mandatory PJ-OSCE across 15 circuits of eight stations (8 min each). Mean score was 603.08 (SD 67.57)/800 points (75.2%); pass rate was 97.1%."
