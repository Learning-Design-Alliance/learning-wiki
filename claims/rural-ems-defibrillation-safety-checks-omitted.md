## Evidence

### Nielsen Anne Møller 2012

Nielsen Anne Møller, Isbye Dan Lou, Lippert Freddy Knudsen, Rasmussen Lars Simon. (2012). Basic life support and automated external defibrillator skills among ambulance personnel: a manikin study performed in a rural low-volume ambulance setting. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-34

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational manikin assessment of AED safety checks (N = 20, Figure 4); the discussion states "only 30% performed both a visual and a verbal hands-off safety check before pushing the shock button". Descriptive proportions only.

> "A fundamental rule in all first aid is ‘safety first’ and therefore it is surprising that more than half of the EMS providers did not perform any hands-off checks during rhythm analysis and only 30% performed both a visual and a verbal hands-off safety check before pushing the shock button."
