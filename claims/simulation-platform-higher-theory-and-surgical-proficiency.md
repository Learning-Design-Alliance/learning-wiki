## Evidence

### Yan Haichao 2025

Yan Haichao, Zhu Ruiying, Yue Ning, Han Zhenyi, Yu Xiaobo, Tanu Cheerly, Wang Ping, Yu Xing. (2025). Utilization of animal experiment-based simulation surgery platforms in the education of endoscopic thyroidectomy: a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07935-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post-training specialty theory scores in the randomized two-group trial (n = 30 per group); the experimental group "outperformed the control group" with P < 0.01 and Cohen's d = 0.85 in Table 1.

> "In the specialized theoretical assessment of thyroid surgery, the experimental group outperformed the control group (83.6 ± 6.2 vs. 78.4 ± 6.1, P < 0.01)"

### Yan Haichao 2025 (2)

Yan Haichao, Zhu Ruiying, Yue Ning, Han Zhenyi, Yu Xiaobo, Tanu Cheerly, Wang Ping, Yu Xing. (2025). Utilization of animal experiment-based simulation surgery platforms in the education of endoscopic thyroidectomy: a preliminary study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07935-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Surgical proficiency was rated by senior surgeons at the end of the one-month rotation; the experimental group was rated "significantly higher" with P < 0.01 and d = 0.82. The authors note the proficiency assessment was non-blinded.

> "Senior surgeons also rated surgical proficiency significantly higher in the experimental group (83.6 ± 5.9) than that in the control group (79.2 ± 4.7) (P < 0.01)"
