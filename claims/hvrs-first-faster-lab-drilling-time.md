Procedural timing analysis in the randomised pilot, with simulator-recorded drilling time in the HVRS session and observer-recorded time in the laboratory session; HVRS-first students showed "greater efficiency in the subsequent laboratory session" at 198.4 s (SD = 65.7) versus 264.7 s (SD = 72.3) for LAB-first students' initial session.

> "Students who began their training with the haptic virtual reality simulator demonstrated greater efficiency in the subsequent laboratory session, completing the task in a reduced average time of 198.4 s (SD = 65.7)."
