## Evidence

### Blanchard 2015

Blanchard, E. G. (2015). Socio-Cultural Imbalances in AIED Research: Investigations, Implications and Opportunities. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0027-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The review reports, citing Mesquita et al.'s literature review, that "convincingly demonstrated that there are cultural differences in the ecology of emotions"; it also lists cultural variations in affective antecedents, subjective experiences, behavioral response, physiological changes and appraisal.

> "As a conclusion to their survey, Mesquita et al. claim that it is “convincingly demonstrated that there are cultural differences in the ecology of emotions” (p. 265)."

### Blanchard 2015 (2)

Blanchard, E. G. (2015). Socio-Cultural Imbalances in AIED Research: Investigations, Implications and Opportunities. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0027-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article's synthesis of cited sources (Henrich et al. 2010a; TALIS 2009; Purdie and Hattie 1996, among others) states that "research has identified cultural variations in educational practices and teachers' beliefs" and in decision making, reasoning, motivation, self-regulation, collaboration, competition and personal values.

> "research has identified cultural variations in educational practices and teachers’ beliefs (TALIS 2009), as well as in many other psychological topics of interest for the AIED research community, such as decision making, reasoning, moti- vation, self-regulation, collaboration, competition, personal values"
