## Evidence

### Megan Ivory 2023

Megan Ivory, Alisa Bettale, Rachel Boren, Ashlyn D. Burch, Jake Douglass, Lisa Hackett, Boris Kiefer, Alina Kononov, Maryanne Long, Mekena Metcalf, Tzula B. Propp, Mohan Sarovar. (2023). Quantum Computing, Math, and Physics (QCaMP): Introducing quantum computing in high schools. [doi:10.1109/qce57702.2023.20318](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce57702.2023.20318)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-camp online survey administered via REDCap to teacher camp participants; fifteen middle and high school teachers completed evaluation surveys. The authors summarize that "teachers reported strong positive impacts of the camp," and Table VIII lists agreement-scale averages for each impact question.

> "Overall, teachers reported strong positive impacts of the camp. In particular, they felt more conﬁdent delivering instruction in areas such as quantum and physics, that their students would beneﬁt from teachers attending QCaMP , and that they learned new ways to engage students in QCaMP material."
