## Evidence

### Gregorio Orlando 2026

Gregorio Orlando, Milan Groshev, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer. (2026). AIfred: Augmented Learning through Functional Robotic Embodiment at the Desk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38737

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the assisted math assignment, graded by four anonymized Gemini grading agents on an eight-criterion rubric (mean inter-agent SD 0.53), scores "do not differ significantly between AIfred and ChatGPT" (6.7/10 vs. 7.3/10; p=.41). No effect size is printed.
