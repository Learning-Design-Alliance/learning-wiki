## Evidence

### Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay 2025

Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay. (2025). Addressing sarcopenia management in primary care in Türkiye: gaps in the field and training needs of family physicians. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1660782

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square/t-test analysis section of the survey: the trained group (n = 65) had mean 4.29 (SD 2.01) versus 3.19 (SD 1.92) for the untrained group (n = 340), t = 4.202, p = 0.000. The article reports "significantly higher proficiency scores" with no standardized effect size.

> "Family physicians who had received palliative care training reported significantly higher proficiency scores in sarcopenia management compared to those without palliative care training (p < 0.001)."
