Widodo 2023¶

Widodo, S. A., Wijayanti, A., Irfan, M., Pusporini, W., Mariah, M., & Rochmiyati, S. (2023). Effects of worksheets on problem-solving skills: Meta-analytic studies. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 9(1), 151-167. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.9.1.151

Meta-analysis of 40 studies (45 comparisons) from research and development, experimental, and quasi-experimental designs, 2013-2022, found via Google Scholar and Garuda. The article reports "the average effect size is 1.281", classified at the very high level per its criteria table; no standardized effect-size statistic beyond this pooled value is printed.

"From Tables 4 and 5, it is found that the average effect size is 1.281. This effect size is at a very high level."

Related Claims¶

  • The meta-analysis corpus comprised 40 studies yielding 45 data comparisons from 2013-2022 — a broader claim this one bears on
  • The mean population correlation between worksheet use and problem-solving skills is r = 0.3705, falling within its confidence interval — related
  • The positive worksheet effect was obtained mainly in secondary-school studies with large samples (more than 30 students) and random sampling — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Sampling error accounts for only 0.015% of the variance, suggesting small bias from sampling error — related
  • Basketball players solved average and percentage problems better when framed by basketball practices than as school math worksheets, using different strategies across contexts — related
