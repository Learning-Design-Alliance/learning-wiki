q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Theme from participant narratives in the group formation subcategory; one participant recalled that “girls were the last classmates selected, and when they did, it was by default”, producing sadness and marginalization.

"Generally, “girls were the last classmates selected, and when they did, it was by default. This made me feel quite rejected, as well as quite sad” (P19)."

Gonzalo Flores-Aguilar 2026 (2)¶

Gonzalo Flores-Aguilar, X`enia Ríos-Sisó, Antonio Muñoz-Llerena. (2026). “I didn't want physical education days to come”: Emotional experiences in physical education of prospective primary education teachers. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102382. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102382

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Participant narrative on anthropometry (evaluation) in CSE; public recording of weight generated sadness, shame and self-categorization. Soccer-dominated free play similarly left girls displaced and excluded, per the article's theme analysis.

"I remember crying … because they forced us to put our weights on a public sheet. I wasn't going through a good time because I didn't like saying my weight. I always felt sad deep down for being categorized"
