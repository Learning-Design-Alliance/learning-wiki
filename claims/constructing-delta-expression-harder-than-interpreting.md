## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Upper-division student difficulties with the Dirac delta function. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.010108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Second interview set directly prompted students to use delta functions to express a line charge density passing through (1,2,0). Of "the eight interview participants who attempted to use δ-functions", three succeeded; four used a single delta function with a vector-difference argument without acknowledging a 3D delta.

> "Of the eight interview participants who attempted to use δ-functions in response to the A2-type prompt, three were able to correctly express the line charge density as the product of two 1D Cartesian δ-functions."
