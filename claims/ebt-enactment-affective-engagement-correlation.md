Observational analysis across 63 introductory statistics classes using Lumen One courseware, with EBT enactment measured via student survey items and affective engagement via a nine-item scale. The article reports "strong (r = 0.61)" and "statistically significant (p < .001)."

> "The correlation between number of EBT practices used and affective engagement is strong (r = 0.61) and statistically significant (p < .001)."
