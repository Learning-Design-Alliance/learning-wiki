Imagine Español 2025¶

Imagine Español. (2025). Evidence Rating: Moderate. Imagine Learning. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/imagine-learning-imagine-espanol/

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

Prospective quasi-experimental study in a southwestern U.S. district during 2024–2025 with 655 students in Grades 2–6 assessed with FastBridge aReading; Imagine Español students showed "significantly greater gains in English reading" (effect size = +0.16).

"Results showed that Imagine Español students in Grades 2–6 made significantly greater gains in English reading than their peers (effect size = +0.16)."
