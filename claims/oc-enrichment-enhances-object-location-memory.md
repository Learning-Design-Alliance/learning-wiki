## Evidence

### Gattas 2022

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Object location memory experiment in the same four-group design (n = 40), run 5–6 months post-enrichment. Planned t-tests showed the OC group significantly differed from the CC group, t(18) = 4.340, p < 0.001, and EH differed from CC, t(18) = 3.136, p = 0.006, at 24 h.

> "a repeated measures ANOVA revealed a signi ﬁcant interaction between the length of delay and group, F(3, 36) = 4.294, p = 0.011, partial η2 = 0.264, and a signi ﬁcant main effect of delay, F(1, 36) = 30.763, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.461;"

### Gattas 2022 (2)

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

OLM experiment with a shortened 7-min sample and 24-h delay in the same four-group design. At this suboptimal sample and increased delay, only mice in the OC group, t(9) = 2.585, p = 0.029, discriminated the displaced object.

> "an ANOVA showed a signi ﬁcant effect of group, F(3, 36) = 3.623, p = 0.022, partial η2 = 0.232, and independent samples t-tests revealed that the OC group showed an enhancement on task performance compared to the EH group, t(18) = −3.041, p = 0.007"
