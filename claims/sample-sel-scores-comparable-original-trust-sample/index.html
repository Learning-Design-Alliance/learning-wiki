Descriptive comparison with Aldrup et al.'s (2020) in-service sample (n = 239): current sample scored 24.87 vs. 22.29 on emotion regulation and 25.62 vs. 23.90 on relationship management, both on higher-is-stronger scales.

"The teachers in our study had an average emotion regulation score of 24.87 out of a possible 33 points, and their average relationship management score was 25.62 out of a possible 38 points."
