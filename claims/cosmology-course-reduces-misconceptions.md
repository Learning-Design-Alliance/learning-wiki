A. Gasparini, A. Müller, F. Stern, L. Weiss. (2025). Cosmology and general relativity in upper secondary school through new targeted teaching materials: a study on student learning and motivation. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/ss9c-lkpj](https://doi.org/10.1103/ss9c-lkpj)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of the 14-item conceptual pre/post test (N = 68) tracked percentages of right answers and of a dominant wrong answer per item; e.g. dark matter as antimatter fell from 37% to 0%, and the article reports "all the potential misconceptions present in the pre-test are overtaken".
