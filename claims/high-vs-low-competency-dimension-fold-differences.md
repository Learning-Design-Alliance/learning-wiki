### Yu Yang 2026

Yu Yang, Luoye Fangxin, You Danhui, Xu Zhenhong, Liu Yuping, Wu Li, Deng Qian, Ning Yanting, Liu Liqiong. (2026). Heterogeneity and associated factors of palliative care core competency among oncology nurses: a latent profile analysis. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21645

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-profile comparison in the cross-sectional LPA of 241 nurses; intergroup differences were significant in total core competency and all dimensions (all P < 0.001). Profile means are printed: low ECC 16.2 ± 2.7, CPC 29.4 ± 7.7, PSA 9.2 ± 2.6; high ECC 29.3 ± 2.7, CPC 69.9 ± 6.4, PSA 18.4 ± 2.7.

> "High-competency nurses scored 1.8-fold higher in ethical care, 2.4-fold higher in clinical practice, and 2.0-fold higher in psychological self-adjustment compared with the low-competency group (all P < 0.001)."
