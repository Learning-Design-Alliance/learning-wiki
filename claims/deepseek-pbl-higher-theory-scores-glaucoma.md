## Evidence

### Gu Zheyao 2026

Gu Zheyao, Xi Ting, Zhang Chunyuan. (2026). DeepSeek-assisted problem-based learning for glaucoma education in an undergraduate ophthalmology clerkship: a randomized educational pilot study. Scientific Reports 16, 24184. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-58447-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pilot study of 74 fourth-year medical students (37 per group) at a single Chinese medical college; independent-sample t test of theory exam scores. The PBL-DeepSeek mean was "81.71 ± 4.41 points" versus "76.91 ± 8.31 points" (p = 0.003; Cohen's d = 0.72).

> "students in the PBL-DeepSeek group achieved an average score of 81.71 ± 4.41 points, which was significantly higher than the average score of students in the traditional group (76.91 ± 8.31 points) (p = 0.003; Cohen’s d = 0.72)"
