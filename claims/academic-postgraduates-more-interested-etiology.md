## Evidence

### Ding Hong 2025

Ding Hong, Li He-Xi, Ai You-Wei, Zhang Qing-Yi, Tang Hai-Tao, Zeng Wen. (2025). Assessment of understanding, attitudes, and clinical practices regarding osteoporosis among postgraduate students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07785-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-choice interest item in Table 3 of the 97-respondent survey; etiology was chosen by 40(95.2%) academic vs 34(61.8%) professional students. Overall interest was highest for prevention (90.7%) and clinical manifestations (88.7%).

> "Academic graduate students were significantly more interested in learning about etiology than professional graduate students (95.2% vs. 61.8%, p < 0.001)."
