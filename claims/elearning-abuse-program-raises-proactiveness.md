## Evidence

### Baba Kaori 2023

Baba Kaori, Kataoka Yaeko. (2023). An E-Learning Program for Continuing Midwifery Education on Handling High-Risk Abuse Cases: A Pretest–Posttest Design. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20136317

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same single-group pretest–posttest study (N = 71); the second efficacy question asked whether participants could respond proactively to suspected abuse, rated on a five-point scale before and after the program. The article reports the rise was "statistically significant (t = 8.1, p < 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "the number of individuals who expressed proactive confidence increased significantly to around 72% (51 individuals). This observed rise in proactive response scores was found to be statistically significant (t = 8.1, p < 0.001)"
