## Evidence

### ev-1

Taboada Barber, A., Urbano Aguilar, S., Lopez Rodriguez, A., and Pan, Y. (2026, September). Performing Is Not Teaching: What the Evidence Says About Reading Fluency Practices for Emergent Bilinguals. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/316

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of the Al-Bogami & Alahmadi (2025) and Begeny et al. (2012) intervention studies reports that intervention students "outperformed their peers in the control group in English reading fluency". No effect sizes are printed for this pooled statement.

> "the two studies that included repeated reading, modeled/assisted reading, and systematic and explicit feedback in their interventions reported that the students who received the intervention outperformed their peers in the control group in English reading fluency"
