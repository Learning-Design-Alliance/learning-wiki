## Evidence

### Leones 2021

Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Huynh, K., Gedney, M., & Dominguez, X. (2021). Splash and Bubbles for Parents App: Station Study Report [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/120

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey data on Activities section use among 19 families. By interview time, a slightly higher percentage (75.86%) mentioned using Activities, including four who had not used it at survey time.

> "Approximately two thirds of the families (65.52%) indicated using the Activities section on their survey. Of these 19 families, a majority (78.95%) reported the activities as the most helpful section followed by the Photo Journal (21.05%). Over two thirds (68.42%) indicated completing two or more activities"

### Leones 2021 (2)

Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Huynh, K., Gedney, M., & Dominguez, X. (2021). Splash and Bubbles for Parents App: Station Study Report [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/120

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey helpfulness ratings for the Activities section. These are descriptive averages with no inferential test reported.

> "Families were asked to rate how much they think the activities support parents to help children learn about science on a scale of 1 to 5 (5 means very helpful) on the survey. The average rating was 4.3 with no families rating lower than 3."
