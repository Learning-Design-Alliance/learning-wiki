## Evidence

### Couto Lucélio B. 2015

Couto Lucélio B., Bestetti Reinaldo B., Restini Carolina B. A., Faria-Jr Milton, Romão Gustavo S. (2015). Brazilian medical students’ perceptions of expert versus non-expert facilitators in a (non) problem-based learning environment. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.26893

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional anonymous survey of all 252 students in the fifth to eighth stages at UNAERP, who had experienced both facilitator types, rating facilitators on a 5-point Likert-type scale. "The overwhelming majority (95%) of students, or 239/252" favored the expert facilitator for building knowledge vs. 6% (p≤0.001).

> "The overwhelming majority (95%) of students, or 239/252, agreed or strongly agreed that the subject-matter expert facilitator was ‘very good’ at building knowledge, whereas only 16 students (6%) agreed similarly for non-subject-matter experts (p≤0.001)."
