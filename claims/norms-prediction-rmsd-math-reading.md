## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Math RMSDs between observed spring RIT and norms-predicted scores were consistently larger for the pilot group, while reading predictions were similar across groups. [→ J. Patrick Meyer 2023](#j-patrick-meyer-2023)

## Evidence

### J. Patrick Meyer 2023

J. Patrick Meyer, Ann Hu, Sylvia Li. (2023). Content Proximity Spring 2022 Pilot Study Research Brief. Psychometric Solutions, NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/content-proximity-spring-2022-pilot-study-research-report/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-student comparison of observed spring RIT to scores predicted by the MAP Growth norms model found math "RMSDs for the pilot group were consistently larger than the RMSDs for the comparison group"; correlations were high and similar.

> "Results in Table 2.5 showed that for math, the RMSDs for the pilot group were consistently larger than the RMSDs for the comparison group. This result was consistent with the difference in actual math scores observed for the students in the pilot study."
