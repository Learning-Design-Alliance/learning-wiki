Results from the 2019 Mathematics and Reading Assessments 2020¶

Results from the 2019 Mathematics and Reading Assessments. (2020). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Statistics. https://www.nationsreportcard.gov

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The NAEP 2019 results by race/ethnicity report average 2019 scores by student group and significance flags against 2017 and earlier years, printing that grade 8 reading was lower for all groups "except for Asian/Pacific Islander students". No effect sizes are reported.

"Average reading scores lower at grade 8 for all racial/ethnic groups compared to 2017 except for Asian/Pacific Islander students"
