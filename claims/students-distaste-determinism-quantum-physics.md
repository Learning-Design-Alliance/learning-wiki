### Baily 2010

Baily, C., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2010). Interpretation in Quantum Physics as Hidden Curriculum. AIP Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515251](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515251)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview study with nineteen students. Most students said they were unfamiliar with the word determinism in physics context, and nearly all believed quantum behavior is probabilistic or fundamentally limited by the Uncertainty Principle.
