Ana Katalinic, Vanja Slavuj, Danijela Jaksic. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Online Education: A Systematic Review of Its Impact on Learner Engagement and Satisfaction. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 389. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16030389

The review reports, citing Dehbozorgi & Dindu (2023) and Abid et al. (2024), that automated assistance can reduce motivation for deep engagement. The review hedges the outcome as possible poorer understanding and retention.

> "Research indicates that easy access to AI-generated answers and automated assistance can reduce learners' motivation to engage deeply with the material, possibly leading to poorer understanding and retention"
