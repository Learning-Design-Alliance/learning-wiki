# Student adjustment mediates the relationship between P-E fit dimensions and academic achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adjustment partially mediated the needs-supplies fit and personality-major fit relationships with achievement, and fully mediated the demand-abilities fit relationship. [→ Azizan Che’ Rozaniza 2021](#azizan-che-rozaniza-2021)

## Evidence

### Azizan Che’ Rozaniza 2021

Azizan Che’ Rozaniza, Roslan Samsilah, Abdullah Maria Chong, Asimiran Soaib, Zaremohzzabieh Zeinab, Ahrari Seyedali, Tchounwou Paul B. (2021). Does a Person-Environment-Fit Promote the Academic Achievement of Hearing-Impaired Students in Malaysian Polytechnics? The Mediating Effects of Satisfaction and Adjustment. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped parallel mediation analysis (5000 subsamples) in the PLS-SEM model of 195 hearing-impaired students. Indirect paths through adjustment were significant for needs-supplies fit and personality-major fit as printed.

> "student adjustment played a partially significant mediating role in the relationship among needs-supplies fit (=0.32, t = 3.337, p = 0.001), personality-major fit (=0.441, t = 5.48, p = 0.000), and academic achievement."

### Azizan Che’ Rozaniza 2021 (2)

Azizan Che’ Rozaniza, Roslan Samsilah, Abdullah Maria Chong, Asimiran Soaib, Zaremohzzabieh Zeinab, Ahrari Seyedali, Tchounwou Paul B. (2021). Does a Person-Environment-Fit Promote the Academic Achievement of Hearing-Impaired Students in Malaysian Polytechnics? The Mediating Effects of Satisfaction and Adjustment. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same bootstrapped mediation analysis. Although the direct demand-abilities path was non-significant, the indirect path through adjustment was significant, which the article describes as full mediation.

> "student adjustment (=0.212, t = 3.055, p = 0.02) fully mediated the relationship among demand-ability fit and academic accomplishment."
