Amy Voss Farris and Pratim Sengupta. (2015). Democratizing Children's Computation: Learning Computational Science as Aesthetic Experience. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.08619](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.08619)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Illustrative case study of Ariana, a fifth grader in a two-week summer course, whose self-chosen side-project writing 'Thomas' merged her biography with the ViMAP turtle; her interview transcript, analyzed inductively, evidences "continuity between her biographical experiences outside her classroom and the programming activity".

> "Ariana’s project shows that the computational agent (the turtle) truly became a transitional object––i.e., she projected her identity onto the turtle. Her way of learning programming was by making the turtle learn how to write Thomas."
