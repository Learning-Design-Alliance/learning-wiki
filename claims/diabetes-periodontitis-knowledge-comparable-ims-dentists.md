`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Periodontal-systemic domain comparison (Table 3). The article notes the small absolute difference (1%) lost significance after Bonferroni correction, and logistic regression confirmed "diabetes-related items showed no significant group difference after adjustment" (DM as risk factor OR = 0.70, p = 0.513; glycemic control OR = 1.00, p = 0.997).

> "Regarding the recognition of diabetes as a risk factor for periodontitis, both groups demonstrated high correct response rates (IMS: 93.5% vs. dentists: 92.5%), with IMS participants showing a marginally higher rate."
