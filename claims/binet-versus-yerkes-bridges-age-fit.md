Kelley, T. L. (1915). Report of the Twenty-fourth Annual Meeting of the American Psychological Association, Chicago, December 28-30, 1915. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meeting-report summary of Hayes's study comparing the Binet-Simon method with the Yerkes-Bridges point scale. The report attributes the age-graded disagreement "to the difference in methods of scoring and not to differences in test questions."

> "showed that in the earlier years the Binet results fitted more closely to the chronological ages of normal children than the Yerkes-Bridges results and that from eight or nine on, the Yerkes-Bridges method gave the better results, the disagreement being quite plainly due to to the difference in methods of scoring and not to differences in test questions."
