## Evidence

### Nandi 2023

Nandi, A., Haberland, N., Kozak, M., & Ngô, T. D. (2023). The gendered effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on adolescent literacy and schooling outcomes in India. npj Science of Learning, 8, 42. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00193-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental analysis of NFHS-5 data using propensity score matching (nearest neighbor and three nearest neighbors) and inverse propensity score weighted regression on 94,868 girls aged 15–17. The estimated reading ability reduction was "1.5%–1.6% lower as compared with the matched pre-COVID group."

> "Among girls, the ability to read a sentence in the post-COVID group ranged from 1.5%–1.6% lower as compared with the matched pre-COVID group."
