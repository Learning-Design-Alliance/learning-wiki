### Darie S. 2026

Darie S., Wainman B.C., Wohl G.R., Wang A.Y., Zhang B.Z., Li A., Ahmad E., Palombella A.S., Rockarts J.L., de SA D.L., Nguyen L.N., DeCarlo B., Downer N., Brewer‐Deluce D. (2026). Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70185

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA of mechanical bone-sawing load data from the quantitative study, in which three transverse cortical bone slices per donor were cut on an Instron 5967 at 0.1 mm/s. The article reports "no solution‐based significant differences" (p > 0.05); equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no solution‐based significant differences in average load required to cut through the bone slices in the mechanical bone sawing test (p > 0.05, Figure 5)."

In the discussion, the authors note that the residents' MES force rankings for bone sawing, like the mechanical test, showed no statistically significant differences across solutions.

> "Results from both of the MES force rankings and mechanical bone sawing tasks were similarly not statistically significant."
