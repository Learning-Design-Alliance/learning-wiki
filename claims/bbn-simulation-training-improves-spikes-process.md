### Servotte J-C 2019

Servotte J-C, Bragard I, Szyld D, Van Ngoc P, Scholtes B, Van Cauwenberge I, Donneau A-F, Dardenne N, Goosse M, Pilote B, Guillaume M, Ghuysen A. (2019). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the cluster-randomized trial, two blinded raters scored SPIKES compliance (14 items, range 0–14) on video-recorded simulations with standardized family members; a significant group-by-time effect (p<0.001) favored the training group.

> "There was a 33.3% improvement (p<0.001) between pre-test and post-test in the TG while there was no significant gain for the CG. The difference between these gains in the groups was statistically significant (p<0.001)."
