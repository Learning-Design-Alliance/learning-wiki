## Evidence

### de Moura Júnior LG 2018

de Moura Júnior LG, de Vasconcelos PRL, Fechine FV, de Moura MS, de Moura RLS, Rocha HAL, de Moraes Filho MO. (2018). Endosuture trainer box simulator as a tool for training and teaching in bariatric laparoscopic surgery. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-018-0412-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed rank comparisons of the 37 participants' ratings found the "two devices were similar" on stereotaxy (P = 0.1627), fulcrum effect (P = 0.1500) and visibility (P = 0.0650); equivalence was not tested, only non-significance reported.

> "The two devices were similar in relation to: 3. perception of depth of intra-corporeal suture tray (stereotaxy) (P = 0.1627); 5. fulcrum effect (reverse motion) relative to real vision (P = 0.1500); and 6. visibility of the operating field and image quality monitor (P = 0.0650)."
