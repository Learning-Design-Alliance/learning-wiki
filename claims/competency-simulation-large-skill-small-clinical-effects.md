### Vermylen JH 2026

Vermylen JH, Cohen ER, Cook DA, McGaghie WC, Issenberg SB, Arnold J, Ballard H, Bayoumi M, Beestrum M, Bremner R, Crawford S, Einstein N, Mannarino C, Misra A, Tomita TM, Waldron H, Yanko F, Kessler DO. `q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Pooled analysis by Kirkpatrick outcome level across the 12 competency-versus-noncompetency studies. The review reports simulation-lab skill effects as large, while "The effects for higher-level Kirkpatrick outcomes, however, were small" and non-significant (behavior SMD = 0.46, P = 0.26; patient effect SMD = 0.21, P = 0.43).

> "Skill outcomes measured in the simulation lab had large effect sizes: for (1) skill nontime pooled analysis SMD = 0.79 (95% CI = 0.22 to 1.36, P = 0.007) and (2) skill time pooled analysis SMD = 1.07 (95% CI = 0.81 to 1.33, P < 0.001)"
