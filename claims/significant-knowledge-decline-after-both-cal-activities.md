## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rajan Kiran 2022

Rajan Kiran, Pandit Anand S. (2022). Comparing computer-assisted learning activities for learning clinical neuroscience: a randomized control trial. BMC Medical Education 22:522. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03578-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired two-tailed t-tests on quiz scores of the randomized trial showed a significant mean decline for both activities; the eModule group fell "-16%, 95% CI -8% to -23%, p = 0.001" and the Wiki group -17%.

> "There was a significant mean decline in the second quiz scores for both the eModule group (-16%, 95% CI -8% to -23%, p = 0.001) and Wiki group (-17%, 95% CI -8% to -26%, p = 0.003)."
