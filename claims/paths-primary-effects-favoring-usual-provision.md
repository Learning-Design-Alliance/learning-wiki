`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

ITT analysis of teacher-rated SDQ Peer Problems in the cluster RCT identified a "statistically significant primary effect favoring the control group", d = 0.07, p = .03, which the authors describe as extremely small.

> "a statistically significant primary effect favoring the control group was identified in relation to both Peer Problems, d = 0.07, 95% CI [− 0.00, 0.15], p = .03 (see Table 4b)"
