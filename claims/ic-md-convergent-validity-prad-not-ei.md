## Evidence

### Fleck 2024

Fleck, L., Amelung, D., Fuchs, A., Mayer, B., Escher, M., Listunova, L., Schultz, J.-H., Möltner, A., Schütte, C., Wittenberg, T., Schneider, I., & Herpertz, S. C. (2024). Interactional competencies in medical student admission– what makes a “good medical doctor”?. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Validation study with N = 70 first-semester medical students completing a five-scenario MMI circuit; Pearson correlation between IC-MD total score and simulation patients' PRA-D ratings showed "a strong significant association" (r = .57, padjust < 0.001).

> "Overall, the evaluation of the IC-MD performance showed a strong significant association with simulation patients’ judgement of the consultation (PRA-D, r = .57, padjust < 0.001)."

### Fleck 2024 (2)

Fleck, L., Amelung, D., Fuchs, A., Mayer, B., Escher, M., Listunova, L., Schultz, J.-H., Möltner, A., Schütte, C., Wittenberg, T., Schneider, I., & Herpertz, S. C. (2024). Interactional competencies in medical student admission– what makes a “good medical doctor”?. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same N = 70 validation study, Bonferroni-Holm-corrected correlations with the MSCEIT performance test and TeiQue self-report were non-significant after correction; equivalence was not tested. The authors note the sample was slightly underpowered.

> "The IC-MD total score was not significantly associated with the EI performance test (MSCEIT, r = .25, padjust = 0.072) or with the EI self-report (TeiQue, r = .19, padjust = 0.110)."
