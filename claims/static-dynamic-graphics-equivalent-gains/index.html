Evidence¶

Anggoro 2012¶

Anggoro, F. K., Stein, N. L., & Jee, B. D. (2012). Cognitive factors that influence children's learning from a multimedia science lesson. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 93-108. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068621

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The ANCOVA on molecular properties gain scores found "no effect of Graphics Condition" with "p = .46" and "ηp2 < .01". Children learned equally well regardless of whether graphics were static or dynamic.

"There was, however, no effect of Graphics Condition, F(1, 279) = 0.54, MSE = .31, p = .46, ηp2 < .01."
