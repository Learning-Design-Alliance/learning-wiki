Perceptual findings from the I-PREP statewide scan of 23 program representatives and 22 stakeholders. The five benefit areas listed are: more rigorous selection of candidates; more rigorous programs with increased authentic and practical preparation due to a more extensive competency-based internship; better preparation across the pre-K through 12 continuum; deeper partnerships; and consistently higher standards statewide.
