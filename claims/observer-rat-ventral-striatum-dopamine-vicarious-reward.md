## Evidence

### Jessica Joiner 2017

Jessica Joiner, Matthew Piva, Courtney Turrin and Steve W. C. Chang. (2017). Social learning through prediction error in the brain. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0009-2

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review reports an animal study of observational learning and vicarious reinforcement measuring dopamine release. Observers showed "significantly more dopamine release in the ventral striatum" for a conspecific's reward versus an empty box; release for other's reward was substantially weaker than for own reward.

> "observer rats vocalized more and experienced significantly more dopamine release in the ventral striatum when seeing another rat receive reward compared to when reward was delivered to an empty box."
