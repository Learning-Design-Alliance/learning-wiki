## Evidence

### Alharbi F 2022

Alharbi F, Alwadei SH, Alwadei A, Asiri S, Alwadei F, Alqerban A, Almuzian M. (2022). Comparison between two asynchronous teaching methods in an undergraduate dental course: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03557-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Modified validated WELEI questionnaire administered after each lecture; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests compared item responses between formats. Largest difference was overall preference (r = 0.656, p < 0.0001); Cronbach's alpha = 0.75.

> "Except for the opportunity to ask questions item (p > 0.05), there were statistically significant differences between the two instructional methods across all other comparable items (p > 0.01 and 0.05), with moderate-to-large effect sizes (r = 0.402 to 0.656)."
