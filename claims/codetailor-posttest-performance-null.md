# Posttest programming scores did not differ significantly between CodeTailor support and receiving an AI-generated solution

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students achieved an equal level of posttest score under both support types; the difference was not statistically significant. [→ Xinying Hou 2024](#xinying-hou-2024)

## Evidence

### Xinying Hou 2024

Xinying Hou, Zihan Wu, Xu Wang, and Barbara J. Ericson. 2024. CodeTailor: LLM-Powered Personalized Parsons Puzzles for Engaging Support While Learning Programming. In Proceedings of the Eleventh ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '24). https://doi.org/10.1145/3657604.3662032

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects study with 14 analyzed participants; posttest performance was the percentage of unit tests passed on two timed, unsupported posttest problems (maximum 10 points per condition). The test showed "W = 2.0, p = .789, CLES = 0.51"; means were 1.4 vs 0.8. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Students achieved an equal level of posttest score when using CodeTailor and the baseline support, W = 2.0, p = .789, CLES = 0.51 (Table 1)."
