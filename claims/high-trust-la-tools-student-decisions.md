## Evidence

### Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023

Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Survey paired t-test for LA-tool item 6 among 65 teaching staff. The article reports item 6 "had the lowest average rating of the gap between predicted and ideal expectations" yet the least ideal expectation, indicating less desirable but high trust (t = 2.301, p = 0.025, Cohen's d = 0.32).

> "The paired t-test of item 6 showed a significant difference between the two types of expectations with a small to medium effect size for item 6 (t = 2.301, p = 0.025, Cohen's d = 0.32)."
