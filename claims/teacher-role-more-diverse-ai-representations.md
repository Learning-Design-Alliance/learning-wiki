## Evidence

### Razan Hamed 2025

Razan Hamed, Amogh Sirnoorkar, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Dual-Role Dynamics in Prompting: Elementary Pre-service Teachers' AI Prompting Strategies for Representational Choices. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.hamed](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.hamed)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of AI-generated representations across the two lab sessions found five types in the teacher role and four in the student role; "cartoons, activities, and graphs were unique representations generated in the teacher role" while stories and equations were unique to the student role.

> "In the teacher role, participants generated pictures (9), cartoons (2), analogies (3), activity (1), and graphs (2). In the student role, participants generated pictures (2), analogies (4), story (1), and equations (5)."
