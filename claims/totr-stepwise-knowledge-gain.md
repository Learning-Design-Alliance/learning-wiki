## Evidence

### Chen Kuo-Su 2021

Chen Kuo-Su, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Huang Min-Ping, Chen Chih-Ken, Hung Ming-Jui. (2021). Academic outcome and moderator of flipped classroom learning program “Teaching on the Run”. Biomedical Journal, 44(5), 636–643. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bj.2020.05.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective before-after analysis of 115 clinical teachers who took all three MCQ tests in eight TOTR sessions (2015–2017); scores rose from "64.84 ± 15.43 to 72.95 ± 13.55" and reached 80.75 ± 13.0 at post-test. No effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Exam scores showed a stepwise increase from pre-test to mid-test (64.84 ± 15.43 to 72.95 ± 13.55, p = 0.001) and post-test [80.75 ± 13.0, p < 0.001 when compared with both pre-test and mid-test, Fig. 1]."
