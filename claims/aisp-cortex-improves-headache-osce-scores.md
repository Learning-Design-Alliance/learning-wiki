## Evidence

### Kridipaka S 2026

Kridipaka S, Ruengwattanachot K, Leksuwankun S, Pongpitakmetha T, Hiransuthikul A, Surawattanawong T, Anukoolwittaya P, Panjasriprakarn P. (2026). AI-powered simulated patients with automated feedback for enhancing headache history-taking skills: a convergent mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09511-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre-post study of 25 third-year medical students completing a one-week AISP practice phase. The paired t-test showed a large significant increase in OSCE scores, with a mean difference of 22.5 points (Cohen's d = 1.93).

> "Mean OSCE score increased from 67.9 (SD 10.8) at pre-test to 90.4 (SD 6.6) at post-test (mean difference 22.5; 95% CI 17.7 to 27.3; p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 1.93)."
