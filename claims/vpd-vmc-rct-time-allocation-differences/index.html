Evidence¶

Lara-Alecio 2024¶

Lara-Alecio, R., Irby, B. J., Tong, F., Sutton-Jones, K. L., Guerrero, C. L., Tang, S., & Pang, H. (2024). Evaluating Pedagogical Practices in Science Classrooms: A Randomized Controlled Trial Study on Teacher Virtual Professional Development with Virtual Mentoring and Coaching. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 16(4), 479-494. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2024.347

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Chi-square analysis of POP-coded observations from 121 fifth-grade science teachers (66 treatment, 55 control) in 68 campuses across 35 Texas districts, 2017-2018, over 13,620 twenty-second video clips. The article reports "Cramer's V = .123" for instructional strategies, with similar significant results for the other domains; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Statistically significant differences were identified regarding teachers' time allocation in the following domains between treatment and control teachers: instructional strategies (χ2(9) = 205.016, p < 0.001, Cramer's V = .123)"
