## Evidence

### Lisa Bardach 2023

Lisa Bardach, Claudia Neuendorf, Kou Murayama, Thorsten Fahrbach, Michel Knigge, Benjamin Nagengast, and Ulrich Trautwein. (2023). Does students' awareness of school-track-related stereotypes exacerbate inequalities in education? npj Science of Learning 8:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00203-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multigroup latent growth curve models (RQ5) controlling for SES, gender, and migration background found no significant slope–slope relationships for any outcome in any track; the article states no support was found for the preregistered hypotheses longitudinally. Equivalence was not tested.

> "we did not find support for any of our hypotheses longitudinally, as changes in students' stereotype awareness were not related to changes in their academic achievement, self-concept of academic aptitude, or engagement"
