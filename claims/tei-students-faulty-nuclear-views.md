### Mirofora Pilakouta 2011

Mirofora Pilakouta. (2011). TEI Piraeus students' knowledge on the beneficial applications of nuclear physics: Nuclear energy, radioactivity - consequences. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.4058)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Preliminary web-based survey of 131 TEI Piraeus engineering students answering a 10-item multiple-choice questionnaire; Figure 1 summarizes correct, incorrect and 'not know' percentages across the first seven questions, showing "inadequate and confused knowledge in these topics".

> "in more than half of the questions, the percentage of students that give incorrect answers is higher than those who answered correctly and much higher than that of the students that declare that they don't know. This suggests that our students have inadequate and confused knowledge in these topics."
