## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2006

Michael C. Wittmann, Jeffrey T. Morgan, Roger E. Feeley. (2006). Laboratory-Tutorial activities for teaching probability. Submitted to Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ungraded pre-test in Fall 2005 in which all students knew a single coin toss was 50-50 and then explained the 11th toss after 10 tosses. Table II reports 57% expecting 50-50 no matter what came before, 24% seeing 4 heads in a row as unlikely, 14% expecting results to end at 50-50, and 7% Bayesian continuation of the 70-30 ratio. The article reports "A majority of students gave the correct answer".

> "A majority of students gave the correct answer, but many gave other responses (see Table II). The results show that many students enter our courses with everyday expectations about what the ensemble values of sets of coin tosses should be and use past results to decide future events."
