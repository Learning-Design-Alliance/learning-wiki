### Samuel Falcon 2023

Samuel Falcon, Jaime Leon. (2023). How do teachers engaging messages affect students? A sentiment analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10230-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability check in which two researchers independently classified students' answers by sentiment label and their classifications were compared with the Microsoft model's using ReCal3. The article reports 80% agreement, Cohen's Kappa of 0.51 and Fleiss' Kappa of 0.50.

> "Results showed an average pairwise percent agreement of 80%, which is quite satisfactory. The value observed for the average pairwise Cohen’s Kappa was 0.51 and for the Fleiss’ Kappa was 0.50, which are moderately good results"
