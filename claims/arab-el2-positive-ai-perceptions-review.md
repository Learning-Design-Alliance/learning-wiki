## Evidence

### Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh 2026

Mohammed Q. Shormani; Muneef Yehia Alshawsh. (2026). AI tools in Arab University English classrooms: Looking back and forward. Preprint, Ibb University, Yemen. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0138-4793

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis of 11 studies included via PRISMA-guided searches of Google Scholar, Web of Science, and Scopus (2023–2025), spanning six Arab countries. The review reports "overwhelmingly positive perceptions" and gains in engagement, confidence, and autonomy. No effect sizes are printed for this synthesis.

> "Findings reveal overwhelmingly positive perceptions of AI-assisted language acquisition/learning. Across the reviewed studies, AI tools were reported to enhance writing quality, vocabulary learning, grammar development, learner engagement, confidence, and autonomy."
