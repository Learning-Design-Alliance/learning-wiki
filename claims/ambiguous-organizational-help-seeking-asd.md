### Sabine Zorn 2022

Sabine Zorn, Minna Puustinen. (2022). Seeking academic help: The case of lower secondary students with autism spectrum disorder and their teachers. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101624

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Video analysis of the same 11 students with ASD's actual help-seeking behavior in everyday classroom situations. The study found help seeking "partly fell into “traditional” help-seeking categories" while ambiguous and organizational help seeking emerged as new categories.

> "Although student help seeking partly fell into “traditional” help-seeking categories (i.e., instrumental help seeking, confirmation-asking), new help-seeking categories also emerged (i.e., ambiguous and organizational help seeking)."
