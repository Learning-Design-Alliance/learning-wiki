## Evidence

### Ana Costa 2023

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the path model (N = 222), trait EI dimensions in 11th grade predicted 12th-grade emotions towards school; "perceptions of managing and regulating emotions (T2) negatively influenced their negative emotions" and reinforced positive emotions.

> "their perceptions of managing and regulating emotions (T2) negatively influenced their negative emotions and fostered positive emotions at the end of secondary school (T3)."

### Ana Costa 2023 (2)

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same path model, ability EI was the only EI variable with a predictive association with achievement: "the ability EI variable (VET) positively predicted students’ Portuguese language, math, and biology grades" in the final year.

> "the ability EI variable (VET) positively predicted students’ Portuguese language, math, and biology grades in 12th grade (T3)."
