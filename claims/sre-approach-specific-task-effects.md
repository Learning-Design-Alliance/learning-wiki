# Approach-specific SRE instruction produced large treatment effects on the assessments aligned to each approach's purpose

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The cognitive-oriented group scored far higher on defending answers with textual evidence (effect size .66) and the reader-response group far higher on response essays (effect size .33). [→ Liang 2011](#liang-2011)

## Evidence

### Liang 2011

Liang, L. A. (2011). Scaffolding Middle School Students' Comprehension and Response to Short Stories. RMLE Online, 34(8). ISSN 1940-4476. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ925248

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of experimental-week scores from a pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 85 sixth graders, CALT reading ability as covariate. The approach-specific measures showed large effects: "an effect size of .66" for defending answers and ".33" for essays.

> "Part 3 of the short-answer quizzes, F(1, 158) = 310.34, p < .001, with an effect size of .66; and for the essays, F(1, 158) = 78.80, p < .001, with an effect size of .33."
