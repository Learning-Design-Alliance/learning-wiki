## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Experimental boys showed significantly greater cardiovascular endurance than control boys after ten weeks, with a negligible effect size (Cohen d = 0.17). [→ Rengasamy et al. 2011](#rengasamy-et-al-2011)

## Evidence

### Rengasamy et al. 2011

Shabeshan Rengasamy, Subramaniam Raju, Wee Akina Sia Seng Lee, Ramachandran Roa. (2011). A Fitness Intervention Program within a Physical Education Class on Cardiovascular Endurance among Malaysia Secondary School Students. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, 2(1). http://www.moj-es.net

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with intact classes of Form Four boys (experimental n = 45, control n = 42); ANCOVA on posttest Cooper's Twelve Minute Run scores with pretest as covariate showed "a significant main effect for boys" with "Cohen d = 0.17".

> "The result of ANCOVA in Table 3 indicates that there was a significant main effect for boys in cardiovascular endurance F(1,84) = 18.17, p <.05; Cohen d = 0.17."
