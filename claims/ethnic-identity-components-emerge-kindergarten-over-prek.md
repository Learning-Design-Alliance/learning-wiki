## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Serrano-Villar 2016

Serrano-Villar, M., & Calzada, E. J. (2016). Ethnic identity: Evidence of protective effects for young, Latino children. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appdev.2015.11.002

`q3 · i?`

Chi-square comparisons by grade in the 674-child sample (Table 2) showed grade differences for self-identification, constancy, and knowledge; the article reports the pattern without effect sizes. The authors interpret this as consistent with the developmental sequencing found by Bernal and colleagues.

> "Consistent with developmental theory, most components of ethnic identity were more likely to be seen in kindergarten, relative to pre-k, children."
