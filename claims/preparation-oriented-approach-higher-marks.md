## Evidence

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis in the Results shows "observed preparation-oriented approach (r = 0.40, p < 0.01)" and "observed weekly learning time (r = 0.37, p < 0.01)" were the strongest correlates of course marks among 271 undergraduates.

> "course marks was significantly correlated with perceptions of integration (r = 0.21, p < 0.01), surface learning approach (r = 0.17, p < 0.01), self-reported learning time above 4 h (r = 0.21, p < 0.01), observed preparation-oriented approach (r = 0.40, p < 0.01), and observed weekly learning time (r = 0.37, p < 0.01)."

### Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi 2025 (2)

Han Feifei & Yang Jingpeiyi (2025). Consistency between self-reported and log data to understand students’ experience of learning in flipped classrooms. Scientific Reports 15, 34369. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17052-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The discussion reports a learning-time difference between the two log-data clusters: preparation-oriented students "on average spent 1.7 h more time than their peers who used assessment-oriented approach", attributed to higher frequencies on all five online activities.

> "Those who used preparation-oriented approach on average spent 1.7 h more time than their peers who used assessment-oriented approach."
