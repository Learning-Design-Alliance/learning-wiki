## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2007

Ayush Gupta, Edward F. Redish, and David Hammer. (2007). Coordination of Mathematics and Physical Resources by Physics Graduate Students. 2007 PERC, AIP Conference Proceedings, Volume 951. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.0012 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.0012](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.0012)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of the seven incorrect homework solutions found a shared initial step: students read Eq. 1(b) as an expansion into real and imaginary parts, although ω remains a complex variable, and solved by equating terms individually to zero.

> "All students who only had access to the expanded-form expression, considered the first and second (without the 'i' factor) terms of Eq. 1(b) as the real and imaginary parts of ε(ω,k) respectively."
