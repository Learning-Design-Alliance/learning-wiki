Evidence¶

Amrita Ganguly 2026¶

Amrita Ganguly, Aditya Johri, Nora McDonald, Areej Ali, Umama Dewan, and Aayushi Hingle Collier. (2026). A Comparative Analysis of Institutional and Course Generative AI Policies within Higher Education: Implications for Instruction in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12296

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Secondary comparative analysis of the linked institutional and course datasets (Figure 2), categorizing the 116 institutions by code coverage into comprehensive, moderately comprehensive, and less comprehensive groups. The authors interpret that comprehensive institutional policies translate better to course level.

"Institutions that have the most comprehensive (8 - 11 codes) GenAI guidelines also include course level guidelines at a moderate to high rate (48.3 - 71.4%)."
