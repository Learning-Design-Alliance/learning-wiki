## Evidence

### Grissa Mohamed Habib et al 2023

Grissa Mohamed Habib et al. (2023). Comparison of simulation and video-based training for acute asthma. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04836-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 69 final-year medical students randomized to HFS (n = 34) or IVC (n = 35); MCQ post-test 1 immediately after a 1-hour teaching session. The HFS group scored "14.5 ± 1.6 vs 13.9 ± 1.6" with p < 0.001 reported in the text.

> "After training session, the HFS group mean post-test 1 score was higher than the IVC score (14.5 ± 1.6 vs 13.9 ± 1.6, respectively; (p < 0.001)."
