# In a randomized experiment, between-teacher variance components in mathematics achievement gains range from 0.123 to 0.135 and in reading gains from 0.066 to 0.074

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Hierarchical linear model analyses of randomized data estimate that teacher effectiveness accounts for a substantial variance component in mathematics achievement gains (0.123-0.135) and a smaller one in reading gains (0.066-0.074). [→ Nye 2004](#nye-2004)

## Evidence

### Nye 2004

Nye, B., Konstantopoulos, S., & Hedges, L. V. (2004). How Large Are Teacher Effects? Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 26(3), 237-257. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=How+Large+Are+Teacher+Effects

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Variance-component estimates from the three-level HLM analysis of the Tennessee Class Size Experiment, in which students and teachers were randomly assigned to classrooms within 79 schools. A companion analysis of reading gains reports components "range from 0.066 to 0.074" for the full model. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The estimated between-teacher variance components in mathematics achievement for the full model range from 0.123 to 0.135."
