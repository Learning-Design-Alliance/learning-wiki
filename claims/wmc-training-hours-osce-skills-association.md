# More self-reported hours of weight management counseling learning in the preclinical years is significantly positively associated with both OSCE score and perceived counseling skill

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across MME and TE students, each one-category increase in preclinical (years 1–2) WMC learning time was associated with an average increase of 0.08 in OSCE score (95% CI 0.01, 0.15) and 0.04 in perceived skills (95% CI 0.02, 0.06). [→ Ockene JK 2021](#ockene-jk-2021)
`q3 i?` Associations with year-3 WMC learning time were positive but smaller and not statistically significant for OSCE score (slope 0.03, 95% CI -0.03, 0.09). [→ Ockene JK 2021 (2)](#ockene-jk-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Ockene JK 2021

Ockene JK, Pbert L, Crawford S, Frisard CF, Pendharkar JA, Sadasivam RS, Faro J, Okuliar C, Eno C, Margo K, Shaw MA, Soleymani T, Stadler DD, Warrier S, White K, Geller AC. (2021). Teaching Medical Students to Help Patients Manage Their Weight: Outcomes of an Eight-School Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-020-06571-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combined MME and TE student analysis of self-reported WMC learning hours in years 1–2 (range 1–8 categories), significantly positively associated with total OSCE score and total perceived WMC skills; slope coefficients with 95% CIs printed, no standardized effect size.

> "an increase of 1 category (e.g., <30 minutes to 30-59 minutes) was associated with an average increase of 0.08 in the OSCE score and of 0.04 in total perceived WMC skills."

### Ockene JK 2021 (2)

Ockene JK, Pbert L, Crawford S, Frisard CF, Pendharkar JA, Sadasivam RS, Faro J, Okuliar C, Eno C, Margo K, Shaw MA, Soleymani T, Stadler DD, Warrier S, White K, Geller AC. (2021). Teaching Medical Students to Help Patients Manage Their Weight: Outcomes of an Eight-School Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-020-06571-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same combined-student analysis for year-3 hours (Table 4): OSCE slope 0.03 (95% CI -0.03, 0.09); perceived skills slope 0.03 (95% CI 0.01, 0.04).

> "The corresponding associations with time in MS3 year also were positive but smaller and not statistically significant for the OSCE score."
