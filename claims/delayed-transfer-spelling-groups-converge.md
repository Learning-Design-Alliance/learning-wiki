## Evidence

### Turner 1912

Turner, E. A.; Markus, H. P. (1912). Rules versus drill in teaching spelling; Some results in manual training. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069736

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Delayed transfer test ('E' test) of the same new words, given five days after the 'D' test to both spelling groups. The report hedges that the drill group "seems to have recovered the ground lost." No effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "the drill group seems to have recovered the ground lost in the 'TV test; the rules group gains a better average than in the ''I)' test, but the influence of the latter test is not so strongly marked as with the drill group."
