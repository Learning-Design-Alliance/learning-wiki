Webb, M. E., Fluck, A., Magenheim, J., Malyn-Smith, J., Waters, J., Deschênes, M., & Zagami, J. (2021). Machine learning for human learners: opportunities, issues, tensions and threats. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09858-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article describes a system reported by Bosch et al. that used computer vision, learning analytics and machine learning to detect students' affect in a school computer lab containing as many as thirty students at a time, with identification accuracy "up to 98% of the time". The authors take this to show machine learning components can assist with educational aspects previously presumed the sole domain of teachers.

> "The system could identify student boredom, confusion, delight, engagement, and frustration in natural environments up to 98% of the time."
