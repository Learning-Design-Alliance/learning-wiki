Feng 2025¶

Feng, M., Li, L., Brezack, N., Huang, C.-W., Luttgen, K., Grady, J., Yang, S., Lavine, A., Collins, K., & Schneider, M. (2025). Scaling teachers' professional development for ASSISTments: Evaluation final report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677852

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

Baseline equivalence analysis in the Methods section of the quasi-experimental study, comparing treatment students (n = 2,855) with matched virtual comparison students on the NWEA MAP Growth pretest. The printed effect size was "effect size = -0.03", which the report notes satisfies the WWC baseline equivalence requirement.

"The baseline equivalence test on the pretest revealed that the average mean of VCGs was very close to the treatment group (effect size = -0.03), implying that the matching algorithm worked as expected"

Related Claims¶

  • In the full-sample analysis, baseline demographic and prior-achievement differences between treatment and matched comparison groups were minimal, with effect sizes below the WWC threshold — related
  • The vPLC-augmented ASSISTments treatment sample comprised 59 teachers and 2,855 students in grades 6-8 from 36 public schools across 24 U.S. states, half in rural areas — related
  • Students with lower baseline performance (below the 50th percentile) showed significantly greater math achievement gains with ASSISTments than matched virtual peers — related
  • Across the full sample, students receiving the ASSISTments intervention performed comparably to their virtual comparison peers on end-of-year MAP Growth — related
  • Propensity score matching produced baseline-equivalent treatment and comparison groups on all measured variables — related
  • Baseline equivalence was established only for low-achieving students, so analyses for other grades and subpopulations do not meet WWC evidence standards — related
  • vPLC-augmented ASSISTments did not significantly improve overall middle school math achievement relative to a virtual comparison group — related
