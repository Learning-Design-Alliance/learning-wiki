## Evidence

### Visher 2016

Visher, M. G., Mayer, A. K., Johns, M., Rudd, T., Levine, A., and Rauner, M. (2016). Scaling academic planning in community college: A randomized controlled trial (REL 2017–204). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory West. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory (non-confirmatory) comparison between the two intervention arms of the RCT; table 2 prints workshop-versus-one-on-one differences of -2.1, -2.1, and -3.7 percentage points with no significance stars. The article states the study "was not powered to definitively determine whether group counseling was as effective as one-on-one counseling."

> "There was no significant difference in session attendance or academic plan completion between the workshop and one-on-one counseling groups."
