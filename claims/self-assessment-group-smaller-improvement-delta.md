## Evidence

### Hillemans Vera 2024

Hillemans Vera, Buyne Otmar, de Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne M.B.I., Verhoeven Bas H., Joosten Maja. (2024). Self-assessment, and not continuous training, improves basic open suturing skills. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2374101

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delta-score comparisons between the three groups (Wilcoxon S-R within groups, Kruskal Wallis between groups) across the four tracked suturing tasks. For Donati composite score the delta was "clinical exposure group: 22.7 vs. continuous training group: 16.4 vs. self-assessment group: 10.1, p = 0.007".

> "This resulted in a smaller delta of time (‘transcutaneous suture', p = 0.013), distance (‘Donati suture' and ‘intracutaneous suture', 0.005 ≤ p ≤ 0.009) or composite score (all tasks, except for knot tying by hand, 0.007 ≤ p ≤ 0.061) in the self-assessment group."
