## Evidence

### Daniel Caravez 2018

Daniel Caravez, Angelica De La Torre, Jayson Nissen, and Ben Van Dusen. (2018). Longitudinal associations between learning assistants and instructor effectiveness. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.015](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.015)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling of LASSO database data from 4,365 students in 93 introductory physics courses, with students nested within courses and times taught without LAs as a level-2 predictor. The model predicts a reliable decrease (p=0.01) of "1.2%/term" in posttest scores as experience without LAs increases.

> "Model 3 shows that as the number of times instruc- tors have taught a course without LAs increases their posttest scores are predicted to reliably (p=0.01) de- crease by 1.2%/term (Table 1)."
