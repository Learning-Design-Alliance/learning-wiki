## Evidence

### The EVALUATE Group 2019

The EVALUATE Group. (2019). Evaluating the impact of virtual exchange on initial teacher education: a European policy experiment. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2019.29.9782490057337

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same quasi-experimental pre-/post-test design measured digital-pedagogical competence using a 17-item shortened version of the Schmidt et al. (2009) TPACK questionnaire. The report states pre-post TPACK correlations indicated "a rho=.492, p<.01" and cautions that this "does not meet normal conventions of >.60".

> "Pre- and post-test Cronbach alphas indicated reliable constructs, and pre-post TPACK correlations indicated a rho=.492, p<.01, indicating that we should treat this construct carefully as it does not meet normal conventions of >.60."
