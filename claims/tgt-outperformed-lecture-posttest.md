## Evidence

### Abdus Salam 2015

Abdus Salam, Anwar Hossain, & Shahidur Rahman. (2015). Effects of using Teams Games Tournaments (TGT) Cooperative Technique for Learning Mathematics in Secondary Schools of Bangladesh. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085938

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 43 grade eight students per group (Table 1): posttest means of 24.56 (SD = 8.42) for the TGT group and 9.65 (SD = 5.81) for the lecture group. The article's prose gives the TGT posttest SD as 12.71, but in Table 1 12.71 is the TGT group's pretest-to-posttest gain; the posttest SD is 8.42. The article calls the posttest difference significant, but the t-tests it prints are within-group pretest-posttest comparisons (TGT t = -7.172, p < .05; control gain 1.75, t = -1.256, p = .213), not a between-group test, and the groups differed at pretest (means 11.85 and 7.90). The authors compare the gains (12.71 against 1.75, a 10.96-point difference).

> "the reported posttest mean score for the experimental group was 24.56 (SD = 12.71) compared to the control group of 9.65 ( SD = 5.81) which showed a remarkable and significant difference in scores."
