## Evidence

### Bos-Bonnie Linda H. A. 2017

Bos-Bonnie Linda H. A., van Bergen Jan E. A. M. J., te Pas Ellen, Kijser Michael A. M., van Dijk Nynke. (2017). Effectiveness of an individual, online e-learning program about sexually transmitted infections: a prospective cohort study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-017-0625-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cohort analysis comparing quickscan scores by time since completing the program (ANOVA with post-hoc Bonferroni). The article reports "no significant differences present" between the two time-since-completion groups, supporting persistence of the effect up to two years.

> "There were no significant differences present between participants who completed the e-learning program less than one year ago and participants who completed the e-learning program more than one year ago (data not shown)."
