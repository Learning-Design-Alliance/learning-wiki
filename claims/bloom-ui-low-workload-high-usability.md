## Evidence

### Abdolali Faraji 2026

Abdolali Faraji, Mohammadreza Molavi, Zohreh Rasoulkhani, Mohammadreza Tavakoli, and Gábor Kismihók. (2026). Cross-Dataset Bloom Question Classification: Supervised Models and Prompted LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13684

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Usability study (RQ3) with N=50 Prolific participants who used the UI for 10 minutes on a real course, then answered three NASA-TLX items (1–5 scale) and the SUS. Means: "mental demand2.32, effort2.46, frustration1.68"; mean SUS 78.2 (SD 14.07).

> "Participants reported low perceived workload (means: mental demand2.32, effort2.46, frustration1.68), and the tool achieved very good usability (mean SUS= 78.2, SD= 14.07)."
