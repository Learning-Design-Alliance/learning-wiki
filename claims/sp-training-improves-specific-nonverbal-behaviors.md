## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Frequency of good facial expression, open body position, no unnecessary silence, no un-purposive movements, and no giggle each changed significantly between pre-course interview and post-course OSCE. [→ Park Kyung Hye 2018](#park-kyung-hye-2018)

## Evidence

### Park Kyung Hye 2018

Park Kyung Hye, Park Seung Guk. (2018). The effect of communication training using standardized patients on nonverbal behaviors in medical students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.90

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons of the 12 checklist items across the 51 students' pre-course interview and post-course OSCE videos. Unnecessary silence, un-purposive movements, and giggle decreased, and "open body position, adequate facial expression, and hand gestures were increased"; Table 2 prints p-values of 0.001, 0.025, and <0.001 for the three decreases, and 0.001 and 0.003 for facial expression and body position.

> "In addition, unnecessary silence, un-purposive movements, and giggle were decreased to 49.02%, 19.61%, and 37.26%, respectively. With the exception of hand gesture, the change in frequency of these NVCs can be interpreted as positive effects of the education."
