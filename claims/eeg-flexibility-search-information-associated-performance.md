## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Shafiei 2024

Shafiei, S. B., Shadpour, S., Sasangohar, F., Mohler, J. L., Attwood, K. & Jing, Z. (2024). Development of performance and learning rate evaluation models in robot-assisted surgery using electroencephalography and eye-tracking. npj Science of Learning 9:3. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00216-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear random intercept model for Suture Sponge performance (66 attempts; subject random effect p = 0.004, pseudo R2 = 0.75). Features came from coherence-based functional brain networks across 21 Brodmann areas and four frequency bands; only regression estimates and p-values are printed.

> "A one-standard deviation increase in the average temporal flexibility in BA 10 at beta-band frequencies was associated with a 0.6-point improvement in the performance score for the suture sponge task, suggesting that enhanced neural flexibility in this area of the brain is associated with better performance."

### Shafiei 2024 (2)

Shafiei, S. B., Shadpour, S., Sasangohar, F., Mohler, J. L., Attwood, K. & Jing, Z. (2024). Development of performance and learning rate evaluation models in robot-assisted surgery using electroencephalography and eye-tracking. npj Science of Learning 9:3. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00216-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same Tubes random intercept model (61 attempts, pseudo R2 = 0.71), reporting "a 0.52-point performance improvement" per SD increase in BA 18 beta-band temporal flexibility. The authors link BA 18 to visual attention and visuo-spatial processing and beta-band activity to logical and conscious thinking, as interpretation rather than a tested mediation.

> "In contrast, a one-standard deviation increase in the average temporal ﬂexibility in Brodmann area 18 (BA 18) at beta band frequencies was associated with a 0.52-point performance improvement"
