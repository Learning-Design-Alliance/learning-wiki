Evidence¶

Ravesh and Tabrizi 2017¶

Mahnaz Mahmoudi Ravesh, Hossein Heidari Tabrizi. (2017). The Effect of Teaching Interlanguage Pragmatics on Interpretation Ability of Iranian Translation Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 8(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.3p.44

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 30 undergraduate translation students split into control and experimental groups; ANCOVA on post-test scores with pre-test as covariate showed the group effect was significant, with post-test means of 27.33 (experimental) versus 19.60 (control). No effect size is printed.

"More importantly, Sig. = 0.000 for the groups is lower than 0.05 and this means that there exists a significant difference between the performances of the control and the treatment groups."
