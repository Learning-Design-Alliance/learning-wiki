Longitudinal relations among self-concept, intrinsic value, and attainment value across secondary school years in three academic domains. The Journal of educational psychology 111 (2019) 4, S. 663-684. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000313

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel model analyses across five annual waves (Grades 5–9, N = 2,116 German secondary students) showed the reciprocal-direction relation in German: "former intrinsic value and attainment value were positively related to later academic self-concept."
