### Babaei Keivan 2023

Babaei Keivan, Sadeghian Efat, Khodaveisi Masoud. (2023). The Predictors of Clinical Competence among Hospital Nurses: A Cross-Sectional Study in Hamadan, West Iran. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v33i1.5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional group comparison among 270 nurses by experience group (≤5 vs >5 years); Table 1 shows total CC means of 38.11±8 versus 44.48±8.3. Effect sizes were not printed, so no standardized magnitude is reported.

> "Moreover, compared with nurses with lower work experience, nurses with higher work experience obtained significantly higher scores for CC and its dimensions (P<005), except for the ensuring quality dimension (P=0.837)."
