### Franziska Greinert 2023

Franziska Greinert, Rainer Müller, Philipp Bitzenbauer, Malte S. Ubben, Kim-Alessandro Weber. (2023). The Future Quantum Workforce: Competences, Requirements and Forecasts. arXiv:2208.08249. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010137](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010137)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of the main 1 questionnaire, in which 56 participants formulated competences for their subfields in a three-aspect format (competence, usefulness, needed expertise level). The analysis found "a three-part structure: QTs/applications, theoretical back- ground, and practical background".

> "Thus, we found a three-part structure: QTs/applications, theoretical back- ground, and practical background, where applications are central."
