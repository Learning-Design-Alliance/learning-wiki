Evidence¶

Adam Chuderski 2021¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

BANOVA on 1017 correct-solution ratings across 20 RAPM items with participant as random factor, in a study of 100 adults. The article reports Suddenness "reliably decreased as a function of the item difficulty" with ηp2 = .07, BF10 > 999.

"Suddenness reliably decreased as a function of the item difficulty, BF 10 > 999, and yielded a moderate effect size of ηp2 = .07."

Adam Chuderski 2021 (2)¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Same BANOVA on correct-solution ratings across the 20 RAPM items. Certainty showed a strong difficulty effect, ηp2 = .24, BF10 > 999, and Pleasure yielded a virtually identical effect.

"A reliable effect was observed also for Certainty, BF 10 > 999, and it was strong, ηp2 = .24 (Pleasure yielded a virtually identical effect)."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Correct RAPM solutions yield higher Suddenness and Certainty and lower Intuition than incorrect solutions, with a Certainty-by-difficulty interaction — related
  • Subjective experience on the easiest RAPM items matches that of insight problems, while difficult items resemble analytic problems — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Intuition ratings are unaffected by RAPM item difficulty, indicating stable strategy across items — related
  • Across participants, RAPM scores correlate positively with Certainty but not with Intuition or Suddenness — related
  • Mean solution time almost perfectly predicts item-level Suddenness and Certainty, and fast performance signals easy items while deliberation signals hard ones — related
  • The four-dimensional subjective-experience ratings are validated against classic insight and analytic problems, with Intuition and Suddenness differentiating the problem categories — related
