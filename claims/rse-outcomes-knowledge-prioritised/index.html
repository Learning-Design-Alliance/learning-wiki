van Leent 2023¶

van Leent, Lisa, Walsh, Kerryann, Moran, Claire, Hand, Kirstine, & French, Sally (2023). Effectiveness of relationships and sex education: A systematic review of terminology, content, pedagogy, and outcomes. Educational Research Review, 39, Article number: 100527. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100527

q3 · i? · review · r3

Outcome mapping across the review corpus. The review reports that "55 did not report on any RSE outcomes" and that few studies measured communication (n=6), access to services (n=6), gender norms (n=3), body image self-esteem (n=1), or critical thinking (n=1). No effect size printed.

"Of the 45 studies reporting student outcomes, the vast majority reported on knowledge (n=19); attitudes or opinions (n=11); self-reported behaviours such as sexual activity and condom use (n=11); and the acceptability of learning experiences (n=10)."
