### Sikorski C 2012

Sikorski C, Luppa M, König H-H, van den Bussche H, Riedel-Heller SG. (2012). Does GP training in depression care affect patient outcome? - A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-12-10

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of RCTs; three studies with GP training as sole intervention (Bosmans 2006, Gask 2004, King 2002) reported no significant symptom change. "The three studies solely providing physician education found no change in symptom severity."

> "The three studies solely providing physician education found no change in symptom severity. Neither lectures for more qualified assessment and treatment [33,38], nor training in brief cognitive behavioural therapy [41] led to significant symptom change in patients of trained physicians."
