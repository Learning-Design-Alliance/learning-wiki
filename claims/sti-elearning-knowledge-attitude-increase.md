## Evidence

### Bos-Bonnie Linda H. A. 2017

Bos-Bonnie Linda H. A., van Bergen Jan E. A. M. J., te Pas Ellen, Kijser Michael A. M., van Dijk Nynke. (2017). Effectiveness of an individual, online e-learning program about sexually transmitted infections: a prospective cohort study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-017-0625-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study comparing before-and-after quickscan scores (4-point Likert) of GPs who completed the e-learning program; paired sample T-tests. The article reports "a statistically significant increase on 5 of the 11 questions", e.g. chlamydia testing mean difference 0.37 (0.22–0.53), p < 0.001.

> "Participants showed a statistically significant increase on 5 of the 11 questions. These questions concerned the sexual history taking when patients presented with questions concerning STI, the feeling of competence regarding the STI-consultation, the STI-testing policy in patients under the age of 25 years, the consideration of the diagnosis of HIV in patients with flu-like symptoms, and the persuasion of an active investigation policy in the GP-practice towards HIV-infections."
