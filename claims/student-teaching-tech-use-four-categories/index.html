Evidence¶

Buss 2017¶

Buss, R. R., Lindsey, L., Foulger, T. S., Wetzel, K., & Pasquel, S. (2017). Assessing a technology infusion approach in a teacher preparation program. International Journal of Technology in Teaching and Learning, 13(1), 33-44. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Assessing+a+technology+infusion+approach+in+a+teacher+preparation+program

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended survey responses describing technology use during student teaching. Responses "ranged from no or little use to daily use of technology as part of instruction," and the nature-of-use theme yielded the four categories, with interactive whiteboard use the most frequent response.

"Broadly speaking, there were four ways TCs employed technology in their classrooms including (a) teacher use, (b) student use --effective, but not transformative, (c) higher level student use, and (d) use of the interactive whiteboard."
