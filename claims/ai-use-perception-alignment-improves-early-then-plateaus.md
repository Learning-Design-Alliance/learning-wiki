## Evidence

### Laura Graf 2026

Laura Graf, Ramona Beinstingel, Stephan Krusche, Oleksandra Poquet. (2026). Students' Perception Accuracy of Partners' AI Use and its Relation to Collaboration Performance. Preprint, Technical University of Munich. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23237

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests comparing absolute misalignment (|d|) between consecutive waves in the 103-pair longitudinal survey; the article prints "r= .35" for the t1–t3 change and a non-significant t2–t3 change (p = .156). At t1, 24% of teams showed substantial misalignment (|d| > 0.2), dropping to 14% by t3.

> "Then, alignment improved significantly over the project (Wilcoxon signed-rank tests t1 to t3: W= 2292, p = .003, r= .35). The change from t2 to t3 was not significant (p = .156), indicating that the alignment took place in the earlier weeks."
