`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on the 320-worker cross-sectional survey data, with variables significant at p < 0.2 in bivariate analysis entered as predictors. The regression found prior training "was the strongest predictor of good knowledge (AOR = 2.6; 95% CI: 1.6–4.3)" and experience >10 years significant at AOR = 1.8 (95% CI: 1.1–3.0).

> "Multivariable logistic regression revealed that receiving prior hypertension training was the strongest predictor of good knowledge (AOR = 2.6; 95% CI: 1.6–4.3), and having > 10 years of experience was also significant (AOR = 1.8; 95% CI: 1.1–3.0)."
