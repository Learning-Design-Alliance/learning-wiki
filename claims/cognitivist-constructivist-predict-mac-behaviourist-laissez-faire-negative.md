## Evidence

### Fonseca-Pinto R and Moreno-Murcia JA 2024

Fonseca-Pinto R and Moreno-Murcia JA (2024). Teaching Self-efficacy and Teaching Methods in the Aquatic Environment. F1000Research 13: 1363. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.150535.1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression on survey responses of 607 aquatic educators predicting MAC from four teaching methodologies. The article reports "an explained variance of 12%" with the standardized coefficients quoted above; no standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "The methodologies that positively predicted MAC were cognitive (ß = .29, p < .01) and constructivist (ß = .13, p < .05), while behaviourist (ß = -.12, p < .05) and let-do (ß = -.14, p < .05) methodologies predicted it negatively."
