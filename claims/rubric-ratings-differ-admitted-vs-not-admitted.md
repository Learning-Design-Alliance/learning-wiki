### Young 2022

Young, N. T., Tollefson, K., Zegers, R. G. T., and Caballero, M. D. (2022). Rubric-based holistic review: a promising route to equitable graduate admissions in physics. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020140)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of faculty rubric ratings for 321 domestic applicants to one physics graduate program across fall 2018-2020, compared by later admission status using Fisher's Exact Test with Holm-Bonferroni-corrected p-values. The study found "statistically signiﬁcant differences in the distribution of all ratings" between the two groups.

> "When looking at how faculty rate applicants who would later be admitted compared to applicants who would not be admitted, we see statistically signiﬁcant differences in the distribution of all ratings (Fig. 2)."
