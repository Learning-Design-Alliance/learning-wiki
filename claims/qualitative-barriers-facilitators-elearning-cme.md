## Evidence

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative interviews and focus group discussions (30 key informant interviews, 44 FGDs) analysed by hybrid thematic analysis; county-level personnel said institutional equipment and laboratory capability prevented practice change. Other reported barriers were not remembering new knowledge and topics being too easy.

> "even if they have improved their knowledge by training, the equipment, available drugs, and laboratory capabilities of their medical institutions cannot support them to change"

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021 (2)

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of interviews and FGDs in the quasi-experiment; public health physicians described content irrelevance to daily work, matching the quantitative null for that group.

> "Many public health physicians in the interviews reported that the training contents they received did not cover the topics in their daily work and were not helpful."
