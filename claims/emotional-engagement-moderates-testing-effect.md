## Evidence

### Kalyva 2026

Kalyva, M., Kavvadia, K., Louca, L., Televantou, I., & Michaelides, M. P. (2026). The “Testing Effect” in Undergraduate Endodontic Education: The Impact of Regular Low Stakes Testing on Learning Outcomes and Student Engagement. International Endodontic Journal, 59(6), 1227–1235. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.70022

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA with emotional engagement as covariate on the questionnaire subset (n = 44 of 81). The interaction "between the effect and EE was significant" (F 1,40 = 6.672, p = 0.013), effect size η² = 0.145, a large effect; the main effect was not significant.

> "the interaction between the effect and EE was significant (F 1,40 = 6.672, p = 0.013, η p 2 = 0.145) suggesting that the gain from the intervention tended to be higher for students who reported higher scores of emotional engagement."
