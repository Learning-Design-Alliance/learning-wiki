### math-output

Noakes, S., Shell, A., Murillo, A., Van Nostrand, P., Ruiz, P., Cornick, S., and Karim, S. (2024, October). An Ethical and Equitable Vision of AI in Education: Learning Across 28 Exploratory Projects. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/232

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Technical findings from the 28-project cohort, summarized in Table 5. Teams reported that in some instances models "generated four options that were all wrong or all right" and produced word problems incoherent with the concepts considered; generating math visuals was also not usable during the projects.

> "This is an area where models are currently not technically capable of high-quality outputs. In particular, AI (or at least ChatGPT4.0 as of spring 2024) is not yet able to generate meaningful multiple choice problems"
