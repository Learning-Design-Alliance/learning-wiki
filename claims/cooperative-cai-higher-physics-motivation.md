## Evidence

### Gambari et al. 2015

Amosa Isiaka Gambari, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf, David Akpa Thomas. (2015). Effects of Computer-Assisted STAD, LTM and ICI Cooperative Learning Strategies on Nigerian Secondary School Students' Achievement, Gender and Motivation in Physics. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, Volume 3, Issue 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of post-survey Physics Motivational Scale scores (23-item, four-point instrument, reliability .82) across the STAD, LTM and ICI groups with pre-motivation as covariate.

> "Table 9 revealed that an F (1, 90) = 64.627, p = 0.000 for the main effect (motivation) was significant, this indicates that the method of instruction produced motivation among the three groups."

### Gambari et al. 2015 (2)

Amosa Isiaka Gambari, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf, David Akpa Thomas. (2015). Effects of Computer-Assisted STAD, LTM and ICI Cooperative Learning Strategies on Nigerian Secondary School Students' Achievement, Gender and Motivation in Physics. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, Volume 3, Issue 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Scheffe post-hoc analysis of motivation means (Table 10); the article also reports significant differences between STAD (X = 4.068) and ICI (2.845) and between STAD and LTM (p = 0.003).

> "It also indicates significant difference in the post-test mean scores of students exposed to LTM (X = 3.686) and those exposed to ICI (2.845)."
