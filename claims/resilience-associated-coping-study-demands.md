## Evidence

### Kvintová Jana 2025

Kvintová Jana, Lacková Lucia, Liu Hongyang, Váchová Lucie, Potmesil Milon, Růžičková Veronika, Kopecký Kamil, Regec Vojtech. (2025). Resilience displays similar associative patterns with academic achievement regardless of the personality and mental health profile of future teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03697-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 793 teacher-training students; whole-sample multiple regression of five CD-RISC-25 resilience domains on the AAQ coping subscale. The model was "highly significant" with adjusted R2 = 0.21; Control showed the strongest unique association (R2 = 0.11, p < 0.001).

> "For coping with study demands, the regression model was highly significant (F[5, 787] = 43.26, p < 0.001), explaining 21% of the variance (adjusted R2 = 0.21). Control (CNT) emerged as the strongest unique association of coping abilities (R2 = 0.11, p < 0.001)"
