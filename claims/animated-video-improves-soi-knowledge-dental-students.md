## Evidence

### Zarabadipour Mahdieh 2025

Zarabadipour Mahdieh, Gholizadeh Negar, Mirzadeh Monirsadat, Khorasani Erfan. (2025). Impact of educational intervention on dental students’ competence in managing injuries caused by sharp objects: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07271-y

Quasi-experimental before-after study of 87 dental students at Qazvin University of Medical Sciences; a "significant difference was found in immediate post-injury performance knowledge (P = 0.001)" and the other listed knowledge items after the video. No effect size is printed for these contrasts.
