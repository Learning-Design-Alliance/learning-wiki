### Marta Isabel Garrido 2016

Marta Isabel Garrido, Chee Leong James Teng, Jeremy Alexander Taylor, Elise Genevieve Rowe and Jason Brett Mattingley. (2016). Surprise responses in the human brain demonstrate statistical learning under high concurrent cognitive demand. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up t-tests in Experiment 1's single-channel analysis of 19 participants showed "larger responses to odds within the narrow than the broad distribution conditions", t(18) = −2.32, P = 0.032; means did not differ between contexts (t(18) = 1.43, P = 0.169).

> "these MMNs, or prediction error responses, were larger under the narrow than the broad distribution (surprise by variance interaction, F(1,18) = 7.99, P = 0.011). Follow-up analysis revealed larger responses to odds within the narrow than the broad distribution conditions (t(18) =− 2.32, P = 0.032)"
