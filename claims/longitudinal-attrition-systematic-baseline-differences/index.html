Evidence¶

John T. E. Richardson 2017¶

John T. E. Richardson. (2017). Student Learning in Higher Education: a Commentary. Educational Psychology Review, 29, 353–362. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9410-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The commentary cites Watkins and Hattie (1985), who surveyed Australian tertiary students in first year and followed them up in third year, finding systematic and significant baseline differences among continuers, dropouts, and decliners. Attributed second-hand; no effect size printed.

"When Watkins and Hattie examined the questionnaire scores obtained by the students in the first survey, there were systematic and significant differences among these three groups"
