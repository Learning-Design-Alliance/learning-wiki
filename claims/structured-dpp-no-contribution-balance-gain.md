## Evidence

### Tsompanoudi et al. 2015

Despina TSOMPANOUDI, Maya SATRATZEMI, Stelios XINOGALOS. (2015). Distributed Pair Programming Using Collaboration Scripts: An Educational System and Initial Results. Informatics in Education. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Contribution-rate analysis of logged code submissions across five projects in the 24-pair evaluation, calculating each student's contribution as the ratio of typed code to total code. The article states the Mann-Whitney test "indicated that the difference between control and experimental condition was not significant (p > 0.05)".

> "It was found that the control group had an average difference of 0.243, while the experimental group had an average difference of 0.165. Values within the range 0 and 0.2, namely a participation rate between 40% and 60%, are sufficient to indicate symmetry in contribution."
