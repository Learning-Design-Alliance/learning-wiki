Nagovitsyn et al. 2018¶

Roman S. Nagovitsyn, Dana K. Bartosh, Aleksandr Y. Ratsimor, Yuri G. Maksimov. (2018). Formation of Social Tolerance Among Future Teachers. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.4.754

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Rogov 'Are you a conflict person?' test results (Table 5) in the 72-student experiment; EG moved from 20.3 to 13.9 points (Wilcoxon Temp = 1, p<0.01), CG from 18.9 to 19.5 (Temp = 160, p<0.01).

"In the course of experimental work, it was found that in the EG the average level of conflict increased by 6.4 points, which is 20 %."
