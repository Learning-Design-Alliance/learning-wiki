## Evidence

### Pelakh 2025

Pelakh, A., Good, M. L., Kuo, E., Tumminia, M., Jamal-Orozco, N., Adelman, A., Antoan, J., Galla, B., & Nokes-Malach, T. J. (2025). Thematic analysis of student perceptions of resources and demands experienced in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.14692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.14692)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive coding of 546 segments from semi-structured focus groups with 24 students screened for negative perceptions (composite Cronbach's alpha 0.86, N = 135 screened). Table V reports classroom/course structures at 69.2% negative with a negative-to-positive ratio of 6.35, instructor 61.7% (4.58), and exam 54.3% (3.53). The study is "a focus on students with more negative beliefs, feelings, and attitudes toward their physics course."

> "TABLE V shows that the codes with the three highest negative to positive ratios and the three highest percentages of negative comments were CLASSROOM, INSTRUCTOR, and EXAM."
