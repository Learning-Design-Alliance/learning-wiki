## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students taught with FCCL demonstrate significantly superior clinical analysis skills compared to LBL students (Cohen's d = 1.53, 95% CI: 0.86 to 2.19, P = 0.00). [→ Shi Xu-Ying 2025](#shi-xu-ying-2025)

## Evidence

### Shi Xu-Ying 2025

Shi Xu-Ying, Lu Bing-Rui, Yin Qing, Wang Qiu-Wen, Fang Yong-Qi, Sun Zhi-Gang. (2025). Whether case-based teaching combined with the flipped classroom is more valuable than traditional lecture-based teaching methods in clinical medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07465-4

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of four studies (1,358 students) under a random-effects model given significant heterogeneity (I2 = 96.61%). The authors report "a large effect size between the two groups" for clinical analytical skills favoring FCCL.

> "The pooled effect size revealed a statistically significant difference in clinical analysis skills between the two groups (Cohen’s d = 1.53, 95% CI: 0.86 to 2.19, P = 0.00) (Fig. 3b)."
