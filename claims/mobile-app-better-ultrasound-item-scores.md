## Evidence

### Fernández-Lao Carolina 2016

Fernández-Lao Carolina, Cantarero-Villanueva Irene, Galiano-Castillo Noelia, Caro-Morán Elena, Díaz-Rodríguez Lourdes, Arroyo-Morales Manuel. (2016). The effectiveness of a mobile application for the development of palpation and ultrasound imaging skills to supplement the traditional learning of physiotherapy students. BMC Medical Education 16:274. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0775-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled study, the m-learning group scored higher on positioning of patient (2.925 ± .266 vs 1.869 ± .344), positioning of ultrasound probe (2.629 ± .791 vs 1.869 ± 1.099), and handling of ultrasound probe (1.703 ± .724 vs 1.173 ± .716).

> "The group with the mobile app also positioned the ultrasound probe better than the control group did (p = .007), and they demonstrated better handling of ultrasound probe (p = .013)."
