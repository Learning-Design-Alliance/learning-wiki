Systematic review of implementation elements across 41 RCTs; the review reports that 20 of 41 studies reported on recruitment and selection, with methods including ads, word of mouth, formal job postings, and agency partnerships.

"Twenty (49%) of the 41 included studies reported on recruitment and selection of peer supporters."
