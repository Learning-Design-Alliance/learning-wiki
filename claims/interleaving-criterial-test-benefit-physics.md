## Evidence

### Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered counterbalanced within-subjects classroom experiment in two lecture sections of an introductory physics course; 290 and 286 students completed all homework in Stages 1 and 2. The article reports "d = 0.40" in Stage 1 and "d = 0.91" in Stage 2, with interleaving improving median test scores over blocking by 50% and 125%.

> "Interleaving yielded higher criterial test scores than blocking in Stage 1, d = 0.40, 95% CI [0.17, 0.65], t(288) = 3.41, p = 0.0008, and in Stage 2, d = 0.91, 95% CI [0.66, 1.20], t(284) = 7.68, p < 0.0001."
