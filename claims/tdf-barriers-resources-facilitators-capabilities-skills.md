## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In qualitative analysis of open-ended six-month responses coded with the Theoretical Domains Framework, the most frequent barriers related to environmental context and resources (e.g., lack of time, unavailable technologies), and facilitators related to skills and beliefs about capabilities. ### Bakwa Kanyinga Felly 2023

Bakwa Kanyinga Felly, Gogovor Amédé, Dofara Suélène Georgina, Gadio Souleymane, Tremblay Martin, Daniel Sam J., Rivest Louis-Paul, Légaré France. (2023). Evaluating the impact of continuing professional development courses on physician behavioral intention: a pre-post study with follow-up at six months. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative thematic deductive coding of open-ended six-month questionnaire responses by two researchers, refined into TDF domains (Tables 5 and 6). Regarding impact on patient safety and health, barriers also related to environmental context and resources and facilitators to skills.

> "barriers to adopting the behavior most frequently related to the TDF domains of environmental context and resources (e.g., not having enough time or not having the relevant technologies in the hospital); while facilitators most frequently mapped to the domains of both skills and beliefs about capabilities"
