### Tripp Brie 2025

Tripp Brie, Ravi Akshaya, Pang Ethan, Furrow Robert E. (2025). Undergraduate STEM Students’ Perceptions of Grading Practices Reveal that Quiz Retakes Positively Impact Drivers of Self-determination. CBE—Life Sciences Education 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-06-0167

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 18 student interviews on grading experiences. "89%, n = 16" were dissatisfied with traditional practices; 78% (n = 14) said such practices harmed well-being and learning, and 55% (n = 10) cited excessive exam coverage promoting memorization.

> "The vast majority of students (89%, n = 16) expressed dissatisfaction with traditional gradings practices such as high-stakes assessments and opaque grade distributions due to curving."
