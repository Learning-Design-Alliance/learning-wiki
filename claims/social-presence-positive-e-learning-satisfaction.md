### Cofini Vincenza 2022

Cofini Vincenza, Perilli Enrico, Moretti Annalucia, Bianchini Valeria, Perazzini Matteo, Muselli Mario, Lanzi Sabrina, Tobia Loreta, Fabiani Leila, Necozione Stefano. (2022). E-Learning Satisfaction, Stress, Quality of Life, and Coping: A Cross-Sectional Study in Italian University Students a Year after the COVID-19 Pandemic Began. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19138214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate Spearman correlation analysis in a cross-sectional survey of 471 Italian university students. The correlation between social presence (SPRES) and e-learning satisfaction was "strongly positive (rho = 0.7911, p = 0.0000)". No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "Students with a high level of social presence had a high level of satisfaction with e-learning, and the correlation was strongly positive (rho = 0.7911, p = 0.0000)."
