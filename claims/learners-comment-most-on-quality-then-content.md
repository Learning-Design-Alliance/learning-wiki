# Learners commenting on an online curriculum comment most on overall module quality (32.7% of comments), followed by module content (16.0%), with few comments on questions, length, or website function

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Thematic analysis of 3000 randomly sampled free-text comments identified five themes, dominated by overall module quality (32.7%) and content (16.0%). [→ Sisson SD 2012](#sisson-sd-2012)

## Evidence

### Sisson SD 2012

Sisson SD, Rastegar DA, Hughes MT, Bertram AK, Yeh HC. (2012). Learner feedback and educational outcomes with an internet-based ambulatory curriculum: a qualitative and quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-55

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative coding of 1000 randomly selected comments from each of academic years 2005/06–2007/08 by three reviewers requiring unanimity; the analysis found "the overall quality of the module (mentioned in 32.7 % of comments)" dominated, followed by content at 16.0%.

> "Thematic analysis of learner feedback identified five themes of learner comments: the overall quality of the module (mentioned in 32.7 % of comments), the content of the module (16.0 % of comments), the questions contained in the module (4.6 % of comments), the length of the module (0.7 % of comments), and website function (2.4 % of comments)"
