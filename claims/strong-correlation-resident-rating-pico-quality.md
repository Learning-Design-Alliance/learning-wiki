## Evidence

### Kang Angela J. 2019

Kang Angela J., Gielissen Katherine, Windish Donna. (2019). Using an Entrustable Professional Activity to Assess Consultation Requests Called on an Internal Medicine Teaching Service. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10854

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

From the same 26 paired observations on the inpatient teaching services, Spearman and Pearson correlations compared instrument points; the article reports "a strong positive correlation was found (r = .65)" between resident opinion and PICO question quality.

> "Furthermore, a strong positive correlation was found (r = .65) when comparing the resident opinion of the intern's ability in calling a consult to how well the clinical question asked of the consultant used the PICO framework."
