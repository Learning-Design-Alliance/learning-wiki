### Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau 2024

Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau, Benjamin J. Dringoli, Jack C. Sankey, Thomas Brunner, and Kenneth Ragan, April Colosimo, Armin Yazdani, Janette Barrington, and Marcy Slapcoff. (2024). Assessing Students' Understanding of Uncertainty in Undergraduate Physics Laboratory Courses at a Major Canadian University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal observational analysis of CDPA scores across McGill physics laboratory courses from 2019-2023 (N=2023 responses), using Kruskal-Wallis tests with Dunn's post hoc comparisons. The authors report "statistically significant jumps" between PHYS 101 and PHYS 257, attributed to students in PHYS 257 being more equipped to deal with uncertainties.

> "We notice statistically significant jumps in the CDPA scores when comparing the scores obtained during the very first mechanics course that contains a lab component, PHYS 101, to a more advanced laboratory, PHYS 257."
