# Posttest TOSLS scores were significantly higher than pretest scores in the project-based, traditional, and biology majors classes at the public research university, but not at the private research university or the midsized state college

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired-sample t tests showed significant pre-to-post gains in three classes at the public research university, with learning gains large for project-based nonmajors, approaching medium for traditional nonmajors, and small for biology majors. [→ Gormally C. 2012](#gormally-c-2012)
`q3 i?` No significant pre-to-post differences were found at the private research university or the midsized state college; effect sizes were negligible for these classes. [→ Gormally C. 2012](#gormally-c-2012)

## Evidence

### Gormally C. 2012

Gormally C., Brickman P., Lutz M. (2012). Developing a Test of Scientific Literacy Skills (TOSLS): Measuring Undergraduates' Evaluation of Scientific Information and Arguments. CBE—Life Sciences Education 11(4):364–377. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-03-0026

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Paired-sample t tests with Cohen's d effect sizes on pre/post TOSLS scores in five courses across three institution types (Table 5). The article reports learning gains were "large in magnitude for the project-based nonmajors class, approaching medium in magnitude for the traditional nonmajors class, and small in magnitude for the biology majors class," and that "There were no significant difference between pre- and posttest scores at the private research university and the midsized state college."

> "Results indicated that posttest scores were significantly higher than pretest scores for the three classes (i.e., project-based, traditional, and biology majors) at the public research university, according to results from paired-sample t tests."
