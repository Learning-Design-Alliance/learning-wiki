## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Karns Christina M. 2015

Karns Christina M., Isbell Elif, Giuliano Ryan J., Neville Helen J. (2015). Auditory attention in childhood and adolescence: An event-related potential study of spatial selective attention to one of two simultaneous stories. Developmental cognitive neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.03.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comprehension questions after each story pair (two alternatives, 12 questions minimum criterion of 58%) showed 3–5-year-olds at 80% correct, "F(4,80) = 19.01, p < .001", while 10-year-olds (99%), 13-year-olds (95%), 16-year-olds (97%), and adults (95%) did not differ significantly (F(3,61) = 1.21, p =.32).

> "A one-way ANOVA was performed to test for age differences in comprehension question accuracy, revealing lower accuracy in 3–5-year-olds (Percent correct = 80%, SD = 11; F(4,80) = 19.01, p < .001), but no significant differences in accuracy between the remaining four age groups"
