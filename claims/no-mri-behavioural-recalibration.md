## Evidence

### Romanovska 2022

Romanovska, L., Janssen, R. & Bonte, M. (2022). Longitudinal changes in cortical responses to letter-speech sound stimuli in 8–11 year-old children. npj Science of Learning 7:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00118-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioural analysis of auditory-only post-test responses in the same 18-child longitudinal fMRI sample. All children responded differently to post-test sounds after "aba" vs "ada" exposure, but the omnibus RM ANCOVA found no significant recalibration effect; equivalence was not tested.

> "a 3 (session) × 2 (“aba” / “ada” exposure block) × 3 (post-test sounds) RM ANCOVA did not show signi ﬁcant main- or interaction effects (all F ≤ 2.19), indicating a lack of a signi ﬁcant behavioural recalibration effect across participants."
