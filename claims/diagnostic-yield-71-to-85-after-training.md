### Anschuetz Lukas 2025

Anschuetz Lukas, Tschopp Samuel, Trippel Mafalda, Huwendiek Sören, Trepp Roman, Caversaccio Marco, von Werdt Moritz, Borner Urs. (2025). A Novel Training Model to Improve Diagnostic Accuracy of Cervical Fine‐Needle Aspiration. Head & Neck. https://doi.org/10.1002/hed.28039

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective comparison of all US-FNAs performed by the participants 12 months before and 6 months after the course, assessed by the same cytopathologist and two independent raters, with purely cystic lesions excluded. The pre/post design is not a controlled comparison; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The ratio of diagnostic to all punctures improved from 71% (SD 18%) before the training to 85% (SD 13%, p = 0.04) after the training, as illustrated in Figure 4."
