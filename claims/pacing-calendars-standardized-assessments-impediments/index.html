Price 2026¶

Price, H., & Fitz, J. (2026). Cultivating math mindsets: Teaching practices that enable equitable classroom learning. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/810.406

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

From interviews with the study's 10 teachers about district-level supports and constraints. The report states teachers viewed "pacing calendars and standardized assessments" as impediments when these structures pressured rushing through material with little regard for student mastery.

"Teachers viewed some district-level structures, such as pacing calendars and standardized assessments, as impediments to establishing positive classroom learning conditions when these structures pressured them to rush through material with little regard for student mastery."
