# Students who analyzed music with the LLM-based teachable agent Chat Melody achieved significantly higher adjusted post-test scores than students who analyzed independently

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an online experiment, the experimental group using the teachable agent scored significantly higher on the music theory post-test than the control group (ANCOVA F = 19.98, p < .001), with adjusted means of 59.65 versus 46.94. [→ Lingxi Jin 2025](#lingxi-jin-2025)

## Evidence

### Lingxi Jin 2025

Lingxi Jin, Baicheng Lin, Mengze Hong, Kun Zhang, and Hyo-Jeong So. (2025). Exploring the Impact of an LLM-Powered Teachable Agent on Learning Gains and Cognitive Load in Music Education. Augmented Educators and AI (CHI 2025 Workshop). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.00636](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.00636)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Online experiment with 28 Chinese university students; the experimental group (n = 16) engaged with the LLM-based TA during a 60-minute analysis task while the control group (final n = 12) worked independently. ANCOVA on post-test scores with pre-test as covariate showed "a statistically significant difference between the groups (𝐹 = 19.98,𝑝 < .001)".

> "The adjusted mean post-test scores were 59.65 (𝑆𝐸 = 1.69) for the experimental group and 46.94 (𝑆𝐸 = 2.00) for the control group. The ANCOVA results indicated a statistically significant difference between the groups (𝐹 = 19.98,𝑝 < .001)."
