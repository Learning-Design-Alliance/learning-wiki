## Evidence

### Palmer Edward 2014

Palmer Edward, Devitt Peter. (2014). The assessment of a structured online formative assessment program: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA over two consecutive cohorts (129 Year 1, 130 Year 2) with improvement score as outcome and year and Group as predictors. The article reports "a significant difference in improvement scores between students in the two years"; no effect size is printed.

> "The ANOVA model showed that after adjusting for group there was a significant difference in improvement scores between students in the two years (mean improvement 8.8 marks (19%) vs. 17.9 marks (38.5%), p < 0.0001)."
