## Evidence

### L. M. Rebull 2018

L. M. Rebull, D. A. French, W. Laurence, T. Roberts, G. K. Squires, M. T. Fitzgerald, and V. Gorjian. (2018). Major Outcomes of an Authentic Astronomy Research Experience Professional Development Program: An Analysis of 8 Years of Data from NITARP. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Self-report survey analysis of 74 NITARP educators (2010-2017 classes) at up to four waypoints, coded into four bins. The paper reports "three-quarters (74% ±13%, assum- ing Poisson statistics)" report some or major change; 47% report some change and 27% major change.

> "In summary, then, three-quarters (74% ±13%, assum- ing Poisson statistics) of the educators report either ma- jor or some change in their understanding of the nature of science as a result of their experience."
