Evidence¶

Azzurra Ruggeri 2022¶

Azzurra Ruggeri. (2022). An Introduction to Ecological Active Learning. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/09637214221112114

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Study by Ruggeri, Swaboda, et al. (2019): 3- and 4-year-olds searched for an egg-shaped shaker hidden in one of four small boxes within two large boxes, having learned either a uniform or skewed hiding distribution. Shaking let children 'hear which large box contained the small box with the egg without risking opening the wrong large box.' No effect size printed.

"Compared with children in the skewed condition, who had a strong intuition as to where the egg would be hidden, children in the uniform condition were more likely to shake a large box first."
