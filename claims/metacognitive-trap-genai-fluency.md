### Daiki Nakamura 2026

Daiki Nakamura. (2026). Augmentation or Erosion? Regulating Cognitive Offloading in Generative AI-Supported STEM Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10224-6

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the review's self-regulation section, building on the monitoring-feeds-control architecture and ease-of-processing cues in judgments of learning. The review cites accumulating empirical signatures including "metacognitive laziness" and degraded performance self-assessment, but the chain itself is argued, not tested here.

> "The fluency, coherence, and confident tone of its outputs produce a powerful sense of cognitive ease, and ease is readily misread as comprehension: a learner who watches a well-formed solution unfold can come away believing they could have produced it themselves."
