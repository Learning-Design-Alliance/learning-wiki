`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square analysis of LLM accuracy across the five DUS oral pathology topics (Table 6). The article reports a significant difference "only in the “Mucosal and Tongue Diseases” category (p < 0.001)", with ChatGPT o1 perfect and Copilot at 19 correct, 11 incorrect. Other topics showed no significant differences. No effect size is printed.

> "Among the models, a statistically significant difference was detected only in the “Mucosal and Tongue Diseases” category (p < 0.001). In this category, the best performing model was ChatGPT o1, which answered all questions correctly, while the worst performance was observed in the Copilot model (19 correct, 11 incorrect)."
