## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The real data analysis indicates that with the restricted estimation procedure, the parsimonious DINA model can produce reasonable and good classification results under an attribute hierarchy. [→ Tu 2019](#tu-2019)

## Evidence

### Tu 2019

Tu, D., Wang, S., Cai, Y., Douglas, J., & Chang, H.-H. (2019). Cognitive Diagnostic Models With Attribute Hierarchies: Model Estimation With a Restricted Q-Matrix Design. Applied Psychological Measurement, 43(4), 255-271. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146621618765721

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the real data analysis of 769 undergraduates' responses to 15 syllogistic reasoning items, in which DINA with restricted estimation produced the lowest impermissible attribute rate (40.7%) among the six models under unrestricted estimation and 0% under restriction. This is a type (e) interpretation the article prints in its conclusion.

> "Especially when using the restricted estimation procedure, the parsimonious model, such as DINA, can also produce the reasonable and good classification results under attribute hierarchy, as indicated by the real data analysis."
