## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Han Jiang 2026

Han Jiang, Stuart J. Johnstone, Yingxin Li, Jinjin Lu & Bo-Bei Mao. (2026). Neurocognitive Academic Training to Improve Symptoms Executive Functions and Chinese Handwriting in Children with ADHD. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00415-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RCT of 56 children with ADHD (30 intervention, 26 control) aged 5-9; parent ADHD-RS ratings at pre- and post-intervention. The interaction for parent-rated inattention was significant, and the intervention group differed from controls at post-test (p < 0.01), with η²p = 0.46 for the "significant interaction".

> "Inattention showed a significant Time main effect (F(1,54) = 58.03, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.52), non-significant Group main effect (F(1,54) = .41, p = 0.535, η²p = 0.01), and significant interaction (F(1,54) = 46.75, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.46)."
