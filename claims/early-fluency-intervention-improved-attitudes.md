# An early-semester presentation on fluency and feeling of learning improved students' attitudes toward active learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2`–`r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a 20-min early-semester presentation on fluency, feeling of learning, and actual learning, over 65% of students reported their feelings about active learning's effectiveness significantly improved over the semester. [→ Deslauriers L. 2019](#deslauriers-l-2019)
`q2 i?` 75% of students reported the beginning-of-semester intervention helped them feel more favorably toward active learning during lectures. [→ Deslauriers L. 2019 (2)](#deslauriers-l-2019-2)
