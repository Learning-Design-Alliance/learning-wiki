The review reports, citing Faust, Ashcraft, & Fleck (1996), that "high-math-anxious participants often sacrifice accuracy for speed, especially as problems become more difficult", which the authors interpreted as an avoidance-like effort to finish quickly.

> "Our results show that high-math-anxious participants often sacrifice accuracy for speed, especially as problems become more difficult, which we interpreted as an avoidance-like effort to finish the testing session as quickly as possible (Faust et al., 1996)"
