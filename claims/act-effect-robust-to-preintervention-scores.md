## Evidence

### Rozek 2016

Rozek, Svoboda, Harackiewicz, Hulleman, & Hyde. (2016). Utility-value intervention with parents increases students' STEM preparation and career pursuit. PNAS. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1607386114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Supplemental analysis of the RCT using 10th-grade WKCE scores collected from school records as a preintervention covariate. Equivalence was not tested; the non-significant group difference is reported as such.

> "we collected and examined high-school state standardized mathematics and science test scores for students from before the intervention [10th grade Wisconsin Knowledge and Concepts Examination (WKCE) scores] and found no significant difference between intervention and control groups on students 'preintervention test scores ( z = −0.29, β = −0.03, P = 0.77). Moreover, even when controlling for these preintervention test scores, the positive effect of the intervention on mathematics and science ACT score was significant ( z = 3.61, β = 0.20, P < 0.001)."
