The review reports the Action Concept Inventory's four most common misconceptions, including that "The number of paths available for an object to move from point A to point B increases as mass decreases" and that macroscopic objects always take the stationary path. Directly addressing misconceptions is noted as most effective, but further work is needed to confirm this for the Many Paths Approach.
