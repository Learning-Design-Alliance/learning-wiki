### Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez 2023 (2)

Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez. (2023). ChatGPT in the Classroom: Boon or Bane for Physics Students' Academic Performance?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey question on perceived accuracy of ChatGPT's information about the studied physics concepts; 41.5 % deemed it accurate, 34.1 % neutral, and 14.6 % considered the information inaccurate.

> "Additionally, 41.5 % of the survey participants deem the information to be accurate, while 34.1 % hold a neutral stance on its accuracy. Conversely, 14.6 % of respondents consider the information provided by ChatGPT to be inaccurate."
