Frank V. Kowalski and Susan E. Kowalski. (2014). Embedded Formative Assessment in the Undergraduate Engineering Classroom. [doi:10.1109/tale.2014.7062618](https://doi.org/10.1109/tale.2014.7062618)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Authors' synthesis of the engineering education and formative assessment literature, citing prior work. They note that in much supporting research "the instructor response has not been well documented" and that even videotaped, trained teachers did not consistently respond appropriately to student feedback.

> "instructor response has been described as the “linchpin” that links together all the parts of the formative assessment process"
