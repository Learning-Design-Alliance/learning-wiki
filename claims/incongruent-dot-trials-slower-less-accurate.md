## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children were significantly less accurate and slower on incongruent than congruent dot comparison trials, and correct incongruent responses were slower than incorrect ones. [→ Gilmore C 2013](#gilmore-c-2013)

## Evidence

### Gilmore C 2013

Gilmore C, Attridge N, Clayton S, Cragg L, Johnson S, Marlow N, Simms V, Inglis M. (2013). Individual Differences in Inhibitory Control, Not Non-Verbal Number Acuity, Correlate with Mathematics Achievement. PLoS ONE 8(6): e67374. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0067374

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 1 accuracy and response-time analysis. Correct congruent responses averaged 1067ms versus 1413ms on incongruent trials (t(79) = 3.54, p = .001), which the authors read as evidence that "correctly solving an incongruent trial necessarily requires an additional processing step".

> "children were significantly less accurate on the incongruent trials compared to the congruent trials ( t(79) = 10.0, p < .001)"
