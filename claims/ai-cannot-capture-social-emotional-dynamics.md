# Both students and teachers believe AI cannot capture social dynamics and emotional feelings, but only teachers additionally feared AI use would reduce interpersonal interactions in class

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students and teachers both emphasized social and emotional aspects of learning that AI cannot capture, such as personal connections when grouping students. [→ social-shared](#social-shared)
`q2 i?` Teachers, but not students, expressed concern that AI and digital device use would lead to less social interaction in the classroom, e.g., discomfort with virtual hand raises. [→ social-teacher-only](#social-teacher-only)
