## Evidence

### Wang Xiaoxia 2024

Wang Xiaoxia, Che Xuan, Tang Xuedong, Xu Zhengfen. (2024). Application of combined teaching method of case-based-learning and clinical pathway in practical gynecological teaching. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.17813

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Postinternship questionnaire comparison of the two randomized groups (n = 104 vs n = 102). Table 3 prints the enthusiasm item at "89.42%" versus 76.47% with P = 0.013; independent-learning item significant at P < 0.001.

> "In total, 88.46% of students in the experimental group believed that improved independent learning ability after the internship; this proportion was 65.68% in the control group, and the difference was statistically significant (P < 0.001)."
