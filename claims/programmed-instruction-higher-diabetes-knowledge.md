Zhang Ying, Zhang Yixuan, Wang Xiaofang, Luo Baoping. (2026). Skinner's programmed instruction improves diabetes knowledge and skills of undergraduate nursing students: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09031-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomised controlled trial (n = 80, 40 per group), post-intervention knowledge was measured with a 25-item questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.85). The total post-teaching score was 91.25 ± 5.56 vs 84.45 ± 5.68, t = -5.409, P < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "At discharge, the experimental group achieved higher total scores and higher scores across all five knowledge domains (diabetes diagnosis, medication, dietary therapy, exercise therapy, and complications) than the control group. The differences were statistically significant (P < 0.05)"
