Automating Constructive Assessment with Large Language Models: Toward Scalable and Repeated Evaluation of Practical Competence. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25790 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.25790](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.25790)

Experiment 3: 80 respondents rated blinded human and ChatGPT feedback on 5-point Likert scales for persuasiveness and usefulness; 74 participants with no missing data were analyzed, and Wilcoxon signed-rank tests found no significant differences.

> "The average persuasiveness scores for Q1–Q3 were high (3.67–4.05) for both human and ChatGPT feedback, with no significant difference according to the Wilcoxon signed-rank test."
