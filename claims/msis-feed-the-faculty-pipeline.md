## Evidence

### Jinann Bitar 2022

Jinann Bitar, Gabriel Montague, Lauren Ilano. (2022). Faculty Diversity and Student Success Go Hand in Hand, So Why Are University Faculties So White? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED626440

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ed Trust analysis of IPEDS 2005-2020 faculty data identifying the top 10 institutions by percentage point change in Black and Latino faculty share. The report found "5 of the top 10 institutions with the highest proportional growth in Black faculty were Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs) or predominantly Black institutions (PBIs)". No effect size is printed.

> "5 of the top 10 institutions with the highest proportional growth in Black faculty were Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs) or predominantly Black institutions (PBIs) and 8 of the top 10 institutions with the highest proportional growth in Latino faculty were Hispanic-serving institutions (HSIs)."
