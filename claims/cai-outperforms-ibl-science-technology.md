# Students taught with computer-assisted instruction significantly outperformed students taught with inquiry-based learning in Science and Technology

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After controlling for prior knowledge and socio-cognitive factors, students receiving CAI scored significantly higher than students receiving IBL (β = 1.38 points out of 20, p = .001). [→ Chevalère Johann 2021](#chevalere-johann-2021)

## Evidence

### Chevalère Johann 2021

Chevalère Johann, Cazenave Loreleï, Berthon Mickaël, Martinez Ruben, Mazenod Vincent, Borion Marie-Claude, Pailler Delphine, Rocher Nicolas, Cadet Rémi, Lenne Catherine, Maïonchi-Pino Norbert, Huguet Pascal. (2021). Computer-assisted instruction versus inquiry-based learning: The importance of working memory capacity. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0259664

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multilevel random intercept modelling of 509 middle-school students in 48 classes in 11 schools, taught identical topics for four to ten weeks. The article reports the significant main effect of instructional method (β = 1.38, p = .001, 95% CI [.79; 1.97]), roughly an improvement from the 50th to the 68th percentile.

> "The results revealed that students who received CAI significantly outperformed students who received IBL in Science and Technology by 1.38 points (out to 20)."
