`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of teacher-recorded grades (scale 1–10) and subject satisfaction ratings for 457 Mexican high school students. PE had the highest grade mean (M = 8.82) and MATH the lowest (M = 7.33); satisfaction means were PE 3.94, GH 3.28, SP 3.08, MATH 2.73, ENG 2.67. These are descriptive means with no significance test.

> "The grades’ mean analysis reveals that PE is the subject with the highest mean (M = 8.82), and MATH the lowest (M = 7.33)."
