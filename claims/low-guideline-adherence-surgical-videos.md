## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across the 25 most-viewed robotic ventral rectopexy videos, median conformity was 13.5% (IQR: 8.1) with the 37-item LAP-VEGaS guidelines and 16.7% (IQR: 13.9) with the 36-item consensus guidelines for reporting robotic surgery videos. [→ Marotta A 2025](#marotta-a-2025)

### Marotta A 2025

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Guideline-conformity assessment of the 25 videos against the 37 LAP-VEGaS items and the 36 consensus reporting items, evaluated by an independent examiner; the consensus guidelines' "median conformity rate was 16.7% (IQR: 13.9)", with maximum conformity 21.6% and 22.2% respectively.

> "Adherence to the LAP-VEGaS guidelines was generally low, with a median conformity rate of 13.5% (IQR: 8.1)."
