## Evidence

### Shirazi 2019

Shirazi, F., & Heidari, S. (2019). The relationship between critical thinking skills and learning styles and academic achievement of nursing students. The Journal of Nursing Research, 27(4), e38. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000307

Cross-sectional survey; GPA compared across demographic variables. The article reports a significant educational-level relationship (p = .01) and non-significant relationships with age and marital status; no effect sizes printed.

> "Finally, a significant relationship was found between academic achievement and educational level, which meant that senior students had the highest level of academic achievement (p = .01). However, academic achievement had no significant relationship with other variables such as age and marital status"
