## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs found higher satisfactory teaching-effect rates for CBL versus LBL (RR=1.38, 95% CI 1.15 to 1.65, p=0.01). [→ Dong Haitao 2022](#dong-haitao-2022)

## Evidence

### Dong Haitao 2022

Dong Haitao, Guo Chunlan, Zhou Lian, Zhao Jizhi, Wu Xiaomin, Zhang Xinyuan, Zhang Xin. (2022). Effectiveness of case-based learning in Chinese dental education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-048497

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of dichotomous teaching-satisfaction data from 8 RCTs (340 CBL and 340 LBL students), random-effects model. The pooled effect size RR=1.38 (95% CI 1.15 to 1.65) with heterogeneity I2=68%. Teaching satisfaction was usually evaluated by a Likert scale in the included studies.

> "The meta-analysis of satisfactory teaching effect on the CBL teaching model found higher satisfactory rates compared with that of the LBL teaching model (RR: 1.38; 95% CI: 1.15 to 1.65; p=0.01)."
