## Evidence

### Laukkanen L 2022

Laukkanen L, Suhonen R, Löyttyniemi E, Leino-Kilpi H. (2022). The usability, feasibility and fidelity of the Ethics Quarter e-learning intervention for nurse managers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03241-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 95 Finnish nurse managers after the EQ intervention within an RCT intervention group. The mean SUS sum score was 85.40 (SD 14.27, range 42.50–100.00), described as "good" usability.

> "A system usability scale (SUS) assessed the overall usability of EQ as good (a mean SUS score of 85.40 out of 100)."
