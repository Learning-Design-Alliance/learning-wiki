Evidence¶

Afrouz Aeineh 2014¶

Afrouz Aeineh, Saeed Moeeni, Hamideh Merati. (2014). The Effect of Learner-generated Illustrations on the Immediate And Delayed Recall of English Idioms. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.3p.28

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on the delayed posttest given after a 2-week interval in the quasi-experiment with 40 female EFL learners; the drawing group (M=16.00, SD=2.02) was "significantly better than the control group" (M=14.55, SD=1.66; t=2.47, df=38, Sig.=.018).

"The result indicated that the experimental group (M=16, SD=2.02) was significantly better than the control group (M=14.55, SD=1.66; P=.01)."
