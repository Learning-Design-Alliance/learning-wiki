### Lin Chia-Shu 2023

Lin Chia-Shu, Yang Cheng-Chieh. (2023). Evaluation of a digital game for teaching behavioral aspects of clinical communication in dentistry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04040-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Non-randomized pre/post study of 42 preclinical dental students who played the 16-scene CDPC game online; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction found "a significant increase in motivation and self-efficacy in learning after playing the CDPC game".

> "In the Preclinical group, participants showed a significant increase in motivation and self-efficacy in learning after playing the CDPC game (p < 0.05, adjusted of multiple comparison)."
