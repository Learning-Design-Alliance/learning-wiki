## Evidence

### Zhu K 2026

Zhu K, Ma Y, Zhang J and Liu J (2026). Perceived AI empowerment and threat in relation to university students' dependence on GenAI: the indirect role of trust in AI. Front. Psychol. 17:1940317. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1940317

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM among 360 GenAI-experienced Chinese university students; standardized STDYX path from perceived AI empowerment to trust in AI was positive, "β=0.576,95%CI[0.493, 0.653]", excluding zero.
