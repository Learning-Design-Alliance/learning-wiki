## Evidence

### Stefana Broadbent 2026

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey attitude items (n=202). Trust item: 27% strongly disagree and 36% somewhat disagree that they trust what GenAI generates; the editing item shows 42% most of the time and 44% always changing outputs.

> "the level of trust in the outputs is very low as 65% of the responsdents indicate that they do not trust the outputs. And 85% of respondents indicate that they systematically modify what GenAI produces as output to suit their needs."

### Stefana Broadbent 2026 (2)

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discouragement item (n=219 responses). The inaccuracy reason was rated 'a lot' by 36.9% and 'a great deal' by 33.8% of respondents, the highest-rated deterrent in the table.

> "70% of the respondents are highly concerned about inaccuracy."
