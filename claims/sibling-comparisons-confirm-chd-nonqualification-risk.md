### Ekmark Sergel S 2026

Ekmark Sergel S, Lundberg M, Magnusson C, Ådén U, Bergman G, Siljehav V. (2026). Academic Achievements in Adolescents With Congenital Heart Disease: A Total Population‐Based Cohort Study. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.126.049927

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Nested sibling sensitivity analysis in 254,670 children from families with at least one sibling not qualified for USS, using conditional logistic regression matched on family identification number and adjusted for nonshared confounders. The Results state sibling comparisons "revealed the same risk of not being qualified for USS across the complexities of CHD".

> "Sibling comparisons revealed the same risk of not being qualified for USS across the complexities of CHD, as when compared with the general population, after excluding MCA."
