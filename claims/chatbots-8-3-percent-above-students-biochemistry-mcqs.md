## Evidence

### Bolgova O 2025

Bolgova O, Shypilova I, Mavrych V. (2025). Large Language Models in Biochemistry Education: Comparative Evaluation of Performance. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/67244

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of chatbot accuracy against medical students' results on the same 200 MCQs from a medical biochemistry course exam database, analyzed with basic statistics in Statistica. The study reports the chatbot mean as "8.3% (P=.02) above the students’ average", and describes the random-guessing benchmark in the same sentence.

> "This result was 8.3% (P=.02) above the students’ average (mean 72.8%, SD 12.7%) and almost 4 times better than randomly generated responses (mean 22%, SD 2.9%) for the same questions."
