Mehrotra 2021¶

Mehrotra, S., Morgan, I., & Socol, A. (2021). #Teachers: Getting Latino Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617546

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of the U.S. Department of Education's 2017-18 Civil Rights Data Collection comparing schools serving the most and fewest Latino students. In Rhode Island the report finds "18% of teachers are novice teachers" in high-Latino schools versus 6% in low-Latino schools, a threefold difference; New York and Connecticut show about twice the percentage.

"In Rhode Island, for example, in schools serving the greatest percentages of Latino students, 18% of teachers are novice teachers. In schools serving the smallest percentages of Latino students, 6% of the teachers are novice teachers."
