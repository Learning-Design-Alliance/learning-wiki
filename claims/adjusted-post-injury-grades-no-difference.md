## Evidence

### Russell Kelly 2019

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression with manual purposeful backward elimination on report-card data, adjusting for pre-injury grades, found "no significant difference in post-injury grades" between injury groups (-0.1%; 95% CI: -2.1, 1.8); results were similar under difference-in-differences regression.

> "Accounting for pre-injury grades, there was no significant difference in post-injury grades among students with a SRC versus SRF (-0.1%; 95% CI: -2.1, 1.8)."

### Russell Kelly 2019 (2)

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?`

The core-subject regression, adjusted for pre-injury core grades, found "no significant difference in post-injury core subject grades" between SRC and SRF students (-0.4%; 95% CI: -3.0, 2.2), with no evidence of effect modification by age, sex, previous concussion or school type.

> "After controlling for pre-injury core subject grades, there was no significant difference in post-injury core subject grades among students with a SRC versus SRF (-0.4%; 95% CI: -3.0, 2.2)."
