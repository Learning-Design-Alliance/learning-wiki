## Evidence

### Hart 2008

Hart, H. M., Sporte, S. E., Ponisciak, S. M., Stevens, W. D., & Cambronne, A. (2008). Teacher and Principal Leadership in Chicago: Ongoing Analyses of Preparation Programs. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-and-principal-leadership-chicago-ongoing-analyses-preparation-programs

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Analysis of CCSR 2007 principal survey and CPS personnel records comparing Fund-supported principals (LAUNCH, NLNS, UIC) to other principals on age and tenure. Tenure differences statistically significant (p<0.01); average tenure 2.55 years elementary, 2.36 years high school for Fund-supported vs 6.14 and 4.46 for others.
