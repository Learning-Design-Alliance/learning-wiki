Narrative review attribution: the review reports Nickerson's (1988) conclusion on mixed empirical evidence for transfer of critical thinking instruction. The review states the success of transfer "appears to depend on what is being taught and how it is being taught"; no effect size is printed.

> "Generally, Nickerson finds that critical thinking education tends to be more successful when it is taught alongside academic content in another course than when it is taught as a standalone subject."
