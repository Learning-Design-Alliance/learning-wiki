## Evidence

### Mazur L 2025

Mazur L, Butler L, Mitchell C, Lashani S, Buchanan S, Fenison C, Adapa K, Tan X, An S, Ra J. (2025). Effect of Immersive Virtual Reality Teamwork Training on Safety Behaviors During Surgical Cases: Nonrandomized Intervention Versus Controlled Pilot Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/66186

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline observational measurement of 101 surgical cases from October 31, 2022, to February 21, 2023, with 83 surgeons, scored with the TENTS instrument. The overall average was "2.2 (range 2‐2.3)" and the groups were comparable before the intervention. No effect size was printed.

> "Preintervention, all safety behaviors averaged slightly above 2 (“acceptable”), with an overall average of 2.2 (range 2‐2.3). There was no significant difference between the intervention and the control group at the preintervention stage."
