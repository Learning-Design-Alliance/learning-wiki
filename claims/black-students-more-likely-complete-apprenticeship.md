`q2 · i?`

Generalized ordered logistic regression results for the apprenticeship phase among 524 LC101 participants. The article reports "being Black (OR=2.751*)" as significantly associated with increased odds of apprenticeship persistence, alongside older age and COVID-19 job/income loss.

> "being between the ages 45-69 (OR=3.655**), being Black (OR=2.751*), and losing a job or income due to COVID-19 (OR=2.708**) were significantly associated with increased odds of persistence across the apprenticeship phase"
