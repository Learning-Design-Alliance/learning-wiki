### Yizhen Huang 2023

Yizhen Huang, Eric Richter, Thilo Kleickmann, Katharina Scheiter, Dirk Richter. (2023). Body in motion, attention in focus: A virtual reality study on teachers' movement patterns and noticing. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104912

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

K-means clustering analysis of time-series movement data from 21 preservice teachers in an IVR classroom, using eight spatial point-pattern summary statistics reduced via PCA. The article reports "a large Silhouette index of 0.85 and a small S_Dbw of 0.09" as validation.

> "The K-means algorithm successfully differentiated three distinctive movement patterns. The quality of the clustering result was validated with a large Silhouette index of 0.85 and a small S_Dbw of 0.09."
