## Evidence

### Stephanie L. Day 2024

Stephanie L. Day, Jin K. Hwang, Tracy Arner, Danielle McNamara, Carol M. Connor. (2024). Choose Your Own Adventure: Interactive E-Books to Improve Word Knowledge and Comprehension Skills. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling of Dragon's Lair Word Knowledge posttest scores tested dummy-coded strategy versions against the word learning reference group; Table 2 shows the summarization coefficient (γ50 = 0.381, p = 0.335) and question generation coefficient (γ40 = -0.134, p = 0.756) were not significant.

> "The summary and question generation versions were not determined to have greater post-test outcomes compared to the word learning version of the book as was tested in the WKe-Book RCT study (Connor et al., 2019) (Table 2)."

### Stephanie L. Day 2024 (2)

Stephanie L. Day, Jin K. Hwang, Tracy Arner, Danielle McNamara, Carol M. Connor. (2024). Choose Your Own Adventure: Interactive E-Books to Improve Word Knowledge and Comprehension Skills. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

In the two-level HLM of Hurricane! Concept Knowledge posttest scores (Table 3), the question generation coefficient was γ40 = 0.474 (p = 0.338) and the summarization coefficient γ50 = 0.504 (p = 0.315), both non-significant against the general comprehension reference group.

> "Like Dragon’s Lair, the summary and question generation versions of Hurricane! were not associated with higher posttest outcomes compared to the general comprehension strategy version of the book."

### Stephanie L. Day 2024 (3)

Stephanie L. Day, Jin K. Hwang, Tracy Arner, Danielle McNamara, Carol M. Connor. (2024). Choose Your Own Adventure: Interactive E-Books to Improve Word Knowledge and Comprehension Skills. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02496)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors tested interactions between strategy version and WMG and L2M pretest scores to assess whether gains depended on baseline skills; for Hurricane! they report that they "found no significant effects for any of the three strategy versions". No effect sizes were printed.

> "However, no significant interactions were found, indicating that the effect of each strategy version did not depend on baseline skills and knowledge."
