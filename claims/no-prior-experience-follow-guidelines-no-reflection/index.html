Evidence¶

Muruvvet Demiral-Uzan 2024¶

Muruvvet Demiral-Uzan, Elizabeth Boling. (2024). Instructional design students’ design judgment development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10361-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case analysis of Britney's semester design project, based on interviews and design documents coded for reflection. The article determined she "could not employ reflection because she did not have any prior experience to base the reflection on" during formative evaluation. Similar patterns are reported for Jian, Aaron, Megan, and Jennifer.

"However, it was determined that when she was making judgments during her formative evaluation, she could not employ reflection because she did not have any prior experience to base the reflection on."
