## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The paper presents system design only, with no empirical results; profile accuracy against human experts and convergence to an accurate learner representation are untested. [→ Yizhou Zhou 2026](#yizhou-zhou-2026)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Authors' stated limitations in the paper's discussion section (§9.3). The paper reports "the system design without presenting empirical results"; formal evaluation with K-12 students measuring learning outcomes, engagement, and profile accuracy is planned.

> "Profile accuracy has not been formally evaluated against human expert assessments. The heuristic rules for self-efficacy and motivation adjustment (fixed increment/decrement steps) are coarse. We do not yet have evidence that the profile converges to an accurate representation of the learner over time."
