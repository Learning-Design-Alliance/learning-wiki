# Commercial SAT preparation courses showed no statistically significant effect on SAT scores in a national propensity-score-matched sample (Domingue & Briggs, 2009)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In a quasi-experimental propensity-score-matched comparison of 706 students from the Educational Longitudinal Survey of 2002, participation in commercial SAT preparation courses showed no statistically significant difference on SAT scores (domain average effect size 0.10, improvement index +4). [→ ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016](#actsat-test-preparation-and-coaching-programs-2016)

## Evidence

### ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Propensity score matched comparison of 353 coached and 353 uncoached students from ELS:02 data.
