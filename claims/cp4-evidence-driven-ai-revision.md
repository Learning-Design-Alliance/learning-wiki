## Evidence

### Amin Jalali 2026

Amin Jalali. (2026). AI-Decision Checkpoints for AI-Augmented Business Process Management: Framework and Educational Instantiation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06207

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative instructor review of CP4 comparative redesign dossiers in a single Master-level course instantiation, triangulated with supervision observations. Groups found the bottleneck lay in the two-person fraud-investigation queue and "reclassified the intake agent from a high-priority candidate to a medium-priority one". No effect sizes reported.

> "the email-intake agent, while technically functional, was projected to shift the bottleneck downstream rather than reduce end-to-end cycle time unless the fraud-investigation subprocess was redesigned with the help of a second AI agent equipped with RAG."
