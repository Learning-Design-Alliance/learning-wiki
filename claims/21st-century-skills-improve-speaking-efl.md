## Evidence

### Hamid Ashraf 2017

Hamid Ashraf, Fatemeh Ahmadi, Mansooreh Hosseinnia. (2017). Integrating 21st Century Skills into Teaching English: Investigating its Effect on Listening and Speaking Skills. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, Vol. 7 No. 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1173769

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on speaking post-test scores after 12 sessions of treatment; the article reports "p-value = 0.006 < α = 0.05". Descriptives: control mean 9.45 (SD 3.30), experimental mean 12.30 (SD 2.40).

> "results of the t-test indicated that there was a significant difference between the mean scores of the control and experimental groups in post-test because: p-value = 0.006 < α = 0.05."
