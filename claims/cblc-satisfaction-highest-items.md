Descriptive analysis of the 12-item, 5-point Likert Satisfaction Survey administered once immediately after the CBLC to 256 respondents. The article reports the highest means for "Relevance and Utility of Topics Discussed; 4.14 ± 0.748" and "Effectiveness of Inter-University Comparison; 3.97 ± 0.820".

> "The highest mean scores were reported for Item 3 (Relevance and Utility of Topics Discussed; 4.14 ± 0.748) and Item 4 (Effectiveness of Inter-University Comparison; 3.97 ± 0.820), indicating strong perceived value of inter-university engagement and content applicability."
