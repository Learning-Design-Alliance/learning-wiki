Keller Tamás, Szakál Péter. (2021). Not just words! Effects of a light-touch randomized encouragement intervention on students’ exam grades, self-efficacy, motivation, and test anxiety. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256960

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Period effect estimated in the preregistered multilevel random-effects model of registry grades (28,156 observations; 15,264 students) from the crossover field experiment. The article reports students “performed worse in their second exam (β2 = -0.075; p < 0.001)” and notes this coefficient has no causal interpretation.
