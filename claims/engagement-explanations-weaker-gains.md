### Henriikka Vartiainen 2025

Classroom Activities and New Classroom Apps for Enhancing Children's Understanding of Social Media Mechanisms. ACM Trans. Comput. Educ. 1, 1, Article 1 (January 2024). Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.16494](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.16494)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content analysis of the third open-ended question on how Instagram gets Jarmo to spend more time on it, coded into five categories. Incorrect answers dropped from 22.40% to 13.09%; category 2 remained the largest (40.98% to 46.07%).

> "Responses in category 4 were nearly absent in the pre-test (0.55%), but the frequency of such advanced reasoning increased in the post-test to 9.42%."
