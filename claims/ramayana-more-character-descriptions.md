## Evidence

### M. Madden 2007

M. Madden, P.W.H. Chung, C.W. Dawson. (2007). The Effect of a Computer-Based Cartooning Tool On Children's Cartoons and Written Stories. Loughborough University Institutional Repository. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/2.5/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Teacher-coded cross-sectional t-tests in the Ramayana sub-study, the only cross-sectional teacher-coded difference significant at the 0.05 level. A qualified teacher unconnected with the sub-study coded a shuffled, unnamed set of 56 stories. No significant corresponding improvement or degradation effect was found.

> "the experimental group's ﬁrst stories and second stories score more highly for the number of strong descriptions of characters or of actions characters do (control ﬁrst stories average: 2.1 descriptions, experimental ﬁrst stories average: 4.6 descriptions, p=0.03; control second stories average: 0.7 descriptions, experimental second stories average: 3.4 descriptions, p=0.01)"
