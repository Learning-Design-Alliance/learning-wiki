Narrative case study using two focus groups, field observations, and reflective notes, coded in nVivo with member checking. The four themes were relationship building, communication openness, attitudinal shifts, and enhanced learner experience.

> "Qualitative data uncovered four major themes pertaining to IDSE (Table 5). It was apparent from the intern narratives that IDSE impacted relationship-building, and their capacity for improved interaction with and reliance on other disciplines."
