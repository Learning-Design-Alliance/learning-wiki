Narrative review of prior profile studies: the article reports, citing Malmberg and Little (2007), that the challenged profile showed "the most maladaptive patterns" with low confidence in abilities and effort, extrinsic motivation, and high perceived task difficulty, reflecting signs of learned helplessness.
