## Evidence

### Cheng Meng 2025

Cheng Meng, Wang Xinle, Zhang Man, Li Sainan, Geng Cuizhi. (2025). Video based on the combination of animation and modules is effective in teaching suture technologies to novices without faculty involvement: a new surgical video course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06915-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison of self-assessment questionnaires administered before and after the course to all 60 participants (28 females, 32 males, average age 23), illustrated in Fig. 2. The article reports a "median increase in self-ratings from 1.85 to 3.84"; no effect size is printed.

> "By comparing pre-course and post-course self-evaluations, each student demonstrated a significant improvement in self-assessed competency across all 12 suturing techniques. Figure 2 illustrates the average score enhancement for these skills, revealing a median increase in self-ratings from 1.85 to 3.84."
