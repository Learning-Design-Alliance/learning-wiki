Garrone-Shufran 2022¶

Garrone-Shufran, S. (2022). "I Think You Have to Be a Fighter": Novice ESL Teachers' Descriptions of Advocacy for Emergent Bilinguals. Teacher Education Quarterly, Summer 2022. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1361502

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview data from the case study: Ally's statement, which supplied the in vivo code "I think you have to be a fighter," framed advocacy as personality-dependent; the author interprets this as suggesting advocates are born and not made.

"Ally said, "If you aren't fighting for [emergent bilinguals], then nobody is, and that I feel falls on the ESL teachers. And if you don't have the personality for it, I think that could be quite challenging.""
