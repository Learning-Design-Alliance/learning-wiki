### Steven J. Pollock and Noah D. Finkelstein 2008

Steven J. Pollock and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2008). Sustaining Educational Reforms in Introductory Physics. Physical Review: Special Topics in Physics Education Research. https://journals.aps.org/prper/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0805.0277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0805.0277)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of FMCE normalized gains across eight first-semester courses (Table 1, N matched 213–400 per term), all of which used Peer Instruction and online homework; the contrast is Tutorial versus traditional recitation. The article reports Tutorial semesters' gains (0.45–0.64) exceed traditional ones (0.25–0.42); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "we note that at CU, all courses with Tutorial experiences lead to learning gains higher than all classes that have traditional recitation experiences."
