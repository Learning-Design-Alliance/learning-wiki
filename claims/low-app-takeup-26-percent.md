Descriptive analysis of MORE@Home usage logs over the summer. Students who accessed the app read on average 4.4 books and completed 47 activities, and the average app user accessed the app over a period of 3.2 days.

> "Among participants, 71 unique students (26% of the sample) accessed the app. Access rates were similar in kindergarten (32%) and first grade (29%), and slightly lower for second graders (17%)."
