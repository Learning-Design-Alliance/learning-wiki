## Evidence

### Gibbs 2014

Gibbs, Chloe R. (2014). Experimental Evidence on Early Intervention: The Impact of Full-day Kindergarten. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Gibbs+full-day+kindergarten

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Narrative review of prior observational ECLS-K studies (Cannon et al. 2006, DeCicca 2007, Lee et al. 2006, Votruba-Drzal et al. 2008), which the article reports found significant differences at kindergarten's end that "failed to persist" over first grade. All prior studies are subject to selection-bias concerns.

> "These full-day kindergarten advantages failed to persist, however, over the first-grade year."
