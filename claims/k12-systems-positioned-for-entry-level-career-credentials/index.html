This is the article's own framing assertion in the CREATE K-12 pillar, offered without empirical data. The article states K-12 systems are "well positioned to provide the foundational competencies and skills" and that districts need supportive policy, technology and data infrastructure, credentialing models, and a connected statewide district network.
