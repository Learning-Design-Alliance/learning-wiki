## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Illustrative quick test on a single item across 60 repetitions, not a systematic experiment: with the screenshot ChatGPT answered correctly "only 10% of the time", and after replacing the picture with a text transcription "The performance drastically improved from 10% to 100% of correctness".

> "We performed this test on item 4 (Fig. 5), on which ChatGPT, when prompted with the screenshot of the task, provided the correct overall strategy and reasoning 100% of the time but answered correctly only 10% of the time."
