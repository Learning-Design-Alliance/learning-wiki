`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of three open-ended acceptability questions answered by all twelve participants after the workshop. Reported changes include learning to "patiently listen to patients' ideas", infer message connotations, and discern and respond to patients' emotions.

> "According to the responses of the study participants, the ACP-SCT program did significantly improve their communication skills. They can now use these skills when needed and are able to make a breakthrough from avoidance to a welcoming attitude"
