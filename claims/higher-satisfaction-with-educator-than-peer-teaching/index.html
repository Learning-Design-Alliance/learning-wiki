Evidence¶

Scott 2014¶

Scott, D. and Jelsma, J. (2014). The effectiveness of peer taught group sessions of physiotherapy students within the clinical setting: A quasi-experimental study. Journal of Peer Learning, 7, 105-117. http://ro.uow.edu.au/ajpl/vol7/iss1/9

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Satisfaction questionnaire administered with the test; Sign Test compared ratings of peer-led versus educator-led sessions. Median satisfaction was 4 for peer teaching and 5 for educator teaching; the article prints Z = 4.130, p < .001 and no effect size.

"When asked to rate their satisfaction with peer teaching (median 4, range 3–5) and with the teaching of the clinical educator (med ian 5, range 4–5), a significantly greater proportion of students reported higher satisfaction with the teaching of the clinical educator (100% of non-tied scores, Z = 4.130, p < .001."
