## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ghassemi 2013

Mojtaba Ghassemi. (2013). The impact of Cooperative Materials Adaptation on Listening Comprehension Performance of Iranian EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n2p45

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study of 90 Iranian EFL seniors randomly assigned to two experimental groups and one control. A One-Way ANOVA on gain scores found significant differences between the three groups, with "13% improvement on students’ listening achievement" reported.

> "Gain scores calculated show 13% improvement on students’ listening achievement. In order to see if there was any significant difference between the mean score of thr ee groups, a One-Way ANOV A was conducted on the gain score. The P value, at 2 and 87 degree of freedom was smaller than .05"
