## Evidence

### Jesus G. Cruz-Garza 2021

Jesus G. Cruz-Garza, Michael Darfler, James D. Rounds, Elita Gao, Saleh Kalantari. (2021). EEG-based Investigation of the Impact of Classroom Design on Cognitive Performance of Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.03629](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.03629)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feature-level analysis of the same EEG dataset showed significant band-power differences in centro-parietal and frontal regions when design conditions were compared with Neutral; the article reports "changes in the transfer of information from centro-parietal to frontal electrodes". Features were predominantly frequency band-power rather than PDC connectivity.

> "Connectivity analysis reinforces this finding by showing that there are changes in the transfer of information from centro-parietal to frontal electrodes in the different classroom design conditions."

### Jesus G. Cruz-Garza 2021 (2)

Jesus G. Cruz-Garza, Michael Darfler, James D. Rounds, Elita Gao, Saleh Kalantari. (2021). EEG-based Investigation of the Impact of Classroom Design on Cognitive Performance of Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.03629](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.03629)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports the discriminative features "are found mostly in the theta and alpha frequency bands (4-12 Hz)" across the 57-electrode scalp data, with band-power features reaching the significance threshold at a higher rate than connectivity features.

> "The features that drive this classification performance are found in centro-parietal and frontal electrode locations (Figure 6)."
