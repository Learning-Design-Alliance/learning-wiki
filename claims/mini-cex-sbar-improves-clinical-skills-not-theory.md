Course achievement comparison in the quasi-experimental study: clinical skills favored the intervention group while "no statistically significant difference in the total theoretical examination score" was found. Equivalence not tested.

> "the clinical skills assessment score(78.83±8.81 vs. 74.82±9.41) of the students in the intervention group were significantly higher than those of control group, and with a statistically significant difference (p <0.01). There was no statistically significant difference in the total theoretical examination score (p >0.05)."
