## Evidence

### Ibrahim Nahla Khamis 2014

Ibrahim Nahla Khamis, Banjar Shorooq, Al-Ghamdi Amal, Al-Darmasi Moroj, Khoja Abeer, Turkistani Jamela, Arif Rwan, Al-Sebyani Awatif, Musawa Al-anoud, Basfar Wijdan. (2014). Medical students' preference of problem-based learning or traditional lectures in King Abdulaziz University, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. Annals of Saudi Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5144/0256-4947.2014.128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Single-item satisfaction measure in the 460-student survey: satisfaction with PBL 3.70 [1.25] vs lectures 3.30 [1.24] (t = 4.94, P<.001). The article reports students "were more satisfied with PBL than with traditional lectures". No effect size printed.

> "Table 3 also demonstrates that students were more satisfied with PBL than with traditional lectures (P<.001)."

### Ibrahim Nahla Khamis 2014 (2)

Ibrahim Nahla Khamis, Banjar Shorooq, Al-Ghamdi Amal, Al-Darmasi Moroj, Khoja Abeer, Turkistani Jamela, Arif Rwan, Al-Sebyani Awatif, Musawa Al-anoud, Basfar Wijdan. (2014). Medical students' preference of problem-based learning or traditional lectures in King Abdulaziz University, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. Annals of Saudi Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5144/0256-4947.2014.128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Single-item future-outcome measure in the same 460-student survey: PBL 3.77 [1.23] vs lectures 3.33 [1.27] (t = 5.24, P<.001). The article reports "a highly statistical significant difference (P<.001)" favouring PBL. No effect size printed.

> "A higher mean score was given for PBL compared to traditional lectures for providing better future learning outcome, with a highly statistical significant difference (P<.001)."
