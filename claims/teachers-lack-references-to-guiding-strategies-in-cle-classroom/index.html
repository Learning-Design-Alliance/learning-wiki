Evidence¶

Vassilis Kollias 2005¶

Vassilis Kollias, Nectarios Mamalougos, Xenia Vamvakoussi, Minna Lakkala, Stella Vosniadou. (2005). Teachers' attitudes to and beliefs about web-based collaborative learning environments in the context of an international implementation. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2005.04.012

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The interview analysis found teachers did not explicitly reference special guiding strategies in the CLE classroom; the authors interpret this as indicating "a need for teachers' better understanding of the guidance of student learning" and classroom social structure.

"the lack of explicit references to special guiding strategies in the CLE classroom indicates that there is a need for teachers' better understanding of the guidance of student learning and of the social structure of the classroom when CLEs are implemented"
