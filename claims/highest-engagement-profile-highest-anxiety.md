## Evidence

### Zhou Tian 2025

Zhou Tian, Zhou Xuejun, Wang Baozhu. (2025). Latent profiles of learning engagement and anxiety in high school students: the mediating role of academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03114-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

BCH-method analysis of learning anxiety differences across the four engagement profiles, with overall chi-square 230.908***. Type III mean was 1.793 versus 1.392 for Type I; the 'differences between the other groups were significant'.

> "The mean scores of learning anxiety across the profiles were ranked as follows: III>IV>II>I. Among these, except for the lack of significant differences in learning anxiety between Type II and Type IV, the differences between the other groups were significant."
