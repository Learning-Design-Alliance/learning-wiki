### Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor 2026

Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor, Komanek Thomas, Grundmann Carla Davina, Dietrich-Kiep Franziska, Wischermann Jan, Frey Ulrich Hermann. (2026). Blended e-learning with handheld ultrasound devices improves practical competence in eFAST: a randomised controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09054-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome measured from first transducer contact until the student declared the examination complete on the high-fidelity simulator. The intervention group was faster, with rank-biserial r = −0.35 (95% CI −0.48 to −0.21), p < 0.001.

> "The examination duration was significantly shorter in the intervention group than in the control group (median [IQR]: 246 [187–383] vs. 370 [309–456] seconds; Table 4; Fig. 5A)."
