## Evidence

### Hardie J.A. 2020

Hardie J.A., Brennan P.A. (2020). Are you surgically current? Lessons from aviation for returning to non-urgent surgery following COVID-19. The British Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjoms.2020.06.012

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing D'Angelo et al., that trainees perceived a larger decline in complex technical skills than routine operations after a year of research time. Perception-based finding, no effect size printed.

> "After one-year away from surgery for research, trainees perceived a larger decline in complex technical skills, such as bowel anastomosis, than for more routine operations such as hernia repairs."

### Hardie J.A. 2020 (2)

Hardie J.A., Brennan P.A. (2020). Are you surgically current? Lessons from aviation for returning to non-urgent surgery following COVID-19. The British Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjoms.2020.06.012

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

> "Six months following laparoscopic training, instrument and tissue-handling skills deteriorated more than speed, particularly fine motor skills"
