# The TGEI subscales show adequate homogeneity and reliability, but the total sum score is not a homogeneous construct

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mokken scale analysis found all three TGEI subscales homogeneous (coefficient H above 0.3) with adequate Cronbach's alpha, but the total summed score showed low homogeneity (coefficient H 0.17). [→ Edelbring Samuel 2020](#edelbring-samuel-2020)

## Evidence

### Edelbring Samuel 2020

Edelbring Samuel, Alehagen Siw, Mörelius Evalotte, Johansson AnnaKarin, Rytterström Patrik. (2020). Should the PBL tutor be present? A cross-sectional study of group effectiveness in synchronous and asynchronous settings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02018-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Psychometric evaluation of the Swedish TGEI using Cronbach's alpha and Loevinger's coefficient H. Table 5 reports subscale coefficients H of 0.41 (cognitive), 0.38 (motivational) and 0.40 (demotivational) and alphas of 0.80, 0.79 and 0.73; the total score had H = 0.17 and alpha = 0.78.

> "All three subscales displayed adequate homogeneity (coefficient H > 0.3) when analysing each subscale separately. However, when taken together as a total summed score, the TGEI did not represent a homogeneous construct on this aggregated level"
