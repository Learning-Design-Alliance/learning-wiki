Bruno Preziosi and Erasmo Recami. (2007). Ettore Majorana's Inaugural Lecture on Theoretical Physics (Neaples, Italy; Jan.13, 1938) with comments by the editors. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.0941 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0709.0941](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0709.0941)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Majorana's own preliminary notes for his 1938 inaugural lecture, a theoretical argument rather than an empirical study. He proposes that students "will be already familiar with such tools" when the time comes to use them, so they can "proceed rapidly to derive the theory from the experimental data."

> "The only way to make life easier for those who begin today the s tudy of atomic physics, without any sacriﬁce of the historical orig in of the ideas and even of the language we use today, is to start with an ample and clear discussion of the mathematical tools that are essential to quantum mechanics."
