# A Delphi panel of five stakeholder groups endorses that all 46 palliative care competencies should be covered in the undergraduate medical curriculum

## Evidence

### Pieters Jolien 2020

Pieters Jolien, Dolmans Diana H.J.M., van den Beuken-van Everdingen Marieke H.J., Warmenhoven Franca C., Westen Judith H., Verstegen Daniëlle M.L. (2020). A National, Palliative Care Competency Framework for Undergraduate Medical Curricula. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072396

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A two-round Delphi study with five stakeholder groups (N = 82 round 1, N = 54 round 2) rated a 46-item competency list; the article reports "high levels of agreement within and between stakeholder groups" and that all 46 competencies need coverage.

> "The results showed high levels of agreement within and between stakeholder groups: All 46 competencies need to be covered in the undergraduate curriculum."
