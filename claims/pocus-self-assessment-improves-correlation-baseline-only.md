## Evidence

### Jonck Christopher 2024

Jonck Christopher, Weimer Andreas Michael, Fundel Beatrice, Heinz Wolfgang, Merkel Daniel, Fiedel Hendrik, Ille Carlotta, Kloeckner Roman, Buggenhagen Holger, Piepho Tim, Weimer Johannes. (2024). Development and evaluation of a point-of-care ultrasound curriculum for paramedics in Germany – a prospective observational study and comparison. BMC Medical Education 24:811. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05816-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subjective evaluations (Likert 1–7 transformed to percentages) of the n = 72 paramedic study group at T1 and T3. The article reports the quoted means and p-value; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "The self-assessment of personal skills was initially low but improved significantly (EvaluationT1: 29.7 ± 20.1; EvaluationT3: 70.9 ± 14.5; p < 0.01)."

### Jonck Christopher 2024 (2)

Jonck Christopher, Weimer Andreas Michael, Fundel Beatrice, Heinz Wolfgang, Merkel Daniel, Fiedel Hendrik, Ille Carlotta, Kloeckner Roman, Buggenhagen Holger, Piepho Tim, Weimer Johannes. (2024). Development and evaluation of a point-of-care ultrasound curriculum for paramedics in Germany – a prospective observational study and comparison. BMC Medical Education 24:811. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05816-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Correlational analysis across the study's measurement points. The article reports the quoted correlation at T1 and states the correlation was no longer observed at the end of the curriculum, which the authors suggest could be explained by the Dunning-Kruger effect.

> "However, the subjective and objective results only correlated strongly with each other at time point T1 (r = 0.43; p < 0.01)."
