### Hertanti Nuzul Sri 2026

Hertanti Nuzul Sri, Nguyen Nga Thi Thuy, Chi Do Thi Kim, Chuang Yeu-Hui. (2026). Psychometric evaluation of the Indonesian version of the confidence scale among primary healthcare nurses in dengue-related prevention practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09471-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Face validity pretesting with 30 PHC nurses and content validity rating by eight experts in the adaptation study; all items were rated clear and relevant, with "the S-FVI/Ave reaching 1.00" and "both the I-CVI and S-CVI/Ave achieving 1.00."

> "The pretesting phase among 30 PHC nurses demonstrated that all five items were clear and understandable, with the S-FVI/Ave reaching 1.00.
