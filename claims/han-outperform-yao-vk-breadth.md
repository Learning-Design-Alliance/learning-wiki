## Evidence

### Mark Feng Teng 2024

Mark Feng Teng, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2024). Ethnic minority multilingual young learners' longitudinal development of metacognitive knowledge and breadth of vocabulary knowledge. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09360-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on four annual PVST vocabulary-breadth measurements. The ethnic group main effect was significant: "F = 666.507, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.751"; the article states Han students significantly outperformed Yao students in VK breadth scores.

> "The ANOVA results further confirmed the significance of ethnic group as the main factor influencing VK breadth, with a substantial effect size (F = 666.507, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.751)."

### Mark Feng Teng 2024 (2)

Mark Feng Teng, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2024). Ethnic minority multilingual young learners' longitudinal development of metacognitive knowledge and breadth of vocabulary knowledge. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09360-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

The time-by-group interaction on VK breadth in the same ANOVA was highly significant: "F = 2107.641, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.905". A repeated ANOVA showed the Han VK breadth growth trend was greater than the Yao trend.

> "The interaction between time and ethnic group was highly significant (F = 2107.641, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.905), indicating that Han and Yao students exhibited different developmental patterns across the four measurements."
