Julia Ericson and Torkel Klingberg 2023¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Simulation of 1000 simulated subjects with parameters sampled from uniform distributions and Gaussian noise matched to the real data's noise level. The article reports "In 88% of the cases, the model predicted the transition day within one day".

"In 88% of the cases, the model predicted the transition day within one day of the true transition day. In 94% of cases the transitions were predicted within two days of the true transition day."
