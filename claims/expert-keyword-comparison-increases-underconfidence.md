# Comparing self-generated keywords with expert keywords increases bias, specifically underconfidence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to the optimization hypothesis, bias (underconfidence) increased from the first to the second measurement time point in the keyword+external standard group but remained unchanged in the keyword-only group. [→ Waldeyer 2021](#waldeyer-2021)

## Evidence

### Waldeyer 2021

Waldeyer, J., & Roelle, J. (2021). The keyword effect: A conceptual replication, effects on bias, and an optimization. Metacognition and Learning, 16, 37–56. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09235-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Planned contrast on bias across measurement time points in the same experiment. The keyword+external standard group's underconfidence increased from T1 to T2 while the keyword group's bias "remained unchanged"; the same contrast was significant for bias, t(105) = 2.45, p = .008 (one-tailed), ηp2 = .05.

> "However, contrary to our expectations, for the students who additionally compared their keywords with expert ones the bias (here: level of underconfidence) increased from the first to the second measurement time point, whereas it remained unchanged in the keyword group."
