Coding of the 176 Freeman et al. (2014) articles for dosage found equal dosage in 29.5%, unequal in 13.1%, and "not specified in 57.4% of the articles." Dosage of the control variable was not specified at all in 45.5% of articles.

> "When comparing the dosage between/among groups, the dosage was equal in 29.5% of the articles, was not equal in 13.1% of the articles, and was not specified in 57.4% of the articles."
