The paper's key findings state that "frequent feedback and coaching for teachers" is among practices "positively associated with charter schools’ achievement impacts," based on moderately strong evidence. No effect size is printed.

"moderately strong evidence suggests that high-dosage tutoring, frequent feedback and coaching for teachers, and policies promoting the use of data to guide teachers’ instructional practices are positively associated with charter schools’ achievement impacts."
