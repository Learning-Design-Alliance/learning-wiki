## Evidence

### Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Cronbach’s Alpha of Domain‑Specific Knowledge Tests Before and After Learning: A Meta‑Analysis of Published Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09982-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic regression with log-transformed number of items (range 3–150, median 20) showed a substantial association with Alpha (p < .001, Table 2). The relation appeared "less quadratic than we had expected" based on the Alpha formula.

> "there was a substantial relation between the number of items and Alpha, potentially with a moderate exponential or quadratic tail in the lower end of the distribution"
