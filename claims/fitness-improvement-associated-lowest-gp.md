# Improvements in cardiorespiratory fitness are longitudinally associated with improvement in an individual's worst (lowest) school-subject grade points

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a two-year observational study of junior high students, changes in cardiorespiratory fitness were positively associated with changes in the lowest GP, an association that persisted after controlling for confounders (Cohen's f2 = 0.02, a small effect). [→ Toru Ishihara 2021](#toru-ishihara-2021)

## Evidence

### Toru Ishihara 2021

Toru Ishihara, Noriteru Morita, Toshihiro Nakajima, Koji Yamatsu, Koichi Okita, Masato Sagawa and Keita Kamijo. (2021). Differential effects of changes in cardiorespiratory fitness on worst- and best- school subjects. npj Science of Learning 6:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00086-8

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pearson's correlation analyses in this two-year longitudinal study of 469 junior high school students (Table 1 reports r = 0.15*, p < 0.05 for fitness change and lowest GP change); "changes in cardiorespira- tory ﬁtness were positively associated with only changes in the lowest GP."

> "However, changes in learning time were positively associated with changes in the lowest and highest GPs, whereas changes in cardiorespira- tory ﬁtness were positively associated with only changes in the lowest GP."

### Toru Ishihara 2021 (2)

Toru Ishihara, Noriteru Morita, Toshihiro Nakajima, Koji Yamatsu, Koichi Okita, Masato Sagawa and Keita Kamijo. (2021). Differential effects of changes in cardiorespiratory fitness on worst- and best- school subjects. npj Science of Learning 6:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00086-8

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analyses with full-information maximum likelihood predicting change in lowest GP gave β = 0.15, p = 0.002, adjusted ΔR2 = 0.02; the association "remained unchanged" after controlling for household income, maternal education, sex, change in BMI, and change in learning time.

> "Multiple regression analyses indicated that these associations between changes in cardiorespiratory ﬁtness with changes in the lowest and highest GPs remained unchanged (Cohen 's f2 = 0.02, 1 −β = 0.87 and Cohen's f2 = 0.003, 1 −β = 0.22, respectively) after controlling for the household income, maternal education, sex, change in BMI, and change in learning time"
