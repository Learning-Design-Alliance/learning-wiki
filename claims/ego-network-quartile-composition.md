## Evidence

### Poquet 2021

Poquet, O., Nguyen, Q., Kovanovic, V., Brooks, C., Dawson, S., & Biotteau, A. (2021). Social Architectures of University Forums: Grade-Based Similarity in Online Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09675

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Ego-network composition analysis across three universities found that "a student interacted the most with peers in the same performance quartile and the least with peers in the opposite performance quartile", a pattern stable across institutions.

> "On average, a student interacted the most with peers in the same performance quartile and the least with peers in the opposite performance quartile."
