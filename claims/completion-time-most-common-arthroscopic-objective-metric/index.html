Evidence¶

Zhou Haixia 2022¶

Zhou Haixia, Xian Chengyao, Zhang Kai-Jun, Yang Zhouwen, Li Wei, Tian Jing. (2022). The frequency of assessment tools in arthroscopic training: a systematic review. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2022.2085317

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review of 28 primary studies on arthroscopic training assessment. Frequency analysis of objective metrics found "completion time, reported in 21 studies (75.0%)"; instrument path length appeared in nine studies (32.1%).

"A variety of objective metrics was used in the included studies. The most common measurement outcome was completion time, reported in 21 studies (75.0%)."
