## Evidence

### Laakkonen Noora 2024

Laakkonen Noora, Jarva Erika, Hammarén Mira, Kanste Outi, Kääriäinen Maria, Oikarinen Anne, Mikkonen Kristina. (2024). Digital Competence among Healthcare Leaders: A Mixed-Methods Systematic Review. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/8435248

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of cross-sectional studies in the review; leaders with prior computer training had more ICT knowledge than those without, and computer ownership and prior use influenced ICT usage.

> "According to Adatara et al. [43], nurse leaders who had had computer training prior to positioning as a nurse leader possessed more knowledge of ICT use compared to nurse leaders who had not had computer training prior to the position and computer ownership and the use of computers before positioning as a nurse leader were factors influencing the nurse leaders usage of ICT."
