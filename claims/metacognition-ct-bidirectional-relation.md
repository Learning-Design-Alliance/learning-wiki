`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the pre-post results in the discussion; the article states "this relation is bidirectional" while acknowledging the quasi-experimental design could not establish a sequence of independent factors.

> "Our data have shown that this relation is bidirectional, so that metacognition improves thinking skills and vice versa. It remains to establish a sequence of independent factors to avoid this confusion, something which the present study has aided with to be able to design future research in this area."
