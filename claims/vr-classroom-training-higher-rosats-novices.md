## Evidence

### Shakir T 2026

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a randomised crossover study of 11 novice surgical trainees allocated to self-directed learning or adjunctive interactive VR classroom training for one week per arm, assessed with R-OSATS. The VR group scored "44.80 vs. 35.33 points, p = 0.006".

> "The VR group achieved significantly higher scores compared with self-directed FRS alone (44.80 vs. 35.33 points, p = 0.006)."
