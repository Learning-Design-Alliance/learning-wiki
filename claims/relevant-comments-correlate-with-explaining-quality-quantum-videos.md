# The number of relevant content-related comments is the surface metric most strongly correlated with the explaining quality of YouTube explanatory videos on quantum entanglement and tunnelling.

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Across the total sample of 60 quantum-physics videos, the number of relevant comments correlates with explaining quality (r = 0.46, p < 0.01), the highest correlation among the surface metrics. [→ Bitzenbauer 2023](#bitzenbauer-2023)
`q2 i3` For the quantum entanglement sub-sample the correlation is higher (r = 0.59, p < 0.01), while for tunnelling it is marginal (r = 0.31, p < 0.1). [→ Bitzenbauer 2023](#bitzenbauer-2023)

## Evidence

### Bitzenbauer 2023

Bitzenbauer, P., Höfler, S., Veith, J. M., Winkler, B., Zenger, T., & Kulgemeyer, C. (2023). Exploring the relationship between surface features and explaining quality of YouTube explanatory videos. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1007/s10763-022-10351-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-022-10351-w)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of 60 YouTube videos on quantum entanglement and tunnelling, rated with a category-based measure of explaining quality. Relevant comments showed the highest correlation, "r = 0.46, p < 0.01", while time online did not differ from zero significantly.

> "The highest correlation is uncovered between the videos' explaining quality and the number of relevant comments ( r = 0.46, p < 0.01), whereas the correlation between the videos' explaining quality and their time online does not differ from 0 with statistical significance."
