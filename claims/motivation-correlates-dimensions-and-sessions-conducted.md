## Evidence

### Saguin Emeric 2024

Saguin Emeric, Salomé Julia, Favodon Benoît, Lahutte Bertrand, Gignoux-Froment Frédérique. (2024). Validation of a didactic model evaluating the usability, usefulness and acceptability of psychological first aid teaching through simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06491-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional Spearman correlation analysis of questionnaire responses from 59 French PFA-O trainers (52.68% of the national workforce), adjusted for years of trainer practice. The article reports "significant moderate or strong correlation ranging from rho = 0.34 to 0.53" and a strong correlation of rho = 0.45 between motivation and sessions per year; no standardised effect size beyond these coefficients is printed.

> "One of the main findings of our study is the correlation between the dimensions of usefulness, usability and acceptability, as defined in our introduction, and teacher motivation (significant moderate or strong correlation ranging from rho = 0.34 to 0.53), correlated in turn with the frequency of conducting training sessions (strong correlation with rho = 0.45)."
