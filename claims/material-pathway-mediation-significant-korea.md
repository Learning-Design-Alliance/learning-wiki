## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The material investment pathway (income → private educational expenditure → perceived academic performance) was statistically significant (β = .006, p < .01). [→ Kang Ran 2026](#kang-ran-2026)
`q3 i?` The relational investment pathway (income → parent–child communication → perceived academic performance) was not significant (β = .001, p = .508). [→ Kang Ran 2026 (2)](#kang-ran-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Kang Ran 2026

Kang Ran. (2026). Household income and academic performance in Korean adolescents: A longitudinal test of dual investment pathways. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0353476

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bias-corrected bootstrap indirect-effect test (5,000 resamples) in the longitudinal parallel mediation model on the KCYPS 2018 cohort (N = 2,387). The article reports the material indirect effect as "β = .006, p < .01".

> "Consequently, the material investment pathway was statistically significant (β = .006, p < .01, 95% CI [.002, .011])."

### Kang Ran 2026 (2)

Kang Ran. (2026). Household income and academic performance in Korean adolescents: A longitudinal test of dual investment pathways. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0353476

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap test of the specific indirect effect through parent–child communication in the same parallel mediation model (N = 2,387). The article reports "β = .001, p = .508"; no effect size magnitude is printed for this null pathway.

> "In contrast, the relational investment pathway was not significant: household income (Wave 1) → parent–child communication (Wave 2) → perceived academic performance (Wave 3) (β = .001, p = .508, 95% CI [–.002, .004])."
