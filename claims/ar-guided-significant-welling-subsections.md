## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The AR group scored significantly higher on defining mastoidectomy margins (3/3 vs 1/3, p = 0.0001), sharpening the sinodural angle (2/2 vs 0/2, p = 0.0002), exposing the tegmen and attic and preserving the ossicles (4/5 vs 2/5, p = 0.0001), antrum exposure and lateral semicircular canal preservation (2.7/3 vs 2/3, p = 0.0473), and thinning and preserving the external auditory canal (1.5/3 vs 1/3, p = 0.0186). No standardized effect sizes printed.
