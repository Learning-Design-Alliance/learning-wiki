# Corpus-tool instruction significantly improves EFL student teachers' production of verb-noun collocations in writing, with a medium-to-large effect

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Controlling for pre-test production, the corpus group produced significantly more correct verb-noun collocations in post-experiment writing than the control group, with a medium-to-large effect size (r = .418, adjusted r = .397). [→ Kartal and Yangineksi 2018](#kartal-and-yangineksi-2018)

## Evidence

### Kartal and Yangineksi 2018

Galip Kartal, Gonca Yangin Eksi. (2018). The Effects of Using Corpus Tools on EFL Student Teachers' Learning and Production of Verb-Noun Collocations. PASAA, Vol. 55. https://so04.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/pasaa

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on collocation production counts from mid-term (pre) and final (post) exam writings of the Writing Skills II course, with pre-test as covariate. The article reports "a medium to large effect size (r=.418, adjusted r= .397)"; Table 3 shows Group F = 40.070, p < .05.

> "there was a statistically significant difference between control and experimental groups regarding the post -test adjusted collocation production mean scores."
