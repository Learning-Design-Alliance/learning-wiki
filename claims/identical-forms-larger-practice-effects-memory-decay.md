# Practice effects are larger for identical test forms than alternate forms, and decline with retest interval for identical forms only

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Identical forms yielded a larger overall effect size (δ = .46) than alternate forms (δ = .24), with non-overlapping confidence intervals. [→ Hausknecht 2005](#hausknecht-2005)
`q3 i3` For identical measures, effect size was negatively related to time interval (β = -.51, p < .01); for alternate forms the coefficient was not significant (β = .22, p = .09). [→ Hausknecht 2005 (2)](#hausknecht-2005-2)

## Evidence

### Hausknecht 2005

Hausknecht, J. P., Halpert, J. A., Di Paolo, N. T., & Moriarty Gerrard, M. O. (2005). Retesting in Selection: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Effects for Tests of Cognitive Ability. Journal of Applied Psychology (in press). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/apl

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Subgroup moderator analysis; 38 samples used identical forms and 64 alternate forms. The article reports a corrected overall effect size of δ = .46 for identical tests versus δ = .24 for alternate forms.

> "results revealed an overall effect size for identical tests (δ = .46) that was larger than that found for alternate forms (δ = .24)."

### Hausknecht 2005 (2)

Hausknecht, J. P., Halpert, J. A., Di Paolo, N. T., & Moriarty Gerrard, M. O. (2005). Retesting in Selection: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Effects for Tests of Cognitive Ability. Journal of Applied Psychology (in press). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/apl

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

WLS regression of effect size onto log-transformed time interval run separately for identical and alternate forms, testing a memory-decay account; the printed regression coefficient is β = -.51. The alternate-form coefficient was not statistically significant.

> "For identical measures, the coefficient relating time interval to effect size was significant and in the expected direction (β = -.51, p \< .01), indicating that larger practice effects were associated with shorter time intervals."

### Hausknecht 2005 (3)

Hausknecht, J. P., Halpert, J. A., Di Paolo, N. T., & Moriarty Gerrard, M. O. (2005). Retesting in Selection: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Effects for Tests of Cognitive Ability. Journal of Applied Psychology (in press). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/apl

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

WLS regression using raw time-interval values to predict the interval at which identical- and alternate-form effects converge. The article reports the predicted value of 685 days.

> "The resulting predicted value, 685 days, suggests that the effect size for identical forms, while higher than that for alternate forms, decreases over time such that practice effects for identical forms will be similar in magnitude to those found for alternate forms after an interval of just under two years."
