# Math and language achievement are positively related to students' perceptions of the learning environment in matching domains but negatively related or unrelated across non-matching domains, supporting the GI/E model for perceptions of instructional quality and student-teacher relations.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Math achievement is positively related to perceived instructional quality in math classes (β = .446) and to relations with math teachers (β = .109), but negatively related to perceived instructional quality in language classes (β = -.097) and relations with language teachers (β = -.108). ## Evidence

### Arens 2016

Arens, A.K., & Möller, J. (2016). Dimensional comparisons in students' perceptions of the learning environment. Learning and Instruction, 42, 22–30. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.11.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent regression path model (Model 3) in a sample of N = 4926 German sixth-graders tested GI/E assumptions with achievement predicting both perception facets. The article reports "positively related to students’ perceptions of the learning environment in the same domain but negatively related to students’ perceptions in the other domain"; no standardized effect sizes beyond path coefficients are printed for the omnibus pattern.

> "Moreover, students’ achievement was found to be positively related to students’ perceptions of the learning environment in the same domain but negatively related to students’ perceptions in the other domain."
