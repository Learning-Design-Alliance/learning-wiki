### Tsuchiya Miyako 2026

Tsuchiya Miyako, Adachi Keiichiro, Kimata Akiko, Kumagai Kaori. (2026). Development of a Web-Based Experiential Learning Intervention for the Public to Reduce Cancer Stigma: Tutorial on the Application of Intervention Mapping. JMIR Cancer. https://doi.org/10.2196/71166

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive delivery-mode preferences from the public needs survey (n=1076). Booklets or leaflets led (529/1076, 49.2%), followed by internet texts and figures (460/1076, 42.8%); apps and DVDs were chosen by few participants.

> "Preferable modes of delivery were booklets or leaflets (529/1076, 49.2%), the Internet (texts and figures: 460/1076, 42.8%; text only: 297/1076, 27.6%; text and video: 155/1076, 14.4%), apps (75/1076, 7%), DVDs (46/1076, 4%), and other (24/1076, 2%)."
