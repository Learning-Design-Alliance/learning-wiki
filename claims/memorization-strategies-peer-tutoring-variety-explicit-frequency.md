Graphic 2 content analysis in the three-case comparison showed variety of memorization strategies was "higher in the peer tutoring strategy instruction group" while frequency was quite high in the explicit instruction group compared to the other groups.

> "the variety of metacognitive strategies used to perform the process of facilitation of memorization was higher in the peer tutoring strategy instruction group. The frequency of using the metacognitive strategies of the explicit strategy instruction group to perform the process of facilitation of memorization was quite high compared to the other groups."
