`q2 i?` Across both experiments, PBC, Cronbach's alpha, and CIF analysis converged on the same candidate constraints, detecting faulty, bad, and good quality constraints. [→ Gálvez 2013](#galvez-2013)

## Evidence

### Gálvez 2013

Gálvez, J., Conejo, R., & Guzmán, E. (2013). Statistical Techniques to Explore the Quality of Constraints in Constraint-Based Modeling Environments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0008-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-method comparison across both experiments (24 and 23 students respectively). Constraints C1, C8, and C17 were diagnosed as good in both experiments; C12 was detected as bad in experiment 2, with inspection revealing a possible slip rather than low knowledge as the cause of violations.

> "The main finding is that the three mechanisms give similar results taking into account the experiments in which they are applied."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

> "Results suggest that the three methods produce consistent diagnosis and may be complementary in some cases. In the experiments we have carried out they were able to detect faulty, bad and good quality constraints."
