Marsh, H. W., Ryan, R. M., Dicke, T., Pekrun, R., Guo, J., Bradshaw, E. L., Reeve, J., Lüdtke, O., Clarke, T., & Waterschoot, J. (2025). q3 · i? · associational · r2

Orthogonal-contrast GLM (C2: autonomy vs competence + relatedness) over the 64-variable nomological network from 1,950 Australian school leaders. The article reports "53 satisfy the a priori rule" and domain means of r = .07 to r = −.11.

"Across all 64 outcomes, 53 satisfy the a priori rule, so autonomy has a strong content signal in this taxonomy. The mean values for C2 at the bottom of Table 4 mirror this pattern: resources (r = .07) and positive outcomes (r = .09) favor autonomy satisfaction, whereas demands (r = −.11) and negative outcomes (r = −.03) favor autonomy frustration."
