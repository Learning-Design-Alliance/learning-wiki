## Evidence

### Rodriguez 2003

Michael C. Rodriguez. (2003). Construct Equivalence of Multiple-Choice and Constructed-Response Items: A Random Effects Synthesis of Correlations. Journal of Educational Measurement, 40(2). https://www.jstor.org/stable/1435344

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random effects synthesis of 56 correlations corrected for attenuation across 29 studies. The article reports "the mean weighted corrected correlation is 0.902" with a standard error ten times the fixed effects standard error, and a confidence interval excluding unity.

> "Using the random effects model, the mean weighted corrected correlation is 0.902 with a standard error of 0.082 (10 times as large as the fixed effects standard error). This results in a 95% confidence interval of (0.868, 0.928)."
