## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2024

Van Dusen, B., Cian, H., Nissen, J., Arellano, L., & Woods, A. (2024). Comparing the efficacy of fixed effect and MAIHDA models in predicting outcomes for intersectional social strata. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1177/00380407241254092](https://doi.org/10.1177/00380407241254092)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulation comparison of mean absolute true errors per strata (Table 4). "The largest mean absolute true error for the fixed effect model was for FG Black women (9.3 points)"; MAIHDA reduced this to 4.9 points, shifting error from 0.47 SD to 0.25 SD given a 20-point student score SD.

> "The largest mean absolute true error for the fixed effect model was for FG Black women (9.3 points), who also had the smallest mean N (3.7)."
