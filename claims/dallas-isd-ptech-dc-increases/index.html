A district case profile of Dallas ISD's Early College partnerships and P-TECH expansion to every comprehensive high school since 2016, reporting "sizable jumps for Black (9% to 30%), Latino (15% to 31%), and economically disadvantaged students (13% to 31%)."

"From 2018-19 to 2020-21, Dallas saw a massive increase in graduates with DC across all student groups, with sizable jumps for Black (9% to 30%), Latino (15% to 31%), and economically disadvantaged students (13% to 31%)."
