## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Three MSLQ motivation items (MSLQ_3 cognitive interference, MSLQ_7 self-efficacy, MSLQ_14 extrinsic goal orientation) differed significantly among high, middle, and low performance groups, while comprehensive subcategory scores did not. [→ Chen Ding-Ping 2024](#chen-ding-ping-2024)

## Evidence

### Chen Ding-Ping 2024

Chen Ding-Ping, Hour Ai-Ling, Tsao Kuo-Chien, Huang Chung-Guei, Lin Wei-Tzu, Hsu Fang-Ping. (2024). Examining the factors influencing academic performance of medical technology students in e-learning: A questionnaire survey. PLoS One 19(12): e0311528. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311528

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's post-test comparing questionnaire scores across three grade-tier groups (high n = 19, middle n = 11, low n = 7) in a survey of "37 senior medical technology students interning at the Department of Laboratory Medicine". "The comprehensive scores of 16 subcategories had no difference among these three groups."

> "the findings showed that the scores of MSLQ_3 (p = 0.042), MSLQ_7 (p = 0.005), and MSLQ_14 (p = 0.043) were significantly different among three academic performance levels"
