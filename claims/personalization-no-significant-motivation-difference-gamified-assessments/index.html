Evidence¶

Rodrigues Luiz 2023¶

Rodrigues Luiz, Palomino Paula T., Toda Armando M., Klock Ana C. T., Pessoa Marcela, Pereira Filipe D., Oliveira Elaine H. T., Oliveira David F., Cristea Alexandra I., Gasparini Isabela, Isotani Seiji. (2023). How Personalization Affects Motivation in Gamified Review Assessments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00326-x

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Confirmatory robust (20% trimmed means) mixed ANOVAs on 58 students across three institutions in a mixed factorial design (design between-subject, session within-subject) testing H1. The article reports all p-values exceeded 0.05, so "no statistically significant difference for all motivation constructs"; no effect sizes were printed.

"All p-values being larger than the 0.05 alpha level reveals no statistically significant difference for all motivation constructs."
