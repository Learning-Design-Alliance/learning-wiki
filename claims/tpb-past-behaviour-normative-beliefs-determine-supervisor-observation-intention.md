## Evidence

### de Jonge Laury P. J. W. M. 2020

de Jonge Laury P. J. W. M., Mesters Ilse, Govaerts Marjan J. B., Timmerman Angelique A., Muris Jean W. M., Kramer Anneke W. M., van der Vleuten Cees P. M. (2020). Supervisors' intention to observe clinical task performance: an exploratory study using the theory of planned behaviour during postgraduate medical training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02047-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four-step hierarchical regression analysis of questionnaire data from 82 GP supervisors (net response rate 41%). The article reports "a further statistically significant increase of 8% in the variance explained to 45%", with normative beliefs β = .27 and past behaviour β = .33 both statistically significant.

> "At step 4, entry of the additional variable 'past behaviour' resulted in a further statistically significant increase of 8% in the variance explained to 45% (R2change = 0.8, Fchange (1,66) =3.57, p = .003). Moreover, both normative beliefs (β = .27, P < 0.05) and past behaviour (β = .33, p < 0.01) had a statistically significant beta weight."

### de Jonge Laury P. J. W. M. 2020 (2)

de Jonge Laury P. J. W. M., Mesters Ilse, Govaerts Marjan J. B., Timmerman Angelique A., Muris Jean W. M., Kramer Anneke W. M., van der Vleuten Cees P. M. (2020). Supervisors' intention to observe clinical task performance: an exploratory study using the theory of planned behaviour during postgraduate medical training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02047-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Step 2 of the same hierarchical regression, entering indirect TPB measures, produced a statistically significant increase in variance explained (R2change = 0.29). "Normative beliefs" and CB factor 2 carried significant positive beta weights of .30 and .25; behavioural beliefs (β = .18) and CB factor 1 (β = .03) were not significant.

> "Normative beliefs (i.e. beliefs about the normative expectations of other people) and CB factor 2 (i.e. feelings of stress induced by practical conditions that limit the performance of DOs) had a significant positive beta weight of .30 and .25 respectively, reflecting their contribution to the predictive equation."
