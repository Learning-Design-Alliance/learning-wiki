## Evidence

### Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox 2026

Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox. (2026). Online professional development to support K‑12 teachers' computer science integration. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10640-z

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Independent t-tests compared pre- and post-survey ratings (1 = not at all confident to 5 = completely confident) from the MICRO evaluation surveys; Welch-Satterthwaite correction was used. The mean rose from "3.59 on the pre" to "4.28 on the post", with "g = 0.72".

> "the mean increased from 3.59 on the pre (n = 377, SD = .36) to 4.28 on the post (n = 341, SD = .90), which was a significant increase in teachers' confidence (t = −9.58 (715.56), p < .001). The effect size was g = 0.72, indicating a large difference in teachers' self-reported confidence."
