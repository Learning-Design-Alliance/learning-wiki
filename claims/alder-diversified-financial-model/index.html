Yun 2025¶

Yun, C., Fitz, J., Nakajima, T., & Mauerman, C. (2025). Educating for equity: Alder Graduate School's teacher residency. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/812.965

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive financial-model findings from the Financial Model section: roughly $14 million in Teacher Quality Partnership and $19 million in Supporting Effective Educator Development funding through 2022–23, with about half going to LEA subgrantees, plus TRGP grants and local control funding formula dollars for stipends. No inferential analysis is printed.

"In 2022–23, Alder was 50% funded through tuition, and it expected to reach its target of 70% to 80% tuition funding by 2026–27. The university did not aim to become fully reliant on student tuition because it intended to keep tuition rates low enough to be able to recruit and enroll candidates from diverse backgrounds."
