Ahmed Dev Ahrar, Rai Kanika, Sharma Amoldeep, Sarin Jyoti. (2024). Advancement in knowledge and skills of nursing students in operation theatre procedures with mobile based learning. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e15

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon Signed Rank test on the experimental group's (n=64) pre- and post-test OSCE scores; Table 3 reports pre-test means of 11.69, 11.84, 9.66, and 6.30 rising to the quoted post-test means, all Z-tests significant at p<0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "The Post-test scores for the skills of Scrubbing (20.52), Gowning (16.70), Gloving (13.77), and assisting in ET Intubation (10.58) were all significantly higher after the administration of mobile based learning."
