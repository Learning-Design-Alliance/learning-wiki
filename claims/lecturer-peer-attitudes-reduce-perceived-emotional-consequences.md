## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Attitudes of lecturers and peers uniquely contributed to less perceived anxiety and depression consequences, but not academic consequences, in regression analyses. [→ Bazen 2022](#bazen-2022)

## Evidence

### Bazen 2022

Bazen, Loes L.; de Bree, Elise H.; van den Boer, Madelon; de Jong, Peter F. (2022). Perceived negative consequences of dyslexia: the influence of person and environmental factors. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-022-00274-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the 123-student sample: attitudes of lecturers and peers had betas of -.293 (anxiety, p=.001) and -.219 (depression, p=.011), but -.089 (academic, p=.317); "more positive attitudes of lecturers and peers were related to less perceived negative anxiety and depression consequences."

> "However, more positive attitudes of lecturers and peers were related to less perceived negative anxiety and depression consequences."
