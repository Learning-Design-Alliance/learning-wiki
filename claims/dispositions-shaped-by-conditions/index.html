Qualitative finding from the dispositions analysis: dispositions "should not be understood only as ﬁxed characteristics"; conversely, limited trust, unclear expectations, or tightly controlled processes could constrain those same dispositions.

"Clear roles, meaningful opportunities to contribute, relational trust, and structures for shared decision-making could support partners and staﬀ in acting with greater openness, adaptability, and shared responsibility."
