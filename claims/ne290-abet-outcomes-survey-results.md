## Evidence

### Aaron J. Berliner and Jake Hecla 2021

Aaron J. Berliner and Jake Hecla. (2021). Nuclear History, Politics, and Futures from (A)toms-to(Z)oom: Design and Deployment of a Remote-Learning Special-Topics Course For Nuclear Engineering Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.11583](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.11583)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course survey (Table I) of the 11-student cohort rating how NE290 aided their agency toward the nontechnical ABET student outcomes; printed averages are O1 7.67, O2 5.67, O3 8.0. The article reports "students feel they gained agency" on O1 and O3 with no effect size.

> "show post NE290, students feel they gained agency in (O1) an ability to recognize ethical and professional re- sponsibilities in engineering situations and ( O3) an abil- ity to acquire and apply new knowledge as needed, using appropriate learning strategies."

### Aaron J. Berliner and Jake Hecla 2021 (2)

Aaron J. Berliner and Jake Hecla. (2021). Nuclear History, Politics, and Futures from (A)toms-to(Z)oom: Design and Deployment of a Remote-Learning Special-Topics Course For Nuclear Engineering Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.11583](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.11583)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same post-course survey, O2 teamwork outcome: the printed average of 5.67 was the lowest of the three, and the authors interpret that "the strategies taken to provide additional practice on engineering teams were insufficient" for an NE290-like course.

> "the feedback for (O2) suggest that the strategies taken to provide additional practice on engineering teams were insuﬃcient – providing an important path forward for educators in preparing to oﬀer an NE290-like course."
