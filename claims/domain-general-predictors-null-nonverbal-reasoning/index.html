Connections between Mathematics and Reading Development: Numerical Cognition Mediates Relations between Foundational Competencies and Later Academic Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000670

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Total effects from the final pruned path model for the three domain-general predictors; the article reports summed total effects of "0.447" for calculations, "0.201" for WPS, and "0.243" for reading, with working memory most predictive of mathematics.

"associations between these three predictors and later outcomes were stronger for calculations (summed total effects were 0.447) compared to WPS (summed total effects were 0.201) and reading (summed total effects were 0.243)."

Null finding from the final pruned mediation model: the article states "nonverbal reasoning was not significantly associated with any outcome," possibly due to differences in mediating variables and inclusion of prior WPS in the model.

"in contrast to our hypotheses, nonverbal reasoning was not significantly associated with any outcome."

Related Claims¶
