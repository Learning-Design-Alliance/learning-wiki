Independent t-tests comparing the two experimental groups and the two control groups with each other on achievement and retention test parts. All reported comparisons were not statistically significant (p>.05), which the article takes to show results did not stem from group structure or student variables.

> "the difference between the means of the first experimental group for the second and the third parts of the achievement tests ( =86.17 &  =87.36) and the second experimental group ( =86.51 &  =89.48) is n ot statistically significant (p>.05)."
