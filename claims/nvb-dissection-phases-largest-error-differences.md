## Evidence

### Mottrie A 2021

Mottrie A, Mazzone E, Wiklund P, Graefen M, Collins J.W., De Groote R, Dell’Oglio P, Puliatti S, Gallagher A.G. (2021). Objective assessment of intraoperative skills for robot‐assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP): results from the ERUS Scientific and Educational Working Groups Metrics Initiative. BJU International. https://doi.org/10.1111/bju.15311

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive phase-level analysis of the Study 2 video-scored data (Fig. 2 and Table 4): per-phase error scores were divided at the median into upper-half and lower-half subgroups. The article interprets the pattern as due to the higher complexity of neurovascular bundle dissection compared to other phases.

> "The largest performance differences in the number of errors were observed for phases VIIa and VIIb."
