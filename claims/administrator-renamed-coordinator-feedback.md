# Participant feedback in macro cycle one prompted renaming the Administrator standard to Coordinator and reordering the standards

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Faculty and organization feedback that the term Administrator was confused with non-faculty leadership roles led to renaming Standard Five to Coordinator and reordering the standards to Instructor, Coordinator, Leader, Researcher, Learner, and Contributor. ## Evidence

### Helen Crompton 2026

Helen Crompton, Diane Burke and Christine Nickel. (2026). Designing faculty standards for technology integration in higher education institutions: a design-based research study. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00584-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Macro cycle one retrospective analysis of coded survey and interview feedback. Participants expressed confusion, asking "Is administrator the best heading?"; the researchers revised the standard name and description accordingly, and reordered the standards to match faculty responsibilities.

> "In reflecting on the feedback, the term Administrator is often connected with those in non-faculty leadership roles, such as the Dean. With this, the standard name Administrator was changed to Coordinator and the word administration was removed from the standard description."
