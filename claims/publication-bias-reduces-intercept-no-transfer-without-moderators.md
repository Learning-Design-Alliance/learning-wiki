Two publication-bias assessments using PET-PEESE and various selection methods were applied to the meta-analytic dataset. The "intercept predictions were substantially reduced, often indicating no positive transfer when none of the aforementioned moderators are present," while moderator estimates held.

> "In two assessments for publication bias using PET-PEESE and various selection methods, the moderator effect sizes were minimally affected. However, the intercept predictions were substantially reduced, often indicating no positive transfer when none of the aforementioned moderators are present."
