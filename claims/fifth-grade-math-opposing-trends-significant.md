## Evidence

### Easton 2003

Easton, J. Q., Correa, M., Luppescu, S., Park, H.-S., Ponisciak, S., Rosenkranz, T., & Sporte, S. (2003). How Do They Compare? ITBS and ISAT Reading and Mathematics in the Chicago Public Schools, 1999 to 2002. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications/briefs/onlineresources.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same standardized citywide trend analysis for fifth-grade mathematics found diverging test trends; the article calls the difference between the opposing trends statistically significant. The authors add that since 2001 and 2002 scores on both tests move in the same direction, the anomaly may not be of much concern.

> "In fifth grade, ITBS scores declined and ISAT scores rose. The difference between these opposing trends is statistically significant."
