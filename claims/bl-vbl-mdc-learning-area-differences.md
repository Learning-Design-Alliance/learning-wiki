## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Baran L 2025

Baran L, Öztürk H. (2025). The effects of video-based and blended learning on medication dosage calculation skills of paramedic students: A randomized, quasi-experimental study. Medicine 104(31):e43651. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043651

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sub-scale analysis of the 20-question MDCST using independent samples t tests; the infusion treatments area showed the largest difference (BL 12.80 ± 4.24 vs VBL 8.84 ± 6.28, t = 4.562, P = .000).

> "There were statistically significant differences between the mean scores of the groups in 3 of 5 learning areas of MDC: SI Units and conversions, tablets and capsules, and infusion treatments. In each learning area, the BL group had higher posttest scores than the VBL group"
