### Jiang Qinxu 2022

Jiang Qinxu, Horta Hugo, Yuen Mantak. (2022). International medical students’ perspectives on factors affecting their academic success in China: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03597-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview study of 40 international MBBS students in China; participants reported confusion, frustration, and teachers resorting to Chinese, and perceived some teachers lacked teaching confidence due to the language barrier. Students' own English weakness was reported by 28% of participants.

> "Half of the participants found it difficult to understand teachers’ accents or poor English, and they hinted that teachers might also have trouble understanding international students’ English."
