## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, L., Li, Y., Wang, L., Zhang, X., Sun, Y., He, S. & Zhu, W. (2026). Effects of Tai Chi on attentional bias in men with methamphetamine use disorder. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00426-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial of 48 men with MUD assigned to Tai Chi (n=24) or treadmill-running control (n=24); ANCOVA on dot-probe attention bias scores adjusted for age, education, years of METH use and baseline values found "a moderate effect size" (ηp2 = 0.134).

> "After 12 weeks of intervention, the Tai Chi group showed a significant decrease in attention bias scores with a moderate effect size compared with the control group (F = 6.023, p = 0.019, ηp2 = 0.134)."
