### Lesselroth Blake; Bruthans Jan; Hidki Asmaa; Yoon Soo-Hyuk; Oh Seok Kyeong; Lim Byung Gun; Lee Ho-Jin 2024

Lesselroth Blake; Bruthans Jan; Hidki Asmaa; Yoon Soo-Hyuk; Oh Seok Kyeong; Lim Byung Gun; Lee Ho-Jin. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT in the In-Training Examination for Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine Residents in South Korea: Observational Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/56859

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

McNemar's test comparison of GPT-4 performance on the same 398 questions in Korean and in ChatGPT-translated English versions, with translations reviewed by two authors. "GPT-4 performed significantly better on English-translated questions" overall; no effect size printed.

> "Overall, GPT-4 performed significantly better on English-translated questions than on Korean originals (75.4% vs 67.8%; difference 7.5%; 95% CI 3.1%-11.9%; P=.001)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Year-by-year McNemar comparisons of GPT-4 Korean versus English performance; "the performance was consistently higher in English than in Korean" but only 2019 was individually significant.

> "When analyzed by year, the performance was consistently higher in English than in Korean, with the difference reaching statistical significance only in 2019 (75.3% vs 62.3%; P=.01)."
