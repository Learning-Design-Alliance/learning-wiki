Comparative analysis of indeterminacy rates across the 15 vignettes in the 20-university document study. The study reports that "Process-only cases produced more indeterminacy than cases with retained AI -generated language", with outline cases at 11 Indeterminate each and Case 13 at 16.

"Process-only cases produced more indeterminacy than cases with retained AI -generated language. Cases 1 and 2 (outline use with and without disclosure) each produced 11 Indeterminate classifications, and Case 13 produced 16. No major-drafting case produced more than one Indeterminate outcome, and Case 12 produced none."
