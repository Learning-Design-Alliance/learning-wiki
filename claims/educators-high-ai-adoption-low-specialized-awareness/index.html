Cross-study integration in Section 5.5 summarizing the Study 2 targeted educator survey (n=116) of technology-engaged educators recruited via the AISE 2024 conference and online distribution. The integration found that "high overall AI adoption among educators (84%) coexists with limited awareness of specialized services (11%)"; 16% of respondents reported no educational AI use.

"Cross-study integration reveals a coherent pattern: high overall AI adoption among educators (84%) coexists with limited awareness of specialized services (11%) and heavy reliance on ChatGPT (44%)."
