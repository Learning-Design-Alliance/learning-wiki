Anastasia Sofroniou and Konstantinos Poutos 2016¶

Anastasia Sofroniou and Konstantinos Poutos. (2016). Investigating the Effectiveness of Group Work in Mathematics. Educ. Sci. 2016, 6, 30. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci6030030

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of end-of-year exam performance ratios for both classes, reported in Section 4.2 and Figure 5; no significance test is printed for this contrast, so the percentages are descriptive.

"The results show that when students worked in groups 47.8% of the class achieved better marks in Integration (11 out of the 23 performance ratios were greater than the value 1) whilst only 37.5% of the students performed better in integration when working in normal class arrangements (6 out of the 16 students)."
