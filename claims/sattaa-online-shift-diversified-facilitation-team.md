## Evidence

### Carmen Fies and Chris Packham 2022

Carmen Fies and Chris Packham. (2022). Technology to scale up diversity in astronomy education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.11129](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.11129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program report of four SATTAA iterations (2018-2021) with facilitation-team composition tracked in Table 1: 2018 had 9 local members and 0 national/international; 2021 had 6 local, 4 national, and 3 international, with "international experts from Spain, Chile, and Mexico facilitated sessions". Gender shifted from 3 female/5 male (2018) to 7 female/6 male (2021).

> "In 2021, international experts from Spain, Chile, and Mexico facilitated sessions, exemplifying the international nature of astrophysics. This was noted by the participating teachers with excitement"
