## Evidence

### Bin 2026

Usman, M. B., Opoku, A., Asafo Adjei, T. A., Ofori-Mankata, D., Dacosta, D. A., Konadu, L., Acheampong, I. O., Issaka, F., Owusu, D., Wanaba, P. N., & Owusu-Afriyie, B. (2026). Pre-tertiary academic preparation, psychological preparedness, and academic performance among nursing students in Ghana: a cross-sectional study. *BMC Medical Education, 26*(1), 904. [doi:10.1186/s12909-026-09281-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09281-w)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression predicting academic referral status among 339 cases (98.3% of 345) following listwise deletion. WASSCE aggregate showed no independent association (OR = 1.04, p = .134); no effect sizes were printed for the null findings.

> "None of the predictors entered into the model reached conventional statistical significance at p < .05 (Table 11)."

### Bin 2026 (2)

Usman, M. B., Opoku, A., Asafo Adjei, T. A., Ofori-Mankata, D., Dacosta, D. A., Konadu, L., Acheampong, I. O., Issaka, F., Owusu, D., Wanaba, P. N., & Owusu-Afriyie, B. (2026). Pre-tertiary academic preparation, psychological preparedness, and academic performance among nursing students in Ghana: a cross-sectional study. *BMC Medical Education, 26*(1), 904. [doi:10.1186/s12909-026-09281-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09281-w)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus model-fit test comparing the full referral model to the null model among 339 cases. The model achieved 64.9% classification accuracy with high sensitivity (96.3%) but very low specificity (9.0%).

> "The complete model did not bring about a statistically significant change in the prediction of academic referral status when compared to the null model (Omnibus χ²(8) = 13.39, p =. 099). The variance explained was very small (Cox & Snell R² =. 039; Nagelkerke R² =. 053)"
