Theoretical/political argument in the Politics section, citing Gulson et al. (2022) and Day (2021). The article links AIED's datafication to results-based accountability trends since the 1980s and warns political actors may buy into AI hype despite lacking evidence of measurable learning improvement.

"The risks here are of automated decision-making replacing human judgment in political choices over education, and the potential for poor quality data being used to make high-stakes decisions that could affect schools, staff or students alike"
