## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Grades had significant positive indirect effects on same-domain course choices and significant negative indirect effects on other-domain course choices, mediated through self-concept and value. [→ Jansen 2020](#jansen-2020)

## Evidence

### Jansen 2020

Jansen, M., Becker, M., & Neumann, M. (2020). Dimensional Comparison Effects on (Gendered) Educational Choices. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(2), 330-350. https://nbn-resolving.org/urn:nbn:de:0111-pedocs-220459

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bayesian estimation of indirect effects through the four self-concept and value facets, in a model matching the maximum-likelihood path pattern. The article found "significant positive indirect effects of grades on course choices within domains" and significant negative cross-domain effects, interpreted as mediated dimensional comparison effects.

> "We found significant positive indirect effects of grades on course choices within domains (e.g., math grades on choosing advanced math courses) and significant negative effects across domains (e.g., math grades on choosing advanced German courses)."
