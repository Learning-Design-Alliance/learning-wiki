## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Taylor A. W. R. 2026

Taylor A. W. R., Patel F. Y., Smyth E. K., Gay S., and Bird T. (2026). AI in the Classroom: Observing Preclinical Students' Use of ChatGPT During Case‐Based Learning at a UK Medical School. The Clinical Teacher 23, no. 2: e70360. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70360

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic observation across all groups; on one occasion ChatGPT incorrectly stated that protamine binding was reversible, noticed only because the lecture had covered the correct mechanism.

> "Across all groups, ChatGPT was used mainly when students were stuck rather than to check understanding, making errors less likely to be noticed."
