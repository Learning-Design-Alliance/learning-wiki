## Evidence

### Liu Xiaonan L. 2014

Liu Xiaonan L., Liang Peipeng, Li Kuncheng, Reder Lynne M. (2014). Brain Correlates of Testing Effect. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ROI fMRI analysis contrasting Test 1 successful-recall activation for pairs again correct on Test 2 versus pairs that dropped to incorrect, involving data from 10 participants. The article reports "significant SMR effects in left PFC, t(9) = 2.75, p = .011" and three further regions; correlations with Test 2 accuracy reached significance in right PFC (r = .64) and right PPC (r = .57).

> "We found significant SMR effects in left PFC, t(9) = 2.75, p = .011, right PFC, t(9) = 2.70, p = .012;right PPC, t(9) = 2.39, p = .021 and left hippocampus, t(9) = 2.09, p = .034"
