## Evidence

### de la Fuente Rene 2025

de la Fuente Rene, Galvez-Yanjari Victor, Delfino Alejandro, Lira Ricardo, Hurtado Claudia, Munoz-Gama Jorge, Sepúlveda Marcos. (2025). Process-oriented feedback for ultrasound-guided central venous access training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03341-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT's intergroup comparison, "there were no significant differences between the CG and POG" on the three classic indicators at posttest (e.g., post GRS 22.5 CG vs 22.75 POG, P = 0.96; post execution time 19 vs 16 min, P = 0.32).

> "Concerning the checklist results, GRS score, and execution times, there were no significant differences between the CG and POG."
