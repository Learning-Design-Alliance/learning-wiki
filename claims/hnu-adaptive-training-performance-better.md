## Evidence

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Controlled study with 73 analyzed participants (36 Adaptive, 37 Control) using the Deep Thought logic tutor with stratified random assignment by pretest. Results show "Cohen's d = .49" for training length, "Cohen's d = .72" for training time, and "Cohen's d = .43" for training accuracy, all favoring Adaptive.

> "Signiﬁcant diﬀerences were found be- tween the two conditions in the training (1) length: t(72) = 1.75, p = .04, Cohen's d = .49, (2) time: t(72) = 2.25, p = .01, Cohen's d = .72, and (3) accuracy: t(72) = 1.65, p = .05, Cohen's d = .43."
