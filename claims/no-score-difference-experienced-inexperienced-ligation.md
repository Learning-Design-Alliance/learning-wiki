## Evidence

### Okada Takuhisa 2022

Okada Takuhisa, Asao Takayuki, Inoue Hitoshi, Harimoto Norifumi, Tanaka Kazumi, Shiraishi Takuya, Sano Akihiko, Ogawa Hiroomi, Sohda Makoto, Shirabe Ken, Saeki Hiroshi. (2022). Retrospective study of online ligation student practice incorporating flipped learning into e-learning. Surgery Today. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00595-022-02490-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of 72 inexperienced versus 31 experienced students using Welch's t test; Table 3 prints p values of 0.862, 0.911 and 0.868 for Instructor 1, Instructor 2 and total average. No effect size is reported.

> "We compared the students without ligation training to those who had received ligation training in other departments; there were no differences in the scores for each instructor or in the total scores (Table 3)."
