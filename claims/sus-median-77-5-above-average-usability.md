# Adapted SUS scores indicated above-average usability, with the round 2 median of 77.5 exceeding the industry average of 68

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Testers' median adapted SUS score rose from 73.80 in round 1 to 77.50 in round 2, above the benchmark average of 68. [→ Lesselroth BJ 2026](#lesselroth-bj-2026)

## Evidence

### Lesselroth BJ 2026

Lesselroth BJ, Douglas A, Monkman H, Marcilly R, Bellavia K, Anderson M, Van Buren J, Parsons C, Mohanty S, Homco J, Richards M, Gold KP. (2026). How to Conduct Usability Testing of Medical Education Simulations: Methods Tutorial Using a Telemedicine Simulation for Maternal Care. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/102697

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Adapted SUS administered to clinician testers after each testing round of the telemedicine simulation. The article reports round 2 median 77.50 (IQR 73.13-80.00) versus 73.80 in round 1, attributing the increase to "The inclusion of a mock EHR in round 2."

> "SUS scores ranged from 65 to 100. For round 1, respondents’ median score was 73.80 (IQR 70.63-82.50); for round 2, the median score was 77.50 (IQR 73.13-80.00). The inclusion of a mock EHR in round 2 may account for the increase in the median SUS score."
