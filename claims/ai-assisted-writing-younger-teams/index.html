Haoyang Wang 2026¶

Haoyang Wang, Mingze Zhang, Yi Bu, Star Xing Zhao, Meijun Liu. (2026). Smaller, Younger, and More Impactful: How AI-Assisted Writing Transforms Research Teams. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27404

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational full-text analysis of 147,074 PLoS and Nature publications (2020-2025) using AI usage scores from a text-based detection algorithm. AI-assisted PLoS teams average career age 17.3 vs 19.9 (pre-GPT) and 19.6 (post-GPT); differences significant at p < 0.01, described as "notably lower mean career age of authors".

"For the PLoS family, the AI-assisted group not only has a notably lower mean career age of authors (17.3) compared to the human-written groups (pre-GPT: 19.9; post-GPT: 19.6), but also exhibits a distribution shifted further to the left."
