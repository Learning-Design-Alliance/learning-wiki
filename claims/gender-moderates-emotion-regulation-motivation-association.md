# Gender moderates the association between emotion regulation and academic motivation, with a stronger positive association among male students

## Evidence

### Zhang Y 2026

Zhang Y, Shi Y and Lu J (2026). The relationship between AI anxiety and academic motivation among university students: the mediating role of emotion regulation and the moderating role of gender. Front. Psychol. 17:1918525. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918525

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS Model 14 regression (Table 3) controlling for daily AI use duration, in the survey of 1,484 students. The emotion regulation × gender interaction was significant (B = −0.160, p < 0.001); gender was coded male = 1, female = 2.

> "The interaction between emotion regulation and gender was significant [B = −0.160, SE = 0.046, p < 0.001, 95% CI (−0.250, −0.071)], indicating that gender moderated the association between emotion regulation and academic motivation."

### Zhang Y 2026 (2)

Zhang Y, Shi Y and Lu J (2026). The relationship between AI anxiety and academic motivation among university students: the mediating role of emotion regulation and the moderating role of gender. Front. Psychol. 17:1918525. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918525

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope analyses illustrated in Figure 2, from the same moderated regression. The positive association held for male students (Bsimple = 0.519, p < 0.001) and female students (Bsimple = 0.358, p < 0.001), and was stronger among male students.

> "emotion regulation was positively associated with academic motivation among both male students ( Bsimple = 0.519, p < 0.001) and female students (Bsimple = 0.358, p < 0.001). However, this positive association was stronger among male students, as indicated by the steeper slope."
