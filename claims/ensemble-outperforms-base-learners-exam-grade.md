Performance evaluation of the stacked-generalization ensemble versus ten base learners on STAT 119 student data, using RMSE for final exam score and course grade. The article reports the ensemble "out-performs any single learner for both outcomes" despite highly correlated base-learner predictions; ensemble RMSEs were 45.3 and 0.887.
