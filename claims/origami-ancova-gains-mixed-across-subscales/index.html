Evidence¶

Akayuure et al. 2016¶

Peter Akayuure, S. K. Asiedu-Addo, Victor Alebna. (2016). Investigating the Effect of Origami Instruction on Preservice Teachers’ Spatial Ability and Geometric Knowledge for Teaching. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology. https://doi.org/10.18404/ijemst.78424

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Covariate-adjusted univariate ANOVAs in the quasi-experiment (experimental n = 52, control n = 42), with pretests as covariates for the dependent variables. Gains were significant for spatial orientation and GKT; the spatial visualization gain was not significant. No effect size values are printed for these adjusted contrasts.

"Furthermore, when the pretests were used as covariates in computing the univariate ANOVAs, the F ratio showed statistically significant gains for two subscales of the dependent variable: spatial orientation and GKT but no statistical significant gains in the third subscale (spatial visualization)."
