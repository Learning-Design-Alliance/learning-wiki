## Evidence

### Liu Yutong 2026

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial assigning 25 students per group during a two-week breast surgery rotation. The Results section reports the experimental group's "significantly higher mean total theoretical examination scores compared to the control group", with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d = 0.600) shown in Table 2.

> "The experimental group achieved significantly higher mean total theoretical examination scores compared to the control group (76.25 ± 4.35 vs. 73.60 ± 4.47, P < 0.05)."

### Liu Yutong 2026 (2)

Liu Yutong, Ling Zijun, Zhang Zicheng, Peng Xinqi, Liu Minfeng. (2026). Integrating online learning with PBL and CBL: a promising approach to improve comprehensive learning outcomes in breast surgery for Chinese eight-year medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09784-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same RCT, Results section, reporting the two examination subsections. Basic theoretical questions reached significance (P = 0.025, d = 0.654), while clinical case analysis was "only borderline significant (P = 0.079)" with d = 0.507.

> "Specifically, the experimental group performed better in basic theoretical questions (40.24 ± 2.29 vs. 38.77 ± 2.19, P < 0.05). For clinical case analysis, although a moderate effect size (d = 0.507) was observed, the between‑group difference was only borderline significant (P = 0.079)."
