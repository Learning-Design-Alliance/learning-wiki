`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational OLS fixed-effects panel analysis of five years (2014-2019) of OSPI school data, 6,256 middle school observations, with district, county and year fixed effects and clustered standard errors. The park-within-800m coefficient is "4.17***" (p < 0.01); the association is "a 4.2 percent increase" in proficiency."

> "the presence of a public park within the 800m buffer area of the school is associated with a 4.2 percent increase in the number of students who met or exceeded proficiency standards on the assessment tests for middle schools"
