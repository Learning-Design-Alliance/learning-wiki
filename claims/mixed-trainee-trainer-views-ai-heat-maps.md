### Dick L 2024

Dick L, Boyle CP, Skipworth RJE, Smink DS, Tallentire VR, Yule S. (2024). Automated analysis of operative video in surgical training: scoping review. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae124

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the one included qualitative study (Hameed et al.), which surveyed trainees and trainers on AI-generated heat maps for laparoscopic cholecystectomy. It reports mixed opinions, with some raising "concerns over potential dependence on it for making intraoperative decisions".

> "Trainees and trainers strongly agreed (73%) that their use after surgery for coaching and feedback could be effective. Most suggested that heat maps should be incorporated in the surgical training curriculum, particularly for those at an earlier stage in training. However, 40% reported that, even if readily available, they would not use it routinely."
