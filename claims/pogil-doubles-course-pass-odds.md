## Evidence

### Walker L 2017

Walker L, Warfa A-R M. (2017). Process oriented guided inquiry learning (POGIL®) marginally effects student achievement measures but substantially increases the odds of passing a course. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0186203

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Odds-ratio meta-analysis of nine studies comparing pass rates (grade C or better) in POGIL versus lecture courses; the null hypothesis was rejected (z = 4.113, p < 0.0001). Relative risk of failure was "0.62, 95% CI: 0.47–0.80".

> "Our analysis found an overall estimated odds ratio of 2.02 [95% CI: 1.45–2.83, Fig 4]. This suggests that the odds of passing were two times higher in a POGIL classroom versus a standard lecture classroom."
