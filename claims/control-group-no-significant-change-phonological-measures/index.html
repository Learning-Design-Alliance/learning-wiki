Evidence¶

Harper 2011¶

Harper, L. J. (2011). Nursery rhyme knowledge and phonological awareness in preschool children. The Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1), 65-78. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097164

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison of N=9 control children on the nursery rhyme completion task before and after the ten-week period. Means were 3.33 at pretest and 3.89 at posttest; the article reports the scores were "not significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-1.089, P=0.276)".

"The pre- and post-test completion task scores for the control group were not significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-1.089, P=0.276)."

Harper 2011 (2)¶

Harper, L. J. (2011). Nursery rhyme knowledge and phonological awareness in preschool children. The Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1), 65-78. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097164

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison of N=9 control children on the pre-K PALS tasks over the same ten-week period. Means were 7.00 at pretest and 8.22 at posttest; the article reports the scores were "not significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-0.768, P=0.443)".

"The pre- and post-test pre-K PALS scores for the control group were not significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-0.768, P=0.443)."
