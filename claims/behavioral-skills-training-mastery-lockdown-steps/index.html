Schildkraut 2020¶

Schildkraut, J., Grogan, K., & Nabors, A. (2020). Should schools be conducting lockdown drills? WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/schools-lockdown-drills/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports, citing Dickson and Vargo (2017), that behavior skills training including "instruction, modeling of skills, and practice" produced mastery of six out of seven lockdown steps among kindergarten students.

"Dickson and Vargo (2017) found that several sessions of behavior skills training (which included instruction, modeling of skills, and practice of those skills) in a lockdown drill resulted in kindergarten students' mastery of six out of seven steps in the lockdown process."

Schildkraut 2020 (2)¶

Schildkraut, J., Grogan, K., & Nabors, A. (2020). Should schools be conducting lockdown drills? WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/schools-lockdown-drills/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The brief reports, citing Schildkraut and Nickerson (2020), that after training and follow-up practice "skill mastery was achieved for three out of four steps" (locking doors, turning off lights, not responding to door knocks), with near-mastery for remaining silent and out of sight.

"following the training, skill mastery was achieved for three out of four steps in the lockdown practice: locking doors, turning off lights, and not responding to door knocks; consistent with other studies, near-mastery was achieved for remaining silent and out of sight"
