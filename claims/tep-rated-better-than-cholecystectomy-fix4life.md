## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2026

Rahimi A. Masie, van Emden Michael, Hardon Sem F., Simons Maarten, Bonjer H. Jaap, Horeman Tim, Daams Freek. (2026). Assessing the realism and face validity of Fix For Life: an embalmed human cadaver model for high-fidelity laparoscopic training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12550-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Friedman test across the three procedures with Wilcoxon Signed Rank post hoc tests and Bonferroni adjustment in the resident face validation data (n = 30). "The TEP was assessed better than the laparoscopic cholecystectomy" on the four listed points.

> "The TEP was assessed better than the laparoscopic cholecystectomy for laparoscopic view (p = 0.009), lifelike manipulation of organs (p = 0.002), lifelike manipulation of tissue (p = 0.005), and overall satisfaction (p = 0.005)"
