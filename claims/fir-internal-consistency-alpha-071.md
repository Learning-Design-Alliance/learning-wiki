### Klusmann Dietrich 2023

Klusmann Dietrich, Knorr Mirjana, Hampe Wolfgang. (2023). Exploring the relationships between first impressions and MMI ratings: a pilot study. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(2), 519-536. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10151-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of station-level ratings from the 2012 Hamburg selection MMI, with rater designations permuted eight times before averaging. The article reports "Internal consistency across stations amounted to Cronbach's α = 0.71 for first impression and α = 0.69 for MMI performance"; a nominal IRT model gave marginal reliabilities of 0.69 and 0.65.

> "Internal consistency across stations amounted to Cronbach's α = 0.71 for first impression and α = 0.69 for MMI performance."
