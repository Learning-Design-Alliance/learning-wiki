## Evidence

### Bobos Pavlos 2021

Bobos Pavlos, Pouliopoulou Dimitra V., Harriss Alexandra, Sadi Jackie, Rushton Alison, MacDermid Joy C. (2021). A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random effects meta-analysis of Pearson correlations between OSCE station scores across included studies. The pooled estimate was "0.27 (95% CI: 0.15, 0.39)" with high heterogeneity I2 = 71.55%. No standardised effect size was printed for this finding.

> "The pooled estimate of Pearson’s correlation in between station score correlation was 0.27 (95% CI: 0.15, 0.39) with high statistical heterogeneity, I2 = 71.55% (Fig 4)."
