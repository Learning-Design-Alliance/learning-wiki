## Evidence

### Nathan T. Moore and Carl D. Ferkinhoff 2017

Nathan T. Moore and Carl D. Ferkinhoff. (2017). Inexpensive Student-fabricated Solar Panels and Some Related Classroom Measurements. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.04029](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.04029)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cost accounting for two classes of 30 students, itemized in Table I, gives an estimated total of $380 for 60 student panels, which the authors describe as roughly "$6 .30/student". They also compute $1.17/Watt from the 5.4 W ideal panel output.

> "The materials you will need to source for this project average about $6 .30/student. This unit cost could certainly be reduced."
