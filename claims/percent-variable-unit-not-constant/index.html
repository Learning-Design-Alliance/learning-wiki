Evidence¶

Herring 1919¶

Herring, J. P. (1919). Derivation of a scale to measure abilities in scientific thinking. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q2 · i? · design · r2

Theoretical argument in the article's methodological section: per cents indicate the order of difficulty but are a variable unit, so they are safe to use only once their variations are determined. No effect size is printed.

"The percent has often been used to measure mental abilities just as if it were a constant unit. It is a variable, and as such, it can be used safely only when the amounts and effects of its variations have been determined."
