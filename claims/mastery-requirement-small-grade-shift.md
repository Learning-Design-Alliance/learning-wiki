## Evidence

### David Toback 2004

David Toback, Andreas Mershin, Irina Novikova. (2004). New Pedagogy for Using Internet-Based Teaching Tools in Physics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0408034](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0408034)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Course-level observational report from the authors' semesters of use: with ~97% of students achieving 100% scores the grade distribution curve was not altered, only a small overall shift of the mean, conditional on the homework/quiz grade portion being no larger than about ten percent. No effect size is printed.

> "Finally, these requirements do not alter the grade distribution curve even with ~97% of the students achieving 100% scores, since this only produces a small overall shift of the mean (provided the homework/quiz portion of the grade is not larger than about ten percent)."
