One-sided RA-CUSUM analysis of textbook outcome in the retrospective 191-patient cohort; the article reports "a learning curve after 83 cases" for the composite TO parameter.

> "Examining the composite parameter TO (Fig. 7), our investigation also revealed a learning curve after 83 cases."

### Seika Philippa 2025 (2)

Seika Philippa, Martin Friederike, Serwah Armanda, Maurer Max Magnus, Winter Axel, Ossami-Saidy Ramin Raul, Ritschl Paul V., Dobrindt Eva, Kurreck Annika, Raakow Jonas, Pratschke Johann, Biebl Matthias, Denecke Christian. (2025). Structured surgical training in minimally invasive esophagectomy (MIE) increases textbook outcome–a risk-adjusted learning curve. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11539-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort comparison in the Results section: TO was achieved in 27.00% of trainer cases versus 40.66% of trainee cases (p = 0.046); the article reports the difference as significant without an effect size.

> "Finally, the occurrence of textbook outcomes was significantly higher in the trainee cohort overall (Trainer vs. Trainee, 27.00% vs. 40.66% (p = 0.046))."
