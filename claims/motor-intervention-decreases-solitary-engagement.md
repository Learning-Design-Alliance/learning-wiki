## Evidence

### Ketcheson et al. 2017

Leah Ketcheson, Janet Hauck and Dale Ulrich. (2017). The effects of an early motor skill intervention on motor skills, levels of physical activity, and socialization in young children with autism spectrum disorder: A pilot study. Autism 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361316650611

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

General linear model (MIXED procedure) on biweekly POPE observations of the experimental group only (n = 11). The article reports a "statistically significant effect of time (for decreasing minutes) in solitary"; no significant time effects for joint engagement, parallel play, or onlooking.

> "results revealed a statistically significant effect of time (for decreasing minutes) in solitary (F(4, 8.76) = 7.94, p < 0.01)"
