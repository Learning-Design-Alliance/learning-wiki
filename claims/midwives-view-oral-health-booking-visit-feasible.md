Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended post-questionnaire responses, feedback forms and emails from the Victorian MIOH pilot found feasibility was a key theme; one participant noted "it is feasible to include two simple questions as part of our first antenatal booking visit".

> "The majority of midwives said that it would be feasible to include oral health in their first antenatal booking visit."
