## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Student engagement is significantly correlated with learning, partial-r(13) = 0.56, p = 0.03, controlling for pre-test performance. [→ Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll 2013](#jared-b-stang-and-ido-roll-2013)
`q2 i2` Pre-test scores alone are not significantly correlated with post-test scores (r(14) = 0.32, p = 0.23). [→ Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll 2013 (2)](#jared-b-stang-and-ido-roll-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll 2013

Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll (2013). Interactions between teaching assistants and students boost engagement in physics labs. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020117)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation of section-level engagement with post-test scores controlling for pre-test, across the 16 sections: engagement was "signiﬁcantly correlated with learning", with printed effect size partial-r(13) = 0.56, p = 0.03, r2 = 0.31.

> "The measure of engagement is signiﬁcantly correlated with learning: partial- r(13) = 0.56, p = 0.03, r2 = 0.31."

### Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll 2013 (2)

Jared B. Stang and Ido Roll (2013). Interactions between teaching assistants and students boost engagement in physics labs. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020117)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same analysis: pre-test performance by itself was "not signiﬁcantly correlated with post-test scores", with printed effect size r(14) = 0.32, p = 0.23; equivalence was not tested.

> "Performance on the pre-test by itself is not signiﬁcantly correlated with post-test scores: r(14) = 0.32, p = 0.23."
