Summary of Table II sub-topic counts, where The Learning Process and Memory each appear eleven times. The author's summary states courses embrace "chapters on the learning process with special emphasis on memory processes" with less attention to thinking and reasoning.

> "Educational psychology as now taught in the colleges and universities of this country embraces chapters on the learning process with special emphasis on memory processes- included under various terms and involving association, perception, imaging, attention and to a less degree chapters on the higher thought processes of thinking and reasoning."
