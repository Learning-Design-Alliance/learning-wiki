`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article reports, citing Buzzell et al.'s study of QM course syllabi from 188 institutions, that textbook choice correlated with instructional approach. Only about 26% of QM courses used the spin-first approach.

> "All courses that used David Griffiths' Introduction to Quantum Mechanics [14] adopted a position-first approach, while all courses using David McIntyre's Quantum Mechanics: A Paradigms Approach [15] adopted a spin-first approach"
