`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Topic-level analysis of exam scores across all participants. The article also reports skills scores "highest on questions about cardiovascular drugs (50.1% (SD 31.4%))" and "lowest on questions about pulmonary drugs (mean 14.6% (SD 35.2%))".

> "In general, students scored the highest on knowledge questions concerning analgesics (mean 83.1%; standard deviation (SD) 23.5%; P < 0.001 compared with all other topics) and the lowest on questions about anticoagulants (mean 62.9% (SD 30.9%), P < 0.001 compared with all other topics)."
