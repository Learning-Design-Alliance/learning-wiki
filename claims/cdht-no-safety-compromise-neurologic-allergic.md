## Evidence

### Hou Qin 2025

Hou Qin, Zhou Bin, Kang Jing-Ping, Zuo Yun-Xia. (2025). Competency-driven hemostasis training reduces vascular complications in transradial hemodynamic monitoring: a self-controlled trial with early-career residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08048-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary endpoints in the same self-controlled trial, analyzed with Fisher's exact test. The article reports no significant differences; equivalence was not tested, and no effect sizes are printed.

> "Both neurological rates (pre: 3.2% vs. post: 1.0%, P = 0.36) and allergic reaction incidence (pre: 4.3% vs. post: 3.2%, P = 0.72) remained statistically comparable before and after training, as analyzed using Fisher's exact test."
