Schroeder 2026¶

Schroeder, N. L., Ambarwati, Y. E., Zhang, Y., & Zhai, C. (2026). A Benchmark for LLM's Understanding of Middle School and High School Science. https://github.com/InviteInstitute/MS-NGSS-Aligned-Benchmark

q2 · i? · design · r2

Human validation study (Part III, RQ3): a former 7-12 science teacher with no role in item generation reviewed 240 question-answer pairs sampled evenly across NGSS areas and question types, judging standard alignment and answer correctness.

"Overall, 236 of the 240 reviewed items (98.3%) were judged to align with their intended NGSS standards, while 4 items (1.7%) were identified as misaligned. No incorrect reference answers were identified during the review."

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of the four misaligned items in Part III: each stayed close to the target standard but missed a key piece of the performance expectation, e.g., asking about frequency rather than the amplitude-energy relation for MS-PS4-1.

"Across the four rejected items, the questions generally addressed content related to the target standard but did not fully reflect critical components of the NGSS performance expectation."
