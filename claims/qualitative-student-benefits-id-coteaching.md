### Felix Gorny 2026

Fostering inclusion in healthcare: a systematic review of collaborative teaching with individuals with disorders of intellectual development. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative study (questionnaires, n = 18, and focus groups, n = 4) of social work students after a course designed and delivered by adults with intellectual disabilities acting as lecturers, tutors, and assessors. The review's synthesis table reports a "Positive impact on students’ empathy and increased feeling of comfort"; a related included study likewise reported deeper student understanding and learning.

> "Positive impact on students’ empathy and increased feeling of comfort in interacting with Individuals with Disorders of Intellectual Development."
