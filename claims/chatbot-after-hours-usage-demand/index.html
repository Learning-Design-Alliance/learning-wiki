Observational analysis of chatbot system analytics (Fig. 2, 24-h usage patterns) across the nine units: 63.2% of interactions occurred 9 AM to 5 PM, with a spike at 5 PM, sustained evening interaction, and minimal engagement between 2 and 6 AM; "a notable 36.8% of interactions happened after hours".

"However, a notable 36.8% of interactions happened after hours, suggesting the value of the chatbot as a source of academic support beyond traditional availability times."
