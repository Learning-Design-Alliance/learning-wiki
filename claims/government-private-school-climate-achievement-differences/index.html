q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional comparison (Rasool & Bhat 2018, N = 240, Srinagar, grades 9–10) using the School Environment Inventory; the review reports the government-versus-private achievement contrast (t = 5.48) and the acceptance-dimension contrast (t = 3.40), both significant at the 0.01 level. No effect size is printed.

"the academic performance of government high school students (mean value is 71.91) was substantially different from that of private high school students (mean value is 82.26), with a calculated t value of 5.48 at the 0.01 level of significance. The acceptance dimension of school climate differed between government schools and private schools, with a t value of 3.40, which is significant at the 0.01 level"
