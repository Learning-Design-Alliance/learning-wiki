Zou, J., Zhai, X., Gao, C. (2026). Beyond Information Retrieval: Generative AI as an Epistemic Arbiter to Enhance Collaborative Problem-Solving. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29788-4_18](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29788-4_18)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square comparison of coded CPS behavior distributions in a six-week quasi-experiment with 201 fifth-grade students in GAI (EG) and control (CG) conditions. The EG had "a significantly higher proportion of social behaviors than the CG (73.137% vs. 63.605%)"; the CG showed more task planning, solution reasoning, and monitoring and correction. Effect sizes were small overall (φ = .103).

> "The EG showed a significantly higher proportion of social behaviors than the CG (73.137% vs. 63.605%; χ2 = 12.122, p < .001, φ = .103), particularly in participation (45.581% vs. 39.341%; χ2 = 4.597, p = .032, φ = .063) and conflict management"
