## Evidence

### Ahtisham 2026

Ahtisham, B., Vanacore, K., Napoli, A., Arens, J., Ionova, K., Cohn, C., Salehi, S., & Kizilcec, R. (2026). Examining Variation in How Guided AI Tutors Resolve Student Impasses. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38346 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.38346](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.38346)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 two-turn sequence analysis (Table 5) on impasse episodes, adjusted for depth, impasse type, and practice, relative to a first scripted question at onset (55.5% recovery). An order-free specification reproduced the pattern (30.5% vs 37.5%).

> "When a scripted question followed another scripted question, recovery fell to 28.1%, halving the odds (AOR =0.48 , [0.34, 0.67], p< .001 ). Moving from a scripted question to addressing the student's error preserved recovery (39.8%; AOR=0.78 , p=.29 , not different from onset)."
