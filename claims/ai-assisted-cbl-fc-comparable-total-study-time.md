Wang, Q., Hu, C., Li, Y., Zhang, T., & Zhang, S. (2026). AI-assisted case-based learning and flipped classroom to improve clinical decision-making: a randomized controlled trial in reproductive medicine. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2670047

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A post-hoc survey quantified pre- and post-learning time for both groups. The article reports that greater pre-class preparation in the intervention group was offset by reduced post-class review time; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Although the AI-assisted CBL + FC model required more intensive pre-class preparation compared to the traditional model, which was offset by a significant reduction in post-class review time. The total cumulative study time remained comparable between the two cohorts (Supplementary Table S2)."
