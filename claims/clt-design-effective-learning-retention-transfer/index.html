Evidence¶

Kala 2023¶

Kala, N., & Ayas, A. (2023). Effect of instructional design based on cognitive load theory on students' performances and the indicators of element interactivity. Journal of Turkish Science Education, 20(3), 468-489. https://doi.org/10.36681/tused.2023.027

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Effective learning scores computed from performance Z and cognitive load Z scores (Paas & van Merriënboer formula) for both groups: "the score of the experimental group is higher than that of the control group" at retention (E = .39 vs -.37) and transfer (E = .38 vs -.36).

"According to the retention and transfer effective learning scores calculated on the basis of these scores, it was detected that the score of the experimental group is higher than that of the control group."

Kala 2023 (2)¶

Kala, N., & Ayas, A. (2023). Effect of instructional design based on cognitive load theory on students' performances and the indicators of element interactivity. Journal of Turkish Science Education, 20(3), 468-489. https://doi.org/10.36681/tused.2023.027

q3 · i? · causal · r2

For the TAT post-test basis, the article states the "effective learning scores (E= - .01) were almost equal to the control group (E= .01)" because the experimental group's mental effort scores were higher, despite a higher performance Z score (.31 vs -.29).

"the thermodynamic academic achievement effective learning scores (E= - .01) were almost equal to the control group (E= .01) since their mental effort scores are also higher"
