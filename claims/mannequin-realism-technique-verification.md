### Touchie Claire 2013

Touchie Claire, Humphrey-Murto Susan, Varpio Lara. (2013). Teaching and assessing procedural skills: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-69

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group realism theme; a participant noted all simulated patients resembled "40 year old males" with young healthy joints, while another conceded that failing to aspirate fluid on the dummy meant failing on a real person. Residents found this congruent with other OSCE stations such as physical examination.

> "The residents commented that the simulators did not represent a realistic situation because of some of the physical characteristics of the mannequins."
