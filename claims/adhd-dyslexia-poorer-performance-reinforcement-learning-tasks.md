## Evidence

### Gabay 2025

Gabay, Y., Jacob, L., Mansour, A. & Hertz, U. (2025). Computational markers show specific deficits for dyslexia and ADHD in complex learning settings. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00323-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-factorial analysis of percentage of games learned in the ADHD study (N = 21 with ADHD, 32 controls), with task as within-subject and group as between-subject factor. The ADHD group was "significantly poorer" than controls, F(1, 51) = 4.57, p = 0.037, ηp² = 0.08.

> "Performance was signiﬁcantly poorer, F (1, 51) = 4.57,p = 0.037, ηp²= 0.08, in the ADHD group (M = 0.62, S.E. = 0.03) than in the control group (M = 0.71, S.E. = 0.02)."

### Gabay 2025 (2)
