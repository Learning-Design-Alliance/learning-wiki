## Evidence

### Lincoln 2026

Wono, A. L., Bell, A., & Bourne, E. (2026). Student Reflections in Simulated Speech and Language Therapy Placements Using Telehealth and In‐Person Formats: A Qualitative Study. *International Journal of Language & Communication Disorders, 61*(2), e70221. [doi:10.1111/1460-6984.70221](https://doi.org/10.1111/1460-6984.70221)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis (reflexive thematic analysis) of required online discussion posts following day one of a 12-day simulation program, analysed separately for the telehealth and in-person datasets and then combined for research question one. The study reports "five themes" with "two to four subthemes in each dataset"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Students’ reflections were centred around five themes: (1) client‐centred communication, (2) emotions, (3) working collaboratively, (4) learning from and with others and (5) therapeutic approaches. Each theme had two to four subthemes in each dataset."
