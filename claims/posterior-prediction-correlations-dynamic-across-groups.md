### Katrin Schuessler 2026

Katrin Schuessler, Jenna Koenen, Elke Sumfleth, Marvin Rost. (2026). Variations in Repeated Measures of Cognitive Load and Interest During Complex Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10105-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Posterior predictions (4000 draws) from the best-fitting Bayesian models, correlated over the 21 measurement points per intervention group in the N = 360 sample. The article prints group-specific coefficients (e.g. prompting, segmenting weakening from r = .31 toward r = .03) but no single effect size for the divergence.

> "The relationship between invested mental effort and perceived task difficulty reveals a clear divergence between interventions."
