Allen Hannah K., Knight Tonya, Hall Becky, Dennis Melissa. (2025). Links Between Gambling and Academic Performance Among Undergraduate College Students: A Scoping Review. Journal of Gambling Studies, 41(4), 1517-1531. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10899-025-10440-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Scoping review synthesis (PRISMA-ScR, JBI methodology) of 13 peer-reviewed quantitative studies. The review reports that "The majority of studies found a negative association between gambling and academic performance," with both general and pathological gambling linked to lower GPA; no pooled effect size is printed.

> "The majority of studies found a negative association between gambling and academic performance in college, with both general gambling behavior and pathological gambling consistently linked to lower grade point average (GPA)."
