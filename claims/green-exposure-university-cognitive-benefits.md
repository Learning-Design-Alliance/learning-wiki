## Evidence

### Lucia Mason 2022

Lucia Mason, Angelica Ronconi, Sara Scrimin, Francesca Pazzaglia. (2022). Short-Term Exposure to Nature and Benefits for Students' Cognitive Performance: a Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09631-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Between-subjects experiment with N = 34 university students (Mage = 20 years) in the USA, comparing a 40-minute park walk after fatiguing tasks with passive relaxation in a lab and walking in an urban area. Table 3 reports more proofreading errors detected after the nature walk; the review marks the effect size as incomplete statistical information.

> "More errors detected after walking in nature than in the other two conditions (c)"

### Lucia Mason 2022 (2)

Lucia Mason, Angelica Ronconi, Sara Scrimin, Francesca Pazzaglia. (2022). Short-Term Exposure to Nature and Benefits for Students' Cognitive Performance: a Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09631-8

`q3 · i3` · `review · r3`

Between-subjects experiment with N = 116 Taiwanese university students (Mage = 20.8 years) comparing walking versus jogging along natural versus built campus routes. Table 3 reports Hedges' g of 4.40 for backward digit span accuracy after green exercise, and g of 2.95 for the forward spatial span test.

> "Greater scores (accuracy) in the DST after green exercise along both routes than before (4.40)"

### Lucia Mason 2022 (3)

Lucia Mason, Angelica Ronconi, Sara Scrimin, Francesca Pazzaglia. (2022). Short-Term Exposure to Nature and Benefits for Students' Cognitive Performance: a Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09631-8

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Within-subjects experiment with N = 10 university students (Mage = 20 years) in the USA, comparing a 30-minute walk on a natural campus trail with walking in an aquatic recreation center after cognitive-fatigue tasks. Table 3 reports g = 0.64 for Stroop response time and g = 0.00 for backward digit span accuracy.

> "Greater scores (response time) in the Stroop test after walking in nature (0.64a)"
