Dahlin 2013¶

Karin I. E. Dahlin. (2013). Working Memory Training and the Effect on Mathematical Achievement in Children with Attention Deficits and Special Needs. Journal of Education and Learning, 2(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v2n1p118

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures mixed effects model estimating treatment effect on the Basic Number Screening Test in the total group (treatment n = 42, control n = 15), with age and gender covariates. The article reports "BNST was significant at Time 2 (T2)" with F(1,26.5) = 5.63, p < .05, but not at T3 (p = .075).

"BNST was significant at Time 2 (T2) (treatment effect = 1.638, SE = 0.690, F(1,26.5) = 5.63, p < .05) but not at Time 3 (T3) (treatment effect =1.690, SE = 0.915, F(1,27.6) = 3.41, p = .075)."
