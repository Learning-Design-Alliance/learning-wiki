`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive results from the Fall 2023 course: "128 (49%) of those students passed the quiz within seven attempts", and more than 90% of passers did so within five attempts. Exam accuracy: 42% original numerical, 30% transfer numerical, 61% original conceptual, 34% transfer conceptual.

> "A total of 262 students made at least one attempt on the quiz, and 128 (49%) of those students passed the quiz within seven attempts (answered all problems correctly on one at-tempt.) No student attempted the quiz beyond their passing attempt. More than 90% of all students who passed the quiz did so within five attempts."
