Chen, C.-C., Huang, C., Gribbins, M., & Swan, K. (2018). Gamify online courses with tools built into your learning management system (LMS) to enhance self-determined and active learning. Online Learning, 22(3), 41-54. https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v22i3.1466

End-of-semester survey of students in the gamified course. "Seventy-two percent of students agreed that gamified activities were either extremely or highly useful"; 82% would recommend implementation in other courses; 68% reported higher motivation; 83% enjoyed the activities.
