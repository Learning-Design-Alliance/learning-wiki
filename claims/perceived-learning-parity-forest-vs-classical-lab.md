Self-reported perception from the same brief online survey of the 16 participants, not a measured learning outcome. The average answer about learning relative to “the same number of hours of classical lab class” was 4.6 on a scale from 1 to 7.

> "Regarding physics learning, to the question “Do you feel you have learned more or less than during the same number of hours of classical lab class?”, the average answer was 4.6 on a scale from 1 (“much less”) to 7 (“much more”)."
