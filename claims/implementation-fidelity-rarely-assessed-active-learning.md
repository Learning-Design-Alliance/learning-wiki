Coding of the 176 Freeman et al. (2014) articles found experimental-group "implementation fidelity for the experimental group occurred in 7.4% of the articles but did not occur in 92.6%"; control-group fidelity occurred in 6.3%. The 2015–2022 sample showed 6.0% for both groups.
