`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second-year ANOVA comparison of motivational indices in PH1140 found the missed-homework difference reached statistical significance (F = 6.01, p = 0.02) in the direction opposite the hypothesis. The authors suggest Bridge students may have felt confident enough to skip homework, or might have out-scored controls by even more had they completed it.

> "Specifically, while Bridge students missed an average of 1.93 homework assignments, Non-bridge students missed an average of only 0.29."
