Evidence¶

Daniel R. Espinas 2024¶

Daniel R. Espinas, Brennan W. Chandler. (2024). Correlates of K-12 Students' Intertextual Integration. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09889-8

q3 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of task features across the 25 included studies. Essay writing was the most common task; the review also reports "In all the studies, the documents were informational rather than narrative" and 92% involved socioscientific topics.

"The most common task (k = 16, 64%) involved having participants read the documents for the purpose of writing an essay. These included writing arguments (k = 9, 36%), opinions (k = 1, 4%), or combinations of arguments and summaries/syntheses (k = 5, 20%)."

Daniel R. Espinas 2024 (2)¶

Daniel R. Espinas, Brennan W. Chandler. (2024). Correlates of K-12 Students' Intertextual Integration. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09889-8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Review synthesis of participant characteristics across 5600 participants (median = 99, range = 44–1434) in studies from five countries, with Norway (k = 9) and Italy (k = 6) most common. Elementary-level students were studied far less than secondary-level students.

"Grade levels ranged from fourth to twelfth, with five studies (20%) involving students at the elementary level (grades K-5) and (80%) at the secondary level (grades 6–12)."
