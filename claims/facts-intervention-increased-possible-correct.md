## Evidence

### Barbara Allen-Lyall 2018

Barbara Allen-Lyall. (2018). Helping Students to Automatize Multiplication Facts: A Pilot Study. IEJEE, Volume 10, Issue 4, 391-396. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018438128

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on the same quasi-experimental comparison of the timed facts inventory: “correct out of all possible” scores were significantly higher for the treatment group (M= .66) than the no-treatment group (M= .47), d= 0.86.

> "Additionally, there was a significant difference in the “correct out of all possible” scores for no treatment (M= .47, SD= .24) and treatment (M= .66, SD= .22); t(291)= 7.274, p< .001, d= 0.86."
