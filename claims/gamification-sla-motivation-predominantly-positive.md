`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of 15 empirical studies of gamification in SLA higher education published 2011-2019. The authors report that "most studies show positive results" across psycho-behavioural and cognitive variables, with three ambiguous and one negative.

> "From a general point of view, most studies show positive results with a balanced attention on both psycho-behavioural and cognitive variables (11), three are ambiguous and just one showed negative results on learning."
