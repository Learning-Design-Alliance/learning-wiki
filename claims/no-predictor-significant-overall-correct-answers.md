### Cvijic Andjelka 2025

The Effect of an Educational Intervention in Dental Traumatology on General Dentists' Knowledge: A Study in the Public Dental Service of Western Norway. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary GLMM analyses of the post-intervention questionnaire (n = 94) testing gender, age, years since education finished, self-assessed knowledge level, and continuing education course as predictors of overall correctness, controlling for clinic clustering and recent trauma caseload. A null result; equivalence was not tested.

> "Using the same statistical model (GLMM), none of the five tested predictors was found to have a statistically significant effect on the individual proportion of correct answers (Figure 1b–f)"
