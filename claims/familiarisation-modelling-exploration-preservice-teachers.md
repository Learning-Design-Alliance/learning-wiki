### Jo Tondeur 2025

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-aggregation of qualitative studies in the Familiarisation synthesis; the Modelling category emerged in 18 studies with sub-categories role models, pedagogical approaches and gamification. The review reports that findings "consistently emphasised the importance of modelling".

> "The findings consistently emphasised the importance of modelling in motivating preservice teachers to integrate digital technologies into their own teaching practice."
