Yoon Hyun Bae, Park Wan Beom, Myung Sun-Jung, Moon Sang Hui, Park Jun-Bean. (2018). Validity and reliability assessment of a peer evaluation method in team-based learning classes. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.78

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comment-categorization analysis of 1,317 written comments for the highest achievers in 11 TBL classes among 141 medical students. Findings show "901 comments were compatible with the criteria provided to the students, while the other 331 comments were not".
