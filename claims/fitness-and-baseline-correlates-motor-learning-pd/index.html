Evidence¶

Wanner 2025¶

Wanner, P., Frisch, N., Rikus, S., Roig, M. & Steib, S. (2025). Cardiovascular exercise enhances motor learning across multiple sessions in people with Parkinson's disease: a randomized controlled pilot trial. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00391-6

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Exploratory Spearman correlation analysis within the exercise group of the randomized pilot trial. The printed effect size is rs(10) = .62, p = .037; |r| = .62 is a large effect size (|r| ≥ .37). Exploratory; authors state such findings require replication.

"higher cardiorespiratory fitness was positively correlated with performance improvements in the EXE group (rs(10) = .62, p = .037;"

Wanner 2025 (2)¶

Wanner, P., Frisch, N., Rikus, S., Roig, M. & Steib, S. (2025). Cardiovascular exercise enhances motor learning across multiple sessions in people with Parkinson's disease: a randomized controlled pilot trial. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00391-6

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Exploratory correlational analysis across both groups of the pilot trial. The printed effect sizes are rs(22) = .66, p < .001 and rs(22) = -.51, p = .011; both |r| values (.66 and .51) are large effect sizes (|r| ≥ .37). The authors state these findings require replication.

"performance gains across the six sessions were significantly correlated with performance in the first session (rs(22) = .66, p < .001; Figure 3a) and MDS-UPDRS III (rs(22) = -.51, p = .011; Figure 3b)"
