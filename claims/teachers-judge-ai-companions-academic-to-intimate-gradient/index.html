Qing Xiao 2026¶

Exploring K-12 Teachers' Perceptions of Students' Relationships with AI Companions: Boundaries, Intervention Strategies, and Design Implications. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13479 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.13479

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Scenario-based interviews with 33 US K-12 teachers; structured cross-scenario responses were counted descriptively. The article reports "24 of 33 teachers namedRomantic Partner" as most concerning, and none named Study Buddy.

"Asked which scenario concerned them most, 24 of 33 teachers namedRomantic Partner(20 alone and four tied withFriendorParent), 10 namedParent, two namedFriend, one namedTutor, and none namedStudy Buddy."
