`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis (Table 1, n = 130): 85 students (65.4%) had PSQI > 5 versus 45 (34.6%) good sleepers; mean PSQI global score was 6.42 ± 2.44. The authors note this exceeds rates reported in some Western contexts.

> "Notably, poor sleep quality was highly prevalent in this cohort, affecting nearly two-thirds of the students."
