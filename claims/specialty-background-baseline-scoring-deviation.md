### Zhang Lina 2026

Zhang Lina, Yan Mengling, Qiu Yiwen, Cai Haolei, Yu Yunxian. (2026). Effectiveness of the “Level-Points” online training model and influencing factors in enhancing scoring accuracy among surgical examiners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10033-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable linear regression with age, gender, professional title, specialty and region entered simultaneously on baseline deviation scores of 670 examiners. "After FDR correction, only specialty was significantly associated with scoring deviation"; unstandardised β coefficients are reported, no standardised effect size.

> "After FDR correction, only specialty was significantly associated with scoring deviation. Compared with those from general surgery, examiners from neurosurgery (β = 1.406, se = 0.445, P = 0.002, adjusted P = 0.007) and urology (β = 2.293, se = 0.529, P < 0.001, adjusted P < 0.001) had significantly higher mean scoring deviations"
