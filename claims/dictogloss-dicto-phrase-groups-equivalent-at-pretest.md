## Evidence

### Hamid Marashi 2013

Hamid Marashi, Mojgan Khaksar. (2013). Dictogloss or Dicto-Phrase: Which Works Better for Listening Comprehension? li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 3 No. 1, January-March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068862

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Homogeneity check on the piloted mock KET proficiency test prior to treatment; pretest means were 70.53 (dictogloss) and 70.18 (dicto-phrase). The article reports "no significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups at the outset"; equivalence was not formally tested beyond this t-test.

> "The results (t = 0.217, p = 0.829 > 0.05) indicate that there was no significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups at the outset."
