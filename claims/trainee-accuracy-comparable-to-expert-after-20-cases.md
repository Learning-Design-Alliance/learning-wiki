## Evidence

### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of the trainee's last 92 navigated cases with 30 cases by an expert surgeon (>2000 THA procedures) during the same period; no significant difference in inclination or anteversion error. Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "there was no significant change in the absolute difference in inclination (trainee’s 92 vs. expert’s 30 cases [2.0°±2.0° vs. 2.3°±2.1°, p = 0.55]) and anteversion (trainee’s 92 vs. expert’s 30 cases [2.3°±2.0° vs. 2.5°±2.2°, p = 0.85])"

### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025 (2)

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same trainee-versus-expert comparison, the trainee's coronal-plane vertical distance error was significantly smaller (p = 0.02); the horizontal distance difference was not significant (p = 0.61). No effect size is printed.

> "The absolute difference of the vertical distance between the preoperative plans and postoperative measurements was significantly less in the trainee’s cases (trainee’s 92 vs. expert’s 30 cases [1.4 mm ± 1.0 mm vs. 2.2 mm ± 2.0 mm, p = 0.02])."
