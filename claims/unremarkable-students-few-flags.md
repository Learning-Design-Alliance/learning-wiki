Brief check of 12 files chosen at random from the 32 students categorised as having no substantial problems, used as a informal specificity check on the flag threshold. The author states this "lends weight to this conclusion, although is not definitive proof".

> "Two students had three flags and in retrospect should perhaps have been classified as 'borderlines', but none had four or more."
