## Evidence

### Baoyi W and Khan FU 2026

Baoyi W and Khan FU (2026). AI and higher education: teachers' perceptions of students' ethical tensions in online pedagogical methods. Front. Psychol. 17:1881032. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1881032

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Findings from the TBLT section of the thematic analysis. Teachers reported scaffolding collapse from skipped developmental stages, the shortcut paradox of product over process, invisible inequity in group assessment, and task realism corrosion as tasks are redesigned to be AI-resistant.

> "Instructors feel that AI-assisted task completion eliminates the cognitive challenge intended to support student development in the learning process. This break in the learning process is possible because students skip the increasingly challenging levels embedded in the curriculum by using AI to generate solutions."
