Evidence¶

Swanson 2010¶

Swanson, E. A., & Vaughn, S. (2010). An Observation Study of Reading Instruction Provided to Elementary Students With Learning Disabilities in the Resource Room. Psychology in the Schools, 47(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/pits.20484

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational results: comprehension totaled 557 minutes (25.6% of instruction) with average quality 3.08. "Reading-comprehension monitoring—comprised mostly of teachers ask- ing questions after reading—represented the most commonly observed type of comprehension instruction"; only three teachers provided comprehension-strategy instruction (148 minutes).
