## Evidence

### Ma Irene W Y 2015

Ma Irene W Y, Pugh Debra, Mema Briseida, Brindle Mary E, Cooke Lara, Stromer Julie N. (2015). Use of an error-focused checklist to identify incompetence in lumbar puncture performances. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.12809

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Internal structure validity evidence from ratings of the 18 videos. Cronbach's alpha was 0.35 for the error-focused checklist versus 0.79 for the conventional checklist; inter-rater ICCs were high for both tools.

> "The internal consistency of the error-focused checklist was 0.35, lower than that reported for our conventionally constructed checklist (0.79)."
