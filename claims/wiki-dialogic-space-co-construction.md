# A wiki with a thinking-together approach can provide a dialogic space in which students co-construct shared understanding

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a Spanish classroom experiment analyzed by Pifarre and Kleine Staarman, a wiki environment gave each participant voice and, with an exploratory-talk approach, allowed students to create a dialogic space to co-construct new understanding. [→ Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2011](#gerry-stahl-friedrich-hesse-2011)

## Evidence

### Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2011

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse. (2011). Let a hundred flowers bloom; let a hundred schools of thought contend. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 6, 139-145. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-011-9118-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The editorial's summary of the Pifarre and Kleine Staarman study, positioned in the small-group dialogicality vein and building on Mercer's and Wegerif's theoretic and analytic work. Students posted their own ideas first, used a "thinking together" approach based on "exploratory talk," and produced wiki content as a shared digital artifact.
