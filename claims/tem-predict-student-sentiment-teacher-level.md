## Evidence

### Samuel Falcon 2023

Samuel Falcon, Jaime Leon. (2023). How do teachers engaging messages affect students? A sentiment analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10230-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel structural equation model (ML-SEM) of data from 963 students of 39 mathematics teachers, examining the partial mediation model. The article reports TEM predict sentiment at both levels, with level-2 β of 0.95 and level-1 relations "all ... positive".

> "In general terms, it can be observed that TEM predict students’ sentiment well, especially at level 2, where the β takes values of 0.95. At level 1, all relations were also positive, with the strongest relationship between TEM and students’ MTL (β = 0.28)."
