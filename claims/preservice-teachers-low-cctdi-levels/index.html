Evidence¶

Temel 2014¶

Senar Temel. (2014). The effects of problem-based learning on pre-service teachers’ critical thinking dispositions and perceptions of problem-solving ability. South African Journal of Education, 34(1), Art. #769. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136398

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of CCTDI scores (Table 3): 90.90% of the experimental group and 92.59% of the control group fell in the low-scoring group at pre-test, with small shifts to the middle-scoring group at post-test; the article concludes levels are "generally low".

"When the CCTDI pre-test and post-test scores of pre-service teachers in both the experimental group and control group were examined, it was determined that the majority scored lowly and none of them scored highly."
