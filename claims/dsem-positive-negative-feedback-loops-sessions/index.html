Berweger 2024¶

Berweger, B., Breitwieser, J., Kracke, B., Brod, G., & Dietrich, J. (2024). Daily Affective-Motivational Experiences During Exam Preparation: Feedback Loops, Stability, and Change Over Time. PsychArchives preprint. https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12034/10953

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Time series analysis (DSEM in Mplus) of medical students' daily motivational and emotional states across a 40-day exam preparation period. The article states that "Dynamic Structural Equation Modeling in Mplus (DSEM) identified both positive and negative feedback loops within and between learning sessions". No effect sizes are printed.
