## Evidence

### Hao Xia-ying 2025

Hao Xia-ying, He Yun, Zhang Dong-xu, Li Xian-yin. (2025). Teacher-focused approach to foster student engagement — a hierarchical linear model based on Chinese college students’ psychological capital. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03683-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Random-coefficient HLM of 3,388 students nested in classes, with gender as control; the article reports self-efficacy (γ20 = 0.22, t = 10.67, p < 0.001) and that these dimensions "are also positively associated with student engagement". Coefficients are unstandardized, so no effect size is printed.

> "resilience (γ30 = 0.21, SE = 0.02, t = 11.44, p < 0.001), optimism (γ40 = 0.12, SE = 0.02, t = 7.21, p < 0.001), and responsibility (γ50 = 0.19, SE = 0.02, t = 9.42, p < 0.001) are also positively associated with student engagement"
