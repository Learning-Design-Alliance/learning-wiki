Evidence¶

Gombert 2024¶

Gombert, S., Fink, A., Giorgashvili, T., Jivet, I., Di Mitri, D., Yau, J., Frey, A., & Drachsler, H. (2024). From the Automated Assessment of Student Essay Content to Highly Informative Feedback: a Case Study. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00387-6

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Manual annotation study on a shared Excel sheet with 100 columns (learning tip by lecture topic combinations): 100 pilot essays were coded in parallel by two of seven coders and Cohen's Kappa was computed. The article reports the coders "determine[d] the overall agreement (0.87)" and resolved disagreements by discussion.

"We then used these individual values to determine the overall agreement (0.87). After this step, fundamental disagreements found during coding were resolved through discussion."
