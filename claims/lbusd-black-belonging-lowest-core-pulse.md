### Powell 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

> "The 2024 PULSE Survey confirmed these findings, maintaining that Black students reported lower scores in having a sense of belonging, as well as identity, agency, and sense of safety, than all other groups in grades 6 through 12."
