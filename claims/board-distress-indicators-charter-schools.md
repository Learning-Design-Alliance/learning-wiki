## Evidence

### National Charter School Resource Center 2021

National Charter School Resource Center, Evan, A., & Sullivan, H. (2021). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools, Part 2: The Roles and Perspectives of Charter School Leaders and Board Members. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616211

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the same interview study of leaders and board members of 11 distressed schools. The report found boards "lacked the capacity to govern" and that struggling schools "were often governed by boards without a long-term strategy for sustainability," including inability to plan beyond six months to a year.

> "Boards also lacked the capacity to govern, either due to a misunderstanding of the board's role related to accountability or diminished capacity in specific board functions."
