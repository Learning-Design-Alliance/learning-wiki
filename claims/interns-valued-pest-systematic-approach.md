### Davar Kusha 2023

Voluntary free-response evaluation comments collected at the end of the two one-hour sessions; interns also enjoyed reviewing the institutional antibiogram, and one noted PEST structured what was informally taught in training.

> "Ten interns commented on the usefulness of the approach. Specifically, interns appreciated the systematic approach to choosing antibiotics guided by reasoning, rather than reflexively choosing the correct antibiotic."
