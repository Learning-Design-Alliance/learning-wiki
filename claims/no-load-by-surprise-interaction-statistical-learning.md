## Evidence

### Marta Isabel Garrido 2016

Marta Isabel Garrido, Chee Leong James Teng, Jeremy Alexander Taylor, Elise Genevieve Rowe and Jason Brett Mattingley. (2016). Surprise responses in the human brain demonstrate statistical learning under high concurrent cognitive demand. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null result in Experiment 2 (17 participants): no significant load-by-surprise interaction. Load and three-way interaction clusters occurred only at uncorrected thresholds, and a Bayesian analysis found "the Null model explained the data better than the Load × Surprise model".

> "We did not ﬁnd a signi ﬁcant relative difference in the surprise effect for the low- and high-cognitive load conditions, despite verifying that the visual load effect was indeed effective"
