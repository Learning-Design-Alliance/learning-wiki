## Evidence

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

ROI analysis of fMRI data from 19 pilots (one High Expertise pilot excluded for deciding to land every time), with the no-decision control condition modeled as baseline; the printed "ES = 0.46" is a medium effect.

> "Lower brain activation was observed during all decisions made (correct and incorrect decisions from land and do not land conditions) in High Expertise pilots (0.97±0.80) compared to Moderate Expertise pilots (1.91±1.16) in bilateral caudate nucleus (t (17) = 2.1, p = .0497; ES = 0.46; see Figure 3)."
