Sensitivity analysis comparing providers with versus without a 6-month follow-up score in the cohort. The article reports patterned missingness, quoted as "significantly better retention (+3.03/month, SE=0.88, p=0.0007)", and entered this variable into the adjusted models.

> "the group with 6-month follow-up did not have a significantly better knowledge acquisition (+1.26, SE=3.69, p=0.7329), but demonstrated significantly better retention (+3.03/month, SE=0.88, p=0.0007)"
