## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The count of students in the appropriate age range for grades three through eight classified as grade 20 fell from 6,180 in 1992 to 773 in 1999, moving them into regular grades where they are counted as not tested or tested but excluded. [→ John Q. Easton 2000](#john-q-easton-2000)

John Q. Easton, Todd Rosenkranz, Anthony S. Bryk, Brian A. Jacob, Stuart Luppescu, Melissa Roderick. (2000). Annual CPS Test Trend Review, 1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Annual+CPS+Test+Trend+Review+1999

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of CPS student information system records (Part 1 and Table A). The brief reports "only 773 were" classified as grade 20 by 1999, and that these students are now counted either as not tested or as tested but excluded. No effect size is printed.

> "In 1992, 6,180 students (in the appropriate age range for grades three through eight) were classified as grade 20. By 1999, only 773 were (see Table A)."
