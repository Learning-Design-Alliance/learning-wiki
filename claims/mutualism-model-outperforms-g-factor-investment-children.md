## Evidence

### Kievit R. A. 2019

Kievit R. A., Hofman A. D., & Nation K. (2019). Mutualistic Coupling Between Vocabulary and Reasoning in Young Children: A Replication and Extension of the Study by Kievit et al. (2017). Psychological Science, 30(8), 1245–1252. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797619841265

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Replication model comparison in a longitudinal sample of 227 children (ages 6–8) tested at Waves 2–4, using latent change score models in lavaan. The article states "The mutualism model showed the best fit" with χ2(8) = 9.849, p = .276, RMSEA = 0.032.

> "The g-factor model fitted most poorly, followed by the investment model. The mutualism model showed the best fit, χ2(8) = 9.849, p = .276, root-mean-square error of approximation (RMSEA) = 0.032"
