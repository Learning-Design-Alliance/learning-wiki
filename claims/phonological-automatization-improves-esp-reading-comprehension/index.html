Evidence¶

Khatib 2011¶

Khatib, M., & Fat'hi, J. (2011). The Effect of Automatization of the Phonological Component on the Reading Comprehension of ESP Students. International Education Studies, 4(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v4n4p192

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized experiment with 60 ESP students assigned to experimental (pronunciation practice) or control (traditional translation) groups over one semester. The independent-samples T-test on post-test means (42.3 vs 35.7) showed the difference "exceeds the t-critical value"; no effect size is printed.

"with 58 degrees of freedom our t-observed at 0.05 level of significance, i.e., t(58)= 2.11, exceeds the t-critical value and means that the observed difference between groups is meaningful"
