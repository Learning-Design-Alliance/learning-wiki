Evidence¶

Mohammad Hadra 2026¶

Mohammad Hadra, Karleen Cambridge and Mostefa Mesbah. (2026). Evaluating the accuracy and reliability of AI content detectors in academic contexts. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00213-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Chi-square analysis of classification correctness across genre (57 humanities, 135 science texts) in the 192-text dataset. Table 4 reports Turnitin accuracy 0.86 humanities vs 0.51 science and Originality 0.96 vs 0.58; the article reports "Turnitin results yielded χ² (1) = 19.109, p = 0.000".

"Pearson’s Chi-square analyses confirmed that the association between text genre and classification accuracy was statistically significant for both detectors; Turnitin results yielded χ² (1) = 19.109, p = 0.000, and for Originality χ² (1) = 26.429, p = 0.000."

Related Claims¶

  • Originality outperformed Turnitin in overall accuracy and macro-average recall on a balanced EFL-relevant dataset, but both showed limited consistency across classes — related
  • Originality showed a borderline non-significant trend toward higher accuracy on professional than EFL student writing, while Turnitin showed no significant difference — related
  • Both detectors performed poorly on hybrid human–AI texts, with Originality showing near-zero recall for the Hybrid class — related
  • Detector accuracy declined significantly as text length increased, with both detectors performing best on short texts — related
  • A detector can achieve high aggregate accuracy while producing materially different over-detection rates across author groups — related
  • After humanization with Undetectable AI, fewer than 4% of AI-labeled rewrites remain detectable (FNR > 96%) for Pangram and GPTZero — related
  • Applied to 1,163 real master's theses, Pangram flagged 529 (45.5%) as containing AI-generated content, mostly at low-to-moderate levels — related
  • On fully AI-generated papers produced with ChatGPT Deep Research, Pangram achieved 65% strict accuracy while Turnitin, GPTZero and Copyleaks classified essentially all papers as false negatives — related
