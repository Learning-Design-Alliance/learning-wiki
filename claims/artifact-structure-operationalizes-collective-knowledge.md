## Evidence

### Gerry Stahl 2014

Gerry Stahl, Ulrike Cress, Nancy Law & Sten Ludvigsen. (2014). Analyzing the multidimensional construction of knowledge in diverse contexts. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. (2014) 9:1–6. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-014-9189-4

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Halatchliyski, Moskaliuk, Kimmerle, and Cress propose this methodological move for extending the concept of collective knowledge to long-term processes and large-scale network structures, exemplified by German Wikipedia articles categorized as educational and/or psychological.

> "in contrast to the analysis of interaction sequences— artifacts and their meaningful interconnected structure offer a unique way of operationalizing knowledge-related processes in collectives."

### Gerry Stahl 2014 (2)

Gerry Stahl, Ulrike Cress, Nancy Law & Sten Ludvigsen. (2014). Analyzing the multidimensional construction of knowledge in diverse contexts. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. (2014) 9:1–6. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-014-9189-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Network analysis of links between German Wikipedia articles defines clusters and categories such as pivotal and boundary-spanning articles; the authors then use the community-level results to analyze individual-level participation, confirming their hypotheses about different kinds of contributors posting to different categories of articles.

> "Using social-network analysis on the links between articles, it defines disciplinary and inter-disciplinary clusters of articles."
