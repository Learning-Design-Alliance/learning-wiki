# Teacher-rated social skills and problem behaviors correlated significantly with posttest decoding and spelling scores in severely struggling middle school readers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` SSRS Social Skills scores correlated with WJ III Basic Reading Skills Cluster scores (r = .33) and Problem Behavior scores correlated with Spelling scores (r = .36), both significant at p < .05. [→ Denton 2008](#denton-2008)

## Evidence

### Denton 2008

Denton, C. A., Wexler, J., Vaughn, S., & Bryan, D. (2008). Intervention Provided to Linguistically Diverse Middle School Students with Severe Reading Difficulties. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 23(2), 79–89. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.00266.x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory correlational analysis of posttest scores against SSRS teacher ratings for the 38 participants. Both correlations were "moderate but positive" per the authors (r = .33 and r = .36), who hypothesize disruptive and off-task behaviors reduced active engagement in intervention.

> "Signiﬁcant correlations were found between students’ SSRS Social Skills and WJ III Basic Reading Skills Cluster standard scores ( r = .33; p < .05) and between Problem Behavior and WJ III Spelling scores ( r = .36; p < .05)."
