### Engerer C 2019

Engerer C, Berberat PO, Dinkel A, Rudolph B, Sattel H, Wuensch A. (2019). Specific feedback makes medical students better communicators. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1470-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pre-post analysis of the intervention group (CST-behav, n = 34) using paired t-tests on the Com-ON-check domains rated by blinded psychologists; the article reports "three domains showing large effects and two medium effects" and a largest improvement of ES = 1.26 in handling patient's emotions.

> "Five out of seven domains improved significantly after training, with three domains showing large effects and two medium effects. The largest improvement was in handling patient's emotions (ES = 1.26)."
