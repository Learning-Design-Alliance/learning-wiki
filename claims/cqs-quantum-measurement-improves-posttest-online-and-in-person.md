## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with quantum measurement of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of pretest and post-test scores (Table II) across three yearly implementations: online class (N = 30), in-person class 1 (N = 23), and in-person class 2 (N = 28), with "reasonably high normalized gains" and per-question Cohen's d reported. The study is quasi-experimental, so other factors may have contributed.

> "Overall, the results are encouraging, and students performed well on the post -test, with reasonably high normalized gains. Effect sizes were generally medium to large for the online class and in-person class 1, and overall they were more modest for in-person class 2."
