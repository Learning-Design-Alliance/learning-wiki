## Evidence

### Luis E C Rocha 2018

Luis E C Rocha, Luana de F Nascimento. (2018). Assessing student's achievement gap between ethnic groups in Brazil. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation drawn from the state-stratified ENEM 2014 analysis: students exposed to similar low socio-economic conditions performed similarly irrespective of ethnicity. The article notes this does not reject the hypothesis that genetics may affect individual intellectual abilities.

> "Overall, our analysis provides evidence that socio-economic variables play a major role in student's performance in mathematics and writing examinations irrespectively of ethnic backgrounds and gives evidence that genetic factors may have little or no eﬀect on group performance."
