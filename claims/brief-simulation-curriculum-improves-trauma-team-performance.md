Uncontrolled before-and-after study of 137 trauma team members (staff surgeons, ED physicians, residents, physician assistants, RNs, RTs, technicians) comparing clinical outcome pre and post team training during actual trauma resuscitations. Found "reduction by 16% of mean overall ED resuscitation time, P < 0.05" and a 76% increase in near-perfect task completion frequency (P < 0.001).
