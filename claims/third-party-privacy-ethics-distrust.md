## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Interviews identified data sharing and data ownership as the two concerns underlying teaching staff distrust of third parties handling LA, including worries about personal data reaching advertising companies and data being sent overseas. [→ Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023](#asma-shannan-alzahrani-2023)

## Evidence

### Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023

Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 24 interviews. Participants consented to sharing educational data but not personally identifiable data; one participant worried student data "with names, mobile numbers and emails" could be sold to advertising companies, and another about "sending educational data out of Saudi Arabia".

> "In terms of trust in third parties, two concerns were identified in teaching staff interviews, including data sharing and ownership."
