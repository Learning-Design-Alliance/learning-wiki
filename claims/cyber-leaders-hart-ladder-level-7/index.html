Evidence¶

Cross et al. 2015¶

Donna Cross, Leanne Lester, Amy Barnes, Patricia Cardoso and Kate Hadwen. (2015). If it’s about me, why do it without me? Genuine student engagement in school cyberbullying education. International Journal of Emotional Education, 7(1), 35-51. www.um.edu.mt/cres/ijee. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085606

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview finding in which leaders self-assessed their level of authentic participation against Hart's (1992) Ladder of Participation. Many reported being happy with this level because it provided independence and the opportunity to shape their school's efforts.

"Most indicated their contribution would be categorised as Level 7 on the ladder, as they initiated and directed their own ideas and strategies, with adults involved only in a supportive or advisory role."
