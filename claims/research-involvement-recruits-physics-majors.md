## Evidence

### Samina S. Masood 2014

Samina S. Masood. (2014). How to Control a Decrease in Physics Enrollment?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.6442](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.6442)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The author's qualitative account of her own eight-year practice of involving students in research, term papers, presentations, assistantships and conference travel. She reports "the success rate is 10-20 percent from the introductory courses of Physics" at a total cost of around $5000. No comparison condition or formal evaluation is reported.

> "This strategy has been regularly used for eight years. Out of a small group of 10-15 students 1 to 2 are new students in the Physics program. Usually they either change major or add Physics as a dual major. It means the success rate is 10-20 percent from the introductory courses of Physics."
