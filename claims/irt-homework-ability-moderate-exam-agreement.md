### Gerd Kortemeyer 2014

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2014). Extending Item Response Theory to Online Homework. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pairwise comparison of ability estimates for the same 256 students in a large physics course, using cosine of the angle between exam and homework ability vectors. The article reports the correspondence is "not very large": "0.64 between exams and homework considering all attempts, and 0.67 when only considering the ﬁrst attempt."

> "The correspondence turns out to be not very large: it is 0.64 between exams and homework considering all attempts, and 0.67 when only considering the ﬁrst attempt."
