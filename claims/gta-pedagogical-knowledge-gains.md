# Pedagogical knowledge rose significantly from pretest to posttest after GTA workshops in a single-group pre-post study with no comparison group (d = 0.47), with statistically indistinguishable change across institutions

## Evidence

### Reeves Todd D. 2018

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on a five-item researcher-developed objective pedagogical knowledge test; the posttest mean was higher, F(1, 48) = 5.79, p < 0.05, ηp2 = 0.11. Table 3 reports overall d = 0.47.

> "Repeated-measures ANOVA showed an overall difference in the knowledge means over time, such that the posttest mean was higher, F(1, 48) = 5.79, p < 0.05, ηp2 = 0.11."

### Reeves Todd D. 2018 (2)

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same repeated-measures ANOVA, there was a main effect of institution, F(2, 48) = 12.90, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.35, but no time-by-institution interaction; knowledge change did not differ by site.

> "However, the interaction between time and institution was not significant, F(2, 48) = 0.71, p = 0.50, meaning that the degree of GTA knowledge change across the three institutions was statistically indistinguishable."
