Evidence¶

van der Zanden 2020¶

van der Zanden, P. J. A. C.; Meijer, P. C.; Beghetto, R. A. (2020). A review study about creativity in adolescence: Where is the social context? Thinking Skills and Creativity, 38, article 100702. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2020.100702

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing a quasi-experimental pre-post study (N = 127, China) by Wang and Murota (2016) on technical creativity, that "explicit creativity teaching by the teacher was more effective in increasing their creativity than peer instruction" for lower-level students; the two were equally effective on average.

"However, for lower-level students explicit creativity teaching by the teacher was more effective in increasing their creativity than peer instruction."
