# Students trained with MPathic-VR report significantly more positive attitudes toward their learning experience than computer-based learning students

## Evidence

### Kron Frederick W. MD 2017

Kron Frederick W. MD, Fetters Michael D. MD, MPH, MA, Scerbo Mark W. PhD, White Casey B. PhD, Lypson Monica L. MD, MHPE, FACP, Padilla Miguel A. PhD, Gliva-McConvey Gayle A., Belfore Lee A. `q3 · i?`

Independent t-test comparing aggregated 12-item attitudinal survey means (coefficient alpha = 0.95) between trial arms. MPathic-VR mean ratings were "significantly higher (e.g., more positive)"; Table 2 reports means of 4.97 versus 4.08.

> "The mean ratings aggregated across the 12 survey items were significantly higher (e.g., more positive) among students with MPathic-VR experience than for students with the CBL experience, t(413)=7.23, p<.0001, r2 =0.1123."
