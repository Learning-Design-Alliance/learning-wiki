## Evidence

### Jackson Mallory A. 2026

Jackson Mallory A., Liu Hongjiao, Moon Sungmin, Doherty Jennifer H., Wenderoth Mary Pat. (2026). Use of a specific set of learner-centered evidence-based teaching practices correlates with higher exam performance across seven STEM departments. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327269

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

LMM analysis of all 146 courses testing PC1 (intensity of use) quartiles against standardized exam scores; all PC1 quartile estimates were non-significant (e.g., Q2 estimate 0.22, p = 0.8014). The article reports "no significant correlation between the PC1 quartiles and student exam scores"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The overall results indicate no significant correlation between the PC1 quartiles and student exam scores when controlling for the PC2 quartiles, covariates, and random effects."
