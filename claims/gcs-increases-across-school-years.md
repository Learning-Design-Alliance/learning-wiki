## Evidence

### Kim S 2020

Kim S, Yang Eunbae B. (2020). Does group cohesion foster self-directed learning for medical students? A longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1962-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA of GCS scores across three years in the same 94-student longitudinal cohort. Means rose from 3.73 to 3.90 to 4.00; the article reports "the GCS scores in the third year were higher than those in the second year".

> "A repeated measures ANOVA was conducted and rendered for scores as a function of each year, F (2,182) = 10.192, MSe = .167, p < .01 (Table 3). An additional Bonferroni post-hoc test showed that the GCS scores in the third year were higher than those in the second year without significant differences between the two, and those in the first year were the lowest, p < .01."
