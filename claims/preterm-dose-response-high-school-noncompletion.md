# Individuals born extremely preterm are at increased odds of not graduating high school by age 22 in a dose-response pattern by gestational age

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After full adjustment, individuals born extremely preterm had higher odds of not obtaining a high school diploma by age 22 years (OR, 1.80) than full-term peers, with odds increasing as gestational age decreased. [→ Loose T 2025](#loose-t-2025)

## Evidence

### Loose T 2025

Loose T, Collet O, Nuyt AM, Goulet-Pelletier JC, Worrell FC, Côté S, Luu TM. (2025). Long-Term Educational Outcomes of Individuals Born Preterm. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.34918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Population-based case-control regression on the Quebec birth cohort of 297,820 individuals (1976–1995), minimally adjusted model with term births as reference; after full adjustment the extremely preterm OR was 1.80 (99% CI, 1.54-2.09), described as "highest odds of not obtaining a high school diploma by age 22 years." No standardized effect size is printed.

> "individuals born extremely preterm were at highest odds of not obtaining a high school diploma by age 22 years (OR, 1.88 [99% CI, 1.66-2.12]), followed by very preterm (OR, 1.43 [99% CI, 1.36-1.51]) and moderate-to-late preterm (OR, 1.22 [99% CI, 1.20-1.25])"
