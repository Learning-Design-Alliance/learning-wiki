## Evidence

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Video and audio coding at 5-minute intervals across the two-hour session (BORIS, Cohen's κ > 0.849 on a reliability sample). Group E was the only table not spending the majority of time in audible discussion; about 40% of their identifiable speech was inaudible.

> "we did observe that they spent over 50% of the their time in the lab in silence, as shown in Fig. 3"

### Anna McLean Phillips 2021 (2)

Anna McLean Phillips, Meagan Sundstrom, David G. Wu, and N. G. Holmes. (2021). Not engaging with problems in the lab: Students’ navigation of conflicting data and models. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020112)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Word count of each group's lab notes (equations and data tables excluded) complemented the video coding. The authors also note Group E did not obtain usable data until over 40 minutes in and did not ask for help.

> "That was further supported by their lab notes: their notes were shorter than any other group, as shown in Fig. 4."
