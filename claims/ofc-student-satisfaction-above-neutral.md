## Evidence

### Delirrooyfard Ali 2026

Delirrooyfard Ali, Sayyah Mehdi, Ahmady Soleiman, Kohan Noushin. (2026). Enhancing diagnostic logic in high-acuity care: evidence from an online flipped classroom intervention in emergency medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09094-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-sample t-test on a 17-item satisfaction questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha = 0.956) completed anonymously by the 45 students at course end. The article reports a mean of 3.47 (SD = 0.71) and "a statistically significant positive perception of the training course (t = 4.34, df = 44, P < 0.001)"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The results indicated a statistically significant positive perception of the training course (t = 4.34, df = 44, P < 0.001). The mean difference of 0.47 (95% CI: 0.27–0.68) confirms that the intervention successfully met the students' educational expectations."
