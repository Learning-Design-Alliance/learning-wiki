The Formation of Students’ Foreign Language Communicative Competence during the Learning Process of the English Language through Interactive Learning Technologies (The Study on the Basis of Kazan Federal University). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control-phase re-testing of the socio-cultural and strategic components. Value ranking reached 87% (up from 67%), traditions/customs 58% (up from 34%), strategies 86% (up from 62%), and the social/socio-cultural component 82% (up from 55%); the article notes the greatest development in the experimental group. No effect sizes are printed.
