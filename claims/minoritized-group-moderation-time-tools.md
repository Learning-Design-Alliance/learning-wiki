## Evidence

### Hensley Lauren 2021

Hensley Lauren, Kulesza Amy, Peri Joshua, Brady Anna C., Wolters Christopher A., Sovic David, Breitenberger Caroline. (2021). Supporting Undergraduate Biology Students' Academic Success: Comparing Two Workshop Interventions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-03-0068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SEM interaction analyses tested intervention condition by minoritized group status with covariates including gender, first-generation status, ACT score, GPA, and exam 1 score. The moderation on the time management tools factor favored minoritized students in the Metacognition+TM condition; no effect size is printed.

> "Additionally, group status moderated the effect of the intervention, as the Metacognition+TM intervention was especially effective in increasing use of time management tools by students from minoritized groups."
