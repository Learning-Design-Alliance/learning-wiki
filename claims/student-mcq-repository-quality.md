## Evidence

### Enrique Abad 2015

Enrique Abad, Pilar Suárez, Julia Gil. (2015). A game-based educational method relying on student-generated questions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.06439](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.06439)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Instructor assessment of suitability of MCQs uploaded by 10 participating students (25 questions each on chapters 1-6); questions were discarded for unsuitable difficulty, ill-posed or ambiguous wording, or poor language, and many were amended. Sixteen of twenty chapter-7 questions were later retained, giving 371 potential exam MCQs.

> "Out of the 550 questions prepared by the students, 195 were discarded, the ﬁnal size of the repository thus became equal to 355"

### Enrique Abad 2015 (2)

Enrique Abad, Pilar Suárez, Julia Gil. (2015). A game-based educational method relying on student-generated questions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.06439](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.06439)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative characterization of the repository reported in the methodology section: questions referred to instructor-explained topics, solved classroom problems and lab work, and in many cases students authored rather interesting questions. The exam MCQs selected from the repository were checked to be of comparable difficulty across groups (average taxonomic level 3.20 experimental vs 3.05 control, no statistically significant difference).

> "The questions written by the students indeed turned out to be very diverse in nature and could be classiﬁed in diﬀerent taxonomic levels of knowledge 10,13,14"
