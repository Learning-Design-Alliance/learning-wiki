Evidence¶

Derek M. Houston 2012¶

Derek M. Houston, Jessica Stewart, Aaron Moberly, George Hollich, and Richard T. Miyamoto. (2012). Word learning in deaf children with cochlear implants: effects of early auditory experience. Dev Sci. 2012 May ; 15(3): 448–461. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2012.01140.x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multiple linear regression analyses tested Age at CI, Aided PTA, and Hearing Age on mean longest look differences in 25 CI children. The analyses revealed a significant effect of pre-CI hearing levels on performance; a scatterplot is displayed in Figure 3a.

"the regression analyses revealed a significant effect of pre-CI hearing levels on performance, suggesting that having some degree of hearing before implantation facilitates word learning."
