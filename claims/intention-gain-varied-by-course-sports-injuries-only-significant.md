## Evidence

### Bakwa Kanyinga Felly 2023

Bakwa Kanyinga Felly, Gogovor Amédé, Dofara Suélène Georgina, Gadio Souleymane, Tremblay Martin, Daniel Sam J., Rivest Louis-Paul, Légaré France. (2023). Evaluating the impact of continuing professional development courses on physician behavioral intention: a pre-post study with follow-up at six months. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04597-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-course Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons among pre-post completers (Table 3). The article cautions the sports injuries course sample of 24 participants "may not, however, have been sufficient to detect a significant difference" for other courses.

> "Mean difference in before/after intention varied across individual courses from − 0.31 (p = 0.17) to 2.25 (p = 0.50). The difference was significant only for the course on sports injuries 0.60 (p = 0.01)"

### Bakwa Kanyinga Felly 2023 (2)

Bakwa Kanyinga Felly, Gogovor Amédé, Dofara Suélène Georgina, Gadio Souleymane, Tremblay Martin, Daniel Sam J., Rivest Louis-Paul, Légaré France. (2023). Evaluating the impact of continuing professional development courses on physician behavioral intention: a pre-post study with follow-up at six months. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04597-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA and exploratory ICC analyses of intention across the nine course cohorts. The article reports ICC estimates "of 0.14 before (Appendix 2) and 0.004 after the courses", suggesting post-course cohort intention scores levelled out.

> "Analysis of variance (ANOVA) showed significant variation of intention, both between course cohorts pre-courses (F value = 3.75; p = 0.0005) and before/after courses (F value = 2.57; p = 0.01). However, intention after courses did not vary significantly across courses (F value = 0.73; p = 0.67)"
