## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Multiple-choice Assessment for Upper-division Electricity and Magnetism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the MC CUE in the E&M 1 sample. The article notes the CUE violates the single-construct assumption, so alpha is a conservative measure; Ferguson's Delta was 0.95 for both MC and FR, similar to the previously reported FR value of 0.99.

> "Using the point value of each item to calculate alpha, we ﬁnd α = 0.80, which is also the commonly accepted cutoff for a good value [8]; thus the MC CUE has an acceptable level of internal consistency."
