`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

County-level analysis shown in Figure 8 of CALPADS enrollment data for 2022–23. The report identifies northern counties (Del Norte, Modoc, Plumas) and central/eastern counties (Inyo, Kings, Stanislaus) each with more than 22% of their ever-EL population designated as LTEL7.

> "many of the less populous and rural California counties had higher percentages of students designated as LTEL7 among their total ever-EL population, even if their overall numbers of students designated as LTEL7 were relatively low."
