Evidence¶

Nouri Haniyeh 2025¶

Nouri Haniyeh, Mahdavi Abdollah, Abedi Ali, Mohammadnia Alireza, Hamedan Mahnaz, Amanzadeh Masoud. (2025). Performance of large language models in medical licensing examinations: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.36

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis by question language (English n=21, Spanish n=11, Japanese n=10, Chinese n=7, Polish n=6, German n=5, other n=3 studies). The review reports the range and states the differences "were not statistically significant (P=0.17)"; equivalence was not tested.

"In terms of question language, pooled LLM accuracy ranged from 62% to 77%, with the highest accuracy observed for German and the lowest for Polish. These differences, however, were not statistically significant (P=0.17)."
