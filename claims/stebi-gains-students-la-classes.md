## Evidence

### Andrew Ferstl 2010

Andrew Ferstl. (2010). Teaching and Learning Science with Learning Assistants. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.3789](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.3789)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post STEBI administration to students in the LA-supported 2009-2010 classes. The article reports descriptive item-level changes, including the example item rising from 5% to 64% agreement; questions 10 and 13 saw negative change. No test statistic is printed.

> "21 of the 23 questions on this survey either saw a positive change or no change.  (Questions 10 and 13 saw a negative change.)  One example is the question "I know the steps necessary to teach science concepts effectively".  Pre only 5% answered "agree or strongly agree"; Post 64% answered "agree or strongly agree"."
