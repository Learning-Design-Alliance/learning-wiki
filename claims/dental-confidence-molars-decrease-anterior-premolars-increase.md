## Evidence

### Tavşan Oğuz 2026

Tavşan Oğuz, Kara Ongun Seden, Aktaş İlkim, Çelikkol Berk. (2026). Dental students’ self-confidence toward endodontics according to educational stages: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09186-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal tooth-type analysis in the same 98-student cohort (Friedman test). The article reports molar confidence declined while "maxillary anterior, mandibular anterior, and premolar teeth" showed increases; maxillary premolars showed no significant difference (p = 0.061).

> "self-confidence for maxillary molars decreased from 3.08 ± 1.03 in the preclinical stage to 2.65 ± 0.96 in the clinical internship, and for mandibular molars from 3.29 ± 1.08 to 2.81 ± 1.08"
