## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, C. R., Vaughn, S., & McClelland, A. M. (2017). Intensive Reading Interventions for Inadequate Responders in Grades K–3: A Synthesis. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 191–210. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714446

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Study in Table 7 of the synthesis: grade 1 inadequate responders were assigned to one or two daily 30-min Tier 3 sessions targeting phonics, word recognition, fluency, and comprehension; controls were at-risk students randomly assigned to receive school services. The two-session condition showed "WRMT-R Word Attack: d = −0.66"; the one-session condition showed "WRMT-R Word Attack: d = 0.81".
