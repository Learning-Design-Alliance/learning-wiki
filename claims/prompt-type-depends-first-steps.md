### Nandana Weliweriya 2016

Nandana Weliweriya, Justyna P. Zwolak, Eleanor C. Sayre, Dean Zollman. (2016). Varied reasoning schema in students’ written solutions. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1611.02262](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1611.02262)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis comparing networks for three problems of different prompt types: Problem 2 (trivial math procedures type) and Problem 3 (diagram type) alongside Problem 1. Central nodes differed: E2 (take the derivatives) became central in Problem 3 because students were asked to find the equation of motion, and C10/C11 boundary-condition codes appeared.

> "The ﬁrst few problem solving steps that students use – the ﬁrst few nodes in the network – depend on the kind of prompt that the problem uses. In particular, we identiﬁed three diﬀerent types of prompts that suggest to students how they should start the problem:"
