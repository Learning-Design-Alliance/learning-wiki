# Prior randomized studies of Future Forward/SPARK met WWC standards Without Reservations and found positive impacts on literacy and attendance

## Subclaims

`q3 i1` Three prior impact studies of FF/SPARK met WWC evidence standards Without Reservations, with reported impacts including 0.12 SD on reading, 0.23 SD on literacy, and 4.53 fewer days absent. [→ Jones 2021](#jones-2021)

## Evidence

### Jones 2021

Jones, C. J., & Li, D. (2021). Testing the Impact and Scalability of the Future Forward Literacy Program. University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. https://uwm.edu/sreed

`q3 · i1` · `review · r?`

The report's summary of its own prior evaluations (Table 1): the 2011-13 pilot found a 0.12 SD reading impact; the 2013-15 RCT found 0.23 SD literacy gains and 4.53 fewer days absent; the 2018-19 RD study found a non-significant literacy impact (β = 0.16, p = 0.394).

> "Each of these three studies meets the Institute for Education Sciences What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) evidence standards Without Reservations (WWC, 2020)."

### Curtis J. Jones and DongMei Li 2021

Curtis J. Jones and DongMei Li, University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. (2021). Testing the Impact and Scalability of the Future Forward Literacy Program. https://uwm.edu/sreed

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

This report narrates the prior i3-funded RCT by Jones & Christian (2020), which found literacy gains of "0.23 standard deviations" and 4.5 fewer days absent after two years of tutoring; the report states the study met WWC standards Without Reservations.

> "Specifically, after two years of tutoring, participants’ literacy assessment scores improved by 0.23 standard deviations. Further, SPARK/FF students were absent from school 4.5 fewer days than students in the BAU condition."
