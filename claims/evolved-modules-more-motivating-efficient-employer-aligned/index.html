Evidence¶

Warin 2011¶

Warin, B., Kolski, C., & Sagar, M. (2011). Framework for the evolution of acquiring knowledge modules to integrate the acquisition of high-level cognitive skills and professional competencies. Computers and Education, 57(2), 1595-1614. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2011.02.013

q2 · i? · design · r?

Three case studies of disciplinary module evolution are presented and analyzed. The authors report that the evolved modules produced teaching that was "more motivating and more efficient" with results "closer to what employers are asking." No effect sizes are printed.

"We show that the lessons taught in these three modules are more motivating and more efficient, producing results that are closer to what employers are asking of their employees."
