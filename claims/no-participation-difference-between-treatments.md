## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Perez Kathryn E. 2010

Perez Kathryn E., Strauss Eric A., Downey Nicholas, Galbraith Anne, Jeanne Robert. (2010). Does Displaying the Class Results Affect Student Discussion during Peer Instruction? CBE Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.09-11-0080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of clicker participation between the bar-graph and control treatments across the 15 study questions found no significant difference on any of the three participation measures (p > 0.05 in all three cases). No effect size was printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in clicker participation among the treatments in overall clicker participation, participation on first click, or participation on second click (p > 0.05 in all three cases)."
