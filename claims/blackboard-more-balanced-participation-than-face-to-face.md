This is the article's literature-based assertion, attributed to Hismanoglu (2012), about equal participation in Blackboard-based instruction; the article offers it as an interpretation and cites Warschauer (1996), Alvine (2000) and Farooq and Gulzar (2011) as congruent conclusions, reporting no direct measurement of participation balance in its own data.

> "whereas face-to-face discussions tend to be relatively unbalanced, -with one or two participants dominating class discussions, communication in Blackboard-based instruction features more balanced participation, with the participants sharing discussions more equally."
