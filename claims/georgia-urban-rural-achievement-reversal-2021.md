County-level analysis of Georgia math achievement rates by 2013 Rural-Urban Continuum classification (Figures 8-9). The quote states the reversal directly: "rural counties slightly edged ahead" by 2021; printed means are urban 66.25% vs rural 62.06% in 2019 and 47.53% vs 50.34% in 2021.

> "Notably, this data suggests that while urban counties had an advantage in 2019, by 2021, rural counties slightly edged ahead, reflecting the differential impacts of the pandemic on educational structures."
