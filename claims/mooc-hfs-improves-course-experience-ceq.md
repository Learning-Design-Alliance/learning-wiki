## Evidence

### Yang K 2026

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Course Experience Questionnaire with five subscales (Good Teaching, Clear Goals and Standards, Appropriate Workload, Appropriate Assessment, Generic Skills Development), Cronbach's α 0.79–0.87. The "experimental group reported significantly better experiences in all subscales" with the largest effect on Good Teaching (d = 2.73).

> "Compared with the control group, the experimental group reported significantly better experiences in all subscales (all P < 0.001) with effect sizes (Cohen's d) ranging from 1.27 to 2.73."
