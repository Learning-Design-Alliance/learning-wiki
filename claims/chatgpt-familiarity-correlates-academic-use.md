## Evidence

### Singh 2023

Singh, H.; Tayarani-Najaran, M.-H.; Yaqoob, M. (2023). Exploring Computer Science Students' Perception of ChatGPT in Higher Education: A Descriptive and Correlation Study. Educ. Sci. 2023, 13, 924. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13090924

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of scaled answers from 430 students in the Discussion section; the correlation between familiarity (Q2) and academic use (Q3) was significant and positive, described as the strongest correlation observed with Question 2.

> "The significant positive correlation between the answers to these two questions simply suggests that the more familiar the students are with the tool, the more they use it for academic purposes, which is easy to understand."
