# Students studying musculoskeletal anatomy with an interactive 3D digital model show greater pre-to-post test score improvement than students studying textbook images

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The 3D Model Group showed a significantly greater change from pre- to post-studying test scores than the Textbook Image Group. [→ Safir AH 2026](#safir-ah-2026)

## Evidence

### Safir AH 2026

Safir AH, Bird MM, Orczykowski ME. (2026). Development of effective 3D digital models for first-time learners of musculoskeletal anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70220

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot trial with 81 undergraduates randomly assigned to study hip and knee muscles for 10 minutes with either the 3D model (n = 39) or textbook images (n = 42). The "3D Model Group (mean change = +9.26 points [+44.08%])" outperformed the textbook group's +5.05 points.

> "There was a significant difference in the change from Pre- to Post-Studying Test scores based on group (t = 4.924, p < 0.0001; 95% CI: 2.51, 5.91), such that the 3D Model Group (mean change = +9.26 points [+44.08%]) showed greater improvements on the test than the Textbook Image Group (mean change = +5.05 points [+24.04%])."
