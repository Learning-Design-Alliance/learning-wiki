Blobstein 2025¶

Blobstein, A., Facciotti, M. T., Igo, M., Karger, D., Ravi, P., Sripathi, K., & Gal, K. (2025). #let's-discuss: Analyzing Students' Use of Emoji when Interacting with Course Readings. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00401-5

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Paragraph-level correlation analysis over an N × 11 matrix of emoji frequencies per paragraph in the FYBIO readings, shown as a heat map in Fig. 4. The article prints a correlation effect size of 0.79 ("the 0.79 correlation score between the emoji #interesting-topic and #real-world-application"), interpreted as related student reactions.

"An example for explanation (a) would be the 0.79 correlation score between the emoji #interesting-topic and #real-world-application."
