## Evidence

### Gorard et al. 2015

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2015). Accelerated Reader Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.gl-assessment.co.uk/products/new-group-reading-test/test-detail

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group comparisons on the NGRT: boys (95 AR versus 82 control) printed effect sizes +0.17 and +0.18; girls (80 AR versus 82 control) +0.35 and +0.37. The report hedges that AR "seems to be more effective for girls than boys".

> "Interestingly, AR seems to be more effective for girls than boys, although the results are positive for both (Tables 8 to 11)."
