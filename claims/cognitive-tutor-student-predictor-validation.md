Kasia Muldner · Winslow Burleson · Brett Van de Sande · Kurt VanLehn (2010). An analysis of students’ gaming behaviors in an intelligent tutoring system: predictors and impacts. User Model User-Adap Inter. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-010-9086-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article applies the same detector and analyses to Algebra Cognitive Tutor log data from 53 students and 775 problems. The regression accounted for 42% of variance, with student's standardized coefficient (.52) exceeding problem's (.35); self-correlation also favored students (r. = .973 vs. r = .55).

> "A linear regression with Per Gamingsp (Eq. 1, Sect. 4) as the dependent variable, and as the independent variables, student (Eq. 2, Sect. 4) and problem (Eq. 3, Sect. 4) produced a significant model (F = 4588, p < 0.001)"
