Evidence¶

Hao 2025¶

Hao, Z.; Cao, J.; Li, R.; Yu, J.; Liu, Z.; Zhang, Y. (2025). Mapping Student-AI Interaction Dynamics in Multi-Agent Learning Environments: Supporting Personalised Learning and Reducing Performance Gaps. https://osf.io/preprints/eduRxiv

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Lag sequential analysis of coded dialogue from the MAIC course (19,365 interaction entries) identified six significant behavioral chains involving AI teacher, AI peers, and human students, from which the authors derived "two main patterns: co-construction of knowledge and co-regulation".

"Based on the significant behavior transition sequences listed above, we have identified two main patterns: co-construction of knowledge and co-regulation. These two patterns may sometimes stand alone, as in (TB1 -> SB4 -> ASB2), but often they can appear in combination"
