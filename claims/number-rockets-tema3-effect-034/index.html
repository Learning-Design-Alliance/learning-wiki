Evidence¶

Number Rockets 2020¶

Number Rockets. (2020). Number Rockets: first-grade math tutoring program description. Tutoring provided through AIR. https://www.air.org

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

One qualifying study evaluated Number Rockets with students below the 38th percentile in math; controls received no tutoring or organized remediation. The program "scored significantly higher than controls, with an effect size of +0.34" on TEMA-3, qualifying it as Strong evidence.

"On TEMA-3 tests, students in Number Rockets scored significantly higher than controls, with an effect size of +0.34. This qualifies the program for the "Strong" evidence category."

Rolfhus 2013¶

Rolfhus, E., Gersten, R., Clarke, B., Decker, L., Wilkins, C., & Dimino, J. (2013). From Efficacy Trial to Large Scale Effectiveness Trial: A Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First Graders with Difficulties in Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20124007/

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

School-level matched-pair randomized effectiveness trial in 76 schools across 4 urban districts; 994 at-risk first graders (615 intervention, 379 control) analyzed with a three-level HLM on multiply imputed data. The estimated impact was 4.28 points with "Effect sizea" of 0.34 favoring intervention.

"Participation had a statistically significant difference (g= 0.34) on the TEMA–3 scores favoring the intervention group over the control group (p < .001)."
