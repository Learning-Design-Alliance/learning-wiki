Results section reporting the 2021 cohort of 18 students in Electric Circuit Analysis, with self-efficacy measured by weekly scores and 4-week moving averages and visualized in Figure 4. The study reports that "Students have improved their self-efficacy with progressive scores" and that from the sixth week students overcame existing and current achievements. No effect size or inferential test statistic is printed; significance was defined as weekly scores outperforming the moving average.

> "With the content of the videos tailored by a course lecturer, the students attempt to study with MATLAB® – a computer simulation program for engineering. Students have improved their self-efficacy with progressive scores. Since the sixth week, students have overcome their existing and current achievements."
