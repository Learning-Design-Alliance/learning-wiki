Evidence¶

Swain 2025¶

Swain, W., Leung-Gagné, M., Maier, A., & Rubinstein, C. (2025). Community schools impact on student outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/541.498

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Matched difference-in-differences analysis of first-cohort CCSPP schools reports overall ELA gains of 0.05 SD (about 36 days of learning per the executive summary) that were "not statistically significant overall", with subgroup-level significance as printed; no standardized effect size for the subgroup contrasts is printed.

"While improvements in English language arts (ELA) were not statistically significant overall, they were notably significant for English learners (p <0.05) and for socioeconomically disadvantaged and Black students (p <0.10)"
