Evidence¶

Sare Şengül 2011¶

Sare Şengül. (2011). Effects of Concept Cartoons on Mathematics Self-Efficacy of 7th Grade Students. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 11(4), 2305-2313. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/estp

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-group t-test on pre-test scores of both groups: no significant difference, t(92)= -.346, p>.05; Levene's test (F =1.733 p=.191) indicated equal variances, so groups were comparable before application. No effect size is printed.

"Results of the independent group t-test conducted on the mathematics self-efficacy perception scale pre-test scores of the experimental and control groups showed no significant difference at a statistical level of significance of 0.05 in terms of the mathematics self-efficacy perceptions between the two groups [t(92)= -.346, p>.05]."
