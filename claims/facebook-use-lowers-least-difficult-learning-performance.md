### James Wakefield & Jessica K. Frawley 2020

James Wakefield & Jessica K. Frawley. (2020). How does students’ general academic achievement moderate the implications of social networking on specific levels of learning performance? Computers & Education. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360131519302477?via%3Dihub

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 505 undergraduate introductory accounting students linked to institutional final examination records, analysed with OLS regressions. The full-sample results show "total time on Facebook is related to significantly lower performance in the least difficult levels of learning" (understanding and application).
