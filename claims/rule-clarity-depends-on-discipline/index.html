Martin Lnenicka and Petr Coufal 2026¶

Martin Lnenicka and Petr Coufal. (2026). Navigating AI in STEM: what secondary students actually do with generative AI-driven tools. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00637-8

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey Question C2 analysis (Fig. 4) in the n = 416 sample. Even in computer science the distribution is bimodal: "22.8% of students report complete clarity, 19.0% state that rules are not at all clear." The article calls this a structural misalignment between regulation and communication.

"In mathematics, the largest plurality (37.3%) reported a complete lack of clarity, mirroring the basic natural sciences (40.9%). A Chi-square test of independence confirmed that rule clarity is significantly dependent on the specific academic discipline (χ2 = 101.65, df = 12, p < .001)."
