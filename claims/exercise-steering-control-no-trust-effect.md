# A control mechanism for steering recommended exercises does not significantly change adolescents' trust in an e-learning platform

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · no dated study recorded · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adolescents who could steer their mastery level with a slider did not differ significantly in trust (measured directly or as competence, benevolence, integrity, intention to return, and perceived transparency) from those without control. [→ Jeroen Ooge 2023](#jeroen-ooge-2023)

## Evidence

### Jeroen Ooge 2023

Jeroen Ooge, Leen Dereu, and Katrien Verbert. 2023. Steering Recommendations and Visualising Its Impact: Effects on Adolescents' Trust in E-Learning Platforms. In 28th International Conference on Intelligent User Interfaces (IUI '23). https://doi.org/10.1145/3581641.3584046

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled experiment comparing the none and control groups with one-sided t-tests and Mann-Whitney U tests. "one-sided tests did not reveal statistical differences between none and control (𝑝 < 0.05)"; only transparency and preference revision were borderline non-significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The first column in Table 1 shows that one-sided tests did not reveal statistical differences between none and control (𝑝 < 0.05). Thus, our sample did not provide evidence against equal means for any measured construct."
