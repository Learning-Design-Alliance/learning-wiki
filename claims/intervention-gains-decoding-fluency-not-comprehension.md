### Kulesz 2024

Kulesz, P. A., Roberts, G. J., Francis, D. J., Cirino, P., Walczak, M., & Vaughn, S. (2024). Latent profiles as predictors of response to instruction for students with reading difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(3). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000832

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Outcome results from the Vaughn et al. (2019) RCT on which this sample was based, reported in the intervention section: significant effects on one decoding measure (ES = 0.58) and one fluency measure (ES = 0.46), with no significant group differences on remaining measures including reading comprehension.

> "Study findings suggested that the treatment condition had statistically significantly higher outcomes on one decoding (ES = 0.58) and one reading fluency (ES = 0.46) measure than the BaU condition. There were no statistically significant group differences on the remaining decoding, fluency, reading comprehension, or spelling measures."
