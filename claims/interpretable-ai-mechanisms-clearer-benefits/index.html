The review's RQ2 synthesis states that "interpretable, well-specified AI mechanisms tend to produce clearer learning benefits", citing rule-based DDA outperforming more complex Bayesian alternatives when the performance-difficulty relationship was transparent, and a debiased SA model maintaining accuracy.

"An important finding is that interpretable, well-specified AI mechanisms tend to produce clearer learning benefits than technically sophisticated but pedagogically underspecified ones."
