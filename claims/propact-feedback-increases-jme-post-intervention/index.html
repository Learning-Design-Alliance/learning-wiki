q3 · i? · causal · r2

Process-level analysis addressing RQ2: JME aggregated two minutes before versus two minutes after feedback showed a significant increase (t[df = 25] = 19.33, p<.0001) in the same 26-dyad within-subjects study. No effect size printed.

"Similarly a test between the JME values before (average for the two minutes before) and after (averaged for two minutes after) the feedback was provided showed a significant increase in the JME of the dyads (t[df = 25] = 19.33, p<.0001, figure 4b)."
