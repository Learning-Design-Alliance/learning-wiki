## Evidence

### Debanjana Kar 2025

Debanjana Kar, Leopold Böss, Dacia Braca, Sebastian Dennerlein, Nina Hubig, Philipp Wintersberger, and Yufang Hou. (2025). MathBuddy: A Multimodal System for Affective Math Tutoring. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19993 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.19993](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2508.19993)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 460 student utterances (235 Emotion OFF, 225 Emotion ON) labeled for engagement, boredom, and neutral intensity; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests across three textual emotion metrics found "no statistical significant differ- ences between conditions across any metric". Boredom predictions were scored 0 throughout and omitted.

> "Results revealed no statistical significant differ- ences between conditions across any metric, sug- gesting that the Emotion ON condition did not pro- duce measurable aggregate changes in emotional expression within student text responses."
