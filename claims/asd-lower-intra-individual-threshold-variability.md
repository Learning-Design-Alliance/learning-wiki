### Sapey-Triomphe 2023

Sapey-Triomphe, L.-A., Sanchez, G., Hénaff, M.-A., Sonié, S., Schmitz, C., & Mattout, J. (2023). Disentangling sensory precision and prior expectation of change in autism during tactile discrimination. npj Science of Learning, 8:54. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00207-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment I, each participant's detection threshold was measured four times; the ASD group's "intra-individual standard deviation" was far lower (0.01 ± 0.01 vs 0.06 ± 0.04), t(61) = 5.8, p < 0.0001, d = 1.46, with extreme Bayesian evidence for the difference.
