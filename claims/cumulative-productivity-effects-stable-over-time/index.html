Cumulative meta-analysis ordered by publication year (2022–2025), adding studies chronologically to update the pooled estimate. The article reports "the cumulative estimate stabilized at a small, consistent productivity gain (g= 0.331) as evidence accumulated" and notes this suggests raw model capability or AI literacy may not be the primary drivers of productivity gains.
