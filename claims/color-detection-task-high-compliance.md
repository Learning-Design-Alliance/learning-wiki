Jianyi Liu, Tengwen Fan, Yan Chen and Jingjing Zhao. (2023). Seeking the neural representation of statistical properties in print during implicit processing of visual words. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00209-3

Behavioral results from 45 participants across the equal probability and three oddball blocks (Table 1): hit rates 99% in every block, false alarms <1%, press latencies 369.00–398.22 ms. Descriptive only; no effect sizes.
