## Evidence

### Zwolak 2015

Zwolak, J. P. and Manogue, C. A. (2015). Assessing student reasoning in upper-division electricity and magnetism at Oregon State University. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of FR midtest (N = 86) and MC midtest (N = 21) scores at OSU in Fall 2013. The authors report a "7-fold increase" on this question, consistent with recognizing answers being easier than generating them; on Q7 students scored 52.5% (FR) vs 69% (MC).

> "On the ﬁrst FR midtest OSU students scored on this problem on average 7.2± 1.9% (N = 86). On the MC version the average score on this question increased to 50± 9.4%."
