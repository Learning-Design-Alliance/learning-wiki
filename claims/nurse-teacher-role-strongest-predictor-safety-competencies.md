Kohanová Dominika, Gurková Elena, Palese Alvisa, Bartoníčková Daniela, Mazalová Lenka, Özsaban Aysel, Bayram Aysun, Basaran‐Acil Seher, Ispir Demir Öznur, Chiappinotto Stefania. (2026). `q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate general linear model with Pillai's Trace testing five CLES-T dimensions against six H-PEPSS domains. The article reports "The role of the nurse teacher showed the largest multivariate effect size" (ηp2 = 0.057) while leadership style was non-significant (p = 0.135).

> "The role of the nurse teacher showed the largest multivariate effect size (V = 0.057, F(6, 1388) = 13.87, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.057). In contrast, leadership style of the ward manager did not yield evidence of multivariate differences, V = 0.007, F(6, 1388) = 1.63, p = 0.135, ηp2 = 0.007."
