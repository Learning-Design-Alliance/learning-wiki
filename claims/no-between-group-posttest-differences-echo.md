## Evidence

### Ding Kewen 2024

Ding Kewen, Chen Mingjing, Li Ping, Xie Zichuan, Zhang Haorong, Kou Ruixing, Xu Jionghui, Zou Ting, Luo Zhen, Song Haibo. (2024). The effect of simulation of sectional human anatomy using ultrasound on students' learning outcomes and satisfaction in echocardiography education: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05337-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group Mann-Whitney comparison in the pilot RCT (traditional n = 8, digital n = 10). The primary outcome, post-test 1 total, showed "no significant difference" (P = 0.191); the section-position sub-score favored the digital group but was not significant (P = 0.051).

> "In the post-test 1, the digital group performed better in terms of the position and orientation of the probe (hereinafter referred to as “section position”), but no significant difference was shown (traditional group: 31.7 ± 22.3 vs. digital group: 54.4 ± 19.3, P = 0.051)."
