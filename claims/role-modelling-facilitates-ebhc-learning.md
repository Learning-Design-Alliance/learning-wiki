Theme derived from thematic content analysis of coordinator interviews; one participant (P10) stated "The most important way of teaching students evidence based medicine is by example". Clinicians on the wards were described as role models whom students look up to.

> "Role modelling of EBHC within the clinical setting emerged as an important facilitator."
