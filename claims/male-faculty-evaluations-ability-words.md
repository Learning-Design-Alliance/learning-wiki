## Evidence

### Heath JK 2019

Heath JK, Weissman GE, Clancy CB, Shou H, Farrar JT, Dine CJ. (2019). Assessment of Gender-Based Linguistic Differences in Physician Trainee Evaluations of Medical Faculty Using Automated Text Mining. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2019.3520

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects logistic regression (clustered on faculty) of n-gram frequencies from 6840 complete evaluations of 521 department of medicine faculty, with faculty gender as the dependent variable. The article reports these odds ratios as "associated with evaluations of male faculty" at the Benjamini-Hochberg threshold; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "written evaluations including art (OR, 7.78; 95% CI, 1.01-59.89), trials (OR, 4.43; 95% CI, 1.34-14.69), master (OR, 4.24; 95% CI, 1.69-10.63), and humor (OR, 2.32; 95% CI, 1.44-3.73) were associated with evaluations of male faculty"
