# The bias-adjusted mean moderator effect in STEM education meta-analyses is g = 0.27 with substantial heterogeneity, and the published record shows overwhelming evidence of publication-bias-type selection

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` RoBMA-PSMA yielded a model-averaged overall moderator effect of g = 0.27 (95% CrI [0.20, 0.34]) with decisive evidence for heterogeneity (τ = 0.48). [→ Bicakci 2026](#bicakci-2026)
`q3 i?` The analysis provided overwhelming evidence for publication-bias-type selection (BFpb = 4.72 × 10^36), consistent with the published literature being a selectively filtered subset. [→ Bicakci 2026 (2)](#bicakci-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Bicakci 2026

Bicakci, M., Heller, F., Stoeger, H., & Ziegler, A. (2026). From RAMSing to Rigor: Improving Moderator Analysis in STEM Education Meta-Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10172-1

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Robust Bayesian meta-analysis with publication-selection model averaging applied to 1,786 moderator effect sizes from 89 STEM education meta-analyses; heterogeneity evidence was decisive with "τ =0 .48, 95% CrI [0.45, 0.52]".

> "RoBMA-PSMA yielded strong evidence for a nonzero overall moderator effect, BF10 =2 .98 × 106, with a model-averaged estimate of g = 0.27, 95% CrI [0.20, 0.34]."

### Bicakci 2026 (2)

Bicakci, M., Heller, F., Stoeger, H., & Ziegler, A. (2026). From RAMSing to Rigor: Improving Moderator Analysis in STEM Education Meta-Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10172-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Same RoBMA-PSMA analysis of the 89-meta-analysis corpus; the authors interpret this as indicating reported moderator findings reflect systematic selection as well as substantive variation.

> "RoBMA-PSMA also provided overwhelming evidence for publication-bias-type selection, BFpb =4 .72 × 1036."
