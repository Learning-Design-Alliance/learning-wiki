## Evidence

### Aksu Miray 2026

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison across three training-participation groups in the survey of 185 new nurse managers; Table 3 reports total score KW = 13.727, p = 0.001, η² = 0.075, a medium effect.

> "Administrators who fully participated in administrator training programmes had significantly higher scores in total score (p = 0.001) and sub-dimensions (general unit management p = 0.002, personnel management p = 0.018, care management p = 0.004)"
