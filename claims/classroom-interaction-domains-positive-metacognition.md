# Classroom-level instructional support, organization, and emotional support are each positively associated with upper elementary students' metacognitive strategy use

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Niels J. de Ruig 2023

Niels J. de Ruig, Peter F. de Jong, Marjolein Zee. (2023). Stimulating Elementary School Students' Self-Regulated Learning Through High-Quality Interactions and Relationships: A Narrative Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09795-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study of N students = 996 and N teachers = 54 in grades 3-6, the only included study examining all three TTI domains against metacognition. The review reports positive associations for all three domains, with instructional support strongest (Table 3 mean β = 0.20 for instructional support).

> "They found that students who reported higher levels of instructional support, classroom organization, or emotional support were also better in using metacognitive strategies than students who reported lower levels of the three domains in their classroom."
