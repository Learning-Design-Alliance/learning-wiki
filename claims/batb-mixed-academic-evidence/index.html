Review of a pre-post study in Dallas Independent School District grades 5-12; "Although unadjusted models showed improvements in reading scores following program participation, adjusted models found no significant changes in reading or math scores over time."

"Another study assessed a districtwide BATB program and found that participants were more than twice as likely to attend school as non-participants [34]."

Related Claims¶

  • Robust RCTs of school breakfast programs failed to find behavioral benefits, with low and variable attendance and a shift from at-home to at-school consumption limiting impact — related
  • School breakfast programs show suggestive positive effects on academic performance, with clearest effects on mathematics and arithmetic grades in undernourished children — related
  • Breakfast consumption shows a mainly positive effect on on-task behavior in the classroom in children — related
  • A low glycaemic load breakfast increases time on-task in young children, but the effect is short-lived and specific to the first observation period — related
  • Universal Free School Meals (including CEP) show the most consistent positive associations with attendance, test scores, graduation, and discipline outcomes — related
  • Quality of habitual breakfast, in terms of variety of food groups and adequate energy, is positively related to school performance — related
  • Acute experimental studies show inconsistent effects of breakfast on classroom behavior, with three of seven showing an on-task advantage — related
  • A mid-morning snack benefits on-task behavior only for children who consumed a small breakfast, correcting the energy deficiency — related
