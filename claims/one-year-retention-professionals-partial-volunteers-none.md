## Evidence

### Podsiadło Paweł 2018

Podsiadło Paweł, Kosiński Sylweriusz, Darocha Tomasz, Sałapa Kinga, Sanak Tomasz, Brugger Hermann. (2018). The Use of E-Learning in Medical Education for Mountain Rescuers Concerning Hypothermia. High Altitude Medicine & Biology. https://doi.org/10.1089/ham.2018.0050

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Twelve-month follow-up in which 57 of the original 187 rescuers repeated the course; median follow-up scores are compared with initial pretest medians by group. Retention was partial for professionals and absent for volunteers.

> "While the professionals showed an increase of knowledge from the initial pretest to the follow-up pretest (from 69% to 77%, p = 0.023), the score of the volunteers remained unchanged (69%)."
