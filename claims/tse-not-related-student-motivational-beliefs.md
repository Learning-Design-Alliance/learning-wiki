# Teacher self-efficacy is not directly related to student self-efficacy or intrinsic motivation when teacher and student reports are combined at the class level

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At the class level, TSE showed no significant associations with student-reported self-efficacy or intrinsic motivation across all three instructional quality models. [→ Burić 2020](#buric-2020)

## Evidence

### Burić 2020

Burić, Irena and Kim, Lisa (2020) Teacher self-efficacy, instructional quality, and student motivational beliefs: An analysis using multilevel structural equation modeling. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101302

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Doubly latent ML-SEM at the teacher level found no significant direct paths from TSE to student motivational beliefs; the authors state the relationship is "not as robust as it has been previously suggested" when both report sources are combined. No effect sizes beyond the non-significant βs are printed.

> "TSE was not related to SSE and intrinsic motivation in classroom management, cognitive activation, and supportive climate models (β = -.094, p =.447 and β = -.171, p = .137; β = -.083, p = .497 and β = -.168, p = .165; β = -.091, p = .469 and β = -.139, p = .296, respectively)."
