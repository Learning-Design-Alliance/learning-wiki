## Evidence

### Skulmowski 2024

Skulmowski, A. (2024). Placebo or Assistant? Generative AI Between Externalization and Anthropomorphization. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 58. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09894-x

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The essay reports, citing Draxler et al. (2024), two studies on text authorship in which "AI users did not claim ownership and authorship over short AI-generated texts" yet did not acknowledge the AI publicly. No effect size is printed.

> "They found that while AI users did not claim ownership and authorship over short AI-generated texts produced on a website, they still did not publicly acknowledge the contributions of the AI."

### Skulmowski 2024 (2)

Skulmowski, A. (2024). Placebo or Assistant? Generative AI Between Externalization and Anthropomorphization. Educational Psychology Review, 36, 58. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09894-x

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The essay reports, citing Draxler et al. (2024), that personalization via survey information had no effect on the ghostwriter effect, while "a stronger influence over the AI-generated texts" raised felt ownership. No effect size is printed.

> "Personalizing the AI model using information gained from a survey did not have an effect on the ghostwriter effect. However, the participants felt a higher degree of ownership if they had a stronger influence over the AI-generated texts."
