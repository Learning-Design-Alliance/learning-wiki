### Leyva 2014

Leyva, D., Yoshikawa, H., Snow, C. E., Treviño, E., Rolla, A., Barata, M. C., & Weiland, C. (2014). Can we improve preschool classroom quality in Chile? A cluster-randomized trial evaluation of a professional development program. SREE Spring 2014 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562728

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Impact analysis on child outcomes (Table 3) assessed with the Woodcock-Muñoz Language Survey Revised Spanish Form and socioemotional rating scales, after two years of intervention. The article reports no significant effects; printed effect sizes were small (vocabulary -0.07, letter-word identification 0.09, early writing -0.03, prosocial behaviors 0.079, impulse control domain 0.161, the latter marginally significant).
