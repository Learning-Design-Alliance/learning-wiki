## Evidence

### Chunrasaksakun 2015

Chunwadee Chunrasaksakun. (2015). Best management practice for understanding learning in sustainable water resource management (WRM) for secondary science teachers in Thailand. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A non-equivalent control group pretest-posttest study of 6 classes of 147 ninth-grade students (68 experimental, 79 control) across large, medium, and small schools. Students taught by WRD-trained teachers significantly outperformed control students on an achievement test.

> "it was found that the students in the experimental group had a much higher learning achievement than the students in the control group, with a statistically significant difference at level . 01."
