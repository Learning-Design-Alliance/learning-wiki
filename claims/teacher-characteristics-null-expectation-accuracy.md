## Evidence

### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Key Stage 2 regressions of teacher expectation accuracy on teacher-reported gender, length of service and class size in the ALSPAC sample. The article reports "little evidence" for all three teacher characteristics, with confidence intervals spanning zero; these data were available for KS2 only.

> "There was little evidence that teacher gender (− 0.039, 95% CI − 0.12, 0.40), teacher experience (e.g. 1–2 years vs 10 or more years: 0.07, 95% CI − 0.15, 0.29), or class size (− 0.025, 95% CI − 0.10, 0.05 per additional 10 pupils) associated with teacher expectation accuracy."
