Related Claims¶

  • In a Vietnamese EFL sample, all six Meta-UTAUT hypotheses on MALL adoption were accepted with p-values below 0.05 — related
  • The measurement model showed adequate reliability and convergent validity, and BI and UB models showed substantial explanatory power — related
  • Social influence has a small positive impact on MALL use in EFL acquisition among Vietnamese learners — related
  • Behavioral intention translates only weakly into self-reported use behavior, and the extended UTAUT model explains intention formation better than enactment — related
  • Metacognitive knowledge and intentions to use effective strategies do not reliably translate into actual strategy use (intention-behavior gap) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Facilitating condition has no direct effect on actual use of MALL, departing from prior literature. — related
  • The modified UTAUT model accounts for 62% of variance in behavioral intention and 19% of variance in use behavior. — related
  • Performance expectancy significantly predicts both behavioral intention and actual use of MALL. — related
  • MALL effect sizes show no visible correlation with grade level or sample size — related
