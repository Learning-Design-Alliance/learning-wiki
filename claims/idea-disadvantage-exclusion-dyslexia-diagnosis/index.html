"In the most recent iteration of IDEA 18, within the deﬁnition of speci ﬁc learning disability (SLD), the current term incorporating dyslexia, excludes children from a diagnosis of dyslexia who have "… environmental, cultural, or economic disadvantage (emphasis added).""
