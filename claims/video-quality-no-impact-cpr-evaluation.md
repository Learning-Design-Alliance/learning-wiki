## Evidence

### Plata Christopher 2021

Plata Christopher, Nellessen Martin, Roth Rebecca, Ecker Hannes, Böttiger Bernd W., Löser Johannes, Wetsch Wolfgang A. (2021). Impact of video quality when evaluating video-assisted cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a randomized, controlled simulation trial. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-021-00486-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized, double-blind simulation trial: 650 video-sequence ratings by 46 paramedics and 47 emergency physicians. The paper reports "no significant differences depending on video quality (p = 0.306)"; correct classification was 71.5%, 76.8% and 77.3% at low, medium and high quality. No effect size is printed.

> "CPR performance was correctly evaluated in 71.5% at low quality, in 76.8% at medium quality, and in 77.3% at high quality level, showing no significant differences depending on video quality (p = 0.306)."
