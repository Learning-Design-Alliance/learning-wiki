## Evidence

### Al-Bakri Moug 2023

Al-Bakri Moug, Skovgaard Anne Mette, Bach-Holm Daniella, Larsen Dorte Ancher, Siersma Volkert, Kessel Line. (2023). School performances in children with cataract: results from a population-based cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Between-group comparison of cataract children with (n=172) versus without (n=103) test results using registry and medical-record data (table 1). Only p values are printed; no effect sizes are reported.

> "Children with cataract who did not have a test score had a greater prevalence of severe somatic disease (p value=0.014) and neurodevelopmental disorders (p value=0.008) and were more likely to have had bilateral surgery (p value=0.034) compared with children with cataract and available test scores"
