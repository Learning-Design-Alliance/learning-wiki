## Evidence

### Smaoui S 2025

Smaoui S, Ganesan S, Williams T. (2025). Dysphagia education in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia: student self-competency ratings during their dysphagia course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07365-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre-post study of 19 SLT master's students (38 responses across two timepoints) using GEE models on modified DCVT responses; the article reports a statistically significant timepoint effect on General Skills with p < 0.001 and no effect size printed.

> "The GEE model for DCVT-GS identified significant main effects of background in SLT (p = 0.018), dysphagia patient exposure (p = 0.019), and survey timepoint (p < 0.001)."
