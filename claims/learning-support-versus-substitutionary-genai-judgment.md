`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Per-scenario descriptive results (Table 4) in the RQ1 section show learning-support scenarios (Q1–Q3) classified ethical at 84.7%–91.7% and substitutionary scenarios (Q4–Q6) classified unethical at 83.4%–94.5%.
