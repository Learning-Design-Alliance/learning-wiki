### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review's account of the Barsuk dissemination study (historical controls, different hospital setting). The review reports retained level-4 patient-safety benefit and highlights the implementation effort needed.

> "The dissemination study from Barsuk [72] shows that the same educational intervention can be transferred to a different hospital setting and still leads to improved patient safety and outcome at Kirkpatrick/Phillips level 4."

### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018 (2)

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review's account of the Barsuk trickle-down study: improved pretraining scores among trainees observing completed peers, interpreted as potential to raise expected mastery level without added cost.

> "In this study, trainees showed improved pretraining procedural scores by simply observing their more experienced colleagues, who had already completed the program. This effect infers the possibility of raising the expected mastery level without adding cost"
