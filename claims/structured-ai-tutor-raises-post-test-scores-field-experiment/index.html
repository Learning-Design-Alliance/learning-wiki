q3 · i? · causal · r2

Complete-case randomized comparison in the preregistered field experiment (86 randomized, 51 post-test completers; analysis sample N=46). The article reports the primary ANCOVA estimate as +6.63 (p=.007) and this inverse-probability-weighted re-estimate, "gives+6.52(p=.005)".
