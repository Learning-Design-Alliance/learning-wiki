## Evidence

### Hall and Mahoney 2013

Carol Hall, Jamie Mahoney. (2013). Response To Intervention: Research And Practice. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, 6(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1073194

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation (e) of the survey findings: district assessment data on academics and behaviors was familiar to teachers, so the problem-solving decision-making model of the RTI process was not new to either group. This is an untested explanatory account, not a measured result.

> "Teachers of both surveyed groups have used the Professional Learning Community forum to discuss student data and make flexible student-grouping decisions for several years ; using data in a decision -making process to plan lessons and drive instruction is not new to these teachers."
