## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). Correlating students’ beliefs about experimental physics with lab course success. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.087](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.087)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis pooling matched post-test E-CLASS scores and final grades (converted to grade points) from two semesters of four core physics lab courses at CU (N=873). The article reports "an overall correlation coeﬃcient of r = 0.05" that is "neither practically or statistically signiﬁcant ( p = 0 .1)".

> "Aggregating across all students in all courses (N=873), we found an overall correlation coeﬃcient of r = 0.05 between ﬁnal course grade and E-CLASS score (fraction of items answered favorably)."
