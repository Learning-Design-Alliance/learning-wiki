Mira S, Bernhisel AA, Rivkin AC, Chan CC, Grandin JC, Lotfi AC, Vanathi M, Golnik KC. (2025). The Ophthalmology Surgical Competency Assessment Rubric for penetrating keratoplasty. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/IJO.IJO_1199_25

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Development study of the rubric draft: after author revision cycles, feedback was solicited from a panel of eight cornea specialists — two from the USA and one each from Iran, the UK, Egypt, Australia, India, and Pakistan — and the rubric was modified until consensus was achieved. The article reports this as establishing face and content validity; no empirical testing with trainees is reported.

> "The rubric was then reviewed for face and content validity by a panel of eight international experts using an iterative feedback process. The draft underwent multiple cycles of feedback and revisions until a consensus was reached"
