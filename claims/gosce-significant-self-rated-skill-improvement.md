## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-GOSCE self-rated skills showed a significantly different median increase compared to pre-GOSCE (exact sign test, p = 0.0001), with 23 of 41 staff improving and 18 unchanged. [→ Fisher H 2022](#fisher-h-2022)

## Evidence

### Fisher H 2022

Fisher H, Zabar S, Chodosh J, Langford A, Trinh-Shevrin C, Sherman S, Altshuler L. (2022). A novel simulation-based approach to training for recruitment of older adults to clinical trials. BMC Medical Research Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-022-01643-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective pre-post self-assessment by 41 research staff using 3- and 4-point Likert items. The article reports "Scores post-GOSCE had a significantly different median increase compared to pre-GOSCE, p = 0.0001"; no effect size is printed.

> "An exact sign test was used to compare pre and post ratings. Scores post-GOSCE had a significantly different median increase compared to pre-GOSCE, p = 0.0001."
