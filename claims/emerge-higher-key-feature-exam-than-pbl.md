## Evidence

### Middeke Angélina 2018

Middeke Angélina, Anders Sven, Schuelper Madita, Raupach Tobias, Schuelper Nikolai. (2018). Training of clinical reasoning with a Serious Game versus small-group problem-based learning: A prospective study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203851

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective monocentric study in which 112 fifth-year medical students self-selected into ten 90-minute sessions of EMERGE or PBL; aggregate key feature scores were compared with Mann-Whitney-U tests. The EMERGE group scored "significantly higher than the PBL group" with 62.5 (IQR: 17.7)% vs. 54.2 (IQR: 21.9)%; p = 0.015.

> "Overall, the EMERGE group (n = 78) scored significantly higher than the PBL group (n = 34) in the key feature examination (62.5 (IQR: 17.7)% vs. 54.2 (IQR: 21.9)%; p = 0.015)."
