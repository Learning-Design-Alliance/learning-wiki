## Evidence

### Feldacker Caryl 2015

Feldacker Caryl, Chicumbe Sergio, Dgedge Martinho, Cesar Freide, Augusto Gerito, Robertson Molly, Mbofana Francisco, O'Malley Gabrielle. (2015). The effect of pre-service training on post-graduation skill and knowledge retention among mid-level healthcare providers in Mozambique. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-015-0011-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-sectional t-test comparison of mean follow-up scores between 112 initial-curriculum and 188 revised-curriculum TMGs, reported in the Aim 1 results. Initial-group means were 52.7% (cases), 62.6% (knowledge), 40.0% (physical exam); revised means were 60.2%, 62.0%, and 47.6% respectively, with "significantly higher" on cases and physical exam.

> "Revised curriculum scores were significantly higher than the scores of the initial group on the clinical cases (p < 0.001) and physical exam (p < 0.001), but not on the knowledge test."
