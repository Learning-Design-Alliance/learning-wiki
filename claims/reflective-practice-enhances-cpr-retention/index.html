Lee Hyeon-Ji 2026¶

Lee Hyeon-Ji. (2026). Training interval, feedback, and retention in cardiopulmonary resuscitation education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09538-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of a limited number of cognitive–psychological studies; the review reports "reflective practice–based interventions were associated with improved long-term skill retention" and that team-based training with structured communication (e.g., TeamSTEPPS) improved confidence and role clarity. Table 3 records a 6-month improved retention finding for reflective practice.
