## Evidence

### Marija Cubric 2015

Marija Cubric, and Amanda Jefferies. (2015). The benefits and challenges of large-scale deployment of electronic voting systems: University student views from across different subject groups. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Jonckheere-Terpstra test for ordered medians comparing students who used EVS in one versus two consecutive academic years, from the same survey of 590 students.

> "The test results revealed a significant trend in the data: as the experience with EVS increased, “learning benefits” increased (J=43917.5, z= 4.09, r=-0.17, p<0.001), “challenges” decreased (J=30583.5, z= -2.97, r=-0.12, p<0.01), and “classroom benefits” increased (J=41043.5, z= 2.57, r=-0.11, p<0.001)."
