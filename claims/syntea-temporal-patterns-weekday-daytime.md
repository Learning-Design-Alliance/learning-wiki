# Syntea usage concentrates on weekdays and daytime hours, with part-time students shifting use to weekends and evenings relative to full-time students

## Evidence

### Kristina Schaaff 2026

Kristina Schaaff, Quintus Stierstorfer and Valerie Hekkel. (2026). Using AI-based Learning Assistants in Higher Education: A Large-Scale Descriptive Analysis. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08748

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of hourly activity counts (one count per unique student-hour-day combination, normalized to 100% per group) shown in Figures 3 and 12-13. The article reports the weekday/daytime concentration and the study-mode contrast; no effect size is printed.

> "Full-timestudents showed a stronger concentration of Syntea use at the beginning of the week (Figure 12) and during late morning to afternoon hours (Figure 13).Part-timestudents, by contrast, exhibited a somewhat flatter weekday pattern and relatively stronger usage on weekends as well as in the late afternoon and evening."
