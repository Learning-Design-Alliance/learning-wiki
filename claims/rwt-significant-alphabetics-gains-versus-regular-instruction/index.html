Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Read, Write & Type!™. http://www.fcrr.org/TechnicalReports/RWTfullrept.pdf

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial comparison of Read, Write & Type!™ (n = 36) with a regular instruction/support group (n = 41) in three schools, analyzed by the WWC. Appendix A3.1 prints effect sizes of 0.80 for CTOPP phoneme segmenting (improvement index +29) and 0.65 for Woodcock word attack (+24); blending (0.38), elision (0.29), and word identification (0.48) were "ns".

"the authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, statistically significant positive effects of Read, Write & Type!™ on one of the phonological awareness measures (phoneme segmenting) and on one of the phonics measures (word attack)"

What Works Clearinghouse 2007 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Read, Write & Type!™. http://www.fcrr.org/TechnicalReports/RWTfullrept.pdf

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

WWC calculations for the RWT versus regular instruction comparison on the CTOPP phoneme segmenting subtest: mean difference 3.70, effect size 0.80, statistically significant at α = 0.05, improvement index +29. The article describes this as a "randomized controlled trial" meeting WWC evidence standards.

"CTOPP: Phoneme Segmenting Subtest Grade 1 77 15.40 (4.70) 11.70 (4.50) 3.70 0.80 Statistically significant +29"
