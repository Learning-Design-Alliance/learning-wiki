Venegas Carolina Lavin 2019¶

Review synthesis of two pilot RCTs: Mache et al. 2016 (psychosocial skills training with cognitive behavioural and solution-focused counselling) and Sood et al. 2011 (SMART program with wait-list control). Both "reported significant improvement in resilience"; no effect sizes printed.

"Two pilot RCTs [15,37] reported our primary outcome measure of resilience measured by validated resilience scales (Tables 1 and 2). Both pilot trials [15,37] reported significant improvement in resilience."
