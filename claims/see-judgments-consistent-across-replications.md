Joy He-Yueya, Noah D. Goodman, Emma Brunskill. (2024). Evaluating and Optimizing Educational Content with Large Language Model Judgments. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02795](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02795)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary analysis of the same Variability Effect SEE data (Figure 6): among low-expertise learners the worked-example group performs significantly better than the practice group, with no significant difference among high-expertise learners. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Additionally, our data from these SEEs also replicates the Expertise Reversal Effect (see Figure 6), suggesting that the LM judgments are consistent."
