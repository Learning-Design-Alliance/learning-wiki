Edpuzzle evidence summary 2025¶

Edpuzzle evidence summary. (2025). Edpuzzle. https://www.edpuzzle.com doi:10.2139/ssrn.5265891

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Additional analyses of the randomized study indicated "a positive dosage relationship": students assigned 30 or more videos outgained controls, with the strongest outcomes above 50 videos. No effect sizes are printed for these dosage contrasts.
