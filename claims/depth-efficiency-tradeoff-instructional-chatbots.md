TeachingCoach: A Fine-Tuned Scaffolding Chatbot for Instructional Guidance to Instructors. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18189 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.18189](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2603.18189)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative findings from the 41-instructor user study, drawn from surveys and 15-minute post-study interviews. Participants distinguished the models on conversational engagement, efficiency of interaction, and breadth of suggestions; "Overall the findings highlight a trade-off between depth and efficiency."

> "Overall the findings highlight a trade-off between depth and effi- ciency: the fine-tuned model supported learning through sustained engagement and reflection, while the baseline model prioritized speed and breadth of suggestions."
