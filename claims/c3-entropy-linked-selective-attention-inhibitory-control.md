## Evidence

### Zheng 2026

Zheng, Y., Lo, T. W., Lau, E. Y., Eskes, G., Hui, L. L., & Hsiao, J. H. (2026). The Role of Eye Movement Consistency in Aging-Related Decline in Face Recognition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00437-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory correlation and hierarchical regression within the same sample; the article reports the Flanker congruency effect correlated with C3 entropy (r(290) = .138, p = .018). It interprets this as lower consistency reflecting difficulty executing learned visual routines due to poorer selective attention and inhibitory control.

> "The congruency effect in RT of the Flanker task explained additional variance in C3 entropy  (β = .159, p = .006, ΔR 2 = .025 ), with age controlled in the first step (F(2, 286) = 8.710, p < .001, Table 1)"
