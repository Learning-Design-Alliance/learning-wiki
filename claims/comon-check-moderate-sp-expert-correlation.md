## Subclaims
`q2 i3` SP ratings on a 10-cm visual analogue scale correlated moderately (rho = 0.40, p < 0.001) with expert global ratings, supporting external validity. [→ Radziej K 2017](#radziej-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Radziej K 2017

Radziej K, Loechner J, Engerer C, Niglio de Figueiredo M, Freund J, Sattel H, Bachmann C, Berberat PO, Dinkel A, Wuensch A. (2017). How to assess communication skills? Development of the rating scale ComOn Check. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1392823

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

External validity analysis using standardized patients' single-item 10-cm visual analogue scale ratings (N = 135, M = 7.52, SD = 1.806, range 1.1-10.0) against expert global ratings of the same consultations. The correlation is reported as "a moderate association" with ρ = 0.40, p < 0.001 (r = .40).

> "There was a moderate association between the two variables of interest, i.e., SP ratings and item F (Global rating for total assessment), (ρ = 0.40, p < 0.001)."
