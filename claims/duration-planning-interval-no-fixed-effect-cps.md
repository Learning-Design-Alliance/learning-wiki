## Evidence

### Eichmann 2019

Eichmann, B., Goldhammer, F., Greiff, S., Pucite, L., & Naumann, J. (2019). The role of planning in complex problem solving. Computers & Education, 128, 1-12. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.08.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GLMM analysis of log data from N = 1346 German students on 21 interactive PISA 2012 CPS tasks found no significant fixed effect for the duration indicator (estimate = -0.05, p = 0.639) but a significant random effect with correlation r = -0.04 with task easiness, showing the effect "varies between tasks but is not related to task di ﬃculty."

> "The correlation between task easiness and the random e ﬀect of the indicator was r = −0.04. The random e ﬀect shows that the duration of the longest interval varies between tasks but is not related to task di ﬃculty."
