Evaluation of ChatGPT 3.5 on 9 CS1 and 9 CS2 programming assignments at a large research-intensive university; questions were given as prompts and graded against official solutions. The study reports it "correctly outputs the correct code for every coding question in CS1 on the very first try," with only a debugging-screenshot part of A6 failed.
