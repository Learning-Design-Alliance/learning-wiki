## Evidence

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mediation analysis of the survey data (Figure 9) found the effect of psychological effect on stress positive (a = 0.52) and the stress-to-performance path "b = -0.69"; the article concludes stress is a critical mediator linking mental health to academic difficulties.

> "the effect of stress on academic performance (b = -0.69) is strongly negative, indicating that stress significantly undermines performance. The indirect effect (a×b = -0.36) is also negative and significant, confirming that stress is a key mechanism through which psychological distress lowers performance."

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025 (2)

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Moderated mediation analysis (Figure 10) of the survey data found workload increases stress (a = 0.47), stress negatively predicts performance (b = -0.58), and the indirect effect (a×b = -0.27) is substantial and negative, with motivation acting as a protective buffer.

> "The interaction term (-0.075) indicates that the relationship between workload and performance is moderated by student motivation: when motivation is higher, the negative effect of workload on performance via stress is slightly reduced."
