Evidence¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu 2024¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu. (2024). The effects of educational robotics in STEM education: a multilevel meta-analysis. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-024-00469-4

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Ad hoc subgroup analysis within the non-significant intervention-duration moderator (F(2, 26) = 1.2442, p > 0.05). Interventions over one month yielded g = 0.672 (p < 0.001); durations of ≤ 1 day (g = 0.263) and > 1 day ≤ 1 month (g = −0.040) were not significant.

"the duration length of “> 1 month” (g = 0.672, p < 0.001) had the largest effect size on robot-assisted STEM learning."
