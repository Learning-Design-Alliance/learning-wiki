The report's extent-of-evidence table (Appendix A5) lists 2 studies, 17 schools, and 120 students for alphabetics and zero studies for the other three domains, stating a rating of "moderate to large" requires a total sample size of at least 350 students or 14 classrooms.

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Stepping Stones to Literacy to be small for alphabetics. No studies that met WWC evidence standards with or without reservations addressed fluency, comprehension, or general reading achievement."
