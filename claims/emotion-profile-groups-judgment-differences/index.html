Evidence¶

Prinz-Weiß 2023¶

Prinz-Weiß, A., Lukosiute, L., Meyer, M., & Riedel, J. (2023). The role of achievement emotions for text comprehension and metacomprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09331-w

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Cluster analysis (Ward method, N = 343 after outlier removal) yielded three profiles: positive (n = 182), neutral (n = 72), negative (n = 89). ANOVA on predictions was significant, Welch's F(2, 156.88) = 13.34, p < .001, η2 = .07; planned contrasts supported the prediction hypothesis.

"the first contrast showed that participants in the positive group provided higher predictions than participants in the other groups, t(301,27) = 4.04, p < .001, η2 = .05."

Prinz-Weiß 2023 (2)¶

Prinz-Weiß, A., Lukosiute, L., Meyer, M., & Riedel, J. (2023). The role of achievement emotions for text comprehension and metacomprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09331-w

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

ANOVA on postdiction accuracy across the three emotion-profile groups was significant, F(2, 340) = 6.25, p = .002, η2 = .04; the negative and neutral groups did not significantly differ (t(340) = 0.22, p = .829).

"the first contrast showed that participants in the positive group provided less underconfident postdictions than participants in the other groups, t(340) = 3.50, p < .001, η2 = .04."
