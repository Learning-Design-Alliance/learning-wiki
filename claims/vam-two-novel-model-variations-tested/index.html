Matthew Johnson 2013¶

Matthew Johnson, Stephen Lipscomb, Brian Gill. (2013). Sensitivity of Teacher Value-Added Estimates to Student and Peer Control Variables. Working Paper 25. Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/sensitivity-of-teacher-valueadded-estimates-to-student-and-peer-control-variables

The abstract describes the paper's novel model variations: substituting teacher-year level averages for classroom average peer characteristics, and demographic variation in the current-to-lagged achievement relationship. No outcome of these tests is printed on the page.

"It also tests two model variations not previously evaluated—replacing classroom average peer characteristics with teacher-year level averages, or allowing for variation in the relationship between current and lagged achievement scores based on student demographic characteristics—to determine whether they affect the VAM estimates."
