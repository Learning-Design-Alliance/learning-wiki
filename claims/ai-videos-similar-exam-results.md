`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same online experiment, multiple-choice exam scores at the end of the session showed no reported difference between the AI-video and human-video treatment groups; the article reports no effect size for this comparison.

> "However, a comparison of exam results between the experimental groups implies that the participants eventually acquired knowledge about the content to a similar degree."
