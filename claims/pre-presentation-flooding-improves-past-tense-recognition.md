## Evidence

### Rikhtegar 2015

Rikhtegar, O., & Gholami, J. (2015). The Effects of Pre- versus Post-Presentation Input Flooding via Reading on the Young Iranian EFL Learners' Acquisition of Simple Past Tense. English Language Teaching, 8(3), 80-88. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p80

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test scores of 61 elementary EFL learners with pre-test as covariate; the pre-presentation-control mean difference of "10.63" was significant at the 0.001 alpha level.

> "the estimated mean differences of pre-presentation and control groups (10.63) is significant at (0.001) alpha level. Therefore, it may be concluded that pre-presentation input flooding via reading has positive and significant effect on the acquisition of the past tense."
