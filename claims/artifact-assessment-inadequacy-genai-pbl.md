### Chengbo Zheng 2024

Chengbo Zheng, Kangyu Yuan, Bingcan Guo, Reza Hadi Mogavi, Zhenhui Peng, Shuai Ma, and Xiaojuan Ma. (2024). Charting the Future of AI in Project-Based Learning: A Co-Design Exploration with Students. [doi:10.1145/3613904.3642807](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642807)

In the assessment-transformation activity, "Most participants felt that traditional assessment methods, such as those based on artifacts, are inadequate for evaluating traits like creative thinking and efficiency, given the generative capabilities of AI"; a minority argued artifacts should retain significant weight.
