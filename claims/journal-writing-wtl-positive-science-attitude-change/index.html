Evidence¶

Pınar 2020¶

Pınar, E. & Yıldız, A. (2020). The Effect of Journal Writing, One of the “Writing to Learn” Activities, on Achievement and Science Attitude in the Fourth Grade Science Course. Journal of Current Researches on Social Sciences, 10(1), 179-196. https://doi.org/10.26579/jocress.354

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed rank test comparing the experimental group's (n=24) pre- and post-test scores on the Attitude Scale towards Science Course after five weeks of journal writing; the article reports a significant difference in favour of post-test (z=3.291, p<.05).

"It can be stated that journal writing as a WTL activity affected experimental group students’ attitudes towards the science course positively (z=3.291, p<.05)."

Pınar 2020 (2)¶
