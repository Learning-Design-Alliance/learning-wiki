### Weimer Johannes Matthias 2025

Weimer Johannes Matthias, Eigenseher Michael, Stiehl Simon Alexander, Nürnberg Dieter, Ludwig Michael, Stäuber Marie, Weimer Andreas, Kloeckner Roman, Dirks Klaus, Lorenz Liv, Buggenhangen Holger, Julia-Weinmann-Menke, Merkel Daniel. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate linear regression analysis of the trial's test results with group allocation, prior experience, qualification, specialty, and workplace as factors. The three named predictors influenced total and pathology scores but not normal findings.

> "Membership of the study group (p < 0.01), a number of > 30 independent ultrasound examinations (p < 0.01), and current employment in internal medicine (p = 0.01) had a significant influence on both the total score theory test and the competence area total score pathology findings, but not on the competence area total score normal finding."
