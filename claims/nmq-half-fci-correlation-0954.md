## Evidence

### Ashutosh Kumar Pathak 2026

Ashutosh Kumar Pathak, Mark A. J. Parker, Andrew J. A. James, Sally E. Jordan, and Jonathan Nylk. (2026). Student responses to a modified Force Concept Inventory: The Newtonian Mechanics Quiz. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.02432](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.02432)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Correlational validity check using all 674 NMQ attempts, comparing total NMQ score (out of 31) with half-length FCI score (out of 14); Figure 3 shows a linear fit y = (1.92 ± 0.02)x + (3.2 ± 0.2). The printed r = 0.954 is a strong correlation.

> "The NMQ score is highly correlated with the score from the selection of ite ms that make up the half-length FCI, with a Pearson linear correlation coeﬃcient of 0 .954 ( p < 10−6)."
