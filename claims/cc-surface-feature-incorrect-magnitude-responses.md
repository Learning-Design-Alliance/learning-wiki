## Evidence

### Kuo 2015

Kuo, E., & Wieman, C. E. (2015). Seeking instructional specificity: an example from analogical instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Response-distribution analysis of the magnitude mid-test question (Table 1): GR 66% correct vs CC 42% correct; the common incorrect answer was given by 55% of CC vs 28% of GR students. The authors attribute CC errors to matching the concentric-circle geometry of the positive-charge case rather than equipotential-line density.

> "In this case, the most common incorrect answer in both conditions is (B), EX > EY > EZ.  CC students give the common incorrect answer twice as often as GR students."
