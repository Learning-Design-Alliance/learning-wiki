### Grills-Taquechel 2012

Grills-Taquechel, Amie E.; Fletcher, Jack M.; Vaughn, Sharon R.; Stuebing, Karla K. (2012). Anxiety and Reading Difficulties in Early Elementary School: Evidence for Unidirectional- or Bi-Directional Relations? Child Psychiatry and Human Development, 43(1), 35-47. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-011-0246-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Series of independent-samples t-tests comparing boys and girls in the 153-child sample on all study scales. The discussion reports small untested effect sizes (T1 ds = .15-.30 and T2 ds = .11-.23) on MASC subscales, contrasting with past research showing greater anxiety in girls.

> "No significant gender differences were found at either time point and subsequent analyses combined boys and girls."
