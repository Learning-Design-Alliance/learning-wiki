### Rafey IA 2026

Rafey IA, Abdullah A, Saeed Z, Khan UU, Mohyuddin W. (2026). Comparative analysis of university lectures, textbooks, and online video lectures: preferences, engagement, and impact on academic performance among medical students of Nowshera Medical College. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09921-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six-item University Lecture perception scale (α = 0.875) administered in the cross-sectional survey. The one-sample t-test showed perceptions "significantly below neutral" with a moderate effect; the gender difference was significant by Mann–Whitney U.

> "A one-sample t-test confirmed that overall perceptions were significantly below neutral (t (354) = − 9.76, p < 0.001, d = − 0.52), representing a moderate effect. Female students reported higher perception scores than males (Mean Rank = 192.19 vs. 163.08), a statistically significant difference, U = 13,161, p = 0.007."
