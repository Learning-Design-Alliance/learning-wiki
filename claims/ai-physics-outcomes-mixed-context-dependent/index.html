The review's narrative synthesis of the 11 included studies concludes outcomes were "mixed and context-dependent" and that favorable patterns "were not consistent across all studies or outcome measures."

"Taken together, the included studies reported mixed and context-dependent outcomes. Some settings with structured feedback, simulation or modeling, and guided collaborative activity showed more favorable findings, but these patterns were not consistent across all studies or outcome measures."
