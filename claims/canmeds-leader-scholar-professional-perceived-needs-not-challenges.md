## Evidence

### McConnell Meghan 2018

McConnell Meghan, Gu Ada, Arshad Aysha, Mokhtari Arastoo, Azzam Khalid. (2018). An innovative approach to identifying learning needs for intrinsic CanMEDS roles in continuing professional development. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1497374

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive comparison of coded frequencies across the two open-ended questions in a survey of 411 specialists; the abstract-style summary is supported in Results, where Scholar responses reached "96 (42.5%) of perceived learning need responses" versus "n = 4; 1.9%" for challenges, and Leader 63 (27.9%) versus six (2.9%).

> "Specifically, Leader (28%), Scholar (43%), and Professional (16%) roles were frequently described perceived learning needs, as opposed to challenges in practice (Leader: 3%; Scholar: 2%; and Professional: 8%."
