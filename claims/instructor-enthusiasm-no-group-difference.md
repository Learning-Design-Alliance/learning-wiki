## Evidence

### Rosegard 2013

Rosegard, E., & Wilson, J. (2013). Capturing students' attention: An empirical study. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 13, No. 5, December 2013. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1017063

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Because instructor enthusiasm had been shown to impact learning and might act as a moderating variable, students rated the instructor on a 7-point semantic differential scale embedded in the exam. The t-test found "no statistically significant difference between groups (p>.05)", providing some evidence the moderating effect of enthusiasm did not differ between conditions. No effect size is printed.

> "An analysis of the 7-point enthusiasm score using an independent t-test found that there was no statistically significant difference between groups (p>.05)."
