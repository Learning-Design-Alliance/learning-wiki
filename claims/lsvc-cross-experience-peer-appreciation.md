`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive qualitative coding of five semi-structured interviews (teachers with 3 to 24 years of experience) and open-ended survey responses. Newer teachers wrote, for example, "By watching other teachers teach, I have learned new strategies I can use in my classroom."

> "Iris and other more experienced teachers specifically expressed how the presence and participation of teachers with fewer years of experience, those newer to the profession, enhanced the culture of the learning community and provided a space that promoted learning with and from other teachers."
