Evidence¶

Karrenbauer 2023¶

Karrenbauer, C., Brauner, T., König, C. M., & Breitner, M. H. (2023). Design, development, and evaluation of an individual digital study assistant for higher education students. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10255-8

q2 · i? · design · r3

Likert-scale questionnaire item in the functionality "Evaluation" during the 3-month field test. The article also reports "Students were indecisive and could only partly imagine using the IDSA regularly (M = 3.1, /u1D70E = 1.6; R.1.9)" and perceived added value of M = 2.79 (SD 1.6), all descriptive ratings without inferential tests.

"An average of 3.58 showed that the students indicated that the IDSA prototype’s functionalities encouraged them to think and reflect more on their individual study goals (R.1.3; /u1D70E = 1.71)."

Karrenbauer 2023 (2)¶

Karrenbauer, C., Brauner, T., König, C. M., & Breitner, M. H. (2023). Design, development, and evaluation of an individual digital study assistant for higher education students. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10255-8

q2 · i? · design · r3

Authors' synthesis in the discussion of the field-test questionnaire results, stating functionalities were "rated slightly above the satisfaction level" and that students "only perceived limited added value from certain functionalities".

"Overall, the functionalities of the IDSA prototype were rated slightly above the satisfaction level (M = 3.0–4.0), and students only perceived limited added value from certain functionalities (M = 2.70, /u1D70E = 1.6)."
