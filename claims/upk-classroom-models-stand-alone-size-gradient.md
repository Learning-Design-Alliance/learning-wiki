# Nearly half of California's UPK classrooms were stand-alone TK classes, but small LEAs were far less able to offer stand-alone TK than medium and large LEAs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of California's 15,425 UPK classrooms in 2022–23, 46% were TK stand-alone, 33% were stand-alone CSPP/Head Start/other early learning classes, 18% were TK–kindergarten combination classes, and 2% were TK combinations with CSPP or locally funded preschool. [→ Wang 2024](#wang-2024)
`q2 i?` Almost all medium-sized LEAs (96%) and all large LEAs offered TK stand-alone classes, compared to 66% of small LEAs. [→ Wang 2024 (2)](#wang-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Wang 2024

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results on classroom models (Figure 1). The report states 33% of UPK classrooms were stand-alone non-TK programs such as CSPP or Head Start, 18% were TK–kindergarten combination classes, and 2% combined TK with CSPP or locally funded preschool.

> "In the 2022–23 school year, almost half (46%) of the state's 15,425 UPk classrooms were Tk stand-alone classrooms."

### Wang 2024 (2)

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey finding on LEA size as a moderator of classroom model. The report attributes the gap to small LEAs having "fewer Tk students and resources, which makes offering a stand-alone Tk classroom difficult," quoting a rural district whose TK sits in a K–2 self-contained classroom.

> "Almost all medium-sized LEAs (96%) and all large LEAs offered Tk stand-alone classes in 2022–23, compared to 66% of small LEAs."
