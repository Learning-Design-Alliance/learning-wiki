Victoria Abramenka-Lachheb 2025¶

Victoria Abramenka-Lachheb, Gamze Ozogul. (2025). Interaction analysis of learning objects in online courses: What are their interactive characteristics and design intent behind them? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10556-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Basic qualitative study with semi-structured interviews (40-60 min) with 15 US higher education instructional designers; thematic analysis identified four interaction categories from participants' objects and descriptions: "Interactions as simulations," visuals, navigation elements, and game-like elements including badges, avatars, and a course designed as a game.
