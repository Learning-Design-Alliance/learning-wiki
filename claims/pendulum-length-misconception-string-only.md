### P. Arun 2010

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical/pedagogical argument in §2: the author states "students carry a wrong notion that the length of the pendulum is the length of the string," true only for a point-mass bob, and gives the rigorous suspension-to-center-of-mass definition. No empirical data on students are reported.

> "students carry a wrong notion that the length of the pendulum is the length of the string. This, however is only true if the bob is dense and considered a point mass with bob's radius far smaller than the length of the string."
