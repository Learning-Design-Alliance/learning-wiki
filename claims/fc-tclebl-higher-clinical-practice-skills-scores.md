## Evidence

### Ding Chun 2019

Ding Chun, Li Shengguo, Chen Baihua. (2019). Effectiveness of flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning on ophthalmology teaching for eight-year program students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1861-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study of 67 eight-year medical students (32 FC-TCLEBL, 35 LBC) taught by the same teacher; clinical practice skills assessed with standardized patients and graded by the same teacher. The article reports "higher in the FC-TCLEBL group than in the traditional lecture-based classroom group" on all teamwork components.

> "The teamwork scores for inquiry skills (85.563 ± 5.808 vs. 75.257 ± 9.727, p = 0.000), visual examination (87 ± 7.721 vs. 78.286 ± 6.438, p = 0.000), slit lamp examination techniques (86 ± 6.643 vs. 73.943 ± 7.874, p = 0.000), and diagnostic strategy selection (81.375 ± 8.657 vs. 75.829 ± 7.857, p = 0.008) were higher in the FC-TCLEBL group than in the traditional lecture-based classroom group."

### Ding Chun 2019 (2)

Ding Chun, Li Shengguo, Chen Baihua. (2019). Effectiveness of flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning on ophthalmology teaching for eight-year program students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1861-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same comparative clinical assessment with standardized patients; individual scores for humanistic care and knowledge assessment were statistically significantly higher in the FC-TCLEBL group, as the article reports the difference "was statistically significant".

> "The individual aspects of humanistic care (70 ± 16.064 vs. 56 ± 17.354, p = 0.001) and knowledge assessment (80.625 ± 10.980 vs. 64.429 ± 9.909, p = 0.000) were compared between the two groups, and the difference was statistically significant."
