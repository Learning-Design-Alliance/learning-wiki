# An RL narrative AI tutor produced no statistically significant overall improvement over a no-support control in either of two randomized studies

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across study 1 and study 2, the improvement (post-test minus pretest) did not differ significantly between the RL narrative condition and the control condition. [→ Sherry Ruan 2024](#sherry-ruan-2024)

## Evidence

### Sherry Ruan 2024

Sherry Ruan, Allen Nie, William Steenbergen, Jiayu He, JQ Zhang, Meng Guo, Yao Liu, Kyle Dang Nguyen, Catherine Y Wang, Rui Ying, James A Landay, Emma Brunskill. (2024). Reinforcement Learning Tutor Better Supported Lower Performers in a Math Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1007/s10994-023-06423-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10994-023-06423-9)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two randomized online experiments with elementary students (study 1: 258 RL vs 68 control analyzed; study 2: 17 vs 18) compared post-test minus pretest improvement. The article reports "no signiﬁcant diﬀerence" in improvement between the RL narrative and control conditions in both studies; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the amount of improvement (post-test - pretest score) between the RL narrative condition and control condition (study 1: Wilcoxon rank test W = 9632.5, p = 0.2, study 2: Wilcoxon rank test W = 185.5, p = 0.281)."
