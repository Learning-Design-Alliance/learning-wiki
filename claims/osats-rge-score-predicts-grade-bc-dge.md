`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc analysis of the prospective multicenter LAELAPS-3 cohort: 192 video-scored RGE anastomoses during RPD, graded with OSATS by two blinded graders. Receiver operator analysis of the total OSATS score showed "significant predictive value ... for grade B/C DGE (AUC: 0.668, P < 0.001)".

> "Operator receiver analysis showed significant predictive value of the total OSATS score for grade B/C DGE (AUC: 0.668, P < 0.001)"

Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000006692

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cutoff analysis in the same 192-patient cohort compared patients with RGE OSATS scores above versus at or below 25 points. Patients above the cutoff had "11.3% vs 34/121, 28.1%" grade B/C DGE, an "OR: 0.325" with a "relative risk reduction = 59.9%" (P = 0.006).

> "An OSATS score exceeding 25 was associated with a reduced risk of grade B/C DGE (8/71, 11.3% vs 34/121, 28.1%; OR: 0.325, relative risk reduction = 59.9%, P = 0.006)"
