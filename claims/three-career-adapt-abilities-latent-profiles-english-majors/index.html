Latent profile analysis in Mplus 8.3 with the 24 Career Adapt-Abilities Scale items as indicators; models with 2 to 5 profiles were fitted. The "3-profile model had proportions of 41.0, 51.2, and 7.8%" and the highest entropy (0.952).

"Regarding profile sizes, the 3-profile model had proportions of 41.0, 51.2, and 7.8%, all exceeding the 5% minimum threshold for meaningful interpretation."
