### Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello 2026

Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Feedback That Clicks: Introductory Physics Students’ Valued Features in AI Feedback Generated From Self-Crafted and Engineered Prompts. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/v3pj-8491](https://doi.org/10.1103/v3pj-8491)

Examination of AI responses to student prompts lacking clear or complete context descriptions; in only 8 cases did AI request clarification or recommend rewording, showing "AI correctly interpreted the context and generated feedback in 285 instances".

> "Of the 293 such cases, AI correctly interpreted the context and generated feedback in 285 instances."

### Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello 2026 (2)

Amogh Sirnoorkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Feedback That Clicks: Introductory Physics Students’ Valued Features in AI Feedback Generated From Self-Crafted and Engineered Prompts. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/v3pj-8491](https://doi.org/10.1103/v3pj-8491)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article illustrates with a case where the AI corrected the potential-energy relationship but, per the article, "did not prompt further clarification or address the incorrect physical interpretation of the slope".

> "Furthermore, though AI demonstrated impressive ‘contextual intuitiveness’ (i.e., the feature of appropriately capturing the right context despite contextually ambiguous prompts) in 97% of cases, these responses were not always fully appropriate or correct."
