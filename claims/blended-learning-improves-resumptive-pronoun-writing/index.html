Evidence¶

Rajabi 2015¶

Rajabi, B., & Hashemian, M. (2015). A Comparative Study of the Effectiveness of Two Methods of Teaching Resumptive Pronouns in Writing: TBLT and Blended Learning. English Language Teaching, 8(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n9p196

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired pretest-posttest comparison of the blended learning group (BLG, N = 20) on a researcher-made resumptive-pronoun test, reported in Results section 4.2. The article reports the pretest mean was "substantially less than the mean score on the posttest"; no effect size is printed.

"In the BLG, the mean score on the pretest (M = 12.65) is substantially less than the mean score on the posttest (M = 15.70)."
