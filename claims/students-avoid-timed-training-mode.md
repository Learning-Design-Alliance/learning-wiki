### Martínez F 2017

Martínez F, Tobar C, Taramasco C. (2017). Implementation of a Smartphone application in medical education: a randomised trial (iSTART). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1010-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Active monitoring of the application's use among the 40 intervention participants over the 4-week period; study mode was used by 34 participants (85%), training mode by 12 (30%), a contrast the authors report as statistically significant (p < 0.001).

> "Participants used the application's training mode less frequently, with only 12 students (30%, 95%CI 16.6–46.5%) registering any activity during this trial."
