Evidence¶

Ferrer 2026¶

Ferrer, I., & Aymerich-Franch, L. (2026). Social robots for education: a global analysis of deployments in real scenarios. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10611-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

Results-by-area section reports that of 65 primary/secondary cases with 14 functions, "Half of the cases (32) involve robots for training programming skills."

"In primary and secondary schools, 65 cases identified 14 functions performed by social robots. Half of the cases (32) involve robots for training programming skills, reinforcing STEM-based curricula by programming robots in real-time (e.g., SIMA) or analyzing data collected by robots (e.g., Nao)."

Ferrer 2026 (2)¶

Ferrer, I., & Aymerich-Franch, L. (2026). Social robots for education: a global analysis of deployments in real scenarios. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10611-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

Primary/secondary results state robots "mainly have the role of teaching assistants (21)," supporting teachers by playing recorded lessons and practicing languages one-to-one.

"In this area, robots mainly have the role of teaching assistants (21), reducing teachers' workload by playing lessons (previously recorded and stored) on robots' screens when a student is not able to attend (e.g., Sanbot Elf) and practicing languages in one-to-one exercises."
