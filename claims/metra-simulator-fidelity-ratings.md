`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course fidelity ratings by the 114 participants with prior HEMS experience comparing MeTra to actual doctor helicopter operations on five items (1 = Different, 5 = Same); vibration scored lowest at 3.45 ± 1.07.

> "Their mean post-course mean scores were vibration 3.45 ± 1.07 (57.90% ≥ 4), noise 3.75 ± 1.03 (71.93% ≥ 4), visual stimuli 3.82 ± 0.98 (65.79% ≥ 4), enclosed environment 4.38 ± 0.74 (91.23% ≥ 4), and communication environment 4.55 ± 0.61 (95.62% ≥ 4)."
