## Evidence

### Shakibaei 2013

Shakibaei, Z., Rahimaghaee, F., & Arish, M. (2013). Effect of Educational Games on Social Skills in Iranian Mentally Retarded Children. li-manager's Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101771

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

First-hypothesis ANCOVA on the self-help eating subscale of Wailand's social growth scale in the same 20-girl pre-test/post-test design; moderated means were 7.415 (test) versus 3.885 (control). The article reports "a significant effect of factor appeared between queries of both groups" with no effect size printed.

> "Moderated mean scores suggest that the test group (based on performance of the educational games) significantly dominates the control group (conventional method) in terms of their scores. Therefore, the educational games have a positive effect on the self-help for eating food."
