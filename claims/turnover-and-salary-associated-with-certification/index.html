Regression analysis of district-level data reported in the section on supporting and retaining new teachers. A second printed statement notes "certification rates tend to be higher in districts with higher beginning teacher salaries, once accounting for other district characteristics." Interviewed leaders emphasized retention, with one stating "our best recruiting method is going to be retention."

"The quantitative analysis reinforced this finding, with districts’ teacher turnover in the previous year negatively associated with certification rates."
