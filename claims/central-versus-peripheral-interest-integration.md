`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

> "Most sessions used central integration, suggesting an AI tutor should default to interest-as-an-organizing-frame when alignment and interest detail are available, while allowing peripheral integration when connections are tenuous or the interest is unfamiliar."
