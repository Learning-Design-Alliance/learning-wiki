Evidence¶

Morra 2019¶

Morra, S.; Bisagno, E.; Caviola, S.; Delfante, C.; Mammarella, I. C. (2019). Working Memory Capacity and the Development of Quantitative Central Conceptual Structures. Cognition and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2019.1636797

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Study II examined 92 sixth- and seventh-graders. The article reports that "a test of the rational number CCS was predicted by working memory capacity and, to a lesser extent, by intrusion errors in the listening span." No effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

"Study II, with 92 sixth- and seventh-graders, found that a test of the rational number CCS was predicted by working memory capacity and, to a lesser extent, by intrusion errors in the listening span."
