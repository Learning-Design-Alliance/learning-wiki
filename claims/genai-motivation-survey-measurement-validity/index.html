Ahmed A and Sultan A 2026¶

Ahmed A and Sultan A (2026) Examining the impact of generative AI on student motivation and engagement: the mediating role of autonomy-support and autonomous motivation in education. Front. Psychol. 17:1852265. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1852265

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Measurement model evaluation of the 38-item, nine-construct questionnaire in the 297-student sample. The article reports all loadings above 0.7, alphas above 0.70 (e.g., perceived autonomy 0.89), CR above 0.70, and AVE above 0.50; HTMT and Fornell–Larcker criteria supported discriminant validity.

"All factor loadings in our study exceeded the recommended threshold of 0.7 (Hair et al., 2010 ), indicating a strong convergent validity."
