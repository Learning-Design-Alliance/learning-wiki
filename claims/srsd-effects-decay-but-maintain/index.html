Efficacy of Self-Regulated Strategy Development Instruction for Developing Writers With and Without Disabilities in Rural Schools: A Randomized Controlled Trial. The authors' comparison of posttest and maintenance effect sizes in the rural RCT reports that "the effectiveness of the intervention decreased over time once instruction was completed" yet significant differences maintained (ES = 0.77 elements; ES = 0.38 words).
