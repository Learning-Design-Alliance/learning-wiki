# Author-reported RD findings count toward effectiveness ratings only if standard errors account for clustering of students at unique forcing variable values

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Author-reported findings are included in effectiveness ratings only when study authors account for clustering of students at unique values of the forcing variable; otherwise the WWC will not rely on the statistical significance of the findings. [→ Schochet 2010](#schochet-2010)

## Evidence

### Schochet 2010

Schochet, P., Cook, T., Deke, J., Imbens, G., Lockwood, J.R., Porter, J., Smith, J. (2010). Standards for Regression Discontinuity Designs. Retrieved from What Works Clearinghouse website: http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/pdf/wwc_rd.pdf.

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The panel's reporting requirement for discrete forcing variables states that if authors "do not account for clustering," the WWC "will not rely on the statistical significance of the findings" for effectiveness ratings, though findings may still be included as substantively important.

> "If the authors do not account for clustering, then t he WWC will not rely on the statistical significance of the findings fro m the study . However, the findings can still be included as “substantively important”"
