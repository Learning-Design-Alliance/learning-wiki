## Evidence

### Serhan Olivia 2025

Serhan Olivia, Girard Simon, Sylvestre Olivier. (2025). Perceived value of student-led near-peer teaching in anatomy education within a problem-based learning medical curriculum. Canadian Medical Education Journal, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.80274

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous voluntary 5-point Likert survey of active members who attended at least one 2023-2024 workshop, with "92 participants (57.5% of attendees) responding". Respondents reported the sessions "substantially improved their understanding of anatomy concepts (77.2% strongly agreeing, mean 4.75/5.00)".

> "Most participants felt the sessions substantially improved their understanding of anatomy concepts (77.2% strongly agreeing, mean 4.75/5.00) and medical pathologies (64.1% strongly agreeing, mean 4.64/5.00)."
