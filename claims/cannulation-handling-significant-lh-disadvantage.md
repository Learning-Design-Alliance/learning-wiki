`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the OSCE cannulation station of the same 54-student comparative study, the handling subscale favored RH students: "LH students scored significantly lower (3.96 ± 0.76) than RH students (4.30 ± 0.465)". No effect size was printed, so the magnitude is unknown; total cannulation scores (p = 0.725) did not differ significantly.

> "As expected, in instrument handling, LH students scored significantly lower (3.96 ± 0.76) than RH students (4.30 ± 0.465) (p-value = 0.04)."
