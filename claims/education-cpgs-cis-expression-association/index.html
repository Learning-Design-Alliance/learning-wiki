Jenny van Dongen et al 2018¶

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Cis ( < 100 kb) methylation–expression analysis using 3002 whole-blood samples from the BIOS consortium with matched DNA methylation and RNA-seq. Confirmed methylation–expression relationships for AHRR, RARA, GPR15, MYO1G, ANPEP, ATP9A and SOCS3; cg05575921 and AHRR transcript level correlated negatively (beta = −4.5, p = 1.03 × 10−81).
