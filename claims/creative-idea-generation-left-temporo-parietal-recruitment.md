# Generating alternative uses relative to ordinary characteristics recruits mainly left temporo-parietal regions (left SMG, left AG, bilateral MTG) in both adolescents and adults

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Whole-brain fMRI contrast AU > OC revealed activation in left SMG, left and right MTG, and left AG, FDR corrected, consistent with prior divergent thinking studies. [→ Kleibeuker 2013](#kleibeuker-2013)

## Evidence

### Kleibeuker 2013

Kleibeuker, S. W., Koolschijn, P. C. M. P., Jolles, D. D., De Dreu, C. K. W., and Crone, E. A. (2013). The neural coding of creative idea generation across adolescence and early adulthood. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 7:905. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00905

`q3 · i?`

Whole-brain voxel-wise t-tests on the contrast AU > OC across all 43 participants. The study reports "a number of regions including left SMG, left and right MTG, and left AG" at FDR-corrected p < 0.05, matching prior work by Fink et al. No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Results revealed a number of regions including left SMG, left and right MTG, and left AG (FDR corrected, p < 0.05; see Figure 3 and Table 3), which is in line with prior studies"
