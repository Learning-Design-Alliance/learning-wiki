Evidence¶

Okpube Nnaemeka Michael 2016¶

Okpube Nnaemeka Michael, Anugwo M. N. (2016). Card Games and Algebra Tic Tacmatics on Achievement of Junior Secondary II Students in Algebraic Expressions. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA of male and female students taught with games and without games (Table 5) showed a significant gender effect (F = 80.374, p < 0.05). Male students had an adjusted mean of 79.44 (SD 8.14, n = 71) versus 64.95 (SD 7.06, n = 65) for females, so "the male students achieved better than their female counterparts".

"From the result in Table 5, the games favored significantly the male students than their female counterparts. That is, the male students achieved better than their female counterparts."
