### Burjonrappa Sathyaprasad C. 2012

Burjonrappa Sathyaprasad C., Nerkar Hrishikesh. (2012). Teaching Single-Incision Laparoscopic Appendectomy in Pediatric Patients. JSLS : Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/108680812X13462882737339

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational evaluation of 20 SILS appendectomy operations performed primarily by 3 PGY III residents, judged by a single attending on satisfactory execution of 5 designated critical steps. The authors report "a significant learning curve" in all residents; descriptive statistics were used because of the small study set.

> "A significant learning curve to successfully execute the critical steps of the SILS procedure was noted in all residents evaluated."
