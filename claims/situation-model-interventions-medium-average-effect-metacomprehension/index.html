Evidence¶

Anja Prinz & Stefanie Golke & Jörg Wittwer 2020¶

Anja Prinz & Stefanie Golke & Jörg Wittwer. (2020). To What Extent Do Situation-Model-Approach Interventions Improve Relative Metacomprehension Accuracy? Meta-Analytic Insights. Educational Psychology Review (2020) 32:917–949. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09558-6

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of 28 effect sizes from 17 records comprising 2,236 participants, with effect sizes weighted by inverse variance. The article reports "g = 0.46, 95% CI [0.33, 0.60]" for the average effect versus control conditions.

"the average effect across all situation-model-approach interventions on relative metacomprehension accuracy was significantly positive and of medium size, g = 0.46, 95% CI [0.33, 0.60], p < .001, k = 26"

Related Claims¶

  • Five of seven situation-model interventions significantly improved relative metacomprehension accuracy, concept mapping only marginally (p = .097), and self-explaining not significantly — related
  • Intervention effects on relative metacomprehension accuracy did not differ between school and university students — a narrower finding that bears on this claim

  • Situation-model-approach interventions also directly improved comprehension, albeit with a small effect — related
  • Rereading significantly improves metacomprehension monitoring accuracy relative to reading once (Experiment 1) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Comprehension-test expectancy tended to be more effective than delayed-keywords listing and rereading, though interventions did not substantially differ overall — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Diagramming scaffold also improves absolute metacomprehension accuracy for causal relations, extending the effect beyond relative accuracy — related
  • Generative and visualizing tasks improve metacomprehension and monitoring accuracy but show small or null effects on learning outcomes — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Situation-model-approach interventions show a medium-sized positive effect on metacomprehension accuracy and a small direct effect on comprehension — related
