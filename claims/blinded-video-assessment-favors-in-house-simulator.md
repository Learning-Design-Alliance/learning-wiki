## Evidence

### Scherer-Quenzer AC 2026

Scherer-Quenzer AC, Reinhart K, Diessner J, Altides A, Buechel J, Blau-Schneider B, Sauer ST, Deschler-Baier B, Woeckel A, Kiesel M. (2026). Comparative validation of a cost-effective in-house 3D-printed LLETZ simulator versus a commercial training model. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-026-08413-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Blinded video evaluation by two independent senior experts in gynecology and colposcopy of the student cohort's excisions, using checklist, GRS, and overall scores. Tables 2–4 print adjusted p values and Cohen's d (e.g., overall mean attempt 5: d = 1.588).

> "The in-house simulator demonstrated a statistically significant advantage over the conventional simulator starting from the fourth attempt for the checklist mean, from the third attempt for the GRS mean and for the overall mean"
