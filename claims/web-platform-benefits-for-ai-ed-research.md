The authors' argument for the Web as an excellent implementation platform for AI-Ed researchers. They expect that ideas developed in these systems "will have a growing use in practical Web-based education," increasing AI-Ed's impact on everyday educational process.

> "Systems developed on the Web have longer lifespan and better visibility. A research idea implemented in a Web-based system has much better chances to influence the research community than an idea simply presented in a paper."
