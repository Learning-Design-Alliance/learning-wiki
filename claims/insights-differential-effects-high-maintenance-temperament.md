Model 2 cross-level interaction (Treatment x high maintenance temperament) in the school-randomized growth models (Table 4, N = 435). The Tx x high maintenance temperament coefficients were -0.49*, 0.07*, and -0.04* for behavior problems, engagement, and off-task behavior respectively.
