## Evidence

### Wu et al. 2021

Huimin Wu, Yiqun Guo, Yingkai Yang, Le Zhao and Cheng Guo. (2021). A Meta-analysis of the Longitudinal Relationship Between Academic Self-Concept and Academic Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

One-stage meta-analytic SEM (OSMASEM) of longitudinal studies in which ASC and achievement were measured at least twice (N = 55 studies). The cross-lagged path from achievement to ASC was significant: "achievement predicted ASC, β = 0.16, p \< 0.01". No standardized effect-size magnitude beyond β is printed for a separate impact coding.

> "achievement predicted ASC, β = 0.16, p \< 0.01; and ASC predicted achievement, β = 0.08, p \< 0.01 (see Fig. 3), supporting the REM."
