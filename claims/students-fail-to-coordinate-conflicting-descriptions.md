David T. Brookes and Eugenia Etkina. (2007). Using conceptual metaphor and functional grammar to explore how language used in physics affects student learning. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.1319 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0704.1319](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0704.1319)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theoretical hypothesis advanced from the authors' data on students and physicists, extending Chi et al.'s ontological-misclassification account. Physicists coordinate wave (process) and particle (matter) descriptions flexibly; the paper argues students hearing language such as potential well learn to think of the graph as a physical object but remain unaware of when this picture is appropriate.

> "Our second hypothesis is that students are failing to co-ordinate appropriately the many diﬀerent descriptions that they learn from physicists' language."
