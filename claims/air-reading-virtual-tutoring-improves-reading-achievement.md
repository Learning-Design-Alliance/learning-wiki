### Neitzel 2025

Neitzel, A.J., & Storey, N. (2025). Air Reading: A randomized evaluation of a virtual tutoring model in Louisiana schools. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://education.jhu.edu/crre

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student-level randomized controlled trial across five elementary schools in a Louisiana district; 170 second and fourth graders below grade level in reading analyzed (89 treatment, 81 control) under an intent-to-treat approach with covariate-adjusted OLS regression. The article reports "an effect size of +0.31 (p < .05)" and "an estimated additional 4.2 months of learning".

> "Air Reading students scored significantly higher on reading assessments compared with control students (ES = +0.31 or 4.2 months of additional learning."
