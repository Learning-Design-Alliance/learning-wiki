# Simulation design and educational practice scores increase with academic year, with first-year students lowest and fourth-year students highest

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ANOVA showed significant differences by academic year: first-year students scored lowest (SDS M = 2.81 ± 1.03; EPQ M = 3.04 ± 1.17) and fourth-year students highest (SDS M = 3.7 ± 0.53; EPQ M = 3.75 ± 0.58). [→ Alsadi 2025](#alsadi-2025)

## Evidence

### Alsadi 2025

Alsadi, M., Alosta, M. R., Othman, E. H., Obiedallah, H., & Nashwan, A. J. (2025). Optimising Educational Practices in Simulation‐Based Nursing Education: Results of A National Survey. Nursing Open, 12(4), e70172. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70172

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA (Welch's test with adjusted F ratio) with Games-Howell post hoc comparisons of SDS and EPQ scores across four academic years in the national survey; Table 4 reports p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "First‐year students have statistically lower overall SDS scores (M = 2.81 ± 1.03) and EPQ scores (M = 3.04 ± 1.17) compared to student scores in other year levels. Also, students in the fourth‐year level have statistically higher overall SDS scores (M = 3.7 ± 0.53) and EPQ scores (M = 3.75 ± 0.58) compared to student scores in other year levels."
