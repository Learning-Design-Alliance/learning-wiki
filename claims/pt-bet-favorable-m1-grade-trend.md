### Eiring Lisa 2026 (2)

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Grade distribution analysis of the oral M1 examination comparing 2018, 2021, 2024, and 2025 cohorts. The 2025 cohort (n = 175) showed the highest proportion of top grades and reduced failure rates compared to previous years.

> "In fall 2025 in turn (n = 175), there was a significant improvement: 14.3% of participants received the highest grade, and the proportion of lower grades decreased again, to 13.0% for grade 4 and 16.0% for grade 5."
