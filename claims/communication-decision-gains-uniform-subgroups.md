## Evidence

### Hammer Silke 2026

Hammer Silke, Lock Johan F., König Sarah, Happel Oliver, Paul Mila M. (2026). Learning gain of an ATLS®-based interprofessional and multidisciplinary in-situ simulation training of trauma resuscitation. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-026-03146-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analyses of retrospective learning gains per CRM factor among 238 participants across anesthesiology, surgery, and radiology. The article reports no significant between-group differences on these factors; nulls are reported as non-significant tests without effect sizes, so equivalence was not tested.

> "Between group analyses demonstrated no significant difference across departments (p = 0.848). Prior simulation training experience did not affect learning gains on the factors (p > 0.05)."
