A randomized controlled trial (Stockard, 2010) comparing 29 Reading Mastery Signature Edition students with 28 control students taught the Scott Foresman Basal Reading Program. The study authors found "statistically significant positive effects" on AIMS Web words read correct; the WWC improvement index for reading fluency is "+19 percentile points for a single finding from one study."

"Stockard (2010) found statistically significant positive effects of Reading Mastery on the AIMS Web Curriculum-Based Measurement Words Read Correct for 4th graders."
