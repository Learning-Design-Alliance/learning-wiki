## Evidence

### Orr Robin M. 2020

Orr Robin M., Cohen Bruce S., Allison Stephen C., Bulathsinhala Lakmini, Zambraski Edward J., Jaffrey Mark. (2020). Models to predict injury, physical fitness failure and attrition in recruit training: a retrospective cohort study. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-020-00260-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Outcome analysis of the same retrospective cohort of 19,769 recruits compared attrition and Recruit Fitness Assessment failure across the two course types. The study reports attrition of "5.2% in the 28-day course and 5.0% in the 80-day course (P = 0.66)" and RFA failure of "30.0% vs. 12.1%" (P < 0.001). The authors note these percentages may be underestimated due to missing values.

> "Attrition rate was not significantly different in the two courses: 5.2% in the 28-day course and 5.0% in the 80-day course (P = 0.66). The rate for failure to pass the final battery of fitness assessments (i.e., the RFA) was significantly higher in the 28-day course (30.0% vs. 12.1% in the 80-day course; P < 0.001)."
