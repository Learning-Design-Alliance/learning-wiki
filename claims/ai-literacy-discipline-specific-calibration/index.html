The review attributes this position to Stolpe et al. (2026) and their Discipline-Specific AI Literacy (DiSAIL) framework, which distinguishes the potential for and enactment of discipline-specific AI literacy. The article endorses this view within its synthesis; no empirical effect size is reported.

"Stolpe et al. (2026) further argue that AI literacy requires discipline-specific calibration because academic fields vary in terms of vocabularies, genres, reasoning strategies, evidentiary conventions, and epistemological standards."
