## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The non-self-reported calibration error is lower at the lowest knowledge levels in all three surveys, conflicting with both perfect metacognition and the DKE prediction. [→ Simone Lackner 2023](#simone-lackner-2023)

## Evidence

### Simone Lackner 2023

Simone Lackner, Frederico Francisco, Cristina Mendonça, André Mata, Joana Gonçalves-Sá. (2023). Some scientific knowledge is a dangerous thing: overconfidence grows non-linearly with knowledge. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01677-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-survey analysis of the EB, GSS and Pew datasets using the conservative non-self-reported calibration error (mean proportion of incorrect answers per knowledge bin). The article reports the error is "consistently lower for the least knowledgeable"; no effect size is printed.

> "We find that this calibration error is consistently lower for the least knowledgeable, in the three surveys, despite these surveys having covered different populations and spanning three decades, from the first EB to the Pew survey."
