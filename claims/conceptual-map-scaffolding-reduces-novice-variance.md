# In one randomized working-paper experiment, conceptual-map scaffolding gave LLM-using novices marginally higher scores (p < .10) and lower score variance, with a marginal benefit concentrated among low-AIC novices

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`
<!-- title before 2026-10-15: Conceptual-map scaffolding modestly improves novice LLM-condition performance and reduces outcome variance, with benefits concentrated among low-AIC novices -->

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Scaffolded novices scored higher than unguided novices (M = .45 vs .38), a difference significant only at p < .10. [→ Bharat Anand 2026](#bharat-anand-2026)
`q3 i?` The scaffolding × AIC interaction was negative and weakly significant, so scaffolding benefited low-AIC participants most and compressed heterogeneity. [→ Bharat Anand 2026 (2)](#bharat-anand-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Bharat Anand 2026

Bharat Anand, Lihi Idan. (2026). Generative AI and the Productivity Divide: Human–AI Complementarities in Education and Knowledge Work (Working Paper). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18143 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.18143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.18143)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Scaffolding sub-condition within the randomized experiment's LLM arm: novices given a conceptual roadmap with recommended sequencing versus unguided novices. Variance in post-intervention scores was lower among scaffolded novices (SD=.14) than unguided novices (SD=.22).

> "The average post-intervention exam score among scaffolded novices wasM=.45,SD=.14, compared toM=.38,SD= .22 in the baseline LLM group, with a weakly significant difference (p>.05,p<.10)."

### Bharat Anand 2026 (2)

Bharat Anand, Lihi Idan. (2026). Generative AI and the Productivity Divide: Human–AI Complementarities in Education and Knowledge Work (Working Paper). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18143

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regression comparing scaffolded novices to baseline LLM novices with a treatment-type × AIC interaction; the authors interpret scaffolding as compressing heterogeneity by disproportionately aiding weaker extractors.

> "The coefficient on the interaction term was negative and weakly significant (p > .05,p < .10), indicating that the marginal benefit of scaffolding diminished as AIC increased."
