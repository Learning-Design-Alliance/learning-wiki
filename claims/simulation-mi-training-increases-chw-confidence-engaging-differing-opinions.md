## Evidence

### Chance-Revels 2026

Chance-Revels, R., Phan, Q., Crawford, K., Steiger, L., & Swan, B. A. (2026). Enhancing Community Health Worker Communication: Evaluating Simulation-Based Training in Motivational Interviewing. Journal of Community Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10900-025-01543-w

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

A paired-samples t-test on pre- and post-training confidence surveys of 277 CHW trainees in the ARCHWAY program showed a statistically significant moderate improvement in confidence engaging with differing opinions (d = 0.50, 95% CI [0.47, 0.62]). This was the strongest effect among the three domains measured.

> "Trainees (n = 277) reported greater confidence in engaging in conversations with individuals who hold differing opinions following the training, t(276) = 8.30, p <.001. The corresponding effect size, d = 0.50, 95% CI [0.47, 0.62], indicated a statistically significant moderate improvement from pre- to post-training."
