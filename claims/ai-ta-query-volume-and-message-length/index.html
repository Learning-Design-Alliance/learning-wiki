Evidence¶

Madeleine Eastwood 2026¶

Madeleine Eastwood, Harshith Narne, Joseph Hilby, Paul Denny, Ashish Aggarwal, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Guardrails or Roadblocks? Effects of Pedagogical Style and Context Awareness in AI Teaching Assistants for Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29995

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Log-data analysis of 2,467 chat messages from the randomized experiment; a Kruskal-Wallis H test found a significant difference in queries across conditions (χ²(3)=13.28, p=.004). The article suggests Socratic guidance without context "required more back-and-forth with the chatbot to make progress".

"Students in the Socratic + No Context condition sent substantially more queries (𝜇= 11.1per problem) than students in the Socratic + Full Context (𝜇= 5.1), Direct + Full Context (𝜇= 4.1), and Direct + No Context (𝜇= 4.7) conditions."

Madeleine Eastwood 2026 (2)¶

Madeleine Eastwood, Harshith Narne, Joseph Hilby, Paul Denny, Ashish Aggarwal, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Guardrails or Roadblocks? Effects of Pedagogical Style and Context Awareness in AI Teaching Assistants for Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29995

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Log-data analysis of average message length across the four conditions; a Kruskal-Wallis H test found a significant difference (χ²(3)=29.96, p<.001). The article suggests that without context, "students often had to describe their situation in more detail before receiving useful support".

"Students in the Direct + No Context condition wrote the longest messages on average (𝜇= 126characters per message), followed by Direct + Full Context (𝜇= 81), Socratic + No Context (𝜇= 69), and Socratic + Full Context ( 𝜇= 54)."
