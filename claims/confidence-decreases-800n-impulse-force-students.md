## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported being confident in delivering 200N and 400N impulse forces with time to peak force under 150ms, but felt less confident delivering 800N impulse forces. [→ Nim Casper et al 2024](#nim-casper-et-al-2024)

## Evidence

### Nim Casper et al 2024

Nim Casper et al. (2024). The association between students’ confidence and ability to modulate spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a cross-sectional study. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-024-00557-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 149 first- to third-year chiropractic students using a 100mm electronic Visual Analogue Scale, with confidence levels plotted in Fig. 2. The study found participants "felt confident in delivering impulse forces of 200N and 400N" but "felt less confident" for 800N.

> "Participants generally felt confident in delivering impulse forces of 200N and 400N within a time to peak force of less than 150ms. However, when asked to deliver impulse forces of 800N, they felt less confident."
