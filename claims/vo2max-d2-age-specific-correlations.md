### González-Fernández Francisco Tomás; Delgado-García Gabriel; Coll Jesús Siquier; Silva Ana Filipa; Nobari Hadi; Clemente Filipe Manuel 2023

González-Fernández Francisco Tomás; Delgado-García Gabriel; Coll Jesús Siquier; Silva Ana Filipa; Nobari Hadi; Clemente Filipe Manuel. (2023). Relationship between cognitive functioning and physical fitness in regard to age and sex. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-023-04028-8

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations between VO2max from the 6MWT and D2 scores in the cross-sectional sample of 187 students, reported per age group; the printed r values are 0.32, 0.48 and -0.43.

> "a positive moderate correlation was found for TA in the 11–12 years old group and for O in the 9 years old group (r = 0.32, p = 0.041 and r = 0.48, p = 0.003, respectively). A negative moderate correlation was found for O in the 11–12 years old group (r = -0.43, p = 0.005)."

### González-Fernández Francisco Tomás; Delgado-García Gabriel; Coll Jesús Siquier; Silva Ana Filipa; Nobari Hadi; Clemente Filipe Manuel 2023 (2)

González-Fernández Francisco Tomás; Delgado-García Gabriel; Coll Jesús Siquier; Silva Ana Filipa; Nobari Hadi; Clemente Filipe Manuel. (2023). Relationship between cognitive functioning and physical fitness in regard to age and sex. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-023-04028-8

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations between VO2max and D2 scores in the 15-year-old subgroup of the same sample; the article reports a "positive large correlation" with printed effect sizes r = 0.59, r = 0.53 and r = 0.52 for TR, TR+ and TR-.

> "a positive large correlation was found for TR, TR + and TR- in students of 15 years old (r = 0.59, p = 0.002 [Fig. 2a]; r = 0.53, p = 0.007 [Fig. 2b], r = 0.52, p = 0.008 [Fig. 2c])."
