## Evidence

### Manoharan A. 2025

Manoharan A., Perialathan K., Krisnan L., Koh WM, Teo CH, Ramdzan SN, Krishnan M., Kanawathy S. (2025). Knowledge, practices, and diagnostic barriers in chronic cough management among primary care doctors in Klang Valley, Malaysia: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Pulmonary Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12890-025-04053-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 170 primary care doctors in Klang Valley (October–December 2024) using an adapted validated questionnaire. The knowledge item on duration thresholds found "18·8% (n = 32/170) considering a cough lasting more than 8 weeks as chronic". No effect size is printed for this descriptive result.

> "Participants defined chronic cough based on different duration thresholds, with 18·8% (n = 32/170) considering a cough lasting more than 8 weeks as chronic, while the majority 64·7%, (n = 110/170) classified it as chronic after 2 weeks"
