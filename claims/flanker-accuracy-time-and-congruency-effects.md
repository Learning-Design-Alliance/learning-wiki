Improving Kindergarten Readiness in Children with Developmental Disabilities: Changes in Neural Correlates of Response Monitoring. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RM-ANOVA on behavioral accuracy in the RCT ERP subsample. Children also "performed better on the congruent (M = 62.86, SD = 14.59) than the incongruent trials (M = 58.01, SD = 13.12)", indicating the expected flanker effect.

> "The RM-ANOVA to examine change in percent of correct trials indicated a main effect of time (see Table 2) such that children performed more accurately at T2 (M = 62.36, SD = 14.28) compared to T1 (M = 56.58, SD = 14.92)."
