## Evidence

### Kuncel et al. 2004

Nathan R. Kuncel, Sarah A. Hezlett, and Deniz S. Ones. (2004). Academic Performance, Career Potential, Creativity, and Job Performance: Can One Construct Predict Them All? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.148

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of transitional criteria (Table 3), based on limited samples (student-teaching k = 5, N = 444). Printed effect sizes: ρ = –.04 for student-teaching ratings and ρ = –.06 for courses completed (k = 3, N = 179), the latter described as "effectively unrelated to MAT scores".

> "Internship and practicum ratings had a small, positive relationship with the MAT (ρ ⫽ .22), whereas student-teaching performance had a near zero negative association with the MAT (ρ ⫽ –.04). In both cases, SDρ equaled zero."
