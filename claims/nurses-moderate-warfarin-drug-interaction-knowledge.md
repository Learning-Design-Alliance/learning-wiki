Subih Maha, Rababa Majdi, Aryan Fatima Sabri, Alnaeem Mohammad, AlRahahleh Mohammad H, Al niarat Tahany Fareed, Saleh Zyad T., Alsulami Ghaida Shujayyi, Almagharbeh Wesam T., Elshatarat Rami A. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07074-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional self-administered questionnaire study of 176 registered nurses in four Amman hospitals. The 26-item drug-interaction subscale yielded a mean of "8.76 out of a possible 26 (SD = 2.26)", described as moderate awareness; item-level correct rates ranged from 33.5% (naproxen) to 53.4% (atenolol, PPIs).

> "The results highlight a range of knowledge among participants regarding the effects of various medications on warfarin anticoagulation, with a mean knowledge score of 8.76 out of a possible 26 (SD = 2.26), suggesting moderate awareness overall"
