## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2011

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Edward F. Redish, and Jessica Watkins. (2011). Students' Views of Macroscopic and Microscopic Energy in Physics and Biology. PERC 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.5801](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.5801)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of five undergraduates, focusing on one student ("Dennis"), conducted twice during an algebra-based introductory physics course. Asked about differences between energy in physics and biology/chemistry, he said his physics class "talks a lot more about physical objects," which he confirmed meant macroscopic objects. No effect size is reported.

> "his physics class "talks a lot more about physical objects, stuff like that, which you don't really talk about in bio or chem."
