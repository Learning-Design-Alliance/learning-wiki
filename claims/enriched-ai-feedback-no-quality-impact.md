Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Hatim Lahza, Jason M. Lodge, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2025). Emotionally Enriched Feedback via Generative AI. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105363)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on RiPPLE quality scores (0-5, integrating student ratings, instructor moderation and algorithmic assessment via Expectation-Maximization and graph-based trust propagation) for 744 control and 751 experimental resources: "no statistically significant difference" (p = 0.470), both medians 4.10; equivalence was not tested.

> "the analysis revealed that there was no statistically significant difference in the quality scores between the experimental and control groups (p= 0.470), with both groups achieving a score of M dn= 4.10 as shown in Table 7."
