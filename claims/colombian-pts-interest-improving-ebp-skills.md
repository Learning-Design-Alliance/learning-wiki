`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported interests and motivation results from the cross-sectional survey of 1,064 Colombian physical therapists. "57.9% stated that they strongly agreed with learning or improving skills" for incorporating EBP; 49.6% strongly agreed and 47.4% agreed on the need to increase use of scientific evidence in daily practice.
