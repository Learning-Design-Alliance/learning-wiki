## Evidence

### Carrier 1992

Carrier, M., & Pashler, H. (1992). The influence of retrieval on retention. Memory & Cognition, 20(6), 633-642. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03202713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus interaction test from the ANOVAs in Experiments 1 and 2, in which cued recall was tested after 5 min on Day 1 and after 21-27 h on Day 2. The article reports the interaction was "not reliable (F < 1)" in both experiments, while testing-day main effects showed forgetting. Mean pairs recalled (pure ST vs TTST) were 5.8 vs 6.4 on Day 1 and 3.7 vs 4.1 on Day 2 in Experiment 1, and 5.7 vs 6.4 and 3.9 vs 4.6 in Experiment 2. A non-reliable interaction shows no detected difference in the advantage between delays, not that the advantages were equal.

> "The interaction of presentation con dition x testing day was not reliable (F < 1). As in the analysis of the results of Experiment 1, we performed a sign test on the number of subjects showing an advantage at cued recall for the TTST condition."
