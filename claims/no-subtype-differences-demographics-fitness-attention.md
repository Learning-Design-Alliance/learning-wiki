## Evidence

### Chang Hui-Ping 2025

Chang Hui-Ping, Lin Tzu-Wen, Chiu Yi-Hsiang, Chou Chien-Chih, Chen Jui-Fu, Pan Cheng-Chen. (2025). Associations of physical fitness with sustained overt attention and academic performance in children with learning disabilities. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19549

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Demographic comparison (Table 1) between 30 language-type and 41 mathematics-type children with LD; the article reports "no significant difference" across all listed variables and no gender difference (χ2 = .26, p > .05). No effect sizes are printed.

> "there was no significant difference among the children with learning disabilities (30 language (42.30%) and 41 mathematics (57.70%)) in terms of age, BMI, IQ, physical activity, physical fitness, and DAUF sustained attention (t = .80, 1.37, −1.07, .05, −.13, 1.56, −1.25, −1.06, .33, & 1.55, p > 0.05)"
