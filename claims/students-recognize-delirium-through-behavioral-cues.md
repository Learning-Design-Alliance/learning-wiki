### Kılıç Gülşen 2026

Kılıç Gülşen, Işık Sevcan Avcı, Çevik Banu. (2026). Nursing students’ first clinical encounters with delirium: a qualitative study of experiential learning and professional development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09980-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 13 fourth-year nursing students found that "removing medical devices, attempting to leave the bed, and resisting care served as primary warning signs" of delirium, reported as participant quotations in the results section.

> "Students primarily recognized early delirium through behavioral and motor cues. Actions such as removing medical devices, attempting to leave the bed, and resisting care served as primary warning signs of clinical deterioration."
