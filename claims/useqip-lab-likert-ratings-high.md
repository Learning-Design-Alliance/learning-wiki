Post-workshop surveys (2013-2025) with response rates from 52% (13/25 in 2025) to 74% (23/31 in 2018) asked students to rate each lab on 4-point Likert scales. Table II reports averages such as QKD interest 3.71/4 and NMR novelty 3.78/4, with N values of 101-118 per lab (novelty 44 or 30).
