## Evidence

### Julien Broisin 2017

Julien Broisin, Rémi Venant & Philippe Vidal. (2017). Lab4CE: a Remote Laboratory for Computer Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 27:154–180. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0079-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the exploratory study: after filtering, 55 students and 17 quiz questions were analyzed; activity was defined as inputs divided by time spent in the system. The printed correlation is "r=0.41, p=.002"; the authors note students scoring above 70% tended to produce more.

> "The scatter plot of Fig. 9 shows a significant posit ive Pearson correlation (i.e., Pearson 's r) between students ' activity and students ' achievement: r=0.41, p=.002."
