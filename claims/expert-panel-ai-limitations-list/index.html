Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J., Lester, J. & Fusco, J. (Eds.) (2020). AI and the future of learning: Expert panel report [Report]. Digital Promise. https://circls.org/reports/ai-report

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Expert-panel discussion at a facilitated online convening; the report records the limitations experts posted, including "Presence of bias in data" and failure to attend to equity and learner differences. This is qualitative expert opinion, not an empirical test.

"The experts were also highly aware of limitations and weaknesses of today’s AI. Some specific limitations that the experts called out include: • Limitation and weaknesses of available datasets, which limit the resulting AI progress • Presence of bias in data"
