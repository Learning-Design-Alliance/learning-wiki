### Borselle D. 2025 (2)

Borselle D., Więckowski A., Patkowski D. (2025). Evaluating suturing skill improvement for pediatric minimally invasive esophageal anastomosis model: an observational cohort study based on simulator training. Scientific Reports 15, 7692. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91307-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

ROC-based odds ratios from day-3 knot results (Tables 5 and Fig. 6) show analytic economy ≥ 9 scores with OR 20.0 (2.33–172), analytic distance ≥ 7.8 with OR 12.0 (2.28–63.3), and OSATS time and motion ≥ 3 with OR 11.6; the article states "economy of movement, distance, Jolt and OSATS time and motion emerged as the most influential factors".

> "several parameters, including economy of movement, distance, Jolt and OSATS time and motion emerged as the most influential factors determining the probability of success"
