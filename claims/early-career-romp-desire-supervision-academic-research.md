A distributed workforce survey of ACPSEM radiation oncology medical physicists, with 182 respondents of whom 151 were registered ROMPs, compared reported actual and desired weekly hours by career stage (Figs 1-2). The authors report "majority of early career members would like to get more involved in supervision and academic work"; no effect size is printed.

> "The survey responses showed that majority of early career members would like to get more involved in supervision and academic work . Similarly, they are interested in doing more research. These trends were not evidence in the 2020 workforce survey for experienced members."
