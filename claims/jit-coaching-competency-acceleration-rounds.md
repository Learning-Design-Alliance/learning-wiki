`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Competency acceleration analysis across up to five successive intubations per trainee in the randomized trial; round three showed 95.8% (46/48) vs 77.6% (45/58), OR 6.64 (1.42 to 31.1), P=0.02.

> "in round two, by which the treatment group had received their second just-in-time training, their first attempt success rate was 90.9% (50/55) versus 73.9% (51/69) in the control group (odds ratio 3.53 (95% CI 1.22 to 10.2), P=0.02)."
