`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Component-ranking item from the same end-of-term survey (N=18). Students "ranked the self-paced+AI block first (mean rank 2.56)" and the test second; free-text comments included "Weekly exams push me to study". No effect sizes printed.

> "Asked which components they would miss most if removed, students ranked the self-paced+AI block first (mean rank 2.56) and the 30-minute closed-book test second (3.22), ahead of mini-lectures, floor-walking, same-session grading, and recap."
