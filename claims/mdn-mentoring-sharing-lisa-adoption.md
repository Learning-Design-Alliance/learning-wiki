A descriptive account in Section 3 of this proceeding: a meeting on mentorship was suggested by the Fermi-LAT representative, members invited collaborators, and Fermi-LAT shared a program proposal and interest forms that the LISA Consortium is using as springboards.

> "The Fermi-LAT group graciously shared their resources with the MDN including a program proposal and interest forms, and encouraged others to use them as springboards for mentoring programs in their own collaborations. This had a direct impact on the LISA Consortium, which is now pursuing a mentoring program."
