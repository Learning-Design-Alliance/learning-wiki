Social validity was assessed with a 6-point Likert-type questionnaire on usefulness of coaching components and strategies, coaching dosage, coach relationship, and sustainability. Each teacher strongly agreed she had a good working relationship with her coach, though some found observations stressful.

> "Overall, the teachers found the coaching strategies (e.g., observation, feedback, action planning) useful."
