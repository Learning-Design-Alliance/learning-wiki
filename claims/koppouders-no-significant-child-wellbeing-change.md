## Evidence

### van der Zanden Rianne AP 2010

van der Zanden Rianne AP, Speetjens Paula AM, Arntz Karlijn SE, Onrust Simone A. (2010). Online Group Course for Parents With Mental Illness: Development and Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1394

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Completers-only paired t tests on the SDQ in the pilot (n = 28). The article reports declines that were "not significant (P < .10)" with small effect sizes; intention-to-treat results were consistent. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Hyperactivity and total problems declined but not significantly (P < .10); effect sizes were small (d = 0.30 and d = 0.23). No significant effects were found for emotional problems."
