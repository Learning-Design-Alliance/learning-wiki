Confirmatory factor analysis in the present sample (N = 821): AI Anxiety Scale showed "CFI = 0.967, TLI = 0.958, SRMR = 0.054, RMSEA = 0.063"; the Job Search Anxiety Scale showed CFI = 0.947, RMSEA = 0.057. Internal consistencies were α = 0.921 and α = 0.870.
