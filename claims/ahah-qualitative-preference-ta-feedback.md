Interactive Cosmology Visualization Using the Hubble UltraDeep Field Data in the Classroom. [doi:10.32374/aej.2021.1.1.003](https://doi.org/10.32374/aej.2021.1.1.003)

Instructors' qualitative observations from the fall 2020 AST 113/SES 123 lab study: students "consistently expressed to instructors a preference for activities with AHaH", and teaching assistants reported higher positive feedback on AHaH labs than standard exercises. No quantitative measure or effect size is printed.

> "Finally, throughout the semester students consistently expressed to instructors a preference for activities with AHaH, as well as greater ease of understanding with the tool. Teaching Assistants regularly reported notably higher amounts of positive feedback on AHaH-related labs as compared to standard lab exercises."
