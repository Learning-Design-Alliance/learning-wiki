## Evidence

### Francois St-Hilaire 2021

Francois St-Hilaire, Nathan Burns, Robert Belfer, Muhammad Shayan, Ariella Smofsky, Dung Do Vu, Antoine Frau, Joseph Potochny, Farid Faraji, Vincent Pavero, Neroli Ko, Ansona Onyi Ching, Sabina Elkins, Anush Stepanyan, Adela Matajova, Laurent Charlin, Yoshua Bengio, Iulian Vlad Serban, and Ekaterina Kochmar. `q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Log-based correlation analysis on Platform B from the comparative study, split by pre-quiz score. The article reports r=−0.58 for participants above 50% on the pre-quiz and r=−0.32 for those below, suggesting interventions were more helpful for lower-scoring students.

> "for participants who scored over 50% on the pre-quiz, there is a stronger negative correlation between their learning gains and the total number of hints they received during the course (r=−0.58). For participants who scored below 50% on the pre-quiz, this correlation is weaker ( r=−0.32)."
