Evidence¶

High School Redirection 2007¶

High School Redirection. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: High School Redirection. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial with control group crossover at the Model Alternative High School in Stockton, California, based on school records for Cohort 1 (374 students). The report states intervention youth were "enrolled 39 more days on average in the first follow-up year than control group youth (110 days versus 71 days)" and 17 more days in year two; dropout differences favored the intervention but were significant only at the 0.10 level.

"The Stockton study indicated that High School Redirection youth were enrolled 39 more days on average in the first follow-up year than control group youth (110 days versus 71 days) and 17 more days on average in the second follow-up year (67 days versus 50 days)."
