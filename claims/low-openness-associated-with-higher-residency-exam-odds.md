## Evidence

### Muntean Lorena Mihaela 2022

Muntean Lorena Mihaela, Nireștean Aurel, Sima-Comaniciu Andreea, Mărușteri Marius, Zăgan Cătălin Andrei, Lukacs Emese. (2022). The Relationship between Personality, Motivation and Academic Performance at Medical Students from Romania. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9332223/

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Prospective pilot study of 179 final-year medical students who completed the DECAS Personality Inventory before the National Residency Exam; Chi-square association testing between openness dimension and exam score. The article reports "the chance that a student with low openness will get a higher grade is 3.92 times higher" with OR = 0.392, p = 0.01, and d = −0.516.
