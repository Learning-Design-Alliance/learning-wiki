Evidence¶

Magdaş and Bontea 2011¶

Ioana Magdaş, Timea Bontea. (2011). Developing digital competences using an educational software. A pedagogical research. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 4, Number 4, ISSN 2065-1430. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056072

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment comparing two XII-grade classes at a technical college (experimental n=22, control n=24) on a common complex final Access task. Table 25 prints means of 7,909091 vs 6,958333; the authors report "statistically significant at P = 0.03" from the t-test in Table 26.

"We observe that the value of t Stat calculated is greater than the value of t corresponding to P = 0.05 significance threshold even higher than P = 0.027928. From this we conclude that the difference between the two areas is statistically significant at P = 0.03 significance threshold."
