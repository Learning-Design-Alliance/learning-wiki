`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot implementation at Liberty High School in Hillsboro, Oregon: a unit was adapted into a digital module using Miro boards, PhET's Wave on a String simulation, Formative.com tasks, Wizer.me assessments, and Google Classroom. The article reports "a total of 50 students aged 14–15". Usability feedback was collected via a teacher interview.

> "The pilot involved two ninth-grade classes, with a total of 50 students aged 14–15. The topic covered was “Waves, Sound, and Sound Propagation.”"
