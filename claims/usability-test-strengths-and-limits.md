## Evidence

### Minji Kim 2025

Minji Kim, Cheolil Lim. (2025). AI chatbot design principles to enhance the collective efficacy in collaborative learning. Asia Pacific Education Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12564-025-10080-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Usability test with eight participants in two groups (chatbot designers and instructors of collaborative learning), using semi-structured interviews of about 30 minutes analyzed thematically. Participants reported the design "enabled learners to address challenges independently" and supported team mediation.

> "The chatbot's capabilities were found to include enabling learners to address challenges independently, facilitating mediation within the team, and offering precise guidance to prevent unproductive time expenditure."
