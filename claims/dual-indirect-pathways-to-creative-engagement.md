## Evidence

### Chen Y 2026

Chen Y (2026) When universities advocate GAI but practice falls short: student appraisals and creative process engagement in art education. Front. Psychol. 17:1955192. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1955192

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM bootstrap analysis of the 425-respondent survey tested both mediation paths. The hindrance pathway was "negative and significant" while the challenge pathway was positive and significant, supporting opposing indirect effects on creative process engagement.

> "The estimated indirect association between inconsistency and creative process engagement through hindrance appraisal was negative and significant ( β = −0.060, p = 0.004), whereas the estimated indirect association through challenge appraisal was positive and significant (β = 0.217, p < 0.001), supporting H1b and H2b."
