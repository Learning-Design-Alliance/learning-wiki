## Subclaims
`q2 i?` DFW rates were lower among URMs who engaged in PLTL whether or not they were concurrently enrolled in the laboratory course (p = 0.024 enrolled; p = 0.040 not enrolled). [→ Snyder JJ 2016](#snyder-jj-2016)

## Evidence

### Snyder JJ 2016

Snyder JJ, Sloane JD, Dunk RDP, Wiles JR. (2016). Peer-Led Team Learning Helps Minority Students Succeed. PLoS Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002398

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two chi-square analyses splitting URM students by concurrent laboratory enrollment, reported in the Our Findings section. Both comparisons show significantly lower DFW rates with PLTL; no effect sizes are printed. The not-enrolled subgroup is small (n = 21).

> "we also found that DFW rates were lower among URMs who engaged in PLTL whether they were enrolled in the laboratory course (x 2 = 5.074, n = 69, df = 1, p = 0.024) or not (x 2 = 4.200, n = 21, df = 1, p = 0.040)"
