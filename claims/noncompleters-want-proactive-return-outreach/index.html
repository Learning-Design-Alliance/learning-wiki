Evidence¶

Nodine 2012¶

Nodine, T., Jaeger, L., Venezia, A., and Bracco, K. R., with research support from Public Agenda. (2012). Connection by Design: Students' Perceptions of Their Community College Experiences. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED541651

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

In the focus groups with non-completers, participants said they wished their college had reached out when they left, encouraged them to return, and sent information about events and classes. The report states "Many former students who have dropped out want colleges to be more proactive in inviting them to return and letting them know how to return." One clear message from non-completers as a whole was welcoming re-enrollment outreach.
