## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Semi-structured interviews showed the experimental group held positive views about the effectiveness of metacognitive strategy instruction while the control group was largely unfamiliar with the strategies. [→ Habibian 2015](#habibian-2015)

## Evidence

### Habibian 2015

Habibian, M. (2015). The Impact of Training Metacognitive Strategies on Reading Comprehension among ESL Learner’s. Journal of Education and Practice, 6(28). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1081318

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews conducted in participants' native language after instruction, analyzed by comparing experimental and control groups. All but one experimental participant agreed the strategies could enhance reading ability; the control group was largely unfamiliar with them.

> "The result indicated that the experimental group have positive view regarding effectiveness of the instruction of metacognitive strategies. The students agreed that, learning and practicing metacognitive strategies can enhance the ir reading ability, with the exception of only one student who remarked that, maybe teaching these strategies could enhance reading ability."
