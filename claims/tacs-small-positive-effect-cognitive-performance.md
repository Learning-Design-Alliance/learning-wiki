## Evidence

### Tae Lee Lee 2023

Tae Lee Lee, Hanall Lee and Nyeonju Kang. (2023). A meta-analysis showing improved cognitive performance in healthy young adults with transcranial alternating current stimulation. npj Science of Learning 8:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00152-9

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 93 cognitive-performance comparisons drawn from 50 qualified studies comparing active tACS with sham, finding "a significant low overall effect of tACS protocols on cognitive performance improvements" with SMD = 0.161. The 56 included studies comprised 1797 healthy young adults.

> "The random-effects meta-analysis on 93 total comparisons from the 50 studies identified a significant low overall effect of tACS protocols on cognitive performance improvements ( SMD = 0.161; SE = 0.027; 95% CI = 0.109–0.214; Z = 6.038; P < 0.001)."
