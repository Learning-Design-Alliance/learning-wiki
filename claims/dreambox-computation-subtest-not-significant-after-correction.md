# The DreamBox effect on the MAP Computation subtest was not statistically significant after WWC multiple-comparison correction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` The MAP Computation subtest showed a raw effect size of 0.15 favoring DreamBox Learning (p = 0.03), but a multiple-comparison correction set a critical p-value of 0.01, so the WWC does not find this result statistically significant. [→ computation](#computation)

## Evidence

### computation

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013, December). Elementary School Mathematics intervention report: DreamBox Learning. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/627

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Supplemental subscale findings from the same RCT (n = 546 for this subtest): intervention mean 163.00 (SD 20.70) versus comparison 158.80 (SD 19.50), effect size 0.15, improvement index +6, p = 0.03. The report notes "the WWC does not find these results to be statistically significant" after correction.
