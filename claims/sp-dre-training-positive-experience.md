## Evidence

### Nikendei Christoph 2015

Nikendei Christoph, Diefenbacher Katja, Köhl-Hackert Nadja, Lauber Heike, Huber Julia, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Herzog Wolfgang, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Jünger Jana, Krautter Markus. (2015). Digital rectal examination skills: first training experiences, the motives and attitudes of standardized patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0292-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Post-training semi-structured interviews with 4 SPs, thematically analysed. SPs unanimously described the training as "very positively, as interesting, informative and empathetic", but some raised that participation was not an everyday activity and was physically very demanding; one SP had doubts about earning money for participation.

> "The SPs responded unanimously that they experienced the training very positively, as interesting, informative and empathetic."
