## Evidence

### Karlsen 2026

Karlsen, N., & Aalbergsjø, S. (2026). Developing pre-service teachers' TPACK for programming simulations. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10674-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Within-between (hybrid) model for Prototype 1 (n = 19). The authors report "the differences between PSTs' TK rather than their individual change in TK" explained TPACK variation, with between-TK 0.80 and within-PCK 0.74.

> "The results, shown in Table 6, indicate that it is the differences between PSTs' TK rather than their individual change in TK, along with the individual changes in PCK within PSTs that explain the variation in TPACK."
