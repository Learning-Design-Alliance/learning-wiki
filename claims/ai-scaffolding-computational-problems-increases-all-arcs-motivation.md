## Evidence

### Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang 2026

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang. (2026). Digital learning innovation: engineering students’ learning motivation for AI scaffolding. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10576-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test comparing post-survey motivational means between conventional (Activity #1) and AI-featured (Activity #1.1) computational problem-solving activities among 103 engineering students. All four ARCS categories showed significantly increased motivation (p < .001).

> "All four categories show significantly increased participants’ motivation (p < .001) when they use AI-featured methods, which implies that AI-featured methods had a substantial impact on various aspects of participants learning motivation."
