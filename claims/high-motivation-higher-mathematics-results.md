### Priyono 2018

Priyono. (2018). The Implementation of PAIKEM (Active, Innovative, Creative, Effective, and Exiting Learning) and Conventional Learning Method to Improve Student Learning Results. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 124-137. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181986

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA of mathematics learning outcomes with learning model and motivation as factors. Table 7 shows high-motivation mean 82.867 versus low-motivation 72.819; Table 11 reports B_Factor F = 27.373, Sig. .000. The article states high-motivation results "were higher than those with low motivation".
