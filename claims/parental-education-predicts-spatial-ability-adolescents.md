### Pilato Jazelle 2025

Pilato Jazelle, Cortes Robert, Peterson Emily Grossnickle, Uttal David, Kolvoord Robert, Green Adam. (2025). Neurocognitive correlates of spatial thinking mediate the relation between socioeconomic status and STEM achievement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-29345-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of the cognitive sample (N=302) using the paper folding task as the spatial ability measure. The article reports "adjusted R2 = 0.04" and "Β = 0.75, t(302) = 3.12, p = .002" for parental education.

> "The model including parental education, gender, and age explained a significant proportion of variance in spatial ability, adjusted R2 = 0.04, F(3, 298) = 4.93, p = .002. As parental education increased, spatial ability scores increased, Β = 0.75, t(302) = 3.12, p = .002"
