### Cai Manyuan 2024

Cai Manyuan, Chen Yanping, Luo Fangting, Zheng Yanqun, Liu Ying, Xiao Bing, Wang Xiaoyan, Sun Lulu, Lin Yi, Zeng Xianhu, Tan Shuni, Liu Ke, Gu Yuanbo, Wang Jinghua, Ning Xianjia, Yuan Jing, Wen Min, Cao Jing. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression in the cross-sectional study of 1916 medical staff: "nurses were 35% less likely to be unqualified than physicians (OR = 0.65, 95% CI: 0.46–0.91, P = 0.013)"; technicians (OR 1.33) and administrators (OR 0.82) were non-significant.

> "When analyzed by profession, nurses were 35% less likely to be unqualified than physicians (OR = 0.65, 95% CI: 0.46–0.91, P = 0.013). Technicians and administrators did not demonstrate any significant differences."
