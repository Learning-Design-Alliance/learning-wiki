## Evidence

### S. Bansal 2025

S. Bansal, M. Yadav, J. Zhou, R. A. Ebert‐Allen, R. M. Klute, W. F. Bond, S. Bhat. (2025). Medical Students' Perception of Automated Note Feedback After Simulated Encounters. The Clinical Teacher 22(6): e70273. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70273

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group thematic analysis identified phrase recognition limitations and false positives as a theme. One student noted "I found a couple of things that they had thought I had written in my note, but I had not actually written them. So, it's just like a false positive."

> "Students reported that the automated system had occasionally erred in phrase recognition or awarded false positives where credit for an item found was given when it should not have."
