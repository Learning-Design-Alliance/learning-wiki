## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The distal chain pathway social support→self-efficacy→learning engagement→academic performance was significant (0.015), and social support influenced learning engagement more strongly (β = 0.41) than self-efficacy did (β = 0.17). [→ Zhang Xiangping 2024](#zhang-xiangping-2024)

## Evidence

Zhang Xiangping, Qian Wensheng. (2024). The effect of social support on academic performance among adolescents: The chain mediating roles of self-efficacy and learning engagement. PLoS One 19(12): e0311597. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311597

### Zhang Xiangping 2024 (2)

Zhang Xiangping, Qian Wensheng. (2024). The effect of social support on academic performance among adolescents: The chain mediating roles of self-efficacy and learning engagement. PLoS One 19(12): e0311597. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311597

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model results from the 265-student sample comparing the two paths into learning engagement; the article prints β = 0.17 for self-efficacy versus β = 0.41 for social support, both P < 0.001.

> "Furthermore, the study revealed that adolescents’ self-efficacy had a lesser impact on their level of learning engagement (β = 0.17, P < 0.001) compared to the influence of social support (β = 0.41, P < 0.001). This suggests that the primary source of learning engagement among adolescents is social support."
