## Evidence

### Siniarski Aleksander 2025

Siniarski Aleksander, Piątek Paweł, Karcińska Aleksandra, del Carmen Yika Alicia, Szostek Kamil, Dziwiński Tomasz, Stąpór Maciej, Golińska-Grzybała Karolina, Piórkowski Adam, Gackowski Andrzej. (2025). Simulation training significantly improves the performance of novice students achieving the standard transesophageal echocardiography views. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06917-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison of the RAR index (cumulative absolute differential of probe coordinates) for the complete examination in 26 novices; the article reports significance for ante-retroflexion and a non-significant trend for rotation, with no effect size printed.

> "A trend toward improvement, although without statistical significance, was observed for probe rotation. In contrast, a statistically significant improvement was noted for ante-retroflexion of the probe (Fig. 7)."

### Siniarski Aleksander 2025 (2)

Siniarski Aleksander, Piątek Paweł, Karcińska Aleksandra, del Carmen Yika Alicia, Szostek Kamil, Dziwiński Tomasz, Stąpór Maciej, Golińska-Grzybała Karolina, Piórkowski Adam, Gackowski Andrzej. (2025). Simulation training significantly improves the performance of novice students achieving the standard transesophageal echocardiography views. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06917-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subanalysis of four representative views (ME mitral commissural, ME bicaval, ME asc Ao, TG basal SAX) in the same 26 students; printed rotation RAR medians fell for every view, e.g. TG Basal SAX 3225 to 2152. No effect size printed.

> "Notably, in a subanalysis of four selected TEE views, we demonstrated a significant decrease in the RAR indexes for probe rotation for all projections between Test 1 and Test 2 (Fig. 8)."
