## Evidence

### Mundy LK 2020

Mundy LK, Canterford L, Hoq M, Olds T, Moreno-Betancur M, Sawyer S, Kosola S, Patton GC. (2020). Electronic media use and academic performance in late childhood: A longitudinal study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0237908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal and cross-sectional models across both NAPLAN domains in the same cohort; Table 2 prints videogame coefficients such as β = 3.4 (95% CI: -7.3 to 14.1, p = 0.54) for >1 hour/day on grade 5 reading. No equivalence test was reported.

> "Associations between time spent playing video games at grade 3 and NAPLAN reading or numeracy scores at grade 5 were very small."
