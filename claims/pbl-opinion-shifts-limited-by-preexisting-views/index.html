Clemens Megan 2026¶

Clemens Megan, Boparai Josheil Kaur, Dawe Russell, Avery Susan. (2026). Problem-based and related learning approaches in family medicine residency: a scoping review of four countries. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.81284

q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of seven studies assessing opinion change. The review reports four found perspective shifts, e.g. on pharmaceutical marketing ethics (p = 0.033), while others found no significant changes, including one study with no significant change in perception after the intervention. Only p-values are printed.

"Seven studies assessed changes in learners' opinions about the topic, corresponding to Level 2A of the adapted Kirkpatrick's Model. Four of these studies found shifts in perspective, such as the ethical appropriateness of pharmaceutical marketing (p = 0.033),"

Clemens Megan 2026 (2)¶

Clemens Megan, Boparai Josheil Kaur, Dawe Russell, Avery Susan. (2026). Problem-based and related learning approaches in family medicine residency: a scoping review of four countries. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.81284

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of two included studies (Agrawal et al.; Colenda et al.) found that learners holding extreme pre-existing opinions, such as a negative perception of managed care, showed no significant changes. The review attributes this pattern to pre-existing opinion strength.

"Notably, learners with strong pre-existing opinions appeared less likely to alter their views,23,24 suggesting that PBL may not equally benefit all participants."
