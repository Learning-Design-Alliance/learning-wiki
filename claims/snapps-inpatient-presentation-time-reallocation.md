## Evidence

### Jain Vishakha 2019

Jain Vishakha, Rao Siddharth, Jinadani Mariya. (2019). Effectiveness of SNAPPS for improving clinical reasoning in postgraduates: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 19: 224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1670-3

`q3 · i?`

Open-label randomized controlled trial in the inpatient setting of a Medicine department, with case presentations as the unit of analysis. SNAPPS presentations "took on average 1.6 min more to make their entire case presentations (7.19 vs. 5.56, p value < 0.01)."

> "The student in the SNAPPS group took on average 1.6 min more to make their entire case presentations (7.19 vs. 5.56, p value < 0.01)."

### Jain Vishakha 2019 (2)

Jain Vishakha, Rao Siddharth, Jinadani Mariya. (2019). Effectiveness of SNAPPS for improving clinical reasoning in postgraduates: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 19: 224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1670-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same trial, same unit of analysis. Summary time did not differ significantly (3.15 vs. 3.48, p value =0.177), while "The time take for discussion was more in SNAPPS group as compared to control group (4.04 vs. 2.07, p value < 0.01)."

> "The time taken to summarize was less in the SNAPPS group (3.15 vs. 3.48, p value =0.177). The time take for discussion was more in SNAPPS group as compared to control group (4.04 vs. 2.07, p value < 0.01)."
