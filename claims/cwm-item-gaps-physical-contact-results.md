## Evidence

### Yanase Yukiko 2026

Yanase Yukiko, Hiragi Saki, Kawahara Yasuhiro. (2026). Effects of generic medical communication training on physiotherapy students’ ability to communicate in movement-based clinical scenarios: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08560-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Chi-squared tests of each of the eight CWM items against group, comparing the learned student group with the PT group (Table 7). Only 5 of 15 learned students scored on physical contact versus 15 of 15 PTs, and 6 versus 15 on explanation of test results; the printed effect sizes were "φ = 0.71", φ = 0.39, and φ = 0.66.

> "Significant associations were found for physical contact (p < 0.001, effect size: φ = 0.71), moving around the patient (p = 0.032, effect size: φ = 0.39) and explanation of test results (p < 0.001, effect size: φ = 0.66) between the learned and PT groups."
