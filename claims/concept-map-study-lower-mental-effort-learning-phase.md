## Evidence

### Dyer 2015

Dyer, J.-O., Hudon, A., Montpetit-Tourangeau, K., Charlin, B., Mamede, S., & van Gog, T. (2015). Example-based learning: comparing the effects of additionally providing three different integrative learning activities on physiotherapy intervention knowledge. BMC Medical Education, 15, 37. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0308-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on nine-point mental effort ratings collected after each learning module in the randomized experiment. The article reports a large condition effect: "F (2, 88) = 7.68, P = .001; η2 = .149".

> "There was a large main learning condition effect on the mean mental effort invested during the learning activity (ANOVA, F (2, 88) = 7.68, P = .001; η2 = .149)."

### Dyer 2015 (2)

Dyer, J.-O., Hudon, A., Montpetit-Tourangeau, K., Charlin, B., Mamede, S., & van Gog, T. (2015). Example-based learning: comparing the effects of additionally providing three different integrative learning activities on physiotherapy intervention knowledge. BMC Medical Education, 15, 37. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0308-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tukey post-hoc HSD comparisons on learning-phase mental effort in the same experiment. The model concept map study condition invested less effort than the concept map completion (P = .001) and self-explanation (P = .015) conditions; only P values are printed, no effect size.

> "The mean mental effort invested in the model concept map study condition was lower than in the concept map completion (Tukey HSD, P = .001) and self-explanation conditions (P = .015) (see Table 2)."
