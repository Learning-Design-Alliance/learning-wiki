## Evidence

### Simonsen Bjoerg O 2014

Simonsen Bjoerg O, Daehlin Gro K, Johansson Inger, Farup Per G. (2014). Improvement of drug dose calculations by classroom teaching or e-learning: a randomised controlled trial in nurses. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-006025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled open trial with parallel group design; 183 nurses randomised to e-learning (n=92) or classroom (n=91) and tested before and after a 2-day course. Post-course scores showed "11.6 (2.0) and after classroom teaching 11.9 (2.0) (p=0.18, NS)". A per-protocol analysis of 141 completers did not alter this finding.

> "The number of correct answers after e-learning was 11.6 (2.0) and after classroom teaching 11.9 (2.0) (p=0.18, NS); improvement were 0.5 (1.6) and 0.9 (2.2), respectively (p=0.07, NS)."
