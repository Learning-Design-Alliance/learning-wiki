## Evidence

### Kjellenberg K 2026

Kjellenberg K, Wang R, Horre JN, Helgadóttir B, Ekblom Ö, Singh A, Nyberg G. (2026). Associations between physical activity, fitness, cognitive and academic performance in Swedish adolescents: Findings from a cross-sectional study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0344087

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM path analysis (n = 688 math model, n = 687 language model) testing cognitive performance as mediator of fitness–grades associations, adjusted for gender, parental education, parental country of birth and pubertal status; mediation model fit was good (RMSEA 0.028/0.026, CFI 0.999).

> "In the math models, cognitive performance accounted for 40% of the association (Fig 2a). Further, in the language model, cognitive performance accounted for 40% of the association (Fig 2b)."
