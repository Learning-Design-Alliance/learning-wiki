### Hwijoon Lee and Saiph Savage 2026

Hwijoon Lee and Saiph Savage. 2026. Understanding Game Coaching on Gig Platforms. In Proceedings of The Annual Symposium on Computer-Human Interaction in Play (CHI PLAY '26). ACM. https://doi.org/10.1145/3831353

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on perspectives on AI. P14 anticipated AI "would give too many general tips, basically, not specific to the person," and P4 argued "You cannot teach AI what is not even known to the internet."

> "Most coaches were skeptical that AI could replace human coaching, citing frequent game updates and the complexity of real-time gameplay."

### Hwijoon Lee and Saiph Savage 2026 (2)

Hwijoon Lee and Saiph Savage. 2026. Understanding Game Coaching on Gig Platforms. In Proceedings of The Annual Symposium on Computer-Human Interaction in Play (CHI PLAY '26). ACM. https://doi.org/10.1145/3831353

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview results on the desired assistant role across pre- and post-session phases. P17 noted "people just didn't trust my coaching after seeing that AI talking to them instead of me" after his AI response tool replied to clients unnotified.

> "coaches consistently drew the same line: AI was welcome when it reduced administrative overhead, but not when it risked displacing the direct interaction through which they built trust and authority with students."

### Hwijoon Lee and Saiph Savage 2026 (3)
