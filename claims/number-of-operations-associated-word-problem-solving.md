### T. Vessonen 2024

T. Vessonen, M. Dahlberg, H. Hellstrand, A. Widlund, J. Korhonen, P. Aunio, A. Laine. (2024). Task Characteristics Associated with Mathematical Word Problem-Solving Performance Among Elementary School-Aged Children: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09954-2

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis (K = 69) of standardized mean differences between single-step and multi-step word problems. The review reports number of operations as the one numerical characteristic related to performance, within the overall g = 0.39 to 4.26 range; no separate pooled estimate for this dimension is printed in the supplied text.

> "one of the two numerical task characteristics (i.e., number of operations) were related (g = 0.39 to 4.26) with elementary school-aged children's mathematical word problem-solving."
