## Evidence

### Krišťák 2014

Krišťák, Ľ., Němec, M., Danihelová, Z. (2014). Interactive Methods of Teaching Physics at Technical Universities. Informatics in Education, 13(1), 51–71. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2014.03

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pedagogical experiment in 2010 with method C (lecture divided into blocks with problems, P&E seminars); hypothesis H1(2010) tested with a non-standardised posttest, Kolmogorov-Smirnov, F-test and Student T-test at significance level 0.05. The article reports the result as "statistically significantly" without an effect size.

> "The research confirmed that the use of the interactive method C in teaching Physics influenced statistically significantly the level of students’ knowledge."
