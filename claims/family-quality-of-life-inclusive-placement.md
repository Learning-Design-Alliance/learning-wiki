# Adult family members of included children reported improved expectations, family activities, and social outcomes, with 71% reporting regular best-friend play and visitation

## Subclaims

`q1 i?` Family members of children in inclusive K–3 settings reported expecting more generally, a better future, and more socially; themes included doing more things as a family and children taking on more responsibilities. [→ Strain 2017](#strain-2017)
`q1 i?` 71% of these families indicated their child had best friends they played with and visited with regularly. [→ Strain 2017 (2)](#strain-2017-2)

`q1 i?` Adult family members of children from the 25 selected pairs enrolled in inclusive settings responded to eight questions by telephone, with themes derived via key-word-in-context qualitative methodology. [→ Strain 2017](#strain-2017-2)

`q1 i?` On the question of best friends, 71% indicated yes. [→ Strain 2017 (2)](#strain-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Strain 2017

Phillip S. Strain. (2017). Four-Year Follow-Up of Children in the LEAP Randomized Trial: Some Planned and Accidental Findings. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education, 37(2), 121–126. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121417711531

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Telephone interviews with adult family members of the 25 included-pair children, transcribed and analyzed with key-word-in-context methodology, yielded themes including "Expect more in general", "Expect better future", and doing more things as a family (Table 2).

> "Adult family members of children from the 25 selected pairs who were enrolled in inclusive settings in kindergarden through third grade responded orally via the telephone to eight questions and their responses were transcribed."

### Strain 2017 (2)

Phillip S. Strain. (2017). Four-Year Follow-Up of Children in the LEAP Randomized Trial: Some Planned and Accidental Findings. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education, 37(2), 121–126. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121417711531

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Question 8 of the family interview asked whether the child had best friends played with and visited regularly; per Table 2, "71% indicate yes". The author notes no prior autism study asked adult family members to reflect on this topic.

> "Does _________ have any best friends that he or she plays with and visits with regularly? 71% indicate yes"
