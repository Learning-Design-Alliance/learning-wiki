`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative video case study, Day 4. Grace and Jill were distracted by a neighboring table's loud conversation; Sara asked about setting the thermometer to Fahrenheit, and after a pause Ashley grabbed the thermometer and switched the unit. The authors report the group was re-engaged and the thermometer read in Fahrenheit.

> "The effects of Sara and Ashley's EER are that the group was re-engaged in the experiment, and the thermometer's temperature read in Fahrenheit."
