### Mallick R. 2020

Subcutaneous immunoglobulin in primary immunodeficiency – impact of training and infusion characteristics on patient-reported outcomes. BMC Immunology. [doi:10.1186/s12865-020-00371-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12865-020-00371-y)

Analysis of total SCIG training time as a dependent variable in the survey cohort found "A shorter total training time was predicted by a greater ease of training"; Table 6 shows OR = 2.44 for ease rated 6–7 (p < 0.001).
