`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Leinonen et al. examined a one-hour session in an introductory thermal physics course at the University of Eastern Finland; the reported analysis focused on 65 students participating in both intervention and examination, using written explanations and peer-discussion recordings.

> "scientifically correct explanations for work in an isobaric process increased from 52% during individual work to 80% after peer discussion. Because the activities formed a sequence, the results do not isolate the contribution of peer discussion alone."
