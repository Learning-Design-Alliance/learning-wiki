## Evidence

### Deng Guangwei 2019

Deng Guangwei, Zhao Di, Lio Jonathan, Chen Xinyu, Ma Xiaopeng, Liang Liang, Feng Chenpeng. (2019). Strategic elements of residency training in China: transactional leadership, self-efficacy, and employee-orientation culture. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1792-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis of survey data from 247 residents in five tertiary hospitals in central and eastern China, testing teachers' transactional leadership style effects on training performance. The article reports "contingent reward (β = 0.47, p < 0.001)" and "contingent punishment (β = 0.27, p < 0.001)" as significant positive predictors; no standardized effect size beyond the regression coefficients is printed.

> "The model 4 of Table 3 shows that: two types of TLSs: contingent reward (β = 0.47, p < 0.001) and contingent punishment (β = 0.27, p < 0.001) all have significant positive impacts on internship performance, supporting Hypothesis 1."
