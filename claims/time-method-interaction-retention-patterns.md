## Evidence

### Ehsanzadeh Seyed Jafar 2025

Ehsanzadeh Seyed Jafar, Dwyer Eric, Barzegar Mohammad, Hamidi Hadi. (2025). Teaching medical terminology: comparing the effectiveness of lecture-based and corpus-assisted methods. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07584-7

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the Results section of the four-group quasi-experiment found "a significant interaction between time and teaching method (F(6, 72) = 3.45, p =.005, η² = 0.22)"; Mauchly's test confirmed sphericity (W = 0.92, p =.314). Figure 1 plots the retention pattern across time intervals.

> "A significant interaction between time and teaching method (F(6, 72) = 3.45, p =.005, η² = 0.22) showed that retention patterns differed by method (see Fig. 1)."
