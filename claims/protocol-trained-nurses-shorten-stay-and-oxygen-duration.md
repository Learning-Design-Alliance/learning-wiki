# Patients cared for by protocol-trained nurses have shorter hospital stays (8.4 ± 6.2 vs 12 ± 9.8 days) and shorter oxygen-therapy duration (7.2 ± 5.4 vs 10.4 ± 8.9 days)

## Evidence

### Diab Samar Salah Eldin Mohamed 2022

Diab Samar Salah Eldin Mohamed, Ali Shaimaa Ahmed Awad, Abed Shaymaa Najm, Elasrag Gehan Abd Elfattah Atia, Ramadan Osama Mohamed Elsayed. (2022). Effectiveness of Standardized Protocol for Oxygen Therapy on Improving Nurses’ Performance and Patients’ Health Outcome. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19105817

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of 55 control and 50 study patients in the quasi-experimental study; oxygen-therapy duration was also shorter in the study group (7.2 ± 5.4 vs 10.4 ± 8.9 days, p = 0.001, Wilcoxon tests). Only p-values printed, no effect size.

> "the mean hospitalization period was 12 ± 9.8 days within the range of 1–60-day for the control group, compared with 8.4 ± 6.2 within the range of 1–29-day for the study group with a highly statistically difference at p = 0.001."
