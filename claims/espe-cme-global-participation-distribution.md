Descriptive cross-sectional analysis of mandatory postcourse surveys completed from August 2022 to September 2025 (N=567). The article reports "global participation, but most learners were practicing in Europe" with the printed regional counts and percentages; no effect size is reported.

> "There was global participation, but most learners were practicing in Europe (n=333, 59%), followed by Asia (n=124, 22%), Africa (n=53, 9%), the Americas (North America, n=45, 8%; and South America, n=11, 2%), and Oceania (n=1, 0%; Figure 1 and Multimedia Appendix 2)."
