# Score improvement after the MVP varies by training level: most medical students and residents improved post-test scores, while fellows did not significantly improve

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 90% of medical students, 77% of residents, but only 60% of fellows had improved post-test scores, and the fellows' improvement was not statistically significant. [→ Jantausch Barbara A. 2023](#jantausch-barbara-a-2023)

## Evidence

### Jantausch Barbara A. 2023

Jantausch Barbara A., Bost James E., Bhansali Priti, Hefter Yosefa, Greenberg Isabella, Goldman Ellen. (2023). Assessing trainee critical thinking skills using a novel interactive online learning tool. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2178871

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the same 62-trainee pre/post study (11 students, 36 residents, 15 fellows) using paired t-tests. Medical students improved at "p=0.001" and residents at "p<0.001", while fellows' improvement was not significant ("p=0.72").

> "Improved post-test scores occurred in 90% of medical students, p=0.001, 77% of residents, p<0.001, 60% of fellows, p=0.72 and 75% of trainees overall, p=<0.001."
