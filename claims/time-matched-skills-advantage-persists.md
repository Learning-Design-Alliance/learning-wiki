Sensitivity analysis matching training duration between a 28-student experimental subgroup and the full control group. The "Structured Clinical Skills Assessment advantage persisted with a 7.3-point difference", statistically significant at p = 0.012.

> "When matching total training duration (experimental subgroup: n = 28 with 5.5−6.0h vs. full control: n = 79), the Structured Clinical Skills Assessment advantage persisted with a 7.3-point difference (t = 2.56, p = 0.012), retaining 83% of the original effect size."
