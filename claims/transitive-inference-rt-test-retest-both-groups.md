## Evidence

### Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge 2018

Belén C. Guerra-Carrillo and Silvia A. Bunge. (2018). Eye gaze patterns reveal how reasoning skills improve with experience. npj Science of Learning 3:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0035-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Bayesian analysis of response times on correct transitive inference trials; accuracy was at ceiling at pre-test, so RTs were the performance measure. The article reports BF10 ≈ 39.24 for the Time-only model and notes the Reasoning group's BF10 ≈ 27.41 with Δ ≈ 18%.

> "the strongest model included only the effect of Time (BF 10 ≈ 39.24 ± 2.41%). Thus, although the evidence of improvement was stronger for the Reasoning group, both groups got faster at solving the task between time points"
