## Evidence

### Fonseca Guilherme Reis Borges Coelho 2026

Fonseca Guilherme Reis Borges Coelho, Tipoe George Lim, Ganotice Fraide Augustin Jr. (2026). Comparing the outcomes of the technology-enhanced learning of sectional anatomy with and without preceding demonstrations: A study about the worked-example effect in anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70267

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year 1 (N=110) and Year 2 (N=59) stratified randomized experiments comparing a worked-example demonstration group (Group B) to a self-directed control group (Group A). The article reports that "the absolute and normalized knowledge gains were higher in Group B than in Group A" in both experiments.

> "In both experiments, the absolute and normalized knowledge gains were higher in Group B than in Group A."

### Fonseca Guilherme Reis Borges Coelho 2026 (2)

Fonseca Guilherme Reis Borges Coelho, Tipoe George Lim, Ganotice Fraide Augustin Jr. (2026). Comparing the outcomes of the technology-enhanced learning of sectional anatomy with and without preceding demonstrations: A study about the worked-example effect in anatomy education. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70267

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year 1 experiment posttest comparison between groups. The article reports that "the knowledge scores of Group A (mean = 74.35; SD = 14.24) were significantly lower (U = 2119.000; p < 0.001) than Group B (mean = 83.84; SD = 10.31)."

> "At posttest, the knowledge scores of Group A (mean = 74.35; SD = 14.24) were significantly lower (U = 2119.000; p < 0.001) than Group B (mean = 83.84; SD = 10.31)."
