### Juuso Jaakola 2026

Juuso Jaakola, Vlad Stirbu, Teiko Heinosaari. (2026). Using Learning Analytics to Study Novice Learners’ Work on Quantum Circuit Simulator Tasks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.09499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.09499)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observed answer-space analysis of submissions to tehtava-virheenkorjaus in the 2024–2025 cohort, reported in Figs. 6, 7, and 8. The article suggests some learners reconstrued the task as a broader circuit-building problem rather than a local correction.

> "The most common wrong pattern was a trivial submission with no change to the circuit. The correct side contained 54 distinct submitted constructions."

### Juuso Jaakola 2026 (2)

Juuso Jaakola, Vlad Stirbu, Teiko Heinosaari. (2026). Using Learning Analytics to Study Novice Learners’ Work on Quantum Circuit Simulator Tasks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.09499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.09499)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the virheenkorjaus answer-space findings in Section VI-D: the aggregate outcome measures hid the instructional-design issue that the answer-space profile surfaced.

> "Most of these learners did eventually reach a correct answer, so the issue would remain largely invisible in completion statistics alone. The answer-space view turns that hidden design problem into something concrete enough to revise."
