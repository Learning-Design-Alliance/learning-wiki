## Evidence

### Ergen 2017

Ergen, B., & Kanadli, S. (2017). The effect of self-regulated learning strategies on academic achievement: A meta-analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 55-74. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Heterogeneity test across the 21 included studies, reported in Table 2. The significant Q and very high I2 indicated the studies do not share a common effect size, so the random effects model was used for pooling.

> "The Q -value was calculated as 740.77 , with 20 degrees of freedom (df). This value exceeds the critical value (31.410) of χ 2 with 24 df and confidence intervals of 95%. The I2 index is 97.30%, which demonstrates a high amount of heterogeneity among the studies."
