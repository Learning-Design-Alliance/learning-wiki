## Evidence

### Meurling Lisbet 2013

Meurling Lisbet, Hedman Leif, Sandahl Christer, Felländer-Tsai Li, Wallin Carl-Johan. (2013). Systematic simulation-based team training in a Swedish intensive care unit: a diverse response among critical care professions. BMJ quality & safety 22(6):485-494. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2012-000994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post SAQ single-item ratings (five-point scale) of experienced collaboration/communication quality, reported as percentages rating it high or very high (figures 2 and 3). Nurse assistants' rating of collaboration with specialists rose from "41%" to "58%" (p=0.04); no other profession-pair changed.

> "Compared to data before simulation-based team training (figure 2) the only difference was registered for nurse assistants who reported a better collaboration/communication with specialists (58% vs 41%, p=0.04)."
