`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Correlational evidence cited in the overview (Simpkins and Davis-Kean 2005; Updegraff and Eccles 1996). The guide uses it to argue that "improving girls' beliefs about their abilities could alter their choices and performance." No effect sizes printed.

> "girls, as well as boys, who have a strong self-concept regarding their abilities in math or science are more likely to choose and perform well in elective math and science courses and to select math- and science-related college majors and careers."
