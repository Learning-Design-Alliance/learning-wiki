## Evidence

### Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al 2021

Nair Akshay Gopinathan et al. (2021). Effectiveness of simulation-based training for manual small incision cataract surgery among novice surgeons: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-90410-4

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a separately tested result. They argue trainees could "repeatedly practice each step in tunnel construction" until assessed competent, at no additional cost in VR simulation, and that repetitive deliberate practice explains the effect favouring the experimental group.

> "Thus, the likely explanation for the observed effect favouring EG is the guided deliberate practice on the HelpMeSee Eye Surgery Simulator."
