# The baseline country difference in GenAI pedagogical readiness was explained by exposure and institutional conditions: the Kazakhstan coefficient fell from B = 0.205 to a non-significant B = 0.041

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In hierarchical regression on 568 faculty, the Kazakhstan coefficient decreased from B = 0.205 to B = 0.041 and was no longer statistically significant after prior GenAI use, recent AI-related training, multilingual resource access, institutional support, perceived permission, policy clarity, risk sensitivity, and disciplinary background were included. ## Evidence

### Bi T 2026

Bi T, Araily S, Lyu A and Xiu S (2026) Research on the optimization of the training system of university faculty development centers in the context of GenAI: a comparative analysis based on Chinese and Kazakhstani universities. Front. Psychol. 17:1890508. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1890508

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Hierarchical regression predicting overall baseline readiness (N = 568), with country, demographics, exposure, and institutional-contextual indicators entered sequentially; Model 4 R2 = 0.442. The unadjusted Kazakhstan coefficient was B = 0.205, SE = 0.035, p < 0.001. No standardized effect size is printed for the final coefficient.

> "When institutional support, permission to experiment, multilingual resource access, policy clarity, and risk sensitivity were included, the coeﬃcient decreased to B = 0.041 and was no longer statisticallysigniﬁcant."
