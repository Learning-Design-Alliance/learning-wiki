## Evidence

### Pereira RI 2024

Pereira RI, da Silva EMK. (2024). Interdisciplinary training program for pediatric cardiorespiratory arrest using rapid cycle deliberate practice: A descriptive cross-sectional study. São Paulo Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1590/1516-3180.2023.0271.16022024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre- and post-performance assessment of chest compressions in a single-group descriptive cross-sectional training study of 302 professionals, measured with RescueNet Code Review software against AHA parameters. Adequate technique rose from "approximately 26%" to 91%; the paired t-test gave P < 0.01. No effect size is printed.

> "The overall quality of chest compressions measured at the pre-intervention educational mastery training time was inadequate, and only approximately 26% had technique quality within the given standards. In the post-intervention analysis, 91%, on average, reported the quality of the technique used according to the guidelines"
