## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Huang Tianlong 2024

Huang Tianlong, Zhou Shun, Wei Qiaoyan, Ding Chun. (2024). Team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning in medical postgraduates training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05650-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported time logs (pre-class preparation, post-class review, review writing) from both groups, compared by independent t-test. TCLEBL front-loaded workload before class and reduced it after; no effect sizes printed.

> "the TCLEBL group had a significant increase in pre-class preparation time compared to the LBL group (55.75 ± 10.166 vs. 106.667 ± 12.383, p < 0.001). However, the TCLEBL group spent significantly less time on post-class review (60.5 ± 11.459 vs. 40.238 ± 10.305, p < 0.001)"

### Huang Tianlong 2024 (2)

Huang Tianlong, Zhou Shun, Wei Qiaoyan, Ding Chun. (2024). Team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning in medical postgraduates training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05650-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same time-log analysis: summed extracurricular time was statistically indistinguishable between LBL and TCLEBL groups (p = 0.898); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "there was no significant difference in the total extracurricular time expended between the two groups (323.75 ± 30.987 vs. 322.619 ± 24.679, p = 0.898)"
