## Evidence

### Or 2023

Or, C. (2023). The role of attitude in the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology: A meta-analytic structural equation modelling study. International Journal of Technology in Education and Science (IJTES), 7(4), 552-570. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijtes.504

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

OSMASEM synthesis of 21 UTAUT studies in educational contexts (N = 21 studies; α = .973), fitting Model 1 with attitude predicting use behavior. The analysis found "attitude (β= .261; p< .001)" significantly predicted UB. In Model 2, attitude was the strongest predictor of BI (β= .297; p< .001).

> "FC emerged as the strongest predictor of UB (β= .376; p< .001), while both attitude (β= .261; p< .001) and BI (β= .204; p< .001) had significant positive effects on UB."
