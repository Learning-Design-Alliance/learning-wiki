## Evidence

### Starch and Elliott 1913

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1913). Reliability of Grading Work in Mathematics. The School Review, 21(4), 254–259. https://doi.org/10.1086/436086

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of per-question marks from 62 returns: 49 teachers graded on a 0-12 scale and 13 on a 0-10 scale. For question ten, Fig. 4 gives median 5.1 and probable error 1.1 on the 0-12 scale; transposed to the 0-100 scale this becomes 8.8, close to the whole-paper 7.5.

> "If we transpose this probable error into terms of the usual scale of o to Ioo by multiplying it by 8, we obtain a probable error of 8.8. This is nearly the same as the probable error of all the marks in Fig. 3, namely 7.5. Hence we see that the marks of the answer for a single question of the paper vary about as widely as those of the entire paper."
