Descriptive survey results from the same 318-student cross-sectional study, reporting proportions with Wilson 95% CIs for the four teaching-perception items; no inferential test of the content-versus-delivery gap is reported.

> "Course content (218/318, 68.6%, 95% CI 63.3–73.4) and overall teaching quality (205/318, 64.5%, 95% CI 59.1–69.5) were rated positively by roughly two‐thirds of students, whereas only about half considered teaching methods diverse (168/318, 52.8%, 95% CI 47.3–58.3) or felt encouraged to express their views (159/318, 50.0%, 95% CI 44.5–55.5)"
