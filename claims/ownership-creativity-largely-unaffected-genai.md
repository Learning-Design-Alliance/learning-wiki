# Only about a third of design students report reduced creativity or project ownership from GenAI use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Only 31% of respondents reported a perceived loss of ownership or creative agency when using GenAI tools. [→ Stefana Broadbent 2026](#stefana-broadbent-2026)

## Evidence

### Stefana Broadbent 2026

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey items on creativity and ownership (n=219 and n=223). 'Less creative since I started using AI' drew 6.4% strongly agree and 24.7% somewhat agree; 'less ownership of my projects' drew 9% strongly agree and 22.3% somewhat agree.

> "only 31% reported a perceived loss of ownership or creative agency when using GenAI tools."

### Stefana Broadbent 2026 (2)

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Ownership-retention item (n=218). Majorities agreed they retain ownership when using GenAI only to correct work (61.3% agree combined), to check nothing was missed, to start them off, or for repetitive tasks.

> "Only to correct my work 25.8% 35.5% 15.2% 7.8% 7.4% 8.3%"
