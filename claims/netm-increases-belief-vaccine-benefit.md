Tümsek Gizem Solmaz and Akmeşe Zehra Baykal. (2026). Reducing parents’ vaccination hesitancy: a new educational training model in Turkish sample. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1808239

Paired samples t-test within the same 50-parent NICU sample; the study reports the belief subscale "increased from 11.96 ± 4.44 (95% CI: 10.70–13.22) to 17.72 ± 4.44 (95% CI: 16.46–18.98)" with t = −4.933, p < 0.001. No standardized effect size printed.

> "the mean score for the “Belief in Vaccine Benefit” subscale increased from 11.96 ± 4.44 (95% CI: 10.70–13.22) to 17.72 ± 4.44 (95% CI: 16.46–18.98), indicating an improvement in positive perceptions toward vaccination."
