# In an online case-based course, students perceive cognitive presence as highest and least variable, and teaching presence as the most variable, among the CoI presences and metacognition dimensions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Graduate students in an online CBI course rated cognitive presence highest (M = 4.509, SD = .428) among the three presences and two metacognition dimensions, followed by self-regulation (M = 4.417, SD = .444), with co-regulation lowest (M = 4.160, SD = .551). [→ Sadaf 2022](#sadaf-2022)
`q2 i?` Teaching presence was the most variable perception across students (SD = .718), while cognitive presence was the least variable (SD = .428). [→ Sadaf 2022](#sadaf-2022)
