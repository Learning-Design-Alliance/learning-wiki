Evidence¶

Baoyi W and Khan FU 2026¶

Baoyi W and Khan FU (2026). AI and higher education: teachers' perceptions of students' ethical tensions in online pedagogical methods. Front. Psychol. 17:1881032. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1881032

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from the CLIL section of the thematic analysis. Teachers reported the "bilingual bluff": polished AI-assisted work misrepresents underlying competence, and conceptual hollowing occurs when students cannot elaborate AI-generated terms in their own words.

"Teachers often notice a "mismatch" where a student's written language is flawless thanks to AI assistance, but their understanding of the topic is quite superficial."
