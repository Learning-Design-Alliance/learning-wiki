# CoI-based online instruction did not significantly change social presence perceptions compared with direct instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no statistically significant difference between the experimental and control groups' social presence scores, though the experimental group's mean was descriptively higher. [→ Aktı-Aslan 2021](#akt-aslan-2021)

## Evidence

### Aktı-Aslan 2021

Aktı-Aslan, S. & Turgut, Y. E. (2021). Effectiveness of community of inquiry based online course: Cognitive, social and teaching presence. Journal of Pedagogical Research, 5(3), 187-197. https://doi.org/10.33902/JPR.2021371365

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test in the same quasi-experiment found no significant social presence difference between groups; the experimental mean (27.42) was descriptively higher than the control mean (25.53). No effect size was printed for this comparison.

> "The results revealed non -significant difference between the groups’ SP scores ( 𝑡𝑡(79) = 1.625; 𝑝𝑝 > .05). However, the students in the experimental group had higher scores (𝑋𝑋 = 27.42) than the students in the control group (𝑋𝑋 = 25.53)."
