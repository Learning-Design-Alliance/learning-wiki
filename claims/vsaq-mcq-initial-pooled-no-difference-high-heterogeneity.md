## Evidence

### Sher Ali 2026

Sher Ali, Arshad Muhammad Moin Ud Din, Abbas Mesum, Asghar Abdul Hannan, Khan Anees Hussain, Ali Junaid, Yaqoob Muhammad Ibrahim, Alam Rana Aftab, Akram Muhammad Bilal, Masood Muhammad Abdullah, Thakur Amit Kumar. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09359-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of six cohorts from three studies (Total No = 1,191) pooling standardized mean differences with a random-effects model. The pooled SMD was -0.52 with a 95% CI of [-1.34, 0.30] and "extreme heterogeneity (I2 = 98%)".

> "The initial pooled analysis revealed no statistically significant difference in students’ performance between VSAQ and MCQ scores (SMD = -0.52; 95% CI [-1.34, 0.30]; p = 0.22). However, this analysis suffered from extreme heterogeneity (I2 = 98%, p < 0.00001)"
