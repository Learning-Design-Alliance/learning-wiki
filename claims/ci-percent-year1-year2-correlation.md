## Evidence

### Chaytor Andrew T 2012

Chaytor Andrew T, Spence Jacqueline, Armstrong Ann, McLachlan John C. (2012). Do students learn to be more conscientious at medical school? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-54

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of year 1 vs year 2 CI % scores for 3 consecutive cohorts and combined data. Individual cohort correlations were R = 0.494, 0.637 and 0.530 (P = 0.001 each); the article states the combined R of 0.54 "would represent a significant effect size in educational terms".

> "Analysis using a Pearson’s correlation test showed a high degree of correlation (P = 0.001, with R = 0.54; Table 2)."
