### Hassan Ahmed Nour Eldin 2026

Hassan Ahmed Nour Eldin, Lasheen Noha N. (2026). Impact of an integrated interdisciplinary team-based learning session of medicine and health sector programs at Galala University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09732-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post within-subject comparison of paired i-RAT and t-RAT scores in a single interdisciplinary TBL session. The article reports "significant improvements were observed across the nine questions (P < 0.05)"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "On comparing the Team Readiness Assurance Test (t-RAT) scores with the Individual Readiness Assurance Test (i-RAT) scores, significant improvements were observed across the nine questions (P < 0.05)."

### Hassan Ahmed Nour Eldin 2026 (2)

Hassan Ahmed Nour Eldin, Lasheen Noha N. (2026). Impact of an integrated interdisciplinary team-based learning session of medicine and health sector programs at Galala University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09732-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test comparison of mean i-RAT vs t-RAT scores in the same session. The article reports the t-RAT mean was "approximately 20% significantly higher" with P < 0.02; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Additionally, the mean score for the t-RAT was approximately 20% significantly higher than that of the i-RAT (P < 0.02), as shown in Fig. 3."
