### Sintayehu Y 2020

Sintayehu Y, Assefa D, Biftu G, Getahun T, Haymanot M, Kasiye S, Teshale M. (2020). Basic neonatal resuscitation skills of midwives and nurses in Eastern Ethiopia are not well retained: An observational study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236194

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable binary logistic regression on 427 participants found profession independently associated with retention; 26.8% of midwives versus 3.5% of nurses retained skills, and "Midwives were seven times more likely to retain NR skills (AOR, 7.39 [95% CI: 2.25, 24.25]) as compared to nurses."
