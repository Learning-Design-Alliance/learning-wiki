## Evidence

### Waechter Jason 2019

Waechter Jason, Reading David, Lee Chel Hee, Walker Mathieu. (2019). Quantifying the medical student learning curve for ECG rhythm strip interpretation using deliberate practice. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc40. [doi:10.3205/zma001248](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001248)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Practice-data analysis of the two-school cohort using paired t-tests with Bonferroni correction; the article reports significant consecutive-module accuracy gains "through modules 1-3 and possibly also continuing up to module 5".

> "Differences in diagnostic accuracy between consecutive modules showed statistically significant increases between modules: 1 and 2; 2 and 3; not 3 and 4, but again 4 and 5, indicating that diagnostic accuracy was continuing to increase through modules 1-3 and possibly also continuing up to module 5"

### Waechter Jason 2019 (2)

Waechter Jason, Reading David, Lee Chel Hee, Walker Mathieu. (2019). Quantifying the medical student learning curve for ECG rhythm strip interpretation using deliberate practice. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc40. [doi:10.3205/zma001248](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001248)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same practice-data analysis: paired comparisons showed per-module practice time significantly decreased between consecutive modules 1 through 8, which the authors interpret as increasing speed of diagnosis.

> "Differences in per module practice time significantly decreased between consecutive modules: 1 and 2; through to modules 7 and 8 inclusive, indicating that speed was continuing to increase throughout all these modules."
