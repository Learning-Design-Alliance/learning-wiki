Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Providing baselines with oracle knowledge-concept selection substantially improves their performance, identifying concept selection as a key bottleneck, though baselines with oracle KCs still trail KT4EQG — related
  • KT4EQG achieves the highest exam score across all practice-round counts, while methods without effective concept selection show only marginal gains as rounds increase — related
  • KT4EQG remains competitive when evaluated with alternative knowledge tracers (BKT and DKT), though its advantage varies across modules — related
  • KT4EQG's education-value objective produces an adaptive KC-selection policy: weaker students are given better-mastered concepts and stronger students progressively more challenging ones — related
