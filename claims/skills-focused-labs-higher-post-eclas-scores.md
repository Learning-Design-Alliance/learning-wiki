# First-year students in skills-focused lab courses had higher postinstruction overall E-CLASS scores than students in concepts-focused courses

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In the aggregate data set, postinstruction overall E-CLASS scores were statistically significantly higher in skills-focused than concepts-focused courses (d = 0.5, p ≪ 0.01). [→ Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016](#bethany-r-wilcox-and-h-j-lewandowski-2016)
`q2 i?` Skills-focused courses showed higher postinstruction scores than concepts-focused courses on all but four E-CLASS items, with eight item-level differences statistically significant. [→ Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)](#bethany-r-wilcox-and-h-j-lewandowski-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). Developing skills vs reinforcing concepts in physics labs: Insight from E-CLASS. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Aggregate raw-score comparison (Table III) across the national data set (N = 4915 matched responses, 147 course instances): skills-focused post 18.7 vs concepts-focused 15.0, "p ≪ 0.01 d = 0.5". The table's Sig. column compares skills vs concepts postinstruction scores.

> "Post 18.7 14.3 15.0 p ≪ 0.01 d = 0.5"

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). Developing skills vs reinforcing concepts in physics labs: Insight from E-CLASS. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

By-item comparison of FY students in skills- vs concepts-focused courses (30 course instances) using Mann-Whitney U with Holm-Bonferroni correction; "skills- focused courses scoring higher in all cases" of the eight significant items. No effect size printed.

> "Diﬀerences between postinstruction scores were statistically signiﬁcant for eight items, with skills- focused courses scoring higher in all cases."
