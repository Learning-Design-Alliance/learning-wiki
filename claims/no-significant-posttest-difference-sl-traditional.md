## Evidence

### Guzel 2016

Guzel, S. & Aydin, S. (2016). The effect of second life on speaking achievement. Global Journal of Foreign Language Teaching. 6(4), 236-245. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED573975

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-tests on post-test scores showed no significant between-group difference in total score (Sig. .93) or in grammar (.96), vocabulary (.65), intelligibility (.18), fluency (.96) or task achievement (.93), despite higher SL-group means.

> "Considering total mean scores, significance value was found to be .93, suggesting there was no significant difference between two groups. Total mean score for control group was 72.83, while it was 81.65 for experimental group"
