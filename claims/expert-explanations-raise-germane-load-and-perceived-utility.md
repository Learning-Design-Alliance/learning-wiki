## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The explanation groups (IE, PE) scored significantly higher on germane cognitive load than the reflection group IR (partial η² = .13). [→ Reiser 2026](#reiser-2026)
`q3 i3` Perceived utility for medical communication was significantly higher in the expert-explanation groups (IE and PE) than in the reflection-prompt groups (IR and PR), partial η² = .19. [→ Reiser 2026 (2)](#reiser-2026-2)
