q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical assertion in the discussion, attributed by the article to Coeckelbergh, Crawford and Hopwood. The article illustrates that when under-resourced schools adopt ChatGPT as a standard tool, 'epistemic injustice may arise through the marginalisation of minority languages and perspectives'.

"AI systems are never neutral tools (Coeckelberg, 2023); they embed cultural assumptions, economic and political interests, and power relations (Crawford, 2021), mediating human activity in distinctive ways (Hopwood, 2023)."
