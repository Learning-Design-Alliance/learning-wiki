## Evidence

### Miller‐Cotto 2026

Miller‐Cotto, D., & Medrano, J. (2026). Does working memory moderate the effect of fading on math performance? British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 108–126. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12781

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis reported in the discussion (correlation table in Appendix S1) of the randomized homework study; the authors attribute the null correlation to broad, subjective measurement of both constructs and cite Anmarkrud et al. (2019) on measurement inconsistencies.

> "working memory and cognitive load were not significantly correlated in the current study despite sharing some commonalities, such as a capacity, the ability to be overloaded, and having some role in schema creation"
