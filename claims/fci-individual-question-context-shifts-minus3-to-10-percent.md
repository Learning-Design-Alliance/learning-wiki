## Evidence

### Stewart et al. 2007

John Stewart, Heather Griffin, and Gay Stewart. (2007). Context sensitivity in the force concept inventory. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 3, 010102. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.3.010102

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the ten paired FCI/FCIT questions using McNemar's statistic for marginal homogeneity; all ten question differences were significant at the 1% level. The printed shift range is "from −3% to 10%". No effect size is printed.

> "The context shift of the ten FCI questions ranged from −3% to 10%. All ten FCI questions were statistically sensitive to context shifts when treated as paired data."
