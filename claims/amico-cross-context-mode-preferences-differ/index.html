Pier Paolo Benedetti 2026¶

Pier Paolo Benedetti. (2026). Design Principles and Observable Indicators for AI-Enabled Pedagogical Accompaniment: Evidence from the Amico Dual-Mode Prototype in Italy and China. Accepted at the 2026 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Education (ICAIE 2026), IEEE. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20665

q2 · i? · design · r2

Cross-context descriptive comparison of pilot observations in Italy (N_IT = 12) and China (N_CN = 18). The authors report context-sensitive preferences rather than divergent outcomes, and note participants in both contexts did not report expectations of substitution or emotional dependency.

"These differences did not indicate divergent outcomes, but rather context -sensitive preferences in how pedagogical mediation is experienced and valued"
