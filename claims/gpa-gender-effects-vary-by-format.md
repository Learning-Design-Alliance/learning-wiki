## Evidence

### Karamdokht Parya 2026

Karamdokht Parya, Babaee Hemmati Yasamin, Dadgaran Ideh, Falahchai Mehran. (2026). Item analysis and performance of EMQ, MCQ, and SAQ in dental education and students’ perception of extended-matching questions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09791-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analyses in the 29-student cohort using independent t-tests. GPA differences appeared only for MCQs; gender differences appeared only for SAQs (P = 0.043), where "females had higher performance".

> "regarding the GPA variable, a significant difference was observed only in the MCQs (independent t-test; P = 0.009), and students with GPA > 16 obtained higher scores. There was no significant difference in the EMQs (P = 0.319) and SAQs (P = 0.061)"
