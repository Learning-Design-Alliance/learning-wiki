Related Claims¶

  • AI-WTC research in EFL contexts has expanded rapidly since 2021, concentrated in Asia and on generative AI chatbots — related
  • Gameplay increased participants' willingness to communicate in English — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Participation in interpersonal audio discussions produced no statistically significant changes in overall willingness to communicate, though most students reported unchanged or increased WTC — reports the opposite
  • English pronunciation self-efficacy partially mediates the association between GenAI-based pronunciation feedback perceptions and willingness to communicate, with the indirect effect accounting for 69.9% of the total effect — related
  • AI use also carries negative outcomes: technical instability, negative emotional impacts, over-reliance, and contextual contingency — reports the opposite
  • AI reduces foreign language anxiety and strengthens self-efficacy and self-confidence, supporting the Affective Foundation Pathway — related
  • English pronunciation self-efficacy is strongly and positively associated with willingness to communicate in English among Chinese EFL learners — related
  • GenAI feedback perceptions, pronunciation self-efficacy, and WTC are all positively intercorrelated, and learners report positive feedback perceptions and moderate self-efficacy and WTC — related
  • Chinese EFL learners' positive perceptions of GenAI-based pronunciation feedback are positively associated with their English pronunciation self-efficacy — related
