### Sophie Wacker 2024

Sophie Wacker, Claudia M. Roebers. (2024). Motivating children to (pre)monitor: positive effects on monitoring accuracy? Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09351-0

Post hoc descriptive analysis of registered pre-monitoring judgments: "88.3% (N = 2692) of the alternatives having received the highest level were also selected in the recognition phase"; the pattern held for both age groups separately.

> "out of 100% (N = 3049) registered pre-monitoring judgments, 88.3% (N = 2692) of the alternatives having received the highest level were also selected in the recognition phase."
