# No-Go accuracy correlates with R SMA beta-power connectivity: positive with right parahippocampal gyrus and negative with left SFG and anterior cingulate cortex

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` R SMA beta connectivity correlates positively with No-Go accuracy at R parahippocampal gyrus (r = 0.39, P = 0.026) and negatively at L SFG (r = −0.59) and L ACC (r = −0.61). [→ Muraskin Jordan 2016](#muraskin-jordan-2016)
`q3 i?` Experts and controls differ in R SMA beta connectivity with L superior frontal gyrus, inverse in experts but positive in controls (t = 4.23, P < 0.001). [→ Muraskin Jordan 2016](#muraskin-jordan-2016)

## Evidence

### Muraskin Jordan 2016

Muraskin Jordan, Dodhia Sonam, Lieberman Gregory, Garcia Javier O., Verstynen Timothy, Vettel Jean M., Sherwin Jason, Sajda Paul. (2016). Brain dynamics of post‐task resting state are influenced by expertise: Insights from baseball players. Human Brain Mapping. https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23321

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

EEG beta power from R SMA used as seed with a No-Go accuracy regressor controlling for expertise. "significant positive correlations between R SMA beta and R parahippocampal gyrus (PHG) (r = 0.39, P = 0.026)" with negative correlations at L SFG and L ACC; no significant correlations using L SMA beta.

> "post‐hoc Pearson coefficient correlation tests revealed significant positive correlations between R SMA beta and R parahippocampal gyrus (PHG) (r = 0.39, P = 0.026) and negative correlations with L SFG (r = −0.59, P < 0.001) and L anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) (r = −0.61, P < 0.001)."
