### Puddey Ian B 2014

Puddey Ian B, Mercer Annette, Andrich David, Styles Irene. (2014). Practice effects in medical school entrance testing with the undergraduate medicine and health sciences admission test (UMAT). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-48

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Theoretical argument in the Discussion, drawing on Chomsky's competence/performance distinction and a prior retesting study where second-occasion gains came from easy rather than difficult items. "the effect of practice is to be able to answer the more difficult items, as well as the easier items, without having improved in competence." No empirical data in this article settle the question.

> "Taking the perspective that greater competence implies answering correctly, not only the easy items, but also the more difficult items, this detrimental perspective implies that the effect of practice is to be able to answer the more difficult items, as well as the easier items, without having improved in competence."
