Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Technical Report 2026¶

Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Technical Report. (2026). NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/spanish-map-reading-fluency-technical-report/

q1 · i? · design · r2

The report attributes this claim to Tapia (2016) in its Test Design section, contrasting Spanish's consistent one-to-one sound-letter correspondence with English's more opaque orthography. It adds that decoding accuracy remains foundational for early readers still developing automaticity.

"In Spanish, because of its relatively transparent orthography, decoding accuracy is typically established earlier; once this occurs, decoding rate (fluency) often becomes a more sensitive predictor of passage comprehension (Tapia, 2016)."
