Evidence¶

Adam Chuderski 2021¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

BANOVA on 1017 correct-solution Intuition ratings across the 20 RAPM items. The null was supported (BF10 = 0.060); the article also notes a small effect size, ηp2 = .03, and that ratings generally fell below the criterion value 10, suggesting analytic rather than intuitive processing.

"Intuition did not vary reliably across the 20 RAPM items, with BF 10 = 0.060, supporting the null effect."
