## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Many students described peer feedback as more honest, constructive and easier to receive because of shared common ground with peers. [→ Carr Sandra E. 2016](#carr-sandra-e-2016)

## Evidence

### Carr Sandra E. 2016

Carr Sandra E., Brand Gabrielle, Wei Li, Wright Helen, Nicol Pam, Metcalfe Helene, Saunders Julie, Payne John, Seubert Liza, Foley Laurie. (2016). “Helping someone with a skill sharpens it in your own mind”: a mixed method study exploring health professions students experiences of Peer Assisted Learning (PAL). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0566-8

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme on giving and receiving feedback, drawn from survey responses, interviews and focus groups; students attributed openness to relatedness and shared understanding of learning needs.

> "Many of the students described how peer feedback is more honest, constructive and ‘a bit more nitty gritty’ and in many cases easier to receive because ‘it is a less stressful feeling receiving feedback from peers, helps because more common ground is shared’"

### Carr Sandra E. 2016 (2)

Carr Sandra E., Brand Gabrielle, Wei Li, Wright Helen, Nicol Pam, Metcalfe Helene, Saunders Julie, Payne John, Seubert Liza, Foley Laurie. (2016). “Helping someone with a skill sharpens it in your own mind”: a mixed method study exploring health professions students experiences of Peer Assisted Learning (PAL). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0566-8

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom-setting theme on enhancing content knowledge notes that “a few students indicated their caution in receiving feedback from a same level peer”, quoting a health science student.

> "They were at the same level, I was not confident that their feedback was accurate – felt the need to clarify this with teacher"
