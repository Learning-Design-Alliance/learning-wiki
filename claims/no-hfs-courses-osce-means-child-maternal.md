## Evidence

### Guerrero 2023

Guerrero, J. G., Rosales, N. S., & Castro, G. M. T. (2023). Impact of high‐fidelity simulation exposure of nursing students with their objective structured clinical examination: A quasi‐experimental study. Nursing Open, 10, 765–772. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per‐course descriptive results (Table 2) for the 51 child health nursing students without HFS exposure: midterm 78.37 (12.00), final 83.52 (8.71), and an 'overall mean percentage of the OSCE midterm and final results of 81.80 (8.49)'.

> "the mean percentage of the OSCE midterm and final results of nursing students without HFS exposure in child health nursing was 78.37 (12.00) and 83.52 (8.71), respectively, with an overall mean percentage of the OSCE midterm and final results of 81.80 (8.49)."
