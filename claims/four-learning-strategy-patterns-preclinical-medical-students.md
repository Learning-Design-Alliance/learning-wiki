## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Hierarchical cluster analyses of LIST subscale scores at two time points one year apart each yielded four identical clusters defining four types of learners. [→ Hogh Annemarie 2021](#hogh-annemarie-2021)
`q2 i?` The four patterns differed most in resource-oriented strategies: relaxed learners scored highest on distractibility, diligent learners highest on effort regulation and time management, and sociable learners highest on peer-learning. [→ Hogh Annemarie 2021](#hogh-annemarie-2021)

## Evidence

### Hogh Annemarie 2021

Hogh Annemarie, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2021). Learning strategies and their correlation with academic success in biology and physiology examinations during the preclinical years of medical school. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0245851

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory longitudinal survey study: hierarchical cluster analyses (Ward method) of totaled LIST subscale scores at T1 and T2 "yielded four identical clusters", defining relaxed, diligent, hard-working, and sociable learner types (T1 n = 17, 25, 33, 48).

> "These analyses were performed for T1 and T2 and at both time points yielded four identical clusters. These clusters identified distinct patterns of learning strategy combinations among medical students and defined 4 types of learners."
