Evidence¶

Young 2016¶

Young, J.R. (2016). Unpacking TPACK in Mathematics education research: A systematic review of meta-analyses. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 2(1), 19-29. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.2.1.19

q3 · i? · review · r2

A systematic review of 13 meta-analyses of technology-enhanced mathematics learning (2001-2015), with Hedges g as the common metric. The review reports that "effects vary by didactical functionality from small to medium" and that the largest variation was for developing conceptual understanding. No pooled effect size is printed for this contrast.
