## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Socratic-condition students rated the chatbot as significantly more motivating for independent thought; the article characterises the standardised coefficient as a large effect. [→ Degen 2025](#degen-2025)

## Evidence

### Degen 2025

Degen, P.-B., & Asanov, I. (2025). Beyond Automation: Socratic AI, Epistemic Agency, and the Implications of the Emergence of Orchestrated Multi-Agent Learning Architectures. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.05116](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.05116)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model on the independent-thinking item in the between-subjects experiment (n=36 ST, n=29 nST). The model showed a robust condition effect (b = –1.18, p = <.001); the article prints the standardised coefficient β = –0.96 and characterises it as indicating a large effect, though no d, r, or η² value is printed.

> "participants in the Socratic group rating the chatbot as significantly more motivating for supporting independent thought (b = –1.18, SE = 0.23, t(65.02) = –5.12, p = <.001). The standardised coefficient was β = –0.96, 95% CI [–1.33, –0.59], indicating a large effect"
