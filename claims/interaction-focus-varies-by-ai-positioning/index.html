Hillary Greene Nolan 2024¶

Hillary Greene Nolan, Ph.D, Merijke Coenraad, Ph.D, Viki Young, Ph.D. (2024). Teaching Partner, Grading Assistant, Substitute Teacher: Three Ways Teachers Positioned an Artificial Intelligence Tool in Writing Instruction. Digital Promise, Center for Inclusive Innovation. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/4f2d86a3-9b66-4538-a5ad-977bb8fbacf7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview-based finding on teacher-student interaction topics during AI use. An assistant teacher's example shows score focus: students revised up to "47 times" chasing a 4 after the teacher offered extra credit for reaching it.

"the focus of interactions with teachers who positioned AI as a teaching partner was writing and learning, the focus for teachers who viewed AI as grading assistants was the score and increasing it, and the focus among those with the substitute approach was completion and compliance."
