### Seabi 2012

Seabi, J. (2012). Feuerstein's mediated learning experience as a vehicle for enhancing cognitive functioning of remedial school learners in South Africa. Australian Journal of Educational & Developmental Psychology, 12, 35-45. http://www.newcastle.edu.au/journal/ajedp/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on post-test RCPM scores with pre-test scores as covariates, comparing 28 experimental and 39 control learners in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports "no statistical significant difference" between the groups on post-test scores.

> "The results revealed no statistical significant difference (F [1, 66] = 0.39, p >0.5) between the experimental and control groups on the RCPM scores."
