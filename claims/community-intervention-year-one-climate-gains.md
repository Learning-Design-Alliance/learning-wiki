## Evidence

### Nelson 2012

Nelson, L. P., McMahan, S. K., & Torres, T. (2012). The Impact of a Junior High School Community Intervention Project: Moving Beyond the Testing Juggernaut and Into a Community of Creative Learners. School Community Journal, 22(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ974689

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental repeated-measures analysis (three-factor ANCOVA, gender x ethnicity x time) of a 44-item school climate survey at the intervention school, with baseline covariates. The article reports significantly strong positive changes (p < .001) across all five scale assets after one year; no effect sizes are printed.
