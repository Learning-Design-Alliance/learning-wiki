Evidence¶

Pate and Miller 2011¶

Michael L. Pate, Greg Miller. (2011). Effects of Think–Aloud Pair Problem Solving on Secondary–Level Students’ Performance in Career and Technical Education Courses. Journal of Agricultural Education. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2011.01120

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation in the conclusions section, citing Veenman et al. (2006): "This may mean the impact of TAPPS depends on student maturity and experience." They also note secondary students' knowledge of basic engine principles was not formally assessed, unlike the postsecondary sample with a prerequisite course.

"Veenman et al. (2006) argued that elementary levels of metacognitive thought develop during early childhood but become more sophisticated and academically oriented through instructional interventions requiring the explicit use of metacognition."
