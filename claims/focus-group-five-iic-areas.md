`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative phase: a virtual 120-minute focus group with six university teachers experienced in IICs, transcribed and analyzed following Glaser and Strauss. The analysis identified "five areas of the IICs", with teachers highlighting collaborative work, simulations, and diagnostic, formative and weekly summative assessment.

> "According to the results obtained in the focus group, five areas of the IICs were identified (Figure 3), the first of which related to their characteristics, the second to the most effective learning methods, the third to the form of assessment, the fourth to the characteristics of the teacher, and the fifth with the characteristics of the student body."
