### Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño 2025

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño. (2025). Dissociable sensory, motor, and visuomotor predictive functions. npj Science of Learning 10:85. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00377-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Perceptual detection task in which participants judged brief changes in a moving dot's direction or speed over durations from 50 to 1000 ms. The article reports highly significant effects of magnitude and duration on detection probability (e.g., angular η2G = 0.806) and smaller RT effects (η2G = 0.106 angular; 0.047 speed), consistent with "temporal i ntegration in perceptual decision-making".

> "Detection probabilities increased with both larger magni- tudes and longer durations, for both angular (Fig. 3b) and speed (Fig. 3c) discrepancies."
