Evidence¶

Chen 2017¶

Chen, O., Kalyuga, S. & Sweller, J. (2017). The Expertise Reversal Effect is a Variant of the More General Element Interactivity Effect. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-016-9359-1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument from the article's abstract: the expertise reversal effect is reinterpreted as also depending on element interactivity, but with the changes driven by "changes in relative levels of expertise" rather than by the material. No new data are reported in the available text.

"the expertise reversal effect also relies on changes in relative levels of element interactivity but in this case, due to changes in relative levels of expertise"
