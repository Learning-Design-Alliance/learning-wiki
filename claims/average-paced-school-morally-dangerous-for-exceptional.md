## Evidence

### William Stern 1910

William Stern. (1910). The Supernormal Child. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument about general (not specialized) ability. Stern reasons that a school "adjusted for the broad average of ability" is uneconomical and "positively morally dangerous" for unusual pupils, breeding indolence and dislike for school. No empirical data are offered in the article for this claim.

> "The exceptionally intelligent pupil, therefore, receives far less than he can retain and assimilate—less, indeed, than he must receive if his superior abilities are to realize their full promise."
