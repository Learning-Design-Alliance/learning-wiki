## Evidence

### Krogh Charlotte Loumann 2016

Krogh Charlotte Loumann, Steinmetz Jacob, Rudolph Søren Steemann, Hesselfeldt Rasmus, Lippert Freddy K., Berlac Peter A., Rasmussen Lars S. (2016). Effect of ultrasound training of physicians working in the prehospital setting. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-016-0289-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison of a 56-question US theory test within the e-learning program, completed by 27 of the 40 participants. Mean score rose from "37.5 (SD: 10.0)" to "51.3 (SD: 5.9)", p < 0.0001; no effect size printed.

> "Comparing pre- and post-learning test results of the e-learning the participants obtained significantly higher post-test mean scores 37.5 (SD: 10.0) vs. 51.3 (SD: 5.9), p < 0.0001."
