# Included RCT evidence is of generally low methodologic quality with high heterogeneity, limiting confidence in the PBL advantage

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Although most included studies were designed as RCTs, 3 did not describe random sequence generation, none described allocation concealment or blinding, and results were highly heterogeneous. [→ Ma Yimei MM 2019](#ma-yimei-mm-2019)

## Evidence

### Ma Yimei MM 2019

Ma Yimei MM, Lu Xiaoxi MD, Hayashi Shogo. (2019). The effectiveness of problem-based learning in pediatric medical education in China: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000014052

`q2 · i?`

Risk-of-bias assessment of the 12 included RCTs (Fig. 2) using Cochrane guidelines: "none of them described the allocation concealment or blinding methods", and the authors state the methodologic qualities were not high. All three outcomes showed I2 above 85%.

> "Although most of the studies in this meta-analysis were designed as RCTs, there were still 3 studies that did not describe their methods for random sequence generation, and none of them described the allocation concealment or blinding methods."
