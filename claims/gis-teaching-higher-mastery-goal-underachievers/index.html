Evidence¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh 2016¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh, Balan Rathakrishnan, Sabariah Sharif, Rosy Talin, Oliver Valentine Eboy. (2016). The Effects of Geography Information System (GIS) Based Teaching on Underachieving Students' Mastery Goal and Achievement. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117621

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Non-equivalent quasi-experimental control group study of 84 Form 4 underachieving Geography students (experiment n=44, control n=40). Table 3 reports post-test means of 20.39 (SD 2.86) versus 18.48 (SD 3.33), p = .005, partial eta squared = .093; the article calls this "a medium-sized effect".

"The mean for the experiment group was significantly higher than for the control group’s. Based on guidelines proposed by Cohen (1988), there was a medium-sized effect of GIS-based teaching on the experiment group student participants’ mastery goals to learn Geography."

Soon Singh Bikar Singh 2016 (2)¶

Soon Singh Bikar Singh, Balan Rathakrishnan, Sabariah Sharif, Rosy Talin, Oliver Valentine Eboy. (2016). The Effects of Geography Information System (GIS) Based Teaching on Underachieving Students' Mastery Goal and Achievement. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117621

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated-measures ANOVA on mastery goal scores; Table 1 shows the Time*Treatment effect was significant with partial eta squared = .050, which the article classifies as a small sized effect. No standardized effect beyond the printed partial eta squared is reported.

"In addition, the analysi s of data in Table 1 shows that there was a significant interaction effect between mastery goal and time and this repre sents a small sized effect."
