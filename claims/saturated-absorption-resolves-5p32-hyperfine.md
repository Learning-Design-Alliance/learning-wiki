# Doppler-free saturated absorption spectroscopy with a grating-feedback diode resolves the 5P3/2 hyperfine structure of rubidium, with dip linewidths of 22±1 MHz (87Rb) and 20±1 MHz (85Rb)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Saturated absorption spectroscopy on the 5S1/2→5P3/2 transition resolves six Doppler-free dips per ground-state line and yields excited-state hyperfine splittings such as δ[(1↔2)]87Rb = 156±2 MHz and δ[(2↔3)]85Rb = 64±1 MHz. [→ V Jacques 2009](#v-jacques-2009)
`q2 i?` The measured dip linewidths (22±1 MHz for 87Rb, 20±1 MHz for 85Rb) remain far above the ~6 MHz natural linewidth because pump-beam saturation, collisions and transit-time broadening set a floor. [→ V Jacques 2009 (2)](#v-jacques-2009-2)

## Evidence

### V Jacques 2009

V Jacques, B Hingant, A Allafort, M Pigeard and J F Roch. (2009). Non-linear spectroscopy of rubidium: An undergraduate experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3115 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Undergraduate saturated absorption experiment using counterpropagating pump and probe beams from the same diode; spectra fitted with the product of six Lorentzians and a Gaussian (figure 3). The fit yields the printed excited-state hyperfine splittings for 85Rb and 87Rb.

> "For the hyperﬁne structure of the 5P 3/2 excited state, we ﬁnally obtain δ [(1↔ 2)]87Rb = 156± 2MHz, δ [(2↔ 3)]87Rb = 264± 3MHz, δ [(2↔ 3)]85Rb = 64± 1MHz, and δ [(3↔ 4)]85Rb = 119± 2MHz"

### V Jacques 2009 (2)

V Jacques, B Hingant, A Allafort, M Pigeard and J F Roch. (2009). Non-linear spectroscopy of rubidium: An undergraduate experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3115 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.3115)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Linewidth measurement in the same saturated absorption spectra. The article notes the natural linewidth predicted from the ~28 ns lifetime is 2γ = 6 MHz, "much smaller than the one measured", and attributes the excess to saturation, collisions and transit-time broadening.

> "The linewidths of the saturated absorption dips are found equal to ∆ν = 22±1 MHz for 87Rb and ∆ν = 20± 1 MHz for 85Rb."
