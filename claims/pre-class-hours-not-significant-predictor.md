## Evidence

### Chen Kuo-Su 2021

Chen Kuo-Su, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Huang Min-Ping, Chen Chih-Ken, Hung Ming-Jui. (2021). Academic outcome and moderator of flipped classroom learning program “Teaching on the Run”. Biomedical Journal, 44(5), 636–643. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bj.2020.05.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate and multivariate analyses of TOTR learning data; the null for pre-class hours is reported without a printed effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect). The authors attribute the null partly to subjectively self-reported, potentially biased time estimates.

> "In our univariate analysis, only class participation, but not hours spent on pre-class learning, was a significant predictor of the final knowledge gain."
