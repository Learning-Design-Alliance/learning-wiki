Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı 2015¶

Veli Batdı & Hacer Batdı. (2015). Effect of Creative Drama on Academic Achievement: A Meta-analytic and Thematic Analysis. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.6.0156

Qualitative documentary review of 14 studies of students' views on creative drama, coded in Maxqda 11: codes included "providing experiential learning", "developing creative thinking skills", "creating a permanent learning environment", "increasing self-esteem", and negatives such as "creating confusion" and "being considered to be time-consuming".

"These findings were presented with the themes “effect of creative drama on the cognitive domain, ” “thinking, creativity, and critical thinking skills, ” “learning environment and process, ” and “social aspects, affective domain, and attitude. ”"

Related Claims¶

  • Effect size of creative drama does not vary significantly by skill type (social, basic language, higher thinking) — related
  • The very large effect found here is consistent with prior meta-analyses of creative drama, which report medium-to-huge positive effects on achievement — related
  • Effect size of creative drama on skills varies significantly with duration of experimental application, with longer applications showing larger effects — related
  • Effect size of creative drama on Social Studies achievement varies by grade level, largest at 8th grade (d = 2.733) and lowest at 4th grade (d = 0.709) — related
  • Effect sizes of creative drama studies are heterogeneous (Q = 60.78, df = 39, p < .05), motivating moderator analyses — related
  • Several learning theories and teaching strategies have very large positive effects on academic achievement (e.g., creative drama ES = 1.453), while the surface approach to learning has a small negative effect (ES = -.366) — related
  • The article identifies creative drama, cooperative learning, educational games, and animations as particularly beneficial vocabulary-teaching methods — related
