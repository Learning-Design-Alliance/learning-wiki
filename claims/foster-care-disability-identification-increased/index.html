Longitudinal analysis of CDE CALPADS administrative data for 2014–15 through 2022–23 comparing four student groups. The report states "nearly one in three students in foster care was identified with at least one disability," a rate far exceeding low-income students and English learners.

"By 2022–23, students in foster care were nearly three times more likely to be identified with a disability compared to all students. By this time, nearly one in three students in foster care was identified with at least one disability"
