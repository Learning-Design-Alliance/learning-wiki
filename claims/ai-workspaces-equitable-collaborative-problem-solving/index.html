Jinhee Kim 2026¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Designing AI systems to support a productive-failure-based learning: insights from adult learners on AI applications and AI system design principles. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10655-6

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Focus group participants discussed collaborative workspaces (e.g., Notion, Miro, Trello) that synthesize contributions and prompt quieter members. Features such as real-time summarization of group input were "perceived as reducing geographical and cultural barriers in online teamwork, promoting accountability."

"Such features were perceived as reducing geographical and cultural barriers in online teamwork, promoting accountability, and fostering a stronger sense of shared responsibility."
