EdTrust analysis of NCES Baccalaureate and Beyond: 2016/2020 data on borrowers who began college in 2016. The brief reports "12.7% of borrowers in repayment had a $0 payment" overall and 25.5% among Black borrowers; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, within four years of completing a bachelor’s degree 12.7% of borrowers in repayment had a $0 payment. Black borrowers — of whom 25.5% had a $0 payment (Table 2) — will be especially harmed by this."
