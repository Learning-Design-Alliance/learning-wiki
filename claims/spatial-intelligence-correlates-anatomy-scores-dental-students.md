## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In dental students, the anatomical sciences score shows a direct relationship with spatial intelligence score (r = 0.319, P = 0.003) and with visual memory score (r = 0.262, P = 0.01). [→ Amin Aspanani 2023](#amin-aspanani-2023)

## Evidence

### Amin Aspanani 2023

Amin Aspanani, Hosein Sadeqhi, Omid Athar. (2023). The relationship between visual memory and spatial intelligence with students’ academic achievement in anatomy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04327-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional correlational analysis of 85 dental students taking an anatomical sciences course; Pearson correlations of course score with visual memory (r=0.262) and spatial intelligence (r=0.319) were both statistically significant, as printed in Table 4.

> "Pearson correlation coefficient showed that in dental students there was a direct relationship between the score of anatomical science theory and the score of visual memory (P-value=0.01 & r=0.262) as well as the score of spatial intelligence (P-value =0.003 & r=0.319) ."
