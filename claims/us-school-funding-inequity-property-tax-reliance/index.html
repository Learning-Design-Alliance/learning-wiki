The Federal Role in Advancing Education Equity and Excellence 2020¶

The Federal Role in Advancing Education Equity and Excellence. (2020). Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED610926

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief reports funding disparities: districts serving the greatest proportion of students of color spend $1,800 less per student than those serving the fewest, and in many states the wealthiest districts spend two to three times what the poorest can spend per pupil.

"School districts serving the greatest proportion of students of color spend $1,800 less per student than districts serving the fewest students of color."
