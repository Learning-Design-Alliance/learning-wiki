## Evidence

### Steele Justin 2026

Steele Justin, Haus Jeff, Renner Ann, Schmidt Cory, Synhorst David, Mariscal Joseph, Whyte Janae, Foxworthy Christian, Sisson-Smith Ryan, Priddy Alexis, Costello Jillian, Mischler Matthew. (2026). Using virtual reality or slideshow to teach ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture: immersion increases fun. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09611-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-lecture Likert survey in the same randomized trial; medians with interquartile ranges reported for enjoyment and boredom, and perceived effectiveness (p = 0.0265). No effect sizes printed; these outcomes were exploratory.

> "Students in the virtual reality intervention enjoyed learning how to perform an ultrasound guided lumbar puncture more than those in the slideshow (5.0 [4.0, 5.0] vs. 4.0 [4.0, 5.0], p = 0.0232), reported less boredom than the slideshow group (2.0 [1.0, 3.0] vs. 3.0 [2.0, 4.0], p = 0.0308)"
