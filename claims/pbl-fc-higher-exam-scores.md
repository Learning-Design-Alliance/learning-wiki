`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of ophthalmology theoretical examination scores compiled after completion of theoretical teaching, analyzed with independent samples t-test in SPSS 25.0. The PBL-FC group outperformed the traditional group on the same textbook, content, instructor and total teaching hours.

> "The experimental group achieved a score of 77.81 ± 8.63, whereas the control one achieved a score of 72.45 ± 10.55. The difference was statistically significant (P = 0.01)."
