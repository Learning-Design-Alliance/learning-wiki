In the same pre–post study of 39 residents, procedural time was recorded from first stitch to final knot and compared with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The article reports mean time "decreased significantly from 22.18 ± 8.45 min pre-training to 18.42 ± 6.88 min post-training".

> "Mean anastomosis completion time decreased significantly from 22.18 ± 8.45 min pre-training to 18.42 ± 6.88 min post-training. Statistically significant time reductions were observed among second- and third-year residents."
