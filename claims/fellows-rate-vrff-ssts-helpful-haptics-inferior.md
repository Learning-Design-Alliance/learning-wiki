## Evidence

### Lei Y 2025

Lei Y, Zhang Z, Zhou F, Xia T, Xu N, Sun Z. (2025). Virtual reality force feedback spine surgery simulator training for pedicle screw placement: assessing the impact of seniority and spinal region. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07695-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire on VRFF-SSTS usefulness administered to the 64 fellows after training; immersive feeling scores were 71.94±10.45 (A), 75.67±9.44 (B), 73.13±10.15 (C), with no significant differences among groups.

> "most participants rated the haptic feedback from VRFF-SSTS as inferior to the sawbone model, with 88.9% of PGY1-5, 83.3% of PGY6-10, and 87.5% of PGY > 10 rating it as inferior. Despite this, immersive feeling scores were relatively high"
