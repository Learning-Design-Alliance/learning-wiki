# IRT shows the scale's items provide overlapping, redundant information concentrated on high communication-skill levels

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A 1-parameter Rasch model fit as well as a 2-parameter model, and items 2, 3 and 4 provided overlapping information, with items 2 and 4 particularly redundant. [→ Piumatti Giovanni 2021](#piumatti-giovanni-2021)
`q2 i?` Item 1 was the most difficult: an examinee needed a latent Theta of 1.28 for a 50% chance of the highest mark, and the item battery informed well only for latent scores from 0 to 2. [→ Piumatti Giovanni 2021 (2)](#piumatti-giovanni-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Piumatti Giovanni 2021

Piumatti Giovanni, Cerutti Bernard, Perron Noëlle Junod. (2021). Assessing communication skills during OSCE: need for integrated psychometric approaches. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02552-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IRT analysis on the whole sample (N = 296) using students' rounded mean item scores recoded to binary. The likelihood-ratio test showed no difference between the 1-PL and 2-PL models, meaning items differed only in difficulty, not discrimination.

> "There was no evidence of a difference between the two-parameter IRT model and the one-parameter model (χ2 = 3.30, df = 3, likelihood ratio test p = 0.348)."

### Piumatti Giovanni 2021 (2)

Piumatti Giovanni, Cerutti Bernard, Perron Noëlle Junod. (2021). Assessing communication skills during OSCE: need for integrated psychometric approaches. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02552-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Rasch (1-PL) results (Table 3, N = 296) and item information graphs (Fig. 1, Fig. 2). The article reports Item 1 as most difficult (coefficient 1.28) and states the battery has low standard error and high information "approximately only for latent scores ranging from 0 to 2".

> "An examinee must have a latent score (i.e., Theta) equal to 1.28 to get a 50% chance to obtain the highest mark for this item."
