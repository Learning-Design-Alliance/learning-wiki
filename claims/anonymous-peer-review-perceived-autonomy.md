## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Petersen 2021

Petersen, F., & Groenewald, B. (2021). Students' Engagement in an Anonymous Peer Review: Using the Open-Source Sakai Platform. Proceedings of the 1st Virtual Conference on Implications of Information and Digital Technologies for Development, 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.09955](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.09955)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic-content analysis of online survey responses from 179 third-year Information Systems students at a historically disadvantaged institution who completed an anonymous peer review on the Sakai platform. Students reported feeling "empowered reviewing another student's work" and that comparing assignments helped them judge and better their own mistakes.

> "Evidence indicates that the anonymous peer review helps students to empower themselves. This is supported by students' quotes: "I actually felt empowered reviewing another student's work""
