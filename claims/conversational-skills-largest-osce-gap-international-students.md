## Evidence

### Huhn D. 2017

Huhn D., Lauter J., Roesch Ely D., Koch E., Möltner A., Herzog W., Resch F., Herpertz S. C., Nikendei C. (2017). Performance of International Medical Students In psychosocial medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0950-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA on the conversational-skills checklist domain in the same 1033-student retrospective OSCE analysis showed the largest domain effect (η 2 = .053), with contrasts showing "students from EU countries (p < .001; d = .52)" and non-EU students (d = .67) poorer than German counterparts.

> "With respect to ‘conversational skills’, group differences again proved to be significant (F(2, 1030) = 28.767: p < .001; η 2 = .053). According to contrasts, students from EU countries (p < .001; d = .52) as well as those from non-EU countries (p < .001; d = .67) scored significantly poorer than their German counterparts"
