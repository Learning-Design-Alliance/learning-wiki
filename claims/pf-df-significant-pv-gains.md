## Evidence

### Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang 2017

Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang. (2017). Comparing the efficacy of digital flashcards versus paper flashcards to improve receptive and productive L2 vocabulary. The EUROCALL Review, Volume 25, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.6964

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test within the PF group (n = 26) using paper flashcards with three VLSs. The article reports significant productive vocabulary gains, t(25) = 4.86, p = .0001, from "M =6.96" to 10.08.

> "A paired t-test showed that the PF group made significant gains in PV from the pre-test (M =6.96, SD = 2.31) to the post-test (M = 10.08, SD = 2.23), t(25) = 4.86, p = .0001."

### Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang 2017 (2)

Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang. (2017). Comparing the efficacy of digital flashcards versus paper flashcards to improve receptive and productive L2 vocabulary. The EUROCALL Review, Volume 25, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.6964

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test within the DF group (n = 26) studying with Quizlet and Cram. The article reports significant productive vocabulary improvements, t(25) = 7.4, p = .0001, from "M = 8.77" to 12.08.

> "The DF group made significant improvements as well from the pre-test (M = 8.77, SD = 2.75) to the post-test (M = 12.08, SD = 2.17), t(25) = 7.4, p = .0001."
