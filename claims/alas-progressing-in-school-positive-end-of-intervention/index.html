Evidence¶

WWC Intervention Report: ALAS 2006¶

WWC Intervention Report: ALAS. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.alasdropoutprevention.com

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial, progressing-in-school outcome conditional on remaining in a district school. Appendix A3.2 reports a Cox effect size of 0.49 and improvement index of 19, statistically significant at α = 0.05; this analysis meets standards with reservations because enrollment depended on not dropping out.

"ALAS students were more likely than control students to be on track to graduate on time at the end of ninth grade (72% compared with 53%). The difference was statistically significant."
