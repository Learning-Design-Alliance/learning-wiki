# Recalling the deep structure of the first contrasting-cases task predicts later transfer regardless of instructional condition

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Experiment 1, structural recall of the clown task predicted delayed transfer performance (r = .22), and condition added further prediction (rmodel = .34) with no condition-by-recall interaction. [→ Schwartz 2011](#schwartz-2011)
`q3 i2` Experiment 2 replicated the recall-to-transfer prediction (r = .34) with condition again adding prediction (rmodel = .39) and no interaction. [→ Schwartz 2011 (2)](#schwartz-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Schwartz 2011

Schwartz, D. L., Chase, C. C., Oppezzo, M. A., & Chin, D. B. (2011). Practicing versus inventing with contrasting cases: The effects of telling first on learning and transfer. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://aaalab.stanford.edu/transfer.html. [doi:10.1037/a0025140](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0025140)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Three-step regression analysis in Experiment 1 with delayed trampoline transfer as the dependent measure; adding condition improved prediction, Fchange(1, 118) = 8.84, p = .004, rmodel = .34, and the condition-by-recall interaction did not (p = .342).

> "In the first step, structural recall of the clown task was regressed against transfer performance; F(1,119) = 5.79, p = .018, r = .22. Students who recalled the deep structure of the clowns were more likely to transfer."

### Schwartz 2011 (2)

Schwartz, D. L., Chase, C. C., Oppezzo, M. A., & Chin, D. B. (2011). Practicing versus inventing with contrasting cases: The effects of telling first on learning and transfer. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://aaalab.stanford.edu/transfer.html. [doi:10.1037/a0025140](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0025140)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2's three-step regression replicated the pattern: adding condition improved fit, Fchange(1, 110) = 5.13, p = .025, rmodel = .39, and the interaction term did not (p = .385).

> "In the first step, structural recall of the clown task was regressed against transfer performance; F(1,111) = 14.37, p < .001, r = .34. Students who recalled the deep structure of the clowns were more likely to transfer."
