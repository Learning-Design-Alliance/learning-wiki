### Girard 2026

Girard, C., Longo, L., Chesnokova, H., Epinat-Duclos, J., & Prado, J. (2026). Home numeracy experiences are associated with number-related brain activity and connectivity in early childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00419-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychophysiological-interaction (PPI) analysis with the left IPS seed in the same fMRI subsample; significant clusters included left superior frontal gyrus (Z = 5.28) and left precentral gyrus (Z = 5.07). No effect size printed; proportion of number words showed no relation.

> "number-speciﬁc connectivity between that left IPS cluster and several prefrontal, parietal, and occipital regions was positively associated with the frequency of all subcategories of home numeracy practices, as well as for the number of children's games at home"
