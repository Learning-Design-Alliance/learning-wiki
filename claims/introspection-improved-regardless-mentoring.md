## Evidence

### Heckmann Markus B. 2025

Heckmann Markus B., Mages Christine, Finke Daniel, Reich Christoph, Haney Ailís Ceara, Riedel Caroline, Bugaj Till Johannes, Frey Norbert, Rahm Ann-Kathrin. (2025). Effects of mentoring on self-reflection and competence in Final year medical students’ internal medicine rotation. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331057

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of SRIS change scores across all completing students in the randomized rotation study. Insight "increased in all groups SIRS-IN 0.25 (−0.13–0.66, IQR) p = 0.0016" while self-reflection showed no change; the authors suggest the rotation itself may contribute to reflective growth.

> "Overall self-reflection measured by SRIS-SR did not change over the course of the 8-week rotation 0 (−0.25–0.35, IQR) p = 0.5963 while insight, measured by SRIS-IN, increased in all groups SIRS-IN 0.25 (−0.13–0.66, IQR) p = 0.0016."
