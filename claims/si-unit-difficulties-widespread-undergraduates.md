Baseline Survey A (N1 = 46) multiple-selection item on principal difficulties with SI electromagnetism. Most selected obstacles were "converting between SI and Gaussian/CGS systems (52%)" and "tracking ε0 and µ0 across derivations (43%)"; perceived clarity of the SI wave equation was below the scale midpoint (mean 2.91).

> "Only 4 of 46 students (9%) selected “None — I ﬁnd SI straightforward,” conﬁrming that diﬃculties with SI units are widespread rather than conﬁned to a small minority."
