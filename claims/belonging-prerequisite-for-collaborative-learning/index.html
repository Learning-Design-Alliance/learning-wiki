q1 · i? · review · r1

The primer's Key Takeaways state this as a foundational argument of the document, asserting that "Belonging is foundational to collaborative learning" and that it is "a prerequisite for effective collaboration." No empirical data are presented in the primer itself; this is the authors' synthesis of prior work (e.g., Surr et al., 2018).

"Belonging is foundational to collaborative learning. Students who feel they belong are better able to engage and learn. Belonging is more than a positive outcome; it is a prerequisite for effective collaboration and overall student success."
