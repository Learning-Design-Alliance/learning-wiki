## Evidence

### Feigerlova Eva 2024

Feigerlova Eva, Ioan Iulia, Pape Elise, Boursier Caroline, Berguer Marion, Hani Hind, Braun Marc. (2024). Team-based learning (TBL) curriculum combined with video vignettes improves performance of undergraduate medical students on OSCE compared with TBL alone. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05861-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group comparison of the clinical skills subscore (percentage of achieved points out of total raw scores) in the same quasi-experimental posttest-only study of 296 fifth-year medical students. Only the printed means and P value are reported; no effect size is printed.

> "students in the intervention group performed significantly better in clinical tasks, with a mean percentage score of 47.63% compared to 44.63% (P = 0.025) for students in the comparator group"
