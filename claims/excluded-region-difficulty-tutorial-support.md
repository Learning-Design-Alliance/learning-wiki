# Students struggle to distinguish the excluded region from the region of interest, and tutorial scaffolding improved this understanding

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r3` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2026 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interviewed students often could not distinguish the excluded region, where image charges must be placed, from the region of interest where potential is calculated. [→ Kashyap 2026](#kashyap-2026)
`q2 i?` After working through tutorial problems labeling the two regions and reflecting on hypothetical-student dialogues, students demonstrated improved understanding. [→ Kashyap 2026 (2)](#kashyap-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Kashyap 2026

Kashyap, J. S., Devaty, R. P. and Singh, C. (2026). Instructor Framing and Incentives Shape Physics Students' Engagement and Learning Gains from an Inquiry-Based Electrostatics Tutorial on the Method of Images. [doi:10.3390/educsci16040594](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16040594)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud interview observations during scaffolded pretest and tutorial engagement: even when students recognized the method of images applied, they "often lacked a clear understanding of how to distinguish the excluded region from the region of interest". One student placed image charges where real charges were present.

> "they often lacked a clear understanding of how to distinguish the excluded region from the region of interest for a given charge configuration."

### Kashyap 2026 (2)

Kashyap, J. S., Devaty, R. P. and Singh, C. (2026). Instructor Framing and Incentives Shape Physics Students' Engagement and Learning Gains from an Inquiry-Based Electrostatics Tutorial on the Method of Images. [doi:10.3390/educsci16040594](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16040594)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview evidence after tutorial revision: two figures asking students to label regions and field lines, plus a John-and-Emily dialogue and a uniqueness-theorem hint, were added; interviewed students' answers showed they recognized image charges belong in the excluded region. No effect size printed.

> "Students demonstrated improved understanding of the concept after working through the tutorial."
