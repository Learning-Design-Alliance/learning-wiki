## Evidence

### Dialogic Reading 2010

Dialogic Reading. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/136

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Randomized controlled trial comparing parent-implemented dialogic reading with a group story time comparison among 19 three- to five-year-olds with mild to moderate language delays. "The WWC did not find statistically significant differences on any measure"; the study's average effect size was 0.11, not significant, and average improvement index +4.

> "The authors did not find statistically significant differ- ences between the parent-led intervention and the comparison group on any of the measures. The WWC did not find statistically significant differences on any measure"
