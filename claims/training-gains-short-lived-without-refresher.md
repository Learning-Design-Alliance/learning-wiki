## Evidence

### Alameddine M 2015

Alameddine M, Mufarrij A, Saliba M, Mourad Y, Jabbour R, Hitti E. (2015). Nurses' evaluation of physicians' non-clinical performance in emergency departments: advantages, disadvantages and lessons learned. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0733-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the paired t-test results across the three cycles, in the discussion of the secondary analysis of nurse evaluations at one academic ED. The article reports the drop as significant but prints no effect size.

> "The improvement in the evaluation scores between the first and second evaluation cycles was a temporary result, as evaluation scores during the third evaluation cycle dropped significantly, to reach close to the levels of the first evaluation cycle. Following the second evaluation, no trainings were offered and the only intervention was the offering of individualized feedback."
