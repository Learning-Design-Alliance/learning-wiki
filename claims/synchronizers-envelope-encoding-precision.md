## Evidence

### Silvia Bonacina 2021

Silvia Bonacina, Stephanie Huang, Travis White-Schwoch, Jennifer Krizman, Trent Nicol and Nina Kraus. (2021). Rhythm, reading, and sound processing in the brain in preschool children. npj Science of Learning 6:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00097-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on envelope-encoding precision with synchronization group as between-group variable (Synchronizers N = 63, Non-synchronizers N = 37) and stimulus and time region as within-subject factors. The article reports "greater envelope precision than “Non-synchroni- zers”(F(1, 87) = 10.860, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.111)".

> "Across all four stimuli ([ba], [da], [ga], and [da]noise), the main effect of synchronization group was found, with the “Synchroni- zers” showing greater envelope precision than “Non-synchroni- zers”(F(1, 87) = 10.860, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.111)"

### Silvia Bonacina 2021 (2)

Silvia Bonacina, Stephanie Huang, Travis White-Schwoch, Jennifer Krizman, Trent Nicol and Nina Kraus. (2021). Rhythm, reading, and sound processing in the brain in preschool children. npj Science of Learning 6:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00097-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

The omnibus three-way interaction of synchronization group, stimulus, and time region on envelope-encoding precision from the same repeated-measures ANOVA; the article reports "group by stimulus by time region ( F(1, 87) = 4.314, p = 0.041, ηp2 = 0.047)".

> "An interaction was found between all the three conditions group by stimulus by time region ( F(1, 87) = 4.314, p = 0.041, ηp2 = 0.047)"
