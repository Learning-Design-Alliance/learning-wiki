# Students with internal LOC show higher GPA and SOC than students with external LOC

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Students with an internal LOC scored significantly higher in all SOC dimensions and overall SOC (Cohen's d ≈ 0.8–1.0) and had higher GPA (Cohen's d ≈ 0.55) than students with an external LOC (p < 0.001). [→ Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026](#dashtegol-ali-nasirzadeh-2026)

## Evidence

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Independent t-test comparison in the cross-sectional sample of 348 students (41.1% internal, 58.9% external LOC). GPA means were 15.72 ± 1.43 (internal) vs 14.98 ± 1.22 (external); SOC differences were described as large (Cohen's d ≈ 0.8–1.0).

> "GPA was also significantly higher among students with an internal LOC than those with an external LOC (p < 0.001). Differences in SOC dimensions and overall, SOC were large (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.8–1.0), while the difference in GPA was moderate (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.55)"
