## Evidence

### Jamaludin 2026

Jamaludin, A., Samyuktha, S. & Tan, A. L. (2026). Regulation competencies and social well-being in at-risk adolescents: a systematic review of behavioral and neurophysiological research. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00435-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a longitudinal Australian community-sample study of adolescent self-injury, in which "ER difficulties and low self-efficacy as predictors with family support buffering risk"; baseline lifetime NSSI was 23.7% (n = 507). No effect size was printed.

> "23.7% reported lifetime NSSI and 11.8% reported past-month NSSI at baseline (n = 507), with follow-up prevalence of 15.2% at 11 months and 17.6% at 22 months, and the study reported ER difficulties and low self-efficacy as predictors with family support buffering risk."
