## Evidence

### Hyde Sarah 2018

Hyde Sarah, Hannigan Ailish, Dornan Tim, McGrath Deirdre. (2018). Medical school clinical placements – the optimal method for assessing the clinical educational environment from a graduate entry perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1113-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke approach, NVivo-supported) of 387 MCPI comments and 55 DREEM comments. The article reports DREEM comments emphasised "interactions with academic staff and organisational aspects of the course, rather than the educational environment".

> "Themes emerging from analysis of the MCPI data were ‘learning at work’, ‘students in placement’, and ‘finding balance’."
