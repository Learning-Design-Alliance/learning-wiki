## Evidence

### Raney Julia H. 2019

Raney Julia H., Morgan Melissa C., Christmas Amelia, Sterling Mona, Spindler Hilary, Ghosh Rakesh, Gore Aboli, Mahapatra Tanmay, Walker Dilys M. (2019). Simulation-enhanced nurse mentoring to improve preeclampsia and eclampsia care: an education intervention study in Bihar, India. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-019-2186-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

GEE logistic regression adjusted for duration between first and last simulations, on 39 matched video pairs. The article reports the epigastric pain question "increased from 43.6 to 51.3% (p = 0.03)"; the Foley catheter increase was a non-significant trend (p = 0.06).

> "The proportion of simulations in which mentees 'asked about epigastric pain' increased from 43.6 to 51.3% (p = 0.03) and the proportion in which 'Foley catheter was inserted' trended upwards from 38.5 to 56.4% (p = 0.06)."
