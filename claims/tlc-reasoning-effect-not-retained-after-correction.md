### Amy E. Barth 2016

Amy E. Barth, Sharon Vaughn, Philip Capin, Eunsoo Cho, Stephanie Stillman-Spisak, Leticia Martinez, and Heather Kincaid. (2016). Effects of a Text-processing Comprehension Intervention on Struggling Middle School Readers. Top Lang Disord. 2016; 36(4): 368–389. https://doi.org/10.1097/TLD.0000000000000101
