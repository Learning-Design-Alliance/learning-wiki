Dahal N, Hasan MK and Islam MT (2026). GenAI and AI tools for STEAM educators: evidence-informed strategies and resources for the future of teaching. Front. Educ. 11:1948064. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1948064

Narrative synthesis of the reviewed corpus: the review attributes the engagement finding to reviews of STEAM and creativity, reporting that interdisciplinary learning becomes more engaging when students connect ideas across media and produce meaningful artifacts. No effect sizes are printed.

"First, GenAI can increase engagement and motivation by supporting personalization, multimodal expression, and authentic production.
