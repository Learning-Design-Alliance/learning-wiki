### Rotem Reut 2024

Rotem Reut, Carey Michael O., McCarthy Claire M., O'Reilly Barry A., Daykan Yair, O'Sullivan Orfhlaith E. (2024). Preserving Essential Skills: The Future of Vaginal Hysterectomy Training in Urogynaecology. BJOG. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.17974

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey item asking respondents to grade the complexity of each vaginal hysterectomy step from very easy to very difficult (Figure 3). "The most difficult step to learn was perceived to be opening the uterovesical fold (103, 21.9%)"; colpotomy was rated easiest (116, 24.9%).

> "The most difficult step to learn was perceived to be opening the uterovesical fold (103, 21.9%), followed by performing the suspensory suture (100, 21.5%). The easiest step to learn was colpotomy (116, 24.9%), followed by suture ligation of the uterosacral ligament (107, 22.7%)."
