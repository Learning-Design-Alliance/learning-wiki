## Evidence

### Fredrik D. Johansson 2018

Fredrik D. Johansson. (2018). Machine Learning Analysis of Heterogeneity in the Eﬀect of Student Mindset Interventions. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.05975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.05975)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 10391 students in 76 schools from the NSLM study using multiple ML estimators; the analysis reports a "positive average eﬀect of 0.26, 95%-CI [0.22, 0.30]". No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "We ﬁnd that the mindset intervention has a positive average eﬀect of 0.26, 95%-CI [0.22, 0.30], and that heterogeneity in the range of [0 .1, 0.4] is moderated by school-level achievement level, poverty concentration, urbanicity, and student prior expectations."
