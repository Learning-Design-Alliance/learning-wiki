Evidence¶

Vavoula 2009¶

Vavoula, G., Sharples, M., Rudman, P., Meek, J., & Lonsdale, P. (2009). Myartspace: Design and evaluation of support for learning with multimedia phones between classrooms and museums. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2009.02.007

q2 · i? · design · r3

Student questionnaire data from the final full-scale trial. The article reports that "48% of the students found Myartspace 'very easy to use'", with no students rating it difficult.

"In a questionnaire administered immediately after the visit, 48% of the students found Myartspace 'very easy to use', 30% 'somewhat easy', and 22% 'average', with none indicating it was 'difficult' or 'very difficult'."

Vavoula 2009 (2)¶

Vavoula, G., Sharples, M., Rudman, P., Meek, J., & Lonsdale, P. (2009). Myartspace: Design and evaluation of support for learning with multimedia phones between classrooms and museums. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2009.02.007

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus group reports from students in the final trial. The article notes students found the visit "less boring" and "more modern" than traditional visits, with photos and audio recording enjoyed most.

"They found that Myartspace made the visit less boring, "more modern" than traditional visits to a museum. Taking pictures and recording audio were highlighted as the functions they enjoyed most."
