Arps 1917¶

Arps, G. P. (1917). Attitude as a determinant in spelling efficiency in immediate and delayed recall. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Arps+attitude+spelling+efficiency

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Same single-learner study of 104 word groups. In delayed recall without prior study, the random procedure produced 104 errors versus 145 for the orderly procedure, the methods' efficiencies being "reversed" relative to immediate recall, with random "exceeding that of the orderly procedure by 16.4%".

"in the case of delayed recall the efficiencies of the two methods are reversed —the random exceeding that of the orderly procedure by 16.4%."
