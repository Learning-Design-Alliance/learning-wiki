## Evidence

### Alexander Small 2017

Alexander Small. (2017). Range restriction, admissions criteria, and correlation studies of standardized tests. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1709.02895](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1709.02895)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Comparison of simulations using a linear admissions model (Table 3) versus the accurate model (Table 1): with equal weights, correlations were similar (e.g., 0.197 vs 0.219 for P,GRE among all admitted). The article states a decreasing marginal rate of substitution would be ideal but may be less important than the curve's overall orientation.

> "getting the precise shape of the admissions curves correct is less important than having curves that satisfy two criteria: 1. The independent variables (i.e. observable student characteristics at the time of admission) receive proper relative weights. 2. One measure can compensate for another, i.e. the curve slopes down and to the right."
