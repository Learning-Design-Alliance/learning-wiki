## Evidence

### Allan Pau 2016

Allan Pau, Chen Yu Sui, Lee Verna Kar Mun, Sow Chew Fei, Alwis Ranjit De. (2016). What does the multiple mini interview have to offer over the panel interview? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29874

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 131 completed MMI scoresheets from applicants who attended both a 30-min PI and a ten-station MMI. The study reports "The correlation coefficient between total PI and MMI scores was 0.438 (p=0.001)"; for a PI score of 15, MMI scores ranged from 60 to 90.

> "The correlation coefficient between total PI and MMI scores was 0.438 (p=0.001), although it should be noted that for any PI score, the range of MMI scores was quite wide"
