Evidence¶

van der Sande 2023¶

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Categorical moderator analysis on affirming motivations: teacher reports d = 1.53 (one study), self-reports d = 0.36 (k = 43), observations d = 0.31 (k = 6); Q(2) = 91.20***. Comprehension was measured by tests in all studies, so no such analysis was conducted for it.

"A significant effect of measurement type was found on affirming motivations, indicating that effects were largest for teacher reports, as compared to self-reports and observations."
