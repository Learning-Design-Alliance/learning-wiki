## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2013 posttest comparison of part B (application/analysis) scores between MS (n = 24) and HS (n = 28) undergraduates. The article reports the group difference appeared only on HS-specific questions and concludes both groups exhibited gains in lower and higher levels of cognition with no significant difference between the two groups.

> "However, HS undergraduates only scored significantly higher than MS undergraduates on the HS-specific questions, as determined by a Mann-Whitney U-test (p < 0.05), and there were no significant differences between the scores of the common questions, suggesting there was no effect of participation in the HS group on higher levels of cognition."
