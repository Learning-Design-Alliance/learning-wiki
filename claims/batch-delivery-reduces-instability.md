### Vinson 2026

Vinson, J., Caronia, C., & Savargaonkar, R. (2026). Input structure–driven instability and convergence in large language model clinical reasoning: a formative study using pediatric residency–level MCQs. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-48326-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional comparative study comparing two delivery formats for the same 77 validated pediatric MCQs across six LLMs. The article reports that sequential delivery in batches of ten "substantially reduced performance dispersion and instability across all evaluated systems." Pairwise comparisons used paired t-tests with p < 0.01 threshold.
