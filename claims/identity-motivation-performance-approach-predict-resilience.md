## Evidence

### Yan Yan 2022

Yan Yan, Gai Xiaosong. (2022). High Achievers from Low Family Socioeconomic Status Families: Protective Factors for Academically Resilient Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315882

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (Table 4) across grades 6, 9, and 11; identity motivation ORs were 1.19, 1.27, and 1.48 and performance-approach ORs were 1.30, 1.59, and 1.77, all significant. Intrinsic and mastery-approach motivations were not significant. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "when the intrinsic motivation of disadvantaged students increases by one unit, the likelihood of becoming a citywide resilient student increased by a corresponding 19%, 27%, and 48% (p < 0.01, p < 0.001, p < 0.001); each unit increase in performance-approach goal orientation increased the likelihood of becoming a citywide resilient students by a corresponding increase of 30%, 59%, and 77% (p < 0.05, p < 0.001, p < 0.001)."
