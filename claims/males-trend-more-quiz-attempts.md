## Evidence

### Pavo Noemi 2022

Pavo Noemi, Niedermaier Thomas, Seitz Stefanie, Jäger Harald, Strametz-Juranek Jeanette, Rieder Anita, Anvari-Pirsch Anahit. (2022). Gender differences in examination behavior of 4th grade medical students: Results from an observational study on ECG e-learning. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-021-01959-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of attempt counts by gender in the 1315-student cohort, reported as a non-significant trend (p = 0.070). Separately, the study reports time spent per attempt decreased with every new attempt (1506 s vs 1272 s vs 1184 s, p = 0.009).

> "There was a trend towards a higher total number of attempts for male students (576 [84%], 84 [12%] and 26 [4%] for females vs 500 [79%], 105 [17%] and 24 [4%] for males completing 1, 2 or 3 attempts, p = 0.070)."
