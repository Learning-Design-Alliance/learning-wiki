### Exact Path Growth: Evidence Rating 2024

Exact Path Growth: Evidence Rating. (2024). https://www.edmentum.com

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental study in Duval County Public Schools (2023-24) comparing grade 6 students who engaged with at least one Exact Path lesson to students who only completed the diagnostic assessment; the math analysis included 4,597 students and found a "statistically significant positive effect" with effect size +0.06.

> "Results indicated that Exact Path had a statistically significant positive effect on both Mathematics (effect size = +0.06) and ELA (effect size = +0.16), with students in the intervention group outperforming their peers."
