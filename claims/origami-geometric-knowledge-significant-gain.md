### Akayuure et al. 2016

Peter Akayuure, S. K. Asiedu-Addo, Victor Alebna. (2016). Investigating the Effect of Origami Instruction on Preservice Teachers’ Spatial Ability and Geometric Knowledge for Teaching. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology. https://doi.org/10.18404/ijemst.78424

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on GKT posttest scores in the quasi-experiment (experimental n = 52, control n = 42). Descriptive posttest means were 67.96 (experimental) versus 61.42 (control) on a 100-point max; the mean difference over treatment was 40.25 versus 31.84. No univariate effect size value is printed.
