Alharbi, M. (2015). Effects of Blackboard’s Discussion Boards, Blogs and Wikis on Effective Integration and Development of Literacy Skills in EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 8(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n6p111

Part C open-ended responses categorized into six features showed interaction with readers (31–32%) and outlet/access for writing (29–30%) as the top two, followed by communicative online writing (17%) and detailed topic reading/writing (13%).

> "The results above indicate that the informants felt the most positive about interaction with readers (31%), then finding an outlet for their writing (29%), and writing in English on the Internet."
