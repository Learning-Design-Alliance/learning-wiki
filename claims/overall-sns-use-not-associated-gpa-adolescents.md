## Evidence

### Aquino-Blanco A 2025

Aquino-Blanco A, Quiroz-Cárdenas F, Montenegro-Espinosa JA, Martín-Calvo N, Yañéz-Sepúlveda R, Gutiérrez-Espinoza H, Olivares-Arancibia J, Rahmati M, Stubbs B, Smith L, López-Gil JF, Jiménez-López E. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02180-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of 583 adolescents from the EHDLA study using robust generalized linear models with Gaussian family, adjusted for sex, age, socioeconomic status, BMI, physical activity, sedentary behaviors, sleep duration, and energy intake. The article reports "The association did not reach statistical significance" for overall SNs use and GPA.

> "This result indicates that for each additional point in SNs use, the GPA decreases by an average of 0.05 points. The association did not reach statistical significance (B = -0.05; 95% CI -0.10 to 0.01; p = 0.092; β = -0.08)."
