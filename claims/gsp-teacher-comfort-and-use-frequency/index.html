Jiang et al. 2015¶

Zhonghong Jiang, Edwin Dickey, Alexander White, Ewelina McBroom, M. Alejandra Sorto, & Alana Rosenwasser. (2015). A Dynamic Geometry-Centered Teacher Professional Development Program and Its Impact. Proceedings of the 37th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education, East Lansing, MI: Michigan State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584322

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Self-report DGIQ administered six times across the school year to the experimental group; 31 teachers completed it. The article reports "77% of them used GSP at lea st one time per week" versus 61% of students, indicating medium-intensity implementation of hands-on student lab use.

"Out of the 31 teachers who completed the questionnaire, 77% of them used GSP at lea st one time per week and 38% at least two times per week. However, the student use was lower, with 61% of them using GSP at least one time per week, and only 10% two times per week."
