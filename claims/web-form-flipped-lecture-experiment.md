## Evidence

### S Staacks 2022

S Staacks, D Dorsel, S Hütz, F Stallmach, T Splith, H Heinke, C Stampfer. (2022). Collaborative smartphone experiments for large audiences with phyphox. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac7830

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Implementation in the lecture experimental physics 1, winter term 2019/20, with about 350 first-semester students; groups of two or three built smartphone pendulums and submitted three length-frequency pairs via a web form. The article reports that "the data set generally reproduces the theoretically expected behavior" of a mathematical pendulum very well, with few outliers.

> "We received data sets from 49 student groups, who each submitted three pairs of pendulum length l and measured frequency f. While most pendulums were limited to a string length of less than 2 m, some setups in stairwells featured string lengths of up to 5 m"
