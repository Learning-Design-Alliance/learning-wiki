### House Joseph B. MD 2017

House Joseph B. MD, Choe Carol H. MD, Wourman Heather L. BA, Berg Kristin M. MD, Fischer Jonathan P. MPH, Santen Sally A. MD, PhD. (2017). Efficient and Effective Use of Peer Teaching for Medical Student Simulation. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2016.11.32753

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Program-level resource accounting from the clerkship implementation, not a controlled comparison. The article reports nine versus three physician hours monthly and estimates "an estimated annual reduction of 60 hours at an estimated cost savings of about $11,000".

> "Each month, our physician-led simulations required a total of nine hours of physician time, while our student-led simulations required only three hours for the single physician to monitor learning and to answer questions."
