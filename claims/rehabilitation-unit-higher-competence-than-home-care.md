## Evidence

### Lotvonen S 2026

Lotvonen S, Parkkila M, Tuomikoski A-M, Kääriäinen M, DiBiasi V, Platani E, Bruno I, Siira H. (2026). Self-Assessed Restorative Care Competence Among Nursing Professionals in Finland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Western Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/01939459261478193

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across work areas in the cross-sectional survey of 438 nursing professionals. The article reports significant work-area associations on five subscales and states "The RC competence of nursing professionals' working in the rehabilitation ward was better in 7 subscales than those working elsewhere".

> "The nursing professionals' work area was statistically significantly associated with RC competence in 5 subscales: understanding the impact of values and social factors in RC (F[3, 432] = 2.76, P = .042, η2p = 0.02), promoting physical functioning (F[3, 432] = 3.95, P = .008, η2p = 0.03), promoting mental well-being and social capability (F[3, 432] = 2.88, P = .035, η2p = 0.02), understanding the basic principles of RC (F[3, 432] = 3.48, P = .016, η2p = 0.02), and utilizing cooperation and technology in RC (F[3, 432] = 6.72, P = .001, η2p = 0.04)."
