## Evidence

### Kuhn Josepha 2022

Kuhn Josepha, Mamede Silvia, van den Berg Pieter, Zwaan Laura, van Peet Petra, Bindels Patrick, van Gog Tamara. (2022). Learning deliberate reflection in medical diagnosis: does learning-by-teaching help? Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10138-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the first explanation videos (only 17 recorded correctly due to technical problems), scored by two raters with Kappa = 0.87 for order, showed "11 residents described the procedure perfectly" and four omitted the one-diagnosis-at-a-time sequencing. This suggests the procedure was learned but not uniformly.

> "Out of the 17 explanation videos we analysed, 11 residents described the procedure perfectly. Four residents described all steps but did not state clearly that you should first analyse one diagnosis and only after that think of the next diagnosis."
