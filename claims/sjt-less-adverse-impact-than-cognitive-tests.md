`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article asserts, citing a 2008 meta-analysis of SJT subgroup differences (Whetzel, McDaniel & Nguyen), that "the adverse impact of SJTs on under-represented groups is generally less than that observed for cognitive or academic tests"; no effect size is printed.

> "There is also literature demonstrating that the adverse impact of SJTs on under-represented groups is generally less than that observed for cognitive or academic tests."
