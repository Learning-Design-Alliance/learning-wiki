## Evidence

### Geary David C. 2017

Geary David C., Nicholas Alan, Li Yaoran, Sun Jianguo. (2017). Developmental Change in the Influence of Domain-General Abilities and Domain-Specific Knowledge on Mathematics Achievement: An Eight-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000159

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 5 of the longitudinal functional data analysis (n = 167) added the 6th-grade fractions comparison measure and estimated effects for grades 6 to 8 only. The fractions effect rose from β = .150 (p = .021) in 6th grade to .178 (p = .002) in 7th and .206 (p = .002) in 8th, while the central executive effect fell from .252 and .248 to .180 (p = .007). Only in 8th grade was the fractions effect at least as large as the central executive's. The data are correlational and, as the authors note, do not support strong causal inferences.

> "the individual effect for fractions comparison (β = .206, p<.002) is at least as important as that of the central executive (β = .18, p<.007)."
