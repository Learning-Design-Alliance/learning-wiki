Sumayyah Qaed Alsulami 2016¶

Stage 2 comparison notes from the single-participant case study; for the rainbow task the article states that everything noticed while comparing his own writing to the model involved lexical features, listing the four words he noted.

"In his notes on the model rainbow paragraph, everything the participant noticed while comparing his own writing to the model involved lexical features. The words that he listed included “droplets,” “strike,” “prism,” and “reflect.”"
