Evidence¶

Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse 2012¶

Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse. (2012). Interactive Digital Kitchen: The impact on Language learning. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. https://digitalinstitute.ncl.ac.uk/ilablearn/kitchen

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Between-group comparison of post-minus-pre-test scores in the comparative experimental study of 54 learners, analysed with independent t-tests in SPSS. The article reports the difference was significant and that "the experimental group performed better than the control group in the post-test."

"Table 2 depicts that the mean score for the experimental group was higher than the control group (t = 2.78, df = 52, p < .05) with a mean difference of 1.83."
