## Evidence

### Allensworth 2005

Allensworth, E. M., & Easton, J. Q. (2005). The On-Track Indicator as a Predictor of High School Graduation. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/track-indicator-predictor-high-school-graduation

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Ten-year trend analysis of CPS freshman cohorts, reported with Figures 6–9. Average course-taking rose from 5.64 to 7.0 courses and pass rates from 76 to 81 percent; the report links course-taking increases to specific CPS policy changes such as double-period math requirements.
