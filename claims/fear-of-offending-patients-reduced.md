`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre/post survey item from the 99-student cohort. Agreement with fearing offense fell from 80.8% to 48.5%, a statistically significant reduction at p < .001, though nearly half still reported fear.

> "Prior to the course, 80.8% of participants (n = 99) somewhat or strongly agreed that they feared offending a patient with a physical disability when speaking about their disability. After the course, this was reduced to 48.5% (p < .001)."
