Refitting the five models with both KS1 and KS2 achievement entered in the 2019 English census. The article reports the correlations with the original models "are now also extremely similar (1.000, .994, .995, .988, .988)" and moderate/large-effect percentages change only very slightly.

> "These results suggest that there is very little benefit to adjusting for early achievement in addition to prior achievement. The school effects are essentially the same."
