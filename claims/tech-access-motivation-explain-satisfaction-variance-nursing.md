## Evidence

### Innab 2023

Innab, A., & Alqahtani, N. (2023). The mediating role of E‐learning motivation on the relationship between technology access and satisfaction with E‐learning. Nursing Open, 10, 2552–2559. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1513

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on cross-sectional survey data from N = 139 Saudi nursing students recruited via social media. The regression found technology access and e-learning motivation "influenced and explained 30.4% of the variance in satisfaction with e‐learning"; motivation had the larger standardized coefficient.

> "technology access (β = 0.209, p < 0.05) and e‐learning motivation (β = 0.373, p < 0.001) influenced and explained 30.4% of the variance in satisfaction with e‐learning (R2 = 0.304, p < 0.001) when controlling for the sample demographic characteristics"
