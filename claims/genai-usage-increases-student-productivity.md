### Fabian Walke 2026

Fabian Walke, Veronika Föller. (2026). Generative AI Usage of University Students: Navigating Between Education and Business. 20th International Conference on Wirtschaftsinformatik. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16307](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16307)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study: eleven part-time students at a German distance learning university were interviewed and the transcripts coded with grounded theory methodology. Participants reported a 'notable increase in productivity' from GenAI usage, with time savings attributed to GenAI taking on non-value-added work such as summarizing content, debugging codes and preparing slides.

> "The consequences of GenAI usage show a  notable increase in productivity. This productivity increasement results in increased study efficiency, work effectiveness and overall optimized performance , leading to time savings ."
