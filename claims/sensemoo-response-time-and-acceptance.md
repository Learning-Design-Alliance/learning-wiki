### Kushan Choksi 2024

Kushan Choksi, Hongkai Chen, Karan Joshi, Sukrutha Jade, Shahriar Nirjon, and Shan Lin. (2024). SensEmo: Enabling Affective Learning through Real-time Emotion Recognition with Smartwatches. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1109/mass62177.2024.00066](https://doi.org/10.1109/mass62177.2024.00066)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Response-time evaluation using software timers with real lecture slides from MIT OpenCourseWare, recorded for analysis without adaptation. The article reports the average response time is "approximately 3.1 seconds".

> "After analyzing the results, we find that the average response time of the feedback system is approximately 3.1 seconds, which is significantly shorter than the duration of any individual lecture slide."

### Kushan Choksi 2024 (2)

Kushan Choksi, Hongkai Chen, Karan Joshi, Sukrutha Jade, Shahriar Nirjon, and Shan Lin. (2024). SensEmo: Enabling Affective Learning through Real-time Emotion Recognition with Smartwatches. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1109/mass62177.2024.00066](https://doi.org/10.1109/mass62177.2024.00066)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feedback-acceptance evaluation tracking manual pace/content adjustments with a software counter. The article reports that "students only make approximately 2.2 interventions per minute", which the authors read as indicating the feedback system assists students in maintaining suitable pace and content.

> "Our experiments show that, on aver- age, students only make approximately 2.2 interventions per minute."
