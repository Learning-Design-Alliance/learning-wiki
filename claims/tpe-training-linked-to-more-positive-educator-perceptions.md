## Evidence

### Slotnik 2014

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2014). Real progress in Maryland: Student learning objectives and teacher and principal evaluation. WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center. http://marylandpublicschools.org/MSDE/programs/tpe/index.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Statewide web survey of 16,314 Maryland educators from 23 districts (response rates 5.7% to 72.6%), comparing trained versus untrained respondents. The report states that "educators who participated in trainings on classroom observation and/or SLOs are more likely to agree on almost every question on the survey," with differences marked statistically significant at the 95% confidence level; no effect size is printed.

> "Survey data confirm that educators who participated in trainings on classroom observation and/or SLOs are more likely to agree on almost every question on the survey"
