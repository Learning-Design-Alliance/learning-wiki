`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis of survey cognitive-load items against course scores in the mixed-methods study. The authors note this pattern "is consistent with the prediction from Cognitive Load Theory" that more effortful materials accompanied lower final grades.

> "The CL (Mean = 2.96/5) was at a moderate level, showing that the materials presented some challenges. Significantly, CL was negatively correlated with scores across all stages (CA1–CA4) and the total score (correlation with Total_CA = −0.316)"
