## Evidence

### Rosen Maya L. 2021

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ROI regression analysis in a longitudinal study of 62 children aged 6–8, with VVS ROIs defined functionally and results FDR-corrected at the level of hypothesis. Greater activation in both the left and right VVS "was associated with higher academic performance during both cued attention (β = 0.367, p = .006; β = 0.460, p = .0007" and memory-guided attention (β = 0.281 and 0.378).

> "greater activation in both the left and right VVS was associated with higher academic performance during both cued attention (β = 0.367, p = .006; β = 0.460, p = .0007, respectively; Fig. 9B) and memory-guided attention (β = 0.281, p = .041; β = 0.378, p = .011, respectively)."

### Rosen Maya L. 2021 (2)

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain regression during cued attention in the same 62-child sample, cluster-corrected with 3dClustSim. A large cluster "spanning left and right ventral temporal cortex was positively associated with academic achievement", encompassing fusiform gyrus, lingual gyrus, cerebellum and right IPS; a second cluster spanned left IPS, cuneus, and lateral occipital cortex.

> "academic achievement was positively associated with activation during cued attention in two clusters (Fig. 8; Table 3). Consistent with our prediction that VVS function may be related to academic performance, a large cluster spanning left and right ventral temporal cortex was positively associated with academic achievement—this cluster encompassed fusiform gyrus, lingual gyrus, cerebellum, and extended into the right IPS."
