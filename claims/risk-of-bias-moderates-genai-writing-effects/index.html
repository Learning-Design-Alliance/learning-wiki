Mark Feng Teng 2026¶

Mark Feng Teng. (2026). Effectiveness of GenAI in enhancing writing performance: a meta-analysis. Asian-Pacific Journal of Second and Foreign Language Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40862-026-00444-2

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Robust meta-regression with HC3 standard errors across the 11-study main synthesis. Among tested moderators, "only risk-of-bias classification (Unrated vs. High) significantly predicted effect size", with Pseudo R2 = 0.50; the article interprets this as methodological quality contributing to observed variation.

"only risk-of-bias classification (Unrated vs. High) significantly predicted effect size (β = 0.93, 95% CI [0.19, 1.68], p = 0.014), explaining approximately 50% of between-study variance (Pseudo R 2 = 0.50)."
