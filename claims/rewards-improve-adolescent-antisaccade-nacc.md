## Evidence

### Constantinidis Christos 2019

Constantinidis Christos, Luna Beatriz. (2019). Neural Substrates of Inhibitory Control Maturation in Adolescence. Trends in Neurosciences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2019.07.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Geier and Luna, that impending rewards raise adolescent antisaccade performance toward adult levels, reflecting increased engagement of executive processes for reward receipt.

> "in the presence of impending rewards AS performance in adolescence improves, approximating adult levels reflecting the ability to push the engagement of executive processes for the purpose of reward receipt [29]."

### Constantinidis Christos 2019 (2)

Constantinidis Christos, Luna Beatriz. (2019). Neural Substrates of Inhibitory Control Maturation in Adolescence. Trends in Neurosciences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2019.07.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing fMRI studies, greater adolescent nucleus accumbens activation during rewarded antisaccade trials; adolescents also showed greater frontal eye field activity than adults during correct rewarded responses.

> "Studies have found that during rewarded AS trials in adolescence, there is greater activation in the nucleus accumbens (NAcc) [60,61], a region rich in dopaminergic inputs and known to underlie reward processing [62,63]."
