Evidence¶

Junko Yamashita 2013¶

Junko Yamashita. (2013). Effects of extensive reading on reading attitudes in a foreign language. Reading in a Foreign Language, 25(2). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated-measures ANOVA on pretest–posttest questionnaire scores of 61 undergraduates in a 15-week ER course found a significant pretest–posttest difference for Comfort with a large effect (r = .54).

"Multiple comparisons identified significant differences between the pretest and the posttest for Comfort, Anxiety, and Intellectual Value (p < .05, Bonferroni-adjustments), but no difference for Practical Value. Effect size was large for Comfort (r = .54)"
