# A 40-hour EBP training program produces statistically significant posttest improvements in all five HS-EBP competency domains relative to no intervention in hospital nurses

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After the training program, between-group differences were statistically significant in the beliefs-attitudes, science, practice, assess, and barriers-facilitators domains of the HS-EBP questionnaire. [→ Yeh M.-L. 2025](#yeh-m-l-2025)

## Evidence

### Yeh M.-L. 2025

Yeh M.-L., Chang B.-Y., Dai H.-D., & Chung M.-H. (2025). Effect of training program on evidence-based practice competencies in hospital nurses in Taiwan: A quasi-experimental study. The Journal of Nursing Research, 33(5), e412. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000701

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttest analysis of the quasi-experimental study with 80 clinical nurses (40 exposure, 40 control) using the HS-EBP questionnaire. The article reports "statistically significant differences" on independent t tests in all five domains, with t and p values as printed; no effect sizes were reported.

> "After the training program, an independent t test was used to assess intergroup differences, resulting in statistically significant differences in the BeAt (t=7.47, p<.001), Science (t=6.49, p<.001), Practice (t=4.74, p<.001), Assess (t=3.94, p<.001), and BaFa (t=3.45, p=.001) domains."
