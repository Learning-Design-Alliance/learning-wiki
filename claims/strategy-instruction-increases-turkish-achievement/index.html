Muhittin ÇALIŞKAN & Ali Murat SÜNBÜL. (2011). The Effects of Learning Strategies Instruction on Metacognitive Knowledge, Using Metacognitive Skills and Academic Achievement (Primary Education Sixth Grade Turkish Course Sample). Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 11(1), 148-153. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ919894

Post-test comparison on a 28-item achievement test (KR-20 = .81, average difficulty .51, average distinctiveness .41) in the 42-student control group design; the difference favored "the experimental group". No effect size printed.

"When the achievement scores that the groups received from the achievement test were compared, a significant difference was observed in favor of the experimental group."
