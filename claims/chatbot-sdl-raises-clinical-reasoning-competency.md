## Evidence

### Han Jeong-won 2025

Han Jeong-won, Park Junhee, Lee Hanna. (2025). Development and effects of a chatbot education program for self-directed learning in nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07316-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with pretest-posttest parallel group design; 60 fourth-year nursing students randomized 1:1, analyzed as 31 experimental and 29 control. The chatbot group post-test mean was 56.10 ± 9.26 versus 47.21 ± 5.51 for control. The article attributes the gain partly to case-based learning: the program "incorporated case-based learning methods, allowing students to utilize clinical judgment effectively."

> "The experimental group, which received the chatbot educational program, showed statistically significant higher levels of Clinical reasoning competency (t = -5.00, p <.001), Confidence (t = -2.62, p =.011)"
