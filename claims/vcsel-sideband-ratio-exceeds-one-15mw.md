Characterization of the modulated laser output using a handmade Fabry-Perot cavity with 40 GHz free spectral range (Fig. 6). The measured "ﬁrst sideband/carrier ratio > 1" occurred at "moderate modulation power ≥ 15 mW (12 dBm)"; no effect size is reported.

> "Due to the high eﬃciency of the high-frequency current modulation, we were able to transfer a large fraction of the optical power in the ﬁrst modulation sideband, achieving the ﬁrst sideband/carrier ratio > 1 for moderate modulation power ≥ 15 mW (12 dBm)."
