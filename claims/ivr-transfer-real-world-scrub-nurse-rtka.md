## Evidence

### Edwards Thomas C. 2021

Edwards Thomas C., Patel Arjun, Szyszka Bartosz, Coombs Alexander W., Liddle Alexander D., Kucheria Rakesh, Cobb Justin P., Logishetty Kartik. (2021). Immersive virtual reality enables technical skill acquisition for scrub nurses in complex revision total knee arthroplasty. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-021-04050-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-arm pre-post study; ten scrub nurses repeated a real-world simulated operation assessment with real equipment before and after a four-session iVR curriculum, showing improvement from "11.3 ± 8.92% correct answers pre-training to 83.5 ± 0.12% after iVR training, p < 0.001". No control group; participants acted as their own controls.

> "Real-world performance of rTKA improved from 11.3 ± 8.92% correct answers pre-training to 83.5 ± 0.12% after iVR training, p < 0.001 (Fig. 4)."
