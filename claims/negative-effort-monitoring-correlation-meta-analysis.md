## Evidence

### Martine Baars & Lisette Wijnia & Anique de Bruin & Fred Paas 2020

Martine Baars & Lisette Wijnia & Anique de Bruin & Fred Paas. (2020). The Relation Between Students' Effort and Monitoring Judgments During Learning: A Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09569-3

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of k = 123 participant groups (5819 participants) found a mean correlation of "r = − .355 (95% CI [ − .408, − .300])", with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 79.57).

> "To answer Research Question 1, we analyzed the mean correlation between effort and monitoring judgment ( k = 123). In support of Hypothesis 1, a negative, small correlation was found, r = − .355 (95% CI [ − .408, − .300])."
