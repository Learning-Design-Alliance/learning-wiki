## Evidence

### Okuyama Ayako 2011

Okuyama Ayako, Martowirono Kartinie, Bijnen Bart. (2011). Assessing the patient safety competencies of healthcare professionals: a systematic review. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3203526/

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's cross-tabulation of Miller level by domain reports that "seven of a total of 10 tools, 70%" at lower levels assessed safety culture. In contrast, it reports most higher-level ('shows how' and 'does') tools assessed team work (17 of 20, 85%), risk management (15 of 20, 75%) and communication (11 of 20, 55%).

> "The majority of tools at the lower levels ('knows' and 'knows how') (seven of a total of 10 tools, 70%) assessed the 'contributing to a culture of patient safety' domain."
