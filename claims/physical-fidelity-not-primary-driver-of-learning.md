`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' interpretation, in this debate article, of the setting-comparison studies they describe: physical context or physical fidelity of the setting "is not the most important aspect for individual and team learning", indicating the semantic and motivational context can be more important. This is an interpretive claim, not a new empirical test.

> "the physical context or physical fidelity of the simulation setting, such as OSS or ISS, is not the most important aspect for individual and team learning, indicating that the semantic and motivational context can be more important"
