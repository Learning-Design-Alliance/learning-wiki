q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Cross-workshop comparison of the faculty and student workshops' differing views on structuring project-based and experiential learning. Faculty proposed defined learning scaffolds; students favored self-selected real-world problems and learning pathways.

"The divergence is thus not aboutwhetherto use real-world projects, but about the degree of structure and instructor direction in how those projects are framed."
