Evidence¶

Kovari 2025¶

Kovari, A. (2025). A systematic review of AI-powered collaborative learning in higher education: Trends and outcomes from the last decade. Social Sciences & Humanities Open, 11, 101335. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssaho.2025.101335

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's discussion of its second research question argues that task complexity and collaboration requirements shape the success of AI-enhanced collaborative learning, citing Zheng (2021) on designing tasks for deep understanding and active participation. This is the authors' interpretive synthesis, not a tested result.

"effective task design needs to strike a careful balance between challenge and accessibility in order not to frustrate or disengage students. While too-simple tasks tend to bore students, too-complex ones may spur anxiety or disengagement"
