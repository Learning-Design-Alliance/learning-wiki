Evidence¶

Fries 2017¶

Fries, M.-H. (2017). Teaching compound nouns in ESP: insights from cognitive semantics. In C. Sarré & S. Whyte (Eds), New developments in ESP teaching and learning research (pp. 93-107). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.cssw2017.747

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of a slideshow micro-corpus from 16 French Master's process-engineering students (eight per group) after one hour of explicit CN teaching. The author infers implicit learning from highly specialised compounds such as "vortex street" and "absorbance spectrum" in the experimental group's slides.

"This seems to indicate that students in the experimental group were able to translate the knowledge gained during their internship into [noun] + [noun] compounds more easily than in the control group."
