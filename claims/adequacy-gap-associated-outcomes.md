## Evidence

### Levin 2022

Levin, J., Baker, B., Lee, J., Atchison, D., & Kelchen, R. (2022). An Examination of the Costs of Texas Community Colleges. Regional Educational Laboratory Southwest. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive quintile analysis for 2019/20: colleges sorted into five adequacy-gap quintiles of roughly equal students served, comparing student-weighted average milestones. The report notes the 0.22 difference equals approximately 1 standard deviation (0.224) of the 2019/20 measure.

> "The colleges with the largest adequacy gaps (quintile 5) earned  an average of 2.24 success points milestones per full-time equivalent student compared with 2.46 for the colleges with the smallest adequacy gaps (quintile 1), a difference of 0.22 success points milestones"
