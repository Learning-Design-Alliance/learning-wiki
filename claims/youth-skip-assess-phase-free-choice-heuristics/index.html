Evidence¶

Gitari 2016¶

Gitari, W. (2016). The Application of School Science by Urban High School Youth through Problem-solving in Everyday Life. Science Education International, 27(3), 344-368. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1118432

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' interpretation of the focus group narratives: youth relied on trial and error, vicarious learning from parents and peers, and help from knowledgeable others (e.g., Raj's dad and an electrician), but rarely revisited or assessed solutions to extract new knowledge.

"Secondly, like Kamkwamba, all the youth engaged in free-choice learning, through the heuristics posited by Reif (2008) and Stokes (2009), albeit not necessarily the assess phase of problem-solving and knowledge application."
