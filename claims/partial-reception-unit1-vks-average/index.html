Evidence¶

Vincy 2018¶

Vincy, R. I. (2018). The impact of explicit instruction and intentional learning in the reception of vocabulary: An experimental study. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, 8(2). doi:10.26634/jelt.8.2.14155

q2 · i? · causal · r1

VKS self-report test on the fifteen Unit 1 prescribed words administered to all 30 participants after Session One; responses were evaluated and tabulated. The result supports the hypothesis that learners "receive vocabulary partially in the selected context".

"The total average performance of the entire class was found to be 14.23 with 21 and 1 as the highest and the lowest scores observed from the test which was on a total of 40."
