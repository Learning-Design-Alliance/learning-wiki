The abstract of the switching-replications quasi-experiment reports the trajectory of keystroke-logged writing speed across the two panels, naming the CSC subgroup exception. No effect sizes or statistics for this finding appear in the supplied text.

Related Claims¶

  • Creative writing instruction improved creative text quality in panel 1 and showed a transfer effect on argumentative text quality moderated by Creative Self-Concept, a pattern the second panel did not replicate
  • Students in both panels were generally positive about the creative writing unit's instructional design, with only two items scoring below neutral and a group difference on one scale
