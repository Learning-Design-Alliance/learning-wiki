Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/mitigating-the-academic-impacts-of-proximity-to-homicide

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Hierarchical linear modeling produced separate school-grade-year estimates of the proximity-to-homicide effect for high schools; the analysis "did, in fact, identify some schools as more successful at mitigating the negative effects" on academic performance.

"When comparing schools serving similar students with similar experiences outside of schools, most student outcomes declined after homicide near their home—but not all schools saw average declines in student outcomes."
