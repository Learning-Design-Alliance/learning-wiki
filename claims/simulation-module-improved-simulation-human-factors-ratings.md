### McInerney NJ 2025

McInerney NJ, Sexton GP, Roche A, Donnelly M, Skinner L. (2025). Development of an undergraduate otorhinolaryngology simulation education and human factors module: its impact on students' attitudes and perceptions. The Journal of Laryngology and Otology. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022215125103009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire analysis of 47 students using Mann–Whitney U tests. Importance of simulation training rose from 3.74 to 4.68 and human factors from 3.76 to 4.63; the article reports "importance of simulation (p < 0.05) and human factors (p < 0.05)". No effect sizes are printed.
