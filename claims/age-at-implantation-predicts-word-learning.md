## Evidence

### Derek M. Houston 2012

Derek M. Houston, Jessica Stewart, Aaron Moberly, George Hollich, and Richard T. Miyamoto. (2012). Word learning in deaf children with cochlear implants: effects of early auditory experience. Dev Sci. 2012 May ; 15(3): 448–461. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2012.01140.x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analyses were rerun without the five better-hearing subjects. Pre-CI hearing did not contribute significantly, but age at implantation accounted for a significant amount of the variance; a scatterplot is displayed in Figure 3b.

> "In this set of analyses, amount of pre-CI hearing did not contribute significantly to the variability in word-learning performance. However, age at implantation did account for a significant amount of the variance in these analyses."
