### Antoun Yaacoub 2025

Antoun Yaacoub, Zainab Assaghir, Lionel Prevost, Jérôme Da-Rugna. (2025). Analyzing Feedback Mechanisms in AI-Generated MCQs: Insights into Readability, Lexical Properties, and Levels of Challenge. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-way ANOVA over the full dataset of 14,832 feedback fields found significant tone × difficulty interactions for all four linguistic metrics, with the printed F statistics and p < 0.001. No effect sizes are reported, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Two-way ANOVAs showed significant interactions (p < 0.001) between feedback tone and difficulty level for all examined linguistic metrics. Specifically, significant effects were observed  for: feedback length (F(4, 14823) = 15.2), readability (F(4, 14823) = 21.7), vocabulary richness (F(4, 14823) = 10.5), and lexical density (F(4, 14823) = 8.9)."
