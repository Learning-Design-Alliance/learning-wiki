### Secchi Agostina 2025

Secchi Agostina, Whiskey Eromona, Nair Sajitha, Shergill Sukhi, Thomas Trudy. (2025). Competence and confidence of health care professionals in using clozapine: a qualitative systematic review and thematic synthesis. Acta Neuropsychiatrica. https://doi.org/10.1017/neu.2025.10024

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Qualitative thematic synthesis of 34 studies of health care professionals managing clozapine. The review reports that professionals "self-reported as competent with guidelines" while expressing less confidence in adherence and side-effect management. The review cautions self-report may overestimate competence.

> "HCPs self-reported as competent with guidelines (local and national), yet they expressed less confidence in their ability to adhere to them and were uncertain about managing side effects."
