## Evidence

### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis conducted using Principal Axis Factoring with Promax oblique rotation on data from 350 ICU nurses. Sampling adequacy findings as printed: KMO 0.912, Bartlett's χ 2 = 3559.18, p < 0.001.

> "Sampling adequacy analysis demonstrated a KMO value of 0.912, indicating excellent suitability of the data for factor analysis. Furthermore, Bartlett's test of sphericity was statistically significant (χ 2 = 3559.18, p < 0.001)."

### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026 (2)

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

EFA identified a three-factor structure consisting of Analytical–Evaluative Clinical Reflection, Person-Centered Ethical Reflection, and Risk-Oriented Preventive Reflection. Variance explained as printed for each factor and total.

> "These three latent factors accounted for 20.38%, 19.37%, and 17.59% of the total variance, respectively, collectively explaining 57.34% of the variance in critical reflection competency in delirium care."

### Magsoodi Fahimeh 2026 (3)

Magsoodi Fahimeh, Ghaffari Fatemeh (2026). Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS) Among Iranian ICU Nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70818

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Factor loadings examined across all 19 items in the rotated pattern matrix. Findings as printed: primary loadings 0.633–0.853, no substantial cross-loadings.

> "All 19 items demonstrated strong primary factor loadings, ranging from 0.633 to 0.853, with no substantial cross-loadings observed across the three factors (Table 2)."
