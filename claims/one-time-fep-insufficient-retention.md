## Evidence

### Thapa R. 2023

Thapa R., Nikolli K., McMahon D., Blakemore S., Tamang S., Bhatta S., Gautam P., Shrestha R., Rajbhandari R. (2023). Novel on-site follow-up and enhancement program (FEP) improves knowledge, clinical skills and enabling environment of skilled birth attendants in Nepal. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0285653

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' conclusion drawn from the mixed-methods findings and the Discussion, which cites a Bihar study of 319 public PHCCs where mentored nurses' task completion scores plateaued after one year, suggesting mentoring needs to be ongoing.

> "However, a one-time FEP is not sufficient in retaining clinical skills and knowledge."
