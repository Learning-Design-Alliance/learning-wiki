## Evidence

### C. Efthimiou 2006

C. Efthimiou, R. Llewellyn, D. Maronde, T. Winningham. (2006). Physics in Films: An Assessment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609154](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0609154)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal evaluation comparing pre-test and post-test average scores for four separate sections taught by three different instructors, using topical questions for each flavor. The "percent gains in correct answers" were 16.1% for Action 2005, 30.3% for Pseudoscience 2005, 41.7% for Pseudoscience 2004, and 26.4% for Superheroes 2004.
