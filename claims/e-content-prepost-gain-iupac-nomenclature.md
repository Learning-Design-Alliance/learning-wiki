G. Devendiran, M. Vakkil. (2017). Effectiveness of e-content package on teaching IUPAC nomenclature of organic chemistry at undergraduate level. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 14(3), 49-54. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1171298
