### Doshmangir Leila 2024

Doshmangir Leila, Alipouri Sakha Minoo, Mostafavi Hakimeh, Kabiri Neda, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Takian Amirhossein. (2024). Essential core competencies for health policy graduates: a multi-method consensus type study. Health Research Policy and Systems. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12961-024-01221-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Consensus phase of a multi-method study: an expert panel of nine members (ministry, professional and academic experts) rated 11 draft competencies on importance, necessity and relevance across two brainstorming sessions, consolidating six into others by consensus. The article reports the final list "presented in no particular order".

> "The final five core competencies, derived from the brainstorming session and presented in no particular order, encompass health policy research, policy analysis, educational competencies, decision-making and multidisciplinary work."
