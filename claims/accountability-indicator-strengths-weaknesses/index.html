Indicator-level descriptive results from the Malang City questionnaire survey (Table 2). The article reports the conducive-atmosphere and harmonious-communication indicators scored highest (243 and 241, High) and vision/mission evaluation indicators lowest (226, Medium).

"The indicator for the variable of the superior school accountability management system that has the highest score is that the school shows a conducive atmosphere for the entire school community and the school shows harmonious communication with the school community."
