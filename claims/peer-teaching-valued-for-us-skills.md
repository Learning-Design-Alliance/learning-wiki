### Wolf Robert 2019

Wolf Robert, Geuthel Nicole, Gnatzy Franziska, Rotzoll Daisy. (2019). Undergraduate ultrasound education at German-speaking medical faculties: a survey. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc34. https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001242

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results on resources and staffing: peer-student-tutors were increasingly used, six faculties offered special tutor training, and "Peer-teaching was thought to be important" at 24 faculties. Descriptive frequencies only.

> "Peer-student-tutors played an increasing role not only in elective, but also in compulsory US courses. Six medical faculties offered special training for peer-student-tutors with a 4-week elective in the US unit or instruction sessions by medical specialists. Peer-teaching was thought to be important for undergraduate medical US education (n=24)."
