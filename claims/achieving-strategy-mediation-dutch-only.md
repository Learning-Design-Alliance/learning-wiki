## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Multi-group SEM showed the model was significantly different across the three ethnic groups (p < 0.025), and autonomous motivation related to GPA via achieving strategy only for Dutch students (β = 0.14). [→ Isik U. 2018](#isik-u-2018)

## Evidence

### Isik U. 2018

Isik U., Wilschut J., Croiset G., Kusurkar R.A. (2018). The role of study strategy in motivation and academic performance of ethnic minority and majority students: a structural equation model. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-018-9840-3

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM across Dutch (n = 477), Western (n = 52), and non-Western (n = 89) students; the group comparison "showed a significant difference between the groups (Δ df = 34, Δ Chi square = 59.1, p < 0.025)". Printed path β = 0.14.

> "Autonomous motivation was related to GPA via achieving strategy only for Dutch students (β = 0.14, SE = 0.03, p < 0.001)."
