Related Claims¶

  • Psychosocial factors (bullying, interpersonal skills) and executive cognitive functions may mediate the obesity–achievement association — related
  • Girls with low mathematics achievement are at higher risk of developing mathematics anxiety and poor mathematics attitudes than low-achieving boys — related
