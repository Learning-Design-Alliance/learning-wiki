## Evidence

### Vinay AV 2025

Vinay AV, Sindhu R, Rasalkar K. (2025). Exploring peer learning module vs. conventional tutorials: effects on engagement and learning outcomes among first-year medical students. BMC Medical Education 25:101. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06549-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed rank comparison of pre- and post-test scores (same 10 MCQs) after Shapiro-Wilk testing showed non-normal data, so medians and interquartile ranges were reported; "the improvement was statistically significant with a p-value of < 0.001" for conventional tutorials.

> "The median score secured by the students participating in conventional tutorials improved from 5 to 7 and the improvement was statistically significant with a p-value of < 0.001"
