## Evidence

### Van Kelsey 2025

Van Kelsey, Tasawar Sana, Brendel Elaina B. K., Law Camille, Mahajan Anisha, Brownell‐Riddell Carissa, Diamond Natalia, Ritchie Kerry, Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a 'Students as Partners' model to develop an authentic assessment promoting employability skills in undergraduate life science education. FEBS Open Bio 15(3): 506–522. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13941

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of self-assessed versus instructor-assessed Data Extraction Assignment grades for survey respondents, shown in Fig. 2C. The correlation was "moderate significant" with r = 0.361 and P < 0.001; average student-assigned grade was 85.9% versus instructor 87.1%.

> "There was a moderate significant correlation between students' self‐assessed assignment grades and the instructor grades (r = 0.361; P < 0.001)"

### Van Kelsey 2025 (2)

Van Kelsey, Tasawar Sana, Brendel Elaina B. K., Law Camille, Mahajan Anisha, Brownell‐Riddell Carissa, Diamond Natalia, Ritchie Kerry, Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a 'Students as Partners' model to develop an authentic assessment promoting employability skills in undergraduate life science education. FEBS Open Bio 15(3): 506–522. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13941

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Distribution analysis of self-assessed versus instructor-assigned grades (Fig. 2D) showed "Most students (60.6%)" graded themselves lower than the instructor, 34.7% graded higher, and only 4.7% assigned themselves the same grade.

> "Most students (60.6%) self‐assessed grade was lower than the instructor‐assessed grade and 34.7% of students provided a self‐assessed grade that was higher than the instructor's assessment."
