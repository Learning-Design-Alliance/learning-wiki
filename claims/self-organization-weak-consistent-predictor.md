## Evidence

### Sternecker K 2026

Sternecker K, Schmitz C, Berndt M. (2026). Students’ Learning Outcomes in a Virtual Microscopic Online and Face-to-Face Anatomy Course: Cross-Over Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/82012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise linear regressions at four levels found self-organization as a predictor in all models, regardless of participation mode. Overall predictive power was low (R² up to 0.12 across levels). The influence was present regardless of whether students participated online or face-to-face.

> "In every calculated model on the 4 levels, the ability for self-organization gave an indication of how great the learning outcomes can be, but the prediction probability for the model was low"
