## Evidence

### Conlin Anne 2016

Conlin Anne, Lea Jane, Bance Manohar, Chadha Neil, Kilty Shaun, Kozak Frederick, Savage Julian, Sidhu Ravindar, Chen Joseph, Westerberg Brian D. (2016). Mental practice in postgraduate training: a randomized controlled trial in mastoidectomy skills. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-016-0162-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group repeated-measures comparison from the randomized trial's primary outcome, with pre-intervention means of 40.8 (SD 11.1) textbook and 42.3 (SD 5.0) mental practice and post-intervention means of 44.2 (SD 15.3) and 46.3 (SD 7.6). The article reports "a non-statistically significant higher total score post-intervention than pre-intervention (p = 0.182)"; no effect size is printed.
