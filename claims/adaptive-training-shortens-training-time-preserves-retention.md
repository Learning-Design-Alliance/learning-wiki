# Adaptive (performance-contingent) training shortens total VR assembly training time by about 5 minutes without increasing workload or impairing retention accuracy

## Evidence

### Pharmer 2025

Pharmer, R. L., Wickens, C. D., Plabst, L., Clegg, B. A., Hirshfield, L. M., Lewis, J. E., Nicoly, J. B., Spencer, C. A., & Ortega, F. R. (2025). The Impact of Simple, Brief, and Adaptive Instructions within Virtual Reality Training: Components of Cognitive Load Theory in an Assembly Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Between-experiment comparison of the adaptive-training group (Experiment 1) against the yoked fixed-training group (Experiment 2). The article reports training time "shortened ... from 44.9 (SD = 11.20) minutes ... to 39.6 (SD = 10) minutes ... (t(116) = - 2.58, p < .05, d = -0.48)".

> "implementing more optimal Germane Load via adaptive training (Experiment 1) shortened the average overall training time for all 8 shapes from 44.9 (SD = 11.20) minutes in the fixed-training condition to 39.6 (SD = 10) minutes in the adaptive training condition (t(116) = - 2.58, p < .05, d = -0.48)."

### Pharmer 2025 (2)

Pharmer, R. L., Wickens, C. D., Plabst, L., Clegg, B. A., Hirshfield, L. M., Lewis, J. E., Nicoly, J. B., Spencer, C. A., & Ortega, F. R. (2025). The Impact of Simple, Brief, and Adaptive Instructions within Virtual Reality Training: Components of Cognitive Load Theory in an Assembly Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combined mixed-measures ANOVA pooling both experiments (N = 118). The article reports "no between-subjects effect of Germane Load Optimality ... on either perceived workload ... or learning retention accuracy"; equivalence was not tested. The authors suggest saved resources were redeployed to learning rather than reflected in lower TLX.

> "The 2 × 2 × 2 mixed measures ANOVA revealed no between-subjects effect of Germane Load Optimality (manipulated by training type) on either perceived workload (F(1,115) = 1.07, p = .303, η2 = .01) or learning retention accuracy (F(1,115) = 0.16, p = 0.69, η2 = .01.)"
