# Blended PbL students attend fewer lectures on site but watch more lecture videos than conventional PbL students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A lower proportion of bPbL students than cPbL students reported attending at least one lecture on site (63% vs. 78%). [→ Servos Ulrike 2022](#servos-ulrike-2022)
`q3 i?` A higher proportion of bPbL students than cPbL students reported watching at least one lecture video (38% vs. 21%). [→ Servos Ulrike 2022 (2)](#servos-ulrike-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Servos Ulrike 2022

Servos Ulrike, Reiß Birger, Stosch Christoph, Karay Yassin, Matthes Jan. (2022). A simple approach of applying blended learning to problem-based learning is feasible, accepted and does not affect evaluation and exam results—a just pre-pandemic randomised controlled mixed-method study. Naunyn-Schmiedeberg's Archives of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00210-022-02306-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Proportions of lecture attendance compared by 2 × 2 contingency tables and Fisher's exact test in the randomised main study. 120 of 153 (78%) cPbL versus 41 of 65 (63%) bPbL students attended at least one lecture on site (p < 0.05).

> "While 120 out of 153 (78%) cPbL students reported having attended at least one lecture on site, only 41 out of 65 (63%) bPbL students did (p < 0.05)"

### Servos Ulrike 2022 (2)

Servos Ulrike, Reiß Birger, Stosch Christoph, Karay Yassin, Matthes Jan. (2022). A simple approach of applying blended learning to problem-based learning is feasible, accepted and does not affect evaluation and exam results—a just pre-pandemic randomised controlled mixed-method study. Naunyn-Schmiedeberg's Archives of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00210-022-02306-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video use was compared with Fisher's exact test in the same randomised main study; 21% of cPbL versus 38% of bPbL students watched at least one lecture video (p < 0.05).

> "In contrast, 21% of the cPbL students reported watching at least one lecture video, while 38% of the bPbL students did (p < 0.05)"
