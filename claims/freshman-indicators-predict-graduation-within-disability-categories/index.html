q2 · i? · associational · r2

Chapter 3 analysis (Figure 8) of the 2001 cohort relating freshman GPA to five-year graduation within each of five categories. The report notes a large decrease in graduation rates when moving down each half-grade point.

"Students with a 2.5 (C+) average or higher are very likely to graduate from high school within five years: 89 percent of students without identified disabilities, 78 percent of students who are two or more years below grade level, 86 percent of students with a learning disability, and 83 percent of students with a mild cognitive disability graduated within five years."
