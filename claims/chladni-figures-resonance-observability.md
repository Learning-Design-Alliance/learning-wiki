## Evidence

### Montalbano 2012

Montalbano, V., & Di Renzone, S. (2012). An empirical study on a learning path on wave physics focused on energy. Department of Physics, University of Siena. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1212.2174](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1212.2174)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account of a laboratory activity in the Sounds and Surroundings path, in which students used a speaker connected to a function generator with metal plates of different shape; the article states that in this case resonance is very easy to recognize.

> "When sound was resonant with one frequency of the plate, salt started jumping leaving from the vibrating surface and cumulating in fixed zones."
