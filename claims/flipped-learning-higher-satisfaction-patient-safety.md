## Evidence

### Budak Vildan 2025

Budak Vildan, Kılıç Hülya Fırat, Cevheroğlu Seda. (2025). The Impact of a Flipped Learning on Nursing Students' Patient Safety Competencies and Satisfaction With the Education Method: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT, posttest satisfaction was measured with the 16-item Satisfaction Questionnaire (range 16–80). The flipped group scored 65.94 ± 5.91 versus 54.23 ± 15.04 in the control group, a statistically significant difference.

> "the SQ scores obtained by the intervention group were found to be statistically significantly higher than those of the control group (t = 4.281; p < 0.001)"
