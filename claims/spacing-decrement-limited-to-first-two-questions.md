## Evidence

### Keith B. Lyle 2022

Keith B. Lyle, Campbell R. Bego, Patricia A. S. Ralston, Jason C. Immekus. (2022). Spaced Retrieval Practice Imposes Desirable Difficulty in Calculus Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09677-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same within-participants ANOVA on quiz scores (N = 180) revealed a "significant interaction between order and timing" with ηp² = 0.131, superseding the order main effect (ηp² = 0.158).

> "Ther e was also a main effect of order, F(2, 358) = 33.67, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.158, but t his was superseded by a significant interaction between order and timing, F(2, 358) = 26.92, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.131."

### Keith B. Lyle 2022 (2)

Keith B. Lyle, Campbell R. Bego, Patricia A. S. Ralston, Jason C. Immekus. (2022). Spaced Retrieval Practice Imposes Desirable Difficulty in Calculus Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09677-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-corrected paired t tests on each question position (N = 180) showed massed performance "significantly better performance on the first question" (gav = 0.42) and second (gav = 0.24), with no significant difference on the third.

> "Massing was associated with significantly better performance on the first question, t(179) = 5.81, p < 0.001, Hedges' gav = 0.42, and second question, t(179) = 3.04, p = 0.003, Hedges' gav = 0.24. The conditions did not differ significantly on the third question, t(179) = 1.33, p = 0.185."
