## Evidence

### Javidialsaadi 2025

Javidialsaadi, M., Albert, S. T., Al Mutairi, B. M. S., & Wang, J. (2025). Protection of savings by reducing the salience of opposing errors. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00352-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Control experiment: 15 participants adapted to an abrupt 24° rotation in a KINARM exoskeleton, washed out with no visual feedback, then re-learned. Exponential model fits showed "almost doubling subject learning rates" with d = 1.33.

> "As expected, prior exposure to the rotation accelerated adaptation, almost doubling subject learning rates (Fig. 1e; paired t-test, t(14) = 5.16, p < 0.0001, Cohen’s d = 1.33)."
