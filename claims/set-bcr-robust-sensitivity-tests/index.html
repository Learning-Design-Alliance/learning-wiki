Evidence¶

Alli Klapp 2017¶

Alli Klapp, Clive Belfield, Brooks Bowden, Henry Levin, Robert Shand, Sabine Zander. (2017). A benefit-cost analysis of a long-term intervention on social and emotional learning in compulsory school. International Journal of Emotional Education, 9(1), 3-19. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Sensitivity analysis reported in Table III of the BCA. The article states the intervention "is unlikely to have a benefit-cost ratio that is less than one" and prints ratios of 6.6 (S1, smaller cohort), 7.0 (S2, heavy users only) and 5.6 (S3, 60% fade-out).

"Finally, even if the fade -out rate is 60% within the first year, the benefits still exceed the costs with 6:1."
