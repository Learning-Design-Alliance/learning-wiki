Descriptive trend in six-year bachelor's completion among four-year college enrollees, shown in Figure 3, rising from 46 percent in 2009 to 50 percent in 2014. The national rate rose from 58 to 60 percent over the same period; no effect size is printed.

> "The 2014 six-year graduation rate for students who enrolled at four-year colleges for CPS gradu- ates is 50 percent—a 4 percentage point increase since 2009 ( see Figure 3 ). This increase reflects 1,100 more four-year college graduates in 2014 versus 2009."
