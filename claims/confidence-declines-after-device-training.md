# Confidence in operating a syringe pump declines substantially in the two weeks after e-Learning-based device training, regardless of learning method

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In Experiment 1, confidence was higher immediately after training than at follow-up (p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.538). [→ Grundgeiger 2023](#grundgeiger-2023)
`q3 i3` In Experiment 2, confidence was likewise higher immediately after training than at follow-up (p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.479). [→ Grundgeiger 2023 (2)](#grundgeiger-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Grundgeiger 2023

Grundgeiger, T., Ertle, F., Diethei, D., Mengelkamp, C., & Held, V. (2023). Improving procedural skills acquisition of students during medical device training: experiments on e-Learning vs. e-Learning with hands-on. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(1), 127-146. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10148-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 (47 nursing students) confidence ratings on a 1-7 scale; a large main effect of test, "p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.538", with no learning method × test interaction, p = 0.635.

> "A significant main effect of the factor test indicated that participants were more confident immediately after completing the e-Learning program compared to the follow-up session (immediate: M = 3.65, SD = 1.06 vs. follow-up: M = 2.72, SD = 1.10; p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.538)."

### Grundgeiger 2023 (2)

Grundgeiger, T., Ertle, F., Diethei, D., Mengelkamp, C., & Held, V. (2023). Improving procedural skills acquisition of students during medical device training: experiments on e-Learning vs. e-Learning with hands-on. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(1), 127-146. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10148-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (56 participants) replicated the confidence decline, "p < 0.001; ηp2 = 0.479", with no learning method × test interaction, p = 0.898.

> "A significant main effect of the factor test indicated that the participants were more confident immediately after completing the e-Learning program compared to in the follow-up session (immediate: M = 4.15, SD = 1.20 vs. follow-up: M = 2.81, SD = 1.31; p < 0.001; ηp2 = 0.479)."
