Evidence¶

Vats Shivangi 2026¶

Vats Shivangi, Thomas Manuel S., Jathanna Vinod. (2026). Efficacy of Hand Gestures and the Hand as Foot Teaching Method Compared to Traditional Methods in Medical and Dental Education: A Scoping Review. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.70190

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The review's Table 2 summarizes an empirical study by Lisk et al. evaluating the Anatomy Glove Learning System, a glove imprinted with anatomically correct bones plus marker-drawing videos. It reports "improved significantly in both groups immediately after learning" and diverging confidence trajectories. No effect size is printed.

"Knowledge of hand anatomy improved significantly in both groups immediately after learning. However, self‐confidence in the 3D group (AGLS) gradually increased over time, while in the 2D group, it began to decline 1 week later."
