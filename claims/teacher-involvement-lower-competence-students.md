## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Teacher involvement correlated negatively with students' weekly competence beliefs (ρ = -.28) and with academic performance (ρ = -.45). [→ Malmberg and & Martin 2019](#malmberg-and-martin-2019)

## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Between-level associations in Model 3 from teacher reports (22 teachers) and student weekly averages. Teachers were "more involved with lower performing students" and less task-focused students; involvement also correlated ρ = -.28 with competence beliefs (p < .001).

> "teacher involvement was higher with lower performing students, ρ = -.45; p < .001, and with less task-focused students ρ = -.42; p < .001"
