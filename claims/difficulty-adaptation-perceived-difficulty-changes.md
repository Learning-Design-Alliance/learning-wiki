## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The level of adaptation interacted with difficulty condition on perceived difficulty, η2p = .06; participants reported lower perceived difficulty when task difficulty was substantially decreased and higher when substantially increased. [→ Koskinen 2023](#koskinen-2023)

## Evidence

### Koskinen 2023

Koskinen, A., McMullen, J., Hannula-Sormunen, M., Ninaus, M., & Kiili, K. (2023). The strength and direction of the difficulty adaptation affect situational interest in game-based learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104694

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on in-game perceived difficulty ratings found "significant interaction effect between level of adaptation and difficulty condition" with η2p = .06; Table 6 shows d = 0.36 for level −3 and d = 0.25 for level +2, while the main effect of difficulty condition was non-significant (p = .11).

> "there was a significant interaction effect between level of adaptation and difficulty condition, F (5, 948) = 11.22, p < .001, η2p = .06"
