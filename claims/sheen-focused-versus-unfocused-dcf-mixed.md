`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis result for Sheen et al. (2009), an 80-participant college study comparing focused DCF, unfocused DCF, and control conditions over 9 weeks, measured by accuracy of a/the in writing (Table 2 reports d = 0.3211 focused, -0.4473 unfocused).

> "One study generated mixed results with a large positive effect size for the Focused DCF group, whereas a large negative effect size was demonstrated for the Unfocused DCF group (Sheen et al., 2009)."
