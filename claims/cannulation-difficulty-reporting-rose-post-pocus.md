## Evidence

### Hill K 2023

Hill K, Jaensch A, Childs J, McDonald S. (2023). Evaluation of point of care ultrasound (POCUS) training on arteriovenous access assessment and cannula placement for haemodialysis. The Journal of Vascular Access. https://doi.org/10.1177/11297298231194100

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of routinely collected cannulation-difficulty data before and after the intervention (n = 40 pre, n = 54 post prevalent patients; over 3500 needle placements across the periods). The article reports the rates descriptively and attributes the rise mainly to increased reporting.

> "The post POCUS implementation data demonstrates a higher cannulation difficulty rate (5%) for the treatments collated."
