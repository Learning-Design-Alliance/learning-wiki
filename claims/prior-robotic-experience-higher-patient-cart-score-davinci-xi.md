## Evidence

### Cannata G 2025

Cannata G, Leone N, Salzano A, Rebecchi F, Morino M (2025). Training in the use of basic functions of the daVinci Xi® robot: a comparative study of residents’ skills. Updates in Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13304-025-02150-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative study of 30 University of Turin residents (15 per group) tested on daVinci Xi patient-cart exercises after two video tutorials, with blinded expert scoring. Table 5 shows mean total cart scores of 11.40 (Group A) versus 9.13 (Group B), and the article states "Group A scored higher on the patient cart exercises" with p = 0.0001. No effect size is printed.

> "Group A scored higher on the patient cart exercises, and the difference reached statistical significance (p = 0.0001)."
