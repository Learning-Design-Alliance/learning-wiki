Descriptive result from the cross-sectional study; academic performance was the two-semester 2016/17 average grade score across all disciplines taken from school records, with the median AP of 70 (IQRs 64.60 and 82.40). The article reports "49.9%" high and "50.10%" low; no effect size applies.

> "The proportion of high AP of school-age children was 49.9% (95% CI: 44.6, 55.0), whereas 50.10% (95% CI: 45.3%, 55.7%) scored low in the Sewena and Dawe Katchen districts."
