## Evidence

### Kameg Brayden 2026

Kameg Brayden, Lepore Madeline, Matthews Emily, Mitchell Ann. (2026). Nursing Students' Perceptions of Small-Group Standardized Patient Simulations to Enhance Therapeutic Communication Skills. Journal of the American Psychiatric Nurses Association. https://doi.org/10.1177/10783903261422688

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus-group study: six in-person focus groups with 47 BSN students after two series of SP simulations across nine scenarios; inductive thematic content analysis. Students reported practicing in a "safe, standardized environment" and becoming "comfortable with discomfort."

> "students felt that exposure to SPs enabled them to practice interpersonal and therapeutic communication skills in a safe, standardized environment"
