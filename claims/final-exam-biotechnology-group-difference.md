### Chrispeels H. E. 2014

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

2012 final exam analysis (MS n = 23, HS n = 37) comparing the two HS-specific biotechnology questions with other exam questions within and between groups. The article reports the asymmetric pattern and adds that HS undergraduates who taught these conceptually complex methods scored significantly higher than the MS undergraduates.
