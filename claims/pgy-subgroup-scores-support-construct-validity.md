### Zdradzinski Michael J. 2019

Zdradzinski Michael J., Backster Anika, Heron Sheryl, White Melissa, Laubscher Deborah, Siegelman Jeffrey N. (2019). A Novel Simulation to Assess Residents’ Utilization of a Medical Interpreter. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10853

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive subgroup analysis from the fall 2016 implementation with 60 residents. The article reports the medians "PGY 3: 20.5; PGY 2: 19.5; PGY 1: 15.0" and interprets the split as construct validity evidence; no statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "Median scores on the assessment were noted to decrease by PGY subgroup (PGY 3: 20.5; PGY 2: 19.5; PGY 1: 15.0), showing a split between junior and senior residents and lending evidence for construct validity of this simulation as an assessment tool."
