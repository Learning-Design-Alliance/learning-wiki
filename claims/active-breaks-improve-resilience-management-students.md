## Subclaims
`q3 i2` University students in management training who performed four daily 15-minute active breaks for 12 weeks improved resilience significantly more than controls (interaction η² = 0.0458). ### Latino Francesca; Tafuri Francesco; Maisuradze Mariam; Tafuri Maria Giovanna 2025

Latino, F., Tafuri, F., Maisuradze, M., & Tafuri, M. G. (2025). Enhancing Academic Performance, Cognitive Functions, and Mental Well-Being Through Active Breaks: Evidence from a Pilot Study with University Student Sample. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. [doi:10.3390/ijerph22111605](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22111605)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A 12-week randomized controlled pilot with 60 management students (30 per group), cluster-randomized by class. The two-way mixed ANOVA showed a significant Group × Time interaction for CD-RISC resilience; Table 3 prints η² = 0.0458 for the interaction, described as "Strongest for Resilience (4.6%)".

> "Group × Time Interaction: Significant for Resilience (F(1,58) = 6.16, p = 0.015)"
