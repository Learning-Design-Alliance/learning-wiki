## Evidence

### Paul W. Irving 2016

Paul W. Irving, Michael J. Obsniuk, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2016). P3: A Practice Focused Learning Environment. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aa7529](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aa7529)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative research interviews with students at the end of the first two P3 iterations, assessing buy-in and practice-focused goals. The article reports the community theme was "evidenced in nearly all of the student interviews conducted"; no effect size applies.

> "The fact the students considered the classroom as a community and the eﬀects of being a member of this community was evidenced in nearly all of the student interviews conducted."

### Paul W. Irving 2016 (2)

Paul W. Irving, Michael J. Obsniuk, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2016). P3: A Practice Focused Learning Environment. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aa7529](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aa7529)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same interview study yielded mixed evidence on computational utility: one student described using code for failure analysis, while the authors note "Several of the students interviewed did not understand why computation was being used in the classroom".

> "We are not saying that all of the students interviewed exhibited such changes in perception. Several of the students interviewed did not understand why computation was being used in the classroom nor what advantages it was aﬀording them."
