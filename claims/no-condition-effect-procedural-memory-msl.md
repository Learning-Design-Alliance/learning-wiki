## Evidence

### Jourde 2026

Jourde, H.R., Sita, K.Z., Eyqvelle, Z., Brooks, M., & Coffey, E.B.J. (2026). Modulating sleep: slow oscillation and spindle stimulation effects on physiology and memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00383-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A repeated measures ANOVA on the Motor Sequence Learning task found a significant main effect of Time (all participants improved) but no Time × Condition interaction ("F(4,96)=0.71,p=0.589"), meaning no stimulation condition differentially changed procedural memory performance.

> "the interactionbetweenTimeandConditionwasnotsignificant, F(4,96)=0.71,p=0.589, 𝜔2 =0.000indicatingthatwhile all participants improved their performance on the task, the experimental conditions did not differentially affect their change in performance on the procedural task (MSL) over time"
