Secondary analysis in the randomized study: the 80 unsuccessful first-attempt participants retried PVC; second-attempt success was 52.9% (NIR), 44.9% (isometric), 50.0% (control), with no significant difference and no printed effect size.

> "Unsuccessful participants (n = 80) were allowed to make a second attempt. There was no statistically significant difference between groups regarding PVC skill acquisition at the second attempt (c2 = 0.319; P =.852)"
