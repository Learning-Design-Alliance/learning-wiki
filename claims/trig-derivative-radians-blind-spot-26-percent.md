### Yossi Ben-Zion 2025

Yossi Ben-Zion, Jianqiang Liu, Chun-Ming Wang, Atanu Rajak, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2025). Global Blind Spot in Understanding Trigonometric Derivatives: A Multinational Analysis. [doi:10.1103/t2fv-2spd](https://doi.org/10.1103/t2fv-2spd)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive statistics from a multinational in-class survey of 769 college students on the radians-versus-degrees case code for trigonometric derivatives (Q3). The article reports "26.3% of participants (202)" correct and 70.7% (544) selecting both as valid; 3% (23) chose degrees.

> "For trigonometric derivatives (Q3), 26.3% of participants (202) correctly identified that the angle must be in radians for the derivative to be valid. However, 70.7% (544) incorrectly selected the option indicating that both radians and degrees were correct"
