## Evidence

### Ziad Mohammed Mahmoud Qabaja 2016

Ziad Mohammed Mahmoud Qabaja, Jamal Subhi Ismail Nafi’, Maisa’ Issa Khalil Abu-Nimah. (2016). The Effect of Using the “SQP2RS via WTL” Strategy through Science Context to 10th Graders’ Reading Comprehension in English in Palestine. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1115849

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 139 tenth graders in two Bethlehem schools, experimental group taught with “SQP2RS via WTL”, control by traditional method, three months, pre/post reading comprehension achievement test analyzed with 3-way ANCOVA. The F value was (88.025) with significant level (0.000).
