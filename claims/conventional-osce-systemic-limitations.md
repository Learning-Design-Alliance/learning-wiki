## Evidence

### Huh Yeon-Ju 2026

Huh Yeon-Ju, Shin Joon Sung, Yoon Narae, Kim Ju Whi, Kim Do Hoon, Kim Chanwoong, Jeong Seoi, Yoon Yejin, Shin Soyeon, Kong Hyoun-Joong, Myung Sun Jung. (2026). Metaverse-based objective structured clinical examinations: an exploratory approach to advancing clinical competency assessment. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.151

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consensus workshop with a multidisciplinary panel of 10 experts including medical educators and specialists across six clinical fields. The workshop identified three key issue areas: restricted scenario range due to SP reliance, reliability concerns from checklist-based scoring, and financial and time burdens on students and institutions.

> "Because current OSCEs rely on SPs, the range of clinical scenarios that can be simulated is inherently limited."
