## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' entity implicit theories of emotional intelligence in 10th grade negatively predicted their perceptions of emotional expression (trait EI) and their EI performance on the Vocabulary of Emotions Test in the following year. [→ Ana Costa 2023](#ana-costa-2023)

## Evidence

### Ana Costa 2023

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same path analysis of the three-wave longitudinal survey (N = 222). The final model showed "entity ITEIs (T1) negatively predicted students’ perceptions of emotional expression" and VET performance in 11th grade; only entity ITEIs were related to the performance EI measure, and the link was negative.

> "whereas entity ITEIs (T1) negatively predicted students’ perceptions of emotional expression (trait EI) and EI performance (VET) in the following year (T2)."
