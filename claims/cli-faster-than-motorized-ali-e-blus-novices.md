## Evidence

### Uysal Daniel 2020

Uysal Daniel, Gasch Claudia, Behnisch Rouven, Nickel Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Hohenfellner Markus, Teber Dogu. (2020). Evaluation of new motorized articulating laparoscopic instruments by laparoscopic novices using a standardized laparoscopic skills curriculum. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-08086-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized cross-over trial of 50 laparoscopic novices performing the E-BLUS examination with each instrument type in a box trainer; a mixed model with sequence, period, and instrument as fixed effects showed "CLI 4:27 min vs. ALI 4:50 min; p-value 0.005". Per-exercise comparisons reached significance only for needle guidance.

> "Overall, using CLI was significantly faster (CLI 4:27 min vs. ALI 4:50 min; p-value 0.005) and associated with fewer exercise failures in needle guidance (CLI 0 vs. ALI 12; p-value 0.0005) than ALI."
