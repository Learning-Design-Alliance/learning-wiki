Multiple regression analyses in the survey of 1,045 undergraduates tested social and contextual factors measured with a researcher-developed instrument. The article reports "ethical and moral education emerged as a negative predictor" of AI-assisted academic dishonesty. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.
