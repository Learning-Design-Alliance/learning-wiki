New Approaches in Educational Research 4(2), 84-90. https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.2015.7.124

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the language post-test ANCOVA (Table 2), the GROUP * CS interaction was significant (F=3.319, p=0.043) even though the main effects were not. The authors' interpretation, offered cautiously with 'Apparently', is that field independents gained most.

> "Apparently cooperative learning benefits field independent participants in this area. It did not help the dependent field and had no effect on intermediates."
