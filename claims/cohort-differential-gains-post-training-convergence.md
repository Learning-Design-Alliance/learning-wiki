## Evidence

### He Lidan 2026

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Cohort-specific analysis of comprehensive competency across three cohorts (N = 10, 10, 8) after 12 months of platform training. The 2023 cohort showed "the most substantial relative improvement" at 22.9%; the 2022 and 2021 cohorts improved 19.1% and 15.4% respectively.

> "The 2023 cohort (first-year residents) demonstrated the most substantial relative improvement, with competency scores increasing by 22.9% from baseline (mean difference 15.7 points, 95% CI [11.6-19.8], Cohen's d=2.26, t = 6.53, p < 0.001)."

### He Lidan 2026 (2)

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-cohort one-way ANOVA on comprehensive competency. Baseline disparities were significant while post-training variation was not; the article also reports inter-cohort SD reduction from 4.04 to 2.12 (47.5% variance reduction). Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "One-way ANOVA comparing cohorts identified disparities in initial competency measurements (F = 4.25, p = 0.025), yet post-training assessments showed no statistically meaningful variation (F = 2.14, p = 0.138)."
