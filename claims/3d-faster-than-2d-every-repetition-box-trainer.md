# 3D-trained novices performed significantly faster than 2D-trained novices on every repetition of both box-trainer tasks

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` With every repetition, 3D subjects completed both tasks significantly faster than 2D subjects, with median time savings of 13 s (Task 1) and 35 s (Task 2). [→ Kunert W 2020](#kunert-w-2020)

## Evidence

### Kunert W 2020

Kunert W, Storz P, Dietz N, Axt S, Falch C, Kirschniak A, Wilhelm P. (2020). Learning curves, potential and speed in training of laparoscopic skills: a randomised comparative study in a box trainer. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07768-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised comparison of 40 novices training Task 1 (ten repetitions) and Task 2 (eleven repetitions) under 2D or 3D vision. The article reports "the 3D subjects performed significantly faster than the 2D subjects" with median time savings of 13 s and 35 s and the printed p values.

> "With every repetition the 3D subjects performed significantly faster than the 2D subjects (Task 1: median time saving 13 s, p < 0.001; Task 2: median time saving 35 s, p < 0.05)."
