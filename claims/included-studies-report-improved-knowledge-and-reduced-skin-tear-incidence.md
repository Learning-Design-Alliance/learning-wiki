The scoping review reports that the four included studies, primarily prospective quasi-experimental studies, showed key outcomes including improved knowledge levels and reduced skin tear incidence. The review notes that all of them show a positive impact on the acquisition of knowledge and on the reduction of ST incidence or prevalence.
