## Evidence

### Sikström 2024

Sikström, P., Valentini, C., Sivunen, A., & Kärkkäinen, T. (2024). Pedagogical Agents Communicating and Scaffolding Students' Learning : High School Teachers' and Students' Perspectives. Computers and Education, 222, Article 105140. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105140

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of teacher and student interviews; participants described PA communication in terms of the two instructional communication dimensions. The conclusion states that "affirmative and relational PA communication can strengthen the student–PA relationship while supporting effective learning management."

> "Both the teachers and students believed that a PA should communicate similarly to a good teacher and described PA communication as having two dimensions that are known from the instructional communication literature: relational and task-related communication."
