## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students learned significantly more and were more motivated with two distinct agents than with one combined mentor agent. [→ Kim 2015](#kim-2015)

## Evidence

### Kim 2015

Kim, Y., & Baylor, A. L. (2015). Research-Based Design of Pedagogical Agent Roles: a Review, Progress, and Recommendations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0055-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the split-persona effect from Baylor and Ebbers (2003): students "learned significantly more and had significantly greater motivation" with two agents than one mentor agent. No effect size printed in this review.

> "we found the split-persona effect, indicating that splitting agent functionality into two distinct agent roles (i.e., Expert and Motivator) is preferable to combining into one agent role (Mentor)"
