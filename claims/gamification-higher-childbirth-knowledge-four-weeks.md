## Evidence

### Behmanesh Fereshteh 2025

Behmanesh Fereshteh, Gholamnia-Shirvani Zeinab, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Nikbakht Hossein-Ali, Nazmi Sana. (2025). Designing, implementing, and evaluating a gamification approach in childbirth training for midwifery students using computer and mobile application. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07370-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In a crossover trial with 34 midwifery students randomized to gamified versus lecture instruction on protocol topics, knowledge was tested before and four weeks after intervention. The gamified group scored "7.95 ± 1.84" versus 6.04 ± 2.00 for the lecture group, a standardized mean difference of -0.99 (-1.49 to -0.48).

> "However, four weeks poststudy, the difference in average knowledge scores increased to 1.91 (95% CI = 0.97–2.84), indicating statistical significance (P < 0.001)."
