The review's thematic synthesis of multiple reviewed studies reports that "using GenAI to generate an entire task is clearly considered cheating by students, while using it for grammar checks or brainstorming is largely seen as acceptable assistance", with middle-ground uses navigated through personal ethical reasoning.
