Narang 2025¶

Narang, Unnati, Vishal Sachdev, and Ruichun Liu. (2025). When AI Wears Many Hats: The Role of Generative Artificial Intelligence in Marketing Education. Journal of Public Policy & Marketing. https://doi.org/10.1177/07439156251328237

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cross-referencing of course syllabi learning objectives (coded as higher-order using Bloom-style wording such as "create" or "develop") with instructors' reported GAI use in the survey of 14 faculty. The article reports higher-order-objective courses "tend to use and emphasize GAI skills more in any of its roles."

"We found that courses requiring higher-order thinking in their course objectives (e.g., objectives containing words like "create" or "develop") tend to use and emphasize GAI skills more in any of its roles."
