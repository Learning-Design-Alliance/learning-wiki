## Evidence

### Orban 2017

Orban, C., Porter, C. D, Smith, J. R. H., and Brecht, N. K., Britt, C. A., Teeling-Smith, R. M., & Harper, K. A. (2017). A Game-Centered, Interactive Approach for Using Programming Exercises in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5058449](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5058449)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey question "Did the programming lab help you understand vectors better?" (Fig. 2, Ntot = 85) across four semesters at OSU Marion. The authors regard these self-reports "primarily as a gauge of student perceptions of how connected (or disconnected) the programming activities are from other course material."

> "The mixed results from this question underscores the need to carefully integrate the exercise with instructional material and targeted assessment as we discuss later."
