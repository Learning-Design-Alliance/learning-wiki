Case documentation from the Journeys Project: Amargosa chose a SIG model not requiring teacher replacement because rural hiring seemed too difficult, yet "more than half of their teachers had left anyway," creating an urgent recruitment challenge.

"The school had just eight weeks to fill 8 of its 14 certified positions, including the critical positions of counselor, reading specialist, and special education teacher."
