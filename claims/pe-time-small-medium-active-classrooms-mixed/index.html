Related Claims¶

  • Physically active classrooms produce a small improvement in academic performance compared with traditional sedentary classrooms
  • Physical activity has null or small-to-medium positive effects on school-age children's and adolescents' academic achievement according to meta-analyses
  • Chronic physical activity shows a medium positive effect on academic achievement while acute physical activity shows a null effect
  • Mathematics is the subject where positive associations with physical activity are found most consistently in systematic reviews, though meta-analytic effects range from null to medium — related
  • Students report higher enjoyment of active classrooms than of traditional classrooms — related
