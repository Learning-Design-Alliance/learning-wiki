## Evidence

### Letteri 2025

Letteri, I. & Vittorini, P. (2025). Enhancing Student Feedback in Data Science Education: Harnessing the Power of AI-Generated Approaches. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00492-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Expectation/experience study with 136 students in AY 2023/24 using combined LLM feedback; paired t-tests on 5-point Likert ratings. The article reports the experience was "better than expected", with the increase "from 3.11 to 3.47 for the feedback usefulness".

> "It shows that we measured an increase in the average ratings from 3.01 to 3.89 for understanding the learning progress and from 3.11 to 3.47 for the feedback usefulness. Both differences are statistically significant."
