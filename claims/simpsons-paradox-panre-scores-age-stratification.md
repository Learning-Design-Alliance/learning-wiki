# Most recent traditional PANRE scores showed a Simpson's paradox: the overall advantage for the PANRE-LA group reversed within most age subgroups

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Age-stratified analysis of most recent traditional PANRE scores (Figure 2B) in the full cohort; the article reports the overall means 509.1 versus 504.7 (P < .001) and subgroup mean differences of 2.8 to 5.6 points favoring the traditional PANRE group within most age groups. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "A notable finding consistent with Simpson's paradox was observed: while the overall analysis demonstrated that PAs who chose the PANRE-LA had a significantly higher mean score on the most recent traditional PANRE compared with those who chose the traditional PANRE (509.1 vs 504.7; P < .001), the subgroup analyses by age group revealed an opposing trend."
