## Evidence

### Luke 2017

Luke, Christina C.; Gibson, Sierra; Francisco, Aubrey T. (2017). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Using Edtech to Support English Learners. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/383e02ba-587a-4192-be68-175543798661 [doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/26](https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/26)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ANCOVA on Cajon Valley fifth graders (pilot N = 89, comparison N = 109 per Figure 1) controlling pre-test Lexile, with slopes constrained equal across students. The adjusted mean difference favored the pilot group, "scored significantly higher, on average" (p < 0.001).

> "the adjusted mean for current Lexile scores was 575.88 for students using Achieve3000 and 497.20 for students who did not use the program, which suggests that students in the pilot treatment scored significantly higher, on average (y01 = 78.679, SEy = 12.490, p < 0.001)"
