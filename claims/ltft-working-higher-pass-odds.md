## Subclaims
`q3 i?` LTFT candidates were 24% more likely to pass written and 14% more likely to pass clinical examinations at first attempt than full-time candidates. [→ Ellis Ricky 2025](#ellis-ricky-2025)

## Evidence

### Ellis Ricky 2025

Ellis Ricky, Knapton Andy, Cannon Jane, Lee Amanda J., Cleland Jennifer. (2025). Differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical examinations: examining the relationship between sociodemographic differences and examination performance. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-025-04034-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression of UKMED first-attempt outcomes adjusted for prior attainment and other sociodemographic factors; LTFT status was a significant independent predictor in both formats, and remained so in UKG-only models (OR 1.30 written; OR 1.33 clinical).

> "LTFT appeared to be a protective factor with LTFT candidates being 24% more likely to pass written and 14% more likely to pass clinical examinations (OR 1.24 (95% CI 1.15 to 1.33) and OR 1.14 (95% CI 1.05 to 1.25), respectively)."
