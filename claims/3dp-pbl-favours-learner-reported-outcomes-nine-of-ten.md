# Learner-reported self-evaluation outcomes favour 3D printing-integrated PBL over lecture-based teaching in 9 of 10 dimensions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Compared with controls, 3DP trainees rated themselves significantly higher on clinical thinking skills, learning initiative, self-study skills, analytical and problem-solving skills, interest, anatomical understanding, comprehension, teamwork, and satisfaction (all p < 0.001), with effect sizes r = 0.575–0.808. [→ Jiang Dingyuan 2025](#jiang-dingyuan-2025)

## Evidence

### Jiang Dingyuan 2025

Jiang Dingyuan, Duan Kui, Li Zhigang, Chen Xueming, Li Nani. (2025). The effect of 3D printing integrated with problem-based learning on AGP training for thoracolumbar fractures: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08152-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Self-report questionnaire with ten five-point Likert dimensions administered after the course in the RCT. The 3DP group scored "significantly higher" on nine dimensions (e.g., clinical thinking r = 0.736; self-study r = 0.575; anatomical understanding r = 0.808).

> "students in the 3DP group scored significantly higher in clinical thinking skills (U = 131.00, p < 0.001, r = 0.736 [95% CI: 0.616, 0.823]), learning initiative (U = 151.50, p < 0.001, r = 0.714 [95% CI: 0.586, 0.807]), self-study skills (U = 281.50, p < 0.001, r = 0.575 [95% CI: 0.407, 0.705])"
