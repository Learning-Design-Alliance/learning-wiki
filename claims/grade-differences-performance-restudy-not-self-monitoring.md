## Evidence

### Mariëtte H. van Loon 2021

Mariëtte H. van Loon, et al. (2021). Connecting teachers' classroom instructions with children's metacognition and learning in elementary school. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09248-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Child-level comparisons of 308 children (139 second graders, 169 fourth graders) on the secret code task. Fourth graders selected more incorrect-response items for restudy (45.10%, SD = 21.54 vs 29.79%, SD = 24.34); overall restudy percentage did not differ (t(306) = .46, p = .64).

> "analyses show that fourth graders' restudy selections were more effective than second graders' selections, t(206) = 2.74, p = .007. That is, fourth graders most accurately differentiated between learned and not-yet learned items."
