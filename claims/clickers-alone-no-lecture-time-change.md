## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Clicker class sessions showed no statistically significant difference from nonclicker sessions in mean percentage of 2-min time intervals with instructor lecturing or student listening. [→ Lewin JD 2016](#lewin-jd-2016)

## Evidence

### Lewin JD 2016

Lewin JD, Vinson EL, Stetzer MR, Smith MK. (2016). A Campus-Wide Investigation of Clicker Implementation: The Status of Peer Discussion in STEM Classes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-10-0224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational COPUS analysis of 264 class sessions (80 clicker, 184 nonclicker) at the University of Maine. The article reports "no statistically significant differences between nonclicker and clicker class sessions" for lecturing and listening; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, when comparing means (Figure 6B), there were no statistically significant differences between nonclicker and clicker class sessions (independent-samples t test, p = 0.4 for instructor lecturing and p = 0.2 for student listening)."
