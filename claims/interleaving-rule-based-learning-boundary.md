# Interleaving is especially useful for inductive, subtle pattern learning without a clear verbal rule, but can equal or exceed blocking in rule-based domains too

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jonathan Firth 2021

Jonathan Firth, Ian Rivers and James Boyle. (2021). A systematic review of interleaving as a concept learning strategy. Review of Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/rev3.3266

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of rule-based learning studies: Noh et al. (2016) found blocking better with a verbal rule for geometric patterns; Zulkiply (2015) found a reduced but present interleaving advantage for top-down learning; Rawson et al. (2015, Experiment 1b) found on-screen definitions disrupted the benefit.

> "While interleaving was found to be of benefit when examples were displayed by themselves, the presence of a definition on screen disrupted this benefit."
