## Evidence

### Fredericks Salim 2021

Fredericks Salim, ElSayed Mostafa, Hammad Mustafa, Abumiddain Omneya, Istwani Leila, Rabeea Abdulla, Rashid-Doubell Fiza, Bella Abdelhaleem M.E. (2021). Anxiety is associated with extraneous cognitive load during teaching using high-fidelity clinical simulation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1994691

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of STAI-S scores between first- and second-exposure subgroups in the same cross-sectional study. The difference was significant at the post-scenario time point only; no effect size was printed.

> "students with their first exposure to simulation (Mdn = 48) had higher STAI-S scores when compared to students on their second exposure to simulation for that academic year (Mdn = 37) at the post-scenario time point only (U = 315.5, p = 0.017)"
