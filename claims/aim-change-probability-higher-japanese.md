# Japanese participants change their aiming direction more frequently than Norwegian participants, regardless of previous hit or miss

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Aim change probability was significantly higher in the Japanese group than the Norwegian group (ηp² = 0.16), with pooled means of 45% vs 26%. [→ Yamada 2025](#yamada-2025)
`q3 i3` The degree of aim change was significantly larger in the Japanese group (ηp² = 0.49) and larger after miss than hit trials (ηp² = 0.36). [→ Yamada 2025 (2)](#yamada-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Yamada 2025

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

A two-way mixed ANOVA on the probability of aim change in the adaptation block of the cross-cultural experiment (24 per group) showed a significant cultural group effect, "ηp 2 = 0.16" (ηp² = 0.16), with pooled means of 45% (Japanese) and 26% (Norwegian).

> "the change probability was signi ﬁcantly higher in the Japanese group than in the Norwegian group,regardless ofwhether the cursor had hit the target on the previous trial (F(1,46) = 13.34,p < 0.001,ηp 2 = 0.16)"

### Yamada 2025 (2)

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

A two-way mixed ANOVA on the degree of aim change (difference in aimed landmarks across consecutive trials) found significant main effects of previous-trial outcome, "ηp 2 = 0.36" (ηp² = 0.36), and cultural group, "ηp 2 = 0.49" (ηp² = 0.49), with differences of 0.9° (Norwegian) vs 3.5° (Japanese).

> "the degree of aim change after the ‘miss’trials was signiﬁcantly larger than after the ‘hit’trials in both groups (F(1, 46) = 31.13, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.36), and it was larger in the Japanese group ( F(1, 46) = 293.27, p < 0.001,ηp 2 = 0.49)"
