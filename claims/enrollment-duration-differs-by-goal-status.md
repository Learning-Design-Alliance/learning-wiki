# Families who documented goals had longer enrollment in Head Start than families without goals

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The goal and no-goal subsamples differed significantly in time enrolled in Head Start, with all goal families enrolled six months or longer. [→ Patricia H. Manz 2013](#patricia-h-manz-2013)

## Evidence

### Patricia H. Manz 2013

Patricia H. Manz, Jaana Lehtinen, and Catherine Bracaliello. (2013). A Case for Increasing Empirical Attention to Head Start's Home-Based Program: An Exploration of Routine Collaborative Goal Setting. School Community Journal, 23(1). http://eclkc.ohs.acf.hhs.gov/hslc/standards/Head%20Start%20Requirements

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of enrollment duration between the two subsamples in the single-program record review was significant, χ2(1) = 4.27, p = 0.03; all families with documented goals were enrolled six months or longer, while some no-goal families were enrolled less than six months.

> "Similarly, these subsamples differed in the amount of time they were enrolled in Head Start (χ2(1) = 4.27, p = 0.03)."
