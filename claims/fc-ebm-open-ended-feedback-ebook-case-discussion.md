## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported that eBook pre-class self-study and active in-class discussion of clinical cases improved their learning effectiveness. [→ Huang Hsien-Li 2020](#huang-hsien-li-2020)
`q2 i?` A few students reported difficulty focusing on and understanding the eBook during self-study at home. [→ Huang Hsien-Li 2020 (2)](#huang-hsien-li-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Huang Hsien-Li 2020

Huang Hsien-Li, Chou Chia-Pei, Leu Steve, You Huey-Ling, Tiao Mao-Meng, Chen Chih-Hung. (2020). Effects of a quasi-experimental study of using flipped classroom approach to teach evidence-based medicine to medical technology students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1946-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative analysis of open-ended questions in the satisfaction survey of FC-EBM students, coded into two categories: effectiveness for learning and adjustment of classroom environment. Students praised organized, short eBook units and teacher-led discussion.

> "In addition, active discussion of clinical cases during class enable students to put their knowledge into practice resulting in improvement of learning effectiveness."

### Huang Hsien-Li 2020 (2)

Huang Hsien-Li, Chou Chia-Pei, Leu Steve, You Huey-Ling, Tiao Mao-Meng, Chen Chih-Hung. (2020). Effects of a quasi-experimental study of using flipped classroom approach to teach evidence-based medicine to medical technology students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1946-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student feedback quoted verbatim in the open-ended questions analysis; some students reported distractions at home, needing repeated readings, and suggested audio could supplement the eBook.

> "As there were too much attractions at home or in the dormitory, I found it difficult to focus on studying the eBook. The effect of self-study was moderate and I had to read it for more than 2 times to understand the content."
