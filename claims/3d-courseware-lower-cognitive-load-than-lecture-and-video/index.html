Evidence¶

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai 2013¶

Liang-Yuan Hsiung & Mu-Hui Lai. (2013). Improving Learning Results and Reducing Cognitive Load through 3D Courseware on Color Management and Inspection Instruction. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1016926

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Posteriori comparison of the 7-point cognitive load questionnaire in the quasi-experiment: Method B (M= 6.052) reported significantly lower cognitive load than Method A (M=8.258); null hypothesis (4) was rejected.

"Fourth, the cognitive load difference of Method B (M= 6.052) and Method A (M=8.258) is reached significant."
