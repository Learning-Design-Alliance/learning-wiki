Evidence¶

Cathy Yun 2022¶

Cathy Yun. (2022). Preparing Transitional Kindergarten to 3rd Grade Educators Through Teacher Residencies. Learning Policy Institute & EDPREPLAB. https://doi.org/10.54300/139.797

q2 · i? · design · r2

An LPI analysis of California TK enrollment projects that by 2025–26 more than 447,700 children will be eligible for TK, "with between 291,000 and 358,000 children enrolled, depending on the rate of uptake," driving the projected teacher need. The brief reports these as policy-analysis projections, not experimental findings.

"In turn, TK will require an additional 11,900 to 15,600 credentialed teachers with early childhood expertise."
