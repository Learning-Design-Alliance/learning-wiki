## Evidence

### BavaHarji 2014

BavaHarji, M., Alavi, Z. K., & Letchumanan, K. (2014). Captioned Instructional Video: Effects on Content Comprehension, Vocabulary Acquisition and Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n5p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A questionnaire administered to the 45 experimental participants found that "All 45 participants affirmed" that captions increased their vocabulary knowledge.

> "All 45 participants affirmed (77.8% Agreed, 22.2 % Strongly Agreed) that the provision of captions attributed to increase in vocabulary"

### BavaHarji 2014 (2)

BavaHarji, M., Alavi, Z. K., & Letchumanan, K. (2014). Captioned Instructional Video: Effects on Content Comprehension, Vocabulary Acquisition and Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 7(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n5p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

On the perception questionnaire, "28 (62.2%) participants disagreed" that reading captions caused them to miss the storyline, suggesting captions were not perceived as a distraction.

> "a larger proportion of the part icipants, i.e. 28 (62.2%) participants disagreed with statement 4 ( When I read the words in the captions, I miss the story line )"
