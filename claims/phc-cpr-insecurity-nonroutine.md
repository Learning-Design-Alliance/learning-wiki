`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study with 35 nursing professionals; 54.3% had participated in a CPR and 94.3% had prior basic life support training, yet "their statements reveal their insecurity in providing this type of care in the context of PHC".

> "Although most participants stated that they had already participated in some CPR (54.3%) and had received prior training in basic life support (94.3%), their statements reveal their insecurity in providing this type of care in the context of PHC."

### Ferreira Caroliny Suhet Xavier; Anacleto Gabriele de Araújo; Agonigi Regina Cavalcante; Corrêa Vanessa de Almeida Ferreira; Nunciaroni Andressa Teoli 2025 (2)

The authors attribute the insecurity to "the perception that this is not a routine situation at this point of care", supported by participant statements that the event does not happen frequently in their practice.
