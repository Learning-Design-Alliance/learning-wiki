## Evidence

### Anna Helga Jonsdottir 2013

Anna Helga Jonsdottir, Gunnar Stefansson. (2013). Enhanced Learning with Web-Assisted Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1310.4667](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1310.4667)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Four IRT models (1PL, common-discrimination, 2PL, 3PL) were fitted separately to each of the four question banks and compared by likelihood ratio test. For the hypotheses-concerning-means bank, model (4) led to "signiﬁcant better ﬁt"; for discrete distributions, continuous distributions, and contingency tables the simpler model (2) was used.

> "In that case model (4) lead to signiﬁcant better ﬁt than the more simple models and was therefore used to describe the data. For the last question bank, containing questions about contingency tables, the ﬁt was not improved using the more complex models."
