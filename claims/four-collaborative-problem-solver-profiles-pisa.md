## Evidence

### Sanna Järvelä 2023

Sanna Järvelä, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2023). Reflecting on what counts as collaboration: Reaching forward without losing what is behind. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 18, 457–464. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09415-y

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Ma, Zhang, Ni and Zhou, a latent profile analysis on a data set (N = 1,677) from the 2015 PISA Assessment. The profiles were based on "time-on-task, actions-on-task, and three specific CPS process skills" for students on a CPS task.

> "The results of latent profile analysis indicate four collaborative problem-solver profiles: Disengaged, Struggling, Adaptive, and Excellent."
