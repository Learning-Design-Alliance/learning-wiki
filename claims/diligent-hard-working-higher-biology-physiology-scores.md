## Evidence

### Hogh Annemarie 2021

Hogh Annemarie, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2021). Learning strategies and their correlation with academic success in biology and physiology examinations during the preclinical years of medical school. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0245851

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of examination results between learning-strategy groups in the longitudinal cohort: Mann-Whitney tests (biology) and unpaired t-tests (physiology) showed the diligent/hard-working group "obtained significantly better scores" than the relaxed/sociable group in both subjects; combined medians were 34 vs 35 (biology) and 48 vs 53.5 (physiology).

> "However, when grouping according to learning preferences–the relaxed and the sociable learners versus the diligent and hard-working ones–the latter obtained significantly better scores and that was true for the biology as well as the physiology examinations (Fig 4)."
