## Evidence

### Liu Zihan 2026

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 samples, 95% CI) on the 1,193-student survey: the indirect effect through class environment and performance approach goal was 0.02, 95% CI[0.01, 0.03], with performance approach goal positively predicting engagement.

> "class environment is significantly positively related to performance approach goal (β = 0.28, p < 0.001), and performance approach goal is significantly positively related to learning engagement (β = 0.16, p < 0.001). The indirect effect was 0.02, 95% CI[0.01, 0.03]."

### Liu Zihan 2026 (2)

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 samples, 95% CI) on the same survey: the indirect effect through performance avoidance goal was − 0.01, 95% CI[-0.02, -0.003], with performance avoidance goal negatively significantly related to learning engagement (β = -0.15, p < 0.001).

> "class environment is significantly positively related to performance avoidance goal (β = 0.12, p < 0.001), and performance avoidance goal is negatively significantly related to learning engagement (β = -0.15, p < 0.001). The indirect effect was − 0.01, 95% CI[-0.02, -0.003]."
