## Evidence

### Mohri P 2025

Mohri P, Koyluoglu YO, Seker ME, Sancak SN, Dikeç M, Mazlumoglu AA, Gurol Y, Sungur IC. (2025). Medical students’ perception of paid E-learning. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317340

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 119 medical students at a single Turkish medical school (25.5% of enrolled students). Respondents reported purposes of paid e-learning use: "87% of medical students reported using e-learning resources for school exams", with lower rates for personal interest, research, and board exams.

> "While all survey respondents reported using paid e-learning resources, 87% of medical students reported using e-learning resources for school exams, 32.7% for personal interest, 28.4% for scientific research, and 20.6% for board exams."
