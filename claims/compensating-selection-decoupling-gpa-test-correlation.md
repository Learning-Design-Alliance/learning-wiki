`q2 i?` In the incumbent populations, GPA–admission-test correlations were not significant (rTMS = −0.10, rHAM-Nat = −0.08), but were significant among all test-takers (rTMS = −0.28, rHAM-Nat = −0.28), a difference the authors attribute to compensating selection. [→ Jaehn M 2025](#jaehn-m-2025)

### Jaehn M 2025

Jaehn M, Hissbach J, Frickhoeffer M, Weppert D, Zimmerhofer A, Hampe W, Kadmon M, Becker N. (2025). Predictive validity of admission tests and educational attainment on preclinical academic performance – a multisite study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07974-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis comparing incumbents (admitted students) with all test-takers, with between-group tests of correlation differences (zTMS = 7.37, zHAM-Nat = 5.15, both p < .001). The article prints the correlations and z statistics shown; the incumbent correlations are reported as not significant.

> "In the population of both TMS and HAM-Nat incumbents, high-school GPA and admission test scores do not show a significant correlation (rTMS = − 0.10 and rHAM−Nat − 0.08). In the population of test-takers, however, a significant correlation was found (rTMS = − 0.28 and rHAM−Nat − 0.28)"
