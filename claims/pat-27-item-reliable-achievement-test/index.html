Evidence¶

Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen 2018¶

Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen (2018). The Effect of Mathematics Instruction through Computer Algebra Systems on the Academic Achievements of Secondary Education Students: Turkey Example. Journal of Education and e-Learning Research, 5(3): 165-173. https://doi.org/10.20448/journal.509.2018.53.165.173

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Test development with a pilot on 128 10th-grade students from three schools; a 32-item draft was reduced to 28 after expert review, and one non-discriminating blank-filling item was removed after 27% upper-lower group item analysis, yielding "27 open-ended questions as its final state".

"In consideration of the data obtained from the pilot study, Cronbach's Alpha internal consistency coefficient of the test has been calculated 0.942. Thereby, it has been decided that the PAT, which is consisted of 27 open-ended questions as its final state, is reliable to use in the research."
