## Evidence

### Whipple 1917

Whipple, G. M. (1917). Notes on the recall of nonsense syllables. Cornell University. https://psycnet.apa.org

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Single-case report: a former student, Mr. C. W. Hagen, Jr., chanced to visualize the classroom and the list returned to him; he wrote it out at the author's request. The article reports "only two minor errors" and correct order, with no effect size.

> "In auditory terms, therefore, the series aa reproduced some nine months after it was learned contains only two minor errors, ish for iss and lum for lorn; the order is correct."
