## Evidence

### de la Fuente Jesús 2020

de la Fuente Jesús, Paoloni Paola, Kauffman Douglas, Yilmaz Soylu Meryem, Sander Paul, Zapata Lucía. (2020). Big Five, Self-Regulation, and Coping Strategies as Predictors of Achievement Emotions in Undergraduate Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17103602

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis across the three emotional contexts (Tables 5–7). The article reports emotion-focused coping "positively predicted negative emotions (NE)", with F9 weighted heavily, while problem-focused coping predicted no emotions; the fourth hypothesis was only partially fulfilled.

> "Emotion-Focused coping strategies, particularly F9 (emotional venting and isolation) positively predicted negative emotions (NE), but not positive emotions (PE). However, problem-focused coping strategies, with special weight in Factor F2 (Search for help and family advice), did not predict emotions of any type, whether positive or negative."
