## Evidence

### Lea Nobbe 2024

Lea Nobbe, Jasmin Breitwieser, Daniel Biedermann & Garvin Brod. (2024). Smartphone-based study reminders can be a double-edged sword. npj Science of Learning 9:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00253-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory follow-up comparing Reminder Group no-reminder days (with mock reminder days mirrored in an odd-even split of the Control Group) using logistic mixed-effects models on log-file data. The article found Reminder Group students "signiﬁcantly less likely to study vocabulary than students in the Control Group" (OR = 0.45, 95% CI [0.22, 0.95]).

> "when looking at the days no reminders were presented, we found that students in the Reminder Group were signiﬁcantly less likely to study vocabulary than students in the Control Group (b = −0.79, 95% CI [−1.53, −0.05], χ2(1) = 4.23, p = 0.037, OR = 0.45, 95%CI [0.22, 0.95])."
