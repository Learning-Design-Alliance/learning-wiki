## Evidence

### Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi 2012

Karim Sadeghi & Negar Ahmadi. (2012). The Effect of Gloss Type and Mode on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 5(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n12p100

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' summary of the three t-test contrasts in the Discussion section: marginal gloss (M = 6.47), audio gloss (M = 6.73), and extended audio gloss (M = 7.87) each exceeded the control group (M = 5.27). No effect sizes are printed.

> "as far as reading comprehension is concerned, the three gloss groups outperformed the control group. Therefore, glosses, either traditional or multimedia, have significant effect on the comprehension of a written text."
