Cross-tabulation analysis of the 181-respondent survey (Table 4) linked deficiency reporting to country, experience, and qualification; the article reports the chi-square statistics above with no effect size. Mid-to-late career professionals (>10 years) were more likely to report gaps in invasive procedures.

> "Chi-square tests confirmed that these differences are statistically significant across all three factors: country (χ2=595.89, p<0.001), experience (χ2=599.67, p=0.038), and qualification (χ2=745.63, p=0.030)."
