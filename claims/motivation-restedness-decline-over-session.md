## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired t-tests showed significant reductions in both self-rated motivation and restedness from pre- to mid- to post-session. [→ Scherff Aline D. 2025](#scherff-aline-d-2025)

## Evidence

### Scherff Aline D. 2025

Scherff Aline D., Kääb Stefan, Fischer Martin R., Berndt Markus. (2025). EYE-ECG2: Addressing medical student feedback in an RCT with eye-tracking videos featuring cued retrospective reporting and modified learning sequences for ECG interpretation skills training. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001771

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-rated 0–100% scales administered pre-, mid-, and post-session in the ~2-hour online training showed all paired t-tests significant for declining motivation and restedness. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "restedness (pre=58.57%±23.49%, mid=44.67%±23.19%, post=37.92%±23.92%) and motivation (pre=74.70%±19.83%, mid=60.12%±22.21%, post=47.08%±26.15%) scores reduced significantly over the course of the session (all paired t-tests significant; motivation: pre-mid t(93)=-6.50, p<.04*10-8, pre-post t(91)=-9.95, p<.03*10-13, mid-post t(91)=-8.06, p<.03*10-10; restedness: pre-mid t(93)=-7.56, p<.03*10-9, pre-post t(91)=-8.16, p<.02*10-10, mid-post t(91)=-3.48, p<.001)."
