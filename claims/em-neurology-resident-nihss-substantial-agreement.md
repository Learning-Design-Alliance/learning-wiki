During the six-month pilot, EM and neurology residents scored each patient independently via REDCap surveys blinded until both were submitted. The article reports "substantial agreement between groups (84.4%, κ = 0.63)"; the matched-survey analysis is described as discussed in a separate paper.

> "Mean overall NIHSS scores were similar between EM and neurology residents, 6.6 (IQR = 2, 10) and 6.7 (IQR = 1, 10), (p < 0.001), respectively, with substantial agreement between groups (84.4%, κ = 0.63)."
