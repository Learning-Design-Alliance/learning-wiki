## Evidence

### Kunal Handa 2023

Kunal Handa, Margaret Clapper, Jessica Boyle, Rose E Wang, Diyi Yang, David S Yeager, Dorottya Demszky. (2023). "Mistakes Help Us Grow": Facilitating and Evaluating Growth Mindset Supportive Language in Classrooms. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.549)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey-based evaluation in which 174 teachers and 1,006 students rated four response types (ORIGINAL, EXPERT, MODEL 0-critique, MODEL 1-critique) on GMSL constructs, controlling for demographics and baseline motivational framework. "MODEL (1- CRITIQUE ) doing 24-85% better" than original utterances.

> "We find the model responses outperform the ORIGINAL teacher response by a large margin along all dimensions, with MODEL (1- CRITIQUE ) doing 24-85% better and MODEL (0- CRITIQUES ) doing 23-75% better."
