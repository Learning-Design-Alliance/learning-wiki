# Linguistic-level discourse analysis of students restating animation descriptions in their own words documents collaborative behavior and informs task redesign

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2010

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse. (2010). A prism of CSCL research. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-010-9087-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The editorial reports, citing Göran Karlsson's case study, that students were asked to put chemical-reaction descriptions from animations into their own words, and that grammatical-level analysis of their transformations documents the collaborative behavior itself. It is a qualitative case study with no statistics.

> "By documenting the processes that actually unfolded during the collaborative-learning interactions of the students with each other, with their task, and with the animations, the analysis provides a detailed description of the student collaborative behavior itself"
