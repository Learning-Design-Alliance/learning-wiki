## Evidence

### Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Gonnermann-Müller, J., Haase, J., Leins, N., Igel, M., Fackeldey, K., & Pokutta, S. (2026). FACET: Multi-Agent AI Supporting Teachers in Scaling Differentiated Learning for Diverse Students. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.24963/ijcai.2026/797](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2026/797)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive survey of 70 K–12 teachers rating the dyslexia-profile worksheet (W5) on additional items covering font readability, language simplification, and spacing/layout. Spacing/layout (M= 4.07) and language simplification (M= 3.94) exceeded the midpoint, indicating "partial alignment with accessibility guidelines." The authors identify this as "a specific improvement target for the generation pipeline."

> "However, font readability (M= 3.30) and overall suit- ability for reading difficulties (M= 3.46) fell near the mid- point, revealing that typographical requirements for dyslexia can be improved."
