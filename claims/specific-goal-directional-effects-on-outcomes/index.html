Related Claims¶

  • University instructors' performance goals split into four classes: appearance and normative components, each approach and avoidance. — related
  • University instructors' achievement goals differentially predict positive affect, attitudes towards help, and self-reported teaching quality, with effects invariant across status groups. — related
  • Pre-actional metacognitive, motivational, and positive-emotional components are positively associated with regulatory behavior in the actional phase, while anger and anxiety are negatively associated — related
  • Mastery-approach goals are positively related to time management, while performance-approach relations are inconsistent and performance-avoidance goals show no association — related
  • Work avoidance and relational goals form goal classes distinct from all other achievement goal classes of university instructors. — related
