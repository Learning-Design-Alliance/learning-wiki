## Evidence

### Sørensen Stine Maya Dreier 2016

Sørensen Stine Maya Dreier, Mahmood Oria, Konge Lars, Thinggaard Ebbe, Bjerrum Flemming. (2016). Laser visual guidance versus two-dimensional vision in laparoscopy: a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-4937-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized superiority trial of 32 surgical novices completing the TABLT test after randomization to LVG or 2D vision. Mean time was 1288 s (95% CI 1188–1388) versus 1354 s (95% CI 1190–1518) (p = 0.45); angular path 24,049° versus 26,014° (p = 0.42); path length 4560 cm versus 5062 cm (p = 0.26). "We found no significant differences between the LVG tool and 2D vision in regard to motor skill performance parameters".
