# Large positive effect on not-standardized language/comprehension measures (mean ES = 1.03) rests on only six studies, so confidence is limited.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The mean effect on not-standardized language/comprehension measures was large (1.03), but only six studies contributed and no moderator analyses were possible. [→ Wanzek 2016](#wanzek-2016)

## Evidence

### Wanzek 2016

Wanzek, J., Vaughn, S., Scammacca, N., Gatlin, B., Walker, M. A., & Capin, P. (2016). Meta-analyses of the effects of tier 2 type reading interventions in grades K-3. Educational Psychology Review, 28, 551-576. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579894

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis restricted to six studies with not-standardized language/comprehension outcomes. The article reports "a mean effect size estimate was 1.03," "a large positive effect," with a wide confidence interval of 0.52 to 1.53.

> "Only six studies provided effect sizes for not-standardized measures of language and comprehension. The mean effect size estimate was 1.03 (p < 0.001; 95% CI = 0.52, 1.53), indicating a large positive effect of intervention on students' language/comprehension ability."
