`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann Whitney subgroup comparisons on post-test scores (Table 3): medium achievement p=0.000<0.05 (experimental M=7.67 vs control M=6.27) and low achievement p=0.002<0.05 (M=6.08 vs 4.58); high achievement p=0.0966>0.05.
