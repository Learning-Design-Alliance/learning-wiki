# In loss maximization, the pattern reverses: performance is more consistent following losses than wins

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Under loss maximization against repetition opponents, actions following losses have a significantly stronger correlation with win rate than actions following wins (z = 3.895, p < 0.001). [→ Yajing Zhang 2023](#yajing-zhang-2023)
`q2 i?` Under loss maximization against alternation opponents, actions following losses again have a significantly stronger correlation than actions following wins (z = 4.057, p < 0.001). [→ Yajing Zhang 2023 (2)](#yajing-zhang-2023-2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`
