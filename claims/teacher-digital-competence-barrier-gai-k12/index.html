Marzano 2025¶

Narrative review section attributing this finding to Alferez-Pastor (2023): teachers "believe they have some knowledge of ICT but do not know how to use them properly". The review also cites a lack of clear frameworks on how AI should be taught (Ng et al.; Su et al.; Van Brummelen et al.).
