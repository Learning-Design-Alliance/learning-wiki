# Researchers conducting MANOVA rarely interpret its multivariate results, instead drawing substantive conclusions from multiple univariate analyses in 84% of studies

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In 84% (66/79) of MANOVA studies, researchers never used the MANOVA results to explain effects of the grouping variables, interpreting multiple univariate analyses instead. [→ Keselman 1998](#keselman-1998)

## Evidence

### Keselman 1998

Keselman, H.J., Huberty, C.J., Lix, L.M., Olejnik, S., Cribbie, R.A., Donahue, B., Kowalchuk, R.K., Lowman, L.L., Petoskey, M.D., Keselman, J.C., & Levin, J.R. (1998). Statistical Practises of Educational Researchers: An Analysis of Their ANOVA, MANOVA and ANCOVA Analyses. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170601

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Content analysis of 79 MANOVA articles from educational journals. The review reports "In an overwhelming 84% (66/79) of the studies , researchers never used the results of the MANOVA(s) to explain effects of the grouping variable(s)." Substantive conclusions were drawn from multiple univariate results rather than from the MANOVA.
