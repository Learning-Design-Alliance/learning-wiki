Evidence¶

Thomas Daniel Ullmann 2019¶

Thomas Daniel Ullmann. (2019). Automated Analysis of Reflection in Writing: Validating Machine Learning Approaches. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2019) 29:217–257. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00174-2

q3 · i? · design · r2

Evaluation of machine learning models trained on a random 80% of labeled sentences and tested on the held-out 20%, in a standardized process applied to all eight category data sets; the article reports "Cohen’s κ ranges between .53 and .85" across categories.

"Overall, the standardized evaluation shows that five out of eight categories can be detected automatically with substantial or almost perfect reliability, while the other three categories can be detected with moderate reliability (Cohen’s κ ranges between .53 and .85)."
