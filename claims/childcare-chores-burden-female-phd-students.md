## Evidence

### Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir 2020

Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir. (2020). Ph.D. in physics as a hurdle race, and the “glass hurdles” for women. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey question "Who is responsible for most childcare work?" (Fig. 2). The article adds that not even one female student said her spouse was the main caregiver; percentages are descriptive.

> "Of the male students, 67% responded that they and their spouses carry the load equally, and only 5% responded that the responsibility lies mainly on themselves. In contrast, of the female students, 43% responded that they and their spouses carry the load equally, and the other 57% responded that the responsibility lies mainly on themselves."

### Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir 2020 (2)

Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir. (2020). Ph.D. in physics as a hurdle race, and the “glass hurdles” for women. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey question on household chores (Fig. 3), showing a similar pattern to childcare; the article notes no female student reported her spouse as responsible for most chores.

> "We observe again an ideology (and probably practice) of equality between the couple, with 67% of female students and 64% of male students stating an equal sharing of the household responsibilities."
