`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Episode analysis of group B on electrostatics: Chris's half-joking account that the cloth could "hold electrons" drew laughter; the authors read the joking frame's ambiguity as "protective for a speaker who ventures an idea the group might judge unfavorably".

> "Such ambiguity can be protective for a speaker who ventures an idea the group might judge unfavorably; Chris may pose the idea as a joke in order to incur less risk in sharing it."
