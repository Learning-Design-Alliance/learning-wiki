Related Claims¶

  • Within the cognitive domain, flipped classrooms show the largest effects on understanding and achievement and a negative effect on evaluation — related
  • Science-related subject areas (mathematics, science, medical science) show larger effects than programming technology and other fields — reports the opposite
  • LLM teaching effectiveness varies substantially across subject domains, strongest where knowledge points apply directly and weakest in physics and chemistry — related
  • Stratified teaching positively affects all seven studied subjects, with larger effects in English, physics, and geography than in chemistry, biology, and mathematics — related
  • Discipline significantly moderates the effects of educational robotics on STEM learning, with larger effects in technology and cross-disciplinary contexts than in science and mathematics — reports the opposite
  • No evidence of publication bias was found in the flipped classroom literature, though dissertations showed a larger (non-significantly different) effect size than journal articles — related
  • The flipped classroom effect on knowledge scores is larger in basic science subjects than in clinical or public health subjects. — related
  • No statistically significant difference in calculus achievement was found among students by major (medicine, applied medical science, engineering, computer science, science) — related
