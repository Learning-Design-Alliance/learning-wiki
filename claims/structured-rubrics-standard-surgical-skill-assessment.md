Narrative review of the survey articles reports that structured rubrics including OSATS, GEARS, and GOALS are "validated to reliably distinguish between different levels of surgical expertise" and serve as the standard for trainee evaluation and for validating curricula and new methods.

> "Because these rubrics have been validated to reliably distinguish between different levels of surgical expertise,14 they are widely regarded as the standard approaches to surgical skill assessment."
