Ozturk, N. (2023). Clash of Traditional and Contemporary Educational Philosophies in Pippi Longstocking. EJER Congress 2023 International Eurasian Educational Research Congress Conference Proceedings, Ani Publishing, pp. 366–382. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638455

Finding from the interpretive content analysis, summarized in Table 3 across book chapters pairing Pippi with the policemen, the teacher, Tommy and Annika, and Rosenblom, each coded as contemporary philosophies "vs. Essentialism".

"While Pippi represented contemporary educational philosophies via her reactions, behaviors, and responses, the society including her friends (Tommy and Annika), the teacher, the policemen, and Rosenblom represented an Essentialist understanding of education"
