Griffith 2025¶

Griffith, M., & Burns, D. (2025). Funding student needs: A review of state funding policies for English learners and students from low-income backgrounds. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/471.440

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's literature review cites a 2015 study of school finance reforms finding spending effects "were higher for students who were from low-income backgrounds," and a 2023 LCFF study reporting causal increases in assessment outcomes and graduation rates.

"Likewise, a 2023 study of California’s Local Control Funding Formula found that increased funding was causally related to increases in outcomes on state assessments and graduation rates, especially for students from low-income backgrounds and particularly for those districts receiving the additional “concentration grant” funding allocated to districts with large proportions of such students."
