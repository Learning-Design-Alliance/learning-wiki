### Once Early-Reading Program: Evidence Rating Summary 2025

Once Early-Reading Program: Evidence Rating Summary. (2025). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/once-early-reading-program/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A randomized controlled trial conducted in 13 schools in a large urban East Coast district during the 2022-2023 school year tested Once on kindergarten and first grade students below grade-level benchmarks. No significant effect on DIBELS; the printed effect size is +0.05. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Students in the program group received an average of 42 sessions, well below the program's intended dosage, and the study did not find a significant effect on end-of-year DIBELS scores (effect size +0.05)."
