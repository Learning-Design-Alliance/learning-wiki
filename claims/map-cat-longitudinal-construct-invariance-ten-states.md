# The longitudinal achievement constructs of MAP reading and mathematics CATs remain invariant across five time points in ten states (except Indiana mathematics)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` With repeated measures, the construct of both MAP reading and mathematics remained consistent at different points in time, supporting configural, weak and strong invariance across states except Indiana mathematics. [→ Wang 2013](#wang-2013)
`q2 i?` All ΔCFI values between nested invariance models were less than 0.01 in both tests, indicating constrained parameters were invariant across time. [→ Wang 2013 (2)](#wang-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Wang 2013

Wang, S., Jiao, H. and Zhang, L. (2013) 'Validation of longitudinal achievement constructs of vertically scaled computerised adaptive tests: a multiple-indicator, latent-growth modelling approach', Int. J. Quantitative Research in Education, Vol. 1, No. 4, pp.383–407. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/validation-of-longitudinal-achievement-constructs-of-vertically-scaled-computerised-adaptive-tests-a-multiple-indicator-latent-growth-modelling-approach/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MLGM invariance analyses of MAP reading and mathematics data from a five-wave panel (Spring 2009 to Spring 2011, grades 5–7) in ten states. The study reports "the construct of both reading and mathematics remained consistent at different points in time", supporting CI, WI and SI except Indiana mathematics.

> "The evidence collected in the study shows that with repeated measures, the construct of both reading and mathematics remained consistent at different points in time, which supports the internal structure of MAP design for intended purposes."

### Wang 2013 (2)

Wang, S., Jiao, H. and Zhang, L. (2013) 'Validation of longitudinal achievement constructs of vertically scaled computerised adaptive tests: a multiple-indicator, latent-growth modelling approach', Int. J. Quantitative Research in Education, Vol. 1, No. 4, pp.383–407. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/validation-of-longitudinal-achievement-constructs-of-vertically-scaled-computerised-adaptive-tests-a-multiple-indicator-latent-growth-modelling-approach/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nested linear MLGM model comparisons for measurement invariance across five waves, evaluated with Δχ2 and the Cheung and Rensvold ΔCFI criterion. The article reports "all ΔCFIs less than 0.01 in both tests", so constrained parameters were judged invariant; no effect size is printed.

> "The value of all ΔCFIs less than 0.01 in both tests indicates that constrained parameters are invariant across time."
