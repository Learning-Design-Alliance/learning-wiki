## Evidence

### Bruno De Lièvre 2006

Bruno De Lièvre, Christian Depover & Pierre Dillenbourg. (2006). The relationship between tutoring mode and learners' use of help tools in distance education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-005-6076-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Regression analysis for the pro-active HT condition. For metacognitive help the two variables "accounts for only 5% of the global explanation ( R2 of 0.053)"; for lexical help R2=0.219, described as high, with promptings weighted more (Beta=0.413) than information items (Beta=0.164).

> "the combination of these two variables accounts for only 5% of the global explanation ( R2 of 0.053) of the use of the metacognitive help."
