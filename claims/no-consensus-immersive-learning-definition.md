Review of definitions adopted in primary studies (RQ1). The review reports that some authors define iL through learning outcomes in educational virtual environments or constructivist engagement phases, while others define it via immersive virtual field trips or transfer to real-world hands-on activities.

> "Through the ﬁndings, we realized that there is no consensus about what is iL. Clearly there is a separation between pedagogical and technological aspects."
