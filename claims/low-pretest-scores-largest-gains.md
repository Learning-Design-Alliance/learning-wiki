## Evidence

### O'Donovan James 2018

O'Donovan James, Kabali Kenneth, Taylor Celia, Chukhina Margarita, Kading Jacqueline C., Fuld Jonathan, O'Neil Edward. (2018). The use of low-cost Android tablets to train community health workers in Mukono, Uganda, in the recognition, treatment and prevention of pneumonia in children under five: a pilot randomised controlled trial. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0315-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis of pooled pilot RCT MCQ data. The authors attribute the negative correlation to "headroom" for improvement; r = −0.46 is a large effect by the correlation code. The authors' headroom interpretation is their own, not a tested result.

> "Pearson's correlation coefficient between pre-training MCQ scores and MCQ change scores was − 0.46 (p < 0.001), suggesting that CHWs with the lowest pre-training scores improved the most"
