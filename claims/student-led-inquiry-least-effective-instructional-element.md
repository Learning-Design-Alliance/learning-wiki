## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Using PISA 2015 results, student-led inquiry-based investigation was identified as the instructional element least associated with science achievement, with investigation steps showing negative associations. [→ Lin Zhang 2022](#lin-zhang-2022)

## Evidence

### Lin Zhang 2022

Lin Zhang, Paul A. Kirschner, William W. Cobern, John Sweller. (2022). There is an Evidence Crisis in Science Educational Policy. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09646-1

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports, citing Forbes et al. (2020), an analysis of PISA 2015 instructional factors in which student-led inquiry dimensions were least associated with achievement. The commentary attributes this finding to that study; no effect size is printed.

> "Forbes et al. (2020) noted that "highly student-driven dimensions of inquiry, particularly procedural activities associated with investigation, are least frequently associated with high levels of student science achievement""
