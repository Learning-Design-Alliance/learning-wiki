## Evidence

### Chu 2022

Chu, H., Tran, T., & Hamburger, L. (2022). Redefining approaches for engaging English Learners with mathematical ideas. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625747

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive analysis of statistics definitions in the textbook scan. Go Math! defines cluster as "a set of closely grouped data" and outlier as "a data point that is very different from the rest of the data in the set" (p. 434), in advance of any examples. The brief reports no clear criteria are offered and none are later formalized.

> "Although these definitions may be broad enough to invite students to initially investigate the ideas, they do not offer students clear criteria for deciding whether data are clustered or outliers, and more formal criteria are never developed in the textbooks reviewed for this brief."
