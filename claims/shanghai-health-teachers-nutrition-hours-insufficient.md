Cross-sectional WeChat/paper questionnaire survey of 904 health teachers from 823 Shanghai K-12 schools (2016–2017), reported in Table 2. The survey found "722 (79.9%) teachers had the experience of teaching nutrition" with most under one hour per year.

> "A total of 722 (79.9%) teachers had the experience of teaching nutrition in their employed school, but most of them (73.7%) spent less than one hour per school year to teach nutrition courses."
