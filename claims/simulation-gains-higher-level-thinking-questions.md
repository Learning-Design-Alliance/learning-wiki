### Luo 2016

Luo, W., Pelletier, J., Duffin, K., Ormand, C., Hung, W., Shernoff, D., Zhai, X., Iverson, E., Whalley, K., Gallaher, C., and Furness, W. (2016). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-question analysis of percentage answering correctly, pretest to posttest, in the same quasiexperiment (Fig. 4). The article reports growth of "15.0%" versus "2.6%" for the five application questions and interprets this as deeper understanding of geological processes.

> "The mean of the percentage growth in correct answers between the pretest and the posttest in the simulation group was 15.0%, whereas the growth for the paper group was only 2.6% (see Fig. 4)."
