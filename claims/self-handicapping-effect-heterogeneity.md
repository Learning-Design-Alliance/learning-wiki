## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Between-study heterogeneity is high (I² = 70.32%), indicating true effects differ across studies. [→ Schwinger 2014](#schwinger-2014)

## Evidence

### Schwinger 2014

Schwinger, M., Wirthwein, L., Lemmer, G., & Steinmayr, R. (2014, February 10). Academic Self-Handicapping and Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0035832

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Heterogeneity analysis within the same random-effects meta-analysis of 49 effect sizes. The authors report "Q ⫽ 156.50, df ⫽ 48, p ⬍ .001" and estimate I² = 70.32%, classified as high, motivating the moderator analyses.

> "Cochran's Q-Test suggested heterogeneity (Q ⫽ 156.50, df ⫽ 48, p ⬍ .001), which means that the individual observed effects differed more than would be expected if sampling error were the only source of variability."
