# Adding reciprocal peer tutoring with audio prompting to incidental exposure produced stronger vocabulary gains than incidental exposure alone for fourth-grade struggling readers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean correct selections were greater for the peer tutoring with audio prompting condition (M = 4.1 of 6) than for incidental learning alone (M = 1.2 of 6). [→ Mackiewicz 2011](#mackiewicz-2011)

## Evidence

### Mackiewicz 2011

Mackiewicz, S. M., Wood, C. L., Cooke, N. L., & Mazzotti, V. L. (2011). Effects of Peer Tutoring With Audio Prompting on Vocabulary Acquisition for Struggling Readers. Remedial and Special Education, 32(4), 345–354. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932510362507

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simultaneous treatments design with eight fourth-grade struggling readers; posttest means by condition are reported in the Discussion, showing "greater for peer tutoring with audio prompting" (M = 4.1) versus incidental learning (M = 1.2). No effect size is printed.

> "The mean performance for all participants was greater for peer tutoring with audio prompting (M = 4.1 correct selections of 6 total) compared to incidental learning (M = 1.2 correct selections of 6 total)."
