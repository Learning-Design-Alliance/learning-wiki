Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two 2018¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two. (2018). Digital Promise and Maker Education Initiative. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/f800e8da-813a-4de3-a10d-fd50b74646d0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Ranking of seven resource types by 2017 survey respondents (n=203, Figure 10), compared with 2016 results (n=130, Figure 9). Ideas for projects and lessons were identified as important in previous research around maker educator communities.

"The biggest identified need continues to be Project Ideas and Guides, with the related need for student project documentation resources and guides trailing close behind"

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two 2018 (2)¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two. (2018). Digital Promise and Maker Education Initiative. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/f800e8da-813a-4de3-a10d-fd50b74646d0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Comparison of ranked resource needs between the 2016 and 2017 surveys, where the most identifiable development was a decrease in demand for professional development. The authors suggest "more Maker Champions have increased access to the learning experiences they need than in the past", but caution providers should not infer they are no longer needed.

"This would seem to suggest that more Maker Champions have increased access to the learning experiences they need than in the past"
