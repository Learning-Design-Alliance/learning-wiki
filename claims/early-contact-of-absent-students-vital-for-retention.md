### MM Casey and MW Kille 2011

MM Casey and MW Kille. (2011). Effective Administration of a Large Undergraduate Physics Class: From Enrolment to Assessment Feedback. Journal of Science Education / Revista de Educación en Ciencias 12 (2). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1102.2581](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1102.2581)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Institutional practice report from the Results section. Improved data accuracy and turnaround let absenteeism be addressed early in the semester rather than at the end; the authors call early contact vital to retention and progression.

> "In the case of the workshop-tests and laboratory classes, students can be contacted within 30-minutes of the end of a workshop-test or the start of a laboratory. Early contact of absent students has proved vital in terms of improving student retention and progression statistics."
