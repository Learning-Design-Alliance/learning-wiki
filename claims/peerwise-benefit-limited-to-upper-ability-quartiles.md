## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Quartile analyses showed significant prediction from mean answer in Q1 and Q2, but the models for lower-scoring quartiles Q3 and Q4 were not significant. [→ Guilding C 2021](#guilding-c-2021)

## Evidence

### Guilding C 2021

Guilding C, Pye RE, Butler S, Atkinson M, Field E. (2021). Answering questions in a co-created formative exam question bank improves summative exam performance, while students perceive benefits from answering, authoring, and peer discussion: A mixed methods analysis of PeerWise. Pharmacol Res Perspect. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.833

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quartile-split regressions on the 2015 cohort with Stage 3 removed from Model 1. The article reports the lower-quartile models "were not significant", with no significant prediction from either measure. No effect size printed.

> "The students scoring higher at Stage 3 (Q1 and Q2) showed the same pattern as the whole cohort (Q1: F(2, 119) = 3.566, p = .031; Q2: F(2, 101) = 5.269, p = .007), with a significant prediction from mean answer but not mean reputation of Stage 4 results."
