## Evidence

### Schneider 2013

Schneider, M., & Hardy, I. (2013). Profiles of Inconsistent Knowledge in Children's Pathways of Conceptual Change. Developmental Psychology, 49(9). https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030976

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square tests of the association between children's profiles at consecutive measurement points in the 161-child sample. The T1–T2 association was significant with Cramer's V = .526; T2–T3 gave V = .645 and T1–T3, one year apart, V = .347, all p < .001; the article prints no standardized effect size for this association.

> "A chi-square test indicated a highly significant relation between the two variables, ␹2(8) ⫽ 88.961, p ⬍ .001, Cramer's V ⫽ .526."
