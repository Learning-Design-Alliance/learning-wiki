Voyager Universal Literacy System® — WWC Intervention Report. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/538

`q2 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two quasi-experimental studies (Frechtling et al., 2006; Hecht, 2003) with over 600 kindergarten students. The WWC-printed domain average effect size was 0.28 (small), with an average improvement index of +11 percentile points, described as "positive and large enough to be considered substantively important" for one study.

> "The average improvement index for alphabetics is +11 percentile points across the two studies, with a range of –8 to +27 percentile points across findings."
