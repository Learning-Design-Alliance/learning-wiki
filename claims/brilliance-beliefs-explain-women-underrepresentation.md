The review attributes this to Leslie et al. and related work (refs 4-6): expectations of brilliance, a trait stereotypically associated with men, are argued to explain women's underrepresentation in fields assumed to require innate talent. No effect sizes are printed.

> "they argue that raw intellectual talent, which is associated more with men than women [4], helps to explain the lack of women in physics, mathematics and philosophy since these disciplines are assumed to require such skills."
