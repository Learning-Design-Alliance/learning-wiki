Evidence¶

Lea Waters 2015¶

Lea Waters, Adam Barsky, Amanda Ridd, Kelly Allen. (2015). Contemplative Education: A Systematic, Evidence-Based Review of the effect of Meditation Interventions in Schools. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-014-9258-2

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The review's comparison of percentages of significant effects across meditation types for well-being outcomes, based on small numbers of effects (n refers to number of effects per group). The abstract cautions this "may be to do with the settings and programme delivery rather than the technique itself."

"The evidence to date shows that TM has more consistent significant effects on well-being (83 % of effects were significant; n=65) in comparison to mindfulness (44 %; n=9) and other meditation techniques (42 %; n=7)."
