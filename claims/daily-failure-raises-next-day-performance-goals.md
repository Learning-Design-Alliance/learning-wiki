# Daily academic failure predicts higher performance goals but not mastery goals the next morning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Academic failure predicted higher performance-avoidance (β = .069) and performance-approach (β = .072) goals the next morning, but not mastery goals, supporting Hypothesis 4; these small standardized coefficients were not robust in all sensitivity analyses. [→ Neubauer 2022](#neubauer-2022)

## Evidence

### Neubauer 2022

Neubauer, A. B., Schmidt, A., Schmiedek, F., & Dirk, J. (2022). Dynamic Reciprocal Relations of Achievement Goals with Daily Experiences of Academic Success and Failure: An Ambulatory Assessment Study. https://osf.io/gfvja/?view_only=b87fe179cf134471948726cb0e482a80

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Across-day effects from the DSEM of 108 Grade 5 students. Failure predicted next-morning performance goals only; no mastery-goal effect was reported. The printed standardized coefficients are small (below d = 0.2 equivalent).

> "Further, and supporting Hypothesis 4, academic failure was associated with higher performance-avoidance goals, β = .069 [.013, .123], and performance-approach goals, β = .072 [.014, .127], in the next morning."

### Neubauer 2022 (2)

Neubauer, A. B., Schmidt, A., Schmiedek, F., & Dirk, J. (2022). Dynamic Reciprocal Relations of Achievement Goals with Daily Experiences of Academic Success and Failure: An Ambulatory Assessment Study. https://osf.io/gfvja/?view_only=b87fe179cf134471948726cb0e482a80

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alternative Model 2 sensitivity analysis: both failure-to-performance-goal cross-lagged effects lost significance when all random effects were allowed to covary.

> "Academic failure now no longer significantly predicted next day's performance-approach goals, β = .049 [-.008, .106], or performance-avoidance goals, β = .040 [-.018, .097]."
