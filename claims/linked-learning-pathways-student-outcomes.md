### Hoachlander 2020

Hoachlander, G. (2020). Building a system of college and career pathways in New Mexico. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/new-mexico-education

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The same SRI evaluation found low-achieving students "made significantly better academic progress" in pathways, pathway students performed better on English language arts proficiency assessments, and African American pathway students' 4-year college-going rate was "12 percentage points higher than peers not participating in pathways."

> "Furthermore, students who had low achievement scores in earlier grades made significantly better academic progress when they participated in pathways in high school. Pathway students also performed better on assessments of proficiency in English language arts. Lastly, the 4-year college-going rate for African Americans in these pathways was 12 percentage points higher than peers not participating in pathways."
