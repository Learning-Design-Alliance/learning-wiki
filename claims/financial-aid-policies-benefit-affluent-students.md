## Evidence

### Mamie Lynch 2011

Mamie Lynch, Jennifer Engle, and José L. Cruz. (2011). Priced Out: How the Wrong Financial-Aid Policies Hurt Low-Income Students. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED520199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The report synthesizes prior analyses of 2007 institutional grant spending (nearly $15 billion at four-year public and private nonprofits), showing "Private nonprofit colleges and universities spent almost twice as much on students from families in the top quintile." It also reports non-need state grants grew at triple the rate of need-based grants over 10 years, and that 61 and 91 percent of tuition tax credit and deduction beneficiaries were middle- and upper-income families.

> "Private nonprofit colleges and universities spent almost twice as much on students from families in the top quintile of family income as they did on those in the bottom quintile. Even public institutions spent roughly the same amount on students from the wealthiest families as they did on those from low-income backgrounds."
