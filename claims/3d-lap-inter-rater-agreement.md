## Evidence

### Laverty JT 2016

Laverty JT, Underwood SM, Matz RL, Posey LA, Carmel JH, Caballero MD, Fata-Hartley CL, Ebert-May D, Jardeleza SE, Cooper MM. (2016). Characterizing College Science Assessments: The Three-Dimensional Learning Assessment Protocol. PLoS ONE 11(9): e0162333. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0162333

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis in which researchers from the development team coded two exams per discipline (63 biology, 48 chemistry, 31 physics tasks, per Table 4) and percent agreement was used to evaluate inter-rater reliability, with a lower limit set at 75%. "The percent agreement was calculated for each pair of coders and those values were averaged" for disciplines with more than two coders. Agreement was defined at the level of whether a task elicited any practice, concept, or core idea.

> "For biology and chemistry (the disciplines with >2 coders), the percent agreement was calculated for each pair of coders and those values were averaged."
