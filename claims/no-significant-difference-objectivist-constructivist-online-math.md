## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adult distance learners taught by objectivist-based and constructivist-based teaching strategies did not differ significantly in mathematics achievement scores. [→ DePriter 2012](#depriter-2012)

## Evidence

### DePriter 2012

DePriter, T. (2012). An Investigation of Teaching Strategy in the Distance Learning Mathematics Classroom. Journal of Educators Online, 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1020037

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA with pretest as covariate compared adjusted posttest means of the two intact sections (n = 33, K = 2). The article reports the observed F value of .823 did not exceed the critical value of 4.17, so "the null hypothesis of no difference between the “adjusted” mean is not rejected".
