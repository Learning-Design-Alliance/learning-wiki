### Kidd 2013

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 120 first-graders (from 443 screened) assigned to patterning, reading, mathematics, or social studies instruction; May posttesting by blind school psychologists. As printed, the patterning group "performed significantly better than each of the other groups" on both patterning tests (Table 1 means: posttest 27.68 vs 12.60–15.61; far generalization 7.90 vs 4.37–4.58).
