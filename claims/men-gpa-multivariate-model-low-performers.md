`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Stepwise multiple regression analysis for GPA in 65 men. Table 4 reports, for example, self-motivation with p = 0.001 and having a romantic partner with p = 0.003; the model R was 0.603.

> "In the multivariate analysis for GPA in men, we found that the most associated variables were the admission exam test, self-motivation, neuroticism, having a romantic partner, having children (negatively) and weekly physical activity hours (negatively), shown in Table 4, which showed a moderate multivariate r = 0.603"
