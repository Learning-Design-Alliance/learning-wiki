Related Claims¶

  • 3D virtual-reality lecture was statistically non-inferior to 2D lecture on knowledge and rated better on spatial understanding and enjoyment (Kockro et al.) — related
  • In one meta-analysis, modality-effect studies using virtual reality head-mounted displays show larger effects than studies using computer screens or audio-tapes — related
  • Immersion principle: people do not necessarily learn better in 3D immersive virtual reality than with a corresponding 2D desktop presentation (d = -0.10 across 9 tests) — reports the opposite
  • Anatomy Glove Learning System improved hand-anatomy knowledge in both groups, with 3D-group self-confidence increasing over time while 2D-group confidence declined — related
