Boet Sylvain 2014¶

Boet Sylvain, Bould M. Dylan, Fung Lillia, Qosa Haytham, Perrier Laure, Tavares Walter, Reeves Scott, Tricco Andrea C. (2014). Transfer of learning and patient outcome in simulated crisis resource management: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Anaesthesia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4028539/

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review synthesis of five studies measuring patient outcomes (Kirkpatrick Level 4), reporting that "Only one of these studies found that simulation-based CRM training made a clearly significant impact on patient mortality." Surrogate measures were grouped into efficiency of patient care, complications, length of stay, and survival/mortality.
