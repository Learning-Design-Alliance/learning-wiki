Evidence¶

Naderi 2018¶

Naderi, S., & Akrami, A. (2018). EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension Development through MALL: Telegram Groups in Focus. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 339-350. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11223a

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of 55 experimental and 48 control intermediate university students; post-test distributions were non-normal so a Mann-Whitney U test was run. The article reports "significant difference" and states "The effect size was .34", described as a moderate association.

"there was significant difference [U=791.500, Z= -3.509, p=.000(two-tailed)] between the mean scores of the experimental and control groups' reading comprehension ability at the post-test scores."
