## Evidence

### Marienko 2026

Marienko, M. V., Markova, O. M., & Semerikov, S. O. (2026). AI literacy in secondary education: framework, assessment, and professional development in the Ukrainian context. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 10, 100605. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100605

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post evaluation (Study 3) of the Prometheus MOOC masterclass; paired-samples t-tests on the 36 completers. "The intervention produced a 24% over­ all increase in AI utilization competence"; Table 5 reports the overall composite at +24 [19.1, 28.9], t(35) = 10.00, p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.67, with all effects large (d > 0.80).

> "Pre-post assessment data were available for 36 participants who completed both assessments. The intervention produced a 24% over­ all increase in AI utilization competence."
