# ChatGPT-5, Gemini 3.0 Pro, and Claude Sonnet 4.5 achieved significantly higher overall scores than mean ophthalmology trainee performance, while ChatGPT-4o did not

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ChatGPT-5 (92.59%), Gemini 3.0 Pro (90.12%), and Claude Sonnet 4.5 (90.12%) scored higher overall than residents (82.30 ± 8.22%) and fellows (86.42 ± 1.23%); the three models differed significantly from all trainees (p = 0.031 each), whereas ChatGPT-4o did not. ## Evidence

### Kim Seonghwan 2026

Kim Seonghwan, Ji Man, Kim Chung Young, Yun Young In, Lee Gahye, Yoon Chang Ho, Kim Mee Kum. (2026). Comparative performance of large language models in answering cornea and cataract surgery questions for resident training. BMC Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12886-026-04767-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparison of LLM scores with six human trainees (three residents, three fellows). The article reports "ChatGPT-5 vs. all trainees, p = 0.031; Gemini 3.0 Pro vs. all trainees, p = 0.031; Claude Sonnet 4.5 vs. all trainees, p = 0.031", while ChatGPT-4o was not significant (p = 0.125).

> "ChatGPT-5 (92.59%), Gemini 3.0 Pro (90.12%), and Claude Sonnet 4.5 (90.12%) achieved higher overall accuracy than both residents (82.30 ± 8.22%) and fellows (86.42 ± 1.23%), whereas ChatGPT-4o (86.42%) demonstrated performance comparable to fellows and superior to residents (Table 1)."
