### Karjanto 2022

Karjanto, N.; Acelajado, M.J. (2022). Sustainable Learning, Cognitive Gains, and Improved Attitudes in College Algebra Flipped Classrooms. Sustainability 2022, 14, 12500. https://doi.org/10.3390/su141912500

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative thematic analysis of unedited student journal comments. Positive comments included enjoying switching between videos, slides, and textbooks and repeated viewing; negative comments included "I felt helpless whenever I had questions, but nobody was there to answer immediately" and internet access failures.

> "From these comments, we observe that there is a mixed reaction from the students regarding their experience with the FCP."
