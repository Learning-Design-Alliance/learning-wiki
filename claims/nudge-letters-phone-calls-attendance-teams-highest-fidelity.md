## Evidence

### Lavigne 2021

Lavigne, H., Caven, M., Bock, G., Zhang, X., & Braham, E. (2021). Exploring implementation of attendance supports to reduce chronic absenteeism in the Providence Public School District (REL 2021–099). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Northeast & Islands. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/exploring-implementation-attendance-supports-reduce-chronic-absenteeism-providence-public-school

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of PPSD-coded fidelity ratings for six attendance supports across 39 schools in 2018/19 (figure 1). The report prints 85 percent for nudge letters and 77 percent each for phone calls and attendance teams, in line with the district's attendance policy requirements.

> "About 85 percent of schools in the analytic sample used nudge letters with moderate or high fidelity (figure 1). Phone calls and attendance teams were also frequently used with moderate or high fidelity (by 77 percent of schools)."

### Lavigne 2021 (2)

Lavigne, H., Caven, M., Bock, G., Zhang, X., & Braham, E. (2021). Exploring implementation of attendance supports to reduce chronic absenteeism in the Providence Public School District (REL 2021–099). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Northeast & Islands. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/exploring-implementation-attendance-supports-reduce-chronic-absenteeism-providence-public-school

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same descriptive fidelity analysis (figure 1): mentorship programs had the lowest moderate-or-high-fidelity share, printed as 38 percent of the 39 schools. Only 15 percent of schools were assigned a parent engagement specialist due to resource limitations.

> "Mentorship programs were less frequently implemented with moderate or high fidelity (38 percent)."
