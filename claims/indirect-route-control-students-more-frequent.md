## Evidence

### Amy C. Crosson & Margaret G. McKeown 2016

Amy C. Crosson & Margaret G. McKeown. (2016). Middle School Learners' Use of Latin Roots to Infer the Meaning of Unfamiliar Words. Cognition and Instruction, 34(2), 148-171. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1145121

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of seventh-grade dynamic assessment transcripts found students in all groups sometimes applied an indirect route using a root-related word as the analytic tool; the article reports "control students made these connections more frequently than intervention students."

> "Students in the intervention had been taught to be aware of orthographic variation in Latin roots, especially for vowels, but students in the control condition had not. Nonetheless, control students made these connections more frequently than intervention students."
