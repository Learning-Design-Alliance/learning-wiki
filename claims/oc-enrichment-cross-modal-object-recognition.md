## Evidence

### Gattas 2022

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognitive functions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tactile-to-visual cross-modal object recognition task (sample 10 min, delays 30 min and 1 h) 4 months post-enrichment in the four-group design. At 1 h only the OC group discriminated, t(9) = −4.357, p = 0.002; no effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Subsequent planned analysis for the 1-h delay using independent samples t-tests revealed a signiﬁcant difference in group performance on the task between the OC and the CC group, t(18) = 4.119, p = 0.001."

### Gattas 2022 (2)

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognitive functions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

CMOR omnibus ANOVA in the four-group design: a significant main effect of group, partial η2 = 0.293, while the delay main effect, F(1, 36) = 1.584, p = 0.216, partial η2 = 0.042, and the delay x group interaction, F(3, 36) = 2.762, p = 0.056, partial η2 = 0.187, were not significant.

> "there was a signi ﬁcant main effect of group F(3, 36) = 4.981, p = 0.005, partial η2 = 0.293."
