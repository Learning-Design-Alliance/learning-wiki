# More than 90 percent of MEU students agreed that learning with activities is fun, and 73 percent agreed they like doing activities to learn about numbers

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a post-test of a school program (program f), more than 90 percent of students agreed or strongly agreed that they like learning things with activities and that science with activities is fun, and 73 percent agreed they like doing activities to learn about numbers. [→ Anastasia Popkova 2023](#anastasia-popkova-2023)

## Evidence

### Anastasia Popkova 2023

Anastasia Popkova, David Blair and David Treagust. (2023). Maths for Einstein's Universe - Tools for Understanding Modern Reality. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.06969](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.06969)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-test Likert-scale attitude statements administered in program f (a two-day school trial, years 5-9) after chessboard, paper-cutting, maths-of-arrows and eclipse activities. The trial reports that "More than 90 percent of children taking part in Maths for Einstein’s Universe agreed or strongly agreed" with the activity statements, with 73 percent agreeing on numbers. No effect size is reported.

> "More than 90 percent of children taking part in Maths for Einstein’s Universe agreed or strongly agreed with the statement, "I like learning things with activities” and “Science with activities is fun” while 73 percent agreed or strongly agreed with the statement, "I like doing activities to learn about numbers.""
