Danni Chen 2024¶

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Speeded-choice ANOVA on overnight Δ%Choose showed a significant feedback main effect, F(1, 32) = 4.83, p = 0.035, η²G = 0.03; no TMR or interaction effects (ps > .316). Delayed Δ%Choose showed no significant effects (ps > 0.283).
