# A semester-long cosmology and GR course for non-specialist upper secondary students produces very large conceptual learning gains (overall d = 2.78)

## Evidence

### A. Gasparini 2025

A. Gasparini, A. Müller, F. Stern, L. Weiss. (2025). Cosmology and general relativity in upper secondary school through new targeted teaching materials: a study on student learning and motivation. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/ss9c-lkpj](https://doi.org/10.1103/ss9c-lkpj)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pre-post study of 70 students (conceptual test completed by 68) across four cohorts (2020-2024) taking the semester-long complementary-option cosmology course; repeated-measures t-test with Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment found "a very high effect size was found (t = 20.3, p < 0.001; d = 2.78)".

> "For the overall score, a statistically highly significant effect with a very high effect size was found (t = 20.3, p < 0.001; d = 2.78)."
