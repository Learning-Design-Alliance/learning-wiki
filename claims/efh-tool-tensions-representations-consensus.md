### Cao 2017

Cao, Y. (2017). High School Students' Group Interaction with the Electric Field Hockey Interactive Simulation. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1707.00177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1707.00177)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study analysis of Day 3 level-3 play; the author reports the unprompted box-checking and concludes "students had a consensus that they would like to see the representations while exploring level 3."

> "on Day 3, at the very beginning when students started with level 3, the operating student checked the three boxes before he placed any balls on the field. Nobody suggested him to do so, nor anybody reacted to him when he did it."
