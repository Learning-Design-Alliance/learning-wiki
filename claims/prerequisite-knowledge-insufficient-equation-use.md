## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2025

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer, and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Investigation of student and faculty problem solving: An example from quantum mechanics. Physics Education Research Conference. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.17487](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.17487)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase II analysis of the modified Q2 with the added instruction to explain reasoning using equations, among 48 MP and 28 QM students. Among the 23 MP students with prerequisite knowledge, 5 (22%) gave correct solutions supported by calculations; among 17 QM students, 3 (18%) did.

> "Out of the 23 students who had the prerequisite knowledge, only 9 students (39%) attempted to use all three equations, and 1 student (4%) used only some of the three equations. Another 9 students (39%) appeared to use their gut feeling as their answers did not refer to equations (despite being explicitly asked to explain their reasoning using equations)."
