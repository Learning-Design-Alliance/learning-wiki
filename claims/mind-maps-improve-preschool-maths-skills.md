## Evidence

### Polat 2017

Polat, O., Yavuz, E., A. & Tunc, A., B., O. (2017). The effect of using mind maps on the development of mathematics and science skills. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 12(5), 32-45. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1140786

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control-group study of 30 nursery-school children (15 per group); Mann Whitney-U test on post-test Maths Skills sub-scale scores showed a significant difference favouring the mind map group, "U= 60.000 p<.05". No effect size is printed.

> "Table 4 shows that there was a meaningful difference between the experiment group and the control group in terms of the MRPS Maths Skills sub-scale (U= 60.000 p<.05). When mean rank was taken into consideration, it was understood that the children who worked with mind maps had better Maths Skills compared to the children who did not."
