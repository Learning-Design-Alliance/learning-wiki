# In a teacher training workshop, most participants chose to report on an Edaq530 demonstration experiment and expected to incorporate the material into their teaching

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Katalin Kopasz 2011

Katalin Kopasz, Péter Makra and Zoltán Gingl. (2011). Edaq530: a transparent, open-end and open-source measurement solution in natural science education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/32/2/020](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/32/2/020)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Report of a two-day teacher training workshop (20–21 March 2010) in which each participant received a personal Edaq530 unit and laboratory practice. "9 out of 11 participants chose the latter", preparing demonstration-experiment reports spanning several physics areas. A survey also found eight of eleven answers between 75% and 100% for expected incorporation into teaching.

> "9 out of 11 participants chose the latter. The experiments spanned several areas of physics from the study of pendulum motion, circular motion or the conservation of linear momentum to the investigation of the effectiveness of thermal insulation."
