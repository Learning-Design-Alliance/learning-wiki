Elias Walsh 2014¶

Elias Walsh, Dallas Dotter. (2014). The Impact of Replacing Principals on Student Achievement in DC Public Schools (In Focus Brief). Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/in-focus-brief-the-impact-of-replacing-principals-on-student-achievement-in-dc-public-schools

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Key findings from the DCPS principal-replacement analysis covering principal changes at the end of school years 2007-2008 through 2010-2011. The brief reports the three-year timeline and states there was "no evidence of even temporary declines"; no effect size is printed for this finding.

"It took three years for new principals to achieve measurable achievement gains. Although the gains took time, there was no evidence of even temporary declines in achievement following the changes in school leadership."
