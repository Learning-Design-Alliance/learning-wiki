# Children get faster at recognising familiar words in speech between 19 and 31 months of age

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Reaction time to shift gaze from distracter to target decreased significantly with age across the three measurement points. [→ Michelle S. Peter 2019](#michelle-s-peter-2019)

## Evidence

### Michelle S. Peter 2019

Michelle S. Peter, Samantha Durrant, Andrew Jessop, Amy Bidgood, Julian M. Pine, Caroline F. Rowland. (2019). Does speed of processing or vocabulary size predict later language growth in toddlers? Cognitive Psychology 115 (2019) 101238. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2019.101238

`q3 · i?`

Mixed effects model on reaction time at 19, 25 and 31 months in the Language 0–5 cohort, with Measurement point as fixed effect and random slope for Participant. Mean RT fell from 729.94 ms at 19 months to 639.14 ms at 31 months, and the model showed "a main effect of Measurement point".

> "The results revealed a main effect of Measurement point (β=−8.32, χ2(1)=49.71, p < .001), indicating that children got faster at recognising familiar words in speech with age."
