## Evidence

### Serkan Perkmen 2026

Serkan Perkmen, Sara Dexter. (2026). From sources of self-efficacy to technology integration intentions: a new social cognitive model. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10623-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis results (Fig. 2) in the 144-participant sample: self-efficacy influenced intentions both directly and indirectly through outcome expectations; outcome expectations' direct path (0.55) exceeded self-efficacy's (0.18); interest's direct path was β = 0.15, p < .05.

> "Outcome expectations had a much stronger direct influence on intentions than self-efficacy (0.55 vs. 0.18). Interest had a small but a significant direct influence on intentions (β = 0.15, p < .05)."
