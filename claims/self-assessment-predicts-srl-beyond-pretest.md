# Self-assessment was associated with 10% additional explained variance in post-test SRL beyond pre-test SRL in one single-group pre-test/post-test study with no comparison group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In one single-group pre-test/post-test study with no comparison group, adding self-assessment to the MSLQ pretest was associated with 10% additional explained variance in post-test SRL (ΔR² = 0.10, p < 0.001), and self-assessment was the only variable reaching significance in later models. [→ Khoiriyah Umatul 2025](#khoiriyah-umatul-2025)

## Evidence

### Khoiriyah Umatul 2025

Khoiriyah Umatul, Roberts Chris. (2025). Investigating the role of self-assessment in enhancing self-regulated learning amongst medical students in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07359-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Hierarchical multiple regression (n = 65) predicting MSLQ post-test: Model 1 (pretest only) R² = 0.48; Model 2 added self-assessment with ΔR² = 0.10. In Models 3 and 4 only self-assessment was significant (p < 0.001). The article prints no standardized effect size.

> "Model 2 (adding self-assessment) significantly improved the prediction (ΔR² = 0.10, p < 0.001), showing that self-assessment independently predicts SRL beyond initial SRL levels."
