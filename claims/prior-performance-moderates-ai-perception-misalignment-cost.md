Second-order polynomial response surface (Fig. 1) with heteroscedasticity-robust standard errors, described by the authors as an illustrative complement (model R² = .37, F = 10.30) given a modest sample and discrepancy scores concentrated near zero; regression coefficients are printed but no standardised effect size.
