## Evidence

### Stuij SM 2018

Stuij SM, Labrie NHM, van Dulmen S, Kersten MJ, Christoph N, Hulsman RL, Smets E. (2018). Developing a digital communication training tool on information-provision in oncology: uncovering learning needs and training preferences. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1308-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

First learning-needs theme from the focus groups and interviews: HCP described wanting patients to "feel at ease", be satisfied, and understand what is said and what to expect, including understanding and adhering to the treatment plan.

> "In general, HCP wanted their patients to feel at ease during consultations; to be satisfied with the consultation; to understand what is being said during consultations and to have a clear understanding of what to expect."
