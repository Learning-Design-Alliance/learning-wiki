Improving adolescent literacy: Effective classroom and intervention practices: A Practice Guide (NCEE #2008-4027). Descriptive report of national NAEP 2007 reading data cited in the guide's overview as the motivation for the recommendations. The guide states that "69 percent of 8th grade students fall below the proficient level" and separately that 26 percent read below the basic level; no effect size applies.

"Data from the 2007 National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP) in read- ing report that 69 percent of 8th grade students fall below the proficient level in their ability to comprehend the meaning of text at their grade level."
