## Evidence

### Stolze Franka 2026

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the 10 inexperienced participants (VG n = 5, PG n = 5) using the Vertebralis catheter; the video group reached the left vertebral artery significantly more often (p = 0.048) and, per Table 3, had fewer path deviations (p = 0.048).

> "The analysis of inexperienced participants in the VG and the PG showed a significantly higher success rate of bringing the Vertebralis catheter into the LV when the video-based method was used compared to the presentation-based method (p = 0.048, Table 3)."

### Stolze Franka 2026 (2)

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney-U subgroup analysis of the 10 inexperienced participants reported effect sizes of r = 0.655 (success, p = 0.040) and r = -0.640 (path deviations, p = 0.046), with a similar pattern for the Headhunter catheter.

> "Inexperienced participants in the VG showed higher success rates (r = 0.655, p = 0.040, n = 10) and made fewer path deviations to advance into the LV (r = -0.640, p = 0.046, n = 10) than participants in the PG, similar to the results of inexperienced participants using the Headhunter catheter."
