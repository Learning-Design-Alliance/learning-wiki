### Bahsoun Soukaina 2026

Bahsoun Soukaina, Morris Mhairi, Akam Elizabeth. (2026). Cartooning in Biological Learning. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors interpret survey and free-form data against the revised Bloom's taxonomy: by planning, creating and presenting cartoons, students were expected to show understanding, application, analysis, evaluation and creativity. Supporting ratings were the Figure 5 averages of 4.2, 4 and 4.3, and students highlighted creativity as a benefit in open-ended comments. This is the authors' interpretation, not a direct measure of cognitive level.

> "The quantitative and qualitative data both contain evidence of students' involvement beyond the first level of learning in Bloom's taxonomy. Data presented in Figure 5 shows that improved understanding, critical thinking, and identifying the main points in the taught topic scored on average 4.2, 4, and 4.3 respectively."
