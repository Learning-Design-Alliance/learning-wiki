## Evidence

### Ragupathi Darnisha 2020

Ragupathi Darnisha, Ibrahim Normala, Tan Kit-Aun, Andrew Beatrice Ng. (2020). Relations of Bedtime Mobile Phone Use to Cognitive Functioning, Academic Performance, and Sleep Quality in Undergraduate Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17197131

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlational analysis (Table 2, n = 385 undergraduates) reporting Pearson's r between BMPU and study variables; the printed correlation of BMPU with academic performance is r = −0.17 (p < 0.01), i.e. "significantly and negatively correlated with academic performance".

> "Bedtime mobile phone use was significantly and negatively correlated with academic performance but was significantly and positively correlated with sleep quality."
