## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The correlation between extraneous cognitive load and usability was not statistically significant (r = −0.15, P = 0.41), and the correlation between germane load and post-training knowledge was not significant (r = 0.37, P = 0.11). [→ Tokuno Junko 2025](#tokuno-junko-2025)

## Evidence

### Tokuno Junko 2025

Tokuno Junko, Knobovitch Ryan M., Botelho Fabio, Fried Howard B., Carver Tamara E., Fried Gerald M. (2025). Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation for Medical Student Procedural Training: Assessment of Cognitive Load and Usability. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506251339920

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis in the secondary analysis (n = 22). The article reports the germane-knowledge correlation was "not statistically significant (P = 0.11)" and the extraneous-usability correlation was not significant (r = −0.15, P = 0.41); equivalence was not tested.

> "There was a slightly positive correlation between germane cognitive load and post-training knowledge (r = 0.37), but this was not statistically significant (P = 0.11). There was no statistically significant correlation between extraneous cognitive load and usability (r = −0.15, P = 0.41)."
