## Evidence

### Winston Kalman A 2013

Winston Kalman A, Van Der Vleuten Cees PM, Scherpbier Albert JJA. (2013). Remediation of at-risk medical students: theory in action. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-132

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Classroom observation of twelve recorded small-group sessions with six teachers and 49 students. The article reports that experienced teachers spent "around ten minutes" on rephrasing and eliciting ideas, while the other teachers averaged about one minute, likening this to experts slowing down when planning.

> "the experienced teachers (T5 and T6) insist that their groups rephrase the question and discuss it in detail, typically spending around ten minutes exploring definitions and relations between terms, thoroughly eliciting ideas before allowing their groups to consider the answer choices."
