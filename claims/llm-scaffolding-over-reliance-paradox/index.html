Kaşarcı İsmail 2026¶

Kaşarcı İsmail, Yurt Eyüp. (2026). Effectiveness of AI-Supported Game-Based Learning: A Systematic Review of Outcomes, Challenges, and Future Directions. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071050

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The review reports a single-group observational study of beginner CFL learners using an LLM tutor that was well-received, in which "positive LLM tutor perceptions do not necessarily translate to learning gains" and content-relevant responses were associated with more superficial engagement.

"Fang et al. (2025) critically found that positive LLM tutor perceptions do not necessarily translate to learning gains, with content-relevant AI responses associated with more superficial engagement (the over-reliance risk)."
