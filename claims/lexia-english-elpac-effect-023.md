### Lexia English Language Development 2022

Lexia English Language Development. (2022). Evidence rating summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/lexia-english/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study in three California public school districts in 2021–2022 examining 1,452 English Learners in grades 1–5 on the ELPAC, compared to matched peers with analyses controlling for prior achievement and demographics. The study reports a statistically significant positive effect, "effect size = +0.23".
