### Gardner P 2016

Gardner P, Slater H, Jordan JE, Fary RE, Chua J, Briggs AM. (2016). Physiotherapy students’ perspectives of online e-learning for interdisciplinary management of chronic health conditions: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0593-5

> "students clearly expressed uncertainty in their knowledge and skills surrounding the use of interprofessional practice as it related to the management of chronic disease in the broader physiotherapy curriculum."
