### Chen Yuanshuai 2026

A virtual patient-based training program to enhance clinical decision-making competencies in general practice residents: mixed-methods study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post paired t-test analysis of OSCE station scores for the full 50-resident cohort. The article reports "not statistically significant" changes with small effect sizes for these two stations; equivalence was not tested.

> "Improvements in physical examination (mean difference = 2.4, 95% CI [-0.1, 4.8], Cohen's d = 0.28) and medical record writing (mean difference = 1.2, 95% CI [-0.1, 2.5], Cohen's d = 0.26) were not statistically significant"
