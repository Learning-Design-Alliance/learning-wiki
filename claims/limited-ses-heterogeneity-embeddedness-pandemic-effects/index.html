Evidence¶

van de Werfhorst 2024¶

van de Werfhorst, H. G., Zwier, D., Geven, S., Bol, T. & Haelermans, C. (2024). Inequality in pandemic effects on school track placement and the role of social and academic embeddedness. npj Science of Learning 9:71. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00283-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multigroup structural equation model estimating embeddedness coefficients separately by SES group on N=402. Self-efficacy is associated with smaller pandemic effects across most groups; motivation shows a near-zero coefficient among the most disadvantaged on seventh-grade track level; parental involvement coefficients show more heterogeneity but none is statistically significant.

"However, despite the ﬁt statistics favoring the model separating effects by socioeconomic background, Fig. 4 shows that point estimates for most embeddedness indicators are pretty consistent across the socioeconomic groups, with overlapping con ﬁdence intervals."
