### Moira McCullough 2016

Moira McCullough, Betsy Keating, Luke Heinkel. (2016). District-Charter Collaboration Grant Implementation: Final Findings from Interviews and Site Visits. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/districtcharter-collaboration-grant-implementation-final-findings-from-interviews-and-site-visits

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with central-office administrators and school principals and teacher focus groups conducted in late 2015 as part of the district-charter collaboration grant evaluation. The report finds collaboration "remains less widespread among school leaders than central office administrators" but is strong among leadership training and residency participants. No effect size is reported.

> "Cross-sector collaboration remains less widespread among school leaders than central office administrators, but is particularly strong among participants in grant-funded intensive leadership training or principal residency programs."
