## Evidence

### Miaoyun Li 2023

Miaoyun Li, Chun Lu, Harrison H. Yang, Di Wu, Xiao Yang. (2023). The influence of organizational factors on the acceptance of online teaching among college faculty during the COVID-19 pandemic: a nationwide study in mainland China. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10245-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Organizational-level path estimates from a multilevel structural equation model fitted to a nationwide Chinese survey of 209,058 faculty in 858 institutions. The model found strategic planning and teaching quality monitoring "were found to be positively related to perceived usefulness," accounting for 2.4% of its variance; the strategic planning path was only marginal (p = .059).

> "Strategic planning (β  = .011, p = .059, 95% CI = [− .000, .023]) and teaching quality monitoring (β = .024, p < .01,95% CI = [.008, .041]) were found to be positively related to perceived usefulness, collectively accounting for 2.4% variance of faculty-perceived usefulness."
