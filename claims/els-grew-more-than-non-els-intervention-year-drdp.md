# During the intervention year, EL preschoolers' DRDP scores were predicted to increase more than Non-ELs' scores in all five DRDP domains after controlling for pretest, with statistically significant differences in ATL-REG and PD

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After controlling for pretest scores, ELs' DRDP scores increased more than Non-ELs' in all five domains, significantly for ATL-REG and PD and non-significantly for SED, LLD, and MATH. [→ McCarthy 2020](#mccarthy-2020)

## Evidence

### McCarthy 2020

McCarthy, E., Morgan, C. & Sussman, J. (2020). Growing strong learners in Galt Joint Union Elementary School District. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613373

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression on DRDP scores from the 2019-2020 intervention year at Fairsite Preschool, each domain analyzed independently, comparing EL and Non-EL growth. The article reports "statistically significant for two DRDP domains (ATL-REG, PD)"; no effect sizes are printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "The results show that after controlling for each child's pretest scores, ELs' scores were predicted to increase more than Non-ELs' scores in all DRDP domains. The difference between ELs and Non-ELs was statistically significant for two DRDP domains (ATL-REG, PD) and was not statistically significant for three DRDP domains (SED, LLD, and MATH)."
