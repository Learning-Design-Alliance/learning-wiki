## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with low self-esteem scored significantly lower on attaching to students' study groups and attending self-development programs (both p<0.001), which the authors identify as protective factors. [→ Alghamdi Saleh A. 2023](#alghamdi-saleh-a-2023)

## Evidence

### Alghamdi Saleh A. 2023

Alghamdi Saleh A., Aljaffer Mohammed A., Alahmari Faisal S., Alasiri Ahmed B., Alkahtani Abdullah H., Alhudayris Fadhah S., Alhusaini Bassam A. (2023). The impact of low self-esteem on academic achievement and the behaviors related to it among medical students in Saudi Arabia. Saudi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.15537/smj.2023.44.6.20230055

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1,099 medical students; Table 3 shows lower mean scores among the low self-esteem group for attaching to students' study groups (2.37 vs 2.69) and attending self-development programs (2.26 vs 2.67), both p<0.001. The design is observational, so no causal protective effect is established.

> "Participating in students' study groups and attending self-development programs were estimated to be the protective factors against low self-esteem"
