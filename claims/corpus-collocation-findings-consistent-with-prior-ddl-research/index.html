Uçar 2015¶

Uçar, S., & Yükselir, C. (2015). The Effect of Corpus-based Activities on Verb-Noun Collocations in EFL Classes. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology – April 2015, volume 14 issue 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057374

q1 · i? · causal · r1

The authors' narrative review of prior concordancing and data-driven learning studies, reported as background rather than systematically synthesized. They cite eight prior studies as supporting the effectiveness of corpus-based collocation instruction over traditional methods.

"The results obtained from this current study is consistent with the previous researches that have found the impact of corpus-based studies on collocation teaching over th e traditional method. (Sun and Wang, 2003; Chan and Liou, 2005; Jaeforpour and Koosha, 2006; Chujo, Utiyama and Miura, 2006; Liu and Jiang, 2009; Le, 2010; Binkai, 2012, Jafarpour, Hashemian and Alipour, 2013)."
