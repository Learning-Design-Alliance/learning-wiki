## Evidence

### Kim J 2021

Kim J, Shin EJ, Han KH, Park S, Youn JH, Jin G, Lee JY. (2021). Efficacy of Smart Speaker–Based Metamemory Training in Older Adults: Case-Control Cohort Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/20177

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative focus group interviews with 28 MMT participants after the course, using eight open-ended questions conducted by a clinical psychologist. One user said the speaker was "convenient because I did not need to press the keyboard or the button."

> "Users of smart speakers reported that they were easier to use and less burdensome than other information and communications technology tools such as computers and mobile phones."
