## Evidence

### Rupprecht 2017

Rupprecht, S., Paulus, P. & Walach, H. (2017). Mind the teachers! The impact of mindfulness training on self-regulation and classroom performance in a sample of German school teachers. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(4), 565-581. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.4.565

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental waitlist-controlled study of 32 German teachers (intervention n=18, control n=14). Table 2 reports Cohen's d of 1.78 for mindful presence and 1.29 for mindful acceptance at post-test, and 1.11 and 1.20 at follow-up. The article states "The between-group effect sizes were large for both post-test and follow-up".

> "t-Tests revealed that teachers in the intervention group reported greater mindful presence and mindful acceptance at post -test and 3 -month follo w-up than controls. The between-group effect sizes were large for both post-test and follow-up (Table 2)."
