### Wilding 2024

Wilding, K., Wright, M., & von Stumm, S. (2024). Using DNA to Predict Education: a Meta-analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09928-4

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Discussion synthesis of the meta-regression results: predictions "were reduced by 57% and 50%, respectively" in African American versus European ancestry samples, with significance only for attainment. The authors attribute this to limited portability of European-GWAS-based scores.

> "polygenic score predictions for educational attainment and achievement were reduced by 57% and 50%, respectively, in samples that were described as African American compared to those of European ancestry, although this effect was only significant for educational attainment"
