## Evidence

### Antoniou Mark 2016

Antoniou Mark, Ettlinger Marc, Wong Patrick C. M. (2016). Complexity, Training Paradigm Design, and the Contribution of Memory Subsystems to Grammar Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158812

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 compared 25 no-feedback learners with the 36 baseline learners in a 2 × 2 ANOVA. Only the grammar main effect was significant (F(1, 59) = 12.2, p = .001, ηp2 = .172; simple 69.3% vs complex 51.6%); "There was no significant main effect of feedback, p = .719, or interaction, p = .877." Working memory also did not correlate with learning in either feedback condition.
