Salazar 2015¶

Salazar, D. A. (2015). Razalas' Grouping Method and Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082724

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group Wilcoxon tests (control W=-28; experimental W=-231, z-observed 4.01) and a between-group Mann-Whitney Test (UA=62, z=1.45) were significant at 0.05. Posttest mean was 9.33 (failing) for control and 14.33 (grade 1.75, Good) for the experimental group.
