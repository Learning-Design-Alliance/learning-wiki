### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation model (SEM) with FIML estimation on 336 Nigerian Catholic priests, modeling exposure as a latent construct of three SBX dimensions and competencies as a latent construct of four MSPCC domains. The standardized path was "β = 0.23" with R2 = 0.055, and model fit was excellent (CFI = 0.991).

> "Consistent with the primary hypothesis, lifetime exposure to suicide significantly predicted higher suicide prevention competencies (β = 0.23, z = 3.28, p = 0.001). The exposure latent factor explained a modest 5.5% of the variance in clergy competencies (R2 = 0.055)"
