## Evidence

### Schneider 2020

Rebecca Schneider, Jörn R. Sparfeldt. (2020). Academic competence and affect self-concepts in elementary school students: Social and dimensional comparisons. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-019-09532-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group GI/E analyses comparing self-concept residual correlations across grades 1–4 (N = 858 plus n = 349 first graders). The study reports significant omnibus and pairwise differences "with lower correlations occurring in higher grades"; eight specific residual correlation comparisons were significant.

> "Significant differences in self-concept residual correlations occurred across grades (multi-group model: Tw = 52.90, df = 18, p < .01) and, more specifically, between first and third grade (Tw = 22.61, df = 6, p < .01) and first and fourth grade (Tw = 29.57, df = 6, p < .01) with lower correlations occurring in higher grades."
