## Evidence

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Controlled study results on posttest solution length: Adaptive mean = 39 (SD = 8) versus Control mean = 44 (SD = 12), U = 495, p = .06, with "Cohen's d = .49"; the difference is only marginally significant.

> "a Mann-Whitney U test on posttest length shows a marginally signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the two HNU conditions ( U = 495, p = .06) with the Adaptive condition (Mean = 39, SD = 8) forming marginally signiﬁcantly shorter proofs than the Control (Mean = 44, SD = 12), and a moderate eﬀect size (Cohen's d = .49)."

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022 (2)

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled study results on posttest time: no significant difference between conditions (t(72) = .03, p = .98); Adaptive mean = 31min versus Control mean = 30min. Equivalence was not tested.

> "on the transformed posttest time, no signiﬁcant diﬀerences (t(72) = .03, p = .98) were found between the two conditions, with students in the Adaptive condition ( Mean = 31min, SD = 30min) spending a similar amount of time on the posttest as the Control ( Mean = 30min, SD = 27min)."
