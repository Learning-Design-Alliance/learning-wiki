Evidence¶

Huang Jichong 2019¶

Huang Jichong, Tang Ying, Tang Jun, Shi Jing, Wang Hua, Xiong Tao, Xia Bin, Zhang Li, Qu Yi, Mu Dezhi. (2019). Educational efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1763-z

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of three RCTs comparing high-fidelity simulation with traditional training. The pooled skill-performance effect size was SMD = 1.63 (95% CI 0.49–2.77), a large benefit favoring high-fidelity simulation, with heterogeneity I2 = 90%.

"The pooled results by meta-analysis showed a large benefit with high-fidelity simulation when compared with traditional training in skill performance (SMD 1.63; 95% CI 0.49–2.77)"

Huang Jichong 2019 (2)¶

Huang Jichong, Tang Ying, Tang Jun, Shi Jing, Wang Hua, Xiong Tao, Xia Bin, Zhang Li, Qu Yi, Mu Dezhi. (2019). Educational efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1763-z

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of the same three RCTs comparing high-fidelity simulation with traditional training on neonatal resuscitation knowledge. The pooled effect size was SMD = 0.69 (95% CI 0.42–0.96), a moderate benefit.

"a moderate benefit in neonatal resuscitation knowledge (SMD 0.69; 95% CI 0.42–0.96) with high-fidelity simulation when compared with traditional training"

Related Claims¶

  • High-fidelity simulation produces a large short-term benefit in neonatal resuscitation skill performance in single-arm pre-post studies (pooled SMD 1.34) — related
  • HF simulation improves ALS skill performance at course conclusion versus LF simulation and traditional training (pooled SMD = 0.63) with high heterogeneity — related
  • HF simulation improves ALS knowledge at course conclusion more than LF simulation or traditional training (pooled SMD = 0.38) — related
  • HF simulation shows no significant benefit for satisfaction, confidence, or teamwork behaviors, and no long-term knowledge retention benefit — related
  • High-fidelity simulation shows only small-to-moderate benefits over low-fidelity simulation in skill performance (SMD 0.64) and knowledge (SMD 0.39) — related
  • Skill benefit of HF simulation holds against LF simulation (SMD = 0.36) but not against traditional training (SMD = -0.08) — reports the opposite
  • High-fidelity simulation training improves professional skills among practising registered nurses (moderate pooled effect) — related
