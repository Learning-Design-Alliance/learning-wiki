`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attribution to Park et al. (2015), an eye-tracking study of visual behaviour with anthropomorphic elements. The review reports these characteristics "capture attention even without influencing individuals’ emotions."

> "Park et al. ( 2015) used eye-tracking measures to evaluate the effect of integrating anthropomorphic elements on visual behaviour and found that these characteristics capture attention even without influencing individuals’ emotions."
