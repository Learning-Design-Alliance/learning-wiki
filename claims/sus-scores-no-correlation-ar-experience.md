## Evidence

### Williams A 2026

Williams A, Sun Z, Vaccarezza M. (2026). Augmented reality for teaching undergraduate human anatomy: An educators' perspective. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70214

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive analysis of SUS responses from 10 educators, comparing scores against self-reported AR experience duration. The four most experienced educators' scores were split between grade F and grade B. The authors state this data "cannot be generalized".

> "the individual SUS scores in this study did not correlate with the educators' experience in AR technology. The usability scores from the four educators who had more than a year's experience with AR were divided; two scores aligned to a grade F, well below average, and the other two scores gave an above average B grade"
