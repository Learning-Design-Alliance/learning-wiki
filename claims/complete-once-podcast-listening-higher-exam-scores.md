## Evidence

### Heltne 2024

Heltne, Sofie Fagervoll; Hovdenakk, Sigrid; Kvernenes, Monika; Tenstad, Olav. (2024). Study preferences and exam outcomes in medical education: insights from renal physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05964-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Podcast-usage cohort comparison in the survey study (Fig. 6 lower panel; ANOVA F (4, 67) = 2.958, p = 0.0259, post hoc p = 0.0217). The comparison of "all episodes once" versus "only some episodes" showed an 11-point mean difference; no effect size printed.
