## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yu Huan 2026

Yu Huan, Li Jing, Jia Yan, Zhang Jiayang, Wang Zhitao, Zou Huiru. (2026). Enhancing online teaching of “subgingival scaling and root planing” through periodontal endoscopy assistance: a study on its application in a teaching-practicing module. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-07706-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous 17-item post-class questionnaire with 13 five-point Likert items; the interest/confidence indicator summed Q8–Q10 for 147 valid responses. Both PE groups exceeded the traditional group; only P values printed, no effect size.

> "When it came to the impact on arousing students’ interest and confidence in future learning and practice, both PE in-person and online groups showed a significantly higher rating than the traditional group (P = 0.012, P = 0.013, respectively). There was no significant difference between the two periodontal endoscopy assisted teaching groups (P > 0.05)"
