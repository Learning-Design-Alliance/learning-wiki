# Patterning and reading groups both scored higher than mathematics and social studies groups on TOWRE Word and TERA reading measures, and patterning exceeded mathematics and social studies on the GORT

## Evidence

### Kidd 2013

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized four-condition first-grade study, blind posttesting in May showed on TOWRE Word and TERA that patterning and reading groups "did not differ significantly" while both beat the other groups (Table 2 grade equivalents up to 2.8 for patterning).

> "On the TOWRE Word and TERA measures of reading, the patterning group attained the highest scores in an absolute sense, but the patterning and reading groups did not differ significantly. Both achieved significantly higher scores than the other two groups."

### Kidd 2013 (2)

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GORT results in the same study: patterning (M = 13.71, GE 2.0) was superior to mathematics and social studies but not to reading; the reading group did not differ significantly from the patterning group on this measure.

> "On the GORT, the only differences were that the patterning group was superior to the mathematics and social studies groups."
