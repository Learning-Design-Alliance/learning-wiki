Stamer Tjorven 2026¶

Stamer Tjorven, Machinek Nora, Steinhäuser Jost, Goujard Mathilde, Flägel Kristina. (2026). The role of digital teaching methods in supporting practical skills training in the academic training of health professions – a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08785-9

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's narrative synthesis of its VR category (48 studies, including surgical simulators and immersive environments). It reports improvements in knowledge, procedural accuracy, error reduction and learner confidence, with greater engagement and satisfaction than conventional training.

"Studies have shown that VR can significantly enhance the acquisition and retention of clinical and procedural skills. Evidence demonstrates improvements in knowledge, procedural accuracy, error reduction and learner confidence"

Stamer Tjorven 2026 (2)¶

Stamer Tjorven, Machinek Nora, Steinhäuser Jost, Goujard Mathilde, Flägel Kristina. (2026). The role of digital teaching methods in supporting practical skills training in the academic training of health professions – a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08785-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of reported limitations across VR studies, naming cybersickness, limited tactile feedback, technical constraints and variable long-term skill transfer as barriers.

"Barriers include reports of simulator or cybersickness, limited tactile feedback compared to real procedures, technical constraints such as object interaction or haptic realism and variability in long-term skill transfer."
