In model 3 of the linear mixed models for females, the parental-mental-disorder coefficient was −16.5 (95%CI −17.09 to −15.9); for males it was −14.49 (−15.06, −13.92). Exposure was defined by specialist-care ICD-10 records in the Inpatient or Outpatient Register.
