## Evidence

### Rhodes Katherine T. 2017

Rhodes Katherine T., Branum-Martin Lee, Washington Julie A., Fuchs Lynn S. (2017). Measuring arithmetic: A psychometric approach to understanding formatting effects and domain specificity. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000189

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Full measurement model fitting in a confirmatory factor analysis of 1,314 third graders' 11 arithmetic, 3 language, and 5 executive attention measures. The Triple Code full model was an approximate good fit (RMSEA=.05, CFI=.95), and its three arithmetic factors correlated r=.68 to r=.76.

> "Completely standardized factor loadings ranged from .54 to .92, and factor correlations for this portion of the model ranged from r=.68 to r=.76, indicating that each of Triple Code Theory's arithmetic cognition factors were separable but highly related."
