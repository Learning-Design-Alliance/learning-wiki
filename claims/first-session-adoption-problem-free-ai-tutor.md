## Evidence

### Shihao Yang 2026

Shihao Yang, Marshall Van Alstyne, and Chrysanthos Dellarocas. (2026). When AI Tutors Speak: Evidence from a Randomized Field Experiment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23958

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Participation analysis in the five-week field period: weekly completion of the 20-minute engaged-time goal was 29/31/28/30/26 of 52 in the AI arm; 27 AI students completed at least four of five weeks.

> "Attrition was concentrated at the start: 14 AI students who consented never held a single session, and 7 holdout students never completed a check-in. This early non-use is a substantive finding about adoption rather than a mere nuisance: a quarter of the students offered a free tutor never tried it once."
