## Evidence

### Kinshuk 2016

Kinshuk, Chen, N.-S., Cheng, I.-L., & Chew, S. W. (2016). Evolution Is not enough: Revolutionizing Current Learning Environments to Smart Learning Environments. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0108-x

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Argumentative/definitional discussion, not an empirical measurement: the paper cites ASTD (2008) and others to argue that informal online learning now occupies about half of learners' daily time, noting "perhaps only 50 % of the learners ’daily learning time."

> "Informal learning methods are easily accessible and available to learners via the Internet, resulting in a shift in the time allocation of formal learning to perhaps only 50 % of the learners ’daily learning time."

### Kinshuk 2016 (2)

Kinshuk, Chen, N.-S., Cheng, I.-L., & Chew, S. W. (2016). Evolution Is not enough: Revolutionizing Current Learning Environments to Smart Learning Environments. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0108-x

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' interpretation of the cited trend: if formal systems do not change, informal learning may eventually replace formal learning, so "formal educational systems" need "fundamental change." No data are reported in this section.

> "With students spending less time in formal learning, it will only be a matter of time before informal learning replaces formal learning if formal educational systems do not change"
