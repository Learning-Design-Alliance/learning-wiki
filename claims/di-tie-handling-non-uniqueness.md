### Joshi 2019

Joshi, P.K., Jain, Y., Khunyakari, R. and Basu, S. (2019). A novel alternative to analyzing multiple choice questions via discrimination index. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1906.07941](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1906.07941)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Illustrative analysis of a ranking scenario with students sharing equal totals at the block boundary showed that different list orderings place different students in the top block, and DI values "have been observed to vary signiﬁcantly" across sorting arrangements.

> "Depending on the number of students in such category, DI values have been observed to vary signiﬁcantly."
