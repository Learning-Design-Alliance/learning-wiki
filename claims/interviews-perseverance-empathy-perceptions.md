## Evidence

### Žuljević Marija Franka 2022

Žuljević Marija Franka, Buljan Ivan. (2022). Academic and non-academic predictors of academic performance in medical school: an exploratory cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03436-1

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of four structured pilot interviews with sixth-year female students (age 23-24, GPA 3.70-4.96), conducted to inform future studies. "Perseverance was perceived by all four participants to be the main predictor of academic success"; no effect size applies.

> "Perseverance was perceived by all four participants to be the main predictor of academic success for medical students, with some participants placing exclusive emphasis on perseverance in terms of studying and fulfilling obligations."

### Žuljević Marija Franka 2022 (2)

Žuljević Marija Franka, Buljan Ivan. (2022). Academic and non-academic predictors of academic performance in medical school: an exploratory cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03436-1

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Theme from the same four structured pilot interviews on medical studies' role in education: "Three participants elaborated that medical studies cannot teach non-academic skills such as empathy". Some participants also stated their ability to empathize decreased over the course of their medical education.

> "Three participants elaborated that medical studies cannot teach non-academic skills such as empathy and that these values are already pre-existing in a person."
