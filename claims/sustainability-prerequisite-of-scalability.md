## Evidence

### Howard 2021

Howard, S. K., Schrum, L., Voogt, J., & Sligte, H. (2021). Designing research to inform sustainability and scalability of digital technology innovations. Educational Technology Research and Development, 69(4). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09913-y

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Conceptual argument in the article, drawing on Coburn (2003) and Kampylis et al. (2013), who treat sustainability and scalability as closely related constructs drawing on the same change strategies. No empirical data are reported for this claim.

> "Innovations that are not sustained, either not adopted at all or not enduring in practice, are unlikely to be scalable or scaled. Therefore, they argue that sustainability is actually a dimension and requirement of scalability."
