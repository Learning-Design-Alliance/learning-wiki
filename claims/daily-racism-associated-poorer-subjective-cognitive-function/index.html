Evidence¶

Coogan P 2020¶

Coogan P, Schon K, Li S, Cozier Y, Bethea T, Rosenberg L. (2020). Experiences of racism and subjective cognitive function in African American women. Alzheimer's & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring. https://doi.org/10.1002/dad2.12067

q3 · i2 · associational · r3

Prospective cohort analysis of 17,323 African American women aged 55+ in the Black Women's Health Study using multinomial logistic regression. The multivariable ORs for poor compared to good SCF "increased from 1.54 (95% CI 1.32 to 1.79) in quartile 2, to 1.88 (95% CI 1.61 to2.19) in quartile 3, to 2.75 (95% CI 2.34 to 3.23) in quartile 4". The printed effect size for the highest versus lowest quartile contrast is OR = 2.75 (95% CI 2.34 to 3.23).

"The multivariable ORs for poor compared to good SCF in the highest quartile of daily racism increased from 1.54 (95% CI 1.32 to 1.79) in quartile 2, to 1.88 (95% CI 1.61 to2.19) in quartile 3, to 2.75 (95% CI 2.34 to 3.23) in quartile 4 (P trend < .0001)"
