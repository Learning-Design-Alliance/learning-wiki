# Learning gains in the elective course were driven by image questions, which improved significantly in all three groups, while text-question scores showed no statistically significant change.

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bork F 2019

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Image-question subgroup analysis in the elective course Magic Mirror group (N = 24): "test scores increased from 29.60 ± 18.37% to 64.89 ± 19.69%", a significant gain with η 2 = 0.47.

> "For the Magic Mirror, test scores increased from 29.60 ± 18.37% to 64.89 ± 19.69% (F(1,48) = 42.94, P < 0.001, η 2 = 0.47)."

### Bork F 2019 (2)

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Image-question analysis for the Anatomage group (N = 24) in the elective course: "participants in the Anatomage group improved from 28.80 ± 21.66% to 59.11 ± 14.60%", significant with η 2 = 0.41.

> "Similarly, participants in the Anatomage group improved from 28.80 ± 21.66% to 59.11 ± 14.60% (F(1,48) = 33.65, P < 0.001, η 2 = 0.41)"

### Bork F 2019 (3)

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Image-question analysis for the atlas-based Theory group (N = 24): "test scores increased from 30.40 ± 14.28% to 59.11 ± 16.89%", significant with η 2 = 0.47.

> "while for the Theory group, test scores increased from 30.40 ± 14.28% to 59.11 ± 16.89% (F(1,48) = 42.13, P < 0.001, η 2 = 0.47)"

### Bork F 2019 (4)

Bork F, Stratmann L, Enssle S, Eck U, Navab N, Waschke J, Kugelmann D. (2019). The Benefits of an Augmented Reality Magic Mirror System for Integrated Radiology Teaching in Gross Anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1864

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' Discussion statement about the elective-course question-type analysis: "no statistically different changes in scores were recorded for text questions", while image-present questions improved significantly. No effect size printed for the text-question null.

> "Interestingly, no statistically different changes in scores were recorded for text questions regarding the topographical anatomy. However, test scores improved significantly when images were present to substantiate the topographical relations between structures subject to the test questions."
