# Students using ChatGPT 3.5 experience lower intrinsic cognitive load than students using Google during a web research task

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students in the LLM condition reported significantly lower intrinsic cognitive load than students in the web search condition, adjusted for prior knowledge (η² = 0.15). [→ Matthias Stadler 2024](#matthias-stadler-2024)

## Evidence

### Matthias Stadler 2024

Matthias Stadler, Maria Bannert, Michael Sailer. (2024). Cognitive ease at a cost: LLMs reduce mental effort but compromise depth in student scientific inquiry. Computers in Human Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2024.108386

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA in the same randomized experiment (n=91) controlling for prior knowledge. LLM group mean ICL was 3.16 (SD 1.46) versus 4.43 (SD 1.60) for web search, with "16.48 <0.001 0.15", i.e., η² = 0.15, a large effect.

> "H1.2-ICL 4.43(1.60) 3.16(1.46) 16.48 <0.001 0.15"
