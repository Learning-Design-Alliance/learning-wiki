Chiungjung Huang. (2012). Discriminant and Criterion-Related Validity of Achievement Goals in Predicting Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0026223

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis compared criterion-related validity of two-, three-, and four-factor models (84, 76, and 19 data points respectively). The author's integrative conclusion is that the "4-factor model as the best choice" follows from combining discriminant and criterion validity findings; no effect size for this model comparison is printed in the available text.

> "Integrating the findings regarding the discriminant and criterion-related validity revealed the 4-factor model as the best choice to facilitate the understanding of learning outcomes."
