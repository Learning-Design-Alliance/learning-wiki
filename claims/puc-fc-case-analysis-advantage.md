## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Case analysis and total theoretical scores showed a significant gradient PUC-FC > FC > LBL (p < 0.001), while basic knowledge scores did not differ (p > 0.05). [→ Cui Ming-Wang 2026](#cui-ming-wang-2026)

## Evidence

### Cui Ming-Wang 2026

Cui Ming-Wang, Zhang Miao-Miao, Dong Yu-Lei, Wen Tao, Xu Wei-Qi, Liu Qi-ZhiAng, Yang Ke-Zhen, Wang Zheng-Rou, Guo Zhu-Ling. (2026). Implementing peer-assisted learning in a dental flipped classroom: a model for postgraduate-undergraduate collaboration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09315-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-group randomized trial of 120 undergraduates stratified by baseline scores. Results section reports no basic-knowledge difference and higher case-analysis scores for PUC-FC, "especially the LBL group (p < 0.001)"; Table 5 prints means (45.8 vs 41.3 vs 35.7).

> "There was no significant difference in theoretical scores among the three groups (p > 0.05); students in the PUC-FC group scored significantly higher in case analysis questions than the other two groups, especially the LBL group (p < 0.001)"
