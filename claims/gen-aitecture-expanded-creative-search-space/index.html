Evidence¶

Kapsalis 2026¶

Kapsalis, T. (2026). Gen-AI-tecture: using generative AI to support architectural students in design tasks. https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1598-6426

q2 · i3 · design · r2

Focus-group study of 16 Level 3-5 architecture students using the Gen-AI-tecture tool. Questionnaire creative-skills subscale showed high means, "particularly strong endorsement for the tool as a stimulus for new ideas (A1 = 4.5) and experimentation (A2 = 4.2)", with A3 = 3.7; Spearman correlations with qualitative indices were ρ = .76 and ρ = .71 (p < .01).

"the mean A-subscale score correlated strongly with qualitative indices for “more ideas than usual” (ρ = .76, p < .01) and “trying unfamiliar styles/materials” (ρ = .71, p < .01), evidencing convergent validity between questionnaire ratings and reﬂexive accounts."

Kapsalis 2026 (2)¶

Kapsalis, T. (2026). Gen-AI-tecture: using generative AI to support architectural students in design tasks. https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1598-6426

q2 · i? · design · r2

Convergent mixed-methods analysis of questionnaire and reflexive discussion data from the two focus groups. The authors conclude the workflow "most robustly expanded students’ creative search space", with refinement of final decisions (A3 = 3.7) supported less uniformly; A3 correlated with justifying or adjusting final choices (ρ = .64, p < .01).

"Overall, the convergent quantitative and qualitative evidence suggests that the workﬂow most robustly expanded students’ creative search space, while supporting, though less uniformly, the reﬁnement of ﬁnal design decisions."
