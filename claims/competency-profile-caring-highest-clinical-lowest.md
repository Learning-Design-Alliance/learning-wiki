`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CINS sub-dimension scores among 249 newly graduated nurses, reported as medians with interquartile ranges and bootstrapped 95% confidence intervals. The CINS total median was 264.0 (70.0–301.0).

> "The highest median score was observed in the 'Caring' subdimension, with a median of 38.0 (range: 7.0–42.0). In contrast, the lowest median scores were recorded in the 'Clinical Biomedical Science' (28.0; range: 7.0–35.0) and 'General Clinical Skills' (42.0; range: 9.0–49.0) sub‐dimensions."
