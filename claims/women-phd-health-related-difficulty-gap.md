## Evidence

### Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir 2020

Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir. (2020). Ph.D. in physics as a hurdle race, and the “glass hurdles” for women. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nationwide weighted survey of Israeli physics Ph.D. students (n = 267; 60 women, 207 men). Asked whether they experienced a period when it was difficult to provide what was professionally expected, 71% of women answered positively versus 63% of men, a reported "71% answered positively, compared to 63% of men".

> "Of the women, 71% answered positively, compared to 63% of men."

### Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir 2020 (2)

Meytal Eran-Jona and Yosef Nir. (2020). Ph.D. in physics as a hurdle race, and the “glass hurdles” for women. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.02251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same survey, closed question on reasons for this difficulty (Fig. 1): "a much larger percentage of women (39%) compared to men (23%) mentioned health-related problems". Percentages are descriptive; no significance test is printed.

> "a much larger percentage of women (39%) compared to men (23%) mentioned health-related problems, both physiological and psychological."
