### Gan Xuejing 2024

Gan Xuejing, Ou Qianmin, Gong Zhuohong, Cai Gengbin, Liu Xinyu, Yang Ruihan, Lin Yuhang, Li Lin, Li Xiaolan, Hong Yun, Chen Zetao. (2024). A “flexible” compulsory dental basic research course for undergraduate students: a three-year retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04874-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of documental GPA data collected from the Department of Student Administration and Teaching Affairs for second-year dental students, 2016–2021. Group A averaged "3.191 ± 0.704" versus Group B "2.904 ± 0.771" (P < 0.001), analyzed by unpaired t test. No effect size is printed.
