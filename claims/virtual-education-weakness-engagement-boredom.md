### Zarifsanaiey N 2024

Zarifsanaiey N, Farrokhi MR, Karimian Z, Hoseini S, Chahartangi F, Shahraki HR. (2024). Lesson learned from assessing teachers’ and students’ perspectives regarding the quality of e-learning in medical education during the pandemic: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05160-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative phase: semi-structured interviews via WhatsApp with 10 virtual education professors and 10 student representatives, analyzed by content analysis with member checking and expert validation. Participants also cited "the insufficient utilization of interactive tools" and reduced social interaction.

> "it is evident that the teaching approaches implemented in the e-learning environment were deemed unsuitable, resulting in online classes being perceived as tedious and uninteresting. This highlights a weakness in the execution of teaching methods within the virtual setting."
