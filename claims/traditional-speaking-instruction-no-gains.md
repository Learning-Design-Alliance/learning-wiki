### Mohammadzadeh 2018

Mohammadzadeh, A., & Sarkhosh, M. (2018). The Effects of Self-Regulatory Learning through Computer-Assisted Intelligent Tutoring System on the Improvement of EFL Learners' Speaking Ability. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 167-184. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11212a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons within the control group (n=15, traditional method): no significant change "from pre-test to the immediate post-test or delayed post-test" (sig>0.05); means moved 4.75, 4.83, 4.61. Equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "Post -hoc tests using the Bonferroni correction divulged that the traditional teaching of speaking did not improve speaking from pre-test to the immediate post-test or delayed post-test (sig>0.05)."
