Survey ratings of 84 topics by QM1 instructors (N=14), with four coverage levels (important, peripheral, assumed prior knowledge, do not cover/use). Table VI lists significant topics; normalization, angular momentum, Hermitian operators, and the time-independent Schrödinger equation each reached 100%.
