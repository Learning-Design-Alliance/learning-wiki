## Evidence

### Ji Zhe 2024

Ji Zhe, Jiang Yuliang, Sun Haitao, Qiu Bin, Chen Yi, Li Mao, Fan Jinghong, Wang Junjie. (2024). Enhancing puncture skills training with generative AI and digital technologies: a parallel cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06217-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Parallel cohort study of 90 refresher doctors in three sequentially assigned groups (30 each), assessed with 60-item validated multiple-choice tests at baseline, post-training, and 3-month follow-up. The results section reports the ordering "Group 3 was better than Group 2, and Group 2 was better than Group 1" with p < 0.05; Table 2 prints post-training means of 81.6 ± 4.56 (G3), 78.5 ± 4.65 (G2), 69.6 ± 4.58 (G1).

> "At post-training and 3-month follow-up, Group 3 was better than Group 2, and Group 2 was better than Group 1, with significant differences between groups (p < 0.05) (Table 2)."
