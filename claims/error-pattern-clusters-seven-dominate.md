### Marcos Daniel Caballero 2011

Marcos Daniel Caballero. (2011). Evaluating and Extending a Novel Course Reform of Introductory Mechanics. Georgia Institute of Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.5593](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.5593)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A cluster analysis of error-code patterns (Table 11) reviewed thirty clusters; the table reports that only seven with "an inter-cluster distance of less than 0.3" were populated by more than 3% of students and that "These seven clusters accounted for 86.5% of students." The bottom 18 clusters were each populated by less than 1% of students.
