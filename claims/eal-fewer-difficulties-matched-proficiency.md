## Evidence

### Whiteside 2017

Whiteside, Katie E.; Gooch, Debbie; Norbury, Courtenay F. (2017). English Language Proficiency and Early School Attainment Among Children Learning English as an Additional Language. Child Development, 88(3), 812-827. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12615

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical multiple regression predicting SDQ total difficulties in the unadjusted model (F(3, 7,262) = 1,047.84, p < .001, 30% of variance); the standardized interaction coefficient was β = −.07, a negligible standardized effect. The article reports that "an increase in CCC–S scores among children with EAL was associated with a smaller increase in total difficulties scores."

> "Moreover, there was a significant CCC–S × EAL Status interaction; compared to monolingual children, an increase in CCC–S scores among children with EAL was associated with a smaller increase in total difficulties scores (see Figure 2)."
