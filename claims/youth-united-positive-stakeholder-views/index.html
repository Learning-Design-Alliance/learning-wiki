Gorard et al. 2016¶

Stephen Gorard, Beng Huat See, Nadia Siddiqui, Emma Smith and Patrick White. (2016). Youth Social Action Trials: Youth United — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581107

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation collecting feedback from teachers, pupils and parents about mode of delivery and programme implementation. Views were positive across all three groups, though these are self-reported perceptions rather than measured outcomes.

"The process evaluation revealed that pupils, teachers , and parents all had very positive views about the intervention, and in general believed that it had a positive impact on the pupils involved."
