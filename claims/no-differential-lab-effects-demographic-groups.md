## Evidence

### Markert 2026

Markert, E., Verostek, M., Dew, M., Chen, X., Lory-Moran, M., Padavan, S., Walia, H., Wittich, P., and Holmes, N.G. (2026). CURE-like, not cure-all: Varying broad relevance in experimentation labs produces similar student outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Exploratory estimated marginal means analysis (Fig. 5) from the hierarchical model with demographic-by-condition interaction terms. The authors state EMMs are "clustered near zero across both SALT and PEPPER conditions" with no meaningful trends; groups with variable EMMs had large standard errors due to small samples.

> "We see no meaningful trends in differences in EMMs between demographic groups for any construct in Fig. 5. For a majority of demographic groups, EMMs are clustered near zero across both SALT and PEPPER conditions, suggesting no strong pattern of differential effect by gender, first generation status, race/ethnicity, or major."
