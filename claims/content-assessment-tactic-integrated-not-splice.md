# Content and assessment oriented tactic is a distinct, integrated tactic rather than a simple combination of content and assessment tactics

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The Content and assessment oriented tactic involves navigating back and forth between content and assessment within sessions, distinguishing it from purely content-focused or assessment-focused tactics. [→ Yizhou Fan 2021](#yizhou-fan-2021)

## Evidence

### Yizhou Fan 2021

Yizhou Fan, Wannisa Matcha, Nora’ayu Ahmad Uzir, Qiong Wang, Dragan Gašević. (2021). Learning Analytics to Reveal Links Between Learning Design and Self-Regulated Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 31, 980–1021. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00249-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sequence analysis of the stacked bar chart distributions for Tactic 2 sessions shows that "several learning actions (i.e., “Content access”, “Assessment” and “Content revision”) are relatively and evenly distributed" across the session, indicating learners navigated back and forth between content and assessment, e.g. attempting a unit test, revisiting videos, then re-attempting the test.

> "Content and assessment oriented tactic is not a literal splicing and a simple combination of Content oriented tactic and Assessment oriented tactic, but a more complex learning tactic."
