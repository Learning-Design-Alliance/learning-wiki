### Dechamps 2025

Dechamps, T., & Skulmowski, A. (2025). The Effective Design of Tasks Involving Learning by Drawing: Current Trends and Methodological Progress in Research on Drawing to Learn. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10026-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that in Fiorella et al. (2020), participants compared drawing, explaining, and rewatching a video lecture on the human kidney; a significant effect of learning strategy on retention and transfer was found, and "the explain group reached significantly better results than the draw and rewatch groups."

> "The explain group reached significantly better results than the draw and rewatch groups (Fiorella et al., 2020)."

### Dechamps 2025 (2)
