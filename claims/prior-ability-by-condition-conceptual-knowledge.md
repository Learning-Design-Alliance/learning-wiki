# Learning condition affected conceptual knowledge differently for advanced and novice learners: advanced learners benefited from concept map study, novices showed no condition effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` For advanced learners, concept map study was more effective than concept map completion for conceptual knowledge acquisition (large effect per the article's t-test). [→ Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017](#montpetit-tourangeau-k-2017)
`q3 i?` For novice learners, there was no effect of the learning condition on conceptual knowledge. [→ Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017 (2)](#montpetit-tourangeau-k-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up t-test on conceptual knowledge averaged across both post-tests, following a significant prior ability × learning condition interaction (medium effect size, ηp² = .060) in the 2×2×2 mixed ANOVA.

> "for advanced learners, concept map study (76.8 ± 7.8%) was more effective (t(27) = 2.532, p = .017, two-tailed, Cohen's d = .949, large effect) for conceptual knowledge acquisition than concept map completion (67.0 ± 12.4%)"

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017 (2)

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up t-test for novice learners on conceptual knowledge averaged across both post-tests; the article reports no significant difference between conditions and no effect size for this contrast.

> "However, for novice learners, there was no effect of the learning condition on conceptual knowledge (concept map completion: 70.9 ± 10.2%; concept map study: 70.3 ± 11.3%; t(30) = .164, p = .871, two-tailed)"
