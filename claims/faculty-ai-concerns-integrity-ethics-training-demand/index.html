John Chick, Laura Morello and Stephanie Staffey. (2026). From fear to innovation: a case study of transformative faculty development for ethical AI integration in higher education. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00225-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Pre-institute survey of ten participants (Tables 9 and 10) with multiple-response items. The article reports these endorsement percentages descriptively; notably, equity and access drew 0% concern.

"Faculty concerns (Table 9) were dominated by fears around academic integrity, with 80% citing plagiarism or cheating as a top issue. Over-reliance on AI by students or faculty (70%) and bias or inaccuracy in AI outputs (50%) also emerged as pressing concerns."
