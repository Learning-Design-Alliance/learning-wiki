Evidence¶

Biscevic 2018¶

Biscevic, I., Pasalic, A., & Memisevic, H. (2018). The effects of executive functions and theory of mind on semantic fluency in preschool children. Problems of Education in the 21st Century, 76(1). https://www.scientiasocialis.lt/pec/

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Two-way ANOVA in a correlational study of 116 preschool children (mean age 58.8 months) tested EF (card-sort pass/fail) and TOM as predictors of animals-category word production in 60 seconds. The article reports "a significant effect of EF on semantic fluency test" (p=.03 in Table 2); no effect size is printed.

"According to the two-way ANOV A test, there was a significant effect of EF on semantic fluency test."
