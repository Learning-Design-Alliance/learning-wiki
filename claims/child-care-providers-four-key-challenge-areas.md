## Evidence

### Carrie Gillispie 2021

Carrie Gillispie, Caitlin Codella, Aaron Merchen, Joseph Davis, Ashley Cappo. (2021). Equity in Child Care Is Everyone's Business. The Education Trust & U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617545

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study by The Education Trust and the U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation: 32 in-depth provider interviews in 10 states and 12 chamber-leader interviews in seven states. Providers reported "funding challenges, safety and health concerns, and talent acquisition/professional development barriers." No effect sizes are reported.

> "Amid the COVID-19 crisis, child care providers, many of whom are women of color, face funding challenges, safety and health concerns, and talent acquisition/professional development barriers."
