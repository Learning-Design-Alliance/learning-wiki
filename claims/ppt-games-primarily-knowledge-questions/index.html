Jason Paul Siko 2011¶

Jason Paul Siko. (2011). Homemade PowerPoint Games. http://it.coe.uga.edu/wwild/pptgames

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Thematic analysis by two subject matter experts coding student game questions with Bloom's taxonomy for the first unit; 60.8% of 1,250 coded questions were Knowledge level and "No questions were ranked higher than application on Bloom's taxonomy."

"After coding 1,250 questions, the majority of the questions were judged to be knowledge level questions."
