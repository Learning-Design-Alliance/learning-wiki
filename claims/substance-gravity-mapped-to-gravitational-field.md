## Evidence

### Ayush Gupta 2013

Ayush Gupta, Andrew Elby, Luke D. Conlin. (2013). How substance-based ontologies for gravity can be productive: A case study. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010113)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors' analysis of Episode 1 transcript data: Lynn, using a substance-based fuel analogy, reconciled her correct answer (objects fall together) with her intuition that heavier objects are pulled harder. The authors write she "has effectively mapped gravity onto the force per unit mass."

> "Note that in trying to reconcile her conflicting ideas, and addressing Daniel`s concerns, Lynn has effectively mapped gravity onto the force per unit mass, which would be the correct definition for gravitational field (i.e., the acceleration due to gravity)."
