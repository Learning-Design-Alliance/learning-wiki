Questionnaire study by Ng (2011) with young adults (average age 19, TOEFL 450-500) using an adapted drama script in groups of four or five, with teacher observation for triangulation. The review reports "88% stating the English lessons were more interesting with RT" and 95% agreeing RT could improve spoken English.

> "The results of part one displayed an overall positive attitude among Chinese EFL learners with 88% stating the English lessons were more interesting with RT and 95 % agreed that RT could be a good way to improve their spoken English."
