# Classroom VLE usage falls into three latent classes distinguished by student and teacher engagement levels

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Classroom-level LCA identified three classes: Low student and teacher engagement (258 classrooms, 25.29%), Low student but high teacher engagement (552, 54.12%), and High student and teacher engagement (210, 20.59%). [→ Walter L. Leite 2021](#walter-l-leite-2021)

## Evidence

### Walter L. Leite 2021

Walter L. Leite, Zeyuan Jing, Huan Kuang, Dongho Kim & A. Corinne Huggins-Manley (2021). Multilevel Mixture Modeling with Propensity Score Weights for Quasi-Experimental Evaluation of Virtual Learning Environments. Structural Equation Modeling: A Multidisciplinary Journal, 28:6, 964-982. https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2021.1919895

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom-level LCA on 19 usage variables (12 selected as indicators), with 1,020 classrooms nested in 631 schools; entropy was 0.931. Class 2 classrooms were ones where "teachers show videos to the whole class, but not require students to answer questions as homework".

> "The third latent class, labelled “High student and teacher engagement”, characterized the 210 classrooms (20.59%) with higher mean student usage and teacher usage of all indicators."
