## Evidence

### Chou E 2026

Chou E, Cooper D, Goel P, Tang J, Henkle G, Zemeida S, Wolfson H, Shyu YL, Chang E, Abram A, Gerriets VA, Cusick JK. (2026). The use of voiceovers and review games in teaching immunology. ImmunoHorizons. https://doi.org/10.1093/immhor/vlag042

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple linear regression on VARK learning-preference survey responses stratified by matriculation year (n = 7, 12, and 41 for 2021, 2022, and 2023). The study reports that "matriculation year explained 7.8% of the variation in preference for visual learning and 9.3% of the variation in preference for auditory learning."

> "Simple linear regression analysis indicated a statistically significant decreasing trend in preferences for visual and auditory learning during the study. Matriculation year explained 7.8% of the variation in preference for visual learning and 9.3% of the variation in preference for auditory learning (P = 0.030 and 0.018, respectively), indicating that learning preferences for students participating in the study changed over time throughout the duration of this study."
