`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap SEM mediation analysis (10,000 resamples, 95% CI) in Mplus 8.3 with gender, age, school, and grade as controls. The study reports a "standardized indirect association of 0.077" through mathematics anxiety.

> "mathematics anxiety had a significant mediating association between teacher support and mathematics achievement, with a standardized indirect association of 0.077, p < 0.01, 95% CI [0.028, 0.135]"
