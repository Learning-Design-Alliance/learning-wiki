`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup meta-analysis pooling two studies of Ethiopian schoolchildren. The review reports underweight children "had a pooled effect size of 0.605 (95% CI: 0.462, 0.749)" and were less likely to achieve high academic scores.

> "Children who were underweight had a pooled effect size of 0.605 (95% CI: 0.462, 0.749) [20, 26], suggesting a notable link to their academic performance. Two studies indicated that underweight students were less likely to achieve high academic scores."
