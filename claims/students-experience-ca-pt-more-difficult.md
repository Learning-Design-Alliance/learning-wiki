## Evidence

### Van Wijk EV 2024

Van Wijk EV, Donkers J, De Laat PCJ, Meiboom AA, Jacobs B, Ravesloot JH, Tio RA, Van Der Vleuten CPM, Langers AMJ, Bremers AJA. (2024). Computer Adaptive vs. Non-adaptive Medical Progress Testing: Feasibility, Test Performance, and Student Experiences. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1345

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire of 526 students (response rate 31.7%) after the CA-PT: "Almost 80%" found it more difficult; open comments (n = 422) most often mentioned missing the option to go back (n = 112) and the question-mark option (n = 87). Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "Almost 80% of the students experienced the CA-PT as more difficult compared to the conventional PT. A total of 76% of students did not think they performed better on the CA-PT, and 24% of students thought that the CA-PT was better adjusted to their level (Figure 3)."
