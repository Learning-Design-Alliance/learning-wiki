Evidence¶

Loyens 2008¶

Loyens, S. M. M., & Gijbels, D. (2008). Understanding the effects of constructivist learning environments: introducing a multi-directional approach. Instructional Science, 36, 351–357. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9059-4

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Summary of the Gijbels, Segers, and Struyf study, which examined the influence of a constructivist learning environment on students' perceptions of assessment demands and approaches to learning, and how changes in approaches relate to changes in assessment demands. No effect size is printed.

"Results demonstrated that a course designed according to constructivist principles led to more deep-level assessment demands. However, this change in perceptions did not inﬂuence students’ approaches to learning, since students reported more frequent use of surface approaches to learning during the course"
