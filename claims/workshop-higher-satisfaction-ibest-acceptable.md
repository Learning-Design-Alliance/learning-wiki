## Evidence

### Richmond Helen 2016

Richmond Helen, Hall Amanda M., Hansen Zara, Williamson Esther, Davies David, Lamb Sarah E. (2016). Using mixed methods evaluation to assess the feasibility of online clinical training in evidence based interventions: a case study of cognitive behavioural treatment for low back pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0683-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Single-item satisfaction measure (1–5) in the exploratory RCT (n=16 workshop, n=15 iBeST); "statistically significant between group difference of nearly 1-point on the 5-point scale in favour of the workshop group", printed effect size −1.57; most iBeST users were 'satisfied'.

> "We observed a statistically significant between group difference of nearly 1-point on the 5-point scale in favour of the workshop group (MD 0.95, 95 % CI 95 % CI 0.52, 1.39). The majority of iBeST users were ‘satisfied’, and the majority of workshop participants were ‘very satisfied’."
