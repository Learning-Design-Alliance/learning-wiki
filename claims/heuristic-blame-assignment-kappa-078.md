Adaeze Nwaigwe developed a heuristic solution to the assignment-of-blame problem to support educational data mining. The article reports the best heuristic "agreed well (kappa=0.78) with human coders" and that Andes3 adopted it, blaming rules via small edits, user corrections, or replacement steps of the same type.

> "She studied several heuristics, and found that the best one agreed well (kappa=0.78) with human coders (Nwaigwe et al. 2007)."
