## Evidence

### Sehrish Basir Nizamani 2026

Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Zannah Ziew, Saad Nizamani, and Khyati Goyal. (2026). Less Deliberate in Teams: Student LLM Use Across Individual and Collaborative Work. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30860 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.30860](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.30860)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multi-select survey measure of prompting techniques across individual and team assignments; the quote reports the printed effect size "V= 0.324, medium effect". On team assignments the proportion reporting no prompting technique rose to 29.7% while copy-paste debugging fell to 6.5%.

> "The distribution of prompting techniques shifted substantially between individual and team contexts (𝜒 2(5)= 82.12,𝑝<. 0001,𝑉= 0.324, medium effect). On individual assignments, students most commonly employed iterative refinement (27.9%), single direct questions (28.7%), and copy-paste debugging (17.7%), with only 7.3% reporting no technique."
