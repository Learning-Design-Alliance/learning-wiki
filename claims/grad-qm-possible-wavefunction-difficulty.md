## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student understanding of quantum mechanics at the beginning of graduate instruction. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06660](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06660)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey Question 3 asked which of three wavefunctions are allowed for an electron in a one-dimensional infinite square well. "Only 34% gave t he correct answer for all three wavefunctions"; 45% accepted the explicit superposition but rejected A sin³(πx/a).

> "Seventy-nine percent of the students could identify that the second wavefunction is a possible wavefunction because it is exp licitly written in the form of a linear superposition of stationary states. Only 34% gave t he correct answer for all three wavefunctions."
