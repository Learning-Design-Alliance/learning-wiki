## Evidence

### Almeida Debora 2020

Almeida Debora, Clark Carol, Jones Michael, McConnell Phillip, Williams Jonathan. (2020). Consistency and variability in human performance during simulate infant CPR: a reliability study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-020-00785-y)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-day MDC95% analysis (MDC = 1.96 x SEM x sqrt 2) from the same three-trial within-day dataset; Table 1 gives MDC values of 2.64 mm (depth), 9.61 cpm (rate), 6.68% (duty cycle) and 0.64 kg (leaning), with the study reporting values "slightly higher for duty cycle (15%) and residual leaning (22%)".

> "MDC values were low for compression depth and compression rate (6 and 9%) and slightly higher for duty cycle (15%) and residual leaning (22%)."
