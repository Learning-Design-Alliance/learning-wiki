### Yuang Wei 2024

Yuang Wei, Yuan-Hao Jiang, Jiayi Liu, Changyong Qi, Linzhao Jia, Rui Jia. (2024). The Advancement of Personalized Learning Potentially Accelerated by Generative AI. SITE 2025 - Orlando, Florida, United States. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.00691](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.00691)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Pankiewicz and Baker, a study using GPT-3.5 to generate personalized hints for programming assignments that were positively received by students and improved their task-solving abilities; the same study found lower initial success without hints. No effect size is printed.

> "However,  students showed a lower success  rate initially when switched to more complex  tasks without GPT-
generated hints, indicating potential reliance on GAI"
