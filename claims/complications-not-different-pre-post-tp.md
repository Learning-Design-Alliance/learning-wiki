## Evidence

### Cheng X 2025

Cheng X, Zhang J, Huang G, Zhou J, Xiong J. (2025). Evaluation of the learning curve for ultrasound-guided distal radial artery cannulation in novices using a cumulative sum analysis: A prospective observational study. Medicine 2025;104:46(e45962). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000045962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null comparison of puncture-related complications (local hematoma, one vasospasm) across the two phases of the 180-procedure cohort; no neurological complications or puncture site infections were observed in either group.

> "However, the incidence of complications was not significantly different between the pre-TP group (6.7%; 95% CI: 1.9–16.2%) and the post-TP group (5.0%; 95% CI: 2.3–10.4%) (P = .253)."
