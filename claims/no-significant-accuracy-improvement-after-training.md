## Evidence

### Moura Sofia 2014

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Diagnostic accuracy of microscopy on 1,028 samples with matched qPCR results (516 pre-course, 512 post-course), analysed by Pearson chi-square and Fisher's exact test. Pooled P values were 0.869 (sensitivity) and 0.792 (specificity).

> "Some laboratories increased sensitivity and specificity and others decreased but none of these variances were significant as shown in Table 5."
