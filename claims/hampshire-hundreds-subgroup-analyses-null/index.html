Sandra McNally 2014¶

Sandra McNally, Amy Challen, Gill Wyness, Anne West, Philip Noden. (2014). Hampshire Hundreds Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581163

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Separate intention-to-treat regressions for each subgroup (Table 7), each controlling for baseline score and missing-data dummies, with standard errors clustered by school (24). All eight subgroup effect sizes were between 0.003 and 0.025 with 95% confidence intervals spanning zero.

"In every case, the point estimate is zero with a wide confidence interval. Once again, w e are unable to say whether the treatment has a positive or negative effect, although we can rule out medium to large positive effects."
