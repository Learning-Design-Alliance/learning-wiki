## Evidence

### Fiona Draxler 2023

Fiona Draxler, Henrike Weingärtner, Maximiliane Windl, Albrecht Schmidt, and Lewis L. Chuang. (2023). Useful but Distracting: Keyword Highlights and Time-Synchronization in Captions for Language Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 61 respondents recruited via university mailing lists on caption usage habits. The authors conclude that "only a few intentionally do so for language learning", pointing to an opportunity to increase learning motivation by adapting caption design.

> "Overall, the survey highlights that subtitles and captions are frequently used. Most participants in our sample activate subtitles for better comprehension, whereas only a few intentionally do so for language learning."
