### Qin Xingna 2026

Qin Xingna, Li Simeng, Sjögren Björn, Ren Ping, Thornberg Robert. (2026). The Role of Parental Influences in Chinese Adolescents’ Academic Achievement through Shaping Friendship Network Dynamics. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02306-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive network analysis of four waves (Table 2) from about 1,419 students. The article reports Moran's I of 0.19–0.26, "a positive correlation between friends’ academic achievement", reciprocity of approximately "50%–53%", transitivity 31%–36%, and Jaccard indices of 0.34–0.36.

> "Moran’s I ranged from 0.19 to.26 for academic achievement across the four waves, which indicates a positive correlation between friends’ academic achievement."
