Melinda Wallace and Sheila Arredondo 2022¶

Melinda Wallace and Sheila Arredondo. (2022). A Standardized, Equitable, and Transparent High-Quality Instructional Materials Review Process. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628368

Outcomes section of the report describes how NDE worked with R15CC and the three RPDPs so that "small and rural districts, that may lack subject matter experts, to be represented by their respective RPDP." Descriptive program account, no outcome data.

"Through their work with the R15CC, NDE was able to overcome this challenge by leveraging the expertise of regional coordinators from the Northeast, Northwest, and Southern Regional Professional Development Programs (RPDPs) to adopt instructional materials in multiple content areas each year."
