## Evidence

### Takako Nomi 2010

Takako Nomi, Elaine M. Allensworth. (2010). The Effects of Tracking with Supports on Instructional Climate and Student Outcomes in High School Algebra. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/effects-tracking-supports-instructional-climate-and-student-outcomes-high-school

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey-based Rasch measures of interactive pedagogy in the cross-cohort discontinuity analysis. The article attributes this to professional development, curricular resources, and added instructional time; compositional controls did not change the coefficients.

> "Post-policy, below-norm students reported much more frequent use of interactive pedagogy than below-norm students pre-policy, the difference in the post-policy changes between below- and above-norm students was .55 SD (P<.001)."
