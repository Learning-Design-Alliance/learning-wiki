# Restricted item pools show higher item use rates than all-grade pools, and use rates drop sharply for off-grade simulees

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In mathematics tests, item use was higher with the on-grade pool (mean 53.7%) than the ±1-grade (46.3%) or all-grade (39.5%) pools, and use rates were relatively lower in simulations with off-grade simulees. [→ Li 2019](#li-2019)

## Evidence

### Li 2019

Li, S., & Meyer, J. P. (2019). Simulation study for evaluating MAP® Growth™ grade-level item pools. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/simulation-study-for-evaluating-map-growth-item-pools-with-grade-level-constraints/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pool utilization analysis (Appendix D) of the percentage of pool items administered per condition. Reading followed a similar pattern (48.4% vs 43.3% vs 37.1%); language usage had the highest rates (60.8% vs 53.9% vs 42.3%). Off-grade conditions showed much lower use (e.g., mathematics 23.6%, 21.1%, 25.1%).

> "In mathematics tests, the degree of item use is higher with the on-grade item pool (mean=53.7%) than for the ±1-grade item pool (mean=46.3%) or all-grade item pool (mean=39.5%)."
