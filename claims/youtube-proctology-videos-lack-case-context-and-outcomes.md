### Mirdha S 2025

Mirdha S, Tokidis E, Le Blevec L, Wilson T. (2025). Should online, free proctology videos be used for self‐directed post‐graduate learning? A proposed evaluation using a colorectal video assessment framework. Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1111/codi.70313

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item-level analysis of the same 98-video cross-sectional assessment (Table 1 medians on a 0–2 scale). Non-peer-reviewed videos had lower medians for formal case presentation (1.0 vs 2.0, p < 0.001), "Position of patient" (1.0 vs 2.0, p < 0.001) and outcome reporting (0.0 vs 1.0, p = 0.001).

> "Overall, non‐peer‐reviewed videos scored lower across most items, particularly for case presentation, outcomes, intraoperative positioning and use of visual aids, whereas both sources performed similarly for technical depiction, anatomy, commentary and image quality (Table 1)."
