McNemar test on paired scenario responses from the same multinational survey. The article prints P = .709 and P = .541 for the null symptom effect in older patients; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The presence or absence of symptoms makes no significant change to the treatment planning decisions for older patients (case scenarios 3 and 4) by both undergraduate (P = .709) and postgraduate (P = .541) students."
