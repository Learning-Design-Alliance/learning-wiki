### Wczysla Karolina 2024

Wczysla Karolina, Sparn Moritz, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2024). There is a need for a paradigm shift in laparoscopic surgical training: results of a nationwide survey among teaching hospitals in Switzerland. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05209-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of heads of departments at all certified Swiss surgical teaching hospitals; 48 of 86 responded. The Results section reports that "Self-training sites are available at 40 (83%) hospitals" and that high-fidelity trainers are present in 16 (33%).

> "Self-training sites are available at 40 (83%) hospitals (Fig. 2). In addition to commercial (27) and homemade (7) box trainers, high-fidelity trainers are available in 16 (33%) hospitals."
