Chi-square and Cramer's V tests compared proportions of regulatory and deliberative interactions between the two cluster types. The deliberative dimension showed "a medium effect size (V = 0.26)" and the regulatory dimension a small effect size (V = 0.12), both significant at p < .001.

> "the deliberative dimension yielded a significant result (χ2 = 131.9; df = 10; p < .001) with a medium effect size (V = 0.26; df = 1). The regulatory dimension showed a significant result (χ2 = 26.8; df = 3; p < .001) with a small effect size (V = 0.12; df = 1)."
