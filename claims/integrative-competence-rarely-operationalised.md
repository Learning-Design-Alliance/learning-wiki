### Pulido Francisco Rodríguez 2026

Pulido Francisco Rodríguez, García Ángeles Rodríguez, Estebaranz Nayra Caballero. (2026). Simulation-based education in undergraduate psychiatry: a focused review and interpretive analysis of clinical competence representation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09885-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's interpretive synthesis of four studies found integrative competence was the least explicitly represented domain; the authors caution this should not be interpreted as evidence that integrative reasoning was absent from simulation-based learning activities.

> "None of the included studies operationalised biopsychosocial formulation or integrative clinical reasoning as clearly defined and independently assessed competence domains. Nevertheless, several simulation scenarios appeared sufficiently complex to require learners to synthesise symptoms, contextual influences and interpersonal information in order to understand the clinical situation."
