## Evidence

### Lord Kathryn 2017

Lord Kathryn, Rapaport Penny, Cooper Claudia, Livingston Gill. (2017). Disseminating START: training clinical psychologists and admiral nurses as trainers in a psychosocial intervention for carers of people with dementia's depressive and anxiety symptoms. BMJ Open 7(8): e017759. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-017759

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic content analysis of semistructured interviews and an admiral-nurse focus group: the team sign-up theme held that raising awareness of START among colleagues, including managers and referrers, "was critical to the implementation process".

> "Raising awareness about the START intervention and its benefits among colleagues, including those who may not be directly involved in delivering it, for example, managers and referrers, was critical to the implementation process."

### Lord Kathryn 2017 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same thematic analysis: psychology respondents cited the "existing skill set of their colleagues and their competency in delivering these types of psychological interventions" as aiding implementation.

> "Within the psychology services, many of the respondents referred to the existing skill set of their colleagues and their competency in delivering these types of psychological interventions as aiding the implementation of START"
