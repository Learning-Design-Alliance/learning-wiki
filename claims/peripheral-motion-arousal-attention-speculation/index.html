Yu 2016¶

Authors' interpretation offered in the Discussion, drawing on prior findings about vection, screen size, and arousal; the article states arousal was not measured for any test subjects, so this is an untested explanatory hypothesis rather than a measured result.

"we speculate that the large screen size of the fulldome planetarium may be interacting with the optical content flowing through the peripheral vision of audience members, to lead them to be more aroused and attentive to the presentation."
