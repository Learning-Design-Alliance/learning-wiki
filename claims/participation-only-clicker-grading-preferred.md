### M. Alamgir Hossain 2021

M. Alamgir Hossain, Petra M. Menz, and John M. Stockie. (2021). An Open-Access Clicker Question Bank for Numerical Analysis. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113](https://doi.org/10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey question Q6 asked 137 respondents their preference for computing the clicker grade; 117 chose participation-only, versus 4 for correctness-only, 7 for a combined grade and 9 for no marks. The course used an all-or-nothing 3% participation grade requiring responses to at least 75% of questions.

> "I like the current formula: participation grade only, no marks for correctness (117)"
