## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Female students showed significantly higher strategic approach scores than male students (p < 0.05, d = -0.46), with no significant gender differences on the other approach or learning environment scales. [→ Bonsaksen T. 2021](#bonsaksen-t-2021)

## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T. 2021

Bonsaksen T., Magne T. A., Stigen L., Gramstad A., Åsli L., Mørk G., Johnson S. G., Carstensen T. (2021). Associations between occupational therapy students’ academic performance and their study approaches and perceptions of the learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02940-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate gender comparison in 174 students (Table 2): strategic approach mean 73.0 (SD 10.4) for women vs 68.5 (SD 9.2) for men, Cohen's d = -0.46. Deep (d = 0.24) and surface (d = -0.20) differences were not significant.

> "There were no significant gender differences related to the learning environment scales. Compared to their male counterparts, the female students showed significantly higher scores on the strategic approach scale (p < 0.05)."
