### Carnevale 2021

Carnevale, S., Di Napoli, I., Esposito, F., & Arcidiacono, C. (2021). Drawingvoice 2.0: classroom joint designing and Facebook interactions to develop reflexivity and awareness. Education Tech Research Dev, 69, 2939–2962. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10042-3

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative finding from thematic analysis of the students' final reports, on the theme the authors call "The Web as a parallel supporting space". A student testified that Facebook "contributed to keeping the group always in contact" and "fostered comparison and dialogue through the exchange of comments and opinions".

> "At the beginning of the course, all the students underlined the risks and the criticalities regarding Facebook’s positive aspects and potential. But in the end, the social network became a real resource:"
