## Evidence

### Marušić and Sliško 2014

Mirko Marušić & Josip Sliško. (2014). High-School Students Believe School Physics Helps in Developing Logical But not Creative Thinking: Active Learning Can Change This Idea. European J of Physics Education, Volume 5 Issue 4. http://www.edpsycinteractive.org/topics/affect/humed.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of the RPQ group (N=91) using two 5-point Likert exploratory questions, analyzed with the Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test; the article reports "an overall positive shift of 11.0%" for logical thinking and 20.9% for creative thinking.

> "After the project, both the females (7.1%) and the males (17.1%) of the RPQ show positive shifts for the first exploratory question, resulting in an overall positive shift of 11.0% (Table 2)."
