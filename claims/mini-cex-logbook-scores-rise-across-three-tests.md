## Evidence

### Bagheri Sara 2025

Bagheri Sara, Sedigh Amin, Zarei Zahra, Kazemzadeh Mozhgan, Sharifi Nader. (2025). Comparison of A Mini-Clinical evaluation exercise with the routine logbook in assessing clinical competence of operating room trainees: A parallel randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07560-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive results (Table 2) on a 100-point scale from the randomized trial: Mini-CEX means 81.57 (SD 7.23), 91.87 (SD 5.43), 97.42 (SD 4.24); Logbook means 44.36, 54.87, 65.64. The article reports these as supporting "a significant improvement in student performance after the third test compared to the initial one".

> "The average scores of students in the Mini-CEX group rose from 81.57 in the initial test to 91.87 and 97.42 in the subsequent two tests. Similarly, in the Logbook group, the average scores increased from 44.36 in the first test to 54.87 and 65.64 in the second and third tests, respectively."
