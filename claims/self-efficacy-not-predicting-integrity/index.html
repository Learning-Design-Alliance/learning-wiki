Evidence¶

Sutherland et al. 2018¶

Kevin S. Sutherland, Maureen A. Conroy, Bryce D. McLeod, James Algina. (2018). Factors Associated with Teacher Delivery of a Classroom-Based Tier 2 Prevention Program. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-017-0832-y

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent growth curve regressions with the 24-item TSES subscales as pretest predictors in the 92-teacher sample. No TSES subscale significantly predicted adherence status or growth; the article reports no effect size for the null adherence findings.

"Contrary to hypotheses, teacher self-efficacy did not predict adherence, and teachers who re- ported higher initial levels of Student Engagement self- efficacy exhibited lower growth in competence over time."

Sutherland et al. 2018 (2)¶

Kevin S. Sutherland, Maureen A. Conroy, Bryce D. McLeod, James Algina. (2018). Factors Associated with Teacher Delivery of a Classroom-Based Tier 2 Prevention Program. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-017-0832-y

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural regression of the competence growth latent variable (Table 2: estimate −0.067, z = −2.017, p = .044). The article reports the standardized coefficient indicated a moderate relationship but prints no effect size value.

"The regression coefficient for Student Engagement was statis- tically significant and negative and, based on the stan- dardized regression coefficient, moderate in size."
