### Rachele Toniolo 2026

Rachele Toniolo, Anita Zanella, Andrea Cottinelli, Giovanni Liuzzi, Sara Ricciardi, Massimo Grassi, Stefano Delle Monache. (2026). AMACA: Astronomy education with a Multi-sensory, Accessible, and Circular Approach. International Journal of Science Education, Part B: Communication and Public Engagement. https://doi.org/10.1080/21548455.2025.2594048

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative focus group at the end of the Phase 4 teacher training, gathering perceptions of flipped roles, the hands-on approach, and multi-sensory teaching. Teachers also valued seeing the workshops in action rather than purely theoretical training.

> "During the discussion, attendees showed high satisfaction with the flipped roles approach, with which HS students become teachers and vice versa."

### Rachele Toniolo 2026 (2)

Rachele Toniolo, Anita Zanella, Andrea Cottinelli, Giovanni Liuzzi, Sara Ricciardi, Massimo Grassi, Stefano Delle Monache. (2026). AMACA: Astronomy education with a Multi-sensory, Accessible, and Circular Approach. International Journal of Science Education, Part B: Communication and Public Engagement. https://doi.org/10.1080/21548455.2025.2594048

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Focus-group finding reported by the authors: the anxiety, in one teacher's words, pushes you out of your comfort zone, resulting in greater empathy for students who experience similar emotions in daily school life. Teachers were also positively surprised that students performed well without marks.

> "For some, this approach led to anxiety, stemming from the fear of not mastering the topic while presenting it to their classes."
