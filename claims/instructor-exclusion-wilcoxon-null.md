### Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe 2015

Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe. (2015). The impact of social integration on student persistence in introductory Modeling Instruction courses. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Methodological pre-analysis on the Fall 2014 M-MI network data comparing network medians with and without instructional staff; no significant differences except closeness, for which two cases (with and without instructors) were therefore analyzed. Equivalence was not tested, only difference.

> "Using the Wilcoxon rank-sum test we found no evidence for a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerences be- tween the two population medians (i.e., with and with- out instructors) for all centralities but closeness."
