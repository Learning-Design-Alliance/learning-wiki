## Evidence

### Grüter Alexander A. J. 2024

Grüter Alexander A. J., Daams Freek, Bonjer Hendrik J., van Duijvendijk Peter, Tuynman Jurriaan B. (2024). Surgical quality assessment of critical view of safety in 283 laparoscopic cholecystectomy videos by surgical residents and surgeons. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10873-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Categorized-score agreement analysis of the 283 double-scored videos, with a McNemar test on the 0–4 versus 5–6 split; the discordant-direction difference was "statistically significant with a P value of 0.02", with residents assigning 0–4 in 63% of discordant cases, suggesting they "score more cautiously".

> "In 71.0% of these cases, there was agreement that both the surgeon and the surgical resident had assigned a total score of 0 to 4, or both had given a total score of 5 or 6."
