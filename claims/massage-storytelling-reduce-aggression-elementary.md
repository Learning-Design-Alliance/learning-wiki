## Evidence

### Gonçalves Lia Lopes 2017

Gonçalves Lia Lopes, Voos Mariana Callil, de Almeida Maria Helena Morgani, Caromano Fátima Aparecida. (2017). Massage and Storytelling Reduce Aggression and Improve Academic Performance in Children Attending Elementary School. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/5087145

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing aggressive-behavior diary records before (April/May) and after (September/October) interventions across three classroom groups (n = 35 each). The article reports a "significant interaction between groups and periods" with F2,117 = 23.49; p < 0.001; effect size = 0.287.

> "We found a significant interaction between groups and periods, with F2,117 = 23.49; p < 0.001; effect size = 0.287."

### Gonçalves Lia Lopes 2017 (2)

Gonçalves Lia Lopes, Voos Mariana Callil, de Almeida Maria Helena Morgani, Caromano Fátima Aparecida. (2017). Massage and Storytelling Reduce Aggression and Improve Academic Performance in Children Attending Elementary School. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/5087145

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post hoc Tukey tests on the same ANOVA of teacher and coordinator diary records. The massage group (p < 0.001) and storytelling group (p = 0.002) each had significantly fewer episodes than the control group; no effect size is printed for these pairwise contrasts.

> "The post hoc Tukey test showed that the number of episodes of aggressive behaviors was significantly lower in MG (p < 0.001) and SG (p = 0.002) compared to the CG after the interventions (Figure 2)."
