Michael Addo 2026¶

Michael Addo, Nana Maryam Munagah, Victor Kumbol, Judith Uchidiuno, and George Boateng. (2026). “Why SuaCode?”: Understanding African Students’ Motivations for Taking a Smartphone-Based Online Coding Course. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22940

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Comparison drawn in the results/discussion of the 450-response SuaCode survey against the Estonian study by White et al., which the article cites. The 4% figure is a descriptive code frequency; no effect size is printed.

"We found that the code "free, understandable, and accessible content" (4%) ranked lower compared to other motivation-based studies. White et al. in an Estonian study [19], found that students cited the cost and accessibility of online programs as one of the three most marked reasons for choosing to join a MOOC."
