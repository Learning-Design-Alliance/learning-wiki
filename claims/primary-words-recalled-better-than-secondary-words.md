## Evidence

### Kate M. Xu 2024

Kate M. Xu, Sarah Coertjens, Florence Lespiau, Kim Ouwehand, Hanke Korpershoek, Fred Paas, David C. Geary. (2024). An Evolutionary Approach to Motivation and Learning: Differentiating Biologically Primary and Secondary Knowledge. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09880-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test within a randomized controlled trial in which 144 first-year psychology students learned 32 Dutch-fictional word pairs, half primary and half secondary. The article reports "recalled more primary knowledge words than secondary knowledge words" with d = 0.49.

> "They also recalled more primary knowledge words than secondary knowledge words (t = 5.85, p < 0.001, d = 0.49; M = 7.29, SD = 3.99 for primary knowledge words; M = 6.08, SD = 4.17 for secondary knowledge words)."
