## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006

S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman. (2006). Exploring Student Understanding of Energy through the Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1063/1.2177024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2177024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interviews in which students worked through the QMCS reflection question thinking out loud; the authors argue seven of the 15 interviewees held a robust misconception that reflection is caused by electrons having a range of energies.

> "Of the 15 students interviewed, we argue that seven had a robust misconception that reﬂection at a barrier is caused by electrons having a range of energies."

### S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman 2006 (2)

S. B. McKagan and C. E. Wieman. (2006). Exploring Student Understanding of Energy through the Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey. [doi:10.1063/1.2177024](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2177024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

When the interviewer asked how students reconciled answer B with the statement that all the electrons have the same energy, three students stuck by their answer, two changed to the correct answer C, two to A, and one to D.

> "In response to this question, three students stuck by their answer, giving detailed justiﬁcations, two students changed their answers to C (the correct answer), two students changed to A, and one student changed to D."
