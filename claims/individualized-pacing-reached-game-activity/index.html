Klubal et al. 2017¶

Libor Klubal, Katerina Kostolanyova and Vojtěch Gybas. (2017). Using Tablet and iTunesU as Individualized Instruction Tools. 13th International Conference Mobile Learning 2017. ISBN: 978-989-8533-61-6. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579278

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational account of the experimental lesson by a teacher and a non-involved researcher. Students worked at their own pace through three activities; by 39 minutes "all of the students were playing the mathematical game".

"With 39 minutes having elapsed, all of the students were playing the mathematical game."

Klubal et al. 2017 (2)¶

Libor Klubal, Katerina Kostolanyova and Vojtěch Gybas. (2017). Using Tablet and iTunesU as Individualized Instruction Tools. 13th International Conference Mobile Learning 2017. ISBN: 978-989-8533-61-6. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED579278

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational account of the parallel frontal lesson. The worksheet task ran to 42 minutes, and with "only 3 minutes left" the teacher skipped the following activity; the game was only handed out at the end.

"The first students finished the tasks 42 minutes into the class. As there were only 3 minutes left, the teacher decided not to proceed to the following activity."
