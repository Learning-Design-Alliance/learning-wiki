## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Improvement or selection? A longitudinal analysis of students' views about experimental physics in their lab courses. arXiv:1706.03681. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pseudo-longitudinal pre/post comparison of the full population in each course (Table III). The article reports "all courses show a negative or neutral shift from pre- to postinstruction, with no positive shifts," with significant shifts for PHYS 1140 and 2150 (Mann-Whitney U p < 0.05).
