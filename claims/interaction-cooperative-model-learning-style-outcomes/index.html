Sugiharto 2015¶

Sugiharto. (2015). The Effect of Cooperative Learning Model and Kolb Learning Styles on Learning Result of The Basics of Politics. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.21. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079121

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANOVA of the 2x2 quasi-experiment (Table 4) shows the interaction source with JK 447.47 and RJK 447.47, the largest effect in the table; the article states it is "proven that there is an interaction effect between learning models and styles of learning". Figure 1 plots the crossing means.

"Based on the results of the second hypothesis testing, the value acquired of F calculate = 61.67 > F table = 3.98 with α = 0.05, so that H o is rejected and H 1 is accepted. Therefore it is proven that there is an interaction effect between learning models and styles of learning on students learning outcomes of the basics of political science"
