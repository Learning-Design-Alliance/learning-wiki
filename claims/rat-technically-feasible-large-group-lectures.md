### Punja R 2026

Punja R, Punja D, Prasad Kamachi Christdoss Ruben. (2026). Large-group virtual anatomy via a smart digital podium: a preliminary cross-sectional study of student perspectives toward remote anatomage teaching in a medical lecture hall. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10019-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study at Kasturba Medical College, Manipal, India, streaming the Anatomage interface over institutional LAN to a smart digital podium for a 47-minute lecture to 125 first-year MBBS students. The session ran without "technical interruption" or "connectivity failure." No inferential statistics were performed given the single-arm, single-session design.

> "The RAT session was delivered successfully without technical interruption. The Anatomage Table maintained a stable LAN connection to the smart digital podium throughout the 47-minute lecture."
