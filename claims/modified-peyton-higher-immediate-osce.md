## Evidence

### Liu Bingyan 2026

Liu Bingyan, Xiang Shufang, Xiao Baolai, Xiang Jinjian, Sun Yimin, Ren Yuan. (2026). Application of a modified Peyton’s four-step approach in clinical skills training for residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09499-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Randomized controlled trial of 120 residents randomized to modified (n = 60) or traditional (n = 60) Peyton training in wound dressing and suture removal; post-training OSCE totals on a 100-point scale showed the experimental group “higher than those of the control group” (t = 3.20, P = 0.002 in Table 5).

> "After training, the OSCE scores of the experimental group (94.80 ± 3.02) were higher than those of the control group (93.15 ± 2.62)."
