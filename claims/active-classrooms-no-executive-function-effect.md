# Active classrooms show no clear effect on executive function or fluid intelligence

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pooled effects on executive function are small and negative (SMD = -0.04) with a CI including no effect and possible benefit; fluid intelligence shows close to no effect. [→ Bedard Chloe 2019](#bedard-chloe-2019)

## Evidence

### Bedard Chloe 2019

Bedard Chloe, St John Laura, Bremer Emily, Graham Jeffrey D., Cairney John. (2019). A systematic review and meta-analysis on the effects of physically active classrooms on educational and enjoyment outcomes in school age children. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0218633

`q3 · i?`

Meta-analysis of three studies (n = 112) on executive functions (inhibition, cognitive flexibility, working memory) pooled to SMD = -0.04 (-0.41 to 0.34); two studies of fluid intelligence (n = 61) showed a mean difference of 0.38 (-3.21 to 3.96). No effect sizes beyond these were printed.

> "Pooled estimates show small negative effects of active classrooms compared to traditional classrooms on measures of EF (SMD = -0.04), however the CI include no effect and possible benefit. Two other studies used Raven’s Standard Progressive matrices to measure fluid intelligence and found close to no effect of active classrooms"
