## Evidence

### Södersved Källestedt 2025

Södersved Källestedt, M.-L., & Hidefjäll, P. (2025). A mixed methods study of self-directed learning in clinical practice using a mobile skills training system. BMC Medical Education, 25, 1515. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08127-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational checklist data from the mobile skills training system. Among healthcare professionals performing a care task more than once, approved performance increased from 59% at first training to 69.6% at last training (Chi-square test p < .01).

> "For those who chose to practice the same care task twice, it appeared that they increased their ability to perform the task (see Table 3)."
