Seeskin 2020¶

Seeskin, A., Mahaffie, S., & Usher, A. (2020). The forgotten year: Applying lessons from Freshman success to Sophomore year. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-forgotten-year

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 2014-15 freshmen by graduation status (Figure 9) shows the three warning indicators flagged "most (70 percent)" of eventual non-graduates. The report also notes 17 percent of 2018 graduates had one or more warning indicators, so flags are not destiny.
