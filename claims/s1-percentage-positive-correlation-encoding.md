# Percentage of stage 1 sleep positively correlates with encoding performance in an exploratory analysis

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In an exploratory analysis, the percentage of S1 sleep correlated positively with multiple-choice test performance (r = 0.42, p = 0.030). [→ Vidal 2025](#vidal-2025)

## Evidence

### Vidal 2025

Vidal, V., Pretel, M. R., Capurro, L., Tassone, L. M., Moyano, M. D., Malacari, R. G., Brusco, L. I., Ballarini, F. M. & Forcato, C. (2025). Short naps improve subsequent learning in a high school setting. npj Science of Learning 10:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00307-4

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory Pearson correlation in the napping group (n = 26) between percentage of stage 1 sleep and test score, printing "r = 0.42, p = 0.030". Alpha and theta power in S1 showed no significant correlation with performance.

> "we found a signiﬁcant positive correlation between the percentage of S1 and performance on the multiple-choice test (Fig. 2c, r = 0.42, p = 0.030)"
