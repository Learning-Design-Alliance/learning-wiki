## Evidence

### Thompson 2026

Thompson, M. B., Gandhi, V., Richardson-Newton, A., & Campitelli, G. (2026). Detecting expertise in decision making under pressure: a virtual reality assessment environment and empirical evaluation. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications, 11, 6. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-025-00695-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis in Study 1 (N=60) examined DMX Score against CRT, NCS, and AOMTB. While Tickets correlated significantly with CRT (r(58) = 0.323, p = 0.012), "DMX did not correlate with the other cognitive measures", with DMX-CRT at r(58) = 0.063 and DMX-NCS at r(58) = 0.091. These null correlations carry no tested effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The fact that Tickets and DMX have a medium size correlation (r(58) = 0.593) suggests that DMX is measuring something different than Tickets and that different component is not related to cognitive reflection or the general abilities involved in solving the CRT."
