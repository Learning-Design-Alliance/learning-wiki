## Evidence

### Kim Stienstra 2024

Kim Stienstra, Antonie Knigge & Ineke Maas. (2024). Gene-environment interaction analysis of school quality and educational inequality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00225-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Final ACE-moderation model (Model 4, Table 2) on the Dutch twin administrative sample. Adding parental SES as moderator reduces the school-SES genetic moderation: "a reduction of 40%", with the remaining effect statistically significant, suggesting school-based processes contribute beyond family SES.

> "the genetic moderation effect by school SES becomes smaller in magnitude (a reduction of 40%) but a substantial part remains and is statistically signiﬁcant"
