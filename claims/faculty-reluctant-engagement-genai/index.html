Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026¶

Qusay H. Mahmoud. (2026). Judgment-Centred Software Engineering Education: A Post-Hype Review and Framework for AI-Augmented Learning. Preprint, September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29473 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.29473

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The review reports Saravanan et al.'s qualitative study of three focus groups with 16 engineering faculty at a single U.S. research-intensive university; participants described "a posture of reluctant engagement with GenAI" and anticipated curricular redesign.

"Participants described a posture of reluctant engagement with GenAI, concerns about cognitive offloading and loss of productive struggle, disruption to established feedback and assessment practices, and frustration with gaps in coordinated institutional policy."
