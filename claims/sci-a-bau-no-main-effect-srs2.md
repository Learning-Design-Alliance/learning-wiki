## Evidence

### Janine P. Stichter 2018

Janine P. Stichter, Melissa J. Herzog, Stephen P. Kilgus, and Alexander M. Schoemann. (2018). Exploring the Moderating Effects of Cognitive Abilities on Social Competence Intervention Outcomes. Behavior Modification. https://doi.org/10.1177/0145445517698654

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel models of the 4-year cluster randomized trial (students nested within schools; 146 SCI-A vs 128 BAU assessed at T1) tested main effects on SRS-2 subscales at T2. The article reports "not significantly different between conditions" on most subscales; Table 2 shows Cognition favored BAU (d = 0.31, p = .013).

> "There was a general pattern across subscales, whereby T2 SRS-2 scores were not significantly different between conditions, indicating no clear efficacy differences for SCI-A versus BAU."
