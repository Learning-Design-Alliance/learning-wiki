## Subclaims

`q3 i?` Pre-test (K1) scores showed no differences between treatment groups (KW: Chi-Square = 2.769, df = 3, p = 0.429) or between genders (MWU: Z = −0.864, p = 0.388). [→ Conradty 2016 (2)](#conradty-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Conradty 2016

Conradty, C. & Bogner, F. X. (2016). Hypertext or Textbook: Effects on Motivation and Gain in Knowledge. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci6030029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Friedman test on the control group's (n=55) knowledge scores across the three test points (K1, K2, K3), showing no significant change without instruction.

> "There was no test effect as the control group did not gain knowledge (FT: Chi-Square = 2.995, df = 2, p = 0.224)."

### Conradty 2016 (2)

Conradty, C. & Bogner, F. X. (2016). Hypertext or Textbook: Effects on Motivation and Gain in Knowledge. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci6030029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney U tests on pre-test (K1) scores confirming baseline equivalence across the three treatment groups and between genders.

> "The K1 showed neither differences between treatment groups (KW Chi-Square = 2.769, df = 3, p = 0.429) nor between the genders (MWU: Z = −0.864, p = 0.388)."
