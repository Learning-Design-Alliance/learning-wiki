## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2022

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Wolters Alissa, Mercado Janet, & Quinn Jamie. (2022). Crosslinguistic Transfer of Higher Order Cognitive Skills and Their Roles in Writing for English-Spanish Dual Language Learners. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000516

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Covariate results of the multiple group model (about half the sample, n = 179, were biliterate). In the English immersion group the article reports being biliterate was significantly associated with higher order cognitive skill (.22, p = .028). The authors state explanations for this finding are not clear.

> "Biliterate students had higher scores in higher order cognitive skills for students in the dual immersion program (.19, p = .004)."
