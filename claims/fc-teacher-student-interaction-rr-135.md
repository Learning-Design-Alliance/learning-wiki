# Flipped classroom improves teacher-student interaction (pooled RR 1.35), based on few studies

## Evidence

### Zheng Hui 2026

Zheng Hui, Ouyang Yun, Zeng Yifei, He Xinyu, Song Liping, Yu Xiangyuan. (2026). Systematic evaluation of the teaching effectiveness of flipped classroom mode in undergraduate preventive medicine courses in China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896815

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Fixed-effects meta-analysis of 3 studies comparing teacher-student interaction; heterogeneity was negligible (I2 < 0.1%, p = 0.952). The article itself notes "the limited number of studies" underlying this estimate and prints no standardized effect size.
