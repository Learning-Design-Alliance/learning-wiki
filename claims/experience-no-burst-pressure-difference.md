## Evidence

### Spagnulo R 2025

Spagnulo R, Marzola F, Corso F, Distefano G, Pescio M, Hayashi K, Barontini F, Dagnino G, Althoefer K, Siciliano B, Ourselin S, Arezzo A. (2025). From precision to strength: computer vision for suture quality assessment—an ex vivo pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12441-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null comparison across the three experience strata in the same 15-participant pilot (45 procedures). Kruskal–Wallis tests found "no significant differences in pressure performance" (p = 0.54 with outliers; p = 0.65 without); equivalence was not tested. Power analysis indicated low sensitivity under the current sample.

> "Stratification by operator experience (Beginner, Advanced, Master) revealed no significant differences in pressure performance, both with (p = 0.54) and without (p = 0.65) two outliers"
