## Evidence

### Kaakinen 2021

Kaakinen, J. K. (2021). What Can Eye Movements Tell us about Visual Perception Processes in Classroom Contexts? Commentary on a Special Issue. Educational Psychology Review, 33, 169-179. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09573-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The commentary reports, citing Rosengrant et al. in the Special Issue, that students' gaze on the lecturer or notes was coded as engaged and gaze elsewhere (e.g., cell phone) as off-task, and that the distribution of these instances over time revealed how engagement fluctuated. No effect size is printed.

> "As indicated by the results of Rosengrant and colleagues, looking at fluctuation of engagement as it evolves across time instead of averaging, for example, on-task and off-task instances across the whole duration of the task provides novel insights into students ' behavior and learning."
