Albart Stephenie Ann, Looi Irene. (2025). Knowledge, practice, and facilitating factors of evidence-based medicine among Malaysian doctors: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06865-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item results on EBM information sources (Table 2) in the 409-doctor study. The text reports "Half of the respondents used EBM journals such as BMJ EBM and BMJ Clinical Evidence in clinical decision-making" while website awareness and usage were poor; the authors attribute this to limited historical access, time constraints, and possibly insufficient online searching skills.

> "However, the usage of EBM websites for clinical decision-making was very low, ranging from 3.2% to 11.7%. More than half of them were unaware of the existence of these websites (53.8%–72.9%)."
