## Evidence

### Heni Martin 2012

Heni Martin, Lammerding-Köppel Maria, Celebi Nora, Shiozawa Thomas, Riessen Reimer, Nikendei Christoph, Weyrich Peter. (2012). Focused didactic training for skills lab student tutors – which techniques are considered helpful? GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 29(3), Doc41. [doi:10.3205/zma000811](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000811)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Global single-choice rating from the retrospective survey asking which of the five modules most improved didactic qualification; counts are as printed (9, 5, 3, 2, 1 of 20 respondents). No inferential test was reported.

> "nearly half (9 out of 20) chose “How to present effectively”, five chose “How to convey practical skills effectively with Peyton’s Method”, followed by “How to explain precisely” (3 out of 20), and “How to deal with incidents” (2 of 20). One participant chose the part “How to explain on impulse”."
