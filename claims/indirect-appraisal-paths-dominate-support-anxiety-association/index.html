Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 resamples, 95% bias-corrected CIs) in AMOS on the 547-student sample. The article reports "the unstandardized total eﬀect was −0.566" with indirect effects of 69.4% (69.5% standardized), and all three specific indirect paths significant at p < 0.01.

"the unstandardized total eﬀect was −0.566, comprising a direct eﬀect of −0.173 and total indirect eﬀect of −0.393; the latter represented 69.4% of the total."
