## Evidence

### Burm Sarah 2020

Burm Sarah, Sebok-Syer Stefanie S., Van Koughnett Julie Ann, Watling Christopher J. (2020). Are we generating more assessments without added value? Surgical trainees’ perceptions of and receptiveness to cross-specialty assessment. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00594-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the four trainee focus groups. Some trainees saw the assessor relationship as transactional, while others said established relationships made it easier for "internalizing the feedback"; all emphasized trust in the assessor's ability to perform and teach the task.

> "We discovered that trainees did not necessarily care who completed assessments for learning, so long as the person provided what they perceived as credible and constructive feedback."
