## Evidence

### Carmen Fies and Chris Packham 2022

Carmen Fies and Chris Packham. (2022). Technology to scale up diversity in astronomy education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.11129](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.11129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive participation analysis across all four program years, reported in Figure 1 as percent of total participants. The authors state teachers joined from districts "spanning Texas east-to-west and north-to-south", at urban and rural schools in mostly less affluent districts.

> "The majority of participants (48%) are current STEM teachers in secondary schools, followed by future secondary STEM teachers (29%). Similarly, of the primary school teachers, more were already teaching (18%) than preparing to teach (5%)"
