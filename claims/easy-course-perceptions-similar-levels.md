## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Open-ended response patterns about what makes a course easy do not differ significantly between 100-level and 300-level students. [→ Wyse 2018](#wyse-2018)

## Evidence

### Wyse 2018

Wyse, S. A., & Soneral, P. A. G. (2018). “Is This Class Hard?” Defining and Analyzing Academic Rigor from a Learner’s Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0278

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Open-ended survey coding of easy-course attributions from the same 100-level (n = 155) and 300-level (n = 39) samples; chi-square comparison found no significant difference (χ2 = 2.46, df = 5, p = 0.7825). Both groups cited appropriate background, manageable workload, high faculty support, logical content, and alignment.

> "The factors identified by 100- and 300-level students about what constitutes an easy course were similar (χ2 = 2.46, df = 5, p = 0.7825; Figure 3)."
