## Subclaims
`q1 i?` M&I outperformed traditional students on topics where it spent more lecture time (magnetostatics, 24% vs 12%) but also on topics where it spent equal or less time (electrostatics 36% vs 38%, DC circuits 15% vs 25%, Faraday's Law 6% vs 11%). [→ Kohlmyer 2009](#kohlmyer-2009)
