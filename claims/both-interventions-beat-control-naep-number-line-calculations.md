## Evidence

### Fuchs 2015

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., Hamlett, C. L., Jordan, N. C., Siegler, R., Gersten, R., & Changas, P. (2015). Supported Self-Explaining during Fraction Intervention. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED557580

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

One-way ANCOVAs on NAEP, number-line, and calculation posttests controlling pretest scores, replicating the multi-component Fraction Face-Off! intervention against a part-whole-focused control. The article reports ESs "ranged from 0.57 to 1.98" (EXP) and "0.70 to 2.08" (WP).

> "NAEP, number line, and calculations performance was stronger for each intervention condition compared to CON. ESs favoring EXP over CON ranged from 0.57 to 1.98; ESs favoring WP over CON ranged from 0.70 to 2.08."
