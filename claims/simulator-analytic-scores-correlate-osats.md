## Evidence

### Borselle D. 2025

Borselle D., Więckowski A., Patkowski D. (2025). Evaluating suturing skill improvement for pediatric minimally invasive esophageal anastomosis model: an observational cohort study based on simulator training. Scientific Reports 15, 7692. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91307-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis comparing simulator analytic scores with blinded OSATS ratings from two researchers for single-knot recordings; the correlation diagram reports a Spearman coefficient ρ of 0.653, and the authors describe a "moderate, but statistically significant positive correlation".

> "A moderate, but statistically significant positive correlation was observed between the automatic Analytic scores for the Single Surgical Knot performance (the Overall Score) and the average OSATS assessments conducted by two researchers."
