## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A common post-scaffolding error on the swing problem was T = mv^2/r - mg instead of the correct T = mv^2/r + mg, likely from treating the centripetal term as an additional force or mishandling the vector direction of acceleration. [→ Lin 2011](#lin-2011)

## Evidence

### Lin 2011

Lin, S.-Y. and Singh, C. (2011). Using Isomorphic Problems to Learn Introductory Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.7.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.7.020104)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of intervention 1 and 2 students' written quiz solutions after learning from the solved problem; the common incorrect answer was "2mvT mg r" with a wrong sign. Descriptive error analysis; no effect size printed.

> "students weren't necessarily able to apply the principles correctly when a change in the details of application was required in order to solve the transfer problem in the new situation"
