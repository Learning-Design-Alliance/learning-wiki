## Evidence

### Sattelmayer Martin 2016

Sattelmayer Martin, Elsig Simone, Hilfiker Roger, Baer Gillian. (2016). A systematic review and meta-analysis of selected motor learning principles in physiotherapy and medical education. BMC Medical Education, 16, 15. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0538-z

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of two RCTs comparing random and blocked practice on orthopaedic surgical tasks, 31 participants on the post-acquisition test. The effect of SMD: 0.63 favoured random practice but was not statistically significant (p: 0.09), with low heterogeneity (I2: 0 %).

> "For the post-acquisition test 31 participants were included. The effect size was moderate (SMD: 0.63) and in favour for random practice (95 % CI -0.10 to 1.36). However, the result was statistically not significant (p: 0.09). Heterogeneity between studies was low (I2: 0 %)."
