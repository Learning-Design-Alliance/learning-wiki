`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Item-level Wilcoxon rank-sum analysis of the 13 diagnostic-proficiency items in the VR assessment (Table 3). Adjusted P-values < 0.0001 for slit lamp (r = 0.486), B-ultrasound (r = 0.444), OCT (r = 0.640), and fundus photography (r = 0.504); other items such as visual acuity and lacrimal duct irrigation showed no significant difference.

> "after adjusting the P-values for multiple comparisons using the Holm method, the experimental group’s significant advantage in the core diagnostic skills of slit lamp examination, eye B ultrasound, OCT examination, and fundus photography was rigorously confirmed"
