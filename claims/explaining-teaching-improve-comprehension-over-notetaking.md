## Evidence

### Sarah Shi Hui Wong 2026

Sarah Shi Hui Wong, Stephen Lim. (2026). The Powerful Teacher: A Power Hypothesis for the Benefits of Learning-by-Teaching. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10097-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Comprehension test (six short-answer questions, 12 points possible) in the same experiment: the method ANOVA was significant, F(2, 236) = 4.75, p=.01, with "d = 0.45 and 0.40" for explaining and teaching versus notetaking.

> "Both the explaining (M = 5.96, SD = 2.49) and teaching (M = 5.90, SD = 2.82) groups displayed better comprehension performance than the notetaking (M = 4.84, SD = 2.45) group, p=.007 and .01, d = 0.45 and 0.40, respectively"

### Sarah Shi Hui Wong 2026 (2)

Sarah Shi Hui Wong, Stephen Lim. (2026). The Powerful Teacher: A Power Hypothesis for the Benefits of Learning-by-Teaching. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10097-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise comparison of teaching versus explaining on the comprehension test showed no significant difference; equivalence was not tested beyond this non-significant result.

> "the explaining and teaching groups did not significantly differ in their comprehension performance, p=.89, d = −0.02"
