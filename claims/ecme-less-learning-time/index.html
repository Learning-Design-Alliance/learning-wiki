Evidence¶

Omrani 2012¶

Omrani, S., Fardanesh, H., Hemmati, N., & Hemmati, N. (2012). Exploring an appropriate instructional design model for continuing medical education. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 13(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ997826

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Reported study-time comparison between the lecture-based control group and the self-paced e-learning group; the e-learning group completed the program in roughly 120±18 minutes versus 6 hours for the control group.

"Participants in this study using e-learning and multimedia formats needed less time for reading and learning than traditional method. The time required for control group based on the CME programs was 6 hours in a day and in experimental group was about 120± 18 minutes."
