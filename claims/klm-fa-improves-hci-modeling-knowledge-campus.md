## Evidence

### Katsanos 2018

Katsanos, C., Xenos, M. and Tselios, N. (2018). Tool-mediated HCI Modeling Instruction in a Campus-based Software Quality Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-post study of campus-based software quality students comparing knowledge test scores after lecture-based instruction and after a KLM-FA mediated essay activity; the dependent samples t-test printed 't(21) = 2.890, p = 0.009, r = 0.533', which the authors call a large observed effect size.

> "A two-tailed dependent samples t-test showed that the difference between students' pretest and posttest scores in the knowledge test was statistically significant; t(21) = 2.890, p = 0.009, r = 0.533."

### Katsanos 2018 (2)

Katsanos, C., Xenos, M. and Tselios, N. (2018). Tool-mediated HCI Modeling Instruction in a Campus-based Software Quality Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Non-parametric confirmation on the same pre-post score differences, conducted because two normality tests gave contradicting results; the Wilcoxon signed rank test printed 'z = 2.601, p = 0.009, r = 0.390'.

> "In agreement to the parametric test, a two-tailed Wilcoxon signed rank test also found that students improved significantly their scores in the knowledge test after using KLM-FA; z = 2.601, p = 0.009, r = 0.390."
