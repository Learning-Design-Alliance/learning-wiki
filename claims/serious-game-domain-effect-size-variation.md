Domain-level ANCOVA results from the randomized trial (Table 3) show adjusted mean differences "most pronounced for "Therapeutic planning skill"" and diagnostic planning, with history taking the smallest but still large difference; the printed MDs are unstandardized, so no effect size is coded.

> "The adjusted mean differences between groups were most pronounced for "Therapeutic planning skill" (MD = 3.96, 95% CI: 3.64 to 4.27) and "Diagnostic planning skill" (MD = 3.70, 95% CI: 3.22 to 4.17). The smallest, yet still very large difference was observed for "History taking skill" (MD = 2.87, 95% CI: 2.47 to 3.27)"
