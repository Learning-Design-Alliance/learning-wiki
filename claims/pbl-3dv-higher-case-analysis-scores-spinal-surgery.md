## Evidence

### Sun Maji 2022

Sun Maji, Chu Fuchao, Gao Chunjiu, Yuan Feng. (2022). Application of the combination of three-dimensional visualization with a problem-based learning mode of teaching to spinal surgery teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03931-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 106 5-year clinical-medicine interns (53 per group) in spinal surgery at the Affiliated Hospital of Xuzhou Medical University. Subjective scores were "38.32 ± 5.52" for the experimental group versus "31.40 ± 5.28" for controls, "T 6.60" "P <0.01" (Table 1).

> "the scores on subjective questions (case analysis questions) achieved by students in the experimental group were significantly higher than those achieved by students in the control group (P < 0.01)"
