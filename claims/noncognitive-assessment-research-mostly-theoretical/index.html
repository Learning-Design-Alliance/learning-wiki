A targeted document review plus expert interviews conducted for the Student Segmentation Initiative reports this gap. The review states that although many instruments exist, most research is "theoretical in nature, not applied to college practice."

"Many instruments are available to postsecondary institutions, but most research reporting findings from noncognitive assessment and segmentation approaches has been theoretical in nature, not applied to college practice."
