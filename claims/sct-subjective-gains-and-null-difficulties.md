## Evidence

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Online questionnaire (wenjuanxing APP) administered to the 96 students at the end of the SCT class as subjective evaluation. Self-reported perceived improvements are descriptive percentages with no inferential test.

> "Most students (more than 50%) considered the abilities of dentists-patients communication and prognosis prediction, integration ability of clinical information, comprehensive outlook of treatment plans, aesthetic restoration expertise and knowledge of materials were improved."

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025 (2)

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Question 7 of the before-and-after Case 1 comparison asked about treatment difficulties; the article reports "no significant difference before and after SCT", with about 40% finding the treatment process difficult. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The results of selection of the difficult issues showed no significant difference before and after SCT."
