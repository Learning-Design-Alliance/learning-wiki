### Geary David C. 2023

Geary David C., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara, Ünal Zehra E. (2023). Sex Differences in Developmental Pathways to Mathematical Competence. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM Model 4 in the longitudinal sample, the model testing the study's main hypothesis, with significant improvement in fit (Δχ2(2) = 78.17, p < .001). The article prints "β = −0.21" and "β = 0.35" for the sex paths.

> "The path from sex to In-Class Attention, β = −0.21, t = −4.43, p < .001, confirmed lower engagement of boys relative to girls in classrooms. The path from sex to Spatial, β = 0.35, t = 7.23, p < .001, confirmed better developed spatial abilities in boys relative to girls."
