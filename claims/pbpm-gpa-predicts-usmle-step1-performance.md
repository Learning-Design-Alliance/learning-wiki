## Evidence

### Schneid Stephen D. 2022

Schneid Stephen D., Kelly Carolyn J., Brandl Katharina. (2022). Relationships between preadmission variables and academic outcomes for postbaccalaureate students in medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10129-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression for the PBPM cohort (n = 59) predicting USMLE Step 1 performance, replicating the preclerkship analysis. The study reports the final step's R² change of 7% and total of 27%, with a power analysis indicating an R² as small as 0.16 detectable with power 0.8.

> "In the final step, PBPM program GPA was added and the R2 changed 7%. All variables entered in the regression analysis accounted for 27% of the variance."
