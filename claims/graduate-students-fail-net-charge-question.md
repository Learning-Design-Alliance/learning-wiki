`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Administration of six protoPIQL questions to 22 graduate physics students, considered a nearly expert population (Fig. 5). On Q15, six students were partially correct (choosing A or E) and six chose a combination of correct and incorrect responses, the most popular being A and D.

> "For ﬁve of the questions (11–14, and 16) at least 18 students were completely correct, but only three of the 22 students correctly answered Q15."
