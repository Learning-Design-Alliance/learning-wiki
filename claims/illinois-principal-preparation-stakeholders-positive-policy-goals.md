### Klostermann 2015

Restructuring principal preparation in Illinois: Perspectives on implementation successes, challenges, and future outlook (IERC 2015-3). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Findings from the statewide scan phase of the I-PREP mixed methods study, based on telephone interviews with 23 program representatives and 22 key policy stakeholders conducted November 2014 through January 2015. Respondents were "largely familiar with and positive about the goals of the policy."

> "Overall, program representatives and statewide stakeholders were largely familiar with and positive about the goals of the policy and had an overall positive view of its future impact on leaders and schools."
