## Evidence

### Mercan Annak 2026

Mercan Annak, İ., and Bulut, H. 2026. "The Effect of Virtual Simulation Game on the Development of Nursing Students' Knowledge and Skills: A Randomized Controlled Trial." Nursing Open 13, no. 5: e70566. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70566

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Procedure-level analysis in the RCT (Table 4) reports post-test between-group comparisons of p < 0.001 (urine evacuation), p = 0.016 (mobilization), p = 0.001 (sterile sampling) and p < 0.001 (catheter removal). The mobilization effect size was calculated as "moderate (d = 0.58)".

> "Across all application steps, including urinary drainage, mobilization, sterile urine sampling and urinary catheter removal, the VSG group consistently demonstrated higher performance compared to the control group (see Table 4)."
