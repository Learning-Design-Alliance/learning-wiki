## Evidence

### Shaeema Zaman Ahmed 2022

Shaeema Zaman Ahmed, Carrie A. Weidner, Jesper H. M. Jensen, Jacob F. Sherson, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Student use of a quantum simulation and visualization tool. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac93c7)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview study of five students working through a single-well quantum exercise, with responses coded in the Predict and Explore/Revise phases. The results section reports that "all students were able to provide a correct answer upon using Composer" when the tool was used correctly. No effect size is reported.

> "unless an answer was not provided in the Explore/Revise phase (which only occurred once), all students were able to provide a correct answer upon using Composer (provided they were using it correctly)"
