## Evidence

### Conlin Anne 2016

Conlin Anne, Lea Jane, Bance Manohar, Chadha Neil, Kilty Shaun, Kozak Frederick, Savage Julian, Sidhu Ravindar, Chen Joseph, Westerberg Brian D. (2016). Mental practice in postgraduate training: a randomized controlled trial in mastoidectomy skills. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-016-0162-2

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Reliability analysis of the trial's blinded video ratings by two experienced otologists reviewing all 24 dissections independently at separate sites. The article reports a printed effect size of "Spearman's rho = 0.495; p = 0.0153" on the primary outcome; the secondary-outcome correlation (rho = 0.350, p = 0.0852) was non-significant.

> "Overall, there was significant correlation (Spearman's rho = 0.495; p = 0.0153) between the two evaluators on the primary outcome measure of the study, the total Task Specific Evaluation of Mastoidectomy score"
