## Evidence

### Antje Kohnle 2014

Antje Kohnle, Charles Baily and Scott Ruby. (2014). Investigating the Influence of Visualization on Student Understanding of Quantum Superposition. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.031](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.031)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Individual interviews with nine volunteers who had completed an introductory quantum physics course; students freely described each of four revised visualizations and then selected statements from a list. The article reports Visualization III was "chosen most often for statements 1, 7 and 13" and least often for incorrect ones; no effect size is printed.

> "Visualization III most often suggested productive ideas about superposition states (with Visualization III chosen most often for statements 1, 7 and 13), while minimizing incorrect ideas"
