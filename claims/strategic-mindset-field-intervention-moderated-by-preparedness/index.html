q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled field experiment with 1070 Secondary 2–4 students across 6 Singapore schools, using mixed-effects models controlling for a significant baseline imbalance in prior year performance. Figure 3 illustrates that "the intervention signiﬁcantly beneﬁtted students who performed at +1 SD above the mean" but not at or below the mean.

"The intervention signiﬁcantly beneﬁtted students who performed at +1 SD above the mean on prior year performance, but the difference was not statistically signiﬁcant for those who scored at the mean or at−1 SD below the mean on prior performance."

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same field experiment: the main effect of condition on LASSI-reported strategy use was not significant after covariating prior year's academic performance due to imbalanced randomization (intervention group had lower baseline). "p = 0.429"; equivalence was not tested.

"Contrary to ourﬁrst hypothesis, we did notﬁnd an effect of condition on students’reported use of effective learningstrategies, controlling for prior year’s academic performance, p = 0.429."
