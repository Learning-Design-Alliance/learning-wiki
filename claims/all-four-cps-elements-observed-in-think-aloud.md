## Evidence

### Van Hooijdonk 2023

Van Hooijdonk, M., Mainhard, T., Kroesbergen, E. H., & Van Tartwijk, J. (2023). Creative problem solving in primary school students. Learning and Instruction, 88, 101823. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101823

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative think-aloud study in which 13 fourth graders (M age = 9.08) completed a structured science problem task while verbalizing their thoughts; protocols were segmented and coded with a directed content analysis scheme based on the CPS model (Cohen's Kappa = 0.85). The study found "every student showed creative problem solving processes belonging to each of the four main elements". No effect size is printed.

> "the main finding of this think aloud study was that every student showed creative problem solving processes belonging to each of the four main elements of the CPS model (understanding the challenge, generating ideas, preparing for action, planning your approach; Isaksen et al., 2011)."
