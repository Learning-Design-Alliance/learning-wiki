`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire-based expert evaluation with 32 STEM instructors (3 physics, 12 mathematics, 5 chemistry, 12 computer science) on five-point Likert items, with display order randomly shuffled; Figure 3 reports ratings across seven dimensions in four STEM domains.

### Xinchen Ma 2026 (2)
