What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2010). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Lindamood Phoneme Sequencing® (LiPS®). Students with Learning Disabilities. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/279

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

WWC review of the LiPS evidence base for students with learning disabilities. Thirty studies were excluded for reasons including no comparison group, samples with less than 50% students with learning disabilities, or non-primary analyses; the report states the extent of evidence is "small" in all five domains examined.

> "Thirty-one studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of LiPS® on students with learning disabilities. One study (Torgesen et al., 2001) is a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC evidence standards."
