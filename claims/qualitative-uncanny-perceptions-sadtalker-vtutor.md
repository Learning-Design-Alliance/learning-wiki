## Evidence

### Eason Chen 2025

Eason Chen, Chenyu Lin, Yu-Kai Huang, Xinyi Tang, Aprille Xi, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth Koedinger. (2025). VTutor: An Animated Pedagogical Agent SDK that Provide Real Time Multi-Model Feedback. arXiv:2505.06676. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99267-4_19](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99267-4_19)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses in the 50-participant evaluation: many participants described SadTalker as "unsettling," "creepy," or "weird", suggesting an uncomfortable, uncanny-valley-like experience.

> "In total, many participants (e.g., P24, P28, P35, P41, P45) described SadTalker as "unsettling," "creepy," or "weird," suggesting an uncomfortable, uncanny-valley-like experience."

### Eason Chen 2025 (2)

Eason Chen, Chenyu Lin, Yu-Kai Huang, Xinyi Tang, Aprille Xi, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth Koedinger. (2025). VTutor: An Animated Pedagogical Agent SDK that Provide Real Time Multi-Model Feedback. arXiv:2505.06676. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99267-4_19](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99267-4_19)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Participant P41 commented that VTutor "was a lot closer to an anime character in the mouth", which made it feel less creepy; the thematic analysis coded this as positive feedback on VTutor's stylized mouth.

> "It didn 't look 'natural,' but [VTutor] was a lot closer to an anime character in the mouth. That in itself made it feel less creepy.'"
