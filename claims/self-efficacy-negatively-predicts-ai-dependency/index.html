Hilit Maizel 2026¶

Hilit Maizel, Maya Kalman Halevi, Miri Sarid, and Rony Tutian. (2026). Understanding Student Dependency on AI: The Role of AI Literacy, Academic Self-Efficacy, and Resource Management Strategies. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 1123. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16071123

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Regression analysis of the same 478-student survey. Academic self-efficacy was also negative (β = −0.197, p < 0.001). The authors read both as one compensatory mechanism: "AI self-efficacy was negatively associated with AI dependency ( β = −0.132, p = 0.016), paralleling the role of academic self-efficacy".
