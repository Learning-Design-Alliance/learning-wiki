Evidence¶

Fabian 2018¶

Fabian, K., Topping, K. J., & Barron, I. G. (2018). Using mobile technologies for mathematics: effects on student attitudes and achievement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9580-3

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 52 Primary 6/7 pupils in one Scottish school; the experimental group used tablets in weekly collaborative sessions over three months. The article reports a significant gain-score difference, t(43) = 4.57, p = 0.005, ES = 0.89, "which indicates that the experimental group had higher gains than the control group".

"A t test of the gain score between group showed a signiﬁcant difference, t(43) = 4.57, p = 0.005, ES = 0.89 which indicates that the experimental group had higher gains than the control group."
