## Evidence

### Resnick PJ 2010

Resnick PJ, Janney AW, Buis LR, Richardson CR. (2010). Adding an Online Community to an Internet-Mediated Walking Program. Part 2: Strategies for Encouraging Community Participation. J Med Internet Res 12(4):e72. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1339

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case-study analysis of one online community's forum logs across a 16-week walking program. The authors' retrospective assessment states the contests were "the most effective single intervention at producing participation" and that prize threads topped thread popularity.

> "The occasional contests, with unspecified token prizes, were the most effective single intervention at producing participation. The 4 most popular threads were all prize threads."
