Evidence¶

Dahlin 2013¶

Karin I. E. Dahlin. (2013). Working Memory Training and the Effect on Mathematical Achievement in Children with Attention Deficits and Special Needs. Journal of Education and Learning, 2(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v2n1p118

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Within-treatment-group Cohen's d comparisons across T1, T2 and T3 (no control-group WM measures due to resources). Table 3 prints d = 1.19 and 1.05 for Span board forward, 0.92 and 0.93 for Span board back, and 0.48 and 0.34 for Digit span back at T3–T1; the article reports neither verbal measure improved significantly.

"Table 3 shows that the effect (d) was large in the total group (n =42) (T2-T1, T3-T1) on Span board forward and Span board back. The effects on Digit span were large, moderate or low."
