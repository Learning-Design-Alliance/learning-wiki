SEM analysis of the 524-respondent questionnaire dataset (Table 7 direct effects). The peer-interaction path to self-efficacy was significant at p<0.05 with "β=0.197", which the article describes as weaker than the content-interaction path (β=0.462).

> "Student-student interaction has a weaker direct effect on students’ self-efficacy (β=0.197), indicating that interaction with peers also positively affects self-efficacy, but to a lesser extent."
