## Evidence

### Kopciuch D 2026

Kopciuch D, Ciosek K, Adamska K, Chojara N, Paczkowska A, Zaprutko T, Ratajczak P, Nowakowska E, Kus K. (2026). Analysis of the knowledge and skills of physiotherapists and physiotherapy students regarding pulmonary rehabilitation in post-COVID-19 patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08552-2

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis analysis of skill self-ratings in the 110-respondent Polish survey. The article reports for postural drainage "H = 33.47; p = 0.0001" with a printed effect size of η²_H = 0.22, described as a large effect.

> "there were statistically significant differences in self-assessed competence for postural drainage (H = 33.47; p = 0.0001; η²_H = 0.22, large effect)"

### Kopciuch D 2026 (2)

BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08552-2

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis analysis of the same survey for manual chest techniques (vibration, springing, percussion), reported with "H = 24.80; p = 0.003" and a printed effect size of η²_H = 0.15, described as a large effect.

> "and manual chest techniques (H = 24.80; p = 0.003; η²_H = 0.15, large effect])."

### Kopciuch D 2026 (3)

Kopciuch D, Ciosek K, Adamska K, Chojara N, Paczkowska A, Zaprutko T, Ratajczak P, Nowakowska E, Kus K. (2026). Analysis of the knowledge and skills of physiotherapists and physiotherapy students regarding pulmonary rehabilitation in post-COVID-19 patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08552-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bonferroni-corrected Dunn's post hoc pairwise comparisons following the two significant Kruskal–Wallis tests. Significance is reported (adjusted p < 0.05) with no effect size for the pairwise contrast.

> "In both cases, physiotherapists with ≥ 6 years of professional experience had significantly higher competence scores than early-year students (Dunn's post hoc tests, adjusted p < 0.05)."
