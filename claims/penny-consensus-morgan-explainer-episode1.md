Epistemic stances toward group work in learning physics: Interactions between epistemology and social dynamics in a collaborative problem solving context. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2005.02425](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2005.02425)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Fine-grained discourse analysis of the first episode of the hour-long session. Penny presents ideas with uncertainty and invites others to weigh in, which the authors associate with "a view of group work as a collective consensus building process". Morgan's immediate didactic responses to Penny's questions are read as the Explainer-Explainee stance. Interpretive qualitative evidence; no effect size.

> "this slower pace of speech and asking questions where her pitch goes up at the end convey a tone of tentativeness and inclusion"
