q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-synthesis finding across the qualitative corpus: the review reports that "online experiences can create transformational opportunities for underrepresented youth". Supporting accounts include LGBTQI+ adolescents describing being able to come out without fear and a non-binary participant describing finding community they could not have in person.

"A recurring thread across the subthemes is the way online experiences can create transformational opportunities for underrepresented youth, who may otherwise feel marginalised in society."
