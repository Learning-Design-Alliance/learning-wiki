## Evidence

### Lepper 2005

Lepper, M. R., Henderlong Corpus, J., & Iyengar, S. S. (2005). Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivational Orientations in the Classroom: Age Differences and Academic Correlates. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.97.2.184

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of report-card GPAs collected in the second school district (n = 216). The quote reports the "significant positive correlation between overall GPA and intrinsic motivation" with r = .34; the correlation was positive at all six grade levels.

> "there was a significant positive correlation between overall GPA and intrinsic motivation (r ⫽ .34, p ⬍ .001)."

### Lepper 2005 (2)

Lepper, M. R., Henderlong Corpus, J., & Iyengar, S. S. (2005). Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivational Orientations in the Classroom: Age Differences and Academic Correlates. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.97.2.184

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of California Achievement Test scores available for a subset of the first school district (n = 136). The quote reports test scores "positively correlated with intrinsic motivation (r ⫽ .27, p ⬍ .01)", an effect size of r = .27.

> "standardized test scores proved both positively correlated with intrinsic motivation (r ⫽ .27, p ⬍ .01) and negatively correlated with extrinsic motivation (r ⫽ ⫺.32, p ⬍ .001)."
