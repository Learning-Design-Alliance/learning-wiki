Evidence¶

Jessaca Spybrook 2016¶

Jessaca Spybrook, Carl D. Westine, Joseph A. Taylor. (2016). Design Parameters for Impact Research in Science Education: A Multistate Analysis. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858415625975

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Comparison of 1-year lag school-level pretest covariates (science, reading, mathematics) in two- and three-level hierarchical models across three state databases. For example, in Michigan Grade 5 the school-level science pretest explains more variance than reading or mathematics pretests; the pattern holds at school and district levels in the three-level model.

"In fact, in every grade within each of the three states, the science pretest explains more variability than either the reading or mathematics pretest."
