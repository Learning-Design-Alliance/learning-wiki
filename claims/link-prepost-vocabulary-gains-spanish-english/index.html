Evidence¶

Baker 2016¶

Baker, D. L., Mogna, V., Rodriguez, S., Farmer, D., & Yovanoff, P. (2016). Building the Oral Language of Young Hispanic Children Through Interactive Read Alouds and Vocabulary Games at Preschool and at Home. Journal of International Special Needs Education, 19(2), 81-94. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1195411

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-tests of pre-test to post-test scores within the treatment group of the quasi-experimental Study 1 (34 children in the group): 34 complete pairs for the receptive measure (RVK) and 31 for the expressive measure (RNA), three children being excluded from the RNA analysis for absences. The article reports effect sizes (Cohen's d) of 0.63 in English and 0.50 in Spanish on the RVK (n = 34), and a significant Spanish RNA gain of 1.66 points with d = 0.61 (n = 31); the English RNA difference was not statistically significant (p = .662). This pre/post analysis has no comparison group, so it shows the gains, not that the intervention caused them.

"the results on the RVK at post-test were, on average, signiﬁcantly higher than at pre-test by 2.02 points on the English version of the assessment (t(33) ¼/C0 3.47, p , .01), and 1.79 points on the Spanish version of the assessment ( t(33) ¼/C0 4.28, p , .01)."
