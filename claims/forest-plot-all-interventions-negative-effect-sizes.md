`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The meta-analysis's forest plot of cyberbullying intervention effectiveness (Figure 2) shows that "all interventions listed have negative effect sizes, though the magnitude varies", with real-time monitoring in high schools and digital literacy in colleges showing larger effects. No numeric effect sizes are printed in the text.

> "The plot reveals that all interventions listed have negative effect sizes, though the magnitude varies. For instance, 'Real-Time Monitoring in High Schools' and 'Digital Literacy in Colleges' show larger and more consistent negative effects, suggesting these interventions are particularly effective at mitigating cyberbullying."
