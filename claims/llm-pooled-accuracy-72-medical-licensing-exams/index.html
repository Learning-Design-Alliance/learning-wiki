Evidence¶

Nouri Haniyeh 2025¶

Nouri Haniyeh, Mahdavi Abdollah, Abedi Ali, Mohammadnia Alireza, Hamedan Mahnaz, Amanzadeh Masoud. (2025). Performance of large language models in medical licensing examinations: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.36

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 36 studies (2,404 records screened) evaluating LLM accuracy on medical licensing examination questions. The review reports "The pooled accuracy of all models was 72%" with very high heterogeneity (I2=99.99%).

"The pooled accuracy of all models was 72% (95% CI, 70.0%–75.0%). The heterogeneity across studies was significant (I2=99.99%, P<0.001)."
