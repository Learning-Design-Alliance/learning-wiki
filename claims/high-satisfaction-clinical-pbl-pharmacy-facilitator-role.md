### Khumsikiew et al. 2015

Jeerisuda Khumsikiew, Sisira Donsamak, and Manit Saeteaw. (2015). A Model of Small-Group Problem-Based Learning In Pharmacy Education: Teaching in the Clinical Environment. The IAFOR Journal of Education, Volume III - Issue II - Summer 2015. https://iafor.org/journals

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of an 11-item satisfaction questionnaire (5-point Likert, Cronbach's Alpha 0.87) completed by the same 36 students after the 15-week PBL course. Satisfaction with the facilitator role scored mean 4.22 (SD 0.54, mode 4); no inferential test was reported for satisfaction.

> "All of questions were scored range from high to highest level of mean score (3.41 - 5.00) and most of modes were 4.Students satisfied with the role of teacher who works as their facilitators in highest level of mean score"
