Related Claims¶

  • Chronic physical activity shows a medium positive effect on academic achievement while acute physical activity shows a null effect
  • Physical activity has null or small-to-medium positive effects on school-age children's and adolescents' academic achievement according to meta-analyses
  • Systematic reviews on regular physical activity and children's cognition reach conflicting conclusions, with math performance the most consistent beneficiary — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Intervention effect sizes on mathematics are negatively associated with participants' age and intervention duration, while intervention type does not explain heterogeneity — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Meta-analyses of physical activity effects on youth cognition and academic performance show small effect sizes (0.1–0.3) with substantial heterogeneity — related
  • Increasing school physical education time shows small-to-medium effects on achievement while active classrooms show mixed effects compared with traditional sedentary classrooms — related
