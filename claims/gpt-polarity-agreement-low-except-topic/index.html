Related Claims¶

  • Feedback generated by GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 was significantly more readable than instructor-generated feedback on open-ended writing tasks — related
  • GPT-4 demonstrated higher reliability of feedback than GPT-3.5, with higher F1 agreement with instructors on most assessment aspects — related
  • Human-LLM agreement remains low across constructs (κ = 0.000 to 0.419), with self-efficacy showing the best alignment and Prior KSAs none — related
  • Prompt quality and detailed scoring rubrics significantly influence the accuracy and consistency of LLM-generated grades — related
  • A human assessor agreed with the majority of GPT-generated feedback comments on assessment aspects that instructors had not commented on — related
  • NLP-based feedback generation for textual student answers in large courses reduced grading effort by up to 85% with high precision (reported from Bernius et al.) — reports the opposite
  • In a self-referential framework, LLMs, particularly GPT-4.1, tend to outperform a single operational human rater at identifying relative weaknesses (negative feedback) across several Language and Content aspects — related
  • GPT-4 achieved almost perfect inter-rater reliability with faculty after three iterations of rubric engineering on three of four medical school OEQ questions — related
