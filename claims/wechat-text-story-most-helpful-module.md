## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 73.47% of students chose text story as the most helpful module, compared with 32.65% for picture and 22.45% for audio and video. [→ Dai et al. 2018](#dai-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Dai et al. 2018

Guiyu Dai, Yang Liu & Shanmeng Cui. (2018). A Study on the Mobile Learning of English and American Literature Based on Wechat Public Account. English Language Teaching, 11(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n6p47

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Question 21 (single choice) asked which module was most helpful. Text story ranked first (73.47%), picture second (32.65%), audio and video last (22.45%). The authors suggest pictures are more direct and audio-video is impractical in quiet places.

> "Nearly three out of four students (73.47%) think the module of text story in the Wechat articles is the most helpful for their literature information learning."
