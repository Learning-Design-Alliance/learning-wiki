## Evidence

### Visier-Alfonso ME 2020

Visier-Alfonso ME, Sánchez-López M, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Jiménez-López E, Redondo-Tébar A, Nieto-López M. (2020). Executive functions mediate the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic achievement in Spanish schoolchildren aged 8 to 11 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231246

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the bootstrap mediation models of CRF effects on language and mathematics grades, indirect effects were significant for inhibition and cognitive flexibility, "except for working memory", which therefore did not significantly mediate the CRF–achievement association.

> "Finally, the indirect effects were statistically significant in for inhibition and cognitive flexibility, confirming that executive function domains acted as a partial mediator in these models, except for working memory."
