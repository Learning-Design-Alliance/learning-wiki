# Chicago Connected eligibility was associated with higher fall 2020 engagement for high pre-pandemic GPA students but lower engagement for low pre-pandemic GPA students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Eligibility was associated with an increase in fall 2020 remote learning engagement for high pre-pandemic GPA students (by 0.04 SD) and a decrease for low pre-pandemic GPA students (by 0.10 SD). [→ Schachner 2025](#schachner-2025)

## Evidence

### Schachner 2025

Schachner, J.N., Marwell, N.P., de la Torre, M., Gwynne, J.A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2025). How did Chicago Connected broadband access eligibility and participation affect remote learning engagement and achievement during the COVID-19 pandemic? Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1153

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

OLS models predicting the fall 2020 engagement index as a function of program eligibility among eligible students, with student and pandemic-era controls. Eligibility showed opposite-signed associations by pre-pandemic GPA group, with the printed magnitudes 0.04 SD and 0.10 SD; no further standardized effect size is printed.

> "For students with high pre-pandemic GPAs, eligibility was associated with an increase in remote learning engagement, measured in fall 2020 (by 0.04 SD), but a decrease in remote learning engagement for students with low pre-pandemic GPAs (by 0.10 SD)."
