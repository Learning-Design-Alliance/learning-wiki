Zhou Yuanyuan, Sechelski Amber, Mowdy Brent, Johnson Tess, Vernardo Nikea, West Courtney A. (2026). Data driven student support: utilize the LASSI to improve academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09519-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across four cohorts of medical students (N = 423) using Pearson's r found Time Management was the only subscale with a statistically significant correlation with GPA outcomes; the article reports "r = 0.11, p = 0.028" for first-semester GPA.

> "Time Management was the only subscale that showed a statistically significant correlation with First-semester GPA (r = 0.11, p = 0.028, n = 423) and pre-clerkship GPA (r = 0.10, p = 0.036, n = 423)."
