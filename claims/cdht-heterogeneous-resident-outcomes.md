Hou Qin, Zhou Bin, Kang Jing-Ping, Zuo Yun-Xia. (2025). Competency-driven hemostasis training reduces vascular complications in transradial hemodynamic monitoring: a self-controlled trial with early-career residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08048-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-resident analysis (Table 3) of hematoma/bruise rates before and after CDHT in the same trial, showing improvement for 8 residents, deterioration for one, and no change for one with zero baseline hematomas.

> "The findings demonstrated that the compression effectiveness of 8 residents improved after CDHT. The incidence of hematoma was notably reduced compared to pre-training. However, in one resident, the incidence of hematoma following CDHT exceeded the pre-training rates."
