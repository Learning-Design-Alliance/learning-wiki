q3 · i? · causal · r?

First-year results of a large-scale randomized controlled study of two comprehensive teacher induction programs; the report states the programs "did not increase teacher retention" during the first year of teaching. No effect size is printed on this page.

"Intensive mentoring and professional development for teachers just entering the profession did not increase teacher retention or generate any significant improvements in classroom practices or student achievement during the first year of teaching."

First-year impact findings from a large-scale randomized controlled study of two comprehensive teacher induction programs; the report states the programs "did not increase teacher retention" in year 1. No effect size is printed.

"Intensive mentoring and professional development for teachers just entering the profession did not increase teacher retention or generate any significant improvements in classroom practices or student achievement during the first year of teaching."
