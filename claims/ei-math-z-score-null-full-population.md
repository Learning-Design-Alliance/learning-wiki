## Evidence

### Stingone JA 2026

Stingone JA, McVeigh KH, Lednyak L. (2026). Early Intervention Developmental Programming and Childhood Academic Outcomes. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.55890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stratified analysis by quintiles of propensity score within the matched LSED cohort. The article reports "a generally null association between EI and math z scores when examining the full population" while ELA and standards-meeting outcomes showed beneficial associations; heterogeneity across strata may reflect effect measure modification, nonlinearity, or residual imbalance.

> "When stratifying by propensity score, we consistently see the magnitude of associations was greater when examining individuals who had a greater probability of receiving EI services. We observed beneficial associations between EI and ELA z score and greater incidence of meeting standards on both math and ELA tests among children who received EI services. In contrast, we saw a generally null association between EI and math z scores when examining the full population."
