### Möltner Andreas 2020

Möltner Andreas, Lehmann Mirijam, Wachter Cornelia, Kurczyk Sonia, Schwill Simon, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). Formative assessment of practical skills with peer-assessors: quality features of an OSCE in general medicine at the Heidelberg Medical Faculty. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(4), Doc42. [doi:10.3205/zma001335](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001335)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Generalizability-theory comparison of the OSCE General Medicine with six summative OSCEs, assuming a parcours of ten stations (Table 6). The article states the comparison "must take into account the different number of stations", and that higher absolute accuracy in comparison OSCEs is "mainly due to the higher variability of the stations".

> "It can be seen that for Eρ2 three of the six comparison OSCEs have both lower and higher values. The absolute accuracy is higher for four comparison OSCEs."
