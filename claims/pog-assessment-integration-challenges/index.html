Terrell 2025¶

Terrell, J. H., Ahigian, R., Garvey, M., & Barzee, S. (2025). Building future-ready students: The state of Portraits of a Graduate Frameworks. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673550

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief's analysis section on assessing POGs, an author interpretation drawn from its document scan rather than a tested result. It argues that integrating POG assessments into systems "centered around standardized tests" is complex because POG competencies "often require more nuanced assessment methods."

"This lack of a standardized framework across the state can lead to inconsistencies in how competencies are measured, making it difficult for education systems to track progress on students' mastery of essential skills across different regions and compare educational outcomes at a broader state or national level."
