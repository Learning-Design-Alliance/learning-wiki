## Evidence

### Eelco Braad 2022

Eelco Braad, Nick Degens, Wolmet Barendregt, Wijnand IJsselsteijn. (2022). Improving metacognition through self-explication in a digital self-regulated learning tool. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10156-2

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples one-tailed t-tests with Bonferroni correction on the in-vivo quasi-experiment (Table 4). The domain-specific group's delta was 3.65 with d = .368; the domain-general group's increase of 2.31 (d = .209) did not reach the corrected threshold. The article reports "the effect size is small to medium".

> "For the domain-specific and domain-general groups, the confidence intervals of the differences do not contain zero and the effect size is small to medium, however, only the increase within the domain-specific group was significant at an alpha level of .05/3 = .017 (H1)."
