# ChatGPT solved 10 of 12 clicker questions, corresponding to a 93% clicker score under the course's credit scheme

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a replayed lecture on momentum with peer-instruction phases, ChatGPT correctly solved 10 of 12 clicker questions; under the course's 60%-credit-for-false-answers scheme this corresponds to a 93% clicker score, which the article says is a lot better than most students in the actual course achieved. ## Evidence

### Gerd Kortemeyer 2023

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2023). Could an Artificial-Intelligence agent pass an introductory physics course? arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010132)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Replay of an actual momentum lecture's clicker sequence, including re-answering peer-instruction questions; ChatGPT held its correct answers under intentionally confusing peer prompts and would score 93% under the printed credit scheme.

> "In summary, ChatGPT correctly solved 10 out of 12 questions. Within the actual course, participation in clicker discussions was encouraged by granting 60% credit for false answers and 100% credit for correct answers [11], so the clicker score of ChatGPT would be 93%."
