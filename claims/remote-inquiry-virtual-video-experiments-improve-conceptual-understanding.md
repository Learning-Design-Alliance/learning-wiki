## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students' conceptual understanding improved significantly from pre-test to immediate post-test after guided inquiry learning with virtual and video experiments during remote schooling, with a large time effect. [→ Flegr 2023](#flegr-2023)

## Evidence

### Flegr 2023

Flegr, S., Kuhn, J., & Scheiter, K. (2023). How to foster STEM learning during Covid-19 remote schooling: Combining virtual and video experiments. Learning and Instruction, 86, 101778. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101778

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Pre-registered randomized experiment with N = 154 seventh-graders learning at home during Covid-19 remote schooling, randomly assigned within classes to three conditions. The two-way mixed ANOVA showed a large main effect of time (partial η2 = 0.62): "students performed better in the immediate post-test (M = 61.47%, SE = 1.62) than in the pre-test (M = 37.23%, SE = 1.16)".

> "The mixed ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of time, F(1, 151) = 244.71; MSE = 44957.24; p < .0001; partial η2 = 0.62 (large effect), suggesting that students performed better in the immediate post-test (M = 61.47%, SE = 1.62) than in the pre-test (M = 37.23%, SE = 1.16)."
