## Evidence

### Kaijser Mirjam A. 2024

Kaijser Mirjam A., Slagter Nienke, de Heide Loek J.M., van Beek André P., Pierie Jean-Pierre E.N., Emous Marloes. (2024). Measuring Small Bowel Length in Bariatric Surgery: An Ex Vivo Laparoscopic Training Experiment. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/15533506241244854

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-center ex vivo training experiment with ten surgical residents in a Dutch teaching hospital; subgroup analysis of the eight residents meeting the minimum practice criterion. The article reports a statistically significant improvement with "Z = −2.5, P = .012"; no effect size is printed.

> "residents with sufficient practice of at least 15 minutes in each week of the study (n = 8) estimated the baseline bowel measurements with an absolute deviation of 24% [14-38]. These residents improved their measurements statistically significantly to 5% [2-17] deviation after the training experiment (Z = −2.5, P = .012)."
