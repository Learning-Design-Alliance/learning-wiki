`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Conventional content analysis (Hsieh and Shannon method) of the 60 included articles' reported benefits and effects. The review lists cost-effectiveness, time-saving, immediate feedback, "self-paced learning", flexibility, ease of participation, and repeated learning as the benefits identified.

> "The benefits of asynchronous e-learning were as follows: cost-effective [25, 30, 32, 39, 47, 57, 70], time-saving and efficient [9, 25, 32, 47, 52, 68, 69, 75, 76], immediate feedback [11, 47, 68], self-paced learning [9, 11, 25, 47, 53, 57, 70, 76], flexibility"
