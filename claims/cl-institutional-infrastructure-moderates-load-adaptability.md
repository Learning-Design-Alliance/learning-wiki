Contextual comparison reported in the discussion of the same survey study. The authors report the path difference as "βestablished = -.296 vs. βemerging = -.382, p <.05" and attribute it to socio-technical interaction between institutional factors and individual cognition.

> "Educational settings with established technology support infrastructures demonstrate weaker negative relationships between cognitive load and technological adaptability (βestablished = -.296 vs. βemerging = -.382, p <.05), aligning with Kamalov et al.'s [56] framework for sustainable technology integration."
