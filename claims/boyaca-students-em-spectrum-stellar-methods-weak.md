## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the questionnaire, 49% of students correctly identified the electromagnetic spectrum regions by wavelength, 17% failed to recognise that beta radiation is not part of the spectrum, and 24% had difficulties establishing the relation. [→ Valderrama 2024](#valderrama-2024)
`q2 i?` On identifying stellar chemical composition, responses were evenly split: 35% associated spectrophotometry with the task, 35% believed it is done only by observation without measurements, and 30% considered it easy to send space probes to stars. [→ Valderrama 2024 (2)](#valderrama-2024-2)

[doi:10.51660/ripie42173](https://doi.org/10.51660/ripie42173)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Question 5 of the exploratory questionnaire presented a wavelength-to-spectral-region figure and asked which option integrated the most regions. "el 49% de los estudiantes identifican correctamente las regiones del espectro electromagnético", with 17% and 24% in the two error patterns.

> "el 49% de los estudiantes identifican correctamente las regiones del espectro electromagnético según la longitud de onda; el 17% no reconoce que la radiación beta no forma parte de este espectro, y un 24% presenta dificultades para establecer dicha relación."

### Valderrama 2024 (2)

[doi:10.51660/ripie42173](https://doi.org/10.51660/ripie42173)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Question 6 of the exploratory questionnaire asked which technique would identify the chemical components of Alpha Centauri. "Un 35% de los estudiantes asocia la espectrofotometría", another 35% believed observation alone suffices, and 30% chose sending space probes to stars.

> "Un 35% de los estudiantes asocia la espectrofotometría con el estudio de los componentes químicos de los astros de manera lógica, mientras que otro 35% cree que esto se realiza solo mediante observación, sin mediciones."
