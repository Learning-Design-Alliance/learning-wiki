Evidence¶

Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023¶

Muñoz-Rodríguez, J. M., Patino Alonso, C., Pessoa, T., & Martín-Lucas, J. (2023). Identity profile of young people experiencing a sense of risk on the internet: A data mining application of decision tree with CHAID algorithm. Computers & Education, 197, 104743. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104743

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Binary logistic regression on the 1991-respondent survey. Significant predictors include gender (OR = 1.481), marks (OR = 0.412), Instagram (OR = 0.497), TikTok (OR = 0.700), videogames (OR = 0.564), pornography (OR = 0.456) and distraction from studies (OR = 0.549); odds ratios are printed but are not standardised effect sizes, so impact is null.

"The logistic regression analysis (Table 2) revealed the following factors linked to a sense of risk: gender, marks (academic record), Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, videogames, and pornography, distraction from studying, feeling isolated when not online, and using media serves no purpose"

Muñoz-Rodríguez 2023 (2)¶

Muñoz-Rodríguez, J. M., Patino Alonso, C., Pessoa, T., & Martín-Lucas, J. (2023). Identity profile of young people experiencing a sense of risk on the internet: A data mining application of decision tree with CHAID algorithm. Computers & Education, 197, 104743. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104743

q2 · i? · associational · r2

CHAID segmentation of the low-isolation segment finds significant differences by videogame use (χ2 = 12.163; p = 0.001) and TikTok use (χ2 = 7.888; p = 0.010), forming three terminal groups; only test statistics are printed.

"the CHAID algorithm finds significant differences regarding the videogame variable (χ2 = 12.163; p = 0.001; df = 1) and TikTok (χ2 = 7.888; p = 0.010; df = 1) forming three terminal groups"
