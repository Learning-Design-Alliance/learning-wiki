## Evidence

### Carmen Fies 2021

Carmen Fies, Chris Packham. (2021). Transitioning STEM-focused Teacher Professional Development from f2f to Online. SITE 2021 Online, AACE. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2109.08568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2109.08568)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding-frequency analysis of 2020 participants' mentions of key benefits, analyzed in NVivo. Technology ranked behind resources (26.6%), pedagogical tools (20.9%), content (16.5%) and community building (15.8%).

> "Technology was mentioned as a key benefit in 13.7% of the instances, and field trips in only 6.5%."

### Carmen Fies 2021 (2)

Carmen Fies, Chris Packham. (2021). Transitioning STEM-focused Teacher Professional Development from f2f to Online. SITE 2021 Online, AACE. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2109.08568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2109.08568)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding-frequency analysis of 2019 f2f participants' benefit mentions in NVivo. The article states "None of the utterances noted technology as key benefit"; field trips accounted for 13.3% and content 6.7% of statements.

> "Coding of the teachers’ mentions of benefits indicate that the highest impact was in terms of resources and pedagogical tools that could be seamlessly integrated in existing practice (33.3% and 26.7% of statements, respectively), followed by community building and networking (20%)."
