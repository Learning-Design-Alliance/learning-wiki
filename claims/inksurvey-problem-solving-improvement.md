## Evidence

### F.V. Kowalski 2013

F.V. Kowalski, Gus Greivel, Todd Ruskell, Tracy Q. Gardner, S.E. Kowalski, Thomas J. Colling, J.V. Gutierrez Cuba, and Enrique Palou. (2013). Using InkSurvey with Pen-Enabled Mobile Devices for Real-Time Formative Assessment II. Indications of Effectiveness in Diverse Educational Environments. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1308.3729](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1308.3729)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In an upper-level engineering physics course, students working individually or in groups submitted responses to three nearly universally applicable problem-solving questions for 11 new problems; responses were later analyzed and scores adjusted for problem difficulty. No effect size is printed.

> "Results show a steady improvement over the semester in problem-solving skills, indicating InkSurvey can potentially nurture higher level thinking skills."
