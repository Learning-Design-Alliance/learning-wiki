### Li Xinyan 2025

Li Xinyan, Pei Xiaoge, Zhao Jing. (2025). Intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy as pathways to innovative teaching: a mixed-methods study of faculty in Chinese higher education. BMC Psychology, 13, 859. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03177-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM invariance testing across gender, age groups, and academic disciplines in the sample of 357 faculty. The gender comparison found "no significant differences in structural paths (Δχ2(4) = 5.23, p = 0.264)"; age (p = 0.305) and discipline (p = 0.547) tests were likewise non-significant.

> "A chi-square difference test confirmed no significant differences in structural paths (Δχ2(4) = 5.23, p = 0.264), indicating consistent relationships among intrinsic motivation, self-efficacy, and innovative teaching practices for both male and female faculty."
