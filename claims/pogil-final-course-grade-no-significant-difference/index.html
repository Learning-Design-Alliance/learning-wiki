Descriptive comparison of final course grades in the same comparative study (N=50 analyzed). Table 1 reports participant mean 83.8% (range 76.9%-93.1%) versus non-participant mean 82.3% (range 76.7%-90.9%); the article reports the grade difference as not significant.
