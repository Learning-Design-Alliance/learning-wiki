## Evidence

### Wang Song 2017

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spherical ROI analysis (10 mm radius) centered on the whole-brain peak and its right-hemisphere mirror showed CNCEE correlated with left DLPFC rGMD (r = 0.36) "but not with the rGMD of the right DLPFC (r = 0.10, p = 0.12)".

> "CNCEE score was correlated with the rGMD of the left DLPFC (r = 0.36, p < 0.001) but not with the rGMD of the right DLPFC (r = 0.10, p = 0.12)."
