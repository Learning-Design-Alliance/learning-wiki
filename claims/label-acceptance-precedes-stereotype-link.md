## Evidence

### Kashikar 2025

Kashikar, L., Lüke, T., & Grosche, M. (2025). Effects of Diagnostic Labels for Students With Learning Problems on Teachers' Stereotypes and Performance Expectations. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/00222194251315187

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Theoretical argument from the introduction, extending labeling theory (Link & Phelan, 2001) with the stereotype content model and the expectation effect model; the authors state this addition is based on "our previous research (Kashikar et al., 2023, 2024)" rather than tested directly here.

> "we postulate that diagnostic labels are only linked to the negative stereotype of low academic competence, resulting in lower performance expectations when perceived, actively remembered, and accepted as accurate"
