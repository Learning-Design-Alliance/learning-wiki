Richard E. West 2020¶

Richard E. West, Peg Ertmer, Susan McKenney. (2020). The crucial role of theoretical scholarship for learning design and technology. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09770-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The editorial summarizes two instructional design framework papers: a "design taxonomy for instructional activities focused on questioning" by Tawfik and colleagues, and Glazewski and Ertmer's principles for problem-based learning aimed at equity and social justice. Attributed summaries; no data reported.

"Andrew Tawfik, Andrew Graesser, Jessica Gatewood, and Jaclyn Gishbaugher begin with a design taxonomy for instructional activities focused on questioning and guiding student inquiry. Similar to inquiry-based education, Krista Glazewski and Peg Ertmer discuss complex problem solving, and encourage us to consider principles for how to apply problem-based learning to foster greater equity and social justice."
