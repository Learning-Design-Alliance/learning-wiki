# Written quiz scores did not differ significantly among the three point-of-view groups, though the reportorial group had the highest mean

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A one-way ANOVA of written quiz scores among the reportorial, subjective, and objective groups was not significant (F = 2.295, p = .114). [→ Katsioloudis et al. 2012](#katsioloudis-et-al-2012)
`q2 i?` Descriptively, the reportorial group achieved the highest mean quiz score (36.21) versus subjective (30.57) and objective (28.66). [→ Katsioloudis et al. 2012 (2)](#katsioloudis-et-al-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Katsioloudis et al. 2012

Petros J. Katsioloudis, Todd D. Fantz, Millie Jones. (2012). A Comparative Analysis of Point-of-View Modeling for Industrial and Technology Education Courses. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1020186

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quiz ANOVA on the same N = 43 quasi-experimental groups after viewing the assigned point-of-view film; Table 5 reports F = 2.295, p = .114, so the quiz difference "was not significant".

> "A one-way ANOVA was run to compare the mean scores for significant differences among the three groups. The result of the ANOVA test, as shown in table 5, was not significant."

### Katsioloudis et al. 2012 (2)

Petros J. Katsioloudis, Todd D. Fantz, Millie Jones. (2012). A Comparative Analysis of Point-of-View Modeling for Industrial and Technology Education Courses. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1020186

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive quiz results from the same study; these are means without a significant test result, so no effect size is claimed. The reportorial group "achieved a higher mean score of 36.21".

> "the groups that received the instruction via subjective views (n = 14) and objective views (n = 15) had similar quiz scores of 30.57 and 28.66 respectively. The group that received instruction via reportorial views (n = 14) achieved a higher mean score of 36.21."
