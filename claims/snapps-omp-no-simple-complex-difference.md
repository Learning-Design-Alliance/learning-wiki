Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00588-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The trial used one simple case (e.g. pneumonia, viral upper respiratory tract infection) and one complex case (e.g. neonatal cholestasis, acute abdominal pain) per student, randomly selected from three cases per category, and compared outcomes across case complexity within each method.

> "There was no difference in the performance of OMP and SNAPPS regarding simple vs complex cases."
