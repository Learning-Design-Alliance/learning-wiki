## Evidence

### Boadum O 2026

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of two successive M1 cohorts across semesters where curriculum type could be determined (traditional n = 519; integrated n = 288). Domain-level differences in IC (η² = 0.019) and SM (η² = 0.016) also favored the traditional curriculum; PI did not meet the corrected threshold.

> "The traditional format was associated with significantly higher total SDL-R than the integrated format (M = 82.82 ± 10.22 vs. M = 80.64 ± 10.13; F(1, 805) = 8.44, p = .004, η² = 0.010) after Bonferroni correction."
