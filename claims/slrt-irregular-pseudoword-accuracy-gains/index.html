Evidence¶

Pfenninger 2015¶

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-post Wilcoxon comparison on reading accuracy for irregular grapheme-phoneme correspondences in the SLRT-I test series in the intervention study; all four groups had very high accuracy scores on the other SLRT-I measures.

"the only statistically significant differences concerning accuracy were found between the results of the SLRT-I (irregular grapheme-pho- neme correspondences) pre- and posttest, on which the Test Dyslexic group made significant gains over time ( z = -2.78, p = .005)"

Pfenninger 2015 (2)¶

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest Mann-Whitney comparisons on irregular pseudoword reading accuracy in the intervention study; the trained dyslexic group closed the gap while the untrained control dyslexic group did not.

"They were even able to reach the performance level of the non-dyslexic groups, in contrast to the Control Dyslexic group, who still achieved significantly lower scores than the two non-dyslexic groups (in both cases U = 95, p < .001)"
