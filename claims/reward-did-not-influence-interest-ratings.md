## Evidence

### Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang 2025

Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang. (2025). Timing of reward cues alters the interaction between reward and interest on memory over time. npj Science of Learning 10:82. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00374-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control ANOVA on interest ratings across experiments. The interest main effect showed clear separation between high- and low-interest conditions (F(1, 81) = 645.60, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.89); no effect size was printed for the null reward and delay effects.

> "These results conﬁrmed that participants’ interest rating was not inﬂuenced by reward and delay conditions in each experi- ment, and the median split group analysis effectively separated high- and low-interest levels"
