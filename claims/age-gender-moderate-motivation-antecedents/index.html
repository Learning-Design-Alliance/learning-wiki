Evidence¶

Bureau 2022¶

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression of meta-analytic correlations on sample mean age. The authors report that "associations between autonomy support from parents and amotivation, identified regulation and intrinsic motivation diminished as students grew older", and that needs became more negatively predictive of amotivation as age increased.

"First, it appeared that associations between autonomy support from parents and amotivation, identified regulation and intrinsic motivation diminished as students grew older."

Bureau 2022 (2)¶

Bureau, J. S., Howard, J. L., Chong, J. X. Y., & Guay, F. (2022). Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research, 92(1), 46–72. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211042426

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression of meta-analytic correlations on the proportion of males in each sample. "When the proportion of males in a sample increased, the correlation between competence and both identified and intrinsic motivation types increased", the only notable gender moderation found.

"When the proportion of males in a sample increased, the correlation between competence and both identified and intrinsic motivation types increased."
