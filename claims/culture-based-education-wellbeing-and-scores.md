### Annette S. Lee 2019

Annette S. Lee, William Wilson, Jeff Tibbetts, Cark Gawboy, Anne Meyer, Wilfred Buck, Jim Knutson-Kolodzne, David Pantalony. (2019). Celestial calendar-paintings and culture-based digital storytelling: cross-cultural, interdisciplinary, STEM/STEAM resources for authentic astronomy education engagement. EPJ Web of Conferences 200, 01002. https://doi.org/10.1051/epjconf/201920001002

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article cites a Hawai'i study gathering data from nearly 3000 students, 600 teachers and over 2000 parents, which concluded that "culture-based education (CBE) positively impacts student social-emotional well-being" and that this in turn positively affects math and reading test scores. No effect sizes are printed; the finding is reported second-hand as support for the NSW approach.

> "First, culture-based education (CBE) positively impacts student social-emotional well-being (e.g., identity, self-efficacy, social relationships). Second, enhanced socio-emotional well-being, in turn, positively affects math and reading test scores."
