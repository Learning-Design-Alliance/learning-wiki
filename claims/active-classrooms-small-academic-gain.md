# Physically active classrooms produce a small improvement in academic performance compared with traditional sedentary classrooms

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Pooled across 20 randomized studies, active classrooms show a small positive effect on academic performance versus traditional classrooms (SMD = 0.28, 95% CI 0.09 to 0.47). [→ Bedard Chloe 2019](#bedard-chloe-2019)

## Evidence

### Bedard Chloe 2019

Bedard Chloe, St John Laura, Bremer Emily, Graham Jeffrey D., Cairney John. (2019). A systematic review and meta-analysis on the effects of physically active classrooms on educational and enjoyment outcomes in school age children. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0218633

`q3 · i1`

Meta-analysis pooling 20 randomized trials (cluster-adjusted n = 842) using random-effects standardized mean differences. The pooled estimate shows "a small increase in performance on academic tests" with SMD = 0.28 (95% CI 0.09 to 0.47); I2 = 48% indicated moderate heterogeneity.

> "Pooled estimates of all measures of academic performance from 20 studies of 842 (cluster adjusted) participants shows a small increase in performance on academic tests following an active classroom intervention compared to a traditional sedentary classroom (SMD = 0.28, 95% confidence interval (CI) range from 0.09 to 0.47"
