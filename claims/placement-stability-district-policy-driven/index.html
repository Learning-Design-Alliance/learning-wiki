Strain 2017 (2)¶

Phillip S. Strain. (2017). Four-Year Follow-Up of Children in the LEAP Randomized Trial: Some Planned and Accidental Findings. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education, 37(2), 121–126. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121417711531

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The author describes a disturbing pattern of child-independent placement decisions; some districts placed by label, others by preschool progress, and parent advocacy occasionally altered policy.

"Simply put, where children were placed was driven by a district-level decision conditioned on opinions about “other” individuals with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and/or historical information."
