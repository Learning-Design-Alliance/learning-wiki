## Evidence

### Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza 2026

Meshesha Yeshitla Meaza, Weldmeskel Fisseha Mikre. (2026). Gender differences in causal attributions, self-regulation, achievement motivation, and academic performance following an intervention. BMC Psychology, 14, 588. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04357-0

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests (n = 84) in a one-group pre-test-post-test quasi-experiment showed significant gains across attribution, self-regulation, and motivation subscales; the authors note "effect sizes ranged from medium to large (Cohen's d = 0.41 to 1.10)". Only task intrinsic value showed no significant change (p = .112).

> "the pattern of significance is highly consistent (17 of 18 comparisons reached statistical significance at p < 0.001), which is unlikely to occur by chance alone. Second, effect sizes ranged from medium to large (Cohen's d = 0.41 to 1.10)"
