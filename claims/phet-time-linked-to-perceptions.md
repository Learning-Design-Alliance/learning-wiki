### Jared B. Stang 2016

Jared B. Stang, Megan Barker, Sarah Perez, Joss Ives, and Ido Roll. (2016). Active learning in pre-class assignments: Exploring the use of interactive simulations to enhance reading assignments. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.078](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.078)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney comparison of self-reported PhET time split on survey perceptions within the two PhET treatments: students "spent more time with the PhET activity than students who reported the assignment as not beneﬁcial to their learning (not enjoyable)."
