Bootstrapping mediation analysis with 1000 resamplings in the same 285-student sample; the direct effect decreased from β = 0.50 to β = 0.30 but remained significant, indicating partial parallel mediation. The article reports "β = 0.20, SE = 0.05 (95% CI = 0.095, 0.308)".
