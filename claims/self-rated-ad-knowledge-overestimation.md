`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey (N = 231) comparing self-rated knowledge categories against ADKS grade distribution (Figure 1). The quote reports the printed percentages for the very knowledgeable group; no effect size printed.

> "Among participants who rated themselves as very knowledgeable, <10% achieved a very good grade, 11.3% attained a good grade, 30.4% obtained a passing grade, and the majority (52.2%) failed."
