Reflective comparison of two parallel 1998 tutoring experiences at Chalmers: an 'ideal group' in the excellently organized 'Computer Science and Engineering in Context' course versus a 'very lazy' group in the 'chaotic' 'Introduction to Electrical Engineering' course, where tutor recruitment finished more than one week after course start.

> "Thus I believe that the picture reﬂects sharply the contrast between two course organizations, which unavoidably inﬂuence the performance of both tutors and students."
