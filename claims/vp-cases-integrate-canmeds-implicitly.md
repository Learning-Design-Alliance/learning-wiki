## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Samira J 2018

Samira J, Wang JY, Richardson L. (2018). Evaluation of virtual patient cases for teaching diagnostic and management skills in internal medicine: a mixed methods study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3463-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative framework-technique analysis of two focus groups (Categories 5 and 6): trainees appreciated not realizing they were learning CanMEDS competencies, e.g. writing admission orders, and that one concept covered multiple competencies such as handover spanning communication and collaboration.

> "The consensus was that VP cases may be a useful resource to integrate CanMEDS roles in medical education."

### Samira J 2018 (2)

Samira J, Wang JY, Richardson L. (2018). Evaluation of virtual patient cases for teaching diagnostic and management skills in internal medicine: a mixed methods study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3463-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Focus group Category 5: participants felt isolating roles like communicator out of context does the physician's job no justice, and that portfolio formats are rigid; the article notes a paucity of literature on integrating CanMEDS.

> "Participants felt that the way CanMEDS breaks down the concept of the physician is reductionist, and not realistic."
