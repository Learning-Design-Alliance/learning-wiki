Multivariate logistic regression (N = 1587) with a bullying × friends social support interaction (Mantel–Haenszel homogeneity p = 0.013). For high friend support the bullying odds ratio was 0.89 (0.40, 1.99, p = 0.775); "Bullied students with moderate (OR = 0.27, 95% CI 0.11–0.65) or low (OR = 0.38, 95% CI 0.19–0.76)" support showed lower odds.

"Bullied students with moderate (OR = 0.27, 95% CI 0.11–0.65) or low (OR = 0.38, 95% CI 0.19–0.76) levels of support had approximately a third of the odds of achieving the academic benchmark, compared to students who were not bullied."
