### Kaldaras 2025

Kaldaras, L., & Wieman, C. (2025). Directed Self-Guided Learning of Blended Math-Science Sensemaking for Historically Marginalized STEM Learners. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1186/s40594-025-00565-z](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00565-z)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of 27 students from backgrounds historically marginalized in STEM at minority-serving 2- and 4-year US colleges completing DSGL activities; the article reports that "Most students started at the lowest and progressed to the highest MSS level". No effect size is printed.

> "Most students started at the lowest and progressed to the highest MSS level and demonstrated considerable short and long-term transfer after completing just one activity, suggesting the effectiveness of this approach for fostering transferable MSS."
