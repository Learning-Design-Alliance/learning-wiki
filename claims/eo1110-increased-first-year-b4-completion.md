# EO 1110 significantly increased first-year completion of the quantitative reasoning (B4) requirement, roughly doubling completion among students needing additional support

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` About 73 percent of the 2018 cohort completed the B4 requirement in their first year versus about 60 percent of the 2017 cohort; among students needing support, completion rose from 25 percent ('not college ready', 2017) to 59 percent (Category III) and 49 percent (Category IV). [→ Bracco 2021](#bracco-2021)

## Evidence

### Bracco 2021

Bracco, K. R., Barrat, V. X., Skjoldhorne, S., & Finkelstein, N. (2021). Student Progress Before and After California State University's Executive Order 1110. WestEd. http://csustudentsuccess.org/multiple-measures

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort comparison of B4 (quantitative reasoning) GE completion in the first year using CSU Chancellor's Office enrollment data for the 2017 and 2018 cohorts. The report states support-designated students' completion "more doubled in the first year of implementation" of the new policy.

> "whereas only 25 percent of the students in the 2017 cohort who were designated as “not college ready ” completed the B4 requirement in their first year, 59 percent of the students in the 2018 cohort who were designated as Cat egory III, and 49 percent of those designated as Category IV, completed the requirement."
