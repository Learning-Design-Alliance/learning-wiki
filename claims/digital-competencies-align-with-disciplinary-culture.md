The preface summarizes case studies by Starkey, Yates, Lundqvist, Ormond and Syvester across disciplines including Applied Statistics, Critical Indigenous Studies, and Geography in New Zealand, concluding that "higher education teachers require digital competencies that align with disciplinary culture and the specific technologies available."
