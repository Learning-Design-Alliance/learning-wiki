## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Features experts identify as relevant to choosing a basic approach are descriptions of states and conditions of the physical situation, including derived or transformed second-order features such as a before-and-after situation or no external forces. [→ Chi et al. 1981](#chi-et-al-1981)

## Evidence

### Chi et al. 1981

Michelene Chi, Paul Feltovich, Robert Glaser. (1981). Categorization and Representation of Physics Problems by Experts and Novices. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0502_2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Study Four feature-citation analysis (Table 11): two experts and two novices stated the problem features leading to their basic approach for 20 problems; experts cited derived features such as "before and after situation" and "no external forces," which the authors call second-order features.

> "Features idenad by the experts can be characterized as descriptions of the states and conditions of the physical situation described by the problem."
