## Evidence

### Sarah Bowen 2012

Sarah Bowen, Kevin Haworth, Joel Grow, Mavis Tsai, and Robert Kohlenberg. (2012). Interpersonal Mindfulness Informed by Functional Analytic Psychotherapy: Findings from a Pilot Randomized Trial. International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy, Vol. 7, No. 2-3. [doi:10.1037/h0100931](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0100931)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomized trial with undergraduate students (N=104) assigned to interpersonal, intrapersonal, or control conditions; ANCOVAs covarying baseline levels showed "Significant differences were found on the AAQ at post-intervention" between control and interpersonal groups, maintained at 48 hours but not at 2 weeks (p = .068).

> "Significant differences were found on the AAQ at post-intervention, F(2, 97) = 5.65, p = 005, specifically between the control and interpersonal group (p = .001). Between group differences remained significant at the 48-hour follow-up F(2, 61) = 4.67, p = .013, with differences maintained between the control and interpersonal group (p = .031)."
