### Kaplan Ceren 2026

Kaplan Ceren, Çalışkaner Melis Ünsal. (2026). Self-Perceived Confidence in Pediatric Dental Procedures Among Final-Year Dental Students. International Journal of Paediatric Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1111/ipd.70142

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A census cross-sectional survey of all 46 final-year dental students at Istanbul Atlas University used a 15-item 5-point Likert questionnaire. Descriptive means show highest confidence for "fissure sealant application (4.24 ± 0.87)" and "oral hygiene instruction and dietary advice (4.07 ± 0.95)".

> "The highest mean confidence scores were observed for fissure sealant application (4.24 ± 0.87), oral hygiene instruction and dietary advice (4.07 ± 0.95), topical fluoride application (4.02 ± 0.93), communication with the parent of a pediatric patient (4.02 ± 0.91), and primary tooth extraction (3.80 ± 0.86)."
