## Evidence

### Yoshii Hatsumi 2012

Yoshii Hatsumi, Watanabe Yuichiro, Kitamura Hideaki, Nan Zhang, Akazawa Kouhei. (2012). Effect of an Education Program on Improving Help-Seeking among Parents of Junior and Senior High School Students in Japan. Global Journal of Health Science. https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v4n1p33

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study of 2690 parents (2465 completing both questionnaires) who answered a help-seeking questionnaire before and one week after viewing the education program; McNemar's test on paired responses showed a significant increase in help-seeking within one week, "for all symptom categories except sleeplessness".

> "After the education program, the rate of participants who sought help within 1 week was significantly higher (p=0.001), as compared with the first questionnaire, for all symptom categories except sleeplessness."
