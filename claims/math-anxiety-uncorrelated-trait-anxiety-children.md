## Evidence

### Wu SS 2012

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the same sample of 162 children comparing SEMA scores with the CBCL Anxiety Problems subscale. The article reports "r = 0.08, p = 0.37, R2 = 0.01", a non-significant correlation; no standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "SEMA scores were not signiﬁcantly correlated with trait anx- iety, as measured by the Anxiety Problems subscale of the CBCL (r = 0.08, p = 0.37, R2 = 0.01)."
