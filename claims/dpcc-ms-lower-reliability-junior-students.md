### Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc 2017

Renaud Jean-Sébastien; Côté Luc. (2017). Validation of the 5-item doctor-patient communication competency instrument for medical students (DPCC-MS) using two years of assessment data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1026-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the junior-senior alpha difference in the phase 1 rotation assessment data. "assessors have clearer and more uniform performance expectations for students at the end of MD training", which they link to more reliable judgment for senior students.

> "One explanation for this finding may be that performance is assessed against an expected level of performance (i.e. superior/expected/borderline/insufficient), but assessors have clearer and more uniform performance expectations for students at the end of MD training"
