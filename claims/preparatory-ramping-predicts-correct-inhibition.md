### Constantinidis Christos 2019

Constantinidis Christos, Luna Beatriz. (2019). Neural Substrates of Inhibitory Control Maturation in Adolescence. Trends in Neurosciences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2019.07.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports neurophysiological recordings from the antisaccade preparatory fixation period in adolescent and adult monkeys, citing Zhou, showing age differences in ramping and trial-level prediction of correct responses.

> "Baseline activity was higher overall in the adult group and increased (“ramped”) with a faster rate prior to the onset of the stimulus (Fig. 3C). The level of this activity was also predictive of behavior, as correct trials were characterized by a higher firing rate prior to the onset of the stimulus."
