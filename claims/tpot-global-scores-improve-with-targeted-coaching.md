## Evidence

### Hemmeter et al. 2015

Mary Louise Hemmeter, Jessica K. Hardy, Alana G. Schnitz, Jessie Morris Adams, and Kiersten A. Kinder. (2015). Effects of Training and Coaching With Performance Feedback on Teachers' Use of Pyramid Model Practices. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121415594924

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The TPOT, administered as an approximately 2-hr observation plus 15- to 20-min interview with 108 indicators under 15 key practice items, was used four times per teacher. Environmental items increased in all classrooms; red flags decreased for two teachers and stayed the same for one.

> "There was an increase in the percentage of indicators on the TPOT from baseline to final data collection by approximately 21% for Bianca, 3% for Kendra, and 26% for Susan."
