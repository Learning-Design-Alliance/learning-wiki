## Evidence

### Žuljević Marija Franka 2022

Žuljević Marija Franka, Buljan Ivan. (2022). Academic and non-academic predictors of academic performance in medical school: an exploratory cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03436-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Attrition-bias analysis comparing the 65 completers with participants lost to follow-up in the prospective cohort study; completers had higher intrinsic motivation (P=0.009). The logistic regression gave OR=1.05 (95%CI=1.01-1.09), which the authors call "the effect size was small"; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Results of a logistic regression model also showed that intrinsic motivation significantly predicted whether a student completed the study, although the effect size was small (OR=1.05, 95%CI=1.01-1.09)."
