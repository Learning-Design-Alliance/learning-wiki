Discussion of challenges from the 2020 survey respondents and authors' observations: Indian optometry students are young and financially and emotionally dependent on family, so "educators do not believe that learners will engage themselves in self-directed learning". Clinical exposure is significantly challenged.

> "Therefore, educators do not believe that learners will engage themselves in self-directed learning. Further, the lockdown has imposed the challenges of solo learning on them. Hence, the total and long term transition to e-learning is challenged."
