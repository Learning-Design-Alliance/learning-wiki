# Treatment teachers received significantly more intensive mentoring activities than control teachers

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Treatment teachers spent more time per week meeting with mentors than control teachers (coefficient = 16.90, p < .05). [→ Hong 2013](#hong-2013)

## Evidence

### Hong 2013

Hong, Y., & Hong, G. (2013). Making Sense of the “Zero Effect” of Comprehensive Teacher Induction Programs: A Mediational Analysis. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563315

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase I analysis of the cluster randomized trial. Treatment teachers had 62.79 (sd = 74.61) minutes per week meeting with mentors; control teachers spent 45.90 (sd = 110.89) minutes per week with mentors.

> "We have identified significant differences in the number of mentors (coefficient =.34, se = .07, t = 4.79. p < .001) and in the intensity of mentoring activities (coefficient = 16.90, se = 7.54, t = 2.24. p < .05) between the treatment and control groups."
