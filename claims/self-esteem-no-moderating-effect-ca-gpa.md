## Evidence

### Al Awaji Nisreen 2022

Al Awaji Nisreen, Zaidi Uzma, Awad Salwa S., Alroqaiba Nouf, Aldhahi Monira I., Alsaleh Hadeel, Akil Shahnaz, Mortada Eman M. (2022). Moderating Effects of Self-Esteem on the Relationship between Communication Anxiety and Academic Performance among Female Health College Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113960

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical moderated regression with GPA as criterion in the survey of 287 students; the third model added the PRCA × RSE interaction (∆R2 = 0.000, R2 = 0.096, F = 0.94, p > 0.05). The article reports "had no significant effect"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Finally, the interaction between self-esteem and PRCA was added to the third model and showed that self-esteem, the hypothesized moderating variable, had no significant effect on the relationships between all predictors and GPA."
