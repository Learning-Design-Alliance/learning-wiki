## Evidence

### Saniee Nadia 2026

Saniee Nadia, Seidi Masoumeh, Amiri Fatemeh. (2026). Blended learning in laboratory science education: comparison of flipped classroom and traditional lectures in immunohematology training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09985-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Parallel-group randomized controlled study over five fall semesters (2020–2024) with 196 students allocated to control (n = 100) or intervention (n = 96). Between-group comparison of laboratory OSPE scores found a significant difference favoring the intervention group, with "effect size = 1.46".

> "the mean (± SD) laboratory OSPE score was 16.470 ± 1.113 in the control group and 18.192 ± 1.247 in the intervention group. A statistically significant difference was observed in the mean laboratory OSPE scores between the two groups (P-value < 0.001)."
