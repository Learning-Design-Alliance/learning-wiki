### Tekin Murat 2026

Tekin Murat, Korkmaz Güneş, Kılınçarslan Mehmet Göktuğ, Toraman Çetin, Tekeş Ender, Çelik Taylan, Akar Sacide Güzin Mazman, Alakurt Turgay, Saygılı Emre Sedar, Alimoğlu Mustafa Kemal, Yurdal Mustafa Onur, Akdikmen Yiğit. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured focus group interviews with 34 Round 1 pilot participants (19 Year-IV, 15 Year-V), triangulated with screen/room video recordings, identified excitement as a theme. One student said, "It was exciting to wait and see where I would apply what I had learned in the puzzle."

> "Medical students experienced great excitement in applying what they learned through the escape rooms. The escape rooms were structured according to the learning outcomes of the committees and clerkships the students had completed."
