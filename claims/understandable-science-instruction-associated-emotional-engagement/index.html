Xin Xia 2026¶

Xin Xia, Robert Tai. (2026). Exploring the association of student perceptions of science instruction and emotional engagement: The variance between grade level. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09800-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Main-effects multilevel model (Model 2) of 6,465 students in 25 schools, which the article identifies as the best model for research question two. The printed coefficient is "b = 0.18, CI = [0.16, 0.21], p-value < 0.001"; no standardized effect size is reported.

"For each unit increase in students' perception of understandable science instruction, the emotional engagement score statistically significantly increases by 0.18, holding all other variables constant ( b = 0.18, CI = [0.16, 0.21], p-value < 0.001)."
