Evidence¶

Guha 2016¶

Guha, R., Hyler, M.E., and Darling-Hammond, L. (2016). The Teacher Residency: An Innovative Model for Preparing Teachers. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-residency

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's synthesis of retention studies, summarized in Table 1, compiles descriptive and regression-based comparisons across programs including Boston, Memphis, Denver, and San Francisco, finding "high retention rates of their graduates". Most studies are descriptive; two rigorous studies found statistically significant retention differences controlling for school and district characteristics.

"Studies of teacher residency programs consistently point to the high retention rates of their graduates, even after several years in the profession, ranging from rates of 80–90% in the same district after three years and 70–80% after five years"
