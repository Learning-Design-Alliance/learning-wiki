## Evidence

### Spaans I 2025

Spaans I, Verbree A-R, de Kleijn R, Dilaver G. (2025). Role models in medicine and beyond: a survey study exploring the relationship between role models, academic performance and sense of belonging. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08257-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of self-reported GPA (scale 1–10) among medical students; valid n = 454 due to missing data. The article reports the uncorrected association (p =.029, d=−0.207) and that after Bonferroni correction the associations were no longer significant (number of role models: p =.135, ρ =.093).

> "Among medical students, having role models correlated positively with their GPA at the conventional significance level (p ≤.05) (p =.029, d=−0.207). However, after Bonferroni correction (p ≤.0036) having role models and the number of role models were no longer significantly associated with GPA"
