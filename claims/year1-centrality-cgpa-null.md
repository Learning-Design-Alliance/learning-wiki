`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Year 1 Mann-Whitney comparisons of CGPA by top-three centrality status (Fig. 5); the article reports negligible effect sizes for in-degree (r = -0.01), closeness (r = 0.05), and betweenness (r = -0.05), all non-significant.

> "This indicates that being highly nominated by peers did not correspond with academic performance in this cohort. The wide interquartile ranges further suggest substantial variation in CGPA regardless of nomination frequency."
