# The general compassion reference factor uniquely predicts only student support, while the trust facet incrementally predicts teacher enthusiasm

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Once variance shared with respectfulness and trust was partialized out, the general compassion reference factor was positively related only to student support (β = .129). [→ Burić 2023](#buric-2023)
`q2 i1` Trust showed incremental value in explaining teacher enthusiasm (β = .120) over and above the general compassion reference factor. [→ Burić 2023](#buric-2023)
