### Kielstra 2022

Kielstra, J., Molenaar, I., van Steensel, R., & Verhoeven, L. (2022). 'Supporting socially shared regulation during collaborative task‑oriented reading'. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 17, 65–105. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09365-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 938 metacognitive episodes coded for the four types of intra-group social metacognitive interaction (ignored, accepted, shared, co-constructed). "47 episodes (9.71%) were coded as co-constructed intra-group metacognitive interactions during pre-task discussions." Diversity across groups was great, e.g. Groups E, H co-constructed most.

> "During pre-task regulation, 363 (75.00%) metacognitive episodes were coded as accepted intra-group interactions. On average per group, accepted interaction occurred during 33 (SD = 12.44) episodes."
