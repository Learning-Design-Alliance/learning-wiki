## Evidence

### Petelinsek S 2025

Petelinsek S, Zhang P, Colbert-Getz J, Hartridge T, Furman N, Awad E, Kelner R, Hughes PG. (2025). Getting lost to find insights - a quasi-experimental evaluation of outdoor experiential learning as a method to teach clinical decision-making and decision science concepts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07533-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative debrief observation after the guided hike in which senior medical students were instructed to intentionally get lost. Students attributed their silence to feeling it was not their place, assuming the senior student knew something they did not, and no one else speaking up.

> "During the debrief many students reported knowing that their group had made incorrect turns, and feeling lost, however very few students reported speaking up or questioning the senior medical student."
