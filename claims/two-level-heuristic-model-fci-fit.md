## Evidence

### Paco Hernando Talero Lopez 2024

Paco Hernando Talero Lopez. (2024). A Computational Model for the Evolution of Learning Physical Micro-Contents in Peer Instruction Methodology. Doctoral thesis, Instituto Politécnico Nacional. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.07055](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.07055)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Monte Carlo virtual experiment (1000 virtual students, 100 virtual universities) fitting the FCI distribution of 786 Bogotá students. The KS test "must be accepted with a significance level of α = 0 .05"; the model implies 53% answered with no knowledge and 47% ruled out one option with certainty.

> "The results found do not allow us to reject the null hypothesis and it must be accepted with a significance level of α = 0 .05, which corresponds to a 5% probability of committing a type I error"
