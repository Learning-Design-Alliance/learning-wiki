## Evidence

### Guan 2026

Guan, K., Zhang, Z., Liu, Z. et al. (2026). Longitudinal associations between sedentary behavior types and mathematics ability mediated by externalizing and internalizing problems. npj Sci. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00424-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Negative binomial regression of Millennium Cohort Study data tested age-11 sedentary behavior against age-17 mathematics ability, stratified by sex. Among females, reading most days was associated with "a 24.4% increase in mathematics ability score" (IRR = 1.244); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Among females, reading at least once a week (IRR = 1.144, 95%CI: 1.027–1.276) or most days (IRR  = 1.244, 95%CI: 1.118–1.385) were associated with a 14.4% increase and a 24.4% increase in mathematics ability score , respectively."
