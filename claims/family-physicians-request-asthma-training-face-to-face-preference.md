## Evidence

### Çifci İ. 2025

Çifci İ., Gök U., Yorgancıoğlu A. (2025). A current assessment of family physicians’ knowledge and attitudes toward asthma management in primary care in Turkey according to the GINA strategy report. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 36, 5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00468-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 596 family physicians from all 12 NUTS regions of Turkey. In the attitude/training-preference items, "64.9%" preferred face-to-face training on asthma diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up, 32% preferred online training, and 3% did not want training.

> "The percentage of those who want to receive face-to-face training on asthma diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up is 64.9%, those who prefer online training is 32%, and those who do not want to receive training is 3%."
