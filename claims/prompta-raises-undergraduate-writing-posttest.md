## Evidence

### Puro Vezolu 2025

Puro Vezolu, Nittala Noel Anurag Prashanth, A Hariharasudan. (2025). PROMPTa—A multifaceted assessment approach to develop writing skills. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0337342

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment comparing an experimental group using PROMPTa's peer, mentor, and expert feedback (n = 46) with a traditionally assessed control group (n = 46). The post-test t-test showed "Cohen's d = 1.25 (95% CI [0.80, 1.69])", a 3.26-point mean difference described as a 24 percent improvement over the control group.

> "The mean difference of 3.26 points was statistically significant (t (81) = 5.88, p < 0.001), indicating the intervention's effectiveness. The standardized mean difference was Cohen's d = 1.25 (95% CI [0.80, 1.69]), indicating a large effect size."
