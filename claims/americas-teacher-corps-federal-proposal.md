### Steven Glazerman 2010

Steven Glazerman, Dan Goldhaber, Susanna Loeb, Douglas O. Staiger, Grover J. Whitehurst. (2010). America's Teacher Corps. Washington, DC: The Brookings Institution. https://www.brookings.edu/research/americas-teacher-corps

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

This is the authors' policy proposal statement, an expert recommendation rather than an empirical test. It argues that teachers documented as highly effective through district or state evaluation systems "that comply with federal standards" would qualify for ATC membership. No data or evaluation of the proposal is reported on the page.

> "We propose the creation through federal legislation of America’s Teacher Corps (ATC). Highly effective K-12 public school teachers, as documented through district or state evaluation systems that comply with federal standards, would qualify for membership in the ATC."
