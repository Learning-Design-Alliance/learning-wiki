Evidence¶

Jinhee Kim 2026¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Adult learners' perspectives of AI applications in supporting andragogy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10621-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Transformative-learning theme from the 20-participant interviews: participants described simulations and exploratory environments immersing them in real-world scenarios requiring belief revision (P4), and AI facilitators guiding structured role-play and debate with context-specific prompts (P15).

"ALs highlighted that conversational agents, such as AI facilitators or thought partners, can guide them through structured role-play and debate"

Jinhee Kim 2026 (2)¶

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Adult learners' perspectives of AI applications in supporting andragogy. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10621-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Experiential-learning theme from the same interviews: AI simulations let learners practice in "safe, risk-free environments that closely resemble actual job scenarios" (P6), and virtual trips help connect learning to otherwise inaccessible real-world settings (P14).

"ALs highlighted the potential of immersive technologies, including AR, VR, and XR, when merged with AI companions or facilitators that guide them through these environments in context-sensitive ways (P8)."
