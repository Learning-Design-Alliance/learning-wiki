Evidence¶

Thibault Asselborn 2021¶

Thibault Asselborn, Wafa Johal, Bolat Tleubayev, Zhanel Zhexenova, Pierre Dillenbourg, Catherine McBride and Anara Sandygulova. (2021). The transferability of handwriting skills: from the Cyrillic to the Latin alphabet. npj Science of Learning 6:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00084-w

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' forward-looking interpretation in the abstract and Discussion, grounded in their finding that the dynamic features evolved similarly across grades in both alphabets and were mostly rank-correlated across scripts. No effect size printed.

"One interesting side-effect of these ﬁndings is that the algorithms that we developed for the diagnosis of handwriting dif ﬁculties among French-speaking children could be relevant for other alphabets, paving the way for the creation of a cross-lingual model for the detection of handwriting dif ﬁculties."
