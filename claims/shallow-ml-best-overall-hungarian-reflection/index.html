Evidence¶

Zsolt Csibi 2026¶

Zsolt Csibi, Kristian Fenech, Mónika Sándor, Mónika Serfőző, Kinga Gyöngy. (2026). Automatic Reflection Level Classification in Hungarian Student Essays. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02402 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.02402

q2 · i? · design · r2

5-fold cross-validated experiment on the Hungarian essay dataset comparing shallow models with balancing techniques and engineered features. The best SMOTEBoost configuration reached "an average score of 0.7176" with 0.7089 accuracy, 0.6808 F1 and 0.7633 ROC-AUC.

"This model achieved an average score of 0.7176(averaged over the 3 metrics), with 0.7089 mean accuracy (with 0.0263 standard deviation), 0.6808 mean F1-score (with 0.0309 standard deviation) and 0.7633 mean ROC-AUC score (with 0.0285 standard deviation) averaged over the 5 folds."

Related Claims¶

  • T-tests show no significant shallow-vs-transformer difference in accuracy, weighted F1 or macro-F1, but a significant ROC-AUC advantage for the shallow model — related
  • Hungarian transformer models achieve slightly lower overall score (about 68%) but better minority-class sensitivity than shallow models — related
