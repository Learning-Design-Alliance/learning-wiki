Evidence¶

Zhao J 2026¶

Zhao J, Yang K, Gao H and Jiang G (2026). Generative artificial intelligence and the transformation of medical education: a scoping review. Front. Educ. 11:1907589. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1907589

q3 · i? · review · r3

Outcome coding across the included corpus using an adapted Kirkpatrick hierarchy. The review warns "Objective" was broad and included immediate task scores, so counts should not be read as demonstrating learning gain.

"75 reports combined an objective learner, task-product, or assessment-process measure with learner-reported outcomes; 37 contained objective measures only; and 41 relied on self-report or qualitative evidence alone."

Zhao J 2026 (2)¶

Zhao J, Yang K, Gao H and Jiang G (2026). Generative artificial intelligence and the transformation of medical education: a scoping review. Front. Educ. 11:1907589. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1907589

q3 · i? · review · r2

Follow-up charting across included reports. Evidence clustered at reaction and short-term learning or performance levels rather than durable behavior or downstream effect.

"Only 13 reports contained an identifiable follow-up or retention signal. Follow-up intervals were generally short, and only a few examined transfer to a new case, later examination, or OSCE. No report evaluated patient health outcomes attributable to a GenAI educational intervention."
