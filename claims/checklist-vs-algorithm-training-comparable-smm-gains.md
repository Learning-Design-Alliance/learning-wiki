`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results of the cluster-randomized comparison of checklist-based (15 teams) versus algorithm-based (14 teams) training. No interaction reached significance for communication or total similarity (partial η² = 0.00 and 0.08); the task responsibility interaction approached but did not reach the α = 0.05 threshold.

> "No time × training group interactions reached statistical significance; task responsibility showed a trend toward a larger increase in the checklist group (F(1,27) = 3.79, p=.06, η²p = .12), whereas communication and total similarity did not (Table 3)."

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The time × group interaction for Task Responsibility Similarity approached significance, F(1,27) = 3.79, p = .06, partial η² = .12, suggesting a potential trend toward larger gains in the checklist group that the authors state warrants confirmation in adequately powered studies.
