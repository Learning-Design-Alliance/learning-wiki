Evidence¶

Kaushal 2019¶

Kaushal, R. K., & Panda, S. N. (2019). A Meta Analysis on Effective Conditions to Offer Animation Based Teaching Style. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 16(1), 129-153. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1219792

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Authors' interpretation of the meta-analytic comparison of weighted mean effect sizes (0.58 LSA vs 0.34 HSA) in the spatial-ability condition. The article offers the explanation that LSA students "cannot make independent mental models" and animations present a ready-made model, but states this belief "needs to be proven statistically".

"Another outcome of this research states that LSA students on the average, performed better than HSA students in the first condition."
