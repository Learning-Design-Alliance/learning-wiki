Evidence¶

Jake Renzella 2026¶

Jake Renzella, Christian Bergh, Natasha Banks, and Alexandra Vassar. (2026). Gender Differences in AI Literacy Workshop Outcomes and Deepfake Engagement. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14718 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.14718

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Pre-workshop logistic regression controlling for year level and school on the baseline survey. The article reports "OR=1.81" for schoolwork use and "OR=2.35" for advice-seeking, both significant at p<.05; problem-solving and recreational use differences were not significant.

"Females were significantly more likely to reportusingAIforschoolwork(65.0%vs.49.5%,OR=1.81,p=.045)andseeking advice (27.0% vs. 14.1%, OR=2.35,p=.021)."
