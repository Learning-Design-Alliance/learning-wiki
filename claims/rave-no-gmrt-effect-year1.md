## Evidence

### McKeown 2018

McKeown, M. G., Crosson, A. C., Moore, D. W., & Beck, I. L. (2018). Word Knowledge and Comprehension Effects of an Academic Vocabulary Intervention for Middle School Students. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831217744181

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed analysis of GMRT extended scale scores for the full Year 1 sample controlling for pretest. Both groups improved over time, but the article reports no treatment advantage; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant interaction of time and treatment condition on GMRT ESS scores, F(1, 102) = 0.84, p = .36. There was no main effect of group on GMRT ESS scores, F(1, 102) = 0.27, p = .60"
