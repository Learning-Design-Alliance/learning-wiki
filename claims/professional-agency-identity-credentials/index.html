The Critical 2007¶

The Critical, Relational Practice of Instructional Design in Higher Education: An Emerging Model of Change Agency. (2007). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-007-9061-6

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of designer narratives in the study; participants reported that credentials affected credibility, with one designer noting the PhD was "the 'ticket'" that got him respect interacting as a faculty member but not as an instructional designer. Designers often entered via alternate career paths, learning design on the job.

"Professional agency is characterized by a feeling of responsibility to the profession – to do instructional design well and to act in a professionally competent and ethical manner."
