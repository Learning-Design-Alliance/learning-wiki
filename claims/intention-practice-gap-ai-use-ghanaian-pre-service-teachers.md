### Amponsah 2026 (2)

Education Quarterly Reviews, 9(3), 51-65. https://doi.org/10.31014/aior.1993.09.03.724

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' discussion of the intention-behaviour gap in the survey findings. They attribute it to "limited access to reliable digital infrastructure, insufficient professional development, and unclear institutional policies".

> "However, despite strong behavioural intentions to use AI, actual classroom integration remained moderate, suggesting that AI adoption among Ghanaian pre-service science teachers is still in its developmental stage."
