Murad Amal A., Sawalha Murad A., Maabreh Roqia Saleem, Elsabagh Eman E., Roque Mark Y., Alharbi Muna, Abdelkader Raghad, Saleh Zyad T. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative theme from interviews and focus groups with 28 male nursing students in a maternal-newborn clinical course at Taibah University. Students said mannequin practice let them apply lecture knowledge in a controlled setting; one stated it "became helpful for me during my licensure examination".

> "Simulation-based training (SBT) using mannequins was recognized as a key factor in bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application in maternal-newborn nursing education."
