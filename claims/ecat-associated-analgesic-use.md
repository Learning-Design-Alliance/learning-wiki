## Evidence

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted logistic regression in the same 160-patient prospective cohort. The article states "each one-unit increase in case difficulty corresponds to a 20% higher likelihood of analgesic intake"; age showed a borderline association (OR 1.03, 95% CI: 1.00–1.07, p = 0.061).

> "A logistic regression model further confirmed that higher E-CAT scores were significantly associated with greater odds of postoperative analgesic use (OR 1.20, 95% CI: 1.06–1.36, p = 0.004)"

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026 (2)

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared analysis of analgesic use across E-CAT groups (low N=53, moderate N=88, high N=19) in the prospective cohort. Analgesic use was "significantly associated with case complexity (p < 0.001)"; sex, tooth type, and diagnosis showed no significant associations.

> "Post-treatment analgesic usage was significantly associated with case complexity (p < 0.001), increasing sharply from 22.6% in the low E-CAT group to 73.7% in the high E-CAT group"
