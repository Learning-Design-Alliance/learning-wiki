## Evidence

### Mandic Ruzica Nikolic 2024

Mandic Ruzica Nikolic, Bjegovic-Mikanovic Vesna, Bukumiric Zoran, Terzic-Supic Zorica, Todorovic Jovana, Grujicic Sandra, Nesic Dejan, Laaser Ulrich. (2024). Exploring factors of physicians' preferences for continuing medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06374-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robust regression analysis of cross-sectional survey data from 2,625 Serbian physicians, with domain scores as dependent variables. The study reports "Female physicians appreciate more than males all competencies grouped in management (B = 3.535, p < 0.001)" and significant associations in every domain.

> "Female physicians appreciate more than males all competencies grouped in management (B = 3.535, p < 0.001), clinical practice (B = 1.182, p < 0.001), communication (B = 0.830, p < 0.001), public health (B = 2.801, p < 0.001), essentials (B = 3.361, p < 0.001), and clinical emergency domains (B=1.601, p < 0.001)."
