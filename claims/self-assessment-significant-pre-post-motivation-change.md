## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the self-assessment (experimental) group, paired-sample t-test differences between pre- and post-motivation questionnaire scores were statistically significant (p = .045). [→ Salimi and Larsari 2015](#salimi-and-larsari-2015)

## Evidence

### Salimi and Larsari 2015

Asghar Salimi, Vahid Nowrozi Larsari. (2015). On the Comparative Impact of Self-assessment and Teacher-assessment on Iranian EFL Learners' Academic Motivation. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.6p.74

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test on pre/post AMTB motivation scores of the 30-learner self-assessment group in a quasi-experiment at Novin-Rezvan institute; the article reports the change as "significant since the level of significance is .045". No effect size is printed.

> "the differences between the scores of the motivation questionnaires in the pre- and post-motivation in the experimental group are significant since the level of significance is .045 which is smaller than the threshold level 0.05."
