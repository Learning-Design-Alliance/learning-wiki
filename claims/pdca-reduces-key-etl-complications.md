> "The incidence rates of oesophageal intubation and laryngeal edema or vocal cord injury in the experimental group were 6.8% (3/44) and 4.5% (2/44), respectively. These rates were significantly lower than those in the control group, which were 27.8% and 30.5%, showing a statistically significant difference (P < 0.01)."
