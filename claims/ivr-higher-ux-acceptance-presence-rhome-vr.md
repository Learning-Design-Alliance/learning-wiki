## Evidence

### Paolo Boffi 2026

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects RHOME-VR study with 161 participants aged 18-35, IVR tested in lab and DVR/control delivered remotely during the COVID-19 pandemic. The article reports that "IVR significantly outperformed control and DVR" on overall UX, and IVR also scored higher on TAM overall and Perceived Usefulness.

> "In overall UX, Welch's test revealed that IVR significantly outperformed control and DVR (IVR vs control:F(1,107) = 12.04,p < .001; IVR vs DVR:F(1,106) = 14.23,p < .001)"

### Paolo Boffi 2026 (2)

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RHOME-VR between-subjects study, presence measured with Witmer and Singer's Presence Questionnaire was higher for IVR than DVR; the difference was "driven by Involvement" and Immersion subscales, while Sensory Fidelity and Interface Quality showed no differences.

> "Presence was higher in IVR than DVR, as revealed by a Welch's test (F(1,106) = 14.54,p < .001), driven by Involvement (F(1,106) = 20.41,p < .001) and Immersion (F(1,106) = 16.34,p < .001)"
