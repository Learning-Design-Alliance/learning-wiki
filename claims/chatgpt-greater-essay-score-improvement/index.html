Fan 2025¶

Fan, Y., Tang, L., Le, H., Shen, K., Tan, S., Zhao, Y., Shen, Y., Li, X., & Gasevic, D. (2025). Beware of Metacognitive Laziness: Effects of Generative Artificial Intelligence on Learning Motivation, Processes, and Performance. British Journal of Educational Technology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13544

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

ANOVA on researcher-scored essays (inter-rater ICCs all above 0.85) in the randomised four-group experiment. Pairwise Tukey comparisons showed the AI group's improvement exceeded CN (mean difference = 1.970, p-adjusted = 0.037), HE (2.120, 0.025) and CL (2.200, 0.012).

"The ANOVA results indicated no signiﬁcant diﬀerences in essay scores (F=1.275, p=0.286, η2=0.033) before revision, but signiﬁcant diﬀerences in score improvements (F=4.549, p=0.005, η2=0.108) after revision."
