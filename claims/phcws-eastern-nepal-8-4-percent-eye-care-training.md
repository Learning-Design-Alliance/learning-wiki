## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 107 government-employed PHCWs in 35 health posts in Sagarmatha zone, Nepal, asking about eye care training since starting their professional career. The study found "Only 8.4% of PHCWs had received eye care training during their professional career post qualification".

> "Only 8.4% of PHCWs had received eye care training during their professional career post qualification. (Table 2). Those that had received eye training as a professional performed slightly better on the knowledge and skills questionnaire compared to those that had not received training"
