# The SYMP Test shows satisfactory test-retest reliability, with overall-score correlations between .72 and .86 across Grades 1–6

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Test-retest correlations for the SYMP Test overall score ranged between .72 and .86 across all grades, indicating satisfactory reliability. [→ Brankaer 2017](#brankaer-2017)

## Evidence

### Brankaer 2017

Brankaer, C., Ghesquière, P., & De Smedt, B. (2017). Symbolic magnitude processing in elementary school children: A group administered paper-and-pencil measure (SYMP Test). Behavior Research Methods, 49, 1361–1373. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-016-0792-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test-retest analysis in a large sample retested 3–5 weeks after first assessment (n = 1,425). Overall scores summed across the two subtests yielded test-retest correlations "rs between .72 and .86, ps < .01," exceeding the .70 criterion the authors cite for adequate reliability.

> "This yielded higher test-retest correlations for these overall scores in all grades (rs between .72 and .86, ps < .01), which suggests that both subtests should be administered at the same time when assessing symbolic magnitude processing."
