Naghshi Maryam, Janati Ali, Raoofi Samira, Khodayari-Zarnaq Rahim. (2024). Exploring the Challenges of Implementing Managers' Competency Assessment Center in the Health System: A Phenomenological Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v34i6.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenological interview study of 33 key informants analyzed by conventional content analysis. In the cultural challenges sub-theme, participants identified senior managers' lack of belief in assessment as "the most important cultural challenge that more than 80% of the participants mentioned." No effect size is reported.

> "The lack of belief in assessment by senior managers, which shows the wrong organizational culture of some organizations, is the most important cultural challenge that more than 80% of the participants mentioned."
