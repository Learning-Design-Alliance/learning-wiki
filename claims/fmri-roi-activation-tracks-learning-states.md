### Tenison 2015

Tenison, C., & Anderson, J. R. (2015). Modeling the distinct phases of skill acquisition. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ROI linear mixed-effects analysis of percent activation with S1-S2 and S2-S3 probability-contrast fixed effects (Table 4). For left HIPS, the S1-S2 slope was .06 (p=.02) and S2-S3 slope .08 (p<.001); random slopes did not improve model fit, suggesting invariance across subjects.

> "We find that increased activity in the left HIPS is associated with increased activity of being State 1 rather than State 2, and of being in State 2 rather than State 3 (Figure 5)."
