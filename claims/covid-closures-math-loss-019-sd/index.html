Evidence¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

Difference-in-differences estimation on school-level panel data of Flemish catholic schools (2015-2020), regressing school-average standardised mathematics scores on a 2020 dummy with school, grade 6, teacher and grade 4 controls. The "effect size ranges between minus 0.18 standard deviations and minus 0.25 standard deviations" across specifications, an effect size below d = 0.2 at its headline magnitude of 0.19.

"The effect size ranges between minus 0.18 standard deviations and minus 0.25 standard deviations, signiﬁcant at, at least, the 5%-level."
