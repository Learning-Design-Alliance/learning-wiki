Inductive coding of trainer responses in the Polish qualitative study; the Empathy-Based Teaching category (30 'Good atmosphere' codes in Table 1) was defined by what "according to the respondents, helps in overcoming barriers related to the fear of technology".

> "Empathy-Based Teaching refers to the trainer’s distinctive approach towards the older adult, an approach full of empathy, patience, and building a positive atmosphere, which, according to the respondents, helps in overcoming barriers related to the fear of technology while also building self-confidence."
