### Bahsoun Soukaina 2026

Bahsoun Soukaina, Morris Mhairi, Akam Elizabeth. (2026). Cartooning in Biological Learning. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quiz-based assessment across the part A and part B modules: question-level averages from two quizzes (10 questions each) were combined into a single dataset of 20 observations and compared with Mann–Whitney U after Shapiro–Wilk and Levene's tests. The article reports "a significant statistical difference in performance when comparing cartooned and non‐cartooned subtopics", with medians of 50.55 vs 81.74. No effect size is printed.

> "Mann–Whitney U test showed a significant statistical difference in performance when comparing cartooned and non‐cartooned subtopics (U = 12.0, p = 0.004, median (IQR) 50.55 (31.69) vs. 81.74 (19.32), respectively)."
