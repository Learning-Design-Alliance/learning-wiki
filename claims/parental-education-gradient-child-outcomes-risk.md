### Fartein Ask Torvik 2020

Fartein Ask Torvik, Eilertsen Espen Moen, Tom A. McAdams, Kristin Gustavson, Henrik Daae Zachrisson, Ragnhild Brandlistuen, Line C. Gjerde, Alexandra Havdahl, Camilla Stoltenberg, Helga Ask, Eivind Ystrom. (2020). Mechanisms linking parental educational attainment with child ADHD, depression, and academic problems: a study of extended families in The Norwegian Mother, Father and Child Cohort Study. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.13197

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of the MoBa extended-family sample of 34,958 children, with ADHD and depression dichotomized at the 95th percentile and academic problems defined as not mastering reading nor arithmetic well (13%). The printed relative risks compare "children of two parents with the shortest education" to "children of two parents with the longest education".

> "Compared to children of two parents with the longest education, children of two parents with the shortest education were 4.46 (95% CI 2.64, 7.54) times more likely to have high levels of ADHD, 3.27 (95% CI 1.77, 6.08) times more likely to have high levels of depression, and 3.39 (95% CI 2.29, 5.03) times more likely to have academic problems."
