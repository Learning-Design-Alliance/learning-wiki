# N3 duration, cuing, and sleep-disruption measures are intercorrelated but represent separable facets of sleep quality in predicting the recall cuing effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A multiple regression model including N3 duration, Sleep Disruption Index, Sleep Fragmentation Index, and number of cues significantly predicted the recall cuing effect (r(17)=0.55, p=0.01), with significant coefficients for Sleep Fragmentation Index (p=0.01), Sleep Disruption Index (p=0.01), and number of name cues (p=0.02). ## Evidence

### Whitmore 2022

Whitmore, N. W., Bassard, A. M. & Paller, K. A. (2022). Targeted memory reactivation of face-name learning depends on ample and undisturbed slow-wave sleep. npj Science of Learning, 7:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00119-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression on data from the same nap experiment, motivated by high intercorrelations among N3 duration and the disruption measures (e.g., N3 duration vs Sleep Disruption Index r(22)=−0.6, p=0.002). Significant coefficients showed the measures represent correlated but separable facets of overall sleep quality.

> "The overall regression model predicted the cuing effect on Δrecall [ r(17) = 0.55, p = 0.01]"
