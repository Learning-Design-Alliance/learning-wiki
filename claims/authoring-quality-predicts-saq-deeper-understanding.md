## Evidence

### Hilton 2022

Hilton, C. B., Goldwater, M. B., Hancock, D., Clemson, M., Huang, A., & Denyer, G. (2022). Scalable Science Education via Online Cooperative Questioning. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 21(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0249

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Marginal structural model for the molecular biology short-answer section (Table 2): intercept 17.828, authoring-score coefficient 8.542 (p < 0.001), self-tested questions coefficient 0.064 (p = 0.046). The authors note the SAQs tested Bloom levels of at least 5 and 6, so "a 0.06% increase for each question used for self-testing" alongside the larger authoring effect.

> "each unit increase in authored-question quality brought a predicted 8.5% increase in exam performance (i.e., accruing up to a maximum boost of 25.5%) and, accordingly, a 0.06% increase for each question used for self-testing"
