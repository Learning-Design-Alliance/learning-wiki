This is the guide's own summary assertion in its closing section, offered without cited data or effect sizes; the article presents it as the rationale for the four key components rather than as a tested result.

"Situating learning within real-world experiences and the simultaneous application of literacy and science opportunities can result in greater learning in both areas than if either were taught separately or outside of an authentic experience."
