`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical design conjecture RW1, supported by cognitive psychology on semantic priming and the Spreading Activation Theory, and by PER case studies (e.g., students blending charge-distribution arguments with everyday experiences about a car door handle).

> "If a task cues students about the presence of a real-world context, then it is more likely to invoke their everyday and curricular knowledge."

### Amogh Sirnoorkar and James T. Laverty 2023 (2)

Amogh Sirnoorkar and James T. Laverty. (2023). Theoretical exploration of task features that facilitate student sensemaking in physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.11478](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.11478)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical design conjecture RW2, grounded in the SIAM account of idea generation through knowledge activation and idea production, with supporting observations that context-rich problems are easier to visualize for students.

> "If a task cues students about the presence of a real-world context, then it is more likely to lead students to generate and connect diverse sets of ideas (conceptual, procedural and intuitive) from their knowledge system."
