## Evidence

### Ryan 2014

Ryan, Q. X., Astolfi, C., Baily, C., & Pollock, S. J. (2014). Validation of a Conceptual Assessment Tool in E&M II. 2014 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Student validation using think-aloud interviews with four students; the interviewer did not interject except to remind students to verbalize their thought processes. Interviews were recorded and analyzed to determine whether student work reflected the intended nature of the question.

> "A few wording and spacing changes were made as a result of these student interviews. For example, Q1b was originally worded as: If this surface cannot be used with Eq. I or Eq.II, briefly explain why not in the space below."
