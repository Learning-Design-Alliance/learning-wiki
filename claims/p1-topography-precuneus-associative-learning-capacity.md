# Better associative learning performance is associated with early (126–148 ms) ERP topographic differences coinciding with P1 and sourced to the precuneus (BA 7)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In an EEG associative learning task, individual d' significantly covaried with ERP scalp topography at 126–148 ms post-stimulus, a period overlapping the P1 component, with source localization implicating the precuneus (BA 7). [→ Raynal 2024](#raynal-2024)

## Evidence

### Raynal 2024

Raynal, E., Schipper, K., Brandner, C., Ruggeri, P. & Barral, J. (2024). Electrocortical correlates of attention differentiate individual capacity in associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00236-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Data-driven TANCOVA on ERPs of 38 young adults during an associative learning task, correlating scalp topography with the behavioral d' index; the analysis "revealed two signiﬁcant time periods", the first at 126–148 ms.

> "The TANCOVA revealed two signiﬁcant time periods (Fig.1a), the ﬁrst at 126–148 ms and the second at 573–638 ms post-stimulus onset."

### Raynal 2024 (2)

Raynal, E., Schipper, K., Brandner, C., Ruggeri, P. & Barral, J. (2024). Electrocortical correlates of attention differentiate individual capacity in associative learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00236-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

sLORETA source estimation of the 126–148 ms covariance map in the same EEG study; the analysis "revealed an implication of the pre- cuneus (BA 7)", an area the authors link to attention and visuospatial processing.

> "Underlying sources for the ﬁrst covariance map (Fig. 1c, upper panel) revealed an implication of the pre- cuneus (BA 7), with the highest activation value at MNI coordinates X = 10, Y= −65, Z = 65."
