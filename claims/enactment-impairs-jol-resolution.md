## Evidence

### Kubik 2022

Kubik, V., Jemstedt, A., Mahjub Eshratabadi, H., Schwartz, B. L., & Jönsson, F. U. (2022). The underconfidence-with-practice effect in action memory: The contribution of retrieval practice to metacognitive monitoring. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09288-2

`q3 · i?`

In the same lab experiment, mean resolution gamma for study–test items was lower after enactive than verbal encoding: "enactment (M = 0.46, SE = 0.06) significantly hampered the resolution of JOLs compared to verbal encoding (M = 0.65, SE = 0.06)".

> "enactment (M = 0.46, SE = 0.06) significantly hampered the resolution of JOLs compared to verbal encoding (M = 0.65, SE = 0.06), F(1, 47) = 4.68, p = 0.036, ω2 = 0.037"
