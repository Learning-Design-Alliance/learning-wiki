## Evidence

### Leidi Antonio 2024

Leidi Antonio, Krauer Juliette, Soret Guillaume, Parent Thibault, Marti Christophe, Meyer Philippe, Juillerat André, Bex Stijn, Suh Noémie, Rouyer Frédéric, Siegenthaler Nils, Grosgurin Olivier. (2024). Heart and lung point-of-care ultrasonography tutoring in internal medicine: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Ultrasound. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40477-024-00968-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-center randomized open-label superiority trial of internal medicine residents after a hands-on POCUS course; 21 participants analyzed for the primary outcome. The intervention group reached "a risk difference of 70%" with number needed to supervise 1.4; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Seven participants in the intervention group (70%) versus zero (0%) in control group reached the primary outcome, corresponding to a risk difference of 70% (95% CI, 42 to 98%) and a number needed to supervise of 1.4 (95% CI, 1.0 to 2.4), p < 0.001."
