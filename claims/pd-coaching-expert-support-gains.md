`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Coding result across the report's reviewed studies; experts ranged from specially trained master teachers and instructional leaders to researchers and university faculty, delivering one-on-one coaching, group workshops, or remote mentoring.

> "The practice of providing coaching or other expert support for educators was identified in 30 of the 35 studies reviewed."

### Darling-Hammond 2017 (2)

Darling-Hammond, L., Hyler, M. E., Gardner, M. (2017). Effective Teacher Professional Development. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-prof-dev. [doi:10.54300/122.311](https://doi.org/10.54300/122.311)

`q4 · i?` · `review · r2`

Powell and colleagues (2010) coaching model for early childhood educators: biweekly coaching sessions with university-based literacy coaches over a semester-long program including 16 hours of workshops and seven coaching sessions. The report states children of participating teachers showed significantly larger language and literacy gains.

> "A two-year randomized control trial found that classrooms led by educators who participated in this coaching model demonstrated larger gains and higher performance on a valid and widely used early childhood classroom quality assessment than did control group classrooms."
