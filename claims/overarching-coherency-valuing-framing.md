## Evidence

### Bing 2011

Bing, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2011). Epistemic Complexity and the Journeyman-Expert Transition. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.8.010105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.8.010105)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative case study of an interview in which a student in Intermediate Mathematical Methods attempted to determine the sign of a Q term in a modified conservation equation, using several framings; the article states his framings produced opposite answers and his confusion persisted.

> "S3 can be said to exhibit an overarching framing, one that values coherency among multiple lines of reasoning. Physical Mapping, Calculation, and Invoking Authority can be seen as subframes nested within this larger coherency-valuing framing."
