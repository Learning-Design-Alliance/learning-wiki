### Hardianti 2017

Hardianti, T. & Kuswanto, H. (2017). Difference among Levels of Inquiry: Process Skills Improvement at Senior High School in Indonesia. International Journal of Instruction, 10(2), 119-130. http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_2_8.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc Tukey HSD procedure (Sig. <0.05) on gain means of the three groups. Table 4 reports mean differences ILL3-ILL4 = 26.44333 and ILL2-ILL4 = 22.47619, both significant at 0.05; ILL2-ILL3 = 3.96714, Sig. 0.697, not significant.

> "(1) there is no significant difference in effectiveness between ILL -2 and ILL-3 in improving process skills; (2) there is significant difference in effectiveness between ILL -2 and ILL-4 in improving process skills; and (3) there is significant difference in effectiveness between ILL -3 and ILL-4 in improving process skills."
