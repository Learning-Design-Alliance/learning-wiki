## Evidence

### Medveczky D 2025

Medveczky D, Mitchell A, Leopardi E, Dawson A. (2025). Benefits of a near-peer program from the tutors’ perspective: a survey of Australian junior doctors in a regional teaching program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06762-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 26 complete NPMT tutor responses. On visual analogue scales, "tutors reported to experience enjoyment" at mean 83.6 and somewhat strong overall benefit at mean 77.4; free-text responses corroborated these findings.

> "tutors reported to experience enjoyment (mean 83.6, 95%CI 77.5–89.6, 0 = did not enjoy at all, 100 = strongly enjoyed) and to receive somewhat strong overall benefit (mean 77.4, 95%CI 71.3–83.5"
