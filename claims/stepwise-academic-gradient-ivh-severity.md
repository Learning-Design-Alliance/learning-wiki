Descriptive trajectory analysis of mean NAPLAN scores across the four groups (Figure and eTable 3) observed a "stepwise reduction in mean overall attainment scores" ordered full-term controls, very preterm controls, low-grade IVH, high-grade IVH. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "A consistent stepwise reduction in mean overall attainment scores was observed across groups, with full-term controls exhibiting the highest scores, followed by very preterm controls, children with low-grade IVH, and children with high-grade IVH"
