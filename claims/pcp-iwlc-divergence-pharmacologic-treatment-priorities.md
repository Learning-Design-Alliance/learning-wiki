Cross-group comparison from the Delphi results: IWLC prioritized non-pharmacological treatments and cautioned about polypharmacy and exercise-related harm, while PCPs and experts agreed non-pharmacologic approaches should come first but wanted more pharmacologic education. The article reports "PCPs prioritized pharmacological strategies more than those in the IWLC group."
