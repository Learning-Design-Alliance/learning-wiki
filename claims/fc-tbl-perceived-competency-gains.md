# FC-TBL students reported greater perceived gains in independent learning, teamwork, thinking, communication and problem-solving than LBL students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On a seven-item competency questionnaire completed by all 109 students, significantly larger proportions of the FC-TBL group than the LBL group agreed that the model stimulated independent learning motivation, enhanced self-learning, fostered independent thinking, improved problem-solving, communication and teamwork (all chi-square tests P < 0.01). [→ Qu Meng 2024](#qu-meng-2024)

## Evidence

### Qu Meng 2024

Qu Meng, Hou Qinlong, Li Xushen, Yu Chunyan, Xia Jichen, Dong Zhiheng. (2024). Application of a flipped classroom incorporating modified team-based learning in molecular biology laboratory teaching: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06450-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire survey of all 109 students (100% response rate, Cronbach's alpha 0.806) analyzed with chi-square tests; Table 1 reports each item significant at P < 0.01 (e.g., χ2 = 27.255 for independent learning motivation). Perceptions are self-reported, not tested performance.

> "compared to the LBL group, a majority of students believed that the FC-TBL teaching model notably stimulated their motivation for independent learning, enhanced their self-learning capabilities, fostered independent thinking, improved problem-solving and analytical skills"
