Evidence¶

Baoyi W and Khan FU 2026¶

Baoyi W and Khan FU (2026). AI and higher education: teachers' perceptions of students' ethical tensions in online pedagogical methods. Front. Psychol. 17:1881032. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1881032

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from the CLL section of the thematic analysis. Teachers reported the trust erosion cycle from secret AI use, the AI Alpha power disparity, collaborative hollowing where groups submit AI-done work without real collaboration, and the counselor's dilemma of concealed learning processes.

"Secret AI use by certain members of the CLL, which is based on the strong support of a learning community, is a catalyst for a cycle of mistrust among peers."
