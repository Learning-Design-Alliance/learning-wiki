## Evidence

### Tavşan Oğuz 2026

Tavşan Oğuz, Kara Ongun Seden, Aktaş İlkim, Çelikkol Berk. (2026). Dental students’ self-confidence toward endodontics according to educational stages: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09186-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal cohort of 98 dental students assessed at three stages using the Friedman test. Mean self-confidence rose for history taking, diagnosis, and anesthesia, e.g. "increased from 3.78 ± 0.80 in the preclinical stage to 4.35 ± 0.65 in the clinical internship". No standardized effect size beyond Kendall's W is interpretable as d, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "the most pronounced improvement in self-confidence was observed in patient history taking, which increased from 3.78 ± 0.80 in the preclinical stage to 4.35 ± 0.65 in the clinical internship; in diagnosis, which rose from 3.33 ± 0.82 to 4.00 ± 0.68; in anesthesia administration, which improved from 3.44 ± 0.93 to 4.08 ± 0.79"
