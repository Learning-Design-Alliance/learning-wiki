### Dwyer Shannon 2026

Dwyer Shannon, Seibel Amalia, Chang Hannah, Georgies Marina, Phelan Rebecca, Alexander Samone, Shariflari Rojin, Nwosu Chisom, Byer Arleigh-Ann, Hong Alexandra, Nadora Denise, Kulkarni Puja, Kalra Shilpi, Ali Layla, Georgies Samuel, Puglisi Jose, Preiss-Farzanegan Sarah. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison within the Hip/Ankle/Foot module analyzed by Mann-Whitney U. Anatomy explanation p = 0.0095, special test explanation p = 0.0032, pathology explanation p = 0.0224; confidence after p = 0.4052, showing the video "was not associated with significantly higher self-reported confidence in performing or interpreting the physical examination."

> "students in the intervention group rated the anatomy explanation, special test explanation, and pathology explanation significantly higher than students in the control group. No significant between-group differences were observed for comfort with anatomy, comfort with special tests, confidence in performing the physical examination, use of the material as a review resource, confidence interpreting special tests, or confidence using physical examination findings to guide the differential diagnosis."
