# Experiment 1: no significant difference between sequences on transfer post-test questions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The lecture–problem-solving and problem-solving–lecture conditions did not differ significantly on transfer questions (t(62) = 1.89, p = 0.06, d = 0.47), so no effect size conclusion beyond the printed d is warranted here. [→ Ashman et al. 2020](#ashman-et-al-2020)

## Evidence

### Ashman et al. 2020

Greg Ashman & Slava Kalyuga & John Sweller. (2020). Problem-solving or Explicit Instruction: Which Should Go First When Element Interactivity Is High? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09500-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomised Year 5 experiment (N = 64); the transfer component (maximum score 28, Cronbach's α = 0.75) showed "no significant difference between the conditions", t(62) = 1.89, p = 0.06. Equivalence was not tested.
