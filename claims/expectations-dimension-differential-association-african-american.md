## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The path from expectations and responsiveness to developmental needs to post mathematics achievement was significant for African American students (b = 0.54, p < .001) but not for non-African American students (b = −0.08, p = .57), and the Wald test of the difference was significant (p < .001). ## Evidence

### Schenke 2017

Schenke, K., Nguyen, T., Watts, T. W., Sarama, J. H., & Clements, D. H. (2017). Differential effects of the classroom on African American and non-African American’s mathematics achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(6), 794–811. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000165

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multigroup multilevel mediation (path b) in the TRIAD RCT sample: the African American path was "b = 0.54, p < .001" while the non-African American path was not significant (b = −0.08, p = .57); the Wald test comparing groups was significant (p < .001).
