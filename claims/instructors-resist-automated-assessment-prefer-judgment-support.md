`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of focus groups with instructors at three Nordic universities on core concerns around MMLA in the simulator. The article reports themes of wants, needs, and resistances, including support for instructors' practice and use of available simulator data.

> "Key themes of wants, needs, and resistances to MMLA in the simulator. Any use of LA in this context should: Support instructors' practice and the fundamental role of their professional expertise"

### Susan Harrington and Charlott Sellberg 2026 (2)

Susan Harrington and Charlott Sellberg. (2026). 12 Heuristics for Learning Analytics in Simulation-Based Professional Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 130-142. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9141

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the discussion of focus group findings, the article reports that instructors described grey areas in assessment where a student's explanation could render a seemingly incorrect response acceptable, and that appropriate feedback timing and level vary individually, underpinning "instructors express significant concern about any automated assessment".

> "Instructors also spoke of providing feedback at the correct time —which varies from bridge to bridge, even during the same simulator session—and at a level appropriate for the students in question—again, a factor which varies individually."
