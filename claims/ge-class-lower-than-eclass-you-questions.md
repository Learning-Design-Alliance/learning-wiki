## Evidence

### Teichmann 2022

Teichmann, E., Lewandowski, H. J., and Alemani, M. (2022). Investigating students' views of experimental physics in German laboratory classes. arXiv:2201.12145. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of Potsdam GE-CLASS responses (111 matched) with a filtered US E-CLASS dataset of physics majors matched on FY/BFY ratio; the pre-instruction difference is significant with Cohen's d=-0.24; post-instruction E-CLASS is 0.750±0.002 with d=-0.20.

> "Before instruction, the E-CLASS overall score is 0.747 ±0.002. The diﬀerence between E-/GE-CLASS is statistically signiﬁcant (Mann-Whitney U-test p ≪0.001) with a small eﬀect size (Cohen’s d=-0.24)."
