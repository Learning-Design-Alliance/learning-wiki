Qualitative thematic analysis of three autoethnographic role plays with ChatGPT-3.5-turbo. The researchers judged realism through the theme of performing realistic characters, grounded in "portraying convincing behaviours, understanding multiple-role scenarios, and exhibiting a capacity for creating and improvising".

> "Our appreciation of LLM's capacity for performing realistic characters was based on its capacity for portraying convincing behaviours, understanding multiple-role scenarios, and exhibiting a capacity for creating and improvising."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same thematic analysis, the first role play showed the chatbot's depiction of a fire survivor was "overly stereotypical", neglecting the more chaotic symptom presentation the authors say is commonly observed, which reduced portrayal authenticity.

> "in certain sections, the authenticity of the portrayal suffered due to an overly stereotypical depiction of the typical behaviours of individuals in the aftermath of stressful events, conforming too closely to standard representations"
