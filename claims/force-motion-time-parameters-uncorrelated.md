## Evidence

Hardon Sem F., Horeman Tim, Bonjer H. Jaap, Meijerink W. J. H. Jeroen. (2018). Force-based learning curve tracking in fundamental laparoscopic skills training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-6090-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of the number of trials needed to reach proficiency for each parameter across the six tasks in six novices; the article reports "A significant correlation was seen in the Time versus Path Length comparison of the first task (p = 0.001)" with no other significant correlations.

> "A significant correlation was seen in the Time versus Path Length comparison of the first task (p = 0.001). No further significant correlation was observed between comparison of Force versus Time, Time versus Path Length and Force versus Path Length for all six tasks."
