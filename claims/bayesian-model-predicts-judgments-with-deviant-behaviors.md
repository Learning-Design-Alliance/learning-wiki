### Duan 2026

Duan, J., Guo, X., Zheng, L. & Yin, J. (2026). A Bayesian perspective on observers' inference of group norms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00405-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bayesian hierarchical linear regression on Experiment 2 data (N = 32, deviant behaviors present) showed the reported positive prediction with converged models (Rhat = 1). When condition was added as a random variable, the frequency-tracking model no longer predicted responses (Pr (β > 0) = 86.50%) while the Bayesian model did (Pr (β > 0) = 96.90%).

> "model-derived posterior probabilities positively predicted participants’ judgmental posterior probabilities, β = 0.87, 95% CrI = [0.50, 1.25], Pr (β > 0) = 100.00%, δt = 16.50."
