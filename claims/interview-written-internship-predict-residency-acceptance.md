## Evidence

### Yun Je-Yeon 2024

Yun Je-Yeon, Ryu Hyunjin, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Hyun Bae, Choi Seung, Park Wan Beom, Bae Eun Jung, Yim Jae-Joon, Myung Sun Jung. (2024). Subtyping of Performance Trajectory During Medical School, Medical Internship, and the First Year of Residency in Training Physicians: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2024.39.e239

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression of residency acceptance among 627 applicants, adjusted for sex, application year, GPA, English proficiency, internship performance, and selection scores. The article reports "higher scores in the interview (aOR, 2.57)" and the other aORs.

> "higher odds of acceptance into the residency program with higher scores in the interview (aOR, 2.57) and written examination (aOR, 1.45) of the residency selection procedures, in addition to the medical internship performance score (aOR, 1.19)"

### Yun Je-Yeon 2024 (2)

Yun Je-Yeon, Ryu Hyunjin, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Hyun Bae, Choi Seung, Park Wan Beom, Bae Eun Jung, Yim Jae-Joon, Myung Sun Jung. (2024). Subtyping of Performance Trajectory During Medical School, Medical Internship, and the First Year of Residency in Training Physicians: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2024.39.e239

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROC analysis (Fig. 1) in the same 627-applicant cohort. The article reports the interview "AUC = 0.899", practical test 0.766, and internship performance 0.739, all above 0.7.

> "The residency selection interview (AUC = 0.899), residency selection practical test (AUC = 0.766), and medical internship performance score (AUC = 0.739) showed good discrimination ability (AUC of > 0.7 for all) between accepted and unaccepted training physicians."
