### Andrew Howard Nichols 2015

Peer comparison of two fairly selective public research universities in the report's database. The report notes "Michigan State has a 72 percent graduation rate for Pell Grant students, compared with 52 percent at the University of Alabama", despite Michigan State serving a higher Pell percentage with slightly lower entering SAT scores.

### Andrew Howard Nichols 2015 (2)

Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). The Pell Partnership: Ensuring a Shared Responsibility for Low-Income Student Success. The Education Trust. https://www.edtrust.org/resource/pellgradrates

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Peer comparison of two private institutions in the same Carnegie Classification, each enrolling 2,500–3,000 students with about one-quarter Pell entrants scoring similarly on the SAT. The report finds "Only 41 percent of Pell students complete a degree in six years at Lipscomp compared with 74 percent" at Redlands.

> "Only 41 percent of Pell students complete a degree in six years at Lipscomb compared with 74 percent of Pell students at University of Redlands."
