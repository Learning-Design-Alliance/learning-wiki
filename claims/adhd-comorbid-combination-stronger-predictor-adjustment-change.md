## Evidence

### Mohamad Nawal 2025

Mohamad Nawal, Rousseau Kim-Louise, Dowlut Fatimah, Gering Milton, Thomas Kevin G. F. (2025). Symptoms of ADHD and Other Common Mental Disorders Influence Academic Success in South African Undergraduates. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547241310659

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression (Table 9) on three-wave completers; significant model fit F(7, 172) = 4.59, p ≤ .001, R2 = .16, accounted for almost entirely by ASRS, BDI-II, and BAI scores; the article notes "the AUDIT score was a non-significant predictor of this outcome". No effect size estimate was printed for the individual predictors.

> "the second hierarchical analysis indicated that the combination of overall ADHD symptoms with symptoms of depression, anxiety, and risky alcohol use was a stronger predictor of a smaller magnitude of academic adjustment than overall ADHD symptoms alone"
