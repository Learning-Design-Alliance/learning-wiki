# Neither URM status nor first-generation status moderates the intervention effects on retrieval practice outcomes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In exploratory moderation analyses, neither underrepresented minority status nor generation status moderated the effects of intervention version on the Hypothesis 1-4 outcomes. [→ Lisi Wang 2023](#lisi-wang-2023)

## Evidence

### Lisi Wang 2023

Lisi Wang, Katherine Muenks, Veronica X. Yan. (2023). Interventions to Promote Retrieval Practice: Strategy Knowledge Predicts Intent, but Perceived Cost Predicts Usage. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000813

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory moderation analyses reported in Results: for each hypothesis, mixed ANOVAs added URM status or generation status as a third factor among the first-session participants. The article reports no moderation by either demographic characteristic; no effect sizes are printed for these tests.

> "Neither URM status nor generation status moderated the effects of intervention version, hence we do not explore them any further."
