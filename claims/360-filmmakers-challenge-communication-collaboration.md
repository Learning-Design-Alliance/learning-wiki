### 360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program 2017

360 Filmmakers Challenge: Key Findings from the Fall 2016 Program. (2017). Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/bf98d40e-980c-4f54-9985-46bf5675d2fa

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey documentation of the Fall 2016 program found communication and collaboration gains: 76% of educators reported students improved in their ability to communicate ideas and 71% reported students became better collaborators. A teacher attributed teamwork growth partly to the project being open-ended, with groups reassigning roles based on strengths.

> "76% of educators reported that their students improved in their ability to communicate their ideas to others."
