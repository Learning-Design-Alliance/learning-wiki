## Evidence

### Kelsey E. Schenck 2024

Kelsey E. Schenck, Mitchell J. Nathan. (2024). Navigating Spatial Ability for Mathematics Education: a Review and Roadmap. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09935-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this to Rimfeld et al. (2017), who administered a battery of gamified spatial measures to twin pairs; the one-factor model fit best. The review also cites Malanchini et al. (2020) finding the one-factor model gave the best fit even controlling for general intelligence. No effect size is printed.

> "results indicated that tests assessed a single spatial ability factor and that the one-factor model of spatial ability fit better than the two-factor model, even when controlling for a common genetic factor (Rimfeld et al., 2017)."
