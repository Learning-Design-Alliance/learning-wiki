## Evidence

### Barış Eren Nadiye; Çiftçi Bahar 2025

Barış Eren Nadiye; Çiftçi Bahar. (2025). The Use of Puzzles as a Teaching Technique in Nursing Education: A Randomized Controlled Study. Journal of Evaluation in Clinical Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/jep.70118

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Dependent-groups t-tests in the RCT showed a significant self-efficacy increase only in the puzzle group; the between-group posttest comparison gave t = 2.800, p = 0.007, ES = 0.690. The article links this to success experiences in Bandura's self-efficacy theory.

> "The experimental group's “Academic Self‐Efficacy Scale” mean score significantly increased from 19.56 ± 4.10 to 21.88 ± 2.49 (t = −2.886, p = 0.007, d = 0.510). In contrast, the control group's mean score rose slightly from 19.24 ± 5.61 to 19.52 ± 4.13, but this change was not statistically significant (t = −0.227, p = 0.822, d = 0.381)."
