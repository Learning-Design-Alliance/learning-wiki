## Evidence

### Kerr Brendan 2013

Kerr Brendan, Hawkins Trisha Lee-Ann, Herman Robert, Barnes Sue, Kaufmann Stephanie, Fraser Kristin, Ma Irene W. Y. (2013). Feasibility of scenario-based simulation training versus traditional workshops in continuing medical education: a randomized controlled trial. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.21312

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group-randomized pilot RCT of 27 conference attendees assigned to a simulation-based or traditional case-based workshop; between-group MCQ comparisons showed "not significantly different between the two groups (p=0.90". Within-group changes were also not significant for either group (p>0.05).

> "Baseline MCQ scores were not different between the two groups (p=0.22; Table 2). Post-workshop MCQ scores were also not significantly different between the two groups (p=0.90; Table 2)."
