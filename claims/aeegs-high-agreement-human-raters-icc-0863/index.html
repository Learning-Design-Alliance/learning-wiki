Olaoye DD 2026¶

Olaoye DD, Owolabi HO and Olaoye OT (2026). Automated software scoring of senior school certificate examination mathematical items in economics using a contextual similarity model. Front. Educ. 11:1669504. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1669504

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational study comparing AEEGS scores with those of 12 human raters, each marking 84 of 1,008 scripts, using ICC based on a two-way mixed effect model with absolute agreement. The article reports "good reliability at single measures with 0.758" and 0.863 at average measures, F(1,007, 1,007) = 7.27, p < .001.

"The results displayed in Table 6 revealed good reliability at single measures with 0.758 and higher reliability at average measures with 0.863 while the F(1,007, 1,007) = 7.27, p < .001"
