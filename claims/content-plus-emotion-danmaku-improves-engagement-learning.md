## Evidence

### Zipeng Ji 2025

Zipeng Ji, Pengcheng An, and Jian Zhao. (2025). ClassComet: Exploring and Designing AI-generated Danmaku in Educational Videos to Enhance Online Learning. [doi:10.1145/3715336.3735762](https://doi.org/10.1145/3715336.3735762)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects experiment with 12 participants watching four educational videos under counterbalanced conditions (No-Danmaku, Emotion-only, Content-only, Content+Emotion), with pre/post quizzes and a 7-point engagement questionnaire; the article reports the combined condition "significantly enhance[d] viewers' engagement and learning outcomes". Detailed ANOVA statistics appear in the portion of the results not supplied.

> "The findings suggest that videos with both content- and emotion-related danmaku could significantly enhance viewers' engagement and learning outcomes compared to the original videos."
