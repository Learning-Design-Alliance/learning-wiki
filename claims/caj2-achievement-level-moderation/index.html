Gambari 2016¶

Gambari, I. A. & Yusuf, M. O. (2016). Effects of Computer-Assisted Jigsaw II Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Achievement and Retention. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(4), 352-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117586

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA on achievement levels within the Jigsaw II group (n = 42) in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports "an F (1, 38 ) = 41.226, p = 0.000" as significant, indicating differences among the three achievement levels.

"Table 3 indicates that an F (1, 38 ) = 41.226, p = 0.000 was significant at 0.05 level. This shows the effect of the main effect ( Computer-assisted Jigsaw II) when achievement levels were considered."

Gambari, I. A. & Yusuf, M. O. (2016). Effects of Computer-Assisted Jigsaw II Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Achievement and Retention. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(4), 352-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117586

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Scheffe's post-hoc analysis of mean scores among achievement levels in the Jigsaw II group. The article reports significant pairwise differences favoring high over medium, high over low (X = 62.27), and medium over low achievers.

"The data in Table 4 indicates significant difference in the posttest mean s cores of high achievers (X = 77.89) and medium achievers (X = 68.72) in favour of high achievers ."
