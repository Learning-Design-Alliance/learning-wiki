### Bigras Catherine 2019

Bigras Catherine, Kairy Dahlia, Archambault Philippe S. (2019). Augmented feedback for powered wheelchair training in a virtual environment. Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-019-0482-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized simulator experiment (40 participants); collision counts on the VE test task at baseline, acquisition and retention analyzed with a mixed-effects model. No significant group differences were found for collisions.

> "Participants made fewer collisions on the acquisition test 20 min after training and the effect was retained for the retention test. The mixed effects model was significant for time from baseline to acquisition (p = .001) and from baseline to retention (p = 0.01)."
