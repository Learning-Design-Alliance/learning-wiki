Quality Improvement in Surgery Combining Lean Improvement Methods with Teamwork Training: A Controlled Before-After Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138490

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational process study of WHO checklist quality using three markers (all items completed, all team members present, active participation), scored by two observers per operation. In the active arm "All three components of T/O were completed in 3/21 (14%) cases" pre-intervention, rising to 71% post-intervention; the active-versus-control change difference was significant (p = 0.032).

> "All three components of T/O were completed in 3/21 (14%) cases in the pre-intervention active arm, which increased to 17/24 (71%) in the post-intervention phase (difference = 57%; 95% CI 29% to 85%)"
