[doi:10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression in the GUSTO cohort (Table 4, Model 2, mutually adjusted domain-specific PA models) printed adjusted mean differences of 0.192 (0.026, 0.359) and 0.221 (0.069, 0.373) z-scores for organized PA/sports and executive function. No standardized effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "children who participated in organized PA/sports at ages 5.5 and 8 years had a higher mean executive function [0.192 (0.026, 0.359) and 0.221 (0.069, 0.373) z-scores, respectively] at age 8.5 years"

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same adjusted regression models printed 0.239 (0.036, 0.441) z-score for organized PA/sports at age 8 years and academic achievement; the age 5.5 exposure showed a consistent but non-significant pattern [0.201 (−0.031, 0.433)]. No standardized effect size was printed.
