Correlational analysis of average self-efficacy, pure calculus, and applied calculus scores for men and women earning an A in General Physics II, shown in Figure 5. The article reports "the inverse relationship (i.e., negative correlation) between calculus proficiency and calculus self-efficacy" for women only.

> "the inverse relationship (i.e., negative correlation) between calculus proficiency and calculus self-efficacy exist within the population of women students, but not within the population of men students."
