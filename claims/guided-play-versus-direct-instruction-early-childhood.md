The review cites the Skene et al. (2022) meta-analysis of guided play in early childhood education, printing effect sizes "g = 0.40, g = 0.24, and g = 0.63" for executive functioning and numeracy favoring guided play over direct instruction.

> "These authors concluded that guided play is more effective than direct instruction for promoting executive functioning and numeracy (early math skills and shape knowledge), with effect sizes of g = 0.40, g = 0.24, and g = 0.63."
