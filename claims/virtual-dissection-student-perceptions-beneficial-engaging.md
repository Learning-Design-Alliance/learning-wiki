## Evidence

### Boscolo-Berto Rafael 2021

Boscolo-Berto Rafael, Tortorella Cinzia, Porzionato Andrea, Stecco Carla, Picardi Edgardo Enrico Edoardo, Macchi Veronica, De Caro Raffaele. (2021). The additional role of virtual to traditional dissection in teaching anatomy: a randomised controlled trial. Surgical and Radiologic Anatomy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00276-020-02551-2

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative assessment via a single written free-expression sentence gathered from students after the trial. Students appreciated enlarging anatomical details and exploring districts, but reported an initial learning curve, occasional table slowing, poor image detail at high zoom, and single-user restriction; gross dissection was "still unanimously reported as the pivotal approach".

> "70% of all the students belonging to the group who used the virtual dissection reported that the use of virtual dissection was beneficial to their learning—especially for three-dimensional expectations—and more engaging experience than studying textbooks and atlases"
