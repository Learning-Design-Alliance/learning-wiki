### Glazerman 2010

Glazerman, S., E. Isenberg, S. Dolfin, M. Bleeker, A. Johnson, M. Grider, and M. Jacobus. (2010). Impacts of Comprehensive Teacher Induction: Final Results From a Randomized Controlled Study (NCEE 2010-4027). `q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Classroom observation study: trained observers used a rubric quantifying evidence of best teaching practice in literacy lessons in 639 classrooms in the first year. The study "found no impacts" on implementation, content, or classroom culture despite treatment teachers receiving more support.

> "We observed teachers giving a literacy lesson in the spring of their first year and found no impacts on teachers’ implementation of the literacy lesson, content of the literacy lesson, or classroom culture."
