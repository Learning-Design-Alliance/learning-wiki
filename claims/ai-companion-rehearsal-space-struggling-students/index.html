Related Claims¶

  • Teachers' most common concern is that agreeable AI companions remove the developmental friction through which adolescents learn to sustain human relationships — related
  • US K-12 teachers judge AI companion roles along a gradient from academic to intimate, ranking Romantic Partner most concerning and Tutor or Study Buddy most acceptable — related
  • Teachers draw intervention boundaries by visibility, academic-versus-relational character, and a safety override, not by perceived harm — related
  • Adult learners expect AI to support transformative and experiential learning through assumption-challenging simulations, role play, and authentic virtual contexts — related
  • Role metaphors positioned GenAI as helper, learning partner, and friend/foe, with a high proportion combining friend and foe — related
  • Adult learners expect AI to foster involvement by sparking questions, tapping interests, and providing continuous feedback and effort acknowledgment — related
  • Loneliness functions as a biological social-feedback signal that motivates relationship cultivation, and AI companions may silence that signal — related
  • Qualitative studies describe distress, relational disruption, and meaningful companion loss when chatbot use becomes emotionally significant — related
