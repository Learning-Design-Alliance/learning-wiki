## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After 5 weeks of LTB training, the parietal P300 response to target tones in an auditory change detection task was significantly reduced compared with before training (W = 67, P = 0.026). [→ Thomaschewski M 2021](#thomaschewski-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2021

Thomaschewski M, Heldmann M, Uter J C, Varbelow D, Münte T F, Keck T. (2021). Changes in attentional resources during the acquisition of laparoscopic surgical skills. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zraa012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post within-subject EEG comparison in the dual-task sessions of the 5-week LTB training study; after excluding two subjects for movement artefacts, 12 subjects were analysed. The parietal cluster test showed a "significant effect at the parietal electrode cluster (W = 67, P = 0.026)"; no effect size was printed.

> "Comparison of the difference target–standard tone between before and after training revealed a significant effect at the parietal electrode cluster (W = 67, P = 0.026)."
