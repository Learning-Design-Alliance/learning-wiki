Systematic review narrative synthesis of eight studies (four RCTs, four non-randomized) of VR training for mental health assessment and treatment outcomes; the review states "VR training interventions can promote knowledge and skills acquisition" but could not meta-analyse due to heterogeneity.

> "The results suggest that VR training interventions can promote knowledge and skills acquisition."
