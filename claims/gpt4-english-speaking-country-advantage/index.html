q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup 1 meta-analysis with random-effects models due to heterogeneity (GPT-4: I²=69% and 93%). The review reports "Statistically significant differences were observed between the results (P=.02)" for GPT-4, while GPT-3.5's 57% versus 58% difference was not significant (P=.72).

"For GPT-4, the integrated accuracy in examinations from English-speaking countries was 86% (95% CI 82%-89%; P<.01), and in examinations from non–English-speaking countries with original languages, it was 80% (95% CI 76-83; P<.01).
