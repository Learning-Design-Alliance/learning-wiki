## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The author reports that designing the first assignment so biology/biochemistry knowledge was needed to identify decoded images improved team cohesion and collaboration. [→ J. M. Deutsch 2013](#j-m-deutsch-2013)

## Evidence

### J. M. Deutsch 2013

J. M. Deutsch. (2013). Biophysics software for interdisciplinary education and research. [doi:10.1119/1.4869198](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4869198)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Author's observational report (§4.2, type (e)) from teaching the course three times. In the first assignment, images decoded via inverse Fourier transform came from biology or biochemistry and "it would have been quite hard for physics majors to correctly identify them." No quantitative measure of cohesion is reported.

> "This kind of interaction between the diﬀerent disciplines from the outset, was found to greatly improve the cohesion of the teams and was later evident in the increased degree of collaboration between members."
