# Group membership moderates the correlation between white matter volume of the left precentral gyrus and implicit sequence learning change, with a negative correlation in TD children but none in DD children

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` A significant group × ISL change interaction was found on day-2 WMV underlying the left precentral gyrus: the TD group showed a negative correlation (r = −0.501, p = 0.034) while the DD group showed no correlation (r = 0.235, p = 0.364). [→ Olivo 2024](#olivo-2024)

## Evidence

### Olivo 2024

Olivo, G., Persson, J. & Hedenius, M. (2024). Exploring brain plasticity in developmental dyslexia through implicit sequence learning. npj Science of Learning, 9:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00250-w

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Voxel-wise whole-brain correlation analysis in CAT12 with group as moderator, on the 17 DD and 18 TD children; the interaction cluster of 744 voxels under the left precentral gyrus reached "p FWE-corr = 0.006; F = 32.13".

> "In particular, while a negative correlation was present in the TD group ( p = 0.034; r = −0.501), no correlation was observed in the DD group (p = 0.364, r = 0.235)"
