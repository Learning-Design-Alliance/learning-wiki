Terry Julia 2025¶

Terry Julia, Wilks Rachel, Davies Joanne. (2025). Simulated learning interventions to improve communication and practice with deaf and hard of hearing patients: a systematic review and qualitative synthesis. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10452-5

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis in the Results outcomes section: across the included studies, greater post-intervention gains were reported for participants with very little prior experience with Deaf communities. Only one included study asked participants about prior experience, with 60% (n = 3) of Anderson et al.'s five participants reporting none.

"Unsurprisingly researchers who cited participants with 'very little' prior experience with Deaf communities reported greater gains in post-intervention performance (Anderson et al., 2020; Sanders et al., 2008)."
