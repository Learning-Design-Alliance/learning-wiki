## Evidence

### Chen Dandan 2023

Chen Dandan, Harman Ann E., Sun Huaping, Ye Tianpeng, Gaiser Robert R. (2023). A comparison of 3- and 4-option multiple-choice items for medical subspecialty in-training examinations. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04277-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kuder and Richardson Formula 20 reliability computed for each exam form from the 2019 and 2020 administrations. The ITE-PA coefficient rose by 0.05 while the ITE-CCM coefficient fell by 0.01 after conversion.

> "For the ITE-CCM, the KR-20 equaled 0.75 for 4-option and 0.74 for 3-option MCIs. For the ITE-PA, the KR-20 equaled 0.62 for 4-option and 0.67 for 3-option MCIs."
