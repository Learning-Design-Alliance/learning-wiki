## Evidence

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding under the institutional theme. Participants reported universities lack tools and technological resources, dedicated collaboration facilities such as well-equipped group discussion rooms, and narrow classrooms, forcing educators to facilitate more than one PBL group per session.

> "One of the problems that affects our effort to implement PBL at our university is the large number of students in a class. We may have more than 50 students per class."
