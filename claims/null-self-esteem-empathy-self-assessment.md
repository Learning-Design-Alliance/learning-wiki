## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Polivka L 2024

Polivka L, Delcour C, Dufresne H, Bartoli S, Bataille P, Bekel L, Bonigen J, Deladrière E, Dimarcq S, Felix A, Havas C, Le Goff H, Levy M, Riback E, Welfringer-Morin A, Houdouin V, Hadj-Rabia S, Bodemer C, Faye A, Melki I. (2024). Breaking bad news: an active learning method for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05821-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-OSCE questionnaire comparison of the two groups: Rosenberg scores (28.53 vs. 27.64 out of 40, p = 0.68) and Jefferson empathy scores (111.2 vs. 105 out of 140, p = 0.12) showed no significant difference; the external and self-evaluated OSCE scores were also not correlated.

> "The intergroup differences in the Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale and Jefferson Scale of Empathy scores were not significant, and both scores were not correlated with the students’ self-assessed score for success in the OSCE."
