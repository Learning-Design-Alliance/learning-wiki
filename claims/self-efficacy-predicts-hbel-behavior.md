## Evidence

### Jiang Xinjun 2023

Jiang Xinjun, Zhang Hua, Wang Tao, Zhang Caihong. (2023). The association of self-control, self-efficacy, and demographic characteristics with home-based E-learning behavior in nursing and midwifery undergraduates: A cross-sectional study under the COVID-19 epidemic. Nurse Education Today. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nedt.2022.105628

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression in the same cross-sectional survey, with HBEL behavior as dependent variable, identified self-efficacy as an independent influential factor, with "self-efficacy (β = 0.169, P < 0.001)".

> "The results of the multiple linear regression analysis showed that self-control (β = 0.250, P < 0.001), self-efficacy (β = 0.169, P < 0.001), university province (β = 0.249, P < 0.001), grade level (β = −0.255, P < 0.001), and personal perceived health status (β = −0.044, P = 0.003) were independent influential factors"
