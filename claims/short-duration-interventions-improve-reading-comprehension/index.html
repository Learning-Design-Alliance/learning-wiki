He Hongxue 2025¶

He Hongxue, Yang Yahui, Sun Jiaojiao, Wang Fang, Zhang Wei and Zhu Fengshu. (2025). Effects of school-based physical activity on academic achievement in children and adolescents: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1651883

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis by intervention duration for reading comprehension. The review reports "SMD = 0.24, 95% CI: 0.03–0.46" for interventions <24 weeks, with high heterogeneity (I² = 69%).

"A subgroup analysis based on intervention duration was conducted (Table 2), indicating a positive association between physical activity and reading comprehension achievement when the intervention duration <24 weeks (SMD = 0.24, 95% CI: 0.03–0.46, p = 0.010; I2 = 69%)."
