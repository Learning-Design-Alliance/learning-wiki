Synthesis of psychometric findings across the 6 included instrument-validation studies, summarized in Table 6. The review reports internal consistency "ranged from acceptable to excellent (Cronbach’s α =0.74–0.97)" and structural validity supported for four named instruments.

"Internal consistency estimates across individual studies ranged from acceptable to excellent (Cronbach’s α =0.74–0.97). Structural validity was supported for the EEQ, TEPI, TCPERSS, and MCI, with acceptable to strong model fit indices."
