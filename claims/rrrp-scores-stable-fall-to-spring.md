## Evidence

### Day 2017

Day, Stephanie L.; Connor, Carol M. (2017). Examining the relations between self-regulation and achievement in third grade students. Assessment for Effective Intervention, 42(2), 97-109. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508416670367

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A repeated measures ANCOVA compared fall and spring RRRP scores in the same sample. The authors report the printed coefficients and conclude that "scores on the RRRP are fairly stable in third grade"; the b values are unstandardized coefficients, not effect sizes.

> "Part 1 scores on the RRRP decreased slightly from time one to time two (b = −.418, p < .001), but Part 2 (color switch) scores on the RRRP did not change over time (b = −.024, p = .811), indicating that scores on the RRRP are fairly stable in third grade."
