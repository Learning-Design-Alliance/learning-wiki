## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean item-excluded discrimination r′ is 0.61 for CR questions versus 0.50 for ITs, with six of eight CR questions discriminating at a greater level than their IT counterparts; the difference is not statistically significant. ## Evidence

### Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell 2014

Aaron D. Slepkov and Ralph C. Shiell. (2014). A comparison of integrated testlet and constructed-response question formats. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric analysis of eight concept-equivalent CR/IT pairs across midterm and final exams. Mean r′ was 0.61 (CR) versus 0.50 (IT); the article notes "there is insuﬃcient statistical power to establish statistical signiﬁcance in discrimination diﬀerences" with only 8 questions.

> "the data suggest that our CR questions are more discriminating than ITs, with six out of eight CR questions discriminating at a greater level than their IT counterparts"
