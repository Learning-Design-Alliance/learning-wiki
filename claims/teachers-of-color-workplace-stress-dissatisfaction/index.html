Carver-Thomas 2025¶

Descriptive analysis of teacher satisfaction indicators by race/ethnicity from the 2020–21 NTPS (Table 7). The report finds "Teachers of color were more likely to report that “the stress and disappointments involved in teaching at this school aren’t really worth it”" (30% vs. 24%).
