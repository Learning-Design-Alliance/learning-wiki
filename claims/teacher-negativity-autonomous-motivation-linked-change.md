## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, J., Kaufman, T., Mastrotheodoros, S., & Branje, S. (2025). Longitudinal associations between academic motivation and school-related stressors in adolescents transitioning to secondary school. Learning and Instruction, 98, 102144. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102144

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate latent growth curve models in the four-wave study of 290 adolescents (Mage = 11.58 at T1). Table 7 reports intercept-intercept β = -.28 (p = .002) and slope-slope β = -.37 (p = .002), i.e. growth factors were "significantly negatively correlated".

> "the intercepts and slopes of perceived negative relationships with teachers and autonomous academic motivation were significantly negatively correlated (see Fig. 1). This indicates that a higher level of perceived negative relationships with teachers was related to a lower level of autonomous academic motivation"
