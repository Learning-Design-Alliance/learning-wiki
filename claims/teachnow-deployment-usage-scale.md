Deployment summary (Table 1 and §4.1) of TeachNow as an opt-in experimental feature in a six-week online CS1 course with around 9,000 students. The article reports "over 15% of the 600 volunteer teachers" provided "a collective 12,300 minutes of 1:1 help to 375 unique students", with sustained weekly usage and 80% of participating teachers returning.
