# Higher ADHD polygenic risk scores in ALSPAC adolescents are associated with worse scores on Key Stage 3 national tests and GCSE examinations

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adolescents with higher ADHD polygenic risk scores achieved worse Key Stage 3 national test scores (β = −1.4, 95% CI −2.0 to −0.8, P = 2.3 × 10−6). [→ Stergiakouli Evie 2017](#stergiakouli-evie-2017)
`q2 i?` Adolescents with higher ADHD polygenic risk scores achieved worse capped GCSE point scores (β = −4.0, 95% CI −6.1 to −1.9, P = 1.8 × 10−4). [→ Stergiakouli Evie 2017](#stergiakouli-evie-2017)

## Evidence

### Stergiakouli Evie 2017

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational regression analysis in the ALSPAC cohort (Table 1) testing standardized ADHD polygenic risk scores against educational outcomes with sex as a covariate. The table reports "Key Stage 3 points" β = −1.4 (−2.0 to −0.8), P = 2.3 × 10−6, R2 = 0.003, and "Capped GCSE points" β = −4.0 (−6.1 to −1.9), P = 1.8 × 10−4, R2 = 0.006.
