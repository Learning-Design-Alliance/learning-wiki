### Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group 3 of the TIMSS 2019 analysis, united on the 'Non-parallel asymmetric congruence and incongruence effect' family with equality constraints between interactive-quadratic and cubic effects of control. The article notes the explained variance "was modest (R2 = 4.1%)" but the relative gain large.

> "Although the variance in mathematics explained by this model was modest (R2 = 4.1%), it represented a 41% increase from the additive model (R2 = 2.9%), underlining important higher-order effects."
