### Shih-Yin Lin 2013

Shih-Yin Lin, Charles Henderson, William Mamudi, Chandralekha Singh, and Edit Yerushalmi. (2013). Teaching assistants' beliefs regarding example solutions in introductory physics. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.9.020115

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of the written solutions 23 TAs produced as a pre-lesson homework artifact. The article reports features related to initial problem analysis are "surprisingly more prominent in TAs actual practice than in their account of liked features".

> "Examination of TAs' own solutions (which 23 TAs provided) indicates that features related to initial problem analysis are surprisingly more prominent in TAs actual practice than in their account of liked features. All TA solutions included a diagram and half included a list of knowns."
