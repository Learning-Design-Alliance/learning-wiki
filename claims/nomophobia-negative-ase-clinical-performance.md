Path analysis in the emerging CB-SEM model (Table 5, N = 585) found "nomophobia has direct but negative association with ASE (β = −0.56, p = 0.002) and clinical performance (β = −0.18, p = 0.008)". Correlations of nomophobia dimensions with ASE ranged from r = −0.23 to −0.29 and with clinical performance from r = −0.14 to −0.22.
