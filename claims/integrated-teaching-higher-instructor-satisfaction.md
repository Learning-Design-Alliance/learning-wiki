## Evidence

### Chen Xiaolin 2026

Chen Xiaolin, Huang Yan, Cai Yuli, Chen Qiong. (2026). The integration of clinical pathway and case-based teaching in standardized residency training in the department of endocrinology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09074-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey of the 87 randomized residents on instructor evaluation; the experimental group's total instructor score was "74.48 ± 0.45 vs. 72.19 ± 0.51, p = 0.001" higher than the control group's.

> "However, overall satisfaction with instructors among trainees in the experimental group was significantly higher than that in the control group (74.48 ± 0.45 vs. 72.19 ± 0.51, p = 0.001)"

### Chen Xiaolin 2026 (2)

Chen Xiaolin, Huang Yan, Cai Yuli, Chen Qiong. (2026). The integration of clinical pathway and case-based teaching in standardized residency training in the department of endocrinology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09074-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subdomain analysis of the same instructor-evaluation survey found significant differences only for "skills training and bedside teaching"; work attitudes, daily work guidance, and medical record revisions showed no significant differences.

> "trainees in the experimental group reported higher levels of satisfaction with the skills training and bedside teaching provided by their instructors compared to those in the control group (18.43 ± 0.30 vs. 17.40 ± 0.37, p = 0.031; 14.09 ± 0.17 vs. 12.98 ± 0.30, p = 0.002)"
