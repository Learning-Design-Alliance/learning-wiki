## Evidence

### Madelon van den Boer 2022

Madelon van den Boer, Loes Bazen, Elise de Bree. (2022). The Same yet Different: Oral and Silent Reading in Children and Adolescents with Dyslexia. Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-022-09856-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 RM ANOVA with 54 adolescents with dyslexia and 24 typical readers (Grade 10): a large main effect of reading mode, F(1,74) = 239.93, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.764, with silent reading faster than oral in both groups.

> "silent text reading was faster than oral text reading (F(1,74) = 239.93, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.764)"

### Madelon van den Boer 2022 (2)

Madelon van den Boer, Loes Bazen, Elise de Bree. (2022). The Same yet Different: Oral and Silent Reading in Children and Adolescents with Dyslexia. Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-022-09856-w

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Study 2 interaction of reading mode × group, F(1,74) = 12.03, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.140, a large effect. Relative to their own oral rate, "silent reading was 47.2% faster for typical readers, and 40.2% faster for adolescents with dyslexia"; both follow-up t-tests were significant.

> "The significant interaction effect indicated that this difference was smaller for adolescents with dyslexia than for typical readers (F(1,74) = 12.03, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.140)"
