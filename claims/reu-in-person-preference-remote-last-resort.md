Focus-group theme contrasting with prior suggestions that remote REUs benefit students facing geographic constraints. One interviewee rejected an in-field REU because it was online, saying she "really wanted to work in a lab in person," and interviewees highly valued social engagement opportunities.

> "multiple interviewees said they took re-mote REUs as a last resort or said that they rejected a re-mote REU in favour of an in-person REU."
