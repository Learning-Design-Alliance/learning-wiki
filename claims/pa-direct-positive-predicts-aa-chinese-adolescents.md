## Evidence

### Pan Wenwen 2025

Pan Wenwen, Zhang Hongbao, Xu Long. (2025). Association between physical activity and academic achievement in adolescents mediated by self-concept and physical and mental health. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-18559-w

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 6, bootstrap 5000 resamples) in a cross-sectional survey of 458 Chinese adolescents controlling for gender, age, and grade. The article reports the direct effect was β = 0.241, "accounting for 68.85% of the total effect."

> "Specifically, the direct effect of PA on AA was β = 0.241, accounting for 68.85% of the total effect. This medium effect size indicates that PA is not only statistically significant but also practically meaningful in predicting adolescents’ AA."
