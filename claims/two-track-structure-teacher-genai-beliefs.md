# Predictors of positive GenAI beliefs explain only 6–7% of variance in plagiarism and creativity concerns, indicating a two-track belief structure

## Evidence

### Xiu RL 2026

Xiu RL, Aguilar SJ, Macías AJ, Shikanova A, Al-Sulaiti R, Junjunia M and Jebril ST (2026) K-12 in-service teachers’ beliefs about generative AI in classrooms: insights from the United States, India, Qatar, Colombia, and the Philippines. Front. Educ. 11:1929017. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929017

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison across the study's three regression models (Models 2 and 3, N = 1,162 and 1,183). Model 2 explained R² = .07 for plagiarism concern and Model 3 R² = .06 for creativity concern, both p < .001, with readiness and support non-significant in the concern models.

> "The predictors that explained 50% of the variance in positive beliefs collectively explained only 6%–7% of the variance in plagiarism and creativity concerns. AI Readiness, Institutional Support, and Behavioral Use, the three pillars of the SDT account, were largely absent from the concern models."
