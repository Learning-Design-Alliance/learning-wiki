### Ibukunoluwa Bukola 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding of reasons for method use among the four SCALE-UP users (Fig. 5b). The article reports institutional factors dominated, with instructors citing fit to student population, class structures, or prior departmental decisions.

> "SCALE-UP is the only method for which instructor values were not the most salient reason for method use, as three out of the four SCALE-UP instructors highlighted institutional factors (second panel of Fig. 5b)"
