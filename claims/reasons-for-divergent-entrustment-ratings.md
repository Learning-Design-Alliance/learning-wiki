## Evidence

### Castanelli Damian J 2024

Castanelli Damian J, Woods Jennifer B, Chander Anusha R, Weller Jennifer M. (2024). Trainee anaesthetist self-assessment using an entrustment scale in workplace-based assessment. Anaesthesia and Intensive Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/0310057X241234676

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative analysis of comment data from the 12 most discrepant low-rating pairs in the results section; an illustrative case describes a spinal anaesthetic where the trainee rated a two and the supervisor a seven after minimal perceived input.

> "With the 12 cases in which trainees rated themselves lower, there was generally one critical aspect of the case when they needed guidance or assistance and hence rated themselves at the lower end of the scale. In these cases, the supervisor had not attached the same significance to this aspect, but rated the trainee's performance higher based on the rest of their management."
