## Evidence

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2019

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Wells Cameron I., Wilkinson Tim J. (2019). Publication in a medical student journal predicts short- and long-term academic success: a matched-cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1704-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Secondary outcome of the matched-cohort study, assessed via online registers of registered doctors and author affiliations. Nine of 49 NZMSJ authors (18.4%) attained a faculty position, versus seven of 98 controls (7.1%); printed effect size OR = 2.90.

> "Publication in the NZMSJ was significantly associated with higher attainment of academic faculty positions (Univariate OR 2.90, 95% CI 1.01–8.30, p = 0.047)."
