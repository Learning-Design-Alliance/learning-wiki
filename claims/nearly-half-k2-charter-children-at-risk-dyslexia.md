## Evidence

### Laura Cassidy 2023

Laura Cassidy, Kayla Reggio, Bennett A. Shaywitz and Sally E. Shaywitz. (2023). Prevalence of undiagnosed dyslexia in African-American primary school children. npj Science of Learning 8:52. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00204-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Screening study in two inner city public charter schools in New Orleans; teachers completed the Shaywitz DyslexiaScreen in early spring 2018. "246 children were screened for dyslexia, and 121 (49.2%) screened as at risk for dyslexia"; 90% of children screened were African-American.

> "246 children were screened for dyslexia, and 121 (49.2%) screened as at risk for dyslexia (Table 1). Of the children screened, 221, 90% were African-American, 8% Hispanic, and 2% white"

### Laura Cassidy 2023 (2)

Laura Cassidy, Kayla Reggio, Bennett A. Shaywitz and Sally E. Shaywitz. (2023). Prevalence of undiagnosed dyslexia in African-American primary school children. npj Science of Learning 8:52. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00204-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Detailed individual testing of 92 at-risk children (76.0% of the 121 at-risk) using KBIT-2, TOWRE-2, CTOPP-2 and DIBELS 8, with consensus classification by experienced clinicians. "57 (62%) had a profile consistent with dyslexia"; 7 were not dyslexic, 7 English language learners, 21 indeterminate.

> "Of these 92 students, 57 (62%) had a profile consistent with dyslexia, and 53 of the 57 (93%) were African-American; 7 (8%) students did not have dyslexia"
