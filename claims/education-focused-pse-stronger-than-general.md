# Education-focused parenting self-efficacy correlates more strongly with home-school partnership outcomes than general parenting self-efficacy

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Education-focused self-efficacy (r = .183) had a statistically significantly stronger association with partnership outcomes than general parenting self-efficacy (r = .114). [→ Tianyi Ma 2024](#tianyi-ma-2024)

## Evidence

### Tianyi Ma 2024

Tianyi Ma, Cassandra L. Tellegen, Julie Hodges, Matthew R. Sanders. (2024). The Associations Between Parenting Self-Efficacy and Parents' Contributions to the Home-School Partnership Among Parents of Primary School Students: a Multilevel Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09937-3

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level mixed-effects model (M2) testing parenting self-efficacy type as a fixed effect across 185 effect sizes. A significant between-group difference was found, F(2) = 23.54, p < .001, with education-focused self-efficacy stronger (r = .183 versus .114).

> "Education-focused self-efficacy had a statistically significantly stronger association with the home-school partnership outcomes than general parenting self-efficacy (r = .183 versus .114 respectively), t(183) = 1.97, p = .050."
