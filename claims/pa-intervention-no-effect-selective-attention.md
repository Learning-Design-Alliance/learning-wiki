## Evidence

### Gall S 2018

Gall S, Adams L, Joubert N, Ludyga S, Müller I, Nqweniso S, Pühse U, du Randt R, Seelig H, Smith D, Steinmann P, Utzinger J, Walter C, Gerber M. (2018). Effect of a 20-week physical activity intervention on selective attention and academic performance in children living in disadvantaged neighborhoods: A cluster randomized control trial. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0206908. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed linear regression (Model 1) on children with complete records, controlling for baseline CP and confounders. No significant intervention effect on d2 concentration performance; equivalence was not tested.

> "After adjusting for confounding variables, this model shows that the physical activity intervention had no statistically significant effect on children’s CP (B = 2.93, 95% CI:-5.01 to 10.86, p = 0.469)"

### Gall S 2018 (2)

Gall S, Adams L, Joubert N, Ludyga S, Müller I, Nqweniso S, Pühse U, du Randt R, Seelig H, Smith D, Steinmann P, Utzinger J, Walter C, Gerber M. (2018). Effect of a 20-week physical activity intervention on selective attention and academic performance in children living in disadvantaged neighborhoods: A cluster randomized control trial. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0206908. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed linear regression (Model 2) with the same covariate set; no significant intervention effect on error percentage. Equivalence was not tested.

> "the physical activity intervention had no significant effect on E% at post-intervention (B = -1.05, 95% CI:-0.69 to 2.78, p = 0.237)"
