Descriptive prevalence from the review's evidence map of 421 studies, coded on the A0–A5 actionability scale under versioned rules. The review calls this "the actionability discontinuity" and reports A2+ at 61.3% versus A3+ at 11.6%.

"The joint map's sharpest quantified gap is the actionability discontinuity: demonstrated output or stronger (A2+: 258/421; 61.3%) versus intended-user evaluation or stronger (A3+: 49/421; 11.6%), a 49.7 percentage-point drop."
