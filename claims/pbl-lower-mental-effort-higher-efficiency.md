## Evidence

### Salari Mohsen 2018

Salari Mohsen, Roozbehi Amrollah, Zarifi Abdolvahed, Tarmizi Rohani Ahmad. (2018). Pure PBL, Hybrid PBL and Lecturing: which one is more effective in developing cognitive skills of undergraduate students in pediatric nursing course?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1305-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on Paas Mental Effort ratings and the two-dimensional Instructional Efficiency Index for the three groups (n = 30/30/35). Table 3 reports mental effort of assessment test F(2, 92) = 5.67, p = .005; posttest mental effort F = 13.69, p = .001; instructional efficiency F = 10.88, p = .001, with COTL efficiency mean −.77 versus .38 and .52.

> "The Tukey HSD test indicated that the means of mental effort during the learning phase test and the mental effort during the posttest for the COTL group were significantly higher than those of the PPBL and HPBL groups."
