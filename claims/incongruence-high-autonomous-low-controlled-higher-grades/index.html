Evidence¶

Mouratidis 2021¶

Mouratidis, A., Michou, A., Sayil, M., & Altan, S. (2021). It is autonomous, not controlled motivation that counts: Linear and curvilinear relations of autonomous and controlled motivation to school grades. Learning and Instruction, 73, 101433. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101433

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In Sample 1 the congruence-line slope was nonsignificant (p = .061) though curvature was significant, and inspection showed grades decreased as both motivations jointly increased up to a point before rebounding. The authors describe the pattern as inconclusive for Hypothesis 2a.

"The slope was nonsignificant (α1 = 1.15, SE = 0.61, t[3093] = 0.1.88, p = .061, 95%-CI: 2.35, 0.03), but its curvature was statistically significant"
