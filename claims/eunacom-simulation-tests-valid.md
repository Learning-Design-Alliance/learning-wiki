## Evidence

### Martínez F 2017

Martínez F, Tobar C, Taramasco C. (2017). Implementation of a Smartphone application in medical education: a randomised trial (iSTART). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1010-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors describe unpublished prior local data validating the 90-question practice tests used as outcomes; the simulations were used because the actual exam is managed independently and kept in strict reserve by ASOFAMECH.

> "previous data has shown that both simulation exams (baseline and final) have good correlation with overall EUNACOM scores (r > 0.7, p < 0.001), as well as an excellent diagnostic accuracy for detecting students at risk of failing the exam(area under the ROC curve 0.95, 95% CI 0.90 to 0.99)"
