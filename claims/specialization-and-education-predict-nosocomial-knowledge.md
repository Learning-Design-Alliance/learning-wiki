## Evidence

### Fortunka Kamila et al 2024

Fortunka Kamila et al. (2024). Knowledge and Training Needs in Nosocomial Infection among Hospital Staff in the City of Kielce, Poland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9243232

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression (forward stepwise, LR test; Hosmer–Lemeshow p=0.295, AUC = 0.605) on the 635-nurse survey. Specialization raised odds ("1.5 times higher among nurses with specialization"); medical high-school and vocational education lowered them as printed in Table 3.

> "the chance of having higher knowledge in the field of nosocomial infections is 1.5 times higher among nurses with specialization (OR = 1,489; 95% CI: 1,050–2,112; p=0.026)"
