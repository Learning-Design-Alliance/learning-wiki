## Evidence

### Karakoç Hediye 2026

Karakoç Hediye, Ozkan Hava. (2026). The effect of wearable simulated birth model on students’ empathy and privacy protection levels: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26, 383. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08441-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Robust mixed ANOVA of childbirth-fear scores across four time points in the three-arm trial; the time effect was significant (F = 530.721, p < 0.001, partial eta squared 0.956 per Table 3), with the post-simulation trimmed mean (4.82) lower than pre-test (5.79) and later follow-ups. The group effect was non-significant (p = 0.163).

> "Regardless of group, the trimmed mean scores for fear of childbirth differed over time (p < 0.001). A difference was observed between the post-simulation follow-up and other follow-ups."
