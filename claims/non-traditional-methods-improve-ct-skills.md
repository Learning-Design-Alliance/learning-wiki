## Evidence

### Lee JuHee 2016

Lee JuHee, Lee Yoonju, Gong SaeLom, Bae Juyeon, Choi Moonki. (2016). A meta-analysis of the effects of non-traditional teaching methods on the critical thinking abilities of nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5025580/

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis using random effects model of six CCTST datasets from five studies (pooled n = 452; experimental = 230, control = 222). Found teaching methods "were also had significantly more effects when compared to the control groups" with SMD = 0.29, 95% CI 0.10–0.48, p = 0.003. High heterogeneity I² = 79%.

> "And six CCTST datasets showed the teaching and learning methods in these studies were also had significantly more effects when compared to the control groups (SMD: 0.29, 95 % CI: 0.10–0.48, p = 0.003)."
