## Evidence

### Karbownik Michał Seweryn 2020

Karbownik Michał Seweryn, Kręczyńska Joanna, Kwarta Paulina, Cybula Magdalena, Wiktorowska-Owczarek Anna, Kowalczyk Edward, Pietras Tadeusz, Szemraj Janusz. (2020). Effect of Supplementation with Saccharomyces Boulardii on Academic Examination Performance and Related Stress in Healthy Medical Students: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051469

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-protocol analysis of the randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial's primary outcome, the computer-based final pharmacology examination, in 25 Saccharomyces and 25 placebo participants. Scores were "41.9 (95% CI 40.1–43.7) vs. 40.1 (95% CI 38.3–42.0)" with F(1,46) = 1.85, p = 0.18; after adjusting for the preexamination test, F(1,45) = 2.15, p = 0.15.

> "A PP analysis found no significant difference in final examination score between the Saccharomyces and placebo groups: 41.9 (95% CI 40.1–43.7) vs. 40.1 (95% CI 38.3–42.0), respectively (F(1,46) = 1.85, p = 0.18)."

### Karbownik Michał Seweryn 2020 (2)

Karbownik Michał Seweryn, Kręczyńska Joanna, Kwarta Paulina, Cybula Magdalena, Wiktorowska-Owczarek Anna, Kowalczyk Edward, Pietras Tadeusz, Szemraj Janusz. (2020). Effect of Supplementation with Saccharomyces Boulardii on Academic Examination Performance and Related Stress in Healthy Medical Students: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051469

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat sensitivity analysis (31 vs. 29 participants) of the primary outcome using MICE imputation for missing covariates. The ITT comparison was "42.8 (95% CI 40.9–44.7) vs. 40.5 (95% CI 38.5–42.4)" and remained non-significant, though closer to threshold (p = 0.094 unadjusted; p = 0.076 adjusted).

> "The comparison of the final examination scores was 42.8 (95% CI 40.9–44.7) vs. 40.5 (95% CI 38.5–42.4), F(1,56) = 2.90, p = 0.094, while, after adjusting for the results of the preexamination test, was 42.8 (95% CI 41.0–44.6) vs. 40.4 (95% CI 38.5–42.3), F(1,55) = 3.27, p = 0.076."
