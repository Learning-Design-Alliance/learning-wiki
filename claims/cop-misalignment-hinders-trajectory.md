### W. Brian Lane and Cortney Headley 2022

W. Brian Lane and Cortney Headley. (2022). Student Representations of Computation in the Physics Community. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020110)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theoretical argument within the Communities of Practice framework, illustrated with a scenario in which a student learns numerical integration in an electromagnetism course but does not activate it in a later computation-free quantum mechanics course unless they perceive it as personally helpful and valid. This is an argued claim, not a tested result; the article reports no empirical test of this transfer scenario.

> "This misalignment between experts’ practices and stu- dents’ perceptions can hinder students’ progress along an inbound trajectory."
