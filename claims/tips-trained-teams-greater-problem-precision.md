## Evidence

### Todd 2019

Todd, A. W., Algozzine, B., Horner, R. H., Preston, A. I., Cusumano, D., & Algozzine, K. (2019). A Descriptive Study of School-Based Problem-Solving. Journal of Emotional and Behavioral Disorders, 27(1), 14-24. https://doi.org/10.1177/1063426617733717

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Direct-observation (DORA) descriptive analysis within a randomized wait-list controlled trial of 38 elementary school teams, comparing precision elements for 196 problems discussed by trained versus untrained teams 6 months after TIPS training. Table 1 reports chi-square comparisons for each element, e.g. why: χ2 = 33.743, p = .000, favoring trained teams. The article reports statistically significant differences with no effect size printed.

> "A comparison of precision elements discussed across trained and untrained teams is in Table 1; problems discussed by teams trained in TIPS were more likely to be defined with greater precision (e.g., who, what, where, when, and why) than untrained teams."
