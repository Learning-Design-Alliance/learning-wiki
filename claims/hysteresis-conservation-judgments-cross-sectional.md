Hartelman, P. A. I., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Molenaar, P. C. M. (1998). Detecting and modelling developmental transitions. British Journal of Developmental Psychology, 16, 97-122. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2044-835X.1998.tb00751.x

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Cross-sectional manipulation study reported as the authors' own prior work: children judged weight equality between a clay ball and a sausage rolled to increasing lengths and then back to ball form. 'Of the remaining 18 children, 10 gave inconsistent, non-interpretable responses, six showed hysteresis and two a sudden jump', which the authors think promising.

> "Of 65 children, 47 gave consistent non-conserving or conserving responses. Of the remaining 18 children, 10 gave inconsistent, non-interpretable responses, six showed hysteresis and two a sudden jump (van der Maas & Molenaar, 1995)."
