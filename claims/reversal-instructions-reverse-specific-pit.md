## Evidence

### Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke 2025

Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke. (2025). Pavlovian to instrumental transfer of control over fight or flight decisions. npj Science of Learning 10:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00331-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 (final N=76, online, single-decision transfer measure) analyzed fight proportions in a 2 (Instruction) x 2 (CS+AB) mixed ANOVA reporting a significant two-way interaction with a large printed effect size, partial eta squared ηp2 = 0.191 (ηp² = 0.191). Standard-instruction participants chose fight more for CS+A (M=61%) than CS+B (M=43%); reversal participants showed the opposite pattern (28% vs 60%).

> "This analysis revealed a signi ﬁcant two-way interaction, F(1, 74) = 17.49, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.191."
