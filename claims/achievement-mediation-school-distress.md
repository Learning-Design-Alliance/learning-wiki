The Role of Academic Achievement in the Relationship between School Ethos and Adolescent Distress and Aggression: A Study of Ninth Grade Students in the Segregated School Landscape of Stockholm. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8116286/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-level structural results from the final ML-SEM model (n = 9776 students, 150 schools). The article reports the indirect pathway "(b = −0.003, p = 0.023)"; the unstandardised indirect effect is the only printed magnitude, so no standardised effect size is reported.

> "However, higher average academic achievement among students was significantly associated with lower psychological distress in the student population. Achievement mediated the effect of school ethos on psychological distress, indicating an indirect pathway for school ethos (b = −0.003, p = 0.023)."
