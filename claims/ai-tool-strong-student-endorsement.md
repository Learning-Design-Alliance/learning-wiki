### Prinz 2026

Prinz, M., Schäfer, E., Bürklein, S. & Donnermeyer, D. (2026) Endodontic diagnostics training in undergraduate dental education: An observational pilot study on AI‐driven virtual patient e‐learning. International Endodontic Journal, 59, 1145–1159. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14277

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive results of terminatory questionnaire (T2) among 72 students (Table 5, items 2.3). Students also agreed the concept is suitable for improving diagnostic skills and boosting confidence, and 70.8% strongly agreed they wanted more (challenging) cases. No inferential statistics were reported.

> "The tool was recommended to be available for endodontic education by 72.2% of the students, who strongly agreed to such a statement. 76.4% of the participants strongly agreed to recommend the use of the tool to other students."
