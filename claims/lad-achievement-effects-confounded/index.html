Rogers Kaliisa 2023¶

Rogers Kaliisa, Kamila Misiejuk, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Mohammad Khalil, Mohammed Saqr. (2023). Have Learning Analytics Dashboards Lived Up to the Hype? A Systematic Review of Impact on Students' Achievement, Motivation, Participation and Attitude. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://laka.ub.uni-siegen.de/index.php/laka/article/view/23

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Review-level analysis of the 38-study corpus examining why larger achievement effects emerged. The review reports LADs were "combined with another type of intervention (and thus had an obvious confounding)" or used non-controlled designs, undermining attribution to dashboards alone.

"In almost all the studies that reported medium or large effect sizes, though few, LADs have either been combined with another type of intervention (and thus had an obvious confounding) or were assessed using a non-controlled design"
