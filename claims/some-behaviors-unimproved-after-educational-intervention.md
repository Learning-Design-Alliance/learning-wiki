## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Behaviors in four elements — exchange of information, operating room management, maintaining standards, and cooperation — were not significantly improved or still scored below average after the intervention. [→ Kalantari Reza 2021](#kalantari-reza-2021)

## Evidence

### Kalantari Reza 2021

Kalantari Reza, Zamanian Zahra, Hasanshahi Mehdi, Faghihi Seyed Aliakbar, Jamali Jamshid, Niakan Hadi. (2021). The Effect of Educational Intervention on the Improvement of Nontechnical Skills in Circulating Nurses. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/5856730

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of the semiexperimental study lists unimproved behaviors including helping the anesthesia team, controlling clinician traffic, information transfer during hand-offs, and not using mobile phones. "Avoiding unnecessary escapes from the operating room and not using mobile phones during surgery were the behaviors with the lowest scores."

> "The significantly unimproved behaviors after the educational intervention or the behaviors that still had lower scores than average belonged to only four elements, namely, exchange of information, operating room management, maintaining standards, and cooperation."
