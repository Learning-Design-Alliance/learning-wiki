Marcin Jukiewicz 2025¶

Marcin Jukiewicz. (2025). A systematic comparison of Large Language Models for automated assignment assessment in programming education: Exploring the importance of architecture and vendor. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26483 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2509.26483

q2 · i? · associational · r2

ICC(2,1) comparison with human grades across model size variants (Table 5). The study reports "the full gpt-4.1 (0.421) significantly outperforms gpt-4.1-nano (0.204)" and similar patterns for gpt-5 and gpt-4o families, suggesting reduced model complexity diminishes consistency.

"For instance, the full gpt-4.1 (0.421) significantly outperforms gpt-4.1-nano (0.204). Similarly, gpt-5 (0.378) performs better than gpt-5-nano (0.314), and gpt-4o (0.438) outperforms gpt-4o-mini (0.365)."
