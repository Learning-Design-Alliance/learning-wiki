`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison on the LAS for the 23-child experimental group taught Arabic grammar with Mind Maps over four months at AlRae'd Kindergarten. The mean difference was (3.6250) with SD (6.32314), and the article reports a statistically significant difference between pre and post test scores.

> "The difference mean scores of pre-post tests of LAS was (3.6250) for the experimental group, with SD (6.32314), and T.value of (3.8580) which was greater than the tabulated value (2.365), at ( α = 0.05)."
