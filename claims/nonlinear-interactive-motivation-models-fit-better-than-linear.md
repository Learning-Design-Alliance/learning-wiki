## Evidence

### Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-comparison results from the TIMSS 2019 cross-national analysis, in which 37 families of polynomial regression models were estimated per country. The article reports that polynomials with nonlinear or interactive effects "always provided a better fit than solutions including linear effects only".

> "polynomials including nonlinear or interactive effects of autonomous and controlled motivation on achievement (i.e., interactive, quadratic, cubic, or interactive-quadratic effects) always provided a better fit than solutions including linear effects only"
