## Evidence

### Singh 2016

Singh, C. and Schunn, C. D. (2016). Connecting Three Pivotal Concepts in K-12 Science State Standards and Maps of Conceptual Growth to Research in Physics Education. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.06024](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.06024)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

This is the authors' synthesis of multiple studies using the Force Concept Inventory (FCI), a standardized multiple-choice assessment used extensively in high schools and introductory college physics courses. The article reports that "a majority of students do not develop a Newtonian view of force and motion after traditional instruction" (citing Hake, 1998); no effect size is printed.

> "A variety of studies with this instrument have shown that a majority of students do not develop a Newtonian view of force and motion after traditional instruction"
