# Visual-estimation accuracy is bound to thickener type: students outperformed practitioners on Level 4 starch liquids but failed almost entirely on Level 4 xanthan gum liquids

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students identified Level 4 starch-based liquids more accurately than practitioners (88.0% vs. 68.4%, p = 0.028) but were far less accurate on Level 4 xanthan gum liquids (2.0% vs. 29.8%, p < 0.001). [→ Fong Raymond 2026](#fong-raymond-2026)
`q2 i?` A McNemar's test showed a highly significant proportional asymmetry between the two Level 4 conditions with negative inter-trial agreement, indicating accuracy is tied to thickener type under video-based conditions. [→ Fong Raymond 2026 (2)](#fong-raymond-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Fong Raymond 2026

Fong Raymond, Kwan Connie C. Y. (2026). Accuracy of Visual Inspection of Thickened Liquids: Implications for Multidisciplinary Dysphagia Management and Clinical Nutrition. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70294

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of independence compared item-level accuracy between cohorts across the four video trials (Table 1). The study reports Level 4 starch accuracy of 88.0% for students versus 68.4% for practitioners (p = 0.028) and Level 4 xanthan gum accuracy of 2.0% versus 29.8% (p < 0.001). No effect sizes are printed.

> "For Level 4 xanthan gum samples, students were significantly worse than practitioners (p < 0.001). For starch‐based samples, students were significantly better than practitioners for both Level 1 (p = 0.046) and Level 4 samples (p = 0.028)."

### Fong Raymond 2026 (2)

Fong Raymond, Kwan Connie C. Y. (2026). Accuracy of Visual Inspection of Thickened Liquids: Implications for Multidisciplinary Dysphagia Management and Clinical Nutrition. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70294

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

McNemar's test and Cohen's Kappa compared paired Level 4 starch and Level 4 xanthan gum conditions in the combined cohort. The authors interpret this as a possible product-specific perceptual bias, since starch becomes cloudy and opaque while xanthan gum stays translucent. No effect size in standardised units is printed.

> "The analysis revealed a profound, highly significant proportional asymmetry between thickener types (McNemar's χ2=50.57‬, p < 0.001), paired with a negative statistical agreement metric (ϰ = −0.1085)."
