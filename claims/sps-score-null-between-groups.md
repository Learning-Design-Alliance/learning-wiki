In the same quasi-experimental OSCE assessment of 100 Taiwanese nurses, standardized patients rated each participant's communication and empathy after the encounter. SP-rated SPS scores were slightly higher in the experimental group but "the difference was not statistically significant"; the SPS global score showed p = .86.
