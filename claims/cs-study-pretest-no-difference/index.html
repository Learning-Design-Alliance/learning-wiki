Evidence¶

Mesgarshahr and Abdollahzadeh 2014¶

Abulfazl Mesgarshahr, Esmaeel Abdollahzadeh. (2014). The impact of teaching communication strategies on EFL learners’ Willingness to Communicate. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2014.4.1.4

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Baseline check in the same intact-class quasi-experiment: an independent-samples t test on the 27-item WTC questionnaire showed "no statistically significant difference before the treatment" between groups (t(113) = 1.035, p>.05). Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.

"There was no statistically significant difference before the treatment in the degree of WTC between the experimental group (M = 45.36, SD = 26.72) and the control group (M = 40.46, SD = 24.39), with t(113) = 1.035, p>.05."
