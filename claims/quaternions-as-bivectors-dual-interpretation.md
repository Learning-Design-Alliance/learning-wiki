Martin Erik Horn. (2006). Quaternions and Geometric Algebra. Deutsche Physikalische Gesellschaft, Frühjahrstagung Kassel 2006. https://worldcat.org/isbn/978-3-86541-190-7

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Analytical derivation in section 5 applying the Jordan-Cartan formulae (i = –iσx, etc.) under the identification of the complex unit with the pseudoscalar. The article concludes: "The purely complex part of a quaternion can thus be interpreted as a bivector, i.e. as a directed plane."

> "If one identifies the complex unity i with the pseudoscalar I, then they can be interpreted as a result of a duality transformation within the context of geometric algebra"
