## Evidence

### Roger E. Beaty & Paul J. Silvia 2013

Roger E. Beaty & Paul J. Silvia. (2013). Metaphorically speaking: cognitive abilities and the production of figurative language. Memory & Cognition. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-012-0258-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM of conventional metaphor aptness (Chiappe & Chiappe fill-in-the-blank task, two raters, α = .97) in 191 undergraduates found "a significant effect of Gc on metaphor ratings ( β 0 .30, p 0 .005)"; Gf and Gr effects were small and nonsignificant.

> "For the conventional metaphor task, the structural model found a significant effect of Gc on metaphor ratings ( β 0 .30, p 0 .005). However, the direct effects of Gf ( β 0 .10, p 0 .371) and Gr ( β 0 .08, p 0 .469) were small and nonsignificant"
