Douglas H. Clements 2013¶

Douglas H. Clements, Julie Sarama, Carolyn Layzer, Fatih Unlu, Christopher B. Wolfe, Mary Elaine Spitler. (2013). Longitudinal evaluation of a scale-up model for teaching mathematics with trajectories and technologies: Persistence of effects three years after the treatment. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563385

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Legacy results from the authors' earlier evaluations of the same intervention (cited as Authors, 2008, 2009, 2011), reported in the Background section. The printed effect sizes are ES=.72 for pre-K mathematics and ES=.13, .16, .29, .36 for kindergarten language competencies; the first-grade follow-through contrast (ES=.28 vs ES=.51) is printed in the same Background passage.

"Our implementation of this model with the Building Blocks pre-K math curriculum has shown positive effects on pre-K mathematics (ES=.72) and on kindergarten language competencies (ES=.13, .16, .29, .36)"
