`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal SEM with baseline (pre-k/kindergarten entry) predictors and end-of-first-grade KTEA-II outcomes for MA students; for DA students the corresponding coefficients were .86 and .23, and the article reports both as significant predictors.
