Theoretical argument in this Perspective: unlike specialised, temporally bounded, passive tools such as books or Montessori materials, AI-based conversational systems function as active or semi-active participants in epistemic processes. The article states the convergence of functions within a single system is 'a scaling effect that is qualitatively distinct in the case of AI technologies'.
