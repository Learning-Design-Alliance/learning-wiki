Thomas Rajotte 2016¶

Thomas Rajotte, Christine Marcotte, Lisa Bureau-Levasseur. (2016). Evaluation of the Effect of Mathematical Routines on the Development of Skills in Mathematical Problem Solving and School Motivation of Primary School Students in Abitibi-Témiscamingue. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(10): 2386-2391. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.041017

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The article's Tables 1 and 3 print non-significant group effects (e.g., intrinsic motivation Sig. 0,60 in Table 1; identified extrinsic motivation Sig. 0,233 in Table 3); the authors attribute the unrevealed effects to reduced statistical power from a relatively small sample.

"Consequently, it is possible that the proposed interventions had an effect on other components of school motivation but we were unable to reveal them."
