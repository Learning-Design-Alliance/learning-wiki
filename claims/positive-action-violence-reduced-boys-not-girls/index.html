WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action 2007¶

WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.positiveaction.net

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

In the randomized controlled trial of 20 Hawaii schools, grade 5 boys in Positive Action schools reported serious violence at 10.8 versus 23.5 in comparison schools ("12.70 0.56 Statistically significant"), an effect size of 0.56. The parallel girls' finding (mean difference -2.20, effect size -0.30) was not statistically significant, so the effect is limited to boys.
