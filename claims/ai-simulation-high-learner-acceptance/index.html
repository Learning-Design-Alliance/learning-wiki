Synthesis of acceptance evidence from 8 studies using standardized instruments (SUS in 3 studies, TAM in 2, implementation measures exceeding 4.6/7) found "System Usability Scale scores ranged from 61.6 to 78.56" and 72%-93% willingness to recommend.
