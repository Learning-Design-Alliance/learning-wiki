## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants on Korbit (full) spent significantly more time studying and interacting with the platform (141 ± 28 min) than Korbit (no feedback) participants (101 ± 29 min), significant at the 95% confidence level (p = 0.031). [→ Francois St-Hilaire 2022](#francois-st-hilaire-2022)
`q2 i?` The two Korbit groups used the same platform with respect to study material and exercises, isolating feedback amount as the difference. [→ Francois St-Hilaire 2022 (2)](#francois-st-hilaire-2022-2)
