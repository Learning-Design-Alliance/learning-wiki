The report's summary of its evidence on the Missouri Career Ladder Program states that district participation "would tend to increase retention in the district and the profession, especially for mid-career teachers." No effect size or sample statistics are printed on the page.

"This report presents evidence suggesting that a school district’s participation in the Missouri Career Ladder Program would tend to increase retention in the district and the profession, especially for mid-career teachers."
