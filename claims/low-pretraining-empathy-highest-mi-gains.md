## Evidence

### Svensson C 2020

Svensson C, Wickström H, Emanuelson U, Bard AM, Reyher KK, Forsberg L. (2020). Training in motivational interviewing improves cattle veterinarians' communication skills for herd health management. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1136/vr.105646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable regression modelling of pre/post MITI scores from 31 veterinarians including Training × Empathy pre-training interactions. The article reports significant interactions with pre-training Empathy for Relational, MI-adherent Behaviours, Affirmations and Complex Reflections, and interprets the highest development in low-Empathy participants as reflecting their bigger gains to make.

> "However, whereas others have found high Empathy skills pre-training to be associated with increased MI skill acquisition,38 this study found the highest skills development in participants with low Empathy pre-training, potentially as these participants had the biggest gains to make."
