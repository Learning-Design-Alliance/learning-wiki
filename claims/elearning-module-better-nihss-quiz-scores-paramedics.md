# Paramedics taught the NIHSS with an interactive e-learning module achieved better overall quiz scores than paramedics taught with the subtitled NIHSS video

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled trial, paramedics who followed the e-learning module had a better overall median quiz score (36/50 vs 33/50, P=.04) than those who watched the video. [→ Koka A 2020](#koka-a-2020)

## Evidence

### Koka A 2020

Koka A, Suppan L, Cottet P, Carrera E, Stuby L, Suppan M. (2020). Teaching the National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale to Paramedics (E-Learning vs Video): Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/18358

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized (1:1) controlled trial with 39 paramedics (20 e-learning, 19 video) in Geneva; primary outcome was overall performance on a 50-question quiz including three full NIHSS video evaluations. The e-learning group's median was "36 (34;37) vs 33 (31;38), P=.04". No effect size printed.

> "Participants who followed the e-learning module performed better than those who followed the video—36 (34;37) vs 33 (31;38), P=.04."
