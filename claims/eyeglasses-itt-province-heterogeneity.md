# The ITT effect of providing eyeglasses is significant in poor Gansu (0.123 SD) but not in wealthier Shaanxi, with a significant province interaction

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Providing free eyeglasses improved myopic students' standardized math scores by 0.123 SD in Gansu (significant at 1%) but had no significant impact in Shaanxi; the interaction measuring the difference between provinces was significant at the 5% level. [→ Du Kang 2022](#du-kang-2022)

## Evidence

### Du Kang 2022

Du Kang, Wang Huan, Ma Yue, Guan Hongyu, Rozelle Scott. (2022). Effect of Eyeglasses on Student Academic Performance: What Matters? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710923

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ITT heterogeneity analysis (Table 2, Columns 3–4) of the two-province RCT, with the treatment-by-province interaction significant at the 5% level. Gansu effect "0.123 standard deviations" significant at 1%; Shaanxi point estimates smaller with large standard errors and no significant impact.

> "In Gansu, when using the unadjusted model, providing free eyeglasses improved myopic students‘ standardized math scores by 0.122 standard deviations compared to the control group (Row 4, Column 3), which is statistically significant at the 1% level. According to the adjusted model, the magnitude of the coefficient is very similar, i.e., 0.123 standard deviations with statistically significant at the 1% level (Row 4, Column 4). In Shaanxi, however, the point estimates are smaller and standard errors are large in both the unadjusted and adjusted models, resulting in no significant impact in either model in Shaanxi (Row 5)."
