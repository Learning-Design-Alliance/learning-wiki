### do Carmo André Luis Santos 2022

do Carmo André Luis Santos, Fredo Fernanda Wagner, Bruck Isac, de Lima Joseli do Rocio Maito, Janke Rebecca Nóbrega Ribas Gusso Harder, Fogaça Thais da Glória Messias, Glaser Jacqueline Andrea, Riechi Tatiana Izabele Jaworski de Sá, Antoniuk Sergio Antonio. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychoeducational evaluation of 73 children from the preterm follow-up cohort (ambispective cross-sectional design), using staff-created standardized reading, writing and mathematics tests graded by age and school year; "57.5% in the reading test" and 42.5% in mathematics, 38.% in writing failed.

> "In the psychoeducational evaluation, 38.% presented flaws in the writing test; 57.5% in the reading test; and 42.5% in the mathematical test."
