System-type frequency analysis across the review's 27 included studies. The review reports LADs as "the most frequently reported system type" and notes the counts sum to 31 because four studies reported more than one system type; within the 21 LAD studies, monitoring was the most frequently coded function (20 studies, 95.2%).

"Learning Analytics Dashboards were the most frequently reported system type (21 studies, 77.8%), followed by Intelligent Tutoring Systems (6, 22.2%) and agentic AI systems (4, 14.8%)."
