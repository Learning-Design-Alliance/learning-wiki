Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Personalized TMR improves error correction (higher incorrect-to-correct transitions) and reduces persistence of incorrect responses, mainly for challenging items — related
  • Personalized TMR improves recall accuracy for the most challenging (L3) word pairs, outperforming both uniform TMR and no-stimulation control in the post-sleep session — related
  • Multivariate classification of SW power, spindle power, and SW-spindle coupling distinguishes the personalized TMR, TMR, and control groups above chance, peaking around 3 s after cue onset — related
  • TMR efficacy depends on memory strength and task difficulty: benefits concentrate on moderately strong and challenging items, with limited advantage for easy (L1) items — related
  • Exploratory analysis finds moderate-magnitude correlation between fast spindle-slow oscillation coupling strength and retention of no-feedback retrieval-practice items — related
  • Cortical delta oscillations during slow-wave sleep support declarative memory consolidation — related
  • Hippocampal sharp-wave ripples during SWS and waking support memory consolidation and spatial task performance — related
