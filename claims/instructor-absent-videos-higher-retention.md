## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among honest students, ability to retain concepts was higher in the instructor-absent (IA) arm than the instructor-present (IP) arm (covariate-adjusted estimate 0.395, 95% CI [0.022, 0.767]). [→ Manjusha Kancharla and Hyunseung Kang 2021](#manjusha-kancharla-and-hyunseung-kang-2021)
