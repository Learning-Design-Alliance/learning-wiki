## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One month after the exercise there was no statistically significant difference between the VP and SP groups in retention of clinical reasoning ability or net gain, although scores decreased slightly from posttest by 0.1 to 1.4 points. [→ Malik Tayyaba Gul 2024](#malik-tayyaba-gul-2024)

## Evidence

### Malik Tayyaba Gul 2024

Malik Tayyaba Gul, Mahboob Usman, Khan Rehan Ahmed, Alam Rabia. (2024). Virtual patients versus standardized patients for improving clinical reasoning skills in ophthalmology residents. A randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05241-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-month follow-up posttest in the same randomized trial (one resident missed the follow-up and was excluded from the final analysis). Net pretest-to-follow-up gain remained significant (p value < 0.00), described as a "slight decrease" from posttest of − 0.1 to − 1.4.

> "In the follow -up posttest, there was a slight decrease in the scores compared to those of the posttest, ranging from − 0.1 to − 1.4. However, the net gain in clinical reasoning skill significantly improved from the pretest to the follow-up posttest (p value < 0.00)."
