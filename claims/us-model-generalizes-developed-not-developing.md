## Evidence

### Xingyu Li 2021

Xingyu Li, Difan Song, Miaozhe Han, Yu Zhang, and René F. Kizilcec. (2021). On the limits of algorithmic prediction across the globe. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.15212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.15212)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Evaluation of the U.S.-trained random forest model in all 65 countries of the 2015 PISA (N = 459,842 students), keeping features constant and imputing with U.S. training-set medians. The article reports "the average accuracy was 60.2%" with the Sweden-to-Indonesia range. No effect size is printed.

> "As expected, the U.S. model was generally less accurate when applied in other countries: the average accuracy was 60.2% and varied between 74% in Sweden and 50% (i.e. no better than chance) in Indonesia."
