### Kliem Paulina S. C. 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Randomized simulator-based trial with 124 physicians at University Hospital Basel comparing three guideline formats. The condensed guideline group achieved "side positioning for airway protection (63% with condensed guideline vs. 25% in controls and 26% in the full guideline group; p<0.001)".

> "Participants using the condensed guideline showed higher adherence to key interventions, with significantly improved rates of side positioning for airway protection (63% with condensed guideline vs. 25% in controls and 26% in the full guideline group; p<0.001)"
