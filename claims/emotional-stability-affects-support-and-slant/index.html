Evidence¶

Matt Dennis 2016¶

Matt Dennis, Judith Masthoff, Chris Mellish. (2016). Adapting Progress Feedback and Emotional Support to Learner Personality. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0059-7

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Crowdsourced 6x2 between-subjects experiment on learner emotional stability; the 2-way MANOVA showed "a significant effect of score on the use of Advice, Reassurance, Praise and Emotional Reflection". No effect sizes are printed.

"H1 is confirmed, as there is a significant effect of score on the use of Advice, Reassurance, Praise and Emotional Reflection."
