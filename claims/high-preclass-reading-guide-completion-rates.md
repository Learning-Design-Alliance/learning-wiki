## Evidence

### Lieu 2017

Lieu, R., Wong, A., Asefirad, A., & Shaffer, J. F. (2017). Improving Exam Performance in Introductory Biology through the Use of Preclass Reading Guides. CBE—Life Sciences Education. [doi:10.1187/cbe.16-11-0320](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-11-0320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

End-of-course survey completed by 677 students on reading guide use timing and frequency. The survey found "∼86% of students reported that they completed the reading guides at least sometimes before class," with the most common completion time being 60–90 minutes per guide.

> "From this survey, ∼86% of students reported that they completed the reading guides at least sometimes before class. Approximately 9% of students reported that they completed the reading guides after class, while ∼5% reported that they did not complete them at all."

### Lieu 2017 (2)

Lieu, R., Wong, A., Asefirad, A., & Shaffer, J. F. (2017). Improving Exam Performance in Introductory Biology through the Use of Preclass Reading Guides. CBE—Life Sciences Education. [doi:10.1187/cbe.16-11-0320](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-11-0320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Daily in-class clicker question ("How did you prepare for class today?") with average response rate of 90.7 ± 4.9% of enrollment, analyzed across the course. Results show "nearly 50% reporting that they completed the entire reading guide before class each day."

> "On average, >75% of students reported completing at least some of the reading guide for a given day of class, with nearly 50% reporting that they completed the entire reading guide before class each day."
