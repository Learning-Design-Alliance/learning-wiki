## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Coaches were perceived as sources of emotional support by URM students, who described feeling cared about and relieved of stress after talking with coaches. [→ Williams 2017](#williams-2017)

## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative framework analysis of 96 interviews with 33 URM students over 3 years; Table 2 codes 19 students as receiving emotional support from coaches. This participant quote illustrates feeling "very relieved after I talked to her" about a stressful lab rotation.

> "There was a time I called because I had issues with one of my rotations … and she [the coach] was there, and we talked for a long time. I was very stressed at that time. But I remember feeling very relieved after I talked to her"

### Williams 2017 (2)

Williams, S. N., Thakore, B. K., & McGee, R. (2017). Providing Social Support for Underrepresented Racial and Ethnic Minority PhD Students in the Biomedical Sciences: A Career Coaching Model. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0021

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same interview study, 15 students were coded as receiving emotional support from peers; this quote shows the "constant encouragement" peers exchanged on qualifying exams and publications.

> "We always root [for] each other on when one of us qualifies or one of us gets a paper published. We have this constant encouragement … for any new achievements we have going on in our lives."
