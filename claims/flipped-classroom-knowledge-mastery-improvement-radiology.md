## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After attending the flipped classroom, the percentage of students reporting mastery of more than half the knowledge rose for case-solving skills, basic feature command, comparison ability, and overall knowledge command. [→ Wu Su 2020](#wu-su-2020)

## Evidence

### Wu Su 2020

Wu Su, Pan Shinong, Ren Ying, Yu Hong, Chen Qi, Liu Zhaoyu, Guo Qiyong. (2020). Existing contradictions and suggestions: flipped classroom in radiology courses of musculoskeletal disease under Chinese medical educational mode from medical imaging student perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1991-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-group questionnaire study of 55 fourth-year radiology students comparing self-reported mastery before and after the flipped classroom; "81.25, 85.42, 85.42, and 85.42% of students, respectively, had mastered more than half of the knowledge" after class, versus 1.25, 68.75, 64.58, and 72.92% before. No statistical test reported.

> "We analysed the same aspect of learning result (overall knowledge command, case-solving skills, basic features of diseases command, and comparison of different diseases); 81.25, 85.42, 85.42, and 85.42% of students, respectively, had mastered more than half of the knowledge (Fig. 3c)."
