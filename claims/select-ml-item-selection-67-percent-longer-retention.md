## Evidence

### Utkarsh Upadhyay 2021

Utkarsh Upadhyay, Graham Lancashire, Christoph Moser, and Manuel Gomez-Rodriguez. (2021). Large-scale randomized experiment reveals machine learning helps people learn and remember more effectively. arXiv. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1038/s41539-021-00105-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00105-8)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Large-scale randomized controlled trial in the iTheorie Führerschein Auto app. After preprocessing, the dataset contains data from about 313,000 study sessions by 11,481 learners. The article reports that select-group learners remember the content "over 92% longer than those whose study sessions are generated at random and 67% longer" than the difficulty group. No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "After controlling for how long learners study a question and how many times they review the question, we find that learners whose study sessions are optimized using our machine learning algorithm remember the content over 92% longer than those whose study sessions are generated at random and 67% longer than those whose study sessions are generated using smarter heuristic"
