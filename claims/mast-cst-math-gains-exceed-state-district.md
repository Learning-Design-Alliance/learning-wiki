Phillips, J. A., McElwain, M. C., & Clemmer, K. W. (2016). Metacognitive Training in Professional Development Can Improve and Sustain Student Achievement. Loyola Marymount University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.07856](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.07856)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational comparison of California Standards Test mathematics results for the seven Alliance schools implementing MAST against all California schools and all LAUSD schools, using normalized change. The article reports Alliance gains of "11% to 29%, <c>= 0.20" versus state and district gains of 0.03 and 0.06.

> "The dramatic rise in the number of students at or above Proficient in the Alliance schools (11% to 29%, <c>= 0.20) was not reflected in the data from the state (31% to 33%, <c>= 0.03) or the district (19% to 24%, <c>= 0.06)"
