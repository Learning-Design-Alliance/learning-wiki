# Quantitative longitudinal studies mostly show minor change in team learning behaviours, with dynamics in boundary crossing and team activity

## Evidence

### Andreas Widmann 2025

Andreas Widmann, Regina H. Mulder. (2025). Change in team learning behaviours in work teams – A systematic review. Educational Research Review 49. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2025.100736

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis of eight quantitative longitudinal studies using surveys, classified by correlations, regression coefficients and means. The review reports the results "indicate no stability but rather minor change", with mean differences between .01 and .15 across measurement points.

> "Finally, the results indicate no stability but rather minor change, as the means vary over time. The differences in the means between the measurement points vary between .01 and .15."

### Andreas Widmann 2025 (2)

Andreas Widmann, Regina H. Mulder. (2025). Change in team learning behaviours in work teams – A systematic review. Educational Research Review 49. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2025.100736

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quantitative study (Widmann et al., 2019) of boundary crossing over three measurement points at three-month intervals, analysed with cross-lagged panel models. The review reports the frequency "marginally decreased" then increased; a second study found dynamics in team activity with an effect of time (B = .37, p < 0.05).

> "Between the first and second measurement point the frequency of engagement in boundary crossing marginally decreased (Mean1 = 3.81, SD = .75; Mean2 = 3.71, SD = .87) and increased between the second and third measurement point (Mean3 = 4.60, SD = .90)."
