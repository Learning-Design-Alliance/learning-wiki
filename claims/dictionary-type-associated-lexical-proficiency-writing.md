# Dictionary type used is significantly associated with lexical proficiency in writing among upper-intermediate Iranian EFL learners

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A chi-square test found a significant relationship between type of dictionary used and lexical proficiency (p = .001), rejecting the first null hypothesis. [→ Vahdany 2014](#vahdany-2014)
`q2 i?` Eta association between dictionary type and lexical proficiency was high (.772 with lexical proficiency dependent), indicating a strong degree of association. [→ Vahdany 2014](#vahdany-2014)

## Evidence

### Vahdany 2014

Vahdany, F., Abdollahzadeh, M., Gholami, S., & Ghanipoor, M. (2014). A Study of the Relationship between Type of Dictionary Used and Lexical Proficiency in Writings of Iranian EFL Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.6p.31

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis of 20 compositions cross-tabulated with dictionary type yielded Pearson Chi-Square = 76.042, df = 40, p = .001; Eta = .969 (lexical proficiency dependent). The study reports "the relationship between type of dictionary used and lexical proficiency was not due to chance".

> "The two-sided asymptotic significance of the chi-square statistic was lower than 0.05, so it is safe to say that the relationship between type of dictionary used and lexical proficiency was not due to chance"

### Vahdany 2014 (2)

Vahdany, F., Abdollahzadeh, M., Gholami, S., & Ghanipoor, M. (2014). A Study of the Relationship between Type of Dictionary Used and Lexical Proficiency in Writings of Iranian EFL Students. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.6p.31

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA on lexical proficiency scores across dictionary-type groups (between groups df = 5) produced F = 42.571, p = .000, indicating "significant difference between the participants who used specific types of dictionaries in terms of their lexical proficiency".

> "the results indicated significant difference between the participants who used specific types of dictionaries in terms of their lexical proficiency. The significance of the F value in the ANOVA table is 0.000"
