`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review reports the Q-methodology study by Berkhout et al. identifying five patterns of clinical students' self-assessed SRL behaviour: "engaged", "critically opportunistic", "uncertain", "restrained" and "effortful", differing in goals, metacognition, communication, effort and dependence on external regulation.

> "Results also showed that the difficulty of learning in a clinical context was reflected in all these SRL patterns by the little‐planned SRL behaviour and limited goal setting."
