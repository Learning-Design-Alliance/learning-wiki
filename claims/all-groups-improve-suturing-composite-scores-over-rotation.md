## Evidence

### Hillemans Vera 2024

Hillemans Vera, Buyne Otmar, de Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne M.B.I., Verhoeven Bas H., Joosten Maja. (2024). Self-assessment, and not continuous training, improves basic open suturing skills. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2374101

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results of a study in which 76 medical students performed four suturing tasks tracked by SurgTrac before and after an eight-week surgical rotation, in three groups (clinical exposure n=44, continuous training n=16, self-assessment n=16). The article reports "a significantly better composite score was found at the after-test compared to the pre-test" with p-values 0.001–0.049; per-task tables show Wilcoxon S-R tests within each group.

> "A significantly better composite score was found at the after-test compared to the pre-test for all groups for all basic suturing tasks (0.001 ≤ p ≤ 0.049)."
