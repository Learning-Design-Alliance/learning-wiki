# Posttest performance of the replication's contingent condition was far below the original study's contingent condition, a large unregistered difference not seen for the three control conditions

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Outcome scores of the replication's contingent condition differed greatly from the 1978 contingent condition (d = 1.55), while verbal, swing, and demonstration means matched. [→ Smit 2025](#smit-2025)

## Evidence

### Smit 2025

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Unregistered two-sample comparison of contingent-condition outcome means between the 1978 study and the replication: t(75) = 4.20, p < .001, d = 1.55 (1978 M = 15.00, replication M = 5.07, per Table 5). The verbal, swing, and demonstration comparisons were all nonsignificant, indicating the discrepancy is specific to the contingent condition.
