## Evidence

### Saputri 2020

Saputri, W., Corebima, A. D., Susilo, H., & Suwono, H. (2020). QASEE: A potential learning model to improve the critical thinking skills of pre-service teachers with different academic abilities. European Journal of Educational Research, 9(2), 853-864. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.9.2.853

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc LSD comparison of the nine learning-model-by-ability combination groups in the quasi-experiment; the three QASEE combination groups shared LSD notation a, with corrected means 67.499, 65.141, and 65.497. The highest corrected mean overall was QASEE upper.

> "the combination group of the QASEE learning model and upper academic ability, QASEE learning model and middle academic ability, as well as QASEE learning model and lower academic ability is not significantly different. This can be interpreted that the QASEE learning model can facilitate the pre-service teachers at various levels of academic ability to improve their critical thinking skills"
