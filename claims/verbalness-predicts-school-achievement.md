## Evidence

### Arthur I. Gates 1923

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The Correlations of Achievement in School Subjects with Intelligence Tests and Other Variables (Concluded). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0066477

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across grades IV to VIII in one school, arranging group tests on a verbalness scale. The article reports the printed effect size partial r = 0.69: "the independent correlation (Partial r first order) with Achievement was 0.69."

> "When the individual Group Tests were arranged for the degree of verbalness, time being eliminated by the technique of partial correlations, the independent correlation (Partial r first order) with Achievement was 0.69."
