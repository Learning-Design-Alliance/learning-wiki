# Hunter's (1983) GATB restricted SDx of .67 is implausible; a later comparable set of 264 studies produced a restricted SDx of .94 and a U ratio of 1.06

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Hunter's pooled-incumbent estimate of restricted SDx (.67, U = 1.5) for the GATB database lacks a reasonable basis, since concurrent studies rarely show restricted SDx below .90; a later set of 264 comparable studies yielded .94 (U = 1.06). [→ Sackett 2022](#sackett-2022)

## Evidence

### Sackett 2022

Sackett, P. R.; Zhang, C.; Berry, C. M.; and Lievens, F. (2022). Revisiting Meta-Analytic Estimates of Validity in Personnel Selection: Addressing Systematic Overcorrection for Restriction of Range. Journal of Applied Psychology, 107(11), 2040-2068. https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/6894

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The authors argue Hunter's (1983) GATB corrected validity of .51 — the value long taken to represent cognitive ability test validity — rests on an implausible restricted SDx of .67, contrasting it with the later 264-study value of .94.

> "A later set of procedurally comparable 264 studies produced a restricted SDx of .94, and thus a U ratio of 1.06 (Sackett, Zhang, & Berry, in press)."
