# In the Torgesen et al. (2006) RCT, SpellRead™ showed indeterminate effects for fifth graders, with no statistically significant or substantively important effects in any domain after six months

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r2`–`r3` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` In a randomized trial of 104 fifth-grade students across eight school units, WWC-calculated alphabetics effects were not statistically significant and the domain-average effect size of 0.19 was below the 0.25 substantively-important threshold. [→ torgesen-alphabetics](#torgesen-alphabetics)
`q3 i0` In the same trial, reading fluency (effect size 0.08) and comprehension (effect size 0.00) domain averages were neither statistically significant nor substantively important. [→ torgesen-fluency-comprehension](#torgesen-fluency-comprehension)

## Evidence

### torgesen-alphabetics

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013, January). Adolescent Literacy intervention report: SpellRead™. http://whatworks.ed.gov

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial assigning 32 school units in Pennsylvania to four reading interventions; 59 fifth graders received SpellRead™ (about 90 hours over six months) and 45 received the standard district curriculum. The alphabetics domain average effect size was 0.19, characterized as an indeterminate effect.

> "The WWC-calculated estimates of program effects were not statistically significant. The average effect across the four outcomes was not large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria (i.e., an effect size of at least 0.25)."

### torgesen-fluency-comprehension

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013, January). Adolescent Literacy intervention report: SpellRead™. http://whatworks.ed.gov

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Same randomized trial: the fluency domain average effect size was 0.08 (Oral Reading Fluency test) and the comprehension domain average was 0.00 (WRMT-R and GRADE Passage Comprehension), both characterized as indeterminate effects.

> "The average effect size across the two outcomes was neither statistically significant nor large enough to be considered substantively important."
