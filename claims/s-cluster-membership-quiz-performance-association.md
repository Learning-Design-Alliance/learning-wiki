### Tom Zhang 2022

Tom Zhang, Michelle Taub, and Zhongzhou Chen. 2022. A Multi-level Trace Clustering Analysis Scheme for Measuring Students' Self-Regulated Learning Behavior in a Mastery-Based Online Learning Environment. In LAK22: 12th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK22). ACM, New York, NY, USA. [doi:10.1145/3506860.3506887](https://doi.org/10.1145/3506860.3506887)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

For each of the three OLM sequences, students were divided into near-equal quiz score tertiles and s-cluster membership distributions were compared with Fisher's exact tests, with post hoc pairwise tests adjusted using the Benjamini, Hochberg, and Yekutieli method. Differences were significant for each sequence (p < 0.0001).

> "Fisher tests conducted for each sequence detected significant differences in s-cluster membership among high, medium and low quiz score cohorts (𝑝 < 0.0001 for each sequence)."
