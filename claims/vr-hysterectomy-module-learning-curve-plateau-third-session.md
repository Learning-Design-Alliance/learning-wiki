# Learning curves on the VR hysterectomy module plateau after the third training session for time, path length and number of movements

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In simulator training parameters, plateaus in total procedure time, total instrument path length and total number of movements were reached after the third training session; idle time showed a visual but not statistically detected plateau, and no plateaus were detectable for injuries. [→ Jokinen Ewa 2020](#jokinen-ewa-2020)

## Evidence

### Jokinen Ewa 2020

Jokinen Ewa, Mikkola Tomi S., Härkki Päivi. (2020). Simulator training and residents’ first laparoscopic hysterectomy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-07270-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of automatically recorded practice-session parameters for the ten intervention participants, using Friedman and Wilcoxon signed rank tests. The study found "the plateau was reached after training with the module the third time"; no plateaus were detectable for vascular and organ injuries.

> "Learning curve plateaus were detected in total procedure time, total path length of instruments, and total number of movements of instruments (Fig. 3). In each parameter, the plateau was reached after training with the module the third time."
