`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Discussion section attributes null findings to "small sample size (n = 8)", ceiling effects from high initial agreement, and low prevalence of complications such as fistulas and abscesses. This is the authors' interpretation of the FDR-adjusted results.

> "The lack of statistical significance for improvement in all variables, despite numerical gains, may stem from small sample size (n = 8), high initial agreement (κ > 0.8) leading to ceiling effects, and the low prevalence of certain complications"
