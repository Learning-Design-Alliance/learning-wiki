Results overview for the Freshman OnTrack outcome in CPS high schools, comparing schools with strong 5Essentials Survey measures (such as Collective Responsibility and School-Wide Future Orientation) to schools with average measure scores. An example notes a school strong in spring 2016 saw a 4 percentage point larger OnTrack increase by spring 2017, beyond what prior performance predicted.
