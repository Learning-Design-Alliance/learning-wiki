## Evidence

### Praet 2013

Praet, M., Titeca, D., Ceulemans, A., & Desoete, A. (2013). Language in the prediction of arithmetic in kindergarten and grade 1. Learning and Individual Differences (manuscript LAEIND-D-12-OO174R1). https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/LAEIND-D-12-OO174R1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

First preparatory regression on 63 kindergarteners using Tedi-Math subtests; procedural counting (Cronbach's alpha = .73) and conceptual counting (alpha = .85) were both significant predictors of kindergarten arithmetic.

> "The regression analysis was significant (F 2, 62) = 9.961, p < .001, R² = .249)  for procedural counting knowledge  (p =.005) and conceptual counting knowledge (p = .037; see Table 2)."
