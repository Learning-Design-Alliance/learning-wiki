## Evidence

### Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah 2013

Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah. (2013). The Impact of Using SMS as Learning Support Tool on Students' Learning. International Education Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n10p112

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with ten randomly selected SMS-group students (5 female, 5 male), each lasting 20 to 30 minutes; responses were coded qualitatively. Students valued SMS as "familiar, easy to use, immediate, convenient, ubiquitous, and quiet" and reported better retention of key concepts.

> "All the interviewed students believed that the use of SMS technology as learning support tool has more advantages than disadvantages."
