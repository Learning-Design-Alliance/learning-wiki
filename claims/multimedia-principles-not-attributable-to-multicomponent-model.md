# Multimedia learning principles are not clearly attributable to the multicomponent model or to applied multimedia theories

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The most important principles for multimedia learning are neither clearly attributable to the basic multicomponent model nor to the applied models (cognitive theory of multimedia learning, cognitive load theory). [→ Judith Schweppe & Ralf Rummer 2015](#judith-schweppe-ralf-rummer-2015)
