Margunayasa 2019¶

Margunayasa, I G., Dantes, N., Marhaeni, A.A.I.N., & Suastra, I W. (2019). The Effect of Guided Inquiry Learning and Cognitive Style on Science Learning Achievement. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 737-750. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201135

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA main effect of cognitive style, measured with the MFFT (reliability = 0.72) in the same 2×2 quasi-experiment; reflective students' total mean was 24.03 vs impulsive 22.23. The article reports "F = 17.45 and p<0.05".
