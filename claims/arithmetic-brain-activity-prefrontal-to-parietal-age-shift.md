## Evidence

### Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review narrative of cross-sectional brain-imaging studies comparing children of different age groups or correlating activity with age. The review states these studies "have generally shown that with increasing age, brain activity decreases in prefrontal areas and increases in the parietal cortex and the occipitotemporal cortex"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "These studies have generally shown that with increasing age, brain activity decreases in prefrontal areas and increases in the parietal cortex and the occipitotemporal cortex."

### Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021 (2)

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review attributes this to the only study it knows of that investigated developmental changes at multiple time points in the same sample. The reported neural changes "showed decreases in brain activity in the prefrontal cortex as well as increases in the hippocampus", accompanied behaviourally by more fact retrieval and less counting; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The results showed decreases in brain activity in the prefrontal cortex as well as increases in the hippocampus."
