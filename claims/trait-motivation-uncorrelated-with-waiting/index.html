Evidence¶

Amrita Bains 2023¶

Amrita Bains, Carina Spaulding, Jessie Ricketts and Saloni Krishnan. (2023). Using a willingness to wait design to assess how readers value text. npj Science of Learning 8:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00160-3

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Pooled-data correlation analysis across both experiments, correlating individual-level task metrics (total waits, average enjoyment, correct answers) with the AMRS and time spent reading yesterday; none of these correlations reached significance. No effect sizes beyond the printed rs.

"we found that decision to wait did not significantly correlate with reading engagement, r = 0.04, p = 0.76, or AMRS, r = −0.09, p = 0.43. Similarly, comprehension scores did not significantly correlate with reading engagement, r = −0.16, p = 0.17, or AMRS, r = −0.07, p = 0.54"
