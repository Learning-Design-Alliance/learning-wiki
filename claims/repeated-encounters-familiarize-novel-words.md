## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students who claimed at pre-test not to have seen a word before reported at post-test that they could guess it, which the authors say was strengthened by repetition. [→ Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban 2011](#ali-karakas-and-arif-saricoban-2011)

## Evidence

### Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban 2011

Ali Karakaş and Arif Sariçoban. (2011). The Impact of Watching Subtitled Animated Cartoons on Incidental Vocabulary Learning of ELT Students. Teaching English with Technology, 12(4), 3-15. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1144953

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' conclusion from self-report VKS shifts in their own experiment: "students who claimed in the pre-test that they had not seen the word before reported in the post-test that they could make a guess". No effect size is printed for this pattern.

> "Those students who claimed in the pre-test that they had not seen the word before reported in the post-test that they could make a guess about the unknown word. This familiarization was definitely strengthened by repetition."
