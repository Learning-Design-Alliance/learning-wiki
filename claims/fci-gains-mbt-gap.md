## Evidence

### Aaron Reinhard 2022

Aaron Reinhard, Alex Felleson, Paula C. Turner, and Maxwell Green. (2022). Assessing the Impact of Metacognitive Post-Reﬂection Exercises on Problem-Solving Skillfulness. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Class-average percentage scores on the FCI, MBT, and MCAI for cohorts 1–3 (enrollment 24, 19, 24) reported in Table 1 with 95% confidence margins; pairwise deletion matched pre/post subsets. MCAI was not measured for cohort 1.

> "When converted to normalized gains (using gain = (⟨post⟩ - ⟨pre⟩)/(1- ⟨pre⟩)), all three cohorts show high FCI gains (46%, 61%, and 47%, respectively), indicative of an eﬀective active-engagement course [2, 8]. For each cohort, the MBT averages trail the FCI post-instruction averages by 15, 21, and 19 percentage points, respectively, which is in the range of typical performance when these metrics are compared [47]."
