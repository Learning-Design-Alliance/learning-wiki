### Otiato Maxwell X. 2024

Otiato Maxwell X., Ma Runzhuo, Chu Timothy N., Wong Elyssa Y., Wagner Christian, Hung Andrew J. (2024). Surgical gestures to evaluate apical dissection of robot-assisted radical prostatectomy. Journal of Robotic Surgery 18(1):245. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-01902-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Video-annotation analysis of 78 AD videos from 20 surgeons at two institutions, split by median caseload (475 cases). More experienced surgeons averaged 75.14% vs. 72.55% cold cut and 43.9% vs. 58.7% peel/push, "smaller proportions of peel/push, spread, and two-hand spread" (p < 0.05).

> "Statistical findings revealed more experienced surgeons (n = 10) used greater proportions of cold cut (p = 0.008) and smaller proportions of peel/push, spread, and two-hand spread (p < 0.05) than less experienced surgeons (n = 10)."
