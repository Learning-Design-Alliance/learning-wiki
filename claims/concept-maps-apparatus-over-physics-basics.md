# Advanced physics students in intermediate semesters concentrate concept-map terms on experimental apparatus rather than the physical basics of holography

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` University students in the advanced laboratory course added concept-map terms describing the experiment structure (laser, object, photo plate) far more than terms explaining the physical basics, and showed extensive lack of appropriate vernacular. ## Evidence

### Martin E. Horn 2003

Martin E. Horn, Helmut F. Mikelskis. (2003). Problems When Studying Holography. German Physical Society Spring Conference Dresden 2000. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0310132](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0310132)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Concept-map study with students of the advanced laboratory course at the University of Potsdam before starting the experiment; five terms were provided and students inserted at least five further central terms. Propositions such as "The virtual image appears as a hologram on the screen" appeared.

> "students concentrated on terms that described the structure of the experiment (e.g. laser, object, photo plate), compared to terms concerning the explanation of the physical basics of holography, which were used only occasionally."
