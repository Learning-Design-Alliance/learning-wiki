Tutorial evidence from Problem 23: two students argued "moment of inertia is analogous to mass, and heavier objects fall faster than lighter objects". The rehearsed answer that all objects fall together drops away in novel contexts.

> "in Problem 23, about objects rolling down a plane, some stude nts reasoned that an object with a large moment of inertia, such as a disc, rolls faster than an o bject with a small moment of inertia, such as a solid sphere."
