Descriptive trend from matched pre/post survey responses plotted in Fig. 1c. The article reports identity dropping "from the maximum 3.63 in year 1 pre to the minimum 2.98 in year 4 post" and attributes the year-4 dip to career decisions, noting only roughly 30% go on to physics graduate school.

> "Physics identity (Fig. 1c), while still largely consistent over time, shows a slight decline from the maximum 3.63 in year 1 pre to the minimum 2.98 in year 4 post (i.e., the span of undergraduate years)."
