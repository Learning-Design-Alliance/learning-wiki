### Levitan 2026

Levitan, S., & Skubel, A. (2026). Examining State Education Agency Perspectives on School Improvement Supports. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682340

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview and survey study of SEAs in 12 jurisdictions; states most commonly monitor progress via improvement plans and support tracking, but "additional work is needed to assess whether these supports are having the desired level of impact."

> "While states shared a variety of data used to monitor identified schools’ progress — most commonly via school/district improvement plans and tracking the supports schools/districts use — additional work is needed to assess whether these supports are having the desired level of impact."
