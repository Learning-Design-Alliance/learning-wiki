## Evidence

### Bakri Awaji 2020

Bakri Awaji, Ellis Solaiman and Lindsay Marshall. (2020). Investigating the Requirements for Building a Blockchain-Based Achievement Record System. [doi:10.1145/3411681.3411691](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411681.3411691)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative interviews with 6 participants (student, registration and admission staff, academic staff, HR, employer) about CV creation and review problems. Participants reported structure confusion, frequent updates, over-describing, unverifiable claimed skills, false information, and fairness and extra-effort concerns.

> "Responding to the second question, they felt that people often over-described their current qualifications and skills. Thus, the initial observation for this question is that over-describing occurs."
