### Lv Pingping 2026

Lv Pingping, Chen Jianpeng, Chen Xijing, Wang Xiao, Xu Xiangrong, Li Jingyi. (2026). Enhancing gynecological examination training for international medical students: a comparative study of peer role-play and peer assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09102-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Post-course Likert-scale (1–5) satisfaction survey administered to both cohorts; the largest effect was for the doctor-patient communication item (4.70 ± 0.46 vs. 4.24 ± 0.57, t = -5.42, Cohen's d = 0.89). The survey had a Cronbach's alpha of 0.93. The article reports "Cohen's d ranging from 0.37 to 0.89".

> "The 2020 student cohort demonstrated statistically significantly higher scores across all measured dimensions compared to the 2019 cohort (p < 0.05), with medium-to-large effect sizes (Cohen's d ranging from 0.37 to 0.89)"
