## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the end of the 2009 short course, 75% of the 12 students rated the AMSC "extremely valuable" and none rated it below "very valuable". [→ Ryan Montgomery 2010](#ryan-montgomery-2010)
`q2 i?` Seven weeks later, 54% of 11 surveyed students reported the AMSC "prepared me very well" for their internship. [→ Ryan Montgomery 2010 (2)](#ryan-montgomery-2010-2)

## Evidence

### Ryan Montgomery 2010

Ryan Montgomery, Dave Harrington, Sarah Sonnett, Mark Pitts, Isar Mostafanezhad, Mike Foley, Eddie Laag, and Lisa Hunter. (2010). The Design and Implementation of the Akamai Maui Short Course. ASP Conference Series. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.4458](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.4458)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course survey of the 2009 AMSC cohort (12 respondents). Results show "75% (9 of 12) students found the AMSC to overall be “extremelyvaluable”"; students also reported gains in problem-solving, communication, and career perspective.

> "The overall value of the AMSC was rated highly: – 75% (9 of 12) students found the AMSC to overall be “extremelyvaluable” – 25% (3 of 12) found it to be “very valuable”"

### Ryan Montgomery 2010 (2)

Ryan Montgomery, Dave Harrington, Sarah Sonnett, Mark Pitts, Isar Mostafanezhad, Mike Foley, Eddie Laag, and Lisa Hunter. (2010). The Design and Implementation of the Akamai Maui Short Course. ASP Conference Series. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1009.4458](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1009.4458)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey at the end of the eight-week Akamai program (11 respondents), seven weeks after the AMSC. "54% (6 of 11) reported that it “prepared me very well”"; cited reasons include presentation practice, teamwork, and mentor-interaction skills.

> "Students felt that the AMSC prepared them for their internship: – 54% (6 of 11) reported that it “prepared me very well” – 27% (3 of 11) felt “adequately prepared”"
