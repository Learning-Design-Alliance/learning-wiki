Wang Yun, Tian Hong, Li Jianyun, Zhou Chongzhi, Feng Yiwen, Li Jinbao, Huang Lina. (2025). Effect of flipped classroom to anesthesia crisis management simulation education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07011-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 40 anesthesiology residents randomly assigned to FC (n = 20) or CL (n = 20), rated on the ANTS system by four blinded video raters. The results section reports "significantly higher overall ANTS scores compared with the CL group" (11.95 ± 2.14 vs. 9.55 ± 2.40, p = 0.002). No effect size is printed.
