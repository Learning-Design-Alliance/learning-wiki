### Zeynep Isvan and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Zeynep Isvan and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Student Understanding of Coulomb's Law and Gauss's Law. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06369](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06369)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Matched pre-/post-test scores partitioned into three pre-test performance ranges across the five tutorials. Low-range students improved markedly, e.g., Tutorial I class 1 (n=24) from 19% pre to 77% post; Tutorial IV class 2 (n=31) from 17% to 85%.
