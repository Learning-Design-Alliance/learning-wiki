Theoretical interpretation built on the study's AHP weight results from 18 experts. The article presents the CK-versus-TPACK weight contrast as evidence of a developmental asymmetry in AI education implementation, extending TPACK theory into the credential evaluation domain.

"By quantifying the relative importance of each knowledge domain through AHP weights, this study reveals that CK (B2, weight 0.3878) currently outweighs integrated TPACK (B3, weight 0.1976) in expert perceptions, indicating a developmental asymme -try in AI education implementation"
