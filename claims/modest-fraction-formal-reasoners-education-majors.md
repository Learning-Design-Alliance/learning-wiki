## Evidence

### Nathan Moore 2011

Nathan Moore, Jacqueline R. O'Donnell. (2011). Building Thinking Skills in Pre-service Elementary School Teachers. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1110.0772](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1110.0772)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Historical local CTSR pre-test data; Table I lists Investigative Science pre-scores of 5.5 (Spring 2008), 5.9 (Spring 2009), 6.4 (Spring 2011) and a university average of 6.25, all in the transitional band. The article says these data corroborate other studies finding small percentages of students with formal reasoning.

> "Historically speaking, according to this measure only a modest fraction of students in the education program enter as formal reasoners."
