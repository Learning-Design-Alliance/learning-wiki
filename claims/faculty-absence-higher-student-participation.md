## Evidence

### Hoffman M 2014

Hoffman M, Wilkinson JE, Xu J, Wiecha J. (2014). The perceived effects of faculty presence vs. absence on small-group learning and group dynamics: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0258-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the paired quasi-experimental survey analysis (n = 114 pairs), students 'reported higher levels of participation when the faculty member was absent (p = 0.03)'; the participation-level item averaged 4.2 vs 4.3, while comfort speaking showed no difference (p = 0.49).
