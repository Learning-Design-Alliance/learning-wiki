## Evidence

### Loibl 2024

Loibl, K., & Leuders, T. (2024). Can failure be made productive also in Bayesian reasoning? A conceptual replication study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09670-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis of the problem-solving worksheets of the 38 students in the PS-I and PS-Its conditions counted solution attempts as an indicator of an effective problem-solving phase. Most students "generated only one solution attempt"; the authors contrast this with the original study's mean of 4.13 attempts (SD = 1.35).

> "Out of the 38 students, 31 students generated only one solution attempt, 6 students generated two solution attempts, and 1 student generated three solution attempts."
