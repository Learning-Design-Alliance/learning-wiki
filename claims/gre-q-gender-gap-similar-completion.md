Petersen Sandra L., Erenrich Evelyn S., Levine Dovev L., Vigoreaux Jim, Gile Krista. (2018). Multi-institutional study of GRE scores as predictors of STEM PhD degree completion: GRE gets a low mark. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206570

Two-way ANOVA with Tukey post hoc comparison of GRE Q means by gender across the four institutions (Fig 1). The discussion adds that women's GRE Q averaged 40 points lower than men's while women completed at rates similar to men (58.6% vs 62.8%, Table 1).
