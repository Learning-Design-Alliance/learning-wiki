Interview-based field scan finding from the Question 1 findings section. CBO leaders reported the partnership took shape amid deep hurt and mistrust of the school system, and described working together as a process in which they "had to heal from the institutional hurt that happened in order for us to work together." They described their work as rooted in a practice of "healing through justice."
