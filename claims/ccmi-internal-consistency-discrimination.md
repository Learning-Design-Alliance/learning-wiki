### Marcos D. Caballero 2016

Marcos D. Caballero, Leanne Doughty, Anna M. Turnbull, Rachel E. Pepper, and Steven J. Pollock. (2016). Assessing Learning Outcomes in Middle-Division Classical Mechanics: The Colorado Classical Mechanics/Math Methods Instrument. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010118)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Classical Test Theory analysis using data from five CM 1 courses at CU (N = 244) and eleven courses at nine other institutions (N = 218). Cronbach's alpha was computed treating each question part as an item because the total number of test items is small.

> "We ﬁnd that the CCMI is a highly internally reliable assessment (α = 0.83). The acceptable range for α is above 0.7, with greater than 0.8 being “good.”"
