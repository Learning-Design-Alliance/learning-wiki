### Bantider 2026

Bantider, W. H., Mulugeta, A. E., Birlie, A. T., Diriba, T., Chekol, D. B., Desse, T. T., Hunie, B. M., Dessalegn, M. K., Fasika, D. S., & Zurbachew, G. N. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In this cross-sectional census study (n = 426), the descriptive analysis by year of study found that "As the years of study increased, the mean CSAS scores progressively decreased", from 62.8 ± 9.7 in second year to 55.9 ± 7.8 among interns.
