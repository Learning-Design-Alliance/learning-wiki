## Evidence

### Monod Cécile 2013

Monod Cécile, Voekt Cora A., Gisin Martina, Gisin Stefan, Hoesli Irene M. (2013). Optimization of competency in obstetrical emergencies: a role for simulation training. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-013-3111-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of their observational study of 168 participants across six courses, of whom 52 (33.3%) reported >10 years of professional experience. The benefit statement is the authors' own reading of the self-assessment data, offered as a study strength.

> "the high proportion of experienced obstetricians and midwives participating (33.3 % >10 years professional experience), who despite significant obstetrical experience also benefited from simulation training. This result might reflect a high level of motivation and the persistence to achieve a high quality of professional practice for the experienced participants"
