### Margaret “Macke” Raymond 2022

Margaret “Macke” Raymond, Ph.D. (2022). The Pace of Recovery after COVID. Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Hoover Institution. https://www.eduref.org/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Table 2 of the sixteen-state projection reports group shares; for example, in Special Ed students reach 38.9 percent with no losses versus 71.1 for those not in Special Ed, and the authors state "the magnitude of the differences with their peers is dramatic, as much as 30 percentage points in some cases."

> "Students who receive lunch subsidies, have Special Education needs, are English Language Learners, are Black, Hispanic or Native American have smaller shares meeting the 12th Grade benchmark in both the no-loss and the 90-day loss scenarios."
