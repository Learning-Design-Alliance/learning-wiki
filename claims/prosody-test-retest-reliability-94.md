Namaziandost, E., Esfahani, F. R., & Hashemifarnia, A. (2018). The Impact of Using Authentic Videos on Prosodic Ability among Foreign Language Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 375-390. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191678

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Test-retest reliability check: recordings of ten students from another institution were rated two weeks apart and correlated with Pearson's formula, giving "94%". The article cites a satisfactory threshold of 0.50 (Stempell, 1981). This is a reliability coefficient, not an outcome effect size.

> "Through applying Pearson's formula, the pilot execution of the test demonstrated that, the correlation coefficient among learners’ ranks on both assessment occasions were computed and observed to be 94%."
