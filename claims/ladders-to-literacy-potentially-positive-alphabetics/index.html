What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). Ladders to Literacy for Kindergarten Students. WWC Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/259

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC synthesis of four studies (760 kindergarten students, more than 14 elementary schools) meeting standards with reservations. The report states "The average improvement index for alphabetics is +25 percentile points across three studies"; the printed domain average effect size for alphabetics is 0.69, and the rating was potentially positive effects.

"The average improvement index for alphabetics is +25 percentile points across three studies, with a range of +8 to +47 percentile points across findings."
