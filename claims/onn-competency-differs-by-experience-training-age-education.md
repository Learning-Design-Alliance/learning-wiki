`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests and one-way ANOVA on 65 nurses' competency scores (Results, Table 3). The quote prints "d = 1.73" for experience; training frequency ANOVA F (2, 62) = 5.82, P = .005, with Tukey post hoc differences of d = 0.74 and d = 0.89 versus once-trained nurses.

> "The largest difference was observed according to nursing experience; nurses with 5 to 10 years of experience (M = 90.15, SD = 10.30) scored significantly higher than those with <5 years of experience (M = 75.73, SD = 7.62), t = -6.01, P < .001, with a large effect size (d = 1.73)."
