## Evidence

### St James Andrew R 2026

St James Andrew R, Widener Camille. (2026). Oral examination as a tool for mastery learning in undergraduate microbiology. FEMS Microbiology Letters. https://doi.org/10.1093/femsle/fnag010

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Across-group comparison using two-sample equal variance t-tests on final exam scores in the same 20-student course. The article reports "no statistically significant difference (P > 0.05)"; equivalence was not formally tested and no effect size is printed.

> "there was no statistically significant difference (P > 0.05) between the final scores on S group questions compared to the U to S group questions, suggesting that by the end of the semester, the performance of students who initially mastered the material relative to those who took advantage of the intervention was indistinguishable."
