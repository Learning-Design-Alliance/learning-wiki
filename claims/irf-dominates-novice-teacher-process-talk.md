## Evidence

### Winston Kalman A 2013

Winston Kalman A, Van Der Vleuten Cees PM, Scherpbier Albert JJA. (2013). Remediation of at-risk medical students: theory in action. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-132

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discourse analysis of Talk Sample 6 (T4, time management, 3rd group meeting). The article reports that although some student-student interaction occurred over content, conversations about process with T1–T4 were "almost exclusively" in the IRF format, which it says does little to promote social-regulatory group dynamics.

> "This was the dominant form of discourse in the groups of less experienced teachers, with students taking turns to engage with the teacher."
