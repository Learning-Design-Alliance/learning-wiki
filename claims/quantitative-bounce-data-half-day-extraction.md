### K. Dunnett and M. H. Magnusson 2025

K. Dunnett and M. H. Magnusson. (2025). Qualitative observations in university physics laboratories: an example from classical mechanics. arXiv:2501.15988. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/adec27](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/adec27)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' own quantitative study, performed outdoors with a brick wall as the vertical scale and video recording, using air rifle pellets to reach a ≈ 2; data extraction from "5 × 15 = 75 runs took around half a day".

> "Extracting quantitative data from 5 × 15 = 75 runs took around half a day, which is likely to be unfeasibly slow within a class setting, but may be suitable for a project-style implementation where quantifying (as here) follows an initial qualitative effort."
