### Arnot Michelle 2025

Arnot Michelle, Kim Jinhee, French Michelle, Lin Sonia Y., Pashley Charlotte, Laposa Rebecca R. (2025). Students Perceive Similar Gains in Collaboration, Communication and Professional Skills in Two Distinct Experiential Learning Courses. Pharmacology Research & Perspectives. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70095

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey comparison of the two courses (103/104 PT SOC respondents; 49/49 CAP respondents) using Mann–Whitney U tests on raw Likert data with p < 0.05 as threshold, cross-confirmed in Excel and R. The article reports "no significant differences between the two courses"; equivalence was not tested.

> "There were overwhelmingly positive student responses on the Likert Scale questions in both courses, with no significant differences between the two courses."
