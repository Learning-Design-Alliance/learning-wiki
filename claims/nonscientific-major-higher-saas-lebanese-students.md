## Evidence

### Hammoudi Halat Dalal 2023

Hammoudi Halat Dalal, Hallit Souheil, Younes Samar, AlFikany Mira, Khaled Sanaa, Krayem Maha, El Khatib Sami, Rahal Mohamad. (2023). Exploring the effects of health behaviors and mental health on students’ academic achievement: a cross-sectional study on lebanese university students. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-023-16184-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1677 Lebanese university students; linear regression with SAAS as dependent variable. The article reports "non-scientific vs. scientific major (Beta = 0.53)" significant at p < 0.001 (95% CI 0.37; 0.70). No standardized effect size beyond the beta is printed.

> "The results of a linear regression taking the SAAS score as the dependent variable, showed that students who have a non-scientific vs. scientific major (Beta = 0.53), and having breakfast ≥ 4 days per week compared to less than 2 days (Beta = 0.28) were significantly associated with higher SAAS scores."
