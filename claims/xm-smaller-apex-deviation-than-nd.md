## Evidence

### Yamamoto R 2026

Yamamoto R, Nojiri T, Yoshida D, Oyamada Y, Fukutoku A, Kon K. (2026). Accuracy of dynamic computer-assisted implant surgery among novice operators using three different patient registration methods: an in-vitro study. International Journal of Implant Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40729-026-00666-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Implant-level analysis (n = 25 per group) of postoperative CBCT measurements in the in vitro d-CAIS study; the Kruskal–Wallis test was significant (H = 17.34, p = 0.00017), and "the XM group showing significantly smaller deviations than the ND group" (means 0.78 vs 1.33 mm).

> "Post hoc analysis using Dunn's test with Bonferroni correction revealed a significant difference between the XM and ND groups (adjusted p = 0.00013), with the XM group showing significantly smaller deviations than the ND group."
