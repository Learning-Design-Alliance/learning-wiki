### Nafis I. Karim 2018

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Exploring one aspect of pedagogical content knowledge of teaching assistants using the Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism. Physical Review Physics Education Research 14, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.14.010117

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative study in which 81 first-year graduate TAs in a mandatory pedagogy course predicted the most common incorrect answer on each CSEM item, compared against algebra-based student post-test data from Ref. [23]. The article reports TAs "struggled to identify many common difficulties of introductory students that persist after traditional instruction."

> "We find that the TAs were thoughtful when attempting to identify common student difficulties and they enjoyed learning about student difficulties this way. However, they struggled to identify many common difficulties of introductory students that persist after traditional instruction."
