Likert responses to the statement "I like doing calculations" in the Australian interventions: scores were below 50% for all students, with no significant change after the programme; the article reports overall that both genders in both countries do not like mathematical calculations.

> "In both interventions in Australia, both genders did not like mathematical calculations. There was no any significant change observed after the programme."
