# Participation in the simulation-based CVC training session significantly increased learner knowledge test scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learner knowledge test scores increased significantly after the simulation-based CVC placement training session (median 68% to 88%). [→ Good Ryan J. MD 2022](#good-ryan-j-md-2022)

## Evidence

### Good Ryan J. MD 2022

Good Ryan J. MD, Mashburn Danielle MPAS, PA-C, Jekich Erika MS, PA-C, Miller Kristen MS, Leroue Matthew K. MD, Woods Jason MD, Czaja Angela S. MD, MSc, PhD. (2022). Simulation-Based Training for Ultrasound-Guided Central Venous Catheter Placement in Pediatric Patients. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11276

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post evaluation of a 17-question knowledge test among a convenience sample of training-session learners. Scores rose from a "median score of 68% before" to a median of 88% after (p < .001, Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test, n = 16). No effect size was printed.

> "Learner performance on the CVC placement knowledge test increased significantly after participation, from a median score of 68% before (interquartile range [IQR]: 59%-80%) to a median score of 88% after (p < .001 by Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test, n = 16"
