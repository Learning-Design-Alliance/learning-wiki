## Evidence

### Saar 2022

Saar, M., Rodríguez-Triana, M. J., & Prieto, L. P. (2022). Towards Data-Informed Teaching Practice: A Model for Integrating Analytics with Teacher Inquiry. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 88–103. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7505

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two case studies in iteration 3: one teacher used the model for two months in 12th-form British literature classes, the other for one semester in a university TEL course, collecting engagement data via questionnaires, apps, notes, and video. The conclusion states "iteration 3 demonstrated the feasibility of the approach".

> "Both teachers found the collected data informative and useful (although their sense-making and interpreting strategies were different)."
