Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The AI feedback system returns responses in under a second for MCQs and about six seconds for open-ended questions, and its reusable prompt is roughly 2.77 times more time-efficient to prepare than equivalent educator text — related
  • LLM-generated retrieval practice was practical to deploy and well received, but high correctness rates (about 70% of items at p=1.0) restricted score variance — related
  • On the live single-node deployment, short-answer rounds take ≈32 s (≈$0.015/round) while multiple-choice rounds are ≈4× faster (≈7.6 s, ≈$0.005/round) because grading is deterministic — related
