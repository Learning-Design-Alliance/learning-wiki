Evidence¶

Reddy and Mint 2017¶

M. Vijaya Bhaskara Reddy & Phyu Phyu Mint. (2017). Impact of Simulation Based Education on Biology Student's Academic Achievement in DNA Replication. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.15. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1143890

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control-group study of 70 B.Ed/D.Ed students (35 per group). The article reports a statistically significant difference between groups (p< 0.001, t value 11.844), with means of "80.56" versus 57.67.

"The post-test results demonstrate that the average marks of the experimental graduate students of the education is 80.56 when compared to their respectiv e control group 57.65."
