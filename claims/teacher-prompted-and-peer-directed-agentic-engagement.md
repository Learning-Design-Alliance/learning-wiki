### Androni Alexandra 2024

Androni Alexandra, Fleer Joke, Smids Laura R., van der Wouden Joke M., Jaarsma A. Debbie C., Schönrock-Adema Johanna. (2024). Identifying Behaviours Representative of Agentic Engagement in Pre-clinical Medical Education PBL Groups Based on Literature and Observations. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1414

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Closer inspection of the observational data from the PBL tutor groups led the authors to differentiate agentic behaviours between student-initiated and teacher-prompted, and to distinguish unilateral from transactional contributions.

> "We observed how students engaged agentically not only by taking their own initiative, but also in response to their tutor’s encouragement to participate constructively in the learning and to thereby influence it."
