### Zambak 2017

Zambak, V. S., Alston, D. M., Marshall, J. C., & Tyminski, A. M. (2017). Convincing Science Teachers for Inquiry-Based Instruction: Guskey's Staff Development Model Revisited. Science Educator, 25(2), 108-116. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1132092

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

EQUIP observation scores for 40 middle school science teachers across five observations. The article reports that the overall mean EQUIP score was highest during the second observation, taken at the end of the first semester, but these changes were not sustainable during the second semester.

> "Figure 2 also shows that participating teachers' overall mean EQUIP score was highest during the second observation, which was taken at the end of the ﬁ rst semester. This result indicates that participating teachers demonstrated the required practices for IBI at the end of the ﬁ rst semester, but these changes in their instructional practices were not sustainable during the second semester."
