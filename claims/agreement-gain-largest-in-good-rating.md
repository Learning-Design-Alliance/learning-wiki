# Student-instructor agreement gains were concentrated at the middle of the rating scale, with the largest gain on items instructors rated "good" and low agreement on "poor" and "fair"

## Evidence

### Scott S. Douglas 2014

Scott S. Douglas, Shih-Yin Lin, John M. Aiken, Brian D. Thoms, Edwin F. Greco, Marcos D. Caballero and Michael F. Schatz. (2014). Peer Evaluation of Video Lab Reports in a Blended Introductory Physics Course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.015](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.015)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Figure 2 analysis of student ratings sorted by instructor rating across the four labs; agreement within "good" rose from a mean of 30% to 44% by Lab 4, the largest additive gain (+21.1% per Fig. 2).

> "Student-instructor agreement within "good" (mean 30%, range 23%-44%) improves from Lab 1 to Lab 4 twice as much as within any other rating, has in Lab 4 the same proportion of agreement as does "very good" (44%), and is the only instructor rating which shows a >10% gain in instructor-student agreement."

### Scott S. Douglas 2014 (2)

Scott S. Douglas, Shih-Yin Lin, John M. Aiken, Brian D. Thoms, Edwin F. Greco, Marcos D. Caballero and Michael F. Schatz. (2014). Peer Evaluation of Video Lab Reports in a Blended Introductory Physics Course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.015](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.015)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive agreement percentages within each instructor rating across labs; agreement at the low end of the scale ("poor" mean 22%, "fair" mean 23%) was barely above the 20% chance level, while "very good" averaged 38%.

> "The mean student-instructor agreement within "poor" (mean 22%, range 15%-34%) and "fair" (mean 23%, range 14%-34%) is substantially less than the mean student-instructor agreement within "very good" (mean 38%, range 32%-44%), and is indeed barely above chance (20% agreement for uniform random guessing)."
