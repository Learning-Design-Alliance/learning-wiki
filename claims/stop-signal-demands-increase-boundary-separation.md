## Evidence

### Fosco 2019

Fosco, W. D., Kofler, M. J., Alderson, R. M., Tarle, S. J., Raiker, J. S., & Sarver, D. E. (2019). Inhibitory Control and Information Processing in ADHD: Comparing the Dual Task and Performance Adjustment Hypotheses. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-018-0504-9

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian mixed ANOVA (Group x Task) in a sample of 144 children aged 8-13. The Tier 2 analysis found "boundary separation was significantly higher during the stop-signal relative to no-tone task" with d = 0.38, consistent with the performance adjustment hypothesis.

> "As shown in Figure 1, boundary separation was significantly higher during the stop-signal relative to no-tone task (BF10 = 6,221.78; d = 0.38)."
