## Evidence

### Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke 2025

Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke. (2025). Pavlovian to instrumental transfer of control over fight or flight decisions. npj Science of Learning 10:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00331-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Study 3 (final N=83 per abstract) follow-up ANOVA within the standard-instruction group, which also showed a strong specific PIT (fight M=84.6 vs flight M=28.3 for CS+A; F(1,39)=73.89, p<0.001). The threat comparison showed the effect was significantly larger under low threat, with a large printed effect size, partial eta squared ηp2 = 0.144 (ηp² = 0.144).

> "This outcome-speciﬁc PIT effect was signiﬁcantly larger when the threat level was low compared to high, F(1, 39) = 6.57,p = 0.014,ηp 2 = 0.144"

### Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke 2025 (2)

Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke. (2025). Pavlovian to instrumental transfer of control over fight or flight decisions. npj Science of Learning 10:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00331-4

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus 2 (Instruction) x 2 (Threat Level) x 2 (CS+AB) x 2 (Response) mixed ANOVA on response frequencies in Study 3. The printed result shows the four-way interaction was not significant with a negligible printed effect size, partial eta squared ηp2 = 0.002 (ηp² = 0.002); equivalence was not tested.

> "the hypothesized four-way interaction effect between CS + AB, Response, Threat Level ,a n d Instruction was not significant, F(1, 81) = 0.17, p = 0.686, ηp 2 = 0.002."
