### Srakoopun Chalinee 2025

Srakoopun Chalinee, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Haddawy Peter, Kaluschke Maximilian, Weller Rene, Yin Myat Su, Aguilar Panuroot, Phumpatrakom Panupat, Pinchamnankool Kriangkrai, Budsaba Kamon, Zachmann Gabriel. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Split-plot ANOVA on difference scores in the RCT (n = 30; 15 per group) found no significant training-method main effect on tooth volume loss, F(1, 28) = 2.19, p = 0.15; the volume loss "did not differ significantly between the VR and PH groups." Equivalent nulls for error scores and time appear in the same analysis.

> "There was no significant main effect of training method, F(1, 28) = 2.19, p = 0.15, indicating that the extent of tooth volume loss did not differ significantly between the VR and PH groups."
