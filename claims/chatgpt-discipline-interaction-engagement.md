# Disciplinary background moderates ChatGPT effects on engagement: non-STEM students were more engaged without ChatGPT, STEM students showed no difference

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` A significant interaction effect between disciplinary background and ChatGPT condition for physical engagement (F(1, 202) = 6.29, p < .05, η² = .03) and a marginal interaction for cognitive engagement (F(1, 202) = 3.37, p = 0.06, η² = .01). [→ Gaoxia Zhu 2023](#gaoxia-zhu-2023)

## Evidence

### Gaoxia Zhu 2023

Gaoxia Zhu, Xiuyi Fan, Chenyu Hou, Tianlong Zhong, Peter Seow, Annabel Chen Shen-Hsing, Preman Rajalingam, Low Kin Yew, Tan Lay Poh. (2023). Embrace Opportunities and Face Challenges: Using ChatGPT in Undergraduate Students' Collaborative Interdisciplinary Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2305.18616](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2305.18616)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Factorial ANOVA interaction tests in the Results section of the quasi-experiment with 130 STEM and non-STEM undergraduates. The condition × disciplinary background interaction was significant for physical engagement and marginal for cognitive engagement (η² = .03 and .01).

> "we found a marginal interaction effect between students' disciplinary background and ChatGPT condition for cognitive engagement (F(1, 202) = 3.37, p = 0.06, 𝜂2 = .01), and a significant interaction effect for physical engagement (F(1, 202) = 6.29, p < .05, 𝜂2 = .03)."
