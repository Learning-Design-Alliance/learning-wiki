## Evidence

### Vincent Zhang 2025

Vincent Zhang, Bryn Jeffries, Irena Koprinska. (2025). A Machine Learning Approach for Predicting Student Progress in Online Programming Education. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00510-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Feature-selection analysis across modules in the Discussion (Q3, Observation 1). The article states these results are consistent with the doer effect (Koedinger et al., 2016), which posits interactive practice is more beneficial than passive reading and video watching.

> "On average, problem slides make up 75% of features selected by the GR feature selection algorithm across modules. Students who complete problem slides, particularly later ones, are more likely to pass the last problem slide in the module, and students who do not make a submission or fail the problem slide are likely to make no submission on the ﬁnal problem slide."
