## Evidence

### Escolano-Pérez 2021

Escolano-Pérez, Elena; Bestué, Marta. (2021). Academic Achievement in Spanish Secondary School Students: The Inter-Related Role of Executive Functions, Physical Activity and Gender. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18041816

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

The Overall AA regression model in 177 students: the article reports each additional EF deficit point decreased Overall AA by 0.034 points, a one-unit PA increase raised it by 0.358 points, and being female raised it by 0.667 points, with "EF deficits (β = −0.442) had the greatest weight in Overall AA".

> "Overall AA was negative and significantly associated with EF deficits, and was positive and significantly associated with PA and gender (F(3169) = 19.65, p ≤ 0.001)."
