## Evidence

### Sparn Moritz B. 2025

Sparn Moritz B., Chatziisaak Dimitrios, Warschkow Rene, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2025). Does mental imagery make a difference in virtual reality simulation training? Results of a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy 39(10). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12012-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison of prior surgical experience collected by questionnaire in the Davos Course RCT (110 participants: 41 control, 69 MI). The authors describe this as "a numerically small but statistically significant difference in the previous experience" that could have distorted results, though likely toward underestimating the MI effect.

> "Participants in the control group had performed more cholecystectomies (8.4 (± 7.3) vs. 6.0 (± 3.0), p = 0.04) and appendicectomies (11.6 (± 13.2) vs. 7.8 (± 6.8), p = 0.038)) than those in the MI group."
