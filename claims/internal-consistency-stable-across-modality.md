## Evidence

### Monteiro S 2018

Monteiro S, Sibbald D, Coetzee K. (2018). i-Assess: Evaluating the impact of electronic data capture for OSCE. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0410-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive reliability analysis across the May, August, and January IENCAP administrations. The article reports alpha values of 0.88, 0.82, 0.84, and 0.87 across studies, with McDonald's omega reported for comparison and within-station omega ranging 0.88 to 0.98.

> "The internal consistency (alpha and omega) of all 12 stations was ≥0.8 in all 3 exams, regardless of whether the modality was paper or tablet."
