## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Mean percentage-point shift differed significantly between occasional users (M = -17.1) and regular/extensive users (M = -11.5), a small-to-medium effect (d = 0.34). [→ Roberts N 2015](#roberts-n-2015)

## Evidence

### Roberts N 2015

Roberts N, Spencer-Smith G, Vänskä R, Eskelinen S. (2015). From challenging assumptions to measuring effect: Researching the Nokia Mobile Mathematics Service in South Africa. South African Journal of Education, 35(2). https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n2a1045

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test comparing usage categories within the same 1,950-learner school-assessment dataset; the article prints "t (1948) = 7.6, p = 5.8 x 10 -14" and "d = 0.34".

> "There was a significant difference in the percentage point shifts between the group of occasional users (0-150 questions) (M = -17.1, SD = 16.1) compared to the group of regular/extensive users (151+ questions) (M = -11.5, SD = 16.7); t (1948) = 7.6, p = 5.8 x 10 -14. The magnitude of Cohen's effect size (d = 0.34) indicates that the effect of the service here was small to medium."
