Evidence¶

Swanson 2010¶

Swanson, E. A., & Vaughn, S. (2010). An Observation Study of Reading Instruction Provided to Elementary Students With Learning Disabilities in the Resource Room. Psychology in the Schools, 47(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/pits.20484

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observation results across 10 resource classrooms with class sizes ranging from 1 to 7 students (average 3.9). The results section reports "The most common grouping structure was whole-group instruction (45.8%)" across all coded instructional events.

"The most common grouping structure was whole-group instruction (45.8%), followed by individualized (27.3%), independent (19.8%), small group (4.6%), then pairing (2.6%)."
