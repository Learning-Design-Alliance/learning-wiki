### Ipinnaiye 2024

Ipinnaiye, Olubunmi; Risquez, Angelica. (2024). Exploring adaptive learning, learner-content interaction and student performance in undergraduate economics classes. Computers & Education, 215, 105047. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105047

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

OLS regression on learning analytics data from 600 first-year Business students in an Irish university. The study reports that "students who completed all five assignments (β = 46.419, p = 0.000 < 0.01) performed better in the timed tests relative to those with fewer than three completed assignments."
