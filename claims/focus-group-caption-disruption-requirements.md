## Evidence

### Fiona Draxler 2023

Fiona Draxler, Henrike Weingärtner, Maximiliane Windl, Albrecht Schmidt, and Lewis L. Chuang. (2023). Useful but Distracting: Keyword Highlights and Time-Synchronization in Captions for Language Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative online focus group with six native German speakers who discussed eight caption variants and generated ideas via 6-3-5 brainwriting. Participants "emphasized that native-language translations immediately and automatically attract attention" and feared not being able to focus on more than one thing at a time.

> "The discussion in the focus group highlighted the importance of avoiding disruptions and considering cognitive demands while catering to situation-dependent information needs."
