Think-aloud interview study of eight students testing LED power output. The article reports that "student engagement with the measurement apparatus (i.e., a photodetector and oscilloscope) included constructing models, revising models, interpreting output, and identifying limitations of the equipment."

> "Zwickl et al (2015) conducted think-aloud interviews during which eight students verbalized their reasoning while testing the power output of a light-emitting diode. One major contribution of this investigation was validation of the Modeling Framework's focus on measurement system models."
