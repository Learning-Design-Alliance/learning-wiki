### Vig 2014

Vig, R., Taylor, M. W., Star, J. R., & Chao, T. (2014). Assessing implementation fidelity: Challenges as seen through the lens of two experimental studies. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2014/annualmeeting/abstracts/view.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Contrasting Cases project, implementation fidelity was assessed three ways: video coding rubrics, a twice-yearly instructional practices survey, and a teacher log. The researchers report that none of the three types of measures clearly linked to the outcomes.

> "the three different types of fidelity measures did not differ in their predictive value of student learning outcomes"
