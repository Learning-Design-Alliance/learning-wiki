## Evidence

### Ahmet Unal 2016

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experiment, measured with the 40-item Attitude towards Web Supported Education Scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.776). The post-test difference "was found to be significant (p<.05)"; no effect size printed.

> "The mean score of the experimental group (X=144,38) was higher than the mean score of the control group (X=131,44). In order to see if this difference is significant or not, t-test was performed and the difference was found to be significant (p<.05)."
