Evidence¶

Monika Undorf 2021¶

Monika Undorf, Iris Livneh, Rakefet Ackerman. (2021). Metacognitive control processes in question answering: help seeking and withholding answers. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09259-7

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Experiment 2a one-way ANOVA across the four conditions on accuracy of submitted answers, F(3, 93) = 5.84, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.16. Follow-up t tests showed "higher in the don't-know condition, t(45) = 2.89, p = 0.006, d = 0.84" but no help-seeking benefit.

"Compared to the no-choice condition, accuracy of submitted answers was higher in the don't-know condition, t(45) = 2.89, p = 0.006, d = 0.84, and in the combination condition, t(47) = 2.29, p = 0.026, d = 0.65, but not in the help-seeking condition, t < 1."

Monika Undorf 2021 (2)¶

Monika Undorf, Iris Livneh, Rakefet Ackerman. (2021). Metacognitive control processes in question answering: help seeking and withholding answers. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09259-7

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Experiment 2b online replication with monetary incentives; the same ANOVA on submitted-answer accuracy, F(3, 173) = 4.89, p = 0.003, ηp2 = 0.78. Help seeking was only "marginally higher in the help-seeking condition, t(85) = 1.87, p = 0.064, d = 0.40".

"Compared to the no-choice condition, accuracy of submitted answers was marginally higher in the help-seeking condition, t(85) = 1.87, p = 0.064, d = 0.40, and significantly higher in the don't-know, t(83) = 3.84, p < 0.001, d = 0.84, and combination, t(79) = 2.68, p = 0.009, d = 0.60, conditions."
