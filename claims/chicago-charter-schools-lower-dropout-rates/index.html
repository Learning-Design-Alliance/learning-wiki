School-by-school comparison of TAP performance and dropout rates across CPS high school types, controlling for the characteristics of incoming students. The report found no school type especially effective on the TAP, while "Charter schools had substan- tially lower dropout rates after controlling for the characteristics of their incoming students."

"No particular type of school (neighborhood, vocational, charter or small, extended elementary, or selective admissions) was especially effective at improving students’ performance on the TAP; individual schools from each category stood out."
