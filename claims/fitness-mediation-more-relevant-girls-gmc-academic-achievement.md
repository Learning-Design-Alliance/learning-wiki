`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sex-specific serial multiple mediation analysis in a cross-sectional study of 293 girls. The article reports that "fitness mediation was more relevant in girls, especially through cardiorespiratory fitness and speed/agility, each contributing up to 20% of the effect."

> "In contrast, fitness mediation was more relevant in girls, especially through cardiorespiratory fitness and speed/agility, each contributing up to 20% of the effect. The multiple pathway was also significant in girls."
