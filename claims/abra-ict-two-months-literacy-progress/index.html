Evidence¶

McNally et al. 2016¶

Sandra McNally, Jenifer Ruiz-Valenzuela and Heather Rolfe. (2016). ABRA: Online Reading Support — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN18254678

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Three-armed randomised controlled trial in 48 analysed schools; the executive summary table reports the ICT effect size of 0.138 with confidence interval "(0.004, 0.273)", estimated at two months' progress against standard literacy provision.
