## Evidence

### Vecchione Michele 2021

Vecchione Michele, Vacca Mariacarolina. (2021). An Italian adaptation of the Child-Adolescent Perfectionism Scale: Testing measurement invariance across grade levels and exploring associations with academic achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255814

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup CFA of mean and covariance structures comparing middle and high school samples with increasingly restricted models. The article reports full metric invariance held, "five items (3 from the SPP scale) had non invariant intercepts", and the partial scalar model met the recommended fit-change criteria.

> "When scalar invariance was tested, we found that five items (3 from the SPP scale) had non invariant intercepts across grade levels. A partial invariance model, in which some invariance restrictions are relaxed, was tested and found to be statistically defensible."
