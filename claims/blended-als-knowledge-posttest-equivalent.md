## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After training, first-year resident physicians taught with mixed methodology including e-learning scored the same on a 25-question ALS knowledge test as those taught traditionally (21.84 vs 21.94 correct questions, p = 0.787). [→ Chaves Juan 2020](#chaves-juan-2020)

## Evidence

### Chaves Juan 2020

Chaves Juan, Lorca-Marín Antonio A., Delgado-Algarra Emilio José. (2020). Methodology of Specialist Physicians Training: From Traditional to e-Learning. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207681

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test–post-test study with non-equivalent control group; 52 control and 58 experimental first-year resident physicians completed the courses. The blended group scored "21.84 (SD = 2.016)" versus 21.94 in the traditional group, with no significant difference.

> "After training, group T obtained an average of 21.94 correct questions in the knowledge post-test (SD = 1.720) compared to 21.84 (SD = 2.016) (p = 0.787)."
