Evidence¶

Hämmerle 2026¶

Hämmerle, L., Kuhlmann, S. L., Krause-Wichmann, T., & Möller, A. (2026). Learning the control-of-variables strategy through self-generated and vicarious errors: the mediating roles of affect, motivation, cognitive load, and error awareness. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09822-2

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

One-way ANOVAs comparing the four conditions on PANAS positive and negative affect factor scores, assessed directly after students finished the learning materials. The study reports "a statistically significant difference between the groups in negative affect (F(3, 250) = 4.49, p < .01, η² = 0.05)" while positive affect did not differ.

"There were no statistically significant differences between the groups in positive affect (F(3, 250) = 1.096, p > .05); however, there was a statistically significant difference between the groups in negative affect (F(3, 250) = 4.49, p < .01, η² = 0.05))"
