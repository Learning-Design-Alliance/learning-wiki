`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that in Leutner et al. (2009), learning the chemistry of water molecules with a multiple-choice comprehension test, "a negative effect of drawing was found when drawing was combined with mentally imagining" as shown by a significant interaction; drawing without additional strategies gave a slight advantage.

> "a negative effect of drawing was found when drawing was combined with mentally imagining the learning contents as indicated by a significant interaction effect of these two factors."
