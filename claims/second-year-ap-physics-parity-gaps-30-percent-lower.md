`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of Table I offerings for the second-year AP physics exams (Physics 2, Physics C Mech, Physics C E&M) between Cohort 1 (n=183) and Cohort 2 (n=141), showing "wide parity gaps of 30% and lower"; totalled physics parity is 52%.

> "Table 1 details the reduced demand for these courses, resulting in a low level of offerings in cohort 2. Only a handful of these schools offer Physics-C (Mechanics), Physics-2, and Physics-C (E&M), resulting in wide parity gaps of 30% and lower."
