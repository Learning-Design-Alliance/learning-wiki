# IPI differs significantly across achievement levels and continents, with Tukey posthoc tests identifying specific pairwise contrasts

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean IPI differs significantly across Distinction, None and Normal achievement groups (F(2,19525)=11.16, p<0.0001), with Distinction-None and Normal-None pairwise differences significant. [→ Sinha 2014](#sinha-2014)

## Evidence

### Sinha 2014

Sinha, T. (2014). "Your click decides your fate": Leveraging clickstream patterns from MOOC videos to infer students' information processing & attrition behavior. B.Tech project report, School of Computing Science and Engineering, VIT University, Chennai. [doi:10.3115/v1/w14-4102](https://doi.org/10.3115/v1/w14-4102)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA over achievement bins in the full-course IPI analysis, followed by Tukey HSD posthoc tests showing "Distinction", "None" (p<0.05) and "Normal", "None" (p<0.01) pairwise differences significant.

> "Though the differences in mean values for “Distinction", “None" and “Normal" groups are significant (F(2,19525)=11.16, p<0.0001), we must be careful in interpretation"

### Sinha 2014 (2)

Sinha, T. (2014). "Your click decides your fate": Leveraging clickstream patterns from MOOC videos to infer students' information processing & attrition behavior. B.Tech project report, School of Computing Science and Engineering, VIT University, Chennai. [doi:10.3115/v1/w14-4102](https://doi.org/10.3115/v1/w14-4102)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA of IPI across continents in the full-course analysis. Tukey HSD posthoc tests reveal significant pairwise contrasts of Africas with Americas (p<0.05), Europe (p<0.01) and Oceania (p<0.05).

> "The change in IPI across continents gives an insight into how significantly (F(4,18215)=3.43, p=0.008) this operationalization varies by demographics."
