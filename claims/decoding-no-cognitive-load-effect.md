## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Haavisto 2025

Haavisto, M., Lepola, J., & Jaakkola, T. (2025). The “simple” view of learning from illustrated texts and videos. Learning and Instruction, 100, 102200. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102200

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the mixed-effects cognitive load models (e.g., CL ~ MOD * DEC, interaction B = .005, p = .957), neither decoding nor its interaction with modality was significant, contradicting Hypothesis 3's prediction that better decoders would experience less effort reading texts.

> "Decoding did not influence cognitive load when modality was controlled, nor did the interaction between modality and decoding significantly predict cognitive load."
