`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported reasons from follow-up questionnaire respondents who partially or did not implement their intended changes. The article reports "forgetting the intended changes (50.0%)" as the most frequent reason, followed by few consultations and time pressure.

> "Reasons for partially or not implementing the intended changes concerned forgetting the intended changes (50.0%), small amount of consultation for STI in daily practice (21.4%), time pressure (14.3%), shame or resistance to talk about STI (7.1%), and the costs of STI-tests (7.1%)."
