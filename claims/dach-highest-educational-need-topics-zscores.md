Descriptive Z-score analysis of global topic scores (relevance + interest − knowledge) standardized to Z-scores and quintiles for the DACH subsample (n = 291), presented in Fig. 4. Positive Z-scores indicate high relevance and interest with comparatively low knowledge; no effect size is printed.

> "Management of behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD) showed the largest positive score (Z-score = 1.70), followed by depression (1.53), chronic pain (1.26), resilience and diversity (1.19), mild cognitive impairment (MCI) and dementia: screening (0.89) and sarcopenia (0.88), all falling in the highest quintile of the Z-score distribution."
