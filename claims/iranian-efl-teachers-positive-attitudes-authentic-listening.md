### Zhafarghandi et al. 2014

Amir Mahdavi Zhafarghandi, Behzad Barekat, & Sepideh Homaei. (2014). A Survey of Iranian EFL Teachers' and Learners' Perceptions Toward Authentic Listening Materials at University Level. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.4p.184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Teacher questionnaire (30 teachers) on attitudes toward authentic listening materials; a majority reported comprehension benefits versus simplified materials, though teachers disagreed on grammar-rule familiarity (Item 15). Descriptive percentages only.

> "around 55% of the participants believed that the using authentic listening materials enhances the listening comprehension ability of the learners compared to the simplified material."
