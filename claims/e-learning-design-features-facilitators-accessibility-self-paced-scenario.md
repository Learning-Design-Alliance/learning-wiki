Scoping review synthesis of facilitators across the included studies, mapped to CFIR Innovation, Inner setting, and Individuals domains. The review lists "straightforward access, mobile-ready platforms, brief interactive scenarios" as facilitators. No effect sizes reported.

> "facilitators included design features that supported accessibility and ease of use (e.g., straightforward access, mobile-ready platforms, brief interactive scenarios) [27–30], adaptability (e.g., self-paced modular formats) [27–29], and perceived usefulness for professional roles [28–30]."
