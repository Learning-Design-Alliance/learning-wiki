## Evidence

### Sukjaitham 2026

Sukjaitham, S., Schaaf, M., Brod, G., & Breitwieser, J. (2026). Adaptivity Makes Feedback Effective: Evidence From AI-Generated Feedback on Children's Plans. Preprint. https://osf.io/4w6rz/overview?view_only=dd3ec8d2878243579a463ccd0d77d723 [doi:10.31234/osf.io/t2eac_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/t2eac_v1)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Cumulative link mixed model on ordinal plan-quality scores from the within-subjects experiment (155 children, three trials per condition). Post hoc comparisons showed no pre-support difference (p = .241) but significantly higher post-support quality after adaptive feedback.

> "Following support, plan quality was significantly higher in the LLM-generated adaptive condition than in the generic guidance condition (estimate = 1.68, SE = 0.14, p < .001)."
