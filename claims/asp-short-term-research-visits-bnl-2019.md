> "Nine ASP alumni went to Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) between July and December 2019. They were placed within different research groups and assigned research advisors according to their majors and physics interests."

The report lists career outcomes of the BNL visit participants: three completed PhDs and became post-docs, three are pursuing graduate studies, and Yves Kini's neutrino-physics mentorship collaboration with BNL researchers continued beyond the visit and led to a published paper; he received the inaugural Augustus Prince Scholar Award in August 2021.

> "After this short-term visit, Diallo Boye, Christelle Ekosso and Heba Sami Abdulraham completed their PhD and are now post-doc; Raymond Yogo, Jesutofunmi Fajemisin and Yes Kini are pursuing graduate studies."
