## Evidence

### Ahmed Dev Ahrar 2024

Ahmed Dev Ahrar, Rai Kanika, Sharma Amoldeep, Sarin Jyoti. (2024). Advancement in knowledge and skills of nursing students in operation theatre procedures with mobile based learning. Investigacion y Educacion en Enfermeria. https://doi.org/10.17533/udea.iee.v42n2e15

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of skills between 64 experimental and 64 comparison nursing students after the intervention; Table 2 reports Z-values from -9.192 (Scrubbing) to -2.882 (Assisting ET Intubation), all with p<0.001 except intubation at p=0.004. No effect size is printed.

> "the results revealed that the mean ranks of skills of students in the experimental group were significantly higher in all areas i.e., Scrubbing, Gowning, Gloving and for Assisting in ET Intubation."
