## Evidence

### Iqbal M 2016

Iqbal M, Velan GM, O’Sullivan AJ, Balasooriya C. (2016). Differential impact of student behaviours on group interaction and collaborative learning: medical students’ and tutors’ perspectives. BMC Medical Education 16(1):217. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0730-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group discussions and tutor interviews on dominant behaviour and criticism; the article reports these were "described as having a negative influence on group interaction, but also as potential triggers for learning". Qualitative perceptions only; no effect sizes.

> "Both of these aspects were described as having a negative influence on group interaction, but also as potential triggers for learning within the group."
