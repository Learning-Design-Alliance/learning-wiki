## Evidence

### Jesper Bruun 2019

Jesper Bruun, Pia J. Ray, Linda Udby. (2019). Network analyses of student engagement with online textbook problems. Preprint submitted to Computers & Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Segregation analysis of cluster attributes; hour, day, week, and year showed no evidence (Z < 1.96), but duration classes (short 5-25min, middle, long, extensive) were significantly segregated with "Z≈ 4". Group A had significant overrepresentation of short sessions; Cluster 4 of long sessions.

> "With this division into time classes, we found signiﬁcant Segregation on clusters ( Z≈ 4)."

### Jesper Bruun 2019 (2)

Jesper Bruun, Pia J. Ray, Linda Udby. (2019). Network analyses of student engagement with online textbook problems. Preprint submitted to Computers & Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantification of show/hide behavior using the parameter mu, the difference between show and hide clicks relative to total show/hide clicks. The article reports the Group A vs Group C difference as "signiﬁcantly more show-clicks without subsequent hide-clicks" with no effect size printed; Group B was intermediate with large uncertainty.

> "Sessions in Group A featured signiﬁcantly more show-clicks without subsequent hide-clicks than did Group C."
