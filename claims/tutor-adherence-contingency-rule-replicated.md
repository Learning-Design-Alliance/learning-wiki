# The replication successfully reproduced the original study's execution of contingent instruction, with tutors applying the contingency rule in 68.9% of interventions versus 70% in 1978

## Evidence

### Smit 2025

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Analysis of coded contingent-condition instruction videos (n = 69 children, four tutors); 398 of 1,278 interventions violated the contingency rule, yielding 68.9% correct versus 70% in 1978. Error-type distributions were very similar, χ2(2) = 3.92, p = .140, BF01 = 32.7 favoring no difference from the original.

> "Overall, the tutors in our replication made 398 errors out of a total of 1,278 interventions during the instruction phase in the contingent condition. This shows that tutors in the replication successfully followed the complicated directions in 68.9% of the interventions"
