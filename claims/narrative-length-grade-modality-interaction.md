### Broc Lucie 2025

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 narrative task (83 students aged 9–12, one dropped): ANOVA on words per narrative, themes and modality counterbalanced across classrooms. The quote reports the significant interaction with η² = 0.13; post-hocs showed Grade 7 typed more words (M = 80, SD = 32) than handwritten (M = 44, SD = 21).

> "The interaction between modality and grade was significant [F(3, 78) = 14.07, p < .001,  η2 = 0.13], as shown in Fig. 3."
