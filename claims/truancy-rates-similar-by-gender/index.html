Comparison of second-semester truancy rates by gender in the CPS ninth grade cohort, used as a test of the gang-recruitment hypothesis since males are more likely to be gang members or targets of harassment. The brief calls the small difference surprising.

"Forty-five per- cent of the females versus 47 percent of the males were absent more than two weeks of instruc- tional time in the second semester of ninth grade."
