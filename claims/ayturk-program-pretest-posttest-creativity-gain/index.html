Evidence¶

Ayşen Ertürkler and Hakan Bağcı 2019¶

Ayşen Ertürkler and Hakan Bağcı. (2019). The effect of enriched creative activities program supported with Aytürk technique on creativity level in music courses. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2019.3692

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest-posttest experimental study with a control group in two fourth-year classes; the experimental group of 18 students received the Aytürk-supported program for 10 weeks. The Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test found "a meaningful difference in favor of posttest" (z=3.26); no effect size was printed.

"A meaningful difference in favor of posttest was found between results of the Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test of the Creativity Pretest -Posttest scores of stud ents in the Experimental Group (z=3.26, p>0.01) (Table 5)."
