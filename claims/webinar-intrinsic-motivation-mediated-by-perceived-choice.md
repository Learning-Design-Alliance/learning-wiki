## Evidence

### Zsifkovits Monika 2025

Zsifkovits Monika, Amplatz Lukas, Triebner Nina, Utz Janine, Kornhuber Johannes, Spitzer Philipp. (2025). Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07481-4

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

IMI interest/enjoyment subscale comparison between the two randomized groups after training; the webinar group scored higher with a small effect, "Cohen's d = − 0.188, 95% CI [-0.310, − 0.066]".

> "Compared with those who completed training through the learning platform, the paramedics who participated in the webinar presented a significantly greater level of intrinsic motivation (IMI; interest) (MLP = 3.75 ± 0.90; MW = 3.91 ± 0.84; t (1042) = -3.03; p =.001, p_adj = 0.013; Cohen's d = − 0.188, 95% CI [-0.310, − 0.066]; Fig. 4A)."

### Zsifkovits Monika 2025 (2)

Zsifkovits Monika, Amplatz Lukas, Triebner Nina, Utz Janine, Kornhuber Johannes, Spitzer Philipp. (2025). Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07481-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis across the whole sample (PROCESS macro, OLS regression with bootstrapping) showed the format effect on intrinsic motivation was "fully mediated by perceived choice"; the article prints no standardized effect size for this indirect effect.

> "The relationship between learning format and intrinsic motivation was fully mediated by perceived choice, with an indirect effect (ab) of 1309 (95% CI [0.0849, 0.1809])."
