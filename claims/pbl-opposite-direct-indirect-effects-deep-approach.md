## Evidence

### Gustin Marie-Paule 2018

Gustin Marie-Paule, Abbiati Milena, Bonvin Raphael, Gerbase Margaret W., Baroffio Anne. (2018). Integrated problem-based learning versus lectures: a path analysis modelling of the relationships between educational context and learning approaches. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1489690

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis (Model 2_All) of the three-context survey decomposed the PBL-versus-lecture effect on deep approaches into direct and perception-mediated paths; the total effect (−0.03) was marked as not significant at the 5% level in Table 6. The article reports it "combined two opposite effects".

> "The PBL-based integrated curriculum had no additional effect (−0.03) on students’ use of deep learning approaches, compared to an integrated lecture-based curriculum: it combined two opposite effects, a direct negative (−0.18) and an indirect positive (0.15) effects."
