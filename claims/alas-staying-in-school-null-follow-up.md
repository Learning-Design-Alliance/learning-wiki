## Evidence

### WWC Intervention Report: ALAS 2006

WWC Intervention Report: ALAS. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.alasdropoutprevention.com

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Follow-up of the full randomized sample at the end of 11th grade. Appendix A4.1 reports a Cox effect size of 0.24, improvement index 10, marked ns (not statistically significant); equivalence was not tested.

> "Two years after the intervention had ended (the end of 11th grade), a larger fraction of ALAS students than control students were enrolled in school (75% compared with 67%), but the difference was not statistically significant."
