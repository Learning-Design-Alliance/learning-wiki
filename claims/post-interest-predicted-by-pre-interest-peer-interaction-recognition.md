### Sonja Cwik 2022

Sonja Cwik, Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Role of inclusiveness of learning environment in predicting students' outcomes in courses in which women are not underrepresented. [doi:10.33423/jhetp.v22i17.5666](https://doi.org/10.33423/jhetp.v22i17.5666)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM (lavaan, maximum likelihood) mediation analysis of the 501-student pre/post survey, controlling for gender, pre self-efficacy, and pre interest. The article prints regression coefficients b but no standardized effect size for these paths.

> "interest at the end of physics 1 was mainly predicted by interest at the beginning of the class (regression coefficient b = 0.72) with smaller effects from peer interaction (b = 0.24) and perceived recognition (b = 0.21)"
