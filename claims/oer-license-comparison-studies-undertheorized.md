## Evidence

### David A. Wiley 2021

David A. Wiley. (2021). Open educational resources: undertheorized research and untapped potential. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09907-w

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the commentary's Limitations section: the author, citing his own involvement in some reviewed articles, argues that OER studies comparing openly licensed with traditionally copyrighted materials without an explanatory mechanism explain the prevalence of "no significant differences" findings.

> "They are essentially media comparison studies or, to be more precise, license comparison studies. Without conceptualizing an explanatory mechanism—a reason to believe a difference might exist—they simply compare the outcomes of students whose required course materials are openly licensed with those whose materials are traditionally copyrighted."
