## Evidence

### Heckmann Markus B. 2025

Heckmann Markus B., Mages Christine, Finke Daniel, Reich Christoph, Haney Ailís Ceara, Riedel Caroline, Bugaj Till Johannes, Frey Norbert, Rahm Ann-Kathrin. (2025). Effects of mentoring on self-reflection and competence in Final year medical students’ internal medicine rotation. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331057

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled educational intervention with 46 final-year medical students randomized to passive or active mentoring during an 8-week internal medicine rotation; SRIS administered at baseline and end. The change "was not significantly different between the groups (p = 0.510 and p = 0.630, respectively, see Fig 3)"; equivalence was not tested.
