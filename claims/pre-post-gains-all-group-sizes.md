## Evidence

### Noerholk LM 2022

Noerholk LM, Morcke AM, Kulasegaram K, Nørgaard LN, Harmsen L, Andreasen LA, Pedersen NG, Johnsson V, Vamadevan A, Tolsgaard MG. (2022). Does group size matter during collaborative skills learning? A randomised study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14791

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Mixed-design repeated-measures anova in the randomised trial (101 completers). OSAUS rose from 37.5% to 56.7% of maximum (F(1,93) = 113.9, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.55); GRS also improved significantly (ηp2 = 0.45).

> "OSAUS scores improved significantly from pre- to post-test for all groups as did GRS scores (OSAUS mean [SD]: 37.5% [12.0)] versus 56.7% [16.8]; F(1,93) = 113.9, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.55"

### Noerholk LM 2022 (2)

Noerholk LM, Morcke AM, Kulasegaram K, Nørgaard LN, Harmsen L, Andreasen LA, Pedersen NG, Johnsson V, Vamadevan A, Tolsgaard MG. (2022). Does group size matter during collaborative skills learning? A randomised study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14791

`q4 · i1` · `causal · r2`

The same repeated-measures anova tested the group size × training interaction; it was not significant (F(3,93) = 1.04, p = 0.38, ηp2 = 0.03), indicating similar gains across group sizes.

> "There was no interaction effect between group size and training (F(3,93) = 1.04, p = 0.38, ηp2 = 0.03)"
