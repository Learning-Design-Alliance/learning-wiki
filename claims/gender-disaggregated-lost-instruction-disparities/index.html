Descriptive disaggregation of 2015-16 CRDC lost-instruction rates by race with gender at the secondary level, quoting the printed rates for Black boys, Black girls, and White girls. No inferential statistics reported.

"Black boys lost 132 days per 100 students enrolled. Black girls had the second highest rate, at 77 days per 100 students enrolled, which was seven times the rate of lost instruction experienced by White girls at the secondary level."
