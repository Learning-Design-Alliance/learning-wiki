Evidence¶

Graziano 2016¶

Graziano, P. A., & Hart, K. (2016). Beyond behavior modification: Benefits of social–emotional/self-regulation training for preschoolers with behavior problems. Journal of School Psychology, 58, 91–111. http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2016.07.004

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Linear mixed models on data from a randomized trial of 45 preschoolers with externalizing behavior problems randomized to three intervention packages showed "greater growth across time in academic achievement" for the enhanced group; the article prints no standardized effect size for this group difference.

"Children in the STP-PreK Enhanced group, however, experienced greater growth across time in academic achievement, emotion knowledge, emotion regulation, and executive functioning compared to children in the other groups."
