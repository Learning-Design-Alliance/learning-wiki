# Recent YPT participation increases perceived usefulness of regular classes and other extracurricular activities, but recent participation in other activities shows no such moderating effect

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who recently participated in YPT rated other extracurricular activities and regular physics classes as more useful than students whose YPT participation was earlier, while more recent participation in other extracurricular activities showed no comparable effect. ## Evidence

### Aneva 2022

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regressions on student survey data with no participation as baseline; printed coefficients for recent YPT participation on usefulness of other activities include Teamwork -0.609 (p = 0.007) for earlier participation, and the authors report no reverse-direction effect.

> "Our results suggest, that students who recently participated in YPT activities consider other extracurricular activities more useful than students that participated in YPT some time back."
