Coughlan C, Nafde C, Khodatars S, Jeanes AL, Habib S, Donaldson E, Besi C, Kooner GK. (2021). COVID-19: lessons for junior doctors redeployed to critical care. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2020-138100

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

This is the authors' collective experiential reflection from their own redeployment to a COVID-19 ICU at an urban teaching hospital, not a measured study; the article reports no effect size, comparison or outcome evaluation for this assertion.

> "However, ICU is a rich learning environment and with support, redeployed junior doctors can become more confident in managing unwell patients and performing advanced practical procedures including arterial and central line insertion."
