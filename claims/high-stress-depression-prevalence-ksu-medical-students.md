Alzahrani AM, Hakami A, AlHadi A, Batais MA, Alrasheed AA, Almigbal TH. (2020). The interplay between mindfulness, depression, stress and academic performance in medical students: A Saudi perspective. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231088. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231088

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the stratified random sample of 289 KSU medical students (85% response rate), reporting that "Moderate and severe levels of stress were seen in 65.1% and 15.2%" of participants; these are descriptive percentages with no effect-size test.

> "Moderate and severe levels of stress were seen in 65.1% and 15.2% of the participants, respectively. The percentages of moderate, moderately severe, and severe depression were 20.4%, 9.3%, and 9%, respectively."
