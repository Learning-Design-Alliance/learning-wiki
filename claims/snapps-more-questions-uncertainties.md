## Evidence

### Fagundes E. D. T. 2020

Fagundes E. D. T., Ibiapina C. C., Alvim C. G., Fernandes R. A. F., Carvalho-Filho M. A., Brand P. L. P. (2020). Case presentation methods: a randomized controlled trial of the one-minute preceptor versus SNAPPS in a controlled setting. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00588-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial, mean questions and uncertainties were 1.6 (SD 0.8) SNAPPS vs 0.5 (0.8) OMP for simple cases and 1.7 (0.9) vs 0.9 (1.0) for complex cases, both p < 0.001. This replicates the only previous SNAPPS-OMP comparison.

> "Students in the SNAPPS group expressed significantly more questions and uncertainties in both simple and complex cases than students in the OMP group (Tab. 1)."
