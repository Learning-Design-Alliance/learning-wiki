## Evidence

### Farooq et al. 2012

Muhammad U. Farooq, AbdulRahman Al Asmari & Choudhary Z. Javid. (2012). A Study of Online English Language Teacher Education Programmes in Distance Education Context in Pakistan. English Language Teaching, 5(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n11p91

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on Course 553 pre- and post-test marks of the 18-student control group. The article reports "the t-value of comparison wa s 10.34", exceeding the table value of 3.965, indicating a significant gain without the online treatment.

> "It was observed that the t-value of comparison wa s 10.34 at the probability level of 0.05 and the degree of freedom was 17. And the t-value was greater than the table valu e which was 3.965. It shows the difference between the marks of pre-test and post-test was significant."

### Farooq et al. 2012 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
