## Evidence

### Martín-Coca A 2025

Martín-Coca A, Martín Herrero JA. (2025). Training program on motivational interviewing for prison professionals. Revista Española de Sanidad Penitenciaria. https://doi.org/10.18176/resp.00108

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis in the quasi-experimental study of 25 prison professionals; means rose from 4.01 to 4.83, with the printed effect size η² = 0.169 (rendered "h2 = 0.169" in the English text). The authors interpret this as participants becoming better at recognizing their own MI-incompatible practices, not as more incompatible behaviour.

> "the incompatible competency with MI presented a significant increase in the ANOVA analysis (F = 4.883, p = 0.012, h2 = 0.169, observed power = 0.779) and in the Friedman test (c2 = 9.711, p = 0.008), which suggests that participants identified more practices unaligned with MI as the training progressed"
