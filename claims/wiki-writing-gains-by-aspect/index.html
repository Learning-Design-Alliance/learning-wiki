Kassem 2017¶

Mohamed Ali Mohamed Kassem. (2017). Developing Business Writing Skills and Reducing Writing Anxiety of EFL Learners through Wikis. English Language Teaching, 10(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n3p151

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Table 2 reports independent-samples t-tests per TBWS aspect, each significant at p = 0.01, comparing the 30-student control and experimental groups on form, organization, content and mechanics.

"The mean score of each item on the experimental group students' post- test is higher than that of the control group (M= 3.63 > 2.07 for form; M= 3.10 > 1.97 for organization; M = 2.67 > 1.73 for content; M = 2.53 > 1.67 for mechanics)."
