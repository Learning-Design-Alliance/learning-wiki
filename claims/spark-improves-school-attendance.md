## Evidence

### Curtis J Jones 2016

Curtis J Jones, Michael Christian, Andrew Rice. (2016). The Results of a Randomized Control Trial Evaluation of the SPARK Literacy Program. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567484

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Attendance was measured as total absences for the 2013-14 and 2014-2015 school years, collected by MPS, and analyzed with the same grade-specific generalized linear models pooled across grades in the RCT. Table 10 reports the overall effect of 5.8 absences with "an effect size (Hedge's g) of .25".

> "Finally, SPARK was found to also significantly impact school attendance. The overall, unstandardized, effect of SPARK was 5.8 absences (Table 10), indicating that SPARK students had 5.8 fewer absences than control students. This corresponds to an effect size (Hedge's g) of .25."
