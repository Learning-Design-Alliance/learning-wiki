# The percentage of questions answered correctly increased from 46.6% pre-intervention to 60% post-intervention (p=0.02)

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Across the five pooled intern groups, the percentage of questions answered correctly increased from 46.6% to 60%, a statistically significant change by paired t-test. [→ Wyssusek K 2021](#wyssusek-k-2021)

`q2 i?` Interns' mean pre-intervention score was 7/15 (SD 2.73) and mean post-intervention score was 9/15 (SD 2.99), a statistically significant improvement (p=0.02). [→ Wyssusek K 2021](#wyssusek-k-2021)
