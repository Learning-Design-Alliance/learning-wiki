## Evidence

### Claude Müller 2018

Claude Müller, Michael Stahl, Mark Alder, Maximilian Müller. (2018). Learning Effectiveness and Students' Perceptions in a Flexible Learning Course. European Journal of Open, Distance and e-Learning, 21(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1208426

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A two-sample test for equivalence (following Meyners, 2012; Wellek, 2010) was applied to the same 2014 IBA final exam data because the project's goal was equivalence, not superiority. The test statistic T = 1.64 fell below the quantile 3.83, supporting statistical equivalence of the groups' results.

> "The additional equivalence test shows a value of the test statistics T  (1.64) lower than the corresponding quantile of the noncentral Fisher distribution (3.83). Thus, the H 0 hypothesis can be rejected, and the exam results for the FLEX and the CONV groups can be regarded as statistically equivalent."
