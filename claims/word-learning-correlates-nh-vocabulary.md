# Word-learning performance correlates with vocabulary production in normal-hearing children but not with sentence length

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Partial correlations controlling for age showed word-learning performance correlated significantly with word production (r = .77) and irregular word production (r = .46), but not with sentence length (r = .07). [→ Derek M. Houston 2012](#derek-m-houston-2012)

## Evidence

### Derek M. Houston 2012

Derek M. Houston, Jessica Stewart, Aaron Moberly, George Hollich, and Richard T. Miyamoto. (2012). Word learning in deaf children with cochlear implants: effects of early auditory experience. Dev Sci. 2012 May ; 15(3): 448–461. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2012.01140.x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlations (controlling for age) between raw MCDI scores at time of testing and mean looking time differences in NH age-matched controls. Word production correlated at r = .77 and irregular word production at r = .46, both p < .05.

> "Partial correlations, controlling for age, revealed that performance on the word-learning task correlated significantly with word production (r = .77, p < .05) and irregular word production (r = .46, p < .05)."
