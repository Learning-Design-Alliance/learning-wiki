## Evidence

### Dutra 2026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot evaluation with 54 ninth-grade students (14–15 years old) in a 2-hour session using the MEEGA+ questionnaire; the article reports "92% of the responses were positive regarding design and aesthetics", 88% favorable on challenge, 85% on perceived learning, and more than 70% on social interaction. No effect sizes or inferential tests are printed.
