## Evidence

### Stingone JA 2026

Stingone JA, McVeigh KH, Lednyak L. (2026). Early Intervention Developmental Programming and Childhood Academic Outcomes. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.55890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup analyses within the matched LSED cohort replicating the full pipeline per stratum. For children of Latina mothers born outside the US, estimates were math 0.093 (0.049-0.136) and ELA 0.108 (0.065-0.150). The article also reports greater benefits for children whose mothers had Medicaid or lower educational attainment, while benefits were absent among children of college-educated mothers.

> "Children of Latina mothers, especially those born to mothers outside the US, also had associations between EI and math (estimate, 0.057; 95% CI, 0.024-0.091) and ELA (estimate, 0.073; 95% CI, 0.038-0.107) test scores and meeting test-based standards (incidence ratios, 1.13 [95% CI, 1.10-1.16] and 1.15 [95% CI, 1.10-1.21], respectively)."
