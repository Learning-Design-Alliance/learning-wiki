# Printed-text students' checklist performance, global assessment and post-test scores rose substantially from pre-test to post-test after CAL exposure in one single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After the printed-text group used the software and was retested, checklist performance improved on every item (checklist total 9.04±1.77 vs 4.08±4, p<0.001). [→ de Sena David P. 2013](#de-sena-david-p-2013)
`q3 i?` The same group's global assessment total rose from 22.68±10.53 to 35.92±6.51 (p = 0.0001) and post-test total from 3.32±0.99 to 4.72±0.46 (p<0.001). [→ de Sena David P. 2013 (2)](#de-sena-david-p-2013-2)

## Evidence

### de Sena David P. 2013

de Sena David P., Fabricio Daniela D., Lopes Maria Helena I., da Silva Vinicius D. (2013). Computer-Assisted Teaching of Skin Flap Surgery: Validation of a Mobile Platform Software for Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0065833

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched within-group comparison (McNemar and Wilcoxon tests) of the 25 printed-text students before and after five minutes of CAL exposure; Table 2 reports the checklist total rising from 4.08±4 to 9.04±1.77 (p<0.001).

> "Improvement in overall performance for each item was observed in the second evaluation, as shown in Table 2."

### de Sena David P. 2013 (2)

de Sena David P., Fabricio Daniela D., Lopes Maria Helena I., da Silva Vinicius D. (2013). Computer-Assisted Teaching of Skin Flap Surgery: Validation of a Mobile Platform Software for Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0065833

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same matched reevaluation of the printed-text group after CAL exposure: global assessment total 22.68±10.53 to 35.92±6.51 (p = 0.0001) and post-test total 3.32±0.99 to 4.72±0.46 (p<0.001), per Table 2.

> "Avg_T	22.68±10.53	35.92±6.51	0.0001"
