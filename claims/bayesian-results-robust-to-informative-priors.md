Sensitivity analysis refitting the unpooled Bayesian one-way ANOVA models with a truncated normal distribution (limits −2 to 2) instead of objective priors. Posterior estimates were nearly identical to the main analysis, indicating robustness despite the small in-group sample size.

> "This shift had virtually no impact on the reported outcomes for ﬁnancial knowledge (explicitﬁnancial education pM: 0.686; pSD 0.3, Stra- tegic Thinking pM:−0.251; pSD: 0.289, Probability pM:−0.44; pSD: 0.303)"
