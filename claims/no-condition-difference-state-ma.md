## Evidence

### Rief 2026

Rief, V., Hladký, M., Yoo, M., Heel, S., Sato, S., Nagashima, T. (2026). Beyond Problem Solving: Large Language Models for Emotional and Reflective Support in Mathematics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02611

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effect model on anticipatory state-MA (STAI-6 administered before pre/posttest) in the 42-student classroom experiment found no significant group, time, or interaction effects; equivalence was not tested.

> "A linear mixed-effect model revealed no statistically significant effects of group, time, and interaction between groups and time for anticipatory state-MA."

### Rief 2026 (2)

Rief, V., Hladký, M., Yoo, M., Heel, S., Sato, S., Nagashima, T. (2026). Beyond Problem Solving: Large Language Models for Emotional and Reflective Support in Mathematics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02611

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Summary of the state-MA mixed-effect models in the classroom experiment: the Mindful condition showed no significant advantage over the Cognitive condition on either state-MA type; no effect size was printed for the between-group contrasts.

> "Thus, the Mindful ITS version did not significantly change the anticipatory or executive state-MA when compared against the Cognitive ITS version without the mindfulness interventions."
