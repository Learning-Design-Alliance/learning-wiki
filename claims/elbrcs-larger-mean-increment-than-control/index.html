Descriptive comparison in Table 2 (N = 45 per group): experimental means 53.26 (SD 6.4201) pre and 59.63 (SD 9.1305) post; control means 54.14 (SD 8.5025) pre and 55.34 (SD 8.9470) post. The article states the "experimental group show cases more increment than that of the control group".

"From the mean score table (Table 2), it is found that the experimental group show cases more increment than that of the control group. The increment can be associated to the affects of the different interventions received by both groups."
