### Squire 1912

Squire, C. R. (1912). Graded Mental Tests. Part I. Attention, Perception, Comprehension and Memory. The Journal of Educational Psychology, III(7). https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Perception-time was computed from the a-test by subtracting motor-time from total time, with time added back for omissions. The author notes "a fair degree of correspondence between perception-time and age" and the same eighth- and twelfth-year gains seen in attention.

> "There is, however, a fair degree of correspondence between perception-time and age. The gain made in the eighth over the seventh and in the twelfth over the eleventh year is again noticeable."
