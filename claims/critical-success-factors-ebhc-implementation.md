## Evidence

### Young Taryn 2015

Young Taryn, Rohwer Anke, van Schalkwyk Susan, Volmink Jimmy, Clarke Mike. (2015). Patience, Persistence and Pragmatism: Experiences and Lessons Learnt from the Implementation of Clinically Integrated Teaching and Learning of Evidence-Based Health Care – A Qualitative Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0131121

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis across interviews with 24 coordinators; Table 7 lists critical success factors including being pragmatic, patience and persistence, needing the 'right' teachers, and leadership acknowledgment. Institutions with postgraduate programmes in clinical epidemiology described building a critical mass of graduates leading EBHC teaching.

> "Critical success factors were pragmatism and readiness to use opportunities for engagement and including EBHC learning in the curriculum; patience; and a critical mass of the right teachers who have EBHC knowledge and skills and are confident in facilitating learning."
