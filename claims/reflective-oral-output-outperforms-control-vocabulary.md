## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Reflective learners receiving oral output-based instruction scored significantly higher on the vocabulary posttest than control learners (r = .596, a large effect size by the article's description). [→ Behnaz Rastegar & Fatemeh Safari 2017](#behnaz-rastegar-fatemeh-safari-2017)

## Evidence

### Behnaz Rastegar & Fatemeh Safari 2017

Behnaz Rastegar & Fatemeh Safari. (2017). Output-Based Instruction, Learning Styles and Vocabulary Learning in the EFL Context of Iran. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 5(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.5n.2p.101

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparing the reflective oral output group (M = 22.38, SD = 1.59, N = 21) with the control group (M = 19.88, SD = 1.73, N = 32) after 15 sessions of intervention. The article reports "r = .596 representing a large effect size" and rejects the first null hypothesis.

> "The results of the independent t-test (t (51) = 5.30, p = .000, r = .596 representing a large effect size) (Table 9) indicate that the reflective oral output group had significantly outperformed the control group on the posttest of vocabulary."
