### Iordanou 2022

Iordanou, K. (2022). Supporting strategic and meta-strategic development of argument skill: the role of reflection. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 399–425. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09289-1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the 10-item immigration knowledge test (KR-20 = 0.62) administered before and after the intervention to 19 students per condition. The interaction "F(1, 36)  = 4.167, p = 0.049, ηp2 = 0.104" favored A&R (final M = 6.316 vs 5.158).
