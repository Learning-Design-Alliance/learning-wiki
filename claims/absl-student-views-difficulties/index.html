Evidence¶

Akbaş 2019¶

Akbaş, Y.; Şahin, İ. F.; Meral, E. (2019). Implementing Argumentation-Based Science Learning Approach in Social Studies: Academic Achievement and Students' Views. Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 9(1), 209-245. https://doi.org/10.33403/rigeo.529139

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Content analysis of interviews about negative sides of the implementation (Table 10): having problems in discussion process (f=6), difficulties due to inexperience at the beginning (f=3), and one student reporting no negativity.

"Students emphasized that they had problems, especially in the discussion process. While the students stated that they experienced difficulties due to their inexperience at the beginning of the implementation process, one student indicated that she/he had no negativity."

Akbaş 2019 (2)¶

Akbaş, Y.; Şahin, İ. F.; Meral, E. (2019). Implementing Argumentation-Based Science Learning Approach in Social Studies: Academic Achievement and Students' Views. Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 9(1), 209-245. https://doi.org/10.33403/rigeo.529139

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview question on difficulties experienced (Table 15): difficulties in creating arguments for concept cartoons (f=4) and a migration story (f=1); three students stated they experienced no difficulties.

"Table 15 shows that students had difficulties in a migration story and the concept cartoons activities. Five students identified that they had difficulties in creating arguments during these activities and two students did not give any reasons why they had difficulties."
