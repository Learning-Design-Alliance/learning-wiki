### Chorianopoulos 2018

Chorianopoulos, K. (2018). A Taxonomy of Asynchronous Instructional Video Styles. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 19(1). https://www.irrodl.org/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis of prior instructional-video studies (Table 1). The review reports that studies used overlapping or inconsistent terms such as talking head and voice-over, so "the production style of instructional videos has been classified in many different, or overlapping categories, which makes it difficult to compare across studies, or to perform meta-analyses."

> "previous research on the production style of instructional video has provided evaluations of particular video formats, but it has not done so in the context of a consistent taxonomy"
