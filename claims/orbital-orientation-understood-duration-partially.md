Review of 23 Essay 1 submissions on transits and occultations; the author reports "91% of students (21 of 23)" understood the orbit-orientation effect, with 55% of those addressing duration correct (6 of 11).

> "91% of students (21 of 23) were able to easily understand how the orientation of the orbit affects whether a transit occurs or not (see Figure 2 and goal IIe), though they did not all address how the transit duration would change. 55% of those who did were correct (6 of 11)."
