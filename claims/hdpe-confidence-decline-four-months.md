## Evidence

### Kelly J 2020

Kelly J, Oza SK, Feinn R, Cassese T. (2020). Implementation of a Hypothesis-Driven Physical Exam Session in a Transition to Clerkship Program. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11043

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Four-month follow-up survey completed by 150 students measuring perceived session effectiveness and current confidence on a four-point scale. The article reports confidence was "uniformly lower" than both earlier measurements, with no effect size printed.

> "Student confidence was uniformly lower at the 4-month follow-up than both pre- and postsession confidence levels as measured immediately after the session. On all items, a smaller percentage of students reported feeling moderately or very confident in each skill, with the greatest changes seen in having an organized approach to a patient with irregular menses or fatigue (Table 1)."
