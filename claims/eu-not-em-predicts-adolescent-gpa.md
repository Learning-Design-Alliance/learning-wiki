## Evidence

### Jolić Marjanović Zorana 2021

Jolić Marjanović Zorana, Altaras Dimitrijević Ana, Protić Sonja, Mestre José M. (2021). The Role of Strategic Emotional Intelligence in Predicting Adolescents’ Academic Achievement: Possible Interplays with Verbal Intelligence and Personality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413166

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression variant in the same 227-student sample replacing overall strategic EI with its branches; "only EU emerged as a significant predictor (β = 0.16, p < 0.05)", accounting for an additional 2% of GPA variance.

> "When SEI was replaced by EU and EM in the last step, the results for the overall model were practically the same (F8218 = 10.83, p < 0.001, 26% of the variance explained), but of the two branches, only EU emerged as a significant predictor (β = 0.16, p < 0.05)"

### Jolić Marjanović Zorana 2021 (2)

Jolić Marjanović Zorana, Altaras Dimitrijević Ana, Protić Sonja, Mestre José M. (2021). The Role of Strategic Emotional Intelligence in Predicting Adolescents’ Academic Achievement: Possible Interplays with Verbal Intelligence and Personality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413166

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Branch-level direct effects structural equation model in the same sample; the EM→GPA coefficient was nonsignificant while EU predicted GPA independently. Model fit was acceptable (χ2(6) = 12.456, p = 0.053).

> "Another important result was that the standardized regression coefficient for the EM→GPA also failed to reach a statistically significant value. On the other hand, the effect of EU on GPA was significant and independent of VI, C, and O."
