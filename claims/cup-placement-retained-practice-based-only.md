### Marschalek Julian 2021

Marschalek Julian, Kuessel Lorenz, Stammler-Safar Maria, Kiss Herbert, Ott Johannes, Husslein Heinrich. (2021). Comparison of a practice-based versus theory-based training program for conducting vacuum-assisted deliveries: a randomized-controlled trial. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-021-06159-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Long-term analysis of the RCT comparing baseline to second post-training test: practice-group distance-to-flexion-point baseline-vs-second p = 0.000 versus theory-group p = 0.262. The article notes cup placement over the flexion point is "a crucial factor for a successful VAD".

> "In the practice-based training group, global and specific OSATS scores, as well as distance of cup application to the flexion point were still significantly improved compared to baseline test, whereas in the theory-based practice group, only the specific OSATS scores were still better compared to the baseline test (Table 2)."
