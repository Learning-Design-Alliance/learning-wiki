`q2 i?` After initial training, first-exam scores of the traditional method group (13.6%) and the concept map method group (15.2%) did not differ significantly by t-test. [→ Krishan 2017](#krishan-2017)

## Evidence

### Krishan 2017

Tamara Mohd Altabieri Krishan. (2017). Concept Map Technique as a New Method for Whole Text Translation. English Language Teaching, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n4p62

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-hour post test for Group 1 (traditional method) and Group 2 (concept map method), 40 students each, compared by t-test (Table 2). The article reports the difference "was not significant" with means of 13.6% and 15.2%; no effect size is printed.

> "The difference between the two groups (traditional and concept map method was not significant, and the mean scores were 13.6% and 15.2% respectively."
