Evidence¶

Pollock 2000¶

Pollock, Edwina Jane. (2000). Assimilating Complex Information. PhD thesis, School of Education, University of New South Wales. https://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/17813

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The thesis reports five experiments generated by cognitive load theory comparing a two-phase isolated-interacting elements approach with a conventional control. The abstract states that "Experiments 1 and 3 demonstrated that when novice student s are required to learn complex information" the isolated-interacting method was superior. No effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.
