### Victoria Borish and H. J. Lewandowski 2024

Victoria Borish and H. J. Lewandowski. (2024). Student reasoning about quantum mechanics while working with physical experiments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of think-aloud lab sessions of two pairs of students in the third lab session (single-photon interferometer), based on transcripts and post-interviews. The authors report that "Both groups used their prior knowledge about classical wave interference" at the start and during the quantum eraser.

> "Both groups used their prior knowledge about classical wave interference when first reasoning through the existence of interference and later explaining the quantum eraser."
