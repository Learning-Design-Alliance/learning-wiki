Regression analysis of cohort 2 graduates (N = 65) showed a significant negative MCAT–publication association, "the correlation was negative (β = −0.25, p = 0.02)"; the Step II association (β = 1.36, p = 0.04) is reported in the same Results section. No standardized effect size was printed.
