### Graves 1911

Graves, S. Monroe. (1911). A Study in Handwriting. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeduapsych

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Average deviation from the median words written, computed separately for the one-minute and five-minute tests; back-hand deviated 5.107 in one minute and 20.7200 in five minutes, the largest of the five styles.

> "1. Back-hand, by the average deviations, is shown to be the least dependable, the least stable in both tests. 2. Medium slant has the least deviation in the one-minute test and is second in the five-minute test."
