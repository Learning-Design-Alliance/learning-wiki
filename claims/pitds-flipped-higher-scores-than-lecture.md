# Dental undergraduates taught with the P-I-T-D-S flipped classroom model scored significantly higher on the Dental Exam, Test, and Total Score than those taught by traditional lecture

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 2023 flipped cohort scored significantly higher than the 2022 traditional cohort on Dental Exam (p = 0.003), Test (p < 0.001), and Total Score (p < 0.001). [→ Ba Ruikai 2026](#ba-ruikai-2026)

## Evidence

### Ba Ruikai 2026

Ba Ruikai, Feng Xinran, Yuan Lixian, Tong Juan, Ban Jinghao, Wu Meiling, Han Bing, Xu Xinyue, Qiu Xinyu, Guo Hao, Xuan Kun. (2026). A novel extended flipped classroom model helps dental undergraduates grow into dentists. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08525-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort comparison of 2023 flipped (N = 39) versus 2022 traditional lecture (N = 38) sophomores in Fundamentals of Stomatology, using Wilcoxon rank-sum tests on median [IQR] scores. The "Test score significantly improved" and Total Score rose from 76.3 to 81.9 (p < 0.001); Cavity Preparation was not significant (p = 0.306).

> "The Dental Exam score in the flipped classroom group was 90.0 [89.0–100.0], compared to 88.0 [83.0–93.0] in the traditional group (p = 0.003). Similarly, the Test score significantly improved from 76.0 [76.0–82.0] in the traditional model to 84.0 [79.0–89.5] in the flipped model (p < 0.001)."
