Pautz Stephenson, S., Bradford, B.C., Wortman, A., Robertson, C., Sorrells, C., & Basu Mallick, D. (2026, May). Educator perspectives on research using digital learning platforms. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/295

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive survey results on adequacy of privacy measures (Figure 7). The report prints N = 10 K-12 educators unaware of the measures and n = 6 higher education educators saying existing measures were adequate.

"more K-12 educators were unaware of the measures being taken (N = 10) compared to higher education educators, who were most likely to say that the existing measures were adequate (n = 6)."
