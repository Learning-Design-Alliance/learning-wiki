`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Ozgonul (2017) compared TBL and lecture-based instruction in a medical ethics clerkship and reported that "TBL groups outperformed lecture groups in follow-up exams at 1 year (8.38/10 vs. 7.13/10) and 2 years (8.16/10 vs. 6.62/10, p < 0.001), indicating superior retention". No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.
