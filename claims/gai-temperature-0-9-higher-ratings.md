# Higher GAI temperature setting (0.9) was associated with higher student ratings of virtual simulated patients, with a small effect

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Temperature settings significantly influenced student ratings, with a small effect (η²=0.02). [→ Daniel García-Torres 2025](#daniel-garcia-torres-2025)
`q2 i?` Post hoc comparisons showed higher ratings at temperature 0.9 than at 0.1 (Dunn's test, P=.04), with no significant differences between 0.1 and 0.5 or 0.5 and 0.9. [→ Daniel García-Torres 2025 (2)](#daniel-garcia-torres-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Daniel García-Torres 2025

Daniel García-Torres, César Fernández, José Joaquín Mira, Alexandra Morales, María Asunción Vicente. (2025). Using AI-Based Virtual Simulated Patients for Training in Psychopathological Interviewing: Cross-Sectional Observational Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78857

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Nonparametric analysis of ratings across temperature settings 0.1, 0.5, and 0.9 (Kruskal-Wallis H=6.93; P=.03); the study reports the effect size was small, "η²=0.02", with temperature explaining "only about 2% of the variance in ratings."

> "The effect size was small (η²=0.02, 95% CI –0.00 to 0.07), suggesting that temperature explained only about 2% of the variance in ratings."

### Daniel García-Torres 2025 (2)

Daniel García-Torres, César Fernández, José Joaquín Mira, Alexandra Morales, María Asunción Vicente. (2025). Using AI-Based Virtual Simulated Patients for Training in Psychopathological Interviewing: Cross-Sectional Observational Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/78857

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Dunn's post hoc test with Holm correction following the Kruskal-Wallis test on ratings by temperature level; "a significant difference was found between 0.1 and 0.9 (P=.04)" favoring temperature 0.9. No effect size printed for this pairwise contrast.

> "However, a significant difference was found between 0.1 and 0.9 (P=.04), suggesting that higher ratings were associated with the 0.9 temperature condition"
