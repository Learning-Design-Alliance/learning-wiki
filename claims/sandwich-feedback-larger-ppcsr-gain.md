## Evidence

### Hosseinpour Azam 2024

Hosseinpour Azam, Nasiri Morteza, Keshmiri Fatemeh, Arabzadeh Tayebeh Tarabzadeh, Sharafi Hossein. (2024). Comparison of the effects of apprenticeship training by sandwich feedback and traditional methods on final-semester operating room technology students' perioperative competence and performance: a randomized, controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05598-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT (n = 30), the mean change in PPCS-R total from pre-test to post-test was 29.80 ± 4.53 in the FBL group versus 16.73 ± 5.59 in the TBT group, with a printed Cohen's d of 2.56. The article reports the change "was significantly higher in the FBL group compared to the TBT group (P < 0.001)".

> "Also, the mean change of the PPCS-R total score from the baseline to the post-test was significantly higher in the FBL group compared to the TBT group (P < 0.001)."
