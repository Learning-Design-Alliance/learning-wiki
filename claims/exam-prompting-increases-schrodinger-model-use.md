## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Removing a second exam question that required the Schrödinger model dropped the percentage of students using it from 72% to 60% (p = 0.01). [→ S. B. McKagan 2007](#s-b-mckagan-2007)

## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Why we should teach the Bohr model and how to teach it effectively. https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1541 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Comparison of final-exam responses between the Fa05 and Sp06 semesters of the same course. The second part of the question required the Schrödinger model; the authors report the percentage "dropped signiﬁcantly ( p = 0.01) from 72% to 60%" and attribute the drop to prompting.

> "after removing the second part of the exam question in Sp06, the percentage using the Schrodinger model dropped signiﬁcantly ( p = 0.01) from 72% to 60%."
