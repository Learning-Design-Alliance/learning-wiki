## Evidence

### Şen Olgay Sevim 2025

Şen Olgay Sevim, Çağan Kişin Şevval. (2025). Validating the Turkish version of the team-based learning assessment scale: a psychometric evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06893-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Test–retest reliability analysis with the scale readministered to 165 students 2 weeks later. The article reports ICCs "between 0.88 and 0.90", test–retest correlations of r = 0.845 to 0.896, and no significant difference between test and retest measurements (p > 0.05).

> "The ICCs for the scales and dimensions were found to be between 0.88 and 0.90."
