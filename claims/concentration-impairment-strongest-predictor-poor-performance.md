## Evidence

### Giusti Laura 2021

Giusti Laura, Mammarella Silvia, Salza Anna, Del Vecchio Sasha, Ussorio Donatella, Casacchia Massimo, Roncone Rita. (2021). Predictors of academic performance during the covid-19 outbreak: impact of distance education on mental health, social cognition and memory abilities in an Italian university student sample. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-021-00649-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Step 3 of a logistic regression predicting poor vs high self-assessed academic performance in 203 surveyed students; the table prints O.R. 8.350 (p = 0.014) for "Learning concentration impairment during distance education". The article reports a statistically significant increase in odds with no printed effect size.

> "In the third step, learning concentration impairment during DE showed significant predictive power, and the likelihood of students’ self-evaluation of their performance as poor increased by more than 8 times."
