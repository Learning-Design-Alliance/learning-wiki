## Evidence

### Anggoro 2012

Anggoro, F. K., Stein, N. L., & Jee, B. D. (2012). Cognitive factors that influence children's learning from a multimedia science lesson. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 93-108. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068621

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A 2x2x2 between-groups ANCOVA on pre-post gain scores for molecular properties of water, controlling for PPVT vocabulary scores. The article reports a main effect of reading condition with "ηp2 = .14", showing tutor-read participants gained more than self-read participants.

> "There was also a main effect of Reading Condition F(1, 279) = 46.72, MSE = .31, p < .05, ηp2 = .14, such that participants in the Tutor-Read condition had greater gain scores (M = .77, SD = .31) than participants in the Self-Read condition (M = .46, SD = .41)."
