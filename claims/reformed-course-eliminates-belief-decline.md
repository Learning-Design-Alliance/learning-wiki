## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall expert-like favorable responses on CLASS shifted down significantly in all four traditional courses (shifts of -3.7 to -8.0) but not in the two reformed courses (+1.0 and -2.1). [→ S. B. McKagan 2007](#s-b-mckagan-2007)

## Evidence

### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Reforming a large lecture modern physics course for engineering majors using a PER-based design. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1063/1.2508685](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2508685)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CLASS pre/post survey of the same six courses (Table 2). Traditional courses shifted down (e.g., Trad. Eng. Sp05 -8.0, N=55); reformed courses shifted +1.0 (N=135) and -2.1 (N=150), not statistically significant.

> "Table 2 shows that while the traditional modern physics courses had large shifts towards novice-like beliefs, there were no statistically signiﬁcant shifts in the overall beliefs of students in the reformed courses."
