Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Interleaving showed robust benefits across a multiverse analysis of data-processing choices, with all key p-values below stated thresholds — related
  • Interleaving disproportionately benefited students with below-average or up-to-0.96-SD-above-average midterm scores, though ceiling effects may contribute — related
  • Controlling for prior knowledge and demographics, interleaving benefited all students on the final exam (β = 0.21, p < 0.001) — related
  • On the midterm, interleaving significantly reduced the gap between URM and non-URM students relative to blocked homework — related
  • Neither URM status nor first-generation status moderates the intervention effects on retrieval practice outcomes — related
  • On raw exam scores, interleaved homework produced no significant midterm difference between sections but a significant final exam advantage for the interleaved section — related
