### A.I. Morales | P. Tuzón 2020

A.I. Morales | P. Tuzón. (2020). Misconceptions, knowledge, and attitudes of secondary school students towards the phenomenon of radioactivity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07746](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07746)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire analysis of 191 secondary students in the Valencia region, with responses to Item 1 analysed by hierarchical clustering (euclidean metric, Ward's algorithm) and per-level categorical analysis. The printed frequencies show the gradient: "below 30% for the ﬁrst ontological group (‘perceptible’)" versus "exceeds the 60% for the last one (‘microscopic’)".

> "Whilst the percentage of positive answers is below 30% for the ﬁrst ontological group (‘perceptible’), it exceeds the 60% for the last one (‘microscopic’)."
