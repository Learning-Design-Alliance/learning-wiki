`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of trainee and educator interviews. The article reports "Stress and anxiety were reported highly with those surgically trained with this model", referring to the apprenticeship model of live surgical teaching.

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`
