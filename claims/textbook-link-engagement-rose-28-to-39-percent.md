`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of interaction logs from the practice exam system at midterm 1. The article reports "approximately 28% of students clicked on provided textbook links" with similar rates across the two link-bearing conditions; no effect size was printed.

> "Analysis of midterm 1 interaction data
showed that approximately 28% of students clicked on provided
textbook links, with similar rates between those who received only
textbook references and those who received both AI feedback and
textbook references."
