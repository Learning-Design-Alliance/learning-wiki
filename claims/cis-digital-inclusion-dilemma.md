### Louise Starkey 2023

Louise Starkey, Anne Yates, Mairead de Roiste, Karsten Lundqvist, Adreanne Ormond, John Randal, Allan Sylvester. (2023). Each discipline is different: teacher capabilities for future-focussed digitally infused undergraduate programmes. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10196-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The Critical Indigenous Studies case study reports that digital technology enables place-based, culturally relevant living testimony from elders and wider dissemination of indigenous knowledge; a qualitative, interpretive account with no measured outcomes.

> "For example, remote areas that were unreachable and therefore unteachable are now reachable through digital technology. Knowledge holders, such as elders, can share their life wisdom, understanding of cultural practices and expertise from their homes, community and ancestral landscape."

### Louise Starkey 2023 (2)

Louise Starkey, Anne Yates, Mairead de Roiste, Karsten Lundqvist, Adreanne Ormond, John Randal, Allan Sylvester. (2023). Each discipline is different: teacher capabilities for future-focussed digitally infused undergraduate programmes. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10196-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-case discussion identifies the inclusion dilemma: greater inclusion via digital connection "can be excluding to those who lack access to the technology". This is the authors' stated tension, not a tested effect.

> "However, the ability of digital technology to provide a more inclusive teaching space creates a dilemma because the opportunity for greater inclusion by connecting with formerly unreachable communities, can be excluding to those who lack access to the technology."
