Evidence¶

Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan 2025¶

Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan, Emine Şendurur. (2025). The effect of cumulative eye movements' guidance of experts on transition from novice to expert. Education Tech Research Dev, 73:59–89. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10414-5

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Eye-tracking analysis of 7 experts' fixations on four AOIs (Toolbox, Workspace, Menu, Right) drawn in OGAMA, with fixation duration means, fixation counts, and mouse clicks analyzed per topic. The authors interpret the Menu pattern as experts "preferring shortcuts over menu clicks."

"The Toolbox AOI was reported as the most frequently visited AOI since the fixations were recorded highest for 5 out of 8 topics. The Menu AOI was reported as the least frequently visited AOI since the fixations were recorded lowest for 5 out of 8 topics."
