## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2022

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with time-development of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in virtual and in-person classes. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac49f4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac49f4)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Comparison of Cohen's d values by question format in the 2021 in-person cohort (N = 25), reported in the conclusions from tables 2 and 3; the largest normalized gain was 0.82 on Q3. The article prints effect sizes "of 1.5-2.0" for open-ended questions.

> "In 2021, effect sizes were more polarized, with the multiple-choice questions giving values between 0.30-0.80, and the open-ended questions boasting remarkable effect sizes of 1.5-2.0."
