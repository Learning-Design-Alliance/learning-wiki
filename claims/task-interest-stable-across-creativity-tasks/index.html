In an observational SEM, perceived interest in Task 1 was strongly associated with interest in Task 2 (β = 0.52) with no significant mediation through task experience, and person-level value of creativity was associated with Task 1 interest mostly directly (β = 0.49), task experience mediating only 6% of that effect. → Kamila Urban 2025

Evidence¶

Kamila Urban 2025¶

Kamila Urban, Marek Urban. (2025). Metacognition and motivation in creativity: examining the roles of self-efficacy and values as cues for metacognitive judgments. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09421-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

In the SEM testing H2c, "there was no mediation" of Task 1 interest on Task 2 interest, while the direct effect remained strong; H2a was also not supported, with task experience mediating only 6% of the value-of-creativity effect on Task 1 interest. These are associations in a structural model of one sample, not evidence that prior interest causes later interest.

"the results did not support this hypothesis, as there was no mediation (β = − 0.01, p =.392). The direct effect of perceived interest in Task 1 on perceived interest in Task 2 remained strong (β = 0.52, p <.001)."
