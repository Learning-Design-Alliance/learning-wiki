## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The indirect effect of cognitive load on innovation through technological adaptability was significant (β = −.171), accounting for 56.3% of the total effect. [→ Su 2026](#su-2026)

## Evidence

### Su 2026

Su, Dongying; Meijuan, Gai; Aikebaier, Simayilijiang. (2026). Cognitive load and teachers' innovative behavior in AI-enhanced English language instruction: A mediation analysis of technological adaptability. PLOS One. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0343002](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0343002)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis with bias-corrected bootstrap confidence intervals (5,000 resamples) on the same N = 600 survey. The indirect effect was β = −.171 with CI [−.211, −.131], excluding zero, and the total effect was β = −.304.

> "The proportion of the total effect mediated by technological adaptability was calculated as |indirect effect|/ |total effect| = |−0.171|/ |−0.304| = 56.3%, indicating that technological adaptability accounts for over half of the relationship between cognitive load and innovative teaching behavior."
