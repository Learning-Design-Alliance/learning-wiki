# In-session autonomy, competence and intrinsic motivation are higher during basketball mathematics than during classroom mathematics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` BM-group students reported significantly higher autonomy (+16.33%, p < .001), competence (+5.21%, p < .05) and intrinsic motivation (+24.37%, p < .001) during basketball mathematics sessions than during classroom mathematics lessons. [→ Jensen 2026](#jensen-2026)

## Evidence

### Jensen 2026

Integrating Fractions into Physical Education classes can improve Mathematical Performance in 11–13-Year-Olds - The Basketball Mathematics project. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the BM group (N = 176), the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory was administered immediately after basketball mathematics and classroom mathematics lessons in weeks 2–5. All three IMI subscales favored the BM setting; only percentage differences and p-values are printed, no effect sizes.

> "Autonomy was significantly higher during BM than CM (+ 16.33%, p < .001), as was competence (+ 5.21%, p < .05). The largest difference was in intrinsic motivation, which was 24.37% higher during BM (p < .001)."
