## Evidence

### Almalki Sultan A. 2024

Almalki Sultan A., AlJameel AlBandary H., Alghomlas Ziyad, Alothman Tameem, Alhajri Fawaz. (2024). Assessing the predictive validity of pre-admission criteria on dental students’ academic performance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03839-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analyses reported with Figures 4 and 5 from the additional analyses of predictor effects section of the questionnaire study. Table 6 prints the Age:High School Grade interaction (Wald Chi-Square 14.110, p < 0.001) and Age:Aptitude Test Score interaction (Wald Chi-Square 7.185, p = 0.007).

> "Age negatively impacted GPA, with older students typically having lower GPA scores. This effect was moderated by both high school grade and aptitude test score, where higher values in these predictors lessened the negative impact of age"

### Almalki Sultan A. 2024 (2)

Almalki Sultan A., AlJameel AlBandary H., Alghomlas Ziyad, Alothman Tameem, Alhajri Fawaz. (2024). Assessing the predictive validity of pre-admission criteria on dental students’ academic performance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03839-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction analysis reported with Figure 3 in the additional analyses section. The article also reports that suspended status's negative effect "turned positive for married students" (Fig. 12) and that English proficiency negatively affected performance at low aptitude scores but positively at higher scores (Fig. 11).

> "However, the interaction of gender with achievement test scores revealed that males outperformed females at lower achievement scores, while the opposite was true at higher scores"
