# In a blind expert evaluation of Italian bar exam papers, Gemini 2.5 Pro (79) and GPT-5 (65) scored above the top human candidate (62) out of 100.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two LLMs significantly surpassed the human candidate's cumulative bar-exam score, with Gemini 2.5 Pro highest at 79 and ChatGPT-5 at 65 against the human's 62. [→ Germana Bertoli 2025](#germana-bertoli-2025)

## Evidence

### Germana Bertoli 2025

Germana Bertoli, Ilaria Amelia Caggiano, Francesca Lagioia, Riccardo Rovatti, Giovanni Sartor, Emiliano Troisi. (2025). What out-of-the-box LLMs can(t) do in law? A Turing test in Italian exams for lawyers, judges and notaries. https://github.com/SSIGPRO/LLMs-in-legal-professions

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blind expert evaluation of bar-exam papers: three examiners who had served on national boards scored five uniformly formatted papers (one top human candidate, four LLMs) on official criteria. Cumulative scores ranged "from a minimum of 26 to 79 out of 100".

> "Two models significantly surpass the human candidate (who scored 62): Gemini 2.5 Pro achieved the highest overall score (79), followed by ChatGPT-5 (65)."
