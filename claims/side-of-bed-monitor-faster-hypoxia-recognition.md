## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Boccio E 2026

Boccio E, Perkins R, Bonz J. (2026). Patient Monitor Position and Operator Ability to Visualize a Desaturation Event During Intubation. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.52933

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover simulation trial of 34 EM residents (68 attempts) on a difficult airway trainer; Cox regression adjusted for laryngoscopy modality and prior intubations with a shared frailty model. "hazard ratios of 2.7 and 3.2 for the left and right groups when compared to the head group" were statistically significant. Median times were 37 sec (head), 32 sec (left), 23 sec (right).

> "Cox regression demonstrated hazard ratios of 2.7 and 3.2 for the left and right groups when compared to the head group, and these findings were statistically significant (P = .04 and P = .03, respectively)."
