## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No strong evidence of positive or negative spillover to same-term GPA in other courses, and no effects on subsequent-term enrollment in the subject, for the overall sample or for women in economics. [→ Meyer 2026](#meyer-2026)

## Evidence

### Meyer 2026

Meyer, Katharine, Lindsay C. Page, Catherine Mata, Eric N. Smith, B. Tyler Walsh, C. Lindsey Fifield, Michelle Tyson, Amy Eremionkhale, Michael Evans, Shelby Frost, and Eye Eoun Jung. (2026). Let's Chat: Leveraging Chatbot Outreach for Improved Course Performance (NBER Working Paper No. 35397). National Bureau of Economic Research. [doi:10.3386/w35397](https://doi.org/10.3386/w35397). Also released as EdWorkingPaper 22-564, Annenberg Institute at Brown University: https://edworkingpapers.com/ai22-564

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ITT analysis of term GPA (including and excluding the focal course) and next-term subject enrollment from administrative records (Table 9). Outcomes examined for the overall sample and for women in economics specifically; none showed significant treatment effects.

> "We do not find strong evidence of positive or negative spillover or medium-term effects for the overall sample. We also consider each outcome for women in economics, where we previously found the strongest within-course effects, and did not observe significant effects of treatment on spillovers or subsequent semester enrollment for that subgroup."
