Evidence¶

Gbenga-Akanmu & Jegede 2019¶

Gbenga-Akanmu, T. O., & Jegede, P. O. (2019). Influence of Meta-cognitive Training and Cognitive Styles on Numeracy Achievement of Nigerian Pupils’ in Computer-mediated Classrooms. World Journal of Education, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v9n1p221

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA (Table 2) of the quasi-experiment, with sex and pre-test score as covariates. The text states the intervention group "expl ained 15% of the variation in performance", but Table 2 has no group row: the partial eta square of 0.15 is printed on the "(CGEFT)" row, the group embedded figure test that measures cognitive style (F = 4.00, p = 0.0145). So no effect size is printed for the intervention on performance. A partial eta square of 0.15 is printed for the Group row of Table 3 (retention, with sex, pre-test and post-test as covariates), but that row's F = 2.16 is reported with p = 0.0269.

"The partial eta square for the intervention group is 0.15. This showed that intervention group expl ained 15% of the variation in performance."
