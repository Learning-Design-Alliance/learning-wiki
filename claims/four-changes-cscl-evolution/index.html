Evidence¶

Baker 2024¶

Baker, M., Reimann, P. (2024). CSCL: a learning and collaboration science?. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09433-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Editorial essay argument, not an empirical study. The authors assert, with cited background literature, that collaborative pedagogies are mainstream, platforms such as Moodle make technology "infrastructure", and "what counts as learning has changed" as large collectives learn as collectives.

"Of the many changes that have taken place over the past thirty years, we would like to emphasise just four of them."
