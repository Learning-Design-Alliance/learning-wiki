### Jokinen Ewa 2020

Jokinen Ewa, Mikkola Tomi S., Härkki Päivi. (2020). Simulator training and residents’ first laparoscopic hysterectomy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-07270-3

Randomized controlled trial; blinded assessors rated overall management on a 0–100 VAS anchored to a young specialist's level. The trial found "55.0 (SD 14.8) in the intervention group and 29.9 (SD 14.9) in the control group (p = 0.001, 95% CI 11.23–39.07)". No effect size printed for VAS.
