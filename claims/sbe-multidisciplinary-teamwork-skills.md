### Mwalabu Gertrude 2024

Mwalabu Gertrude, Msosa Annie, Tjoflåt Ingrid, Urstad Kristin Hjorthaug, Bø Bodil, Furskog-Risa Eva Christina, Mapulanga Patrick, Msiska Masauko. (2024). Simulation-Based Education as a Solution to Challenges Encountered with Clinical Teaching in Nursing and Midwifery Education in Malawi: A Qualitative Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/1776533

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A clinical instructor in the qualitative interview data described simulation as providing "a more practical—hands-on experience of more skills in one scenario" involving medical officers, dietitians, physiotherapists, radiologists, and laboratory staff.

> "The more students simulate, the more they develop multi-disciplinary skills, how to communicate and relate with other professionals, which is currently missing in our nursing and midwifery education."
