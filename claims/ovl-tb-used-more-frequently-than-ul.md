## Evidence

### Rafey IA 2026

Rafey IA, Abdullah A, Saeed Z, Khan UU, Mohyuddin W. (2026). Comparative analysis of university lectures, textbooks, and online video lectures: preferences, engagement, and impact on academic performance among medical students of Nowshera Medical College. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09921-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Frequency-of-use data from the same cross-sectional survey (n = 354). Descriptively, 92.1% reported often or always using OVL, 88.8% TB, and just 37.5% UL, with "a large effect size" for the overall Friedman test.

> "Friedman test confirmed significant differences across modalities, χ² (2, N = 354) = 477.34, p < 0.001, with Kendall's W = 0.674, indicating a large effect size. Both TB and OVL were used significantly more than UL, whereas the difference between TB and OVL was small and not statistically significant (Z = − 2.401, r = 0.128)."
