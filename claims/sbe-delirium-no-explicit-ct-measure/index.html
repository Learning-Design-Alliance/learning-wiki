This is the review authors' narrative assessment of the corpus of 14 included studies, offered with no empirical test. They state plainly that "None of the included studies directly measured critical thinking as an explicit outcome", extrapolating critical-thinking development as an implicit outcome from consistent improvements in reasoning, reflection and decision accuracy.
