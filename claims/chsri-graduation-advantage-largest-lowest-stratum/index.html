q2 · i? · associational · r3

Stratified analysis of the 2004 cohort: students were grouped into five equal strata by pre-high school probability of graduating, then CHSRI and non-CHSRI graduation rates compared within strata. CHSRI students graduated at higher rates in every stratum. No effect size printed.

"Among the lowest quintile (Stratum 1), the difference in graduation between CHSRI and non-CHSRI students was slightly more than six percentage points; the difference in the fifth quintile (Stratum 5) was about two percentage points"
