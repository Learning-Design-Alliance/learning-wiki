## Evidence

### Avia Ben-Ari 2025

Avia Ben-Ari, Tess Gavrielle Levinson, Jessica Blake-West, Marina Umaschi Bers, Rinat B. Rosenberg-Kima. (2025). From avoidance to ownership: preschool teachers learn to teach code—a case study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10548-0

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of in-depth semi-structured interviews with the two teachers, conducted at the end of the seven-month implementation, corroborated by coding assessments. The article reports the shift "from initial resistance due to perceived low self-efficacy and concerns about early-age screentime to voluntary coding engagement".

> "The teachers shifted from initial resistance due to perceived low self-efficacy and concerns about early-age screentime to voluntary coding engagement, learning alongside their students while enhancing teacher-student relationships."
