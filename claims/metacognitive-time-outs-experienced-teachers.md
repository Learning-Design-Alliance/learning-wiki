## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experienced teachers frequently paused for metacognitive time-outs recapping process and generalising skills; inexperienced teachers almost never did. [→ Winston Kalman A 2013](#winston-kalman-a-2013)

## Evidence

### Winston Kalman A 2013

Winston Kalman A, Van Der Vleuten Cees PM, Scherpbier Albert JJA. (2013). Remediation of at-risk medical students: theory in action. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-132

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discourse analysis of transcribed small-group sessions in the ELLS remediation course. Talk Sample 2 shows T5 recapping a dense question, letting students admit difficulty, and generalising the rephrasing skill; the article reports such time-outs "happened multiple times" with experienced teachers and almost never otherwise.

> "These 'metacognitive time-outs' for attending to process happened multiple times in the classrooms of experienced teachers, but almost never with the inexperienced teachers."
