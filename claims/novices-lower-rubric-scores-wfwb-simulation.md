`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In an independent-measures simulation study of 41 participants (12 novices, 13 intermediates, 16 experts), ANOVA of rubric scores stratified by experience level showed "novices had significantly lower rubric scores compared to intermediate‐level medical professionals" and experts.

> "Pairwise comparisons showed that novices had significantly lower rubric scores compared to intermediate‐level medical professionals (31.3 ± 6.2 vs. 37.8 ± 5.8, p = .002) and experts (40.9 ± 2.5, p <.001, Figure 5)."
