q1 · i? · causal · r1

Thematic analysis of brief interviews with four intervention-cohort students and the instructor. Students reported videos helped them anticipate difficult practical steps; the instructor observed students completed two samples by around Week 8 or 9 versus around Week 11 previously.

"Third, the GenAI module was described as a supplementary tool for early-stage visual ideation. Students used it mainly to explore possible visual directions, while the development and refinement of final design outcomes continued to rely on instructor feedback, material testing, and hands-on studio work."
