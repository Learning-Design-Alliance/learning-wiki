## Evidence

### Alhurishi Sultana A. 2021

Alhurishi Sultana A., Aljuraiban Ghadeer S., Alshaikh Fahdah A., Almutairi Mona M., Almutairi Khalid M. (2021). Predictors of students' academic achievements in allied health professions at King Saud University: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02525-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate linear regressions on the retrospective cohort of allied health graduates identified gender, college, program, graduation year and the three admission scores as potential predictors of graduation GPA at "p < 0.05"; these were then entered into the multivariate model.

> "The univariate analyses identified gender, type of college, type of program, year of graduation, the HSGA, APT, and ACT tests scores as potential predictors of academic achievement i.e., GPA at graduation (p < 0.05)."
