## Evidence

### Öcal Mustafa 2025

Öcal Mustafa. (2025). The role of video and direct laryngoscopy in medical student intubation training: a comparative study on success rates and learning curves. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07239-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

T-test on average intubation times in the mannequin study of 130 students. The Mann-Whitney U test confirmed the same pattern, with the first-attempt difference "not statistically significant (p = 0.128)". Descriptive means were 2.0/1.89/1.55 min for DL and 1.79/1.55/1.05 min for VL across attempts.

> "The results of the T-Test, conducted to assess whether the differences in average intubation times were statistically significant, indicated that the differences in the 2nd and 3rd attempts were statistically significant (p < 0.05), whereas the difference in the 1st attempt was not statistically significant (p = 0.13)."
