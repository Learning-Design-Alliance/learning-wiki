## Evidence

### Blackburne LK 2014

Blackburne LK, Eddy MD, Kalra P, Yee D, Sinha P, Gabrieli JDE. (2014) Neural Correlates of Letter Reversal in Children and Adults. PLoS ONE 9(5): e98386. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0098386

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Whole-brain fMRI analysis of the letter reversal task (15 children, 15 adults) at voxel-wise p <.01 with FDR correction p <.05. Children's reversed-minus-typical activation was negative in phonological and early visual regions, with the sole reversed-greater region being "the middle frontal gyrus"; adults had no activation for the typical-greater contrast. No effect size was printed.

> "In children, there was greater activation for typical than reversed letters in left inferior parietal lobule, left superior temporal gyrus, and early visual regions (Figure 4). Children showed greater activation for reversed letters than normal letters in the middle frontal gyrus."
