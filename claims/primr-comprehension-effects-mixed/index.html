Stephanie Simmons Zuilkowski & Benjamin Piper 2014¶

Stephanie Simmons Zuilkowski & Benjamin Piper. (2014). Improving Reading Outcomes in Kenya: First-Year Effects of the PRIMR Initiative. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562806

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' summary of the DID analysis of the PRIMR RCT covering all three outcome measures; the article states the positive impact came "although not on all combinations of language, grade, and school type."

"The analysis has shown that the first year of PRIMR had a positive impact on the three outcomes of interest—oral reading fluency, the percentage of pupils who read at the MOE’s benchmark, and reading comprehension—although not on all combinations of language, grade, and school type."
