## Evidence

### Knox S 2015

Knox S, Cullen W, Dunne CP. (2015). A national study of Continuous Professional Competence (CPC) amongst pre-hospital practitioners. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-1197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National anonymous online survey of all registered Irish pre-hospital practitioners; response rates 43% in both cohorts (789 Paramedics/APs, 399 EMTs). "Registration with the Regulator was considered personally important" for the large majorities quoted; 74% of Paramedics/APs and 95% of EMTs agreed CPC should be a condition of registration.

> "Registration with the Regulator was considered personally important by 89 % (697) of Paramedics/APs and 97 % (381) of EMTs."
