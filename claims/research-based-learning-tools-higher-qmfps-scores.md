## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2019

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Validation and Administration of a Conceptual Survey on the Formalism and Postulates of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Quasi-experimental comparison across undergraduate QM courses, with N = 43 students in courses using research-based learning tools. Students without such tools averaged 32% (S.D.=16%), and "There is a significant difference between the scores of students who used research-based learning tools and those who did not (p-value of t-test<0.0001)".

> "The average posttest score for the upper-level students who used concept tests and group discussion and Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorials (QuILTs) was 58% (S.D.=20%)."
