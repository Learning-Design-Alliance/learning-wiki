# PF students scored significantly higher than DI students on near transfer (butterfly effect problem)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Being in the PF treatment had a significant positive impact on near transfer scores compared to DI (η² = .038), with no significant teacher experience effect or interaction. [→ Jacobson 2017](#jacobson-2017)

## Evidence

### Jacobson 2017

Jacobson, Michael, Markauskaite, Lina, Portolese, Alisha, Kapur, Manu, Lai, Polly, & Roberts, Gareth (2017). Designs for learning about climate change as a complex system. Learning and Instruction, 52, pp. 1-14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.007

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the butterfly effect near transfer problem, which required appropriate use of climate and complex systems ideas, in the quasi-experiment with 110 students. Treatment main effect favored PF (η² = .038); teacher experience and interaction were not significant.

> "For the “butterfly effect” near transfer problem, a two-way ANOVA revealed there was a significant main effect of treatment group (F(1,106) = 4.144, p = .022, one-tailed, = .038)."
