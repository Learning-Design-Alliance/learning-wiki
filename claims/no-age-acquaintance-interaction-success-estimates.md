## Evidence

### Harvey J. GINSBURG 2016

Harvey J. GINSBURG, Rebecca CAMERON, Roque V. MENDEZ & Michael WESTHOFF. (2016). Helping others use social media: age stereotypes when estimating learner's success. Psychology, Society, & Education, 8(1), 1-12. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1123456

`q3 · i?`

The omnibus repeated measures 2x3 ANOVA over six within-subject scenarios reported the age main effect and the null acquaintance effect; the article states "No significant age x acquaintance interaction was observed". No interaction effect size is printed.

> "Results from repeated measures 3 (age) x 2 (acquaintance) ANOVA showed significant main effects for age, F(1.847,3.89.617) = 171.305, p< .001. No significant main effect was shown for acquaintance type, F(1,211) = 1.71, p= .193"
