`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group analysis of change scores across the short, medium and long courses in the randomized dose-finding trial. The article prints no effect size for the between-group contrasts; "between-group differences in change scores were not statistically significant".

> "Although there were trends toward a decrease in improvement in self-efficacy with increased course length, and an increase in improvement in interpersonal problems with increased course length, between-group differences in change scores were not statistically significant."
