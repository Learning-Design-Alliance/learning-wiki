# Students perceived the intended expansive versus bounded framing manipulation (d = 1.4)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` On the post-tutoring survey, expansive-condition students scored a mean of 39.4 versus 32.4 in the bounded condition on the expansive framing score (d = 1.4; F(1,19) = 10.6, p = .004). [→ Randi A. Engle 2011](#randi-a-engle-2011)

## Evidence

### Randi A. Engle 2011

Randi A. Engle, Phi D. Nguyen, Adam Mendelson. (2011). The influence of framing on transfer: initial evidence from a tutoring experiment. Instr Sci 39:603–628. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9145-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation check using a nine-question Likert-style post-tutoring survey aggregated into an expansive framing score, analyzed with 2x2 ANOVA in the tutoring experiment. "There was a large main effect (d = 1.4) of condition".

> "There was a large main effect (d = 1.4) of condition, with students in the expansive condition scoring a mean of 39.4 (SD = 3.1) and those in the bounded condition scoring a mean of 32.4 ( SD = 6.4; F(1,19) = 10.6; p = .004)"
