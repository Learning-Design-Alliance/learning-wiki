Firdausi 2026¶

Firdausi, H.; Wasis; Sholikah, R. W.; Lemantara, J.; Rosyadi, F. D.; Ginardi, R. V. H.; Pradityo, M. H. A.; Hakim, A. R. (2026). International Journal of Intelligent Engineering and Systems, Vol.19, No.8. https://doi.org/10.22266/ijies2026.0831.09

q2 · i? · design · r2

Reliability analysis of essay scoring by three high school physics teachers of 106 eleventh-grade responses (Methodology §3.1, Table 2). Disagreements over one point were resolved by discussion with a third rater as arbiter; final labels were mean scores rounded to integers.

"all six FRISCO dimensions yielded mean PoA values above 75%, satisfying the reliability threshold [24] and mean kappa values ranging from 0.78 to 0.84, indicating substantial to almost perfect agreement"
