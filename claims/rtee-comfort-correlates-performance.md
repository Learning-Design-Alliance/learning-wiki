## Evidence

### Ablordeppey E 2025

Ablordeppey E, Terian E, Murray CT, Wallace L, Huang W, Blustein E, Croft A, Romo E, Agarwal M, Theodoro D. (2025). Low Frequency, High Complexity: Assessing Skill Decay in Transesophageal Echocardiography Post-Simulation Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.35857

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

One-year online 16-item Likert survey via REDCap with a 100% response rate from the seven participants; Kendall tau correlations between comfort ratings and image-acquisition test performance. The article reports r = .91 (P = .004) at month 7 for omniplane position.

> "Strong correlations were found between comfort with view naming, probe manipulation, and simulation performance, particularly for omniplane position (0.73, P = .06 at month 6; .91, P = .004 at month 7)."
