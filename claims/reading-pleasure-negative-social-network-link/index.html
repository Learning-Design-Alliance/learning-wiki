Miccoli 2023¶

Miccoli, L., Arias, M. Á. P., & Santiago, J. (2023). A network analysis on digital media use, reading enjoyment, and orthography precision in a highly educated sample. Computers & Education, 207, 104932. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104932

Network structure result: "a negative direct link, although weak (-.1), between social networks use and reading enjoyment". The authors speculate this may hint at opposed attitudes underlying reading pleasure and digital media use.
