### Hrasnica F 2026

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Formative evaluation with 13 participants (12 residents, 1 student) using the UEQ. The article reports "All 6 UEQ categories were positively evaluated" with the printed means and SDs; no effect sizes were reported.

> "All 6 UEQ categories were positively evaluated. Attractiveness (mean 2.064, SD 0.672) and stimulation (mean 1.827, SD 0.949) were the highest-rated categories, whereas dependability was the lowest-rated category (mean 1.115, SD 0.740; Figure 1)."
