## Evidence

### Chunpeng Zhai 2024

Chunpeng Zhai, Santoso Wibowo and Lily D. Li. (2024). The effects of over-reliance on AI dialogue systems on students' cognitive abilities: a systematic review. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-024-00316-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Study reported in the review's Table 2: Gao et al. evaluated ChatGPT's ability to generate abstracts using an AI plagiarism detection tool and critical analysis by a blinded human reviewer. The review adds that the capacity to generate credible references "might be constrained by the presence of DOIs and the accessibility of online articles".

> "Of the abstracts generated by ChatGPT, 68% were correctly identified as such (true positives), while 14% of authentic abstracts were mistakenly categorized as chatbot-generated (false positives)."
