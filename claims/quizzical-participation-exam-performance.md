## Evidence

### Riggs C. Daniel 2020

Riggs C. Daniel, Kang Sohee, Rennie Olivia. (2020). Positive Impact of Multiple-Choice Question Authoring and Regular Quiz Participation on Student Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education 19(2):ar16. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-09-0189

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on combined 2017–2018 data from a sophomore molecular biology class of roughly 500 students per term, with engagement categorized as Low/Mid/High by participation percentile and incoming GPA as covariate. The article reports a "highly significant course performance difference between the three categories" with no difference between years.

> "We found that there was a highly significant course performance difference between the three categories (p = 3.037 × 10−14; see Supplemental Table S1-3), one that is not based on previous academic ability, and that there was no significant difference between the 2 years."
