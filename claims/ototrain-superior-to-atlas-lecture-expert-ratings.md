## Evidence

### Wickens Brandon 2015

Wickens Brandon, Lewis Jordan, Morris David P, Husein Murad, Ladak Hanif M, Agrawal Sumit K. (2015). Face and content validity of a novel, web-based otoscopy simulator for medical education. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-015-0060-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey comparison component in which 11 experts ranked OtoTrain against traditional atlas/lecture teaching. Percentages rating the simulator superior were 63.6% for external auditory canal anatomy, 63.6% for otoscopy technique, 54.5% for availability, and 45.5% for tympanic membrane anatomy (tied with equivalent at 45.5%).

> "In each domain, the simulator tool was deemed superior to the atlas/lecture-based method, with the exception of learning tympanic membrane anatomy in which an equal percentage of experts considered it to be superior or equivalent."
