Sandra Schulz · Bruce M. McLaren · Niels Pinkwart. (2023). Towards a Tutoring System to Support Robotics Activities in Classrooms – Two Wizard-of-Oz Studies. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 33. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00305-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of student interviews after the first WOZ I intervention with LEGO robots, in which 14 students were interviewed. Students “described that they had problems mostly in the area of software” (N = 10), specifically in using the programming environment (N = 6); problems in hardware, environment and math/physics were rare.
