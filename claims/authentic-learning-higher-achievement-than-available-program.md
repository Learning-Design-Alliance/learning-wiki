## Evidence

### Gürgil 2018

Fitnat Gürgil. (2018). The Effect of Authentic Learning Approach in Social Studies Teaching on the Academic Success. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2061-2068. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061002

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 92 experimental and 92 control students across three Ankara schools. Independent-samples t-test with Bonferroni correction showed the posttest difference was significant, "(p = .000, η2 = .65)", while the pretest difference was not (p = .918).

> "However, the difference between the posttest scores of the groups is statistically significant (p = .000, η2 = .65)."
