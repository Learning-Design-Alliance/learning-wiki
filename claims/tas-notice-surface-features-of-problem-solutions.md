## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2011

Shih-Yin Lin, Chandralekha Singh, William Mamudi, Charles Henderson, Edit Yerushalmi. (2011). TA-Designed vs. Research-Oriented Problem Solutions. 2011 PERC Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3680043](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680043)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of 24 first-year TAs' worksheets and discussion in a TA training course, using a GAIQ artifact-comparison activity with three instructor solutions. The authors report that most noticed features were "surface features" and that deeper features like F6 and F12 were less commonly identified.

> "Yet, most features that the TAs noticed were "surface features" such as F1 (drawing), F3 (separate overview), and F9 (length) that one is likely to be aware of even if s/he doesn't know much about physics problem solving."
