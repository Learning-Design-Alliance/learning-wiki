# Reflecting with the 3D manipulable animation made Session #3 reflective discussions more student-centered than reflecting with video only

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In Session #3, the experimental condition (reflection with 3D animation) showed significantly more student-centered reflection along the x-axis than the control condition (video only), d = 0.60. [→ Mochizuki 2022](#mochizuki-2022)

## Evidence

### Mochizuki 2022

Mochizuki, T., Sasaki, H., Wakimoto, T., Kubota, Y., Eagan, B., Hirayama, R., Yamaguchi, Y., Yuki, N., Funaoi, H., Suzuki, H., & Kato, H. (2022). Mediating and perspective-taking manipulatives: Fostering dynamic perspective-taking by mediating dialogic thinking and bolstering empathy in role-play and reflection for microteaching. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09382-w

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of reflective-discussion ENA mean plots between the experimental (13 triads analyzed, N = 39 plotted units) and control (11 triads, N = 33) conditions in Session #3. The groups differed significantly ("p = 0.01, d = 0.60"), with the animation group more student-centered and the video group more teacher-centered as interpreted from the ENA axes.

> "we conducted two sample t-tests (assuming unequal variances) between the two conditions in Session #3, showing that there was a significant difference along the x-axis (the experimental condition (N = 39): M = 0.12, SD = 0.48; the control condition (N = 33): M = −0.14, SD = 0.36; t(69.05) = 2.61, p = 0.01, d = 0.60)"
