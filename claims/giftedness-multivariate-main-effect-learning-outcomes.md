## Evidence

### Lim 2023

Lim, S., Jung, J. Y., & Kalyuga, S. (2023). Effectiveness of invention tasks and explicit instruction in preparing intellectually gifted adolescents for learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09616-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate ANOVA in the Results section of the randomized study of 199 seventh graders, with instruction, contrast and giftedness as independent variables and five dependent measures. The article reports a significant multivariate main effect for giftedness with the printed effect size η2 p = .178, along with Wilks' λ = .822, F (5, 186) = 8.08, p < .001.

> "The multivariate ANOVA revealed a significant multivariate main effect for the dependent variables col- lectively for giftedness (Wilks' λ = .822, F (5, 186) = 8.08, p < .001, η2 p = .178)"
