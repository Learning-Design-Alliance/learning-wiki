## Evidence

### Eisenmann D 2018

Eisenmann D, Stroben F, Gerken JD, Exadaktylos AK, Machner M, Hautz WE. (2018). Interprofessional Emergency Training Leads to Changes in the Workplace. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2017.11.35275

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Nonresponse bias evaluation after 50% dropout between first assessment and follow-up, using exploratory statistics. No significant responder/non-responder differences were found in age (p = 0.340) or gender (p = 0.294), but response differed by profession.

> "However, trainees of emergency nursing (n=14; 77.8% response rate on follow-up) and student paramedics (n=13; 59.1%) were significantly more likely to respond than medical students (n=1; 6.7%; p < 0.01; Pearson's chi-squared test)."
