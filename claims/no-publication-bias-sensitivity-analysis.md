## Evidence

### Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter 2017

Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of Educator Training to Improve Implementation of Interventions for Students With Disabilities. Remedial and Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932516653477

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication-bias sensitivity analysis of the 12 study effect sizes using a funnel plot, Egger test, and trim-and-fill. The Egger test was nonsignificant (p = .41) and "No studies were removed or added through trim-and-fill analysis," so no publication-bias evidence was found.

> "Results from an Egger's suggest the positive association between effect size and standard error is not greater than what would be expected by chance, β = 1.16, t(10) = 0.86, p = .41. No studies were removed or added through trim-and-fill analysis, so this analysis did not provide any evidence of publication bias."
