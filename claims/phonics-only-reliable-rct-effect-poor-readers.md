## Evidence

### Genevieve McArthur and Anne Castles 2017

Genevieve McArthur and Anne Castles. (2017). Helping children with reading difficulties: some things we have learned so far. npj Science of Learning (2017) 2:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0008-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article reports the key findings of a systematic review by Galuschka et al. of all RCTs assessing interventions in poor readers. The review covered coloured lenses or overlays, medication, motor training, phonemic awareness, phonics, reading comprehension, fluency, sound processing, and sun flower therapy.

> "A third key ﬁnding is that only one type of intervention produced a statistically reliable effect. This was phonics training, which focuses on improving a proximal cause of poor word reading (i.e., letter-sound mappings)."

### Genevieve McArthur and Anne Castles 2017 (2)

Genevieve McArthur and Anne Castles. (2017). Helping children with reading difficulties: some things we have learned so far. npj Science of Learning (2017) 2:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0008-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The same systematic review of RCTs in poor readers, as reported in the article. It found only 22 gold-standard trials despite the wide array of interventions claiming scientific proof, and the majority of those trials assessed phonics training.

> "One key ﬁnding of this review is that it only identiﬁed 22 RCTs, which is a small number of gold-standard intervention studies given the huge number of interventions that claim to help poor readers."
