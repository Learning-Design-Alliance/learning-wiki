`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey question examining strategies employed by medical students to respond to five-option SBA assessments (86 responses). Rote memorisation was the most cited revision strategy (59.1%), followed by practice questions and past papers (42%).

> "Responses were broadly categorised into five response strategies, with the most often cited strategy being the use of the 'process of elimination' (63; 73.3%)."
