## Evidence

### Bersamin 2013

Bersamin, M., Zamboanga, B.L. & Orsak-Neff, N. (2013). Understanding course content through letter writing: Do informal writing assignments improve grades? Psychology Teaching Review, 19(1). https://www.bps.org.uk/journal/psychology-teaching-review

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary ANCOVA (Table 3) adding number of letters completed as a covariate; the letters-completed term itself was also significant (F=5.43, partial η²=.13, p=.02). The authors state this "provides additional support" for the assignment while highlighting student motivation.

> "When controlling for treatment intensity, the results of the writing treatment were statistically significant at p =.02. This provides additional support for the letter writing assignment as a promising effective learning strategy"
