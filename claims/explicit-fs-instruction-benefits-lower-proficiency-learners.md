## Evidence

### Liou 2018

Liou, H.-C., & Chen, W.-F. (2018). Effects of explicit instruction on learning academic formulaic sequences for EFL college learners' writing. Taiwan Journal of TESOL, 15(1), 61-100. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176537

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Participants divided into high (n = 8) and low (n = 7) groups based on FS pretest scores. The low group's performance "had improved enough to catch up with that of the high level group."

> "the mean score gap between the high and low FS levels was 22.50 (out of 100.00) while the mean score gaps in the posttest ( 89.50 vs. 89.71) and delayed -posttest (89.00 vs. 90.29) were reduced from 22.50 to -0.21 and -0.71, respectively."
