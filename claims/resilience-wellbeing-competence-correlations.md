## Evidence

### Yang Bin 2025

Yang Bin, Song Shoujun, Zhang Lili, Xu Chunze. (2025). The relationship between resilience and professional well-being among early childhood teachers: the chain mediating role of teacher pedagogical competence and self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03227-5

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis (Table 4) in the 1,377-teacher survey: the article reports "teacher resilience was significantly and positively correlated with teacher professional well-being (r = 0.87, p < 0.01)"; well-being also correlated with self-efficacy (r = 0.31) and competence (r = 0.54).

> "teacher resilience was significantly and positively correlated with teacher professional well-being (r = 0.87, p < 0.01), pedagogical competence (r = 0.50, p < 0.01), and teacher self-efficacy (r = 0.33, p < 0.01)"
