## Evidence

### Zabolotna 2025

Zabolotna, K., Nøhr, L., Iwata, M., Spikol, D., Malmberg, J., & Järvenoja, H. (2025). How does collaborative task design shape collaborative knowledge construction and group-level regulation of learning? A study of secondary school students' interactions in two varied tasks. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09442-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparative analysis of video-coded interactions from 34 eighth-graders on SimSketch and 15 ninth-graders on Simua. The Mann–Whitney U test showed CKC episodes "significantly longer in the conceptually oriented physics task SimSketch than in the robotics task Simua," with mean frequencies of 10 versus 29 per group.

> "interactions containing CKC were significantly longer in the conceptually oriented physics task SimSketch than in the robotics task Simua, as confirmed through a Mann–Whitney U test (U = 4760.0; p < 0.001). However, the average frequency count per group indicated that CKC interactions were almost three times more frequent in Simua than in SimSketch."
