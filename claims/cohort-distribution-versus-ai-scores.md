Comparison of AI score distribution against a Gaussian reflecting Physics in Society student scores (Fig. 3); top students "are able to outscore the AI essays" while most of the cohort performs comparably or worse.

> "A signiﬁcant trait of the AI generated answers were their consistent quality, as seen in the tight distribution of AI scores compared with student scores for the module.
