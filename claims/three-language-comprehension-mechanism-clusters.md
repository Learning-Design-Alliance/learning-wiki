### Vyshedskiy 2024

Vyshedskiy, A., Venkatesh, R., Khokhlovich, E. & Satik, D. (2024). Three mechanisms of language comprehension are revealed through cluster analysis of individuals with language deficits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00284-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Data-driven Unsupervised Hierarchical Cluster Analysis (Ward's method, Euclidean distance) of 15 parent-reported abilities in 17,848 verbal participants aged 4 to 22. The dendrogram (Fig. 1a) showed three clusters "signi ﬁcantly larger than the distances between subclusters"; a hyperactivity control item clustered separately.
