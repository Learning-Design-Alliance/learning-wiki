`q2 · i0` · `design · r2`

Comparison on the CT Skills instrument using human data synthesized from three prior studies (341 college students, 25 gifted science teachers, 44 sophomores), reported in Section 3.3 and Table 5. Overall z = -0.15; problem-solving z = -4.25.

> "Conversely, o1-preview scored poorly on problem-solving (M = 1.0, SD = 0.0), significantly lower than the human mean (M = 3.68, SD = 0.63), with a z-score of -4.25."

### Latif 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Five-round ATTA administration to o1-preview reported in Section 3.3 and Table 6; human benchmarks ranged from novices and social sciences (9.11) to mathematics (15.70), all exceeded by the model's perfect score.
