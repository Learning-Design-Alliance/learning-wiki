### Oanh Tran Thi Hoang 2024

Oanh Tran Thi Hoang, Thuy Luu Thi, Huyen Ngo Thi Thu. (2024). The effect of simulation-based training on problem-solving skills, critical thinking skills, and self-efficacy among nursing students in Vietnam: a before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.24

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Component-level paired-sample t-tests within the same 173-student study. The article reports "a significant difference in problem-solving confidence" (t172=3.33, P=0.001) and states that among the 3 components, a significant difference was only observed in problem-solving confidence.
