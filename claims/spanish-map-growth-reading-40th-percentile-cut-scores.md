# Spanish MAP Growth Reading universal screening cut scores correspond to the 40th percentile of Spanish norms, established by linking to English cut scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Spanish reading cut scores were derived by equipercentile linking of Spanish and English MAP Growth Reading scores for a small Grade 3 group, placing the Spanish benchmarks at the 40th percentile of Spanish norms. [→ He 2021](#he-2021)

## Evidence

### He 2021

He, W., & Meyer, P. (2021). MAP Growth universal screening benchmarks: Establishing MAP Growth as an effective universal screener. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A linking study with a small group of Grade 3 students who took both the Spanish pilot test and English Reading test in Spring 2019 used the equipercentile procedure; the derived Spanish cut scores appear in Table 3.1 at the 40th percentile for all grades and terms.

> "the Spanish reading score corresponding to the English Grade 3 spring cut score was at the 40th percentile of the Spanish MAP Growth Reading norms. Using this percentile, cut scores for the other grades and terms were identified."
