## Evidence

### van der Gulden R 2020

van der Gulden R, Heeneman S, Kramer AWM, Laan RFJM, Scherpbier-de Haan ND, Thoonen BPA. (2020). How is self-regulated learning documented in e-portfolios of trainees? A content analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02114-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Content analysis of 90 e-portfolios rated against Zimmerman's goal-setting criteria. "Learning goals and plans were specific in less than half (44,0%) of the e-portfolios"; challenging goals in 97.8% and congruent goals in 87.8% of e-portfolios.

> "Learning goals and plans were specific in less than half (44,0%) of the e-portfolios, mainly because motives for the learning goals (‘why’) and a time or place indication (‘when’ and ‘where’) were missing."
