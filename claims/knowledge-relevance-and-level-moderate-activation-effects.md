`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Wetzels et al. (2011b), a comparison of written open-ended mobilization to perspective taking. The authors explained mobilization lets students freely activate loosely connected concepts, whereas perspective taking requires activating a particular schema.

> "They found that open-ended mobilization was more beneficial for high school students presumed to have lower topic knowledge, compared to undergraduate students assumed to have higher topic knowledge."
