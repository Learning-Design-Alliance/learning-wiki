## Evidence

### Sela Y 2025

Sela Y, Cassuto M, Capua T, Dadbaeiv L, Molad J, Hallevi H, Gadoth A, Sadan O, Seyman E. (2025). Acute neurology simulation-based training boosts confidence and reduces anxiety in novice neurology residents and nursing staff. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07948-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on scenario-specific self-readiness items in the pre-post SBME cohort; the smallest change was for acute neurological deficit (z=-2.9, p = 0.004). No effect sizes are printed.

> "Additionally, post-intervention self-readiness levels to manage common ED emergency neurology scenarios including: Acute Coma, acute neurological deficit, status epilepticus, acute CNS infection, and acute confusional state, all showed significant increases (z=-5.4, p < 0.001, z=-2.9, p = 0.004, z=-5.96, p < 0.001, z=-5.11, p < 0.001, z=-5.12, p < 0.001, respectively)."
