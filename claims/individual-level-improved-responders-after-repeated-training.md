## Evidence

### Nina Franiatte 2024

Nina Franiatte, Esther Boissin, Alexandra Delmas, Wim De Neys. (2024). Boosting Debiasing: Impact of Repeated Training on Reasoning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Individual-level direction-of-change classification (Raoelison & De Neys, 2019; categories from Boissin et al., 2021, 2022) of all 120 Session 1 reasoners into biased, correct, improved, and other categories.

> "They amounted to 11.8% of reasoners in the control group and 47.9% in the training group."

### Nina Franiatte 2024 (2)

Nina Franiatte, Esther Boissin, Alexandra Delmas, Wim De Neys. (2024). Boosting Debiasing: Impact of Repeated Training on Reasoning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Session 2 individual-level classification: 70.9% of the training group were correct responders giving majority "11" patterns (vs 27.4% in control), improved reasoners rose 15.1% (vs 4.8% spontaneous improvement in control), and only 12.1% remained biased.

> "Finally, in the training group there only remained 12.1% biased responders (vs  66.1% in the control group"
