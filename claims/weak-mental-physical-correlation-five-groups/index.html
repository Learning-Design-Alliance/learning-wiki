Evidence¶

Woolley 1915¶

Woolley, Helen Thompson. (1915). A new scale of mental and physical measurements for adolescents, and some of its uses. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(9), 521-550. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075644

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of the fourteen- and fifteen-year composite scores of the working-child sample, showing mental and manual ability correspond only loosely. The authors use this to distinguish five rough groups: poor, medium, and good in both, plus high-manual/low-mental and high-mental/low-manual groups.

"There is a positive correlation between rank in mental tests and rank in physical tests, but it is small. The Pearson co-efficient is .21 P. E. .064 at fourteen and .33 P. E. .060 at 15 years."
