## Evidence

### Casey W. Miller 2019

Casey W. Miller, Benjamin M. Zwickl, Julie R. Posselt, Rachel T. Silvestrini, Theodore Hodapp. (2019). Typical Physics PhD Admissions Criteria Limit Access to Underrepresented Groups but Fail to Predict Doctoral Completion. Draft preprint; journal version in Science Advances. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1126/sciadv.aat7550](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aat7550)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null result across all four logistic regression samples. The article prints the All Students logit coefficient as -0.001 ± 0.002 and states this counters "a myth has propagated in the physics community that GRE -V is a good predictor of completion"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "GRE-V scores were not associated with physics PhD completion in any model, and were consistently the weakest predictor among the admissions criteria in the model."
