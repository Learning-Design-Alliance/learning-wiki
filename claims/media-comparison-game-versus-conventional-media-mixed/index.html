Evidence¶

Mayer 2014¶

Mayer, Richard E. (2014). Computer Games for Learning: An Evidence-Based Approach. The MIT Press. http://direct.mit.edu/books/monograph/4028/bookpreview-pdf/2362507

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Media comparison study from the author's lab comparing learning from the Design-a-Plant game against the same material presented as frames with printed text and graphics. The game group showed "better transfer test performance". No effect size is printed in the text.

"we found better transfer test performance from students who learned by playing the Design-a-Plant game than from students who learned the same material presented as a series of frames with printed text and graphics"

Mayer 2014 (2)¶

Mayer, Richard E. (2014). Computer Games for Learning: An Evidence-Based Approach. The MIT Press. http://direct.mit.edu/books/monograph/4028/bookpreview-pdf/2362507

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Media comparison studies of games with more complex and intrusive narrative themes, Crystal Island and Cache 17, found the slideshow groups outperformed the game groups on transfer posttests. No effect sizes are printed in the text.

"students performed worse on a transfer posttest after playing Crystal Island, about the spread of disease, or Cache 17 than from slideshows of the same material featured in each game"
