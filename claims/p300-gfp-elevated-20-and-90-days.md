## Evidence

### Linlin Dong 2025

Linlin Dong, Yufeng Ke, Xiaodong Zhu, Shuang Liu & Dong Ming. (2025). Long-term cognitive and neurophysiological effects of mental rotation training. npj Science of Learning 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00309-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Point-by-point and averaged P300 GFP analyses over 200–300 ms in pooled participants. The article reports "a signiﬁcant increase in P300 GFP aftertraining," that the increase was "even more pronounced during the post2-test compared to the post1-test," and that GFP "remained signiﬁcantly elevated compared to the post1-test" at post3.

> "Signiﬁcant main effects of Time on the averaged P300 GFP were demonstrated by bootstrap-based one-way RM-ANOVAs (75°: F(3, 99) = 8.93, P = 0.005, partial η2 = 0.213; 150°: F(3, 99) = 16.39, P < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.332)"
