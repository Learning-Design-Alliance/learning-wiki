## Evidence

### Riggs C. Daniel 2020

Riggs C. Daniel, Kang Sohee, Rennie Olivia. (2020). Positive Impact of Multiple-Choice Question Authoring and Regular Quiz Participation on Student Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education 19(2):ar16. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-09-0189

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive plotting of median daily attempts sorted by final course grade category (Figure 3, 2018 term; similar 2017 pattern unpublished). The article reports the "F" students "attempted the most questions" and that A–C students' test practice increased for later exams.

> "Surprisingly, the “F” students attempted the most questions, followed by the “A” students, with “B,” “C,” and “D” students exhibiting similar but lower engagement levels."
