`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Table 3 learning-channel item (p = 0.001) in the survey of 97 postgraduates: journals 19(45.2%) academic vs 6(10.9%) professional; internet 17(30.9%) professional vs 4(9.5%) academic; textbooks 45(46.4%) overall.

> "Beyond textbooks, academic graduate students preferred to learn about osteoporosis from journals, and professional graduate students tended to learn about it from the internet."
