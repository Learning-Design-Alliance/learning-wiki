## Evidence

### YANGÜEZ MARC 2021

YANGÜEZ MARC, BEDIOU BENOIT, HILLMAN CHARLES H., BAVELIER DAPHNE, CHANAL JULIEN. (2021). The Indirect Role of Executive Functions on the Relationship between Cardiorespiratory Fitness and School Grades. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002630

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple mediation SEM for the grammar, spelling, and vocabulary outcome in the same child sample. The article reports the "global indirect effect through executive function components (β = 0.12, SE = 0.03, P < 0.001)".

> "the total effect on grammar, spelling, and vocabulary was significant (β = 0.23, SE = 0.09, P = 0.007, R2 = 0.26), which was explained by the global indirect effect through executive function components (β = 0.12, SE = 0.03, P < 0.001)."
