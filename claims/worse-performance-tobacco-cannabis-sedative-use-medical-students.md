Mann–Whitney comparisons of the academic performance coefficient between users and non-users (Table 3) in this cross-sectional pilot study support the reported "lower achievement coefficient in the course" with previous use of tobacco, marijuana, amphetamines or ecstasy and hypnotics or sedatives; no effect size is printed.
