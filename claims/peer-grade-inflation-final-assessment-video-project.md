## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the General Physics 2 video project, peers skewed grades upward when they recognized the grade as final, indicating training and a clear rubric are necessary for peer review to work as an assessment tool. [→ Graul 2020](#graul-2020)

## Evidence

### Graul 2020

Graul, B., Rollins, M. L., Powers, N., & Della Corte, D. (2020). Student created video content for assessment, experiential learning, and service. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2010.14632](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2010.14632)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of peer and TA grades in the 344-student video project. The authors report that "peers skew the grades when they recognize the grade they are giving as final", based on Figure 1-a correlation analysis of student versus TA grades; no effect size is printed.

> "As shown i n Figure 1-a, peers skew the grades when they recognize the grade they are giving as final. This suggests that training and a clear rubric are necessary for peer reviews to be properly used as an assessment tool."
