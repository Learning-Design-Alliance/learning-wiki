`q3 i1` Meta-analysis of controlled pharmacy education studies found PBL students performed better on midterm examinations (OR = 1.46; 95% CI 1.16–1.89). [→ Galvao Tais F. 2014](#galvao-tais-f-2014)

## Evidence

### Galvao Tais F. 2014

Galvao Tais F., Silva Marcus T., Neiva Celiane S., Ribeiro Laura M., Pereira Mauricio G. (2014). Problem-Based Learning in Pharmaceutical Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3950357/

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Systematic review with meta-analysis of five controlled studies comparing PBL to traditional instruction in graduate and undergraduate pharmacy courses, using a random-effects model. The pooled midterm result favors PBL: "OR = 1.46; IC 95%: 1.16–1.89".

> "The OR estimates were also statistically significant for the midterm exams (OR = 1.46; IC 95%: 1.16–1.89) and for the final exams (OR = 1.60; IC 95%: 1.06–2.43), which indicates a better performance by PBL students compared with those participating in traditional learning methods."
