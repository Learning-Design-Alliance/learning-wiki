Hensley Lauren, Kulesza Amy, Peri Joshua, Brady Anna C., Wolters Christopher A., Sovic David, Breitenberger Caroline. (2021). Supporting Undergraduate Biology Students' Academic Success: Comparing Two Workshop Interventions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-03-0068

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports, citing Credé and Phillips (2011), a meta-analytic association between metacognitive self-regulation and college grades; this is background literature, not a finding tested in the present study. No effect size is printed.

> "Examining studies that together included nearly 20,000 college students, a meta-analysis revealed that metacognitive self-regulation was positively associated with course grade and overall grade point average (GPA), as well as with indicators of academic engagement such as intrinsic motivation and elaborative learning strategies"
