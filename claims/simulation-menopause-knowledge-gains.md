Pre/post comparison of a 6-question knowledge test among 34 residents who completed a didactic session plus three standardized patient encounters. The article reports a "25% increase" with the paired t test result quoted; average presession score was 44.11% and postsession 69.12%.

> "Overall, knowledge increased by 1.5 points out of 6 (95% CI, 1.0–1.93; P < .0001), representing a 25% increase."
