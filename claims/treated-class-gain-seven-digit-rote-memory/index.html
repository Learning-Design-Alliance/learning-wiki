Evidence¶

Emma Kohnky 1913¶

Emma Kohnky, M.A. (1913). Preliminary Study of the Effect of Dental Treatment upon the Physical and Mental Efficiency of School Children. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Results table for the seven-digit rote memory series (Whipple's Test 38), scored in percents for correct digits and order. The treated class (Room 21) moved from 74.9% to 85.7%; the untreated class stayed at 71.4%. Descriptive percents only; no inferential statistic is printed.

"Room 21 leads at the beginning by 3.5% and by 14.3% in the second group, finishing with a gain of 10.8% over its first record as against no gain in the other class."
