## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Across all three interaction categories, students participate more when they were correct in the solo phase; in MA interactions the difference was significant with a negligible effect size (d = .17). [→ Joss Ives 2016](#joss-ives-2016)

## Evidence

### Joss Ives 2016

Joss Ives, Matias de Jong Van Lier, Nutifafa Kwaku Sumah, and Jared B. Stang. (2016). Examining Student Participation in Two-Phase Collaborative Exams through Video Analysis. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.038](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.038)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Video-coded participation comparison of students correct vs incorrect in the solo phase (Figure 2). In MA interactions means were 2.61 (correct) vs 2.39 (incorrect), "differed signiﬁcantly" with "an effect size of d = .17, calculated using Cliff's delta".

> "the mean participation scores in the MA interaction category were 2.61 (correct) and 2.39 (incorrect); the distributions in the two groups differed signiﬁcantly (Mann-Whitney U = 50456, ncorrect = 290, nincorrect = 297, p = 2 .4 × 10−5) with an effect size of d = .17"
