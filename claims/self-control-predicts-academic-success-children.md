## Evidence

### Tim Schulz van Endert 2021

Tim Schulz van Endert. (2021). Addictive use of digital devices in young children: Associations with delay discounting, self-control and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253058

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational and regression analysis of self-control versus most recent semester grade average in 75 children aged 10–13 from a Berlin elementary school. "self-control was also correlated to academic success (r = -0.31 p = 0.007)".

> "Furthermore, self-control was also correlated to academic success (r = -0.31 p = 0.007). Again, to take into account possible effects of gender, age, years of ownership of digital devices and pocket money a regression analysis with the latter variables, self-control and self-reported usage predicting grade average was performed."
