## Evidence

### Campos MEC 2022

Campos MEC, Monteiro MVC, Kakehasi FM. (2022). Residency training for minimally invasive surgery. Revista do Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões. https://doi.org/10.1590/0100-6991e-20213040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kirkpatrick-based questionnaire completed by participants at program end with five-point Likert self-assessment items; descriptive percentages only, no statistical test reported for the pre-post shift in this quote.

> "After training, self-assessments of knowledge/skills in laparoscopy changed to poor (1.8%), fair (3.6%), good (51.8%), very good (41.1%), and excellent (1.8%)."

### Campos MEC 2022 (2)

Campos MEC, Monteiro MVC, Kakehasi FM. (2022). Residency training for minimally invasive surgery. Revista do Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões. https://doi.org/10.1590/0100-6991e-20213040

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive Likert questionnaire results from the same end-of-program survey of the 43 residents; the authors link the insecurity to the lack of in vivo training. No inferential test printed.

> "only 10.7% completely agreed that, after training, they became capable of carrying out a low complexity laparoscopic procedure, while 75% partially agreed, 10.7% partially disagreed, and 3.6% totally disagreed."
