## Evidence

### Dong Xue 2026

Dong Xue, Chen Yihong, Tan Xin, Zeng Xi, Guo Tao. (2026). A feasibility and preliminary effectiveness study of a tiered gynecological laparoscopic training curriculum in southwestern China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09652-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional satisfaction survey of 112 enrolled gynecologists (junior n=34, intermediate n=47, senior n=31) using a 5-point Likert scale; the study reports "no statistically significant differences" between the three levels for the five satisfaction dimensions and total satisfaction.

> "Kruskal–Wallis test results found no statistically significant differences regarding satisfaction with the training between the three levels of participants for Usefulness, Productivity, Reasonableness, Prospective utility and Total satisfaction (Table 2)."
