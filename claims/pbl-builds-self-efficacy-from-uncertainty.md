Gupta Nidhi, Sunil Sruthi, Ali Kamran, Fink Trine, Du Xiangyun. (2026). Learner agency in a problem-based learning curriculum: A qualitative study on perspectives of undergraduate dental students. PLOS One. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13020853/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group thematic analysis; participants recalled feeling overwhelmed during initial PBL sessions yet noted that "working through PBL projects not only boosted their confidence but also deepened their understanding of professional knowledge."

> "Participants described their journey in PBL as one that often began with uncertainty but gradually evolved into a stronger belief in their ability to learn independently."
