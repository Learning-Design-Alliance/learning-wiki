## Evidence

### Ali-Masri Hadil 2018

Ali-Masri Hadil, Hassan Sahar, Fosse Erik, Zimmo Kaled M., Zimmo Mohammed, Ismail Khaled M. K., Vikanes Åse, Laine Katariina. (2018). Impact of electronic and blended learning programs for manual perineal support on incidence of obstetric anal sphincter injuries: a prospective interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1363-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stratified logistic regression among parous women (181 in phase-1, 347 in phase-2, 436 in phase-3), adjusted for episiotomy, BMI and second-stage duration. OASIS fell from 8.3% to 2.0% then 1.1% across phases; both "training-methods had an effect on the odds for OASIS".

> "Both training-methods had an effect on the odds for OASIS; training-method 1 (aOR: 0.18, CI; 0.07–0.49, p = 0.001) and training-method 2 (aOR: 0.11, CI; 0.04–0.32, p < 0.001)."

### Ali-Masri Hadil 2018 (2)

Ali-Masri Hadil, Hassan Sahar, Fosse Erik, Zimmo Kaled M., Zimmo Mohammed, Ismail Khaled M. K., Vikanes Åse, Laine Katariina. (2018). Impact of electronic and blended learning programs for manual perineal support on incidence of obstetric anal sphincter injuries: a prospective interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1363-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stratified analysis among primiparous women (195 phase-1, 279 phase-2, 256 phase-3). The e-learning-only reduction was "not statistically significant after adjusting" (aOR 0.81, p = 0.465); blended learning was significant (aOR 0.39, CI 0.21–0.74, p = 0.004).

> "Although OASIS incidence was reduced following training-method 1 (12.5% (35/279) vs 15.9% (31/195)), this reduction was not statistically significant after adjusting for the identified significant risk factors associated with OASIS (vacuum assisted delivery and duration second stage of labor ≥60 min); (aOR:0.81, CI; 0.47–1.4, p = 0.465)."
