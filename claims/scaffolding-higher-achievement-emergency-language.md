## Evidence

### Zhu An 2026

Zhu An, Ye Jian-Hong. (2026). The Impact of Scaffolding on Academic Achievement in Undergraduate Emergency Language Courses. Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-026-10254-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In an 8-week quasi-experiment with 217 valid undergraduates (112 scaffolding, 105 control), the scaffolding group scored higher on the post-test (M = 60.87 vs. 55.59). The article reports "the t-value was -4.942" and Cohen's d = 0.680, confirming H8.

> "The p-value was 0.000 (p < 0.001) and the t-value was -4.942, which proved that the experimental group had a more significant increase in the post-test scores compared to the control group."
