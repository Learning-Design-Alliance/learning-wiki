## Evidence

### Kubo Taro 2022

Kubo Taro, Takayama Tatsuya, Fujisaki Akira, Nakamura Shigeru, Teratani Takumi, Sata Naohiro, Kitayama Joji, Nakai Hideo, Iwami Daiki, Fujimura Tetsuya. (2022). Novel high-quality and reality biomaterial as a kidney surgery simulation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263179

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Perfusion evaluation using saline stained as blood-like fluid delivered via a 70 cm3 water column into the renal artery of NVP-fixed kidneys. After resection and suturing, blood-like fluid flow persisted from the dissected section, supporting partial-nephrectomy-like training.

> "When blood-like fluids flowed into the renal artery of the NVP-fixed kidney, the renal parenchyma turned red evenly over time, confirming the sustenance of the vasculature’s structure in the NVP-fixed kidney and the transport of liquid components in the vascular lumen"

### Kubo Taro 2022 (2)

Kubo Taro, Takayama Tatsuya, Fujisaki Akira, Nakamura Shigeru, Teratani Takumi, Sata Naohiro, Kitayama Joji, Nakai Hideo, Iwami Daiki, Fujimura Tetsuya. (2022). Novel high-quality and reality biomaterial as a kidney surgery simulation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263179

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Electrocautery evaluation of the NVP-fixed kidney compared with living-pig surgery. The article reports the FA-fixed kidney performed poorly in both blood-like fluid flow and electrocautery incision.

> "The incision of the NVP-fixed kidney, using electrocautery was similar to that of living pigs in terms of resistance at the time of incision and thermal degeneration of the cross-section"
