## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Across 15 RCTs with 2172 participants, students taught with CBL scored significantly higher on exams than students in lecture-based groups (Hedges' g = 0.68, 95%CI 0.49–0.88). [→ Wu Fanghui 2023](#wu-fanghui-2023)

## Evidence

### Wu Fanghui 2023

Wu Fanghui, Wang Tao, Yin Danxu, Xu Xiaoxiao, Jin Cancan, Mu Nan, Tan Qingrong. (2023). Application of case-based learning in psychology teaching: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04525-5

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis pooling 15 RCTs (1086 CBL vs 1086 lecture students) using Hedges' g under a random effects model; the article reports "Hedges’ g = 0.68, 95%CI (0.49, 0.88)" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 78.90%).

> "the total effect of CBL yielded better results than lecture-based learning [Hedges’ g = 0.68, 95%CI (0.49, 0.88), p < 0.00]. The heterogeneity was relatively high (I2= 78.90%, p < 0.00), suggesting that a random effects model should be applied."
