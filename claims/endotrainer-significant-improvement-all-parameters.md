## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hernansanz A 2023

Hernansanz A, Rovira R, Basomba J, Comas R, Casals A. (2023). EndoTrainer: a novel hybrid training platform for endoscopic surgery. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-023-02837-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post repeated-trials study of the 30-degree camera navigation exercise: 80 gynaecologists each executed 30 trials in a single dry-lab session with no prior training trials. Learning-curve analysis of initial (1-3) versus final (28-30) trials found statistically significant improvements in all studied criteria; the authors note "More compact interquartiles indicate that the training benefits are valid for all type of users". No effect sizes are printed.

> "The LCs points out that all users have obtained statistically significant improvements in all the studied criteria. More compact interquartiles indicate that the training benefits are valid for all type of users, independently from their previous experience."
