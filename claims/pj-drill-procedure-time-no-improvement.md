### Yoshioka Ryuji 2021

Yoshioka Ryuji, Imamura Hiroshi, Ichida Hirofumi, Gyoda Yu, Mizuno Tomoya, Mise Yoshihiro, Saiura Akio. (2021). Simulation training in pancreatico-jejunostomy using an inanimate biotissue model improves the technical skills of hepatobiliary-pancreatic surgical fellows. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244915

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way repeated-measures ANOVA on procedure time across the five drills in the eight-fellow study found no significant change (P = 0.788); equivalence was not tested. The authors note the large standard deviation decreased during the drills.

> "However, no change in the time taken for completion the procedure was observed with repeated executions of the drill (P = 0.788) (Fig 5)."
