### Tapio Rasa 2022

Tapio Rasa, Elina Palmgren, Antti Laherto. (2022). Futurising science education: students' experiences from a course on futures thinking and quantum computing. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09572-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phenomenographic content analysis of post-course semi-structured interviews with 21 of the 22 course participants. The theme appeared in 52% of interviews; students "compare their pre-course and current views" reporting more hope or reduced fears.

> "A frequently identified theme is 1A More optimistic or hopeful outlook on the future (52%), in which students compare their pre-course and current views and find either a generally more optimistic outlook or mitigation of their previous fears"
