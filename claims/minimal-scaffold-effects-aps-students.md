## Evidence

### Sarah I. Hofer 2025

Sarah I. Hofer, Frank Reinhold. (2025). Scaffolding of learning activities: Aptitude-treatment-interaction effects in math?. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102177. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102177

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

GLMM interaction analyses in the APS subgroup (192 of 332 students) showed no notable scaffold-by-characteristic interactions; the article attributes this to the intervention being more challenging for VS students, who had greater potential to benefit from scaffolding.

> "To summarize, variation both in learner characteristics and in experimental conditions within the APS subgroup had little impact on knowledge about fractions on the number line (Fig. 6)."
