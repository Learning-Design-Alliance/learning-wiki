`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analyses across the 71-effect-size data set found no statistically significant differences for academic area, cultural context, publication bias status, research quality, report type, or publication year; equivalence was not tested. Only school level and characteristic type were significant moderators.

> "Finally, the distribution of effect sizes did not differ significantly in terms of academic area, cultural context, publication bias status, research quality, research report type, and publication year variables (see Table 2, p = 0.09, p = 0.23, p = 0.32, p = 0.18, p = 0.74, and p = 195, respectively)."
