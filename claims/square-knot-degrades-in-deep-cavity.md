## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A statistically significant within-group difference in burst pressure between flat surface and deep cavity was found for the square knot (p = 0.03) but not for the RHAP knot (p = 0.77). [→ Sykes EA 2021](#sykes-ea-2021)

## Evidence

### Sykes EA 2021

Sykes EA, Lemke M, Potter D, Li T, Mir ZM, Sheahan G, Wu V, Zevin B. (2021). Evaluating bowel enterotomy closures in simulated deep body cavities using the reversing half-hitch alternating post and square knots: a randomized controlled trial. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.016719

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group comparisons across surfaces were reported for the 20 randomized novices. Square-knot burst pressure differed significantly by surface (p = 0.03); RHAP repairs showed no statistically significant within-group difference (p = 0.77); no effect sizes were printed.

> "There was a statistically significant within-group difference in burst pressure for enterotomy repair using a square knot on a flat surface and a deep body cavity (p = 0.03). There was no statistically significant within-group difference between the 2 surfaces for enterotomy repairs using the RHAP knot (p = 0.77)"
