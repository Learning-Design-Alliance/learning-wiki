### Shiri 2015

Saeed Shiri. (2015). The Application of Podcasting as a Motivational Strategy to Iranian EFL Learners of English: A View toward Listening Comprehension. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.3p.155

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative thematic analysis of individual semi-structured interviews with four high-scoring and four low-scoring learners, transcribed and analyzed inductively. Learners also valued podcasts for home practice; perceptions are self-reported, not tested.

> "The students unanimously perceived improvement in their listening comprehension ability. They believed they were able to learn a lot of new vocabulary and they have now sharper ears for spoken language."
