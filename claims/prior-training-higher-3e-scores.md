`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison within the 59-participant study using the validated LLM judge. Those with prior training "had higher overall 3E scores than those without prior experience", which the authors read as evidence the assessor captures variation related to training experience.

> "Participants with previous communication-training experience (n= 16) had higher overall 3E scores than those without prior experience (n= 43) (0.468 versus 0.426; one-sidedp= 0.013; Fig. 5a)."
