## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants' perceptions of being graded on critique quality positively affect both evaluation accuracy and critique quality; perceptions of being graded only on evaluation accuracy do not significantly affect either. [→ Babik 2024](#babik-2024)

## Evidence

### Babik 2024

Babik, D., Gehringer, E., Kidd, J., Sunday, K., Tinapple, D., & Gilbert, S. (2024). A systematic review of educational online peer-review and assessment systems: charting the landscape. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10349-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

An experiment in Peerceptiv/SWoRD reported by Patchan et al. (2017) and reviewed in this article found that perceptions of accountability for critique quality positively affected "evaluation accuracy", while accountability for evaluation accuracy alone did not. No effect size is printed.

> "Both types of participants' perceptions about being held accountable (i) and (iii) positively affect evaluation accuracy; at the same time, participants' perceptions of (ii) does not significantly affect evaluation accuracy"
