## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xu Yun 2022

Xu Yun, Liang Yongqi, Ye Hui, Xu Yue. (2022). Literature review of the research on nursing students' professional self-concept. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2153396

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of 17 effect surveys treating professional self-concept as result, dependent or intermediary variable. Examples include a survey of 1083 nursing college students finding a positive professional self-concept could reduce academic burnout. No pooled effect sizes are printed.

> "This paper described 17 effect surveys and found that professional self-concept had an important impact on students' mental health, academic performance, and professional values, and so on."

### Xu Yun 2022 (2)

Xu Yun, Liang Yongqi, Ye Hui, Xu Yue. (2022). Literature review of the research on nursing students' professional self-concept. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2153396

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports negative conclusions from studies by Eskandari et al. (2019) in Iran and Nikolina et al. (2020) in Croatia, where professional self-concept showed no correlation with two ability outcomes. Null associations limit the generality of the effect claims.

> "The results showed that professional self-concept did not correlate with students' psychiatric nursing ability and clinical decision-making ability [46,47]."
