## Evidence

### Ricci A 2025

Ricci A, Minearo I, Hielscher A. (2025). Focused anatomy workshops for clerkships and the USMLE Step 1 examination. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2536

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Within-session pre/post comparison across five optional workshops (brachial plexus, MSK, pelvic, GI, head and neck) using identical quizzes; the article reports "significantly higher compared to the pre‐session quizzes in all five workshops" with p ≤ 0.05.

> "The mean scores on the post‐session quizzes were significantly higher compared to the pre‐session quizzes in all five workshops."

### Ricci A 2025 (2)

Ricci A, Minearo I, Hielscher A. (2025). Focused anatomy workshops for clerkships and the USMLE Step 1 examination. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.2536

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis plan as printed: "Two‐tailed paired t‐tests" compared pre- and post-session quiz means, with r2 effect sizes and 95%CI reported for each pair; p ≤ 0.05 was the significance threshold.

> "Two‐tailed paired t‐tests were used to determine if there was a statistically significant difference between the mean scores of the pre‐ and post‐session quizzes. Effect sizes (r 2) and 95% confidence intervals (95%CI) are reported for each pair."
