`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Offline clinical skill test written medical records scores in the three-group RCT, analyzed by one-way ANOVA (F = 6.17, P = 0.003). The article prints group means, SDs, and pairwise p-values; the note about no FSP–TT difference appears despite the printed Pa value, an inconsistency in the article.

> "The scores of the FSP-TCM and OSP-TCM groups were better than that of the TT group (TT: mean 78.77 SD 4.61; FSP-TCM: mean 81.93 SD 3.90, OSP-TCM: mean 82.20 SD 4.09, Pa = 0.01, Pb = 0.006, Pc = 0.97) but no significant difference between the FSP-TCM and TT groups was found."
