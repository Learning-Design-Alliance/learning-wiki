Regression Model 2 in the same cross-sectional survey of 276 university students. Teacher support was "signiﬁcantly and negatively associated with AI dependency" with β = −0.217, R2 = 0.047, f 2 = 0.049, a small effect by Cohen's criteria.

"Teacher support was also signiﬁcantly and negatively associated with AI dependency ( β = −0.217, p < 0.001, 95% CI [ −0.337, −0.102]), accounting for 4.7% of the variance ( R2 = 0.047, f 2 = 0.049), supporting Hypothesis 2."
