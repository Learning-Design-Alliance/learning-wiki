Evidence¶

Spénard Sarah 2022¶

Spénard Sarah, Postlow Fabiana, Curran Vernon. (2022). E-learning use in the review of neonatal resuscitation program in physicians: a scoping review. Journal of Perinatology. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-022-01411-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

In the randomized pretest-posttest control group study of the ANAKIN computerized simulator (N = 60 medical students), knowledge deteriorated significantly and similarly in both groups at 4 months; skill retention also significantly decreased over the 8-month period (P < 0.05).

"Curran et al. showed that knowledge levels deteriorated significantly and similarly in the two groups 4 months after an NRP workshop."

Spénard Sarah 2022 (2)¶

Spénard Sarah, Postlow Fabiana, Curran Vernon. (2022). E-learning use in the review of neonatal resuscitation program in physicians: a scoping review. Journal of Perinatology. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-022-01411-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

In the randomized controlled trial of booster modalities (N = 44 family medicine residents), average written knowledge score fell from 223/239 (93%; SD = 10%) to 191/239 (80%; SD = 16%) at 6-8 months, with follow-up scores significantly lower (P < 0.0001) in all three groups.

"The follow-up test scores were significantly lower (P < 0.0001) in three groups, despite exposure to a video or hands-on booster in two groups."
