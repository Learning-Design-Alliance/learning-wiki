Andrews 2011¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVAs on modified Fishman categories in the eight-week randomized experiment; the Results section also reports significant differences for concurrent (F = 9.218, p < .01), terminal (F = 13.650, p < .001), evaluative (F = 20.989, p < .001), explicative (F = 9.876, p < .01), affective positive (F = 7.983, p < .01), whole, part, outcome and space subcategories.

"Subcategories of the Form category were auditory ( F = 21.260, p < .001), auditory visual ( F = 5.297, p < .05), and auditory, tactile and visual ( F = 4.672, p < .05)."
