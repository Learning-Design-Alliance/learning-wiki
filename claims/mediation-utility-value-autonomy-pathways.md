# Effects on maintained situational interest and resource requests are mediated by perceived utility value (utility value manipulation) and perceived autonomy and triggered interest (choice manipulation)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Perceived utility value mediated effects of the utility value manipulation on maintained situational interest (b = 0.16, p < .001) and resource requests (b = 0.03, p < .001). [→ Asher 2025](#asher-2025)
`q3 i?` The choice manipulation promoted maintained situational interest via perceived autonomy (b = 0.29, p < .001) and triggered situational interest (b = 0.06, p = .009). [→ Asher 2025 (2)](#asher-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Asher 2025

Asher, M. W., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2025). Using Choice and Utility Value to Promote Interest: Stimulating Situational Interest in a Lesson and Fostering the Development of Interest in Statistics. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000921

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-stage bootstrapped path analysis (lavaan in each study, then meta-analyzed indirect effects) across Studies 1–7 found "perceived utility value mediated effects of the utility value manipulation" on both maintained situational interest and resource requests.

> "we found evidence that perceived utility value mediated effects of the utility value manipulation on maintained situational interest in regression, b = 0.16, p < .001, and resource requests, our behavioral indicator of emerging individual interest, b = 0.03, p < .001."

### Asher 2025 (2)

Asher, M. W., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2025). Using Choice and Utility Value to Promote Interest: Stimulating Situational Interest in a Lesson and Fostering the Development of Interest in Statistics. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000921

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Meta-analyzed indirect effects showed "the choice manipulation may have promoted maintained situational interest in regression via two different mediators: perceived autonomy" and triggered situational interest during the lesson.

> "the choice manipulation may have promoted maintained situational interest in regression via two different mediators: perceived autonomy, b = 0.29, p < .001, and triggered situational interest during the lesson, b = 0.06, p = .009."
