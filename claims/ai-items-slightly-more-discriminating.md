# AI-generated questions showed slightly higher average discrimination than standardized test questions (αAI = 1.3 vs αSTD = 1.2), with moderate evidence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` AI-generated questions averaged slightly higher IRT discrimination than standardized test items; the posterior difference favors AI items with Pr(δα > 0) ≈ 0.85, and both types averaged 'moderate' discriminating power. [→ Calvin Isley 2025](#calvin-isley-2025)

## Evidence

IRT discrimination estimates from the same field-study 2PL model, comparing AI-generated and standardized items; the article reports E[δα] = 0.09 with 95% credible interval [−.0918, .253] and Pr(δα > 0) ≈ 0.85, calling this moderate evidence.

> "The AI-generated questions had an average discrimination of ¯αAI = 1.3 across items. In contrast, standardized test questions were slightly less discriminating, with an average of ¯αSTD = 1.2."
