# Several RECALL effect sizes in the Xie et al. (2018) meta-analysis could not be reproduced, and one reported value (−0.639) is inconsistent with the primary study's data

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Recalculated SMDs for Kuehl et al. (2014a) RECALL discrepant from the meta-analysis's reported values could only be recreated by combining RECALL with PICTORIAL MATCHING outcomes. [→ Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021](#sophia-c-weissgerber-2021)
`q2 i?` The reported RECALL effect size of −0.639 for Eitel et al. (2014) text-only conditions could not be reproduced in multiple attempts; the primary data indicate an effect size of about −0.14. [→ Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021 (2)](#sophia-c-weissgerber-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021

Sophia C. Weissgerber, Matthias Brunmair & Ralf Rummer. (2021). Null and Void? Errors in Meta-analysis on Perceptual Disfluency and Recommendations to Improve Meta-analytical Reproducibility. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09579-1

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Recalculation of standardized mean differences from the primary studies' reported means and standard errors yielded discrepant values; the reported values "could only be recreated when combining RECALL outcomes with PICTORIAL MATCHING outcomes" for each (sub-)group.

> "when using the same approach and formula to calculate the SMD for the two (sub-)groups in Kuehl et al. (2014a) for RECALL, we surprisingly obtained discrepant values as those reported by Xie and colleagues in Figure 2"

### Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021 (2)

Sophia C. Weissgerber, Matthias Brunmair & Ralf Rummer. (2021). Null and Void? Errors in Meta-analysis on Perceptual Disfluency and Recommendations to Improve Meta-analytical Reproducibility. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09579-1

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

For Eitel et al. (2014) text-only conditions, the commentary reports the original values "indicate merely a small negative disfluency effect" and that applying Xie et al.'s approach "the effect size should be −0.14".

> "The discrepancy could not be resolved in multiple attempts, so we do not know how Xie et al. (2018) obtained the effect size of −0.639, which they report in Figure 2 for RECALL."
