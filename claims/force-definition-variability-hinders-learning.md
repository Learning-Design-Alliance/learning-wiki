## Evidence

### Lars Rikard Stavrum 2015

Lars Rikard Stavrum, Berit Bungum, Jonas R. Persson. (2015). “Never at rest”: developing a conceptual framework for definitions of 'force' in physics textbooks. Programme for Teacher Education, Norwegian University of Science and Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.06562](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.06562)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors' interpretation (type e), not a tested result: the case analysis of RST 1 showed definitions of force vary within one book, often without students being made aware of the variability. The article states this "may in itself be a source of the conceptual problems" students have, and may cause students to stick to intuitive everyday conceptions.

> "This may in itself be a source of the conceptual problems that many students have with understanding and using the concept appropriate in describing and solving physics problems."
