## Evidence

### Rau 2014

Rau, M. A., Aleven, V., Rummel, N., & Pardos, Z. (2014). How Should Intelligent Tutoring Systems Sequence Multiple Graphical Representations of Fractions? A Multi-Methods Study. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0011-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

In a classroom experiment with 599 4th- and 5th-graders using the Fractions Tutor as supplemental instruction, students improved significantly with "a medium effect size of d=0.40 at the posttest" and retained gains with "an effect size of d=0.60" a week later.

> "After 10 h of instruction with the Fractions Tutor, students improved significantly with a medium effect size of d=0.40 at the posttest ( p<0.01). When we administered a delayed posttest a week later, we found that students retained these learning gains with an effect size of d=0.60 (p<0.01)."
