## Evidence

### Ulfa Yunefit 2021

Ulfa Yunefit, Igarashi Yukari, Takahata Kaori, Shishido Eri, Horiuchi Shigeko. (2021). A comparison of team-based learning and lecture-based learning on clinical reasoning and classroom engagement: a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02881-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis using knowledge score data from the authors' previous quasi-experimental study correlated knowledge with clinical reasoning in this trial's sample. The correlations r = .488 and r = .522 are moderate-to-strong, but the design is correlational and cannot establish causality.

> "the knowledge and clinical reasoning on PPH scores showed a significant correlation at post-test (r = .488, p = .000) and at 2 weeks post-test (r = .522, p = .000)"
