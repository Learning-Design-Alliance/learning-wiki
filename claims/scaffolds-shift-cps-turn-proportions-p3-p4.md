# Scaffolded ill-structured tasks elicit a higher proportion of solution-evaluation (P4) turns and a lower proportion of problem-solving (P3) turns than non-scaffolded tasks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Groups showed a significantly higher mean proportion of P4 turns and lower proportion of P3 turns on the scaffolded task compared to the non-scaffolded task. [→ Parks 2026](#parks-2026)

## Evidence

### Parks 2026

Parks, T., Shehab, S., & Mercier, E. (2026). Investigating Students' Collaborative Problem-Solving Interactions during Scaffolded and Non-Scaffolded Ill-Structured Tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09816-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA across 11 undergraduate engineering groups comparing turn proportions on scaffolded vs. non-scaffolded tasks. Mean P3 proportion was 0.768 (non-scaffolded) vs. 0.586 (scaffolded); mean P4 was 0.070 vs. 0.221. The article reports "a main effect of task on the proportion of P3" with η2 = 0.644 and P4 with η2 = 0.516.

> "A repeated measures analysis of variance indicated a main effect of task on the proportion of P3, F(1,10) = 18.12, p =.002, η2 = 0.644, and the proportion of P4, F(1,10) = 10.67, p =.008, η2 = 0.516."
