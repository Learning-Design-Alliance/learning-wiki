Nguyen Phuong Bao Tran 2025¶

Nguyen Phuong Bao Tran, Tran Ngoc Bao Chau, Nguyen Minh Tan. (2025). Music-mediated Pedagogy to Boost Efl Students' Listening Engagement and Fluency in Emi Contexts: A Scoping Review (2015–2025). F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.170296.1

Thematic synthesis of the digital-platform studies (Spotify, LyricsTraining, YouTube); the review attributes effects partly to "real-time feedback, customizable difficulty, and self-paced practice". No effect sizes printed.

"Studies employing these tools (e.g., Huang & Chen, 2024; Marsela et al., 2024; Sari et al., 2023; Aprilina et al., 2024) consistently reported positive learner perceptions and heightened motivation, with several also documenting measurable improvements in listening skills."
