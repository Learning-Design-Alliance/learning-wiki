Review of meta-analytic research on age and job performance. The review reports that "a meta-analytic estimate of 0.06" with a wide confidence interval, and that little evidence of moderation by performance measure type or job type was found.

> "McEvoy & Cascio (1989) found the correlations between age and performance ranged from −0.44 to +0.66, with a meta-analytic estimate of 0.06 and a 95% confidence interval from −0.18 to +0.30"
