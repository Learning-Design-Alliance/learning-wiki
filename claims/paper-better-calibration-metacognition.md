### Clinton 2019

Virginia Clinton. (2019). Reading from paper compared to screens: A systematic review and metaanalysis. Journal of Research in Reading. https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9817.12269

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of calibration measures across k = 11 studies (n = 698) found more accurate performance judgements from paper. This result is reported in the article's abstract and results summary; the detailed results section was not available in the supplied text, so the printed effect size g = .20 is reported as printed.
