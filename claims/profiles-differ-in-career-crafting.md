## Evidence

### Song Hongfeng 2026

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

BCH auxiliary analyses comparing Wave 3 career outcomes across the four profiles, adjusting for classification uncertainty; the omnibus test for career crafting was significant.

> "The omnibus Wald tests indicated significant profile differences in career crafting, χ2(3) = 52.841, p < 0.001, and self-perceived employability, χ2(3) = 27.734, p < 0.001."

### Song Hongfeng 2026 (2)

Song Hongfeng, Hu Anqi, Li Xueyan. (2026). Beyond Positive or Negative: Latent Profiles of University Students’ AI Attitude and Their Career-Development Correlates. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091460

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

BCH-adjusted pairwise comparisons (Table 6) of career crafting means across profiles; Anxious Acceptance also exceeded Low-Perception Detached.

> "For career crafting, High-Perception High-Vigilance (M = 4.22) and Positive Empowerment (M = 3.91) did not differ significantly, but both reported higher levels than Anxious Acceptance (M = 3.69) and Low-Perception Detached (M = 3.41)."
