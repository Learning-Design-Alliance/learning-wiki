## Evidence

### Cen Shanduo 2025

Cen Shanduo, Zhao Miaomiao, Wang Feng, Tang Lei. (2025). Gender differences in the relationship between mental health and academic performance among undergraduate students at a medical school in Shanghai: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21697-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stratified comparison of SCL-90 indicators by year of study and gender (Table 2), using t-tests/ANOVA. The study reports "total SCL-90 score (134.35 ± 38.03 vs 128.32 ± 37.56, p < 0.01)" for first-year females vs males, and lowest fourth-year scores.

> "First-year female students scored significantly higher than males in multiple dimensions, including total SCL-90 score (134.35 ± 38.03 vs 128.32 ± 37.56, p < 0.01), SOM (1.34 ± 0.39 vs 1.28 ± 0.37, p < 0.001), OCS (1.84 ± 0.58 vs 1.76 ± 0.61, p < 0.01), INTS (1.62 ± 0.57 vs 1.55 ± 0.55, p < 0.01), DEPR (1.52 ± 0.52 vs 1.41 ± 0.50, P < 0.001), ANX (1.50 ± 0.50 vs 1.41 ± 0.47, p < 0.001), and PHOA (1.35 ± 0.43 vs 1.26 ± 0.38, p < 0.001)."
