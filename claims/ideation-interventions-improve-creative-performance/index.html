Gu 2026¶

Gu, X., Ritter, S. M., Frossard, F., van den Boom, L., Delfmann, L. R. & Barajas, M. (2026). Fostering creativity in formal schooling: A systematic review of explicit and implicit interventions for young learners. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100784

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of ideation studies within RQ1. The review reports, citing Levav-Waynberg and Leikin, that multiple-solution geometry tasks enhanced mathematical creativity, and that repeated divergent thinking tasks and formal brainstorming exercises have been shown to improve creative performance. No effect sizes are printed.

"Levav-Waynberg and Leikin (2012), for example, demonstrated that encouraging students to generate multiple solutions to geometry problems enhanced mathematical creativity."
