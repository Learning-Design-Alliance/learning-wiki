## Evidence

### Shravika Mittal 2026

Shravika Mittal, Su Lin Blodgett, Q. Vera Liao. (2026). Learning by Chatting? Investigating the Impact of Generative AI on Information Seeking and Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11669 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.11669](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.11669)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of diary entries (98 ChatGPT, 140 Google) coded with Ellis's model. Narrow starts took the form of personally tailored conversational queries and continuing prior interactions; the article attributes this to the conversational interface invoking a social interaction-oriented schema and to context retention and personalization.

> "participants in the ChatGPT condition were more likely to begin their information-seeking with a narrow or specified query, a behavior observed in32.65%of their entries (nC=32/98) compared to only3.57%of entries from Google participants (nG=5/140)."
