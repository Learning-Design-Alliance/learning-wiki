## Evidence

### Archana Prabu Kumar 2026

Archana Prabu Kumar, Padmavathi R, Maheshkumar K, Abirami Omprakash, Prabu Kumar Chokkalingam Mani, B W C Sathiyasekaran, Maruthy K N. (2026). E-Learning Modules Based on Bloom Taxonomy and the Miller Pyramid for First-Year Indian Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Study in Medical Education. JMIR Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.2196/84339

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 690 first-year MBBS students (control n=370, intervention n=320) assessed via end-of-block internal assessments. The intervention group scored higher at every Bloom level, with "Effect sizes ranged from small to large" across remember, understand, application, and analysis.

> "Effect sizes ranged from small to large: remember (d=0.48; 6.52 vs 5.26; P<.001), understand (d=0.29; 2.92 vs 2.55; P<.001), application (d=0.81; 3.43 vs 2.56; P<.001), and analysis (d=0.48; 3.01 vs 2.53; P<.001)."
