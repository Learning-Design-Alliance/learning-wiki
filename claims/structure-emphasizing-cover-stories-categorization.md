# Structure-emphasizing example sets, using varied cover stories across problem types, lead students to categorize problems by structure rather than surface features

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2`–`r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students studying structure-emphasizing statistical example sets sorted problems by structure more often than surface-emphasizing and no-example peers. [→ Atkinson 2000](#atkinson-2000)
`q2 i?` Structure-emphasizing students correctly selected the appropriate statistical test more frequently than surface-emphasizing peers. [→ Atkinson 2000 (2)](#atkinson-2000-2)

## Evidence

### Atkinson 2000

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Quilici and Mayer, an experiment in which participants studied structure- or surface-emphasizing example sets for three statistical problem types and completed a sort task; structure-emphasizing students 'sorted by structure more often'.

> "they found that the students in the structure-emphasizing condition sorted by structure more often than their counterparts in the surface emphasizing and the no-example conditions, which did not differ significantly from each other."

### Atkinson 2000 (2)

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Quilici and Mayer's follow-up experiment on two problem types with a test-selection posttest, that structure-emphasizing participants 'correctly selected the appropriate test statistically more frequently' than surface-emphasizing peers.

> "The authors found that the participants in the structure-emphasizing condition correctly selected the appropriate test statistically more frequently than their peers in the surface-emphasizing condition."
