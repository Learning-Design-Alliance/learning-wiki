Non-parametric correlation analysis of DPI domain-sum scores against the ordinal level 1–3 classifications for the same 201 cases. The results state the correlation was "statistically significant but weak positive"; the printed coefficients (r s = 0.202–0.364) appear in Table 3, which the supplied results text does not quote numerically.
