Evidence¶

Janneke van de Pol 2024¶

Janneke van de Pol, Eleanor Rowan, Eva Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2024). Effects of availability of diagnostic and non-diagnostic cues on the accuracy of teachers' judgments of students' text comprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09383-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Ranking-task analysis in the same study: after condition 3, teachers ranked all eight cues from most to least predictive; "The mean Spearman rank correlation between the teachers' ranking and the actual ranking was 0.55", indicating substantial but imperfect awareness.

"The mean Spearman rank correlation between the teachers' ranking and the actual ranking was 0.55 (SD = 0.36; range: -0.48 to 0.93)"
