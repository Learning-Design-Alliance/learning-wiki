Random-effects meta-analysis of 22 effect sizes from the review's included studies of medical program selection. Table 1 reports the pooled effect size as OR = 0.6 (95% CI 0.55–0.65), a 40% reduction in "the likelihood of acceptance" for disadvantaged households.

> "For “household economic and educational disadvantage,” meta-analysis of 22 effect sizes showed that being from a disadvantaged household reduced the likelihood of acceptance by 40% (Table 1)."
