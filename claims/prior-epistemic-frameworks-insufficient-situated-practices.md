## Evidence

### Sarit Barzilai & Clark A. Chinn 2024

Sarit Barzilai & Clark A. Chinn. (2024). The AIR and Apt-AIR Frameworks of Epistemic Performance and Growth: Reflections on Educational Theory Development. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09927-5

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors' interpretive analysis of two prevalent psychological approaches to epistemic thinking—the developmental approach and the epistemic beliefs approach—argues they fell short of explaining learners' performance in their studies. They report that "it also mattered which particular criteria or strategies learners were using as they engaged in inquiry and how well these were adapted to their tasks."

> "One main issue was that these frameworks described epistemic thinking as a set of broad beliefs or perspectives, which did not seem to adequately capture the practical and situated aspects of epistemic thinking"
