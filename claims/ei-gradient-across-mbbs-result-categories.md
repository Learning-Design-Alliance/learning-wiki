### Wijekoon Chandrani Nirmala 2017

Wijekoon Chandrani Nirmala, Amaratunge Heshan, de Silva Yashica, Senanayake Solith, Jayawardane Pradeepa, Senarath Upul. (2017). Emotional intelligence and academic performance of medical undergraduates: a cross-sectional study in a selected university in Sri Lanka. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1018-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing mean total Genos EI scores across the four final MBBS results categories in the cross-sectional sample (n = 130), showing EI higher among those with better academic performance.

> "The Mean total EI scores among those in categories second-class upper division, second-class lower division, pass and repeat were 249.4, 246.6, 240.2 and 227, respectively (p = 0.015)"
