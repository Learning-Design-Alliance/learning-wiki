## Evidence

### Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen 2025

Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen, Bono Beyene Dobo, Zinabu Samuel Assefa, Jilo Kedir Woliy. (2025). Enhancing secondary school students’ science process skills through guided inquiry-based laboratory activities in biology. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320692

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test within the experimental group (N=46), applied because the paired data were non-parametric. The article reports 26 students with positive ranks, 13 negative, and 7 equal, and a "statistically significant increase in SPS scores from pretest to posttest".

> "The analysis showed a statistically significant increase in SPS scores from pretest to posttest, with a z-value of -3.041 and a p-value of.002 (p <  0.05), indicating that the GIBLEI intervention had a substantial positive effect on the students’ science process skills."
