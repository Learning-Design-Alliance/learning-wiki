Network analysis of classroom observation networks built from COPUS codes as nodes and chronological transitions as weighted edges, comparing 18 broader implementations to high-fidelity implementations (Fig. 3b). The study reports "lower cosine similarity values across all active learning methods than the fidelity measured with regard to proportions of class time spent on each activity".
