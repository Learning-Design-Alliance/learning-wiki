### Melanie Good 2018

Physics teaching assistants' views of different types of introductory problems: Challenge of perceiving the instructional benefits of context-rich and multiple-choice problems. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Worksheet responses on context-rich problem use; "The majority of TAs did not envision any possible situation in which context-rich problems could be used effectively," including not in group problem solving or homework.
