## Evidence

### Xaverius Pamela K. 2014

Xaverius Pamela K., Grady Mary Alice. (2014). Centering Pregnancy in Missouri: A System Level Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/285386

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive results from the pretest-posttest survey of 223 health care personnel attending CP seminars at 17 sites. The article reports that "Thirty-four percent of participants heard of CP before the information session" with posttest understanding and explanation rates of 91.2% and 91.7%; no inferential test or effect size is printed for these proportions.

> "Thirty-four percent of participants heard of CP before the information session, and after the information session, 91.2% understood the increased value of CP and 91.7% could provide a brief explanation of CP."
