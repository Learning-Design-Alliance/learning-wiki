q2 · i? · review · r2

Thematic synthesis of teaching strategies across the 41 included studies, charted in the review's Table 3. The review reports the six strategies were identified from the learning activities and defines each in Table 4 with examples of studies utilizing them.

"The strategies were (1) establishing a clear learning topic; (2) offering pre-reading materials on the topic; (3) reviewing the roles and responsibilities of each profession; (4) participating in a collaborative activity; (5) ensuring the extent of consensus between faculties; and (6) conducting debriefing for reflection."
