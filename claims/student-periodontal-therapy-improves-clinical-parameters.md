### Herz Marco M. 2025

Herz Marco M., Schamuhn Judith, Krumm Boris, Bartha Valentin. (2025). Student-performed periodontal therapy: retrospective cohort study on outcomes and related recommendations for enhancing undergraduate periodontal education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07699-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cohort analysis of medical records of 107 patients with stage III periodontitis treated by undergraduates between 2004 and 2018. The article reports "All parameters (BOP, PD, PCR) improved statistically significantly during step 1, 2 and 4 of periodontal therapy." Table 2 shows BOP fell from a mean of 41% at T0 to 27% at T1 (p < 0.001).
