## Evidence

### Yang J 2026

Yang J, Liu X, Zhao W and Wang T (2026). GenAI literacy and GenAI-assisted self-regulated learning behaviors: the roles of learning agency and challenge emotions. Front. Psychol. 17:1902668. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1902668

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap serial mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) with gender and year of study as covariates; the indirect path through learning agency had β = 0.232 with a 95% bootstrap CI excluding zero. The article reports it as "statistically significant".

> "The indirect association of GenAI literacy with SRLB through learning agency was statistically significant (B = 0.345, SE = 0.058, 95% bootstrap CI [0.234, 0.461], β = 0.232)."
