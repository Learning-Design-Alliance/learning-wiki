## Evidence

### Yanagita Yasutaka 2025

Yanagita Yasutaka, Shikino Kiyoshi, Yokokawa Daiki, Ishizuka Kosuke, Tsukamoto Tomoko, Li Yu, Uehara Takanori, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2025). Appropriate semantic qualifiers increase diagnostic accuracy when using a clinical decision support system: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07294-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of SQ appropriateness in the randomized controlled trial: of 420 diagnoses, 133 of 220 (60.5%) in the CDSS group and 115 of 200 (57.5%) in the control group had appropriate SQs; the article reports the proportion "did not differ between the groups". No effect size is printed.

> "The proportion of appropriate SQ settings did not differ between the groups (χ2(1) = 0.38, p > 0.05)."
