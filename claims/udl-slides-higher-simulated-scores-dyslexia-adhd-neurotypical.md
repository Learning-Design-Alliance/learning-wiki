## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In LLM simulations, learners with dyslexia, ADHD, and neurotypical learners on average scored higher against control groups when accessing UDL-format slides with fewer slides and web accessibility. [→ Wendy Wong 2026](#wendy-wong-2026)

## Evidence

### Wendy Wong 2026

Wendy Wong, Yuchao Jiang, and Yuekang Li. 2026. DiverseClaire: Simulating Students to Improve Introductory Programming Course Materials for All CS1 Learners. Proceedings of the 57th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V.2 (SIGCSE TS 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3770761.3777340

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

RCT-style simulation using Claude 4 Sonnet student personas with RAG; each persona completed five simulations and responses were scored as the proportion correct across six Bloom's taxonomy levels. The article reports simulated learners "on average scored higher against control groups"; no effect size is printed.

> "The prediction accuracy of the simulated student suggests that with improvements to slides (Section 2), learners with dyslexia, ADHD, and neurotypical learners would be able to access slides in a UDL-format withfewer slides and web accessibilityand on average scored higher against control groups."
