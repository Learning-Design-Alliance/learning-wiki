Pilot trials of the Powers of the Universe module using pre-test and post-test comparisons; the article also reports "nearly 100% of students were able to use powers of two" for describing doubling processes. Results are descriptive percentages with no statistical test or effect size reported.

> "In the post-test, 78% of students preferred to use powers of ten notation over multiple zeros for big and small numbers. According to post-test results, 98% of students expanded their understanding of the scale of the Universe by demonstrating knowledge of cosmological objects and subatomic particles"
