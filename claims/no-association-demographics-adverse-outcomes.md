### Taşdemir Murat 2026

Taşdemir Murat, Keskin Büşra Ekin, Aydoğan Ayaz Elif, Çelik Davut. (2026). Adverse outcomes of post-core restorations performed by undergraduate and postgraduate dental students: a retrospective comparison. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09270-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparisons of 160 restorations across demographic and tooth-related variables. The article reports "No statistically significant associations" for all three variables, printing φ = 0.052 (gender), Cramér’s V = 0.157 (region), and φ = 0.024 (location).

> "No statistically significant associations were found between adverse outcomes and gender (χ²(1) = 0.425, p = 0.515, φ = 0.052), tooth region (χ²(2) = 3.928, p = 0.140, Cramér’s V = 0.157), or tooth location (χ²(1) = 0.093, p = 0.761, φ = 0.024)"
