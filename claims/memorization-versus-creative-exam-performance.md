Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto. (2015). Report on Development of strategies to improve the students' learning by participation in class and out-of-classroom. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

This is the report author's observational experience from helping compose standardized midterm and final exams under rules forbidding books and calculators. The report contrasts this with sample-problem-based questions, "which produce a very high average score among the students".

> "However, when an exam question requires new thinking, beyond the scope the memorizing, the overall performance of the students drops dramatically."
