Evidence¶

Guilherme Lichand 2026¶

Guilherme Lichand, Lêticia Lopes, Sachin Allums. (2026). Reading ability conflates SES creativity gaps. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00404-y

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Observational comparison within Study 1's 523-student sample between the SES gap (among proficient readers) and the gap in the creativity summary measure across ROAR-SRE reading-ability levels (0.470 s.d., p < 0.001).

"It is also striking that, even among students who read proficiently, the SES creativity gap is 2/3 lower than the creativity gap across reading ability levels in the whole sample (0.470 s.d., p-value of difference < 0.001)."
