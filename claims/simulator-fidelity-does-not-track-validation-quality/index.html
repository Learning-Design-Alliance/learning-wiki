q3 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis in the review's discussion of fibreoptic intubation simulators, comparing high-fidelity VR models with low-fidelity bench models such as the 'choose-the-hole' model, which also demonstrated predictive validity in an RCT.

"Overall, although high-fidelity VR simulators such as ORSIM® demonstrate the strongest evidence for predictive validity, their educational superiority over simpler models has not yet been demonstrated."

Related Claims¶

  • AccuTouch VR simulator training improved live fibreoptic intubation performance in inexperienced paediatric residents, reducing time to intubation from 5.15 to 0.88 minutes — related
  • Predictive validity of airway simulators is rarely evaluated, and the two tracheal-intubation studies assessing it found no improvement in real-world neonatal intubation outcomes after simulator training — related
  • Training on a simple low-fidelity 'choose-the-hole' bench model transferred to real clinical fibreoptic intubation performance in an RCT — related
  • The ORSIM bronchoscopy simulator shows the most comprehensive validation among fibreoptic simulators, with construct validity (experts outperforming novices) and predictive validity — related
  • High-fidelity simulation methods (simulators, 3D models, cadaver-based practice) are associated with superior Acquisition retention, though some studies report contradictory findings — reports the opposite
  • RCT evidence comparing cadaveric with low-fidelity simulation is mixed: two RCTs favour cadaveric, one shows equivalence, one favours the bench model — related
