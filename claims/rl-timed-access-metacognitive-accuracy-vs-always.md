## Evidence

### Janne Rotter 2026

Janne Rotter, Pau Benazet i Montobbio, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). Access Timing as Scaffolding: A Reinforcement Learning Approach to GenAI in Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.15850](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.15850)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Same controlled lab experiment (N=105); metacognitive accuracy was the count of matching correctness judgments on the unassisted task (0-8). The RL vs Always contrast printed "t(71) = 2.32,p= .023,d= 0.54"; RL vs Never was not significant (t(67) = 0.29, p = .769).
