### Swanson et al. 2013

H. Lee Swanson, Michael J. Orosco, Cathy Lussier. (2013). Does Cognitive Strategy Training on Word Problems Compensate for Working Memory Capacity in Children with Math Difficulties? SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' stated pattern from the posttest comparisons in the randomized study, reported in the Conclusions section. The article offers two possible explanations: the operation span measure was novel and strategies may have provided working memory practice, or embedded calculation in the training and the span measure accounted for the transfer.

> "children without MD did not benefit from strategy conditions on posttest measures of solution and calculation accuracy, but benefited from strategy conditions on the posttest operation span measure."
