## Evidence

### D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola 2022

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola. (2022). The Impasse on Gender Differences in Intelligence: a Meta-Analysis on WISC Batteries. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09705-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 28 studies (48 samples) for the gSM factor; the standardization-sample estimate was the same (d = -0.04), and neither battery version nor mean age was a significant moderator. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The estimated mean effect size was near zero, d = -0.04 [-0.09, 0.01], p = 0.09."
