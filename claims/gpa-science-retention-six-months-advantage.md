## Evidence

### Celal Boyraz 2017

Celal Boyraz, Gökhan Serin. (2017). Science Instruction through the Game and Physical Activities Course: An Interdisciplinary Teaching Practice. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051119

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on the achievement retention test administered six months after the five-week unit to the same 82-student sample; the article reports F(3,78)=9.641, p<.05, partial eta-squared ƞ2=.270, a large effect with statistical power .99.

> "there was a significant difference between the groups in terms of their retention levels six months after the unit "Let's Learn About Force" was covered, F (3,78)=9.641, p<.05, ƞ2= .270."

### Celal Boyraz 2017 (2)

Celal Boyraz, Gökhan Serin. (2017). Science Instruction through the Game and Physical Activities Course: An Interdisciplinary Teaching Practice. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051119

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni tests on the retention test showed all three experimental groups significantly higher than the control (p=.000, .000, .001), with Cohen's d of 1.27, 1.33 and 1.07 respectively, described as large in favour of the experimental groups.

> "it was found as d=1.27 for the control and Experiment 1 groups, d=1.33 for the control and Experiment 2 groups, and d=1.07 for the control and Experiment 3 groups."
