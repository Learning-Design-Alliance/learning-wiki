## Evidence

### Nilsson C. 2017

Nilsson C., Sorensen J. L., Konge L., Westen M., Stadeager M., Ottesen B., Bjerrum F. (2017). Simulation-based camera navigation training in laparoscopy—a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-5210-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory outcome using a 22-item Intrinsic Motivation Inventory after the transfer test: the control group scored higher on interest/enjoyment (p = 0.030) and perceived choice (p = 0.033) than the camera group; no differences on perceived competence or pressure/tension.

> "On interest/enjoyment, the camera group scored 5.5 (95% CI, 5.2–5.9) versus the control group 6.4 (95% CI, 5.9–6.9), indicating higher interest and enjoyment in the control group."
