Evidence¶

Ozdemir M. 2018¶

Ozdemir M., Sahin C., Arcagok S., & Demir M. K. (2018). The effect of augmented reality applications in learning process: A meta-analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 74, 165-186. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2018.74.9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 16 experimental studies (2007-2017, SSCI journals) comparing AR experimental groups with traditional-method control groups; effect sizes pooled under a random-effects model. The article reports "the effect value was found to be .517" with a 95% CI of .259 to 0.775, classified as a medium effect.

"In this respect, when the 16 studies comparing the effect of a learning environment supported by AR and the effect of a traditional learning environment not supported by AR on academic success were analyzed according to the Random-Effects Model, the upper and lower limits of a 95 confidence interval turned out to be 0.775 and .259, respectively, and the effect value was found to be .517."
