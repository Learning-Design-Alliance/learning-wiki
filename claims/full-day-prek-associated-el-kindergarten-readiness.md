## Evidence

### de la Torre 2021

de la Torre, M., Freire, S., & Blanchard, A. (2021). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: An exploration of the influence of pre-k and early grade years. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/English-learners-in-Chicago-Public-Schools-an-exploration-of-the-influence-of-pre-K

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The report's key findings state that "Attending a full-day pre-k classroom was associated with stronger ELs pre-k attendance and their kindergarten readiness" in English language development and early literacy. The association is correlational; no effect size is printed. Only 19 percent of ELs in CPS pre-k attended full-day classrooms between 2016 and 2018, versus a district average of 34 percent.

> "Attending a full-day pre-k classroom was associated with stronger ELs pre-k attendance and their kindergarten readiness in terms of English language development and early literacy."
