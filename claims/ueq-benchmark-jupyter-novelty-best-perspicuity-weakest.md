## Evidence

### Jozef Hanˇc 2020

Jozef Hanˇc, Peter ˇStrauch, Eva Paˇnkov´a and Martina Hanˇcov´a. (2020). Teachers' perception of Jupyter and R Shiny as digital tools for open education and science. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.11262](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.11262)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal quantitative study (2018-2020) administering the 26-item Slovak UEQ to N*=28 pre-service physics teachers after a flipped calculus course. Table 4 reports Novelty M=0.92 (SE 0.19) as excellent, Efficiency M=1.47 as good, Attractiveness M=0.92 and Perspicuity M=0.78 below average; "no aﬀective aspect of Jupyter is bad."

> "These results demonstrate that no aﬀective aspect of Jupyter is bad. Jupyter technology leaves the best personal impression in motivational aspect Novelty."

### Jozef Hanˇc 2020 (2)

Jozef Hanˇc, Peter ˇStrauch, Eva Paˇnkov´a and Martina Hanˇcov´a. (2020). Teachers' perception of Jupyter and R Shiny as digital tools for open education and science. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.11262](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.11262)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same UEQ administration (N*=28). The authors report Perspicuity, "the usability aspect how easy to learn Jupyter", as the dimension with the weakest personal affective attitude, with very similar below-average impression for overall Attractiveness.

> "On the contrary, Perspicuity, the usability aspect how easy to learn Jupyter, appears as a dimension with the weakest personal aﬀective attitude."
