## Evidence

### Manjusha Kancharla and Hyunseung Kang 2021

Manjusha Kancharla and Hyunseung Kang. (2021). A Robust, Differentially Private Randomized Experiment for Evaluating Online Educational Programs With Sensitive Student Data. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2112.02452](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.02452)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive estimates from the same RP-RCT (n = 72): "the estimated proportion of cheaters for the Attention and Judgement of Learning related questions were smaller (24% and 32%, respectively)" than for retention (40%) and comprehension (38%). The authors interpret this as students being more apprehensive about sharing learning-ability outcomes.
