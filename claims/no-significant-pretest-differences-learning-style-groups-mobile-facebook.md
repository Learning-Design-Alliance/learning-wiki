## Evidence

### Chen 2015

Chen, Y. (2015). Linking Learning Styles and Learning on Mobile Facebook. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 16(2). https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/1973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A pre-test of 14 questions on course content knowledge was administered to all 134 students before the nine-week treatment. "After analyzing the pre -test scores using ANOVA, the results showed that there was no significant difference among different learning style groups." Equivalence was not tested.

> "After analyzing the pre -test scores using ANOVA, the results showed that there was no significant difference among different learning style groups (F=14.98, p=.82)."
