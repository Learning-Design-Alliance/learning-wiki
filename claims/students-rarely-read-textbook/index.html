Stelzer 2008¶

Stelzer, Gladding, Mestre, and Brookes. (2008). Comparing the efficacy of multimedia modules with traditional textbooks for learning introductory physics content. https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0405 doi:10.48550/arxiv.0806.0405

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Self-report survey question administered at the end of the retention session to all three groups; the article notes this usage was similar to a three-year UIUC survey (Table I) in which about 70% of both majors reported rarely or never reading before class.
