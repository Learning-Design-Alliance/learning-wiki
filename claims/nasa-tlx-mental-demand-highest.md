Unweighted NASA-TLX administered after each testing round of the telemedicine simulation. The article reports "Mental workload was highest," with medians of 37.50 (round 1) and 60.00 (round 2), and total effort medians of 42.50 and 55.00.

> "Mental workload was highest, with a median score of 37.50 (IQR 21.25-57.50) for round 1 and a median score of 60.00 (IQR 56.3-63.8) for round 2. The median scores for total effort (a composite of mental and physical effort) were 42.50 (IQR 27.50-57.50) and 55.00 (IQR 46.3-63.8) for rounds 1 and 2, respectively."
