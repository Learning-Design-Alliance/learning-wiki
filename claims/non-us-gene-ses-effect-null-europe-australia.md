## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Non-US (Western Europe and Australia) studies show a trivial, non-significant G × SES effect (a′ = −.027, p = .223), and p-curve analysis found no evidential value for a non-null effect outside the US. [→ Tucker-Drob 2016](#tucker-drob-2016)

## Evidence

### Tucker-Drob 2016

Tucker-Drob, E. M., & Bates, T. C. (2016). Large Cross-National Differences in Gene × Socioeconomic Status Interaction on Intelligence. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797615612727

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

p-curve analysis of non-US a′ effect sizes (k = 25 from 6 studies, N = 14,095 pairs) using the p-checker app; the meta-analytic point estimate was a′ = −.027 (SE = .022, p = .223), not significantly different from zero.

> "when only non-US studies were entered into p-curve analysis, p-checker did not indicate significant right skew (z = −1.264, p = .103), indicating that there was no evidence for a non-null a’ effect outside of the US."
