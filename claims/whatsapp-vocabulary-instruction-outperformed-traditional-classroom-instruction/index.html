Evidence¶

Sanaz Jafari & Azizeh Chalak 2016¶

Sanaz Jafari & Azizeh Chalak. (2016). The Role of WhatsApp in Teaching Vocabulary to Iranian EFL Learners at Junior High School. English Language Teaching, 9(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n8p85

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study the authors call a true experimental design, with 60 elementary-level Iranian EFL students (30 male, 30 female) at two junior high schools, chosen by random sampling and assigned to control and experimental groups (four English classes); the article does not say the assignment to groups was random. Pre-test scores did not differ significantly between groups. An independent-sample t-test on post-test scores showed the experimental group outperformed the control group, "p < 0.001". No effect size is printed.

"The mean post-test score of CG and EG was 19.23 and 23.93 with standard deviation of 1.99 and 3.66, respectively. There was a statistically significant difference in vocabulary po st-test scores for the learners in control and experimental groups ( p < 0.001, Table 3)."
