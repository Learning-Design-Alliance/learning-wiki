# EA-based training changes minimum-spanning-tree network topology in theta (larger diameter and average eccentricity) and alpha (smaller leaf fraction) bands only in the EA group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the EA group, theta-band MST diameter (t17 = 3.617, p = 0.002) and average eccentricity (t17 = 3.315, p = 0.004) increased, and alpha-band leaf fraction decreased (t17 = −2.638, p = 0.030), from pre- to post-test; control-group MST measures were independent of training. ## Evidence

### Chen 2023

Chen, Y.-C., Chang, G.-C., Huang, W.-M. & Hwang, I.-S. (2023). Quick balance skill improvement after short-term training with error amplification feedback for older adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00151-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MST graph-metric comparisons (leaf fraction, diameter, average eccentricity, BCmax) within the EA group (n = 18) across pre/post stabilometer stance; all control-group MST measures were independent of training. ηp² values printed in Table 4 but not quoted here; impact null.

> "For the theta band, post-hoc analysis showed that the diameter was larger in the post-test than in the pre-test ( t17 = 3.617, p = 0.002), whereas the average eccentricity was greater in the post-test than in the pre-test ( t17 = 3.315, p = 0.004). For the alpha band, the leaf fraction was smaller in the post-test than in the pre-test ( t17 = −2.638, p = 0.030). The changes in MST measures indicated more line-like EEG networks in the theta and alpha bands after visual EA training."
