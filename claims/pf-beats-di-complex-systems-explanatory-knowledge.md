# Ninth-grade students in a Productive Failure design scored significantly higher than Direct Instruction students on complex systems explanatory knowledge about climate

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The PF experimental group scored significantly higher than the DI comparison group on complex systems explanatory knowledge (η² = .167), with a significant main effect of teacher experience but no significant interaction. [→ Jacobson 2017](#jacobson-2017)

## Evidence

### Jacobson 2017

Jacobson, Michael, Markauskaite, Lina, Portolese, Alisha, Kapur, Manu, Lai, Polly, & Roberts, Gareth (2017). Designs for learning about climate change as a complex system. Learning and Instruction, 52, pp. 1-14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.007

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA on post-test complex systems explanatory knowledge with treatment group and teacher experience as factors and pre-test as covariate, in a quasi-experiment with 110 Year Nine students (56 PF, 54 DI). The treatment main effect was significant (η² = .167) as was teacher experience (η² = .115); the interaction was not significant.

> "For complex systems explanatory knowledge, a two-way ANCOVA found that there was a significant main effect for the experimental treatment group (F(1,105) = 21.126, p < .001, = .167), a significant main effect of teacher experience (F(1,105) = 13.601, p < .001, = .115)"
