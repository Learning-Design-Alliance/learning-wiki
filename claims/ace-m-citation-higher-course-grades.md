## Evidence

### Jeffrey A. Phillips 2016

Jeffrey A. Phillips, Jeremy E. McCallum, Katharine W. Clemmer, Thomas M. Zachariah. (2016). A Problem-Solving Framework to Assist Students and Teachers in STEM Courses. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.07853](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.07853)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-instruction survey asking Calculus students what instruction was most helpful, cross-referenced with course grades. Students citing ACE-M earned grades "almost a full letter grade higher" than non-citing students, a statistically significant difference (p< 0.05); no effect size is printed.

> "Calculus students who explicitly cited ACE-M (or even just one of the components) had course grades that were almost a full letter grade higher than those who didn't mention ACE-M. This statistically significant difference (p< 0.05)"
