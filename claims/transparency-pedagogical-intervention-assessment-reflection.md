Authors' interpretation (type e) from Discussion section 5.3 of the qualitative study. Transparency appeared to prompt students to question assumptions about objectivity, accuracy, and fairness regarding AI's capacity to interpret context, intent, and effort.

> "By explicitly disclosing that ChatGPT generated the evaluation, the assessment process was reframed from a routine instructional practice into an object of reflection and inquiry."
