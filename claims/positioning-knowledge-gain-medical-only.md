## Evidence

### Patel Mandeep K. 2026

Patel Mandeep K., Timko‐Swaim Lynn, Conklin Perrilynn, Lu Wei‐Hsin. (2026). Curriculum Integration of an Interactive Blood Pressure Measurement Module to Improve Healthcare Students' Knowledge, Confidence, and Skills. The Journal of Clinical Hypertension. https://doi.org/10.1111/jch.70234

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-squared tests on pre/post positioning knowledge. Medical students improved significantly (77.8% to 94.9%, p = 0.001); PA students did not (93.2% to 94.4%, p = 0.55) because over 90% already knew correct positioning at baseline.

> "the percentage of medical students that selected both patient positions (Positions A‐B in Table 5) increased from 77.8% (pre‐training) to 94.9% (post‐training) [χ [2] = 10.17, p = 0.001]. As for the PA students, we did not see a significant gain in their knowledge regarding patient positioning from before to after the training (pre: 93.2% vs. post 94.4% selected both Positions A and B) [χ 2 = 0.07, p = 0.55]"
