Ximenes Barrat 2025¶

Ximenes Barrat, V., Diercks, C., Berliner, B., Derby, K., Chen Gaddini, M., & Pivnick, L. (2025). Revisiting California's invisible achievement gap: Trends in education outcomes of students in foster care in the context of the local control funding formula. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resource/revisiting-californias-invisible-achievement-gap/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CDE CALPADS data, 2017–18 through 2022–23, on the percentage of students attending just one school each year. The report notes roughly one in three students in foster care attended two or more schools in the same year, with cited consequences of learning loss and interpersonal instability.

"The stability rate for students in foster care remained largely lower than for other students despite some mild improvement from 62 percent in 2016–17 to 66 percent in 2022–23, leaving roughly one in three students in foster care attending two or more schools"
