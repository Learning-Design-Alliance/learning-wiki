# Post-test biology knowledge and pre-test AI knowledge significantly predicted post-test AI knowledge

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Shenghua Zha 2025

Shenghua Zha, Marcy Maulucci Bragdon, Na Gong, Jinhui Wang, Silas Leavesley, Rachel Eaton, Erin Bosarge. (2025). A Case Study of Integrating AI Literacy Education in a Biology Class. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00476-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multiple regression with post-test AI knowledge as outcome, post-test biology knowledge as predictor and pre-test AI knowledge as control. The model was significant, F(2, 26) = 25.87, p < .01, R2 = 0.64; printed equation: AIpost-test = 0.39*Biology post-test + 0.63*AIpre-test + 1.63.

> "Results of the regression analysis showed that students' overall AI knowledge at the beginning of the lessons and their biology knowledge at the end of the lesson significantly predicted their overall AI knowledge after the lesson, F(2, 26) = 25.87, p <.01, R2 = 0.64."
