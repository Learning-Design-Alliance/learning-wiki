## Evidence

### Cate 2015

Cate, C. A., & Albright, G. (2015). Supporting Student Veterans: Utilizing Game-Based Role-Plays with Virtual Humans to Build Military Cultural Competency and Helping Behaviors in Faculty and Staff. Online Learning, 19(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061484

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A repeated-measures ANOVA on the GBS self-efficacy subscale (four items on a 4-point scale) showed "statistically significant increases in participants’ GBS composite scores of self-efficacy" across the three time points.

> "The third repeated-measures ANOVA showed statistically significant increases in participants’ GBS composite scores of self-efficacy with regard to helping student veterans in psychological distress between pretraining, posttraining, and follow-up scores F(2, 725) = 520.37, p < . 01."

### Cate 2015 (2)

Cate, C. A., & Albright, G. (2015). Supporting Student Veterans: Utilizing Game-Based Role-Plays with Virtual Humans to Build Military Cultural Competency and Helping Behaviors in Faculty and Staff. Online Learning, 19(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061484

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A repeated-measures ANOVA on the GBS likelihood subscale (two items on a 4-point scale) showed "statistically significant increases in participants’ GBS composite scores of likelihood" across the three time points.

> "A repeated-measures ANOVA showed statistically significant increases in participants’ GBS composite scores of likelihood to help student veterans in psychological distress between pretraining, posttraining, and follow-up scores F(2, 742) = 228.0, p < .01."
