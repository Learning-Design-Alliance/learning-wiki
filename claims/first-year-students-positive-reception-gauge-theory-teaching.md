## Evidence

### N.-E. Bomark 2020

N.-E. Bomark. (2020). Teaching gauge theory to first year students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2009.02162](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2009.02162)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A small anonymous voluntary electronic questionnaire given after the exam to students of the 2020 particle physics and cosmology course at the University of Agder; six of eleven exam-taking students answered. The author reports the replies as "encouragingly positive" while cautioning that non-responders may have been less positive.

> "A small questionnaire among the students exposed to this teaching, indicates a signiﬁcant interest in this and in general a positive recept ion."
