Multiple-response material-selection items with denominator N = 88 in the survey. The results report that "MTA (88.6%)" led for capping and pulpotomy while "calcium hydroxide was the most frequently selected material (69.3%)" for indirect pulp capping.

> "For direct pulp capping, partial pulpotomy, and total pulpotomy, the most frequently selected materials were MTA (88.6%), Biodentine (72.7%), and calcium hydroxide (54.5%). For indirect pulp capping, calcium hydroxide was the most frequently selected material (69.3%), followed by Biodentine (48.9%) and MTA (46.6%)."
