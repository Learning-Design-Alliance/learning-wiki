## Evidence

### Zou Ya 2026

Zou Ya, Xie Wenzhao, Huang Hui, Liu Dan, Zhou Qing, Yi Qifeng, Tong Xiaoliang, Mao Ping. (2026). The effects of case-based, problem-based, and team-based learning on nursing students’ problem-solving, self-directed learning, and communication skills: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08602-3

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 7 studies involving 731 nursing students, with high heterogeneity ("I2 = 97%"). Pooled "SMD = 1.80" favors modern teaching methods over controls.

> "The meta-analysis demonstrated that modern teaching methods significantly improved students’ communication skills (SMD = 1.80, 95% CI: 0.80–2.80, I2 = 97%, P = 0.0004)"
