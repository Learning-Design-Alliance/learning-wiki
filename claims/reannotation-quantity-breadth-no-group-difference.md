## Evidence

### Yiqiu Zhou and Jina Kang 2026

Yiqiu Zhou and Jina Kang. (2026). From individual annotations to shared attention: How re-annotation supports collaborative knowledge building in CSCL. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-026-09486-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U tests on four quantity/breadth metrics across the same 16 groups found no significant differences (e.g., unique initiating annotations U = 22.5, p = 0.366; unique re-annotations U = 20.0, p = 0.234). Equivalence was not tested. The paper notes "limited sample size" as a possible reason.

> "While high-learning groups generated a greater number of initiating annotations, low-learning groups exhibited a higher total number of re-annotations. However, these differences were not statistically significant."
