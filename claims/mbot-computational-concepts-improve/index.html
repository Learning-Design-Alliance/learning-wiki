Evidence¶

Sáez-López 2019¶

Sáez-López, J.-M., Sevillano-García, M.-L., & Vazquez-Cano, E. (2019). The effect of programming on primary school students’ mathematical and scientific understanding: educational use of mBot. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 1405–1425. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09648-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Participant-observation estimation scale (24 items) compared via Mann–Whitney U; e.g. robot programming mean rank 82.58 (experimental) versus 19.58 (control), Z = −8.957, sig. .000. More than 60% of robot-programming evaluations were good or excellent.

"There was an improvement over the control group in the understanding of sequences, loops, conditionals, parallel execution, events and robotic use, as shown by the Mann–Whitney U test (Fig. 6 and Tables 6, 7 and 8)."
