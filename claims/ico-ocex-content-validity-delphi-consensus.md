## Evidence

### Gull Ambreen 2026

Gull Ambreen, Yasmin Rahila, Mahboob Usman, Golnik Karl C., Moin Muhammad. (2026). Validation of the ICO-OCEX tool for competency-based assessment in ophthalmology residency programs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09154-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

A multiphase mixed-method study used a modified Delphi technique with 24 national and international ophthalmology experts across three rounds. All items met predetermined criteria: 'All items had a median greater than 4, an IQR less than 1, and more than 75% of the experts rated them in the top two categories.' The S-CVI/Ave was 0.97 and average clarity score was 2.98.

> "The modified Delphi technique achieved consensus on all 33 items, confirming stability and a scale content validity index of 0.97."
