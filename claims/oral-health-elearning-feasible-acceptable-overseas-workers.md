### Sasayama Kiriko 2023

Sasayama Kiriko, Momoi Yasuko, Gilmour Stuart, Ota Erika. (2023). Development and feasibility of an oral health e-learning program for long-term Japanese overseas workers: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03361-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Open-ended acceptability questionnaire at three months with the intervention group of the pilot RCT; the printed percentages are 75.0% behaviour change, 83.3% not difficult to understand, 91.7% perceived impact, and feedback "was positive from all responding participants."

> "27 (75.0%) said that the e-learning program had changed their behaviour, 30 (83.3%) reported that the e-learning program was not difficult to understand, and 33 (91.7%) participants indicated that the e-learning materials have an impact on the promotion of oral health among overseas workers."

### Sasayama Kiriko 2023 (2)

Sasayama Kiriko, Momoi Yasuko, Gilmour Stuart, Ota Erika. (2023). Development and feasibility of an oral health e-learning program for long-term Japanese overseas workers: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03361-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trial flow of the pilot RCT: 82 participants (82.8%) completed the post-test and were analysed, with a dropout rate of 17.2% and differential retention between groups (75% intervention vs 90.2% control).

> "A total of 82 (82.8%) participants, 36 (75%) in the intervention group and 46 (90.2%) in the control group, who completed the post-test were included in the analysis. The dropout rate was 17.2%."
