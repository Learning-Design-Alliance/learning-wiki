# Responsibility for achieving agency should not rest solely on teachers and students, since contextual factors and educational social processes determine its fluctuation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The achievement of teacher and learner agency is determined jointly by teachers, students, interdependent relationships, contextual factors and educational social processes, not by teachers and students alone. [→ Scanlon 2021](#scanlon-2021)

## Evidence

### Scanlon 2021

Scanlon, D & Connolly, C. (2021). Teacher agency and learner agency in teaching and learning a new school subject, Leaving Certificate Computer Science, in Ireland: Considerations for teacher education. Computers and Education, 174, 104291. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104291

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The authors' explicit argument, presented through the case study of LCCS teaching, that agency is not an individual product. They state it is "a combination of the teacher and the students and the other interdependent relationships within their figurations" that determines its fluctuation.

> "We explicitly argue that the responsibility should not be placed on the teacher and student in achieving agency (as a product) but it is a combination of the teacher and the students and the other interdependent relationships within their figurations, alongside contextual factors, and educational social processes, which determine the fluctuation of the process of achieving teacher and learner agency."
