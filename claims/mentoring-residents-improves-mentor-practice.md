### Guha 2016

Guha, R., Hyler, M.E., and Darling-Hammond, L. (2016). The Teacher Residency: An Innovative Model for Preparing Teachers. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-residency

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report cites survey data from NCTR network mentors in 2014-15, in which the large majority "reported that mentoring residents made them a more effective teacher". A study of eight urban residency mentors also found mentors saw their role as part of urban reform and viewed a year-long placement as necessary for developing student-centered practice.
