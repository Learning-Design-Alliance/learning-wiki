## Evidence

### Miçooğullari and Kirazci 2016

Bülent Okan Miçooğullari, Sadettin Kirazci. (2016). Effects of 6 Weeks Psychological Skill Training on Team Cohesion, Self-confidence & Anxiety: A Case of Youth Basketball Players. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(12): 2761-2768. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.041210

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed design ANOVA on TSCI scores across five measurements in the two-team quasi-experiment; the article reports the interaction (η2 = .11), time (η2 = .28), and group (η2 = .45) effects all significant at p < .05, with the experimental group showing positive self-confidence implications.

> "A time x group interaction was found to be significant, F (2.40, 81.57) = 4.34, p < .05 η 2 = .11. Test of time effect was found to be significant, F (2.40, 81.57) = 13.21, p < .05 η2 = .28. Test of group effect was found to be significant, F (1, 34) = 32.09, p < .05 η2 = .45."
