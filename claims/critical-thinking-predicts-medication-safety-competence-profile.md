## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, P., Yu, G., Xu, R., Cao, S., Lin, M., Liu, Y., & Wu, Y. (2026). Factors associated with medication safety competence among clinical nurses in China: A latent profile analysis. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000749

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (N=1,228) with Profile 4 as reference found total critical thinking scores significantly predicted membership in Profiles 1, 2, and 3 (all p<.001). Table 4 reports for Profile 1: β=-0.570, OR=0.565. The article states "Higher critical thinking scores were found to predict greater medication safety competence in this study."
