### Talvio 2015

Talvio, M., Berg, M., Ketonen, E., Komulainen, E., & Lonka, K. (2015). Progress in Teachers’ Readiness to Promote Positive Youth Development among Students during the Lions Quest Teaching Workshop. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i6.869

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures GLM analysis of 153 LQ participants versus 61 non-participating teachers, controlling for gender, work experience and job title. The article reports that "the gain scores on the importance of promoting students’ SEL differed significantly between groups (p = 0.005, d = 0.39)".
