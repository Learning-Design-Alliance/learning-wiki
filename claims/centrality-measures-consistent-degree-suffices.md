# Eight centrality measures yield highly consistent rankings across the Physics, Mathematics, and integrated networks, so degree centrality alone suffices to rank content importance

## Evidence

### Paula Tuzón 2025

Paula Tuzón, Antoni Salvà Salvà, and Juan Fernández-Gracia. (2025). Complex networks approach to curriculum analysis and subject integration: a case study on Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis of eight centrality rankings (degree, in-/out-degree, closeness on original and reversed networks, betweenness directed and undirected) on the Physics concept network. The article reports a "minimum Spearman correlation of 0.61" and concludes degree centrality can assess importance without full network knowledge.

> "with a minimum Spearman correlation of 0.61 ( p < 0.001, Figure 3). This strong correlation suggests that the rankings derived from these measures are largely consistent"

### Paula Tuzón 2025 (2)

Paula Tuzón, Antoni Salvà Salvà, and Juan Fernández-Gracia. (2025). Complex networks approach to curriculum analysis and subject integration: a case study on Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same correlation analysis on the Mathematics network (36 contents, 5 blocks). Betweenness departs slightly from the other measures with a Spearman rank correlation of 0.89 (p <0.001), yet the article judges the agreement high enough to rely on degree.

> "This is reflected in its lower Spearman rank correlation of 0.89 (p <0.001) with the remaining measures. Despite this, the overall agreement among centrality rankings remains high, reinforcing the reliability of degree as a representative measure."

### Paula Tuzón 2025 (3)

Paula Tuzón, Antoni Salvà Salvà, and Juan Fernández-Gracia. (2025). Complex networks approach to curriculum analysis and subject integration: a case study on Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis on the integrated 90-content Physics-plus-Mathematics network. The article reports a minimum Spearman rank correlation of 0.91 (p < 0.001) among the eight centrality measures, less variation than in the Physics network alone.

> "with a minimum Spearman rank correlation of 0.91 ( p < 0.001). This consistency further supports the robustness of the centrality rankings across different network structures."
