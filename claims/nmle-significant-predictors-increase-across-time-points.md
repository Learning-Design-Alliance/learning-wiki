## Evidence

### Shioiri Toshiki 2024

Shioiri Toshiki, Nakashima Michiyo, Tsunekawa Koji. (2024). When can we identify the students at risk of failure in the national medical licensure examination in Japan using the predictive pass rate? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05948-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of seven consecutive cohorts (n = 637, 2012–2018) at Gifu University School of Medicine, run at five time points. The article reports "2 factors at admission, 3 at 1st and 2nd, 4 at 4th, and 6 at 6th grade"; odds ratios and 95% CIs are printed in Table 3.

> "medical students who passed the NMLE had significant factors at each of the 5 time points, and the number of these factors increased with the advancement of their grade in school (i.e., 2 factors at admission, 3 at 1st and 2nd, 4 at 4th, and 6 at 6th grade)"
