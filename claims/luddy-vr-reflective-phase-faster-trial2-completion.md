## Evidence

### Bueckle 2022

Bueckle, Andreas; Buehling, Kilian; Shih, Patrick C.; Börner, Katy. (2022). Optimizing Performance and Satisfaction in Matching and Movement Tasks in Virtual Reality with Interventions Using the Data Visualization Literacy Framework. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.3389/frvir.2021.727344](https://doi.org/10.3389/frvir.2021.727344)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Luddy VR study: 68 subjects navigated a virtual building in two sets of 24 tasks; the experiment cohort inspected a 3D trajectory and bar-graph visualization of their trial-1 data before trial 2. The article reports the experiment cohort "achieved significantly faster completion times in their second trial (Welch's t-test, p = 0.014)". No effect size is printed.

> "In Luddy VR, on the other hand, subjects in the experiment cohort achieved significantly faster completion times in their second trial (Welch's t-test, p = 0.014) while also scoring higher in a mid-questionnaire"
