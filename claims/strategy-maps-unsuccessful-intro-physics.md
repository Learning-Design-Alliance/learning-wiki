## Evidence

### Aaron Reinhard 2022

Aaron Reinhard, Alex Felleson, Paula C. Turner, and Maxwell Green. (2022). Assessing the Impact of Metacognitive Post-Reﬂection Exercises on Problem-Solving Skillfulness. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anecdotal and course-evaluation evidence from the fall 2018 intervention year, where students updated ongoing strategy maps each Friday with 15-20 minutes of paired class time. The article calls this qualitative result: "We determined that this exercise was not developmen-tally appropriate for students in an introductory physics course."

> "The interactions among students were relatively sparse during in-class map creation time, and the quality of many maps never rose to a useful level. Maps were often disjointed, lacking the salient characteristics of each problem type, and contained weak, confusing, or circular links between characteristics."
