## Evidence

### Jacob T. Stanley 2017

Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Notebook coding results for the voltage divider activity (Activity 1), where initial prediction, measurement, and model revision were the most commonly documented components while "the initial comparison and new comparison were the least commonly documented components." Descriptive coding result, no effect size.

> "Conversely, the initial comparison and new comparison were the least commonly documented components."

### Jacob T. Stanley 2017 (2)

Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of documented comparisons in the photometer activity, where quantitative comparisons stated a percentage difference, ratio, or order-of-magnitude difference. The authors report "students did not state a threshold for this comparison to distinguish good agreement from poor agreement." Qualitative finding, no effect size.

> "Generally, students did not state a threshold for this comparison to distinguish good agreement from poor agreement, and thus it was unclear how these comparisons were used as justiﬁcation for subsequent revision"
