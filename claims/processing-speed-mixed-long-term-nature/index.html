Evidence¶

Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska 2022¶

Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska. (2022). Effects of Nature (Greenspace) on Cognitive Functioning in School Children and Adolescents: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1217–1254. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09658-5

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports these two long-term-exposure quasi-experiments with the Digit Letter Substitution test, in which participants convert digits to letters against a key within short time periods. The review attributes the inconsistency either to true inconsistency of nature exposure or to difficulty establishing causal effects for long-term studies.

"No significant positive effect was found on processing speed in the living plants classroom wall exposure study in Dutch primary school children (van den Berg et al., 2017). However, a significant positive effect was reported in the Greening of School study in Dutch school children aged 7–11 years (van Dijk-Wesselius et al., 2018)."
