## Evidence

### Finstad Anne Strand 2023

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group findings at both time points. The surgical team leader was unclear to most participants before training; participants stated it was easier to act when the leader spoke loud and clear.

> "Awareness of each person’s role and action in SBTT was experienced as significant for transfer of learning and made teamwork easier in clinical practice."

### Finstad Anne Strand 2023 (2)

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group findings on communication. One anaesthesiologist described receiving specific messages from the gynaecologist team leader in the second scenario, and a participant noted "The use of closed loop communication improves awareness".

> "Several participants experienced improved communications in the second scenario, which could be crucial for transfer of learning to clinical settings."
