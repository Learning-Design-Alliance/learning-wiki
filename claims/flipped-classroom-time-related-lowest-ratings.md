`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive item-level results from the same 14-item perception survey (N=70 intervention-group respondents). All three time-related items scored lowest on the 5-point scale; means are descriptive with no test reported.

> "The three lowest items perceived by the students were: Flipped classroom session should have allotted more time for each topic (M = 3.11, SD = 1.07), flipped classroom session requires a long time for preparation and conduction (M = 3.23, SD = 1.04), and flipped classroom session reduces the amount of time needed for study when compared to lectures (M = 3.26, SD = 1.07)."
