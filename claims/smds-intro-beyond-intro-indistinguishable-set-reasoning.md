## Evidence

### Emily M. Stump 2023

Emily M. Stump, Mark Hughes, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Comparing intro and beyond-intro students' reasoning about uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020147)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey comparison of intro and beyond-intro physics students at 10 universities, with SMDS responses coded as point or set reasoning. The article reports both groups were "indistinguishable in the rates at which each group applied point and set reasoning," with set reasoning at 67% and 77% respectively.

> "For the SMDS probe, intro and beyond-intro students were indistinguishable in the rates at which each group applied point and set reasoning. Both intro and beyond-intro students primarily displayed set reasoning in their explanations (67% and 77%, respectively)."
