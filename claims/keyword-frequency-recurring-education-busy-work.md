`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Frequency analysis of keywords from preceptor nurses' weekly reflection journals, visualized with WordClouds (Figure 2). The authors state busy work and patient were not mentioned separately in the content analysis but reflect daily post-work concerns.

> "A total of 541 keywords were extracted from 107 (59.4%) responses regarding preceptors’ feelings or thoughts about their preceptorship. Among them, “recurring education” was most frequently expressed (n = 31, 5.7%), followed by “busy work” (n = 25, 4.6%), “patient” (n = 24, 4.4%), and “practical training” (n = 22, 4.1%)."
