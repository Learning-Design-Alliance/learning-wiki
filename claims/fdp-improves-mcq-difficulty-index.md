## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall difficulty index values improved significantly from pre- to post-training (χ²=11.61, p=0.003), with moderate-difficulty items rising from 62.5% to 76.3% and easy items falling from 36.7% to 22.5%. [→ Abdulghani 2015](#abdulghani-2015)
`q2 i?` Per-course ANOVA showed significant pre- to post-training variation in difficulty index for respiratory (F=4.964, p=0.027), cardiovascular (F=6.253, p=0.013) and renal (F=7.852, p=0.006) examinations. [→ Abdulghani 2015 (2)](#abdulghani-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Abdulghani 2015

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single-group analysis of 240 final-exam MCQs per year across three courses, comparing pre-training (2012–2013) and post-training (2013–2014) items. The article reports "significant improvement of P-value" with χ²=11.61, p=0.003; easy items decreased from 36.7 to 22.5%.

> "The overall result showed significant improvement of P-value ( x2511.61, p50.003), however, non-significant improvement of DI values (x252.27, p50.131) was obtained through MCQs for the academic year 2013–2014 and the academic year 2012–2013 for all the three courses"

### Abdulghani 2015 (2)

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA of item analysis per course block, reported in Table 3. Each of the three courses showed a significant pre- to post-training difference in difficulty index: "respiratory (F 54.964, p 50.027)", cardiovascular F=6.253, p=0.013, renal F=7.852, p=0.006.

> "The items analysis showed significant variation between the pre- and post-training in the P-values of respiratory (F 54.964, p 50.027), cardiovascular (F56.253, p 50.013) and renal block examinations (F 57.852, p50.006) (Table 3)."
