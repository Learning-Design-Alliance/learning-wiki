`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Design-based research with teacher inquiry networks over the project's first 4 years. The authors report that "repositioning the teacher's role is a gradual process" and that most of the almost 100 collaborating teachers showed change toward the envisioned approach; no effect size is printed.

> "However, we saw evidence of change toward the envisioned Project READI approach in approximately 90 of the almost 100 teachers with whom we worked over the project's first 4 years."
