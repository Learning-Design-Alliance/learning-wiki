## Evidence

### Silvia Bonacina 2021

Silvia Bonacina, Stephanie Huang, Travis White-Schwoch, Jennifer Krizman, Trent Nicol and Nina Kraus. (2021). Rhythm, reading, and sound processing in the brain in preschool children. npj Science of Learning 6:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00097-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on phase-locking consistency comparing synchronization groups; the article reports "greater phase-locking consistency than “Non-synchronizers” (F(1,76) = 4.498, p = 0.037, ηp2 = 0.056)" and a group × time region interaction (F(1,76) = 9.182, p = 0.003, np2 = 0.108).

> "“Synchronizers” display greater phase-locking consistency than “Non-synchronizers” (F(1,76) = 4.498, p = 0.037, ηp2 = 0.056)"

### Silvia Bonacina 2021 (2)

Silvia Bonacina, Stephanie Huang, Travis White-Schwoch, Jennifer Krizman, Trent Nicol and Nina Kraus. (2021). Rhythm, reading, and sound processing in the brain in preschool children. npj Science of Learning 6:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00097-5

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Within the same PLC ANOVA, frequency range was a within-subject variable; the article reports "greater PLC in the higher frequency range (500 –1000 Hz) than the lower frequency range (100–400 Hz) ( F(1,76) = 10.585, p = 0.002, ηp2 = 0.122)" and no significant polarity effect (p = 0.457).

> "participants exhibited greater PLC in the higher frequency range (500 –1000 Hz) than the lower frequency range (100–400 Hz) ( F(1,76) = 10.585, p = 0.002, ηp2 = 0.122)"
