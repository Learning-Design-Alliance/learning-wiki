## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For multivariate multiple baseline designs across participants, the data can be modeled by allowing correlation among outcome measures at each time point and heterogeneous variances across measures, so effects on each dependent variable can be estimated in one model rather than separate analyses. [→ Man Chen 2023](#man-chen-2023)

## Evidence

### Man Chen 2023

Man Chen, James E. Pustejovsky, David A. Klingbeil, and Ethan R. Van Norman. (2023). Between-Case Standardized Mean Differences: Flexible Methods for Single-Case Designs. Journal of School Psychology, vol. 98. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2023.02.002

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article's introduction describes its third extension, illustrated by Calder and colleagues (2020), an explicit grammar intervention for nine children with developmental language disorder across outcomes including expressive morphosyntax production and grammaticality judgements. The full demonstration section was truncated; the printed support is the article's stated rationale for the extension.

> "Extending the BC-SMD to this design would allow researchers to estimate the effect of an intervention on each dependent variable in a single model rather than running multiple, separate analyses, while ignoring the correlation between outcome measures."
