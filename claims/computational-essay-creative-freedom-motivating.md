### Tor Ole B. Odden and Marcos D. Caballero 2020 (2)

Tor Ole B. Odden and Marcos D. Caballero. (2020). Computational Essays: An Avenue for Scientific Creativity in Physics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.odden](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.odden)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported time data from the pilot interviews: the replaced mandatory assignment was expected to take roughly 4-8 hours, yet half of the interviewed students reported 14 or more hours, plus background reading of published literature.

> "all groups reported spending over 6 hours on the project, and half (5 of the 10) reported spending upwards of 14 hours per person"
