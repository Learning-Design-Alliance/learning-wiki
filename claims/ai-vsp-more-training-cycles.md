## Evidence

### Sun Ning 2026

Sun Ning, Zhou Xintong, Yang Zhongqian, Zhou Yu, Ma Ruihang, Wang Zhong. (2026). Application of AI-based virtual standardized patients in physician-patient communication training: a study based on the SEGUE framework. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1768518

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Training-frequency comparison in the RCT; cycles were manually counted for the traditional SP group and system-extracted for the AI-VSP group. The article reports "a small-to-moderate effect size (r = 0.25)".

> "the AI-VSP group completed a higher number of training cycles than the traditional SP group (mean ± SD: 4.10 ± 0.67 vs. 3.79 ± 0.52). This difference was statistically significant based on the Mann–Whitney U test (U = 639.5, P = 0.03), with a small-to-moderate effect size (r = 0.25)."
