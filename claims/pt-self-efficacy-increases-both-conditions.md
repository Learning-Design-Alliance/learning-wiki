## Evidence

### van Lankveld W 2019

van Lankveld W, Maas M, van Wijchen J, Visser V, Staal JB. (2019). Self-regulated learning in physical therapy education: a non-randomized experimental study comparing self-directed and instruction-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1484-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures multivariate ANOVA on PTSE subscales with baseline and follow-up assessments in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports a significant time effect "(Multivariate F (3,72) = 28.8, p < .0001)" and a non-significant time-by-group interaction, meaning change was similar in both conditions.

> "In the repeated measures ANOVA there was a time effect within subjects effect on physical therapy self-efficacy (Multivariate F (3,72) = 28.8, p < .0001). The interaction effect of time and groups was not significant (Multivariate F (3,72) = 1.09, p = n.s.)."
