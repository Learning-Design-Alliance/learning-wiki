`q2 i?` In classical twin modeling, both genetic and environmental variance components of EA were smaller in higher SES groups. [→ Eveline L. de Zeeuw 2019](#eveline-l-de-zeeuw-2019)

## Evidence

### Eveline L. de Zeeuw 2019

Eveline L. de Zeeuw, Kees-Jan Kan, Catharina E. M. van Beijsterveldt, Hamdi Mbarek, Jouke-Jan Hottenga, Gareth E. Davies, Michael C. Neale, Conor V. Dolan and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2019). The moderating role of SES on genetic differences in educational achievement in the Netherlands. npj Science of Learning 4:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0052-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Genetic covariance structure modeling in a classical twin design (2479 MZ and 4450 DZ pairs), with means and variance components estimated per SES group and corrected for censoring; variance components decreased with increasing SES (Δ−2LL = 89.7, p < 0.001; Table 1).

> "Noticeably, genetic and environmental variance differed across the groups and depended on SES ( Δ−2LL = 89.7, Δdf = 9, p < 0.001). Speci ﬁ-cally, the genetic and environmental variance components decreased with increasing SES."
