`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the correlation analysis, each procedure's basic-skills score summed the group values (1–3) of its required skills from Delphi round#2, then was compared with the round#3 expert Likert ratings; the scores were "well correlated" with R2 = .75 shown in Figure 1.

> "The obtained scores are well correlated to the scores based on basic skills (Figure 1) with a coefficient of determination R2 = .75."
