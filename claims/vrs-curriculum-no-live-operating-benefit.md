## Evidence

### Mooney SS 2022

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective cohort study at an Australian tertiary centre comparing novice-trained (NT, N=14 at six months) with novice-control (NC, N=7) trainees on live laparoscopic salpingectomy assessed with OSA-LS; "did not confer a benefit in live performance compared to NC" with no significant association in regression analyses.

> "For NT, having access to our LapSim®-integrated program did not confer a benefit in live performance compared to NC. There was no association between the access to the LapSim® and six‐month OSA‐LS scores for novice trainees"

### Mooney SS 2022 (2)

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired within-group analysis of the eight experienced-trained trainees from baseline to six months found "no improvement in OSA‐LS scores over the six‐month training period", with all comparisons Holm-Sidak adjusted P > 0.23.

> "On paired analysis, ET demonstrated no improvement in OSA‐LS scores over the six‐month training period, irrespective of LapSim® logged time (all Holm‐Sidak adjusted, P > 0.23)."
