Evidence¶

Jansen 2017¶

Jansen, R. S., Lakens, D., & IJsselsteijn, W. (2017). An integrative review of the cognitive costs and benefits of note-taking. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.10.001

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of two experiments (n = 90 and n = 240) comparing continuously related, discontinuously related, and unrelated lecture segments with and without note-taking; the pattern replicated across both experiments on free recall tests.

"Results from these experiments thus indicate that structuring a lecture leads to worse memory performance. This is not in line with intuitive judgment and the authors could only speculate about the possible cause."
