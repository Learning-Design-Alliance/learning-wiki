## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Compared with nurses receiving no training, those with 5-9 training sessions in the past 5 years scored 10.255 points higher (P<.001) and those with 10 or more sessions scored 7.364 points higher (P=.03); the 1-4 session coefficient was not significant.
