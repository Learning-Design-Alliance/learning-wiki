## Evidence

### Buchanan 2015

Buchanan, E., Morrison, J., Walker, M., Aston, H., & Cook, R. (2015). Tutor Trust Secondary: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581539

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Finding from the qualitative process evaluation interviews with senior leaders, teachers, pupils and tutors in eight case-study schools. The report frames this as perception data, not a tested effect. It also reports that teachers believed there was a need for more affordable high-quality tuition which the Tutor Trust helped meet.

> "Tuition was perceived to be most effective when tutors possessed strong pedagogical skills and subject knowledge; engaged and interacted successfully with pupils; and targeted sessions appropriately."
