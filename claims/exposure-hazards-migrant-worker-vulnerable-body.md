### Bilas Paul 2026

Bilas Paul. (2026). Who Gets to Do Physics? Occupational Stereotypes in AI-Generated Problem Sets. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.19161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.19161)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the 49 exposure-related problems in the 600-problem corpus: 22 Migrant Worker, 15 Nurse, 9 Construction Worker, 3 CEO, zero Physicist or Teacher. Fisher's exact test gave "odds ratio = 4.94, p < 10−5".

> "The Mi-
grant Worker alone accounted for 45% of all exposure-related
hazards despite representing only one-sixth of the dataset
(Fisher’s exact test: odds ratio = 4.94,p <10 −5)."
