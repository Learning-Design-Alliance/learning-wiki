## Evidence

### Alharbi F 2022

Alharbi F, Alwadei SH, Alwadei A, Asiri S, Alwadei F, Alqerban A, Almuzian M. (2022). Comparison between two asynchronous teaching methods in an undergraduate dental course: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03557-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover clustered randomized pilot trial of 32 fourth-year dental students; Mann–Whitney U tests compared post-test medians between formats for two lectures. "insignificant differences" were found in both periods (period 2: U = 122.5, z = −0.22, p > 0.05).

> "For period 1, the results showed insignificant differences between the intervention group (Md = 8, n = 16) and the control group (Md = 5.5, n = 16, U = 93.5, z = − 1.33, p > 0.05)."
