### Bahramnezhad F 2025

Bahramnezhad F, Keshmiri F. (2025). "Establishing pathways to excellence in the teaching profession": an exploration of teachers' experiences on sustaining motivation in Medical sciences universities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06844-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 37 interviews with Iranian medical sciences university teachers. Participants described volition and agency in teaching and scholarship; one said 'The process of developing and investigating new ideas motivates me', and another valued a 'supportive network without borders'.

> "Autonomy for exploration is a critical factor in sustaining teacher motivation, rooted in the fulfillment of a fundamental psychological need."
