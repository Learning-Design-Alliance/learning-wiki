## Evidence

### Dou 2018

Dou, R., Brewe, E., Potvin, G., Zwolak, J. P., & Hazari, Z. (2018). Understanding the development of interest and self-efficacy in active-learning undergraduate physics courses. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2018.1488088](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2018.1488088)

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Dependent samples t-test on PID survey physics interest items from three MI courses (N = 221), pre to post. The article reports a decrease of "2.23 points or 6.2% of the scale" with "t(220) = 5.24, p < .001, Cohen's d = 0.35".

> "Similarly, students' physics interest decreased significantly by 2.23 points or 6.2% of the scale [t(220) = 5.24, p < .001, Cohen's d = 0.35]."

### Dou 2018 (2)

Dou, R., Brewe, E., Potvin, G., Zwolak, J. P., & Hazari, Z. (2018). Understanding the development of interest and self-efficacy in active-learning undergraduate physics courses. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2018.1488088](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2018.1488088)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Dependent samples t-test on PID survey general science interest items (N = 221), pre to post. The article reports "a small, but significant negative trend of one-point" with "t(220) = 2.84, p < .01, Cohen's d = 0.19".

> "A small, but significant negative trend of one-point was also observed in students' general science interest from pre to post [t(220) = 2.84, p < .01, Cohen's d = 0.19]."
