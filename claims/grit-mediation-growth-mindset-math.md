### Dong Jia 2026

Dong Jia, Yang Xiqing, Yang Xi, Tan Dingliang, Wang Kexin. (2026). The relationship between growth mindset and mathematics achievement in Chinese middle school students: The role of achievement goals and grit. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05153-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test in the same 691-student sample found four significant simple mediation pathways, including through grit (indirect effect = 0.0193, 95% CI [0.0007, 0.0410]); the quote also covers the three goal mediators.

> "Growth mindset was positively associated with mathematics achievement through the simple mediation of: mastery-avoidance goals (indirect effect = 0.0124, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0000, 0.0302]), performance-approach goals (indirect effect = 0.0212, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0038, 0.0451]), performance-avoidance goals (indirect effect = 0.0147, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0031, 0.0299]), and grit (indirect effect = 0.0193, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0007, 0.0410])."
