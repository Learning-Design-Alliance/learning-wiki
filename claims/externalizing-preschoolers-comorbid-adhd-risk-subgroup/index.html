Evidence¶

Feil 2016¶

Feil, E. G., Small, J. W., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., Golly, A., Frey, A., & Forness, S. R. (2016). Early intervention for preschoolers at risk for attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder: Preschool First Step to Success. Behavioral Disorders, 41(2), 95-106. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581149

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Retrospective subgroup construction using parent and teacher Conners' ADHD Scales total scores at 1 SD or more above the mean; the article reports "45 of the 126 children participating in the efficacy trial (35%) met criteria". Only t statistics are printed, no effect sizes.

"the identified and nonidentified children differed significantly on five of 10 baseline measures and approached significance ( p , .10) on three other baseline measures."
