`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 80 ED doctors at three Johannesburg hospitals; descriptive reporting of 12 MCQ knowledge scores. The survey found scores "low for single best answer questions (50%, 39–67)" and complications, higher for contraindications and indications. No effect size reported.

> "Knowledge scores (median, IQR) were low for single best answer questions (50%, 39–67) and complications (50%, 38–75), but higher for contraindications (83%, 67–100) and indications (75%, 50–100)."
