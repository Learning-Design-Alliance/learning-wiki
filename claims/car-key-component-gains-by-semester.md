`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cross-sectional field study comparing CAR scores by individual key component between first-semester ecology students (n = 10) and third-semester physiology students (n = 7). The article reports that "students at both levels struggled with key component 4 (situating their findings within the proper scope; t15 = 1.67, p = 0.116) and key component 5 (addressing future studies; t15 = 1.45, p = 0.184)."

> "we only detected a statistically significant gain in student ability to address key component 1 (connect data to claims; t15 = 3.52, p = 0.003) and key component 2 (inform biological rationale model; t15 = 2.95, p = 0.0099)."
