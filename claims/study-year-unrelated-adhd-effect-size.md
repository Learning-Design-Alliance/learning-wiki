`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Mixed-effects moderator analysis on the 123 between-group studies testing publication year. The article reports no significant relationship and no printed effect size for this moderator.

> "The results of a mixed-effects model indicated that study year did not relate significantly to the size of the effect size; the coefficient and standard errors to the second decimal point were all zero."
