### Shikino Kiyoshi 2024

Shikino Kiyoshi, Nishizaki Yuji, Kataoka Koshi, Fukui Sho, Yokokawa Daiki, Shimizu Taro, Yamamoto Yu, Nagasaki Kazuya, Kobayashi Hiroyuki, Tokuda Yasuharu. (2024). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel logistic regression with resident physicians nested within hospitals, adjusting for physician- and hospital-level covariates. The university-hospital result is printed separately: "training at a university hospital was associated with a lower likelihood of a correct CSV-IE answer (adjusted OR: 0.624; 95% CI: 0.435–0.896; P = .0107)".

> "In the multivariable analysis, a higher number of emergency outpatients per year (adjusted OR: 1.025; 95% CI: 1.003–1.047; P = .0230) and being in the PGY-2 (adjusted OR: 1.387; 95% CI: 1.104–1.742; P = .005) were independently associated with a higher likelihood of correctly answering the CSV-IE question."
