`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interview study of medical students at a public medical university in northern China; thematic analysis of transcripts. A participant (PH003) reports that "teachers who are friendly and approachable" receive high scores "even if their teaching quality isn't that great."

> "My friends and people around me tend to give high scores to teachers who are friendly and approachable. Such teachers are popular! Even if their teaching quality isn't that great, they have good rapport with students."
