`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat linear mixed effects analysis of the pilot cluster-randomised trial (four district hospitals, 44 providers recruited, 37 completing endline), adjusted for baseline scores, found endline total skill scores 3.67 points (95% CI −2.90, 10.24) and knowledge scores 0.59 points (95% CI −1.40, 2.52) higher in the intervention arm.

> "The endline knowledge and total skill scores were 0.59 (95% confidence interval (CI) = −1.40, 2.52) points and 3.67 (95% CI = −2.90, 10.24) points higher in the intervention arm than the control arm, after adjusting for corresponding baseline score."
