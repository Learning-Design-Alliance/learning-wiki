## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Ashley B. Heim, Barum Park, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Introductory physics students' recognition of strong peers: Gender and racial/ethnic bias differ by course level and context. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM analysis of peer-nomination networks in three remote introductory physics courses (Fall 2020), with gender tie types as predictors and man→man as base term. The study found men "significantly under-nominate women" in the named networks; Course B's lecture network had the largest coefficient magnitude.

> "The coefficient estimates for the man → woman variable, shown in dark green dots in Fig. 3, indicate that men significantly under-nominate women in the lecture perception network of Course A and both networks of Course B."
