## Evidence

### Paschke 2025

Paschke, P., Steinmayr, R., Mertens, A., Neubauer, A. B., & Spinath, B. (2025). Re‐Examination of the Reciprocal Relation Between Intrinsic Motivation and Competence Beliefs in Math Using the Random‐Intercept Cross‐Lagged Panel Framework. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70028

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model comparison using AIC and BIC showed "the two RI‐CLPMs outperformed the two CLPMs" in Dataset 2 with seven measurement points, with AIC differences ranging from ΔAIC = 72.37 to ΔAIC = 77.96.

> "In Dataset 2 with seven measurement points, the two RI‐CLPMs outperformed the two CLPMs, but none of the two RI‐CLPMs was clearly superior to the other."
