## Evidence

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison in the fMRI landing decision study of 20 pilots (12 CFII/ATP, 8 IFR). The article reports a significant d′ difference with "ES = 1.37", a large effect, from a t-test on sensitivity (hits minus false alarms).

> "High Expertise pilots were more accurate than Moderate Expertise pilots in making a “land” versus “do not land” decision (CFII/ATP: d′ = 3.62±2.52; IFR: d′ = 0.98±1.04; (t (18) = 3.23; p<.01; ES = 1.37)."

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014 (2)

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same study's secondary accuracy measure: overall accuracy rates differed significantly between the two expertise groups, with no standardized effect size printed for this contrast.

> "As with d′ there was a significant difference in overall accuracy in the landing decision task between High Expertise pilots (CFII/ATP: Mean = 80.44%±5.65%) and Moderate Expertise pilots (IFR: Mean = 62.41%±4.01%) (t (18) = 2.35, p<.05)."
