Evidence¶

Janssen 2010¶

Janssen, J., Erkens, G., Kirschner, P. A., & Kanselaar, G. (2010). Task-related and social regulation during online collaborative learning. Metacognition and Learning. doi:10.1007/s11409-010-9061-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Omnibus multiple regression result for 101 groups across three studies; the four interaction factor scores jointly predicted group performance with R=.494, F(4,96)=23.471, p=.000. A MANOVA confirmed aggregation across studies was justified (Pilai's trace V=.11, F(8,192)=1.35, p=.22).

"In total, the four interaction factor scores explain almost 50% of the variance in group performance scores ( R=.494, F(4, 96)=23.471, p=.000)."
