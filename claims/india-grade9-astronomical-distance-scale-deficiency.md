`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Nationwide survey question asking students to arrange Sun, Moon, Stars, and Neptune by increasing distance from Earth. "only 35% of students could answer this question correctly," with stars misplaced in top alternative answers.

> "We found that only 35% of students could answer this question correctly. Looking at the top choices we note that students can arrange Sun, Moon, and Neptune accurately, but they are unsure about the placement of stars."
