This is the author's discussion-section observation from the two cohorts, describing students helping each other by providing feedback. One student suggested another remove the separate legend and integrate the key into the main visualisation.

> "Furthermore, because every student had a different dataset, it meant that they could help each other, and learn from each other. In a few classes, it was clear that the students came into the class and shared with each other what they had achieved that week."
