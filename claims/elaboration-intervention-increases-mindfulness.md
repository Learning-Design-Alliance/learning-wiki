## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Participants in the experimental group with elaboration intervention reported higher mindfulness (M = 4.69, SD = 0.47) than those without it (M = 4.19, SD = 0.93), a significant and large effect (ηp² = .88). [→ Wagner et al. 2013](#wagner-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### Wagner et al. 2013

Kai Wagner, Martin Klein, Eric Klopp, Thomas Puhl & Robin Stark. (2013). Effects of Instruction-Supported Learning with Worked Examples in Quantitative Method Training. Higher Education Studies, 3(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v3n3p1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-factorial ANOVA comparing the two experimental groups (n = 26 with, n = 27 without elaboration intervention) on a three-item mindfulness scale (Cronbach's α = .71), administered after the 60-minute training session. The article reports the effect as "significant and large ( F(1,51) = 6.86, p = .012, ηp² = .88".

> "The effect of the elaboration intervention was significant and large ( F(1,51) = 6.86, p = .012, ηp² = .88, power = .73). Participants of the experimental group with elaboration intervention ( M = 4.69; SD = 0.47) reported higher mindfulness than did participants of the experimental group without elaboration intervention ( M = 4.19; SD = 0.93)"
