`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In supplementary bivariate cross-lagged models for the 954-student cohort, adding body fat percentage to the model rendered the predictive association of aerobic and muscular fitness with future GPA marginally significant.

> "The predictive association of both aerobic and muscular fitness with future academic achievement became marginally significant when body fat percentage were added to the bivariate models"
