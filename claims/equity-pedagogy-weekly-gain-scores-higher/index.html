Phuong 2017¶

Phuong, A. E., Nguyen, J., & Mariea, D. (2017). Evaluating an Adaptive Equity-Oriented Pedagogy: A Study of its Impacts in Higher Education. The Journal of Effective Teaching, 17(2), 5-44. https://uncw.edu/jet

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated-measures weekly gain scores (posttest minus pretest on 10-question tests each class meeting) compared between the two sections. The article states "the treatment group outperformed the control group" consistently, with treatment gains rising to the low 60s versus about 30 points weekly in control.

"In the beginning, students in the treatment had an average gain score that started in the low 40s and eventually increased to the low 60s. By contrast, the students in the control group had an average gain score of about 30 points every week"
