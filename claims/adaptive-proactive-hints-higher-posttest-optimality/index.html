Evidence¶

Maniktala 2020¶

Maniktala, M., Cody, C., Isvik, A., Lytle, N., Chi, M., & Barnes, T. (2020). Extending the Hint Factory for the Assistance Dilemma: A Novel, Data-driven Help-Need Predictor for Proactive Problem-solving Help. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 4. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/450

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Controlled study in the Deep Thought logic tutor with 123 participants (111 completers: 66 Adaptive, 45 Control), stratified by pretest performance. The t-test on posttest optimality showed "a significant difference between the two conditions" with "a moderate effect size, Cohen's d = 0.4"; higher optimality reflects shorter, more efficient solutions.

"A t-test on the posttest optimality shows a signiﬁcant difference between the two conditions, t(110) = 1.74, p = .04, with a moderate effect size, Cohen's d = 0.4."
