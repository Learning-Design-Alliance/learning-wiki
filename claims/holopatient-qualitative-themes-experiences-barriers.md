## Evidence

### Kang Yun 2025

Kang Yun, Lee Insook. (2025). HoloPatient-based simulation education to improve nursing students’ learning motivation and attitude: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07987-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured focus group interviews with 10 students (two groups of 5) conducted one week after the simulation; member checking was used. The article states the sample may not have achieved data saturation.

> "Regarding participants’ views of the experiences in the HoloPatient-based simulation education, this study identified three main themes: positive experience, influencing factors, and barriers to HoloPatient-based simulation education"

### Kang Yun 2025 (2)

Kang Yun, Lee Insook. (2025). HoloPatient-based simulation education to improve nursing students’ learning motivation and attitude: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07987-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Barrier sub-theme from the same focus groups; some HoloLens wearers reported cybersickness symptoms, and some reported inconsistent Wi-Fi prevented quick access to the HoloPatient application.

> "Some participants reported that HoloLens wearers experienced symptoms of cybersickness such as nausea, headache, dizziness and difficulty concentrating"
