## Evidence

### Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro 2014

Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro, Wood Guilherme, Knops André, Krinzinger Helga, Lonnemann Jan, Starling-Alves Isabella, Willmes Klaus, Haase Vitor Geraldi. (2014). In How Many Ways is the Approximate Number System Associated with Exact Calculation? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111155

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression of ANS acuity on age run separately per group. The w decreased with age in TA children (β = −0.184, p = 0.015) but remained stable in MD children (β = −0.016, p = 0.897).

> "The w was found to decrease monotonically with age in the TA children (β = −0.184, p = 0.015), but it remained stable in the children with MD (β = −0.016, p = 0.897)."

### Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro 2014 (2)

Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro, Wood Guilherme, Knops André, Krinzinger Helga, Lonnemann Jan, Starling-Alves Isabella, Willmes Klaus, Haase Vitor Geraldi. (2014). In How Many Ways is the Approximate Number System Associated with Exact Calculation? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111155

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of estimation cv on age in both groups showed similar decreases (TA β = −0.338, p<0.001; MD β = −0.425, p = 0.006). The addition cv decreased in both groups but was only marginally significant in TA and non-significant in MD.

> "Second, the cv from non-symbolic estimation was found to monotonically decrease with age more or less to the same extent in both the TA and MD groups (TA: β = −0.338; p<0.001; MD: β = −0.425; p = 0.006)."

### Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro 2014 (3)

Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro, Wood Guilherme, Knops André, Krinzinger Helga, Lonnemann Jan, Starling-Alves Isabella, Willmes Klaus, Haase Vitor Geraldi. (2014). In How Many Ways is the Approximate Number System Associated with Exact Calculation? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111155

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A bootstrap analysis with 10,000 resampled samples of N = 40 found TA comparison coefficients were negative in 91.27% of samples versus only 53.67% in MD, confirming the trajectory difference was not a power artifact.

> "Crucially, TA showed significant improvement in non-symbolic comparison in contrast to their MD peers, who definitively did not show any sign of improvement in this task during development."
