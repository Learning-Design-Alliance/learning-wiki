## Evidence

### Neubauer R 2025

Neubauer R, Recker F, Weimer J, Jenssen C, Nürnberg D, Karlas T, Westerway SC, Ruppert JP, Daum N, Gschmack A, Dong Y, Möller K, Nourkami-Tutdibi N, Barth G, Şirli R, Lucius C, Prats M, Sänger SL, Keil M, Zervides C, Hoffmann B, Dietrich CF. (2025). Verifying learning success: assessment and certification of student ultrasound education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07469-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Steinmetz et al., a study in which students' self-assessed ultrasound skills exceeded external examiner evaluation "on average 68% better", indicating subjective self-ratings overestimate practical ultrasound competence.

> "A study by Steinmetz et al. revealed that students estimated their ultrasound skills on average 68% better in self-assessment than they would have been evaluated by an external examiner"

### Neubauer R 2025 (2)

Neubauer R, Recker F, Weimer J, Jenssen C, Nürnberg D, Karlas T, Westerway SC, Ruppert JP, Daum N, Gschmack A, Dong Y, Möller K, Nourkami-Tutdibi N, Barth G, Şirli R, Lucius C, Prats M, Sänger SL, Keil M, Zervides C, Hoffmann B, Dietrich CF. (2025). Verifying learning success: assessment and certification of student ultrasound education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07469-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes to Blanch-Hartigan's three meta-analyses the finding that students are "moderately able to self-assess" general medical knowledge and skills, with no significant overall over- or underestimation tendency; overestimation was more common in standardized patient encounters.

> "results of three meta-analysis conducted showed that students are moderately able to self-assess their general medical knowledge and skills, with accuracy improving over the course of medical school"
