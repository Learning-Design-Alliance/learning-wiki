### Phalet 2004

Phalet, K., Andriessen, I., & Lens, W. (2004). How Future Goals Enhance Motivation and Learning in Multicultural Classrooms. Educational Psychology Review, Vol. 16, No. 1. https://hdl.handle.net/1874/23520

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Spencer et al. (1999) and Steele and Aronson (1995), a series of experimental studies on stereotype threat specifying when concerns with self-identity negatively affect school achievement. No effect sizes are printed.

> "ethnic and gender identity inhibit academic perfor- mance in experimental task situations where negative stereotypes are made salient and self-relevant (Spencer et al., 1999; Steele and Aronson, 1995)."

### Phalet 2004 (2)

Phalet, K., Andriessen, I., & Lens, W. (2004). How Future Goals Enhance Motivation and Learning in Multicultural Classrooms. Educational Psychology Review, Vol. 16, No. 1. https://hdl.handle.net/1874/23520

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports, citing Shih et al. (1999), an experimental priming study in which positive stereotypes facilitated performance: "the priming of ethnic identity yields higher math scores" while gender-identity priming lowered them.

> "among Asian American female undergraduates, the priming of ethnic identity yields higher math scores, whereas the priming of gender identity results in lower math scores (Shih et al., 1999)."
