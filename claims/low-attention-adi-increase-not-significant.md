`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-hoc exploratory descriptive analysis of eight low-attention participants (week-1 ADI ≤ 0.619, evenly split across groups). An independent-samples t-test found the difference not statistically significant (t(6)=0.576, p=0.585); a Bayesian t-test (BF10=0.578) gave "anecdotal evidence for the null hypothesis".

> "For this subset of participants, those in the attentive group showed a greater average ADI increase (𝑀=+ 0.086,𝑆𝐷= 0.092) than their non-attentive counterparts (𝑀=+ 0.026,𝑆𝐷= 0.187) from week 1 to week 3."
