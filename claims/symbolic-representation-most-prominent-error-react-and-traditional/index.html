Evidence¶

Sari 2018¶

Sari, D.P., Darhim, & Rosjanuardi, R. (2018). Errors of students learning with REACT strategy in solving the problems of mathematical representation ability. Journal on Mathematics Education, 9(1), 121-128. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1173659

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest error analysis of 47 eighth-grade students in a quasi-experimental static-group comparison (REACT class of 23, traditional class of 24). Item 3 concerned the surface area of a pyramid; the printed error percentages were "47.83%" (REACT) and "91.67%" (traditional).

"Students who experienced error about number 3 who obtained learning with REACT strategy as much as 47.83%, while students who received traditional learning 91.67%."
