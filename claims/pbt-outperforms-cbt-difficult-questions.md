## Evidence

### James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann 2023

James Pengelley · Peter R. Whipp · Nina Rovis-Hermann. (2023). A Testing Load: Investigating Test Mode Effects on Test Score, Cognitive Load and Scratch Paper Use with Secondary School Students. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09781-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the counterbalanced repeated-measures study of 263 year 9 students, hierarchical linear modelling of test scores showed "students performed better on difficult PBT questions (M = 1.50, 95% CI = 0.73, 2.26) compared to difficult CBT questions (M = 1.03, 95% CI = 0.28, 1.79)"; the article states this difference represents 11.5% of the total possible score of 4.
