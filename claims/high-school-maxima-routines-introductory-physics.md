## Evidence

### Danilo T. Alves 2011

Danilo T. Alves, Silvio C. F. Pereira Filho. (2011). Computer algebra systems as tools for learning Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1104.4461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1104.4461)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

A pedagogical experience with public high-school students in which Maxima was chosen instead of Maple because it is free; students developed small routines such as the Conversor routine for transformations between multiples of the meter. The authors report students could "create question s, answer and test with the routines" and call the experience successful.

> "Again the students could apply their own routines, create question s, answer and test with the routines. It was again a successful experience."
