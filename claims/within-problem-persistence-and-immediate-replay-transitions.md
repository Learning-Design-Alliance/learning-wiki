## Evidence

### Shan Zhang 2026

Shan Zhang, Siddhartha Pradhan, Ji-Eun Lee, Ashish Gurung, and Anthony F. Botelho. 2026. Let Me Try Again: Examining Replay Behavior by Tracing Students' Latent Problem-Solving Pathways. LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Markov chain analysis of within-problem attempt sequences from 777 seventh graders using FH2T log data; transition matrices showed the strongest within-problem transitions were "persistence in ongoing states" and "immediate replay after successful solutions."

> "Within problems, the strongest transitions reflected persistence in ongoing states (e.g., incomplete→incomplete ) and immediate replay after successful solutions (optimal→replay_optimal_end )."
