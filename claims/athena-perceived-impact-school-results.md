## Evidence

### Andreas Müller 2020

Andreas Müller, Madeleine Rousset Grenon. (2020). Athéna – A pre-university study programme at the University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Questionnaire items on perceived impact on school results in the three-edition evaluation, scaled from -2 = very negative through 0 = no impact to +2 = very positive; pupils reported "+0.8" for the corresponding subject and "almost no impact (-0.1)" for other subjects.

> "In the subject corresponding to their Athena studies (physics or mathematics), the pupils generally perceive the impact on their school results as positive (+0.8). For other school subjects, the programme is perceived as having almost no impact (-0.1)."
