## Evidence

### Adhikari Yadav Saroj 2022

Adhikari Yadav Saroj, Poudel Sangeeta, Pandey Oshna, Jaiswal Dhiraj Prasad, Malla Bhupendra Prakash, Thakur Brajesh Kumar, Gautam Swotantra, Pandey Sirish Raj. (2022). Performance and preference of problem-based learning (PBL) and lecture-based classes among medical students of Nepal. F1000Research 11:183. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.107103.1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of PAHS medical students; 99 of 107 completed a 50-item vignette-based MCQ test, half covering PBL-taught and half lecture-taught topics. Scores were not normally distributed (Shapiro-Wilk p = 0.015 lectures, 0.024 PBL), so medians were compared: "16 for lectures and 17 for PBL".

> "Thus, the median score was computed, which was 16 for lectures and 17 for PBL, as shown in Table 1."
