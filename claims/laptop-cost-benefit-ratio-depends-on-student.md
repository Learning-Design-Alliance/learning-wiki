## Evidence

### Day Alison J. 2021

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct statistical comparison of the two correlations reported in Results; the article states the off-task relationship "was similar to the effect size of the relationship between slide viewing and exam score, r = 0.25, z = 0.15, p = 0.88" and both were small, each accounting for 5–6% of variance.

> "The negative relationship of off-task activity to exam score, r = -0.23, was similar to the effect size of the relationship between slide viewing and exam score, r = 0.25, z = 0.15, p = 0.88. Both effect sizes were small according to Cohen [42]"
