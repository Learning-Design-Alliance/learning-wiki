## Evidence

### Walker L 2017

Walker L, Warfa A-R M. (2017). Process oriented guided inquiry learning (POGIL®) marginally effects student achievement measures but substantially increases the odds of passing a course. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0186203

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 20 studies (Npogil = 2599, Ncontrol = 5277) using Hedge's g; the null hypothesis of no effect was rejected [z = 4.12, p < 0.0001]. The summary effect of "g+ = 0.29" places the median POGIL student at the 62nd percentile of the lecture group.

> "POGIL had a small but statistically significant effect on student achievement measures with an overall positive effect size [g+ = 0.29, 95% CI = 0.15 to 0.43, Fig 2]"
