### Krijnen 2024

Krijnen, M. A., Wansink, B. G. J., van den Berg, Y. H. M., van Tartwijk, J., & Mainhard, T. (2024). Citizenship in the Elementary Classroom Through the Lens of Peer Relations. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09870-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Hendrickx et al. (2016), that "a range of 0.06–0.29 for density" and centrality from 0.11 to 0.34 were found, meaning in some classrooms all children were about equally accepted while in others a few children held a dominant social position.

> "Hendrickx et al. (2016) reported a range of 0.06–0.29 for density (M = 0.14, SD = 0.04). This illustrates how some classrooms have stronger overall social connections than others. Furthermore, centrality ranged from 0.11 to 0.34 (M = 0.19, SD = 0.05)."
