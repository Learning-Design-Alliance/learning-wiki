Evidence¶

Matthew Johnson 2019¶

Matthew Johnson, Alicia Demers. (2019). Ewing Marion Kauffman School Year 7 Impacts. Oakland, CA: Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/ewing-marion-kauffman-school-year-7-impacts

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental evaluation matching Kauffman students to a comparison group of students in other Kansas City district and charter schools with similar demographics and 4th-grade achievement, then comparing outcomes in grades 5 through 11. The report states the school had "substantial positive impacts on student achievement growth" in all three subjects.

"During its first seven years of operation, the Kauffman School had substantial positive impacts on student achievement growth in mathematics, English language arts, and science, beyond the growth achieved by students in other Kansas City public schools."
