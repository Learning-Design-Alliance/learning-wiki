Hii 2013¶

Hii, S. C., & Fong, S. F. (2013). Effects of Multimedia-Based Graphic Novel Presentation on Critical Thinking among Students of Different Learning Approaches. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018032

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-hoc comparisons following the significant one-way ANCOVA main effect of treatment mode showed that "the students exposed to the GT mode obtained significantly higher mean posttest score compared to the students who were exposed to the GTN mode", suggesting redundancy of combined text and narration with graphics.
