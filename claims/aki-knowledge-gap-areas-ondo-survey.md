Assessment of Knowledge of Acute Kidney Injury among Non-Nephrology Doctors in Two Government Hospitals in Ondo City, Southwest, Nigeria. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v27i2.7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the same 81-doctor cross-sectional survey, reported as frequencies of correct answers on the 32-item questionnaire. The article reports "Only 33(40.7%) of the respondents knew that serum creatinine may be normal in patients with AKI".

> "Only 33(40.7%) of the respondents knew that serum creatinine may be normal in patients with AKI while 42(51.9%) knew that urine output may be adequate in AKI patients."
