### Zhelev Zhivko 2013

Zhelev Zhivko, Garside Ruth, Hyde Christopher. (2013). A qualitative study into the difficulties experienced by healthcare decision makers when reading a Cochrane diagnostic test accuracy review. Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1186/2046-4053-2-32

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparison across the three reviews read by different participant groups. The article reports that even participants who initially struggled "immediately grasped the implications of the results when the same information was presented as natural frequencies", and this format was welcomed by everybody who read the Galactomannan review.
