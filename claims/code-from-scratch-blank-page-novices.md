## Evidence

### Alberto Simões and Ricardo Queirós 2020

Alberto Simões and Ricardo Queirós. (2020). On the Nature of Programming Exercises. OpenAccess Series in Informatics, ICPEC 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.14476](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.14476)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Conceptual analysis in Section 3.1 of the Code from Scratch exercise type; the authors argue, without empirical data, that students face "the blank page syndrome" and may start with auxiliary code and focus on the evaluated algorithm too late.

> "For the students, they have a blank sheet, and they will need to code from scratch. In the student's point of view, this is the worst problem situation. Just like a writer or a painter, they may have the blank page syndrome."
