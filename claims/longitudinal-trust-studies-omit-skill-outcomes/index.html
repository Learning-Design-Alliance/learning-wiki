Related Claims¶

  • Error timing and stakes shape trust in AI systems: later errors may be more damaging, early errors may matter more, and trust recovers quickly after good post-error performance, with high-stakes errors causing abrupt collapse — related
  • The review reports some evidence that over-dependence on AI contributes to cognitive offloading and decreased independent reasoning, while guided AI use can be helpful — related
  • A systematic review reports that over-reliance on AI dialogue systems fosters complacency and dependency and may weaken students' decision-making — related
