Comparison analysis in the District Results section: Highline seventh graders using Ratio Rancher were compared to seventh grade math students who did not use the tool on benchmark ratio skills. Users "scored 7.3% higher than their counterparts"; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

### Luke 2016 (2)

Luke, Christina C., Ph.D. & Francisco, Aubrey T., Ph.D. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Lessons Learned from Math Trials in Six Districts. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/3d9bb6ec-6ef2-4b64-a130-ce9c19574443

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired T-test analysis of pre- and post-benchmark scores for Mathspace districts, reported in District Results. "students’ benchmark scores in Mineola and Vista increased by 17%"; Kettle Moraine and Rowan Salisbury saw modest but significant gains. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The analysis showed that students’ benchmark scores in Mineola and Vista increased by 17% from the beginning to the end of the pilot."
