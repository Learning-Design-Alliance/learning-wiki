### Awofala 2013

Awofala, A. O. A., Arigbabu, A. A., & Awofala, A. A. (2013). Effects of Framing and Team Assisted Individualised Instructional Strategies on Senior Secondary School Students' Attitudes toward Mathematics. Acta Didactica Napocensia, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1053630

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of post-treatment ATMI scores with pre-treatment scores as covariates showed a statistically significant main effect of treatment among the three groups (framing, TAI, traditional method).

> "Further analysis of the post-treatment attitudes scores of the students in the two experimental and one control groups using the Analysis of Covariance as contained in Table 2 below showed that the difference in means among the three groups was statistically significant ( F(2,349)=7.43, p=0.001, η2 p=0.42)."
