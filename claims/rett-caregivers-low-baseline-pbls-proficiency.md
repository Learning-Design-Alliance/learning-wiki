### Kocyigit S 2026

Kocyigit S, Akgun-Dogan O, Hatirnaz Ng O, Alanay Y, Guven F. (2026). Pilot study: the impact of simulation-based pediatric basic life support training on the performance of caregivers of children with Rett syndrome. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09158-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-training single-rescuer PBLS simulation assessment of ten caregivers using a 16-step checklist scored by two independent expert instructors. The article reports a mean of 15.9 out of 100.

> "Pre-training scores indicated limited PBLS proficiency (mean:15.9/100)."

### Kocyigit S 2026 (2)

Kocyigit S, Akgun-Dogan O, Hatirnaz Ng O, Alanay Y, Guven F. (2026). Pilot study: the impact of simulation-based pediatric basic life support training on the performance of caregivers of children with Rett syndrome. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09158-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Checklist-item analysis in the same pilot. The article notes that none of the caregivers had prior PBLS training or a healthcare background; all were laypersons without clinical experience.

> "Prior to the training, the success rate (defined as a score of 10) was low or nonexistent for most PBLS steps."
