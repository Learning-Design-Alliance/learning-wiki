Llorente, C., Pasnik, S., Moorthy, S., Hupert, N., Rosenfeld, D., & Gerard, S. (2015). Teacher survey pre/post data (Table 4) show large pre-to-post increases in both technology conditions, e.g., counting rose from 51% to 90% in the Supplement condition and from 33% to 84% in Technology & Media, with significance markers per contrast.
