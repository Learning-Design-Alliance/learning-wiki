## Evidence

### Marshall J 2025

Marshall J, Shiu C, Raatz M, Penman A, Beak K, Clarke S, Ward EC. (2025). Telesimulation for Training in Infant Feeding: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Dysphagia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00455-024-10746-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-groups main effect of time in the randomized controlled trial, using Greenhouse-Geisser correction after sphericity violation. Confidence "increased across the three timepoints" with partial ƞ2=0.594; change was significant pre- to post- and pre- to 4-weeks post.

> "Overall, for the main effect of time within groups, self-perceived confidence increased across the three timepoints, and this change was significant (F1.21,44.61=54.07; p < 0.001, partial ƞ2=0.594; ε = 0.603)"

### Marshall J 2025 (2)

Marshall J, Shiu C, Raatz M, Penman A, Beak K, Clarke S, Ward EC. (2025). Telesimulation for Training in Infant Feeding: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Dysphagia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00455-024-10746-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-groups test in the same trial showed "no significant difference in improvements in overall self-perceived confidence" between the telesimulation and in-person groups (partial ƞ2=0.025). All individual confidence statement changes were also significant regardless of modality.

> "There was no significant difference in improvements in overall self-perceived confidence measures between the in-person simulation and telesimulation groups (Mean difference telesim = 32.68; Mean difference in-person = 27.88; F1,37=0.958; p = 0.334; partial ƞ2=0.025)."
