### Rim Mekonnen Hadgu 2016

Rim Mekonnen Hadgu, Sophia Hoang-Vy Huynh, & Chaya Gopalan. (2016). The Use of Pre-recorded Lectures on Student Performance in Physiology. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v5n1p105

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group comparison of question-type performance among students who used PRL and those who did not: the PRL+ contrast was significant at p<0.01 and the PRL- contrast at p<0.05. The results figures report PRL+MQ at 81% versus PRL+CQ at 51%, and PRL-MQ at 72% versus PRL-CQ at 56%. No effect size is printed.
