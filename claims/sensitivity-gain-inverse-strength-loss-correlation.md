`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of T0–T1 change scores in the same cohort (Table 3), printing r² values of 0.396, 0.394 and 0.632 as variance-explained coefficients rather than standardised effect sizes. The authors note no studies have explicitly documented this relationship in a young healthy population and propose a neuromuscular-control readjustment as a possible explanation.

> "An increase in the sensitivity of the medial ring finger (r2 = 0.396*) and ulnar ring finger (r2 = 0.394*) in the dominant hand is associated with a loss of strength in the dominant hand, while in the non-dominant hand, the association is in the same direction, but in the meniscus (r2 = 0.632**)."
