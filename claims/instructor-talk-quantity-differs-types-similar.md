## Evidence

### Seidel 2015

Seidel, S. B., Reggi, A. L., Schinske, J. N., Burrus, L. W., & Tanner, K. D. (2015). Beyond the Biology: A Systematic Investigation of Noncontent Instructor Talk in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparative analysis of 25 individually taught class sessions (Instructor A n = 17, Instructor B n = 8; four cotaught sessions excluded), using two-tailed t tests with a Mann-Whitney posttest. The study reports "a significant difference in the overall quantity of Instructor Talk (p < 0.001)"; only a p-value is printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Instructor A used an average of 28 (SE = 2.6) quotes per class session (n = 17 class sessions), while Instructor B used an average of 10 (SE = 0.7) quotes per class session (n = 8 class sessions), showing a significant difference in the overall quantity of Instructor Talk (p < 0.001)."

### Seidel 2015 (2)

Seidel, S. B., Reggi, A. L., Schinske, J. N., Burrus, L. W., & Tanner, K. D. (2015). Beyond the Biology: A Systematic Investigation of Noncontent Instructor Talk in an Introductory Biology Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 14(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative-quantitative comparison of category and subcategory usage across the same two instructors' class sessions. The authors describe "a strong overlap in the categories and subcategories used," with both instructors using "all five categories" and 14 of 17 subcategories common to both.

> "Both instructors used all five categories of Instructor Talk, and 14 of the 17 subcategories were commonly used, as well."
