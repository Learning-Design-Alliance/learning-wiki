Gouvea 2017¶

Gouvea, J., Passmore, C. (2017). 'Models of' versus 'Models for'. Science & Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11191-017-9884-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument from the philosophy of science: the authors bring forward an agent-based account of models, drawing on conceptions of models in the philosophy of science, and argue that "the aims and intentions of a cognitive agent" are constitutive of what a model is. No empirical data are offered.

"Models, according to this account, can only be understood with respect to the aims and intentions of a cognitive agent (models for), not solely in terms of how they represent phenomena in the world (models of)."
