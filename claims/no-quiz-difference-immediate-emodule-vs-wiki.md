# Interactive eModules and a passive Wikipedia-like webpage produce no significant difference in immediate quiz scores for clinical neuroscience learning

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` First-quiz scores did not differ significantly between the eModule group (86%) and Wiki group (85%). [→ Rajan Kiran 2022](#rajan-kiran-2022)

## Evidence

### Rajan Kiran 2022

Rajan Kiran, Pandit Anand S. (2022). Comparing computer-assisted learning activities for learning clinical neuroscience: a randomized control trial. BMC Medical Education 22:522. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03578-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the randomized trial (16 per group), a ten multiple-choice-question quiz was completed immediately after the 20–30 min learning activity and compared with an unpaired two-tailed t-test; means were "86% (95% CI 82% to 91%)" versus 85%, p = 0.8.

> "The mean score of the first quiz was 86% (95% CI 82% to 91%) for the eModule group and 85% (95% CI 76% to 94%) for the Wiki group with no evidence of a statistical difference (p = 0.8)."
