Shihui Feng & Nancy Law. (2021). Mapping Artificial Intelligence in Education Research: A Network-based Keyword Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 31, 277–303. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00244-4

Authors' synthesis of knowledge clusters and high-degree keywords from meso- and micro-level analysis of the 1830-article corpus. The enumerated list continues with (5) affect, (6) engagement, and (7) learning design (e.g. inquiry learning, exploratory learning environment, prerequisite).

"we summarize that the prominent educational foci within AIED during 2010 –2019 are: (1) online learning; (2) game-based learning (e.g. educational game, gamification); (3) collaborative learning (e.g. computer supported collaborative learning, team); (4) assessment"
