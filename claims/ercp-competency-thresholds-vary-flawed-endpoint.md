## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Published ERCP competency thresholds range widely (from 35 to more than 350 procedures), leading the authors to conclude that absolute thresholds have limited value because trainees learn at variable rates and single interventions such as biliary cannulation do not capture global competence. [→ Yang D. 2020](#yang-d-2020)

## Evidence

### Yang D. 2020

Yang D., Wagh M.S., Draganov P.V. (2020). The status of training in new technologies in advanced endoscopy: from defining competence to credentialing and privileging. Gastrointestinal Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2020.05.047

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis of ERCP training studies: early studies suggested a threshold as low as "35", later target-measure studies "100 to 200 ERCPs", a Mayo Clinic study recommended "more than 350 ERCPs", and the 2015 systematic review reported 79 to 300. The review reports these as second-hand findings.

> "a systematic review published in 2015 reported that the threshold for competency, defined by a high rate of successful selective duct cannulation, ranged from 79 to 300 ERCPs"
