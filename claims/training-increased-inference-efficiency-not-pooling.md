# Training improved inference drawing by increasing the efficiency of transforming pooled information into inferences rather than by increasing how often information was pooled

## Evidence

### Deiglmayr 2011

Deiglmayr, A., & Spada, H. (2011). Training for fostering knowledge co-construction from collaborative inference-drawing. Learning and Instruction, 21(3), 441-451. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2010.06.004

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way mixed model ANOVA on information-pooling frequency in the 36-dyad experiment found "a positive main effect for information distribution" (partial η2 = .19) but no effect of kind of training; pooling of shared information was higher (M = 0.98, SD = 0.06) than of unshared information (M = 0.89 in both distribution conditions).

> "confirmed a positive main effect for information distribution , F(2, 64) = 7.91 , p < .01 , partial η2 = .19, but not for k ind of training, F(3, 32) = 1.76, ns"
