## Evidence

### Massimiliano Di Blasi 2023

Massimiliano Di Blasi, Ilaria De Angelis, Adriana Postiglione. (2023). The usage of Kahoot! during activities for schools on physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.06322](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.06322)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal feedback from the teachers of the involved classes, reported in the feedback section: students "had a lot of fun and enjoyed the whole course", and the teachers themselves were satisfied and would use the materials and Kahoot! with their pupils.

> "They told us not only that their students had a lot of fun and enjoyed the whole course, but also that themselves were satisﬁed, so much that they would have used both the materials we have proposed during our course and Kahoot! with their pupils."
