## Evidence

### Benjamin P. Koester 2016

Benjamin P. Koester, Galina Grom, and Timothy A. McKay. (2016). Patterns of Gendered Performance Difference in Introductory STEM Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis across the full array of 116 large courses, comparing male and female students' average grade anomalies. The article reports GPDs "as large as -0.28 and averaging -0.11 letter grades" and that after optimal matching "the average GPD across all STEM courses is still -0.10"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Even after optimal matching the average GPD across all STEM courses is still -0.10."
