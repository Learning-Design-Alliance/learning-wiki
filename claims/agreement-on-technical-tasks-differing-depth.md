## Subclaims
`q2 i?` While editors agree on scientific technical tasks, expected depth and detail of reviewer reports differs across journals, apparently linked to journal resources and editors' own abilities. [→ Glonti K 2019](#glonti-k-2019)

## Evidence

### Glonti K 2019

Glonti K, Boutron I, Moher D, Hren D. (2019). Journal editors' perspectives on the roles and tasks of peer reviewers in biomedical journals: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033421

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 56 journal editors; thematic analysis. The article reports this pattern in the results on assessing manuscript content, illustrated by journals employing "statistical review by default" versus relying on reviewers for statistics.

> "Generally, the level of detail expected of peer reviewers seemed to differ according to the resources that journals had, as well as the editors' own abilities."
