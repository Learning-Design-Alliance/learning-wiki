Kulik 2016¶

Kulik, J. A., & Fletcher, J. D. (2016). Effectiveness of Intelligent Tutoring Systems: A Meta-Analytic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315581420

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Primary meta-analysis of the 50 qualifying controlled evaluations. The article reports that gains were "larger than 0.25 standard deviations" — the What Works Clearinghouse threshold for substantive importance — in 39 of the 50 studies. No effect size is attached to this proportion finding.

"Students who received intelligent tutoring outperformed control students on posttests in 46 (or 92%) of the 50 studies. In 39 (or 78%) of the 50 studies, tutoring gains were larger than 0.25 standard deviations"
