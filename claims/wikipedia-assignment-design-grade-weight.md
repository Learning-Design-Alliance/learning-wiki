### Shani Evenstein Sigalov 2025

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Course-design section of the 222-educator questionnaire. Grade weighting was reported descriptively: "the assignment’s grade comprised less than 25% of the total course grade" for most, with about 11% allocating over 50% and 5% the entire grade.

> "for most respondents (63%), the assignment’s grade comprised less than 25% of the total course grade. Specifically, 18.6% allocated 10–15% of the total grade to the Wikipedia assignment, and 17.7% allocated 21–25%."
