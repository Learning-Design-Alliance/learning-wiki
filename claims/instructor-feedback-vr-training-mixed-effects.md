RCT (Strandbygaard et al., 2013) summarized in the review's VR table: the intervention group received up to three standardized instructor feedback sessions during virtual laparoscopy training; controls practiced independently until a predefined proficiency level. Both directions of effect were reported.

> "The intervention group required significantly less time and fewer repetitions to reach the proficiency level than the no-feedback group. The no-feedback group achieved a higher final performance score."
