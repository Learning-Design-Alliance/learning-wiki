## Evidence

### Mooney SS 2022

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within the prospective cohort, simulator performance improved on the time domain only: "mean time improvement 61 sec, 95% CI −116 to −5, P = 0.034" for novice- and experienced-trained trainees; no other LapSim domains changed.

> "The total time to complete a LapSim® salpingectomy reduced at six months (mean time improvement 61 sec, 95% CI −116 to −5, P = 0.034) for NT and ET but there were no other associations between LapSim® usage and other LapSim® domains at six months"

### Mooney SS 2022 (2)

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression linking six-month LapSim metrics to live OSA-LS scores found one association: VRS Blood Loss "inversely proportional to score for ‘care for the ovary, ovarian artery and pelvic side wall’" (adjusted P < 0.0001).

> "There was a single association found between LapSim® scores and OSA‐LS scores at six months; ‘Blood Loss’ (mL) on the VRS was inversely proportional to score for ‘care for the ovary, ovarian artery and pelvic side wall’ on OSA‐LS (adjusted P‐values < 0.0001)."
