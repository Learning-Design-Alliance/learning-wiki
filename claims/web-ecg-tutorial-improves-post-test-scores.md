## Evidence

### Rolskov Bojsen S 2015

Rolskov Bojsen S, Räder SBEW, Holst AG, Kayser L, Ringsted C, Hastrup Svendsen J, Konge L. (2015). The acquisition and retention of ECG interpretation skills after a standardized web-based ECG tutorial–a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0319-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomised study of 203 medical students who completed a pre-test, a 3h25m unsupervised web-based ECG tutorial, and an immediate post-test. The article reports "a significant improvement in the overall mean test score from pre-test to post-test" with the printed means and p-value; no effect size is printed.

> "The overall mean test score improved significantly from 52.7 (SD 16.8) in the pre-test to 68.4 (SD 12.3) in the post-test (p < 0.001)."
