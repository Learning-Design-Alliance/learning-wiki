Evidence¶

Victoria Jackson 2023¶

Victoria Jackson, Brittani Williams, and Jalil B. Mustaffa. (2023). Parent PLUS Loans Are a Double-Edged Sword for Black Borrowers. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/parent-plus-loans-are-a-double-edged-sword-for-black-borrowers

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The brief reports, citing a Century Foundation analysis of the Federal Reserve Survey of Household Economics and Decision Making, that "nearly 37% of Black parent borrowers said they were unable to pay their student loan bill in full or had to make a partial payment versus nearly 23% of White borrowers," and that 22.1% of Black parent borrowers had borrowed from retirement accounts versus 8.9% of White parent borrowers.

"nearly 37% of Black parent borrowers said they were unable to pay their student loan bill in full or had to make a partial payment versus nearly 23% of White borrowers."

Victoria Jackson 2023 (2)¶

Victoria Jackson, Brittani Williams, and Jalil B. Mustaffa. (2023). Parent PLUS Loans Are a Double-Edged Sword for Black Borrowers. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/parent-plus-loans-are-a-double-edged-sword-for-black-borrowers

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The same Century Foundation analysis, as reported by the brief, found that "after 10 years in repayment, Black parent borrowers whose children attended the top colleges for Black enrollment owed 96% of their original balance compared to only 47%" for White parent borrowers at top colleges for White enrollment.

"after 10 years in repayment, Black parent borrowers whose children attended the top colleges for Black enrollment owed 96% of their original balance compared to only 47% for White parent borrowers"
