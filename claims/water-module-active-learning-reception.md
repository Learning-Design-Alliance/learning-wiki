## Evidence

### Singer 2015

Singer, K. P., Foutz, T., Navarro, M., & Thompson, S. (2015). Investigating The Extent That An Integrative Learning Module Broadens The Perception Of First-Year Students About The Engineering Profession. American Journal of Engineering Education, 6(2), 99-110. https://www.cluteinstitute.com/ojs/index.php/AJEE/article/view/9603

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

End-of-semester interviews with Water Module students evaluating teaching methods. Students quoted appreciating "dealing with like actual situations as opposed to just like complete hypotheticals" and the active learning approach, even though they found it more challenging than passive methods.

> "Students in the “Water Module” group concurred that the course stimulated independent and critical thinking. Students admitted that they were unaccustomed to this teaching style, which caused some discomfort initially. However, in the end, they seemed to convey a positive attitude regarding the method."
