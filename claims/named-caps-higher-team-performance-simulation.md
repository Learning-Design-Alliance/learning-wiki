## Evidence

### Garnier Sylvain 2026

Garnier Sylvain, Mattatia Laurent, Boulet Nicolas, Bosselut Gregoire, Serrand Chris, Benenati Sylvain, Cuvillon Philippe, Muller Laurent, Brunel Lionel, Roger Claire. (2026). Impact of named caps on anesthesia and intensive care team performance: a randomized simulation study. Brazilian Journal of Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjane.2026.844812

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled simulation study; 112 participants in 32 sessions assessed by 19 instructors using mixed-effects linear regression with random intercepts for team. The named-cap group scored higher on the "44-point TEAM score (37 ± 7 vs. 33 ± 7; p = 0.026)" and the total score.

> "The TEAM score was significantly higher in the intervention group than in the control group, both for the 44-point TEAM score (37 ± 7 vs. 33 ± 7; p = 0.026) and for the total score (45 ± 8 vs. 40 ± 9; p = 0.020)."
