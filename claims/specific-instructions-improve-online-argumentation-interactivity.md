### Spatariu 2010

Spatariu, A., & Winsor, D. L. (2010). A Qualitative Perspective: Pre-Service Teachers Discuss Educational Technology Online. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102777

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preliminary t-test analysis comparing treatment (N=23) and control groups in an online Educational Psychology course discussion over two weeks. The article reports treatment means of M= 3.088 and M= 2.934 for argumentation, "performed better both weeks in the treatment group" than control.

> "argumentation was measured and results indicated subjects performed better both weeks in the treatment group (first week M= 3.088, SD= .361; second week M= 2.934, SD= .816; N=23) than in the control group"
