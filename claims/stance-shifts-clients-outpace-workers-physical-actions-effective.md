## Evidence

### Nummijoki 2018

Nummijoki, J., Engeström, Y., & Sannino, A. (2018). Defensive and expansive cycles of learning: A study of home care encounters. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 27(2), 224-264. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2017.1412970

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of stance shifts across the four detailed cases. The authors report workers shifted five times each direction, note no significant difference in direction frequency, and observe clients shifted stances nearly twice as often as workers.

> "the clients shifted 10 times from a defensive action to an expansive action and eight times from an expansive action to a defensive action"

### Nummijoki 2018 (2)

Nummijoki, J., Engeström, Y., & Sannino, A. (2018). Defensive and expansive cycles of learning: A study of home care encounters. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 27(2), 224-264. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2017.1412970

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors compare Excerpt 1 (verbal hint extinguished) with Excerpts 2 and 6 (physical-action initiatives that triggered shifts), concluding that physical-action initiatives were more effective triggers than verbal hints alone.

> "Initiatives taken by one party in the form of or tightly connected to physical actions seemed to be more effective in this respect"
