Ghimire R, Kharel S, Giri S, Hamilton AJ. (2024). The Growing Need for Web-Based Simulation in Low- and Middle-Income Countries. JNMA J Nepal Med Assoc. https://doi.org/10.31729/jnma.8814

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of telesimulation studies, citing works such as Mikrogianakis et al. and Patel et al. The review reports that "enhanced self-reported knowledge and confidence have been achieved through tele simulation" at levels in line with in-person simulation. Outcomes are self-reported; no effect sizes are printed.
