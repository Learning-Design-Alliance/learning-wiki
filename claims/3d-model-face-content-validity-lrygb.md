`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert questionnaire study in which 5 experts performed LRYGB on both 3D-printed and porcine models and rated face validity on Likert items (Mann-Whitney tests). Experts gave the models median scores of 4–5 on most items, with "higher aesthetics, size, and the appearance of the stomach (P < 0.05)" for silicone.

> "Compared with porcine models, silicone models showed higher aesthetics, size, and the appearance of the stomach (P < 0.05), while exhibiting lower reaction to the ultrasonic knife and suture tactile sensations of silicone small intestine (P < 0.05)."

BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03141-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert questionnaire comparison of content validity between the two models after performing LRYGB on each. The article reports "no significant difference in content validity between the two models (P > 0.05)"; technical realism differed only for ligament of Treitz identification (P = 0.008, favoring 3D) and side-to-side anastomosis (P = 0.016, favoring porcine).

> "There was no significant difference in content validity between the two models (P > 0.05)."
