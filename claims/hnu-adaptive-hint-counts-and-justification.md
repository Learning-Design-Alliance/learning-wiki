# HNU Adaptive students received significantly more total hints than Control, with no difference in on-demand hints or overall hint justification rate

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Total hints received differed significantly between conditions (Adaptive: 37 vs Control: 9), driven by proactive hints, while on-demand hint counts did not differ. [→ Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022](#mehak-maniktala-min-chi-tiffany-barnes-2022)
`q2 i?` Overall hint justification rate did not differ significantly between conditions (Adaptive 93% vs Control 86%), suggesting proactive hints were well accepted. [→ Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022 (2)](#mehak-maniktala-min-chi-tiffany-barnes-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled study hint-usage analysis: on-demand hints did not differ significantly (Adaptive mean = 10, SD = 11; Control mean = 9, SD = 11, p = .28), but total hints differed significantly (p < .01) because Adaptive students additionally received proactive hints (mean 27 proactive hints, 19.6% of steps).

> "we observed no signiﬁcant diﬀerences in the number of on-demand hint between the two HNU conditions ( U = 583.5, z = 1,1, p = .28), with HNU Adaptive condition having Mean = 10 ( SD = 11), and Control having Mean = 9 ( SD = 11). Next, a Mann Whitney U test on the total hints for the two HNU conditions shows a signiﬁcant diﬀerence ( U = 82, z = 6.5, p < .01)."

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022 (2)

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled study hint justification rate analysis: overall HJR did not differ significantly between conditions (Adaptive mean = 93%, SD = 6%; Control mean = 86%, SD = 25%, p = .44). On-demand HJR was significantly higher for Adaptive (96% vs 86%, p = .02).

> "the total hints HJRs are high for both conditions (Adaptive: Mean = 93%, SD = 6%; Control: Mean = 86%, SD = 25%), and no signiﬁcant diﬀerences were found between the two conditions (U = 562.5, z = 0.16, p = .44)"
