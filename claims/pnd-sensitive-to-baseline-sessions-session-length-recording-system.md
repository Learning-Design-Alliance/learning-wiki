## Evidence

### Pustejovsky 2018

Pustejovsky, J. E. (2018). Procedural sensitivities of effect sizes for single-case designs with directly observed behavioral outcome measures. Psychological Methods. https://osf.io/hkzsm/

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Monte Carlo simulation using the alternating renewal process model for state behaviors, with 2000 simulated phase-pairs per condition. The simulation found PND sensitive to baseline sessions, session length, and recording system, "but not to the number of sessions in the treatment phase."

> "Results in the top row of Figure 1 indicate that PND is sensitive to the number of sessions in the baseline phase, observation session length, and recording system, but not to the number of sessions in the treatment phase."
