## Evidence

### An et al. 2013

Song An, Mary Margaret Capraro, Daniel A. Tillman. (2013). Elementary Teachers Integrate Music Activities into Regular Mathematics Lessons: Effects on Students' Mathematical Abilities. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/0js732gf

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-tests on five pre and five post MSA assessments for the third-grade class (n=25). The article reports the strategy gain "with an effect size of 3.44", and separately reports modeling effect size 3.40 and application effect size 3.00 in the same results passage.

> "the third grade students improved from 1.84±0.52 on the five pretests to 3.39 ±0.38 on the five posttests with an effect size of 3.44"
