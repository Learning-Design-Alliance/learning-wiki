Dignath 2022¶

Dignath, C., Rimm-Kaufman, S., van Ewijk, R., & Kunter, M. (2022). Teachers' Beliefs About Inclusive Education and Insights on What Contributes to Those Beliefs: a Meta-analytical Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09695-0

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Separate meta-analysis of Cohen's d between special and regular education teachers, computed for cognitive appraisals because too few studies reported subgroup means for the other components. The synthesis reports "more positive views about inclusion than regular education teachers (d = 0.41)".
