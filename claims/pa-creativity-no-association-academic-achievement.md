## Evidence

### Vasilopoulos Fotini 2023

Vasilopoulos Fotini, Jeffrey Holly, Wu Yanwen, Dumontheil Iroise. (2023). Multi-level meta-analysis of whether fostering creativity during physical activity interventions increases their impact on cognitive and academic outcomes during childhood. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-35082-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-regressions across 47 academic achievement studies (114 effect sizes, sample size 14,386), plus mathematics (42 studies) and language (32 studies) sub-analyses, testing creativity rating as predictor; all non-significant, including sensitivity analyses.

> "There was no association between creativity rating of the interventions and their effect size on academic achievement overall, or mathematics and language outcomes separately, even after influential studies were excluded"
