Catherine De Rijdt 2012¶

Catherine De Rijdt, Janine van der Rijt, Filip Dochy, Cees van der Vleuten. (2012). Rigorously selected and well trained senior student tutors in problem based learning: student perceptions and study achievements. Instructional Science, 40, 397-411. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-011-9173-6

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Focus group finding coded under the domain specific expertise factor; students said staff tutors use more difficult terminology and are more aware of latest developments, yet this mattered little to them in year one.

"Nevertheless, respondents noted that in the ﬁrst year domain speciﬁc expertise is not so very important."
