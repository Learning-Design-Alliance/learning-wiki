# Input-based FFI was more effective than output-based FFI at both beginner and intermediate proficiency levels, and both types showed larger effects for intermediate learners

## Evidence

### Schenck 2024

Schenck, A. (2024). Learning How to Use Input-Based and Output-Based Form-Focused Instruction: A Meta-Analytic Comparison of Persian EFL Learners. Journal of Second Language Acquisition and Teaching, 30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1457419

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analytic comparison by proficiency designation (Table 3: beginner input mean .9881, output .9355; intermediate input 3.6230, output 3.3086). Only 28 of 41 treatment groups had proficiency designations; none were advanced.

> "Input-based instruction was more effective at both the beginner and intermediate levels. At the beginner level, the difference was .05, which is a nominal difference in effect."
