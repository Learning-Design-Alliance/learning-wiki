## Evidence

### Onah BI 2023

Onah BI, Ede MO, Eze BN, Uzoegwu CR, Mgboji C, Agbo GC, Uzuagu AU, Chiamagu C, Agbo PA, Oneli JO, Ogbueghu SN. (2023). Effectiveness of rational emotive behavior education (REBE) on computer anxiety among school teachers enrolled in e-learning. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000034303

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA results from the randomized controlled trial across pretest, posttest, and follow-up; the article reports "no significant difference among teachers with gender" at all three levels, printing only test statistics and partial eta squared values.

> "there was no significant difference among teachers with gender in the mean computer anxiety score in Nigeria, F (1101) = 0.131, P = .718, ηp2 = 0.002; F (1101) = 0.511, P = .477, ηp2 = 0.006; and F (1101) = .088, P = .767, ηp2 = 0.001, respectively."
