# In Experiment 3, norm and desire posterior probabilities followed distinct patterns, and the Independent Effect model best predicted judgments, indicating norm inference is independent of desire inference

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Duan 2026

Duan, J., Guo, X., Zheng, L. & Yin, J. (2026). A Bayesian perspective on observers' inference of group norms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00405-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 3 (N = 32) measured both norm- and desire-related posterior probabilities (Table 1: norm 0.32–0.85 across 25–100%; desire 0.55–0.74). In LOO-CV the IE model had the smallest LOOIC (-20.34) versus FC (-15.47) and DM (-10.70), and the DM model did not positively predict judgments (Pr (β > 0) = 31.61%).
