## Evidence

### Seeskin 2022

Seeskin, A., Massion, T., and Usher, A. (2022). Elementary on-track: Elementary school students' grades, attendance, and future outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/middle-grades-network-improvement-community

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of CPS students in grades 3-8 from the 2010-11 school year (N=93,617 across Figures 8-9), linking end-of-year GPA and attendance categories to four-year high school graduation and immediate college enrollment. The report states students in the On-Track category graduated "at over twice the rate of students in the Intensive Support category."

> "students in grades 3-8 in the On-Track category graduated high school at over twice the rate of students in the Intensive Support category (93 percent vs. 39 percent) and enrolled in college at over four times the rate of students in the Intensive Support category (72 percent vs. 16 percent)."
