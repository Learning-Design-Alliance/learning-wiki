Barbara B. Lockee 2021¶

Barbara B. Lockee. (2021). Shifting digital, shifting context: (re)considering teacher professional development for online and blended learning in the COVID-19 era. Education Tech Research Dev 69. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09836-8

The author's interpretive argument (type e) in the abstract: the framework is flagged as potentially limited because "teachers are experiencing appreciably different learning and performance contexts" under the mandated shift. No data are offered for this assertion.

"A potential limitation of the resulting framework in this study is that TPD for OBL needs may currently differ, as teachers are experiencing appreciably different learning and performance contexts related to the mandated shift in professional practice to address continuity of instruction."
