## Evidence

### Qi Liu 2025

Qi Liu, Siyu Zhu, Can Liu, Yanmiao Yang, Chunmei Lan, Xinwei Song, Benjamin Becker, Keith M. Kendrick, Xinqi Zhou & Weihua Zhao. (2025). Neural signals gradually shift towards inferior frontal gyrus and long-term effects following facial imitation-based social learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00372-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Parallel mediation analysis in R (version 4.2.3) using improved MNS NPS (fNIRS) and MNS–OFC informational connectivity (fMRI) as mediators: total effect path c = 2.265 (p = 0.018), direct effect c' = 2.010 (p = 0.037), and "the improvement in positive imitative learning performance by repetitive FISL was mediated by NPS of the MNS"; only the MNS–OFC pathway among tested IC paths was significant.

> "the improvement in positive imitative learning performance by repetitive FISL was mediated by NPS of the MNS (indirect effect 1 = 0.834, 95% CI = [0.312, 1.443])"
