## Evidence

### Nakayama N 2021

Nakayama N, Ejiri H, Arakawa N, Makino T. (2021). Stress and anxiety in nursing students between individual and peer simulations. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.680

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Baseline STAI-JYZ measurement before simulation practice in the same observational study (individual n = 50; peer n = 59). The article reports the scores "did not differ between the individual and peer simulations"; equivalence was not tested and no effect sizes were printed.

> "The scores of state and trait anxiety before practice as obtained by the STAI did not differ between the individual and peer simulations (p = .352, 0.077; Figure 3)."
