The fairness chapter reports Mantel-Haenszel DIF analyses using ETS classification levels on operational item data; the summary states "the vast majority of items show negligible DIF across gender and racial/ethnic groups." No effect-size statistic is printed in the available text.

"Item content is reviewed for bias and sensitivity, with differential item functioning (DIF) analyses indicating that the vast majority of items show negligible DIF across gender and racial/ethnic groups."
