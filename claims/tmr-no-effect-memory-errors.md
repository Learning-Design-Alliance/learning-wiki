## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Memory error showed no significant main or interaction effects of TMR or feedback in the post-learning (ps > 0.487) or delayed phase (ps > 0.230), and no significant TMR effect post-TMR. [→ Danni Chen 2024](#danni-chen-2024)

## Evidence

### Danni Chen 2024

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Memory-error ANOVA on the absolute numerical difference between recalled and presented peers' ratings in the post-TMR phase: no significant main effect of TMR and no significant interaction; the text prints "no signiﬁcant main effect of TMR." A higher-vs-lower trend (F(1, 33) = 4.01, p = 0.054) was also non-significant.

> "However, no signiﬁcant main effect of TMR (F (1, 33) = 0.96, p = 0.333, η2 G < 0.01), and the interaction effect was observed (F (1, 33) = 0.02,p = 0.879,η2 G < 0.01)"
