Member-check analysis: the LLM classified Diego's utterance "I feel like it's twenty-eight for some reason" as Claim and Disagree; Diego explained it was a guess. The article states "There were no codes in the coding scheme for brainstorming or guessing, so this function was missed."

> "Diego clarified, "No, it was just like a guess, I was just guessing I wasn’t claiming that it was.. wasn’t denying it wasn’t also 28." In this moment, Diego was able to make a guess about a possible solution and hold multiple possibilities in his head."
