### Caballero 2012

Caballero, M. D., Wilcox, B. R., Pollock, S. J., & Pepper, R. E. (2012). ACER: A Framework on the Use of Mathematics in Upper-division Physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1063/1.4789659](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789659)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observation from the think-aloud interview studies of eight classical mechanics students. Although students mapped meaning onto symbols and focused on relevant variables, "seven participants" used the variable-small-compared-to-1 criterion indiscriminately.

> "However, seven participants claimed their various expansions provided a “good” approximation to the original expressions if the variable was “small compared to 1” regardless of the expression under consideration or the presence of a natural comparative"
