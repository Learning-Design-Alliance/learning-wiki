### Antonie Knigge 2022

Antonie Knigge, Ineke Maas, Kim Stienstra, Eveline L. de Zeeuw and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2022). Delayed tracking and inequality of opportunity: Gene-environment interactions in educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 7:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00122-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Selection analysis (Table 3) of tracking choice by parental education in the twin sample, tested with a Rao–Scott corrected chi-square test (p = 0.111, not significant). The article reports delayed tracking "hoovers around the average of 27.0%" across parental-education categories, providing reassurance that parental SES does not confound the main association.

> "No matter what the education of parents, the percentage of children with delayed tracking hoovers around the average of 27.0% (from low to high parental education: 23.5%, 27.1%, 29.3%, 25.9%)."
