Irfan Ullah 2018¶

Irfan Ullah, Rabia Tabassum and Muhammad Kaleem. (2018). Effects of Peer Tutoring on the Academic Achievement of Students in the Subject of Biology at Secondary Level. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 112. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8030112

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Knowledge-level subscale of the 32-item posttest in the same equivalent-group experiment (n = 20 per group); t = 3.83 exceeded the 0.05 table value, rejecting the null hypothesis for the knowledge level of the cognitive domain.

"The mean (20.6) of the experimental group was higher than that of the control group (15.5) on the posttest. The difference between t-value 3.83 > 2.04 and table value at 0.05 level was significant."
