The commentary reports simple-slope analyses from a classroom spelling study showing an aptitude-treatment interaction: interleaving helped "students with high and average levels of prior knowledge ... but not for those with low prior knowledge". No effect size is printed in this passage.

> "Klimovich and Richter (2025) demonstrate through simple-slope analyses that interleaving improves spelling skills for students with high and average levels of prior knowledge (i.e., students who had committed few or an average numbers of errors already at pretest), but not for those with low prior knowledge."
