### Szucs Denes 2013

Szucs Denes, Devine Amy, Soltesz Fruzsina, Nobes Alison, Gabriel Florence. (2013). Developmental dyscalculia is related to visuo-spatial memory and inhibition impairment. Cortex, 49(10). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2013.06.007

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the 12 DD vs 12 control comparison, verbal STM (Digit Recall, Word Recall) and verbal WM (Listening Recall and Processing) measures were non-significant between groups, contrasting with robust visuo-spatial memory differences.

> "In contrast, verbal STM/WM were intact including both digit and word span."
