Evidence¶

Fulya Turk 2017¶

Fulya Turk. (2017). Evaluation of the Effects of Conflict Resolution, Peace Education and Peer Mediation: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Education Studies; Vol. 11, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p25

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis across the 23 meta-analyzed studies comparing conflict resolution (8 studies), peace education (4) and peer mediation (11). Peace education showed the largest point estimate, "a value of g= 1,892", but the program comparison was not statistically significant (Q = 1.460 < 5.991).

"the effect size values for studies in which peace edu cation programs have been used are greater in comparison with the other education programs with a value of g= 1,892"
