### Galuschka Katharina 2014

Galuschka Katharina, Ise Elena, Krick Kathrin, Schulte-Körne Gerd. (2014). Effectiveness of Treatment Approaches for Children and Adolescents with Reading Disabilities: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0089900

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of ten phonics instruction comparisons that conducted spelling tests before and after treatment. The pooled Hedges g effect size was g = 0.336 with a 95% CI of [0.062, 0.610] and I2 = 22%, described as "a small but statistically significant mean effect size".

> "Ten studies evaluated the effect of phonics instruction on spelling performance. These revealed a small but statistically significant mean effect size (g' = 0.336; 95% CI [0.062, 0.610]; I 2 = 22%)."
