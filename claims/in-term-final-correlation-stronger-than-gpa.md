### David J. Webb 2025

David J. Webb, Cassandra A. Paul. (2025). High stakes exams inflate a gender gap and contribute to systematic grading errors in one introductory physics series. [doi:10.1103/ngrs-dxtf](https://doi.org/10.1103/ngrs-dxtf)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across the database of classes in the introductory physics series, reported in Section IV C as justification for using in-term scores as controls in the final-exam HLM. The article prints r = 0.65 and r ≈ 0.56 but no standardized effect-size framing, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "we find a correlation coefficient between the in-term exam aver- age and the final exam score of 0.65 (withp <10 −3) but a smaller correlation between student GPA and either in-term or final exam score (both correlation coefficients are about 0.56,p <10 −3)."
