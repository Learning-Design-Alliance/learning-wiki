## Evidence

### Nawka MT 2020

Nawka MT, Hanning U, Guerreiro H, Flottmann F, Van Horn N, Buhk J-H, Fiehler J, Frölich AM. (2020). Feasibility of a customizable training environment for neurointerventional skills assessment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238952

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Controlled comparison of six physicians (three inexperienced, three experienced) catheterizing three 3D-printed aneurysm models on the HANNES simulator, with fluoroscopy-recorded times. The study reports "median; IQR: 47; 30-84s" for inexperienced versus 21; 13-58s for experienced operators, p = 0.011 (Mann-Whitney U).

> "Inexperienced operators showed significantly longer probing times for all three aneurysm models (median; IQR: 47; 30-84s) than experienced operators (21; 13-58s, p = 0.011)."
