Schroeder 2026¶

Schroeder, N. L., Ambarwati, Y. E., Zhang, Y., & Zhai, C. (2026). A Benchmark for LLM's Understanding of Middle School and High School Science. https://github.com/InviteInstitute/MS-NGSS-Aligned-Benchmark

q2 · i? · design · r2

Item-level psychometric analysis (Part II, RQ2) of difficulty and discrimination for every item across standards and question types, with items classified as trivial (95%+ correct) or broken (5% or less correct). Table 5 reports per-standard means; HS-ETS1 recall reached 1.00 difficulty and 1.00 trivial share.

"generally speaking, we observed high mean difficulty and low item discrimination. There was only one standard and question type (MS-LS2, Recall) with a single broken question at the middle school level, but we saw a range of trivial questions depending on the standard and question type. At times, the number of trivial questions exceeded 90%."
