> "Newton and Salvi (2020) completed a systematic review of research into educators' belief in and use of learning styles. They identified 37 studies from 2009 to early 2020, which captured the insights of 15,405 educators from 18 countries. In summary, 89% of teachers agreed that students learn better when taught with their preferred learning style."
