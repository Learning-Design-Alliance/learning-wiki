## Evidence

### Brockbank 2025

Brockbank, E., Verma, A., Lloyd, H., Huey, H., Padilla, L., & Fan, J. E. (2025). Evaluating convergence between two data visualization literacy assessments. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-025-00622-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Combined-sample analysis of 1,113 US adults completing both assessments in one session; the article reports mean accuracy of GGR 80.4% and VLAT 74.6% and a correlation of "ρ = 0.56" between individual scores, with no standardized effect size beyond the correlation.

> "there was also substantial individual variability in test performance (GGR SD = 15.0%, VLAT SD = 15.1%), with an individual's score on one test being moderately predictive of their score on the other (ρ = 0.56, 95% CI: [0.52, 0.60 ]"
