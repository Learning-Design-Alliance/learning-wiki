### Wee H.G. 2022

Wee H.G., Yang Z., Xing L., Tsang K.K.-T., Ruan H.D., Li Y. (2022). Nighttime Sleep Awakening Frequency and Its Consistency Predict Future Academic Performance in College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19052933

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive correlational analysis in the 45-student sample comparing GPA for the sleep-tracking semester courses (mean 3.06, SD 0.51) with GPA for all compulsory psychology courses taken afterwards (mean 3.08, SD 0.50). The printed effect size was r = 0.758, p < 0.001.

> "The correlation between the two types of GPAs was statistically significant (r = 0.758, p < 0.001), indicating a high stability of academic achievement."
