Evidence¶

Narang 2025¶

Narang, Unnati, Vishal Sachdev, and Ruichun Liu. (2025). When AI Wears Many Hats: The Role of Generative Artificial Intelligence in Marketing Education. Journal of Public Policy & Marketing. https://doi.org/10.1177/07439156251328237

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of 14 full-time marketing faculty covering familiarity with AI (5-point scale), current incorporation of AI, areas of use, and perceived effectiveness by role. The article reports "64% of marketing educators incorporate AI in their courses" and that most use GAI as a tool, followed by teammate and tutor.

"The responses revealed that 42% of the marketing educators consider themselves to be very or extremely familiar (i.e., ratings of four and above) with GAI technologies and that 64% of marketing educators incorporate AI in their courses."
