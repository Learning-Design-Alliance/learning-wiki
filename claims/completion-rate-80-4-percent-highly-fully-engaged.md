Platform log analysis of the pilot cohort using a mark-as-completed button as an engagement proxy, with engagement categories assigned via "cut-off points by observing the distribution". The authors report that "80.4% of students were highly or fully engaged".

> "Using how many topics students have marked as completed, i.e., the completion rate, as a proxy for engagement, we found that 80.4% of students were highly or fully engaged."
