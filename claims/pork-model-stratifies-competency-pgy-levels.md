## Evidence

### Jin Piaopiao 2026

Jin Piaopiao, Weng Xiaoyu, Zhang Xiaoyu, Fang Tian, Geng Lei, Chen Shaohua, Liang Tingbo, Hu Qida. (2026). A highly feasible simulation model based on nipple-preserving pork portions for practicing superficial mass resection. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03840-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

In this single-institution feasibility study, 56 surgery residents (24 PGY-1, 20 PGY-2, 12 PGY-3) performed superficial mass resection on the pork-based model and were scored with a procedure-specific rating scale. The results "suggesting the pork-based simulation model be able to stratify different levels of surgical competency performance".

> "Their final scores varied from 32 to 100, and the performance scores of the PGY-2 and PGY-3 residents were significantly higher than those of PGY-1 residents (68.17 ± 12.14 points for PGY-1; vs. 81.85 ± 14.58 points for PGY-2, p = 0.01; vs. 86.25 ± 12.14 points for PGY-3, p < 0.01; Tukey's test)"

### Jin Piaopiao 2026 (2)

Jin Piaopiao, Weng Xiaoyu, Zhang Xiaoyu, Fang Tian, Geng Lei, Chen Shaohua, Liang Tingbo, Hu Qida. (2026). A highly feasible simulation model based on nipple-preserving pork portions for practicing superficial mass resection. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03840-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Surgical DOPS evaluations across PGY groups on the pork-based model confirmed that "PGY-2 and PGY-3 residents had better competency performance quantified in global summary level" than PGY-1 residents, with printed means and p values.

> "PGY-2 and PGY-3 residents had better competency performance quantified in global summary level in comparison to PGY-1 residents (2.13 ± 1.19 for PGY-1; vs. 2.95 ± 1.15 for PGY-2, p = 0.04; vs. 3.42 ± 0.79 for PGY-3, p < 0.01; Tukey's test)"

### Jin Piaopiao 2026 (3)

Jin Piaopiao, Weng Xiaoyu, Zhang Xiaoyu, Fang Tian, Geng Lei, Chen Shaohua, Liang Tingbo, Hu Qida. (2026). A highly feasible simulation model based on nipple-preserving pork portions for practicing superficial mass resection. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03840-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

All trainees completed the simulated operation on the pork-based model within 15 min. Completion time was compared across PGY groups by one-way ANOVA with Tukey's test, showing "the mean operation time of the PGY-3 residents was shorter than the other residents".

> "the mean operation time of the PGY-3 residents was shorter than the other residents (12.77 ± 0.63 min for PGY-3; vs. 13.61 ± 0.94 min for PGY-2, p = 0.02; vs. 13.69 ± 0.89 min for PGY-1, p = 0.01; Tukey's test post one-way ANOVA; Fig. 2a)"

### Jin Piaopiao 2026 (4)

Jin Piaopiao, Weng Xiaoyu, Zhang Xiaoyu, Fang Tian, Geng Lei, Chen Shaohua, Liang Tingbo, Hu Qida. (2026). A highly feasible simulation model based on nipple-preserving pork portions for practicing superficial mass resection. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03840-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

The comparison between PGY-3 and PGY-2 residents on the rating scale showed no statistically significant difference (p = 0.70); equivalence was not tested. The authors interpret that the procedure suits basic rather than advanced training.

> "the PGY-3 residents achieved slightly, but not significantly, higher performance scores compared to the PGY-2 residents (p = 0.70; Tukey's test), indicating that superficial mass resection is suitable for basic surgical training rather than an advanced training program"
