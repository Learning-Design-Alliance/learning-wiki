Evidence¶

Dwi Sulisworo 2017¶

Dwi Sulisworo, Novitasari Sutadi. (2017). Science Learning Cycle Method to Enhance the Conceptual Understanding and the Learning Independence on Physics Learning. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), 6(1). http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Separate MANOVA analysis for the independence-learning outcome in the same quasi-experiment. The article reports F = 519.518 and R2 = 0.896, stating "89.6% of students’ learning independence was affected by the learning method, while 10.4% is influenced by other variables".

"The learning method variable also has a significant influence on students’ independence learning. It was concluded by the value of F equal to 519.518 on error level less than 5%. The determination coefficient (R 2) is 0.896"
