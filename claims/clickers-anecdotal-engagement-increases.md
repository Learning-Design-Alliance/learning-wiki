## Evidence

### M. Alamgir Hossain 2021

M. Alamgir Hossain, Petra M. Menz, and John M. Stockie. (2021). An Open-Access Clicker Question Bank for Numerical Analysis. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113](https://doi.org/10.1080/10511970.2021.1954113)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anecdotal instructor observations comparing the January–April 2020 clicker semester with two other recent offerings of the same course, covering attendance, before/after-class questions, and Canvas discussion postings. The authors "make no claim that clicker polling has had any direct connection with improvements in students' grades".

> "The percentage of enrolled students attending lectures (when there was no in-class quiz) was between 70–85% and normally lay closer to the high end of this range. This is in comparison with a 50% attendance estimated from other recent oﬀerings of the course."
