Evidence¶

Swanson et al. 2013¶

H. Lee Swanson, Michael J. Orosco, Cathy Lussier. (2013). Does Cognitive Strategy Training on Word Problems Compensate for Working Memory Capacity in Children with Math Difficulties? SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563039

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed regression recomputation of the randomized experiment's solution-accuracy analysis adding pretest reading, calculation, and fluid intelligence measures. The article reports these basic-skills variables "did not interact with treatment conditions" while "the significant treatment x WMC interaction was retained".

"The results indicated reading (composite of TORC and WRAT), arithmetic (WIAT) and fluid (Raven) intelligence did not interact with treatment conditions, all ps > .10. More importantly, the significant treatment x WMC interaction was retained, F (3,139)=5.39, p < .01."
