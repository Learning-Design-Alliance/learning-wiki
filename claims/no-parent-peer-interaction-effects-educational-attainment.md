## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lorijn 2021

Lorijn, S. J.; Engels, M. C.; Huisman, M.; Veenstra, R. (2021). Long-Term Effects of Acceptance and Rejection by Parents and Peers on Educational Attainment: A Study from Pre-Adolescence to Early Adulthood. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-021-01506-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 2 of the ordinal logistic regression added four interaction terms on the TRAILS sample (N = 2229). The article reports "OR = 0.82, p = 0.821" for the parental × peer acceptance interaction; model fit did not improve (AIC increased from 4742.9 to 4744.7).

> "There was no interaction between perceived parental acceptance and peer acceptance (OR = 0.82, p = 0.821), thus no enhancing positive effect when pre-adolescents were accepted by both parents and peers, contrary to H5a."

### Lorijn 2021 (2)

Lorijn, S. J.; Engels, M. C.; Huisman, M.; Veenstra, R. (2021). Long-Term Effects of Acceptance and Rejection by Parents and Peers on Educational Attainment: A Study from Pre-Adolescence to Early Adulthood. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-021-01506-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 2 interaction test in the same regression. The article reports "OR = 3.25, p = 0.396" for the parental × peer rejection interaction, and also reports no buffering interactions (parental acceptance × peer rejection OR = 1.54, p = 0.682; parental rejection × peer acceptance OR = 2.41, p = 0.521).

> "No “dual-hit effect” was found between perceived parental rejection and peer rejection (OR = 3.25, p = 0.396); thus, H5b was not supported."
