## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The study concludes that managerial and domain knowledge can develop when choosing or proposing a project, domain and nature-of-science knowledge when executing it, and new knowledge when creating a final deliverable. [→ Lassen 2021](#lassen-2021)

## Evidence

### Lassen 2021

Lassen, I. C., Arielle-Evans, A. A., Ríos, L., Lewandowski, H. J., & Dounas-Frazer, D. R. (2021). Student ownership and understanding of multi-week final projects. [doi:10.1119/perc.2021.pr.lassen](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2021.pr.lassen)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The authors' summary sentence of their emergent thematic analysis of 15 student interviews, synthesizing the phase-by-phase findings: managerial and domain knowledge "can be developed when choosing or proposing a project," and these instances are interactional elements of the ownership-as-relationship model. No effect size is reported.

> "Our results suggest that managerial and domain knowl- edge can be developed when choosing or proposing a project, domain knowledge and knowledge about the nature of sci- ence can be developed when executing the project, and new knowledge about the project can be generated when creat- ing a ﬁnal deliverable."
