## Evidence

### Zhou Yong 2021

Zhou Yong, Chen Wanlu, Zhao Xiaoxian, He Bingwei, Huang Wenxiu, Wu Dong, Chen Jiang. (2021). Application evaluation of virtual reality technology in dental implant training: a new dental implant training system: a CONSORT-compliant trial. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027355

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Objective evaluation of 30 physicians performing three simulated implant operations per tooth position across three dental arch areas. Table 3 reports group A deviations of 0.73 mm (mesiodistal), 0.78 mm (buccal and tongue), 0.61 mm (depth), and 6.66° (angle) versus group B's 0.85 mm, 1.12 mm, 0.89 mm, and 9.68° respectively, with Z or t values from −2.012 to −2.705 and P values from .012 to .044.

> "After the training of simulated mandibular implants in group A, the deviations in the 4 dimensions of mesiodistal, buccal and tongue, depth, and angle were lower than those of group B, and the differences were statistically significant (P < .05)"
