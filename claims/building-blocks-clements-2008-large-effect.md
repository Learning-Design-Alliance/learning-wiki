# In a 28-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 1.07 (improvement index +36)

## Subclaims
`q4 i3` Clements & Sarama (2008), a cluster randomized controlled trial with 202 children in 28 preschool classrooms, found a statistically significant effect of Building Blocks on the REMA (effect size 1.07, improvement index +36, p < 0.01). [→ study-2008](#study-2008)

## Evidence

### study-2008

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, December). Building Blocks. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/733

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Cluster randomized controlled trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards Without Reservations; classrooms randomly assigned to Building Blocks or business as usual in Head Start and state-funded preschool sites in New York. Effect size 1.07 (improvement index +36); intervention mean 61.78 (7.46) versus comparison 53.22 (8.38) on the REMA.
