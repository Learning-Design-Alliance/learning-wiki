## Evidence

### Pilato Jazelle 2025

Pilato Jazelle, Cortes Robert, Peterson Emily Grossnickle, Uttal David, Kolvoord Robert, Green Adam. (2025). Neurocognitive correlates of spatial thinking mediate the relation between socioeconomic status and STEM achievement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-29345-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain parametric regression analysis of fMRI data during the Mental Rotation Task in the neural subset (N=44), with parental education and family income as separate regressors. The significant cluster peaked in left premotor cortex (BA 6, k = 108 voxels).

> "One significant cluster in the left premotor cortex (peak MNI coordinate: x = -44, y = 4, z = 50, BA = 6, k = 108 voxels) showed increased BOLD activation that was correlated to increases in parental education (Fig. 6). Family income was not significantly related to activation in the brain during the MRT task via whole-brain analysis"
