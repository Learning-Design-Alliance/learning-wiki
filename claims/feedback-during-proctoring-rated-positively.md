## Evidence

### Günther V 2025

Günther V, Nees F, Maass N, von Otte S, Ruchay Z, Pape J, Ackermann J, Alkatout I. (2025). How effective and sustainable is proctoring in robotic surgery? A retrospective analysis based on interviews with surgeons. Surgical Endoscopy 39(3):1985–1995. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11503-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire-item results from the 40-surgeon interview study: feedback importance and communication were rated 1–2 on the 6-point scale by "92.5% and 7.5%; 1 and 2 on the response scale" and 87.5% vs 12.5% respectively. A moderation by proprietorship and function was significant at p = 0.028.

> "The majority of the surgeons considered feedback very important (92.5% and 7.5%; 1 and 2 on the response scale). Communication of the feedback during proctoring was rated excellent (87.5% and 12.5%; 1 and 2 on the response scale)."
