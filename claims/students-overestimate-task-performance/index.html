Evidence¶

Nugteren 2018¶

Nugteren, M. L., Jarodzka, H., Kester, L., & Van Merriënboer, J. J. G. (2018). Self-regulation of secondary school students: self-assessments are inaccurate and insufficiently used for learning-task selection. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9448-2

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

In the explorative experiment (N = 15), median performance was 2.63 steps correct while the median performance estimate was 3.63 steps, so participants on average overestimated by one step out of five; the Wilcoxon test gave "z =- 2.70, p = .01, r =- 0.49", a large printed effect size.
