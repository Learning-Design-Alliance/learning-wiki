## Evidence

### Okyay 2017

Okyay, O. & Kandir, A. (2017). Impact of the Interactive Story Reading Method on Receptive and Expressive Language Vocabulary of Children. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 395-406. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1149725

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon analysis for 22 experiment group children on pretest, posttest and follow-up (approximately five months after posttest): pretest-follow-up significant for receptive (z=-4.019, p=0.000) and expressive (z=-4.032, p=0.000); posttest-follow-up not significant (z=-2.263, p=0.429; z=-.889, p=0.374).

> "the children in the experiment group are observed to exhibit a statistically significant difference in terms of their pre -test and follow -up test scores in the Receptive Language Vocabulary Sub -Test"
