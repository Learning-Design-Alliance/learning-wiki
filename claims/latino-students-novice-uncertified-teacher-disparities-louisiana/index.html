Educator Diversity State Profile: Louisiana. (2022). EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED622849

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The brief's Louisiana disparity table for Latino versus non-Latino students shows "Percent of Students in schools with High (≥20%) percentages of Novice Teachers 30% 24%" but reverses direction for uncertified teachers (21% vs 23%). Schools serving the most Latino students had 15% novice teachers versus 20% in schools serving the fewest.
