## Evidence

### Favier V. 2020

Favier V., Kimmoun A., Gatin A., Gallet P. (2020). Percutaneous tracheostomy simulation training for ENT physicians in the treatment of COVID-19-positive patients. European Annals of Otorhinolaryngology, Head and Neck Diseases. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anorl.2020.06.002

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational error analysis from the authors' initial training sessions, reported in the Discussion. The authors state "in half of the scenarios, the balloon got pierced or the patient was unintentionally extubated", with causes including focusing on the needle instead of catheter positioning. No test statistics are reported.

> "in half of the scenarios, the balloon got pierced or the patient was unintentionally extubated, either of which can lead to ventilation circuit leakage, impairing oxygenation for the patient and incurring viral risk for the care personnel"
