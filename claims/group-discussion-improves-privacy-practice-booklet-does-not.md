# Group discussion significantly improves nursing students’ observed practice toward patient privacy while an educational booklet does not

## Evidence

### Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen 2016

Adib-Hajbaghery Mohsen, Faraji Mona. (2016). Comparison of effect between group discussion and educational booklet on Iranian nursing students’ attitude and practice toward patient privacy. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.29

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Covert observations of student–patient interactions using the 11-item SPTPP checklist before and after training. Discussion group practice rose from 14.34±1.54 to 18.15±2.52 (P=0.001); booklet group changed from 14.79±2.95 to 15.48±2.42 with within-group P=0.203, i.e., "the educational booklet did not significantly affect it".

> "our findings showed that the group discussion could significantly improve the nursing students’ practice toward patient privacy, but the educational booklet did not significantly affect it."
