`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study at Islamic Boarding School Al-Hidayah Jambi using parallel classes, with a control group taught conventionally and experimental classes using the debate model. The article reports the conclusion that the debate model "can increase the effective, critical thinking skills" but prints no test statistics or effect sizes.
