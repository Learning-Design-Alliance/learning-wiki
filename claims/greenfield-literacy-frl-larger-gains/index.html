Greenfield Learning's Comprehensive Literacy: Making the Connections 2025¶

Greenfield Learning's Comprehensive Literacy: Making the Connections. (2025). Evidence summary, Promising evidence rating. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/comprehensive-literacy-making-the-connections/

q2 · i2 · causal · r?

In the same quasi-experimental study of 482 third graders across 15 elementary schools, the subgroup analysis reported an effect size of +0.39 for students eligible for free or reduced-price lunch, who "showed larger gains" on the Spring 2025 CAASPP ELA assessment than the overall +0.23 effect.

"Students eligible for free or reduced-price lunch (+0.39) and students receiving special education services (+0.33) showed larger gains."
