## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhao L 2026

Zhao L, He J, Han Z, Zhang Y, Li L. (2026). Impact of problem-based learning on the development of clinical reasoning skills in esophageal cancer teaching. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047451

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-evaluation questionnaire (0–10 Likert) in the same cohort. PBL interns reported higher satisfaction, "Welch t = 7.068, P < .001", and greater perceived critical-thinking improvement, "Welch t = 6.462, P < .001"; all six rated dimensions favored PBL.

> "Overall instructional satisfaction was higher in the PBL cohort (8.9 ± 0.9) compared with the control group (7.6 ± 1.2), Welch t = 7.068, P < .001, and perceived improvement in independent critical thinking ability was also greater (8.8 ± 1.0 vs 7.5 ± 1.3), Welch t = 6.462, P < .001."
