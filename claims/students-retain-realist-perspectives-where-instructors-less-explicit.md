## Evidence

### Baily 2010

Baily, C., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2010). Interpretation in Quantum Physics as Hidden Curriculum. AIP Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515251](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-instruction survey responses (~100 students per course) to the statement 'An electron in an atom has a definite (but unknown) position at each moment in time.' As the article reports, "students from every course were more likely to agre e with this statement than disagree".
