Evidence¶

Gambarı and Yusuf 2015¶

Amosa Isiaka Gambarı, Mudasiru Olalere Yusuf. (2015). Effectiveness of Computer-Assisted Stad Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Problem Solving, Achievement and Retention. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085945

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA of post-test scores for 84 students in two intact classes (46 STAD, 38 ICI) with pre-test as covariate. The treatment main effect was significant: "an F (1, 81) = 5.092, p = .027". Mean gain scores were 44.71 (STAD) versus 41.57 (ICI).

"Table 1 reveals that an F (1, 81) = 5.092, p = .027 for the main effect (treatment) was significant; this indicates that the method of instruction produced a significant effect on the post-test achievement scores of students when covariate effect (pre -test) was controlled."
