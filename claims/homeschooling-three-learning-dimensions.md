## Evidence

### Jamaludin 2015

Jamaludin, K. A., Alias, N., & DeWitt, D. (2015). Research and Trends in the Studies of Homeschooling Practices: A Review on Selected Journals. TOJET, 14(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1067705

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of positive outcomes across the majority of selected studies. It reports "three dimensions on how homeschooling works in improving learning" drawing on Kraftl, Ice and Hoover-Dempsey, Mazama and Lundy, and others.

> "There were three dimensions on how homeschooling works in improving learning; a) to provide one-to-one support to learning, b) to create a safe and healthy learning environment, and c) to provide adequate and appropriate learning supports for special-needs and gifted children."
