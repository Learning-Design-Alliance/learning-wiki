## Evidence

### Aman Bansal 2021

Aman Bansal, Preey Shah, and Sahil Shah. (2021). Eye: Program Visualizer for CS2. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3408877.3439684](https://doi.org/10.1145/3408877.3439684)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous survey after both experiments asked subjects to estimate frustration reduction assuming equal debug time, on a 0-100% scale; the average response was 61.43%. This is a self-reported estimate, not a measured effect.

> "Q1(b) Question: Assuming same time spent on debugging with Eye and without Eye, how much do you think debugging with Eye can help in reducing frustration? Options: Ranging from 0% to 100%. Response: Eye reduces frustration by 61.43% on average."
