## Evidence

### Murray 2023

Murray, E., Horner, A. J., & Göbel, S. M. (2023). A Meta-analytic Review of the Effectiveness of Spacing and Retrieval Practice for Mathematics Learning. https://osf.io/

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of the isolated-material subset (8 studies, 23 effect sizes) under controlled conditions. The weighted mean was "g = 0.384 (se = 0.123)"; difference-in-fit analyses showed two influential studies but confidence intervals remained above zero when either was removed.

> "The overall weighted mean effect size of spaced versus massed practice for isolated material is g = 0.384 (se = 0.123) (95% confidence interval [0.085, 0.683])."
