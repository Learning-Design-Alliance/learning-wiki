`q2 i?` Self-rated confidence rose by 0.88 to 1.38 Likert points from pre-test to post-test after simulation sessions, in one uncontrolled pre-post study; each comparison was statistically significant. [→ DerGarabedian B 2025](#dergarabedian-b-2025)

## Evidence

### DerGarabedian B 2025

DerGarabedian B, Lacovara L, Delic J, Rajagopalan S. (2025). BRAIN-SIM: Leveraging Simulation for Neurocritical Care Education with an Innovative Multidisciplinary Approach. Journal of Intensive Care Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/08850666251327156

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of eight surveys from three acute ischemic stroke and brain code case-based sessions, analyzed with paired two-tailed t-tests. All four condition comparisons were statistically significant, with respiratory failure at P = .0016.

> "Mean learner confidence levels in managing neurocritical patients improved by 0.88 to 1.38 points on the Likert scale from pre- to post-simulation with acute ischemic stroke [3.75 ± 0.59 versus 4.63 ± 0.43 (P < .001)], intracerebral hemorrhage [3.25 ± 0.74 versus 4.63 ± 0.43 (P < .001)], intracranial hypertension [3.25 ± 0.74 versus 4.63 ± 0.43 (P < .001)], and respiratory failure [3.5 ± 0.77 versus 4.63 ± 0.43(P = .0016)]"
