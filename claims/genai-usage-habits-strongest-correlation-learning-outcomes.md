Pearson product-moment correlation analysis of all dimensional variables from the 1110-response survey. The article reports coefficients "from 0.106 to 0.418" and states the sets are interrelated but not highly coupled, with usage habits strongest; no single standardized effect size is printed for the habits correlation itself.

> "The correlation coefficients range from 0.106 to 0.418, which indicates that the two sets of sub-dimensions are interrelated, however, they have not yet reached the level of high coupling. Among usage dimensions, usage habits exhibit the strongest correlation with perceived learning outcomes."
