## Evidence

### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Horosov Roy, Gidron Yori, Ekshtein Aya, Gordon Barak, Iversen Zev, Tehori Omer, Ben-Ari Oded. (2025). Pre-training heart rate variability as a predictor of Air Force Academy completion. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327406

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Historical prospective analysis of records of 185 candidates (169 males, 16 females) for one Israeli Air Force pilot training; independent-samples t-test comparing RMSSD from 10-second ECGs taken 2–3 months before the course. The article reports "significantly higher" HRV in eventual passers, with a printed Cohen's d of 0.54 for RMSSD in Table 1.

> "The mean initial HRV in those who later passed the pilots course (M = 115.15ms, s.d = 95.8ms) was significantly higher than in those who failed (M = 82.74ms, s.d = 49.72ms), t(183)= 2.731, p < 0.001)"
