## Evidence

### Alexander S. Browman 2025

Alexander S. Browman. (2025). Centering Social Fit, Self-Concept Fit, Goal Fit, and Resource Fit as Core Elements of Students' Experiences of Belonging at School. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10009-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The author's own daily diary study (Browman et al., 2025) tracked university students for two to three weeks, measuring daily connectedness to valued identities, mental burnout, and concern about academic abilities. On lower connectedness days students reported "stronger feelings of mental burnout" and stronger ability concerns; the article cites relatable results among working professionals in STEM (Hall et al., 2015).

> "We found that on days when students experienced a lower (versus higher) sense of connectedness to their valued identities (i.e., lower self-concept fit), they reported stronger feelings of mental burnout—indicative of being-cognition disfluency—and stronger concerns about their academic abilities"
