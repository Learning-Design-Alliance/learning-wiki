## Evidence

### Subramaniam et al. 2016

Maithreyi Subramaniam, Ruzaika Omar Basaree, Jaffri Hanafi, Abu Talib Putih. (2016). Combining Anderson's Model in the Teaching of Art Appreciation for Undergraduate Students. li-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, Vol. 3(13), October - December 2016. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133234

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson bivariate correlation analysis (Table 3, Figure 3) on the 33 students' pre-test and lesson 1 scores, showing a "moderate positive correlation" of r=.594; the article interprets that poor pre-test knowledge would accompany poor lesson 1 performance.

> "the Pearson's bivariate correlation coefficient matrix of r=.594, shows a moderate positive correlation, n=33 and p=.002 indicated that there is a statistically significant between pre-test and lesson 1."
