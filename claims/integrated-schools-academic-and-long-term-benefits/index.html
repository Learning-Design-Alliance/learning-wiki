Evidence¶

George 2021¶

George, J., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2021). Advancing integration and equity through magnet schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/magnet-schools

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's narrative review of integration research states that a synthesis of 4 decades of research demonstrates academic benefits of diverse schools, including "higher achievement in math, science, language, and reading" and increased likelihood of high school and college graduation. No effect sizes are printed.

"A substantial body of research has found that racially integrated learning environments have positive impacts on academic achievement for students of all races."

George 2021 (2)¶

George, J., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2021). Advancing integration and equity through magnet schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/magnet-schools

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report attributes to economist Rucker Johnson a large-scale study of court-ordered desegregation effects on students born between 1945 and 1970, in which "graduation rates climbed by 2 percentage points" per year of integrated schooling; 5 years of exposure was associated with a 15% wage increase and an 11 percentage point decline in annual poverty rates.

"economist Rucker Johnson found that graduation rates climbed by 2 percentage points for every year a Black student attended an integrated school."
