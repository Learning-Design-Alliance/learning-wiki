## Evidence

### Pintner 1917

Pintner, R. (1917). The Mentality of the Dependent Child, Together with a Plan for a Mental Survey of an Institution. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075825

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational mental examinations of 82 state charity cases (56 by Binet, 26 by Yerkes-Bridges Point Scale), diagnosed by both C. M. A. and Three Per Cent. Hypothesis methods. The article reports the combined distribution shows "at best only 37% of the children who test up to normal."

> "Even the more conservative estimate of 19.5% feeble-minded is high and indicates that this group of State Charity cases is made up of very poor mental material. There are at best only 37% of the children who test up to normal."
