### Wiley 2019

Wiley, J. (2019). Picture this! Effects of photographs, diagrams, animations, and sketching on learning and beliefs about learning from a geoscience text. Applied Cognitive Psychology, 33, 9-19. https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3495

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Confidence bias was the signed difference between prediction and outcome composites in the 120-participant experiment. The ANOVA showed "a significant effect of condition on confidence bias" with η² = .15, F(5, 114) = 3.91, p < . 01.

> "There was a significant effect of condition on confidence bias, F(5, 114) = 3.91, p < . 01, partial eta squared = .15. The most overconfidence was demonstrated by readers in the photograph condition, and the least in the sketching condition."
