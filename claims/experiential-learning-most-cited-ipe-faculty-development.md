This scoping review found that "15 addressed learning theories to guide faculty development initiatives in IPE," with experiential learning cited in the largest number of studies, followed by adult learning, meaningful learning, constructivist approach, competency-based learning, and social learning.

> "Of the 26 publications included, 15 addressed learning theories to guide faculty development initiatives in IPE: experiential learning(22,23,30,34–36,38-40,45), adult learning(35,41,44), meaningful learning(28,40), constructivist approach(35), competency-based learning (competency-based teaching)(13), and social learning(32)."
