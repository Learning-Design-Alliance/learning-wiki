Related Claims¶

  • Pooled analysis finds no significant association between active commuting to school and mathematics performance — related
  • Active commuting to and from school is not significantly associated with cognitive performance in children and adolescents (pooled ES = −0.02) — related
  • Active commuting to and from school is not significantly associated with mathematics-related skills (pooled ES = −0.33, considerable heterogeneity) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Subgroup analyses by mode of ACS, age, and sex show no significant differences in pooled effect sizes for academic outcomes — related
  • Active commuting to school shows a positive association with at least one academic achievement measure in a majority of reviewed adolescent studies — reports the opposite
