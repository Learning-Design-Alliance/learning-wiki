## Evidence

### Galada 2025

Galada, Aditi and Baytar, Fatma. (2025). Design and evaluation of a problem-based learning VR module for apparel fit correction training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311587

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Linear regression analysis of the 40-participant pretest-posttest data, reported in the Results section. The ASVT pre-test score "was a significant predictor of the Δ FCST score" with a positive coefficient; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "As seen in Fig 4, the ASVT pre-test score, that is, the spatial visualization skill before undergoing training was a significant predictor of the Δ FCST score, that is, the increase in fit correction skill after undergoing the training (p < 0.05)."

### Galada 2025 (2)

Galada, Aditi and Baytar, Fatma. (2025). Design and evaluation of a problem-based learning VR module for apparel fit correction training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311587

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same linear regression model predicting ΔFCST, shown in Fig 4. The printed unstandardized slope was that "a unit increase in ASVT pre-test score resulted in a 0.23 times higher increase in FCST score"; this coefficient is not a standardized effect size.

> "A unit increase in ASVT pre-test score resulted in a 0.23 times higher increase in FCST score."
