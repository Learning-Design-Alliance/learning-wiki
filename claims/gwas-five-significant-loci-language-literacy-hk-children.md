Genome-wide association analysis of 34 literacy/language phenotypes in a Hong Kong sample of bilingual children (total N = 1046 including twins and singletons) identified 5 independent loci; the description quotes the article's own significance criteria: "reached genome-wide (GW) significance (p < 5e-08)". No effect size was printed.
