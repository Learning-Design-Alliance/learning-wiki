## Evidence

### Lin 2016

Lin, S.-Y. and Singh, C. (2016). Using Analogies to Learn Introductory Physics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06388](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06388)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intervention 1 comparison of reproduction scores on the solved problem versus transfer scores on the quiz problem (Table 5). The article reports that reproduced scores "could be superficial" regarding transfer ability, with the 4.6 bottom-calculus score and a 1.7-point average algebra-based drop.

> "Indeed, the score on the quiz problem dropped to 4.6 for the “bottom” calculus-based students. An average drop of 1.7 points was also found for the algebra-based students."
