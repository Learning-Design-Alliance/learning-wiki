## Evidence

### EL-Andari R 2023

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial reported in the review: 40 surgical trainees with fewer than 10 hours of da Vinci experience were randomized to wet laboratory, dry laboratory, virtual reality simulation or control after an instructional video; skills were evaluated with the Global Evaluative Assessment of Robotic Skills using time-based scores (time subtracted from 720).

> "Participants in the wet laboratory, dry laboratory and control groups showed significant improvements in their scores, whereas participants in the virtual reality group did not."
