## Evidence

### Annabella Sakunkoo and Jonathan Sakunkoo 2026

Annabella Sakunkoo and Jonathan Sakunkoo. 2026. From Clicking to Moving: Embodied Micro-Movements as a New Modality for Data Literacy Learning. CHI'26 Workshop on Data Literacy, Barcelona, Spain. [doi:10.1145/3773077.3812187](https://doi.org/10.1145/3773077.3812187)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary within-subjects study with 16 participants (9 female, 7 male) who each completed three 15-minute sessions with Kinetiq, IXL, and Khan Academy in counterbalanced order. The Feeling Scale showed "mean valence +2.4 points, p < .05" favoring Kinetiq; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Affective valence scores were significantly higher during Kinetiq sessions compared to both IXL and Khan Academy (mean valence +2.4 points, p < .05), with no significant difference between the two conventional platforms."
