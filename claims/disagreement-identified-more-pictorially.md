## Evidence

### Gayle Geschwind 2023

Gayle Geschwind, Michael Vignal, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Representational differences in how students compare measurements. [doi:10.1119/perc.2023.pr.geschwind](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2023.pr.geschwind)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Answer-option analysis (Table III, N = 670). Option E was selected by 10 ± 2% on the NRI versus 1.3 ± 0.9% on the PRI; students "identify this disagreement more frequently when presented with the data pictorially, where it is clear that the error bars are very far from one another."

> "many more students selected answer option E for the NRI than the PRI; this answer option is the only one where the two measurements definitely do not agree."
