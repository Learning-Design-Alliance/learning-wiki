### Varela-Centelles Pablo 2015

Varela-Centelles Pablo, Insua Angel, Seoane-Romero Juan M., Warnakulasuriya Saman, Rapidis Alexander, Diz Pedro, Seoane Juan. (2015). Available web-based teaching resources for health care professionals on screening for oral cancer. Medicina Oral, Patología Oral y Cirugía Bucal. https://doi.org/10.4317/medoral.20163

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content analysis of the 22 included websites using the oral cancer content review instrument V, reporting the proportion of sites with updated information per topic. The study found updating "very variable among web sites" across topics.

> "Updating of contents resulted to be very variable among web sites, ranging between 81% (n=18) for information related to visual examination, and 18.2% (n=4) for molecular biomarkers."
