Assignment-wise cross-validation experiment (Table 3) on CodeWorkout: hyperparameters tuned via grid search on fold 0 and fixed across folds. The authors conclude prior gaps "may stem, at least in part, from insufficient optimization of baseline models".

Related Claims¶

  • The survey states that deep learning knowledge tracing models exhibit superior performance to Bayesian and logistic models but have significant room for improvement in interpretability. — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • Hyperparameter sensitivity in PKT is an inherent characteristic of programming interaction data, varying across models and across assignments within the same model — related
  • Under causally valid, tuned settings, code-level embeddings do not consistently improve performance: rectified Code-DKT underperforms tuned DKT on several assignments, while ECKT remains competitive — related
  • Task-specific fine-tuning substantially improves open-source LLMs on MedGame Bench and narrows the gap with commercial models — related
  • Diagnostic model performance remains relatively stable across moderate hyperparameter ranges, with 8 attention heads and a 512 hidden dimension yielding optimal results — related
  • Code-DKT's attention softmax applied across the temporal dimension (dim=1) is causally invalid and inflates reported performance; rectifying it to the path dimension (dim=2) removes the artificial gain — related
  • Failing to sort student attempt sequences by ServerTimestamp violates temporal causality and can systematically overestimate PKT model performance — related
