## Evidence

### Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace 2005

Ian D. Beatty and William J. Gerace. (2005). Probing physics students' conceptual knowledge structures through term association. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0508133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary observational analysis of PPTE response lists for problem D6 after a week of work-energy instruction; the authors interpret this as subjects retaining "a strong inclination to react to the problem as an exercise in kinematics," perhaps due to longer experience and greater confidence with that topic.

> "twelve of the sixteen subjects included "kinematics" or a clear equivalent among their responses to D6, including seven of the nine who included "work" or "energy," and five of the seven who didn't."
