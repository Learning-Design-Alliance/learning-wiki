`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group finding on the advanced directive theme. The same interviews also revealed ambivalence: interviewees felt deceived, questioned whether the AD was signed, and doubted whether patients understood the scope of their decision; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Thus, several interviewees perceived the appearance of living will as a clarifying event that relieved them of the decision-making."
