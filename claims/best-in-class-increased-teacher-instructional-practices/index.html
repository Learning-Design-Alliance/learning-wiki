Evidence¶

Maureen A. Conroy 2019¶

Maureen A. Conroy, Kevin S. Sutherland, James Algina, Crystal Ladwig, Brittany Werch, Jose Martinez, Gabriel Jessee, Maria Gyure. (2019). Outcomes of the BEST in CLASS Intervention on Teachers' Use of Effective Practices, Self-Efficacy, and Classroom Quality. School Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.17105/SPR-2018-0003.V48-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multisite cluster randomized trial of 186 early childhood teachers (92 BEST in CLASS, 94 comparison); TCIDOS partial-interval observations of teacher–child dyads at pretest and posttest analyzed with two-level models in Mplus. The article reports the treatment effect was significant for all TCIDOS variables, with adjusted means e.g. "AMB = .07 and AMC = .01 for behavior-specific praise".

"However, at posttest, teachers in the BEST in CLASS condition increased their use of rules, precorrection, behavior-specific praise, corrective feedback, and instructive feedback from pretest levels compared to the control condition."
