Saad and Ahmed 2015¶

Saad, I., & Ahmed, M. (2015). The Significance of Journal Writing in Improving Listening and Reading Comprehension in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). Universal Journal of Educational Research 3(11): 815-821. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2015.031106

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison of final outcomes for the three classes as printed in the results table and text. The control class graduated with "50% in listening (with 0% level 2+ and level 3), 66% in reading," while the experimental classes reached 100% and 71% listening and 100% and 86% reading. Percentages are descriptive; no significance test is printed.

"The third class, the control class, graduated with 50% in listening (with 0% level 2+ and level 3), 66% in reading (with 50% level 2+ and level 3), and 100% in speaking at level 1+ (with 0% level 2 and level 3)."
