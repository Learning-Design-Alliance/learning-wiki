## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Classrooms combining high levels of two or three need-supportive practices do not show the greatest engagement benefits. [→ Olivier 2021](#olivier-2021)

## Evidence

### Olivier 2021

Olivier, E., Galand, B., Morin, A., & Hospel, V. (2021). Need-supportive teaching and student engagement in the classroom: Comparing the additive, synergistic, and global contributions. Learning and Instruction, 71, 101389. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101389

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same multilevel analysis of 1,193 students in 57 math classrooms, the study reports that results "failed to support the synergistic hypothesis (H2)". No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Results also failed to support the synergistic hypothesis (H2), which predicted that the greatest benefits would emerge in classrooms characterized by a high level of two or three of these practices."
