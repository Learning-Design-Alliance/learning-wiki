# The training group outperforms controls on social problem resolution, with a significant group effect and a marginal interaction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across testing times the experimental group scored higher on the RES (5.34 ± 0.76) than the control group (5.04 ± 0.87; group effect η² = 0.044), and the interaction was marginal (p = 0.069). [→ Honoré N 2020](#honore-n-2020)

## Evidence

### Honoré N 2020

Honoré N, Houssa M, Volckaert A, Noël M-P and Nader-Grosbois N (2020) Training Inhibition and Social Cognition in the Classrooms. Front. Psychol. 11:1974. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01974

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on 121 experimental and 119 control children on the Social Problem Resolution Task; the group effect was significant (F = 10.92, p = 0.001, η² = 0.044) and the group×time interaction marginal (F = 3.34, p = 0.069, η² = 0.014).

> "Significant effects of group were observed in the RES [with higher performance in the experimental group (5.34 ± 0.76) than in the control group (5.04 ± 0.87)]"
