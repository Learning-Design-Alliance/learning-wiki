Evidence¶

Nguyen Van Han & Henriette van Rensburg 2014¶

Nguyen Van Han & Henriette van Rensburg. (2014). The Effect of Computer Assisted Language Learning (CALL) on Performance in the Test of English for International Communication (TOEIC) Listening Module. English Language Teaching, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n2p30

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

The two-way mixed between-within ANOVA in section 4.3 examined the effect of CALL instruction on English language proficiency and found a significant pre-to-post effect with printed effect size partial eta-squared = .231: "(F = 14.452, p = 0, partial eta-squared = .231)". The article notes both groups improved.

"It revealed statistically significant effects for test type (pre- vs. post-) (F = 14.452, p = 0, partial eta-squared = .231)"
