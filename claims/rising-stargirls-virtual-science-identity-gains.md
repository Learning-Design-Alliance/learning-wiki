### Maya Silverman 2024

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Matched retrospective pre/post survey analysis of the three virtual workshops, first science-identity item. The article reports "a large effect size dAw = 0.8201" with ⟨g⟩ = 0.4500 and cave = 0.5085.

> "Collectively analyzing all virtual workshop responses (NB, NA) = (66, 66) to the question ‘I believe I can do well in science’ showed a mod- erate increase ⟨g⟩ = 0.4500 and cave = 0.5085 (with 11 re- sponses dropped from the calculation), as well as a large effect size dAw = 0.8201."
