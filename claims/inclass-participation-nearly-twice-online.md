## Evidence

### Manher Jariwala 2016

Manher Jariwala, Jada-Simone S. White, Ben Van Dusen, and Eleanor W. Close. (2016). In-class vs. online administration of concept inventories and attitudinal assessments. 2016 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.039

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experimental design assigning students in three introductory physics courses (693 at start) to paper-in-class versus online testing conditions for their concept inventory and CLASS, with participation rates compared across conditions; the paper reports the online-versus-in-class contrast as "nearly twice as high" with p < 0.001.

> "participation rates in class were nearly twice as high as participation rates online (p < 0.001). In both formats, there was a drop in participation in the post -tests as compared to the pre- tests."
