### Sunsundegui P 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 198 items categorized as easy (DI > 0.7, n = 95) versus non-easy (n = 103); 92.6% of easy items contained at least one low-functioning distractor versus 52.4% of non-easy items (p < 0.001).

> "Factual questions were significantly more likely to be classified as easy compared to clinical case-based questions (OR: 2.62; 95% CI: 1.38–4.99; p = 0.003). Conversely, the absence of low-functioning distractors was inversely associated with item ease (OR: 0.1; 95% CI: 0.04–0.24; p < 0.001)."
