Peng Wang 2024¶

Peng Wang, Lesya Ganushchak, Camille Welie, Roel van Steensel. (2024). The Dynamic Nature of Emotions in Language Learning Context: Theory, Method, and Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09946-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review reporting an empirical finding attributed to Resnik and Dewaele (2023); the review also notes TEI and learner autonomy showing a strong positive correlation. No effect sizes are printed in the quoted passage.

"Learners with higher trait emotional intelligence (TEI) tend to experience more positive emotions and fewer negative emotions during language development across different contexts. Similarly, more autonomous learners generally enjoy language classes more, especially in challenging circumstances like emergency remote teaching."
