## Evidence

### Laurence Caroline O 2013

Laurence Caroline O, Zajac Ian T, Lorimer Michelle, Turnbull Deborah A, Sumner Karen E. (2013). The impact of preparatory activities on medical school selection outcomes: a cross-sectional survey of applicants to the university of Adelaide medical school in 2007. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-159

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 1097 applicants to the University of Adelaide medical school in 2007; logistic regression related the number of UMAT preparatory activities to offer of an interview. The article reports "the odds of being offered an interview increased by 1.22" per additional activity (P < 0.001). Mean activities were 2.8 for those not offered an interview and 3.2 for those offered one.

> "Regarding the UMAT, for every one additional activity that applicants undertook, the odds of being offered an interview increased by 1.22 (95% confidence interval [CI] 1.11 - 1.33; P < 0.001)."
