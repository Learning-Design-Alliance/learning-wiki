Content analysis and thematic reading of 52 assessment plans from state and private higher-education institutions, coded with criteria informed by the assessment literature. The article reports that formative activities were often reduced to preparatory steps toward summative scoring and oral exams standardized for reliability.

"However, in most reviewed plans, even these practices were ultimately structured and interpreted through an accumulatively summative lens. Formative activities were often reduced to preparatory steps toward summative scoring"
