## Evidence

### Chen Hao 2024

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

A study (Hojat et al., USA) of 371 medical students used JSPE and clinical teachers' global ratings of clinical competence in core clerkships; the review reports the quoted positive relationship.

> "Their findings indicated a positive relationship between empathy and clinical competence, particularly in the areas of history taking and physical examinations."

### Chen Hao 2024 (2)

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Ogle et al.'s cross-sectional study of 57 Australian medical students assessed competence via OSCE and empathy via JSPE self-report and observer ratings; the review reports the quoted contrast between observed and self-rated empathy.

> "the clinical competence of medical students was positively correlated with the observed empathic behaviors, but not with the self-evaluation empathy score"
