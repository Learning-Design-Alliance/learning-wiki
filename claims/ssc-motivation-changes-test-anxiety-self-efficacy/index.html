Evidence¶

Bowering 2017¶

Bowering, E. R., Mills, J., & Merritt, A. (2017). Learning How to Learn: A Student Success Course for At Risk Students. The Canadian Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 8(3). http://ir.lib.uwo.ca/cjsotl_rcacea/vol8/iss3/12

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-post MSLQ motivation-scale analysis in the Results section; the article reports practice effects were unable to explain improved scores in MSLQ value, expectancy, or affect (ps > 0.07), and that participants "were anxious at both of the pre- and post -tests".

"While our participants were anxious at both of the pre- and post -tests, their test anxiety significantly decreased over time (M = 4.9 to 4.2, p < 0.001)"

Bowering 2017 (2)¶

Bowering, E. R., Mills, J., & Merritt, A. (2017). Learning How to Learn: A Student Success Course for At Risk Students. The Canadian Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 8(3). http://ir.lib.uwo.ca/cjsotl_rcacea/vol8/iss3/12

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same pre-post MSLQ motivation analysis: three of six subscales changed significantly in the desired direction. The article notes the task value and control beliefs subscales "did not significantly improve" and hypothesizes students may need academic success before those components improve.

"As well, b oth intrinsic goal orientation (M = 5.2 to 5.4, p < 0.05) and self -efficacy (M = 5.8 to 6.0, p < 0.05) significantly increased"
