Evidence¶

Hughes 2018¶

Hughes, J. N., West, S. G., Kim, H., & Bauer, S. S. (2018). Effect of Early Grade Retention on School Completion: A Prospective Study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7), 974-991. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000243

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' synthesis in the Discussion of prior publications from this same longitudinal sample (grade-level propensity comparisons through grade 9). The quoted sentence states the disconnect; the underlying findings are from the cited prior studies, not newly tested here.

"there is no evidence that grade retention harms students' educational achievement or motivation at the point of entry into high school; yet it increases the probability of dropping out of school prior to earning a high school diploma."
