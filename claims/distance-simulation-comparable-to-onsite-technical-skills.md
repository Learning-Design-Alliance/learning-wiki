`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review following PRISMA of 104 studies; 38 comparative studies (29 RCTs, 9 non-RCTs) compared distance simulation with on-site training. The review reports "distance simulation had comparable effectiveness in 29 studies (91%)" for skill outcomes.

> "Skills assessment was the primary outcome in the remaining 32 studies (Kirkpatrick level 2). Among those studies, distance simulation had comparable effectiveness in 29 studies (91%)."
