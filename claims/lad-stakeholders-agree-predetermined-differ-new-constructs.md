## Evidence

### de Vreugd 2024

de Vreugd, L., van Leeuwen, A., Jansen, R., & van der Schaaf, M. (2024). Learning Analytics Dashboard Design and Evaluation to Support Student Self-Regulation of Study Behaviour. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3), 249–262. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8529

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Focus groups with students (n=4) and tutors (n=3) using a Nominal Group Technique rated predetermined constructs on 7-point Likert scales. The article reports students and tutors "rated the importance of constructs similarly" with fear of failure showing the biggest difference (M=1.54).

> "Overall, students and tutors rated the importance of constructs similarly, with “fear of failure” showing the biggest difference ( M=1.54)."
