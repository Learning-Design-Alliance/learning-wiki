### Fraj-Andrés E. 2018

Fraj-Andrés E., Lucia-Palacios L. & Pérez-López R. (2018). How extroversion affects student attitude toward the combined use of a wiki and video recording of group presentations. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.12.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

PLS analysis of survey data from 106 first-year undergraduates who recorded group presentations uploaded to a class wiki. The article reports "a positive and significant effect of extroversion on enjoyment" (H1 supported).

> "According to the results, H1 and H2 are supported because there is a positive and significant effect of extroversion on enjoyment and a negative and significant effect on stress."
