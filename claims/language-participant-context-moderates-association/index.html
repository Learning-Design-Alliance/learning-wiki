Evidence¶

Maria Adelina Ianos 2026¶

Maria Adelina Ianos, Andrei Rusu, Ramona Paloş, Cristina Petreñas, Clara Sansó. (2026). Linking Language Attitudes to Proficiency Across Learning Contexts: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Correlational Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10193-w

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level moderator crossing participant background with language status across three contexts. RVE was only partially convergent: the local–foreign contrast was marginal (β = 0.11, p = .056) and the immigrant–local contrast non-significant (β = −0.05, p = .476).

"The local participants – foreign language context ( k = 49, r =.39, 95% CI [.34, .43]) consistently yielded the strongest association, followed by the local participants – local language context ( k = 68, r =.29, 95% CI [.19, .39]), with the immigrant participants – local language context producing the smallest effect ( k = 12, r =.23, 95% CI [.13, .33])."
