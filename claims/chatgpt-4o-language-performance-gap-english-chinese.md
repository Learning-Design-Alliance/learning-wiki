`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-dataset comparison within the same prospective study revealed a reversed pattern between the AI and human participants: the model favored English materials while all human cohorts favored Chinese materials, suggesting a language-related performance gap rather than a content difficulty effect alone.

> "Interestingly, while ChatGPT-4o performed better on English questions than on Chinese ones, the human participants (across all three experience levels) performed better on the Chinese question set than on the English one."
