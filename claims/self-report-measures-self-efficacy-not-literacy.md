## Evidence

### Thomas K. F. Chiu 2025

Thomas K. F. Chiu, Murat Çoban, Ismaila Temitayo Sanusi, Musa Adekunle Ayanwale. (2025). Validating student AI competency self-efficacy (SAICS) scale and its framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10512-y

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

A conceptual argument in the literature-review section, not a tested result. The authors reason that self-reported items capture feelings and thinking, and conclude that "both AI self-efficacy and AI literacy should be included in a more comprehensive assessment of student learning."

> "However, a self-reported approach can only examine participants' perceptions of using and evaluating AI (i.e., AI self-efficacy) but not their abilities and skills (i.e., AI literacy). AI literacy should be measured by tests or examinations similar to international literacy assessment programs"
