Li, Y., & Leibnitz, N. (2026). Gender expression appraisals in introductory physics courses: A cross-institutional replication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.26143)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table VI) between binary discrepancy indicators and demographic, course-type, and social-psychological variables in the 1845-student sample, with correlations of r = 0.17 to 0.23. Gender was also correlated with all three indicators (r = 0.21, 0.18, 0.18, all p < 0.001).

> "gender stigma consciousness showed the strongest and most consistent positive associations with the discrepancy indicators, with correlations ranging from 0.17 to 0.23 (all p < 0.001)."
