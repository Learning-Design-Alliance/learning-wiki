Traxler, A. L., Cid, X. C., Blue, J., Barthelemy, R. (2015). Enriching gender in PER: A binary past and a complex future. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1507.05107](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1507.05107)

The review attributes to Ong's qualitative study of women of color in physics the finding that fragmentation strategies serve performance-related goals, with examples such as changing from a skirt to pants for an all-male lab. These are described as survival mechanisms responding to microaggressions.

> "She argued that women of color often employ fragmentation strategies, which include gendered passing and racial passing (i.e., actively or passively seeking to be perceived and accepted as a member of a more dominant group)"
