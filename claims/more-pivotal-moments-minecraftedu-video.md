## Evidence

### Gandolfi 2026

Gandolfi, E., Soyturk, I., Kosko, K. W., Ferdig, R. E., & Clements, R. (2026). Measuring presence in virtual world teacher education: validation of the eXtended Reality Presence Scale. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10619-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Coding of open-ended answers (n = 78, Cohen's kappa = 0.91) against four pre-defined pivotal moments; peer support (68 vs 7) and teamwork (73 vs 6) were noticed more in the Minecraft.edu video, while disengagement and teacher intervention were reported after both conditions.

> "Regarding pivotal moments, participants noticed more of them after watching the Minecraft.edu video (m = 3.6, SD = 0.7) than after the real classroom one (m = 2.0, SD = 0.6)."
