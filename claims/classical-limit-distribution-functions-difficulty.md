## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students had great difficulty with the condition under which the Fermi-Dirac and Bose-Einstein distributions approach the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution, with some claiming it occurs when T → 0 K. [→ Paul Justice 2019](#paul-justice-2019)

## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student understanding of Fermi energy, the Fermi-Dirac distribution and total electronic energy of a free electron gas. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Written question 4 responses and interviews after traditional instruction. The correct limit is high temperature where the de Broglie wavelength is small and wavefunction overlap negligible; students reasoning only from mathematical expressions often did not "think physically about whether their mathematical reasoning made sense conceptually", a math-versus-physics-mode dichotomy the authors note has been observed in other contexts.

> "Some students claimed that the quantum distribution functions will approach the MB distribution function when 𝑇 → 0 𝐾 (which is the exact opposite case in which the quantum effects are important)."
