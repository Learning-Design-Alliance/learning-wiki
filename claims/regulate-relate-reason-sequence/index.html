q1 · i? · design · r1

A theoretical argument in the introduction, attributed to Bruce Perry's Neurosequential Model of Education: challenging or defiant behavior may reflect a developing brain unready for cognitive tasks, and adults must first support regulation before reasoning. No empirical data are reported in the document itself.

"Once students are regulated and feel support by those around them, they are ready to reason. These three Rs (regulate, relate, reason) underlie the Neurosequential Model of Education (NME) developed by Bruce Perry"
