## Evidence

### David Gijbels 2009

David Gijbels, Sofie M. M. Loyens. (2009). Constructivist learning (environments) and how to avoid another tower of Babel: reply to Renkl. Instructional Science, 37, 499–502. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-009-9100-2

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is a conceptual argument in a reply to a commentary, not an empirical study; the article offers no data for it. The authors define constructivism as 'a theory about how we learn, grounded in philosophy with implications for instruction' and argue from that definition.

> "we do not regard it as a paradox that constructivists talk about con- structivist learning environments, since they are focusing on the implications of a learning theory"
