Evidence¶

Scheiter 2007¶

Scheiter, K., & Gerjets, P. (2007). Learner Control in Hypermedia Environments. Educational Psychology Review 19, 285–307. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-007-9046-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review analyzing the literature on learner-controlled hypermedia. The authors state their purpose is to explain "the ambiguous results of studies" on hypermedia learning effectiveness, treating this ambiguity as the established starting point of their analysis.

"we analyze the instructional potentials of learner-controlled hypermedia environments as well as possible reasons for the ambiguous results of studies that have aimed at determining the effectiveness of hypermedia learning"
