## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Item non-response and careless answering correlate negatively with self-reported and teacher-reported grit and self-control, including a Spearman correlation of -0.216 with teacher-reported grit. [→ Zamarro Gema 2020](#zamarro-gema-2020)

## Evidence

### Zamarro Gema 2020

Zamarro Gema, Nichols Malachi, Duckworth Angela L., D'Mello Sidney K. (2020). Validation of survey effort measures of grit and self-control in a sample of high school students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235396

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis (Table 3) of survey effort measures against self-reports and teacher reports in 513 high school seniors; the table row for teacher-reported grit prints "-0.216*" for item non-response. The article notes the magnitudes "appear small".

> "As expected, item non-response rates and careless answering were negatively correlated with self-reported grit and self-control as well as teacher-reported grit, self-control, and homework completion."

### Zamarro Gema 2020 (2)

Zamarro Gema, Nichols Malachi, Duckworth Angela L., D'Mello Sidney K. (2020). Validation of survey effort measures of grit and self-control in a sample of high school students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235396

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Partial rank correlations controlling for KBIT scaled score, age, ethnicity, gender, FRL, SPED, ELL, and household income showed a "similar pattern" with smaller partial correlations against teacher reports and tasks.

> "We observed a similar pattern compared to the zero-order correlations, but partial correlations with teacher reports and performance task measures were smaller."
