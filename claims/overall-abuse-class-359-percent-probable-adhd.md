## Evidence

### Sanderud K. 2016

Sanderud K., Murphy S., Elklit A. (2016). Child maltreatment and ADHD symptoms in a sample of young adults. European Journal of Psychotraumatology. https://doi.org/10.3402/ejpt.v7.32061

`q3 · i?`

Chi-square analysis and cross-tabulations (Table 2) in the national survey of 2,980 young adults; 431 (14.5%) scored above cutoff. Emotional abuse: 25.5%, sexual abuse: 15.3%, non-abused: 10.1%. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "A Chi-square analysis indicated significant association between childhood trauma groups and ADHD symptoms [χ2(3) =88.44, p<0.001]. Cross tabulations revealed that the overall abuse latent class was especially associated with ADHD symptoms, with a total of 35.9% scoring higher than the cut off score on the ASRS (see Table 2)."
