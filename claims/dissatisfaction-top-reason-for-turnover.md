Analysis of TFS 2012–13 leaver and mover reason reports (Figures 4–5, Table 1); testing/accountability was the most frequent dissatisfaction (25% of voluntary leavers), followed by administration (21%) and the teaching career (21%).

> "These kinds of dissatisfactions were noted by 55% of those who left the profession and 66% of those who left their school to go to another school."
