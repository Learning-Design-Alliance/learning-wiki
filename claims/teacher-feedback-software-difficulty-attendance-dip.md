## Evidence

### Katy Chubb 2017

Katy Chubb, Rosie Hood, Thomas Wilson, Jonathan Holdship, and Sarah Hutton. (2017). Discovering New Variable Stars at Key Stage 3. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa649f](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa649f)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative teacher feedback forms filled out after every session at each school. Teachers noted attendance dropped mid-course at one school, with software issues, forgetting sessions and possible frustration as potential reasons; attendance "increased again after encouragement and weekly reminders". No quantitative measures reported.

> "It was found, however, that attendance dropped oﬀ in the middle of the sessions, at one school in particular. Software issues, forgetting the sessions were on and possible frustration were noted as potential reasons for this dip."
