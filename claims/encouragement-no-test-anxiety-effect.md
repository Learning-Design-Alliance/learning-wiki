Keller Tamás, Szakál Péter. (2021). Not just words! Effects of a light-touch randomized encouragement intervention on students’ exam grades, self-efficacy, motivation, and test anxiety. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256960

Bivariate raw comparison (Fig 5) of primary and secondary outcomes in treated versus control groups in the University of Szeged field experiment. Differences in “exam grades and test anxiety” were “not statistically significant”; the multivariate test-anxiety table falls beyond the supplied text, so no effect size is reported.

> "The differences between the means of treated and control students are statistically significant in the case of self-efficacy (p < 0.005) and motivation (p = 0.032) and are not statistically significant in the case of exam grades and test anxiety."
