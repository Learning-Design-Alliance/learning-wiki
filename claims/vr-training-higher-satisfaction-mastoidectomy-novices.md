## Evidence

### Tapiala Jesse 2023

Tapiala Jesse, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti, Timonen Tomi, Vrzáková Hana, Dietz Aarno. (2023). Impact of virtual reality training on mastoidectomy performance: a prospective randomised study. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-023-08143-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Structured evaluation forms completed after training (pre) and repeated after the procedure (post), compared with the Mann–Whitney U test; post-procedure overall grade means were 4.4 (SD 0.6) for VRT versus 3.4 (SD 0.6) for TT.

> "However, the overall grade given for the VRT method remained the same between the evaluations and was significantly better than the grade for the TT method (p < 0.001) after the procedure."

### Tapiala Jesse 2023 (2)

Tapiala Jesse, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti, Timonen Tomi, Vrzáková Hana, Dietz Aarno. (2023). Impact of virtual reality training on mastoidectomy performance: a prospective randomised study. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-023-08143-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 2 mean 3D-perception ratings from the same evaluation forms: VRT scored higher pre (p = 0.002) and post (p = 0.013). In open feedback participants "described the advantages of the VRT method in terms of 3D perception and topographical anatomy".

> "3D perception (1–5 points) Pre 4.3 (SD 0.8) 3.3 (SD 0.9) 0.002 Post 4.0 (SD 1.0) 2.9 (SD 1.2) 0.013"
