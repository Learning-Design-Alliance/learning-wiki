## Evidence

### Kievit R. A. 2019

Kievit R. A., Hofman A. D., & Nation K. (2019). Mutualistic Coupling Between Vocabulary and Reasoning in Young Children: A Replication and Extension of the Study by Kievit et al. (2017). Psychological Science, 30(8), 1245–1252. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797619841265

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interpretation of the fitted mutualism latent change model in the Oxford sample: the positive coupling from vocabulary to reasoning development had β of 0.329 and 0.449 for Waves 2 and 3, versus 0.203 in the adolescent sample.

> "This demonstrates that children with higher vocabulary scores made larger gains in reasoning ability, and children with lower vocabulary scores made smaller gains in reasoning ability"
