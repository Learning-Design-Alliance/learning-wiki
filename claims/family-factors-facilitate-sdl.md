## Evidence

### Du Xiangyu 2025

Du Xiangyu, Du Jiayi, Shang Lin, Yin Zhao, Jiang Li. (2025). Exploring influencing factors and facilitating strategies for self-directed learning from the perspective of medical trainees: a multicentre qualitative study in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-088688

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group theme on family factors. One participant described supportive relationships balancing parenthood and study: "You study well there and I'll take care of the kids for you, including my mother-in-law and husband, who are all very supportive."

> "Participants emphasised that positive support and parental affirmation can facilitate SDL by inspiring a person's desire to learn and succeed. Conversely, criticism can lead to a decrease in motivation and even cessation of the learning process."
