## Evidence

### Yu Jian 2025

Yu Jian, Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang. (2025). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of dental residents in managing traumatic dental injuries. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07382-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Clinical practice section (four items) of the survey of 256 residents. The disparity in autonomy was highly significant (P = 0.000); the authors attribute it to attending-driven, surgically focused Oral Surgery departments limiting resident autonomy.

> "63.8% of General Dentistry residents said they had ''occasionally'' treated trauma cases independently (without direct supervisor intervention), and an additional 12.1% had done so ''often.'' In contrast, Oral Surgery residents had the lowest level of independent practice"

### Yu Jian 2025 (2)

Yu Jian, Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang. (2025). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of dental residents in managing traumatic dental injuries. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07382-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same practice section: overall oral health education rates were low, especially in oral surgery, which the authors read as cognitive biases toward curative rather than preventive dentistry roles.

> "General Dentistry and Oral Medicine residents were the most proactive in the patient education on TDI prevention and aftercare, with 41.4% of General Dentistry residents often providing education, compared to 12.9% in Oral Surgery (P = 0.002)."
