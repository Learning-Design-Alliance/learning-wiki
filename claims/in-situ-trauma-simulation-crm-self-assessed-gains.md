## Evidence

### Hammer Silke 2026

Hammer Silke, Lock Johan F., König Sarah, Happel Oliver, Paul Mila M. (2026). Learning gain of an ATLS®-based interprofessional and multidisciplinary in-situ simulation training of trauma resuscitation. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-026-03146-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective questionnaire analysis of routine educational data from 238 participants after 36 training sessions (03/2022–11/2023) at a single Level I trauma center; 230 questionnaires fully completed. Mean gain was "4.95 ± 4.80 (12.4% of the possible 40-point gain)"; paired t-tests confirmed significant pre–post improvements (all p < 0.001, Fig. 2).

> "In the whole cohort, learning gain scores ranged between minimum − 3 and maximum 24 with a low mean value of ± SD of 4.95 ± 4.80 (12.4% of the possible 40-point gain)."
