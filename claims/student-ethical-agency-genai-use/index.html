Ji Ying 2026 (2)¶

Ji Ying. (2026). Academic Integrity in the Age of Generative AI: a Scoping Review of Research on Higher Education Student Voices. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09752-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Barus et al. (2025), that the policy and training vacuum pushes students into "de facto policymaking roles", developing informal personal ethical frameworks; the review also notes students interpret lack of clear institutional guidance as silent approval of their actions.

"the ubiquitous policy and training vacuum pushes students into what Barus et al. (2025) identify as de facto policymaking roles, where they develop informal, personal ethical frameworks based on personal values, contextual norms, peer norms, and perceived consequences of using GenAI"
