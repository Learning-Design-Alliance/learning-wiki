### Christopher 2012

Christopher, M. E., Miyake, A., Keenan, J. M., Pennington, B., DeFries, J. C., Wadsworth, S. J., Willcutt, E., & Olson, R. K. (2012). Predicting Word Reading and Comprehension with Executive Function and Speed Measures Across Development: A Latent Variable Analysis. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3360115/

`q3 · i?`

Confirmatory factor analysis of the three reading factors with two comprehension tests cross-loaded onto word reading, fit to the combined sample. The article reports "The high CFI (.99), low RMSEA (.03)" and a significant but low chi-square, indicating good fit.

> "The high CFI (.99), low RMSEA (.03), and low χ2 ([79] = 115.16, p = .01) show that this model fits the data well."
