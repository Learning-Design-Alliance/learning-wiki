Evidence¶

Erdem Onan 2024¶

Erdem Onan, Felicitas Biwer, Roman Abel, Wisnu Wiradhany & Anique de Bruin. (2024). Optimizing self-organized study orders: combining refutations and metacognitive prompts improves the use of interleaved practice. npj Science of Learning 9:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00245-7

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Generalized linear mixed-effects models (random intercepts for subjects and items) with interleaving proportion predicting item-level classification accuracy in the self-organized tasks; each switch increased odds of correct classification by 4% (paintings) and 5% (birds). Correlational, not causal.

"The results revealed that the use of interleaved practice was associated with higher classiﬁcation accuracy when students learned painting styles (post intervention task), Estimate = 1.56,SE = 0.54,z = 2.77,p < 0.005."
