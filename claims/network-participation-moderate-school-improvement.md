### Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project 1998

Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project. (1998). https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Principal survey self-report data on the impact of working with external partners. About 60 percent attributed moderate improvement, 27 percent were more positive, and only 12 percent saw minimal improvement; improvements were mainly in organizational development such as new curricular programs and professional development opportunities.

> "About 60 percent of the principals attributed moderate improvement to these relationships."

### Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project 1998 (2)

Initial Accomplishments of Implementation Networks: A Baseline Technical Report of the Chicago Annenberg Research Project. (1998). https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interviews with 30 external partners of Annenberg networks. Almost two-thirds noted some school improvement, and "Nearly three-quarters of those who spoke of school improvement pointed particularly to the development of within-school teacher collaboration," with between-school teacher networking discussed by nearly one-third.

> "Nearly three-quarters of those who spoke of school improvement pointed particularly to the development of within-school teacher collaboration."
