Claims

In vocabulary-app review logs, a successful recall lengthened memory half-life more when the review came at a lower recall probability, less as the memory strengthened, and less for harder words

Rules fitted to 220 million review logs from a vocabulary app show the spacing effect at item level (a recall at lower predicted recall probability multiplied half-life more), diminishing gains as half-life grows, smaller gains for harder words, and, after a failed recall, a new half-life that is longer for stronger memories and shorter the later the review.
