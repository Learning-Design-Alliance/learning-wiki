Evidence¶

Wongpinunwatana 2013¶

Nitaya Wongpinunwatana. (2013). Enhancing Information Systems Auditing Knowledge with Role-Playing Game: An Experimental Investigation. International Education Studies, 6(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n9p72

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 75 graduate accounting students. Mean perceived knowledgeability rose from 2.146 at pretest to 3.733 at posttest; the paired-samples t-test showed "significance for the effect of level of teaching-methods-activity-fit on the perceived knowledgeability". No effect size is printed.

"The results of a paired-samples t-test in Table 2 indicated significance for the effect of level of teaching-methods-activity-fit on the perceived knowledgeability (t=20.835, p < 0.001, see Table 2)"
