## Evidence

### Kawasoe 2026

Kawasoe, R., Matsumura, K., Shinohara, T., Arima, K., Takeo, Y., Ikeda, T. & Sugata, H. (2026). Wakeful targeted memory reactivation during short rest periods modulates early motor learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00407-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with learning phase (early, intermediate, late) as within-subject factor and Group as between-subject factor, in the TMRregular (16), TMRfast (16), and TMRrandom (15) groups after exclusions. The article prints "𝜂2 = 0.132" for the interaction; the main effect of learning phase reached η2 = 0.683 in the same ANOVA (Fig. 3B caption: "p < 0.001").

> "Additionally, there was a significant Group × Learning Phase interaction (𝐹(4.88) = 3.36, p = 0.013, 𝜂2 = 0.132)"
