Roach Pamela, Hernandez Santanna, Carbert Amanda, Jalil Rabiya, Panaccione Remo, Ruzycki Shannon M. (2022). Anti-Indigenous bias of medical school applicants: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03739-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of bias measures stratified by application status (Table 3). Matriculants' median explicit preference was 32 (IQR 27-35) versus 50 (IQR 40-55) for those not admitted, Wilcoxon p = 0.01; the implicit comparison was p = 0.12 and not significant.

> "applicants who were offered admission and who matriculated in the Class of 2024 had a median explicit bias score that suggested a preference for Indigenous people over white people compared to participants who were not offered admission."
