Evidence¶

Ismail et al. 2018¶

Mohd Nasir Ismail, Nurfaezah Mamat, Adnan Jamaludin. (2018). The Effects of Webopac Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration on Information Literacy Skills among First Year Degree Students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165734

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA comparing the two logical thinking levels (HLT vs LLT), classified by GALT scores of 7-12 and 0-6 respectively, in the 3x2 factorial design (n=150). The article prints "F-value = 83.252, p-value = 0.000" with effect size 0.254 for Level in Table 3.

"There is a significant difference in the gain scores for the information literacy (IL) skill between HLT and LLT students (F-value = 83.252, p-value = 0.000)."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Within each method, HLT students scored significantly higher than LLT students under WSTTG and WSTT, and the article reports the T-method contrast as non-significant despite printing p=0.011 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • LLT students showed the largest mean-score gap relative to traditional instruction under the guided-exploration method, with the article stating LLT students taught via WSTTG benefited more than HLT students taught via the same method — related
  • No statistically significant interaction effect between instructional method and logical thinking level on information literacy skill (F=1.638, p=0.198) — related
  • Instructional method significantly affects information literacy skill, with WSTTG outperforming both WSTT and traditional instruction (F=13.409, p=0.000) — related
