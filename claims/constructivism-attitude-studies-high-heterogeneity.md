## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The included studies were heterogeneous (Q(42) = 564,690, p < .05; I² = 92.562%), so the random effect model was used for effect size calculation. [→ Toraman 2016](#toraman-2016)

## Evidence

### Toraman 2016

Toraman, C., & Demir, E. (2016). The effect of constructivism on attitudes towards lessons: A meta–analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 62, 115-142. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.62.8

`q3 · i?`

Q analysis of the 43 included studies in the meta-analysis. The article reports the degree of heterogeneity "was at a level of 93% (I 2 = %92,562)", exceeding the 75% threshold the authors cite, so the random effect model was adopted.

> "At the end of the analysis conducted for his study, the 43 studies were defined as heterogenous (Q (42)=564,690, p<.05). The significant nature of the Q statistical figure confirmed the assumption that the studies differed from one another."
