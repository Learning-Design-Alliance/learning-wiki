### Mazgutova N 2026

Mazgutova N, Mokra D, Calkovska A, Plevkova J, Biringerova Z, Mokry J. (2026). Teaching Physiology-First Experience With Using a Simulator in Teaching Physiology of the Cardiovascular System. Physiological Research. https://doi.org/10.33549/physiolres.935831

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous online questionnaire administered at the end of the practical session to 110 respondents (68 Slovak-program, 42 English-program). Students reported the simulations improved understanding of lecture material and found activities easy to perform, with open-ended comments including "helpful" and "enjoyable".

> "Respondents (n=110; 68 Slovak- and 42 English-program students) perceived the simulation-based teaching very positively. Most students reported that simulations improved their understanding of cardiovascular physiology topics previously covered in lectures."
