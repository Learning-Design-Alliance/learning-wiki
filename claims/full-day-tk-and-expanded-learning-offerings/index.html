Statewide LEA survey (N = 1,415 LEAs with TK), reported in the TK Service Delivery Models section and Figure 3. Full-day TK is defined as more than 4 hours; part-day as 3 to 4 hours.

"Eighty-four percent of all LEAs offered full-day TK; 82% did so in 2022–23. In 2023–24, 78% of LEAs offered only full-day TK, and 6% offered both full-day and part-day options."
