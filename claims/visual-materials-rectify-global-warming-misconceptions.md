# Instruction with visual materials rectified more global warming misconceptions than traditional instruction (about 40% versus about 12% success)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Preservice teachers taught with visual materials reduced misconceptions about the reasons for global warming from 14 to 6, while the control group reduced them only from 10 to 9. [→ Aykut Emre Bozdogan 2011](#aykut-emre-bozdogan-2011)

## Evidence

### Aykut Emre Bozdogan 2011

Aykut Emre Bozdogan. (2011). The Effects of Instruction with Visual Materials on the Development of Preservice Elementary Teachers' Knowledge and Attitude Towards Global Warming. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932241

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of open-ended question answers (Table 2) in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 47 preservice teachers: experimental group misconceptions fell from 14 to 6, deficient knowledge from 4 to 3; control group misconceptions fell only from 10 to 9. The article reports "approximately 40%" versus "approximately 12%" success.

> "The number of preservice teachers who had misconceptions about the reasons for global warming decreased from 14 to 6. The number of those with deficient information decreased from 4 to 3."
