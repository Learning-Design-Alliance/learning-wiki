## Evidence

### Alqudah 2019

Alqudah, D., Bidin, A., & Md Hussin, M. A. H. (2019). The Impact of Educational Infographic on Students’ Interaction and Perception in Jordanian Higher Education: Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 12(4), 669-688. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12443a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of short quizzes on a 10-point scale after three chapters taught over 16 lectures; experimental groups received infographic-supported lectures and control groups conventional instruction. The article reports the IT experimental mean of "(7.9/10)" against a control mean of 6.6/10, and separately reports the Business experimental mean at 7.5/10 against a control mean of 5.7/10; no significance test or effect size is printed for these comparisons.

> "After the infographic experiment was finished, the findings indicated t hat the mean of marks in the experimental group from the IT College was (7.9/10), which was higher than that of the control group at (6.6/10)."
