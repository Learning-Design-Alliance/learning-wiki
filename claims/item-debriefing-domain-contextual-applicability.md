`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Debriefing-domain analysis in the cognitive interview study: participants had not undergone a formal structured postactivity debrief, so the authors attribute the 4-of-5 difficulties to possible "limited contextual applicability" and retained all original debriefing wording.

> "Because PEARLS was developed specifically for structured simulation debriefing [7,16], these difficulties may have reflected limited contextual applicability rather than wording alone. The original wording of all debriefing questions was therefore retained."
