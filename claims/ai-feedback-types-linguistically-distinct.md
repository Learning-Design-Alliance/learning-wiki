## Evidence

### Omar Alsaiari 2026

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Omid Noroozi, Dragan Gašević, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). DIRECTIVE, METACOGNITIVE OR A BLEND OF BOTH? A COMPARISON OF AI-GENERATED FEEDBACK TYPES ON STUDENT ENGAGEMENT, CONFIDENCE, AND OUTCOMES. A PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on coded feedback texts from the RCT's Suggestions segments, with Tukey post hoc tests. The omnibus test showed a large effect, F(2, 1024) = 3329.39, p< .001, "η2 =.867", with all pairwise comparisons significant (all adjusted p<10−12).

> "The Metacognitive condition exhibited the highest frequency (M= 4.45,SD= 1.07), the Hybrid condition showed intermediate levels (M= 2.20,SD= 0.65), and the Directive condition contained virtually none (M= 0.01,SD= 0.12)."

### Omar Alsaiari 2026 (2)

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Omid Noroozi, Dragan Gašević, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). DIRECTIVE, METACOGNITIVE OR A BLEND OF BOTH? A COMPARISON OF AI-GENERATED FEEDBACK TYPES ON STUDENT ENGAGEMENT, CONFIDENCE, AND OUTCOMES. A PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on the same coded feedback corpus. The omnibus test was significant with a very large effect size, F(2, 1024) = 2340.75, p< .001, "η 2 =.821", with all pairwise contrasts significant (all adjusted p< .001).

> "The Directive condition contained the highest frequency of imperatives per 100 words (M= 2.91,SD= 0.81), the Hybrid condition showed moderate use (M= 1.34,SD= 0.52), and the Metacognitive condition contained none (M= 0.00,SD= 0.00)."

### Omar Alsaiari 2026 (3)

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Omid Noroozi, Dragan Gašević, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). DIRECTIVE, METACOGNITIVE OR A BLEND OF BOTH? A COMPARISON OF AI-GENERATED FEEDBACK TYPES ON STUDENT ENGAGEMENT, CONFIDENCE, AND OUTCOMES. A PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on word counts of feedback texts. The omnibus test showed a large effect, F(2, 1024) = 1019.56, p< .001, "η 2 =.666"; Hybrid exceeded Directive by 50.89 words and Metacognitive by 37.04 words (all adjusted p< .001).

> "The Hybrid condition produced the longest text (M= 134.14, SD= 17.66, 95% CI [132.23, 136.05]), the Metacognitive condition generated intermediate-length responses (M= 97.10,SD= 11.71), and the Directive condition yielded the shortest text (M= 83.25,SD= 15.63)."
