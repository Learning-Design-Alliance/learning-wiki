Two-way repeated measures ANOVA of dichotomized SP encounter scores (interrater reliability 88%) rated by two blinded non-investigator reviewers in the pilot (n = 12). The article reports the significant main effect of domain and time; no effect size is printed.

> "the main effect of domain and time was significant (F(1, 10) = 5.002, p =.049), confirming the significant improvement in Communication and Examination skills after the intervention."
