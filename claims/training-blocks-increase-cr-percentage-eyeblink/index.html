Evidence¶

Leila Etemadi 2023¶

Leila Etemadi, Dan-Anders Jirenhed and Anders Rasmussen. (2023). Effects of working memory load and CS-US intervals on delay eyeblink conditioning. npj Science of Learning 8:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00167-w

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Linear mixed effects model of CR percentage across 10 training blocks in 42 participants, with subject ID as random effect. The model shows "For each block of 10 trials, the CR percentage increases by an average of 2.15%" with a significant t statistic; no standardized effect size was printed.

"For each block of 10 trials, the CR percentage increases by an average of 2.15%. Over ten blocks, this translates to 2.1510 = 21.5%. This change is statistically signi ﬁcant (t = 8.4339, P = 4.4686e–16**, CI = 1.65–2.65%)."
