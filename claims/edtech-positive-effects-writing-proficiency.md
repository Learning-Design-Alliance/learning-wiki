### Rebecca D. Silverman 2025

Rebecca D. Silverman, Kristin Keane, Elena Hammond, S. Khanna. (2025). The Effects of Educational Technology Interventions on Literacy in Elementary School: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research, 95(5), 972-1012. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241261073

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis of elementary-school educational technology literacy interventions reports positive effects on writing proficiency, with the article's caveat that "effect sizes differed dramatically across outcomes"; no pooled effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Findings indicate positive effects of educational technology interventions on constrained skills, unconstrained skills, reading comprehension, and writing proficiency though effect sizes differed dramatically across outcomes."
