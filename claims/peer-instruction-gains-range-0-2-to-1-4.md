## Evidence

### Ibukunoluwa Bukola 2025

Ibukunoluwa Bukola, Meagan Sundstrom, Justin Gambrell, Olive Ross, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Eric Brewe. (2025). Multi-institutional assessment of Peer Instruction implementation and impacts using the Framework for Interactive Learning in Lectures. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.bukola](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.bukola)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational multi-institution study of seven introductory mechanics instructors; Hedges' g computed from matched pre/post concept inventory responses. The article reports effect sizes "range from 0.2 (Instructor A) to 1.4 (Instructor G)" and overlapping confidence intervals for most pairs; comparisons were qualitative, no statistical tests were run due to small sample size.

> "All but one implementation of Peer Instruction exhibited positive gains in student conceptual learning (the confidence intervals of Hedges’ g values do not overlap with zero for all instructors except for Instructor C"
