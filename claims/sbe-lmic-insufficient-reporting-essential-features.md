### Robinson SJA 2024

Robinson SJA, Ritchie AMA, Pacilli M, Nestel D, McLeod E, Nataraja RM. (2024). Simulation-Based Education of Health Workers in Low- and Middle-Income Countries: A Systematic Review. Glob Health Sci Pract. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-24-00187

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's narrative synthesis of reporting quality across included studies: few studies report methods sufficiently to compare against reporting guidelines, which the authors attribute to the recency of guideline development and their lack of emphasis on LMIC perspectives. Learner groups, facilitator training, and TTT approaches could not be meaningfully extracted.

> "Nonetheless, there is typically minimal reporting of simulation design, prebriefing, and debriefing, and limited discussion of educational factors, such as deliberate practice and mastery learning."
