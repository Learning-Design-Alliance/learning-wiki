Krebs 2012¶

Krebs, S.S., Roebers, C.M. (2012). The impact of retrieval processes, age, general achievement level, and test scoring scheme for children's metacognitive monitoring and controlling. Metacognition and Learning 7, 75–90. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9079-3

Baseline finding of the N = 150 experimental study: the article reports that "All children showed adequate metacognitive processes before and during test taking," spanning both age groups and both achievement levels, with no effect size printed.
