## Evidence

### Puddester Derek 2015

Puddester Derek, MacDonald Colla J., Clements Debbie, Gaffney Jane, Wiesenfeld Lorne. (2015). Designing faculty development to support the evaluation of resident competency in the intrinsic CanMEDS roles: practical outcomes of an assessment of program director needs. BMC Medical Education 15:100. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0375-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Under the 'Superficial' sub-theme of the interview analysis, directors gave examples: "We watch them talk to patients and if the patients are reasonably satisfied then they are rated as ‘good communicators’. We don’t have any measuring sticks"; another cited fear of reprisals for trainees and faculty.

> "PDs reported evaluation of the intrinsic roles was ‘superficial’. One PD explained: “I have very good trainees. When I get their ITER forms … everything is skewed … everything is outstanding."
