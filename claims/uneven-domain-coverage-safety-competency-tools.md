## Evidence

### Okuyama Ayako 2011

Okuyama Ayako, Martowirono Kartinie, Bijnen Bart. (2011). Assessing the patient safety competencies of healthcare professionals: a systematic review. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3203526/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's domain synthesis reports that "25 of our tools (74%) assessed the 'managing safety risks' competencies". It further reports 16 tools (47%) for communication, 12 (35%) for safety culture, six (18%) for adverse event disclosure, and only four (12%) for optimising human and environmental factors.

> "25 of our tools (74%) assessed the 'managing safety risks' competencies, and 24 tools (71%) measured the 'working in teams for patient safety' competencies."
