Evidence¶

Pala Ferhat Kadir 2020¶

Pala Ferhat Kadir, Erdem Mukaddes. (2020). Development of a participation style scale for online instructional discussions. Educational Technology Research and Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09817-x

q2 · i? · design · r3

Exploratory factor analysis with 450 participants using principal component analysis with varimax rotation found "Four-factor structures were formed in both dimensions," with the Why dimension explaining 53.58% and the How dimension explaining 54.31% of total variance. No effect size is printed for this finding.

"In the EFA, principal component analysis was applied with the varimax rotation method (Osborne 2015) with the eigenvalue of the items over 1. Four-factor structures were formed in both dimensions. According to the findings of the EFA, the Why dimension explained 53.58% of the total variance, whereas the How dimension explained 54.31% of the total variance."

Related Claims¶

  • The scale's internal consistency is acceptable, with Cronbach's alpha of 0.89 for the full scale and 0.70–0.85 for its four style factors
  • Focus group interviews with active and non-participating (lurking) students indicated that reasons for participation form a determinant dimension of online participation
  • Confirmatory factor analysis supports a second-level participation style model with four combined styles and acceptable fit indices
  • Four factors of ICT use explain 57.4% of the total variance in university students' types of ICT use — related
  • Motivation and learning-strategy scales cluster into three factors: High-Level Learning Strategies, High-Level Motivation, and Low-Level Learning Strategies and Motivation — related
  • Factor analysis identifies three components of ICT consideration: help/importance, tools for an end, and negative valuation — related
