  • Group awareness tools showing peer presence, peer activities, or multiple artifacts are integrated in many educational VRLs, but performance and teacher-presence indicators are never standalone — related
  • Synchronous communication is the most common collaboration support integrated in educational VRLs, and few labs offer none — related
  • Regulation tools for collaboration are very rare in educational VRLs, identified in only 8 laboratories, mostly supporting regulation by teachers — related
