## Evidence

### Lindamood Phoneme Sequencing® (LiPS®): Beginning Reading 2015

Lindamood Phoneme Sequencing® (LiPS®): Beginning Reading. (2015). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/280

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Randomized trial with 74 first-grade students at risk for reading difficulty in three schools; students received on average 84.5 hours of LiPS instruction in groups of three, four 50-minute sessions weekly from October through May. The printed domain average effect size for alphabetics is 0.63 (improvement index +24); WWC confirmed significance after multiple-comparison adjustment.

> "Torgesen et al. (2010) found statistically significant and substantively important positive effects of LiPS ® on word accuracy/fluency, phonemic decoding, reading comprehension, phonological awareness, and spelling when compared to the basal reading program alone."
