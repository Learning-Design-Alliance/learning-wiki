## Evidence

### Piñeros-Alvarez JL 2026

Piñeros-Alvarez JL, Portuguez-Jaramillo NE, Urbano-Cano AL. (2026). Effect of Real-time Feedback on the Quality of Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation for Medical Students in Colombia. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.50647

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized single-blind study of 98 fourth-year medical students; intervention trained with a PRESTAN real-time feedback device, control without. The intervention group's median compression target was "72% (40.7–81)" vs "3.4% (0–40)" in controls.

> "For the hands-only CPR test, statistically significant differences were found in the compression target: intervention group, 72% (40.7–81) vs control group, 3.4% (0–40) (P < .05)"
