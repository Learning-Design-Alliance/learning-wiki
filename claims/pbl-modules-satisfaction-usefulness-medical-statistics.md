## Evidence

### Bukumiric Zoran 2022

Bukumiric Zoran, Ilic Aleksandra, Pajcin Mirjana, Srebro Dragana, Milicevic Sasa, Spaic Dragan, Markovic Nenad, Corac Aleksandar. (2022). Effects of problem-based learning modules within blended learning courses in medical statistics – A randomized controlled pilot study. PLoS ONE 17(1): e0263015. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263015

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous online five-point Likert questionnaire administered to the hPBL group after the course; all 26 responding hPBL students endorsed the modules' usefulness, with no printed effect size.

> "All the students in hPBL group (100.0%) thought that the PBL modules helped them to achieve the desirable knowledge in medical statistics."

### Bukumiric Zoran 2022 (2)

Bukumiric Zoran, Ilic Aleksandra, Pajcin Mirjana, Srebro Dragana, Milicevic Sasa, Spaic Dragan, Markovic Nenad, Corac Aleksandar. (2022). Effects of problem-based learning modules within blended learning courses in medical statistics – A randomized controlled pilot study. PLoS ONE 17(1): e0263015. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263015

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Course satisfaction comparison between the two randomized groups on the anonymous Likert questionnaire; the article reports medians and a significant group difference (p = 0.012) with no printed effect size.

> "Students in hPBL group had a significantly higher score on total satisfaction with the course (median 5, range 4–5) compared to the students in BL group (median 5, range 3–5), (p = 0.012)."
