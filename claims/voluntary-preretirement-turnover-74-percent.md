Descriptive analysis of the 2020–21 NTPS and 2021–22 TFS (Figure 4) classifying turnover as voluntary preretirement, retirement, or involuntary. The report finds "74%" of turnover was voluntary preretirement, about 14% retirement, and nearly 12% involuntary.

> "nearly three quarters of teachers who moved or left teaching did so voluntarily and for reasons other than retirement (74%), slightly more than 14% of teachers retired, and nearly 12% moved schools or left the profession involuntarily."
