`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis (Table 4, N = 248) found academic rank significantly predicted expert (0.217, p < 0.01) and delegator (0.175, p < 0.05) styles; the article interprets this as seniors being more expert and delegator teachers than juniors. Coefficients are unstandardised, so no effect size is reported.
