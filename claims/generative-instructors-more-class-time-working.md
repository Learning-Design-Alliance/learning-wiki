## Evidence

### Andrews Tessa C. 2019

Andrews Tessa C., Auerbach Anna Jo J., Grant Emily F. (2019). Exploring the Relationship between Teacher Knowledge and Active-Learning Implementation in Large College Biology Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education 18(4). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-01-0010

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparison of two instructor groups (five generative, eight active) from a study of 13 large life sciences courses, using Welch's unequal variance t tests on proportion of class time students worked. The article reports "a very large effect" of g = 3.6 (95% CI: 1.6–5.6).

> "Instructors exhibiting generative active learning taught lessons that engaged students in a significantly greater proportion of work during class time (t = 6.70, p < 0.0001). The effect size for this comparison was 3.6 (95% confidence interval: 1.6–5.6)."
