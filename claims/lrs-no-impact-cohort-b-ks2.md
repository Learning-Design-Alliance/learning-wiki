## Evidence

### Wiggins et al. 2017

Meg Wiggins, Mary Sawtell and Dr John Jerrim. (2017). Learner Response System: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581136

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Table 1 of the cluster RCT reports maths (cohort B) effect size -0.08 (-0.21 to 0.05), p 0.24, estimated -1 months progress, 3,127 pupils; reading (cohort B) was -0.04 (-0.17 to 0.10), p 0.60, 3,013 pupils. The report states "no evidence that the LRS improved Key Stage 2 results" for either cohort.
