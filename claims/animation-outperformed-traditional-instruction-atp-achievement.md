`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate Analysis of Variance comparing post achievement test scores of the control group (n = 20, traditional instruction) and experimental group (n = 40, animation-cum-discussion instruction) in a non-equivalent quasi-experimental design. The article reports F(1/118) = 323.42, p < 0.01, with means 12.51±2.58 versus 25.62±2.79; no standardized effect size is printed.
