# L2 anxiety is negatively correlated with L2 achievement, with a mean correlation of r = −.36 across 216 effect sizes

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Across 216 effect sizes from 105 independent samples, L2 anxiety correlates negatively with language achievement at a mean of r = −.36. [→ Yasser Teimouri 2019](#yasser-teimouri-2019)

## Evidence

### Yasser Teimouri 2019

Yasser Teimouri, Julia Goetze, and Luke Plonsky. (2019). SECOND LANGUAGE ANXIETY AND ACHIEVEMENT: A META-ANALYSIS. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 41, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263118000311

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 97 reports contributing 105 independent samples (N = 19,933) from 23 countries; the aggregate of 216 correlations yielded "a mean of r = −.36 for the relationship between L2 anxiety and language achievement."

> "In the aggregate, the 216 effect sizes (i.e., correlations) reported in the primary studies yielded a mean of r = −.36 for the relationship between L2 anxiety and language achievement."
