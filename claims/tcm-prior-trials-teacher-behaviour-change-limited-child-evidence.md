`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The protocol reports this second trial of TCM in isolation, describing its scale (11 schools, 22 teachers, 207 children aged 4–7 years) and its finding of "detected changes in teacher's behaviour on both observation and self-report". The article notes neither trial has been published in peer reviewed journals.

### Ford Tamsin 2012 (2)

Supporting teachers and children in schools: the effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of the incredible years teacher classroom management programme in primary school children: a cluster randomised controlled trial, with parallel economic and process evaluations.
