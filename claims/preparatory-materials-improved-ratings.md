`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive comparison of Likert feedback medians across the first and third iterations of the CSC. Sessions without preparatory materials "demonstrated comparatively lower ratings for teaching materials, most notably the PAFB and FESS sessions"; responses were analyzed descriptively as medians with median absolute deviations, and no effect size was printed.
