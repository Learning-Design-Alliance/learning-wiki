Evidence¶

Marina Milovanoviü 2013¶

Marina Milovanoviü, Jasmina Obradoviü, Aleksandar Milajiü. (2013). APPLICATION OF INTERACTIVE MULTIMEDIA TOOLS IN TEACHING MATHEMATICS – EXAMPLES OF LESSONS FROM GEOMETRY. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008863

End-of-research survey of the two multimedia groups (25 students each). Students explained preferences with reasons such as "Picture is essential for understanding geometry, and it is even better with animation and movements in 3-D space" and easier comprehension via step-by-step animation.
