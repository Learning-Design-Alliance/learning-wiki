# Students in the Control condition held less positive BSDS attitudes averaged across time than students in the Display and Activity conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` BSDS scores averaged across all three time points were significantly higher (less positive) in the Control condition than in the Display or Activity conditions, which did not differ from each other. [→ Maurer and Keim 2018](#maurer-and-keim-2018)

## Evidence

### Maurer and Keim 2018

Trent W. Maurer, Cassidy Keim. (2018). Teaching about prejudice with a Bogardus Social Distance Scale activity: Replication and extension. IJ-SoTL, Vol. 12, No. 1, Art. 7. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2018.120107

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects effect for Condition in the repeated-measures MANOVA, F(2, 234) = 11.29, p < .001, partial eta-squared = 0.09, with Bonferroni post hoc tests showing Control higher than Display and Activity. The authors interpret this as "a pre-existing difference between the students in the Control condition".

> "Bonferroni-corrected post hoc tests for Condition indicated that BSDS scores averaged across all three time points were significantly higher in the Control condition than the Display (mean difference = 0.69, SE = 0.17, p < .001) or Activity (mean difference = 0.75, SE = 0.17, p < .001) conditions, which were not significantly different from one another."
