Self-reported frequency items in the survey of 88 final-year dental students. The results state that "93.2% of students reported performing direct pulp capping either frequently or sometimes" in mature teeth, with lower proportions for pulpotomy procedures.

> "In mature teeth, 93.2% of students reported performing direct pulp capping either frequently or sometimes. The corresponding proportions were 53.4% for partial pulpotomy and 54.5% for total pulpotomy."
