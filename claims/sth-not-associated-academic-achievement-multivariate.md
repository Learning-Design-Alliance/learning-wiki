`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the cross-sectional multivariate regression analyses of school grades in boys (n = 664) and girls (n = 613); girls' STH estimates approached but did not reach significance (e.g., p = 0.053 for end-of-year results). The article reports no evidence of an STH–achievement association in the multivariate models.

> "Contrary to previous studies [23, 54, 55], we did not find evidence in the multivariate analyses that STH infections are associated with academic achievement (although a tendency was observed among girls)."
