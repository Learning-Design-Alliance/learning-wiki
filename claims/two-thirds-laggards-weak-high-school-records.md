Analysis of high school records for 78 laggard-list students against the proposed entrance requirement of at least 75 per cent (or 80 per cent where passing was 75). The article reports 47, or practically two-thirds, would not have been certified.

> "Of 78 students on the laggard list about whom I received reports from the superintendents or principals, 47, or practically two-thirds, would not have been certified to the university for admis-sion on the basis of their high school records if our new re-quirement had been in effect."
