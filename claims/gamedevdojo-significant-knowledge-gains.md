## Evidence

### Michael Holly 2024

Michael Holly, Lisa Habich, and Johanna Pirker. (2024). GameDevDojo - An Educational Game for Teaching Game Development Concepts. Proceedings of the 19th International Conference on the Foundations of Digital Games (FDG 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3649921.3649992

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In an AB user study with 57 middle and high school students, paired knowledge questions were administered before and after the learning session. McNemar's test showed that "Both groups significantly increased their knowledge outcomes."

> "To detect significant changes within the paired knowledge data, we used the McNemar’s test. Both groups significantly increased their knowledge outcomes."
