## Evidence

### Cofini Vincenza 2022

Cofini Vincenza, Perilli Enrico, Moretti Annalucia, Bianchini Valeria, Perazzini Matteo, Muselli Mario, Lanzi Sabrina, Tobia Loreta, Fabiani Leila, Necozione Stefano. (2022). E-Learning Satisfaction, Stress, Quality of Life, and Coping: A Cross-Sectional Study in Italian University Students a Year after the COVID-19 Pandemic Began. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19138214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate Spearman correlation in a cross-sectional survey of 471 Italian university students showing that "satisfaction increased with increasing age (rho = 0.3023; p = 0.0000)". The final regression model also found age positively associated (beta = 0.123; p < 0.001). No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "As reported in Table 4, the Spearman correlation analysis showed that the satisfaction increased with increasing age (rho = 0.3023; p = 0.0000)."
