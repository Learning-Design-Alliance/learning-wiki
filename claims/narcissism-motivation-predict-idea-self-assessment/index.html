Lebuda 2025¶

Lebuda, I., Hofer, G., & Benedek, M. (2025). Determinants of creative metacognitive monitoring: creativity, personality, and task-related predictors of self-assessed ideas and creative performance. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09432-2

Hierarchical multiple regression (N = 425) predicting self-assessed idea creativity; because divergent thinking was virtually unrelated to the outcome, the creative-ability step was omitted, and the final model retained narcissism and motivation as unique predictors.

"people with higher narcissism and task-related motivation tended to self-assess their idea creativity more positively."
