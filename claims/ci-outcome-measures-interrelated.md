### Derek M. Houston 2012

Derek M. Houston, Jessica Stewart, Aaron Moberly, George Hollich, and Richard T. Miyamoto. (2012). Word learning in deaf children with cochlear implants: effects of early auditory experience. Dev Sci. 2012 May ; 15(3): 448–461. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2012.01140.x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlations among outcome measures (GAEL-P, PSI, LNT, PPVT) at two years post-CI, displayed in Table 3. Correlations were all positive with some reaching statistical significance, consistent with other findings that language abilities are highly interrelated in children with CIs.

> "As can be seen, correlations among the outcome measures were all in the positive direction with some of them reaching statistical significance even with small sample sizes."
