## Evidence

### Ahmed 2026

Uqaili, A. A., Abbas, U., Khan, A. M., Memon, A., Farrukh, G. M., & Shah, T. (2026). Undergraduate medical students’ perceptions of case-based learning in preclinical physiology within a resource limited setting: a qualitative study. *Scientific Reports, 16*, 10163. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-40750-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-40750-w)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of four focus group discussions with 30 second-year MBBS students at a resource-limited public-sector medical university; students contrasted CBL with didactic teaching, saying “these sessions keep us active. We actively think and participate”.

> "Students consistently described case-based learning as a more engaging and interactive alternative to traditional lectures, shifting learning from passive listening to active discussion and problem-solving."
