## Evidence

### Marissa L. Weichman 2018

Marissa L. Weichman. (2018). The Forgotten Night: The Number Devil Explores Spherical Geometry. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.3342

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The pedagogical device throughout the chapter is dialogic elicitation: Robert proposes each step, such as the polar-angle complement, and the Number Devil confirms it. Similar prompted derivations carry the coordinates, the "Polka Dot Product," the Bermuda Triangle angles, and Euler's formula.

> "That would make the angle between that line and the North Pole 90 minus theta degrees" said Robert.
"Perfect!" declared the Number Devil."
