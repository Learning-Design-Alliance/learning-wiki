Freytag J, Stroben F, Hautz WE, Penders D, Kämmer JE. (2021). Effects of using a cognitive aid on content and feasibility of debriefings of simulated emergencies. GMS J Med Educ 38(5):Doc95. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001491.shtml
