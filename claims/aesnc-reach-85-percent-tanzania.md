`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational implementation study at one zonal hospital and three health centres in Mwanza. Of 246 providers, 90% (221) were eligible and the consent rate of eligible providers was 90% (n=195). The authors report "85% (195/231) of providers" reached, spanning medical, nursing and clinical officer cadres.

> "aESNC reached 85% (195/231) of providers: 75 medical, 53 nursing and 21 clinical officers; 110 (56%) were at the zonal hospital and 85 (44%) at health centres."
