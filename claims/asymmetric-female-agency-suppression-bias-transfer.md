## Evidence

### Ariyan Hossain 2026

Ariyan Hossain, Kazi Kamruzzaman Rabbi, Farig Sadeque, S M Taiabul Haque. (2026). Contaminated Collaboration: Measuring Gender Bias Transfer in LLM-Assisted Student Writing. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15914

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA (N=123) predicting agency score by biography subject gender and prompt condition. The interaction effect was F=4.41, p=.014, η²p=.070; biography gender was the strongest predictor (F=20.17, p<.001, η²p=.147).

> "The two-way ANOV A (Table 4) revealed a signif- icant Gender× Condition interaction alongside main effects of gender and prompt condition."

### Ariyan Hossain 2026 (2)

Ariyan Hossain, Kazi Kamruzzaman Rabbi, Farig Sadeque, S M Taiabul Haque. (2026). Contaminated Collaboration: Measuring Gender Bias Transfer in LLM-Assisted Student Writing. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15914

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effects analysis within the 123-participant experiment. Female agency was significantly lower in the biased condition than neutral (p=.023) and control (p=.037); control and neutral did not differ (p=.982). The article prints F and p values for these simple effects but no effect size.

> "Prompt condition significantly affected agency in female-target essays ( F(2,60) = 4.71 , p= .013) but not male-target essays (F(2,57) = 2.16 , p=.125 ), with all male pairwise contrasts non- significant (allp>.10 )."
