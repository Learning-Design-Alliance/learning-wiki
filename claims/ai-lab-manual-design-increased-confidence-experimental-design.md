# AI-supported lab manual design increased students' confidence in experimental design in an upper-division analytical chemistry laboratory course

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' median self-assessed confidence in experimental design rose from 3 (neutral) to 4 (agree) after the AI-supported lab manual design project. [→ Yim 2026](#yim-2026)

## Evidence

### Yim 2026

Yim, K.-H.; Lui, M. Y. (2026). Supporting Undergraduate Students' Learning in Practical Chemistry Courses through AI-Supported Experimental Design. Journal of Chemical Education. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.5c01311

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre- and postsurvey (five-point Likert) in a course of 38 third-year analytical chemistry undergraduates in 8 groups who designed AI-assisted lab manuals; 31 students took part in data collection, and the confidence item had 29 presurvey and 32 postsurvey respondents. Median rose from 3 to 4, with "60% of respondents selecting agree or strongly agree". Students attributed improvement to project completion and professional validation.

> "the postsurvey results indicated an increase in students' confidence in designing experiments. For the same question, the median response rose to 4 (agree), with 60% of respondents selecting agree or strongly agree (n = 32)"
