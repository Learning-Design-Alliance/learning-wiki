## Evidence

### Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D. 2026

Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D., Teresa-Morales Cristina, Rodríguez-Pérez Margarita, Torres-Enamorado Dolores, Feria-Ramírez Carmen, Urcola-Pardo Fernando. (2026). Nursing students’ evaluations of the flipped classroom using the FLAS scale: a quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08625-w

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of first- (n = 155) and third-year (n = 277) nursing students' FLAS scores after a flipped classroom intervention. The article reports "third-year students achieved significantly higher average scores on the FLAS Scale (U = 25,872.0, p < .001, r = .12)"; Table 4 prints r = .17 for the total scale and r = .22, .11, .12 for the dimensions.

> "A comparative analysis of the perception of the use of the flipped classroom methodology among first- and third-year students confirmed this trend, revealing that third-year students achieved significantly higher average scores on the FLAS Scale (U = 25,872.0, p < .001, r = .12)"
