# SBI significantly improves seventh-grade students' general mathematical problem solving (GMADE) relative to business-as-usual instruction, contrary to prior SBI studies

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` For the full sample, SBI students scored significantly higher than control students on the GMADE general problem-solving posttest (g = 0.32). [→ Jitendra 2017](#jitendra-2017)

## Evidence

### Jitendra 2017

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Karl, S. R., Im, S-H., & Slater, S. C. (2017). Investigating a Tier 1 intervention focused on proportional reasoning: A follow-up study. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 83(4), 340-358. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402917691017

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the GMADE Process and Applications posttest in the randomized cluster trial; the difference was ".32 SD". The article reports this as g = 0.32 and notes it contrasts with prior SBI studies, with only one-third of GMADE items reflecting concepts taught.

> "For the GMADE posttest Table 5 shows that SBI was a significant predictor with an SBI vs. control difference of .32 SD or 63% of SBI students scored above the mean of control students."
