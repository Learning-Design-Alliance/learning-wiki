In the cross-sectional survey (N = 218), high burnout was reported among 40% (n = 20) of fourth-year students versus 11.9% (n = 5) of second-year students, with the association by academic level significant at P = 0.050.

> "High burnout was reported among 40% (n = 20) of students in their fourth year compared to 11.9% (n = 5) of students in their second year of medical school. The overall association between high burnout and academic level was significant (P = 0.050)."
