## Evidence

### Popa-Velea Ovidiu 2021

Popa-Velea Ovidiu, Pârvan Irina, Diaconescu Liliana Veronica. (2021). The Impact of Self-Efficacy, Optimism, Resilience and Perceived Stress on Academic Performance and Its Subjective Evaluation: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression on the upper GPA quartile (GPA above 9.64, N = 29 per Table 3) found no significant model: "None of the models proved to be statistically significant"; the study did not detect an effect and equivalence was not tested.

> "None of the models proved to be statistically significant (F = 1.323, p < 0.284 at model 1; F = 0.815, p < 0.585 at model 2)."

### Popa-Velea Ovidiu 2021 (2)

Popa-Velea Ovidiu, Pârvan Irina, Diaconescu Liliana Veronica. (2021). The Impact of Self-Efficacy, Optimism, Resilience and Perceived Stress on Academic Performance and Its Subjective Evaluation: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The discussion attributes the weak prediction of high GPA to unexplored factors such as learned resourcefulness, conscientiousness, sense of coherence or self-esteem, noting only "4.8% of the GPAs variance" was predicted.

> "this was predicted in a much lesser amount (namely 4.8% of the GPAs variance) by the considered psychological variables."
