## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Venetsanos 2026

Venetsanos, D. T. (2026). A Tripartite Feedback Framework for AI-Assisted Assessment of Complex Reports in Higher Education. AI Educ. 2026, 2, 26. https://doi.org/10.3390/aieduc2030026

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical argument in Section 2.5.1 of this conceptual paper. The article argues the consequence is that institutional decisions about AI integration are made "without principled guidance", defaulting to blanket automation or blanket rejection, which the tripartite framework addresses via an operational taxonomy.

> "both frameworks were developed prior to the emergence of capable AI systems and are consequently silent on a question that has become practically urgent: which specific feedback tasks are appropriately supported by technology, and which must remain human responsibilities?"
