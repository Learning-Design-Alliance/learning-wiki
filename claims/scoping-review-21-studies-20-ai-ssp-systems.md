`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic scoping review searching eight databases plus manual searching, with two independent reviewers screening records and prospective OSF registration. The review reports that 21 studies describing 20 unique AI-SSP systems were included, with "15 (71%) published in 2024 or later".

> "Of 5987 database records and 4 identified through other methods, 21 studies describing 20 unique AI-SSP systems were included. Studies were published between 2008 and 2026, with 15 (71%) published in 2024 or later."
