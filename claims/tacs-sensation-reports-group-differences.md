# Sensations during stimulation did not differ across groups except for metallic/iron taste, which was lower in the Sham group than the Parietal Theta group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Itching, pain, burning, warmth/heat, fatigue/decreased alertness, and general feeling did not differ across the four groups, but metallic/iron taste did, with Sham significantly lower than Parietal Theta (p = 0.006). [→ Limor Shtoots 2024](#limor-shtoots-2024)

## Evidence

### Limor Shtoots 2024

Limor Shtoots, Asher Nadler, Roni Partouche, Dorin Sharir, Aryeh Rothstein, Liran Shati & Daniel A. Levy. (2024). Frontal midline theta transcranial alternating current stimulation enhances early consolidation of episodic memory. npj Science of Learning 9:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00222-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adverse-effects questionnaire completed after 20 min of tACS/Sham in the four-group experiment. Univariate GLM analyses found no group differences on most sensations (e.g., itching F(3,68) = 1.507, p = 0.221), but "Sham group participants reported signiﬁcantly lower levels of metallic/iron taste in comparison to the Parietal Theta tACS group, p = 0.006." No effect size is printed.

> "Sham group participants reported signiﬁcantly lower levels of metallic/iron taste in comparison to the Parietal Theta tACS group, p = 0.006; which aligns with the fact that metallic/iron taste is a known side effect of transcranial electrical stimulation 33"
