Multiple regression comparing yearly change in EL-Non-EL readiness gaps between the 2018-2019 prior-year cohort and the 2019-2020 intervention cohort, shown in Figure 5; the quote describes the direction of the estimated EL versus Non-EL growth differences.

"each of the bars for the prior year (in blue) take negative values, indicating that the gap between ELs and Non-ELs widened during the year. In contrast, each of the bars for the intervention year (in red) take positive values"

McCarthy 2020 (2)¶

McCarthy, E., Morgan, C. & Sussman, J. (2020). Growing strong learners in Galt Joint Union Elementary School District. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613373

q2 · i? · design · r2

Significance tests contrasting prior-year and intervention-year regression coefficients in the same cohort-comparison model. For LLD and MATH the article reports the estimated differences were small and not statistically significant.

"The brackets show that the between-year differences were statistically significant for the ATL-REG, SED, and PD domains of the DRDP. For these three domains, the statistical evidence indicates that the intervention year supported more equitable outcomes for ELs than the prior year."
