Evidence¶

Goetz 2006¶

Goetz, T., Frenzel, A. C., Pekrun, R., Hall, N. C., & Lüdtke, O. (2006). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel analysis of emotion homogeneity within each of four domains in both grade-level samples. "the strongest within-domain relations were found for mathematics" and German was by far the weakest; all h(e) differences were significant (p < .05) except physics–English in Grade 11.

"In both samples, the strongest within-domain relations were found for mathematics, followed by physics, English, and German. Within-domain relations in German were by far the weakest."
