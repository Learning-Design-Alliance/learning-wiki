# Mastery goals are negatively associated with academic dishonesty across studies

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Mastery goals (without valence specification) show a statistically significant average negative association with academic dishonesty (r = -0.234). [→ Tanja Fritz 2023](#tanja-fritz-2023)

## Evidence

### Tanja Fritz 2023

Tanja Fritz, Hernán González Cruz, Stefan Janke, Martin Daumiller. (2023). Elucidating the Associations Between Achievement Goals and Academic Dishonesty: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09753-1

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level random-effects meta-analysis of 163 effect sizes from 33 studies (N = 19,787). The pooled correlation for unvalenced mastery goals was r = -0.234, 95% CI [-0.317, -0.147], confirming the expected negative association.

> "For mastery goals without specification of their valence, statistically significant average negative associations were obtained based on 12 effect sizes (all negative correlations) from 7 studies ( r = –0.234, 95% CI [–0.317, –0.147])"
