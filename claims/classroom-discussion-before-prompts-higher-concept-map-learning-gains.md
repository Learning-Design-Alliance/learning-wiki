# Teacher-guided classroom discussion before reflection prompts yields higher learning gains than all other support conditions, especially on application-level items

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in the example + discussion + prompts condition showed significantly higher learning gains on application-level items than all other conditions, and higher conceptual-level gains than the control and example conditions. [→ Elise H. Eshuis 2022](#elise-h-eshuis-2022)
`q3 i?` The mixed MANOVA showed a significant Time*Condition interaction for both conceptual and application level items. [→ Elise H. Eshuis 2022 (2)](#elise-h-eshuis-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Elise H. Eshuis 2022

Elise H. Eshuis, Judith ter Vrugte, Ton de Jong. (2022). Supporting reflection to improve learning from self-generated concept maps. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09299-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 144 secondary vocational students (final sample) in four cumulative conditions; mixed MANOVA with Bonferroni-controlled post-hoc comparisons on learning gains. The discussion condition beat "all other conditions" on application level (control p = 0.026; example p = 0.046; example + prompts p = 0.014).

> "Post-hoc comparisons (Bonferroni controlled) on these learning gains (i.e., pretest score to posttest score) showed that the found multivariate effect of Condition could be explained from significantly higher learning gains on the application level in the example + discussion + prompts condition compared to all other conditions"

### Elise H. Eshuis 2022 (2)

Elise H. Eshuis, Judith ter Vrugte, Ton de Jong. (2022). Supporting reflection to improve learning from self-generated concept maps. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09299-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed MANOVA on pre- and posttest scores (conceptual and application level as dependent variables) found a significant multivariate Time*Condition interaction, F(6, 278) = 2.56, p = 0.020, ηp2 = 0.052, indicating condition differences in learning gains.

> "a significant interaction effect of Time*Condition, F(6, 278) = 2.56, p = 0.020; Wilk's Λ = 0.898, ηp2 = 0.052"
