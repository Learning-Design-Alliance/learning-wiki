## Evidence

### Biwer Felicitas 2022

Biwer Felicitas, de Bruin Anique, Persky Adam. (2022). Study smart – impact of a learning strategy training on students’ study behavior and academic performance. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9397154/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three-way mixed ANOVA comparing the 2020 Study Smart cohort (n = 110) with the 2019 control cohort (n = 158) on exam 1, exam 2, and final exam. The trained cohort rose "from 79% in the first exam to 94% in the final exam"; the control stayed around 85%. Exams overlapped about 85% across years.

> "There was a significant interaction effect between exam and cohort, F (2, 556) = 137.80, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.31, showing that exam performance over time differed between the two cohorts."
