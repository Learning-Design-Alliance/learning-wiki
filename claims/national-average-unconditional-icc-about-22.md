## Evidence

### Hedges 2007

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mixed linear model (Stata XTMIXED, restricted maximum likelihood) analysis of national probability longitudinal survey samples for mathematics, Grades K-12, yielded per-grade unconditional ICCs; the article reports that "The average unconditional intraclass correlation estimate across all grades is .220."

> "Table 2 is a presentation of results from the entire national sample in mathematics. The average unconditional intraclass correlation estimate across all grades is .220."

### Hedges 2007 (2)

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The parallel mixed-model analysis of the national samples for reading found the values "generally quite similar to those in mathemat- ics", with a mean unconditional ICC of .224 across grade levels.

> "The intraclass correlation and adjusted intraclass correlation values in reading are generally quite similar to those in mathemat- ics. The mean (across grade levels) unconditional intraclass correlation in reading was .224."
