## Evidence

### Ginzburg Denise M. 2012

Ginzburg Denise M., Bohn Christiane, Höfling Volkmar, Weck Florian, Clark David M., Stangier Ulrich. (2012). Treatment specific competence predicts outcome in cognitive therapy for social anxiety disorder. Behaviour Research and Therapy. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2012.09.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling analysis of 34 CT patients nested within 10 therapists, drawn from a multicenter randomized controlled trial. Competence ratings from videotaped sessions predicted the primary outcome; the model "accounted for 48% of the variance" and competence was the only significant predictor (β = .79).

> "The HLM model accounted for 48% of the variance in the primary outcome variable (CGI-I). Only competence was a significant predictor of CGI-I (β = .79, p = <.001)."
