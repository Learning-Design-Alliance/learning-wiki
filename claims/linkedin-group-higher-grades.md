## Evidence

### Hazzam 2024

Hazzam, J., Wilkins, S., Southall, C., & Ibrahim, B. (2024). The influence of LinkedIn group community on postgraduate student experience, satisfaction and grades. Computers & Education, 216, 105052. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105052

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent sample t-test on grades among 40 experimental and 42 control students. The "experiment group (M = 60.423, SD = 13.555)" scored significantly higher than controls. ANCOVA confirmed the difference remained significant (F = 13.111, p < 0.001, partial eta squared = 0.127).
