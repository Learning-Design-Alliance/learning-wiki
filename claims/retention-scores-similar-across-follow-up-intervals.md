### Rolskov Bojsen S 2015

Rolskov Bojsen S, Räder SBEW, Holst AG, Kayser L, Ringsted C, Hastrup Svendsen J, Konge L. (2015). The acquisition and retention of ECG interpretation skills after a standardized web-based ECG tutorial–a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0319-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intergroup comparison by ANOVA of retention-test scores among the short (N=63), medium (N=74), and long (N=66) follow-up groups. The article reports no statistically significant difference (p = 0.328); equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "However, there were no statistically significant differences in mean retention-test score between the three groups (p = 0.328; Figure 2)."
