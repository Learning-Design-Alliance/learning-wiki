Liang, S., Yau, K. W., Meng, H., Chiu, T. K. F., King, I., Yam, Y., & Chai, C. S. (2026). From disengaged to self-determined: A latent transition analysis of students' AI learning motivation. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-13998-y

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Latent transition analysis of 2,086 students across one academic year estimated T1-to-T2 transition probabilities (Table 7). The quote reports the "substantial upward trend" out of the Disengaged profile; the article also reports 64% stability for Developing, 80% for Self-Determined, and only 2% downward from Self-Determined to Disengaged.

"Students in the Disengaged group showed substantial upward trend, with 43% transitioning to the Developing profile and 21% progressing directly to the Self-Determined profile, while 36% remained in the same profile."
