Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/california-covid-19-teacher-workforce [doi:10.54300/987.779](https://doi.org/10.54300/987.779)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview findings from California district leaders in fall 2020. Leaders estimated "teacher workloads have at least doubled" and expressed concern about burnout and turnover; one rural superintendent reported workload had tripled.

> "With district leaders estimating that teacher workloads have at least doubled, many were concerned that the stressors of managing the challenges of the pandemic on top of the challenges of an increased workload could lead to teacher burnout and increased turnover rates."
