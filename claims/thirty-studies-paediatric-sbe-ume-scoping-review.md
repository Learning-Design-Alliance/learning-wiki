Systematic search of PubMed, Scopus, Embase and Cochrane (January 2010–July 2025) following PRISMA-ScR guidelines. The review reports that "Thirty studies met the inclusion criteria" and that randomized controlled trials and quasi-experimental designs predominated, with sample sizes ranging from n = 17 to n = 806.

> "Thirty studies met the inclusion criteria. The United States (n = 10), France (n = 3), and Brazil (n = 3) were the most represented countries. Randomized controlled trials (n = 11) and quasi-experimental designs (n = 10) predominated."
