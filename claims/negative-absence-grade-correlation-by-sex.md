# Correlation coefficients between absence rate and final grade were negative for both sexes, with a stronger relationship among male students

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Male students showed strong negative correlations between absence rate and final grade (r = -0.5945 first semester; r = -0.5555 second semester). [→ A. R. Hakami 2021](#a-r-hakami-2021)
`q2 i1` Female students showed weak negative correlations between absence rate and final grade (r = -0.2269 and r = -0.2328). [→ A. R. Hakami 2021](#a-r-hakami-2021)

## Evidence

### A. R. Hakami 2021

A. R. Hakami. (2021). Effect of absenteeism on the performance of medical sciences students: gender differences. Medical Education Online, 26(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1875531

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of absence rate against final grade for 115 third-year Clinical Virology students across two semesters; the article reports "an inverse relationship between the absence rate and the final grade", with male r = -0.5945/-0.5555 and female r = -0.2269/-0.2328. Some outliers did not conform to this pattern.

> "The correlation coefficients were negative (male: r = −0.5945 for the first semester/r = −0.5555 for the second semester; female: r = −0.2269/-0.2328). This indicates an inverse relationship between the absence rate and the final grade."
