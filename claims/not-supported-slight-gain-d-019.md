# Unsupported low-achieving students showed only a slight achievement increase (d = 0.19) under traditional instruction

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` The not-supported control group's pretest-posttest gain was statistically significant but negligible in magnitude, with a printed effect size of d = 0.19. [→ Sarı et al. 2017](#sar-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Sarı et al. 2017

İrfan Sarı, Nadir Çeliköz, Süleyman Ünal. (2017). The Effect of Peer Support on University Level Students’ English Language Achievements. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131776

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Paired T-test for the unsuccessful control group: pretest M = 41.41, posttest M = 43.02, t = -2.76, df = 199, p = .006, n = 200. The article prints d = 0.19 and characterizes it as a "slight increase" per Green and Salkind (2005).

> "the d of the supported group is 0,59 which i s at the acceptable level but 0,19 (slight increase) for the not supported group (Green and Salkind, 2005)."
