### S. J. Cooper 2021

S. J. Cooper, A. C. Green, H. R. Middleton, C. P. L. Berry, R. Buscicchio, E. Butler, C. J. Collins, C. Gettings, D. Hoyland, A. W. Jones, J. H. Lindon, I. Romero-Shaw, S. P. Stevenson, E. P. [doi:10.1119/10.0003534](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0003534)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of 171 visitors to the RSSE exhibit using electronic and paper questionnaires with Likert-scale items. The authors report that "54% replied Highly or Very highly interested before the visit, rising 83% after" for interest in physics. Appendix C adds that 80% reported a large increase in awareness of gravitational waves and 66% in knowledge of LIGO.

> "Feedback from 171 exhibit visitors was collected via surveys, as detailed in Appendix C. The results indicate that we had a positive impact on those who attended: of participants who rated their interest in physics, 54% replied Highly or Very highly interested before the visit, rising 83% after."
