## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with a contextual learning model scored significantly higher on argumentative writing than students taught with a non-contextual model. [→ Hasani 2016](#hasani-2016)

## Evidence

### Hasani 2016

Hasani, A. (2016). Enhancing argumentative writing skill through contextual teaching and learning. Educational Research and Reviews, 11(16), 1573-1578. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1113515

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 52 second-year university students in Banten, Indonesia (26 experimental, 26 control), post-test design. The Tukey post-hoc test found "a significant difference between experiment and control groups"; descriptive means were 74.46 (contextual) vs 72.50 (non-contextual).

> "Further, according to statistical test (Tukey Post -Hoc), there is a significant difference between experiment and control groups"
