## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Experiment 1, the recognition posttest showed no interaction effect with sorting, so contrasting did not affect recognition accuracy. [→ Baxter 2022](#baxter-2022)

## Evidence

### Baxter 2022

Baxter, P.A.; Bekkering, H.; Dijkstra, A.F.J.; Droop, W.; Hurk, M.M. van den; Léoné, F.T.M. (2022). Contrasting orthographically similar words facilitates adult second language vocabulary learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101582

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 recognition posttest mixed-effects regression found main effects of time and similarity but "no interaction effect with sorting"; recognition was above chance immediately (Mprop_cor = 0.84) and a week later (0.73).

> "Regarding the variables of interest for our hypotheses, there was no interaction effect with sorting."
