# Schools implementing restorative justice report decreases in exclusionary discipline such as suspensions and expulsions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Empirical studies reviewed report decreases in exclusionary discipline and harmful behavior after RJ implementation, with large percentage drops in suspensions reported in several sites. [→ Fronius 2016](#fronius-2016)

## Evidence

### Fronius 2016

Fronius, Persson, Guckenburg, Hurley, & Petrosino. (2016). Restorative Justice in U.S. Schools: A Research Review. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED596786

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of empirical studies of RJ in U.S. schools. The review reports that all reviewed studies found decreases in exclusionary discipline, citing Armour (2013): an "84-percent drop in out-of-school suspensions" and a 19-percent drop in all suspensions in one Texas school's first RJ year.

> "For example, Armour (2013) reports an 84-percent drop in out-of-school suspensions among sixth graders in one Texas school during the first year RJ was introduced, and a 19-percent drop in all suspensions."
