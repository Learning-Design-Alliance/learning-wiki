Lin John C, Shin Christopher, Greenberg Paul B. (2024). The impact of the medical school admissions interview: a systematic review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.76138

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of eight nonrandomized comparative observational studies comparing students admitted with and without interviews. The review reports "few differences between students admitted with and without unstructured and semi-structured interviews" across demographics, USMLE scores and PGY-1 ratings; no effect sizes were printed.

> "There were few differences between students admitted with and without unstructured and semi-structured interviews. They had similar gender and racial/ethnic demographics,21 academic and clinical performance in medical school,21 United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) Steps 1 and 2 scores,21 and residency program director ratings in post-graduate year 1 (PGY-1).21"
