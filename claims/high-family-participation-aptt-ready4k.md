# Family participation in Growing Strong Learners was high and similar for EL and non-EL families: 97% attended at least one APTT meeting and 92% participated in Ready4K

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 97% of families attended at least one APTT meeting, 72% attended at least two, 92% participated in Ready4K, and 81% remained active throughout the year, with similar levels for EL and non-EL families. [→ McCarthy 2020](#mccarthy-2020)

## Evidence

### McCarthy 2020

McCarthy, E., Morgan, C. & Sussman, J. (2020). Growing strong learners in Galt Joint Union Elementary School District. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613373

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Participation analysis of attendance records and Ready4K analytics (166 active enrollments, 15,349 message deliveries) during 2019-2020. The article reports Ready4K participation of 92% with 81% remaining active, similar for EL and non-EL families.

> "Nearly all Fairsite Preschool families attended at least one APTT parent meeting or APTT individual meeting with their teacher or an administrator during the year (97%), and the majority of families attended at least two APTT events (72%)."
