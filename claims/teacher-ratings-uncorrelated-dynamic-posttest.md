## Evidence

### Kirsten W. J. Touw 2019

Kirsten W. J. Touw, Bart Vogelaar, Merel Bakker, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2019). Using electronic technology in the dynamic testing of young primary school children: predicting school achievement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09655-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Spearman correlations of teacher ratings (overall school performance and learning potential) with test measures for the training group (N = 69) and control group (N = 71). Ratings correlated strongly with Cito scores (up to r = 0.781) but were non-significant with the trained group's dynamic post-test.

> "Non-significant correlations were found between teacher ratings and the post-test of the trained group (dynamic measures) (r = 0.142 and r = 0.192)"
