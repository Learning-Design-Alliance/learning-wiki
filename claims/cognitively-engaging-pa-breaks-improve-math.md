# Both cognitively engaging PA break programs (combo and cognition) improve children's mathematics performance more than an aerobic PA break program

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Mathematics improved significantly more in the combo and cognition groups than in the aerobic group (ηp2 = .096), with no difference between the two cognitively engaging groups. [→ Egger Fabienne 2019](#egger-fabienne-2019)

## Evidence

### Egger Fabienne 2019

Egger Fabienne, Benzing Valentin, Conzelmann Achim, Schmidt Mirko. (2019). Boost your brain, while having a break! The effects of long-term cognitively engaging physical activity breaks on children's executive functions and academic achievement. PLoS ONE 14(3): e0212482. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0212482

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on standardized mathematics scores (Heidelberger Rechentest) pre-to-post across the three conditions; the article reports "only mathematics differed significantly between the three groups (F(2, 138) = 7.34, p = .001, ηp2 = .096)" favoring both cognitively engaging groups over aerobic.

> "only mathematics differed significantly between the three groups (F(2, 138) = 7.34, p = .001, ηp2 = .096), with post-hoc tests revealing both the combo (p = .001) and the cognition group (p = .002) to displaying a significantly better mathematic performance when compared against the aerobic group"
