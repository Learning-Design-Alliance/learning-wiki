## Subclaims
`q3 i?` By-participant aggregated comparisons showed reliable RT decreases for all seven coherence levels, whereas accuracy increases were statistically reliable only for intermediate coherence levels. [→ Cochrane 2023](#cochrane-2023)

## Evidence

### Cochrane 2023

Cochrane, A., Sims, C. R., Bejjanki, V. R., Green, C. S., & Bavelier, D. (2023). Multiple timescales of learning indicated by changes in evidence-accumulation processes during perceptual decision-making. npj Science of Learning 8:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00168-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Preliminary behavioral check on the four-day dot-motion data (21 participants, coherence levels 0.8% to 51.2%): RT reliably decreased at all coherence levels; accuracy increased numerically everywhere but reached statistical reliability only at intermediate coherences. No effect sizes are printed.

> "by- participant aggregated comparisons indicated that RT reliably decreased for all coherence levels. Accuracy meanwhile increased numerically for all coherence levels, however, this was only statistically reliable for intermediate coherence levels"
