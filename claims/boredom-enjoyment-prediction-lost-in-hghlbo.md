## Evidence

### Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu 2026

Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu. (2026). Testing the Relative Universalism of Psychological Theories: A Person-Centered Analysis of Control-Value Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10155-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixture regression models predicting three motivational outcomes (Persistence, Uncertain Control, Failure Avoidance) from CVT components. The article reports full-sample explained variance of 12.5% (Failure Avoidance) to 20% (Persistence) and 26% (Uncertain Control), with latent-profile models roughly halving explained variance.

> "For example, in the HghLBO profile, boredom and enjoyment—key predictors of persistence in the full-sample and multigroup analyses—lost nearly all predictive power."
