Sukitkanaporn 2014¶

Sukitkanaporn, T., & Phoocharoensil, S. (2014). English Presentation Skills of Thai Graduate Students. English Language Teaching, 7(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n3p91

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cross-sectional survey of 26 conference attendees rating presenters on a five-point Likert scale. The article reports significant differences favoring CR 752 students on eye contact, body language, confidence, and preparedness, with p-values as printed.

"both groups performed significantly different in terms of consistently making eye contact with the audience ( p = 0.0189), using body language to clarify a point ( p = 0.0045), appearing confident while making a presentation (p = 0.0006) and appearing well-prepared (p = 0.0008)."
