Exploratory Spearman bivariate analysis of survey responses from 141 first-year medical students. The printed correlations are r = 0.19 (p = 0.02) and r- 0.294 (p < 0.001); the article reports "a positive correlation between being female and higher Likert responses" on these two items.

> "There was a positive correlation between being female and higher Likert responses to ‘The AT has helped me think critically about my study skills’ (r = 0.19, p = 0.02); and ‘The AT has helped me to think about how I intend to achieve my goals’(r- 0.294, p < 0.001)."
