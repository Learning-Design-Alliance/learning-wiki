Descriptive comparison of standardised coefficients from linear mixed models of each participant's judgement policy (probability of death regressed on the seven vignette cues) at t = 1 and t = 2, against the previous study's experts. "adjusted how they weighted the information to become more similar to the experts"; the overemphasis reading is the authors' interpretation.

> "Students in the intervention arm adjusted how they weighted the information to become more similar to the experts at (t = 2); focusing on the same four factors that the experts did. The results suggest that the students who received the training overemphasised the weighting of the factors presented."
