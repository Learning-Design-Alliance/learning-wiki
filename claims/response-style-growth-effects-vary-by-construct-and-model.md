# The effect of response styles on growth estimates varies by psychological construct, response style, and IRT model used

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Effects of response styles on growth estimates vary by psychological construct, response style, and the IRT model used for scoring. [→ James Soland 2020](#james-soland-2020)

`q2 i?` Response styles can affect estimates of growth parameters including the slope, and the effects vary by psychological construct, response style, and model used. [→ James Soland 2020](#james-soland-2020)

`q2 i?` Response styles can affect estimates of growth parameters including the slope when longitudinal survey scores are used in growth models. [→ James Soland 2020](#james-soland-2020)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Comparative analysis across constructs, response styles, and IRT scoring models in the empirical and simulation analyses. The article reports the effects "vary by psychological construct, response style, and IRT model used," so no single direction or magnitude holds universally.
