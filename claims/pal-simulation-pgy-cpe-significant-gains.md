## Evidence

### Chiu Po-Wei 2025

Chiu Po-Wei, Chu Shao-Chung, Yang Chia-Han, Lee Huan-Fang, Hung Hsuan-Man, Hsu Hsiang-Chin. (2025). Peer-assisted learning in critical care: a simulation-based approach for postgraduate medical training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2497333

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Follow-up PGY-CPE questionnaire completed by 18 of 29 trainees three months after the course; pre/post comparisons analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test. All four domains improved with "p-values less than 0.001". Scores are self-reported; no effect size is printed.

> "Confidence levels rose markedly, with scores increasing from a pre-course mean of 1.38 (SD = 0.78) to 4.72 (SD = 0.75). Reasoning ability in clinical situations showed a substantial increase from 1.67 (SD = 0.76) to 4.39 (SD = 0.67)."
