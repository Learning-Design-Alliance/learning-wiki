### Kates Meghan 2026

Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70043

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course Likert survey of assignment contribution to learning (n = 106 students, 3 cohorts). For the TED-style Talk assignment, "39.6% of students agreed" and "28.3% of students strongly agreed" it contributed to their learning experience.

> "Specifically, for the TED-style Talk assignment, 39.6% of students agreed and 28.3% of students strongly agreed that this assignment contributed to their learning experience (Figure 3A)."
