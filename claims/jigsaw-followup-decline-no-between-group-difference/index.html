Evidence¶

Er 2017¶

Harun Er. (2017). The Impact of Teaching the Subjects under "Science in Time" Unit in the Social Studies Class in the 7th Grade Using Jigsaw Technique on the Academic Success of the Students. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(5): 838-847. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050516

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent t test comparing final-minus-follow-up score differences between the jigsaw and control groups (n=23 each) found "no significant difference between the groups"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"The results of the t-test conducted to see whether the post-test and follow -up test results of the experimental and control groups was significant revealed that there was no significant difference between the groups (t=1.502, p(.000)>.05)."

Er 2017 (2)¶

Harun Er. (2017). The Impact of Teaching the Subjects under "Science in Time" Unit in the Social Studies Class in the 7th Grade Using Jigsaw Technique on the Academic Success of the Students. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(5): 838-847. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050516

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent t test within the jigsaw group (n=23) one month after instruction; the control group also declined significantly (t=3.581, p (.002) <5). No effect size is printed.

"As a result of the dependent t test a meaningful difference between the experiment group final test and follow -up test results was determined (t= 10.681, p(.000)<.05)."
