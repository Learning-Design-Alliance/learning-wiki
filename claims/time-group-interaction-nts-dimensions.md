# The Time-by-Group interaction on AS-NTS performance is significant for teamwork/leadership and team orientation but not for planning/problem-solving

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The factorial ANOVA interaction effect (Time*Group) was significant on AS-NTS dimensions two and three but not on dimension one. [→ Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021](#moll-khosrawi-parisa-2021)

## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021

Moll-Khosrawi Parisa, Zöllner Christian, Cencin Nadine, Schulte-Uentrop Leonie. (2021). Flipped learning enhances non-technical skill performance in simulation-based education: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02766-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Factorial two-way ANOVA on AS-NTS scores of 102 analysed students (intervention n = 35, control n = 45). Table 4 prints interaction effects for dimension two (F = 4.89, p = .030, partial η² = 0.06) and dimension three (F = 6.39, p = .014, partial η² = 0.08); dimension one's interaction was not significant (F = 3.34, p = .072).

> "There was no significant interaction effect and no significant main effects of “Group” for dimension one. Only the mean effect of “Time” was significant, indicating, that there were changes over time across the whole sample, but with no difference between the study group."
