`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors' assessment of applying the framework to the think-aloud laboratory interviews: it "captures and diﬀerentiates students’ model-based reasoning". This is the authors' interpretive claim about the framework's utility, not a tested comparison.

> "When applied to the think- aloud interviews, the framework captures and diﬀerentiates students’ model-based reasoning and helps identify areas of future research."

### Benjamin M. Zwickl 2014 (2)

Benjamin M. Zwickl, Dehui Hu, Noah Finkelstein, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2014). Model-Based Reasoning in the Upper-Division Physics Laboratory: Framework and Initial Results. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1410.0881](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1410.0881)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding reliability for the three interviews analyzed in detail: two authors independently coded the transcripts and, after refining criteria, reached "an agreement level exceeding 95% for the high-level modeling codes".

> "As the coding criteria were reﬁned, the two coders were able to come to an agreement level exceeding 95% for the high-level modeling codes presented."
