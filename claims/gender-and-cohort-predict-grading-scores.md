### Lopera-Oquendo 2024

Lopera-Oquendo, C., Lipnevich, A. A., & Mañez, I. (2024). Rating writing: Comparison of holistic and analytic grading approaches in pre-service teachers. Learning and Instruction, 94, 101992. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101992

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the final HLM (N = 229, 916 observations), the male gender coefficient was negative and significant (b = -0.26, p = .050) with a significant gender-by-cohort interaction (b = 0.10, p = .006).

> "Gender (Male = 1, Female = 0) was a statistically significant and negative predictor of grades (b = -0.26, p = .050). However, the main effect of gender on grades depended on the cohort (b = 0.10, p = .006)."
