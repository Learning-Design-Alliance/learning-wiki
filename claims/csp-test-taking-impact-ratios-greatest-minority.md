## Evidence

### Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown 2019

Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown. (2019). Estimating the Effect of a Teacher Training Program on Advanced Placement® Outcomes. International Journal of Computer Science Education in Schools, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207479

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ATT impact-ratio analysis (Figure 6) for Computer Science Principles test participation by student group in the matched school sample. The article reports the program increased the number of Black students taking Computer Science Principles more than ten-fold on average, and Hispanic students nearly six-fold; impact ratios are not standardized effect sizes.

> "Whereas the program effect, in essence, increases test participation for all students by a factor of more than 5, the effect is almost twice that for Black students (10.13)."
