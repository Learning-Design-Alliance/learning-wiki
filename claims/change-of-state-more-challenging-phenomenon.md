## Evidence

### Mason 2016

Lucia Mason & Maria Caterina Tornatora (2016). Analogical encoding with and without instructions for case comparison of scientific phenomena. Educational Psychology, 36(2), 391-412. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2014.953038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the change-of-state results: the condition × phenomenon comparison showed the harder phenomenon produced fewer condition effects, consistent with a previous study with 8th graders using lower similarity scenarios (Mason, 2004).

> "Even in the situation that is potentially most conducive to analogical encoding, the causal relevance of the identiﬁed differences, the explanation of the required phenomenon, the rating of similarity, and the justiﬁcation for this rating were substantially the same as in the other conditions."
