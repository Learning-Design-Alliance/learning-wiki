de Laat 2007¶

de Laat, M., Lally, V., Lipponen, L. & Simons, R.-J. (2007). Online teaching in networked learning communities: A multi-method approach to studying the role of the teacher. Instructional Science, vol. 35, no. 3, pp. 257-286. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-006-9007-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Critical event recall interview with the beginner teacher of NLC2, stimulated by content analysis summaries. She reported feeling overwhelmed by the complexity of postings and at times not responding because she felt she lacked the skills and know-how.

"One of the students was very dominant and had a very strong online presence, and had a very combating and critical tone in his messages towards me and gave me, at one stage, a major crisis."
