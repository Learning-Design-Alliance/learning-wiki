Haataja 2021¶

Haataja, E., Malmberg, J., Dindar, M., & Järvelä, S. (2021). The pivotal role of monitoring for collaborative problem solving seen in interaction, performance, and interpersonal physiology. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 241–268. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09279-3

q3 · i? · associational · r1

GEE model predicting neutral valence monitoring duration from physiological measures in 19 groups. The tonic EDA slope was a "significant negative predictor, meaning that an increase in arousal was related to less monitoring interactions with neutral valence."

"the model predicting monitoring with neutral valence included the tonic EDA slope as a significant negative predictor, meaning that an increase in arousal was related to less monitoring interactions with neutral valence"
