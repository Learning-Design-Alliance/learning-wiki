### Melcarne R 2026

Melcarne R, Cappellacci F, Carannante F, Carbone L, Ferracci F, Fracon S, Manigrasso M, Milone E, Muttillo EM, Piccioni S, Sofia S, Turri G, Deraco M, Macrì A, Roviello F, Picciariello A, COSTRUIRE-Collaborative Group. Updates in Surgery 78(4):1811-1824. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13304-026-02557-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey (COSTRUIRE) of 189 Italian surgical residents and early-career surgeons, distributed via the Italian Society of Surgical Oncology newsletter. The article reports that "only 7.9% reported its actual availability within their residency programs" while 91.0% rated it as a crucial educational expectation. No inferential statistics were performed.

> "While 91.0% of participants rated communication training as a crucial educational expectation for General Surgery residents (scores 4 or 5 on a 5-point Likert scale; Question 22), only 7.9% reported its actual availability within their residency programs (Question 21)."
