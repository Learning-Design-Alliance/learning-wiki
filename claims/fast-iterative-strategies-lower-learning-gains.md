Research Question 1 analysis of Study 2 gameplay logs examined whether dichotomous fast/slow solution modes could be detected and how they related to learning gains. The article reports that "students who use mainly fast iterative solution strategies achieved lower learning gains" than slow or balanced players, supporting the 2SM prediction.

> "Congruent with the 2SM, students who use mainly fast iterative solution strategies achieved lower learning gains than students who preferred slow, elaborate solutions, or a more balanced mix of the two."
