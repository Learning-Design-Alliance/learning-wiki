Evidence¶

Berthold 2009¶

Berthold, K., Eysink, T.H.S. & Renkl, A. (2009). Assisting self-explanation prompts are more effective than open prompts when learning with multiple representations. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9051-z

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The authors' concluding interpretation of the three-condition experiment (open prompts, assisting prompts, no prompts) names the pattern of findings: "the elicitation of high-quality self-explanations and the acquisition of deep understanding". This is the authors' summary label for the reported results.

"We call this the assisting self-explanation prompt effect which refers to the elicitation of high-quality self-explanations and the acquisition of deep understanding."

Related Claims¶

  • Prompting learners to self-explain improves understanding and problem solving on immediate tests by a moderate average amount, with little evidence yet on delayed or classroom gains. — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Students who self-explain worked examples learn more effectively than those who do not, and the quality of self-explanations relates to explaining deep structure — related
  • Explanation prompts direct attention to targeted information and can reduce attention to other important information — related
  • Self-explanation prompting is potentially effective across a range of instructional conditions — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Self-explanation beats unguided studying for equal time, but instructional explanations, problem solving and retrieval practice can be equally or more effective — related
  • Both open and assisting self-explanation prompts foster procedural knowledge when learning with multiple representations, an effect mediated by self-explanations directed to domain principles — related
  • Assisting self-explanation prompts particularly foster conceptual knowledge, an effect mediated by self-explanations on the rationale of a principle — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Instructor-scripted self-explanation prompts have limitations, motivating computer-generated prompts — related
