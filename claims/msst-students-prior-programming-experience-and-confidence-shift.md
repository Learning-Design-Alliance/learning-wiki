### Jeffrey 2022

Teaching computational thinking to space science students. 4th Symposium on Space Educational Activities, Barcelona. [doi:10.5821/conference-9788419184405.121](https://doi.org/10.5821/conference-9788419184405.121)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymised alumni questionnaire with mostly open-ended written responses, analysed by affinity mapping. Respondents reported prior experience across languages, but "few students claim to have been confident programmers before joining the course".

> "All respondents reported some prior programming experience, across a range of languages, but few students claim to have been confident programmers before joining the course (Fig. 1)."

### Jeffrey 2022 (2)

Jeffrey, R., Lundy, M., Coffey, D., McBreen, S., Martin-Carrillo, A., & Hanlon, L. (2022). Teaching computational thinking to space science students. 4th Symposium on Space Educational Activities, Barcelona. [doi:10.5821/conference-9788419184405.121](https://doi.org/10.5821/conference-9788419184405.121)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same alumni questionnaire. Although "most students (16 out of 29) had taken dedicated programming modules", written responses emphasised learning by writing code in labs, with formal classes experienced as disconnected from the rest of their learning.

> "Although most students (16 out of 29) had taken dedicated programming modules, this is not reflected in their written responses, which emphasise learning by writing code in labs."

### Jeffrey 2022 (3)

Teaching computational thinking to space science students. 4th Symposium on Space Educational Activities, Barcelona. [doi:10.5821/conference-9788419184405.121](https://doi.org/10.5821/conference-9788419184405.121)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Alumni questionnaire self-assessed confidence ratings before and after the MSc. "All respondents said that their confidence in their abilities increased after the course".

> "All respondents said that their confidence in their abilities increased after the course (Fig. 1)."
