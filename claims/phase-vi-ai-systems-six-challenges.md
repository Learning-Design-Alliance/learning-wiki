### Tripathi 2026

The chapter identifies six challenges for Phase VI systems, citing prior work. Neural models produce predictions without showing their reasoning; LLMs sometimes generate factually incorrect content without built-in flagging; prompt sensitivity creates reproducibility challenges; computational cost and economic viability at scale have not been demonstrated; traditional software testing is insufficient for dynamically generating systems; and rigorous controlled studies measuring skill transfer over time remain scarce.

> "most Phase VI systems remain research prototypes; rigorous controlled studies measuring skill transfer over time remain scarce [94]."
