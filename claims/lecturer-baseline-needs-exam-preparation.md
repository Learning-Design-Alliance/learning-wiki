## Evidence

### Amir Yunus 2025

Amir Yunus, Gay Peng Rend, Lee Oon Teng (2025). From Co-Design to Metacognitive Laziness: Evaluating Generative AI in Vocational Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.12306](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.12306)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with twenty lecturers conducted before POC development, captured in written notes. "Lecturers consistently identified content volume as the primary challenge during exam preparation", alongside last-minute cramming and difficulty applying knowledge beyond memorisation.

> "Lecturers consistently identified content volume as the primary challenge during exam preparation, with students struggling to manage material across multiple documents, pages, and case studies."

### Amir Yunus 2025 (2)

Amir Yunus, Gay Peng Rend, Lee Oon Teng (2025). From Co-Design to Metacognitive Laziness: Evaluating Generative AI in Vocational Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.12306](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.12306)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre-implementation survey of the same twenty lecturers using 5-point Likert-scale items. Lecturers "acknowledged that there was insufficient time and resources for personalised feedback (Q6 M = 2.15, range 1-3)"; these are descriptive means with no effect size.

> "They acknowledged that there was insufficient time and resources for personalised feedback (Q6 M = 2.15, range 1-3), consistent with Johnson et al.'s (2022) identification of time and workload constraints as barriers in educator practice."
