## Evidence

### Mutluhan Yilmaz 2026

Mutluhan Yilmaz, Andrea Gauthier, Mutlu Cukurova. (2026). Supporting online learners' regulation skills with the help of learning analytics and generative artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10595-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the quasi-experiment's high cluster (26 students per condition), Mann–Whitney comparisons of the composite SRLs score found no significant difference post-intervention (GenAI mean rank 25.88 vs tutor 27.12, p = .770); equivalence was not tested.

> "Accordingly, no statistically significant differences were found in SRLs proxies at either the pre- or post-stage for high-cluster students."
