Log-derived per-exercise indicator analysis across the 21 obligatory exercises, collated in the Fig. 4 heatmap. The heatmap shows "outcome difficulty is uniformly low — even the hardest exercise leaves only 4% of valid triers unsolved".

> "Second, mean valid attempts is the clearest process-difficulty signal in this cohort: a small set of exercises (pyyhi,kuten-ccx,kumoaa-2, yhteenlasku-A) requires an order of magnitude more iterative effort than its neighbours, even though nearly all learners eventually succeed."
