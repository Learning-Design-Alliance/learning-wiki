## Evidence

### Amanda Vite 2024

Amanda Vite, Erika A. Patall, Man Chen. (2024). Relationships Between Experiences of Autonomy and Well(Ill)-Being for K-12 Youth: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2024) 36:127. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09967-x

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects multilevel meta-analysis (RVE) of correlations from 90 reports on K-12 youth. The review reports that "the overall average correlations between ANS and well-being and ANF and ill-being were statistically significantly positive"; Table 2 lists r = .37 for ANS-WB and r = .34 for ANF-IB.

> "As expected, the overall average correlations between ANS and well-being and ANF and ill-being were statistically significantly positive (see Table 3 for full results)."
