## Evidence

### Chun-Yi Lee 2015

Chun-Yi Lee. (2015). The Effects of Online Peer Assessment and Family Entrepreneurial Experience on Students' Business Planning Performance. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057346

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple main effects analysis (Table 7) within the ANCOVA for students with family entrepreneurial experience; the ordering peer > author > reviewer was significant (F=29.004, p=0.000).

> "among participants whose family had entrepreneurial experience, those in the peer group demonstrated greater learning gains than those in the author group, and those in the author group outperformed those in the reviewer group (F=29.004; p<0.01)."

### Chun-Yi Lee 2015 (2)

Chun-Yi Lee. (2015). The Effects of Online Peer Assessment and Family Entrepreneurial Experience on Students' Business Planning Performance. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057346

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple main effects analysis (Table 7) for students without family entrepreneurial experience: F=2.606, p=0.081, no significant group differences; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "Among students whose families had no entrepreneurial experience, no significant difference in learning performance was observed among the three groups."
