### Alessio Focardi 2026 (2)

Alessio Focardi, Franco Bagnoli, Andrea Guazzini, Giorgio Gronchi. (2026). Introducing the Physics of Complex Systems through Videogames. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.06230](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.06230)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same anonymous survey section, reporting enjoyment and difficulty for the fireflies synchronization game: liked by "almost 80% of the players" while difficult for more than half; only percentages are printed, no effect size.

> "The last game proposed, the one about fireflies synchronization, was liked quite a lot, much, or very much by almost 80% of the players, as shown in fig. 23-(a), although it was also difficult for more than half of the participants (see fig. 23-(b))."
