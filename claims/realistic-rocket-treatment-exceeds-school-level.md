# A fully realistic treatment of a rocket launch would exceed the difficulty level feasible for school teaching, so the unit uses idealized vertical ascent

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors state that a realistic analysis of a rocket launch surpasses what school teaching can handle, and that real trajectories are computed numerically, not analytically; the unit therefore idealizes the ascent as vertical against gravity. [→ Nielbock 2019](#nielbock-2019)
