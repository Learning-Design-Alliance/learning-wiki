### Katherine N. Quinn 2020

Katherine N. Quinn, Michelle M. Kelley, Kathryn L. McGill, Emily M. Smith, Zachary Whipps, and N.G. Holmes. (2020). Group roles in unstructured labs show inequitable gender divide. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cluster analysis examining the range of roles within individual groups by lab type. The article notes traditional-lab groups typically had more members, yet proportionally fewer inquiry-lab groups had members in identical clusters, supporting greater task division in inquiry labs.

> "In the traditional labs, 43% of groups had all members in the same cluster (predominantly the paper cluster) whereas only 14% of groups in the inquiry labs had all members in the same cluster"
