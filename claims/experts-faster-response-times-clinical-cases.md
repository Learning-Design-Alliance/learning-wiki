Expertise Related Changes in Resting‐State Functional Connectivity Patterns Following a Clinical Reasoning and Decision‐Making Task. Brain and Behavior 16, no. 1: e71153. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.71153

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA of mean response time on the 16-scenario clinical reasoning task showed "the expert group (5484 ms ± 1517 ms)" responded faster than novices (8229 ms ± 2608 ms), F (1, 36) = 17.93, p < 0.0002. No effect size is printed.
