Theoretical argument, not a tested result: established personal respect and trust allows genuine public conversation about tacit core principles, so that newly clarified principles become ethically compelling and individuals act in collectively desirable fashion.

"In catalyzing such productive action, social trust constitutes a moral resource to the organization."
