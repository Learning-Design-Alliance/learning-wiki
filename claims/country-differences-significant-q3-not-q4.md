## Evidence

### Yossi Ben-Zion 2025

Yossi Ben-Zion, Jianqiang Liu, Chun-Ming Wang, Atanu Rajak, and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2025). Global Blind Spot in Understanding Trigonometric Derivatives: A Multinational Analysis. [doi:10.1103/t2fv-2spd](https://doi.org/10.1103/t2fv-2spd)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Matched-sample secondary analysis restricted to first- and second-year undergraduate physics students from Israel, the US, and China (no Indian students met inclusion criteria). Both χ2 tests were non-significant, indicating similar response patterns across the three countries.

> "For Q3, the proportion of students correctly identifying radians as the valid unit for differentiation remained low and exhibited minimal variation between countries ( χ2(4) = 5 .776, p= .216). Likewise, for Q4, which assessed students' recognition of radians in the context of harmonic motion, the distribution of responses was nearly identical across the three countries (χ2(4) = 5 .57, p= .632)"
