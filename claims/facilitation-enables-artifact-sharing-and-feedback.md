### Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting 2021

Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting. 2021. Together we learn better: leveraging communities of practice for MOOC learners. In Asian CHI Symposium 2021, May 8–13, 2021, Yokohama, Japan. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3429360.3468176

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation of field-study observations: in Design thinking and Creative problem solving communities members shared work and received feedback comments, and one member proposed doing an assignment as a group activity; programming communities stayed narrowly task-focused.

> "This suggests that provided the engagement and facilitation, community members are willing to share artifacts and feedback with confidence. Specifically, the Design thinking Community 1 reflected to phase as a group activity to do activities related to the course."
