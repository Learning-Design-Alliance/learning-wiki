Analytical worked example (Sections IV–V) applying the APA propagation equations to the author's laboratory course, using per-category uncertainty estimates from Table II. The article concludes each grade carries an uncertainty of "±0.5 points (or 2 .5%) with a conﬁdence level of 90%".

> "We thus conclude that the a priori estimate for the grade each student receives in this course is ±0.5 points (or 2 .5%) with a conﬁdence level of 90%."
