# In a 50-participant blind comparison, users strongly preferred VTutor over a SadTalker-generated talking head

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Eason Chen 2025

Eason Chen, Chenyu Lin, Yu-Kai Huang, Xinyi Tang, Aprille Xi, Jionghao Lin, Kenneth Koedinger. (2025). VTutor: An Animated Pedagogical Agent SDK that Provide Real Time Multi-Model Feedback. arXiv:2505.06676. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99267-4_19](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99267-4_19)

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Online Prolific experiment with 50 participants aged 20 to 50 (30 males, 20 females); videos were blind-labeled Agent A and Agent B with counterbalanced left/right ordering. The chi-square test on forced preference yielded χ2(1, N= 50) = 9 .68, p = .0019, and a printed effect size of Cramer's V of V = .44, which the authors call a "moderate to strong effect size".

> "36 preferred VTutor, while only 14 favored Sad Talker. The statistical analysis yielded a chi-square statistic of χ2(1, N= 50) = 9 .68, with a p-value of p = .0019 and Cramer's V of V = .44"
