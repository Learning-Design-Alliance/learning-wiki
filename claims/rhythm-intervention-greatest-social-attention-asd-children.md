`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled pilot study reported in the review: 36 children with ASD aged 5–12 years received 32 intervention sessions over 8 weeks comparing human trainer and robot trainer movement-based conditions. "Social attention was improved most in the rhythm group, followed in sequence by the robot group and comparison group."

> "Social attention was improved most in the rhythm group, followed in sequence by the robot group and comparison group. The authors reported that rhythmic and whole-body interpersonal synchrony games led to high levels of social attention compared to sedentary activities."
