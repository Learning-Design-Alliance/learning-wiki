## Evidence

### Fink Maximilian C 2021

Fink Maximilian C, Reitmeier Victoria, Stadler Matthias, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Assessment of Diagnostic Competences With Standardized Patients Versus Virtual Patients: Experimental Study in the Context of History Taking. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/21196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis across both assessment methods in the 86-student experiment found the predicted negative relationships; intrinsic load showed "1-tailed r=−0.30; P=.003" with diagnostic accuracy.

> "As assumed, intrinsic cognitive load (1-tailed r=−0.30; P=.003) and extraneous load (1-tailed r=−0.29; P=.003) correlated negatively with the combined score for diagnostic accuracy."

### Fink Maximilian C 2021 (2)

Fink Maximilian C, Reitmeier Victoria, Stadler Matthias, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Assessment of Diagnostic Competences With Standardized Patients Versus Virtual Patients: Experimental Study in the Context of History Taking. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/21196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same analysis: extraneous load showed "1-tailed r=−0.29; P=.003" with diagnostic accuracy. Explorative follow-ups found germane load (r=−0.25; P=.010) and total cognitive load (r=−0.31; P=.004) also correlated negatively.

> "As assumed, intrinsic cognitive load (1-tailed r=−0.30; P=.003) and extraneous load (1-tailed r=−0.29; P=.003) correlated negatively with the combined score for diagnostic accuracy."
