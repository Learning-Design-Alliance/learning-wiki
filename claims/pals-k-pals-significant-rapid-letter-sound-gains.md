### Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies 2012

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/364

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Randomized controlled trial (Stein et al., 2008) in 67 schools with 2,959 kindergarten students, reviewed by the WWC; the report confirms "a statistically significant positive difference between the K-PALS group and the comparison group" on Rapid Letter Sounds. Appendix C.1 shows a domain average effect size of 0.86 (+30 improvement index), p < 0.01 per comparison.

> "a statistically significant positive difference between the K-PALS group and the comparison group for each of the three K-PALS conditions (workshop, workshop plus booster, and workshop plus booster plus helper) on Rapid Letter Sounds for kindergarten students."
