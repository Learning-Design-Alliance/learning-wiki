## Evidence

### Williams 2017

Williams, E. A., Zwolak, J. P., Dou, R., & Brewe, E. (2017). Linking engagement and performance: The social network analysis perspective. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1706.04121](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.04121)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped multiple regression on the week-6 collection network (73-student MI course) showed outdegree became significant controlling for GPA, with R2adj = 0.296 versus 0.175 for GPA alone. The authors note "a student's self-reported social interactions matter, but not interactions reported by their peers," pointing to engagement beliefs.

> "the outdegree model's predictive power emerges the earliest, at week 6, with R2ad j= 0.296 (1.5 times better than the GPA model alone). As such, it is the best choice for predicting performance early on in the semester."
