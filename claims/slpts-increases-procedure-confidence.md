## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bohrer Maxwell B. MD 2025

Bohrer Maxwell B. MD, Rodgers David L. EdD. (2025). Improving Confidence in Performing Clinical Procedures Through Peer-Driven Training Sessions for Preclinical Medical Students. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11542

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-group presession/postsession survey of 31 preclinical medical learners using a 1-10 confidence scale, analyzed with paired t tests. The article reports "significant increases in confidence after participating in the SLPTS" for all four procedures; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Statistically significant improvements in mean confidence levels in performing all four procedures (p < .001 for all) were noted by the survey respondents (Table 2)."
