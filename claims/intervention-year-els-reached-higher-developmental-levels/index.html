Evidence¶

McCarthy 2020¶

McCarthy, E., Morgan, C. & Sussman, J. (2020). Growing strong learners in Galt Joint Union Elementary School District. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613373

q2 · i? · design · r2

Comparison of ELs' DRDP developmental-level distributions (Figure 6a-e) between spring 2019 and spring 2020, with cohorts showing similar fall mean scores. For Non-ELs the article reports either no discernable difference between years or a slight prior-year advantage.

"The results show that, for all DRDP domains, ELs were more likely to be in the higher developmental levels at the end of the intervention year than they were at the end of the prior year."
