q2 · i? · review · r?

A footnote observation by the WWC across the O'Connor et al. and Slocum et al. studies, noting "the effects on measures that were more aligned with the nature of the Phonological Awareness Training (i.e., blending, segmenting) were larger than the effects on measures less aligned."
