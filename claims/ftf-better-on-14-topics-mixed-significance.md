### Dreyer Tom 2025

Dreyer Tom, Papadopoulos Symeon, Wiesner Rudolf, Karay Yassin. (2025). Classroom teaching versus online teaching in physiology practical course – does this lead to different examination results? GMS Journal for Medical Education, 42(1), Doc8. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2025-42/zma001732.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-topic comparison of test scores between FtFgroup and Ogroup (Table 1) among the randomized physiology course students. The article reports "The FtFgroup scored better than the online group on 14 topics" with significance only for three topics.

> "The FtFgroup scored better than the online group on 14 topics. The differences for the topics nerve, pH-Regulation and muscle were significant at the 5% level, each with a small effect size."

### Dreyer Tom 2025 (2)

Dreyer Tom, Papadopoulos Symeon, Wiesner Rudolf, Karay Yassin. (2025). Classroom teaching versus online teaching in physiology practical course – does this lead to different examination results? GMS Journal for Medical Education, 42(1), Doc8. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2025-42/zma001732.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-topic comparison for the EEG topic in the same randomized course: the online groups scored M=6.19 versus M=6.06 for the FtFgroup, and the article states "this difference is not significant".

> "Only for the topic EEG did students from the online groups achieve marginally more points (M=6.19) than the FtFgroup (M=6.06). However, this difference is not significant."
