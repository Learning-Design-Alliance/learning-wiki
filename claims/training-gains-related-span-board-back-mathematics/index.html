Evidence¶

Dahlin 2013¶

Karin I. E. Dahlin. (2013). Working Memory Training and the Effect on Mathematical Achievement in Children with Attention Deficits and Special Needs. Journal of Education and Learning, 2(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v2n1p118

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Pearson correlation analyses within the treatment group relating WM measures, training index scores and mathematics tests. Span board back and BNST correlated r = .60 (T1), r = .50 (T2), r = .49 (T3), all p < .01; the same pattern occurred for the total group although slightly weaker.

"The outcome scores of training index (max index minus start index) for boys were significantly related to the outcome scores of Span board back (T3 - T1) ( r = .48, p < .01). Span board back (T3-T1) was also related to addition results (T3 -T1) ( r = .41, p < .05)."
