The article's abstract attributes this finding to prior research: "significant course access gaps between ELs and non-ELs," while noting such studies "provide little information" on time spent as an EL. This is a second-hand summary, not a result reanalyzed here.

"Prior studies that examine high school course-taking find significant course access gaps between ELs and non-ELs but provide little information on the relation between course-taking and time spent as an EL."
