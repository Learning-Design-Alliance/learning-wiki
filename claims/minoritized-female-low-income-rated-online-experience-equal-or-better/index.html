Means 2020¶

Means, B., & Neisler, J. (2020). Unmasking Inequality: STEM Course Experiences During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/c978880f-6afe-4778-9df2-dac931a9c471

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report's executive summary states an exception to the challenges-satisfaction pattern "was found for students from minoritized race/ethnicity groups, females, and lower-income students," who rated online quality as good as or better than in person. Only summary-level detail is available in the supplied text.

"these students were more likely to rate the quality of their experiences when their STEM course was online as just as good as, or even better than, when the course was meeting in person."
