# Clinical knowledge scores across all training modules rose from pre-test to post-test after the interprofessional clinical skills boot camp, in one uncontrolled pre-post study, with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d = .77)

## Evidence

### Wu Helen 2025

Wu Helen, Mansilla-Rivera Kenia, Wang Biju, Clark-Dufner Petra, Yi Robin Pugh, Gould Bruce. (2025). Clinical skills boot camp in a program to train healthcare professionals to provide primary care to underserved communities. Canadian Medical Education Journal, 16(5), 81-82. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79253

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Within-group t-test comparisons of online clinical knowledge assessments across all boot camp modules (Table 1): means rose from "73.05 (27.26)" to "90.34 (16.6)", p <.0001, Cohen's d = .77, based on 2705 pre-test and 2664 post-test responses.
