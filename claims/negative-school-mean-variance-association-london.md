## Evidence

### Leckie 2023

Leckie, G., Parker, R., Goldstein, H., & Tilling, K. (2023). Mixed-Effects Location Scale Models for Joint Modeling School Value-Added Effects on the Mean and Variance of Student Achievement. [doi:10.3102/10769986231210808](https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986231210808)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Scatterplot analysis (Figure 3) of Model 2 posterior mean predictions of school means against school variances of student adjusted achievement for 465 London schools. The printed effect size is r = -0.54: "the negative correlation is moderate to large 𝑟 = −0.54", while many schools show similar means but noticeably different variances.

> "While the negative correlation is moderate to large 𝑟 = −0.54, having a high school mean by no means guarantees having a low variance."
