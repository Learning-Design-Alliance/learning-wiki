MANCOVA on data from 89 undergraduates (G1: n = 27; G2: n = 28; G3: n = 34) in an applied statistics course, with baseline as covariate. The article reports "Wilks’Λ=.664" and "partialη2 =.185". Pretest performance did not significantly predict the combined outcomes (p = .918).
