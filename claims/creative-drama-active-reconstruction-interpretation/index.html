Kaf 2017¶

Kaf, O., & Uygungul Yilmaz, O. (2017). Effects of creative drama method on students’ attitude towards social studies, academic achievement and retention in Turkey. European Journal of Educational Research, 6(3), 289-298. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.6.3.289

Authors' theoretical interpretation offered in the discussion, not a separately tested result; they frame drama's effects as consistent with "the cognitive learning approach" and constructivism. No additional evidence or argument is printed for this framing.
