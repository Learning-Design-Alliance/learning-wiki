## Evidence

### Detlef Urhahne · Lisette Wijnia 2023

Detlef Urhahne · Lisette Wijnia. (2023). Theories of Motivation in Education: an Integrative Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09767-9

`q3 · i2` · `review · r2`

Meta-analysis reported in the interest theory section determined a "mean correlation coefficient of r = .31 between interest and academic achievement" for K-12 studies; PISA within-country correlations were smaller (r = .16, r = .15).

> "Schiefele et al. (1992) determined a mean correlation coefficient of r = .31 between interest and academic achievement for studies in K-12 classes."

### Detlef Urhahne · Lisette Wijnia 2023 (2)

Detlef Urhahne · Lisette Wijnia. (2023). Theories of Motivation in Education: an Integrative Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09767-9

`q2 · i1` · `review · r2`

Sundararajan and Adesope's meta-analysis on seductive details, reported in the interest theory section, found "an average negative effect of g = − .33" on recall or transfer; no meta-analyses of situational interest were known to the authors.

> "They found an average negative effect of g = − .33 for the relation between seductive details and recall or transfer of presented information."
