Evidence¶

Hans van der Meij 2015¶

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Ruth Harmsen. (2015). Animated pedagogical agents effects on enhancing student motivation and learning in a science inquiry learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9378-5

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Pre-test/post-test with 27 multiple-choice items in the 61-student experiment; the article reports a significant time effect at d = 1.10 and no condition, gender, or interaction effects. Table 4 prints gains for all conditions (e.g., control boys 5.10, agent girls 3.55).

"An ANOVA with condition and gender as ﬁxed factors and the test scores as dependent variable showed a signiﬁcant effect over time, F(1,55) = 60.23, p = 0.00, d = 1.10. There was no main effect of condition or gender, and there was also no interaction effect. In all conditions, boys and girls made signiﬁcant progress."
