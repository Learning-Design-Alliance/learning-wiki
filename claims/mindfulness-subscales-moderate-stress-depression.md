## Evidence

### Alzahrani AM 2020

Alzahrani AM, Hakami A, AlHadi A, Batais MA, Alrasheed AA, Almigbal TH. (2020). The interplay between mindfulness, depression, stress and academic performance in medical students: A Saudi perspective. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231088. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231088

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Moderation analysis using least-square means of interactions, with mindfulness categorized by sample median, in the 289-student survey; the authors report "an inverse moderator role" for describing and non-judging, and a direct proportional role for observing.

> "Only two subscales of mindfulness were found to play an inverse moderator role: describing and non-judging of inner experience (P = 0.0164, P < 0.001, respectively; Figs 2 and 3). However, the observing subscale was found to have a direct proportional moderating role (P < 0.0438, Fig 4)."
