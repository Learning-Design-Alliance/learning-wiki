### Mercan Annak 2026

Mercan Annak, İ., and Bulut, H. 2026. "The Effect of Virtual Simulation Game on the Development of Nursing Students' Knowledge and Skills: A Randomized Controlled Trial." Nursing Open 13, no. 5: e70566. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70566

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OSCE timing analysis in the RCT (Table 4): post-test catheter removal was longer in the VSG group (131.97 ± 32.27 vs 116.27 ± 29.51 s, p = 0.031) while mobilization was shorter (p = 0.017); sterile sampling showed no difference (p = 0.253).

> "Consistent with the findings in Table 4, students in the VSG group tended to spend more time performing certain procedures, particularly sterile urine sampling and urinary catheter removal, suggesting a more careful, structured approach."
