## Evidence

### Vedula S. Swaroop 2016

Vedula S. Swaroop, Malpani Anand O., Tao Lingling, Chen George, Gao Yixin, Poddar Piyush, Ahmidi Narges, Paxton Christopher, Vidal Rene, Khudanpur Sanjeev, Hager Gregory D., Chen Chi Chiung Grace. (2016). Analysis of the Structure of Surgical Activity for a Suturing and Knot-Tying Task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149174

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of gesture annotations (including whether each gesture was performed with error) across 135 dVSS trials by 18 surgeons. The abstract prints error-gesture counts of 1.00 (95% CI 0.61-1.39) for experts vs 2.84 (95% CI 2.3-3.37) for novices; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "Trials by expert surgeons (experience-based skill definition) involved fewer error gestures than trials by novice surgeons; the difference between the groups was statistically significant."
