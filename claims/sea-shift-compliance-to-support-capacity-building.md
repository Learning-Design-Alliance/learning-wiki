# SEA administrators report shifting from compliance monitoring and grants management toward meaningful support and capacity-building for identified schools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` State administrators leading school improvement work describe an intentional shift away from compliance-oriented practices toward providing meaningful support and capacity-building opportunities to identified schools. [→ Levitan 2026](#levitan-2026)

## Evidence

### Levitan 2026

Levitan, S., & Skubel, A. (2026). Examining State Education Agency Perspectives on School Improvement Supports. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682340

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with SEA administrators leading school improvement work across 11 states and Washington, D.C., analyzed with inductive content analysis in Dedoose. Participants described "moving beyond compliance monitoring and grants management" toward resources, capacity-building, and technical assistance.

> "SEA administrators expressed a clear intention — and highlighted some ongoing efforts — to reimagine their role, moving beyond compliance monitoring and grants management toward developing meaningful resources, building local capacity, and offering technical assistance."
