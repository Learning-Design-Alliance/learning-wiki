# Time-window colour resets gave teachers up-to-date readiness information, while continuously student-set colours could be outdated or confusing

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Resetting colours within a time window (request feedback) made teachers less doubtful that lamp colours reflected current student readiness. [→ Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020](#nine-sellier-and-pengcheng-an-2020)
`q2 i?` Under continuous feedback, teachers were unsure whether colours were up to date, and outdated colours sometimes misled them about students' questions. [→ Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020 (2)](#nine-sellier-and-pengcheng-an-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020

Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An. (2020). How Peripheral Interactive Systems Can Support Teachers with Differentiated Instruction: Using FireFlies as a Probe. To appear in DIS '20, July 6–10, 2020, Eindhoven, Netherlands. https://doi.org/10.1145/3357236.3395497

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Teacher report from the field tests: with request feedback the colours reset after a time window, and P7 explained that this removed doubt about stale colours, stating "The information is up to date for all students"; teachers had previously asked students repeatedly whether they understood.

> "Because the colours reset, I know that someone did not accidently show his colour from previous time. The information is up to date for all students."

### Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020 (2)

Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An. (2020). How Peripheral Interactive Systems Can Support Teachers with Differentiated Instruction: Using FireFlies as a Probe. To appear in DIS '20, July 6–10, 2020, Eindhoven, Netherlands. https://doi.org/10.1145/3357236.3395497

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Teacher report on continuous feedback: P1 described all-green lamps that turned out to hide unasked questions, and a student whose lamp stayed red after he forgot to change it back, so "the colours sometimes confused them" about readiness.

> "I thought the students were doing fine because all the FireFlies were green, yet then it turned out that actually many of them had questions"
