Evidence¶

Huang Q 2026¶

Huang Q, Tu S, Lin J, Lu L and Lv C (2026). The dark side of AI in education: AI dependency as a mediator linking academic self-efficacy and teacher support to learning burnout among university students. Front. Psychol. 17:1889053. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1889053

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

One-way ANOVA in the group-difference analyses of the survey sample. AI dependency "signiﬁcantly varied across frequency of AI use" with F(4,271) = 14.569, η 2 = 0.177, which the authors call a relatively larger effect size.

"AI dependency signiﬁcantly varied across frequency of AI use [ F(4,271) = 14.569, p < 0.001, η 2 = 0.177]."

Huang Q 2026 (2)¶

Huang Q, Tu S, Lin J, Lu L and Lv C (2026). The dark side of AI in education: AI dependency as a mediator linking academic self-efficacy and teacher support to learning burnout among university students. Front. Psychol. 17:1889053. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1889053

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

One-way ANOVA across academic years in the same group-difference analyses, described as "a small eﬀect size" with F(3,272) = 2.858, η 2 = 0.031; seniors reported higher AI dependency than juniors.

"a signiﬁcant diﬀerence was observed across academic years with a small eﬀect size [ F(3,272) = 2.858, p = 0.037, η 2 = 0.031], and post-hoc comparisons further showed that senior students reported higher levels of AI dependency than junior students."
