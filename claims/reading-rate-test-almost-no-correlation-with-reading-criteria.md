## Evidence

### Wyman 1921

Wyman, J. B., & Wendle, M. (1921). What is Reading Ability? The Journal of Educational Psychology, 518-531. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=%22What+is+Reading+Ability%22

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Grade VIII reading study: the rate subtest of the Monroe Silent Reading Test correlated +0.10 ± 0.11 with the teachers' criterion, the lowest of all tests administered, and 0.16 with the weighted-combination criterion. No standardised effect size is printed for this contrast, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Rate of Reading cannot be considered a test of reading at all in so far as our criteria measure reading ability."
