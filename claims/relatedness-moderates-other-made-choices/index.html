Evidence¶

Bong 2026¶

Bong, M., Jeong, D., Park, S., Suh, J., Kim, S. G., Park, J. C., & Kim, J. (2026). Cultural Insights into the Motivation Paradox of East Asian Learners. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10140-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review proposes, citing Bao and Lam (2008) and Deci and Ryan (2000), that 'a sense of relatedness becomes an influential moderator' of internalizing others' choices. It further states that positive effects of others' choices diminish without belongingness, when the importance of personal choice may become more pronounced.

"As might be expected, a sense of relatedness becomes an influential moderator that facilitates East Asian learners' internalization of others' choices (Bao & Lam, 2008; Deci & Ryan, 2000)."
