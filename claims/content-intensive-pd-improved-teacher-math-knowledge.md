## Evidence

### Garet 2016

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on teachers' mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive professional development (NCEE 2016-4010). Washington, D.C.: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20164010

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment: grade 4 teachers from 94 schools randomly assigned within schools; final sample 165 teachers from 73 schools. Knowledge assessed with an adaptive NWEA-based test in fall 2013 and spring 2014; impacts statistically significant at the 0.05 level. The study reports "7 points higher" in fall and 6 in spring; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Treatment teachers' average knowledge score was 7 points higher than control teachers' average score in the fall and 6 points higher in the spring ( Figure 1)."
