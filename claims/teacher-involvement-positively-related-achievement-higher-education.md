## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who report that their teacher has helped them with their doubts when consulted obtain higher final marks, in fixed-effects regressions on 86,038 records. [→ Ayllón Sara 2019](#ayllon-sara-2019)

## Evidence

### Ayllón Sara 2019

Ayllón Sara, Alsina Ángel, Colomer Jordi. (2019). Teachers’ involvement and students’ self-efficacy: Keys to achievement in higher education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216865

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression models with fixed effects (subject, year, lecturer) on achievement from 86,038 teaching-evaluation records at the University of Girona (2014–2016); the INVOL_1 coefficient is 0.043*** (p<0.01) in the fully controlled specification. The finding is that "Only INVOL_1, EFFICACY_1, and EFFICACY_2, in columns (B) to (D), are positively related to achievement at standard significant levels."
