### Jere-Folotiya 2014

Jere-Folotiya, J., Chansa-Kabali, T., Munachaka, J. C., Sampa, F., Yalukanda, C., Westerholm, J., Richardson, U., Serpell, R., & Lyytinen, H. (2014). The effect of using a mobile literacy game to improve literacy levels of grade one students in Zambian schools. Education Tech Research Dev, 62, 417-436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-014-9342-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis linking GraphoGame log-based letter-sound knowledge with the Ojanen et al. spelling test at baseline (r = .50) and posttest (r = .42), both significant at p < .01. This supports the game measure as a predictor of conventional decoding performance.

> "letter-sound knowledge was signiﬁcantly correlated with decoding (spelling) test scores at baseline r = .50, p\ .01, N = 94 and at posttest r = .42, p\ .01, N = 72."
