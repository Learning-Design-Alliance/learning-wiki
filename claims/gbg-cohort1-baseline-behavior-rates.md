Descriptive analysis of fall baseline observations for Cohort 1 (18 schools, 71 classrooms, 1401 students). The article reports the printed percentages and adds that aggressive behavior was rare: "2.2 and 3.0 percent of students were identified as engaged in aggressive behaviors".
