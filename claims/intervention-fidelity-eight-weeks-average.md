Implementation fidelity was monitored through weekly/biweekly parent-survey data and weekly student work collected in take-home folders. Parents were expected to engage in at least four weeks to reach minimum fidelity; materials supported up to 15 weeks.

> "The average engagement level with the at-home numeracy intervention for the 12 parents in the treatment group was eight weeks, ranging from four weeks (n = 7), seven weeks (n = 1), and 15 weeks (n = 4)"
