# Interleaving is most useful when differences between items are subtle; blocking can be preferable when category members are diverse

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The interleaving advantage is moderated by item and category similarity: interleaving helps when exemplars are similar and easily confused, while blocking may benefit diverse categories. [→ Jonathan Firth 2021](#jonathan-firth-2021)

## Evidence

### Jonathan Firth 2021

Jonathan Firth, Ian Rivers and James Boyle. (2021). A systematic review of interleaving as a concept learning strategy. Review of Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/rev3.3266

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Narrative synthesis of studies varying within- and between-category similarity, including MacKendrick's (2015) systematic HW/LW/HB/LB manipulation, where interleaving benefited HB-HW and especially HW-LB categories. The review concludes interleaving is "of greatest use when differences between items are subtle".

> "Carvalho and Goldstone (2014a, 2015b) presented results which suggest that if category members are all alike then interleaving is best, while if category members are more diverse then blocking may be beneficial."
