# Dental students free to combine e-learning with lectures attend significantly fewer face-to-face lectures than students in a mandatory-attendance traditional course

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled trial, the technology-enhanced learning group attended significantly fewer face-to-face lectures (mean 2.5 ± 3.2) than the traditional group (8 ± 0), p < .0005. [→ Nkenke Emeka 2012](#nkenke-emeka-2012)

## Evidence

### Nkenke Emeka 2012

Nkenke Emeka, Vairaktaris Elefterios, Bauersachs Anne, Eitner Stephan, Budach Alexander, Knipfer Christoph, Stelzle Florian. (2012). Acceptance of technology-enhanced learning for a theoretical radiological science course: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-18

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 42 third-year dental students were block-randomized to a traditional lecture course or a TEL course with optional lecture attendance. The TEL group attended "2.5 ± 3.2 lectures" versus all 8 in the traditional group, p < .0005; no effect size was printed.

> "The technology-enhanced learning group attended the face-to-face lectures significantly less than the traditional group (8 ± 0 lectures attended by the traditional group, 2.5 ± 3.2 lectures attended by the technology-enhanced learning group, p < .0005)."
