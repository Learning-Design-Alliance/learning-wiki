University of Chicago School Mathematics Project 2016¶

University of Chicago School Mathematics Project. (2016). University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP) — WWC Intervention Report, Secondary Mathematics. What Works Clearinghouse. https://ucsmp.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · causal · r?

WWC effectiveness rating for UCSMP Algebra in the general mathematics achievement domain, based on one study meeting group design standards with reservations; the report states the study "showed a statistically sig- nificant positive effect" and assigns the rating of potentially positive effects with a small extent of evidence. No effect size is printed in this rating statement.
