## Evidence

### Al Essa A 2026

Al Essa A, Schmidt HG, Mamede S, Knežević K, Zwaan L, Magzoub M. (2026). Premature closure underlies bias in medical diagnosis in students: A randomised controlled experiment. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70229

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results of the same within-group incomplete block experiment: the SDF-at-the-end condition showed reduced accuracy relative to No-SDF while total processing time per word was statistically indistinguishable from No-SDF.

> "When SDFs were presented at the end of the case, diagnostic accuracy was also reduced (mean 0.17; 36% reduction), but processing time did not differ from the No‐SDF condition."
