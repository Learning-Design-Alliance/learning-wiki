## Evidence

### Erbeli 2021

Erbeli, F., Shi, Q., Campbell, A. R., Hart, S. A., & Woltering, S. (2021). Developmental Dynamics Between Reading and Math in Elementary School. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.13004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A bivariate latent change score model re-estimated with UNIT nonverbal cognitive ability as a covariate (Supplementary Table SI1, Figure SI1). The same reading-to-math coupling model was selected, and "the path estimates changed minimally," with nonverbal ability significantly related to all intercept and growth parameters.

> "The model-fiting results with nonverbal cognitive abilities as a covariate supported the same best fitting model without the covariate, indicating that the findings regarding reading influencing subsequent math growth as the most parsimonious model held."
