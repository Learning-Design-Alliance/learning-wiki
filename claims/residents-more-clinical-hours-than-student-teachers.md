## Evidence

### Carver-Thomas 2024

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & Jeannite, D. (2024). Tackling teacher shortages: What we know about California's teacher workforce investments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/137.196

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 2021 California TPP completer surveys, plotted in Figure 7 comparing estimated clinical hours for residents and student teachers. Residents had more intensive clinical experiences and support than student teachers.

> "Two thirds of residents (66%) reported completing at least 600 hours of student teaching (see figure 7). Just 51% of candidates in a traditional student teaching program completed as many hours, and interns completed little or no student teaching."

### Carver-Thomas 2024 (2)

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung-Gagné, M., & Jeannite, D. (2024). Tackling teacher shortages: What we know about California's teacher workforce investments. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/137.196

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey analysis of 2021 California TPP completer data comparing pathway ratings. The report attributes this to residents' more intensive clinical experiences and support relative to student teachers.

> "An analysis of 2021 California TPP completer surveys found that residents were more likely to rate their programs as very effective compared to completers of other pathways.41 Residents had more intensive clinical experiences and support than did student teachers and received high levels of feedback."
