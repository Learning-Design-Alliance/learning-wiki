## Evidence

### Jorm Anthony F 2010

Jorm Anthony F, Kitchener Betty A, Sawyer Michael G, Scales Helen, Cvetkovski Stefan. (2010). Mental health first aid training for high school teachers: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-244X-10-51

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized trial teacher outcome on school practices and policies. Odds ratios printed for policy awareness and implementation; no standardized effect size printed.

> "trained teachers were more likely to report that the school had a written policy to deal with students with mental health problems (OR = 4.57, P = 0.019 at post-test, OR = 7.28, P = 0.003 at follow-up) and that the policy had been implemented in the previous month (OR = 7.23, P = 0.070 at post-test, OR = 13.30, P = 0.028 at follow-up)."
