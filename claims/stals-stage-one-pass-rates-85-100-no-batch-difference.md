Observational cohort implementation study: 91 first- and second-year residents trained and assessed in five batches from January 2021 to December 2023. The article reports "The overall pass rates ranged between 85% and 100%" with no significant batch difference (P = 0.387).

> "The overall pass rates ranged between 85% and 100%. No significant differences were observed among the pass rates of the batches (P = 0.387, Table 3)."
