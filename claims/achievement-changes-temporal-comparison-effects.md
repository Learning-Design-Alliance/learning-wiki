## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Achievement changes show small positive effects on self-concepts within subjects (standardized coefficients below 0.2). [→ Wolff et al. 2020](#wolff-et-al-2020)
`q3 i?` Achievement changes show near-zero effects on self-concepts between subjects, confirmed by equivalence tests against boundary values of β = –.10 and β = .10. [→ Wolff et al. 2020](#wolff-et-al-2020)

## Evidence

### Wolff et al. 2020

Fabian Wolff, Fabio Sticca, Christoph Niepel, Thomas Götz, Jan Van Damme, and Jens Möller. (2020). The Reciprocal 2I/E Model: An Investigation of Mutual Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept Levels and Changes in the Math and Verbal Domain Across Three Countries. https://osf.io/7f6gb/

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 2I/EM sub-model results (N = 1,508). Temporal comparison effects within subjects were β = .16 to .21 (below d = 0.2 equivalent); between-subject effects were near zero with 90% CIs within the equivalence boundaries. Study 3 effects were smaller still (math β = .06, Dutch β = –.00).

> "there were small to moderate positive effects of achievement changes on self-concepts within subjects (all .16 ≤ β ≤ .21, all p < .001), whereas the effects of achievement changes on self-concepts between subjects were close to zero and significantly different from the boundary values"
