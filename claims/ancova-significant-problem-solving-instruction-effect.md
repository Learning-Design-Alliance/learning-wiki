Mandina Shadreck, Ochonogor Chukunoye Enunuwe. (2017). Problem Solving Instruction for Overcoming Students' Difficulties in Stoichiometric Problems. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 10, Number 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164987

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of between-subjects effects (Table 5) testing the main effect of the Selvaratnam-Frazer and Ashmore, Casey and Frazer problem-solving strategies, with pretest as covariate (pretest effect Sig. .088). The group effect was reported as significant (Sig. .000); the null hypothesis of no difference was rejected.
