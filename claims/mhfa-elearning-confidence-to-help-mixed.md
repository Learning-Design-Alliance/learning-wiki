## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Davies E. Bethan 2018

Davies E. Bethan, Beever Emmeline, Glazebrook Cris. (2018). A pilot randomised controlled study of the mental health first aid eLearning course with UK medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1154-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat repeated measures ANOVA on the self-rated confidence item in the pilot RCT (N = 55). Confidence increased over time in both arms combined, but "the group x time interaction failed to reach significance".

> "Confidence to help a friend with mental health problems did increase over time (F(1,51) = 21.22, p = <.001) but the group x time interaction failed to reach significance (F(1,51) = 2.98, p = .09)."

### Davies E. Bethan 2018 (2)

Davies E. Bethan, Beever Emmeline, Glazebrook Cris. (2018). A pilot randomised controlled study of the mental health first aid eLearning course with UK medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1154-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Non-ITT repeated measures ANOVA restricted to responders who completed baseline and follow-up (N = 35). Confidence "improved significantly in the intervention group (Z = − 3.01, p = .003)".

> "There was also a significant main effect (F(1,31) = 47.46, p = <.001) for confidence to help a friend and an interaction between time and group (F(1,31) = 14.73, p = .001)."
