# Hints generated with PromptHive produced learning gains on par with hints manually authored by subject matter experts without generative AI

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mohi Reza 2025

Mohi Reza, Ioannis Anastasopoulos, Shreya Bhandari, Zachary A Pardos. (2025). PromptHive: Bringing Subject Matter Experts Back to the Forefront with Collaborative Prompt Engineering for Educational Content Creation. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3714051](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714051)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning gain study comparing PromptHive-generated hint pathways to human-only curated OATutor hints; 358 undergraduates were recruited via Prolific and randomly assigned to conditions, yielding 225 unique participants and 549 completed lesson submissions after exclusions. The article reports hints "on par with those authored manually"; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "show that users can successfully use PromptHive to create hints that are on par with those authored manually by subject matter experts who did not make use of generative AI, while cutting mental workload by more than half"
