Pooled label distribution from the production run of the HERO-QA pipeline with a Qwen3-32B judge. The article concludes explicit contradiction is rare and compatible differences in detail are more common; separately, 73.6% of generated answers are absent.

"Of 1,772,261 comparisons, 1,615,385 (91.1%) are ABSENT. Among 156,876 non-absent pairs, 89,822 (57.3%) are COMPLEMENTARY, 35,910 (22.9%) CONSISTENT, 31,057 (19.8%) DIVERGENT, and 87 (0.06%) CONTRADICTORY."
