Elaheh Sanoubari, Neil Fernandes, Keith Rebello, Alicia Pan, Andrew Houston, and Kerstin Dautenhahn. (2026). Play-Testing REMind: Evaluating an Educational Robot-Mediated Role-Play Game. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00300 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.00300

q2 · i? · design · r2

Paired t-test on empathy items (adapted from Pöyhönen et al.) in the play-testing study; the increase of 15.7 points was "not statistically significant, t(17) = 1.62, p = .062". No effect size is printed.

"Empathy scores increased by 15.7 points, but the change was not statistically significant, t(17) = 1.62, p = .062. H3(a) was not confirmed."

Elaheh Sanoubari 2026 (2)¶

Elaheh Sanoubari, Neil Fernandes, Keith Rebello, Alicia Pan, Andrew Houston, and Kerstin Dautenhahn. (2026). Play-Testing REMind: Evaluating an Educational Robot-Mediated Role-Play Game. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00300

q2 · i3 · design · r2

Spearman correlation in the same study between pre-test human-directed and post-test robot-directed empathy: "Spearman's ρ= .62, p = .006", indicating children with higher baseline empathy were more likely to empathize with the robot.

"a significant positive correlation was found between empathy toward the human character at pre-test and the robot avatar at post-test, Spearman's ρ= .62, p = .006, confirming H3(b)"
