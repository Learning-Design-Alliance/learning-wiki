## Evidence

### Diego Pablo de la Hera 2019

Diego Pablo de la Hera, Mariano Sigman and Cecilia Ines Calero. (2019). Social interaction and conceptual change pave the way away from children 's misconceptions about the Earth. npj Science of Learning 4:12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0051-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on counts of adopted responses by shift valence. For Children L the effect was significant, F(2, 42) = 12.94, p = 4.18E−05, η² = 0.37, with positive shifts (M = 3.93) exceeding negative (M = 1.13) and no-shift (M = 1.40) counts; for Children M the ANOVA was not significant.

> "A one-way ANOVA found signiﬁcant differences between the average number of adopted responses leading to either positive, negative or no knowledge level shifts, F(2, 42) = 12.94, p = 4.18E−05, η² = 0.37"
