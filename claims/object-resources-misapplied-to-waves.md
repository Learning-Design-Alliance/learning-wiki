### Michael C. Wittmann 2018

Michael C. Wittmann. (2018). Research in the Resources Framework: Changing environments, consistent exploration. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/revperv2.5.5](https://doi.org/10.1119/revperv2.5.5)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis of written and interview responses to mechanical wave questions in the 1999 Physics Teacher study, in which the author and colleagues found "evidence of thinking using resources commonly associated with objects, not waves" and described student ideas as mixtures of particle and wave behavior.

> "Student responses to a whole series of seemingly unrelated questions about mechanical waves showed evidence of thinking using resources commonly associated with objects, not waves. Students seemed to interpret the peak of a wave as if it was the center-of-mass of an object."
