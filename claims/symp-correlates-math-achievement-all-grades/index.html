Correlational analysis of the subsample of children with data on both standardized achievement tests (n = 942), using z-scores per grade. The paper-and-pencil test correlated with the curriculum-based mathematics test in all grades, with "one-digit r =. 28" and "t w o - d i g i tr = .30".
