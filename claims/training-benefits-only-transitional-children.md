### van der Maas 1992

van der Maas, H. L. J., & Molenaar, P. C. M. (1992). Stagewise Cognitive Development: An Application of Catastrophe Theory. Psychological Review, 99(3), 395-417. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.99.3.395

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument (divergence-of-linear-response flag applied to training experiments): if the distance to a catastrophe point is small, training could induce enough divergence and loss of stability for a transition. The authors add "As only transitional children will profit from training, this sample heterogeneity will seriously affect the outcomes of training studies."

> "According to the catastrophe theoretical interpretation of Piaget's stage hypothesis, a positive effect of training may occur for transitional children"
