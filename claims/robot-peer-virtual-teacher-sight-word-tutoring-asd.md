## Evidence

### DiPietro Joan 2019

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Small-n study combining virtual reality technology with social robotics: a virtual teacher instructing sight words with a humanoid robot emulating a peer, with three children with ASD aged 6–8 years. "Participants acquired, maintained, and generalized 100% of the words explicitly instructed to them" and vicariously learned 94% of robot-instructed words.

> "Results indicated that participants acquired, maintained, and generalized 100% of the words explicitly instructed to them, made fewer errors while learning the words common between them and the robot peer, and vicariously learned 94% of the words solely instructed to the robot."
