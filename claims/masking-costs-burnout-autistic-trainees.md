The guide asserts, citing Syharat et al. (2023) and a 2024 meta-ethnography of social camouflaging experiences, that masking consumes a tremendous amount of cognitive energy and contributes to burnout and mental health problems. The guide reports no new empirical data.

> "Most autistic people attempt to mitigate this by eﬀortfully “masking” or camouﬂaging their autistic traits. Masking consumes a tremendous amount of cognitive energy and contributes to social isolation, burnout, and mental health problems [4,5]."
