# Algerian university teachers of English lack sufficient knowledge of cognitive load theory

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a 2020 questionnaire survey, slightly more than half of 21 Algerian university English teachers indicated they were not familiar with cognitive load theory. [→ Houichi 2020](#houichi-2020)

## Evidence

### Houichi 2020

Houichi, A., & Sarnou, D. (2020). Cognitive Load Theory and its Relation to Instructional Design: Perspectives of Some Algerian University Teachers of English. Arab World English Journal. https://dx.doi.org/10.24093/awej/vol11no4.8

`q2 · i?`

Questionnaire survey of 21 permanent English language teachers from Algerian universities, analyzed with SPSS. On the familiarity item, "(47.6%) of the respondents confirmed their knowledge of the theory while (52.4%) indicated their ignorance." No effect size is reported.

> "Responses to this question were divided almost equally; (47.6%) of the respondents confirmed their knowledge of the theory while (52.4%) indicated their ignorance."
