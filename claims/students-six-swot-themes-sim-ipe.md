Phase 1 qualitative SWOT analysis with 10 of 30 enrolled master's students (90% women, mean age 26.1 ± 7.1 years); thematic analysis identified the six themes, with students citing debriefing and scenario realism as strengths and incomplete prebriefings as weaknesses. The article states "six main themes were identified".
