# Attending to a poem's title as a structural cue was evident in novice but not expert protocols, and the title jolted a novice into revising a misinterpretation.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One strategy guiding meaning appeared in the novice, but not the expert, protocols: giving interpretive weight to the title, which corrected a novice's misreading of the poem as being about a flower. [→ Peskin 1998](#peskin-1998)

## Evidence

### Peskin 1998

Peskin, J. (1998). Constructing meaning when reading poetry: An expert-novice study. Cognition and Instruction, 16(3), 235-263. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1603_1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Protocol analysis in the same think-aloud study. Novice Two, misreading the poem as about a flower, glanced at the title and exclaimed: "Oh no! It 's On a Drop of Dew.  That 's what it 's on."

> "interestingly there was one strategy guiding meaning that was evident in the novice, but not the expert, protocols. This was the consideration of the title of the poem."
