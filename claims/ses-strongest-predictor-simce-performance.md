## Evidence

### Navarro Juan-José 2015

Navarro Juan-José, García-Rubio Javier, Olivares Pedro R. (2015). The Relative Age Effect and Its Influence on Academic Performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141895

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical multiple regression on the SIMCE global score of 15,234 Chilean eighth graders; SES entered first explained 19% of variance with standardised β = .438 (t = 60.148, p < .001), institution type added 0.7% in Model 2.

> "the variable SES has a significant impact on the academic performance, explaining 19% of it (Model 1), followed by the type of institution, which additionally explains 0.7% of the variance (Model 2)."
