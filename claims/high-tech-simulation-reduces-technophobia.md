Quasi-experimental between-group comparison (Mann-Whitney U) of pre-to-post difference scores in two matched groups of second-year OT students; the high-tech reduction exceeded the low-tech reduction at p < 0.001, and the discussion attributes a "medium effect size" to this difference.

> "In the low-tech group, the reduction in technophobia was smaller compared to the reduction in the high-tech group (p < 0.001, Fig. 3a)."
