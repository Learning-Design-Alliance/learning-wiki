## Evidence

### Pir Suhail Ahmed Sarhandi 2017

Pir Suhail Ahmed Sarhandi, Ayman Bajnaid & Tariq Elyas. (2017). Impact of Smartphone Based Activities on EFL Students' Engagement. English Language Teaching, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n6p103

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of initiation times recorded in teachers' activity journals across 19 treatment sessions with 50 Saudi male EFL students. The experimental group's mean initiation time was "M=0.00, SD= 0.00" versus 14.21 seconds for controls, t(18) = -22.28, p < 0.001.

> "An independent sample T-Test affirmed that the initiation times of the experimental group (n= 19, M=0.00, SD= 0.00) were significantly different from the initiation times of the control group (n= 19, M=14.21, SD=2.78); t (18) = -22.28, p < 0.001."
