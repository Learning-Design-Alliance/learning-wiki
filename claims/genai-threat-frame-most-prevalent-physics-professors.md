Vidar Skogvoll and Tor Ole Odden. (2026). How Physics Professors Use and Frame Generative AI Tools. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae55e0](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae55e0)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of interviews with twelve physics professors at one Scandinavian university. The authors report threat-frame warrants “appeared roughly twice as often compared to warrants for any other frame”, calling the frame “more of an overarching concern the professors held”.

> "This theme of AI as a threat permeated the discussions with the professors. Warrants for it appeared roughly twice as often compared to warrants for any other frame."
