# Simulation training strategy showed no significant effect on intraoperative complication rates or total operation time in trainees' first three phacoemulsification surgeries

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ng DS 2023

Ng DS, Yip BHK, Young AL, Yip WWK, Lam NM, Li KK, Ko ST, Chan WH, Aryasit O, Sikder S, Ferris JD, Pang CP, Tham CC. (2023). Cost-effectiveness of virtual reality and wet laboratory cataract surgery simulation. Medicine 2023;102:40(e35067). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035067

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Video-recorded assessment of 66 cataract surgeries by the 22 trainees recorded complications per group; the article reports "no significant difference in intraoperative complication rates" across Groups A, B, and C. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond non-significance.

> "The 3 groups of trainees had no significant difference in intraoperative complication rates: errant capsulorhexis occurred in 2, 1, and 1 case(s); posterior capsule rupture (PCR) in 2, 2, and 2 cases and anterior vitrectomy was performed in 1, 2 and 1 case(s) of operations by Groups A, B, and C, respectively."

### Ng DS 2023 (2)

Ng DS, Yip BHK, Young AL, Yip WWK, Lam NM, Li KK, Ko ST, Chan WH, Aryasit O, Sikder S, Ferris JD, Pang CP, Tham CC. (2023). Cost-effectiveness of virtual reality and wet laboratory cataract surgery simulation. Medicine 2023;102:40(e35067). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000035067

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Operation time recorded by a research assistant during the 3 sequential surgeries showed no statistically significant difference (P = .10) among Groups A, B, and C; means are descriptive with no effect size printed.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in total operation time (34.75 ± SD 10.76 vs 38.91 ± SD 9.31 vs 39.92 ± SD 4.68 minutes, P = .10) between groups A, B, and C, respectively."
