## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In linear regressions per task, most EAPGS effects were very close to 0 and failed to reject the null after Bonferroni correction; a few task-specific associations (e.g., a negative EAPGS–N-Back change association) were significant at the uncorrected one-tailed 0.05 level. The article reports results "show small effects that are very close to 0 and failed to reject the null after Bonferroni correction for multiple testing".
