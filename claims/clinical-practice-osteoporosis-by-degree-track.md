### Ding Hong 2025

Ding Hong, Li He-Xi, Ai You-Wei, Zhang Qing-Yi, Tang Hai-Tao, Zeng Wen. (2025). Assessment of understanding, attitudes, and clinical practices regarding osteoporosis among postgraduate students in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07785-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`
