Haynes Joanna C., Rettedal Siren I., Ushakova Anastasia, Perlman Jeffrey M., Ersdal Hege L. (2024). How Much Training Is Enough? Low-Dose, High-Frequency Simulation Training and Maintenance of Competence in Neonatal Resuscitation. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Profession-specific threshold analysis of ventilation rate, the only skill element retaining a profession fixed effect in the GLME models. "The midwives and the pediatricians were predicted to succeed...without any training", while anesthetists required 36 cases.

> "The midwives and the pediatricians were predicted to succeed (ie, ventilate with a rate of 30–60 inflations/minute) without any training, that is, training load predicting success in these 2 groups is a total case number = 0. This contrasts with the group needing the highest number of cases to predict success, the anesthetists (36 cases)"
