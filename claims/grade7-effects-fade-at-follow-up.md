# Seventh graders' treatment effects fade by follow-up, with only the strategy knowledge contrast remaining significant

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` At follow-up, the modeling competency effect remained significant overall (part. η² = .05) but the worked-examples-vs-control contrast was no longer significant (p > .10). [→ Hänze 2022](#hanze-2022)
`q3 i?` At follow-up, strategy knowledge showed still greater use of adequate strategies after worked examples than control (p < .05), though the omnibus effect was marginal (part. η² = .03). [→ Hänze 2022 (2)](#hanze-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Hänze 2022

Hänze, M., & Leiss, D. (2022). Using heuristic worked examples to promote solving of reality-based tasks in mathematics in lower secondary school. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09583-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up ANCOVA roughly eight weeks after treatment for seventh graders: the omnibus modeling competency effect persisted ("part. η2 = .05") but the worked-examples contrast showed "no effect for modeling competency (p > .10)".

> "The treatment effects faded out somewhat by the follow-up measurement (modeling competency: F(2, 163) = 3.86, p < .05, part. η2 = .05; strategy knowledge: F(2, 163) = 2.83, p = .06, part. η2 = .03). Regarding contrast Cworked-examples, the results indicated no effect for modeling competency (p > .10)"

### Hänze 2022 (2)

Hänze, M., & Leiss, D. (2022). Using heuristic worked examples to promote solving of reality-based tasks in mathematics in lower secondary school. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09583-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same follow-up analysis, the worked-examples contrast for strategy knowledge remained significant, showing "greater use of adequate strategies after worked examples compared to control condition (p < .05)"; no effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "Regarding contrast Cworked-examples, the results indicated no effect for modeling competency (p > .10) but still greater use of adequate strategies after worked examples compared to control condition (p < .05)."
