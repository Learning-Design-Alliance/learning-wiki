## Evidence

### Mukherjee 2023

Mukherjee, S., Rohles, B., Distler, V., Lenzini, G., & Koenig, V. (2023). The effects of privacy-non-invasive interventions on cheating prevention and user experience in unproctored online assessments: An empirical study. Computers and Education, 207, 104925. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative sentence-completion data from treatment-group participants revealed distraction reports (~16%) and negative emotions including worry about false accusations, stress, annoyance, and distrust. Positive emotions were less frequent in the warning condition (12%) versus honor code (24%) or monitoring (23%).

> "The qualitative sentence-completion responses revealed that around 16% of participants were distracted by the interventions (P651: "It slowed me down because I had to spend time reading it")."
