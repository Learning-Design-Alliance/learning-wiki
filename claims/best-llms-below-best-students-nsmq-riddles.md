Comparison of LLM real-time proxy results against the retrospective real-world performance of the best-performing student teams on the same 2019 riddles, using a metadata sheet recording which clue each school answered on. The article concludes the best LLMs currently perform below the smartest NSMQ students.

> "The best performance of LLMs of 75.64% EM accuracy and 366 points is lower than the best students' performance of 78.21% EM accuracy and 377 points."
