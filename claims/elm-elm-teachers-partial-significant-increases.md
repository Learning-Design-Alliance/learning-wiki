### Doabler 2018

Investigating the Longitudinal Effects of a Core Mathematics Program on Evidence-Based Teaching Practices in Mathematics. Learning Disability Quarterly, 41(3), 144–158. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948718756040

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal latent variable analysis of the ELM-ELM teacher group across the two trial years, reported as Hedges's g contrasts on log-transformed rates in Figure 3. The article prints no effect size value for these within-group changes in the quoted text.

> "For the ELM-ELM group (third panel from the top of Figure 3), although change was positive for all four rates, only for two rates, group responses and teacher demonstrations, was the magnitude of change big enough to be significant."
