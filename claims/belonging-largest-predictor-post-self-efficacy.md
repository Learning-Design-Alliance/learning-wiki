## Evidence

### Yangqiuting Li 2020

Yangqiuting Li, Kyle Whitcomb, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). How learning environment predicts male and female students' physics motivational beliefs in introductory physics courses. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.li)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of R² values across seven SEM models in Table III (all R² significant with p < 0.001). Belonging-only model: post-SE R² = 0.75 versus 0.56 for recognition-only and 0.61 for peer-interaction-only; the full model reached 0.79.

> "there is 75% of the variance of post-self-efficacy explained by the model which only includes belonging, which is larger than that explained by the other two single construct models and is very close to that explained by the model including all of the three constructs"
