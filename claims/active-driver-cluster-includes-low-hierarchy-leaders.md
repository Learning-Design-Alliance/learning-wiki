Cress, U., Stahl, G., Ludvigsen, S., & Law, N. (2015). The core features of CSCL: Social situation, collaborative knowledge processes and their design. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 10, 109-116. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-015-9214-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editors describe the surprising cluster-analysis result of the Rhem et al. study: a fourth group of "the most active participants" acted as drivers and leaders of the learning communities despite including low-status members.

> "They authored the most contributions and those with the highest quality. Half of the members in this group were from a high and the other half from a low position in the hierarchy."
