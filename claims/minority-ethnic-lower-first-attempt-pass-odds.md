## Evidence

### Ellis Ricky 2025

Ellis Ricky, Knapton Andy, Cannon Jane, Lee Amanda J., Cleland Jennifer. (2025). Differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical examinations: examining the relationship between sociodemographic differences and examination performance. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-025-04034-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression on UKMED first-attempt data adjusted for prior attainment and other sociodemographic factors; all minority ethnic groups had significantly lower odds than White candidates, with the largest gap for Black or Black British candidates.

> "The biggest attainment gap existed for candidates identifying as Black or Black British, who were less than half as likely to pass written (OR 0.49 (95% CI 0.45 to 0.53)) and clinical (OR 0.49 (95% CI 0.43 to 0.54)) examinations compared to White candidates."
