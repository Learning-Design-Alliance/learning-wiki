q3 · i? · review · r2

Domain coding of the 42 reviewed studies in the systematic review. The review reports the distribution across six subject domains and cautions that findings from the three least-studied domains should be interpreted with caution.

"The majority of studies were situated in environmental sciences (n = 16; 38.1%) and biology (n = 13; 31.0%), followed by earth sciences (n = 7; 16.7%), physics ( n = 3; 7.1%), interdisciplinary contexts ( n = 2; 4.8%), and geology (n = 1; 2.4%)."

Jina Kang 2026 (2)¶

Jina Kang, Chungsoo Na, Morgan Diederich, Hillary Swanson, Lili Yan. (2026). Cultivating science data literacy in K-16 science education through data practices: A systematic review. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09803-5

q3 · i? · review · r3

Cross-domain frequency comparison of core data practices (Fig. 3) in the review. The review reports Cleaning Data percentages by domain and that disseminating results fell below 50% in most subjects except the single geology study.

"In contrast, Cleaning Data was less common in biology (46.2%) compared to environmental science (75.0%) and earth science (57.1%). Disseminating results were generally underrepresented (< 50.0%) across most subjects, except for the single geology study, which included this practice."
