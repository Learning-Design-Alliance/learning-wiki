## Evidence

### Elsbeth Stern 2017

Elsbeth Stern (2017). Individual differences in the learning potential of human beings. npj Science of Learning 2:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-016-0003-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of twin and adoption research states "50 –80% of IQ variation is due to genetic differences." The review attributes the range across studies to the reaction norm of intelligence-related genes and to differing societal access to schooling, health care, and nutrition. No effect size is printed for this pooled range.

> "Altogether, twin and adoption studies suggest that 50 –80% of IQ variation is due to genetic differences."
