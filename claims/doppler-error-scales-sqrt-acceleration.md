Kenichiro Aoki, Takahisa Mitsui and Yuki Yamamoto. (2009). Direct quantitative measurements of Doppler effects for sound sources with gravitational acceleration. https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.3819 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0911.3819](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0911.3819)

Analytical/numerical analysis in Section IV of the trade-off between measurement time and frequency resolution for accelerating sources; the article states errors are "inversely proportional to N , T" but, due to the peak criterion, "can be decreased only as √a".

> "Errors ∆ f, ∆v are inversely proportional to N , T . However, due to Eq. (3), ∆ f, ∆v can be decreased only as √a."
