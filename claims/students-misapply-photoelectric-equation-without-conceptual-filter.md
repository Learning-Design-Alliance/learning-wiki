## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish 2006

Edward F. Redish. (2006). Problem Solving and the Use of Math in Physics Courses. Invited talk, World View on Physics Education in 2005, Delhi. Originally published 2005. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608268](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0608268)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Instructor's classroom observation in his third-semester engineering physics class after posing the longer-wavelength photoelectric problem. The author was "quite taken aback at the result"; students interpreted the equation differently than he expected, lacking the conceptual filter that kinetic energy must be positive.

> "Almost one quarter of my students said something like, "We use Einstein's photoelectric equation. Changing the wavelength changes the frequency. Since we had zero before, if we change f we won't have zero anymore so we should get electrons out.""
