# Learning gains under the coupled simulation-plus-formative-assessment model show no statistically significant correlation with student learning style (ILS) in engineering physics students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pearson correlations between learning gains and each ILS learning-style continuum were not statistically significant for any of the five simulations, contradicting the hypothesis that the model favors certain styles.
