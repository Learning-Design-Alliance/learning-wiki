## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Technostress affected CIT both directly (β = -0.373) and indirectly through TPACK (β = -0.078), a complementary partial mediation with total effect -0.451. [→ Su Wenhao 2025](#su-wenhao-2025)

## Evidence

### Su Wenhao 2025

Su Wenhao, Fadzil Hidayah Mohd, Rauf Rose Amnah Abd. (2025). Moderated mediation of technostress, TPACK, and growth mindset on competency in interdisciplinary teaching among STEM lecturers. Scientific Reports 15, 37633. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-21476-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) in SmartPLS 4.0 on the 405-lecturer sample. Both effects significant and directionally consistent, classified by the authors as complementary mediation; the indirect effect "β=−0.078, p < 0.001" supported H4.

> "Results demonstrated that Technostress had significant negative direct (β=−0.373, p < 0.001) and indirect effects (β=−0.078, p < 0.001) on CIT through TPACK, leading to a significant negative total effect of −0.451 (p < 0.001)."
