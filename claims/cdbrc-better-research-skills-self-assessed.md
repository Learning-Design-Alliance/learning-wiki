## Evidence

### Gan Xuejing 2024

Gan Xuejing, Ou Qianmin, Gong Zhuohong, Cai Gengbin, Liu Xinyu, Yang Ruihan, Lin Yuhang, Li Lin, Li Xiaolan, Hong Yun, Chen Zetao. (2024). A “flexible” compulsory dental basic research course for undergraduate students: a three-year retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04874-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Likert-scale self-assessment of seven research abilities (raising scientific problems, experimental design, experimental skills, data analysis, paper writing, project application, academic exchange) compared between groups; six of seven favored Group A with printed P values from 0.0026 to 0.0438. No effect sizes are printed.

> "compared with Group B, the students in Group A achieved better average scores in self-assessment of basic research skills (P < 0.05), in addition to the “Ability of experimental design”."

### Gan Xuejing 2024 (2)

Gan Xuejing, Ou Qianmin, Gong Zhuohong, Cai Gengbin, Liu Xinyu, Yang Ruihan, Lin Yuhang, Li Lin, Li Xiaolan, Hong Yun, Chen Zetao. (2024). A “flexible” compulsory dental basic research course for undergraduate students: a three-year retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04874-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null finding from the same skills comparison: ability of experimental design showed no significant difference (P = 0.1478). The authors interpret both nulls as indicating content needing improvement in future reforms.

> "The gaps in “Management and analysis of research results” (P = 0.2849) and “Ability of experimental design” (P = 0.1478) between Group A and Group B are not significantly different, suggesting that more related courses are needed in further course reforms of the CDBRC."
