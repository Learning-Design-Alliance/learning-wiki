### Exact Path Growth Evidence Rating Summary 2024

Exact Path Growth Evidence Rating Summary. (2024). Edmentum. https://www.edmentum.com/products/exact-path

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental study in Duval County Public Schools, Florida, during the 2023-24 academic year, comparing 6th-grade students who engaged with at least one Exact Path lesson to students who only completed the diagnostic assessment on the Florida Assessment of Student Thinking. The reading analysis included "4,367 in the reading analysis" students; the printed ELA effect size is +0.16.

> "Results indicated that Exact Path had a statistically significant positive effect on both ELA (effect size = +0.16) and Mathematics (effect size = +0.06), with students in the intervention group outperforming their peers."
