## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2019

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Validation and Administration of a Conceptual Survey on the Formalism and Postulates of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Related-measures study in which 44 graduate students enrolled in the first semester of a graduate-level core QM course took the QMFPS at the end of the semester; the figure caption reports correlation coefficient R=0.58. The authors take this as further construct-validity evidence since the final exam covered similar concepts.

> "Figure 3 shows that there is a moderate correlation between students' scores on the QMFPS and their final exam in the graduate-level QM course."
