## Evidence

### Hsieh Shu-Shih 2018

Hsieh Shu-Shih, Tsai Jia-Ren, Chang Shao-Hsi, Cheng Chih-Fu, Sung Yao-Ting, Hung Tsung-Min (2018). The Relations between 3-year Changes in Physical Fitness and Academic Performance in Nationally Representative Sample of Junior High School Students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-34370-2

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted ANOVA in the same nationwide sample tested four muscular-endurance-change groups on BCTJH scores. The article reports the H-H group "outscored the N-H (277.2 ± 95.2; d = 0.08), the H-N (264.2 ± 97.2; d = 0.22), and the N-N (264.7 ± 95.0; d = 0.22) groups".

> "Muscular endurance was also associated with exam scores, F(3,382252) = 827.71; p < 0.05; ηp2 = 0.006). Again, the H-H group (285.2 ± 95.0) outscored the N-H (277.2 ± 95.2; d = 0.08), the H-N (264.2 ± 97.2; d = 0.22), and the N-N (264.7 ± 95.0; d = 0.22) groups."
