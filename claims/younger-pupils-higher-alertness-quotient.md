Age-grade scores for one large city (table VI) were inspected by age within each grade; the article describes the pattern as a "general downward trend" and offers no statistical test or effect size.

> "Excluding the roughness of the curve at the extremities where the number of cases is small, there is a general downward trend of the magnitude of A.Q.'s as the older pupils in the same grade are tested."
