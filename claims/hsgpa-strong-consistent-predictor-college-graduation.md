# HSGPA has a strong, positive relationship with college graduation that does not vary significantly in its linear slope across high schools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For every standard deviation increase in HSGPA, the odds of graduating from college double (odds coefficient = 2.02), and the linear slope does not vary significantly by high school. [→ Allensworth 2019](#allensworth-2019)

## Evidence

### Allensworth 2019

Allensworth, E. M., & Clark, K. (2019). High School GPAs and ACT Scores as Predictors of College Completion: Examining Assumptions about Consistency across High Schools. University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-gpas-and-act-scores-as-predictors-of-college-completion

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical logistic models of six-year college graduation for 17,753 CPS four-year college enrollees, controlling for student background and college institutional variables. The article reports the "odds coefficient = 2.02" for standardized HSGPA and that the linear slope does not vary significantly by high school.

> "The linear component shows that for every standard deviation increase in HSGPA, the odds of graduating from college double (odds coefficient = 2.02) at the point where the quadratic term is zero"
