## Evidence

### Xu Liyuan 2025

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Clinical skill test results from the randomized controlled trial's formative evaluation after 4 weeks of training. Total score improvement was "significantly greater in the experimental group compared to the control group" (Cohen d=1.09, P＜.001); baseline total scores had not differed significantly.

> "Additionally, the improvement in the total scores of the virtual clinical reasoning training system test was significantly greater in the experimental group compared to the control group (mean 42.60, SD 9.56 vs mean 31.63, SD 7.24; F1, 59=25.10; Cohen d=1.09; 95% CI 34.51-39.72; P＜.001)."
