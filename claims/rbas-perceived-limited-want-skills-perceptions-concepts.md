## Evidence

### Adrian Madsen 2015

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Matthew “Sandy” Martinuk, Alexander Bell, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Research-based assessment affordances and constraints: Perceptions of physics faculty. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.01342)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The phenomenographic interview analysis found the Limited Measure family was the largest, discussed by 23 of 24 participants, with subcategories on limitations of understanding, alignment with course, difficulty interpreting results, and wanting assessments of skills, concepts, and student perceptions.

> "Faculty think that RBAs are limited and don’t measure many of the things they care about, or aren’t applicable in their classes. Instead they want assessments to measure skills, perceptions and specific concepts."
