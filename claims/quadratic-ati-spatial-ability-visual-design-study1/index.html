Evidence¶

Tim Kühl 2022¶

Tim Kühl, Benedict C. O. F. Fehringer, Stefan Münzer. (2022). Unifying the Ability-as-Compensator and Ability-as-Enhancer Hypotheses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09650-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multi-level model analysis of the experiment with N = 77 university students performing a mental integration task under static and dynamic visual designs (within-subjects), with mental pathway span as continuous predictor. The quadratic terms were 'significantly different (z = −2.60, p = .009)', showing the inverted-U progression was stronger for the dynamic design.

"The significant quadratic terms for the interaction of mental pathway span with visual design, dynamic and static, b [(mental pathway span × dumOpt)2] = −.08, p < .001 and b [(mental pathway span × dumSub)2] = −.04, p = .036 (Table 2), indicate non-linear relations with negative quadratic trends for both presentation forms."
