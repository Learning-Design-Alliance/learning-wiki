## Evidence

### Shabani 2017

Shabani, S., Ramazani, M., & Alipoor, I. (2017). The Effect of Impulsivity vs. Reflectivity on Reading Comprehension of Iranian EFL Learners. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 5(3), 52-56. http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.5n.3p.52

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing reflective group (n=25) with control group (n=22) on IELTS reading test. The difference was not statistically significant (t(45)=-.285, Sig=0.77), supporting the null hypothesis that reflectivity has no effect on reading comprehension.

> "The reflective group's mean (M=244.2, SD=3.68) is slightly lower than the control group (M=24.5, SD= 3.5)."
