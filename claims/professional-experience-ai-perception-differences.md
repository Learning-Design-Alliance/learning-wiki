## Evidence

### Ciğercci FM and Uygun N 2026

Ciğercci FM and Uygun N (2026). Evaluation of primary school teachers' use and perceptions of artificial intelligence in primary school mathematics instruction: a mixed-methods study. Front. Psychol. 17:1741116. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1741116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparisons by professional experience in the quantitative phase (N = 302). The article reports statistically significant differences with post-hoc contrasts favoring the 6–10 year group; no effect size is printed in the reported text.

> "In general, teachers in the earlier and mid-career groups—especially those with 6ñ10 years of experience—reported more positive scores than teachers with 21 years or more of experience."
