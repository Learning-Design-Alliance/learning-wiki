## Evidence

### McGlashan H.L. 2017

McGlashan H.L., Blanchard C.C.V., Sycamore N.J., Lee R., French B., Holmes N.P. (2017). Improvement in children's fine motor skills following a computerized typing intervention. Human Movement Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.humov.2017.10.013

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on MABC-2 standard scores for 25 children (intervention n = 16, control n = 9) pre and post a four-week typing-game intervention. The article reports "a significant interaction between time and group, F(1,23) = 25.0, p < .001". No effect size was printed for this ANOVA finding, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect); the descriptive gains (SS up 3.78 ± 0.66 vs. down 1.13 ± 0.63) were reported in the surrounding text.
