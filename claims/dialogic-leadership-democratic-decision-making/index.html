Shiza Khaqan 2024¶

Shiza Khaqan, Gisela Redondo-Sama. (2024). A systematic review of the role of dialogic leadership: Characterization and impacts. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100618

q2 · i? · review · r2

Thematic synthesis in the review's results section; the review reports that "every participating individual has agency and power" in dialogic leadership, illustrated by Roma families feeling valued when asked their opinion and by adults each having a say in decisions. No effect size is printed.

"The essence of dialogic leadership is in its democratic style, whereby every participating individual has agency and power (Amestoy et al., 2014; Aubert et al., 2016; G´omez-Cuevas & Valls-Carol, 2022; Redondo-Sama, 2016, 2020; Smith et al., 2017; Valls et al., 2017)."
