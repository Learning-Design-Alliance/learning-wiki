Rachel Langgin, Bradlee Tejeda and Carl-Johan Haster. (2026). GW Explorer: A Beginner's Guide –– Developing a Computational Gravitational-Wave Outreach Curriculum for High School Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.10717](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.10717)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-survey (N = 17) at Las Vegas Academy: 88% familiar with black holes, 41% with neutron stars, 59% had prior coding experience, 94% comfortable with algebra, 59% pursued STEM careers. The undergraduate pre-assessment (N = 20) similarly printed 30% prior GW/LIGO exposure.

> "Table 1 shows that after the short lecture, most students had some familiarity with basic astronomy, including black holes, the solar system, and galaxies, but relatively few encountered neutron stars or GWs."
