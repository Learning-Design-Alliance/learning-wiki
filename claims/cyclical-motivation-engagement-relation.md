### Andrew J. Martin 2023

Andrew J. Martin. (2023). Integrating Motivation and Instruction: Towards a Unified Approach in Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09774-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review reports a study by Martin et al. (2017) using data collected around the Wheel that tested the direction of the motivation-engagement relation. Both directions of prediction were supported, "ultimately indicating a cyclical process." No effect sizes are printed.

> "Harnessing data collected around the Wheel, Martin et al. (2017) tested this contention. They found support for the claim that prior motivation is an impetus for subsequent engagement; but there was also support for a role for prior engagement predicting subsequent motivation, ultimately indicating a cyclical process."
