Andres Karjus 2026¶

Andres Karjus, Janika Leoste, Tiia Õun. (2026). Student Evaluation of Repeated AI Feedback Across a Semester of Writing. arXiv:2607.16115 [cs.CY]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16115 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.16115

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of 2988 essay–feedback–appraisal triplets from one semester course, LLM-assisted annotation validated against human coding (96% match for helpfulness). The corpus shows over half the students found feedback helpful and 99% of feedback scored 4 or 5 on specificity.
