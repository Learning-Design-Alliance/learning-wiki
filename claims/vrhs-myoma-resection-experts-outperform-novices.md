Phase 1 baseline comparison on the Myoma Resection Module among 3 experts and 11 novices; one novice perforation occurred. The article reports the differences between novices and experts were significant (P=0.0153 per the abstract).

> "the experts removing a mean of 97.3% (range, 97% to 98%) of the myoma within the 3-minute exercise time. The novices removed a mean of 66.1% of the virtual myoma (range, 12% to 92%)"
