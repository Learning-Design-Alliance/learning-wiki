The author's classroom anecdote from teaching introductory physics for engineers: a student stably recalled a billiard-ball track-race demonstration into the next semester, but "it was not what he remembered" — the dipped-track ball reaches the end first.

> "As a result, the one on the dipped track reaches the end first. This had to be what Calvin saw. But it was not what he remembered."
