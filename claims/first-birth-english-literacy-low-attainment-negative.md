## Evidence

### Psaki Stephanie R. 2019

Psaki Stephanie R., Soler-Hampejsek Erica, Saha Jyotirmoy, Mensch Barbara S., Amin Sajeda. (2019). The Effects of Adolescent Childbearing on Literacy and Numeracy in Bangladesh, Malawi, and Zambia. Demography. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13524-019-00816-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fixed-effects linear regression analyses of longitudinal data from the MSAS (Malawi) and BALIKA (Bangladesh) studies, restricted to girls in school at baseline who had not yet had a birth. The article reports "a significant negative effect on English literacy" at low attainment and a reversed direction at higher attainment; only coefficient signs and significance markers are printed, so no effect size is available.

> "In MSAS and BALIKA, among those with low levels of grade attainment (less than grade 6), giving birth has a significant negative effect on English literacy. However, for students who reach the last year of primary school or transition to secondary school, this effect reverses direction, and those who have ever had a birth have slightly higher English literacy scores than those who have not had a birth."
