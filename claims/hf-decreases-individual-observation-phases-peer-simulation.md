## Evidence

### Nakayama 2023

Nakayama, N., Ejiri, H., Arakawa, N., & Makino, T. (2023). Role of peer learning and self‐efficacy in parasympathetic activity during the simulation learning process in nursing students. Nursing Open, 10, 552–559. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1321

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational‐comparative study; paired t‐tests compared each phase with Control or Rest. The HF component, an index of parasympathetic activity, "decreased significantly from Phases 1–4 compared with that during the Control" and from Phases 7–9 versus Rest.

> "In Scene 1, the HF component decreased significantly from Phases 1–4 compared with that during the Control. In addition, in Scene 2, the HF component decreased significantly from Phases 7–9 compared with that during Rest"
