# Racial disparities in 2024-25 closure likelihood hold after accounting for enrollment declines and poverty

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a logistic regression of 2024-25 closures, at each enrollment-drop level examined, schools serving predominantly Black students were more likely to close than those serving few Black students. [→ Isaacs 2025](#isaacs-2025)
`q2 i?` For a high-poverty school with a 50% enrollment drop, the predicted closure rate for an entirely Black student body (7.3%) was double that of a school serving no Black students (3.6%). [→ Isaacs 2025](#isaacs-2025)

## Evidence

### Isaacs 2025

Isaacs, J., Kuhfeld, M., Postell, S., & Hashim, A. (2025). Post-Pandemic School Closure Trends: Schools Serving Black Students Disproportionately Closed. NWEA White Paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/post-pandemic-school-closure-trends-schools-serving-black-students-disproportionately-closed/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression predicting 2024-25 closure from enrollment drops 2019-20 to 2023-24, percentage Black enrollment, percentage FRPL, and urbanicity. The paper states "enrollment drops do significantly increase the likelihood of closures" yet racial disparities persist at every enrollment-drop level.

> "the predicted closure rate for a school with an entirely Black student body (7.3%) was double the rate for a school serving no Black students (3.6%)."
