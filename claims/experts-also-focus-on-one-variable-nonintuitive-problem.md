Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Student Expertise in Introductory Physics with Isomorphic Problems, Part I: Performance on Non-intuitive Problem Pair from Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06499)

Problem (1) was given to twenty college physics faculty as reported in the article's analysis (citing the author's earlier study); most admitted little intuition about how final speed depends on the friction coefficient. "Seventeen out of the twenty professors" focused on one variable only.

> "Seventeen out of the twenty professors concentrated almost exclusively on one of the two essential features of the problem, either the frictional force or the time to start rolling."
