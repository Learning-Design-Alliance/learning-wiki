# Perceived learning control enhanced transfer via knowledge retention only for students in the non-generative conditions (provided examples, instruction only), not for example generators

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (4 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the provided-examples condition, all direct paths (PC→KR, PC→KT, KR→KT), the indirect effect and the total effect were significant and positive. [→ Krause-Wichmann 2025](#krause-wichmann-2025)
`q3 i?` In the instruction-only condition, PC→KR and KR→KT were significant but PC→KT was not; the indirect effect was significant, the total effect was not. [→ Krause-Wichmann 2025 (2)](#krause-wichmann-2025-2)
`q3 i?` In the self-generated-examples condition, the direct, indirect, and total effects all failed statistical significance. [→ Krause-Wichmann 2025 (3)](#krause-wichmann-2025-3)
`q3 i?` The indirect effect differed significantly between the example-based conditions, more pronounced with provided examples. [→ Krause-Wichmann 2025 (4)](#krause-wichmann-2025-4)

## Evidence

### Krause-Wichmann 2025

Krause-Wichmann, T., Klopp, E., Stark, L., & Stark, R. (2025). Promoting future teachers' pedagogical knowledge: The role of self-generated vs. provided illustrative examples after instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09694-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multi-group mediation analysis (lavaan, one model per condition) in the randomized experiment; Table 4 prints significant positive standardized paths PC→KR = .60, PC→KT = .27, KR→KT = .45, indirect Std = .27, total Std = .54 in I-PE, indicating "all direct paths were significant and positive".

> "In contrast, for students who learned with instruction followed by provided examples (I-PE), all direct paths were significant and positive."

### Krause-Wichmann 2025 (2)

Krause-Wichmann, T., Klopp, E., Stark, L., & Stark, R. (2025). Promoting future teachers' pedagogical knowledge: The role of self-generated vs. provided illustrative examples after instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09694-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis for the instruction-only condition; higher perceived control related to transfer only indirectly through knowledge retention, with no significant total effect.

> "the direct paths from perceived control to knowledge retention (PC → KR) and from knowledge retention to transfer (KR → KT) were significant, but not the path from perceived control to knowledge transfer (PC → KT). The indirect effect was significant, but the total effect did not reach statistical significance."

### Krause-Wichmann 2025 (3)

Krause-Wichmann, T., Klopp, E., Stark, L., & Stark, R. (2025). Promoting future teachers' pedagogical knowledge: The role of self-generated vs. provided illustrative examples after instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09694-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis for the self-generation condition; neither perceived control nor retention related to transfer, contrary to the hypothesized model.

> "In the condition instruction followed by self-generated examples (I-GE), the results of the mediation analysis did not align with our assumptions: The direct, indirect, and total effects failed statistical significance."

### Krause-Wichmann 2025 (4)

Krause-Wichmann, T., Klopp, E., Stark, L., & Stark, R. (2025). Promoting future teachers' pedagogical knowledge: The role of self-generated vs. provided illustrative examples after instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09694-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wald-test planned contrast of indirect effects across conditions (research question 4); the mediating role of retention was more emphasized with provided than generated examples.

> "The contrasts showed a significant difference between the example-based conditions (I-GE/I-PE), with a more pronounced effect in the condition instruction followed by provided examples (I-PE)."
