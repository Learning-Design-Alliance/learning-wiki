### Vera Montalbano 2012

Vera Montalbano. (2012). On Understanding Statistical Data Analysis in Higher Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1202.1794](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1202.1794)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The author's observation from higher education teaching practice, offered without systematic data: fragmentation of statistics and laboratory content leaves students to assemble data-analysis skills on their own, and the author adds it is not obvious the lack could be recovered in further courses.

> "Undergraduate students usually deal with undestanding data analysis by themselves during the preparation of the final experimental thesis, sometimes missing some important aspect."
