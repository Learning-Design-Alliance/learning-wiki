# Segmenting multimedia instruction into learner-paced segments improves retention and transfer performance with small-to-medium effects

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 56 investigations and 88 pairwise comparisons, people learn better when multimedia instructions are presented in meaningful, coherent learner-paced segments rather than as continuous units, with significant small-to-medium effects for retention and transfer. [→ Rey 2019](#rey-2019)
