## Subclaims
`q2 i?` From first to final draft, the online corrective feedback group reduced sentence structure errors by 47.4%, grammar errors by 70.6% and vocabulary errors by 45.4%, while the conventional group reduced sentence structure by 23.2% and vocabulary by 17.6% with no grammatical improvement. The quote gives the online group's percentage decreases; the conventional group's decreases were 23.2% and 17.6% with no grammar improvement.

> "From the first draft to the final draft it was found that students who received online corrective feedback had a decreased number of errors committed for sentence struct ure (47.4%), grammar (70.6%) and vocabulary (45.4%)."
