## Evidence

### Sánchez Judit 2024

Sánchez Judit, Lesmes Marta, Rubio Margarita, Gal Beatriz, Tutor Antonio S. (2024). Enhancing academic performance and student engagement in health education: insights from Work Station Learning Activities (WSLA). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05478-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis of pre- and post-test grades from the 2019–2020 (n = 268) and 2020–2021 (n = 233) first-year medical student cohorts across three WSLA sessions, revealing "significant differences (p < 0.001) between the pretest and posttest in all the WSLA and academic years analyzed." No effect size is printed.

> "A paired sample Wilcoxon signed-rank test was used to determine the presence of statistically significant differences in the scores obtained. The results, shown in Fig. 3, revealed significant differences (p < 0.001) between the pretest and posttest in all the WSLA and academic years analyzed."
