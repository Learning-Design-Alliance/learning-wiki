## Evidence

### Chloë Marshall 2017

Chloë Marshall. (2017). Montessori education: a review of the evidence base. npj Science of Learning 2:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0012-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Experimental manipulation within one Montessori school serving 3–6-year-olds: supplementary materials were removed from two of three classrooms. The review reports significantly greater gains in the removal classrooms on letter-word identification and executive function.

> "Over a period of 4 months children in the classrooms from which supplementary materials were removed made signi ﬁcantly greater gains than children from the unchanged classroom on tests of letterword identiﬁcation and executive function"

### Chloë Marshall 2017 (2)

Chloë Marshall. (2017). Montessori education: a review of the evidence base. npj Science of Learning 2:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0012-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Same removal experiment: the review reports no significant gains on vocabulary, theory of mind, maths or social problem-solving. The authors acknowledge weaknesses including a small sample (just 52 across the three classrooms) and short duration.

> "although not on measures of vocabulary, theory of mind, maths, or social problem-solving"
