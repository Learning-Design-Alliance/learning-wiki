`q1 i?` The authors state that the effects of the two methods could not be independently quantified and that the small sample size may have amplified Type II errors, reducing statistical power. [→ Lin Wenxue 2025](#lin-wenxue-2025)

## Evidence

### Lin Wenxue 2025

Lin Wenxue, Yu Jian, Peng Xiaoping, Xia Jianfu, Huang Bingchen, Li Rizeng. (2025). Study on the application of progressive training method combined with imagery training method in laparoscopic suturing skills training for resident physicians. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06928-y

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' own stated limitations in the Discussion: the combined application of the two methods is rare and their independent effects and interaction were not quantified; the small sample may amplify Type II errors. This is an author interpretation, not a tested result.

> "However, due to the inability to independently quantify the effects of each method, future studies should further explore the independent effects and interactions of each method. Secondly, the small sample size of this study may lead to an amplification of Type II errors, thereby reducing the power of the statistical results."
