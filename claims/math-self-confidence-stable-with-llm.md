`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the Prolific experiment (N=356): participants' math self-confidence was measured before guidance and after the task. The difference was "not statistically significant with 𝑝 = 0.079"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The average confidence before receiving any guidance is5.04 (SD = 1.41). After they complete the problem-solving task, this confidence is 5.16 (SD = 1.62). The observed difference between these two stages is not statistically significant with 𝑝 = 0.079."
