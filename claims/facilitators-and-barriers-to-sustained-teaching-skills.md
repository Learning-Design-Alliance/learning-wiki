### Mousset RA 2026

Mousset RA, de Vos tot Nederveen Cappel WH, Boonstra JJ, Langers AMJ, Brand PLP. (2026). Time to Refresh: Design and Evaluation of Refresher Training to Sustain Procedural Teaching Skills. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2443

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews conducted at the start of the refresher training (mean duration 14 minutes), coded inductively by one researcher with team consensus. The article reports that "Institutional support emerged as a key facilitator".

> "Institutional support emerged as a key facilitator, consistent with previous research [1120]. Dedicated time for briefings (Set) between trainers and residents facilitated consistent application, whereas lack of time and limited opportunities for endoscopy training hindered development of effective supervision."
