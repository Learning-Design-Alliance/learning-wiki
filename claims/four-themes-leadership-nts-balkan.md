### Hodza-Beganovic R 2025

Hodza-Beganovic R, Berggren P, Edelbring S. (2025). The role of leadership in enhancing non-technical skills in healthcare: a qualitative study in a Balkan context. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-025-01022-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative study using reflexive thematic analysis of data from three workshops in Sarajevo, Pristina, and Podgorica, plus focus groups and semi-structured interviews with healthcare leaders. The analysis "resulted in four themes that captured the key leadership dimensions related to NTS development."

> "Four key themes emerged regarding the role of leadership in the development of NTSs: (1) defining roles and responsibilities, (2) fostering communication and teamwork, (3) promoting readiness for change, and (4) developing leadership competencies."
