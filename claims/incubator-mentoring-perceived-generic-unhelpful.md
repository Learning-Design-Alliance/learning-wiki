# IT entrepreneurs perceive incubator-provided mentoring as generic, formal and unhelpful

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Entrepreneurs report that incubator mentors are professionals rather than entrepreneurs, that incubator mentoring is a formality, and that assigned mentor relationships failed because support was generic and not individualised. [→ Jamshed Memon 2018](#jamshed-memon-2018)

## Evidence

### Jamshed Memon 2018

Jamshed Memon, Mohd Zaidi Abd Rozan, Kamariah Ismail, Agha Jahanzeb. (2018). Mentor Protégé Matchmaking Framework for IT Entrepreneurs: A Qualitative Study. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1808.04724](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1808.04724)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from semi-structured interviews across phases: "mentors provided by the incubator are not entrepreneurs, but professionals who mentor the entrepreneurs". A growth-phase respondent described an assigned incubator mentor as giving the same instructions to everyone, and one conception-phase team wanted business and marketing support but received only technical mentoring.

> "Entrepreneur E5P2 reported that mentors provided by the incubator are not entrepreneurs, but professionals who mentor the entrepreneurs, because it is their job."
