## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Many science students reported that actively animating forces and relations between molecules through movement helped them better understand polymer science concepts. [→ Baljon 2021](#baljon-2021)

## Evidence

### Baljon 2021

Baljon, A.R.C., Alter, J., & Bresciani Ludvik, M. (2021). Embodied engagement with scientific concepts: An exploration into emergent learning. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.03992](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.03992)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative focus-group and reflective-paper study of the shared science-dance sessions; a science student reported that "working out how to move our bodies in a way to portray a polymer's behaviour" accelerated understanding beyond in-class sessions. No effect size is reported.

> "But surprisingly, working out how to move our bodies in a way to portray a polymer's behaviour in certain situations actually accelerated my understanding of the topics themselves."
