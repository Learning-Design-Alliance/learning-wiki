# A collaboration script does not improve objective CRM skills-test performance in simulation-based training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant difference between scripted and control learners on the expert-rated video-based CRM skills test; equivalence was not tested. [→ Zottmann Jan M. 2018](#zottmann-jan-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Zottmann Jan M. 2018

Zottmann Jan M., Dieckmann Peter, Taraszow Tatjana, Rall Marcus, Fischer Frank. (2018). Just watching is not enough: Fostering simulation-based learning with collaboration scripts. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma001181](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001181)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Objective measure: learners analysed a brief expert-designed posttest video case of a resuscitation situation against CRM heuristics; a medical expert rated the written analyses on grades from 1 (excellent) to 6 (failed).

> "There was no significant difference between the experimental conditions regarding performance in the heuristic strategies skills test. Learners in both conditions, with and without script, performed slightly below average, according to the expert rater, t(28.97)=0.47, p=.642, n.s."
