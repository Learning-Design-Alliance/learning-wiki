`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the editorial, drawing on cited fraction-comparison research: children applying a larger-numerator-larger-fraction strategy and those applying a larger-denominator-larger-fraction strategy can show "similar overall accuracy but are based on fundamentally different misconceptions". No new empirical data are reported.

> "Thus, using accuracy as a measure would assign both groups of learners to the same performance group, while their way of reasoning and, therefore, their needs, are very different."
