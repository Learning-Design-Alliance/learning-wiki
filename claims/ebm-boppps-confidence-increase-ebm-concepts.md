## Evidence

### Chen Yaxi 2025

Chen Yaxi, Xiao Zhanqin, Gu Xiang, Lang Qi, Chen Chanxi, Zhang Junhuai. (2025). Effect of the EBM-integrated BOPPPS model on clinical competence and EBM confidence in neurology clerkships for three-year junior college medical clerks. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1676073

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post survey within the EBM-BOPPPS group (n = 47) using the 22-item, 3-point EBM Confidence Scale (Cronbach's α = 0.76); the total rose from "20.1 ± 2.8" to "30.2 ± 3.3" (p < 0.001 per the abstract).

> "The pre-course total score, with a mean of 20.1 ± 2.8 out of a possible 44 points (based on 22 questions, each scoring a maximum of 2 points), showed a significant increase in confidence, as evidenced by the post-course total score mean of 30.2 ± 3.3"

### Chen Yaxi 2025 (2)

Chen Yaxi, Xiao Zhanqin, Gu Xiang, Lang Qi, Chen Chanxi, Zhang Junhuai. (2025). Effect of the EBM-integrated BOPPPS model on clinical competence and EBM confidence in neurology clerkships for three-year junior college medical clerks. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1676073

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Item-level analysis reported in the discussion: only 2 of 10 statistical terms improved, attributed to insufficient medical statistics training, while 8 of 12 EBM concepts improved significantly (Figure 3 reports 8/12 and 2/10 significant at p < 0.05).

> "Out of 10 terms assessed, only 2 showed improved scores, potentially due to the insufficient medical statistics training of medical clerks as previously noted (35). In contrast, out of the 12 terms related to EBM, 8 showed significant improvement in scores"
