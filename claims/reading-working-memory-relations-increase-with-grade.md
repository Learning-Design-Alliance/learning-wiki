## Evidence

### Peng 2020

Peng Peng, Kievit Rogier A. (2020). The Development of Academic Achievement and Cognitive Abilities: A Bidirectional Perspective. Child Development Perspectives. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdep.12352

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A meta-analysis of 197 studies of typically developing individuals (4–80 years old) found reading–working memory relations increased with grade level after controlling for publication type, task type, materials, and bilingual status. The article adds that reading development may strengthen verbal working memory's role in reading.

> "the relations between reading and working memory increased with grade level, even after controlling for confounding variables (i.e., publication type, types of tasks, working memory materials, and bilingual status)"
