## Evidence

### Li Y 2025

Li Y, Cheng S, Liu D, Jiang W. (2025). “Learning, Thinking & Conveying” training with experiential learning in the emergency department for undergraduate nursing students: A quasi-experimental study. Medicine 2025;104:28(e43231). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000043231

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 90-student quasi-experiment, the experimental group's total CTDI-CV rose from 289.00 ± 21.635 to 300.53 ± 22.358 (within-group P < .001); post-training between-group total difference P = .007. The training effect persisted in regression (β = 0.268, P = .012).

> "No difference in pretraining CTDI-CV scores was found between the experimental and control groups but the dimensions of truth-seeking, open-mindedness, analyticity, critical thinking self-confidence, inquisitiveness and total scores were significantly different between the 2 groups following the internship."
