# Masked (automatic) word recognition uniquely predicts oral reading fluency over and above unmasked accuracy and vocabulary in middle-school students

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Roembke Tanja C. 2019

Roembke Tanja C., Hazeltine Eliot, Reed Deborah K., McMurray Bob. (2019). Automaticity of Word Recognition is a Unique Predictor of Reading Fluency in Middle-School Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000279

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression in a study of 56 middle-school students (after exclusions) entered vocabulary, then unmasked task accuracy, then masked accuracy. The article reports that "the masked versions of Find the Picture and Find the Rhyme significantly predicted oral fluency over and above unmasked variants" with ΔR2 values of 0.08 and 0.073.

> "Crucially, the masked versions of Find the Picture and Find the Rhyme significantly predicted oral fluency over and above unmasked variants (masked Find the Picture: ΔR2= 0.08; masked Find the Rhyme: ΔR2= 0.073)."
