## Evidence

### Yue Yang 2025

Yue Yang, Mengyao Guo, Yuxuan Wu, Wally Niu, Emmanuel Corona, Bruce Daniel, Christoph Leuze, Fred Baik. (2025). VR MRI Training for Adolescents: A Comparative Study of Gamified VR, Passive VR, 360◦ Video, and Traditional Educational Video. [doi:10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects study (N=11) using a pre-study anxiety survey (average 3.5 on a 5-point composite scale) and retrospective post-test after the first modality. The article reports "no significant difference between conditions in the anxiety reduction scale"; equivalence was not tested.

> "As demonstrated in fig. 5 (a), there is no significant difference between conditions in the anxiety reduction scale. The average post-intervention anxiety score, averaging all modalities, dropped to 2.6 (“a little” anxious)."
