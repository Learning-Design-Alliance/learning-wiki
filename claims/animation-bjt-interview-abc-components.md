Content analysis of four semi-structured interviews with students in the animation group, conducted at the end of learning and focused on tracing the factors leading to the students' achievements. Categories found in at least three interviews included "Animation reduces the cognitive load imposed on the learner", contributing to organization of knowledge, creating interest in direct and indirect channels, and attracting the student's attention.
