# Secondary students report near-maximum confidence in passive digital tasks but sharply lower confidence in active creation and algorithmic logic

## Subclaims

The Illusion of Competence: Self-Perceived Digital Literacy and AI Readiness Among European Secondary Students. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26010

Cross-sectional multicenter survey (N=243) of students in Spain and Ireland using a 5-point Likert self-efficacy questionnaire; descriptive results show "near-maximum confidence levels" for basic tasks versus much lower scores for active creation skills.

> "For basic functional skills, such as turning on a computer (Q8) or managing privacy (Q23), students reported near-maximum confidence levels (𝑀= 4.88and 𝑀= 4.81, respectively)."
