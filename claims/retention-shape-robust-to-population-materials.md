## Evidence

### Amy E. Wenzel and David C. Rubin 1996

Amy E. Wenzel and David C. Rubin. (1996). One Hundred Years of Forgetting: A Quantitative Description of Retention. Psychological Review. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.103.4.734

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

A narrative review summary of prior retention studies. The article adds that "The testing procedures used do have an influence but, except for the savings, these appear to be relatively minor in practice," suggesting the same function with the same parameters may hold over a range of conditions once initial learning is controlled.

> "In summary, the studies to date indicate that aspects of the participant population and materials have little effect on the shape of the retention function if the level of initial learning is controlled."
