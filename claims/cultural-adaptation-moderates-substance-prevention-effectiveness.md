### Bajpai Tewari 2026

Bajpai Tewari, D., Gautam, U., Jaiswal, V., Pooja, Mishra, A., & Das, P. (2026). Integrated Education Models for Addressing Substance Use Among Schoolchildren: A Scoping Review of School-Based Educational Interventions. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23081073

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis (Section 3.2.3) of kiR-family studies as a moderating factor. The review reports culturally grounded adaptations produced "more favourable substance use attitudes and behaviours," and separately that a cross-national Mexican transfer reduced alcohol and drug use frequency, particularly marijuana use among males. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Culturally grounded adaptations for Mexican descent students produced more favourable substance use attitudes and behaviours, particularly among English-dominant high-risk students [68]."

### Bajpai Tewari 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Same synthesis section, reporting a counter-result within the kiR family: the Acculturation Enhancement version increased substance use expectancies by 8th grade. This mixed evidence qualifies the cultural-tailoring pattern.

> "However, the kiR Acculturation Enhancement version [69] increased substance use expectancies by 8th grade (Table 4)."
