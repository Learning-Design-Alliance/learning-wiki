Palmer 2004¶

Palmer, Susan B.; Wehmeyer, Michael L.; Gipson, Krista; Agran, Martin. (2004). Promoting access to the general curriculum by teaching self-determination skills. Exceptional Children, 70(4), 427-439. https://hdl.handle.net/1808/10978

q2 · i? · causal · r?

An alternating-treatments intervention study with middle school students with intellectual disabilities, in which groups alternately received problem-solving or study planning instruction. The article reports both intervention groups showed significantly more capacity on the targeted skill than the alternating control group; no effect size is printed.
