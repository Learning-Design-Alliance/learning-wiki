Hauke Sandhaus, Maria Teresa Parreira, and Wendy Ju. (2024). Student Reflections on Self-Initiated GenAI Use in HCI Education. LLMs as Research Tools Workshop at CHI conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.01467)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Group interviews conducted by two teaching assistants with students from the Fall 2023 interactive device design course, recruited after final grades. Students described code generation and debugging assistance in §3.3.1, saying GenAI "allows you to be faster with the prototyping and the execution".
