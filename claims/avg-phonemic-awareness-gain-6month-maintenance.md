## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bertoni 2024

Bertoni, S., Andreola, C., Mascheretti, S., Franceschini, S., Ruffino, M., Trezzi, V., Molteni, M., Sali, M. E., Salandi, A., Gaggi, O., Palazzi, C., Gori, S. & Facoetti, A. (2024). Action video games normalise the phonemic awareness in pre-readers at risk for developmental dyslexia. npj Science of Learning 9, 25. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00230-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired bootstrap t-test on phoneme discrimination in the 33 AVG children who returned at follow-up; the post-training to follow-up difference was non-significant, indicating maintenance of the gain.

> "The paired sample t-test upon PD for 1000 bootstrap resamples revealed that there are no statistically signiﬁcant differences between the post-training (mean = −0.390, SD = 1.356) and 6-months follow-up (mean = −0.272, SD = 1.179) sessions"

### Bertoni 2024 (2)

Bertoni, S., Andreola, C., Mascheretti, S., Franceschini, S., Ruffino, M., Trezzi, V., Molteni, M., Sali, M. E., Salandi, A., Gaggi, O., Palazzi, C., Gori, S. & Facoetti, A. (2024). Action video games normalise the phonemic awareness in pre-readers at risk for developmental dyslexia. npj Science of Learning 9, 25. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00230-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired bootstrap t-tests in the same follow-up subsample showed significant PD improvement from pre-training to post-training and from pre-training to the 6-month follow-up, both at p = 0.001.

> "T0-T1: t-test(32) = −5.889, mean difference = −1.706, 95% CI = −2.265/−1.109, p = 0.001; and, T0-T2: t-test(32) = −6.336, mean difference = −1.824, 95% CI = −2.372/−1.221, p = 0.001"
