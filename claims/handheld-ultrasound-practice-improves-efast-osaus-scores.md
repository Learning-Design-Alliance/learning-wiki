## Evidence

### Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor 2026

Platte Lukas Alexander Theodor, Komanek Thomas, Grundmann Carla Davina, Dietrich-Kiep Franziska, Wischermann Jan, Frey Ulrich Hermann. (2026). Blended e-learning with handheld ultrasound devices improves practical competence in eFAST: a randomised controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09054-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the trial's primary outcome comparison (n = 173 analysed; 89 control, 84 intervention), a blinded rater scored a standardised simulator-based eFAST. The intervention group's median modified OSAUS was 16.0 versus 13.0, with rank-biserial effect size r = 0.42 (95% CI 0.29–0.54).

> "The intervention group had a significantly higher median modified OSAUS score than the control group (16.0 [13.0–21.0] vs. 13.0 [11.0–16.0], p < 0.001)"
