### Rogers 2025

Rogers, L. K., Nguyen, T. D., Timmer, M., & Samson, J. (2025). Principal Burnout: A Systematic Review. Working paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED666666

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review coding of 99 studies found 92% examined at least one antecedent, while only 38% examined correlates and 15% examined consequences. "Only 10 studies in our search modeled burnout as a predictor or mediator for some other outcome."

> "nearly all studies in our review (92%) investigated at least one antecedent or determinant of principal burnout"
