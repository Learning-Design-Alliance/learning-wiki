### Bostjan Bubnic 2024

Bostjan Bubnic, Željko Kovačević, Tomaž Kosar. (2024). Can metacognition predict your success in solving problems? An exploratory case study in programming. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3699538.3699593](https://doi.org/10.1145/3699538.3699593)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling (CB-SEM, AMOS 26) in an exploratory case study of students from Croatia (N = 111) and Slovenia (N = 83) tested H1. The path from metacognitive awareness to programming was "positive and significant" (γ1 = 0.21, p < 0.05); no effect size was printed.

> "The findings showed that MCA had a positive and significant impact on PROG (𝛾1 = 0.21, p < 0.05). Hence, we can support our H1."
