## Evidence

### Benjamin W. Dreyfus 2013

Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Julia Gouvea, Benjamin D. Geller, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Chemical energy in an introductory physics course for the life sciences. [doi:10.1119/1.4870391](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4870391)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One of 48 semi-structured interviews with 23 students across the first two years of the course. Betsy had not fully resolved the catabolic/anabolic contradiction at interview time but had identified the conflicting ideas and begun seeking reconciliation; the authors report she made these connections on her own.

> "it feels like all of my classes are contradicting each other all the time, but the physics is kind of helping me pull it all together and understand that different things apply at different times."
