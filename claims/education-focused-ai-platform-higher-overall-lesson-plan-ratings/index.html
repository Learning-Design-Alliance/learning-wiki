Karaismailoglu 2026¶

Karaismailoglu, F., Surmeli, H., & Yildirim, M. (2026). Suitability of Artificial Intelligence Supported Lesson Plans from the Perspective of Science Education Experts. Journal of Science Education and Technology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-026-10342-4

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison of eleven experts' mean ratings of two AI-generated lesson plans across eight quality criteria (Table 3). The study reports "C2 received numerically higher average scores than C1 across all eight criteria," with differences of +1.4, +1.2, and +1.0 points; no inferential tests were run.

"From this expert panel, C2 received numerically higher average scores than C1 across all eight criteria. The largest numerical differences were observed in 'Duration' (+ 1.4 points), 'Customization to context' (+ 1.2 points), and 'Attention grabbing' (+ 1.0 points)."
