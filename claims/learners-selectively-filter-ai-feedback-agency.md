Thematic analysis of reflection reports and interviews. Of 114 codes related to critical rejection of feedback, Socratic groups showed relatively higher frequencies; of 446 complete-adoption codes, the C3 group accounted for 122 instances, described as highly task-oriented behavior.

> "These data suggest that learners did not adopt AI-generated feedback passively; instead, they actively filtered and selectively incorporated suggestions into their revisions."
