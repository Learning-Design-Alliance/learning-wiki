## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2010

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2010). Using Analogy to Solve a Three-Step Physics Problem. PERC Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515228](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515228)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison groups in calculus-based and algebra-based introductory physics courses solved the putty problem with no scaffolding. The article reports the comparison group “averaged 6.4/10 and 2.3/10 respectively” in the two courses, calling the problem “almost impossible to the algebra-based student.”

> "The average scores of the comparison group students in the two courses were 6.4/10 and 2.3/10 respectively, indicating that the putty problem is challenging for the calculus-based students and almost impossible to the algebra-based student."
