# Social support partially mediates the relationship between empathy and prosocial behavior

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a structural equation model, social support significantly mediated the empathy–prosocial behavior link (95% CI [0.008, 0.130]), with the mediating effect accounting for 7.64% and the direct effect 92.36%. [→ Fu W 2022](#fu-w-2022)

## Evidence

### Fu W 2022

Fu W, Wang C, Chai H, Xue R. (2022). Examining the relationship of empathy, social support, and prosocial behavior of adolescents in China: a structural equation modeling approach. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-022-01296-0

`q3 · i?`

SEM analysis with bootstrap test (bootstrap = 5000) in Mplus8.3 on the 1171-adolescent data; model fit indexes were acceptable (χ2/df = 2.434, RMSEA = 0.035). The article reports the "mediating effect accounted for 7.64%" and the direct effect 92.36%. No standardized effect size for the indirect path is printed.

> "the 95% con ﬁdence interval of “empathy → social support → prosocial behavior ” path was [0.008, 0.130], and the con ﬁdence interval did not include 0, indicating that the mediating effect of social support was signi ﬁcant"
