q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Cluster randomized controlled trial of the Pre-K Mathematics intervention scaled to the state level across pre-K programs representing low-income families in California. The study reports the intervention "remains effective at the state level, with positive and statistically significant effects" with an effect size of .30 (p < .01) on the ECLS-B Mathematics Assessment.

"We find that Pre-K Mathematics remains effective at the state level, with positive and statistically significant effects (effect size on the Early Childhood Longitudinal Study, Birth Cohort Mathematics Assessment = .30, p < .01)."
