# Prompts increased learners' self-efficacy during learning within the prompted group but did not produce between-group differences at mid-learning or post-learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Self-efficacy increased significantly over the three measurement points in the prompted group (ηp2 = .26) but not in the control group, producing a time × condition interaction (ηp2 = .07). [→ Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020](#nadja-gentner-tina-seufert-2020)
`q3 i?` Despite the within-group increase, prompted and non-prompted learners did not differ in self-efficacy 20 minutes into learning or immediately after learning. [→ Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020 (2)](#nadja-gentner-tina-seufert-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020

Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert. (2020). The Double-Edged Interactions of Prompts and Self-efficacy. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09227-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA (condition between-subjects, time before/during/after learning within-subjects) on self-efficacy ratings. The time × condition interaction was significant, ηp2 = .07; within the prompted group self-efficacy rose significantly (F(2,66) = 11.91, p < .001, ηp2 = .26) but not in the control group (F(2,66) = 0.39, ns).

> "the analysis indicated an interaction effect between time and condition F(2,134) = 4.70, p =. 0 1 ,ηp2 = .07 (medium effect), ε = .886."

### Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020 (2)
