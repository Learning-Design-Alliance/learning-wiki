## Evidence

### Bedyńska S 2018

Bedyńska S, Krejtz I and Sedek G (2018) Chronic Stereotype Threat Is Associated With Mathematical Achievement on Representative Sample of Secondary Schoolgirls: The Role of Gender Identification, Working Memory, and Intellectual Helplessness. Front. Psychol. 9:428. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00428

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling with moderated multiple mediation tested interaction paths. The article reports "the interaction of chronic stereotype threat and gender identity ( β =−0.288, p < 0.05)" as a significant predictor of FAWMT, and separately reports the interaction term predicting helplessness (β = 0.267, p < 0.05).

> "there was a signiﬁcant relation between FAWMT and its predictors: helplessness (β =−0.124, p < 0.05), gender identity (β = 0.276, p < 0.05), and the interaction of chronic stereotype threat and gender identity ( β =−0.288, p < 0.05)"
