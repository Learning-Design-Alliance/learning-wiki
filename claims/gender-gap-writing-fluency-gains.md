## Evidence

### DeMichele 2015

DeMichele, M. (2015). Improv and ink: Increasing individual writing fluency with collaborative improv. International Journal of Education & the Arts, 16(10). http://www.ijea.org/v16n10/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-study observation reported in the discussion of Study 2 results. The author notes the gender gap is "based on a sample too small to draw firm conclusions" and links it to NAEP data showing female students write at higher proficiency levels.

> "Also consistent with Podlozny’s (2000) findings on transference, both male and female students showed clear increases, however in all categories, female students showed greater improvement than male students in both studies."
