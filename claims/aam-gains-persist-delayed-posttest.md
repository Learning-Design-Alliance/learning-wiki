## Evidence

### Kayaoglu 2013

Kayaoglu, M. N. & Caylak, N. (2013). What is needed for correct pronunciation: a model or a concern? Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 53, 269-290. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1060386

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Delayed post-test administered four weeks after the immediate post-test to all 36 participants. The comparison of pre-test and delayed test scores showed a statistically significant difference (t=2.679, p=.016), suggesting students retained their newly acquired pronunciation of inter-dental fricatives on formal assessment tasks.

> "The one-sample t-test in Table 9 displays the comparison between the pre-test and the delayed test, and the data shows a statistically significant difference at the specified .05 level, t= 2.679, p= .016, 95% CI [1.3940, 11.7237]."
