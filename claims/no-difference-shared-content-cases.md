## Evidence

### Middeke Angélina 2018

Middeke Angélina, Anders Sven, Schuelper Madita, Raupach Tobias, Schuelper Nikolai. (2018). Training of clinical reasoning with a Serious Game versus small-group problem-based learning: A prospective study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203851

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Case-by-case analysis of the formative key feature examination in the same prospective self-selected cohort. The article reports "no significant difference in performance levels" for the four remaining cases, including the two shared-content cases; the authors read this as non-inferiority of EMERGE despite eightfold lower case depth.

> "There was no significant difference in performance levels between the EMERGE group and the PBL group in the other four cases. This includes the cases ‘sarcoidosis’ and ‘Hodgkin lymphoma’ which had been discussed in both instructional formats during the training phase."
