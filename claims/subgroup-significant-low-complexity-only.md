## Evidence

### Hu Shuai 2026

Hu Shuai, Du Fei, Fang Can, Zheng Xiyan, Shi Xianjie, Zhou Zheng. (2026). Effectiveness of an AI-augmented teacher feedback model in improving medical students' clinical documentation skills: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09953-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis stratified by disease complexity (low: 0–5 points, n = 23; medium-to-high: ≥ 6 points, n = 27) with ANCOVA performed separately for each stratum. The effect was "significantly higher" in the low-complexity group (p = 0.007) but not in the medium-to-high complexity group (p = 0.798).

> "For the low-complexity group, the adjusted post-rotation mean of the AI group was significantly higher than that of the traditional group (p = 0.007). However, in the medium-to-high complexity group, although the adjusted mean of the AI group was higher than that of the traditional group, the difference did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.798)."
