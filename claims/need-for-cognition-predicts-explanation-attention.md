## Evidence

### Cristina Conati 2019

Cristina Conati, Oswald Barral, Vanessa Putnam, Lea Rieger. (2019). Toward Personalized XAI: A Case Study in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. Preprint submitted to the JAI – Special Issue on Explainable Artificial Intelligence. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.04464)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Among the 24 explanation-condition participants who accessed explanations, ANCOVAs ran each user characteristic as a single covariate on eye-tracker-based Explanation Attention. N4C was significant, F(1,22)=8.51, p=.008, ηp2=.28; "Participants with a high level of N4C show higher values of Explanation Attention than participants with low N4C."

> "We found that Need for Cognition (N4C from now on) has a statistically significant effect on Explanation Attention with a medium effect size (F(1,22)=8.51, p=.008, ηp2=.28)"
