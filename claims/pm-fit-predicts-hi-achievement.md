## Evidence

### Azizan Che’ Rozaniza 2021

Azizan Che’ Rozaniza, Roslan Samsilah, Abdullah Maria Chong, Asimiran Soaib, Zaremohzzabieh Zeinab, Ahrari Seyedali, Tchounwou Paul B. (2021). Does a Person-Environment-Fit Promote the Academic Achievement of Hearing-Impaired Students in Malaysian Polytechnics? The Mediating Effects of Satisfaction and Adjustment. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413381

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM structural model analysis of survey data from 195 hearing-impaired students in five Malaysian polytechnics offering the Special Skills Certificate program. The structural path from personality-major fit to academic achievement was significant, with the article reporting "=0.443, t = 4.931, p-value = 0.000".

> "Furthermore, the route coefficients show a connection between the personality-major fit (=0.443, t = 4.931, p-value = 0.000) and academic accomplishment."
