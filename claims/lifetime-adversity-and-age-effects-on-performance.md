## Evidence

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025

Reubenson Alan A., Riddell Hugh, Brewer Margo L., Ng Leo, Gucciardi Daniel F. (2025). Individual differences and self-regulatory factors are credible determinants of physiotherapy student performance on clinical placement: Insights from a measurement burst design study. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-person (level 1) covariate models from the same multilevel SEM, using a lifetime adversity inventory (number and frequency of event categories). The article reports the number of adverse events "were positively associated with the professional dimension"; only unstandardised estimates printed.

> "Regarding professional performance, age and frequency of adverse lifetime events were negatively associated with supervisor assessments of the professional dimension of the APP, whereas the number of adverse events were positively associated with the professional dimension of the APP."

### Reubenson Alan A. 2025 (2)

Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10453-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-person covariate effect on the clinical APP dimension in the same models. The article reports "older participants performed more poorly"; no standardised effect size printed.

> "Conversely, age negatively predicted mean levels of clinical performance, indicating that older participants performed more poorly."
