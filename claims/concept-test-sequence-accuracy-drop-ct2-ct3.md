## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu. (2016). Improving Students’ Understanding of Quantum Mechanics by Using Peer Instruction Tools. 2010 PERC Proceedings. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05461)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Classroom data from one junior-senior level QM class of 18 responding to concept test question 2, which asked which 1D potential energy functions allow both bound and scattering states. The article reports "About 72%" chose the correct models.

> "About 72% of the students in a class of 18 students chose the correct models, i.e., the 1D finite square potential energy well and the 1D delta function potential energy well."

### Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu 2016 (2)

Chandralekha Singh and Guangtian Zhu. (2016). Improving Students’ Understanding of Quantum Mechanics by Using Peer Instruction Tools. 2010 PERC Proceedings. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05461)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Performance on CT3, the difficult question in the sequence, presented with graphical rather than verbal representation of the potentials and requiring application of prior knowledge to novel situations. "Only 33% of the students (out of 18)" answered correctly and 50% wrongly included option (3).

> "Only 33% of the students (out of 18) chose the correct answer C and 50% of them incorrectly believed that option (3) also allows both bound and scattering state wavefunctions."
