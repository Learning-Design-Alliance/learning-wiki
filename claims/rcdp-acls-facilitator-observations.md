## Evidence

### Blanchard E 2022

Blanchard E, Booker D, Peterson DT, Carter T. (2022). Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice: Application to Adult Advanced Life Support. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11269

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative facilitator observations of 84 junior anesthesiology residents across eight course offerings, assessed against checklists during the simulations. Facilitators "noted that by the end of the simulations, residents were communicating more effectively." No quantitative performance measure was reported.

> "Facilitators noted that by the end of the simulations, residents were communicating more effectively, working more cohesively as a team, and appropriately delegating roles."
