## Evidence

### Deutschmann Michael W 2013

Deutschmann Michael W, Yunker Warren K, Cho John J, Andreassen Meri, Beveridge Shari, Bosch John Douglas. (2013). Use of a Low-fidelity simulator to improve trans-nasal fibre-optic flexible laryngoscopy in the clinical setting: a randomized, single-blinded, prospective study. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/1916-0216-42-35

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial; the omnibus two-way ANOVA with interaction tested simulation and repeat endoscopy effects. The article reports "No interaction between simulation and repeat endoscopy was detected" with ANOVA p values of 0.10, 0.77, and 0.38 in Table 2; no effect size is printed.

> "A two-way ANOVA with interaction was used to explore the effect of both simulation and endoscopy. No interaction between simulation and repeat endoscopy was detected. Time to adequate visualization of the glottis, the number of mucosal contacts, and the percentage of time adequate visualization of the airway were maintained were not affected (Table 2)."

### Deutschmann Michael W 2013 (2)

Deutschmann Michael W, Yunker Warren K, Cho John J, Andreassen Meri, Beveridge Shari, Bosch John Douglas. (2013). Use of a Low-fidelity simulator to improve trans-nasal fibre-optic flexible laryngoscopy in the clinical setting: a randomized, single-blinded, prospective study. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/1916-0216-42-35

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group-level comparison across all 65 analyzed learners; Table 4 prints p values of 0.36 to 1.0 across nine measures. The article reports no significant differences and prints no effect size.

> "Data from the simulation group and control group, irrespective of the first and second endoscopy were compared. No significant differences in any of the quantitative or qualitative measures described were identified (Table 4)."
