Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017). Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306

Moderator analysis of outcome construct (Table 3): retention k = 243 (g = 0.63), transfer k = 11 (g = 0.53), mixed retention and transfer k = 15 (g = 0.37). "there was no statistically significant difference between retention (g = 0.63) and transfer (g = 0.53) outcomes."
