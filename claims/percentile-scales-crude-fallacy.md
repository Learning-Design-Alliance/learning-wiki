# Percentile-difference scales are crude and embody a fundamental fallacy because percentile units are not equal intervals

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r1` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The article argues that scales built on percentile differences are crude and fallacious, and that raw percentile values from different tests cannot be equated, translated or combined. [→ Wells 1915](#wells-1915)
`q1 i?` Adjacent percentile intervals differ in size, unlike equal-unit scales: the gap between the 0th and 10th percentiles is not equal to the gap between the 50th and 60th. [→ Wells 1915 (2)](#wells-1915-2)
