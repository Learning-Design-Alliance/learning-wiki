## Evidence

### Mohsenzadeh Habibeh 2025

Mohsenzadeh Habibeh, Farsi Zahra, Afaghi Effat. (2025). The effects of personalized video feedback of basic cardiac life support on knowledge and skills levels in undergraduate nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06935-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA within each group of the randomized trial showed significant within-group improvement over time for skills (experimental F = 313.479; control F = 138.260, both P < 0.0001) and knowledge (F = 120.522 and F = 168.028), i.e., an "ascending trend" in both groups.

> "In both groups, the BCLS skills mean values of the nursing students had an ascending trend immediately, one week, and one month after training (p < 0.001)"
