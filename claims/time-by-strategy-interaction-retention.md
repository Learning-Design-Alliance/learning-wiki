## Evidence

### Salari Mohsen 2018

Salari Mohsen, Roozbehi Amrollah, Zarifi Abdolvahed, Tarmizi Rohani Ahmad. (2018). Pure PBL, Hybrid PBL and Lecturing: which one is more effective in developing cognitive skills of undergraduate students in pediatric nursing course?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1305-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mixed between-within subjects ANOVA over posttest and delayed posttest for the three groups. Table 5 reports significant T × G interactions for overall (F = 46.072, p = .000, η² = .500), higher-order (F = 31.157, η² = .404), lower-order performance (F = 10.249, η² = .182), and instructional efficiency (F = 8.06, η² = .15); Figures 2a–d show COTL efficiency decreasing while PBL efficiency increased.

> "The rates of the decreases were greater for the COTL group from posttest to delayed posttest than for the PPBL and HPBL strategy groups."

### Salari Mohsen 2018 (2)

Salari Mohsen, Roozbehi Amrollah, Zarifi Abdolvahed, Tarmizi Rohani Ahmad. (2018). Pure PBL, Hybrid PBL and Lecturing: which one is more effective in developing cognitive skills of undergraduate students in pediatric nursing course?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1305-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same mixed ANOVA, mental effort measure: the T × G interaction was not significant (F = 1.48, p = .232, η² = .03 as printed in Table 5), while a substantial main effect for time showed mental effort increasing across the two periods in all groups.

> "However, it was found that there was not a significant interaction effect between the times and instructional strategy group for mental effort."
