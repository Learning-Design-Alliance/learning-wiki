# A school SAT preparation course improved SAT scores at one Texas school (Filizola, 2008; domain average effect size 0.28), driven by the mathematics subtest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` In a quasi-experimental matched comparison of 24 students, intervention students scored statistically significantly higher than matched comparison students on SAT scores (domain average effect size 0.28, improvement index +11), with the mathematics subtest showing the largest effect (effect size 0.67, +18, p .008). [→ ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016](#actsat-test-preparation-and-coaching-programs-2016)

## Evidence

### ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study at a parochial Texas school where 17 students who paid a $350 fee took eight 4-hour SAT preparation sessions and were matched on gender, ethnicity, and GPA to non-participants. The SAT Math row reports intervention 498.16 (SD 96.24) versus comparison 460.17 (SD 56.48), effect size 0.67, +18, p .008; the WWC reports the domain average as effect size 0.28, +11, statistically significant.
