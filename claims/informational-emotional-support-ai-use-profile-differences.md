# Self-efficacy profiles differ in AI use for informational support (η² = .28) and emotional support (η² = .18)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Informational support use differed significantly across profiles, F(2,99) = 18.92, p < .001, η2 = .28, with High profile students reporting more frequent use to locate, synthesize, and organize academic information (M = 4.51). [→ Suriá-Martínez R 2026](#suria-martinez-r-2026)
`q2 i3` Emotional support use also differed significantly, F(2,99) = 10.47, p < .001, η2 = .18, with the High profile highest (M = 3.96) and the Low profile lowest (M = 2.76). [→ Suriá-Martínez R 2026 (2)](#suria-martinez-r-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Suriá-Martínez R 2026

Suriá-Martínez R, García-Castillo F, López-Sánchez C and García del Castillo JA (2026). Between perceived competence and artificial intelligence: academic self-efficacy profiles in university students with motor disabilities. Front. Educ. 11:1956192. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1956192

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA on the informational support dimension of the Perception of AI Utility Questionnaire across the three profiles (N = 102). The quote fragment "F(2,99) = 18.92, p < .001, η2 = .28" grounds the large effect; means were 4.51, 3.74, and 2.83.

> "Similarly, AI use for informational support differed significantly across the three latent profiles, F(2,99) = 18.92, p < .001, η2 = .28."

### Suriá-Martínez R 2026 (2)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`
