## Evidence

### Dorta‐Guerra Roberto 2019

Dorta‐Guerra Roberto, Marrero Isabel, Abdul‐Jalbar Beatriz, Trujillo‐González Rodrigo, Torres Néstor V. (2019). A new academic performance indicator for the first term of first‐year science degrees students at La Laguna University: a predictive model. FEBS Open Bio 9, 1493–1502. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.12707

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Forward multiple linear regression on the Mathematics cohort (N = 79); adjusted R² values as printed in Table 7, with overall model F = 66.48***. The article states the variables "together explained 71.6% of the variance in the API".

> "In particular, for the Mathematics degree, the final model included the high school grade point average, and the grades obtained in the modality subject and in the specific phase. These variables significantly predicted the API and together explained 71.6% of the variance in the API. Concretely, the first model, with only the high school grade point average as independent variable, accounted for 63.2% of the variance in the API."

### Dorta‐Guerra Roberto 2019 (2)

Dorta‐Guerra Roberto, Marrero Isabel, Abdul‐Jalbar Beatriz, Trujillo‐González Rodrigo, Torres Néstor V. (2019). A new academic performance indicator for the first term of first‐year science degrees students at La Laguna University: a predictive model. FEBS Open Bio 9, 1493–1502. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.12707

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Forward regression on the Biology cohort (N = 113); final model adjusted R² = 42.8% (F = 21.98***), GPA alone 20.6%. The article notes "this value is smaller than that in the previous analysis".

> "With respect to the Biology degree, the high school grade point average and the grades obtained in the specific phase, in the modality subject and in the Foreign Language were selected by the forward selection method. These variables explained 42.8% of the variance in the API. It is worth noting that this value is smaller than that in the previous analysis, although still quite significant."
