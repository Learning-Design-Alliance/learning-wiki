## Evidence

### Javier Pulgar 2021

Javier Pulgar, Alexis Spina, Carlos Ríos. (2021). Investigating how students collaborate to generate physics problems through structured tasks. arXiv. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of 295 minutes of transcribed audio from four groups in an undergraduate Newtonian mechanics course, coded in NVivo 12 with Cohen's Kappa of 0.94. The article reports decision-making themes occupied "approximately 75% of the message units analyzed" (Table II: 598 message units total).

> "From Table II, readers might notice that groups spent most of their time addressing themes related to Decision Making, which included approximately 75% of the message units analyzed."
