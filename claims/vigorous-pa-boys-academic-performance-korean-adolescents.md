# Vigorous physical activity 2–4 times a week is associated with greater odds of average or above-average academic performance in Korean adolescent boys, but ≥5 times a week with lesser odds

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Boys doing vigorous PA twice, three, or four times a week had greater odds of reporting average or above-average academic performance than boys doing none, with ORs from 1.198 to 1.287. [→ So Wi-Young 2012](#so-wi-young-2012)
`q2 i?` Boys doing vigorous PA 5 or more times a week had lesser odds of average or above-average academic performance (OR = 0.886). [→ So Wi-Young 2012](#so-wi-young-2012)

## Evidence

### So Wi-Young 2012

So Wi-Young. (2012). Association between physical activity and academic performance in Korean adolescent students. BMC Public Health 12, 258. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-12-258

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression analysis of KYRBWS-V 2009 data for 39,612 boys, adjusted for age, BMI, parents' education level, and family income, comparing each vigorous PA frequency with no vigorous PA. Twice-weekly vigorous PA gave "OR = 1.198" and thrice-weekly "OR = 1.287"; 5+ times weekly gave OR = 0.886 (p = 0.020).

> "those who participated in vigorous PA twice a week (OR = 1.198, 95% CI = 1.080–1.329, p = 0.001), thrice a week (OR = 1.287, 95% CI = 1.156–1.433, p < 0.001), 4 times a week (OR = 1.265, 95% CI = 1.117–1.433, p < 0.001)"
