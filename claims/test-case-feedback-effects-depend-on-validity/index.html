Hasnain Heickal and Andrew Lan 2026¶

Hasnain Heickal and Andrew Lan. (2026). A Classroom Study of LLM-Generated Feedback Intervention in Introductory Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08807 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.08807

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Categorization of AI-generated test cases in the classroom dataset as valid, false-positive, or no-effect, with descriptive associations between validity category and pass rates. The authors attribute lower performance to false-positive and no-effect cases.

"We find that 66% of generated test cases are valid, 24% are false positives, and 10% have no effect. Submissions receiving valid test cases exhibit the highest average pass rates"
