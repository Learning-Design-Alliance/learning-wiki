`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive between-group comparisons at T3 in the KaziAfya trial (n = 559): age and BMI differed significantly across arms, while stunting prevalence was comparable (p = 0.61) and height and weight did not differ significantly. No effect size was printed for age.

> "The mean age significantly differed between groups (p < 0.001), with the PA + MMNS group being the oldest (12.4 years) and the placebo group the youngest (11.1 years)."
