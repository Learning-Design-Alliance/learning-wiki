Knowing its name, not its nature: word association mapping of student AI cognition and evidence-based micro-credential design in Turkish higher education. q2 · i? · associational · r2

Frequency analysis of the full WAT corpus (N = 436; 1376 coded responses) found that the conceptual vocabulary of AI governance was entirely absent. The article states "This is not a low-frequency result; it is a structural zero"; no effect size applies to an absence.

"bias, transparency, privacy, accountability, regulation, fairness - none of these appear in any form across 65 canonical codes and 1376 total responses. This is not a low-frequency result; it is a structural zero."
