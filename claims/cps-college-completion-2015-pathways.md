# Six-year college completion among the CPS class of 2015 varied sharply by enrollment pathway: 55.8% for immediate four-year enrollees, 32.5% for immediate two-year enrollees, and 7.8% for delayed/non-enrollees

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among the CPS graduating class of 2015, 51.1% of immediate four-year enrollees completed a bachelor's degree within six years, 9.9% of immediate two-year enrollees completed a bachelor's degree plus 22.6% an associate degree or certificate, and only 2.8% of delayed/non-enrollees completed a bachelor's degree. [→ Usher 2023](#usher-2023)

## Evidence

### Usher 2023

Usher, A., Mahaffie, S., & Nagaoka, J. (2023). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2022. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort tracking of the CPS graduating class of 2015, the most recent cohort with six years of available college data, disaggregated by immediate enrollment status. More than two thirds (67.5%) of immediate two-year enrollees had not completed any credential within six years.

> "55.8% of students who immediately enrolled in a four-year college and 32.5% of students who immediately enrolled in a two-year college completed a bachelor’s degree, associate degree, or certificate within six years."
