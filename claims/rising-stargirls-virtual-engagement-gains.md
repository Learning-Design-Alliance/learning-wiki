### Maya Silverman 2024

Rising Stargirls: Benefits of a Creative Arts-Based Approach to Astronomy Education for Middle-School Girls from Underrepresented Groups. Astronomy Education Journal, Vol. 04, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.32374/2024.4.1.089ra

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective pre/post survey analysis of three virtual Rising Stargirls workshops (2021–2023), matched responses, analyzed with Hake's gain, normalized change, and the nonparametric effect size dAw. The item showed "a moderate increase ⟨g⟩ = 0.4906" and "a large effect sizedAw = 1.0700".

> "Collectively analyzing all virtual workshop responses (NB, NA) = (66, 66) to the question ‘I talk to my family and friends about science’ showed a moderate increase ⟨g⟩ = 0.4906 and cave = 0.4958 (with 3 responses dropped from the calculation), as well as a large effect sizedAw = 1.0700."

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`
