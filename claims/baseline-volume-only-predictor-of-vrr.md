## Evidence

### Chytiris S 2024

Chytiris S, Teliti M, Croce L, Coperchini F, Grillini B, Cerutti M, Fonte R, Magri F, Rotondi M. (2024). Proficiency in performing radiofrequency ablation procedure for non-functioning benign thyroid nodules: a qualitative rather than quantitative matter. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2024.1399912

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the 179-procedure cohort with VRR as dependent variable and chronological order, age, composition, location, baseline volume, and applied energy as covariates. "The only independent predictor of 6-month VRR was nodule baseline volume"; chronological order had β = 0.01, p = 0.899.

> "The only independent predictor of 6-month VRR was nodule baseline volume (β = -0,31, 95% confidence interval = -0,501, -0,123, p = 0,001)."
