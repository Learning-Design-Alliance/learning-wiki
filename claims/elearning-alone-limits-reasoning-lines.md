## Evidence

### Major ME 2020

Major ME, Ramaekers SPJ, Engelbert RHH, Van der Schaaf M. (2020). Preparing undergraduate students for clinical work in a complex environment: evaluation of an e-learning module on physiotherapy in the intensive care unit. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02035-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Result of thematic analysis across the three themes from focus groups and expert interviews in the proof of concept study. Students described feeling overwhelmed by situations such as "wires becoming undetached, alarms going off, or dealing with emergency situations in the ICU".

> "Higher level clinical reasoning skills, handling of lines and wires and dealing with out-of-textbook situations could not be achieved with the e-learning module alone."
