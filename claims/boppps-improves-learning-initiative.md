## Evidence

### Zhu Jing 2025

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects pooling of four studies (595 participants) with continuous learning-initiative measures; pooled effect size SMD = 0.73 (95% CI 0.48–0.98), with I² = 0.5 reported in Table 5.

> "Four studies employed continuous variables. These studies demonstrated a significant improvement in learning initiative within the BOPPPS group compared to the control group (SMD 0.73, 95% CI 0.48–0.98; P < 0.001; Table 5)."

### Zhu Jing 2025 (2)

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects pooling of three studies (579 participants) with dichotomous learning-initiative variables, effect size OR = 3.65 (95% CI 1.17–11.41), I² = 0.56, favoring BOPPPS.

> "In three studies utilizing dichotomous variables, a significant difference was also observed between the two groups (OR 3.65, 95% CI 1.17–11.41; P = 0.03; Table 5)."
