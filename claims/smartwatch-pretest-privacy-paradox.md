## Evidence

### Meredydd Williams 2019

Meredydd Williams, Jason R C Nurse, Sadie Creese. (2019). Smartwatch Games: Encouraging Privacy-Protective Behaviour in a Longitudinal Study. [doi:10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of pretest concern questionnaires (e.g., 80% concerned about location sharing, 90% about app data collection) against 18 days of behaviour logs from 10 participants. The authors conclude "concerns and behaviour appear misaligned" in the smartwatch environment.

> "Based on these results, concerns and behaviour appear misaligned. Our smartwatch game seeks to mitigate this issue."
