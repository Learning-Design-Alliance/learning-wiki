## Evidence

### Kane 2013

Michael T. Kane. (2013). Validating the Interpretations and Uses of Test Scores. Journal of Educational Measurement. https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the article's opening statement of the argument-based approach. The article argues that the same scores can support different interpretations of differing plausibility, so "it is the proposed score interpretations and uses that are validated and not the test or the test scores."

> "Validity is not a property of the test. Rather, it is a property of the proposed interpretations and uses of the test scores."
