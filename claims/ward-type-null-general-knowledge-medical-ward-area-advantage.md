## Evidence

### Fortunka Kamila et al 2024

Fortunka Kamila et al. (2024). Knowledge and Training Needs in Nosocomial Infection among Hospital Staff in the City of Kielce, Poland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9243232

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ward-type comparison within the 635-nurse survey. The null on general knowledge coexists with a significant area-I advantage for medical wards (p=0.031) and roughly one-third insufficient knowledge in both ward types.

> "The general level of knowledge did not turn out to be statistically significantly differentiated by the type of ward (p=0.993). However, it should be noted that an insufficient level of knowledge was found in every third respondent from the conservative and surgical ward (97; 34.04% vs 119; 34.00%)"
