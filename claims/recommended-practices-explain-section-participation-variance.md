## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adding recommended practices reduced section-level variance by 15% for CBT pretests and 45% for CBT posttests. [→ Nissen 2018](#nissen-2018)

## Evidence

### Nissen 2018

Nissen, J. M., Jariwala, M., Close, E. W., & Van Dusen, B. (2018). Participation and Performance on Paper- and Computer-Based Low-Stakes Assessments. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1186/s40594-018-0117-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-018-0117-4)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HGLM Model 2 added a level-3 recommended-practices variable (0 to 4) for the CBT conditions. The article states this "large decrease in variance" indicates the practices "accounted for a large proportion of the diﬀerence in participation rates between sections on the assessments administered as CBTs".

> "The variance in Model 2 is 15% lower (from 0.820 to 0.700) for CBT pretests and 45% lower (from 1.220 to 0.670) for the CBT posttests than in Model 1."
