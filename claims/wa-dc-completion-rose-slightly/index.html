Kristen Hengtgen 2023¶

Kristen Hengtgen, Hector Biaggi. (2023). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641932

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of Washington State Report Card data on completion of at least one DC course, SY 2018-19 vs 2021-22, by race and income. The brief reports "increased by 4 percentage points" for historically underserved groups versus 3 points for all students; two-thirds of public high school students complete a DC course.

"Completion rates for Black and Latino students and students from low-income backgrounds increased by 4 percentage points, which is similar to the 3 percentage point increase for all students."
