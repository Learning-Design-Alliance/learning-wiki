### Rauf A 2026

Rauf A, Backhaus J, Hammer S, König S. (2026). Comparing the Weighted Gain Score and a Rasch-Based Approach for Estimating Learning Outcomes in Medical Education: Quantitative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/75516

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on the simulated dataset (n=1000) with randomly assigned teaching format and learning disposition, showing no significant interaction for WGS at any µ-value or for RLG, consistent with random group assignment.

> "In the simulated dataset, no significant interaction between teaching format and learning disposition was observed when learning gains were calculated using the WGS, regardless of the µ-value applied (F1,996=0.39; P=.53; partial η²<0.001)."
