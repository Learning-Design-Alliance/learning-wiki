Evidence¶

Putwain 2025¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Test anxiety, emotion regulation, and achievement: Lagged and contemporaneous reciprocal relations. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://repository.essex.ac.uk/40904/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

CCLPM results for the acceptance and self-blame strategies showed "Test anxiety showed positive contemporaneous effects on acceptance and self-blame, at T2 and T4, but not vice versa", indicating these strategies follow rather than cause anxiety.

"Test anxiety showed positive contemporaneous effects on acceptance and self-blame, at T2 and T4, but not vice versa."

Putwain 2025 (2)¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Pekrun, R. (2025). Test anxiety, emotion regulation, and achievement: Lagged and contemporaneous reciprocal relations. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://repository.essex.ac.uk/40904/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the models for refocus on planning and positive reappraisal, higher test anxiety preceded lower use of these strategies at both waves, with no reverse paths, per the printed CCLPM coefficients.

"Test anxiety showed negative contemporaneous effects on refocus on planning and positive reappraisal, at T2 and T4, but not vice versa."
