## Evidence

### Kim Mi Ji 2020

Kim Mi Ji, Park Hae Yean, Yoo Eun-Young, Kim Jung-Ran. (2020). Effects of a Cognitive-Functional Intervention Method on Improving Executive Function and Self-Directed Learning in School-Aged Children with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Single-Subject Design Study. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/1250801

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Parent-rated BRIEF was administered at baseline, post-intervention, and follow-up in the A-B-A study; GEC T-scores "decreased in the GEC after the intervention from 77 (98%) to 72 (97%) for participant 1", with parallel decreases for participants 2 and 3.

> "T-scores decreased in the GEC after the intervention from 77 (98%) to 72 (97%) for participant 1; from 74 (97%) to 67 (94%) for participant 2; and from 79 (98%) to 67 (94%) for participant 3."
