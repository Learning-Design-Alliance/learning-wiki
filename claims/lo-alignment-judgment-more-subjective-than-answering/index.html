lo-alignment-agreement-1¶

Yumou Wei, John Stamper, and Paulo F. Carvalho. 2026. Generate-Then-Validate: A Novel Question Generation Approach Using Small Language Models. In LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2026), Bergen, Norway. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785100

q3 · i? · design · r2

In the same 64-MCQ evaluation, judges made binary Yes/No judgments on whether each MCQ tested a specified learning objective under control (original LO) and treatment (different LO) conditions. Agreement was moderate: "Fleiss' kappa = 0.58".

"The seven human judges achieved moderate agreement with each other (Fleiss' kappa = 0.58), indicating that while there was some consensus among the human judges, there were also notable differences in their judgments regarding the alignment of MCQs with learning objectives."

lo-alignment-agreement-2¶

Yumou Wei, John Stamper, and Paulo F. Carvalho. 2026. Generate-Then-Validate: A Novel Question Generation Approach Using Small Language Models. In LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2026), Bergen, Norway. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785100

q3 · i? · design · r2

Average pairwise Cohen's kappa with human judges on LO alignment was 0.65 (SE = 0.02) for Gemini versus 0.62 (SE = 0.08) for Phi-2, against a human-human baseline of 0.58. The article reports agreement statistics, not effect sizes.

"Gemini achieved slightly better alignment with human judges (mean Cohen's kappa = 0.65, SE = 0.02)."
