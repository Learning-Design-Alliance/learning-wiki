## Evidence

### Golos Anat 2019

Golos Anat, Tekuzener Esti. (2019). Perceptions, expectations and satisfaction levels of occupational therapy students prior to and after practice placement and comparison of practice placement models. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1762-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA across the three placement levels found differences only for professional skills, with a large effect size (Partial η2 = .172). Post-hoc Bonferroni comparisons showed first-level students (n = 64) scored significantly lower than second (n = 53) and third (n = 37) level students.

> "students' scores in the first placement level (n = 64; pre-score: M = 3.16, SD = .55; post-score: M = 3.92, SD = .42) were significantly lower (p < .001) than those of students in the other two placement levels"
