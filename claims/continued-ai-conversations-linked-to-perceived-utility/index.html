Evidence¶

Sperber 2025¶

Sperber, Lisa; MacArthur, Marit; Minnillo, Sophia; Stillman, Nicholas; Whithaus, Carl. (2025). Peer and AI Review + Reflection (PAIRR): A human-centered approach to formative assessment. Computers and Composition 76. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compcom.2025.102921

q2 · i? · design · r2

Spring post-intervention survey (n=386) and student interviews (n=8) added questions on continued conversations; the authors interpret these findings as indicating continuers perceived more utility in the feedback.

"35% of students continued a feedback conversation. Among students who continued AI feedback conversations, 71% preferred AI and peer feedback together, while among those who did not continue the conversation, only 50% preferred AI and peer feedback together"
