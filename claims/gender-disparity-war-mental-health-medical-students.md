## Evidence

### Korda Mykhaylo 2025

Korda Mykhaylo, Shulhai Arkadii, Shevchuk Oksana, Shulhai Oleksandra, Shulhai Anna-Mariia. (2025). Psychological well-being and academic performance of Ukrainian medical students under the burden of war: a cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1457026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of DASS-21 severity levels between 180 male and 596 female students; "the level of anxiety was 1.54 times higher (p = 0.001), and the level of stress was 1.43 times higher (p = 0.001) among the female students". IES-R data showed women 1.58 times more likely to experience PTSD symptoms (p = 0.001).

> "Comparing gender prevalence of anxiety and stress symptoms, it was found that the level of anxiety was 1.54 times higher (p = 0.001), and the level of stress was 1.43 times higher (p = 0.001) among the female students due to severe and extremely severe manifestations"
