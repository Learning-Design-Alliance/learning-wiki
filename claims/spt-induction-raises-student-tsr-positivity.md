# Students of treatment teachers perceived more positive relationships with their teachers (Cohen's d = 0.82)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Focal students of treatment teachers reported more positive teacher-student relationships than focal students of control teachers (Cohen's d = 0.82, 95% CI 0.29-1.37). [→ Gehlbach 2021](#gehlbach-2021)

## Evidence

### Gehlbach 2021

Gehlbach, H., Mascio, B., & McIntyre, J. (2021). Social perspective taking: A professional development induction to improve teacher-student relationships and student learning. Journal of Educational Psychology (in press). https://osf.io/suhvj/

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered student-level regression (controlling for gender and grade) from the results robustness table, using student-reported TSR-positivity from the school-wide Panorama survey. The estimate was Cohen's d = 0.82, "0.82 (0.29, 1.37)", robust across most specifications though the FIML estimate was smaller (0.28).
