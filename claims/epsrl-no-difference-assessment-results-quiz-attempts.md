# Using the ePSRL Management System shows no significant difference in mean assessment results or mean attempts per quiz between users and non-users

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no significant difference in mean assessment result between students who used the system and those who did not. [→ Khiat 2022](#khiat-2022)
`q2 i?` There was no significant difference in mean attempts per quiz between students who used the system and those who did not. [→ Khiat 2022 (2)](#khiat-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Khiat 2022

Khiat, H., & Vogel, S. (2022). A self-regulated learning management system: Enhancing performance, motivation and reflection in learning. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 19(2), 43-59. https://doi.org/10.53761/1.19.2.4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of mean summative assessment scores between the 76 intervention and 79 control students. The test "was not significant with U = 805.500 and p = 0.542 > 0.05"; no effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "The Mann–Whitney U test was not significant with U = 805.500 and p = 0.542 > 0.05. Thus, there was no significant difference in the mean assessment result between participants who used the ePSRL Management System and those who did not."

### Khiat 2022 (2)

Khiat, H., & Vogel, S. (2022). A self-regulated learning management system: Enhancing performance, motivation and reflection in learning. Journal of University Teaching & Learning Practice, 19(2), 43-59. https://doi.org/10.53761/1.19.2.4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of mean attempts per quiz (students had up to six attempts per summative assessment) between intervention and control students. The test "was not significant with U = 821.000 and p = 0.612 > 0.05"; no effect size was printed.

> "The Mann–Whitney U test was not significant with U = 821.000 and p = 0.612 > 0.05. Thus, there was no significant difference in the mean attempts per quiz between participants who used the ePSRL Management System and those who did not."
