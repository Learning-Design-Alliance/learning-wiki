## Evidence

### Kuckuck Karl 2018

Kuckuck Karl, Schröder Hanna, Rossaint Rolf, Stieger Lina, Beckers Stefan K, Sopka Sasa. (2018). Comparison of a newly established emotional stimulus approach to a classical assessment-driven approach in BLS training: a randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-017705

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Voluntary 6-month follow-up test in the randomised trial; only 65 participants' performance data were collected, so the comparison rests on a small, self-selected subset. Compression depth also showed no significant group difference (P=0.14).

> "After 6 months, the volunteering participants from the EG showed an adequate mean CR according to the ERC criteria (119.0/min ±12.3), while participants from the AG performed slightly over the ERC recommendations on average (121.5/min ±11.6), however revealing no statistically significant difference (P=0.47)."
