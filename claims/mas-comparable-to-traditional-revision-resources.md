Bartlett Richard D., Radenkovic Dina, Mitrasinovic Stefan, Cole Andrew, Pavkovic Iva, Denn Peyton Cheong Phey, Hussain Mahrukh, Kogler Magdalena, Koutsopodioti Natalia, Uddin Wasima, Beckley Ivan, Abubakar Hana, Gill Deborah, Smith Daron. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison in the same three-arm RCT (n = 9 per group). Mean score changes were 4.0, 9.1 and 7.3 raw marks for no resources, traditional resources and MAS respectively, but the ANOVA on improvements "was not statistically significant (p = 0.059)".

> "However, when assessed by one-way ANOVA, the differences between the three groups was not statistically significant (p = 0.059)."
