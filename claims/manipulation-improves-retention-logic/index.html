Tessa Eysink 2002¶

Tessa Eysink, Sanne Dijkstra, Jan Kuper. (2002). The role of guidance in computer-based problem solving for the development of concepts of logic. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1016018210897

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the same 72-student experiment in Tarski's World, the manipulation variable was tested against a retention test administered after two weeks of non-instruction. The abstract reports significant post- to retention-test results; no effect size is printed.

"Manipulation, which was operationalised by giving the learners a visual representation in which geometrical objects could be manipulated, yielded significant results from post- to retention test."
