### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2023

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Keegan Shea Tonry, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2023). Analyzing Physics Majors’ Specialization Low Interest Using Social Cognitive Career Theory. [doi:10.1119/perc.2023.pr.zohrabi_alaee](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2023.pr.zohrabi_alaee)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study of nine physics majors at one large private research university who gave the lowest interest ratings on a 0–10 scale. The article reports "a more negative perception of theory (T)" with average rating 2.2 versus 4.2 for computation; no inferential test is printed.

> "Based on numerical ratings, students had a more negative perception of theory (T) (ratings: 1,2,2,2,4; Av. = 2.2) compared to the computation (C) (ratings: 3,3,5,5,5; Av. = 4.2)."
