## Evidence

### Bigras Catherine 2019

Bigras Catherine, Kairy Dahlia, Archambault Philippe S. (2019). Augmented feedback for powered wheelchair training in a virtual environment. Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-019-0482-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Transfer test in a hospital hallway with a real PW, two days after simulator training, for participants of both groups (three participants excluded for technical problems). Time was extracted from video recordings; collisions were too few to analyze.

> "Overall, participants improved their real PW driving by 7.5 s two days after training. The mixed effects model found that this effect was significant (p < .001). However, the effect of feedback was not significant (p = .202)."
