## Evidence

### Acaray Ali 2026

Acaray Ali. (2026). How digital stress affects academic procrastination and subjective well-being: mediating effect of ego depletion. BMC Psychology, 14, 449. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04164-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the 768-student survey. Four of the five digital stress subdimensions were printed with positive correlations to ego depletion, e.g. "connection overload (r = 0.24, p < 0.01)"; no availability-stress correlation is printed in this sentence.

> "There was a positive relationship between digital stress dimensions and ego depletion (approval anxiety (r = 0.17, p < 0.01); FOMO (r = 0.20, p < 0.01); connection overload (r = 0.24, p < 0.01); online vigilance (r = 0.23, p < 0.01))."
