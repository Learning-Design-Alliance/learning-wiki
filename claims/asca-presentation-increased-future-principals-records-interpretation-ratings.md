## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Future principals who received the ASCA National Model presentation rated interpreting student records/test results as significantly more appropriate after the intervention (d = 0.73). [→ Bringman 2010](#bringman-2010)

## Evidence

### Bringman 2010

Bringman, N. M., Mueller, S. M., & Lee, S. M. (2010). Educating future school principals regarding the role of professional school counselors. Journal of School Counseling, 8(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ885032

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Table 1 paired t-test for the experimental group (n = 18): interpreting student records/test results pre-test mean 5.67 (SD 2.99) rose to post-test mean 8.11 (SD 2.37), t = -2.91, p < .05, effect size d = 0.73. This is the only appropriate activity showing a significant increase.
