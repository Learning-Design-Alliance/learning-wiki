# Few EBP intervention studies used high-quality instruments, and no high-quality instrument covers all five EBP steps

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Of 24 previously developed instruments used, only 6 (25%) were rated high quality (≥3 types of established validity evidence). [→ Albarqouni Loai 2018](#albarqouni-loai-2018)
`q3 i?` High-quality instruments were used in 14 of 52 skills studies, 14 of 39 knowledge studies, and 8 of 35 attitude studies; none measuring self-efficacy or behaviour were high quality. [→ Albarqouni Loai 2018](#albarqouni-loai-2018)

## Evidence

### Albarqouni Loai 2018

Albarqouni Loai, Hoffmann Tammy, Glasziou Paul. (2018). Evidence-based practice educational intervention studies: a systematic review of what is taught and how it is measured. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1284-1

`q3 · i?`

Psychometric quality assessment of the 24 instruments used in the 85 included studies, based on Shaneyfelt et al. criteria (inter-rater reliability, objective measures, ≥3 validity types). The review reports "6 (25%) instruments were rated as high quality" and none evaluated all five EBP steps.
