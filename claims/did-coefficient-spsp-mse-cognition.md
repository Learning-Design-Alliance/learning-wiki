### Singano Violeth E. 2024

Singano Violeth E., Millanzi Walter C., Moshi Fabiola. (2024). Effect of standardized patient simulation-based pedagogics embedded with lecture in enhancing mental status evaluation cognition among nursing students in Tanzania: A longitudinal quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05562-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-model difference-in-differences analysis of repeated pre/post measurements in the quasi-experiment, with intervention as a fixed effect and a 5% significance level. The article reports the "coefficient for the Difference-in-Differences (D-I-D) analysis" as 4.6950 with p < 0.0001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The coefficient for the Difference-in-Differences (D-I-D) analysis, comparing the intervention group to the control arm, was 4.6950. This suggests that the change in cognition from baseline to end line was significantly higher in the intervention group compared to the control group."
