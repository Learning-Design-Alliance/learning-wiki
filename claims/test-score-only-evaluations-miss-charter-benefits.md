`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The report's summary draws a methodological implication from its attainment findings: the large positive results "demonstrate that evaluations limited to test scores may fail to capture important benefits of charter schools." This is the authors' interpretation of their own findings.

> "The large positive results suggest promise for multi-grade charter high schools (and perhaps charter high schools more generally) and demonstrate that evaluations limited to test scores may fail to capture important benefits of charter schools."
