Evidence¶

Whipple 1914¶

Whipple, G. M., & Curtis, J. N. (1914). Preliminary Investigation of Skimming in Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=preliminary+investigation+of+skimming+in+reading

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Auxiliary Experiment 3 (Rates of Reading), in which five observers read eight educational reprints by each of five methods with stop-watch timing. The article reports the relative speed order with skimming fastest: "maximal silent 60, and skimming 40" relative to normal aloud as 100.

"If we take the time for normal aloud, the slowest speed, as 100, then maximal aloud would be 83, normal silent 74, maximal silent 60, and skimming 40."
