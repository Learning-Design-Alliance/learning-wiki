Evidence¶

Thomas-Tate et al. 2013¶

Shurita Thomas-Tate, Carol McDonald Connor, & Lakeisha Johnson. (2013). Design Experiments: Developing and Testing an Intervention for Elementary School-age Students who use Non-mainstream American English Dialects. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563056

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pairwise comparisons from the same randomized design experiment. The DVAR contrast versus control was marginal (p = .060) and the editing contrast versus editing-only was significant at the α = .075 threshold, with "mean difference = 10.17, p = .069". No effect sizes printed.

"had lower post-intervention DVARs (mean difference = 9.66, p = .060, see Figure 1, bottom). Students in the editing + dialect awareness condition also achieved significantly higher editing scores than did students in the editing alone intervention (mean difference = 10.17, p = .069)"
