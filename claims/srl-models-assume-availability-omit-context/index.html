Theoretical review of SRL frameworks synthesized in Table 1 (Zimmerman; Winne & Hadwin; Pintrich; Efklides; Boekaerts; Corno; the authors' own model). The authors conclude models "implicitly include an assumption that all learners are available to learn and focus only on how individuals should learn."

"Third, and most relevant to our discussion of the ecological modeling of the learner, very few models include references to the broader social context in which the task is situated"
