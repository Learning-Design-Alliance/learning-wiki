# SRL activity only partially mediates the effect of SRL interventions on achievement in higher education

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Meta-analytic structural equation modeling provides evidence for only partial mediation of the effect of SRL interventions on achievement by SRL activity, contrary to the assumption of full mediation. [→ Jansen 2019](#jansen-2019)

## Evidence

### Jansen 2019

Jansen, R. S., van Leeuwen, A., Janssen, J., Jak, S., & Kester, L. (2019). Self-regulated learning partially mediates the effect of self-regulated learning interventions on achievement in higher education: A meta-analysis. Educational Research Review, 28, 100292. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2019.100292

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

MASEM analysis pooling correlations across studies of SRL interventions, SRL activity, and achievement in higher education tested the mediation model; the article reports that "the results only provide evidence for partial mediation" rather than full mediation. No effect size for the mediation is printed in the available text.

> "Contrary to popular belief, the results only provide evidence for partial mediation."
