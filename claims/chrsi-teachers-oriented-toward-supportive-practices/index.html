Stevens 2006¶

Stevens, W. D., with Kahne, J. (2006). Professional Communities and Instructional Improvement Practices: A study of small high schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/professional-communities-and-instructional-improvement-practices-study-small-high

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative fieldwork during the 2004-05 school year in seven CHSRI schools, including interviews and staff meeting observations, found supportive practices dominated. The report states there were "very few formal and structured discussions in teacher groups around instructional improvement."

"Most discussions about instruction happened informally between pairs of teachers and provided short-term assistance with things such as problematic student behavior or what content to cover in class."
