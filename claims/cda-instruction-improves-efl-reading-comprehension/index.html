Evidence¶

Javadi 2019¶

Javadi, J., & Mohammadi, M. (2019). The Effect of Critical Discourse-Based Instruction on Iranian English Major Students’ Reading Comprehension. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 33-52. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201136

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-test/post-test experimental study with repeated-measures ANOVA on 112 Iranian EFL undergraduates in control and experimental groups over 18 sessions; the article reports the experimental group's post-test mean rose to 79.32 from 71.89, and the article states "increased significantly in the post test compared to the pre-test".

"The mean scores of students in the experimental groups (both intermediate and advanced) have increased significantly in the post test compared to the pre-test. It means that the students' reading comprehension after passing the course through critical teaching has increased."
