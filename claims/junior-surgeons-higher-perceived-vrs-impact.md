Comparison of residents/fellows (group B, n = 128) versus senior surgeons (group A, n = 41) using Student t tests. "Junior surgeons rated the impact of VRS training on their surgical practice higher than that of senior surgeons" (5.8 vs. 4.5; p = 0.032); seniors also spent more simulator time (151 vs. 28 h; p = 0.04).

> "Junior surgeons rated the impact of VRS training on their surgical practice higher than that of senior surgeons on a scale of 0 (no impact) to 10 (major impact, 5.8 vs. 4.5; p = 0.032)."
