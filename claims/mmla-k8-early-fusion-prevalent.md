The review's fusion analysis (Table 3) categorized studies using Chango et al.'s (2022) early/late/hybrid scheme. Only Moon et al. (2022) explicitly reported decision-level (late) fusion. Seven studies neither reported nor were observed to use fusion, though some used data triangulation, keeping modalities separate.

> "The results showed that seven studies applied early fusion techniques, with one explicitly reported (Olsen et al., 20202) and six observed based on the reported data analysis process."
