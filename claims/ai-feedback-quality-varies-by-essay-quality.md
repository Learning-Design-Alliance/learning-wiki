## Evidence

### Steiss 2023

Steiss, Jacob; Tate, Tamara; Graham, Steve; Cruz, Jazmin; Hebert, Michael; Wang, Jiali; Moon, Youngsun; Tseng, Waverly; Warschauer, Mark. (2023). Comparing the Quality of Human and ChatGPT Feedback on Students' Writing. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Comparing+the+Quality+of+Human+and+ChatGPT+Feedback+on+Students+Writing

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA across three essay-quality groups found AI accuracy ratings of 4.42, 4.05, and 3.31 for low, average, and high essays; "the accuracy of AI feedback declined as the quality of the underlying essay rose." Prioritization means were 2.98, 3.22, 2.69; supportive tone 3.56, 4.1, 4.16.

> "Specifically, the accuracy of AI feedback declined as the quality of the underlying essay rose, with ratings of 4.42, 4.05, and 3.31 for low-, average-, and high-quality essays, respectively."
