Authors' experience-based report from mentoring students in a graduate evidence analysis course; no empirical data collected. The framework is said to help "identify common problems in students’ evolving competence" and separate skill deficits from meta-strategic deficits.

> "We have found that using this framework helps to identify common problems in students’ evolving competence of the evidence analysis process toward production of a SRMA and provides a way to determine whether the problems are a result of inadequacy with a discrete evidence analysis skill or a result of a deficit in meta-strategic thinking"
