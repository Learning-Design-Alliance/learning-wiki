## Evidence

### Stauffer Tobias 2026

Stauffer Tobias, Gutiez Blanca, Babst Reto, Lohmeyer Quentin, Meboldt Mirko. (2026). Augmented reality enhanced physical simulation for femoral nailing training. International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-026-03646-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retention analysis with only 18 of 23 participants returning after a minimum one-day interval: insertion time was "remained stable at 68.0 s" and still significantly lower than run 1. However, fluoroscopic image count differences were no longer significant at retention, and long-term retention was not evaluated.

> "During the retention session, the average completion time remained stable at 68.0 s (median 69.1 s) and was still significantly lower than in the first run (p < 0.001)."
