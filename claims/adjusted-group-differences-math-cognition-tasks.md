# After controlling for domain-general abilities and reading, MLD children show deficits on all mathematical cognition tasks while LA-Severe children show a specific counting-string intrusion deficit

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A repeated measures ANCOVA with IQ, working memory, processing speed, and reading covariates yielded significant group and group-by-task effects, indicating more than one underlying mechanism. [→ Geary 2012](#geary-2012)

## Evidence

### Geary 2012

Geary, D. C., Hoard, M. K., & Bailey, D. H. (2012). Fact Retrieval Deficits in Low Achieving Children and Children with Mathematical Learning Disability. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 45(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219410392046

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated measures analysis of covariance on standardized intercept scores for intrusion errors, d-prime, number line, and procedural competence, covarying verbal and nonverbal IQ, working memory components, RAN RT, word reading, and retrieval error frequency. The significant interaction suggests, per the article, more than one mechanism.

> "The results revealed significant group, F(3,206) = 32.95, p<.0001, and group by task effects, F(9,618) = 93.37, p<.0001."
