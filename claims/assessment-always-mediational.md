## Evidence

### Peter Smagorinsky 1995

Peter Smagorinsky. (1995). The Social Construction of Data: Methodological Problems of Investigating Learning in the Zone of Proximal Development. Review of Educational Research, 65(3), 191-212. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170682

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument drawing on Moll and Greenberg (1990) and Gardner (1983): the article argues that 'the instruments of data elicitation are never neutral' and that what is measured may be cultural compatibility between learner and mediation rather than progress toward a telos. Support is argumentative, not empirical.

> "The ZPD's developmental nature suggests that the instruments of data elicitation are never neutral but, rather, are always mediational."
