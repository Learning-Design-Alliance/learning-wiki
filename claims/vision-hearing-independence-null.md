Schmitz, J., Abbondanza, F., Marianski, K., Luciano, M. & Paracchini, S. (2023). Identification of loci involved in childhood visual acuity and associations with cognitive skills and educational attainment. npj Science of Learning, 8:25. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00175-w

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Bivariate partial Pearson correlation in the ALSPAC sample overlapping the authors' previous hearing study. The analysis found no evidence for a correlation between visual acuity and hearing threshold (r = −0.02), suggesting independence of hearing and vision phenotypes.

> "Partial Pearson correlation analysis adjusting for sex and age did not reveal evidence for a correlation between visual acuity and hearing threshold ( r(4927) = −0.02, 95% CI = [−0.05, −0.005], p = 0.104)"
