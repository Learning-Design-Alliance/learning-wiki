## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experienced (second- or third-year) residents achieved a higher median overall normalized FVS score than first-year novices (median 160.0 vs 57.5, P = 0.005). [→ Bangura Marie 2025](#bangura-marie-2025)

## Evidence

### Bangura Marie 2025

Bangura Marie, Ganyaglo Gabriel, Kupualor David, Boateng Alex, Sepenu Perez, Koranteng Isaac, Lawrence Emma R., Rooney Deborah, Schmidt Payton. (2025). Evaluating Fundamentals of Vaginal Surgery for Use Among Obstetrics and Gynecology Residents in Accra, Ghana. International Urogynecology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00192-025-06343-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 34 residents at a teaching hospital in Accra, Ghana. Participants performed five timed FVS tasks; median overall normalized scores were compared by experience level with the Mann–Whitney U test. The article reports "160.0 (95.8, 312.0) for experienced participants (P = 0.005)".

> "The median (IQR) overall normalized score for the FVS hands-on skills assessment was 57.5 (6.3, 113.8) for novice and 160.0 (95.8, 312.0) for experienced participants (P = 0.005)."
