## Evidence

### Sijmkens 2024

Sijmkens, E., De Cock, M., & De Laet, T. (2023). Scaffolding Students' Use of Metacognitive Activities Using Discipline- and Topic-Specific Reflective Prompts. *Metacognition and Learning, 18*(3), 811–843. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09363-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the prior study cited as background for the current investigation. The authors note that self-reported questionnaires are not very accurate measurements for metacognition, citing Veenman et al. (2006), which motivated the current study's use of a strategy score as an indirect measure.

> "In our previous work (Sijmkens et al., 2022), we did not find any significant relationship between students' interaction with the DLC and their metacognitive abilities as measured by the self-reported Motivated Strategies for Learning Questionnaire (Pintrich & De Groot, 1990) at the end of the course."
