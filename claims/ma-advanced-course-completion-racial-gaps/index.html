Kristen Hengtgen and Hector Biaggi 2024¶

Kristen Hengtgen and Hector Biaggi. (2024). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641974

q2 · i? · associational · r2

EdTrust analysis of Massachusetts DESE and NCES data on 11th and 12th graders, reported by race/ethnicity for SY 2018-19 and 2021-22. The analysis found "only half of Latino students" completed at least one advanced course versus 64% of all students, with Asian students highest (85%/83%).

"In 2021-22, only half of Latino students and slightly more than half of Black students completed at least one advanced course, compared to 64% of all students. There were no sizable differences in completion rates from 2018-19 to 2021-22."
