Study 4 used the LISA data set with math and German grades from Grades 6–8 and self-concepts from Grades 5 and 8, estimating a 2I/E model with prior self-concepts (assessed in Grade 5, before the first achievement measure) as additional predictors. The article reports the relations "were shown when prior self-concepts were controlled for."
