## Evidence

### Lisa Bardach 2024

Lisa Bardach, Aki Schumacher, Ulrich Trautwein, Enkelejda Kasneci, Maike Tibus, Franz Wortha, Peter Gerjets & Tobias Appel. (2024). Taking another look at intelligence and personality using an eye-tracking approach. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00252-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' overall interpretation comparing the gaze-based model (35.91% variance) with the personality models (3.18% traits, 7.67% facets) in the same 182-adult sample: "behavioral data strongly out-performed“traditional” questionnaire data (personality traits and facets) in predicting intelligence test performance".

> "Overall, it must be acknowledged that behavioral data strongly out-performed“traditional” questionnaire data (personality traits and facets) in predicting intelligence test performance."
