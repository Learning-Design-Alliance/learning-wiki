### Shulruf Boaz 2018

Shulruf Boaz, Damodaran Arvin, Jones Phil, Kennedy Sean, Mangos George, O'Sullivan Anthony J., Rhee Joel, Taylor Silas, Velan Gary, Harris Peter. (2018). Enhancing the defensibility of examiners' marks in high stake OSCEs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1112-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Maximum-likelihood factor analysis with oblimin rotation plus Cronbach's alpha was applied to each of the nine OSCE stations (Table 2); the article reports items "were loaded on a single factor with a high level of reliability", with alphas from .796 (O&G) to .853 (Surg.B) as printed in the table.

> "Table 2 demonstrates that the responses to assessment criteria (items) within each station were loaded on a single factor with a high level of reliability, which confirms the suitability of these data for the OBM2."
