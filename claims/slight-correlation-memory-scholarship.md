## Evidence

### Pyle 1911

Pyle, W. H. (1911). Retention as related to repetition. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 2*(6), 311–321. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073818

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

The author reports informal correlational checks in his own classes, finding only a slight correlation of "something like .30 by the Pearson formula" (r = .30) between memory and class standing, and attributes the modest size to habits of study and other factors. The article notes some prior investigators found positive correlation and others did not.

> "We have several times tried to find how the matter stood in our own classes, and have found only a slight correlation—something like .30 by the Pearson formula."
