### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Student difficulties with determining expectation values in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.075)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-ended quiz and exam question Q2 at the University of Pittsburgh over several years, answered by 127 UG and 65 G students after traditional instruction and graded with rubrics with inter-rater reliability better than 95%. The article reports "only 29% of the UG students and 52% of the G students" correct.

> "Table II shows that only 29% of the UG students and 52% of the G students were able to obtain a correct expression for the expectation value of 𝑄 for state |Ψ〉 using a basis of eigenstates |𝑞𝑛〉 and eigenvalues 𝑞𝑛."
