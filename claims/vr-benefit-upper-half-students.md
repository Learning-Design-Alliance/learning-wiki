## Evidence

### Philip Blanco 2019

Philip Blanco, Gur Windmiller, William Welsh, and Sean Hauze. (2019). Lessons Learned from Teaching Astronomy with Virtual Reality. ASP Conference Series, Vol. 524, Astronomical Society of the Pacific. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.12393](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.12393)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory subgroup analysis plotted in Figure 2, comparing average change in quiz score for students above versus below the median overall course grade across the three cohorts. The authors label this conclusion tentative and note the small sample sizes; no effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "We were also interested in which students found the VR activity most helpful, so despite the small sample sizes, we split each cohort into two groups by course grade. From this we tentatively conclude that the students who benefitted the most were in the VR cohort and in the upper 50% of the class."
