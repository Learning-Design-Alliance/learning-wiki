Evidence¶

Özcan and Kiliç 2017¶

Mehmet Fatih Özcan, Latife Kirbaşoğlu Kiliç. (2017). The Effects of Animation Supported 5E Model on Teaching 'Indicative and Subjunctive Moods' in 7th Grade Turkish Lesson. Universal Journal of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051407

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Content analysis of student interview responses in the experimental group to the question "Did using animation in grammar topics affect your success? Why?". Positive themes included permanence, repetition, understanding, imagination, interest, attention and participation; one student reported distraction from the computer.

"29 of the students stated that the animation use affected their success positively and one of the students stated it affected negatively. That 97% of the students stated positively can be interpreted that the animation enables learning to be permanent and repeatable"
