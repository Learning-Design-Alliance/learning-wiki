Ciğercci FM and Uygun N 2026¶

Ciğercci FM and Uygun N (2026). Evaluation of primary school teachers' use and perceptions of artificial intelligence in primary school mathematics instruction: a mixed-methods study. Front. Psychol. 17:1741116. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1741116

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Independent-samples t-tests in the quantitative phase (N = 302) compared perception scores by gender. The article reports the overall difference as statistically significant with the printed t and p values; no effect size is printed for this finding.

"The overall mean score was calculated as 3.73 (SD = 0.64) for males and 3.57 (SD = 0.59) for females, and this diﬀerence was found to be statistically signiﬁcant ( t = 2.116; p = 0.035)."
