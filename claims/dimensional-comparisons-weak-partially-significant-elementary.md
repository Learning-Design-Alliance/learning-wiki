# Dimensional comparison processes in elementary school are weak and only partially significant, with a mixed pattern across grades and domains

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schneider 2020

Rebecca Schneider, Jörn R. Sparfeldt. (2020). Academic competence and affect self-concepts in elementary school students: Social and dimensional comparisons. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-019-09532-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GI/E path analyses in grades 2–4 (N = 858) examining non-corresponding-domain paths. The study reports a "mixed result pattern" with only three significant small negative paths; all remaining dimensional paths did not differ substantially from zero.

> "Significant path coefficients were found from the reported grade in mathematics on the reading affect self-concept in second grade (β = − .19), from the German reported grade on the mathematics competence self-concept in fourth grade (β = − .18), and from the German reported grade to the mathematics affect self-concept factor in third and fourth grades (β = − .21)."
