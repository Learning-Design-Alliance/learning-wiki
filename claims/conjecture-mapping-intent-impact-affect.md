Sarah McHale, Tor Ole B. Odden, and Ken Heller. (2026). Studying Impact and Intent of Design: Conjecture Mapping for Affect-Centered Analysis. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09878](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09878)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparative qualitative case study of one professor and one student in a computationally integrated modern physics lab course, using ethnographic observations, semi-structured interviews, and document analysis. The authors report the method "to distinguish between the professor's intent behind the activity design and the impact" on affective outcomes.

> "we introduce a novel modification of conjecture mapping to distinguish between the professor's intent behind the activity design and the impact on a student's physics computational literacy and physics identity"
