## Evidence

### Wallin 1915

Wallin, J. E. W. (1915). Age Norms of Psycho-Motor Capacity. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://search.worldcat.org/search?q=Age+Norms+of+Psycho-Motor+Capacity

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of form-board records of over 4000 bright, average, dull, feeble-minded, and epileptic children, summarized in the article's enumerated conclusions. The author states the increase holds "for every age investigated from two to seventeen" and notes development appears to continue beyond 17, though how far is undetermined.

> "First, there is an increase in psycho-motor capacity as measured by this test for every age investigated from two to seventeen (see the Graphs). This disproves the statement that there is no improvement after the age of 12 or 14."
