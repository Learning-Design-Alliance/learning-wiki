### Gao Ning 2024

Gao Ning, Fu Kun, Li Na, He Wei. (2024). Research on the application of CBL combined with BOPPPS teaching mode in oral and maxillofacial surgery teaching: a randomized controlled study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-77432-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teacher efficacy comparison using the Personal Teaching Efficacy Scale and TSTE on a 6-point Likert scale (total range 27–162). Table 3 reports Personal Teaching Efficiency "60.62 ± 3.73" versus "65.31 ± 1.65", TSTE "38.85 ± 3.85" versus "43.69 ± 1.49", and total "99.46 ± 6.16" versus "109.00 ± 2.24" (p = 0.018, 0.007, 0.002).

> "After adopting the teaching mode of CBL combined with BOPPPS, the Personal Teaching Efficiency, TSTE and total score were all higher than those before the implementation, with statistically significant difference (P < 0.05),as shown in Table 3."
