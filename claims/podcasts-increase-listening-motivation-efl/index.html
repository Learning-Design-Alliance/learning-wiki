Evidence¶

Shiri 2015¶

Saeed Shiri. (2015). The Application of Podcasting as a Motivational Strategy to Iranian EFL Learners of English: A View toward Listening Comprehension. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.3p.155

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon Signed Rank test on the experimental group's (n = 17) pre- and post-administration of the researcher-made ELCMS, run in SPSS 13. The article reports "a statistically significant difference between the ranks", so the first null hypothesis was rejected; no effect size is printed.

"Table 4.2. indicates that the p value of .00 is much lower than .05. This indicates that there exists a statistically significant difference between the ranks."
