`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview case analysis of one student (Alex) on a PIQL proportional reasoning item, analyzed via CBT. Although dichotomously incorrect, the reasoning used productive resources; "There is weakness in the projection between the blended space and the input spaces."

> "Alex recognized that they were looking for an invariant quantity, but their reasoning broke down when trying to reason with a general variable."
