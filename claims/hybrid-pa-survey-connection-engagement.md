## Evidence

### Joyce P 2025

Joyce P, Cunningham M, Alexander L. (2025). Evaluation of a hybrid model of a Physician Associate Studies programme: students, teachers, and examiner perspectives. Irish Journal of Medical Science 194(2):431-438. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-025-03918-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of all first-year hybrid students (n = 15 eligible, 80% response rate) administered via the university's Quality Enhancement Office, analysed in Survey Monkey. The study reports "only half the sample felt connected to other class members" while other engagement items drew majority agreement (Table 2 reports 83% agreement on engagement items, 92% on recommending the hybrid option).

> "While the majority of statements received agreement or strong agreement, it is noteworthy to highlight that only half the sample felt connected to other class members. Thirty-three percent of the sample remained neutral on this statement, and 17% disagreed."
