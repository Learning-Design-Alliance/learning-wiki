`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Numerical binomial analysis of the 25X grade data: per-bin A-grade probabilities were estimated at 95% confidence and used to bound P(≥8 A's | 16 upper-division terms). The article reports the success probability is "negligible at 95 percent conﬁdence level" below SAT-M 600.

> "We obtain a threshold at aroun d SAT-M 600: the probability of success (suﬃcient for graduate school admission) is neglig ible at 95 percent conﬁdence level for SAT-M less than 600."
