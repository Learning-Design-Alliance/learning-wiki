`q2 i?` Compared to the U.S. cohort (N = 38), Vietnamese participants reported higher recommendation rates (100% vs. 95%), higher perceived realism (86% vs. 70.3%), and lower discomfort (28.6% vs. 40.5%), with no comparison reaching significance (p = 0.5040, 0.1306, 0.3839; usefulness p = 0.3447).
