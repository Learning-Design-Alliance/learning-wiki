Evidence¶

Shenghua Zha 2025¶

Shenghua Zha, Marcy Maulucci Bragdon, Na Gong, Jinhui Wang, Silas Leavesley, Rachel Eaton, Erin Bosarge. (2025). A Case Study of Integrating AI Literacy Education in a Biology Class. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00476-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative a priori coding of open-ended worksheet answers in Lessons 1, 3, and 4 using an adapted IRC framework; two raters reached consensus with Cohen's Kappa of 0.88. In 76 answers across the three lessons only one case explained the biological aspect well without transferring AI knowledge.

"First, students who applied their prior knowledge ( transferred) all offered explanations in AI and biology domains, though some were vague or inaccurate (Fig. 8)."
