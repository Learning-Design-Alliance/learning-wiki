## Evidence

### Johnsson V 2026

Johnsson V, Petersen OB, Svendsen MBS, et al. (2026). To guide or not to guide: A randomized study on the use of needle guide for chorionic villus sampling training. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.70108

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on the secondary outcome, number of emptied balloons during training, showed "a significant difference in the number of emptied cavities across the intervention groups" (ηp2 = 0.13). Post hoc Tukey tests showed the needle-guided group emptied significantly fewer cavities than both other groups (mean difference −10.9, p = 0.005 each).

> "There was a significant difference in the number of emptied cavities across the intervention groups (F[2, 1259.2] = 7.0, p = 0.002, ηp2 = 0.13)."
