## Evidence

### Jacquemyn Xander 2024

Jacquemyn Xander, Guerrier Karine, Harvey Evan, Tackett Sean, Kutty Shelby, Wetzel Glenn T. (2024). pECGreview: Assessment of a Novel Tool to Evaluate the Accuracy of Pediatric ECG Interpretation Skills. Pediatric Cardiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00246-024-03556-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Validation analysis of the Accuracy score using extended Bland–Altman plots, Pearson correlations, and ICCs across four blinded evaluators of varied experience. The 95% LOA was 11.3, and "all correlations being equal to or above 0.75 and statistically significant" per Fig. 4C.

> "Absolute agreement, ICC1, was 0.74 (95% CI 0.69–0.80), p < 0.001, and ICC2, 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.83), p < 0.001, respectively."
