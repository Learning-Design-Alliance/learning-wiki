## Evidence

### Benoit Bediou 2023

Benoit Bediou, Melissa A. Rodgers, Elizabeth Tipton, Richard E. Mayer, C. Shawn Green, and Daphne Bavelier. (2023). Effects of Action Video Game Play on Cognitive Skills: A Meta-Analysis. Technology, Mind, and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1037/tmb0000102

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Intervention meta-analysis of 91 effect sizes from 28 studies with active control groups (total N = 2,739 reported for the data set), using pre-post between-group differences. The abstract reports "g = 0.30, 95% CI [0.11, 0.50]" for the causal effect of action game training.

> "Action video game play was causally related to improvements in cognitive skills in the intervention meta-analysis (small effect, g = 0.30, 95% CI [0.11, 0.50])"
