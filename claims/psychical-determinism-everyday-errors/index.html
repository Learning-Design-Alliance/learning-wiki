Mayer 1911¶

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument summarized in a review of Jones's article: everyday inadequacies of mental functioning are usually attributed to chance, but the author argues determinism prevails in "even the most trivial bit of mental functioning." No empirical data are reported.

"The author argues that there is no chance and accident in mental opera- tions. On the contrary, determinism prevails in even the most trivial bit of mental functioning."
