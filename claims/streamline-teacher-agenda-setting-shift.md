## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Valerie Otero 2012

Ben Van Dusen and Valerie Otero. (2012). Changing Roles and Identities in a Teacher-Driven Professional Development Community. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1209.1369](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1209.1369)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video coding of forty-two hours of meetings, with agenda items coded primary (1 point) or secondary (0.5 points) and averaged within early, middle, and late periods. "During the late section (c), the teachers provided the majority of the agenda items."

> "As shown in Figure 3(a), researchers provided all of the meeting agenda items during the early time period."

### Mike Ross 2014

Mike Ross, Ben Van Dusen, Valerie Otero. (2014). Becoming Agents of Change through Participation in a Teacher-Driven Professional Research Community. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1408.2502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1408.2502)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of group meeting videos, with agenda-item introduction coded for teacher versus researcher and averaged within early, middle, and late periods. In the early period "researchers provided all of the meeting agenda items," while in the late period teachers provided the majority. No effect size is printed.

> "As shown in Figure 4(a), researchers provided all of the meeting agenda items during the early time period."
