Disaggregation of pandemic learning gains by gender (Figure 4) and school poverty level (Figure 5), where low poverty meant <25% FRPL-eligible and high poverty >75% FRPL-eligible in 2018-19. The report observes "Large differences by school poverty, particularly in reading," with high-poverty-school BIPOC students making only 65-81% of historical reading gains.

"In both subjects, male BIPOC students made a lower percentage of pre-pandemic gains compared with female BIPOC students. Large differences by school poverty, particularly in reading, were also observed. BIPOC students in low poverty schools made over 90% of pre-pandemic reading gains"
