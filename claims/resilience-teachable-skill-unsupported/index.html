q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Resilience and persistence under challenge theme from the workshop's thematic analysis. Teachers described students who "froze, abandoned problems after initial failure" and treated resilience as teachable, yet unsupported by current routines and dashboards.

"Teachers described students who froze, abandoned problems after initial failure, or lacked independent problem- solving strategies. They treated resilience as a teachable skill that current rou- tines and dashboards did little to support."
