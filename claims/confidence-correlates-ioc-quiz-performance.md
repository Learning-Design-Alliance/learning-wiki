## Evidence

### Hannah Emily M. 2025

Hannah Emily M., Bloom Matthew B., Schwaitzberg Steven D. (2025). Project interpret cholangiogram at the SAGES 2024 Education & Innovation Center: skill deficiency in bile duct injury recognition among surgical residents. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12145-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation between Likert-scale confidence ratings and quiz scores across all 88 participants; the article reports "Higher overall average confidence was correlated with better overall quiz performance (rs: + 0.293, p = 0.006)", an effect size of rs = +0.293.

> "Higher overall average confidence was correlated with better overall quiz performance (rs: + 0.293, p = 0.006) for all participants"
