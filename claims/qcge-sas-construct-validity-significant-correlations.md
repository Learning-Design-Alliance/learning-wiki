## Evidence

### Loris T. Jeitziner 2025

Loris T. Jeitziner, Lisa Paneth, Oliver Rack, Susanne Bleisch, Carmen Zahn. (2025). Measuring the quality of collaborative group engagement: Development and validation of the QCGE self-assessment scale (QCGE-SAS). Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09445-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Construct validity analysis was conducted on a subsample (N = 112) using Kendall's tau correlations (Fig. 2) between the QCGE-SAS subscales and the TWE, FAT cohesion, MSUS meaningful cognitive engagement, and the self-reported learning outcome. The article reports "significant correlations with related scales"; no correlation coefficient values were available in the supplied text.

> "Construct and criterion validity were supported by significant correlations with related scales and with self-reported and objective learning outcomes."
