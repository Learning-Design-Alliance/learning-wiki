Bobrowicz, Katarzyna; Mendes, Angélica. (2026). Spatial skills in early childhood education. Learning and Instruction, 103, 102308. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102308

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-national document analysis of 81 early childhood curricular-guideline documents from fifty-two countries across six continents, coded with deductive thematic analysis. The article reports that "all countries but two mentioned diverse spatial skills"; no effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that all countries but two mentioned diverse spatial skills, but the extent of such representation did not vary across curricula directed at infancy and toddlerhood, or the preschool years, or the entire early childhood."
