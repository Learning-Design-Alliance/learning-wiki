Effects and Characteristics of Mathematics Interventions for Students with Co-Occurring Difficulties in Mathematics and Reading: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10184-x

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Heterogeneity assessment in the CHE meta-analysis of 60 effect sizes nested in nine studies. The Q-test was significant; the article reports "The I-squared value of 83.78 indicates that 83.78% of the variance in observed effects between effect sizes within studies reflects true heterogeneity" and a prediction interval from -0.96 to 1.90.

"The Q-statistic for the test of heterogeneity in effect sizes across studies was significant (Q (59) = 1015.47, p < 0.001)."
