# AIHEd reviews focus mostly on General AIEd (47.0%) and Profiling and Prediction (28.8%), while key findings predominantly concern adaptive systems and personalisation (54.5%)

### Bond 2024

Bond, M., Khosravi, H., De Laat, M., Bergdahl, N., Negrea, V., Oxley, E., Pham, P., Chong, S. W. & Siemens, G. (2024). A meta systematic review of artificial intelligence in higher education: a call for increased ethics, collaboration, and rigour. `q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Categorisation of the 66 AIHEd reviews using the Zawacki-Richter et al. (2019) typology for review focus and key findings. The printed percentages show adaptive systems and personalisation leading key findings while assessment/evaluation and ITS foci were rare. No effect sizes are printed.

> "More than half of the reviews (54.5%, n = 36) discussed applications related to adaptive systems and personalisation, closely followed by profiling and prediction (48.5%, n = 32), 39.4% (n = 26) discussed findings related to assessment and evaluation, and only 21.2% (n = 14) looked into intelligent tutoring systems."
