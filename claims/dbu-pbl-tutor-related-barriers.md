# Tutor-related barriers including lack of motivation, work overload from a shortage of trained tutors, and lack of PBL training and experience impede PBL implementation

## Evidence

### Tefera Aklile Semu 2024

Tefera Aklile Semu, Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Urgie Besufekad Mulugeta, Hassen Erzik Muhammed, Tamene Tilahun Deresse, Gebeyaw Enguday Demeke. (2024). Barriers to implementing problem-based learning at the school of medicine of Debre Berhan University, Ethiopia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05252-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviews with tutors and academic leaders in the qualitative study found that "There is no adequate PBL training for newly engaged staff" and that tutors cited no special recognition or reward for facilitating PBL sessions, diminishing motivation. Staff shortage created heavy workload for tutors.

> "There is no adequate PBL training for newly engaged staff; many of us are asked to facilitate PBL sessions without taking introductory training on how to lead PBL sessions."
