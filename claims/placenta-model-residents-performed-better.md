`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing de Oliveira et al. 2018, this comparative finding from the ex vivo cadaver/placenta category. The review also notes the placenta model required more extended time to complete the task and was more effective for sylvian fissure splitting and aneurysm dissection.

> "Oliveira et al. (2018) [13] showed that residents training on placenta models performed better than residents training on cadavers or only having surgical videos available."
