### C. Savage 2009

C. Savage, D. McGrath, T. McIntyre, M. Wegener, M. Williamson. (2009). Teaching Physics Using Virtual Reality. https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.5776 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0910.5776](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0910.5776)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey of a 200-student sample from ANU and UQ who had completed the RTR lab, reporting descriptive percentages only: "77% found the lab to be interesting" and 70% wanted more simulations. The authors take this as students believing the simulation supports their learning; no inferential test is printed.

> "For a sample of 200 students from The Australian National University and The University of Queensland who had completed the RTR lab we found that 77% found the lab to be interesting and that 70% would like to use more simulations in their studies."
