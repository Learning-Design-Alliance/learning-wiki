## Subclaims
`q3 i3` For every unit increase of the application score decile rank (1 best, 10 worst), the odds of allocation to a higher-preference foundation school were multiplied by 0.58 (β=−0.551, p<0.001). [→ Kumwenda B 2018](#kumwenda-b-2018)

## Evidence

### Kumwenda B 2018

Kumwenda B, Cleland JA, Prescott GJ, Walker K, Johnston PW. (2018). Relationship between sociodemographic factors and selection into UK postgraduate medical training programmes: a national cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-021329

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable ordinal logistic regression (model 3) in a national cohort of 8467 Foundation Programme doctors, adjusting for application score and foundation school competition ratio. The article reports the odds were "multiplied by a factor of 0.58 (β=−0.551, p<0.001)" per worsening decile.

> "for every unit increase of the Foundation Training ‘decile’ application score rank (1 best, 10 worst), the odds of an applicant getting allocated to a foundation school they had ranked higher in their order of preference decreased, as it was multiplied by a factor of 0.58 (β=−0.551, p<0.001)"
