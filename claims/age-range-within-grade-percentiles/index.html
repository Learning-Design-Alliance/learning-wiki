Pressey 1920¶

Pressey, S. L. (1920). Two Important Points with Regard to Age-Grade Tables. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Two+Important+Points+with+Regard+to+Age-Grade+Tables

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive percentile analysis of the same two-city age-grade tables, comparing 10th and 90th percentile ages per grade. The author interprets this as City C dealing severely with children who have once dropped behind, with about the same acceleration as City F.

"the range of ages in a given grade in City C is much greater, particularly for the second and third grades, where the distance between the 10 and 90 percentiles is almost twice as great in City C as in City F."
