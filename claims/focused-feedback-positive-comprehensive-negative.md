## Evidence

### Albatool Abalkheel & Tara Brandenburg 2020

Albatool Abalkheel & Tara Brandenburg. (2020). Effects of Written Corrective Feedback: A Synthesis of 10 Quasi-Experimental Studies. English Language Teaching, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v13n7p97

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Cross-study comparison in the meta-analysis (Table 3) of effect sizes by CF type and focus. The synthesis reports that "focused feedback had a positive effect size in both DCF and ICF", though focused ICF was supported by only one study.

> "What proved to be more telling of the efficacy of feedback was the focus, rather than the type of feedback. In all cases, focused feedback had a positive effect size in both DCF and ICF."

### Albatool Abalkheel & Tara Brandenburg 2020 (2)

Albatool Abalkheel & Tara Brandenburg. (2020). Effects of Written Corrective Feedback: A Synthesis of 10 Quasi-Experimental Studies. English Language Teaching, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v13n7p97

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Same synthesis of effect sizes by focus (Table 3). Comprehensive feedback showed "negative effect sizes for all DCF studies" and split results for comprehensive ICF, with Lalande (1982) and Van Beuningen et al. (2011) negative and Truscott & Hsu (2008) and Hartshorn et al. (2010) positive.

> "On the other hand, the results for comprehensive feedback demonstrated negative effect sizes for all DCF studies and mixed, with half negative (Lalande, 1982; VanBueningen et al., 2011) and half positive (Truscott & Hsu, 2008; Hartshorn et al., 2010) for comprehensive ICF studies."
