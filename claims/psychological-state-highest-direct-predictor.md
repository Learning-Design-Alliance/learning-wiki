Path coefficient comparison from the forest plot (Figure 4) of the mediation model. The article concludes psychological state is the strongest direct correlate of admission success among the studied variables.

> "At the top of the list was psychological state (B = 0.442, 95% CI [0.209, 0.658], P < 0.001), then academic foundation (B = 0.178, 95% CI [0.080, 0.283], P < 0.001), and finally behavioral engagement (B = 0.153, 95% CI [0.026, 0.267], P < 0.05)"
