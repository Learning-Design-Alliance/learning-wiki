## Evidence

### Fidan Özlem 2026

Fidan Özlem, Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp, Ok Durdu. (2026). Intensive Care Nurses' Adherence to Evidence‐Based Clinical Practice Guidelines for the Prevention and Treatment of Pressure Ulcers and Their Self‐Efficacy: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70998

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on PUM‐SES scores from the cross‐sectional survey; the model was significant (F = 23.597, p < 0.001, R² = 0.680), with "age (β = 0.293, p = 0.012)" and weekly PU care significant positive predictors.

> "According to the analysis results, age (β = 0.293, p = 0.012) and the number of PU care per week (β = 0.804, p = 0.021) had a significant positive effect on nurses' self‐efficacy scores."

### Fidan Özlem 2026 (2)

Fidan Özlem, Zeyrek Arife Şanlialp, Ok Durdu. (2026). Intensive Care Nurses' Adherence to Evidence‐Based Clinical Practice Guidelines for the Prevention and Treatment of Pressure Ulcers and Their Self‐Efficacy: A Cross‐Sectional Study. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.70998

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same regression analysis: non‐participation in pressure ulcer education (β = −2.720, p = 0.011) and not attending conferences or reading articles (β = −2.774, p = 0.003) were associated with "significantly lower" self‐efficacy.

> "Self‐efficacy scores of nurses who did not participate in pressure ulcer‐related education were significantly lower than the scores of those who received education (β = −2.720, p = 0.011)."
