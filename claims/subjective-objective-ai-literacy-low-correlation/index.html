Daniele Verí. (2026). Beyond AI Literacy: A Structured Review and Exploratory Meta-Analysis of Measures for Competent Generative-AI Use. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15624

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Exploratory random-effects meta-analysis of three directly reported same-sample correlations (combined reported N = 2,765), all from one research program using the MAILS family as subjective comparator. The pooled r = .055 with a Hartung-Knapp interval of "[-.047, .156]" includes zero.

"The primary REML pooled correlation wasr =.055, with a Hartung-Knapp 95% interval of [-.047, .156] and a normal-theory interval of [.018, .092]."
