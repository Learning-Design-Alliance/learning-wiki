## Evidence

### Ono Yuko 2018

Ono Yuko, Tanigawa Koichi, Kakamu Takeyasu, Shinohara Kazuaki, Iseki Ken. (2018). Out-of-hospital endotracheal intubation experience, confidence and confidence-associated factors among Northern Japanese emergency life-saving technicians: a population-based cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021858

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank-order correlation analysis of the survey's confidence items: "moderate positive correlations between confidence levels in ETI and SGA insertion (rs=0.468, p<0.001)" and bag-valve-mask ventilation (rs=0.419, p<0.001).

> "There were moderate positive correlations between confidence levels in ETI and SGA insertion (rs=0.468, p<0.001) and bag-valve-mask ventilation (rs=0.419, p<0.001; online supplementary table S2)."

### Ono Yuko 2018 (2)

Ono Yuko, Tanigawa Koichi, Kakamu Takeyasu, Shinohara Kazuaki, Iseki Ken. (2018). Out-of-hospital endotracheal intubation experience, confidence and confidence-associated factors among Northern Japanese emergency life-saving technicians: a population-based cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021858

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of ETI frequency against ELST demographics in the same survey found "negligible correlations between ETI frequency and age (rs=−0.101, p<0.001)" and similarly small coefficients for experience and OHCA case load.

> "There were negligible correlations between ETI frequency and age (rs=−0.101, p<0.001), years of experience after achieving basic-level (rs=−0.106, p<0.001) or advanced-level (rs=−0.062, p=0.002) ELST status and annual OHCA case load per capita (rs=0.055, p=0.005)."
