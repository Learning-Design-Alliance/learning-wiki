## Evidence

### Holger Maus 2026

Holger Maus, Fabian Kieser, Stefan Petersen, Peter Wulff, and Paul Tschisgale. (2026). Developing an LLM-Based Feedback System Grounded in Evidence-Centered Design to Support Physics Problem Solving. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1142/s2661339526500125](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339526500125)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary evaluation with 38 Physics Olympiad students who produced 64 problem-level ratings on a 5-point single Likert-type item after receiving feedback twice. The rating-level mean was "M= 3.6,SD= 1.3", and the estimate was stable across aggregation methods (M = 3.8 by problem).

> "The collected ratings indicate that the LLM-generated feedback was generally perceived as useful (M= 3.6,SD= 1.3 by rating; see Fig. 4)."
