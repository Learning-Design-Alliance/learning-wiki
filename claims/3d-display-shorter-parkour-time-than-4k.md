## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Overall Parkour time was significantly shorter with 3D than 4K (712.5 s ± 17.5 s vs 999.5 s ± 25.1 s, P < 0.001) for all levels of experience. [→ Wahba R 2020](#wahba-r-2020)

## Evidence

### Wahba R 2020

Wahba R, Datta R, Bußhoff J, Bruns T, Hedergott A, Gietzelt C, Dieplinger G, Fuchs H, Morgenstern B, Möller D, Hellmich M, Bruns CJ, Stippel DL. (2020). 3D Versus 4K Display System – Influence of “State-of-the-art”-Display Technique on Surgical Performance (IDOSP-study) in Minimally Invasive Surgery: A Randomized Cross-over Trial. Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000004328

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized cross-over trial (IDOSP) with 128 participants (49 medical students, 39 non-board certified, 40 board certified surgeons) performing a standardized laparoscopic training Parkour. The "overall Parkour time in seconds comparing 3D versus 4K" favored 3D with P < 0.001.

> "The overall Parkour time in seconds comparing 3D versus 4K was 712.5 s ± 17.5 s versus 999.5 s ± 25.1 s (P < 0.001) combined for all levels of experience."
