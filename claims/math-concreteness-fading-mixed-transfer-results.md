# Concreteness fading studies in mathematics show mixed transfer results, with some studies favoring fading and others favoring generic symbols or reversed sequences

## Subclaims

### Tommi Kokkonen & Lennart Schalk 2021 (2)

Tommi Kokkonen & Lennart Schalk. (2021). One Instructional Sequence Fits all? A Conceptual Analysis of the Applicability of Concreteness Fading in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09581-7

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Narrative review of undergraduate studies on the commutative group of order three. The review reports Kaminski et al.'s conclusion favoring generic symbols, De Bock et al.'s replication finding concreteness fading led to worse transfer than two concrete or two idealized representations, and McNeil and Fyfe's three-step replication finding "Concreteness fading results in the best transfer performance."

> "Kaminski et al. ( 2008) conclude that learning with only the generic symbols of the target concept results in better transfer than does a concreteness fading sequence."
