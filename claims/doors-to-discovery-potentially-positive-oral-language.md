# Doors to Discovery™ shows potentially positive effects on preschool oral language (PPVT-III effect size 0.27, not statistically significant)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In one randomized controlled trial meeting standards without reservations, Doors to Discovery™ had a substantively important but statistically non-significant positive effect on oral language (effect size 0.27). ## Evidence

### Doors to Discovery™ WWC Intervention Report 2013

Doors to Discovery™ WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013). Doors to Discovery™ Intervention Report, Early Childhood Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/153

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial in four Head Start classrooms (37 children; 21 intervention, 16 comparison), with the WWC computing effects from child-level data. The PPVT-III effect size was 0.27 (improvement index +11), "not statistically significant" but substantively important; WWC-computed p-value 0.63.

> "WWC analyses of the Christie et al. (2003) data show that the effect for the PPVT-III is not statistically significant, but large enough (0.27) to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria"
