`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulation-based internal discrimination analysis comparing Evaluator scores across prompt-engineered competence conditions within the same Patient-agent profiles. The article reports that all criterion-level planned contrasts remained significant after Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate correction (q<0.05).

> "For MI, the overall mean score increased from 1.18 (95% CI 1.16‐1.20) for the novice profile to 1.75 (95% CI 1.69‐1.81) for the intermediate profile and to 3.57 (95% CI 3.48‐3.66) for the expert profile"
