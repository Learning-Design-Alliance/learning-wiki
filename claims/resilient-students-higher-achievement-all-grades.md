## Subclaims
`q3 i3` One-way ANOVA showed significant achievement differences among the three disadvantaged groups, with resilient students highest in grades 6, 9, and 11 (ηp² = 0.83, 0.83, 0.82). [→ Yan Yan 2022](#yan-yan-2022)

## Evidence

### Yan Yan 2022

Yan Yan, Gai Xiaosong. (2022). High Achievers from Low Family Socioeconomic Status Families: Protective Factors for Academically Resilient Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315882

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA with student type as independent variable and total academic achievement as dependent variable (Table 3): grade 6 F = 33,834.44, ηp² = 0.83; grade 9 F = 51,698.45, ηp² = 0.83; grade 11 F = 29,194.37, ηp² = 0.82.

> "there was a significant difference (p < 0.001) between resilient students, disadvantaged average achievers, and disadvantaged low achievers, and the resilient students had significantly higher academic achievement compared to disadvantaged average achievers and disadvantaged low achievers, whether in grade 6, 9, or 11."
