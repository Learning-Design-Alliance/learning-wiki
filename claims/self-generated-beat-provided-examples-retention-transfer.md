# Generating own examples after instruction produced better retention and transfer than studying provided examples

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Self-generated examples yielded higher knowledge retention than provided examples (d = 0.51). [→ Krause-Wichmann 2025](#krause-wichmann-2025)
`q3 i2` Self-generated examples yielded higher knowledge transfer than provided examples (d = 0.66). [→ Krause-Wichmann 2025 (2)](#krause-wichmann-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Krause-Wichmann 2025

Krause-Wichmann, T., Klopp, E., Stark, L., & Stark, R. (2025). Promoting future teachers' pedagogical knowledge: The role of self-generated vs. provided illustrative examples after instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09694-4

`q3 · i2`

Planned contrast I-GE vs. I-PE on knowledge retention in the randomized three-condition experiment (N = 105); Table 3 prints d = 0.51 for this contrast, a medium effect favoring example generation.

> "the contrasts revealed that the outcomes differed significantly between students who learned with self-generated examples (I-GE) and students who learned with provided examples (I-PE) with medium effect size. Students who generated examples achieved better results."

### Krause-Wichmann 2025 (2)

Krause-Wichmann, T., Klopp, E., Stark, L., & Stark, R. (2025). Promoting future teachers' pedagogical knowledge: The role of self-generated vs. provided illustrative examples after instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09694-4

`q3 · i2`

Planned contrasts on knowledge transfer in the same experiment; Table 3 prints d = 0.66 for I-GE/I-PE (and d = 1.17 for I-GE/I), and "Students who generated examples achieved the best results".

> "Concerning knowledge transfer (KT), the contrasts indicated that the performance of students who learned with self-generated examples (I-GE) differed significantly from those who learned with provided examples (I-PE) and from those who restudied instruction only (I)."
