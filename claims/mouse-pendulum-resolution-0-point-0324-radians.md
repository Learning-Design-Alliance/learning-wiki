### Vadas Gintautas and Alfred Hübler 2009

Vadas Gintautas and Alfred Hübler. (2009). A simple, low-cost, data-logging pendulum built from a computer mouse. https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4319 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0901.4319](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0901.4319)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Calibration of the authors' built apparatus: one full roller turn was measured in cursor pixels, giving "a conversion factor of 0 .0324 radians per pixel". The reported resolution corresponds to a fractional uncertainty of 0.52% out of a full turn; resolution is limited by the number and spacing of slots in the encoder disk.

> "For the apparatus we built, a rotation of 360 ±1 degrees gave a change in the position of the cursor of 194 ± 1 pixels. This results in a conversion factor of 0 .0324 radians per pixel."
