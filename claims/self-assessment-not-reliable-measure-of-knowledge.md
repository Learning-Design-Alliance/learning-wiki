### Pawlaczyk-Kamieńska T 2025

Pawlaczyk-Kamieńska T, Jastrzębski B, Kamieńska P, Kamieńska W, Marushko O, Marushko O, Kruszyńska D. (2025). The evaluation of declarative and objective knowledge of first aid for dental traumatic injuries among medical students: development and validation of a competency assessment tool. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08153-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of self-assessment ratings against TDI-KNOW Index scores in the cross-sectional survey. The study reports the overall correlation was not significant (p = 0.25), while for 6th-year students "the correlation was moderate, negative, and statistically significant (p = 0.003)".

> "In the overall student group, no statistically significant correlation was identified between self-assessment of knowledge and the TDI-KNOW Index (p = 0.25). There were no significant correlations for 4th-year (p = 0.64) and 5th-year students (p = 0.75). However, for 6th-year students, the correlation was moderate, negative, and statistically significant (p = 0.003)."
