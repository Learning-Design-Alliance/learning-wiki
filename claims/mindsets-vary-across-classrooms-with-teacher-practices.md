`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports, citing Farrington, Porter, & Klugman (forthcoming), that "the same student can have different mindsets from one classroom to the next" and that grades rise or fall accordingly. It further attributes these mindset differences to differences in teachers' instructional practices. No effect size is printed.

> "research has shown that the same student can have different mindsets from one class room to the next, and that students earn higher or lower grades in their different classrooms as their mindsets change."
