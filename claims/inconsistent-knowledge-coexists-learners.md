## Evidence

### Schneider 2013

Schneider, M., & Hardy, I. (2013). Profiles of Inconsistent Knowledge in Children's Pathways of Conceptual Change. Developmental Psychology, 49(9). https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030976

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile transition analysis of multiple-choice test scores from 161 third-graders at three measurement points identified a fragmented profile with above-average scores on all three knowledge types. The authors state they "found clear evidence for the coexistence of inconsistent pieces of knowledge in learners."

> "We found clear evidence for the coexistence of inconsistent pieces of knowledge in learners. Most notably, one latent class of students exhibited a knowledge profile (the fragmented profile) with aboveaverage misconceptions, everyday conceptions, and scientific concepts."
