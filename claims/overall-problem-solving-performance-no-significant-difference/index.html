Sabrina Ludwig 2026¶

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

q3 · i? · causal · r2

T-test on total problem-solving performance (maximum 31 credits) in the 40-min problem-based supplier selection scenario. The DI-PS group scored "MDI-PS = 22.13 vs. MPS-DI = 19.24", a non-significant difference (p = .08); equivalence was not tested.

"the DI-PS group obtained a higher average problem-solving performance in the problem-solving scenario than the PS-DI group (MDI-PS = 22.13 vs. MPS-DI = 19.24). However, the t-test revealed no significance (p = .08)."
