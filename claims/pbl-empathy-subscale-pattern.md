## Evidence

### Kim 2020

Kim, K.-J. (2020). Project-based learning approach to increase medical student empathy. Medical Education Online, 25(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2020.1742965

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sub-scale paired t-tests in the same pre/post study of 45 students; Table 1 prints significant changes for the lower group in fantasy (t = 3.457, p = .003), empathic concern (t = 3.492, p = .002) and personal distress (t = 2.390, p = .027). No effect sizes printed.

> "Empathy scores increased for students with lower empathy in three of four sub-scales in the empathy measurement; i.e. fantasy, empathic concern, and personal distress."
