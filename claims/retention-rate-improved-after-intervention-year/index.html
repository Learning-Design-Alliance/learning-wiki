Evidence¶

Kelchner 2017¶

Kelchner, V. P., Evans, K., Brendell, K., Allen, D., Miller, C., & Cooper-Haber, K. (2017). The Effect of a School-Based Transitional Support Intervention Program on Alternative School Youth's Attitudes and Behaviors. The Professional Counselor, 7(2), 169-184. https://doi.org/10.15241/vpk.7.2.169

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive observational comparison reported in the discussion, not a tested experimental contrast: 59% of returnees were no longer in school the year before the intervention versus 14% in the intervention year. No statistical test or effect size is reported.

"In the school year prior to the intervention, 59% of students returning from the alternative school setting to the home school were no longer in school at the end of the year. At the end of the school year after the intervention took place, the number of students returning from the alternative school setting that were no longer in school was reduced to 14%."
