Core-Plus Mathematics 2010¶

Core-Plus Mathematics. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Core-Plus Mathematics, High School Math. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.wmich.edu/cpmp

q2 · i? · review · r3

The WWC's review of the available literature (publicly available through March 2010). Most excluded studies were quasi-experiments in which "the analytic intervention and comparison groups are not shown to be equivalent" or were ineligible for not examining intervention effectiveness.

"Seventeen studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of Core-Plus Mathematics on high school students. One study is a quasi-experimental design that meets WWC evidence stan-dards with reservations. The remaining 16 studies do not meet either WWC evidence standards or eligibility screens."
