## Evidence

### Dircks Monika 2017

Dircks Monika, Mayr Andreas, Freidank Annette, Kornhuber Johannes, Dörje Frank, Friedland Kristina. (2017). Advances in clinical pharmacy education in Germany: a quasi-experimental single-blinded study to evaluate a patient-centred clinical pharmacy course in psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1092-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 22-question student satisfaction survey, developed from previously used university questionnaires, administered after all students completed the course; 76 of 84 students (90%) responded. The article reports these percentages descriptively; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

> "Of the students, 93% rated the course as practice-orientated, 90% felt better prepared for patient contact, and 92% gave a positive answer about their overall satisfaction."
