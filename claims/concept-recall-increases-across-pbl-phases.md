## Evidence

### Chng Esther 2011

Chng Esther, Yew Elaine H. J., Schmidt Henk G. (2011). Effects of tutor-related behaviours on the process of problem-based learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9282-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the concept recall tests administered to 223 students at the end of each PBL phase, plus the essay test. The largest jump in recalled concepts occurred across the self-directed learning phase; the means are descriptive and no significance test of the phase-to-phase change is reported.

> "The means and standard deviations of the concept recall test administered after the problem analysis phase (M = 5.63, SD = 3.35); self-directed learning phase (M = 9.64, SD = 4.08); reporting phase (M = 9.90, SD = 3.83) as well as the essay test (M = 3.57, SD = 1.94) are shown in Table 2."
