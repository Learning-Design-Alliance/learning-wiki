### Barrow 2019

Barrow, L., & Sartain, L. (2019). GoCPS: A first look at ninth-grade applications, offers, and enrollment. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of Round 1 and Round 2 offer outcomes from GoCPS selection data for about 25,238 choice applicants. The report states 93 percent of Round 1 applicants received an offer at a ranked program, and by Round 2's end "94 percent of choice applicants had been offered a seat."

> "For 51 percent of applicants, the offer was for their top-ranked program; 81 percent received an offer from one of their top-three ranked programs."
