### Ahari Saeid Sadeghieh S 2026

Ahari Saeid Sadeghieh S, Khodabandeh S, Zandian H, Kamran A. (2026). Exploring the effects of the health transformation plan on the quality of clinical education in teaching hospitals: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08848-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding; Participant 1 stated there is no quality or quantity of education in performance evaluation indicators and that the number of patients determines the fee. Participant 17 stated education was almost completely forgotten after HTP implementation. Participants interpreted this as shaping organizational priorities and resource allocation.

> "In my performance evaluation indicators for paying for services, there is no quality or quantity of education. It is the number of patients that determines the amount of my fee"
