## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jaeger 2024

Jaeger, A. J., & Fiorella, L. (2024). Metacognitive effects of instructional visuals: the role of cue use and judgment type. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09370-x

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 ANOVA on draw relative accuracy (Pearson) with 48 undergraduates reading chemistry texts. Main effect of cue type: "F(1, 43) = 5.42, p = 0.025, ƞp2 = 0.07". Gamma analysis mirrored this, F(1, 43) = 4.63, p = 0.037, ηp² = 0.10. The interaction with image condition was not significant.

> "There was also a main effect of cue type indicating that participants who reported basing their judgments on representation-based cues had higher relative accuracy than participants who used heuristic cues, F(1, 43) = 5.42, p = 0.025, ƞp2 = 0.07."
