## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2012

Van Dusen, B., & Otero, V. (2012). Influencing Students' Relationships With Physics Through Culturally Relevant Tools. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.4789739](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789739)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative observation of a sound lab in an urban high-school physics class, from the FINDINGS section. Pitch-identification apps let students evaluate PVC pipe frequencies themselves; the authors report "tasks were transformed from teacher-as-authority to evidence-as-authority."

> "With these three applications, the iPad transformed the task of asking the teacher to evaluate the sound into a different task in which students could use a mathematical, audio-matching, or visual-spatial model to assess the sound."

### Van Dusen 2012 (2)

Van Dusen, B., & Otero, V. (2012). Influencing Students' Relationships With Physics Through Culturally Relevant Tools. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.4789739](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789739)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors generalize from observed lab exemplars: "There were many situations such as this" in which students could "reason with evidence instead of deferring to an external authority."

> "There were many situations such as this, where tasks were transformed from teacher-as-authority to evidence-as-authority."
