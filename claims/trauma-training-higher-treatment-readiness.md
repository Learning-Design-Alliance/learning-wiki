European Journal of Psychotraumatology, 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/20008198.2021.1995265

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the final hierarchical regression model (F(10, 508) = 19.65, p < .001, adjusted R2 = .27, Cohen's f2 = .38) on the vignette-survey data, trauma-specific training was the only first-block predictor contributing to the model. No standardized effect size is printed for this predictor.
