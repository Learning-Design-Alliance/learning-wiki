Evidence¶

Wu Zeyu 2024¶

Wu Zeyu, Huang Yiling, Lyu Ling, Huang Yu, Ping Fan. (2024). The efficacy of simulation-based learning versus non-simulation-based learning in endocrinology education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06010-z

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of nine RCTs assessing learning interest, synthesized separately for count data (six studies, RR = 1.56) and continuous data (three studies, SMD = 1.39). The results demonstrated that "SBL was more effective in stimulating learning interest compared to NSBL."

"six studies [16, 19, 23, 29, 31, 35] reporting count data (RR = 1.56; 95% CI: 1.22–1.99, P = 0.0004, I2 = 77%) (additional file 4) and three studies [21, 24, 28] reporting continuous data (SMD = 1.39; 95% CI: 1.11–1.67, P < 0.00001, I2 = 0%)"
