Preliminary multiple regression analyses (11 backward elimination analyses with 25 or 27 potential confounds as predictors) assessing confound-dialogue relations. PreRead (M) predicted Elaborated, Reasoned, the Reasoned/Non-Dialogic Ratio and Reflection (ßs=-.25 to .49); PreMaths (SD) predicted Querying and Reflection.
