# Prior attainment predicted several dialogue variables in preliminary regression analyses

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In backward elimination regressions, PreMaths (M) predicted frequencies of Elaborated and Querying, the Elaborated/Non-Dialogic Ratio and Monitoring ratings; PreRead (M) and PreMaths (SD) also predicted dialogue variables. [→ Howe 2018](#howe-2018)

## Evidence

### Howe 2018

Howe, C., Hennessy, S., Mercer, N., Vrikki, M., & Wheatley, L. (2018). Teacher-Student Dialogue during Classroom Teaching: Does it Really Impact upon Student Outcomes? To appear in The Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=ES/M007103/1

`q2 · i?`

Preliminary multiple regression analyses (11 backward elimination analyses with 25 or 27 potential confounds as predictors) assessing confound-dialogue relations. PreRead (M) predicted Elaborated, Reasoned, the Reasoned/Non-Dialogic Ratio and Reflection (ßs=-.25 to .49); PreMaths (SD) predicted Querying and Reflection.
