This is the review's own characteristics-of-included-studies result: after screening 135 deduplicated records and 30 full texts, thirteen studies met the criteria. The article states the corpus comprised "all of which were RCTs and pilot studies conducted between 2014 and 2025".

### Ruslin Muhammad 2026 (2)

Ruslin Muhammad, Sjamsudin Endang, Hanafiah Olivia Avriyanti, Stevanie Carolina, Kurniawan Sri Hastuti, Anshar Muh, Boffano Paolo, Forouzanfar Tymour, Sukotjo Cortino. (2026). Exploring the impact of immersive reality for enhancing clinical training in dental education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09739-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's JBI risk-of-bias assessment (Table 2) reports seven of thirteen studies met every JBI criterion as low risk, five as minor risk. The authors also state high heterogeneity meant "comparisons and meta-analysis were impossible".

> "Most of the included studies exhibit a very low risk of bias, indicating a high level of methodological quality across the literature."
