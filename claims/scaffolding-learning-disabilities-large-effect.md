## Evidence

### Belland Brian R. 2017

Belland Brian R., Walker Andrew E., Kim Nam Ju. (2017). A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis to Synthesize the Influence of Contexts of Scaffolding Use on Cognitive Outcomes in STEM Education. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317723009

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Education population moderator analysis (Figure 4): the learning-disabilities estimate of ḡ = 3.13 came from four outcomes in a single intelligent tutoring system study in elementary mathematics; the article flags possible regression to the mean since six participants were excluded for pre-assessment scores above 60% correct.

> "Scaffolding for students with learning disabilities had the largest effect size estimate (ḡ = 3.13) by a large margin. This effect size estimate should be considered tentative, as the MCMC sampling was based on four outcomes from a single study."

### Belland Brian R. 2017 (2)

Belland Brian R., Walker Andrew E., Kim Nam Ju. (2017). A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis to Synthesize the Influence of Contexts of Scaffolding Use on Cognitive Outcomes in STEM Education. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317723009

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Education population moderator analysis (Figure 4) reporting a large pre–post effect for English language learners (ḡ = 0.92); traditional students had the greatest number of outcomes and the tightest credible interval.

> "ELL also had a large pre–post effect size (ḡ = 0.92)."
