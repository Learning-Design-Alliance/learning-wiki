## Evidence

### Tuncer 2014

Tuncer, M., & Bahadır, F. (2014). Effect of screen reading and reading from printed out material on student success and permanency in Introduction to Computer Lesson. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1034226

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same 78-student pretest–posttest experiment, delayed (permanency) test scores corrected for posttest were compared via ANCOVA. The article reports "a significant difference between permanency test averages" favoring the control group, with "Calculated effect size is η2=.069" (corrected averages 56.13 vs 53.59).

> "According to Table 8, a significant difference between permanency test averages corrected according to posttest was noticed [F (1,75) =5.524, p<.05].  LSD test applied on corrected posttest scores show that this significant difference is in favour of control group. Calculated effect size is η2=.069."
