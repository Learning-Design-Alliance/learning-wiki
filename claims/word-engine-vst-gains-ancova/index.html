Claims

Classes assigned weekly Word Engine online flashcard use gained significantly more on the VST than an extensive-reading control class over one academic year, in a quasi-experiment with acknowledged selection and time-on-task confounds

In intact classes assigned weekly Word Engine flashcard homework, adjusted VST posttest scores were significantly higher than in an extensive-reading control over one academic year; the authors call the Word Engine contribution plausible because selection and time-on-task confounds were not controlled
