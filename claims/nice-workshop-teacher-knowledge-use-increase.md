## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers rated their knowledge of network science points substantially higher after the workshop than before, and likewise rated their likelihood of classroom use higher after than before. [→ Towlson EK 2018](#towlson-ek-2018)

## Evidence

### Towlson EK 2018

Towlson EK, Sheetz L, Gera R, Roginski J, Cramer C, Uzzo S, Sayama H. (2018). NiCE Teacher Workshop: Engaging K-12 Teachers in the Development of Curricular Materials That Utilize Complex Networks Concepts. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.29173/cmplct29350](https://doi.org/10.29173/cmplct29350)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-workshop survey of the 21 participating teachers. Teachers rated each network-related query on a 1-6 scale for knowledge and likelihood of use, before and after the workshop; the article reports "large increases in both their understanding of the network concept, and in their likelihood to make use of the network concept in their lessons." No test statistics are printed.

> "On all points, the teachers indicated large increases in both their understanding of the network concept, and in their likelihood to make use of the network concept in their lessons."
