## Evidence

### Conroy 2014

Conroy, M. A., Sutherland, K. S., Algina, J. J., Wilson, R. E., Martinez, J. R., & Whalon, K. J. (2014). Measuring Teacher Implementation of the BEST in CLASS Intervention Program and Corollary Child Outcomes. Journal of Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/1063426614532949

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-condition analysis of 130 preschool-aged children (66 treatment, 64 comparison) observed via partial-interval coding; at post-test engagement t = 5.55, p = 0.00 and disruption t = −5.21, p = 0.00; at maintenance engagement t = 3.77, p = 0.00 and disruption t = −3.40, p = 0.00.

> "the overall percentage of child engagement significantly increased for the intervention group from baseline to post-test and maintenance in comparison with the control group at baseline, post-test, and maintenance. The overall percentage of disruptive, aggressive, defiant behavior significantly decreased for the intervention group from baseline to post-test, and maintenance in comparison with the control group."
