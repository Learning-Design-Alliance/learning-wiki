## Evidence

### Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Homework accuracy analysis from the same classroom experiment, computer-scored by research assistants blind to condition. The article attributes the deficit to interleaved assignments containing nine problem types versus three on blocked assignments, making blocked assignments easier.

> "students correctly solved more blocked than interleaved homework problems (Table 2), with a mean deficit on interleaved assignments of 0.05 and 0.09 proportion correct in Stages 1 and 2, respectively."

### Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021 (2)

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-assignment metacognitive judgments collected on each homework assignment; a higher proportion of medium-to-difficult ratings followed interleaved assignments and a higher proportion of well-to-extremely-well learning judgments followed blocked assignments.

> "students tended to rate interleaved assignments as more challenging and yielding less mastery (Table 2)."
