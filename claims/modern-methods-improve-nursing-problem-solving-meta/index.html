Evidence¶

Zou Ya 2026¶

Zou Ya, Xie Wenzhao, Huang Hui, Liu Dan, Zhou Qing, Yi Qifeng, Tong Xiaoliang, Mao Ping. (2026). The effects of case-based, problem-based, and team-based learning on nursing students’ problem-solving, self-directed learning, and communication skills: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08602-3

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of 11 controlled studies (3 RCTs, 13 quasi-experimental with control groups overall) using a random-effects model due to high heterogeneity ("I2 = 93%"). The pooled "SMD = 1.05" favors modern methods over traditional teaching.

"The pooled effect of eleven trials showed that modern teaching methods significantly improved students’ problem-solving abilities (SMD = 1.05, 95% CI: 0.57–1.53, I2 = 93%, P < 0.0001)"
