### Dialogic Reading 2010

Dialogic Reading. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/136

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Systematic WWC review of 59 studies, of which two randomized controlled trials met evidence standards (52 children with language delays). The report states it "found potentially positive effects of dialogic reading" in this domain; the domain average improvement index is +11 percentile points, range –2 to +45.
