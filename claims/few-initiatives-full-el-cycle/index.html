Review-level synthesis of teaching and learning strategies across the 100 included articles. The review reports that strategies ranged from small interprofessional groups to reflective activities, "but few initiatives utilized a full EL cycle."

"A variety of teaching and learning strategies were employed, such as small interprofessional groups of students, team huddles, direct provision of care, and reflective activities, but few initiatives utilized a full EL cycle."
