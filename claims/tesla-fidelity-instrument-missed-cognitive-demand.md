# In the TESLA project, the fidelity instrument failed to capture important variation in the ways teachers did or did not maintain cognitive demand

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The fidelity instrument failed to capture important variation in how teachers maintained cognitive demand, including a distinctive dip in task demand in some lessons. [→ Vig 2014](#vig-2014)

## Evidence

### Vig 2014

Vig, R., Taylor, M. W., Star, J. R., & Chao, T. (2014). Assessing implementation fidelity: Challenges as seen through the lens of two experimental studies. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2014/annualmeeting/abstracts/view.asp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the TESLA program, a two-day lesson focused on pattern exploration was built around a task designed to require high-level cognitive demand according to the Task Analysis Guide. Observers noted that teachers appeared to implement the task as instructed but something was missing.

> "the fidelity instrument failed to capture (what we feel was) the important variation in the ways teachers did or did not maintain cognitive demand. In particular, we noticed a very distinctive “dip” in the cognitive demand of the task in some teachers’ lessons"
