Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of self-confidence between T2 and T3 in the single-group study detected a "significant slight drop"; no standardized effect size was printed, and performance over the same interval was not significantly lower.

> "After the 15 days that separated the first session from the second, a significant slight drop in the self-confidence level was detected (Δ = −1.4 ± 2.7, p = 0.038)."
