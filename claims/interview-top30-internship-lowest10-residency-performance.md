## Evidence

### Yun Je-Yeon 2024

Yun Je-Yeon, Ryu Hyunjin, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Hyun Bae, Choi Seung, Park Wan Beom, Bae Eun Jung, Yim Jae-Joon, Myung Sun Jung. (2024). Subtyping of Performance Trajectory During Medical School, Medical Internship, and the First Year of Residency in Training Physicians: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2024.39.e239

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression in 211 first-year residents (2016–2020), adjusted for GPA, English scores, internship performance, and selection procedures. The article reports "higher scores in the residency selection interview (aOR, 1.35)" and the lowest-10% aOR of 0.79.

> "had higher odds of achieving performance in the top 30% if they had higher scores in the residency selection interview (aOR, 1.35); a lower medical internship performance score was associated with increased odds of performance within the lowest 10% in the first year of residency (aOR, 0.79)"

### Yun Je-Yeon 2024 (2)

Yun Je-Yeon, Ryu Hyunjin, Kim Ju Whi, Yoon Hyun Bae, Choi Seung, Park Wan Beom, Bae Eun Jung, Yim Jae-Joon, Myung Sun Jung. (2024). Subtyping of Performance Trajectory During Medical School, Medical Internship, and the First Year of Residency in Training Physicians: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2024.39.e239

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same regression (Table 3, lower rows): the lowest-10% outcome was predicted by the internship performance score with "aOR, 0.79" (95% CI 0.66–0.95, P = 0.013).

> "a lower medical internship performance score was associated with increased odds of performance within the lowest 10% in the first year of residency (aOR, 0.79)"
