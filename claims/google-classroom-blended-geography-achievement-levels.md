## Evidence

### Olha V. Bondarenko 2018

Olha V. Bondarenko, Svitlana V. Mantulenko, Andrey V. Pikilnyak. (2018). Google Classroom as a Tool of Support of Blended Learning for Geography Students. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.55056/ceur-ws.org/vol-2257/paper17.pdf](https://doi.org/10.55056/ceur-ws.org/vol-2257/paper17.pdf)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Monitoring of students' academic achievements at the final stage of study during 2016 and 2017, comparing Group I taught traditionally with Group II taught by blended learning with Google Classroom in the course Regional Economic and Social World Geography, with results presented in Fig. 2. The article reports "an increase in the number of students with a sufficient level and a decrease in those who have shown a low level" in Group II. No effect size or significance test is printed for the Group II shifts.

> "In Group II, the number of high-level students increased from 7.1 % to 10.7 %; with sufficient – from 32.2 % to 42.9 %; with an average decreased from 46.4 % to 42.9 %; with a low – from 14.3 % to 3.5 %. There were no significant changes in Group I."
