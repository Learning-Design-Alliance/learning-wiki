## Evidence

### Weixian Xu 2026

Weixian Xu, Yanzhe Zhang, Zora Zhiruo Wang, Changyu Chen, Diyi Yang. (2026). SHERPA: TEACHINGLLMS TOTEACHADAPTIVELY. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.08778](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.08778)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Design evaluation pairing a GPT-5.6-Luna teacher using six fixed teaching strategies with students holding one of six preferences (Figure 3), measuring selectivity via differentiated improvement and informativeness via differentiated complaint rate. Prompted students "show neither a clear diagonal advantage in improvement nor consistently lower complaint rates".

> "On the other hand, our archetypes show both desired properties: improvement is concentrated along the diagonal, while complaint rates are lower for matched pairs."
