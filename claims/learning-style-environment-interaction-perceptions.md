# Learning style interacts with discussion environment: TDE disadvantages global learners while CFDE shows no such inequality

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cognitive learning perception differed significantly by group and learning style (F=3.00, p=0.03); the highest perceptions were among CFDE users regardless of sequential or global style, and the lowest among global learners using TDE. [→ Yilmaz 2016](#yilmaz-2016)
`q3 i?` For affective learning perception the group-by-style difference was not significant (F=1.67, p=0.18). [→ Yilmaz 2016 (2)](#yilmaz-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Yilmaz 2016

Yilmaz, E. O., & Yurdugul, H. (2016). Design and effects of a concept focused discussion environment in e-learning. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 353-374. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.20

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way analysis of the Felder-Soloman sequential-global dimension across the 90 students: CFDE sequential (33.74) and global (33.33) groups did not differ, while TDE global learners (28.46) had the lowest cognitive perception per the Duncan test.

> "According to Table 7, cognitive perceptions differ based on students’ learning choices and whether they used TDE and CFDE to an extent that is statistically significant (F=3.00; p≤0.05)."
