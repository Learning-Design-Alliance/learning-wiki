q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical illustration plus simulation study by the authors showing conditions under which the Rothstein test rejects VAMs despite "no bias in estimated teacher effects". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"we illustrate—theoretically and through simulations—plausible conditions under which the Rothstein falsification test rejects VAMs even when there is no bias in estimated teacher effects"
