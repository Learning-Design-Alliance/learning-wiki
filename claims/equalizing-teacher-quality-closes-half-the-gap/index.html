Haycock 1998¶

Haycock, Kati. (1998). Good Teaching Matters: How Well-Qualified Teachers Can Close the Gap. Thinking K-16, v3 n2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED457260

q2 · i? · review · r1

The article's own interpretive estimate, presented in its introduction, that equalizing teacher quality across student groups would eliminate roughly half the achievement gap; it notes estimates vary somewhat depending upon the statistical model used. This is the authors' claim, not a printed effect size from a specific analysis.

"If we only took the simple step of assuring that poor and minority children had highly qualified teachers, about half of the achievement gap would disappear."
