# The training shows no significant transfer to academic learning tasks, with only a marginal post-test advantage in number conservation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant group×time interaction emerged for any academic task; a post-hoc t-test showed only a marginal difference in the Number Conservation Task (K3), t = 1.85, p = 0.067, favouring the experimental group. [→ Honoré N 2020](#honoré-n-2020)
`q3 i?` Post-test comparison in number conservation: experimental 1.31 ± 1.71 vs control 0.79 ± 1.33. [→ Honoré N 2020 (2)](#honoré-n-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Honoré N 2020

Honoré N, Houssa M, Volckaert A, Noël M-P and Nader-Grosbois N (2020) Training Inhibition and Social Cognition in the Classrooms. Front. Psychol. 11:1974. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01974

`q3 · i?`

Repeated-measures ANOVAs on language (K3 and P1), number conservation, additions (K3) and arithmetic problems (P1) found no significant group×time interaction; equivalence was not tested.

> "The repeated-measures analyses revealed no signiﬁcant interaction eﬀect between test time and group for any of the tasks assessing academic learning ( Table 7 )."

### Honoré N 2020 (2)

Honoré N, Houssa M, Volckaert A, Noël M-P and Nader-Grosbois N (2020) Training Inhibition and Social Cognition in the Classrooms. Front. Psychol. 11:1974. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01974

`q3 · i?`

Post-hoc independent-samples t-tests on post-test scores, justified by equivalent pre-test performance; only the K3 number conservation comparison was marginal (t = 1.85, p = 0.067), with no effect size printed.

> "indicate a marginal difference between the two groups in the Number Conservation Task (K3), with a higher score in the experimental group (1.31 ± 1.71) than in the control group (0.79 ± 1.33)."
