## Evidence

### Zhang Duo 2024

Zhang Duo, Zheng Haoyang, Zhao Ya, Wang Xiaoxiao, Xiao Qi, Zhao Ruiyi, Wang Lan, Hu Kaili, Chen Zelin, Zhou Yanrong. (2024). Unlocking academic achievement of nursing master’s students: ethical leadership, teacher-student relationship and growth mindset – a cross-sectional survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06436-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM mediation analysis in the 262-participant survey. The bootstrap (5000 samples) indirect effect via the teacher-student relationship was 0.050 with 95% CI [0.023, 0.085], described as "13.05%" of the total effect; the CI excluded 0, so mediation was considered established.

> "Ethical leadership positively predicted the teacher-student relationship (β = 0.214, t = 3.702, P < 0.001), and the teacher-student relationship positively predicted academic achievement (β = 0.232, t = 6.167, P < 0.001)."
