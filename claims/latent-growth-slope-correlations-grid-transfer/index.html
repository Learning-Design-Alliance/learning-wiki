Evidence¶

Julia Ericson and Torkel Klingberg 2023¶

Julia Ericson and Torkel Klingberg. (2023). A dual-process model for cognitive training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00161-2

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Multivariate latent growth curve model of improvement on the 4 tasks in 1300 children (RMSEA = 0.034, CFI = 0.987). The article reports significant slope correlations of "OOO (0.53 ± 0.17) and FI (0.43 ± 0.13)"; the printed correlation r = 0.53 corresponds to a large effect.

"we observed signi ﬁcant correlations between the slopes of the trained task and two of the transfer tasks (Fig. 4b), OOO (0.53 ± 0.17) and FI (0.43 ± 0.13), validating our hypothesis that the slope of Grid was related to the slopes of OOO and FI."
