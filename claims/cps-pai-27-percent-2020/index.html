Descriptive projection (Figure 7) combining the 2020 graduation rate (83.2 percent), 2020 immediate enrollment rates, and 2013 completion rates; of 100 ninth-graders, 83 would graduate, and 27 of the original graduates would complete any credential within six years post-graduation.

Malone 2021 (2)¶

Malone, M., Mahaffie, S., Hernandez, G., Usher, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2021). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2020. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/hs

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Disaggregated PAI calculation by race/ethnicity and gender (Table 1, Figure 9) using the same cohort structure as the district PAI; PAIs range from 13.0 percent (Black young men) to 65.8 percent (Asian/Pacific Islander young women), and "Young men of all race/ethnicity groups" graduated, enrolled, and completed "at a rate lower than young women of the same race/ethnicity."

"If the mos t recent available rates of attainment for CPS students were not to change over the next decade, no race/ethnicity and gender group would have 75 percent of students attain a college degree or certificate in 10 years."
