LUO Lan, WANG Anqi, ZHOU Muzhi, ZHU Junhua, CAI Jie, YU Ao, and PAN Hui. (2024). ParaTutor: Coordinating Parent-Child Math Tutoring through Role-Separated LLM Scaffolding. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18030 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.18030](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.18030)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

User study finding comparing parent-side breakdowns. Session coding showed Mode A included 11 cases where dyads could not reach a justified solution within the 10-minute limit, involving five families. Breakdowns reflected method uncertainty or repeated explanations that failed.

> "Actionable guidance helped parents recover from uncertainty and avoid repeated explanation loops.For parents, tutoring breakdowns in Mode A took two related forms. In some cases, parents became stuck because they did not know which method to use or could not recall how to solve the problem."
