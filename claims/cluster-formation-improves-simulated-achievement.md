## Evidence

### Velásquez-Rojas 2021

Velásquez-Rojas, F., & Laguna, M. F. (2021). The knowledge acquisition process from a complex system perspective: observations and models. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.06926)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

ABM1 cluster simulations compared with individual work; the article reports "final knowledge is always greater when individuals form clusters (the bigger the better) than when they work individually".

> "Another significant result is that final knowledge is always greater when individuals form clusters (the bigger the better) than when they work individually (what corresponds to having a number of clusters equal to N)."

### Velásquez-Rojas 2021 (2)

Velásquez-Rojas, F., & Laguna, M. F. (2021). The knowledge acquisition process from a complex system perspective: observations and models. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.06926)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Per-group simulation results; the article reports "the LA students are most favored by the interaction, with an improvement of about 7.3% when forming a large cluster" and that "a similar improvement of ~7.2% was also found for the AA group".

> "We found that the LA students are most favored by the interaction, with an improvement of about 7.3% when forming a large cluster. A similar improvement of ~7.2% was also found for the AA group."

### Velásquez-Rojas 2021 (3)

Velásquez-Rojas, F., & Laguna, M. F. (2021). The knowledge acquisition process from a complex system perspective: observations and models. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.06926)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Validation against the real course; the article reports "the real case is very well reproduced for the model", with the students' average final grade falling within the simulated values at the cluster number observed in the real data.

> "One is that the real case is very well reproduced for the model, as the average final grade of the students fall within those obtained with the simulations for a number of clusters coinciding with that of the real data."
