## Evidence

### Sawatsky Adam P. 2015

Sawatsky Adam P., Zickmund Susan L., Berlacher Kathryn, Lesky Dan, Granieri Rosanne. (2015). Understanding the challenges to facilitating active learning in the resident conferences: a qualitative study of internal medicine faculty and resident perspectives. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27289

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-comparison of themes from three faculty focus groups (20 faculty) with seven resident focus groups (41 residents). Residents attended for a break from clinical work and food; faculty cited role modeling and curriculum evaluation. The study found "motivations for attending conferences and the type of content that was desired" differed.

> "The main differences between faculty and residents included motivations for attending conferences and the type of content that was desired."

### Sawatsky Adam P. 2015 (2)

Sawatsky Adam P., Zickmund Susan L., Berlacher Kathryn, Lesky Dan, Granieri Rosanne. (2015). Understanding the challenges to facilitating active learning in the resident conferences: a qualitative study of internal medicine faculty and resident perspectives. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27289

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme comparison across the faculty and resident focus groups. Residents desired content with direct application to clinical practice at the resident or general internist level; faculty wanted subspecialty-relevant and cutting-edge research topics, illustrated by the warfarin-versus-dabigatran example quote.

> "Residents were interested in understanding the evidence that guides current clinical practice, including landmark articles."
