alrashed Latifah; Bodanese Eliane. (2026). Impact of real-time respiratory function monitoring on neonatal mask ventilation training: a multicentre simulation-based crossover study. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2026-004894

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video-recorded adjustment analysis across both manikins in the crossover simulation study. The article reports "24 of 30 adjustments (80%)" in the preterm and "40 of 58 adjustments (69%)" in the term manikin occurred under visible feedback; preterm adjustments mainly reduced inspiratory pressure, term adjustments mainly repositioned the mask.

> "In the preterm manikin, 24 of 30 adjustments (80%) occurred when the RFM was visible. Similarly, in the term manikin, 40 of 58 adjustments (69%) occurred when the RFM was visible."
