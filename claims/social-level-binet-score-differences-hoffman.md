The review attributes to Hoffman's 1913 comparative intelligence testing of 'one hundred and fifty-six subjects' ages seven, nine and ten the finding that higher-social-level Vorschule children tested about one-half year above the lower-social-level Volksschule children, except at age ten; the annotation adds that more must be examined before drawing the conclusion. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The "Vorschule" children coming from a higher social level tested about one-half year above the "Volksschule" children who come from a lower social level, except at age ten."
