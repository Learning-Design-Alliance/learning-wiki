## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The proportion of trainees correctly identifying the screening initiation age varied without a consistent upward trend across postgraduate years (PGY-1 80.6% to PGY-4 50.0%), and the authors report postgraduate year was not associated with improved knowledge. ## Evidence

### Denison Madaliene 2025

Denison Madaliene, Cai Jinman, Roberson Hannah H., Lockhart Ellen, Adkins Farrell. (2025). Trainee Awareness of US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) Colorectal Cancer Screening Guidelines. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02638-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive stratification of the same 116-respondent survey by postgraduate year shows non-monotonic proportions of correct responses, from "PGY-1, 25 of 31 (80.6%)" down to PGY-4 at 50.0% and back to 77.8% at PGY-5. Only descriptive proportions are printed; no test statistic or effect size.

> "Stratified by postgraduate year, the proportion of correct responses was as follows: PGY-1, 25 of 31 (80.6%); PGY-2, 20 of 31 (64.5%); PGY-3, 16 of 27 (59.3%); PGY-4, 8 of 16 (50.0%); PGY-5, 7 of 9 (77.8%); and PGY-6, 1 of 2 (50.0%)."
