# School effectiveness estimates from fall-to-spring versus spring-to-spring growth correlate only moderately to strongly (.399 to .705), below the .90 threshold at which rank orderings diverge in accountability

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Correlations between school-level fall-to-spring and spring-to-spring growth estimates range from .399 in reading to .705 in math (centered at 2nd grade), all below the .90 threshold Koedel and Betts (2010) identify as consequential for school rankings. [→ Soland 2019](#soland-2019)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlations (rho) of conditional Z-scores of school-level gains, produced via contrast matrices from CP models fit to all five years of MAP Growth data, comparing spring-to-spring gains to fall-to-spring gains in grades three and four (Figure 3). The printed correlations are r = .399 and r = .705.
