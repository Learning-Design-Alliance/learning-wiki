# Students using the eliciting agent showed JoL ratings correlated with system learning metrics, strongest for mastery

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In the elicit condition, JoL ratings correlated positively with system confidence (r = 0.287), progress (r = 0.326) and mastery (r = 0.408), all statistically significant. [→ Graf 2026](#graf-2026)

## Evidence

### Graf 2026

Graf, L., Bassner, P., Anzinger, M., Dietrich, F., Krusche, S., & Poquet, O. (2026). Interactive learning dashboards: rethinking learning visualisations as engagement tools. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-14082-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Across 104 JoLs in the elicit condition of the five-week case study, JoL accuracy (correlation of student to system ratings) was positive for confidence and progress; the article adds "The strongest correlation was observed for mastery, with r = 0.408 (p < 0.001)".

> "In the elicit condition (n = 104 JoLs), students appeared more accurate in their judgement. For confidence, the correlation was r = 0.287 (p = 0.003). For progress, the correlation was r = 0.326 (p = 0.001)."
