## Evidence

### Thomaschewski M 2025

Thomaschewski M, Vonthein R, Keck T, Laubert T, Benecke C. (2025). Laparoscopic simulation training improves operating room performance of surgical residents: a multicenter randomized trial (NOVICE). International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000002304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multicenter prospective randomized two-arm single-blind trial at 11 German surgical centers; 18 residents in the full analysis set. Blinded GOALS rating of videotaped cholecystectomies found the median improvement of "8.5 GOALS score points in contrast to 2 points in the control group" (P = 0.013).

> "The median improvement in the LTB-group between CHE I and CHE II was 8.5 GOALS score points in contrast to 2 points in the control group. This difference was statistically significant (95%CI: 1–15, P = 0.013)"
