`q1 i?` Students articulate tension between the taught idea that entropy increases toward disorder and the observed spontaneous formation of organized structures such as micelles. [→ Benjamin D. Geller 2013](#benjamin-d-geller-2013)

## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2013

Benjamin D. Geller, Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Julia Gouvea, Vashti Sawtelle, Chandra Turpen, and Edward F. Redish. (2013). Entropy and spontaneity in an introductory physics course for life science students. Department of Physics, University of Maryland, College Park. [doi:10.1119/1.4870389](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4870389)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case study interview with student Gavin, conducted prior to the entropy unit in NEXUS/Physics at UMCP, prompted by slides of micelle formation. Gavin states "the complete opposite happens where it becomes more organized" and says he lacks complete information. No quantitative claims are made.

> "if you are going to want everything to spread out, then you are going to assume that everything will spread out... but you know the complete opposite happens where it becomes more organized... I feel it disagrees."
