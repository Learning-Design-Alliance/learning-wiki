## Evidence

### Farey John E. 2018

Farey John E., Bui David T., Townsend David, Sureshkumar Premala, Carr Sandra, Roberts Chris. (2018). Predictors of confidence in anatomy knowledge for work as a junior doctor: a national survey of Australian medical students. BMC Medical Education 18:174. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1280-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional national survey of 1101 Australian medical students (84% completion) analysed with multivariable ordinal regression. Students reporting "adequate assessment of anatomy" had adjusted OR 2.17 (95% CI 1.69–2.81) for confidence versus those reporting too little assessment.

> "Items associated with increased self-reported confidence in anatomy knowledge included adequate assessment of anatomy (Odds Ratio 2.17 [95% CI 1.69–2.81])"
