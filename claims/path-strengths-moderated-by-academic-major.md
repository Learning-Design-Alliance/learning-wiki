# Academic major moderates e-learning acceptance paths: perceived usefulness affects intention more strongly for STEM students, perceived ease of use affects intention more strongly for non-STEM students, and the perceived ease of use to intention moderation is not supported

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The path from perceived usefulness to behavioural intention was supported as stronger for STEM students than for non-STEM students (H5). [→ Thongsri 2019](#thongsri-2019)
`q2 i?` The path from perceived ease of use to perceived usefulness was stronger for non-STEM students (H7 supported), but the moderation of the perceived ease of use to intention path was not supported (H8). [→ Thongsri 2019 (2)](#thongsri-2019-2)
