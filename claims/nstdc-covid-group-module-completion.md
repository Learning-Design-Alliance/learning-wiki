Review of self-study module and question bank completion data for members of learning groups identified as created in response to COVID-19. > "All undergraduate laboratory coursework learning groups required completion of all 7 self-study modules to "satisfy learning outcomes for a required STI lab that has been modified…[in] response to COVID-19." All 89 members completed all self-study modules."
