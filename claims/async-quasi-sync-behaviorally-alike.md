Social network analysis of the three debates' interaction ties (Table 8: outdegree centralization 0.09 async, 0.31 sync, 0.13 quasi-sync); Discord also had the greatest reciprocity at 0.36.

> "When comparing network measures, the asynchronous and quasi-synchronous debates were more alike than they were with the synchronous debate. One clear example was their similarly low outdegree centralization, suggesting that responses sent out to one another were more evenly distributed across the debate than in the synchronous format."
