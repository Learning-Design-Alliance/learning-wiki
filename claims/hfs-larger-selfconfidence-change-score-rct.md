### Yurt Seher 2026

BMC Medical Education, 26, 477. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08828-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial of 38 senior nursing students (19 per group), independent-samples change-score comparison after a simulated shock scenario versus routine theoretical instruction. The change-score analysis "revealed that the total Self-Confidence change score was significantly higher in the experimental group (M = 13.474)".

> "the total Self-Confidence change score was significantly higher in the experimental group (M = 13.474) than in the control group (M = 2.368; t = 2.441, p = 0.024)"
