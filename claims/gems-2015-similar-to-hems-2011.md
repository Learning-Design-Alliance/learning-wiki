## Evidence

### Langdalen H 2018

Langdalen H, Abrahamsen EB, Sollid SJM, Sørskår LIK, Abrahamsen HB. (2018). A comparative study on the frequency of simulation-based training and assessment of non-technical skills in the Norwegian ground ambulance services and helicopter emergency medical services. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3325-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's exact test comparison of GEMS 2015 (n = 998) with HEMS 2011 (n = 155) data from a previous study. Table 4 prints p-values of 0.024 for communication training and 0.004 for coping-with-fatigue training (and 0.007 for its assessment), with other NTSs non-significant.

> "The frequency of simulation-based training in and assessment of NTSs observed in the HEMS during 2011 [34] is statistically insignificantly different from the frequency observed in the GEMS during 2015 (Fig. 4 and Table 4), except for communication and coping with fatigue."
