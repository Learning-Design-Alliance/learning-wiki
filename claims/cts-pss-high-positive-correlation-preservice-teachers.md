## Evidence

### Irwanto 2018

Irwanto, Saputro, A. D., Rohaeti, E., & Prodjosantoso, A. K. (2018). Promoting Critical Thinking and Problem Solving Skills of Preservice Elementary Teachers through Process-Oriented Guided-Inquiry Learning (POGIL). International Journal of Instruction, 11(4), 777-794. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1191708

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Spearman's rho correlation across all 48 participants' scores in the quasi-experimental study found "a high positive and significant correlation between critical thinking and problem solving skills (r=.619; p=.000)". The article interprets this as problem solving rising when critical thinking rises.

> "The findings indicated that there was a high positive and significant correlation between critical thinking and problem solving skills (r=.619; p=.000)"
