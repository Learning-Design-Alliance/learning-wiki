## Evidence

### Karsikas 2025

Karsikas, E., Meriläinen, M., Koivunen, K., & Kanste, O. (2025). Health and social care managers' self‐assessed competence in knowledge management: A descriptive cross‐sectional study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 81, 787–797. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16237

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey results (Table 2, N=116): the managing a culture of competence dimension (8 items, α .935) was the strongest, with only 9.5% rating themselves fair.

> "Conversely, the highest dimension of KM competence was managing a culture of competence, with a median of 3.188 and a range of 4.00. In this dimension, the competence level was perceived as “good” or “very good” by 90.5% of the managers, with 9.5% considering it “fair.”"
