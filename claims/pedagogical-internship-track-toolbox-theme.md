Ramo Thomas, Pålsson Paul, Wallberg Hanna, Hult Helena Vallo. (2026). Developing early-career clinical educators – a qualitative study of a pedagogical internship track for junior doctors in Sweden. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09549-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview quote from Respondent 1 in Theme 1, illustrating that participants valued the formal coursework in pedagogy and simulation instruction as providing practical tools for supervision. This is a self-reported perception from a qualitative content analysis of ten interviews.

> "Both the basic pedagogical course and the simulation instructor course give you a lot of tools to use when supervising and it feels like you can put more thought into it, even if you have a stressful everyday life."
