Evidence¶

Breakstone 2021¶

Breakstone, J., Smith, M., Connors, P., Ortega, T., Kerr, D., & Wineburg, S. (2021). Lateral reading: College students learn to critically evaluate internet sources in an online course. Harvard Kennedy School (HKS) Misinformation Review. https://doi.org/10.37016/mr-2020-56

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Coded strategy analysis of pretest and posttest responses to the climate-change website task. The article also reports links-based judgments fell from 21 to 7, appearance-based from 14 to 4, and About-page reliance from 14 to 2; the modules "directly addressed why each of the above strategies could lead to erroneous conclusions."

"At pretest, 23 students believed that a dot-org domain conferred reliability, a misconception common not only among college students but among adults (Wineburg & Ziv, 2019). On the posttest, the number fell to seven, a decrease of 69%."
