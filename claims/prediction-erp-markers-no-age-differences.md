# Predictability enhances P3 amplitude, CNV amplitude, and shortens target-P3 latency equally at all ages, indexing mature prediction encoding, preparation, and implementation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r1`–`r2` · `q3` · `i3` large
> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person · **overdue for a recheck since 2020**: baselines and conditions change

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` P3 amplitude to predictive sequence stimuli S2 and S3 increased with predictability, with no effect of age group and no predictability × age interaction. [→ Thillay A 2015](#thillay-a-2015)
`q3 i3` CNV amplitude preceding targets and S3 stimuli was enhanced by predictability, with no age interaction, and target-P3 latency was shortened by predictability irrespective of age. [→ Thillay A 2015 (2)](#thillay-a-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Thillay A 2015

Thillay A, Roux S, Gissot V, Carteau-Martin I, Knight RT, Bonnet-Brilhault F and Bidet-Caulet A (2015) Sustained attention and prediction: distinct brain maturation trajectories during adolescence. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 9:519. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2015.00519

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

rmANOVAs comparing ERPs to the same physical stimuli embedded or not embedded in the predictive sequence, in the 36-participant sample. Progressive P3 amplitude enhancement through the predictive sequence ("F(1,33) = 22.56, p < 0.001, ηp = 0.406" for S3) indicates predictive-value encoding and memorization, with no predictability × age interaction.

> "P3 amplitude to S2 and S3 displayed a significant effect of predictability ( F(1,33) = 11.87, p = 0.002, ηp = 0.265, power = 0.917; or F(1,33) = 22.56, p < 0.001, ηp = 0.406, power = 0.996, respectively), but no effect of age group"

### Thillay A 2015 (2)

Thillay A, Roux S, Gissot V, Carteau-Martin I, Knight RT, Bonnet-Brilhault F and Bidet-Caulet A (2015) Sustained attention and prediction: distinct brain maturation trajectories during adolescence. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 9:519. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2015.00519

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

rmANOVAs on CNV amplitude at Cz and on target-P3 latency at CPz in the same sample. The study also reports "The P3 ERP to predTs was found earlier in latency than to randTs at CPz (F(1,33) = 19.27, p < 0.001, ηp = 0.369, power = 0.989)" with no age group effect or interaction, indicating prediction implementation in all ages.

> "A significant effect of predictability was found on CNV amplitude preceding targets ( F(1,33) = 23.57, p < 0.001, ηp = 0.417, power = 0.997), and preceding S3 ( F(1,33) = 9.24, p = 0.005, ηp = 0.219, power = 0.839), but not preceding S2"
