## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Open-ended versus guided laboratory activities: Impact on students' beliefs about experimental physics. arXiv:1608.01621. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of 22 BFY project courses (N = 231) with 31 BFY non-project open-ended courses (N = 306); post means 19.8 vs 19.6 (p = 0.5), pre 18.4 vs 18.7 (p = 0.1). Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence in the average E-CLASS scores either before or after instruction for students in these two sets of courses."
