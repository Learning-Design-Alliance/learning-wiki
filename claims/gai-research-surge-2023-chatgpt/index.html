Marzano 2025¶

Marzano, D. (2025). Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI) in Teaching and Learning Processes at the K-12 Level: A Systematic Review. Technology, Knowledge and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10758-025-09853-7

Descriptive bibliometric analysis of the review's 197-article corpus: 54% of articles (70) were published in 2023, 22% (29) in 2024, 13% (17) in 2022, with only 1% (1) in 2016. The author attributes the 2023 surge to ChatGPT's late-2022 release, increased funding, and journal prioritization.

"The sharp increase in publications in 2023 can be attributed to the release of ChatGPT in late 2022, which sparked significant interest in the role of GAI in education."
