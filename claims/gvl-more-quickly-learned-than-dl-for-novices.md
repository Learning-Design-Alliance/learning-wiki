## Evidence

### Sakles John C MD 2014

Sakles John C MD, Mosier Jarrod MD, Patanwala Asad E. PharmD, Dicken John BS. (2014). Learning Curves for Direct Laryngoscopy and GlideScope® Video Laryngoscopy in an Emergency Medicine Residency. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2014.9.23691

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article's synthesis in the Discussion cites a randomized manikin trial by Ambrosio et al. (novices 47.4% success with DL, 100% with GVL) and a study by Nouruzi-Sedeh et al. (novices 51% with DL, 90% with GVL) as supporting "other studies".

> "Our results, considered in the context of previous research, suggest that for operators with less experience, the GVL is more quickly learned and is associated with higher first pass intubation success than DL."
