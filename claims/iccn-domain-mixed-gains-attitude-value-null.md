## Evidence

### Gundo Rodwell 2021

Gundo Rodwell, Gundo Beatrice, Chirwa Ellen, Dickinson Annette, Mearns Gael Janine. (2021). Effect of an educational programme on critical care nurses' competence at two tertiary hospitals in Malawi. Malawi Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.4314/mmj.v33i4.3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Domain-level analysis of the same pre/post self-assessment study (N=41). Table 2 prints Knowledge 145.5 (15.7) to 170.9 (8.7), Skill 146.8 (21) to 168.6 (14.2), Experience 142.5 (21.0) to 170.0 (10.0), each p <0.0001; Attitude and value 173.5 (10.9) to 175.6 (6.5), p = 0.147.

> "With regards to ICCN-CS-1 domains, the increase on attitude and value domain was not statistically significant, p <.147 (two-tailed)."
