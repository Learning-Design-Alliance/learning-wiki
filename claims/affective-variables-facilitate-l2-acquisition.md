## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Krashen's Affective Filter Hypothesis holds that low anxiety, high self-confidence, and motivation facilitate L2 acquisition. [→ Diaz 1988](#diaz-1988)

## Evidence

### Diaz 1988

Diaz, Diana M. (1988). First Language/Second Language: Acquisition, Writing, and Cognitive Development. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED294203

`q1 · i?`

Theoretical exposition of the Affective Filter Hypothesis in the article's summary of Krashen's five hypotheses; no empirical data are reported. The article lists motivation, self-confidence, and anxiety as three primary affective variables, stating highly motivated students generally do better and high self-confidence facilitates acquisition.
