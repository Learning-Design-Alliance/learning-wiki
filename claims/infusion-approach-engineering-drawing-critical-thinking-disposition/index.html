Evidence¶

Norazlinda Mohd Darby & Abdullah Mat Rashid 2017¶

Norazlinda Mohd Darby & Abdullah Mat Rashid. (2017). Critical Thinking Disposition: The Effects of Infusion Approach in Engineering Drawing. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n3p305

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with non-equivalent control group design: 29 control and 31 treatment students at two Technical Matriculation Colleges, 8 weeks of Engineering Drawing instruction. One-way ANOVA on MACTI post-test showed "a significant diff erence (p < .05)" between the groups.

"The results from the analysis reveal ed that there was a significant diff erence (p < .05) in Critical Thinking Disposition between the two groups on the post-test: F (1, 58) = 6.79, p = .012"
