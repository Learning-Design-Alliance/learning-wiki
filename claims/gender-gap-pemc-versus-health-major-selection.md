## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Gender strongly influences choice of PEMC and health science majors in opposite directions: men's risk of majoring in PEMC is 3.60 times women's, and their risk of majoring in health fields is 0.74 times women's; predicted PEMC probabilities are 14.9% (men) versus 4.7% (women). ## Evidence

### Perez-Felkner L 2017

Perez-Felkner L, Nix S and Thomas K (2017) Gendered Pathways: How Mathematics Ability Beliefs Shape Secondary and Postsecondary Course and Degree Field Choices. Front. Psychol. 8:386. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00386

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression full models of major choice two years after secondary school, estimated on the ELS analytic sample; these results are each reported as highly significant (p < 0.001). Predicted probabilities put women's PEMC chance at 4.7% versus men's 14.9%, a 10.2 percentage-point decrease from being female.
