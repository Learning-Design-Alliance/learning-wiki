Vashti Sawtelle & Chandra Turpen. (2013). Leveraging a Relationship with Biology to Expand a Relationship with Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.5471)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data (Clip 7, two-thirds through the year) in which Violet recounts manipulating the Gibbs free energy expression in class and connects work in physics to enzyme catalysis in biology; the authors read this as her epistemological stance expanding in two ways. No effect sizes reported.

> "You get this equation in chemistry and biology and now in this class. But you learn it from all different ways, all different angles. And I feel like in this class it's so much combined with biology, you have to put those two realms together"
