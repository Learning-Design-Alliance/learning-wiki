National cross-sectional self-administered online survey of 1371 clinical and pre-clinical medical students across five regions of Saudi Arabia, collected July–August 2021. The abstract reports "59.88% (n = 821) preferred synchronous modalities of eLearning, and 33.33% (n = 457) avoided interaction".

> "Of the participants, 59.88% (n = 821) preferred synchronous modalities of eLearning, and 33.33% (n = 457) avoided interaction during synchronous lectures."
