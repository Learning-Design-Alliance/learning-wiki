## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students described democratic teams where leadership roles shifted with the situation rather than being assigned to one person. [→ Elina Riivari 2021](#elina-riivari-2021)

## Evidence

### Elina Riivari 2021

Elina Riivari, Marke Kivijärvi, Anna-Maija Lämsä. (2021). Learning teamwork through a computer game: for the sake of performance or collaborative learning? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10009-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of student reflections on internal team leadership actions; Table 1 records that team leadership was not assigned to one single person but relied on members' information and technological prowess, with roles shifting across tasks.

> "Often, the person who possessed the required piece of information or tool, or a person who was the first to come up with the idea of the task at hand, served as a leader."
