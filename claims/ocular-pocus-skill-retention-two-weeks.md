In the same randomized trial, all 32 participants repeated the ocular POCUS scans on the same standardized patients two weeks later without interim practice; scores were compared via an equivalence test using a generalized estimating equation. No effect size is printed; only lsmeans and CIs are reported.

> "The lsmeans (SE) of the total score were 21.4 (0.22) for the first scan and 21.5 (0.26) for the second scan."
