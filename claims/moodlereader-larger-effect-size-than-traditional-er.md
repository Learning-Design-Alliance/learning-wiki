Sepideh Alavi, Afsaneh Keyvanshekouh. (2012). Using the MoodleReader as an Extensive Reading Tool and its Effect on Iranian EFL Students' Incidental Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 5(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n6p135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "the magnitude of di fference between the pretests and posttests of productive vocabulary for the control group was 0.4, which is a moderate effect size, while that of the experimental group was 0.85, which is a large effect size."
