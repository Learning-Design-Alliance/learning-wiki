Heinrich Tobias, Baily Spencer, Chen Kuan-Wu, DeOliveira Jack, Park Sanghoon, Wang Navida Chun-Han (2025). AI-assisted grading and personalized feedback in large political science classes: Results from randomized controlled trials. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0328041

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-specified subgroup analysis of the randomized trial on the binary helpfulness outcomes; among lower-ACT/SAT percentile students both coefficients exceed the pooled estimates, while the authors note "both effects remain small".

> "Among lower-ACT/SAT percentile students, helpfulness increases (mean: 6.00, SE: 0.98) and unhelpfulness decreases (mean: −4.75, SE: 0.68), though both effects remain small."
