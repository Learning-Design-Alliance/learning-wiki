### Yu Jian 2025

Yu Jian, Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang. (2025). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of dental residents in managing traumatic dental injuries. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07382-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 256 dental residents at Wuhan University (55.1% response rate) using a validated electronic questionnaire. Self-rated familiarity was highest in General Dentistry (77.6%) and Oral Medicine (72.3%), lower in Oral Surgery (61.3%) and Others (54.8%), "varied by specialty (P = 0.038)".
