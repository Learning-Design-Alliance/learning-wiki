## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Salivary cortisol, heart rate and blood pressure measured in real life before exams corroborate the self-reported anxiety increases. [→ Hahn Henry 2017](#hahn-henry-2017)

## Evidence

### Hahn Henry 2017

Hahn Henry, Kropp Peter, Kirschstein Timo, Rücker Gernot, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2017). Test anxiety in medical school is unrelated to academic performance but correlates with an effort/reward imbalance. PLoS ONE 12(2): e0171220. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171220

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Real-life physiological sampling before written and oral physiology exams in the same 48-student cohort; salivary cortisol rose significantly before both exams, with "mean increases before oral and written exams were 2.4-fold and 2.1-fold". Heart rate peaked at both exams and returned to baseline one day after the oral exam.

> "The mean increases before oral and written exams were 2.4-fold and 2.1-fold, respectively"
