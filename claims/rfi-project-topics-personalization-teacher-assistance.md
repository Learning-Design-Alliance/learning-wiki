Thematic analysis of 178 submissions by project type (Figure 4). The report cautions the analysis "is not a landscape scan of existing products" and that findings are "in part aspirational" since most concepts were not yet on market.

> "two topics dominated as the focus: 1) Using AI to personalize learning, especially with a focus on making content culturally responsive (22%) and 2) providing teachers with assistance (25%) to reduce workload, provide real-time data to improve instruction, and/or responsive content generation"
