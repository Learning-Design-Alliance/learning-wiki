## Evidence

### Hendy 2020

Hendy, Elizabeth and Cuevas, Joshua Dr. (2020). The Effects on Instructional Conversations on English Language Learners. Georgia Educational Researcher: Vol. 17 : Iss. 2 , Article 5. https://digitalcommons.georgiasouthern.edu/gerjournal/vol17/iss2/5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA with Unit 4 language usage posttest as dependent variable, condition as grouping variable, and language usage pretest as covariate. Language usage was scored from open-ended responses by two raters using a teacher-created rubric with names hidden. No effect size printed.

> "When controlling for the initial language usage, the result was F(1,32) = .011, p = .919. These results indicate that there was not a statistically significant difference in correct academic language usage between the two groups."
