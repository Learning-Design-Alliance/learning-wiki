## Evidence

### McKinlay JB 2025

McKinlay JB, Kearns K. (2025). Improving the learning experience in an undergraduate course on microbial metabolism by using an illustrated story. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21869

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Impressions survey across four semesters (participation 85–100%) scored feeling words as negative, neutral, or positive; the study reports negative responses decreased and positive increased, and "changes in impressions for “Metabolism is…” was significantly improved by about 1 point" in three of four years. No effect size printed.

> "Across all four years, responses associated with negative emotions decreased from week 4 to week 16 while responses associated with positive emotions increased (Figure 4). In three of the four years, changes in impressions for “Metabolism is…” was significantly improved by about 1 point"
