# Congenitally blind children perform slightly less accurately on a non-visual behavioral ToM task than sighted children, controlling for age

## Evidence

### Richardson H. 2023

Richardson H., Saxe R., Bedny M. (2023). Neural correlates of theory of mind reasoning in congenitally blind children. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2023.101285

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Behavioral ToM task administered outside the scanner to 12 blind and 21 sighted children aged 4–12. Blind children scored "blind M(SE)=0.70(0.05); sighted M(SE)=0.80(0.04)" with a significant group effect controlling for age. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "Blind children performed slightly less accurately than sighted children (blind M(SE)=0.70(0.05); sighted M(SE)=0.80(0.04); effect of group, controlling for age: b=−0.53, t=−2.2, CI=[−1.0,−0.03], p=.04; Fig. 1)."
