# At-risk college students receiving equal combinations of human–animal interaction and content show higher SELFREGULATION scores than at-risk students receiving content only, at posttest and follow-up, while HAI-only shows no effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At-risk students in the HAI-E condition showed significantly higher SELFREGULATION at posttest (B = 0.501, p = 0.031) and follow-up (B = 0.630, p = 0.007) than at-risk students in the ASM condition. [→ Pendry Patricia 2020](#pendry-patricia-2020)

## Evidence

### Pendry Patricia 2020

Pendry Patricia, Carr Alexa M., Gee Nancy R., Vandagriff Jaymie L. (2020). Randomized Trial Examining Effects of Animal Assisted Intervention and Stress Related Symptoms on College Students’ Learning and Study Skills. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17061909

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ITT multivariate regression on the randomized trial found a significant risk × condition interaction on SELFREGULATION at posttest, R2 = 0.084, F(6,302) = 4.60, p < 0.001; "at-risk students assigned to the HAI-E condition (B = 0.501, p = 0.031) experienced significantly higher levels of SELFREGULATION at posttest".

> "Results show that at-risk students assigned to the HAI-E condition (B = 0.501, p = 0.031) experienced significantly higher levels of SELFREGULATION at posttest than at-risk students assigned to the ASM condition."

### Pendry Patricia 2020 (2)

Pendry Patricia, Carr Alexa M., Gee Nancy R., Vandagriff Jaymie L. (2020). Randomized Trial Examining Effects of Animal Assisted Intervention and Stress Related Symptoms on College Students’ Learning and Study Skills. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17061909

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same model, the HAI-only condition did not improve SELFREGULATION for at-risk students relative to ASM at posttest, and the null persisted at follow-up (B = 0.364, p = 0.148); equivalence was not tested.

> "No interaction effects were found for students assigned to the HAI-O condition, (B = 0.268, p = 0.215) at posttest."
