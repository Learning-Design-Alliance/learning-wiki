### Dávidovics A 2025

Dávidovics A, Dávidovics K, Hillebrand P, Rendeki S, Németh T. (2025). Virtual patient simulation to enhance medical students’ clinical communication and decision-making skills: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08507-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Matched pre-post online questionnaire of eleven undergraduate medical students in a single-arm VR pilot course, analysed descriptively with no inferential testing. Means are reported as “from 3.0 to 4.5” (communication) and “from 2.9 to 3.8” (decision-making); no effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
