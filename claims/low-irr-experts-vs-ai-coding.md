## Evidence

### Nygren 2025

Nygren, T., Samuelsson, M., Hansson, P.-O., Efimova, E., & Bachelder, S. (2025). AI Versus Human Feedback in Mixed Reality Simulations: Comparing LLM and Expert Mentoring in Preservice Teacher Education on Controversial Issues. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00484-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cohen's kappa was calculated for every category and pair of coders. Agreement between Expert 2 and ChatGPT-4o "ranges from none for most categories to minimal".

> "Inter-rater reliability between one of the experts (Expert 2) and ChatGPT-4o ranges from none for most categories to minimal (McHugh, 2012). An additional attempt with Claude 3.5 Sonnet did not improve IRR, yielding similar results."
