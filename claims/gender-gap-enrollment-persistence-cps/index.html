Nagaoka 2021¶

Nagaoka, J., Lee, J.S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2021). Navigating the maze: Understanding CPS graduates' paths through college. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/navigating-the-maze

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational finding from the six-year tracking of CPS graduates in the classes of 2010-2012. The study states the gender difference in immediate enrollment and persistence holds "both in four-year and two-year colleges"; no statistics are printed for this contrast.
