## Evidence

### Auerbach A. J. 2018

Auerbach A. J., Higgins M., Brickman P., Andrews T. C. (2018). Teacher Knowledge for Active-Learning Instruction: Expert–Novice Comparison Reveals Differences. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-07-0149

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported teaching practices measured with Section 3 of the TPI and compared with Welch's two-sample t tests; experts scored M = 12.5 (SD = 1.7) versus novices M = 10.1 (SD = 2.8) out of 15. The authors note this measure relies on self-report.

> "Finally, experts reported using significantly more research-based practices in large undergraduate biology courses than did novices (t = 3.5, p = 0.0012). The mean score on Section 3 of the TPI was 12.5 (SD = 1.7) for experts and 10.1 (SD = 2.8) for novices, out of 15 possible points."
