## Evidence

### Niyongombwa Irenee 2025

Niyongombwa Irenee, Sibomana Isaie, Wallace Lorraine S., Stein David. (2025). Exploring medical students’ simulation learning experience in Rwanda: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07981-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from Theme 4 of the qualitative study: participants wanted simulation delivered in a structured and consistent manner throughout medical education, noting most students are not exposed to procedural skills via simulation until the overloaded final year.

> "“The first step should be integrating simulation into medical curriculum…starting teaching students procedural skills early in the training because this will reduce patients’ adverse outcomes” (Student 12)."
