### Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly 2025

Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly. (2025). Astronomy Identity Framework for Undergraduate Students and Researchers. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/wf14-c1yp](https://doi.org/10.1103/wf14-c1yp)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding: two participants independently cited Carl Sagan and televised programs as formative influences. The authors call the "ubiquitous mention of popular culture" a differentiating feature of astronomy identity compared with physics identity frameworks.

> "For both Diego and Luis, popular culture was a key early influence on their astronomy interest and career aspirations. Televised programs presented astronomy as a relevant, authentic scientific pursuit, while introducing role models who sparked wonder in individuals by making astronomy simple and accessible."
