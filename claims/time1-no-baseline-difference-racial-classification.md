### Cherry et al. 2014

Stephen M. Cherry, Amy Lucas, Kim A. Case, and William T. Hoston. (2014). Who is White? Assessing Students' Perceptions of Whiteness through Active-Learning Effectiveness and the Construction of Racial Doubt. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.3, 2014, 68-83. http://www.understandingrace.org/home.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-lesson assessment in both courses (N = 97): treated students identified on average approximately 9 of 27 nationalities as White, 12 as non-White, and 6 as not sure; untreated students approximately 7, 13, and 6. The article reports t-tests found no statistically significant differences; equivalence was not tested.
