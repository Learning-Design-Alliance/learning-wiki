`q2 i?` After a pre-quiz showing nearly identical baseline knowledge (70.37% experimental vs 70.83% control), the experimental group using the online module scored 70.37% on the post-quiz while the control group scored 66.67%. [→ Gryczka et al. 2016](#gryczka-et-al-2016)

## Evidence

### Gryczka et al. 2016

Patrick Gryczka, Edward Klementowicz, Chappel Sharrock, Jin Kim Montclare. (2016). Interactive Online Physics Labs Increase High School Students' Interest. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 6(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.3926/jotse.191

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in an all-female high school physics class of approximately 17 students randomly split into control (8) and experimental (9) groups. The article reports the experimental group "demonstrated lower performance" was not the case for the module group; the control scored 66.67% versus 70.37%. No statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "The experimental group scored 70.37% overall while the control group demonstrated lower performance as demonstrated by the 66.67% score"
