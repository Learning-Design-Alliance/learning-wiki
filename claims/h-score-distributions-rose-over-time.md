Distributional analysis of 3,893 student-semester pairs and 42,695 homework submissions across six offerings, shown in Figure 2; the text reports "Spring 2026 (mean =11.9)". Maximum H-scores reached 81 in Spring 2026 versus 40–45 earlier. No effect size is printed.

> "A more pronounced shift appears in Spring 2025 (mean =4.2), and Spring 2026 (mean =11.9). This suggests a large change in student behavior towards the use of LLM-based assistance, rather than a change driven by a small number of outliers."
