Meg Wiggins, Constanza Gonzalez Parrao, Helen Austerberry and Anne Ingold. (2017). Foreign Language Learning in Primary School: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581156

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation (e) comparing this evaluation with two large-scale retrospective cohort studies in the United States (Rafferty, 1986; Taylor-Ward and LaFayette, 2010) that showed an impact of foreign language learning on English language attainment. The article offers this as a possible explanation, not a tested result.

> "This variation may be a result of the shorter length and reduced intensity of this intervention compared to those studied in the US (18 weeks/60 minutes per week vs. one to three years/minimum 150 minutes per week)."
