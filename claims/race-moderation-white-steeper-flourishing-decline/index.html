Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • As cumulative ACEs increase, children's probability of flourishing (learning interest, task completion, self-regulation) and family resilience decrease across race/ethnicity and income groups
  • The moderated-mediation effect of family resilience on the ACEs-curiosity-in-learning relationship is significantly greater for Black than for White children
  • Family resilience partially mediates the association between ACEs and child flourishing, with mediation proportions varying by race/ethnicity and income
  • ACE exposure is unevenly distributed: children below 400% FPL and Black and Hispanic children have higher prevalence of two or more ACEs
  • Moderation of ACE-school outcome associations is sparse, mixed, and outcome-specific — related
