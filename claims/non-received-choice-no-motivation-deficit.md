## Evidence

### Meyer 2013

Meyer, A., Meyer-Ahrens, I., & Wilde, M. (2013). The Beneficial Effects of Non-received Choice: A Study on Intrinsic Motivation in Biology Education. European Journal of Educational Research, 2(4), 185-190. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086332

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Orthogonal contrast within the choice condition (N=73) between students who received their preferred topic (N=30) and those who did not (N=43). The article reports "no difference between these two groups for any of the subscales", with all contrasts p=ns; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "There was no difference between these two groups for any of the subscales: interest/enjoyment ( CI:-.110±.427, p=ns), perceived competence ( CI: .063±.361, p=ns), perceived choice ( CI:-.109±.420, p=ns)"
