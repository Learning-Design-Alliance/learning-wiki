## Evidence

### Qian He 2025

Qian He, Jiahao Li, Ye Xiao, Jingjing Tian, Ningbo Mao, Sheng Lu, Yu Zhang, Yu Zhao. (2025). An immersive mixed reality surgical self-training system for precise pedicle screw placement: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07978-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Likert-scale questionnaires after assessment in the RCT: the IMR group reported significantly greater perceived confidence on all three items, higher curriculum-satisfaction scores, greater usability (total 42.44 ± 4.50 vs. 38.75 ± 5.53, p = 0.047), and greater confidence using the tutorial (4.63 ± 0.50 vs. 3.56 ± 0.89, p < 0.01).

> "For perceived confidence, the perceived score of the IMR group was significantly greater than that of the control group (instrument usage: 4.81 ± 0.40 vs. 4.19 ± 0.66, p = 0.003; autonomy: 4.56 ± 0.51 vs. 4.00 ± 0.89, p = 0.037; procedure: 4.81 ± 0.40 vs. 4.31 ± 0.60, p = 0.010)."
