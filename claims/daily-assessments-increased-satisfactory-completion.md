## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students who took daily assessments were more likely to earn a satisfactory grade (A, B, or C) than students who completed daily homework. [→ Bush 2011](#bush-2011)

## Evidence

### Bush 2011

Bush, H. F., & Walsh, V. K. (2011). Today‟s Program Is Brought To You By The Letters – Debit And Credit And By The Number Income. American Journal of Business Education, 4(5). https://clutejournals.com/index.php/AJBE

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same quasi-experimental comparison reports satisfactory completion rates of 82.7 percent with daily assessments versus 70.2 percent without, using A, B, and C as satisfactory grades.

> "Using the grades of A, B, and C as satisfactory grades, only 70.2 percent successfully completely the course without the assessment, while 82.7 percent successfully completed the course with the assessments."
