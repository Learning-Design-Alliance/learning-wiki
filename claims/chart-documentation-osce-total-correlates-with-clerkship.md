### Komasawa Nobuyasu 2021

Komasawa Nobuyasu, Terasaki Fumio, Nakano Takashi, Kawata Ryo. (2021). Correlation of student performance on clerkship with quality of medical chart documentation in a simulation setting. PLoS ONE 16(3): e0248569. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248569

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of 120 medical students who took the 2020 Post-CC OSCE and completed the 32-week basic CC in 2019, using Pearson's correlation test. The article reports the combined correlation was "statistically significant (P<0.001)"; total score R = 0.749, coefficient 0.561 (Table 3).

> "When all OSCE scores were combined, the correlation was statistically significant (P<0.001)."
