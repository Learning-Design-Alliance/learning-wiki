### Li Lei 2020 (2)

Li Lei, Tam Chi Wing, Wang Ning, Cheung Fan, Zhou Qing, Zhang Cheng, Cheng Chien-shan, Xiong Lei, Feng Yibin. (2020). Effectiveness of blending E-learning with field trip on Chinese herbal medicine education: quasi-experimental study. BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-020-03034-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same questionnaire (n=42 valid): 71.4% positive for self-test exercises, 66.7% for the atlas, 52.4% liked sharing work; means ranged 3.64–3.93 on the five-point scale.

> "students appreciated the self-test exercises most; 71.4% of the students commented that this helped them to prepare for the test."
