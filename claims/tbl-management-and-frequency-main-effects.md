## Evidence

### Jeong Somi 2025

Jeong Somi, Kim Seung Jung, Lee Kyoung Eun, Ko Ara. (2025). The moderating effect of team-based learning participation frequency on the relationship between medical students’ perception of systematic team-based learning class management and active class participation. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression main effects from the moderation model (Table 4) on 575 medical students' feedback, controlling for academic year and semester. Both predictors were significant; the β values are standardized regression coefficients and no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Results indicated that both students’ perception of systematic TBL management (β=0.624, p<0.001) and TBL participation frequency (β=0.651, p<0.001) had significant positive effects on active class participation."
