## Evidence

### Bauermann Moritz 2026

Bauermann Moritz, Schindler Ann-Kathrin, Kuchenbaur Marco, Rühl Jasmin, Reinert Patrick, Kunz Miriam, Friedrich-Welz Sarah, André Elisabeth, Rotthoff Thomas. (2026). Smiling and first impressions in ad hoc entrustment decisions: An avatar-based simulation study. GMS Journal for Medical Education 43(2): Doc16. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume43/zma001810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic mixed-effects model (H2-1) with random intercepts for participants and avatars, testing 72 entrustment decisions per participant. The smile effect was null: "an (OR) of 1.01 (95% CI [0.95–1.07], p=.796)". No effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "When testing facial expression as a predictor, H2 was not supported – a DS did not increase the likelihood of positive entrustment, as indicated by an (OR) of 1.01 (95% CI [0.95–1.07], p=.796)."
