## Evidence

### Starch 1910

Starch, D. (1910). The Measurement of Efficiency in Spelling, and the Overlapping of Grades in Combined Measurements of Reading, Writing and Spelling. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=The+Measurement+of+Efficiency+in+Spelling

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Concurrent-validity comparison in one school of 256 pupils: correlations by grade were 80, 69, 73, 40, 39, 84 (third through eighth), averaging 69. The author attributes the low sixth-grade coefficient to "the inaccurate marking of the teacher," whose pupils were marked almost all Good or Excellent.

> "Coefficients of correlation were computed between the marks and the test scores for grades 3 to 8 in a school of 256 pupils."
