## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2015

Trevor I. Smith, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Student Understanding of the Boltzmann Factor. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020123)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Written PRQ pretest administered after lecture instruction to seven successive classes at School 1 (N = 50) and once before instruction at School 2 (N = 32); responses coded by answer and by whether the Boltzmann factor was used. "only 48% of students at School 1 used the Boltzmann factor on part A."

> "only 48% of students at School 1 used the Boltzmann factor on part A and only 68% did so on part B, indicating that lecture instruction alone was not suﬃcient for all students to gain a robust understanding of when and how to use the Boltzmann factor."
