`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 23 prenatal clinicians found that "clinicians in this study described having little to no formal training in trauma-informed care (TIC) throughout their continuing education". One participant stated "As an employee, I am required to do one video on trauma-informed care a year. That’s it. That’s what we do. It’s a video".
