## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across all three assessments, differences between the experimental and control groups were more pronounced in the operation-oriented Refractive Classroom than in the memory-based Amblyopia Classroom. [→ Yang K 2026](#yang-k-2026)

## Evidence

### Yang K 2026

Yang K, Kanwen W, Jing C, Zidan G, Chenggong J, Xue L. (2026). A MOOC-based and high-fidelity surgical simulation-integrated clinical internship mode in optometry teaching for ophthalmology graduate students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09610-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-module comparison within the same randomized trial. The experimental group "performed better in both modules, with larger effects in the Refractive Classroom," the module focused on clinical and surgical skills trained via HFS.

> "In post-class tests, significant differences were observed for the Refractive Classroom (t = 5.74, P < 0.001) and Amblyopia Classroom (t = 2.67, P = 0.01). The experimental group performed better in both modules, with larger effects in the Refractive Classroom."
