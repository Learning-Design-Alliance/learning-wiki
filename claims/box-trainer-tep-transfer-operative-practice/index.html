Pelly T. 2021¶

Pelly T., Vance-Daniel J., Linder C. (2021). Characteristics of laparoscopic and open hernia repair simulation models: a systematic review. Hernia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8251686/

q2 · i? · review · r2

Study of an elaborate box trainer for TEP repair tested on 21 surgical residents in control and training groups using videos and the moulded rubber hernia simulator; the review reports "higher composite scores on assessment than the control group (P = 0.01)". No effect size is printed.

"After the programme, the training groups were found to have higher composite scores on assessment than the control group (P = 0.01)."
