`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparisons (Fisher's ANOVA) in the RCT results: delta global GOALS 3.7 ± 2.8 vs. 3.4 ± 2.4 vs. 4.1 ± 3.1, p = 0.725; delta task-specific OSATS p = 0.758. "no significant differences in delta values" were found; equivalence was not tested.

> "The three groups had no difference in pre-test and post-test performance assessment scores compared to one another. Comparison of the pre- and post-test differences (delta) for each test between the groups revealed no significant differences in delta values (Table 3)."
