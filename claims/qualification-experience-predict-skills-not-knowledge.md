## Evidence

### Al‐Rawashdeh 2026

Al‐Rawashdeh, A. B., Shahin, M. A. H., Elgazzar, S. E., Ibrahim, A. M., & Hussien, R. M. (2026). Bridging Critical Care and Mental Health: Jordanian ICU Nurses' Self‐Reported Knowledge, Skills, Attitudes and Confidence in Caring for Patients With Mental Illness. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70948

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Table 6, skills model) in the survey of 502 nurses; gender, age, ICU experience and prior mental health experience were not significant (all p > 0.05).

> "Higher educational qualification (β = 0.129, p = 0.011) and longer professional nursing experience (β = 0.229, p = 0.005) were associated with higher self‐reported skills."

### Al‐Rawashdeh 2026 (2)

Al‐Rawashdeh, A. B., Shahin, M. A. H., Elgazzar, S. E., Ibrahim, A. M., & Hussien, R. M. (2026). Bridging Critical Care and Mental Health: Jordanian ICU Nurses' Self‐Reported Knowledge, Skills, Attitudes and Confidence in Caring for Patients With Mental Illness. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70948

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression for the perceived knowledge‐and‐beliefs domain; all predictors (gender, age, qualification, nursing years, ICU years, mental health experience) failed to reach significance. The article notes the domain includes belief items and is not objective knowledge.

> "The overall model was not statistically significant, and none of the included demographic or professional variables were significantly associated with this score."
