Gordillo, A.; López-Fernández, D.; Mayor, J. (2024). Examining and Comparing the Effectiveness of Virtual Reality Serious Games and LEGO Serious Play for Learning Scrum. Appl. Sci. 2024, 14, 830. https://doi.org/10.3390/app14020830

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Perception questionnaire item 18, asked only of the 22 VR-group students in the quasi-experiment; the Spearman correlation analysis found "no statistically significant correlations" between dizziness and any other item, including future-use intention. All students completed the activity without major issues.
