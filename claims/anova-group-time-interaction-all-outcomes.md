`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (Table 4) in the pilot RCT testing group (experimental vs. control) by time (pre vs. post) interactions across all outcome domains, with Greenhouse-Geisser corrections where sphericity was violated.

> "The analysis shows a significant interaction × Group Time for all key variables. This confirms that the improvement in physiological parameters (HR, HRV), stress (AESI, PSS), cognitive functions (Digit Span, Stroop), well-being, and grades in Italian is attributable to the experimental intervention"
