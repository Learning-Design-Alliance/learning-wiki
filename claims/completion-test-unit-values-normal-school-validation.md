## Evidence

### Walter R. Miles and Julian E. Butterworth 1912

Walter R. Miles and Julian E. Butterworth. (1912). A Tentative Standardization of a Completion Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, pp. 329-336. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Tentative+Standardization+of+a+Completion+Test%22

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Dr. Butterworth administered the same test the following winter to "one hundred first year normal school girls as a mass test", using stop-watch timing as each finished a red-line-delimited unit; the computed unit percentages closely matched the university values (e.g., unit 1: 12.8 in both).

> "The values last secured from the hundred girls are strikingly like those obtained by more accurate methods from the twenty-five more advanced subjects in the University of Iowa."
