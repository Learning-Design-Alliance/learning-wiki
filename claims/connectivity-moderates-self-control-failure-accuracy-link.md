## Evidence

### Wei 2026

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Percentile bootstrapping moderation analysis (1000 resamples) with accuracy as dependent variable, SVUSCF as predictor, and caudate–claustrum connectivity as moderator. The interaction was only "marginally significant" (β = -0.023, p = .055); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "A moderation analysis revealed a marginally significant interaction between SVUSCF scores and caudate–claustrum connectivity on memory accuracy (β = -0.023, p =.055). Simple slope tests indicated that the relationship between SVUSCF scores and accuracy was non-significant at low connectivity (β = 0.001, p > .05) but became significantly negative at high connectivity (β = -0.008, p < .05)."
