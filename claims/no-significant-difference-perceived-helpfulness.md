`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Likert-scale helpfulness ratings (1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree), aggregated without differentiating by snippet, analyzed with Kruskal-Wallis H tests; the test found "no statistically significant differences in helpfulness ratings across groups." Mean helpfulness was highest for DD (3.68) and lowest for DG (3.50).
