## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interviewed TAs responded similarly to written respondents across all ranking categories even when explicitly reminded to think about well-designed problems in general. [→ Melanie Good 2018](#melanie-good-2018)

## Evidence

### Melanie Good 2018

Melanie Good, Emily Marshman, Edit Yerushalmi, and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Physics teaching assistants' views of different types of introductory problems: Challenge of perceiving the instructional benefits of context-rich and multiple-choice problems. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Follow-up think-aloud interviews with 12 former participants, conducted at least one semester later; interviewed TAs "responded in a similar manner to those who gave written responses" across all categories despite reminders about well-designed problems in general.

> "interviewed TAs responded in a similar manner to those who gave written responses for all of the preceding categories (i.e., “instructional beneﬁt,” “challenge,” “like,” and “use”) even when explicitly reminded to think more generally about well-designed multiple-choice problems"
