## Evidence

### Grissom TE 2020

Grissom TE, Samet RE. (2020). The Anesthesiologist's Role in Teaching Airway Management to Nonanesthesiologists: Who, Where, and How. Advances in Anesthesia 38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aan.2020.08.002

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of studies using cumulative summation analysis to define direct laryngoscopy competency, "allowing for multiple passes, external coaching, and assistance." No effect size is printed.

> "Using CUSUM and a cutoff failure rate less than 20%, the number of intubations from these studies suggest that between 22 and 45 attempts are needed to achieve this target, allowing for multiple passes, external coaching, and assistance"

### Grissom TE 2020 (2)

Grissom TE, Samet RE. (2020). The Anesthesiologist's Role in Teaching Airway Management to Nonanesthesiologists: Who, Where, and How. Advances in Anesthesia 38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aan.2020.08.002

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that at a stricter success target the required attempt count rises, and that improvement continues beyond 100 procedures. No effect size is printed.

> "When choosing a more conservative failure rate of less than 10%, the number jumps to 57 to 75 attempts, with continued improvement being seen after 100 procedures"
