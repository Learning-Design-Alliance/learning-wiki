# Student proportion of words spoken in whole-class talk increased in both dose groups after VSDR

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both groups showed an increased percentage of student contributions: low dose 20.27% to 30.85%; high dose 13.98% to 25.98%. [→ Pengjin Wang · Fan Chen · Deliang Wang · Gaowei Chen 2025](#pengjin-wang-fan-chen-deliang-wang-gaowei-chen-2025)

## Evidence

### Pengjin Wang · Fan Chen · Deliang Wang · Gaowei Chen 2025

Pengjin Wang · Fan Chen · Deliang Wang · Gaowei Chen (2025). Enhancing students' dialogic reflection through classroom discourse visualisation. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09443-2

`q2 · i?`

Analysis of recordings from the first two versus last two lessons (~30 min each) captured with a stationary camera. "the low-dose group’s contributions rose from 20.27% to 30.85%, whilst the high-dose group’s contributions increased"; teacher total words decreased in both groups. No statistical test or effect size is reported for this change.
