Analysis of recordings from the first two versus last two lessons (~30 min each) captured with a stationary camera. "the low-dose group’s contributions rose from 20.27% to 30.85%, whilst the high-dose group’s contributions increased"; teacher total words decreased in both groups. No statistical test or effect size is reported for this change.
