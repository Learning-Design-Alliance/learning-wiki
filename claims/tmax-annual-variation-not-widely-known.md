### Thierry Lahaye 2012

Thierry Lahaye. (2012). Measuring the eccentricity of the Earth orbit with a nail and a piece of plywood. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/33/5/1167](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/33/5/1167)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Daily elevation measurements in Toulouse at two dates (Fig. 2) show the time of maximal elevation shifts by a few minutes across dates. The author attributes students' unfamiliarity to the effect being small though perfectly measurable.

> "what appears also clearly on Fig. 2 is that the timetmax at which this maximum occurs also depends on the date; this however is not widely known by the students, nor even by some physicists, probably because the effect is relatively small (a few minutes) though perfectly measurable even with our crude setup."
