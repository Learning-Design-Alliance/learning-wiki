### Yigiter 2025

Yigiter, Mahmut Sami. (2025). The effect of socioeconomic status on academic achievement: A big data study across countries and time with integrative data analysis. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335485

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel meta-analytic random effects model over 267 country-cycle coefficients per variable. In mathematics, "school SES has a moderate effect on mathematics achievement (B = 0.37, 95% CI [0.34, 0.40])", roughly double the student SES coefficient of 0.18 printed in the same sentence.

> "The multivariate meta-analytic random effects model demonstrates that student SES has a moderate effect on mathematics achievement (B = 0.18, 95% CI [0.16, 0.20]) and school SES has a moderate effect on mathematics achievement (B = 0.37, 95% CI [0.34, 0.40])."
