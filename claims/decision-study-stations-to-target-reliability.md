Schneider Simon, Stricker Daniel, Berendonk Christoph, Baron-Stefaniak Joanna, Walter Robin. (2026). Abdominal ultrasound performance assessment: a comparison of generic and extended OSCE rating scales. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08994-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A generalizability-theory decision study (reported in Appendix 5) projected how reliability grows with added stations; the extended scale required 13 stations to reach Phi ≥ 0.65 versus ten for the generic scale, indicating greater efficiency for the generic format.

> "In a subsequent decision study, reliability increased steadily as more stations were added, with the extended rating scale reaching a projected Phi ≥ 0.65 at 13 stations and the generic rating scale reaching the same target at ten stations (see Appendix 5)."
