## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized sham-controlled experiment, the alpha NF group showed a significant enhancement in Corsi-block tapping performance at posttest 2 (7 days after training) relative to pretest, while the sham group showed no significant change. [→ Zhou 2024](#zhou-2024)

## Evidence

### Zhou 2024

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Behavioral results of a randomized, single-blind, sham-controlled experiment with 40 participants equally assigned to NF and sham groups, five daily training sessions. For the sham group, "Friedman’sr a n kt e s t did not reveal signiﬁcance (χ2ð2Þ¼ 2:77; p ¼ 0:25)".
