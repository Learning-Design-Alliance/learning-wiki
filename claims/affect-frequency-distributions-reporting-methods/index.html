Descriptive frequency analysis of all RCR affect reports (Figure 2(a)) shows "Curious, optimistic, and confused were the most frequently reported states," with all 7 states appearing across groups. A second comparison (Figure 2(b)) found self-caught reports more evenly distributed than probe-caught reports.

"Curious, optimistic, and confused were the most frequently reported states, followed by surprised, disengaged, frustrated, and conﬂicted. All 7 states appeared across groups, indicating coverage of the full label set in the dataset."
