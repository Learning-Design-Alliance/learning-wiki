Friedman test of students' L2 error reports on the formative assessment platform showed a significant difference, X2(2) = 41.847, p = 0.000; Wilcoxon post-hoc tests with Bonferroni-adjusted alpha of 0.017 confirmed the ME > LE > PE ordering.

> "students mainly reported morpho-syntactic errors, followed by lexical errors, then followed by phonological errors (ME > LE > PE) at the two measured times (T1 and T2)."
