## Evidence

### Hafize Er-Türküresin 2020

Hafize Er-Türküresin. (2020). The effect of using creative drama method on student achievement in the social studies course: A meta-analysis study. International Online Journal of Education and Teaching (IOJET), 7(4), 1881-1896. http://iojet.org/index.php/IOJET/article/view/1056

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 19 Turkish experimental studies (4 articles, 15 theses) on creative drama in Social Studies, computed with Hedges' g in the CMA program under a random-effects model. The article reports the average effect size as "1.171" with a 95% CI of 0.892-1.449, a very large effect favoring experimental groups.

> "In this study in which the random-effects model was used, the average effect size value was calculated as 1.171, standard error as 0.142, the lower limit of the confidence interval of the average effect size as 0.892 and the upper limit as 1.449."
