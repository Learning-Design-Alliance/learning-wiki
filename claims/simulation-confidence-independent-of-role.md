# Students' self-reported confidence after simulation cases is independent of the role they took during the simulation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a repeated measures ANOVA, self-reported confidence after each case did not differ by role (leader, member, observer) during the simulation. [→ Stroben Fabian 2016](#stroben-fabian-2016)

## Evidence

### Stroben Fabian 2016

Stroben Fabian, Schröder Therese, Dannenberg Katja A., Thomas Anke, Exadaktylos Aristomenis, Hautz Wolf E. (2016). A simulated night shift in the emergency room increases students' self-efficacy independent of role taking over during simulation. BMC Medical Education 16:177. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0699-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the simulation pilot data: case as within-subject and role as between-subject factor showed confidence was independent of role, F(2,52) = 0.123, p = 0.884; no effect size printed.

> "In a repeated measures ANOVA with case as the within subject and role as the between subject factor, the self-reported confidence of participants is independent of their role during the simulation (F(2,52) = 0.123 p = 0.884)."
