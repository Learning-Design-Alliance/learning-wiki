## Evidence

### Edwards Thomas C. 2021

Edwards Thomas C., Patel Arjun, Szyszka Bartosz, Coombs Alexander W., Liddle Alexander D., Kucheria Rakesh, Cobb Justin P., Logishetty Kartik. (2021). Immersive virtual reality enables technical skill acquisition for scrub nurses in complex revision total knee arthroplasty. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-021-04050-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-participant repeated-measures analysis of the three unguided iVR assessment sessions in ten scrub nurses, part of a single-arm pre-post study at a university-affiliated hospital simulation centre. Quote reports the 47% operative-time reduction and begins the 75% prompt reduction; errors, hand and head motion reductions are reported in the same passage.

> "All participants improved across the three unguided assessment sessions (Fig. 3), reducing their operative time by 47% (mean 55.5 ± 17.6 min in unguided Session 1 to 29.3 ± 12.1 min in Session 3, p > 0.001), assistive prompts by 75%"
