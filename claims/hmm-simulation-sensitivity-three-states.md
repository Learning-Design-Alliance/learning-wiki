## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Simulated datasets generated by speedup functions were consistently identified as multi-state, but power versus exponential forms could not be distinguished. [→ Tenison 2015](#tenison-2015)

## Evidence

### Tenison 2015

Tenison, C., & Anderson, J. R. (2015). Modeling the distinct phases of skill acquisition. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000204

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simulation analysis (Appendix A) generating samples from one-, two-, and three-state models using power, exponential, and no-speedup functions to test HMM sensitivity. The authors conclude the data represent three states with within-state speedup.

> "Without exception, when the data was best fit by a three-‐state speedup function it was generated by a speedup function. The ability, however, to discriminate between the power function and exponential functions was poor."
