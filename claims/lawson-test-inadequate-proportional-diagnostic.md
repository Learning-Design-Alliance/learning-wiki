## Evidence

### Andrew Boudreaux 2014

Andrew Boudreaux, Stephen E. Kanim, and Suzanne White Brahmia. (2014). Student facility with ratio and proportion: Mapping the reasoning space in introductory physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1511.08960](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1511.08960)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Theoretical argument in the review of prior research: the Lawson test 'contains just four proportional reasoning items' involving only a single context, water poured from one cylinder to another, analyzable by the equivalent fractions method, and the revised multiple-choice version has ambiguous answer choices.

> "While the Lawson test may be useful for highlighting the existence of student difficulties, it is a primitive as a diagnostic of proportional reasoning as used in physics."
