## Evidence

### Biagioni Gabriela 2026

Biagioni Gabriela, Comodo Fernanda, Coelho Marcelo Santos. (2026). Technical Quality and Students' Perception of Endodontic Preclinical Training Using Natural or LikeReal Artificial Teeth. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire with 10 questions sent to students of both groups after completing clinical endodontic training; 12 students responded per group (26.67% NTG, 38.7% ATG). Natural teeth group rated access and instrumentation simulation higher; no difference for irrigation, filling, or radiographs.

> "The NTG group responded more frequently than the ATG that their teeth properly simulated the clinical findings for access and instrumentation (p < 0.05); there was no difference in the students' perception for irrigation, root canal filling, and radiographs (p > 0.05). Overall, the students found that natural teeth are better for endodontic preclinical training (p < 0.05)."
