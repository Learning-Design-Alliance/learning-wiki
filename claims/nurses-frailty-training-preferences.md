Descriptive analysis of an 11-item educational-needs questionnaire among the 558 willing participants. A blended online-offline format was preferred by 66.85%, well above purely online (24.19%) or face-to-face (8.96%); top practical skills were health education (91.22%) and nutritional intervention (91.04%).

> "Of the 610 respondents, 558 (91.48%) expressed willingness to participate in frailty-management training, whereas 52 (8.52%) did not; the educational-needs analysis was restricted to the 558 willing participants"
