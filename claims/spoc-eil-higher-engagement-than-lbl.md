## Evidence

### Wang Siqi 2025

Wang Siqi, Yang Di, Zhao Wenbo, Yan Xiaoyuan, Zhang Xiyue, Gao Wenqing, Guo Jiajie. (2025). The combination of a SPOC with evaluative interactive learning in dental adhesion education: a comparison with a lecture-based learning mode. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07261-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled comparison of 79 fourth-year dental students (LBL n=39, EIL n=40) in a dental adhesion course; researchers counted speeches and interactions. The EIL response rate was "4.72" versus 1.09 in LBL, and postclass interactions were 20 versus 1.

> "The response rate in the LBL group was 1.09, whereas the response rate in the EIL group was 4.72 (Fig. 3D)."
