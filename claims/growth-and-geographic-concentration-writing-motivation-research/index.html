Systematic review characteristics synthesis: the review reports publication growth (n = 38 in the last decade vs. n = 18 earlier) and place distribution — almost 60% of studies in North America (USA n = 28), Asia 28% (Turkey n = 9), Europe 11%, and one study from Australia; none from Africa or South America.
