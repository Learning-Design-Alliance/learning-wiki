# MFAS produced a statistically significant positive effect of 0.24 on Grade 1 student mathematics achievement

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Grade 1 students of MFAS teachers scored 0.24 standard deviations higher on the CCSS student mathematics assessment. [→ Laura B. Lang 2014](#laura-b-lang-2014)

## Evidence

### Laura B. Lang 2014

Laura B. Lang, Robert R. Schoen, Mark LaVenia, and Maureen Oberlin. (2014). Mathematics Formative Assessment System – Common Core State Standards: A Randomized Field Trial in Kindergarten and First Grade. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562773

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Three-level multilevel model of Z-scored assessment scores for 2,515 Grade 1 students, controlling for school percent FRPL, found an effect size of 0.24 (p < .01) for MFAS, which the authors infer represents about two additional months of learning.

> "A statistically significant positive main effect was found for both grade levels: 0.20 (p < .05) in Kindergarten and 0.24 (p < .01) in Grade 1."
