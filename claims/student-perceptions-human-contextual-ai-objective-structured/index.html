Li 2026¶

Li, X., Zhang, Y., & Liu, T. (2026). Comparative analysis of peer group and AI-generated feedback in peer assessment: Insights into feedback quality and student perceptions in higher education. Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 42(4), 129–146. https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.11467

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Thematic analysis of student perceptions in the mixed methods study of 13 peer groups; students described group feedback as "contextually rich but sometimes subjective" and AI feedback as structured but sometimes contextually insensitive.

"Thematic analysis further revealed that students perceived group feedback as contextually rich but sometimes subjective, whereas ChatGPT-4o feedback was viewed as more objective and well structured, though occasionally lacking contextual sensitivity."
