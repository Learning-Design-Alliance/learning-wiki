Descriptive analysis of aggregated exam data for all first-year courses (Table 6, Fig. 4), comparing baseline cohorts B1–B4 with intervention Years 1–4. The authors are cautious about direct grade comparisons because lecturers of other courses, including Analysis, changed over the years and bonus-point policies varied.
