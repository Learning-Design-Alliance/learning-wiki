## Evidence

### Beck Stefanie 2016

Beck Stefanie, Ruhnke Bjarne, Issleib Malte, Daubmann Anne, Harendza Sigrid, Zöllner Christian. (2016). Analyses of inter-rater reliability between professionals, medical students and trained school children as assessors of basic life support skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0788-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ICC comparisons between assessor pairs at the school events: peer-professional (n = 339), peer-student (n = 191), peer-peer (n = 303). The article reports "ICC: 0.76", "ICC: 0.88" and "ICC: 0.91" with their confidence intervals.

> "demonstrated strong inter-rater reliability between peer- and professional assessors (ICC: 0.76; 95 % CI: 0.71 to 0.81), optimal inter-rater reliability between peer- and student-assessors (ICC: 0.88; 95 % CI: 0.84 to 0.91) and excellent inter-rater reliability between peer- and other peer-assessors (ICC: 0.91; CI: 0.89 to 0.93)"
