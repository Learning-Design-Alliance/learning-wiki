## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2021

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021). Pathway to Academic Success Project: Intervention Report | English Learners Topic Area. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/724

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Observational implementation data from the Olson et al. (2016) trial, rated by trained observers using the Pathway Quality Checklist. The authors "found that Pathway to Academic Success Project teachers implemented" the strategies "at a higher rate than comparison group teachers" only at the final spring year-2 observation.

> "Authors found that Pathway to Academic Success Project teachers implemented Pathway to Academic Success Project–specific strategies and activities at a higher rate than comparison group teachers at the final observation in the spring of year 2."

### What Works Clearinghouse 2021 (2)

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021). Pathway to Academic Success Project: Intervention Report | English Learners Topic Area. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/724

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

End-of-year-1 classroom observations in the Kim et al. (2011) randomized trial found "no differences" in observed use of program reading or writing activities between conditions; teacher surveys, however, showed nearly twice as many intervention teachers reporting time devoted to writing skills.

> "Authors found no differences in the use of specific Pathway to Academic Success Project reading or writing activities during classroom observations between intervention and comparison classrooms."
