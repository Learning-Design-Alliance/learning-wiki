## Evidence

### Onivehu and Ohawuiro 2018

Adams Ogirima Onivehu, Onyiyeche Emilia Ohawuiro. (2018). Effect of PowerPoint Presentation on Students' Cognitive Achievement in Geography. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, VII(1), 46-60. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120184

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact classes of 45 senior secondary two students each in Ilorin, Nigeria; experimental group taught selected Geography concepts with PowerPoint, control with chalkboard lecture. The article reports "a significant difference between the scores of both groups (t (88)=8.34, p<.005)". No effect size is printed.

> "the mean score of the experimental group was higher than the mean score of the control group. These results show that there is a significant difference between the scores of both groups (t (88)=8.34, p<.005) after instruction."
