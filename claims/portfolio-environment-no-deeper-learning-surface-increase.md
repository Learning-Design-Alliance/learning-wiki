### Remy M. J. P. Rikers 2008

Remy M. J. P. Rikers, Tamara van Gog, Fred Paas. (2008). The effects of constructivist learning environments: a commentary. Instr Sci (2008) 36:463–467. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9067-4

`q3 · i?`

The commentary reports Baeten and colleagues' pretest/posttest study in a portfolio-based learning environment. It found no change in assessment preferences or depth of learning and "a significant increase in the use of surface approaches to learning". No effect size is printed.

> "Using a pretest/posttest-design the authors demonstrate that portfolio-based learning environments do not change the student's assessment preferences and do not lead to a deeper level of learning. Surprisingly, the results showed a significant increase in the use of surface approaches to learning."
