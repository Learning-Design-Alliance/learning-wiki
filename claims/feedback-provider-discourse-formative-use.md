`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discourse analysis of the 54-article archive; the feedback-provider discourse frames patients as a rich source of feedback supporting trainee development, with data used formatively rather than for progression decisions.

> "The assessment data that patients provided were thus “collected and summarized to provide residents with individual feedback on their competencies” [12]. In other words, the assessment data was not used to make decisions about trainee progression in a program, in most contexts."
