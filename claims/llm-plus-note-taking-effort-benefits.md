### Yvonne Rogers 2025

Yvonne Rogers. (2025). Why it is Worth Making An Effort when Learning With GenAI. International Journal of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, Volume 06, Special Issue. https://doi.org/10.56734/ijahss.v6nSa1

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Kreijkes et al. (2024), a comparison of using LLMs with traditional note-taking versus just note-taking or just LLMs for comprehension and retention tasks with 14-15 year olds. The review attributes these findings to the cited study; no effect sizes are printed here.

> "They found 14-15 years olds valued LLMs for making complex material more accessible and reducing cognitive load, but that making notes , themselves without the AI, required more cognitive effort but resulted in deeper engagement while enhancing their memory of the content."
