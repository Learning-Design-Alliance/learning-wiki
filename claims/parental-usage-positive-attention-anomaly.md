## Evidence

### Gong Qiong 2024

Gong Qiong, Tao Ting. (2024). The relationship between short video usage and academic achievement among elementary school students: The mediating effect of attention and the moderating effect of parental short video usage. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309899

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the regression model predicting attention, parental short video usage duration carried a positive coefficient (β = 0.079, p < .001, per Table 7). The authors state this "seems counterintuitive" and that the complex reasons "need further exploration in subsequent research".

> "this study also found that in this moderated mediation model, the duration of parental short video usage positively influences children's attention, which seems counterintuitive."
