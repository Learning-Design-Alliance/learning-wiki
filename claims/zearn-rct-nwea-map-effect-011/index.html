Evidence¶

Zearn Math evidence review 2024¶

Zearn Math evidence review. (2024). https://zearn.org

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

A school-level randomized controlled trial in a large urban Texas district across the 2022-23 and 2023-24 school years, with 64 schools randomly assigned and over 10,000 students in Grades 3-5. The review reports "significantly outperformed those in control schools on the NWEA MAP (effect size = +0.11 overall".

"Students in Zearn schools significantly outperformed those in control schools on the NWEA MAP (effect size = +0.11 overall; +0.13 for students not proficient at baseline)."
