Dures Emma, Hewlett Sarah, Ambler Nicholas, Jenkins Remona, Clarke Joyce, Gooberman-Hill Rachael. (2014). Rheumatology clinicians’ experiences of brief training and implementation of skills to support patient self-management. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2474-15-108

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 finding from the thematic analysis of interviews with 16 rheumatology clinicians. The article reports that participants “gained validation in their role as a healthcare professional from problem-solving on behalf of patients,” conflicting with self-efficacy theory.

> "Several participants identified that they gained validation in their role as a healthcare professional from problem-solving on behalf of patients, which conflicted with the theory of enhancing patients’ self-efficacy."
