## Evidence

### Correa-Burrows P 2016

Correa-Burrows P, Burrows R, Blanco E, Reyes M, Gahagan S. (2016). Nutritional quality of diet and academic performance in Chilean students. Bulletin of the World Health Organization. https://doi.org/10.2471/BLT.15.161315

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression in the same cohort (n = 395): in the fully adjusted Model 4, the mathematics odds ratio for unhealthy versus healthy diet was "0.35 (0.15–0.82)" (Table 3), and fair diet was also associated with lower odds of good mathematics performance.

> "Also, participants with a fair diet remained less likely to perform well in mathematics (OR: 0.57; 95% CI: 0.32–0.99), compared to those with a healthy diet."
