## Evidence

### Régent Alexis 2023

Régent Alexis, Thampy Harish, Singh Mini. (2023). Assessing clinical reasoning in the OSCE: pilot-testing a novel oral debrief exercise. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04668-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of scoring in this pilot study: the seven-member CR examiner group generated 42 assessments of all 6 OD videos and the sixteen-member participant group generated 48 assessments of 3 videos; Fleiss kappa was computed for binary competent/not-yet-competent categorisation. The participant group "achieved fair to good agreement for the three domains with good agreement for the global judgement."

> "The participant examiner group showed lower Kappa values though still achieved fair to good agreement for the three domains with good agreement for the global judgement."
