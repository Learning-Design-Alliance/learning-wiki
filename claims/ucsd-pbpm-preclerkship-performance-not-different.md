## Evidence

### Schneid Stephen D. 2022

Schneid Stephen D., Kelly Carolyn J., Brandl Katharina. (2022). Relationships between preadmission variables and academic outcomes for postbaccalaureate students in medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10129-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective analysis of admissions data, preclerkship course performance and USMLE Step 1 results over three years at one medical school for UCSD PBPM graduates (n = 25), other PBPM participants (n = 34) and non-PBPM students (n = 329). The study reports "not significantly different" preclerkship exam performance for UCSD PBPM students; no effect size is printed.

> "Despite the lower GPA and MCAT scores, UCSD PBPM program students’ exam performance in preclerkship courses was not significantly different from students who did not participate in a PBPM program."
