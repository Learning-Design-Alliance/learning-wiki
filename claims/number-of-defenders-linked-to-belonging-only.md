### Laninga-Wijnen 2023

Laninga-Wijnen, L., van den Berg, Y. H. M., Garandeau, C. F., Mulder, S., & de Castro, B. O. (2023). Does Being Defended Relate to Decreases in Victimization and Improved Psychosocial Adjustment Among Victims? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000712

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nonpreregistered exploratory sensitivity analysis on the victimized students only, who were the only ones who could report having defenders. The article reports the defender count "positively associated with victims’feelings of belonging" (B = .019, p = .003) with null links to the other outcomes.
