Evidence¶

Johnson 2022¶

Johnson, A. (2022). Achievement and growth for English Learners. Educational Policy, 37(4), 1044–1069. https://doi.org/10.1177/08959048211049419

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The discussion summarizes the study's trajectory findings: ever-ELs were "closing in on the national average in math and English reading achievement by the end of 4th grade," with subgroup patterns varying by race/ethnicity as shown in supplemental figures.

"Specifically, Asian and White ever-ELs surpassed the national average in 2nd grade; Black and Hispanic ELs continued to reduce the disparities between themselves and the national average in math during all five years."
