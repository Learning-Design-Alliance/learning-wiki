# The MICRO online PD significantly increased teachers' belief that CT can be integrated into other content areas (g = 0.66)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Teachers' rating of how practically CT could be integrated into other content areas rose significantly from pre to post, with a printed moderate effect size of g = 0.66. [→ Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox 2026](#lauren-e-margulieux-brendan-d-calandra-bryan-cox-2026)

## Evidence

### Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox 2026

Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox. (2026). Online professional development to support K‑12 teachers' computer science integration. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10640-z

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Independent t-tests on pre/post survey items (1 = not practically to 5 = easily) in the MICRO evaluation. The mean moved from "3.08 on the pre-test" to "3.53 on the post-test", with Hedges' g of "0.66, a moderate effect size".

> "The mean changed from 3.08 on the pre-test (n = 377, SD = .70) to 3.53 on the post-test (n = 340, SD = .65), which represents a significant increase (t = −8.78 (714.29), p < .001), in teachers' view that CT can be integrated into other areas. The effect size, derived from Hedges' g due to the participants not representing the full group of teachers, was 0.66, a moderate effect size."
