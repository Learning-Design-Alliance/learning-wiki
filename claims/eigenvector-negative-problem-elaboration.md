## Evidence

### J. Pulgar 2019

J. Pulgar, Cristian Candia, P. Leonardi. (2019). Undergrad classroom cooperation and academic performance: Beneficial for real-world-like problems but detrimental for algebra-based problems. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.06923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.06923)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

OLS regression of problem elaboration on network predictors including eigenvector centrality and its interaction with section (Fig. 6C), for the 67-student sample. The article reports the effect size moved closer to zero once the interaction was included, indicating Mixed and Treatment mainly drove the effect.

> "Figure 6C shows that eigenvector centrality to be negatively related to problem elaboration. In other words, students who are linked to well-connected others in the network of information seeking perform worse in problem elaboration than students who are not well-connected."
