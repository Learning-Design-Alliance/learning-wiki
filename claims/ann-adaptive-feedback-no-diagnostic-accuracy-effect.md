## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Automatic adaptive feedback did not facilitate pre-service teachers' diagnostic accuracy compared with static feedback. [→ Sailer 2022](#sailer-2022)

## Evidence

### Sailer 2022

Sailer, Michael; Bauer, Elisabeth; Hofmann, Riikka; Kiesewetter, Jan; Glas, Julia. (2022). Adaptive Feedback from Artificial Neural Networks Facilitates Pre-Service Teachers' Diagnostic Reasoning in Simulation-based Learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same N = 178 experimental study, diagnostic reasoning was operationalised as diagnostic accuracy and quality of justifications; the article reports the adaptive-feedback benefit held for justifications "but not their diagnostic accuracy". No effect size for the accuracy null is printed.

> "Results show that adaptive feedback facilitates pre-service teachers' quality of justifications in written assignments, but not their diagnostic accuracy."
