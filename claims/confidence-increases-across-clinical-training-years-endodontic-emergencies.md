### Chuy Virginie 2025

Chuy Virginie, Matyjasik Pierre, Devillard Raphaël, Kérourédan Olivia. (2025). Stress and Confidence of Undergraduate Dental Students With Different Levels of Clinical Experience in Managing Endodontic Emergencies: A Cross‐Sectional Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13072

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire of 227 undergraduate dental students (68 fourth, 73 fifth, 86 sixth year) at the University of Bordeaux, 2022–2023. Cumulative confidence scores over 50 items were compared across years with a Kruskal–Wallis test; the score "significantly increased with the year of study" (p < 0.001).

> "The mean cumulative confidence score for managing endodontic emergencies significantly increased with the year of study: it was 3 points higher in the fifth year than in the fourth year, and 10 points higher in the sixth year than in the fifth year (p < 0.001)."
