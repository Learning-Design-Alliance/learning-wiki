# No group differences on immediate posttests for either feedback type on either measure

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2006 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After adjusting for pretest differences, there was no effect for group on the immediate oral imitation posttest, and no group difference on the immediate grammaticality judgment posttest. [→ Rod Ellis 2006](#rod-ellis-2006)

## Evidence

### Rod Ellis 2006

Rod Ellis, Shawn Loewen and Rosemary Erlam. (2006). Implicit and Explicit Corrective Feedback and the Acquisition of L2 Grammar. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060141

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on the oral imitation test immediate posttest for the three groups (n = 34). "no effect for group on the immediate posttest, F (2, 34) = 0.961, p = .394"; equivalence was not tested. The GJT immediate posttest was likewise nonsignificant (F = 0.714, p = .498).

> "there is no effect for group on the immediate posttest, F (2, 34) = 0.961, p = .394; however, there is on the delayed posttest"
