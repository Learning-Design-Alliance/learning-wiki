## Evidence

### Takeuchi 2020

Takeuchi, M.A. (2020). Geopolitical configuration of identities and learning: Othering through the institutionalized categorization of “English language learners. Cognition and Instruction. Advance online publication https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2020.1825438

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of Excerpt 3, a small-group brainstorming interaction in which the teacher encouraged Daniel to write ideas in Spanish on post-it notes and praised him with a French phrase. Meaning making through gestures was also significant. The article states, however, that “the positioning of Daniel as a multilingual did not endure throughout the transforming recess unit.”

> "This interaction illustrates the practice of “translanguaging” (García & Leiva, 2014, p. 204), which is characterized as a fluid discursive space where new languaging realities emerge from the interactions among multiple languages."
