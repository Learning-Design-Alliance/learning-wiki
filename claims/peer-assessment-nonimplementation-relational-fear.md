## Evidence

### Biesma Regien 2019

Biesma Regien, Kennedy Mary-Claire, Pawlikowska Teresa, Brugha Ruairi, Conroy Ronan, Doyle Frank. (2019). Peer assessment to improve medical student's contributions to team-based projects: randomised controlled trial and qualitative follow-up. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1783-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of six focus group discussions with intervention and control teams, transcribed and coded in NVivo. The analysis found "fears and concerns associated with the possibility of having to mark other team members negatively and the tension this may introduce"; three main themes were identified.

> "Overall, intervention teams did not implement peer assessment with the main reason being fears and concerns associated with the possibility of having to mark other team members negatively and the tension this may introduce."
