Holistic-rubric results from the same three-iteration faculty-versus-GPT-4 comparison. The article reports question 1H never reached almost perfect agreement, and reliability declined between iterations 2 and 3.

> "After refining scoring rubrics and prompts, the IRR increased for question 1H to substantial in iteration 2 (kw=0.64, 95% CI: 0.54–0.73, 63% agreement) but decreased to moderate in iteration 3 (kw=0.54, 95% CI: 0.43–0.66, 61% agreement)."
