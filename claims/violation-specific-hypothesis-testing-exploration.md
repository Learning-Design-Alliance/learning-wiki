# Infants' exploratory behavior after a core knowledge violation is tailored to the type of violation seen

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Stahl Aimee E. 2019

Stahl Aimee E., Feigenson Lisa. (2019). Violations of Core Knowledge Shape Early Learning. Topics in Cognitive Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/tops.12389

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Behavioral analyses from the same 11-month-old exploration study (Stahl & Feigenson, 2015) coded infants' actions toward the violating object. Exploration differed qualitatively by violation type, with banging after solidity violations and dropping after support violations.

> "Infants who saw a solidity violation, in which the object passed through the wall, tended to explore that object by banging it with their hands or against the high chair tray. Infants who saw a support violation, in which the object appeared to float in mid-air, repeatedly dropped it"
