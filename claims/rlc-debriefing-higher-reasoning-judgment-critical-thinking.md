### Almomani Emad 2025

A reflective learning conversation debriefing model for interprofessional simulation based education. BMC Medical Education, 25:1434. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07765-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group Mann-Whitney U comparisons in the quasi-experimental ALS course study (65 per group), reported in the Results section. For CREST the difference "emerging from the second observation (p =.016)" strengthened at the third (p <.001); LCJR p =.002 then p <.001; CTR p <.001 at both points. No effect size is printed.

> "The Mann-Whitney U test results showed statistically significant differences between the groups from the second observation onward, with the experimental group showing progressively higher mean ranks than the control group."
