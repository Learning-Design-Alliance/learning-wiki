Descriptive comparison of four-year graduation rates by English Learner status in the 2018–19 CPS ninth-grade cohort: Active English Learners (N=2,224) at 80.8%, Former English Learners (N=6,135) at 87.4%, and Never English Learners (N=16,961) at 83.2%.
