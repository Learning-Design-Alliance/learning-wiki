## Evidence

### Georgios P. Georgiou 2026

Georgios P. Georgiou. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Pronunciation Teaching: Use and Beliefs of Foreign Language Teachers. University of Nicosia. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/02607476.2026.2676279](https://doi.org/10.1080/02607476.2026.2676279)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Ordinal logistic regression models on belief ratings from the 117-teacher survey. TrainingHigh differed significantly from TrainingLow on the replacement belief (estimate –1.97, p = 0.01) and the data-collection belief (estimate –1.71, p = 0.02).

> "Finally, significant differences were found between TrainingHigh and the baseline group on the beliefs "AI may replace the human element" and "Concerns about data collection"; teachers with more extensive training were less likely to agree with these beliefs."
