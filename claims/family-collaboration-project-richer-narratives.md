`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative analysis of open-ended reflection responses from both groups. The article reports that "teacher candidates in the intervention group provided more detailed narratives" about parent collaboration while comparison candidates mentioned classroom teachers. No effect size is printed.

> "teacher candidates in the intervention group provided more detailed narratives when reflecting upon their learning experiences in collaborating and communicating with parents, while those in the comparison group primarily mentioned their experience with their classroom teachers."
