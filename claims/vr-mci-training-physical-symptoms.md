## Evidence

### Heldring Sara 2025

Heldring Sara, Jirwe Maria, Wihlborg Jonas, Lindström Veronica. (2025). Acceptability and applicability of using virtual reality for training mass casualty incidents- a mixed method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07319-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Summative content analysis of open-ended questionnaire answers on physical symptoms from the 95 participants. The analysis found "A majority of the participants expressed some dizziness during or after the VR training (61%)," mostly mild; 28% reported nausea, severe for 2%, worsened by rapid movements and level changes.
