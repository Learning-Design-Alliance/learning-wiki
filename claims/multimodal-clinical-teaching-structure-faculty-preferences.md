Theme 5 faculty interview data on balancing teaching modalities; one faculty member recommended a two-thirds bedside, one-third skills-lab split with students "weaned off" the skills lab, reflecting a personal pedagogical leaning rather than a tested optimum.

> "I would recommend two thirds of the time by the bedside and one third in the skills lab…Gradually the students should be weaned off from skills lab."
