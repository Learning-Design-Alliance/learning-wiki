## Evidence

### Mutmainna 2025

Mutmainna, Istiyono, E., Haryanto, Retnawati, & Setiawan, C. (2025). Physics Teachers' Perceptions about Diagnostic Assessment of Students' Physics Misconceptions: A Phenomenological Study. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.10422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.10422)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview finding on contextual elements (theme 3.1): training received generally concerned curriculum socialization; Budi recalled that under national exams "our target was the national exam", prioritizing memorized formulas over understanding concepts, which he linked to a high chance of misconceptions.

> "For training on middle school students' physics misconceptions, six participants confirmed that such training had never been conducted."

### Mutmainna 2025 (2)

Mutmainna, Istiyono, E., Haryanto, Retnawati, & Setiawan, C. (2025). Physics Teachers' Perceptions about Diagnostic Assessment of Students' Physics Misconceptions: A Phenomenological Study. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.10422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.10422)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Same interview study: Nana noted islands with no network at all and Aswar that schools are very far apart, so physics teacher community communication is not intensive; at higher education, Mita reported monthly seminars and training on creating four-tier questions.

> "One hindering factor is the inadequate transportation and limited internet connectivity in certain areas."
