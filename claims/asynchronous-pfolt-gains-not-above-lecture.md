### Archer 2024

Archer, H. N., Simon, M. N., Mead, C., Prather, E. E., Brunkhorst, M., and Hunsley, D. (2024). Bringing Lecture-Tutorials Online: An Analysis of A New Strategy to Teach Planet Formation in the Undergraduate Classroom. Astronomy Education Journal, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.32374/AEJ.2024.4.1.090ra

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tukey HSD pairwise comparison between the asynchronous PFOLT condition and the lecture-only condition; the article reports no statistically significant difference (p = .56), and equivalence was not tested. No effect size is printed.

> "However, the asynchronous PFOLT was not significantly different from lecture (p = .56)."

### Archer 2024 (2)

Archer, H. N., Simon, M. N., Mead, C., Prather, E. E., Brunkhorst, M., and Hunsley, D. (2024). Bringing Lecture-Tutorials Online: An Analysis of A New Strategy to Teach Planet Formation in the Undergraduate Classroom. Astronomy Education Journal, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.32374/AEJ.2024.4.1.090ra

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tukey HSD pairwise comparison between in-person and asynchronous PFOLT modalities on student-level normalized gains; the article reports significantly higher gains in-person (p < .001). No effect size is printed.

> "Finally, testing also indicates that the in-person implementation of the PFOLT was significantly more effective than the asynchronous implementation (p < .001)."
