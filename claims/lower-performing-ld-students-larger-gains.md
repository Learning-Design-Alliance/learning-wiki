`q2 i?` When change equations were recalculated at one standard deviation below the LD group's reading comprehension mean, lower-performing LD students showed larger estimated change scores than average-performing LD students. [→ Quinn 2020](#quinn-2020)

## Evidence

### Quinn 2020

Quinn, Jamie M.; Wagner, Richard K.; Petscher, Yaacov; Roberts, Greg; Menzel, Andrew J.; Schatschneider, Christopher. (2020). Differential Co-Development of Vocabulary Knowledge and Reading Comprehension for Students with and without Learning Disabilities. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000382

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis recomputing the LD change-score equations at reading comprehension one standard deviation below the group mean (change scores of 1.41, 1.01, 0.70 across the three intervals, versus 1.11, 0.70, 0.38 at the average starting point). Descriptive model-estimated values with no significance test.

> "Children with an LD who start with worse reading comprehension grow more than students with an LD who start with average reading comprehension."
