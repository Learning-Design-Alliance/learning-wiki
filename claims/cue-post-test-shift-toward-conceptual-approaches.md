## Evidence

### Nandana Weliweriya 2017

Nandana Weliweriya, Eleanor C Sayre, Dean A Zollman. (2017). The Effectiveness of “Pencasts” in Physics Courses. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.09349](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.09349)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Qualitative analysis of CUE pre/post responses in the same twenty-student course. The article reports the shift descriptively with no statistics, and notes the CUE "is an overall assessment of their learning in the course, not one specifically tied to pencast production".

> "However, in the post-test students mostly used equations and diagrams to represent the physical system  and were much more successful at solving problems. Students frequently used a conceptual approach rather than long algorithmic calculations in their responses, a mark of conceptual understanding."
