## Evidence

### Meike Jost 2017

Meike Jost, Peter Brüstle, Marianne Giesler, Michel Rijntjes, Jochen Brich. (2017). Effects of additional team-based learning on students’ clinical reasoning skills: a pilot study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-017-2614-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of mandatory MCQE scores between the 24 voluntary key-feature examination participants and the other 97 students in the neurology course, reported with means and p = 0.193 and no effect size.

> "Overall performance of the 24 students participating in the key feature problem examination (2 students of the control group did not participate in the MCQE because of being sick at the time point of the MCQE) was 35.5 out of 40 points compared to 34.5 of 40 points of the remaining 97 students of the neurology course (p = 0.193)."
