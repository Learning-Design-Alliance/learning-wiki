### Han Jiang 2026

Han Jiang, Stuart J. Johnstone, Yingxin Li, Jinjin Lu & Bo-Bei Mao. (2026). Neurocognitive Academic Training to Improve Symptoms Executive Functions and Chinese Handwriting in Children with ADHD. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00415-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

THPC standardized handwriting assessment in the RCT. For Accuracy the interaction was F(1,54) = 43.43, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.45, with the control group showing a modest significant increase in accuracy problem scores; lower scores indicate fewer errors.

> "For Construction, significant effects emerged for Time (F(1,54) = 101.97, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.65), Group (F(1,54) = 60.28, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.53), and their interaction (F(1,54) = 113.75, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.68)."
