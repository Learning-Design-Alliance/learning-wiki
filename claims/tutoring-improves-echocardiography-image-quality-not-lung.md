## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Significant expert-rated image-quality advantages for intervention participants appeared for apical five-chamber (65% vs 11%, p = 0.001), subcostal four-chamber (78% vs 44%, p = 0.04) and subcostal IVC views (78% vs 26%, p = 0.001), and for interpretable RVD (83% vs 39%, p = 0.006) and volemia (72% vs 22%, p = 0.003) images; lung US showed no significant difference. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blinded expert assessment of image quality on a 6-point scale during the same evaluation session. Cardiac view contrasts reached significance while "There was no significant difference observed when testing for lung US", so the benefit is heart-specific.

> "The proportion of participants obtaining high quality images was higher in the intervention group for all focused echocardiographic views, with a difference reaching statistical significance for apical five-chamber (65% versus 11%, p = 0.001), subcostal four-chamber (78 versus 44%, p = 0.04) and subcostal inferior vena cava views (78% versus 26%, p = 0.001)."
