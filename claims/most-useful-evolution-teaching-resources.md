### Hidaka BH 2015

Hidaka BH, Asghar A, Aktipis CA, Nesse RM, Wolpaw TM, Skursky NK, Bennett KJ, Beyrouty MW, Schwartz MD. (2015). The status of evolutionary medicine education in North American medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0322-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ratings of usefulness for 13 teaching resources by 50 responding curriculum deans, reported in Results and Figure 3. Resources rated least useful were an evolutionary biologist on rounds, a textbook, and a summer course for faculty.

> "The three resources that curriculum deans reported would be most useful were model test questions and answers with explanations, model curricula for adding evolution content to existing courses and rotations, and case studies with facilitator guides."
