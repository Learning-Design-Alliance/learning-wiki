`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Entropy weight method applied to the 52 tertiary indicators in the 906-student study to generate dimension weights for the composite competency score. The article reports "public health practice ability (w = 0.248)" as the highest-weighted dimension, reflecting the emphasis on practice in public health training.

> "the ranking of entropy weights for the five dimensions in this study is as follows: public health practice ability (w = 0.248) > professional knowledge and skills (w = 0.225) > professional literacy (w = 0.217) > cognitive characteristics (w = 0.165) > personal traits (w = 0.145)"
