## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Despite a significant questionnaire increase in perceived ability to communicate with patients, interviewees agreed the SAT program did not help them improve communication with patients. [→ Yeung C 2017](#yeung-c-2017)

## Evidence

### Yeung C 2017

Yeung C, Friesen F, Farr S, Law M, Albert L. (2017). Development and implementation of a longitudinal students as teachers program: participant satisfaction and implications for medical student teaching and learning. BMC Medical Education 17:28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0857-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Group interview of five purposively selected participants, coded inductively by three investigators. Interviewees felt they did not consciously apply SAT teaching skills in clinical contexts, citing minimal patient exposure at their training stage.

> "there was a general consensus among interviewees that the SAT program did not help them to improve their communication skills with patients."
