## Evidence

### Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu 2023

Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu. (2023). The longitudinal association between children’s growth mindset in senior primary school and their parents’ growth mindset. Front. Psychol. 14:1110944. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110944

`q3 · i?`

Parallel process latent growth curve model linking mothers' and children's growth mindset trajectories. The unconditional model showed a significant intercept-to-intercept path (β = 0.127, p = 0.03); in the conditional model controlling for child gender and age and parental education and income, the path was marginally significant (β = 0.109, p = 0.064).

> "the initial level of maternal growth mindset could positively predict the initial level of children’s growth mindset ( β = 0.127, p = 0.03), which means that maternal growth mindset at Time 1 positively predicts children’s growth mindset at Time 5"
