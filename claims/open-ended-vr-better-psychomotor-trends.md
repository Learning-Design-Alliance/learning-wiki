## Evidence

### Hing Lie 2023

Hing Lie, Kachina Studer, Zhen Zhao, Ben Thomson, Dishita G Turakhia, John Liu. (2023). Training for Open-Ended Drilling through a Virtual Reality Simulation. [doi:10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects user study with 26 students in an introductory Mechanical Engineering Tools course (n=12 VR, n=14 control per the results section). After training, participants performed a real drilling task; researchers recorded time, real-time errors converted to weighted scores, and completion within tolerance. The study reports "75% vs 64%" completion favoring VR, with no statistically significant differences.

> "Overall, the VR group successfully completed the authentic drilling task at a higher rate than the control group (75% vs 64%). While not statistically significant, the VR group also committed less severe mistakes (1.75 vs 2.14), asked less questions per capita (0.92 vs 1.5), and spent less time (17.67 mins vs 21.57 mins)."
