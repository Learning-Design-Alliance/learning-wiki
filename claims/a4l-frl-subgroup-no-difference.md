# The A4L impact on grade 4 CCU outcomes did not differ significantly between free/reduced-price lunch and non-FRL students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Subgroup analyses found no statistically significant differences between free/reduced-price lunch and non-FRL students on grade 4 CCU Assessments. [→ Jonathan Nakamoto 2015](#jonathan-nakamoto-2015)

## Evidence

### Jonathan Nakamoto 2015

Jonathan Nakamoto, Sandy Sobolew-Shubin, Martin Orland. (2015). The Beaverton School District Arts for Learning (A4L) Lessons Project, An Investing in Innovation (i3) Development Grant: Student Impact Findings from Years 1, 2, and 3. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED560945

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analyses with grade 4 CCU data in 12 schools; the report interprets this as the program having an equally positive impact for both FRL and non-FRL students. No effect size is printed for this contrast, so impact is null.

> "the free/reduced-price lunch subgroup analyses based on the CCU Assessments with the students in grade 4 did not reveal any statistically significant differences between the free/reduced-price lunch and non-free/reduced-price lunch students."
