## Evidence

### Hou Yujiao 2020

Hou Yujiao, Mei Guang, Liu Yutong, Xu Weisheng. (2020). Physical Fitness with Regular Lifestyle Is Positively Related to Academic Performance among Chinese Medical and Dental Students. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/5602395

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 212 medical and 104 dental students using college physical fitness test scores and course grades, with fitness score entered in six multiple linear regression models. The article reports that "better physical fitness can contribute to students' academic achievement, for both medical and dental students"; no standardised effect size is printed for this finding.

> "It was consistent with former studies that better physical fitness can contribute to students' academic achievement, for both medical and dental students."
