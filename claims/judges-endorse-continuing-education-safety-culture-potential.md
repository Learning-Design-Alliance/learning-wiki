## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the General aspects domain, judges rated the simulation as usable as a continuing education strategy to raise awareness of patient safety culture among nurses (mean 4.4, CVI 1, CVC 0.88, CVR 1) and as having potential to strengthen patient safety culture in care scenarios (mean 4.8, CVI 1, CVC 0.96, CVR 1).
