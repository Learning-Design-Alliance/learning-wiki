### Larson et al. 2010

Heidi A. Larson, Mera K. El Ramahi, Steven R. Conn, Lincoln A. Estes, and Amanda B. Ghibellini. (2010). Reducing Test Anxiety Among Third Grade Students Through the Implementation of Relaxation Techniques. Eastern Illinois University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ885222

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group t-test analyses compared each randomly partitioned experimental subgroup with the control group at post-test. The article reports these "yielded non-significant results," t(107) = – 0.79, p = .431 and t(119) = – 0.57, p = .573; the authors state these findings did not support the third hypothesis.

> "Post-test differences between the respective experimental subgroups and control group yielded non-significant results (t(107) = – 0.79, p = .431, and t(119) = – 0.57, p = .573, respectively)."
