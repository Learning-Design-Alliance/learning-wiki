`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Operational resource analysis of two consecutive Year-1 medical cohorts extracted from timetabling and estates records compared structural requirements of PBL versus TBL. Weekly delivery of TBL required 59 staff hours and 13 rooms compared with 160 staff hours and 80 rooms for PBL, equating to a reduction of roughly 63% in staff time and 84% reduction in room utilisation.

> "In total, weekly delivery of TBL requires 59 staff hours and 13 rooms, compared with 160 staff hours and 80 rooms for PBL; this equates to a reduction of roughly 63% in staff time and an impressive 84% reduction in room utilisation"

BMC Medical Education, 26(1466). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09612-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective mixed-methods evaluation of two consecutive Year-1 medical cohorts (2023-24, n = 435; 2024-25, n = 426) using anonymous end-of-semester surveys. TBL maintained high student satisfaction with 74% rating sessions as excellent whilst simultaneously reducing weekly staff facilitator hours by 63% and room use by 84%.

> "TBL maintained high student satisfaction (74% rated sessions "excellent") whilst simultaneously reducing weekly staff facilitator hours by 63% (160 h vs. 59 h) and room use by 84% (80 h of bookings vs. 13)"
