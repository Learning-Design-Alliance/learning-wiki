## Evidence

### Jennie K. Grammer 2021

Jennie K. Grammer, Keye Xu and Agatha Lenartowicz. (2021). Effects of context on the neural correlates of attention in a college classroom. npj Science of Learning 6:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00094-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Supplementary oscillatory analyses reported in the discussion section provide converging evidence for the alpha interpretation; no effect size is printed. The authors read this as "a neural shift from higher to lower oscillations".

> "supplemental analyses (c.f., SI) additionally reveal that teacher-initiated activities also increased theta-band (4 –7 Hz) power and decreased both beta (13 –20 Hz) and gamma (30 –50 Hz) power, consistent with a neural shift from higher to lower oscillations, typical of transitions to lower wakefulness"
