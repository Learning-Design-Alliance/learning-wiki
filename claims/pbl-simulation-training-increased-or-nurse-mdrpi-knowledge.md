## Subclaims
`q2 i3` After the eight-session hybrid PBL and simulation program, OR nurses' total PZ-PUKT knowledge score rose from 40.58 ± 9.00 to 53.23 ± 6.78 (P < 0.001, dz = 2.231). [→ Zhang Feng 2026](#zhang-feng-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang Feng 2026

Zhang Feng, Chen Zhi Lan, Qi Li En, Wang Xiao Yan, Zhang Li, Zhang Jing, Wang Ping. (2026). Pre–post changes in operating room nurses’ knowledge and attitudes toward medical device–related pressure injuries after a problem-based and simulation-based training program: a single-group pretest–posttest study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09147-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pretest–posttest quasi-experiment with 80 OR nurses at one tertiary hospital; paired t-tests with Holm–Bonferroni adjustment. Table 3 reports the "Total PZ-PUKT score" rising from 40.58 ± 9.00 to 53.23 ± 6.78 with Cohen's dz = 2.231 (95% CI 2.01–2.45), P < 0.001.
