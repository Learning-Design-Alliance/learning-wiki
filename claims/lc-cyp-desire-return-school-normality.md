### MacLean A 2023

MacLean A, Wild C, Hunt K, Nettleton S, Skea ZC, Ziebland S. (2023). Impact of Long Covid on the school experiences of children and young people: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-075756

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of narrative interviews with 22 CYP (aged 10–18) with Long Covid and 15 parents, conducted October 2021–July 2022. The analysis found "a strong desire for their lives to return to the way they were before having Covid", with absences described as stressful and isolating.

> "Analysis revealed a strong desire for their lives to return to the way they were before having Covid and CYP spoke about being able to go back to school as a major means of regaining some normality"
