Inductive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke approach as cited) of open-ended reflection data from the post-interaction instrument. The most frequent limitation theme was content "that exceeds the cognitive level of preschoolers" (45%); vagueness/repetition (32%) and feasibility (23%) followed.
