Table 4 entry for Dornhecker et al. (2015), a non-randomized controlled study across 3 elementary schools (N=282, grades 2–4) using the BOSS observation method over 5 months. Engagement favored the standing-desk group in both semesters (p = 0.003 each); grade-level differences were not significant. No standardized effect size is printed.
