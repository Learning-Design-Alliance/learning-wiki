## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jones 2025

Jones, C. and Cominos, N. (2025). The Future of Healthcare—Simulation‐Based Learning Curricula and Preparing Future Midwives for Effective Rapport‐Building in Telehealth Settings. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16927

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Educator-perspective interviews (Section 5.2) in the qualitative descriptive study. Only one educator felt moderately prepared after receiving "step by step instructions as to how you would actually guide the telehealth education session"; educators attributed gaps to "assumed knowledge that the clinical midwives that are coming to teach it (telehealth scenarios) do this in their workplace".

> "This was no doubt due to the absence of any specific training in teaching telehealth competencies in the simulation education."
