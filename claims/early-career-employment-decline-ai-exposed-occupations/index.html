q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports Brynjolfsson et al.'s (2025) payroll analysis of a provider covering over 25 million U.S. workers, with an analysis sample of 3.5 to 5 million workers per month by age and AI exposure. It found a "16% relative decline" for the youngest cohort with older-worker growth in the same occupations. No standardized effect size is printed.

"workers ages 22 to 25 experienced a 16% relative decline in employment between October 2022 and September 2025, even after controlling for firm-level shocks. In contrast, employment for workers aged 35-49 in those same occupations grew by over 8%"
