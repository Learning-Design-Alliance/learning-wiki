### Gomes Sara Hora 2023

Gomes Sara Hora, Trindade Marta, Petrisor Cristina, Costa Dinis, Correia-Pinto Jorge, Costa Patrício S., Pêgo José M. (2023). Objective structured assessment ultrasound skill scale for hyomental distance competence – psychometric study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04146-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of 225 ratings (45 videos * 5 assessors) of videorecorded HMD measurements by 15 participants, rated blindly by five assessors. The study reports "a Cronbach’s alpha of 0.916" across the four OSAUS items used.

> "The internal consistency of OSAUS scale for ultrasound HMD measurement was evaluated with a Cronbach’s alpha of 0.916."
