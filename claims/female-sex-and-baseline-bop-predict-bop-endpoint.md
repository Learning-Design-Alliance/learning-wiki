Nominal regression analysis in the retrospective cohort study of 107 student-treated patients, with predictors tested univariately then in models adjusted for age and sex. The abstract reports EP1 at T1 was "positively affected by being female and negatively by T0 BOP (OR3.90, p = 0.008, OR0.95, p < 0.001)"; Table 3 confirms female OR 3.90 and BOP (T0) OR 0.95 in Model 2.
