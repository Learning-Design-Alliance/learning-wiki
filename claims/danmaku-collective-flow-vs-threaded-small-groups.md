# Danmaku hosts mass participation as a continuous collective flow while the discussion board divides learners into threaded small groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a MOOC lecture, danmaku messages flowed continuously along video playback as an undivided collective space, whereas discussion-board participation was divided into 98 threads averaging about 7.54 posts each. [→ Bo Yang 2024](#bo-yang-2024)

## Evidence

### Bo Yang 2024

Bo Yang. (2024). Combining Danmaku and Discussion Boards: Toward A Scalable and Sociable Environment for Mass Collaboration in MOOCs. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09426-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of mass-participation patterns (RQ1) across all 2435 danmaku and 739 posts from an informal Grade-10 math MOOC lecture. The analysis found danmaku displayed "in a continuous flow along with video playback," while the 269 discussion-board learners were "divided into small-group discussions" in 98 threads.

> "There was a total of 98 threads on the discussion board, and on average, about 2.74 learners sent about 7.54 posts in each thread. As a result, these 269 learners’ participation was divided into small-group discussions accommodated in each thread on the discussion board."
