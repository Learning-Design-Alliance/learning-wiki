Kristin Reedy 2015¶

Kristin Reedy, Ed.D., and Natalie Lacireno-Paquet, Ph.D. (2015). Implementation and Outcomes of Kansas Multi-Tier System of Supports: Final Evaluation Report—2014. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559729

q2 · i? · design · r3

Analysis of the annual Kansas MTSS School Survey of Effective Instructional Practices (555 respondents in 2014), classifying schools into Fixsen-based implementation stages. The report prints the year as "2103" in this sentence; Table 1 shows 67.9% for 2013.

"the percentage of schools scoring in the initial implementation stage increased significantly, from 32.8% in 2012, to 67.9% in 2103, to 72.4% in 2014"

Kristin Reedy 2015 (2)¶

Kristin Reedy, Ed.D., and Natalie Lacireno-Paquet, Ph.D. (2015). Implementation and Outcomes of Kansas Multi-Tier System of Supports: Final Evaluation Report—2014. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559729

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Longitudinal analysis of the 292 schools responding to the survey in both 2013 and 2014 (Table 2). The report attributes backward movement partly to stringent full-implementation scoring criteria, where one or two item responses can shift a school's stage.

"Of those schools at full implementation in 2013, 37.8% remained at full implementation and 62.2% scored at the initial implementation stage."
