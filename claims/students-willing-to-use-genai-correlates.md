## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported a positive attitude toward GenAI, wanting to integrate it in learning practices (Mean = 3.85) and future careers (Mean = 4.05). [→ Chan 2023](#chan-2023)

## Evidence

### Chan 2023

Chan, C. K. Y., & Hu, W. (2023). Students’ voices on generative AI: perceptions, benefits, and challenges in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 20:43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00411-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive Likert-scale results from the survey of 399 students. Students valued perceived usefulness for unique insights (Mean = 3.74), personalized feedback (Mean = 3.61), 24/7 availability (Mean = 4.12) and anonymous support (Mean = 3.77).

> "students have a positive attitude toward GenAI technologies. They would like to integrate GenAI technologies like ChatGPT in their learning practices (Mean = 3.85, SD = 1.02), as well as future careers (Mean = 4.05; SD = 0.96)."

### Chan 2023 (2)

Chan, C. K. Y., & Hu, W. (2023). Students’ voices on generative AI: perceptions, benefits, and challenges in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 20:43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00411-8

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis in the willingness results section reports "r = 0.189; p < 0.001" for knowledge and "r = 0.326; p < 0.001" for frequency of use, both positive associations with willingness.

> "students’ perceived willingness to use GenAI technologies is positively correlated with both knowledge of GenAI (r = 0.189; p < 0.001) and frequency of use (r = 0.326; p < 0.001), indicating that students who are more knowledgeable about these technologies and use them more frequently are more likely to use them in the future"
