### Squire 1912

Squire, C. R. (1912). Graded Mental Tests. Part I. Attention, Perception, Comprehension and Memory. The Journal of Educational Psychology, III(7). https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Auditory memory was tested with the Binet sentences as modified by Whipple, each sentence given once with the child's back turned; substitutions, insertions, or omissions counted as errors. Excluding the retarded group brought out "a very close correlation between mental growth and immediate memory".

> "The exclusion of the retarded group has brought out a very close correlation between mental growth and immediate memory."
