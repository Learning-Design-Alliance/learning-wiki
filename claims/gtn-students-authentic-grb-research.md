### Lynn Cominsky 2014

Lynn Cominsky, Kevin McLin, Aurore Simonnet and the Swift E/PO Team. (2014). Fourteen Years of Education and Public Outreach for the Swift Gamma-ray Burst Explorer Mission. eConf C1304143. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Program-reported participation count for the Global Telescope Network, a 38-partner network of small college, high school and amateur observatories pairing students with science-team members to produce multi-wavelength data on targets of scientific interest.

> "Over 200 diverse high school and college students have used the GTN to obtain data in conjunction with SSU E/PO programs."

### Lynn Cominsky 2014 (2)

Lynn Cominsky, Kevin McLin, Aurore Simonnet and the Swift E/PO Team. (2014). Fourteen Years of Education and Public Outreach for the Swift Gamma-ray Burst Explorer Mission. eConf C1304143. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Observational account of the SSU 14-inch GORT robotic telescope, which caught its first autonomously-triggered afterglow in December 2008 and again in April 2013, demonstrating that students in the network could obtain publishable GRB afterglow data.

> "In April 2013, GRB 130420A was caught by GORT, and is reported in GCN 14427 and 14445. The telescope was on target about 100 seconds after the trigger, while the burst was still brightening."
