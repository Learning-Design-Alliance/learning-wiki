# DialogRPT scores were highly sensitive to the presence of a teacher prefix: identical GPT-4 responses scored 0.52 on updown with the prefix but 0.40 without it.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Prepending a "teacher:" prefix to the same GPT-4 responses raised the DialogRPT updown score from 0.40 to 0.52, an effect the authors describe as unanticipated metric sensitivity. [→ Anaïs Tack 2023](#anais-tack-2023)

## Evidence

### Anaïs Tack 2023

Anaïs Tack, Ekaterina Kochmar, Zheng Yuan, Serge Bibauw, Chris Piech. (2023). The BEA 2023 Shared Task on Generating AI Teacher Responses in Educational Dialogues. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.bea-1.64](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.bea-1.64)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Automated evaluation-phase comparison of two Cornell submissions differing only by a teacher prefix. The article calls this outcome remarkable, highlighting "the unanticipated sensitivity of the DialogRPT metric to the presence or absence of a prefix."

> "The responses generated by GPT-4 were the most likely to receive upvotes on average (0.52) when they were submitted with a teacher prefix. However, when the team submitted the same responses without the prefix, they received a much lower score (0.4) and ranked sixth on the same metric."
