## Evidence

### Maarten van der Velde 2026

Maarten van der Velde, Florian Sense, Jelmer P. Borst & Hedderik van Rijn. (2026). Explaining forgetting at different timescales requires a time-variant forgetting function. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00447-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-fitting analysis in which data were split into bins of equal width on a logarithmic scale by between-session interval, with parameters fitted per bin. The article reports that "this procedure produces a strong correspondence between data and model, irrespective of which parameter we vary." No effect size is printed.

> "As Figs.1d, 1f, and1h show, this procedure produces a strong correspondence between data and model, irrespective of which parameter we vary."
