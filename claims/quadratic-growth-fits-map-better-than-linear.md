Nested model comparisons of linear versus quadratic MLGM fitted to MAP reading and mathematics data in ten states, using AIC, BIC and χ2 difference tests. The article reports "all differences between quadratic and linear models are greater than 10" on BIC; no effect size is printed.

> "For BIC, all differences between quadratic and linear models are greater than 10 in both reading and mathematics, which indicate that the quadratic model fits the data better than the linear model."
