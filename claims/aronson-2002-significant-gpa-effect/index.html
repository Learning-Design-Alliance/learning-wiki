Growth Mindset Interventions 2022¶

Growth Mindset Interventions. (2022). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, NCEE 2022-006. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q4 · i3 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial with low attrition at a private four-year university in California; WWC-calculated means were 3.46 (0.30) intervention versus 3.19 (0.33) comparison, with a printed effect size of 0.85, improvement index +30, and p < .01. The WWC calculated the effect size and p-value because the authors did not report them for this contrast.
