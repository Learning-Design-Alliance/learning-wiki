This is the authors' theoretical argument, not a measured result: in verification labs students must coordinate equipment, theory, equations, data, notebooks, and teamwork, while demonstrations structure focus on predicting and explaining. The article offers no measured cognitive load data.

> "We argue that a veriﬁcation lab is a high cognitive load activity, while an enhanced lecture demonstration is a much lower cognitive load activity."
