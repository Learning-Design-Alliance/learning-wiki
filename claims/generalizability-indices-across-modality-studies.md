## Evidence

### Monteiro S 2018

Monteiro S, Sibbald D, Coetzee K. (2018). i-Assess: Evaluating the impact of electronic data capture for OSCE. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0410-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

G-studies using G_String version 6.3.8 on average station scores from four administrations. The article reports "unchanged at 0.3" inter-station reliability and an inter-modality reliability of 0.8 for the August administration, suggesting strong cross-modality agreement.

> "The inter-station reliability, or ability to generalize from one station to another station, regardless of modality, was unchanged at 0.3 in the August exam (n = 46 and n = 44), the January exam (n = 116) and the May exam (n = 154)."

### Monteiro S 2018 (2)

Monteiro S, Sibbald D, Coetzee K. (2018). i-Assess: Evaluating the impact of electronic data capture for OSCE. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0410-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalizability analysis of the January scan-sheet-only control exam compared with the May tablet/scan-sheet exam. The article reports dependability indices of 0.83, 0.65, 0.73, and 0.75, with the May drop interpreted as specious rather than a modality effect.

> "A conservative index of dependability of the January exam (n = 116), which was scored on scan sheets only, was 0.83. However, the index of dependability of the May exam was lower at 0.65."
