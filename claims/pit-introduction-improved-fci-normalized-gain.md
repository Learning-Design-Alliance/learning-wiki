## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2016

Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux and Stephen E. Kanim. (2016). Developing Mathematical Creativity with Physics Invention Tasks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.02033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.02033)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Historical comparison in the Extended Analytical Physics course at Rutgers: matched pre/post FCI data from 2003 (n=102, pre-PITs) and combined 2013/14 (n=144, post-PITs), same lecturer. Mean scores moved from "pre = 40%±1.6% and post = 61%±1.5%" to "pre = 36%±1.3%, and post = 66%±1.4%".

> "The normalized gain of the course averages using matched samples in 2003 is 0.35 ± 06 and in 2013 is 0.47 ± 05, which are both in the range of interactive engagement courses. The trend shows an improvement in learning as measured by the FCI."
