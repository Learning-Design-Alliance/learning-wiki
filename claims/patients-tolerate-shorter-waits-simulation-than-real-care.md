## Evidence

### Rogan A 2026

Rogan A, Watt E, Murphy S, Wheeler E, Woods L, Galwankar S, Peckler B. (2026). Worth the Wait? Comparison of Emergency Department Patients' Waiting Room Tolerance for Real Patient Care vs Training/Simulation Scenarios. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.48916

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of 827 ED waiting-room patients using mixed-effects models with participant as random intercept; the model included scenario, age, sex, wait time, and time of day as fixed effects. Participants "tended to tolerate shorter wait times for simulation scenarios than for almost any other medical scenario (all P < .05)."

> "after controlling for age, sex, waiting time, and time of day of recruitment, participants tended to tolerate shorter wait times for simulation scenarios than for almost any other medical scenario (all P < .05)"
