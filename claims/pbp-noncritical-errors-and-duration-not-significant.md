### Mascheroni J 2024

Mascheroni J, Stockburger M, Patwala A, Mont L, Rao A, Retzlaff H, Garweg C, Gallagher AG, Verbelen T. (2024). Surgical skill simulation training to proficiency reduces procedural errors among novice cardiac device implanters: a randomized study. Europace. https://doi.org/10.1093/europace/euae229

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Randomized video-scored comparison (n = 15 per group): the 57.1% fewer Errors finding for PBP was the one primary outcome reported as not significant (P = 0.140 in the abstract's statistical reporting).

> "In comparison to the SIM group, the PBP group completed, on average, 11.0% more procedural Steps and made 57.1% fewer Errors, 61.2% fewer Critical Errors, and 60.7% fewer All Errors Combined (total Errors + Critical Errors). Apart from Errors, all differences were statistically significant"
