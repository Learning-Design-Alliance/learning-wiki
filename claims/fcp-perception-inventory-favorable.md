Qualitative analysis of an FCP perception inventory completed after the module. Table 5 lists item ratings from 88% (retention of subject matter) to 94% (positive attitudes toward mathematics); items on motivation, confidence, and cooperation each drew 92%. No inferential statistics reported.

> "All the ten items yield either more than 90% or nearly 90% of students' ratings, where the highest percentage (94%) belongs to the seventh item that the FCP promotes a positive attitude toward mathematics."
