# Substantial crossover occurred in the Head Start impact study comparison group, with 17.3% of the 3-year-old and 13.9% of the 4-year-old comparison groups enrolling in non-study Head Start programs

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Despite random assignment, a portion of the comparison group enrolled in Head Start programs not selected for the study, diluting the contrast. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2015](#what-works-clearinghouse-2015)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2015

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2015). Head Start: WWC intervention report. http://www.acf.hhs.gov/programs/ohs

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

In the DHHS ACF (2010) randomized controlled trial, comparison-group parents were free to enroll children in any non-study program. The report also states that 13.9% of the 4-year-old baseline comparison group enrolled in non-study Head Start programs, and that about 50% of the 3-year-old comparison group enrolled in Head Start as 4-year-olds.

> "Authors reported that 17.3% of the 3-year-old baseline comparison group were enrolled in Head Start programs that were not part of the study in spite of their study group assignment."
