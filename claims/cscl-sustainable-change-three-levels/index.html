The editorial reports this as Hod et al.'s response to the Wise and Schwarz provocation about CSCL's impact on educational change; the editorial offers no independent evidence, attributing the argument to the squib authors.

"They emphasize that some major CSCL initiatives have actually produced sustainable change at the three mentioned levels, and the design-centric research practice partnership model will continue to do so in the coming years."
