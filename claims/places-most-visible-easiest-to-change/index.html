Evidence¶

Purkey 1988¶

Purkey, William W.; Novak, John M. (1988). Education: By Invitation Only. Fastback No. 268. Phi Delta Kappa Educational Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED296471

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' practical argument in the Places section, where places are called 'an excellent beginning point' because 'they are so visible', illustrated with a middle school office renovation that improved the working atmosphere. The article adds that improving the physical environment may not have tangible benefits immediately, so facilities should be assessed continuously.

"Places are the most obvious element in any school and are the easiest to change. They provide an opportunity for immediate improvement."
