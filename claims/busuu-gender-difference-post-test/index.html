Evidence¶

Kétyi 2015¶

Kétyi, A. (2015). Practical evaluation of a mobile language learning tool in higher education. Critical CALL – Proceedings of the 2015 EUROCALL Conference, Padova, Italy. http://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2015.000350

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Secondary gender analysis of the experimental group's language test results in the same eight-week study. The article reports a significant difference (p=.032) with female members at 54.5% and male members at 44.2%, males decreasing 4% while females increased 2.2%.

"we found significant difference (p=.032) in the progress of the experimental group where the female members performed better (54.5%) than the male members (44.2%)."
