## Evidence

### Gorard et al. 2015

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2015). Accelerated Reader Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.gl-assessment.co.uk/products/new-group-reading-test/test-detail

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A simple multivariate regression model using the overall reading score as dependent variable, with prior KS2 English points, age, sex, FSM, SEN, ethnicity and EAL as predictors. The model R value was 0.64; the report notes this modelling "does not have the force of a trial" and is not a test of causation.

> "The coefficient for the flag variable (treatment or control group) was 0.13, suggesting an effect from the treatment over and above an individual's prior attainme nt."
