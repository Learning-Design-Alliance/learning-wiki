# Inhibition and working-memory performance are associated with mixing costs (goal representation) at ages 4;6 and 5;3 but not at age 3;9

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At ages 4;6 and 5;3, Go/No-Go and Nebraska Barnyard performance significantly interacted with trial type, with higher scores predicting smaller mixing costs. [→ Chevalier N. 2012](#chevalier-n-2012)
`q3 i?` At age 3;9, neither inhibition nor working memory interacted with trial type, indicating no relation to mixing costs at that age. [→ Chevalier N. 2012 (2)](#chevalier-n-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Chevalier N. 2012

Chevalier N., Sheffield T. D., Nelson J. M., Clark C. A. C., Wiebe S. A., Espy K. A. (2012). Underpinnings of the Costs of Flexibility in Preschool Children: The Roles of Inhibition and Working Memory. Developmental Neuropsychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3308575/

`q3 · i?`

Multilevel modeling of Shape School accuracy at ages 4;6 and 5;3 found the GNG × trial type interaction significant (γ = .069, p = .002; γ = .054, p = .011), so "Higher GNG performance resulted in smaller mixing costs"; NB showed parallel interactions (γ = .058, p = .003; γ = .034, p = .022).

> "In contrast to the earlier time point, at ages 4;6 and 5;3, GNG performance significantly interacted with trial type (γ = .069, p = .002 at age 4;6; γ = .054, p = .011 at age 5;3). Higher GNG performance resulted in smaller mixing costs."

### Chevalier N. 2012 (2)

Chevalier N., Sheffield T. D., Nelson J. M., Clark C. A. C., Wiebe S. A., Espy K. A. (2012). Underpinnings of the Costs of Flexibility in Preschool Children: The Roles of Inhibition and Working Memory. Developmental Neuropsychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3308575/

`q3 · i?`

At age 3;9 the same multilevel models showed no GNG × trial type or NB × trial type interaction (p = .069 and p = .118), so "mixing costs do not depend on inhibition and working memory at this age."

> "At age 3;9, neither GNG nor NB performance interacted with trial type (p = .069 and p = .118, respectively), suggesting that mixing costs do not depend on inhibition and working memory at this age."
