## Evidence

### Graf Joachim 2020

Graf Joachim, Loda Teresa, Zipfel Stephan, Wosnik Annette, Mohr Daniela, Herrmann-Werner Anne. (2020). Communication skills of medical students: survey of self- and external perception in a longitudinally based trend study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02049-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sub-group analysis of standardized patients' external ratings (Table 5; overall empathy mean = 4.27 across 8484 sheets) found the 2011–2014 difference was 0.11 [95%-CI: 0.02; 0.2], p = 0.0147; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The dimension of empathy deteriorated most severely: we found a statistically significant worsening between the first (summer semester 2011) and the last cohort (summer semester 2014)."
