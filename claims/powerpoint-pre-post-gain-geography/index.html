Onivehu and Ohawuiro 2018¶

Adams Ogirima Onivehu, Onyiyeche Emilia Ohawuiro. (2018). Effect of PowerPoint Presentation on Students' Cognitive Achievement in Geography. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, VII(1), 46-60. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120184

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group pre-post comparison of the 45-student PowerPoint group over a three-week treatment, measured with the Geography Achievement Test. The article reports means of 32.67 and 35.50 and a significant difference; the printed test statistic (t(88)=35.50) appears internally inconsistent with the reported means, and no effect size is given.

"the pre -test mean score of the experimental group was M=32.67 before instruction, and the post- test mean score was M= 35.50 after instruction. These results show that there was a significant difference between mean scores of the pre-tes t and post- test of the experimental group (t(88)=35.50, p<.005)."
