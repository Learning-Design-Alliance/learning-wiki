### Molabe Manoko 2026

Molabe Manoko, Rasesemola Richard, Matlala Sidwell, Matshaka Lerato. (2026). Virtual simulation as a catalyst for equitable and authentic interprofessional learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09773-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same four online focus groups, participants described high-fidelity scenarios where patient vital signs deteriorated and teams debated next steps. One student called seeing consequences in real-time "a powerful lesson in speaking up and collaborating under pressure". Perceptions only; no competency was measured.

> "Making a wrong choice in that simulation and seeing the consequences in real-time, without harming anyone, was a powerful lesson in speaking up and collaborating under pressure that you just don't get from a textbook"
