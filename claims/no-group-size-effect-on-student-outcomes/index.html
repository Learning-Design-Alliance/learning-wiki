Evidence¶

Clarke 2017¶

Clarke, B., Doabler, C. T., Kosty, D., Kurtz Nelson, E., Smolkowski, K., Fien, H., & Turtura, J. (2017). Testing the efficacy of a kindergarten mathematics intervention by small group size. AERA Open, 3(2), 1–16. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858417706899

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Fully nested multilevel Time × Condition analysis comparing the 120 two-student and 295 five-student ROOTS condition students. Estimated gain differences were negligible: −4.8 for ASPENS (g = −0.14), 0.15 for RAENS (g = 0.03), 1.0 for SESAT (g = 0.03), and 0.5 for SAT-10 (g = 0.02).

"We found no statistically significant differences by ROOTS group size in gains from fall to spring (p > .15 for all measures)."
