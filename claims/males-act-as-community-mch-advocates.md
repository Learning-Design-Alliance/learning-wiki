## Evidence

### Kadomoto Noriko 2011

Kadomoto Noriko, Iwasa Hajime, Takahashi Miyako, Dulnuan Marcelyn M, Kai Ichiro. (2011). Ifugao males, learning and teaching for the improvement of maternal and child health status in the Philippines: an evaluation of a program. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-11-280

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported practice measures collected three months after the session from Group 1 via yes/no questionnaire items with open-ended follow-ups. Reported assistance included referring to midwives, offering transportation or money, advising hospital visits, childcare, and prayers.

> "Out of the 140 participants, 85% talked about MCH topics to other people in the community. Also, 79% said that they assisted pregnant women, such as their wives, sisters, daughters, daughter-in-laws, neighbors, and friends."
