### Tara Hofkens 2022

Tara Hofkens, Jessica Whittaker, Robert Pianta, Virginia Vitiello, Erik Ruzek, Arya Ansari. (2022). Pathways of mathematics achievement in preschool: Examining executive function and task orientation. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appdev.2022.101432

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Longitudinal mediation analysis in the study of 467 preschoolers found that growth in task orientation partially mediates the association between EF and mathematics achievement; the article reports partial, not full, mediation.

> "We found that growth in children’s task orientation partially mediates the association between EF and mathematics achievement for the preschoolers in our study."
