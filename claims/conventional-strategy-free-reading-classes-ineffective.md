## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The article argues that conducting reading classes with no reading strategies, using monotonous question-answer technique over the 100 Basic Works, makes students passive and does not improve reading performance. [→ Erkan Çer 2016](#erkan-cer-2016)

## Evidence

### Erkan Çer 2016

Erkan Çer, Ertuğrul Şahin. (2016). Improving Reading Comprehension Skills with Children's Books through Metacognitive Strategy: The Turkish Context. Journal of Education and Training Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i9.1566

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion section, not a separately tested result; the empirical basis is the control group's lack of improvement in the quasi-experiment. The article offers this as an interpretive conclusion attributed to the study's findings.

> "This study also revealed the uselessness of conventional reading classes. Conducting reading classes with no strategies for reading the books on the list of the 100 Basic Works cannot improve reading performance."
