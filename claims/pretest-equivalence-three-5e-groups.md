## Evidence

### Daşdemir 2016

İkramettin Daşdemir. (2016). The Effect of the 5E Instructional Model Enriched With Cooperative Learning and Animations on Seventh-Grade Students' Academic Achievement and Scientific Attitudes. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 9(1), 21-38. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1126710

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on SAT pre-test scores of the three intact seventh-grade classes before the 4-week light unit. The article concludes the groups had similar prior knowledge, which it calls an excellent opportunity to compare the methods.

> "it was revealed that there was no significant difference between the animation, control and cooperative groups (F(2, 81)= 2.353, p> 0.05). In this context, it can be stated that the groups had similar prior knowledge."

### Daşdemir 2016 (2)

İkramettin Daşdemir. (2016). The Effect of the 5E Instructional Model Enriched With Cooperative Learning and Animations on Seventh-Grade Students' Academic Achievement and Scientific Attitudes. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 9(1), 21-38. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1126710

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA on Scientific Attitude Scale pre-test scores. The article concludes the groups' scientific attitude skills were similar before implementation; no effect sizes are printed for either pre-test comparison.

> "it was found that there was no statistically significant difference between the animation, cooperative, and control groups. (F(2, 81)= 2.598, p= 0.08, p>0.05)."
