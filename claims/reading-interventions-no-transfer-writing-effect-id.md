## Evidence

### Bakken 2021

Bakken, R.K.; Næss, K.-A.B.; Lemons, C.J.; Hjetland, H.N. (2021). A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Reading and Writing Interventions for Students with Disorders of Intellectual Development. Educ. Sci. 2021, 11, 638. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci11100638

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of transfer-writing effect sizes from two studies, 92 participants (46 treated, 46 control), mean pretreatment age 5.7 years. The effect "was g = 0.04 (95% CI = [−0.36, 0.44], p < 0.844)"; heterogeneity was small (Q(1) = 0.03, p = 0.868).

> "Figure 4 shows that the mean effect on transfer writing for the treated and control groups was g = 0.04 (95% CI = [−0.36, 0.44], p < 0.844), which may indicate that the instructions had no effect on the transfer writing skills"
