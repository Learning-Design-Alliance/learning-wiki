`q3 i2` FSM-eligible pupils gained more than all pupils: effect sizes of 0.368 (ICT FSM) and 0.396 (non-ICT FSM), each equivalent to five months' progress. [→ McNally et al. 2016](#mcnally-et-al-2016)

## Evidence

### McNally et al. 2016

Sandra McNally, Jenifer Ruiz-Valenzuela and Heather Rolfe. (2016). ABRA: Online Reading Support — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN18254678

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis in the three-armed RCT comparing FSM-eligible pupils with all pupils; the executive summary table reports ICT FSM effect size 0.368 (0.089, 0.646) and non-ICT FSM 0.396 (0.195, 0.596), both five months' progress.

> "The impact was higher for children eligible for free school meals (FSM) for both ABRA and the non-ICT intervention, with both groups making the equivalent of five months' progress."
