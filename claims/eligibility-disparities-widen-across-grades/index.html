Descriptive plotting of eligibility proportions at grades 3, 5, and 7 (Figure 2) using the same four-state student-level database. The article reports the printed grade-3 and grade-5 eligibility percentages for ELP-only versus ELP-plus-non-ELP states.

"States relying solely on ELP criteria (A and B) show a sharp increase in eligibility rates, rising from 2-3 percent in grade 3 to 17-19 percent in grade 5. In contrast, states that incorporate non-ELP assessments alongside ELP criteria (C and D) see slower progress toward eligibility, with rates increasing only from 1-2 percent in grade 3 to 2-6 percent in grade 5."
