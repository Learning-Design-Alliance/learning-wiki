## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2014

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Myers Gil, Khatib Yasmin, Dacre Jane. (2014). How well do doctors think they perform on the General Medical Council's Tests of Competence pilot examinations? A cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2013-004131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PMQ-region comparison in the cross-sectional study. The article reports non-UK-trained doctors estimated significantly higher knowledge test performance (F (2521)=6.06, p<0.01) with no actual difference by PMQ region (F (2519)=2.28, p=0.10), and separately that UK-trained doctors outperformed non-UK-trained doctors on actual OSCE performance (F (5521)=37.96, p<0.01).

> "Non-UK trained doctors estimated significantly higher than UK-trained doctors (F (2521)=6.06, p<0.01). However, there were no actual differences in knowledge test performance between doctors of different PMQ regions (F (2519)=2.28, p=0.10)."
