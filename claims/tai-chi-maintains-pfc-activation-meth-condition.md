## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, L., Li, Y., Wang, L., Zhang, X., Sun, Y., He, S. & Zhu, W. (2026). Effects of Tai Chi on attentional bias in men with methamphetamine use disorder. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00426-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

fNIRS results for the METH-congruent dot-probe condition in the same RCT; significant FDR-corrected group differences across ROIs, e.g. R-FPA: F = 11.514, pFDR = 0.010, ηp2 = 0.233; L-VLPFC: F = 9.267, pFDR = 0.010, ηp2 = 0.196.

> "the post -intervention TC group was better able to maintain a certain level of activation and the presence of moderate or large effect sizes in various brain regions of the prefrontal cortex in the performance of a dot-probe task (when the point of detection was aligned with the METH-related stimulus) on average, compared with the control group."
