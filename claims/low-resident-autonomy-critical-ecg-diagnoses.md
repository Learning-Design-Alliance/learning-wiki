`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Resident survey results stratified by diagnosis (Fig. 3). Autonomy was "low (< 30% for bifascicular bock, failure to capture, and Brugada pattern)"; for PGY-5, greatest gaps were bifascicular block and acute MI with RBBB (both 63.5% autonomous).

> "This ranged from low (< 30% for bifascicular bock, failure to capture, and Brugada pattern), to mid-range (40–60% for PE, WPW, pericarditis, and hyperkalemia), to high (> 80%) for monomorphic and polymorphic ventricular tachycardia (VT)."
