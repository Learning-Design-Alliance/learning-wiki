Evidence¶

Kiili 2025¶

Kiili, C., Kiili, K., Räikkönen, E., & Coiro, J. (2025). Explicit Video-Based Instruction Enhanced Students' Online Credibility Evaluation Skills: Did Storifying Instruction Matter?. Computers and Education, 229, Article 105252. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105252

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental ANCOVA across storified (n = 106), non-storified (n = 123), and control (n = 102) conditions, controlling for pre-test, reading comprehension, topic order, and home language. The main effect on questioning credibility was significant with the printed effect size ηp2 = 0.18 ("F(2, 323) = 35.15, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.18"), and both intervention conditions outperformed control in post-test questioning.

"There was a statistically significant main effect of the condition on questioning the credibility after adjusting for the covariates (F(2, 323) = 35.15, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.18). Multiple comparisons showed that both the non-storified condition (MDiff = 0.79, p < .001; 95% CI = [0.54; 1.04]) and storified condition (MDiff = 0.72, p < .001; 95% CI = [0.47, 0.98]) performed better in questioning the credibility than the control condition."
