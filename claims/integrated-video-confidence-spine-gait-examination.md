# Integrated instructional videos increased first-year medical students' self-reported confidence performing spine/gait examinations compared with standard preparation materials

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial comparing intervention (custom video plus standard materials) versus control (standard materials) among first-year medical students. Mann-Whitney U analysis of Likert-scale responses showed confidence after p = 0.0006, anatomy explanation p < 0.0001, special test explanation p < 0.0001, and pathology explanation p < 0.0001, with "significantly higher confidence in performing the Spine/Gait examination" in the intervention group.

> "After viewing the assigned preparatory materials, students in the intervention group reported significantly higher confidence in performing the Spine/Gait examination. The intervention group also provided significantly higher ratings for the perceived quality of the anatomy, special test, and pathology explanations"
