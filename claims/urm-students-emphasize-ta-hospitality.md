Descriptive percentages from the hospitality theme (Table 6) in the 1,678-response dataset; 7.61% of all responses called for improved TA hospitality, and ERT-period calls (4.43%) fell below the pre- and post-pandemic rates above 10.0%.

> "Underrepresented minority students (Latino (11.3%), other gender (14.3%), and other URM (11.5%)) emphasized hospitality most frequently while response rates for other demographics (White, Asian, Asian/White, domestic, international, male, female) were less than 8.00%."
