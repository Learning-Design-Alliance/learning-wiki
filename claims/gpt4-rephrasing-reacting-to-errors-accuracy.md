## Evidence

### Jionghao Lin 2024

Jionghao Lin, Zifei Han, Danielle R. Thomas, Ashish Gurung, Shivang Gupta, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2024). How Can I Get It Right? Using GPT to Rephrase Incorrect Trainee Responses. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00970

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests on expert ratings for the Reacting to Errors lesson; GPT-4 median accuracy was 1 versus human median 0 (Table 9 mean accuracy 0.86 few-shot vs 0.07 human).

> "the accuracy of responses rephrased using the few-shot approach was significantly higher than those rephrased by humans (p< 0.01). Even the zero-shot rephrased responses were more accurate than human alterations (p< 0.05)"

### Jionghao Lin 2024 (2)

Jionghao Lin, Zifei Han, Danielle R. Thomas, Ashish Gurung, Shivang Gupta, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2024). How Can I Get It Right? Using GPT to Rephrase Incorrect Trainee Responses. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00970

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Responsiveness ratings for Reacting to Errors clustered between 0 and 1 for both GPT and human revisions; average word count per response remained consistent between GPT and human revisions.

> "most of the scores from the GPT-revised and human-revised responses were clustered between 0 and 1, with no significant difference in responsiveness between them"
