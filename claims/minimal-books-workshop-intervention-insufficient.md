## Evidence

### Leyva 2014

Leyva, D., Yoshikawa, H., Snow, C. E., Treviño, E., Rolla, A., Barata, M. C., & Weiland, C. (2014). Can we improve preschool classroom quality in Chile? A cluster-randomized trial evaluation of a professional development program. SREE Spring 2014 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562728

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the cluster-randomized trial, Comparison schools received "10 books distributed per classroom and one workshop on self-care issues for teachers and aides". The article reports Comparison teachers and aides "did not improve the quality of their interactions with children during the pre-kindergarten year to the degree that teachers and aides in the UBC program did".

> "They also suggest that simply providing some books to teachers and aides and offering a workshop (the Comparison-condition intervention) is not sufficient to make a positive and significant impact on pre-kindergarten classroom quality in Chile."
