## Evidence

### Schott CK 2024

Schott CK, Devore JA, Kelly MYB, Mayr FB. (2024). Asynchronous training for ultrasound-guided peripheral IV placement among critical care nurses. The Journal of Vascular Access. https://doi.org/10.1177/11297298221129675

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-study survey (Kirkpatrick level 1) of nurses who completed the asynchronous cart-based education, reported in Table 3 across three respondent groups. Among those completing pre and post training, 80% agreed they enjoyed improved PIV placement skills in patients.

> "The most commonly reported elements the nurses did not enjoy were having technical issues using the computer to train (33%) and technical issues when using the ultrasound machine (20%). No one reported that they did not enjoy this self-directed learning or learning without an instructor present"
