## Evidence

### Liborius 2019

Liborius, P., Bellhäuser, H., & Schmitz, B. (2019). What makes a good study day? An intraindividual study on university students' time investment by means of time-series analyses. Learning and Instruction, 60, 310-321. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spectral analysis of trend-adjusted aggregated time series from the daily diary study. The article reports "a periodicity with a frequency of seven" in both time series, with lecture time peaking on Tuesdays and approaching zero on weekends, and independent study lowest on Saturdays.

> "Analysis for both lecture- and independent study time investments shows a periodicity with a frequency of seven, which indicates that a weekly rhythm is observable in both time series."
