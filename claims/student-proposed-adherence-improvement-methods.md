Student-proposed improvement methods reported in the barriers section of the cross-sectional survey (N = 420). The study reports "Regular infection control training sessions, easy access to PPE and other infection control supplies, more supervision or guidance from faculty and access to online infection control modules" at 86.2%, 81.9%, 79% and 67.1%, respectively.

> "Regular infection control training sessions, easy access to PPE and other infection control supplies, more supervision or guidance from faculty and access to online infection control modules were reported by 86.2%, 81.9%, 79% and 67.1% of the participants, respectively as methods that can improve adherence to infection control practices"
