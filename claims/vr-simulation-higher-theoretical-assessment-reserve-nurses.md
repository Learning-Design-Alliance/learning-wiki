## Evidence

### Zhang Dandan 2021

Zhang Dandan, Liao Hongwu, Jia Yitong, Yang Wenren, He Pingping, Wang Dongmei, Chen Yongjun, Yang Wei, Zhang Yin-Ping. (2021). Effect of virtual reality simulation training on the response capability of public health emergency reserve nurses in China: a quasiexperimental study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-048611

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective quasiexperimental study with control group of 120 nurses randomly divided into two groups; postintervention theoretical assessment means were 86.62±3.91 (intervention) versus 82.92±4.55 (control), t=4.783, p<0.001. The article reports "significantly higher than those in the control group".

> "The score of theoretical assessment and the capacity of emergency care in the intervention group were significantly higher than those in the control group (p<0.001, table 3)"
