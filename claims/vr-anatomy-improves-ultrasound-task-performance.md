# VR-enhanced anatomy training improves overall ultrasonography task performance in novice medical students

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Novice learners receiving VR anatomy training scored significantly higher on ultrasonography task performance than control learners (Cohen's d = 1.03). [→ Hu Kai-Chun 2020](#hu-kai-chun-2020)

## Evidence

### Hu Kai-Chun 2020

Hu Kai-Chun, Salcedo Daniel, Kang Yi-No, Lin Che-Wei, Hsu Chin-Wang, Cheng Chung-Yi, Suk Fat-Moon, Huang Wen-Cheng. (2020). Impact of virtual reality anatomy training on ultrasound competency development: A randomized controlled trial. PLoS ONE 15(11): e0242731. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242731

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 101 third-year medical students randomized to a VR-enhanced or control ultrasound workshop. The Mann-Whitney U test showed "significantly higher scores of ultrasonographic task performance"; Table 2 reports Cohen's d = 1.03 with observed power 1. Medians were 16 versus 10.

> "Mann-Whitney U test demonstrated that students in the intervention group had significantly higher scores of ultrasonographic task performance than in the control group (Mann-Whitney U = 595; P < 0.01)."
