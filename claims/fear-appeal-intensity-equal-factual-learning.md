## Evidence

### Janis 1953

Janis, Irving L., and Feshbach, Seymour. (1953). Effects of Fear-Arousing Communications. Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology 48: 78-92.

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A controlled experiment with 200 Connecticut high-school students assigned to three appeal conditions and a control group, with a pretest one week before, an immediate posttest, and a one-week follow-up. The results state the three forms were "equally effective in teaching the factual content."

> "All three forms were equally effective in teaching the factual content of the communication and modifying beliefs concerning the characteristics of the proper kind of toothbrush."
