# Individual learners who make more image-to-text and text-to-image gaze transitions score higher on the first posttest

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Image-to-text transitions correlate with first posttest score (r = 0.47), as do text-to-image transitions (r = 0.48). [→ Sharma Kshitij 2020](#sharma-kshitij-2020)

## Evidence

### Sharma Kshitij 2020

Sharma Kshitij, Leftheriotis Ioannis, Giannakos Michail. (2020). Utilizing Interactive Surfaces to Enhance Learning, Collaboration and Engagement: Insights from Learners’ Gaze and Speech. Sensors (Basel). https://doi.org/10.3390/s20071964

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Individual-level Pearson correlations during the poster phase of the within-group study (36 participants). Printed effect sizes: "r (34) = 0.47, p = .003" for image-to-text and "r (34) = 0.48, p < .002" for text-to-image transitions.

> "We observe a significant correlation between the transitions from image to text and the first posttest score (r (34) = 0.47, p = .003). The participant having high proportion of the image to text transitions score high in the first posttest"
