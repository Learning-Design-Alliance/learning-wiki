# The identity interaction on mixed-trial accuracy held marginally when controlling for language abilities, which themselves did not predict performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among seventh graders, the Turkish × German identity interaction on mixed-trial accuracy remained marginally significant with language abilities controlled (β = 0.03, p = 0.081), while language abilities did not interactively predict accuracy controlling for identity (β = 0.00, p = 0.955). [→ Spiegler O and Leyendecker B 2017](#spiegler-o-and-leyendecker-b-2017)

## Evidence

### Spiegler O and Leyendecker B 2017

Spiegler O and Leyendecker B (2017) Balanced Cultural Identities Promote Cognitive Flexibility among Immigrant Children. Front. Psychol. 8:1579. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01579

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post hoc regression analyses with seventh graders only added self-reported Turkish and German language abilities as controls; the identity interaction held only marginally, and language abilities did not interactively predict accuracy when identity was controlled. The authors call these analyses highly underpowered.

> "This was the case but only at a marginal significant level, β= 0.03, p= 0.081, F(10, 51)= 2.75, p= 0.009, R2= 0.35."
