## Evidence

### De Cassai Alessandro 2021

De Cassai Alessandro, Geraldini Federico, Pasin Laura, Boscolo Annalisa, Zarantonello Francesco, Tocco Martina, Pretto Chiara, Perona Matteo, Carron Michele, Navalesi Paolo. (2021). Safety in training for ultrasound guided internal jugular vein CVC placement: a propensity score analysis. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01460-0

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation (Discussion) of prior studies they report: Mey et al. found success and complication rates dependent on the ultrasound operator's proficiency, and Rando et al. found non-significant differences only within ultrasound-only subgroups (95 vs 86% success, 8.3 vs 7.8% complications) with small sample sizes. The review of these results is attributed, not re-analyzed.

> "Technology in ultrasound machines and probes has undergone dramatic change since its inception, leading to images with high spatial and contrast resolution [11]. Therefore, it is not surprising that in Mey’s study [5], performed 20 years ago, the ultrasound operator’s proficiency was essential for the correct visualization of the anatomical structure and for the success of the procedure."
