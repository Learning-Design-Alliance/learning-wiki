Trial-by-trial strategy classification across the 11-week practice period, summarized in Tables 1 and 2. Across children, sum (34%) and retrieval (22%) were most frequent; variability was not explained by knowledge level, since the heaviest retrieval user ranked seventh of eight in percentage correct.

> "As shown in Table 2, each child used at least five strategies. The second type of variability was the relative frequency of use of each strategy by different children. Use of the sum strategy ranged from 0% to 69%, use of retrieval from 1% to 61%"
