## Evidence

### Hillemans V 2025

Hillemans V, Verhoeven DJ, van de Mortel X, Bökkerink GMJ, Botden SMBI, Joosten M, de Blaauw I. (2025). Optimizing skill acquisition: the role of self-assessment during a continuous training program for laparoscopic suturing. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11682-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In this three-group training experiment (55 participants: 19 control, 18 periodic SA, 18 continuous SA), Kruskal-Wallis tests compared groups at three test moments. For time, "no significant differences were found between groups at the pre-test" (p = 0.362); distance likewise showed no significant differences (baseline p = 0.923, after-test p = 0.468).

> "For the primary outcome parameter time, no significant differences were found between groups at the pre-test (control group 614.0 s, periodic SA group 692.5 s, continuous SA group 532.0 s, p = 0.362)"
