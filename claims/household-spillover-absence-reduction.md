## Evidence

### Rogers and Feller 2016

Todd Rogers, Avi Feller. (2016). Parent Beliefs and Student Absences: Large Absence-Reduction Field Experiment. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2016/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory spillover analysis (RQ4) of households with two eligible students, comparing non-focal students across assigned conditions. The article also reports focal students' absences reduced by approximately 1.3 days or 7.4% (p<.01) in these households.

> "The non-focal student’s absences in the Self and Norms groups were also reduced by 0.8 days or 4.8% (p<.01) as compared to that of non-focal students in the Control and Encourage groups."
