## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Al-Bakri Moug 2023

Al-Bakri Moug, Skovgaard Anne Mette, Bach-Holm Daniella, Larsen Dorte Ancher, Siersma Volkert, Kessel Line. (2023). School performances in children with cataract: results from a population-based cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Pooled linear-regression analysis of Danish national mathematics tests (grades 3, 6 and 8) with inverse-probability weighting and GEE. Table 2 prints an adjusted mean difference of −4.78 (95% CI −8.18 to −1.38, p value=0.006); the mean difference is unstandardised, so no effect size is coded.

> "The cataract cohort had significantly lower pooled test scores in mathematics (grades 3, 6 and 8 pooled) compared with the control cohort, see table 2."
