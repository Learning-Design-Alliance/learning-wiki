## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Genç et al. 2025, Turkey) of structured digital-based education delivered over 8 weeks in 15 sessions, combining active learning, mobile applications, computer simulation games, film analysis, online training modules, disaster drills, and a technical visit, in 74 senior nursing students. The review reports "p < 0.05"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Significant improvement in disaster literacy and disaster preparedness belief levels in the intervention group compared with the control group (p < 0.05); moderate to strong group × time effects across most outcomes; increased adoption of disaster‐related mobile applications, AFAD volunteering, and family disaster plans."
