## Evidence

### Wu Fanghui 2023

Wu Fanghui, Wang Tao, Yin Danxu, Xu Xiaoxiao, Jin Cancan, Mu Nan, Tan Qingrong. (2023). Application of case-based learning in psychology teaching: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04525-5

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Galbraith plot analysis of the 15 RCTs identified four studies as potential heterogeneity sources; the refitted forest plot after their removal still showed significantly higher CBL exam scores with reduced heterogeneity.

> "four studies [18, 26, 32, 33] outside the parallel lines were considered to be the potential sources of heterogeneity. After removing them, no obvious heterogeneity was found across studies (I2= 21.50%, p = 0.24). However, even after removing these four studies, a forest plot (Fig. 5) indicated that the total examination scores of students in the CBL group were still significantly higher than those in the control group [Hedges’ g = 0.65, 95% CI (0.54, 0.76), p < 0.00]."
