### Karimi Hossein 2025

Karimi Hossein, Clarke Samantha, Watson Emily. (2025). Comparing Clinical Preparedness of Newly Qualified Diagnostic Radiographers Trained With Immersive Virtual Reality vs. Traditional Simulation: A Mixed‐Methods Study. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.882

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA in the 80-radiographer comparative study tested university and its interaction with training type. The article reports no statistically significant difference for either term; equivalence was not tested. Table 2 prints F = 0.320 (university) and F = 2.600 (interaction).

> "However, no significant main effect of university (p = 0.574) or interaction effect between Training Type and University (p = 0.111) was found."
