# Cognitive load is negatively associated with critical thinking and with self-regulated learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Critical thinking correlates negatively with cognitive load (r = −0.19, p < 0.01), and cognitive load correlates negatively with self-regulated learning (r = −0.16, p < 0.05), while critical thinking correlates positively with self-regulated learning (r = 0.62, p < 0.01). [→ Arshad A 2026](#arshad-a-2026)

## Evidence

### Arshad A 2026

Arshad A, Lone A, Arickswamy L, Hassan K, Alnaim AA and AlFarhan MF (2026) From AI use to critical thinking among medical students: a moderated mediation perspective on cognitive load and self-regulated learning. Front. Psychol. 17:1883053. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1883053

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations in the same 480-student survey. Critical thinking correlated with self-regulated learning at "r = 0.62, p < 0.01"; cognitive load showed small negative correlations with critical thinking (r = −0.19) and self-regulated learning (r = −0.16).

> "Critical thinking was negatively correlated with cognitive load (r =−0.19, p < 0.01), while positively correlated with self-regulated learning (r = 0.62, p < 0.01). Cognitive load also was found to have a small but statistically significant (r = −0.16,p <0.05) negative relationship with self-regulated learning."
