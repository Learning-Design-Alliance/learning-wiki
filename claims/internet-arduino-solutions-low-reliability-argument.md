`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Theoretical argument in the article's problems section (§2.1), not an empirical test. The authors contend that because most shared solutions are not expert-developed, "the standards and rules of correct application of the parts are often violated", and that questionable solutions spread quickly, making reliability rather low. No data are reported for this assertion.

> "On the other hand, most of the solutions are not developed by experts, therefore the standards and rules of correct application of the parts are often violated."
