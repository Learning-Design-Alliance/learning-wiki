## Evidence

### Gupta 2010

Gupta, A., & Elby, A. (2010). Beyond epistemological deficits: Incorporating flexible epistemological views into fine-grained cognitive dynamics. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0461)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case-study analysis of one clinical interview episode, using discourse and framing analysis of gestures and word choice. The authors argue the impasse reflects "his perception of how difficult it would be to reconcile common sense and the formal result".

> "The reason he gets and stays stuck, we argue, is that his perception of how difficult it would be to reconcile common sense and the formal result (while he's "on the spot" in the interview!) stabilizes his epistemological stance"
