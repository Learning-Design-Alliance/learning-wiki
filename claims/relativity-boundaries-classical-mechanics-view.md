### Gousopoulos 2023

Gousopoulos, D., Kapotis, E., & Kalkanis, G. (2023). Students' Difficulties in Understanding the Basic Principles of Relativity After Standard Instruction. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.09914](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.09914)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the qualitative questionnaire and interview analysis of the main study, 73,3% of the 45 students held that "Relativity deals with phenomena that occur at relativistic speeds or due to large amounts of mass", as one student quote about speeds near the speed of light illustrates.

> "A prevailed view among students (73,3%) was that Relativity deals with phenomena that occur at relativistic speeds or due to large amounts of mass."
