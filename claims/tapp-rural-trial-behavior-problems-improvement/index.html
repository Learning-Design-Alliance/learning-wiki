Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP) Family Engagement evidence summary 2012¶

Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP) Family Engagement evidence summary. (2012). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/teachers-and-parents-as-partners-tapp/

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Second randomized controlled trial extending TAPP to 45 rural schools with 267 K–3 students and 152 teachers. Teacher BASC-2 ratings showed a "significant improvement in school-related behavioral problems" for TAPP (ES +0.45).

"Teacher-reported outcomes again favored the TAPP group, with a significant improvement in school-related behavioral problems on the BASC-2 (effect size = +0.45)."
