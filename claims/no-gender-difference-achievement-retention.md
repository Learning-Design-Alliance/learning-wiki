## Evidence

### Badeleh 2011

Alireza Badeleh. (2011). The Effect of Laboratory Training Model of Teaching and Traditional Method on Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Skills-Components of Achievement, Total Achievement and Retention Level in Chemistry. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, Vol. 7, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102743

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent sample t-tests comparing male (n = 61) and female (n = 59) students on overall achievement (Table 10) and retention (Table 12, t = -.847, p = .398). No significant difference; equivalence was not tested and no effect sizes are printed.

> "the t-value of -.096 was found to be statistically insignificant as the p value of .924 was larger than the cut-off .05. This showed that there was no significant difference between male and female students with respect to post scores in overall achievement in chemistry"
