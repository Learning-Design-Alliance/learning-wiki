# Experimental groups across martial arts, sports games and complex-coordination specializations achieved larger gains in breaststroke and back crawl technique than control groups

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Martial-arts experimental students scored 4,24 (S = 0,43) on breaststroke technique (68.2% gain) versus 3,71 (S = 0,64) in control (44.9%), and 4,34 (S = 0,43) on back crawl (66.9% gain) versus 3,79 (S = 0,61) in control (44.6%). [→ Yermahanova 2016](#yermahanova-2016)
`q2 i?` Sports-games experimental students scored 4,28 (S = 0,29) on breaststroke (63.9% gain) versus 3,89 (S = 0,44) in control (50.7%), and 4,53 (S = 0,38) on back crawl (68.4% gain) versus 4,01 (S = 0.45) in control (52.4%). [→ Yermahanova 2016 (2)](#yermahanova-2016-2)
