## Evidence

### Kremser Wolfgang 2021

Kremser Wolfgang, Kranzinger Stefan, Bernhart Severin. (2021). Design and Implementation of a Gesture-Aided E-Learning Platform. Sensors. https://doi.org/10.3390/s21238042

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author visual labeling of the 491 webcam-recorded GEC executions collected in the monitoring log during the two-week field test, compared against reference gestures and labeled Correct, Poor, or Wrong. The log contained "491 GEC executions", more than the anticipated 456.

> "After the trial period ended, the log contained 491 GEC executions. These were more than the anticipated 456 videos. During labeling, it became apparent that some participants stopped and restarted mid-lesson. Based on these videos, 340 (69.2%) GEC executions were rated “Correct”, 95 (19.3%) were rated “Poor”, and 56 (11.4%) were rated “Bad”."

### Kremser Wolfgang 2021 (2)

Kremser Wolfgang, Kranzinger Stefan, Bernhart Severin. (2021). Design and Implementation of a Gesture-Aided E-Learning Platform. Sensors. https://doi.org/10.3390/s21238042

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary analysis of the labeled executions identified which gestures were performed worst. The article reports that "The majority (54.3%) of “Poor” and “Bad” GEC executions" occurred during Aufmerksamkeit and Papier, attributed to background depth cues and hand-clipping issues.

> "The majority (54.3%) of “Poor” and “Bad” GEC executions occurred during two gestures: “Aufmerksamkeit” (eng.: “attention”; putting a hand behind an ear and leaning back) and “Papier” (eng.: “paper”; crumbling a piece of paper and throwing it away)."
