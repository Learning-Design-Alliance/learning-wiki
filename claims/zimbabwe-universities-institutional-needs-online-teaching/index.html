Chimbunde 2023¶

Chimbunde, P. (2023). Funding the online teaching and learning in developing countries: insights from Zimbabwe. Education Tech Research Dev, 71, 753–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10163-3

Qualitative interview and WhatsApp-discussion study of 15 Heads of Departments across Zimbabwean universities; the article reports institutional needs "for example, the absence of well-trained personnel, poor digital infrastructure and cyber security" and attributes most of them to inadequate funding.
