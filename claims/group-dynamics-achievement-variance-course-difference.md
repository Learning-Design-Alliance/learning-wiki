## Evidence

### Premo 2022

Premo, J., Wyatt, B. N., Horn, M., & Wilson-Ashworth, H. (2022). Which Group Dynamics Matter: Social Predictors of Student Achievement in Team-Based Undergraduate Science Classrooms. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 21(3). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-06-0164

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The biology RQ2 regression model (n = 116) reached adjusted R² = 0.41, while the chemistry model (n = 185) reached adjusted R² = 0.15, suggesting "dynamics of group learning were less impactful on student achievement in chemistry than in biology."

> "Group qualities were able to predict a much higher percentage of a student's grade in biology (41% of a student's final grade point average in the course) than in chemistry (15%)."
