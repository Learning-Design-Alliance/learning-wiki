Evidence¶

Priyono 2018¶

Priyono. (2018). The Implementation of PAIKEM (Active, Innovative, Creative, Effective, and Exiting Learning) and Conventional Learning Method to Improve Student Learning Results. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 124-137. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181986

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent-samples t-test within the PAIKEM group (n = 20) comparing high- and low-motivation students. Table 8 reports t = 5.786, df = 18, Sig. .000, mean difference 13.17582. A parallel test within the conventional group (Table 9) reports t = 2.498, df = 26, Sig. .019.

"Table 8 shows that the significance value was below 0.05 (<0.05) meaning that there were differences on Mathematics learning results between V graders at State Elementary School 2 & 3 Cakul Dongko District of the academic year 2009/2010 for students with high and low motivation by implementing PAIKEM method."
