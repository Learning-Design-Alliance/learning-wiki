`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic analysis (Table 2, Fig. 1b) of the same 4870-participant survey on readiness to practice. The analysis found "the opposite trend was observed for students living in Eastern Indonesia (OR 0.629 [95% CI: 0.44–0.89], p = 0.009)" relative to Java, while male sex, public institutions, Central Indonesia, prior volunteering and low family income (OR 1.51, p = 0.011) were associated with better readiness.

> "students residing in Sumatra were also more likely to yield better readiness when compared to students living in Java (OR 1.56 [95% CI: 1.15–2.12], p = 0.004), while the opposite trend was observed for students living in Eastern Indonesia (OR 0.629 [95% CI: 0.44–0.89], p = 0.009)"
