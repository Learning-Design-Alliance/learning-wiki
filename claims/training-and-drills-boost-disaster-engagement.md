## Evidence

### Ličen Sabina 2025

Ličen Sabina, Prosen Mirko. (2025). Disaster Nursing Competencies in a Time of Global Conflicts and Climate Crises: A Cross‐Sectional Survey Study. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney and Kruskal–Wallis comparisons on the roles and responsibilities subscale in the cross-sectional survey (e.g., drill participation H = 30.094, p = 0.000). Nurses with more than five drills "scored significantly higher than those with a lower frequency of participation".

> "Participation in workplace‐organised training (p = 0.000) and frequent participation in emergency drills (p = 0.000) were significantly associated with higher levels of engagement. A post‐hoc analysis revealed that nurses who participated in more than five drills scored significantly higher than those with a lower frequency of participation (all p = 0.000)."
