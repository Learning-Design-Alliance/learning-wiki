# Bioscience students who took the CLASP series had statistically significantly higher upper-division GPAs than students who took the traditional Physics 5 series

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a direct comparison of bioscience graduates of 1998-1999, CLASP students' upper-division GPAs were statistically significantly higher than Physics 5 students' (p = 0.05), though selection bias and prior interactive labs in Physics 5 are acknowledged confounds. [→ Potter 2018](#potter-2018)

## Evidence

### Potter 2018

Potter, W., Webb, D., West, E., Paul, C., Bowen, M., Weiss, B., Coleman, L., & De Leone, C. (2018). Sixteen years of Collaborative Learning through Active Sense-making in Physics (CLASP) at UC Davis. https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6970

`q2 · i1`

Observational comparison of upper-division GPA (intro-physics grades removed) for bioscience graduates of 1998-1999 who took Physics 5 (779 students, UDGPA 3.093) versus CLASP (651 students, UDGPA 3.142). The article reports the distributions are "statistically signiﬁcantly diﬀerent, p = 0 .05". No effect size is printed.

> "A t-test shows that these Physics 5 and CLASP distributions are statistically signiﬁcantly diﬀerent, p = 0 .05."
