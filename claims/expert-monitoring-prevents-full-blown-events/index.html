Evidence¶

H. P. A. Boshuizen 2016¶

H. P. A. Boshuizen. (2016). Teaching as regulation and dealing with complexity. Instructional Science, 44, 311–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9377-x

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The editorial reports, citing Wolff et al. (this issue), that novices are distracted by possibly irrelevant phenomena while experts use monitoring strategies. Reported second-hand; the study's design is not described here.

"They found that novices’ are distracted by phenomena that may not even be relevant for the goals pursued, whereas experts have developed monitoring strategies that may help them identify upcoming complications before these have become full-blown."
