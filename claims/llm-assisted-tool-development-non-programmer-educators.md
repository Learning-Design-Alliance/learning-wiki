### Yifeng Liu 2025

Yifeng Liu, Min Li, Zhaojun Zhang, Youkang Fang, and Meibao Qin. (2025). Developing a ChatGPT-Based Tool for Physics Experiment Teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.13011](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.13011)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

A demonstration case study: the authors prompted ChatGPT-4.0-Turbo to generate Python code, refined it interactively, debugged it in Cursor, and packaged it with PyInstaller. The article offers no evaluation data for the efficiency claim; this is the authors' own interpretation.

> "With these tools, even physics teachers with no programming experience can easily generate fully functional physics teaching tools, thereby improving teaching efficiency and optimizing students’ experimental experience."
