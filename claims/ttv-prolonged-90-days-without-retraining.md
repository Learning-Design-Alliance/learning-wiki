## Evidence

### Liu Zi-Jia 2017

Liu Zi-Jia, Yi Jie, Chen Wei-Yun, Zhang Xiu-Hua, Huang Yu-Guang, Martin Samantha. (2017). Comparison of learning performance of 2 intubating laryngeal mask airways in novice: A randomized crossover manikin study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000006905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Skill-retention reevaluation in the same randomized crossover manikin study, conducted after 90 days without additional demonstration or practice. Times taken for the two devices "were both significantly longer than the initial training", each comparison P < .001.

> "In follow-up, times taken for i-gel and Aura-i were both significantly longer than the initial training (for i-gel, 11.8 ± 2.9 seconds vs 14.9 ± 3.6 seconds, P < .001; for Aura-i, 22.4 ± 5.2 seconds vs 28.9 ± 10.0 seconds, P < .001)."
