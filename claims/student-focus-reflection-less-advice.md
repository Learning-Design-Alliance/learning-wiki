## Evidence

### Päuler-Kuppinger and Jucks 2018

Lena Päuler-Kuppinger & Regina Jucks. (2018). Out-of-Class Instruction in Higher Education – Impact of Approaches to Teaching and Discipline. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p199

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental 2x2 manipulation in the online study (N = 70): reflection instructions made one teaching approach salient before answering the student email. The article reports the advice-proportion difference with b* = -0.62, p = .039; no effect size.

> "Academics who received the student-focused instructions to reflect on their teaching practice gave less advice than academics who received the teacher-focused instructions, b* = -0.62, z = -2.07, p = .039."
