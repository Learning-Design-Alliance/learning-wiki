LearnLM Team, Google. (2025). Towards an AI-Augmented Textbook. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Immediately after the Immediate Assessment in the RCT, students rated its difficulty on a 1-5 scale. The article reports the means and states the difference "is not statistically significant"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Students using Learn Your Way rated the assessment’s difficulty as3.6on average (on a scale of1− 5), slightly higher than those in the control group who gave an average rating of3.37, but the difference is not statistically significant between the groups using the Mann-Whitney U test."
