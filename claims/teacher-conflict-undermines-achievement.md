## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Low academic achievement predicted increased conflict with teachers in grade 7 fall, and increased conflict with teachers predicted decreased achievement in grade 7 spring. [→ Kiuru 2020](#kiuru-2020)

## Evidence

### Kiuru 2020

Kiuru, N., Wang, M.-T., Salmela-Aro, K., Kannas, L., Ahonen, T., & Hirvonen, R. (2020). Associations between Adolescents' Interpersonal Relationships, School Well-being, and Academic Achievement during Educational Transitions. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-019-01184-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Combined cross-lagged SEM for conflict (N = 848). The article reports both cross-lagged paths between achievement and teacher conflict as significant, while closeness to teachers had no unique effects on later well-being or achievement.

> "low academic achievement predicted the adolescents' increased conflict with teachers in the fall of the seventh grade. Finally, increased conflict with teachers in the fall of the seventh grade predicted the adolescents decreased academic achievement in the spring of the seventh grade"
