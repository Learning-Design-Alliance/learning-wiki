## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Parental positive learning involvement was not significantly correlated with children's academic achievement; its effect was indirect via individual factors and school belonging. [→ Seol 2013](#seol-2013)

## Evidence

### Seol 2013

Seol, K. O., & Jung, S. W. (2013). School Belonging as a Mediator of the Relationship Between Individual and Parental Factors and Academic Achievement of Elementary School Students. The Korean Journal of School Psychology, 10(1), 41-58. https://koreapsych.org

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Correlational analysis in the same 250-child sample (Table 1 shows r=.08 for involvement and grades). The article reports "상관이 없는 것으로 나타났다" between parental positive involvement and achievement, while involvement correlated with the hypothesized mediators.

적학습관여와 아동의 학업성적과는 유의미한
상관이 없는 것으로 나타났다."
