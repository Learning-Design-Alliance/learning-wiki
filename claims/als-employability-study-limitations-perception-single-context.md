María-Jesús Martínez-Argüelles, Dolors Plana-Erta, Àngels Fitó-Bertran. (2023). Impact of using authentic online learning environments on students' perceived employability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10171-3

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' stated limitation in the discussion: the quantitative analysis is based on students' perceptions of employability, which are conditioned by personal characteristics such as self-regulation and self-efficacy, and the study was performed in a specific online context. This is an interpretive statement, not a tested result.

> "Secondly, we must emphasize that the research was performed in a specific online context. Therefore, additional research is required to analyze to what extent these results can be extrapolated to other courses, disciplines, LMS and cultural contexts."
