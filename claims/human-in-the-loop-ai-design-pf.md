## Evidence

### Jinhee Kim 2026

Jinhee Kim, Xi Lin, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick. (2026). Designing AI systems to support a productive-failure-based learning: insights from adult learners on AI applications and AI system design principles. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10655-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Within the human-AI collaboration theme of the focus group findings, participants argued for human-in-the-loop approaches in which AI models, which can be flawed and lack contextual nuance, are refined by human feedback from learners and instructors.

> "Rather than letting AI provide a final answer, HITL design can use AI as a tool to allow ALs to identify potential areas for improvement in their learning process and tasks and make the final decisions themselves"
