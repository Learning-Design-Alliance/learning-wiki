Curricular integration of a 4-year simulation-based education program on clinical reasoning for physiotherapy students: a descriptive design and implementation report. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09289-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive implementation report of a longitudinal SBEP at a single institution. The article reports that 2,047 students started the degree with the SBEP embedded, "of whom 1441 had completed all four years of the program at the time of writing (2025)". Approximately 32 faculty members (80%) were trained as simulation facilitators.

> "Since the full roll-out of the longitudinal program, a total of 2047 students have started the degree with the SBEP embedded in the regular curriculum, of whom 1441 had completed all four years of the program at the time of writing (2025)"
