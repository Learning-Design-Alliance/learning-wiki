Reliability analysis of the six blinded evaluators' scores in the randomized cross-over study. The article reports the total-score ICC as 0.988 and dimension-specific ICCs ranging from 0.935 to 0.965; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The two-way absolute-agreement average-measures ICC for the total drawing score was 0.988. Dimension-specific ICCs were also high, with values of 0.935 for structural completeness, 0.950 for proportional accuracy, 0.965 for adjacency accuracy, and 0.959 for artistic quality."
