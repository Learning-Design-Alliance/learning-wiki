## Evidence

### Baş 2016

Baş, G. (2016). The effect of multiple intelligences theory-based education on academic achievement: A meta-analytic review. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 16, 1833–1864. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.6.0015

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis by course type: science (d = 0.863), social science (d = 0.968), foreign language (d = 1.958), ability courses (d = 1.139). The fixed effects model was adopted after the heterogeneity test indicated homogeneity.

> "the studies included in the meta-analysis were seen to be homogeneous, thus there was not a significant difference in terms of the effect sizes when grouped according to course type"
