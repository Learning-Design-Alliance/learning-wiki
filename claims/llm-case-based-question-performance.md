### Yilmaz Birkan Eyup 2025

Yilmaz Birkan Eyup, Gokkurt Yilmaz Busra Nur, Ozbey Furkan. (2025). Artificial intelligence performance in answering multiple-choice oral pathology questions: a comparative analysis. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05926-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square analysis of LLM responses to the case-based subset of the 100 DUS oral pathology questions, classified by two experts. The article reports a significant difference among models (p = 0.034), with "ChatGPT o1 with 27 correct answers" highest and Claude close behind at 26. No effect size is printed.

> "In case-based questions, a statistically significant difference was found among the models (p = 0.034). In this category, the model with the highest number of correct answers was ChatGPT o1 with 27 correct answers (p = 0.330), while Claude demonstrated a similar performance with 26 correct answers."
