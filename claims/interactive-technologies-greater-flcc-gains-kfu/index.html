Evidence¶

Fahrutdinova 2014¶

Fahrutdinova, R. A., Yarmakeev, I. E., & Fakhrutdinov, R. R. (2014). The Formation of Students’ Foreign Language Communicative Competence during the Learning Process of the English Language through Interactive Learning Technologies (The Study on the Basis of Kazan Federal University). English Language Teaching, 7(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n12p36

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Re-testing at the end of the school year compared the experimental group taught with interactive technologies against the control group taught traditionally. The article reports both groups grew, but "the greatest increase is observed in the experimental group". No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"Therefore, the average level of th e development of communicative competence has grown in both study groups. However, the greatest increase is observed in the experimental group comparing with the control one."

Related Claims¶

  • Linguistic-component scores rose to 79% (vocabulary) and 94% (grammar) at re-testing, with the largest gains in the experimental group — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Socio-cultural and strategic component scores rose substantially at re-testing (87% value ranking, 58% traditions, 86% strategies, 82% social) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • CALL instruction produced significantly better TOEIC listening score gains than traditional textbook instruction in a quasi-experiment with two groups of 25 Vietnamese college students — related
  • University students in a blended learning German course showed significantly greater achievement gains than students in a traditional face-to-face course over 10 weeks — related
  • Baseline testing showed weak discursive (32%) and socio-cultural knowledge (34%) but stronger strategic (62%) and value-ranking (67%) performance — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
