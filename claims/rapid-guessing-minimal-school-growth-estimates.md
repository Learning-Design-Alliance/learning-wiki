# Rapid guessing had mostly minimal effects on estimates of school contributions to student growth in this sample

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Only the fall-to-fall linear growth difference between unadjusted and filtered models was significant at the .05 level; other parameter differences were not significant and magnitudes were modest. [→ Kuhfeld 2019](#kuhfeld-2019)

## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, M. & Soland, J. (2019). Using Assessment Metadata to Quantify the Impact of Test Disengagement on Estimates of Educational Effectiveness. (The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/using-assessment-metadata-to-quantify-the-impact-of-test-disengagement-on-estimates-of-educational-effectiveness-paper/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-level HLMs of MAP Growth reading RIT scores for 22,055 students in 343 schools compared unadjusted, filtered, and effort-moderated outcomes. The article reports "significant at the .05 level" only for the filtered fall-to-fall linear growth contrast; linear growth estimates ranged from 6.25 to 6.67 points.

> "differences in estimates of school contributions to fall-to-fall linear growth between the unadjusted and filtered models are significant at the .05 level. None of the other differences are significant."

### Kuhfeld 2019 (2)

Kuhfeld, M. & Soland, J. (2019). Using Assessment Metadata to Quantify the Impact of Test Disengagement on Estimates of Educational Effectiveness. (The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/using-assessment-metadata-to-quantify-the-impact-of-test-disengagement-on-estimates-of-educational-effectiveness-paper/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 1 scatterplots compare empirical Bayes school random-effect estimates from original RIT scores with filtered and effort-moderated estimates. The text states "correlations range from .914 to .978" under filtering and roughly .99 or above for effort-moderated scores.

> "When filtering is used, correlations range from .914 to .978. For effort-moderated scores, correlations are all roughly at or above .99."
