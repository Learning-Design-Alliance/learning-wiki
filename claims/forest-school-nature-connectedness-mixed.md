### Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia 2023

Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia. (2023). Psychological Benefits of Attending Forest School for Preschool Children: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09750-4

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

Review's account of Barrable & Booth (2020a, 2020b), a cross-sectional quasi-experimental comparison of 132 forest-nursery and 84 indoor-nursery children using the parent-reported CNI-PPC; printed β values of 0.59 and 0.76.

> "The outdoor group demonstrated higher scores on the dimensions of enjoyment ( β = 0.59, p  < 001) and awareness ( β = 0.76, p  < 001), while t he two groups did not show significant differences on the other dimensions of connectedness to nature (empathy and responsibility)."

### Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia 2023 (2)

Enrico Sella · Monica Bolognesi · Emma Bergamini · Lucia Mason · Francesca Pazzaglia. (2023). Psychological Benefits of Attending Forest School for Preschool Children: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09750-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review's synthesis of two studies using the Nature Relatedness and Environmentally Responsible Behavior tool; nature relatedness differed in one study (p < 0.05) but not the other (p = 0.22, ηp2 = 0.02).

> "Both of these studies (Elliot et al., 2014; Müller et al., 2017) found no significant differences in environmentally responsible behavior between children in nature preschools and indoor preschools."
