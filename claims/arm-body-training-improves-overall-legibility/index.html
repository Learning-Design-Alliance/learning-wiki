Evidence¶

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro 2024¶

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro, Natacha Boissicat, Fanny Gimbert, Céline Pobel-Burtin, Marie-Caroline Croset, Marie-Line Bosse, and Cécile Nurra. (2024). Involving the Body to Improve Letter Knowledge and Script: An Experimental Study in French Kindergarten. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09923-9

q4 · i1 · causal · r3

Ordinal regression on overall letter legibility (18-point rated copying task) in the RCT; arm-body students were "75% more likely to score higher on this skill", pencil-and-paper students 211% less likely; arm training was non-significant.

"The main effect on acquisitions in overall legibility was positive and significant at 5% for the arm-body training group (b = 0.560, OR = 1.751, d = .309, p = .020), negative and significant at 5% for the pencil-and-paper training group (b = -0.749, OR = 0.473, d = -.413, p < .001)"
