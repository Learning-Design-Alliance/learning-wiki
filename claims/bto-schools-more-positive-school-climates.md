### Voight 2013

Voight, A., Austin, G., and Hanson, T. (2013). A climate for academic success: How school climate distinguishes schools that are beating the achievement odds (Report Summary). San Francisco: WestEd. http://www.wested.org/cs/we/view/rs/1310

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of 1,715 California middle and high schools administering the California Healthy Kids Survey in 2008/09 and 2009/10, comparing School Climate Index scores of the 40 BTO schools with all other secondary schools. The report states "BTO schools had climate scores at the 82nd percentile, on average, whereas other secondary schools were at the 49th percentile, on average."
