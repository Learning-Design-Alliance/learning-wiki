## Evidence

### Laukkanen L 2022

Laukkanen L, Suhonen R, Löyttyniemi E, Leino-Kilpi H. (2022). The usability, feasibility and fidelity of the Ethics Quarter e-learning intervention for nurse managers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03241-w

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Baseline background factors (n=169) compared across three groups using Fisher's exact (categorical) and Kruskal-Wallis (continuous) tests. Age was the only statistically significant difference (all other p >.005).

> "NMs who completed the EQ and participated in measurements were statistically significantly older (p<0.005) than those who received the password, but did not sign in to the EQ and those who signed in, but did not participate in measurements."
