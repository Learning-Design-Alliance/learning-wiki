## Evidence

### Ommering 2021

Ommering, Belinda W. C., Van Blankenstein, Floris M., van Diepen, Merel, Gruis, Nelleke A., Kool, Ada, Dekker, Friedo W. (2021). The importance of motivation in selecting undergraduate medical students for extracurricular research programmes. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0260193

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on the same prospective cohort of 59 ERP students from university files. Drop-out odds were "significantly higher in the <7 group (OR = 3.82, 95% CI = 1.29–11.28)", adjusted OR = 4.25; 70% versus 38% dropped out per the tables.

> "The odds for ERP drop-out were significantly higher in the <7 group (OR = 3.82, 95% CI = 1.29–11.28), also after adjusting for gender and motivational baseline scores (OR = 4.25, 95% CI = 1.29–13.94)."
