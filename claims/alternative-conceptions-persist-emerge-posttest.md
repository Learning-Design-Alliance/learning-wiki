## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Eser Ültay & Nedim Alev 2017

Eser Ültay & Nedim Alev. (2017). Investigating the Effect of the Activities Based on Explanation Assisted REACT Strategy on Learning Impulse, Momentum and Collisions Topics. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.7. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137601

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Frequency analysis of Table 8 alternative conceptions plus interview excerpts. The article reports AC7 persisted despite being stressed by activities, and that AC18 and AC19's frequencies increased after the intervention.

> "It is seen that some alternative conceptions (i.e “The greater the mass, the greater the momentum” ) were not eliminated although they were stressed by the activities and experiments."

### Eser Ültay & Nedim Alev 2017 (2)

Eser Ültay & Nedim Alev. (2017). Investigating the Effect of the Activities Based on Explanation Assisted REACT Strategy on Learning Impulse, Momentum and Collisions Topics. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.7. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137601

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post- and delayed-test IMCCT coding in the experimental group revealed newly emerged conceptions. The authors interpret this as showing alternative conceptions interact within a thinking system and do not originate from one point.

> "The alternative conception of “Momentum is preserved all time” was emerged in the experimental group’s post and de layed tests"
