### Zhao Xin 2020

Zhao Xin, Wang Haien, Maes Joseph H. R. (2020). Training and transfer effects of extensive task-switching training in students. Psychological Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on mixing cost across 21 training sessions in the trained group; the Session main effect was significant (F(20,600)=10.36, p<0.001, ηp2=0.26 per Table 1) and "did not interact with CSI condition".

> "The effect of Session was also significant, but did not interact with CSI condition."
