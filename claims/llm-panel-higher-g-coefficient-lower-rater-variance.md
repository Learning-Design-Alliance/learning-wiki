Generalizability theory analysis using a fully crossed item × rater design compared the 5-LLM panel with the 10-judge faculty panel; the LLM panel showed higher G and Φ coefficients and substantially lower rater-related variance (2.64% vs 16.40%).

> "The LLM panel achieved a G coefficient of 0.823 and a Φ coefficient of 0.815 despite comprising only 5 models."
