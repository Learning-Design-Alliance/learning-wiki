### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026

Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin. (2026). Student background, admission routes, and academic success: a structural mediation analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09068-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM direct effects in Table 2: mother academic -0.031 (p = 0.543), father academic -0.035 (p = 0.496), financial support 0.065 (p = 0.238); no test detected a direct effect on M1 grade.

> "Parental background variables show little direct relevance for academic performance. Neither parental education nor financial support from the family is directly associated with study success."

### Tsikas Stefanos A.; Kieca Martin 2026 (2)
