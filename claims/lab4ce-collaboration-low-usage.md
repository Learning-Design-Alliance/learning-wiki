Usage analysis of the exploratory study's collaboration data across the four sessions. The authors attribute the low collaboration to individual report requirements, students' unfamiliarity with remote collaboration, and activities not advanced enough to require peer help.

> "Only 75 messages were posted on the public chat room of the experiment, most of them being without pedagogical interest. Only two students worked collaboratively on the same virtual machine and exchanged 7 posts within the matching private chat room."
