Simon Cullen, Judith Fan, Eva van der Brugge and Adam Elga (2018). Improving analytical reasoning and argument understanding: a quasi-experimental field study of argument visualization. npj Science of Learning 3:21. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0038-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline check on the equivalated LSAT pretest administered to both groups at semester start. The article reports means and a d = 0.27 pretest difference that was "nor statistically reliable"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Pretest scores were higher in the control group (M = 17.4) than in the seminar group ( M = 15.9), however this difference was neither large ( d = 0.27) nor statistically reliable, t(114) = 1.4, p = 0.152"
