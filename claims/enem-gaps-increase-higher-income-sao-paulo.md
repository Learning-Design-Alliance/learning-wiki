### Luis E C Rocha 2018

Luis E C Rocha, Luana de F Nascimento. (2018). Assessing student's achievement gap between ethnic groups in Brazil. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1801.10544)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the state-stratified ENEM 2014 observational analysis: the increasing-gap pattern at higher income was observed for Sao Paulo students, where "Yellow students score best in mathematics", while the pattern was not statistically significant for other regions.

> "Our results also indicate increasing achievement gaps between ethnic groups at higher household income levels for Sao Paulo students but these gaps are not statistically significant for other regions. In Sao Paulo, Yellow students score best in mathematics, followed by White, Pardo, Black and then Indigenous students."
