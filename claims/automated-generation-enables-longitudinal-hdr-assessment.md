## Evidence

### Satoshi Takahashi 2026

Satoshi Takahashi, Atsushi Yoshikawa, Megumi Kose, Kenichi Suzuki, Chieko Inoue, Yumi Watanabe, and Mari Sawada. (2026). Automating Constructive Assessment with Large Language Models: Toward Scalable and Repeated Evaluation of Practical Competence. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25790

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' discussion (Section 6.3) interpreting their Experiment 1 reproducibility results: traditional reuse of the same problem leads to memory dependence and answer reuse, making continuous evaluation of genuine ability difficult.

> "ChatGPT, as employed in this study, can generate multiple cases that are structurally equivalent but contextually different, enabling evaluation while suppressing item bias, even in re-measurement."
