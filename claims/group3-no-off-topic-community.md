`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of Group 3's coded ties across the full 42-minute episode (Figs. 2 and 3c). The group showed exclusively on-topic interaction; the authors interpret this as a sign the group may not be developing as a community, since both on-topic and off-topic practices support community building. No effect size is printed.

> "In fact, this group had no off-topic interactions amongst themselves during the entire activity (see Fig. 2), which suggests that this table may not be actively developing as a community."
