q3 · i? · associational · r3

Multigroup RI-CCLPM variants by gender and initial ability. The temporal structure was "highly consistent across low-, moderate-, and high-achieving students"; by gender, the French lagged self-concept effect was weaker among male than female adolescents, but allowing effects to vary across gender did not improve model fit.

"Multigroup analyses by initial ability level (Fig. S5, SM-E) likewise indicated that the temporal structure of reciprocal effects was highly consistent across low-, moderate-, and high-achieving students, providing no evidence that the observed fast-slow asymmetry depends on initial skill level."
