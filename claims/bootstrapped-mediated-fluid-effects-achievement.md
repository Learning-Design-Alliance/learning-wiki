### Carpentier Philippe 2022

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bootstrapping (1000 iterations) of direct and indirect paths in both samples (Tables 3–5). The article reports that "the total mediated estimates varied from .27 to .30 in QNTS" for visual-spatial skills; these are mediated path estimates, not printed standardized effect sizes, so no impact magnitude is coded.

> "Specifically, for cognitive visual-spatial skills, the total mediated estimates varied from .27 to .30 in QNTS, and from .19 to .26 in QLSCD; for cognitive flexibility, they varied from .07 to .10 in QNTS (only); and for short-term memory, from .09 to .10 in QNTS, and from .06 to .17 in QLSCD."
