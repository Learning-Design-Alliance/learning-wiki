The editorial's methodological-considerations section states this validity challenge, citing Richardson (2004) and Perry and Winne (2006). It is a conceptual assertion about questionnaire validity, offered without new data in this article.

> "The validity of a questionnaire 's data is especially challenged: learners may not be able to accurately report what they generally do or what they have done in finishing an assignment; or questionnaires may measure the learners ' perception rather than strategies actually performed"
