## Evidence

### Zeng Qin 2023

Zeng Qin, Wang Kai, Liu Wei-xin, Zeng Jiu-zhi, Li Xing-lan, Zhang Qing-feng, Ren Shang-qing, Xu Wen-ming. (2023). Efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in advanced life support training: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04654-x

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of the knowledge outcome by participant seniority (Supplement 3). Residents ("SMD = 0.28; 95% CI: 0.13–0.43, P = 0.0003") and third/fourth-year students benefited; first-year students showed no significant benefit (P = 0.18) with high heterogeneity (I2 = 85%).

> "The result show that residents (SMD = 0.28; 95% CI: 0.13–0.43, P = 0.0003, I2 = 0%) and three- and four-year medical/nurse students (SMD = 0.37; 95% CI: 0.04–0.69, P = 0.03, I2 = 77%) were benefit from HF simulation. However, fist-year medical/nurse students had no benefit from HF simulation (SMD = 0.66; 95% CI: − 0.31–1.64, P = 0.18, I2 = 85%)."

### Zeng Qin 2023 (2)

Zeng Qin, Wang Kai, Liu Wei-xin, Zeng Jiu-zhi, Li Xing-lan, Zhang Qing-feng, Ren Shang-qing, Xu Wen-ming. (2023). Efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in advanced life support training: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04654-x

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of skill performance by participant seniority (Supplement 5). "only residents benefit from HF simulation in skill (SMD = 0.55; 95% CI: 0.05–1.05, P = 0.03, I2 = 86%)".

> "Furthermore, a subgroup analysis depending on years of participation of the participant also been conducted and found that only residents benefit from HF simulation in skill (SMD = 0.55; 95% CI: 0.05–1.05, P = 0.03, I2 = 86%)."
