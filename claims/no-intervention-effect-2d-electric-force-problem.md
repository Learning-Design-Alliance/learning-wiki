## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). To Use or Not to Use Diagrams: The Effect of Drawing a Diagram in Solving Introductory Physics Problems. Originally published 2010. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.05467](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.05467)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the intervention groups on the two-dimensional three-charge equilateral-triangle problem, examining both quiz scores and percentages of expert-like diagrams. The article reports no statistically significant differences on either measure; equivalence was not tested.

> "For Problem 2, a two dimensional (2D) problem, there were no statistically significant differences between the different intervention groups, both in terms of scores and in terms of percentages of students drawing expert-like diagrams."
