`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's summary of questioning research: broadening asks "what if" scenarios beyond the current case, targeting directs questions to specific team members, and up-the-ladder asks the same question of progressively more advanced learners.

> "Three types of questioning have been found to be the most effective for learning: broadening, targeting, and up-the-ladder."
