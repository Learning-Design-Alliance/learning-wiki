## Evidence

### Lancaster 2019

Lancaster, J., & Bain, A. (2019). Designing University Courses to Improve Pre-Service Teachers' Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Evidence-Based Inclusive Practice. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 44(2). https://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol44/iss2/4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures analysis of the three quizzes (weeks 5, 7 and 13) in the same 40-participant comparison group study. The article reports the repeated measures ANOVA (F1, 38 = 46.90 p<.0001) and separate one-way ANOVAs per quiz, "in favor of the embedded design condition in each case".

> "In the applied experience condition mastery quiz scores ranged from 34-80%. In the embedded design condition all quizzes exceeded a predetermined mastery threshold of 80%, with scores ranging from 81-92.5%."
