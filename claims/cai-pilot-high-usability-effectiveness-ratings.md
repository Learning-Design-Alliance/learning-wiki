## Evidence

### García de Diego Laura 2015

García de Diego Laura, Cuervo Marta, Martínez J. Alfredo. (2015). Development of a Learning-Oriented Computer Assisted Instruction Designed to Improve Skills in the Clinical Assessment of the Nutritional Status: A Pilot Evaluation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0126345

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-phase pilot evaluation: a heuristic evaluation with 8 nutrition experts followed by a questionnaire study with 30 postgraduate students and graduates. Participants rated usability (90% yes), innovation (70%), diagnostic assistance (97.7%) and interface efficiency (96.7%), with the overall mean score of 8.28 ± 0.99 reported as printed.

> "According to participants, the overall assessment of the CAI on a scale ranging from 1 (lowest or poorest) to 10 (highest or best) is 8.28 ± 0.99"
