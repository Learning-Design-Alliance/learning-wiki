The review's Results section reports pretest‐adjusted effect sizes from the single included quasi‐experimental study: GPA, truancy, and alcohol use did not differ between RHS and comparison students, with all three confidence intervals including zero. Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "students in the RHS condition reported levels of grade point averages (= 0.26, 95% CI [‐0.04, 0.56]), truancy (= 0.01, 95% CI [‐0.29, 0.31]), and alcohol use (= 0.23, 95% CI [‐0.07, 0.53]) similar to participants in the comparison condition."
