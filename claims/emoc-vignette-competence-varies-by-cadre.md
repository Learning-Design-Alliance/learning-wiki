## Evidence

### Lohela Terhi Johanna 2016

Lohela Terhi Johanna, Nesbitt Robin Clark, Manu Alexander, Vesel Linda, Okyere Eunice, Kirkwood Betty, Gabrysch Sabine. (2016). Competence of health workers in emergency obstetric care: an assessment using clinical vignettes in Brong Ahafo region, Ghana. BMJ Open. [doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010963)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional health facility assessment of the most experienced delivery provider in all 64 delivery facilities in seven districts of Brong Ahafo, Ghana; median scores compared by cadre using Kruskal-Wallis tests. The abstract reports scores "differed significantly by respondent cadre" with doctors at 70%, midwives 55%, others 25% (p<0.001).

> "Vignette scores were moderate overall, and differed significantly by respondent cadre ranging from a median of 70% correct among doctors, via 55% among midwives, to 25% among other cadres such as health assistants and health extension workers (p<0.001)."
