Leanne Doughty and Marcos D. Caballero. (2014). Rubric Design for Separating the Roles of Open-Ended Assessments. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.014](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.014)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater reliability check of the CCMI differential-equation question grading rubric: a random sample of 25 student answers scored by three independent untrained graders. "The graders scores agreed for 24 of the 25 student answers" and Cohen's kappa was 0.95, described as 'almost perfect' agreement.

> "To check inter-rater reliability for the grading rubric, a random sample of 25 student answers were scored by three independent untrained graders and their scores were compared. The graders scores agreed for 24 of the 25 student answers. Cohen's kappa was calculated to be 0.95, which indicates 'almost perfect' agreement"
