# Media print exposure did not differ between the ER and no-ER groups at any point, though both groups changed over time

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Chi-square showed no statistical difference between the two groups in media reading (ads, e-mail, Internet, magazines, newspapers) at the three moments of the study. [→ Rodrigo 2014](#rodrigo-2014)
`q3 i?` A MANCOVA repeated measures analysis showed a significant change over time in media print exposure but no interaction between time and approach and no group or language difference. [→ Rodrigo 2014 (2)](#rodrigo-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Rodrigo 2014

Rodrigo, V., Greenberg, D., & Segal, D. (2014). Changes in reading habits by low literate adults through extensive reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 26(1), 73-91. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analyses of survey items on frequency of reading ads, e-mail, Internet, magazines, and newspapers (never/sometimes/frequently) at pre, post, and follow-up; no group or language differences were found. Magazines, newspapers, and advertisements were the most-read categories.

> "Chi Square did not show any statistical difference between the two groups at the three moments of the study (p  > .05) for any of the 'media' readings. Nor was there a difference when the language variable was considered."

### Rodrigo 2014 (2)

Rodrigo, V., Greenberg, D., & Segal, D. (2014). Changes in reading habits by low literate adults through extensive reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 26(1), 73-91. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA repeated measures on a composite media print exposure score with group as independent variable and language as covariate. The article reports F(1,102) = 8.512, p = .004 for time, with no time-by-approach interaction or group difference (Eta = .077, power = .824); no standardized effect size for the null contrasts is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "These figures show a significant change of the two groups over time, F(1,102) = 8.512, p = .004, but there was neither interaction between time and approach nor difference between the instructional groups or language groups, Eta = .077, power = .824."
