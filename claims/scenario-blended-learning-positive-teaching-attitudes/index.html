Evidence¶

Samsa 2010¶

Samsa, S., Akyüz, H.İ., Keser, H. and Numanoğlu, G. (2010). The effects of scenario based blended learning environment on attitudes of preservice technologies teachers toward teaching profession. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 11(2), Article 8. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Single-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 37 CEIT preservice teachers using the 34-item Üstüner (2006) attitude scale. Means rose from 80.54 to 88.27; the t-test showed the difference was "significantly different [t36=-2.55, p<.05]". No effect size is printed.

"From the t-test, having been performed in order to glance upon the significance of the difference (7.72) between the average scores prior to, and after the practice, the averages prior to, and after the practice have been found significantly different [t36=-2.55, p<.05]."
