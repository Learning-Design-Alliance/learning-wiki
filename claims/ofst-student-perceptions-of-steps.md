## Evidence

### Wang Ting-Huai 2025

Wang Ting-Huai, Xu Xiao-Dan, Long Zhi-Yuan, Cao Ying-Nan, Lai Ning-Yi, Ma Yong-Hong, Tan Xiao-Qiu, Xin Min, Jiang Jin-Yan, Yang Da-Ya. (2025). Exploring the integration of OFST and IPE in cross-regional physiology teaching-a novel approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07412-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported questionnaire from 68 voluntary respondents evaluating the teacher-guidance step of interprofessional OFST. The article reports 92.6% (63/68) perceived Step 1 as helping clarify learning direction; it separately reports 94.1% (64/68) found Step 2 helpful for self-regulated learning.

> "Survey data showed that 92.6% (63/68) of students perceived Step 1 as helping in clarifying their learning direction, enabling a smoother transition to the self-regulated learning phase"

### Wang Ting-Huai 2025 (2)

Wang Ting-Huai, Xu Xiao-Dan, Long Zhi-Yuan, Cao Ying-Nan, Lai Ning-Yi, Ma Yong-Hong, Tan Xiao-Qiu, Xin Min, Jiang Jin-Yan, Yang Da-Ya. (2025). Exploring the integration of OFST and IPE in cross-regional physiology teaching-a novel approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07412-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported questionnaire from the same 68 respondents evaluating the team learning and experience sharing steps. The article also reports 82.4% considered Step 4 helpful in broadening knowledge and inter-professional knowledge learning, and 98.5% (67/68) found OFST highly helpful for knowledge understanding.

> "A total of 88.2% (60/68) of the students recognised Step 3 could help improve students’ teamwork ability"
