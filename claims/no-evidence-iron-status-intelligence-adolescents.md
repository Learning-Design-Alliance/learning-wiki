## Evidence

### Samson 2022

Samson, K. L. I., Fischer, J. A. J., & Roche, M. L. (2022). Iron Status, Anemia, and Iron Interventions and Their Associations with Cognitive and Academic Performance in Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Nutrients, 14(1), 224. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14010224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis across 13 cross-sectional studies reporting intelligence measures and intervention studies; no evidence of influence on intelligence was found for status, intake, or any intervention type.

> "We found no evidence to indicate that iron status, anemia, nor dietary iron intake influenced intelligence from the included studies. Furthermore, there was no suggestion that iron supplementation during the adolescent years, with or without additional micronutrients, nor food-based interventions improved intelligence."
