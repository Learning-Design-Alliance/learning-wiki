`q2 i?` Post-scores are independent of pre-scores (coefficient 0.0±0.2, p = 0.96), normalized gain measures show robust negative pre-score dependence (coefficients −0.5 to −0.6), and math-test score (coefficient 0.13±0.04) predicts post-score better than pre-score (0.03±0.02). Regression analysis of 2018 McLEAP data excluding students with maximal pre-score, comparing math-test and pre-test predictors of post-score. The article reports the math test "is a better predictor of the post-score than pre-score".

> "Multivariate regression on the data ex- cluding students with the maximal pre-score shows that the score on the math test (coeﬃcient 0 .13± 0.04) is a better predictor of the post-score than pre-score (coeﬃ- cient 0.03± 0.02)."
