## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini 2025

Giulia Polverini, Jakob Melin, Elias Önerud, and Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT on tasks involving physics visual representations: the case of the Brief Electricity and Magnetism Assessment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010154](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010154)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative evaluation of both chatbots on all 31 BEMA items, each screenshot submitted 60 times to ChatGPT-4 and 30 times to ChatGPT-4o, scored 0/1 per response. The article reports the 6.3-point gap "when coding by meaning" and notes ChatGPT-4o outperformed on nearly half the items while ChatGPT-4 was significantly better on six.

> "Although the difference in both models’ performance on the test as a whole is only 6.3 percentage points, it signifies an advantage in ChatGPT-4o’s ability to handle tasks involving the interpretation of graphical physics representations."
