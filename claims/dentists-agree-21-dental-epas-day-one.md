`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey of 536 contacted dentists in the Rhine‐Main region; 120 responded (20.7%) and 100 questionnaires were evaluable. Respondents rated each of 21 EPA statements on a 6‐point Likert scale, and the article reports "substantial agreement on the EPAs chosen".

> "Overall, there was substantial agreement on the EPAs chosen to represent the tasks expected of young dentists on their first day in practice (Figure 2)."
