## Evidence

### Anderson Steven Mark 2025

Anderson Steven Mark, Gillanders Sarahlouise, Hickey Anne, Offiah Gozie, Davis Niall. (2025). Video Killed the Radio Star—A Meta‐Analysis on Video‐Based Coaching to Improve Surgical Skill. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1002/wjs.12494

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis comparing validated coaching models (e.g., GROW, modified PRACTICE) against unspecified models. Final scores were not significantly higher (SMD 0.46, p = 0.13), while change scores were significantly higher with an effect size of SMD 1.06 (95% CI 0.43, 1.68).

> "A further subgroup analysis was performed comparing the use of a previously validated coaching model for participants receiving VBC did not result in significantly higher mean final performance scores (SMD 0.46, 95% CI −0.13, 1.05, Z = 1.53, p = 0.13, and Supporting Information S1: Figure S3), though significantly higher mean change scores were seen (SMD 1.06, 95% CI 0.43, 1.68, Z = 3.32, and p < 0.0001"
