## Evidence

### Ghimire A and Singh C 2025

Ghimire A and Singh C. (2025). Peer Influence on Physics Self-Efficacy and Grades: A comparative study of students in an introductory calculus-based course who typically worked alone or in groups before and during the pandemic. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-group comparison across time periods shown in Fig. 2, contrasting COVID-era versus pre-COVID students within each working mode. The article reports COVID TWG students "ended up with higher grades, PISE, and physics self-efficacy than their pre-COVID peers," while TWA students differed significantly only in PISE.

> "For students who TWG, we found a significant difference. Those who collaborated during COVID ended up with higher grades, PISE, and physics self-efficacy than their pre-COVID peers. But for students who TWA, the only significant difference was in PISE with higher PISE among COVID students."
