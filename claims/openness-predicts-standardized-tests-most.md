### Nicolas Hübner 2022

Nicolas Hübner, Marion Spengler, Benjamin Nagengast, Lex Borghans, Trudie Schils, and Ulrich Trautwein. (2022). When Academic Achievement (Also) Reflects Personality: Using the Personality-Achievement Saturation Hypothesis (PASH) to Explain Differential Associations Between Achievement Measures and Personality Traits. American Psychological Association. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000571

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Aggregated adjusted regression for English across the three TOSCA studies; openness was "positively associated with standardized test performance (β = .20, p < .001)" and conscientiousness not significant (β = -.01, p = .289).

> "When predicting standardized achievement-test scores in English, we found that openness was positively associated with standardized test performance (β = .20, p < .001). Most interestingly, conscientiousness did not turn out to be statistically significantly related to standardized test scores (β = -.01, p = .289)."
