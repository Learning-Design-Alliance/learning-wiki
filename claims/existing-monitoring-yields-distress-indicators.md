## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Authorizers' existing formal and informal data collection activities provide ample opportunities to collect indicators of distress and identify struggling schools. [→ Aimee Evan 2021](#aimee-evan-2021)

## Evidence

### Aimee Evan 2021

Aimee Evan, Hannah Sullivan, and Laura Groth (2021). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools: Tools to Support Authorizer Data Collection. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616212

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Based on authorizer interviews conducted for the report's Part 1 predecessor publication, the toolkit reports that both formal activities (site visits, annual reports, audits, compliance reports) and informal ones (check-in calls with board chairs or leadership teams) yielded distress indicators. No effect size is reported.

> "While the scope, frequency, and nature of data collection by authorizers related to identifying indicators of distress varied by type and capacity of the authorizer, both formal and informal reviews provided authorizers with ample opportunities to collect indicators of distress and identify struggling schools."
