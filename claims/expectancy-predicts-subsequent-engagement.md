## Subclaims
`q3 i?` T3 expectancy positively predicted T4 engagement (β = .22, p = .001) over and above T2 engagement and T1 achievement. [→ Putwain 2018](#putwain-2018)

## Evidence

### Putwain 2018

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., Pekrun, R., Becker, S., & Symes, W. (2018). Expectancy of Success, Attainment Value, Engagement, and Achievement: A Moderated Mediation Analysis. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/key-stage-2-tests-2016-mathematics-test-materials

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation model of the five-wave longitudinal data from 586 Year 6 pupils. The path from T3 expectancy to T4 engagement was significant: "T4 engagement was predicted by T3 expectancy (β = .22, p = .001)". No effect size beyond the standardised coefficient is printed.

> "T4 engagement was predicted by T3 expectancy (β = .22, p = .001) and the interaction between T3 expectancy and T3 attainment value (β = -.28, p = .008) over and above the variance accounted for by T2 engagement (β = .46, p < .001) and T1 achievement (β = .21, p = .04)."
