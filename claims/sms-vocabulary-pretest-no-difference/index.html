Evidence¶

Haisen Zhang 2011¶

Haisen Zhang, Wei Song, Jack Burston. (2011). REEXAMINING THE EFFECTIVENESS OF VOCABULARY LEARNING VIA MOBILE PHONES. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ944968

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest using a 30-item TOEFL vocabulary multiple-choice section administered to both intact classes before treatment. The test showed "no significant difference" between groups; equivalence was not formally tested beyond this comparison.

"When the difference between the two groups was measured by a two-tailed independent-samples T test, there remained no significant difference in the vocabulary level of the two groups (t(60)=-1.01, p>.05), as was also evidenced by the TOFEL test."
