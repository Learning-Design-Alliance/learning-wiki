## Evidence

### Tovli 2014

Tovli, E. (2014). "The Joy of Reading" - An Intervention Program to Increase Reading Motivation for Pupils with Learning Disabilities. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 2(4). http://dx.doi.org/10.11114/jets.v2i4.496

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 108 second-grade special-education pupils. Quantity of reading was measured by three questions (books liked, books remembered, books recognised from a 10-book list), each showing significant differences (t=6.88, 9.97, 8.13; all p<.001), then combined into a total grade.

> "The table shows that quantity of reading in the research group (11.6) is significantly higher ( p<.001) than quantity of reading in the control group (4.75)."
