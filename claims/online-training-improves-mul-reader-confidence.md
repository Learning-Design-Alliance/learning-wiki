## Evidence

### Goedegebuure E.P. 2026

Goedegebuure E.P., Boellaard T.N., van Dijk-de Haan M.C., et al. (2026). Improving membranous urethral length measurements on prostate MRI: a comparison of online training methods. European Radiology 36(8):6419-6427. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-026-12468-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Confidence was rated per case (unconfident, equivocal, confident) on 20 test cases pre- and post-training for 81 participants. A group-based mixed model tested the change. Self-reported confidence on a 5-point Likert scale also improved in the questionnaire (69 respondents, 85% response rate).

> "Confidence scores recorded during MUL measurements showed a statistically significant increase across all groups following training (p < 0.001)."
