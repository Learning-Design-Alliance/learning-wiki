The game rules section specifies the scoring consequence of an invalid placement: 'the player who points out the mistake earns one point, and the player who made the mistake receives one negative point.' The same rule applies in both parts of the game. No empirical evaluation of this mechanic is reported.

> "the player who points out the mistake earns one point, and the player who made the mistake receives one negative point, after which the incorrectly placed cards are returned to the bottom of the corresponding piles."
