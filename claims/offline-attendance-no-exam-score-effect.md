## Evidence

### Zhi Fei-Hang 2025

Zhi Fei-Hang, Liu Wei, Zhang Xinyao, Zhang Tao, Su Yuhan, Huang Ruoxuan, Feng Yan-Fen, Lei Yi-Yan. (2025). Exploration of CBL combined with “Metronomic” online classroom in thoracic trauma and closed chest drainage teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of the seven students who missed the offline class with the 36 who attended, on all exam sections. No significant differences on any measure; attendee means were higher overall (Fig. 5A) but no effect size is printed.

> "The results showed no significant difference in exam scores between attendees and non-attendees (p = 0.84), including in basic knowledge mastery (p = 0.65) and case analysis performance (p = 0.76)."
