## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire given to 93 participants with 90 completed (96 percent return rate), using a 5-dimension, 32-item 5-point Likert instrument adapted from Selim (2003); these are descriptive percentages with no inferential test.

> "78.6 percent of the participants think the sharing mechanism positively facilitates pre-reading, is helpful for their learning at a social course by 75 percent, and makes their learning easier by 71.7 percent"

### Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011 (2)

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same questionnaire's ease-of-use dimension (Cronbach alpha .766); the article reports 70 percent found VPEN easy to use, and attributes frustration to the VPEN Session protection mechanism causing lost annotations.

> "70 percent of participants think that the VPEN tool is easy to use. They particularly believe that they could easily find useful information in classmates’ annotations by 63.3 percent."
