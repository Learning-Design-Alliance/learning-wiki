Summary of difficulty patterns from large-scale administration of the validated instrument at six institutions (per-item data in Table 4, topic-level scores in Figures 3-6). The article lists "difﬁculty reasoning with multiple quantities simultaneously" and constraint-application difficulties among those displayed.

> "Some of the conceptual difﬁculties displayed on the survey instrument include difﬁculty reasoning with multiple quantities simultaneously, difﬁculty in systematically applying various constraints (for an isothermal, adiabatic, isochoric, reversible, or irreversible process, isolated system, etc.), difﬁculty due to oversimpliﬁcation of the ﬁrst law and overgeneralization of the second law."
