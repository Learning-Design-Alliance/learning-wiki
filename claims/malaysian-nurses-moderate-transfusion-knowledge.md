## Evidence

### Mohd Noor Noor Haslina 2021

Mohd Noor Noor Haslina, Saad Noor Hafiza, Khan Mohammad, Hassan Mohd Nazri, Ramli Marini, Bahar Rosnah, Mohamed Yusoff Shafini, Iberahim Salfarina, Wan Ab Rahman Wan Suriana, Zulkafli Zefarina, Islam Md Asiful. (2021). Blood Transfusion Knowledge among Nurses in Malaysia: A University Hospital Experience. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182111194

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 200 registered nurses at Hospital Universiti Sains Malaysia using the modified RBTKQ; the article reports the overall knowledge score was "estimated to be 54.9% ± 7.6%", classified as moderate under the <50%/50–74%/≥75% scoring bands.

> "Based on the scoring system, the overall knowledge of blood transfusion among Malaysian nurses was estimated to be 54.9% ± 7.6%, which was considered as a moderate level of knowledge."
