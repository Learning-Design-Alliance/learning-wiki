Evidence¶

Ghanizadeh A 2011¶

Ghanizadeh A. (2011). Sensory Processing Problems in Children with ADHD, a Systematic Review. Psychiatry Investig 2011;8:89-94. https://doi.org/10.4306/pi.2011.8.2.89

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that "co-morbidity with oppositional defiant disorder and anxiety are predictors of more severe sensory processing problems in children with ADHD". Children with sensory over-responsivity had higher anxiety than ADHD children without it and non-ADHD children.

"However, co-morbidity with oppositional defiant disorder and anxiety are predictors of more severe sensory processing problems in children with ADHD."
