## Evidence

### George Leckie and Lucy Prior 2022

George Leckie and Lucy Prior. (2022). A Comparison of Value-Added Models for School Accountability. [doi:10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763](https://doi.org/10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Census-based analysis of 2019 Progress 8 data (502,899 students, 3,196 schools) comparing five conventionally fitted linear regression models. The article reports the school effect variance "reduces substantially across the five models (0.226, 0.069, 0.043, 0.039, 0.032)" and that moving Raw to VA sees "by far the biggest decrease in variance from 0.226 to 0.069".

> "The variance and therefore magnitude of the school effects reduces substantially across the five models (0.226, 0.069, 0.043, 0.039, 0.032) as more adjustments are made for factors argued beyond the control of the school."
