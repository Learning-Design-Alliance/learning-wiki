## Evidence

### Guillaume Lagubeau 2020

Guillaume Lagubeau, Silvia Tecpan, and Carla Hernandez. (2020). Active Learning reduces academic risk of students with non- formal reasoning skills. Evidence from an introductory physics massive course in a Chilean public university. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.023101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.023101)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pilot comparing 4 active-learning sections with 4 traditional control sections in a massive introductory physics course (304 students attending all evaluations). The active group's failure rate was "36.1%", a "9.1% improvement" with p(H0) = 0.937 via a Bayesian cut-efficiency analysis.

> "By contrast, active learning students failing rate was 36.1%, evidencing a 9.1% improvement ( p(H0) = 0 .937) consistent with improvement reported in literature for physics and STEM [5]."
