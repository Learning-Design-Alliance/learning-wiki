### Kamel Senosy AM 2026

Kamel Senosy AM, Sallam G, Abdel Fattah HA, El Sharkawi SS, Ahmed ZA, Maki S, Zafar I, Al Awar S, Magzoub ME. (2026). Relation of Anxiety Levels and Objective Structured Clinical Examination among Undergraduate Nursing Students. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.173432.1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A descriptive cross-sectional online survey of 121 second-year nursing students at one Egyptian university, administered three days before the OSCE, found "58.7% of the participants reported a mild degree of anxiety". Percentages are descriptive; no effect size or inferential test is printed for this distribution.

> "Before attending the OSCE, 58.7% of the participants reported a mild degree of anxiety, 33.1% had a moderate level of anxiety, and only 10.7% had a severe level of trait anxiety."
