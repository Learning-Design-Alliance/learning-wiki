### Ličen Sabina 2025

Ličen Sabina, Prosen Mirko. (2025). Disaster Nursing Competencies in a Time of Global Conflicts and Climate Crises: A Cross‐Sectional Survey Study. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the Sl-DNCC barriers subscale (8 items, range 8–56) in the same survey of 379 Slovenian nurses. Barriers mentioned by the article include "inadequate training, limited resources and insufficient organisational support".

> "the median score for barriers to developing core competencies (M = 48) was significantly higher than the hypothesised median score of 32 (Z = 15.029, p = 0.000), indicating the significant challenges nurses face in developing these competencies"
