## Evidence

### Stieglitz Verena 2023

Stieglitz Verena, Buggenhagen Holger, Strelow Kai-Uwe, Schmidtmann Irene, Skala Christine, Kurz Sandra. (2023). Action competence in obstetric emergencies—can this be achieved via e-learning? Interprofessional blended learning module on diagnostics and emergency treatment of shoulder dystocia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04335-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney-U-test at α = 0.05 comparing medical students with and without prior vocational education on the simulation score. The article reports "no significant group difference: p = 0.602"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The medical students who had completed a vocational education beforehand scored a mean of 38.9 points (SD 5.86), while the others scored 38.2 points (SD 6.71). Again, there was no significant group difference: p = 0.602 (α = 0.05)."

### Stieglitz Verena 2023 (2)

Stieglitz Verena, Buggenhagen Holger, Strelow Kai-Uwe, Schmidtmann Irene, Skala Christine, Kurz Sandra. (2023). Action competence in obstetric emergencies—can this be achieved via e-learning? Interprofessional blended learning module on diagnostics and emergency treatment of shoulder dystocia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04335-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney-U-test at α = 0.05 comparing medical students with and without previous experience dealing with obstetric emergencies. The article reports "no significant group difference: p = 0.687"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The medical students who had experienced obstetric emergencies beforehand scored a mean of 38.5 points (SD 5.78), whereas the students with no previous experience in this field scored 38.2 points (SD 6.82). Similarly, there was no significant group difference: p = 0.687 (α = 0.05)."

### Stieglitz Verena 2023 (3)

Stieglitz Verena, Buggenhagen Holger, Strelow Kai-Uwe, Schmidtmann Irene, Skala Christine, Kurz Sandra. (2023). Action competence in obstetric emergencies—can this be achieved via e-learning? Interprofessional blended learning module on diagnostics and emergency treatment of shoulder dystocia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04335-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney-U-test at α = 0.05 comparing male and female participants on the simulation score. The article reports "no significant group difference was found: p = 0.683"; equivalence was not tested.

> "When evaluating a possible influence of the study participants' gender, male participants scored a mean of 38 points (SD 6.9), while females scored 38.8 points (SD 5.9). Again, no significant group difference was found: p = 0.683 (α = 0.05)."
