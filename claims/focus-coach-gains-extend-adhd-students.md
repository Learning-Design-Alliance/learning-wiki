### Michael D. Mrazek 2026

Michael D. Mrazek, Dustyn S. Ransom, Amy R. Texter, Calvin V. Tower, Bryce A. Rutila, Thomas F. Kennedy, Elijah J. Woodson, Jonathan W. Schooler, Alissa J. Mrazek. (2026). Theory-driven design and validation of a tool to enhance academic focus. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10730-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 2 sensitivity analysis restricted to students with an ADHD diagnosis (n = 87) found the across-session effect significant, b = 0.04, p = .008, while the within-session effect was not significant, b = 0.01, p = .36. No effect size was printed for either slope.

> "A third sensitivity analysis confirmed that the across-session effect remained significant when restricting the sample to the 87 students diagnosed with attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder, b = 0.04, SE = 0.01, t (400.9) = 2.68, p = .008."
