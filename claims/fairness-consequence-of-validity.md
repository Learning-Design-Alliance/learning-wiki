## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Once an external criterion is fixed, fairness consists in applying it identically to all students, making fairness follow from validity. [→ Mathieu Bouville 2008](#mathieu-bouville-2008)

## Evidence

### Mathieu Bouville 2008

Mathieu Bouville. (2008). Exam fairness. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4235 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Concluding argument of the Fairness and Beyond section: the author reports that a purely relative concept of fairness cannot yield concrete decisions, so an external reference such as better engineers had to be introduced, reframing fairness as uniform application of that reference.

> "Fairness is then the way this external criterion is applied: it must be the same for all students — this is not a direct comparison of students. Moreover, this means that fairness is a necessary consequence of the validity of the exams, rather than a separate criterion."
