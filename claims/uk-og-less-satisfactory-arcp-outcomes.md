## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In univariable analysis, UK OGs showed the largest difference from UK graduates in odds of a less satisfactory ARCP outcome (OR 2.36, 95% CI 2.12 to 2.62), and also higher odds than EEA graduates (OR 1.29) and IMGs (OR 1.20). [→ Tiffin Paul A 2018](#tiffin-paul-a-2018)

## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2018

Tiffin Paul A, Orr James, Paton Lewis W, Smith Daniel T, Norcini John J. (2018). UK nationals who received their medical degrees abroad: selection into, and subsequent performance in postgraduate training: a national data linkage study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023060

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariable multilevel ordinal logistic regression on 99,293 ARCP outcomes for 34,755 doctors (table 6). UK OGs vs UKG: "OR 2.36 (2.12 to 2.62)" including exam failures; vs EEAG OR 1.29; vs IMG OR 1.20. Descriptively, 36.77% of UK OG ARCPs were unsatisfactory versus 24.28% for UK graduates.

> "graduate group was associated with an increased odds of receiving a less satisfactory ARCP outcome at review, with UK OGs showing the largest difference with UKGs as the comparison category"
