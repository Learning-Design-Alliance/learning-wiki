# Emerging and high implementation were associated with higher secondary students' cultural connectedness

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Secondary students at emerging implementation schools scored 0.81 points higher, and at high implementation schools 1.43 points higher, on the SCCS cultural connectedness scale than students at limited implementation schools. [→ Boal 2025](#boal-2025)

## Evidence

### Boal 2025

Boal, A., McCullough, S., & Chin, A. (2025). Exploring implementation of trauma-engaged practices in Alaska schools (REL 2025–011). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Northwest. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/rel/Products/RWR/Publication/108318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects multilevel regression of spring 2022 SCCS secondary student data found cultural connectedness scores significantly higher in emerging and high implementation schools than limited implementation schools, with printed differences of 0.81 and 1.43 points. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Attending a school with emerging implementation was significantly associated with scores on the cultural connectedness scale that were 0.81 point higher than scores at schools with limited implementation. Attending a school with high implementation was significantly associated with scores that were 1.43 points higher than scores at schools with limited implementation."
