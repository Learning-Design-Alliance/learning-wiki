## Evidence

### Fein and Jones 2015

Lance Fein, Ed.D. & Don Jones, Ed.D. (2015). Using Emergence Theory-Based Curriculum to Teach Compromise Skills to Students with Autistic Spectrum Disorders. Administrative Issues Journal: Connecting Education, Practice, and Research, 5(1), 35-54. https://doi.org/10.5929/2015.5.1.4

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Between-subjects ANOVA performed as part of the repeated-measures analysis, comparing average pretest-posttest TOPS 2 scores per participant across the control and treatment groups. The printed partial η was .149, a large effect, though the test did not account for groupings.

> "These results were significant, F(2, 52) = 9.107, p = .004. Although this test did not account for groupings, it did indicate a significant difference between the average pretesting and posttesting score totals of the control group per participant and treatment group per participant."
