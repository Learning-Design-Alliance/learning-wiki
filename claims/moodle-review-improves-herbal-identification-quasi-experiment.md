## Evidence

### Li Lei 2020

Li Lei, Tam Chi Wing, Wang Ning, Cheung Fan, Zhou Qing, Zhang Cheng, Cheng Chien-shan, Xiong Lei, Feng Yibin. (2020). Effectiveness of blending E-learning with field trip on Chinese herbal medicine education: quasi-experimental study. BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-020-03034-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective quasi-experiment: 49 year-2 BChinMed students, 5-year cohort (n=26) had Moodle access to the 32 Group A herbs, 6-year cohort (n=23) served as active control. Score out of 64: "significantly higher in the 5-year cohort (29.65 ± 5.0) than in the 6-year cohort (21.65 ± 6.5)".

> "For this group of plants, we found that the average score was significantly higher in the 5-year cohort (29.65 ± 5.0) than in the 6-year cohort (21.65 ± 6.5) (P < 0.01)"

### Li Lei 2020 (2)

Li Lei, Tam Chi Wing, Wang Ning, Cheung Fan, Zhou Qing, Zhang Cheng, Cheng Chien-shan, Xiong Lei, Feng Yibin. (2020). Effectiveness of blending E-learning with field trip on Chinese herbal medicine education: quasi-experimental study. BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-020-03034-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same crossover quasi-experiment, 6-day Moodle self-study, fill-in-the-blank assessment via Moodle. For the 32 Group B herbs the "average score was significantly higher in the 6-year cohort (28.68 ± 4.7)" than the control 5-year cohort.

> "We found that the average score was significantly higher in the 6-year cohort (28.68 ± 4.7) than in the 5-year cohort (24.26 ± 7.7) (P < 0.01)"
