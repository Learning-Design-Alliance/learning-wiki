Theoretical/interpretive section citing the disparities literature, not a study by these authors. The article argues pattern recognition "may involve stereotypes or cognitive shortcuts" and that "Bias is often unconscious and may differ from consciously expressed views," illustrated by a case in which a student attributed asthma exacerbations to cocaine use.

> "Cognitive shortcuts involving race, ethnicity or gender may contribute to health care disparities and prevent physicians from treating diverse patient populations effectively, particularly when these shortcuts are combined with bias, defined as unjustifiable negative beliefs about others."
