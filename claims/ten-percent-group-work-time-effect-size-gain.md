### Weir Laura K. 2019

Weir Laura K., Barker Megan K., McDonnell Lisa M., Schimpf Natalie G., Rodela Tamara M., Schulte Patricia M. (2019). Small changes, big gains: A curriculum-wide study of teaching practices and student learning in undergraduate biology. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0220900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-averaged regression estimate from the 31-section observational analysis; the article reports the correlation as descriptive of the fitted model, noting effect sizes above 0.20 are of interest in educational policy decisions.

> "In our averaged model, a 10% increase in group work time (five minutes in a 50-minute class) correlates with a 0.30 increase in effect size—roughly a 3% improvement in student performance (almost one letter grade, depending on the institution), holding other variables constant."
