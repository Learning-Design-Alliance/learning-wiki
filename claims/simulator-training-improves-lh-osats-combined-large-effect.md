## Evidence

### Jokinen Ewa 2020

Jokinen Ewa, Mikkola Tomi S., Härkki Päivi. (2020). Simulator training and residents’ first laparoscopic hysterectomy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-07270-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with blinded video assessment of first live laparoscopic hysterectomies. The LH-OSATS difference was significant at "p = 0.012, 95% CI 1.02–7.05"; no effect size is printed for this outcome alone.

> "In LH-OSATS, the mean score was 20.0 (SD 3.3) in the intervention group and 16.0 (SD 2.8) in the control group (p = 0.012, 95% CI 1.02–7.05)"

### Jokinen Ewa 2020 (2)

Jokinen Ewa, Mikkola Tomi S., Härkki Päivi. (2020). Simulator training and residents’ first laparoscopic hysterectomy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-07270-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with blinded video assessment; the combined OSATS comparison gave a printed effect size of Cohen's d 1.83, meaning a large effect, with means 37.0 vs 27.5 and p = 0.002, as the article states "giving Cohen’s d 1.83, meaning a large effect".

> "When combining both OSATS forms, the mean score was 37.0 (SD 6.2) in the intervention group and 27.5 (SD 5.2) in the control group (p = 0.002, 95% CI 3.96–15.12), giving Cohen’s d 1.83, meaning a large effect."
