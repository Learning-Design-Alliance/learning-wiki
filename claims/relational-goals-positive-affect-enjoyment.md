`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis finds relational goals, conceptualized solely as approach goals, are "positively linked to positive affect and enjoyment," consistent with expectations for approach-oriented social goals.

> "Relational goals are positively linked to positive affect and enjoyment."

### Tanja Bross 2024 (2)

Tanja Bross, Ulrike Elisabeth Nett, & Martin Daumiller. (2024). Interrelations Among Achievement Goals and Achievement Emotions: A Meta-Analytic Examination. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09931-9

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The article reports, citing primary studies, that relational goals correlate with enjoyment (r = [.55; .74]), pride (r = .10) and shame (r = .12), indicating links with both positive and negative emotions.

> "Studies have shown small to large associations with positive emotions such as enjoyment ( r = [.55; .74]) (Wallhead et al., 2013) and pride ( r = .10) (Rinas et al., 2020), as well as small associations with shame (r = .12) (Rinas et al., 2020)."
