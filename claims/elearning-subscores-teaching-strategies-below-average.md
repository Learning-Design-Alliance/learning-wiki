## Evidence

### Zarifsanaiey N 2024

Zarifsanaiey N, Farrokhi MR, Karimian Z, Hoseini S, Chahartangi F, Shahraki HR. (2024). Lesson learned from assessing teachers’ and students’ perspectives regarding the quality of e-learning in medical education during the pandemic: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05160-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional descriptive study of 400 SUMS students using a validated 27-item questionnaire with a test value of 2.50, analyzed with SPSS-21. Table 3 reports teaching-learning strategies at 2.47 ± 0.67 (P = 0.36 vs test value) and course structure at 2.90 ± 0.66 (P < 0.001), showing "the teaching-learning strategies subscale mean score was lower than the average level".

> "Our findings revealed that the student support system, the course structure, and the infrastructure and technology subscales’ mean scores were significantly higher than the average level However, the teaching-learning strategies subscale mean score was lower than the average level"
