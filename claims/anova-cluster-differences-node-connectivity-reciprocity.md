## Evidence

### Pingjing Yang 2025

Pingjing Yang, Jennifer Cromley, and Jana Diesner. (2025). More Expert-like Eye Gaze Movement Patterns are Related to Better X-ray Reading. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98414-3_28](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98414-3_28)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA comparing mean BFD scores across time-series clusters for eleven network metrics (Table 1). Only node connectivity and reciprocity reached significance; Cluster 1 showed a higher BFD for node connectivity and Cluster 2 for reciprocity. No effect size is printed.

> "Significant differences were observed for node connectivity (F = 4.205, p = 0.015) and reciprocity (F = 4.462, p = 0.035), indicating that students with better performance may exhibit more sophisticated visual exploration strategies and deeper engagement with the task."
