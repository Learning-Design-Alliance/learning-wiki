Authors' interpretive claim (type e) in the working definitions section, citing Evans and Taylor (2025b). The guide describes the post-2020 proliferation of terms as productive but risky, and points to Badrinarayan et al. (2025) as a synthesis resource.

"it risks contributing to the “jingle-jangle fallacy” (Kelley, 1927) in educational assessment where similar concepts are given different names (jangle), or different concepts are given the same name (jingle)."
