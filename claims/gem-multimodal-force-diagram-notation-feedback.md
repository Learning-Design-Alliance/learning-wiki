Eugenio Tufino, Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Creating a customisable Socratic AI physics tutor. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae0d23](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae0d23)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Illustrative use case 1: a dynamics problem (72-kg stunt performer) in which a student uploads a hand-drawn force diagram using simplified labels (W, F A). The Gem, referencing its Knowledge file, prompts label revision to the two-subscript convention (F EonS, F AonS) and validates the corrected diagram.

> "The Gem analyzes the image and, referencing the rules in its "Knowledge" file, initiates a dialogue to guide the student. It first praises the student for identifying the correct interactions, then prompts them to reconsider the labels based on the provided guide."
