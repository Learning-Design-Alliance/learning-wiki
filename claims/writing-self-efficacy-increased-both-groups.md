## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The control group's writing self-efficacy rose from 3.30 to 3.81, a statistically significant increase. [→ Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018](#altunkaya-and-topuzkanamis-2018)

## Evidence

### Altunkaya and Topuzkanamis 2018

Hatice Altunkaya, Ersoy Topuzkanamış. (2018). The Effect of Using Facebook in Writing Education on Writing Achievement, Attitude, Anxiety and Self-efficacy Perception. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2133-2142. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061010

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dependent samples t-test on the same scale for the control group (n=48) receiving paper-based instruction. The article reports a significant difference and concludes self-efficacy improved in the control group too.

> "the control group writing self-efficacy pre-test mean score was 3.30, and the posttest mean score was 3.81. It was observed that there was a significant difference between these two scores [t(47) = 8.850; p < .05]"
