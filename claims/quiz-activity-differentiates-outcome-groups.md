Bonferroni post hoc comparisons of quiz task engagement among the three outcome groups. The article reports Mastering exceeded Passing (p = 0.004) and Non-passing on quiz views, with no Passing–Non-passing difference; similar patterns held for submissions, repetitions, and scores.
