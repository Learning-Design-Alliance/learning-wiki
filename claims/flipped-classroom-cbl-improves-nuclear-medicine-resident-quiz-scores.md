## Evidence

### Ji Y 2026

Ji Y, Zhang X, Han X, Xiong G, Chen X, Lu Y, Zhang Q, Sui S, Yang Z, Yu S, Xu W, Zheng G, Zhang W, Liu Y, Xin J. (2026). Enhancing nuclear medicine residency standardization training: a comprehensive exploration of integrating a flipped classroom and case-based learning for effective instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09808-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective single-group pre/post analysis of 40 nuclear medicine residents using a 20-question knowledge test. The median correct response rate rose from 45.00% to 69.45%, with a significant difference (P = 0.0265). No control group was included, so improvements cannot be definitively attributed to the intervention.

> "After the class, 36 participants (90%, 36/40) took the knowledge test, resulting in an average score of 14.5 ± 3.989 points. The median correct response rate rose to 69.45%. A significant difference in correct response rates between pre- and post-test was observed (P = 0.0265)"
