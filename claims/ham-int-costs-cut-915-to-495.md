## Evidence

### Hissbach Johanna C 2014

Hissbach Johanna C, Sehner Susanne, Harendza Sigrid, Hampe Wolfgang. (2014). Cutting costs of multiple mini-interviews – changes in reliability and efficiency of the Hamburg medical school admission test between two applications. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-54

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Detailed cost accounting of the 2009 and 2010 HAM-Int procedures (Tables 6 and 7), itemizing scientist, admin, actor and student hours. The article reports "The total costs per candidate were cut to $495 in 2010", with station development costs almost halved.

> "The total costs were roughly $73,100 plus $10,150 additional costs for the first implementation, i.e. $1040 per candidate ($915 without additional costs). The total costs per candidate were cut to $495 in 2010. The largest reduction of costs was related to station development costs which were almost halved from $36,600 to $18,900."
