q2 · i? · associational · r2

Interpretive statement accompanying Figures 1-2 (third grade/nine-year-olds, mean grade equivalents 1990-1998). The text reports the adjustment raises third grade scores by roughly one month in reading and half a month in math, with adjusted 1998 above 1997 in both subjects.

"In both reading and math, the adjusted scores are higher than the unadjusted scores. Removing the retained students increases the scores by approximately one month (in terms of mean grade equivalent) in reading and one-half month in math."

John Q. Easton 1998 (2)¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Interpretive statement accompanying Figures 3-4 (fourth grade/ten-year-olds). Adding predicted scores of retained third graders lowers fourth grade adjusted 1998 scores, which are lower than 1997 in reading and about the same in math.

"Adjusted 1998 scores are lower than the unadjusted scores by about two months in reading and one and one-half months in math, since the weaker retained students are added in here."
