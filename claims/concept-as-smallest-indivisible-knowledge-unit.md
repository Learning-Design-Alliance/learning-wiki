# The proposed system's content model treats concepts as the smallest indivisible parts of a topic that are transferred to the knowledge base for relocation

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The proposal defines a concept as the smallest part of a topic that cannot be divided further, equivalent to a physical-class lesson, and transfers concepts to the knowledge base so the system can relocate or remove parts for a learner.
