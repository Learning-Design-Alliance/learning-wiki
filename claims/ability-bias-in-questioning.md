### Koomen 2016

Michele Hollingsworth Koomen. (2016). Practitioner Inquiry with Early Program Teacher Candidates. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i11.1915

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Mohammed and Jennifer studied flexible ability grouping in a 6th grade classroom. Despite teaching the same lessons to both sections, they called on higher-ability students significantly more in math (X2=7.8, df=1, p<0.0051) and science (X2=48.761, df=1, p<0.0001).

> "Their research revealed that across both their math and science lessons they called on more higher ability students in math (X2=7.8, df=1, p <0.0051) and also in science (X2= 48.761, df=1, p <0.0001)."
