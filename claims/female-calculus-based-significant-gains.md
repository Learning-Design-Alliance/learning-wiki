Pre/post modified CLASS comparison by section type and gender in a remote physics laboratory. "Female students in the calculus-based course had signiﬁcantly ( p <0.05) more expert-like beliefs at the end of the semester" with ⟨c⟩ = 0.25; algebra-based females had ⟨c⟩ = −0.01. No effect size is printed for these contrasts.

> "Female students in the calculus-based course had signiﬁcantly ( p <0.05) more expert-like beliefs at the end of the semester compared to the beginning, with a ﬁnal self-eﬃcacy score of 10.9 ± 0.9 and a positive normalized change of 0.25. On the other hand, female students in the algebra-based sections were the only group to report negative normalized change (see Table III)."
