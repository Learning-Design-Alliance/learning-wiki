National Charter School Resource Center 2021¶

National Charter School Resource Center, Evan, A., & Sullivan, H. (2021). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools, Part 2: The Roles and Perspectives of Charter School Leaders and Board Members. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616211

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview evidence on culture-domain indicators. The report describes "a negative or toxic staff culture, including a lack of what research calls 'collective teacher efficacy'," as one of the first signs of a struggling school, plus weak family and community ties.

"Many school leaders and board members also discussed the lack of consistent disciplinary policies contributing to a chaotic student culture."
