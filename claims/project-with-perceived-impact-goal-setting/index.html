Nakamoto 2025¶

Nakamoto, J., Carter, C., & Russo, S. (2025). Implementation and impact of the Project With curriculum in juvenile justice facilities and group homes. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673580

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of exit-survey impact items (n = 173) in which youth rated on a 1 (much less likely) to 5 (much more likely) scale whether the program made them more likely to engage in behaviors. "81 percent of Project With participants reported that the curriculum made them" more likely to make plans to reach their goals; 75 percent said the same for caring about doing well in school.

"81 percent of Project With participants reported that the curriculum made them “much more likely” or “somewhat more likely” to make plans to reach their goals."
