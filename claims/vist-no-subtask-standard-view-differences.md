## Evidence

### Eimer Christine 2020

Eimer Christine, Duschek Max, Jung Andreas Emanuel, Zick Günther, Caliebe Amke, Lindner Matthias, Weiler Norbert, Elke Gunnar. (2020). Video-based, student tutor- versus faculty staff-led ultrasound course for medical students – a prospective randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02431-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis of the same randomized OSCE data (77 sheets), stratified by the seven AU and TTE standard views; all subtask comparisons reported as n.s. in Table 3. No effect sizes printed.

> "A stratified analysis for the different distribution of standard views examined in the OSCE showed no significant different means between the two groups."

### Eimer Christine 2020 (2)

Eimer Christine, Duschek Max, Jung Andreas Emanuel, Zick Günther, Caliebe Amke, Lindner Matthias, Weiler Norbert, Elke Gunnar. (2020). Video-based, student tutor- versus faculty staff-led ultrasound course for medical students – a prospective randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02431-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-factorial ANOVA with group (ViST vs FS) and standard view as nominal covariate with seven categories; the omnibus model showed no significant covariate influence or interaction. No effect size printed.

> "This covariate neither showed a significant influence nor significant interaction."
