## Evidence

### Briley L. Lewis 2023

Briley L. Lewis, K. Supriya, Graham H. Read, Kaitlin L. Ingraham Dixie, Rachel Kennison, and Anthony R. Friscia. (2023). Eﬀects of Popular Science Writing Instruction on General Education Student Attitudes Towards Science: A Case Study in Astronomy. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.32374/aej.2023.3.1.049ra](https://doi.org/10.32374/aej.2023.3.1.049ra)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coded pre/post responses to "Do you think you could write about a new scientiﬁc discovery?" in the writing-intensive GE astronomy course; pre-course students hedged with "maybe" and "with help" while post-course responses showed more confidence. Counts as printed; no effect size.

> "9 students felt more conﬁdent about writing a new scientiﬁc discovery, 1 remained neutral, and 4 entered with conﬁdence in their abilities. There is some evidence of additional understanding of diﬀerent modes/audiences of scientiﬁc writing, and some students said their conﬁdence level depended on the audience."
