## Evidence

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of Pre-Test scores (out of ten) among 43 distance-learning students, randomly divided into three scheduling groups; within each group, students chose whether to prepare with OnLabs and a tutor-led Skype session, so the With-OnLabs and Without-OnLabs groups were self-selected. Scores were analyzed with Classical Test Theory and shown in Fig. 4. The authors observe that "With-OnLabs students had better scores on their Pre -Tests, in all three groups". No statistical test or effect size is reported for this contrast.

> "Depending on Fig. 4, our first general observation is that the With-OnLabs students had better scores on their Pre -Tests, in all three groups , regardless of the test difficulty."
