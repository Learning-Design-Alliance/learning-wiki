# Students taught a week about discussions scored significantly higher on the first exam than control students, with the advantage gone by the second exam

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The experimental class outperformed the control class on the first exam (M = 85.22 vs 77.65) but performed similarly on the second exam, shown by a significant semester × exam interaction. [→ Brank 2013](#brank-2013)

## Evidence

### Brank 2013

Brank, E. and Wylie, L. (2013). Let's discuss: Teaching students about discussions. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 13, No. 3, August 2013, pp. 23–32. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1017045

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-semester quasi-experiment (fall control n = 34, spring experimental n = 42) with repeated measures GLM on two exams; the semester × exam interaction was significant, F(1, 73) = 15.10, p<.01, η2 = .17, with the quoted first-exam means. "both classes performed similarly on the second exam" (Experimental M = 87.00; Control M = 88.18).

> "Students did significantly better on the first exam in the experimental class (M = 85.22, SD = 10.12) as compared to the control class (M = 77.65, SD = 12.73), but both classes performed similarly on the second exam"
