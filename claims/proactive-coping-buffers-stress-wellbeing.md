## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The perceived stress × proactive coping interaction is significant (B = 0.126, p = .001); the stress–well-being link is significant and negative when proactive coping is high but not when it is low. [→ Ma Chenwei 2025](#ma-chenwei-2025)

## Evidence

### Ma Chenwei 2025

Ma Chenwei, Li Jiangyu. (2025). Self-transitioning or other-transitioning? The positive effects of LGBTQ + college students' positive coping and perceived academic support. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07204-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped moderation analysis (2,000 resamples, 95% CIs) within the SEM of 359 Chinese LGBTQ+ college students. At high proactive coping (+1 SD) the stress path was significant, "B = -0.396, SE = 0.086, 95% CI = [-0.568, -0.231"; at low coping it was not significant. The high–low difference was significant (B = -0.226, CI [-0.369, -0.080]).

> "The interaction term of perceived stress and proactive coping was significant (B = 0.126, SE = 0.041, p =.001). In addition, from Table 5; Fig. 2, although proactive coping was low (B = -0.170, SE = 0.122, 95% CI = [-0.407, 0.038]), it was insignificant; high (B = -0.396, SE = 0.086, 95% CI = [-0.568, -0.231) was significant."
