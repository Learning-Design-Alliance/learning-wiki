# Explicit information mediated PI's superiority over PBI on interpretation: PI-EI and PBI-EI performed equally, so the PI+EI advantage was attributed to EI, not structured input alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` When EI was absent, PI-EI and PBI-EI groups scored equally on interpretation at both posttests, indicating the PI+EI > PBI+EI contrast reflected a facilitative effect of explicit grammatical explanation rather than structured input alone. [→ Adem Soruç 2017](#adem-soruc-2017)

## Evidence

### Adem Soruç 2017

Adem Soruç, Jingjing Qin, YouJin Kim. (2017). Comparing the Effectiveness of Processing Instruction and Production-Based Instruction on L2 Grammar Learning: The Role of Explicit Information. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(2), 49–70. http://dx.doi.org/1018806/tesl.v34i2.1262

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the interpretation-task results from the 144-learner five-group quasi-experiment. The article attributes the PI+EI advantage to "the beneficial and facilitative effect of explicit grammatical explanation for the PI group" given equal PI-EI and PBI-EI gains.

> "this study found that the higher scores of the PI+EI group over PBI+EI on the interpretation task (in the immediate posttest at least) was not only due to structured input (given that PI-EI and PBI-EI groups made equal gains), but also because of the beneficial and facilitative effect of explicit grammatical explanation for the PI group"
