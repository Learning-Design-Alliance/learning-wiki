## Evidence

### Absi 2011

Absi, R., Nalpas, C., Dufour, F., Huet, D., Bennacer, R., Absi, T. (2011). Teaching Fluid Mechanics for Undergraduate Students in Applied Industrial Biology: from Theory to Atypical Experiments. International Journal of Engineering Education Vol. 27, No. 3, pp. 550-558. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.0656](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.0656)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semantic analysis of students' free comments: quotations grouped into semantic fields, with occurrence n divided by the number of participating students N (P17 N=58). Figure 7b shows the n/N comparison across the four cohorts.

> "The parameter “n/N” for year P17 (solid line) is above the other lines (three other years) for criteria “experience”, “clarity” and “interactivity”."

### Absi 2011 (2)

Absi, R., Nalpas, C., Dufour, F., Huet, D., Bennacer, R., Absi, T. (2011). Teaching Fluid Mechanics for Undergraduate Students in Applied Industrial Biology: from Theory to Atypical Experiments. International Journal of Engineering Education Vol. 27, No. 3, pp. 550-558. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.0656](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.0656)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Counter-expectation finding from the same semantic analysis: the concrete field's n/N fell for P17. The authors attribute this to students not being obliged to write comments and to redundancy between the rated criteria and free comments.

> "However, we notice that the criteria “concrete” decreases for P17, while atypical experiments should raise this criterion."
