# The graphomotor difficulty of the first letter affects handwriting duration, velocity, stops and size in all grades

## Evidence

### Danna 2021

Danna, J., Longcamp, M., Nalborczyk, L., Velay, J.-L., Commengé, C., & Jover, M. (2021). Interaction between orthographic and graphomotor constraints in learning to write. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3596-880X

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on first-letter duration in the main experiment (65 students, Grades 1, 3 and 5). The article reports "a main effect of Graphomotor constraint, F(1, 62) = 74.24, p <.001. η²p = 0.54", confirming the letter f took longer than t; letter size showed an even larger effect (η²p = 0.83).

> "Finally, the analysis also revealed a main effect of Graphomotor constraint, F(1, 62) = 74.24, p <.001. η²p = 0.54, confirming that the letter f required more time to be written than the letter t."
