## Evidence

### Frederick 2014

Frederick, M. L., Courtney, S., & Caniglia, J. (2014). With a Little Help from My Friends: Scaffolding Techniques in Problem Solving. Investigations in Mathematics Learning, 7(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1052936

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three-item survey administered after the unit test measuring attitudes toward preferred scaffolding types and students' rationale for their choices. Responses were qualitative; the article notes a few patterns emerged, including appreciation of "support they received from group members". Some students instead judged whole-class teacher-directed instruction best.

> "Students indicated an appreciation for the support they received from group members when solving non-routine problems. In particular, students stated that they appreciated working with partners."
