## Evidence

### Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019

Beaudoin Pier-Luc, Labbé Mathilde, Fanous Amanda, Young Meredith, Rappaport Jamie, Park Yoon Soo, Manoukian John, Nguyen Lily H. P. (2019). Teaching communication skills to OTL-HNS residents: multisource feedback and simulated scenarios. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-019-0329-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Separate analysis of ratings over time by medical observers (OTL-HNS staff and observing residents) in the three-year cohort. Ratings of 3.67 "increased by 0.31 points per year" (p < 0.001); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Mean ratings from medical observers (OTL-HNS staff, observing residents) were 3.67 and increased by 0.31 points per year (p < 0.001)."

### Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019 (2)

Beaudoin Pier-Luc, Labbé Mathilde, Fanous Amanda, Young Meredith, Rappaport Jamie, Park Yoon Soo, Manoukian John, Nguyen Lily H. P. (2019). Teaching communication skills to OTL-HNS residents: multisource feedback and simulated scenarios. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-019-0329-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Separate analysis of ratings over time by non-medical observers (lawyer or ethicist and standardized patients). Mean ratings of 3.60 showed an "increase of .06 points per year" (p = .721), which was not significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "Non-medical observers (lawyer or ethicist and standardized patients) mean ratings were 3.60 with an increase of .06 points per year (p = .721)."
