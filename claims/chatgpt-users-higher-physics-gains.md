### Qurat-ul-Ann Mirza and N. Sanjay Rebello 2025

Qurat-ul-Ann Mirza and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Help or Hype? Students' Engagement and Perception of Using AI to Solve Physics Problems. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.25642](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.25642)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 49 undergraduates in a calculus-based introductory physics course at a community college; students self-selected into AI use on Problem 2. The article reports "an average increase of 0.43 points" for AI users versus an average decrease of 1.8 points for non-users, with a Mann–Whitney U p-value of 0.06 at α = 0.10.

> "Among the 39 students who used AI tools, 14 showed improved scores from Prob- lem 1 to Problem 2, with an average increase of 0.43 points. In contrast, among the 10 students who chose not to use AI tools, only two students showed improved scores from Prob- lem 1 to Problem 2, with an average decrease of 1.8 points."
