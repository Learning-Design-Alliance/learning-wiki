The brief reports, citing Knuth et al. (2006) and Rittle-Johnson & Star (2009), this association; the brief is a narrative attribution, not a study the authors conducted, and no effect sizes are printed.

> "Success in middle grades mathematics has been associated with relational understandings of the equals sign (Knuth et al., 2006) and the ability to flexibly compare approaches that focus more on structure and relation than on mechanical multiplication or numerical evaluation of algebraic expressions (Rittle-Johnson & Star, 2009)."
