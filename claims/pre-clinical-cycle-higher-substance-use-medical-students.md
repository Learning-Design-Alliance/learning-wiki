## Evidence

### Ferreira Pedro Marques 2022

Ferreira Pedro Marques, Alves Rauni Jandé Roama, Zantut-Wittmann Denise Engelbrecht. (2022). Impact of the use of illicit and licit substances and anxiety disorders on the academic performance of medical students: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03752-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of substance use across curricular stages (basic, pre-clinical, clinical/internship) in the pilot cohort; the article reports that "the pre-clinical cycle had a higher average consumption of amphetamines or ecstasy" and inhalants, with only p-values printed and no effect size.

> "The classes in the pre-clinical cycle had a higher average consumption of amphetamines or ecstasy (p = 0.0006) and inhalants (p = 0.0256) than the other classes (data not shown)."

### Ferreira Pedro Marques 2022 (2)

Ferreira Pedro Marques, Alves Rauni Jandé Roama, Zantut-Wittmann Denise Engelbrecht. (2022). Impact of the use of illicit and licit substances and anxiety disorders on the academic performance of medical students: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03752-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-stage comparison of tobacco use in the pilot cohort; the article reports that "students in the Clinical cycle—Internship used less tobacco and its derivatives than the other classes", with only a p-value printed and no effect size.

> "As for the curricular stages, students in the Clinical cycle—Internship used less tobacco and its derivatives than the other classes (p = 0.0133)."
