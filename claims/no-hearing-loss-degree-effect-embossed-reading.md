### Sella Munyendo & Franciscah Irangi Wamocho 2010

Sella Munyendo & Franciscah Irangi Wamocho. (2010). The Effect of Embossed Picture Technique on Reading Performance of Learners with Hearing Impairments: A Case of Kambui School for the Deaf. Journal of the American Academy of Special Education Professionals, Spring/Summer 2010. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1137051

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis one-way ANOVA comparing post-test rank sums across four hearing-loss groups in the experimental group; the article reports the p-value is >0.05 and the null hypothesis was accepted. Descriptively, pupils with profound hearing loss had the highest rank sum (16) versus moderate (12), severe (11) and moderately severe (6).

> "Finally, the study revealed that there is no significant performance difference in reading of pupils with hearing impairment at different degrees of hearing loss when taught with embossed picture technique."
