The review describes a fully qualitative egocentric study of Latina/o college students using essays, semi-structured interviews, and sociograms with alter categories such as family and clubs. It reports "Latina/o students do not build a network that can support this transition" toward professional trajectories.

> "An interesting finding is that during college, Latina/o students do not build a network that can support this transition, and heavily rely on the networks they built when getting in and fitting in, even when their families don't have resources to provide career guidance."
