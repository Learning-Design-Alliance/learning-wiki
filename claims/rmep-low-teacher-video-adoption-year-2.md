### Nagle 2016

Nagle, K., Pratt-Williams, J., Schmidt, R., Swantek, C., Lyulchenko, M., & McGhee, R. (2016). Evaluation of the Rural Math Excel Partnership Project: Final Report. SRI International. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/projects/other/i3_ruralmath.asp

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

This finding comes from the implementation study's tracking of video assignments through MARi platform data across all participating schools. The percentage was distributed evenly across schools, and at most sites a small group of teachers accounted for most assignments.

> "SRI noted that, in fall semester 2014, few teachers assigned more than 10 videos in total and that 61 percent of participating math teachers had assigned no videos during the semester."
