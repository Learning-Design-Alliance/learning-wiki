### Pečjak and Pirc 2018

Sonja Pečjak, Tina Pirc. (2018). Developing Summarizing Skills in 4th Grade Students: Intervention Programme Effects. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, June 2018, Volume 10, Issue 5, 571-581. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018541306

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Hierarchical regression on pretest data: model 1 explained 33% of summarizing variance in EG and 25% in CG, with GRC the only significant predictor (β = .51 EG, β = .47 CG); MKR was not significant.

> "students’ achievement by summarizing 1 before the intervention programme was moderately predicted only by GRC (β = .51 in EG vs. β = .47 in CG)."
