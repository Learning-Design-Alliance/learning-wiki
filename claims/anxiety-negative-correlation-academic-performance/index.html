Ariel A. Gonzalez 2019¶

Ariel A. Gonzalez, Katherine L. Bottenhorn, Jessica E. Bartley, Timothy Hayes, Michael C. Riedel, Taylor Salo, Elsa I. Bravo, Rosalie Odean, Alina Nazareth, Robert W. Laird, Matthew T. Sutherland, Eric Brewe, Shannon M. Pruden and Angela R. Laird. (2019). Sex differences in brain correlates of STEM anxiety. npj Science of Learning 4:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0058-9

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

FDR-controlled Pearson correlations (0.25) between anxiety and performance among female physics students at pre-instruction: course grade correlated negatively with math anxiety (r = −0.321) and clinical anxiety (r = −0.534).

"course grade was negatively correlated with math anxiety ( r(46) = −0.321, P = 0.030, αFDR = 0.09) and clinical anxiety (r(46) = −0.534, P<0.001, αFDR = 0.03)"

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

The same correlation analysis among male students at post-instruction found GPA and course grade each negatively correlated with science and math anxiety (e.g., GPA with math anxiety r = −0.449). The article concludes that high post-instruction STEM anxiety was associated with poor academic performance.

"Among male students at post-instruction, GPA was negatively correlated with science anxiety ( r(49) = −0.370, P = 0.009, αFDR = 0.09) and math anxiety ( r(49) = −0.449, P = 0.001, αFDR = 0.03), and similarly, grade was also negatively correlated with science anxiety ( r(55) = −0.354, P = 0.008, αFDR = 0.06) and math anxiety ( r(55) = −0.422, P = 0.001, αFDR = 0.03)"
