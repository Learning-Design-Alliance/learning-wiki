Mary Beth Calhoon 2013¶

Mary Beth Calhoon, Hollis S. Scarborough, Brett Miller. (2013). Interventions for struggling adolescent and adult readers: instructional, learner, and situational differences. Read Writ 26:489–494. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-013-9442-7

The editorial reports percentages of adolescent learners classified as gainers under Calhoon and Petscher's individual gain classification (less than one vs. one or more years of normative growth), quoting the printed ranges: "42–87 % of students in the most effective condition" versus 14–65% in the least effective modality.
