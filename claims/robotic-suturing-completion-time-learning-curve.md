## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2023

Rahimi A. Masie, Hardon Sem F., Willuth E., Lang F., Haney Caelan M., Felinska Eleni A., Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Müller-Stich Beat P., Horeman Tim, Nickel F., Daams Freek. (2023). Force-based assessment of tissue handling skills in simulation training for robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-09905-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests comparing first and last trial in the 60-participant study showed "all groups were able to complete the trials faster"; printed medians and p-values only, no effect sizes.

> "Comparing the first and sixth trial, all groups were able to complete the trials faster: novices (164 s vs. 97 s p = 0.011), intermediates (103 s vs. 89 s p = 0.011) and experts (106 s vs. 72 s p = 0.002)"
