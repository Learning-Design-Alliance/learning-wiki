## Evidence

### Zhaoquan Fu 2025

Zhaoquan Fu, Yaping Wang, Jiayu Pei. (2025). Effects of reflective diaries guided by Kolbs experiential learning theory on learning engagement and academic emotions in nursing undergraduates a quasi-experimental study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-25940-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests in the same single-group study of 65 nursing students examined the two negative-emotion dimensions of the academic emotion questionnaire; both declined significantly post-intervention with the printed d values of -0.71 and -0.54.

> "the scores of negative activity orientation and negative outcome orientation were significantly decreased from pretest to post-test, from 33.02 to 30.91 (t = 5.74, p < 0.001, d = − 0.71) and from 36.67 to 34.02 (t = 4.35, p < 0.001, d = − 0.54), respectively. This suggested that participants’ negative academic emotions were reduced"
