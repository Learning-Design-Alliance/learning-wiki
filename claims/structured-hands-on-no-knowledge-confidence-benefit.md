## Evidence

### Grundgeiger 2023

Grundgeiger, T., Ertle, F., Diethei, D., Mengelkamp, C., & Held, V. (2023). Improving procedural skills acquisition of students during medical device training: experiments on e-Learning vs. e-Learning with hands-on. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(1), 127-146. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10148-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 knowledge test (14 multiple-choice items) in the mixed ANOVA; "no significant difference" between groups. Knowledge declined from immediate (M = 0.65, SD = 0.15) to follow-up (M = 0.61, SD = 0.16), p = 0.031, ηp2 = 0.083.

> "We observed no significant difference between the e-Learning + hands-on group (M = 0.63, SD = 0.15) and the e-Learning only group (M = 0.63, SD = 0.16) in the knowledge test, p = 0.914."

### Grundgeiger 2023 (2)

Grundgeiger, T., Ertle, F., Diethei, D., Mengelkamp, C., & Held, V. (2023). Improving procedural skills acquisition of students during medical device training: experiments on e-Learning vs. e-Learning with hands-on. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(1), 127-146. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10148-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 confidence rating (1-7 scale) administered before the skills test; "no significant difference" between groups. The authors suggest confidence might have differed had it been rated after the skills test.

> "For the confidence rating, we observed no significant difference between the e-Learning + hands-on group (M = 3.65, SD = 1.41) and the e-Learning only group (M = 3.32, SD = 1.11), p = 0.249."
