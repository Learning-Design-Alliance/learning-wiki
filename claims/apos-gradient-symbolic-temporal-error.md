`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In Question 1, six students showed a correct object conception of the symbolic representation of the temperature gradient. Students 2 and 3 chose an incorrect representation including temporal rate of change. Student 2 stated "a change of time would affect the temperature so it would affect the heat flux."

> "Only students 2 and 3 chose the symbolic representation of the temperature gradient that includes the temporal rate of change of temperature."
