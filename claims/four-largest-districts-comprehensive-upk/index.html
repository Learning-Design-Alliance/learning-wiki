Leung-Gagné 2023¶

Leung-Gagné, M., Wang, V., Melnick, H., & Mauerman, C. (2023). How are California school districts planning for universal prekindergarten? Results from a 2022 survey. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/109.432

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Case summary of Los Angeles, San Diego, Fresno, and Long Beach unified school districts, which together serve about 14% of state enrollment. "All four districts plan to offer early admittance TK (ETK)"; all also planned full-day TK, dual language programs, and DRDP use.

"All four districts plan to offer early admittance TK (ETK). The four districts plan to expand TK eligibility faster than the legislated rollout."
