# Exploratory analyses: daily teacher and classmate support raise self-reported motivation, average parent and teacher support raise it, but average classmate support lowers it

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On days with more teacher or classmate support, self-reported academic motivation is higher (within-person). [→ Kramer 2024](#kramer-2024)
`q2 i?` Higher average parent and teacher support predicts higher self-reported motivation, while higher average classmate support predicts lower motivation (between-person). [→ Kramer 2024 (2)](#kramer-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Kramer 2024

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory source-specific models showed positive within-person effects of teacher support (β= 0.10) and classmate support (β= 0.11) on self-reported motivation, each with a 95% CI excluding zero.

> "on days when participants reported more social support from teachers (β= 0.10, 95% CI = [0.02, 0.18]) and classmates (β= 0.11, 95% CI = [0.03, 0.19]) their self-reported academic motivation was also higher (within-person effects)."

### Kramer 2024 (2)

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-person effects were positive for parent (β= 0.28) and teacher (β= 0.57) support and negative for classmate support, "β=-0.37, 95% CI =-0.62, -0.11]"; no friend-support relation was found.

> "when participants' overall experienced social support from parents (β= 0.28, 95% CI = 0.04, 0.52]) and teachers (β= 0.57, 95% CI = 0.36, 0.77]) was higher, they also experienced higher academic motivation (between-person effects). Receiving more overall social support from classmates (β=-0.37, 95% CI =-0.62, -0.11]), however, was negatively related to self-reported academic motivation (between-person effect)."
