## Evidence

### Mehmet Fatih Ayaz 2015

Mehmet Fatih Ayaz, Hanifi Şekerci. (2015). The Effects of the Constructivist Learning Approach on Student's Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis Study. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077612

`q3 · i?`

Moderator analyses (Table 2, analogue ANOVA) for sample size, practice time and method. The article reports no statistically significant difference for these moderators; equivalence was not tested. Descriptively, method effects ranged from 7E (ES = -0.051) to invention (ES = 2.776).
