Evidence¶

Scheiter 2020¶

Scheiter, K., Ackerman, R., & Hoogerheide, V. (2020). Looking at Mental Effort Appraisals through a Metacognitive Lens: Are they Biased? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09555-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

Van Gog et al. (2012) investigated how immediate effort ratings provided after each task item relate to a single delayed rating at the end of a task sequence. The review reports that "single delayed estimates of effort were higher than the average of all the immediate ratings," though the delayed rating was not as high as the effort reported for the most complex task, suggesting itemized appraisals better capture fluctuations.

"They found that single delayed estimates of effort were higher than the average of all the immediate ratings."
