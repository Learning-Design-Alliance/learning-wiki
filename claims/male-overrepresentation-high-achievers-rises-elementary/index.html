Kadir Bahar 2026¶

Kadir Bahar. (2026). Longitudinal Analysis of Gender Disparities in Mathematics Achievement among Elementary Students. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2026/02/18/longitudinal-analysis-of-gender-disparities-in-mathematics-achievement-among-elementary-students/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

MFR analysis pooling both cohorts, defining high achievers as students scoring at least one grade level above enrolled grade on i-Ready. Kindergarten MFR rose from 0.33 (fall) to 2.17 (spring); Grade 3 values exceeded 4.50 in both windows, indicating "pronounced male overrepresentation".

"The largest disparities are observed in Grade 5, where MFR values reach 6.75 in fall and 8.00 in spring."
