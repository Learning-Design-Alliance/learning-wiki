Evidence¶

Kaakinen 2021¶

Kaakinen, J. K. (2021). What Can Eye Movements Tell us about Visual Perception Processes in Classroom Contexts? Commentary on a Special Issue. Educational Psychology Review, 33, 169-179. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09573-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The author's commentary synthesis of the Special Issue studies, which combined specialized eye trackers and automated image processing of classroom video. She concludes the studies "demonstrate that the eye tracking methodology" can be utilized in complex naturalistic environments. No effect sizes are printed.

"The papers included in this Special Issue demonstrate that the eye tracking methodology that has long been used mainly in laboratory settings (for reviews, see Rayner1998, 2009)c a n also be utilized to study behavior in complex naturalistic environments."
