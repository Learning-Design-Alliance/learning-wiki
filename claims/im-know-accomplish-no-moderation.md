## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither main effects nor interactions of IM to Know and IM to Accomplish with relative achievement were significant; the tests did not detect moderation. [→ Mendoza 2025](#mendoza-2025)

## Evidence

### Mendoza 2025

Mendoza, N. B., Zadorozhnyy, A., & Dizon, J. I. W. T. (2025). A rising tide lifts all boats: The social contagion of achievement in L2 classrooms and the role of intrinsic motivation and engagement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1155–1175. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12752

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Models 4 and 5 (766 students, 30 classrooms) tested IM-to-Know and IM-to-Accomplish as ACE moderators. The article reports their main effects and interactions "were significant" nowhere — all p values (.53, .27, .30, .89) exceeded thresholds. No effect sizes printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect); equivalence was not tested.

> "Neither the main effects of IM to Know [β = .09, SE = .14, t(735.17) = .63, p = .53] and IM to Accomplish [β = −.20, SE = .18, t(734.16) = −1.12, p = .27] nor their interactions with relative achievement [IM to Know: β = −.03, SE = .03, t(735.02) = −1.04, p = .30; IM to Accomplish: β = .01, SE = .03, t(731.70) = .14, p = .89] were significant"
