## Evidence

### Willingham 2008

Daniel T. Willingham. (2008). Critical Thinking: Why Is It So Hard to Teach? Arts Education Policy Review, 109(4), 21-32. https://doi.org/10.3200/AEPR.109.4.21-32

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

An experiment in which subjects first read four problems with solutions, including a garden problem solved by the least common multiple, then received a band problem with the same deep structure. Only "just 19 percent" recognized the similarity and reused the solution.

> "Yet few subjects— just 19 percent—saw that the band problem was similar and that they could use the garden problem solution."
