Density-function simulation of screening accuracy (Results, Research question 2; Table 2) computing PPV and NPV from cumulative distribution functions for progress monitoring alone at three effectiveness levels (r = 0.40, 0.55, 0.70) as the baseline for judging PGS's incremental value.
