## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the i-Ready fidelity sample, students using the product at the recommended level were significantly more likely to meet proficiency than matched BAU students (odds ratio 2.765, p = .002, Cox effect size 0.62). [→ Brasiel 2016](#brasiel-2016)

## Evidence

### Brasiel 2016

Brasiel, S., Jeong, S., Ames, C., Lawanto, K., Yuan, M., & Martin, T. (2016). Effects of educational technology on mathematics achievement for K-12 students in Utah. Journal of Online Learning Research, 2(3), 205-226. https://www.learntechlib.org/j/JOLR/

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Propensity-score matched logistic regression on the i-Ready fidelity sample of 190 students who met the 30-minutes-per-week benchmark. Table 7 reports an odds ratio of 2.765 with significance level 0.002 and effect size 0.62, well above the 0.16 comparison point from Cheung and Slavin (2013).
