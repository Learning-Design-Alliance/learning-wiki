`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive attendance analysis in the 4-week randomized trial: teachers recorded participation in the daily classroom break sessions, and the benchmark of 13 sessions was based on the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans. The article reports "37% of the intention-to-treat students" met the benchmark; no effect size is printed.

> "An evaluation of the attendance record showed that 19 students (9 males and 10 females) (37% of the intention-to-treat students) met the 13 session benchmark for minimum participation."
