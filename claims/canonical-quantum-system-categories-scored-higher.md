## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Expertise in Quantum Mechanics using Categorization Task. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02948](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02948)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scoring analysis of the categorization task, in which four of the 20 problems belonged to each of the four named systems. The article reports hydrogen atom categories reached at least 50% (3 out of 6) for three of four hydrogen atom questions but not for any infinite square well question; no effect size is printed.

> "The overall scores (by the three faculty members who evaluated all of the categorizations) of concrete categories such as “hydrogen atom” or “harmonic oscillator” were higher than other concrete categories such as “inﬁnite square well” or “free particle”"
