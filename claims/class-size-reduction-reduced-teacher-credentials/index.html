A study of California's K-3 class size reduction experiment (CSR Research Consortium, 1999), reported in Table 8, raises cautionary notes about teacher quality impacts, especially in high-poverty schools. Percentages are descriptive; no standardized effect size is printed.

"In the two years between 1995 and 1997, the proportion of K-3 teachers without a full credential jumped from 1 to 12%. There were also increases in the proportion of K-3 teachers with only a bachelor's degree, or less (from 17% in 1995 to 23% in 1997)."
