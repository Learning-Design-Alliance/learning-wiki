Qualitative analysis of focus group and interview transcripts found varied causal explanations; the article states the clear majority pointed to depressive thoughts, with the root “described as due to marital conflict and/or financial worries.” No effect size is printed.

> "The most consistently stated cause of pre-eclampsia by participants was psychological, with the clear majority pointing to depressive thoughts. The root of these depressive thoughts was described as due to marital conflict and/or financial worries."
