## Evidence

### Kostantinos Mattas 2025

Kostantinos Mattas, Antonio Lucas-Alba, Tomer Toledo, Óscar M. Melchor, Shlomo Bekhor, Biagio Ciuffo. (2025). Learning to Car-Follow Using an Inertia-Oriented Driving Technique: A Before-and-After Study on a Closed Circuit. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-effects model (SPSS 29, REML, compound symmetry) of circuit data from drivers 3–6 across three teams (Table 4): fuel consumption dropped from M = 9.62 to 7.74 l, F(1, 38.03) significant at p < .001.

> "Finally, fuel consumption (based on tractive energy computations ; see Appendix 1) also revealed a significant main effect of time. The estimated  marginal mean average consumption decreased in the posttest."

### Kostantinos Mattas 2025 (2)

Kostantinos Mattas, Antonio Lucas-Alba, Tomer Toledo, Óscar M. Melchor, Shlomo Bekhor, Biagio Ciuffo. (2025). Learning to Car-Follow Using an Inertia-Oriented Driving Technique: A Before-and-After Study on a Closed Circuit. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same LMM analysis of closed-circuit data (Table 4): average distance to leader rose from M = 21.21 to 26.91 m (p < .001) and distance dispersion from 8.07 to 10.53 m (p = .001), consistent with the DI pattern of larger, more variable spacing.

> "Coming to the average distance, the analysis revealed a significant main effect of time, and the estimated marginal mean average distance increased in the posttest, as did distance dispersion."
