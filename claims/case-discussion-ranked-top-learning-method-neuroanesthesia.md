## Evidence

### Raksakietisak M 2024

Raksakietisak M, Lertsiripatarajit V, Aroonpruksakul N, Plailaharn N, Raksamani K. (2024). Test-enhanced learning in Neuroanesthesia for the First Year anesthetic residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 24:905. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05887-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire asked all 40 participants to vote their first, second and third most learning-enhancing methods, weighted 3, 2 and 1. "Discussing the case with supervisors for important points" scored highest (85 of the weighted points in Table 4); self-testing ranked lowest. Self-report only.

> "The discussion of cases with supervisors for crucial insights received the highest score, followed by providing anesthesia for patients under close supervision by supervisors, and then providing anesthesia with distance supervision."
