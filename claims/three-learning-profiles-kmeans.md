# Three learning profiles emerge among university students: organized-deep, unorganized-deep, and dissonant

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` K-means cluster analysis of approaches to learning identified three profiles: organized students applying a deep approach (n = 230), unorganized students applying a deep approach (n = 113), and a dissonant profile scoring high on all approaches (n = 119). [→ Tuononen 2023](#tuononen-2023)

## Evidence

### Tuononen 2023

Tuononen, T., Hyytinen, H., Räisänen, M., Hailikari, T., & Parpala, A. (2023). Metacognitive awareness in relation to university students' learning profiles. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 37–54. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09314-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

K-means cluster analysis on the three approaches-to-learning scales in the survey of 462 students. Table 3 prints cluster sizes of 230, 113 and 119; the third, the Dissonant profile, "comprised students who scored high on all approaches".

> "The first, Organized students applying a deep approach, included students who scored the highest on the deep approach and organized studying and lowest on the unreflective approach. The second profile, Unorganized students applying a deep approach, included students who scored high on deep approach but the lowest on organized studying."
