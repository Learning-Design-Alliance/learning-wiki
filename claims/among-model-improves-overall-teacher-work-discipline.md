# Overall teacher work discipline rose from 2.08 (low) to 4.25 (very high) in a one-group pre-test/post-test study with no comparison group across three action-research cycles of the "Among" leadership model

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Overall mean teacher work discipline increased consistently across three cycles of the "Among" leadership model application, from 2.08 before the action to 4.25 in the third cycle. [→ Rusman 2020](#rusman-2020)

## Evidence

### Rusman 2020

Rusman, Sunarti, & Rahadian. (2020). Application of the "Among" Leadership Model to Improve Teacher Work Discipline. International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.07284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.07284)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

School action research with 16 class teachers at SDN 11 Simpang Rimba using a one-group pretest-posttest design across three cycles, measured by observation and documentation with Likert-scaled indicators. The overall mean rose from "2.08 (low)" to "4.25 (very high)"; only descriptive means are reported, with no effect size or significance test.

> "Before the action, the mean of teacher work discipline was 2.08 (low), increased to 3.04 (moderate) in the first cycle, increased again to 3.65 (high) in the second cycle, and increased again in the third cycle to 4.25 (very high)."
