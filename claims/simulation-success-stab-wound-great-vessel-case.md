### Karakuş Akan 2014

Karakuş Akan, Duran Latif, Yavuz Yücel, Altintop Levent, Çalişkan Fatih. (2014). Computer-based simulation training in emergency medicine designed in the light of malpractice cases. BMC Medical Education, 14, 155. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-155

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-case analysis of the stab wound case (Table 1: OR 5.0, 95% CI 1.31-19.07, p = 0.030). Success required recognizing great vessel injury, examining lower-extremity pulses and choosing Doppler ultrasonography; the study reports "83.3% (n = 20) higher than traditionally trained students (50%; n = 12; p = 0.030)".
