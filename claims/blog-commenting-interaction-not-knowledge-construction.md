Carlos R. del Blanco, Ivan García-Magariño. (2018). The use of blogs in the education field: A qualitative systematic review. https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05421

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of the collaborative-learning category, where almost all studies used self-reported data and none had a control group. The review reports "the commenting feature of blogs was successfully used to give comments and suggestions" and that "there was not collaboration for social construction of knowledge".

> "Overall, results indicated that the commenting feature of blogs was successfully used to give comments and suggestions, and also to promote th e inter- action among participants (students and instructors). However , there was not collaboration for social construction of knowledge. Higher ord er compe- tencies, such as coaching, were not acquired by the students eith er."
