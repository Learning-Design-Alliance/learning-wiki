### Zheng Que 2022

Zheng Que, Cheng Yu Yan, Sonuga-Barke Edmund, Shum Kathy Kar-man. (2022). Do Executive Dysfunction, Delay Aversion, and Time Perception Deficit Predict ADHD Symptoms and Early Academic Performance in Preschoolers. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-022-00937-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the same hierarchical regression models predicting academic performance in 131 preschoolers. The authors call this "rather unexpected" given prior literature linking executive dysfunction to early academic performance.

> "By contrast, neither the IAC nor DAv component predicted academic skills assessed concurrently or one year later."
