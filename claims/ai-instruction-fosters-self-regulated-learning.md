# AI-mediated language instruction fosters self-regulated learning among EFL learners more than traditional instruction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Self-regulation scores rose in both groups, but the enhancement substantially differed between groups, favoring AI-mediated instruction (Group × Time η2 = 0.38). [→ Wei L 2023](#wei-l-2023)

## Evidence

### Wei L 2023

Wei L (2023) Artificial intelligence in language instruction: impact on English learning achievement, L2 motivation, and self-regulated learning. Front. Psychol. 14:1261955. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1261955

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on the 63-item Self-Regulation Questionnaire (pilot reliability 0.83). Post-test means: Experimental M = 3.94 (SD = 0.73) versus Control M = 3.37 (SD = 0.76); main effects for Group (η2 = 0.64) and Time (η2 = 0.24) were also significant.

> "Furthermore, the interaction effect between Group and Time was significant [ F(1, 58) = 7.68, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.38], indicating that the enhancement in self-regulated learning substantially differed between the experimental and control groups as a result of AI-mediated instruction."
