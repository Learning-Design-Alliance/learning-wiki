Evidence¶

Awanui Te Huia 2015¶

Awanui Te Huia. (2015). Exploring goals and motivations of Māori heritage language learners. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(4), 609-635. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135051

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview study of 19 Māori HL2 learners (11 beginner-intermediate undergraduates, 8 advanced graduates) analysed with thematic analysis; the study reports that learners were motivated by "their cultural heritage connection to the language and to other ingroup members."

"Results demonstrated how Māori heritage learners were motivated to learn due to their cultural heritage connection to the language and to other ingroup members."
