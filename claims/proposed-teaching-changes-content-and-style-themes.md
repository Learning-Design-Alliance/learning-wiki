Katz-Sidlow Rachel J., Baer Tamar G., Gershel Jeffrey C. (2016). Providing rapid feedback to residents on their teaching skills: an educational strategy for contemporary trainees. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.56dc.908a

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative hand-coding of resident free-text survey responses by the first author. Table 1 lists subthemes such as modifying the amount of content taught (10 comments), providing visual aids (12 comments), and making sessions more interactive (6 comments); no effect size applies.

> "Residents' free-text answers to the survey question "What might you change?" were coded into two themes: modifications relating to session content, and those that related to residents' personal teaching styles (Table 1)."
