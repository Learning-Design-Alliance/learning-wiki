## Evidence

### Ira Nichols-Barrer 2022

Ira Nichols-Barrer, Maria Bartlett, Thomas Coen, Philip Gleason. (2022). The Impacts on College Enrollment of a Charter Network Serving Disadvantaged Students: Evidence from KIPP Middle School Lotteries. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness (online ahead of print). https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impacts-on-college-enrollment-of-a-charter-network-serving-disadvantaged-students-evidence

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quasi-experimental study of 13 KIPP middle schools using admission lotteries for random assignment, measuring college enrollment and persistence over the first two postsecondary years. The abstract reports "The magnitude of the estimated impact of a middle school admission offer (6.9%)... is substantial relative to nationwide disparities in college enrollment across racial groups."

> "Using admission lotteries as a random assignment instrument, we estimate the impacts of 13 KIPP middle schools on college enrollment and persistence in college over the first two postsecondary years."

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The study reports the admission-offer impact estimate of 6.9% and characterizes it as substantial relative to nationwide racial disparities in college enrollment. Only percentage impacts are printed; no standard error or effect size is shown in the text provided.

> "The magnitude of the estimated impact of a middle school admission offer (6.9%) and enrollment at a KIPP school (12.9%) is substantial relative to nationwide disparities in college enrollment across racial groups."
