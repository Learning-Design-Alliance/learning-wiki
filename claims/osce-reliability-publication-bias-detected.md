## Evidence

### Peng Qiyun 2025

Peng Qiyun, Luo Jiani, Wang Chunyan, Chen Lei, Tan Shenglan. (2025). Impact of station number and duration time per station on the reliability of Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) scores: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06691-0

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Publication bias assessment using funnel plots and Egger's regression test across all 26 included alpha values. The authors attribute the asymmetry to significant baseline differences across studies rather than to selective reporting alone.

> "The funnel plot showed some asymmetry (Fig. 3), which aligned with the results of the Egger regression test (p < 0.001). This asymmetry may be attributed to significant baseline differences across the included studies, potentially introducing bias."
