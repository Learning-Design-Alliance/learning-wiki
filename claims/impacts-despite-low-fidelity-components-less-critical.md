## Evidence

### Dominique Bradley 2015

Dominique Bradley, Evan Crawford, Sara E. Dahill-Brown. (2015). Fidelity of Implementation in a Large-Scale, Randomized, Field Trial: Identifying the Critical Components of Values Affirmation. SREE Spring 2015 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562183

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors report, citing their impact analysis (Borman & Grigg, 2014), significant positive impacts on the intended audience despite low fidelity of some critical components, and interpret this as questioning the necessity of some originally specified delivery and stealth features.

> "even with the threats to the fidelity of delivery and stealth we find significant impacts of the intervention on the intended audience (Borman & Grigg, 2014). These significant positive impacts despite low fidelity of some critical components suggests that there may be aspects of delivery and stealth that are less important as they are defined and understood in the original intervention protocol."
