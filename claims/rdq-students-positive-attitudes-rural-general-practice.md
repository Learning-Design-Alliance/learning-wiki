## Evidence

### Krauthausen M 2026

Krauthausen M, Wingender L, Deyerl V, Hagen PE, Leutritz T, Simmenroth A. (2026). Rural admission quota in Germany: students’ attitudes, challenges and the need for support programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08776-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey at all five Bavarian medical faculties; 158 RDQ students rated Likert-scale attitude items. The item "I would be interested in general practice even without a contractual obligation" had M = 3.96 (SD 1.17) and pride in becoming a rural GP M = 3.92 (SD 1.10) on a 1–5 scale.

> "They reported both interest in general practice independent of the contractual service requirement and pride in the prospect of becoming a rural GP, indicating a predominantly favourable orientation toward rural primary care."
