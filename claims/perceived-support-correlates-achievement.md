# Perceived teacher support in the hypothesis and experimentation phases correlated positively with post-test achievement in both groups; argumentation-support and enjoyment correlations emerged only across the pooled sample

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Argumentation-support and inquiry-experience perceptions were not significantly correlated with achievement within each group but showed significant pooled-sample associations (ρ = 0.317 and 0.253). [→ Fan Chen 2026](#fan-chen-2026)

## Evidence

### Fan Chen 2026

Fan Chen, Gaowei Chen. (2026). How a technology-augmented inquiry approach affects teacher practice and student learning in disadvantaged science classrooms. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09788-1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Pooled-sample Spearman correlations were significant while within-group correlations were not; ρ = 0.253 is a small association. Associations are correlational with no causal claim.

> "Correlations between post-test achievement and perceptions of argumentation- related support or inquiry experience were nonsignificant within each group (all p > 0.05). However, when analyzed across the full sample, both argumentation-related support [ Spear-man’ s ρ = 0.317, p = 0.002] and inquiry experience [Spearman’ s ρ = 0.253, p = 0.014] showed significant positive associations with post-test achievement."
