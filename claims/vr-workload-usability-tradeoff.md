## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median UMUX-LITE usability scores were 92 (2D video), 90 (360° video), 88 (Passive-VR), and 85 (Game-VR), all above the good-usability threshold, while Game-VR had the highest workload (mean ≈ 30/100) and a significant overall workload difference across conditions (X² = 24.5, p < 0.001). ## Evidence

### Yue Yang 2025

Yue Yang, Mengyao Guo, Yuxuan Wu, Wally Niu, Emmanuel Corona, Bruce Daniel, Christoph Leuze, Fred Baik. (2025). VR MRI Training for Adolescents: A Comparative Study of Gamified VR, Passive VR, 360◦ Video, and Traditional Educational Video. [doi:10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar-adjunct68609.2025.00081)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects study (N=11) using UMUX-LITE after each modality and NASA-TLX for workload. The article reports a significant workload difference (X² = 24.5, p < 0.001) and "significantly higher performance for Game-VR compared to 2D video (p < 0.001)".

> "A Friedman test revealed significant differences in overall workload across the four conditions (X² = 24.5, p < 0.001). Post-hoc pairwise comparisons showed that participants reported significantly higher performance for Game-VR compared to 2D video (p < 0.001), 360 ◦ video (p < 0.05), and Passive-VR (p < 0.05)."
