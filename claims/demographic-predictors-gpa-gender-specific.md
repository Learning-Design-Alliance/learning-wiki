### Cen Shanduo 2025

Cen Shanduo, Zhao Miaomiao, Wang Feng, Tang Lei. (2025). Gender differences in the relationship between mental health and academic performance among undergraduate students at a medical school in Shanghai: a cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-21697-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression of demographic factors on binary GPA (Table 3), adjusted for covariates. The study reports "aOR = 4.410, 95% CI: 2.497–7.788" for male minority students and "aOR = 1.958, 95% CI: 1.464–2.617" for female minority students, with progressively lower year-of-study odds for both genders.

> "Both male and female students from ethnic minorities demonstrated higher risks of poor academic performance compared to Han ethnicity students (males: aOR = 4.410, 95% CI: 2.497–7.788; females: aOR = 1.958, 95% CI: 1.464–2.617)."
