### Karadağ et al. 2018

Mustafa Karadağ, Resul Ağirtaş, Atilla Pulur. (2018). Effects of Peer Education on Attitudes of Secondary School Students Towards the Course of Physical Education and Sports. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i4a.3171

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-groups t-test on the experimental group found a significant increase for item 18 (t = -2.249, p = 0.028), from pretest X = 4.10 to posttest X = 4.60, reported as "significant difference (p <.05)".

> "there is significant difference (p <.05) in the opinion item 18 “I believe that free time is better made use of by exercising and sporting”. While the students expressed, at the pretest, their opinion about this opinion item at the level of “I agree”"
