Amrita Ganguly 2026¶

Amrita Ganguly, Aditya Johri, Nora McDonald, Areej Ali, Umama Dewan, and Aayushi Hingle Collier. (2026). A Comparative Analysis of Institutional and Course Generative AI Policies within Higher Education: Implications for Instruction in Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12296

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Institutional document coding of 116 institutions found privacy cautions directed at instructors; the course-level analysis found 34% (N=33) of syllabi mentioned implications including veracity of GenAI output (20%), privacy (19%), hindering learning (17%), and skill development (7%).

"About three in five institutions (N =69, 60%) caution instructors about privacy concerns with GenAI."
