## Evidence

### Devine 2017

Devine, A., Hill, F., Carey, E., & Szűcs, D. (2017). Cognitive and Emotional Math Problems Largely Dissociate: Prevalence of Developmental Dyscalculia and Mathematics Anxiety. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000222

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the 1757-child sample, DD was defined as mathematics performance below 1 SD below the mean with reading performance above 1 SD below the mean; "99 children (5.6%) were included in the group of children with DD". Chi-square showed no significant gender difference (χ2 = 1.82, p =.18; φ = .032).

> "Using the absolute threshold definition of DD used by Devine et al (2013), 99 children (5.6%) were included in the group of children with DD."
