## Evidence

### Connell 2016

Connell, G. L., Donovan, D. A., & Chambers, T. G. (2016). Increasing the Use of Student-Centered Pedagogies from Moderate to High Improves Student Learning and Attitudes about Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0062

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two additional questions administered to Extensive-section students with the end-of-quarter VASS through the course management system, asking about attitudes toward the pedagogies and workload; 67% thought the out-of-class preparation "was worth the time they put into it" while 19% did not.

> "Fifty three percent of students reported that they prefer active-learning pedagogies as opposed to lecture, while 33% reported that they prefer lecture (the remaining students either preferred both pedagogies equally or did not like either pedagogy; Figure 6)."
