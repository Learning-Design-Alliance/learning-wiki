## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Rismanchian 2024

Rismanchian, S., & Doroudi, S. (2024). Reconciling Different Theories of Learning with an Agent-based Model of Procedural Learning. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.13364](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.13364)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical prediction derived from the model's structure for knowledge components with no associated rationale, such as English articles and Chinese vocabulary in KLI terms; no numerical experiment is reported for this section, so no effect size is available.

> "If there is a knowledge graph with no edges, the benefit of learning or reinforcing edges in active, constructive, and interactive learning disappears. Moreover, passive learning becomes the best form of learning because the mastery equation for all four modes becomes equivalent"
