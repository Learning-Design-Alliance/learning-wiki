## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Peter Reimann 2021

Peter Reimann. (2021). Socio-technical configurations for productive talk. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:301–306. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09354-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Whole-school-year case study of about 20 Grade 5 students working on the human body with Knowledge Forum extended by Idea Thread Mapper, combining classroom observations, video-recorded metacognitive meetings, log-file, qualitative and social-network analyses with dynamically student-formed groups. The article's remark is an authors' evaluative interpretation of this design, not a comparative test.

> "It is remarkable that students so young can maintain productive collaboration over such an extended period, with the teacher remaining in a facilitative rather than guiding role."
