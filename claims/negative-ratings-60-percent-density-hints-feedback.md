## Evidence

### Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan 2015

Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan. (2015). Dynamic Cultural Contextualisation of Educational Content in Intelligent Learning Environments using ICON. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 25:249–270. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0033-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Graphical analysis (Fig. 5) of feedback ratings across density levels found "a divergent trend in student ratings towards the negative end of the rating scale at 60 % cultural density"; the trend repeated for hints. The authors note it was not detected by the tests, possibly due to small sample size.

> "However, the graph reveals a divergent trend in student ratings towards the negative end of the rating scale at 60 % cultural density, indicated by the dashed line in the figure."
