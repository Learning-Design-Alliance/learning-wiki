Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 1787–1811. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10359-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of lesson plans from four elementary school teachers in an exploratory experiment. The study found that "teachers utilised various user-generated microgames and debriefing approaches to address different topics while they administered fairly similar scenarios." Topics included addition and subtraction, fractions, solid geometry, and line charts.
