# The MLM group showed greater preference for the study materials over their current textbook than the two text-based groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The MLM group preferred the study materials over their traditional textbook significantly more than the combined Textbook and MLM-script groups (p < 0.02, Fisher exact test); the two text-based groups did not differ (p = 0.4). [→ Stelzer 2008](#stelzer-2008)

## Evidence

### Stelzer 2008

Stelzer, Gladding, Mestre, and Brookes. (2008). Comparing the efficacy of multimedia modules with traditional textbooks for learning introductory physics content. https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0405

`q2 · i1`

End-of-study survey comparing study materials with the students' current textbook; the two text-based presentations were combined for this comparison because the small sample made a chi-squared test invalid. Table IV shows 13 of 16 MLM participants preferring the study materials versus 5 of 13 and 8 of 16.

> "the MLM group showed a greater preference for the study materials encountered during the intervention over their traditional textbook as compared to the Textbook and the MLM-script groups (p < 0.02, Fisher exact test)."
