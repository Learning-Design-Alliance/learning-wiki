## Evidence

### Liu Hsing-Yuan 2019

Liu Hsing-Yuan, Wang I-Teng. (2019). Creative teaching behaviors of health care school teachers in Taiwan: mediating and moderating effects. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1641-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Baron-and-Kenny multiple regression mediation analysis in a cross-sectional survey of 53 teachers. Model 3 showed self-efficacy "positively and significantly related to teachers' characteristics and motives (β = 0.57, p < .001)" while teamwork became non-significant, indicating full mediation.

> "Teachers' creative teaching self-efficacy was positively and significantly related to teachers' characteristics and motives (β = 0.57, p < .001); teamwork was positive but not significant (β = 0.16, p = 0.18). Therefore, our results indicated that teachers' creative teaching self-efficacy fully mediated the effect of teamwork on teachers' characteristics and motivations."
