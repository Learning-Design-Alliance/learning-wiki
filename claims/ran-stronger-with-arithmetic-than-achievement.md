## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Math domain (arithmetic vs. achievement) was a significant between-study moderator (β = .12, p < .05), with arithmetic measures showing higher correlations. [→ Koponen 2017](#koponen-2017)

## Evidence

### Koponen 2017

Koponen, T., Georgiou, G., Salmi, P., Leskinen, M., & Aro, M. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of the Relation Between RAN and Mathematics. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(7), 977-992. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000182

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Within- and between-study meta-regression with robust variance estimation (D-MR approach), moderators partitioned and analyzed separately. The moderator β = .12 is a regression coefficient, not a printed effect size. The article interprets that arithmetic tasks tap "the same capacities as RAN."

> "the achievement versus arithmetic task (i.e., domain of math assessed 1) was a significant moderator (β = .12, p < .05). Arithmetic math measurements were associated with higher correlation coefficients."
