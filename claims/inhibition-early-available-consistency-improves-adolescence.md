## Evidence

### Constantinidis Christos 2019

Constantinidis Christos, Luna Beatriz. (2019). Neural Substrates of Inhibitory Control Maturation in Adolescence. Trends in Neurosciences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2019.07.004

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Klenberg, that single correct inhibitory responses are available early and that developmental gains lie in the proportion of correct trials, implying that "the circuitry that can dampen reflexive sensorimotor responses" is available early in development.

> "while the ability to generate a single correct inhibitory response is evident early in development, what improves through adolescence is the proportion of trials with correct inhibitory responses [16]."
