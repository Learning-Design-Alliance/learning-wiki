q3 · i? · causal · r?

A large-scale randomized control trial of four reading comprehension curricula with fifth-grade students in disadvantaged urban districts. The report states "the curricula had no positive impact on student test scores, and in some cases, had a negative impact." No effect sizes are printed on this page.

"Overall, the curricula had no positive impact on student test scores, and in some cases, had a negative impact."

Related Claims¶

  • After one school year of implementation (first cohort), the curricula produced no statistically significant positive impacts, and four statistically significant negative impacts were observed. — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no statistically significant impacts on students one year after the end of their implementation (Cohort 1 follow-up in sixth grade). — related
  • Reading for Knowledge had a statistically significant negative impact on students' reading comprehension scores in the study's first year — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The evaluation was a large-scale randomized control trial spanning 268 teachers, 6,350 students, 89 schools, 10 districts, and 8 states — related
  • Project CRISS, ReadAbout, and Read for Real showed no significant impacts on reading comprehension in the study's first year — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Impacts of the curricula were not statistically significantly larger after schools had one year of experience using them (Cohort 2 fifth graders). — related
  • LLI had no impact on secondary students' reading comprehension in the Oakland RCT — related
  • In the Torgesen et al. (2006) RCT, SpellRead™ showed indeterminate effects for fifth graders, with no statistically significant or substantively important effects in any domain after six months — related
  • The four reading interventions did not improve state assessment (PSSA) scores; for fifth graders they lowered reading and mathematics scores — related
  • A national evaluation design was produced to assess supplemental reading comprehension programs for fifth-grade students — related
