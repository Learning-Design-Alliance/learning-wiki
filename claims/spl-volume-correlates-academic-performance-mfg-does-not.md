## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Bilateral SPL volume correlated with academic performance (r = 0.24, p = 0.003; △R2 = 3.9%, β = 0.20, p = 0.012), while right MFG volume showed no significant correlation (r = −0.08, p = 0.346). [→ Wang Song 2019](#wang-song-2019)

## Evidence

### Wang Song 2019

Wang Song, Zhao Yajun, Li Jingguang, Wang Xu, Luo Kui, Gong Qiyong. (2019). Brain structure links trait conscientiousness to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-48704-1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the same sample testing whether GMV in the regions identified by the whole-brain regression predicted CNUEE scores. The printed correlation for SPL volume was r = 0.24, a small-to-medium association; the MFG correlation (r = −0.08) was non-significant, so equivalence was not tested.

> "We found a significant correlation between bilateral SPL volume and academic performance (r = 0.24, p = 0.003). However, we did not find a significant correlation between right MFG volume and academic performance (r = −0.08, p = 0.346)."
