Sensitivity analysis within the same meta-analysis: 91 effects came from Mikulincer's laboratory experiments, whose weighted average d was -0.39 versus 0.47 for the remaining data. After exclusion, "the weighted variance of d was .92" and 33% of remaining effects were still negative. No effect size is printed for the variability itself.
