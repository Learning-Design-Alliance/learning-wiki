## Evidence

### Kulak 2015

Kulak, V., & Newton, G. (2015). An Investigation of the Pedagogical Impact of Using Case-based Learning in a Undergraduate Biochemistry Course. International Journal of Higher Education, 4(4), 13. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v4n4p13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blooming Biology Tool applied to final exam questions, with accuracy calculated per Bloom level per student. Non-CBL students scored ~57% at knowledge level vs ~78% for CBL students; no effect size printed.

> "At the knowledge level (level one) of Bloom’s taxonomy, the CBL group performed significantly better than the non-CBL group (p<0.001) (Figure 6), which suggests that use of CBL may have increased student’s general knowledge base in biochemistry. There were no differences between the CBL and non-CBL group at the levels of comprehension (level two) or application (level three)."
