## Evidence

### Taylor 2007

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Standard regression analysis at the class level (N = 51 teachers) tested whether class-average perceived student self-determination predicted each reported strategy. The authors report "autonomy support (ß = .39, p < .001), involvement (ß = .46, p < .001), and structure (ß = .07, p < .05)" as significant predictors.

> "Teacher perceptions of class average self-determination significantly predicted their reported use of autonomy support (ß = .39, p < .001), involvement (ß = .46, p < .001), and structure (ß = .07, p < .05)."
