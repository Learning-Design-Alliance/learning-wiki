### Deroncele-Acosta Angel 2025

Deroncele-Acosta Angel, Norabuena-Figueroa Roger Pedro, Norabuena-Figueroa Emerson Damian. (2025). Women’s mental health in the doctoral context: Protective function of the psychological capital and academic motivation. Women’s Health. https://doi.org/10.1177/17455057251315318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Non-parametric Kruskal–Wallis comparison across study cycles in the 108-woman cross-sectional survey; the article reports significant differences for academic motivation (p-value = 0.008) and UAP (p-value = 0.001).

> "there are significant differences between the study cycles with academic motivation (p-value = 0.008) and UAP (p-value = 0.001). Both variables reached their highest peaks at the beginning and the end of the doctoral career, evidence of a curve in their behavior."
