## Evidence

### Zoanetti N 2013

Zoanetti N, Beaves M, Griffin P, Wallace E M. (2013). Fixed or mixed: a comparison of three, four and mixed-option multiple-choice tests in a Fetal Surveillance Education Program. BMC Medical Education 13:35. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-35

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Statistical comparison of the two fixed-option forms administered to FSEP practitioners (three-option test to 646, four-option to 763, booklets randomly distributed across sessions), reporting a paired t-test of facility across items showing the three-option form "was higher than the mean item facility on the four-option test by 5.7%" with t=-6.358, p<0.001.

> "the mean CTT item facility on the three-option test was higher than the mean item facility on the four-option test by 5.7%. This difference was significant when calculated via a paired sample t-test of item facility across all items (t=-6.358, df=59, p<0.001). A total of 46 of the 60 items became easier upon removal of the least attractive incorrect option."
