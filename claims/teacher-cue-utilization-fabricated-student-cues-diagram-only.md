## Evidence

### Janneke van de Pol 2021

Janneke van de Pol, Selia N. van den Boom-Muilenburg, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Exploring the relations between teachers' cue-utilization, monitoring and regulation of students' text learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09268-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of 125 think-aloud protocols, coded with a scheme of four categories and 27 subcategories (Krippendorff's alpha = 0.82), showed teachers "used fabri- cated student cues for 50% of the students", e.g. guessing gender or intelligence from the work.

> "Furthermore, as expected, teachers in the diagram-only condition used fabri- cated student cues for 50% of the students."

### Janneke van de Pol 2021 (2)

Janneke van de Pol, Selia N. van den Boom-Muilenburg, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Exploring the relations between teachers' cue-utilization, monitoring and regulation of students' text learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09268-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The think-aloud coding showed diagram-only condition cue use was dominated by performance cues: "teachers mainly used performance cues (78%)", with task cues and miscellaneous cues each at 11%.

> "In the diagram-only condition, teachers mainly used performance cues (78%)."
