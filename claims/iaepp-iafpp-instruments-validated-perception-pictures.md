## Evidence

### Zähringer 2024

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric validation with the classroom sample; both five-item instruments exceeded the stated thresholds, with "IaePP: αC = 0.87 [0.85, 0.89]" and "IafPP: αC = 0.82 [0.80, 0.85]" internal consistency.

> "In particular, internal consistencies (IaePP: αC = 0.87 [0.85, 0.89]; IafPP: αC = 0.82 [0.80, 0.85]) are well above of the threshold for group level measurements of 0.7 recommended by the European Federation of Psychological Associations [75]."
