## Evidence

### Claussen AH 2018

Claussen AH, Bitsko RH, Holbrook JR, Bloomfield J, Giordano K. (2018). Impact of Tourette Syndrome on School Measures in a Nationally Representative Sample. Journal of Developmental & Behavioral Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1097/DBP.0000000000000550

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted regression analysis of pooled 2007–2008 and 2011–2012 National Survey of Children's Health parent-report data (n = 129,353 children aged 6–17), comparing children currently with TS to those who never had TS. The fully adjusted prevalence ratio for IEP was 1.4 (95% CI 1.0–1.8, p = 0.03), showing "children with TS were more likely to have an IEP even when other conditions were taken into account."

> "Only the IEP status remained significant after adjusting for co-occurring conditions, i.e., children with TS were more likely to have an IEP even when other conditions were taken into account"
