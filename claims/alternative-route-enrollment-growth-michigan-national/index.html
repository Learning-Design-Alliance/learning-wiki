The pathways section, citing MDE Educator Workforce Data Report data, reports Michigan alternative enrollment rising from under 500 in 2017-18 to nearly 2,000 in 2019-20, with "just over 40%" of candidates in alternative programs by 2023; national NCES data show traditional enrollment down nearly 30% and alternative enrollment nearly doubled from 2012-2021.
