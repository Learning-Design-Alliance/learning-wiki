Post-eclipse online survey of 31 event organisers who used a disco ball projector; respondents estimated visitor engagement. The survey found "a majority (75-100%) of their visitors interacted with the disco ball display" for 41.9% of respondents; no effect size is printed.

> "Nearly half (41.9%) of respondents said that a majority (75-100%) of their visitors interacted with the disco ball display, while others reported lower engagement numbers."
