## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Immediately after practice, total time was significantly lower for both left- and right-PMd stimulation groups than for the RP-sham and IP-sham groups, with no difference between the two stimulation sites. [→ Taewon Kim 2021](#taewon-kim-2021)

## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2021

Taewon Kim, John J. Buchanan, Jessica A. Bernard and David L. Wright. (2021). Improving online and offline gain from repetitive practice using anodal tDCS at dorsal premotor cortex. npj Science of Learning 6:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00109-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the four-condition experiment (N=64 right-handed undergraduates), a 4 (Condition) × 2 (Test: BASE, IMM) mixed ANOVA on total time showed the stimulation groups significantly faster than sham at the immediate test, with printed effect size ηp² = 0.36. Post hoc means: L-PMd 1556 ms, R-PMd 1551 ms, RP-Sham 1909 ms, IP-Sham 1949 ms.

> "Immediately after practice, TT was similar for individuals exposed to anodal tDCS at either right or left PMd during RP which in turn was signi ﬁcantly lower than TT for individuals in the sham conditions, F(3,60) = 11.01, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.36."
