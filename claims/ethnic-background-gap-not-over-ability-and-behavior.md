## Evidence

### Webb 1993

Webb, N. M. (1993). Collaborative Group Versus Individual Assessment in Mathematics: Processes and Outcomes. Educational Assessment, 7(2), 131-152. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis in the whole-sample regression section: a t test comparing the combined White and Asian-American group with the combined Hispanic and African-American group on individual test means of .89 versus .55, followed by the regression finding of no additional variance explained.

> "Although ethnic background was significantly related to scores on the individual test (White and Asian-American students scored higher than Hispanic and African-American students, M = .89 and .55, respectively; t[51] 3.14, p < .01), ethnic background did not account for additional variance over and above ability and student behavior."
