## Evidence

### Tüzün Hande 2026

Tüzün Hande, Cebeci Fatma, Çetin Songül Bişkin. (2026). Medication safety in emergency departments: a mixed methods study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01615-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA and t-tests on MSCS subdimension scores. Table 2 reports η² = 0.06 for age and η² = 0.04 for experience, described as "small, medium, and large effect sizes" benchmarks; no significant differences in total scale score.

> "Statistically significant differences were observed only in the patient-centered medication management subdimension, where nurses aged 30–39 years and those with ≥ 5 years of experience reported higher scores (p < .05)."
