## Evidence

### Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan 2012

Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati & Jayakaran Mukundan. (2012). The Role of Cognitive Style in the Collocational Knowledge Development of Iranian EFL Learners through Input Flood Treatment. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p105

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing the field-independent (n=17) and field-dependent (n=15) subgroups of the input flood group, reported in Table 3. The article reports significance at "p= .000< .05" for both post-tests; no effect size is printed.

> "As Table 3 reveals, the field-independent learners significantly (p= .000< .05) outperformed the field-dependent ones in both immediate and delayed post-tests. Thus, the input-flood treatment was proved more beneficial for the field-independent learners."
