Descriptive statistics from expert scoring of 40 pre-service educators (24 PET, 16 CE) on the observation scale. The article reports "overall game playing competencies ... at a sufficient level (M = 3.22, SD = 0.39)", with Preparation (M = 3.32) rated Very Good and Evaluation (M = 3.03) lowest. Descriptive only; no inferential test on this pattern.
