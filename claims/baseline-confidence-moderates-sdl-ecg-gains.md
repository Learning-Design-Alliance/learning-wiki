## Evidence

### de Lima Marie Jane 2025

de Lima Marie Jane, Jamil Naser, Vazhuthakkadu Seema, Mosleh Sultan, Al-Nsair Nezam. (2025). Impact of self-directed e-learning on nurses' competency in arrhythmia interpretation in cardiology. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis relating pre-intervention self-reported confidence (4-point Likert scale) to knowledge gains: "somewhat confident" or "neutral" nurses improved significantly (p < 0.001); very confident nurses did not (p = 0.398).

> "nurses who reported being "somewhat confident" or "neutral" showed the most significant improvements after the intervention (p < 0.001). On the other hand, those who were "very confident" before the training showed only minimal improvement, and this was not statistically significant (p = 0.398)"
