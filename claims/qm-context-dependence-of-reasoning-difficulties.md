## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reasoning difficulties are context dependent, and question wording affects responses; explicitly mentioning a difficulty in a statement raises the percentage of students endorsing it. [→ Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016](#chandralekha-singh-and-emily-marshman-2016)

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2016

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2016). Review of Student Difficulties in Upper-Level Quantum Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020117)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review's methodological synthesis of the studies it covers. Illustrating example: about one out of ten students incorrectly claimed ĤΨ = EΨ is not true for all wavefunctions when generating an answer themselves, while more than one-third incorrectly agreed when explicitly evaluating a statement containing the incorrect expression.

> "In particular, even if only 5− 10% of the students show a certain type of difﬁculty in a particular context, it is likely that a higher percentage will display the same difﬁculty in a different context."
