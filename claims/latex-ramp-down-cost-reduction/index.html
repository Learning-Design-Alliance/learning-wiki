Paired survey time items (Block 3) probing LaTeX ramp-up cost at beginning versus end of term; Figure 5 plots the distributions. The quote carries the printed means, SDs, medians, and reduction percentage.

"At the beginning of the term the cohort reported a mean extra time of M= 87 min per assignment (SD = 63, median = 60); by the end of the term this had fallen to M= 47 min (SD = 48, median = 30), a mean reduction of≈46% ."
