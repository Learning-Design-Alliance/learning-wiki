## Evidence

### Shaddeau Angela 2015

Shaddeau Angela, Nimz Abigail, Sheeder Jeanelle, Tocce Kristina. (2015). Effect of novel patient interaction on students’ performance of pregnancy options counseling. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.29401

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome from the randomized trial: standardized patients, blinded to assignment, rated observed comfort during the OSCE encounter. The article reports "5% NPI vs. 18% control, p=0.006" on this standardized-patient-perceived comfort measure.

> "The standardized patients classified a greater proportion of students in the control group as appearing ‘somewhat uncomfortable’ with delivering pregnancy test results than the NPI group (5% NPI vs. 18% control, p=0.006)."
