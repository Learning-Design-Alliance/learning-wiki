q1 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative student comment (S1) on how students generally evaluated ChatGPT's answers, reported in the Qualitative Assessments section. The student found the answers useful but "insufficient when I ask for more detailed information".

"I find the answers given by ChatGPT useful. I believe the information he provided is correct. From what I have seen from the answers , they are short and simple. However, I think it will be insufficient when I ask for more detailed information."
