Passonneau 2017¶

Passonneau, R. J., McNamara, D., Muresan, S., & Perin, D. (2017). Preface: Special Issue on Multidisciplinary Approaches to AI and Education for Reading and Writing. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0158-8

The editors report, citing Passonneau et al., a manual content-model method derived from summaries by proficient individuals (a wise crowd); "The wise-crowed method correlates well with a rubric" and two automated NLP methods also correlate well. No coefficients are printed.

"The wise-crowed method correlates well with a rubric designed to rate summaries written by community college students. Two automated NLP methods to apply wise-crowd content assessment also correlate well with the educational rubric."
