## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The average change in LCTSR reasoning scores did not differ between conditions, though gains were statistically significant within each condition. [→ Jensen Jamie L. 2015](#jensen-jamie-l-2015)

## Evidence

### Jensen Jamie L. 2015

Jensen Jamie L., Kummer Tyler A., Godoy Patricia D. d. M. (2015). Improvements from a Flipped Classroom May Simply Be the Fruits of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0129

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison of the 24-point LCTSR in both sections found equal gains between conditions; paired-samples t tests showed significant within-condition gains in both (p < 0.001 in each). The original-method group's gain of 2.8 was statistically equivalent (p = 0.09).

> "Results of ANOVA show that the average change in reasoning was equal between conditions (MΔnonflipped = 2.07, MΔflipped = 1.57, F(1, 90) = 0.86, p = 0.36)."
