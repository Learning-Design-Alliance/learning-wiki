## Evidence

### Rae 2017

Rae, M. G., & McCarthy, M. (2017). The impact of vodcast utilisation upon student learning of Physiology by first year graduate to entry medicine students. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 17(2), 1-23. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v17i2.21125

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experimental design, using the EOY exam taken nearly seven months after module completion. The vodcast-access cohort scored significantly higher; no effect size is reported.

> "illustrate a significant improvement in performance for the 13-14 versus the 12-13 year group when performance was averaged across the 17 identical questions used for analysis (68.6 ± 4.8% in 12-13 vs 73.2 ± 4.1% in 13-14, P = 0.01; paired Student's t-test, n = 17)."
