## Evidence

### Burgess Annette 2014

Burgess Annette, Roberts Chris, Clark Tyler, Mossman Karyn. (2014). The social validity of a national assessment centre for selection into general practice training. BMC Medical Education 14:261. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0261-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic result from the post-NAC survey of 886 respondents. The authors interpret this as candidates learning about general practice "through the selection process as a form of situated learning", implying passive information is insufficient.

> "71/886 (8%) of respondents found their experience at the NAC provided them with insight and understanding of what is required to work in general practice, and that the experience of the selection process itself would be helpful for them to apply again in the future if they were unsuccessful."
