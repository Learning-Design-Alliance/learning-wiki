## Evidence

### Kölbel Benny 2024

Kölbel Benny, Ragnitz Julian, Schäle Kevin, Witzenhausen Moritz, Axt Steffen, Beltzer Christian. (2024). 3D vs. 2D-4 K: Performance and self-perception of laparoscopic novices in a randomized prospective teaching intervention using standard tasks and box trainers. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03515-5

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome of the randomized intervention: after crossover each group performed task 3 on the non-familiar system. Neither time (partial η2 = 0.01, p = 0.49) nor success rate (partial η2 = 0.03, p = 0.30) differed significantly between groups. The sample was not powered for task 3.

> "The intervention groups did not differ significantly regarding time for task 3, with Ft_task_3(1, 36) = 0.48, p = 0.49, partial η2 = 0.01, or success rate, with Fs_task_3(1, 36) = 1.11, p = 0.30, partial η2 = 0.03."
