Evidence¶

Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026¶

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Nicolas Leins, Thomas Kosch, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). LLM-Based Educational Simulation: Evaluating Temporal Student Persona Stability Across ADHD-Related Persona Specifications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06307 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Human-rater validation study: five M.Sc.-level psychologists, blind to persona condition, independently rated 20 narratives sampled from the Experiment I workday dataset, compared with three LLM raters. The article states this validates agreement on scoring generated text, not correspondence with lived experience of people with ADHD.

"Human inter-rater reliability was excellent (ICC(2,1) =.92,95%CI[ .85,. 96]), LLM reliability was good (ICC(2,1) =.87,95%CI[.55,.95]), and human–LLM rating convergence was excellent (ICC(2,1) =.95,95%CI[.57,.99])."
