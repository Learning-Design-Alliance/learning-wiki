# High response confidence is associated with shorter visit durations on both item questions and options

> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Visit duration on the question shows a significant main effect of confidence (d = 0.20), with low confidence related to longer visit durations. [→ P Klein 2020](#p-klein-2020)
`q3 i1` Visit duration on the options shows a significant main effect of confidence with small effect size d = 0.34. [→ P Klein 2020 (2)](#p-klein-2020-2)

## Evidence

### P Klein 2020

P Klein, A Lichtenberger, S Küchemann, S Becker, M Kekule, J Viiri, C Baadte, A Vaterlaus and J Kuhn. (2020). Visual attention while solving the test of understanding graphs in kinematics: An eye-tracking analysis. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab5f51](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab5f51)

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on visit duration on the question AOI with accuracy and confidence as factors, from the approx. 3000 paired responses. The printed effect size is d = 0.20 for the confidence main effect; accuracy had no significant impact.

> "For visit duration on the question, we found a statistically signiﬁcant main eﬀect of conﬁdence [ F (2, 2984) = 44 .6, p ≪ 10−20, small eﬀect size d = 0 .20], whereas the response accuracy had no signiﬁcant impact"

### P Klein 2020 (2)

P Klein, A Lichtenberger, S Küchemann, S Becker, M Kekule, J Viiri, C Baadte, A Vaterlaus and J Kuhn. (2020). Visual attention while solving the test of understanding graphs in kinematics: An eye-tracking analysis. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab5f51](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab5f51)

`q3 · i1`

Two-way ANOVA on visit duration on the options AOI. The printed effect size is d = 0.34 for the confidence main effect and no accuracy impact; low confidence is related to longer visit durations.

> "For visit duration on the options we found a signiﬁcant main eﬀect of conﬁdence with small eﬀect size ( d = 0 .34) whereas the accuracy had no impact on the visit duration on the options."
