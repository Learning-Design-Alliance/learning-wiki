Anishan V, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). Variable practice is superior to self-directed training for laparoscopic simulator training: a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10688-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized trial (n = 36, 18 per group), novices trained four basic skills tasks to proficiency; the variable group's mean time was 119 min (SD: 93) versus 182 min (SD: 46), "significantly faster for the basic skills during the intervention phase" (p = 0.015).
