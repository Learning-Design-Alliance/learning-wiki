`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the discussion of the observational study, the authors interpret the strategy descriptions against Ramsden's framework, stating "The descriptions of the strategies Lecturing, Demonstrating and Intervening are in accordance with the teaching perspectives viewing teaching as Telling or transmission of knowledge". This is the authors' interpretation, not a tested result.
