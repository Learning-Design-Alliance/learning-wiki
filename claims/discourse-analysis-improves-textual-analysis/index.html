Evidence¶

Kapanadze 2018¶

Dilek Ünveren Kapanadze. (2018). THE EFFECT OF USING DISCOURSE ANALYSIS METHOD ON IMPROVING COGNITIVE AND AFFECTIVE SKILLS IN LANGUAGE AND LITERATURE TEACHING. European Journal of Education Studies, Volume 4, Issue 5. https://www.oapub.org/edu

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same pretest-posttest control group quasi-experiment (20 experimental, 24 control eleventh-graders). The article states the achievement level of the experimental group in textual analysis "changed positively and on large scale"; no effect size is printed.

"In terms of textual analysis skill, as a result of t test conducted on pretest and post test scores of treatment and control groups; it was observed that arithmetic mean of control group was increased from 2,25 to 3,16 whereas it increased from 2,95 to 7,0 5 in experimental group."
