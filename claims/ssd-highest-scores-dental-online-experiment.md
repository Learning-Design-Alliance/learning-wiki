## Evidence

### Lim Jaeseo 2022

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Online randomized four-group experiment with 114 dental students assigned via Zoom breakout rooms. Pairwise comparisons show SSD significantly higher on total score, with "effect size Cohen’s d = 1.299" against SW and larger ds against LD and LW; similar significant differences held for verbatim and transfer items.

> "The SSD group had achieved significantly higher total scores than the SW, LD, and LW groups: t (78) = 3.42, P = 0.001, effect size Cohen’s d = 1.299; t (78) = 5.18, P < 0.001, d = 1.747; t (78) = 5.28, P < 0.001, d = 1.568, respectively."
