### van Wijk 2024

van Wijk, E. V., van Blankenstein, F. M., Donkers, J., Janse, R. J., Bustraan, J., Adelmeijer, L. G. M., Dubois, E. A., Dekker, F. W., & Langers, A. M. J. (2024). Does 'summative' count? The influence of the awarding of study credits on feedback use and test-taking motivation in medical progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10324-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-squared comparison of reasons for not using ProF among students who did not consult feedback: "15 (22%) versus 1 (2%)" cited importance for formative versus summative groups (Table 3).

> "Students who reported not to consult the feedback after the formative PT on the questionnaire more often considered the test as not important (15 (22%) versus 1 (2%), p: <0.001 for formative versus summative) (Table 3)."

### van Wijk 2024 (2)

van Wijk, E. V., van Blankenstein, F. M., Donkers, J., Janse, R. J., Bustraan, J., Adelmeijer, L. G. M., Dubois, E. A., Dekker, F. W., & Langers, A. M. J. (2024). Does 'summative' count? The influence of the awarding of study credits on feedback use and test-taking motivation in medical progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10324-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of reasons for not preparing: "27% of the students in the formative PT-group" versus 1% in the summative group stated the PT was not important (p < 0.001).

> "Regarding the reasons why students did not prepare for the PT, 27% of the students in the formative PT-group stated on the questionnaire that the PT was not important compared to 1% of the students in the summative PT-group (p < 0.001, Online Resource 9)."
