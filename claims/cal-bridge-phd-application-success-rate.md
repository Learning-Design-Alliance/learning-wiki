Descriptive program outcome reporting for the main Cal-Bridge program since 2014, covering scholars drawn from CSU and community college campuses. "72% of the scholars who applied (N=127) accepted to a PhD program" and 18% in MS programs planning PhD study; two scholars from the first cohort of 5 had received PhDs at writing.
