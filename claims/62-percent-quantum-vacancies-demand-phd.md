Observational analysis of the Quantum Computing Report jobs database, excluding academic and non-profit vacancies and limited to quantum companies. The study reviewed over 750 vacancies, finding "62 percent demand the candidates for a PhD degree", with higher-education requirements stronger in Europe than in the US and Canada.

> "We investigated over 750 vacancies currently available of which 62 percent demand the candidates for a PhD degree. Furthermore, the requirement of higher education for job vacancies is significantly stronger in Europe than in the US and Canada."
