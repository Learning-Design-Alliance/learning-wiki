## Evidence

### Romero 2019

Romero, M. (2019). Computers in Secondary Schools: Educational Games. In Encyclopedia of Education and Information Technologies. Springer. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The chapter reports, citing Seaborn & Fels (2015) and Toda, Valle, & Isotani (2017), that "gamification has showed mixed results" for learning performance. The review attributes this mixed evidence to gamification built on elements such as scores and leaderboards; no effect sizes are printed.

> "In terms of learning performance, gamification has showed mixed results (Seaborn & Fels, 2015), specially when the gamification is based in game elements such scores and leaderboards (Toda, Valle, & Isotani, 2017)."
