`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analyses in the same survey (Table 2, Models 1–4). The article reports non-significant interaction terms (p > 0.05) for these four traits; no effect sizes were printed, so the null is not an equivalence test.

> "the interaction terms between Neuroticism, Conscientiousness, Agreeableness, and Extraversion with academic stress were not significant (p > 0.05), indicating that these traits did not significantly moderate the relationship between academic stress and academic achievement."
