## Evidence

### Schmid 2023

Schmid, R., Pauli, C., & Petko, D. (2023). Examining the use of digital technology in schools with a school-wide approach to personalized learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10167-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative multiple-case analysis of School B, a small private school pursuing personalized learning since 1998. Interviews showed students "are relatively free in terms of when they go to the computer within the assignments," and teachers reported more flexibility and more student choice.

> "Type B uses digital technology selectively according to individual student preference. Especially in the first lesson and during the self-directed learning phases, the students can decide by themselves whether they want to use the computers for assignments."
