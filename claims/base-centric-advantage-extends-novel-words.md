`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Frequency analysis across both experiments (Fig 7), with words classified via the English Lexicon Project into high-frequency (43 words over 10 per million), low-frequency (38 words) and zero-frequency (32 words) categories.

> "The similar pattern of results obtained across the three frequency conditions shows that the memory advantage in the base-centric condition extends to low-frequency and novel words. This is consistent with the hypothesis that the base-centric morphological matrix is a useful tool for learning new words."
