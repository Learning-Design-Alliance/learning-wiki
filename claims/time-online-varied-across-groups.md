## Evidence

### Palmer Edward 2014

Palmer Edward, Devitt Peter. (2014). The assessment of a structured online formative assessment program: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on Year 2 time spent online with Group as predictor, followed by post hoc tests. The article reports Group B higher than A, C and D (p < 0.0001, p = 0.007, p < 0.0001) and no significant differences among A, C and D; no effect size is printed.

> "The ANOVA model demonstrated that there was a significant difference between the four groups from Year 2 in the time spent online (p < 0.0001). The mean time spent online by students was highest in Group B (median = 1045 minutes) and lowest in Group D (median = 560 minutes)."
