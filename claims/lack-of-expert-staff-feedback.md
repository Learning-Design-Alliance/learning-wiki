## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents perceived that being observed in clinical settings did not necessarily yield appropriate feedback, and that staff expert feedback was beneficial but rarely given. [→ Touchie Claire 2013](#touchie-claire-2013)

## Evidence

### Touchie Claire 2013

Touchie Claire, Humphrey-Murto Susan, Varpio Lara. (2013). Teaching and assessing procedural skills: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-69

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group direct observation and feedback theme; one resident said "I have never had a staff give me feedback at the bedside even if they are watching me", and another noted resident supervisors may avoid confrontational feedback. Residents wanted to be able to initiate staff feedback on the wards.

> "Participants perceived that when they were observed doing a procedure in a clinical setting, that they were not necessarily getting appropriate feedback."
