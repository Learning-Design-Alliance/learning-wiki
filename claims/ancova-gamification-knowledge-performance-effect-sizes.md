## Evidence

### Behmanesh Fereshteh 2025

Behmanesh Fereshteh, Gholamnia-Shirvani Zeinab, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Nikbakht Hossein-Ali, Nazmi Sana. (2025). Designing, implementing, and evaluating a gamification approach in childbirth training for midwifery students using computer and mobile application. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07370-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The ANCOVA model entered the baseline value of the dependent variable and group type. Table 6 reports "F 25.20" for knowledge and "77.48" for performance, adjusted R squared of 0.30 and 0.59, effect sizes of 0.23 and 0.54, and observed power of 0.99 and 1.00. Homogeneity of variances was confirmed via Levene's test with p greater than 0.05.

> "The ANCOVA results revealed significant differences in knowledge and performance scores between the two groups after controlling for baseline values (p < 0.001)."
