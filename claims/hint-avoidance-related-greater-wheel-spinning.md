### Kai 2018

Kai, S., Heffernan, C., Almeda, M. V., Heffernan, N., & Baker, R. S. (2018). Decision Tree Modeling of Wheel-Spinning and Productive Persistence in Skill Builders. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 10, No 1. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/210

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Finding from the J48 decision tree analysis of ASSISTments Skill Builder log data; Figure 4 shows the proportion of wheel-spinning is much lower once the minimum number of hints requested in any problem increases beyond 1.

> "Our findings also indicate that not requesting any hints in at least one problem was related to greater wheel-spinning."
