# Five-year-old children reveal metacognitive knowledge of self, others, universal properties of cognition, and task-specific strategies during peer interactions in mathematical measurement activities

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` During 16 measurement activities over 10 weeks, two 5-year-old children expressed metacognitive knowledge in all four coded categories (self, others, universal properties, task-specific strategies) through peer interactions. [→ Ebru Aydın 2022](#ebru-aydn-2022)

## Evidence

### Ebru Aydın 2022

Ebru Aydın, Çağlayan Dinçer. (2022). “I did it wrong, but i know it”: Young children’s metacognitive knowledge expressions during peer interactions in math activities. Thinking Skills and Creativity 45 (2022) 101104. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2022.101104

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative case study of two 5-year-old boys in a Istanbul kindergarten class; 16 measurement activities video-recorded over 10 weeks, 28 hours of transcripts coded with a four-category system. The study concludes "they reveal metacognitive knowledge of self, others, universal properties of cognition, and task-specific strategies."

> "The results of the research show that when children have opportunities to interact with their peers, they reveal metacognitive knowledge of self, others, universal properties of cognition, and task-specific strategies."
