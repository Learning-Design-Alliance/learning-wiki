`q2 · i0` · `review · r2`

Null result from the LCA of 891 cases: these elements showed no evidence of discriminating between the five classes. The review explains that in the sample typically a single practice was used (e.g., setting = formal, alignment to curriculum = yes).

> "The three peer assessment design elements that were not statistically significant at the 0.05 level and had very small effect sizes are alignment ( p = 1.000; R2 = 0.003), rewards (p = 0.130; R2 = 0.007), and se tting ( p = 0.210; R2 = 0.027)."
