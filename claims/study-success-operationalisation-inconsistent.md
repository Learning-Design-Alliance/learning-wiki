Content analysis of the 46 key publications' operationalised study success measures, tabulated in Table 1. The review reports counts of terms such as 'course completion' (n = 7), 'student at-risk' or 'dropout' (n = 14), and 'student performance' (n = 9).

> "Different conceptualisations of study success were provided in the articles, including the more precise descriptions, with positive factors of study success defined, for example, as 'course completion' (n = 7) and 'student retention' (n = 1) and negative factors utilising terms relating to attrition or dropout, such as 'student at-risk' or 'dropout' (n = 14)"
