## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mason 2018

Mason, A. J., & Bertram, C. A. (2018). Consideration of Learning Orientations as an Application of Achievement Goals in Evaluating Life Science Majors in Introductory Physics. University of Central Arkansas, Department of Physics and Astronomy. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010125)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Course GPA cross-tabulation of learning orientation by major (Table V, n = 218). Within-major orientation contrasts are all non-significant; within-orientation major contrasts all favor biology majors. No effect size printed.

> "A check of  learning orientations by choice of major showed no difference within each major by learning orientation (p > 0.05, all comparisons within respective major groups ), but a consistent difference in favor of biology majors over health science majors for each learning orientation (p < 0.05, all comparisons  within respective orientation groups)."
