q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis limited to students first enrolling in charters between 2014-15 and 2017-18, grouped by years of charter attendance and compared with their VCRs; third-year progress did not differ from TPS in either subject.

"Students in their first year of charter school attendance have 35 and 100 fewer days of learning progress than their TPS counterparts in reading and math, respectively."
