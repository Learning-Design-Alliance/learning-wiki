# Using the graspable multimedia system GraspLearn did not improve preschoolers' working memory capacity significantly more than the conventional multimedia system CLearn

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After seven days of ESL lessons, preschoolers taught with GraspLearn showed no significant WMC advantage over those taught with CLearn. [→ Chau et al. 2018](#chau-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Chau et al. 2018

Kien Tsong Chau, Zarina Samsudin, Wan Ahmad Jaafar Wan Yahaya. (2018). Graspable Multimedia: A Study of the Effect of a Multimedia System Embodied with Physical Artefacts on Working Memory Capacity of Preschoolers. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165766

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental factorial study of 248 preschoolers (128 GraspLearn, 120 CLearn) analysed with MANCOVA at alpha 0.05. Descriptive WMC means were nearly identical; the article reports a "Low difference of average of 0.01 signals the absence of expansion of the WMC."

> "For WMC scores, GraspLearn and CLearn modes were 59.76 (SD=4.09) and 59.44 (SD=4.08) respectively. Low difference of average of 0.01 signals the absence of expansion of the WMC."
