Morra 2019¶

Morra, S.; Bisagno, E.; Caviola, S.; Delfante, C.; Mammarella, I. C. (2019). Working Memory Capacity and the Development of Quantitative Central Conceptual Structures. Cognition and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2019.1636797

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Study I, a cross-sectional validation study of 779 children from preschool to grade 5, validated a measure of the whole number CCS. The article reports that "children's understanding of whole number advances by one developmental level per additional unit of working memory capacity." No effect size is printed.

"Study I, with 779 children from preschool to grade 5, validated a measure of the whole number CCS and found that children's understanding of whole number advances by one developmental level per additional unit of working memory capacity."
