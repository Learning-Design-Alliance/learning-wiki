## Evidence

### Smith Daniel T 2018

Smith Daniel T, Tiffin Paul A. (2018). Evaluating the validity of the selection measures used for the UK’s foundation medical training programme: a national cohort study. BMJ Open 2018;8:e021918. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Secondary correlational analysis of the three selection measures across the full cohort. The article reports Spearman's rho values of 0.30, 0.28 and 0.12, indicating the measures capture partly distinct information about applicants.

> "The three selection scores intercorrelated to some degree: the highest correlation being between EPM decile and SJT z-score (Spearman’s rho=0.30, N=14 128) and the lowest between educational achievements and SJT z-score (Spearman’s rho=0.12, n=14 131), with the correlation between EPM decile and educational achievements at rho=0.28 (n=14 131)."
