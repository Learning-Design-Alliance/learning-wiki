## Evidence

### Scherer 2016

Scherer, R., Jansen, M., Nilsen, T., Areepattamannil, S., & Marsh, H. W. (2016). The Quest for Comparability: Studying the Invariance of the Teachers' Sense of Self-Efficacy (TSES) Measure across Countries. PLoS ONE, 11(3), e0150829. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150829

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multi-group invariance testing (Table 4) across the 32-country sample: the scalar invariance ESEM model showed ΔCFI = –.071 relative to metric invariance, exceeding the applied ΔCFI ≤ .010 cut-off. The article states "only metric invariance could be achieved" for both approaches.

> "For both ESEM and CFA, only metric invariance could be achieved. Nevertheless, invariance levels beyond metric invariance are better achieved with ESEM within selected country clusters."
