Healthcare worker in-depth interviews from the qualitative study. One counselor stated that training "just once in the clinic" for the six-month follow-up "was not such a good idea" and advocated refresher training.

"They mentioned that the initial training offered to caregivers on measuring MUAC was often insufficient to ensure high accuracy and reliability of measurements throughout the six-month study period. They emphasized the importance of building measurement skills through retraining."
