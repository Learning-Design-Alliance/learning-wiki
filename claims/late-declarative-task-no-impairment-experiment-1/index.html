Evidence¶

Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard 2025¶

Mohan W. Gupta and Timothy C. Rickard. (2025). The Effects of Declarative Learning on Early and Late Motor Skill Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00386-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 1: 98 right-handed undergraduates randomly assigned to a cued-recall (n=50) or vowel-counting (n=48) group; 15-sequence trials with 10 s breaks, 17 training and 2 test trials, 4-minute intervening task. The post-rest gain comparison "we found no sig nificant effect: t(96) = 0.1, p = .91, d = .015, BF01 = 4.68". Bayes factor favored the null.

"Based on the mean RT difference between the last two training trials and the two test trials, we found no sig nificant effect: t(96) = 0.1, p = .91, d = .015, BF01 = 4.68"
