q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Whole-student-body survey at Sierra, where a 2019–20 needs assessment had found only 4% of students reported a caring adult; the brief reports improvement to 31% by 2022, "especially impressive during a global pandemic and school closures."

"The number of students who reported that they had a caring adult in their life who regularly checks on them improved from 4% to 31% over the course of 3 years (2019–2022)."
