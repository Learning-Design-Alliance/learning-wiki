Evidence¶

Altintas 2015¶

Altintas, E., & Ozdemir, A. S. (2015). Evaluating a Newly Developed Differentiation Approach in Terms of Student Achievement and Teachers' Opinions. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.4.2540

q2 · i? · causal · r1

A quasi-experimental comparison with non-gifted 6th graders in a public school (n = 60) on Tables and Graphics content. The differentiation-approach group outperformed the national-curriculum group on both current and enriched objective scores, with "a significant difference ... that favored the experimental group". No effect size was reported.

"However, after implementation, there was a significant difference between the non-gifted students' current (U = 1.00, p = .00 < .05) and enriched (U = .50, p = .01 < .05) scores in the control and experimental groups that favored the experimental group."
