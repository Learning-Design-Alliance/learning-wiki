Comparing major declaration, attrition, migration, and completion in physics with other STEM disciplines: A sign of inequitable physics culture?. Originally published 2021. `q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Drop rates by gender (Fig. 3b) from ten cohorts of institutional records show patterns largely mimicking overall rates, with large errors. The article supplements this with interviews with a few women in introductory physics courses, reported elsewhere, and states no statistical significance can be claimed.

> "although we cannot claim any statistical significance, interviews with a few women (reported elsewhere (Doucette and Singh, 2020c)) in introductory physics courses hint at the possibility that they may be more likely to decide not to major in physics"
