## Evidence

### Reiff FS 2024

Reiff FS, Bischoff C, Woelfler H, Roehling S. (2024). Influence of clinical expertise and practical experience on transfer accuracy in guided dental implant placement - an in vitro study. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10006-024-01269-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In vitro comparison of 72 implant positions across three expertise groups; mean 3D tip deviation was 0.76 mm (DOC), 0.68 mm (TEC), 0.89 mm (OFC). The article reports "no statistically significant differences" across groups.

> "Considering the group comparison, no statistically significant differences were found between the control group DOC and the test groups TEC (p = 0.709) and OFC (p = 0.381). The comparison between the two test groups also showed no statistically significant difference in terms of this TA parameter (p = 0.070)."

### Reiff FS 2024 (2)

Reiff FS, Bischoff C, Woelfler H, Roehling S. (2024). Influence of clinical expertise and practical experience on transfer accuracy in guided dental implant placement - an in vitro study. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10006-024-01269-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Region-wise comparison of 3D base deviation across the three operator groups; mean base deviation was 0.52 mm (DOC), 0.42 mm (TEC), 0.63 mm (OFC). The article prints only "no statistically significant difference was found for any group (p > 0.05)".

> "Comparing the 3D offset at implant base, the groups DOC and OFC achieved smaller deviations in the free-end situation than in the inter-dental gap, whereas TEC achieved larger deviations. However, no statistically significant difference was found for any group (p > 0.05)."
