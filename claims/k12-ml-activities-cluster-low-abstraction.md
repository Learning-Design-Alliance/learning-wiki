Systematic review of 126 K-12 ML activities coded against a taxonomy of algorithmic abstraction ranging from invisible use to independent creation. The review reports the lowest abstraction levels account for "almost 60% of all coded supervised learning entries"; no inferential statistics are printed.

> "invisible use, button interaction, and model deployment together account for almost 60% of all coded supervised learning entries, while genuinely open-ended creation tasks appear only twice in the entire corpus"
