## Evidence

### Holger Maus 2026

Holger Maus, Fabian Kieser, Stefan Petersen, Peter Wulff, and Paul Tschisgale. (2026). Developing an LLM-Based Feedback System Grounded in Evidence-Centered Design to Support Physics Problem Solving. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1142/s2661339526500125](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339526500125)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same voluntary evaluation of 38 Olympiad students with 64 problem-level ratings of the statement that the feedback appeared factually correct. The pooled estimate was "M= 4.4,SD= 1.0 by rating" and stable across weighting schemes (M = 4.6 by problem).

> "The collected ratings indicate that the LLM-generated feedback was generally perceived as highly correct (M= 4.4,SD= 1.0 by rating, see Fig. 4)."
