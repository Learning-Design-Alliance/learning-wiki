## Evidence

### Friederichs Hendrik 2016

Friederichs Hendrik, Brouwer Britta, Marschall Bernhard, Weissenstein Anne. (2016). Mastery learning improves students skills in inserting intravenous access: a pre-post-study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001055](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001055)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unannounced voluntary follow-up test taken by 106 of the 109 students eight days after the course. The article reports "The effect size (Cohen's d) in comparison to the mean score at baseline is 2.43" and that 74.5% passed, 20.8% scored 19, 2.8% scored 18 and 1.9% scored 17.

> "About one week (8 days) after the educational intervention, 106 students performed a follow-up test, in which they scored significantly less (20.06 [0.94] points) than in the previous test (post-test) (20.50 [0.56] points) (P < 0.001)."
