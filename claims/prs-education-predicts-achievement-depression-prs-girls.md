## Evidence

### López-López José A. 2021

López-López José A., Kwong Alex S.F., Washbrook Liz, Tilling Kate, Fazel Mina S., Pearson Rebecca M. (2021). Depressive symptoms and academic achievement in UK adolescents: a cross-lagged analysis with genetic covariates. Journal of Affective Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2021.01.091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariate results from fully adjusted RI-CLPM models in the ALSPAC cohort, with PRS computed using PRSice2 at a 0.05 p-value threshold for loci selection. The education PRS was positively associated with achievement in both sexes; sensitivity analyses showed this finding was largely robust to the loci-selection threshold. The printed values are unstandardised-style regression coefficients, not effect sizes.

> "The PRS for education showed positive associations with academic achievement in both sexes (M5 estimates 0.12, 0.08 to 0.16 in boys and 0.10, 0.06 to 0.14 for girls)."

### López-López José A. 2021 (2)

López-López José A., Kwong Alex S.F., Washbrook Liz, Tilling Kate, Fazel Mina S., Pearson Rebecca M. (2021). Depressive symptoms and academic achievement in UK adolescents: a cross-lagged analysis with genetic covariates. Journal of Affective Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2021.01.091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariate results from the fully adjusted RI-CLPM for girls. For boys there was no strong evidence of an association between the depression PRS and either outcome; in girls the depression PRS was negatively associated with achievement, which the authors read as pointing towards sex-specific directionalities. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Conversely, there was evidence of a negative association with academic achievement in girls (B=-0.04, 95% CI -0.07 to -0.01 in M5)."
