## Evidence

### Xiong Jian 2024

Xiong Jian, Dai Xiaoqin, Zhang Yuyang, Liu Xingchao, Zhou Xiyuan. (2024). Augmented reality for basic skills training in laparoscopic surgery: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11387-5

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of the eight qualitative studies (including ProMIS, eoSim, ART systems). The review reports participants with AR training "consistently demonstrated shorter task completion times, higher" GOALS and OSATS "scores, and fewer errors", and that AR "effectively differentiates between varying proficiency levels".

> "the evidence suggested that AR technology significantly shortens learning curves, enhances technical skills, and reduces cognitive load in laparoscopic training"
