## Evidence

### Devang Kulshreshtha 2022

Devang Kulshreshtha, Muhammad Shayan, Robert Belfer, Siva Reddy, Iulian Vlad Serban, Ekaterina Kochmar. (2022). Few-shot Question Generation for Personalized Feedback in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv. [doi:10.3233/faia220062](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia220062)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of feedback variants in the same Korbit ITS deployment (Table 6): non-question feedback reached 41.67 ± 19.72 first-attempt gain versus 22.58 for the minimal baseline; question-based feedback reached 66.67. The article reports the relative improvements as 18% and 26% respectively.

> "The Non-question Feedback model improves over minimal feedback baseline by 18%, because it additionally informs about correct and incorrect/missing components."
