# Gender differences in BUSS scores are inconsistent across studies: none for tenacity in either study, and for self-composure only in Study 1

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Study 1 found no significant gender difference in tenacity scores but a significant difference in self-composure scores, with females scoring higher (16.61 vs 16.08) at a trivial printed effect size (Eta Squared = .006). `q3 i0` Study 2 found no significant gender differences in either tenacity or self-composure scores, with negligible printed effect sizes (Eta Squared = .001 and .003). [→ Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020 (2)](#kannangara-chathurika-sewwandi-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020

Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi, Allen Rosie Elizabeth, Carson Jerome Francis, Khan Samia Zahraa Noor, Waugh Gill, Kandadi Kondal Reddy. (2020). Onwards and upwards: The development, piloting and validation of a new measure of academic tenacity- The Bolton Uni-Stride Scale (BUSS). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235157

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA in Study 1 (n=1117) comparing male and female BUSS scores. Tenacity showed no significant difference (p = .240); self-composure differed significantly (p < .05) with females higher, but the printed effect size was negligible (Eta Squared = .006).

> "A one-way ANOVA demonstrated that there were no significant differences in tenacity (factor 1) scores between males (28.23) and females (27.98), f(1, 1078) = 1.380, p = .240 (Eta Squared = .001). However, there was a significant difference in self-composure (factor 2) scores between male (16.08) and female (16.61) students, f(1, 1078) = 6.665, p = < .05, (Eta Squared = .006)."

### Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi 2020 (2)

Kannangara Chathurika Sewwandi, Allen Rosie Elizabeth, Carson Jerome Francis, Khan Samia Zahraa Noor, Waugh Gill, Kandadi Kondal Reddy. (2020). Onwards and upwards: The development, piloting and validation of a new measure of academic tenacity- The Bolton Uni-Stride Scale (BUSS). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235157

`q3 · i0` · `design · r3`

One-way ANOVA in Study 2 (n=340) comparing male and female BUSS scores on both factors. Neither contrast was significant (tenacity p = .257; self-composure p = .366), with negligible printed effect sizes (Eta Squared = .001 and Eta Squared = .003).

> "the tenacity score did not significantly differentiate between male participants (26.72) and female participants (26.46), f(1, 318) = .257, p = .257 (Eta Squared = .001). Unlike study 1, which demonstrated a significant gender difference in reported self-composure scores, study 2 found no significant differences, f(1, 318) = .819, p = .366, (Eta Squared = .003)."
