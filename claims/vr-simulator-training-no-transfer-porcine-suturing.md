The article attributes this to a prior single-blind randomized controlled trial (Vargas et al.) in which one group trained to proficiency on a Mimic dV-Trainer VR simulator and the other received simple orientation before porcine suturing; this is second-hand reporting, not a finding of the present study.

> "Surprisingly, no significant differences emerged in either time or GEARS scores, raising doubts about the real effectiveness of virtual training alone for technically complex tasks in biological environments."
