## Subclaims
`q3 i?` By the end of the lab quarter, nearly all interviewed students believed MLec was not beneficial for MLab performance, and surveyed students who had versus had not taken MLec did not differ significantly in agreement that MLec completers would earn a higher lab grade. ## Evidence

### Sato Brian K. 2017

Sato Brian K., Lee Amanda K., Alam Usman, Dang Jennifer V., Dacanay Samantha J., Morgado Pedro, Pirino Giorgia, Brunner Jo Ellen, Castillo Leanne A., Chan Valerie W., Sandholtz Judith H. (2017). What’s in a Prerequisite? A Mixed-Methods Approach to Identifying the Impact of a Prerequisite Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0260

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Online survey of students who completed MLab Winter 2015 (63% response rate) rating agreement with the statement that MLec completers would earn a higher lab grade, on a 5-point Likert scale; MLec takers (n = 34) and non-takers (n = 28) did not differ, p = 0.28. A majority gave neutral or negative responses. No effect size is printed.

> "Responses from students who did or did not take MLec were not significantly different (p = 0.28 by Kruskal-Wallis rank sum test)."
