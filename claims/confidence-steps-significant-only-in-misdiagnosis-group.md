# Misdiagnosis students showed significant confidence increases at Step 2 and decreases at Step 3, while correct-diagnosis students showed no significant confidence changes

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In the misdiagnosis group, confidence differed significantly between Steps 1–2 (P = .01, d = 0.36) and Steps 2–3 (P = .03, d = 0.31). [→ Kojima J 2025](#kojima-j-2025)
`q2 i0` In the correct diagnosis group, confidence did not differ significantly between Steps 1–2 (P = .93, d = 0.18) or Steps 2–3 (P = .21, d = 0.33); equivalence was not tested. [→ Kojima J 2025 (2)](#kojima-j-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Kojima J 2025

Kojima J, Uehara T, Ohira Y, Tsukamoto T, Shikino K, Yokokawa D, Li Y, Yanagita Y, Sato R, Ikusaka M. (2025). Potential for reducing confirmation bias using the OMP model “6-microskills” with verbalizing discordance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06869-6

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Sub-analysis: one-way repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni comparisons within the misdiagnosis group. Both contrasts were significant with small effects: "P =.01, Cohen’s d = 0.36" and "P =.03, Cohen’s d = 0.31".

> "there was a significant difference in confidence between Steps 1–2 (P =.01, Cohen’s d = 0.36, df = 75) and 2–3 (P =.03, Cohen’s d = 0.31, df = 75) in the misdiagnosis group"

### Kojima J 2025 (2)

Kojima J, Uehara T, Ohira Y, Tsukamoto T, Shikino K, Yokokawa D, Li Y, Yanagita Y, Sato R, Ikusaka M. (2025). Potential for reducing confirmation bias using the OMP model “6-microskills” with verbalizing discordance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06869-6

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Sub-analysis within the correct diagnosis group (n = 31) using one-way repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni comparisons. Neither contrast reached significance: "P =.93, Cohen’s d = 0.18" and "P =.21, Cohen’s d = 0.33"; equivalence was not tested.

> "while there was no significant difference in confidence between Steps 1–2 (P =.93, Cohen’s d = 0.18, df = 30) and 2–3 (P =.21, Cohen’s d = 0.33, df = 30) in the correct diagnosis group"
