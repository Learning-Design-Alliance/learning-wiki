Kuhfeld 2022¶

Subgroup analysis of the MAP Growth observational dataset comparing 2021-22 achievement with pre-pandemic norms. The brief reports "uneven impacts of the pandemic across racial/ethnic groups" with the largest declines for Hispanic, Black, and AIAN students.
