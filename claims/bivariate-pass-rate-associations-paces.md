## Evidence

### Unwin Emily 2018

Unwin Emily, Potts Henry W. W., Dacre Jane, Elder Andrew, Woolf Katherine. (2018). Passing MRCP (UK) PACES: a cross-sectional study examining the performance of doctors by sex and country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1178-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive bivariate analysis of 6820 candidates with complete data, of whom just under half (49%) passed at first attempt. The article prints chi-square statistics and pass-rate percentages for sex, ethnicity, PMQ region, GMC registration and centre location; no effect sizes are given, so impact is unquantified.

> "Passing candidates were statistically more likely to be female rather than male [56% vs. 42%; χ2(1) = 144, P < 0.001]; white rather than BME [65% vs. 38%; χ2(1) = 463, P < 0.001];"
