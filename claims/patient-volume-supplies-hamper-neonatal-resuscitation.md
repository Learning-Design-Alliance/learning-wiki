Myrnerts Höök Susanna, Abrahamsson Marielle, Namusoko Sarah, Byamugisha Josaphat, Bergström Anna. (2025). Midwives' knowledge and perspectives on neonatal resuscitation and survival before and after Helping Babies Breathe training: a qualitative study in Uganda. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-094012

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of individual interviews and focus group discussions with 45 midwives at Mulago National Referral Hospital, Uganda. Participants described only two working ambubags and waiting while others resuscitated, stating "things are not enough".

> "The confluence of high patient volumes and inadequate supplies poses significant challenges to the performance of MWs, thereby directly impacting neonatal resuscitation and neonatal survival rates."
