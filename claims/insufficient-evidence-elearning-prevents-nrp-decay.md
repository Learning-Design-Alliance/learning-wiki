### Spénard Sarah 2022

Spénard Sarah, Postlow Fabiana, Curran Vernon. (2022). E-learning use in the review of neonatal resuscitation program in physicians: a scoping review. Journal of Perinatology. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-022-01411-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review following PRISMA guidelines searched five databases until June 2020; among 593 abstracts, 38 full texts were assessed and five studies were included. The review concludes there is "not enough evidence to conclude that e-learning interventions can prevent" NRP decay.

> "There is not enough evidence to conclude that e-learning interventions can prevent neonatal resuscitation knowledge and performance decay in already-certified providers."
