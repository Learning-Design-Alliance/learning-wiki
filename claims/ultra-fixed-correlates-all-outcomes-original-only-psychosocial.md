Limeri, Lisa B. (2025). Intelligence in Context: A Context-specific Mindset Measure Better Predicts Outcomes for Science and Math Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0229

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent factor correlations from a survey of 1537 science and math undergraduates at six institutions, analyzed with confirmatory factor models. The "ULTrA fixed factor significantly (p < 0.01) correlated with all outcomes in this study (r ranged |0.10|–|0.33|)" while the original measure correlated only with psychosocial outcomes (r ranged |0.13|–|0.18|).

> "The ULTrA fixed factor significantly (p < 0.01) correlated with all outcomes in this study (r ranged |0.10|–|0.33|). In contrast, the original mindset measure significantly correlated with only the psychosocial outcomes (r ranged |0.13|–|0.18|) and did not significantly correlate with the academic outcomes"
