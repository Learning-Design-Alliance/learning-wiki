Robert A. Mason, Reinhard A. Schumacher and Marcel Adam Just. (2021). The neuroscience of advanced scientific concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00107-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-participant discriminative classification using the 120 most stable voxels per cross-validation fold, trained on 4 of 6 presentations and tested on the mean of the remaining two. Individual participant rank accuracies were above p < 0.001 chance for 8 of 10 participants and above p < 0.05 for the remaining two; excluding occipital voxels reduced mean accuracy to 0.75.
