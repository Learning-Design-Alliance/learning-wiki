## Evidence

### Susan Harrington and Charlott Sellberg 2026

Susan Harrington and Charlott Sellberg. (2026). 12 Heuristics for Learning Analytics in Simulation-Based Professional Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 130-142. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9141

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Think-aloud sessions with four experts (in gamification/LXD, HCI/diversity, nautical operations, and UX/usability) evaluated draft heuristics against paper prototypes in roughly 45-minute audio-recorded sessions. The article reports these three feedback themes and that "the heuristics were well received, with experts offering feedback to aid the design process".

> "Key findings from this phase included feedback around (1) clarity of language, (2) differentiating between usability and accessibility, and (3) meaningful interpretation of data."
