## Evidence

### Hu 2022

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with measurement uncertainty of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

From an in-person-only free-response question asking students to explain in their own words what an uncertainty relation between two observables means. The authors state these results support the findings of a previous study, and that only CQS 2.5 explicitly discussed the uncertainty principle for spin-1/2 systems.

> "some framed their answers in terms of the position -momentum uncertainty relation (commonly cited as 𝜎𝑥𝜎𝑝𝑥 ≥ ℏ 2), which is not related to two -state syste ms, rather than using the generalized uncertainty principle."
