## Evidence

### Krauthausen M 2026

Krauthausen M, Wingender L, Deyerl V, Hagen PE, Leutritz T, Simmenroth A. (2026). Rural admission quota in Germany: students’ attitudes, challenges and the need for support programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08776-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-sample tests for equality of proportions and independent t-tests compared study funding of 158 RDQ vs 201 Non-RDQ students. Group differences reached p = 0.004 (parental support), p < 0.001 (partner support, vocational employment), and weekly work hours during lectures 10.36 (4.62) vs 8.12 (5.26), p = 0.002.

> "RDQ students relied less on parental/relative, more on partner support, and were more likely employed in jobs requiring completed vocational training, whereas Non-RDQ students more often held typical student side jobs."
