# Teacher Big Five domains show their largest associations with burnout for emotional stability and conscientiousness, but none of the burnout effects are statistically significant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pooled burnout correlations were largest for emotional stability (r = .21) and conscientiousness (r = .19) but none reached statistical significance, based on only k = 5 to 6 effect sizes. [→ Lisa E. Kim 2019](#lisa-e-kim-2019)

## Evidence

### Lisa E. Kim 2019

Lisa E. Kim, Verena Jörg & Robert M. Klassen. (2019). A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Teacher Personality on Teacher Effectiveness and Burnout. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9458-2

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Burnout meta-analysis from the same random-effects synthesis of 25 studies; the burnout outcome drew only 5 to 6 effect sizes per domain. The article states "none of the effects were statistically significant" and that results should be approached with caution.

> "Nevertheless, the greatest effect sizes were for emotional stability (r =. 2 1 ,p > .05), conscientiousness (r =. 1 9 ,p > .05), extraversion (r =. 1 3 ,p >. 0 5 ) , agreeableness (r =. 1 3 ,p > .05), and lastly openness (r =. 0 4 ,p > .05)."
