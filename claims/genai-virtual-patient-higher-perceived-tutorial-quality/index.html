q2 · i? · causal · r1

Regression analysis of the 8-item Learner Perceptions Survey completed by 26 second-year students (13 per condition) before and after the tutorial. The time-by-condition interaction favored genAI only; the article reports "beta = 5.77, 95% CI [1.27, 10.27], t(48) = 2.58, p = .013" with no standardized effect size.

"Linear regression modeling of the learner perceptions survey data (formula: survey_total ~ time * condition) revealed a significant effect of time (pre- vs. post-tutorial) for the genAI condition only [beta = 5.77, 95% CI [1.27, 10.27], t(48) = 2.58, p = .013"
