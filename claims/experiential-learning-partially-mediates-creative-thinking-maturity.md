## Evidence

### Nasabi Narjes Alsadat 2026

Nasabi Narjes Alsadat, Yusefi Ali Reza, Bordbar Najmeh. (2026). The impact of creative thinking on organizational maturity and the mediating role of experiential learning among knowledge management professionals. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39905-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation modeling in AMOS on data from 505 participants, with demographic controls. The model showed good fit (χ²/df = 2.14, CFI = 0.943, TLI = 0.928, RMSEA = 0.061, SRMR = 0.046); the indirect effect was "significant (β = 0.35, p < 0.001)".

> "The indirect effect of creative thinking on organizational maturity via experiential learning was tested. The indirect effect was significant (β = 0.35, p < 0.001), and the direct effect remained significant, indicating partial mediation."
