Evidence¶

Ziden and Rahman 2013¶

Azidah Abu Ziden, Muhammad Faizal Abdul Rahman. (2013). The Effectiveness of Web-Based Multimedia Applications Simulation in Teaching and Learning. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085400

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental 2x2 factorial study in two Malaysian secondary schools: 40 experimental students taught with PowerPoint plus web-based virtual simulation, 40 controls taught with PowerPoint only. Experimental mean 49.50 (SD 9.20) versus control mean 26.05 (SD 9.38); the study reports "t = 14.01, df = 39, p = 0.000".

"The t-test results in Table 2 shows a significant difference (p ≤ 0.05) in mean scores between the experimental group and control group for their achievements in the pilgrimage topic where t = 14.01, df = 39, p = 0.000."
