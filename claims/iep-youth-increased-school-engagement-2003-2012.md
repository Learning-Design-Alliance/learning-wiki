The fact sheet reports, from the National Longitudinal Transition Study 2012 comparing 2003 to 2012, that "youth with an IEP have become more engaged in school and extracurricular activities," with little change in disciplinary outcomes. No effect sizes are printed.

> "In the past decade, youth with an IEP have become more engaged in school and extracurricular activities, but there was little change in grade retention, suspensions, and expulsions."
