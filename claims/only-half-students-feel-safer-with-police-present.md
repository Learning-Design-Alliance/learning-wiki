## Evidence

### Duchesneau 2023

Duchesneau, N., & Zapata, M. (2023). Creating Safer Schools: A Case Study. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641760

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Stakeholder surveys administered in both districts (55 students and 55 staff in TSD; 143 students and 74 staff in RSD) asked about perceptions of safety with police present. The survey found "only a moderate percentage of students (46% and 51%, respectively) agreed" they feel safer with police. No effect size was reported.

> "At both TSD and RSD, only a moderate percentage of students (46% and 51%, respectively) agreed that they feel safer in school when police are present."
