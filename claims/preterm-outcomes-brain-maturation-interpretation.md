Authors' interpretation in the Discussion section, not a tested result of this study; the article adds that "Social circumstances and nurturing also play an important role" and that better understanding requires future mediation and prevention studies.

> "The estimated adverse effects of preterm birth on educational achievement and cognitive performance, as demonstrated in several studies, are to some extent likely to be due to the incomplete maturation of the brain at birth and the current lack of treatment options to fully compensate for such effects."
