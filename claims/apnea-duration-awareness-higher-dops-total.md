## Evidence

### Tang Lijun 2025

Tang Lijun, Zhang Yi, Chen Lianhua, Li Jinbao, Huang Shiwei, Fang Bin, Huang Xiaojing, Huang Lina. (2025). Advancing anesthesiology trainee proficiency in airway management via simulation-based training: a non-hypoxic apnea duration approach. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19555

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental trial at Shanghai General Hospital; 30 trainees randomly allocated 1:1 (n = 15 per group) with blinded assessment. The intervention group receiving apnea duration training outperformed the conventional group on total DOPS, "(total score: 51.4 ± 4.4 vs. 43.0 ± 5.4, P < 0.001)". No effect size printed.

> "The non-hypoxic apnea duration application group demonstrated significantly superior performance in the modified DOPS assessment compared to the conventional training group (total score: 51.4 ± 4.4 vs. 43.0 ± 5.4, P < 0.001)"
