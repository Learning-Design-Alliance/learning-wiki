Description of the study design from the report's overview: a comparative outcomes and impact study using student-level data from five of nine consortium colleges. The report separately states that "because of time limitations, this study is not able to assess increases in employment and earnings in the longer term."

> "To examine the program’s impact, the study compares outcomes for students who completed a certification track course at Broward College with those for students who took an SCM course at the college in 2014 but did not participate in LINCS."
