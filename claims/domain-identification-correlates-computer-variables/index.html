Evidence¶

Koch 2008¶

Koch, S. C., Müller, S. M., & Sieverding, M. (2008). Women and computers. Effects of stereotype threat on attribution of failure. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2008.05.007

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Correlational analysis among participants showed "computer domain identiﬁcation was positively associated with computer ownership", self-efficacy, motivation, knowledge, and external attribution of failure, with correlations ranging from r = 0.26 to r = 0.65.

"Computer domain identiﬁcation was positively associated with computer ownership (r(85) = 0.36; p < 0.01), computer self-efﬁcacy (r(85) = 0.49; p < 0.01), computer-related intrinsic motivation (r(85) = 0.65; p < 0.01), practical computer knowledge (PRACOWI) (r(85) = 0.29; p < 0.01), and external attribution of failure (r(85) = 0.26, p < 0.05)."
