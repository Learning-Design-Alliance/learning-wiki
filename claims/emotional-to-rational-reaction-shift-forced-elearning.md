### Sarli Leopoldo 2022

Sarli Leopoldo, Artioli Giovanna, Bogotto Sonia, Labelli Elsa, Pittella Francesco, Guasconi Massimo, De Simone Rosangela, De Luca Enrico, Rossi Sandra, D'Apice Clelia. (2022). From classroom training to e-learning: a journey through the quality of learning life of nurse students in post-graduate education - A longitudinal qualitative study. Acta Bio Medica : Atenei Parmensis. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9534212/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Longitudinal qualitative study of 24 post-graduate health-profession students interviewed at T0 (May-June 2020) and 16 at T1 (January-February 2021), analyzed with Braun and Clarke thematic analysis. The first theme, "Reactions to change in educational life", showed this meaning shift, with sub-themes including moving "from not being part of the group to a more solitary idea of training".

> "The students' meanings of their lived experience changed from a first predominantly emotional reaction, in the forced transition from traditional teaching to e-learning, to a more rational, analytic and realistic one in the continuation of e-learning teaching."
