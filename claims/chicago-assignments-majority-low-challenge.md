# A majority of writing and mathematics assignments in 12 Chicago Annenberg schools fell into the lowest two challenge categories

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across grades three, six, and eight, most writing and math assignments provided no challenge or minimal challenge for authentic intellectual work. [→ Newmann 1998](#newmann-1998)

## Evidence

### Newmann 1998

Newmann, F. M., Lopez, G., & Bryk, A. S. (1998). The Quality of Intellectual Work in Chicago Schools: A Baseline Report. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/quality-intellectual-work-chicago-schools-baseline-report

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Documentary analysis of 349 assignments from 74 teachers in 12 Chicago Annenberg schools, spring 1997, scored against authentic intellectual work standards and plotted in Figure 11. The report states that "a majority of the writing and math assignments fell into the lowest two categories."

> "Nevertheless, across all three grade levels, a majority of the writing and math assignments fell into the lowest two categories, no challenge and minimal challenge."

### Newmann 1998 (2)

Newmann, F. M., Lopez, G., & Bryk, A. S. (1998). The Quality of Intellectual Work in Chicago Schools: A Baseline Report. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/quality-intellectual-work-chicago-schools-baseline-report

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same assignment analysis, the report reports that in third grade 43 percent of writing assignments and the same percentage of mathematics assignments provided no challenge, typically requiring fill-in-the-blank or routine algorithm application.

> "For example, in third grade 43 percent of the writing assignments fell into the no challenge category."
