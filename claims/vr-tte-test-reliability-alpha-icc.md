## Evidence

### Nielsen Martine S. 2022

Nielsen Martine S., Clausen Jesper H., Hoffmann-Petersen Joachim, Konge Lars, Nielsen Anders B. (2022). Can virtual-reality simulation ensure transthoracic echocardiography skills before trainees examine patients? International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.6321.8e5d

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal-structure analysis of the 36-participant test data using Cronbach's alpha; projections alone reached "Alpha = .97, 95% CI [.95, .99]" and the anatomy quiz was lowest at alpha = .81.

> "For the complete test content, including projections, estimated EF, diagnoses for both cases and score for the anatomical quiz, alpha = .88; 95%CI [.80, .94]."

### Nielsen Martine S. 2022 (2)

Nielsen Martine S., Clausen Jesper H., Hoffmann-Petersen Joachim, Konge Lars, Nielsen Anders B. (2022). Can virtual-reality simulation ensure transthoracic echocardiography skills before trainees examine patients? International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.6321.8e5d

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Intraclass correlation analysis of the same 36-participant dataset assessing performer consistency; the authors state "the risk of a performer achieving a high score through luck is very low".

> "The ICC on all parameters for both cases was r(35) = .81; 95%CI [.69, .89],  p <.001, which shows a relatively high consistency of the performer."
