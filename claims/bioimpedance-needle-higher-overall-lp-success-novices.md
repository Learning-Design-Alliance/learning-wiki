# Novice medical students using a bioimpedance needle achieve a higher overall lumbar puncture success rate on a lumbar phantom than those using a conventional spinal needle

## Evidence

### Lilja Helmiina 2024

Lilja Helmiina, Talvisara Maria, Eskola Vesa, Heikkilä Paula, Sievänen Harri, Palmu Sauli. (2024). Novice providers' success in performing lumbar puncture: a randomized controlled phantom study between a conventional spinal needle and a novel bioimpedance needle. BMC Medical Education 24:520. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05505-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled, partly blinded phantom trial in which 60 medical students with no prior LP experience were assigned 30 per group to conventional or bioimpedance needle. The reported contrast is "12 out of 30 participants (40%)" versus "27 out of 30 participants (90%)" successful (p < 0.001).

> "With the conventional spinal needle (group A), 12 out of 30 participants (40%) succeeded in performing the LP procedure, whereas with the bioimpedance needle (group B), 27 out of 30 participants (90%) were successful (p < 0.001)."
