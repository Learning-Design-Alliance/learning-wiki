Descriptive analysis of CSSS scale response distributions (Table 3); mean agreement for all staff was above 80 percent on Instructional Equity and close to 90 percent on Respect for Diversity, but strong agreement was under 50 percent on both.

> "However, less than half of the respondents in this sample strongly agreed on either scale (46% for Instructional Equity and 45% for Respect for Diversity), indicating room for improvement in these crucial aspects of school climate and student success."
