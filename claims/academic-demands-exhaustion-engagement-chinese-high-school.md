# Perceived academic demands are positively related to emotional exhaustion and negatively related to schoolwork engagement in Chinese high school students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After controlling for gender, SES, and school types, perceived academic demands positively predict emotional exhaustion (β = 0.41) and negatively relate to schoolwork engagement. [→ Teuber Ziwen 2021](#teuber-ziwen-2021)

## Evidence

### Teuber Ziwen 2021

Teuber Ziwen, Nussbeck Fridtjof W., Wild Elke. (2021). The Bright Side of Grit in Burnout-Prevention: Exploring Grit in the Context of Demands-Resources Model among Chinese High School Students. Child Psychiatry and Human Development, 52(3), 464–476. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01031-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation modeling in a self-report study of N = 1527 Chinese high school students, controlling for sex, SES, and school type, showed demands positively related to exhaustion; the article also reports demands negatively related to engagement. The model explained "32% of the variance in emotional exhaustion".

> "Exhaustion was positively related to academic demands (β = 0.41, p < 0.001)."
