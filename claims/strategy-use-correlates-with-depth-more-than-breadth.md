## Evidence

### Teng 2015

Teng, F. (2015). Assessing the Relationship between Vocabulary Learning Strategy Use and Vocabulary Knowledge. PASAA, 49, 39-64. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077911

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Two-tailed Pearson correlation analysis among strategy use, breadth of VK (M=42.31) and depth of VK (M=29.33) for the 145 participants, answering research question 2. All inter-correlations were statistically significant at the 0.01 level, with 'depth of VK has a higher correlation with strategy use ( r=0.77) than does the breadth of VK ( r=0.65)'.

> "Additionally, the depth of VK has a higher correlation with strategy use ( r=0.77) than does the breadth of VK ( r=0.65). This provided evidence for the second question that the depth of vocabulary knowledge has a hi gher correlation with their strategy use than the breadth of vocabulary knowledge."
