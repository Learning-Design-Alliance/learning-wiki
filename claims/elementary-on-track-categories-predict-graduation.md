# Elementary students with 90%+ attendance and GPA 3.0+ in grades 3-8 were more likely to graduate high school with a 3.0+ GPA than peers in other EOT categories

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a sample of 93,617 Chicago students in grades 3-8 (SY2010-11), On-Track students (GPA 3.0+, attendance 90%+) had higher four-year graduation rates and were more likely to graduate with a 3.0+ GPA than Intensive, Academic, or Attendance Support peers. [→ UChicago Consortium on School Research 2024](#uchicago-consortium-on-school-research-2024)

Consortium Elementary On-Track analysis (Seeskin, Massion, & Usher, 2022) of 93,617 CPS students in grades 3-8 from SY2010-11; the printed figure shows, for example, 72% of On-Track students versus 34% of Intensive Support students graduated with a 3.0+ GPA.
