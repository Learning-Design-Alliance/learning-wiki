## Evidence

### Paech Daniel 2022

Paech Daniel, Lehnen Nils, Lakghomi Asadeh, Schievelkamp Arndt, Gronemann Christian, Bode Felix J., Radbruch Alexander, Dorn Franziska. (2022). School of Thrombectomy—A 3-Step Approach to Perform Acute Stroke Treatment with Simulator Training and Virtual Supervision by Remote Streaming Support (RESS). Clinical Neuroradiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00062-022-01242-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study after each of the first 15 consecutive RESS-proctored thrombectomies per trainee (75 total). Means ± SD are printed per trainee for each step, e.g. extracranial catheterization (4.46 ± 0.81, 3.60 ± 0.71, 3.33 ± 0.94, 4.6 ± 0.49, 3.4 ± 0.71). A benefit of audio-video supervision "was seen during intracranial catheterization and the retrieval maneuver itself". No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, a high level of safety was reported by the trainees during the first 15 independently performed thrombectomies during all steps"
