## Evidence

### Hettiarachchi Manjula 2014

Hettiarachchi Manjula, Lakmal Fonseka Chathuranga, Gunasekara Priyanka, Jayasinghe Prasanjanie, Maduranga Dasun. (2014). How does the quality of life and the underlying biochemical indicators correlate with the performance in academic examinations in a group of medical students of Sri Lanka? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.22772

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Re-analysis of WHOQOL-BREF scores comparing the n=24 students with class passes (distinctions) against simple-pass students among the 54 who passed; the supporting data were not shown in the article, so domain-level statistics are unavailable.

> "It was evident that students who perform better at the examination had significantly higher QoL scores at each domains of study (data were not shown)."

### Hettiarachchi Manjula 2014 (2)

Hettiarachchi Manjula, Lakmal Fonseka Chathuranga, Gunasekara Priyanka, Jayasinghe Prasanjanie, Maduranga Dasun. (2014). How does the quality of life and the underlying biochemical indicators correlate with the performance in academic examinations in a group of medical students of Sri Lanka? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.22772

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-group pre/post comparison for the simple-pass subgroup; the Results report a significant total-score improvement (p<0.05) from 54.7±7.2 pre-exam to 61.4±7.0 post-exam.

> "Further, those who got a simple pass had significant improvement (p<0.05) in the total score at the post-exam assessment (61.4±7.0) when compared with their pre-exam QoL score (54.7±7.2)."
