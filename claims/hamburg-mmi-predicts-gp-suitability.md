## Evidence

### Knorr M 2018

Knorr M, Schwibbe A, Ehrhardt M, Lackamp J, Zimmermann S, Hampe W. (2018). Validity evidence for the Hamburg multiple mini-interview. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1208-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Study 2: suitability rated on a four-point scale by GPs and up to two practice-team members per student (two cases missing suitability ratings, n = 58). Spearman correlation "ρ = .42, p < .01" with 95% CI .17/.64, whose lower bound the authors call more trustworthy than the other correlations'.

> "However, MMI results correlated significantly with GP psychosocial competencies (ρ = .32, p < .05), GP suitability (ρ = .42, p < .01) and OSCE score (ρ = .23, p < .05)."

### Knorr M 2018 (2)

Knorr M, Schwibbe A, Ehrhardt M, Lackamp J, Zimmermann S, Hampe W. (2018). Validity evidence for the Hamburg multiple mini-interview. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1208-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robust regression (M estimator) for GP suitability: MMI b = 0.65 (SE 0.17, t = 3.77, p < .01, df = 53); gender and age non-significant (Table 5). No effect size printed.

> "The robust regression models for each of the three outcomes showed that the MMI remained a significant predictor when the control variables were part of the model."
