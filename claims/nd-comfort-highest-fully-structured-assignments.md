# Neurodivergent students' comfort is highest for fully structured assignments and lowest for assignments with no structure

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both ND and NT students reported most comfort with fully structured assignments and least with unstructured assignments, with ND students more comfortable at high structure and less comfortable without structure. [→ Cynthia Zastudil 2026](#cynthia-zastudil-2026)

## Evidence

### Cynthia Zastudil 2026

Cynthia Zastudil, Srishty Muthusekaran, Rayhona Nasimova, and Stephen MacNeil. (2026). “I can’t read your mind”: A Study of Neurodivergent Computing Students’ Experiences with Collaborative Active Learning. Proceedings of the 31st ACM Conference on Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education V. 1 (ITiCSE 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3803400.3809386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey distributions of comfort with assignment structure, reported as percentages of participants somewhat to very comfortable. ND students were most comfortable with "fully structured assignments (ND: 87.5%; NT: 65%)" and least comfortable with "no structure provided (ND: 16.67%; NT: 35%)".

> "They felt most comfortable with fully structured assignments (ND: 87.5%; NT: 65%), then assignments with outlines or guidelines provided (ND: 66.67%; NT: 60%), and lastly assignments with no structure provided (ND: 16.67%; NT: 35%)."
