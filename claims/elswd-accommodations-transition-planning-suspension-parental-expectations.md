Descriptive comparison of accommodations, transition planning, suspension, and parental expectations for ELSWDs versus other students with disabilities in the NLTS 2012. The study reports ELSWDs are "less likely than other SWDs to receive extra time on schoolwork" while meeting attendance is comparable and suspensions lower.
