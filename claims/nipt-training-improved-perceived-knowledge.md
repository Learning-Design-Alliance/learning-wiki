## Evidence

### Oxenford K 2017

Oxenford K, Daley R, Lewis C, Hill M, Chitty LS. (2017). Development and evaluation of training resources to prepare health professionals for counselling pregnant women about non-invasive prenatal testing for Down syndrome: a mixed methods study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1315-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study using the 6-item self-perceived knowledge scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.92) with Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of Q1 and Q3; the article reports "a statistically significant improvement in perceived knowledge about NIPT". No effect size was printed.

> "There was a statistically significant improvement in perceived knowledge about NIPT pre-training (Mdn = 1, IQR = 3) and post-training follow-up (Mdn = 8, IQR = 4, Z = 9.765, p < 0.001)"
