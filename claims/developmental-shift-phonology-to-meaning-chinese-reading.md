`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

MASEM moderator model with grade level (kindergarten k = 90; grades 1-2 k = 96; grades 3+ k = 102) predicting the five paths, kindergarten as reference; omnibus test significant, Δχ² (5) = 43.445, p < .001. In kindergarten, OA (β = .181) and PA (β = .201) exceeded MA and vocabulary.

> "As children transited to the following stage (from preliterate to learning-to-read and from learning-to-read to reading-to-learn), the effect of PA significantly decreased (Δβ = -.075, p < .001), while MA and vocabulary knowledge showed significantly stronger effects (Δβ = .07, p < .001 and Δβ = .08, p < .001 respectively). The unique effect of RAN and OA remained stable across grade levels"
