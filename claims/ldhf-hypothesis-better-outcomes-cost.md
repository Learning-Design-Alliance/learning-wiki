`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The article offers this as the authors' stated hypothesis motivating the trial, with support drawn from an earlier Ghana study by Jhpiego/Ghana Health Services that reported "early promising results including improved skills, health outcomes and cost effectiveness". No evidence for the hypothesis is tested in this protocol article itself.

> "The authors hypothesized that simulation-based LDHF/m-Mentoring will result in better satisfaction, knowledge and skill outcomes as well as will be more cost effective compared to a group-based approach among birth attendants in Kogi and Ebonyi states."
