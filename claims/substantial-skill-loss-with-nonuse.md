## Evidence

### Arthur 1998

Arthur, W., Jr., Bennett, W., Jr., Stanush, P. L., & McNelly, T. L. (1998). Factors That Influence Skill Decay and Retention: A Quantitative Review and Analysis. Human Performance, 11(1), 57-101. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327043hup1101_3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic synthesis of 189 independent data points from 53 articles on skill decay, using sample-weighted mean d with original-learning performance as the control score. The review reports "substantial skill loss with nonpractice or nonuse" across retention intervals.

> "Results indicate that there is substantial skill loss with nonpractice or nonuse, with the amount of skill loss ranging from an effect size (d) of -0.01 immediately after training to a d of -1.4 after more than 365 days of nonuse."
