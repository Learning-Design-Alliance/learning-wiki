q3 · i? · causal · r?

First-year results from a large-scale randomized controlled study of two comprehensive teacher induction programs; the report found no significant improvements in "student achievement during the first year of teaching." No effect size is printed on this page.

"Intensive mentoring and professional development for teachers just entering the profession did not increase teacher retention or generate any significant improvements in classroom practices or student achievement during the first year of teaching."
