## Evidence

### Aldarsouni Fayez G. 2024

Aldarsouni Fayez G., Alkhulaiwi Hani, Alruwaite Hosam, Alsubaie Shara, Abdulkareem Faye, Alsubaie Norah. (2024). Advanced trauma life support training outcomes in Saudi Arabia: a four-year multicenter analysis of influential characteristics and factors (2019-2023). Annals of Saudi Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5144/0256-4947.2024.394

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Level-of-participant analysis in the 603-participant cohort (Table 1); the intern subset (n=258) had a 43% failure rate (Table 3). No effect size is printed.

> "In examining the levels of participants, 50.2% of interns failed the course, representing a significantly larger proportion of failures compared to their successes of 38.5% (P=.003)."

### Aldarsouni Fayez G. 2024 (2)

Aldarsouni Fayez G., Alkhulaiwi Hani, Alruwaite Hosam, Alsubaie Shara, Abdulkareem Faye, Alsubaie Norah. (2024). Advanced trauma life support training outcomes in Saudi Arabia: a four-year multicenter analysis of influential characteristics and factors (2019-2023). Annals of Saudi Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5144/0256-4947.2024.394

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intern-subset analysis (Table 3, Figure 3) of 258 interns, with a 66.7% response rate on elective-rotation data. The association is observational; no effect size is printed.

> "Of those who passed, 57.1% had completed an elective in a trauma-related specialty, compared to 45.9% of those who failed (P=.049) (Table 3) (Figure 3)."
