## Evidence

### Tsai Pi-hua 2015

Tsai Pi-hua. (2015). Computer-Assisted Pronunciation Learning in a Collaborative Context: A Case Study in Taiwan. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077659

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative analysis of 90 Taiwanese college students (30 per group) rated by four blind raters on pronunciation, intonation, timing, and overall performance; the ANOVA on progress scores found "all p-values > 0.05" across the three mediation conditions. No effect size was printed.

> "a one-way ANOVA involving Time (pretest, posttest) and mediation type (self-access, collaboration and MP3) did not reveal any significance of mediation in the practice of Part I of Cinderella (with all p-values > 0.05)"
