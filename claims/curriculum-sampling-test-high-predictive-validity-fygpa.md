## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2018

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2018). Admission testing for higher education: A multi-cohort study on the validity of high-fidelity curriculum-sampling tests. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198746

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Observational multi-cohort study of applicants to a Dutch undergraduate psychology program (851, 823, and 654 applicants in 2013-2015) required to take admission tests. Aggregated correlations corrected for indirect range restriction show the reported "high for first year academic performance in terms of GPA (r¯c=.56)", a printed correlation effect size of rc = .56, with moderate validity for credits (r¯c=.42) and dropout (r¯c=−.32).

> "The validity of the literature-based curriculum-sampling test was consistent across cohorts and the aggregated operational validity was high for first year academic performance in terms of GPA (r¯c=.56) and moderate for obtained credits (r¯c=.42) and for dropout in the first year (r¯c=−.32)."
