## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' attitudes toward history lessons changed positively after the virtual museum application, with a statistically significant difference between pre-test and post-test scores. [→ Ulusoy 2010](#ulusoy-2010)

## Evidence

### Ulusoy 2010

Ulusoy, K. (2010). Open Education Students' Perspectives on Using Virtual Museums Application in Teaching History Subjects. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 11(4). https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/tojde/issue/16922/176560

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pre-test/post-test experimental study with 20 open education faculty students found a statistically significant improvement in attitudes toward history lessons after a virtual museum application, with pre-test mean "68,15" and post-test mean "84.75".

> "a differentiation at a statistical significant level can be seen between the pre -test and post -test score (t18=2,417, p<0,000). The mean of attitude pre -test scores of the application students is ( =68,15) and the mean of attitude post -test scores is ( =84.75)."
