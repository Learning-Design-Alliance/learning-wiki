`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Faculty self-report from the interview study; simulation described as an intentional strategy with opportunities to observe, prompt, and assess multiple soft skills in an integrated manner.

> "But I rely heavily on simulation; I think we are able to structure it, plan very specific objectives, and even define which soft skills we want to work on in a given scenario."
