## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Professionals with different expertise levels make RBP assessments differently: less experienced monitors try to interpret protocol language, whereas experienced professionals develop intuition and link mental images to scales. [→ Alina Striner and Jennifer Preece 2018](#alina-striner-and-jennifer-preece-2018)

## Evidence

### Alina Striner and Jennifer Preece 2018

Alina Striner and Jennifer Preece. (2018). Appraising Human Impact on Watersheds: The Feasibility of Training Citizen Scientists to make Qualitative Judgments. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1805.01894](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1805.01894)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Findings from 5 in-depth semi-structured interviews with professional monitors (2 to 23 years of experience), each about 1.5 hours. Interviewees reported linking "mental images" from experience to protocol scales, and one protocol developer said they could evaluate a stream "without even scoring it."

> "Two interviewees, a senior biologist and ecologist, said they linked “mental images” from their experiences to the protocol scales."
