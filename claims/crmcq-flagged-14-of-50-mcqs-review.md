Luetsch Karen, Burrows Judith. (2016). Certainty rating in pre-and post-tests of study modules in an online clinical pharmacy course - A pilot study to evaluate teaching and learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0783-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of individual CRMCQ results across the five modules against four review parameters defined by the authors. The review flagged 14 of 50 MCQs, and the article states this led to changes in the course material and/or MCQs for the following year.

> "Applying these parameters to CRMCQ results flagged 14 out of 50 deployed for a review in terms of MCQ formulation and the delivery of examined content, with a) occurring 8 times, b) once, c) once and d) 4 times."
