### Zhang Danqing 2022

Zhang Danqing, Hong Jintao, Chen Sitong, Liu Yang. (2022). Associations of physical activity with academic achievement and academic burden in Chinese children and adolescents: do gender and school grade matter? BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-13886-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive characteristics of the cross-sectional sample of 2653 students: "only 10.7% of study participants met the PA guidelines". Table 2 reports more boys met the guideline than girls (13.8% vs. 7.8%, p < 0.001) and prevalence decreased from primary (19.3%) to middle (9.6%) to high school (6.0%).

> "In total, 2653 children and adolescents (1359 girls, 51.2%) were included, with a mean age of 13.0 years (Standard Deviation, SD = 2.4). Over 68.0% of children and adolescents reported above-average AA and only 9.9% of children and adolescents reported no AB. Moreover, only 10.7% of study participants met the PA guidelines."
