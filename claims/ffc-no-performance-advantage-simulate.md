## Evidence

### Chahal Baldev 2023

Chahal Baldev, Aydin Abdullatif, Ahmed Kamran. (2023). Virtual reality vs. physical models in surgical skills training. An update of the evidence. Current Opinion in Urology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MOU.0000000000001145

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The SIMULATE international prospective trial examined transfer of ureteroscopy skills from URO Mentor VR, Advanced Scope Trainer, Uro-Scopic Trainer and fresh frozen cadavers to the operating room, finding "no statistically significant difference in performances between FFC and non-FFC cohorts".

> "The study raised the issue of cost-effectiveness of FFCs, as there was no statistically significant difference in performances between FFC and non-FFC cohorts in the operative setting"
