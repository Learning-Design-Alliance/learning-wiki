## Evidence

### Østdal TB 2024

Østdal TB, Tang DHY, Olsen RG, Olsen LM, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). 3D monitors improve performance on the HUGO™ RAS system: a randomised trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11615040/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Visual and physical discomfort questionnaire (symptoms rated 1–5) administered after each session and compared with the Mann–Whitney U test. Difficulty focusing medians were 2 (2–3) vs 1 (1–1) in session 1, p = 0.001; "None of the other side effects (dizziness, eye strain, headache, or nausea) significantly differed between the two groups in either session".

> "the Visual Discomfort questionnaire demonstrated that the intervention (3D) group experienced significantly more difficulty focusing than the control (2D) group (p = 0.001) during the first session of the study but not during the second session (p = 0.07)"
