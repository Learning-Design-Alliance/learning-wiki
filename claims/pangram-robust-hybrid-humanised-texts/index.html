q2 · i? · design · r2

Same classification analysis for the comparison tools. The article reports Copyleaks at 30.0% (hybrid) and 22.5% (humanised), and Turnitin at 60.0% declining to 50.0% on humanised texts; no effect sizes are printed.

"For Hybrid papers, GPTZero classified all 40 papers as False Negatives with an accuracy of 0.0%. Its performance on Humanised papers was nearly identical, with a single paper correctly identified (2.5% accuracy)."
