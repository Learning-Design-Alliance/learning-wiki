`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Newmann, Bryk, and Nagaoka analyzed about 2,000 assignments and standardized test scores from up to 2,100 students per year across 12 to 18 under-resourced Chicago elementary schools over three years. They found "one-year student gains on the ITBS were 20 percent greater than the national average" in authentic-assignment classrooms.

> "Controlling for student race, socioeconomic status, gender, and prior achievement, this study found that in classrooms with authentic assignments, one-year student gains on the ITBS were 20 percent greater than the national average in both reading and mathematics."
