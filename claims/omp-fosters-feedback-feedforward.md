## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Tutors described how following the OMP structure facilitated more specific and timely positive feedback and feedforward, and served as a reminder not to overlook positive feedback. [→ Rothlind E 2025](#rothlind-e-2025)
`q2 i?` Many tutors still described difficulties verbalising feedforward, citing perceived limited mandate during short rotations and unease about critiquing students. [→ Rothlind E 2025](#rothlind-e-2025)

## Evidence

### Rothlind E 2025

Rothlind E, Salminen H, Rolfhamre K, Karlgren K, Toth-Pal E. (2025). Adapting the one minute preceptor model to a Swedish context – clinical tutors’ experiences of its usage in primary health care. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2025.2508370

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of the follow-up group interviews: "discussions on how adhering to the OMP structure and giving students time to outline their reasoning could facilitate both positive feedback and feedforward, mainly by enabling it to be specific and timely." Informants also reported barriers of limited mandate and unease about pointing out errors.
