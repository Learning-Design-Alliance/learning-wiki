## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No significant ROI effects were found when initial-encoding activation of Test 1-correct trials was partitioned by Test 2 accuracy, ruling out encoding as the source of the retrieval effect. [→ Liu Xiaonan L. 2014](#liu-xiaonan-l-2014)

## Evidence

### Liu Xiaonan L. 2014

Liu Xiaonan L., Liang Peipeng, Li Kuncheng, Reder Lynne M. (2014). Brain Correlates of Testing Effect. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Control ROI analysis of initial-study activation for all Test 1-correct trials, partitioned by Test 2 accuracy. The article reports "no significant effects found in any of the six ROIs (all t's<1)", so the Test 1–Test 2 correlation is not attributable to the preceding study phase.

> "There were no significant effects found in any of the six ROIs (all t's<1) based on the first study phase prior to Test 1, making it very unlikely that the correlation between activation at Test 1 and accuracy at Test 2 can be attributed to the study phase preceding Test 1."
