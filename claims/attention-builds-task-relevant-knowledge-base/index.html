Evidence¶

Aydinbek C 2022¶

Aydinbek C. (2022). Contributions of cognitive theory to the problem of automatization of grammatical structures in teaching foreign language. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2022.4230

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review quotes Logan (1997) on obligatory encoding: attention to objects and events in the course of performing a task causes a task-relevant knowledge base to be built up in memory, and the intention to learn has no effect except that it guarantees attention. This is a theoretical argument reported in the review, with no effect size printed.

"Learning and storing seem to be a side effect of attending"
