## Evidence

### Scott Secrest and Ingrid Novodvorsky 2005

Scott Secrest and Ingrid Novodvorsky. (2005). Identifying Student Difficulties with Understanding Induced EMF. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0501093](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0501093)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview demonstration in which a magnet was held stationary while the radius of a loop around it was decreased. "only a few correctly stated that it was because the area was being decreased"; others cited stronger force or field at closer distances.

> "Again, most students stated that a current was induced in the loop. However, only a few correctly stated that it was because the area was being decreased."
