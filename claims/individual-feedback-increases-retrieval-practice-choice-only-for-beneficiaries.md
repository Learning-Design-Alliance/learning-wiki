# Individual performance feedback increased retrieval practice choice only among students who had actually benefited from retrieval practice (Experiment 1)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Retrieval practice decisions after feedback depended on which strategy the student benefited from: a significant group × decision interaction (F(2, 57) = 8.42, p < .001, ηp2 = .23). [→ Luotong Hui 2021](#luotong-hui-2021)
`q3 i?` The retrieval-benefit group significantly increased retrieval practice choice after feedback, the same-score group showed no difference, and the restudy-benefit group significantly reduced retrieval practice choice. [→ Luotong Hui 2021 (2)](#luotong-hui-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Luotong Hui 2021

Luotong Hui, Anique B. H. de Bruin, Jeroen Donkers, & Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2021). Does Individual Performance Feedback Increase the Use of Retrieval Practice? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 1 repeated measures ANOVA on retrieval practice choice before and after individual feedback, grouping participants by which strategy benefited them. The "interaction effect between the groups and retrieval practice decisions" was statistically significant, ηp2 = .23.

> "we found an interaction effect between the groups and retrieval practice decisions that was statistically significant (F(2, 57) = 8.42, p <. 0 0 1 ,ηp2 = .23)"
