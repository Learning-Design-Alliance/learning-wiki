Evidence¶

Chang Peng Kee 2013¶

Chang Peng Kee, Kamisah Osman & Fauziah Ahmad. (2013). Challenge in Enhancing the Teaching and Learning of Variable Measurements in Quantitative Research. International Education Studies, 6(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n6p15

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental action research with 143 communication undergraduates split into two intervention groups; General Linear Model analysis of pre/post subjective-section scores. The article reports a "significant increase in understanding over all of the subjective section" with F (1, 141) = 100.838, p = .000; no effect size is printed.

"Value of difference test F (1, 141) = 100.838, p = .000 proving there was a significant increase in understanding over all of the subjective section."
