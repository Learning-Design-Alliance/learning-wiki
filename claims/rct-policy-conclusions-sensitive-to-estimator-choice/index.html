Peter Z. Schochet 2009¶

Peter Z. Schochet. (2009). Technical Methods Report: The Estimation of Average Treatment Effects for Clustered RCTs of Education Interventions. Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Empirical comparison of standard errors across the considered estimators in the five clustered education RCTs. The report finds that "standard errors sometimes differ", and frames this as a potential sensitivity of policy conclusions to estimator choice; no specific standard error values are printed in the available text.

"However, standard errors sometimes differ, suggesting that policy conclusions from RCTs could be sensitive to the choice of estimator."
