# Seventh-grade students taught social studies with the ABSL approach scored significantly higher on post-test academic achievement than students in two traditionally taught control groups, with a large effect size

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Post-test academic achievement mean scores of the experimental group were statistically higher than both control groups after an 8-week ABSL implementation. [→ Akbaş 2019](#akbas-2019)
`q3 i3` The effect size of the ABSL implementation on post-test achievement was large (η² = 0.30). [→ Akbaş 2019](#akbas-2019)

## Evidence

### Akbaş 2019

Akbaş, Y.; Şahin, İ. F.; Meral, E. (2019). Implementing Argumentation-Based Science Learning Approach in Social Studies: Academic Achievement and Students' Views. Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 9(1), 209-245. https://doi.org/10.33403/rigeo.529139

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 94 seventh graders (EG n=33, CG1 n=30, CG2 n=31) taught a Population in Our Country unit for 8 weeks. One-way ANOVA on PCAAT post-test scores gave "F (2, 91)  = 20.342, p = 0.00" with "η² = 0.30", described as a large effect size.

> "Results of PCAAT Post -test One -Way ANOVA revealed statistically significant difference between groups, [F (2, 91)  = 20.342, p = 0.00]. The effect size value (Eta squared) was calculated as η² = 0.30 and this value indicated a large effect size."

### Akbaş 2019 (2)

Akbaş, Y.; Şahin, İ. F.; Meral, E. (2019). Implementing Argumentation-Based Science Learning Approach in Social Studies: Academic Achievement and Students' Views. Review of International Geographical Education Online (RIGEO), 9(1), 209-245. https://doi.org/10.33403/rigeo.529139

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tukey multiple-comparison post-hoc test on the same post-test data. Mean differences of 18.479 (EG-CG1) and 18.148 (EG-CG2) were significant at p=.00, both "in favor of EG"; CG1 and CG2 did not differ significantly.

> "We examined the results of multiple comparison Tukey test between the experimental and control groups, verified that the significant difference between EG and CG1, EG and CG2 in the final test were in favor of EG. There was no statistical difference between C G1 and CG2."
