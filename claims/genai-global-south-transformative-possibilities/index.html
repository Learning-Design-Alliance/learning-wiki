The review's synthesis of 75 papers (2022-2025) on GenAI perceptions in Global South higher education reports that "GenAI offers transformative possibilities for personalised learning, research support, and administrative efficiency". This is the review's narrative synthesis, not a pooled effect estimate.

"The analysis reveals that GenAI offers transformative possibilities for personalised learning, research support, and administrative efficiency in higher education across the Global South"
