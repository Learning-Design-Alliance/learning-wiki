## Evidence

### Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay 2015

Nergiz Ercil Cagiltay, Erol Ozcelik, Nese Sahin Ozcelik. (2015). The effect of competition on learning in games. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on the Turkish IMMS (36 items, four ARCS subscales) administered after game play; the competition group "had higher motivation than ones in the control group" (M = 136.26 vs 129.76, t(140) = 2.09, p = .04).

> "It was found that the participants in the competition group ( M ¼ 136.26, SD ¼ 18.94) had higher motivation than ones in the control group (M ¼ 129.76, SD ¼ 18.03), t(140) ¼/C0 2.09, p ¼ .04."
