# Medical students trained with a gamified VR cricothyrotomy simulation achieved significantly higher total procedural-step scores than students who watched an educational video

## Evidence

### Speck I 2024

Speck I, Merk A, Burkhardt V, O Flayyih, Huber C, Widder A, Everad F, Offergeld C. (2024). Virtual reality cricothyrotomy - a case-control study on gamification in emergency education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05133-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized case-control study of 149 fourth-year medical students assigned to VR cricothyrotomy training or an educational video, tested on a plastic puncture cricothyrotomy model two days after intervention. The study group scored "8.9 ± 0.4 vs. control group: 8.5 ± 0.9" of 9 points, with p = 0.04; no effect size is printed.

> "The study group reached a higher total score in the cricothyrotomy than the control group (study group: 8.9 ± 0.4 vs. control group: 8.5 ± 0.9). The difference reached statistical significance (p = 0.04, Fig. 5)."
