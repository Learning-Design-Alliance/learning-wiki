Evidence¶

Khumsikiew et al. 2015¶

Jeerisuda Khumsikiew, Sisira Donsamak, and Manit Saeteaw. (2015). A Model of Small-Group Problem-Based Learning In Pharmacy Education: Teaching in the Clinical Environment. The IAFOR Journal of Education, Volume III - Issue II - Summer 2015. https://iafor.org/journals

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The article reports, citing Galvao, Silva, Neiva, Ribeiro & Pereira (2014), a meta-analysis of problem-based learning in pharmaceutical education; the odds ratios and confidence intervals are quoted as printed in this article's discussion, not independently verified.

"PBL students performed better on midterm examinations (odds ratio [OR]=1.46;95%CI : 1.16,1.89) and final examinations (OR =1.60; 95%CI:1.06,2.43) compared with students in the traditional learning groups. However, no difference was found between the groups in the subjective evaluations."
