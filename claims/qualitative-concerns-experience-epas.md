Qualitative feedback from optional survey comments was analyzed using a coding framework with responses categorized into themes. One participant noted complex endodontic treatment "is not expected of a newly licensed dentist"; another noted communication "requires innate talent".

> "Concerns raised mostly included the perceived lack of experience for certain tasks. For example, one participant shared that being able to perform complex endodontic treatment (EPA 8) is not expected of a newly licensed dentist, only the treatment of simple cases is expected."
