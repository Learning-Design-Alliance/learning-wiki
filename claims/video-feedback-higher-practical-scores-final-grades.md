## Evidence

### Donkin Rebecca 2019

Donkin Rebecca, Askew Elizabeth, Stevenson Hollie. (2019). Video feedback and e-Learning enhances laboratory skills and engagement in medical laboratory science students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1745-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 28 first-year histology students allocated to video (n=14) or control (n=14) groups, assessed by two histology experts on embedding and microtomy techniques. The video group scored "82.4%, ± SD 8.66" versus "55.6% ± SD 24.46" for controls (P value 0.01).

> "Students who participated in the video recording of the histology techniques (video group) in 2017 had an improved summative practical assessment (82.4%, ± SD 8.66) and showed a significant increase in final grade (75.6%, ± SD 12.74), compared to those in the 'control' group (55.6% ± SD 24.46; P value 0.01)."

### Donkin Rebecca 2019 (2)

Donkin Rebecca, Askew Elizabeth, Stevenson Hollie. (2019). Video feedback and e-Learning enhances laboratory skills and engagement in medical laboratory science students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1745-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cohort comparison of the 2017 video group against the 2016 cohort on practical assessment and final grade, showing significant improvements of "9.5% for the practical assessment (P value 0.03)" and "9.8% for the final grade (P value 0.03)".

> "In comparison to the 2016 cohort, the 2017 'video' group significantly improved on average by 9.5% for the practical assessment (P value 0.03) and 9.8% for the final grade (P value 0.03)."
