### Latifa Jackson 2020

Latifa Jackson, Heriberto Acosta Maestre. (2020). The Data Science Fire Next Time: Innovative strategies for mentoring in data science. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3394486.3411077](https://doi.org/10.1145/3394486.3411077)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey of attitudes toward computer science among 2019 BPDM participants. Pretest mean 4.40 (N=26) versus posttest 4.35 on a possible 5; the authors report the scores "did not significantly change" and attribute this to already-high pretest enthusiasm. No effect size printed.

> "Interestingly, we found that actual student attitudes towards computer sciences (x Pretest=4.40 versus x Posttest= 4.35) did not significantly change over the course of the workshop, although we note that these mean scores were quite high in our pretest population (N=26, x Pretest= 4.40, SD= 0.52477, SE Mean=0.10292) out of a possible 5 score."
