### Vyshedskiy 2024

Vyshedskiy, A., Venkatesh, R., Khokhlovich, E. & Satik, D. (2024). Three mechanisms of language comprehension are revealed through cluster analysis of individuals with language deficits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00284-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article reports this comparison from its recent study comparing typical children (~5 years) with autistic adolescents (~18 years); the success with canonical instructions is attributed by the authors to routinization through extensive Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy. No effect size is printed.

> "In contrast, autistic adolescents were able to complete the canonical stacking cups task but only half of them were able to complete the same task under the condition of non-canonical-word-order (participants were not time- limited and the instruction was repeated multiple times)."
