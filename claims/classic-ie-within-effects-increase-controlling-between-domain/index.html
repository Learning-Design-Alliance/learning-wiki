Evidence¶

Fabian Wolff 2026¶

Fabian Wolff, Jennifer Schumacher, Jens Möller. (2026). Revisiting the Role of Social and Dimensional Comparisons in the Internal/External Frame of Reference Model: A Systematic Comparison of the Classic and the Difference I/E Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10212-w

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Reanalysis of PISA 2000 data (N = 55,582 students from 26 countries) comparing classic and difference I/E model specifications using manifest constructs in Mplus 7. The authors report that "we found increases in the within-domain effects in Model 2 as well as decreases in Model 3 in all analyses if the between-domain effects in the classic I/E model were negative."

"As hypothesized, we found increases in the within-domain effects in Model 2 as well as decreases in Model 3 in all analyses if the between-domain effects in the classic I/E model were negative."
