## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multivariable analysis, ever receiving NR refresher training was not significantly associated with skill retention (AOR 2.72, 95% CI 0.81–3.63, p = 0.15), although it was significant in crude analysis (COR 3.94). [→ Sintayehu Y 2020](#sintayehu-y-2020)

## Evidence

### Sintayehu Y 2020

Sintayehu Y, Assefa D, Biftu G, Getahun T, Haymanot M, Kasiye S, Teshale M. (2020). Basic neonatal resuscitation skills of midwives and nurses in Eastern Ethiopia are not well retained: An observational study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236194

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 427 participants; Table 2 reports AOR 2.72 (95% CI 0.81, 3.63), p = 0.15 for refresher training. The article states "receiving NR refresher training was not associated with the multivariate logistic analysis of this study".

> "Paradoxically, receiving NR refresher training was not associated with the multivariate logistic analysis of this study, but in many studies, NR training was found to be significantly associated with retention of NR skill"
