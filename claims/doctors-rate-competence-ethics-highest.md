# Doctors rate medical competence and ethics most important and never rank communication first

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Questionnaires and studies targeting doctors' opinions rated medical competence and ethics most important, and in none of the included studies was communication rated as the most important attribute of a good doctor. [→ Steiner-Hofbauer Verena 2017](#steiner-hofbauer-verena-2017)

## Evidence

### Steiner-Hofbauer Verena 2017

Steiner-Hofbauer Verena, Schrank Beate, Holzinger Anita. (2017). What is a good doctor? Wiener Medizinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10354-017-0597-8

`q2 · i?`

Synthesis of the doctor-targeted questionnaires and studies among the 20 included sources (e.g. Herzig et al. 2006, Lambe and Bristow 2010, Kim et al. 2015). The review reports doctors valued medical competence and ethics most, with interpersonal qualities and communication "much less valued", and that "In none of the included studies was communication rated as the most important attribute".

> "Questionnaires and studies with focus on doctors’ opinions rated “medical competence” and “ethics” most important. “General interpersonal qualities” and “communication and patient involvement” are much less valued."
