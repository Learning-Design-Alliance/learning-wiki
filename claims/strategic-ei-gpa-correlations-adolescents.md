## Evidence

### Jolić Marjanović Zorana 2021

Jolić Marjanović Zorana, Altaras Dimitrijević Ana, Protić Sonja, Mestre José M. (2021). The Role of Strategic Emotional Intelligence in Predicting Adolescents’ Academic Achievement: Possible Interplays with Verbal Intelligence and Personality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413166

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlations in the 227-student sample; GPA was positively related to VI, A, C, O, strategic EI, and its branches, with "the strength of the association for EU (r = 0.20, 95% CI [0.08, 0.32])" slightly below meta-analytic expectations.

> "The strength of the association for EU (r = 0.20, 95% CI [0.08, 0.32]) was slightly lower than would be expected from meta-analytical findings (95% CI [0.28, 0.43]), while for EM (r = 0.17, 95% CI [0.04, 0.29]), it was right within the expected range"
