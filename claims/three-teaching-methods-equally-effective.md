## Evidence

### Maurer and Keim 2018

Trent W. Maurer, Cassidy Keim. (2018). Teaching about prejudice with a Bogardus Social Distance Scale activity: Replication and extension. IJ-SoTL, Vol. 12, No. 1, Art. 7. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2018.120107

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures MANOVA with 240 students in a 3x2 design (Control, Display, Activity conditions; homosexual and atheist targets). The Time x Condition interaction fell just below significance, p = .05, so "The nonsignificant Time x Condition interaction failed to support either Hypothesis 1a (conformity) or 2a (activity)".

> "a within-subjects effect for the Time x Condition interaction did not, F (3.58, 419.32) = 2.48, p = .05. The nonsignificant Time x Condition interaction failed to support either Hypothesis 1a (conformity) or 2a (activity)."
