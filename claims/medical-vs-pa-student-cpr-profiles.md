## Evidence

### Gupta R 2021

Gupta R, DeSandro S, Doherty NA, Gardner AK, Pillow MT. (2021). Medical and Physician Assistant Student Competence in Basic Life Support: Opportunities to Improve Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.11.48536

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Manikin study comparing 48 medical students with 32 PA students on compression and ventilation metrics (Tables 2 and 3). The study reports medical students "compress at a greater mean depth than PA students" while PA students led on rate, ventilation rate, hands-off time, cycles, and hand positioning.

> "Medical students were found to compress at a greater mean depth than PA students, whereas PA students compressed at a greater mean rate per minute. PA students exhibited a significantly higher average ventilation rate per two-minute cycle, less hands-off time, higher number of completed CPR cycles, and higher rate of correct hand positioning when compared to medical students."
