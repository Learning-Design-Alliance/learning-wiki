## Evidence

### Luminica Topale 2016

Luminica Topale. (2016). The strategic use of lecture recordings to facilitate an active and self-directed learning approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0723-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey results on reasons for non-participation (Table 3) among the 281 respondents. The printed percentages name lecturer communication skills, perceived value, engagement, and recording availability as distinct reasons for missing lectures.

> "Choices to attend live lectures depended primarily on the communication skills of the lecturer (30.9 %), the perceived value of attending lecture over reading notes and texts (18 %), and how engaging past lectures had been (14.7 %), with only 12.9 % reporting the availability of recordings as their primary reason missing lectures."
