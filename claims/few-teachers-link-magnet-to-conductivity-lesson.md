A questionnaire survey of science teachers in Tongzhou District, Beijing, run via the Questionnaire Star app, motivated the study: most teachers do not spontaneously associate the out-of-unit concept magnet with the electrical unit lesson on conductors and insulators.

> "Among the 57 valid questionnaires, only 16 mentioned that the magnet was involved in the last teach about "conductors and ins ulators", accounting for 28.1% of the valid questionnaires"
