## Evidence

### Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin 2026

Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin. (2026). The Effect of 3D Printer and Moulage Use on Improving Stoma Care Knowledge and Skills in Nursing Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.71068

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank within-group comparisons. Control improved from 75.66 to 84.87 (95% CI 9.210, 5.670 to 12.750; effect size 0.473); experimental from 77.24 to 93.02 (95% CI 15.780, 12.590 to 18.970; effect size 0.619), both p < 0.001.

> "While the stoma care knowledge level score before the education was 75.66, it was observed that there was an increase in the stoma care knowledge level with 84.87 after the education. A statistically significant difference was found between the knowledge level scores of the experimental group participants before and after the education. (p < 0.001) While the stoma care knowledge level score before the education was 77.24, it was observed that there was an increase in the stoma care knowledge level with 93.02 after the education."
