Evidence¶

Howe 2017¶

Howe, Christine; Zachariou, Antonia. (2017). Small-group collaboration and individual knowledge acquisition: The processes of growth during adolescence and early adulthood. Learning and Instruction. doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.007

q2 · i? · associational · r?

In the same study of 46 pairs aged 10 to 22, the abstract reports that "collaborative constructions turned out to have little relevance" to post-test performance. No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.

"Associations between group dialogue and post-test performance confirmed a key role for differing opinions while collaborative constructions turned out to have little relevance."
