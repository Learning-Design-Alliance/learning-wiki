Evidence¶

Isham Shah bin Hassan 2011¶

Isham Shah bin Hassan, Ramlee Mustafa & Mohd Arif Ismail. (2011). Mobile Technology and CAD Technology Integration in Teaching Architectural Design Process for Producing Creative Product. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 8(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102114

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent t-test on simulation-stage products evaluated with a 20-item instrument (alpha 0.9570). The article reports "t (118) = 12.267, significant", concluding the integrated method produced better design ideas.

"Inferential data shows there is a significant difference between final design idea produced in simulation activity using CAD technology integration (M=80.5) compared to final design idea produced in simulation activity using conventional method (M=66.9), t (118) = 12.267, significant."
