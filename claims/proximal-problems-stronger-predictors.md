Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-021-02039-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Objective-2 z tests compared intercept coefficients of linear regression models set at 1½, 3, or 10 years in the Generation R cohort; the article reports the age gradient reached significance only for attention problems.

> "For all problem scales, the problems at an older age tended to be a stronger negative predictor of school attainment than the earlier problems. Although only the attention problems reached statistical significance (ps < 0.001)."
