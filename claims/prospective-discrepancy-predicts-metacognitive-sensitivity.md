### Terneusen 2024

Terneusen, A., Quaedflieg, C., van Heugten, C., Ponds, R., & Winkens, I. (2024). The many facets of metacognition: comparing multiple measures of metacognition in healthy individuals. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09350-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis in a study of 128 healthy volunteers predicting metacognitive sensitivity (AUROC2) on a word-pair memory task. The article reports "higher prospective discrepancy scores (β = 0.233, p = .014)" as a significant predictor; no standardized effect size beyond the beta is printed.

> "Significant predictors were higher prospective discrepancy scores (β = 0.233, p = .014) and lower retrospective discrepancy scores (β=-0.185, p = .038)."
