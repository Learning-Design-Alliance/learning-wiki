## Evidence

### Wang Wei 2020

Wang Wei, Ma Hucheng, Ren Haozhen, Wang Zhongxia, Mao Liang, He Ningning. (2020). The Impact of Surgical Boot Camp and Subsequent Repetitive Practice on the Surgical Skills and Confidence of Residents. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00268-020-05669-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Colleague survey in which each resident was rated by 3 doctors and 2 nurses on a 1-5 scale. Ratings were higher on all items for the experimental group, reaching significance on the three named items (p < 0.05); no effect sizes are printed.

> "The residents' colleagues rated the experimental group significantly more competent than the control group to "quickly adapt to a new environment," "establish a good doctor-patient relationship," and "deal with on-call duty well" (Table 4)."
