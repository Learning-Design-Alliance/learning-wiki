Evidence¶

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies 2012¶

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/364

q3 · i1 · causal · r1

Comprehension outcome from the randomized field trial of 41 first-grade nonresponders; Appendix C.3 shows a mean difference of –0.22, effect size –0.31, and improvement index –12, with p > 0.05. The WWC judged the effect "substantively important" despite non-significance.

"McMaster et al. (2005) reported a negative mean difference between the PALS group and the comparison group on the Comprehension subtest of the Comprehensive Reading Assessment Battery (CRAB) for students in grade 1."
