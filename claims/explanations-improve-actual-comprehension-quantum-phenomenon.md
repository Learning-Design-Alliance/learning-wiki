## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the same experiment, explanation condition showed a statistically significant omnibus effect on actual comprehension of the quantum phenomenon (η2= 0.055). [→ Meinsma 2026](#meinsma-2026)
`q3 i?` Control participants scored significantly lower on actual comprehension than both the non-metaphorical and the metaphorical explanation groups; no effect size was printed for these pairwise contrasts. [→ Meinsma 2026 (2)](#meinsma-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Meinsma 2026

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus ANOVA in the same pre-registered randomized online experiment, with actual comprehension of the quantum phenomenon (0-4 coded open answer) as dependent variable; the condition difference was significant with η2= 0.055.

> "Statistically significant differences between the conditions were found for perceived comprehension (F(2,1164) = 8.49, p <.001, η2= 0.014) and actual comprehension (F(2,1131) = 32.6, p <.001, η2= 0.055)."

### Meinsma 2026 (2)

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons: "the control group scored significantly lower than the non-metaphorical group" and the metaphorical group on actual comprehension (mean difference = -0.43 and -0.31); only unstandardized mean differences are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "For actual comprehension, the control group scored significantly lower than the non-metaphorical group (mean difference = -0.43, SE = 0.06, p <.001) and the metaphorical group (mean difference = -0.31, SE = 0.05, p = <.001)."
