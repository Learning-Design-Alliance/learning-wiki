## Evidence

### Hu Jia 2025

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post comparison within the 62-student experimental group: total theory score increased from 71.45 ± 13.65 to 82.10 ± 14.50, with both dimensions significant (p = 0.000 each). The article reports the platform "considerably improved the theoretical scores (p = 0.000)"; no effect size is printed.
