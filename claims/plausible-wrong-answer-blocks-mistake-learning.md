## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2008

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2008). Reflection and Self-Monitoring in Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3266713](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266713)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the think-aloud interviews on problem 2, the article reports student 6 confused energy eigenstates with position eigenstates and "failed to learn from mistakes on his midterm exam attempt because his solution, while incorrect, gave an answer that seemed correct". In contrast, student 10 simply could not remember enough of the solution to replicate it.

> "Student 6 displayed common errors such as confusing energy eigenstates with position eigenstates, and said that he failed to learn from mistakes on his midterm exam attempt because his solution, while incorrect, gave an answer that seemed correct"
