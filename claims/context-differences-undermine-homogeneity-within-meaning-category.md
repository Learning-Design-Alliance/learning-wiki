## Evidence

### diSessa et al. 2004

Andrea A. diSessa, Nicole M. Gillespie, Jennifer B. Esterly. (2004). Coherence versus fragmentation in the development of the concept of force. Cognitive Science 28 (2004) 843–900. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogsci.2004.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the extension study, the authors probed contexts beyond those in the quasi-replication. They report "substantial differences in the way subjects thought about the new contexts," which undermined homogeneity even within the best-surviving meaning category.

> "We found substantial differences in the way subjects thought about the new contexts we asked about, which undermined claims for homogeneity within even the category of subjects (having one particular meaning associated with "force") that best survived our quasi-replication"
