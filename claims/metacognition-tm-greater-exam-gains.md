## Evidence

### Hensley Lauren 2021

Hensley Lauren, Kulesza Amy, Peri Joshua, Brady Anna C., Wolters Christopher A., Sovic David, Breitenberger Caroline. (2021). Supporting Undergraduate Biology Students' Academic Success: Comparing Two Workshop Interventions. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-03-0068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 17 lab sections assigned to two workshop conditions with 249 consenting students; residualized-change SEM compared post-intervention outcomes holding pre-intervention scores and covariates constant. The abstract reports "greater increases in their exam scores" for Metacognition+TM students.

> "Based on three exams and self-report surveys administered before the intervention and at the end of the semester, students who participated in the Metacognition+TM intervention experienced greater increases in their exam scores and degree commitment than those in the baseline intervention."
