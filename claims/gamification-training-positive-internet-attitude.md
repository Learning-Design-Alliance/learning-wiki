## Evidence

### Taghipour Esmaeel 2023

Taghipour Esmaeel, Vizeshfar Fatemeh, Zarifsanaiey Nahid. (2023). The effect of gamification-based training on the knowledge, attitudes, and academic achievement of male adolescents in preventing substance and internet addiction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04858-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment using the Attitude to Internet Questionnaire (40 items, four subscales). Total internet-attitude mean rose from 112.77 ± 20.10 pre-test to 180.33 ± 9.79 post-test in the intervention group versus 109.46 ± 17.52 in controls (P = 0.000).

> "In contrast, the intervention group's average score for their attitude toward the Internet significantly increased immediately and one month after the intervention, suggesting a more positive attitude."
