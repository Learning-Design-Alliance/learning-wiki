## Evidence

### Umoren RA 2025

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlation between self-reported app activity and 6-month performance in the Lagos cohort; the article reports r = 0.30 (P = .02) for BVM ventilation, while the correlation with ENC 1 case scenario A was not significant (r = 0.24; P = .09).

> "BVM ventilation scores 6 months after the training were higher with increasing self-reported app use (Spearman r = 0.30; P = .02)."

### Umoren RA 2025 (2)

Umoren RA, Ezeaka C, Berkelhamer SK, et al. (2025). Essential Newborn Care Virtual Simulations for Skills Retention in Newborn Care. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.60565

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis comparing 61 participants' self-reported use with the vENC app's logged activity uploaded to a DHIS2 database; the article characterizes both correlations as modest, with r = 0.30 and r = 0.28.

> "The correlations between self-reported activity and the logged number of scenarios performed (Spearman r = 0.30; P = .02) or logged time in the app (Spearman r = 0.28; P = .03) were modest."
