## Evidence

### Hou Jie 2026

Hou Jie, Huang Yanhong, Wang Qing, Yao Xiaoxue, Zhang Peizheng, Zhao Fengqing. (2026). AI Dependence and Self-Reported Creativity Among Chinese College Students: The Roles of Motivation for AI Use and Perceived AI Literacy. Behavioral Sciences, 16(9), 1712. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091712

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary parallel multiple mediation analysis (lavaan, 5000 bootstrap resamples) in the same 5764-participant survey. The escape-motivation indirect effect was "significant and negative (β = −0.075" with the printed bootstrap confidence interval.

> "The indirect effects through escape motivation and social motivation were significant and negative (β = −0.075, 95% Boot CI [−0.103, −0.048], β = −0.022, 95% Boot CI [−0.046, −0.0006])."
