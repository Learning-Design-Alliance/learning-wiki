## Evidence

### Armstrong G 2010

Armstrong G, Blashki G, Joubert L, Bland R, Moulding R, Gunn J, Naccarella L. (2010). An evaluation of the effect of an educational intervention for Australian social workers on competence in delivering brief cognitive behavioural strategies: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired comparison within the control group (n = 14) of the RCT; the Results section prints a mean increase of 4.32 points, paired t(13) = 3.83, p = 0.002. The testing-effect explanation is the authors' interpretation, not a tested result.

> "The control group had a small but statistically significant improvement in objective competence between baseline and follow-up which can largely be explained by a testing effect, whereby the participants performed better due to repeating the same testing measure."
