## Evidence

### Wang Weitao 2025

Wang Weitao, Gao Lejun, Lin Yun, Gao Peng. (2025). Virtual reality is emerging training applications for anesthesia simulation. European Journal of Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40001-025-03054-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review attributes this to Cléber et al., describing a dental anesthesia VR simulator that performed well in simulating needle insertion tasks including insertion position, depth and tissue-resistance perception. No effect sizes are printed.

> "reported a VR simulator specifically for the training of inferior alveolar nerve block, which greatly improved the success rate of the operation and the satisfaction of the training"

### Wang Weitao 2025 (2)

Wang Weitao, Gao Lejun, Lin Yun, Gao Peng. (2025). Virtual reality is emerging training applications for anesthesia simulation. European Journal of Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40001-025-03054-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review attributes to Elen a study using VR simulators to train clinical dental students in inferior alveolar dental anesthesia procedures, finding "higher accuracy and confidence" with immersive training. No effect size is printed.

> "students receiving immersive instruction or immersive training provided higher accuracy and confidence in anesthesia management"
