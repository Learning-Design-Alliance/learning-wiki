### Fares Fawzi 2026

Fares Fawzi, Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Marta Knezevic, Tanya Nazaretsky, and Tanja Käser. (2026). REFINE: Real-world Exploration of Interactive Feedback and Student Behaviour. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-hoc validation of the live classroom deployment (39 students) using the human-aligned Interactive Feedback Judge, with judge–human agreement κ≥0.83. Exam-preparation performance remained similarly high (95.9% relevance, 93.6% actionability, 99.1% tool relevance, 86.7% correctness).

> "On the in-class study dataset Dintstudy, responses received positive judgments of 96.0% actionability, 94.4% relevance, 93.6% correctness, and 100% tool relevance."
