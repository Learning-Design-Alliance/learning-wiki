## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Students asked to plot the electric field in each region first (Group 1) outperformed students who also evaluated the field at three points per region (Group 2) on part (a)(i) (p = 0.006, Cohen's d = 0.816) and part (a)(iii) (p = 0.005, Cohen's d = 0.843). ## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2016

Shih-Yin Lin, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student Difficulties in Translating between Mathematical and Graphical Representations in Introductory Physics. Originally published 1997. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.05466](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.05466)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental comparison across the three recitation groups of the same quiz. T-tests showed Group 1 outperformed Group 2 with "Cohen’s d = 0.816" in part (a)(i) and d = 0.843 in part (a)(iii).

> "Also, students in Group 1 outperformed students in Group 2 in part (a) (i) (p = 0.006, Cohen’s d = 0.816) and in part (a) (iii) (p = 0.005, Cohen’s d = 0.843)."
