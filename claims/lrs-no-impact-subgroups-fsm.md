## Evidence

### Wiggins et al. 2017

Meg Wiggins, Mary Sawtell and Dr John Jerrim. (2017). Learner Response System: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581136

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Subgroup analyses of the cluster RCT re-estimated the main model separately for boys, girls, and pupils ever eligible for FSM. Table 1 reports FSM effects of -0.01 (cohort A maths), -0.01 (cohort A reading), -0.09 (cohort B maths) and -0.04 (cohort B reading), all with confidence intervals spanning zero. The report states there was "no evidence of any positive impact for any pre-specified sub-group".

> "There was no evidence of any positive impact for any pre-specified sub-group (boys, girls, or pupils ever eligible for FSM )."
