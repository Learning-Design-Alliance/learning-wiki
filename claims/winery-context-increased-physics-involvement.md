## Evidence

### Roberto Benedetti 2013

Roberto Benedetti, Emilio Mariotti and Vera Montalbano. (2013). First Steps into Physics in the Winery. Department of Physics, University of Siena. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1302.3767](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1302.3767)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative evaluation by direct observation during activities, teacher interviews and a few available student reports (20 first-lab reports, 13 final reports of one class); the authors state involvement "increased significantly" once the enology link was seen. No quantitative assessment was possible.

> "As soon as students realized the relationship with some aspects of enology, their involvement increased significantly. A first analysis of students' reports on laboratory experience shows the necessity of paying more attention for integrating this activity in the previous knowledge of students."
