BMC Medical Education, Volume 25, Article 31. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06613-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke six-step method, two independent coders) of 17 students' focus group transcripts. Table 5 lists the four themes: authentic task skills, execution of clinical reasoning strategies, exposing clinical reasoning difficulties, and motivation to seek holistic feedback.

> "Qualitative analysis of all focus group transcriptions identified four predominant themes and 10 subthemes, as summarized in Table 5."
