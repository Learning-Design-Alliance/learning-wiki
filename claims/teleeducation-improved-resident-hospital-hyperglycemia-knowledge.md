### Lopes Jivago da Fonseca 2026

Lopes Jivago da Fonseca, Silva Gabryela Mariane Costa e, Caetano Conrado Reinoldes, Riboli Raissa Rigon, Pinheiro Emanoela Gomes, Bortoluzzi Bárbara Luíza Petter, Nunes Gustavo Dutra Leite, de Souza Maiane Siewes, Herdt Maria Carolina Wensing, Scharnberg Aline, Rech Carolina Garcia Soares Leães, Weinert Leticia Schwerz, Bohlke Maristela. (2026). Tele-education to improve residents’ knowledge and quality of care in hospital hyperglycemia: a multicenter randomized clinical trial. Archives of Endocrinology and Metabolism. https://doi.org/10.20945/2359-4292-2026-0041

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multicenter open-label randomized trial in which clinical teams at four Brazilian teaching hospitals were block-randomized to tele-education or routine practice; post-intervention questionnaire scores showed "significantly higher in the intervention group compared with the control group" (median 8 vs. 6; p = 0.005). Fifty residents completed both assessments.

> "After the intervention, knowledge scores were significantly higher in the intervention group compared with the control group (median 8 [IQR 8-9] vs. 6 [IQR 5-8]; p = 0.005)."

### Lopes Jivago da Fonseca 2026 (2)

Archives of Endocrinology and Metabolism. https://doi.org/10.20945/2359-4292-2026-0041

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre-post comparison in the same randomized trial: the intervention group gained "an improvement of 2.5 points in the intervention group from preto post-test" with p < 0.001; the control group also improved modestly (5.5 to 6; p = 0.009).

> "Within-group analysis showed an improvement of 2.5 points in the intervention group from preto post-test (p < 0.001)."
