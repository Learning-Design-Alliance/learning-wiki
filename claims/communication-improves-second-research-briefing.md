## Evidence

### Baxter AL 2023

Baxter AL, Lang RF, Zywicki C, Gardner SM, Kopec A, Jung A. (2023). A scalable approach to undergraduate research in physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2303.05384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2303.05384)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructors' observational assessment in the Lesson Effectiveness section, based on comparing first and second research briefings across course iterations. The authors interpret this as students improving science communication skills and gaining confidence. This is instructor observation, not a measured comparison; no effect size is reported.

> "But during the second research briefing, they do communicate the bigger question and bring their peers up to speed, before going into the details of their research. We find that later in the semester, more discussions arise between the research team and the instructors, and among the research teams"
