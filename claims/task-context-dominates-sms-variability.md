## Evidence

### Yunhee Kim 2026

Yunhee Kim, Doyeon Chae, Jaehwan Jang, Kyeong Taek Oh, Byeong-Sun Park & Min-gu Kim. (2026). Does tapping in sync reflect cognitive control? Links between sensorimotor synchronization, social context, and executive function in early childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00443-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Extended LMM on 140 children's log-transformed DTW scores across three task contexts; music showed the highest variability (M = 4.87) versus metronome conditions (M = 1.08 and M = 1.06), with ηp2 = .96.

> "The main effect of Task Context was particularly large, F (2, 1656) = 18299.31, p < .001, ηp2 = .96, indicating substantial differences in synchronization variability across rhythmic contexts."

### Yunhee Kim 2026 (2)

Yunhee Kim, Doyeon Chae, Jaehwan Jang, Kyeong Taek Oh, Byeong-Sun Park & Min-gu Kim. (2026). Does tapping in sync reflect cognitive control? Links between sensorimotor synchronization, social context, and executive function in early childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00443-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise comparison within the same extended LMM: the 150 bpm metronome (M = 1.08) and 50 bpm bimanual (M = 1.06) conditions did not significantly differ; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "whereas the latter two conditions did not significantly differ from one another (p = .609)"
