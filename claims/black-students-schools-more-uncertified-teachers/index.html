Sarah Mehrotra 2021¶

Sarah Mehrotra, Ivy S. Morgan, Allison Socol. (2021). Getting Black Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617544

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive CRDC 2017-18 analysis of uncertified-teacher percentages by school Black-enrollment quartile. The report finds "more than twice the percentage (5%) of uncertified teachers" in highest-quartile schools versus 2% in lowest-quartile schools, with twofold gaps in 18 of 31 eligible states.

"Schools with the greatest percentages of Black students on average have more than twice the percentage (5%) of uncertified teachers as schools serving the fewest percentages of Black students (2%)."
