Evidence¶

Osama Swidan 2026¶

Osama Swidan. (2026). Responding to AI-generated emotional alerts: teachers' intervention and students' engagement in the mathematics classroom. ZDM-MathematicsEducation. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01810-7

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative analysis of one episode during individual work after collective discussion; the teacher stated aloud that students were doing well with less stress and no one was confused. The author interprets the outcome as stabilization of activation, with no branching because engagement did not require reorganization.

"Rather than approaching individual students immediately, the teacher consulted the class-level emotion display of the Dash4Emotion, which showed a decline in negative emotional alerts and an increase in positive ones over time."
