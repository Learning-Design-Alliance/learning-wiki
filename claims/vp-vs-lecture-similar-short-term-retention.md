## Evidence

### Courteille Olivier 2018

Courteille Olivier, Fahlstedt Madelen, Ho Johnson, Hedman Leif, Fors Uno, von Holst Hans, Felländer-Tsai Li, Möller Hans. (2018). Learning through a virtual patient vs. recorded lecture: a comparison of knowledge retention in a trauma case. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5aa3.ccf2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial in which 170 volunteers, stratified for student/resident and gender, were allocated to a VP session or a 13-minute video-recorded lecture; repeated-measures ANOVA on the 12-question knowledge test showed "no significant difference in the knowledge acquisition test (Test 1) between the VP and L groups".

> "The repeated-measures analysis of variance showed that there was no significant difference in the knowledge acquisition test (Test 1) between the VP and L groups, implying similar short-term retention in both groups."
