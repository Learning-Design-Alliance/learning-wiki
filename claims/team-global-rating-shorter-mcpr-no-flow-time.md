## Evidence

### Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan 2026

Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan. (2026). Nontechnical Skills and Leadership Are Associated With More Efficient Mechanical Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Deployment in Real‐World Cardiac Arrest. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.125.049012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective video-based observational study of 85 MCPR cases in a tertiary emergency department, analyzed with multivariable linear mixed-effects models adjusted for patient characteristics and team leader clustering. The adjusted model showed "a 6‐second reduction in cumulative no‐flow time" per TEAM point (P=0.009).

> "each 1‐point increase in the overall TEAM global rating was associated with a 6‐second reduction in cumulative no‐flow time (adjusted effect, −6 seconds [95% CI, −10 to −1]; P=0.009)"

### Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan 2026 (2)

Lien Chun‐Ju; Huang Shuo‐Kuen; Chang Jia‐How; Huang Edward Pei‐Chuan. (2026). Nontechnical Skills and Leadership Are Associated With More Efficient Mechanical Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Deployment in Real‐World Cardiac Arrest. Journal of the American Heart Association. https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.125.049012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis of the same 85-case cohort using log-transformed outcomes retaining the same fixed-effect covariates and random-intercept structure; the association "remained statistically significant" (β=−0.34, P<0.001).

> "In the adjusted log‐transformed model, the inverse association between higher TEAM global rating and shorter cumulative no‐flow time remained statistically significant (β=−0.34 [95% CI, −0.50 to −0.18]; P<0.001)"
