## Evidence

### Zearn Math evidence review 2024

Zearn Math evidence review. (2024). https://zearn.org

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A matched comparison study conducted during the 2016-17 and 2017-18 school years in a large urban district that implemented Zearn as a pilot curriculum without full implementation support. The review reports "no significant differences between Zearn and comparison students"; equivalence was not tested.

> "found no significant differences between Zearn and comparison students overall or on the state assessment in the subset of tested grades, though Zearn was associated with usage-linked gains and broadly positive stakeholder perceptions."
