# In VR resuscitation simulation, participants primarily relied on the virtual patient's physical examination rather than the cardiorespiratory monitor as their source of clinical data

## Evidence

### Loeb Daniel 2025

Loeb Daniel, Shoemaker Jamie, Ely Kelly, Zackoff Matthew. (2025). Deconstructing Participant Behaviors in Virtual Reality Simulation: Ethnographic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65886

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Video-based focused ethnography of 15 interprofessional physician-nurse dyad VR simulations of pediatric pneumonia and sepsis. Ethnographic observation found "participants consistently prioritized soliciting data from the virtual patient," with first-person video showing most time spent looking at the patient. No effect size is printed.

> "After orientation to the VR environment, participants consistently prioritized soliciting data from the virtual patient. This early prioritization was evident in their frequent comments on the patient’s movements, mental status, and perfusion."
