### H. J. Lewandowski and Noah Finkelstein 2015

H. J. Lewandowski and Noah Finkelstein. (2015). Redesigning a junior-level electronics course to support engagement in scientific practices. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.043](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.043)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the coded write-up data from the traditional course (N = 47), where 83% "identiﬁed the input impedance of the me- ter as the source of the discrepancy" but only 27% refined the model. This is the authors' reading of their own results, not a separately tested hypothesis.

> "Despite our students having come through transformed theory courses in the prior sequences, they do not resolve discrepancies be- tween model (theory) and measurement, even if they are aware of them."
