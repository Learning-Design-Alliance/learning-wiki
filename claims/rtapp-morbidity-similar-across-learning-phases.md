## Evidence

### Solaini L. 2023

Solaini L., Cavaliere D., Rocco G., Avanzolini A., Di Pietrantonio D., Ercolani G. (2023). Differences in the learning curve of robotic transabdominal preperitoneal inguinal hernia repair according to surgeon's robotic experience. Hernia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10029-023-02846-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-surgeon comparison of 90-day postoperative morbidity before versus after learning-phase completion in the retrospective cohort: trainee 5% versus 7.0% (p = 1.000), expert 16.6% versus 0% (p = 0.066); morbidity "was similar to those after its completion".

> "was similar to those after its completion for the trainee (pre-learning 1, 5% versus post-learning 3, 7.0%; p = 1.000) and the expert surgeon (pre-learning 5, 16.6% versus post-learning 0, 0%; p = 0.066)"
