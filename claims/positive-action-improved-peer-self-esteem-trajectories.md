### Silverthorn 2017

Silverthorn, N., DuBois, D. L., Lewis, K. M., Reed, A., Bavarian, N., Day, J., Ji, P., Acock, A. C., Vuchinich, S., & Flay, B. R. (2017). Effects of a School-Based Social-Emotional and Character Development Program on Self-Esteem Levels and Processes: A Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2158244017713238

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Growth-curve analysis of a matched-pair cluster-randomized trial in 14 Chicago schools following a cohort from Grades 3 to 8 (N = 1,170). The article reports "peer self-esteem (B = 0.04, p = .01; ES = 0.37)" favoring PA schools, with PA students showing a less marked decline over time.

> "students in schools that received PA showed more positive change over time on the measures of peer self-esteem (B = 0.04, p = .01; ES = 0.37)"
