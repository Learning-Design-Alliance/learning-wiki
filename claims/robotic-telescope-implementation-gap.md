## Evidence

### E.L. Gomez & M.T. Fitzgerald 2017

E.L. Gomez & M.T. Fitzgerald. (2017). Robotic Telescopes in Education. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1080/21672857.2017.1303264](https://doi.org/10.1080/21672857.2017.1303264)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis of the formal-school literature. The authors state there has been "considerable success in terms of technical capacity" while widespread inquiry-based use was not achieved, and cite multiple publications noting a significant lack of deep research on classroom translation. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The last two decades have shown considerable success in terms of technical capacity but not in producing and supporting widespread inquiry-based astronomy education in formal education as was the usual intention [81]."
