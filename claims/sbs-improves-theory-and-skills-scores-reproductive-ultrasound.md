A novel step-by-step teaching method improves training outcomes in transvaginal ultrasound for postgraduate reproductive medicine students: an exploratory randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education 24:1270. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06257-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory randomized comparison of 50 postgraduate reproductive medicine students in an ultrasound room; after the same training period the SBS group "demonstrated superior theoretical examination and skills assessment scores compared to the control group" (86.00 ± 5.30 vs. 81.56 ± 6.32; 87.20 ± 4.02 vs. 83.40 ± 4.78).

> "the experimental group, who received SBS teaching, demonstrated superior theoretical examination and skills assessment scores compared to the control group, who received traditional teaching methods (86.00 ± 5.30 vs. 81.56 ± 6.32 and 87.20 ± 4.02 vs. 83.40 ± 4.78, respectively)."
