# Teacher burnout dimensions show the strongest correlations with CMSE among teacher-level psychometric constructs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Diminished personal accomplishment has the strongest positive correlation with CMSE (r=0.415), and emotional exhaustion (r=-0.289) and depersonalization (r=-0.281) correlate negatively, based on 13-14 studies each. [→ Duan 2024](#duan-2024)
`q3 i2` Teacher commitment (r=0.371) and job satisfaction (r=0.302) correlate moderately and positively with CMSE, while teacher stress correlates negatively (r=-0.134) and constructivist beliefs positively (r=0.159). [→ Duan 2024](#duan-2024)
`q3 i1` Among the Big Five traits, openness (r=0.220), extraversion (r=0.212), and conscientiousness (r=0.121) correlate positively with CMSE, whereas agreeableness and neuroticism show no relationship. [→ Duan 2024](#duan-2024)

## Evidence

### Duan 2024

Duan, S., Bissaker, K., & Xu, Z. (2024). Correlates of teachers' classroom management self-efficacy: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09881-2

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of teacher-level factors across the 87 included studies (Table 2). The pooled results show "the strongest correlation with CMSE was personal accomplish PA (r=0.415)"; job satisfaction r=0.302, teacher commitment r=0.371, teacher stress r=-0.134, constructivist beliefs r=0.159, and openness r=0.220 were also significant.

> "Overall, the strongest correlation with CMSE was personal accomplish PA (r=0.415, CI [0.318, 0.504]). Openness showed the largest correlation among the Big Five personality traits (r =0.220, CI [0.135, 0.303]), followed by extraversion (r =0.212, CI [0.121, 0.298])"
