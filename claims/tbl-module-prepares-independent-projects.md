## Evidence

### Peterson 2015

Peterson, E.K. and Carrico, P. (2015). Laboratory Exercise in Behavioral Genetics Using Team-based Learning Strategies. Bioscene 41(2). https://visionandchange.org/files/2011/03/Revised-Vision-and-Change-Final-Report.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observational report on end-of-semester final projects, which replaced a final examination: small groups designed and implemented projects addressing a novel research question in behavioral genetics. The article reports students "retained the conceptual knowledge learned in the module" and worked with more precision and confidence.

> "We found that students not only retained the conceptual knowledge learned in the module, but that they were practicing science, designing their experiments, problem-solving, and working with the behaviors and animals with more precision and confidence than before."
