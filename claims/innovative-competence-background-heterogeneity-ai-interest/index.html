Evidence¶

Liu Xu 2026¶

Liu Xu, Zhang Meiqi, Du Jiaoyang. (2026). Pre-Service Teachers' Perceptions of AI Technological Support and School-Based Intelligent Environment Support: Model-Based Indirect Associations with Innovative Competence via AI Self-Efficacy and Self-Regulated Learning. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081378

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Difference analysis (ANOVA/t-tests) in the survey of 3003 pre-service teachers. The article reports "η2 = 0.116" for AI interest level and "η2 = 0.029" for AI usage frequency, both exceeding printed effect-size criteria, and concludes AI interest has the stronger association.

"medium-to-high AI usage frequencies [F (2, 3000) = 44.017, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.029], and medium-to-high AI interest levels [F (2, 3000) = 197.276, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.116] exhibited significantly higher overall innovative competence."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Null difference test from the same 3003-participant survey: no statistically significant difference by gender or institution type; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"Difference analysis results showed no significant differences in pre-service teachers' innovative competence by gender or institution type."
