A quasi-experimental between-group comparison of 404 first-semester medical students found the simulation group's theoretical knowledge gain "significantly greater in the simulation-based group (3.00) compared with the control group (2.00; p < 0.0001)". No standardized effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Importantly, the gain in theoretical knowledge (post- minus pre- scores) was significantly greater in the simulation-based group (3.00) compared with the control group (2.00; p < 0.0001; Table 1; Figure 1)."
