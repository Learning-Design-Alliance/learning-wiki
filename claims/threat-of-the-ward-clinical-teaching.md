### Tamachi Shameena 2018

Tamachi Shameena, Giles James A., Dornan Tim, Hill Elspeth J. R. (2018). “You understand that whole big situation they’re in”: interpretative phenomenological analysis of peer-assisted learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1291-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview account from a peer tutor in the IPA study at SRFT; he contrasted PAL’s informal set-up with ward rounds, where “you’re less likely to ask questions” for fear of feeling stupid.

> "the informal set up of PAL in the sense that on a ward round with a consultant [attending physician], you’re less likely to ask questions. There’s always that fear you could get shot down and feel stupid."
