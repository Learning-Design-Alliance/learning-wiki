## Evidence

### Yangchu Huang 2026

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-test on decision times across the two recipient conditions in the same 92-participant two-step task. The article reports "comparable between the two recipients, t(91) = 0.95, p = 0.344" with d = 0.07; no equivalence test was reported.

> "The time required to make decisions was comparable between the two recipients, t(91) = 0.95, p = 0.344, 95% CI [-7.44, 21.10], d = 0.07"
