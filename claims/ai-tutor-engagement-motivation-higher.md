## Evidence

### Kestin 2025

Kestin, G., Miller, K., Klales, A., Milbourne, T. & Ponti, G. (2025). AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting. Scientific Reports 15:17458. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-point Likert perception ratings after each lesson in the crossover RCT (Fig. 3). Engagement means were 4.1 (AI) versus 3.6 (in-class); motivation was 3.4 versus 3.1, t(311) = -3.4, p < 0.001.

> "the students in the AI group agreed more strongly (Mean = 4.1, SD = 0.98) than those in the in-class active learning group (Mean = 3.6, SD = 0.92), t(311) = − 4.5, p < 0.0001."
