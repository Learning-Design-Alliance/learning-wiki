### Bao Xuemei 2026

Development and psychometric validation of a health literacy competency scale for nurses, and its application in a cross-sectional survey: a methodological study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1978033

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis on the EFA subsample of 467 nurses (KMO 0.958), principal component analysis with varimax rotation. The quote reports the extracted factors and variance: "explaining 72.558% of the variance" with loadings 0.511–0.855.

> "Six factors with eigenvalues > 1 were extracted, explaining 72.558% of the variance, and all 39 items had factor loadings exceeding 0.50 (range: 0.511–0.855)"

### Bao Xuemei 2026 (2)

Bao Xuemei, Liu Shuhan, Hu Xiaoxiao, Zhang Qinghua, Jiang Mei, Gan Yingting and Wang Yu. (2026). Development and psychometric validation of a health literacy competency scale for nurses, and its application in a cross-sectional survey: a methodological study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1978033

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Confirmatory factor analysis on the independent validation subsample of 393 nurses testing the six-factor structure. The quote reports the printed fit indices, including "χ2/df = 2.223" and "CFI = 0.942"; GFI was slightly below the ideal 0.90.

> "the model demonstrated good fit: χ2/df = 2.223 (< 3.0), RMSEA = 0.056 (< 0.08), CFI = 0.942, NFI = 0.900, IFI = 0.942, TLI = 0.937 (all > 0.90), and GFI = 0.830 (> 0.80)"
