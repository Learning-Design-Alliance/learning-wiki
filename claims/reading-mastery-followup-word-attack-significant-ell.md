# One year after the intervention, Reading Mastery showed a statistically significant effect on word attack for English language learners, with positive but non-significant differences on other reading measures

## Evidence

### WWC Intervention Report: Reading Mastery SRA/McGraw-Hill 2006

WWC Intervention Report: Reading Mastery SRA/McGraw-Hill. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Follow-up data from Gunn et al. (2002), reported in Appendix A4 of the WWC review, show Word Attack with a mean difference of 24.36, effect size 1.02, statistically significant at α = 0.05, improvement index +35. The other four measures had effect sizes of 0.24 to 0.70, all marked 'ns'.

> "After one year, three of the five outcome measures showed substantively important effects (word attack, reading vocabulary, and passage comprehension but not oral reading fluency or letter/word identification)."
