### Li Siyan 2024

Pearson's correlation analysis of the 31-participant user study relating PAS components to pre-to-post L2 grit changes. The article reports that "perceived chatbot appreciation correlates positively with changes in total L2 grit"; no correlation coefficients are printed in the main text.
