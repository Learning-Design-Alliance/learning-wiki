## Evidence

### Vaz DV 2021

Vaz DV, Ferreira EMR, Palma GB, Atun-Einy O, Kafri M, Ferreira FR. (2021). Testing a new active learning approach to advance motor learning knowledge and self-efficacy in physical therapy undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education 21:62. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02486-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Content analysis of 132 biweekly forms submitted by the 28 intervention students, coded with 25 predetermined ML elements plus emergent codes; inter-coder kappa averaged 0.928. Students mentioned on average 13 ± 7.3 codes per form; the frequency of mistakes was generally low.

> "All 25 predetermined ML codes were identified in the logs except the codes "task-oriented/task-specific training" and "specificity of practice.""
