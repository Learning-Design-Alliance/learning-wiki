Madia 2025¶

Madia, J. E., Gruijters, R. J., Raabe, I. J. & Hübner, N. (2025). Growth mindset and socioeconomic inequality in academic achievement across seventy-three PISA countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00365-8

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Four-way decomposition of PISA 2022 math scores across 73 countries; Table 1 reports INT_med = 0.006 across all mediator-fixing specifications, with Prop. INT_med = -0.010.

"The Mediated Interaction (Q4 in Table2), representing how SES and mindset interact when SES inﬂuences mindset formation, shows a small but positive effect (0.006)."

Discussion¶

Learner Variables¶

  • Mindset — predictor: learners who differ on it differ in outcomes
  • Socioeconomic Status — predictor: outcomes differ by group: a gap reflecting circumstances and treatment, not a cause

Related Claims¶

  • The proportion of the SES effect mediated by growth mindset varies across countries, reaching at most 0.11 in a few countries while most fall between 0 and 0.08 — related
  • Growth mindset mediates only a small share (about 2.9–3.2%) of the effect of SES on academic achievement across 73 countries — related
  • The direct effect of SES on achievement dominates: the controlled direct effect accounts for about 98% of the total SES effect in math — related
  • Children from low-SES backgrounds hold more negative academic self-views, which partially mediate the SES-achievement association across 70 countries — related
