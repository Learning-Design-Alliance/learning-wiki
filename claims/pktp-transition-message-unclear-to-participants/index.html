Findings on communication about the importance of transition from the developmental evaluation's participant reports. While pre-K teachers reported PKTP contributed to their understanding of transitions, teachers from both grades indicated a more explicit integration of transition into PKTP activities would be helpful for clarifying goals and intended outcomes.

"However, at the close of the first full year of implemen tation, PKTP's communication about the importance of transitions was less clear to teacher and parent participan ts. Parents who participated in PKTP activities did not necessarily understand that there was a focus on the importance of transition."
