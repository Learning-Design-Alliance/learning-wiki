CREDO 2020¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2020). Charter School Performance in the State of Washington. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-the-state-of-washington-2/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Growth-period disaggregation of the VCR matched analysis. The report states that during 2015-2016 charter students demonstrated growth of "approximately 59 more days of learning in reading and 94 additional days in math" compared with TPS peers, though these gains were not statistically different; later periods showed smaller positive gains.

"The gains of Washington charter school students during the 2015- 2016, the 2016-2017, and the 2017 -18 growth period do not differ statistically from the performance of their TPS peers."
