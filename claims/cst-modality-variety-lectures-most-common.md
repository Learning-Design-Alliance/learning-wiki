`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Frequency analysis of training modalities across the 69 papers describing a cultural safety training intervention. The review reports all interventions described more than one modality, with teaching/lectures, workshops, discussion sessions, and immersive experiences most common.

> "Teaching/lectures was the most common training modality, described in almost a third of papers (n = 21; 30%). Workshops (n = 18; 26%), discussions sessions (n = 16; 23%), and immersive experiences/community visits (n = 13; 19%) were also common"
