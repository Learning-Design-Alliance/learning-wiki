Purvi Sevak 2021¶

Purvi Sevak, Kathleen Feeney, Todd Honeycutt, Emily Peterson. (2021). Linking Learning to Careers Demonstration: Impacts 24 Months After Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/linking-learning-to-careers-demonstration-impacts-24-months-after-enrollment

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Null employment finding from the 24-month RCT impact analysis: no effect on employment for all participants, though later enrollees showed an 11 percentage point increase in likelihood of earnings. Equivalence was not tested.

"LLC did not affect employment for all participants within the first 24 months of program enrollment, but it did increase the likelihood of earnings among later enrollees by 11 percentage points."
