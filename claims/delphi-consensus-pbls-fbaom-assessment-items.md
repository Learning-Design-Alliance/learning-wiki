### Hasselager AB 2018

Hasselager AB, Lauritsen T, Kristensen T, Bohnstedt C, Sønderskov C, Østergaard D, Tolsgaard MG. (2018). What should be included in the assessment of laypersons' paediatric basic life support skills? Results from a Delphi consensus study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine 26:9. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-018-0474-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Modified Delphi consensus survey conducted November 2015–March 2016 with 28 participating experts (26 physicians, two nurses) from 13 countries; 23 completed the third-round re-rating. The consensus result was that "nine and eight essential assessment items for PBLS and FBAOM skills, respectively" were identified.

> "The ratings and comments from the last two rounds resulted in nine and eight essential assessment items for PBLS and FBAOM skills, respectively."
