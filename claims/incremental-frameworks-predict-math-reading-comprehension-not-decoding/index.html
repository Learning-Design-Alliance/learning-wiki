Gunderson 2018¶

Gunderson, E. A., Sorhagen, N. S., Gripshover, S. J., Dweck, C. S., Goldin-Meadow, S., & Levine, S. C. (2018). Parent Praise to Toddlers Predicts Fourth Grade Academic Achievement via Children's Incremental Mindsets. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000444

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Zero-order correlations in the longitudinal sample (n = 53 with missing data handled by FIML). Frameworks predicted "4th grade achievement in math" and reading comprehension but not decoding; the math–decoding correlation difference was significant (t = 2.20, p = .03).

"incremental motivational frameworks in 2nd or 3rd grade predicted children's 4th grade achievement in math, r(48) = .49, p < .001, and reading comprehension, r(48) = .32, p = .02, but not reading decoding, r(48) = .19, p = .19"
