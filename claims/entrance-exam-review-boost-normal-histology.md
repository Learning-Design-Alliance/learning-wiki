# Web-based review materials plus a course entrance examination significantly boosted second-year medical students' understanding of normal histology relative to historical controls

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cell knowledge and tissue knowledge both increased significantly versus historical controls, with similar magnitude for both. [→ Helle L 2013](#helle-l-2013)

## Evidence

### Helle L 2013

Helle L, Nivala M, Kronqvist P. (2013). More Technology, Better Learning Resources, Better Learning? Lessons from Adopting Virtual Microscopy in Undergraduate Medical Education. Anatomical Sciences Education 6:73-80. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1302

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of the 2010/2011 experimental cohort against 2007/2008 historical controls on a normal histology test taken one week into the course, analyzed with independent sample T-tests. The article reports "the increase was statistically significant in both cases"; no effect size is printed.

> "Cell knowledge increased from M = 18.8 (SD = 7.0) to M = 27.5 (SD = 9.1) and tissue knowledge from M = 3.5 (SD = 1.7) to M = 5.2 (SD = 1.4), and the increase was statistically significant in both cases [t(79) = −4.43, P < 0.001; t(79) = −3.87, P < 0.001]."
