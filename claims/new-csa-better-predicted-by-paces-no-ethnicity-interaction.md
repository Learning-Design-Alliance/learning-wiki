## Evidence

### Wakeford Richard 2015

Wakeford Richard, Denney MeiLing, Ludka-Stempien Katarzyna, Dacre Jane, McManus I C. (2015). Cross-comparison of MRCGP & MRCP(UK) in a database linkage study of 2,284 candidates taking both examinations: assessment of validity and differential performance by ethnicity. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0281-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Forward-entry multiple regression of CSA performance on PACES, BME, CSA type and their interactions (Figure 1). Slopes differed significantly for PACES × BME (b = -.095, p = .009) and PACES × CSA type (b = .098, p = .007), but there was not a significant PACES × BME × CSA type interaction.

> "PACES was the most significant predictor of CSA performance (n = 741, beta = .465)."
