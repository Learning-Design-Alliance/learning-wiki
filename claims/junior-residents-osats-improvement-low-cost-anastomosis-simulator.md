## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In junior residents, significant improvements were found in S-T-S use of Castroviejo needle holder (P=0.042), needle angles (P=0.036), and needle transfer (P=0.029), and in E-T-S graft orientation, spacing, needle holder use, needle angles, suture management, and time (all P<0.05). ## Evidence

### Cubas W Samir MD 2024

Cubas W Samir MD, MSc, Paredes-Temoche Anna MD, Dongo Wildor R., Inga Katherine E., Luna-Victoria Wilfredo MD, Velarde-Revilla Enrique MD. (2024). Construction and Surgical Training of Coronary Anastomosis on a Low-Cost Portable Simulator: Experience in a Peruvian Multicenter Study. Brazilian Journal of Cardiovascular Surgery. https://doi.org/10.21470/1678-9741-2023-0479

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational multicenter study of 140 residents from nine centers, with 55.71% juniors, evaluated over 90 days in two 45-day periods with paired tests. The article reports "a significant improvement in the use of Castroviejo needle holder (P=0.042)" and reduced anastomosis time for juniors.

> "Of the residents, 55.71% were classified as juniors, and it was identified that in S-T-S anastomoses (FP vs. SP), there was a significant improvement in the use of Castroviejo needle holder (P=0.042), needle angles (P=0.036), needle transfer (P=0.029), and anastomosis time (25.39 min. vs. 11.22 min., P=0.037)."
