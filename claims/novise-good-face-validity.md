## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study of 14 surgeons and trainees (4 experts, 7 novices, 3 gastroenterologists) rating 15 face validity statements on a 5-point Likert scale after three simulated procedures. Participants felt looping was "the most realistic aspect of the simulation" and were mainly dissatisfied with the feel of the endoscope hardware.

> "In 14 out of 15 statements ≥50% of all participants’ responses were positive (ie, “agreed” or “strongly agreed”). A majority (n = 9) of participants indicated that the simulator, as well as the difficulty of the simulated procedure, were realistic."
