### Tor Ole B. Odden and Benjamin Zwickl 2024

Tor Ole B. Odden and Benjamin Zwickl. (2024). How Physics Students Develop Disciplinary Computational Literacy. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.15028](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.15028)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Narrative case study of two pairs of 3rd-semester physics students, based on series of interviews before, during, and after an open-ended computational essay project. The authors state "it was their foundation of disciplinary computational literacy that allowed them to begin to engage with the computational literature and plan their analyses."

> "both pairs began a process of epistemic negotiation: that is, negotiation between their interests (as stated in the first interviews), the goal of the assignment as stated in the assignment description and rubric, the computational and theoretical tools at their disposal, and their existing basis of disciplinary computational literacy."
