# Student reports of social skills and perseverance were declining before the pandemic, fell further during remote learning in the middle grades, and remained below 2011 levels through 2022-23

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gwynne 2024

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., Young, C., & Hart, H. (2024). Student experiences through the COVID-19 pandemic: Attendance, online learning platform use, and annual survey reports. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/district-data/metrics/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

5Essentials survey trend analysis (Figure 6, Social Skills and Perseverance measures, grades 6-8 and 9-12, 2011 baseline). Middle-grade reports fell "by 0.16 s.d. and 0.19 s.d. from 2019 to 2021"; no standardized effect size beyond these s.d. changes is printed.

> "They declined even more at the middle grade level during the remote/hybrid year, falling by 0.16 s.d. and 0.19 s.d. from 2019 to 2021. Students continued reporting lower levels of social skills and perseverance through the 2022–23 school year, at levels that were about 0.30 s.d. below what they were in 2011."
