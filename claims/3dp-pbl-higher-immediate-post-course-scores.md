# 3D printing integrated with PBL yields higher immediate post-course knowledge scores than lecture-based teaching for AGP trainees on thoracolumbar fractures

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In a parallel-group RCT, the 3DP group's immediate post-course knowledge test score (median 80.0) was significantly higher than the control group's (median 70.0), with a small-to-medium effect size r = 0.301. [→ Jiang Dingyuan 2025](#jiang-dingyuan-2025)

## Evidence

### Jiang Dingyuan 2025

Jiang Dingyuan, Duan Kui, Li Zhigang, Chen Xueming, Li Nani. (2025). The effect of 3D printing integrated with problem-based learning on AGP training for thoracolumbar fractures: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08152-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group RCT of 80 AGP trainees (3DP n = 40; control n = 40) at Zhuzhou 331 Hospital, analysed with two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests. The "significantly higher" 3DP score on the immediate post-course test carried r = 0.301 [0.087, 0.488].

> "in the post-course test, the score of the 3DP group (median = 80.0, IQR: 75.0–85.0) was significantly higher than that of the control group (median = 70.0, IQR: 67.5–75.0) (U = 523.50, p = 0.007, r = 0.301, 95% CI [0.087, 0.488])"
