A 14-item learning style questionnaire administered to the 125 eleventh-grade students revealed diverse learning styles, with "most students exhibiting a visual learning style (53.06% at SHS A)". The figure-reported distribution is descriptive, with no effect size.

> "With most students exhibiting a visual learning style (53.06% at SHS A), followed by auditory and kinesthetic styles, it is crucial to develop interactive multimedia that is visually engaging and integrates auditory and kinesthetic elements."
