Evidence¶

Lujain Ibrahim 2026¶

Lujain Ibrahim, Franziska Sofia Hafner, Myra Cheng, Cinoo Lee, Rebecca Anselmetti, Robb Willer, Luc Rocher, Diyi Yang. (2026). Sycophantic AI makes human interaction feel more effortful and less satisfying over time. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07912

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Within-subjects study (N=500): participants sampled unlabeled sycophantic, neutral, and challenging AIs in randomized order, then chose one to continue with. Choosers of the sycophantic AI reported it "understood me best" (χ²(1)=32.77, p<0.001 vs. neutral) and "was easiest to talk to"; usefulness ratings did not differ across choice groups (χ²(2)=3.89, p=0.14).

"A majority (54.6%) chose the sycophantic AI, significantly above chance (p <0.001;χ 2(2) = 103.35,w= 0.46)."
