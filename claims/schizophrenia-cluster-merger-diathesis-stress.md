The review reports a study (Kimmerle et al. 2010) analyzing link structures of the Wikipedia article on schizophrenia and related articles in six annual cross-sectional snapshots from 2003 to 2008, using SNA-based clustering (Weaver software). No effect size is printed.

> "Later, in 2008, the social and the biological clusters even merged into one common cluster which mirrored the so-called diathesis-stress model, currently the most accepted explanation model for the genesis of schizophrenia."
