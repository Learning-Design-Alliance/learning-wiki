N.G. Holmes and Z. Yasemin Kalender. (2020). Preliminary evidence for available roles in mixed-gender and all-women lab groups. arXiv:2007.14833v1 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.14833](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.14833)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Dominant roles were estimated by converting each student's activity frequencies to z-scores relative to the class; the dominant-role histogram showed "women in single-gender groups were represented across all activity roles, while women in mixed-gender groups were dominant only on computer, paper, or discussing with their group".

> "We see that women in single-gender groups were represented across all activity roles, while women in mixed-gender groups were dominant only on computer, paper, or discussing with their group. Men were never dominant computer or paper users."
