Instructor report from a Year 2 implementation of the Boltzmann Factor tutorial in an upper-division thermal physics course at a comprehensive public university in the western United States. The instructor split the tutorial into two class periods and gave a short lecture on Taylor series at the start of the second period.

> "The instructor of this course reported that many of his students had great diﬃculty using the Taylor series expansion in the tutorial context even after having com- pleted the pre-tutorial homework."
