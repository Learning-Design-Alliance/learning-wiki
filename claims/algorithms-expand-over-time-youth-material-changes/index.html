A second coding iteration on the same video corpus analyzed variations of the baseline repetitions to answer how the algorithm changed over time, revealing "intra-actions of materials and humans" that pushed back on human intentions.

"The variations physically increased the number of routines that were possible because they added new routines. This process of doing and undoing revealed intra-actions of materials and humans"
