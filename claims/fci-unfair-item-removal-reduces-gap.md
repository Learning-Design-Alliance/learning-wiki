### Traxler 2018

The article's summary of its RQ6 re-analysis: with the gender-unfair items removed, "a substantial portion of the canonical gender gap disappears in at least some samples." The abstract states removing all significantly gender-unfair items halves the gender gap in the main sample.

> "Finally, if these items are removed, as would be common psychometric practice during test development, a substantial portion of the canonical gender gap disappears in at least some samples."
