Xinchen Ma 2026¶

Xinchen Ma, Shuimu Wang, Gaole He, Yanbin Zhang, Chunyang Wang, Yunshi Lan, Weining Qian. (2026). From Content Generation to Learning Support: Pedagogy-Guided Generative Video Tutors for STEM Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24083 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.24083

q2 · i? · design · r2

Reported in the human evaluation section with 32 STEM instructors; experts viewed a pedagogically structured video, a quiz with a simulated incorrect answer, and a remediation video, then rated the workflow. The 96.9% figure is a descriptive percentage, not an effect size.

"Furthermore, 96.9% of participating experts reported that the workflow of “instructional video → assessment → targeted remediation” provides a more effective learning experience than instructional videos alone."
