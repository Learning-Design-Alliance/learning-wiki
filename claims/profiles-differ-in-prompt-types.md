## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The four profiles differed significantly in total metacognitive, cognitive and modeling prompts required (Wilks'Λ = .35, partial η² = .29). [→ Bosma et al. 2017](#bosma-et-al-2017)
`q3 i?` Children in the General profile required significantly fewer metacognitive and cognitive prompts than children in the other profiles (p ≤ .01). [→ Bosma et al. 2017](#bosma-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Bosma et al. 2017

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA with total metacognitive, cognitive and modeling prompts as dependent variables and profile as between-subjects factor in the trained group, with printed effect size partial η² = .29. Follow-up ANOVAs were significant for metacognitive (partial η² = .32), cognitive (partial η² = .57) and modeling prompts (partial η² = .14).

> "Here we found a significant effect for profile: Wilks'Λ = .35, F(9, 129.14) = 7.74, p < .001, partial η²= .29."
