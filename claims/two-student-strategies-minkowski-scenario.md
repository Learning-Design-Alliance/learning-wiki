Qualitative analysis of limited dialogues (episodes) from a teaching experiment with four Greek students of different educational levels during school year 2013-2014. The author reports that "two main students’ strategies were identified", one algebraic-formal and one intuitive. No effect sizes or tests are reported; the study is strictly qualitative.

> "in our research two main students’ strategies were identified. One of them (belonging to the two boys) makes a creative use of preexisting school knowledge in Algebra and Geometry in order to fit the numerical data and provide the required formula."
