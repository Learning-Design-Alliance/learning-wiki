## Evidence

### Sutapa Dey Tithi 2025

Sutapa Dey Tithi, Xiaoyi Tian, Min Chi and Tiffany Barnes. (2025). Investigating the Impact and Student Perceptions of Guided Parsons Problems for Learning Logic with Subgoals. CSEDM'25 Workshop. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.04712](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.04712)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time comparison across the two training conditions in the controlled tutor experiment; Table 2 reports training 0.81 (0.37) vs 1.52 (0.77) hours, level-end 1.87 vs 1.50, posttest 1.02 vs 0.91 hours.

> "The training time for GPP was significantly higher than the control group (GPP: 1.52 hrs vs. Control: 0.81 hrs). But in the training level-end test problems as well as posttest problems, GPP students spent less time on average (no significant difference), suggesting improved problem solving efficiency"
