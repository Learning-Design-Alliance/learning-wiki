## Evidence

### Aneva 2022

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Student survey (stage one) with 308 responses from nine countries asked students to rate the usefulness of regular physics classes, YPT-related activities, and other extracurricular activities for soft-skill development; "the median evaluation of the usefulness of YPT -related activities for soft-skill development was 4 (out of 5)".

> "Although we observed some variation between different types of soft skills, the median evaluation of the usefulness of YPT -related activities for soft-skill development was 4 (out of 5)."
