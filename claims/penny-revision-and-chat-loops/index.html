Evidence¶

Steve Woollaston 2026¶

Steve Woollaston, Brendan Flanagan, Yuko Toyokawa and Hiroaki Ogata. (2026). Penny: Transition Network Analysis of Learner-Chatbot Interactions in Scaffolded EFL Writing. Preprint, accepted at the 1st Workshop on Transition Network Analysis (TNA) at LAK '26. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14575 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.14575

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Transition Network Analysis of log data from 119 Japanese junior high EFL learners using Penny over four months. The network (10 nodes, 26 edges, density 0.29, reciprocity 0.31) showed "a distinct “Revision Loop” and “Chat Loop”"; after feedback, 42.6% revised and 38.3% moved to user_chat.

"The transition probabilities reveal a distinct “Revision Loop” and “Chat Loop” within the learning process."
