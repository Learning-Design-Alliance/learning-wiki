## Evidence

### Aniaku Joshua Kwabena BPH 2019

Aniaku Joshua Kwabena BPH, Amedonu Edem Kojo BPH, Fusheini Adam PhD. (2019). Assessment of Knowledge, Attitude and Vaccination Status of Hepatitis B among Nursing Training Students in Ho, Ghana. Annals of Global Health 85(1): 18. https://doi.org/10.5334/aogh.750

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 358 student nurses in Ho, Ghana, using a self-administered structured questionnaire analyzed in SPSS. Knowledge was a composite of 18 items; "the mean knowledge score was 29.6 (SD ± 6.98)" with 78.2% correctly identifying the viral cause.

> "About 78.2% knew the disease is caused by a virus. Also, 69.8% reported transmission through needle stick injuries, and the mean knowledge score was 29.6 (SD ± 6.98)."
