## Evidence

### Kaijomaa M 2023

Kaijomaa M, Gissler M, Äyräs O, Sten A, Grahn P. (2023). Impact of simulation training on the management of shoulder dystocia and incidence of permanent brachial plexus birth injury: An observational study. BJOG. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.17278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Documentation review of SD cases across pre- and post-training periods. The study reports "The interruption of oxytocin infusion (0% vs 15.8%, p < 0.001)" rose significantly. The authors attribute improved documentation to a fill-in SD checklist and emphasis on documentation during simulation sessions.

> "The interruption of oxytocin infusion (0% vs 15.8%, p < 0.001) and successful posterior arm delivery (11.3% vs 23.4%, p = 0.04) were documented significantly more often in the post‐training period."
