## Evidence

### Kostantinos Mattas 2025

Kostantinos Mattas, Antonio Lucas-Alba, Tomer Toledo, Óscar M. Melchor, Shlomo Bekhor, Biagio Ciuffo. (2025). Learning to Car-Follow Using an Inertia-Oriented Driving Technique: A Before-and-After Study on a Closed Circuit. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.13694)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pretest/posttest comparison of 11 participants' WDC simulator evaluations (Table 1), following a cyclically accelerating-decelerating lead vehicle with eight virtual DD followers. Speed SD fell from 7.59 to 3.20 m/s (d = 1.40, p = .001); fuel consumption fell from 569.78 to 224.17 l (d = 1.11, p = .004).

> "No differences were observed in the mean speed between the pretest and posttest, but there were differences in dispersion, which was lower in the posttest. This lower speed variability in the posttest also translates into lower fuel consumption."
