## Evidence

### Gehret 2025

Gehret, A. U., & Elliot, L. B. (2025). Perceptions of e-learning by deaf and hard of hearing students using asynchronous multimedia tutorials. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10476-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey feedback from DHH students on asynchronous multimedia tutorials showed the most prevalent improvement suggestions concerned enhancing playback and quizzing features, aligning with the CTML segmenting principle.

> "The most prevalent feedback received for improvements to the tutorials described enhancing the playback and quizzing features. This was not surprising as an earlier finding in the survey indicated that nearly all respondents identified the ability to control playback as a feature they used and as one that was helpful."
