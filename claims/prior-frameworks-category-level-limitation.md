## Evidence

### Fiorella 2023

Fiorella, L. (2023). Making Sense of Generative Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09769-7

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

Theoretical argument in the review's Summary and Limitations of Prior Frameworks section. The author argues the existing frameworks share general mechanisms (organizing, integrating, inference generation) and notes 'we do not have a coherent framework' for how instructor-provided and learner-generated representations interact. No empirical data; no effect size.

> "they emphasize differences across categories of learning activities (generative vs. non-generative or constructive vs. active) rather than differences within categories. Consequently, the frameworks are unable to specify how different types of GLAs uniquely and collectively contribute to sense-making."
