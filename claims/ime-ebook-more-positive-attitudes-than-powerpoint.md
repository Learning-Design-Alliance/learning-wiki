## Evidence

### Hsiao Chih-Cheng 2016

Hsiao Chih-Cheng, Tiao Mao-Meng, Chen Chih-Cheng. (2016). Using interactive multimedia e-Books for learning blood cell morphology in pediatric hematology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0816-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-question five-point Likert feedback questionnaire administered at the end of the randomized trial. Agreement percentages favored IME on every question; for example, "only 24% answered "agree" in the TPP group versus 92.3% in the IME group" on core skills.
