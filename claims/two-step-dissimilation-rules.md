# Dissimilation is stated as a two-step rule: delete the generic cognate noun, then replace the verb with a pro-verb

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The rules for dissimilation consist of deleting the generic cognate noun under F (or O) and replacing the verb with the appropriate pro-verb, both optional with verbs like sing. [→ Babcock 1967](#babcock-1967)
