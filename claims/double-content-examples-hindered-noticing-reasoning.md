# Studying double-content examples before video-based analyses hindered student teachers' noticing and reasoning compared to studying without them

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Student teachers studying without double-content examples showed significantly greater increases in noticing + reasoning than those studying with complete or faded double-content examples. [→ Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025](#jasmin-lilian-bauersfeld-2025)

## Evidence

### Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-post study of N = 410 student teachers in online self-study seminars; one-way ANOVAs on residualized change scores showed a medium effect for noticing + reasoning, η² = .068, F(2,406) = 14.68, p < .001, with the control group outperforming both intervention groups.

> "One-way ANOV As showed significant differences between the groups for noticing + reasoning (with a medium effect), concepts of classroom management (with a large effect), and the focus on students (with a small effect)"
