## Evidence

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Module-level analysis of the mixed-effects model of questionnaire scores (Table 2). All modules improved significantly; baseline was lowest for Module 2 (2.7) and Module 4 (3.1), covering cCMV effects and prevention strategies.

> "Investigation of changes in adjusted mean scores by module revealed significant improvements across all modules between T1 and T2 (P < 0.001). Participant knowledge varied across modules, with the lowest adjusted baseline mean scores for Module 2 and Module 4 (adjusted mean T1 scores 2.7 and 3.1, respectively)."

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022 (2)

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Module 5, on the midwife's role in counselling about CMV, showed high scores at all time points (adjusted means 4.5, 4.9, 4.7) with no significant change between T2–T3 or T1–T3, indicating a ceiling effect.

> "Participants had high scores at all time points for Module 5 (adjusted mean at T1:4.5, T2 4.9, T3 4.7) with no significant changes observed between T2 and T3 or between T1 and T3."
