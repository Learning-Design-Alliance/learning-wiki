### Koomen 2016

Michele Hollingsworth Koomen. (2016). Practitioner Inquiry with Early Program Teacher Candidates. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i11.1915

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Beatrice and Hannah tallied active attention at two-minute intervals across five science and math lessons in their second grade classroom. Despite planning attention getters, they rarely used them during teaching and instead talked over students.

> "They were astonished to find that for the majority of the 350 minutes of instruction only 25% of their students were actively attentive in the content that they were teaching (F=11.68, df=2,p <.002)."
