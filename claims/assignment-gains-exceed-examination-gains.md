Lei Fan and Fangxue Liu. (2026). Impacts of generative AI skill development on academic adaptation and learning outcomes among university students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.30599](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.30599)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-person contrast (assignment vs. examination gains, D1 vs. D2) in the 313-respondent retrospective questionnaire; mean difference 0.204 points, dz = 0.294, Holm-adjusted p < 0.001. Interviewees attributed limited examination benefits partly to restrictions on GenAI use.

> "Overall academic improvement was endorsed by 78.9% of students, while 74.7% reported assignment-grade gains compared with 61.4% for examination grades."
