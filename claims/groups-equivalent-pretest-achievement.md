## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-test scores did not differ significantly between groups (t = 1.6, p = 0.11), supporting group comparability. [→ Coşkun and Eker 2018](#coskun-and-eker-2018)

## Evidence

### Coşkun and Eker 2018

İsmail Coşkun, Cevat Eker. (2018). The Effect of Teaching Activities Done by Using Activity Based Posters on the Students' Academic Achievements, Retention Levels in Their Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(4): 585-597. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.060402

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t test on pre-test scores of 30 experimental and 30 control ninth graders; Table 5 prints means of 9.63 and 8.03, t = 1.6, p = 0.11. The article concludes the groups were "similar in terms of academic achievement scores before the experiment".

> "At the end of pre-testing of the groups, t test was  used to determine whether there was a significant difference between the scores obtained and it was found that there was no statistically significant difference with the value obtained [p <0.05]."
