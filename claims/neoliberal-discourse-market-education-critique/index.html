Edda Sant 2019¶

Edda Sant. (2019). Democratic Education: A Theoretical Review (2006–2017). Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654319862493

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's analysis of the neoliberal version, connected to aggregative theories of democracy, states "Neoliberals recommend the replacement of public education with free market practices (Ichilov, 2012)," citing school choice policies and standards/accountability regimes such as PISA and the Common Core as examples.

Edda Sant 2019 (2)¶

Edda Sant. (2019). Democratic Education: A Theoretical Review (2006–2017). Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654319862493

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

In the Debates and Critiques section the review reports that "Research conducted in Australia, the United States, and the United Kingdom suggests that "choice" practices privilege the middle and the upper classes (Meens & Howe, 2016; Perry, 2009)," and that standards and assessment procedures contribute to inequality by sorting students.
