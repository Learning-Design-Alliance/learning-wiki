### Lachner 2024

Lachner, A., Sibley, L., & Wagner, S. (2024). Practice Recommendations or Not? The LoGeT Model as Empirical Approach to Generate Localized, Generalized, and Transferable Evidence. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09876-z

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article reports, citing Fyfe et al. (2021), a ManyClasses within-participants study randomly assigning treatment orders per class assignment and classes to incentive conditions, implemented in "38 different classrooms" with "46 different instructors and N = 2081 students." No effect of feedback timing or incentives was detected.

> "Surprisingly, the authors did not find an effect of delayed versus immediate feedback and study incentives. Preregistered moderation analyses of 40 different moderators did not obtain strong evidence for systematic interactions."
