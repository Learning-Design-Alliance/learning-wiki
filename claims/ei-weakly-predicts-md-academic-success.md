`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Three-level multilevel meta-analysis of 20 studies (105 effect sizes; N = 4,227) published 2005–2021, synthesizing Pearson correlations between EI and academic success. The pooled effect was "r = .13", significant at p < .01, described by the authors as weak.

> "Findings from 20 studies (m = 105; N = 4,227) indicated a positive correlation between EI and academic success (r = .13, 95% CI [.08, – .27], p < .01)."
