# Experts rated the haptic interaction between instruments and artificial anatomy as less realistic and less learnable than intermediates and novices did

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Statistically significant group differences were found for haptic interaction ratings, with experts (2.58 ± 0.76) rating realism lower than intermediates (3.71 ± 0.89, p = 0.002) and novices (3.54 ± 0.89, p = 0.015). [→ Gmeiner M 2025](#gmeiner-m-2025)

(2025). Development and external validation of a mixed-reality aneurysm clipping simulator. Neurosurgical Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10143-025-03846-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Group comparison in the 40-neurosurgeon validation study: experts rated haptic interaction realism significantly lower than intermediates and novices, tested with Mann–Whitney U or Student's T-test as appropriate at p ≤ 0.05.

> "Statistically significant differences were found for the haptic interaction between instruments and artificial anatomy (experts: 2.58 ± 0.76, intermediates: 3.71 ± 0.89, novices: 3.54 ± 0.89) with p-values 0.002 for experts vs. intermediates and 0.015 for experts vs. novices"

### Gmeiner M 2025 (2)

Gmeiner M, Schrempf A, Thurner T, Fenz W, Sabrowsky-Hirsch B, Giretzlehner M, Prückl R, Schaffelhofer S, Major Z, Lämmermann S, Baumgartner M, Drabauer L, Nagy J, Esposito G, Colombo E, Stroh-Holly N, Gruber A. (2025). Development and external validation of a mixed-reality aneurysm clipping simulator. Neurosurgical Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10143-025-03846-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same validation study, the learnability of haptic interaction with the aneurysm was rated significantly lower by experts than novices (p-value: 0.016), with intermediates at 3.65 ± 1.19.

> "Consequently, experts rated that the haptic interaction with the aneurysm can be learned with 2.92 ± 1.11, where the rates of intermediates and novices were 3.65 ± 1.19 and 4.00 ± 0.74, respectively. Statistically significant differences were found here between experts and novices (p-value: 0.016)."
