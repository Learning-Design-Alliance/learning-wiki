## Evidence

### Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis 2019

Casey Inez Canfield & Baruch Fischhoff & Alex Davis. (2019). Better beware: comparing metacognition for phishing and legitimate emails. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09197-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of detection accuracy across the pooled sample, compared with similar internal consistency observed by Kleitman et al. (α = 0.81 legitimate, α = 0.80 phishing). The article states these differences support analyzing phishing and legitimate emails separately.

> "Cronbach’s alpha for detection accuracy was higher for legitimate ( α = 0.77) and phishing (α = 0.77) emails considered separately, than for all the emails ( α = 0.59), indicating greater consistency within the two categories than overall."
