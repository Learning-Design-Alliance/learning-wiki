## Evidence

### Si Chen 2026

Si Chen, Haocong Cheng, Suzy Su, Lu Ming, Sarah Masud, Qi Wang, and Yun Huang. (2026). Redesigning Educational Videos for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Learners. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3772318.3791470](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791470)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

TLX post-hoc analysis in the 16-participant study (five clip pairs for D-Align); interview participants found D-Align generally helpful in optimizing when and where to look, though three noted challenges focusing on captions and visuals simultaneously.

> "D-Align also led to a significant increase in learning satisfaction (EMM = 0.68, t(39.3) = -2.61, p < .05), though it showed no significant effects on mental demand, physical demand, or temporal pressure."

### Si Chen 2026 (2)

Si Chen, Haocong Cheng, Suzy Su, Lu Ming, Sarah Masud, Qi Wang, and Yun Huang. (2026). Redesigning Educational Videos for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Learners. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3772318.3791470](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791470)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

TLX post-hoc analysis in the 16-participant study (three clip pairs for D-Declutter); interviews reported positive feedback on colorized text but some participants found it distracting or harder to read, and raised accessibility concerns for DeafBlind learners.

> "D-Declutter also led to a significant increase in learning satisfaction (EMM = 0.81, t(69.1) = -2.68, p < .05), though it showed no significant effects on other demands."
