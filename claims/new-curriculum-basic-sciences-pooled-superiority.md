### Hamamoto Filho PT 2021

Hamamoto Filho PT, Lourenção PLTA, Abbade JF, Cecílio-Fernandes D, Caramori JT, Bicudo AM. (2021). Exploring pooled analysis of pretested items to monitor the performance of medical students exposed to different curriculum designs. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0257293

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of first-year cohorts at BMS-UNESP on the same pretested items, analyzed with fixed-effects meta-analysis per content area. The pooled basic sciences result showed "the 2019 students had superior performance (OR = 1.172" with p = 0.043; item-level differences were three superior and one inferior among 17 items.

> "In the basic sciences, the pooled analysis showed that the 2019 students had superior performance (OR = 1.172 [CI95% 1.005 CI 1.366], p = 0.043). Among the 17 items, the 2019 students’ performance was statistically different on four items: superior on three items and inferior on one."
