Once Early-Reading Program: Evidence Rating Summary 2025¶

Once Early-Reading Program: Evidence Rating Summary. (2025). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/once-early-reading-program/

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

A quasi-experimental study in 13 schools in a large urban Tennessee district during the 2024-2025 school year compared Once kindergarten students, who "scored below the 50th percentile on a beginning-of-year literacy screener", with comparison peers on end-of-year FastBridge scores. The printed effect size is +0.17.

"Once students outperformed comparison peers on end-of-year FastBridge Early Reading Composite scores (effect size +0.17), qualifying Once for a Promising rating."
