### Siddiq F. 2015

Siddiq F., Scherer R. & Tondeur J. (2015). Teachers’ Emphasis on Developing Students’ Digital Information and Communication Skills (TEDDICS): A New Construct in 21st Century Education. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.10.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational SEM analysis (Table 4) of the ICILS 2013 teacher sample showed positive associations between the three TEDDICS factors and ICT self-efficacy, ICT use, and perceived usefulness; the highest correlation was for the collaboration-assigned factors (ρ = .72).

> "In sum, our findings indicated that high levels of TEDDICS were significantly related to high levels of self-efficacy, ICT use, and perceived usefulness."
