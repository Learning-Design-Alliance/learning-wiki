Descriptive section (§2.3) on remote sensing data as a source of geographic knowledge and skills. It lists knowledge gained in practical work, including "definition of geographical objects size (length, width, perimeter, area, volume) and distances in the area" and identification of indicators such as Earth surface temperature and vegetation spectral brightness. This is methodological argument, not an empirical test.
