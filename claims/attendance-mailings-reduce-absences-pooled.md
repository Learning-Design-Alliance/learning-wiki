## Evidence

### Rogers and Feller 2016

Todd Rogers, Avi Feller. (2016). Parent Beliefs and Student Absences: Large Absence-Reduction Field Experiment. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2016/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized field experiment in a major metropolitan district (about 30,000 students, grades 1-12, 2014-15), four randomly assigned conditions with 5 mailings; OLS regressions and one-sided Fisher Randomization Tests. The article reports pooled mailings "reduce absences by 0.9 days (or a 5.3% decrease)" versus control, p<.01.

> "We see strong evidence that the attendance mail reduced absences. All treatment mailings pooled reduce absences by 0.9 days (or a 5.3% decrease) as compared to the Control condition (p<.01)."
