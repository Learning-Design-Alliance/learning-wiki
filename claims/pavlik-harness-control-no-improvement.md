## Evidence

### Moktar Joel MD 2016

Moktar Joel MD, Bradley Catharine S. BScPT MSc, Maxwell Alexandra BSc, Wedge John H. MD FRCS(C), Kelley Simon P. MBChB FRCS(Tr&Orth), Murnaghan M. Lucas MD MEd FRCSC. (2016). Skill Acquisition and Retention Following Simulation-Based Training in Pavlik Harness Application. The Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery. American volume. https://doi.org/10.2106/JBJS.15.00905

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Eleven randomly allocated control residents and allied health professionals were retested thirty minutes after baseline without module exposure. Paired-sample t tests showed no change, and no control participant was scored as competent on any measure at either time point.

> "the controls were found to be significantly worse than all other groups at time point 2 for all three measures (p < 0.001). Paired-sample t tests revealed no difference for the control subjects between time point 1 and time point 2 testing for any of the measures."
