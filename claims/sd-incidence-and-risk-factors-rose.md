## Evidence

### Kaijomaa M 2023

Kaijomaa M, Gissler M, Äyräs O, Sten A, Grahn P. (2023). Impact of simulation training on the management of shoulder dystocia and incidence of permanent brachial plexus birth injury: An observational study. BJOG. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.17278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Register-based analysis of all 113,785 vertex deliveries. The study reports "the incidence of SD increased from 0.1% (1.2/1000 deliveries) to 0.3% (3.4/1000 deliveries) (p < 0.001)"; Table 1 additionally shows gestational diabetes (12.3% vs 18.5%), induction (21.4% vs 25.2%) and vacuum extraction (12.5% vs 13.3%) significantly higher post-training.

> "the incidence of SD increased from 0.1% (1.2/1000 deliveries) to 0.3% (3.4/1000 deliveries) (p < 0.001)."
