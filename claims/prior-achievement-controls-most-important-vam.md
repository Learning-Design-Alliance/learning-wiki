## Evidence

### Koedel 2015

Koedel, C., Mihaly, K., & Rockoff, J. E. (2015). Value-Added Modeling: A Review. Preprint, forthcoming at Economics of Education Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Value-Added+Modeling+A+Review+Koedel

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review report of Chetty, Friedman and Rockoff (2014a) sensitivity analyses varying lagged-achievement controls within their quasi-experimental design. Bias "rises from 3.82 percent" to "10.25 percent (statistically significant)" as controls are stripped down; the review concludes prior-achievement controls are the most important covariates by far.

> "Their quasi-experimental estimate of bias rises from 3.82 percent (statistically insignificant) with all controls for prior achievement, to 4.83 percent (statistically insignificant) with only controls for a student's own prior scores in both subjects, to 10.25 percent (statistically significant) with only controls for a student's prior score in the same subject."
