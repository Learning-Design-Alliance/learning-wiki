### Kwan Connie Ching-Yin 2025

Kwan Connie Ching-Yin, Weir Kelly Ann, Fong Raymond, Pereira Valerie Jane, Law Thomas. (2025). Integration of team-based learning and simulation-based learning in clinical education of infant feeding and swallowing assessment and management of speech-language pathology students: a retrospective pre-post intervention study. `q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Voluntary pre/post self-rating survey (20 questions, 5-point Likert, CBOS-derived areas) completed by 25 students; Wilcoxon signed rank test with significance set at p <.05. The article reports "z = 4.37, p <.05, r =.87".

> "students' overall clinical confidence after the clinic (Mean = 62.1) was significantly higher than that before the clinic (Mean = 30), z = 4.37, p <.05, r =.87."
