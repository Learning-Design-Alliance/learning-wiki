## Evidence

### Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024

Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino, Anita Zanella, Luca Di Mascolo, Michele Ginolfi, Nicolò Carpita, Francesco Trovato Manuncola, Massimo Grassi. (2024). Evaluating the effectiveness of sonification in science education using Edukoi. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-024-01809-5

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Behavioural test in T1 with 146 middle-school students using Edukoi with laptop screens covered; one-sample t-test against 33% chance gave d = 3.59, with "an average colour identification performance of 86% and a median value of 100%".

> "Overall, the performance of the students was much higher than chance (i.e. 33%): t(145) = 30.70, p < 0.001, d = 3.59, BF = 5.180432e + 61."

### Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024 (2)

Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino, Anita Zanella, Luca Di Mascolo, Michele Ginolfi, Nicolò Carpita, Francesco Trovato Manuncola, Massimo Grassi. (2024). Evaluating the effectiveness of sonification in science education using Edukoi. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-024-01809-5

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Behavioural test in T2: 148 students identified red/blue colour of twelve stylised galaxy sketches through sound, with no prior re-explanation of the mapping; d = 4.12 and average performance was 93%.

> "The students’ responses were highly above chance: t(147) = 35.48, p < 0.0001, d = 4.12, BF = 1.920099e + 70."
