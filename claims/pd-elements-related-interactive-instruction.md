# Frequency, content, and quality of professional development are each positively related to elementary teachers' use of interactive instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers who participate more frequently in professional development make greater use of interactive teaching methods than teachers on average. [→ Mark A. Smylie 2001](#mark-a-smylie-2001)
`q2 i?` Teachers receiving 15 or more hours of professional development in small group instruction or integrated curriculum were more likely to use interactive approaches than average. [→ Mark A. Smylie 2001](#mark-a-smylie-2001)
