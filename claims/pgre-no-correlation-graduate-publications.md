## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Emily M. Levesque 2015

Emily M. Levesque, Rachel Bezanson, & Grant R. Tremblay. (2015). Physics GRE Scores of Prize Postdoctoral Fellows in Astronomy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis comparing self-reported PGRE percentiles to first-author publication counts at career stages, shown in Figure 4 with Pearson r statistics and p values per panel. The undergraduate correlation is "marginal evidence" with r = 0.16; the graduate-school correlation is not significant.

> "While there is marginal evidence ( p = 0.047) of a weak (r = 0.16) correlation between PGRE score and number of papers published as an undergraduate, there is no statistically signiﬁcant ( p < 0.05) correlation between PGRE score and total ﬁrst author papers during or exiting graduate school among 147 prize fellows"
