Evidence¶

Jacob 2019¶

Jacob, L., Lachner, A., & Scheiter, K. (2019). Learning by Explaining Orally or In Written Form? Text Difficulty Matters. https://aspredicted.org/sv3zv.pdf

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Mixed factorial ANCOVA on inference-based questions with learning activity as between-students factor and text difficulty as within-students factor, controlling for prior knowledge. The article reports "a significant interaction between learning activity and text difficulty"; planned contrasts showed oral explaining outperformed written explaining, t(111) = 1.81, p = .037, d = 0.32.

"However, in line with our hypothesis, we found a significant interaction between learning activity and text difficulty, F(2, 111) = 4.67, p = .011, η²p = .023."
