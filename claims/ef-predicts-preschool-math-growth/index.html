Tara Hofkens 2022¶

Tara Hofkens, Jessica Whittaker, Robert Pianta, Virginia Vitiello, Erik Ruzek, Arya Ansari. (2022). Pathways of mathematics achievement in preschool: Examining executive function and task orientation. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appdev.2022.101432

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Longitudinal study of a racially/ethnically and linguistically diverse sample of 467 preschoolers testing whether EF at the beginning of pre-kindergarten predicts growth in mathematics achievement across the pre-kindergarten year; the abstract states this as the study's first research question.

"we used longitudinal data from a racially/ethnically and linguistically diverse sample of 467 preschoolers to examine (1) if children’s EF at the beginning of pre-kindergarten predicts growth in their mathematics achievement across the pre-kindergarten year"
