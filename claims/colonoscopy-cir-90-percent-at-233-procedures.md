## Evidence

### Ward Stephen Thomas 2014

Ward Stephen Thomas, Mohammed Mohammed A, Walt Robert, Valori Roland, Ismail Tariq, Dunckley Paul. (2014). An analysis of the learning curve to achieve competency at colonoscopy using the JETS database. Gut. https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2013-305973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Moving average analysis of 297 UK trainees' 36,730 colonoscopies from the JETS e-portfolio, with mean CIR calculated over blocks of 20 procedures. The study reports "The mean CIR reached the standard of 90% at 233 colonoscopies."

> "The mean CIR was 67.9%, 76.2% and 84.1% after 100, 150 and 200 procedures, respectively. The mean CIR reached the standard of 90% at 233 colonoscopies."
