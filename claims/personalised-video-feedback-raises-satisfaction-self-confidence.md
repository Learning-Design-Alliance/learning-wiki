## Evidence

### Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema 2026

Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema. (2026). The Effect of Personalised Feedback in Simulation on Knowledge and Skills in the Management of Bleeding During Pregnancy: A Parallel Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70603

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Parallel RCT with 58 second-year midwifery students randomised to personalised video-assisted feedback or structured self-debriefing. The study group scored higher on the Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale, "associated with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.89)".

> "post‐test total scores for student satisfaction and self‐confidence were significantly higher in the study group (44.8 ± 3.0) than in the control group (38.8 ± 9.0) (Mann–Whitney U = 245.0, p = 0.006). The difference between groups was associated with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.89)."
