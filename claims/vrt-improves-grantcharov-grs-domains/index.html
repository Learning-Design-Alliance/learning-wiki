Humm Gemma; Mohan Helen Peter; Fleming Christina; Harries Rhiannon; Wood Christopher; Dawas Khaled; Stoyanov Danail; Lovat Laurence B. (2022). The impact of virtual reality simulation training on operative performance in laparoscopic cholecystectomy: meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrac086

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Single-centre RCT randomizing 20 participants to VR training or no additional training, assessed in the operating theatre pre- and post-intervention using the authors' five-domain GRS with eight participants analysed per arm; "economy of movement (P = 0.003) and error (P = 0.003)". The article notes only part of the procedure was assessed, excluding dissection of the hepatocystic triangle.

"This study found that the VR group had significantly greater improvement in their economy of movement (P = 0.003) and error (P = 0.003) GRSs compared with the control group39"
