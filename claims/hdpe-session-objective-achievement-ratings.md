### Kelly J 2020

Kelly J, Oza SK, Feinn R, Cassese T. (2020). Implementation of a Hypothesis-Driven Physical Exam Session in a Transition to Clerkship Program. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11043

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postsession survey of 140 students rating achievement of learning objectives on a four-point Likert scale, with Not at All/Slightly and Moderately/Completely responses collapsed. The article reports "More than 80%" achieved objectives and "85%" learned differential-driven maneuver selection.

> "More than 80% of students indicated that they had moderately or completely achieved all of the of the expressed learning objectives, with 85% indicating that they learned to make broad differential diagnoses and determine which PE maneuvers were most useful based on the differential diagnosis and clinical reasoning (Table 2)."
