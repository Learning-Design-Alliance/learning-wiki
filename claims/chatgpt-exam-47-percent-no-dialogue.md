## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On the mechanics final exam taken without dialogue or retries, ChatGPT scored 14 of 30 points (47%) on answer correctness; hand-grading would realistically have yielded between 46% and 50%, since five wrong answers stemmed from numerical errors while five correct answers used flawed reasoning.
