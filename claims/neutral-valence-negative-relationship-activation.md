# Neutral-valence segments show a significant negative relationship with physiological activation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When groups showed no observable emotional expressions (neutral valence), they were less often in a physiologically activating state; no significant relationships emerged between positive, negative, or mixed valence and activation. [→ Törmänen 2021](#tormanen-2021)

## Evidence

### Törmänen 2021

Törmänen, T., Järvenoja, H., & Mänty, K. (2021). Exploring groups’ affective states during collaborative learning: what triggers activating affect on a group level? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10037-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Adjusted residual analysis of the valence × activation contingency table (Table 2) found "z = − 3.0, p < 0.001" for neutral valence with activation; positive, negative, and mixed valences showed no significant relationships.

> "The significant negative relationship was found between neutral valence and physiologically activating (z = − 3.0, p < 0.001) segments"
