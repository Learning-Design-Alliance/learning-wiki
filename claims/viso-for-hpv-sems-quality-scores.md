### Wang D 2016

Wang D, Cunningham R, Boom J, Amith M, Tao C. (2016). Towards a HPV Vaccine Knowledgebase for Patient Education Content. Studies in Health Technology and Informatics. [doi:10.3233/978-1-61499-658-3-432](https://doi.org/10.3233/978-1-61499-658-3-432)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Automated evaluation of the initial HPV ontology using the SEMS web-based semiotic metric tool, which rates syntactic, semantic, and pragmatic quality. The authors report "The overall quality score based on the three quality aspects amounted to 0.73", with a semantic aspect score of 0.93 (interpretability 0.88, consistency 0.97, clarity 0.96) and a pragmatic score of 0.50 based only on comprehensiveness.

> "The overall quality score based on the three quality aspects amounted to 0.73. The syntactic score is 0.76 and is comprised of lawfulness and richness qualities, which are 1.00 and 0.51, respectively."
