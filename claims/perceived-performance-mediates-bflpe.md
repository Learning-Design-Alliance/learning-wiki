## Evidence

### Lisa Hasenbein 2024

Lisa Hasenbein, Ulrich Trautwein, Jens-Uwe Hahn, Stephan Soller, Richard Göllner. (2024). An experimental test of the Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect using an immersive virtual reality classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09646-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

A regression-based mediation analysis (Hayes procedure, 10,000 bootstrapped samples) in the IVR experiment added perceived performance level as mediator, reducing the direct 80% vs. 20% effect to nonsignificance (β = −0.04, p = 0.267) with a significant indirect effect of β = −0.05; no standardized effect size for the indirect path beyond β is printed.

> "The indirect effect on situational self-concept calculated with 10,000 bootstrapped samples for the pairwise comparison of the 80% vs. 20% hand-raising condition and the perceived performance level as a mediator was small but statistically significant (β = −0.05, SE = 0.02, p < 0.001, 95% CI [−0.08, -0.02])"
