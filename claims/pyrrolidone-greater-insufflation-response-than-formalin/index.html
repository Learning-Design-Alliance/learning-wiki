Descriptive comparative study reported in the review (Nagase et al. 2020), comparing pyrrolidone-embalmed and formalin-embalmed donors in the same cohort; the review reports the significantly greater insufflation response and hedges it as possibly suggesting better suitability. No effect size printed.

"Due to its softer anatomy, the changes in abdominal diameter and circumference in response to CO2 insufflation was significantly greater in the pyrrolidone group, which possibly suggests its better suitability than formalin embalmed body donors for laparoscopic simulation training."
