### Singh 2023

Singh, H.; Tayarani-Najaran, M.-H.; Yaqoob, M. (2023). Exploring Computer Science Students' Perception of ChatGPT in Higher Education: A Descriptive and Correlation Study. Educ. Sci. 2023, 13, 924. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13090924

Descriptive result from Question 11 on how ChatGPT best aids learning computer science, from the 430-student survey; generating codes was chosen by around 18%, writing reports by 12%, and preparing for assessments was lowest at around 6%.

> "Around 47% of the students have indicated that they think the tool is most useful if it is used to obtain explanations about their doubts."
