`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's challenge analysis (Section 3.2.1) argues that "some students may be tested on content they did not have an opportunity to learn," citing Standard 12.8's requirement that evidence of opportunity to learn be provided before high-stakes use. Potential solutions include physical control via custom blueprints or statistical control via removing or down-weighting untaught items.

> "If a distributed blueprint is used in a TCSA, but districts follow different pacing guides that advocate conflicting instructional sequences, some students may be tested on content they did not have an opportunity to learn."
