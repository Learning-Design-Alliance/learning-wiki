Comparative evaluation of knowledge levels and attitudes regarding periodontal health and periodontal disease among internal medicine physicians and dentists in Turkey: a cross-sectional survey study. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analysis (Table 6) comparing specialists (n = 47) and residents (n = 60) with Mann–Whitney U tests; domain-level differences were significant in all four domains (Domain 1 p = 0.001, Domain 2 p = 0.004, Domain 3 p = 0.024, Domain 4 p = 0.031).

> "Specialists demonstrated significantly higher total knowledge scores than residents (16.94 ± 2.90 vs. 14.38 ± 3.73, p < 0.001)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IMS-only linear regression (Table 9, n = 107); age was not a significant predictor (p = 0.885) when specialist status was included, which the authors interpret as the gap reflecting training level and clinical experience rather than age.

> "Within the IMS group, specialist status was independently associated with 2.65 points higher knowledge after adjusting for age, sex, and marital status (B = 2.654, 95% CI: 0.75–4.56, p = 0.007; Table 9)."
