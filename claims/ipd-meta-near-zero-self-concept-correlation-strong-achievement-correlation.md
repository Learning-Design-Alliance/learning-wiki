# Students' math and verbal self-concepts are near-zero correlated even though their math and verbal achievement levels are strongly positively correlated

## Subclaims

`q4 i0` Across the pooled IPD meta-analysis dataset, the correlation between math and verbal self-concepts was close to zero and nonsignificant (r = .02), whereas math and verbal achievement levels correlated strongly positively (r = .65). [→ Fabian Wolff · Jens Möller 2022](#fabian-wolff-jens-moller-2022)

`q3 i0` In Study 1, math and German achievements correlated strongly positively at each measurement point (r = .46 to .55) while self-concept correlations were small or close to zero (r = .04 to .13). [→ Wolff et al. 2020](#wolff-et-al-2020)

## Evidence

### Fabian Wolff · Jens Möller 2022

Fabian Wolff · Jens Möller. (2022). An Individual Participant Data Meta-analysis of the Joint Effects of Social, Dimensional, and Temporal Comparisons on Students' Academic Self-Concepts. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:2569–2608. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09686-1

`q4 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Preliminary bivariate correlation analysis of the pooled IPD dataset (N = 45,248) in the meta-analysis. The near-zero self-concept correlation ("r = .02, p = .71") alongside the strong achievement correlation ("r = .65, p < .001") replicates the paradox motivating the I/E model.

> "the correlation between students' math and verbal self-concepts was close to zero and nonsignificant (r = .02, p = .71), whereas the correlation between their math and verbal achievement levels was strongly positive (r = .65, p < .001)"

### Wolff et al. 2020

Fabian Wolff, Fabio Sticca, Christoph Niepel, Thomas Götz, Jan Van Damme, and Jens Möller. (2020). The Reciprocal 2I/E Model: An Investigation of Mutual Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept Levels and Changes in the Math and Verbal Domain Across Three Countries. https://osf.io/7f6gb/

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 preliminary correlational analysis (N = 1,508). Achievement correlations were r = .46 to .55, whereas math–German self-concept correlations were r = .13 at T2 and r = .04 to .06 at T4 and T6, replicating the paradoxical I/E pattern the model explains via social and dimensional comparisons.

> "Whereas students' math and German achievements showed strong positive correlations at each measurement point (all .46 ≤ r ≤ .55, all p < .001), the correlations between their math and German self-concepts were small and positive at T2 (r = .13, p < .001) and close to zero at T4 and T6 (all .04 ≤ r ≤ .06, all p ≥ .11)."
