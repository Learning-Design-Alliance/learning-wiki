# Students' math and verbal self-concepts are near-zero correlated even though their math and verbal achievement levels are strongly positively correlated

## Subclaims
`q4 i0` Across the pooled IPD meta-analysis dataset, the correlation between math and verbal self-concepts was close to zero and nonsignificant (r = .02), whereas math and verbal achievement levels correlated strongly positively (r = .65). ## Evidence

### Fabian Wolff · Jens Möller 2022

Fabian Wolff · Jens Möller. (2022). An Individual Participant Data Meta-analysis of the Joint Effects of Social, Dimensional, and Temporal Comparisons on Students' Academic Self-Concepts. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:2569–2608. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09686-1

`q4 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Preliminary bivariate correlation analysis of the pooled IPD dataset (N = 45,248) in the meta-analysis. The near-zero self-concept correlation ("r = .02, p = .71") alongside the strong achievement correlation ("r = .65, p < .001") replicates the paradox motivating the I/E model.

> "the correlation between students' math and verbal self-concepts was close to zero and nonsignificant (r = .02, p = .71), whereas the correlation between their math and verbal achievement levels was strongly positive (r = .65, p < .001)"
