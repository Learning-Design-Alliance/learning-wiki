### Hu 2022

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with measurement uncertainty of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-year comparison of post-test scores for questions unchanged between years (Q1, Q2, Q4a, Q5a-c) found post-test performance does not differ appreciably between years. The Peer Instruction feature was largely forgone online but realized in full in person.

> "It is interesting that students performed about equally well on the post-test for both administrations, given that the online learning environment had greatly reduced opportunity for peers to discuss their responses with each other ."
