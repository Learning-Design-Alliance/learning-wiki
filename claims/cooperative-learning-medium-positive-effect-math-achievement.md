`q3 i2` Across 26 studies (36 comparisons, 1988–2010), the random-effects combined effect size of cooperative learning on mathematics achievement was d++ = 0.59 (95% CI 0.38–0.80), a medium effect under Cohen's classification. [→ Gulfer Capar 2015](#gulfer-capar-2015)

## Evidence

### Gulfer Capar 2015

Gulfer Capar, Kamuran Tarim. (2015). Efficacy of the Cooperative Learning Method on Mathematics Achievement and Attitude: A Meta-Analysis Research. Educational Sciences Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.2.2098

`q3 · i2`

Meta-analysis of 26 experimental studies (n = 36 comparisons) from 1988–2010 with pre/post-test and control or equated group designs, computed with MetaWin 2.0 under a random effects model. The combined effect size was "the effect size mean ES = 0.59", classified as medium per Cohen (1988).

> "In general, according to the random effect size model, the upper limit of the confidence interval was found to be 0.80, the lower limit was 0.38 and the effect size mean ES = 0.59. This indicated that the cooperative learning method was more influential than the traditional learning method for academic achievement."
