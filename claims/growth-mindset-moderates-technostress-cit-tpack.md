## Evidence

### Su Wenhao 2025

Su Wenhao, Fadzil Hidayah Mohd, Rauf Rose Amnah Abd. (2025). Moderated mediation of technostress, TPACK, and growth mindset on competency in interdisciplinary teaching among STEM lecturers. Scientific Reports 15, 37633. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-21476-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis in the PLS-SEM model (n = 405) with mean-centered interaction terms. The Technostress×GM term predicted CIT at β = 0.152 and TPACK at β = 0.223 (both p < 0.001); simple slopes showed the negative technostress effect weakened as GM increased.

> "the interaction effect of GM significantly predicts CIT (β = 0.152, p < 0.001), demonstrating that GM moderates the relationship between Technostress and CIT"
