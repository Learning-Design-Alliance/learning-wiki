## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Higher trait self-control predicts higher major GPA (β = 0.07, p = 0.008) after controlling for fluid reasoning, conscientiousness, gender, age, HISEI, and migration status. [→ Schmidt FTC 2020](#schmidt-ftc-2020)

## Evidence

### Schmidt FTC 2020

Schmidt FTC, Lindner C, Etzel JM and Retelsdorf J (2020) Self-Control Outdoes Fluid Reasoning in Explaining Vocational and Academic Performance—But Does It? Front. Psychol. 11:757. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00757

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Simultaneous multivariate multiple regression in Mplus with cluster-robust standard errors on N = 3,146 vocational trainees; Table 2 prints the TSC slope for mGPA as 0.07 [0.02, 0.11], p = 0.008, showing "higher trait self-control was associated with higher mGPA".

> "On average, higher trait self-control was associated with higher mGPA but not with higher test scores in mathematics and physics."
