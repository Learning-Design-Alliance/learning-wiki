## Evidence

### Wong Wei Jin 2025

Wong Wei Jin, Lee Shaun Wen Huey, Lee Ronald Fook Seng. (2025). Pharmacy students’ perspective on remote flipped classrooms in Malaysia: a qualitative study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group study of 20 Bachelor of Pharmacy students in 8 groups over 2 rounds; inductive thematic analysis identified flexibility as a theme. Students reported "time flexibility to engage with lectures at their own convenience" and appreciated reviewing material via playback for a thorough grasp of concepts.

> "First, there was a time flexibility to engage with lectures at their own convenience since the lectures were recorded"
