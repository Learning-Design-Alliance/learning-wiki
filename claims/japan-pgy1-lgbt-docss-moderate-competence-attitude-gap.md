### Nagasaki Kazuya 2026

Clinical competence and attitudes towards LGBT patients among medical residents in Japan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08510-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 280 PGY-1 residents in Japan administered immediately after the GM-ITE entrance exam; descriptive means on the LGBT-DOCSS-JP show "highest for Attitudinal Awareness (5.3 [0.9])" and lowest preparedness and training subscale scores.

> "The mean (SD) total LGBT-DOCSS-JP score (1–7 scale) was 4.0 (0.6). Subscale scores were highest for Attitudinal Awareness (5.3 [0.9]), followed by Basic Knowledge (3.9 [1.2]), and lowest for Clinical Preparedness (2.5 [1.0]) and Clinical Training (2.3 [1.4])."
