## Evidence

### Al-Jasmi Fatma 2010

Al-Jasmi Fatma, Moldovan Laura, Clarke Joe TR. (2010). Hunter disease eClinic: interactive, computer-assisted, problem-based approach to independent learning about a rare genetic disease. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-72

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

User/audience feedback questionnaires collected at meetings and conferences where the program was presented, rating the eClinic and eBook chapters. The article reports response rates "between 70 and 75% of the audience" and consistently very high or excellent overall scores; the only requested change was bookmarking and word-search capability.

> "On average, feedback response rates were ranged between 70 and 75% of the audience. The program consistently received very high or excellent overall scores."

### Al-Jasmi Fatma 2010 (2)

Al-Jasmi Fatma, Moldovan Laura, Clarke Joe TR. (2010). Hunter disease eClinic: interactive, computer-assisted, problem-based approach to independent learning about a rare genetic disease. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-72

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online usage statistics documented for the web version, collected since January 2010 after the research group acquired a unique domain. The article reports hits averaging "in the range of 12 000" per month from users worldwide; no learning outcome measures were reported.

> "The number of "hits" per month averages in the range of 12 000 from users from throughout the world; top accessing countries are United States, Canada, Russian Federation, China, Japan, Great Britain, Australia, and the European Union."
