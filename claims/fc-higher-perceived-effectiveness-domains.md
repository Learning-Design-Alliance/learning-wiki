`q3 i?` FC residents rated clinical practice skills development, learning interest, self-learning capacity, problem-solving proficiency, and team collaboration more favorably than TC residents; theoretical knowledge ratings did not differ (p = 0.207). [→ Hu Jing 2026](#hu-jing-2026)

## Evidence

### Hu Jing 2026

Hu Jing, Mei Qiao, Wu Juan, Liu Qiuyuan, Zhang Peipei, Ding Hao, Jie Lei, Fan Renyu, Han Wei. (2026). Evaluation of a flipped classroom approach based on micro-videos in the standardized gastroenterological resident training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09803-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous three-point-scale questionnaires completed by both groups after the objective examinations in the randomized trial. The quote reports the printed means, SDs, and p values for four perceived-effectiveness domains favoring FC.

> "compared with the TC group, the FC group demonstrated statistically advantages in multiple domains: clinical practice skills development (FC: Mean = 2.54, SD = 0.82; TC: Mean = 2.25, SD = 0.42; p = 0.028), interest in learning (FC: Mean = 2.41, SD = 0.76; TC: Mean = 2.04, SD = 0.85; p = 0.015), self-learning capacity (FC: Mean = 2.48, SD = 0.69; TC: Mean = 2.16, SD = 0.78; p = 0.023), and problem-solving proficiency (FC: Mean = 2.61, SD = 0.59; TC: Mean = 2.25, SD = 0.62; p = 0.002)."
