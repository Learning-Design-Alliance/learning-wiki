### Stubbs 2026

Stubbs, C. W., Huang, D., Koh, J., Woods, M., & Plazas Malagón, A. A. (2026). Lessons Learned from Integrating Generative AI into an Introductory Undergraduate Astronomy Course at Harvard. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.04389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.04389)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal focus group facilitated by three researchers during the final 20 minutes of a class session, with instructors out of the room and anonymous audio-recorded responses. Students reported confusion about GAI's purpose that a mid-semester rationale lecture addressed.

> "Overall, the focus group findings indicate that GAI use should be explicitly motivated early on in a course, as students cannot be assumed to fully understand its intended role in their learning."
