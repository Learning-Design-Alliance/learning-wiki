## Evidence

### Kodikara Kaumudee 2023

Kodikara Kaumudee, Seneviratne Thilanka, Premaratna Ranjan. (2023). Pre-clerkship procedural training in venipuncture: a prospective cohort study on skills acquisition and durability. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04722-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion of their prospective cohort study. They self-reported only "2-5 venipunctures per student" during the clerkship month and state the delayed findings "do not say anything in terms of the effect of the intervention".

> "Due to the design of this study, the participants' skill decay may have been masked by superimposed clinical training, albeit one month, and low procedural volumes."
