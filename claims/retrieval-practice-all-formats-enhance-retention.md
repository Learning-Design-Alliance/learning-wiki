## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Experiment 1, subjects in all retrieval practice conditions performed better than the no retrieval practice condition on a final short-answer assessment 1 week later, for both verbatim and inference questions. [→ Smith 2014](#smith-2014)

## Evidence

### Smith 2014

Megan A. Smith and Jeffrey D. Karpicke. (2014). Retrieval practice with short-answer, multiple-choice, and hybrid tests. Memory, 22(7), 784-802. https://doi.org/10.1080/09658211.2013.831454

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 (80 Purdue undergraduates, five between-subjects retrieval-format conditions, final short-answer assessment 1 week later) showed a testing effect: "Subjects in all retrieval practice conditions performed better than those in the no retrieval practice condition" on both question types.

> "Subjects in all retrieval practice conditions performed better than those in the no retrieval practice condition on the final assessment when measured with both verbatim and inference questions; all Fs(1, 30) 8.95, psB.01."
