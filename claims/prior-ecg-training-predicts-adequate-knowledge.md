`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on adequate knowledge (≥ 7/10) among 100 ICU nurses; the article reports the printed effect measure "OR = 2.85, 95% CI: 1.12–7.26, p = 0.028" for prior ECG training.

> "After adjusting for age, gender, education, ICU type, years of experience and prior ECG training, only prior ECG training remained a significant independent predictor (OR = 2.85, 95% CI: 1.12–7.26, p = 0.028)."
