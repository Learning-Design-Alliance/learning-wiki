Survey of 148 edtech coaches (Figure 6): "only 41 percent" maintained or increased deep coaching; coaches reported stopping or reducing deep coaching to direct attention to more teachers and diversified areas of need.

"During school closures, only 41 percent of edtech coaches provided deep coaching to their individual teachers at the same rate as or more than before COVID-19 (Figure 6)."
