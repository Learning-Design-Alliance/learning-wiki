Evidence¶

Gates 1922¶

Gates, Arthur I. (1922). Educational psychology at the Princeton meeting of the American Psychological Association. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Conference report of Florence R. Robinson's (University of Chicago) study in which students went without sleep for thirty hours, some for forty-eight, taking mental and motor tests at intervals; no statistics are printed.

"The loss of efficiency was no greater than the amount of gain due to practice although "feelings of fatigue" were reported."
