Evidence¶

Zhanyong Qi 2026¶

Zhanyong Qi, Jiayi Wang, Yue Du, Xinyue Zhang. (2026). The effect of blended learning approaches on vocational school students' practical skills performance and learning behaviors: virtual simulations or hands-on activities while accessing instructional videos. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09793-4

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

ANCOVA of the 2x2 experiment (n = 130) on the 4-item interest scale (Cronbach's α = 0.847): video reviewing main effect ηp2 = 0.043 was significant; operation type and interaction effects were not.

"Regarding interest, ANCOV A revealed a significant main effect of video reviewing (F [1, 125] = 5.58, p = 0.020, ηp2 = 0.043). Nevertheless, there was no main effect on type of operation (F [1, 125] = 0.269, p = 0.605, ηp2 = 0.002), and the interaction effect was also insignificant (F [1, 125] = 1.46, p = 0.230, ηp2 = 0.012)."

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pairwise contrast from the same ANCOVA showing the video-review interest benefit only "under virtual simulation conditions"; the discussion states there was no significant interest difference between video and non-video students in hands-on activities.

"Under virtual simulation conditions, participants with video exposure demonstrated higher interest perception than those without (MD = 1.12, p = 0.008)."
