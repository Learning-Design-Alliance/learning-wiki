# When compositional effects are constrained equal across countries, the overall peer spillover effect is negative and non-significant while the BFLPE remains negative and significant

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Under a restricted multi-group doubly latent model with equal compositional effects, the overall effect of class-average achievement on subsequent achievement was βcomp = -.041 (ES = -.152), non-significant, while the BFLPE was βcomp = -.336 (ES = -.693), significant. ## Evidence

### Televantou 2023

Televantou, I., Marsh, H. W., Xu, K. M., Guo, J., & Dicke, T. (2023). Peer Spillover and Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effects with SIMS80: Revisiting a Historical Database Through the Lens of a Modern Methodological Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09816-3

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Restricted multi-group doubly latent model imposing equality of compositional effects across the three countries. The pooled peer spillover effect was non-significant with ES = -.152, whereas the pooled BFLPE was βcomp = -.336 (ES = -.693) and significant; model fit was not substantially affected by the restriction.

> "we found an overall negative and non-significant compositional effect of class-average achievement on subsequent mathematics achievement (βcomp = − .041, SD = .023, ES = − .152)"
