Nagpal 2025¶

Nagpal, M., Lin, T.-J., Kraatz, E., Kim, S., Ha, S. Y., & Glassman, M. (2025). Reciprocal interactions between teachers' instructional moves and students' social reasoning during collaborative small group discussions. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09718-7

This reciprocal direction (Research Question 2) is stated in the article's abstract summary of the SDA findings; the corresponding results-table text was not available in the supplied excerpt, so no effect sizes are reported. The contrast names teachers' high-level prompting versus specific praise as the two outcomes.

"Conversely, students' social reasoning was less likely to be followed by teachers' high-level prompting but was more likely to be followed by teachers' use of specific praise."
