## Evidence

### Navarro-Ibáñez 2026

Navarro-Ibáñez, M., Balladares, J., Sprovera, I., Manaut, C., & Infante, M. (2026). Help! I Need Somebody: A Systematic Review of Academic Help-Seeking Among Pre-Service Teachers. Educational Psychology Review, 38, 115. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10208-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Descriptive synthesis of instruments across the 32 included studies. The review reports that the MSLQ and OSLQ measure AHS via a four-item subscale and that "the AHS items comprise 9% of the MSLQ and 17% of the OSLQ", which the authors argue may obscure the construct's multidimensional nature.

> "In terms of percentage, the AHS items comprise 9% of the MSLQ and 17% of the OSLQ."
