## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In consultations where the GP knew the patient and how the patient communicates, communication continued freely without much exploration of the patient's background. [→ Essers G 2011](#essers-g-2011)
`q2 i?` GPs with prior knowledge of the patient's medical history referred to prior episodes or connected current problems to that history, contrasting with consultations where the GP had no prior knowledge of the problem. [→ Essers G 2011](#essers-g-2011)

## Evidence

### Essers G 2011

Essers G, van Dulmen S, van Weel C, van der Vleuten C, Kramer A. (2011). Identifying context factors explaining physician's low performance in communication assessment: an explorative study in general practice. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-138

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Observational results across the 17-consultation sample: the authors state "In 14 of the 17 consultations we observed the patient and GP discussing the patient's social and/or family circumstances", with communication continuing "without much exploration of the patient's background". Knowledge of the patient was judged an influential context factor.
