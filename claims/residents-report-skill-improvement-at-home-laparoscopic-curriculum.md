### Brian Riley MD 2024

Brian Riley MD, MAEd, Bayne David MD, MPH, Ito Traci MD, Lager Jeannette MD, MPH, Edwards Anya MD, MBA, Kumar Sandhya MD, Soriano Ian MD, O'Sullivan Patricia EdD, Varas Julian MD, MS, Chern Hueylan MD. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postcurriculum survey of 22 residents (47% of invited) after the four-month at-home curriculum. Self-reported improvement and continuation endorsement were each 86%. Of the three reporting no improvement, two preferred in-person skills sessions.

> "Among survey respondents, 19 (86%) reported that their laparoscopic skills improved through completion of the curriculum, and the same 19 (86%) felt that the curriculum should be continued for future residents."
