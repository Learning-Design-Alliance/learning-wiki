Student's t-tests on mean knowledge test scores with 95% CIs for the two groups at T0, T1 and T2, reported in the Results section of the RCT; the article prints the between-group differences and P values but no standardized effect size for this contrast.

> "The between-group difference was indifferent or in favor of the intervention group, starting from 0.034 (Student's t-test, non-significant, P=0.34) at T0, and increasing to 0.072 (P<0.05) and 0.084 (P<0.05) at T1 and T2, respectively."
