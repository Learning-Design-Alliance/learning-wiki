## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In blind peer evaluation, none of the differences between student-generated and AI-generated resources in code correctness, explanation correctness or helpfulness ratings were statistically significant. [→ Paul Denny 2023](#paul-denny-2023)
`q3 i?` Overall quality decisions and rater confidence also showed no statistically significant difference between student-generated and AI-generated resources. [→ Paul Denny 2023 (2)](#paul-denny-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Paul Denny 2023

Paul Denny, Hassan Khosravi, Arto Hellas, Juho Leinonen, Sami Sarsa. (2023). Can We Trust AI-Generated Educational Content? Comparative Analysis of Human and AI-Generated Learning Resources. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.10509](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.10509)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blind peer evaluation in the learnersourcing platform compared student and AI resources on three rated criteria. The article reports "the medians are the same (4 for all evaluated aspects)" with no significant differences on any criterion; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "while the medians are the same (4 for all evaluated aspects), there are slight differences in the means, although none of the differences are statistically significant (U = 885785.0, p = 0.46 for code correctness; U = 940809.5, p = 0.13 for explanation correctness; U = 894970.0, p = 0.72 for helpfulness)."

### Paul Denny 2023 (2)

Paul Denny, Hassan Khosravi, Arto Hellas, Juho Leinonen, Sami Sarsa. (2023). Can We Trust AI-Generated Educational Content? Comparative Analysis of Human and AI-Generated Learning Resources. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.10509](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.10509)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the overall 'decision' field and rater confidence found medians of 4 across the board with slight mean differences that were not statistically significant; the decision contrast (p = 0.07) is near the threshold and the article flags it as marginal territory for larger samples.

> "the differences are not statistically significant (U = 860373.5, p = 0.07 for decision; U = 922883.5, p = 0.43 for confidence)."
