Evidence¶

Carrie Gillispie 2021¶

Carrie Gillispie, Caitlin Codella, Aaron Merchen, Joseph Davis, Ashley Cappo. (2021). Equity in Child Care Is Everyone's Business. The Education Trust & U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617545

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The report cites survey research on PPP relief reaching child care providers, reporting "less than 1% of the $521.4 billion in PPP loans" went to the sector. Denial reasons included lack of program funding, credit scores, and lacking a business checking account. No effect size is printed.

"less than 1% of the $521.4 billion in PPP loans provided to businesses nationwide actually went to child care providers, and only a fraction of child care providers — less than 6% — benefited."
