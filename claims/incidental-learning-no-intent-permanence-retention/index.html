Whipple 1917¶

Whipple, G. M. (1917). Notes on the recall of nonsense syllables. Cornell University. https://psycnet.apa.org

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument by the author about why the case is unusual: the material was nonsense syllables unrelated to the observer's stock of ideas, learning was incidental, and recall was spontaneous. No empirical test or effect size is offered.

"it seems to me to be at least unusual, because (1) the material was nonsense syllables and hence unrelated to the observer's general stock of ideas"
