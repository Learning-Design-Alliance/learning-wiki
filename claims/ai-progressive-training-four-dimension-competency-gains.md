## Evidence

### He Lidan 2026

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples pre-post analysis of the four-dimensional competency assessment in 28 residents after 12 months of platform-based training. Clinical reasoning rose 16.5 points with "Cohen's d = 2.36"; clinical skills rose 15.9 points (t = 12.83, d = 2.56). Single-site pre-post design without control group.

> "Clinical reasoning competency showed the most pronounced improvement, increasing from 68.4 ± 8.1 points pre-training to 84.9 ± 5.7 points post-training, representing a mean difference of 16.5 points (t = 13.57, p < 0.001, 95% CI [14.0–19.0], Cohen's d = 2.36)."
