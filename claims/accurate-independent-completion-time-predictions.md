In the same mixed-effects calibration analysis, the independent condition showed "no significant difference between the actual and predicted completion times" (β=−2.52, p=0.775). Two category-level exceptions were reported: underestimation for C2 and overestimation for C4, with no significant effect across difficulty levels.

> "we found no significant difference between the actual and predicted completion times in the independent completion condition (β=−2.52,SE=8.83,z=−0.29 ,p=0.775), which suggests that people's predictions are generally accurate."
