## Evidence

### Kelchner 2017

Kelchner, V. P., Evans, K., Brendell, K., Allen, D., Miller, C., & Cooper-Haber, K. (2017). The Effect of a School-Based Transitional Support Intervention Program on Alternative School Youth's Attitudes and Behaviors. The Professional Counselor, 7(2), 169-184. https://doi.org/10.15241/vpk.7.2.169

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-group pretest-posttest study of 52 at-risk youth (24 treatment, 28 control) returning from a 45-day alternative school placement; repeated measures MANOVA with SAAS-R as a dependent variable. The article reports "a significant effect of treatment on SAAS-R: F(1,50) = 5.159, p < .027"; no effect size is printed.

> "The test of between-subjects effects results show that there was a significant effect of treatment on SAAS-R: F(1,50) = 5.159, p < .027."
