### Wang Leshi 2025

Wang Leshi, Chen Peiwen, Wang Xiao, Wei Sijia, Lin Jiayi, Jing Xi. (2025). Integrating team-based and peer-teaching strategies for standardized dental residency: a path to active learning and professional growth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07023-y

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis in the Results section using Pearson correlation after Shapiro-Wilk normality testing; engagement was associated with academic performance in both cohorts (r = 0.7235 and r = 0.6727, "significant positive correlation in both the 2021–22 cohort"). Correlation does not establish causation.

> "The results showed a significant positive correlation in both the 2021–22 cohort (r = 0.7235, p < 0.0001) and the 2022–23 cohort (r = 0.6727, p < 0.0001), indicating that higher levels of student engagement were associated with better academic performance"
