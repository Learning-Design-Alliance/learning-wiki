### Farrés-Tarafa M 2021

Farrés-Tarafa M, Bande D, Roldán-Merino J, Hurtado-Pardos B, Biurrun-Garrido A, Molina-Raya L, Raurell-Torredà M, Casas I, Lorenzo-Seva U. (2021). PLoS ONE 16(7): e0255188. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255188

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis using generalised least squares in EQS 6.2 on the 489-student sample, testing the two-dimensional structure of the original version. Table 4 prints CFI 0.980, GFI 0.988, AGFI 0.982, RMSEA 0.039 and χ2 = 274,234; gl = 64; p < 0.0001. No effect size is printed.

> "The Chi square test was statistically significant but the fit ratio was 4.28, so if it is between 2–6 the fit is reasonably good [51]."
