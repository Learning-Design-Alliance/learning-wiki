Mehmet Erkol & Mehmet Tamer Kaya; Tuğba Yurdakul (Kül). (2023). The Effect of Argumentation-Based Teaching on Scientific Process Skills of 7th Grade Students. Open Journal for Educational Research, 7(2), 79-92. https://doi.org/10.32591/coas.ojer.0702.02079e

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Content analysis of semi-structured interviews with the 18 experimental-group students. Themes with frequencies: fun/beautiful 16 (47.1%), instructive/formative 8 (23.5%), hard 5 (14.7%). Half of liked opinions concerned experimenting (50.0%); about 30% disliked filling out worksheets.
