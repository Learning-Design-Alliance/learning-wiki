`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey and interview documentation of youth participants in the Fall 2016 program found perspective-taking gains; 84% of students reported they "became more open to and curious about other people's perspectives" and 76% of educators reported increased student facility in taking others' perspectives. One student cited the wrestling-team film as building understanding of the team's commitment.
