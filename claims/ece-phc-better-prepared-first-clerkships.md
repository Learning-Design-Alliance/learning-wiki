`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group study of 60 Indonesian preclinical students: 30 partly trained in PHC centers (PT), 30 exclusively in a skills laboratory (NPT), interviewed within half a year of starting clerkships. The abstract reports "Clerkship students who had participated in ECE in PHC centers felt better prepared to perform their clinical skills during the first clerkships than peers who had only practiced in a skills laboratory."
