Fu Jing, Li Yi. (2025). The impact of peer-assisted learning combined with scenario simulation on the trauma first aid skills and comprehensive abilities of emergency department interns. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1666950

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment comparing 90 interns per group (historical control from the prior year). Post-training t-tests in Table 3 show hemostatic bandaging t = 11.037 and team cooperation t = -15.189, both p < 0.001, with CPR (t = -0.571, p = 0.569) and defibrillation non-significant. No effect sizes printed.
