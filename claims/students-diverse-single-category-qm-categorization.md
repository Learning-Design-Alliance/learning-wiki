## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Categorization of Quantum Mechanics Problems by Professors and Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational finding from the categorization task given as a quiz to 22 juniors and seniors in two quantum mechanics courses, who were told a problem could be placed in more than one category. The article reports students' categorizations "were diverse" and multi-category placement was rare.

> "We ﬁnd that the categorizations of a problem performed by the stu dents were diverse and they seldom placed a problem in more than one category although they were explicitly told they could do so if they wish."
