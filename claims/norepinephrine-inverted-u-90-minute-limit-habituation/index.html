q2 · i? · review · r2

Review narrative citing Ursin's (2012) paratrooper study as an extreme example of habituation through repeatedly experiencing safe stressful situations. The review links this to Kapur's finding that productive failure improves students' tolerance for uncertainty in later problem-solving.

"in a study of paratroopers, Ursin (2012) found that on the first day of parachute training, (nor)epinephrine spiked to nearly double its baseline. However, it returned to normal within a week of daily jumps as the brain rapidly habituated to the stimulus."
