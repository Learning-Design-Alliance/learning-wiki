## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students taught with FCCL achieve significantly higher theoretical scores than students taught with LBL (Cohen's d = 0.60, 95% CI: 0.17 to 1.04, P = 0.01). [→ Shi Xu-Ying 2025](#shi-xu-ying-2025)

## Evidence

### Shi Xu-Ying 2025

Shi Xu-Ying, Lu Bing-Rui, Yin Qing, Wang Qiu-Wen, Fang Yong-Qi, Sun Zhi-Gang. (2025). Whether case-based teaching combined with the flipped classroom is more valuable than traditional lecture-based teaching methods in clinical medical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07465-4

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of eight studies (1,473 students) using a random-effects model after observing significant heterogeneity (I2 = 93.29%). The pooled estimate showed "a statistically significant difference in theoretical scores between the two groups" favoring FCCL, with a moderate effect size.

> "The pooled effect size revealed a statistically significant difference in theoretical scores between the two groups (Cohen’s d = 0.60, 95% CI: 0.17 to 1.04, P = 0.01) (Fig. 3a)."
