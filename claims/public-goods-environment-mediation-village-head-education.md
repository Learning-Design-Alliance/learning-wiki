## Evidence

### Li Jing 2025

Li Jing, Deng Huan, Li Jun. (2025). The impact of village heads' educational levels on adolescent academic performance: Evidence from rural China. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0315822

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baron-Kenny three-step mechanism analysis on the CFPS sample (1,291 observations in mechanism models), using logged public-service expenditure and road cleanliness as mediators. Table 5 prints coefficients 0.223, 0.146, 0.029, and 0.030; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "Models 17 and 18 examine the effect of the village head's educational level on the supply of public goods and environmental pollution management, with both showing p-values less than 0.01. The results in Models 19 and 20 indicate that the supply of public goods and environmental pollution management in the village significantly influence adolescent academic performance"
