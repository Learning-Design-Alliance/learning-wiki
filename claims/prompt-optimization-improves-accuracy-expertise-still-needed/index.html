Evidence¶

Huber 2024¶

Huber, S. E., Kiili, K., Nebel, S., Ryan, R. M., Sailer, M., & Ninaus, M. (2024). Leveraging the Potential of Large Language Models in Education Through Playful and Game-Based Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09868-z

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

This perspective article (not an empirical study or systematic review) states, citing Li et al. (2023) and Yang et al. (2023), that prompt optimization can vastly improve output accuracy; it attributes this to those works and adds that critical evaluation still requires domain expertise. No independent data are presented.

"Although optimizing prompts has been shown to be capable of vastly improving the accuracy of outputs generated by LLMs (Li et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2023), the fact remains that the critical evaluation of resulting outputs still requires domain expertise."
