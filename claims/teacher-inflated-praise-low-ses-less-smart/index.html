Evidence¶

Brummelman 2024¶

Brummelman, E., van Atteveldt, N., Wolf, S. & Sierksma, J. (2024). Using social and behavioral science to address achievement inequality. npj Science of Learning, 9, 44. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00259-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial reports, citing Schoneveld and Brummelman, two pre-registered experiments: teachers "delivered more inﬂated praise" to low-SES successes, and children seeing inflated praise judged the recipient more hardworking but less smart. No effect sizes are printed.

"A pre-registered experiment showed that when a child from a low-SES (vs. high-SES) background succeeded in school, teachers delivered more inﬂated praise"
