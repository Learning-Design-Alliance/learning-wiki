Bayesian hierarchical sensitivity analysis (variational Bayes, section random intercepts) fitted to the same aggregated section counts. The article reports that "Posterior summaries under the hierarchical model are highly consistent with the pooled frequentist results" and that clustering did not materially change the pooled estimates, mainly widening posterior intervals.
