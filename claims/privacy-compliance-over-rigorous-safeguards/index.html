Evidence¶

anchor-compliance¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Developer interview finding in the trust proxies section. Several edtech founders described spending tens of thousands of dollars per year on certifications from multiple bodies with inconsistent rigor, some requiring only a policy rather than actual testing.

"Consequently, the incentive for edtech providers is to seek out cheap certifications they can put on their website in lieu of taking substantive measures to ensure safety."

anchor-transparency¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Statement from the head of a state CTO council reported in the trust section, noting this remains a major point of worry. Another educator noted vendors hide system prompts as industry secrets, making reliability hard to gauge.

"Currently, large language model providers state that student data is not being used to train their models, but there is no independent way for a district to prove it."
