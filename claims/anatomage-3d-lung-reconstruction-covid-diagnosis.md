### Bartoletti-Stella Anna 2021

Bartoletti-Stella Anna, Gatta Valentina, Mariani Giulia Adalgisa, Gobbi Pietro, Falconi Mirella, Manzoli Lucia, Faenza Irene, Salucci Sara. (2021). Three-Dimensional Virtual Anatomy as a New Approach for Medical Student's Learning. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413247

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hasni et al., a study reconstructing 2D CT scans in 3D through the Anatomage Table, comparing RT-PCR-negative patients with normal CT to RT-PCR-positive patients with abnormal scans; volumes "appeared reliable and accurate for the diagnosis". No effect size is printed.

> "Anatomage allowed to obtain 3D unventilated lung volumes that appeared reliable and accurate for the diagnosis."
