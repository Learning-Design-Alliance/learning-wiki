## Evidence

### Knox S 2013

Knox S, Cullen W, Dunne C. (2013). Continuous professional competence (CPC) for emergency medical technicians in Ireland: educational needs assessment. BMC Emergency Medicine, 13, 25. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-227X-13-25

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Relevance ratings of 22 listed CPC activities in the survey results: "practical, hands-on activities were preferred over theoretical/non-practice type activities", with fewer negative responses for practical skills. Top-rated items included practical training scenarios (97%, 313/321) and CFR/CPR revalidation (97%, 311/322). No effect size printed.

> "The results showed that practical, hands-on activities were preferred over theoretical/non-practice type activities. Also, there were less negative responses regarding activities related to practical skills than to theoretical skills."
