## Evidence

### Michelle S. Peter 2019

Michelle S. Peter, Samantha Durrant, Andrew Jessop, Amy Bidgood, Julian M. Pine, Caroline F. Rowland. (2019). Does speed of processing or vocabulary size predict later language growth in toddlers? Cognitive Psychology 115 (2019) 101238. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2019.101238

`q3 · i?`

Growth curve model of change in expressive vocabulary between 19 and 25 months, with children divided by median split into high and low vocabulary groups at 19 months. Visual inspection of Fig. 4 suggested "processing speed has a positive effect on vocabulary growth for children with lower vocabularies".

> "An interaction between Mean RT and Vocabulary Level, however, did significantly improve the model fit for the linear term (β=0.30 [0.03, 0.57], SE=0.14, χ2(1)=1.53, p=0.038), indicating that the effect of processing speed on linear vocabulary growth was different for children with high versus low vocabularies."
