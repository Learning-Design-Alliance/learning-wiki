## Subclaims
`q2 i?` During the first 2 months of the major initial COVID-19 surge in the United States, monthly new learner registrations on the NSTDC rose from a prior 12-month average of 992 to 1330 in March 2020 and 1850 in April 2020, a 34% and 86% increase respectively. [→ Bauer KM 2021](#bauer-km-2021)

## Evidence

### Bauer KM 2021

Bauer KM, Corcorran MA, Budak JZ, Johnston C, Spach DH. (2021). Leveraging E-Learning Infrastructure in Times of Rapid Change: Use of the National Sexually Transmitted Diseases Curriculum in the Era of COVID-19. Sex Transm Dis. https://doi.org/10.1097/OLQ.0000000000001462

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational program evaluation of NSTDC registration data comparing March–April 2020 with the previous 12-month average of 992 new learners per month. The study found registrations "increased to 1330 in March 2020 and 1850 in April 2020," a 34% and 86% rise; no effect size was reported.
