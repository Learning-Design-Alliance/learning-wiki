## Evidence

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2019

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Wells Cameron I., Wilkinson Tim J. (2019). Publication in a medical student journal predicts short- and long-term academic success: a matched-cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1704-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable conditional logistic regression model built to adjust for confounding between publication, higher degrees and faculty positions (n = 147). Printed effect size for NZMSJ publication: OR = 2.64; the model also found faculty appointment highly predictive (OR 22.22) with overall fit R2 = 0.18.

> "This identified that post-graduate publication was independently predicted by student publication in the NZMSJ (OR 2.64, 95% CI 1.23–5.68, p = 0.01), when adjusted for these factors."
