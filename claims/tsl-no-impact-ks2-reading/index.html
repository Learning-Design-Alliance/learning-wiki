Torgerson et al. 2016¶

Carole Torgerson, Hannah Ainsworth, Hannah Buckley, Gillen Hampden-Thompson, Catherine Hewitt, Deborah Humphry, Laura Jefferson, Natasha Mitchell, David Torgerson. (2016). Affordable Online Maths Tuition: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581116

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Secondary analysis of KS2 English reading SAT fine marked scores using an analogous linear mixed model with KS1 reading scores as pre-test, in the same 64-school cluster randomised trial. The article reports no statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"There was also no evidence that the intervention had an impact on the Key Stage 2 reading test, or a differential impact on pupils who were eligible for free school meals or pupils who took part in more tutoring sessions."
