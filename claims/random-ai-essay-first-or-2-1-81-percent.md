Will Yeadon, Elise Agra, Oto-obong Inyang, Paul Mackay, Arin Mizouri. (2024). Evaluating AI and Human Authorship Quality in Academic Writing through Physics Essays. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad669d](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad669d)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A z-score probability calculation on the AI-authored essay score distribution, not an experiment. The article links this to Dell'Acqua et al.'s finding that below-average performers gained more from AI, suggesting AI is "leveling the playing field in physics essay writing".

> "choosing a random essay from those created by the prompts detailed in Section 2.2 could result in a first or upper second-class grade 81.43% of the time, according to the cumulative probability from a simple z-score ( µ = 65.73, σ = 6.41) calculation with a value of 60 - the boundary for a 2:1."
