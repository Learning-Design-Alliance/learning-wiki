## Evidence

### Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini 2011

Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini. (2011). Comparative Effectiveness of Contextual and Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 4(1). http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest comparison after matching on pretest scores; whole-group means were 47.1 and 47.92. The article reports "p values were found .801, .946, .335 and .745 on 0.05 level which was insignificant", establishing baseline equivalence. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond these non-significant t tests.
