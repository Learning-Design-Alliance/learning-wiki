## Evidence

### Falco Gabrielle 2025

Falco Gabrielle, Dunaway Alex, Ranney Stephen, Sweeney William, Taylor Zachary, Dyke Christopher. (2025). We're talking about practice: examining the shifting role of FLS in surgical education. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12414-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 31 general surgery residents across three programs (~35% response rate); descriptive statistics only, no inferential testing. Among FLS completers, "28.6% (n = 4) agreed and 7.1% (n = 1) strongly agreed that they regularly practiced independently prior to the exam"; after completion only 7.2% (n = 1) reported continued regular practice, with 71.4% disagreeing.
