## Evidence

### Martín Monteiro 2022

Martín Monteiro, Cecilia Stari, Arturo C. Marti. (2022). Mobile-device sensors: an innovative tool in the teaching of physical sciences. Modelling in Science Education and Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.12064](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.12064)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

In the Results and Discussion section the authors describe, citing their 2021 American Journal of Physics paper, how sensors replace the traditional practice of repeatedly dropping a ball and measuring distance. They state sensors enable "cientos o miles de medidas en pocos segundos", supporting study of statistical error concepts; no effect sizes are reported.

> "Los sensores permiten transformar este tipo de experimentos tomando cientos o miles de medidas en pocos segundos (Monteiro, Stari, Cabeza, & Martí, 2021) y luego estudiar conceptos tales como la desviación estándar, el error estándar, distribuciones normales y criterios estadísticos."
