Multiple linear regression from the cross-sectional survey; the abstract reports higher skills/attitudes (β = 2.17) and fewer barriers (β = −0.88) among public-university graduates.

> "Graduates from public universities presented higher scores for skills and attitudes (β = 2.17; 95%CI: 1.15 to 3.18; p < 0.001) and reported fewer barriers (β = −0.88; 95%CI: −1.61 to −0.15; p < 0.01)"
