# Project performance improved significantly from pre- to post-feedback across all three feedback conditions with no significant differences between feedback sources

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims

`q3 i?` Students' project performance improved significantly from pre- to post-feedback in all conditions (rrb = 0.77), with no statistically significant differences between AI and human feedback sources; equivalence was tested between GPT-o4-mini and teacher feedback, and non-inferiority for DeepSeek R1. [→ Grion 2026](#grion-2026)

`q3 i?` GPT-o4-mini feedback was practically equivalent to teacher feedback, and DeepSeek R1 feedback was non-inferior to teacher feedback in effects on project performance. [→ Grion 2026](#grion-2026)

### Grion 2026

Grion, Valentina; Doria, Beatrice; Agostini, Daniele; Slaviero, Giorgia. (2026). Artificial intelligence and feedback in university education: effectiveness and student perceptions. Assessment and Evaluation in Higher Education. https://dx.doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2026.2697962

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study: 47 student groups (N = 238) randomly assigned to GPT-o4-mini, DeepSeek R1, or expert teacher feedback conditions; changes analysed with non-parametric tests, robust models, and non-inferiority and equivalence analyses. The article reports "significant improvement in project performance" with rrb = 0.77.

> "Results showed significant improvement in project performance from pre- to post-feedback across all conditions (rrb = 0.77), with no significant differences between feedback sources."
