Evidence¶

Li 2019¶

Li, L., Maher, B., Weiser, G., Orenstein, A., Chow, E., & Atienza, S. (2019). Hero Elementary playlist pilot study in after-school programs. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608115

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pilot measuring attitudes with the 14-item Learning Activation Lab survey (pre-survey reliability 0.78). The adjusted difference was 0.39 with p ~ 0.55, described as no significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

"The results did not show a significant difference in post-implementation science attitudes (as measured by Emerging STEM Learning Activation Survey), with an a of 0.05 (point estimate of 0.39; p ~ 0.55; see Table 9)."
