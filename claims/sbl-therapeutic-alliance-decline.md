## Evidence

### Cuyul-Vásquez I 2026

Cuyul-Vásquez I, Aravena-Páez H, Araya-Quintanilla F, Guinez-Molinos S, Núñez-Cortés R, Gutiérrez Espinoza H. (2026). Collaborative Simulation-Based Learning to Develop Competencies in Physical Therapy Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85442

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on Working Alliance Inventory–Observer Version: ηp²=0.294, P=.008. Descriptive mean changes were −3.58 (collaborative) and −5.94 (individual). Group effect and interaction were nonsignificant.

> "The mixed ANOVA indicated a moderate-to-large main effect of time for WAI-O scores (F1,21=8.744, ηp²=0.294; P=.008), reflecting a decrease in observer-rated working alliance from pre- to post-intervention across both conditions."

### Cuyul-Vásquez I 2026 (2)

Cuyul-Vásquez I, Aravena-Páez H, Araya-Quintanilla F, Guinez-Molinos S, Núñez-Cortés R, Gutiérrez Espinoza H. (2026). Collaborative Simulation-Based Learning to Develop Competencies in Physical Therapy Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85442

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-corrected paired post hoc tests: neither group's decline reached corrected significance (PBonf=.52 and .11), though the authors note the moderate and large effect sizes suggest "a descriptive reduction in alliance scores across both conditions."

> "Post hoc analyses revealed that the pre-to-post decline did not survive Bonferroni correction in either the collaborative group (t11=1.637, d=−0.472; PBonf=.52) or the individual group (t10=2.558, d=−0.771; PBonf=.11)."
