A randomized trial conducted in Missouri during 2021-2022 compared over 3,000 K-2 students in PCP schools with peers in control schools on the Acadience Reading assessment. The study found "significantly greater gains on the Acadience Reading assessment than peers in control schools (effect size = +0.09)", with gains across grades in phonemic awareness, decoding, and oral reading fluency.
