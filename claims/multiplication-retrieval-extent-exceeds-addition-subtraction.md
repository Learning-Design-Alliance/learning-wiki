Authors' interpretation in the discussion of the four SEM models (n = 207), supporting both hypotheses that multiplication relies more on fact retrieval than subtraction and that partially distinct retrieval processes exist. The article states "the difference is slight in the regressive power" and that addition and subtraction had notably similar retrieval associations.
