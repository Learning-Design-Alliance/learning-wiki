## Evidence

### Peikert S. A. 2026

Peikert S. A., Wiegel S. V., Blank J., Woelber J. P., Ratka‐Krüger P. (2026). Assessment of Knowledge Levels in a Blended‐Learning CPD Programme in Periodontology: A Cross‐Sectional Comparative Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70099

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive self-report data collected alongside the patient case from 32 Master and 26 Curriculum graduates. Participants rated the case difficulty and their expected own performance; means are reported without inferential tests.

> "The self‐assessment in group 2 was worse, with a mean of 3.25 (SD 1.08), compared to group 1 with 2.46 (SD 0.65). The degree of difficulty of the case was assessed similarly by both groups, with group 1 rating it 4.14 (SD 1.04) and group 2 rating it 4.07 (SD 1.07)."
