## Evidence

### Kainulainen et al. 2017

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' interpretation of the latent transition results: during the rational number teaching period between Times 2 and 3, none of the main pathways showed development toward a more correct understanding not already achieved, but movement toward intermediate profiles did occur.

> "These results suggest that instructional periods played a significant role not in the construction of a final mathematically correct rational number concept but in the deconstruction of natural number–biased concepts."
