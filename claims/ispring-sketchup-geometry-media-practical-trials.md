## Evidence

### Nurwijayanti 2019

Nurwijayanti, A., Budiyono, & Fitriana, L. (2019). Combining Google SketchUp and Ispring Suite 8: A breakthrough to develop geometry learning media. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 103-116. https://jme.ejournal.uns.ac.id/index.php/jme

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Individual trial questionnaire with seven randomly chosen students. The average of 3.55 was "in the strongly agree category if the media used further"; students asked for deeper material, and some computers encountered errors running the media.

> "This test was performed to seven students who randomly chosen from one of the schools. It used a questionnaire as the instrument of the test. The total scoring score for this test was 894 with its average 3.55."

### Nurwijayanti 2019 (2)

Nurwijayanti, A., Budiyono, & Fitriana, L. (2019). Combining Google SketchUp and Ispring Suite 8: A breakthrough to develop geometry learning media. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 103-116. https://jme.ejournal.uns.ac.id/index.php/jme

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Limited-scale trial in one class of 35 students plus teachers. Students averaged 3.27 and teachers 3.52 in the good category; observation found the media ran well, though a video could not be displayed and had to be shown separately.

> "It was conducted to one class consisted of 35 students and teachers by using questionnaire. The result from the students indicated a total score of 4122 with an average of 3.27."
