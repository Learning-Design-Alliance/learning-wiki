## Evidence

### Dekker 2022

Dekker, I., Luberti, M., & Stam, J. (2022). Effects of supplemental instruction on grades, mental well-being, and belonging: A field experiment. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6858-4001

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Post-test ANOVA in the randomized field experiment (S-WEMWBS scale, T1 n = 320) found no significant well-being difference between the offered-SI and control groups; equivalence was not tested. Covariate adjustment did not change the result.

> "Students in the treatment and control group did not differ significantly in terms of mental well-being (F = 0.62, df = 1, 319, p = 0.43), or sense of belonging (F = 0.54, df = 1, 319, p = 0.59) on the post-test."
