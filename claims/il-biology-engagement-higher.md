Behavioral comparison in the 2002 and 2003 correlational biology study: Table 2 reports 80% versus 73% class attendance, 47% versus 28% extra-credit submission, and 74% versus 28% exam-prep attendance for IL versus biology-only students. The article presents these descriptively.

> "Students from the IL course were more likely to attend the biology class at a higher rate, submit more extra-credit projects, and attend exam preparation sessions in comparison to the biology-only students."
