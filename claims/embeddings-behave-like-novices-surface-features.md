`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Synthetic-data experiments are offered as a possible explanation for the modest score-similarity relationship; the article reports embeddings "behaving like novices when categorizing physics-problem solutions." The article also cites prior work reporting embeddings of arithmetic expressions dominated by surface properties.

> "Experiments with a synthetic data set suggest that this may be due to embeddings behaving like novices when categorizing physics-problem solutions, that is, their similarity geometry is strongly influenced by surface features rather than conceptual, semantic structure."
