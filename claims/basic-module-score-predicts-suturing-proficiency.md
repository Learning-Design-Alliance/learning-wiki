## Evidence

### Iaroseski Júlia 2026

Iaroseski Júlia, Bigolin André Vicente, Galvão Lima Sofia, Cazella Silvio César. (2026). Quality over quantity: early performance predicts suturing proficiency in robotic surgery simulation. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03702-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of 57 trainees' MentorLearn records at a single Brazilian simulation center. The article reports that better early performance was associated with "faster and higher-quality suturing"; correlations were small-to-moderate (r = -0.269; r = 0.277).

> "Mean score in the Basic module was negatively correlated with total suturing time (r = -0.269, p = 0.043) and positively correlated with the final Suture Score (r = 0.277, p = 0.037), indicating that better early performance was associated with faster and higher-quality suturing."

### Iaroseski Júlia 2026 (2)

Iaroseski Júlia, Bigolin André Vicente, Galvão Lima Sofia, Cazella Silvio César. (2026). Quality over quantity: early performance predicts suturing proficiency in robotic surgery simulation. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03702-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Same retrospective cohort correlation analysis (Table 2). The Fundamentals-module mean score correlated negatively with total suturing time (r = -0.325, p = 0.014); its correlation with Suture Score was not significant (r = 0.159, p = 0.238).

> "Mean score in the Fundamentals module was also negatively correlated with total suturing time (r = -0.325, p = 0.014)."
