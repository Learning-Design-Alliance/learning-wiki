### Karacop 2017

Ataman Karacop. (2017). The Effects of Using Jigsaw Method Based on Cooperative Learning Model in the Undergraduate Science Laboratory Practices. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(3): 420-434. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050314

Correlational analysis of the same quasi-experimental sample: Pearson correlation between GPA (used as an indicator of prior knowledge) and SLPAT scores was r=0.774, p<0.01, N=48 — a "high level significant positive correlation".

> "The results of the Pearson correlation analysis showed a high level significant positive correlation between the SLPAT scores and the GPA of the students (r=0.774; p<0.01; N=48)."
