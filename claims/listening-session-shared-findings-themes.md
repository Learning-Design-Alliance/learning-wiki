Qualitative findings from community listening sessions held December 2024 to March 2025, facilitated by WestEd to inform implementation of the two initiatives. Table 1 organizes findings into eight themes; the quote reports the access and affordability theme: "Providers shared that they commonly struggle with getting children in the subsidy program placed in their care." Themes are descriptive stakeholder input, not tested outcomes.

> "Providers shared that they commonly struggle with getting children in the subsidy program placed in their care, and families shared that they commonly struggle with long waiting lists for placement"
