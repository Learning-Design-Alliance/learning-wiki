### İlhan 2018

İlhan, B. (2018). Length in listening texts as a single determiner of difficulty for comprehension. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 336-346. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193259

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative thematic analysis using categorical aggregation of reflection reports from 38 B1 students after listening to a three-minute-twenty-seconds text; six themes were categorized and frequency counts computed in SPSS 22. The article states length was "a less determining factor" than the other named factors.

> "According to reflection reports of students on the difficulties that they had while listening to a long text, text length (f= 5) is a less determining factor among other factors such as focusing on single words (f= 14) and speech rate (f= 9)."
