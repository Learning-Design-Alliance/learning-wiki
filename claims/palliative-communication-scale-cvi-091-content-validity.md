`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validity evaluation from the expert consultation stage, in which 20 experts from seven cities rated item importance, clarity, and relevance. The authors state the high CVI "suggested a strong alignment between the scale items and the research concepts".

> "The CVI for individual items ranged from 0.78 to 1.00, with an overall CVI of 0.91, reflecting a high level of consensus among experts regarding the appropriateness of the items [27]."
