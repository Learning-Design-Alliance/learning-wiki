## Evidence

### Jurkovič 2010

Jurkovič, V. (2010). Effect of Explicit Language Learning Strategy Instruction on Language-test and Self-assessment Scores. English Language Teaching, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v3n1p16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression (ENTER method) on 77 first-year students with membership in the treatment group and initial OPT score as predictors of "test difference". The article reports membership in the treatment group "did not have any statistically significant effect" (beta weight 0.03, Table 2).

> "The results of regression analysis presented in Table 2 show that membership in the treatment group did not have any statistically significant effect on language progress as measured by the OPT"
