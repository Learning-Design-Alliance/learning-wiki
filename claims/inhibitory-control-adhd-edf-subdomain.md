# Inhibitory control is the EDF subdomain most repeatedly linked to ADHD

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Among executive function subdomains, inhibitory control is the one that has repeatedly emerged as related to ADHD in research studies. [→ Meltzer 2007](#meltzer-2007)

## Evidence

### Meltzer 2007

Meltzer, L. (Ed.). (2007). Executive function in education: From theory to practice. The Guilford Press. https://www.guilford.com

`q1 · i?`

In Denckla's narrative review chapter on EF and EDF, the author reports that research on comorbidity between ADHD and learning disabilities has yielded one robust finding: inhibitory control is "repeatedly emerged as ADHD-related." No effect size is printed; this is the author's synthesis of prior studies.

> "The one subdomain of EDF that has repeatedly emerged as ADHD-related is that of inhibitory control"
