### Seyyed Kazem Banihashem 2022

Seyyed Kazem Banihashem, Omid Noroozi, Stan van Ginkel, Leah P. Macfadyen, Harm J.A. Biemans. (2022). A systematic review of the role of learning analytics in enhancing feedback practices in higher education. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2022.100489

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Sun et al. (2019), a study using survey data including a cognitive load scale, that "it can increase the mental load" for warning-only feedback, while adding encouragement raises engagement; the review concludes LA feedback should include encouragement.

> "If LA-based feedback relies only on warning systems, then it can increase the mental load, while putting warning systems with the encouragement of what to do can lead to higher emotional and cognitive engagement (Sun et al., 2019)."
