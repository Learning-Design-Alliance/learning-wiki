## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Visual analysis of group-mean learning curves found plateaus only in cutting and translocating of objects; the other seven tasks had not plateaued after five repetitions. [→ Jokinen E 2019](#jokinen-e-2019)

## Evidence

### Jokinen E 2019

Jokinen E, Mikkola TS, Härkki P. (2019). Effect of structural training on surgical outcomes of residents' first operative laparoscopy: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06657-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simulator data were visually analyzed using group means in each skill task and chosen parameters to define plateaus in learning curves. The authors interpret this as indicating the intervention with a fixed number of repetitions was insufficient.

> "Plateau was reached in two of the nine skill tasks; in cutting and in translocating of objects."
