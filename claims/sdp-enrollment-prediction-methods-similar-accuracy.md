Comparative analysis of four prediction techniques using prior-years district data at the school-by-grade level in the School District of Philadelphia. The study reports that "The predictions differ little in accuracy: predicted cohort size differs from actual cohort size by roughly six students across all methods."

> "This study compared the accuracy of four statistical techniques for predicting fall enrollment at the school-by-grade level, using data from prior years, to assess which approach might be the most useful for planning school staffing in SDP. The predictions differ little in accuracy: predicted cohort size differs from actual cohort size by roughly six students across all methods"
