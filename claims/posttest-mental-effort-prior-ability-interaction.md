## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

2×2×2 mixed ANOVA on mental effort ratings across the immediate and delayed post-tests; the article reports no significant main effects and prints no effect size for these null contrasts.

> "There was no significant main effect of the learning condition or of learners' prior ability on the mental effort invested by learners across post-tests"

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017 (2)

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up t-test after a significant prior ability × test moment interaction (medium effect size) on post-test mental effort; the article states the difference was not observed on the delayed post-test.

> "novice learners invested more mental effort (7.2 ± .9) than advanced learners (6.4 ± 1.1, t(59) = 3.326, p = .003, Cohen's d = .782, large effect size) on the immediate post-test"
