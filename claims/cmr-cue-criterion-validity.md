## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2014

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2014). Coupled Multiple-response vs. Free-response Conceptual Assessment: An Example from Upper-division Physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.6310](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.6310)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Criterion-validity analysis of the split-class comparison using standardized (z-score) exam and course scores calculated separately per class. Both formats correlated r = 0.79 with aggregate exam scores; course-score correlations were CMR r = 0.76 and FR r = 0.73.

> "The CMR CUE scores correlate strongly with aggregate exam scores (Pearson Correlation Coeﬃcient r=0.79, p < 0.05). For comparison, the correlation for the 49 students who took the FR version was also high (r = 0.79, p < 0.05)."
