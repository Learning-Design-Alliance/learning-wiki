## Evidence

### Andrej Favia 2013

Andrej Favia, Neil F. Comins, Geoffrey L. Thorpe. (2013). The Elements of Item Response Theory and its Framework in Analyzing Introductory Astronomy College Student Misconceptions. I. Galaxies. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1206.2302](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1206.2302)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Weighted-mean misconception factor coordinates (Table 8) computed from IRT CRC intersection coordinates for 403 college students yield different factor orderings for the two audiences. No effect size is printed.

> "Based on our MFCs, the most logical sequence for adults is in the order of factors 1, 2, 3, whereas the most logical sequence for children and adolescents is 2, 1, 3."
