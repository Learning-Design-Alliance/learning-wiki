## Evidence

### Eddy et al. 2021

Laura D. Eddy, Arthur D. Anastopoulos, Melissa R. Dvorsky, Paul J. Silvia, Jeffrey D. Labban & Joshua M. Langberg (2021). An RCT of a CBT Intervention for Emerging Adults with ADHD Attending College: Functional Outcomes. Journal of Clinical Child & Adolescent Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/15374416.2020.1867989

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Latent growth curve analysis of registrar-recorded credits earned in the RCT found no significant change in either condition and no between-group slope difference.

> "For number of earned credits, neither the immediate ACCESS (b = -.06, SE = .03, p = .065) condition nor the DTC (b = -.04, SE = .03, p = .149) condition changed and the slopes did not differ between the groups, Wald (1) = .20, p = .655, d = .05 [-.20, .30]."
