Klugman, J., Gordon, M. F., Sebring, P. B., & Sporte, S. E. (2015). A First Look at the 5Essentials in Illinois Schools. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://illinois.5-essentials.org/2014_public/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The original Chicago longitudinal study compared schools classified as strong or weak on the essential supports using teacher and student survey responses against learning gains on the ITBS. The report states that "Elementary and middle schools strong in most supports were about 10 times more likely than schools weak in most supports to show substantial gains in both reading and mathematics."
