q3 · i? · review · r2

Pre-post evaluation of a multifaceted intervention (lectures, interactive sessions, small group discussions, seminars) for 1st-year dental students in the US. Table 3 reports "More likely to read dental and medical journals: 53/104 vs. 19/90, P<0.001", plus confidence (95% vs. 71%, P<0.001) and experience (84% vs. 67%, P<0.05) gains.
