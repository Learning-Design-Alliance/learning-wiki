`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Ma et al. (2014), Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper (2014), and VanLehn (2011), that meta-analyses found "no significant differences in cognitive learning effects" between ITS and human tutoring or small-group instruction. The review does not print effect sizes for this contrast.
