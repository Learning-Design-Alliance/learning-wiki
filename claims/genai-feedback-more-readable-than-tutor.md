## Subclaims
`q3 i?` GenAI-produced feedback scored significantly higher on expert-rated readability than tutor-generated feedback. [→ Mutluhan Yilmaz 2026](#mutluhan-yilmaz-2026)

## Evidence

### Mutluhan Yilmaz 2026

Mutluhan Yilmaz, Andrea Gauthier, Mutlu Cukurova. (2026). Supporting online learners' regulation skills with the help of learning analytics and generative artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10595-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blind expert raters scored all feedback from both sources on a five-point readability scale in the nine-week MOODLE statistics course; the Mann–Whitney U test showed a significant difference in readability, "favouring GenAI (U = 300.5, p < 0.01)". No effect size was printed.
