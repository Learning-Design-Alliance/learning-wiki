### Demirbag 2014

Demirbag, M., & Gunel, M. (2014). Integrating Argument-Based Science Inquiry with Modal Representations: Impact on Science Achievement, Argumentation, and Writing Skills. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(1), 386-391. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.1.1632

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of multi-modal representation coherence and argument quality scores from ABSI activity reports in the same quasi-experimental study; the article reports r = .646 and cites Cohen (1992) to call it strong.

> "The Pearson's Correlation Coefficient was found to be .646. According to Cohen (1992), such a coefficient value can be interpreted as an indicator of a strong relationship between multi-modal representation coherence and argument quality."
