Evidence¶

Brian R. Flay 2014¶

Brian R. Flay, D.Phil. (2014). Replication of effects of the Positive Action program in randomized trials in Hawai'i and Chicago schools. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT01025674

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Longitudinal growth-curve analyses of the SECD scale in both cluster-randomized trials. The article also reports that a third small CRCT (4 schools per condition) in a rural southeastern location, conducted independently, showed a similar though weaker pattern with an ES of .22.

"Scores on the SECD scale items decreased as students got older in both Hawai'i and Chicago, but the decline was mitigated in the PA condition of both trials (ESs .46 and .58, respectively)"

Brian R. Flay 2014 (2)¶

Brian R. Flay, D.Phil. (2014). Replication of effects of the Positive Action program in randomized trials in Hawai'i and Chicago schools. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT01025674

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Moderation analysis reported in the article for three SECD subscales in the trials. No effect size is printed for the gender moderation itself, so the magnitude is unreported.

"Effects were moderated by gender occurred for three subscales of SECD; namely, program effects on honesty, self-control and respect for teachers were stronger for girls than boys"
