Shu Zhao 2026¶

Independent samples t-test on pretest scores of both groups (Levene's test indicated homogeneity of variances, F(1,50) = 2.973, p =.091). The article reports the pre-test difference was "not statistically significant (t(50) = 1.86, p =.069)"; no effect size is printed.

"According to the results of the independent samples t-test, the difference between the variances was not statistically significant (t(50) = 1.86, p =.069). This indicates that, despite being grouped according to differences in CT, the two groups demonstrated comparable levels of prior coding knowledge"
