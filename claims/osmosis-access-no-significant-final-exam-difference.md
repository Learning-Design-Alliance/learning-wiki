## Evidence

### Hoyt Garrik 2024

Hoyt Garrik, Adegboyega Samuel, Constantouris Gus, Basu Paramita. (2024). Study of the impact of introducing a multimedia learning tool in podiatric medical courses. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.12018

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A posttest-only nonequivalent-group quasi-experiment comparing two consecutive second-year cohorts (C = 87, T = 86 students) taking the same two courses. The treatment group with Osmosis access showed a slight upward trend, but "Welch's two sample t‐tests comparing average final exam scores between the groups indicated no statistically significant difference" in either course.

> "The difference in means between the two groups was 1.079 in Pharmacology (p = 0.441) and 0.848 in Podiatric Medicine I (p = 0.541). Welch's two sample t‐tests comparing average final exam scores between the groups indicated no statistically significant difference."
