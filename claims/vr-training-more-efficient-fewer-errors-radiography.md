## Evidence

### Shiner N 2023

Shiner N. (2023). Time for a simulation strategy? Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.685

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Rowe et al.'s comparison study in the same issue, that VR training (Virtual medical coaching) produced "a more efficient student, undertaking fewer errors" and faster diagnostic imaging than traditional simulation. No effect sizes are printed in the editorial.

> "Rowe et al. 8 provide striking evidence to suggest that training with VR software (Virtual medical coaching) produces a more efficient student, undertaking fewer errors and producing a diagnostic image in less time, compared to students trained using traditional simulation."
