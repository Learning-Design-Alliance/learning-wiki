## Evidence

### Dervisbegovic Selma 2025

Dervisbegovic Selma, Laky Markus, Tur Dino, Grundnig Julia, Rausch-Fan Xiaohui, Moritz Andreas, Holzinger Anita. (2025). Educational videos as a teaching approach to enhance dental students’ practical skills in preclinical courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07807-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Practical test results (Table 1, item 2.2): the repeated-viewing group (median 2) outperformed the single-viewing group (median 1) on correct application of the interdental brush, z −3.690, p 0.000, "effect size" −0.410 as printed.

> "Regarding the application of the IDB, Group 2 showed significantly higher precision Compared to Group 1."
