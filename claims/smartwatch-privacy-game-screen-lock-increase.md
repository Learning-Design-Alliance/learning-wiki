## Evidence

### Meredydd Williams 2019

Meredydd Williams, Jason R C Nurse, Sadie Creese. (2019). Smartwatch Games: Encouraging Privacy-Protective Behaviour in a Longitudinal Study. [doi:10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomised two-group field study in which five treatment participants played a privacy-themed Wear OS game and five controls played the same app without the privacy theme, with settings monitored for two months. The article reports "their usage of screen locks signiﬁcantly increasing ( p = 0.043)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The treatment group improved their protection, with their usage of screen locks signiﬁcantly increasing ( p = 0.043). In contrast, 80% of the control group continued to never restrict their settings."
