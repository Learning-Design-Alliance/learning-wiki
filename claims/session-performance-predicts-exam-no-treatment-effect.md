## Evidence

### Jakob Schwerter 2022

Jakob Schwerter, Franz Wortha, Peter Gerjets. (2022). E-learning with multiple-try-feedback: Can hints foster students' achievement during the semester? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10105-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OLS regressions with heteroskedastic robust standard errors on 87 students' standardized final exam scores. The article reports session performance significant in all models except columns (5) and (7), with mean correct answers coefficients ranging from 1.625 to 2.271.

> "As expected, the first row in all columns shows that treatment group assignment does not affect exam grades. The coefficient is small and insignificant."
