Pathblazer evidence summary 2020¶

Pathblazer evidence summary. (2020). https://www.edgenuity.com/support/technical-requirements/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The Floyd County study (Wolf et al., 2020) gathered teacher surveys, principal interviews, and focus groups with teachers and students as implementation measures. Responses were positive across groups, and teachers particularly liked that the program addressed gaps in students' knowledge and skills. No effect size is printed for these perceptions, so impact is null.

"Responses were overall quite positive about the usage of Pathblazer and its impacts on improving reading achievement. All three participant groups indicated that they would recommend the program to others."
