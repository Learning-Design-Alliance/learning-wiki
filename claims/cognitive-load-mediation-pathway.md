# Cognitive load mediates the effect of personalized paths on learning outcomes, with motivation and self-efficacy as complementary mediators

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Personalized paths significantly reduced cognitive load (β = −0.72), and higher cognitive load was associated with poorer post-test performance (β = −0.48), supporting a mediation pathway; the abstract reports an indirect effect of 0.28, 95% CI (0.19, 0.37). [→ Feng Z and Huang K 2026](#feng-z-and-huang-k-2026)

## Evidence

### Feng Z and Huang K 2026

Feng Z and Huang K (2026) Bayesian cognitive diagnosis optimizes personalized learning paths via mediation of cognitive load and Hidden Markov Model state transitions. Front. Psychol. 17:1879982. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1879982

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 resamples, bias-corrected 95% CIs) in the randomized experiment. The structural paths show "β = −0.72" for paths on load and "β = −0.48" for load on post-test; the abstract reports indirect effect = 0.28, CI (0.19, 0.37).

> "The direct eﬀect of intervention type on cognitive load was negative (β = −0.72, p < 0.001), indicating that personalized paths signiﬁcantly reduced cognitive load. The direct eﬀect of cognitive load on post-test performance was negative (β = −0.48, p < 0.001)"
