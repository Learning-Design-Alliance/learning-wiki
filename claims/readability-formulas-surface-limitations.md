## Evidence

### Madjid Sadallah 2024

Madjid Sadallah. (2024). User-Centered Course Reengineering: An Analytical Approach to Enhancing Reading Comprehension in Educational Content. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.11944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.11944)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Narrative review of readability assessment research. The review reports that over 200 formulas exist (e.g., Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch-Kincaid, Fog Index, SMOG) and that they "perform poorly in predicting readers' comprehension judgments". No effect sizes are printed.

> "they have been criticized for focusing on surface structure and not accounting for deep syntactic and semantic features, which limits their validity from a psycholinguistic perspective"
