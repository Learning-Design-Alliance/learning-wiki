## Evidence

### Rachel Freed 2022

Rachel Freed, David McKinnon, Michael Fitzgerald, and Christina M. Norris. (2022). Development and validation of an astronomy self-efficacy instrument for understanding and doing. Physical Review Physics Education Research 18, 010117. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.18.010117

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA with cohort membership as the independent variable on the balanced N = 116 dataset, testing the hypothesis that APSE would not differ since both cohorts took the same-level astronomy course. Descriptive means were 46.259 (lab) and 49.690 (nonlab) out of 80.

> "Table VIII shows that there is no significant difference in the APSE scores showing that both groups were almost equally assured about their astronomical knowledge [ Fð1; 114Þ¼ 1.628], p ¼ 0.205."
