## Evidence

### Alkhammash L 2025

Alkhammash L, Saraco AN, Goes Schaurich C, Lo BSY, Krebs C. (2025). The impact of an immersive virtual reality anatomy lab for informal science education. Anat Sci Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70087

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the same cohort (SSQ n = 70) showing negative links between cognitive load/cybersickness and relevance and confidence. The article reports these were "negatively correlated with the sense of relevance and confidence"; only one participant experienced slight to moderate cybersickness symptoms.

> "both cognitive load and symptoms of cybersickness, while rare in this study population, were negatively correlated with the sense of relevance and confidence (p < 0.05)"
