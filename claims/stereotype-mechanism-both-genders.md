The authors' theoretical interpretation in the Discussion, grounded in social cognitive learning theory, proposes that both negative stereotypes affecting disadvantaged students and positive stereotypes causing advantaged students to "choke under pressure" convey the association; no direct test of this mechanism is reported.

> "In this way, gender inequality is negatively associated with academic achievement for both boys and girls through gender stereotypes."
