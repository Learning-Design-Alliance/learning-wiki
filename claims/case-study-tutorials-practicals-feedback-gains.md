Posner Mareike G., Dempsey Nina C., Unsworth Amanda J. (2023). The Benefits of Using Case Study Focussed, Problem Based Learning Approaches to Unit Design for Biomedical Science Students. British Journal of Biomedical Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10340524/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Feedback-survey comparison across the 20/21, 21/22 and 22/23 cohorts for the statement on case study-based tutorials, expressed as positive/neutral/negative percentages. The article reports “a 25% increase in positive ratings in 22/23 vs. 20/21” and no negative ratings after redesign; no test statistic or effect size is printed.

> "Changes implemented in the 21/22 and 22/23 academic years to the case study focussed tutorials, led to none of the students surveyed rating the tutorials as negative compared with the 20/21 academic year (Figure 3B) with a 25% increase in positive ratings in 22/23 vs. 20/21."
