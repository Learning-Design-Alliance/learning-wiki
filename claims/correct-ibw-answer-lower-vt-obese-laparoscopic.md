## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey: respondents choosing 'height' as the most important variable (correct answer group, n = 63) versus others (n = 19) set lower median VT/kgIBW in laparoscopic surgery (p < 0.001).

> "Among cases of laparoscopic surgery, the median VT/kgIBW in the correct answer group was lower than that in the incorrect answer group, at 8.4 (7.8–9.2 [6.9–9.8]) vs. 9.6 (8.8–10.2 [8.2–12.1]) mL/kg (p < 0.001)."

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018 (2)

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same survey, obese-patient vignette (BMI 41.52): correct answer group set lower median VT/kgIBW (p = 0.014); in non-laparoscopic surgery (BMI 18.12 vignette) the difference was not significant (p = 0.109).

> "In cases of obese patients, the median VT/kgIBW in the correct answer group was lower than that in the incorrect answer group, at 7.8 (7.6–8.3 [6.1–9.1]) vs. 8.8 (7.6–9.1 [6.4–12.1]) mL/kg (p = 0.014)."
