## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in basic medical education (β=−0.52), clinical medical education (β=−1.36) and clerkship rotation (β=−2.10) gave lower evaluations than general education students, with satisfaction ORs of 0.86, 0.66 and 0.58 respectively. [→ Wang Cixiao 2020](#wang-cixiao-2020)

## Evidence

### Wang Cixiao 2020

Wang Cixiao, Xie A’na, Wang Weimin, Wu Hongbin. (2020). Association between medical students’ prior experiences and perceptions of formal online education developed in response to COVID-19: a cross-sectional study in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-041886

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression with learning phase as a categorical covariate in the 99,559-student survey; the article reports a monotonic gradient, with clerkship rotation students lowest ("β=−2.10").

> "Students in basic medical education (β=−0.52; 95% CI −0.64 to 0.41; p<0.01), clinical medical education (β=−1.36; 95% CI −1.48 to 1.24; p<0.01) and clerkship rotation (β=−2.10; 95% CI −2.26 to 1.95; p<0.01) gave lower evaluations of online education than those in general education (table 5)."
