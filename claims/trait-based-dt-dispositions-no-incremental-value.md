Liu Hsing-Yuan. (2026). Skill-based competencies, not traits, are associated with design thinking–aligned teaching behaviours in nursing education: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09830-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression Model 3 among the same 95-faculty sample: entering DTTQ-TW after skill-based competencies produced no significant variance increase; the article reports "trait-based competencies were not statistically associated with teaching behaviours".

> "The inclusion of DTTQ-TW was not accompanied by a meaningful change in explained variance (ΔR² = 0.001, F-change = 0.076, p = 0.784), and trait-based competencies were not statistically associated with teaching behaviours (β = 0.110, p = 0.124)."
