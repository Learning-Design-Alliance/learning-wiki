### Yin David 2019

Yin David, Cabana François, Tousignant-Laflamme Yannick, Bédard Sonia, Tousignant Michel. (2019). Can a physiotherapy student assume the role of an advanced practice physiotherapist in Orthopaedic surgery triage? A prospective observational study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-019-2864-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Satisfaction was measured with a modified Visit-Specific Satisfaction Instrument completed confidentially at discharge in the 86-patient study; the mean total score was "90.0% (95% CI: 67.5–100%)". Very few patients refused being seen by the student.

> "Patient satisfaction with the outpatient clinic model was high with an average total score of 90.0% (95% CI: 67.5–100%). Mean time taken by the PS to perform his evaluation was 30.9 min."
