## Evidence

### Feng Shuo 2024

Feng Shuo, Sun Ying-Jin, Zhu Qi-Rui, Shi Si-Feng, Zhang Yong-Shuo, Yuan Feng. (2024). Integration of 3D printing and case-based learning in clinical practice for the treatment of developmental dysplasia of the hip. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05934-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 120 fourth-year clinical medical students allocated by random number table into two groups of 60, within a 6-month orthopaedic course. Independent samples t tests showed the post-course theory difference was significant: "t = 2.254, P = 0.026". No effect size is printed.

> "The theory scores of the CBL group (62.88 ± 7.98) and 3D + CBL group (66.35 ± 8.85) were significantly different (t = 2.254, P = 0.026)"
