Core-Plus Mathematics 2010¶

Core-Plus Mathematics. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Core-Plus Mathematics, High School Math. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.wmich.edu/cpmp

q2 · i? · causal · r2

SAT math outcome from the same quasi-experiment by Schoen and Hirsch (2002). The report describes a "positive, though not statistically significant, effect" meeting the substantively important threshold; the exact effect size value is not printed.

"The study also reported a positive, though not statistically significant, effect on SAT math scores; this finding was large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria (i.e., an effect size of at least 0.25)."
