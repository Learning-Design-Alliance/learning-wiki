`q2 · i3` · `causal · r?`

The article reports this early study of HOPS implemented by school mental health professionals with 47 students identified as having ADHD and academic problems, with caregiver-rated "organized actions (Cohen's d = 0.88)" and the other listed effects; teacher ratings did not improve.

> "caregivers in HOPS reported their children made significant improvements in organized actions (Cohen's d = 0.88), task planning (d = 1.05), and homework completion behaviors (d = 0.85). Outcomes based on teacher ratings did not show improvement"
