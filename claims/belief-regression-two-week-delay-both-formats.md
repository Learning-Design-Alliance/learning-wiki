## Evidence

### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 2 compared immediate and two-week delayed retention groups: "belief in corrected misinformation was significantly lower in the immediate condition than in the delayed condition (reflecting belief regression)" for both formats (t(527) = −8.89; t(527) = −10.39, ps < .001).

> "Belief in corrected misinformation was significantly lower in the immediate condition than in the delayed condition (reflecting belief regression) for both the refutation and truth sandwich conditions, t(527) = −8.89, p < .001; t(527) = −10.39, p < .001 respectively."
