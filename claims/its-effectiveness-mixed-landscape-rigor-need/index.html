Meriem Zerkouk 2025¶

Meriem Zerkouk, Miloud Mihoubi and Belkacem Chikhaoui. (2025). A Comprehensive Review of AI-based Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Applications and Challenges. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18882 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2507.18882

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic literature review following Kitchenham's three-stage method and PRISMA reporting, analyzing qualified ITS studies published 2010–2025. The review reports "a complex landscape regarding the effectiveness of ITS" with both advancements and persistent challenges, and calls for more rigorous experimental design and data analysis.

"The results reveal a complex landscape regarding the effectiveness of ITS, highlighting both advancements and persistent challenges. The study also identifies a need for greater scientific rigor in experimental design and data analysis."
