The chapter reports, citing Prather, Rudolph, Brissenden, and Schlingman (2009), that students "regardless of the institution type, class size, or their backgrounds, are capable of achieving similar learning gains in Astro 101," countering claims that entrance-filtered four-year students hold an advantage.

> "Prather, Rudolph, Brissenden, and Schlingman (2009) showed that all of these students, regardless of the institution type, class size, or their backgrounds, are capable of achieving similar learning gains in Astro 101 (note this is part of the same study as the Rudolph et al. 2010 data)."
