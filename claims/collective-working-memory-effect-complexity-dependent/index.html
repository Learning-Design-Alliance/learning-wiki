Kirschner 2018¶

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

A theoretically derived hypothesis that the article reports was experimentally confirmed by F. Kirschner et al. (2011); evidence is attributed secondhand and the text does not print the experiment's sample, statistics or effect sizes. The efficiency is 'affected by the trade-off between the possibility to divide information processing amongst the WMs of the group members' and the costs of communication and coordination.

"for high-complexity tasks, group members would learn in a more efficient way - both in terms of the learning process and outcomes - than individual learners, while for low-complexity tasks, individual learning would be more efficient"
