## Evidence

### Lorijn 2021

Lorijn, S. J.; Engels, M. C.; Huisman, M.; Veenstra, R. (2021). Long-Term Effects of Acceptance and Rejection by Parents and Peers on Educational Attainment: A Study from Pre-Adolescence to Early Adulthood. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-021-01506-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null result from ordinal logistic regression (Model 1, N = 2229), with peer acceptance measured as proportions of best-friend nominations at T1. The article reports "OR = 1.19, p = 0.716"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Peer acceptance was not found to change the odds of educational attainment in early adulthood (OR = 1.19, p = 0.716), contrary to the third hypothesis"
