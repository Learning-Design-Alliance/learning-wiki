### de Oliveira Carlota Rocha 2026

de Oliveira Carlota Rocha, da Silva Barboza Andressa, de Andrade Juliana Silva Ribeiro, Lund Rafael Guerra. (2026). Scoping Review on Active Teaching and Learning Methodologies in Dentistry. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13109

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of controlled studies found that "most studies with control groups did not find significant differences in student performance" between active and traditional methods in clinical subjects. No pooled effect size is reported.

> "Contrary to expectations, most studies with control groups did not find significant differences in student performance between active methodologies and traditional teaching in clinical subjects"
