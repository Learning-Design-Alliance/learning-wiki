# Advancement to WRL 7 was observed only after industry-embedded exposure, not additional coursework

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the observed cases, the WRL 6 to 7 transition occurred only after industry-embedded exposure such as production installs and co-op placements; this is a description, not a tested hypothesis. [→ Smith 2026](#smith-2026)

## Evidence

### Smith 2026

Smith, Dalton Ross; Whittington, Wilburn; Martinez, Alejandro; Duncan, Aidan; Li, Gang. (2026). A Conceptual Framework for Enhancing Workforce Readiness for Smart Manufacturing in the AI Era. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11540

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation from the RSG continuation case, where three Fall 2025 students reached WRL 7 via an on-site production install, and from Case 4 co-op placements. The authors state the observation "is a description, not a hypothesis that was set and tested in advance".

> "This continuation project is the strongest indication in the present data set that the WRL 6→7 transition is gated by industry-embedded exposure rather than by additional coursework, though the evidence describes what happened rather than proving cause and effect."
