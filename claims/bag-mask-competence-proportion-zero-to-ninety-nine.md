## Evidence

### KC Ashish 2017

KC Ashish, Wrammert Johan, Nelin Viktoria, Clark Robert B., Ewald Uwe, Peterson Stefan, Målqvist Mats. (2017). Evaluation of Helping Babies Breathe Quality Improvement Cycle (HBB-QIC) on retention of neonatal resuscitation skills six months after training in Nepal. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-017-0853-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Figure 1 and its describing text report competence on the 7-step bag-and-mask checklist at ≥80% among the 137 health workers: "99% of the health workers were competent 6 months after the training", a slight non-significant increase over the immediate post-training level.

> "There were no health workers who were competent in bag-and-mask skills before the training; 93% of health workers were competent immediately after the training (p < 0.001); and 99% of the health workers were competent 6 months after the training (p = 0.8)"
