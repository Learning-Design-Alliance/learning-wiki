An Individual Participant Data Meta-Analysis to Support Power Analyses for Randomized Intervention Studies in Preschool: Cognitive and Socio-Emotional Learning Outcomes. q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The article's review of prior multilevel design-parameter research reports these median R2Child values for US preschool samples, with most estimates using sociodemographic characteristics plus a baseline measure. No R2Group estimates were available in those studies.

"Applying these covariates, typical values for cognitive outcomes were Mdn R2 Child = 0.24/0.21 when using standardized tests/parent reports, and Mdn R 2 Child = 0.26/0.18 for SEL outcomes when using teacher/parent reports."
