Evidence¶

Ye Mao 2018¶

Ye Mao, Chen Lin, and Min Chi. (2018). Deep Learning vs. Bayesian Knowledge Tracing: Student Models for Interventions. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 10, No 2. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/318

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis over both datasets (Section 4.2) motivating learning-gain prediction: correlations of r = 0.76 and r = 0.673 for pre-post scores versus r = −0.04 and r = −0.02 for pretest and NLG, showing high scorers do not necessarily benefit more from the tutor.

"we found a signiﬁcant strong positive correlation between students’ pre- and post-test scores: r = 0.76 andp = 4.60× 10−33 for Cordillera, andr = 0.673 andp = 6.04× 10−46 for Pyrenees respectively; however, no strong correlation was found between students’ pretest and Normalized Learning Gain:r =−0.04 and p = 0.607 for Cordillera, andr =−0.02 andp = 0.683 for Pyrenees."
