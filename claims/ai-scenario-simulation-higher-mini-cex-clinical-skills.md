## Evidence

### Zheng Koulong 2024

Zheng Koulong, Shen Zhiyu, Chen Zanhao, Che Chang, Zhu Huixia. (2024). Application of AI-empowered scenario-based simulation teaching mode in cardiovascular disease education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05977-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment of 34 vs 32 medical undergraduates using a Mini-CEX sheet adapted for cardiovascular medicine (Cronbach's alpha 0.90). Table 2 reports 76.24 ± 12.302 vs 70.19 ± 11.255 with "t 2.08" and P < 0.05. No effect size is printed.

> "Regarding Mini-CEX examination scores, students within the experimental group attained an average score of 76.24, which was notably greater than the average score of 70.19 achieved by students in the control group (p < 0.001)."
