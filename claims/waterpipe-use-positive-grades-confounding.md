`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple linear regression with robust estimators in the cross-sectional study of 403 medical students found "waterpipe use was positively associated with higher average grades and was statistically significant" in both models; the printed B values are unstandardized, so no standardized effect size is reported.

> "waterpipe use was positively associated with higher average grades and was statistically significant (Model 1: B = 2.13, 95% CI 0.61–3.65, p = 0.006; Model 2: B = 2.67, 95% CI 1.10–4.24, p < 0.001)"
