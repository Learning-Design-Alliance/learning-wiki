## Evidence

### Jang 2020

Jang, Sung-Soo. (2020). The efficacy of different types of metalinguistic information in L2 written corrective feedback. English Teaching, 75(4), 33-56. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.75.4.202012.33

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Delayed posttest results for dictogloss writing accuracy in the four-session experiment, two weeks after the final CF treatment. The study reports all experimental groups "performed better than the CO group on the delayed posttest" with large effect sizes, including the MC group (d = 1.139).

> "All the experimental groups performed better than the CO group on the delayed posttest. Effect sizes between the CO group and ea ch experimental group were large (d = 1.772 for DR; d = 1.139 for MC; d = 2.112 for MS; d = 1.186 for MN)."
