## Evidence

### Alhurishi Sultana A. 2021

Alhurishi Sultana A., Aljuraiban Ghadeer S., Alshaikh Fahdah A., Almutairi Mona M., Almutairi Khalid M. (2021). Predictors of students' academic achievements in allied health professions at King Saud University: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02525-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression in a retrospective cohort of 1634 complete graduate records from three King Saud University allied health colleges showed higher admission scores predicted higher GPA: "HSGA (β = 0.237, 95 % CI = 0.035 to 0.049)", with smaller coefficients for APT and ACT.

> "In addition, the higher HSGA (β = 0.237, 95 % CI = 0.035 to 0.049), APT (β = 0.096, 95 % CI = 0.005 to 0.012), and ACT (β = 0.061, 95 % CI = 0.001to 0.008) likely to have a higher cumulative GPA."
