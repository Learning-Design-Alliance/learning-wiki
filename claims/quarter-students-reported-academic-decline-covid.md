Cross-sectional online survey administered during a SARS-CoV-2 screening campaign at Sapienza University of Rome (Feb–Apr 2022). "A total of 25.4% of the participants reported a decline in academic performance" among the 1,134-student analytical sample.

> "A total of 25.4% of the participants reported a decline in academic performance. In addition, Coronavirus Anxiety Scale scores revealed that 133 (11.5%) students had a dysfunctional anxiety problem due to COVID-19."
