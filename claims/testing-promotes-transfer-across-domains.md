`q2 i?` Repeated testing improved students' ability to transfer facts and concepts to a different domain compared with restudy, when students were told the studied information applied. [→ Brame CJ 2015](#brame-cj-2015)

## Evidence

### Brame CJ 2015

Brame CJ, Biel R. (2015). Test-Enhanced Learning: The Potential for Testing to Promote Greater Learning in Undergraduate Science Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-11-0208

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Butler (2010), experiments in which undergraduates studied six passages and then restudied or repeatedly took cued-recall tests with feedback, with a final test 1 week later including inferential far-transfer questions. Students were explicitly told studied information could answer final-test questions.

> "Butler observed that repeated testing improved students’ ability to transfer knowledge to different domains when compared with restudy."
