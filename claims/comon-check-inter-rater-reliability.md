## Evidence

### Radziej K 2017

Radziej K, Loechner J, Engerer C, Niglio de Figueiredo M, Freund J, Sattel H, Bachmann C, Berberat PO, Dinkel A, Wuensch A. (2017). How to assess communication skills? Development of the rating scale ComOn Check. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1392823

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Psychometric evaluation in which two trained psychologist raters independently rated 135 videotaped student consultations after consensus development. Absolute agreement ranged from 31% to 77% across items; the authors report ICCs between 0.41 and 0.84, describing them as "sufficiently good." Item A2 showed a low ICC despite medium-high absolute agreement.

> "The average ICCs indicated moderate (items B1, B2, and C1) to high (items F, A1, C2, D, and E1-E5) inter-rater reliabilities with the exception of item A2 (Patient's perception), which despite a medium-high rater absolute agreement, was characterized by a low ICC."
