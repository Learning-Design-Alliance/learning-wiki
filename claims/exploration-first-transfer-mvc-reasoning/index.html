Evidence¶

Saba 2025¶

Saba, J., Kapur, M., & Roll, I. (2025). Learning about multivariable causality with interactive simulations: exploration before instruction may hurt immediate gains but benefits transfer. Instructional Science, 53, 1603–1632. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09726-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing MVC reasoning on the predation pretest and transfer Task 3 found a significant condition effect (ηp2 = 0.077) and task effect (ηp2 = 0.298); "Students from Exploration-first condition significantly improved their MVC reasoning compared to students from Instruction-first condition". Predation pretest scores did not differ between conditions.

"showed a significant main effect for condition, F(1,69) = 5.743, p = 0.019, ηp2 = 0.077 with a medium effect size (Fig. 5) and a significant main effect for the task, F(1,69) = 29.287, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.298 with a large effect size. Students from Exploration-first condition significantly improved their MVC reasoning compared to students from Instruction-first condition."
