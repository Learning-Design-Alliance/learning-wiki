Yun 2025¶

Yun, C., & Fitz, J. (2025). Successful teacher residencies: What matters and what works. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/480.783

q2 · i? · associational · r?

From the same CTC 2021–22 and 2022–23 program completer survey analyses: residency completers "reported more intensive clinical experiences with higher levels of support and feedback" and had higher first-attempt performance assessment passage. No effect sizes are printed.

"In addition, residency completers reported more intensive clinical experiences with higher levels of support and feedback and were more likely to pass teaching performance assessments on their first attempt."
