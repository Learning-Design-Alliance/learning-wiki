## Evidence

### Van de Sande 2019

Van de Sande, E., Kleemans, T., Verhoeven, L., & Segers, E. (2019). The linguistic nature of children's scientific reasoning. Learning and Instruction, 62, 20-26. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.02.002

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path model (Fig. 1) in the 269-child sample; visuospatial inferencing correlated r = .48, .26, and .23 with the three components (Table 3) but was not retained for Generating conclusions in the parsimonious model. No effect sizes printed for the paths.

> "Fig. 1shows that the number of significant predictors for scientiﬁc reasoning varies across the diﬀerent stages. All three cognitive predictors relate to Hypothesis validation, both inferencing skills but not academic vocabulary relate to Experimentation, and only linguistic inferencing relates to Generating conclusions."
