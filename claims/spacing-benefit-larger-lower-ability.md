## Evidence

### Paulo F. Carvalho 2020

Paulo F. Carvalho, Faria Sana and Veronica X. Yan. (2020). Self-regulated spacing in a massive open online course is related to better learning. npj Science of Learning 5:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0061-1

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Regression predicting quiz grade from spacing, final exam grade, and their interaction, with pretest and time covariates and random factors. The article reports a "signiﬁcant spacing by ﬁnal exam grade interaction, β = −0.04"; marginal effects are plotted in Fig. 3a.

> "while there was still an overall beneﬁt of spacing, β = 0.08, SE = 0.01, t(5644) = 5.24, p < 0.001, there was also a signiﬁcant spacing by ﬁnal exam grade interaction, β = −0.04, SE = 0.01, t(6017) = −3.12, p = 0.002."
