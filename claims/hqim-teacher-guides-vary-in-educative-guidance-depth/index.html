The report's curriculum review of Illustrative Mathematics, Carnegie Learning, i-Ready, and Savvas found that guidance embedded in all four HQIM includes the five Practices, but with varying justification. For example, the i-Ready materials "state the practices and why they are important and provide guidance on how teachers should do the work, including examples," while Illustrative Mathematics states the practices without expanding on why they matter.
