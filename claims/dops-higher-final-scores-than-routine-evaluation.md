## Evidence

### Habibi Hengameh 2015

Habibi Hengameh, Raiesifar Afsaneh, Khaghanizade Morteza, Mahmudi Hosein, Seyed Mazhari Marjan, Ebadi Abbas. (2015). The Effect of Applying Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (DOPS) on Nursing Students’ Clinical Skills: A Randomized Clinical Trial. Global Journal of Health Science. [doi:10.5539/gjhs.v7n7p17](https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v7n7p17)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized clinical trial at Nursing Army College, Tehran: intervention group evaluated via DOPS, control via routine instructor judgment. Independent t-test showed "a significant difference in resulted scores from the two different evaluation methods"; Table 2 reports IV catheterization 112.34±10.24 vs 86.08±10.92 (T=-10.373, Df=68, P=0.000) and change dressing 92.22±8.15 vs 66.62±9.82 (T=-11.858, Df=68, P=0.000).

> "The independent t-test indicates a significant difference in resulted scores from the two different evaluation methods (Table 2)."
