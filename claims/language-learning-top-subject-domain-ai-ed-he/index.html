Crompton 2023¶

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

A priori coding of subject domains across the 138 studies into 14 areas. The review reports "language learning at 17%, computer science at 16%, and engineering at 12%", with the management of students category third at 14%, and notes prior reviews also found language learning dominant.

"There is not a wide difference in the percentages of top subject domains, with language learning at 17%, computer science at 16%, and engineering at 12%."
