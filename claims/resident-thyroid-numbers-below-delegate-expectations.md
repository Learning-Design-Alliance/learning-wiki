Survey results section comparing national board requirements with delegate expectations for thyroid procedures during residency; medians and ranges are reported descriptively with no inferential test, so no effect size is available.

> "The minimum number of total/near-total thyroidectomies to be performed/assisted during residency varied between 0 and 40 (median 0.5)/0 and 50 (median 0) and these values were far below the values expected to be sufficient from the national delegates’ perspective: 0–50 (median 10) and 0–50 (median 20), respectively."
