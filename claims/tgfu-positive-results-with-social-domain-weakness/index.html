The review's purpose-and-results synthesis of the 12 studies reports that most objectives targeted game-development aspects and that "these studies provide positive results about how the use of the TGfU model leads to an improvement". Comparison studies against the technical model, prescriptive feedback or CSAM are described as sources for understanding the model's weaknesses.

"Generally, these studies provide positive results about how the use of the TGfU model leads to an improvement in some specific aspect of student performance and understanding of the game."
