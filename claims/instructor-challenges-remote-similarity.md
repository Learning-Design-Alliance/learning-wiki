### Michael F. J. Fox 2020

Michael F. J. Fox, Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Teaching labs during a pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020 and an outlook for the future. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.01271](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.01271)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Likert rankings of transition challenges (Fig. 2) identified "making the remote class as similar to the in-person version as possible" as the most common reported challenge, with time and technology constraints also cited as major.

> "The most common reported challenge instructors faced was making the remote class as similar to the in-person version as possible. Instructors also cited time and technology constraints as major challenges."

### Michael F. J. Fox 2020 (2)

Michael F. J. Fox, Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Teaching labs during a pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020 and an outlook for the future. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.01271](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.01271)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Instructors reported that grading "did not seem to be a problem for too many people," which the authors attribute to institutions switching "to pass/fail grading schemes" and encouraged grading leniency.

> "perhaps because a large number of institutions switched to pass/fail grading schemes, or because many instructors were encouraged to be more lenient with their grading in the remote situation"
