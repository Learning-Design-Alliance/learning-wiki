The review attributes, citing [39], a risk that open-domain chatbots prioritizing speed and fluency amplify the illusion of fluency bias, reducing cognitive struggle and weakening critical thinking. This is the review's synthesis, not a new empirical test.

> "AI tools that prioritize speed, fluency, and convenience—such as open-domainchatbots—riskamplifyingthisbias,reducingcognitivestruggleand weakening critical thinking [39]."
