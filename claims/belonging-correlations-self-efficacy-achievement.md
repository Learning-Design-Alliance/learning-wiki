The review reports, per Korpershoek et al.'s meta-analysis, that "the correlations of belonging with secondary school students' achievement (standardized test scores and school grades) are small to moderate." The review also notes belonging may not be essential for learning to occur, citing Gillen-O'Neel and Fuligni (2013).

> "the correlations of belonging with secondary school students' achievement (standardized test scores and school grades) are small to moderate, as Korpershoek et al. explained in their meta-analysis of studies of secondary students' belonging."
