## Evidence

### Liu Mei-Yuan 2025

Liu Mei-Yuan, Liao Li-Ling, Huang Yu-Ting, Lee Yi-Chen, Lai I-Ju. (2025). Effectiveness of a scenario-based simulation course on improving the clinical communication skills of dietetic students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06684-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests within the control group (n = 51), which received no clinical communication skills intervention. Confidence and self-efficacy improved significantly; competence did not change significantly.

> "In the control group, significant improvements were observed in communication confidence and self-efficacy (p = 0.001 and p < 0.001), but there was no statistically significant change in communication competence."
