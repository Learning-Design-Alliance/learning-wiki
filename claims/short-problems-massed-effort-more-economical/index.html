Evidence¶

Pechstein 1921¶

Pechstein, L. A. (1921). Massed vs. Distributed Effort in Learning. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070137

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Table I reports mean trials, time and errors for two groups of rats (nine per group) and two groups of humans (six per group) learning Maze A by the part method under distributed versus massed conditions, with massed records lower in each section. The author concludes it is "more economical to mass learning effort than to distribute it".

"First, provided the maze problem is short, it is more economical to mass learning effort than to distribute it, irrespective whether economy is estimated in terms of trials, time or errors."
