## Evidence

### Hayek Joyce 2021

Hayek Joyce, de Vries Hein, Tueni Maya, Lahoud Nathalie, Winkens Bjorn, Schneider Francine. (2021). Increased Adherence to the Mediterranean Diet and Higher Efficacy Beliefs Are Associated with Better Academic Achievement: A Longitudinal Study of High School Adolescents in Lebanon. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18136928

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model analysis of three-wave longitudinal data from 563 Lebanese adolescents (362 at 6 months, 345 at 12 months), adjusting for demographics and parenting style. The article reports "An increase in the adherence to the Mediterranean diet was significantly associated with an increase in academic achievement" with B 0.25 (95% CI 0.19, 0.30); no standardized effect size was printed.
