Classroom comparison study in the authors' reformed modern physics course, reported in Section IVB. The curriculum included interactive lectures and homework using the Photoelectric Effect simulation; the description quotes the printed percentages "an average of 83% of students" versus 20% and 40%. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "an average of 83% of students answered correctly with correct reasoning in the courses using the simulation, compared to 20% of students in a traditional course and 40% of students in a traditional course supplemented by a research-based computer tutorial."
