Gordon 2025¶

Gordon, C. S., Brake, N., Hammond, S. P., Picton, I., Notley, T., Rowston, K., Gutierrez, A., Basarkod, G., Costello, K., Gupta, M., West, M., & Dicke, T. (2025). Adolescent flourishing in a digitally connected world: A qualitative systematic review and meta-synthesis. Preprint. https://osf.io/j48k5/?view_only=8b6423082746439abb4106526e953aaf

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Synthesis under the competence theme: a participant using Scratch described "It's creative conﬁdence. Feeling empowered, not just a consumer who plays games on the computer". Other subthemes reported pride in moderating forums and social confidence among shy adolescents that transferred to offline relationships.

"Adolescents' experiences highlight how online programs let them "explore [their] imagination", (12-year-old male; Brennan, 2021, p.588), fostering creative confidence, empowerment, and resilience."
