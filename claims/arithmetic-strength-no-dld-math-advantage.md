# A strength in arithmetic skills did not distinguish geometric or fraction skills among DLD children

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The MANCOVA for arithmetic strengths showed no distinctions in geometric or fraction skills between DLD children with and without an arithmetic strength. [→ Kleemans 2025](#kleemans-2025)

## Evidence

### Kleemans 2025

Kleemans, T., Vissers, C. T. W. M., & Segers, E. (2025). Linguistic precursors of sixth-grade geometric and fraction skills in children with and without Developmental Language Disorder. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102019. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102019

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up MANCOVA within the DLD group comparing children with versus without an arithmetic-strength profile; neither geometric skills nor fraction skills differed between the two subgroups, and no effect sizes were printed for these null tests.

> "The MANCOVA for arithmetic skills, however, did not show distinctions in geometric and fraction skills between DLD children with and without a strength in arithmetic skills (geometric skills: F(1,41) = .34, p = .562; fraction skills: F(1,41) = 2.20, p = .145)."
