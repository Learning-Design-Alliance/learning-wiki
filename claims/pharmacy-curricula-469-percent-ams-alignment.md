Ade-Yusuf Oyinlola Rodhiat et al. (2026). From classrooms to practice: does pre-service training for one health professions provide readiness for antimicrobial stewardship? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09843-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Curriculum-mapping analysis of five pharmacy pre-service curricula (UniPort, JKZ, UniLome, OAU, UNN) against WHO AMS competencies. The article reports overall alignment of 46.9%, with UniPort at 67% and UniLome at 39%. No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, AMS alignment with WHO framework was 46.9% (Table 4). Pharmacy pre-service curricula aligned more consistently with core pharmacological science and advisory functions, but relatively lower alignment with operational processes (dosage advice, dispensing accuracy, supply timeliness, documentation). By universities, UniPort showed the strongest alignment (67%), while UniLome had the weakest (39%)"
