`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design-rationale statement in the introduction, citing PER studies on student reasoning about probability distributions. Activity 5 has students vary trial count and seed and estimate SE(p̂) = √(p̂(1−p̂)/N_trial), explaining why spread decreases without implying the state changed.

> "The finite-sampling activity deserves particular emphasis because it addresses a persistent misconception that is systematically underemphasized in short superposition modules: finite-run frequencies fluctuate even when the quantum state and theoretical probabilities remain unchanged [8, 14]."
