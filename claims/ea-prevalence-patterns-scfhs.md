`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

> "a universal topic review- a program designed to help healthcare professionals build strong, practical knowledge in key medical areas through updated, evidence-based content- which was employed by 69% of all programs, and core specialty topic reviews, which were utilized in 52% of the programs across diverse disciplines."

### Alrehaily Ali 2026 (3)

Alrehaily Ali, Alomair Abdulmohsen M., Kattan Thuraya, Alrumaihi Nouf, Carrick Frederick Robert. (2026). Educational activities as a pathway to postgraduate standardization: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08922-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Discussion-level interpretation of the document-analysis frequency data: journal club most frequently mentioned across SCFHS programs; simulation least utilized, which the authors attribute to limited resources and program-structure factors.

> "journal club was the most frequently mentioned, consistent with PGME literature showing that journal clubs remain near-universal and are routinely required in many training programs [32]. In contrast, simulation is often underused due to limited resources and factors related to program structure, despite substantial educational value [33], which explains our result of the least utilization of simulation."
