## Evidence

### Fogel Tempelhof Ortal 2024

Fogel Tempelhof Ortal, Mezad-Koursh Daphna, Hilely Assaf, Gaton Dan, Kurtz Shimon. (2024). Bridging gaps in ophthalmology residency programs: the link between practice, training and confidence in ocular examination and gonioscopy for diagnosing glaucoma, a blinding disease. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05665-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported survey item on gonioscopy practice; the article reports the count of 46 respondents omitting gonioscopy in predisposed patients.

> "One-third of the respondents (n = 46) reported not performing gonioscopy, even for a patient with anatomical predisposition for acute or chronic angle closure glaucoma (e.g., a phakic patient with a shallow AC)."

### Fogel Tempelhof Ortal 2024 (2)

Fogel Tempelhof Ortal, Mezad-Koursh Daphna, Hilely Assaf, Gaton Dan, Kurtz Shimon. (2024). Bridging gaps in ophthalmology residency programs: the link between practice, training and confidence in ocular examination and gonioscopy for diagnosing glaucoma, a blinding disease. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05665-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item on clinical encounters with acute angle closure after dilation; the article additionally reports the group contrast as n = 1 (4.3%) for glaucoma specialists versus n = 45 (39.8%) for non-specialists, p < 0.001.

> "Over one-third (n = 48, 35.3%) of the respondents had encountered a patient of theirs who presented with AACG following pupil dilation, which might have been prevented if following a gonioscopic assessment it had been known that the patient was at risk."
