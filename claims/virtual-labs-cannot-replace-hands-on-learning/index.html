Evidence¶

Owolabi Joshua O. 2025¶

Owolabi Joshua O., Gardner Katresa, Agboola Rasheed, Yesudas Rekha R., Shaw Jennifer H. (2025). Use of simulation for teaching biomedical sciences to undergraduate medical students- a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07819-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, from a Chinese study of first-year medical students in an experimental lab skills course, that "virtual labs reportedly contribute to increased familiarity and confidence, they did not satisfactorily replace hands-on experimental learning". No effect size printed.

"However, while virtual labs reportedly contribute to increased familiarity and confidence, they did not satisfactorily replace hands-on experimental learning."
