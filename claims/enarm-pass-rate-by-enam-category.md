Flores-Cohaila Javier A., Miranda-Chavez Brayan, Copaja-Corzo Cesar, Benavente-Chalco Xiomara C., Rios-García Wagner, Moreno-Ccama Vanessa P., Samanez-Obeso Angel, Rivarola-Hidalgo Marco. (2025). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive category analysis of 28,872 applicants reported in Table 1 of the repeated cross-sectional study; pass was defined as ENARM score ≥ 11 using the ENAM standard setting. The overall mean ENARM score was 11.69 (SD 2.33) with a 61.6% pass rate.

> "the ones with the highest mean were those with scores above 15 in the ENAM (pass rate: 99.2%), followed by those with scores between 13 and 15 (pass rate: 90.1%), and those who received honors in medical school (pass rate: 82.5%)."
