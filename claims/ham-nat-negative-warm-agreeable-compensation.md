## Evidence

### Mielke I 2025

Mielke I, Hampe W, Ehrhardt M, Knorr M. (2025). Spoilt for Choice in Undergraduate Medical Admission: Selection of Confident and Considerate Students Using Professional Prequalification and Situational Judgement Test. Perspectives on Medical Education 14(1). https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1571

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the regression finding that HAM-Nat was negatively related to warm-agreeable ratings (β = –0.18, 95% CI [–0.33; –0.03], p = 0.022) while HAM-SJT was positively related, in the same models on 210 students.

> "An SJT also has the potential to compensate for negative selection effects of cognitive admission tests on the communal characteristics of applicants as shown by the opposite results of the HAM-SJT and HAM-Nat."
