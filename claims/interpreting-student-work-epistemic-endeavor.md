## Evidence

### Fives 2017

Fives, Helenrose, Barnes, Nicole, Buehl, Michelle, Mascadri, Julia, & Ziegler, Nathan. (2017). Teachers’ epistemic cognition in classroom assessment. Educational Psychologist, 52(4), pp. 270-283. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2017.1323218

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the Dr. Jones example on interpretation: reviewing essays or performance assessments requires revisiting epistemic aims, judging accuracy, depth of explanations, and soundness of justifications, and comparing students' presentations to the teacher's own epistemic ideals to assign grades and devise feedback.

> "This is inherently an epistemic endeavor for teachers as they engage in evaluating the knowledge representation of others."
