## Evidence

### Jansen et al. 2020

Malte Jansen, Oliver Lüdtke, and Alexander Robitzsch. (2020). Disentangling Different Sources of Stability and Change in Students' Academic Self-Concepts: An Integrative Data Analysis Using the STARTS Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000448

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled STARTS model estimates across 11 longitudinal studies (coordinated IDA of published covariance matrices, more than 20,000 students) show a "stable, completely unchanging component underlying the domain-specific ASC" across primary and secondary school.

> "The stable trait variance accounted for 26% (pooled estimate) of the total variance in mathematics and 25% in the verbal domain."
