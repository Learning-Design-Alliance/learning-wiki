## Evidence

### van Rijt 2022

van Rijt, Jimmy; Myhill, Debra; De Maeyer, Sven; Coppen, Peter-Arno. (2022). Linguistic metaconcepts can improve grammatical understanding in L1 education: evidence from a Dutch quasi-experimental study. PLoS ONE 17(2): e0263123. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263123

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian multivariate analysis of the No Concept Score (times students chose the everyday-language alternative) found post-intervention means systematically lower for both groups; posterior effect-size distributions (Fig 3, panel B) indicated "medium and large" with higher probability for large effects in Group 1.

> "For the No concept score we learn that the intervention has resulted in lower scores. The 95% most credible estimates of effect of the intervention for Group 1 are situated between -1.97 and -0.90 and for Group 2 between -1.54 and -0.76."
