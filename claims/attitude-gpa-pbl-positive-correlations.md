## Evidence

### Budakoğlu Işıl İrem 2023

Budakoğlu Işıl İrem, Coşkun Özlem, Özeke Vildan. (2023). e-PBL with multimedia animations: a design-based research. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04298-x

`q3 · i2` · `design · r3`

Pearson correlation analysis (N = 91; e-PBL: 60, f2f: 31) of attitude scores, GPA, and PBL scores. All three printed correlations are small-to-moderate (r = .231, .211, .247) and statistically significant at p < .05.

> "There were positive, statistically significant relationships between the attitude scores, GPA, and PBL scores (attitude-GPA: r = .231; p < .05), (attitude-PBL: r = .211; p < .05), (PBL-GPA: r = .247; p < .05)"
