Keselman 1998¶

Keselman, H.J., Huberty, C.J., Lix, L.M., Olejnik, S., Cribbie, R.A., Donahue, B., Kowalchuk, R.K., Lowman, L.L., Petoskey, M.D., Keselman, J.C., & Levin, J.R. (1998). Statistical Practises of Educational Researchers: An Analysis of Their ANOVA, MANOVA and ANCOVA Analyses. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170601

q2 · i? · review · r2

Content analysis of 61 between-subjects univariate articles. The review reports "The issue of power and/or effect size calcul ations arose in only 10 articles (16.1%)." Effect sizes were calculated in six of these, but the statistic used was not routinely reported, and main effects were more often of interest than interactions.

"The issue of power and/or effect size calcul ations arose in only 10 articles (16.1%)."

Keselman 1998 (2)¶

Keselman, H.J., Huberty, C.J., Lix, L.M., Olejnik, S., Cribbie, R.A., Donahue, B., Kowalchuk, R.K., Lowman, L.L., Petoskey, M.D., Keselman, J.C., & Levin, J.R. (1998). Statistical Practises of Educational Researchers: An Analysis of Their ANOVA, MANOVA and ANCOVA Analyses. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170601

q2 · i? · review · r2

Content analysis of 79 MANOVA articles. The review reports "Effect size index values were reported in onl y eight of the 79 articles." Seven studies used univariate indexes and one reported multivariate eta-squared values; an F value was usually reported without degrees of freedom or the test criterion.

"Effect size index values were reported in onl y eight of the 79 articles."
