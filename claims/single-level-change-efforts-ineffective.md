### Joel C. Corbo 2014

Joel C. Corbo, Daniel L. Reinholz, Melissa H. Dancy, Stanley Deetz, and Noah Finkelstein. (2014). A Framework for Transforming Departmental Culture to Support Educational Innovation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1412.3034](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1412.3034)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article reports, citing the meta-analysis, that the four categories are disseminating curriculum and pedagogy (30.4%), developing reflective teachers (33.5%), enacting policy (27.7%), and developing shared vision (8.4%), and that "85.3% of the articles analyzed fit into a single category." The review concluded single-category efforts are "clearly not effective" in isolation.

> "In a 191-article meta-analysis of change in STEM education [10], the authors found that efforts fit cleanly into a four-category typology."
