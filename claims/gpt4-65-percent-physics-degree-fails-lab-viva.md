### Pimbblet 2024

Pimbblet, K.A., & Morrell, L.J. (2024). Can ChatGPT pass a physics degree? Making a case for reformation of assessment of undergraduate degrees. European Journal of Physics. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad9874](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad9874)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors applied GPT-4 to all summative assessments of the University of Hull BSc (Hons) Physics degree using a 'maximal intelligent cheating' approach, marking outputs against published criteria. They report "GPT-4 obtains a final rounded grade of 65%" but note it "cannot pass the degree due to compulsory laboratory elements."

> "Overall then, GPT-4 obtains a final rounded grade of 65%. In UK terms, this equates to a 2.1 degree (upper second class honours)"
