Contreras-Espinosa 2022¶

Contreras-Espinosa, R.S.; Eguia-Gomez, J.L. (2022). Game Jams as Valuable Tools for the Development of 21st-Century Skills. Sustainability 2022, 14, 2246. https://doi.org/10.3390/su14042246

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Post-event survey and interview data from the three jams. The authors had used a pre-event survey based on Zook and Riedl to classify skills, but "a more detailed screening of participant skills would be necessary", including software proficiency level.

"Participants indicated that a more detailed skill selection in jammers could have benefited the teams."
