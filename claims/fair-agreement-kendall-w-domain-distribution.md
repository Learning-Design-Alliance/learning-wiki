### Paakkari 2024

Paakkari, O., Kulmala, M., Lyyra, N., Saaranen, T., Lindfors, P., Tyrväinen, H. (2024). The core competencies of a health education teacher. Health Promotion International. https://doi.org/10.1093/heapro/daae078

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Concordance analysis of the third-round rankings (n = 24). The article reports "fair according to Kendall's W (0.22; Χ²(39) = 205.86; p < 0.001)". W is an agreement statistic, not an effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Overall, the agreement between experts in the third round could be regarded as fair according to Kendall's W (0.22; Χ²(39) = 205.86; p < 0.001)."

### Paakkari 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive distribution of the 15 top-ranked competencies across domains, shown in Figure 1 and reported in the Results text: "Most of the important competencies (n = 8) belonged to the domain of Pedagogic and subject-specific didactic competence". Counts are descriptive; no effect sizes.

> "Most of the important competencies (n = 8) belonged to the domain of Pedagogic and subject-specific didactic competence.
