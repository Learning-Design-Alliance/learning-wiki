## Evidence

### Kroesbergen 2023

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic unconditional model of 513 working-memory effect sizes from 111 studies. The pooled MLD deficit was "Hedges's g = 0.68", p < .001; trim-and-fill adjusted it to g = 0.61.

> "On average, there were significant differences of a medium effect size between groups, Hedges's g = 0.68, p < .001."

### Kroesbergen 2023 (2)

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic unconditional model of 69 RAN effect sizes from 29 studies. The pooled MLD deficit was "Hedges's g = 0.70", p < .001; trim-and-fill adjusted it to g = 0.59. The authors caution these results should be interpreted with extreme caution due to small degrees of freedom.

> "The unconditional model returned a significant medium effect size of Hedges's g = 0.70, p < .001."
