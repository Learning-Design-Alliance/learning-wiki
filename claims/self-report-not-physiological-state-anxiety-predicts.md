# Only self-reported, not physiological, indices of math-related state anxiety correlate with both math anxiety and math performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Self-reported state anxiety (anticipatory and post-task) is associated with both math anxiety and math performance, while HR, SCL, and PEP are not. [→ Daker 2023](#daker-2023)

## Evidence

### Daker 2023

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Necka, E.A., Green, A.E. & Lyons, I.M. (2023). Does anxiety explain why math-anxious people underperform in math? npj Science of Learning, 8:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00156-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational screening of multiple state anxiety indices with Bonferroni correction (alpha 0.003), controlling for general anxiety, word task performance, and corresponding word-block indices. Anticipatory self-report: printed effect sizes "rpartial(79) = 0.440" with math anxiety and "rpartial(79) = −0.459" with performance. None of the biological indices were significant (all ps > 0.05).

> "Results shown in Fig. 1 indicate that of all the indices of state anxiety we tested, only participants' subjective feelings of in-the-moment anxiety associated with math were related to both math anxiety and math performance."
