# Latino students' access to novice and uncertified teachers in Louisiana is mixed relative to non-Latino peers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 30% of Latino students versus 24% of non-Latino students attend schools with high (≥20%) percentages of novice teachers, but Latino students are slightly less likely to be in schools with high (≥10%) uncertified-teacher percentages (21% vs 23%). ## Evidence

### Educator Diversity State Profile: Louisiana 2022

Educator Diversity State Profile: Louisiana. (2022). EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED622849

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The brief's Louisiana disparity table for Latino versus non-Latino students shows "Percent of Students in schools with High (≥20%) percentages of Novice Teachers  30% 24%" but reverses direction for uncertified teachers (21% vs 23%). Schools serving the most Latino students had 15% novice teachers versus 20% in schools serving the fewest.
