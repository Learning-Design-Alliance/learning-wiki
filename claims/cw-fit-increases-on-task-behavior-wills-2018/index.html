wills-2018-ontask¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, May). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams (CW-FIT). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/729

q4 · i2 · causal · r3

Multisite randomized replication trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards Without Reservations, with 324 students in K to 6 across 21 schools in Missouri, Tennessee, and Utah, measured at multiple time points in late fall to early spring. On-task percentage averaged 80.00 (17.50) versus 58.80 (15.61), effect size 1.38, improvement index +42, statistically significant (p<0.01).
