# The experimental group's gains were not sustained: at post-test 2 its problem recognition was lower than both the control group and its own post-test 1, and problem-solving showed no significant change

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In post-test 2 the experimental group's problem recognition was lower than the control group's and lower than its own post-test 1, though the decline from post-test 1 to post-test 2 was not statistically significant (p = 0.7, d = 0.4 small effect). [→ Meintjes A 2015](#meintjes-a-2015)
`q2 i0` The experimental group's problem-solving showed no statistically significant change from post-test 1 to post-test 2 (p = 1.0), with d = 0.1 indicating no practically significant effect. [→ Meintjes A 2015 (2)](#meintjes-a-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Meintjes A 2015

Meintjes A, Henrico A & Kroon J. (2015). Teaching problem-solving competency in Business Studies at secondary school level. South African Journal of Education, 35(3), Art. #1102. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n3a1102

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Figure 1 trend for problem recognition across post-test 2; the article reports the Bonferroni p value was 0.7 between post-test 1 and post-test 2 and the d value of 0.4 indicates a small, insignificant decline.

> "However, in post -test 2, the experi -mental group’s achievement was  not only lower than the control group, but also lower than in post -test 1. This is an indication that the intervention should be continuously implemented and integrated with the curriculum, failing which, the effect might not be sustainable."

### Meintjes A 2015 (2)

Meintjes A, Henrico A & Kroon J. (2015). Teaching problem-solving competency in Business Studies at secondary school level. South African Journal of Education, 35(3), Art. #1102. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n3a1102

ANOVA of the experimental group's problem-solving scores between post-test 1 (LS mean 2.31) and post-test 2 (LS mean 2.35); "The d value of 0.1 also indicates that the slight increase had no practically significant effect".

> "Table 5 also shows that the Bonferroni p value after the second intervention  was 1.0, indicating no statistically significant difference between post -test 1 and post-test 2 for the problem-solving scores of  the experimental group."
