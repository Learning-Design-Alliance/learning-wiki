`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same pre-post quiz comparison (n = 149) as the fundamental-question analysis, using the chi-square test on identical checkpoints. The article reports the correct rate "improved from 22.8% (34/149) in Quiz 1 to 55.7% (83/149) in Quiz 2".

> "For practical application questions, such as those involving the “application of the ventricular pressure-volume loop for cardiac function evaluation,” the correct rate improved from 22.8% (34/149) in Quiz 1 to 55.7% (83/149) in Quiz 2 (χ² = 33.79, P < 0.001)"
