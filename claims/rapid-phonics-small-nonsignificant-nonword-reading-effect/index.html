Evidence¶

King 2015¶

King, B., & Kasim, A. (2015). Rapid Phonics Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581293

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Sensitivity analysis of the individually randomised trial analysing participants by actual treatment received, necessitated by six swapped pupils. The printed effect size was g = 0.24; the intention-to-treat PhAB effect was g = 0.17 (-0.24, 0.58); raw-score analyses gave 0.27 and 0.37, all non-significant.

"There was a small effect size of 0.24 for the PhAB non-word reading scores after adjusting for the pre -test scores. This, however, failed to reach statistical significance as the lower confidence interval crossed zero"
