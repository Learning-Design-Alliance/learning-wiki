`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

State anxiety measured immediately before each simulation with the STAI-S; the mean was "48.93 ± 10.75", with 68 respondents (63.6%) in the moderate range and 15 (13.9%) in the high range; Cronbach's alpha was 0.95.

> "The mean STAI-State (S-anxiety) score on simulation days was 48.93 ± 10.75. Among respondents, 24 (22.4%) scored below 40, indicating absent or low state anxiety; 68 (63.6%) scored between 41 and 60, consistent with moderate anxiety; and 15 (13.9%) scored between 61 and 73 (maximum possible score = 80), reflecting high levels of state anxiety."
