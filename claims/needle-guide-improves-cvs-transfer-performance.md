## Evidence

### Johnsson V 2026

Johnsson V, Petersen OB, Svendsen MBS, et al. (2026). To guide or not to guide: A randomized study on the use of needle guide for chorionic villus sampling training. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.70108

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial's linear mixed-effects model of transfer-test scores, a significant main effect of needle-guide use was found (F[1, 266.0] = 49.5, p < 0.001). Post hoc, "performance scores were significantly higher in transfer tests performed with a needle guide compared to the freehand technique" (mean difference 10.9 [7.8–13.9], p = 0.001).
