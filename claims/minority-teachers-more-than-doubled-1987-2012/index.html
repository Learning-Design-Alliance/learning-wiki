Ingersoll 2017¶

Ingersoll, R., May, H., & Collins, G. (2017). Minority teacher recruitment, employment, and retention: 1987 to 2013. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/minority-teacher-recruitment

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Trend analysis of NCES Schools and Staffing Survey data across seven survey cycles from 1987–88 to 2011–12, reported in Table 1 and Figure 1. The analysis found "the number of minority teachers increased by 104%" versus 38% for nonminority teachers, with minority teachers rising from about 325,000 to 666,000.

"While the number of nonminority teachers increased by 38%, the number of minority teachers increased by 104% (see Figure 1)—at about the same rate as the growth in the nation's minority population"
