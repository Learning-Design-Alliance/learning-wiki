### Innwoo Park and Michael J. Hannafin 1993

Innwoo Park and Michael J. Hannafin. (1993). Empirically-Based Guidelines for the Design of Interactive Multimedia. Educational Technology Research and Development, Vol. 41, No. 3, pp. 63-85. http://www.jstor.org/stable/30218388

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review asserts, citing Ausubel's cognitive-structure theory and schema research, that "Related prior knowledge is the single most powerful influence in mediating subsequent learning." When schemata are rich, learners relate and integrate new knowledge; when insufficient, little assists integration. The stated design implication is to "Layer information to accommodate multiple levels of complexity and accommodate differences in related prior knowledge."

> "Related prior knowledge is the single most powerful influence in mediating subsequent learning."
