`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student Likert-scale survey at QSYS 2023, a competitive two-week program, averaging ratings over 29 respondents across 11 hands-on lab activities on a four-point scale. The QKD lab scored 3.3 for interesting and engaging, 3.6 for lecture connection, and 3.5 for novelty.

> "The QKD lab was consistently rated highly by students, with students rating the statement "I found the experiment interesting and engaging" with a score of 3.3, "The experiment connected to and helped clarify the content from lectures" with a score of 3.6 (the highest of any lab activity in QSYS)"
