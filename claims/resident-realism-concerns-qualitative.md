Thematic analysis of open-ended resident feedback collected alongside the structured surveys. Residents "frequently requested additional preparatory materials, particularly for the PAFB session," and "Concerns regarding simulation realism were also noted, including limitations related to mannequin fidelity and variability in cadaver quality." No quantitative effect sizes accompany this qualitative result.
