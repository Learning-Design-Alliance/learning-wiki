## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students' Understanding of Quantum Measurement. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary evaluation with two equivalent test versions in different but equivalent classes at two similar universities; the experimental group included 6 students on Test A and 7 on Test B. The comparison class average "was 26% including both versions (Tests A and B)."

> "The class average in the comparison group with traditional lectures was 26% including both versions (Tests A and B)."

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students' Understanding of Quantum Measurement. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary evaluation of the experimental group after instruction with both concept tests and the measurement QuILT; the article reports their "average score including both versions after the QuILT was 91%", up from 68% after concept tests alone.

> "Then, the experimental group students worked on the QuILT in class and their average score including both versions after the QuILT was 91%."

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016 (3)

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students' Understanding of Quantum Measurement. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06926)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 1 in the preliminary evaluation lists percentage correct for Q0 and Q1-Q3 by group: comparison 37%, 35%, 10%, 5%; concept test 71%, 86%, 36%, 64%; QuILT 100%, 100%, 90%, 100%. Numbers of students answering each question are printed in parentheses in the table.

> "Comparison Concept test QuILT Q0 37% (15) 71% (6) 100% (7) Q1 35% (10) 86% (7) 100% (5) Q2 10% (10) 36% (7) 90% (5) Q3 5% (10) 64% (7) 100% (5)"
