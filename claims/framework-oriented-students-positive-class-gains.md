University of Central Arkansas, Department of Physics and Astronomy. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.009](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.009)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of pre-post CLASS survey data from 218 students in an introductory algebra-based physics course, reported by learning orientation. Framework-oriented students "experienced a non-trivial positive gain" while the other orientations slightly declined, within SE of zero gain. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Framework-oriented students experienced a non-trivial positive gain (SE = +/- 0.03 for all majors in each orientation). The other orientations each averaged a slight decline (albeit within SE of zero gain)"
