## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Johansson 2024

Johansson, A. M., Savi, A. O., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Hofman, A. D. (2024). Three Strikes and Who is Out? Individual Differences in Error-Induced Quitting. https://github.com/ann1ejohansson/three-strikes

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multi-State Survival Model fit to Prowise Learn gameplay data from 24,859 users (grades 3-8) spanning 2023-05-29 to 2023-07-30, replicating a prior study. The covariate model improved fit, "χ2 (44) = 1238246, p < .001". The soft-quit to hard-quit transition rate was 0.0284 versus 0.0227 from persisting.

> "The proportional hazards model revealed that sequential errors predict transitions into both soft-quit and hard-quit states (Figure 1)."
