## Evidence

### Erin Ronayne Sohr 2015

Erin Ronayne Sohr, Benjamin W. Dreyfus, Ayush Gupta, and Andrew Elby. (2015). “Because math”: Epistemological stance or defusing social tension in QM?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1507.00667](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1507.00667)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative discourse analysis of one episode in which five physics majors in a first-semester QM course work through the Particle in a Box tutorial; the authors read the group's “because math” joke as “epistemological humor” that relieved tension and repositioned math as a partial resource.

> "through the use of epistemological humor —humor about the types of knowledge they are drawing upon and constructing —the group transactionally constructs an epistemic stance that positions math as a primary, but not complete, intellectual resource."
