# Students given weekly access to the Rori AI math tutor showed substantially higher math growth scores than control students (d = 0.36, p < 0.001)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Treatment students' math growth scores were significantly higher than control students' (M = 5.13 vs M = 2.12), with an effect size of Cohen's d = 0.36. [→ Owen Henkel 2024](#owen-henkel-2024)

## Evidence

### Owen Henkel 2024

Owen Henkel, Hannah Horne-Robinson, Nessie Kozhakhmetova, Amanda Lee. (2024). Effective and Scalable Math Support: Experimental Evidence on the Impact of an AI- Math Tutor in Ghana. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_34](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_34)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

School-level randomized experiment in 11 Rising Academies schools in Ghana: 241 control and 236 treatment students in grades 3-8 completed baseline and endline assessments 8 months apart; growth scores were endline minus baseline raw scores. The "3.01 difference in growth scores was highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)"; pooled-SD effect size d = 0.36.

> "An independent samples t-test between the control (M = 2.12, SD = 6.30) and the treatment group (M = 5.13, SD = 7.03) revealed that the 3.01 difference in growth scores was highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
