## Evidence

### Mohammad Aliakbari and Ali Mansouri Nejad 2013

Mohammad Aliakbari and Ali Mansouri Nejad. (2013). On the Effectiveness of Team Teaching in Promoting Learners' Grammatical Proficiency. Canadian Journal of Education / Revue canadienne de l'éducation 36:3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057969

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest analysis of the same quasi-experimental study: a textbook-based proficiency pretest covering first-term material was administered to both classes. Table 1 prints T 0.727, DF 57, Sig (2-tailed) 0.470, mean difference –.878; the raw-score range (20 – 7 = 13) was also cited as indicating homogeneous competence. No effect size was reported.

> "Since the t observed (f = .727) is not significant at p < .05, it can be safely claimed that there was no significant difference between the control and experimental groups with regard to the general knowledge of English."
