## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Danny Doucette and Chandralekha Singh 2020

Danny Doucette and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Why are there so few women in physics? Reflections on the experiences of two women. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/1.5145518](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5145518)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study interview evidence: Paulette described male students pairing up amongst themselves, saying things and not being listened to, and deciding to withdraw from the honors lab rather than continue what she called mental stress.

> "after a certain point I didn't want to go to that class anymore, and it was, I'm just going to withdraw. I didn't want to deal with the mental stress of that."
