## Evidence

### Bukola 2026

Bukola, Sundstrom, Gambrell, Green, Traxler, and Brewe. (2026). Measuring fidelity of implementation of named active learning methods in physics. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/xcdt-txzh](https://doi.org/10.1103/xcdt-txzh)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of COPUS-coded classroom observations from one high-fidelity SCALE-UP site and seven broader SCALE-UP implementations, comparing the fraction of two-minute intervals spent on codes mapped to critical components. The study reports instructors "spend similar fractions of class time on all critical components", with student presentations (SP) a possible exception.

> "Between the high-fidelity and broader implementations of SCALE-UP, instructors spend similar fractions of class time on all critical components"
