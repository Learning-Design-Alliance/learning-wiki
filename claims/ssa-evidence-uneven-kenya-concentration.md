## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The evidence base is not evenly distributed across topics or countries: over 30% of studies come from Kenya alone, and topics relevant to the African context such as multi-grade or multi-shift teaching are not under rigorous study at all. [→ Katharine Conn 2014](#katharine-conn-2014)

Author's synthesis of the literature-search and coding stage of the 60-study review. The author reports "over 30% of the studies in this meta-analysis come from Kenya alone" and cautions program effects may differ by country; no effect size is attached to this distributional finding.
