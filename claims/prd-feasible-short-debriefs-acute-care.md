# Implementing a structured PRD tool is feasible in acute clinical areas, with debriefs averaging 14.2 minutes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 114 debriefings over 12 months, average debrief duration was 14.2 minutes, which the authors state reinforces that PRD is feasible and realistic for acute clinical areas; training and tool accessibility were also implemented feasibly. [→ Kam April J. 2022](#kam-april-j-2022)

## Evidence

### Kam April J. 2022

Kam April J., Gonsalves Clarelle L., Nordlund Samantha V., Hale Stephen J., Twiss Jennifer, Cupido Cynthia, Brar Mandeep, Parker Melissa J. (2022). Implementation and facilitation of post-resuscitation debriefing: a comparative crossover study of two post-resuscitation debriefing frameworks. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-022-00707-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Implementation data from the 12-month crossover study, in which training was delivered by nurse educators and tools were attached to code carts. The article states the "average time of all debriefings was quite short, lasting an average of 14.2 min, and reinforces that PRD is feasible".

> "Overall, the average time of all debriefings was quite short, lasting an average of 14.2 min, and reinforces that PRD is feasible and realistic for acute clinical areas."
