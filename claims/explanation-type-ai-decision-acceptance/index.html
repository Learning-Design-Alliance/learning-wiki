Papaneophytou 2025¶

Papaneophytou, C.; Nicolaou, S.A. (2025). Promoting Critical Thinking in Biological Sciences in the Era of Artificial Intelligence: The Role of Higher Education. Trends High. Educ. 2025, 4, 24. https://doi.org/10.3390/higheredu4020024

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of perceived legitimacy of algorithmic decisions, reporting the Shulner-Tal et al. [50] finding on explanation types. The review frames this as supporting explainable AI (XAI) in educational and scientific contexts.

"Their study found that the type of explanation matters: people were more accepting of AI decisions when explanations emphasized sensitivity analysis (how changes in input data affect output) or certification (expert endorsement or validation)."
