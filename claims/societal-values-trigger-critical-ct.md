## Evidence

### Marianthi Grizioti 2025

Marianthi Grizioti. (2025). Computational thinking beyond coding: exploring student computational practices while playing and modifying a socio-scientific simulation game with integrated computational tools. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10477-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Co-occurrence analysis of incidents coded against Salen and Zimmerman game elements, with the consequences-values code split into scientific and societal sub-codes. The article reports "societal values" triggered more disagreements and conversations leading to more modifications.

> "The analysis showed that the "scientific values" were less modified or discussed by the students in the modding and design stages. In contrast, the "societal values" triggered more disagreements and conversations which in turn led to more modifications"
