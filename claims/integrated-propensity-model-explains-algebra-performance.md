## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In one reviewed study (Jones & Byrnes, 2006), an overall model combining propensity factors and classroom factors significantly explained students' Algebra II test performance. [→ Silla 2026](#silla-2026)

## Evidence

### Silla 2026

Silla, E. M., Guba, T.-P., Rodrigues, A., Anisiobi, O. C., Scanniello, A., & Barbieri, C. A. (2026). A Systematic Review of Mathematical, Motivational, and Socioemotional Propensities Related to Algebraic Thinking. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10111-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports, citing Jones and Byrnes (2006), that an O-P-model-based analysis including mathematical and motivational/socioemotional propensities plus classroom factors significantly explained Algebra II test performance; no effect size is printed.

> "This overall model significantly explained students' performance on Algebra II tests, with propensity factors explaining a significant amount of variance in the model."
