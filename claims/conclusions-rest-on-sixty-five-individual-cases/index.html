Evidence¶

Carrie Bansom Squire 1910¶

Carrie Bansom Squire. (1910). Graded Mental Tests. Part III. Judgment, Conclusions and Summary of Results. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Graded+Mental+Tests+Squire

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The article's stated limitation: with "only eighty children in all, ten for each group", and fifteen retarded, conclusions rest on sixty-five individually tested cases, which the author argues are more reliable than a larger group-tested sample.

"The number of children tested was too small to be made the basis of sweeping generalizations. There were only eighty children in all, ten for each group; of these eighty, fifteen were found upon closer examination of all their school records to belong to the retarded class. So the con- clusions are drawn from sixty-five cases."
