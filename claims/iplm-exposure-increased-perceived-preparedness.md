## Evidence

### Dabbagh 2010

Dabbagh, N., & Beattie, M. (2010). Student and Instructor Perceptions of the Usefulness of Computer-Based Microworlds in Supporting the Teaching and Assessment of Computer Networking Skills: An Exploratory Study. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076143

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental survey study with 72 community college IT students across three intact-class groups. Students exposed to the IPLM reported significantly higher preparedness (M = 4.5) than those taught traditionally (M = 2.8), with t = 6.30 at the 95% confidence level.

> "Mean values on the two survey items that specifically addressed this research question in each of the student surveys (items 1&3 from iPS1 and items 1&6 from iPS3, see appendix) were 3.6 for the iPLM groups and 2.9 for group one; and 4.5 for the iPLM groups and 2.8 for group one respectively"
