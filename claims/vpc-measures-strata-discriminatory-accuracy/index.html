q2 · i? · review · r2

Definitional/interpretive statement in the exposition of the simple intersectional (null) model. The VPC is the proportion of total outcome variance located at the strata level and serves as a global measure of intersectionality and discriminatory accuracy.

"A high VPC indicates that intersectional strata are useful for understanding individual differences in, for example, students' reading achievement. By contrast, a VPC of 0 would indicate that intersectional strata resemble random samples from the student body"
