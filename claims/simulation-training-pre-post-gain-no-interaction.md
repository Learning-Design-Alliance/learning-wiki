## Evidence

### Tolsgaard MG 2015

Tolsgaard MG, Madsen ME, Ringsted C, Oxlund BS, Oldenburg A, Sorensen JL, Ottesen B, Tabor A. (2015). The effect of dyad versus individual simulation‐based ultrasound training on skills transfer. Medical Education 49: 286–295. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.12624

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design (2 × 2) repeated-measures ANOVA on pre- and post-test OSAUS scores in the randomised trial. The article reports a significant main effect of training (d.f.(1,28) = 509.933, p < 0.01) with "large effect sizes (Cohen's d = 3.85)".

> "There was a significant main effect of simulation‐based training in terms of performance improvements from pre‐ to post‐test (d.f.(1,28) = 509.933, p < 0.01), with large effect sizes (Cohen's d = 3.85)."
