## Evidence

### Newman 2016

Newman, D., Lazarev, V., & Zacamy, J. (2016). Comparing a Program Implemented Under the Constraints of an RCT and in the Wild. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567633

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey comparison of teacher and principal responses across the two contexts; Table 3 prints Confidence level 3.65 (Scale-up) vs 3.64 (RCT), p = 0.97. The positive-outcome interpretation is the authors' inference from the confidence-outcome association, not a direct outcome comparison.

> "RCT and Scale-up groups of schools do not differ on that characteristic (Table 3, Appendix B) suggesting that the program implemented in the wild has the same potential to affect student outcomes positively."
