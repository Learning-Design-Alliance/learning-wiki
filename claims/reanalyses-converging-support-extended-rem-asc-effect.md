### Fernando Núñez-Regueiro 2025

Fernando Núñez-Regueiro, Herbert W Marsh, Reinhard Pekrun, Oliver Lüdtke, Jiesi Guo. (2025). The Reciprocal Effects Model is Robust to Alternative Modeling Specifications: A Response to Sorjonen et al., 2025. Educational Psychology Review, 37 (3), pp.79. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10059-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The authors reanalyzed two data sources: the simulated data constructed by Sorjonen et al. and the original PALMA dataset used in Marsh et al. (2024), applying their comparative modeling framework to both. The article reports that "our reanalyses provide converging support for the extended REM and, in particular, for an effect of academic self-concept on achievement."

> "Using this comparative framework, we reanalyzed both the simulated data constructed by Sorjonen et al. and the original PALMA dataset used in Marsh et al. (2024)."
