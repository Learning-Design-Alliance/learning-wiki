## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2011

Trevor I. Smith, John R. Thompson, Donald B. Mountcastle. (2011). Student Understanding of Taylor Series Expansions in Statistical Mechanics. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1112.4231](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1112.4231)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The authors combine the written Taylor series pretest data with Year 1 teaching interview data on generating the entropy expansion. They state this combined evidence shows interpretation and application of a given series do not imply generation in a novel context.

> "students may be able to interpret and apply a Taylor series that is given to them, but not be able to generate an appropriate expansion in a novel context."
