# Turn-level dialogue codes cluster into three components: Elaborated, Reasoned and Non-Dialogic

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Principal components analysis of turn-code frequencies across 72 classes yielded a strong three-cluster solution (Elaborated, Reasoned, Non-Dialogic) explaining 83% of the variance. [→ Howe 2018](#howe-2018)

## Evidence

### Howe 2018

Howe, C., Hennessy, S., Mercer, N., Vrikki, M., & Wheatley, L. (2018). Teacher-Student Dialogue during Classroom Teaching: Does it Really Impact upon Student Outcomes? To appear in The Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=ES/M007103/1

`q2 · i?`

Principal components analysis (Oblimin rotation, Kaiser normalization) of turn-code frequencies across the 72 classrooms, reported in Data preparation. The six ubiquitous codes fell into Elaborated (ELI .92, EL .94), Reasoned (REI .90, RE .94) and Non-Dialogic (OI .89, UC .82) clusters, with "83% of the variance explained".

> "principal components analysis4 of the frequencies (Oblimin rotation with Kaiser normalization; 83% of the variance explained) indicated that these six codes fell into three clusters"
