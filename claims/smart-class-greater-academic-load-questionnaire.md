# Students in the Smart Class teaching module reported a significantly greater academic load than students in the Traditional Class teaching module

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On the questionnaire's academic-load category, Smart Class students reported a significantly greater load (2.64±0.54 vs 3.02±0.65, P<0.001), including extra burden (Question 15, P<0.001) and difficulty of preview and review (Question 16, P=0.003). [→ Lin Qiang 2021](#lin-qiang-2021)

## Evidence

### Lin Qiang 2021

Lin Qiang, Qiu Yaxian, Liang Junjie, Zheng Yuxin, Liao Yujun, Huang Huina, Hou Lingying, Lang Shijuan, Zhao Biyi, Yiming Zulipiya, Yan Qinghua, Ou Haining, Zhang Yanni. (2021). The Smart Class Teaching Module for Rehabilitation Medicine English Education in China. Medical Science Monitor. https://doi.org/10.12659/MSM.929834

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subjective online questionnaire (5-point Likert items, 210 valid responses) administered one week after the course. The academic-load category differed significantly between modules, as did Question 15 (2.82±0.83 vs 3.35±0.86, P<0.001) and Question 16 (2.50±0.78 vs 2.84±0.82, P=0.003).

> "The Smart Class teaching module group reported a greater academic load than the Traditional Class teaching module group (Smart Class teaching module: 2.64±0.54, Traditional Class teaching module: 3.02±0.65; P<0.001)."
