## Evidence

### Lees-Murdock D. J. 2024

Lees-Murdock D. J., Khan D., Irwin R., Graham J., Hinch V., O'Hagan B., McClean S. (2024). Assessing the Efficacy of Active Learning to Support Student Performance Across Undergraduate Programmes in Biomedical Science. British Journal of Biomedical Science. https://doi.org/10.3389/bjbs.2024.12148

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of assessment marks for the post-Smartwork cohorts (n = 403 across 2021–22 and 2022–23), stratified by Smartwork engagement level and shown in Figure 5. Fully-engaged students showed "significant (p < 0.5 to p < 0.001) increment in marks for all aspects of assessment"; no effect size is printed.

> "Fully-engaged students showed significant (p < 0.5 to p < 0.001) increment in marks for all aspects of assessment including class test, exam, coursework, and overall module in two academic years (2021–22 to 2022–23)."
