Systematic review of 45 observational studies, most cross-sectional, synthesized under PRISMA 2020 with Newcastle–Ottawa quality assessment. The review reports that "stress, anxiety, and depressive symptoms showed predominantly negative associations with academic performance" in 67–80% of relevant articles; no pooled effect size is printed.
