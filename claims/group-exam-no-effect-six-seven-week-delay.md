## Evidence

### Joss Ives 2014

Joss Ives. (2014). Measuring the Learning from Two-Stage Collaborative Group Exams. Dept. of Physics & Astronomy, University of British Columbia. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.027](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.027)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects logistic regression on diagnostic-test responses of students in an introductory calculus-based physics course (first midterm, n = 679), with random intercepts for student ability and condition group. The model "correctly predicted 72% of the cases" and treatment was not significant, "p(β3) = .40".

> "It was found that treatment had no statistically significant predictive power for the diagnostic test questions associated with midterm one, p(β3) = .40."
