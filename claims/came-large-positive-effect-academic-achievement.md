## Evidence

### Demir 2014

Demir, S., & Başol, G. (2014). Effectiveness of Computer-Assisted Mathematics Education (CAME) over Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis Study. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(5), 2026-2035. http://www.edam.com.tr/estp

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 40 experimental and quasi-experimental studies from Turkey using the random effect model, with a .1032 standard deviation and a 95% confidence interval from .6687 to 1.1311. The article reports "the average effect size was calculated as ES = .8999". The confidence interval does not contain zero.

> "the average effect size was calculated as ES = .8999. This means that CAME raises academic achievement in mathematics by .90 standard deviations."
