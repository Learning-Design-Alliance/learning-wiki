The author argues foundational questions such as "What constitutes a motive" and how motives relate and can be developed in educational procedure cannot yet be readily answered, naming Woodworth's Dynamic Psychology as a stimulating contribution.

> "Much of the current discussion of motivation in education is either superficial or mystical. What constitutes a motive, how are motives related to each other, how can motives be developed and utilized in educational procedure are questions that cannot be readily answered. An adequate psychology of motivation is yet to be written."
