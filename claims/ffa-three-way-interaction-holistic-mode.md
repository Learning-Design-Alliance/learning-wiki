## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kok Ellen M. 2021

Kok Ellen M., Sorger Bettina, van Geel Koos, Gegenfurtner Andreas, van Merriënboer Jeroen J. G., Robben Simon G. F., de Bruin Anique B. H. (2021). Holistic processing only? The role of the right fusiform face area in radiological expertise. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256849

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory 2×2×2 ROI-based ANOVA (expertise level, trial length, left/right FFA location) on beta values from the diagnostic-reasoning runs. The article reports "F(1,26) = 4.395, p = .046, η2p = 0.145", described as tentative support that the right FFA responds more in the holistic mode.

> "The three-way interaction between expertise level, trial length and location was significant, F(1,26) = 4.395, p = .046, η2p = 0.145 (medium effect size)."
