Question 4 of the 72-response website questionnaire asked respondents to describe what is known about a media-announced 'Earth 2.0'. "High school stu- dents did the poorest here", while the majority of scientists and non-scientists selected the correct statement that only radius or minimum mass is known. No effect sizes are printed.
