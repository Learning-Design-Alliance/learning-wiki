Evidence¶

Abdulkadir Kırbas 2018¶

Abdulkadir Kırbas. (2018). The Effect of Interactive Whiteboard Applications Supported by Visual Materials on Middle School Students' Listening Comprehension and Persistence of Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(11): 2552-2561. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061120

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline comparison in the same pre-test post-test control group experiment (n=36 per group). The independent samples t test on pre-test LAT scores yielded t = -1.181, p = .241; the article reports "no significant difference between the groups at .05 significance level". Equivalence was not framed as a formal equivalence test.

"The mean LAT score of the students in the control group was 53.33 and that of the students in the experimental group was 51.25. The t value calculated based on the scores of the two groups was -1.181. The independent samples t test that was carried out to determine whether there was any difference between the groups yielded p=.241, which indicated that there was no significant difference between the groups at .05 significance level."
