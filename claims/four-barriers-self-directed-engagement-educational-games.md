## Evidence

### Angela Y. He 2017

Angela Y. He. (2017). Overcoming Barriers to Self-Directed Student Engagement with Educational Games: A Mixed-Methods Case Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative case study analysis using NVivo coding: an initial open-coding round yielded "49 total descriptive codes" and iterative category consolidation, through which the four barriers emerged in the stated frequency order. Sample was ten high school students (4 males, 6 females) randomly assigned to two game-version groups.

> "Employing the within-case and cross-case approach and triangulation of data, four educational-game specific barriers emerged (from most common to least common): 1) negative perceptions of educational games, 2) incompatible audience, 3) incompatible difficulty, and 4) price."
