# In Experiment 1, reminders speeded and interference slowed recognition response times, with reminder+interference faster than interference alone

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i1`–`i3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2022 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Recognition response times were faster for reminded pairs than non-reminded pairs (main effect of reminder, η² = 0.32). [→ Michael Batashvili 2022](#michael-batashvili-2022)
`q2 i1` Recognition response times were slower for interfered pairs than non-interfered pairs (main effect of interference, η² = 0.01). [→ Michael Batashvili 2022 (2)](#michael-batashvili-2022-2)
`q2 i2` Correct associative recognition was significantly faster in the reminder+interference condition than in the interference-alone condition, opposite to the reconsolidation-interference expectation. [→ Michael Batashvili 2022 (3)](#michael-batashvili-2022-3)

## Evidence

### Michael Batashvili 2022

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A repeated-measures ANOVA on response times in Experiment 1 (40 participants) showed a significant reminder main effect with Mean = 3055 ms for reminders versus 3919 ms without; the article prints "η² = 0.32" for this effect.

> "For RT, there was a signi ﬁcant main effect of reminder, F(1,39) = 28.95, p < 0.001, η² = 0.32, BF 10 = 1.00, showing that participants had faster reaction time for pairs where a reminder was employed"

### Michael Batashvili 2022 (2)

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

The same response-time ANOVA in Experiment 1 (40 participants) showed a significant interference main effect, with Mean = 3572 ms under interference versus 3402 ms without; the article prints "η² = 0.01".

> "There was also a signi ﬁcant main effect of interference, F(1,39) = 4.455, p = 0.04, η² = 0.01, BF 10 < 0.001, showing that participants had a slower reaction time for pairs when an interference was employed"

### Michael Batashvili 2022 (3)

Michael Batashvili, Rona Sheaffer, Maya Katz, Yoav Doron, Noam Kempler and Daniel A. Levy. (2022). Behavioural reconsolidation interference not observed in a within-subjects design. npj Science of Learning 7:26. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00143-w

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc paired samples t-tests on response times (Table 2) in Experiment 1 (40 participants): Interference–Reminder+Interference t(39) = 4.75, p < 0.001, d = 0.55, significant after Bonferroni correction.

> "the comparisons indicated that correct associative recognition of stimuli in the reminder + interference condition was signi ﬁcantly faster than in the interference-alone condition, the opposite of what would be expected if reminders engender reconsolidation interference."
