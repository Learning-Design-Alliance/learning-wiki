## Evidence

### Önal 2024

Önal, A. & Seren İntepeler, Ş. (2024). Comparison of self-competency and executive (charge) nurse competency assessments of clinical nurses. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.12955

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study at a university hospital comparing 162 service nurses' self-assessments with charge nurses' assessments on the Nurse Competence Scale VAS. The article reports "The nurses' total VAS score was 85.81 ± 10.70" versus 82.40 ± 15.48 for managers, with p = 0.022. No effect size is printed for this contrast in the quoted text.

> "There was a statistically significant difference between the mean VAS scores (p = 0.022). The nurses' total VAS score was 85.81 ± 10.70, and the nurse managers' total VAS score was 82.40 ± 15.48."
