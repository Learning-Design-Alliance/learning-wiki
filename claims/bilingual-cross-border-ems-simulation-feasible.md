## Evidence

### Ruebsam Marie-Luise 2023

Ruebsam Marie-Luise, Orsson Dorota, Metelmann Bibiana, Orsson Jakub, Hahnenkamp Klaus, Metelmann Camilla. (2023). Cross-border simulation training for German and Polish emergency medical teams is feasible: conception and evaluation of a bilingual simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04823-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility report of the INTERREG-VA InGRiP project: planned five-day mixed-group simulation training was curtailed by the pandemic; the authors state "we could still show, that a cross-border simulation training for German and Polish emergency medical teams is feasible." No effect size reported.

> "With increasing COVID-19 incidences in March 2020, the integrated CB training had to be stopped after three out of nine training modules."
