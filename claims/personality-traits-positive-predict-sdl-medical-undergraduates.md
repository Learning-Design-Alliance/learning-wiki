### Li Chenrui 2026

Li Chenrui, Hu Fan, Qiao Yongxia, Zhang Meihui, Gao Quyige, Wang Fengyi, Du Naiya, Xia Yunyi, Pan Meizhu, Cai Yong, Wang Ying. (2026). The role of AI-mediated learning contexts in the personality–self-directed learning relationship among Chinese medical undergraduates: a cross-sectional study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

General linear model in a cross-sectional survey of 1,208 medical undergraduates controlling for demographic, socioeconomic, and academic covariates. Conscientiousness showed "the strongest association with SDL, with a large effect (β = 2.76" and η²p = 0.16; self-esteem and openness showed small effects; self-control was weakly associated (β = 0.13, p = 0.036, η²p = 0.004).

> "Conscientiousness showed the strongest association with SDL, with a large effect (β = 2.76, 95% CI [2.39, 3.12], p < 0.001, η²p = 0.16). Self-esteem (β = 0.70, 95% CI [0.50, 0.91], p < 0.001, η²p = 0.04) and openness (β = 0.70, 95% CI [0.35, 1.05], p < 0.001, η²p = 0.01) were also positively associated with SDL"
