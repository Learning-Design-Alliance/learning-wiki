## Evidence

### Benjamin P. Koester 2016

Benjamin P. Koester, Galina Grom, and Timothy A. McKay. (2016). Patterns of Gendered Performance Difference in Introductory STEM Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of student record data across 116 large University of Michigan courses (627,998 student/course pairs, Fall 2008-Winter 2015), comparing course grades to GPAO. The article reports STEM lecture grade penalties "as large as -0.62 and averaging -0.22 letter grades"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "At Michigan, these are as large as -0.62 and averaging -0.22 letter grades (typical standard error < 0.01)."
