# Jigsaw cooperative learning produced higher achievement than traditional teaching in the 7th-grade unit "Science within Time"

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yapıcı 2016

Hakkı Yapıcı. (2016). Use of jigsaw technique to teach the unit "science within time" in secondary 7th grade social sciences course and students' views on this technique. Educational Research and Reviews, 11(8), 773-780. https://www.academicjournals.org/ERR

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test control group quasi-experiment with 53 7th graders (experimental n=25, control n=28) taught the unit for five weeks. The article reports a significant post-test difference favoring jigsaw, with means "XExperimental = 73,60; XControl = 51,28"; no effect size is printed.

> "there is a significant difference in the mean scores of the control and experimental group's swtAT post -test. t (51) = 9 ,76; p<0,05"

### Er 2017

Harun Er. (2017). The Impact of Teaching the Subjects under "Science in Time" Unit in the Social Studies Class in the 7th Grade Using Jigsaw Technique on the Academic Success of the Students. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(5): 838-847. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050516

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with 23 students per group in a Bartın public secondary school; the jigsaw group's final-test mean exceeded the control group's and the independent t test showed "the difference was meaningful in favor of the experiment gro up". No effect size is printed.

> "The result of the independent t test which was administered in order to determine whether or not the data regarding the final test of the experiment and control groups were meaningful showed that the difference was meaningful in favor of the experiment gro up (t= 41.318, p(.000)<.05)."
