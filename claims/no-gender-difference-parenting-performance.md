## Evidence

### Ali Nayab 2023

Ali Nayab, Ullah Asad, Khan Abdul Majid, Khan Yunas, Ali Sajid, Khan Aisha, Bakhtawar, Khan Asad, Din Maaz Ud, Ullah Rahat, Khan Umar Niaz, Aziz Tariq, Ahmad Mushtaq. (2023). Academic performance of children in relation to gender, parenting styles, and socioeconomic status: What attributes are important. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kendall's Tau-c analysis controlling for gender in the 448-student sample. Associations were positive and significant for both "males" and "female respondents", with Tc = 0.129 (P = 0.002) and Tc = 0.139 (P = 0.008); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Results in Table 5 showed that the influence of parenting style on the academic performance of children in the context of respondents’ gender was positive (Tc = 0.129) and significant association (P = 0.002) in the above-mentioned variables for males. The association of the above-said variables was also positive (Tc = 0.139) and significant (P = 0.008) for female respondents."

### Ali Nayab 2023 (2)

Ali Nayab, Ullah Asad, Khan Abdul Majid, Khan Yunas, Ali Sajid, Khan Aisha, Bakhtawar, Khan Asad, Din Maaz Ud, Ullah Rahat, Khan Umar Niaz, Aziz Tariq, Ahmad Mushtaq. (2023). Academic performance of children in relation to gender, parenting styles, and socioeconomic status: What attributes are important. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' interpretation of the gender-controlled analysis in the same sample: parenting style "emerged as a universal factor" regardless of gender. This is the authors' stated reading of similar Tc values across genders, not a separate formal equivalence test.

> "The similar Tc chi-square values indicate that the effects of parenting style on the academic performance of children were almost identical for both genders. Therefore, parenting style emerged as a universal factor in influencing the academic performance of children irrespective of the gender of the respondents."
