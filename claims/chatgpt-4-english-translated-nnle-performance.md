# On English-translated NNLE MCQs, ChatGPT 4.0 significantly outperformed ChatGPT 3.5 and Google Bard on theoretical questions, but not ChatGPT 3.5 on practical questions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For English-translated NNLE theoretical MCQs, ChatGPT 4.0 (71.5%) significantly outperformed ChatGPT 3.5 (55.7%) and Google Bard (49.8%). [→ Wu Zelin 2024](#wu-zelin-2024)
`q2 i?` For English-translated NNLE practical MCQs, ChatGPT 4.0 (67.8%) and ChatGPT 3.5 (60.9%) both significantly outperformed Google Bard (46.8%), but the ChatGPT 4.0 vs ChatGPT 3.5 difference was not statistically significant (P=.12). [→ Wu Zelin 2024 (2)](#wu-zelin-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Wu Zelin 2024

Wu Zelin, Gan Wenyi, Xue Zhaowen, Ni Zhengxin, Zheng Xiaofei, Zhang Yiyi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on Nursing Licensure Examinations in the United States and China: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/52746

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Evaluation of 235 English-translated NNLE theoretical MCQs input to all three LLMs, with accuracy rates of 71.5% (168/235), 55.7% (131/235), and 49.8% (117/235) respectively. The article reports the ChatGPT 4.0 advantage was statistically significant (P<.001 for both comparisons).

> "ChatGPT 4.0 had a higher accuracy rate than ChatGPT 3.5 (P<.001) and Google Bard (P<.001) for the English-translated version of NNLE Theoretical MCQs while the difference was statistically significant"

### Wu Zelin 2024 (2)

Wu Zelin, Gan Wenyi, Xue Zhaowen, Ni Zhengxin, Zheng Xiaofei, Zhang Yiyi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on Nursing Licensure Examinations in the United States and China: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/52746

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of 233 English-translated NNLE practical MCQs with accuracy rates of 67.8% (158/233), 60.9% (142/233), and 46.8% (109/233) for ChatGPT 4.0, ChatGPT 3.5, and Google Bard. Both ChatGPT versions beat Google Bard significantly, but ChatGPT 4.0 vs ChatGPT 3.5 was not significant.

> "the accuracy rates of both ChatGPT 4.0 (P<.001) and ChatGPT 3.5 (P=.002) were higher than those of Google Bard, and the difference was statistically significant; however, unlike before, the difference in accuracy rates between ChatGPT 4.0 and ChatGPT 3.5 was not statistically significant (P=.12)"
