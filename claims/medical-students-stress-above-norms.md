Preliminary one-samples t-tests against the age-adjusted population norm found the PT cohort's Time 1 mean (M = 16.73, SD = 6.07) was "significantly higher than the population norm adjusted for the similar age group"; the traditional cohort was also reported as significantly higher.

> "one-samples t-test showed that the average PSS scores for the PT cohort at Time 1 (M = 16.73, SD = 6.07) was significantly higher than the population norm adjusted for the similar age group (M = 14.4, SD = 6.2; Cohen et al., 1983), t(189) = 5.73, p < .001"
