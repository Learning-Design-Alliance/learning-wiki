## Evidence

### Khaleel Asyraaf Mat Sanusi 2025

Khaleel Asyraaf Mat Sanusi, Deniz Iren, Nardie Fanchamps, Mai Geisen, Roland Klemke. (2025). Virtual virtuoso: a systematic literature review of immersive learning environments for psychomotor skill development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10449-2

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Methodology section describing the PRISMA-based selection: the initial search yielded 1196 manuscripts, reduced to 1177 after duplicate removal, with title/abstract screening achieving Cohen's Kappa of 0.71 and full-text eligibility voting achieving Fleiss' Kappa of 0.55, ultimately selecting 39 articles.

> "Using Cohen's Kappa, a suitable measure for assessing agreement between two raters (Cohen, 1960), the first author and a co-author achieved a score of 0.71, indicating substantial agreement (Landis & Koch, 1977)."
