# Participants who under-used problem-solving or support-seeking showed increased problem-solving or support-seeking coping, and those who used escape-avoidance showed decreased escape-avoidance coping from pre-test to post-test within the reported groups

## Evidence

### Maunder Robert G 2010

Maunder Robert G, Lancee William J, Mae Reet, Vincent Leslie, Peladeau Nathalie, Beduz Mary Agnes, Hunter Jonathan J, Leszcz Molyn. (2010). Computer-assisted resilience training to prepare healthcare workers for pandemic influenza: a randomized trial of the optimal dose of training. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-72

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup pre-post analysis among participants scoring below 1.5 on problem-solving at T1. "a significant pre-post increase" was found (n = 72, p < 0.001); a parallel increase occurred for under-utilizing support-seeking (n = 72, p = 0.003).

> "Among those who were under-utilizing problem-solving at T1, there was a significant pre-post increase (n = 72, pre: 0.9 ± SD 0.9; post 1.2 ± 0.6; 95% CI of difference 0.2 - 0.4, p < 0.001)."

### Maunder Robert G 2010 (2)

Maunder Robert G, Lancee William J, Mae Reet, Vincent Leslie, Peladeau Nathalie, Beduz Mary Agnes, Hunter Jonathan J, Leszcz Molyn. (2010). Computer-assisted resilience training to prepare healthcare workers for pandemic influenza: a randomized trial of the optimal dose of training. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-72

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup pre-post analysis among participants reporting any escape-avoidance use at T1 (score > 0). "a pre-post decrease in this mode of coping" was found (n = 135, p = 0.01); no effect size is printed.

> "Among participants who reported any use of escape-avoidance at T1 (score > 0), there was a pre-post decrease in this mode of coping (n = 135, pre: 0.7 ± SD 0.5; post 0.6 ± 0.5; 95% CI of difference -0.1 - 0.0, p = 0.01)."
