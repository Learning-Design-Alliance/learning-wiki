### Yoo Hyo Hyun 2018

Yoo Hyo Hyun, Shin Sein, Lee Jun-Ki. (2018). Effects of medical communication curriculum on perceptions of Korean medical school students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.106

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Frequency trends across the three semantic network analyses showed post-LBC gains in terms such as 'active listening' (F=6) and 'rapport' (F=6) that then declined; the article states these "concepts such as active listening and respect were not instilled in students."

> "Important words like 'active listening' and 'respect' emerged post-LBC; however, with the exception of 'empathy' and 'communication' these words decreased in number post-CBC, thereby indicating that concepts such as active listening and respect were not instilled in students."
