## Evidence

### Luo Tao 2026

Luo Tao, Huang Qingpeng, Mai Jingyan. (2026). Development status of young new farmers from the perspective of vocational ability: A research analysis based on 21 cities in Guangdong Province. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA on vocational ability scores across academic backgrounds found science and engineering participants scored lowest in communication skills (2.54), while economics and management scored highest (4.43).

> "The results of the variance analysis showed that there was a statistically significant difference in communication skills among those with different academic backgrounds (F = 6.27, p < 0.01). Among them, the participants with a science and engineering background scored the lowest."

### Luo Tao 2026 (2)

Luo Tao, Huang Qingpeng, Mai Jingyan. (2026). Development status of young new farmers from the perspective of vocational ability: A research analysis based on 21 cities in Guangdong Province. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Economics and management background farmers scored significantly lower in career sustainability (2.65) than other groups, per ANOVA results.

> "Further statistical analysis revealed that their average sustainability score was significantly lower than that of other groups (F = 5.84, p < 0.01)."

### Luo Tao 2026 (3)

Luo Tao, Huang Qingpeng, Mai Jingyan. (2026). Development status of young new farmers from the perspective of vocational ability: A research analysis based on 21 cities in Guangdong Province. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349224

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Humanities background farmers scored lowest in practical innovation ability (2.44) and sustainable development ability (1.75), with significant ANOVA differences across disciplines.

> "The analysis of variance results showed that the differences in practical innovation ability among different academic backgrounds are statistically significant (F = 7.12, p < 0.01), with the group from humanities backgrounds experiencing more noticeable adaptation pressure."
