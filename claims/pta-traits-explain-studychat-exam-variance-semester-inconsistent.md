# PTA-derived traits significantly improve explained exam-score variance over a prior-outcome baseline in one semester of StudyChat but not the other

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adding 10 PTA traits to a prior-outcome regression significantly improves explanatory R2 for exam scores in Fall 2024 (generic pool, ΔR2 +0.102, p=.030) but not in Spring 2025. [→ Hunter McNichols 2026](#hunter-mcnichols-2026)

## Evidence

### Hunter McNichols 2026

Hunter McNichols, Kai Du, Andrew Lan. (2026). PRINCIPAL TRAIT ANALYSIS: TOWARDS DERIVING "SKILLS" IN HUMAN-AI COLLABORATION. A preprint - August 13, 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11460 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.11460](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.11460)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of the StudyChat corpus (1,540 sessions by 171 students), comparing a prior-exam-average baseline against a model adding 10 PTA traits, reporting in-sample ΔR2 gains of +0.102 (p=.030) Fall 2024 and +0.066 (p=.059) Spring 2025 for the lens-ensemble pool. The effect "does not hold in the Spring 2025 semester."

> "On the StudyChat dataset, we observe that the addition of the 10 PTA traits improves explainability significantly in the Fall 2024 semester when using traits derived from the generic trait pool. However, we see that this result does not hold in the Spring 2025 semester."
