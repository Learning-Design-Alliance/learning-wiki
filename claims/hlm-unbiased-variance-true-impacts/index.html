Evidence¶

Henry May 2014¶

Henry May. (2014). Distortions in Distributions of Impact Estimates in Multi-Site Trials: The Central Limit Theorem is Not Your Friend. SREE Fall 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562702

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Monte Carlo simulation finding: although the shape of the distribution could not be recovered, the article reports random effects HLM "do[es] a reasonable job of producing an unbiased estimate of treatment effect variation" across uniform, lognormal, and negative lognormal true distributions.

"Results from the Monte Carlo simulations suggests that random effects HLM models do a reasonable job of producing an unbiased estimate of treatment effect variation (i.e., the variance of the true impacts ) regardless of the shape of the true distribution of impact estimates."
