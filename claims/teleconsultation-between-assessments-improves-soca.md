# Completing an EQClinic teleconsultation between two face-to-face consultations improved medical students' SOCA communication scores, while teleconsultation before the first face-to-face consultation did not

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group B, which completed its teleconsultation between the two face-to-face consultations, showed significant pre-to-post improvement in mean SOCA score (12.58 to 13.53; P=.005), whereas group A showed no significant increase (P>.05). [→ Liu C 2016](#liu-c-2016)

## Evidence

### Liu C 2016

Liu C, Lim RL, McCabe KL, Taylor S, Calvo RA. (2016). A Web-Based Telehealth Training Platform Incorporating Automated Nonverbal Behavior Feedback for Teaching Communication Skills to Medical Students: A Randomized Crossover Study. J Med Internet Res 18(9):e246. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.6299

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t tests compared SOCA scores across both face-to-face consultations for students who completed EQClinic. "Group B alone showed significant improvement in their mean SOCA score"; group A's change (13.02 to 13.28) was not significant (P>.05).

> "Group B alone showed significant improvement in their mean SOCA score (mean preexposure score 12.58 vs postexposure score 13.53; t48= –2.96; P=.005)."
