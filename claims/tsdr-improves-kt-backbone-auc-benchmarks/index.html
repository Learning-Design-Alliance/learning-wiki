Peilin Zhan 2026¶

Peilin Zhan, Wei Chen, Weilin Chen, Shuyi Pan, Ruichu Cai. (2026). Temporal Smoothness Doubly Robust Learning for Debiased Knowledge Tracing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05958 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.05958

q2 · i? · design · r2

Empirical benchmark study (RQ1, Table 1) applying TSDR to DKT, AKT, simpleKT, FoLiBiKT, SparseKT and DisKT on nine datasets with student-stratified 5-fold cross-validation, evaluated via AUC, ACC and RMSE. The article reports "TSDR yields improvements across diverse model architectures."

"Regarding the primary evaluation metric of AUC, TSDR yields improvements across diverse model architectures. These gains are particularly notable on datasets characterized by high sparsity or strong student autonomy."

Related Claims¶

  • TSDR consistently improves calibration (ACC and RMSE) even on datasets where AUC gains plateau — related
  • In ablations, temporal smoothness works best on the imputation branch, and the full TSDR variant achieves the best AUC and ACC on all six backbones — related
  • TSDR is robust to the smoothness coefficient, with a stable sweet spot for lambda in (0.3, 1.0) — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Under controlled synthetic MNAR bias, TSDR-equipped models improve across the entire bias spectrum, with the RMSE advantage diminishing beyond gamma > 0.8 — related
  • Meta-behavioral pattern extraction outperforms knowledge-concept-based collaborative extraction, which fails to consistently boost performance due to data sparsity — related
  • TSDR's AUC gains are largest on open-ended platforms and limited on EdNet, whose mandatory bundle completion policy constrains selection bias — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
