Ido Davidesco 2026¶

Ido Davidesco, Yushuang Liu, Kim Chaloner, Emma Laurent, Gabriella A. Ali, Sarah C. Creider, Sean Hughes, Laura Noejovich, Henry Valk, Dana Bevilacqua, David Poeppel & Suzanne Dikker. (2026). The dynamics of student engagement in cooperative science learning: a multimodal approach. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00457-z

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Video coding of cooperative learning sessions for 30 students with adequate video data, coded in 5-second segments into six categories by trained research assistants. Percentages are descriptive; no inferential test is reported for the distribution.

"On average, students appeared to be on-task 60% of the time, either physically (i.e.,manipulating objects in a task-related manner), verbally (i.e., talking about the task), or both physically and verbally (i.e., talking about the task while building their cell model)."
