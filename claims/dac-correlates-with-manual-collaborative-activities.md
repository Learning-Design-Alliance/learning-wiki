# Automatically coded dialogue acts correlate with manually coded collaborative activities as expected

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Correlating automatically coded dialogue acts (form) with manually coded collaborative activities (content) across four manual dimensions and 15 categories was used to establish convergent validity, with weak to moderate correlations expected. [→ Erkens 2008](#erkens-2008)

## Evidence

### Erkens 2008

Erkens, G., & Janssen, J. (2008). Automatic coding of dialogue acts in collaboration protocols. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 3, 447-470. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9052-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Validity analysis correlating automatic dialogue act coding with a manual coding scheme for collaborative activities (task-related activities, regulation of task-related activities, social activities, and regulation of social activities; 15 categories). The manual scheme's reliability was Cohen's kappa .86 in Study 1 (601 segments) and .94 in Study 2 (796 segments). Expected correlations included arguments with TaskExch and confirmations with SociUnd+; the article states "Weak to moderate correlations are thus to be expected."

> "For the current analysis, the manually coded collaborative activities were correlated with the automatically coded dialogue acts."
