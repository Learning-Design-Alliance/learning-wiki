How perception of being recognized or not recognized by instructors as a “physics person” impacts male and female students’ self-efficacy and performance. Survey comparison in Table I of matched students (233 female, 464 male) followed from physics 1 to physics 2 at a large public university. The row prints perceived recognition means of 2.55 (male) vs 2.14 (female), p < 0.001, with Cohen's d = 0.55 favoring male students, a moderate effect size.
