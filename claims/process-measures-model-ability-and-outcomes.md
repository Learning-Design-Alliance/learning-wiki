## Evidence

### Wood 1999

Wood, H., & Wood, D. (1999). Help seeking, learning and contingent tutoring. Computers & Education, 33, 153-169. https://www.elsevier.com/locate/compedu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analyses on the 42-pupil QUADRATIC log data (§3.1). The model of time per operation, error frequency and help seeking "accounts for 63% of the variance in pre-test scores", with all three measures as independent contributors.

> "Putting together time per operation with frequency of errors and help seeking gives us a model which accounts for 63% of the variance in pre-test scores (and all three process measures are independent contributors)."
