## Evidence

### Tee Yu-San 2024

Tee Yu-San, Cheng Chi-Tung, Li Pei-Hua, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Liao Chien-Hung, Fu Chih-Yuan. (2024). Optimizing modern surgical simulation through instructor feedback – insights from a retrospective observational study in a tertiary hospital. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06090-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training questionnaire completed by all participating trainees and summarized in Supplementary File 1. The article also prints 75% expecting reduced training time on real patients, 83.6% expecting fewer clinical errors, and 89.1% expecting smoother learning curves; these are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.

> "Overall, the trainees expressed high satisfaction with the SBT, with a mean satisfaction 9.3/10. Furthermore, 96.4% of trainees demonstrated a willingness to use modern surgical simulators as part of their training."
