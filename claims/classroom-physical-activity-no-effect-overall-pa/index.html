Watson Amanda, Timperio Anna, Brown Helen, Best Keren, Hesketh Kylie D. (2017). Effect of classroom-based physical activity interventions on academic and physical activity outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Random-effects meta-analysis of 3 studies measuring physical activity over the study duration. The review reports the interventions "did not affect physical activity" with SMD = 0.40 (95% CI: -0.15,0.95).
