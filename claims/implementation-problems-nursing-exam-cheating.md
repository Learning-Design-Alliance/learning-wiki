### Mousavi SK 2025

Mousavi SK, Kamali M. (2025). Explaining reasons for cheating in exams among undergraduate nursing students: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07693-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview findings on implementation problems across faculty, exam organizers, and the physical environment. One participant said organizers "do not take cheaters seriously or do not do their job properly" and may "leave the hall during the exam" (P 9).

> "According to the participants, the existence of some weaknesses in faculty members, such as difficulties in establishing proper communication with students or weaknesses in academic abilities, leads them to cheat."
