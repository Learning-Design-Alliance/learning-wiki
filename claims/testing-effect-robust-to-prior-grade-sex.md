## Evidence

### Kalyva 2026

Kalyva, M., Kavvadia, K., Louca, L., Televantou, I., & Michaelides, M. P. (2026). The “Testing Effect” in Undergraduate Endodontic Education: The Impact of Regular Low Stakes Testing on Learning Outcomes and Student Engagement. International Endodontic Journal, 59(6), 1227–1235. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.70022

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design repeated measures ANOVA on the 81 students' exam subscores adding prior Endodontics II grade and sex. The adjusted difference "remained significant with a moderate to large effect size" (F 1,78 = 9.392, p = 0.003), effect size η² = 0.107, a medium-to-large effect.

> "The difference remained significant with a moderate to large effect size (F 1,78 = 9.392, p = 0.003, η p 2 = 0.107) after adding the grade in the previous course as a covariate and sex as a between‐participant variable."

### Kalyva 2026 (2)

Kalyva, M., Kavvadia, K., Louca, L., Televantou, I., & Michaelides, M. P. (2026). The “Testing Effect” in Undergraduate Endodontic Education: The Impact of Regular Low Stakes Testing on Learning Outcomes and Student Engagement. International Endodontic Journal, 59(6), 1227–1235. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.70022

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same mixed-design ANOVA: prior course grade had a significant effect (F 1,78 = 18.869, p < 0.001, η² = 0.195), while interactions with the testing effect "were not significant, suggesting a homogeneous effect of the testing intervention on the material."

> "The prior course grade had a significant effect on the exam scores (F 1,78 = 18.869, p < 0.001, η p 2 = 0.195), but interactions of prior course grade and sex with the within covariates were not significant, suggesting a homogeneous effect of the testing intervention on the material."
