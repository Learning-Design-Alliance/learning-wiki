Evidence¶

Kraft and Rogers 2013¶

Matthew A. Kraft and Todd Rogers. (2013). Teacher-to-Parent Communication: Experimental Evidence from a Low-Cost Communication Policy. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563011

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive coding of teachers' written sentences comparing the two message types. Needs-improvement sentences were "18 percentage points more likely to be about an out-of-class issue" (p<0.001) and "two words longer on average" (p<0.001) than positive sentences.

"just over 84% of all needs-improvement sentences were actionable while only 8.5% of positive sentences referenced specific actions (p<0.001)"
