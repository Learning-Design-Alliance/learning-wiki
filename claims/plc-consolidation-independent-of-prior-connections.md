## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There were no significant differences between Type 1, Type 2, and Type 3 PLCs (formed without prior acquaintance, without prior teamwork, or as established teams) on any PLC dimension. [→ Layton-Jaramillo 2025](#layton-jaramillo-2025)

### Layton-Jaramillo 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors interpret the null connection-type differences as an effect of I3P team monitoring, stating "Type 1 PLCs required greater support from I3P professionals throughout the project than Type 2 and Type 3 PLCs".

> "This result is directly related to the monitoring carried out for the teams since Type 1 PLCs required greater support from I3P professionals throughout the project than Type 2 and Type 3 PLCs."
