# Activating emotions (enjoyment, shame, anxiety) relate positively and boredom negatively to intended political participation, with a significant indirect effect of classroom climate via enjoyment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Enjoyment, shame, and anxiety relate positively and boredom negatively to intended political participation at the within level; enjoyment mediates the climate-participation link positively, anxiety shows a significant negative indirect (competitive mediation) effect. [→ Graf 2023](#graf-2023)

## Evidence

### Graf 2023

Graf, E., Stempfer, L., Muis, K. R., & Goetz, T. (2023). Classroom emotions in civic education: A multilevel approach to antecedents and effects. Draft version 1.0, University of Vienna / McGill University (not peer-reviewed preprint). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101869

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel mediation models on intended political participation (N = 1,117, 90 classes). The article also reports that "shame revealed a positive association with participation (R2w = .131), and boredom related negatively to participation (R2w = .125)" at the within level.

> "we found a significant positive indirect effect in the model of enjoyment (R2w = .266) and a significant negative indirect effect in the model of anxiety (R2w = .133) at the within level"
