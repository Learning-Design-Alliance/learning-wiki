### Menzies et al. 2016

Victoria Menzies, Adetayo Kasim, Dimitra Kokotsaki, Catherine Hewitt, Nasima Akhter, Clare Collyer, Kirsty Younger, Andy Wiggins and Carole Torgerson. (2016). Hallé SHINE on Manchester: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581134

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process evaluation finding from the mixed-methods evaluation of Saturday school delivery across Manchester schools; the report identifies three stated causes of low attendance among eligible pupils. Further analysis taking attendance into account did not alter the overall null finding.

> "Reasons for low attendance included pupils’ lack of availability to attend the Saturday sessions, variable parental engagement with the programme, and limited time at the beginning of the programme for schools to engage children and parents."
