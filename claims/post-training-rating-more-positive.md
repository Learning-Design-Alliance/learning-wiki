### Ruebsam Marie-Luise 2023

Ruebsam Marie-Luise, Orsson Dorota, Metelmann Bibiana, Orsson Jakub, Hahnenkamp Klaus, Metelmann Camilla. (2023). Cross-border simulation training for German and Polish emergency medical teams is feasible: conception and evaluation of a bilingual simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04823-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive pre-post comparison using the training evaluation inventory by Ritzmann on a 5-point Likert scale; post-training response rate was considerably lower (n = 5; 31%) than pre-training (n = 13; 81%). Means are descriptive; no paired test result is printed.

> "After attending CB simulation training, it was ranked more positively (pre 3.1 ± 0.5 vs post 3.4 ± 0.3)."
