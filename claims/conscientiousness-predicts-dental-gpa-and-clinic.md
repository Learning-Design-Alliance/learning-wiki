## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jaber Mohamed 2022

Jaber Mohamed, Al-Samarrai Basim, Salah Afraa, Varma Sudhir Rama, Karobari Mohmed Isaqali, Marya Anand. (2022). Does General and Specific Traits of Personality Predict Students' Academic Performance? BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9422299

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of 144 dental students' BFI scores against weighted GPA of four years of academic courses and the clinical dentistry course GPA. Printed effect sizes: r = 0.30 for GPA and r = 0.31 for clinical marks, both p < 0.01; the article reports conscientiousness "predicted GPA (r = 0.30, p < 0.01)".

> "Table 5 shows that conscientiousness recorded the strongest association with the academic and clinical performance of dentistry students compared to the other BF model domains; conscientiousness predicted GPA (r = 0.30, p < 0.01) and clinical marks (r = 0.31, p < 0.01)."
