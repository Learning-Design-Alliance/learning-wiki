## Evidence

### Walsh et al. 2015

Mary Walsh, Anastasia Raczek, Erin Sibley, Terrence Lee-St. John, Chen An, Bercem Akbayin, Eric Dearing, Claire Foley. (2015). City Connects: Building an Argument for Effects on Student Achievement with a Quasi-Experimental Design. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 1's two-level weighted regression models predicted standardized MCAS scores in ELA and Math in Grades 3 to 8 and weighted GPA in Grades 6 to 8, with propensity score weights making treatment and control groups statistically equivalent at baseline. The article reports "strong and positive impacts on both MCAS ELA and Math as well as GPA in almost every grade." No standardized effect size is printed for these middle school impacts.
