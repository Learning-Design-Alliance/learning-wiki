## Evidence

### Bamidele 2013

Bamidele, E.F. & Oloyede, E. O. (2013). Comparative Effectiveness of Hierarchical, Flowchart and Spider Concept Mapping Strategies on Students’ Performance in Chemistry. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p66

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-Way ANOVA of PSATC pre-test scores (Table 2: Between Groups F = 1.189, Sig. .307; group means 30.81, 31.13, 33.93 for flowchart, spider, hierarchical). The article reports "no significant difference in the mean score of the three groups" at entry.

> "Since p > 0.05, this indicated that there was no significant difference in the mean score of the three groups with respect to their performance in Chemistry as shown by their result in the pre-test."
