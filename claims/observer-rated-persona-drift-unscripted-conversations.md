## Evidence

### Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Nicolas Leins, Thomas Kosch, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). LLM-Based Educational Simulation: Evaluating Temporal Student Persona Stability Across ADHD-Related Persona Specifications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06307 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM analysis of Experiment II unscripted education conversations with observer ratings at turns 3, 6, and 9. Descriptively, high-intensity personas declined by ∆ = −4.0 points (11.1%) and moderate by ∆ = −2.82 (7.8%); the article reports the turn effect as "highly significant and substantial (β = −1.13...)". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "For unscripted education observer ratings, the turn effect was highly significant and substantial (β = −1.13, SE = 0.05, t = −20.80, p<. 001). Post-hoc pairwise comparisons confirmed significant differences between all turn pairs (allp < .001)."
