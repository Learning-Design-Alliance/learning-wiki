## Evidence

### Lai Nai Ming 2011

Lai Nai Ming, Teng Cheong Lieng. (2011). Self-perceived competence correlates poorly with objectively measured competence in Evidence Based Medicine among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-25

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of questionnaire sum-ratings and Fresno test sum-scores in 45 students. Self-rated searching was 63.4% vs 43.6% objectively; self-rated appraising was 77.9% vs 49.2% objectively.

> "Comparing the students' sum-ratings and their sum-scores in percentages, the students in general appeared to rate themselves higher than their actual performances."
