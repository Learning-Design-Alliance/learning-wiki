## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lang Chen 2022

Lang Chen, Hyesang Chang, Jeremy Rudoler, Eydis Arnardottir, Yuan Zhang, Carlo de los Angeles and Vinod Menon. (2022). Cognitive training enhances growth mindset in children through plasticity of cortico-striatal circuits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00146-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Task-based event-related parametric fMRI whole-brain analysis (post vs. pre) of 38 training-group children during math problem solving; activation-growth-mindset correlations survived controls for age, IQ, and math-skill changes for right dACC and right hippocampus. Control group showed no significant relations (ps > 0.447).

> "increases in brain activation in the dorsal ACC in both hemispheres, right dorsal striatum, and right hippocampus were correlated with increases in growth mindset in the training group"
