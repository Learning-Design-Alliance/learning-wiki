### Haolin Feng 2026

Detective scaffolding for within-session reasoning development: a three-phase framework evaluated in polymer engineering and pre-university outreach. arXiv preprint. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Dual-accuracy analysis of the undergraduate polling data (Fig. 3), comparing a strict textbook-correct measure with an inclusive measure defined a priori from the dataset's process logic; the two measures diverge only at Q3, where "step changes" (52%) was judged a structurally valid characterisation.

> "When step-change responses are included, Q3 accuracy rises to 62%, and the overall trajectory shows a monotonic increase from 21% to 100%."
