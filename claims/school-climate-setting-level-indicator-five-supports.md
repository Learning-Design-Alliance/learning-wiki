### Allensworth 2018 (2)

Allensworth, E.M., Nagaoka, J., & Johnson, D.W. (2018). High school graduation and college readiness indicator systems: What we know, what we need to know. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://ncs.uchicago.edu/freshman-on-track-toolkit

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review states that Chicago schools receive setting-level indicator data from annual surveys of teachers and students, reported in five areas called the five essential covers covering safety, instruction, collaboration, and leadership, and that "the survey responses have been found to be highly predictive of teacher and school performance." No effect size is printed.

> "The survey responses have been found to be highly predictive of teacher and school performance."
