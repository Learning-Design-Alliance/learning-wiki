`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The time-varying coefficient from the functional regression (Figs 3–4) traces a U-shape across the four years, with the printed values 54, 5, and 48 minutes per GPA point at the named semesters; these are point estimates from the coefficient curve, not hypothesis tests of the U-shape itself.

> "The association was strongest during freshman year, peaking at approximately 54 minutes per GPA point during Spring 2016. The relationship then declined progressively through sophomore and junior years, reaching its minimum of approximately 5 minutes per GPA point during Spring 2018. Notably, the relationship strengthened again during senior year, returning to approximately 48 minutes per GPA point by Spring 2019."
