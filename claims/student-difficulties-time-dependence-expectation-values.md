## Evidence

### Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Development and Evaluation of a Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial on Larmor Precession Of Spin. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Written survey on time dependence of expectation values given to 39 students before QuILT development, using open-ended surveys and multiple-choice questions. The article reports "46% of students did not realize" that an operator commuting with the Hamiltonian is a constant of motion.

> "However, 46% of students did not realize that since the Hamiltonian governs the time-evolution of the system, any operator ˆQ that commutes with it must be a constant of motion and its expectation value must be time-independent."

### Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Development and Evaluation of a Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial on Larmor Precession Of Spin. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Same 39-student written survey probing recognition of stationary states: with the magnetic field along z and the initial state an eigenstate of Sz, "51% of students surveyed incorrectly stated that <Sx> and <Sy> depend on time in this case."

> "However, 51% of students surveyed incorrectly stated that ⟨Sx⟩ and ⟨Sy⟩ depend on time in this case."

### Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2016 (3)

Benjamin R. Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Development and Evaluation of a Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial on Larmor Precession Of Spin. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.008)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Same written survey probing confusion between stationary states and eigenstates of other observables: "49% of students claimed that if a system is initially in an eigenstate of Sx or Sy, the system will remain in that eigenstate."

> "For example, for Larmor precession with the magnetic ﬁeld in the z direction, 49% of students claimed that if a system is initially in an eigenstate of ˆSx or ˆSy, the system will remain in that eigenstate."
