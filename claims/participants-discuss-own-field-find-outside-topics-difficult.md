## Evidence

### Santos 2021

Santos, D. J. D., Goto, T., Lu, T.-Y., Ho, S. C.-C., Wang, T.-W., On, A. Y. L., Hashimoto, T., & Young, S.-C. (2021). Investigative Study on Preprint Journal Club as an Effective Method of Teaching Latest Knowledge in Astronomy. arXiv:2106.01688. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-tailed Fisher exact tests on 2×4 contingency tables of research fields versus discussed, easy, and difficult topics (Table VI). Research field vs difficult topics: enrollees p = 0.003 (significant), volunteers p = 0.095; field vs discussed topics and easy topics were not significant. Audiences could not be tested due to very low sample size.

> "Nevertheless, our results show that most par- ticipants tend to discuss papers in their research ﬁelds as they ﬁnd topics outside their research ﬁeld diﬃcult to understand."
