Polonio-López Begoña et al. (2025). Academic performance and anxiety in the evaluation of health science undergraduate students using osce: a pre- and post-covid-19 cohort study. BMC Medical Education 25:1171. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07780-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive within-cohort comparison of STAI-SA before and after the OSCE in both cohorts; state anxiety "decreased after the test" in both, by 4.01 and 1.92 points respectively. Values are descriptive; no test statistic or effect size is printed for these drops.

> "The values of state anxiety (STAI-SA) in the prepandemic group decreased after the test (4.01 points), as did those in the postpandemic group (1.92 points), although to a lesser extent in this group"
