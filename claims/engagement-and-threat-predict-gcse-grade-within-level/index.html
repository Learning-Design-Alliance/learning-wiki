Evidence¶

Putwain 2021¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Kutuk, G. (2021). Warning Students of the Consequences of Examination Failure: An Effective Strategy for Promoting Student Engagement? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1329876

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Within-level ML-SEM outcome paths. Behavioral engagement and threat evaluation predicted GCSE mathematics grade over and above Year 9 mathematics grade (β = .42, p < .001). Fear appeals and challenge evaluation showed no direct prediction. No standardised effect size beyond β printed.

"GCSE mathematics grade was predicted positively by T2 behavioral engagement (β = .20, p <.001), and negatively by T2 threat (β = -.21, p <.001), but not T2 fear appeals (β = -.05, p =.24) or T2 challenge (β = .03, p =.56)"
