DiD comparison of proactive versus reactive tutoring on relative MAP growth (Table 4), using AI-only tutoring as baseline. The IK-bandwidth interaction coefficient was 0.75 (p = .065, marginally significant); a similar pattern in the full sample was 0.78 (p = .010).
