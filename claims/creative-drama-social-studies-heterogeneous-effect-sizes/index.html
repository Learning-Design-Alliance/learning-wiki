Hafize Er-Türküresin 2020¶

Heterogeneity testing within the same meta-analysis of 19 studies, using Q and I² statistics under fixed and random effects models. The article reports Q = 90.019, p < .05, and describes the I² value as indicating "a high level of heterogeneity with 78,893%".
