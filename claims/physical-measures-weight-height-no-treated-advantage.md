`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Physical tests taken by the school's physical director: standing height and weight in October and May. Room 18's median weight rose from 67.5 to 76.5 lbs (gain 9 lbs) against Room 21's 67.25 to 73 lbs (gain 5.75 lbs). Height gains were 2.9 and 2.5 inches. Descriptive medians only.

> "Here we find that while the median for the two classes was almost identical, in October-Room 18 leads in gain by 3.25 lbs."
