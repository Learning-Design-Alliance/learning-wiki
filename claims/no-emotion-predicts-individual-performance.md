## Evidence

### Grewal 2026

Grewal, K., Azher, S., Moreno, M., Pekrun, R., Wiseman, J., Flake, J., Lajoie, S., Sun, N.‐Z., Fried, G. M., Khalil, E., & Harley, J. M. (2026). Medical trainees' emotions and their effects on perceptions of performance and team mood in team‐based simulations. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 306–336. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70017

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-level model of post-simulation emotions predicting perceived individual performance with presimulation emotions, role, PGY and specialty as covariates; "no post‐simulation emotion was a significant predictor" in the full model, though pride was significant before covariates (b = .25, η² = .06).

> "While the predictor‐only model revealed that post‐simulation pride was a significant positive predictor of perceived individual performance (b = .25, p = .001, η 2 = .06, power > .99); once covariates were added to create the full model, and no post‐simulation emotion was a significant predictor of perceived individual performance."
