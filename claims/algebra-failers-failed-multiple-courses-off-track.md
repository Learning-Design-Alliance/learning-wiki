# Most students needing Algebra I credit recovery failed multiple ninth-grade courses and few were on track to graduate

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` About three of every four students who failed an Algebra I semester also failed three or more semesters of core courses, and only 13% were on track to graduate versus 91% of students who passed Algebra I. [→ Rickles 2016](#rickles-2016)

## Evidence

### Rickles 2016

Rickles, J., Heppen, J., Taylor, S., Allensworth, E., Michelman, V., Sorensen, N., Walters, K., & Clements, P. (2016). Getting Back on Track: Who Needs to Recover Algebra Credit After Ninth Grade? (Research Brief 3). American Institutes for Research & University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.air.org/CreditRecovery

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Course-failure analysis of ninth-grade records for 36,423 CPS students. Average core semester courses failed was five for students who failed Algebra I versus less than one for students who passed; by year's end 13% versus 91% were on track to graduate.

> "About three of every four students who failed an Algebra I semester credit also failed three or more semesters of their core courses (see Figure 4), and 28% of students failed more than six semesters of their ninth-grade core academic courses"
