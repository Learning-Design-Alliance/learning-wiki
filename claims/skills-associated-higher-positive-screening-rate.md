### Qiu Yanglin 2025

Qiu Yanglin, Fan Xinyu, Wu Yanjuan, Wang Shibin, Tan Wenyan, Liao Jing. (2025). Variation in dementia screening outcomes: the influence of primary care providers' occupations and knowledge, attitudes, skills. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-025-02886-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fully adjusted (Model 3) multilevel logistic regression of 2823 screened older adults nested in 252 PCPs. The article reports the skills odds ratio of "OR = 1.024, 95%CI: 1.004–1.046" and interprets it as "a 2.4% increase in the positive rate" per unit; the article prints no standardised effect size, so the magnitude is coded null.
