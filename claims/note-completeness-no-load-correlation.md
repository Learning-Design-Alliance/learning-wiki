## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Note completeness was not significantly related to either germane or extraneous load, contradicting hypotheses 5 and 6. [→ Jamie Costley 2021](#jamie-costley-2021)

## Evidence

### Jamie Costley 2021

Jamie Costley, Mik Fanguy. (2021). Collaborative note-taking affects cognitive load: the interplay of completeness and interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09979-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis within the treatment group (n = 74), with completeness scored against rubrics of 930 meaningful units across 56 lecture videos. Both completeness correlations were "not statistically significant". No effect size was printed.

> "Furthermore, while both germane and extraneous load had a positive relationship with the completeness of notes, these correlations were not statistically significant and were smaller than the effect of the amount of collaboration (hypotheses 5 and 6 are rejected)."
