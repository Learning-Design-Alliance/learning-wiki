# Students were significantly more positive about classroom academic environments and teacher attention in Summer Bridge than in the school year

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who attended Summer Bridge reported significantly more positive perceptions of classroom academic environments and teacher attention in summer than during the school year. [→ Melissa Roderick 2003](#melissa-roderick-2003)

## Evidence

### Melissa Roderick 2003

Melissa Roderick, Mimi Engel, Jenny Nagaoka, with Brian A. Jacob, Sophie Degener, Alex Orfei, Susan Stone, Jen Bacon. (2003). Ending Social Promotion: Results From Summer Bridge. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/ending-social-promotion-results-summer-bridge

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Based on student surveys collected during both the school year and summer 1999 plus qualitative interviews, students portrayed summer classrooms as places where they were expected to work hard and where teachers were supportive.

> "students who attended Summer Bridge were significantly more positive in the summer than in the school year about the academic environments of their classrooms and the attention they received from teachers"
