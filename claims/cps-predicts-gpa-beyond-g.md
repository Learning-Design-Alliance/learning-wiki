## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MicroDYN predicted GPA over and above the CFT measure of g, but the CFT was more strongly related to GPA and the incremental variance was small and appeared only in Grades 8 and 10. [→ Greiff 2013](#greiff-2013)

## Evidence

### Greiff 2013

Greiff, S., Wüstenberg, S., Molnár, G., Fischer, A., Funke, J., & Csapó, B. (2013, February 18). Complex Problem Solving in Educational Contexts—Something Beyond g: Concept, Assessment, Measurement Invariance, and Construct Validity. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031856

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Manifest path analyses per grade predicted self-reported GPA from MicroDYN only, CFT only, and both simultaneously, comparing explained variance to assess incremental validity of MicroDYN. "MicroDYN added a small percentage of variance" beyond the CFT in Grades 8 and 10; global model fit was good (RMSEAs = .000–.001).

> "Results displayed in Table 5 show that although MicroDYN predicted performance in GPA, the CFT was more strongly related to GPA. Additionally, MicroDYN added a small percentage of variance when predicting GPA together with the CFT in Grades 8 and 10."
