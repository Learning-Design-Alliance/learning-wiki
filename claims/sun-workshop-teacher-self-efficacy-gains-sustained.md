`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial, self-efficacy was measured with a modified Science Teaching Efficacy Belief Instrument, In-Service A, administered after the workshop and 1 yr later. The article reports "significant effects" for self-efficacy with sustained treatment effects at 1 yr; no effect size is printed for this finding.

> "We determined significant effects of the SUN workshop for all groups in terms of knowledge and self-efficacy, and we additionally found that the treatment group sustained significant effects even 1 yr later for each outcome."
