## Evidence

### Armstrong G 2010

Armstrong G, Blashki G, Joubert L, Bland R, Moulding R, Gunn J, Naccarella L. (2010). An evaluation of the effect of an educational intervention for Australian social workers on competence in delivering brief cognitive behavioural strategies: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-10-304

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression (Table 3) of the two CTS subscales in the RCT: General Therapeutic Skills difference 7.86 (95% CI 3.81–11.90, p < 0.001) and Conceptualisation, Strategy and Technique difference 6.34 (95% CI 2.73–9.96, p < 0.001). No standardised effect sizes are printed.

> "The overall difference between the study groups in competence at follow-up was also statistically significant in each of the two competency subscales, when controlling for baseline competency scores."
