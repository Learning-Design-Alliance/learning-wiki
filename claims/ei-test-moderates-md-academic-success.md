Emotional intelligence weakly predicts academic success in medical programs: a multilevel meta-analysis and systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04417-8

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis within the three-level meta-analysis testing EI test as a categorical moderator. The EQ-i test was "highly correlated with academic success compared with other EI tests"; EQ-i r = .14, MSCEIT r = .10, SSEIT r = .11 (non-significant), other r = .09 per Table 3.

> "Moderator analysis showed that the mean effect size significantly varied according to the EI test, Q(3) = 42.93, p < 0.001, and EI subscale, Q(3) = 18.87, p = 0.04"
