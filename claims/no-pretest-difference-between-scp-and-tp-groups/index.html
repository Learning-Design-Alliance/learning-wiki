Evidence¶

Corlu 2011¶

Corlu, M. S., Capraro, R. M., & Corlu, M. A. (2011). Developing computational fluency with the help of science: A Turkish middle and high school grades study. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2), 72-81. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932227

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline check in the quasi-experiment: after the shared first intervention, pre-test scores did not differ between groups (p=0.70), with subgroup checks at p=0.31 (middle) and p=0.46 (high school). The article reports "no statistically significant difference (p=0.70) in pre-test scores between the groups"; equivalence was not formally tested.

"Independent samples t-test at 95% confidence level provided no statistically significant difference (p=0.70) in pre-test scores between the groups after the first intervention, and it was concluded that the groups were at a close starting point before the second intervention"
