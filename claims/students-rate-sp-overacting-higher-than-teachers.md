## Evidence

### Himmelbauer M 2018

Himmelbauer M, Seitz T, Seidman C, Löffler-Stastka H. (2018). Standardized patients in psychiatry – the best way to learn clinical skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1184-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of student (n=529) and teacher (n=29) questionnaire responses after the psychiatry workshop-seminars. The article reports Cohen's d = 0.27 overall and names the two statements where the significant difference appeared.

> "Only a slight difference between students’ and teachers’ evaluations was observed (Cohen’s d = 0.27). The only significant difference appeared in the statements “SP overacted”, to which more students agreed, and “SP could be a real patient”, to which more students disagreed."
