Conceptual analysis in the review's Pathway 3 section, contrasting conventional search with AI synthesis. The review notes traditional search was never transparent or neutral, and that this can weaken verification while improving surface product quality, making epistemic deficits harder for teachers to detect. No empirical test is reported.

> "Conversational synthesis can hide that plurality behind one voice. The learner's task changes from selecting among visible sources to recovering the evidential structure of an answer that appears already integrated."
