Lena S. Geiger 2024¶

Lena S. Geiger, Torsten Wüstenberg, Zhenxiang Zang, Mirjam Melzer, Stephanie H. Witt, Marcella Rietschel, Markus M. Nöthen, Stefan Herms, Franziska Degenhardt, Andreas Meyer-Lindenberg & Carolin Moessnang. (2024). q2 · i? · associational · r1

Group-level curve fitting of error rates across sessions s1-s5 of a modified Sternberg task in 23 healthy adults (11 Val, 12 Met carriers), comparing linear and exponential models via likelihood ratio tests. The authors report "asuperiority of the exponential model for all conditions" with strongest evidence in the practiced condition.

"The comparison of models within genotype groups using likelihood ratio tests revealed asuperiority of the exponential model for all conditions, with strongest evidence in thepracticed condition in the Met group (see Table 2)."

Related Claims¶

  • In the practiced condition, 23 brain parcels showed genotype differences in exponential model fit, mostly favoring the Met group and concentrated in frontal and striatal regions — related
  • BDNF Val66Met genotype is associated with behavioral learning curves: Met-allele carriers show higher learning gain and, per the authors, less efficient early learning — related
  • Repeated procedural training produces exponential decay of brain activation in a distributed set of regions including fronto-striatal circuits, paralleling exponential improvement in task performance — related
  • Behavioral self-regulation development between ages three and seven is best described by an exponential (nonlinear) growth curve — related
