### Madjar Nir 2012

Madjar Nir, Bachner Yaacov G, Kushnir Talma. (2012). Can achievement goal theory provide a useful motivational perspective for explaining psychosocial attributes of medical students? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation matrix analysis of the same 143-student questionnaire dataset, examining relations among goal orientations, PMI and LFT. The matrix printed "r = -.21, p < .05" for mastery with LFT; the regression retained a significant negative association (β = -.23, p < .01).

> "scores on the mastery goal orientation measure were positively associated with scores on the PMI (r = .16, p < .05), and negatively related to the LFT level (r = -.21, p < .05)"
