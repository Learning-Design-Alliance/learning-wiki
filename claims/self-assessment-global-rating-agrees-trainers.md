# First-year medical students' global self-rating of patient-physician communication shows no significant difference from trainers' global rating on a 10-point scale

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the overall 10-point global communication rating, students' self-evaluation did not differ significantly from trainers' evaluation (p > 0.05). [→ Akan Hülya 2025](#akan-hulya-2025)

## Evidence

### Akan Hülya 2025

Akan Hülya, Delibaş Hamza Yasin, Koran Serhat, Yiğit Pakize, Tanriöver Özlem. (2025). Enhancing self-regulated learning: a comparison of medical students’ self-assessment and faculty evaluations on communication skills in simulated patient interviews. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07794-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Dependent-sample comparison of 193 first-year students' self-ratings and trainer ratings on the global 10-point item after a simulated patient interview; means were 7.49 (students) and 7.56 (trainers), with "no significant difference was found between trainers and between students and trainers".

> "The last question, “How would you rate your patient-physician communication in general?” was scored on a 10-point scale and no significant difference was found between trainers and between students and trainers (p > 0.05)."
