Evidence¶

Huang and Hsu 2011¶

Chin-Wen Huang, Chun-Pin Hsu. (2011). Using Online Games To Teach Personal Finance Concepts. American Journal of Business Education – December 2011 Volume 4, Number 12. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056617

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two sessions of Personal Finance at a northeastern public university, 22 non-business majors each, same instructor and exams. Session B used games as homework; session A used traditional essay/problem-solving homework. The midterm means were "62.81, as compared to 77.81 for session B".

"the average midterm score for session A was 62.81, as compared to 77.81 for session B. The average final exam score was 66.22 for session A and 75.63 for session B."
