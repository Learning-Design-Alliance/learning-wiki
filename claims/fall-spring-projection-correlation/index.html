Hafen 2015¶

Hafen, C. A., Ruzek, E. A., Gregory, A., Allen, J. P., & Mikami, A. Y. (2015). Focusing on teacher–student interactions eliminates the negative impact of students’ disruptive behavior on teacher perceptions. International Journal of Behavioral Development. https://doi.org/10.1177/0165025415579455

Preliminary/main analysis of the randomized trial's two-level model in MPlus 7, reporting the within-student stability of teacher projections across the school year: a printed correlation of r = .66, p < .001.

"At the student level, the within-student correlation between the teachers’ projection in the Fall and Spring was strong ( r ¼ .66, p < .001)."
