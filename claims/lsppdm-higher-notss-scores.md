## Evidence

### Pan Ruijun 2024

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

NOTSS ratings in the same live-pig cholecystectomy assessment, n = 30 per group. Mean NOTSS score was 4.25(0.28) versus 3.99(0.27); situation awareness showed no significant difference (t = 0.48, P = 0.630); no effect sizes are printed.

> "Trainees in the pedagogical group performed better in 'Decision making' (t = 2.44, P = 0.018), 'Communication and teamwork' (t = 3.29, P = 0.002), 'Leadership' (t = 2.98, P = 0.004) and 'Mean NOTSS score' (t = 3.49, P = 0.001). There were no differences in situational awareness between the two groups."
