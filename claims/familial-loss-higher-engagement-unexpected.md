## Evidence

### Cruz 2025

Cruz, I. N., Moreno, K. A., Stovall, B., de la Vega, L. P., & Chavira, G. (2025). Connecting the dots: Examining stressful life events, campus climate, and school engagement on academic achievement and psychological distress in a predominantly Latine sample. Journal of Research on Adolescence, 35, e70096. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.70096

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of the four SLE types on school engagement (N = 293). Alongside the negative effect of school-related challenges, "familial loss" emerged as a significant positive predictor (β = .17); the authors describe this as surprising and interpret it via culturally grounded coping strategies supported by a nurturing campus climate.

> "both school‐related challenges (B = −0.69, β = −.45, SE = 0.08, p < .001) and familial loss (B = 0.24, β = .17, SE = 0.08, p = .003) were significant predictors of school engagement"
