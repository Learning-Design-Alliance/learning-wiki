`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 26 studies coding causes of digital distraction into TPE-based categories; Table 7 reports Technology Distractors at 51.95%, Personal Needs at 37.66%, and Instructional Environment Factors at 10.39% of coded causes. The review reports "The Technology Distractors (51.95%) category" as the largest.

> "The Technology Distractors (51.95%) category includes factors related to the use of social media for networking and obtrusive distractors involved in the digital devices usage."

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's narrative synthesis of the 26 studies reports that within technology distractors, "constant social networking and com- munication activities" driven by the need to stay connected were most prevalent, with Internet/smartphone addiction also frequently noted (Table 7: 15.58% each for networking and communication).

> "The most prevalent technology distractors included constant social networking and com- munication activities reported as driven by the need to stay connected."
