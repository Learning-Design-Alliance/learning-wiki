### Wootton 2021

Wootton, J. R., Harkins, F., Bronn, N. T., Carrera Vazquez, A., Phan, A., & Asfaw, A. T. (2021). Teaching quantum computing with an interactive textbook. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1109/qce52317.2021.00058](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce52317.2021.00058)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of weekly view data from July–November 2020, shown in Fig. 4, using the ratio of views for Section 2.5 to Section 1.3 as an estimate of completion. The ratio is "typically around 20%"; no effect size or statistical test is reported.

> "The ratio is typically around 20%, but interestingly shows dips during the same weeks as there are peaks for the views of Section 1.3."
