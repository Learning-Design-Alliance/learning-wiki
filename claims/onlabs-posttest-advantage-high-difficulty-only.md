## Evidence

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-Test comparison in the low- and medium-difficulty groups from the same 43-student study, shown in Fig. 4. The authors state the tutorial "filled successfully the knowledge gaps for the Without-OnLabs students when their tests were of low or medium difficulty". No test statistic is printed.

> "After the face -to-face tutorial the scores in Post-Tests are almost equal for all students  in the 1 st and 2 nd group."

### Evgenia Paxinou 2017 (2)

Evgenia Paxinou, Vasilis Zafeiropoulos, Athanasios Sypsas, Chairi Kiourt, and Dimitris Kalles. (2017). Assessing the Impact of Virtualizing Physical Labs. Hellenic Open University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11502)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-Test comparison in the high-difficulty group of the same study (Fig. 4). The authors report that With-OnLabs students "had also higher scores in their Post-Tests" relative to Without-OnLabs students. No significance test or effect size is reported.

> "In the 3 rd group, where both Pre and Post -Tests are the most difficult, the With-OnLabs students were not only better prepared with the Skype session, but had also higher scores in their Post-Tests."
