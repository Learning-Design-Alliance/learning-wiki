## Evidence

### Sampasa-Kanyinga 2022

Sampasa-Kanyinga, H., Hamilton, H. A., Goldfield, G. S., & Chaput, J.-P. (2022). Problem Technology Use, Academic Performance, and School Connectedness among Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(4), 2337. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19042337

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Ordered logistic regression (Table 3, Model 2, adjusted for age, ethnoracial background, subjective SES, tobacco, alcohol and cannabis use) in the cross-sectional OSDUHS sample. Problem technology use was measured with the 6-item SPIUT (Cronbach's alpha 0.78); moderate-to-serious problem use was defined as scores of 14 or higher. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Having moderate-to-serious symptoms of problem technology use was associated with lower academic performance among males (AOR = 0.68, 95% CI = 0.53–0.87) and females (AOR = 0.66, 95% CI = 0.52–0.84)."
