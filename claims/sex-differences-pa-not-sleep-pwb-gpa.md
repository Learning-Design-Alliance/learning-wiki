### Sanyaolu Olutosin 2026

Relationships Between Physical Activity, Sleep, Psychological Well-Being, and Academic Performance Among Native American College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23040491

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square test in the ACHA-NCHA secondary analysis comparing biological sex with meeting PA guidelines; the printed proportions were 56.72% of males versus 40.55% of females.

> "The results indicated a significant association between biological sex and PA, χ2 (1, N = 1902) = 40.60, p < 0.001. More males than females met the weekly PA guideline. Specifically, 56.72% of males met the guideline, compared to 40.55% of females."

### Sanyaolu Olutosin 2026 (2)

Sanyaolu Olutosin, Reeves-Doyle Brandy, Olaniyan Afolakemi C., Max Tarenina, Asala Adetoun, Osime Esther. (2026). Relationships Between Physical Activity, Sleep, Psychological Well-Being, and Academic Performance Among Native American College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23040491

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Welch's t-test on the ACHA-NCHA secondary sample found no significant PWB difference (females M = 44.50, males M = 43.80). Chi-square tests also found no sex-sleep association (p = 0.550), and a Mann–Whitney U test found no sex difference in academic performance (p = 0.124).

> "The small effect size, d = −0.07, 95% CI: −0.17–0.03, indicates a negligible difference in PWB between males and females."
