## Evidence

### Jewer J 2019

Jewer J, Parsons MH, Dunne C, Smith A, Dubrowski A. (2019). Evaluation of a Mobile Telesimulation Unit to Train Rural and Remote Practitioners on High-Acuity Low-Occurrence Procedures: Pilot Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/14587

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the pilot RCT, the trained groups' modified OSATS checklist scores more than doubled pretest to posttest, while the control group's increase was negligible. The authors report this as indicating similar skill acquisition for remote and face-to-face training.

> "The average scores on the checklist more than doubled from the pretests to posttests for the intervention (from 3 to 6.54) and comparison groups (from 2.96 to 6.22). However, the increase in the scores for the control group was negligible, increasing by only 0.33 points (from 2.91 to 3.24)."
