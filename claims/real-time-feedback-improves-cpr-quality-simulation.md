## Evidence

### Sabery Mahdieh 2026

Sabery Mahdieh, Batooli Zahra, Sabbarifard Ebrahim, Ghaffari Faezeh. (2026). Simulation-based feedback systems in cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a scoping review. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01553-4

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 31 experimental, quasi-experimental and RCT studies published 2006–2022, screened with the Jadad scale (score ≥ 3 required). The review's narrative synthesis reports that "Most studies reported that real-time feedback significantly improved CPR quality" across the four CPR quality parameters.

> "Most studies reported that real-time feedback significantly improved CPR quality, particularly in achieving the recommended compression rate and depth, correct hand placement, and complete chest recoil."
