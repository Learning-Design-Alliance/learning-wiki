# Access to a standard GPT-4 chat tutor during practice substantially improves practice performance in high school mathematics

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students with GPT Base access scored 0.137 higher (out of 1) on assisted practice problems than control students, a 48% improvement over the control mean. [→ Bastani Hamsa 2025](#bastani-hamsa-2025)

## Evidence

### Bastani Hamsa 2025

Bastani Hamsa, Bastani Osbert, Sungu Alp, Ge Haosen, Kabakcı Özge, Mariman Rei. (2025). Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Intention-to-treat regression from the RCT (2,848 observations, classroom-clustered SEs) on assisted practice performance; the GPT Base coefficient was 0.137 (P < 0.01) against a "mean performance of 0.28". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "GPT Base and GPT Tutor substantially increased student scores in the GPT-assisted practice sessions by 0.137 and 0.361 (out of 1), respectively, relative to the control arm that only had access to textbooks (mean performance of 0.28)."
