## Evidence

### Michel H. Villanueva 2023

Michel H. Villanueva, Sudhir Malik, Meirin Oan Evans. (2023). Software Training in High Energy Physics. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2438/1/012063](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2438/1/012063)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Every training format uses a pre-training and post-training survey; the post-training survey "is focused on what has been learnt and compares the skills outcome before and after the training", with Uproot and Git shown as example modules in Figure 4. No effect size is printed.

> "One such example is shown in Figure 4, where a comparison is made of knowledge in Uproot and Git modules before and after the training."

### Michel H. Villanueva 2023 (2)

Michel H. Villanueva, Sudhir Malik, Meirin Oan Evans. (2023). Software Training in High Energy Physics. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2438/1/012063](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2438/1/012063)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The before and after survey samples differ in size, and the authors state that getting everyone to take surveys, "especially after the training", has been a challenge they are trying to address. The authors interpret this as the training transforming participants' skill-sets.

> "Note the diﬀerence in the statistics of people who took the survey before and after the training. Getting everyone to take surveys, especially after the training, has been a challenge ad we are trying to address that."
