## Evidence

### Köksal Adem 2025

Köksal Adem, Aygün Ali, Çaltekin İbrahim, Tomakin Mesut, Sarıbaş Mehmet Seyfettin, Özkay İshak, Tokpinar Adem, Baş Orhan. (2025). The effect of reversed screen orientation versus standard orientation in bedside ultrasound on vascular access skill acquisition: a cadaver study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07899-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

First-attempt comparisons in the randomized cadaver study of 60 novice medical students. The article reports these outcomes "similar between the two groups (p = 0.131, p = 0.229, and p = 0.677, respectively)". No effect sizes printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "The time to complete the procedure, total number of complications, and number of successful punctures were similar between the two groups (p = 0.131, p = 0.229, and p = 0.677, respectively)."

### Köksal Adem 2025 (2)

Köksal Adem, Aygün Ali, Çaltekin İbrahim, Tomakin Mesut, Sarıbaş Mehmet Seyfettin, Özkay İshak, Tokpinar Adem, Baş Orhan. (2025). The effect of reversed screen orientation versus standard orientation in bedside ultrasound on vascular access skill acquisition: a cadaver study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07899-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Second-attempt comparisons after crossover in the same cadaver study. The article reports time and successful punctures "similar between the groups (p = 0.1129 and p = 0.251, respectively)". No effect sizes printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "The time to complete the procedure and number of successful punctures were similar between the groups (p = 0.1129 and p = 0.251, respectively)."
