## Evidence

### Moliner 2020

Moliner, Alegre. (2020). Effects of peer tutoring on middle school students’ mathematics self-concepts. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231410

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

T-tests comparing gain scores between experimental and control groups, reported in Table 5. Mean differences ranged from 0.41 to 0.53 with t-values of 1.99* to 3.30* (p < .05), showing "Statistically significant differences were reported individually for each grade and also overall."

> "Statistically significant differences were reported individually for each grade and also overall."

### Moliner 2020 (2)

Moliner, Alegre. (2020). Effects of peer tutoring on middle school students’ mathematics self-concepts. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231410

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Effect sizes calculated from gain scores of the experimental group (n = 188), reported in the Results section. Grade-specific Hedges' g values were 0.40, 0.41, and 0.37; "The global effect size for the peer tutoring program reported a Hedge’s g value of 0.48."

> "Calculation of effect sizes showed a Hedge’s g value of 0.40 for 7th graders, 0.41 for 8th graders, and 0.37 for 9th graders. The global effect size for the peer tutoring program reported a Hedge’s g value of 0.48."
