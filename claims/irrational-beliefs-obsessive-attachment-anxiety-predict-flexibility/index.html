Evidence¶

Bülent GÜNDÜZ 2013¶

Bülent GÜNDÜZ. (2013). The Contributions of Attachment Styles, Irrational Beliefs and Psychological Symptoms to the Prediction of Cognitive Flexibility. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2079-2085. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2013.4.1702

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Stepwise regression analysis in the same 436-student correlational study. Irrational beliefs entered first (12% of variance); later steps added negligent attachment (14%), secure attachment (16%), somatization (20%), obsessive attachment (30%), anxiety (38%), depression (39%) and negative ego (40%). The article states "30% per cent of cognitive flexibility are explained by the variables of irrational beliefs, obsessed attachment and anxiety"; no standardized coefficients are printed.

"According to the findings of stepwise regression analysis, it is found out that the variables of irrational beliefs explain 12% per cent of total variance"
