Tom Zhang, Michelle Taub, and Zhongzhou Chen. (2021). Measuring the Impact of COVID-19 Induced Campus Closure on Student Self-Regulated Learning in Physics Online Learning Modules. Learning Analytics and Knowledge '21. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1145/3448139.3448150](https://doi.org/10.1145/3448139.3448150)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of linear models of the fraction of first AC attempts under 15 seconds per module (Table 4 reports F (slope) 23.034*** for 20A-B). The discussion notes a similar trend in Fall 2019, which the authors attribute partly to content difficulty or end-of-semester engagement decline unrelated to closure.

> "In 2020, the proportion of Short First Attempts increased significantly more rapidly in Segment B than in Segment A. This rapid shift in slope is not detected in data from Fall 2019, for which there was no significant difference observed between the intercepts of the regression lines in each segment."
