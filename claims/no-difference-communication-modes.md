## Evidence

### Lara-Alecio 2024

Lara-Alecio, R., Irby, B. J., Tong, F., Sutton-Jones, K. L., Guerrero, C. L., Tang, S., & Pang, H. (2024). Evaluating Pedagogical Practices in Science Classrooms: A Randomized Controlled Trial Study on Teacher Virtual Professional Development with Virtual Mentoring and Coaching. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 16(4), 479-494. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2024.347

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result in the communication mode domain from the POP-coded RCT observations; the printed percentages are descriptive and no standardized effect size is reported. Equivalence was not tested.

> "the following student communication behaviors were the most frequently observed in both conditions: aural (32.3% in control, 32.6% in treatment), and in combinations of verbal and aural communication (41.5% in control, 42.6% in treatment). No significant difference was  identified in these communication modes."
