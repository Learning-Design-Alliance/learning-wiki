## Evidence

### Redish 2012

Redish, E. F. (2012). The role of context and culture in teaching physics: The implication of disciplinary differences. Keynote address, World Conference on Physics Education 2012, Istanbul. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1207.4840](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1207.4840)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Classroom demonstration in which students memorize a 24-word list and free-recall it; many confidently report non-presented test words. The author states "typically more than half of the students" show this false recall, illustrating that memory is reconstructed from bits and plausible stock items.

> "When I give this task to my class, typically more than half of the students put one or both of the test words on their list and are shocked to discover that they weren't there."
