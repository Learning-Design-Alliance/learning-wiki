## Evidence

### Schleepen TMJ 2016

Schleepen TMJ, Van Mier HI, De Smedt B (2016) The Contribution of Numerical Magnitude Comparison and Phonological Processing to Individual Differences in Fourth Graders' Multiplication Fact Ability. PLoS ONE 11(6): e0158335. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0158335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the multiplication task (N = 63, 56 single-digit problems): paired t-tests comparing small vs. large problems showed the problem-size effect, t(62) = -6.59 for RT and t(62) = -5.54 for accuracy, both p < .001.

> "Both for reaction time and accuracy, the typical problem-size effect was found, showing increased reaction times and decreased accuracy rates for large vs. small multiplication problems (reaction time: t(62) = -6.59, p < .001; accuracy: t(62) = -5.54, p < .001, respectively)."
