## Evidence

### Kusumawati 2017

Kusumawati, E., & Widiati, U. (2017). The Effects of Vocabulary Instructions on Students’ Reading Comprehension across Cognitive Styles in ESP. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(2). https://journals.iiste.org/index.php/JEP/article/view/2894

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study of 54 Mechatronics students at the Electronic Engineering Polytechnic Institute of Surabaya; independent samples t-test and one-way ANOVA showed the explicit group “outperformed the group receiving implicit vocabulary instruction in comprehending ESP reading.” Posttest means were 77.4074 (EVI) vs 67.6852 (IVI).

> "Independent samples t-test and one way ANOVA used in analyzing the data revealed that the group receiving explicit vocabulary instruction outperformed the group receiving implicit vocabulary instruction in comprehending ESP reading."
