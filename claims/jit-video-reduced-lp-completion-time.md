# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, median LP completion time among EM interns fell from 12.9 to 10.3 minutes after viewing a brief just-in-time instructional video

## Evidence

### McGuire SS 2024

McGuire SS, Finch AS, Thomas JM, Lazaro O, Hevesi SA, Mullan AF, Homme JL. (2024). Improving emergency medicine resident pediatric lumbar puncture procedural performance through a brief just-in-time video intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05654-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot quasi-experimental study of all 9 EM interns in one academic Midwestern ED, comparing timed simulator LP performance before and after a JIT video. Interns "completed the procedure faster with a median time of 10.3 (9.7–11.3) minutes (p = 0.004)" versus 12.9 minutes at baseline.

> "Post-intervention, interns had logged an additional median of 4 (0–11) LPs, including median 2 (IQR 0–5) logged as “performed” and completed the procedure faster with a median time of 10.3 (9.7–11.3) minutes (p = 0.004)."
