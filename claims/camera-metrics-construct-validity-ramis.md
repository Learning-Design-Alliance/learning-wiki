## Evidence

### Jarc Anthony M. 2017

Jarc Anthony M., Curet Myriam J. (2017). Viewpoint matters: objective performance metrics for surgeon endoscope control during robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-5090-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational simulation study in which 39 RAMIS surgeons (18 new, 8 intermediate, 13 experienced) each completed 25 da Vinci Skills Simulator exercises once; Student's t tests compared group means per exercise. The abstract reports "camera movement frequency 23/25, camera movement duration 22/25, camera movement interval 19/25" significant comparisons.

> "Both camera metrics and efficiency metrics showed construct validity (p < 0.05) across most exercises (camera movement frequency 23/25, camera movement duration 22/25, camera movement interval 19/25, overall score 24/25, completion time 25/25)."
