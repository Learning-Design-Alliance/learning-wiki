## Evidence

### Bensouda Brahim 2018

Bensouda Brahim, Mandel Romain, Mejri Abdelwaheb, Lachapelle Jean, St-Hilaire Marie, Ali Nabeel. (2018). Effect of an audience on trainee stress and performance during simulated neonatal intubation: a randomized crossover trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1338-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized crossover trial of 49 first-year residents, each serving as own control under condition A (staff only) and condition B (audience of five). The paired t-test on the within-subject mean difference in intubation time gave p = 0.07, with means of "30.9 (27.7, 34.1)" seconds under condition A and 34.8 seconds under condition B.

> "Time to intubation (sec), mean (95% CI)	30.9 (27.7, 34.1)	34.8 (31.7, 37.9)	−3.94 (− 8.2, 0.4)	0.07"
