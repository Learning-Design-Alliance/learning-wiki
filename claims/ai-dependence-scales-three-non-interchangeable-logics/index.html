Evidence¶

Yan D 2026¶

Yan D (2026). A critical narrative synthesis of psychological correlates, measurement, and reported findings on conversational AI engagement and dependence-related constructs. Front. Psychol. 17:1827795. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1827795

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's psychometric synthesis (Section 3.3, Table 3) of instruments developed 2024-2026, finding that "Addiction-component instruments emphasize impaired control and harm" while attachment and reliance instruments target different content. Evidence for invariance, temporal stability, and discriminant validity remains uneven.

"The measures follow three broad logics. Addiction-component instruments emphasize impaired control and harm. Attachment instruments emphasize emotional closeness, anxiety, avoidance, or social substitution. Reliance instruments emphasize cognitive outsourcing and task delegation."
