Coding of analytical methods across the included studies (methods in three or more studies only). The review found "descriptive statistics being the dominant category (N = 74, 53.6%)", with process mining second (N = 19), and standard statistics persisting even where multiple data streams were combined.

"descriptive statistics being the dominant category (N = 74, 53.6%) across years and approaches. Notably, this prevalence of standard statistics is also observed in the integrated approach (N = 19 studies, 13.8%)"
