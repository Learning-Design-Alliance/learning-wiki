Evidence¶

Joep van der Graaf 2015¶

Joep van der Graaf, Eliane Segers, Ludo Verhoeven. (2015). Scientific reasoning abilities in kindergarten: dynamic assessment of the control of variables strategy. Instr Sci 43:381–400. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-015-9344-y

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Independent-samples t-tests comparing K1 and K2 children on CVS and nonverbal reasoning scores. K2 children scored higher on both CVS scores with large printed effect sizes (Cohen's d of 1.21 and 1.22); nonverbal reasoning showed a medium effect size (d = .60, p = .049).

"The differences in the K1 versus K2 children's CVS scores were large, as indicated by a Cohen's d of 1.21 for experiment correct scores and a Cohen's d of 1.22 for variable correct scores, while nonverbal rea-soning scores showed a medium effect size"
