Evidence¶

Rina Oktaviyanthi 2015¶

Rina Oktaviyanthi, Yani Supriani. (2015). Experimental Design: Utilizing Microsoft Mathematics in Teaching and Learning Calculus. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.25. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1078520

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with 22 first-year students randomly divided into an experimental group taught with Microsoft Mathematics and a control group taught conventionally. Post-test means were 79.4545 versus 73.3636; the article reports "a difference is 6.09" as a significant result, printing no effect size.

"In Table7 noted that the higher mean is earned by the experimental group with a difference is 6.09. This illustrates a significant result for the study that the experimental group had better performance than the control group in the post test."
