## Evidence

### Cai L 2022

Cai L, Li Yl, Hu Xy, Li R, Khoshnood A. (2022). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with case-based learning: a promising and effective teaching modality in undergraduate pathology education. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000028782

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment assigning 117 third-year clinical medicine students to FC (n = 59) or LBC (n = 58) groups with demographic match; independent samples t test on post-class quiz scores. The FC group "gained higher scores (the average of 2 quizzes)" with P < .001. No effect size printed.

> "the students in the FC group gained higher scores (the average of 2 quizzes) in the post-class quizzes than those in the LBC group (78.73 ± 1.53 vs 65.52 ± 1.48, P < .001)"
