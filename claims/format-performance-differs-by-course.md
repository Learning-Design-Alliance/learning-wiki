### Karamdokht Parya 2026

Karamdokht Parya, Babaee Hemmati Yasamin, Dadgaran Ideh, Falahchai Mehran. (2026). Item analysis and performance of EMQ, MCQ, and SAQ in dental education and students’ perception of extended-matching questions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09791-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated measures analysis of performance by course in the 29-student cohort (Table 3). The format effect held in pediatric dentistry, endodontics, and prosthodontics but not orthodontics, a scope limit on the format effect.

> "there was no significant difference among the performance scores of the three types of questions only in the orthodontics course (P = 0.948). In the remaining three courses, there was a significant difference among the three question formats in terms of performance score (P < 0.05, Table 3)"
