### Kothe Christian 2013

Kothe Christian, Hissbach Johanna, Hampe Wolfgang. (2013). The Hamburg Selection Procedure for Dental Students – Introduction of the HAM-Nat as subject-specific test for study aptitude. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000889](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000889)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression models with GPA alone as predictor across four cohorts; "Grade point average mostly explained less than 5% of the academic performance variance of subjects in the first two semesters" and the NVP, with exceptions such as R2=0.204 for the WiSe 2006/07 chemistry course.

> "Grade point average mostly explained less than 5% of the academic performance variance of subjects in the first two semesters (see Table 3 (Tab. 3)) and the NVP (see Table 4 (Tab. 4)), but there were also some prominent coefficients of determinations, e.g. for the chemistry course of the cohort WiSe 2006/07 (R2=0.204)."
