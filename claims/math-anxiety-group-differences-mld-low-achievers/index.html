Evidence¶

Yılmaz Mutlu 2019¶

Yılmaz Mutlu. (2019). Math Anxiety in Students With and Without Math Learning Difficulties. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 11(5), 471-475. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2019553343

q3 · i? · associational · r1

One-way ANOVA comparing math anxiety scores of four achievement groups (MLD 0-10%, LA 11-25%, NA 26-95%, HA 96-100%) classified by achievement test scores. "a statistically significant difference between groups" was found, F(3,284)= 36.584, p= .000.

"there is a statistically significant difference between groups as determined at the p<.05 level by one-way ANOVA (F(3,284)= 36.584, p= .000)"

Yılmaz Mutlu 2019 (2)¶

Yılmaz Mutlu. (2019). Math Anxiety in Students With and Without Math Learning Difficulties. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 11(5), 471-475. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2019553343

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Tukey post hoc comparisons following the significant ANOVA. The MLD group (M= 1.96) differed significantly from the NA and HA groups; the quote continues that the MLD group "did not significantly differ from the LA group (M= 1.76, sd= 0.27)".

"Post hoc comparisons using the Tukey test indicated that the mean score for MLD group ( M= 1.96, sd= 0.30) was significantly different than the NA group ( M= 1.41, sd= 0.84) and HA group (M= 1.24, sd= 0.28)."
