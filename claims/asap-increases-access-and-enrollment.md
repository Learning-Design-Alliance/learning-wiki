# ASAP likely increases college access and enrollment rates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In two RCTs meeting WWC standards, ASAP produced a positive, statistically significant effect on access and enrollment, with a domain-average effect size of 0.34 and improvement index of +13. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
`q4 i2` In the Scrivener et al. (2015) RCT, 25.1 percent of ASAP students enrolled in a four-year college by the sixth semester versus 17.3 percent of comparison students (effect size 0.29, p = .004). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
