# Included VRS simulation studies show low instrument-validity reporting, the lowest-scoring MERSQI domain

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Validity of the evaluation instrument was the lowest-scoring MERSQI domain, with only one study adequately reporting all three validity items. [→ Rasmussen Rasmus Christian 2019](#rasmussen-rasmus-christian-2019)

## Evidence

### Rasmussen Rasmus Christian 2019

Rasmussen Rasmus Christian, Grauslund Jakob, Vergmann Anna Stage. (2019). Simulation training in vitreoretinal surgery: a systematic review. BMC Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12886-019-1098-x

`q2 · i?`

MERSQI quality assessment of the seven included studies performed by the review authors. The highest scoring domains were type of data and data analysis; one study scored no points on instrument validity.

> "When assessing individual item scores, validity of the evaluated instruments in the included studies was low. Thomsen et al. [22] was the only study to adequately report data for all three items."
