# Acquisition of a novel paper-and-pencil matrix task follows a power function of practice at group and individual levels

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Individual learning curves over 20 matrix repetitions fit a power function, with a median individual R2 of 0.68 in the analyzed sample of 125 children. [→ Marinelli CV 2021](#marinelli-cv-2021)
`q3 i?` Response times improved significantly across the 20 repetitions of the experimental task. [→ Marinelli CV 2021 (2)](#marinelli-cv-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Marinelli CV 2021

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual power-function fits to the paper-and-pencil matrix task in fifth graders: the analyzed sample "showed a time reduction according to a power function with a median R2 = 0.68"; twelve children with R2 below 0.3 were excluded.

> "The remaining sample of 125 children showed a time reduction according to a power function with a median R2 = 0.68 (range 0.3–0.93)."

### Marinelli CV 2021 (2)

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on response times across the 20 matrix repetitions: the article reports "a significant learning effect across the 20 repetitions", F(19, 2,356) = 361.21, p < 0.0001.

> "an ANOVA with repetition indicated a significant learning effect across the 20 repetitions ( F(19, 2,356)  = 361.21; p < 0.0001)"
