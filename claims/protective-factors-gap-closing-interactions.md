# Protective factors attenuate the effects of risk as risk rises, producing gap-closing effects represented analytically as Risk × Protective Factor interactions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Protective factors buffer against negative outcomes by diminishing the impact of risk factors, with the higher risk group showing more benefit than the lower risk group; they are represented as interaction terms in linear models. [→ Slomowitz R. F. 2025](#slomowitz-r-f-2025)

## Evidence

### Slomowitz R. F. 2025

Slomowitz R. F., Narayan A. J., & McGrath L. M. (2025). Resilience research in learning disabilities: Guiding principles from developmental psychopathology. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102090

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Definitional statement in this theoretical paper: protective factors contribute to a "gap-closing" effect between higher and lower risk groups and are "analytically represented as interaction terms in linear models (Risk x Protective Factor)". No effect size is printed.

> "Protective factors partially or completely attenuate the effects of risk factors on outcomes, specifically as the level of risk rises."
