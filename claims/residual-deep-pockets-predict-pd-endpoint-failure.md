## Evidence

### Herz Marco M. 2025

Herz Marco M., Schamuhn Judith, Krumm Boris, Bartha Valentin. (2025). Student-performed periodontal therapy: retrospective cohort study on outcomes and related recommendations for enhancing undergraduate periodontal education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07699-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nominal regression analysis of factors influencing the PD-based treat-to-target endpoint in the retrospective cohort of 107 student-treated patients. Table 3 shows PD ≥ 6 mm (n) (T0) with OR 0.98, p < 0.001 for the T1 PD endpoint, and PD ≥ 6 mm (n) (T1) with OR 0.69, p < 0.001 for the T2 PD endpoint, the latter "remained significant across models".

> "EP2 at T1 was achieved by 29% of all patients and influenced by the number of PD ≥ 6 mm at T0 (OR0.98, p < 0.001)."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`
