## Evidence

### Sten Ludvigsen 2018

Sten Ludvigsen, Ulrike Cress, Carolyn P. Rosé, Nancy Law, & Gerry Stahl. (2018). Developing understanding beyond the given knowledge and new methodologies for analyses in CSCL. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 13, 359–364. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9291-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The editorial reports, from Ingulfsen et al.'s detailed interaction analysis of an ocean acidification experiment with real-time labs, that students struggling with graphs needed "both procedural and conceptual support" and that the teacher used "eliciting and elaboration strategies" and contextual guidance.

> "The teacher serves as an adaptive expert whose input closed cognitive, social, and design gaps in order to support the students’ understanding of real-time labs."
