Evidence¶

James Soland 2021¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th grade: The role of social-emotional learning trajectories. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12463

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal study fitting growth models on four years of SEL data from students in a large urban district, about 3,000 students per timepoint. The article reports that "status is more frequently significant" than growth as a predictor of being off track in 9th grade; no effect size is printed for this comparison.

"Results indicate that, while status is more frequently significant, growth for self-management is also predictive above and beyond status on that construct."
