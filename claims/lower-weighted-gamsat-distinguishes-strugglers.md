## Evidence

### Garrud Paul 2012

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U tests (used because score distributions were skewed, Kolmogorov-Smirnov ps < 0.01) compared admission GAMSAT scores of the study and comparison groups. The weighted mean overall score was significantly lower; the unweighted mean was non-significant (65.0 vs 66.1, p = 0.077).

> "significantly lower for the weighted mean overall score (64.4 vs 66.1, z = −2.589, p = 0.010)"

### Garrud Paul 2012 (2)

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the three separate GAMSAT section scores in the same retrospective comparison. Section 3 showed a non-significant trend (Strugglers 63.28 ± 9.56 vs Comparison 65.99 ± 9.64); sections 1 and 2 showed no difference (p = 0.72, 0.99).

> "a non-significant trend toward lower scores on section 3 (reasoning in biological and physical sciences; means and SDs: Strugglers 63.28 ± 9.56, Comparison group 65.99 ± 9.64; p = 0.06)"
