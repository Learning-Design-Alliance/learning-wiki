`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive group comparison in the results section: "Experts consistently performed faster than non-experts" on insertion times, with the gap shrinking from 61.7 s in run 1 to 15.1 s at retention. Authors suggest this indicates the simulator trains skills relevant to real surgical performance.

> "Experts consistently performed faster than non-experts (Run 1: 89.1 s vs. 150.8 s; Run 2: 93.6 s vs. 97.4 s; Run 3: 67.0 s vs. 72.6 s; Retention: 56.2 s vs. 71.3 s), with the performance gap decreasing across runs."
