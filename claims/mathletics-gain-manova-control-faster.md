## Evidence

### Letwinsky 2017

Letwinsky, K. M., & Berry, M. D. (2017). Technology Integration and the Effect on Mathematics Fact Fluency in the Middle East. Journal of International Education and Leadership, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1153618

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent-groups study of 28 fourth graders (control n = 14; treatment n = 14) using Math Fact Master pre/posttests. The gain-score MANOVA was significant, "partial η2 = .21", and follow-up ANOVA showed only completion time reached significance (F(1, 26) = 6.94, p < .025, partial η2 = .21).

> "Using gain scores to detect significant differences in the between group posttest dependent variables, the MANOVA results revealed significant differences among the groups on the combined dependent variables (F(2, 25) = 3.40, p < .05; Wilks' λ = .79; partial η2 = .21)."
