## Evidence

### Xiaoxia Wang 2018

Xiaoxia Wang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Tianhao Huang, Renqiang He, Weina Hao, Li Zhang. (2018). Development and validation of Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory for Chinese medical college students (CTDI-M). Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis (principal component analysis with varimax rotation) of the closed-ended questionnaire in 442 valid surveys. KMO was 0.943 and Bartlett's test suggested "the appropriateness of the factor analysis model"; the three factors were named open-mindedness, systematicity/analyticity, and truth seeking.

> "The factor analysis identified a three-factor model, with the eigenvalues of 3 factors exceeded one, accounting for 57.66% of extraction sums of squared loadings"
