Evidence¶

Adesope 2017¶

Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017). Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of 272 independent effect sizes from 118 articles with 15,427 participants. The fixed-effects model showed "the overall weighted mean effect size was moderately large and statistically significant" (g = 0.61, p < .001); the random-effects model showed g = 0.70, p < .001, with high heterogeneity (I2 = .81).

"Under a fixed-effects model, Table 1 shows that the overall weighted mean effect size was moderately large and statistically significant, indicating the effectiveness of learning with practice tests (g = 0.61, p < .001)."
