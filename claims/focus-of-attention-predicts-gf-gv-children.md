## Evidence

### Gray Shelley 2016

Gray Shelley, Green Samuel, Alt Mary, Hogan Tiffany P., Kuo Trudy, Brinkley Shara, Cowan Nelson. (2016). The Structure of Working Memory in Young Children and Its Relation to Intelligence. Journal of Memory and Language. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5157932/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling incorporating four KABC-II nonverbal intelligence subtests into the best-fitting working memory models in the same sample. "only the focus of attention factor was significantly related to the two intelligence factors" in the Cowan model.

> "When all three working memory factors were allowed to predict the intelligence factors, only the focus of attention factor was significantly related to the two intelligence factors for the Cowan model (in keeping with Cowan et al., 2005)."

### Gray Shelley 2016 (2)

Gray Shelley, Green Samuel, Alt Mary, Hogan Tiffany P., Kuo Trudy, Brinkley Shara, Cowan Nelson. (2016). The Structure of Working Memory in Young Children and Its Relation to Intelligence. Journal of Memory and Language. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5157932/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of the Cowan model factors with Gf and Gv factors in the same sample. The phonological storage and rehearsal factor was "nonsignificantly and very weakly related to the intelligence factors," a null finding for storage-driven accounts.

> "As expected, the correlations between the phonological storage and rehearsal factor were nonsignificantly and very weakly related to the intelligence factors."
