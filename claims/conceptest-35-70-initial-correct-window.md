# ConcepTests with roughly 35% to 70% of students answering correctly before discussion appear to produce the highest engagement and most effective discussions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Based on the authors' teaching observation, an initial correct response rate of roughly 35% to 70% prior to discussion seems to lead to the highest degree of engagement and the most effective discussions. [→ Crouch 2003](#crouch-2003)

## Evidence

### Crouch 2003

Crouch, C. H., Watkins, J., Fagen, A. P., & Mazur, E. (2003). Peer Instruction: Engaging Students One-on-One, All At Once. Research-Based Reform of University Physics. https://scholar.google.com

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' observation from teaching with ConcepTests at Harvard and Swarthmore, not a controlled test: an initial correct response "of roughly 35% to 70% prior to discussion seems to lead to the highest degree of engagement." Below 35% questions may be ambiguous; above 70% discussion offers little benefit.

> "we have observed that an initial correct response of roughly 35% to 70% prior to discussion seems to lead to the highest degree of engagement and the most effective discussions."
