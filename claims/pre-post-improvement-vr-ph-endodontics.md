## Evidence

### Srakoopun Chalinee 2025

Srakoopun Chalinee, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Haddawy Peter, Kaluschke Maximilian, Weller Rene, Yin Myat Su, Aguilar Panuroot, Phumpatrakom Panupat, Pinchamnankool Kriangkrai, Budsaba Kamon, Zachmann Gabriel. (2025). Virtual reality simulation for learning minimally invasive endodontics: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07889-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 30 fourth-year preclinical dental students (15 VR, 15 phantom head) with three 1-hour training sessions and pre/post assessments on TradAC and ConsAC preparations. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in Table 2 show p-values from 0.001 to 0.04 for all pre-post comparisons, indicating "improved across all outcomes in each training and technique combination."

> "Post-training performance (mean ± SD) improved across all outcomes in each training and technique combination. Exact p-values for these comparisons are provided in Table 2."
