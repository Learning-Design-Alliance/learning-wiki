Evidence¶

Carolyn Penstein Rosé & Oliver Ferschke 2016¶

Carolyn Penstein Rosé & Oliver Ferschke. (2016). Technology Support for Discussion Based Learning: From Computer Supported Collaborative Learning to the Future of Massive Open Online Courses. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0107-y

q2 · i? · design · r2

Early observational results from the Fall 2014 dual-layer MOOC (DALMOOC) on edX with the ProSolo layer, run as part of a design-based research process. Learners "experience some uncertainty and discomfort in transitioning from instructor-driven to self-regulated learning" and the authors conclude better scaffolding is needed.

"Early results from DALMOOC indicate that learners experience some uncertainty and discomfort in transitioning from instructor-driven to self-regulated learning. Learners within the edX course found the distributed structure of DALMOOC confusing at times. Better scaffolding and support is needed to assist learners in transitioning between structured instruction and open engagement in social systems"
