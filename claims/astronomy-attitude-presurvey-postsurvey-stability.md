## Evidence

### Moire K. M. Prescott 2024

Moire K. M. Prescott, Laura Madson, Sandra M. Way, Kelly N. Sanderson. (2024). Prevalence of a growth mindset among introductory astronomy students. arXiv:2407.06147 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010140)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of presurvey versus postsurvey composite scores across five semesters, reported for all students and by age, gender, and race/ethnicity. The article reports "moderate Spearman's correlation coefficients of ρ = 0.5–0.6" with significant correlations at p < 0.05 shown in Table X.

> "Overall, we find that the pre- and postsurvey responses are largely consistent with one another for the affinity, competence, difficulty, and mindset beliefs scores, showing moderate Spearman's correlation coefficients of ρ = 0.5–0.6"
