## Evidence

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Simultaneous-entry multiple linear regression on the 348-student sample; the overall model was significant (F = 23.512, p < 0.001) and "explained 28.1% of the variance in academic achievement (adjusted R² = 0.281)".

> "SOC (β = 0.496, p < 0.001), female gender (β = 0.129, p = 0.005), and nursing major (β = 0.127, p = 0.036) emerged as significant independent predictors of academic achievement."

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026 (2)

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise regression performed to confirm robustness of the simultaneous model in the same sample; nursing major and LOC did not remain in the final stepwise model.

> "This model confirmed SOC (β = 0.529, p < 0.001) and female gender (β = 0.133, p = 0.004) as the final predictors, accounting for 28.3% of the variance (Adj. R² = 0.283)."
