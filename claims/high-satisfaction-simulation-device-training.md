Salomez-Ihl Cordélia, Chapuis Claire, Bedouch Pierrick, Albaladejo Pierre, Picard Julien. (2025). Assessment of a simulation-based implementation method to support the introduction of a new invasive device: a prospective cross-over study. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003029

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-questionnaire data from the cross-over study: 54 caregivers answered the training-satisfaction questions, all satisfied (mean satisfaction level 9.60/10), and all 51 users who responded were confident about using the new IABPS in the clinic after training. These are descriptive self-report results with no effect size.

> "100% of caregivers who answered to these questions (n=54) believe that this training will help them use the device more effectively."
