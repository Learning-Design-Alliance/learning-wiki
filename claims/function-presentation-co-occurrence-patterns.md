`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative interpretation of the prompt type co-occurrence network (Fig. 5), describing which function labels combined with which presentation labels. The article also notes question prompts co-occurring with metacognitive and reflection prompts, suggesting questions may be seen as suitable for eliciting metacognitive processes.

> "For example, self-explanation prompts co-occurred with labels referring to response format (e.g., open, focused, menu-based) and adaptive presentation (e.g., fading). Metacognitive prompts also co-occurred with adaptive prompts. Self-regulated learning prompts showed co-occurrence with embedded and video prompts"
