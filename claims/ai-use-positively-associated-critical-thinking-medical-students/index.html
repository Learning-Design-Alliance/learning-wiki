Evidence¶

Arshad A 2026¶

Arshad A, Lone A, Arickswamy L, Hassan K, Alnaim AA and AlFarhan MF (2026) From AI use to critical thinking among medical students: a moderated mediation perspective on cognitive load and self-regulated learning. Front. Psychol. 17:1883053. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1883053

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational survey of 480 undergraduate medical students with prior AI experience, using Pearson correlations. AI use correlated with critical thinking at "r = 0.46, p < 0.01" and with self-regulated learning at r = 0.48, p < 0.01.

"The use of AI in educational technology was found to be positively correlated with both critical thinking (r = 0.46, p < 0.01) and self-regulated learning ( r = 0.48, p < 0.01)."
