# Fund-supported principals plan fewer total years in the principalship but more years in education than other principals

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When current experience is added to planned future years, Fund-supported principals plan to spend three fewer years in the principalship than other principals, but plan more additional years in education. [→ Hart 2008](#hart-2008)

## Evidence

### Hart 2008

Hart, H. M., Sporte, S. E., Ponisciak, S. M., Stevens, W. D., & Cambronne, A. (2008). Teacher and Principal Leadership in Chicago: Ongoing Analyses of Preparation Programs. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-and-principal-leadership-chicago-ongoing-analyses-preparation-programs

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey analysis of CCSR 2007 principal survey future-plans items, collapsing elementary and high school principals together (65 Fund-supported, 159 other principals answering all three questions). Fund-supported principals also plan two additional years as school leaders and four additional years in education.

> "However, when we add time already spent as a principal to respondents' expectations for the future, Fund-supported principals plan to spend three fewer years in the principalship than do other leaders."
