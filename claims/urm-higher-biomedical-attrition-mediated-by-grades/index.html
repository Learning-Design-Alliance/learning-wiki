Evidence¶

Rosenzweig 2021¶

Rosenzweig, E. Q., Harackiewicz, J. M., Hecht, C. A., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., Asher, M. W., & Hyde, J. S. (2021). College Students’ Reasons for Leaving Biomedical Fields: Disenchantment with Biomedicine or Attraction to Other Fields? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000456

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Demographic-only logistic regression predicting attrition in the 1193-student sample. The article reports "OR = 1.25" with 22.5% of URM students leaving versus 15.0% of majority students.

"URM students were more likely to leave biomedical fields, b = 0.22, z = 2.16, p = .03, OR = 1.25; 22.5% of URM students left biomedical fields as compared to 15.0% of majority students."

Rosenzweig 2021 (2)¶

Rosenzweig, E. Q., Harackiewicz, J. M., Hecht, C. A., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., Asher, M. W., & Hyde, J. S. (2021). College Students’ Reasons for Leaving Biomedical Fields: Disenchantment with Biomedicine or Attraction to Other Fields? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000456

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Regression analyses testing group differences on the psychological variables. URM students' lower grades (β = −0.18, p < .001) but not lower interest (β = −0.02, p = .495) supported the grades pathway.

"Results suggested that URM students did not report significantly lower interest in Biology, β = −0.02, p = .495, but they did receive significantly lower grades, β = −0.18, p < .001 (see Table 8)."
