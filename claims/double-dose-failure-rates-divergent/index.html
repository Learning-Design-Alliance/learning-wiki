Takako Nomi 2010¶

Takako Nomi, Elaine M. Allensworth. (2010). The Effects of Tracking with Supports on Instructional Climate and Student Outcomes in High School Algebra. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/effects-tracking-supports-instructional-climate-and-student-outcomes-high-school

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Log-odds failure models with percentage-point interpretations, two-cohort discontinuity sample. Including classroom variables reduced the above-norm increase to one percentage point, no longer significant (0.08 logits, p>0.1).

"For above-norm students, failure rates increased post-policy by about 3 percentage points despite improvements in test scores (Model 1). In comparison, students just below the policy cut off score had lower failure rates post-policy, by 4 percentage points, compared to their pre-policy counterparts"

Takako Nomi 2010 (2)¶

Takako Nomi, Elaine M. Allensworth. (2010). The Effects of Tracking with Supports on Instructional Climate and Student Outcomes in High School Algebra. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/effects-tracking-supports-instructional-climate-and-student-outcomes-high-school

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Model 2 of the failure regressions. Interactive pedagogy related to lower failure only after controlling for academic demand; failure was unrelated to the percent of classmates taking support courses (Model 5).

"below-norm and above-norm students were more likely to fail in classes with greater academic demand and especially when in classrooms with more students with disciplinary problems."
