# Lower REACH ratings predicted teacher exit: more than half of unsatisfactory-rated teachers left CPS and low-VAM teachers moved out of tested grades/subjects

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The lower the evaluation rating, the more likely teachers were to leave their schools; more than one-half of unsatisfactory-rated teachers exited CPS and an additional 16 percent switched schools within CPS. [→ Sartain 2020](#sartain-2020)
