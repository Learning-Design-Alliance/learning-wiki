## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Azer Samy A 2013

Azer Samy A, Hasanato Rana, Al-Nassar Sami, Somily Ali, AlSaadi Muslim M. (2013). Introducing integrated laboratory classes in a PBL curriculum: impact on student's learning and satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-71

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Five-point Likert questionnaire completed at the end of each class, with response rates of 93%, 95%, 89%, and 92% for the four classes and Cronbach alpha values between 0.70 and 0.86. Table 5 reports, for example, 61.1% strongly agreeing they enjoyed working on cases in the CSF class.

> "The questionnaire indicated that most students expressed positive attitude towards working on tasks and applying knowledge learnt. Students also felt that conducting laboratory procedures and interpreting laboratory findings were valuable to their learning."

### Azer Samy A 2013 (2)

Azer Samy A, Hasanato Rana, Al-Nassar Sami, Somily Ali, AlSaadi Muslim M. (2013). Introducing integrated laboratory classes in a PBL curriculum: impact on student's learning and satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-71

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary analysis of questionnaire responses by gender using the Mann–Whitney U test for ordinal data; the article reports no significant differences and no effect sizes.

> "Perceptions were evaluated on the basis of gender and no significant differences were found. The response rates were 93% for the first laboratory class, 95% for the second, 89% for the third class, and 92% for the fourth class."
