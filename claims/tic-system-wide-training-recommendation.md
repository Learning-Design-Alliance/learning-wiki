### Itani Mohammad S. 2026

Itani Mohammad S., Shankar Megha, Koenig Mary Dawn, Johnson Sara, Goldstein Ellen. (2026). Prenatal clinicians’ perspectives on trauma-informed care competency and training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09347-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 23 prenatal clinicians found that "participants emphasized that all members of the healthcare workforce should receive TIC training". One participant stated "if you want a trauma-informed environment, everyone’s got to get trained, right? It’s got to be front desk, security, nursing, clinicians, administration".
