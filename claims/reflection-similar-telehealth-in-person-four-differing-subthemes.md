## Evidence

### Lincoln 2026

Wono, A. L., Bell, A., & Bourne, E. (2026). Student Reflections in Simulated Speech and Language Therapy Placements Using Telehealth and In‐Person Formats: A Qualitative Study. *International Journal of Language & Communication Disorders, 61*(2), e70221. [doi:10.1111/1460-6984.70221](https://doi.org/10.1111/1460-6984.70221)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative comparison of two separately analysed datasets: telehealth (23 consenting students) and in-person (16 consenting students). The article reports "four subthemes" of difference; format-unique subthemes included video-call communication and mask-related communication difficulties. No effect sizes are printed.

> "In relation to research question two, differences between the telehealth and in‐person datasets are represented in four subthemes, two within client‐ centred communication and one each within working collaboratively and therapeutic approaches."
