## Evidence

### Hsin-Yun Wang & Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun 2026

Hsin-Yun Wang & Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun. (2026). Understanding online knowledge communities via social networks and self-regulation in synchronous VR co-creation for distance learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev, 74, 1037–1061. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10588-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA in SPSS 20 on the Sun et al. (2017) self-regulation scale (post-test Cronbach's alpha .88) with 22 students per group. The quote prints the significant intergroup effect; the covariate-by-dependent-variable interaction was not significant (F = .67, p = .42).

> "with the pretest effect removed, the effect of the co-creation environment was significant (F = 19.76, p <.00). In other words, the post-test self-regulation score was influenced by the co-creation environment; the co-creation environment played a causal role in co-creators' self-regulation."
