In transcript excerpt 4, Michael and Lisa emphasised it was easier to connect with students' thoughts with a profound understanding of a phenomenon and that it was "beneficial to elementarise this understanding in advance"; the article cites this as an exceptional case of CK-PCK synergy.

> "And what you also need in any case is that you clarify the phenomenon from a physics point of view beforehand. [. . . ] And then elementarise the whole thing in advance so that you know what the student is actually aiming at."
