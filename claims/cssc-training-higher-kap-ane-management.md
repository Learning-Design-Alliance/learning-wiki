## Evidence

### Shen Yi 2026

Shen Yi, Wang Ye, Wu Shiyu, Yang Rumei, He Ming, Ma Qi. (2026). A cross-sectional comparison of nursing knowledge attitudes and practices in adverse event management based on simulation training experience. Scientific Reports 16, 8401. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-39781-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 931 nurses across five Chinese hospitals; Mann-Whitney U comparisons of KAP medians by self-reported CSSC training. Table 6 reports effect sizes r = 0.74 (knowledge), 0.86 (attitude), 0.92 (practice), all P < 0.001; medians 8 (8,8) vs 6 (6,7), 50 (48,50) vs 39 (40,40), 50 (50,50) vs 41 (41,42).

> "Nurses who received CSSC training had significantly higher KAP scores compared to those without CSSC training. The corresponding test statistics and P values for these two-group comparisons are reported in Table 6."
