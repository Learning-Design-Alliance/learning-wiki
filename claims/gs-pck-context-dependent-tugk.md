## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of five TUG-K items (1, 4, 10, 16, 18) requiring determination of areas under curves: on questions 1, 4 and 16 graduate-student identification reached 96%, 84% and 84%, while on question 10 only 52% identified the most common difficulty. No effect size is printed.

> "Based upon these variations, it appears that the PCK performance of graduate students in identifying area -slope and area-height confusion of introductory students is context dependent."
