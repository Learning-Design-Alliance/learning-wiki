## Evidence

### Vail Brennan 2018

Vail Brennan, Morgan Melissa C., Spindler Hilary, Christmas Amelia, Cohen Susanna R., Walker Dilys M. (2018). The power of practice: simulation training improving the quality of neonatal resuscitation skills in Bihar, India. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1254-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational analysis of 252 live deliveries with non-vigorous neonates (of 3195 total deliveries), rated by mentors via a smartphone application and compared with the Pearson Chi-Squared Test across early versus later training weeks.

> "From early to later weeks of training, the percentage of deliveries in which mentees' identification of non-vigorous neonates, suctioning, and PPV 'went well' increased by 20.7, 25.4, and 22.7 percentage-points respectively (all p < 0.01)."
