# Gesture-enriched learning improves 8-year-olds' L2 vocabulary translation and free recall compared with non-enriched learning, persisting up to 6 months

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (4 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Gesture enrichment produced significantly higher L1-L2 translation scores than non-enriched learning in Experiment 1 (η²p = 0.11). [→ Andrä 2020](#andra-2020)
`q3 i3` Gesture enrichment produced significantly higher L2-L1 translation scores than non-enriched learning in Experiment 1 (η²p = 0.19). [→ Andrä 2020 (2)](#andra-2020-2)
`q3 i2` Gesture enrichment produced significantly higher free recall scores than non-enriched learning in Experiment 1 (η²p = 0.13). [→ Andrä 2020 (3)](#andra-2020-3)
`q3 i3` Gesture enrichment benefits replicated in Experiment 2 for both translation directions (η²p = 0.18 and 0.39). [→ Andrä 2020 (4)](#andra-2020-4)

## Evidence

### Andrä 2020

Andrä, C., Mathias, B., Schwager, A., Macedonia, M., & von Kriegstein, K. (2020). Learning Foreign Language Vocabulary with Gestures and Pictures Enhances Vocabulary Memory for Several Months Post-Learning in Eight-Year-Old School Children. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09527-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a 2×2×3 within-subjects classroom study with 54 German third-graders trained 5 days on 40 English words. Gesture-enriched words showed higher L1-L2 translation scores than non-enriched words, "a significant main effect of learning condition" with η²p = 0.11.

> "A three-way ANOV A with the factors learning condition, word type, and time point yielded a significant main effect of learning condition [ F (1, 53) = 6.29, p <0 . 0 5 ,η2 p = 0.11]."

### Andrä 2020 (2)

Andrä, C., Mathias, B., Schwager, A., Macedonia, M., & von Kriegstein, K. (2020). Learning Foreign Language Vocabulary with Gestures and Pictures Enhances Vocabulary Memory for Several Months Post-Learning in Eight-Year-Old School Children. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09527-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same Experiment 1 within-subjects design (n = 54 analyzed). L2-L1 translation scores were significantly higher for gesture-enriched words, with a "significant main effect of learning condition" of η²p = 0.19.

> "A three-way ANOV A with the factors learning condition, word type, and time point yielded a significant main effect of learning condition [ F (1, 53) = 12.81, p <0 . 0 1 ,η2 p = 0.19]."

### Andrä 2020 (3)

Andrä, C., Mathias, B., Schwager, A., Macedonia, M., & von Kriegstein, K. (2020). Learning Foreign Language Vocabulary with Gestures and Pictures Enhances Vocabulary Memory for Several Months Post-Learning in Eight-Year-Old School Children. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09527-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 free recall test in the same within-subjects classroom study. Children's free recall scores were "significantly higher for words learned with gesture enrichment compared with words learned without enrichment" (η²p = 0.13).

> "A three-way ANOV A with the factors learning condition, word type, and time point yielded a significant main effect of learning condition [F (1, 53) = 8.19, p <0 . 0 1 ,η2 p = 0.13]."

### Andrä 2020 (4)

Andrä, C., Mathias, B., Schwager, A., Macedonia, M., & von Kriegstein, K. (2020). Learning Foreign Language Vocabulary with Gestures and Pictures Enhances Vocabulary Memory for Several Months Post-Learning in Eight-Year-Old School Children. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09527-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 replicated the gesture benefit with 43 children and a reduced 24-word set, with children reciting both German and English words. L2-L1 translation showed "a significant main effect of learning condition" (η²p = 0.39); L1-L2 also showed a main effect (η²p = 0.18).

> "A three-way ANOV A with the factors learning condition, word type, and time point on L2-L1 translation test scores yielded a significant main effect of learning condition [ F (1, 42) = 26.42, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.39]."
