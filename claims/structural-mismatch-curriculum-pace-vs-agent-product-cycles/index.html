Nama 2026¶

Nama, R. (2026). Agentic Literacy Debt: A Structural Problem the AI Literacy Field Has Not Yet Named. Author's accepted manuscript, AI and Ethics (Springer Nature). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27396

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Conceptual argument about institutional timescales: the author contrasts multi-year curriculum reform cycles with month-scale AI product cycles, concluding "This is not a temporary lag." No empirical data support the timeline claims.

"National curriculum updates typically unfold over five to seven years; university program redesign often takes several years from proposal to implementation. Agentic AI capabilities, by contrast, evolve on product cycles measured in months."
