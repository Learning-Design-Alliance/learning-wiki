## Evidence

### Janjigian Michael 2021

Janjigian Michael, Dembitzer Anne, Srisarajivakul-Klein Caroline, Hardower Khemraj, Cooke Deborah, Zabar Sondra, Sauthoff Harald. (2021). Design and evaluation of the I-SCAN faculty POCUS program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02453-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational cohort; confidence measured with a retrospective pre-post survey on a 4-point Likert scale from Not at all confident to Very confident, administered at the end of the two-day course and again after 1 year. The article reports the medians and p-values above.

> "Confidence ratings increased from 1.5 pre- to 3.1 post-course (out of possible 4, p < 0.001) and were 2.73 at one-year (NS for incremental change)."
