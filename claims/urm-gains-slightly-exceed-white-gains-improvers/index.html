Evidence¶

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré 2015¶

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré, Meredith Welch, and Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). Rising Tide: Do College Grad Rate Gains Benefit All Students? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566656

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the 255 public institutions that improved overall graduation rates and enrolled at least 50 first-time, full-time URM and 50 white students. The report finds URM gains "increased slightly more than the rate for white students (6.3 versus 5.7 percentage points)."

"Among the 255 gainers, the graduation rate for underrepresented students increased slightly more than the rate for white students (6.3 versus 5.7 percentage points, Figure 3)."
