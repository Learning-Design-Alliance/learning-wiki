## Evidence

### Ankit A. Ravankar 2018

Ankit A. Ravankar, Shotaro Imai, Abhijeet Ravankar, Tomomichi Kato, Taichi Takasuka, Teruyuki Tsuji, Kaori K. Shigetomi, Ken Saito. (2018). Towards Better Problem Finding and Creativity in Graduate School Education. [doi:10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088](https://doi.org/10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report questionnaire item from the Findings section of the 2017-18 course evaluation (N = 26): perceived improvement of "creative thinking skills" (M = 4.51). These are perceptions, with no test and no objective creativity measure.

> "For the creativity, majority of the students believe that the course improved their creative thinking skills (M = 4.51, SD = 0.58) and students felt that team discussion improved their understanding of the problem and generated creative ideas (M = 4.65, SD = 0.48)."

### Ankit A. Ravankar 2018 (2)

Ankit A. Ravankar, Shotaro Imai, Abhijeet Ravankar, Tomomichi Kato, Taichi Takasuka, Teruyuki Tsuji, Kaori K. Shigetomi, Ken Saito. (2018). Towards Better Problem Finding and Creativity in Graduate School Education. [doi:10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088](https://doi.org/10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second self-report item from the same Findings-section sentence (N = 26): students felt "team discussion improved their understanding of the problem and generated creative ideas" at M = 4.65, SD = 0.48. Descriptive only.

> "For the creativity, majority of the students believe that the course improved their creative thinking skills (M = 4.51, SD = 0.58) and students felt that team discussion improved their understanding of the problem and generated creative ideas (M = 4.65, SD = 0.48)."
