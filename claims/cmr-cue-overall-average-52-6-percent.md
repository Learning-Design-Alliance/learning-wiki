# The CMR CUE yields an overall average score of 52.6 ± 0.9% (by student) across 15 courses at 9 institutions, with a slightly non-normal, positively skewed score distribution

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-test CMR CUE scores averaged 52.6 ± 0.9% treating students as data points (N=421), and 50.3 ± 2.5% averaging by course, a difference the authors argue is not of practical significance. [→ Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015](#bethany-r-wilcox-and-steven-j-pollock-2015)

## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Validation and analysis of the coupled multiple response Colorado upper-division electrostatics (CUE) diagnostic. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Multi-institution post-test administration of the CMR CUE in 15 courses at 9 institutions (N=421). The distribution was slightly non-normal (Anderson-Darling test, p = 0.03) due to slight positive skew; the by-course average was "50 .3 ± 2.5%".

> "Using the nuanced grading rubric described in Sec. I, the overall average on the CMR CUE is 52.6 ± 0.9% when treating students as data points. The distribution of N=421 scores are shown in Fig. 2."
