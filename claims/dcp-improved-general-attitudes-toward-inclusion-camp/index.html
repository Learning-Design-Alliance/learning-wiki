Evidence¶

Papaioannou 2014¶

Papaioannou, C., Evaggelinou, C., & Block, M. (2014). The effect of a disability camp program on attitudes towards the inclusion of children with disabilities in a summer sport and leisure activity camp. International Journal of Special Education, 29(1), 121-127. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1033944

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

A 2x2 repeated measures ANOVA on general attitude scores of 387 campers found significant main effects of pre-post measure and group, plus a significant interaction (F (1, 385) = 65.74, p < .001, partial η² = .146): the experimental (DCP) group's attitudes improved while the control group's did not change. Campers were divided into experimental (n = 197) and control (n = 190) groups with no random assignment reported, so the comparison is quasi-experimental and pre-existing differences between the groups cannot be ruled out.

"Analysis of variance revealed two significant main effects regarding pre-post measure (F (1, 385) = 63.62, p < .001, partial η² = .142) and group (F (1, 385) = 99.08, p < .001, partial η² = .205)."
