## Evidence

### Holt JM 2025

Holt JM, Talsma A, Lloren JIC, Eljarrah I, Woehrle L, Avdeev I. (2025). Human-Centered Design in Graduate Nursing Education: Outcomes From a Cohort Study. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA comparing construct means between the three workshop dates, run separately for pretest and posttest responses, with Bonferroni correction (.05/11 = .0045). Pretest means did not differ between workshops; posttest means did, for all three instruments, with two TDT subscales non-significant. Pre-post change was analysed separately with linear mixed-effects models. All measures are self-report, and the design is a single-group pretest-posttest with no control group.

> "differences were observed in the posttest responses, with statistically significant higher mean responses by instrument construct (CSE P < .001; PE P < .001; TDT P < .001)"

### Holt JM 2025 (2)

Holt JM, Talsma A, Lloren JIC, Eljarrah I, Woehrle L, Avdeev I. (2025). Human-Centered Design in Graduate Nursing Education: Outcomes From a Cohort Study. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-effects models with survey year and test as fixed effects and subject as random effect. The article concludes "statistically significant increases in the CSE, PE, and TDT constructs" when later workshops are compared with the April 2022 workshop.

> "When comparing against the first workshop (conducted in April 2022), the August 2022 and 2023 workshops' linear mixed-effects regression analyses demonstrated statistically significant increases in the CSE, PE, and TDT constructs and most of the subconstructs."
