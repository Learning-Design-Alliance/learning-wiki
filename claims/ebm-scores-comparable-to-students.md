## Evidence

### Berger B 2010

Berger B, Steckelberg A, Meyer G, Kasper J, Mühlhauser I. (2010). Training of patient and consumer representatives in the basic competencies of evidence-based medicine: a feasibility study. BMC Medical Education 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-16

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-course competence test of 123 course participants compared by unpaired t-test with 43 undergraduate students of Health Sciences and Education who completed a comparable EBM course. The article states "Differences between the scores of study participants and students were not statistically significant."

> "Participants passed the competence test with a mean score of 14.7 (SD 3.0, n = 123) out of 19.5 points. The comparison group of students achieved a mean score of 14.4 (SD 3.3, n = 43)."
