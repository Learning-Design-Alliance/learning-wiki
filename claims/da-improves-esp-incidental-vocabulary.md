## Evidence

### Hanifi et al. 2016

Sepideh Hanifi, Mahdi Nasiri, Hesamuddin Aliasin. (2016). Dynamic Assessment of Incidental Vocabularies: A Case of Iranian ESP Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.2p.163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental paired-sample t-test compared pre- and post-test vocabulary scores of 25 intact-group BA electronic engineering students at the University of Zanjan. The article reports a statistically significant difference, with the null hypothesis rejected.

> "The results revealed that the difference between the pretest and the posttest was statistically significant [t (24) = 13.936, p < .05]. Therefore, the null hypothesis is rejected."
