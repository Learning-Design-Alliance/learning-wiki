## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reported intrinsic motivation was highest in third grade and lowest in eighth grade, with a highly significant linear trend across grades. [→ Lepper 2005](#lepper-2005)

## Evidence

### Lepper 2005

Lepper, M. R., Henderlong Corpus, J., & Iyengar, S. S. (2005). Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivational Orientations in the Classroom: Age Differences and Academic Correlates. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.97.2.184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across six grade levels in the cross-sectional sample of 797 students. Means fell from "third grade, M ⫽ 4.07" to "eighth grade, M ⫽ 3.42", and the weighted linear contrast was highly significant, accounting for 96% of between-groups variance. No effect size is printed.

> "levels of reported intrinsic motivation were highest for the youngest students (third grade, M ⫽ 4.07, SD ⫽ .67) and lowest for the oldest students (eighth grade, M ⫽ 3.42, SD ⫽ .75), F(5, 791) ⫽ 19.27, p ⬍ .001."
