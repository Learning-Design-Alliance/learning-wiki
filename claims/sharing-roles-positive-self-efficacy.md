### Jacob Feinleib 2026

Jacob Feinleib, Matthew Dew, and N.G. Holmes. (2026). Share, Rotate, Split: The Effects of Group Work Role Distributions on Student Outcomes. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/1n3c-v2fj](https://doi.org/10.1103/1n3c-v2fj)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling results for attitude constructs (self-efficacy, perceived agency, belonging, recognition) across the three lab courses. Sharing time positively predicted all attitude post-scores controlling for pre-scores, but only the self-efficacy effect was statistically significant; the article prints standardized effect sizes in its table but the quoted sentence carries no value.

> "In all cases, more time spent sharing roles corresponded to the strongest positive effect sizes (statistically significantly greater than zero only for self-efficacy)."
