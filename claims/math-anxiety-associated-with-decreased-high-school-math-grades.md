## Evidence

### Rachel G. Pizzie and David J. M. Kraemer 2023

Rachel G. Pizzie and David J. M. Kraemer. (2023). Strategies for remediating the impact of math anxiety on high school math performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00188-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear model on combined-sample pre-intervention grades (average of quarter 1 and quarter 2), controlling for class subject matter and teacher, in two US high school samples (N = 224 combined). The analysis found "a main effect of math anxiety on pre-intervention grades" with no significant group differences or group-by-anxiety interaction before intervention.

> "Before the intervention was implemented, we ﬁnd a main effect of math anxiety on pre-intervention grades F(1192) = 55.69, p < 0.001, such that increased math anxiety is associated with decreased grade performance during the pre-intervention semester."
