## Subclaims
`q3 i0` In cross-lagged models, baseline depressive symptoms negatively predicted academic engagement one year later (β = −0.09, p < 0.001), and the reverse path was weaker (β = −0.05, p = 0.017); both standardized coefficients are small. [→ Ji Lei 2021](#ji-lei-2021)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Two-wave cross-lagged path analysis in the 2615-student cohort, controlling for gender, residence and mother's education; the article reports "DS at baseline predicted AE at follow-up (β = −0.09; p < 0.001)" and the weaker reverse path. The standardized β = −0.09 is below d = 0.2, hence the small-magnitude coding.

> "DS at baseline predicted AE at follow-up (β = −0.09; p < 0.001). Similarly, the reversed negative effect was supported since AE at baseline had a negative cross-lagged effect on DS (β = −0.05; p = 0.017)."
