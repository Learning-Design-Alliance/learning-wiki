Evidence¶

Daniel M. Getuno 2015¶

Daniel M. Getuno, Joel K. Kiboss, Johnson M. Changeiywo, & Leo B. Ogola. (2015). Effects of an E-Learning Module on Students' Attitudes in an Electronics Class. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.36. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086475

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental Solomon-three study: independent-sample t-test comparing attitude gains of group E (pre-test mean 97.0811, post-test 104.7568, gain 7.6757) and group C1 (gain -9.4706). The article reports the gain difference was significant "in favour of the experimental group, E"; no effect size is printed.

"that the differences in gain from pre-test to post-test were significant, where, t (.000, 69) = 10.345 > critical t (.05, 69) = 1.644854 in favour of the experimental group, E."
