Chase Matthew L., Drews Reed, Zumberg Marc S., Ellis Leslie R., Reid Erin G., Gerds Aaron T., Lee Alfred I., Hobbs Gabriela S., Berry Jonathan, Freed Jason A. (2023). Consensus recommendations on peripheral blood smear review: defining curricular standards and fellow competency. Blood Advances. https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2023009843

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Consensus results from a modified nominal group technique with eight academic hematologists in two focus groups; all statements in Table 2 had unanimous consensus exceeding the prespecified >70% strong-consensus threshold. The group agreed on "a structured approach that includes the identification of monolayer".

> "All agreed that trainees should learn PBS review through a structured approach that includes the identification of monolayer, use of various magnifications, switching between magnifications, and systematic review of each cell line."
