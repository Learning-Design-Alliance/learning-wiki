Evidence¶

West et al. 2016¶

Martin R. West, Beth A. Morton, Corinne M. Herlihy. (2016). Achievement Network's Investing in Innovation Expansion: Impacts on Educator Practice and Student Achievement. Center for Education Policy Research, Harvard University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED565458

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Year 2 leader and teacher survey impact analysis (67-school survey sample, cluster-adjusted OLS with matched-pair fixed effects) on standardized data-use frequency scales. Both leader and teacher reports showed higher frequency of data review, and teachers reported more frequent instructional use of data.

"School leaders and teachers in ANet schools reported reviewing student data more frequently, and teachers in ANet schools reported using student data more frequently to inform their instruction"
