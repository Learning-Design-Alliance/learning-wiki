# Drop rates did not vary with course structure except for an increase in the final high-enrollment quarter

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across five quarters, drop rates of 1.8–3.6% showed no heterogeneity as structure changed (χ2 = 3.9, df = 4, p = 0.42), but significant heterogeneity appeared when the 6.1% Autumn 2009 drop rate was added (χ2 = 21.5, df = 5, p < 0.001). [→ Freeman Scott 2011](#freeman-scott-2011)

## Evidence

### Freeman Scott 2011

Freeman Scott, Haak David, Wenderoth Mary Pat. (2011). Increased Course Structure Improves Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-08-0105

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A χ2 analysis of drop rates across quarters from Spring 2002 through Autumn 2009. The authors attribute the Autumn 2009 increase to a change in enrollment from 350 to 700 and a departmental policy loosening restrictions on repeating the course.

> "A χ2 analysis indicates no heterogeneity in drop rate as course design changed from low to medium to high structure (χ2 = 3.9, df = 4; p = 0.42). Significant heterogeneity occurs when the drop rate of 6.1% in Autumn 2009 is added to the analysis, however (χ2 = 21.5, df = 5; p < 0.001)."
