q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on standardized practice retrieval performance in the three retrieval conditions of the 213-participant experiment; raw means were 5.0 (free recall), 3.18 (practice quiz), and 7.42 (test generation) on open-ended or quiz formats. The Bayes factor favored the null.

"There were no significant differences in practice retrieval scores between the three conditions, F(2,128) = 0.04, p =.97, BF01 = 13.15"
