## Evidence

### Choi Naya 2022

Choi Naya, Sheo Jiyeon, Jung Suji, Choi Jisu. (2022). Newspaper Reading in Families with School-Age Children: Relationship between Parent–Child Interaction Using Newspaper, Reading Motivation, and Academic Achievement. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(21), 14423. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192114423

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrapping analysis with 5000 samples and a 95% percentile confidence level on the SEM sample of 238 households. The indirect effect was "significant (β = 0.08, p < 0.01)" and the direct effect was insignificant, supporting full mediation. Total effect printed in Table 5 was 0.15 (p < 0.05).

> "The mediating effect of reading motivation in the association between parent–child interaction and academic achievement was found to be significant (β = 0.08, p < 0.01). Since the direct effect of parent–child interaction was insignificant, it can be said that reading motivation is fully mediating the relationship between parent–child interaction and academic achievement."
