## Evidence

### Pharmer 2025

Pharmer, R. L., Wickens, C. D., Plabst, L., Clegg, B. A., Hirshfield, L. M., Lewis, J. E., Nicoly, J. B., Spencer, C. A., & Ortega, F. R. (2025). The Impact of Simple, Brief, and Adaptive Instructions within Virtual Reality Training: Components of Cognitive Load Theory in an Assembly Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a 2 × 2 within-subjects repeated-measures study of 60 university students assembling eight shapes in VR under adaptive training. Intrinsic Load was manipulated by bar attributes (two vs. three). The article reports "a significant main effect of Intrinsic Load (F(1,58) = 53.74, p < .001, η2 = .11)"; retention test error rate showed no main effect (F(1,58) = .012, p = .91).

> "A 2 × 2 repeated measures ANOVA examining the effects of Intrinsic and Extraneous Load on training time revealed a significant main effect of Intrinsic Load (F(1,58) = 53.74, p < .001, η2 = .11) and a significant main effect of Extraneous Load (F(1,58) = 23.38, p < .001, η2 = .05)."

### Pharmer 2025 (2)

Pharmer, R. L., Wickens, C. D., Plabst, L., Clegg, B. A., Hirshfield, L. M., Lewis, J. E., Nicoly, J. B., Spencer, C. A., & Ortega, F. R. (2025). The Impact of Simple, Brief, and Adaptive Instructions within Virtual Reality Training: Components of Cognitive Load Theory in an Assembly Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.20943)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 replicated the design with 58 participants under yoked fixed training. The ANOVA "revealed a significant lengthening of training time imposed by increasing Intrinsic Load (F(1,57) = 38.14, p < .001, η2 = .12)"; retention test error rate again showed no main effect (F(1,57) = 1.43, p = .24).

> "revealed a significant lengthening of training time imposed by increasing Intrinsic Load (F(1,57) = 38.14, p < .001, η2 = .12) and a significant detrimental effect of increasing Extraneous Load (F(1,57) = 21.15, p < .001, η2 = .05)."
