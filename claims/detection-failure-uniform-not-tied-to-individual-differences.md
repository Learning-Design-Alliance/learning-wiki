## Evidence

### Lixiang Yan 2026

Lixiang Yan, Yueqiao Jin, Xibin Han, Dragan Gasevic. (2026). Socially fluent AI decouples conversational signals from source identity in online interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23426

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Participant-level d' estimates clustered near zero (mean d' = −0.012, SD = 0.697), showing "identity discrimination was uniformly poor across participants". Sensitivity was not credibly associated with AI literacy (r = 0.075, p = .160, n = 349), personality (all |r| ≤ 0.073), or demographics.

> "These individual-level sensitivity estimates clustered tightly around zero (mean 𝑑′ = −0.012, 𝑆𝐷 = 0.697, 95% CI [−0.085, 0.062]), indicating that the group-level detection failure did not arise from averaging over heterogeneous strategies or a minority of high-performing detectors."
