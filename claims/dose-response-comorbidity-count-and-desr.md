Surman Craig B. H., Biederman Joseph, Spencer Thomas, Miller Carolyn A., McDermott Katie M., Faraone Stephen V. (2013). Understanding deficient emotional self-regulation in adults with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a controlled study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive dose–response analysis in ADHD probands relating the number of current comorbid mental health conditions to mean DESR scores (Table 3 means rising from 5.9 at zero conditions to 18.7 at six). The study reports "individuals with higher numbers of current comorbid conditions tended to have higher DESR scores."

> "As shown in Fig. 2 and Table 3, among the ADHD probands, individuals with higher numbers of current comorbid conditions tended to have higher DESR scores."
