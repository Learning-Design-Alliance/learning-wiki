The final version of the DQC-2stap questionnaire was administered to students of existing two-state-approach courses in Hungary, Slovenia and Italy. Results were analysed with a coding scheme developed iteratively from the data. The authors report that "preliminary results seem to indicate statistically significant differences between the courses".

> "Preliminary results seem to indicate statistically significant differences between the courses both in the correctness of responses and the distribution of codes associated with the responses."
