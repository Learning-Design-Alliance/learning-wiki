## Evidence

### Rotgans 2011

Rotgans, J. I., & Schmidt, H. G. (2011). Cognitive engagement in the problem-based learning classroom. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 16(4), 465–479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9272-9

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Zero-order correlations among the five engagement measurements and course grades in the 208-student sample (Table 2). The article reports coefficients "ranging from .15 to .88", with engagement–achievement correlations of r = .15, r = .17, r = .25, r = .28 and r = .21; the printed r = .15 and r = .17 are small effects (i = 1).

> "All correlation coefficients were statistically significant and were medium to strong, ranging from .15 to .88, suggesting that the level of cognitive engagement in one situation is associated with another during the one-day PBL process as well as students' academic achievement"
