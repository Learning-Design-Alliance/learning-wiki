## Evidence

### Doku A 2025

Doku A, Tuglo LS, Osuoji C, Edzeame J, Broni M, Mainoo DD, Acquah AE, Honny K, Peters RJG, Agyemang C. (2025). Ghana Heart Initiative Training for Cardiac Arrest Management Among Health Care Professionals: Outcomes Evaluation Study (2019-2024). JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/75536

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of the 229 BLS trainees' post-training scores comparing tertiary with primary facilities (reference category). The article reports "AOR 0.20, 95% CI 0.07-0.59; P=.003"; the AOR is an odds ratio but no standardized effect size beyond it is printed.

> "Logistic regression analysis showed that respondents working in tertiary health care facilities were 80% less likely (adjusted odds ratio [AOR] 0.20, 95% CI 0.07-0.59; P=.003) to have adequate BLS knowledge and skills compared to those in primary health care facilities."
