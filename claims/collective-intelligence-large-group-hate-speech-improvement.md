Mixed model repeated-measures analysis of mean answer scores across the seven phases in the Collective Learning experiment, comparing G1 (n = 123), G2 (n = 18) and G3 (n = 20). The authors state "we observed a significant effect in group G1 from phase 5 onward"; no effect size is printed.

> "For question 1, we observed a significant effect in group G1 from phase 5 onward (phase 4–5; p < 0.001), whereas such an effect was not significant in the smaller groups G2 and G3."
