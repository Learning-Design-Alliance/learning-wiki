## Evidence

### Amasha 2014

Amasha, M., & Alkhalaf, S. (2014). The Effect of Using Facebook Markup Language (FBML) for Designing an E-Learning Model in Higher Education. International Journal of Research in Computer Science, 4(5), 1-9. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1501.05576)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same quasi-experimental study measured skill performance with a designed skills test. The experimental group mean was 21.14 (SD 7.958) versus control 92.72 (SD 4.300), reported as "a statistic significant distinction between means of the students' degrees."

> "Results indicate that there is a statistic significant distinction between means of the students' degrees of the experimental group in favor of the control group in students' cognitive attainment after teaching for skills, t(56, N = 29) = 42.616; p = .05."
