## Evidence

### Hill K 2023

Hill K, Jaensch A, Childs J, McDonald S. (2023). Evaluation of point of care ultrasound (POCUS) training on arteriovenous access assessment and cannula placement for haemodialysis. The Journal of Vascular Access. https://doi.org/10.1177/11297298231194100

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed quantitative and qualitative patient survey (validated dialysis fear of injections tool plus open-ended questions) completed by 17 patients with AVF or AVG assessed with POCUS at the second site visit; analysed by frequencies and thematic analysis. Patients reported the ultrasound 'helps the nurses'.

> "The patients that completed the PROMs (n = 17) overall supported the ease and use of POCUS for haemodialysis cannulation and felt that it contributed to the nursing staff competency in cannulation."
