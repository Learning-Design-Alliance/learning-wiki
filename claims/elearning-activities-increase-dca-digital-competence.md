## Evidence

### Feola 2016

Elvia Ilaria Feola. (2016). Digital Literacy and New Technological Perspectives. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2174-2180. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040929

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Almost-experimental one-group study of 162 students in the Laboratory of General Education and Educational Technology course at the University of Salerno, delivered in e-learning. The Instant DCA was administered at the beginning and end; differences between averages were tested with T tests at a 95% confidence interval, and "increased significantly at the end of the experiment" on all three dimensions. No effect size is printed.

> "The statistical analysis found that the average value of the group, for each investigated dimensions: technological, cognitive, ethical, increased significantly at the end of the experiment, as shown in the graph (Figure 2)"
