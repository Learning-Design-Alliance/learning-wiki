## Evidence

### Bakwa Kanyinga Felly 2023

Bakwa Kanyinga Felly, Gogovor Amédé, Dofara Suélène Georgina, Gadio Souleymane, Tremblay Martin, Daniel Sam J., Rivest Louis-Paul, Légaré France. (2023). Evaluating the impact of continuing professional development courses on physician behavioral intention: a pre-post study with follow-up at six months. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04597-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post observational study of physicians attending nine CPD courses at the FMSQ 2019 annual meeting, using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. The article reports "a significant mean difference of 0.45 (p = 0.002)" in intention on a 7-point scale, measured with CPD-REACTION before and after courses.

> "Intention scores among participants who completed CPD-REACTION both before and after courses (n = 81) were 5.99 (SD = 1.31) and 6.44 (SD = 0.80) respectively, a significant mean difference of 0.45 (p = 0.002)."
