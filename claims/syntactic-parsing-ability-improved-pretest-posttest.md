## Evidence

### Chen et al. 2018

Xiaokan Chen, Hongmei Li, Min Gui. (2018). Instructional Effects of Syntactic Parsing on Chinese College Students’ EFL Reading Rates. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 6, No. 11. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i11.3470

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group pretest-posttest comparison for the experimental group only, using an independent t-test in SPSS 23.0 (Table 2 reports t=-10.956, df=71). Pretest mean was 38 of 50 for 34 participants; posttest mean was 47 of 50 for 39 participants. No effect size is printed.

> "As shown in Table 2, a significant difference in syntactic parsing ability was found between the syntactic parsing ability pretest and the posttest (p= .000<0.05)."
