## Evidence

### Rolland-Debord C 2026

Rolland-Debord C, Juret L, Simon M, El Mouhajer A, Londner C, Morélot-Panzini C, Chenivesse C, Similowski T. (2026). Impact of a Structured Training Program on Medical Student Confidence and Behavior During Their First Radial Arterial Puncture: Comparative Study. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Puncture success outcomes recorded for students' first clinical arterial punctures; first-attempt success did not differ significantly. A separate comparison found "The median number of attempts required for success was significantly higher in group 1 than in group 2 (median 1, IQR 0.5‐2 vs median 1, IQR 0‐1; P=.02)."

> "The first-attempt success rate was 23.1% (3/13) in group 1 and 48.3% (14/29) in group 2 (P=.18)."
