Evidence¶

Zhang Y 2026¶

Zhang Y, Shi Y and Lu J (2026). The relationship between AI anxiety and academic motivation among university students: the mediating role of emotion regulation and the moderating role of gender. Front. Psychol. 17:1918525. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918525

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bias-corrected bootstrap conditional indirect effects (5,000 resamples) from PROCESS Model 14 in the survey of 1,484 students. The negative indirect association was 'stronger among male students' (indirect effect = −0.089) than among female students (indirect effect = −0.062), with both confidence intervals excluding zero.

"Specifically, the negative indirect association was stronger among male students [indirect effect = −0.089, Boot SE = 0.019, 95% CI (−0.129, −0.055)] than among female students [indirect effect = −0.062, Boot SE = 0.015, 95% CI (−0.094, −0.036)]."

Zhang Y 2026 (2)¶

Zhang Y, Shi Y and Lu J (2026). The relationship between AI anxiety and academic motivation among university students: the mediating role of emotion regulation and the moderating role of gender. Front. Psychol. 17:1918525. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1918525

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Index of moderated mediation test from the same PROCESS Model 14 analysis. The index was significant with a 95% confidence interval of (0.008, 0.056), 'confirming that the indirect association differed significantly by gender.'

"The index of moderated mediation was significant [index = 0.028, Boot SE = 0.012, 95% CI (0.008, 0.056)], confirming that the indirect association differed significantly by gender."
