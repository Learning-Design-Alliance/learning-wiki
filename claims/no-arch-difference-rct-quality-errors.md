## Evidence

### El-Ma’aita Ahmad M. 2024

El-Ma’aita Ahmad M., Mahasneh Sari A., Hamandi Maryam A., Al-Rabab’ah Mohammad A. (2024). Molar root canal treatment performed by undergraduate dental students; an observational study of procedural errors and student perception. BMC Medical Education 24. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05397-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-squared tests by arch reported in Table 1 for each procedural error (e.g., underextension 1.855, p 0.173) across the 213 assessed RCTs, of which 75.6% were first molars and 24.4% second molars. The Results report no statistically significant arch difference; no effect size was printed.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in the quality of RCTs provided or the prevalence of procedural errors between maxillary and mandibular molars."
