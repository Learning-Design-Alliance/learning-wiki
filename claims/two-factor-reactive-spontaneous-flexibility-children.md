# Cognitive flexibility in children is best modeled as a two-factor structure with reactive flexibility and spontaneous flexibility as separate but related components

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Confirmatory factor analysis in Study 1 showed best fit for a two-factor solution with reactive flexibility and spontaneous flexibility as separate but related components. [→ Arán Filippetti 2020](#arán-filippetti-2020)

## Evidence

### Arán Filippetti 2020

Arán Filippetti, Vanessa; Krumm, Gabriela Liliana (2020). A hierarchical model of cognitive flexibility in children: Extending the relationship between flexibility, creativity and academic achievement. Child Neuropsychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/09297049.2019.1711034

`q3 · i?`

Study 1, a confirmatory factor analysis with 112 children aged 8 to 12, tested competing latent structures of performance-based flexibility tasks. The CFA "showed best fit for the two-factor solution" distinguishing reactive from spontaneous flexibility as related factors.

> "In S1 (n = 112 8- to 12-year-old children), confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) showed best fit for the two-factor solution with reactive flexibility (RF) and spontaneous flexibility (SF) as separate but related components."
