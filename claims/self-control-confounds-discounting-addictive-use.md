## Evidence

### Tim Schulz van Endert 2021

Tim Schulz van Endert. (2021). Addictive use of digital devices in young children: Associations with delay discounting, self-control and academic performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253058

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression (Table 3) predicting DASC score from controls, self-reported usage, self-control and LDR proportion in 75 children; the full model explained R2 = 0.59 (F = 13.21, p<0.001). "with the variable self-control in the model the LDR proportion no longer significantly predicted the DASC score".

> "As displayed in Table 3, self-control (β = -0.58, p<0.001) and self-reported usage (β = 0.32, p = 0.003) were the only significant predictors of the DASC score. Notably, with the variable self-control in the model the LDR proportion no longer significantly predicted the DASC score (β = -0.15, p = 0.100)."
