### Lee Hyunkyu 2015

Lee Hyunkyu, Boot Walter R., Baniqued Pauline L., Voss Michelle W., Prakash Ruchika Shaurya, Basak Chandramallika, Kramer Arthur F. (2015). The Relationship between Intelligence and Training Gains Is Moderated by Training Strategy. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0123259

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc correlation in the HVT group of the same 30-hour Space Fortress training experiment (25 participants). The article reports "r = -.023, p = n.s. for HVT", a negligible and non-significant association between fluid intelligence and training gain.

> "In contrast, in HVT, there was no difference on performance improvement between participants who started with lower and higher fluid intelligence."
