### Spence AD 2025

Spence AD, Carr D, Kajamaa A, Gormley GJ. (2025). 'Overcoming and owning challenges': A qualitative study exploring the manifestation of agency in learners. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.15631

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview findings from twelve second-year medical students after simulation; participants reported that recalling performance in the debrief "provided a mirror of their capabilities and inabilities" and enhanced critical reflection and ownership of their actions.
