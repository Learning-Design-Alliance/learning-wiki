Cross-site finding from focus groups, surveys, and School C observations; the interactivity feature was added mid-study and "was available only for Schools C, D, and E," so the finding is scoped to those contexts.

"This finding suggests that when interactivity was available and visible to participants, it promoted deeper engagement with AI feedback."
