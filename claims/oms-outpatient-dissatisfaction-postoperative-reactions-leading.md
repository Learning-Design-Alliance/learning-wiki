## Evidence

### Long Huimin 2025

Long Huimin, Nie Xin, Wang Chi. (2025). Learning needs of residents in oral and maxillofacial surgery outpatient clinics: a dual-source survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06980-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of dissatisfaction feedback collected from all outpatient patients at the Stomatological Hospital of Wenzhou Medical University, 2021–2023, with specialist telephone follow-up and staff categorization. Among 723 dissatisfaction cases, "84 cases (11.6%) involved resident trainees".

> "From 2021 to 2023, a total of 723 cases of patient dissatisfaction were reported in the outpatient department of oral and maxillofacial surgery. Among these, 84 cases (11.6%) involved resident trainees."

### Long Huimin 2025 (2)

Long Huimin, Nie Xin, Wang Chi. (2025). Learning needs of residents in oral and maxillofacial surgery outpatient clinics: a dual-source survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06980-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Categorization of the 84 resident-involved feedback cases by professional staff. The leading category was that the "resident trainee performed the operation independently, but the patient experienced severe postoperative reactions (42.9%)", followed by inability to solve problems independently (26.2%).

> "Resident trainee unable to solve the problem independently, requiring intervention by the supervising teacher(26.2%). Resident trainee performed the operation independently, but the patient experienced severe postoperative reactions (42.9%)."
