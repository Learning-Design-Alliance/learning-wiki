## Evidence

### Haataja 2021

Haataja, E., Malmberg, J., Dindar, M., & Järvelä, S. (2021). The pivotal role of monitoring for collaborative problem solving seen in interaction, performance, and interpersonal physiology. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 241–268. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09279-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

GEE model predicting negative valence monitoring duration from physiological measures in 19 groups during a business simulation. The tonic EDA slope and %DET were "significant positive predictors, meaning that increase in physiological arousal and higher physiological synchrony were related to more monitoring interactions with negative valence."

> "the model predicting monitoring with negative valence included the tonic EDA slope and %DET as significant positive predictors, meaning that increase in physiological arousal and higher physiological synchrony were related to more monitoring interactions with negative valence"
