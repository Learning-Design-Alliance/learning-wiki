### Paula Tuzón 2025

Paula Tuzón, Antoni Salvà Salvà, and Juan Fernández-Gracia. (2025). Complex networks approach to curriculum analysis and subject integration: a case study on Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Network analysis of the combined Physics and Mathematics curriculum network (90 contents, 1016 of 4005 possible connections, average degree 22.58, density 0.25, single component, diameter 2, average path length 1.75). The article states the increase is "due to the numerous links between contents of both subjects".

> "Compared to when the subjects are treated as separate networks, the average number of connections increases due to the numerous links between contents of both subjects; however, the density is lower than that of either subject's individual network."
