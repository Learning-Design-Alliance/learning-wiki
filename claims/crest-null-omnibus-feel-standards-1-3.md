## Evidence

### Krystal M. Hinerman 2014

Krystal M. Hinerman, Darrell M. Hull, Darrell M. Hayes, Marvin G. Evans, Maria L. Reyes. (2014). Childhood Resiliency Effects from Schoolwide Treatment: A Cluster Randomized Trial. SREE Fall 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562710

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat HLM analysis of the Standards 1-3 Feelings about Behavior composite in the cluster-randomized trial showed no statistically significant main treatment effect; the authors interpret this as both groups reporting similar levels of concern about negative behavior.

> "While the absence of a statistically significant main treatment effect on the mirrored “Feel” items seems to suggest both treatment and control groups report similar levels of concern about negative behavior and appreciation for approval when engaging in positive behavior."
