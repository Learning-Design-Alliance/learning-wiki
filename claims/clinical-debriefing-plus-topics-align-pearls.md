Coggins Andrew, Santos Aaron De Los, Zaklama Ramez, Murphy Margaret. (2020). Interdisciplinary clinical debriefing in the emergency department: an observational study of learning topics and outcomes. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-020-00370-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Content analysis of 71 debriefing forms from a 9-month prospective observational study at an Australian tertiary ED, coded by two investigators using a modified PEARLS framework. The study reports "232 'plus' learning points were recorded of which 195 (84.1%) were coded" to PEARLS domains.
