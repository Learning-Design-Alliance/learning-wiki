The 2011 qualitative study found advisors exchanged a phone call or email with 80% of the treatment group and 51% met at least once; the article concludes "active outreach was necessary to mobilize students to take up the offer of summer support."

> "For ethical reasons, the control group had been informed during their last month of high school of the availability of summer help; however, only 2% sought out support from uAspire. Clearly, active outreach was necessary to mobilize students to take up the offer of summer support."
