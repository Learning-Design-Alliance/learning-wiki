Evidence¶

Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016¶

Kyriakoulis Konstantinos, Patelarou Athina, Laliotis Aggelos, Wan Andrew C, Matalliotakis Michail, Tsiou Chrysoula, Patelarou Evridiki. (2016). Educational strategies for teaching evidence-based practice to undergraduate health students: systematic review. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5066070/

q3 · i? · review · r2

Quasi-experimental study in Spain among nursing students (n=148; IG=75, CG=73) with a multifaceted intervention of lectures, practicals, group discussions, teamwork, and presentations. Mean skills scores improved from 2.75 to 4.01; the review reports "significantly improved versus baseline in skills (4.01 vs. 2.75)".

"The EBP competence questionnaire was administered before and at two months after the 15-week intervention period and results showed that the mean scores of the intervention group were significantly improved versus baseline in skills (4.01 vs. 2.75) dimensions."

q3 · i? · review · r2

Same quasi-experimental nursing study; the review reports attitude scores improved from 3.33 to 4.28 and knowledge scores from 2.82 to 3.92 versus baseline, described as "significantly improved versus baseline after the EBP course".

"Ruzafa-Martinez et al. [4] showed that attitudes and knowledge scores were significantly improved versus baseline after the EBP course (4.28 vs. 3.33 and 3.92 vs. 2.82, respectively) [9]."
