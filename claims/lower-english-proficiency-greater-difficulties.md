## Evidence

### Whiteside 2017

Whiteside, Katie E.; Gooch, Debbie; Norbury, Courtenay F. (2017). English Language Proficiency and Early School Attainment Among Children Learning English as an Additional Language. Child Development, 88(3), 812-827. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12615

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical multiple regression on the population cohort; the unadjusted model explained 30% of variance in SDQ total difficulties, with CCC-S score β = .58, p < .001, and this association persisted in the adjusted model (β = .55). The article states "lower English language proficiency" significantly predicted "greater total difficulties scores."

> "higher CCC–S scores (i.e., lower English language proficiency) significantly predicted greater total difficulties scores and EAL status significantly predicted lower total difficulties scores."
