`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Formative study with 10 visual design novices browsing Reddit r/UI_design for 20 minutes, followed by semi-structured interviews analyzed with reflexive thematic analysis. "Six participants reported that they struggled to find posts" matching their interests (challenge C1).

### Xia Chen 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same formative study: challenge C4 emerged from interviews, with participants noting they had to manually copy images and comments into a separate document, which one said would "increase the information processing workload".

> "Three participants raised concerns that in the Reddit ODC interface, they were unable to organize and review the learned knowledge in the explored design examples and comments (C4)."
