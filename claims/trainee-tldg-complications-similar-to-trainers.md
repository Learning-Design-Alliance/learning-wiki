### Zhang Shun 2020

Zhang Shun, Orita Hajime, Egawa Hiroyuki, Matsui Ryota, Yamauchi Suguru, Yube Yukinori, Kaji Sanae, Takahashi Toru, Oka Shinichi, Inaki Noriyuki, Fukunaga Tetsu. (2020). World Journal of Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.3748/wjg.v26.i13.1490

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective comparison of postoperative complications, stratified by Clavien-Dindo grade 2 or higher, between 52 trainee-operated and 40 trainer-operated TLDG cases. The study reports "Four patients in the trainee group (7.7%) and two patients in the trainer group (5%) had complications"; no surgical intervention or mortality was needed.

> "Four patients in the trainee group (7.7%) and two patients in the trainer group (5%) had complications (Table 4). The most frequent complication was intra-abdominal abscess (3.8%) in the trainee group."
