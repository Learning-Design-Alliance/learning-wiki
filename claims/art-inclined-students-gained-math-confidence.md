`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative participant feedback from the summer school course; one participant wrote it was the first time combining mathematics and arts and that, as mathematics was not a strong point, they could "deduce and connect many aspects seen from the arts perspective".

> "Especially those participants more interested in arts were surprised by their growing interest and abilities in solving mathematical problems."
