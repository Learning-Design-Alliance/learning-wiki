## Evidence

### Mehmet Bicakci 2026

Mehmet Bicakci, Heidrun Stoeger, Diana Wengler, Albert Ziegler. (2026). Recalibrating impact in STEM education: Empirical benchmarks for interpreting intervention effects. Educational Research Review 52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100803

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Robustness analyses (precision weighting and one-per-primary-study specification, Table 3) show that after weighting "the relative ordering of subgroup distributions changed little". One-per-primary-study quartiles were 0.23, 0.52, 0.91.

> "the overall 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles declined from 0.21, 0.48, and 0.86 to 0.14, 0.32, and 0.59 after publication-bias adjustment, and further to 0.09, 0.22, and 0.49 under inverse-variance weighting. Despite this attenuation, the relative ordering of subgroup distributions changed little"
