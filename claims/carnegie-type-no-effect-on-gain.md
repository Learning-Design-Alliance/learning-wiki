## Evidence

### Von Korff 2016

Von Korff, J., Archibeque, B., Gomez, K.A., Heckendorf, T., McKagan, S.B., Sayre, E.C., Schenk, E.W., Shepherd, C., Sorell, L. (2016). Secondary Analysis of Teaching Methods in Introductory Physics: a 50k-Student Study. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.00516](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.00516)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multifactor ANOVA with Tukey HSD follow-up on the class-level database. Pre-test scores correlated with Carnegie classification but gain did not; the result is illustrated for the FCI.

> "We used a multifactor ANOVA to look for effects of test (FCI vs. FMCE), teaching method (IE vs. traditional), and institutional type (Carnegie Classification) on normalized gain, then a Tukey HSD test to look for differences. After controlling for teaching method and test, institutional type does not affect normalized gain."
