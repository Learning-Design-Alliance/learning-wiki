### Pham Mia 2024

Pham Mia, Aldous Robert, Brincat Stephan. (2024). Implementation of ultrasound-guided cannulation training for foundation doctors. Clinical Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinme.2024.100256

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-course electronic survey of 65 responding trainees (of 98 surveyed) at St George's Hospital, September–October 2023; respondents reported a median confidence of 2/5 for difficult venous access and 1/5 for US-PIVC.

> "About 38.5% of participants reported that they had not had any previous ultrasound teaching and 53.8% had never had specific US-PIVC teaching."
