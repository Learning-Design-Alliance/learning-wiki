Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-school-improvement

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

First-phase results for the newly added elementary outcome of GPA. The report describes this as a new finding and an important addition because students' grades are stronger predictors of their success in high school than test scores; GPA growth was not part of the original validation study.

> "We found that our additional elementary outcome, GPA, also improved more in schools with strong scores on the 5Essentials Survey."
