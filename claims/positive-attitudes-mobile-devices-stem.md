### Aziz Amaaz 2024

Aziz Amaaz, Abderrahman Mouradi, Moahamed Erradi, Ali Allouch. (2024). Moroccan pre-service elementary teachers: attitudes toward STEM education and mobile devices. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v13i5.28205

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire survey of 226 BEES students; Table 7 reports overall attitude toward mobile devices M=3.91 (SD=0.63), with intention items IU1 M=4.27 and perceived ease of use for teachers PEU1 M=4.23.

> "The analysis of BEES students' attitudes toward the use of mobile devices in integrated STEM education as seen in Table 7 revealed that these attitudes are generally positive, as the mean scores of the items used to measure these attitudes were centered on the "agree" option."
