Evidence¶

Lester Loschky 1994¶

Lester Loschky. (1994). Comprehensible Input and Second Language Acquisition: What is the Relationship? Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 16, Issue 3, pp. 303-323. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100013103

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Across the three experimental conditions (unmodified input with no interaction, premodified input with no interaction, and unmodified input with negotiated interaction), the article reports "significant gains on both measures were found for all three groups." No effect sizes are printed.

"Furthermore, there was no correlation found between differences in moment-to-moment comprehension and gains in vocabulary recognition and acquisition of structures, though significant gains on both measures were found for all three groups."
