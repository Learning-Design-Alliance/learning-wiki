Evidence¶

Song 2025¶

Song, M., Li, L., He, D. & Cai, Q. (2025). Reading experience reveals shared and idiosyncratic neural patterns during text comprehension. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00390-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

AnnaK model IS-RSA analysis of fMRI data from 39 participants reading two narratives, correlating a neural RDM with a print-exposure predicted RDM after regressing out education, across 153 ROIs with FDR correction (q < .05). The article reports "positively predicted variability in neural dynamics" in these semantic regions.

"During narrative reading, the dissimilarity in exposure positively predicted variability in neural dynamics in the right medial frontal gyrus (MFG), anterior regions of bilateral middle temporal gyrus (aMTG), as well as the right posterior middle temporal gyrus (pMTG)."
