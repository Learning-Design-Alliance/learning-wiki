## Evidence

### Ekblad Solvig 2013

Ekblad Solvig, Mollica Richard F, Fors Uno, Pantziaras Ioannis, Lavelle James. (2013). Educational potential of a virtual patient system for caring for traumatized patients in primary care. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-110

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative follow-up telephone interviews (10 of 11 participants, 15-30 minutes) analyzed by inductive content analysis; "8 out of 10" participants saw the VP as an examination tool that "eliminates a lot of biases".

> "A common answer from the participants (8 out of 10) was that VP could be used as an examination tool on many levels of competence, since it eliminates a lot of biases as everything which is used with this VP is the same."
