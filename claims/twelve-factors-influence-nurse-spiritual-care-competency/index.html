Evidence¶

Kaiyue Wu 2025¶

Kaiyue Wu, Xinsheng Wang, Linbo Sun, Jiayi Feng, Changping Song. (2025). Nurse Competency in Providing Spiritual Care and Associated Influencing Factors: A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70192

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of influencing factors across the 16 included cross-sectional studies, analysed with odds ratios and 95% CIs in RevMan and STATA; the abstract reports all listed factors at "p < 0.01". No individual effect sizes are quoted here.

"Meta‐analysis results show that the following factors influenced nurses' spiritual care ability: educational background, whether or not they have received spiritual education courses, religious belief, working hours, working environment, hospital grade, marital status, emotional intelligence, nursing age, income, position and empathy level."
