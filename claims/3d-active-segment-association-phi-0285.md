## Evidence

### Cooper M. M. 2024

Cooper M. M., Caballero M. D., Carmel J. H., Duffy E. M., Ebert-May D., Fata-Hartley C. L., Herrington D. G., Laverty J. T., Nelson P. C., Posey L. A., Stoltzfus J. R., Stowe R. L., Sweeder R. D., Tessmer S., Underwood S. M. (2024). Beyond active learning: Using 3-Dimensional learning to create scientifically authentic, student-centered classrooms. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0295887

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square analysis of 417 video-coded instructional segments (82 class sessions) designated active or not and 3D or not using a 50% time criterion. The article reports "chi-square, 33.9, p_fisher <0.001, phi = 0.285".

> "we find a significant association between 3D and active segments with a small-to-medium effect size (chi-square, 33.9, p_fisher <0.001, phi = 0.285)"

### Cooper M. M. 2024 (2)

Cooper M. M., Caballero M. D., Carmel J. H., Duffy E. M., Ebert-May D., Fata-Hartley C. L., Herrington D. G., Laverty J. T., Nelson P. C., Posey L. A., Stoltzfus J. R., Stowe R. L., Sweeder R. D., Tessmer S., Underwood S. M. (2024). Beyond active learning: Using 3-Dimensional learning to create scientifically authentic, student-centered classrooms. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0295887

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-hoc standardized residual analysis of the contingency table; the article states the critical value is 9.14 and that co-occurring segments drive the association.

> "the major driver of significance is the higher-than-expected number of segments in which 3D & active learning co-occur."
