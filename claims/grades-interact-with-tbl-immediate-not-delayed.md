## Evidence

### Tan Nigel CK 2011

Tan Nigel CK, Kandiah Nagaendran, Chan Yiong Huak, Umapathi Thirugnanam, Lee Sze Haur, Tan Kevin. (2011). A controlled study of team-based learning for undergraduate clinical neurology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-91

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Formal interaction testing within the mixed model ANCOVA of the 49-student study. The grade-by-TBL interaction was significant for the immediate outcome only; the article also found "no significant interaction of gender or TBL topic" with the primary outcome.

> "the effect of examination grades on improvement in test scores was significant from baseline to post-test 1 (p = 0.007), but was not significant from baseline to post-test 2 (p = 0.22)"
