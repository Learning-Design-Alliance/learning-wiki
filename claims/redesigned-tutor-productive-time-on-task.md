## Evidence

### Qianru Lyu 2026

Qianru Lyu, Conrad Borchers, Meng Xia, Karen Xiao, Paulo F. Carvalho, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Evaluating a Data-Driven Redesign Process for Intelligent Tutoring Systems. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory log-data analysis of practice behavior in the crossover study, comparing conditions on time-on-task computed by subtracting idle periods (more than two minutes of inactivity) from logged-in time. Redesigned students also logged in more (M = 47.7 vs. 39.3, t(235) = 2.44, p = .015); idle time was comparable (t(234) = 0.46, p = .650), indicating "greater productive engagement".

> "They also engaged in more active, non-idle time (M= 38.9,SD= 21.8) compared to the Original Tutor (M= 31.6,SD= 20.4),t(234) = 4.62,p < .001."
