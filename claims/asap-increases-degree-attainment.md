# ASAP likely increases community college students' graduation and degree attainment rates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r1` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In two RCTs meeting WWC standards without reservations, ASAP produced a positive, statistically significant effect on attainment, with a domain-average effect size of 0.45 and improvement index of +17 percentile points. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
`q4 i2` In the Scrivener et al. (2015) RCT, 51.9 percent of ASAP students earned any degree within six years versus 40.4 percent of comparison students (effect size 0.28, p < .01). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
`q4 i3` In the Sommo et al. (2018) Ohio RCT, 19.0 percent of ASAP students earned a degree or certificate within two years versus 7.9 percent of comparison students (effect size 0.61, p < .01). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2019](#what-works-clearinghouse-2019)
