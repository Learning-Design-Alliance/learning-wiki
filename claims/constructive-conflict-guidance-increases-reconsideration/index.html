Howard Ziyu Han 2026¶

Howard Ziyu Han, Nikolas Martelaro. (2026). Enacting Constructive Conflicts with AI Agents to Enhance Reconsideration among Novice Interaction Designers. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04166

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Between-subjects experiment with 45 design students in three conditions; Kruskal–Wallis on the post-survey item showed "significant group differences" with the printed omnibus effect size ε² = .19, a large effect.

"On the critical thinking item“To what extent do you reconsider what makes a design effective in this challenge?”, significant group differences were found (𝐻( 2)= 8.30, 𝑝=. 016,𝜀2 =. 19, large effect)."
