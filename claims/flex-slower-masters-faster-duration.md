## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Spagnulo R 2025

Spagnulo R, Marzola F, Corso F, Distefano G, Pescio M, Hayashi K, Barontini F, Dagnino G, Althoefer K, Siciliano B, Ourselin S, Arezzo A. (2025). From precision to strength: computer vision for suture quality assessment—an ex vivo pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12441-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Duration analysis of the 45-procedure platform comparison using Kruskal–Wallis and post hoc tests. "Flex was associated with significantly longer task times compared to both laparoscopy and dVRK"; mean times were 3141.13 s (Flex) vs 1341.13 s (laparoscopy) and 1594.53 s (dVRK).

> "Statistically significant differences in execution time were identified across devices (p < 0.01) before and after one outlier removal. Post hoc comparisons demonstrated that Flex was associated with significantly longer task times compared to both laparoscopy and dVRK"

### Spagnulo R 2025 (2)

Spagnulo R, Marzola F, Corso F, Distefano G, Pescio M, Hayashi K, Barontini F, Dagnino G, Althoefer K, Siciliano B, Ourselin S, Arezzo A. (2025). From precision to strength: computer vision for suture quality assessment—an ex vivo pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12441-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experience-level duration analysis in the same pilot: no significant differences before outlier exclusion; after removing four outliers, experts differed significantly from beginners and advanced users (p < 0.05), with Masters fastest on all platforms (mean 1774.53 s).

> "After removing four outliers, the Kruskal–Wallis test revealed significant differences (p < 0.05) between experts and both beginners and advanced users"
