Egenberg Signe, Karlsen Bjørg, Massay Deodatus, Kimaro Happiness, Bru Lars Edvin. (2017). “No patient should die of PPH just for the lack of training!” Experiences from multi-professional simulation training on postpartum hemorrhage in northern Tanzania: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0957-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding on communication and leadership: although calling for help was already in the national and local PPH protocol, there had been no proper response because teamwork had not been emphasized; informants described the PPH shout as now sending a clear alert message.

> "After the training, leadership during emergencies was established and many informants expressed how shouting for help mobilized equal attention from the team during an emergency, regardless of who was calling:"
