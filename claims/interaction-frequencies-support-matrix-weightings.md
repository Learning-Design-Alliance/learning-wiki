## Evidence

### Dietzel 1974

Dietzel, C. S.; Abeles, Norman. (1974). Client-Therapist Complementarity and Therapeutic Outcome. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED091646

`q2 · i?`

Discussion-section analysis of the distribution of all rated client-therapist interactions across the 16 matrix cells, treating relative frequency as reward value. The authors report "55%" of interactions in weight-3 cells, "29%" in weight-2 cells, and "16%" in weight-1 cells, taken as support for the weighting scheme.

> "the greatest proportion of client-therapist interactions (55%) occurred in the cells weighted by "3"; 29% of the interactions occurred in the cells weighted by "2"; and 16% occurred in the cells weighted by "1.""
