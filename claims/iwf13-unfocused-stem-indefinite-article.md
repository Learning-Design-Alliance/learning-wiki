## Evidence

### Giacomo Cassano 2026

Giacomo Cassano, Daniel Ebbert, Negin Mirriahi, Nicoletta Di Blas. (2026). Instructors’ perceptions of a large language model framework for generating automated multiple-choice questions from video transcripts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10646-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Researcher inspection of formal IWF criteria across the evaluated items found only two violated criteria, IWF 1 (one case) and IWF 13. The study reports "IWF 13 was observed in 31.13% of cases"; among the 80 affected items, 64 stemmed from the indefinite article "a".

> "IWF 13 was observed in 31.13% of cases, most frequently due to the use of an indefinite article (e.g., “a key factor”) instead of a definite one, potentially implying multiple correct answers."
