### Strubbe 2019

Strubbe, L. E., Madsen, A. M., McKagan, S. B., & Sayre, E. C. (2019). Beyond teaching methods: highlighting physics faculty's strengths and agency. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.09586](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.09586)

### Strubbe 2019 (2)

Strubbe, L. E., Madsen, A. M., McKagan, S. B., & Sayre, E. C. (2019). Beyond teaching methods: highlighting physics faculty's strengths and agency. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1912.09586](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.09586)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The article reports, citing Finkelstein & Pollock, that "faithful implementation of a teaching method in a new context can lead to similar student learning gains", while noting "the contexts were both large U.S. research universities".

> "While Finkelstein & Pollock demonstrated that faithful implementation of a teaching method in a new context can lead to similar student learning gains [36], the contexts were both large U.S. research universities"
