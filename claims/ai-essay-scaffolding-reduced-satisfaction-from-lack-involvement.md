`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of interview data from 11 participants (interview #2.1) revealed that reduced involvement in the problem-solving process when using AI for essay writing decreased satisfaction, though participants appreciated AI's efficiency.

> "Participants felt much less satisfaction when they submitted the AI-generated essay (Activity #2.1) as they sensed they were not involved enough in the problem-solving process."
