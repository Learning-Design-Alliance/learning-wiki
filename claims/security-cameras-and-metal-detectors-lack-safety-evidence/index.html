Evidence¶

dePaoli 2023¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports that despite 91% of schools using cameras in 2019–20, only one study examined their impact, finding cameras "not associated with reduced crime or social disturbance."

"There is little evidence on the impact of security cameras on school safety. The one study that examined the impact of implementing security cameras on school safety found security cameras were not associated with reduced crime or social disturbance."

dePaoli 2023 (2)¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports a literature review identifying only two studies, both survey-based and neither allowing causal interpretation. The second study, using national survey data, found no relationship between metal detectors and student reports of victimization.

"one study of New York City high school students found a relationship between the presence of metal detectors in schools and reduced rates of students reporting carrying weapons to school, but no relationship between the presence of metal detectors and the number of threats or physical fights."
