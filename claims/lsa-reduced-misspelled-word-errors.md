## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The average number of misspelled word errors per message dropped from 1.41 (pre-LSA, N=87) to 0.31 (post-LSA, N=85), a statistically significant reduction (p < 0.01). [→ Austin 2017](#austin-2017)

## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, T. L., Sigmar, L. S., Mehta, G. B., & Shirk, J. L. (2017). The impact of web-assisted instruction on student writing outcomes in business communication. Journal of Instructional Pedagogies, 20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1178728

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on misspelled word errors: pre-LSA mean 1.41 (SD 1.483, N=87) vs. post-LSA mean 0.31 (SD 0.708, N=85), t = 6.276, p = 0.000.
