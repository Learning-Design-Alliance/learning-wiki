## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The failure mark differs in kind from ranking marks: it signals that the student would better not proceed along a line of work without repeating the subject. [→ Kohl 1911](#kohl-1911)

### Kohl 1911

Kohl, C. C., & Hughes, P. (1911). Communications and discussions. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

Conceptual argument from Hughes's communication on college marking systems, offered without empirical data. The article argues that "the mark of failure is not a ranking mark" and therefore the ideal number of failures must always be just none at all, unlike ranking marks whose percentages follow a standard.

> "the mark of failure is not a ranking mark; it indicates that the student would better not proceed along a certain line of work without repeating the subject in which he has failed"
