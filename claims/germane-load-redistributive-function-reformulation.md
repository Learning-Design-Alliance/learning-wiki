# Germane cognitive load is reformulated as redistributing working memory resources from extraneous to intrinsic processing rather than adding to total load

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Empirical studies showing total load falls when extraneous load is reduced motivated redefining germane load as redistributive rather than additive. [→ Sweller 2019](#sweller-2019)

## Evidence

### Sweller 2019

Sweller, J., van Merriënboer, J. J. G., & Paas, F. (2019). Cognitive Architecture and Instructional Design: 20 Years Later. Educational Psychology Review, 31, 261–292. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09465-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Theoretical reformulation in this narrative review, motivated because "Numerous empirical studies indicated a reduction in load following a reduction in extraneous load," contradicting the 1998 assumption that germane load substitutes for extraneous load at constant total load.

> "The current formulation eliminates this problem by assuming that germane cognitive load has a redistributive function from extraneous to intrinsic aspects of the task rather than imposing a load in its own right."
