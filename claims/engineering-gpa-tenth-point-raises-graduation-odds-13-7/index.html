The two-year transfer analysis reports an odds-based association: "for every tenth of a grade point increase in engineering GPA, the odds of a black student graduating with an engineering degree in six years improves by 13.7 percent." The 13.7 percent figure is the printed magnitude; no standardized effect size is given.
