## Evidence

### Yang Yu 2026

Yang Yu, Xue Jiarui, Hu Lingfei, Fang Kai, Wei Chaobing, Wu Changfan, Jin Huixiang. (2026). A combined PBL and flipped classroom intervention enhances self-directed learning ability and empathy in ophthalmology undergraduates. Scientific Reports 16, 11696. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47584-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental controlled intervention with third-year clinical medicine students from eight classes (PBL-FC n = 43, traditional n = 44), non-randomized by class assignment. Between-group independent t-test after class showed "175.72 ± 16.20 vs. 156.19 ± 12.72"; Table 2 reports total Cohen's d = 1.34, mean difference 19.54 (95% CI 13.34 to 25.74), P < 0.001.

> "After class, the PBL-FC group demonstrated significantly higher total self-directed learning ability scores (175.72 ± 16.20 vs. 156.19 ± 12.72) and scores across all sub-dimensions (P < 0.05)."
