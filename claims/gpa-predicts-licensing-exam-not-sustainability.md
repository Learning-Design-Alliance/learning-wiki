### Walkiewicz M 2026

Walkiewicz M. (2026). Why academic selection fails to protect against physician burnout: a brief research report from a 25-year longitudinal programme. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1836438

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal cohort followed from 1999; predictive validity of baseline measures for outcomes assessed 4 years after graduation, from multiple linear regression (forward selection) reported in Tartas et al. (22). The article reports "accounting for 21% of variance" for the licensing exam, while "academic achievement showed no significant association with career satisfaction [R² = 0.01, F(6,48) = 0.05, ns], burnout [R² = 0.01, F(6,48) = 0.09, ns]".

> "Academic achievement during medical school, indexed by six-year grade-point average, predicted performance on the postgraduate licensing examination, accounting for 21% of variance [F(6,270) = 11.95, p < 0.001]."
