## Evidence

### Demedts 2024

Demedts, D., Magerman, J., Goossens, E., Tricas-Sauras, S., Bilsen, J., De Smet, S., & Fobelets, M. (2024). Using simulation to teach nursing students how to deal with a euthanasia request. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0299049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic content analysis of three audio-recorded group interviews with 17 final-year nursing students who completed the euthanasia simulation module. Students preferred the reflective DML format; one said "I like that reflecting better, half the CRM-principles are not covered." No effect size reported.

> "The students recognized the value of this approach and indicated that using CRM-principles for debriefing was less suitable for this type of simulation."
