`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Transcriptome-wide association analyses (S-PrediXcan/S-MultiXcan using GTEx v7 elastic-net weights) linked genetically regulated expression to phenotypes; the entry quotes the DUS3L result across four brain regions, with HSD3B7-EVA_total (FDR p = 9.55E-20) the most significant S-MultiXcan association and SEMA6C-CVB_Total (FDR p = 2.77E-12) the strongest for Chinese literacy. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "The most signiﬁcant associations were observed for DUS3L, which showed signiﬁcant associations (FDR < 0.05) with EWR_Total in four brain regions including amygdala, caudate basal, cerebellar hemisphere and putamen"
