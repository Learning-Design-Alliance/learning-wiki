## Subclaims
`q2 i?` NRP providers randomised to eSim plus NRP textbook preparation performed several steps of the NRP algorithm more correctly than the textbook-only group, with no difference between groups in time to perform key NRP steps. [→ Ghoman SK 2020](#ghoman-sk-2020)

## Evidence

### Ghoman SK 2020

Ghoman SK, Patel SD, Cutumisu M, von Hauff P, Jeffery T, Brown MRG, Schmölzer GM. (2020). Serious games, a game changer in teaching neonatal resuscitation? A review. Archives of Disease in Childhood. Fetal and Neonatal Edition. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2019-317011

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomised trial of 255 NRP providers from four US institutions comparing eSim plus NRP textbook (n=167) with textbook only (n=88) preparation before the in-class NRP course; performance was video-recorded during a standardised simulation. No effect size is printed.

> "HCPs randomised to pre-NRP course preparation of eSim+NRP textbook performed several steps of the NRP algorithm more correctly compared with HCPs in the textbook-only group (ie, initial steps, mask adjustment, reposition airway)"

### Ghoman SK 2020 (2)

Ghoman SK, Patel SD, Cutumisu M, von Hauff P, Jeffery T, Brown MRG, Schmölzer GM. (2020). Serious games, a game changer in teaching neonatal resuscitation? A review. Archives of Disease in Childhood. Fetal and Neonatal Edition. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2019-317011

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Same randomised trial of 255 NRP providers; the review reports no difference between the two preparation groups in time to perform key NRP steps. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.

> "However, there was no difference in time needed to perform key NRP steps (ie, time to start positive-pressure ventilation (PPV), chest compressions)"
