## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pilato Jazelle 2025

Pilato Jazelle, Cortes Robert, Peterson Emily Grossnickle, Uttal David, Kolvoord Robert, Green Adam. (2025). Neurocognitive correlates of spatial thinking mediate the relation between socioeconomic status and STEM achievement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-29345-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (1000 samples) in the cognitive sample, with gender and age as covariates, run separately for parental education (N=303) and family income (N=104). Both indirect effects were 0.05 with CIs excluding zero; both concluded partial mediation.

> "The bootstrapped standardized indirect effect was 0.05, and the 95% confidence interval ranged from 0.01 to 0.09. Since the confidence interval did not cross zero, the partial indirect effect was statistically significant"
