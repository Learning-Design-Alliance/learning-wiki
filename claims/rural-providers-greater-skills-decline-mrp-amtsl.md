### Mwilike Beatrice Erastus 2026

Long-term knowledge and skills retention following Helping Mothers Survive and Helping Babies Survive training in Tanzania: a mixed-methods follow-up study. `q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing skills decline by residence among 210 providers. MRP showed η2 = 0.067 and AMTSL η2 = 0.029, with Cohen's d = 0.54 and d = 0.35 respectively.

> "Participants working in rural areas had a statistically significantly higher drop in mean skills scores for manual removal of placenta [F(1, 208) = 14.917, p < 0.001] and for active management of third stage of labor [F(1, 208) = 6.271, p = 0.013]"
