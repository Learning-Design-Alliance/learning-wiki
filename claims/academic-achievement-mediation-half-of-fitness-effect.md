## Evidence

### Andersen Mikkel Porsborg 2017

Andersen Mikkel Porsborg, Starkof Liis, Sessa Maurizio, Mortensen Rikke Nørmark, Vardinghus-Nielsen Henrik, Bøggild Henrik, Lange Theis, Torp-Pedersen Christian. (2017). The indirect and direct pathways between physical fitness and academic achievement on commencement in post-compulsory education in a historical cohort of Danish school youth. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-017-4712-y

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Imputation-based nested-counterfactual mediation analysis (natural effect model, logistic regression) in the same 1084-pupil cohort. The article reports "strong evidence of a natural indirect effect of physical fitness mediated through academic achievement", with a proportion mediated of 51% (95% CI: 27%; 122%).

> "The natural indirect effect component of the total effect represents an OR of 1.37 (95% CI: 1.20; 1.57), resulting in strong evidence of a natural indirect effect of physical fitness mediated through academic achievement."
