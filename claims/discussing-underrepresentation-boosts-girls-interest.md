The review reports follow-up work by Hazari's group surveying only high-school girls on five hypothesized factors: single-gender classes, women guest speakers, women physics teachers, discussing women scientists, and discussing underrepresentation. Only the last correlated positively; no coefficient is printed.

> "Of these variables, only the discussion of the underrepresentation of women positively correlated with an increase in girls' interest in a career in the physical sciences"
