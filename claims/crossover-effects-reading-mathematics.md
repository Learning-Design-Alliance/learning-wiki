## Evidence

### Spencer Mercedes 2022

Spencer Mercedes, Fuchs Lynn S., Geary David C., Fuchs Douglas. (2022). Connections between Mathematics and Reading Development: Numerical Cognition Mediates Relations between Foundational Competencies and Later Academic Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000670

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

From the final pruned mediation model's crossover-effect results in the Discussion, controlling earlier calculations and WPS scores and domain-general abilities; the printed path coefficients were 0.126 (calculations) and 0.149 (WPS) per SD increase in early reading ability, unstandardized effects with no standardized effect size printed.

> "Each SD increase in early reading ability was associated with 0.126 and 0.149 SD increases in later calculations and WPS, respectively."

### Spencer Mercedes 2022 (2)

Spencer Mercedes, Fuchs Lynn S., Geary David C., Fuchs Douglas. (2022). Connections between Mathematics and Reading Development: Numerical Cognition Mediates Relations between Foundational Competencies and Later Academic Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000670

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mathematics-to-reading crossover effects from the same final pruned model; the article reports "a 0.062 and 0.017 increase in later reading performance" for early calculation skill and WPS respectively, and notes these appeared weaker than the reverse direction.

> "each SD increase in early calculation skill and WPS, respectively, was associated with a 0.062 and 0.017 increase in later reading performance."
