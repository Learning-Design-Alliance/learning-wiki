## Evidence

### Scior Katrina 2014

Scior Katrina, Bradley Caroline E, Potts Henry W W, Woolf Katherine, de C Williams Amanda C. (2014). What predicts performance during clinical psychology training? The British Journal of Clinical Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjc.12035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In regressions of 274 trainees, supervisor ratings significantly predicted exam 1 (B = 0.21, p = .001), exam 2 (B = 0.26, p < .001), exam 3 (B = 0.16, p = .01), exam 4 (B = 0.33, p < .001) and case report marks (IRR = 0.8, p < .001), plus placement concerns (t262 = 3.9, p < .001) and viva outcome (t262 = 3.1, p = .002).

> "Retrospective research supervisor ratings correlated with all outcomes and predicted case report marks and three of the four exam marks suggesting that these may measure global course ability, rather than just research skills."
