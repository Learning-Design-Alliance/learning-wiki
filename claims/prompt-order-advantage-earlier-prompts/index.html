DeSimone and Buzza 2013¶

Frank DeSimone and John Buzza. (2013). Experiential Learning: Improving The Efficacy Of An Undergraduate Business Degree. American Journal Of Business Education, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1054187

Descriptive prompt-order analysis within the Wagner College study; the article also reports a parallel Monmouth pattern where beginning prompt 1 to ending prompt 3 declined 17.32% while earlier-positioned comparisons improved. The authors call the order effect "one of the weaknesses of the research study."
