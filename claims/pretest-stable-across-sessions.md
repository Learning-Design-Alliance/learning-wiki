# Phoneme-category response curves along the /aba/–/ada/ continuum remained stable across sessions, with a younger-child response bias toward /aba/

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-test /aba/ response proportions to the nine continuum sounds did not change across sessions, but interacted with baseline age. [→ Romanovska 2022](#romanovska-2022)

## Evidence

### Romanovska 2022

Romanovska, L., Janssen, R. & Bonte, M. (2022). Longitudinal changes in cortical responses to letter-speech sound stimuli in 8–11 year-old children. npj Science of Learning 7:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00118-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-test in a mock scanner (98 presentations of each of 9 tokens) used to individually determine the most ambiguous sound. A session × sound RM ANCOVA showed a sound main effect and a sound × baseline age interaction; younger children's responses were "biased more towards /aba/".

> "revealed a main effect of sound [F(2,24) = 4.087, p < 0.05, Greenhouse-Geisser corrected] and an interaction between sound and children ’s age in months during session 1 (baseline age) [F (2,24) = 5.629, p < 0.01, Greenhouse-Geisser corrected]."
