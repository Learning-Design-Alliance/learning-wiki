### Hehl 2026

Hehl, M., Toyonaga, T., Carson, R. E., Dupont, P., Van Laere, K., Swinnen, S. P. & Cuypers, K. (2026). No significant changes in synaptic density and gray matter volume following motor learning—a pilot study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00451-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed model on progress-test scores of the 1:1 subtask in the 22-participant pilot, with SUBJECT as random effect and GROUP and BTT time as fixed effects. The article reports "a signiﬁcant effect on task performance on the 1:1 task" of training time, with no group effect (p = 0.9671).

> "The time spent on the BTT training paradigm (β = 8.1053, SE = 0.6741, df = 110, t = 12.023, p < 0.0001) had a signiﬁcant effect on task performance on the 1:1 task, but not GROUP ( p = 0.9671)"
