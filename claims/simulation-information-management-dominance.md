## Evidence

### Fornander L 2024

Fornander L, Garrido Granhagen M, Molin I, Laukkanen K, Björnström Karlsson K, Berggren P, Nilsson L. (2024). The use of specific coordination behaviours to manage information processing and task distribution in real and simulated trauma teamwork: an observational study. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-024-01287-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational coding of four video-recorded simulated trauma resuscitations (1728 utterances) with the modified CoMet-E system. The abstract reports information management at "68% during simulation" and the Results state there was "no difference regarding the proportion of information- and task management communication" between domains.

> "The analysis showed that although the teamwork was task-oriented, information management dominated task management at 64% of all the utterances in-real-life and 68% during simulation."
