### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Sejun Oh. (2026). LLM-Driven Rubric-Based Assessment of Algebraic Competence in Multi-Stage Block Coding Tasks with Design and Field Evaluation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3390/app162110595](https://doi.org/10.3390/app162110595)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field study comparing Rubric 3 outcomes across scenario engagement levels using a 70% correctness threshold to classify 42 students into path-completed and path-incomplete groups. The comparison was significant, "t = 4.28, p < 0.001"; no effect size is printed.

> "the path-completed group achieved substantially higher Rubric 3 scores ( M = 78.8, SD = 18.7) than the path -incomplete group (M = 52.5, SD = 21.3), with Welch's t-test confirming the significance of the difference (t = 4.28, p < 0.001)."
