# Too Good for Drugs shows no discernible effects on student knowledge, attitudes, and values across two randomized controlled trials

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

WWC aggregation of follow-up findings from both RCTs (Bacon 2000: 1,060 sixth-graders; Bacon 2003: 935 grades 3-4 students). Study averages were 0.20 and 0.11, domain average 0.16, all "neither statistically significant nor substantively important"; individual outcomes ranged from 0.08 to 0.27, all ns.

> "When the WWC aggregated the results across all outcomes in this domain in each of the studies, the domain average effect size in each of the studies was neither statistically significant nor substantively important."
