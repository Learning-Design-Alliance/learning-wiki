`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 of the qualitative thematic analysis with 11 GP registrars. Participants "identified professional pressures, such as lack of time and heavy workload, as barriers to revisiting this knowledge", compounded by the vast scope of medical knowledge and limited accessible resources, and by patient expectations.

> "The registrars recognised the relevance and utility of basic sciences in clinical practice, but identified professional pressures, such as lack of time and heavy workload, as barriers to revisiting this knowledge"
