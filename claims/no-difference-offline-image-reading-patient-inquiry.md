### Wang Qianyun 2025

Wang Qianyun, Wang Feifei, jiang Can, Guo Jun. (2025). The role of cardiac ultrasound virtual simulation technology in the construction of clinical diagnostic reasoning of structural heart diseases. BMC Medical Education 25:634. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07225-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized experiment, offline image reading (10 double-blind graded questions worth 20 points) showed no significant group difference; equivalence was not tested. The authors state both groups "adopted the same method for acquiring basic knowledge before class."

> "The score for offline image reading in the VR group was better than that in the control group but the differences were not statistically significant (14.34 ± 3.03 VS 14.27 ± 3.05, P > 0.05)."
