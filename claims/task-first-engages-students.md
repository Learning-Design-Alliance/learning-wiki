## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers reported a consensus that having the challenge first was highly engaging, with students eagerly awaiting the challenge. [→ Russo and Hopkins 2017](#russo-and-hopkins-2017)

## Evidence

### Russo and Hopkins 2017

James Russo, Sarah Hopkins. (2017). How does lesson structure shape teacher perceptions of teaching with challenging tasks? Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(1), 30-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1152737

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of three teacher-participants; Rachel described "a certain buzz when they came in and had to do the challenge first" and Sally named students who wanted to be first to work the challenge out in their heads.

> "there also seemed to be a consensus amongst teachers that having the challenge first was a highly engaging way in which to begin the lesson."
