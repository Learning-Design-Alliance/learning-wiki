# Transfer between tasks is linearly dependent on the unsquared intertask correlation corrected for attenuation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the validation experiment, regression of transfer measures on the unsquared, attenuation-corrected intercorrelation showed a significant linear component, with some coefficients in the .80's. [→ Flammer 1973](#flammer-1973)

## Evidence

### Flammer 1973

Flammer, A. (1973). A Conceptual Model Relating Transfer of Learning and Correlation: Theoretical Paper No. 43. Wisconsin Research and Development Center for Cognitive Learning. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED085637

`q3 · i1`

Validation experiment with 385 German-speaking Swiss fifth graders in three experimental groups and one control group, trained on letter-series tasks via programmed booklets; transfer was mean experimental gain minus control gain. The article reports that "Some coefficients are of an impressive height, i.e., in the .80's."

> "With very few exceptions the rTc's are statistically significant, even in the cross-validation cases. Some coefficients are of an impressive height, i.e., in the .80's."
