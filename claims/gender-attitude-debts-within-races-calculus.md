### Jayson M. Nissen 2021

Jayson M. Nissen, Ian Her Many Horses, and Ben Van Dusen. (2021). Investigating society's educational debts due to racism and sexism in student attitudes about physics using quantitative critical race theory. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 010116. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.010116

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear model predicted means for CLASS scores in calculus-based courses, comparing men and women within each racial group. The article reports "men had more expertlike attitudes than women for all racial groups," with pretest debts of 2.4–4.6 and post-test debts of 1.0–4.3 percentage points; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "In the calculus-based courses, men had more expertlike attitudes than women for all racial groups. These educational debts owed by society ranged from 2.4 to 4.6 percentage points on the pretest and 1.0 to 4.3 percentage points on the post-test."
