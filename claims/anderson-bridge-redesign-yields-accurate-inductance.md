Authors' own test of a bridge designed per their analysis (headphone resistance about 100Ω, inductance about 22 mH). The first Table 4 reading gave L = 136.7 mH for a 130 mH coil at 3 kHz, and "For increase of ±1Ω in the ﬁrst reading we could detect increase in sound intensity."
