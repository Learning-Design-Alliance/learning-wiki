## Evidence

### Nicholas Feroce 2025

Nicholas Feroce, Jenny Liu, Rajendra Chattergoon. (2025). Exploring the impact of a CALL tool for emergent bilinguals. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10462-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression on a binary proficiency outcome (performance level 4, 'well developed') for the full sample. Odds ratios for program use exceeded 1 in the expected direction for overall and oral ELPAC but were non-significant; the article suggests intervention intensity as a possible reason.

> "Students who used the CALL program and students who did not were found not to be significantly different in terms of English proficiency, both for the overall ELPAC and subdomains."
