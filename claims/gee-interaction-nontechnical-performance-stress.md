# GEE analysis confirms a significant interaction between non-technical performance and stress on technical CPR performance

`q3 i?` The GEE model found a significant ANTS score by stress interaction (interaction coefficient 9.9, adjusted p=0.001). [→ Krage Ralf 2017](#krage-ralf-2017)

## Evidence

### Krage Ralf 2017

Krage Ralf, Zwaan Laura, Tjon Soei Len Lian, Kolenbrander Mark W, van Groeningen Dick, Loer Stephan A, Wagner Cordula, Schober Patrick. (2017). Relationship between non-technical skills and technical performance during cardiopulmonary resuscitation: does stress have an influence? Emergency Medicine Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/emermed-2016-205754

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalised estimating equation regression on the transformed technical score from the cross-over simulator study, accounting for repeated observations per participant. The article reports the interaction "unadjusted p=0.015, adjusted for period effects=0.001"; in the adjusted model a 1-point ANTS increase was associated with a 10.2-point increase under stressors.

> "The GEE analysis provides strong evidence for an interaction between non-technical performance and stress (unadjusted p=0.015, adjusted for period effects=0.001), confirming that the relationship between non-technical and technical performance varies depending on the presence or absence of external stressors"
