### Agrawal A 2026

Agrawal A, Nimonkar S, Belkhode V, Godbole S, Zilpilwar N, Munje R, Nimonkar P. (2026). Assessment of the knowledge and awareness among general medical practitioners toward dental intervention in diagnosing and treating obstructive sleep apnea: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08867-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Composite score analysis of the 125-practitioner survey: knowledge items 1–12 (range 0–12) and awareness items 13–15 (range 0–3). The article reports "a correlation exists between knowledge and awareness (X^2 = 127.31, df = 36, p < 0.0001)"; 62.4% of respondents fell in the low awareness band (0–3).

> "The average composite knowledge score was 6.72 on a scale of 1–12 (with a Standard Deviation of 3.08); this indicates the majority of respondents showed some familiarity with the fundamental concepts, but the range of results varied greatly."
