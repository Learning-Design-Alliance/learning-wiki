### Borman Geoffrey D. 2026

Borman Geoffrey D., Yang Hyunwoo. (2026). Cumulative access to print books improves literacy achievement: Evidence from a five-year randomized trial in high-poverty schools. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2521416123

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of a five-year cluster-randomized trial (30 treatment, 30 control schools) using a two-level hierarchical linear model with student covariates and the spring 2022-23 Wisconsin Forward Exam ELA outcome. The treatment "effect size of d = 0.100" was statistically significant (P = 0.049).

> "Students in treatment schools at baseline statistically significantly outperformed their peers in control schools on the spring 2022–23 reading assessment, with an estimated treatment effect coefficient of 0.106 (SE = 0.054, P = 0.049), which is equivalent to a treatment effect size of d = 0.100."
