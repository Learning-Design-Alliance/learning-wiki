## Evidence

### Bahrick 1991

Bahrick, H. P., & Hall, L. K. (1991). Lifetime maintenance of high school mathematics content. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 120(1), 20-33. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.120.1.20

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional regression analysis of Algebra 1 test performance for 1,050 participants tested up to 50 years after acquisition, with profiles generated from the regression equation. The article reports that "Those who have taken three or more college mathematics courses with the highest level course above calculus show minimal losses" and that those with no further courses "reduce their original score to the control group level during the retention interval."

> "Those who have taken three or more college mathematics courses with the highest level course above calculus show minimal losses during the course of more than 50 years even though they report no rehearsal activities during that period."
