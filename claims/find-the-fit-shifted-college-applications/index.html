Linkow 2021¶

Linkow, T., Parsad, A., Martinez, A., & Miller, H. (2021). Study of Enhanced College Advising in Upward Bound: Impacts on Where and How Long Students Attend College (NCEE 2021-2022-002r). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The study's prior report (Martinez et al. 2018), summarized in Exhibit 5 with survey data from 1,920 Find the Fit and 1,710 regular advising students; the exhibit notes differences "statistically significant at the .05 level." No standardized effect size is printed.

"Students offered Find the Fit were about 10 percentage points more likely to apply to four or more colleges and apply to at least one college at the very competitive level or higher, compared to students in regular UB advising projects."
