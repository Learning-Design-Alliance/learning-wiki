Evidence¶

Markos Stamatakis 2026¶

Markos Stamatakis, Omkar Gavali, Joshua Berger, Christian Wartena, Anett Hoppe, and Ralph Ewerth. (2026). Are LLM-based Chatbots Good Enough to Support Computer Science Students in Multiple-Choice Exercises? https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15919 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.15919

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Questionnaire with five-point Likert scales and open-text feedback in both user-study scenarios (Sec. 5). The article reports that "students view these models as useful for learning but requiring critical use", with trust more skeptical for CV-MCQs than IVDA-MCQs. Self-report only; no effect size.

"Overall, students view these models as useful for learning but requiring critical use (RQ4)."
