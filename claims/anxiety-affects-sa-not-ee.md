## Evidence

### Riquin Élise 2026

Riquin Élise, Le Nerzé Thomas, Goulin Jeanne, Rony Louis, Boucher Sophie, Martin Ludovic, Schmitt Françoise. (2026). Student self-assessment: feasibility, advantages and limitations; example of a workshop for trainee surgeons using a suture score. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08223-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of 70 trainee doctors' scores against STAI Y-A anxiety found no significant correlation with EE checklist scores but "a low but significant inverse correlation between the SA by checklist and the STAI Y-A scores".

> "The checklist score in EE was not correlated with the STAI Y-A score (r = −0.20; p = 0.096). However, there was a low but significant inverse correlation between the SA by checklist and the STAI Y-A scores (r = −0.29; p < 0.05)."
