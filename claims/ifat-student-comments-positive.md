`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative collection of written comments from end-of-semester course evaluation forms after the scratch-off review exercises. The authors report "All the comments were positive; we received no negative comments", with comments citing seeing where they struggled and working in groups to figure out answers.

> "We asked students to provide us some written comments, either positive or negative, regarding the IF -AT cards.  Students liked using the IF -AT cards."
