Evidence¶

Brumpt Eléonore 2023¶

Brumpt Eléonore, Bertin Eugénie, Tatu Laurent, Louvrier Aurélien. (2023). 3D printing as a pedagogical tool for teaching normal human anatomy: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04744-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Cai et al. and Smith et al., comparisons against dry bones: "significantly better test results for the groups who used 3DPAM"; no effect sizes are printed.

"The studies by Cai and Smith found significantly better test results for the groups who used 3DPAM [51, 63]."

Brumpt Eléonore 2023 (2)¶

Brumpt Eléonore, Bertin Eugénie, Tatu Laurent, Louvrier Aurélien. (2023). 3D printing as a pedagogical tool for teaching normal human anatomy: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04744-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Chen et al., that 3DPAM students were "better at recognizing structures (skull)" with "no difference in MCQ results", a mixed pattern across outcome measures.

"Chen and colleagues specified that students who used the 3D model were better at recognizing structures (skull) but that there was no difference in MCQ results [69]."
