### Stegers-Jager K. M. 2016

Stegers-Jager K. M., Brommet F. N., Themmen A. P. N. (2016). Ethnic and social disparities in different types of examinations in undergraduate pre-clinical training. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-016-9676-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Authors' interpretation of the cohort results: Western students' OSCE pass rates (70 % year 2, 74 % year 3) did not differ significantly from Dutch students, and no lower clinical skills level was found that could explain previously reported lower clerkship grades for first-generation students.

> "it is remarkable that in the current study no underperformance was found in the two OSCEs for Western students. Similarly, the current study also found no indications for a lower level of clinical skills that could explain the previously reported lower clerkship grades for first-generation university students"
