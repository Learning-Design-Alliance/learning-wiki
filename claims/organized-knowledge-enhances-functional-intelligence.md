Theoretical argument in the section on enhancing novel problem solving: the article argues that domain-specific expertise (crystallized intelligence) supports reasoning on novel problems and, via the chunking effect, may increase working memory processing capacity. No empirical data are reported.

> "Such extensive and well-organized knowledge base could improve functional use of intelligence, the ability to use intelligence in productive ways. For example, it could provide potential analogies and general domain principles to be used while reasoning and solving truly novel problems."
