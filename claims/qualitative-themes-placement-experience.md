## Evidence

### Quail Michelle 2016

Quail Michelle, Brundage Shelley B, Spitalnick Josh, Allen Peter J, Beilby Janet. (2016). Student self-reported communication skills, knowledge and confidence across standardised patient, virtual and traditional clinical learning environments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0577-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke procedures, NVivo 10) of four open-ended post-placement questions from the 62 students; themes were Challenge, Realism and Authenticity, Clinical Value, Value of the Clinical Educator, Absence of Context, and Physical Environment.

> "The VLE (referred to as the 'avatar' by participants) was reported as more challenging in comparison to the other two conditions. Interestingly, most of the reported challenges were deemed positive, and promoted reflection and professional development"
