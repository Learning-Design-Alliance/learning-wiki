### McCarthy Roisin 2026

McCarthy Roisin, Maher Liam, O'Dea Angela. (2026). Validation status of definitive airway management simulators: a systematic review. BJA: British Journal of Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2026.01.027

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review discussion of the single included FONA study (Kovatch 2020), a 3D-printed paediatric cricothyrotomy model validated only by expert opinion (n=6), with no construct or predictive assessment, no control groups, and no clinical performance outcomes.

> "Unlike intubation or fibreoptic techniques, real-world predictive validity cannot be ethically or practically evaluated for FONA procedures."
