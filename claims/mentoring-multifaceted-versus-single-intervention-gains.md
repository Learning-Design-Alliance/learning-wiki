## Evidence

### Abdullah 2014

Abdullah, G., Rossy, D., Ploeg, J., Davies, B., Higuchi, K., Sikora, L., & Stacey, D. (2014). Measuring the Effectiveness of Mentoring as a Knowledge Translation Intervention for Implementing Empirical Evidence: A Systematic Review. Worldviews on Evidence-Based Nursing, 11(5), 284-300. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.12060

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Cluster-randomized trial (Gattellari et al., 2005) in Australian primary care with 277 physicians implementing a lower urinary tract symptoms guideline; the review reports the knowledge and belief (OR = .31) contrasts favoring the mentoring multifaceted arm over educational materials alone.

> "Compared to educational materials alone, physicians exposed to mentoring as part of a multifaceted intervention reported improved knowledge of prostate cancer screening (M = 6.1/7, 95% CI = 5.9 – 6.3 vs. M = 4.8/7, 95% CI = 4.6 – 5.0, p < .001), and changes in their beliefs regarding medico-legal risk concerning prostate-specific antigen (PSA) screening (odds ratio = .31, 95% CI = .19–.51, p < .001; Gattellari et al., 2005)."

### Abdullah 2014 (2)

Abdullah, G., Rossy, D., Ploeg, J., Davies, B., Higuchi, K., Sikora, L., & Stacey, D. (2014). Measuring the Effectiveness of Mentoring as a Knowledge Translation Intervention for Implementing Empirical Evidence: A Systematic Review. Worldviews on Evidence-Based Nursing, 11(5), 284-300. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.12060

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cluster-randomized trial (Levin et al., 2011) in US home care with 46 nurses; the mentoring multifaceted arm showed greater and sustained EBP beliefs and EBP implementation (F1,15 = 10.39, p = .006) versus educational meetings, with no knowledge difference.

> "Compared to educational meetings, nurses who received mentoring as part of a multifaceted intervention had increased beliefs in EBP (F1, 15 = 3 3.105, p < .001) and had sustained beliefs at 9 months post intervention (F1, 15 = 7.335, p = .016; Levin et al., 2011). No differences were reported in nurses’ knowledge."

### Abdullah 2014 (3)

Abdullah, G., Rossy, D., Ploeg, J., Davies, B., Higuchi, K., Sikora, L., & Stacey, D. (2014). Measuring the Effectiveness of Mentoring as a Knowledge Translation Intervention for Implementing Empirical Evidence: A Systematic Review. Worldviews on Evidence-Based Nursing, 11(5), 284-300. https://doi.org/10.1111/wvn.12060

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Cluster-randomized trial (Johnston et al., 2007) in tertiary care with 141 nurses implementing a pain management guideline; the review reports improved pain management knowledge and pain assessment documentation (15% to 58% vs. 24% to 9%) favoring the mentoring arm over audit and feedback.

> "Compared to audit and feedback, mentoring as part of a multifaceted intervention improved nurses’ knowledge of pain management (p < .0001; Johnston et al., 2007)."
