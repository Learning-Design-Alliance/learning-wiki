Evidence¶

Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc 2018¶

Ersin Nacaroglu & Onder Karakoc. (2018). Effects of Eight Week Plyometric Study on the Balance Performance of Hearing Impaired Athletes. International Education Studies, Vol. 11, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n6p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent Samples T-test comparison of pretest-posttest differences between the experimental (plyometric plus volleyball) and control (volleyball only) groups, reported in Findings Table 6. Overall Stability-R difference was 0.39±0.46 vs -0.03±0.21 (t=2.897, p=0.008), Medial Lateral-R -0.13±0.59 vs 0.51±0.63 (t=-2.579, p=0.017), Overall Stability-L 0.46±0.45 vs 0.07±0.34 (t=2.369, p=0.027). The article prints no effect sizes.

"For the experimental and control groups, statistical significance was found in the values of overall stability R, medial lateral R, and overall stability L in comparing the mean values of the pretest values and the post-test values of the static balance measurement parameters (p <0.05)."
