## Evidence

### Devine 2025

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Autoregressive models after establishing longitudinal measurement invariance in the 252-child sample (223 seen at both waves). The article reports "moderate rank-order stability in each latent factor over time."

> "There was moderate rank-order stability in each latent factor over time. There were no significant differences between the rank-order stability of child-rated well-being, std. est. = .61, p < .0001, and caregiver-rated well-being, std. est. = .68, p < .0001, χ2(1) = 0.84, p = .36."

### Devine 2025 (2)

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate latent change score model for child self-rated well-being across the Reception-to-Year-1 transition. The article reports "a significant mean decrease in child-rated well-being" with marked interindividual differences in change.

> "On average, there was a significant mean decrease in child-rated well-being as indicated by the mean latent change score, est. = −0.205, SE = 0.08, p < .01, as well as significant interindividual differences in the degree of change over time, est. = 0.442, SE = 0.10, p < .0001."

### Devine 2025 (3)

Devine, R. T., Gray, L., Edwards, M., Jess, M., Dempsey, C., Heng, J., Mehrotra, M., D'Souza, H., Fink, E., & Hughes, C. (2025). Changes in Children's Well-Being and Mental Health Across the Early School Years: Links With Academic and Social Competence. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001962

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate latent change score model for caregiver-rated well-being; a companion model showed caregiver-rated mental health difficulties increased significantly (est. = 0.157, SE = 0.08, p = .038).

> "mean caregiver-rated child well-being also decreased over time, as indicated by the mean latent change score, est. = −0.252, SE = 0.07, p < .0001, and again there were marked interindividual differences in change over time, est. = 0.529, SE = 0.11, p < .0001."
