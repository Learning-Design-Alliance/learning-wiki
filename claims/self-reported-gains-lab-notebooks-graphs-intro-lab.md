## Evidence

### H. J. Lewandowski 2019

H. J. Lewandowski, Daniel R. Bolton, and Benjamin Pollard (2019). Initial impacts of the transformation of a large introductory lab course focused on developing experimental skills and expert epistemology. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.lewandowski](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.lewandowski)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-instruction Likert-style survey questions (Q1, Q2, Q3) answered by 94% of enrolled students, administered in the final week of the course as required. As shown in Figs. 1 and 2, "over 70% of students reported moderate-to-great improvement" on notebook keeping and graphing, and "over 80% said they preferred lab notebooks to reports." No effect size is printed.

> "over 70% of students reported moderate-to-great improvement in their capacity to create a high quality graph, or to maintain a lab notebook. Additionally, over 80% said they preferred lab notebooks to reports."
