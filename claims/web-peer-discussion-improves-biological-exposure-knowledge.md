### Chen Lulin 2026

Chen Lulin, Liang Danyan, Zhu Yuhuan, Huang Weihong, Li Chenglin, Liang Xiong, Liang Yutao, Lu Yunyun, Gan Yongjiang, Zhong Yao. (2026). The effect of web-based learning with peer-facilitated discussion on nursing students’ knowledge and practices in work-related biological exposure: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09333-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study comparing 251 experimental and 259 control nursing students at a tertiary teaching hospital in southwest China. The intervention used web-based learning with peer-facilitated discussion while the control used traditional lectures. Knowledge was assessed immediately after a one-hour training session using a 20-item questionnaire.

> "The total scores of knowledge about work-related biological exposure in the experimental group were higher than those in the control group (18.80 ± 1.77 vs. 14.90 ± 2.11, p < 0.05)."
