## Evidence

### Ebadi et al. 2013

Rohollahzadeh Ebadi, M., Mohd Saad, M. R., & Abedalaziz, N. (2013). Explicit Form Focus Instruction: The Effects on Implicit and Explicit Knowledge of ESL Learners. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086281

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA in an experimental pretest-treatment-posttest design with 91 lower-intermediate ESL learners randomly assigned to experimental (n=47) and control (n=44) groups. The printed effect size is eta squared = .74, which the article calls a moderate to large effect size per Cohen (1988).

> "after controlling for the effect of the pretest, there is a significant difference between the experimental group and control group in implicit knowledge,  F (1, 88) = 260.89, p =.00, eta squared =.74"
