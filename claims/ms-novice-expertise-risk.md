## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Beginners at a motor task risk rehearsing the skill incorrectly and worsening performance, whereas novices generate stronger mental practice effects for cognitive than physical tasks and experienced subjects benefit equally for both. [→ Meer 2009](#meer-2009)
