## Evidence

### La Cerra Carmen 2019

La Cerra Carmen, Dante Angelo, Caponnetto Valeria, Franconi Ilaria, Gaxhja Elona, Petrucci Cristina, Alfes Celeste M, Lancia Loreto. (2019). Effects of high-fidelity simulation based on life-threatening clinical condition scenarios on learning outcomes of undergraduate and postgraduate nursing students: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open 9(2):e025306. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025306

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effect meta-analysis of objective performance outcomes measured by direct observation or objective instruments (e.g., ACLS Mega Code Performance Score Sheet). The pooled effect favoured HFPS: "d=0.50, 95% CI [0.19 to 0.81], Z-test=3.12, p=0.001".

> "HFPS sessions showed significant larger effects sizes for knowledge (d=0.49, 95% CI [0.17 to 0.81], Z-test=3.06, p=0.003) and performance (d=0.50, 95% CI [0.19 to 0.81], Z-test=3.12, p=0.001) than any other teaching method"
