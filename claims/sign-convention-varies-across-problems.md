### Gupta 2010

Gupta, A., & Elby, A. (2010). Beyond epistemological deficits: Incorporating flexible epistemological views into fine-grained cognitive dynamics. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0461)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Within the same interview data, Jim's earlier problem solution showed he "took downward as positive", which the authors use to rule out a globally robust sign conception as the source of the impasse.

> "At first glance, we might think Jim's behavior stems from the robust conception that downward always corresponds to negative. But solving an earlier problem, Jim took downward as positive."
