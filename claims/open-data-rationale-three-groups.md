Thematic analysis of ten semi-structured interviews with early adopter educators, reported in the Discussion. The article states that "the rationale of interviewees could be classified into three groups": advanced possibilities (A, G), authentic inquiry for public engagement (C, F, J), and motivation and data literacies (B, D, E, H, K).
