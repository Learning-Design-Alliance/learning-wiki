## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Boys doing moderate PA once to 5+ times a week had greater odds of average or above-average academic performance than boys doing none, peaking at thrice weekly (OR = 1.460). [→ So Wi-Young 2012](#so-wi-young-2012)

## Evidence

### So Wi-Young 2012

So Wi-Young. (2012). Association between physical activity and academic performance in Korean adolescent students. BMC Public Health 12, 258. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-12-258

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted multivariate logistic regression on KYRBWS-V data for 39,612 boys comparing moderate PA frequencies with no moderate PA. All frequencies from once to 5+ times weekly showed greater odds; 4 times weekly gave "OR = 1.363" and 5+ times weekly OR = 1.135 (p = 0.029).

> "those who participated in moderate PA once a week (OR = 1.121, 95% CI = 1.021–1.232, p = 0.017), twice a week (OR = 1.337, 95% CI = 1.216–1.471, p < 0.001), thrice a week (OR = 1.460, 95% CI = 1.315–1.621, p < 0.001)"
