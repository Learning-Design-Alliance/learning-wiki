`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In-class test data shown in Fig. 4 from two modern physics classes; the article reports the fraction selecting answers implying energy loss as a lower bound on the fraction of students, because even students who answered correctly often second-guessed themselves about energy loss.

> "The fraction of students who selected one of these three answers (62% in ENGsp05 and 53% in PHYsp05)), shown in Fig. 4, gives us a lower bound on the fraction of students who hold the misconception that energy is lost in tunneling."
