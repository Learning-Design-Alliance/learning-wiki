## Evidence

### Binterová and Komínková 2013

Helena Binterová, Olga Komínková. (2013). Using Computers in Relation to Learning Climate in CLIL Method. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 6, Number 1, 2013. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1053646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors report that pupils "became more motivated to learn something new and even difficult" and that they "gradually got rid of their fears of both English and Mathematics".

> "They became more motivated to learn something new and even difficult because they found this new method attractive. This was the result of the combination of interactive educational tools and foreign languages teaching. It was proved that pupils gradually got rid of their fears of both English and Mathematics."
