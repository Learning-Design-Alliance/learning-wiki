# Teacher self-efficacy has its largest effect on perceived enjoyment of GenAI tools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Self-efficacy in solving technical problems and learning GenAI tools autonomously has a significant positive effect on perceived enjoyment, the largest effect size in the model. [→ Guillén-Gámez 2026](#guillen-gamez-2026)

## Evidence

### Guillén-Gámez 2026

Guillén-Gámez, F. D., Tomczyk, Ł., Habibi, A., & Díaz Vargas, B. L. (2026). Transforming curriculum design with generative AI: a model for assessing teacher digital competence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10647-6

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

PLS-SEM path analysis in the same 434-teacher sample. The self-efficacy to enjoyment path was significant with printed effect size β = 0.805 (t-value = 22.672, p <.05).

> "as well as in the perceptions of enjoyment (H10, β = 0.805, t-value = 22.672, p <.05)"

### Guillén-Gámez 2026 (2)

Guillén-Gámez, F. D., Tomczyk, Ł., Habibi, A., & Díaz Vargas, B. L. (2026). Transforming curriculum design with generative AI: a model for assessing teacher digital competence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10647-6

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Effect-size evaluation of the structural model in the 434-teacher PLS-SEM study, using thresholds of 0.35, 0.15 and 0.02 for large, medium and small effects. The SE–PE relationship had printed effect size f2 = 1.839, the largest in the model.

> "it is observed that the largest effect sizes are found in the causal relationships between the SE and PE factors (f2 = 1.839), which indicates a substantially large impact"
