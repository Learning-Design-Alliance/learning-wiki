## Evidence

### Baek Gyuli; Lee Eunju 2026

Baek Gyuli; Lee Eunju. (2026). Effectiveness of a virtual reality simulation based on experiential learning theory for enhancing diabetic ketoacidosis management competencies: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08970-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the same RCT (N=44) showed significant group (F = 15.85, p < .001), time (F = 23.22, p < .001), and "interaction between group and time point (F = 4.87, p = .033)" effects on performance confidence. No effect size printed.

> "The performance confidence of the experimental group increased from 3.55 ± 0.42 before the intervention to 3.99 ± 0.63 after, whereas the control group decreased from 4.08 ± 0.46 to 3.46 ± 0.61"
