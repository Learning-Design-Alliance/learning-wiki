Liu 2026¶

Liu, W.; Li, Y.; Yan, Y.; Wang, J.; Wang, J.; Zhang, H. (2026). Exploring University Faculty’s AI Well-Being: A Structural Equation Model of Social Supports, AI Literacy, and Technological Self-Efficacy. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071168

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bootstrapping mediation analysis (2000 resamples, bias-corrected 95% CIs) reported in Results §4.3 of the 502-faculty survey. The article reports both indirect effects as significant, with AI literacy accounting for the majority of the indirect effect.
