## Evidence

### DiPietro Joan 2019

DiPietro Joan, Kelemen Arpad, Liang Yulan, Sik-Lanyi Cecilia. (2019). Computer- and Robot-Assisted Therapies to Aid Social and Intellectual Functioning of Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55080440

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Controlled study in the Netherlands with three sibling pairs (children with ASD with IQs above 80) randomly assigned to baseline lengths of three, four, or five sessions, using a NAO robot in five weekly 30-minute LEGO Therapy sessions. "Results demonstrated no statistically significant changes in collaborative behaviors."

> "Results demonstrated no statistically significant changes in collaborative behaviors in any of the three measurement targets for the children with ASD. Of interest is the social validity measure result, indicating that the children with ASD reported the robot sessions were less enjoyable than the nonrobot sessions"
