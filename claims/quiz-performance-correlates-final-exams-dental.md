## Evidence

### Lundegren N 2021

Lundegren N, Jönsson A, Lindberg P. (2021). An upgrade of the Malmö model by implementing case‐based teaching and learning, in an undergraduate dental education. European Journal of Dental Education, 25(4), 649-656. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.12642

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article's own tentative observational finding from its early evaluation of the upgraded model; the correlation between quiz performance and final examination scores is asserted without a printed coefficient or test statistic.

> "This is a topic for future research, but for now, we find that the quizzes are a good way for the student to test their knowledge, and they also enjoy and learn from the discussions around the different MCQ alternatives in the seminars."

### Lundegren N 2021 (2)

Lundegren N, Jönsson A, Lindberg P. (2021). An upgrade of the Malmö model by implementing case‐based teaching and learning, in an undergraduate dental education. European Journal of Dental Education, 25(4), 649-656. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.12642

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Figure 3 example: a single-best-answer periodontology quiz item on the pseudo-pocket answered by 61 second-semester students, with a discrimination index of 0.62 and a high answer distribution creating a good basis for discussion.

> "When the question was answered individually by 61 students, the discrimination index was 0.62, which is considered as very good, 46 and the distribution of answers was high, which created a good basis for discussion both in the small and the large groups."
