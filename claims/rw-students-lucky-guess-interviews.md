Emily M. Reiser and Mark E. Markes. (2005). An inferential analysis of the effect of activity based physics instruction on the persistent misconceptions of lecture students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0506208](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0506208)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttest interviews (footnote 26) of 21 AB students; common responses were "lucky guess the first time", "thought too much about it the second time", "became confused the second time", and "cannot remember". This grounds the assumption that rw responses are fortuitous and equivalent to ww' responses.
