Evidence¶

Arps 1917¶

Arps, G. P. (1917). Attitude as a determinant in spelling efficiency in immediate and delayed recall. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Arps+attitude+spelling+efficiency

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Observational study of one six-and-one-half-year-old learner spelling 104 word groups (54 orderly, 54 random; 808 words total) from Jones's Second Reader. In immediate restoration the orderly procedure produced 33 errors versus 40 for random, a 9.4% advantage described as "more efficient by 9.4% than the random procedure".

"In the case of immediate reproduction, the orderly procedure is more efficient by 9.4% than the random procedure;"
