# Teaching electrochemical cells via STAD cooperative learning combined with models raises undergraduate achievement more than STAD alone or traditional teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Post-test achievement of the cooperative learning with models group (CLMG) was significantly higher than both the STAD-only group and the control group after ANCOVA adjustment. [→ Ataman Karaçöp 2016](#ataman-karacop-2016)

## Evidence

### Ataman Karaçöp 2016

Ataman Karaçöp. (2016). Effects of Student Teams-Achievement Divisions Cooperative Learning with Models on Students' Understanding of Electrochemical Cells. International Education Studies, 9(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n11p104

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent comparison-group study with 70 first-year science teacher education students in three groups (CLMG N=24, CLG N=24, CG N=22). ANCOVA with pre-EcAT as covariate showed a significant group difference, "F (2,66) =7.459", with effect size f = 0.47 interpreted as high.

> "Analysis results in Table 3 showed that there was a statistically significant difference between mean post ECAT scores of the study groups (F (2,66) =7.459; p < .05). In addition, the calculated effect size value (f = 0.47) was interpreted as the effect of the experimental application was high (f > 0.4)."
