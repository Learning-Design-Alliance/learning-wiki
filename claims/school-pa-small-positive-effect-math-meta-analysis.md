# School-based physical activity interventions show a small positive pooled effect on children's mathematics performance (d = 0.23) across 11 randomised trials

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Meta-analysis of 11 randomised controlled trials found an overall small positive effect (d = 0.23) of school-based PA interventions on mathematics performance, with high heterogeneity (I² = 69.6%). [→ Sneck S. 2019](#sneck-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Sneck S. 2019

Sneck S., Viholainen H., Syväoja H., Kankaapää A., Hakonen H., Poikkeus A.-M., Tammelin T. (2019). Effects of school-based physical activity on mathematics performance in children: a systematic review. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-019-0866-6

`q3 · i1`

Meta-analysis of 11 randomised controlled trials using Cohen's d with a random effect model, restricted to studies with baseline measures and control groups. The review reports "a small positive effect (d=0.23)" and high heterogeneity (I² = 69.6%).

> "Overall, on average a small positive effect (d=0.23) was found for all the interventions. The level of statistical heterogeneity between the intervention groups was high, I2 = 69.6%."
