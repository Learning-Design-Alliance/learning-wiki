## Evidence

### Amanda Vite 2024

Amanda Vite, Erika A. Patall, Man Chen. (2024). Relationships Between Experiences of Autonomy and Well(Ill)-Being for K-12 Youth: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2024) 36:127. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09967-x

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random-effects multilevel meta-analysis of correlations from 90 reports on K-12 youth; the review reports the ANS-ill-being and ANF-well-being pooled correlations were "statistically significantly negative", with Table 2 listing r = -.25 (ANS-IB) and r = -.25 (ANF-WB).

> "Further, the overall average correlations between ANS and ill-being and ANF and well-being were statistically significantly negative (see Table 2 for full results)."
