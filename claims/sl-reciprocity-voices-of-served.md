### Toronyi 2020

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11.

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Meta-synthesis finding; reciprocity required PSTs to develop meaningful relationships with ELLs showing true interest in their aspirations and advancement toward those goals.

> "Six studies pointed out the relevance of listening to the voices of those who were being “served”"

### Toronyi 2020 (2)

Toronyi, A. (2020). Preservice Teachers Working with English Language Learners: A Meta-Synthesis of Service-Learning as an Effective Pedagogical Tool. Journal of Service-Learning in Higher Education, 11.

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-study comparison within the synthesis; traditional placements restricting PSTs to classroom-only interactions did not allow deeper connections that would let ELLs lower their affective filter.

> "Relationship building was much better achieved in studies where a community-based SL or after-school tutoring programs were offered"
