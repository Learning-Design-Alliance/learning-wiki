Meta-regression of 239 estimates comparing the 2021 assessment-timing moderator against the 2020 reference category; the 2021 coefficient is non-significant, so equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "There is no statistically significant difference in student performance between assessments that have taken place several months or more than one year after the outbreak of the coronavirus and those that have occurred in the early stages of the pandemic."
