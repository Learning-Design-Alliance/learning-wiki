# Skill group, temporal and spatial occlusion interact: skilled keepers lose little accuracy from hip-only occlusion at ball contact while less-skilled keepers lose much more

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The group x temporal x spatial occlusion interaction was significant for correct response, with the skilled group maintaining accuracy in the hip condition at ball contact. [→ Causer Joe 2017](#causer-joe-2017)
`q3 i2` The same three-way interaction held for correct direction, with a 9% decrement for skilled versus 22% for less-skilled keepers at ball contact. [→ Causer Joe 2017 (2)](#causer-joe-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Causer Joe 2017

Causer Joe, Smeeton Nicholas J., Williams A. Mark. (2017). Expertise differences in anticipatory judgements during a temporally and spatially occluded task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171330

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Three-way interaction on correct response, F2, 44 = 11.63, p < 0.001, partial eta squared = 0.35: skilled keepers' ball-contact accuracy fell only "MD = 6%" from full body to hip region, versus "MD = 19%" for less-skilled keepers.

> "the skilled group’s response accuracy in the ball contact condition did not significantly differ between the full body and hip region condition (MD = 6%), whereas the less-skilled group showed a significant decrease in accuracy (MD = 19%)."

### Causer Joe 2017 (2)

Causer Joe, Smeeton Nicholas J., Williams A. Mark. (2017). Expertise differences in anticipatory judgements during a temporally and spatially occluded task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171330

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Three-way interaction on correct direction, F2, 44 = 5.567, p = 0.007, partial eta squared = 0.20: at ball contact the skilled group's full-body-to-hip decrement was 9% versus 22% for the less-skilled group.

> "the performance decrement from full body to hip region for the skilled group’s response accuracy in the ball contact condition was 9%, compared to 22% for the less-skilled group."
