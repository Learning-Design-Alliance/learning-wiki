Latent profile analysis of z-standardized PU, PEOU, AT, SI, and FC scores from 128 pre-service teachers; the four-profile solution was selected over two-to-five-profile competitors. The article reports "The entropy value was 0.985" and a significant BLRT (116.70, p = .0099).
