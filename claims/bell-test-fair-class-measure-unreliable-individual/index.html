Thomas H. Briggs 1920¶

Thomas H. Briggs. (1920). Results of the Bell Chemistry Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0074597

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The author's interpretation of the Table II correlations in the article's running text: the test measures "a cla6s knowledge of chemistry" fairly well, yet detailed pairings show it is unreliable for judging an individual pupil.

"These raw coefficients may be interpreted as showing that the test is a fairly good measure of a cla6s knowledge of chemistry; but a study of the detailed pairings indicate that it is unreliable for an individual."
