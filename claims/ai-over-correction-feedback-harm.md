Ceci n'est pas une explication: Evaluating Explanation Failures as Explainability Pitfalls in Language Learning Systems. MIRAGE Workshop @ IUI 2026. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.26145](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.26145)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' analysis of the prioritisation dimension, presented as a characteristic failure pattern observed in preliminary evaluation of frontier AI models on L2-Bench feedback tasks; the claim is argued, not experimentally tested. They write that "feedback overwhelms rather than guides, leaving learners unable to distinguish critical issues from minor ones."

> "If the AI system corrects every minor error, learners may become anxious or focus excessively on form rather than meaning, reducing fluency."
