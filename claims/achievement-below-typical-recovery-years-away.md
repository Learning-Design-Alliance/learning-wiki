`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The brief concludes that "student achievement remains lower than in a typical year and full recovery is likely still several years away," based on MAP Growth data from 7 million students in grades 3-8 collected between 2020-21 and fall 2022.

> "While the evidence of rebounding is encouraging, student achievement remains lower than in a typical year and full recovery is likely still several years away."
