Mallin Mike MD, Schlein Sarah MD, Doctor Shaneen MD, Stroud Susan MD, Dawson Matthew MD, Fix Megan MD. (2014). A Survey of the Current Utilization of Asynchronous Education Among Emergency Medicine Residents in the United States. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000000170

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional SurveyMonkey survey of 226 emergency medicine residents at 12 U.S. residencies, analyzed with descriptive statistics and chi-square. Respondents reported weekly extracurricular education time: "97.7% or 216/221" at least one hour, "34.4% or 76/221" two to four hours.

> "Almost all of the respondents (97.7% or 216/221) reported spending some time (at least one hour) per week engaging in extracurricular education. About a third of the residents (34.4% or 76/221) indicated that they spend two to four hours per week (P < .001)."
