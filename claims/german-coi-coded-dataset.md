### Dornauer 2024

Dornauer, V., Netzer, M., Kaczkó, É., Norz, L.-M., & Ammenwerth, E. (2024). Automatic Classification of Online Discussions and Other Learning Traces to Detect Cognitive Presence. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00335-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Manual quantitative content analysis of posts from a postgraduate Health Information Management course at UMIT TIROL; each meaningful unit was coded to exploration, integration, resolution, or non-cognitive presence, with the triggering phase excluded. The abstract states "the first fully CoI-coded German dataset" for future research.

> "After this manual quantitative content analysis, our dataset comprised 1,521 meaningful units (identified within 1,147 posts)."
