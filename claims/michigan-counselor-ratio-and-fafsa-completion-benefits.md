### Pierce 2026

Pierce, C. (2026). Ready for Rigor: Expanding Access to Rigorous Coursework in Michigan. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682555

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The report cites research that lower student-to-counselor ratios are associated with improved attendance, discipline rates, and graduation rates, and that seniors who complete the FAFSA are "84% more likely to immediately enroll in postsecondary education." Only 61% of Michigan's Class of 2025 completed the FAFSA.

> "While the recommended student-to-counselor ratio is 250:1, Michigan schools faced an average ratio of 565:1 in the 2024-25 school year"
