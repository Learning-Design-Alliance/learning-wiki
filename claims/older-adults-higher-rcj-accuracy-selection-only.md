# Older adults show higher retrospective metacognitive accuracy than young adults in the selection-only task, especially on homogeneous unit-digit problems, but comparable accuracy in the selection-execution task

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The Age x Task interaction on RCJ accuracy was significant, with young adults lower than older adults in selection-only, an effect larger on homogeneous (.71 vs. .84) than heterogeneous (.55 vs. .60) unit digits. [→ Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire 2017](#marie-geurten-patrick-lemaire-2017)

## Evidence

### Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire 2017

Marie Geurten & Patrick Lemaire. (2017). Metacognition for Strategy Selection during Arithmetic Problem Solving in Young and Older Adults. https://www.google.com/search?q=Metacognition+for+Strategy+Selection+during+Arithmetic+Problem+Solving+in+Young+and+Older+Adults

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design 2 (Age) x 2 (Task) x 2 (Unit Digit) ANOVA on RCJ A'ROC in 66 adults. The Age x Task interaction reached "F(1,61) = 5.48, MSe = 1.65, η²p = .08, B = 3.23"; pairwise comparisons showed young adults lower than older adults in selection-only, larger on homogeneous (.71 vs. .84) than heterogeneous (.55 vs. .60) digits.

> "the Age x Task, F(1,61) = 5.48, MSe = 1.65, η²p = .08, B = 3.23, the Task x Unit Digit, F(1,61) = 8.43, MSe = 1.47, η²p = .12, B = 8.61, and the Age x Task x Unit Digit interactions, F(1,61) = 6.89, MSe = 1.47, η²p = .10, B = 4.67, were significant"
