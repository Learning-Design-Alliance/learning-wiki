### Huang Qingyu 2025

Huang Qingyu, Dou Xue, Liu Jing, Feng Rui, Zhang Xiang, Yue Jinbo. (2025). National competition reveals common errors in target delineation among young clinicians in radiation therapy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07477-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of simulation imaging techniques in the competition data; enhanced CT alone "constituting 70% of cases" overall, and 96%, 79%, and 56% for chest, abdominal-pelvic, and head and neck tumors respectively (Fig. 2c-e).

### Huang Qingyu 2025 (2)

Huang Qingyu, Dou Xue, Liu Jing, Feng Rui, Zhang Xiang, Yue Jinbo. (2025). National competition reveals common errors in target delineation among young clinicians in radiation therapy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07477-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison across tumor sites in the competition data: "multiple image fusions was found to be higher in head and neck tumors" than in chest and abdominal-pelvic sites (Fig. 2c). The discussion attributes this to the complexity of critical structures in that region.

> "Furthermore, the proportion of multiple image fusions was found to be higher in head and neck tumors when compared to the other two tumor sites."
