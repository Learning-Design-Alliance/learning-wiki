What Works Clearinghouse 2015¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2015). Head Start: WWC intervention report. http://www.acf.hhs.gov/programs/ohs

q3 · i? · causal · r3

In the DHHS ACF (2010) randomized controlled trial, three social-emotional outcomes were measured at the end of the intervention year. The average improvement index was +1 percentile points (range -1 to +5); the WWC characterized the effect as indeterminate because the mean effect was "neither statistically significant nor substantively important."

"The authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant or substantively important effects on any of these measures for either the 3-year-old or the 4-year-old cohorts."
