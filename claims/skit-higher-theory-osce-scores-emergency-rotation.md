## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a 3-month rotation, residents taught with SKIT scored significantly higher than traditionally taught controls on both theoretical assessment and OSCE (p < 0.001). [→ Zhang Dalong 2026](#zhang-dalong-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang Dalong 2026

Zhang Dalong, Gao Jiankai, Niu Xingguo, Si Haoran, Sun Yang, Li Mingzhe, Yao Tianyi, Tang Juhua. (2026). "Script Killing" immersive teaching improves the competency of emergency medicine residents rotating across departments. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1806065

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort-allocated controlled educational study of 36 rotating residents (18 per group) at Zhengzhou People's Hospital; the Results section reports "significantly higher than those of the control group (p < 0.001)" for both outcomes, with Table 2 giving means and MDs.

> "The theoretical assessment scores and OSCE scores of the observation group were significantly higher than those of the control group (p < 0.001) (see Table 2; Figure 3)."
