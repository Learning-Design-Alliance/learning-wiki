## Evidence

### Gao Jie 2026

Gao Jie, Zhang Haolin, Qin Wen, Liu Xin. (2026). Application of retrospective teaching method in stomatology undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09665-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled study of 42 fifth-year undergraduate orthodontic students randomly assigned to CBL (n = 21) or LBL (n = 21). The CBL group scored significantly higher on a 40-minute closed-book examination. The article reports "95.2 ± 1.7 vs. 66.2 ± 3.5, p < 0.001".

> "Students in the experimental group achieved significantly higher orthodontic examination scores than those in the control group (95.2 ± 1.7 vs. 66.2 ± 3.5, p < 0.001)."
