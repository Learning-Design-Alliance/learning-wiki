## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yu S 2026

Yu S, Lin Y and Chen C (2026) Designing for AI literacy: psychological mechanisms and self-determination. Front. Psychol. 17:1937845. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1937845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5,000 resamples) in the PLS-SEM model. Instructional approach was partially mediated via autonomy and competence; the relatedness pathway was not significant.

> "Instructional approach had signiﬁcant indirect eﬀects on AI literacy performance through autonomy (β = 0.035, 95% CI [0.003, 0.073]) and competence (β = 0.079, 95% CI [0.037, 0.125]). The indirect eﬀect through relatedness was not signiﬁcant (β = 0.011, 95% CI [−0.004, 0.030])."

### Yu S 2026 (2)

Yu S, Lin Y and Chen C (2026) Designing for AI literacy: psychological mechanisms and self-determination. Front. Psychol. 17:1937845. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1937845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bootstrap mediation for task type in the same model: only the autonomy pathway was significant; competence and relatedness pathways were not. The authors conclude the pathways are differentiated rather than uniform.

> "Task type had a signiﬁcant indirect eﬀect through autonomy (β = 0.029, 95% CI [0.002, 0.062]). The indirect eﬀects through competence (β = 0.031, 95% CI [−0.008, 0.073]) and relatedness (β = 0.015, 95% CI [−0.006, 0.041]) were not signiﬁcant."
