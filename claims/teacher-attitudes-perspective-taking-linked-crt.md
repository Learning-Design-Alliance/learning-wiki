## Evidence

### Abacioglu 2020

Abacioglu, C. S., Volman, M., & Fischer, A. H. (2020). Teachers' multicultural attitudes and perspective taking abilities as factors in culturally responsive teaching. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12328

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 143 Dutch primary school teachers; multivariate multiple regression with perspective taking and multicultural attitudes as predictors and the two CRT components as outcomes, controlling for minoritized student concentration. The article reports the associations as significant with no standardized effect size printed (Model R 2 = .28).

> "Teachers' more positive Multicultural Attitudes and higher Perspective Taking Abilities were significantly associated with engaging more in both the Culturally and Socially Sensitive aspects of Culturally Responsive Teaching."
