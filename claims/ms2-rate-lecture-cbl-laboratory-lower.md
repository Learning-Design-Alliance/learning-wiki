### Zinski Anne 2017

Zinski Anne, Blackwell Kristina T.C. Panizzi Woodley, Belue F. Mike, Brooks William S. (2017). Is lecture dead? A preliminary study of medical students' evaluation of teaching methods in the preclinical curriculum. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.59b9.5f40

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-tailed t-tests compared Likert ratings by year in the cross-sectional survey. The article reports MS2 students rated lecture, small group CBL, and laboratory "significantly lower than their MS1 counterparts" with the printed t and p values.

> "By cohort, MS2 students rated lecture (t(114)=3.48, p=0.0007), small group CBL (t(118)=3.13, p=0.002), and laboratory (t(117)=2.86, p=0.005) significantly lower than their MS1 counterparts."
