Harerimana A 2026¶

Harerimana A, Mtshali N, Mchunu G. (2026). Under surveillance: mapping remote proctoring practices in the assessment of nursing students— a scoping review. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00229-7

q2 · i? · review · r3

Scoping review synthesis of two included studies (Aplin-Snider et al. 2021; Castaño et al. 2021) reporting that "98–100% of graduate NP students agreed webcam proctoring/lockdown browsers deterred cheating"; the review also notes frequent minor alerts caused false positives and time-intensive faculty review.
