## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hammer 2014

Hammer, C. S., Hoff, E., Uchikoshi, Y., Gillanders, C., Castro, D., & Sandilos, L. E. (2014). The Language and Literacy Development of Young Dual Language Learners: A Critical Review. Early childhood research quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.05.008

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports two metalinguistic-task studies: Foursha-Stevenson and Nicoladis (2011) found French-English DLLs had better syntactic awareness, and Davidson et al. (2010) found five- and six-year-old Urdu-English DLLs outperformed both monolingual groups, though three- and four-year-olds outperformed only Urdu monolinguals.

> "Foursha-Stevenson and Nicoladis (2011) found that French-English DLLs had better syntactic awareness than their monolingual peers."
