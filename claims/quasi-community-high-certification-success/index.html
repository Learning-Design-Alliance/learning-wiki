Emad 2016¶

Emad, G. R., & Roth, W.-M. (2016). Quasi-Communities: Rethinking Learning in Formal Adult and Vocational Education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9386-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Outcome reported from the ethnographic case study of the eight-week, 120-hour course in which fifteen mariners participated. No pass-rate number or statistical test is printed; the finding is a qualitative observational report.

"As the course progressed, the students became competent in working with marine charts and answering these types of tests, which later became evident in the students' very high success rate in the certification examination that they attended at the end of the course."
