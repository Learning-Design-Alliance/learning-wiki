## Evidence

### Cruz 2025

Cruz, I. N., Moreno, K. A., Stovall, B., de la Vega, L. P., & Chavira, G. (2025). Connecting the dots: Examining stressful life events, campus climate, and school engagement on academic achievement and psychological distress in a predominantly Latine sample. Journal of Research on Adolescence, 35, e70096. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.70096

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Series of three multiple regressions (N = 293) with the four SLE types as predictors, Bonferroni-corrected alpha of .01. School-related challenges were significant for all three outcomes; the achievement model had R² = .262 and the engagement model R² = .219, with "school‐related challenges" the strongest predictor.

> "school‐related challenges (B = −1.43, β = −.49, SE = 0.16, p < .001) as a significant predictor, indicating that those with more school‐related challenges had lower levels of achievement."
