## Evidence

### Mette Mira 2021

Mette Mira, Hänze Martin. (2021). Arrogant or caring? Influence of transactive communication in interprofessional learning on knowledge gains and stereotype changes. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001462

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlative regression analysis of survey data from the interprofessional practice session, analysed separately by profession. The physiotherapy trainees' trac significantly predicted medical students' knowledge gains, with "β=.43, t(123)=5.10, p<.01"; no standardised effect size beyond β is printed.

> "A bivariate regression analysis (predictor variable: the other trac, criterion variable: knowledge gains) confirmed that the trac of physiotherapy trainees had a positive influence on the medical students’ knowledge gains about physiotherapists (F(1, 123)=26.05, p<.01, β=.43, t(123)=5.10, p<.01)."
