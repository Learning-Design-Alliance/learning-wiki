## Evidence

### Baraza W. 2026

Baraza W., Staples G., Wells C., Barbier L., Tonga I., Singh P., Puttick M., Webster C. (2026). Artificially Intelligent Simulated Patients in Undergraduate Surgical Teaching: A Single Blinded Randomized Controlled Study. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70486

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis of the randomized study comparing the four vCSA domain scores between groups by independent t-tests. The article reports "no significant difference in performance when comparing the individual domains"; only means and p-values were printed, with no effect sizes.

> "There was no significant difference in performance when comparing the individual domains for intervention and control groups: history taking (3.6 vs. 3.1 p = 0.15), clinical judgement (3.0 vs. 2.9, p = 0.4), interpretation of results (3.2 vs. 2.9, p = 0.09) and clinical management (3.5 vs. 3.2, p = 0.15, t-tests)."
