# At-risk college students receiving equal combinations of human–animal interaction and academic stress management content show higher WILL (anxiety, attitude, motivation) scores than at-risk students receiving content only, at posttest and six-week follow-up

## Evidence

### Pendry Patricia 2020

Pendry Patricia, Carr Alexa M., Gee Nancy R., Vandagriff Jaymie L. (2020). Randomized Trial Examining Effects of Animal Assisted Intervention and Stress Related Symptoms on College Students’ Learning and Study Skills. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17061909

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat multivariate regression in a randomized trial (N = 349 enrolled; analyses of participants attending baseline and at least one session) found a significant risk × condition interaction on WILL, R2 = 0.197, F(6,302) = 12.31, p < 0.001; at-risk students in HAI-E had "significantly higher levels of WILL at posttest than those at-risk assigned to the ASM condition".

> "Pooled results suggest that at-risk students assigned to the HAI-E condition (B = 0.582, p = 0.005) and HAI-O condition (B = 0.481, p = 0.021) showed significantly higher levels of WILL at posttest than those at-risk assigned to the ASM condition, i.e., the reference group."

### Pendry Patricia 2020 (2)

Pendry Patricia, Carr Alexa M., Gee Nancy R., Vandagriff Jaymie L. (2020). Randomized Trial Examining Effects of Animal Assisted Intervention and Stress Related Symptoms on College Students’ Learning and Study Skills. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17061909

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same ITT regression model run at six-week follow-up showed the risk × condition interaction persisted; high-risk students in HAI-E (B = 0.626, p = 0.005) and HAI-O (B = 0.490, p = 0.038) showed "significantly higher levels of WILL at follow-up than those at-risk assigned to the ASM condition".

> "Regression analyses predicting WILL scores at the six-week follow-up also support a significant interaction between presence of mood risk and treatment condition on students’ scores on WILL, R2 = 0.126, F(6,302) = 7.26, p < 0.001."
