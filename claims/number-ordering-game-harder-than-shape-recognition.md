## Evidence

### Luis Ortegano 2019

Luis Ortegano, Esmitt Ramírez. (2019). Serious Educational Reinforcement Game in Preschool. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.10337](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1909.10337)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Usability evaluation with the 11 preschool children comparing the two pilot games. The shape-recognition game was completed by almost all children without assistance except one, while for the number-ordering game "el restante 18% lo pudo hacer sin explicación alguna". Teachers' reports later showed the number-ordering game represented the greater challenge, with a 30% to 60% win rate.

> "El 72% de los niños requirió una explicación sencilla para comprender el objetivo del juego, el restante 18% lo pudo hacer sin explicación alguna."
