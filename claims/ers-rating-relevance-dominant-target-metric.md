Systematic review of 28 papers selected from 1395 candidates across IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, and SpringerLink. The review reports that "The most popular target metric is user-rating. Almost half of the papers used it when evaluating the effectiveness of their system" and that twelve papers used learning-centered metrics.
