q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports, citing Rzayeva (2025), a study of adult EFL learners: "Rzayeva (2025) found that gestures improved VL in adult EFL learners, while facial expressions had a smaller direct effect on memory". When combined, effects were complementary and associated with greater word retention.

"Rzayeva (2025) found that gestures improved VL in adult EFL learners, while facial expressions had a smaller direct effect on memory but increased student engagement and contributed to a more positive classroom atmosphere."

  • Semantically congruent iconic gestures improve L2 vocabulary retention relative to meaningless or incongruent gestures — related
