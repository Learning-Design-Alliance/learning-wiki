# A gamified exergaming intervention improves basic psychological needs in primary-school PE compared with a traditional didactic intervention

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In a non-randomised controlled natural experiment, all basic psychological needs as a whole improved more in the gamified exergaming group than in the control group. [→ Quintas 2020](#quintas-2020)

## Evidence

### Quintas 2020

Quintas, A., Bustamante, J.-C., Pradas, F., & Castellar, C. (2020). Psychological effects of gamified didactics with exergames in Physical Education at primary schools: results from a natural experiment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103874

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Non-randomised controlled natural experiment in four primary schools comparing 12-session gamified exergaming and traditional PE treatments via 2x2 factorial ANOVA. An "interaction effect was observed on BPN" with ηp2=.023 (a small effect), plus an interaction effect on perceived relatedness with ηp2=.025; all needs as a whole improved in the experimental group.

> "An interaction effect  was observed on BPN (F(1)=7.35, p=.007, ηp2=.023, Mexp-control=1.359) (see Fig. 8). An interaction effect (F(1)=7.99, p=.005, ηp2=.025, Mexp-control=.456) and a time effect (F(1)=11.7, p=.001, η p2=.036, Mexp-control=-.577) on perceived relatedness  took place . All the needs as a whole improved in the experimental group compared to the control group."
