## Evidence

### Sykes EA 2021

Sykes EA, Lemke M, Potter D, Li T, Mir ZM, Sheahan G, Wu V, Zevin B. (2021). Evaluating bowel enterotomy closures in simulated deep body cavities using the reversing half-hitch alternating post and square knots: a randomized controlled trial. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.016719

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the RCT, 20 novices trained on knot-tying boards until meeting a validated 5-of-6 checklist criterion. Times "were equivalent (p = 0.33)" between groups with no significant difference; equivalence testing was not performed and no effect size was printed.

> "The mean times required for each participant to become proficient in knot tying for the RHAP (23 [SD 3] min) and square knots (21 [SD 2] min) were equivalent (p = 0.33)"
