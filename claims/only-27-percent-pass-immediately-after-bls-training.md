A novel retraining strategy of chest compression skills for infant CPR results in high skill retention for longer. European Journal of Pediatrics 181(12):4101-4109. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04625-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive result from the observational study: after a two-hour pediatric BLS education package plus 30 minutes of two-finger compression practice, 32 of 118 participants (27%) passed the assessment, which required meeting all four quality metrics concurrently. The 86 who failed used real-time feedback for reinforcement.

> "A total of 118 participants undertook iCPR training and were assessed immediately after training (time point 0). Of the 118 participants, a total of 32 (27%) 'PASSED' on the first attempt and were asked to return the following month for another assessment."
