## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen 2018

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen. (2018). Systemic inequities in introductory physics courses: the impacts of learning assistants. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical Linear Model (Model 3) nesting students within courses, fit to the 4,365-student LASSO dataset. The model reliably (p ≤0.001) "identiﬁes gaps in posttest scores across student demographics"; fixed-effect estimates in Table II are γ10 = -3.54 (p = 0.001) for underserved gender and γ20 = -4.08 (p < 0.001) for underserved race/ethnicity. The article states the model "predicts that students in non-LA-supported courses who are underserved by gender and race/ethnicity will score 7.6% lower" with equivalent pretest scores.
