`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Synthesis of four retention studies (Block, 1972; Omelich & Covington, 1981; Wentling, 1973; Anderson, Scott, & Hutlock, 1976) retesting students two to four weeks after instruction, or four months in one case; the printed effect size of .62 is the average effect size for retention across studies, a printed effect size with its value.

> "The average effect size for retention across studies was .62. A limitation of these studies, however, is that all but one measured retention over relatively short time periods."
