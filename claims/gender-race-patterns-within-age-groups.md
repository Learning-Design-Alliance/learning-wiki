## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Age-by-gender analysis of examination choice (Figure 3) in the full cohort; the article reports the pattern held in every age group (all P < .001) with the largest female-versus-male difference of "34.3% vs 43.7%" in the 55 to 64 group. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "In each age group, male PAs were more likely than female PAs to choose the traditional PANRE (all P < .001). The largest proportional difference between female and male PAs in choosing the traditional PANRE (34.3% vs 43.7%) was observed in the 55 to 64 age group."

### Kozikowski A 2026 (2)

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Age-by-race analysis of examination choice (Figure 4) in the full cohort; the article reports higher traditional PANRE selection among African American/Black PAs across all age subgroups (all P < .05) except ages 65 and older (P = .472). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "PAs who identified as African American/Black had a higher proportion selecting the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA than did other racial groups across all age subgroups (all P < .05) except among those ages 65 years and older, where the difference was not statistically significant (P = .472)."
