## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Using EDI quartiles, the negative divorce–GPA association was relatively stronger in the second and fourth quartiles than the first, but adjusting for parental education removed the significant Q1–Q4 difference. [→ Nilsen 2020](#nilsen-2020)

## Evidence

### Nilsen 2020

Nilsen, S. A., Breivik, K., Wold, B., Askeland, K. G., Sivertsen, B., Hysing, M., & Bøe, T. (2020). Divorce and adolescent academic achievement: Heterogeneity in the associations by parental education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229183

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analysis regressing GPA on divorce-by-income-quartile interactions. The Q1–Q3 difference was not statistically significant, and "Adjusting the analyses for parental educational qualifications attenuated and removed the significant difference between Q1 and Q4".

> "the negative association between divorce and GPA was relatively stronger among adolescents in the second income quartile (Q2; b = - 0.16, p < 0.01) and in the fourth quartile (Q4; b = -0.15, p = 0.02) compared to those in the first quartile (Q1)"
