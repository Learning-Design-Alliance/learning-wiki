## Evidence

### Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus repeated-measures ANOVA on one-week delayed test scores in this school experiment found the predicted three-way interaction: "element interactivity, guidance and expertise was significant", with partial eta squared ηp² = .076. Follow-up tests for Year 10 novices found a significant generation effect on low element interactivity material (t(32) = 2.63, p = .01, d = 0.83).

> "The 3-way interaction of element interactivity, guidance and expertise was significant, F(1, 76) = 6.254, MSe = 349.45, p = .015, Wilks' Lambda = .924, ηр²  = .076."

### Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016 (2)

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Simple-effects test for Year 10 novices on delayed high element interactivity material: "the effect of guidance was close to significance", d = 0.65, with worked examples numerically superior to problem solving but not significant on the reported two-tailed test (p = .06); the authors note a directional prediction could justify a 1-tailed test.

> "the effect of guidance was close to significance, t(32) = 1.97, SEdiff = 3.98, p = .06, d = 0.65."
