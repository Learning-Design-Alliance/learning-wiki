# Teacher home visits slightly improved student attendance in the following school year (ES = +0.02)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Parent Teacher Home Visits 2021

Parent Teacher Home Visits. (2021). Evidence summary reporting a 2021 Mathematica Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic quasi-experimental study. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/parent-teacher-home-visits-attendance/

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental study by the Regional Education Laboratory of the Mid-Atlantic at Mathematica used matched comparison group propensity scores in District of Columbia Public Schools grades 1–5, with 3,996 students. Visited students averaged "95.28 percent" attendance versus 94.93 percent for nonvisited students; the printed effect size is negligible, "ES = +0.02".

> "During the school year following a home visit, the study found that, on average, a home visit significantly improved student attendance slightly (ES = +0.02). The attendance rate averaged 95.28 percent for visited students and 94.93 percent for nonvisited comparison students."
