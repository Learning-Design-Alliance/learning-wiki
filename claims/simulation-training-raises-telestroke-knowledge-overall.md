## Evidence

### Richard Sébastien PhD 2016

Richard Sébastien PhD, Mione Gioia MD, Varoqui Claude MD, Vezain Arnaud, Brunner Arielle MD, Bracard Serge PhD, Debouverie Marc PhD, Braun Marc PhD, Peralta. Luis Pinet. (2016). Simulation training for emergency teams to manage acute ischemic stroke by telemedicine. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000003924

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post knowledge assessment of 225 learners from 6 telestroke spoke emergency teams trained in sixteen sessions, analyzed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test because of non-normal distributions. The article reports "significantly increased from 59 ± 13%" to 82 ± 10% with P < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, the learners median percentage of correct answers significantly increased from 59 ± 13% for the pretraining test to 82 ± 10% in posttraining test (P < 0.001)."
