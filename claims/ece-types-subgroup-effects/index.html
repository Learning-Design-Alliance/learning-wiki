Pang Xuefei 2026¶

Pang Xuefei, Wang Huilin, Li Jinggui, Gui Haijun, Cao Xia, Cai Ming. (2026). The Impact of Early Clinical Exposure on Competency Development in Dental Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109483

q4 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis by ECE type in the meta-analysis. All three modalities were significant; the real-clinical-settings estimate had the widest interval and highest heterogeneity (I² = 98.3%).

"significant benefits were found for exposure to real clinical settings (SMD = 1.74, 95% CI = 0.83-2.66, I² = 98.3%, P < .001), simulated clinical interactions (SMD = 1.06, 95% CI = 0.46-1.67, I² = 96.0%, P < .001), and virtual environment learning (SMD = 0.93, 95% CI = 0.29-1.58, I² = 88.2%, P < .001)"
