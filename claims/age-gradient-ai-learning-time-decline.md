# The post-ChatGPT learning-time decline follows an age gradient: largest in high school (31.3% cumulative), small in middle school (9.0%), and undetectable in Grade 5

## Subclaims

### Rismanchian 2026

Rismanchian, S., Uzun, H., Matayoshi, J., Cosyn, E., & Kurd-Misto, E. (2026). Faster Completion, Less Learning: Generative AI Reduced Study Time on Math Problems and the Knowledge They Build. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21629

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same topic-by-quarter ramp models estimated separately by age group on ALEKS learning interactions. High school "β=− 0.0341, p< 0.001" (31.3% cumulative); middle school "β=− 0.0086, p< 0.01" (9.0% cumulative).

> "High school topics show a larger effect of 3.35% per quarter (β=− 0.0341, p< 0.001), corresponding to a 31.3% cumulative decline (CI:−37.3%, −25.4%). Middle school courses show a smaller but statistically significant effect of 0.86% per quarter"
