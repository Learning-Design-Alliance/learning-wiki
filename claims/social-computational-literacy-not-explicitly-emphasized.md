Odden, Lockwood, and Caballero. (2019). Physics Computational Literacy: An Exploratory Case Study Using Computational Essays. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020152](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020152)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Describing the University of Oslo physics program context, where students take programming courses in their first semester and use computation in later physics courses, the authors state "Social computational literacy, as far as we know, has not yet been explicitly emphasized in any courses". The computational essay project was designed partly to elicit social practices such as presenting essays in mock research-group meetings.
