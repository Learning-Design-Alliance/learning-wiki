`q3 i?` Youth from food-secure households during gestation had 1.65-fold higher adjusted odds of achieving University Entrance than those from food-insecure households (PIF definition). [→ Leon I. 2023](#leon-i-2023)

## Evidence

### Leon I. 2023

Leon I., Tautolo E.-S., Plank L.D., Rush E. (2023). Pacific Islands Families Study: Household Food Security during Pregnancy and Secondary School Educational Achievement. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15194131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of the Pacific Islands Families birth cohort (649 youths with NZQA records) assessed sex and household food security as predictors of University Entrance. The abstract and Table 6 report the food-secure adjusted OR as "1.6-fold (95% CI 1.1, 2.5 p = 0.026) higher if the household was food secure"; Table 6 prints the adjusted OR as 1.65 (1.07, 2.55), p = 0.026.

> "Logistics regression was applied to assess the influence of sex and household food security on the achievement of a UE qualification."
