### Munro Malcolm G. MD 2020

Munro Malcolm G. MD, Advincula Arnold P. MD, Banks Erika H. MD, Auguste Tamika C. MD, Chahine E. Britton MD, Grace Chen Chi Chiung MD, MHS, Curlin Howard L. MD, Jorgensen Elisa M. MD, Kim Jin Hee MD, MS, King Cara R. DO, MS, Lucas Joelle MD, Milad Magdy P. MD, MS, Mourad Jamal DO, Siedhoff Matthew T. MD, MSCR, Solnik M. Jonathon MD, Destephano Christopher C. MD, MPH, Thayn Kim PhD, for the Essentials in Minimally Invasive Gynecology (EMIG) Steering Committee. (2020). Essentials in Minimally Invasive Gynecology Manual Skills Construct Validation Trial. Obstetrics and Gynecology. [doi:10.1097/AOG.0000000000003936](https://doi.org/10.1097/AOG.0000000000003936)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective cohort study at 15 US and Canadian sites comparing 77 novice (PGY-1) and 70 mid-level (PGY-3) residents on five laparoscopic exercises. Mean times were "significantly less than for the novices" on every exercise, e.g. L-1 187 (±45) vs 256 (±59) seconds; significance was defined as a 95% CI around the mean difference excluding 0 (α=0.05).

> "In general, the mean times in seconds (±SD) for the mid-level participants for all exercises were significantly less than for the novices: L-1, 187 (±45) vs 256 (±59); L-2, 232 (±55) vs 274 (±38); L-3, 284 (±107) vs 344 (±101); L-4, 376 (±141) vs 481 (±126); and L-5, 420 (±100) vs 494 (±106)."
