Roy Lyster, Kazuya Saito, and Masatoshi Sato. (2013). Oral corrective feedback in second language classrooms. Language Teaching, Volume 46, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0261444812000365

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review synthesizes questionnaire and perception studies comparing learner and teacher preferences for error correction, reporting "a tendency for learners to prefer receiving CF more than teachers feel they should provide it". The mismatch is characterized as a tendency, not a universal finding.

> "Research on CF preferences is then reviewed, revealing a tendency for learners to prefer receiving CF more than teachers feel they should provide it."
