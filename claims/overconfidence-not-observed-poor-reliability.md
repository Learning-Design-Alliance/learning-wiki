## Evidence

### Prakash Shivesh 2017

Prakash Shivesh, Bihari Shailesh, Need Penelope, Sprick Cyle, Schuwirth Lambert. (2017). Immersive high fidelity simulation of critically ill patients to study cognitive errors: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0871-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis (Cronbach's alpha and Kappa) of the assessor questionnaire across the 70 reviewed sessions. The article reports "No instances of overconfidence were identified" and attributes the poor reliability to highly skewed observations towards absence of overconfidence.

> "The inter-rater agreement and internal consistency was good for all cognitive errors assessed, except ‘overconfidence' (Cronbach's alpha 0.46; Kappa statistic 0.17 for overconfidence)."
