### Lazaros Stavrinou 2025

Lazaros Stavrinou, Argyris Constantinides, Marios Belk, Vasos Vassiliou, Fotis Liarokapis, and Marios Constantinides. 2025. The Reel Deal: Designing and Evaluating LLM-Generated Short-Form Educational Videos. CHIGreece 2025. https://doi.org/10.1145/3749012.3749048

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same between-subjects experiment: mean revisits were "M = 2.90" (LLM) versus 3.52 (No-LLM); the Mann-Whitney U test was not statistically significant (U = 402.50, p = 0.2679). No effect size is printed.

> "The mean number of video revisits for the LLM group was M = 2.90, SD = 1.58, compared to M = 3.52, SD = 1.98 for the No-LLM group, as illustrated in Figure 3 (right). A Mann-Whitney U test indicated that this difference was not statistically significant, U = 402.50, p = 0.2679"
