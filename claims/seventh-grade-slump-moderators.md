# The seventh-grade slump is moderated by DT test, task content domain, and gender, with larger drops for WKCT, figural tasks, and a higher proportion of males

## Evidence

### Said-Metwaly 2019

Said-Metwaly, S., Fernández-Castilla, B., Kyndt, E., Van den Noortgate, W., & Barbot, B. (2019). Does the Fourth-Grade Slump in Creativity Actually Exist? A Meta-Analysis of the Development of Divergent Thinking in School-Age Children and Adolescents. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Does+the+Fourth-Grade+Slump+in+Creativity+Actually+Exist

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Post-hoc moderator analysis in Results of the Grade 6–7 overall DT difference found task content domain significant, explaining 65.61% of between-study variance; figural tasks showed the larger significant drop (-0.30, p = .003). No standardized effect size is printed for the moderation itself.

> "For task content domain, a significant negative SMD was obtained when using figural tasks (-0.30, p = .003) compared to a non-significant negative SMD when using verbal tasks (-0.14, p = .15)."

### Said-Metwaly 2019 (2)

Said-Metwaly, S., Fernández-Castilla, B., Kyndt, E., Van den Noortgate, W., & Barbot, B. (2019). Does the Fourth-Grade Slump in Creativity Actually Exist? A Meta-Analysis of the Development of Divergent Thinking in School-Age Children and Adolescents. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Does+the+Fourth-Grade+Slump+in+Creativity+Actually+Exist

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Post-hoc moderator analysis in Results found gender significant for the Grade 6–7 overall DT difference; the printed B = -0.002 is an unstandardized coefficient, so no effect size is coded. The authors note the gender effect was robust across all three DT indicators.

> "For gender, a larger proportion of males was associated with a larger negative SMD (B = -0.002, p = .02). Gender explained 28.61% of the between-study variance and 59.51% of the within-study variance for the overall analysis."
