# Women who drop STEM majors earn statistically significantly higher STEM GPAs than men who drop the same majors

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Among students who dropped chemistry, computer science, engineering, mathematics, or physics majors, women earned statistically significantly higher STEM GPAs than men (p < 0.001, Cohen's d of 0.58, a medium effect size). [→ Kyle M. Whitcomb and Chandralekha Singh 2020](#kyle-m-whitcomb-and-chandralekha-singh-2020)
`q2 i2` Among students who dropped biology, economics, geology, neuroscience, or statistics majors, women earned statistically significantly higher STEM GPAs than men (p < 0.001, Cohen's d of 0.54, a medium effect size).
