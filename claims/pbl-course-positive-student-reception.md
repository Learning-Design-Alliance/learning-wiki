## Evidence

### Schaller Michael D. 2023

Schaller Michael D., Gencheva Marieta, Gunther Michael R., Weed Scott A. (2023). Training doctoral students in critical thinking and experimental design using problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04569-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

End-of-course Likert surveys (Kirkpatrick Level 1) with response rates of 77–93% for course evaluations and 87–96% for facilitator evaluations across the two offerings; facilitator evaluation medians were 4.5–5. The article reports the medians descriptively, with no inferential test.

> "The median score for each question was 4 on a scale of 5 in 2020. In 2021, the median scores for the questions about active learning and hypothesis testing were 5 and the median score of the other questions was 4."
