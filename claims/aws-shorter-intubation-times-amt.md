## Evidence

### Murakami Y 2021

Murakami Y, Ueki R, Niki M, Hirose M, Shimode N. (2021). Three-day tracheal intubation manikin training for novice doctors using Macintosh laryngoscope, McGRATH MAC videolaryngoscope and Pentax AirwayScope. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023886

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Timing analysis of the same 15-participant AMT training trials, with total, visualization, and tube-passage times compared across devices. The article reports the AWS "significantly shorter ... on all 3 days" and, for tube passage, "There were no significant differences in the time from visualizing the vocal cords to tracheal intubation between the 3 scopes".

> "Compared with the Mac group, total intubation time and the time from holding a device to visualizing the vocal cords were significantly shorter in the AWS group on all 3 days (Fig. 3 (A, B), 4 (A, B))."
