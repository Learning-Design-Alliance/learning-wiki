## Evidence

### Aronoff N 2017

Aronoff N, Stellrecht E, Lyons AG, Zafron ML, Glogowski M, Grabowski J, Ohtake PJ. (2017). Teaching evidence-based practice principles to prepare health professions students for an interprofessional learning experience. Journal of the Medical Library Association. https://doi.org/10.5195/jmla.2017.179

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Author-developed anonymous 5-point Likert evaluation administered after the in-person activity in the pilot. Medians of 5 (IQR 4–5) on both statements for both modules indicate "The majority of students indicated that they learned new information".

> "The majority of students indicated that they learned new information (median [IQR]; module 1: 5 [4–5]; module 2: 5 [4–5]). The students also felt that they would be able to use the information in their future practice (module 1: 5 [4–5]; module 2: 5 [4–5])."

### Aronoff N 2017 (2)

Aronoff N, Stellrecht E, Lyons AG, Zafron ML, Glogowski M, Grabowski J, Ohtake PJ. (2017). Teaching evidence-based practice principles to prepare health professions students for an interprofessional learning experience. Journal of the Medical Library Association. https://doi.org/10.5195/jmla.2017.179

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative comments from the same evaluation, with themes identified by 2 investigators. The article reports "Many students (n=10) commented that they were appreciative of the content, especially in regard to the PubMed module."

> "A total of 19 out of 39 students (49%) provided additional feedback. Many students (n=10) commented that they were appreciative of the content, especially in regard to the PubMed module."
