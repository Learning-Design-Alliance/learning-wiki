## Evidence

### Pan Ruijun 2024

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 60 first- and second-year surgical residents, 30 per group, at a single Chinese centre; live-pig laparoscopic cholecystectomy was blindly scored by five trained assessors using GOALS and NOTSS. The article reports "higher technical and non-technical skill scores in the pedagogical group (P < 0.050)".

> "Trainees performing laparoscopic cholecystectomy in animal models demonstrated higher technical and non-technical skill scores in the pedagogical group (P < 0.050)."
