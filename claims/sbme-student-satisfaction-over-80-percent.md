## Evidence

### Wang S 2021

Wang S, Ren X, Ye J, Wang W, Huang H, Qin C. (2021). Exploration of simulation-based medical education for undergraduate students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000025982

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online feedback survey (20 items, 1–5 scale) completed by 331 of the 334 SBME-group students after the final exam. The printed satisfaction percentages for the course and each project all exceed 80%, and 79.3% were satisfied with teaching-aid fidelity.

> "Nevertheless, >80% of the students were satisfied with our SBME and its projects (SBME course, 81.2%; tension suture and suture tying, 82.4%; changing infected wound dressings, 84.1%; removing drainage tubes, 82.9%; and CPR, 83.6%)."
