# ECG diagnostic accuracy rose marginally from without case vignettes to with case vignettes among 176 participants in a single-group pre-post study with no comparison group (Cohen's d = 0.35)

## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2021

Viljoen Charle André, Millar Rob Scott, Manning Kathryn, Hoevelmann Julian, Burch Vanessa Celeste. (2021). Clinically contextualised ECG interpretation: the impact of prior clinical exposure and case vignettes on ECG diagnostic accuracy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02854-x

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional within-participant study of 176 trainees (67 junior students, 55 senior students, 54 residents) who each interpreted the same 25 ECGs without vignettes (Test 1) and with vignettes (Test 2), each serving as their own control. The article reports "a marginal improvement in accuracy of ECG interpretation" with Cohen's d = 0.35, p < 0.001.

> "Overall, all groups showed a marginal improvement in accuracy of ECG interpretation when the clinical context was known to them (Cohen’s d = 0.35, p < 0.001)."
