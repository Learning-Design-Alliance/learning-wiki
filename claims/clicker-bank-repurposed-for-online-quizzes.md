`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor's account of the unexpected mid-March 2020 switch to online instruction, when weekly in-person quizzes were transformed into online multiple-choice format using bank questions. This is an experiential report, not a controlled evaluation.

> "Based on this experience, I can conﬁrm that the clicker bank is also an excellent source of multiple-choice and true-false questions for use on quizzes or tests."
