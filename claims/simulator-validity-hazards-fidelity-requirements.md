### Sayantan Adak 2026

Sayantan Adak, Rima Hazra, Koyel Mukherjee, Digbalay Bose, Somnath Banerjee. (2026). Do LLM Tutors Actually Teach? A Process-Centered Synthesis of the Tutoring Loop. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.15692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.15692)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of simulator validity hazards (Section 7.5): each hazard flatters the tutor by making the learner easier to teach or rewarding what the simulator favors.

> "Appendix Table 9 lists five recurring hazards: fake confusion, competence or answer leakage, pro- file leakage, simulator–judge circularity, and mis- matched learning dynamics, the last directly evi- denced by real-learner comparisons."

### Sayantan Adak 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Martynova et al. 2025 and Scarlatos et al. 2026, that teacher evaluation surfaces qualitative departures and real-learner comparisons quantify behavioral mismatch.

> "Teachers still flag unrealistic attentiveness, language, emotion, and consistency (Martynova et al., 2025), and real-versus-simulated comparisons find residual gaps in dialogue acts, errors, and learning dynamics (Scarlatos et al., 2026)."
