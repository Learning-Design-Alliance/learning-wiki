## Evidence

### Dabbagh 2010

Dabbagh, N., & Beattie, M. (2010). Student and Instructor Perceptions of the Usefulness of Computer-Based Microworlds in Supporting the Teaching and Assessment of Computer Networking Skills: An Exploratory Study. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076143

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The study compared students with more extensive networking experience to those with less. No significant differences were found between prior class count and perceived CbMW benefits, while engagement time did correlate with perceived benefits.

> "while the results of this study revealed no significant differences between the number of prior classes taken (i.e., background knowledge), and the perceived benefits of the CbMW, the results did reveal a significant correlation between time spent using the CbMW and its perceived benefits"
