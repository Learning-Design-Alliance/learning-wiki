## Evidence

### Ungerer 2026

Ungerer, T., Rastle, K., & Armstrong, B. C. (2026). Bringing the Reading Sciences Into the Classroom: Insights for Phonics Instruction. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/17456916261438004

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Experimental work by Apfelbaum et al. (2013): first-grade students practiced vowel GPCs with example words in which the vowel was surrounded by either highly variable consonants (e.g., pat, fan, cab) or low-variability consonants (e.g., bat, pat, hat); the high-variability condition won. No effect size is printed.

> "The high-variability condition led to significantly better learning."
