Landscape analysis of state ESA statutes and secondary sources reports that "13 states currently have ESA programs in operation." The brief also states ESAs served over 326,400 students at the beginning of the 2023/24 school year, and that Alabama, Georgia, Louisiana, and Wyoming enacted new programs in 2024.
