BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10160-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory item-level comparisons in the same randomized sample found no statistically significant group differences for the critical thinking item (p = 0.21) or the empathy awareness item (p = 0.10); no effect size magnitude beyond small printed r values was interpreted as significant.

> "However, critical thinking (S1) and empathy-driven communication awareness (A1) did not reach statistical significance (p > 0.05)."
