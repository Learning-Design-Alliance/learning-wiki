Baseline ANOVA on the 40-item psychology achievement test administered to both intact classes before treatment. The article reports "no statistically  significant difference in psychology pretest scores"; equivalence was not formally tested beyond this comparison.

> "The results of the ANOVA analysis show no statistically  significant difference in psychology pretest scores (F  (1, 108) = .258; p = .613) between the experimental group (M = 18.86, SD = 4.58) and the control group (M = 19.31, SD = 4.79)"
