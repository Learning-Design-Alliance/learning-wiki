Evidence¶

Sayı 2018¶

Sayı, A. K. (2018). Teachers’ Views about the Teacher Training Program for Gifted Education. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n4p262

q2 · i? · design · r2

Likert items on instructor qualifications from 71 teachers. Pedagogical qualifications scored “M: 5.82, SD: 0.53”, including positive attitude and behavior “M: 5.91, SD: 0.28”.

"Participants reported in the survey results that the instructors taught the course clearly (M: 5.84, SD: 0.40), were able to use necessary tools and equipment effectively when teaching (M: 5.61, SD: 0.59) and could utilize necessary methods and techniques in a way that enables effective participation (M: 5.72, SD: 0.50)."
