Case-level proportions on the post-encounter form across the three videotaped scenarios: leading-diagnosis accuracy "was lowest in Case 1 (residents 10%, internists 13%) and highest in Case 3 (residents 40%, internists 53%) for both groups", with treatment-plan proportions following the same pattern.

> "Overall, the proportion of participants able to achieve a correct response on the PEF for leading diagnosis was lowest in Case 1 (residents 10%, internists 13%) and highest in Case 3 (residents 40%, internists 53%) for both groups."
