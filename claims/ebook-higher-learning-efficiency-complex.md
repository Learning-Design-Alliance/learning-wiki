## Evidence

### Bjarne Skjødt Worm 2013

Bjarne Skjødt Worm. (2013). Learning from Simple Ebooks, Online Cases or Classroom Teaching When Acquiring Complex Knowledge. A Randomized Controlled Trial in Respiratory Physiology and Pulmonology. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0073336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive learning-efficiency measure (improvement in correct questions per minute of study) in Table 1: eBook 0.92 versus eCase 0.51 and classroom 0.59 for complex content. The article attributes this to the longer learning times in case-based formats.

> "The improvement in the recall and learning efficiency of simple knowledge was equal in all groups, while group 1a (eBook) was significantly less effective in the recall of complex knowledge but had higher learning efficiency."
