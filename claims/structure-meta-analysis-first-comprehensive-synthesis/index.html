Patall 2023¶

Patall, E. A., Yates, N., Lee, J., Chen, S., Bhat, P., Lee, K., Beretvas, S. N., Guan, S., Cohen, M., Zhai, F., Brecht-Heitz, S., DeLorenzo, D., & Gonzales, K. (2023). Educational Psychologist. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED631356

The article positions its contribution against prior reviews (Wang et al., 1993; Simonsen et al., 2008; Marzano, 2003; Stroet et al., 2013; Korpershoek et al., 2016), noting that "this synthesis is the first to comprehensively meta-analyze evidence" on these specific outcome constructs across PK-12. This is the article's own claim about its contribution, not a tested empirical result.

"In sum, this synthesis is the first to comprehensively meta-analyze evidence on the associations between classroom structure and preschool through high school students’ (dis)engagement, competence beliefs, and achievement."

Related Claims¶

  • Teachers' provision of classroom structure correlates positively with students' achievement, engagement, and competence beliefs, and shows a statistically non-significant negative relationship with disengagement — related
  • Classroom structure interventions produce significant positive average effects on achievement and engagement and reduce disengagement, with a non-significant effect on competence beliefs — related
  • Consistent with a dual process model of engagement, structure shows stronger associations with engagement than with disengagement — related
  • Structure-outcome relationships appear largely universal across grade levels — related
