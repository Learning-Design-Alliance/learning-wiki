### Thoonen Bart P. A. 2024

Thoonen Bart P. A., Scherpbier-de Haan Nynke D., Fluit Cornelia R. M. G., Stalmeijer Renée E. (2024). How Do Trainees Use EPAs to Regulate Their Learning in the Clinical Environment? A Grounded Theory Study. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1403

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Grounded theory analysis of ten semi-structured interviews with GP-trainees at RadboudUMC, plus two confirmatory PICTOR interviews. The authors report that "The majority of EPAs’ positive influences on learning are evident in the taking control stage"; no effect size is printed for this qualitative finding.

> "The majority of EPAs’ positive influences on learning are evident in the taking control stage which constitutes the bulk of each placement."
