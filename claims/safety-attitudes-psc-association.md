## Evidence

### Chen Daoming 2026

Chen Daoming, Li Chenxi, Su Yubin, Wu Fang, Fang Xiaoru, Chu Hongwei, Bai Shuang. (2026). Patient Safety Competency Among Operating Room Nurses: Associations With Safety Attitudes, Systems Thinking and Burnout in a Multicentre Cross‐Sectional Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70831

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Primary mixed-effects model; bivariate Pearson correlation for safety attitudes was r = 0.700 (p < 0.001). The authors note conceptual overlap between the PSC scale and Safety Attitudes Questionnaire "may have inflated the observed association".

> "Higher safety attitudes (B = 0.683, 95% CI 0.565–0.801; p < 0.001), stronger systems thinking (B = 0.270, 95% CI 0.098–0.443; p = 0.002), and greater personal accomplishment (B = 0.217, 95% CI 0.045–0.388; p = 0.013) were associated with higher PSC scores"
