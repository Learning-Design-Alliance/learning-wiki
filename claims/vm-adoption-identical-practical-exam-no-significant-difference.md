# Transitioning histology practical exams from microscopes to a virtual microscope was not associated with a significant difference in identical practical exam performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no significant difference between practical exam scores administered with traditional microscopes (2007) and with the VM (2009) on an identical exam. [→ Triola Marc M 2011 (2)](#triola-marc-m-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Triola Marc M 2011

Triola Marc M, Holloway William J. (2011). Enhanced virtual microscopy for collaborative education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective comparison of identical summative practical exam scores from one year before and one year after the microscope-to-VM transition (165 students pre-VM, 164 using the VM). Means were 80.1 (SD = 5.38) vs 81.8 (SD = 11.9); "There was no significant difference between the exam scores across the two modalities."

> "The 2007 pre-VM summative exam was administered to 165 students using traditional microscopes and had a mean score of 80.1 (SD = 5.38). The identical exam was administered in 2009 to 164 students using the VM and had a mean of 81.8 (SD = 11.9). There was no significant difference between the exam scores across the two modalities (t: -1.69, P-Value = 0.093, 95% CI = -3.73 < μ1-μ2 < 0.289)."
