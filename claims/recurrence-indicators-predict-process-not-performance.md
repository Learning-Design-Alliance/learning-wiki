Laboratory experiment with 44 learners in dyads using a PCA during a jigsaw-like knowledge integration task; gaze synchronization, language conformance, and facial-expression emotional matching predicted process variables, but "no significant correlation was established" with learning performance.

> "Subsequently, numerous dependent variables in the collaborative learning process were predicted using the three proposed indicators. However, no significant correlation was established between learning performance and the indicators used."
