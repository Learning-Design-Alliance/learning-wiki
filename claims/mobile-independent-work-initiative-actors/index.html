Evidence¶

Wagner 2016¶

Wagner, M.-N. L., Donskaya, M. V., Kupriyanova, M. E., & Ovezova, U. A. (2016). Perspectives of Introduction of the Mobile-Assisted Language Learning (Mall) Technology. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(15), 8562-8571. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q2 · i? · design · r2

From the same observational implementation study of MALL with Mobl21: the authors report that independent work on personal mobile devices produced "mobilization of attention and improvement of perception" and that students became initiative actors controlling their own learning. No quantitative measures are printed.

"Independent work with the use of personal mobile devices led to the development of their cognitive field - mobilization of attention and improvement of perception. The students turned into initiative actors and controllers of their own learning activity."
