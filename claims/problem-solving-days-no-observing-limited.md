## Evidence

### Eric Matthew Hickok 2015

Eric Matthew Hickok. (2015). Teaching practices of graduate teaching assistants. San José State University Master's Project. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07740)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of two problem-solving session observations of one TA against regular lab and 50W sessions (Fig. 4.6). The author explicitly states "I cannot make any generalizations comparing these sessions to the regular sessions." No effect sizes are printed.

> "Interestingly, there was no Observing taking place during the problem solving sessions. Looking over the time series data for these two observations, it appears the TA gave a short lecture on the basic physics involved and a few problem solving strategies lasting seven to fourteen minutes and then proceeded to only interact with individual students when they approached with questions."
