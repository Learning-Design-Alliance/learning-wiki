## Evidence

### Yeh M.-L. 2025

Yeh M.-L., Chang B.-Y., Dai H.-D., & Chung M.-H. (2025). Effect of training program on evidence-based practice competencies in hospital nurses in Taiwan: A quasi-experimental study. The Journal of Nursing Research, 33(5), e412. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000701

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

K-means cluster analysis of HS-EBP data in the quasi-experimental study, performed in IBM SPSS 20.0 with no predefined number of clusters. The article prints cluster sizes (n=20 each) and a cluster quality of 0.7; no effect size applies.

> "In the pretest, as some in the exposure group were identified as being relatively more competent in EBP, this group was further divided into exposure A (n=20) and exposure B (n=20) groups with a high cluster quality (0.7) for subsequent data analysis work."
