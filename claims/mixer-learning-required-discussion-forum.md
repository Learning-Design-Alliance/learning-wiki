Qualitative analysis of children's narratives from the 20-minute Classroom Discussion Forums led by a trained researcher after the EEQ. Children recalled the game, identified the differing rules, and expressed in-group views such as "The red team cheated".

> "This learning only occurred with the inclusion of the classroom discussion forum, where the lessons of MIXER were discussed and reinforced by teacher led discussions."
