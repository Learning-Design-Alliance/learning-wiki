Evidence¶

Fumie Watanabe 2026¶

Fumie Watanabe, Tota Suko, Takashi Ishida, Yuko Kuma, Manabu Kobayashi, Shigeichi Hirasawa, and Gendo Kumoi. (2026). Interaction Effects Between Learner Characteristics and Dialogue Format in TTS Dialogue-Based Lessons. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20822 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.20822

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Linear mixed-effects model analysis of ARCS motivation from 222 first-year high school students viewing three formats, with participant intercepts as random effects. The article reports small-to-medium effect sizes (CE×TT f=.17; RCE×TT f=.15) and Marginal R2 = .095. Simple slopes shifted for CE from "negative in the TS format (−0.151) to near zero in the TT format (0.011)".

"the interaction between the RCE factor and the TT format was significant (b=−0.238,p=.002), as was the interaction between the CE factor and the TT format (b= 0.162,p<.001)"
