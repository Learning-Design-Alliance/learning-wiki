## Evidence

### Kunpalin Yada 2025

Kunpalin Yada, Kik Charlotte C., Lebouthillier Francis, Abbasi Nimrah, Ryan Greg, Spoor Jochem, Looi Thomas, Kulkarni Abhaya V., Van Mieghem Tim. (2025). Fetoscopic Robotic Open Spina Bifida Treatment (FROST): A Preclinical Feasibility and Learning Curve Study. BJOG: An International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.18161

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

LC-CUSUM learning curve analysis of four surgeons (an MFM expert, a neurosurgical consultant, an MFM fellow and a neurosurgical resident, none with prior robotic experience) on the FROST simulator. Expert 1 reached the control limit of 0.85 after 21 procedures and "Novice 1 required 18 procedures to become competent"; Expert 2 and Novice 2 required 15 and 16 respectively. Success required repair time ≤ 120 min, suture interval < 3 mm and GEARS score > 21/30.

> "Expert 2 and Novice 2 required a total of 15 and 16 procedures, respectively, to reach competence"
