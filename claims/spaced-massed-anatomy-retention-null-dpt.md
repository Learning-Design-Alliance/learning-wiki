## Evidence

### Amabile Amy H. 2021

Amabile Amy H., Nixon-Cave Kim, Georgetti Larry J., Sims Ashley C. (2021). Front-loading of anatomy content has no effect on long-term anatomy knowledge retention among physical therapy students: a prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02925-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective cohort pre-test post-test study comparing a spaced two-semester DPT anatomy cohort with a massed 10-week cohort; independent-samples t-tests found "no statistically significant differences in the course grades, cumulative GPA, pre-test, post-test, or improvement factor". No effect sizes were printed.

> "Student’s t-tests for independent samples showed no statistically significant differences in the course grades, cumulative GPA, pre-test, post-test, or improvement factor between the groups."

### Amabile Amy H. 2021 (2)

Amabile Amy H., Nixon-Cave Kim, Georgetti Larry J., Sims Ashley C. (2021). Front-loading of anatomy content has no effect on long-term anatomy knowledge retention among physical therapy students: a prospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02925-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA on improvement factor in the same two-cohort prospective study, with covariates of age, sex, non-traditional status, and years elapsed between undergraduate and DPT anatomy; the article reports "still no statistically significant difference in improvement factor between the two cohorts". No effect size printed.

> "After adjustment for these variables, there was still no statistically significant difference in improvement factor between the two cohorts."
