Evidence¶

Mahdizadeh and Aminafshar 2018¶

Molood Mahdizadeh, Naeemeh Aminafshar. (2018). The Impact of Timed Reading Activity on Iranian EFL learners Reading Speed, Comprehension, and Attitudes at Intermediate Level. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.5p.11

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Open-ended questionnaire responses from the 20 experimental-group participants: "14 students mentioned" initial anxiety from time pressure that disappeared as they became used to the activity.

"As for the possible disadvantages, 14 students mentioned that at the beginning of the course, timed reading activity increased their anxiety level since they felt the pressure of time. However, as the time went by, their anxiety disappeared since the students got used to this activity."

Mahdizadeh and Aminafshar 2018 (2)¶

Molood Mahdizadeh, Naeemeh Aminafshar. (2018). The Impact of Timed Reading Activity on Iranian EFL learners Reading Speed, Comprehension, and Attitudes at Intermediate Level. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.5p.11

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative questionnaire analysis: participants attributed comprehension gains to vocabulary knowledge rather than reading fast, and noted fast reading may force rereading when passages contain many new words.

"Nearly all of the students believed that timed reading activity had no special effect on improving their reading comprehension. They mentioned that reading comprehension can be improved by increasing their vocabulary knowledge rather than reading fast."
