Inclusive Strategies for Children With Developmental Disabilities in Mainstream Classrooms in African Countries: A Systematic Review of Stakeholder Experiences, Attitudes, and Perspectives. q2 · i? · review · r3

Thematic synthesis of qualitative study reports from the review. Teachers added life skills such as "combing hair and tying shoelaces" to the curriculum, differentiated tasks while "focusing on the same objective", and adapted assessments with expanded opportunities and alternative response modes. No effect sizes are reported, as expected for qualitative synthesis.

"It was apparent in several studies that teachers applied a key inclusive education principle: the responsiveness to a diverse range of learner needs, through curriculum and task adaptations and individualized instruction and selection of strategies."
