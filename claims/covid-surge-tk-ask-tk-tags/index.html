Evidence¶

Zahava Scherz · Asaf Salman · Giora Alexandron · Yael Shwartz 2023¶

Zahava Scherz · Asaf Salman · Giora Alexandron · Yael Shwartz. (2023). WhatsApp Discourse Throughout COVID-19: Towards Computerized Evaluation of the Development of a STEM Teachers Professional Learning Community. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00320-3

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Content analysis of the annotated L-PLC WhatsApp discourse found a significant increase in technological knowledge tags across cohorts: "from 9 tags in cohort I to 206 tags in cohort III" for TK and 6 to 82 for ASK-TK. Participants actively requested and shared technological tools in cohort III; no effect size is printed.

"there was a significant increase in the number of both TK (from 9 tags in cohort I to 206 tags in cohort III), and ASK-TK (from 6 tags in cohort I to 82 tags in cohort III) tags across cohorts."
