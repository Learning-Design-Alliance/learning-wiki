Bahadur 2013¶

Bahadur, G. K. & Oogarah, D. (2013). Interactive whiteboard for primary schools in Mauritius: An effective tool or just another trend? IJEDICT, 9(1), 19-35. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu//viewarticle.php?id=1559

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' classroom observation during the three-class experiment; they attribute the enthusiasm to a "novelty factor" and note it "did not translate into any significant improvements in performance".

"From an observational perspective, the IWB classes generated more enthusiasm and attention from the students. This could be the result of their novelty factor, however, and as shown, this did not translate into any significant improvements in performance."
