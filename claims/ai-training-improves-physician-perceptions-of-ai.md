`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post perception assessment completed by 250 of the 326 participants (Figure 4, Table S4/S5). The article reports the proportion perceiving high AI accuracy (>80% of the time) in diagnosis and treatment planning nearly tripled after the intervention. Only significance is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "All 5 perception changes (questions 1-5) were statistically significant (Table S5 in Multimedia Appendix 4)."

### Sapci A Hasan 2026 (2)

Effectiveness of Informed AI Use on Clinical Competence of General Practitioners and Internists: Pre-Post Intervention Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/75534

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Moderator analysis of perception shifts (Table S6) in the same study. No significant perception-shift differences were observed by sex, specialty, type of patients served, or previous AI training. Only P values are printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "participants who were unfamiliar with AI before the course exhibited a greater shift in their perception of AI diagnostic accuracy (P=.01) and greater willingness and confidence to incorporate AI tools into clinical practice (P<.001; Table S6 in Multimedia Appendix 4)."
