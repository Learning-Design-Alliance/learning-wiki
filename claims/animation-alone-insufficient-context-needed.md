## Evidence

### Laurence Arcadias and Robin Corbet 2022

Laurence Arcadias and Robin Corbet. (2022). An Astro-Animation Class: Optimizing Artistic, Educational and Outreach Outcomes. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1162/leon_a_02241](https://doi.org/10.1162/leon_a_02241)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' synthesis of survey results including MSC visitor surveys (N = 25 general audience reporting mixed feelings) and teacher surveys (N = 37, more reserved). The article states context "may be as little as a few explanatory sentences"; no effect size is printed.

> "We found that simply screening the animations on their own has a visual impact and may raise scientific awareness. However, for science learning and education it is essential to provide some additional context, which may be as little as a few explanatory sentences."
