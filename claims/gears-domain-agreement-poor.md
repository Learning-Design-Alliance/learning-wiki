## Evidence

### Marotta A 2025

Marotta A, Schena CA, Ascanelli S, Azzolina D, Calabrese P, Iovino DP, Sani V, Gavriilidis P, Laterza V, Marchegiani F, Pellino G, Celentano V, de'Angelis N. (2025). Robotic ventral rectopexy videos on youtube: reliability of quality and educational value assessment among raters with different degrees of surgical experience. Int J Colorectal Dis 40:152. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-04936-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

GEARS ratings of surgeon performance in the 25 videos were made independently by three trainees, three fellows, and three senior surgeons on a 5-point Likert scale; agreement was assessed with Krippendorff's alpha, with "poor agreement" below 0.4 and marginal agreement 0.4-0.6.

> "KF indicated poor agreement among trainees, fellows, and seniors in the domains of force control (KF = 0.32), tissue handling (KF = 0.37), autonomy (KF = 0.12), and robot control (KF = 0.42). Marginal agreement was observed in the domains of depth perception (KF = 0.48), bimanual skill (KF = 0.56), and efficiency (KF = 0.52)."
