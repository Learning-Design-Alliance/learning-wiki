## Evidence

### Chatziisaak D 2024

Chatziisaak D, Sparn M, Krstic D, Bauci G, Warschkow R, Brunner W, Schmied B, Hahnloser D, Bischofberger S. (2024). Be prepared! Impact of structured video-assisted coaching on performance in a simulated bleeding exercise during laparoscopic surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11173-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Crossed-effects analysis of coaching with demographics and laparoscopic experience in the 27-trainee dataset; only bariatric experience (5 of 27 participants) moderated the effect, reflecting, in the authors' words, greater overall laparoscopic experience.

> "experience in laparoscopic bariatric surgery (1–50) was associated with an increased GOALS sum score by 0.89 (95%CI: 0.30–1.48, P = 0.049) with a decreased effect of structured video-assisted coaching of −0.68 (95%CI: −1.00 to −0.35, P < 0.001)"
