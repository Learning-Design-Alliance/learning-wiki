## Evidence

### Meister Tara 2023

Meister Tara, Foessleitner Philipp, Breuer Georg, Winder Franziska M., Favero Martine, Friemann Margareta, Krischer Benedict, Weiss Martin, Windsperger Karin (2023). The impact of gender on the self-confidence of practical and surgical skills among OBGYN residents: a trinational survey. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-023-07202-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate regression analysis of the trinational resident survey (F[5,415] = 8.86; p < 0.001; n = 421) on confidence in obstetric interventions such as forceps and vacuum delivery. The article reports "OR − 3.43" for being female, with women rated 3.47 (± 1.01) versus men 3.91 (± 1.23).

> "Thus, being a woman significantly lowers confidence level (OR − 3.43; CI − 5.28 to − 1.58; p < 0.001)."
