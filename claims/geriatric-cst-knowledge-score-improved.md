Secondary outcome in the same quasi-randomized comparison: a three-item knowledge score (0–3) on MMSE and BADL items from the questionnaire. After the CST, more students correctly rated the pentagon drawing (28.2% vs 65.8%, P < 0.001) and the non-BADL activity (56.3% vs 86.8%, P < 0.001). No effect size printed.
