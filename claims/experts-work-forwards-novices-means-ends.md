## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Physics problem-solving studies found experts tend to work forwards from given information to the goal, whereas novices use a working-backwards strategy based on means-ends analysis. [→ Marcus 1998](#marcus-1998)

## Evidence

### Marcus 1998

Marcus, N. (1998). Understanding Instructions. PhD thesis, University of New South Wales. https://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/70247

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The thesis reports, citing Larkin, McDermott, Simon, and Simon (1980) and Simon and Simon (1978), thinking-aloud comparisons of experts and novices on physics problems. Novices select equations containing the goal and set subgoals, using means-ends analysis, while schema-driven experts proceed directly forward.

> "Overall, they found that experts tend to "work forwards" from the given information to the goal."
