## Evidence

### Gick 1983

Gick, M. L., & Holyoak, K. J. (1983). Schema Induction and Analogical Transfer. Cognitive Psychology, 15, 1-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(83)90002-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Prior experiments by the authors (Gick & Holyoak, 1980), reported in this article's background section: subjects memorized the military story under a story-recall guise, then attempted the radiation problem without a hint. Assuming a 10% no-analogy base rate, the authors conclude only a third or less of subjects who could apply the analogy spontaneously noticed it.

> "only about 30% of our subjects produced the convergence solution, as opposed to about 75% who produced it when a hint was given."
