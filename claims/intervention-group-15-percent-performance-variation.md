ANCOVA (Table 2) of the quasi-experiment, with sex and pre-test score as covariates. The text states the intervention group "expl ained 15% of the variation in performance", but Table 2 has no group row: the partial eta square of 0.15 is printed on the "(CGEFT)" row, the group embedded figure test that measures cognitive style (F = 4.00, p = 0.0145). So no effect size is printed for the intervention on performance.
