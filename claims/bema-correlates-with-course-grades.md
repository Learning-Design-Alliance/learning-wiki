## Evidence

### S. J. Pollock 2009

S. J. Pollock. (2009). A Longitudinal Study of Student Conceptual Understanding in Electricity and Magnetism. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. https://www.compadre.org/PER/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0907.5597](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0907.5597)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis across multiple semesters of Physics II (over 2600 matched valid BEMA results) and of upper-division BEMA versus course grade (r=0.57, N=167). The article takes these correlations as evidence the BEMA is "a useful and relevant measure of learning" in these courses.

> "the correlation coefficient (r) of BEMA post -score to course grade ranges between .45 and .65 for different terms"
