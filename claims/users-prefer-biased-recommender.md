`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

As reported in the review, S3 (Wang et al., 2022) developed a gender-debiased college major recommender and evaluated it via ML metrics and an online user study with 202 participants; the debiased system was fairer (lower UPAR) and more accurate by NDCG@K, yet users preferred the biased version.

> "However, when evaluated through an online user study of more than 200 college students, participants preferred the original biased system over the debiased system (S3). The findings of this study highlight that fair algorithms may not meet the expectations of end users, for refusing to confirm existing human bias."
