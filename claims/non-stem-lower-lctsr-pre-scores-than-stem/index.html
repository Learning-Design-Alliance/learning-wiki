Evidence¶

Moore 2011¶

Moore, J. C., & Rubbo, L. J. (2011). Scientific reasoning abilities of non-science majors in physics-based courses. https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.2764 doi:10.48550/arxiv.1110.2764

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Observational comparison of pre-instruction LCTSR scores: the authors' non-STEM population (N = 109, avg. = 54%) scored significantly lower than the STEM comparison sample (N = 1208, avg. = 75%) reported by Bao et al. No significant difference was found between physics and astronomy courses or between the two institutions.

"Table I shows average LCTSR pre-instruction scores (N = 1208, avg. = 75%) for science and engineering majors enrolled in a calculus-based introductory physics course, as reported by Bao, et al."
