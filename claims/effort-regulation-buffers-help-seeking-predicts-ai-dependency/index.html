q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same multiple regression (N = 478). The MSLQ help-seeking subscale measures general academic help seeking from human sources, not help seeking from AI tools, so the positive association cannot indicate substitution of AI for human helpers.

"effort regulation was negatively associated (β = −0.134, p = 0.013), help seeking was positively associated (β = 0.124, p = 0.003), and time and study environment management was not significant (β = −0.046, p = 0.374)"
