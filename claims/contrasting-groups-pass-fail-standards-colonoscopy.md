## Evidence

### Preisler Louise MD 2015

Preisler Louise MD, Svendsen Morten Bo Søndergaard MScEng, Nerup Nikolaj MD, Svendsen Lars Bo MD, DMSc, Konge Lars MD, PhD. (2015). Simulation-Based Training for Colonoscopy: Establishing Criteria for Competency. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000440

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Standard setting for the virtual-reality simulator test using the contrasting-groups method with novices as the noncompetent group and consultants as the competent group. The pass score of 15.5 points/min was set at the intersection of the two score distributions.

> "The pass/fail score was set at the intersection of distributions = 15.5 points/minutes (Figure 1A), and the consequences of this standard were explored showing that 1 of the consultants did not pass the test"

### Preisler Louise MD 2015 (2)

Preisler Louise MD, Svendsen Morten Bo Søndergaard MScEng, Nerup Nikolaj MD, Svendsen Lars Bo MD, DMSc, Konge Lars MD, PhD. (2015). Simulation-Based Training for Colonoscopy: Establishing Criteria for Competency. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000440

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Standard setting for the phantom-model test using the contrasting-groups method. The pass/fail standard of 0.79 points/minute was set at the intersection of the score distributions; all experienced endoscopists passed, plus a single novice outlier.

> "The pass/fail standard was set to 0.79 points/minute. One novice managed to pass the test"
