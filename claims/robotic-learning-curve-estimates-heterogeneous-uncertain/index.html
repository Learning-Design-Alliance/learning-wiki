Evidence¶

Soomro N. A. 2020¶

Soomro N. A., Hashimoto D. A., Porteous A. J., Ridley C. J. A., Marsh W. J., Ditto R. and Roy S. (2020). Systematic review of learning curves in robot‐assisted surgery. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/bjs5.50235

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of the 27 duration-of-surgery studies reporting whether the learning curve was overcome, covering 22 unique procedures of which only five were supported by multiple studies. The review reports estimates "varied substantially between studies", citing the distal pancreatectomy contrast (40 versus not overcome in 11 and 83 patients).

"the number of patients required to overcome the learning curve for these varied substantially between studies. For example, three studies evaluated the learning curve for duration of surgery for robot‐assisted distal pancreatectomy. In one study47 the learning curve was overcome after 40 patients"
