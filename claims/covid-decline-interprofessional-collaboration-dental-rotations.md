## Evidence

### Shukla A 2025

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post t-test in Results §3.4. Pre-COVID student self-reported scores were slightly higher than faculty-assigned scores; post-COVID faculty scores remained higher than students' self-reported scores.

> "Faculty mean scores for Interprofessional Collaboration decreased from 4.46 in 2018‐2020 to 4.35 in 2020–2022, with a statistically significant difference (p < 0.05). Similarly, student mean scores decreased from 4.49 in 2018–2020 to 4.2 in 2020–2022, indicating a significant decline (p < 0.05)."

### Shukla A 2025 (2)

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cohort ANOVA result in Results §3.4: student scores declined from 4.50 (Class of 2019) to 4.15 (Class of 2022) with significance for students but not faculty, making the evidence pattern mixed across rater groups.

> "The changes in student scores are significant (p‐value < 0.05), while faculty scores show less significant differences (p‐value > 0.05)."
