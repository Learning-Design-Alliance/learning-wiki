## Evidence

### Al-Asmar Ayah A. 2021

Al-Asmar Ayah A., Oweis Yara, Ismail Noor H., Sabrah Alaa H. A., Abd-Raheam Islam M. (2021). The predictive value of high school grade point average to academic achievement and career satisfaction of dental graduates. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-021-01662-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the survey of 333 matched graduates (265 female, 68 male in dichotomous responses), Pearson correlations and χ2 tests found no significant relation between gender, AA-GPA, and the tested career and satisfaction variables (p > 0.05); no effect size printed.

> "As for the correlation with gender, there was no statistically significant correlation between gender and the different tested factors as seen in Table 6. Moreover, χ2 test results indicated there is no relation between AA-GPA and gender and any of the tested variables (p > 0.05)."
