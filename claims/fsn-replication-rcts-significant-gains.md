Andy J. Frey, Jason W. Small, Hill M. Walker, Brandon Mitchell, John R. Seeley, Edward G. Feil, Jon Lee, and Steven R. Forness. (2023). First Step Next: A Best-Evidence Synthesis of Replication Randomized Controlled Trials From 2009 to 2021. Remedial and Special Education, 44(1), 60-72. https://doi.org/10.1177/07419325211068145

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Best-evidence synthesis of five replication RCTs of First Step Next (N = 200, 286, 379, 126, 160) plus four subsample analyses. The review reports "small to large ESs" and "statistically significant behavioral gains" relative to randomized control conditions; no pooled effect size is printed.

> "The best-evidence synthesis, along with subsample analyses, resulted in small to large ESs, very positive improvement indices, and statistically significant behavioral gains compared with students randomized to control conditions."
