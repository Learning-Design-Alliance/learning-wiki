## Subclaims
`q3 i0` After adding the intervention condition, learners received significantly higher academic emotional regulation (d = 0.14, p = 0.03) than without intervention. [→ Tan Jing 2021](#tan-jing-2021)

## Evidence

### Tan Jing 2021

Tan Jing, Mao Jie, Jiang Yizhang, Gao Ming. (2021). The Influence of Academic Emotions on Learning Effects: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18189678

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects main-effect test for the academic emotion outcome (Table 3: k = 54, N = 3654, p = 0.03, d = 0.14, 95% CI [0.01, 0.27]). The review reports the pooled effect as "the average effect size of learners' academic emotion was 0.14".

> "In terms of academic emotion, the average effect size of learners' academic emotion was 0.14. The upper and lower limits of 95%CI were also greater than 0, indicating that the effect size was not caused by accidental factors."
