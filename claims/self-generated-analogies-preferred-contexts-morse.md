Amogh Sirnoorkar, Winter Allen, Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Examining Student and AI Generated Personalized Analogies in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.04290](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.04290)

Qualitative coding of student self-generated descriptions in preferred everyday contexts from the same survey; "810 (96.5%) generated comparisons," with contexts ranging from sports to beekeeping, and non-analogy responses favoring disciplinary domains like chemistry.

> "Of the 839 responses, 810 (96.5%) generated comparisons with only 5 reflecting similarity statements and the rest 805 analogies. However, in only three of the 805 cases, students employed multiple analogical contexts to describe the curve."
