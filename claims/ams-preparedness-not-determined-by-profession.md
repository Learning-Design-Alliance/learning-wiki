`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-profession comparison of the nine mapped curricula. The article reports that "alignment varied both within and across the OH professions reviewed", citing the strongest alignments in UniLorin veterinary medicine, UniPort pharmacy and ABU Zaria medicine. No effect size is printed.

> "AMS preparedness among OH graduates in Africa was not determined by profession alone, as alignment varied both within and across the OH professions reviewed. The strongest alignment was observed in UniLorin veterinary medicine (77%), UniPort pharmacy (67%), and ABU Zaria medicine (51%)"
