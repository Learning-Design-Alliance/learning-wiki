## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Expertise in Introductory Physics Using Categorization Task. Originally published 1974. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.08781](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.08781)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA on summed category frequencies over the 15 common problems, plus per-problem t-tests whose p-values "ranged from 0.42 to 0.96". The article also reports version II involving Chi problems was categorized worse than version I; equivalence was not tested beyond these null results.

> "The result was that there was no statistical significance between the two groups over all 15 problems that were present on both problem set versions (p = 0.90)."
