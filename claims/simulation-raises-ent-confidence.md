## Evidence

### MK Goutham 2023

MK Goutham, Saldanha Marina, Bhat Vadisha S., A Rajeshwary, Vincent Mark Jittu, Ravikumar Aishwarya. (2023). Simulation-based training in ear, nose and throat skills and emergencies. Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjorl.2022.01.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Confidence was measured with modified Likert scale items in the pre/post questionnaire and reported per group in Figure 4. The article reports higher confidence in simulation-trained groups; no statistical test or effect size is printed for this measure.

> "The participants had better scores in their confidence levels at the end of the program and these confidence levels were more in the groups which received simulation training"
