## Evidence

### Hertanti Nuzul Sri 2026

Hertanti Nuzul Sri, Nguyen Nga Thi Thuy, Chi Do Thi Kim, Chuang Yeu-Hui. (2026). Psychometric evaluation of the Indonesian version of the confidence scale among primary healthcare nurses in dengue-related prevention practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09471-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analyses in the validation study: internal consistency estimated among 458 nurses (α = 0.92, ω = 0.92) and test-retest stability over two weeks in a 30-nurse subsample (ICC = 0.74, 95% CI 0.52–0.86); SEM was 1.32 and MDC 3.66.

> "The C-Scale-I showed acceptable internal consistency among 458 nurses, with Cronbach's α = 0.92 and McDonald's ω = 0.92. The test-retest reliability, assessed among 30 nurses over a two-week interval, showed a single-measures ICC of 0.74 (95% CI, 0.52–0.86)"
