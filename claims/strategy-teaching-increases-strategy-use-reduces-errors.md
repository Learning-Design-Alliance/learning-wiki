`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive classification of responses to PRT questions (Table 8) by strategy used and error type before and after the intervention. Before the process, strategy use was 15.5% (experimental) and 10.4% (control), with 84.1% and 89.4% of responses showing error types.

> "It is seen that the percent of using problem-solving strategies in solving ratio problems after the experimental process were 81.1% in the experimental group students and 37.4% in the control group students. 18.7% of the experimental group students and 62.4% of the control group students are included in the types of error."
