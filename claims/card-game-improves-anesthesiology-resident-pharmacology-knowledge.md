## Evidence

### Anchalee Santi 2026

Anchalee Santi, Deekiatphaiboon Chanya, Prathep Sumidtra, Jitpakdee Wilasinee. (2026). Effectiveness of game-based learning of anesthetic drugs in anesthesiology residents: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10011-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In this cross-sectional observational pilot study, 19 anesthesiology residents completed identical pre- and posttests (total score: 20 points) before and after game-based learning. The total score

> "The total score significantly increased from 14.26 (pretest) to 18.91 (posttest) (p < 0.001)."

### Anchalee Santi 2026 (2)

Anchalee Santi, Deekiatphaiboon Chanya, Prathep Sumidtra, Jitpakdee Wilasinee. (2026). Effectiveness of game-based learning of anesthetic drugs in anesthesiology residents: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10011-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same study reported category-level pre- and posttest comparisons showing

> "A comparison of the pre- and posttest scores revealed improvements across all categories: 5.00 vs. 7.47 (p < 0.001) for induction drugs, 2.59 vs. 3.92 (p < 0.001) for volatile anesthetics, and 6.67 vs. 7.51 (p < 0.001) for neuromuscular agents."
