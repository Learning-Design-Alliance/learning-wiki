## Evidence

### Mahmoud Sulaiman Hamad Bani Abdelrahman & Muwafaq Saleem Bsharah 2014

Mahmoud Sulaiman Hamad Bani Abdelrahman & Muwafaq Saleem Bsharah. (2014). The Effect of Speed Reading Strategies on Developing Reading Comprehension among the 2nd Secondary Students in English Language. English Language Teaching, 7(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n6p168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with two intact sections of 21 male students each at Al-Ashrafieh secondary school for boys; the experimental section was trained on skimming and scanning. The article reports a significant post-test difference favoring the experimental group, with the null hypothesis rejected at (α≤ 0.05).

> "The results showed that there significant difference between the means of both groups due to the use of speed readin g strategies. As a result of that, the Null hypothesis was rejected at (α≤ 0.05)."
