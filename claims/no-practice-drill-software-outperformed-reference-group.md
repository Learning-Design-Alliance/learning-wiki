## Evidence

### Nicholes 2016

Nicholes, J. (2016). Measuring the Impact of Language-Learning Software on Test Performance of Chinese Learners of English. TESL-EJ, 20(2). https://tesl-ej.org/pdf/ej76/a3.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 15-week classroom quasi-experiment with 78 Chinese ELLs in a cross-border higher education program assigned four software supplements to four writing sections. The abstract reports "no practice-and-drill software group (RS, TMM, and MEM) significantly outperforming the reference software group (WOW)" despite some significant subsection gains.

> "no practice-and-drill software group (RS, TMM, and MEM) significantly outperforming the reference software group (WOW) although statistically significant improvements appeared among subsections of the test and among test totals"
