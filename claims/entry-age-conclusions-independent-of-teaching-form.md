Winch, W. H. (1914). When Should a Child Begin School? A Reply to Professor Strayer. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com/books?q=%22When+Should+a+Child+Begin+School%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Winch defends against the objection that results depend on the system of instruction by citing his multi-school design: he worked in schools with different attitudes to young children's training and found the same result in all of them, for the ages studied.

> "England has at present a very elastic educational system and by working in schools which presented very different educational attitudes towards the training of young children and by finding the same result in all of them, I hoped I had given some grounds for the belief that, so far as the ages I dealt with were concerned, the conclusions were independent of the form of teaching adopted."
