Chen J, Cui J, Li X, Yang J, Yang X, Zhong H, Wang F, Gu Q, Duan C. (2026). Application of a SPOC-based blended teaching system in full-cycle training for medical imaging interns: A randomized controlled educational intervention study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analyses performed to evaluate the robustness of the unadjusted between-group comparisons in the randomized study. The experimental group had higher scores across all 4 outcomes in both Welch independent-samples t tests and Mann–Whitney U tests, and "all 4 principal between-group comparisons remained statistically significant after exploratory Holm correction for multiple testing."

> "all 4 principal between-group comparisons remained statistically significant after exploratory Holm correction for multiple testing. These analyses supported the direction and statistical robustness of the unadjusted findings, although they did not eliminate the possibility of residual confounding"
