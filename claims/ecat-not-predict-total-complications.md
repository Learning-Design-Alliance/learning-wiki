## Evidence

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Negative binomial/Poisson count model with robust standard errors in the 160-patient cohort. "increasing case difficulty did not independently predict a higher risk of adverse events"; no other analyzed variables were significantly related to complication rates.

> "Conversely, when adjusting for the same confounding variables, E-CAT scores were not significantly associated with the total number of complications (IRR 1.03, 95% CI: 0.98–1.09, p = 0.286)"

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026 (2)

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same cohort. The authors suggest complications may be more influenced by operator experience, patient-related variables, or unexpected anatomical variations rather than treatment length or complexity alone.

> "Notably, the overall number of complications was not significantly correlated with treatment duration, number of sessions, or E-CAT scores"
