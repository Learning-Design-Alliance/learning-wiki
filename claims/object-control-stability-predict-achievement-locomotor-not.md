## Evidence

### Wang Lijing 2025

Wang Lijing, Sun Jiancui, Wang Lijuan, Zheng Danheng, Zhou Yulan. (2025). Fundamental motor skills, executive functions, and academic achievement of Chinese schoolchildren: Testing a mediating model. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0336470

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of study variables (N = 733, Table 2). Object control showed the strongest FMS correlation with Chinese achievement ("OC: r = 0.271"), and both object control and stability skills correlated positively with mathematics.

> "Object control and stability skills were significantly and positively correlated with academic achievement in Chinese (OC: r = 0.271; SS: r = 0.200, p < 0.01) and mathematics (OC: r = 0.182; SS: r = 0.197, p < 0.01)."

### Wang Lijing 2025 (2)

Wang Lijing, Sun Jiancui, Wang Lijuan, Zheng Danheng, Zhou Yulan. (2025). Fundamental motor skills, executive functions, and academic achievement of Chinese schoolchildren: Testing a mediating model. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0336470

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (N = 733, Table 2). Locomotor skills showed only a small significant correlation with Chinese (r = 0.082) and no significant correlation with mathematics; locomotor was also non-significant in both final regression models (p = 0.070 and p = 0.617).

> "locomotor skills had a significantly positive correlation with academic achievement in Chinese (r = 0.082, p < 0.05) but an insignificant correlation with mathematics (r = 0.042, ns)"
