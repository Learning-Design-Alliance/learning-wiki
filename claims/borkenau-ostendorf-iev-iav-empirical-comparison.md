## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Confirmatory IEV factor analysis of the 30-item personality test almost completely recovered the nominal five-factor loading pattern, while individual time series obeyed two-, three-, or four-factor models. [→ Molenaar 2004](#molenaar-2004)
`q2 i?` Participants whose time series had the same number of factors still differed in loading patterns and values, measurement error variances, and the process model of latent factor scores. [→ Molenaar 2004](#molenaar-2004)

## Evidence

### Molenaar 2004

Molenaar, P. C. M. (2004). A Manifesto on Psychology as Idiographic Science: Bringing the Person Back Into Scientific Psychology, This Time Forever. Measurement, 2(4), 201-218. https://www.fmg.uva.nl/ml/publicaties_ml.cfm

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Reanalysis of the Borkenau and Ostendorf replicated time-series experiment: "There were 22 participants participating in this experiment, who were each measured on 90 consecutive days," yielding 30-variate daily score series. The article concludes "the obtained structures of IEV and IAV differ substantially" and the behavior space of the 22 participants is quite heterogeneous.

> "The time series of some participants obey a two-factor model, of others a three-factor model, and again others a four-factor model."
