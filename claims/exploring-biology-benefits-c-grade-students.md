## Evidence

### Wienhold Caroline J.; Branchaw Janet 2018

Wienhold Caroline J.; Branchaw Janet. (2018). Exploring Biology: A Vision and Change Disciplinary First-Year Seminar Improves Academic Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Grade-shift analysis of the 592 students with a recorded second-semester grade (Exploring Biology n = 306; comparison n = 286). "more often passed the second semester than comparison students who earned a “C”" (χ2, p = 0.021); no significant shifts for B-or-better students.

> "Exploring Biology students who received a “C” in the first semester of Introductory Biology more often passed the second semester than comparison students who earned a “C” in the first semester (χ2, p = 0.021). We did not detect statistically significant shifts for students who passed the first semester with a grade of “B” or better"
