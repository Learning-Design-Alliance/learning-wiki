## Evidence

### van de Werfhorst 2024

van de Werfhorst, H. G., Zwier, D., Geven, S., Bol, T. & Haelermans, C. (2024). Inequality in pandemic effects on school track placement and the role of social and academic embeddedness. npj Science of Learning 9:71. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00283-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Counterfactual cohort comparison using Dutch register data (~27% of each cohort, N=46,893 control and N=50,313 treatment in 2018/2019), matching pandemic-cohort students to pre-pandemic peers on prior test scores, school performance, SES, and gender. The study reports effects that are "small but statistically signiﬁcant effects".

> "The average pandemic effect is -0.068 levels for track recommendation, -0.039 levels for seventh-g rade track enrollment, and +0.011 levels for ninth-grade track level. These are small but statistically signiﬁcant effects."
