Evidence¶

Junko Yamashita 2013¶

Junko Yamashita. (2013). Effects of extensive reading on reading attitudes in a foreign language. Reading in a Foreign Language, 25(2). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl doi:10.64152/10125/66872

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

In the repeated-measures ANOVA of 61 Japanese EFL undergraduates, multiple comparisons showed a significant pretest–posttest increase in Intellectual Value with an effect size described as close to medium but still small (r = .26); Table 1 shows the mean rose from 3.97 to 4.10.
