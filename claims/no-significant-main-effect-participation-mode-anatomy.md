# No significant main effect of participation mode (online vs face-to-face) on microscopic anatomy learning outcomes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learning outcomes did not differ significantly between synchronous online and face-to-face participation in a microscopic anatomy course using a 3D virtual microscopy slide box. [→ Sternecker K 2026](#sternecker-k-2026)

## Evidence

### Sternecker K 2026

Sternecker K, Schmitz C, Berndt M. (2026). Students’ Learning Outcomes in a Virtual Microscopic Online and Face-to-Face Anatomy Course: Cross-Over Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/82012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model with restricted maximum likelihood estimation on examination scores from 415 students in a cross-over design. The model revealed "no significant main effect of participation mode (F1, 413=0.16; P=.69)". Mean scores were 11.6 (SD 2.7) online and 11.7 (SD 2.6) face-to-face.

> "The linear mixed-effects model, including participation mode (online vs face-to-face) and course topic (liver vs. kidney) as fixed effects and examination results as dependent variable, revealed no significant main effect of participation mode (F1, 413=0.16; P=.69)"
