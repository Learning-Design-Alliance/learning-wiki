## Evidence

### Stegers-Jager KM 2020

Stegers-Jager KM, Savas M, van der Waal J, van Rossum EFC, Woltman AM. (2020). Gender-specific effects of raising Year-1 standards on medical students' academic performance and stress levels. Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression using a compound score dividing participants into four groups by median PSS (26.00) and HCC (25.30), reference group LowLow; the HighHigh group showed the printed OR 0.26 (95% CI 0.11-0.62).

> "Only students with high values (above median) on both the PSS and HCC were significantly at risk of lower Year-1 academic performance (Wald χ2 (1) = 9.22, P < .01; OR 0.26, 95% CI 0.11-0.62), particularly male students"
