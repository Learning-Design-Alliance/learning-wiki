## Evidence

### İlter İlhan 2025

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Pearson correlations among 363 sixth-graders' survey scores and national exam scores; all teacher support correlations were significant and positive, with the largest r = 0.457 with self-efficacy, a correlation magnitude coded large.

> "The results showed that perceived TS was significantly and positively correlated with ASE (r = 0.457, p < 0.001), AB (r = 0.382, p < 0.001), and MP (r = 0.402, p < 0.001)."

### İlter İlhan 2025 (2)

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same correlation analysis; parental involvement correlated positively with self-efficacy (r = 0.448), buoyancy (r = 0.349), and mathematics performance (r = 0.332), all p < 0.001.

> "Similarly, PI was positively associated with ASE (r = 0.448, p < 0.001), AB (r = 0.349, p < 0.001), and MP (r = 0.332, p < 0.001)."
