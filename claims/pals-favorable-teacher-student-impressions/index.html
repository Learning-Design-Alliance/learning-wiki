Evidence¶

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies (PALS) 2001¶

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies (PALS). (2001). Evidence Rating: strong. https://www.evidenceforessuccess.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Implementation notes report teacher survey data from the Mathes & Babyak (2001) study, with "high mean ratings" across eight areas including reading achievement gains and social skills improvement; no effect sizes are printed for these ratings.

"teacher survey responses indicated generally favorable impressions of PALS, with high mean ratings for (a) Sounds and Words (b) Pretend Read (c) Partner Read-Aloud, (d) Retell, (e) reading achievement gains, (f) social skills improvement"

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies (PALS) 2001 (2)¶

Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies (PALS). (2001). Evidence Rating: strong. https://www.evidenceforessuccess.org

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Implementation notes summarize impressions from a second study, Mathes et al. (2001), where "both teachers and students conveyed mostly positive impressions" of the program and its reading impacts; no statistics are printed.
