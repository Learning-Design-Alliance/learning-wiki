## Evidence

### Eichmann 2019

Eichmann, B., Goldhammer, F., Greiff, S., Pucite, L., & Naumann, J. (2019). The role of planning in complex problem solving. Computers & Education, 128, 1-12. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.08.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Likelihood ratio tests comparing nested GLMMs for each indicator on log data from N = 1346 German students on 21 interactive PISA 2012 CPS tasks showed all more complex models fit better (all p < 0.001), confirming that "the models including both overall ( ﬁxed) and task- dependent random eﬀects ﬁt the data better."

> "The model comparisons for research questions 1 –3 show that the models including both overall ( ﬁxed) and task- dependent random eﬀects ﬁt the data better than models including only overall e ﬀects, as shown in Table 2.
