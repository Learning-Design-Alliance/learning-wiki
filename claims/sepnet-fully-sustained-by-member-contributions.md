`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

A descriptive account by the SEPnet team of the network's funding history across three phases. The authors state SEPnet "achieved its long-term goal of being fully sustained by the contributions of the partners", with partners paying membership fees and co-funding placements.

> "The external funding for SEPnet has reduced through its different phases and we are now at the point where SEPnet has achieved its long-term goal of being fully sustained by the contributions of the partners."
