## Evidence

### Jakob Schwerter 2022

Jakob Schwerter, Franz Wortha, Peter Gerjets. (2022). E-learning with multiple-try-feedback: Can hints foster students' achievement during the semester? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10105-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Random-effects panel regression of the bias score based on the last try of each task (Schraw, 2009) across twelve sessions for 87 students. The effect was marginal and the article treats it cautiously.

> "The negative coefficient in the model explaining the bias score (column (5)) is significant at the 10% level and corresponds to a 2% reduction in bias score."
