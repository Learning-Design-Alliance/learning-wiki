### Lechuga 2023

Lechuga, C. G., & Doroudi, S. (2023). Three Algorithms for Grouping Students: A Bridge Between Personalized Tutoring System Data and Classroom Pedagogy. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00309-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical evaluation against a baseline distribution of all possible size-5 groups from the class roster. The article reports the within-method averages were "almost always above the 90th percentile" and "above the 95th percentile half of the time"; the score method reached at least the 80th percentile the majority of the time. No effect sizes are printed.

> "the percentiles for the averages of (2) and (3) for the within-module grouping method were almost always above the 90th percentile among the distribution of all possible groups of size 5, and above the 95th percentile half of the time"
