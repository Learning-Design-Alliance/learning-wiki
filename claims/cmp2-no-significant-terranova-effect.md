# CMP2 did not have a statistically significant effect on grade 6 students' TerraNova mathematics achievement in a cluster randomized trial of Mid-Atlantic schools

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Grade 6 students in schools randomly assigned to adopt CMP2 scored no better than control-school peers on the TerraNova mathematics posttest (effect size = 0.02). [→ Martin 2012](#martin-2012)

## Evidence

### Martin 2012

Martin, T., Brasiel, S. J., Turner, H., & Wise, J. C. (2012). Effects of the Connected Mathematics Project 2 (CMP2) on the Mathematics Achievement of Grade 6 Students in the Mid-Atlantic Region (NCEE 2012-4017). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED530513

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Cluster RCT with 65 schools randomly assigned to CMP2 or business-as-usual, analyzed with a two-level hierarchical linear model of students nested within schools for 5,677 students. The main analysis found an impact of "less than one point (0.60)" on TerraNova posttest, not statistically significant.

> "The impact of CMP2 on student TerraNova posttest scores was less than one point (0.60), and was not statistically significant (effect size = 0.02; p = .777)."
