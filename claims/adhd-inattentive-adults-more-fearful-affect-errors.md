# Adults with ADHD-Inattentive type make significantly more fearful affect recognition errors than non-ADHD controls on the DANVA

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The ADHD-I group made significantly more fearful emotion errors than the control group (p = .018), with a large effect size (d = 0.93). [→ Miller 2011](#miller-2011)

## Evidence

### Miller 2011

Miller, M., Hanford, R. B., Fassbender, C., Duke, M., & Schweitzer, J. B. (2011). Affect Recognition in Adults with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3950374/

`q3 · i3`

One-way ANOVA on DANVA fearful affect errors across three diagnostic subgroups (ADHD-I n=16, ADHD-C n=17, control n=18) in a controlled cross-sectional study of 51 adults. "Post hoc tests using Tukey's correction for multiple comparisons revealed that the ADHD-I group made significantly more errors than did the control group (p = .018)." Table 2 shows Cohen's d = 0.93 for the ADHD-I versus control contrast on fearful errors.
