## Evidence

### Dormegny L 2026

Dormegny L, Schaeffer M, Neumann N, Yaïci R, Solecki L, Dechriste G, Koestel E, Gaucher D, Sauer A, Lansingh VC, Lejay A, Chakfé N, Bourcier T. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Prospective monocentric study of 47 surgeons in five experience groups completing two standardized MSICS runs on the HelpMeSee simulator; group comparison of simulator scores by ANOVA/Kruskal–Wallis. The "overall total score progressively and significantly increased (p < 0.001)" across ophthalmic experience levels.

> "The overall total score progressively and significantly increased (p < 0.001) from the novice residents' group (32.4 ± 10.7 out of 72) to the expert ophthalmic surgeons group (50.1 ± 9.41), while non-ophthalmic expert surgeons presented a lower mean score of (16.8 ± 18.0)."
