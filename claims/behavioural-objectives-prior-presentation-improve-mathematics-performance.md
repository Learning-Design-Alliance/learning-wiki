## Evidence

### M. F. Salman 2012

M. F. Salman, L. A. Yahaya, A. Yusuf, M. A. Ahmed & J. O. Ayinla. (2012). Effects of Behavioural Objectives-based Instructional Strategy on Senior School Students’ Academic Performance in Mathematics in Omu-Aran, Nigeria. Journal of Education and Learning, 1(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v1n2p121

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental, non-randomized, non-equivalent pre-test/post-test control group study of 179 SS2 students (88 experimental, 91 control) taught set theory for two weeks. Post-test means were 55.86 (SD 10.70) experimental versus 41.88 (SD 13.12) control; the article reports "significant differ ence in the post-test mean scores".

> "the calculated t-value was 7.80 and the ttab was 1.96 at α = 0.05 significant level. Since the calculated t-value is greater than that of the table value (i.e., 7.80>1.96) it means that there was significant differ ence in the post-test mean scores of the experimental and the control groups."
