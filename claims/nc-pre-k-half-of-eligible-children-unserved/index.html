The report's review of early childhood programs (Head Start, Smart Start, NC Pre-K, child care subsidies, and disability services) states that funding restricts access so "only about 50% of eligible children are able to attend NC Pre-K". The unserved count is cited to Barnett (2019); no effect size is printed.
