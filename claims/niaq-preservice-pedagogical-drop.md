## Evidence

### Christine Lindstrøm 2016

Christine Lindstrøm, Vinesh Rajpaul, Morten Brendehaug, and Megan C. Engel. (2016). Perspectives on astronomy: probing Norwegian pre-service teachers and middle school students. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.044](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.044)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Pre/post questionnaire study of 41 pre-service teachers at HiOA enrolled in Physics II in 2013–2014, analyzed with grounded-theory coding of free responses. Responses were categorized as pedagogical if they considered the hypothetical students, otherwise authoritative. The article reports "fewer than 10% of responses exhibited such characteristics" post-instruction.

> "Pre-instruction, more than a third responded in a pedagogical manner, displaying a clear focus on helping students learn and a willingness to admit deficiencies in their own knowledge; post-instruction, however, fewer than 10% of responses exhibited such characteristics"
