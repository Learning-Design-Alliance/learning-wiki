Samian et al. 2016¶

Hosein Vafadar Samian, Thomas Chow Voon Foo & Hassan Mohebbi. (2016). The Effects of Giving and Receiving Marginal L1 Glosses on L2 Vocabulary Learning by Upper Secondary Learners. English Language Teaching, 9(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n2p66

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-ANOVA Bonferroni analysis of translation test scores at posttest 1 and 2 found the giver group performed better than the receiver and control groups, with giver-control differences significant at p < .001 at both times. No effect size is printed.

"The results revealed that the giver group performed better than the receiver and control groups in the posttest 1 and 2 w ith a significant difference between the giver and control group, p < .001 in both testing times."
