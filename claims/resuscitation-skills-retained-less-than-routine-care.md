`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of the skills checklist for all 53 participants (Table 1). The article reports mean resuscitation skills at "66% vs 80, 72 and 74% respectively" for the other care domains, with items such as calling for help at 51% and giving 30–50 breaths per minute at 57%.
