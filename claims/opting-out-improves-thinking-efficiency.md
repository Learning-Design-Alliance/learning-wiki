Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman. (2024). Opting Out as an Untapped Resource in Instructional Design: Review and Implications. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09879-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Ackerman (2014), that in problem-solving contexts "allowing opting out cuts down wasted effort on attempts doomed to fail". The review characterizes this evidence as initial signs only.

> "Indeed, beyond the improvement in the quality of the provided information, initial signs from problem-solving contexts indicate that allowing opting out cuts down wasted effort on attempts doomed to fail (Ackerman, 2014)."
