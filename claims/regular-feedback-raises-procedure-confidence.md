The impact of gender on the self-confidence of practical and surgical skills among OBGYN residents: a trinational survey. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of their regression models in the Discussion; the Results report receiving regular feedback as a significant independent variable (gynaecological p = 0.01; hospital specialist p < 0.001). No gender difference in feedback receipt was found (p = 0.277).
