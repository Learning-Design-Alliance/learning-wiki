## Evidence

### Watkins 2010

Watkins, J., Hall, K., Redish, E. F., & Cooke, T. J. (2010). Understanding How Students Use Physical Ideas in Introductory Biology Courses. University of Maryland, College Park. [doi:10.1063/1.3515237](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515237)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview evidence from one student, Ashlyn, conducted after the diffusion-equation lecture as part of eleven student interviews across the semester. She reported memorizing equations but found that less useful than thinking about the concepts, and responded negatively to equations as referents for biological phenomena.

> "I think that biology is just —it's supposed to be tangible, perceivable, and to put that in terms of letters and variables is just very unappealing to me"
