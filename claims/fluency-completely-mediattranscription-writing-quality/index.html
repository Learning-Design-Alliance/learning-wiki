Evidence¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2024¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace. (2024). Writing Fluency: Its Relations With Language, Cognitive, and Transcription Skills, and Writing Quality Using Longitudinal Data From Kindergarten to Grade 2. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(4). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000841

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal SEM of kindergarten-to-Grade-2 data (N = 261) testing theory-guided mediation. Grade 1 transcription skills predicted fluency but were "not directly related to Grade 2 writing quality after accounting for Grade 2 writing fluency (ps ≥ .14)".

"Grade 1 handwriting fluency (.39, p < .001) and spelling (.26, p = .005) independently predicted Grade 2 writing fluency, but they were not directly related to Grade 2 writing quality after accounting for Grade 2 writing fluency (ps ≥ .14)."
