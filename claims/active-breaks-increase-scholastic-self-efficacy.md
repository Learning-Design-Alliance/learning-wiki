# A 12-week classroom-based physical activity break program significantly increases scholastic self-efficacy in adolescents compared with regular lessons

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Experimental-group students showed a large significant increase in scholastic self-efficacy from pre- to post-test, with a significant Time × Group interaction, while the control group did not change. [→ Latino Francesca 2023](#latino-francesca-2023)

## Evidence

### Latino Francesca 2023

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Francesco, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Domenico. (2023). Classroom-Based Physical Activity as a Means to Improve Self-Efficacy and Academic Achievement among Normal-Weight and Overweight Youth. Nutrients 15(9):2061. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15092061

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled study of 100 students (EG n = 50, CG n = 50) randomly assigned; two-way repeated-measures ANOVA on the 19-item scholastic self-efficacy scale showed a "meaningful “Time × Group” interaction" with η2p = 0.90; post hoc t = 40.34, d = 5.70 for the experimental group.

> "The results of a two-factor repeated measure ANOVA showed a meaningful “Time × Group” interaction for self-efficacy tests (F1,99 = 981.36, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.90, large effect size)."
