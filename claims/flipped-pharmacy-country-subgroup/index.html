Evidence¶

Cui He 2023¶

Cui He, Xie Xinyu, Wang Boyang, Zhao Yuan. (2023). Effectiveness of flipped classroom in pharmacy education – a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04865-2

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup meta-analysis by country (Table 3). Printed effect sizes: US SMD = 1.63 (1.53,1.72) across 22 comparing groups; Non-US SMD = 0.74 (0.58,0.91) across 6 comparing groups, all from Asian countries. Both subgroups showed high heterogeneity.

"The subgroup analysis by country of origin showed that the advantage of flipped classroom was observed in both US and Non-US countries. Non-US studies were all from Asia, involving China, Qatar, Thailand, and Malaysia."
