### Justyna P. Zwolak 2018

Justyna P. Zwolak, Michael Zwolak, and Eric Brewe. (2018). Educational commitment and social networking: The power of informal networks. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of five repeated social network survey collections across four MI-M sections, shown in Fig. 2. The article reports that "by midsemester all networks are basically fully developed" with about 95% of eventual members present; percentages are descriptive.

> "the evolution of the out-of-class networks shows that by midsemester all networks are basically fully developed and nearly all students (about 95 %) who will end up being in the network by the end of the semester are already present"
