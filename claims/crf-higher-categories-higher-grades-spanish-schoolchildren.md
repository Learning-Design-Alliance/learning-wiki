## Evidence

### Visier-Alfonso ME 2020

Visier-Alfonso ME, Sánchez-López M, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Jiménez-López E, Redondo-Tébar A, Nieto-López M. (2020). Executive functions mediate the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic achievement in Spanish schoolchildren aged 8 to 11 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231246

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA models of schoolchildren's language and mathematics grades by CRF categories, controlling for age, gender and mother educational level, found grades "significantly higher" in higher categories (p < 0.05), with no significance for "mean grades by CRF categories in boys".

> "The mean grades in both language and mathematics were significantly higher in children in the higher categories of CRF and executive function domains (p < 0.05), except for mean grades by CRF categories in boys and language by inhibition categories in girls"

### Visier-Alfonso ME 2020 (2)

Visier-Alfonso ME, Sánchez-López M, Martínez-Vizcaíno V, Jiménez-López E, Redondo-Tébar A, Nieto-López M. (2020). Executive functions mediate the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic achievement in Spanish schoolchildren aged 8 to 11 years. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231246

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Partial correlation analysis controlling for age and mother educational level, stratified by gender, found CRF "positively correlated with language, mathematics, inhibition and cognitive flexibility" in both genders; the CRF–working memory correlation was significant in boys only.

> "CRF was positively correlated with language, mathematics, inhibition and cognitive flexibility in both genders and was positively correlated with working memory in boys (p < 0.05), but the correlation coefficient between CRF and working memory was not significant in girls."
