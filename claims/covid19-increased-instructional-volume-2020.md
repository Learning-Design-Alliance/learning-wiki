Gore, J., Miller, A., Fray, L., Harris, J., & Prieto, E. (2021). The impact of COVID-19 on student learning in New South Wales primary schools: an empirical study. The Australian Educational Researcher, 48, 605-637. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13384-021-00436-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Teacher surveys at three time points in 2020 and Term 4 2019 reported weekly instructional hours. The study reports "more time was spent on literacy in the 2020 group" and more time on reading for comprehension than in 2019, with numeracy receiving more time in Term 4 2020 (x̄ = 7.04) after the return to schooling.

> "Overall, more time was spent on literacy in the 2020 group ( x = 9.52 h per week) compared to 2019 ( x = 8.48) and on reading for comprehension in 2020 ( x = 3.48 h per week) compared to 2019 ( x = 3.07)."
