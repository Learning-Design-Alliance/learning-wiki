Ji Hye Shin & Peggy Albers. (2015). An Analysis of the Effect of a Cyber Home Learning System on Korean Secondary School Students' English Language Achievement and Attitude. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 32(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083964

Survey items on intended use and tutoring substitution. Separately, 73.13% answered they would use CHLS twice a week or more. Students cited cost and stress of tutoring as reasons it could be replaced, and distraction and slow answers as reasons it could not.

> "Over 50% reported that they would never or rarely replace private face-to-face tutoring with CHLS, while another 25% would only sometimes replace private tutoring with CHLS"
