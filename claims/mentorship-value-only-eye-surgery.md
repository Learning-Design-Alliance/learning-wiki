## Evidence

### Jones Lee 2019

Jones Lee, Taylor Deanna J, Sii Freda, Masood Imran, Crabb David P, Shah Peter. (2019). The Only Eye Study (OnES): a qualitative study of surgeon experiences of only eye surgery and recommendations for patient safety. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on the value of surgical mentor: participant P3 said "A good mentor, I would say is key". P4 advised anyone doing only eye surgery to "find a mentor who's been doing this kind of surgery, talk to them, learn from them, how they approached it".

> "You need to be a good surgeon to do only eyes with confidence, and that means you need to have good teachers, who teach more than just the technique. A good mentor, I would say is key."

### Jones Lee 2019 (2)

Jones Lee, Taylor Deanna J, Sii Freda, Masood Imran, Crabb David P, Shah Peter. (2019). The Only Eye Study (OnES): a qualitative study of surgeon experiences of only eye surgery and recommendations for patient safety. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on mentoring barriers: participant P8 stated the mentoring role "has to be recognised". The discussion reports barriers including lack of formal recognition of the role, resulting in time commitment issues and a scarcity of appropriate mentors.

> "If trainees have a mentor who has been through lots of things, they can come through for support when things don't go right. But obviously that role has to be recognised."
