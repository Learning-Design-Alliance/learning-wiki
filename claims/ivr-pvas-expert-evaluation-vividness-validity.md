## Evidence

### Liu 2025

Liu, T.-C., & Lin, Y.-C. (2025). Development and implementation of an IVR-based assessment system for student teachers' professional vision. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10463-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Phase 4 evaluation of the beta version by four in-service teachers and one student teacher, using a vividness rating scale, a classroom-events appropriateness scale, and retrospective interviews on screen recordings. The article reports the vividness medians and that 'the appropriateness of classroom events was satisfactory for all the teachers.'

> "On a 7-point scale, most of the items (10 of 11) had a median score of 6 or 7. Furthermore, the appropriateness of classroom events was satisfactory for all the teachers, with median scores of 7 for reasonableness, authenticity, and necessary processing."
