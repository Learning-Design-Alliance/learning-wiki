## Evidence

### Sato Hiroyasu 2025

Sato Hiroyasu, Ogasawara Katsuhiko, Sakurai Hidehiko. (2025). Performance Evaluation of 18 Generative AI Models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity) in 2024 Japanese Pharmacist Licensing Examination: Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/76925

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Benchmark evaluation administering all 345 questions of the 107th JNLEP in Japanese to 18 OC-LLMs with no prompt engineering. The results section reports the four flagship models "was over 80%", above the 62.9% passing criteria; Table 2 lists Perplexity Pro highest at 0.872 (301/345).

> "The performance of the 4 flagship models (ChatGPT o1, Gemini 2.0 Flash Experimental, Claude 3.5 Sonnet [new], and Perplexity Pro) was over 80%, which was markedly higher than that of the passing criteria."
