`q2 · i2` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Borghans et al. (2016), Brookhart et al. (2016), and Hübner et al. (2020), that grade–test-score correlations "range from r =.30 to r =.60". The review attributes this to prior studies it did not re-analyze.

> "Evidence of the validity of grades is often sought by computing correlations between teacher-assigned grades and external performance measures, such as standardized test scores, which were found to range from r =.30 to r =.60 in prior studies"
