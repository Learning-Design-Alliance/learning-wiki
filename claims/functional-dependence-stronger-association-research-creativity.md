# Functional dependence shows a substantially stronger positive association with graduate students' research creativity than existential dependence (β = 0.457 vs β = 0.147)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Functional dependence's total association with research creativity (β = 0.457) is substantially larger than existential dependence's (β = 0.147) in separate mediation models. [→ Yin S 2026](#yin-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Yin S 2026

Yin S, Wu R, Liu X and Li R (2026) GenAI dependence and graduate students' research creativity: the roles of critical thinking and human–AI collaboration quality. Front. Psychol. 17:1896963. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1896963

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Differential-effects analysis comparing total effects from two separate PROCESS Model 6 mediation models (N = 1,157). The article reports "functional dependence exhibited a stronger positive association with research creativity in the present sample." The comparison is descriptive, not a formal test of coefficient difference.

> "the total association between functional dependence and research creativity (β = 0.457) was substantially larger than that observed for existential dependence (β = 0.147), suggesting that functional dependence exhibited a stronger positive association with research creativity in the present sample."
