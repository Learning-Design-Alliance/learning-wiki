## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ulfa Yunefit 2021

Ulfa Yunefit, Igarashi Yukari, Takahata Kaori, Shishido Eri, Horiuchi Shigeko. (2021). A comparison of team-based learning and lecture-based learning on clinical reasoning and classroom engagement: a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02881-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary outcome in the cluster RCT: the eight-item Classroom Engagement Survey (range 5–40, neutral 24) was administered after each of three sessions. Total CES means were 33.53 vs 22.34, 33.61 vs 21.68, and 34.03 vs 20.94, all p < .001.

> "There was a significant difference in the Total CES scores among the CES 1, CES 2, and CES 3 between the intervention group and the control group"

### Ulfa Yunefit 2021 (2)

Ulfa Yunefit, Igarashi Yukari, Takahata Kaori, Shishido Eri, Horiuchi Shigeko. (2021). A comparison of team-based learning and lecture-based learning on clinical reasoning and classroom engagement: a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02881-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same trial, the intervention group showed no significant CES changes across sessions (p > .05), whereas the control group's engagement declined significantly between consecutive sessions. No effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "There was a significant decrease in the mean CES scores from sessions 1 to 2 (p = .038), and from sessions 2 to 3 (p = .001) in the control group"
