## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Children who heard the brace label were more likely than control children to choose the braced building as the stronger one in the transfer task. [→ Zheng 2025](#zheng-2025)

## Evidence

### Zheng 2025

Zheng, Y. S., Goldwater, M., & Gentner, D. (2025). Structural Alignment and Linguistic Contrast Help Children Learn a Key Principle of Spatial Construction. Cognitive Science, 49(12), e70149. https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.70149

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 transfer task, logistic regression showed a significant language effect (odds ratio = 3.15, p = .001); the label group chose the braced building at "M Label = 0.88" versus 0.50 for controls. The label was delivered without pointing to the brace.

> "And those who heard the brace label were more likely to choose the braced building than those who did not ( M Label = 0.88, SD Label = 0.34; M Control = 0.50, SD Control = 0.51)."
