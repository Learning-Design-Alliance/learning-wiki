Ahmadi, P., Islam, K., & Yousaf, S. (2017). An Empirical Study of Teaching Methodologies and Learning Outcomes for Online and in-class Networking Course Sections. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.11411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.11411)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of average scores per assessment method plotted across sections (Figure 2). The article reports the DL section "did not perform well in labs as compared with the in-class sections", offering the help-access explanation as a conjecture.

> "The DL section did not perform well in labs as compared with the in-class sections perhaps because the students in F2F sections get in -class help, whereas the DL section students need to remotely contact the instructor and GTA for help/support."
