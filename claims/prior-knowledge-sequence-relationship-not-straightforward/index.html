Cheng-Wen He 2025¶

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Interpretation of the two randomized experiments (combined n = 505): Experiment 1's PS-I advantage supports productive failure while Experiment 2's I-PS advantage supports cognitive load theory, so "there may not be a straightforward relationship between students' level of prior knowledge and the optimal instructional sequence".
