Quantitative scoring of three LLMs on the 20-item 2025 CSAT Earth Science I section under full-page PNG input (4 pages at 300 DPI), compared against the official answer key. The article reports "Gemini 2.5 Flash and GPT -4o performed worse than random" and that even Gemini 2.5 Pro struggled with basic problem recognition.

> "In Experiment 1, Gemini 2.5 Flash scored 4 points (8%), GPT -4o scored 7 points (14%), and Gemini 2.5 Pro scored 14 points (28%) (Table 3). Compared to the expected value of random choice (10 points, 20%), Gemini 2.5 Flash and GPT -4o performed worse than random"
