## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T. 2021

Bonsaksen T., Magne T. A., Stigen L., Gramstad A., Åsli L., Mørk G., Johnson S. G., Carstensen T. (2021). Associations between occupational therapy students’ academic performance and their study approaches and perceptions of the learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02940-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same hierarchical regression (n = 174). The surface approach scale carried a negative standardized coefficient: "lower scores on surface approach (β = -0.20, p < 0.01)". The authors interpret this as supporting Ward's view that decreasing surface behaviors may be more beneficial than increasing deep behaviors.

> "Adjusting for all variables in the final model, being female was associated with obtaining better exam results (β = 0.22, p < 0.01). None of the learning environment scales were associated with the outcome. Among the study approach scales, better exam grades were associated with higher scores on strategic approach (β = 0.31, p < 0.001), and lower scores on surface approach (β = -0.20, p < 0.01)."
