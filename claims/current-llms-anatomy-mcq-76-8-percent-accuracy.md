# Current LLMs answer USMLE-style anatomy MCQs with an average accuracy of 76.8%, far above GPT-3.5's 44.4% a year earlier and above random guessing

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Four current LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) answered on average 76.8 ± 12.2% of 325 anatomy MCQs correctly, 27.7% above GPT-3.5 (44.4 ± 8.5%) and 3.7 times better than random responses (19.4 ± 5.9%). [→ Bolgova Olena 2025](#bolgova-olena-2025)

## Evidence

### Bolgova Olena 2025

Bolgova Olena, Mavrych Volodymyr (2025). Evolution of AI in anatomy education study based on comparison of current large language models against historical ChatGPT performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-22437-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Benchmark evaluation in January 2025 of four chatbots on a validated question bank of 325 USMLE-style MCQs from a Gross Anatomy course, each attempted three times (4,875 answers analyzed). The article reports "76.8 ± 12.2%" correct on average, versus GPT-3.5's 44.4% in October 2023 and 19.4% random responses.

> "on average, four tested LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude, Copilot, and Gemini) accurately answered 76.8 ± 12.2% out of 325 MCQs from 7 topics in the Gross Anatomy course"
