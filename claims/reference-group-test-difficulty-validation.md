Validation analysis with a separate reference group of emergency medicine interns; pre- and post-test difficulty did not differ significantly, "p = 0.76". No effect size printed.

> "Pre- and post-tests were found to be of comparable difficulty by utilizing a separate group of reference learners, emergency medicine interns, whose mean pre-test score was 55.83% ± 15.51 and mean post-test score was 58.33% ± 11.68. This difference was not significant with a mean difference of −2.5% [−17.67 to 12.67]; p = 0.76."
