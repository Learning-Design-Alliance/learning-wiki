## Evidence

### Goedegebuure E.P. 2026

Goedegebuure E.P., Boellaard T.N., van Dijk-de Haan M.C., et al. (2026). Improving membranous urethral length measurements on prostate MRI: a comparison of online training methods. European Radiology 36(8):6419-6427. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-026-12468-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ICC analysis of 81 participants' MUL measurements on 20 test cases before and after training. Pre-training ICCs fell in the "moderate" range (0.41–0.60) and post-training ICCs reached the "substantial" range (0.61–0.80) per the Landis-Koch scale cited in the article.

> "A positive effect was also seen for interreader agreement, with ICCs improving in all groups, from a baseline range of 0.52–0.56 to a post-training range of 0.74–0.80."
