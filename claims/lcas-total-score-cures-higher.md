## Evidence

### Corwin Lisa A. 2015

Corwin Lisa A., Runyon Christopher, Robinson Aspen, Dolan Erin L. (2015). The Laboratory Course Assessment Survey: A Tool to Measure Three Dimensions of Research-Course Design. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-03-0073

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Full-instrument known-groups comparison (n = 115: 60 CURE, 55 traditional) with Welch-corrected t test. Printed means were 75.10 versus 68.15 with "t(85.70) = 3.05, p < 0.01" and a small printed d of 0.13.

> "CURE students (M = 75.10, SD = 8.67) had significantly higher total ratings than traditional lab students (M = 68.15, SD = 14.76; t(85.70) = 3.05, p < 0.01, d = 0.13)."
