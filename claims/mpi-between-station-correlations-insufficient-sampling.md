The article's generalization-level critique reports "low correlations (r = 0.06–0.27)" between common constructs across MPI stations and concludes the four-station format does not sufficiently sample the assessed constructs. The lower bound r = 0.06 is a negligible printed effect size.

> "Additionally, low correlations (r = 0.06–0.27) between common constructs across stations ('between-station correlations') suggest that there are not enough MPI stations to sufficiently sample the assessed constructs and overcome context specificity; between-station correlations are high when constructs are aptly sampled [12]."
