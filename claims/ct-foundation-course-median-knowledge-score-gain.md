## Evidence

### Singh Veena Kumari 2025

Singh Veena Kumari, Singh Shruti, Tiwari Meenakshi, Haq Ansarul, Sharma Sarsij, Moudgil Tania. (2025). Unlocking minds: fostering critical thinking in postgraduates trainees through a foundation course - a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07355-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post study of 52 first-year postgraduate trainees at one institute, same 15-question instrument before and after a 15-hour module. The article reports "The students' median score increased from 17 to 23 out of 27" with Wilcoxon signed-rank testing at p < 0.05.

> "The students' median score increased from 17 to 23 out of 27, indicating an improvement (p < 0.05) in their overall median score post-course."
