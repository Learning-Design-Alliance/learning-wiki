Anne Podolsky and Leib Sutcher 2016¶

Anne Podolsky and Leib Sutcher. (2016). California Teacher Shortages: A Persistent Problem. Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606761

Survey results on staffing solutions (Figure 5): substandard credentials 55%, substitutes 24%, out-of-field 22%, vacancies 17%, larger classes 9%, canceled courses 8%. The quoted sentence gives the high- vs. low-poverty contrast.

"High-poverty districts report filling their vacancies with teachers who have substandard credentials more than twice as often as low-poverty districts (71% vs. 30%)"
