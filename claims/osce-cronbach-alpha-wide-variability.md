Descriptive synthesis of all 26 alpha values from 23 studies. The wide range (0.43-0.93) and the finding that 15 of 23 studies fell below the good-reliability threshold motivated the subgroup analyses by station number and duration.

> "Among the 23 studies analyzed, Cronbach's alpha values ranged from 0.43 to 0.93 (Fig. 2). 11 studies demonstrated good internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha >= 0.8), while 15 studies did not reach the good standard (Cronbach's alpha falling between 0.43 and 0.79)."
