## Evidence

### Fiona Draxler 2023

Fiona Draxler, Henrike Weingärtner, Maximiliane Windl, Albrecht Schmidt, and Lewis L. Chuang. (2023). Useful but Distracting: Keyword Highlights and Time-Synchronization in Captions for Language Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Vocabulary pre-test, immediate post-test, and 2-day delayed post-test (modeled after Nation's Vocabulary Size Test) in the 49-participant within-subject study. On average participants answered 88.0% correctly before, 87.5% right after, and 89.0% in the delayed test; "clear ceiling effects" were observed and H4 was not confirmed.

> "There were no differences in the score changes from before watching the videos to the 2-day delayed post-tests when differentiated by caption type. We observed clear ceiling effects: some participants already knew all the words tested for a condition and could, therefore, not improve their score."
