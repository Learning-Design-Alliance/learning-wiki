## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A median-split comparison found an effect of students' initial performance on their post-test score; t(20) = 2.589, p = 0.018, which the authors read as lower initial performers improving more. [→ Katsanos 2018](#katsanos-2018)
`q2 i?` Students' initial performance had no effect on their normalized learning gain; Mann-Whitney U z = 1.292, p = 0.197. [→ Katsanos 2018 (2)](#katsanos-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Katsanos 2018

Katsanos, C., Xenos, M. and Tselios, N. (2018). Tool-mediated HCI Modeling Instruction in a Campus-based Software Quality Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Median-split low and high pretest performance groups of the campus-based cohort; an independent samples t-test on post-test scores printed 't(20) = 2.589, p = 0.018', with no effect size reported.

> "A two-tailed independent samples t-test found an effect of student's initial performance on their post-test score; t(20) = 2.589, p = 0.018."

### Katsanos 2018 (2)

Katsanos, C., Xenos, M. and Tselios, N. (2018). Tool-mediated HCI Modeling Instruction in a Campus-based Software Quality Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-91238-7_10)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same median-split groups analyzed on normalized learning gain, using a non-parametric test because the high initial performance group's gain distribution violated normality; the article prints 'z = 1.292, p = 0.197'.

> "In addition, a two-tailed Man-Whitney U test found no effect of students' initial performance on their normalized learning gain; z = 1.292, p = 0.197."
