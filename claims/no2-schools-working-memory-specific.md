## Evidence

### Gartland Nicola 2022

Gartland Nicola, Aljofi Halah E., Dienes Kimberly, Munford Luke Aaron, Theakston Anna L., van Tongeren Martie. (2022). The Effects of Traffic Air Pollution in and around Schools on Executive Function and Academic Performance in Children: A Rapid Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19020749

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's quality-weighted synthesis of NO2 evidence: only two high-quality studies measured NO2, both Barcelona cohort studies, reporting "negative effects of NO2 on long-term working memory" with no attentional impact. No effect size is printed for this synthesis.

> "Only two studies rated high-quality measured levels of NO2; Sunyer and colleagues [50] and Forns and colleagues [46] both report negative effects of NO2 on long-term working memory but no impact on attention."

### Gartland Nicola 2022 (2)

Gartland Nicola, Aljofi Halah E., Dienes Kimberly, Munford Luke Aaron, Theakston Anna L., van Tongeren Martie. (2022). The Effects of Traffic Air Pollution in and around Schools on Executive Function and Academic Performance in Children: A Rapid Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19020749

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

A cross-sectional London study (n = 719, 9–10-year-olds) cited by the review found no relation between estimated outdoor NO2 and working memory on the Search and Memory Task; the review attributes mixed cross-sectional findings partly to climatic and pollution-level differences.

> "Clark and colleagues [45] did not find a relation between estimated outdoor NO2 levels (using the King's College London Emissions Toolkit) and working memory, as assessed by the Search and Memory Task in 9–10-year-olds."
