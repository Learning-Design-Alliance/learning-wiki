# Generative AI-generated hints can be as effective as human-generated hints for mathematics performance, but ensuring accuracy requires validation procedures that discard a substantial share of generated hints

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Generative AI-generated hints were as effective as human-generated hints at improving student mathematics performance, yet expert review discarded 25% to 47% of GPT-3.5-generated hints depending on topic, and a GPT-4 self-verification procedure identified 14% of hints as inaccurate or grade-inappropriate. Narrative review reporting Pardos and Bhandari's expert-review procedure for GPT-3.5-generated hints, with "between 25% and 47%" discarded depending on topic. The review separately cites evidence that AI-generated hints matched human-generated hints in effectiveness.
