Evidence¶

Salmerón 2023¶

Salmerón, L., Altamura, L., Delgado, P., Karagiorgi, A., & Vargas, C. (2023). Reading comprehension on handheld devices vs. on paper: A narrative review and meta-analysis of the medium effect and its moderators. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000830

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The review's synthesis of prior meta-analyses (Delgado et al., 2018; Clinton, 2019; Schwabe et al., 2022), attributed as the review attributes it. It notes no previous study had directly compared genre and medium using exclusively handheld devices.

"In sum, previous research has identified text genre as a relevant moderator of the screen inferiority effect, with stronger negative effects for expository than narrative texts."
