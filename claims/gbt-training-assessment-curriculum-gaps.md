### Dong Mohan 2024

Dong Mohan, Liu Changhao, Yan Junfang, Zhu Yong, Yin Yutian, Wang Jia, Zhang Ying, Wei Lichun, Zhao Lina. (2024). A survey on brachytherapy training of gynecological cancer focusing on the competence of residents in China. Radiation Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13014-024-02433-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of senior residents across 28 Chinese training bases; Table 1 shows 46.97% (62/132) attended special GBT evaluation and 56.82% (75/132) participated in a GBT curriculum. The article reports "no specific tests for GBT were conducted during their training" for half of residents.

> "Regarding entrustability for finishing brachytherapy, 50.76% (67/132) of residents confirmed that no specific tests for GBT were conducted during their training."
