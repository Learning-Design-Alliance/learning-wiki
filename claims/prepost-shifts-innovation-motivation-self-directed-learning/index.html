Hong Qu 2026¶

Hong Qu, Zichao Ling, Yadie Yang. (2026). Integrating Flipped Learning and Generative AI for Practice-Based Design Education: Evidence from a Knit Yarn Design Course. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18505 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.18505

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre–post questionnaire comparison within the intervention cohort (n = 16). Innovation and Motivation rose from 3.64 to 4.16 (change 0.52, CI [0.23, 0.83]); Self-Directed Learning Ability rose from 3.59 to 4.08 (change 0.48, CI [0.22, 0.75]). No effect size printed.
