# Directly prompted Claude 3.7 macro-level deep feedback was judged effective for 93.03% of 201 paragraph-level comments

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After third-rater resolution, 187 of 201 macro feedback comments generated by Claude 3.7 were finalized as effective (93.03%), with strong inter-rater agreement (Gwet's AC1 = 0.89). [→ Adnan Labib 2026](#adnan-labib-2026)

## Evidence

### Adnan Labib 2026

Adnan Labib, Yixuan Huang, Jiahui Wu, John Maurice Gayed, Zheng Yuan, Qiao Wang. (2026). WrAFT: a Modularized Automated Writing Evaluation System for Argumentative Essays. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14524 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.14524](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2607.14524)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Human evaluation of Claude 3.7 macro feedback on 201 paragraphs across the 40 test essays, rated for effectiveness by two expert raters with a third resolving disagreements; inter-rater AC1 was 0.89 (95% CI 0.82-0.93, p <.001).

> "After a third rater resolved the disagreement of the two raters, the number of effective comments was finalized as 187 (93.03%) and that of ineffective comments, 27 (6.97%)."
