Laboratory measurements using a photoelectric photometer for light and a microwave transmitter-receiver, plotted as relative intensity versus 1/r2 (Fig. 1a, b). The printed fits include I/I0 = 0.0151x with R2 = 0.998 for light and R2 = 1 for microwaves.

> "The analysis of these graphs shows that in- tensities of visible light as well as invisible microwaves decrease inversely proportional to the square of the dis- tance from the source."
