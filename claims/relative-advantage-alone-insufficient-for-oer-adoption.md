This is the editorial's own interpretive synthesis of the seven responses, offered as an argument rather than a tested result. It contrasts cost-savings framings with "expansive openness" (Kimmons 2016). No empirical data or effect size is printed for this takeaway.

> "First, relative advantage of OER alone will not lead to their rapid adoption and the achievement of their proposed benefits of democratization, equity, and educational improvement, especially if relative advantage is merely conceptualized as cost savings (as compared to more "expansive openness" Kimmons [2016])."
