## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of six SLP-related multiple-choice questions versus 74 other questions on the 2012 cumulative final exam (MS n = 23, HS n = 37), a summative assessment about 12 wk after the service-learning experience. The article reports scores were "significantly higher on the four genetics questions compared with the rest of the exam questions" (p < 0.05).

> "Both MS and HS undergraduates scored significantly higher on the four genetics questions compared with the rest of the exam questions, as determined by an exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test (p < 0.05), suggesting that students had better comprehension of this material than the rest of the course material."
