## Evidence

### Causby RS 2017

Causby RS, McDonnell MN, Reed L, Fryer CE, Hillier SL. (2017). A qualitative evaluation of scalpel skill teaching of podiatry students. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-017-0202-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive qualitative content analysis of educator interviews; the article reports the primary controversy regarded the use of inanimate objects versus real skin, with participants quoting that "practising on real skin works best". No effect size is printed.

> "Many participants highlighted that whilst wax models or similar may be useful for initial practice and technique development, it cannot completely replace real skin."
