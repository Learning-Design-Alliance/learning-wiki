## Evidence

### Christensen L 2020

Christensen L, Rasmussen CS, Benfield T, Franc JM. (2020). A Randomized Trial of Instructor-Led Training Versus Video Lesson in Training Health Care Providers in Proper Donning and Doffing of Personal Protective Equipment. Disaster Medicine and Public Health Preparedness. https://doi.org/10.1017/dmp.2020.56

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observation from viewing calendars submitted after evaluation in the pilot trial: videos were watched on average 4.9 times (donning) and 5.2 times (doffing), and were "most watched on the first and last days." The authors suggest just-in-time viewing may explain some video-group participants outperforming instructor-trained peers.

> "Although video group participants could watch the videos as many times as they wished and whenever they wanted, it was observed that most of them had watched the video on the assessment day, effectively receiving “just-in-time” training."
