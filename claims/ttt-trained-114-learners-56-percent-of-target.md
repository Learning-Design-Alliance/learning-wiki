## Evidence

### Mayrhofer A 2016

Mayrhofer A, Goodman C, Smeeton N, Handley M, Amador S, Davies S. (2016). The feasibility of a train-the-trainer approach to end of life care training in care homes: an evaluation. BMC Palliative Care 15:11. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0081-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Programme audit data from the nine-month TTT pilot across 17 care homes in the East of England. The article reports "28 trainers and 114 learners (56 % of the targeted number of learners) had been trained"; three homes met or exceeded the 12-learner target and two trained none.

> "At the end of the TTT programme 28 trainers and 114 learners (56 % of the targeted number of learners) had been trained (median per home 6, range 0–13)."
