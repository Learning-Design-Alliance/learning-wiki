Evidence¶

Desimone and Hill 2017¶

Laura M. Desimone, Kirsten Lee Hill. (2017). Inside the Black Box: Examining Mediators and Moderators of a Middle School Science Intervention. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373717697842

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Structural equation model using data from 10,281 students and 124 teachers in a clustered RCT. The article reports teachers in the CS condition "report levels of overall implementation 0.63 standard deviations higher than teachers in the control condition" and a 0.05 SD achievement increase per SD of implementation (p = .06); no standardized effect size is printed for these paths.

"teachers in the CS condition report levels of overall implementation 0.63 standard deviations higher than teachers in the control condition, even after controlling for major and years of experience (p = .000)"

Desimone and Hill 2017 (2)¶

Laura M. Desimone, Kirsten Lee Hill. (2017). Inside the Black Box: Examining Mediators and Moderators of a Middle School Science Intervention. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373717697842

q3 · i? · causal · r2

SEM results section: the fitted model (CFI = 0.873, RMSEA = 0.019) showed the treatment had a direct but nonsignificant effect on achievement, and the article concludes the effect "is fully mediated by these paths."

"there was no significant direct effect of the CS intervention after inclusion of implementation and content knowledge in our model, suggesting that the effect is fully mediated by these paths"
