Evidence¶

Vereș 2020¶

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' recommendation from the conclusions, based on their documentation phase finding that, unlike the USA (brainpop.com), Romania lacks an animation film database intended for Science learning. This is a stated recommendation, not a tested result.

"Consequently, we recommend the setup of several animated films in Romanian language for learning Sciences, not only for the primary education, but also for the other education cycles."
