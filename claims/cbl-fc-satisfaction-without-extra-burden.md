# CBL/FC students are more satisfied with classes than LBT students without spending more preparation time

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` CBL/FC students in Grade 4 were more satisfied with the class (4.21 ± 0.72 vs. 3.54 ± 0.83, P < 0.01) while pre-class time spent showed no difference between groups (2.92 ± 0.28 vs. 3.17 ± 0.96, P = 0.23). [→ Hu Bibi 2024](#hu-bibi-2024)
`q3 i?` Grade 5 CBL/FC students were also more satisfied (4.27 ± 0.55 vs. 3.09 ± 0.97, P < 0.01) with no difference in pre-class time (3.18 ± 1.14 vs. 3.14 ± 0.64, P = 0.87). [→ Hu Bibi 2024 (2)](#hu-bibi-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Hu Bibi 2024

Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire data from the randomized study of nephrology-rotation students: Grade 4 satisfaction favored CBL/FC while time spent before class did not differ between groups. The article prints only means and P values, no effect size.

> "The time spent before the class showed no difference between the two groups (2.92 ± 0.28 vs. 3.17 ± 0.96, P = 0.23). However, the CBL/FC group students were more satisfied with the class (4.21 ± 0.72 vs. 3.54 ± 0.83, P < 0.01)."

### Hu Bibi 2024 (2)

Hu Bibi, Wang Li, Wu Junxia, Zhu Lina, Chen Zhida. (2024). A combination of case-based learning with flipped classroom improved performance of medical students in nephrology bedside teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05973-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The Grade 5 questionnaire results showed the same pattern: higher satisfaction in the CBL/FC group and no group difference in pre-class time spent. The article notes prior studies found students reported extra burden with CBL or FC, but this study did not.

> "The time spent before the class showed no difference between the two groups (3.18 ± 1.14 vs. 3.14 ± 0.64, P = 0.87). However, the CBL/FC group students were more satisfied with the class (4.27 ± 0.55 vs. 3.09 ± 0.97, P < 0.01)"
