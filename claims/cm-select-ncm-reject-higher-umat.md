## Evidence

### Shulruf Boaz 2020

Shulruf Boaz, O'Sullivan Anthony, Velan Gary. (2020). Selecting top candidates for medical school selection interviews- a non-compensatory approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02031-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of mean UMAT scores between the two disagreement groups in the six-cohort applicant data (Table 5 reports means of 62.27 vs. 59.93). The article reports the difference as "significantly higher" without printing a test statistic or effect size.

> "Interestingly however, the mean UMAT scores of the 'CM select and NCM reject' category were significantly higher than that of the 'CM reject and NCM select' group (Table 5)."
