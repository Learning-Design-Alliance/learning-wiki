## Evidence

### Jasmin Breitwieser 2026

Jasmin Breitwieser, André Bittermann, Maria Theobald, Tim Lauer, Mirijam Schaaf, Garvin Brod. (2026). Digital Prompting in Education: A Design Framework and Bibliometric Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10164-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Network analysis of prompt type co-occurrences within studies using multi-level modularity optimization, identifying ten clusters. The article reports the printed co-occurrence statistics and notes that, apart from the strong co-occurrence between cognitive and metacognitive prompts, most frequencies were relatively low.

> "In fact, the majority of prompt labels (224 of the 283 types; 79.15%) did not co-occur with any other labels. This indicates that most studies focus narrowly on a single prompt characteristic, with limited exploration of how different strategies or formats interact or compare."
