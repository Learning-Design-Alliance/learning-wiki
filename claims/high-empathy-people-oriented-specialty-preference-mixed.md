## Evidence

### Chen Hao 2024

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Santos et al.'s cross-sectional study of 226 Brazilian medical students used self-reported questionnaires (JSPE, IRI) for empathy and specialty preferences; the review reports the quoted contrast, consistent with several other included studies.

> "compared with students who preferred technology-oriented majors, students with an inclination toward people-oriented specialties typically exhibit more empathy"

### Chen Hao 2024 (2)

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

An Iranian study of 459 medical students (Benabbas, 2016) used convenient sampling, JSE, and two-way ANOVA to assess empathy and specialty preference; the review reports no statistically significant relationship, and other studies reached similar conclusions.

> "there was no statistically significant relationship between them, as evidenced by the results"
