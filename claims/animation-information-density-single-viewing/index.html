Vereș 2020¶

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

Content analysis of the film's soundtrack by visual and thematic methods, reported in the discussion of the film analysis. The film covers four concepts and 11 named cosmic bodies.

Vereș 2020 (2)¶

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' conclusion drawn from the low Test 2 gains and the film's information density. This is an interpretive statement, not a separately tested result.

"However, a large amount of information offered by an animated film while presented in a short period of time, cannot be learned following a viewing and can have a negative effect on their understanding and learning."
