## Evidence

### Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly 2025

Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly. (2025). Astronomy Identity Framework for Undergraduate Students and Researchers. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/wf14-c1yp](https://doi.org/10.1103/wf14-c1yp)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding: Diego experienced imposter syndrome when starting exoplanet research but, welcomed by his research community and supported by two mentors, came to see himself as an astronomer. The article cites prior work that graduate students were 1.77 times more likely to persist with a secondary mentor.

> "I feel very welcomed in the community. And I feel like that is what has allowed me to see myself as more of an astronomer."

### Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly 2025 (2)

Zachary Richards; Angela M. Kelly. (2025). Astronomy Identity Framework for Undergraduate Students and Researchers. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/wf14-c1yp](https://doi.org/10.1103/wf14-c1yp)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative finding: Leticia built competence through hands-on research, learning programming, and learning from failures despite gender and ethnic biases; Alejandra and Diego similarly reported growing confidence through research tasks.

> "Learning how to program has made me way more confident in my research abilities. And just the practice of being in these academic spaces has made me more confident"
