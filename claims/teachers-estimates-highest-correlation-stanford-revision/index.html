Evidence¶

Chassell 1921¶

Chassell, C. F., & Chassell, L. M. (1921). A survey of the three first grades of the Horace Mann School by means of psychological tests and teachers' estimates, and a statistical evaluation of the methods employed. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(5), 243-252. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0068285

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of survey data from three first grades, comparing teachers' rankings in maturity and ability in reading with Stanford Revision mental ages. The article reports the printed correlation r = .72 (60 cases), which codes as a large effect (i = 3).

"An examination of the correlations reported for the Stanford Revision shows that the highest correlation between this measure and any other single measure appears in the case of teachers' estimates, nameh", .72 (60 cases being included)."
