Evidence¶

Emily Wolk 2018¶

Emily Wolk, Sharon Bi, Tran Keys. (2018). Are test and academic disengagement related? Implications for measurement and practice. Educational Assessment 24(2), 119-134. https://www.nwea.org/research-publications/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The study analyzed whether academic disengagement indicators (chronic absenteeism, course failures) relate to test disengagement (rapid guessing); "Our results provide evidence that academic and test disengagement are related".

"we examine whether behaviors indicative of academic disengagement like chronic absenteeism and course failures are related to behaviors indicative of test disengagement like rapidly guessing on items"
