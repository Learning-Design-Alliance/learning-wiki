### Michael J. O'Brien and John R. Thompson 2008

Michael J. O'Brien and John R. Thompson. (2008). Effectiveness of Ninth-Grade Physics in Maine: Conceptual Understanding. The Physics Teacher. http://scitation.aip.org/tpt/ [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0901.4692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0901.4692)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-test comparison of five sub-groups in the seven-school Maine study (Table II). Honors 9th-grade groups (N=28 traditional, N=76 Modeling) reached normalized gains of 35% with p-values of 0.000 versus pretest, described as "even above that of the 12th grade classes" (23%).

> "The honors-level 9th graders had the highest post-test scores and normalized gains of any of the sub-groups, even above that of the 12th grade classes."
