## Evidence

### Gambari 2016

Gambari, I. A. & Yusuf, M. O. (2016). Effects of Computer-Assisted Jigsaw II Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Achievement and Retention. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(4), 352-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117586

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of retention scores in the same quasi-experimental study (n = 80), administered after four weeks. The article reports "an F (1, 77 ) = 17 .436, p = 0.000" for the retention main effect, a statistically significant difference favoring Jigsaw II.

> "Table 6 revealed that an F (1, 77 ) = 17 .436, p = 0.000 for the main effect ( retention) was significant"

### Gambari 2016 (2)

Gambari, I. A. & Yusuf, M. O. (2016). Effects of Computer-Assisted Jigsaw II Cooperative Learning Strategy on Physics Achievement and Retention. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(4), 352-367. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1117586

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of pre-test to retention-test mean gain scores in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports Jigsaw II retention gain of 42.95 in Table 7 and describes Jigsaw II as having "better retention".

> "Jigsaw II had highest mean gain scores of 63.02 while the (ICI) had the least mean gain scores of 56.47. This indicates that the two groups benefited fro m the treatment, with Jigsaw II having better retention."
