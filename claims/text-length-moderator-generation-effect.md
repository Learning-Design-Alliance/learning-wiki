## Evidence

### Julia Schindler · Tobias Richter 2023

Julia Schindler · Tobias Richter. (2023). Text Generation Benefits Learning: a Meta-Analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09758-w

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subset moderator analysis of text length as a four-category variable in the multi-level meta-analysis. Significant pairwise differences emerged between short vs. 301–600 words and between 900+ words and the shorter categories; the 900+ category had only k = 12 effect sizes.

> "The effect was medium-sized for studies based on short texts (0–300 words, g = 0.36, p < 0.001), largest for studies with texts ranging from 301–600 words (g = 0.63, p < 0.001), somewhat smaller in studies with texts ranging from 601–900 words (g = 0.43, p < 0.001), and small and nonsignificant in texts of more than 900 words (g = 0.05, p = 0.738)."
