Spreckelsen C., Juenger J. (2017). Repeated testing improves achievement in a blended learning approach for risk competence training of medical students: results of a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1016-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis comparing participants with profiling scores above 80% (roughly the top 25%) to those at or below 80%, using bootstrapped confidence intervals and line charts (Fig. 3). The article reports no positive repeated-testing effect in the high-scoring subgroup; no effect size is printed.

> "Students with high profiling score did not profit equally from repeated testing: In this group the study did not find a positive effect of repeated testing on top of the effect of the standard training."
