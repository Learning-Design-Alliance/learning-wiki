## Evidence

### Shiying Wang 2026

Shiying Wang, Joan Bosson-Heenan, Andrew Grotzinger, Jan C. Frijters & Jeffrey R. Gruen. (2026). Shared genetic architecture of reading and attention disorders using genomic structural equation modeling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00433-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analysis in Genomic SEM fitting a parallel model in which ADHD was replaced with anxiety disorders from the PGC GWAS. The article reports anxiety "did not load significantly and model fit was poor (SRMR >0.1)"; leave-one-reading-trait-out models kept fit good and the ADHD loading stable. No effect size is printed.

> "In the model including reading and anxiety disorders (Supplemental Note 2),  anxiety disorders did not load significantly and model fit was poor (SRMR >0.1), indicating that the common factor model is specific to reading and ADHD."
