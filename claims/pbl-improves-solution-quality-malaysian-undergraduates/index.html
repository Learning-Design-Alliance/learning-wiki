Evidence¶

Z. Abdul Kadir 2016¶

Z. Abdul Kadir, N. H. Abdullah, E. Anthony, B. Mohd Salleh & R. Kamarulzaman. (2016). Does Problem-Based Learning Improve Problem Solving Skills?—A Study among Business Undergraduates at Malaysian Premier Technical University. International Education Studies, 9(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n5p166

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study (n=50, 25 per group). Mann-Whitney U on post-test solution quality: "the value was 20.88" for non-PBL versus 30.12 for PBL; the article reports z = -2.913 and p = 0.004 for quality.

"From the aspect of quality, the mean rank value pres ented was 30.12 for PBL group and for the non-PBL group, the value was 20.88. Thus, it can be concluded that students in PBL group had better quality in their solutions of the problems."
