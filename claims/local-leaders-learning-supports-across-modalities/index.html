Hayley Weddle 2022¶

Hayley Weddle, Ayesha K. Hashim, Ogechi Irondi. (2022). District and school leaders’ perspectives on leading & learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Policy Innovation Collaborative. https://epicedpolicy.org/district-leaders-perspectives-on-the-covid19-pandemic/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

This is the second research question of the EPIC report on Michigan district responses to COVID-19. The Return to Learn legislation directed understanding of how schools met educational goals "across in-person, hybrid, and remote instructional modalities."

"b) How did local leaders support student learning in similar and different ways across instructional modalities?"
