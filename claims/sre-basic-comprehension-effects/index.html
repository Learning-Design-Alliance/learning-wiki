Evidence¶

Liang 2011¶

Liang, L. A. (2011). Scaffolding Middle School Students' Comprehension and Response to Short Stories. RMLE Online, 34(8). ISSN 1940-4476. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ925248

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

ANCOVA of basic comprehension quiz scores (multiple-choice 0–10 points; short-answer Part 1 0–4 points) during the experimental weeks. Both SRE treatments fostered basic understanding of the stories, with effect sizes of ".08" and ".09".

"A significant main effect was noted for treatment on the multiple-choice quizzes, F(1, 158) = 13.98, p < .001, with an effect size of .08; for Part 1 of the short-answer quizzes, F(1, 158) = 15.49, p < .001, with an effect size of .09;"
