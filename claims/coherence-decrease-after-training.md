# Coherence scores decreased after game training for both novices and advanced learners

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The coherence measure showed a decrease after training with the game for both novices and advanced learners, which was significant for advanced learners and weakly significant for novices. [→ Wouters 2011](#wouters-2011)

## Evidence

### Wouters 2011

Wouters, P., van der Spek, E. D., & van Oostendorp, H. (2011). Measuring learning in serious games: A case study with structural assessment. Educational Technology Research and Development, 59(6), 741–763. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-010-9183-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Related-samples Wilcoxon tests on coherence scores from 19 participants. The article reports the novices' decrease was "weakly signiﬁcant" (Z = -1.82, p = .069) while the decrease "was signiﬁcant for the advanced learners" (Z = -2.19).

> "For novices the decrease in coherence after the game was weakly signiﬁcant ( Z =- 1.82, p was .069), while the decrease was signiﬁcant for the advanced learners ( Z =- 2.19, p\ .05)."
