Rudmann 2023¶

Rudmann, O., Batruch, A., Visintin, E. P., Sommet, N., Bressoux, P., Darnon, C., ... Butera, F. (2023). Cooperative Learning Reduces the Gender Gap in Perceived Social Competences. A Large-Scale Nationwide Longitudinal Experiment. Journal of Educational Psychology (accepted for publication). https://osf.io/qarxk/?view_only=e772332576a5421cbf61b3092e567ac9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Model 3 tested the three-way interaction on utility value of social competences; the increase in gender differences was more pronounced in the two control conditions than in the Jigsaw condition.

"our third and focal experimental hypothesis, which focused on the three-way interaction between the conditions, gender, and time, was also supported, c2 (4, N = 23,329) = 2.93, p = .020 (see Model 3)."

Rudmann 2023 (2)¶

Rudmann, O., Batruch, A., Visintin, E. P., Sommet, N., Bressoux, P., Darnon, C., ... Butera, F. (2023). Cooperative Learning Reduces the Gender Gap in Perceived Social Competences. A Large-Scale Nationwide Longitudinal Experiment. Journal of Educational Psychology (accepted for publication). https://osf.io/qarxk/?view_only=e772332576a5421cbf61b3092e567ac9

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Comparison of standardized gender differences at Time 3 on utility value; the Jigsaw gap (β = -.15) was 50% smaller than Business-as-usual (β = -.35) and, per the article, 25% smaller than Resource independence (β = -.20).

"The gender gap at Time 3 was reduced by 50% in the Jigsaw classroom condition (β = -.15, SE = .05, p = .002) when compared to the Business-as-usual condition (β = -.35, SE = .05, p < .001)"
