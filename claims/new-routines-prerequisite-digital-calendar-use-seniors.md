## Evidence

### Baric 2019

Baric, V., Andreassen, M., Öhman, A., & Hemmingsson, H. (2019). Using an interactive digital calendar with mobile phone reminders by senior people - a focus group study. BMC Geriatrics, 19, 116. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-019-1128-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of the focus groups ('RemindMe challenges habits' category): participants changed habits such as carrying the phone in a pocket rather than a handbag, scheduling reminders earlier, and using a pencil to press buttons when answering SMS.

> "The development of new routines or adjustments to already established daily routines were described by participants as a prerequisite for using RemindMe. The participants mentioned developing new routines that required scheduling reminders earlier so they could receive them before leaving home."
