## Evidence

### Klostermann 2015

Klostermann, B. K., Pareja, A. S., Hart, H., White, B. R., & Huynh, M. H. (2015). Restructuring principal preparation in Illinois: Perspectives on implementation successes, challenges, and future outlook (IERC 2015-3). Edwardsville, IL: Illinois Education Research Council at Southern Illinois University. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/restructuring-principal-preparation-illinois-perspectives-implementation-successes

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Challenge reported in the I-PREP statewide scan. Respondents reported that districts are being asked to do more under the new policy, including investing staff time and dollars to support internships, provide mentors, and complete considerable amounts of paperwork, and that principal candidates experience challenges with increased out-of-pocket expenses.

> "Programs faced with decreased enrollment experience fewer tuition dollars, despite providing a more comprehensive principal preparation program with higher standards."
