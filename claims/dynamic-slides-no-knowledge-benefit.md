### Reuter 2025

Reuter, T., Lazzara, L., & Leuchter, M. (2025). Static, dynamic, or human? The role of slide dynamics and instructor cues in video lectures. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10572-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the between-subjects experiment with 238 preservice teachers, hierarchical multilevel regression on knowledge of samples showed that "The planned contrasts showed no effect concerning the three different lecture formats." The same non-significant pattern was reported for research designs and measurement.
