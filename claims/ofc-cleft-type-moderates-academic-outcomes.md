Narrative synthesis across seven cited studies of isolated orofacial clefts (Bell et al., Clausen et al., Fitzsimons et al., Persson et al., Watkins et al., Wehby et al.). The review reports cleft lip only carried lower risk; no pooled effect size was printed for this moderator pattern.

> "Findings from most studies of children with isolated OFC agreed that poorer academic outcomes were associated with cleft type, reporting a lower risk for children with cleft lip only, who often performed similarly to controls"
