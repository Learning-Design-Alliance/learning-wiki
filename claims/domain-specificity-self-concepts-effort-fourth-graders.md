## Evidence

### Lohbeck 2019

Annette Lohbeck. (2019). Social and dimensional comparison effects on academic self-concepts and self-perceptions of effort in elementary school children. Educational Psychology, 39:1, 133-150. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2018.1527018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis with 200 fourth graders comparing nested models (Table 1). Self-concept difference tests: math Δχ² = 10.355, German Δχ² = 10.611, df = 1, p < .001; the six-factor combined model was also permissible (CFI = 0.956).

> "Similarly, for self-perceptions of effort, the global 1-factor model fitted significantly poorer to the data than the 2-factor model, supporting the domain-specificity of selfperceptions of effort (chi-square difference test: Model 4 vs. Model 5: Dv2 ¼ 127.617, df ¼ 1, p < .001)."
