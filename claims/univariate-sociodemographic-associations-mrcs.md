## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Chi-square tests found statistically significant associations between first-attempt MRCS pass rates and gender, ethnicity, age, graduate status, parental education, POLAR quintile, school type, free school meals and IMD quintile for Part A, and a partly overlapping set for Part B (all p < 0.05). ## Evidence

### Ricky Ellis 2022

Ricky Ellis, Peter A Brennan, Amanda J Lee, Duncan SG Scrimgeour, Jennifer Cleland. (2022). Differential attainment at MRCS according to gender, ethnicity, age and socioeconomic factors: a retrospective cohort study. Journal of the Royal Society of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/01410768221079018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate chi-square analysis of first-attempt pass/fail outcomes for 5780 Part A and 2600 Part B candidates. The article reports significant associations across the listed sociodemographic variables with "all p < 0.05"; no effect sizes are printed for these univariate tests.

> "We found statistically significant associations between MRCS Part A pass rates and gender, ethnicity, age, graduate status, parental education, POLAR quintile, school type, entitlement to free school meals and IMD quintile (all p < 0.05)."
