### Rafey IA 2026

Rafey IA, Abdullah A, Saeed Z, Khan UU, Mohyuddin W. (2026). Comparative analysis of university lectures, textbooks, and online video lectures: preferences, engagement, and impact on academic performance among medical students of Nowshera Medical College. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09921-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Platform usage data from the cross-sectional survey (Table 2), analyzed by chi-square across academic years. Females showed significantly greater use of YouTube, Sketchy, and Osmosis than males; no gender differences for Prepladder, BNB, or Kaplan.

> "YouTube was the most popular platform across all academic years, but especially among pre-clinical students. In contrast, platforms like Prepladder, BNB, Kaplan, Sketchy, and Osmosis were minimally used by pre-clinical students but their popularity increased sharply from 3rd year onward."
