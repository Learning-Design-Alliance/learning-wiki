### Andy Hegedus 2018

Andy Hegedus, Ed.D. (2018). Evaluating the Relationships between Poverty and School Performance. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research-data-galleries/poverty-and-school-performance

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same observational analysis of MAP Growth schools, plotting median student growth percentile against school %FRL (Figure 2.2). The article reports "a weak negative relationship between %FRL and median student growth in a school" and that the difference between schools at either end of the %FRL continuum is about 4 percentile points, suggesting growth is less biased by the income of the population a school serves.

> "As shown in Figure 2.2, there is a weak negative relationship between %FRL and median student growth in a school (about 1% of median growth is accounted for by %FRL)."
