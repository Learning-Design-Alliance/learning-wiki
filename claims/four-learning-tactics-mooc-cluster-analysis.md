# Cluster analysis of MOOC trace data identifies four learning tactics: Search oriented, Content and assessment oriented, Content oriented, and Assessment oriented

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yizhou Fan 2021

Yizhou Fan, Wannisa Matcha, Nora’ayu Ahmad Uzir, Qiong Wang, Dragan Gašević. (2021). Learning Analytics to Reveal Links Between Learning Design and Self-Regulated Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 31, 980–1021. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00249-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cluster analysis (EM algorithm) of 201,038 learning sessions from 8,788 learners in a flipped-classroom MOOC, using "the proportion of learning actions", "the total time spent online and the total number of actions" as inputs. Four tactics were detected, with the largest cluster accounting for 31.86% of sessions.

> "the application of the approach detected four learning tactics (Search oriented, Content and assessment oriented, Content oriented and Assessment oriented) which were used by MOOC learners."
