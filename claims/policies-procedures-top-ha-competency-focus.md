### Jabarethina G 2025

Jabarethina G, John KC, Anitha R. (2025). Evolving roles of hospital administrators in India: a qualitative study on health information technology competencies for improved healthcare delivery. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-092133

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey-style mapping in which 27 administrators selected pertinent competencies from the HITCOMP administrative interaction quadrant; table 4 reports focused-area percentages, with policies and procedures at 63% and risk and compliance at 48%.

> "The results given in table 4 indicate that policies and procedures represent 63% of the HA-focused area of competencies, with 48% coming from risk and compliance, 37% from standards and protocols, 33% from general management and legal, 22% from financial and account management, and 22% from project management."
