Evidence¶

Mery 2014¶

Mery, Y., DeFrain, E., Kline, E., & Sult, L. (2014). Evaluating the Effectiveness of Tools for Online Database Instruction. Communications in Information Literacy, 8(1). https://journals.lib.pdx.edu/index.php/informationliteracy

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive result from the control group (n = 30) of the randomized experiment, which took the 16-item post-test without tutorial instruction. The article reports the mean as "M = 51.04%" and interprets it as a general skills gap.

"Furthermore, the control group’s low scores (M = 51.04%) indicate that undergraduate students who have not received library instruction generally lack these basic research skills."
