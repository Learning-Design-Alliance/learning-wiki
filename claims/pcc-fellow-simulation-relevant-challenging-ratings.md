## Evidence

### Mannarino Candace MD 2020

Mannarino Candace MD, Bradley Erin MD, Puro Amanda MD, Sung Deborah MD, Wolfe Katie MD. (2020). Pathophysiology for the Pediatric Critical Care Fellow: Three Representative Simulation Cases. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10931

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous 5-point Likert evaluation forms completed by the six fellow learners in each of two representative simulation cases (hepatic encephalopathy; sepsis with coagulopathy and acute kidney injury). All six "strongly agreed that the content was relevant to their clinical practice". No effect size is reported.

> "For cases 1 and 2, all six fellow learners indicated that they strongly agreed that the content was relevant to their clinical practice, that the case was challenging, and that this simulation event would improve their clinical practice."
