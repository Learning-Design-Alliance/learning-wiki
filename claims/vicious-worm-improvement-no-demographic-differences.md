## Evidence

### Hobbs Emma C. et al 2018

Hobbs Emma C. et al. (2018). Preliminary assessment of the computer‐based Taenia solium educational program ‘The Vicious Worm’ on knowledge uptake in primary school students in rural areas in eastern Zambia. Tropical Medicine & International Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/tmi.13029

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate and multivariable Poisson regressions on QS2 individual data (n = 59) testing neighbourhood, gender and age effects on improvement, controlling for potential for improvement. No significant differences were detected; equivalence was not tested.

> "On average, 4.0 questions improved from being answered incorrectly in the ‘pre’ questionnaire round to correct in the ‘post’ round (4.3 for males, 3.6 for females). No significant differences were found between villages (P = 0.503), gender (P = 0.446) or age (P = 0.926)."
