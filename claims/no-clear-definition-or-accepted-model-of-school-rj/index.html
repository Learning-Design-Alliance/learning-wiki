Evidence¶

Hurley 2015¶

Hurley, N., Guckenburg, S., Persson, H., Fronius, T., & Petrosino, A. (2015). What Further Research is Needed on Restorative Justice in Schools? WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559727

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study of 43 RJ experts and practitioners conducted between January and October 2014; the article reports this as an expert reservation, offering no evidence beyond the interviews. Experts stated "There is neither a clear definition nor a widely accepted model of RJ."

"Experts also expressed some reservations about RJ research, chiefly: There is neither a clear definition nor a widely accepted model of RJ."
