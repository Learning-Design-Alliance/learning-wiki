## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students reported that the app's preformatted questions and low time investment made engaging in structural reflection easy and reduced mental workload. [→ Ivo Bril-Broers 2026](#ivo-bril-broers-2026)

## Evidence

### Ivo Bril-Broers 2026

Ivo Bril-Broers, Nick Degens, Joke Fleer. (2026). Exploring the Potential for Smartphone-Based Reflection to Support Socioemotional Competence Development in Student Nurses' Clinical Placement: Mixed Methods Field Trial. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/91083

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative survey responses during the field trial: a student described the daily reflection feature's ease of use, saying "the questions make it easy, so it takes less time." The discussion attributes reduced mental workload to removing practical barriers.

> "It brings me a lot. And the questions make it easy, so it takes less time."
