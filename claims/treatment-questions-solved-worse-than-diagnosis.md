### Strasser Livia Maria 2026

Strasser Livia Maria, Anschuetz Wilma, Dennstädt Fabio, Hastings Janna. (2026). Performance Evaluation of Large Language Models in Multilingual Medical Multiple-Choice Questions: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/81399

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis of accuracy by question purpose (diagnosis, treatment, other) across models and languages. The article reports treatment questions significantly worse than diagnosis (OR 0.55, 95% CI 0.34-0.91; P<.001); other-related questions appeared worse but were not significant after correction.

> "Compared to diagnosis, treatment questions were solved significantly worse (mean accuracy 81%, SD 0.39% vs mean accuracy 70%, SD 0.45%; OR 0.55, 95% CI 0.34-0.91; P<.001)"
