Evidence¶

Cabrera 2018¶

Cabrera, P., Castillo, L., González, P., Quiñónez, A., & Ochoa, C. (2018). The impact of using Pixton for teaching grammar and vocabulary in the EFL Ecuadorian context. Teaching English with Technology, 18(1), 53-76. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170640

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-questionnaire of the 85 experimental students after the four-month intervention, reported in section 4.1 and Table 1; for vocabulary, 54.97% said Pixton was very useful and 13.90% highly useful. These are descriptive percentages with no inferential test.

"the use of Pixton for learning EFL grammar was very useful (51.06%). Furthermore, an significant amount of students (19.14%) asserted that comic strips are highly useful for learning grammar."
