# Most active learning comparison studies differ on more than one course feature, limiting attribution of effects to single features

## Subclaims

## Evidence

Feature-difference analysis across the 59 studies, examining the 14 course features. The review reports "seven of the 59 (11.9%) studies where only one feature differed"; the active learning conditions averaged 5 course features. Descriptive counts only.
