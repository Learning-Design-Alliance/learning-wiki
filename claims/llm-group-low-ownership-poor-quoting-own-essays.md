## Evidence

### Nataliya Kosmyna 2009

Nataliya Kosmyna, Eugene Hauptmann, Ye Tong Yuan, Jessica Situ, Xian-Hao Liao, Ashly Vivian Beresnitzky, Iris Braunstein, Pattie Maes. (2009). Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task. Preprint, under review. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08872

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-assessment interviews conducted after each session with participants in all three groups. The article reports that "The reported ownership of LLM group's essays in the interviews was low" and that the LLM group's quoting ability lagged the other groups. No effect sizes are printed for these interview findings.

> "The reported ownership of LLM group's essays in the interviews was low. The Search Engine group had strong ownership, but lesser than the Brain-only group. The LLM group also fell behind in their ability to quote from the essays they wrote just minutes prior."
