## Evidence

### David Wittman 2010

David Wittman. (2010). Shaping Attitudes Toward Science in an Introductory Astronomy Class. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3321 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.3321](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.3321)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post matched survey in the author's general education astronomy course, with n = 63 students matched by student ID. The largest gains included the evidence-based science item at 57% (4.44, 4.76) and the atoms-from-stars item at 52% (3.30, 4.19); the author notes "Several attitudes improved by many times the statistical uncertainty, with a normalized gain of ∼50%".

> "Several attitudes improved by many times the statistical uncertainty, with a normalized gain of ∼50%, comparable to the typical normalized gain for subject matter in eﬀective courses."
