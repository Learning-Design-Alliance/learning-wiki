Evidence¶

Feng Z and Huang K 2026¶

Feng Z and Huang K (2026) Bayesian cognitive diagnosis optimizes personalized learning paths via mediation of cognitive load and Hidden Markov Model state transitions. Front. Psychol. 17:1879982. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1879982

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Between-group comparison on an adapted six-dimension NASA-TLX (mental demand, time pressure, effort, frustration, attention, learning clarity; higher scores indicate greater load) in the 120-participant randomized experiment, with "d ranging from 1.12 to 1.80".

"The experimental group exhibited signiﬁcantly lower cognitive load on all six dimensions, with eﬀect sizes all at a large level (d ranging from 1.12 to 1.80). Among them, the learning clarity dimension showed the largest between-group diﬀerence (d = 1.80)"
