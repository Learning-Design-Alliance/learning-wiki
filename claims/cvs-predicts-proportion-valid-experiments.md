## Evidence

### Gerdesmann 2026

Gerdesmann, D., Schwichow, M., & Koerber, S. (2026). Factors that explain simulation-based inquiry performance in physics learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09806-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression at the trial level, where each trial comparing conditions A and B was coded valid when exactly one independent variable was varied, tested hypothesis H2.3. "Higher CVS skill (OR = 1.20, CI [1.16, 1.23]) emerged as the strongest predictor"; quasi-binomial and beta sensitivity checks yielded nearly identical results.

> "Higher CVS skill (OR = 1.20, CI [1.16, 1.23]) emerged as the strongest predictor, with smaller effects from extraneous load (OR = 0.93, CI [0.89, 0.97]) and content knowledge (OR = 1.06, CI [1.03, 1.09])."
