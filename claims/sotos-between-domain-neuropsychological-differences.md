## Evidence

### Butti 2025

Butti, N., Urgesi, C., Decio, A., Pezzani, L., Milani, D., and Montirosso, R. (2025). A Further Characterisation of the Neuropsychological Profile, Social Perception, and Academic Skills in Sotos Syndrome. Journal of Intellectual Disability Research, 69: 664–674. https://doi.org/10.1111/jir.13250

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

RM-ANOVA on the six NEPSY-II domain scores in 23 participants with Sotos syndrome (single-cohort cross-sectional study). The omnibus effect was significant with a large printed effect size (n 2 p = 0.16), with memory and learning exceeding language and visuospatial processing after Bonferroni correction.

> "The analysis highlighted significant differences between domains (F 5,110  = 4.16, p = 0.002, n 2 p  = 0.16). Bonferroni‐corrected post hoc tests clarified that the memory and learning domain had higher scores than language (p = 0.011) and visuospatial processing (p = 0.036)."
