## Evidence

### Swain 2025

Swain, W., Leung-Gagné, M., Maier, A., & Rubinstein, C. (2025). Community schools impact on student outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/541.498

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Statewide matched difference-in-differences analyses across attendance, suspension, and achievement outcomes found "the largest benefits were among Black students, English learners, and socioeconomically disadvantaged students"; the executive summary translates differential impacts for Black students to roughly 130 days of additional learning in math and 151 in ELA.

> "For both absences and achievement, we find the largest benefits were among Black students, English learners, and socioeconomically disadvantaged students."
