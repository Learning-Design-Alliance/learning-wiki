## Evidence

### Feldacker Caryl 2015

Feldacker Caryl, Chicumbe Sergio, Dgedge Martinho, Cesar Freide, Augusto Gerito, Robertson Molly, Mbofana Francisco, O'Malley Gabrielle. (2015). The effect of pre-service training on post-graduation skill and knowledge retention among mid-level healthcare providers in Mozambique. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-015-0011-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate generalized estimating equations (N = 600) modelling each exam type with a time-by-curriculum interaction (Aim 3 results, Table 6). The physical exam interaction was "7.12% over time (CI: 3.21, 11.02)"; the knowledge-test interaction was not significant.

> "Clinical case scores overall decreased an average of 3.85% (CI: −6.51, −1.18) over time. However, the revised group increased an additional 6.71% over time (CI: 3.32, 10.10), meaning that the revised group scored 2.86% higher, on average, at follow-up than their peers."
