## Evidence

### van Eeden 2017

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank matched pairs test on the intervention group (N = 29) comparing cognitive subscale composite scores before and after the programme. The article reports "a significant increase after the intervention (Z = -4.32, p < 0.01)" on the Cognitive Scale; no effect size is printed.
