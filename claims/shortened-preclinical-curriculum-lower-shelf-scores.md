### Astroth C 2026

Astroth C, Lunow-Luke T, Smith M, Chang J, Grigorian A, O'Leary M P. (2026). Shelf help: what powers medical student subject exam performance in the United States. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2708427

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reports observational associations between shortened pre-clinical timelines and reduced shelf exam performance, citing Kelley et al. and Huerta et al. A 2025 JAMA study found 69.5% of 157 schools shortened pre-clinical curriculum to 1.5 years or less.

> "Such changes have been found to be associated with lower shelf exam scores [7,8], and there is a growing body of evidence to suggest that the foundational medical knowledge, gained during the preclinical years or otherwise, may be essential for future success on shelf exams."
