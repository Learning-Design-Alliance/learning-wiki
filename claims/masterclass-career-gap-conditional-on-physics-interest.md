## Evidence

### Sandra Leone 2016

Sandra Leone. (2016). The Masterclass of particle physics and scientific careers from the point of view of male and female students. 38th International Conference on High Energy Physics. [doi:10.22323/1.282.0319](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.282.0319)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis of the questionnaire data: the sample was divided into students interested and not interested in physics outside school, and within those groups "the two distributions are very similar, for male and female students" for the would-you-like-to-be-a-scientist question. Descriptive only.

> "We divided the sample in students who declared to be (not to be) interested in physics outside school, and their answer to the previous question is shown in fig. 3 left (right). Now the two distributions are very similar, for male and female students."
