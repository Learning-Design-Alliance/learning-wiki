Albardía MS 2025¶

Albardía MS, Peña-Fernández S and Agirreazkuenaga I (2025) Technology, education and critical media literacy: potential, challenges, and opportunities. Front. Hum. Dyn. 7:1608911. https://doi.org/10.3389/fhumd.2025.1608911

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of 141 university students in Communication and Education programs. The article reports a contrast between the 97.8% who "recognize the importance of acquiring tools for critical media analysis" and the 13.8% who say the skills were addressed in coursework, suggesting educational systems may not adequately address media literacy.

"Although most young respondents (97.8%) recognize the importance of acquiring tools for critical media analysis, only 13.8% say these skills were addressed in their coursework."
