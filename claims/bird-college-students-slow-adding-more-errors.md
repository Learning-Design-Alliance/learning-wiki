## Evidence

### Bird 1921

Bird, Grace E. (1921). The Devious Path of Slow Work. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ123456

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In Bird's experiment with "one hundred college students" using Courtis research tests, rapid adding for two minutes yielded a median of three errors; when "cautioned to work slowly and accurately," the median rose to four with quartile deviation 0.8.

> "For two minutes the students were required to work as quickly as possible. The median number of errors was found to be three, the quartile deviation 0.5."
