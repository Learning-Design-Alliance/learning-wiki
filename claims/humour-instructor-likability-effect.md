## Evidence

### Bender 2026

Bender, L., Renkl, A., & Endres, T. (2026). Punchline with(out) purpose: Integrating research on instructional humour and seductive details. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA/MANCOVA planned contrasts in both studies. Study 1: likability F(1,111) = 4.61, p = .034; Study 2: F(1,146) = 5.65, p = .019. Situational interest and atmosphere contrasts were non-significant in both.

> "In both studies, irrelevant humorous elements increased perceived instructor likability, but had no clear effect on situational interest or the learning atmosphere compared to the conditions without humour."
