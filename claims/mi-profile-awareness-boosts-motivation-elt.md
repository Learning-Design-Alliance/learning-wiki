## Evidence

### Madkour and Mohamed 2016

Magda Madkour & Rafik Ahmed Abdel Moati Mohamed. (2016). Identifying College Students’ Multiple Intelligences to Enhance Motivation and Language Proficiency. English Language Teaching, 9(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n6p92

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two groups of level-3 English students (n=108 total). Motivation was measured via grades, attendance, and class observation; the article reports motivation reached "91% after integrating the theory of multiple intelligences," an increase of 63 percentage points over the 28% baseline.

> "As displayed in Figure 4, students’ motivation reached 91% after integrating the theory of multiple intelligences in teaching English, based on analysis of students’ grades and attendance percentage, and class observation, indicating an increase of 63%."
