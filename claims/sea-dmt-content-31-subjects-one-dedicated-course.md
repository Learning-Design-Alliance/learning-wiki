`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase 1 documentary review of study plans and syllabi at four SEA dental schools (75 courses Cambodia, 61 Malaysia, 31 Singapore, 114 Thailand). The review "revealed a total of 31 subjects that incorporated at least some content related to DMT," with the remaining subjects spread across oral and maxillofacial surgery, paediatric dentistry, endodontics and other specialties.

> "This revealed a total of 31 subjects that incorporated at least some content related to DMT. Among these, only one course at CMU offered a dedicated subject in dental traumatology."
