### O'Reilly 2026

O'Reilly, F., Loeb, S., Farrell, A., & Scerif, G. (2026). Supporting Parents to Support Children: A U.K. Randomized Controlled Trial Testing a Text Message Intervention to Cultivate the Home Learning Environment. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000973

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis replicating the appointed evaluators' intention-to-treat impact analysis of the two-arm RCT, using multilevel mixed-effects models on the YARC primary outcome. The article reports no significant improvement in reading-related skills; no effect size for this null finding is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "Our analysis found no significant improvements in reading-related skills or socioemotional outcomes nor any meaningful differences in response to the program based on these subgroups."
