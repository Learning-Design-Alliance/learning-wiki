# State of the Evidence

> Generated by `scripts/build_evidence_report.py` from the claim pages' evidence codes and the `[±~][SMW]` markers on the pages that cite them. Do not edit by hand.

What the wiki's evidence adds up to. Every number counts **distinct studies**, keyed by DOI (or by author-year where a study has none), because one source often yields many claims. Nothing here is a verdict: tiers are reported as ranges and counts, and the impact codes are never averaged. `q`, `i`, kind and rigour are defined in the [Schema & Guide](CLAUDE.md) and in `evidence-scales.json`.

## Toward pooled estimates

A pooled effect size needs effect sizes with their uncertainty, on one scale, for comparable contrasts and outcomes. Those are recorded in `observations/`, which holds **19 study records**: 2 report a standardised mean difference directly and 6 more report a statistic convertible to one (a correlation, an odds ratio, a partial η²). Fewer than three studies are on that scale at all, so **no pooled estimate is reported**. The wiki's `i` codes are not a substitute: they are bins, and averaging them would state a precision nobody measured.
